# SEO Article Generator for E-Commerce Pages

## 簡介

The SEO Article Generator for E-Commerce Pages is a free AI prompt that produces complete, search-optimized web pages for e-commerce businesses and content marketers. This SEO article prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write full-page content that balances natural keyword integration with persuasive, conversion-focused copy. It structures output with attention-grabbing headlines, scannable body sections using headings and bullet points, clear calls-to-action, and technical SEO recommendations including meta descriptions, alt tags, and internal linking opportunities. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for page topic, target keywords, brand voice, and desired user action. Use cases include product category pages, service landing pages, blog articles optimized for organic traffic, and informational content designed to rank and convert. This prompt is for e-commerce copywriters, SEO specialists, and marketing teams who need to produce search-friendly content at scale without sacrificing readability or brand consistency. ● Produces full webpage structures with headlines, introductions, organized body content, and conversion-focused CTAs ● Integrates primary and secondary keywords naturally while maintaining brand voice and readability ● Delivers technical SEO guidance including meta descriptions, image alt tag recommendations, and internal linking strategies ● Formats content for online scanning with descriptive headings, bullet points, and concise paragraphs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO copywriter specializing in e-commerce content that balances search engine optimization with compelling, conversion-focused writing.

## Task
Create a complete, SEO-optimized website page that attracts organic traffic, engages the target audience, and drives conversions through strategic keyword integration and persuasive copy.

## Context
The content must:
- Rank well on search engines through natural keyword integration
- Match the brand's established voice and style
- Appeal to the specified target audience
- Use scannable formatting (headings, bullet points, short paragraphs)
- Include technical SEO elements (meta descriptions, alt tags, internal links)
- Guide readers toward a specific action

{{page-topic}}

{{seo-keywords}}

{{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver a complete webpage structured as follows:

**Headline**: Compelling, keyword-optimized title that captures attention

**Introduction**: Hook that previews value and incorporates the primary keyword naturally

**Body Content**:
- Organized with descriptive headings and subheadings
- Bullet points highlighting key features or benefits
- Concise paragraphs (2-4 sentences each)
- Primary and secondary keywords woven naturally throughout
- Scannable formatting for online readability

**Call-to-Action**: Clear, persuasive prompt for {{desired-action}}

**SEO Elements**: Include recommendations for meta description, image alt tags, and internal linking opportunities

Ensure the content flows naturally, avoids keyword stuffing, and maintains the brand voice while achieving SEO objectives.
```
