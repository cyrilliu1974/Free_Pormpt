# Freemium Product Strategy Builder for SaaS and Apps

## 簡介

The Freemium Product Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete freemium business model for SaaS products, mobile apps, and digital platforms. This freemium product strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your product and market overview as input and outputs a structured plan covering target market analysis, value proposition definition, feature differentiation between free and premium tiers, user lifecycle tactics from acquisition through retention, pricing models, key performance indicators, and a phased implementation roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering actionable recommendations grounded in conversion optimization and monetization best practices. Product managers use it to design balanced feature sets that attract users with free functionality while reserving high-value capabilities for paid plans; founders rely on it to validate pricing strategies before launch; growth teams apply it to diagnose conversion bottlenecks in existing freemium funnels. ● Maps the full user journey from acquisition to retention with specific tactics for onboarding, engagement, and conversion triggers. ● Clearly delineates which features belong in free versus premium tiers to maximize sign-ups while preserving upgrade incentives. ● Proposes pricing models, tier structures, and price points aligned with target market willingness to pay. ● Defines measurable KPIs and a phased implementation roadmap so teams can execute and iterate systematically. ## Prompt
