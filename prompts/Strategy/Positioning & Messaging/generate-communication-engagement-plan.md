# External Communication Engagement Plan Generator

## 簡介

The External Communication Engagement Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive outreach strategies for organizations seeking to engage customers, partners, and stakeholders effectively. This communication engagement plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your organization context, key stakeholder groups, and communication goals as inputs and produces a complete strategic plan covering objectives, audience profiles, tailored messaging, channel selection with tactical recommendations, implementation timelines, success metrics, and budget estimates. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and follows strategic communication best practices to ensure clarity, consistency, and measurable impact across email, social media, PR, events, and other channels. Use it when launching new initiatives, managing stakeholder relations, building brand awareness, or coordinating crisis communications that require targeted, multi-audience outreach. ● Identifies and profiles multiple audience segments with their unique communication needs and preferences ● Defines clear objectives, core messages, and channel rationales aligned to stakeholder characteristics ● Provides actionable tactics, phased timelines, ownership assignments, and KPI frameworks for execution ● Outputs budget estimates and resource requirements to support realistic planning and approval processes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic communication expert who designs comprehensive, multi-channel outreach plans to engage stakeholders and drive measurable outcomes.

## Task
Develop a structured external communication plan for {{organization-context}} that identifies key audiences, defines clear objectives, and outlines tailored strategies and tactics to reach customers, partners, and stakeholders.

## Context
The plan must:
- Address the following stakeholders and their needs: {{key-stakeholders}}
- Achieve these communication goals: {{communication-goals}}
- Leverage best practices in messaging, channel selection, and engagement
- Ensure clear, compelling, and consistent messaging across all channels
- Avoid generic or one-size-fits-all approaches

## Output
Provide a complete communication plan using this structure:

**Communication Objectives:**
- Objective 1
- Objective 2
- Objective 3

**Key Audiences:**
For each audience (minimum 3), provide:
1. Name
2. Profile (demographics, role, characteristics)
3. Communication Needs (preferences, concerns, information requirements)

**Key Messages:**
List 3-5 core messages tailored to the identified audiences

**Communication Channels:**
For each channel (minimum 3), provide:
1. Channel Name
2. Rationale (why this channel for these audiences)
3. Tactics (specific actions and content types)

**Timeline and Responsibilities:**
Outline phasing, milestones, and ownership for plan implementation

**KPIs and Measurement:**
Define specific metrics and tracking approach to measure success against objectives

**Budget and Resources:**
Estimate costs and personnel/tools required for execution
```
