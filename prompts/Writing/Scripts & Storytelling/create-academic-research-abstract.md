# Interview Questions Generator for Journalists

## 簡介

The Interview Questions Generator for Journalists is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive, logically structured set of interview questions tailored to any interviewee profile. This interview questions prompt for ChatGPT walks you through six distinct stages - opening rapport, career trajectory, deep expertise, public discourse, personal dimension, and forward reflection - to ensure every conversation reveals depth, insight, and narrative arc. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to the interviewee's background to produce questions that balance respect with probing curiosity. Journalists use it to prepare for profiles, podcasts, feature articles, and video interviews where thoughtful preparation makes the difference between surface-level Q&A and compelling storytelling. Reach for this prompt when you need to interview a founder, creator, executive, artist, researcher, or public figure and want questions that engage both the subject and your audience. ● Produces six thematic sections that guide the conversation from warm-up to reflection ● Balances open-ended rapport questions with specific, expertise-focused inquiries ● Includes a section for addressing controversies or public positions with respect and directness ● Tailors question depth and tone to the interviewee profile you provide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced journalist crafting interview questions that reveal depth, insight, and narrative.

## Task
Develop a structured set of interview questions for {{interviewee-profile}} that elicit thoughtful responses and uncover compelling insights about their work, journey, and perspectives.

## Context
The questions should progress logically from establishing rapport to exploring professional milestones, expertise, and personal dimensions. Balance respect with probing curiosity to create a conversation that engages both the interviewee and audience.

## Structure

**Opening (Rapport & Context)**
Begin with open-ended questions about recent work or background that set a comfortable tone.

**Career Trajectory**
Explore pivotal moments, challenges overcome, and key achievements. Focus on how experiences shaped their perspective and approach.

**Deep Expertise**
Ask questions requiring detailed responses that showcase their knowledge—current trends, future predictions, advice for others in the field.

**Addressing Public Discourse**
If applicable to {{interviewee-profile}}, include respectful but direct questions about notable controversies or public positions, allowing clarification or elaboration.

**Personal Dimension**
Include questions about life outside work—passions, hobbies, influential figures—to reveal the person behind the professional.

**Forward & Reflection**
Conclude with questions about future goals, aspirations, and any message they wish to share with your audience.

## Output
Deliver questions formatted clearly, each designed to build narrative momentum. Ensure each question adds value and respects the interviewee's time while serving audience interest.
```
