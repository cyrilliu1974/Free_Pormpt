# Multi-Channel Content Promotion Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Multi-Channel Content Promotion Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops actionable promotion plans across social media, email, and advertising channels for marketers and business owners looking to boost sales. This content promotion strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured plan covering platform selection, posting cadence, email campaign sequences, audience targeting parameters, budget allocation, and a measurement framework with KPIs. It works by taking your business context and campaign details, then generating channel-specific tactics aligned with industry trends and your unique selling points. Marketers use it to plan product launches, content campaigns, and lead-generation initiatives across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive promotion roadmap that coordinates messaging, timing, and budget across multiple channels instead of planning each platform in isolation. ● Produces separate strategy sections for social media (platform rationale, engagement tactics, influencer opportunities), email marketing (campaign sequences, personalization, nurture flows), and targeted ads (audience parameters, creative angles, budget splits) ● Includes a measurement framework with channel-specific KPIs, attribution models, and optimization triggers so you can track performance and iterate ● Tailors recommendations to your competitive landscape and audience, emphasizing differentiation through your unique selling points ● Delivers concrete, implementable tactics rather than high-level theory - posting schedules, subject line strategies, ad formats, and community-building approaches ready to execute ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketing strategist specializing in multi-channel promotion.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content promotion strategy to boost sales across social media, email marketing, and targeted advertising channels. Deliver an actionable plan with clear tactics for each channel.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{content-or-campaign-details}}

Align the strategy with current industry trends and differentiate against competitors by emphasizing the unique selling points.

## Output
Structure your strategy with these sections, using bullet points for specific tactics:

### Social Media Strategy
- Platform selection and rationale
- Content themes and posting cadence
- Engagement and community-building tactics
- Influencer or partnership opportunities

### Email Marketing Plan
- Campaign sequence and timing
- Segmentation and personalization approach
- Subject line and CTA strategies
- Nurture and retention flows

### Targeted Advertising Approach
- Platform and ad format recommendations
- Audience targeting parameters
- Budget allocation across channels
- Creative concepts and messaging angles

### Measurement Framework
- Key performance indicators for each channel
- Attribution model
- Optimization triggers

Provide concrete, implementable recommendations tailored to the audience and competitive landscape described above.
```
