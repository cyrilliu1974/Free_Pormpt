# Anti-Discrimination Training Methods for Managers

## 簡介

The Anti-Discrimination Training Methods for Managers is a free AI prompt that generates 10 actionable training approaches to help managers recognize and prevent workplace discrimination and harassment before incidents escalate. This anti-discrimination training prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered list of methods tailored to your organizational context, each with a clear title, description, implementation steps, and measurable outcomes. It addresses both overt discrimination and subtle microaggressions, combining legal knowledge with practical intervention protocols. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Use it when building compliance training programs for managers who lack formal HR backgrounds but serve as first responders to workplace misconduct. ● Produces 10 distinct training methods that accommodate different learning styles and risk scenarios, from bystander intervention to scenario-based simulations. ● Provides concrete tools for identifying early warning signs, understanding legal implications, and implementing cost-effective intervention protocols. ● Includes implementation steps and measurable outcomes for each method, enabling organizations to track behavioral change and training effectiveness. ● Adapts to your organizational size, industry, and existing training infrastructure through the organizational-context variable. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a workplace compliance specialist and organizational development consultant with expertise in designing anti-discrimination training programs for diverse organizations.

## Task

Develop 10 comprehensive training methods that equip managers to proactively identify and address discrimination and harassment risks before they escalate. Managers typically lack formal HR training yet are critical first responders to workplace misconduct, so methods must be legally sound, practical, and immediately actionable.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

Each training method should:
- Address different learning styles and risk scenarios (both overt discrimination and subtle microaggressions)
- Combine theoretical knowledge with practical application
- Provide concrete identification tools and clear intervention protocols
- Be cost-effective and include measurable outcomes
- Help managers recognize early warning signs, understand legal implications, and take appropriate preventive action

## Output

Provide a numbered list (1-10) with each method including:
- **Method Title**: Clear, descriptive name
- **Description**: Overview of the approach and why it works
- **Implementation Steps**: Specific, actionable steps to deploy the method
- **Expected Outcomes**: Measurable results and behavioral changes to anticipate
```
