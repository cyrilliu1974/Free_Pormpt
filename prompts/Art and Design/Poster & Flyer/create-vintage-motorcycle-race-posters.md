# Vintage Motorcycle Race Poster Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Motorcycle Race Poster Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic 1970s-style motorcycle race event posters with period-accurate design elements and bold visual impact. This vintage motorcycle race poster prompt for Midjourney produces high-contrast posters featuring dynamic motorcycle silhouettes in action poses, a bold orange and red color palette with yellow accents, and retro display typography with weathered texture. The prompt renders a screen-print or risograph aesthetic complete with halftone dots, paper grain, and distressed edges, using dramatic diagonal composition with low-angle perspective to emphasize speed and movement. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to deliver print-ready designs with clean margins and strong type hierarchy.
