# Satellite Image Generator for Landmarks and Landscapes

## 簡介

The Satellite Image Generator for Landmarks and Landscapes is a free AI prompt that creates realistic, high-resolution satellite views of any location or subject from specified perspectives and altitudes. This satellite imagery prompt for Midjourney produces photo-realistic aerial shots styled after Maxar Technologies satellite photography, complete with adjustable viewing angles, altitude ranges, and 16:9 aspect ratios suitable for presentations, concept art, and geographic visualization. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models that support photographic realism and technical parameters. The prompt is ideal for designers creating location-based visuals, researchers needing overhead perspectives, content creators illustrating geographic features, or anyone who needs convincing bird's-eye views without accessing actual satellite databases. ● Control viewing perspective (top-down, angled, oblique) and altitude to match your exact composition needs. ● Generate imagery styled after professional Maxar satellite photography with inherent realism and detail. ● Output in widescreen 16:9 format with moderate stylization (--s 250) that balances realism with visual appeal. ● Works for natural landmarks, urban centers, infrastructure, terrain features, or any geographic subject. ## Prompt

```
{{perspective}} view of {{subject}}, seen from {{altitude}}, satellite image by Maxar Technologies, high-resolution --ar 16:9 --s 250
```
