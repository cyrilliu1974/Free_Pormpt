# Candid Portrait Grid Layout Generator

## 簡介

The Candid Portrait Grid Layout Generator is a free AI prompt that produces four distinct street-style photographs arranged in a unified grid for personal branding and content creation. This candid portrait grid prompt for Midjourney creates a 1:1 square layout featuring four coordinated shots: a full-body action shot of the subject mid-stride and laughing, a seated candid looking thoughtfully away, a medium shot waving at the camera, and a close-up with a surprised expression. Each photograph uses natural daylight with soft shadows, blurred environmental backgrounds, and authentic expressions that mimic professional street photography. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering the variety typically captured across an entire photoshoot in a single generation. Creators, entrepreneurs, and marketers use it to produce cohesive visual assets for social profiles, websites, and campaigns without scheduling traditional photo sessions. ● Outputs four distinct compositions in a single grid: full-body, seated candid, medium waving shot, and expressive close-up portrait. ● Maintains consistent natural lighting and realistic street photography aesthetics across all four images with soft shadows and professionally blurred backgrounds. ● Accepts custom subject descriptions and environment settings to match specific branding or visual identity needs. ● Produces square 1:1 aspect ratio grids ready for Instagram, LinkedIn, website headers, and portfolio presentations. ## Prompt

```
A 4-grid layout showing four candid street-style photographs of {{subject-description}}, 1:1 aspect ratio.

Style: Realistic photography with natural daylight, soft lighting creating authentic shadows and highlights. Blurred {{environment}} backgrounds keeping focus on the subject.

Grid composition:
• Top-left: Full-body shot mid-stride, laughing with natural joyful expression
• Top-right: Candid portrait sitting on a bench, looking thoughtfully to the side with one hand near chin
• Bottom-left: Medium shot standing and waving with warm friendly smile, looking directly at camera
• Bottom-right: Close-up portrait with surprised or amused expression, hands casually raised near face

All four images unified by consistent natural lighting and cohesive candid aesthetic.
```
