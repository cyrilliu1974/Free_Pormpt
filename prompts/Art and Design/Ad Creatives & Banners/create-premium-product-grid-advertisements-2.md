# Premium Product Grid Advertisement Prompt

## 簡介

The Premium Product Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates nine-panel commercial photography grids for food and beverage brands seeking editorial-quality visual storytelling. This product grid advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a complete 1:1 square layout with hero product shots, pour moments, macro ingredient textures, lifestyle contexts, and dramatic mood scenes unified by your brand color palette and modern typography. It orchestrates studio lighting, natural light, and low-key drama across nine equal panels to create Instagram grid takeovers, website hero blocks, and social media campaigns that communicate craft quality and brand confidence. Reach for this prompt when you need a full visual narrative that covers product identity, usage rituals, sensory appeal, and lifestyle integration in a single cohesive asset. ● Panel-by-panel direction for hero product, pour action, ingredient macro, ritual moment, brand mark, urban lifestyle, sensory texture, on-the-go energy, and night mood finale. ● Commercial editorial hybrid aesthetic with soft directional light, rich shadows, minimal clutter, and high realism that avoids flat lighting and over-saturation. ● Customizable product name and color palette variables to align every panel with your specific brand identity and packaging design. ● Output designed for Instagram grid takeovers, website content blocks, digital ads, and multi-channel brand storytelling campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Create a bold, premium 3×3 grid advertisement in 1:1 square format for {{product-name}}. Each panel is equal size. Visual style: commercial editorial hybrid photography, high realism, moody yet clean, minimal clutter. Color palette: {{color-palette}}. Modern serif or bold sans-serif typography.

**Panel 1 – Hero Product:** Studio shot of the product in primary container, upright. Soft directional light with subtle highlights on dark neutral or natural surface. Premium, bold, craft mood.

**Panel 2 – Pour/Serve Moment:** Product being poured, served, or dispensed. Focus on liquid flow, texture, serving action. Refreshing, smooth mood.

**Panel 3 – Ingredient Detail:** Macro shot of primary raw ingredient (coffee beans, fruit, herbs, grains). Rich tactile texture conveying depth and quality.

**Panel 4 – Ritual Moment:** Person enjoying the product in quiet personal moment. Natural morning or afternoon light. Calm energy, everyday routine mood.

**Panel 5 – Brand Mark:** Minimal brand logo or product label close-up. Focus on typography and label texture. Identity, confidence.

**Panel 6 – Urban Lifestyle:** Product on café table, park bench, or city surface. Modern urban lifestyle setting, everyday mood.

**Panel 7 – Sensory Texture:** Macro shot of secondary texture or temperature element like ice, foam, condensation, steam. Cool, crisp mood.

**Panel 8 – On-the-Go Energy:** Hand holding the product in motion. Subtle movement blur. Energy, momentum.

**Panel 9 – Night Mood Finish:** Product under low light with dramatic shadows. Moody low-key lighting. Nocturnal, smooth power.

Overall mood: bold, smooth, modern, energizing, crafted, confident. Natural light mixed with controlled studio lighting, balanced exposure with rich shadows ranging from soft to dramatic.

Avoid: flat lighting, weak or diluted product appearance, cheap-looking packaging, over-saturated colors, cluttered scenes, unreadable labels, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
