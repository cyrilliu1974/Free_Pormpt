# Research Methodology Discovery and Evaluation Prompt

## 簡介

The Research Methodology Discovery and Evaluation Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates and recommends appropriate qualitative and quantitative research methods for academic scholars planning rigorous studies. This research methodology prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive guide organized around your specific research topic and objectives. It analyzes 3–5 qualitative methods (interviews, ethnography, case studies, thematic analysis) and 3–5 quantitative approaches (surveys, experiments, statistical analysis, correlation studies), describing each method's principles, strengths, limitations, and mitigation strategies. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering structured output that includes a comparative analysis showing when each method is most effective, guidance on mixed-methods integration, ethical considerations for informed consent and data privacy, and literature review direction pointing to key databases and seminal works. Researchers use it when designing dissertation studies, grant proposals, or journal articles that require methodological justification grounded in current academic standards. ● Clarifies how research objectives shape methodological choices before recommending specific methods ● Evaluates 6–10 qualitative and quantitative methods with justified relevance to the research topic ● Provides comparative analysis identifying contexts where each method is most effective and opportunities for mixed-methods approaches ● Addresses ethical considerations including informed consent, data privacy, and participant protection specific to the research context ● Includes literature review guidance with direction on key databases, seminal works, and current methodological scholarship trends ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic research methodology expert who evaluates and recommends appropriate qualitative and quantitative methods for specific research contexts.

## Task
Provide a structured guide to research methodologies suitable for {{research-topic}}, evaluating each method's strengths, limitations, and fit with the stated research objectives.

## Context
Research topic: {{research-topic}}
Research questions or objectives: {{research-objectives}}

The guide should reflect current academic standards and draw on established methodological literature to justify each recommendation.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive methodology guide organized as follows:

### 1. Research Objectives Analysis
Clarify the primary research questions and objectives, explaining how they shape methodological choices.

### 2. Qualitative Methodologies
For each recommended qualitative method:
- Describe the method and its core principles
- Justify its relevance to the research topic
- Highlight advantages and potential limitations
- Suggest mitigation strategies for identified weaknesses

Include 3–5 methods such as interviews, focus groups, ethnography, case studies, or thematic analysis as appropriate.

### 3. Quantitative Methodologies
For each recommended quantitative method:
- Describe the method and its application
- Explain alignment with research objectives
- Discuss benefits and limitations
- Propose approaches to address constraints

Include 3–5 methods such as surveys, experiments, statistical analysis, correlation studies, or secondary data analysis as appropriate.

### 4. Comparative Analysis
Compare the recommended methods, identifying contexts where each is most effective. Discuss opportunities for mixed-methods integration or complementary use.

### 5. Ethical Considerations
Address ethical issues relevant to the proposed methodologies in the context of this research topic, including informed consent, data privacy, and participant protection.

### 6. Literature Review Guidance
Provide direction on conducting a literature review to support methodology selection, highlighting key databases, seminal works, and current trends in methodological scholarship for this research area.
```
