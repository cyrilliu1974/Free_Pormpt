# Pilot Episode Script Builder for TV Series

## 簡介

The Pilot Episode Script Builder for TV Series is a free AI prompt that creates structured pilot episode frameworks for television screenwriters and content creators. This pilot episode script prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to develop three-dimensional characters, craft a narrative arc that balances character introduction with forward momentum, write authentic dialogue that reveals motivation, and design a cliffhanger ending that drives viewer demand for episode two. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating a logline, series title, main character profiles, plot summary, key scenes with dialogue snippets, and a suspenseful conclusion. Screenwriters use it to transform a single series setting into a complete pilot blueprint that establishes tone, premise, and the narrative hooks needed to launch a successful television series. This prompt is ideal for writers developing original series concepts, showrunners pitching to networks, or creative teams building episodic content who need a tested structure to avoid common pilot mistakes like overcomplicated plots or flat character introductions. ● Develops main characters with defined roles, motivations, and relationships that drive the narrative forward ● Structures a pilot arc that introduces the premise without overwhelming viewers, balancing exposition with story momentum ● Generates key scenes with slug lines and sample dialogue that establish tone and character voice ● Creates a cliffhanger ending designed to ensure audience return and leave clear pathways for ongoing development ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert television screenwriter specializing in pilot episodes that launch hit series. You craft compelling narratives that hook audiences, establish tone and premise, and drive viewer demand for the next episode.

## Task
Develop a complete pilot episode script framework for a new television series based on the provided setting. Create a narrative foundation that introduces core characters, establishes the central conflict, and ends on a cliffhanger that ensures audience return.

## Context
Setting: {{series-setting}}

## Requirements
- Develop three-dimensional characters whose conflicts and relationships drive the narrative
- Structure a pilot arc that introduces characters, establishes premise, and concludes with an intriguing cliffhanger
- Write authentic dialogue that reveals character motivation and personality
- Balance exposition with forward momentum; avoid overwhelming viewers with excessive characters or overcomplicated plotting
- Leave clear pathways for ongoing character and plot development beyond the pilot

## Output
Deliver the pilot framework in this structure:

**Series Title:** [Catchy title reflecting genre, tone, or premise]

**Logline:** [One-sentence pitch summarizing central conflict and characters]

**Setting:** [Time period, location, and social context]

**Main Characters:**
1. [Name]: [Key characteristics and role]
2. [Name]: [Key characteristics and role]
3. [Name]: [Key characteristics and role]

**Plot Summary:** [Concise overview of pilot events, introductions, and conflicts]

**Key Scenes:**
1. [Slug Line]: [Scene description]
   Key Dialogue: "[Impactful dialogue]"
2. [Slug Line]: [Scene description]
   Key Dialogue: "[Impactful dialogue]"
3. [Slug Line]: [Scene description]
   Key Dialogue: "[Impactful dialogue]"

**Cliffhanger:** [Suspenseful ending description]
```
