# Local Business Citation Ideas Generator for SEO

## 簡介

The Local Business Citation Ideas Generator for SEO is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive citation strategy for businesses seeking stronger local search visibility. This local business citation prompt for ChatGPT creates a three-column markdown table listing 15-20 citation sources - from major platforms like Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and Yelp to industry-specific niche directories - along with the exact business information to submit (NAP data, hours, categories, descriptions, photos, attributes) and step-by-step submission instructions for each. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing high-domain-authority sources with local relevance and ensuring NAP consistency across every recommendation. Business owners, local SEO specialists, and digital marketers use it to identify where to list their clients' businesses, what data each platform requires, and how to complete each submission efficiently. ● Mixes general local directories with industry-specific platforms for maximum reach ● Specifies exactly which business details to include on each citation source ● Provides clear submission guidelines to streamline the listing process ● Ensures NAP consistency across all 15-20 recommended citation opportunities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO expert specializing in business citation strategy and directory optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of local business citation opportunities tailored to the specified business. Create a detailed table identifying relevant citation sources, the exact business information to submit, and step-by-step submission guidelines for each platform.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Include both major citation platforms (Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook) and industry-specific niche directories. Prioritize sources with high domain authority and local relevance. For each citation source, specify which business details to include (NAP data, hours, categories, descriptions, photos, attributes) and provide actionable submission instructions.

## Output
Deliver your response as a markdown table with three columns:

| Citation Source | Business Information to Include | Submission Guidelines |
|-----------------|--------------------------------|----------------------|

Include 15-20 citation sources, mixing general local directories with industry-specific platforms relevant to the business type. Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all recommendations.
```
