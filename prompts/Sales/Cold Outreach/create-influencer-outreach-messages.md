# Influencer Outreach Message Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Outreach Message Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized cold outreach messages for connecting with influencers in any niche. This influencer outreach prompt for ChatGPT produces three distinct outreach messages, each tailored to a specific influencer's content and style, along with a reusable follow-up email template. It works by requiring you to specify your niche and outreach purpose, then generating messages that include concrete compliments tied to real aspects of each influencer's work, a clear statement of intent, and a specific, low-friction call to action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Use cases include brand partnership outreach, product collaboration requests, sponsored content pitches, and affiliate program invitations. This prompt is built for marketers, brand managers, agency owners, and startup founders who need to scale personalized influencer communication without sacrificing authenticity. ● Creates three unique outreach messages with influencer-specific compliments and profile summaries. ● Includes a follow-up email template that recaps the original message, adds new information, and states next steps. ● Enforces concrete, low-friction calls to action tailored to your outreach purpose. ● Ensures every message references something specific about the influencer's actual work or content style. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write personalized influencer outreach messages. Your output is genuine, specific to each influencer, and free of generic filler.

## Context

Niche: {{niche}}
Outreach purpose and offering: {{outreach-purpose}}

## Task

Craft outreach messages for three influencers in the niche above. For each influencer, write a personalized message that includes a greeting, a specific compliment tied to their work, the purpose of the outreach, and a clear call to action. Then produce one follow-up email template for use after the initial message goes unanswered.

Rules:
- Tailor every message to that specific influencer's profile and content style.
- Avoid generic language. Reference something concrete about their work.
- Each call to action must be specific and low-friction.
- The follow-up email must recap the prior message, add one new piece of relevant information, and state clear next steps.

## Output

**Influencer 1:**
Name: [Name]
Profile Summary: [Brief summary of the influencer's profile]
Outreach Message: "[Greeting], [Compliment]. [Purpose of reaching out]. [Call to action]."

**Influencer 2:**
Name: [Name]
Profile Summary: [Brief summary of the influencer's profile]
Outreach Message: "[Greeting], [Compliment]. [Purpose of reaching out]. [Call to action]."

**Influencer 3:**
Name: [Name]
Profile Summary: [Brief summary of the influencer's profile]
Outreach Message: "[Greeting], [Compliment]. [Purpose of reaching out]. [Call to action]."

**Follow-up Email Template:**
Subject: [Subject line]
Body: "[Recap of previous communication]. [Additional information]. [Next steps]."
```
