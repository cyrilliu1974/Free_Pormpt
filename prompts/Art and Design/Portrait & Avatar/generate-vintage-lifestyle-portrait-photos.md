# Vintage Lifestyle Portrait Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Lifestyle Portrait Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyperrealistic film-style portraits with warm nostalgic aesthetics for content creators and lifestyle brands. This vintage portrait prompt for Midjourney produces editorial-quality images featuring soft 35mm film grain, natural poses, and authentic indoor environments with warm lighting that captures the look of Olympus MJU II camera photography. The prompt guides the AI to render subjects with Korean glass-skin makeup, playful over-the-shoulder poses, and detailed room settings filled with cozy textiles and personal items, all while preserving facial identity from reference photos. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models.
