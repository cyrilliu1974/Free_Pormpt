# Book Research Package Generator for Authors

## 簡介

The Book Research Package Generator for Authors is a free AI prompt that gathers, evaluates, and organizes credible sources into a structured research document for authors developing non-fiction books. This book research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a five-step process: defining scope, collecting sources across academic journals, expert opinions, primary documents, and trusted publications, extracting key data and insights, organizing findings by theme and source type, and citing everything in your preferred format. It surfaces statistics, anecdotes, and case studies relevant to your topic and delivers them in a document ready to reference as you write. Authors use it when beginning a book project that requires authoritative grounding, fact-checking support, or a foundation of credible material before drafting chapters. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Collects sources across four categories - academic papers, expert opinions, primary documents, and trusted publications - ensuring diversity and credibility. ● Extracts statistics, anecdotes, and case studies from each source and flags them for easy integration into manuscript drafts. ● Organizes findings under clear thematic headings and documents every source with title, author, date, and link in your chosen citation style. ● Produces a single, accessible research document that serves as a reference foundation throughout the book-writing process. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert book researcher skilled in gathering, evaluating, and organizing information from credible sources to support book development.

## Task

Conduct thorough research on {{research-topic}} and deliver a structured research package that provides a rich foundation of accurate, relevant, and up-to-date information. The package will include insights, statistics, anecdotes, and case studies to enhance the book's content and credibility.

## Process

1. **Define scope**: Clarify the research boundaries to maintain focus and relevance

2. **Source collection** across four categories:
   - **Academic and Scholarly Sources**: Recent studies, papers, and publications from academic journals
   - **Expert Opinions**: Interviews, articles, and books from recognized authorities in the field
   - **Primary Sources**: Firsthand accounts, original documents, or legal records directly related to the topic
   - **Trusted Publications**: Articles and reports from reputable news outlets, magazines, and platforms

3. **Extract and summarize**: For each source, capture key points, data, and insights relevant to the book. Flag significant statistics, compelling anecdotes, and illustrative case studies

4. **Organize findings**: Group similar information under clear headings and subheadings by theme or source type for easy reference

5. **Document sources**: Cite all materials with title, author, publication date, and link (when available) using {{citation-style}} format

## Context

{{research-parameters}}

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive research document containing:

- Brief overview establishing the topic's significance
- Summarized findings organized by source type (academic papers, expert opinions, primary sources, trusted publications)
- Highlighted key insights, statistics, anecdotes, and case studies integrated throughout
- Complete citations formatted in {{citation-style}} style

The document should be accessible, well-structured, and ready to support the author in writing an informative, engaging, and authoritative book.
```
