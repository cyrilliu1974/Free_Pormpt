# Research Paper Abstract Generator

## 簡介

The Research Paper Abstract Generator is a free AI prompt that produces multiple polished abstracts for academic research papers, tailored for scholars, students, and researchers who need clear, structured summaries. This research paper abstract prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar principles to create three distinct abstract variants for each input paper. It captures the research question, methodology, key results, and implications in well-formed sentences that emphasize logical relationships between concepts. Each variant offers a different structural approach or emphasis while maintaining the same core content, giving you options for different submission contexts or audiences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to condense complex research into concise, publication-ready abstracts that highlight critical contributions without redundancy. ● Produces three meaningfully different abstract variants per paper, each with distinct emphasis or structure ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure logical sentence hierarchies and clear relationships between concepts ● Captures essential elements - research question, methodology, key results, and implications - in concise, substantive summaries ● Outputs formatted abstracts ready for submission, review, or presentation in academic contexts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research paper abstract generator specializing in dependency grammar frameworks.

## Task
Generate 3 distinct abstracts for each research paper provided. Each abstract must:
- Succinctly capture the paper's core contribution and findings
- Emphasize the most critical points (research question, methodology, key results, implications)
- Use dependency grammar structure to ensure coherent, well-formed sentences that clearly show logical relationships between concepts
- Remain concise while being substantive

## Context
Dependency grammar focuses on the syntactic relationships between words, creating clear hierarchies that mirror the logical structure of research findings. Apply this framework to produce abstracts where each sentence's dependencies reinforce the paper's argumentative flow.

## Input
{{research-papers}}

## Output
For each paper, provide:

**Paper Title:** [exact title]

**Abstract 1:**
[First variant]

**Abstract 2:**
[Second variant]

**Abstract 3:**
[Third variant]

Each abstract should offer a meaningfully different emphasis or structural approach while covering the same core content. Avoid redundancy across the three versions.
```
