# Project Risk Assessment Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Project Risk Assessment Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that systematically identifies and analyzes business risks across four critical categories for project managers, consultants, and business leaders. This project risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your specific business context and risk tolerance to produce a markdown table with at least 10 tailored risk entries covering financial, operational, strategic, and compliance dimensions. Each risk receives a probability rating (Low/Medium/High or percentage), a detailed impact description, and a practical mitigation strategy. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to industry-specific regulatory requirements, market conditions, resource constraints, technological dependencies, and stakeholder dynamics rather than generating generic placeholder risks. Use it when launching a new project, conducting periodic risk reviews, or preparing for stakeholder presentations that require documented risk management frameworks. ● Categorizes risks into financial, operational, strategic, and compliance areas for complete coverage ● Assigns probability and impact ratings to prioritize which risks demand immediate attention ● Generates actionable mitigation strategies tailored to your business context and risk tolerance ● Produces a markdown table format that integrates easily into project documentation and reports ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant conducting a comprehensive project risk assessment.

## Task
Identify and analyze potential risks across financial, operational, strategic, and compliance categories. For each risk, assess its probability of occurrence and potential impact, then develop practical mitigation strategies.

## Context
Business and project details:
{{business-and-project-context}}

Risk tolerance: {{risk-tolerance}}

Systematically evaluate risks specific to this business type, industry, and geographic operating environment. Consider regulatory requirements, market conditions, resource constraints, technological dependencies, stakeholder dynamics, and external threats relevant to the context provided.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with exactly 4 columns:

| Risk Description | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |

Provide at least 10 comprehensive, tailored risk assessments. Each row should be specific to the business and project context, not generic placeholder risks. Probability should be rated (Low/Medium/High or percentage), Impact should describe severity and scope, and Mitigation Strategy should be actionable and realistic.
```
