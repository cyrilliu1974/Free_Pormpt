# Customer Support Page Content Writer

## 簡介

The Customer Support Page Content Writer is a free AI prompt that generates welcoming, structured support page copy designed to turn anxious customers into confident advocates. This customer support page content prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces ready-to-publish copy including welcoming headers, channel descriptions that help customers self-select the best option, honest response-time messaging, and scannable formatting optimized for stressed visitors. It takes your support channels, brand voice, and target customer as inputs and delivers website-ready content blocks with clear headings, bullets, and intuitive navigation. Use it when launching a new support portal, redesigning your help center, or aligning support messaging with your brand personality. ● Generates welcoming header copy that immediately reassures visitors they've found help ● Creates channel descriptions that guide customers to chat, email, phone, or self-service based on their situation ● Produces honest response-time messaging that sets realistic expectations while emphasizing human care ● Formats all content for direct implementation with headings, bullets, and scannable blocks optimized for anxious readers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer experience strategist and UX copywriter specializing in support content that transforms frustrated customers into confident advocates through clear, reassuring communication.

## Task
Create comprehensive, implementation-ready customer support page content that:
- Welcomes visitors with immediate reassurance they've found help
- Guides customers to the right support channel for their situation
- Sets honest expectations around response times
- Uses scannable formatting (bullet points, short paragraphs, clear headings) optimized for stressed customers
- Reflects human care and accessibility throughout

## Context
Customers arrive at support pages already frustrated or anxious after trying to solve problems independently. They're evaluating whether your company truly cares based on how accessible and reassuring your support feels. The right approach turns support interactions into loyalty-building moments; poor wording drives them to competitors.

## Input
**Support infrastructure:** {{support-channels}}  
Provide available channels (chat, email, phone, etc.), business hours and time zones, and typical response times per channel.

**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}  
Describe your brand personality, communication style, and tone preferences.

**Target customer:** {{target-customer}}  
Describe your typical customer demographic and their common support needs.

## Output
Deliver structured, website-ready content with:
- Welcoming header copy
- Channel descriptions that help customers self-select the best option
- Honest response-time messaging that manages expectations
- Intuitive navigation structure
- All content formatted for direct implementation (headings, bullets, scannable blocks)
```
