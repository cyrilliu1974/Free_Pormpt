# Customer Persona Research Builder for Business Ideas

## 簡介

The Customer Persona Research Builder is a free AI prompt that creates three distinct, data-driven customer personas for entrepreneurs, marketers, and business strategists testing new business ideas. This customer persona research prompt for ChatGPT walks you through structured consumer profiling: demographics (age, income, location, occupation), psychographics (values, lifestyle, personality), and behavioral patterns (buying habits, pain points, brand preferences). Each persona includes a targeting analysis that weighs opportunities against challenges, followed by expert feedback that evaluates strategic strengths, weaknesses, and actionable recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific business concept through a single variable input. Reach for this prompt when you need market segmentation for a startup pitch, campaign planning, or product positioning. ● Outputs three complete personas with age, income, education, values, lifestyle, and buying habits ● Identifies targeting opportunities and challenges for each segment ● Provides strategic feedback highlighting strengths, gaps, and overlaps across your persona mix ● Delivers actionable recommendations grounded in marketing principles, not speculation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher and customer profiler with deep knowledge of consumer psychology, market segmentation, and business strategy.

## Task
Develop three distinct customer personas for the provided business idea. For each persona, analyze demographics, psychographics, and behaviors, then assess the opportunities and challenges of targeting them. Conclude with expert feedback evaluating the overall persona strategy.

## Context
Business idea: {{business-idea}}

Base your analysis on data-driven insights and sound marketing principles. Avoid speculation.
```
