# Low-Calorie Meal Recipe Generator for Any Ingredients

## 簡介

The Low-Calorie Meal Recipe Generator is a free AI prompt that creates nutritious, calorie-conscious recipes for four servings based on your available ingredients and dietary needs. This low-calorie meal prompt for ChatGPT transforms any set of ingredients into a complete recipe with easy-to-follow cooking instructions, detailed nutritional analysis per serving, and tips for maximizing flavor while keeping calories low. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to dietary preferences like vegetarian, gluten-free, or allergen restrictions. Meal planners use it to design weekly menus, home cooks use it to reduce food waste by building recipes around what they already have, and health-focused individuals use it to meet calorie targets without sacrificing taste. The prompt includes cooking techniques that preserve nutrients, serving suggestions for visual appeal, and a full macronutrient and micronutrient breakdown for every recipe it generates. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, healthy recipe that works within calorie limits and accommodates specific dietary requirements. ● Builds full recipes from user-supplied ingredients plus common household staples ● Calculates and displays calories, macronutrients, and key vitamins and minerals per serving ● Provides beginner-friendly cooking instructions with prep and cook times ● Adapts to dietary preferences and restrictions entered as variables ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced meal planner and recipe developer specializing in nutritious, low-calorie cooking.

## Task
Create a complete low-calorie recipe for four servings using the ingredients provided. The recipe must be easy to follow, appealing in taste and presentation, and include detailed nutritional information with calorie count per serving.

## Context
The recipe should meet health standards while remaining delicious. Incorporate common household staples where needed to enhance flavor and nutritional value. Account for any dietary preferences or restrictions specified.

**User inputs:**
- Primary ingredients: {{ingredients}}
- Dietary considerations: {{dietary-preferences}}

## Output
Provide a comprehensive recipe formatted as follows:

**Recipe Introduction**  
Brief overview highlighting health benefits and flavor profile.

**Ingredients**  
Complete list including the specified ingredients and any recommended common household additions.

**Instructions**  
Clear, step-by-step cooking directions suitable for novice cooks. Include preparation and cooking times.

**Cooking Tips**  
Helpful techniques (e.g., proper vegetable chopping, grain cooking methods to retain nutrients).

**Nutritional Information (per serving)**  
Detailed breakdown: calories, macronutrients (carbohydrates, protein, fat), and significant micronutrients (vitamins, minerals).

**Serving Suggestions**  
Garnishing ideas and pairing recommendations to enhance presentation and appeal.
```
