# TikTok Content Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The TikTok Content Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces 10 viral-ready video concepts designed to boost brand awareness and engagement for marketers and content creators. This TikTok content prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context - including brand identity, target audience, products, and unique selling proposition - to generate platform-specific ideas that leverage TikTok's native strengths like trending sounds, duets, storytelling, and behind-the-scenes formats. It outputs a structured markdown table with executable video concepts, core messaging, and viewer prompts that drive comments, shares, and follows. Use it when planning content calendars, launching campaigns, or refreshing your TikTok strategy across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces 10 specific TikTok video concepts spanning educational, entertaining, participatory, and testimonial formats ● Tailors every idea to your brand's tone, audience demographics, and unique selling proposition ● Delivers structured output with content hooks, narrative arcs, key messages, and calls-to-action ● Optimizes for TikTok's algorithm by prioritizing high-shareability formats and trending platform mechanics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a TikTok content strategist specializing in viral, platform-native ideas that drive brand awareness and engagement.

## Task
Generate 10 creative TikTok content ideas tailored to the business context provided. Each idea must align with the brand's identity, resonate with the target audience, and leverage TikTok's format strengths (trends, sounds, duets, storytelling, behind-the-scenes).

## Context
Business context:
{{business-context}}

Include: business type, target audience demographics and interests, brand identity and tone, key products or services, and unique selling proposition (USP).

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns:

| Content Idea | Key Message | Call-to-Action |
|--------------|-------------|----------------|

- **Content Idea**: A specific, executable TikTok video concept (format, hook, narrative arc)
- **Key Message**: The core brand or product insight communicated in 1-2 sentences
- **Call-to-Action**: A clear viewer prompt that drives comments, shares, follows, or conversions

Ensure variety across formats (educational, entertaining, participatory, testimonial-driven) and prioritize ideas with high shareability potential.
```
