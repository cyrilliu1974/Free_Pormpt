# Ultra-Realistic Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Ultra-Realistic Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional studio portraits with photorealistic detail for photographers, designers, and content creators. This ultra-realistic portrait prompt for Midjourney produces full-body studio shots with soft professional lighting, clean backdrops, and shallow depth of field. You control the subject description and aspect ratio to generate images with natural, relaxed poses - hands in pockets, eye-level framing - that mirror the quality of professional photography sessions. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, making it practical for profile pictures, character concepts, marketing materials, and editorial content. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade portrait photography without a studio setup or when iterating on character designs that demand realistic human rendering. ● Produces full-body portraits with natural, relaxed posing and professional studio aesthetics ● Ensures soft, flattering lighting and seamless backdrops in every generation ● Supports custom subject descriptions and flexible aspect ratios for different use cases ● Delivers shallow depth of field and eye-level framing that mirrors high-end portrait photography ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic full-body studio portrait of a {{subject-description}} with relaxed posture, both hands in pockets. Clean seamless backdrop, soft professional lighting, eye-level camera angle, shallow depth of field, photorealistic rendering --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
