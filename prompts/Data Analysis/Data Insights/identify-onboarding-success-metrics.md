# Onboarding Success Metrics Identifier for SaaS

## 簡介

The Onboarding Success Metrics Identifier for SaaS is a free AI prompt that identifies seven actionable metrics to measure onboarding effectiveness and drive business outcomes for SaaS companies. This onboarding metrics prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your business context and generates metrics that go beyond surface-level indicators like time-to-first-login. Instead, it focuses on behavioral engagement patterns, value realization signals, and predictive indicators of long-term retention. Each metric includes a clear definition, measurement methodology with formulas, target benchmarks, relevance to current best practices, and specific next steps for optimization. Product teams use it to identify early warning signals for churn, customer success managers apply it to track feature adoption momentum, and growth teams rely on it to correlate onboarding behaviors with customer lifetime value. This prompt is built for SaaS companies that need to move beyond vanity metrics and measure what actually predicts retention, expansion, and product adoption depth. ● Delivers metrics that balance leading indicators (predict future success) and lagging indicators (confirm current effectiveness). ● Provides measurement formulas, target benchmarks, and specific action steps for each metric. ● Focuses on behavioral momentum, feature adoption depth, and signals that correlate with customer lifetime value. ● Prioritizes early warning signals for churn prevention and optimization opportunities over surface-level engagement data. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert customer success strategist and data analytics specialist with deep experience optimizing onboarding for high-growth SaaS companies.

## Task

Identify 7 actionable metrics that accurately measure onboarding success and drive meaningful business outcomes. Focus on metrics that go beyond surface-level indicators like time to first login to measure behavioral engagement, value realization, and predictive indicators of long-term retention.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Prioritize metrics that:
- Correlate directly with customer lifetime value, product adoption depth, and churn prevention
- Provide early warning signals and actionable insights for optimization
- Capture feature adoption patterns and behavioral momentum
- Balance leading indicators (predict future success) and lagging indicators (confirm effectiveness)
- Are measurable and actionable, not vanity metrics

## Output

For each of the 7 metrics, provide:

1. **Metric name and definition**
2. **Measurement methodology** – specific formulas or tracking approach
3. **Why it matters** – relevance to current best practices
4. **Target benchmarks** – what numbers to aim for
5. **Actionable next steps** – how to act on the data in bullet points

Structure each metric with clear headings and use bullet points for maximum clarity and implementation speed.
```
