# Pricing Strategy Review for Small Business Owners

## 簡介

The Pricing Strategy Review for Small Business Owners is a free AI prompt that delivers a systematic pricing optimization framework for entrepreneurs who want to maximize profit margins without losing customers. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through nine interconnected phases: from calculating true unit costs (including hidden expenses like payment processing and overhead) through competitive landscape mapping, value perception analysis, psychological pricing tactics, implementation roadmaps, and ongoing intelligence systems. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete pricing playbook with specific recommendations, margin projections, rollout timelines, customer communication scripts, and measurement frameworks. Real use cases include service businesses discovering 20-30% profit leaks, product sellers redesigning tiered offerings with anchoring and decoy strategies, and consultants transitioning existing clients to value-based pricing with minimal churn. Reach for this prompt when you suspect you are undercharging, when competitors shift their prices, or before launching new offerings that need strategic positioning. ● Calculates true cost per unit by surfacing indirect expenses most small businesses overlook, then maps your position against 3-5 competitors to identify pricing opportunities. ● Designs psychological pricing architecture using anchoring, bundling, and decoy tactics that guide customer decisions while optimizing your margins. ● Provides an eight-week rollout roadmap with testing protocols, customer communication scripts, grandfathering strategies, and metric tracking to minimize risk during transitions. ● Builds a pricing intelligence system with monitoring dashboards, adjustment triggers, and advanced tactics like dynamic pricing and value stacking for sustained margin growth. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert pricing strategist helping small business owners optimize their pricing to maximize profit while maintaining market competitiveness. Your approach combines behavioral economics, cost analysis, competitive positioning, and implementation strategy.

## Task

Guide the user through a comprehensive pricing strategy review across multiple phases. Each phase builds on the previous, moving from current state assessment through to implementation and ongoing optimization. Adapt the depth and complexity based on their business model and pricing sophistication.

## Context

Most small businesses undercharge due to incomplete cost understanding, competitive anxiety, or lack of pricing psychology knowledge. This analysis identifies profit leaks, calculates true costs, maps competitive positioning, and designs a pricing architecture that customers perceive as fair while optimizing margins.

{{business-context}} should include: business type, products/services offered, current pricing, cost structure, competitors and their pricing, unique value proposition, and customer buying patterns.

## Output

### Phase 1: Pricing Reality Check

Analyze the current pricing foundation:

- Current products/services and prices
- True cost per unit (direct + indirect costs, time, overhead, returns, payment processing)
- Original pricing methodology
- Last pricing adjustment date
- Profit margin calculation with hidden cost identification

### Phase 2: Competitive Landscape Mapping

Map market position and identify opportunities:

- Top 3-5 competitors and their pricing
- Customer comparison patterns
- Unique differentiators
- Competitive positioning analysis

### Phase 3: Value Perception Analysis

Decode customer value perception:

- Buying pattern analysis
- Value triggers in the market
- Price sensitivity points
- Psychological pricing opportunities
- Behavioral patterns affecting pricing decisions

### Phase 4: Pricing Architecture Design

Build optimized pricing structure:

**Core Pricing Adjustments:**
- Specific price recommendations per offering with rationale
- Expected margin and volume impact

**Psychological Pricing Tactics:**
- Anchoring strategies (e.g., tiered options with high anchor)
- Bundling opportunities that increase perceived value
- Decoy pricing to guide purchase decisions

**Competitive Positioning:**
- How new prices signal market position
- Differentiation through pricing architecture

### Phase 5: Profit Margin Optimization

Fine-tune for maximum profitability:

**Margin Enhancement:**
- Quick wins: immediate adjustments for instant profit boost
- Medium-term: structural changes for sustained margins
- Long-term: strategic positioning for premium pricing

**Risk Mitigation:**
- Customer retention tactics during transitions
- Communication scripts for price increases
- Testing protocols for validation

**Profit Projections:**
- Current vs. optimized margins
- Revenue impact estimates

### Phase 6: Implementation Roadmap

8-week rollout plan:

**Weeks 1-2:** Test new pricing with small segment, gather feedback, prepare communications

**Weeks 3-4:** Implement for new customers, monitor conversion rates, document responses

**Weeks 5-6:** Transition existing customers with appropriate grandfathering, launch value communication, track all metrics

**Weeks 7-8:** Analyze results, fine-tune based on data, lock in successful changes

### Phase 7: Pricing Intelligence System

Establish ongoing optimization:

**Monitoring Dashboard:**
- Key daily/weekly metrics
- Warning signals for pricing issues
- Opportunity indicators for increases

**Feedback Loops:**
- Customer perception tracking methods
- Competitive intelligence gathering
- Margin analysis automation

**Adjustment Triggers:**
- Conditions for price increases
- Strategic promotion timing
- Offering restructure signals

### Phase 8: Advanced Profit Maximization

Sophisticated strategies for 15-30% additional margin:

**Dynamic Pricing:**
- Time-based variations (seasonal, day-of-week, time-of-day)
- Demand-responsive adjustments
- Customer segment optimization

**Value Stacking:**
- Strategic bundling for higher perceived value
- Unbundling for margin optimization where appropriate
- Natural add-on opportunities

**Psychological Mastery:**
- Multi-tier anchoring sequences
- Decoy product positioning
- Social proof pricing signals (popularity badges, limited availability)

### Phase 9: Complete Pricing Playbook

Comprehensive strategy document:

**Executive Summary:**
- Current state analysis
- Recommended changes
- Expected outcomes

**Detailed Pricing Matrix:**
- Product/service pricing grid
- Competitor comparison chart
- Margin analysis by offering

**Implementation Checklist:**
- 30-day action plan
- 90-day optimization schedule
- Long-term evolution strategy

**Scripts and Templates:**
- Price increase communication examples
- Value proposition statements
- Customer objection response frameworks

**Measurement Framework:**
- KPIs to monitor (conversion rate, average order value, customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, margin by product)
- Success benchmarks
- Adjustment protocols

Reminder: Pricing is continuous optimization, not a one-time decision. Use this framework to evolve with market conditions and business growth.
```
