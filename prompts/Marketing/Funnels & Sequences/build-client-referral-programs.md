# Client Referral Program Builder

## 簡介

The Client Referral Program Builder is a free AI prompt that designs systematic referral marketing systems for service businesses looking to turn satisfied customers into active referral sources. This client referral program prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete 60-day communication plan that addresses the three reasons businesses lose 80% of potential referrals: asking too early, focusing on their own needs instead of client benefits, and failing to maintain post-sale communication. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing client segmentation criteria, day-by-day touchpoint calendars, multi-channel message templates for postcards, letters, emails, and texts, reward program structures, and implementation checklists. Real-world applications include service-based businesses building automated referral sequences, agencies designing retention-focused communication flows, and consultants who need to keep their brand visible without overwhelming recent buyers. Reach for this prompt when you want a structured system that moves beyond one-time asks and creates ongoing referral generation through strategic timing and client-benefit framing. ● Produces client segmentation criteria based on transaction value, satisfaction signals, and relationship strength to prioritize referral outreach. ● Maps 60-day communication calendars with specific channel and message type for each touchpoint, from immediate post-purchase through long-term nurturing. ● Generates multi-channel copy for postcards, letters, emails, and text messages that frame referrals as exclusive client benefits rather than favors. ● Delivers referral program structures with automated reminder systems, participation guidelines, and reward delivery mechanisms that maintain visibility without annoying clients. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert referral marketing architect specializing in systematic client retention and referral generation for service businesses.

## Task

Design a complete referral marketing system that overcomes the three reasons businesses lose 80% of potential referrals: asking too early, focusing on their own needs instead of client benefits, and failing to maintain post-sale communication. The system must address client hesitation, referral amnesia, and natural forgetfulness even among satisfied clients.

## Context

Business context: {{business-context}}

Current communication methods: {{current-communication}}

Referral incentives: {{referral-incentives}}

## Requirements

**Client Segmentation Strategy**
- Identify most profitable and referral-likely clients from recent transaction history
- Establish criteria for segmentation based on transaction value, satisfaction signals, and relationship strength

**60-Day Communication Timeline**
- Map strategic touchpoints from immediate post-purchase through long-term nurturing
- Specify day-by-day timing for each client communication across multiple channels
- Balance education about referral benefits with decision reinforcement

**Multi-Channel Message Templates**
- Create specific copy for postcards, letters, emails, and text messages
- Frame referrals as an exclusive client benefit, not a favor
- Design a "Welcome to Our Family" package that introduces the referral program naturally

**Referral Program Structure**
- Center messaging on what clients receive for participating
- Build automated reminder systems that maintain top-of-mind presence without annoying clients
- Establish clear participation guidelines and reward delivery

**Implementation Checklist**
- Provide actionable setup steps in logical sequence
- Include automation setup, template deployment, and tracking mechanisms

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive implementation plan with clear section headings matching the requirements above. Use bullet points for all actionable steps. Present the 60-day timeline in day-by-day or week-by-week format with specific channel and message type for each touchpoint.
```
