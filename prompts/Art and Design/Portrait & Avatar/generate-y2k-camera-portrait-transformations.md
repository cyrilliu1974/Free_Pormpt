# Y2K Camera Portrait Transformation Prompt

## 簡介

The Y2K Camera Portrait Transformation Prompt is a free AI prompt that recreates the iconic early-2000s digital camera aesthetic for fashion and lifestyle photography. This Y2K portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces cinematic low-angle shots styled after Canon IXUS and Sony Cyber-shot cameras, complete with direct flash lighting, shallow depth of field, film grain texture, and the signature pinkish-purple color grading of vintage digital photography. It transforms subjects into glamorous vehicle-interior scenes with detailed makeup, fashion styling, luxury accessories, and warm nostalgic lighting that captures the authentic feel of early digital camera shots. Perfect for fashion brands, social media creators, and marketers seeking retro-inspired visual content that taps into millennial and Gen Z nostalgia. ● Produces 8K resolution portraits with authentic Y2K camera traits: direct flash shadows, film grain, shallow depth of field, and pinkish-purple hue. ● Preserves original facial features while applying detailed styling specifications including makeup, hair, outfit, accessories, and vehicle interior settings. ● Creates cinematic low-angle compositions with bokeh effects, warm interior lighting, cool dashboard glow, and night-time city atmospheres. ● Includes precise fashion and luxury brand details, customizable through subject-description, vehicle-interior, and outfit-top variables for consistent aesthetic control. ## Prompt
