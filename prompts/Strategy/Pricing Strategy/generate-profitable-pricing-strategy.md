# Customer Lifetime Value Growth Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Customer Lifetime Value Growth Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that produces tactical retention and upsell strategies for entrepreneurs and business owners looking to maximize revenue from their existing customer base. This customer lifetime value prompt for ChatGPT takes your product description and customer context, then delivers 20 actionable ideas: 10 ways to enhance your core offering without major pivots, and 10 complementary products designed for cross-sell or upsell. Each idea is scored on impact potential (0–10) and implementation effort (0–10) in a prioritization table, so you can quickly identify high-leverage opportunities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs structured markdown with an introduction, two categorized idea lists, and a sortable analysis table. Use it when you want to grow revenue per customer, improve retention, or find new monetization angles that stay close to your core domain. ● Produces 10 core-product enhancements and 10 complementary upsell ideas in one run ● Scores every idea on impact and effort so you can prioritize execution ● Emphasizes strategies that foster repeat purchases, subscriptions, community, and emotional connection ● Stays within your product domain to minimize customer context switching and maximize perceived value ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative business strategist specializing in customer retention and lifetime value growth.

## Task
Generate unconventional strategies to increase customer lifetime value for the entrepreneur's business, staying within the same domain as their primary product. Deliver:

1. **10 ways to enhance existing product value** – tactical ideas that improve the core offering or customer experience without major pivots
2. **10 complementary product ideas for up-sell or cross-sell** – closely related offerings that minimize context switching and maximize perceived value
3. **Impact and Effort Analysis** – a prioritization table scoring each of the 20 ideas on potential impact (0-10) and implementation effort (0-10)

## Context
**Product:** {{product-description}}

**Customer base:** {{customer-context}}

## Criteria
- Enhance perceived value without proportional cost increases
- Encourage repeat purchases, subscriptions, or ongoing engagement
- Foster community, exclusivity, or emotional connection
- Stay close to the core product domain
- Favor ideas with favorable impact-to-effort ratios

## Output
Structure your response in markdown:

### Introduction
Brief synthesis of the business context and objective.

### 10 Ways to Increase Existing Product Value
- Idea 1: [concise description]
- Idea 2: [concise description]
- …

### 10 Complementary Products to Up-Sell or Cross-Sell
- Idea 1: [concise description]
- Idea 2: [concise description]
- …

### Impact and Effort Analysis
| Idea | Description | Impact Score (0-10) | Effort Score (0-10) |
|------|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| … | … | … | … |

*Higher impact = greater LTV potential. Lower effort = easier to implement.*
```
