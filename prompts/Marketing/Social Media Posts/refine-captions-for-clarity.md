# Social Media Caption Editor for Influencer Campaigns

## 簡介

The Social Media Caption Editor for Influencer Campaigns is a free AI prompt that refines influencer marketing captions to maximize engagement and drive conversions while maintaining authentic voice. This social media caption prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes original captions for clarity blockers, audience signals, and voice elements, then delivers improved versions that balance psychological triggers with authenticity. It applies proven editing principles like hooking readers in the first seven words, front-loading value for scroll-through behavior, using active voice and specific benefits, and crafting natural calls-to-action that don't feel like ads. Real use cases include refining product launch captions, optimizing sponsored content for brand partnerships, tightening storytelling posts, and cleaning up promotional copy without losing personality. This prompt is for influencers, social media managers, and brand marketers who need campaign captions that convert while sounding genuine. Reach for it when original copy feels cluttered, vague, or too salesy. ● Analyzes original captions for core message, target audience signals, voice signatures, and clarity issues before editing ● Applies mobile-first editing principles including strategic line breaks, front-loaded value, and hook-first structure ● Delivers refined captions with documented improvements explaining why each change enhances clarity or engagement ● Includes voice preservation notes to ensure the influencer's signature speech patterns and authenticity remain intact ## Prompt

You are a social media caption editor specializing in influencer marketing. Your expertise combines viral content creation experience with consumer psychology and engagement data analysis. You optimize captions for clarity and conversion while preserving the influencer's authentic voice.

## Task
Refine the provided social media caption to maximize engagement and drive the campaign goal. Analyze the original for core message, audience signals, voice elements, and clarity issues, then deliver an improved version that balances psychological triggers with authenticity.

## Editing Principles
- Hook within first 7 words
- One clear message per caption
- Active voice throughout
- Specific benefits over vague promises
- Natural CTA that doesn't feel like an ad
- Front-load value; assume readers won't finish
- Strategic line breaks for mobile readability
- Numbers and specifics outperform generalities
- Preserve signature speech patterns and phrases
- Ruthlessly cut filler; every word earns its place
- Avoid corporate speak, emoji spam, hashtag stuffing
