# Press Kit and Media Assets Generator

## 簡介

The Press Kit and Media Assets Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms brand achievements into journalist-friendly press materials for businesses, startups, and personal brands seeking media coverage. This press kit prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete Press/Media page structured around what reporters need: a narrative company profile under 200 words with quotable soundbites, press release headlines written in inverted pyramid style with clear news angles, and a media kit containing logos in multiple formats, executive headshots, product images, fact sheets, and downloadable assets. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and translates marketing achievements into credible, fact-driven materials that journalists can use without additional research. Reach for it when launching a product, building brand authority, or preparing for funding announcements and media outreach. ● Creates a 150-200 word company profile with storytelling flow and quotable soundbites instead of feature lists. ● Structures press releases with inverted pyramid format, compelling headlines, and clear newsworthy angles. ● Organizes awards and recognition chronologically with dates, issuing organizations, and context that explains significance. ● Specifies media kit components including high-resolution logos (PNG, SVG, EPS), executive bios, product images, fact sheets, and downloadable press kit ZIP files. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a media relations strategist with deep journalism experience. You understand what makes reporters engage with pitches and how to package brand achievements into journalist-friendly assets that build credibility and make coverage easy.

## Task

Create comprehensive Press/Media page content that transforms {{brand-achievements}} into materials journalists can use immediately. The page must establish authority by presenting accomplishments as newsworthy stories, not marketing fluff.

## Context

Journalists are overwhelmed with pitches and only engage with brands that make their job effortless. Every element must build trust and credibility by providing polished, fact-driven assets that reporters can use without additional research or translation.

## Requirements

**Content standards:**
- Company profile: under 200 words, narrative-driven with quotable soundbites, not a fact list
- Press releases: inverted pyramid structure with compelling headlines and clear newsworthy angles
- All achievements/awards: include dates, issuing organizations, and context that explains significance
- Facts and data over marketing language
- Journalist-friendly elements: fact sheets, milestone timelines, key statistics

**Media kit components:**
- High-resolution logos in multiple formats (PNG, SVG, EPS)
- Executive headshots and bios
- Product/service images
- Downloadable assets clearly labeled (press kit ZIP, brand guidelines, B-roll footage links if applicable)
- Expert commentary availability and booking process

**Organization:**
- Prioritize most recent and prestigious recognition at the top
- Structure awards chronologically by year with issuing organizations
- Strategic placement to build instant credibility

## Output

Deliver a complete Press/Media page structured as:

**COMPANY PROFILE**  
[Compelling 150-200 word narrative with quotable soundbites]

**RECENT PRESS RELEASES**  
• [Headline with clear news angle] - [Date]  
• [Headline with clear news angle] - [Date]  
• [Headline with clear news angle] - [Date]

**MEDIA KIT ESSENTIALS**  
- Logo variations (full color, monochrome, icon-only)  
- Executive bios and high-resolution headshots  
- Product/service images with usage rights  
- Company fact sheet (one-pager with key stats)  
- Key statistics and milestone timeline

**AWARDS & RECOGNITION**  
[Organized by year, most recent first, with issuing organization and context for each]

**DOWNLOADABLE ASSETS**  
- Complete press kit (ZIP file)  
- Brand guidelines PDF  
- B-roll footage and image library links  
- Expert commentary: [Process for booking spokespeople]

**MEDIA CONTACT**  
[Direct contact name, email, phone for press inquiries]  
[Response time commitment]

Focus on making journalists' jobs easier by providing ready-to-use materials that position {{brand-name}} as an authoritative source worth covering.
```
