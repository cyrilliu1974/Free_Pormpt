# Internal Mobility Strategy Prompt for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Internal Mobility Strategy Prompt for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that designs career lattice frameworks to replace linear advancement models and improve employee retention for organizations of any size. This internal mobility strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete implementation plan that analyzes structural and cultural barriers to movement, maps employee capabilities and career goals, and creates visibility platforms like job shadowing programs, cross-functional project matching, and internal gig boards. HR leaders use it to shift from promotion-only pathways to lateral and diagonal moves that unlock hidden talent, reduce bottlenecks, and keep high performers engaged. The output includes manager training curricula on talent sharing, phased rollout timelines with ownership assignments, and KPIs for mobility rates and retention lift. Reach for this prompt when your organization faces retention challenges, promotion bottlenecks, or untapped skill pools that traditional career ladders cannot address. ● Conducts barrier analysis to surface structural, cultural, and systemic obstacles preventing internal movement. ● Designs skill inventory and aspiration mapping systems that match employees to stretch assignments and project opportunities. ● Builds manager enablement programs with training on succession planning and lattice-based development conversations. ● Delivers measurement frameworks with retention lift KPIs, mobility rate tracking, and employee satisfaction benchmarks. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational development strategist specializing in internal mobility systems. You design career lattice frameworks—multi-directional career pathways (lateral, diagonal, vertical)—that replace linear ladder models, unlock employee potential, reduce bottlenecks, and improve retention.

## Task
Design a comprehensive internal mobility strategy for the organization described below. Include:

- **Barrier analysis**: Identify current obstacles to internal movement (structural, cultural, systemic)
- **Skill & aspiration mapping**: Systems to inventory employee capabilities and career goals
- **Opportunity visibility platforms**: Job shadowing, stretch assignments, cross-functional project matching, internal gig boards
- **Manager enablement**: Training on talent sharing, succession planning, and lattice-based development conversations
- **Measurement framework**: KPIs for mobility rates, retention lift, skill progression, and employee satisfaction
- **Implementation roadmap**: Phased rollout with timelines, ownership, and change management tactics suited to the organization's culture and readiness

## Context
{{organization-profile}}

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear section headings (##). Use bullet points for action steps. For each major initiative, specify:

- Timeline (quarters or months)
- Accountable role or function
- Success metrics
- Dependencies and risks

Prioritize practicality: the strategy must integrate with existing HR systems and align with the organization's change capacity.
```
