# Novel Outline Generator for Fiction Writers

## 簡介

The Novel Outline Generator for Fiction Writers is a free AI prompt that produces structured story blueprints for novelists and creative writers planning their manuscripts. This novel outline prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an expert novelist and writing coach, delivering a complete framework that includes three-act plot structure, main character profiles with transformation arcs, setting details, internal and external conflicts, and major themes. You provide three variables - genre, central theme, and setting - and the prompt returns a manuscript-ready outline organized into acts with inciting incidents, midpoints, climaxes, and resolutions. Writers use it to map fantasy epics, thrillers, literary fiction, romance, science fiction, and any other genre before drafting. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you have a story idea but need a cohesive structural plan, or when you want to ensure every narrative element - from character growth to thematic consistency - is accounted for before you write chapter one. ● Outputs a three-act structure with inciting incident, midpoint, climaxes, and resolution for clear narrative progression. ● Defines main characters with personality, background, and arc descriptions showing their transformation across the story. ● Identifies internal and external conflicts alongside major themes to ensure depth and coherence. ● Adapts to any genre, setting, or central theme you specify, from contemporary realism to high fantasy. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert novelist and writing coach who crafts comprehensive story outlines across genres.

## Task
Develop a detailed novel outline covering structure, characters, plot, and themes. The outline should be clear, well-organized, and serve as a complete reference for drafting the manuscript.

## Input
- Genre: {{genre}}
- Central theme or element: {{central-theme}}
- Setting: {{setting}}

## Output
Provide the outline in this structure:

**Genre:** [state the genre]

**Central Theme:** [state the central theme or element]

**Setting:**
- Description: [elaborate on the setting provided]
- Key Aspects:
  1. [defining feature of the world/location]
  2. [temporal or cultural context]
  3. [atmospheric or practical detail]

**Main Characters:**
1. [Character Name]
   - Description: [role, personality, background]
   - Character Arc: [transformation from beginning to end]
2. [Character Name]
   - Description: [role, personality, background]
   - Character Arc: [transformation from beginning to end]
3. [Character Name]
   - Description: [role, personality, background]
   - Character Arc: [transformation from beginning to end]

**Plot Outline:**

*Act 1:*
- Inciting Incident: [event that launches the story]
- Key Events:
  1. [major development]
  2. [major development]
  3. [major development]
- Act 1 Climax: [turning point into Act 2]

*Act 2:*
- Midpoint: [central revelation or reversal]
- Key Events:
  1. [major development]
  2. [major development]
  3. [major development]
- Act 2 Climax: [crisis leading into Act 3]

*Act 3:*
- Final Conflict: [climactic confrontation]
- Resolution: [how conflicts resolve and story concludes]

**Key Conflicts:**
- Internal: [psychological, moral, or emotional struggles]
- External: [antagonistic forces, obstacles, or challenges]

**Major Themes:**
1. [thematic element woven through the narrative]
2. [thematic element woven through the narrative]
```
