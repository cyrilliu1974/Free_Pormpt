# Research Paper Summary Generator for General Audiences

## 簡介

The Research Paper Summary Generator for General Audiences is a free AI prompt that transforms dense academic research into clear, accessible summaries for readers without specialized knowledge. This research paper summary prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze scholarly papers and produce structured 500-word summaries that maintain scientific integrity while removing technical barriers. It works by systematically extracting the research question, methodology, key findings, and implications, then recasting them in plain language. Researchers, science communicators, and educators use it to make cutting-edge studies understandable to broader audiences. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting three variables: the paper title, field context, and specific focus areas you want emphasized. Reach for this prompt when you need to communicate academic work to stakeholders, students, or the public without sacrificing accuracy or nuance. ● Extracts and explains research objectives, methodology, findings, and implications in accessible language ● Maintains scientific accuracy and nuance while removing technical jargon and discipline-specific terminology ● Produces structured summaries with clear beginning, middle, and end sections for easy comprehension ● Allows you to specify field context and focus areas to tailor the summary to your audience's interests ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic research expert skilled at synthesizing complex studies into accessible summaries for general audiences.

## Task
Analyze the research paper "{{paper-title}}" and create a comprehensive summary that preserves the integrity and depth of the original work while making it understandable to non-specialists.

## Context
Field of interest or relevance: {{field-context}}

Specific aspects to emphasize: {{focus-areas}}

## Process
1. **Identify Key Elements**: State the paper's research question or objective—what the study aims to explore, discover, or achieve.
2. **Summarize Methodology**: Explain in simple terms how the research was conducted (study design, data collection methods, analytical approaches).
3. **Outline Main Findings**: Highlight significant results clearly and precisely, avoiding technical jargon.
4. **Discuss Implications**: Explain why these findings matter—their broader impact on the field, potential applications, and future research directions.
5. **Provide Context**: Include brief background information to help readers understand the field or problem being addressed.
6. **Incorporate Key Terms**: Use and explain important concepts from the paper in ways non-specialists can understand.

## Output
Deliver a structured summary with:
- **Beginning**: Introduce the research and its objectives
- **Middle**: Detail methodology and findings
- **End**: Discuss implications and significance

The summary should be engaging, jargon-free, and accessible while maintaining the depth and nuances of the original research. Keep the summary concise and thorough—approximately 500 words unless the context specifies otherwise.
```
