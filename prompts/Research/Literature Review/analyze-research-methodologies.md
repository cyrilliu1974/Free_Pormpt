# Research Methodology Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Research Methodology Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive evaluations of research methodologies used to study complex phenomena for academics, graduate students, and researchers. This research methodology prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to deliver a structured academic analysis that compares quantitative, qualitative, and mixed methods approaches, complete with landmark study examples, strength-weakness comparisons, and properly formatted APA citations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to generate publication-ready methodology reviews. The prompt is designed for literature reviews, dissertation chapters, grant proposals, and research design courses where understanding the trade-offs between different methodological paradigms is essential. ● Produces a full methodology analysis with introduction, three detailed approach sections, comparative table, conclusion, and reference list ● Includes landmark study examples for each approach with research questions, methodology details, and key findings ● Generates a comparison table organizing strengths, weaknesses, and suitable research scenarios by methodology type ● Outputs proper APA citation formatting for all referenced sources and studies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher specializing in analyzing research methodologies across various fields of study.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive analysis of the methodological approaches used in studying the specified complex phenomenon. Compare and contrast quantitative, qualitative, and mixed methods approaches, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses. Include examples of landmark studies utilizing each approach. Ensure all sources are properly cited using APA citation style.

## Context
The analysis should examine: {{complex-phenomenon}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Introduction**
- Brief overview of the complex phenomenon being studied
- Importance of understanding the methodological approaches used in research

**Quantitative Approach**
- Definition and key characteristics
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Example landmark study:
  • Study title
  • Author(s) and year of publication
  • Research question/hypothesis
  • Methodology
  • Key findings
  • Citation

**Qualitative Approach**
- Definition and key characteristics
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Example landmark study:
  • Study title
  • Author(s) and year of publication
  • Research question/hypothesis
  • Methodology
  • Key findings
  • Citation

**Mixed Methods Approach**
- Definition and key characteristics
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Example landmark study:
  • Study title
  • Author(s) and year of publication
  • Research question/hypothesis
  • Methodology
  • Key findings
  • Citation

**Comparative Analysis**
Create a table with these columns:
- Approach (Quantitative, Qualitative, Mixed Methods)
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Suitable Research Scenarios

**Conclusion**
- Summarize the key points
- Discuss the importance of selecting the appropriate methodological approach based on the research question and context
- Encourage further exploration of the topic

**References**
List all sources using APA citation style.
```
