# Legal Contract Template Generator for Business

## 簡介

The Legal Contract Template Generator for Business is a free AI prompt that creates versatile, jurisdiction-specific contract frameworks for companies and legal professionals. This legal contract template prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured agreements with clearly labeled sections, plain-language clauses, and built-in customization points. You provide the industry, jurisdiction, and contract requirements, and the prompt drafts a logically organized template that balances legal protection with practical usability. Real use cases include vendor agreements, service contracts, employment terms, NDAs, and licensing deals where businesses need a solid starting point that reflects appropriate risk allocation and compliance with local laws. Reach for this prompt when you need a contract draft quickly, want to standardize agreement structures across your organization, or require a template that non-lawyers can understand and adapt without sacrificing legal rigor. ● Identifies all essential contract components for the specified industry and use case, ensuring no critical clauses are omitted. ● Drafts clear, unambiguous language using plain terms where possible, making contracts accessible to business users while maintaining legal precision. ● Structures agreements with logical hierarchy, proper cross-references, and flagged areas that require additional legal review based on specific circumstances. ● Includes inline notes highlighting customization options, variables, and flexibility points where the template should be adapted to match your exact business context. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert contract lawyer drafting a comprehensive, legally sound contract template.

## Task
Create a versatile contract framework that balances legal protection with practical usability. The template must be easily customizable for the client's specific business context.

## Context
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Jurisdiction: {{jurisdiction}}
- Business requirements: {{contract-requirements}}

The contract should reflect appropriate risk allocation and comply with relevant laws in the specified jurisdiction.

## Approach
1. Identify and include all essential contract components for this industry and use case
2. Draft clear, unambiguous clauses using plain language where possible
3. Structure the agreement logically with proper hierarchy and cross-references
4. Build in flexibility points where customization is typically needed
5. Flag areas requiring legal review based on specific circumstances

## Output
Deliver the contract template in a structured format with:
- Clearly labeled sections and subsections
- Bullet points highlighting key elements within each clause
- Brief inline notes indicating customization options or variables
- Standard boilerplate clauses appropriate to the jurisdiction

Ensure each clause is detailed enough to provide meaningful legal protection while remaining accessible to non-lawyers.
```
