# Keyword Research & SEO Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Keyword Research & SEO Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive keyword research and delivers a prioritized list with search volume and difficulty metrics for digital marketers and SEO professionals. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT takes your campaign details - goals, target audience, industry, geography, and competitors - and produces a structured markdown table ranking keywords by opportunity. It expands seed keywords into semantic clusters, question-based queries, and long-tail variations, then evaluates each for search intent, competition, and ranking feasibility. The output highlights quick-win opportunities (low difficulty, high relevance) and long-term strategic targets (high volume, competitive value), complete with a summary of search behavior patterns. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable across text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when planning content calendars, launching new campaigns, or auditing existing SEO strategies to ensure every keyword decision is data-driven and aligned with business objectives. ● Expands seed keywords into semantic clusters, question-based queries, and long-tail variations ● Evaluates search volume, keyword difficulty, and alignment with campaign goals in a single pass ● Flags quick-win opportunities and long-term strategic targets in a sortable markdown table ● Summarizes search behavior patterns and keyword strategy in plain language ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in search engine optimization and keyword research.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research for a marketing campaign. Analyze search intent, competition, and opportunity to deliver a prioritized keyword list with actionable metrics.

## Context
Campaign & Business:
{{campaign-details}}

Include your campaign goals, target audience, industry, geographic focus, and main competitors in a single brief.

## Process
1. Extract seed keywords from the campaign brief
2. Expand the list using semantic clustering, question-based queries, and long-tail variations
3. Evaluate each keyword for search volume, difficulty, and alignment with campaign goals
4. Prioritize by balancing traffic potential against ranking feasibility
5. Flag quick-win opportunities (low difficulty, relevant intent) and long-term targets (high volume, strategic value)

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Keyword Difficulty |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|
| … | … | … |

Sort by priority (highest opportunity first). After the table, provide 2–3 sentences summarizing the keyword strategy and any notable patterns in search behavior for this campaign.
```
