# Fashion Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates studio-quality fashion editorial images with hyper-realistic detail and professional composition. This fashion photography prompt for Midjourney produces shots of models in designer outfits against neutral backgrounds, with customizable shot types, color palettes, and product focus - ideal for e-commerce brands, fashion designers, lookbooks, and social media campaigns. The prompt specifies studio lighting, eye-level camera angles, shallow depth of field, and 8K resolution output, working across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-grade fashion visuals without a photo shoot: catalog images, brand campaigns, or portfolio pieces that require consistent lighting and professional staging. ● Specify shot type (full-body, waist-up, close-up) and subject details to match your creative direction. ● Define color palettes for the outfit's brocade fabric and highlight a specific product or accessory. ● Outputs 8K resolution images with shallow depth of field that isolates the subject against clean, neutral backgrounds. ● Uses fashion editorial conventions - studio lighting, eye-level perspective, 4:5 aspect ratio - for professional, print-ready results. ## Prompt

```
{{shot-type}} photograph of {{subject-description}} wearing an outfit in {{color-palette}} patterned brocade fabric, carrying a {{product-focus}} against a neutral seamless background; fashion editorial, hyper-realistic style; studio lighting, eye-level camera angle, shallow depth of field, 8K resolution --ar 4:5
```
