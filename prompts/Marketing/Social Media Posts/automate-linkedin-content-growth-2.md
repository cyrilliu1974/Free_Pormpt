# LinkedIn Content Automation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Content Automation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end automation plans for businesses looking to grow their presence and engagement on LinkedIn. This LinkedIn content automation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your business context and target metrics, then produces a structured strategy document covering five key areas: a content calendar with optimal posting times and themes, post crafting guidelines for high-performing formats, daily engagement workflows, performance tracking frameworks, and tool recommendations for scheduling and analytics. Marketing teams use it to replace guesswork with a systematic approach to LinkedIn growth, while solo entrepreneurs rely on it to build sustainable content routines that fit their available time. ● Produces a content calendar framework with posting frequency, timing recommendations, and pillar topics aligned to business goals ● Includes engagement playbooks specifying daily activities for comments, outreach, and community building ● Defines which KPIs to track weekly and monthly, with interpretation guidance and adjustment processes ● Recommends specific automation tools and clarifies what to schedule versus what requires authentic personal interaction ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn content strategist developing an automation plan to grow business presence and engagement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable LinkedIn content automation strategy covering scheduling, engagement, and analytics.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Include:
- Business type and target audience
- Current posting frequency
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Available resources for content creation and management

Desired metrics: {{desired-metrics}}

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with clear headings covering:

### Content Calendar Strategy
- Optimal posting times and frequency recommendations based on the target audience
- Content themes and pillar topics aligned with business goals
- Monthly/weekly scheduling framework

### Post Crafting Guidelines
- Techniques for writing engaging LinkedIn posts that drive interaction
- Content formats to rotate (text, polls, documents, videos, carousels)
- Hooks, structure, and calls-to-action that perform well

### Engagement Strategy
- Daily activities for interacting with followers and comments
- Outreach tactics for connecting with industry peers and potential clients
- Community-building approaches specific to the target audience

### Metrics & Optimization
- Which KPIs to track weekly and monthly
- How to interpret performance data
- Process for adjusting strategy based on results

### Automation Tools & Workflow
- Recommended scheduling and analytics platforms
- Workflow for batching content creation
- What to automate vs. what requires personal attention

Tailor all recommendations to the specific business type, audience behaviors, and resource constraints provided.
```
