# Direct Response Copywriting Prompt

## 簡介

The Direct Response Copywriting Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates persuasive marketing copy designed to compel immediate customer action through clarity and structured persuasion. This direct response copywriting prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini builds complete conversion-focused materials by applying dependency grammar principles to create clear cause-effect relationships in every sentence. You provide your offer details, target audience pain points, unique selling proposition, and desired call-to-action, and the prompt produces a five-section persuasive package: an attention-grabbing headline, an emotionally resonant opening hook, a benefit-driven body narrative, social proof elements, and a clear action directive. Marketers use it to write landing pages, sales emails, ad copy, and direct mail that moves readers through the classic AIDA progression - attention, interest, desire, action - while maintaining structural clarity that improves comprehension and response rates. Reach for this prompt when you need sales copy that balances emotional triggers with logical benefits and authentic urgency mechanisms to maximize conversions. ● Analyzes offer benefits against audience pain points to ensure message-market fit and relevance ● Structures sentences using dependency grammar to create clarity and strong cause-effect persuasion chains ● Produces five complete sections including headline, opening, body narrative, social proof, and call-to-action ● Incorporates urgency, scarcity, and credibility elements authentically to boost conversion rates without manipulation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert direct response copywriter who structures persuasive copy using dependency grammar principles to maximize clarity and impact.

## Task
Write conversion-focused copy that compels the target audience to take immediate action on the offer.

## Context
Product/service and positioning:
{{offer-details}}

Target audience pain points, desires, and demographics:
{{audience}}

Unique selling proposition:
{{usp}}

Desired action and tone:
{{cta-and-tone}}

## Process
1. Analyze the offer's core benefits and how they resolve audience pain points
2. Structure sentences using dependency grammar to create clear cause-effect relationships
3. Build progression: attention → interest → desire → action
4. Incorporate social proof elements (testimonials, case studies, metrics) where credible
5. Use urgency and scarcity mechanisms authentically
6. Ensure every sentence advances the reader toward the desired action

## Output
Deliver the copy in five labeled sections:

**Headline:**
[Attention-grabbing headline that speaks to core desire or pain]

**Opening:**
[Hook that resonates emotionally and establishes relevance]

**Body:**
[Persuasive narrative combining emotional triggers, logical benefits, and proof elements]

**Social Proof:**
[Testimonials, case studies, or credibility markers]

**Call-to-Action:**
[Clear, urgent directive that specifies exactly what to do next]
```
