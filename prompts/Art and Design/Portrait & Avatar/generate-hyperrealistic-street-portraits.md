# Hyperrealistic Street Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Street Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates magazine-quality outdoor portraits with natural sunlight, botanical shadows, and film-stock color grading for fashion brands, content creators, and lifestyle marketers. This street portrait prompt for Midjourney produces 8K images of subjects leaning against textured urban walls under bright midday sun, with high-contrast lighting, botanical shadow patterns, and Kodak Gold 200 film emulation. You control the outfit and styling details, wall and background setting, and overall color mood to match editorial or social-media needs. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering sharp medium-shot compositions with sunkissed makeup, contoured features, and the relaxed contrapposto stance typical of high-end fashion photography. Use it when you need authentic street-style visuals without location shoots or model bookings. ● Produces 8K hyperrealistic portraits with harsh midday sun, crisp botanical shadows, and Fujifilm X-T4 film emulation. ● Customizable outfit and styling, wall texture and urban setting, and color mood variables. ● Delivers medium-shot 4:5 compositions with high clarity, defined skin texture, and background sharpness at f/8. ● Ideal for fashion lookbooks, Instagram content, lifestyle blogs, and editorial campaigns that require authentic outdoor aesthetics. ## Prompt
