# Golden Hour Portrait Photo Editor

## 簡介

The Golden Hour Portrait Photo Editor is a free AI prompt that enhances portrait photos with professional lighting, makeup, and hair styling while keeping facial features unchanged. This golden hour portrait editing prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana applies bright morning sunlight effects, volumetric hair styling with loose messy buns and face-framing strands, and reflective silver glitter eyeshadow to any portrait photo. You provide three variables (skin tone, hair color, eye color) and the AI regenerates the image with professional beauty enhancements and directional golden lighting that creates natural shadows. Ideal for beauty brands, influencer content creators, and photographers who need consistent, polished portrait edits without reshooting or manually retouching every image. ● Applies bright morning sunlight from a strong directional source with realistic shadow placement across face and hair. ● Styles hair into a loose messy bun with volumetric lift, wet-look forehead strands, and long face-framing pieces. ● Adds highly reflective silver glitter eyeshadow with sparkling particles while maintaining original eye color and pupil clarity. ● Preserves the original face structure, features, and likeness throughout the transformation. ## Prompt

```
Edit this photo without changing the face: a beautiful woman with {{skin-tone}} skin and {{hair-color}} hair, {{eye-color}} eyes with clear pupils. Bright morning sunlight from a strong golden directional light source creating clear shadows on her face and hair.

Hair styled in a loose messy bun on top of the head, back section lifted looking thick and voluminous. A few wet-look strands curving on the forehead in an aesthetically neat random pattern. Two long straight strands on both sides softly framing the face. Natural shine throughout.

Eyes feature highly reflective silver glitter eyeshadow with tiny sparkling particles.
```
