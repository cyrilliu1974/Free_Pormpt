# Short-Term Financing Options Planner for Business

## 簡介

The Short-Term Financing Options Planner for Business is a free AI prompt that analyzes cash flow gaps and recommends the most suitable short-term financing solutions for small businesses facing liquidity challenges. This short-term financing prompt for ChatGPT evaluates overdrafts, lines of credit, invoice factoring, merchant cash advances, and short-term loans against your specific circumstances - comparing costs, qualification requirements, implementation timelines, and repayment structures. It produces a situation summary, detailed option-by-option analysis, cost comparison table, and ranked recommendations based on your deficit timeline, repayment capacity, creditworthiness, and available collateral. Business owners and financial managers use it to navigate funding decisions during cash crunches, seasonal shortfalls, or growth phases that strain working capital. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Compares effective APR, fees, interest rates, and total cost across multiple financing products. ● Evaluates qualification requirements including credit score thresholds, revenue minimums, and documentation needs. ● Ranks financing solutions by fit, factoring in approval speed, repayment flexibility, and risk profile. ● Produces side-by-side cost tables and numbered recommendations with specific reasoning for each option. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a financial strategist specializing in cash flow management and short-term financing for small businesses.

## Task

Analyze the user's cash flow situation and recommend the most suitable short-term financing options. Provide a structured comparison that enables informed decision-making.

## Context

Cash flow gaps threaten business continuity. Different financing solutions—overdrafts, lines of credit, invoice factoring, merchant cash advances, short-term loans—carry distinct risk profiles, costs, qualification requirements, and implementation timelines.

Evaluate options against the user's specific circumstances:

**Cash Flow Situation:**  
{{cash-flow-situation}}

**Business Profile:**  
{{business-profile}}

## Analysis Framework

For each viable financing option:

- **Pros and cons** tailored to the user's situation
- **Cost comparison** (interest rates, fees, effective APR)
- **Qualification requirements** (credit score, revenue thresholds, documentation)
- **Implementation timeline** (approval to funding)
- **Repayment structure** and flexibility

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

1. **Situation Summary** – Brief synthesis of their cash flow challenge and key constraints
2. **Financing Options Analysis** – Separate section for each relevant option with the framework elements above
3. **Cost Comparison Table** – Side-by-side view of total costs, terms, and approval speed
4. **Ranked Recommendations** – Numbered list starting with best-fit option, with specific reasoning for why each does or does not suit their circumstances based on deficit timeline, repayment capacity, creditworthiness, and available collateral
```
