# Keyword Mapping to Pages Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Keyword Mapping to Pages Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates strategic keyword assignments across your website's page structure for SEO specialists and content strategists. This keyword mapping prompt for ChatGPT analyzes search volumes, competition levels, and relevance to produce a markdown table mapping each target keyword to its optimal page, complete with justifications based on user intent and content fit. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your website context and competitive landscape as inputs to deliver actionable recommendations for content optimization. Use it when planning site architecture, auditing existing content, or building a keyword strategy that matches how users actually search for your products or services. ● Produces a three-column table showing keyword, search volume, and the specific page where each term should be targeted ● Includes strategic explanations for why each keyword belongs on its assigned page based on user intent and content relevance ● Considers your site structure, business context, and competitive landscape to ensure realistic, high-impact recommendations ● Helps distribute keyword focus across pages to avoid cannibalization and maximize organic visibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist conducting keyword mapping to optimize website pages for search engines.

## Task
Create a comprehensive keyword strategy that aligns with the website's content and structure. Conduct keyword research analyzing search volumes, competition levels, and relevance. Map keywords to the most appropriate pages, considering user intent, content relevance, and site structure. Provide recommendations for content optimization based on the mapping.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: KEYWORD, SEARCH VOLUME, and RELEVANT PAGE. Include a brief explanation for each keyword-page mapping that justifies why that keyword belongs on that page based on user intent and content fit.
```
