# Customer Survey Design and Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Survey Design and Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates complete survey instruments, analysis frameworks, and strategic recommendations for businesses seeking to understand customer preferences, satisfaction, and pain points. This customer survey prompt for ChatGPT builds a structured research package including 10-15 targeted questions using multiple formats (multiple choice, rating scales, open-ended), an analysis framework with key metrics and segmentation approaches, and 3-5 prioritized recommendations tied directly to your business goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your business context into a ready-to-deploy survey with built-in analysis methodology. Use it when launching new products, investigating customer churn, refining service delivery, or validating market assumptions. ● Creates 10-15 unbiased, goal-aligned survey questions with clear rationale for each question type ● Defines key metrics, benchmarks, and data segmentation methods for systematic analysis ● Generates anticipated insight areas and priority-ranked recommendations tied to your specific business challenges ● Balances quantitative and qualitative data collection through mixed question formats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher specializing in customer survey design, analysis, and strategic recommendations.

## Task
Create a complete customer survey instrument, analyze anticipated results, and deliver actionable recommendations tailored to the business context provided.

## Context
Business: {{business-context}}

The survey should:
- Address customer preferences, pain points, and satisfaction levels relevant to the stated goals and challenges
- Use a balanced mix of question types (multiple choice, rating scales, open-ended questions) to capture quantitative and qualitative data
- Ensure all questions are unbiased, clear, and directly aligned with business objectives
- Identify key trends, patterns, and correlations in the data
- Generate insights that directly support the primary business goals and address current challenges

## Output
Deliver a structured report with the following sections:

**1. Survey Instrument**
- 10-15 targeted questions with clear instructions
- Question type identified for each
- Rationale for key questions

**2. Analysis Framework**
- Key metrics and benchmarks to track
- Data segmentation approach
- Methods for identifying trends and correlations

**3. Sample Insights & Recommendations**
- 3-5 anticipated insight areas based on the business context
- Specific, actionable recommendations for each
- Priority ranking and implementation considerations

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout for readability.
```
