# Risk Assessment Matrix Generator for Business Controls

## 簡介

The Risk Assessment Matrix Generator for Business Controls is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive risk control framework tailored to your business context and risk tolerance level. This risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table that categorizes potential risks, rates their likelihood and impact as Low, Medium, or High, and prescribes specific control measures aligned with industry best practices. It covers financial, operational, strategic, and compliance risks while adapting to your stated business environment and regulatory requirements. Run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok by providing your business context and risk tolerance, and receive a structured matrix ready for board presentations or operational planning. Use cases include audit preparation, insurance underwriting support, strategic planning cycles, and compliance documentation. This prompt is designed for risk managers, compliance officers, CFOs, and business owners who need a systematic approach to identifying and controlling material risks without hiring external consultants. ● Categorizes risks across four critical domains: financial, operational, strategic, and compliance ● Rates each risk's likelihood and impact using a consistent Low / Medium / High scale for clear prioritization ● Proposes feasible, specific control measures rather than generic advice, tailored to your stated risk tolerance ● Adapts to industry-specific risks while covering universal business threats, ensuring comprehensive coverage ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant developing a comprehensive risk control framework.

## Task
Create a detailed risk assessment matrix that identifies, evaluates, and mitigates potential risks across financial, operational, strategic, and compliance domains. Categorize each risk, assess its likelihood and impact, and propose clear, actionable control measures aligned with industry best practices.

## Context
Business and regulatory environment:
{{business-context}}

Risk tolerance: {{risk-tolerance}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with these columns: Risk Category | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Control Measures

Structure:
- Each row addresses one distinct risk
- Likelihood and Impact: rate as Low / Medium / High
- Control Measures: specific, feasible actions the business can implement
- Cover industry-specific risks relevant to the business type plus general business risks (financial, operational, strategic, compliance)
- Tailor all recommendations to the stated risk tolerance level

Ensure the matrix is comprehensive yet focused on the most material risks for this business.
```
