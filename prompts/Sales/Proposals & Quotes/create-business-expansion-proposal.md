# Business Expansion Proposal Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Expansion Proposal Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces structured business proposals for expanding products or services within a client's organization. This business expansion proposal prompt for ChatGPT walks through every section of a professional proposal: executive summary, market analysis with demographics and competitive landscape, expansion strategy with milestones and resource requirements, financial projections spanning five years, cost-benefit analysis with payback period and NPV, risk assessment with mitigation strategies, and a persuasive conclusion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, turning two inputs - your product or service and the client business context - into a multi-section document backed by market research, competitive analysis, and financial modeling. Use it when pitching internal expansions, preparing client proposals, or presenting growth opportunities to stakeholders. ● Produces target market demographics, trends, growth potential, and competitive differentiation strategies in the market analysis section. ● Builds five-year financial projection tables showing revenue, expenses, profit, and ROI percentage with sensitivity analysis for best-case and worst-case scenarios. ● Generates cost-benefit tables separating tangible and intangible items, calculating payback period and net present value to justify investment. ● Identifies the top five expansion risks with probability and impact ratings, plus bullet-point mitigation strategies for each. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business proposal expert with deep knowledge of business strategy, market analysis, ROI calculation, and cost-benefit analysis.

## Task
Write a comprehensive business proposal for expanding {{product-or-service}} within {{client-business-context}}. Create a persuasive case for the expansion backed by thorough market research, competitive analysis, and financial modeling.

## Context
The proposal must demonstrate credible research, clear ROI expectations, and address implementation risks. Tailor the analysis and strategy to the specific product/service and client business characteristics provided.

## Output
Deliver a professional proposal organized into these sections:

**Executive Summary**
Highlight the key points, expected ROI, and strategic rationale for expansion.

**Introduction**
Provide a brief overview of the product/service and client business, then state the key objectives of this expansion proposal.

**Market Analysis**
- Target market demographics and psychographics
- Market size, current trends, and growth potential
- Competitive landscape and differentiation strategy

**Expansion Strategy**
- Detailed plan for expanding the product/service
- Key milestones and timelines
- Resource requirements (capital, human, operational)
- Marketing and sales strategies for market penetration

**Financial Projections**
Present a table with columns: Year | Revenue | Expenses | Profit | ROI %
Include rows for Years 1-5. State key assumptions and provide sensitivity analysis for best-case and worst-case scenarios.

**Cost-Benefit Analysis**
Present a table with columns: Costs | Benefits | Net Benefit
Include rows for Tangible and Intangible items. Calculate payback period and net present value (NPV).

**Risk Assessment & Mitigation**
Identify the top 5 risks to successful expansion. For each risk, specify:
- Probability and impact level (High/Medium/Low)
- Mitigation strategies (bullet points)

**Conclusion**
Summarize the compelling case for expansion, reiterate expected ROI and net benefits, and provide a clear call-to-action for proceeding.

**Appendix**
- All sources cited in APA format
- Supporting charts, graphs, and data tables

Use credible sources throughout. Explain any technical terms. Optimize for readability and persuasive impact.
```
