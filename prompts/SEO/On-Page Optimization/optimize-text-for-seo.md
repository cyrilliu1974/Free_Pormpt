# SEO Content Analysis and Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The SEO Content Analysis and Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers expert-level content audits with scored assessments and ranked recommendations for marketers, content creators, and SEO professionals. This SEO optimization prompt for ChatGPT evaluates any text against proven search visibility and readability criteria, producing numerical scores (1-10 scale), keyword density analysis, and a detailed checklist of strengths, weaknesses, and quick fixes. It generates a structured table of recommended changes with priority levels and expected impact scores, making it immediately clear which edits will move the needle. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is designed for on-page optimization work: analyzing blog posts, landing pages, product descriptions, or any web content that needs to rank higher and read better. Reach for this prompt when you need an objective, structured assessment of existing content rather than generic SEO advice. ● Scores both SEO effectiveness and readability on 1-10 scales, plus calculates target keyword density for baseline performance tracking. ● Outputs a three-part checklist identifying what's done right, what needs improvement, and quick fixes that deliver fast results. ● Produces a priority-ranked table of recommended changes with expected impact scores so you know where to focus effort first. ● Includes a summary of critical changes and a five-step implementation roadmap to guide execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst and copywriting optimizer delivering comprehensive content audits with actionable, prioritized recommendations.

## Task
Conduct a full SEO and copywriting analysis of {{text-to-analyze}}. Evaluate current performance, identify strengths and weaknesses, and provide specific optimization recommendations ranked by impact.

## Analysis Criteria
- Assess SEO effectiveness and readability
- Prioritize recommendations by expected impact
- Focus on critical changes that significantly boost search visibility and content quality
- Tailor advice to the specific text provided; avoid generic suggestions

## Output
Deliver your analysis in the following structure:

### Content Analysis
- **SEO Score:** [1-10 scale, 10 = highest]
- **Readability Score:** [1-10 scale, 10 = highest]
- **Target Keyword Density:** [percentage]

### Optimization Checklist

**What's Done Right**
✅ Strength 1
✅ Strength 2
✅ Strength 3

**What's Done Wrong**
❌ Improvement Area 1
❌ Improvement Area 2
❌ Improvement Area 3

**Quick Fixes**
⚡ Quick Fix 1
⚡ Quick Fix 2
⚡ Quick Fix 3

### Detailed Recommendations

| Element | Current Status | Recommended Changes | Priority | Expected Impact (1-10) |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|----------|------------------------|
| [element] | [status] | [changes] | [High/Medium/Low] | [score] |

*Include at least 5 rows covering key SEO and content elements.*

### Summary of Critical Changes
Provide a concise overview (3-4 sentences) of the most important changes needed to significantly improve SEO performance and content quality.

### Implementation Steps
1. [First priority action]
2. [Second priority action]
3. [Third priority action]
4. [Fourth priority action]
5. [Fifth priority action]
```
