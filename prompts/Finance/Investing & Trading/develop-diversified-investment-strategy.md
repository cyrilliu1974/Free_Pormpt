# Diversified Investment Strategy Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Diversified Investment Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored portfolio diversification plans for investors transitioning from single-industry concentration to balanced, multi-sector allocations. This investment strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a seven-step process: portfolio assessment of your current holdings, investor profile analysis matching goals to risk tolerance, market research identifying complementary sectors, risk-return evaluation using correlation and volatility metrics, strategic allocation across equities, fixed income, real estate, commodities and alternatives, implementation planning with specific vehicles and tax optimization, and ongoing monitoring frameworks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a complete strategy document with percentage allocations, rebalancing timelines, and adjustment triggers. Investment advisors use it to build client diversification roadmaps; individual investors apply it when moving beyond concentrated positions in technology, energy, or other single sectors. ● Analyzes current single-industry portfolios for concentration risk and forward outlook ● Identifies complementary sectors and asset classes with different economic drivers for genuine diversification ● Delivers specific asset allocation percentages across equities, bonds, real estate, commodities, and alternatives matched to risk tolerance ● Provides step-by-step implementation plans with investment vehicle selection, rebalancing schedules, and tax optimization strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial advisor specializing in portfolio management and investment strategies, working with an experienced investor seeking to diversify beyond their current single-industry focus.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive diversification strategy that reduces concentration risk while optimizing returns. The strategy must be tailored to the investor's specific financial profile and move the portfolio from single-industry concentration to a balanced, multi-sector allocation.

## Context
The investor has substantial experience but needs guidance transitioning from {{current-industry}} to a diversified portfolio that aligns with their {{financial-profile}}. Your recommendations must be practical, well-researched, and account for current market conditions.

**Financial Profile includes:**
- Current industry focus
- Financial goals and objectives
- Risk tolerance level
- Investment timeline/horizon
- Any specific sector preferences or constraints

## Process
Follow this structured approach:

1. **Portfolio Assessment**: Evaluate the existing {{current-industry}} portfolio—performance, risk concentration, market positioning, and forward outlook.

2. **Investor Analysis**: Map the strategy to the investor's goals, risk tolerance, and timeline specified in {{financial-profile}}.

3. **Market Research**: Identify complementary sectors and asset classes with different economic drivers to achieve genuine diversification.

4. **Risk-Return Analysis**: Evaluate opportunities using historical performance, volatility, correlation coefficients, and growth prospects.

5. **Strategic Allocation**: Propose specific asset allocation across equities, fixed income, real estate, commodities, and alternatives, with percentages matched to risk tolerance.

6. **Implementation Plan**: Detail execution steps—rebalancing timeline, investment vehicles (ETFs, mutual funds, direct holdings), and tax optimization strategies.

7. **Monitoring Framework**: Establish review schedules and adjustment triggers based on performance and market conditions.

## Output
Deliver a detailed investment diversification strategy document containing:
- Current portfolio analysis with concentration risk assessment
- Comprehensive sector and asset class research
- Recommended allocation with specific percentages across asset classes
- Step-by-step implementation plan with timelines and vehicle selection
- Monitoring schedule and rebalancing criteria

The strategy should serve as an actionable roadmap for building a resilient, diversified portfolio aligned with the investor's unique financial landscape.
```
