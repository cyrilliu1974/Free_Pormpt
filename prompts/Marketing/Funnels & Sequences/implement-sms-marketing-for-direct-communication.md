# SMS Marketing Strategy Generator for Direct Sales

## 簡介

The SMS Marketing Strategy Generator for Direct Sales is a free AI prompt that builds complete mobile messaging campaigns for marketers and business owners driving sales through text. This SMS marketing prompt for ChatGPT takes a campaign brief - audience demographics, product details, goals, and duration - and produces a structured strategy covering audience segmentation, personalized message templates with CTAs, send schedules, compliance checkpoints for TCPA and GDPR, and A/B testing recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering numbered strategy components with specific tactics, timing windows, and success metrics. Use it when launching promotional campaigns, event registrations, cart-recovery sequences, or location-based offers that require consent management and anti-fatigue scheduling. ● Segments audiences by behavior, purchase history, and engagement level for targeted delivery ● Creates under-160-character message templates with single-focus calls-to-action and personalization variables ● Defines send schedules and frequency caps to prevent subscriber fatigue and maintain list health ● Embeds compliance frameworks covering consent verification, opt-out mechanisms, sender identification, and carrier guidelines ● Includes A/B testing recommendations for copy variants, send times, and segmentation logic to improve conversion rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SMS marketing strategist specializing in high-conversion mobile campaigns.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive SMS marketing strategy that drives sales and engagement through targeted, compliant messaging.

## Context
**Campaign Parameters:**
{{campaign-brief}}
(Include: target audience demographics and behaviors, product/service being promoted, unique selling proposition, campaign duration, and primary goal—e.g., increase sales by 25%, boost event registrations, drive store traffic)

**Requirements:**
- Craft attention-grabbing opening hooks (under 160 characters)
- Personalize messages using customer data and segmentation
- Design clear, single-focus calls-to-action
- Optimize message timing and frequency to avoid fatigue
- Ensure full compliance with TCPA, GDPR, and carrier guidelines (consent, opt-out, identification)
- Segment audiences by behavior, purchase history, or engagement level
- Include A/B testing recommendations for copy and timing

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a **numbered list** where each main strategy component is a heading followed by bullet points detailing specific tactics, message examples, timing windows, segmentation logic, and compliance checkpoints. Structure should cover: audience segmentation, message templates with CTAs, send schedule, compliance framework, success metrics, and optimization plan.
```
