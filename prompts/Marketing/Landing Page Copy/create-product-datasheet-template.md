# Product Datasheet Template Generator

## 簡介

The Product Datasheet Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional, comprehensive product datasheets for businesses and product teams. This product datasheet prompt for ChatGPT transforms raw product details into a structured document that presents technical specifications, key features, competitive benefits, and real-world applications in a format accessible to both engineers and decision-makers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, organizing information into logical sections - product overview, feature highlights, categorized technical specs, customer benefits, industry applications, and visual guidance - so potential buyers can quickly evaluate fit and value. Product managers, technical writers, marketing teams, and sales engineers reach for this prompt when they need to compile scattered product information into a single, authoritative reference document that supports purchasing decisions. ● Structures product information into eight standard sections - name, description, key features, technical specifications by category, benefits, applications, diagrams, and images - for consistent presentation across product lines. ● Balances technical accuracy with readability by organizing specs into logical categories (physical, performance, compatibility) while translating features into tangible customer benefits. ● Highlights competitive advantages and unique selling points without marketing fluff, keeping content practical and decision-focused. ● Guides visual asset inclusion by specifying where diagrams and high-quality images should appear to illustrate functionality and build confidence. ## Prompt

```

You are a product datasheet developer compiling technical information into a professional, comprehensive document for both technical and non-technical audiences.

## Task
Create a complete product datasheet that enables potential customers to evaluate the product's suitability for their needs. Present specifications, features, benefits, and applications in a clear, accurate, and accessible format.

## Context
The datasheet should highlight competitive advantages and unique selling points while remaining practical and easy to navigate. Use technically accurate language without unnecessary jargon, and organize content so readers can quickly locate relevant information.
```
