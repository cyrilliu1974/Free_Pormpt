# Niche Market Opportunity Finder for Low-Investment Ideas

## 簡介

The Niche Market Opportunity Finder for Low-Investment Ideas is a free AI prompt that identifies five viable business niches matched to a user's skills, available resources, and budget constraints. This niche market research prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing a user profile - skills, time, tools, networks, and financial limits - and returning a structured table of market opportunities. Each row details the niche's key characteristics, target audience, potential products or services, and estimated startup costs. The prompt also requires three to five cited sources, grounding every recommendation in real market data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for entrepreneurs, freelancers, and side-hustle seekers who need actionable intel without expensive consultants or upfront capital. Use this prompt when you want evidence-based business ideas that fit what you already have - no guesswork, no generic listicles. ● Returns a five-row comparison table with target audience, product ideas, and cost estimates for each niche. ● Matches opportunities to the user's stated skills, tools, networks, and spending limits. ● Requires citation of reputable sources so recommendations are verifiable and grounded in real trends. ● Filters for niches that demand minimal upfront capital, reducing financial risk for first-time founders and bootstrappers. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business strategist specializing in low-investment niche market research.

## Task
Identify 5 promising niche market opportunities that align with the user's profile and require minimal upfront investment. Each opportunity should be viable, evidence-based, and tailored to the available resources.

## Context
**User Profile:**
{{user-profile}}

Include: skills, available resources (time, tools, networks), and budget constraints.

## Output
Provide a comprehensive table with these columns:

| Niche Market | Key Characteristics | Target Audience | Potential Products/Services | Estimated Startup Costs |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------|
| [Market 1] | [Characteristics] | [Audience] | [Products/Services] | [Cost range] |
| [Market 2] | [Characteristics] | [Audience] | [Products/Services] | [Cost range] |
| [Market 3] | [Characteristics] | [Audience] | [Products/Services] | [Cost range] |
| [Market 4] | [Characteristics] | [Audience] | [Products/Services] | [Cost range] |
| [Market 5] | [Characteristics] | [Audience] | [Products/Services] | [Cost range] |

**Requirements:**
- Focus on markets achievable within the stated budget
- Provide specific, actionable product/service ideas
- Estimate realistic startup costs (include ranges if appropriate)
- Cite 3-5 reputable sources supporting your recommendations

**Sources:**

3. [Source with link/citation]
...
```
