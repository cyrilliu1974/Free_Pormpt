# Guest Blogging Strategy Prompt for SEO and Link Building

## 簡介

The Guest Blogging Strategy Prompt for SEO and Link Building is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed, actionable guest blogging plan for content marketers and SEO professionals in any industry. This guest blogging strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a five-phase roadmap: identifying high-authority target websites using domain metrics, analyzing content gaps and opportunities on each site, developing 3-5 compelling article pitches that allow for natural backlinks, crafting personalized outreach emails to editors, and tracking referral traffic and domain authority gains over time. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, organizing deliverables into a structured checklist format that includes a tracking spreadsheet framework. Real-world use cases include SaaS companies building thought leadership, agencies scaling link-building campaigns, and independent consultants establishing industry authority through bylines on tier-one publications. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic approach to guest posting that prioritizes ROI over volume, emphasizing relationship-building and editorial fit rather than mass outreach. ● Produces a prioritized target list framework with domain authority, traffic, and contact tracking fields ● Guides content gap analysis to uncover underserved topics that align with editorial standards ● Generates personalized outreach email templates that demonstrate familiarity with each publication ● Includes a post-publication tracking system for measuring referral traffic, backlink acquisition, and ongoing contributor relationships ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content and SEO strategist with deep knowledge of guest blogging best practices across various industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive guest blogging strategy for {{industry}}, providing a detailed, actionable checklist for identifying relevant websites, crafting compelling content pitches, and securing valuable backlinks. Prioritize steps that yield the greatest ROI in terms of referral traffic and domain authority.

## Output
Organize the strategy into five progressive steps:

**Step 1: Identify Top Industry Websites**
- Use tools like Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Google to find the most influential blogs and publications in {{industry}}
- Create a spreadsheet to track domain metrics (DR, UR, organic traffic) and contact information
- Prioritize sites with engaged audiences and strong social media presence

**Step 2: Analyze Content Gaps & Opportunities**
- Thoroughly review the content on each target site
- Look for topics they haven't covered comprehensively or recently
- Identify content that performed well and brainstorm fresh angles or updated information you could provide
- Note specific content gaps and opportunities for each site in your tracking sheet

**Step 3: Develop Compelling Content Ideas**
- Brainstorm 3-5 unique, valuable article ideas for each target site based on your content gap analysis
- Ensure each topic aligns with the site's editorial standards and audience interests
- Angle the topics to naturally allow for 1-2 relevant, contextual links back to your site
- Add your topic ideas to the tracking sheet for each site

**Step 4: Craft Personalized Outreach Emails**
- Find the appropriate editor or content manager's contact info for each site
- Write a concise, compelling email pitch customized to each contact and site
- Demonstrate your familiarity with their content and how your ideas provide unique value to their readers
- Highlight your relevant industry expertise and writing credentials
- Include 2-3 of your strongest topic ideas and invite them to select one or request additional ideas

**Step 5: Execute & Track Results**
- Aim to send 3-5 outreach emails per week to your prioritized list of targets
- Upon securing a guest post opportunity, deliver your highest-quality work, incorporating approved backlinks
- Once live, promote your guest post via your own social channels and email list
- Track post performance, referral traffic, and new backlinks in your spreadsheet
- Continue building relationships with editors and seek to become a regular contributor to the top sites
```
