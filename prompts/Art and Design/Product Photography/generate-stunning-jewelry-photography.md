# Jewelry Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators

## 簡介

The Jewelry Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality close-up images of gemstone necklaces styled as minimal flat-lay compositions. This jewelry photography prompt for Midjourney produces a macro shot of a hand holding a crystal pendant on a delicate gold chain, arranged overhead on soft linen with blush and sand-neutral backgrounds, dried floral accents, and warm directional morning sunlight that illuminates every facet of the gemstone. The prompt specifies a 100mm macro lens perspective at f/2.8, ensuring the pendant stays in razor-sharp focus while the background melts into a creamy blur. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering ultra-detailed, high-resolution renders with a clean, soft editorial mood and natural color grading. Use it when you need product photography that balances technical precision with lifestyle warmth - ideal for e-commerce listings, social media campaigns, or editorial features in fashion and design publications. ● Macro perspective with 100mm lens simulation and f/2.8 shallow depth of field for sharp gemstone detail and soft background blur. ● Warm directional morning sunlight specification that catches facets and creates natural highlights on metal and crystal. ● Styled flat-lay composition with soft linen, blush and sand tones, scattered dried florals, and hand placement for organic editorial appeal. ● High-resolution output parameters (--ar 4:5 --style raw --s 250 --q 2) tuned for ultra-detailed, luminous results with natural color grading. ## Prompt
