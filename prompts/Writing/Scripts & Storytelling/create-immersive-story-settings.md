# Immersive Story Setting Builder for Fiction Writers

## 簡介

The Immersive Story Setting Builder for Fiction Writers is a free AI prompt that creates vivid, detailed fictional worlds for novelists, screenwriters, and game designers. This story setting prompt for ChatGPT works by taking a single inspiration phrase and expanding it into a complete world-building framework: physical geography and climate, cultural systems and beliefs, magic or technology rules, historical backstory, three fully realized key locations, and a detailed analysis of how the setting influences plot events, character development, thematic depth, and emotional atmosphere. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured world-building documentation that writers can immediately use to anchor their narratives. Real use cases include designing fantasy realms for novels, building sci-fi colonies for screenplays, creating atmospheric backdrops for short fiction, and developing game worlds with internal consistency. This prompt is for fiction writers, narrative designers, and game developers who need a setting that does more than provide a backdrop - one that actively shapes story and character. ● Develops four core world-building pillars: geography, culture, magic or technology systems, and formative history ● Describes three key locations with physical detail, atmosphere, and story significance ● Explains how the setting influences plot conflict, character motivation, thematic exploration, and emotional tone ● Generates three story concepts that emerge organically from the world you've built ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert storyteller and world-builder who crafts immersive settings that shape narrative and character.

## Task
Create a vivid, detailed setting for a story based on the provided inspiration. Develop world-building elements, key locations, and explain how the setting influences plot, characters, themes, and atmosphere.

## Context
Setting inspiration: {{setting-inspiration}}

## Output
Provide your response in the following structure:

**Setting Inspiration**
Restate the inspiration and your initial vision.

**World-Building Elements**
- **Geography**: Physical landscape, climate, notable natural features
- **Culture**: Social structures, customs, beliefs, daily life
- **Magic or Technology**: Systems of power, innovation, or supernatural elements that define the world
- **History**: Key past events that shaped the current state of the world

**Key Locations**
Describe three significant places:
1. **[Location Name]**: Physical description, atmosphere, and narrative significance
2. **[Location Name]**: Physical description, atmosphere, and narrative significance
3. **[Location Name]**: Physical description, atmosphere, and narrative significance

**Setting Influence**
- **Plot**: How geography, culture, or history creates conflict and drives events
- **Characters**: How the environment shapes character motivations, skills, and worldviews
- **Themes**: What larger ideas the setting embodies or explores
- **Mood and Atmosphere**: The emotional tone the setting evokes

**Story Ideas**
Provide three distinct story concepts that emerge naturally from this setting, each highlighting different aspects of the world you've built.
```
