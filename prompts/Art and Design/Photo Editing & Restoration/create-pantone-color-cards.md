# Pantone Color Card Generator From Photo

## 簡介

The Pantone Color Card Generator From Photo is a free AI prompt that transforms any photograph into a professional Pantone-style design card with extracted color swatches and codes. The prompt takes a close-up photograph as the background and overlays a centered white frame containing a solid color swatch sampled from the image's most dominant hue, labeled with "PANTONE" text and the matching Pantone code in clean, minimal typography. This Pantone color card prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and similar AI image models is ideal for designers, marketers, and visual creators who want to turn product photos, nature shots, or brand imagery into graphic design assets that communicate color in a universally recognized format. Use it to create mood boards, brand color documentation, social media graphics, or portfolio pieces that blend photography with graphic design aesthetics. ● Extracts the most dominant color from your photograph and displays it as a solid swatch inside a centered white frame. ● Automatically labels the swatch with bold "PANTONE" branding and the matching Pantone color code for authenticity. ● Maintains the original photograph as the full background, creating a layered design that blends photography with graphic elements. ● Produces modern, minimal, and graphic aesthetics that mimic official Pantone design cards without manual color sampling or design software. ## Prompt

```
A visually aesthetic image with a close-up photograph of {{source-image}} as the full background. Overlay a centered white Pantone-style frame in the middle of the image. Inside the frame, display a solid color swatch sampled from the most dominant color in the background photograph. Below the swatch, write "PANTONE" in bold black text and beneath that, include the Pantone code matching the swatch color. The swatch and text should be clean, minimal, and centered inside the white frame, mimicking official Pantone design cards. Maintain a modern, graphic, and clean aesthetic.
```
