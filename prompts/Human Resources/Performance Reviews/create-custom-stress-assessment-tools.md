# Occupational Stress Assessment Tool Generator

## 簡介

The Occupational Stress Assessment Tool Generator is a free AI prompt that builds profession-specific stress evaluation frameworks for HR professionals, managers, and organizational wellness teams. This stress assessment prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured table listing five key stressors unique to your specified profession or industry, each rated by frequency (1-5) and impact (1-5) with calculated total scores, plus three actionable management tips tailored to that field's challenges. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a ready-to-use assessment tool with scoring guide and field-specific coping strategies. Use it to create workplace wellness surveys, benchmark occupational health risks, or design intervention programs for teachers, nurses, software developers, retail workers, or any other profession. ● Identifies five stressors most relevant to the specified profession rather than generic workplace stress factors ● Produces a frequency-impact scoring matrix that quantifies which stressors demand priority attention ● Includes a clear 1-5 scoring guide so anyone can complete the assessment without specialized training ● Generates three practical management tips grounded in the unique challenges of the target profession ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in occupational stress assessment, designing evaluation frameworks tailored to specific professions and industries.

## Task
Create a customized stress assessment tool for {{profession-or-industry}} that identifies the five most relevant stressors and provides actionable management strategies.

## Output

### Stress Assessment Table for {{profession-or-industry}}

| Stress Factor | Frequency (1-5) | Impact (1-5) | Total Score |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|
| [Factor 1] | [1-5] | [1-5] | [Frequency × Impact] |
| [Factor 2] | [1-5] | [1-5] | [Frequency × Impact] |
| [Factor 3] | [1-5] | [1-5] | [Frequency × Impact] |
| [Factor 4] | [1-5] | [1-5] | [Frequency × Impact] |
| [Factor 5] | [1-5] | [1-5] | [Frequency × Impact] |

**Scoring Guide:**
- Frequency: How often this stressor occurs (1 = rarely, 5 = constantly)
- Impact: How severely it affects well-being (1 = minimal, 5 = severe)
- Total Score = Frequency × Impact (higher scores indicate more significant stressors)

### Stress Management Tips for {{profession-or-industry}}
1. [Practical tip addressing the most common stressors]
2. [Field-specific coping strategy]
3. [Preventive measure suited to this profession]

Focus on stressors and strategies that are directly relevant to the unique challenges faced in {{profession-or-industry}}. Make the assessment actionable and the tips immediately applicable.
```
