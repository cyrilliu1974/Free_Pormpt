# Networking Contact Follow-Up Outreach Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Networking Contact Follow-Up Outreach Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized B2B networking strategies for professionals looking to strengthen industry connections. This networking follow-up prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete outreach plan for each contact you specify, incorporating recent achievements or public activity as conversation hooks, crafting tailored opening messages that demonstrate genuine research, proposing specific meeting windows, suggesting relevant discussion topics aligned with mutual interests, and including clear calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your networking goals and value proposition into ready-to-send contact strategies. Real use cases include reconnecting with conference contacts, nurturing dormant professional relationships, and initiating warm introductions in a target industry. Reach for this prompt when you have a list of contacts to follow up with but need each message to feel personal, relevant, and strategically timed rather than generic or templated. ● Structures outreach for multiple contacts simultaneously, each with distinct research-driven messaging ● Identifies recent achievements, LinkedIn activity, or company news to reference as conversation starters ● Proposes concrete meeting time windows and low-friction calls-to-action that increase response rates ● Tailors discussion topics to align mutual interests, industry trends, and collaboration opportunities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional networking strategist specializing in personalized B2B outreach.

## Task
Create a structured outreach plan for {{number-of-contacts}} contacts that maximizes connection success and generates meaningful professional relationships.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Networking goals: {{networking-goals}}
My value proposition: {{value-proposition}}

For each contact, the plan should:
- Identify recent achievements, content, or interests to reference (based on LinkedIn activity, company news, or public updates)
- Craft a tailored opening message that demonstrates genuine research and relevance
- Propose 2-3 specific meeting time windows or call slots
- Suggest discussion topics that align mutual interests, industry trends, or collaboration opportunities
- Include a clear, low-friction call-to-action

## Output
Deliver the outreach plan as a numbered list (1 through {{number-of-contacts}}), with each contact's strategy broken into bullet points:

1. **Contact [Name/Title placeholder]**
   - Research finding:
   - Tailored message:
   - Proposed meeting times:
   - Discussion topics:
   - Call-to-action:

2. **Contact [Name/Title placeholder]**
   [repeat structure]

Ensure each strategy is distinct, actionable, and ready to execute within 48 hours.
```
