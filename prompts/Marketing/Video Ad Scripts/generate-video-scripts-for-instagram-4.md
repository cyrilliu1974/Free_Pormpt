# Instagram Video Script Generator for Engagement

## 簡介

The Instagram Video Script Generator for Engagement is a free AI prompt that creates attention-grabbing video script outlines designed to promote products or services, build brand awareness, and motivate Instagram viewers to take action. This Instagram video script prompt for ChatGPT works by applying dependency grammar principles to ensure each section of your script flows logically into the next - your hook establishes context, the body delivers on the hook's promise, and the call-to-action follows naturally from the value you've demonstrated. You input your product or service, target audience, brand voice and unique selling points, and desired call-to-action; the prompt outputs a complete script outline with hook, key messages, CTA, and visual pacing notes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for teams across different platforms. Use it when you need Instagram Reels or Stories scripts that stop the scroll in the first 2 seconds and convert views into measurable actions - whether you're a social media manager, content creator, or brand strategist launching campaigns. ● Outputs structured script outlines with hook (0-3 seconds), key messages, call-to-action, and visual pacing notes ● Uses dependency grammar to ensure each script element builds logically on the previous for coherent storytelling ● Tailors content to your specific product, target audience, brand voice, and desired viewer action ● Includes guidance on on-screen elements, transitions, and timing to maximize viewer retention on Instagram ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a video content strategist specializing in Instagram video scripts that drive engagement and conversions.

## Task
Generate a structured Instagram video script outline that promotes the offering, builds brand awareness, and motivates viewers to act. Use dependency grammar principles to create logical, coherent flow where each element builds on the previous.

## Context
**Offering:** {{product-service}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Brand voice & key selling points:** {{brand-voice-and-usp}}

Instagram video content must capture attention in the first 2 seconds, deliver value quickly, and end with a clear next step. Structure the script so each section depends logically on what precedes it—hook establishes context, body delivers on the hook's promise, call-to-action follows naturally from the value demonstrated.

## Output
Provide a script outline using this structure:

**HOOK (0-3 seconds)**
- Opening line or visual that stops the scroll
- Why this matters to the viewer now

**KEY MESSAGES (Main body)**
- 3-4 talking points that highlight benefits and address audience needs
- Each point should flow from the previous, building a case
- Weave in brand voice naturally

**CALL-TO-ACTION (Close)**
- Specific action: {{cta}}
- Clear next step (link in bio, comment, share, follow, etc.)
- Urgency or incentive if applicable

**VISUAL/PACING NOTES**
- Suggested on-screen elements, transitions, or timing to maximize retention

Format as a clear outline with headings and bullet points.
```
