# Job Offer Negotiation Strategies and Scripts

## 簡介

The Job Offer Negotiation Strategies and Scripts prompt is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive negotiation guides for professionals seeking optimal salary, benefits, and work conditions. It delivers a structured output including 5-7 tailored strategies with implementation steps, 3-4 realistic negotiation scenarios with multi-turn dialogue scripts, a detailed preparation checklist table, and credible source citations. This job offer negotiation prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing guides customized to your specific profession and context that emphasize market research, value documentation, and collaborative win-win approaches rather than adversarial tactics. Professionals preparing for compensation discussions, career changers entering new industries, and anyone facing an employment offer will find actionable guidance grounded in ethical, legally compliant negotiation practices. ● Produces 5-7 evidence-based negotiation strategies tailored to the user's profession, industry, and specific situation with clear implementation steps for each ● Provides 3-4 realistic multi-turn dialogue scenarios demonstrating how to apply strategies in actual conversations with hiring managers and recruiters ● Generates a complete preparation checklist covering market research, value documentation, priority identification, walk-away points, and practice sessions ● Includes 4-6 credible source citations supporting the advice, ensuring all guidance is grounded in established negotiation research and ethical practices ## Prompt

```

You are an expert negotiation strategist and career coach specializing in job offer negotiations. Provide evidence-based guidance to help professionals secure optimal salary, benefits, and work conditions.

## Task
Create a comprehensive job offer negotiation guide tailored to the professional's context. Include actionable strategies, realistic scenario scripts, and a preparation checklist with credible source citations.

## Context

Emphasize preparation, market research, and understanding one's value. Adopt a collaborative, win-win approach rather than adversarial tactics. All advice must be ethical and legally compliant. Avoid unrealistic guarantees about outcomes.
```
