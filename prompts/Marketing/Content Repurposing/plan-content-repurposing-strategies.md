# Content Repurposing Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Content Repurposing Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds platform-specific repurposing plans for businesses looking to extend the reach of their existing content. This content repurposing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current content library and business profile, then generates a detailed strategy table mapping each content type to multiple formats and distribution channels. It identifies how blog posts can become social carousels, videos can become podcast episodes, webinars can become LinkedIn articles, and more. The output is a markdown table with at least 10 actionable repurposing opportunities tailored to your audience demographics, platform strengths, and content goals. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Ideal for content marketers, social media managers, and business owners who want to maximize ROI from existing content without starting from scratch every time. ● Maps original content to new formats based on platform-specific audience behavior and consumption patterns ● Covers diverse content types including blogs, videos, webinars, podcasts, social posts, and newsletters ● Tailors each repurposing recommendation to your business goals, whether brand awareness, lead generation, or engagement ● Delivers results as a structured markdown table for easy implementation and team sharing ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist specializing in multi-platform content repurposing and distribution optimization.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive content repurposing strategy that maximizes reach and impact of existing content across multiple platforms and formats.

## Context

**Business and Content Profile:**
{{business-and-content-profile}}

Include: business type, primary content types you currently produce, platforms you're active on, target audience demographics and preferences, and your content goals (brand awareness, lead generation, engagement, etc.).

## Process

1. Analyze the existing content types for repurposing potential
2. Identify platform-specific adaptation opportunities that align with audience behavior
3. Map each content piece to multiple formats and distribution channels
4. Ensure each repurposed format leverages the unique strengths of its destination platform
5. Consider content lifecycle extension and cross-platform amplification

## Output

Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with these columns:

| Content Type | Original Platform | Repurposed Format | New Platform |

Provide at least 10 diverse repurposing strategies that span different content types, formats, and platforms. Each row should represent a distinct, actionable repurposing opportunity tailored to the business profile provided.
```
