# Communication Plan Builder for Organizations

## 簡介

The Communication Plan Builder for Organizations is a free AI prompt that creates actionable communication strategies for businesses, nonprofits, and institutions of any size. This communication plan prompt for ChatGPT works by gathering your organization's context - industry, size, target audiences, and budget - then outputs a complete framework covering objectives, stakeholder segmentation (internal and external), core messaging, channel selection, phased timelines, risk mitigation, and measurable KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Use cases include product launches, organizational change initiatives, crisis communication preparation, and ongoing stakeholder engagement programs. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable structure to align messaging across departments, ensure consistency in brand voice, and anticipate communication obstacles before they derail your strategy. ● Maps both internal and external stakeholders so no audience is overlooked ● Defines three-phase timelines with specific deliverables for each stage ● Identifies realistic challenges and pairs each with a practical mitigation strategy ● Establishes success metrics that tie directly back to strategic objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a communication strategist developing a comprehensive internal and external communication plan.

## Task
Create a structured communication plan that establishes clear, consistent messaging across all stakeholders. The plan must be actionable and address both opportunities and obstacles.

## Context
Organization: {{organization-context}}

Provide: organization name, industry, size (number of employees), primary target audiences, and available communication budget.

## Output
Deliver the plan in this structure:

**Communication Objectives:**
- Three strategic objectives the plan will achieve

**Key Stakeholders:**
*Internal:*
- List three primary internal stakeholder groups

*External:*
- List three primary external stakeholder groups

**Key Messages:**
1. Three core messages aligned with organizational goals and values, to be conveyed consistently across all communications

**Communication Channels:**
*Internal:*
- Three channels selected for effectiveness with internal audiences

*External:*
- Three channels appropriate for external stakeholder engagement

**Communication Timeline:**
Structure as three phases, each containing:
- Duration
- 3-4 key activities with clear deliverables

**Potential Challenges:**
- Identify three realistic obstacles that may arise during implementation

**Mitigation Strategies:**
- Provide practical, feasible strategies to address each challenge

**Success Metrics:**
- Define three specific, measurable metrics aligned with the communication objectives
```
