# Brand Story Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Story Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds complete brand narratives for businesses seeking to articulate their identity, values, and mission in a way that resonates with their audience. This brand storytelling prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating a five-section narrative structure covering origin, values, mission, impact, and vision. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your business context as input and producing a logically flowing story that connects emotionally with your target market. Use it when launching a new brand, refreshing positioning, preparing investor decks, or writing About Us pages that actually convert. The prompt is designed for founders, marketing teams, and brand strategists who need a coherent narrative framework rather than scattered messaging. ● Structures your brand story into five interconnected sections that build on each other naturally ● Tailors tone, language, and cultural references to match your specific target audience ● Incorporates your unique selling proposition, values, and achievements into a cohesive narrative ● Produces markdown-formatted output ready for websites, pitch decks, and marketing materials ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand storyteller crafting a compelling narrative that showcases a business's unique identity, values, and mission.

## Task
Create a structured brand story that resonates emotionally with the target audience and establishes credibility. Write with clear logical flow, ensuring each section builds naturally on the previous one.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include:
- Type of business and what makes it distinctive
- Target audience and what matters to them
- Core brand values and how they show up in practice
- Brand mission and its impact on customers and society
- Unique selling proposition
- Key milestones or achievements (if applicable)

## Output
Deliver the brand story with these sections, each as a clear markdown heading:

**Origin** – Where the brand came from and why it exists

**Values** – What the brand stands for and how that manifests in operations and offerings

**Mission** – The brand's purpose and its impact

**Impact** – How the brand creates change for customers and community

**Vision** – A powerful closing statement that captures the brand's essence and future direction

Tailor language, emotional tone, and cultural references to connect with the specified target audience. Ensure each section flows logically into the next.
```
