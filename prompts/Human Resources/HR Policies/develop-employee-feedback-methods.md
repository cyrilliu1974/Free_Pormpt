# Employee Feedback Methods for New Policies

## 簡介

The Employee Feedback Methods for New Policies is a free AI prompt that produces five actionable employee feedback collection methods for HR professionals and managers preparing policy changes. This employee feedback prompt for ChatGPT delivers a numbered list of formal and informal feedback channels - anonymous surveys, focus groups, digital suggestion systems, and more - each with specific implementation steps, timing recommendations, anonymity protections, and analysis approaches. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and scales across company sizes, capturing perspectives from all organizational levels to build genuine buy-in before policy rollout. Use it when you need structured feedback mechanisms that balance psychological safety with actionable insights, whether you're launching a remote-work policy, revising benefits, or testing culture initiatives. ● Covers a mix of digital and in-person approaches tailored to different company cultures and sizes. ● Includes timing considerations so feedback collection aligns with decision-making cycles. ● Specifies how each method protects anonymity and fosters psychological safety. ● Provides analysis guidance to turn raw feedback into policy refinements and communication plans. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR strategist specializing in employee engagement and policy implementation.

## Task
Provide five comprehensive, practical methods for gathering meaningful employee feedback before implementing new policies. Each method must include:
- Specific implementation steps
- Timing considerations
- Analysis approach
- How it ensures anonymity and psychological safety

Cover a mix of formal and informal channels, digital and in-person approaches, and techniques that scale across different company sizes and cultures. Focus on methods that capture diverse perspectives from all organizational levels and build authentic employee buy-in.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list with clear headings for each method. Include implementation steps and expected outcomes for maximum actionability.
```
