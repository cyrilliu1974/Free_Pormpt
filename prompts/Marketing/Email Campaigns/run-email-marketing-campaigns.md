# Email Marketing Campaign Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Campaign Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end email marketing strategies for businesses, marketers, and growth teams looking to drive engagement and conversions. This email marketing campaign prompt for ChatGPT walks through five core components: audience segmentation by behavior and demographics, content strategy across welcome, nurture, promotional, and re-engagement emails, subject line formulas with A/B testing guidance, CTA placement and action-oriented design, and performance KPIs including open rate, click-through rate, and conversion tracking. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a structured, step-by-step implementation plan grounded in data-driven best practices and compliance with CAN-SPAM and GDPR regulations. Marketing managers use it to map multi-email sequences, e-commerce teams apply it to launch seasonal promotions, and SaaS founders rely on it to build automated drip campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need a full campaign blueprint - not just a single email - that ties messaging, design, and analytics into one actionable document. ● Segments audiences by engagement level, purchase history, and demographic attributes for personalized messaging ● Maps email types - welcome, nurture, promotional, and re-engagement - with tone and messaging hierarchy for each ● Provides subject line formulas and A/B testing recommendations proven to lift open rates ● Specifies primary and secondary CTA placement, design, and action-oriented copy to maximize conversions ● Defines KPIs and analysis methods so teams can measure success and iterate on performance data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email marketing campaign strategy that drives engagement and conversions. The strategy must include:

- **Audience segmentation**: Define segments within the target audience based on behavior, demographics, or engagement level
- **Content strategy**: Outline email types (welcome, nurture, promotional, re-engagement), tone, and messaging hierarchy
- **Subject line optimization**: Provide formulas and A/B testing recommendations to improve open rates
- **CTA placement and design**: Specify primary and secondary CTAs, their positioning, and action-oriented language
- **Performance metrics**: Identify KPIs (open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate) and analysis methods

## Context
**Target audience**: {{target-audience}}
**Business type**: {{business-type}}
**Campaign goal**: {{campaign-goal}}
**Key offerings and USP**: {{offerings-and-usp}}

Ensure the strategy complies with CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and other applicable email regulations. Ground recommendations in data-driven best practices for the specified business type and audience.

## Output
Deliver the strategy in a structured format with clear headings and subheadings for each component. Present the plan step-by-step so it can be implemented sequentially.
```
