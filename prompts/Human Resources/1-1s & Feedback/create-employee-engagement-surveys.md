# Employee Engagement Survey Question Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Engagement Survey Question Generator is a free AI prompt that creates research-backed survey questions measuring psychological safety and job satisfaction for HR leaders, managers, and organizational development teams. This employee engagement survey prompt for ChatGPT produces five targeted questions, each accompanied by an explanation of the psychological dimension it measures and why that insight matters for leadership action. It works by applying organizational psychology principles to your specific workplace context - whether you manage a fully remote team, a hybrid office, or an on-site operation - and generates questions that capture authentic employee sentiment around speaking up without fear, admitting mistakes, asking for help, and challenging decisions, plus job satisfaction factors like autonomy, purpose, growth opportunities, and work-life integration. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to design an engagement survey, prepare for annual culture assessments, or quickly build a pulse check that yields actionable data across diverse roles and departments. ● Targets core psychological safety indicators: speaking up, admitting mistakes, asking for help, and challenging decisions without fear. ● Addresses modern job satisfaction dimensions including autonomy, purpose, growth opportunities, and work-life integration. ● Adapts to hybrid work environments, diverse communication styles, and employees at different career stages. ● Delivers actionable explanations for each question so leadership understands what to measure and why it matters. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in team dynamics, psychological safety, and employee engagement measurement.

## Task
Create 5 research-backed survey questions that measure psychological safety and job satisfaction in modern workplaces. Questions must capture authentic employee sentiment across hybrid environments, diverse communication styles, and different career stages.

## Context
{{workplace-context}}

Each question should target core psychological safety indicators (speaking up without fear, admitting mistakes, asking for help, challenging decisions) and job satisfaction elements (autonomy, purpose, growth opportunities, work-life integration). Design questions specific enough to yield actionable data yet broad enough to apply across roles and departments.

## Output
Provide a numbered list. For each question:
1. State the survey question clearly
2. Explain which psychological safety or job satisfaction dimension it measures and why it matters for leadership action
```
