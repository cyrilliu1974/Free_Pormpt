# Clinical Product Advertisement Panel Generator

## 簡介

The Clinical Product Advertisement Panel Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 2-panel vertical healthcare advertisements for personal care and wellness brands. This healthcare advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 3:4 portrait layouts that pair clinical studio product photography with authentic daily wellness lifestyle scenes, all rendered in a clean, trust-driven visual style that balances medical credibility with modern consumer appeal. The prompt structures each panel with specific lighting, composition, and mood directives to ensure consistent commercial-grade output across health product categories. Marketing teams at wellness brands, e-commerce managers, and health product startups use it to generate social media ad creatives, product page visuals, and campaign assets without coordinating physical photo shoots. The top panel delivers a hero studio shot with soft lighting and medical-grade clarity, while the bottom panel shows real-world usage in natural settings with confident, healthy expressions. ● Studio hero shots with controlled lighting, sharp focus on product details, and clean medical-grade branding that builds consumer trust. ● Lifestyle usage panels showing authentic daily wellness routines in natural morning light with fresh, confident expressions. ● 3:4 portrait format optimized for Instagram ads, TikTok promotions, e-commerce listings, and vertical social placements. ● Minimalist composition rules that avoid harsh shadows, cluttered backgrounds, and over-processed CGI effects while maintaining high realism. ## Prompt

```
Create a clean, trust-driven 2×1 vertical advertisement combining clinical credibility with modern daily lifestyle appeal.

**Composition**
3:4 portrait aspect ratio, two stacked vertical panels with minimalist healthcare advertising aesthetic.

**Panel 1: Hero Product & Technology**
Studio hero shot of {{product-name}} in its primary resting or charging position. Bright, soft studio lighting with clinical clarity. Clean white or soft gradient background. Sharp focus on functional head detail, handle texture, and logo visibility. Medical-grade styling, premium consumer health branding.

**Panel 2: Daily Wellness Lifestyle**
Adult using {{product-name}} in {{setting}}. Natural morning light, fresh and confident expression. Convey healthy habits, everyday wellness, and self-care routine.

**Visual Style**
Professional commercial healthcare photography with high realism. Soft, diffused, shadow-controlled lighting. Bright, clean, balanced exposure with minimal natural shadows. Modern, minimalist composition emphasizing trust, precision, and freshness.

**Mood**: Clean, healthy, trustworthy, modern, fresh, confident.

**Avoid**: Harsh shadows, dirty or cluttered environments, cheap plastic appearance, unreadable branding, over-processed expressions, CGI or cartoon style, oversaturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
