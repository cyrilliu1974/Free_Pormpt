# Literature Gap Identification Prompt for Research

## 簡介

The Literature Gap Identification Prompt for Research is a free AI prompt that helps educational researchers discover significant gaps in existing academic literature through systematic review and analysis. This literature gap identification prompt for ChatGPT walks through current peer-reviewed journals, conference proceedings, and academic databases to surface what is missing or underexplored in a specific research topic and education subfield. It examines key themes, methodologies, and findings across recent publications, then pinpoints unexplored areas, conflicting results, and methodological limitations. Each identified gap includes an explanation of why it matters and how addressing it could advance educational practice or policy. The prompt adapts to different academic levels and target audiences, making it useful for dissertation planning, grant proposals, systematic reviews, and research agenda development. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Educational researchers, graduate students, and academic teams use this prompt when scoping new projects, validating research questions, or preparing literature review chapters. ● Systematically reviews current academic publications to uncover what has not been studied ● Identifies conflicting results and methodological limitations that warrant further investigation ● Explains the significance of each gap and its potential to advance educational practice or policy ● Customizes analysis based on research topic, education subfield, target audience, academic level, and research purpose ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literature researcher specializing in educational research, skilled at identifying research gaps through systematic analysis of academic publications.

## Task
Identify significant gaps in the existing literature for {{research-topic}} within {{education-subfield}}. Conduct a comprehensive review that examines:

- Recent peer-reviewed journals, conference proceedings, and academic databases
- Key themes, methodologies, and findings in current research
- Unexplored areas, conflicting results, or methodological limitations
- Practical implications for educational practice and policy

## Context
This literature gap analysis is intended for {{target-audience}} at the {{academic-level}} level. The analysis will support {{research-purpose}}.

## Output
Provide your findings as a bullet point list. For each identified gap, explain:

- What is missing or underexplored in the current literature
- Why this gap matters to the field
- How addressing it could advance educational practice or policy
```
