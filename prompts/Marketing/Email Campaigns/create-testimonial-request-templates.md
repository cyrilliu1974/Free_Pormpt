# Testimonial Request Email Templates

## 簡介

The Testimonial Request Email Templates prompt is a free AI prompt that generates two polished, persuasive email templates for businesses seeking customer testimonials and reviews. This testimonial request prompt for ChatGPT produces an initial outreach email and a follow-up reminder, both written in your chosen tone and tailored to your product or service and target audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Each template includes a compelling subject line, a warm opening that acknowledges the customer's experience, a value-proposition reminder, social-proof framing, a clear testimonial request with simple instructions, and a gracious closing. The follow-up message gently nudges without pressure, restating the benefit and expressing appreciation. Use it when you need conversion-ready copy that turns customer satisfaction into visible social proof - whether for e-commerce products, SaaS tools, consulting services, or membership communities. ● Produces both an initial request and a follow-up reminder in a single output, saving drafting time. ● Incorporates social proof and value restatement to motivate customers naturally. ● Adapts tone of voice - formal, friendly, or conversational - to match your brand. ● Structures each template with clearly labeled sections (subject line, salutation, body, sign-off) for immediate use. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email copywriter specializing in customer testimonial and review request campaigns.

## Task
Create two ready-to-use email templates: an initial testimonial request and a follow-up reminder. Both should encourage satisfied customers to share their experience with {{product-or-service}} in a way that resonates with {{target-audience}}.

## Context
The templates will be sent to customers who have purchased or used the offering. Write in {{tone-of-voice}}. Use clear, concise language that highlights value, incorporates social proof, and makes the request feel natural and appreciated—not pushy.

## Output
Provide the templates in this structure, with no additional explanation:

**Subject Line:**  
[Compelling subject that grabs attention]

**Email Body:**  
Salutation: [Warm greeting]  

Opening Paragraph: [Set context and acknowledge their experience]  

Value Proposition: [Remind them of the key benefit they received]  

Social Proof: [Mention how others have benefited or the community impact]  

Testimonial Request: [Clear, persuasive ask with simple instructions]  

Closing Paragraph: [Express appreciation and reinforce value]  

Sign Off: [Professional, warm close]  

---

**Follow-Up Message:**  
Reminder: [Gentle nudge referencing the original request]  

Benefit Restatement: [Briefly recall the value they received]  

Gentle Request: [Restate the ask without pressure]  

Appreciation: [Thank them for their time and consideration]
```
