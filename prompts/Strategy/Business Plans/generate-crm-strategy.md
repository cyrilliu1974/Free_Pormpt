# CRM Strategy Generator for Customer Retention

## 簡介

The CRM Strategy Generator for Customer Retention is a free AI prompt that builds a complete customer relationship management strategy for businesses seeking to improve satisfaction and retention. You describe your current CRM state - including satisfaction metrics, retention rates, customer feedback themes, and key challenges - and the prompt returns a structured, actionable strategy with analysis, objectives, initiatives, tactics, KPIs, a phased implementation plan, and expected outcomes. This CRM strategy prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Marketing teams, customer success managers, and business strategists use it to transform qualitative feedback and scattered metrics into a cohesive, measurable CRM roadmap. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic advice and produce a detailed plan that addresses your specific customer touchpoints, pain points, and business context. ● Analyzes current satisfaction metrics, feedback themes, and relationship challenges to establish a clear baseline ● Defines three measurable CRM objectives aligned with business goals and customer segments ● Proposes three major initiatives, each with three specific tactics and success KPIs ● Structures a three-phase rollout with timelines, resource requirements, and activity descriptions ● Projects concrete outcomes with estimated impact on customer satisfaction and retention rates ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic CRM consultant with deep expertise in customer satisfaction, retention, and loyalty.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive customer relationship management strategy to improve customer satisfaction and retention. Analyze the current state, identify improvement areas, and propose specific initiatives with actionable tactics for every customer touchpoint.

## Context

{{current-crm-state}}

Provide:
- Current customer satisfaction metrics and retention rates
- Key customer feedback themes and pain points
- Main challenges in customer relationships
- Any relevant business context (industry, customer segments, company size)

## Output

Deliver a detailed, actionable CRM strategy structured as follows:

**Current State Analysis:**
- Key metrics summary
- Customer feedback themes
- Key challenges

**CRM Strategy Objectives:**
Define 3 clear, measurable objectives aligned with business goals.

**Initiatives and Tactics:**
For each of 3 major initiatives, provide:
- Description of the initiative
- 3 specific tactics to execute it
- KPIs to measure success

**Implementation Plan:**
Outline a 3-phase rollout, with each phase including:
- Description of activities
- Timeline
- Resources required

**Expected Outcomes:**
Identify 3 concrete outcomes with projected impact on customer satisfaction and retention.

Ensure all recommendations are specific, actionable, and tied to measurable results.
```
