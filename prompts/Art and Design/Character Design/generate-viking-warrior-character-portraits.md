# Viking Warrior Character Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Viking Warrior Character Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces dark, cinematic character images of a late-30s Norse warrior woman in post-conflict scenes for game developers, fantasy authors, and visual storytellers. This Viking warrior portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers a specific composition: a mid-wide, slightly low-angle shot of a fierce, exhausted fighter walking toward the viewer with tangled blonde hair, smudged war paint, and battle-worn leather and fur costume. The prompt specifies authentic details like rune pendants, scraped bracers, a blood-stained axe, and weathered fabrics marked by combat, ash, and frost. Two customizable variables let you control the setting (ruins, longhouse wreckage, snow-covered terrain) and lighting-mood (cold dawn, twilight glow, overcast grey) to match your narrative tone. Use it when you need a single compelling hero shot that conveys mythic survival, loss, and determination without organizing costume design or location shoots. ● Produces realistic weathered costumes including dark brown fur-lined tunic with faded red markings, torn wool trousers, carved rune jewelry, and leather bracers scarred by axe strikes. ● Captures specific emotional storytelling through facial expression (fierce, exhausted, eyes full of loss), body language, and post-battle props like a chipped Viking axe. ● Renders immersive background elements such as collapsed longhouses, smoldering fire pits, abandoned shields, circling ravens, and distant snow-covered mountains. ● Delivers dark cinematic realism with moody tones, subtle grain, crisp weathering details, and 16:9 aspect ratio for game concept art, book covers, and brand storytelling. ## Prompt
