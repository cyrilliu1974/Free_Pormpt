# Local PR Strategy Prompt for Media Outreach

## 簡介

The Local PR Strategy Prompt for Media Outreach is a free AI prompt that builds a structured public relations plan for businesses seeking to increase local visibility through targeted press releases and media coverage. This local PR strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target location's media landscape, identifies three newsworthy story angles with headlines, and provides a complete distribution plan with local SEO optimization tactics. It maps out specific local outlets and beat reporters, recommends strategic timing, identifies geo-targeted backlink opportunities, and establishes measurement protocols for tracking media placements and local search rankings. The output includes structured checklists for local keyword integration, NAP markup, and content amplification through owned channels. Use it when launching in a new market, announcing business milestones, or building relationships with community media. ● Provides local market analysis identifying relevant trends, issues, and media outlets with editorial focus areas ● Develops three press release angles with attention-grabbing headlines tied to current local concerns ● Includes local SEO checklist covering neighborhood-level keywords, landmark references, structured data, and geo-targeted backlinks ● Delivers step-by-step distribution plan with media list building, timing strategy, partnership opportunities, and amplification tactics ● Establishes measurement framework tracking media pickups, local search rankings, geographic traffic, and engagement protocols for ongoing refinement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert public relations strategist specializing in local market penetration and local SEO tactics.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategy for crafting and distributing press releases to local media outlets that will enhance the business's local presence and search visibility.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}
Target location: {{target-location}}

## Output
Provide a structured strategy with the following sections:

**Local Market Analysis**
- Key local issues and trends relevant to this business type
- Local media landscape with specific relevant outlets and their editorial focus

**Press Release Angles**
Three newsworthy angles, each including:
- Description of the story opportunity
- Attention-grabbing headline
- Connection to current local issues or concerns

**Local SEO Optimization**
Checklist covering:
- Targeted local keywords (including neighborhood and city-level terms)
- References to local landmarks, neighborhoods, or institutions
- Structured data markup for NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
- Geo-targeted backlink opportunities
- Locally-relevant images with optimized alt text

**Distribution Plan**
Step-by-step approach including:
- Building a targeted media list (beat reporters, local outlets, community publications)
- Strategic timing recommendations
- Local partnership and co-promotion opportunities
- Amplification through owned channels (website, social media, email)

**Measurement and Follow-up**
Key metrics and actions:
- Track media placements, mentions, and pickup rates
- Monitor local search rankings and organic traffic from target geography
- Engagement protocols for local journalists and influencers
- Content repurposing strategies for social media and blog posts
- Performance-based refinement recommendations

Present all information using clear bullet points, checklists, and numbered lists.
```
