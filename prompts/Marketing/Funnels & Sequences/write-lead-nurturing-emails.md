# Lead Nurturing Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Lead Nurturing Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multi-stage email campaigns designed to build trust and move prospects through the buyer's journey. This lead nurturing email prompt for ChatGPT produces 3-5 complete email templates, each tailored to a specific funnel stage (awareness, consideration, decision). Every email includes an optimized subject line, personalized opening, pain-point-focused body copy, stage-appropriate call-to-action, and mobile-responsive design notes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for your target audience, unique selling proposition, key pain points, and desired outcome. Use it to create email sequences for SaaS onboarding, B2B lead warming, e-commerce cart recovery, or any funnel where prospects need multiple touchpoints before converting. This prompt is ideal for email marketers, growth teams, and founders who need a structured sequence without starting from scratch. ● Creates 3-5 emails mapped to buyer journey stages (awareness, consideration, decision) ● Includes subject lines, preview text, full body copy, and CTAs for each email ● Embeds personalization opportunities and design notes for mobile responsiveness ● Accepts variables for audience, USP, pain points, and desired outcome to tailor every sequence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist specializing in lead nurturing sequences that build trust and guide prospects through the sales funnel.

## Task
Create a complete email nurturing sequence with 3-5 emails addressing different stages of the buyer's journey (awareness, consideration, decision). Each email must include:

- Compelling subject line optimized for open rates
- Personalized opening that acknowledges the recipient's context
- Body content showcasing expertise and addressing pain points
- Clear, stage-appropriate call-to-action
- Mobile-responsive design considerations

Structure each email for clarity and flow. Use concrete examples, relevant insights, and personalization tokens where appropriate.

## Context
**Target audience and industry:** {{audience-and-industry}}

**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

**Key pain points to address:** {{pain-points}}

**Desired outcome:** {{desired-outcome}}

## Output Format
Deliver as a series of email templates in markdown:

**Email [Number]: [Funnel Stage]**
- **Subject Line:** [subject]
- **Preview Text:** [preview]
- **Body:** [full email copy]
- **CTA:** [call-to-action]
- **Notes:** [personalization opportunities, design tips]

Separate each email template with a horizontal rule (---).
```
