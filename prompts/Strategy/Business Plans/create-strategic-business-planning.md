# Strategic Business Planning Framework Prompt

## 簡介

The Strategic Business Planning Framework Prompt is a free AI prompt that develops complete strategic business plans with long-term objectives, internal and external market analysis, and phased implementation roadmaps for organizations across any industry. This strategic business planning prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through a structured planning process that examines your organization's strengths, weaknesses, core competencies, market trends, competitive landscape, and customer needs. It produces an executive-ready strategic plan with 3-5 major initiatives, each accompanied by clear milestones, success metrics, timelines, resource allocation requirements, and stakeholder communication strategies. Business leaders use it to align teams around a shared vision, identify growth opportunities, anticipate market threats, and create accountability frameworks for multi-year objectives. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific organization and market context through a single variable input. This prompt is for executives, business strategists, and management teams who need to translate high-level vision into structured, actionable strategic plans with measurable outcomes. ● Conducts dual-perspective analysis covering internal capabilities and external market forces in one cohesive framework ● Structures 3-5 strategic initiatives with concrete milestones, performance metrics, and realistic timelines ● Includes resource allocation planning and stakeholder communication strategies to ensure organizational alignment ● Provides monitoring and evaluation criteria so leadership can track progress and adjust course as conditions change ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business planning expert specializing in long-term objective setting, market analysis, and actionable roadmap development.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategic planning framework to establish clear, ambitious yet achievable long-term business objectives for the user's organization.

## Context
Organization and market context: {{organization-and-market}}
(Include: organization description, industry sector, target market, current position)

Analyze both internal factors (strengths, weaknesses, core competencies) and external factors (market dynamics, competitive landscape, customer needs). The plan must align the organization toward its envisioned future with structured milestones and metrics. Focus on long-term strategic direction, not short-term tactics.

## Output
Deliver a structured strategic plan using this format:

**Executive Summary**
● Overview of the strategic plan

**Internal Analysis**
● Strengths: [List strengths]
● Weaknesses: [List weaknesses]
● Core Competencies: [List core competencies]

**External Analysis**
● Market Trends: [Describe market trends]
● Customer Needs: [Outline customer needs]
● Competitive Landscape: [Analyze competitive landscape]
● Opportunities: [Identify opportunities]
● Threats: [Identify threats]

**Long-term Objectives**
1. [Objective 1]
2. [Objective 2]
3. [Objective 3]

**Strategic Initiatives**
For each initiative (3-5 total):
● Description: [Describe the initiative]
● Milestones: [List milestones]
● Metrics: [Define metrics]
● Timeline: [Provide timeline]

**Resource Allocation**
● [Outline resource allocation plan]

**Communication Plan**
● [Describe communication plan for stakeholders]

**Monitoring and Evaluation**
● [Explain tracking framework and adjustment process]

**Next Steps**
● [Provide implementation and refinement actions]
```
