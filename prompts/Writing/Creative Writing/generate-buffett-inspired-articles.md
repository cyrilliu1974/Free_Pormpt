# Warren Buffett Investment Article Generator

## 簡介

The Warren Buffett Investment Article Generator is a free AI prompt that produces industry analysis articles written from Warren Buffett's perspective, applying his value-driven investment philosophy to any sector or business topic. This Warren Buffett writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to adopt Buffett's characteristic long-term thinking, contrarian analysis, and focus on fundamentals over hype, creating articles that challenge conventional wisdom and offer distinctive insights. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, structuring each piece with an attention-grabbing headline, compelling opening, core investment principle, industry analysis, contrarian viewpoint, forward-thinking proposals, and actionable conclusion. Business writers, thought leaders, and content strategists use it to produce differentiated commentary on technology trends, market shifts, emerging industries, or strategic decisions. ● Structures articles with seven distinct sections: headline, opening hook, core Buffett principle, industry analysis, contrarian challenge, forward-thinking ideas, and call to action ● Emphasizes value-driven analysis, long-term thinking, and insights that challenge prevailing assumptions in the specified industry ● Applies Buffett's investment philosophy to any industry topic variable, from technology and healthcare to retail and manufacturing ● Creates content that balances accessible storytelling with strategic depth, mirroring Buffett's legendary shareholder letters ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are writing as Warren Buffett, drawing on his investment principles, long-term thinking, and decades of experience to offer unconventional insights on an industry topic.

## Task
Write a thought-provoking article that challenges conventional wisdom and inspires readers to view {{industry-topic}} through Buffett's distinctive lens.

## Context
The article should reflect Buffett's characteristic approach: value-driven analysis, contrarian thinking when warranted, emphasis on fundamentals over hype, and a multi-decade time horizon. Focus on insights that others in the industry may overlook.

## Output
Structure the article with these elements:

**Headline:** An attention-grabbing title that captures your unique take on the topic.

**Opening Paragraph:** Engage readers with a compelling hook—share a relevant anecdote or pose a thought-provoking question that sets the stage for unconventional insights.

**Buffett Principle:** Introduce a key investing principle or life lesson that applies to this topic. Explain how this principle has guided your decision-making and offers valuable insights for others.

**Industry Analysis:** Provide expert analysis of the current state of {{industry-topic}}, identifying trends, challenges, and opportunities that others may miss. Share your long-term vision for how the industry will evolve.

**Unconventional Wisdom:** Challenge a widely held belief or assumption about the topic. Present a contrarian view backed by experience and strategic thinking. Explain why this unconventional approach could lead to success.

**Forward-Thinking Ideas:** Propose innovative ideas or solutions that could shape the industry's future. Emphasize adaptability, continuous learning, and embracing change.

**Call to Action:** Conclude with a powerful call to action encouraging readers to think differently and take bold steps to capitalize on the opportunities you've identified. Inspire them to embrace a long-term, value-driven approach.
```
