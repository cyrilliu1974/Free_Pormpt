# Social Media Content Theme Planner

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Theme Planner is a free AI prompt that builds a week-long content calendar with cohesive daily themes and platform-specific story ideas for social media managers and content creators. This social media content theme planner prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your brand description, audience details, content goals, and target platform, then outputting a markdown table with seven days of themes and three story post concepts for each day. It considers current trends, engagement best practices, and the unique format requirements of platforms like Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook Stories. Content teams use it to maintain consistency while introducing daily variety, agencies use it to pitch clients structured campaigns, and solo creators use it to plan a week's worth of Stories content in one session. Reach for this prompt when you need to map out a content week that balances thematic coherence with fresh daily angles, or when you want story ideas that align with specific business goals and brand voice. ● Produces a markdown table with seven daily themes, each supported by three concrete story post ideas. ● Tailors every theme and idea to your specified brand voice, audience preferences, and platform format. ● Incorporates current engagement trends and platform-specific best practices into each recommendation. ● Delivers actionable, specific story concepts rather than generic content buckets. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in content planning and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive 7-day content theme plan with story post ideas. For each day of the week, create one cohesive daily theme and three engaging story post ideas that support that theme.

## Context
**Brand and Audience:**
{{brand-and-audience}}

**Content Goals:**
{{content-goals}}

**Platform:**
{{platform}}

Ensure themes and story ideas are:
- Cohesive across the week while offering day-to-day variety
- Aligned with the brand voice and audience preferences
- Informed by current trends and engagement best practices
- Actionable and specific to the platform's story format

## Output
Provide your content plan as a markdown table with the following structure:

| Day | Theme | Story Post Ideas |
|-----|-------|------------------|
| Monday | [Theme name] | 1. [Idea]<br>2. [Idea]<br>3. [Idea] |
| Tuesday | [Theme name] | 1. [Idea]<br>2. [Idea]<br>3. [Idea] |

Continue this format for all seven days of the week.
```
