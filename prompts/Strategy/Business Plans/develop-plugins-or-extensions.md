# Plugin and Extension Development Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Plugin and Extension Development Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a complete implementation roadmap for building and launching digital products on established platforms. This plugin development prompt for ChatGPT walks you through the entire lifecycle of creating a plugin or extension, from identifying profitable market gaps to launching on platform marketplaces. It generates structured, phase-by-phase guidance covering ideation strategies (market gap analysis, competitor research, user pain point validation), development best practices (technical requirements, UX design principles, platform compliance, MVP scoping), pricing models (competitive analysis, value-based pricing, freemium vs. premium strategies), and promotional tactics (marketplace SEO, community engagement, launch campaigns). The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts its recommendations to any platform you specify - browser extensions, WordPress plugins, Shopify apps, Figma plugins, or other ecosystems. Designed for solopreneurs building digital products without large teams or budgets, this prompt delivers actionable steps that respect resource constraints while maintaining professional standards. ● Provides phase-specific guidance with main headings, subheadings, bullet points, and milestone checkpoints for tracking progress ● Adapts technical and compliance recommendations to the specific platform you're targeting ● Includes concrete pricing frameworks and promotional channel strategies tailored to marketplace algorithms ● Delivers actionable steps with examples relevant to your business context and platform ecosystem ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital product strategist specializing in plugin and extension development for established platforms.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for solopreneurs to successfully create and market a plugin or extension. Cover the full lifecycle from initial concept through launch and promotion.

## Context
Platform: {{platform}}
Business context: {{business-context}}

The guide should address:
- Ideation: identifying profitable niches through market gap analysis, competitor research, and user pain point validation
- Development: technical requirements, user experience design principles, platform compliance, and MVP scope
- Pricing: competitive analysis, value-based pricing models, freemium vs. premium strategies
- Promotion: marketplace optimization (SEO, screenshots, descriptions), social media tactics, community engagement in forums and groups, launch strategies
- Market trends and platform-specific submission requirements

## Output
Deliver a structured implementation guide using:
- **Main headings** for each major phase (Ideation, Development, Pricing, Promotion)
- **Subheadings** for key activities within each phase
- **Bullet points** for specific action steps
- Concrete examples relevant to the specified platform
- Milestone checkpoints and success metrics

Make every recommendation actionable and tailored to solopreneur constraints.
```
