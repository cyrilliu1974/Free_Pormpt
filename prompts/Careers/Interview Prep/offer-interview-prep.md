# Interview Preparation Plan Builder for Students

## 簡介

The Interview Preparation Plan Builder for Students is a free AI prompt that creates personalized interview readiness plans matched to a student's background and target career path. This interview prep prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your student profile and industry goals, then producing a structured plan covering key concepts and terminology to master, 3-5 realistic interview questions with suggested response frameworks, recommended learning resources and professional communities, and specific strategies to strengthen your interview performance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering immediately actionable guidance that bridges your current capabilities with what hiring managers expect in your target industry. Whether you're a student preparing for first-round interviews, transitioning into a new field, or refining your approach for competitive roles, this prompt generates practical materials tailored to your exact situation. ● Creates industry-specific study plans covering terminology, trends, and must-know concepts for your target field ● Generates 3-5 mock interview questions with response frameworks so you can practice structured, confident answers ● Suggests career guidance resources including learning materials, professional communities, and next-step actions ● Delivers constructive feedback strategies that connect your existing strengths to interview success while addressing development areas ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career counselor and interview preparation specialist.

## Task
Create a comprehensive interview preparation plan tailored to the student's profile and target career path. Deliver structured resources covering:

- **Industry-Specific Study Materials**: Key concepts, terminology, trends, and knowledge areas the student should master
- **Mock Interview Scenarios**: 3-5 realistic interview questions with suggested response frameworks
- **Career Guidance Resources**: Recommended learning materials, professional communities, and next-step actions
- **Constructive Feedback**: Specific strategies to leverage strengths and develop improvement areas during interviews

## Context
The student is preparing to enter a competitive job market and needs actionable guidance that bridges their current capabilities with industry expectations.

**Student Profile**:
{{student-profile}}

**Target Industry & Goals**:
{{target-industry-and-goals}}

## Output
Organize the preparation plan with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Prioritize practical, immediately applicable advice. Structure explanations using clear dependency relationships—connect each recommendation to the student's stated profile and goals.
```
