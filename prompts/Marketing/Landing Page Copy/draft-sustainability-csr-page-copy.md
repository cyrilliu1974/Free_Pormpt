# Sustainability & CSR Landing Page Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Sustainability & CSR Landing Page Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates credible, evidence-based corporate responsibility content for brands that need to communicate environmental and social impact authentically. This sustainability landing page copy prompt for ChatGPT structures content around measurable outcomes, third-party verification, and transparent reporting rather than performative claims. It outputs hero messaging, initiative descriptions organized by theme (environmental, social, sourcing, community), narrative impact stories with before-and-after scenarios, quantifiable data with benchmarks, verification methods, and visual content recommendations. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok for marketing teams, CSR directors, and sustainability communications professionals building trust with skeptical conscious consumers. ● Structures initiatives by theme with specific actions, timelines, and measurable outcomes rather than vague commitments. ● Generates impact stories that include stakeholder testimonials, concrete data points, and before-and-after scenarios. ● Provides transparency mechanisms like third-party certifications, audit reports, and verification methods that address consumer skepticism. ● Suggests visual content types (infographics, data visualizations, interactive dashboards) that enhance credibility through clear presentation of results. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sustainability communications strategist who transforms corporate responsibility initiatives into credible, data-driven brand narratives that build trust with skeptical, conscious consumers.

## Task
Create compelling sustainability and CSR landing page content that positions environmental and social initiatives as genuine competitive differentiators. Prioritize measurable outcomes, transparency, and authentic storytelling over performative claims.

## Context
Modern consumers instantly detect greenwashing. Every sustainability claim requires concrete data, third-party verification, or transparent reporting methods. Address skepticism proactively while showcasing real impact through narrative frameworks that weave together ethical sourcing, community programs, environmental initiatives, and social responsibility.

**Brand & Impact Details:**
{{company-context}}

**Primary Audience:**
{{target-audience}}

## Output
Structure the landing page content with these sections:

**Hero Message** – Opening statement that positions the brand's sustainability commitment authentically

**Key Initiatives** – 3–5 core programs organized by theme (environmental, social, sourcing, community) with specific actions and timelines

**Impact Stories** – Narrative case studies featuring before-and-after scenarios, stakeholder testimonials, and concrete outcomes that demonstrate real change

**Data & Metrics** – Quantifiable results presented clearly (carbon reduction percentages, waste diverted, communities served, certifications earned) with comparison benchmarks where relevant

**Transparency & Verification** – Methods for consumers to verify claims (third-party certifications, audit reports, live dashboards, independent assessments)

**Visual Recommendations** – Specific suggestions for infographics, photography, interactive elements, and data visualizations that enhance credibility

Use bullet points for actionable elements. Provide concrete examples tailored to the industry and audience. Prioritize transparency and specificity over aspirational language.
```
