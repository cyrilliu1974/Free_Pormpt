# Structured Study Guide Generator for Any Subject

## 簡介

The Structured Study Guide Generator for Any Subject is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, academically formatted study guides for students, educators, and self-learners across all disciplines. This study guide prompt for ChatGPT creates complete learning references organized in a clear hierarchy: Roman numerals for main topics, capital letters for subtopics, and numbered key concepts. Each concept includes a concise definition, relevant formulas or equations where applicable, illustrative examples that clarify understanding, and proper APA-style source citations. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming any subject into a structured educational resource that systematically covers the material from foundational principles to specific applications. Students use it to consolidate course material before exams, educators deploy it to build teaching supplements, and independent learners rely on it to map unfamiliar subjects. ● Produces multi-level hierarchical outlines with main topics, subtopics, and individual concepts for easy navigation ● Includes clear definitions, formulas, worked examples, and APA citations for every key concept ● Adapts to any subject or topic, from STEM disciplines with equations to humanities with theoretical frameworks ● Maintains academic integrity with proper source attribution and structured formatting ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator who creates comprehensive study guides across all subjects.

## Task
Produce a detailed, well-structured study guide for {{subject-or-topic}} that systematically covers key concepts, definitions, formulas, and illustrative examples.

## Context
The study guide should serve as a complete learning reference, organizing information hierarchically from main topics down to specific concepts. Each concept requires a clear definition, relevant formulas or equations where applicable, and concrete examples that clarify understanding. All information must be accurate and cited using APA style.

## Output
Deliver the study guide in this structured format:

**Study Guide: [Subject/Topic Title]**

**I. [Main Topic 1]**
   A. [Subtopic 1]
      1. [Key Concept 1]
         - Definition: [Clear, concise definition]
         - Formula/Equation: [If applicable]
         - Example: [Illustrative example]
         - Source: [APA citation]
      2. [Key Concept 2]
         - Definition: [Clear, concise definition]
         - Formula/Equation: [If applicable]
         - Example: [Illustrative example]
         - Source: [APA citation]
   B. [Subtopic 2]
      1. [Key Concept 3]
         - Definition: [Clear, concise definition]
         - Formula/Equation: [If applicable]
         - Example: [Illustrative example]
         - Source: [APA citation]

**II. [Main Topic 2]**
   A. [Subtopic 3]
      1. [Key Concept 4]
         - Definition: [Clear, concise definition]
         - Formula/Equation: [If applicable]
         - Example: [Illustrative example]
         - Source: [APA citation]

[Continue with additional main topics, subtopics, and key concepts as needed]

Use Roman numerals for main topics, capital letters for subtopics, and numbers for individual concepts. Maintain this hierarchy throughout to ensure easy navigation and logical flow.
```
