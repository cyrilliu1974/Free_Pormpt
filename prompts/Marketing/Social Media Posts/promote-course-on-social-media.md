# Social Media Course Promotion Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Course Promotion Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a complete social media marketing plan for educators and marketers promoting online courses. This course promotion prompt for ChatGPT takes your course details, target audience, and three chosen platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, etc.) and outputs a structured marketing strategy tailored to each platform's unique strengths. It produces platform-specific content ideas, realistic posting schedules, engagement tactics, paid advertising recommendations with targeting guidance, and trackable KPIs. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping course creators and digital marketers plan campaigns that respect audience behavior on each platform while maintaining consistent brand messaging. Real use cases include launching a new certification program, re-energizing enrollment for an existing course, or testing which platforms deliver the best ROI for educational content. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn course information into an actionable, multi-platform marketing plan without starting from a blank page. ● Produces 4-5 content format ideas per platform (short-form video, carousels, live Q&A, testimonials) matched to each network's algorithm and user behavior ● Recommends realistic posting frequencies and scheduling guidance based on platform best practices and audience engagement windows ● Identifies paid advertising opportunities with targeting approaches, ad format suggestions, and budget allocation guidance for each platform ● Outputs measurable KPIs (enrollment clicks, video completion rates, cost-per-lead) so you can track campaign performance and optimize spend ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in social media campaigns for online education.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social media marketing plan to promote the course and drive enrollments across three platforms. The plan must showcase the course's value proposition while respecting each platform's unique features and audience behavior.

## Context
**Course & Audience:**
{{course-and-audience}}
(Include: course name, what it teaches, target audience demographics, pain points it solves, and key benefits)

**Platforms:**
{{platforms}}
(Specify the three social media platforms to focus on)

## Requirements
- Tailor content formats and messaging to each platform's strengths (e.g., short-form video, carousels, live sessions, community features)
- Maintain consistent brand voice and core value proposition across all platforms
- Include specific content types and themes that will resonate with the target audience
- Recommend realistic posting frequencies for each platform
- Suggest engagement tactics (responding to comments, community building, user-generated content)
- Identify paid advertising opportunities and targeting approaches where appropriate
- Propose metrics to track campaign effectiveness

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a **bullet-point list organized by platform**. For each platform, include:
- Platform-specific content ideas (at least 4-5 formats/themes)
- Recommended posting schedule
- Engagement and community-building tactics
- Paid advertising recommendations (targeting, ad formats, budget allocation guidance)
- Key performance indicators to monitor
```
