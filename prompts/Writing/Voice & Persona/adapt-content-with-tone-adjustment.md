# Adapt Content with Tone Adjustment Prompt

## 簡介

The Adapt Content with Tone Adjustment Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites existing text to match a target audience and desired tone while keeping the core message intact. This tone adjustment prompt for ChatGPT takes your original content and transforms its vocabulary, sentence structure, and style to resonate with different readers - whether you're adapting a technical white paper for a general audience, shifting a formal report into conversational social media copy, or tailoring marketing materials for different demographics. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure every rewritten sentence remains clear and logically structured, and it incorporates examples and analogies that will be meaningful to your intended readers. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your original content, target audience description, desired tone, and the key message you want to preserve. Reach for it whenever you need to repurpose a single piece of content for multiple channels, audiences, or brand voices without starting from scratch. ● Analyzes original text to extract key points and core message before rewriting ● Adjusts vocabulary, sentence length, and structure to suit beginner, expert, or general audiences ● Applies dependency grammar framework to maintain sentence clarity and logical flow ● Generates well-organized output with headings and paragraphs appropriate to the target tone ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content rewriter who adapts text for specific audiences while preserving core meaning.

## Task
Rewrite the provided content to match the target audience and tone. Analyze the original text to identify its key points, then adjust vocabulary, sentence structure, and style to resonate with the intended readers. Use dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherence—structure sentences so that each element logically connects to others. Include relevant examples or analogies that will be meaningful to the target audience.

## Context
**Original content:** {{original-content}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Desired tone:** {{tone}}

**Key message to preserve:** {{key-message}}

## Output
Present the rewritten content with clear structure, appropriate headings, and well-organized paragraphs that maintain the original message while being engaging and accessible to the intended readers.
```
