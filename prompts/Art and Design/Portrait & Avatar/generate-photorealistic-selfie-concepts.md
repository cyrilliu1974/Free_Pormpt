# Photorealistic Selfie Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Selfie Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates hyper-realistic in-car selfie images with natural smartphone camera aesthetics for content creators and visual designers. This photorealistic selfie prompt for Midjourney produces images of a subject sitting inside a vehicle, captured at eye level with the smartphone visible in frame, styled with shallow depth of field backgrounds and golden hour window lighting. The prompt specifies candid expressions, Instagram-ready composition, and modern smartphone camera characteristics, delivering images that replicate the look of authentic social media selfies. It runs on Midjourney with a 4:5 aspect ratio optimized for mobile displays and portrait feeds. Reach for this prompt when you need realistic self-portrait concepts for social media mockups, character reference images, or visual storytelling that requires believable in-car photography scenarios. ● Specifies in-vehicle setting with visible smartphone for authentic selfie framing and context ● Controls lighting with natural window glow and golden hour warmth for flattering, realistic tones ● Applies shallow depth of field to replicate modern smartphone portrait mode backgrounds ● Outputs 4:5 portrait ratio with Instagram aesthetic and candid expression styling ## Prompt

```

Shot on modern smartphone camera, eye-level close framing, natural window lighting, golden hour glow, candid expression. --ar 4:5 --q 2 --s 50
```
