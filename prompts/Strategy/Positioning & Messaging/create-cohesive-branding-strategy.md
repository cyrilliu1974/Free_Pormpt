# Brand Guidelines Document Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Guidelines Document Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive brand standards documentation for businesses, agencies, and marketing teams building cohesive visual and verbal identities. This brand guidelines prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured, nine-section document that covers brand essence, personality, mission statement, logo usage rules, color palettes with hex codes, typography hierarchy, imagery style, brand voice and tone, and real-world applications across web, social media, print, packaging, and signage. It transforms four simple inputs - brand name, core values, target audience, and unique selling proposition - into a professional standards manual that internal teams and external partners can follow. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering plain-language guidelines accessible to designers and non-designers alike. Use this prompt when launching a new brand, refreshing an existing identity, onboarding agencies, or ensuring consistency across departments and channels. ● Produces nine organized sections from brand essence and personality to logo rules, color palettes, typography, and channel-specific applications ● Includes practical elements like hex codes, font specifications, clearspace requirements, and usage do's and don'ts ● Written in plain language so marketers, partners, and non-design stakeholders can apply standards consistently ● Adaptable structure that scales from startup brands to enterprise rebranding projects ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic branding consultant creating a comprehensive brand guidelines document that ensures cohesive visual identity and consistent messaging across all touchpoints.

## Task
Develop a complete brand guidelines document organized into nine core sections: Brand Essence, Brand Personality, Mission Statement, Logo Guidelines, Color Palette, Typography, Imagery Style, Brand Voice and Tone, and Applications.

## Context
Brand name: {{brand-name}}
Core values: {{core-values}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

## Guidelines
- Make the document thorough yet accessible to both internal teams and external partners
- Ensure all visual and verbal elements can be applied consistently across touchpoints
- Avoid jargon; use plain language for non-designers
- Provide clear examples and templates wherever possible
- Focus on creating a strong, cohesive identity that accurately reflects the brand's essence

## Output
Deliver the guidelines document in this structure:

**Brand Essence:**
[Brand essence statement]

**Brand Personality:**
● [Personality trait 1]
● [Personality trait 2]
● [Personality trait 3]

**Mission Statement:**
[Mission statement]

**Logo Guidelines:**
● Logo variations: [Describe variations]
● Logo clearspace and minimum size: [Specify requirements]
● Logo usage rules: [List rules]

**Color Palette:**
● Primary colors: [List with hex codes]
● Secondary colors: [List with hex codes]
● Usage rules: [Describe application guidelines]

**Typography:**
● Headlines: [Specify font family and weights]
● Body copy: [Specify font family and weights]
● Typographic hierarchy: [Describe hierarchy rules]

**Imagery Style:**
● Photography style: [Describe guidelines]
● Illustration style: [Describe guidelines]
● Iconography style: [Describe guidelines]

**Brand Voice and Tone:**
● Brand voice attributes: [List 3-5 attributes]
● Tone per channel: [Describe variations]
● Messaging do's and don'ts: [List key guidelines]

**Applications:**
● Website: [Provide guidelines]
● Social media: [Provide guidelines]
● Advertising: [Provide guidelines]
● Print collateral: [Provide guidelines]
● Packaging: [Provide guidelines]
● Signage & environments: [Provide guidelines]
```
