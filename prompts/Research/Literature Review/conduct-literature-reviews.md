# Literature Review Generator for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Literature Review Generator for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, citation-based literature reviews for researchers and academic writers. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to conduct a thorough survey of high-impact, peer-reviewed studies from the past five years in any specified field. It structures the output as a formal review article complete with abstract, methodology section documenting search strategy, synthesized key findings with proper citations, emerging trends analysis, future research directions grounded in identified gaps, and a full reference list. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, ensuring the AI moves beyond simple summaries to deliver insightful synthesis and critical analysis of the scholarly landscape. Use it when you need to map the state of research in a topic, prepare a manuscript literature section, or identify unexplored questions in your field. ● Focuses exclusively on peer-reviewed, high-impact studies from the last five years to ensure relevance and rigor ● Documents the literature search methodology, inclusion criteria, and selection process for transparency ● Synthesizes findings across studies to reveal patterns, convergences, and divergences rather than listing isolated results ● Proposes concrete future research directions anchored in gaps identified through systematic analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and academic writer specializing in synthesizing findings from recent studies to produce comprehensive literature reviews.

## Task
Conduct a thorough literature review focusing on the most relevant and impactful research from the past 5 years. Synthesize key findings, identify emerging trends and patterns, and propose future research directions.

## Context
Topic/field: {{topic-or-field}}

## Requirements
- Focus on high-impact, peer-reviewed studies from the last 5 years
- Properly cite all sources using appropriate academic citation format
- Provide insightful analysis and synthesis, not just summaries
- Propose thought-provoking future research directions grounded in identified gaps

## Output
Structure your review article as follows:

**Title:** Review Article: Recent Advances and Future Directions in [topic/field]

**Abstract:**
[Concise summary of scope, methods, key findings, and conclusions]

**Introduction:**
[Background context, significance of the topic, and scope of this review]

**Methods:**
[Literature search strategy: databases searched, keywords used, inclusion/exclusion criteria, number of studies identified and selected]

**Key Findings:**
- [Summary of key study 1 with citation]
- [Summary of key study 2 with citation]
- [Summary of key study 3 with citation]
- [Continue for all major studies identified]

**Emerging Trends:**
[Synthesize patterns, convergences, and divergences across the literature. Identify what the collective body of work reveals beyond individual studies.]

**Future Directions:**
- [Research question or gap 1]
- [Research question or gap 2]
- [Research question or gap 3]
- [Continue as warranted]

**Conclusion:**
[Summarize main contributions of recent research, implications for the field, and priority areas for future investigation]

**References:**
[Complete list of cited sources in appropriate format]
```
