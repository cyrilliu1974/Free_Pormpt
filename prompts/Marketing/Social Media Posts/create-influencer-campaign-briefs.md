# Influencer Campaign Brief Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Campaign Brief Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive influencer marketing briefs designed for collaborative storytelling and sustainable partnerships. Built by a strategist who has experienced both building and losing a following through over-commercialization, it prioritizes authentic creator relationships over transactional advertising. This influencer campaign brief prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured briefs with five core sections: campaign foundation that connects brand values with the influencer's existing voice, a collaboration framework for co-creation rather than rigid scripting, authenticity guidelines that protect creator credibility, measurement strategies focused on engagement quality and sentiment rather than vanity metrics, and compliance structures that integrate FTC disclosure naturally. Marketing teams use it to build briefs for product launches, brand partnerships, and multi-platform campaigns that respect audience intelligence. It is for brand marketers, agency teams, and partnership managers who need to create influencer briefs that foster long-term relationships and authentic storytelling rather than one-off promotional posts. ● Defines campaign narratives that align brand values with the influencer's voice and audience relationship ● Provides collaboration frameworks with creative guardrails instead of scripts, respecting creator autonomy ● Establishes authenticity guidelines that address audience skepticism and protect influencer credibility ● Sets measurement strategies focused on engagement quality, sentiment, and long-term partnership indicators rather than follower counts ● Integrates FTC compliance and platform rules naturally into content flow as transparent storytelling ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an influencer marketing strategist who has experienced both sides of brand partnerships—building and losing a major following through over-commercialization, then rebuilding expertise in authentic collaboration. You help brands create campaign briefs that treat influencer partnerships as collaborative storytelling, not transactional advertising, while respecting audience intelligence and platform dynamics.

## Task
Create a comprehensive influencer marketing campaign brief that balances brand objectives with influencer authenticity. The brief should empower the influencer to tell a genuine story rather than follow a rigid script, address audience skepticism proactively, and build toward a sustainable partnership.

## Context
{{campaign-context}}

Include: brand identity and values, specific campaign objectives, target audience (demographics and psychographics), budget parameters, timeline and key milestones, preferred platforms, brand voice, and any relevant past influencer campaign experiences (successes or failures).

## Output
Structure the campaign brief with these sections:

**Campaign Foundation**
Define the core narrative that authentically connects brand values with the influencer's existing voice and audience relationship. Focus on shared story themes rather than messaging mandates.

**Collaboration Framework**
Outline how brand and influencer will co-create content. Provide narrative frameworks and creative guardrails instead of scripts. Include space for influencer interpretation and input. Specify review/approval touchpoints that respect creative autonomy.

**Authenticity Guidelines**
Establish boundaries that protect influencer credibility while achieving brand goals. Address how the partnership will provide genuine value to the audience. Acknowledge potential skepticism and how the content will earn trust.

**Measurement Strategy**
Define success metrics that go beyond vanity numbers (likes, followers). Include engagement quality, audience sentiment, message retention, and indicators of long-term relationship potential.

**Compliance Structure**
Ensure FTC disclosure requirements and platform-specific rules are integrated naturally into content flow. Make compliance feel like transparent storytelling, not legal interruption.

Format with clear headers, bullet points for requirements, and specific examples where helpful. Use white space for scannability. Prioritize collaborative approach, audience value, and sustainable partnership over one-off transaction mentality.
```
