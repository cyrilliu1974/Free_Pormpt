# Employee Offboarding Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Employee Offboarding Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds seven comprehensive exit strategies designed to preserve relationships and capture institutional knowledge during employee departures. This offboarding prompt for ChatGPT produces actionable frameworks that address the emotional, practical, and relational dimensions of employee exits. Each strategy includes psychological rationale, step-by-step implementation guidance, and measurable KPIs that demonstrate value for both the departing employee and the organization. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming what is often a rushed, transactional process into an opportunity for knowledge transfer, brand advocacy, and future collaboration. HR teams use it to design exit experiences that maintain team morale, protect organizational reputation, and convert departing employees into long-term advocates. Reach for this prompt when you need to redesign offboarding workflows, capture critical knowledge before employees leave, or build a people-first exit culture that treats departures as new beginnings rather than endings. ● Addresses emotional closure, recognition needs, and future security for departing employees while protecting team morale and organizational reputation ● Provides specific knowledge transfer processes that retain institutional insights and prevent critical information loss ● Includes measurable KPIs for each strategy so you can track relationship preservation, alumni engagement, and brand advocacy outcomes ● Covers seven distinct approaches ranging from structured exit interviews to alumni network development and post-departure touchpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources strategist and organizational psychologist specializing in employee lifecycle management and offboarding excellence.

## Task
Develop seven comprehensive strategies that transform the offboarding process into a positive, relationship-preserving experience. Each strategy must include:
- **Rationale**: Why the approach works psychologically and organizationally
- **Implementation steps**: Specific, actionable tactics
- **Measurable outcomes**: KPIs that demonstrate value for both the departing employee and the organization

## Context
Most offboarding processes are rushed, impersonal, and damage long-term relationships. They miss critical opportunities for knowledge transfer, brand advocacy, and future collaboration. Address:
- Emotional needs: closure, recognition, and future security for the departing employee
- Practical concerns: knowledge retention, team morale, and reputation management for the organization
- Relationship maintenance: turning potential adversaries into advocates and transforming endings into new beginnings

**Organization and offboarding context:**
{{organization-context}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with seven distinct strategies. For each strategy:
1. **Clear title** summarizing the approach
2. **Detailed explanation** of rationale and psychological/practical impact
3. **Specific action steps** for implementation
4. **Measurable outcomes** with concrete KPIs
```
