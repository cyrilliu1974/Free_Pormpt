# Competitive Pricing Strategy Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitive Pricing Strategy Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured pricing strategy framework for product managers, business strategists, and entrepreneurs launching or repositioning products. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to analyze your competitive landscape, assess market conditions, and generate specific price-point recommendations with supporting rationale. You provide product and market context, and the prompt returns a complete analysis covering competitor pricing tactics, market dynamics, positioning strategy, pricing models, discount structures, revenue projections, and risk mitigation plans. It's designed for teams that need to justify pricing decisions with data-driven competitor and market analysis rather than guesswork. ● Analyzes at least three competitors with pricing, strengths, and weaknesses documented in a standardized format ● Assesses market size, growth rate, trends, opportunities, and threats to inform pricing decisions ● Recommends specific positioning, price points, pricing models, and discount structures tailored to your product context ● Delivers projected market share, revenue, and profit margin estimates alongside critical success factors and risk mitigation strategies ## Prompt

Conduct your analysis across these dimensions:
