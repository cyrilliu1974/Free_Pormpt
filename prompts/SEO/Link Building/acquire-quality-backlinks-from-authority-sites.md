# Link Building Outreach Strategy for Authority Backlinks

## 簡介

The Link Building Outreach Strategy for Authority Backlinks is a free AI prompt that creates a complete outreach plan for acquiring high-quality backlinks from authoritative websites in your industry. This link building prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered step-by-step strategy covering how to identify relevant authority sites, craft personalized pitches, and establish mutually beneficial link partnerships. It generates 2-3 ready-to-use outreach email templates with optimized subject lines, personalized opening hooks, clear value propositions, and professional calls-to-action. The prompt accepts your industry, website URL, target audience, unique selling proposition, and existing content assets to deliver a tailored outreach roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is built for SEO specialists, digital marketers, and website owners who need a structured approach to earning backlinks that improve search rankings and drive organic traffic. ● Produces a complete numbered outreach strategy from prospect identification through relationship maintenance ● Generates 2-3 sample email templates with proven components for higher response rates ● Tailors recommendations to your specific industry, audience, and available content assets ● Focuses on establishing genuine partnerships rather than transactional link requests ## Prompt

```

## Task
Develop a comprehensive link building outreach strategy. Create a step-by-step plan that covers:
- Identifying authoritative websites in the industry
- Crafting compelling, personalized pitches
- Establishing mutually beneficial relationships with potential link partners
- Sample outreach email templates optimized for response rates

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Website: {{website-url}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Content assets available for link building: {{content-assets}}

## Output
Provide your strategy as a numbered list, with each step of the link building process clearly detailed. Include 2-3 sample outreach email templates that demonstrate:
- Strong subject lines
- Personalized opening hooks
- Clear value propositions
- Specific collaboration opportunities
- Professional calls-to-action
```
