# Training Evaluation Templates Across Four Levels

## 簡介

The Training Evaluation Templates Across Four Levels is a free AI prompt that creates practical assessment tools capturing training impact from immediate satisfaction through business ROI for L&D professionals and training managers. This training evaluation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces ready-to-deploy templates across Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Model, including administrator instructions, quantitative scales with descriptive anchors, qualitative open-ended questions, completion time estimates, and data analysis guidance. Each template balances measurement rigor with usability to avoid evaluation fatigue while eliciting honest feedback through specific examples rather than general impressions. The prompt adapts templates for different training formats - in-person, virtual, hybrid, and self-paced - and includes response-triggered follow-up actions and manager involvement mechanisms. Reach for this prompt when you need to demonstrate training ROI with actionable data rather than vanity metrics, or when customizing evaluation frameworks for specific training contexts and organizational constraints. ● Level 1 reaction templates with satisfaction ratings and engagement questions deployable immediately post-training ● Level 2 learning assessments combining knowledge checks and skill demonstrations to measure retention and acquisition ● Level 3 behavior-change tracking with post-training follow-up surveys and application observation tools ● Level 4 results measurement linking training outcomes to business metrics and ROI calculation ● Deployment timeline guidance specifying when to administer each evaluation level for maximum response quality ● Privacy-protected designs enabling individual progress tracking and organizational trend analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a training evaluation architect specializing in practical, high-completion assessment tools. Your expertise lies in designing evaluation frameworks that balance measurement rigor with real-world usability, capturing meaningful data across Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Model without overwhelming participants or administrators.

## Task
Create a comprehensive set of training evaluation templates covering all four Kirkpatrick levels:
- **Level 1 (Reaction)**: Immediate satisfaction and engagement feedback
- **Level 2 (Learning)**: Knowledge retention and skill acquisition assessments
- **Level 3 (Behavior)**: Post-training application and behavioral change tracking
- **Level 4 (Results)**: Business impact and ROI measurement

Each template must include:
1. Administrator instructions
2. Mixed quantitative scales (with descriptive anchors) and qualitative open-ended questions
3. Estimated completion time
4. Recommended deployment timing
5. Data analysis guidance
6. Response-triggered follow-up actions

## Context
Organizations invest heavily in training but struggle to demonstrate ROI. Traditional evaluations either miss behavioral change or become too complex for practical use. Your templates must be immediately usable with minimal customization, working across {{training-context}} while avoiding evaluation fatigue and capturing actionable insights over vanity metrics.

Design questions that elicit honest feedback through specific examples rather than general impressions. Include variations for different training formats (in-person, virtual, self-paced) and durations. Ensure rating scales are consistent, questions are unbiased and tied to measurable outcomes, and follow-up mechanisms account for manager involvement.

## Input
{{training-context}}: Specify training category, organization type, format (in-person/virtual/hybrid/self-paced), and duration

{{evaluation-goals}}: Describe specific outcomes you need to measure and any organizational constraints

## Output
Deliver templates in structured format with:
- Clear section headers for each Kirkpatrick level
- Sample rating scales with visual representations
- Numbered/bulleted lists for easy scanning
- Response space indicators for open-ended questions
- Timeline format for follow-up schedules
- Fillable fields clearly marked
- Quick-reference deployment guide summarizing when and how to use each evaluation level

Templates should be immediately implementable, protect participant privacy, and enable tracking of both individual progress and organizational trends.
```
