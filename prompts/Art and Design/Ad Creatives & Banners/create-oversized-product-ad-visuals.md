# Oversized Product Ad Visual Generator

## 簡介

The Oversized Product Ad Visual Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-end beauty editorial photographs featuring a model leaning casually on a giant, oversized version of your product in a minimal luxury studio. This oversized product ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana builds scroll-stopping campaign visuals where the product is scaled to tower beside the model - shoulder-height or taller - while maintaining realistic retail form, legible branding, and photorealistic material reflections. Designers use it to produce hero images for beauty, skincare, and cosmetics campaigns that emphasize product presence through unexpected scale. Ideal for creative directors, brand marketers, and social media teams building standout ad creatives without a physical shoot. ● Scales your product to furniture-like proportions - towering beside a fashion model while keeping cap, label, and brand text clearly legible and realistically rendered ● Generates a confident editorial model pose: slim athletic build, dark ponytail, natural skin tone, elbow and forearm resting on the product, fingers near lips ● Accepts three variables - product brand, product description, and wardrobe style - so you can match outfit aesthetics to campaign tone ● Delivers soft studio lighting with specular highlights on product surfaces, tack-sharp 50mm framing, and visible skin texture for luxury advertising quality ## Prompt

```
High-end beauty editorial photograph: a confident female model in her early 20s leaning casually against a massively oversized {{product-brand}} {{product-description}}, treating the giant beauty product like furniture. The product towers beside her—its height matching or exceeding her shoulder level—closed with cap attached in realistic retail form, brand text clearly legible and correctly proportioned. Single product only, no stacks or abstractions.

Model: slim athletic build, light natural skin tone, dark brown hair in high ponytail with loose front strands framing her face. Soft jawline, refined nose, expressive eyes with confident editorial gaze. Upper body relaxed, elbow and forearm resting naturally on the oversized product, fingers near lips, thoughtful pose. Wearing {{wardrobe-style}} outfit—coordinated top, bottom, socks, and shoes all matching the style.

Minimal luxury photo studio: seamless neutral backdrop and floor, clean beauty campaign aesthetic. Soft editorial studio lighting with subtle highlights on the model's face and strong specular shine across the product surface, controlled soft shadows.

Medium-close editorial framing, 50mm lens: model's face and upper body dominant in composition while the product's massive scale remains unmistakable. Tack-sharp focus on face and product edges. Photorealistic render with visible skin texture, fabric folds, and cosmetic plastic reflections. Luxury advertising quality.
```
