# Research Review Process Builder for Education

## 簡介

The Research Review Process Builder for Education is a free AI prompt that creates structured, multi-stage evaluation frameworks for research programs in educational institutions. This research review process prompt for ChatGPT guides you through designing a complete evaluation system that analyzes current research strengths and gaps, assigns stakeholder roles at each stage, defines measurable success criteria, and anticipates implementation challenges. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a markdown table framework that organizes review stages, participant mapping, timeline milestones, and expected outcomes, followed by a risk mitigation section. Use it when you need to formalize research evaluation, align diverse academic stakeholders around improvement goals, or build sustainable review processes that balance thoroughness with institutional timeline constraints. ● Analyzes current research state to identify strengths, gaps, and improvement opportunities before designing the review process ● Maps which stakeholders participate at each stage, ensuring faculty, administrators, and other groups are involved where appropriate ● Defines measurable outcomes and success criteria for every review phase, making progress trackable ● Identifies resource constraints, resistance to change, and coordination risks with concrete mitigation strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in research program evaluation and continuous improvement frameworks.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, multi-stage review process for a research area within an educational institution. The framework should analyze the current state, incorporate diverse stakeholder input, and provide a clear implementation roadmap.

## Context
**Institution & Research Area:** {{institution-and-research-area}}

**Stakeholders & Goals:** {{stakeholders-and-objectives}}

**Timeline Constraints:** {{timeline-constraints}}

## Process
1. Analyze the current state of the research area, identifying strengths, gaps, and improvement opportunities
2. Design a multi-stage review process with clear objectives for each phase
3. Map stakeholder involvement to each stage
4. Define measurable outcomes and success criteria
5. Identify potential challenges (resource constraints, resistance to change, coordination issues) and mitigation strategies
6. Provide implementation guidance and sustainability recommendations

## Output
Deliver your framework as a **markdown table** with these columns:
- **Review Stage** (name and brief description)
- **Stakeholders** (who participates)
- **Timeline** (duration or milestones)
- **Expected Outcomes** (measurable results)

Follow the table with a brief section on implementation risks and mitigation strategies.
```
