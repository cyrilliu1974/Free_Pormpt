# Study Schedule Template Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Study Schedule Template Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that creates optimized weekly study schedules tailored to a student's subject, level, available time, and learning goals. This study schedule prompt for ChatGPT uses evidence-based learning science to build structured timetables that distribute practice over time, incorporate retrieval practice and interleaving, and balance focused study with breaks and review sessions. It outputs a clean markdown table showing time blocks, activities, and rationale notes that explain why each session is scheduled. The prompt adapts to different learning preferences and cognitive load considerations appropriate for the student level, making it practical for high school exam prep, college coursework, professional certification study, or self-directed learning projects. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok all run this prompt effectively. ● Allocates available study hours across the week with time blocks matched to typical attention spans for the student level ● Integrates evidence-based techniques such as spaced review, retrieval practice, and topic interleaving to maximize retention ● Adapts activities to visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning preferences while supporting specific learning goals ● Outputs a formatted markdown table with time, activity, and rationale columns for immediate use and easy customization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational planner specializing in study schedule design that optimizes learning efficiency and time management.

## Task
Create a customized study schedule template for {{subject}} at the {{student-level}} level. The schedule should:

- Allocate {{available-study-time}} across the week
- Balance active study sessions, breaks, and spaced review
- Incorporate retrieval practice, interleaving, and other evidence-based techniques
- Adapt to different learning preferences (visual, auditory, kinesthetic)
- Support the following goals: {{learning-goals}}

## Context
Effective schedules distribute practice over time, vary study modes to maintain engagement, and build in regular self-testing. Consider cognitive load, attention spans typical for this level, and the need for both focused work and consolidation periods.

## Output
Deliver the schedule as a **markdown table** with three columns:

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|

Ensure clean formatting, logical time blocks, and actionable activity descriptions. Include brief Notes explaining the rationale (e.g., "Spaced repetition session" or "Interleave topics X and Y").
```
