# Competitive SEO Analysis Prompt for Any Industry

## 簡介

The Competitive SEO Analysis Prompt for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that produces detailed competitor intelligence reports for SEO professionals and marketing teams in any sector. This competitive SEO analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines the top three competitors in your specified industry, breaking down their keyword strategies, backlink profiles, content marketing approaches, and overall digital presence. It delivers a structured report with clearly marked strengths and weaknesses for each competitor, a comparative chart for side-by-side benchmarking, and actionable recommendations tailored to your market. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Use cases include market entry research, quarterly competitive audits, keyword gap analysis, and content strategy planning. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand how competitors rank, what keywords they target, and where opportunities exist in your industry's search landscape. ● Identifies the top five keywords for each of three competitors, with analysis of how they deploy those terms ● Evaluates backlink profiles and content marketing tactics to surface what drives competitor visibility ● Outputs a comparative chart that lets you benchmark all three competitors at a glance ● Provides key takeaways and concrete recommendations you can act on immediately ## Prompt
