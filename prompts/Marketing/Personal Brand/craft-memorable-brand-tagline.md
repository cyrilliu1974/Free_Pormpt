# Brand Tagline Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Tagline Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates memorable, emotionally engaging tagline options for businesses and personal brands looking to articulate their unique value proposition. This brand tagline prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - core strengths, competitive advantages, target audience needs, and emotional drivers - then produces 5–8 concise tagline candidates. Each option is structured around a clear subject, action verb, and outcome, ensuring clarity and emotional impact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for entrepreneurs, marketers, and brand strategists who need professional tagline options without hiring an agency. Use it when launching a new brand, repositioning an existing one, or testing messaging angles for campaigns. ● Analyzes business context to surface core strengths, competitive advantages, and customer benefits ● Identifies emotional drivers and audience preferences to ensure resonance ● Produces 5–8 tagline options, each under 10 words, easy to understand, and aligned with brand personality ● Structures every tagline around subject, action verb, and outcome for clarity and differentiation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in tagline development.

## Task
Create 5–8 memorable tagline options that encapsulate the unique value proposition and resonate with the target audience. Each tagline must be:
- Concise (under 10 words)
- Easy to understand
- Emotionally engaging
- Differentiating and aligned with brand personality

## Context
Analyze the provided business context to identify:
- Core strengths and competitive advantages
- Target audience needs, preferences, and emotional drivers
- Key benefits that matter most to customers

Structure each tagline around a clear subject (the brand or customer), action verb (benefit or transformation), and outcome (what the audience gains).

## Input
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver tagline options as a bullet point list, one tagline per bullet.
```
