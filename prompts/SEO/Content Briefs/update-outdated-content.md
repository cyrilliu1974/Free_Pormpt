# Content Update and SEO Optimization Audit Prompt

## 簡介

The Content Update and SEO Optimization Audit Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured content audit and prioritized action plan to revitalize outdated website pages for content strategists, SEO specialists, and digital marketers. This content optimization prompt for ChatGPT walks through a four-stage process: conducting a content audit to surface outdated information, broken links, and weak meta elements; performing keyword analysis to flag gaps and cannibalization; building a prioritized optimization plan with specific header, meta, and multimedia recommendations for each page; and establishing success metrics to track organic traffic, bounce rate, and conversions post-update. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing an executive summary, audit findings table, ranked action items, and an implementation roadmap aligned with modern SEO best practices and user intent. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically identify which pages require updates, what changes will yield the highest SEO and engagement impact, and how to sequence the work for maximum efficiency. ● Identifies outdated pages, thin content, broken links, and suboptimal keyword integration through a structured audit framework ● Maps primary and semantic keywords to page intent, flagging cannibalization and content gaps ● Delivers ranked recommendations specifying content rewrites, header hierarchy fixes, meta improvements, internal linking opportunities, and multimedia additions ● Includes an implementation roadmap and KPI tracker (organic traffic, bounce rate, time on page, conversions) to measure post-update success ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist specializing in website optimization, SEO, and audience engagement.

## Task

Revise and optimize existing website content to improve engagement metrics and search engine rankings. Conduct a content audit, then deliver a prioritized action plan with clear, actionable recommendations.

## Context

**Website:** {{website-url}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Primary keywords:** {{primary-keywords}}

Current content may contain outdated information, broken links, suboptimal keyword integration, or weak meta elements. The update plan should align with modern SEO best practices and user intent.

## Process

1. **Content Audit**: Identify pages with outdated information, broken links, thin content, missing or poor meta descriptions, weak headers, and low engagement signals.
2. **Keyword Analysis**: Map primary and semantic keywords to page intent; flag keyword cannibalization or gaps.
3. **Optimization Plan**: Prioritize pages by traffic potential and business impact. For each, specify:
   - Content additions, deletions, or rewrites needed
   - Header structure improvements (H1, H2, H3 hierarchy)
   - Meta title and description recommendations
   - Internal linking opportunities
   - Multimedia or interactive elements to add (images, videos, infographics, calculators, FAQs)
4. **Readability**: Ensure clear, concise sentences with logical flow and scannable formatting.

## Output

Deliver a structured content update plan with:

- **Executive Summary**: High-level findings and impact forecast
- **Audit Findings**: Table or bullet list of issues by page
- **Priority Recommendations**: Ranked action items with expected SEO and engagement benefits
- **Implementation Roadmap**: Sequenced tasks aligned with available update capacity
- **Success Metrics**: KPIs to track post-update (organic traffic, bounce rate, time on page, conversions)

Use clear headings, bullet points, and tables for easy reference.
```
