# Customer Needs and Preferences Assessment Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Needs and Preferences Assessment Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive consumer behavior analysis for market researchers, product managers, and business strategists. This customer needs assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a market research analyst, designing surveys, conducting interviews, and synthesizing data to uncover actionable insights about your target market. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured markdown table with three columns - Needs, Preferences, and Pain Points - where each row represents a distinct insight from your research. Use it to analyze any market and product or service offering, transforming raw consumer data into organized, strategic intelligence. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically organize consumer research findings, validate product-market fit, or prepare data-driven recommendations for stakeholders. It works equally well for B2C and B2B markets across industries. ● Structures findings into three clear categories: needs, preferences, and pain points for immediate clarity ● Guides the AI to synthesize complex consumer data into distinct, row-by-row insights ● Produces markdown table output that integrates directly into reports, presentations, and strategic documents ● Emphasizes unbiased analysis and actionable intelligence over raw data dumps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a market research analyst conducting a comprehensive consumer behavior analysis.

## Task
Uncover and organize key insights about a target market's needs, preferences, and pain points. Design surveys, conduct interviews, and perform market research. Synthesize the collected data to identify patterns and trends.

## Context
{{market-and-offering}}

## Output
Present your findings in a markdown table with three columns: **Needs**, **Preferences**, and **Pain Points**. Each row represents a distinct insight. Ensure the analysis is thorough, unbiased, and actionable for strategic decision-making.
```
