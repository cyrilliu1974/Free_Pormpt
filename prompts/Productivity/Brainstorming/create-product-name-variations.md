# Product Name Generator Prompt for Brand Naming

## 簡介

The Product Name Generator Prompt for Brand Naming is a free AI prompt that produces 70 unique product name variations across seven professional naming strategies for entrepreneurs, marketers, and brand consultants. This product naming prompt for ChatGPT delivers ten variations in each of seven naming styles: descriptive words (Band Aid), real words (Whole Foods), made-up words (Doritos), blended portmanteaus (Pinterest), creative misspellings (Kleenex), non-English roots (Audi), and acronyms (IBM). The prompt considers SEO performance, trademark availability, ease of pronunciation, and competitive landscape, returning results in a structured table with naming style, variation, and rationale columns. Runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Ideal for launching new products, rebranding initiatives, or generating creative naming options when existing ideas feel flat or overused. ● Produces 70 variations across 7 proven naming frameworks used by professional brand agencies ● Prioritizes SEO-friendly keywords, easy pronunciation, and domain registration viability ● Includes rationale for each name to help you understand the strategic positioning ● Filters out names with major competitive conflicts to save trademark research time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer specializing in product naming with expertise in brand memorability and SEO optimization.

## Task
Generate 70 product name variations across 7 naming styles (10 variations per style) for the product described below.

## Naming Styles
1. **Descriptive words** – Clear function indicators (e.g., Band Aid, Post-Its)
2. **Real words** – Dictionary terms combined or standalone (e.g., Whole Foods, Microsoft Word)
3. **Made-up words** – Original coined terms (e.g., Doritos, Frisbee)
4. **Blended words** – Portmanteaus merging two concepts (e.g., Pinterest, McNuggets)
5. **Misspelled words** – Phonetic or creative spelling variants (e.g., Kleenex, HedBanz)
6. **Non-English names** – International language roots (e.g., Audi, Nissan)
7. **Acronyms** – Initials or abbreviations (e.g., IBM, MTV)

## Criteria
- Prioritize catchy, memorable names that are easy to pronounce and spell
- Favor keywords that are simple to type for better SEO performance
- Avoid names with existing large competitors in the same space
- Each name should be suitable for trademark and domain registration

## Product Information
{{product-description}}

## Output Format
Return a table with columns: **Naming Style** | **Name Variation** | **Rationale**
```
