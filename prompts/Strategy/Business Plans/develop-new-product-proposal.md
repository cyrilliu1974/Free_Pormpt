# New Product Launch Research Proposal Prompt

## 簡介

The New Product Launch Research Proposal Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional, data-driven proposals for bringing new products to market. Built for product managers, entrepreneurs, and business strategists, this prompt produces a complete research document with executive summary, market analysis, competitive landscape, go-to-market strategy, financial projections, risk assessment, and implementation timeline. This product launch prompt for ChatGPT runs on all major text AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming two simple inputs - your product description and target market - into a multi-section proposal grounded in evidence and formatted with APA citations. Reach for this prompt when you need to build a compelling case for stakeholders, investors, or internal teams before committing resources to a product launch. ● Delivers a complete proposal structure with executive summary, market sizing, customer segmentation (demographics, psychographics, buying behaviors), and competitor analysis ● Generates financial projections including revenue forecasts, cost structure, break-even analysis, and funding requirements tailored to your product and market ● Identifies market, competitive, and operational risks with concrete mitigation strategies ● Outputs a phased timeline covering product development, testing, launch milestones, and post-launch initiatives with measurable KPIs ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert researcher composing a comprehensive, evidence-based research proposal for a new product launch.

## Task

Develop a detailed research proposal that outlines the strategy, market fit, and execution plan for successfully launching the product. Provide data-driven insights, actionable recommendations, and cite all sources in APA format.

## Context

**Product and Market:**
{{product-description}} (include key features, benefits, and unique value proposition)

{{target-market}} (specify industry, geography, and market segment)

## Output

Structure the proposal as a professional document with these sections:

**Executive Summary**

**1. Product Overview**
- Product description
- Key features and benefits
- Unique value proposition

**2. Market Analysis**
- Market size and growth trends
- Target customer segments
  • Demographics
  • Psychographics
  • Buying behaviors
- Competitor landscape
  • Direct competitors
  • Indirect competitors
  • Competitive advantages and disadvantages
- Regulatory considerations

**3. Go-to-Market Strategy**
- Pricing strategy
- Distribution channels
- Promotional plan (advertising, public relations, digital marketing)
- Sales enablement
- Success metrics and KPIs

**4. Financial Projections**
- Revenue forecast
- Cost structure
- Break-even analysis
- Funding requirements

**5. Risk Assessment**
- Market risks
- Competitive risks
- Operational risks
- Mitigation strategies

**6. Timeline and Milestones**
- Product development
- Testing and validation
- Launch phases
- Post-launch initiatives

**Conclusion**

**References** (APA format)

Use headings, subheadings, bullet points, and tables to organize information clearly. Ground all findings and recommendations in evidence; avoid unsupported claims.
```
