# Product-Market Fit Validation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Product-Market Fit Validation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured, phase-by-phase validation plan for solopreneurs launching new products or testing market assumptions. This product-market fit prompt for ChatGPT helps founders systematically move from customer discovery through problem validation, solution testing, willingness-to-pay experiments, MVP feedback loops, and pivot-or-persevere decision points. You provide your product concept, target market, and resource constraints (budget, time, team size), and the prompt returns a markdown table breaking down each validation step with clear execution instructions and the specific tools, frameworks, or templates needed. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting the strategy to your real-world limitations instead of prescribing resource-heavy methods unsuitable for solo founders. ● Outputs a three-column table (Steps, Description, Resources) that walks through customer discovery, problem validation, solution validation, pricing tests, and MVP feedback in order ● Tailors validation methods to your stated resource constraints so every step is actually executable by a solopreneur ● Identifies the tools, templates, and frameworks needed at each phase, from interview scripts to survey platforms ● Includes criteria for recognizing true product-market fit and signals that a pivot is required ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product strategist specializing in lean validation and product-market fit assessment for early-stage ventures.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step validation strategy tailored to a solopreneur's context. The strategy should cover customer research, hypothesis testing, prototype validation, and iterative improvement cycles. Each step must be actionable and appropriate for the available resources and timeline.

## Context
{{product-and-market}}

{{resource-constraints}}

## Output
Present the strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Steps | Description | Resources |
|-------|-------------|----------|

Each row should contain:
- **Steps**: Sequential validation phase (numbered and named)
- **Description**: Clear explanation of what to do, why it matters, and how to execute it
- **Resources**: Specific tools, templates, frameworks, or methods needed to complete the step

Cover the complete validation journey: initial customer discovery, problem validation, solution validation, willingness-to-pay testing, MVP feedback loops, and criteria for determining product-market fit or pivot signals.
```
