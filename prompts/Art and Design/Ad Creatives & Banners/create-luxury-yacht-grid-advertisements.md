# Luxury Yacht Grid Advertisement Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Yacht Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates premium 2×3 grid layouts for yacht brands seeking cinematic, editorial-quality marketing visuals. This luxury yacht advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces six equal panels in a 3:4 portrait grid: a hero yacht cruising in open ocean, close-up hull and line details, an aerial deck lifestyle scene with guests and champagne, a wide interior lounge shot with customizable material finishes, macro detail photography of hand-stitched leather and polished fixtures, and a golden-hour silhouette across calm water. Each panel is art-directed for natural daylight, cinematic marine photography, and editorial composition that communicates elegance, power, and exclusivity. Marine brands, yacht brokerages, and luxury lifestyle agencies use it to create cohesive visual assets for brochures, website hero sections, and high-end advertising campaigns without the cost and logistics of professional shoots. This prompt is ideal for creative directors, yacht marketing teams, and brand designers who need cohesive, ultra-premium visual content that reflects craftsmanship and freedom. ● Produces six panel types - hero yacht, hull detail, deck lifestyle, interior lounge, material macro, and golden-hour silhouette - in a single unified grid ● Customizable brand name and material palette variables (leather, wood, chrome finishes) to match yacht and brand identity ● Art-directed for natural daylight, golden-hour atmospheres, balanced exposure, and cinematic marine composition ● Outputs 3:4 portrait aspect ratio optimized for social media, print brochures, and vertical web placements ## Prompt

```
Create a refined 2×3 grid advertisement in 3:4 portrait aspect ratio for {{brand-name}} luxury yacht, highlighting elegance, craftsmanship, ocean freedom, and ultra-premium lifestyle appeal.

Grid structure: 6 equal panels arranged 2 columns × 3 rows.

**Panel 1 – Hero Yacht Presence:** Full yacht cruising in calm open ocean waters under bright natural daylight with ocean reflections. Mood: power, freedom, prestige.

**Panel 2 – Signature Hull & Lines:** Close-up of the yacht's hull design and windows. Focus on sleek lines, glossy paint finish, and craftsmanship. Mood: elegance, engineering precision.

**Panel 3 – Luxury Deck Lifestyle:** Aerial view of the sun deck with teak flooring, white lounge seating, and guests relaxing with champagne. Warm daylight. Mood: relaxation, exclusivity.

**Panel 4 – Interior Lounge:** Wide interior shot of the yacht's main lounge with {{material-palette}} finishes. Ambient natural light filtering through panoramic windows. Mood: comfort, refinement.

**Panel 5 – Material & Detail Craft:** Macro detail of interior finishes—hand-stitched leather textures, polished chrome fixtures, exotic wood grain. Shallow depth of field for tactile clarity. Mood: craftsmanship, luxury.

**Panel 6 – Golden Hour Serenity:** Yacht silhouette at golden hour with warm glow across calm water and soft horizon. Mood: calm, timelessness, dream-like.

Brand personality: elegant, powerful, exclusive. Logo presentation: minimal, metallic or embossed placement. Overall color palette draws from {{material-palette}}.

Visual style: luxury yacht advertising, high realism, cinematic marine photography, editorial lifestyle composition, ultra-premium branding. Professional marine and lifestyle photography with natural daylight and cinematic enhancements. Balanced exposure with rich highlights, soft elegant shadows.

Avoid: cheap materials, distorted yacht proportions, rough or stormy seas, overly saturated colors, cluttered decks, unreadable branding, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
