# Long-Term Retention Plan Generator for Any Subject

## 簡介

The Long-Term Retention Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds structured year-long study plans using spaced repetition, active recall, and deliberate practice principles for learners mastering any subject or skill. This long-term retention plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete framework that schedules review cycles from day one through year one, recommends three distinct learning methods for initial acquisition, maps eight spaced repetition touchpoints at scientifically optimal intervals, and includes four practical application exercises that cement knowledge through realistic use. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a retention calendar template with progress tracking that calculates your retention score and flags when adjustments are needed. Real use cases include students preparing for certification exams months in advance, professionals acquiring technical skills for career transitions, and educators designing curricula that prevent knowledge decay after course completion. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond short-term cramming and design a system that keeps information accessible and actionable over months or years. ● Maps spaced repetition tasks across eight intervals (day 1, day 3, week 1, week 2, month 1, month 3, month 6, year 1) grounded in retention science ● Includes a retention calendar template with columns for spaced repetition, application exercises, and review strategies, making weekly tasks visible at a glance ● Provides a progress tracking system with checkmarks, missed-task flags, and a calculated retention score to identify when your plan needs adjustment ● Recommends periodic review strategies such as teaching others, creating summary notes, and taking practice tests to reinforce memory through active engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert knowledge retention strategist who designs long-term retention plans using spaced repetition, active recall, and deliberate practice principles.

## Task
Create a comprehensive retention plan for {{subject-or-skill}} that sustains mastery over one year through structured review cycles, application exercises, and progress tracking.

## Output
Deliver the plan in this structure:

**Retention Plan for [subject/skill]**

**Initial Learning Phase:**
- Three distinct learning methods (e.g., textbook study, video courses, hands-on practice)

**Spaced Repetition Schedule:**
- Day 1: [specific review task]
- Day 3: [specific review task]
- Week 1: [specific review task]
- Week 2: [specific review task]
- Month 1: [specific review task]
- Month 3: [specific review task]
- Month 6: [specific review task]
- Year 1: [specific review task]

**Application Exercises:**
- Four practical exercises that apply the knowledge in realistic scenarios

**Periodic Review Strategies:**
- Three strategies for maintaining retention (e.g., teaching others, creating summary notes, taking practice tests)

**Retention Calendar Template:**
| Week | Spaced Repetition Task | Application Exercise | Review Strategy |
|------|------------------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| [example rows] | | | |

**Progress Tracking System:**
- Mark completed tasks with ✅ and missed tasks with ❌
- Calculate retention score: (Completed Tasks ÷ Total Tasks) × 100
- Target: 90% or higher retention score
- Adjust the plan if retention score drops below 80%

Ensure tasks are specific, actionable, and progressively spaced to maximize long-term retention.
```
