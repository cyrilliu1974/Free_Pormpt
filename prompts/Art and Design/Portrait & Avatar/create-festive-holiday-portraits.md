# Festive Holiday Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Festive Holiday Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates warm, cinematic holiday portraits featuring a subject under a beautifully decorated Christmas tree. This holiday portrait prompt for Midjourney produces intimate, cozy scenes with soft golden tree lights, wrapped gifts with festive ribbons, and dreamy bokeh backgrounds. The prompt uses specific Midjourney parameters (chaos 5, stylize 600, a custom style reference) to deliver expressive, joyful portraits with warm candlelight glow and a 3:4 aspect ratio optimized for portrait framing. Use it for seasonal greeting cards, social media holiday content, family portrait concepts, or festive marketing visuals that capture the magic of Christmas. ● Produces portraits with soft golden illumination from Christmas tree lights and gentle bokeh effects that create depth and warmth. ● Includes wrapped gifts, festive ribbons, and plush rug details that ground the scene in cozy holiday tradition. ● Uses Midjourney style reference 170932194 and chaos 5 for controlled creative variation while maintaining a cheerful, intimate mood. ● Outputs in 3:4 portrait aspect ratio with raw processing and stylize 600 for polished, expressive results. ## Prompt

```
An attractive young woman smiling warmly while sitting under a beautifully decorated Christmas tree in a cozy home. Soft golden lights illuminate her face, highlighting her expressive eyes and joyful expression. Wrapped gifts with festive ribbons rest beside her on a plush rug. The background glows with warm bokeh from the tree lights and gentle candlelight, creating a dreamy, holiday atmosphere. The scene feels intimate, cheerful, and cinematic. --chaos 5 --ar 3:4 --raw --sref 170932194  --stylize 600
```
