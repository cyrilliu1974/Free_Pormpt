# Email Campaign Automation Prompt for Lead Nurture

## 簡介

The Email Campaign Automation Prompt for Lead Nurture is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end email marketing campaigns from a single business description for marketers, founders, and growth teams. This email campaign automation prompt for ChatGPT produces a full strategy including 2-3 audience personas with pain points and motivations, a complete 5-email sequence (welcome, education, social proof, offer, urgency) with subject lines, messaging, and CTAs, tailored copywriting tips, automation workflow recommendations with triggers and segmentation logic, and six performance metrics to track success. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering structured text output you can hand directly to Mailchimp, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, or any email service provider. Use it when launching a new product, onboarding trial users, re-engaging dormant customers, or designing a lead-nurture funnel from scratch. ● Creates 2-3 customer personas with demographics, behaviors, pain points, and desired outcomes tailored to your business ● Writes a 5-email sequence covering awareness, education, trust-building, conversion, and urgency with subject lines, messaging, and calls to action ● Provides copywriting guidelines specific to your audience, including tone, personalization tactics, and objection handling ● Recommends email platforms, automation triggers, delays, branching logic, and segmentation strategies matched to your business size ● Defines six KPIs with benchmarks for open rates, click-through rates, conversions, list growth, unsubscribes, and revenue per email ## Prompt
