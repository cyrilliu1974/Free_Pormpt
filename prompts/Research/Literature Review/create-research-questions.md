# Research Question Generator for Academic Inquiry

## 簡介

The Research Question Generator for Academic Inquiry is a free AI prompt that develops compelling, open-ended research questions grounded in current scholarship for academic researchers, graduate students, and scholars planning new studies. This research question prompt for ChatGPT helps you formulate 4-6 field-specific questions that address knowledge gaps, challenge existing paradigms, or introduce new perspectives. By providing your topic, field context, and research scope, the prompt generates questions with accompanying justifications that explain their significance, how they fill knowledge gaps, and their potential to generate meaningful findings. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying a structured process that grounds questions in current debates, ensures they are researchable and focused, and evaluates their potential impact on theory, policy, or practice. Researchers use it to move beyond simple yes/no questions toward inquiry that invites substantive exploration and discussion. Reach for this prompt when you need to develop the foundation of a dissertation, grant proposal, or academic paper and want questions that balance originality, feasibility, and significance. ● Generates 4-6 open-ended research questions tailored to your topic, field context, and scope ● Provides 2-3 sentence justifications explaining each question's significance and contribution to the field ● Identifies how each question addresses a knowledge gap or offers new insight ● Balances ambition with feasibility by evaluating potential impact on theory, policy, or practice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher specializing in research question formulation across disciplines.

## Task
Develop compelling, open-ended research questions for the specified topic that address knowledge gaps, challenge existing paradigms, or offer new perspectives. Each question must be researchable, focused, and justified by its potential contribution to the field.

## Context
Strong research questions form the foundation of meaningful academic inquiry. They should emerge from understanding current literature, identify specific gaps or debates, and balance ambition with feasibility. Questions must avoid simple yes/no structures and instead invite exploration and discussion.

## Process
1. Ground questions in current trends, gaps, and debates within the field
2. Apply the defined scope parameters to ensure focused inquiry
3. Generate questions that are specific, researchable, and field-relevant
4. Refine to ensure open-ended framing that allows substantive investigation
5. Evaluate potential impact on theory, policy, or practice
6. Prioritize questions balancing originality, feasibility, and significance

## Input
Topic: {{topic}}

Current landscape: {{field-context}}

Research scope: {{scope}}

## Output
Provide 4-6 refined research questions, each followed by:
- A brief justification of its significance to the field
- How it addresses a knowledge gap or offers new insight
- Its potential to generate meaningful findings

Format each as:
**Question:** [the research question]
**Significance:** [2-3 sentence justification]
```
