# Lead Generation Webinar Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Webinar Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end webinar campaigns for businesses looking to attract qualified leads and drive conversions. This lead generation webinar prompt for ChatGPT produces a four-part strategy: content development with session outlines and engagement tactics, multi-channel promotional campaigns including email sequences and landing page requirements, delivery best practices with technical setup guidance, and post-webinar follow-up sequences that segment attendees by engagement level. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, translating your offering, unique selling points, target audience, and company context into a structured plan ready for execution. Teams use it to design product demo webinars, educational workshops, and virtual events that feed the sales pipeline. Reach for this prompt when you need to plan a webinar that functions as a lead generation engine rather than just a broadcast event. ● Creates session outlines with timing, audience engagement tactics, and demonstration plans tailored to your offering ● Designs promotional campaigns across email, social, and partnerships with registration incentives and landing page essentials ● Provides lead scoring criteria and follow-up sequences for attendees versus no-shows ● Outputs sales handoff processes and metrics to track conversion success ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert webinar strategist and lead generation specialist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive webinar strategy that showcases the offering, attracts qualified leads, and converts attendees into customers. Deliver a complete plan covering content development, promotion, and post-webinar follow-up.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{offering}}

**Target Audience & Industry:** {{audience-and-industry}}

**Unique Selling Points:** {{usp}}

**Company Background:** {{company-context}}

## Output
Structure your strategy using clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points:

### 1. Webinar Content Strategy
- Core narrative and value proposition positioning
- Session outline with timing
- Key demonstrations or examples
- Audience engagement tactics (polls, Q&A, interactive segments)

### 2. Promotional Campaign
- Target channels and messaging angles
- Email sequences (invitation, reminders)
- Social media and partnership outreach
- Landing page essentials
- Registration incentives

### 3. Delivery Best Practices
- Technical setup and platform recommendations
- Presenter guidelines
- Real-time engagement techniques

### 4. Lead Conversion Follow-Up
- Post-webinar email sequence (attendees vs. no-shows)
- Lead scoring and segmentation criteria
- Sales handoff process
- Nurture content for different engagement levels
- Metrics to track success

Ensure all content uses clear, benefit-focused language that directly addresses audience pain points and decision criteria.
```
