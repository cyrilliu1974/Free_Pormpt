# Sales Incentive Plan Aligned to Strategic Goals

## 簡介

The Sales Incentive Plan Aligned to Strategic Goals is a free AI prompt that builds a structured compensation framework connecting team rewards to measurable business outcomes for sales leaders and HR strategists. This sales incentive prompt for ChatGPT takes your company name, strategic objectives and KPIs, and chosen incentive types, then outputs a markdown table with four columns: Incentive Type, Performance Metric, Payout Structure, and Alignment with Company Goals. Each row pairs a specific reward mechanism (commission, bonus, SPIF) with a measurable KPI, defines the calculation method and threshold (for example, "3% commission on deals over $100K" or "$1,500 bonus when NPS exceeds 8.0"), and explains how that incentive drives your strategic vision. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Sales operations teams use it to replace spreadsheet guesswork with a clear, defensible comp structure; HR leaders use it to ensure every dollar spent on incentives pulls the same direction as corporate strategy. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or audit a sales compensation plan, onboard a new sales team with transparent targets, or present incentive rationale to finance and executive stakeholders. ● Connects each incentive type to a specific, trackable KPI so teams know exactly what behavior earns which reward. ● Defines clear payout structures with thresholds, percentages, and dollar amounts that finance can budget and reps can calculate. ● Articulates strategic alignment for every row, making it easy to justify comp decisions to leadership and maintain team buy-in. ● Outputs a ready-to-share markdown table that serves as both planning document and communication tool for sales kickoffs and one-on-ones. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales compensation strategist designing incentive structures that align team performance with business objectives.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive sales incentive plan presented as a markdown table. Analyze the provided company context, identify how each incentive type connects to performance metrics, define clear payout structures, and demonstrate alignment with strategic goals.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}

Business objectives and KPIs:
{{strategic-objectives-and-kpis}}

Incentive types to structure:
{{incentive-types}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:
- **Incentive Type**: the specific reward mechanism
- **Performance Metric**: the KPI being measured
- **Payout Structure**: threshold, calculation method, and reward amount/percentage
- **Alignment with Company Goals**: how this incentive drives strategic objectives

Ensure each row connects one incentive type to measurable criteria and explains the strategic rationale. Use clear, actionable payout formulas (e.g., "5% commission on revenue above $50K monthly quota" or "$2K bonus when customer retention hits 90%").
```
