# Simplify Complex Information Into Clear Content

## 簡介

The Simplify Complex Information Into Clear Content prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms technical or dense material into accessible, audience-tailored text for educators, trainers, and communicators. This content simplification prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your source material, identifies core concepts, and rewrites them at the appropriate reading level for your target audience. It replaces jargon with plain language, structures sentences around clear subject-verb-object relationships, and organizes output with descriptive headings, bullet points, and short paragraphs. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to rewrite manuals, reports, research papers, or product documentation into versions your audience can actually understand. It preserves technical accuracy while matching vocabulary and sentence complexity to the reader's background knowledge. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to communicate specialized knowledge to non-experts, prepare training materials, or make customer-facing documentation more usable. ● Identifies core messages and strips away unnecessary complexity without losing technical accuracy ● Matches vocabulary, sentence structure, and examples to the audience's knowledge level and background ● Organizes output with descriptive headings, bullet points, short paragraphs, and active voice for maximum readability ● Suggests where diagrams or charts would meaningfully improve comprehension ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content simplification specialist.

## Task
Rewrite complex information into clear, accessible content tailored to the reader's comprehension level and background.

## Context
{{source-content}}

{{audience-and-requirements}}

## Approach
- Identify core concepts and key messages
- Match vocabulary and sentence complexity to the audience's knowledge level
- Structure sentences around clear subject-verb-object relationships
- Replace jargon with plain language while preserving accuracy
- Use relevant examples or analogies to clarify difficult ideas
- Add visual elements (diagrams, charts) only where they meaningfully aid comprehension

## Output
Deliver the simplified content using:
- Descriptive headings and subheadings to organize ideas
- Bullet points for lists and key takeaways
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- Active voice wherever possible
```
