# Cinematic Urban Night Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Urban Night Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional street photography with subjects, luxury vehicles, and glowing urban backdrops for photographers, marketers, and content creators. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and other image generators produces nighttime city scenes featuring a customizable subject leaning against a white luxury car, styled in your chosen outfit, with wristwatch, sunglasses, and white sneakers. The composition includes glowing skyscrapers, bokeh light effects, palm trees wrapped in fairy lights, and blurred car headlights - all rendered with sharp details, soft lighting, and a modern street-style aesthetic. It's ideal for fashion lookbooks, automotive lifestyle campaigns, social media content, and editorial visuals that demand a polished, cinematic feel.
