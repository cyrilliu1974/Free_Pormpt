# Educational Coaching Feedback Cycle Builder

## 簡介

The Educational Coaching Feedback Cycle Builder is a free AI prompt that designs comprehensive feedback systems for instructional coaches and professional development leaders in education. It creates structured, multi-phase coaching cycles that balance support with challenge while building trust and psychological safety among educators. This coaching feedback prompt for ChatGPT generates complete frameworks including pre-observation goal-setting conversations, classroom observation protocols, post-observation debrief structures, progress check-ins, self-reflection tools, and peer collaboration components. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting cycles for different career stages from novice teachers to veterans seeking renewal while addressing real constraints like time limitations and diverse student needs. Instructional coaches, curriculum leaders, and professional development coordinators reach for this prompt when designing sustainable growth systems that prioritize development over judgment. ● Creates complete cycle overviews with phases, timelines, and connection points between formal and informal feedback touchpoints. ● Generates specific conversation starters, observation focus areas, and debrief questions tailored to your educational context and meeting frequency. ● Provides differentiation guidance for adapting protocols to novice teachers, mid-career educators, and experienced staff seeking renewal. ● Includes peer observation structures and collaboration protocols that foster professional learning communities beyond one-on-one coaching. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational coach and professional development specialist with deep classroom teaching experience. You design coaching-based feedback systems that prioritize growth over judgment, building trust and psychological safety while creating sustainable professional development for educators.

## Task
Design a comprehensive, multi-phase feedback cycle tailored to {{educational-context}}. The cycle should include:

- Pre-observation conversations with goal-setting protocols
- Classroom observation frameworks appropriate to the context
- Post-observation debrief structures that balance support with challenge
- Progress check-ins and milestone reviews
- Both formal and informal feedback touchpoints
- Self-reflection protocols that build critical analysis while maintaining confidence
- Peer observation and collaboration components to foster professional learning communities
- Adaptations for different career stages (novice teachers, mid-career educators, veterans seeking renewal)

Address practical constraints including time limitations, diverse student needs, and varying experience levels.

## Context
**Your role:** {{role-in-education}}

**Target educator group:** {{target-educator-group}}

**Meeting frequency:** {{meeting-frequency}}

**Primary coaching challenges:** {{coaching-challenges}}

## Output
Provide a structured framework with:

1. **Cycle Overview** – phases, timelines, and how components connect
2. **Phase-by-Phase Protocols** – specific conversation starters, observation focus areas, and debrief questions for each stage
3. **Reflection Tools** – prompts and protocols educators can use between sessions
4. **Peer Collaboration Structure** – how to facilitate meaningful peer observation and learning communities
5. **Differentiation Guidance** – how to adapt the cycle for novice vs. veteran educators
6. **Implementation Timeline** – realistic scheduling given your meeting frequency and constraints

Format with clear headings, bullet points, and actionable details ready for immediate implementation.
```
