# Email Lead List Builder for Cold Outreach

## 簡介

The Email Lead List Builder for Cold Outreach is a free AI prompt that researches and compiles qualified prospects into organized tables for sales and marketing teams. This lead list building prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your business type, target audience criteria, and custom table columns, then generating 15-20 vetted prospects complete with contact details, segmentation analysis, and prioritization guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering markdown-formatted tables that include email addresses and any custom fields you specify - company size, industry, location, decision-maker role, or other qualifying data. Sales development representatives use it to populate CRM systems, marketers use it to launch cold email campaigns, and business development teams use it to identify partnership opportunities while maintaining compliance with data protection standards. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly build a targeted prospect list without manual research or when scaling outbound campaigns across new market segments. ● Produces markdown tables with fully customizable columns tailored to your CRM or outreach workflow ● Delivers 15-20 qualified leads per run with accurate, current contact information prioritizing email addresses ● Includes segmentation analysis identifying patterns and high-value prospect clusters within your list ● Provides prioritization frameworks and next-step recommendations to improve conversion rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a lead generation specialist building an email list of qualified prospects.

## Task
Research and compile a structured list of potential clients that match the target profile. Gather accurate contact information, prioritizing email addresses, and organize the data into a table with the specified columns. Ensure all information is current, relevant, and compliant with data protection regulations.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}
(Describe the business type, target audience characteristics, and geographic focus)

**Table Structure:**
{{table-columns}}
(List the column names needed for the lead list)

## Output
1. Present the lead list as a markdown table using the specified column names
2. Include 15-20 qualified leads unless otherwise specified
3. Add a brief explanation below the table covering:
   - Key patterns or segments within the lead list
   - Recommended prioritization approach
   - Suggested next steps for outreach

Ensure all data is accurate, up-to-date, and sourced responsibly.
```
