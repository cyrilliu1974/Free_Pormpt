# Brand Voice Generator for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Brand Voice Generator for Marketing Teams is a free AI prompt that creates a complete brand voice guide defining how any brand communicates across every touchpoint. This brand voice prompt for ChatGPT builds a structured document that includes personality traits, core values, tone and style attributes, messaging principles, vocabulary lists, and four ready-to-use copy examples written in the defined voice (homepage hero, social media post, customer service response, and advertisement). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by accepting three variables: brand name, brand category, and target audience. Marketing teams use it to replace vague style guides with concrete, actionable voice frameworks that prevent inconsistent messaging across websites, social channels, customer support, and advertising. ● Defines three personality traits, three core values, and five tone and style attributes specific to the brand ● Generates four complete copy examples demonstrating the voice in action across different contexts ● Provides six brand-specific vocabulary words and two signature phrases for consistency ● Includes three actionable rules to maintain voice coherence across all marketing channels ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a brand strategist creating a comprehensive brand voice guide that defines how the brand communicates across every touchpoint.

## Context

- Brand name: {{brand-name}}
- Brand category: {{brand-category}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

Produce a complete brand voice guide using the structure below. Every section must reflect the brand's identity and target audience. All examples must be written in the defined voice, not described abstractly.

## Output

### Brand Overview
- Brand name
- Brand category
- Target audience

### Brand Personality
- Three distinct personality traits that define how the brand behaves

### Brand Values
- Three core values that drive brand decisions and messaging

### Tone and Style
- Three tone attributes (emotional quality: warm, authoritative, playful, etc.)
- Two style attributes (structural qualities: concise, conversational, technical, etc.)

### Key Messaging Principles
- Three guiding principles for all brand communication

### Vocabulary and Phrases
- Six words the brand uses consistently
- Two signature phrases that embody the brand voice

### Brand Voice Examples

**Website homepage hero:**
[Write 2-3 sentences in the defined voice]

**Social media post:**
[Write a complete post in the defined voice]

**Customer service response:**
[Write a support interaction in the defined voice]

**Advertisement:**
[Write ad copy in the defined voice]

### Guidelines for Consistency
- Three actionable rules to maintain voice consistency across channels

**Quality bar:** The voice must be distinctive and specific to this brand, not generic. Every example must demonstrate the defined tone and vocabulary in action. Tone, style, and word choices must stay consistent across all four examples.
```
