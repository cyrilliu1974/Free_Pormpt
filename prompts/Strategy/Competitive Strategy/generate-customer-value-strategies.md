# Customer Value Strategy Generator for Retention

## 簡介

The Customer Value Strategy Generator for Retention is a free AI prompt that produces unconventional approaches to increase customer lifetime value while staying within your core product domain. This customer value strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a creative business strategist, delivering two focused lists: ten ways to enhance your primary product's value and ten complementary products for up-selling or cross-selling. It then scores each idea on impact potential and implementation effort in a comparative table. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it practical for product managers, founders, and growth teams who need fresh retention tactics that avoid resource-intensive pivots. Use it when you need to boost repeat purchases, foster exclusivity, or identify low-effort, high-impact enhancements tailored to your specific product and customer base. ● Produces 10 core product enhancements and 10 complementary offering ideas in a single session ● Scores every strategy on a 0-10 scale for both impact on lifetime value and ease of implementation ● Focuses on perceived value increases, repeat engagement, community building, and staying close to your existing offering ● Outputs a structured table that lets you compare all 20 ideas at a glance and prioritize by effort versus reward ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative business strategist specializing in customer retention and growth.

## Task
Generate unconventional strategies to increase customer lifetime value (LTV) while staying within the same domain as the primary product. Deliver two complementary lists: ways to enhance the core product's value, and ideas for complementary products to up-sell or cross-sell. Then assess each idea's impact and implementation effort.

## Context
**Product and customers:**
{{product-and-customer-context}}

Focus on strategies that:
- Enhance perceived value without significantly increasing costs
- Encourage repeat purchases, subscriptions, or long-term engagement
- Foster community or exclusivity among customers
- Stay close to the core offering and avoid resource-intensive pivots

## Output

### Introduction
Brief recap of the business context and LTV goals.

### 10 Ways to Increase Core Product Value
List 10 unconventional ideas to enhance the primary product's value. Each idea should be concise and target a specific aspect of the product or customer experience.

### 10 Complementary Products to Up-Sell or Cross-Sell
List 10 ideas for related products that existing customers would value. Each should be closely tied to the primary product to minimize context switching and maximize perceived value.

### Impact and Effort Analysis
Create a table with four columns:

| Idea | Description | Impact Score (0-10) | Effort Score (0-10) |
|------|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|

- **Impact Score**: Potential to increase customer lifetime value (higher = greater potential)
- **Effort Score**: Relative ease of implementation (lower = easier to implement)

Include all 20 ideas in the table with scores and brief descriptions.
```
