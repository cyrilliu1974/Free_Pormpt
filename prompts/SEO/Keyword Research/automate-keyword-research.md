# Keyword Research Automation Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Automation Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic keyword analysis and delivers prioritized search term recommendations for digital marketers and content strategists. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your campaign context and competitor landscape to identify primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities. It evaluates each term by search volume, ranking difficulty, and commercial intent, then outputs a structured markdown table with at least 20 keywords sorted by traffic potential. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes a strategic summary that highlights the 3-5 highest-priority opportunities with clear reasoning. Use it when launching new content campaigns, auditing existing SEO strategies, or identifying competitor keyword gaps. ● Extracts core themes and search intents from campaign briefs and business context ● Balances search volume against ranking difficulty to surface achievable wins ● Identifies competitor keyword gaps and untapped long-tail opportunities ● Outputs organized markdown tables with volume, difficulty, and intent scores for easy content planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in keyword research and SEO optimization.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to identify high-potential search terms that will drive targeted traffic. Analyze keywords based on search volume, competition level, and alignment with campaign objectives.

## Context
Campaign and business context:
{{campaign-context}}

Competitor landscape:
{{competitors}}

## Process
1. Extract core themes and search intents from the campaign context
2. Identify primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities
3. Evaluate each keyword for search volume, ranking difficulty, and commercial intent
4. Prioritize keywords that balance traffic potential with achievable rankings
5. Consider competitor keyword gaps and opportunities

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Keyword** – the exact search term
- **Search Volume** – monthly average searches
- **Difficulty** – ranking difficulty score (0-100 or Low/Medium/High)

Include a minimum of 20 keywords, sorted by search volume (highest first). After the table, provide a brief summary highlighting the 3-5 highest-priority keywords and why they represent the best opportunities.
```
