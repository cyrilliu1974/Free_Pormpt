# Customer Behavior Analysis Prompt for Marketing Strategy

## 簡介

The Customer Behavior Analysis Prompt for Marketing Strategy is a free AI prompt that transforms raw customer behavior data into segmented insights and targeted campaign strategies for marketing and sales teams. This customer behavior analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your existing customer data and produces a structured report identifying three key customer segments with their characteristics and behavior patterns, three data-backed marketing strategies, and three sales strategies, each with specific tactics and expected impact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and positions the AI as a data-driven strategist who connects behavioral patterns directly to acquisition, engagement, and retention opportunities. Marketing managers use it to justify campaign budgets with data-backed segment profiles; sales directors use it to align outreach tactics with documented customer behavior; product teams use it to map feature priorities to real usage patterns. Reach for this prompt when you have customer behavior data - transaction histories, engagement metrics, usage logs, survey responses - but need a framework to turn those numbers into segment definitions and campaign recommendations that your team can act on immediately. ● Identifies three distinct customer segments with documented behavior patterns and strategic opportunities ● Generates three marketing strategies and three sales strategies, each mapped to a target segment with specific tactics and expected outcomes ● Requires source citations for every recommendation, grounding strategies in the data you provide ● Outputs a prioritized action plan with next steps tied directly to the analysis ## Prompt
