# SEO Article Headline Generator

## 簡介

The SEO Article Headline Generator is a free AI prompt that produces multiple high-performing headlines tailored to balance reader engagement with search engine visibility. This headline prompt for ChatGPT takes a single topic variable and outputs 5–10 distinct headlines, each applying a different proven technique - action-driven verbs, data-backed specificity, question formats, urgency signals, or power words. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring each headline places primary keywords near the beginning, stays under 60 characters for full SERP display, and accurately reflects the article's core message. Writers, content marketers, and bloggers use it to A/B test titles, match different audience search behaviors, and improve click-through rates without sacrificing clarity or misleading readers. Reach for this prompt when you need a range of headline options fast - whether you're drafting a blog post, news article, or landing page and want to test which angle resonates best with both readers and algorithms. ● Outputs 5–10 headline variations per topic, each using a distinct engagement strategy ● Optimizes keyword placement and character length for search engine result pages ● Labels each headline by technique - action-driven, data-backed, question, urgency, or power words - so you understand why it works ● Balances creativity with accuracy to avoid clickbait while maximizing appeal ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a journalist specializing in high-impact headlines that balance reader engagement with search engine optimization.

## Task

Generate 5–10 SEO-optimized headlines for an article on {{topic}}. Each headline should use a distinct approach to appeal to different reader interests and search behaviors.

## Requirements

**Content principles:**
- Accurately reflect the topic's core themes and key messages
- Balance creativity with clarity—be compelling but never misleading
- Vary your angle across headlines to cover different facets of the topic

**SEO optimization:**
- Place the primary keyword near the beginning of each headline
- Keep headlines under 60 characters when possible for full display in search results
- Integrate high-value search terms naturally

**Craft each headline using one of these techniques:**
- **Action-driven:** Lead with a strong verb ("Discover," "Reveal," "Transform")
- **Data-backed:** Include numbers or statistics for specificity
- **Question format:** Pose a question the article answers
- **Urgency:** Signal timely or essential information
- **Power words:** Use evocative adjectives that create emotional pull

## Output

Deliver 5–10 distinct headlines, each on its own line. Label the technique used in parentheses after each headline for reference.
```
