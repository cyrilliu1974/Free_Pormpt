# Emotional Intelligence Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The Emotional Intelligence Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces evidence-based articles examining how EI skills impact career success within any profession. This emotional intelligence research prompt for ChatGPT creates structured reports that identify the 4-5 most critical EI components for a given field, provide three concrete improvement strategies per component, and include a practical self-assessment table with 8-10 behavioral questions. The output synthesizes established frameworks from Goleman, Mayer-Salovey-Caruso, and Bar-On models, tailored to the interpersonal dynamics, stress factors, and decision-making contexts unique to the profession you specify. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need career-development content, training materials, or professional coaching resources grounded in EI research and customized to a specific occupation. ● Identifies key emotional intelligence components most relevant to any profession you specify ● Provides three actionable improvement strategies per EI component with concrete steps ● Generates a self-assessment table with behavioral questions and rating scales ● Includes reference citations from established emotional intelligence research frameworks ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert researcher and content strategist specializing in emotional intelligence (EI) and career success.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive investigation into how emotional intelligence drives career success in {{profession}}. Identify the key EI components most relevant to this field, provide actionable improvement strategies, and create a practical self-assessment tool.

## Context

Emotional intelligence—the ability to recognize, understand, and manage emotions—is a critical predictor of career performance across professions. Each field faces unique EI challenges based on interpersonal dynamics, stress factors, and decision-making contexts. Your analysis should highlight the specific EI demands and opportunities within {{profession}}.

## Output

Deliver a structured, evidence-based article with the following sections:

# Introduction
- Brief overview of emotional intelligence and its link to career success
- Specific focus on {{profession}} and the unique EI challenges this field presents

# Key EI Components
For each of 4-5 components most relevant to {{profession}}:

## [Component Name]
- Definition and why it matters in {{profession}}
- Three concrete strategies for improvement
  1. [Strategy with specific action]
  2. [Strategy with specific action]
  3. [Strategy with specific action]

# Self-Assessment Checklist
Create a table with 8-10 questions across the identified components:

| EI Component | Self-Assessment Question | Always | Often | Sometimes | Rarely | Never |
|--------------|--------------------------|--------|-------|-----------|--------|-------|
| [Component] | [Behavioral question] | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ |

# Conclusion
- Synthesize key takeaways specific to {{profession}}
- Encourage readers to prioritize EI development as a career investment

# References
Cite 3-5 credible sources in this format:
- [Author], [Article Title], [Publication], [Year], [URL or DOI]

**Requirements:**
- Tailor all content to the unique interpersonal, cognitive, and emotional demands of {{profession}}
- Ensure strategies are actionable, not generic advice
- Use clear, accessible language appropriate for working professionals
- Base recommendations on established EI frameworks (Goleman, Mayer-Salovey-Caruso, or Bar-On models)
```
