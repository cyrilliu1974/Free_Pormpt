# No-Code MVP Builder Prompt for Lean Startups

## 簡介

The No-Code MVP Builder Prompt for Lean Startups is a free AI prompt that helps founders design, test, and iterate minimum viable products without technical skills or budget. This no-code MVP prompt for ChatGPT takes a single startup concept and produces four structured deliverables: ten actionable MVP ideas buildable in under an hour for zero dollars, a success measurement framework centered on your One Metric That Matters, targeted customer feedback collection strategies, and ten follow-on experiments to expand the core value proposition. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Founders use it to validate ideas before investing in development, solo entrepreneurs rely on it to launch testable prototypes within days, and accelerator cohorts apply it to iterate rapidly based on real user signals. ● Produces 10 specific no-code MVP concepts requiring no technical skills, zero dollars, and less than one hour to build ● Defines your One Metric That Matters alongside engagement measures and quantitative success thresholds ● Recommends feedback collection methods matched to your product format and target audience ● Suggests 10 value-add experiments to test incremental improvements with real users ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a startup advisor specializing in lean product development, MVP validation, and rapid experimentation.

## Task

Help design, test, and iterate a no-code MVP by:

1. **Generating 10 no-code MVP concepts** that cost $0, require no technical skills, and can be built in under 1 hour. Each should test the core value proposition.

2. **Defining success metrics** including:
   - The One Metric That Matters (OMTM) for this MVP
   - User engagement measurement approaches
   - Clear success/failure criteria (draw a quantitative line in the sand)

3. **Recommending customer feedback methods** suited to this MVP's format and audience.

4. **Proposing 10 value-add experiments** to test with the target audience that build on the core concept.

## Context

{{startup-concept}}

## Output

Structure your response with numbered sections matching the four tasks above. Be specific and actionable. Tailor all suggestions to the product concept and audience provided.
```
