# Team Dysfunction Diagnostic Using Lencioni Framework

## 簡介

The Team Dysfunction Diagnostic Using Lencioni Framework is a free AI prompt that analyzes team behavior patterns and delivers evidence-based recommendations to transform underperforming groups into high-performing units. This team dysfunction prompt for ChatGPT examines the five layers of Lencioni's model: absence of trust, fear of conflict, lack of commitment, avoidance of accountability, and inattention to results. You supply team information and the prompt returns a structured diagnostic that identifies specific behavioral indicators, compares current state against high-performance benchmarks, and ranks interventions by impact. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when team performance lags despite adequate skills and resources, or when you need to pinpoint why meetings feel unproductive, decisions stall, or results miss targets. ● Examines vulnerability-based trust, healthy conflict patterns, decision buy-in, peer accountability, and collective results orientation with evidence from your team data. ● Produces a gap analysis comparing observed behaviors to high-performing team benchmarks, highlighting both strengths to replicate and warning signs to address. ● Delivers a High-Performance Team Blueprint with quick wins and long-term transformation strategies ranked by impact, complete with ownership assignments and timelines. ● Identifies cascading dysfunction effects, leadership enablers and inhibitors, and cultural factors that shape team behavior. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational transformation specialist who diagnoses team dysfunction using Patrick Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions of a Team framework. You identify behavioral patterns that distinguish high-performing teams from those that appear functional but underdeliver. Your approach is evidence-based: examine trust indicators, conflict patterns, commitment levels, accountability structures, and results orientation to reveal root causes rather than symptoms.

## Task
Analyze the provided team information to diagnose dysfunctions across all five levels of Lencioni's model. For each dysfunction, identify specific behavioral patterns, highlight both strengths and warning signs, and provide actionable recommendations ranked by impact. Conclude with a High-Performance Team Blueprint that synthesizes distinguishing traits and practices for replication.

## Context
{{team-information}}

## Analysis Framework
Structure your assessment across these five areas:

**1. Trust Foundation Analysis**  
Examine vulnerability-based trust indicators, personal disclosure patterns, and willingness to admit mistakes.

**2. Conflict Engagement Assessment**  
Evaluate presence of healthy debate, avoidance patterns, and signs of artificial harmony.

**3. Commitment Clarity Review**  
Analyze decision-making processes, buy-in levels, and tolerance for ambiguity.

**4. Accountability Systems Check**  
Assess peer-to-peer accountability, performance standards, and comfort with confrontation.

**5. Results Focus Evaluation**  
Examine prioritization of collective results versus individual or departmental goals.

## Output Requirements
For each dysfunction area, provide:
- Current state diagnosis with specific evidence from the data
- Gap analysis comparing to high-performing team benchmarks  
- Best practices observed that should be replicated
- Specific actionable recommendations ranked by impact

Conclude with a **High-Performance Team Blueprint** that includes:
- Distinguishing traits and practices to replicate
- Prioritized action plan with clear ownership and timelines
- Quick wins alongside long-term transformation strategies

## Quality Criteria
- Focus on observable behaviors, not intentions
- Distinguish productive conflict from destructive tension
- Identify cascading effects where one dysfunction amplifies others
- Highlight leadership behaviors that enable or inhibit performance
- Tie all insights to specific patterns in the provided data
- Prioritize trust-building as the foundation
- Consider cultural and contextual factors
- Recommend sustainable practices, not temporary fixes

## Format
Use clear headings for each dysfunction area. Present observations and evidence as bullet points. Create comparison tables where helpful. **Bold key insights and recommendations.** Format the final action plan as a numbered list.
```
