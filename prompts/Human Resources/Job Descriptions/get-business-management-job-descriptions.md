# Business Management Job Description Generator

## 簡介

The Business Management Job Description Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete, structured job postings for Business Management roles tailored to your company and position requirements. This business management job description prompt for ChatGPT takes your company context and position details, then produces a professional job posting with seven clearly labeled sections: role overview, key responsibilities (6-8 points), required qualifications (4-6 points), preferred skills (4-6 points), benefits and perks (4-6 points), company culture summary, and application instructions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and acts as an experienced HR professional to ensure industry-standard terminology and a tone that appeals to qualified candidates. Use it when you need to post a Business Management opening and want to communicate expectations, requirements, and company value in a format that attracts top talent. ● Produces a role overview paragraph plus six detailed sections covering responsibilities, qualifications, skills, benefits, culture, and application steps. ● Balances required versus preferred qualifications so you attract ambitious candidates without overwhelming them. ● Includes a benefits and company culture section to differentiate your posting in competitive talent markets. ● Outputs clear application instructions with a checklist of required documents, reducing back-and-forth during candidate screening. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced HR professional writing a comprehensive job description for a Business Management position.

## Task
Create a structured job description that attracts qualified candidates by clearly communicating role expectations, requirements, and company value proposition.

## Context
Company information:
{{company-context}}

Position details:
{{position-details}}

## Output
Structure the job description with these sections:

**Role Overview**
A one-paragraph summary highlighting the position's key aspects and overall responsibilities.

**Key Responsibilities** 📋
6-8 bullet points detailing primary responsibilities and tasks.

**Required Qualifications** 🎓
4-6 bullet points covering essential education, experience, and skills.

**Preferred Skills** ⭐
4-6 bullet points listing advantageous but not required qualifications.

**Benefits & Perks** 🎯
4-6 bullet points describing compensation, benefits, and incentives.

**Company Culture** 💫
2-3 sentences describing work environment, values, and culture as they relate to this role.

**How to Apply** 📤
Step-by-step application instructions, including required documents.

Use clear, concise language with industry-standard terminology. Focus on accuracy and appeal to top talent.
```
