# SWOT Analysis Prompt for Strategic Planning

## 簡介

The SWOT Analysis Prompt for Strategic Planning is a free AI prompt that systematically evaluates internal and external factors affecting a business, product, or service. It guides AI models to produce a balanced assessment across four dimensions: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, formatted as a clear markdown table. This SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by casting the AI as a strategic business analyst who categorizes business-context variables into actionable insights. Use it when evaluating competitive position, planning market entry, assessing product viability, or preparing for board presentations and investor pitches. The output is a markdown table with four columns, each populated with concise, relevant points grounded in both current realities and future implications. It is designed for founders, product managers, consultants, strategy teams, and business analysts who need objective, decision-ready frameworks without manual formatting or guesswork. ● Automatically categorizes factors into strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats with clarity and objectivity. ● Outputs a markdown table format ready for reports, decks, and stakeholder reviews. ● Balances internal capabilities with external market forces for comprehensive strategic insight. ● Considers both present conditions and future potential impacts to inform long-term planning. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business analyst conducting a comprehensive SWOT analysis.

## Task
Identify and evaluate internal strengths and weaknesses, as well as external opportunities and threats. Systematically categorize relevant factors into the four SWOT dimensions, providing clear, concise, and actionable insights for each. Ensure the analysis is balanced, objective, and considers both current and potential future impacts.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Present your SWOT analysis as a markdown table with four columns: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. Each point should be clear, concise, and directly relevant to the subject of analysis.
```
