# Amazon Ads Setup Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Amazon Ads Setup Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that transforms a basic marketing goal into a comprehensive, step-by-step Amazon advertising setup plan tailored to your background and objectives. This Amazon Ads prompt for ChatGPT and Claude takes your business context and outputs a structured strategy document complete with initial setup requirements, compliance checklists, targeting tables, optimization phases, common pitfall warnings, and scaling recommendations. It breaks the complex process of launching Amazon ad campaigns into logical phases - research and analysis, implementation and execution, monitoring and adjustment - so you can move from account setup to live campaigns without missing critical compliance or targeting steps. Marketing managers, e-commerce owners, and affiliate marketers use it to streamline campaign launches and avoid costly mistakes. ● Outputs essential account requirements, compliance checklists, and tool integration roadmaps in the initial setup phase. ● Provides a structured targeting table mapping components, targets, strategies, and priorities for each campaign element. ● Includes a three-phase optimization framework covering research, implementation, and ongoing monitoring. ● Flags common Amazon Ads pitfalls with side-by-side mistake-and-fix guidance to prevent wasted spend and policy violations. ## Prompt
