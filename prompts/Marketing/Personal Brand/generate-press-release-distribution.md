# Press Release Distribution Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Press Release Distribution Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end press release management systems for PR professionals, communications teams, and organizations of any size. This press release distribution prompt for ChatGPT creates a tailored framework covering every stage of the PR lifecycle: content creation guidelines and approval workflows, media outlet database design with journalist contact management, personalized distribution processes with timing strategies, analytics and reporting structures with KPIs, and team role definitions with collaboration protocols. You provide your organization context (industry, team size, news angles, compliance needs) and target media landscape (key outlets, beat reporters, geographic focus, tier priorities), and the prompt generates actionable, step-by-step guidance for each component. Use it when building a new PR function, standardizing scattered press release practices, onboarding communications staff, or optimizing media outreach for better pickup rates. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces content guidelines, format specs, and multi-stage approval workflows for creating newsworthy releases ● Designs media database structures with outlet categorization, journalist contact management, and maintenance schedules ● Defines distribution processes including outlet targeting criteria, personalized pitch techniques, timing strategies, and follow-up cadences ● Specifies analytics frameworks with pickup rates, share of voice, sentiment tracking, reporting formats, and optimization insights ● Maps team roles, collaboration processes, stakeholder intake methods, and training protocols ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert press release manager with deep knowledge of media relations, PR strategy, and effective communication techniques.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive framework for managing and distributing press releases to relevant media outlets. Cover the entire lifecycle: creation, targeting, distribution, and impact measurement.

## Context
**Organization profile:** {{organization-context}}
(Include: industry, PR team size, primary news angles, and any compliance/regulatory considerations)

**Media landscape:** {{target-media}}
(Include: key outlets, beat reporters you've worked with, geographic focus, and media tier priorities—national, trade, local, digital-first, etc.)

## Output
Provide a detailed, step-by-step framework organized into these sections:

### 1. Press Release Creation
- **Content Guidelines:** What makes a newsworthy story, inverted pyramid structure, quote sourcing, boilerplate standards
- **Format Specifications:** Length, headline/subhead rules, multimedia requirements, accessibility
- **Approval Process:** Draft → legal/compliance review → executive sign-off → final edit workflow

### 2. Media Outlet Database
- **Database Structure:** Required fields (outlet name, contact, beat, tier, past coverage, CRM integration)
- **Outlet Categorization:** Tier system, beat/topic tags, geographic segmentation
- **Journalist Contact Management:** Research process, contact verification, preference tracking (email vs. call, embargo protocol)
- **Database Maintenance:** Quarterly audits, movement tracking, attribution of wins

### 3. Distribution Process
- **Outlet Targeting:** Scoring criteria for relevance, segmentation by news type
- **Personalized Pitches:** Customization techniques, subject line formulas, prior relationship references
- **Timing and Scheduling:** Embargo strategy, optimal send windows by outlet type, time-zone considerations
- **Follow-ups and Relationship Management:** Cadence guidelines, value-add touchpoints beyond pitches, rapport-building tactics

### 4. Analytics and Reporting
- **KPIs to Track:** Pickup rate, share of voice, sentiment, referral traffic, message pull-through, tier distribution
- **Monitoring Tools:** Media monitoring platforms, Google Alerts, social listening, analytics dashboards
- **Reporting Format:** Executive summary, outlet-by-outlet breakdown, trend analysis, SOV vs. competitors
- **Insights and Optimization:** What worked/didn't, journalist feedback integration, iterative refinement

### 5. Team and Responsibilities
- **Roles and Responsibilities:** Who drafts, who approves, who distributes, who monitors, who owns journalist relationships
- **Collaboration Processes:** Intake from internal stakeholders, cross-functional alignment (comms, legal, product), handoff protocols
- **Training and Best Practices:** Onboarding for new team members, style guide adherence, media training for spokespeople, crisis readiness

For each section, provide actionable steps and guidelines tailored to the organization and media landscape described above.
```
