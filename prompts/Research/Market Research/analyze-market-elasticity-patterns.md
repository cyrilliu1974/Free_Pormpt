# Market Elasticity Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Market Elasticity Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that calculates elasticity coefficients and forecasts market responses to economic shifts for businesses, economists, and pricing strategists. This market elasticity prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, historical sales data, and economic scenarios and produces a structured analytical report covering price elasticity of demand, income elasticity, cross-price elasticity, and predictive modeling for conditions like recession, inflation, and supply chain disruptions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, employing regression analysis and time-series decomposition to identify baseline demand patterns, critical price points, seasonal variations, and sensitivity factors including consumer income levels, substitute availability, brand loyalty, and market saturation.
