# AI Business Idea Generator With Strategic Analysis

## 簡介

The AI Business Idea Generator With Strategic Analysis is a free AI prompt that produces ranked startup concepts with detailed feasibility assessments for entrepreneurs and business strategists. This AI business idea prompt for ChatGPT generates ten distinct digital business concepts that incorporate artificial intelligence, evaluates them across feasibility, market potential, and societal impact, then delivers comprehensive startup analysis for the top three ranked opportunities. The prompt produces MVP specifications, go-to-market strategies, revenue models with five-year projections, and a complete SWOT analysis for the highest-ranked idea. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your industry focus, risk tolerance, and experience level through three customizable variables. Use cases include identifying opportunities in new markets, validating AI integration strategies, and preparing investor-ready business frameworks. This prompt is built for entrepreneurs exploring AI-driven ventures, startup advisors conducting opportunity assessments, and business teams evaluating digital transformation pathways. ● Produces ten AI-based business ideas with technology stacks, target markets, and core benefits clearly defined ● Ranks all concepts by feasibility, market potential, and impact, then delivers detailed MVP plans and revenue projections for the top three ● Generates a complete SWOT analysis and candid advisor-level guidance on the highest-potential opportunity ● Adapts output based on industry focus, risk tolerance, and entrepreneurial experience level through three prompt variables ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an AI-powered business idea generator and startup advisor with deep expertise in artificial intelligence, digital technologies, and entrepreneurship.

## Task
Generate 10 AI-based digital business ideas spanning diverse industries, then evaluate and provide strategic analysis for the most promising opportunities.

## Context
Focus areas: {{industry-focus}}
Risk tolerance: {{risk-tolerance}}
Entrepreneurial experience: {{experience-level}}

## Output

**Business Ideas (10 total):**

For each idea provide:
- Concise description
- Key technologies involved
- Target market
- Potential benefits

**Idea Rankings (1-10):**

Rank all ideas based on:
- Feasibility
- Market potential
- Societal impact

**Top 3 Ideas – In-Depth Analysis:**

For each of the top 3 ranked ideas, provide:
- Proposed minimum viable product (MVP)
- Go-to-market strategy
- Revenue model
- 5-year revenue projections (Y1-Y5)

**Top Idea – SWOT Analysis:**

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

**Candid Advice on Top Idea:**

Provide honest, constructive guidance covering both merits and risks. Avoid superficial evaluations or generic advice.
```
