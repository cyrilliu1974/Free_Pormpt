# Speech Opening Paragraph Writer for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Speech Opening Paragraph Writer is a free AI prompt that crafts compelling speech introductions for public speakers, presenters, and communicators. This speech opening prompt for ChatGPT produces a single cohesive paragraph designed to capture attention immediately, introduce your main theme, and transition smoothly into the body of your speech. It works by applying dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow while incorporating powerful hooks, vivid language, rhetorical devices, and engaging elements like statistics, anecdotes, or provocative questions. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to open a presentation, keynote, toast, or any spoken address with maximum impact. ● Creates a powerful hook tailored to your specific speech context and audience ● Weaves in rhetorical devices - metaphor, repetition, contrast - to enhance memorability ● Incorporates concrete engagement tactics like relevant statistics, anecdotes, or questions ● Ensures smooth syntactic flow so the paragraph sounds natural when read aloud ● Ends with a bridge statement that transitions seamlessly into your speech body ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speechwriter specializing in compelling openings.

## Task
Craft an engaging opening paragraph for a speech that immediately captures attention and sets the tone. Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and coherent connections between ideas.

## Requirements
The opening paragraph must:
- Begin with a powerful hook directly related to the speech topic
- Introduce the main theme concisely
- Employ vivid language and rhetorical devices (metaphor, repetition, contrast)
- Incorporate a relevant statistic, anecdote, or provocative question
- Build smooth transitions through clear syntactic dependencies
- Conclude with a bridge statement that leads naturally into the speech body

## Context
{{speech-context}}

## Output
Deliver a single cohesive paragraph—no bullet points, no numbered steps. The paragraph should feel natural when spoken aloud and create immediate engagement with the audience.
```
