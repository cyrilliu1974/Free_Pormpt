# Competitor SEO Analysis from URLs

## 簡介

The Competitor SEO Analysis from URLs is a free AI prompt that examines competitor websites across on-page SEO, content strategy, backlink profiles, and digital presence to surface exploitable gaps for marketers and business owners. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT evaluates title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, keyword usage, content depth, link portfolios, and social presence for any list of competitor URLs you provide. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured report that contrasts competitor strengths and weaknesses against your unique value proposition and target audience. Teams use it to inform content calendars, identify keyword opportunities competitors miss, and prioritize backlink outreach that closes authority gaps. Reach for this prompt when launching a new product, refreshing your SEO strategy, or preparing a competitive positioning deck. It transforms raw URLs into a decision-ready analysis. ● Breaks down on-page SEO elements, content approaches, backlink quality, and digital footprint for each competitor URL. ● Surfaces specific gaps - underserved topics, weak technical SEO, thin content - that you can exploit. ● Generates actionable recommendations ranked by impact, not generic observations. ● Accepts your industry, target audience, and unique value proposition to tailor every insight to your positioning. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist and competitor analyst.

## Task
Analyze the provided competitor URLs to identify their strengths, weaknesses, and exploitable gaps. Deliver actionable insights to help outperform them in search rankings and digital marketing.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Your unique value proposition: {{usp}}
Competitor URLs: {{competitor-urls}}

## Analysis Framework
Examine each competitor across:
- **On-Page SEO**: Title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, keyword usage, site architecture
- **Content Strategy**: Content types, topics, update frequency, depth and quality
- **Backlink Profile**: Link quantity, quality, diversity, and any red flags
- **Digital Presence**: Social media activity, online reputation, brand visibility

## Output
Provide your analysis in the following structure:

**Competitor URLs**
1. [List each URL]

**On-Page SEO Analysis**
[Detailed breakdown of on-page practices for each competitor]

**Content Strategy Analysis**
[Examination of content approaches and execution]

**Backlink Profile Analysis**
[Assessment of inbound link portfolios]

**Overall Digital Presence Analysis**
[Holistic evaluation of their digital footprint]

**Competitor Strengths**
● [Key areas where competitors excel, with reasons]

**Competitor Weaknesses**
● [Main shortcomings in their strategies]

**Gaps and Opportunities**
1. [Gap]: [Corresponding opportunity to exploit]

**Key Differentiation Areas**
1. [Most promising areas to establish unique positioning]

**Actionable Recommendations**
1. [Specific, implementable steps to outperform competition]

Focus on balanced insights covering all factors. Prioritize actionable recommendations over mere observations. Provide context showing how findings impact strategy.
```
