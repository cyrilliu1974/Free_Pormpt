# Brand Voice Guide Generator for Businesses

## 簡介

The Brand Voice Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive brand identity document defining how a business communicates across all channels. This brand voice guide prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your business context and produces a structured markdown guide covering brand personality, voice and tone standards, grammar rules, key messaging, content guidelines, and annotated examples. The output serves as the reference document for marketing teams, customer service, content writers, and any stakeholder responsible for brand communication - from social media posts to crisis messaging to customer emails. It moves beyond generic templates by tailoring every guideline to your specific business, audience, and market position. Use this prompt when launching a new brand, onboarding content teams, standardizing messaging after a rebrand, or establishing governance for multi-channel communication. ● Defines brand archetype, personality traits, and how the brand relates to its audience ● Specifies baseline voice characteristics and tone variations for different contexts like social media, support, and crisis communication ● Establishes grammar, point of view, vocabulary, and formatting conventions tailored to the business ● Delivers key messaging including tagline, mission, value propositions, and brand story elevator pitch ● Provides before/after examples showing the voice applied across website copy, email, and social posts ● Includes content governance, approval processes, and channel-specific guidelines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business communications expert specializing in brand voice development and identity systems.

## Task
Create a comprehensive brand voice guide that defines tone, style, personality, and messaging standards to ensure consistent, authentic communication across all channels.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-context}}

The guide will serve as the reference document for all internal and external communications, from marketing copy to customer service interactions.

## Output
Deliver a structured brand voice guide in markdown with these sections:

### 1. Business Overview
- Business type, audience, and market position
- Unique value proposition
- Strategic communication goals

### 2. Brand Personality
- Brand archetype and core traits
- Personality descriptors
- How the brand "speaks" and relates to its audience

### 3. Voice and Tone
- Baseline voice characteristics (consistent across contexts)
- Tone variations for specific situations (social media, crisis, celebration, support)
- What to embrace and what to avoid

### 4. Grammar and Style
- Point of view and tense
- Sentence structure preferences
- Vocabulary guidelines (industry terms, accessibility, complexity)
- Formatting conventions

### 5. Key Messaging
- Tagline
- Mission statement
- Core value propositions
- Brand story elevator pitch

### 6. Content Guidelines
- Priority content themes
- Recommended formats by channel
- Quality standards and approval process
- Governance and update cadence

### 7. Examples
- Three concrete before/after or annotated examples showing the voice in action across different contexts (e.g., website copy, email, social post)

Use clear, actionable language. Make every guideline specific to this business—avoid generic advice. Structure writing to emphasize relationships between ideas (dependency grammar approach).
```
