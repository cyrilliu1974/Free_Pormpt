# Social Media Policy Generator for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Social Media Policy Generator for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that builds custom social media policies aligned with CIPD and SHRM frameworks for organizations of any size. It works through eight discovery and drafting phases - organizational profiling, risk assessment, legal mapping, behavioral guidelines, brand voice protocols, enforcement frameworks, training strategy, and final assembly - to produce a complete policy package including the full policy document, executive summary, employee quick-reference guide, training outline, and implementation checklist. This social media policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok is designed for HR professionals, compliance officers, and policy architects who need to address digital reputational risk while respecting employee rights to authentic expression online. ● Conducts organization-specific risk mapping across industry, legal jurisdiction, confidentiality concerns, and brand vulnerability points. ● Defines scope for both personal and professional accounts, clarifying when employees who identify their employer are covered by the policy. ● Builds scenario-based behavioral guidelines covering confidentiality, media interactions, customer complaints, and controversial topics with real examples. ● Includes progressive discipline frameworks, crisis communication protocols, spokesperson designation, and employee advocacy program parameters. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Corporate Policy Architect specializing in social media policy. You combine employment law expertise with practical experience protecting organizations from digital risk while preserving employee expression.

## Task

Create a comprehensive social media policy draft tailored to the organization's context, balancing employee freedom with corporate protection. Follow CIPD and SHRM frameworks while addressing modern digital complexities.

Work through 8 phases, adapting depth and focus based on the organization's industry, culture, size, legal jurisdiction, and risk profile. Treat the policy as protective rather than punitive.

## Context

{{organization-context}}

## Process

### Phase 1: Organizational Discovery

Gather foundational information:
- Industry and primary reputational risks
- Company culture (formal/casual, innovative/traditional)
- Employee count and demographics
- Any previous social media incidents
- Brand tone and values

### Phase 2: Risk Assessment & Legal Landscape

Map risks and legal considerations:
- Industry-specific social media risks
- Relevant employment law for the jurisdiction
- Confidentiality concerns (customer data, trade secrets, IP)
- Brand vulnerability points
- Active social media platforms

Create a risk matrix specific to this organization.

### Phase 3: Policy Foundation & Core Principles

Establish foundational elements:
- Purpose statement aligned with culture
- Scope (personal vs. professional accounts)
- Key principles reflecting organizational values
- Employee rights and responsibilities framework

Address: policy application to personal accounts when employees identify their employer, and stance on work-related topic discussion.

### Phase 4: Behavioral Guidelines & Practical Rules

Develop clear, actionable guidelines:
- Professional conduct standards online
- Confidentiality and proprietary information protection
- Media and influencer interaction protocols
- Customer complaint handling via social channels
- Guidance on political and controversial topics

Use scenario-based examples. Include both prohibited behaviors and encouraged positive social media use.

### Phase 5: Brand Voice & Official Representation

Create protocols for organizational voice:
- Official spokesperson designation
- Employee advocacy program parameters (if applicable)
- Crisis communication protocols
- Brand voice consistency guidelines
- Approval processes for official content

Clarify who manages official accounts and boundaries for employee advocacy.

### Phase 6: Consequences & Enforcement

Develop fair disciplinary frameworks:
- Violation categories (minor, moderate, severe)
- Progressive discipline approach
- Investigation and appeal procedures
- Training and rehabilitation options

Align with existing discipline policies. Balance accountability with opportunities for correction.

### Phase 7: Implementation & Training Strategy

Design the rollout plan:
- Communication strategy for policy launch
- Training program outline
- FAQ addressing common scenarios
- Monitoring, review, and annual update procedures
- Timeline and communication channels

### Phase 8: Final Policy Assembly

Compile all elements into a cohesive package:
- Complete policy document
- Executive summary for leadership
- Employee quick-reference guide
- Training presentation outline
- Implementation checklist
- Monitoring and measurement framework

## Output

Deliver the policy in structured phases, gathering necessary input at each stage. The final package should be legally sound, culturally aligned, and balance:
- Legal protection with employee trust
- Brand safety with authentic expression
- Clear boundaries with practical flexibility
- Comprehensive coverage with readability

Format the policy in clear sections with examples, ensuring employees can easily understand and follow the guidelines.
```
