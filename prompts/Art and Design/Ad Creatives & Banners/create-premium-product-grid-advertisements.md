# Premium Product Grid Advertisement Prompt

## 簡介

The Premium Product Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional 3×2 product photography grids across six equal landscape panels, designed for brands and marketers creating cohesive visual campaigns. This product grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces six coordinated scenes: a hero studio shot emphasizing craftsmanship, immersive indoor use, active urban lifestyle, close-up technology detail with functional overlay, macro comfort and build quality, and everyday portability context. Each panel follows a consistent visual language with controlled lighting, premium materials rendering, and modern minimalist composition. Use it to create website hero sections, social media carousels, e-commerce landing pages, and digital ad campaigns that require multiple product perspectives in a single asset. Reach for this prompt when you need commercial-quality product visuals that communicate features, lifestyle fit, and premium positioning without coordinating multiple photoshoots. ● Produces hero product shot, lifestyle usage, urban context, technology detail, comfort macro, and everyday companion scenes in unified visual style. ● Customizable by product name and color palette to match brand identity and campaign requirements. ● Outputs high-realism commercial photography aesthetic with soft studio lighting, natural daylight balance, and minimalist premium tech branding. ● Eliminates need for expensive studio sessions by generating all six product perspectives in a single render. ## Prompt

```
Create a premium 3×2 grid advertisement showcasing {{product-name}} across six equal landscape panels (3:2 aspect ratio).

**Panel 1 – Hero Product Shot**
Studio shot of {{product-name}} on minimalist surface. Soft directional studio light with subtle reflections. Neutral gradient background in {{color-palette}} primary tone. Emphasize premium materials, surface texture, logo clarity. Mood: precision, luxury, craftsmanship.

**Panel 2 – Immersive Use**
Adult using {{product-name}} in focused, immersive moment. Indoor modern living space or creative studio. Soft ambient lighting. Mood: immersion, calm, focus.

**Panel 3 – Active Urban Lifestyle**
Person moving through city environment wearing or carrying {{product-name}}. Natural daylight with urban reflections. Slight motion blur in background. Mood: freedom, confidence, everyday use.

**Panel 4 – Technology Detail**
Close-up of key functional feature with subtle graphic overlay visualizing the technology. Shallow depth of field, high precision focus on technology indicator and functional detail. Mood: technology, control, performance.

**Panel 5 – Comfort & Build**
Macro shot of comfort-critical surfaces and adjustable construction details. Showcase soft contact materials and premium build surfaces. Diffuse, even lighting. Mood: comfort, ergonomics, long-use ease.

**Panel 6 – Everyday Companion**
{{product-name}} placed in lifestyle context—inside bag, on desk, or at café table. Warm natural light. Mood: portability, lifestyle, versatility.

**Visual Style**: Premium tech advertising, minimalist, high realism, modern lifestyle, clean composition, professional commercial photography. Brand personality: modern, refined, technology-driven. Subtle logo presentation (embossed or minimal print). Soft studio light mixed with natural daylight, balanced exposure, high dynamic range, soft controlled shadows.

**Exclude**: Low resolution, plastic or cheap-looking materials, distorted proportions, unreadable branding, overexposed highlights, harsh reflections, cluttered backgrounds, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
