# Twitter Thread Generator From Long-Form Content

## 簡介

The Twitter Thread Generator From Long-Form Content is a free AI prompt that transforms articles, blog posts, and essays into compelling Twitter threads for content creators and social media strategists. This Twitter thread prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your original content to identify core arguments and stylistic patterns, then restructuring it into a numbered sequence of tweets that each stay under 280 characters. It adapts your message to Twitter's conversational format while maintaining your original voice, using proper formatting (line breaks, bullets, numbered points) and strategic cliffhangers that drive readers from one tweet to the next. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Content marketers use it to repurpose blog posts, newsletters, and LinkedIn articles into Twitter-native formats that maximize engagement and reach. Reach for this prompt when you have valuable long-form content that deserves a second life on Twitter, or when you need to distill complex ideas into bite-sized, shareable insights without losing substance or personality. ● Analyzes narrative flow and identifies natural break points that fit Twitter's 280-character limit ● Maintains tone, vocabulary level, and rhetorical style from the source material ● Applies formatting patterns from viral thread examples you provide as reference ● Creates hook endings and curiosity gaps that encourage readers to continue through the entire thread ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist specializing in content repurposing and Twitter audience engagement.

## Task
Transform the provided post into a compelling Twitter thread that breaks down key points into a sequence of tweets, each under 280 characters. Adapt the content to Twitter's conversational style while preserving the original voice and structure.

## Context
Great Twitter threads hook readers tweet-by-tweet with clear formatting (line breaks, listicles, numbered points), strategic cliffhangers that drive continuation, and tone-matched writing. Match the style, formatting patterns, and rhetorical devices shown in the viral example.

## Process
1. Analyze the original post: identify core arguments, narrative flow, and stylistic fingerprints (sentence rhythm, vocabulary level, rhetorical techniques)
2. Outline the thread structure: determine natural break points that fit 280-character limits while maintaining logical progression
3. Draft tweets with:
   - Proper formatting (line breaks for readability, bullets/numbers where appropriate)
   - Hook endings that create curiosity gaps
   - No emojis or hashtags unless present in the original post
   - Tone and vocabulary consistent with the source material

## Input
**Original post:**
{{post-content}}

**Viral thread example (for formatting reference):**
{{thread-example}}

## Output
Deliver the complete Twitter thread as a numbered sequence (1/n format), formatted exactly as shown in the example thread, maintaining the original post's voice throughout.
```
