# Co-Teaching Models Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Co-Teaching Models Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comparative research tables on collaborative teaching approaches for educators and instructional designers. This co-teaching models prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to identify 5-7 widely-recognized co-teaching approaches tailored to your specified education level - elementary, secondary, or higher education - and organize findings into a markdown table with your chosen columns. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering substantive analysis of each model's characteristics, implementation strategies, and benefits in a format designed for quick comparison. Teachers planning collaborative classrooms, instructional coaches evaluating partnership structures, and school administrators researching inclusive teaching methods use this prompt to build decision-ready references. Reach for this prompt when you need a side-by-side overview of co-teaching models rather than scattered research across multiple sources. ● Compares 5-7 established co-teaching models (One Teach One Assist, Station Teaching, Parallel Teaching, Team Teaching, and others) in one table ● Accepts custom table columns so you can focus on implementation steps, student outcomes, teacher roles, or resource requirements ● Tailors findings to your education level, accounting for developmental and instructional differences across grade bands ● Outputs clean markdown tables that paste directly into lesson plans, professional development materials, or research reports ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational researcher specializing in co-teaching models and collaborative learning environments.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive research on co-teaching approaches for {{education-level}} and present the findings in a structured markdown table. Identify the most prominent co-teaching models, analyze each model's key characteristics, implementation strategies, and potential benefits, then organize the information for easy comparison.

## Context
Co-teaching models vary significantly across education levels, and practitioners need clear, comparative overviews to select and implement appropriate approaches for their collaborative learning environments.

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with these columns: {{table-columns}}. Include 5-7 of the most widely-recognized co-teaching models as rows, ensuring each cell provides substantive, actionable information relevant to the column heading.
```
