# Statistical Analysis Guide Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Statistical Analysis Guide Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that creates customized statistical analysis guides for research students at any educational level. This statistical analysis guide prompt for ChatGPT walks through data collection methods, common statistical tests (t-tests, ANOVA, chi-square, regression, correlation), analysis procedures, and interpretation best practices. It structures content using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow from foundational concepts to practical application. The prompt adapts vocabulary, depth, and mathematical rigor to match the student's educational level and prior knowledge, making it effective for high school students conducting their first science fair project or graduate students tackling thesis research. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce guides with clear headers, worked examples, and step-by-step procedures. Reach for this prompt when you need to create instructional materials that bridge the gap between statistical theory and research practice, or when students need a reference that matches their current skill level. ● Covers the full research workflow from sampling strategies and data collection through test selection, execution, and interpretation ● Adapts technical depth and vocabulary to match educational level while maintaining statistical accuracy ● Includes decision criteria for choosing appropriate tests and common pitfalls to avoid when interpreting results ● Structures explanations using dependency grammar to build concepts logically and improve comprehension ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert statistician and educational content creator.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive statistical analysis guide structured using dependency grammar principles. The guide must be tailored to {{educational-level}} students conducting {{research-context}}.

## Content Requirements
Organize the guide to flow logically from foundational concepts to application:

- **Overview**: Introduce statistical analysis and its role in research
- **Data Collection**: Explain collection methods, sampling strategies, and their importance for validity
- **Statistical Tests**: Detail common tests (t-tests, ANOVA, chi-square, regression, correlation) with clear decision criteria for when each is appropriate
- **Analysis Procedures**: Provide step-by-step instructions for conducting each analysis, from data preparation through execution
- **Interpretation Best Practices**: Cover significance levels, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and common pitfalls
- **Practical Examples**: Include worked examples relevant to the research domain throughout each section

Adjust vocabulary, depth, and mathematical rigor to match the educational level. Address prior knowledge gaps as needed—assume {{prior-knowledge}} as the baseline.

## Output
Structure the guide with clear headers, subheaders, and bullet points for easy navigation. Use concrete examples and numbered steps for procedures. Ensure explanations are accessible to the target audience while maintaining statistical accuracy.
```
