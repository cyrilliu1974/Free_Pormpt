# Relationship-Building Skills Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Relationship-Building Skills Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates actionable networking plans structured around your specific industry, career stage, and professional challenges. This relationship-building prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar to show exactly how each tactic connects to larger goals - from high-level principles down to immediate, implementable steps you can take today. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. You provide your industry, career stage (entry-level, mid-career, senior, executive, or transition), networking goals, and biggest networking challenge. The AI builds a numbered strategy that covers both in-person and digital channels, explains how to initiate connections, sustain relationships over time, and overcome your stated obstacle. Every recommendation is structured to reveal its logical parent: you see why each action matters and how it fits into your broader professional objectives. This prompt is ideal for professionals who need a clear, hierarchy-driven networking roadmap rather than generic advice - whether you're entering a new industry, expanding your influence, or navigating a career transition. ● Tailors advice to your industry and career stage, from entry-level to executive. ● Structures every tactic using dependency grammar so you understand how actions connect to goals. ● Covers both digital platforms and in-person events with practical initiation and follow-up steps. ● Addresses your specific networking challenge with targeted solutions you can implement immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert networking strategist who designs actionable relationship-building plans for professionals.

## Task
Create a comprehensive networking strategy tailored to the user's industry and career context. Structure each recommendation using dependency grammar principles—every tactic must explicitly connect to its parent goal or principle, forming a clear hierarchy from objective down to specific action.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Career stage: {{career-stage}}
Networking goals: {{networking-goals}}
Biggest challenge: {{networking-challenge}}

Address both in-person and digital channels. Include guidance on initiating connections, sustaining relationships over time, and overcoming the stated challenge. Tailor advice to fit the career stage (entry-level, mid-career, senior, executive, or transition).

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list. For each item, show the dependency chain: state the overarching principle or goal, then indent subordinate tactics beneath it, making the logical linkage explicit. Prioritize practical, implementable steps the user can begin immediately.
```
