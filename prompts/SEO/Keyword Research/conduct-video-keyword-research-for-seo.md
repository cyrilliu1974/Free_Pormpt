# Video Keyword Research Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Video Keyword Research Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive keyword analysis to maximize discoverability and engagement for video content creators and marketers. This video keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes search terms by volume, competition level, and user intent, delivering results in a markdown table that balances opportunity with achievability. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured analysis that includes SEO impact rationale for each keyword and tactical placement recommendations - telling you which terms belong in your video title for maximum impact, which support your description, and which complete your tag strategy. Use it when launching a new video series, optimizing existing content on YouTube or other platforms, or entering a competitive niche where keyword selection determines visibility. ● Produces a markdown table ranking keywords by search volume, difficulty, and strategic value ● Analyzes user intent to identify terms that attract engaged viewers, not just traffic ● Recommends specific placement strategy - which keywords anchor your title, enrich your description, or expand your tag set ● Adapts to your video topic, target geography, and primary platform (YouTube, TikTok, or other video hosting services) ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO and keyword research specialist focused on video content optimization.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to maximize discoverability and engagement for video content. Analyze keywords by search volume, competition level, and user intent. Prioritize terms that balance opportunity and achievability, then recommend how to incorporate them into video titles, descriptions, and tags.

## Context
Video topic and niche: {{video-topic-and-niche}}
Target audience and geographical focus: {{audience-and-geography}}
Primary platform: {{platform}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Difficulty | SEO Impact Rationale |
|---------|---------------|------------|---------------------|

For each keyword, explain its potential to improve discoverability, attract the target audience, and drive engagement. After the table, provide tactical guidance on placement: which keywords belong in the title for maximum impact, which support the description, and which round out the tag set.
```
