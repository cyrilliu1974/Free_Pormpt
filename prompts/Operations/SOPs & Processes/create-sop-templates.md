# SOP Template Generator for Business Processes

## 簡介

The SOP Template Generator for Business Processes is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive Standard Operating Procedure documents for any business process and user audience. This SOP prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete nine-section document including introduction, definitions, responsibilities, step-by-step procedures, safety considerations, quality control measures, exception handling, references, and revision history tracking. You provide the business process context and intended users; the prompt structures a ready-to-deploy SOP with clear headings, bullet points, placeholders for flowcharts and diagrams, and plain-language instructions written at a Gunning Fog index of 8. Operations managers use it to standardize manufacturing workflows, HR teams build onboarding procedures, compliance officers document regulatory processes, and IT departments create system administration guides. Reach for this prompt when you need a professional SOP framework quickly, whether you are documenting an existing informal process or designing a new one from scratch. ● Defines roles and responsibilities explicitly to clarify accountability for each process step ● Includes dedicated sections for safety considerations and quality control to maintain standards ● Provides exception-handling guidance so users know how to respond to deviations ● Tracks revision history to maintain document version control and compliance audit trails ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Business Process Consultant specializing in Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) development.

## Task
Create a comprehensive SOP document that streamlines the specified process and ensures consistency across operations.

## Context
{{business-and-process}}

Target audience: {{intended-users}}

## Output
Deliver a structured SOP document with these sections:

**Introduction**
- State the purpose of the SOP
- Define scope and applicability

**Definitions and Acronyms**
- Explain technical terms and abbreviations used

**Responsibilities**
- List key roles and their specific duties

**Procedure**
- Provide step-by-step instructions
- Include placeholders for flowcharts where processes involve decision points or complexity

**Safety Considerations** (if applicable)
- Highlight hazards and precautions

**Quality Control Measures**
- Outline steps to ensure consistency and quality

**Exception Handling**
- Provide guidance for unexpected situations or deviations

**References and Related Documents**
- List supporting materials

**Revision History**
- Track document changes and updates

**Formatting requirements:**
- Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points
- Include placeholders for tables and diagrams where they aid understanding
- Write at a Gunning Fog index of 8: use simple, direct language; avoid adjectives, adverbs, and complex words unless necessary
- Tailor detail level to the intended users
- Emphasize critical steps and safety measures
- Align with industry best practices and regulatory requirements
```
