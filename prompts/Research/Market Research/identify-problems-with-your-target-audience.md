# Target Audience Problem Identification Prompt

## 簡介

The Target Audience Problem Identification Prompt is a free AI prompt that uncovers critical, monetizable problems faced by any defined target audience for solo entrepreneurs and one-person businesses. This target audience problem identification prompt for ChatGPT acts as a professional customer researcher, generating 10 specific problems ranked by severity (0–10) and required expertise (0–10). For each problem it surfaces two realistic solution paths - one no-code product and one content product - that a solo founder can build without months of development or years of specialized training. It prioritizes recurring, high-consequence issues and intentionally includes 50% non-mainstream opportunities that most market research overlooks. The output is a four-column table ready to guide product ideation, content planning, or service positioning. Use it when validating business ideas, exploring niche markets, or searching for hidden gaps your competitors have missed. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Returns 10 ranked problems with importance scores (0–10) and expertise-level assessments so you can prioritize realistically ● Surfaces 50% non-mainstream problems - hidden opportunities most competitor research and surveys miss ● Provides two solution paths per problem: a no-code product idea and a content product idea, both feasible for one person ● Outputs a clean four-column table (Problem, Importance, Expertise Required, Possible Solutions) you can drop straight into a business plan or pitch deck ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional customer researcher specializing in identifying monetizable problems for one-person businesses.

## Task
Generate 10 specific problems faced by the target audience below. Focus on opportunities that a solo entrepreneur can realistically address and monetize.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Criteria
- Prioritize critical, recurring problems with severe consequences if ignored
- Include 50% non-mainstream problems (hidden opportunities overlooked by most researchers)
- Solutions must be buildable by one person without months of development or years of specialized expertise
- Be specific and concise

## Output
Return a table with four columns:

1. **Problem** — the specific issue the audience faces
2. **Importance** — severity rating from 0-10 (10 = highest)
3. **Expertise Required** — skill level needed to solve it, 0-10 (10 = highest)
4. **Possible Solutions** — two options:
   - First: a no-code product solution
   - Second: a content product solution

Briefly describe each solution.
```
