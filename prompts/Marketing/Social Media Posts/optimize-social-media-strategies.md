# Social Media Strategy Audit for Local Businesses

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Audit for Local Businesses is a free AI prompt that evaluates current social media engagement tactics and delivers a structured action plan to grow local presence. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your existing platform usage, posting frequency, content types, and community management approach, then produces a detailed audit divided into five sections: an overview of current strategies, a list of what is working well, weaknesses that need attention, missed opportunities for engagement, and five tailored recommendations followed by a step-by-step local engagement growth plan. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for local businesses that want expert-level feedback without hiring a consultant. Use this prompt when you need to understand why your local social media efforts are underperforming or when you want to validate and improve what you are already doing. ● Conducts a full audit of social media platforms, posting habits, and engagement tactics ● Highlights strengths to build on and weaknesses to address ● Identifies untapped opportunities specific to local audience engagement ● Provides five actionable recommendations and a phased growth plan tailored to the business type ## Prompt
