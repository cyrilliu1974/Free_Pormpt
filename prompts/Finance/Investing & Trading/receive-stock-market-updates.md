# Daily Stock Market Update Briefing Prompt

## 簡介

The Daily Stock Market Update Briefing Prompt is a free AI prompt that compiles expert-level daily market analysis for financial advisors, traders, and investors who need actionable intelligence on market movements. This stock market analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to deliver a 500-word briefing covering major indices (Dow Jones, S&P 500, Nasdaq), sector performance, notable stock movements, economic and geopolitical factors, sentiment analysis using technical indicators and volatility signals, and a forward-looking outlook on upcoming trading sessions. You provide market context - date, major events, upcoming earnings, sentiment signals - and the prompt structures a professional briefing with clear sections on market overview, sector drivers, individual stock catalysts, broader economic influences, and investment perspective. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly synthesize daily market information into a client-ready format, prepare pre-market or post-market commentary, or maintain a consistent analytical framework across trading days. ● Structures updates around major indices, sector trends, and individual stock catalysts with context on earnings, regulatory news, and analyst actions ● Integrates economic data releases, central bank announcements, and geopolitical developments into market behavior analysis ● Analyzes sentiment using technical indicators, volatility indices like VIX, and investor positioning to gauge fear or optimism ● Provides forward-looking perspective referencing scheduled earnings, Fed meetings, and upcoming events that could move markets ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial advisor analyzing daily stock market performance for clients who need actionable intelligence on market movements and trends.

## Task
Compile a detailed yet concise daily stock market update (maximum 500 words) that provides both a snapshot of today's events and forward-looking perspective on implications for future trading sessions.

## Context
{{market-context}}

Include: the date of analysis, any major economic or geopolitical events influencing today's session, upcoming earnings reports or corporate announcements on the horizon, and your read on current market sentiment (bullish/bearish/cautious, technical indicators, volatility signals).

## Output
Structure the briefing with bullet points or short paragraphs covering:

**Market Overview**
Summarize overall performance of major indices (Dow Jones Industrial Average, S&P 500, Nasdaq). Note significant percentage changes or historical milestones reached.

**Sector Performance**
Highlight specific sectors or industries that outperformed or underperformed, with insights into possible drivers (e.g., commodity prices, policy changes, consumer trends).

**Notable Stock Movements**
Identify individual stocks with significant moves. Provide context: earnings reports, regulatory news, major contracts, analyst upgrades/downgrades, or shifts in investor sentiment.

**Economic & Geopolitical Factors**
Discuss broader events that influenced market behavior: central bank announcements, economic data releases (employment, inflation, GDP), or significant global developments.

**Market Sentiment Analysis**
Analyze current sentiment using technical indicators, investor surveys, or volatility indices (VIX). Address whether fear or optimism is driving decisions.

**Forward Outlook**
Forecast implications for upcoming sessions. Reference scheduled economic reports, earnings announcements, Federal Reserve meetings, or geopolitical developments that could move markets.

**Investment Perspective** (optional)
Offer a recommendation or caution for investors based on the day's analysis—areas of opportunity, risks to watch, or positioning advice.

Keep the tone informative and analytically rigorous while remaining accessible. Prioritize clarity and actionable insight.
```
