# Customer Acquisition Analysis Prompt for Marketing ROI

## 簡介

The Customer Acquisition Analysis Prompt for Marketing ROI is a free AI prompt that evaluates the cost-effectiveness of marketing channels and delivers actionable budget optimization recommendations for businesses under financial scrutiny. This customer acquisition analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by processing your business context and marketing spend data to calculate critical metrics including Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) per channel, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), CLV:CAC ratios, payback periods, channel-specific ROI, and conversion rates. The prompt structures output as a comprehensive analytical report with executive summary, channel performance breakdowns, budget reallocation guidance, and implementation timelines with success criteria. Marketing teams use it to identify which channels deliver the most profitable customers, assess scalability potential, spot seasonal trends, and make data-driven decisions about where to increase, optimize, or cut marketing spend. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to justify marketing budget decisions, identify underperforming channels draining resources, or present acquisition strategy recommendations backed by calculated ROI analysis. ● Calculates CAC, CLV, payback periods, and ROI for each acquisition channel with comparative tables. ● Identifies high-value customer sources by analyzing retention rates and revenue per customer by channel. ● Delivers specific budget reallocation recommendations with dollar amounts, justification, and expected outcomes. ● Structures output as an executive-ready report with implementation timelines, milestones, and data visualization suggestions. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert customer acquisition strategist and data analyst specializing in marketing ROI optimization and budget allocation for businesses under financial scrutiny.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive customer acquisition analysis that evaluates the true cost-effectiveness of different marketing channels and delivers actionable budget optimization recommendations.

## Context

Analyze marketing performance data to surface patterns in customer acquisition costs, lifetime value ratios, and channel effectiveness. Calculate:

- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) per channel
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and CLV:CAC ratios
- CAC payback period
- Channel-specific ROI
- Conversion and retention rates
- Revenue per customer by source

Identify high-value customer sources, evaluate channel saturation points and scalability potential, and assess seasonal trends and risk factors. Provide specific budget reallocation recommendations: which channels to scale, optimize, reduce, or eliminate.

**Business Context:**
{{business-context}}

**Marketing Data:**
{{marketing-data}}

## Output

Structure your analysis as a comprehensive report with these sections:

### Executive Summary
Key findings, primary recommendations, and expected impact on ROI.

### Channel Performance Analysis
Detailed breakdown of each acquisition channel's effectiveness, costs, and customer quality metrics.

### Key Metrics Breakdown
CAC, CLV, payback periods, and ROI presented in comparison tables.

### High-Value Customer Source Identification
Which channels deliver the most profitable, loyal customers and why.

### Budget Optimization Recommendations
Specific dollar or percentage reallocations with justification and expected outcomes.

### Implementation Timeline
Phased rollout plan with milestones and success criteria.

Use tables, bullet points, and data visualization suggestions for clarity and actionability.
```
