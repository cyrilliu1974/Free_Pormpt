# Direct Response Marketing Message Generator

## 簡介

The Direct Response Marketing Message Generator is a free AI prompt that creates targeted marketing copy designed to convert prospects into customers through benefit-driven messaging and clear calls-to-action. This direct response marketing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your target audience's needs, pain points, and demographics, then craft persuasive copy structured around proven conversion principles. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing complete marketing messages with attention-grabbing headlines, problem-aware introductions, benefit-focused body copy, and compelling calls-to-action. It incorporates your unique selling proposition and brand voice while applying dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and clarity. Use it to write landing page copy, email campaigns, ad text, or sales letters that speak directly to your audience's needs. This prompt is for marketers, business owners, copywriters, and growth teams who need conversion-focused messaging that resonates with specific audience segments. ● Structures messages with proven direct response elements: hook, problem acknowledgment, solution presentation, and clear call-to-action ● Incorporates audience demographics, psychographics, and pain points to create personalized copy that resonates ● Emphasizes unique selling propositions and concrete benefits rather than features ● Adapts tone and voice to match your brand personality, from professional to conversational to bold ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert direct response copywriter.

## Task
Write a personalized marketing message that drives immediate action. Analyze the audience's needs and pain points, then craft copy that demonstrates how the offering solves their specific problems.

## Context
**Target Audience & Offering:**
{{target-audience-and-offering}}
(Include: who they are, demographics/psychographics, key pain points, what you're selling, and how it addresses their needs)

**Unique Selling Proposition:**
{{usp}}

**Call-to-Action:**
{{cta}}

**Brand Voice:**
{{brand-voice}}
(e.g., professional, conversational, bold, empathetic)

## Output
Structure the marketing message with these sections:

**Headline:** Attention-grabbing, benefit-driven

**Introduction:** Hook the reader by acknowledging their pain point or desire

**Body:** Present the solution, emphasize the USP, use persuasive language and concrete benefits

**Call-to-Action:** Clear, compelling directive that tells the reader exactly what to do next

Keep copy engaging, concise, and tailored to resonate with the specific audience. Use dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow.
```
