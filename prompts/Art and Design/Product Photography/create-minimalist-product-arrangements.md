# Minimalist Product Arrangement Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Product Arrangement Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clean, top-down product photographs with neutral backdrops and professional knolling layouts for brands, designers, and marketers. This minimalist product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces ultra-wide-angle, high-resolution images with soft diffused lighting, warm earth tones, and ample negative space. You customize two variables - composition style (flat lay, grid knolling, scattered) and product collection (eco-friendly items, tech accessories, cosmetics) - and the prompt renders a 16:9 product shot with visible texture, crisp detail, and natural material grain. Use it for e-commerce hero images, brand lookbooks, social media content, or pitch decks that require a modern, editorial aesthetic. Reach for this prompt when you need polished product visuals without a physical studio setup or when prototyping packaging concepts and catalog layouts. ● Produces 16:9 ultra-wide-angle, top-down views with grid knolling or custom arrangement styles. ● Applies soft diffused lighting and warm earth tones for a natural, editorial look. ● Highlights material texture and craftsmanship with high-resolution detail and negative space. ● Accepts any product category - sustainable goods, tech, beauty, stationery, or food items. ## Prompt

```
Minimalist {{composition-style}} of {{product-collection}} arranged on a neutral backdrop; high-resolution product photograph; ultra-wide-angle, top-down view capturing the full scene; soft diffused lighting, crisp detail with visible texture, warm earth tones palette, grid knolling arrangement with ample negative space, highlighting natural material grain and sustainable craftsmanship --ar 16:9
```
