# Personalized Learning Plan Prompt for Gifted Children

## 簡介

The Personalized Learning Plan Prompt for Gifted Children is a free AI prompt that creates tailored homeschooling plans for educational consultants, parents, and educators working with gifted and talented students. This gifted education prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes a child's profile - age, grade level, abilities, interests, and learning style - then outputs evidence-based acceleration strategies, enrichment activities designed to prevent boredom, and a three-year talent development roadmap. Use it when designing individualized curricula that challenge advanced learners while supporting their social-emotional and holistic growth. The prompt structures output into clear sections: child profile summary, three acceleration strategies, three enrichment activities, boredom prevention methods, a milestone table spanning three grade levels, and recommendations for well-being across creative, physical, and emotional domains. ● Summarizes the child's abilities, domains of giftedness, interests, and preferred learning style into an actionable profile ● Recommends three evidence-based acceleration strategies specific to the child's readiness and strengths ● Suggests enrichment activities that challenge and engage without overwhelming or causing disengagement ● Provides a three-year talent development roadmap with grade-level milestones and focus areas ● Includes social-emotional, physical, and creative recommendations to support holistic development ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in gifted education, with deep knowledge of personalized learning plans that nurture the unique needs and talents of gifted children.

## Task
Create a comprehensive homeschooling plan tailored to the child's abilities, interests, and learning style. Focus on evidence-based acceleration strategies, enrichment activities, and boredom prevention while providing a clear roadmap for talent development.

## Context
{{child-profile}}

## Output
Organize your response in the following format:

**Child Profile:**
- Summarize the child's age, grade level, key abilities/domains of giftedness, main interests, and learning style

**Acceleration Strategies:**
1. Three evidence-based acceleration strategies, each with a brief description

**Enrichment Activities:**
- Three challenging activities designed to engage the gifted child, each with a brief description

**Boredom Prevention Methods:**
• Three methods to maintain engagement and prevent disengagement

**Talent Development Roadmap:**

| Grade Level | Key Focus Areas | Milestones |
|-------------|-----------------|------------|
| [Year 1] | | |
| [Year 2] | | |
| [Year 3] | | |

Create a three-year progression showing how to nurture the child's unique talents.

**Additional Recommendations:**
→ Three recommendations for supporting holistic development (social-emotional, physical, creative domains)

---

**Criteria:**
- Tailor all recommendations to the child's unique profile
- Avoid generic, one-size-fits-all approaches
- Prioritize evidence-based strategies
- Balance academic challenge with well-being
```
