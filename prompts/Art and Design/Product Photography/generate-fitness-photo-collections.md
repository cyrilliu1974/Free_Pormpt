# Fitness Photo Collection Generator for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Fitness Photo Collection Generator for AI Image Models is a free AI prompt that creates high-energy fitness and workout photography across any setting, time of day, or activity. This fitness photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and other AI image models produces realistic or stylized images of workouts, yoga, running, gym sessions, and outdoor training by combining photo type, subject, scene, camera medium, and aspect ratio into a single structured command. Whether you need sunrise beach yoga, nighttime urban runs, or gym action shots, the prompt adapts to your creative brief and delivers consistent results. It works by letting you specify the exact photo style (portrait, action shot, candid), the activity or athlete, the environment and lighting, the camera or film aesthetic, and the output dimensions - making it ideal for fitness brands, social media creators, personal trainers, and health-focused marketing campaigns. ● Control photo type, subject, scene, camera medium, and aspect ratio through five intuitive variables. ● Produce images that range from dawn beach workouts to studio gym sessions to nighttime city runs. ● Tailor camera aesthetics - film grain, DSLR sharpness, vintage Polaroid, or modern iPhone styles. ● Output in any aspect ratio to fit Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, website banners, or print materials. ## Prompt

```
{{photo-type}} of {{subject}}, {{scene}}, shot on {{camera-medium}} --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
