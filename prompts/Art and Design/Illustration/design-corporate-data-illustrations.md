# Corporate Data Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Corporate Data Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates flat vector editorial illustrations depicting stylized human figures working with abstract data elements for data-driven companies and analytics platforms. This corporate illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces clean vector-style visuals where geometric characters analyze, arrange, or point toward bar charts, line graphs, pie segments, dashboards, and floating data blocks. The output features bold solid colors - deep green, blue, yellow, soft pink, and neutral cream - on flat backgrounds with no gradients, shadows, or textures. It is ideal for technology websites, business reports, analytics dashboards, SaaS marketing materials, and any context where abstract data storytelling needs a polished, professional visual treatment. Reach for this prompt when you need intelligent, structured imagery that communicates information without photorealism or literal screen mockups. ● Produces stylized human figures with elongated limbs, simplified forms, and minimal facial features that convey professionalism without distraction. ● Integrates abstract data shapes - bar charts, line graphs, pie segments, floating blocks - naturally into layered compositions. ● Delivers bold flat vector aesthetics with clean edges, no gradients, and a curated palette of deep green, blue, yellow, soft pink, and cream. ● Outputs 1024×1024 or 16:9 illustrations suitable for web headers, blog posts, slide decks, and marketing pages. ## Prompt

```
Modern corporate editorial illustration representing {{concept}}. The scene shows {{scene-description}} in a symbolic, non-literal way.

Human figures are stylized and geometric, with elongated limbs and simplified forms. Characters analyze, arrange or point toward data elements such as bar charts, line graphs, pie segments, abstract dashboards and floating data blocks. Data shapes float around the figures and integrate naturally into the composition.

Flat vector illustration style with bold solid colors and clean edges. No gradients, no shadows, no textures. Color palette: deep green, blue, yellow, soft pink and neutral cream. Background is a single flat color.

Smooth vector shapes with consistent clean outlines. Abstract geometric proportions. Minimal facial features, neutral expressions. Layered and interconnected composition with focus on clarity and flow of information.

Overall feeling: intelligent, structured, forward-thinking and suitable for data-driven companies and analytics platforms.

Avoid: realistic data screens, photorealism, 3D elements, heavy shadows, gradients, neon colors, cluttered charts, childish cartoon style.

1024×1024, 16:9 aspect ratio.
```
