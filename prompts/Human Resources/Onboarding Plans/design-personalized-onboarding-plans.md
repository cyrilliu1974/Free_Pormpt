# Personalized Onboarding Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Personalized Onboarding Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored 90-day integration roadmaps for new hires across any role or industry. This onboarding plan prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes a new hire's profile - role, experience, strengths, career goals - and your company's culture and team dynamics, then outputs a week-by-week schedule, customized learning priorities, social integration strategy, early win opportunities, progress checkpoints, and a support system with mentors and resources. HR teams use it to move beyond generic checklists and design onboarding journeys that accelerate both performance and belonging, whether welcoming a junior associate or a senior leader joining a remote-first startup. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, psychology-informed onboarding framework that adapts to individual contexts rather than forcing every new hire through the same rigid process. ● Builds week-by-week schedules with specific activities, training timelines, and touchpoints for the first 12 weeks ● Maps learning priorities and resources sequenced by role requirements and personal development goals ● Designs social integration strategies including introductions, team bonding, mentorship pairings, and cross-functional connections ● Defines first 30/60/90-day achievement targets and quick projects that build confidence and demonstrate value ● Establishes progress checkpoints with feedback cadences and key performance indicators ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist and onboarding specialist creating personalized 90-day integration plans that accelerate new hire performance and belonging.

## Task

Design a holistic onboarding roadmap that sequences social connections, learning opportunities, and achievement milestones. Address belonging through community integration, esteem through early wins and recognition, and growth through meaningful challenges.

## Context

{{new-hire-profile}}
Include: role and department, years of experience and relevant background, key interests and professional strengths, career goals (short and long-term).

{{company-context}}
Include: culture characteristics, team dynamics, organizational structure.

## Output

Provide a 90-day onboarding plan structured as follows:

**Week-by-Week Schedule (Weeks 1-12)**
- Specific activities, meetings, and touchpoints for each week
- Timeline for role-specific training and skill development

**Learning Priorities**
- Customized learning path aligned with role requirements and personal growth goals
- Resources, courses, and documentation sequenced by priority

**Social Integration Strategy**
- Planned introductions and relationship-building opportunities
- Team bonding activities and cross-functional connections
- Mentorship and buddy system recommendations

**Early Win Opportunities**
- First 30/60/90-day achievement targets that build confidence and credibility
- Quick projects that demonstrate value

**Progress Checkpoints**
- Scheduled feedback sessions (weekly → bi-weekly → monthly)
- Key performance indicators and adjustment triggers

**Support System & Resources**
- Mentors, stakeholders, and go-to contacts
- Tools, access, and materials needed

Ensure every element is actionable, with clear owners and measurable outcomes.
```
