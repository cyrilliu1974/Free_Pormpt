# Knowledge Test Creator for Any Subject

## 簡介

The Knowledge Test Creator for Any Subject is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive assessments to measure depth and breadth of understanding in any topic. Whether you're an educator creating exams, a trainer designing certification tests, or a self-learner building practice quizzes, this knowledge test prompt for ChatGPT delivers a complete, professionally structured assessment package. The prompt produces 10 multiple-choice questions covering foundational theories, 5 short answer questions testing real-world application, and 3 essay questions requiring critical thinking and concept integration. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes a detailed answer key with explanations that functions as a learning tool, plus a scoring rubric for essays that evaluates clarity, depth of analysis, accuracy, and concept integration. Real use cases include college course exams, corporate training assessments, professional certification practice tests, and self-study validation. Reach for this prompt when you need a rigorous, multi-format test that goes beyond surface recall to assess true mastery and identify knowledge gaps for targeted improvement. ● Delivers 18 questions across three formats to assess recall, application, and critical thinking ● Includes comprehensive answer explanations that serve as study aids, not just solutions ● Provides essay scoring rubrics with specific criteria and point allocations for objective grading ● Outputs administration guidelines covering environment, format, and time allocation recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Learning Assistant specializing in assessment design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive knowledge test that measures depth and breadth of understanding in {{topic}}. The test should accurately assess mastery, identify strengths, and pinpoint knowledge gaps to guide further study.

## Test Structure

**1. Multiple-Choice Questions (10 questions)**
- Cover foundational theories and concepts of {{topic}}
- Include four options per question with one correct answer
- Assess both recall and application of knowledge

**2. Short Answer Questions (5 questions)**
- Require concise responses testing understanding of key terms, processes, and real-world application
- Focus on practical scenarios relevant to {{topic}}

**3. Essay Questions (3 questions)**
- Encourage critical thinking and integration of multiple concepts
- Allow demonstration of in-depth understanding and clear articulation of complex ideas

**4. Answer Key**
- Provide comprehensive answers and explanations for all question types
- Include reasoning behind correct answers and how concepts apply
- Design the key as a learning tool, not just a solution sheet

**5. Scoring Rubric for Essays**
- Detail criteria: clarity of argument, depth of analysis, accuracy of information, integration of concepts
- Assign point values to each criterion for transparent evaluation

**6. Administration Guidelines**
- Recommend a quiet, comfortable environment
- Suggest closed-book format for accurate knowledge assessment
- Provide appropriate time allocation

## Output
Deliver a complete test document with all question types, detailed answer key with explanations, and essay scoring rubric. Ensure questions are clear, objective, and aligned with current field standards.
```
