# Content Tone & Audience Rewriter

## 簡介

The Content Tone & Audience Rewriter is a free AI prompt that adapts existing text to resonate with a specific target audience while preserving the original message. This content rewriting prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your source material, identify key points, and restructure the text using dependency grammar principles to match your desired tone and reader preferences. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, handling everything from technical documentation adapted for beginners to casual blog posts rewritten for executive audiences. The prompt systematically adjusts vocabulary, sentence structure, and stylistic choices to align with your audience's knowledge level and expectations while maintaining coherent flow and engagement. This tool is ideal for content marketers, editors, copywriters, and communication professionals who need to repurpose content across different channels, demographics, or brand voices without starting from scratch. ● Extracts core message and key points before rewriting to ensure nothing essential is lost ● Applies dependency grammar framework for clear sentence structure and natural readability ● Adapts vocabulary and phrasing based on audience knowledge level and preferences ● Maintains coherence and logical flow while transforming tone from formal to casual, technical to accessible, or any other style shift ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content writer and editor specializing in audience adaptation and tone adjustment.

## Task
Rewrite the provided content to resonate with the target audience while preserving the core message. Adjust vocabulary, sentence structure, and tone to match the specified style and reader preferences. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and natural flow.

## Context
{{content-to-rewrite}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Desired tone:** {{desired-tone}}

**Additional context:** {{context}}

## Process
1. Extract the key points and core message from the original content
2. Identify the characteristics, knowledge level, and preferences of the target audience
3. Restructure sentences for optimal readability and impact
4. Adapt vocabulary and phrasing to align with the audience and tone
5. Ensure coherence and natural flow throughout
6. Refine for maximum engagement

## Output
Provide the rewritten content in clear paragraph format, maintaining the structure and flow of the original while fully adapted to the specified tone and audience.
```
