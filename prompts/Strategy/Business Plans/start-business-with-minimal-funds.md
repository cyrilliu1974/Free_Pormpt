# Bootstrap Business Strategy Planner With Minimal Budget

## 簡介

The Bootstrap Business Strategy Planner With Minimal Budget is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed, resource-constrained startup roadmap for solopreneurs and founders launching businesses with limited capital. This business strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes five inputs (industry, initial budget, primary skills, target market, and location) and produces a comprehensive markdown table covering market research, product or service development, marketing tactics, financial management, resource allocation, cost-cutting measures, and revenue generation strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing free and low-cost tools at every stage. Each recommendation is grounded in bootstrapping best practices and tailored to the constraints you specify, so you get actionable steps that match your real-world situation instead of generic advice. Reach for this prompt when you need a concrete launch plan but lack venture funding, when you want to validate an idea before committing significant capital, or when you are ready to turn a side-project into a sustainable business on a shoestring budget. ● Outputs a structured three-column table (Steps, Description, Resources) for easy implementation ● Prioritizes free and low-cost platforms, tools, and methods to keep cash outlay near zero ● Covers end-to-end launch phases from market validation through early revenue generation ● Tailors every recommendation to the specific industry, geographic location, and founder skill set you provide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in bootstrapped startups and resource-constrained business launches.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step strategy for starting a business with minimal funds. Cover market research, product/service development, marketing, financial management, resource allocation, cost-cutting measures, and revenue generation strategies. Each step must be detailed, actionable, and realistic for a solopreneur operating under tight budget constraints.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Initial budget: {{initial-budget}}
Founder's primary skills: {{primary-skills}}
Target market: {{target-market}}
Location: {{location}}

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Steps**: Sequential action items numbered in logical order
- **Description**: Clear, actionable guidance for each step (2-4 sentences)
- **Resources**: Specific tools, platforms, or methods needed (prioritize free/low-cost options)

Ensure the strategy is tailored to the specific industry, budget level, available skills, target market, and location provided. Focus on bootstrapping techniques that maximize impact while minimizing cash outlay.
```
