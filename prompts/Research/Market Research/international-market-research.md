# International Market Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The International Market Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive market entry analysis for businesses evaluating expansion into new geographic regions. This international market research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a five-factor framework covering cultural dynamics (language, customs, consumer behavior), legal requirements (regulations, IP laws, compliance), economic indicators (market size, competition, purchasing power), technological readiness (infrastructure, adoption rates), and political climate (stability, government policies). You provide your product or service and target markets as variables, and the model delivers a structured report with executive summary, market overview, detailed factor analysis for each region, identified opportunities and challenges, and prioritized recommendations for market entry strategy. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and explicitly requires source citations for all research data. This prompt is built for product managers, business development teams, and entrepreneurs assessing international expansion opportunities who need systematic, multi-dimensional market intelligence. ● Analyzes five critical dimensions - cultural, legal, economic, technological, and political - for each target market ● Produces executive summary, detailed factor breakdowns, and region-specific opportunities and risks ● Delivers prioritized market entry strategies with localization approaches and partnership recommendations ● Requires full source attribution for every data point, ensuring research transparency and credibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher conducting comprehensive international market analysis.

## Task
Produce a detailed international market research report for {{product-or-service}} covering {{target-markets}}. Analyze cultural, legal, economic, technological, and political factors that impact market entry and operations in each region.

## Analysis Framework
For each target market, evaluate:

**Cultural factors**: Language, customs, values, and preferences; how these influence consumer behavior and product acceptance.

**Legal factors**: Regulations, trade barriers, intellectual property laws, compliance requirements affecting market entry and operations.

**Economic factors**: Market size, growth potential, competition landscape, consumer purchasing power, economic stability.

**Technological factors**: Digital infrastructure, technology adoption rates, readiness levels impacting product delivery and customer engagement.

**Political factors**: Political stability, government policies, international relations influencing market viability and long-term success.

Gather data from reliable sources: government databases, industry reports, market research firms, and local experts.

## Output
Deliver a structured report with these sections:

1. **Executive Summary**: Brief overview of key findings and recommendations
2. **Market Overview**: General characteristics and trends of each potential market
3. **Factor Analysis**: Detailed findings for cultural, legal, economic, technological, and political factors in each market
4. **Opportunities and Challenges**: Specific opportunities and risks for market entry based on the analysis
5. **Recommendations**: Prioritized market entry strategy, localization approaches, and potential partnerships or collaborations tailored to the product's unique value proposition

**Critical requirement**: Cite every source used in your research with full attribution.
```
