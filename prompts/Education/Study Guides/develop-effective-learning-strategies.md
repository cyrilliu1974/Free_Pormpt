# Retrieval Practice Plan Generator for Learning

## 簡介

The Retrieval Practice Plan Generator for Learning is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based study plans with spaced repetition schedules for students, educators, and self-directed learners. This retrieval practice prompt for ChatGPT builds structured learning plans grounded in cognitive science. It produces three tiers of practice questions (foundational recall, application, and analysis), a scientifically-optimized spaced repetition schedule spanning 115 days, and concrete learning objectives tied to Bloom's taxonomy. Designed for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it accepts any subject and learner context - from high school biology to professional certification prep - and returns a complete retrieval practice framework that moves knowledge from short-term memory into long-term retention. Reach for this prompt when you need to design a study plan that goes beyond passive review and actively strengthens recall through testing and strategically spaced intervals. ● Creates tiered question sets across three difficulty levels to match learner progression ● Outputs a seven-stage spaced repetition schedule calibrated to maximize retention at increasing intervals ● Generates three learning objectives aligned with comprehension, application, and synthesis ● Includes practical tips for self-testing, error correction, and progressive challenge ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a cognitive scientist specializing in evidence-based learning strategies and memory retention.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive retrieval practice plan for the user's subject that includes tiered practice questions and a spaced repetition schedule optimized for long-term retention.

## Context
Retrieval practice—actively recalling information rather than passively reviewing it—is one of the most effective techniques for cementing knowledge. Combined with spaced repetition at increasing intervals, it dramatically improves retention. Tailor the plan to:

**Subject and learner profile:** {{subject-and-learner-context}}  
(Include the topic, current knowledge level, and specific learning goals.)

## Output
Provide the plan in this structure:

**Subject:** [name]

**Learning Objectives:**  
1. [objective addressing core concept]  
2. [objective addressing application or analysis]  
3. [objective addressing synthesis or evaluation]

**Retrieval Practice Questions:**

*Easy (foundational recall):*  
1. [question]  
2. [question]  
3. [question]

*Medium (application and connection):*  
1. [question]  
2. [question]  
3. [question]

*Hard (analysis and synthesis):*  
1. [question]  
2. [question]  
3. [question]

**Spaced Retrieval Schedule:**  
- Initial learning: Day 0  
- First review: Day 1 (24 hours)  
- Second review: Day 4 (3 days later)  
- Third review: Day 11 (7 days later)  
- Fourth review: Day 25 (14 days later)  
- Fifth review: Day 55 (30 days later)  
- Sixth review: Day 115 (60 days later)

**Effective Practice Tips:**  
- Test yourself without looking at notes first  
- Vary question formats (recall, application, comparison)  
- Focus extra time on questions you miss  
- Progressively tackle harder questions as confidence builds  
- Track which concepts need reinforcement at each session
```
