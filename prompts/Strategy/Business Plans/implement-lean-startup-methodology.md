# Lean Startup Implementation Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Lean Startup Implementation Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates actionable Lean Startup strategies for entrepreneurs and business owners using cost-effective bootstrapping methods. This Lean Startup prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table mapping specific bootstrapping techniques to execution steps and measurable business outcomes. You specify your business type and the bootstrapping techniques you want to incorporate, and the prompt generates a three-column implementation guide covering each technique's execution method, actionable steps, and concrete benefits. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for founders who need to validate business ideas without significant capital investment. The output focuses on cause-and-effect relationships between each technique and its results, ensuring every recommendation is grounded in practical application. ● Outputs a structured markdown table with technique names, execution instructions, and specific benefits for immediate implementation ● Tailors recommendations to your business type and preferred bootstrapping methods for relevant, actionable strategies ● Emphasizes minimal capital techniques that maximize validation and learning, perfect for resource-constrained startups ● Connects each method to measurable outcomes so you can track progress and pivot based on data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Lean Startup expert designing a practical implementation plan for a business using bootstrapping techniques.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Lean Startup implementation strategy that validates business ideas, minimizes waste, and maximizes learning through cost-effective methods. Present the plan as a structured guide the business owner can immediately act on.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}

Bootstrapping techniques to incorporate: {{bootstrapping-techniques}}

Focus on techniques that require minimal capital investment while delivering maximum validation and learning. Each method should demonstrate clear cause-and-effect relationships between the technique, its execution, and measurable business outcomes.

## Output
Deliver your implementation plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Technique | Description | Benefits |
|-----------|-------------|----------|

For each bootstrapping technique:
- **Technique**: Name the method clearly
- **Description**: Explain how to execute it in 2-3 sentences, with specific actions
- **Benefits**: List 2-4 concrete outcomes this technique delivers for the business

Ensure descriptions are actionable and benefits are specific to the business type provided.
```
