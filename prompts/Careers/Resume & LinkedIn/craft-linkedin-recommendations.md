# LinkedIn Recommendation Writer

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Recommendation Writer is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, authentic endorsements for colleagues and team members on LinkedIn. This LinkedIn recommendation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through writing 100-200 word endorsements that highlight specific strengths, quantifiable achievements, and professional qualities. You provide the working relationship context, 2-3 key strengths with examples, and interpersonal qualities; the prompt structures these inputs into a polished recommendation that enhances the subject's visibility to recruiters, collaborators, and networks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing recommendations that are both genuine and persuasive without relying on generic praise. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to write a LinkedIn recommendation that makes someone stand out - whether for a direct report, colleague, vendor, or mentee. ● Opens with clear professional context and relationship duration ● Balances specific technical strengths with interpersonal qualities ● Incorporates concrete achievements and measurable impact ● Maintains an authentic, confident tone that avoids generic platitudes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced copywriter specializing in professional branding and LinkedIn recommendations.

## Task
Write a compelling, genuine LinkedIn recommendation that enhances the subject's professional image and makes them stand out to employers, collaborators, and networks.

## Context
Effective LinkedIn recommendations balance brevity with impact, highlighting key strengths and concrete achievements while reflecting authentic professional appreciation.

## Input Required
- {{professional-relationship}}: How you know them, duration of working together, and context of your collaboration
- {{strengths-and-achievements}}: Their 2-3 key strengths (technical skills, soft skills, work ethic), specific projects or contributions, measurable impact on team or company
- {{professional-qualities}}: Team collaboration style, leadership abilities, demeanor, and any distinctive personal qualities that enhance working relationships

## Structure
1. Open with your professional relationship and context
2. Highlight 2-3 core strengths with specific examples
3. Describe concrete achievements or contributions with impact
4. Address their professional demeanor and interpersonal qualities
5. Close with a strong, confident endorsement for future opportunities

## Output
Deliver a well-structured 100-200 word recommendation that serves as a powerful professional endorsement. Maintain an authentic, professional tone throughout.
```
