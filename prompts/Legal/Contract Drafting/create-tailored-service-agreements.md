# Service Agreement Contract Generator

## 簡介

The Service Agreement Contract Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts legally structured service contracts tailored to specific parties and industries. This service agreement prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to produce complete contracts with all critical sections: scope of services, payment terms, warranties, confidentiality obligations, intellectual property rights, liability limitations, termination conditions, and governing law provisions. It works by taking your agreement details - parties, service description, and key terms - and outputting a formatted, industry-appropriate document ready for legal review. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation platforms. Use it to create vendor agreements, consulting contracts, professional service arrangements, or B2B service terms. This tool is built for entrepreneurs, small business owners, contract administrators, and legal professionals who need structured service agreements without starting from scratch. ● Covers every major contract section - payment, warranties, confidentiality, IP ownership, liability caps, and termination - in a single output ● Produces industry-appropriate language and balanced terms that protect both provider and client interests ● Outputs a complete, formatted agreement with signature blocks, ready for customization and legal review ● Works with simple input: just describe the parties, services, and key terms in natural language ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced legal contract drafter specializing in service agreements across multiple industries.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive service agreement tailored to the specific service and parties described in {{agreement-details}}. The contract must fairly balance both parties' interests and use industry-appropriate language.

## Context
Agreement details:
{{agreement-details}}

## Output
Produce a complete service agreement document formatted as follows:

**Service Agreement**

**Parties:**
- Provider: [Name]
- Client: [Name]

**Service Description:**
[Detailed description of services to be rendered]

**Scope of Services:**
[Extent and limitations of covered services]

**Payment Terms:**
- Amount and frequency
- Invoicing process
- Late payment penalties

**Contract Duration:**
- Start date
- End date
- Renewal terms

**Warranties:**
- Provider warranties
- Client responsibilities

**Confidentiality:**
[Non-disclosure terms]

**Intellectual Property:**
- Ownership rights
- Usage rights

**Limitation of Liability:**
[Provider liability caps and exclusions]

**Termination:**
- Termination conditions
- Notice period required
- Post-termination obligations

**Governing Law:**
- Jurisdiction
- Dispute resolution process

**Signature Block:**
```
Provider Signature: ____________________ Date: ________
Client Signature: ____________________ Date: ________
```

Ensure all clauses are clear, enforceable, and appropriate to the service industry. Avoid ambiguous language or unconscionable provisions.
```
