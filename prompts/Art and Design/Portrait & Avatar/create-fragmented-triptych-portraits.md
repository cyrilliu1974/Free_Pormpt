# Fragmented Triptych Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Fragmented Triptych Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates striking three-panel portraits with intentional misalignment and layered focus effects for artists, photographers, and visual designers. This fragmented triptych portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana splits a close-cropped face into three horizontal strips: the top strip renders sharp, direct-gaze eyes; the middle strip applies heavy motion blur that smears facial contours; and the bottom strip shows lips and chin in soft focus. The strips are deliberately misaligned to create visual tension and a disjointed, modern aesthetic. Designers use it for editorial photography, album covers, brand campaigns, and gallery art that demands attention through controlled distortion. Reach for this prompt when you need unconventional portrait compositions that convey emotion and movement without traditional photo editing software. ● Produces three horizontal strips with distinct focus zones - sharp eyes, motion-blurred mid-face, and soft lower features - for dynamic emotional impact. ● Creates imperfect alignment between strips to generate visual tension and modern, editorial aesthetics. ● Supports full customization of subject and aesthetic style, from minimalist monochrome to vibrant surrealism. ● Frames subjects in extreme close-up to fill the entire canvas and maximize dramatic effect. ## Prompt

```
Fragmented triptych portrait in three horizontal strips, imperfectly aligned. Top strip: sharp focus on {{subject}}'s eyes looking directly at viewer. Middle strip: heavy motion blur with smeared facial contours. Bottom strip: soft focus on lips and chin. Disjointed {{aesthetic-style}} style. Subject framed extremely close, filling the entire frame.
```
