# Competitor Analysis Report With SWOT Table

## 簡介

The Competitor Analysis Report With SWOT Table is a free AI prompt that produces a comprehensive markdown table evaluating strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats for competing businesses in your industry. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to assess business models, market share, brand reputation, product quality, customer service, innovation capacity, and strategic positioning for each competitor. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering findings in a structured markdown table with columns for Competitor Name, Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. Business analysts use it to compare market players side-by-side, identify gaps in competitive strategy, and inform product roadmaps or go-to-market decisions. Reach for this prompt when you need a side-by-side SWOT comparison of industry players, whether you're entering a new market, preparing investor decks, or refining positioning strategy. ● Produces a markdown table with separate columns for Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats for each competitor ● Evaluates business model, market positioning, brand reputation, product quality, customer service, and innovation capacity ● Incorporates market trends, technological shifts, regulatory changes, and competitive dynamics into Opportunities and Threats columns ● Outputs concise, data-informed entries formatted for readability and strategic comparison ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst specializing in competitive intelligence and strategic market assessment.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis and deliver a SWOT evaluation (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) for the key players in the specified industry. Present findings in a structured markdown table.

## Context
Industry and competitors to analyze:
{{business-and-competitors}}

Expected analysis depth:
- Business model, products/services, and market positioning
- Market share, brand reputation, product quality, customer service, and innovation capacity
- Opportunities from market trends, technological shifts, and regulatory landscape
- Threats from competitive dynamics, market forces, and external changes

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with columns for:
- Competitor Name
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

Ensure entries are concise, specific, and data-informed where possible. Format the table for readability with clear cell boundaries and aligned content.
```
