# Architectural Design Concept Image Generator

## 簡介

The Architectural Design Concept Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates architectural visualization images by specifying building type, setting, and reference style for designers and architects. This architectural design prompt for Midjourney takes three variables - building type (residential, commercial, civic), setting (urban, rural, coastal), and reference style (modernist, brutalist, or a specific architect's aesthetic) - and renders concept images in 16:9 aspect ratio. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other text-to-image models that support style parameters. Use it for client presentations, portfolio pieces, design exploration, or mood boards when you need a quick architectural visual that reflects a particular design language. Reach for this prompt when you need to visualize a building concept before drafting, compare stylistic approaches side-by-side, or communicate design intent to stakeholders without producing full CAD drawings. ● Specify any building type - from single-family homes and office towers to museums and transit hubs - and place it in the environment that matters. ● Reference historical or contemporary architectural styles by name or invoke the design language of specific architects to guide the aesthetic. ● Output 16:9 images suitable for presentation decks, design reviews, and portfolio documentation. ● Works across Midjourney versions, Flux, and other image models that accept style and aspect-ratio parameters. ## Prompt

```
{{building-type}} in {{setting}}, architectural style of {{reference-style}} --ar 16:9
```
