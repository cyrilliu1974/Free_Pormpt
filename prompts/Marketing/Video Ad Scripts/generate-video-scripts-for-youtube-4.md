# Video Script Generator for Social Media Marketing

## 簡介

The Video Script Generator for Social Media Marketing is a free AI prompt that writes structured video scripts designed to promote products or services and achieve specific marketing goals across social platforms. This video script prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and maximum engagement. It produces complete scripts divided into three sections: shot-by-shot visual descriptions, audio narration with music cues, and on-screen text overlays including captions and calls-to-action. The prompt accounts for platform-specific constraints like video length, aspect ratio, and caption conventions on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and other channels. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Real use cases include product launches, brand awareness campaigns, lead generation videos, and promotional content tailored to audience attention patterns and brand voice. Marketing teams, content creators, social media managers, and video producers reach for this prompt when they need ready-to-shoot scripts that hook viewers in the first three seconds and drive measurable action. ● Structures scripts into visuals, audio, and on-screen text sections for clear production handoff ● Applies dependency grammar framework to maintain logical narrative flow and coherence ● Adapts to platform-specific video length, format, and audience attention span requirements ● Generates hooks engineered to capture attention within the first three seconds ● Aligns script language, tone, and calls-to-action with your brand voice and marketing objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video script writer specializing in social media marketing content.

## Task
Write a compelling video script that promotes {{product-service}} and achieves {{marketing-goal}} on {{platform}}. Structure the script using dependency grammar principles to ensure clear, logical flow and maximum engagement.

## Context
Target audience: {{audience}}
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Account for platform-specific constraints (video length, aspect ratio, caption norms) and audience attention patterns. Ensure the hook grabs attention in the first 3 seconds, the body delivers value clearly, and the close drives action aligned with the marketing goal.

## Output
Deliver the script in this format:

**VISUALS:**
- [Shot-by-shot description of what appears on screen]

**AUDIO:**
- [Voiceover, dialogue, or narration text]
- [Music/sound effect cues]

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
- [Graphics, captions, CTAs that appear as overlays]

Use bullet points within each section. Keep language tight and scannable.
```
