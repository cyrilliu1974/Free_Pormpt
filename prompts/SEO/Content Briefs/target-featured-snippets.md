# Featured Snippet Target Blog Post Generator

## 簡介

The Featured Snippet Target Blog Post Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, search-optimized articles designed to win position zero in search engine results for content strategists and SEO professionals. This featured snippet prompt for ChatGPT analyzes competitor content, identifies gaps, and produces complete blog posts with optimal heading hierarchy, scannable formatting, and a concise introductory paragraph tailored for snippet extraction. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating the clear causal relationships search engines favor. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering markdown-formatted articles with table of contents, bullet points, and natural keyword integration that balances reader value with technical optimization. Use it when you need to outrank existing articles on a specific keyword or when search intent demands the authoritative, direct answers that earn featured snippets. ● Analyzes competitor URLs to identify content gaps and opportunities for superior positioning ● Structures output with heading hierarchy, short paragraphs, and scannable lists optimized for extraction ● Generates a 40-60 word intro paragraph that directly answers the query in featured-snippet format ● Applies dependency grammar framework to create logical sentence-to-sentence flow throughout the article ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO content strategist optimizing blog posts to capture featured snippets in search results.

## Task
Write a comprehensive, well-structured blog post targeting the featured snippet for {{target-keyword}}. Use dependency grammar principles to create logical connections between ideas—each sentence should build meaningfully on the previous one, creating clear causal and hierarchical relationships throughout the article.

## Context
- **Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
- **Industry/niche:** {{industry-niche}}
- **Word count:** {{word-count}}
- **Competing articles to outperform:** {{competitor-urls}}

Analyze the competitor URLs to identify content gaps, angles they missed, and opportunities to provide superior value.

## Output
Deliver the article with:

- **Table of contents** at the beginning, linking to all major sections
- **Clear heading hierarchy** (H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections)
- **Concise introductory paragraph** (40-60 words) that directly answers the main query—optimized for featured snippet extraction
- **Bullet points and numbered lists** where appropriate for scannability
- **Short paragraphs** (2-4 sentences) for readability
- **Natural keyword integration** without over-optimization
- **Substantive, accurate information** that demonstrates expertise and satisfies search intent

Format the entire article in markdown.
```
