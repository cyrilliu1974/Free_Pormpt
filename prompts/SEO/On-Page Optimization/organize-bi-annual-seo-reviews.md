# Bi-Annual SEO Review Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Bi-Annual SEO Review Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive two-period assessment framework for off-page SEO performance tracking and optimization. This SEO review prompt for ChatGPT creates a detailed six-month planning structure across two consecutive review periods, specifying measurable milestones, tracking KPIs, data analysis techniques, and conditional tactical adjustments tied directly to performance data. It accepts your existing off-page SEO strategies and current KPI tools as inputs, then outputs a complete review calendar with three milestones per period, three trackable KPIs, two analysis methods, and two if-then adjustment rules - all formatted with an executive summary and detailed performance review sections. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for SEO strategists conducting periodic audits of link-building campaigns, content distribution efforts, brand mentions, and referral traffic initiatives. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable, time-bound framework to assess off-page SEO tactics every six months and document the logic behind strategic pivots. ● Outputs two distinct six-month review periods with start and end dates, milestones, and KPIs tailored to your off-page strategies ● Includes conditional tactical adjustments in if-then format, linking performance thresholds to specific optimization actions ● Provides an executive summary and detailed performance review template for stakeholder reporting ● Ensures all milestones are time-bound and all KPIs are quantifiable, eliminating vague goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist and analyst specializing in off-page SEO performance assessment.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive bi-annual review plan for assessing off-page SEO performance. The plan must cover two distinct six-month review periods with specific milestones, KPIs, data analysis methods, and tactical adjustments based on performance insights.

## Context
Off-page SEO strategies: {{off-page-seo-strategies}}
Current KPIs and data analysis tools: {{current-kpis-and-tools}}

## Output
Structure the review plan as follows:

**Review Period 1:** [Start Date] – [End Date]
- **Milestones:** Three specific achievements or goals to reach during this period
- **KPIs to Track:** Three measurable key performance indicators relevant to the off-page strategies
- **Data Analysis Methods:** Two robust techniques to analyze data and derive actionable insights
- **Tactical Adjustments:** Two conditional statements (If [condition], then [adjustment]) based on performance

**Review Period 2:** [Start Date] – [End Date]
- **Milestones:** Three specific achievements building on Period 1 progress
- **KPIs to Track:** Three measurable indicators (may evolve from Period 1)
- **Data Analysis Methods:** Two analysis techniques appropriate for the period
- **Tactical Adjustments:** Two conditional optimization strategies

**Reporting Format:**
- **Executive Summary:** Key findings and strategic recommendations
- **Detailed Performance Review:** In-depth KPI analysis with insights
- **Tactical Adjustments:** Planned changes with expected impact quantification

Ensure all KPIs are measurable, milestones are time-bound, and tactical adjustments are directly linked to performance data.
```
