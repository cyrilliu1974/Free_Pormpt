# Dog Photography Wind and Motion Prompt

## 簡介

The Dog Photography Wind and Motion Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic product photography of dogs in dynamic moving-vehicle scenes with professional lighting and motion effects. This dog photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images of dogs leaning out of vehicle windows on coastal roads, capturing wind-swept fur, joyful expressions, and golden hour side-lighting with warm color grading and teal shadows. The prompt specifies technical camera parameters including 85mm lens focal length, shallow depth of field at f/2.8, and motion blur on backgrounds to create professional-quality product photography.
