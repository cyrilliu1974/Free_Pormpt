# Lead Magnet Brainstorming Prompt for Online Businesses

## 簡介

The Lead Magnet Brainstorming Prompt for Online Businesses is a free AI prompt that generates original, high-converting opt-in asset concepts tailored to your niche, audience pain points, and paid product suite. This lead magnet prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context - including your ideal customer profile, pain points, desires, and paid offerings - then brainstorming 3–5 unique lead magnet concepts that address specific subscriber needs while naturally leading into your paid products. Each concept includes an eye-catching title, content breakdown, delivery format (PDF, video, or email course), and a detailed explanation of why it matches your ideal customer profile. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text AI platforms. Use it when launching a new funnel, refreshing your opt-in strategy, or looking to stand out in a crowded market with lead magnets that showcase your unique expertise rather than generic checklists. ● Produces 3–5 complete lead magnet concepts with titles, content breakdowns, and format recommendations in a single generation ● Ensures each concept addresses at least two pain points and two desires from your ideal subscriber profile ● Maps the natural bridge between free opt-in value and your paid product suite to improve funnel conversion ● Emphasizes quick-to-consume formats that deliver immediate actionable value and build authority ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a lead magnet strategist helping an online business owner design high-converting opt-in assets that attract qualified prospects and naturally lead into paid offers.

## Task
Brainstorm 3–5 lead magnet concepts tailored to the business context below. Each concept must be original, highly relevant to the niche, and showcase the business owner's expertise while standing out from generic market offerings.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Each lead magnet should:
- Address at least 2 pain points and 2 desires from the ideal subscriber profile above
- Seamlessly flow into the paid offer(s)
- Be quick to consume and deliver immediate, actionable value
- Highlight the business owner's unique approach and build authority
- Leave the reader wanting more

## Output
For each concept, provide:

**Concept [NUMBER]:**  
**Title:** [Eye-catching, benefit-driven title]  
**What's Included:** [Brief rundown of content and key takeaways]  
**Delivery Format:** [PDF, video, or 5-day email course]  
**Why It's Perfect for the ICP:** [Breakdown of how it matches their specific needs and desires]
```
