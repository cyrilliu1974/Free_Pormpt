# Product Marketing Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Product Marketing Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a polished 3×3 grid of nine themed product photography shots from a single product image for brands, marketers, and creative studios. This product marketing grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a cohesive 1:1 aspect ratio layout with nine square cells, each showcasing the same product in a different styled scene: hero centerpiece compositions, extreme macro liquid details, sculptural arrangements, high-speed splash captures, toasted textures, and surreal still-life fusions. Every cell maintains locked product accuracy - no label distortion, color shifts, or shape changes - while applying clean studio lighting, soft shadows, and controlled highlights for a premium editorial aesthetic. Use it when you need a full suite of campaign-ready product visuals from one source image, whether for social media carousels, pitch decks, or e-commerce galleries. ● Preserves exact product shape, labels, and colors across all nine grid cells with no distortion ● Delivers nine distinct scenes: hero shot, macro liquid drip, sculptural textures, melting surfaces, abstract sculpture, mid-air splashes, glossy elements, ingredient close-ups, and surreal fusion ● Applies studio lighting, soft diffused shadows, and glossy beauty highlights for high-end commercial quality ● Outputs a single 1:1 aspect ratio grid ready for social posts, campaign assets, and presentation decks ## Prompt
