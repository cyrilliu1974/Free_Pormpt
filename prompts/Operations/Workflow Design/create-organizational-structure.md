# Organizational Structure Design Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Organizational Structure Design Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive org charts and role definitions for businesses looking to clarify accountability and streamline operations. This organizational structure prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company profile and delivers a full blueprint including structure type (functional, matrix, divisional, or flat), a text-based hierarchy diagram, five critical role definitions with responsibilities and reporting relationships, communication channels, collaboration mechanisms, accountability measures, and agility enablers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across all major text AI models. Real-world use cases include startups formalizing their first org chart, mid-size companies restructuring during growth phases, and teams clarifying cross-functional reporting lines. Reach for this prompt when you need to define who does what, who reports to whom, and how teams coordinate - whether you're launching a new division, onboarding leaders, or documenting structure for stakeholders. ● Specifies optimal structure type (functional, matrix, divisional, flat) with rationale based on company size, industry, and culture ● Defines five critical roles with responsibilities, reporting lines, and accountability measures ● Includes text-based org chart, communication channels, collaboration mechanisms, and agility enablers ● Balances structural clarity with operational flexibility to support rapid adaptation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational design expert creating an optimal structure that defines roles, responsibilities, reporting relationships, and communication channels to maximize efficiency and effectiveness.

## Task

Design a comprehensive organizational structure tailored to the company described in {{company-profile}}. Your design should foster collaboration, accountability, and agility while aligning with the company's strategic objectives.

## Context

Effective organizational design balances structural clarity with operational flexibility. Consider how the company's goals, size, industry, and culture shape the optimal structure type (functional, divisional, matrix, flat, etc.). Define key roles to avoid overlap, establish clear reporting lines for efficient decision-making, and build in mechanisms that enable rapid adaptation to market changes.

## Output

Provide your organizational design in the following format:

# Company Overview
● Goals: [extract from company profile]
● Size: [extract from company profile]
● Industry: [extract from company profile]
● Culture: [extract from company profile]

# Organizational Structure
● Type: [specify structure type with rationale]
● Description: [2-3 paragraphs explaining why this structure fits the company]
● Diagram: [text-based org chart showing hierarchy and relationships]

# Key Roles
Define 5 critical roles:

## Role 1
● Title: [role title]
● Responsibilities: [3-5 key responsibilities]
● Reports to: [reporting relationship]

[Continue for Roles 2-5]

# Communication Channels
1. [Primary channel with usage context]
2. [Secondary channel with usage context]
3. [Tertiary channel with usage context]

# Collaboration Mechanisms
1. [Mechanism with implementation details]
2. [Mechanism with implementation details]
3. [Mechanism with implementation details]

# Accountability Measures
1. [Measure with frequency and metrics]
2. [Measure with frequency and metrics]
3. [Measure with frequency and metrics]

# Agility Enablers
1. [Enabler with specific practices]
2. [Enabler with specific practices]
3. [Enabler with specific practices]
```
