# Pastel Dream Icon Retexture Prompt

## 簡介

The Pastel Dream Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms existing images into soft, iridescent gel icons with dreamy pastel color blends for designers and digital artists. This pastel icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana retextures uploaded images by applying a 70% transparent gel membrane surface, organic symmetry, and a five-color gradient of mint green, baby blue, soft rose pink, butter yellow, and lavender. The prompt generates rounded silhouettes with slightly blurred edges, matte sheen, and soft ambient lighting against a pure black background. Designers use it to create UI icons, app graphics, social media assets, and branding elements that need a calm, surreal aesthetic with reduced glow and subtle core tint. Reach for this prompt when you need to unify a set of icons or photos under a cohesive pastel gel aesthetic without manual editing in Photoshop or Illustrator. ● Applies iridescent gel membrane texture at 70% transparency with smooth, fluid surface and gentle matte sheen. ● Blends five dreamy pastel hues in seamless multi-directional gradients with muted white highlights and soft fade edges. ● Creates rounded, organically symmetrical silhouettes with slightly blurred edge definition for a soft digital surrealism look. ● Renders on pure black background with soft ambient lighting and barely-there bloom for a calm, reduced glow aesthetic. ## Prompt

```
Retexture the attached image using these specifications:

**Structure**: Rounded, soft silhouette with organic symmetry and slightly blurred edge definition.

**Materials**: Iridescent gel membrane surface at 70% transparency. Smooth, fluid texture with gentle matte sheen, low gloss, and very subtle core tint glow.

**Colors**: Dreamy pastel gradient blending mint green, baby blue, soft rose pink, butter yellow, and lavender in multi-directional diffusion with seamless, soft transitions. Low glow intensity. Muted white highlights with pastel fade edges.

**Lighting**: Soft ambient lighting creating a calm, dreamy mood with barely-there bloom glow behavior.

**Background**: Pure black solid background.

**Aesthetic**: Soft digital surrealism with reduced glow aesthetic.

Apply {{texture-modifications}} to the retexturing process.
```
