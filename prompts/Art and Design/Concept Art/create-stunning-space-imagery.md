The Astronaut Space Scene Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic, photorealistic images of astronauts in space for designers, concept artists, and visual storytellers. This space imagery prompt for Midjourney produces highly detailed scenes of an astronaut floating in zero gravity, seated on the bridge of an advanced spaceship while gazing at Earth through a panoramic window. The prompt specifies cinematic composition, dramatic lighting, and hyper-realistic photography aesthetics in high resolution, with a customizable viewing angle variable. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models that support detailed scene descriptions and aspect ratio controls.
