# Brand Messaging Strategy Builder for PR

## 簡介

The Brand Messaging Strategy Builder for PR is a free AI prompt that creates structured messaging frameworks to communicate your company's unique value proposition, mission, and competitive positioning across all channels. This brand messaging prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company information, industry context, and target audience to produce a complete messaging architecture - including brand voice profiles, message hierarchies, audience-specific variations, and channel application guidelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for PR professionals, marketing teams, and brand strategists who need to establish or refresh their company's core messaging before launching campaigns, pitching media, or repositioning in the market. The prompt examines your unique value proposition and mission statement against competitor positioning to identify differentiation opportunities and messaging gaps. ● Produces a brand voice and tone profile with characteristics, do's and don'ts, and example phrases that reflect company values ● Creates a core message architecture with a primary message, three to five supporting pillars, and proof points for each claim ● Generates audience-specific messaging variations tailored to key segments while maintaining brand consistency ● Provides channel application guidelines that show how messages adapt across PR, social media, website, and sales materials ● Delivers differentiation analysis that clearly articulates competitive advantages and unique positioning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in messaging architecture and market positioning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive brand messaging strategy that clearly communicates the company's unique value proposition, mission, and vision to its target audience.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique value proposition: {{unique-value-proposition}}
Mission statement: {{mission-statement}}

## Process
1. Analyze the provided company information against industry standards and competitor positioning
2. Identify messaging gaps and opportunities for differentiation
3. Define a unified brand voice and tone that resonates with the target audience while reflecting company values
4. Create a hierarchy of key messages that emphasize unique selling points and competitive advantages
5. Establish guidelines for maintaining consistency across PR, marketing, and brand positioning channels

## Output
Deliver a structured brand messaging strategy that includes:
- **Brand Voice & Tone Profile**: Characteristics, do's and don'ts, example phrases
- **Core Message Architecture**: Primary message, supporting pillars (3-5), proof points for each
- **Audience-Specific Messaging**: Tailored variations for key audience segments
- **Channel Application Guidelines**: How messages adapt across different touchpoints while maintaining consistency
- **Differentiation Analysis**: Clear articulation of what sets this brand apart from competitors

Structure all written elements using clear hierarchical relationships that build from foundational concepts to specific applications.
```
