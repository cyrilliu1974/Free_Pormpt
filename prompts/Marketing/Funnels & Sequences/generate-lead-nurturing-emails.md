# Lead-Nurturing Email Campaign Builder

## 簡介

The Lead-Nurturing Email Campaign Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email marketing campaigns designed to nurture leads through the customer journey and drive conversions. This lead-nurturing email prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete campaign plan including audience segmentation, a strategic lead magnet, a five-email sequence with subject lines and full body copy, landing page specifications, KPIs with benchmarks, and A/B testing hypotheses. It applies behavioral psychology and email marketing best practices to design messages that engage prospects at each stage, from initial contact through conversion. Marketing teams use it to build cohesive campaigns for product launches, service onboarding, and lead conversion programs across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, psychology-informed email sequence rather than isolated messages, or when building campaigns for new products, audiences, or sales funnels. ● Builds a five-email sequence with subject lines, preview text, body copy, CTAs, and send-timing rationale grounded in consumer psychology ● Segments your target audience into three personas with characteristics that inform message personalization ● Designs a lead magnet with format, title, value proposition, and call-to-action aligned to your unique selling proposition ● Includes landing page structures, KPI benchmarks, and A/B test plans to optimize performance after launch ## Prompt
