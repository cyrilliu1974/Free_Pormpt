# SEO Competitor Analysis Prompt for On-Page Tactics

## 簡介

The SEO Competitor Analysis Prompt for On-Page Tactics is a free AI prompt that examines how competitors optimize their websites and delivers prioritized recommendations for SEO professionals. This on-page SEO competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT systematically evaluates up to three competitors across keyword density, meta tag structure, header hierarchy, content format, internal linking patterns, and readability scores. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce side-by-side comparison charts that highlight tactical differences in title tag optimization, keyword placement strategies, and content structuring choices. SEO analysts use it to identify competitive gaps before launching new content campaigns, auditing existing pages, or planning keyword targeting strategies for client websites. Reach for this prompt when you need structured competitor intelligence that goes beyond surface observations and reveals the specific on-page tactics driving search visibility in your industry. ● Breaks down keyword usage by density percentage, placement in titles and headings, and primary versus secondary targeting patterns across multiple competitors ● Evaluates meta tag effectiveness including title tag length, description quality, and header tag hierarchy to spot optimization opportunities ● Analyzes content structure decisions such as word count benchmarks, format preferences, internal linking frequency, and readability scores ● Delivers 3-5 ranked recommendations that separate quick wins from long-term strategies and explain which competitive tactics to adopt or avoid ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO analyst specializing in competitor research, on-page optimization strategies, and comparative analysis.

## Task
Conduct an in-depth analysis of the top three competitors in {{industry}}, examining their on-page SEO tactics. Create a comprehensive comparison chart highlighting similarities and differences in their approaches, then provide actionable recommendations prioritized for maximum impact.

## Context
Focus exclusively on on-page SEO factors: keyword usage, meta tag optimization, and content structuring. Do not analyze off-page factors such as backlinks or social media presence.

Analyze these competitors:
{{competitor-details}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Competitor Analysis: On-Page SEO Strategies in the [Industry] Sector**

For each competitor, provide:

**Competitor [N]: [Name]**  
URL: [website]

**Keyword Usage:**
- Primary and secondary keywords identified
- Keyword density percentage
- Keyword placement analysis (titles, headings, body, URL)

**Meta Tags:**
- Title tag (content and length)
- Meta description (content and length)
- Header tag hierarchy (H1, H2, H3 usage and optimization)

**Content Structure:**
- Word count
- Content format (listicles, long-form, how-to guides, etc.)
- Internal linking strategy and frequency
- Readability score (Flesch-Kincaid or equivalent)

---

**Comparative Analysis:**

Provide side-by-side comparisons across:
- Keyword strategies (targeting approach, density patterns, placement tactics)
- Meta tag optimization (consistency, length optimization, keyword inclusion)
- Content structuring (length preferences, format choices, internal linking patterns)

**Recommendations:**

List 3-5 actionable insights ranked by impact potential, explaining:
1. What competitive gaps or advantages exist
2. Which tactics to adopt or avoid
3. Quick wins versus long-term strategies
```
