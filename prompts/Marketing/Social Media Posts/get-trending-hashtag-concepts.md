# Trending Hashtag Concepts Generator for Social Media

## 簡介

The Trending Hashtag Concepts Generator for Social Media is a free AI prompt that creates strategic, trend-aware hashtag recommendations tailored to your target audience, business, and campaign objectives. This hashtag prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current social media trends, audience behavior patterns, and competitive landscape to deliver 10 hashtag concepts, each accompanied by a relevance explanation and an impact prediction. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, evaluating platform-specific best practices and hashtag performance signals like search volume, engagement rates, and saturation levels. Use it when planning social media campaigns, launching products, or refreshing your content strategy to ensure your hashtags are catchy, relevant, and optimized for virality. ● Provides 10 hashtag concepts tailored to your specific target audience, business description, and campaign goals. ● Includes relevance explanations showing why each hashtag fits your brand and audience. ● Delivers impact predictions that estimate reach and engagement potential for each hashtag. ● Analyzes current trends, audience behavior, and competitive factors to ensure timely, effective recommendations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in hashtag strategy and trend analysis.

## Task
Generate 10 trending hashtag concepts designed to boost engagement and maximize reach. Each hashtag should be catchy, relevant, and optimized for virality.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Business overview: {{business-description}}

Campaign objectives: {{campaign-goals}}

Analyze current social media trends, audience behavior patterns, and competitive landscape to inform your recommendations. Consider platform-specific best practices and hashtag performance signals (search volume, engagement rates, saturation levels).

## Output
Provide 10 hashtags in bullet point format. For each:
- The hashtag itself
- Why it's relevant to this business and audience
- Its potential impact on reach and engagement

Format:
• #HashtagName - [Relevance explanation] | Impact: [Expected performance and why]
```
