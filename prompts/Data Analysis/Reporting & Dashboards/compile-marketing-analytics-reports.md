# Marketing Analytics Report Generator

## 簡介

The Marketing Analytics Report Generator is a free AI prompt that builds data-driven marketing reports with KPI tracking and strategic recommendations for marketing analysts and digital marketers. This marketing analytics prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing performance data across your specified marketing channels and time period, then organizing findings into a structured markdown table with your chosen metric categories. The prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to identify relevant KPIs for your business type, analyze trends across channels, and deliver both quantitative insights and actionable optimization strategies. Use it to compile weekly performance reviews, monthly marketing reports, quarterly campaign summaries, or ad-hoc analysis when stakeholders need clear, data-backed recommendations. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform raw marketing data into stakeholder-ready reports that highlight what's working, what needs attention, and what to do next. ● Identifies the most relevant KPIs based on your specific business type and marketing channels ● Creates markdown tables with customizable metric categories like CTR, conversion rate, ROAS, engagement, or any metrics you specify ● Analyzes each metric to surface trends, patterns, and performance gaps across channels ● Generates specific, actionable recommendations to improve underperforming areas and scale winning strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing analyst creating a comprehensive marketing analytics report.

## Task
Track key performance indicators and optimize marketing strategies through structured, data-driven analysis.

## Context
{{business-and-channels}}

{{reporting-period}}

## Process
1. Identify the most relevant KPIs for the business type
2. Analyze data from all specified marketing channels
3. Structure findings in a table with columns for: {{metric-categories}}
4. Populate the table with accurate, current data
5. Analyze each metric, highlighting trends and improvement areas
6. Develop actionable optimization recommendations

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with {{metric-categories}} as column headers, followed by:
- **Analysis**: Key trends, patterns, and notable performance insights for each metric
- **Recommendations**: Specific, actionable strategies to optimize marketing performance based on the data
```
