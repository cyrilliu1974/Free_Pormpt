# White Paper Generator Prompt for Thought Leadership

## 簡介

The White Paper Generator Prompt for Thought Leadership is a free AI prompt that creates authoritative, research-driven white papers designed to establish industry expertise and generate qualified leads for businesses and organizations. This white paper prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok through a systematic ten-step process that transforms your topic, target audience, industry context, and business objectives into professional-grade white papers. The prompt ensures each document includes an executive summary, hierarchical structure with clear headings, data-backed analysis with credible source citations, actionable recommendations, suggested visual placements for charts and infographics, and strategic calls-to-action. Content strategists, marketing teams, and B2B companies use this prompt to produce lead-generation assets that demonstrate deep subject-matter expertise while addressing audience pain points and guiding readers toward meaningful engagement. ● Conducts thorough research gathering relevant data, statistics, and expert opinions to support claims ● Structures content with logical flow using dependency grammar principles for coherence between sections ● Generates executive summaries, callout boxes for critical findings, and complete reference sections ● Provides strategic visual placement suggestions describing charts, infographics, and data visualizations that illustrate key points ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in authoritative white papers that establish thought leadership and generate leads.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, well-researched white paper that addresses {{topic}} for {{target-audience}}. The white paper should demonstrate deep expertise, provide actionable insights, and guide readers toward meaningful engagement.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Business objectives: {{business-context}}

## Process
1. Research the topic thoroughly, gathering relevant data, statistics, and expert opinions
2. Analyze the target audience's needs, pain points, and interests
3. Structure content with logical flow, ensuring each section builds coherently on the previous
4. Develop a compelling introduction highlighting the topic's importance and relevance
5. Present in-depth analysis with insights supported by credible sources
6. Provide actionable recommendations or solutions addressing identified challenges
7. Suggest placements for visuals (charts, infographics, data visualizations) that illustrate key points
8. Craft a strong conclusion reinforcing main takeaways
9. Include a clear call-to-action guiding readers on next steps
10. Ensure professional tone, clarity, and consistency throughout

## Output
Deliver the white paper with:
- Clear hierarchical structure using headings and subheadings
- Bullet points for key insights and recommendations
- Callout boxes for statistics or critical findings
- Suggested visual placements with descriptions
- Executive summary at the beginning
- References section citing all sources
```
