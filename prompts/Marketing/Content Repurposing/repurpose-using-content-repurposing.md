# Content Repurposing Prompt for Multi-Platform Adaptation

## 簡介

The Content Repurposing Prompt for Multi-Platform Adaptation is a free AI prompt that transforms a single piece of existing content into three optimized formats - blog post, social media post, and email newsletter - for content strategists and marketers. This content repurposing prompt for ChatGPT extracts core messages and key themes from your original material, then adapts structure, length, tone, and calls-to-action to match each platform's requirements. It generates 400–800 word blog posts with SEO-optimized headings, 150–280 character social media posts with hook-first openings and hashtags, and scannable email newsletters complete with subject lines and CTA button text. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to ensure logical sentence flow and coherence across all three formats. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it compatible with all major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need to maximize the reach of existing articles, webinars, reports, or presentations by adapting them for different audiences and channels while preserving your brand voice and message integrity. ● Extracts core themes and takeaways, then adapts structure and length for blog, social, and email formats ● Incorporates SEO keywords naturally in blog posts and includes hashtag-ready hooks for social media ● Maintains consistent brand voice while tailoring tone to platform norms (professional for blog, conversational for social, direct for email) ● Generates format-specific CTAs and includes strategic notes explaining key adaptation choices for each version ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in multi-format content repurposing.

## Task
Transform existing content into three formats—blog post, social media post, and email newsletter—optimized for the target audience and platform. Use dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous, creating coherent and scannable content.

## Context
**Original content:** {{existing-content}}

**Target audience & topic:** {{audience-and-topic}}

**Brand voice & goals:** {{brand-voice-and-goals}}

## Process
1. Extract the core messages, themes, and key takeaways from the original content
2. Adapt structure and length for each format:
   - **Blog post:** 400–800 words, section headings, SEO-optimized
   - **Social media post:** 150–280 characters, hook-first, hashtag-ready
   - **Email newsletter:** Scannable paragraphs, clear CTA, subject line included
3. Incorporate relevant keywords naturally for discoverability
4. Craft attention-grabbing headlines and opening lines for each format
5. Maintain consistent brand voice while tailoring tone to platform norms
6. Include format-appropriate calls-to-action that align with the primary objective
7. Ensure all versions preserve the original message's integrity while adding platform-specific value

## Output
Provide three sections with clear headings:

### Blog Post
- Headline
- Introduction
- Body (with subheadings)
- Conclusion with CTA
- SEO keywords used

### Social Media Post
- Hook line
- Body copy
- Hashtags
- CTA

### Email Newsletter
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Body copy
- CTA button text

For each format, include bullet points highlighting key strategic choices made during adaptation.
```
