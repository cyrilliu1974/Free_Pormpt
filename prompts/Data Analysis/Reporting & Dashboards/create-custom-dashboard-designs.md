# Goal-Tracking Dashboard Design Prompt for Business

## 簡介

The Goal-Tracking Dashboard Design Prompt for Business is a free AI prompt that creates polished dashboard layouts to monitor progress toward your priority objectives. This dashboard design prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete template in your preferred software (Excel, Google Sheets, Tableau, Power BI, or others). It produces a structured goal-tracking table with columns for Goal, Current Status, Target, and Progress %, then recommends 2-3 visualization types - charts, gauges, or graphs - optimized for your business model. The prompt applies data visualization best practices to ensure clarity and stakeholder engagement, and it outputs detailed design specifications including color palettes, typography, and interactive elements like filters or drill-downs. Use it when launching a new initiative, preparing board reports, or aligning teams around quarterly objectives. ● Produces a mock-up table with sample data for three priority goals, complete with current status, target, and progress percentage columns ● Recommends specific chart and graph types suited to your business type and objectives ● Includes design specifications covering color schemes, typography, and interactive features to enhance usability ● Adapts output to your chosen dashboard software, from spreadsheets to full BI platforms ## Prompt
