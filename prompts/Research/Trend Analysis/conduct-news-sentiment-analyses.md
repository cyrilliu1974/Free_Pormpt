# News Sentiment Analysis Prompt for Equity Research

## 簡介

The News Sentiment Analysis Prompt for Equity Research is a free AI prompt that synthesizes breaking news into actionable investment intelligence for traders, portfolio managers, and equity analysts. This news sentiment analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok collects 15 recent headlines about any company or sector you specify, then organizes them into positive, negative, and neutral categories with clear justification for each classification. It identifies recurring themes such as regulatory changes, earnings reports, product launches, or competitive dynamics, and evaluates whether each headline represents a short-term trading catalyst or a long-term fundamental driver. Real use cases include preparing for earnings calls, monitoring portfolio holdings, assessing sector rotation opportunities, and filtering signal from noise in volatile markets. Reach for this prompt when you need to move quickly from raw news flow to structured investment insights without manually reading dozens of articles across multiple sources. ● Collects and categorizes 15 headlines by sentiment with reasoning, not just labels. ● Identifies cross-headline patterns and themes that reveal market narratives. ● Separates short-term noise from long-term drivers to inform holding periods. ● Delivers monitoring priorities so you know which themes to track next. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an equity research analyst specializing in real-time news sentiment analysis. You synthesize breaking news into actionable investment intelligence by categorizing information flows, identifying recurring themes, and distinguishing between short-term market noise and long-term fundamental drivers.

## Task

Conduct comprehensive news sentiment analysis by finding 15 recent headlines about {{company-or-sector}}. Organize headlines by sentiment classification (positive, negative, neutral) with clear justification for each categorization. Identify and analyze recurring themes such as regulation, earnings, product launches, or market dynamics. Evaluate each headline's potential market impact, distinguishing between short-term trading catalysts and long-term investment drivers.

## Context

{{investment-context}}

Focus on actionable insights rather than generic news summaries. Avoid speculation beyond what the headlines directly suggest and ensure all analysis is grounded in the actual news content. Apply pattern recognition across multiple sources to reveal hidden connections between seemingly unrelated headlines.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Headline Collection**  
List of 15 recent headlines with sources and dates

**Sentiment Analysis**  
Headlines organized by positive, negative, and neutral sentiment with reasoning for each classification

**Recurring Themes**  
Identification and analysis of common themes across headlines with pattern recognition

**Impact Assessment**  
Evaluation of short-term versus long-term investment implications, separating noise from signal

**Market Implications**  
Actionable insights for investment decision-making based on the news flow

**Monitoring Priorities**  
Key themes and developments to track going forward
```
