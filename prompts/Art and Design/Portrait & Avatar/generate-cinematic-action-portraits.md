# Cinematic Action Portrait With Fire and Smoke

## 簡介

The Cinematic Action Portrait With Fire and Smoke is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic portraits of a character walking toward the camera through a night scene with raging flames and smoke behind them. The prompt captures macro-level facial detail - skin texture, cigarette smoke, firelight reflections in the eyes - while keeping the explosive background slightly out of focus for a shallow depth-of-field cinematic effect. This action portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana lets you customize the setting, wardrobe, and background scenario (burning buildings, vehicle explosions, or other pyrotechnic scenes) to match your project, delivering 8k-resolution images with wet asphalt reflections and gritty dramatic lighting. Reach for this prompt when you need Hollywood-style hero shots for movie posters, book covers, album art, game character concepts, or social media content without hiring a film crew or pyrotechnics team. ● Produces sharp, macro-level facial features with realistic skin texture, smoke, and firelight reflections in the eyes while the explosive background stays artistically blurred. ● Accepts three variables - setting (urban street, industrial zone, desert highway), wardrobe (tactical gear, suit, casual clothing), and background scenario (burning car, collapsing warehouse, wildfire) - for full creative control. ● Renders high-resolution 8k output at 1200×1200px with wet asphalt reflections, deep shadows, and orange fire highlights that create a gritty, blockbuster aesthetic. ● Delivers cinematic shallow depth of field and photorealistic lighting effects suitable for professional book covers, game art, promotional posters, and visual storytelling projects. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic portrait of a man walking toward the camera in {{setting}} at night, smoking a cigarette. He wears {{wardrobe}} and worn leather shoes. The camera is very close, capturing his face sharply—textures of skin, smoke curling from his lips, and subtle reflections of firelight in his eyes. Behind him, slightly out of focus, {{background-scenario}}, with huge flames, roaring firestorms, and thick black smoke rising high. The fiery glow casts dramatic orange highlights on his clothing and face, creating deep shadows and a gritty, cinematic mood. Wet asphalt reflects the blaze, enhancing the dramatic atmosphere.

Style: Cinematic, photorealistic, gritty dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, 8k resolution. Dimensions: 1200×1200px.
```
