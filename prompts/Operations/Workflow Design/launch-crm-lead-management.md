# CRM Lead Management System Architecture Prompt

## 簡介

The CRM Lead Management System Architecture Prompt is a free AI prompt that designs end-to-end customer relationship management architectures for businesses building or redesigning lead tracking systems. This CRM lead management prompt for ChatGPT walks through eight critical dimensions: business development stages, data entity design, core features for pipeline tracking, workflow automations, third-party integrations (email, calendar, marketing tools), analytics and KPI dashboards, security and compliance frameworks (GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2), and scalability planning. Instead of producing implementation code, it delivers a high-level blueprint that product managers, CRM consultants, and technical architects can hand off to development teams. Real-world use cases include startups specifying their first CRM build, enterprises consolidating fragmented lead databases, and agencies designing client solutions that balance automation with human touchpoints. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, vendor-neutral CRM specification before selecting platforms like Salesforce, HubSpot, or custom-built solutions. ● Outputs organized sections for stages, data models, features, workflows, integrations, analytics, security, and scalability ● Includes compliance checkpoints for GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2 alongside access controls and encryption requirements ● Describes automation triggers for lead progression, follow-up scheduling, and status updates without prescribing specific tools ● Details analytics and reporting needs such as conversion rates, pipeline velocity, revenue forecasting, and lead source performance ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a CRM system architect specializing in business development processes, lead management best practices, and workflow optimization.

## Task

Design a comprehensive business development CRM system architecture that efficiently manages leads, tracks progress, automates workflows, and provides insightful analytics. Deliver a high-level design covering structure, features, and requirements—not implementation specifics or technology choices.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Organize your architecture design into these sections:

### 1. Key Stages and Requirements
Outline the stages of the business development process and requirements for each stage.

### 2. Data Model
Describe entities, attributes, and relationships required for the CRM system.

### 3. Essential Features
List and describe the core features needed for lead management, pipeline tracking, and user collaboration.

### 4. Workflows and Automations
Detail workflows and automations that streamline lead progression, follow-ups, and status updates.

### 5. Integrations
Identify necessary integrations with other systems (email, calendar, marketing tools, accounting) and their purposes.

### 6. Analytics and Reporting
Describe reports, KPIs, insights, and real-time metrics the system should provide (conversion rates, pipeline velocity, revenue forecasting, lead source performance).

### 7. Security and Compliance
List security measures (access controls, encryption, audit logs) and compliance standards (GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2) the system must meet.

### 8. Scalability and Performance
Discuss scalability considerations (data volume growth, concurrent users) and performance optimizations required.

Use markdown formatting with clear headings and bullet points throughout.
```
