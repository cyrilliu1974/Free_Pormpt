# Freelance Pitch Writer for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Freelance Pitch Writer for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates compelling service pitches tailored to your target clients and professional positioning. This freelance pitch prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete outreach document that opens with attention-grabbing statements, articulates your unique value proposition, showcases your best portfolio work, and weaves in testimonials to establish credibility. It structures your pitch to address client pain points directly while emphasizing the advantages of working with a solopreneur - agility, direct communication, and specialized focus - before closing with a clear call-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a client-ready document formatted with headings and bullet points that you can customize for email outreach, proposal introductions, or website copy. ● Builds a logical narrative flow that establishes credibility early, then layers evidence through portfolio examples and testimonials ● Adapts messaging to resonate with your specific target audience's needs and pain points ● Highlights solopreneur advantages like personalized service and specialized expertise that larger agencies cannot match ● Outputs a formatted, client-ready document you can immediately adapt for cold outreach, warm introductions, or proposal cover letters ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a freelance pitch strategist specializing in solopreneur positioning and client acquisition.

## Task
Write a persuasive pitch that promotes the solopreneur's services to their target audience. The pitch must:

- Open with an attention-grabbing statement that resonates with the audience's needs
- Articulate a clear unique value proposition
- Showcase portfolio work that demonstrates expertise
- Incorporate testimonials strategically to build credibility
- Address the target audience's key pain points
- Highlight the advantages of working with a solopreneur (agility, direct communication, specialized focus)
- Close with a strong call-to-action

Structure the writing to ensure logical flow: establish credibility early, build value through evidence, and guide the reader toward action.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Solopreneur profile:** {{solopreneur-profile}}
(Include: top 3 unique skills, niche/specialization, best portfolio piece with brief description, and one strong testimonial)

## Output
Deliver the pitch using clear headings and bullet points for readability. Format as a client-ready document the solopreneur can adapt for outreach.
```
