# Business Viability Feasibility Study Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Viability Feasibility Study Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive feasibility reports to assess whether new business ventures should move forward. This business viability prompt for ChatGPT delivers a six-part analysis covering executive summary, market analysis (target segments, market size, trends, competitive landscape), financial analysis (startup costs, revenue forecasts, break-even calculations), operational analysis (production process, supply chain, staffing), SWOT framework, and go/no-go recommendations with next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only a venture description as input. Entrepreneurs use it to evaluate startup ideas, corporate teams apply it to expansion plans, and consultants deploy it for client assessments before committing capital. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-based analysis that balances optimism with realistic risk assessment, turning a raw business concept into a structured decision framework. ● Produces market sizing estimates, competitive positioning analysis, and demand trend evaluation in a single report. ● Calculates three-year revenue projections, itemized startup costs, and break-even timelines with transparent assumptions. ● Delivers SWOT analysis highlighting internal strengths and weaknesses alongside external opportunities and threats. ● Provides confidence-rated recommendations, key success factors, potential roadblocks, and prioritized next steps. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in comprehensive feasibility studies for new ventures.

## Task
Assess the viability of the proposed business venture and deliver a structured feasibility report with clear, evidence-based recommendations on whether to proceed.

## Context
Evaluate all critical dimensions: market demand, competitive landscape, financial projections, operational requirements, and risk factors. Base your analysis on realistic assumptions and present both opportunities and challenges objectively.

**Venture Details:**
{{venture-description}}

## Output
Deliver a feasibility report structured as follows:

### 1. Executive Summary
High-level overview of key findings and recommendations.

### 2. Market Analysis
- **Target Market:** Identify and profile the primary customer segments
- **Market Size:** Estimate total addressable market and serviceable obtainable market
- **Market Trends:** Analyze current and emerging trends affecting demand
- **Competitive Landscape:** Assess competitors, their positioning, strengths, and weaknesses

### 3. Financial Analysis
- **Startup Costs:** Itemize initial capital requirements
- **Revenue Projections:** Forecast revenue for years 1-3 with underlying assumptions
- **Break-Even Analysis:** Calculate break-even point in units and timeline
- **Financial Risks:** Identify cash flow, funding, and profitability risks

### 4. Operational Analysis
- **Production Process:** Outline how products/services will be created and delivered
- **Supply Chain:** Analyze sourcing, logistics, and supplier dependencies
- **Staffing Requirements:** Define roles, headcount, and talent acquisition needs
- **Operational Challenges:** Identify scalability, quality control, and execution risks

### 5. SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths:** Internal advantages and competitive differentiators
- **Weaknesses:** Internal limitations and capability gaps
- **Opportunities:** External factors that favor growth
- **Threats:** External risks to viability

### 6. Recommendations
- **Overall Feasibility:** Go/No-Go assessment with confidence level
- **Key Success Factors:** Critical elements required for success
- **Potential Roadblocks:** Anticipated challenges and mitigation strategies
- **Suggested Next Steps:** Prioritized actions based on the assessment

**Format Requirements:**
- Use headings, subheadings, bullet points, and tables for clarity
- Support conclusions with data and reasoning
- Avoid definitive predictions; acknowledge inherent uncertainties
- Present actionable, well-reasoned guidance to inform decision-making
```
