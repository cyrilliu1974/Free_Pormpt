# Coastal Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Coastal Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic portraits of subjects reclining on rocky cliffs overlooking turquoise ocean waves, designed for content creators, travel brands, and fashion marketers. This coastal portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images with natural body proportions, realistic limb positioning, bright sunlight, and layered compositions featuring rocky foregrounds, crashing ocean midgrounds, and rolling hills under blue skies. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering high-detail, film-quality results in 1:1 aspect ratio. Reach for this prompt when you need lifestyle imagery that captures the wanderlust aesthetic of seaside destinations without coordinating location shoots or model bookings. ● Produces photorealistic portraits with correct body proportions, natural reclining poses, and no limb distortions. ● Captures cinematic lighting on rocky cliff edges with turquoise ocean waves and green rolling hills in the background. ● Offers two variables for tailoring subject appearance and setting details to match brand aesthetics. ● Delivers high-detail, 35mm film-style imagery suitable for Instagram, travel blogs, fashion campaigns, and editorial content. ## Prompt

```
A beautiful young woman reclining naturally on a rocky cliff edge overlooking turquoise ocean waves. {{subject-appearance}}, beige flat espadrilles. Relaxed, natural reclining pose with anatomically correct limbs—natural leg positioning, realistic body proportions, no distortions. {{setting-details}}. Foreground: rocky cliff edge. Midground: turquoise ocean waves crashing below. Background: green rolling hills, clear blue sky with scattered clouds. Bright natural sunlight, cinematic lighting, photorealistic. High detail, shot on 35mm film, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
