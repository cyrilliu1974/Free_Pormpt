# Editorial Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Editorial Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-detailed fashion portraits preserving a reference subject's exact facial features and proportions while rendering them in high-fashion editorial styling. This editorial fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces studio-quality images with professional lighting, designer couture outfits, and magazine-ready presentation - perfect for lookbooks, campaigns, and brand visuals that require identity consistency across styling variations. Fashion brands, content creators, and marketing teams use it to visualize outfit concepts on the same model without organizing multiple photo shoots. ● Preserves uploaded reference face, body proportions, and identity 100% while transforming outfit, pose, and styling elements for consistent model representation. ● Generates professional studio lighting with controlled shadows, sharp focus, and editorial-grade contrast on dark backdrop environments. ● Customizes pose and outfit description variables to produce versatile high-fashion imagery with couture silhouettes and refined fabric textures. ● Delivers ultra-sharp, high-clarity results with polished hair styling, skin detail, and magazine-quality presentation suitable for commercial use. ## Prompt
