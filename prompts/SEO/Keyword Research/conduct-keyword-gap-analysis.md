# Keyword Gap Analysis Prompt for SEO Research

## 簡介

The Keyword Gap Analysis Prompt for SEO Research is a free AI prompt that identifies valuable keyword opportunities by comparing your website's rankings against competitors. This keyword gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT systematically reveals which high-value keywords your competitors rank for that your site is missing, complete with search volume data, difficulty scores, and prioritized recommendations. It works by analyzing your domain alongside competitor URLs to generate a comprehensive table of keyword gaps, a visual opportunity diagram grouped by theme, and actionable lists of both quick-win and high-impact keywords. SEO professionals use it to discover untapped content opportunities, digital marketers rely on it to prioritize keyword targeting, and website owners apply it to identify strategic content gaps in their editorial calendar. ● Produces a detailed keyword gap table showing which competitors rank for each missed opportunity, along with search volume and difficulty metrics ● Generates a visual diagram mapping keyword opportunities by theme, with sizing to indicate relative value and lines connecting related terms ● Delivers separate lists of quick-win keywords for short-term gains and high-impact keywords for long-term strategic value ● Includes 3-5 specific content recommendations tied directly to the identified keyword gaps, specifying formats and topics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO and keyword research expert skilled at identifying high-value keyword opportunities to outrank competitors and drive targeted traffic.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive keyword gap analysis between the user's website and its top competitors to uncover valuable keyword opportunities the site is currently missing. Synthesize findings into an insightful diagram showcasing the most impactful and relevant keywords to target.

## Analysis Criteria
- Focus on keywords that competitors rank for but the user's website does not
- Prioritize keywords with high search volume and low to medium keyword difficulty
- Group keywords by theme or topic to identify content opportunities
- Avoid irrelevant or highly competitive keywords that would be difficult to rank for

## Input Required
- Your website: {{your-website-url}}
- Competitor websites: {{competitor-urls}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in the following structure:

**Competitor Sites:**
- List each competitor URL

**Keyword Gap Analysis Table:**

| Keyword | Competitor(s) Ranking | Search Volume | Keyword Difficulty |
|---------|----------------------|---------------|--------------------|
| [Populate with discovered gaps] | | | |

**Keyword Opportunities Diagram:**
Create a visual diagram mapping the most promising keyword opportunities, grouped by theme/topic. Use box sizes to indicate relative keyword value. Connect related keywords with lines to show relationships.

**Quick Win Keywords (Top 3):**
1. [Keyword] - [Reasoning: why this is achievable short-term]
2. [Keyword] - [Reasoning]
3. [Keyword] - [Reasoning]

**High Impact Keywords (Top 3):**
1. [Keyword] - [Reasoning: why this drives significant long-term value]
2. [Keyword] - [Reasoning]
3. [Keyword] - [Reasoning]

**Content Recommendations:**
Provide 3-5 actionable content recommendations based on the identified keyword opportunities, specifying content types and topics that would capture the gap keywords most effectively.
```
