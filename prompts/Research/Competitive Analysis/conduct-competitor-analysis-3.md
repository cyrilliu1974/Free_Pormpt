# Competitor Analysis Prompt for Social Media Strategy

## 簡介

The Competitor Analysis Prompt for Social Media Strategy is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic competitive intelligence research for businesses analyzing rival social media presence and performance. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides you through identifying top competitors in your industry, evaluating their activity across specified platforms, assessing content formats and posting cadence, estimating engagement metrics, and extracting actionable strategic takeaways. It outputs findings in a markdown table showing competitor name, platform, content type, engagement rate, and key insights, followed by trend analysis and 3-5 strategic recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams using different text-generation models. Use it when launching a social media campaign, benchmarking your current strategy, or preparing quarterly competitive reviews. ● Maps competitor positioning, content approach, and engagement data across multiple social platforms in table format ● Calculates or estimates engagement rates relative to follower counts for apples-to-apples comparison ● Surfaces cross-competitor trends and patterns that reveal market opportunities ● Delivers 3-5 prioritized strategic recommendations grounded in the analysis findings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in competitive intelligence and platform analytics.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis of {{business-type}} social media strategies. Identify the top {{number-of-competitors}} competitors, analyze their presence and performance across {{platforms}}, evaluate content types and engagement rates, and extract strategic insights.

## Process
1. Identify the leading competitors in the {{business-type}} space
2. Analyze their activity and positioning on {{platforms}}
3. Assess content formats, posting frequency, and creative approaches
4. Calculate or estimate engagement rates (likes, comments, shares relative to follower count)
5. Extract key strategic takeaways from each competitor's approach

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with these columns:
- Competitor Name
- Platform
- Content Type
- Engagement Rate
- Key Takeaways

After the table, provide:
- A summary of overall trends across competitors
- 3-5 strategic recommendations based on the analysis
```
