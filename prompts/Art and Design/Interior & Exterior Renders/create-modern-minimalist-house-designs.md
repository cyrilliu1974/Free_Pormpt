# Modern Minimalist Architecture Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Modern Minimalist Architecture Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic renderings of contemporary architectural designs for architects, designers, and visualization artists. This modern minimalist architecture prompt for Midjourney produces ultra-detailed images featuring clean concrete columns, expansive terraces with outdoor furniture, warm ambient lighting, and lush tropical vegetation in golden hour light. The prompt specifies eye-level perspective with a 24mm wide-angle view, cinematic mood with natural soft shadows, and deliberately excludes people, cars, or clutter to maintain architectural focus. It works across Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation platforms to create presentation-quality architectural photography. Reach for this prompt when you need portfolio-grade architectural visualizations, client presentations, or concept exploration for modern residential or commercial buildings. ● Customizable building type and view variables let you adapt the scene to houses, villas, pavilions, or other structures overlooking gardens, oceans, forests, or cityscapes. ● Technical parameters (24mm wide angle, 16:9 aspect ratio, high quality and stylization settings) ensure consistent professional output. ● Golden hour lighting and natural shadow instructions produce warm, inviting atmospheres ideal for marketing and portfolio work. ● Clean exclusion rules (no people, cars, clutter) keep the focus on architectural form and spatial design. ## Prompt
