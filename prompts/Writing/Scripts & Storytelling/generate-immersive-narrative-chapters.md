# Scene Expansion Prompt for Immersive Chapter Writing

## 簡介

The Scene Expansion Prompt for Immersive Chapter Writing is a free AI prompt that transforms skeletal scene outlines into richly detailed narrative chapters for novelists, screenwriters, and creative writers. This scene expansion prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to enrich your brief scene with vivid sensory descriptions, natural dialogue exchanges that reveal character dynamics, internal thoughts that deepen psychological realism, and cohesive structure built on dependency grammar principles. Writers use it to turn a paragraph of action beats or a scene sketch into a fully realized chapter while preserving the original plot points, character arcs, and tone. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you have a story outline or scene summary and need to expand it into publication-ready prose without losing narrative focus or introducing unintended plot threads. ● Expands brief scene descriptions into full-length chapters with sensory detail and narrative depth ● Generates natural dialogue that reveals character personality, motivation, and relationships ● Incorporates internal monologue and character thoughts to create psychological realism ● Applies dependency grammar structure to ensure clear, flowing sentences and paragraph cohesion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert narrative writer specializing in scene expansion, dialogue craft, and character development.

## Task
Expand the brief scene provided below into a full, immersive chapter. Enrich the original with:

- **Vivid sensory details** that bring the setting and action to life
- **Natural dialogue exchanges** that reveal character personalities and relationships
- **Internal thoughts and motivations** that deepen character dimensionality
- **Cohesive narrative structure** using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and flow

## Constraints
- Preserve the core plot points, character arcs, and tone of the original scene
- Expand to chapter length while maintaining narrative focus
- Do not introduce new major characters or plot threads not implied by the original

## Output Format
**Original Scene:**  
{{brief-scene}}

**Expanded Chapter:**  
[Your expanded narrative here]
```
