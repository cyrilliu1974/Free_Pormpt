# Landscape Image Generator for AI Art Tools

## 簡介

The Landscape Image Generator for AI Art Tools is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric landscape images with golden hour lighting and dramatic cloud formations for artists, designers, and content creators. This landscape image prompt for Midjourney works by combining your scene description with pre-configured golden hour lighting and dramatic cloud effects, then rendering the composition in your chosen aspect ratio. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, producing cinematic natural scenes ideal for concept art, marketing visuals, desktop wallpapers, and creative projects. The prompt handles mountains, coastlines, forests, deserts, and any terrain you specify, always framed in warm, late-afternoon light that adds depth and emotion to the composition. Reach for this prompt when you need polished landscape imagery without manually tuning lighting parameters or atmospheric effects. ● Produces golden hour lighting automatically, giving every landscape warm tones and long shadows that create mood and visual interest. ● Includes dramatic cloud formations by default, adding scale and atmosphere to skies without extra prompting. ● Accepts any landscape description as input while maintaining consistent lighting and weather treatment across renders. ● Supports custom aspect ratios so you can match the output to vertical social posts, widescreen headers, or square thumbnails. ## Prompt

```
{{landscape-description}}, golden hour lighting, dramatic clouds --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
