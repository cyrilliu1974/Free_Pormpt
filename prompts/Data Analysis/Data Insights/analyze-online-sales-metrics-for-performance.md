# Online Sales Metrics Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Online Sales Metrics Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that diagnoses e-commerce performance gaps and produces data-driven optimization strategies for online retailers and marketing teams. This online sales metrics analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine conversion rates, average order value, customer acquisition cost, and other core KPIs, then compare your store's performance against historical data and industry benchmarks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting a three-column table (Metric, Current Performance, Target Performance) followed by a prioritized list of actionable strategies tailored to your business profile and primary challenge. Real use cases include identifying cart-abandonment fixes, optimizing product pricing, and reducing customer acquisition spend. Reach for this prompt when you need to diagnose why sales are stagnant, justify a marketing budget shift, or build a quarterly optimization roadmap backed by data rather than guesswork. ● Evaluates conversion rates, average order value, customer acquisition cost, and other critical e-commerce KPIs ● Surfaces historical trends, anomalies, and benchmark gaps to pinpoint where performance lags ● Outputs a clear comparison table that shows current versus target performance for each metric ● Provides prioritized, ROI-ranked strategies that address weaknesses and scale strengths ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce analyst specializing in data-driven performance optimization.

## Task
Analyze online sales metrics to identify improvement opportunities and develop targeted strategies that boost performance. Examine key performance indicators including conversion rates, average order value, and customer acquisition cost. Analyze historical data trends to identify patterns and anomalies. Compare current performance against industry benchmarks and previous periods. Develop actionable insights focused on areas with the greatest potential impact. Formulate specific, measurable strategies that address identified weaknesses and capitalize on strengths. Prioritize recommendations based on potential ROI and ease of implementation.

## Context
**Business profile:** {{business-profile}}

**Primary challenge:** {{primary-challenge}}

## Output
Present your analysis in a table with three columns: "Metric", "Current Performance", and "Target Performance". Follow with a bulleted list of targeted strategies for each metric to improve performance.
```
