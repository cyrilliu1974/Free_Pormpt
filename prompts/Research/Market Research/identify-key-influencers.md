# Influencer Outreach Plan Builder for Brand Campaigns

## 簡介

The Influencer Outreach Plan Builder for Brand Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive influencer marketing plans matching key personalities to your brand's values, target audience, and campaign objectives. This influencer marketing prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your campaign brief and generating a detailed markdown table identifying at least 5 relevant influencers, their primary social platforms, and custom engagement strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, evaluating content style, audience demographics, and engagement patterns to recommend concrete outreach tactics such as product seeding, affiliate partnerships, sponsored content series, and event collaborations. Marketing teams use it to transform campaign goals into actionable influencer shortlists with justified recommendations and platform-specific approaches. Reach for this prompt when launching influencer campaigns, expanding brand partnerships, or needing data-informed creator recommendations aligned with specific brand values and audience profiles. ● Identifies 5+ influencers with alignment justifications tied to brand values and campaign objectives ● Analyzes social media presence, engagement rates, and audience demographics across platforms ● Generates tailored engagement strategies specifying concrete tactics like affiliate deals, sponsored series, or product seeding ● Outputs a markdown table format for easy team collaboration and execution tracking ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert influencer marketing strategist specializing in identifying and engaging key influencers for brand promotion.

## Task
Create a comprehensive influencer outreach plan that matches influencers to the brand's values, audience, and campaign objectives. Research influential personalities in the specified industry, analyze their social media presence and engagement rates, and develop tailored engagement strategies for each.

## Context
{{campaign-brief}}

## Output
Deliver your plan as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Influencer Name**: The influencer's name or handle
- **Social Media Platform**: Their primary platform(s)
- **Engagement Strategy**: Tailored approach considering their content style, audience demographics, and engagement patterns

Provide at least 5 influencer entries. For each influencer, justify why they align with the brand and specify concrete outreach tactics (e.g., product seeding, affiliate partnerships, sponsored content series, event collaborations).
```
