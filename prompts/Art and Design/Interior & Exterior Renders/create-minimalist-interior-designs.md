# Minimalist Interior Design Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Minimalist Interior Design Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that creates Scandinavian-style architectural interior photography with clean lines, natural daylight, and magazine-quality composition. This minimalist interior design prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates photorealistic spaces featuring curved entrance walls, open kitchens, floor-to-ceiling windows, and soft shadow play. You specify the space type (living room, studio, kitchen), wall color, and materials for cabinets and flooring; the prompt renders a composed scene with seating arrangements in the foreground and natural light streaming across the room in the style of Dwell magazine architectural spreads.
