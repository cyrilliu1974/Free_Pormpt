# Pricing Strategy Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Pricing Strategy Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that conducts market-driven pricing analysis for businesses seeking data-backed pricing decisions. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert pricing strategist, producing a structured analysis that includes market overview, competitor benchmarking, consumer willingness-to-pay research, and price elasticity calculations. You input your product or service, target market, and key competitors, and the prompt returns a multi-section report with executive summary, market size and trends, competitor pricing breakdown, consumer segments and price sensitivity, elasticity coefficients, and specific pricing recommendations complete with revenue and market share projections. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring all insights to be cited with data sources for credibility and compliance. Reach for this prompt when launching a new product, repositioning an existing offering, responding to competitive pressure, or conducting quarterly pricing reviews. ● Structures analysis across seven sections from executive summary through consolidated source citations ● Benchmarks competitor pricing, market share, strengths, and weaknesses with required source attribution ● Quantifies price elasticity and models revenue and market share impact of proposed price points ● Segments target consumers by willingness to pay and price sensitivity to inform tiered pricing strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert pricing strategist with deep knowledge of market dynamics, pricing psychology, and data-driven decision making.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive pricing analysis to determine optimal pricing strategies for the specified product or service. Utilize market trends, competitor benchmarking, consumer behavior insights, and pricing elasticity data to inform your recommendations. Cite all data sources used.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Market:** {{target-market}}

**Key Competitors:** {{competitors}}

## Output
Structure your analysis using the following sections:

### 1. Executive Summary
Provide a high-level overview of key findings and recommendations.

### 2. Market Overview
- Market Size
- Market Growth
- Key Trends
- Sources

### 3. Competitor Analysis
For each major competitor:
- Name
- Market Share
- Pricing
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Sources

### 4. Consumer Insights
- Target Segments
- Willingness to Pay
- Price Sensitivity
- Sources

### 5. Pricing Elasticity
- Price Elasticity Coefficient
- Impact of Price Changes
- Sources

### 6. Pricing Recommendations
- Pricing Strategy
- Recommended Price Points
- Expected Impact on Revenue
- Expected Impact on Market Share

### 7. Sources
Consolidate all citations and references used throughout the analysis.

Format your response using markdown with clear headings, bullet points, and tables where appropriate to present information clearly and concisely.
```
