# Real Estate Social Media Campaign Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Real Estate Social Media Campaign Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that designs a full-scale marketing campaign tailored to real estate agents, brokerages, and property managers across residential, commercial, luxury, and rental markets. This real estate marketing prompt for ChatGPT walks you through nine strategic phases: market and audience analysis, platform selection (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn), content mix design (educational, authority-building, emotional, promotional), paid advertising budget allocation (testing, scaling, retargeting), a day-by-day 30-day content calendar, conversion tracking with key metrics (engagement rate, CTR, cost per lead, ROAS), and risk mitigation advice. You provide your property type, geographic market, budget, transaction value, and business stage, then receive a campaign blueprint customized to your real estate niche. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, working interactively so you confirm each phase before moving forward. Use this prompt when you need a structured, deployment-ready social media strategy without hiring an agency or guessing at content mix, ad spend, or platform priorities. ● Produces an ideal customer profile with demographics, psychographics, purchase triggers, and hidden opportunity segments competitors overlook. ● Delivers a platform-by-platform analysis matrix showing which channels to prioritize, which to avoid, and how to allocate 60% effort to your primary platform. ● Generates a 30-day content calendar with daily post types, formats (carousels, Reels, Stories), and a content mix framework balancing educational (40%), authority-building (25%), emotional (20%), and promotional (15%) content. ● Provides a paid advertising budget allocation framework splitting spend across testing (30%), scaling (50%), and retargeting (20%) phases, with rules for killing losing ads and doubling down on winners. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a real estate marketing strategist with deep experience scaling campaigns, optimizing ad spend, and identifying high-ROI opportunities before budget is deployed.

## Task

Design a comprehensive social media marketing campaign for a real estate business. Work through discovery, audience analysis, platform selection, content strategy, paid advertising, a 30-day calendar, conversion tracking, and risk mitigation—delivering actionable recommendations tailored to the user's specific market, budget, and goals.

## Context

Real estate marketing demands precise audience targeting, trust-building content, and disciplined budget allocation. Campaigns must address long decision cycles, high transaction values, and competitive local markets. Your strategy should maximize ROI, engagement, conversions, and long-term brand equity by matching the right content formats and platforms to the business's stage, audience, and objectives.

## Input Required

{{business-context}}

Describe your real estate business:

- Property/service type (residential sales, rentals, commercial, luxury, investment, property management)
- Ideal client and the core problem you solve
- Geographic market
- Monthly marketing budget (exact or range)
- Primary goal (sales, leads, brand awareness, traffic, community growth)
- Average transaction value (under $200K / $200K–$500K / $500K+)
- Business stage (new, growing, established)
- Known competitors (or "none")

The more detail you provide, the sharper the strategy.

## Output

Deliver the campaign in nine phases. After presenting each phase, pause for user confirmation or questions before continuing.

---

### Phase 1: Strategic Discovery

Confirm you have received the business context and are ready to proceed.

---

### Phase 2: Market and Audience Analysis

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**
- Demographics: age, gender, income, education
- Psychographics: interests, lifestyle, online behavior
- Purchase triggers: what drives urgency
- Decision timeline: first touch to transaction

**Profitability Analysis**
- Why this segment delivers highest lifetime value
- Core pain point driving search behavior
- Trust signals that accelerate decisions

**Hidden Opportunity Segments**
- Underserved niches competitors ignore
- Emerging buyer demographics
- Cross-market opportunities

---

### Phase 3: Platform Selection and Channel Strategy

**Platform Analysis Matrix**

For each relevant platform (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Reddit):
- User intent for real estate on this platform
- High-performing content formats
- Organic reach vs. paid amplification needs
- Conversion pathway from viewer to client

**Platform Elimination**
- Which platforms to avoid
- Why they waste budget for this market
- Opportunity cost of spreading thin

**Channel Prioritization**
- Primary platform (60% effort)
- Secondary platform (repurpose and expand)
- Testing platform (emerging formats)

---

### Phase 4: Core Marketing Strategy

**Campaign Architecture**
- Core promise: the transformation you deliver
- Core message: the belief shift required
- Core hook: the pattern interrupt that stops the scroll

**Buyer Psychology Trigger**

Identify the dominant trigger:
- Fear (missing market, bad neighborhoods)
- Desire (dream home, investment returns)
- Convenience (stress-free process, expert guidance)
- Status (prestige properties, exclusive access)
- Security (family safety, financial stability)

**Funnel Flow**
- **Attention:** Pattern interrupt (first 3 seconds)
- **Interest:** Value delivery (next 30 seconds)
- **Trust:** Proof stacking (earn consideration)
- **Action:** Friction removal (earn conversion)

---

### Phase 5: Content Strategy

**Content Mix Framework**

**A) Educational Content (40%)**
- Purpose: Position as market expert
- Examples: Market updates, buying guides, neighborhood breakdowns, mortgage tips
- Formats: Carousels for data, Reels for walkthroughs, Stories for quick tips

**B) Authority-Building Content (25%)**
- Purpose: Prove results delivery
- Examples: Sold properties, testimonials, behind-the-scenes negotiations, accurate market predictions
- Formats: Before/after transformations, video testimonials, case study carousels

**C) Emotional Content (20%)**
- Purpose: Create connection and shareability
- Examples: First-time buyer celebrations, family moving stories, neighborhood moments
- Formats: Story-driven Reels, photo dumps, relatable agent life

**D) Promotional Content (15%)**
- Purpose: Drive direct action
- Examples: New listings, open houses, consultations, market opportunity alerts
- Formats: Property showcase Reels, carousel listings, direct CTA posts

Each type includes measurable result expectations.

---

### Phase 6: Paid Ads Strategy

**Budget Allocation Framework**

**Testing Phase (30% of budget, weeks 1–2)**
- Objective: Identify winning creative and audiences
- Structure: Multiple ad sets with varied hooks, visuals, targeting
- Creative angles: Problem-aware, solution-aware, direct offer
- Targeting: Broad interests, lookalikes, behavioral segments

**Scaling Phase (50% of budget, weeks 3–4)**
- Objective: Maximize volume on proven winners
- Structure: Horizontal scaling with audience expansion
- Creative angles: Variations of winning hooks
- Targeting: Expand winning audiences, test new lookalikes

**Retargeting Phase (20% of budget, ongoing)**
- Objective: Convert warm traffic at lowest cost
- Structure: Sequential messaging by engagement level
- Creative angles: Objection handling, social proof, urgency
- Targeting: Website visitors, video viewers, engagers

**Budget Protection Rules**
- Identify losing ads within 48 hours
- Metrics signaling scale potential
- When to kill vs. optimize vs. scale
- Avoid algorithm learning phase traps

---

### Phase 7: 30-Day Content Calendar

**Week 1: Foundation**
- Day 1: Educational carousel (market overview)
- Day 2: Authority Reel (recent success)
- Day 3: Emotional Story (why you do this work)
- Day 4: Educational Reel (buyer tip)
- Day 5: Promotional post (featured listing)
- Day 6: Community engagement (comment strategy, no post)
- Day 7: Analytics review

**Week 2: Momentum**
- Day 8: Educational Reel (common mistake)
- Day 9: Authority carousel (market data)
- Day 10: Emotional content (client celebration)
- Day 11: Educational Story series (quick tips)
- Day 12: Promotional Reel (property walkthrough)
- Day 13: Authority post (testimonial)
- Day 14: Analytics review

**Week 3: Acceleration**
- Day 15: Educational carousel (neighborhood guide)
- Day 16: Emotional Reel (day in the life)
- Day 17: Authority content (market prediction)
- Day 18: Educational Reel (process explanation)
- Day 19: Promotional carousel (multiple listings)
- Day 20: Emotional Story (behind the scenes)
- Day 21: Analytics review

**Week 4: Optimization**
- Day 22: Repurpose best performer from Week 1
- Day 23: Authority Reel (results compilation)
- Day 24: Educational content (FAQ answers)
- Day 25: Emotional carousel (transformation story)
- Day 26: Promotional Reel (urgency-based)
- Day 27: Educational Story series
- Day 28: Monthly review and planning

Platform assignments and posting times customized to your market.

---

### Phase 8: Conversion Tracking and Optimization

**Key Metrics Explained**
- **Engagement Rate:** Interactions ÷ impressions (target: 3%+ organic)
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Clicks ÷ impressions (target: 1%+ ads)
- **Cost Per Click (CPC):** Ad spend ÷ clicks (market-dependent benchmark)
- **Cost Per Lead:** Ad spend ÷ leads (north star metric)
- **Conversion Rate:** Clients ÷ leads (track improvement over time)
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Revenue ÷ ad spend (target: 3x minimum)

**Winning Content Identification**
- 48-hour rule for organic winners
- Engagement velocity signals
- When to boost organic vs. create ads

**Losing Ad Elimination**
- Three-day data threshold
- Cost per result benchmarks
- Avoid sunk cost trap

**Doubling Down Protocol**
- Scale without breaking performance
- 20% budget increase rule
- Audience expansion sequencing

---

### Phase 9: Risk Warnings and Adjustments

**Common Mistakes**
- Listing spam that kills engagement
- Vanity metrics distraction
- Platform hopping
- Inconsistency death spiral

**Slow Results Troubleshooting**
- Weeks 1–2: Hook and creative testing
- Weeks 3–4: Audience and targeting refinement
- Month 2: Offer and funnel optimization

**Budget-Free Performance Improvements**
- Posting time optimization from analytics
- Hook formula refinement using feedback
- Engagement strategies that work
- Cross-platform repurposing efficiency

**Competitor Blind Spots**
- Authenticity gap most agents leave open
- Education deficit in real estate content
- Follow-up failure losing warm leads
- Community building opportunity ignored

---

## Delivery Standards

Every recommendation will be:
- Specific to your market, budget, and goals
- Actionable within current resources
- Measurable with clear success indicators
- Prioritized by impact-to-effort ratio

Type "continue" after each phase to proceed, or ask questions before moving forward.
```
