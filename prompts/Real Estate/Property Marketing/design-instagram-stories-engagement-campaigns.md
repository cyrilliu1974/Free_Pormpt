# Instagram Stories Engagement Campaign for Real Estate

## 簡介

The Instagram Stories Engagement Campaign for Real Estate is a free AI prompt that designs interactive 30-day content calendars with polls, Q&A mechanics, contests, and lead conversion workflows for real estate agents. This Instagram Stories prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through eight strategic phases: market positioning, engagement psychology mapping, poll architecture, Q&A sticker campaigns, contest structures with compliance templates, algorithm optimization schedules, and DM-to-appointment conversion scripts. Real estate professionals receive daily content prompts, engagement tactics tailored to their niche (luxury, first-time buyers, investors, neighborhoods), and tracking dashboards that measure Story views, poll participation, Q&A submissions, DM conversations, and booked appointments. The output includes specific poll questions ("Dealbreaker: No garage / Small closets / Outdated bathrooms?"), Q&A series frameworks (Expert, Behind-the-Scenes, Market Insider), and three ready-to-launch contest campaigns with official rules and prize partnership strategies. Use this prompt when you need a systematic approach to turn Instagram engagement into qualified buyer and seller leads without relying on paid advertising or generic real estate templates. ● Produces 30 days of specific poll questions, Q&A prompts, and contest mechanics mapped to algorithm training, lead qualification, and relationship nurturing goals. ● Includes DM conversation scripts for initial outreach, 3-day and 7-day follow-up sequences, and appointment-setting language that converts engagement into consultations. ● Delivers compliance-ready contest rules templates with entry mechanics, eligibility requirements, prize claim processes, and partnership co-promotion strategies. ● Provides posting schedules optimized for audience active hours, Story sequence formulas (Hook, Value, Engagement, CTA), and performance tracking dashboards with conversion rate breakdowns by content type. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an Instagram Stories strategist specializing in real estate lead generation through interactive engagement mechanics (polls, Q&A stickers, contests). You focus on algorithm-friendly content that converts casual viewers into qualified leads through conversation, not advertising.

## Task

Design an 8-phase Instagram Stories campaign with a 30-day execution plan, including daily content, engagement tactics, DM conversion scripts, and performance tracking.

## Context

Real estate agents compete in oversaturated markets where professional ads underperform authentic, interactive content. Success depends on training the Instagram algorithm through consistent, high-engagement Stories, using interactive features to qualify leads while building relationships, converting interactions into DMs and appointments, and sustaining momentum with a repeatable content system.

{{agent-profile}} should describe: real estate specialty and target market (first-time buyers, luxury, investors, specific neighborhoods), current Instagram presence (follower count, Story frequency, typical engagement rates), and qualified lead definition (what makes someone sales-ready, lead volume goals).

## Output

### Phase 1: Market Position Discovery
Analyze {{agent-profile}} to identify:
- Niche-specific engagement triggers and content angles
- Algorithm optimization opportunities given current presence
- Campaign architecture that positions the agent as the go-to expert

### Phase 2: Engagement Psychology Mapping
Map psychological triggers for the agent's target market:
- Curiosity gaps that compel taps and votes
- Decision anxieties polls can address (financing fears, timing concerns, market confusion)
- Insider knowledge angles that build authority
- Conversation starters that naturally lead to DMs

Deliver an engagement trigger profile with content themes specific to this market segment.

### Phase 3: Poll Strategy Architecture
Create a 30-day poll content calendar with three poll types:

**Market Research Polls** (algorithm training):
- "Which kitchen style would you renovate first: Modern white / Warm wood tones?"
- "Dream backyard: Pool / Outdoor kitchen / Both?"
- "Dealbreaker: No garage / Small closets / Outdated bathrooms?"

**Qualifying Polls** (lead identification):
- "Thinking about buying/selling: This year / Next year / Just exploring?"
- "What's holding you back: Finding the right home / Getting approved / Selling current home first?"

**Engagement Polls** (relationship building):
- "This listing is priced at $X. Your take: Too high / Just right / Great deal?"
- "Should I show you the inside? Yes / Absolutely yes"

Map each poll to campaign goals (reach, qualification, nurture). Provide 30 days of specific poll prompts tailored to {{agent-profile}}.

### Phase 4: Q&A Sticker Campaigns
Design Q&A sticker strategies across three campaign types:

**The Expert Series**: "Ask me anything about buying in [neighborhood]" / "Mortgage questions? No judgment, just answers"

**Behind-the-Scenes**: "What do you want to know about this listing?" / "Ask me what really happens at inspections"

**Market Insider**: "Which neighborhood should I feature next?" / "What's confusing about the current market?"

Include:
- Response strategy (answer publicly for expertise, DM personally for conversations)
- Scripts that transition Q&A responses into lead conversations
- Highlight curation plan for ongoing lead generation

### Phase 5: Contest and Giveaway Engine
Build 3 ready-to-launch contest campaigns using screenshot-and-DM mechanics:

**Contest Structure**:
- Entry: Follow + screenshot specific Story moment + DM the image
- Bonus entries: Answer polls, share to Story with tag
- Winner selection from DM participants

**Prize Strategy**:
- High-value, low-cost: Local gift cards, home décor items
- Service-based: Free valuation, buyer consultation, staging advice
- Partnership prizes: Co-promote with local businesses (coffee shops, furniture stores, home improvement retailers)

**Compliance**: Include official rules template with "no purchase necessary" language, eligibility requirements, winner announcement protocol, and prize claim process.

### Phase 6: Algorithm Optimization System
Create the posting and engagement system:

**Posting Schedule**:
- Optimal times based on the agent's audience active hours
- Frequency balance (visibility without fatigue)
- Story sequence formula: Hook → Value → Engagement → CTA

**Engagement Velocity**:
- First 30-minute response protocol for all interactions
- DM conversation starters that boost ranking signals
- Cross-promotion between feed posts and Stories

**Consistency Framework**: Daily Story minimum (3-5 frames), weekly content themes, monthly campaign cycles.

### Phase 7: Lead Capture and Conversion Flow
Build the engagement-to-client pipeline:

**Poll to Pipeline**: Identify hot responses (buying this year, ready to sell). DM script: "Hey [name]! Saw you voted [response]—would love to chat about what you're looking for. No pressure, just curious!"

**Q&A to Appointment**: Categorize questions by intent (browsing vs. serious buyer/seller signals), escalate with public answer → personalized DM → phone call offer.

**Contest to Client**: Winner follow-up sequence, non-winner nurture messages ("Thanks for participating! I'd still love to help with your real estate goals"), participation appreciation strategy.

Provide DM templates for initial outreach, 3-day and 7-day follow-up sequences, and appointment-setting scripts.

### Phase 8: 30-Day Campaign Launch Plan
Assemble the complete execution roadmap:

**Week 1 - Foundation**: Daily market research polls, first Q&A session, audience warm-up content, engagement baseline establishment

**Week 2 - Acceleration**: Contest launch, increased DM engagement, conversation initiation with poll responders, qualifying question integration

**Week 3 - Peak Engagement**: Contest winner announcement, user-generated content sharing, lead qualification intensification, hot lead identification

**Week 4 - Conversion Focus**: Hot lead follow-up, appointment setting push, performance review, campaign optimization recommendations

**Success Metrics Dashboard**: Story views trend, poll participation rate, Q&A submission volume, DM conversations started, qualified leads generated, appointments booked, conversion rate by content type.

Deliver the complete 30-day content calendar with daily specific actions, weekly milestone goals, and performance tracking template.
```
