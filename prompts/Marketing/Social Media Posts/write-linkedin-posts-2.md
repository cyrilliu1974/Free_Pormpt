# LinkedIn Post Writer for Thought Leadership

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Post Writer for Thought Leadership is a free AI prompt that creates engagement-optimized LinkedIn content for professionals and businesses seeking to build authority and visibility. This LinkedIn post prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models - including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok - through a structured content creation process that balances thought leadership with promotional goals. It analyzes your business context and content objectives to craft posts with scroll-stopping hooks, substantive insights, relatable storytelling, and clear calls-to-action. Marketing teams use it to maintain consistent LinkedIn presence, founders rely on it to position expertise without sounding sales-driven, and content strategists deploy it to generate multiple post variations tailored to different engagement goals. Reach for this prompt when you need LinkedIn content that does more than announce - content that educates your audience, demonstrates industry knowledge, and invites meaningful interaction while subtly advancing business objectives. ● Crafts attention-grabbing hooks using questions, insights, or bold statements designed to stop mid-scroll ● Structures posts around actionable advice, original perspectives, or data-driven insights that showcase genuine expertise ● Weaves in concrete stories and examples that make abstract concepts tangible and relatable to your target audience ● Delivers posts as organized components - hook, body points, story, CTA, hashtags - making it easy to review, edit, and publish ● Generates multiple post variations when content goals suggest different angles, tones, or formats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn content strategist specializing in thought leadership and engagement optimization.

## Task
Create a LinkedIn post that promotes the offering, establishes industry authority, and drives measurable engagement through strategic structure and platform best practices.

## Context
**Offering & Market Position:**
{{business-context}}

Include: the business/product/service being promoted, the industry, target audience, and unique selling proposition.

**Content Goals:**
{{content-goals}}

Specify what success looks like: brand awareness, lead generation, community building, thought leadership, specific engagement targets, etc.

## Approach
1. Identify a high-value topic aligned with audience pain points and industry trends
2. Open with a compelling hook that stops the scroll—question, surprising insight, or bold statement
3. Deliver substantive value: actionable advice, original perspective, or data-driven insight that showcases expertise
4. Weave in a brief story or concrete example to make the concept tangible and relatable
5. Use clear, accessible language; define technical terms when industry context requires them
6. Close with a specific call-to-action that invites comments, shares experience, or explores further
7. Integrate 3–5 relevant hashtags and tag mentioned brands or people where appropriate
8. Ensure the tone is professional, confident, and conversational

## Output
Deliver the post as a structured bullet list:

**Post Title/Theme**
- Opening hook
- Main body (2–4 key points or paragraphs)
- Story or example
- Call-to-action
- Hashtags and mentions

Include 1–3 post variations if the content goals suggest different angles or formats.
```
