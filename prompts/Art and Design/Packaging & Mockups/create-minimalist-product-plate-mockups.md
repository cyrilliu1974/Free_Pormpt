# Minimalist Product Plate Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Product Plate Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic overhead flat-lay scenes for product photography and e-commerce listings. This product plate mockup prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana takes your uploaded product image and composes it on a centered dinner plate with matching fork and knife, preserving the original shape, label, and colors of your item. You control the surface material (marble, wood, concrete), tableware style (ceramic, porcelain, metal), and lighting mood to match your brand aesthetic. The result is a professional flat-lay mockup with accurate contact shadows and reflections, ideal for food products, cosmetics, supplements, or any small consumer goods that benefit from lifestyle staging. Reach for this prompt when you need fast, consistent product photography without a physical shoot or when testing packaging concepts across different table settings. ● Perfect 90-degree overhead composition with symmetrical fork-and-knife placement ● Preserves your product's exact shape, label artwork, and color palette without alteration ● Customizable surface materials, tableware styles, and lighting moods via three variables ● Outputs 1:1 or 4:5 high-resolution mockups suitable for Instagram, Amazon listings, and pitch decks ## Prompt

```
Perfect 90-degree overhead flat lay of a single centered dinner plate on a {{surface-material}} background. The uploaded product is placed naturally in the center of the plate at realistic scale, completely unaltered—preserve its exact shape, label, colors, and proportions. A {{tableware-style}} plate with matching fork (left) and knife (right) arranged symmetrically. {{lighting-mood}} lighting that respects the product's existing light direction, with natural soft contact shadow beneath the product and accurate reflections. Ultra-photorealistic materials and textures. 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio, very high resolution. No text, logos, watermarks, or overlay. The product must be the real uploaded image integrated seamlessly, not a generated replacement or placeholder object.
```
