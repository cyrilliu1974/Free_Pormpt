# Customer Review and Backlink Incentive System Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Review and Backlink Incentive System Builder is a free AI prompt that designs a complete review generation system for businesses looking to drive testimonials with SEO value. This customer review system prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a strategic framework that spans customer segmentation, multi-channel solicitation campaigns (email, social media, in-app), a two-tier incentive structure rewarding both standard and backlink-inclusive reviews, a four-step moderation workflow, showcase strategies for your website, and measurement KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured markdown plan with clear headings, bullet lists, and a process flow diagram. Use it when you need to systematically attract high-quality testimonials that improve SEO rankings while maintaining content quality and customer engagement. ● Identifies customer segments most likely to deliver valuable, backlink-rich testimonials based on your business context ● Designs a multi-channel solicitation campaign across email drip sequences, social platforms, landing pages, and in-app messaging ● Structures a tiered reward program that differentiates between text-only and backlink-inclusive reviews ● Maps a four-step moderation workflow from submission through publication, including quality review and approval cycles ● Defines 3-5 KPIs to track review submission rates, backlink inclusion, SEO traffic impact, and conversion influence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in customer engagement, funnel optimization, and review generation systems.

## Task
Design a comprehensive end-to-end system to incentivize customers to provide reviews and testimonials that include backlinks. Create both the strategic framework and a detailed implementation plan.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include: your website URL, target customer segments, and preferred outreach channels (email, social media platforms, in-app, etc.).

## Output
Deliver a structured review incentive system with the following components:

**Review Incentive System Overview**
- High-level description of the proposed system and its objectives

**Customer Segmentation Strategy**
- Identify customer segments most likely to provide high-quality, backlink-rich reviews
- List key customer attributes for each segment

**Multi-Channel Solicitation Campaign**
- Email drip campaign with specific touchpoint sequences
- Targeted social media outreach across specified platforms
- Custom landing page with prominent calls-to-action
- In-app/on-site messaging strategy

**Incentive Structure**
Two-tier rewards program:
1. Tier 1: Reward for text-only review
2. Tier 2: Enhanced reward for review with backlink

**Review Moderation & Approval Workflow**
Define a 4-step process covering:
- Submission intake
- Quality review
- Approval/revision cycle
- Publication

**Review Showcase Strategy**
- Dedicated reviews page
- Homepage carousel featuring top reviews
- Social proof widgets on pricing, features, and FAQ pages
- Automated social media posts highlighting latest reviews

**Measurement & Optimization**
Define 3-5 key performance indicators to track:
- Review submission rate
- Backlink inclusion rate
- Traffic/SEO impact
- Conversion influence

**Visual Flow Diagram**
Describe a detailed process flow diagram showing the customer journey from solicitation through publication, including decision points, automation triggers, and feedback loops.

Use markdown formatting with clear headings, bullet points, and numbered lists.
```
