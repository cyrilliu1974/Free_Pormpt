# Hyperrealistic Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces professional studio-quality portrait images for fashion brands, e-commerce retailers, and content creators. This hyperrealistic portrait photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana specifies 8K resolution, DSLR aesthetics with soft film grain, precise subject features, clothing and footwear details, relaxed sitting poses, and soft natural lighting against minimalist backgrounds. The prompt includes three customizable variables - footwear, surface-or-prop, and subject-details - so you can adapt the model's appearance, accessories, and environment while maintaining photorealistic texture and lighting consistency. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-ready portrait photography without booking studio time or hiring models. ● Produces 8K photorealistic portraits with DSLR film grain, natural skin tones, and studio lighting. ● Lets you customize footwear, sitting surface, and subject characteristics through three template variables. ● Defines exact pose (sitting, elbow on knee, hand touching cheek), expression (slight playful smile), and camera angle. ● Outputs clean minimalist compositions ideal for lookbooks, product showcases, social media, and lifestyle campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic 8K portrait, DSLR photography with soft film grain and natural colors. Young woman with fair skin, delicate features, shoulder-length straight black hair with blunt bangs, slight playful smile, looking at camera. Black fitted long-sleeve top, light blue loose high-waisted frayed-hem jeans cropped to expose ankles, {{footwear}} fully visible. Sitting casually on {{surface-or-prop}}, relaxed pose with one elbow resting on knee, hand gently touching cheek. {{subject-details}}. Clean minimalist background with curved white wall, soft natural lighting from above. Highly detailed, photorealistic render.
```
