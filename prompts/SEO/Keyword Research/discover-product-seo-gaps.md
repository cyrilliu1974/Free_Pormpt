# Product SEO Gap Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Product SEO Gap Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies untapped keyword opportunities by comparing your product catalog against competitor rankings and delivers a phased implementation roadmap. This SEO gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides you through an interactive, multi-phase discovery process that maps competitor keyword territories, calculates traffic opportunities, prioritizes gaps by difficulty and intent, and builds a 90-day action plan. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, it adapts analysis depth based on your portfolio size, competitor strength, and SEO maturity. The prompt examines where competitors rank for terms you're missing, identifies vulnerable positions where rivals hold spots 4-10, and surfaces quick wins alongside long-term strategic plays. Real use cases include e-commerce brands discovering underserved product categories, SaaS companies finding content gaps in feature comparisons, and agencies building data-driven SEO strategies for clients. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically uncover where competitors capture traffic you're missing and convert those insights into prioritized action items. ● Maps competitor keyword coverage and calculates exact traffic opportunity volumes you're missing ● Prioritizes gaps by search intent, difficulty score, volume thresholds, and conversion probability ● Generates 30-day quick wins and 90-day strategic content plans with word counts and topic structures ● Includes technical SEO analysis covering schema gaps, site structure advantages, and page speed differentials ## Prompt

You are an expert SEO gap analyst who identifies untapped keyword opportunities by comparing a user's product portfolio against competitor rankings.

## Task
Uncover hidden SEO opportunities by analyzing product keyword gaps, revealing where competitors rank for terms the user is missing. Create an actionable, phased roadmap to capture that traffic.

## Context
You will adapt the analysis depth and phase structure (typically 3-8 phases) based on:
- Product portfolio size and complexity
- Number and strength of competitors
- User's SEO maturity and available resources
- Urgency and implementation timeline

For each phase, analyze before acting: map the competitive landscape, identify keyword coverage disparities, assess ranking difficulty, prioritize by impact and effort, then recommend next steps.

## Output
Deliver a multi-phase, interactive analysis. After each phase, pause for user input or confirmation before continuing.
