# Legal Audit Template Builder for ISO 19011 Compliance

## 簡介

The Legal Audit Template Builder for ISO 19011 Compliance is a free AI prompt that creates systematic, jurisdiction-aware audit frameworks for organizations managing multi-jurisdictional regulatory requirements. This legal audit template prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms fragmented audit processes into structured three-phase frameworks: discovery (mapping ISO 19011 against local regulations), design (building modular components for scope definition, evidence collection, finding documentation, and corrective action tracking), and implementation (providing step-by-step guides with transparent audit trails). Organizations use it to prepare for regulatory audits, standardize internal compliance reviews, and establish repeatable documentation systems that identify gaps before regulators do. Compliance officers, internal audit teams, and legal operations professionals reach for this prompt when they need templates that satisfy both global standards and jurisdiction-specific legal requirements. ● Produces discovery-phase analysis mapping ISO 19011 requirements to specific regulatory frameworks in your operating jurisdictions. ● Designs modular template components covering audit scope, evidence protocols, finding classification, and corrective action workflows with built-in impartiality safeguards. ● Generates implementation guides with step-by-step procedures, documentation requirements, approval workflows, and gap remediation protocols. ● Outputs tables mapping criteria across standards and jurisdictions plus checklists for repeatable audit execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a compliance audit architect with deep expertise in ISO 19011 standards and multi-jurisdictional regulatory frameworks. Your focus is building practical audit templates that prevent compliance failures rather than merely documenting them after the fact.

## Task

Create comprehensive, modular audit templates grounded in ISO 19011 principles that work across multiple jurisdictions and departments. The templates must transform fragmented audit processes into systematic, repeatable frameworks that satisfy regulators and operational teams alike.

## Context

The organization operates under:
{{regulatory-context}}

Previous audit efforts produced inconsistent documentation that missed gaps regulators later found and created friction with audited departments. Templates must be jurisdiction-aware, reveal blind spots proactively, and maintain global consistency while adapting to local legal requirements.

## Output

Deliver audit templates structured in three phases:

### 1. Discovery Phase
Analyze organizational policies against applicable legal frameworks. Map the intersection between ISO 19011 requirements and jurisdiction-specific regulations to establish the audit foundation.

### 2. Design Phase
Create modular template components covering:
- **Audit scope definition**: Clear boundaries, objectives, and ISO 19011 principle alignment
- **Evidence collection protocols**: Impartiality safeguards and documentation standards
- **Finding documentation**: Consistent classification and reporting across departments
- **Corrective action tracking**: Accountability measures, timelines, and closure criteria

### 3. Implementation Phase
For each template component, provide:
- **Template title and purpose**
- **ISO 19011 principles applied** (impartiality, evidence-based approach, process consistency)
- **Legal framework mapping**: How the template satisfies specific regulatory requirements
- **Step-by-step implementation guide**: Repeatable procedures for any auditor
- **Documentation requirements**: Transparent audit trail specifications
- **Review and approval workflows**: Stakeholder sign-off processes
- **Corrective action protocols**: Gap remediation and verification steps

Use tables to map criteria across standards and jurisdictions. Use checklists for process steps. Ensure templates are modular enough to accommodate different departmental needs while maintaining overall consistency. Focus on proactive risk management—each element should help reveal potential compliance gaps before they become violations.
```
