# Instagram Ad Copy Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Instagram Ad Copy Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates engagement-focused Instagram advertisements tailored to specific customer segments and conversion goals. This Instagram ad copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write concise, visually-oriented captions (125–150 words) optimized for Instagram's fast-scrolling feed environment. It structures each ad around six core elements: an attention-grabbing hook, a clear value proposition that differentiates your product or service, sensory language that helps audiences visualize benefits, optional social proof for credibility, an urgent call-to-action, and complementary visual suggestions. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone and messaging to match your target customer's interests and pain points. Marketing teams use it to quickly draft multiple ad variants for A/B testing, while small business owners rely on it to compete with larger brands without hiring agencies. ● Produces hook-first captions designed to stop mid-scroll and capture attention in crowded feeds ● Balances feature highlights with emotional benefits using sensory, tangible language ● Integrates social proof and testimonials when provided to build trust and relatability ● Delivers clear, actionable CTAs with urgency and exclusivity to drive the desired next step ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in Instagram ad copy that drives engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write an Instagram ad that showcases {{product-service}} to {{target-customer}}, highlighting what makes it uniquely valuable and motivating them to {{desired-action}}.

## Context
The ad must cut through a crowded feed by speaking directly to the interests, needs, and pain points of {{target-customer}}. Instagram's visual-first, scroll-fast environment demands concise, sensory language and a clear value proposition delivered in the first few lines.

## Structure
1. **Hook** – Open with a provocative question, surprising fact, or bold statement that stops {{target-customer}} mid-scroll and speaks directly to their situation.
2. **Value proposition** – Clearly explain what sets {{product-service}} apart from competitors and why it matters to {{target-customer}}. Be specific about features and benefits that solve their problems or fulfill their desires.
3. **Sensory appeal** – Use vivid, tangible language to help {{target-customer}} visualize or feel the product's impact on their life.
4. **Social proof** (if provided in {{product-service}}) – Weave in a testimonial snippet or user endorsement that builds credibility and relatability.
5. **Call-to-action** – End with a direct, urgent CTA that tells {{target-customer}} exactly what to do next: {{desired-action}}. Create a sense of exclusivity or timeliness.
6. **Visual suggestion** (optional) – Recommend a complementary image or video concept that amplifies the message and engages the senses.

## Output
Deliver the ad copy optimized for Instagram's format: concise (125–150 words for the caption), front-loaded with the strongest elements, and designed to pair with eye-catching visuals. Ensure the CTA is unmissable.
```
