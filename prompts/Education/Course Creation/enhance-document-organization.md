# Educational Content Structure Builder for Courses

## 簡介

The Educational Content Structure Builder for Courses is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive seven-part learning framework for educators, instructional designers, and technical writers developing courses or training materials. This educational content structure prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete instructional blueprint covering introduction, foundational concepts, intermediate topics, advanced subjects, practical applications, conclusions, and further learning resources. It works by taking your topic and audience familiarity level as inputs, then generating a logical progression that breaks complex ideas into digestible sections with clear transitions, examples, case studies, and hands-on exercises. Educators building online courses, corporate trainers designing employee development programs, and technical writers structuring documentation will find it transforms high-level subject matter into accessible learning pathways. The prompt runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Creates seven distinct content sections ensuring logical knowledge progression from basics to cutting-edge topics ● Tailors complexity and depth to specified audience familiarity levels, from novice to expert ● Includes practical application guidance with exercises, tools, and real-world case studies for active learning ● Provides curated resource recommendations and encourages continuous exploration beyond the core content ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert technical writer specializing in educational content design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content structure that guides learners from foundational concepts to advanced topics. The structure must be logical, accessible, and tailored to the audience's familiarity level, ensuring smooth progression and engagement throughout.

## Context
Topic: {{topic}}
Audience familiarity: {{audience-level}}

The structure should facilitate comprehension by breaking down complex ideas into digestible parts, using clear transitions between sections, and incorporating practical applications.

## Output
Develop a seven-part content structure:

**1. Introduction to the Topic**
- Provide a broad overview of {{topic}}, outlining its importance and relevance
- List prerequisites or background knowledge needed, with resources for readers to prepare

**2. Foundational Concepts**
- Introduce key foundational concepts in simple terms accessible to beginners
- Use examples and analogies to clarify complex ideas

**3. Intermediate Topics**
- Build on foundational knowledge with deeper exploration of {{topic}}
- Include case studies or real-world applications demonstrating these concepts in action

**4. Advanced Topics**
- Progress to cutting-edge aspects and latest research in {{topic}}
- Discuss potential future directions and their impact on the field

**5. Practical Application**
- Provide guidance on applying the knowledge: relevant tools, techniques, and methodologies
- Offer exercises or projects for hands-on experience

**6. Conclusion**
- Summarize key takeaways and reinforce the importance of staying current
- Encourage further exploration and continuous learning

**7. Resources for Further Learning**
- Recommend books, online courses, communities, and other resources for deeper study

Present this structure in a format ready for direct application to articles, course syllabi, or training materials.
```
