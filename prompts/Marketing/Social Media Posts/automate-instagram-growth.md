# Social Media Automation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Automation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, actionable automation plans to grow your online presence and engagement on any social platform. This social media automation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a hierarchical strategy document covering seven critical areas: hashtag research and content discovery, content creation frameworks, engagement protocols, follower outreach programs, analytics tracking, automation tool recommendations, and implementation schedules. You provide your platform (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.) and business context, and the prompt structures a complete plan with actionable tactics under each heading. Use cases include launching new brand accounts, systematizing content workflows for agencies, scaling community engagement for creators, and building repeatable growth processes for startups. Reach for this prompt when you need a comprehensive roadmap that connects automation tools to measurable growth goals, not just scattered tips. ● Produces hashtag research frameworks that identify trending, niche-relevant, and seasonal tags specific to your industry ● Delivers a content calendar structure with format mix recommendations (video, image, stories) aligned to business goals ● Outlines engagement automation protocols for comments, DMs, and community interaction without sacrificing authenticity ● Recommends specific automation tools, integration approaches, and budget considerations for scheduling and analytics ● Includes implementation timelines with posting frequency, optimal timing, and monthly review checkpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist developing a comprehensive automation strategy for platform growth.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable plan that leverages automation tools and techniques to boost online presence and engagement. Structure your strategy with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for detailed tactics.

## Context
Platform: {{platform}}
Business context: {{business-context}}

Include these strategic components:

**Hashtag Research & Content Discovery**
- Identify trending and niche-relevant hashtags
- Competitive hashtag analysis
- Seasonal and evergreen tag recommendations

**Content Creation Strategy**
- Content pillars aligned with business goals and audience needs
- Content calendar framework
- Format mix (video, image, text, stories)

**Engagement Tactics**
- Automated response frameworks
- Community interaction protocols
- Comment and DM management approaches

**Follower Outreach Program**
- Target audience identification methods
- Connection and networking tactics
- Collaboration and partnership opportunities

**Analytics & Performance Tracking**
- Key metrics to monitor
- Reporting cadence and benchmarks
- A/B testing recommendations

**Automation Tools & Software**
- Recommended platforms for scheduling, analytics, and engagement
- Integration suggestions
- Budget considerations

**Implementation Schedule**
- Content posting frequency and optimal times
- Engagement activity blocks
- Monthly strategy review checkpoints

## Output
Deliver the complete automation strategy using clear hierarchical structure with main headings (bold), subheadings, and bullet points for granular tactics. Ensure each section is actionable and specific to the platform and business context provided.
```
