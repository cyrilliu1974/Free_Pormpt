# Keyword Gap Analysis Prompt for SEO Research

## 簡介

The Keyword Gap Analysis Prompt for SEO Research is a free AI prompt that identifies high-value keyword opportunities your competitors rank for but your website does not. This keyword gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by systematically comparing your site's keyword rankings against multiple competitors in your industry sector, evaluating each gap keyword for search volume, difficulty, and strategic fit, then organizing the findings into a prioritized markdown table. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a structured report that shows which competitor owns each opportunity keyword and explains the potential traffic and conversion impact. SEO analysts and content strategists use it to uncover blind spots in their keyword strategy, prioritize content creation efforts, and build data-driven plans to capture organic search traffic their competitors are already winning. Reach for this prompt when you need to benchmark your keyword footprint against 2-5 direct competitors and want a clear, actionable view of where to invest your optimization effort. ● Analyzes keyword rankings across your website and each named competitor to find gaps ● Evaluates every gap keyword for monthly search volume, competition difficulty, and relevance to your industry ● Delivers 5-8 prioritized opportunities per competitor in a markdown table with strategic value explanations ● Surfaces keywords with strong search demand and manageable competition that represent real growth potential ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst conducting a comprehensive keyword gap analysis.

## Task
Identify untapped keyword opportunities for the target website compared to its top competitors. Deliver actionable insights organized by competitor, showing high-value keywords they rank for that the target does not.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Industry: {{industry-sector}}
Competitors to analyze: {{competitor-list}}

Focus on keywords with strong search volume, manageable competition, and high relevance to the industry. Prioritize keywords that represent the most significant gaps and growth opportunities.

## Process
1. Analyze current keyword rankings for the target website and each competitor
2. Identify keywords where competitors rank but the target website does not
3. Evaluate each gap keyword for search volume, competition level, and strategic fit
4. Select the most promising opportunities based on potential traffic and conversion value
5. Organize findings to show which competitor "owns" each opportunity keyword

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Competitor** (name)
- **Gap Keyword** (the keyword opportunity)
- **Search Volume** (monthly estimate)
- **Difficulty** (Low/Medium/High)
- **Strategic Value** (brief explanation of why this keyword matters and its potential impact)

Include 5-8 keyword opportunities per competitor. Rank opportunities by potential value, with the highest-impact keywords listed first.
```
