# Voice Search SEO Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Voice Search SEO Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a complete voice search optimization roadmap for businesses and SEO professionals looking to rank for voice-activated queries. This voice search SEO prompt for ChatGPT works with Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a numbered, actionable plan that addresses conversational keyword research, featured snippet optimization, Schema.org structured data implementation, and local SEO adjustments tailored to voice devices. You provide your website URL, target audience, offerings, location, and current SEO challenges, and the prompt returns specific implementation steps with priority levels, timelines, and measurement criteria for each recommendation. SEO agencies use it to audit client sites for voice readiness, e-commerce teams deploy it to capture question-based product queries, and local service businesses apply it to dominate near-me voice searches. Reach for this prompt when migrating from text-only SEO to voice-first optimization or when launching in a new geographic market where voice assistant adoption is high. ● Identifies conversational long-tail keywords and question phrases that mirror natural speech patterns ● Provides structured data markup recommendations using Schema.org vocabulary to help search engines surface content in voice results ● Delivers featured snippet optimization tactics to capture position-zero rankings that voice assistants read aloud ● Includes local SEO enhancements for Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, and proximity-based voice queries ● Outputs technical checklist items for mobile performance and page speed that affect voice search eligibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in voice search optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, actionable voice search optimization plan that enhances website visibility and ranking for voice-based queries. Address conversational keywords, featured snippet optimization, structured data implementation, and local SEO considerations for voice-activated devices.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Primary products/services: {{offerings}}
Location (for local SEO): {{location}}
Current SEO challenges: {{seo-pain-points}}

Voice search queries are typically longer and more conversational than traditional text searches. Your strategy should account for natural language patterns, question-based queries, and the prominence of position-zero results (featured snippets) in voice responses.

## Output
Provide your voice search optimization strategy as a numbered list. Each main recommendation should include:
1. The optimization technique
   a. Specific implementation steps
   b. Expected impact on voice search visibility
   c. Priority level and timeline
   d. Measurement criteria

Cover these key areas:
- Conversational keyword research and integration
- Featured snippet optimization tactics
- Structured data markup implementation (Schema.org)
- Local SEO enhancements for voice devices
- Content structure adjustments for natural language queries
- Technical considerations (page speed, mobile optimization)
- Monitoring and iteration framework
```
