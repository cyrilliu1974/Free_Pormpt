# Keyword Research Strategy Generator for Blog SEO

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Strategy Generator for Blog SEO is a free AI prompt that produces a structured list of primary keywords tailored to your blog topic and audience, balancing search volume against ranking difficulty. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an SEO specialist who breaks your topic into core themes, identifies 5-8 high-volume short-tail keywords and 10-15 targeted long-tail phrases, and evaluates each term's search volume, difficulty score, and strategic fit. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by walking through a five-step research process that mimics professional SEO workflows: theme identification, keyword discovery using industry-standard tool methodologies, short-tail selection for broad visibility, long-tail targeting for conversion intent, and metric-based justification. Use it when you need a strategic keyword foundation for a new blog, content calendar planning, or competitive repositioning. ● Produces both short-tail keywords for broad topic authority and long-tail phrases for specific search intent and lower competition ● Evaluates each keyword with search volume estimates, difficulty scores, and relevance justification to prioritize attainable wins ● Organizes output into a structured list that maps directly to content roadmaps and SEO objectives ● Mirrors professional SEO research workflows using Google Keyword Planner, SEMrush, Ahrefs, and Moz methodologies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist conducting keyword research for blog content optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive primary keyword list for a blog, balancing search volume against competitiveness. Include both short-tail (1-2 words) and long-tail (3+ words) keywords that match search intent and improve discoverability.

## Context
{{blog-topic-and-audience}}

## Process
1. **Identify Core Themes:** Break the topic into 3-5 core themes or content categories
2. **Research Keywords:** Use available SEO tools (Google Keyword Planner, SEMrush, Ahrefs, Moz, or similar) to find high-volume, manageable-competition terms for each theme
3. **Short-tail Keywords:** Select 5-8 broad, high-volume keywords (1-2 words) that define the topic's main search territory
4. **Long-tail Keywords:** Identify 10-15 specific, lower-competition phrases (3+ words) that reflect clear search intent and conversion potential
5. **Balance Metrics:** For each keyword, evaluate the search volume vs. difficulty trade-off; prioritize attainable keywords with meaningful traffic
6. **Justify Selections:** Explain each keyword's relevance to content strategy, audience needs, and ranking potential

## Output
Provide a structured list organized as:

**Short-tail Keywords**
- [Keyword] – search volume estimate, difficulty score, rationale for selection

**Long-tail Keywords**
- [Keyword phrase] – search volume estimate, difficulty score, search intent analysis, rationale

Include actionable insights on how these keywords support the blog's content roadmap and SEO objectives.
```
