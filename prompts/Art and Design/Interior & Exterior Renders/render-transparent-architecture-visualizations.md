# Transparent Architecture Visualization Generator

## 簡介

The Transparent Architecture Visualization Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-realistic renderings of radical transparent buildings for architects, developers, and visualization studios. This transparent architecture prompt for Midjourney produces full-height ultra-clear glass facades with seamless joints, minimal steel structures, and floating roof effects. The prompt generates wide-angle eye-level exterior shots with physically accurate reflections, shadows, and glass refractions that show maximum daylight penetration and warm interior glow. You customize three variables - building type, material accents, and setting context - to generate unique architectural visualizations that work across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Real use cases include client presentations, property marketing materials, concept studies, and portfolio imagery for commercial, residential, or institutional projects. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade transparent building renders that showcase radical minimalism and structural clarity without complex 3D modeling software. ● Pure rectangular geometry with frameless entrances and invisible doors that emphasize radical transparency ● Physically accurate reflections, shadows, and glass refractions for professional visualization quality ● Customizable building type, material accents, and setting to match specific project requirements ● Calm, premium, futuristic atmosphere free of ornamentation or decorative facade elements ## Prompt
