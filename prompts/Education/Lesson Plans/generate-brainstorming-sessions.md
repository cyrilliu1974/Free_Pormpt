# Collaborative Learning Activities Prompt for Teachers

## 簡介

The Collaborative Learning Activities Prompt for Teachers is a free AI prompt that generates interactive group exercises designed to promote active participation, critical thinking, teamwork, and knowledge retention for educators at any level. This collaborative learning prompt for ChatGPT produces 3-5 ready-to-use activities customized to your educational level, subject matter, and specific classroom requirements. Each exercise includes clear step-by-step instructions, estimated time frames, defined learning objectives, and adaptability notes so you can modify activities for different student needs. Teachers use it to quickly design brainstorming sessions, peer-review workshops, problem-solving challenges, and other group-based learning experiences that align with active learning pedagogy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for K-12 teachers, college instructors, corporate trainers, and curriculum developers who need structured collaborative activities without spending hours on lesson planning. ● Customizes activities to any educational level, from elementary school through university and professional training ● Provides complete activity frameworks including instructions, timing, objectives, and adaptation guidance ● Focuses on evidence-based collaborative learning strategies that boost engagement and retention ● Outputs 3-5 distinct activities per generation, giving you multiple options to fit your lesson flow ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational activities designer specializing in collaborative learning.

## Task
Generate interactive group exercises that promote active participation, critical thinking, teamwork, and knowledge retention. For each activity, provide:

- Clear step-by-step instructions
- Estimated time frame
- Specific learning objectives
- Adaptability notes

## Context
Educational level: {{educational-level}}
Subject: {{subject}}
Key constraints and requirements: {{activity-requirements}}

## Output
Deliver 3-5 collaborative learning activities in bullet point format. Each activity should be practical, immediately usable, and aligned with best practices in active learning pedagogy.
```
