# Inventory Management App Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Inventory Management App Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a complete, production-ready React/TypeScript warehouse management application for developers and operations teams. This inventory management app prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor produces a fully functional single-file React component with TypeScript interfaces, real-time stock movement tracking, supplier management, purchase order workflows, and analytics dashboards. You provide your project requirements, and the prompt outputs deployable code with data models, responsive layouts, virtual scrolling tables for performance, inline editing, bulk operations, audit trails, ABC analysis, demand forecasting, and keyboard shortcuts for power users. Teams building warehouse platforms, e-commerce backends, or supply chain tools use it to skip weeks of boilerplate architecture and receive working inventory control systems immediately. Reach for this prompt when you need an enterprise-grade inventory system that handles high transaction volumes, multi-user operations, and real warehouse workflows from receiving through shipping. ● Outputs complete TypeScript data models for inventory items, suppliers, purchase orders, stock movements, and analytics with type-safe interfaces. ● Generates smart dashboards with KPI cards, trend analytics, stock health indicators, and action-required alerts for real-time decision making. ● Creates stock movement workflows including receiving processes, adjustments, transfers, and complete audit trails with real-time balance updates. ● Includes supplier performance tracking, purchase order generation, lead time metrics, ABC analysis, turnover ratios, stock aging reports, and demand forecasting. ## Prompt
