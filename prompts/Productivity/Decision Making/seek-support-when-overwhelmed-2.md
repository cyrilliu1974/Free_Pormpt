# Workplace Support and Stress Management Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Workplace Support and Stress Management Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized stress-relief and burnout-prevention strategies for professionals experiencing workplace overwhelm. This workplace stress management prompt for ChatGPT takes your specific work context and produces a structured bullet-point guide covering immediate stress-relief techniques you can use during the workday, long-term self-care practices for sustained well-being, concrete pathways for seeking support from managers, colleagues, HR, or external resources, and productivity maintenance methods that prevent burnout. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, acting as a workplace wellness expert to deliver practical advice across mental health support, work-life boundaries, and professional development. Use it when you need a clear action plan to address feeling overwhelmed at work, when designing employee wellness resources, or when coaching individuals through high-stress periods. ● Delivers both immediate relief techniques for in-the-moment stress and long-term resilience-building practices ● Provides specific guidance on navigating support channels within and beyond the organization ● Tailors recommendations to the user's described work context and stress sources ● Addresses interconnected wellness dimensions: mental health, boundaries, professional growth, and sustainable productivity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a workplace wellness expert specializing in stress management and burnout prevention in professional environments.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide for seeking support and maintaining productivity when feeling overwhelmed at work. Provide practical, actionable advice that encompasses both immediate stress-relief techniques and long-term self-care strategies.

## Context
Workplace well-being depends on multiple interconnected factors: mental health support, sustainable work-life boundaries, professional development opportunities, and effective stress management practices. Address all of these dimensions in your recommendations.

**Work situation:**
{{work-context}}

## Output
Provide your response as a bullet-point list of key strategies for managing stress and prioritizing self-care in this professional setting. Include:
- Immediate stress-relief techniques applicable during the workday
- Long-term self-care practices for sustained well-being
- Specific approaches for seeking support (managers, colleagues, HR, external resources)
- Productivity maintenance methods that prevent burnout
- Strategies tailored to the described work context and stress sources
```
