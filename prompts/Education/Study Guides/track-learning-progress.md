# Learning Progress Tracker Builder for Students

## 簡介

The Learning Progress Tracker Builder for Students is a free AI prompt that creates personalized progress tracking systems for learners working toward specific educational or skill-development objectives. This learning progress tracker prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your subject matter, current skill level, available timeframe, and main learning objective to build a three-column markdown table mapping SMART goals to concrete milestones and achievement documentation. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering at least three populated example rows tailored to your learning context, complete with target dates, completion criteria, and reflection space. It also provides adaptation strategies that guide you on when to accelerate progress, revise scope, or adjust your approach in response to obstacles. This prompt is ideal for students, self-learners, online course participants, and anyone pursuing structured skill acquisition who needs to translate broad learning ambitions into actionable checkpoints and measurable outcomes. ● Establishes SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) aligned with your subject and timeframe ● Breaks each goal into sequential milestones with clear completion criteria and target dates ● Includes a reflection framework for documenting specific achievements and extracting lessons learned ● Provides context-specific adaptation strategies for accelerating, revising scope, or overcoming obstacles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert learning strategist specializing in personalized progress tracking and goal architecture.

## Task
Create a comprehensive learning progress tracker that helps the learner set SMART goals, break them into manageable milestones, document achievements, and adapt their plan based on progress.

## Context
{{learning-context}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the subject, current skill level, timeframe, and main objective to establish appropriate SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
2. Decompose each goal into concrete, sequential milestones with clear completion criteria
3. Design a reflection framework for documenting achievements and extracting lessons learned
4. Recommend adjustment strategies: when to accelerate, when to revise scope, and how to respond to obstacles

## Output
Deliver the progress tracker as a markdown table with three columns:

| Goals | Milestones | Achievements |
|-------|-----------|-------------|

Provide at least three example rows populated with realistic entries tailored to the learner's context. Each row should demonstrate:
- **Goals**: A single SMART goal aligned with their objective
- **Milestones**: 2-4 intermediate checkpoints with target dates
- **Achievements**: Specific outcomes or competencies gained, with space for reflection notes

After the table, include a brief section on adaptation strategies specific to their learning scenario.
```
