# Customer Referral Program Builder for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Customer Referral Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored referral marketing strategies for businesses of any size. This customer referral program prompt for ChatGPT generates a structured plan covering rewards architecture, technology platform recommendations, attribution models, and promotional tactics aligned to your company profile and budget. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a seven-section framework: company overview, measurable goals, tracking system specifications, three-tiered rewards structure with examples, multi-channel promotion strategy, success metrics with targets, and actionable launch steps. Marketing teams use it to design programs that incentivize both referrers and new customers while keeping participation friction low. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete referral marketing blueprint that balances customer psychology, operational feasibility, and ROI tracking. ● Outputs a multi-tiered rewards structure that recognizes customer milestones and scales with engagement ● Recommends specific technology platforms, attribution models, and three-step tracking processes for accurate measurement ● Includes three promotion tactics with implementation details tailored to your brand and budget ● Provides three measurable success metrics with specific targets and a reporting cadence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert consultant specializing in customer referral program design across industries and company sizes.

## Task
Develop a complete referral program plan tailored to the company's market position and budget. Deliver a multi-tiered rewards structure, tracking system, promotion strategy, and success metrics that maximize participation and ROI.

## Context
Company details:
{{company-profile}}

Referral program budget: {{budget}}

## Requirements
- Design a multi-tiered rewards structure that incentivizes both referring customers and new customers
- Specify technology platform, attribution model, and tracking processes for seamless measurement
- Provide promotion tactics aligned with the company's brand and values
- Keep the referral process simple to encourage participation
- Include measurable targets and success metrics

## Output
Deliver a structured referral program plan with:

### 1. Company Overview
- Company name, industry, target market
- Current customer base snapshot
- Marketing budget allocation

### 2. Referral Program Goals
List 3 specific, measurable goals

### 3. Referral Tracking System
- Recommended technology platform
- Attribution model
- 3-step tracking process
- Reporting cadence

### 4. Rewards Structure
- Tier 1: Milestone and reward (with example)
- Tier 2: Milestone and reward (with example)
- Tier 3: Milestone and reward (with example)

### 5. Promotion Strategy
- Tactic 1 with implementation details
- Tactic 2 with implementation details
- Tactic 3 with implementation details

### 6. Success Metrics
List 3 key metrics with specific targets

### 7. Next Steps
List 3 actionable next steps to launch the program

Format with clear markdown headers, bullet points, and numbered lists.
```
