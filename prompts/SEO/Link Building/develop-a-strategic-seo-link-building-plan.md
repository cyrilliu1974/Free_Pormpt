# SEO Link Building Strategy Plan Generator

## 簡介

The SEO Link Building Strategy Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored link acquisition roadmaps for businesses looking to improve search engine rankings through ethical backlink strategies. This link building prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your website's niche, existing backlink profile, and competitor patterns to produce a tactical markdown table with 8-12 specific outreach opportunities. It covers guest posting, resource link building, digital PR, broken link replacement, partnership opportunities, industry directories, content collaboration, and unlinked brand mentions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable tactics paired with expected ranking and traffic outcomes for each prospective website type. Marketing teams, SEO consultants, and business owners use it when they need a structured approach to earning high-quality backlinks that comply with current search engine guidelines. ● Analyzes competitor link acquisition patterns and industry-specific opportunities to identify realistic outreach targets ● Produces a structured table format with prospective websites, actionable tactics, and measurable expected outcomes ● Focuses exclusively on ethical, high-quality backlinks that align with search engine quality guidelines ● Covers diverse tactics from digital PR and content collaboration to broken link replacement and unlinked brand mentions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in white-hat link building.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive link building strategy to improve search engine rankings and drive organic traffic. Analyze the provided context, then create a tactical plan with specific outreach targets and measurable outcomes.

## Context
**Website/Business:** {{website-or-business}}
**Industry & Audience:** {{industry-and-audience}}
**SEO Baseline:** {{current-rankings-and-competitors}}

Consider:
- The website's niche and existing backlink profile
- Competitor link acquisition patterns
- Industry-specific link opportunities (directories, associations, publications)
- Current search engine guidelines and quality signals

Focus exclusively on high-quality, relevant backlinks that provide genuine value. Avoid manipulative tactics.

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Prospective Websites | Link Building Tactics | Expected Outcomes |
|---------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| [Specific site types or examples] | [Actionable outreach methods] | [Ranking/traffic/authority impact] |

Include 8-12 rows covering diverse tactics: guest posting, resource link building, digital PR, broken link replacement, partnership opportunities, industry directories, content collaboration, and unlinked brand mentions.
```
