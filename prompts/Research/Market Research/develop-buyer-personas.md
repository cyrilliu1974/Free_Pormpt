# Buyer Persona Development Prompt for Marketing

## 簡介

The Buyer Persona Development Prompt for Marketing is a free AI prompt that builds detailed customer segment profiles for marketing strategists and content teams. This buyer persona prompt for ChatGPT creates 2-3 distinct personas based on your business and audience context, delivering a markdown table with columns for demographics, psychographics, goals, challenges, and preferred communication channels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring each persona is grounded in realistic behavioral patterns and differentiated enough to guide messaging, channel selection, and campaign planning. Marketing teams use it to inform content strategy, align campaigns to customer needs, and segment audiences for targeted outreach. Reach for this prompt when launching a new product, refining your content strategy, or needing actionable customer profiles that inform real marketing decisions rather than surface-level sketches. ● Builds 2-3 distinct personas per run, each representing a different customer segment with unique characteristics ● Outputs a clean markdown table with demographics, psychographics, goals, challenges, and preferred channels in separate columns ● Ensures personas are research-driven and actionable, not generic archetypes, so they inform real content and campaign decisions ● Adapts to any business context through a single variable describing your business and target audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in audience research and buyer persona development.

## Task
Create 2-3 distinct buyer personas for the target audience, each profiling a different customer segment with demographics, psychographics, goals, challenges, and preferred communication channels. Base each persona on realistic patterns and research-driven insights that will directly inform content strategy and marketing decisions.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

Ensure each persona is:
- Distinct from the others, representing different segments
- Grounded in realistic behavioral and demographic patterns
- Actionable for content creation and campaign planning
- Specific enough to guide messaging and channel selection

## Output
Present your buyer personas in a markdown table with these columns:

| Persona Name | Demographics | Psychographics | Goals | Challenges | Preferred Channels |

Include 2-3 personas, each in its own row with detailed information in every column.
```
