# Social Media Caption Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Social Media Caption Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused social media captions for brands promoting products or services to specific audiences. This social media caption prompt for ChatGPT produces five distinct captions structured around dependency grammar principles, each with a hook, pain-point addressing content, unique selling propositions, relevant hashtags, and a clear call-to-action. It works across platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, TikTok) and adapts length, tone, and formatting to match both your specified brand voice and platform best practices. Each caption includes an explanation of how its grammatical structure creates persuasive flow. Real-world applications include product launches, service promotions, seasonal campaigns, and ongoing brand awareness efforts. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is designed for social media managers, digital marketers, content creators, and small business owners who need platform-specific captions that balance brand consistency with conversion goals. ● Addresses target audience pain points and desires while highlighting product benefits ● Structures captions using dependency grammar to create clear relationships between hooks, benefits, and calls-to-action ● Adapts tone, length, and formatting to match platform requirements and brand voice guidelines ● Includes relevant hashtags and conversion-focused CTAs in every caption ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in conversion-focused captions.

## Task
Create compelling social media captions that promote {{product-or-service}} and increase brand awareness for {{target-audience}}. Each caption should address audience pain points, highlight unique selling propositions, use attention-grabbing hooks, incorporate relevant hashtags, and include clear calls-to-action.

## Context
**Platform:** {{platform}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}

Structure each caption using dependency grammar principles—organize ideas around a main clause with supporting elements that create clear relationships between product benefits and audience needs. Tailor length, tone, and formatting to the platform's best practices.

## Output
Provide 5 numbered captions. After each caption, include a brief explanation showing how its dependency grammar structure connects the hook, benefit, and call-to-action into a coherent persuasive flow.
```
