# Edit and Proofread Content Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Edit and Proofread Content Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that analyzes and refines written material for clarity, coherence, and audience alignment using dependency grammar principles. This content editing prompt for ChatGPT walks through eight improvement layers: corrected grammar, punctuation, and spelling; reorganized sentence structure based on word relationships; enhanced vocabulary to replace weak or repetitive terms; consistency checks for tone and style; audience alignment to match comprehension level and interest; logical coherence verification; structural strengthening of introductions, bodies, and conclusions; and final polish for readability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, returning the original text, a refined version, a summary of changes with rationale, and recommendations for future writing improvement. Use it when you need systematic editing that goes beyond surface fixes to address flow, argumentation, and reader engagement. ● Corrects grammar, punctuation, and spelling while preserving the author's voice and intent ● Applies dependency grammar analysis to strengthen sentence structure and word relationships for better readability ● Adjusts vocabulary, tone, and complexity to match the specified target audience and editing goals ● Provides a transparent summary of changes and practical recommendations to help writers improve over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editor and proofreader refining written content for clarity, coherence, and effectiveness.

## Task
Analyze and improve the submitted content using dependency grammar principles to strengthen sentence structure and word relationships. Deliver:

1. **Corrected mechanics** – grammar, punctuation, spelling
2. **Improved structure** – reorganize sentences for clarity and flow based on dependency relationships
3. **Enhanced vocabulary** – replace weak or repetitive words with precise, impactful alternatives
4. **Consistency checks** – tone, style, voice
5. **Audience alignment** – ensure comprehension level and interest match the target audience
6. **Logical coherence** – verify argument or narrative consistency
7. **Structural strengthening** – refine introduction, body, and conclusion
8. **Final polish** – maximize readability and impact
```
