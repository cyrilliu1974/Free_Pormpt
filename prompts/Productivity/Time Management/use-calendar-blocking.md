# Calendar Blocking Strategy for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Calendar Blocking Strategy for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a complete time-blocking schedule tailored to solo business owners juggling multiple responsibilities. This calendar blocking prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table mapping your full workday into focused time blocks, each justified by energy levels, task dependencies, and strategic priorities. You input your business focus, working hours, top priorities, and biggest time management challenge; the prompt returns 8+ time blocks with durations and rationale, plus implementation guidance on maintaining discipline and adapting when disruptions occur. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping solopreneurs balance strategic work with operational demands when delegation isn't an option. ● Delivers a structured workday table with time blocks, activities, durations, and placement rationale based on your energy patterns and priorities ● Addresses the solopreneur reality of wearing multiple hats with limited delegation, balancing strategic and operational work ● Includes practical guidance on staying consistent with your blocks and flexibly adjusting when unexpected demands arise ● Optimizes focus by minimizing context-switching and protecting high-priority work from distractions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in time management for solopreneurs who juggle multiple roles and responsibilities.

## Task
Create a comprehensive calendar blocking strategy that maximizes productivity and efficiency. Develop a structured approach that optimizes time allocation, minimizes distractions, and enhances focus on high-priority tasks.

## Context
**Business focus:** {{business-focus}}
**Working hours:** {{working-hours}}
**Top priorities:** {{top-priorities}}
**Time management challenge:** {{time-management-challenge}}

Consider the unique constraints of solopreneurship: wearing multiple hats, limited delegation options, and the need to balance strategic work with operational tasks. Address how to maintain discipline with the blocking system and adjust the schedule when unexpected demands arise.

## Output
Present your calendar blocking strategy in a markdown table with three columns: TIME BLOCK, ACTIVITY, and DURATION. Provide at least 8 time blocks covering a full workday.

For each time block, include:
- A brief explanation justifying its placement in the day (based on energy levels, external dependencies, or strategic importance)
- Why that duration is appropriate
- How it addresses the stated priorities and time management challenge

After the table, provide actionable guidance on:
- Implementing the strategy effectively
- Maintaining consistency and discipline
- Adapting blocks when priorities shift or disruptions occur
```
