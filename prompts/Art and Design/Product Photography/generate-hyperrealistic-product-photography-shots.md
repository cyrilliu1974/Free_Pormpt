# Hyperrealistic Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates professional-grade product visuals with adaptive styling, dynamic motion effects, and premium commercial composition for brands and marketers. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, DALL·E, and other AI image generators produces studio-quality scenes where your product becomes the hero object in a refined setup with complementary surfaces (stone, glass, ceramic, metal, liquid) and category-specific elements like botanical accents, condensation, vapor, or organic interactions arranged asymmetrically. It preserves original product details and branding while building sophisticated commercial environments with natural dynamic effects - mist, splash, steam - frozen mid-motion for authentic advertising realism. The prompt controls lighting (soft directional key with natural shadow gradients), camera perspective (customizable angle and 85mm macro look), and output resolution while avoiding flat layouts, artificial CGI appearance, and unrealistic physics. Reach for this prompt when you need e-commerce hero images, social media ad creatives, or digital campaign visuals that rival professional photography studios without the shoot cost or complexity. ● Positions products as hero objects on texture-matched surfaces with category-intelligent props and freshness cues ● Captures dynamic effects like splash, vapor, and condensation frozen mid-motion with lifelike surface behavior ● Maintains exact product shape, label, and branding while building premium commercial environments around them ● Controls camera angle, macro 85mm commercial framing, f/8 aperture, and output resolution for consistent brand assets ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic premium product photography. The product is the hero object in a refined commercial setup, positioned slightly off-center on a surface whose texture and tone complement the product's material (stone, glass, ceramic, metal, or liquid). Surrounding elements are custom-selected based on the product category: freshness cues, botanical accents, mineral forms, condensation, ice, vapor, or organic liquid interaction, arranged asymmetrically. Subtle dynamic effects like mist, splash, or steam interact naturally with the product, frozen mid-motion. The product label, shape, and details remain exactly as shown. High-end advertising aesthetic, extreme realism, lifelike surface behavior, physically accurate reflections and shadows, professional studio quality.

Lighting: soft directional key light with adaptive fill and natural shadow gradients. Camera: {{camera-angle}}, 85mm macro commercial look, f/8 aperture, critically sharp focus on product with subtle depth falloff on secondary elements. Clean negative space, premium advertising layout. {{output-resolution}}.

Avoid: generic layout, flat lay symmetry, unrelated props, added text or typography, logos generated by the model, artificial CGI look, plastic materials, unrealistic liquid physics, harsh reflections, overexposure, low detail, blur, noise, illustration, cartoon style, watermark.
```
