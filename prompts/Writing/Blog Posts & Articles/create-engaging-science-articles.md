# Science Article Writer for General Audiences

## 簡介

The Science Article Writer for General Audiences is a free AI prompt that transforms complex scientific concepts into captivating, readable articles for non-specialist readers. This science writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to produce fully structured articles complete with attention-grabbing headlines, hook introductions, background context, 3-4 key points explained through concrete examples and analogies, real-world applications, fun facts, summaries, and source citations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles throughout to ensure sentences are clear and relationships between ideas are immediately understandable. Science communicators, educators, bloggers, and content creators use it to make specialized knowledge accessible without sacrificing accuracy. Reach for this prompt when you need to explain technical topics to general audiences or create educational content that balances scientific rigor with readability. ● Produces complete article structure from headline through sources, including background, key points, applications, and fun facts ● Applies dependency grammar framework to maximize clarity and logical flow between concepts ● Balances scientific accuracy with readability through concrete examples and relatable analogies ● Includes visual descriptions and real-world applications that connect abstract ideas to everyday experience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert science writer and educator creating accessible, engaging articles for a general audience.

## Task
Write a captivating science article on {{science-topic}} that breaks down complex concepts into clear, relatable content. Use dependency grammar principles throughout to enhance clarity: structure sentences so that relationships between ideas are immediately clear, with main concepts anchored and supporting details logically connected.

## Context
The article should make specialized knowledge accessible to non-expert readers through concrete examples, analogies, and visual descriptions. Balance scientific accuracy with readability.

## Output
Structure your article with these components:

**Title:** An attention-grabbing headline that captures the essence of the topic

**Intro:** Hook readers with an intriguing opening that states the topic and why it matters

**Background:** Provide context and foundational information needed for understanding

**Key Points:**
- Present 3-4 main ideas, each explained using clear dependency grammar structure
- Clarify concepts with concrete examples and relatable analogies
- Describe relevant visuals or diagrams that would aid comprehension

**Real-world Applications:** Discuss how the topic applies to everyday life or its potential future impact

**Fun Facts:** Include 2-3 interesting tidbits, surprising statistics, or engaging facts that spark curiosity

**Conclusion:** Summarize key takeaways and encourage further exploration

**Sources:** Cite 3-5 reputable sources supporting the information presented
```
