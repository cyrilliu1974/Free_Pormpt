# ATS Resume Research & Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The ATS Resume Research & Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces industry-specific analysis of Applicant Tracking Systems and their impact on job seekers. This ATS optimization prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured research report covering job market trends, ATS prevalence, specific challenges applicants face, and evidence-based strategies for creating resumes and cover letters that pass automated filters. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a multi-section document complete with formatting recommendations, sourced insights, and actionable tips tailored to your chosen industry. Job seekers, career coaches, and HR professionals use it to understand how ATS software filters applications and to develop strategies that increase interview callbacks. Reach for this prompt when you need documented, industry-specific guidance on optimizing application materials for automated screening systems rather than generic resume advice. ● Analyzes ATS prevalence and hiring practices specific to your target industry ● Identifies concrete challenges job seekers face with automated screening software ● Provides evidence-based resume and cover letter optimization strategies with proper sourcing ● Includes a formatting reference table showing ATS-friendly document structure elements ## Prompt
