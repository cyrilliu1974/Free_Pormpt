# Leadership Emotional Intelligence Development Framework

## 簡介

The Leadership Emotional Intelligence Development Framework is a free AI prompt that creates tailored EQ development plans for leaders across different organizational roles. The prompt acts as an expert emotional intelligence coach, analyzing the unique challenges, team dynamics, and stakeholder relationships of a specific leadership position to generate practical strategies across self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, and social skills. This leadership emotional intelligence prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured framework with three concrete strategies per EQ competency, role-specific recommendations that address the particular challenges of the position, and a phased implementation plan spanning three months with ongoing practices. Use cases include onboarding newly promoted managers, supporting executives through organizational change, developing first-time team leads, and creating personalized coaching roadmaps. The framework moves beyond generic advice by tailoring every strategy to the realities of the specified role. This prompt is ideal for leadership coaches, HR development professionals, organizational psychologists, and self-directed leaders who need a structured approach to building emotional intelligence in a specific leadership context. ● Covers all five core emotional intelligence competencies with three actionable strategies each, customized to the leadership role ● Includes role-specific strategies that address unique challenges, opportunities, and stakeholder relationships inherent to the position ● Provides a week-by-week and month-by-month implementation timeline that makes EQ development concrete and trackable ● Defines expected outcomes that connect personal development to team performance and organizational impact ## Prompt
