# Historical Portrait Face Transformation Prompt

## 簡介

The Historical Portrait Face Transformation Prompt is a free AI prompt that converts modern faces into hyper-realistic Victorian-era portraits while preserving exact facial features, proportions, and expressions. This historical portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 64K-quality images featuring elaborate period costumes - ornate gowns with floral embroidery, puffed sleeves, high ruffled collars, decorative Victorian hats adorned with roses and lace, pearl jewelry, and hand-held bouquets. The prompt uses customizable variables for pose-and-composition and color-palette, rendering each portrait with soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field, and a dreamy painterly atmosphere that evokes historical romance. Use cases include social media content, creative portfolio pieces, character design references, and personal keepsake imagery that blends modern identity with timeless elegance. Reach for this prompt when you need museum-quality period portraits without photo shoots, costume rentals, or manual photo editing. ● Preserves exact facial features, proportions, and expressions from reference images while applying Victorian-era styling. ● Renders intricate period costume details including embroidered bodices, layered skirts, ornate hats with roses, and pearl accessories. ● Produces cinematic 64K-quality output with soft romantic lighting, shallow depth of field, and painterly atmospheric backgrounds. ● Includes customizable variables for pose-and-composition and color-palette to match different creative visions. ## Prompt
