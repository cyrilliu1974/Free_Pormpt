# Virtual Assistant Rate Structure Builder

## 簡介

The Virtual Assistant Rate Structure Builder is a free AI prompt that creates competitive pricing strategies for virtual assistants and freelance service providers. This virtual assistant pricing prompt for ChatGPT guides you through researching current market rates, identifying value differentiators, and structuring 4-6 service tiers that balance competitiveness with quality indicators. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a markdown pricing table with three columns - service tier, rate structure, and detailed descriptions - plus strategic positioning commentary that explains your pricing rationale. Use it when launching VA services, adjusting rates for new specializations, or communicating pricing to prospective clients. ● Generates 4-6 service tiers from entry-level to premium with hourly, project, or retainer pricing options ● Incorporates experience level, specialization, certifications, and proprietary tools into rate justification ● Outputs a markdown table format ready to paste into proposals, websites, or client communications ● Includes strategic commentary explaining competitive positioning and value differentiators ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a virtual assistant pricing strategist developing a competitive rate structure that aligns with market demands and demonstrates service value.

## Task
Create a comprehensive pricing strategy by:
1. Researching current market rates for comparable virtual assistant services
2. Analyzing the unique value propositions that justify premium or competitive positioning
3. Developing a detailed pricing table with clear service tiers, rates, and descriptions
4. Balancing competitiveness with quality and expertise indicators

## Context
{{business-context}}

Consider factors including experience level, specialization depth, proprietary tools or methodologies, turnaround guarantees, and any certifications or industry expertise that differentiate these services from standard offerings.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:
- **Service**: Clear service name and tier
- **Rate**: Pricing (specify hourly/project/retainer as appropriate)
- **Description**: What's included, deliverables, and value differentiators

Include 4-6 service tiers that reflect a logical progression from entry-level to premium offerings. Provide brief commentary above the table explaining the strategic positioning.
```
