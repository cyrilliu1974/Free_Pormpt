# Negative Keyword List Generator for Paid Search

## 簡介

The Negative Keyword List Generator for Paid Search is a free AI prompt that compiles targeted exclusion lists to prevent wasted ad spend and sharpen campaign targeting for digital marketers. This negative keyword prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business, product, and target audience to identify 15-25 search terms that should be excluded from your paid search campaigns. It categorizes each keyword by waste-risk impact level (high, medium, or low) and provides clear rationale tied to audience fit, conversion likelihood, and budget efficiency. The prompt works across text-based AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured markdown table that prioritizes exclusions by their effect on ad performance. Real-world use cases include filtering out competitor brand names, eliminating low-intent informational queries, blocking irrelevant product variations, and excluding terms that attract the wrong demographic or price-sensitive searchers who won't convert. This prompt is essential for PPC managers, digital marketing strategists, and businesses running paid campaigns on Google Ads, Bing Ads, or other search platforms who need to maximize return on ad spend. ● Identifies irrelevant search variations, misspellings, and non-converting product categories that drain budget ● Flags competitor brands, trademark terms, and low-intent informational queries unlikely to convert ● Categorizes each exclusion by waste-risk impact level with specific reasoning tied to your target audience ● Outputs a structured markdown table of 15-25 keywords ranked from highest to lowest effect on ad performance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in paid search optimization and audience targeting.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive list of negative keywords for {{advertising-platform}} that will prevent wasted ad spend and improve campaign targeting for {{business-product}}.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Analyze the business, product, and audience to identify search terms that should be excluded. Focus on:
- Irrelevant variations and common misspellings
- Related but non-converting product categories
- Competitor brand names and trademark terms
- Low-intent or informational queries that won't convert
- Terms that attract the wrong demographic or price sensitivity

Categorize keywords by their potential impact (high/medium/low waste risk) and provide clear rationale for each exclusion that ties back to audience fit, conversion likelihood, or budget efficiency.

## Output
Present your recommendations in a markdown table with three columns:

| Keyword | Impact Level | Reason for Exclusion |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|

Include 15-25 negative keywords organized from highest to lowest impact on ad performance.
```
