# Personal Logo Design Brief Generator

## 簡介

The Personal Logo Design Brief Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive logo design specifications for solopreneurs building their personal brand identity. This personal logo design prompt for ChatGPT analyzes business context and produces a structured brief covering color palettes with hex codes and psychological rationale, typography recommendations, symbolic visual elements, layout compositions, and brand personality direction. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver design specifications that account for scalability from favicon to billboard, versatility across digital and print platforms, and strategic alignment with target audience psychology. Graphic designers and solopreneurs use it to establish clear design parameters before beginning visual work, ensuring every element - from shape language to whitespace - serves the brand's unique positioning and core values. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate business goals into actionable design direction, whether you're briefing a designer or guiding your own logo creation process. ● Delivers color schemes with hex codes and psychology-based rationale tied to brand values ● Specifies typography, symbolism, and composition with scalability from small icons to large format ● Explains how each design choice differentiates the brand and resonates with the target audience ● Provides horizontal, vertical, and icon-only layout variations for platform flexibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer specializing in personal brand identity and logo design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive logo design brief for a solopreneur that establishes a strong, memorable brand identity. Analyze the business context and develop detailed specifications covering all essential design elements.

## Context
The logo must:
- Reflect the industry and resonate with the target audience
- Communicate brand values and unique positioning clearly
- Apply color psychology principles strategically
- Remain versatile and scalable across digital and print platforms
- Work in monochrome, color, and at sizes from favicon to billboard

**Business Context:**
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide a structured design brief with the following sections:

**Color Scheme**
- Primary and secondary palette with hex codes
- Rationale tied to brand values and audience psychology
- Color variations for different backgrounds

**Typography**
- Font family recommendations (or custom lettering direction)
- Weight, style, and spacing considerations
- Legibility across scales

**Symbolism & Visual Elements**
- Core imagery or iconography
- Geometric or organic shapes that represent the brand
- Connection to industry and unique selling proposition

**Layout & Composition**
- Arrangement of text and visual elements
- Balance, hierarchy, and whitespace usage
- Horizontal, vertical, and icon-only lockups

**Brand Personality**
- Tone conveyed (professional, playful, luxury, approachable, etc.)
- How the design differentiates from competitors

Include brief rationale for each design choice, explaining how it serves the solopreneur's brand strategy.
```
