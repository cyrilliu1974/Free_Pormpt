# Ergonomic Workspace Design Prompt for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Ergonomic Workspace Design Prompt for Any Profession is a free AI prompt that creates customized workplace optimization plans based on your specific job demands, existing health concerns, and space limitations. This ergonomic workspace prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the physical demands and occupational health risks unique to your profession, then delivers actionable recommendations organized into workstation setup (desk height, monitor placement, lighting), equipment selection (chairs, peripherals, adjustable furniture), and daily ergonomic practices (posture habits, break schedules, stretching routines). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, grounding every suggestion in current ergonomic research while respecting real-world budget and space constraints. Use it when setting up a new workspace, addressing chronic pain or fatigue, or preventing long-term injury in desk-based or physically demanding roles. ● Tailors workstation layout, lighting, and equipment to the physical demands of your specific profession and work routine ● Explains the ergonomic principle behind each recommendation so you understand why it matters for injury prevention ● Accounts for existing health concerns, budget limits, and workspace constraints to ensure practical implementation ● Provides break schedules, stretching routines, and posture habits that fit seamlessly into your daily workflow ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert ergonomic designer specializing in workplace optimization and injury prevention.

## Task
Design a comprehensive ergonomic workspace plan that enhances productivity and prevents burnout. Analyze the specific physical demands, common occupational health risks, and workflow patterns of the profession. Base all recommendations on current ergonomic research and ensure they are practical for real-world implementation.

## Context
{{profession-and-work-routine}}

{{workspace-constraints}}

{{health-and-budget}}

## Output
Provide your recommendations in a bullet-point list organized under these headings:

- **Workstation Setup** – desk height, monitor placement, lighting, spatial arrangement
- **Equipment Recommendations** – chairs, peripherals, support tools, adjustable furniture
- **Ergonomic Practices** – posture habits, break schedules, stretching routines, movement patterns

For each recommendation, briefly explain the ergonomic principle it addresses and why it matters for this profession.
```
