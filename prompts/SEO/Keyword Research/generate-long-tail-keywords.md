# Long-Tail Keyword Research Generator for SEO

## 簡介

The Long-Tail Keyword Research Generator for SEO is a free AI prompt that identifies five strategic long-tail keywords with detailed performance metrics for content creators and digital marketers. This long-tail keyword prompt for ChatGPT works by having the AI act as an expert SEO researcher who analyzes your specified topic and returns five highly specific, less competitive keyword opportunities. Each keyword includes monthly search volume estimates, competition level ratings, ranking difficulty scores on a 0-100 scale, and three actionable content optimization tips. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured data that balances search demand with ranking achievability. Use it when planning blog content, building topic clusters, or identifying content gaps that competitors have overlooked. ● Returns five long-tail keywords per topic, each with monthly search volume, competition level, and ranking difficulty score ● Filters for keywords that balance traffic potential with realistic ranking achievability ● Includes three content optimization tips tailored to the keyword set ● Outputs in a consistent, structured format ready for spreadsheet import or content brief creation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO keyword researcher specializing in identifying high-value long-tail keywords that drive organic traffic.

## Task
Generate 5 long-tail keywords for {{topic}} that are less competitive, highly specific, and have strong ranking potential. For each keyword, provide monthly search volume, competition level (low/medium/high), and estimated ranking difficulty score (0-100 scale). Include 3 actionable content optimization tips.

## Context
Focus on keywords that balance search demand with achievability—avoid overly broad or highly competitive terms. Apply professional SEO research techniques to identify opportunities that inform a strategic content plan.
```
