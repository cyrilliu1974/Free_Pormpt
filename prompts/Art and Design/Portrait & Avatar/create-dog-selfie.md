# Man and Dog Selfie Generator

## 簡介

The Man and Dog Selfie Generator is a free AI prompt that creates candid, lifestyle photography showing the bond between a person and their pet. The prompt produces an ultra-realistic selfie featuring a rugged man with light brown hair and blue eyes holding a calm apricot pug on a modern patio, captured in natural daylight with cinematic depth and smartphone perspective. This dog selfie prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers authentic social media content with detailed skin texture, realistic fur, soft shadows, and an intimate emotional tone. Reach for it when you need compelling pet-owner photography for social posts, portfolio pieces, or lifestyle content that captures genuine companionship. ● Produces ultra-realistic selfie composition with handheld smartphone perspective and shallow depth of field. ● Captures natural lighting, authentic textures (skin, fabric, fur), and architectural modern setting details. ● Conveys quiet emotional bond through relaxed expressions, protective hand positioning, and intimate framing. ● Delivers social media-ready aesthetic with cinematic color grading, warm highlights, and candid spontaneity. ## Prompt

```
A candid, natural selfie of a rugged, handsome man holding a large, calm pug dog close to the camera. The man has medium-length, slightly messy light brown hair, light stubble, and striking blue eyes, wearing a dark zip-up jacket with subtle sporty detailing. His expression is relaxed yet slightly serious, conveying a quiet bond with the dog.

The setting is a modern outdoor patio or terrace with wooden slat walls and geometric architectural lines. Natural daylight illuminates the scene softly from the side, creating realistic shadows and warm highlights. Background elements are slightly out of focus, maintaining attention on the man and the dog.

Photographic style is ultra-realistic, casual lifestyle photography with a slightly cinematic feel. Natural colors, authentic skin texture, visible fabric details, realistic fur texture, shallow depth of field, handheld smartphone selfie perspective, high realism, intimate and emotional tone, spontaneous moment, authentic social media aesthetic.
```
