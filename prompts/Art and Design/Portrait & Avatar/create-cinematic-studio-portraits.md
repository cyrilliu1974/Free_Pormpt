# Cinematic Studio Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Studio Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces hyper-realistic fashion editorial portraits with professional lighting and elegant indoor compositions for photographers, brands, and content creators. This studio portrait prompt for ChatGPT (via DALL·E), Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates half-body shots of subjects seated in armchairs, leaning forward with chin resting on hand, set against sophisticated beige walls with classic framed art. The prompt applies warm golden-hour cinematic lighting from the left, casting natural highlights and soft shadows across facial features, and renders fine film grain with shallow depth of field and strong background bokeh. Fashion brands use it to generate editorial-style imagery for lookbooks and campaigns; social media managers create high-end lifestyle content without booking studio time; portrait photographers explore lighting and composition concepts before live shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality portrait photography with a luxurious, warm aesthetic and professional production values. ● Configurable subject description and chair style, with automatic application of warm golden-hour lighting, soft shadows, and natural skin texture. ● Half-body composition with the subject leaning forward, chin on hand, framed against beige walls and classic art in golden frames. ● Professional shallow depth-of-field rendering with strong bokeh, fine film grain, and 8k photorealistic detail. ● Fashion editorial aesthetic ideal for lifestyle marketing, brand campaigns, social media content, and lookbook photography. ## Prompt

Hyper-realistic studio portrait of {{subject-description}}, seated in a {{chair-style}} armchair, leaning slightly forward, resting chin on right hand. ```
