# Marketing Strategy Builder Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Marketing Strategy Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, multi-channel marketing plans tailored to your business context. This marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through seven core components: market position and audience analysis, content marketing strategy with publishing cadence, platform-specific social media plans, segmented email campaigns with automation triggers, paid advertising budget allocation, an integrated execution roadmap, and a complete KPIs and measurement framework. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured bullet-point plans with timelines, resource requirements, and specific tactics for each channel. Marketers use it to turn high-level business goals into concrete action plans that coordinate content, social, email, and paid efforts into a cohesive strategy. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured marketing plan that goes beyond theory and delivers channel-specific tactics, recommended schedules, and clear success metrics. ● Analyzes competitive landscape, target demographics, psychographics, and current brand perception before building strategy ● Designs content marketing plans with specific content pillars, formats, distribution channels, and publishing schedules ● Creates platform-specific social media plans with posting calendars, engagement tactics, and content themes ● Builds segmented email marketing campaigns with automation triggers, sequence design, and performance benchmarks ● Allocates paid advertising budgets across channels with targeting parameters and creative recommendations ● Integrates all tactics into a unified roadmap showing how channels reinforce each other, with resource requirements and timing ● Establishes channel-specific KPIs, reporting cadence, and success thresholds for measurable results ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist developing a comprehensive plan to increase brand awareness and drive sales.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable marketing strategy structured as follows:

1. **Market Position & Audience Analysis** – assess competitive landscape, target demographics, psychographics, and current brand perception
2. **Content Marketing Strategy** – content pillars, formats, creation guidelines, distribution channels, and publishing cadence
3. **Social Media Plan** – platform selection, posting schedule, engagement tactics, content themes
4. **Email Marketing Campaign** – segmentation approach, sequence design, automation triggers, performance benchmarks
5. **Paid Advertising** – channel mix, budget allocation across platforms, targeting parameters, creative recommendations
6. **Integrated Execution Roadmap** – how tactics reinforce each other, timing, resource requirements
7. **KPIs & Measurement Framework** – specific metrics for each channel, reporting cadence, success thresholds

Ensure each section builds logically on the previous one, with specific tactics, recommended timelines, and resource allocations throughout.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a structured bullet-point plan with clear headings for each major section and sub-bullets detailing specific tactics, schedules, and budget recommendations.
```
