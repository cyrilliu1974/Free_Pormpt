# Modern Character Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Modern Character Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates stylized side-angle character portraits with contemporary fashion details and dramatic lighting for designers, marketers, and content creators. This character portrait prompt for Midjourney produces close-up images featuring sharp facial features, modern hairstyles, and trendy outfit elements like graphic tees and silver jewelry against clean backgrounds. The prompt structures every detail - from expression and skin tone to clothing layers and accessories - so you get consistent, fashion-forward portraits in a 3:4 aspect ratio. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you swap in different subject descriptions, headwear options, and aesthetic styles through three simple variables. Reach for this prompt when you need eye-catching character visuals for social media profiles, branding mockups, or editorial design without organizing a photo shoot. ● Produces side-angle portraits with detailed facial features, wavy hair, sharp eyebrows, and natural skin tones in a calm yet intense mood. ● Includes layered outfit details like open jackets, graphic T-shirts, silver chain necklaces, and earrings for a street-style aesthetic. ● Lets you customize subject description, headwear choice, and overall aesthetic through three variables while maintaining a cohesive modern look. ● Outputs 3:4 aspect ratio images with front lighting that highlights expression and fashion elements against bright, clean backgrounds. ## Prompt

```
Close-up side-angle portrait of a {{subject-description}} with a calm but intense expression. Short, wavy light gray hair in a modern, slightly messy style partially covering the forehead. Fair skin with sharp features, straight neat dark eyebrows, and naturally pink lips with a slight sheen. Wearing {{headwear}}, a black zip-up jacket left open over a black T-shirt with green and red graphic art on the chest. Thin silver chain necklace and small silver earring. Front lighting highlighting facial features and outfit details. Bright blue sky background. {{aesthetic}} style. Striking, stylish, modern and edgy mood with focus on expression and trendy fashion details. 3:4 aspect ratio.
```
