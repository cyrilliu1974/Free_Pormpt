# Peer Review System Design Prompt for Education

## 簡介

The Peer Review System Design Prompt for Education is a free AI prompt that creates structured peer review frameworks for educators building collaborative learning environments in their classrooms. This peer review system prompt for ChatGPT generates a ready-to-implement framework covering student training guidelines, grading rubrics, interaction workflows, and mitigation strategies for common challenges like participation gaps and feedback quality. Educators provide their educational context (class size, subject, student demographics) and receive a detailed implementation guide with clear expectations for constructive feedback, assessment criteria, platform recommendations, timelines, and methods to address grade anxiety and bias. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce structured output with numbered steps and bullet points ready for classroom use. Teachers use it when launching peer assessment programs, redesigning existing review systems, or adapting collaborative learning methods to diverse student abilities. ● Generates student training guidelines with examples of effective and ineffective peer feedback to build evaluative skills. ● Creates grading rubrics that assess both the work being reviewed and the quality of feedback students provide. ● Delivers step-by-step workflows with platform recommendations, timelines, and methods for anonymous or attributed reviews. ● Includes mitigation strategies for unequal participation, disagreements, bias reduction, and supporting struggling learners. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in collaborative learning systems and peer assessment design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive peer review framework that enables students to give and receive constructive feedback on each other's work. The system must be practical, scalable, and promote meaningful learning.

## Context
{{educational-context}}

The framework should:
- Accommodate diverse learning styles and abilities
- Build critical thinking and evaluative skills
- Anticipate and address common implementation challenges (participation gaps, grade anxiety, feedback quality)
- Fit the realities of the class size and subject matter

## Output
Deliver a structured implementation guide with:

### 1. Peer Review Guidelines
- Clear expectations for constructive, respectful feedback
- Specific criteria students should evaluate
- Examples of effective vs. ineffective comments
- Training steps to prepare students

### 2. Grading Rubric
- Assessment criteria for the work being reviewed
- Optional: criteria for evaluating the quality of peer feedback itself
- Point distributions and performance levels

### 3. Interaction Platform & Process
- Recommended platform or tool (digital or analog)
- Step-by-step workflow from assignment to final review
- Timeline and milestones
- Methods for anonymous or attributed feedback

### 4. Challenge Mitigation
- Strategies for unequal participation
- Handling disagreements or hurt feelings
- Ensuring fairness and reducing bias
- Supporting struggling students

Format with clear headings, bullet points, and numbered steps for immediate implementation.
```
