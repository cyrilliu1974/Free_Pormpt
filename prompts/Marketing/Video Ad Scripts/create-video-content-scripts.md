# Affiliate Video Script Generator for Marketing Content

## 簡介

The Affiliate Video Script Generator for Marketing Content is a free AI prompt that writes conversion-optimized video scripts for affiliate marketers promoting products or services to specific audiences. This affiliate video script prompt for ChatGPT takes your topic, target audience, affiliate offer, and desired benefits, then produces a complete 5-minute script structured with timestamps, persuasive hooks, objection handling, and strategic calls-to-action. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows naturally with strong verbs and clear logical connections. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it adaptable across text-generation models. Real use cases include YouTube affiliate reviews, course promotion videos, software walkthrough scripts, and product comparison content where the goal is to inform viewers while driving clicks and conversions. Reach for this prompt when you need a full video script that educates your audience, addresses their concerns, and naturally integrates affiliate links without sounding like a hard sell. ● Builds complete scripts with introduction hooks, 3-5 benefit sections, objection handling, and timed conclusions ● Structures sentences using dependency grammar for natural spoken flow and logical persuasion ● Includes mid-roll and final calls-to-action that feel conversational rather than forced ● Addresses audience pain points, incorporates social proof, and creates urgency where appropriate to the offer ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video script writer specializing in affiliate marketing content that converts viewers into customers.

## Task
Write a complete video script that promotes the affiliate product or service while delivering genuine value to the target audience. Structure each sentence using dependency grammar principles—build clauses around strong main verbs, with modifiers and subordinate elements clearly attached to create natural, persuasive flow.

## Context
**Topic & Audience:**
{{topic-and-audience}}

**Affiliate Offer:**
{{affiliate-offer}}

**Key Benefits & Desired Action:**
{{benefits-and-cta}}

## Script Requirements

**Introduction (0:00-0:30)**
- Open with a hook that immediately addresses a pain point or desire relevant to the topic
- Establish credibility and the video's value proposition
- Preview what viewers will learn

**Main Content (0:30-4:00)**
- Present 3-5 core benefits of the affiliate offer, each supported by specific evidence or examples
- Address the top 2-3 objections or concerns the target audience typically has
- Weave in social proof, use cases, or relatable scenarios
- Include a mid-roll call-to-action that feels natural, not forced

**Conclusion (4:00-5:00)**
- Summarize the transformation or outcome viewers can expect
- Deliver a strong, specific final call-to-action with clear next steps
- Create urgency or incentive where appropriate to the offer

## Output Format
Deliver the script with clear section headings (Introduction, Main Content, Conclusion) and timestamp markers. Write in spoken language—conversational, direct, and energetic. Each sentence should flow naturally into the next through dependency grammar structure, where supporting clauses clearly modify and extend main ideas rather than standing as isolated statements.
```
