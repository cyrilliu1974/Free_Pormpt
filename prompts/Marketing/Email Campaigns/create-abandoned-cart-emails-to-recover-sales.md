# Abandoned Cart Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Abandoned Cart Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multi-email recovery campaigns for e-commerce businesses looking to recapture lost checkout conversions. This abandoned cart email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete 3-5 message sequence with strategic timing recommendations, mobile-optimized subject lines, persuasive body copy, and clear calls-to-action tailored to your business context. Each email in the sequence layers psychological triggers like scarcity, social proof, and loss aversion while escalating incentives across touchpoints. The prompt structures personalization elements including product-specific references, customer names, and cart-value segmentation, then suggests A/B test variables for continuous optimization. E-commerce managers use it to design data-driven recovery flows that respect customer psychology while maximizing conversion rates. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete cart abandonment strategy that goes beyond a single reminder email and builds a relationship-focused recovery arc. ● Produces 3-5 sequenced emails with specific send-time recommendations based on abandonment event ● Writes mobile-first copy using cause-and-effect dependency grammar to connect customer needs with product benefits ● Includes both primary and A/B variant subject lines for each message with testing focus areas ● Provides strategic rationale explaining the escalation logic and incentive progression across the entire sequence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing specialist designing an abandoned cart recovery sequence.

## Task
Create a 3-5 email sequence to recover sales from customers who added items to their cart but did not complete checkout. For each email, provide:

- Subject line (attention-grabbing, mobile-optimized)
- Email body copy (persuasive, using clear cause-and-effect relationships between customer needs and product benefits)
- Primary CTA (specific action, clear placement)
- Timing recommendation (hours/days after cart abandonment)

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Strategy Requirements

**Psychological triggers to layer in:**
- Scarcity or urgency where authentic (limited stock, time-bound offers)
- Social proof (reviews, popularity indicators)
- Loss aversion (remind what they're leaving behind)
- Reciprocity (offer help, not just a sale)

**Personalization elements:**
- Reference specific cart items by name and image
- Use customer's first name
- Tailor messaging to cart value tier if relevant

**Technical considerations:**
- Write for mobile-first reading (short paragraphs, scannable)
- Escalate incentive or urgency across the sequence
- Suggest A/B test variables for subject lines and CTA copy

## Output Format

For each email in the sequence:

### Email [Number]: [Descriptive Name]
**Timing:** [When to send]  
**Subject Line:** [Primary version]  
**Subject Line (A/B variant):** [Alternative to test]  
**Body Copy:** [Full email text]  
**CTA:** [Button text and action]  
**A/B Test Focus:** [What element to test]

---

Include strategic rationale after the final email explaining the escalation logic across the sequence.
```
