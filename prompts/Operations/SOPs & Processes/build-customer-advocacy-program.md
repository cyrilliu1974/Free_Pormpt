# Customer Advocacy Program Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Advocacy Program Builder is a free AI prompt that designs complete advocacy ecosystems tailored to your business context. It maps the customer journey to identify natural delight touchpoints, then builds structured programs that activate those moments through tiered ambassador frameworks, authentic storytelling campaigns, and user-generated content strategies. This customer advocacy prompt for ChatGPT works equally well on Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a full implementation roadmap with recruitment criteria, progression paths, recognition systems, and a 90-day launch timeline. Reach for this prompt when you need to scale word-of-mouth marketing through systematic engagement rather than manufactured promotion, or when building a customer community that drives authentic brand loyalty. ● Ambassador program framework with tiered progression paths, onboarding sequences, and ongoing engagement activities tailored to your business model. ● Testimonial and storytelling campaign designs that collect authentic customer narratives through natural prompts and distribution tactics. ● User-generated content strategy with brand-aligned themes, creative participation incentives, and submission workflows. ● Recognition and reward systems that balance appreciation with non-transactional perception, including milestone celebrations and exclusive access benefits. ● 90-day implementation roadmap with sample communication templates, key milestones, and measurable KPIs for tracking advocacy impact. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer advocacy strategist specializing in transforming satisfied customers into brand ambassadors through authentic engagement, recognition systems, and community-driven content.

## Task
Design a comprehensive customer advocacy program tailored to the business context provided. The program should cultivate genuine advocacy through value exchange and emotional connection, not manufactured promotion.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Authentic advocacy grows from identifying natural delight touchpoints in the customer journey and activating those moments strategically. Design multi-tiered programs that scale from casual supporters to power advocates, with clear progression paths and participation frameworks customers genuinely want to join.

## Output
Deliver a complete advocacy ecosystem structured under these sections:

**Ambassador Program Framework**
- Recruitment criteria and ideal advocate profile
- Onboarding process and initial engagement sequence
- Tiered progression paths with advancement triggers
- Ongoing engagement activities and communication cadence

**Testimonial & Storytelling Campaigns**
- Authentic collection strategies and storytelling prompts
- Campaign frameworks that feel natural, not scripted
- Distribution and amplification tactics

**User-Generated Content Strategy**
- Content themes aligned with brand values
- Participation incentives and creative prompts
- Submission and curation workflows

**Recognition & Reward Systems**
- Tiered benefits structure (public acknowledgment, exclusive access, tangible rewards)
- Balance between appreciation and transactional perception
- Milestone celebrations and ongoing recognition touchpoints

**Implementation Roadmap**
- 90-day launch timeline with key milestones
- Sample communication templates for each program phase
- Success metrics and KPIs for measuring advocacy impact

Provide actionable strategies in bullet points with specific examples and implementation steps for immediate execution.
```
