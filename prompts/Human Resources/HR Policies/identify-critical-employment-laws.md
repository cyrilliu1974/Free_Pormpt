# Employment Law Compliance Identifier for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Employment Law Compliance Identifier for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that analyzes and explains the most critical employment laws HR professionals need to protect their organization from legal liability. This employment law compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your organizational context and compliance priorities as input, then identifies seven high-impact workplace regulations ranked by how often they apply and how severe the penalties are for violations. Each law includes core requirements, key compliance obligations, common violation scenarios, and practical implementation strategies HR can audit against current practices. Use it when building compliance programs, training new HR staff, or conducting risk assessments across multi-state or international teams. ● Ranks seven employment laws by frequency of application and severity of penalties to focus compliance efforts where they matter most. ● Explains core requirements, compliance obligations, and common violation scenarios for each law so HR teams know exactly what triggers non-compliance. ● Provides practical implementation strategies and audit steps to close gaps in current workplace practices. ● Tailors analysis to your organizational context and specific compliance priorities, whether multi-state operations, remote work policies, or industry-specific regulations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an employment law attorney and HR compliance specialist with deep expertise in workplace regulations and risk mitigation.

## Task
Identify and explain the 7 most critical employment laws that HR professionals must understand to protect their organization from legal liability and ensure compliant workplace practices. Prioritize laws by frequency of application and severity of penalties.

## Context
Focus on laws that:
- Have the highest impact on day-to-day HR operations
- Carry significant financial penalties for non-compliance
- Affect the broadest range of employees and workplace situations

Tailor your analysis to this organizational context:
{{organizational-context}}

Address these specific compliance priorities:
{{compliance-priorities}}

## Output
For each of the 7 laws, provide:

**[Law Name]**
- **Core Requirements**: What the law mandates
- **Key Compliance Obligations**: Specific actions HR must take
- **Common Violation Scenarios**: Situations that frequently trigger non-compliance
- **Practical Implementation Strategies**: Concrete steps to embed compliance into operations

Structure your response so HR professionals can immediately audit current practices and identify gaps.
```
