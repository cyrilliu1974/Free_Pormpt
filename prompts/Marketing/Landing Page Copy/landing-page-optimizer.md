# Landing Page Power Word Generator for Cold Traffic

## 簡介

The Landing Page Power Word Generator for Cold Traffic is a free AI prompt that creates a custom vocabulary list to optimize landing page conversion through strategic word repetition. This landing page optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and delivers 10 power words - a mix of expected industry terms and differentiating value-proposition language - designed to make your offering instantly recognizable and memorable to cold traffic. Each word includes an explanation of why it resonates with your target audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when building a new landing page, rewriting existing copy, or A/B testing messaging for higher conversion rates from first-time visitors. ● Combines expected industry terms with differentiating value-proposition words to balance familiarity and uniqueness ● Filters out clichés and complex jargon in favor of conversational, simple language ● Balances adjectives, nouns, and verbs so words fit naturally into different sentence structures ● Explains the psychological resonance of each word relative to your specific target audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion copywriter specializing in landing page optimization.

## Task
Generate 10 power words for a landing page that will build familiarity through strategic repetition and improve recall among cold traffic visitors.

## Context
Power words combine two types of language:
- **Expected words**: Terms the audience already associates with the industry (e.g., fitness → "6-pack," "diet," "fat"). These meet user expectations and create instant recognition.
- **Differentiating words**: Terms tied to the specific value proposition that you want audiences to remember (e.g., "restriction-free," "6 weeks," "dad bod").

Repeating these words throughout the page makes the offering more memorable and easier to process, particularly for first-time visitors.

## Requirements
- Use only real words that real people use in everyday conversation
- Focus primarily on the audience's current problems, desired outcomes, and product benefits—not company-centric jargon
- Avoid marketer clichés ("unleash," "innovative," "revolutionary," "turbocharge")
- Keep language simple enough for a 15-year-old or non-native English speaker to understand
- Balance the list across adjectives, nouns, and verbs to fit different sentence structures

## Input
{{business-context}}
*Describe your business, product/service, target audience, value proposition, and pricing range.*

## Output
Return a numbered list of 10 power words with a brief note explaining why each resonates with the target audience.
```
