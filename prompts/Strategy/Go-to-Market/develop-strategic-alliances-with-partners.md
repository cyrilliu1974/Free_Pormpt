# Strategic Partnership Plan Generator for B2B Growth

## 簡介

The Strategic Partnership Plan Generator for B2B Growth is a free AI prompt that produces structured partnership roadmaps for companies exploring collaborative market expansion. This strategic partnership prompt for ChatGPT analyzes potential partners through the lens of market synergy, resource complementarity, and long-term revenue potential, then outputs a three-column markdown table detailing partner names, alliance benefits, and specific action items. Designed for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, the prompt works by taking your company profile, a list of potential partners, and your strategic assets - then returning an actionable plan that balances mutual benefit with risk mitigation. Use it when evaluating joint ventures, channel partnerships, co-marketing opportunities, or technology integrations where clarity on next steps and measurable outcomes matters. ● Outputs a markdown table mapping each potential partner to measurable benefits and sequenced next steps ● Evaluates market synergy, shared resources, and growth trajectories for every alliance scenario ● Includes risk mitigation strategies embedded in the action-item sequence ● Adapts to any industry vertical, partner type, or stage of partnership maturity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic partnership manager developing alliances to expand market reach and drive revenue growth.

## Task
Create a comprehensive partnership plan that analyzes potential partners, evaluates alliance benefits, and outlines specific action items. Consider market synergy, resource complementarity, and long-term growth potential. Develop a strategy that maximizes mutual benefits while minimizing risks.

## Context
**Company & Market:**
{{company-and-industry}}

**Partnership Landscape:**
{{potential-partners}}

**Strategic Assets:**
{{products-and-target-market}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Partner Name | Alliance Benefits | Action Items |
|--------------|-------------------|-------------|

**Requirements:**
- Each row must provide detailed, actionable information
- Alliance Benefits should address market synergy, resource complementarity, and growth potential
- Action Items must be specific, measurable, and sequenced
- Include risk mitigation considerations for each partnership
```
