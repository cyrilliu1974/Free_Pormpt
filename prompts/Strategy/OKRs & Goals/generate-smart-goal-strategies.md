# SMART Goal Builder for Strategic Planning

## 簡介

The SMART Goal Builder for Strategic Planning is a free AI prompt that generates three detailed strategic goals with complete breakdowns across all five SMART dimensions for any organizational context. This SMART goal prompt for ChatGPT takes a situation or context you provide and produces three fully structured goals, each explicitly defining what makes it Specific (clear objective), Measurable (KPIs and metrics), Achievable (feasibility analysis), Relevant (strategic alignment), and Time-bound (concrete deadlines). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, acting as a strategic planning expert that translates general situations into actionable, accountable objectives. Use it when launching new initiatives, setting quarterly targets, responding to market changes, or communicating objectives across teams and stakeholders. ● Produces three distinct goals, each with all five SMART components explicitly defined and explained ● Includes KPIs, feasibility factors, strategic alignment reasoning, and specific deadlines for each goal ● Ensures goals address the core challenges or opportunities present in your situation ● Structures output for easy communication to executives, team members, and stakeholders ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic planning expert who defines SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) for organizational contexts.

## Task
Develop three strategic goals for the given situation. For each goal, break down all five SMART components to ensure clarity and actionability.

## Context

{{situation}}

## Output
Provide exactly three goals in this structure:

**Goal 1:**
- **Specific:** Clear, focused objective addressing a particular outcome
- **Measurable:** KPI or metric to track progress and determine success
- **Achievable:** Factors demonstrating feasibility within available constraints and resources
- **Relevant:** How this goal aligns with the overall strategy and broader objectives
- **Time-bound:** Concrete deadline creating urgency and accountability
```
