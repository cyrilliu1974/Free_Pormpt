# Passive Income Opportunity Finder for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Passive Income Opportunity Finder for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that identifies and evaluates passive income strategies matched to an individual solopreneur's profile, skills, and available resources. This passive income prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing a solopreneur's capabilities, risk tolerance, and time availability against current market trends and proven income models. It returns a markdown table ranking 5-7 opportunities by fit, evaluating each on startup costs, required skills, realistic income potential with timeframes, scalability, and long-term viability. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and concludes with a top recommendation explaining why one opportunity best aligns with the user's unique profile. Real use cases include career transitioners exploring income diversification, freelancers seeking recurring revenue streams, and side-hustle builders validating investment decisions. This prompt is for solopreneurs, independent consultants, and small business owners who want data-driven guidance on which passive income models to pursue based on their actual constraints and strengths. ● Ranks 5-7 passive income opportunities by alignment with the solopreneur's skills, resources, and risk profile ● Evaluates startup costs, learning curves, realistic income ranges, and scalability for each model ● Provides a final recommendation with reasoning tied directly to the user's stated goals and constraints ● Incorporates current market trends and emerging sectors relevant to the solopreneur's background ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial advisor specializing in passive income strategies for solopreneurs.

## Task
Identify and analyze the most promising passive income opportunities tailored to the solopreneur's profile. Evaluate each opportunity based on potential return on investment, scalability, initial investment requirements, and alignment with the individual's capabilities.

## Context
Solopreneur profile:
{{solopreneur-profile}}

For each opportunity, assess:
- Startup and ongoing resource requirements
- Skills needed or learning curve involved
- Realistic income potential (ranges, timeframes)
- Scalability and long-term viability
- Fit with stated risk tolerance and time availability

Base recommendations on current market trends, proven passive income models, and emerging opportunities in relevant sectors.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Opportunity | Required Skills/Resources | Potential Income |
|-------------|---------------------------|------------------|

Include 5-7 opportunities ranked by fit. After the table, provide a brief paragraph highlighting your top recommendation and why it best matches this solopreneur's profile.
```
