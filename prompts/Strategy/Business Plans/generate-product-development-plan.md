# Product Development Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Product Development Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a complete strategic roadmap guiding products from concept through market launch for entrepreneurs, product managers, and startup teams. This product development prompt for ChatGPT walks you through five distinct phases: ideation and research, planning and design, development and testing, pre-launch and marketing, and launch with post-launch iteration. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a detailed plan that includes market research steps, competitive analysis, feature prioritization, prototyping guidance, quality assurance checkpoints, go-to-market strategy, and performance monitoring frameworks. Real-world use cases include SaaS launches, physical product rollouts, service businesses entering new markets, and internal innovation projects that need structured execution plans. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform a product idea into an actionable timeline with clear milestones, resource requirements, and decision points at every stage. ● Produces a product overview with target market definition and unique value proposition ● Maps five development phases with specific activities, from market research and wireframing through launch execution and feedback loops ● Identifies critical risk factors, resource needs, and success metrics tailored to your product context ● Includes an executive summary synthesizing timeline, milestones, and expected outcomes for stakeholder alignment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic product development consultant creating a comprehensive roadmap from ideation through post-launch.

## Task
Develop a detailed product development plan structured across five phases, incorporating best practices and actionable steps at each stage. The plan should serve as a clear roadmap guiding the process to successful market entry.

## Context
Product and market details:
{{product-overview}}

## Output
Structure your plan using this format:

**Product Overview:**
• Product/service description
• Target market
• Unique value proposition

**Development Phases:**

**Phase 1: Ideation and Research**
• List key activities: market research, competitive analysis, concept validation, user needs assessment

**Phase 2: Planning and Design**
• List key activities: requirements definition, feature prioritization, wireframes/prototypes, technical architecture, timeline and budget

**Phase 3: Development and Testing**
• List key activities: iterative build cycles, quality assurance, user testing, refinement based on feedback

**Phase 4: Pre-launch and Marketing**
• List key activities: go-to-market strategy, marketing materials, sales enablement, beta program, launch logistics

**Phase 5: Launch and Post-launch**
• List key activities: launch execution, performance monitoring, customer feedback loops, iteration roadmap, scale planning

**Key Considerations:**
• Identify critical risk factors, resource requirements, decision points, and success metrics throughout the development process

**Executive Summary:**
• Provide a concise synthesis of the plan highlighting timeline, milestones, and expected outcomes
```
