# Institutional Knowledge Capture Strategies

## 簡介

The Institutional Knowledge Capture Strategies prompt is a free AI prompt that helps organizations preserve critical knowledge from departing employees through structured, practical capture plans. It produces five distinct strategies covering both documented procedures and irreplaceable tacit insights - judgment calls, relationship context, decision-making rationales, and organizational nuances - tailored to your specific timeline, resources, and employee role. This institutional knowledge prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok generates implementation steps, scheduling guidance, and success tips for methods ranging from structured interviews to shadowing and mentorship handoffs. Reach for it when an employee gives notice and you need a comprehensive transition plan that prevents knowledge loss. ● Spans multiple capture methods - exit interviews, documentation reviews, job shadowing, recorded walkthroughs, and mentorship sessions - to address diverse knowledge types. ● Provides sequenced implementation steps with timeline estimates and scheduling considerations for each strategy. ● Balances explicit knowledge capture (procedures, databases, system access) with tacit knowledge transfer (relationships, judgment, decision context). ● Adapts recommendations to your organization's context, the departing employee's role and responsibilities, and available timeline and resources. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert knowledge management consultant specializing in organizational memory preservation for enterprises.

## Task
Provide 5 comprehensive, actionable strategies for capturing institutional knowledge from a departing employee. Address both explicit knowledge (documented procedures, data) and tacit knowledge (judgment, relationship insights, decision-making rationales, organizational nuances).

## Context
Departing employees hold irreplaceable insights that don't appear in manuals or databases. The strategies must be practical, cost-effective, and adapted to:
- The urgency of departure timelines
- Different types of knowledge complexity
- Varying communication styles
- Real-world organizational constraints

**Organization context:** {{organization-context}}

**Departing employee details:** {{employee-role-responsibilities}}

**Knowledge capture constraints:** {{timeline-resources}}

## Output
Structure each of the 5 strategies as follows:

**Strategy [Number]: [Clear Strategy Name]**
- **Implementation steps:** Specific, sequenced actions to execute the strategy
- **Timeline:** Estimated time requirements and scheduling considerations
- **Success tips:** Practical advice for maximizing knowledge retention and overcoming common obstacles

Ensure strategies span different capture methods (interviews, documentation, shadowing, etc.) and knowledge types to create a comprehensive transition plan.
```
