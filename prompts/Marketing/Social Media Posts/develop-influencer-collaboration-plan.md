# Influencer Collaboration Plan Builder for Co-Branded Content

## 簡介

The Influencer Collaboration Plan Builder for Co-Branded Content is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable roadmaps for brands and marketers planning influencer partnerships across any industry. This influencer collaboration prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every phase of a co-branded content campaign, from initial outreach and contract negotiation to content creation, distribution, and performance measurement. You provide three variables (your industry, campaign goals, and target audience), and the prompt produces a sequential guide complete with key actions, realistic timelines in weeks, expected outcomes, and proven best practices. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams using different text models. Real use cases include beauty brands partnering with lifestyle creators, SaaS companies collaborating with tech reviewers, and fitness brands working with wellness influencers. This prompt is ideal for social media managers, marketing directors, and brand strategists who need a repeatable framework for influencer campaigns but want flexibility to adapt each plan to different verticals and objectives. ● Outputs a multi-step guide with clear actions, realistic week-by-week timelines, and measurable outcomes for each phase. ● Tailors recommendations to your specific industry, campaign goals, and target audience demographics. ● Includes a curated list of best-practice tips to maximize partnership value and avoid common pitfalls. ● Structured format makes it easy to share with team members, influencers, and stakeholders for alignment. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert influencer marketing strategist specializing in co-branded content collaborations.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for organizing a successful influencer collaboration focused on co-branded content creation for {{industry}}.

## Context
**Campaign goals:** {{campaign-goals}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

Tailor all recommendations to this specific industry and these objectives. Provide realistic timelines and actionable best practices that ensure a mutually beneficial partnership.

## Output
Structure your guide as follows:

**Step 1:** [Step Title]  
- [Key Action 1]  
- [Key Action 2]  
- [Key Action 3]  
**Timeline:** [X] weeks  
**Expected Outcome:** [Outcome]

**Step 2:** [Step Title]  
- [Key Action 1]  
- [Key Action 2]  
- [Key Action 3]  
**Timeline:** [X] weeks  
**Expected Outcome:** [Outcome]

[Continue for all necessary steps]

**Top Tips for Success:**  
1. [Tip 1]  
2. [Tip 2]  
3. [Tip 3]  
4. [Tip 4]
```
