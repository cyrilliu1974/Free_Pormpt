# Cinematic Portrait With Light Trails Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait With Light Trails Generator is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric, low-angle portrait images with dynamic light-trail effects and analog film texture for photographers, visual artists, and creative directors. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney produces dramatic compositions where your subject is illuminated by customizable lighting in a dark environment, captured from a low angle with a wide-angle lens. Long-exposure light trails blur around the figure, creating motion and energy while the subject's intense gaze anchors the frame. The output mimics analog film stock with rich color tones and fine grain texture, delivering a professional, moody aesthetic. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image-generation models. Use it when you need evocative character portraits, editorial visuals, or concept art that combines technical photography effects with cinematic mood. ● Produces low-angle, wide-lens portraits with long-exposure light trails that add motion and atmosphere around the subject. ● Renders an analog film look with rich color grading and fine grain texture for a timeless, cinematic feel. ● Supports two variables - subject and lighting - so you can quickly iterate on characters, moods, and light sources. ● Outputs in 2:3 aspect ratio, ideal for vertical editorial layouts, social media, and character-focused storytelling. ## Prompt

