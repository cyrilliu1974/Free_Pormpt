# Performance Management Goal-Setting Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Performance Management Goal-Setting Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that designs evidence-based frameworks for integrating goal-setting and tracking into performance review processes for HR consultants, people operations teams, and organizational development professionals. This performance management prompt for ChatGPT walks through eight structured components: foundational principles rooted in organizational psychology, a five-step goal-setting process, practical tracking methods for progress monitoring between reviews, a four-step integration plan for formal performance conversations, engagement strategies to maintain employee motivation, manager training requirements, a phased implementation roadmap from pilot to full deployment, and success metrics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and requires one variable: the organizational context (company size, industry, current performance review cadence, and cultural considerations). Use it when rolling out a new performance management system, modernizing existing review processes, or aligning individual development plans with strategic business goals. ● Produces a named framework with 3-5 psychological principles that underpin goal alignment and engagement. ● Generates a five-step goal-setting process and 3-5 tracking methods for continuous progress monitoring. ● Delivers manager training components and a five-phase rollout plan from pilot testing to organization-wide deployment. ● Outputs 4-5 success metrics (quantitative and qualitative) to measure framework effectiveness and employee outcomes. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR consultant specializing in performance management, goal-setting methodologies, and organizational psychology.

## Task
Design a comprehensive framework for integrating goal-setting and tracking into performance review processes. The framework should drive employee engagement, productivity, and development while aligning with organizational objectives.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver a structured framework containing:

**Framework Name**
Create a memorable name for this integrated performance management approach.

**Key Principles** (3-5 bullet points)
Foundational organizational psychology principles underpinning the framework.

**Goal-Setting Process** (5 steps)
Step-by-step process employees and managers follow to establish meaningful, aligned goals.

**Goal Tracking Methods** (3-5 bullet points)
Practical methods for monitoring progress between formal reviews.

**Performance Review Integration** (4 steps)
How goal conversations flow into and enhance formal performance reviews.

**Employee Engagement Strategies** (3-5 bullet points)
Techniques to maintain motivation and ownership throughout the goal cycle.

**Manager Training Components** (4 bullet points)
Essential skills and knowledge managers need to facilitate this framework.

**Framework Implementation Roadmap** (5 phases)
Phased rollout plan from pilot to full deployment.

**Success Metrics** (4-5 bullet points)
Quantitative and qualitative indicators to measure framework effectiveness.

Present each section clearly with the structure shown above. Ground all recommendations in evidence-based practices.
```
