# Social Media Caption Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Social Media Caption Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates high-conversion captions tailored to specific social networks, audiences, and brand voices for marketers and content creators. This social media caption prompt for ChatGPT produces five complete captions optimized for your chosen platform - whether Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, TikTok, or Facebook - each featuring an attention-grabbing hook, a concise value proposition highlighting your offering's benefits, and a clear call-to-action. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by applying dependency grammar principles to structure sentences around strong verbal or nominal heads, ensuring clarity and impact while respecting character limits and platform-specific formatting conventions like hashtag strategy and tone. Use it when you need multiple caption variations quickly, want to maintain consistent brand voice across platforms, or need to test different messaging approaches for the same product or service. ● Produces five unique captions per run, each with dependency grammar explanations showing how sentence structure creates emphasis and flow ● Adapts tone, hashtag density, and formatting to match platform conventions and your specified brand voice ● Includes hook, value proposition, and call-to-action in every caption to maximize engagement and conversions ● Accepts detailed input about your offering, target audience, key benefits, and unique selling points to ensure relevant, compelling copy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in high-conversion captions.

## Task
Write 5 engaging, platform-optimized captions that promote the offering and drive user action. Each caption must include an attention-grabbing hook, concise value proposition, and clear call-to-action tailored to the platform's character limits and features.

## Context
**Platform:** {{platform}}
**Offering & Audience:** {{product-and-audience}}
(Describe what you're promoting, who it's for, and 2-3 key benefits or unique selling points)
**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}
(e.g., professional, playful, inspirational, conversational)

Use dependency grammar principles to structure each caption: build sentences around strong verbal or nominal heads, with modifiers and complements arranged for maximum clarity and impact. Ensure tone, hashtag strategy, and formatting conventions match the platform.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of 5 captions. After each caption, provide a brief explanation of its dependency grammar structure, identifying the main head and how dependent elements create emphasis and flow.
```
