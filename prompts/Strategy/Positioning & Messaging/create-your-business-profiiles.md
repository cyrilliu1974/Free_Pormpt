# Business Profile Generator Prompt for Strategic Planning

## 簡介

The Business Profile Generator Prompt for Strategic Planning is a free AI prompt that builds complete business profile documents for entrepreneurs, consultants, and business owners establishing their market positioning. This business profile prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a seven-section strategic document including an executive summary, client demographics table, pain points analysis, goals and objectives, service offerings, value propositions, and a cohesive summary statement. The prompt conducts market analysis, competitor research, and demographic validation to ground every recommendation in real market data. Use it when launching a new venture, repositioning an existing business, preparing for investor conversations, or documenting your strategic foundation for a marketing team. ● Generates client demographics tables with specific psychographic and behavioral data, not vague audience descriptions ● Links pain points directly to measurable business problems your services solve, with impact analysis ● Structures service offerings as client outcomes with delivery methods and pricing frameworks ● Produces defensible value propositions grounded in competitor analysis and market opportunities ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a business strategist who creates foundational business profile documents that guide marketing, sales, and client acquisition.

## Task

Build a comprehensive business profile document structured around these sections:

1. **Executive Summary** – A paragraph capturing the business essence and positioning.
2. **Client Demographics Table** – Demographics, psychographics, and behavioral patterns in columns.
3. **Pain Points Analysis Table** – Problems, impact levels, and how services address each.
4. **Goals and Objectives Table** – Timeframes, success metrics, priority levels.
5. **Service Offerings Table** – Service names, client outcomes, delivery methods, pricing structures.
6. **Value Propositions Table** – Your unique advantages compared to standard industry offerings.
7. **Summary Statement** – A paragraph tying all elements into a cohesive business identity.

## Context

**Business and market information:**
{{business-context}}

**Research requirements:**
Conduct market analysis of the industry, competitor positioning, target demographic trends, industry pain points, successful value propositions in the sector, and market opportunities. Validate findings across multiple sources. Ground all recommendations in research and market data.

**Quality criteria:**
- Client demographics must be specific and actionable, not vague generalizations
- Pain points linked to measurable business problems the services directly address
- Goals concrete and time-bound where possible
- Services described as client outcomes, not just features
- Value propositions unique and defensible, not generic claims
- Each element supports client acquisition and business development
- Avoid jargon that obscures positioning

## Output

Write in concise prose targeting a Gunning Fog index of 8. Use clear logical sentences. Avoid unnecessary adjectives, adverbs, and complex vocabulary. Structure tables with consistent formatting and clear column headers. Ensure each section provides actionable insights for future business development.
```
