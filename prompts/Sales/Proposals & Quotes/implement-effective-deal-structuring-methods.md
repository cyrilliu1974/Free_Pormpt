# Deal Structuring Strategy Prompt for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Deal Structuring Strategy Prompt for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive pricing, packaging, and negotiation frameworks for B2B and B2C sales professionals. This deal structuring prompt for ChatGPT takes your product description, target market, current pricing strategy, and competitive landscape, then produces a multi-section strategy document. It analyzes value propositions, designs tiered pricing models, develops persuasive sales scripts and email templates, and maps negotiation tactics that protect margins while meeting customer needs. The output includes volume discount frameworks, bundling options, payment term recommendations, and objection-handling playbooks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Use this prompt when launching a new offering, entering a competitive market, or refining your sales approach to balance profitability with customer satisfaction. ● Produces an executive summary, value proposition analysis, and deal structuring toolkit with specific discount and bundling models ● Includes ready-to-use sales scripts, email templates, and negotiation playbooks tailored to your competitive context ● Maps a stage-by-stage implementation roadmap from prospect identification to closing ● Defines KPIs and feedback loops to refine pricing and packaging over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert deal structuring specialist focused on maximizing profitability and customer satisfaction through strategic pricing, packaging, and negotiation frameworks.

## Task
Create a comprehensive deal structuring strategy covering sales approaches, negotiation tactics, and pricing models. Deliver actionable guidelines for each stage of the deal lifecycle, from initial contact through closing.

## Context
**Offering and Market:**
{{product-service-description}}

**Target Market:**
{{target-market}}

**Current Pricing Strategy:**
{{current-pricing-strategy}}

**Competitive Landscape:**
{{competitive-context}}
(Include key competitors and your unique selling proposition)

## Approach
1. Analyze the offering's key value propositions relative to the target market's needs and pain points
2. Evaluate deal structuring techniques: volume discounts, bundling, tiered pricing, payment terms, and value-added services
3. Develop clear, persuasive sales scripts and email templates
4. Design negotiation strategies that balance margin protection with customer satisfaction
5. Integrate competitive positioning and market trends into pricing and packaging recommendations
6. Outline customer feedback loops for continuous refinement

## Output
Structure your response with:
- **Executive Summary**: core strategy and expected outcomes
- **Value Proposition Analysis**: what drives purchase decisions in this market
- **Deal Structuring Toolkit**: specific pricing models, discount frameworks, and package options
- **Sales & Negotiation Playbook**: scripts, email templates, objection handling, and closing techniques
- **Implementation Roadmap**: stage-by-stage guidelines from prospect identification to contract signature
- **Metrics & Refinement**: KPIs to track and how to incorporate feedback

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout.
```
