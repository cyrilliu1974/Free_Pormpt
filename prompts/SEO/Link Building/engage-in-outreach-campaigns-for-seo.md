# SEO Link Building Outreach Email Generator

## 簡介

The SEO Link Building Outreach Email Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized backlink outreach campaigns for websites seeking to improve search engine rankings through high-quality link partnerships. This link building outreach prompt for ChatGPT produces complete email templates with three subject line variations, personalization placeholders for recipient details, a two-email follow-up sequence with timing recommendations, and a comprehensive best practices checklist covering partner qualification, research tactics, CRM tracking, and response optimization. You input your website's value proposition, target niche, and link building objectives, and the prompt structures persuasive emails that emphasize mutual benefits and value exchange. SEO specialists, digital marketers, and content teams use it to streamline their backlink acquisition workflow and scale outreach without sacrificing personalization quality. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces three subject line variations to test messaging angles and maximize open rates ● Generates email templates with clearly marked placeholders for personalization research points ● Includes a two-email follow-up sequence with strategic timing to improve response rates ● Delivers a best practices checklist covering partner qualification criteria, tracking setup, and optimization tactics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert outreach specialist creating personalized backlink outreach emails that improve search engine rankings.

## Task
Write a compelling outreach email template that:
- Proposes a mutually beneficial linking partnership
- Personalizes to the recipient's site and content
- Clearly articulates value exchange
- Uses clear, persuasive structure with strong subject line
- Includes strategic follow-up guidance

## Context
**Website & Value Proposition:**
{{website-and-usp}}

**Target Niche:**
{{niche-and-audience}}

**Link Building Objectives:**
{{outreach-goals}}

## Output
Deliver:

1. **Subject line** (3 variations)
2. **Email template** with [PERSONALIZATION PLACEHOLDERS] clearly marked for recipient name, their site/article, specific alignment points
3. **Follow-up sequence** (2 follow-up email templates with appropriate timing)
4. **Best practices checklist** covering:
   - Partner identification and qualification criteria
   - Personalization research steps
   - Tracking and CRM setup
   - Response rate optimization tactics
   - Timing and frequency guidelines
```
