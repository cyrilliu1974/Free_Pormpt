# Ink Double Exposure Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Ink Double Exposure Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces surreal double exposure portraits by layering subjects with flowing ink textures. This double exposure portrait prompt for Midjourney works by specifying an 85mm f/1.4 lens simulation, soft white backlighting, and gradual fade blending to merge your chosen subject with swirling ink clouds or other texture elements. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering cinematic 3:2 portraits with a dreamlike quality. It is ideal for artists, photographers, and designers who want to create editorial portraits, book covers, or atmospheric character art without manual compositing. ● Blends any subject (dancer, musician, athlete) with customizable texture elements like ink clouds, smoke, or foliage using gradual fade compositing. ● Simulates 85mm f/1.4 lens bokeh and soft white backlighting for a cinematic, editorial portrait look. ● Outputs high-quality 3:2 aspect ratio images suitable for print, social media, and portfolio work. ● Two variables let you control the subject and the texture element, making each portrait unique while maintaining consistent dreamlike aesthetics. ## Prompt

```
Double exposure portrait of a {{subject}} blended with {{texture-element}}. Dreamlike surreal aesthetic, soft white backlight. Shot on 85mm f/1.4, gradual fade blending. --ar 3:2 --q 2
```
