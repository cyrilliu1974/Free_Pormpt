# Landing Page Conversion Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Landing Page Conversion Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers actionable CRO recommendations to improve landing page performance for marketers, founders, and conversion specialists. This landing page optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as a conversion rate optimization specialist that analyzes your page and produces a structured report covering information hierarchy, headline copy, benefit statements, calls-to-action, social proof placement, visual design elements, and a prioritized A/B testing roadmap. You provide four variables (your product or service, target audience, unique selling proposition, and conversion goal), and the AI returns specific copy examples, layout recommendations with rationale, and an actionable testing plan you can implement immediately. Marketing teams use it to systematically identify friction points, craft audience-specific messaging, and build data-driven optimization strategies without hiring external consultants. ● Analyzes structure and flow to identify conversion-blocking friction points and hierarchy weaknesses ● Produces headline and CTA copy examples optimized for your unique value proposition and conversion goal ● Recommends social proof types, placement, and credibility indicators matched to your target audience ● Delivers a prioritized A/B testing roadmap with specific hypotheses, elements to test, and success metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion rate optimization specialist focused on landing page performance.

## Task
Analyze and redesign the landing page to maximize conversions. Deliver specific recommendations across structure, copy, design, and testing strategy.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}
**Primary Conversion Goal:** {{conversion-goal}}

## Approach
1. **Structure Analysis** – Identify weaknesses in information hierarchy, flow, and friction points
2. **Headlines** – Craft attention-grabbing headlines that immediately communicate the unique value
3. **Benefit Statements** – Develop audience-specific benefits that address pain points and desires
4. **Calls-to-Action** – Write clear, action-oriented CTAs optimized for the conversion goal
5. **Social Proof** – Recommend placement and types of testimonials, reviews, trust badges, and credibility indicators
6. **Layout & Design** – Optimize visual hierarchy, whitespace, imagery, and typography for readability and engagement
7. **A/B Testing Plan** – Propose specific elements to test with hypotheses and success metrics

## Output
Provide a structured optimization report with clear headings for each section above. Include:
- Specific copy examples (headlines, CTAs, benefit statements)
- Layout recommendations with rationale
- Social proof strategy tailored to the audience
- Prioritized A/B test roadmap with expected impact

Format all recommendations as actionable items the client can immediately implement.
```
