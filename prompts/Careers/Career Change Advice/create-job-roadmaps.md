# Job Roadmap Generator for Career Planning

## 簡介

The Job Roadmap Generator for Career Planning is a free AI prompt that creates structured, evidence-based career progression plans tailored to any job title and current career situation. This job roadmap prompt for ChatGPT analyzes job market trends, industry requirements, and proven career progression patterns to deliver a markdown table with three actionable columns: required education and certifications, essential versus desirable skills (technical and soft), and career development milestones from entry to senior levels. It adapts to your specific context, whether you are a recent graduate, mid-career professional, or career changer, and works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Real-world use cases include advising clients on paths into software engineering, healthcare management, data science, or any specialized field where clear progression steps matter. This prompt is ideal for career counselors building client roadmaps, HR professionals designing internal development tracks, and individuals mapping their own career trajectory with realistic timelines and networking strategies. ● Produces a three-column markdown table separating education paths, skill requirements, and career milestones for clarity and action. ● Distinguishes must-have skills from competitive-advantage skills so users prioritize effectively. ● Includes alternative learning paths like bootcamps, online courses, and self-study resources alongside traditional degrees. ● Provides typical timelines, networking strategies, and advancement opportunities specific to the target role and industry. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career counselor specializing in evidence-based career progression planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive job roadmap for {{job-title}} that outlines the necessary education, skills, and career development steps. Analyze current job market trends, industry requirements, and proven career progression patterns in the field.

## Context
The roadmap should be tailored to someone with {{current-situation}} (specify education level, years of experience, and industry if applicable). Make the roadmap both realistic and aspirational, providing guidance that works for individuals at various career stages—from entry-level to advanced positions.

## Output
Present the roadmap as a markdown table with three columns:

| Education | Skills | Career Development |
|-----------|--------|--------------------|

**Education column**: List required degrees, certifications, continuing education, and alternative learning paths (bootcamps, online courses, self-study resources).

**Skills column**: Separate essential skills (must-have for the role) from desirable skills (competitive advantages). Include both technical and soft skills.

**Career Development column**: Outline key milestones and progression steps from entry to senior levels, typical timelines, networking strategies, and advancement opportunities.

Ensure each column provides comprehensive, actionable information specific to the job title and industry context provided.
```
