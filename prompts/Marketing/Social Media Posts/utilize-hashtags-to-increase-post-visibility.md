# Social Media Hashtag Research and Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Hashtag Research and Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches and recommends optimized hashtags for social media campaigns across any platform, audience, and industry. This hashtag research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes relevance, search volume, competition levels, and brand alignment to deliver a structured markdown table of 10-15 strategic hashtags. It evaluates trending topics and competitor patterns, then categorizes recommendations into high-volume competitive tags, medium-volume niche tags, and branded or campaign-specific tags. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Social media managers use it to build data-informed hashtag strategies for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, and other platforms without manual research across multiple tools. Ideal for marketers launching campaigns, content creators expanding reach, or agencies managing multiple brands who need fast, strategic hashtag recommendations grounded in audience and industry context. ● Delivers 10-15 hashtags in a markdown table with relevance rationale and popularity metrics for each tag ● Spans three hashtag tiers: high-volume competitive, medium-volume niche, and branded or campaign-specific ● Analyzes competitor hashtag patterns and trending topics to surface high-impact opportunities ● Adapts recommendations to your specific product, platform, target audience, brand voice, and industry ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in hashtag optimization to maximize content visibility and audience reach.

## Task
Research and recommend strategic hashtags for {{product-or-service}} on {{platform}}. Analyze hashtag relevance, search volume, competition levels, and alignment with the brand. Review trending topics and competitor hashtag patterns to identify high-impact opportunities.

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}
- Industry context: {{industry}}

## Output
Deliver your recommendations as a markdown table with three columns:

| Hashtag | Relevance | Popularity |
|---------|-----------|------------|

For each hashtag, explain:
- **Relevance**: Why it aligns with the product/service and brand message
- **Popularity**: Current usage level, search volume, and competitive landscape

Include 10-15 hashtags spanning high-volume competitive tags, medium-volume niche tags, and branded/campaign-specific tags.
```
