# Virtual Assistant Services List for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Virtual Assistant Services List for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a customized catalog of virtual assistant services matched to a solopreneur's specific industry and operational needs. This virtual assistant services prompt for ChatGPT works by combining your niche (e.g., e-commerce, consulting, content creation) with a virtual assistant's expertise to produce 8–12 actionable service descriptions. Each entry explains not just the task but the concrete benefit - time saved, productivity gained, or revenue protected - so solopreneurs can quickly identify which responsibilities to delegate first. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and outputs a bullet-point list covering administrative support, marketing execution, customer service, and specialized niche tasks that are practical to implement remotely and scale as the business grows. Reach for this prompt when you're a solopreneur evaluating which tasks to hand off, a virtual assistant building a service menu, or an agency packaging offers for solo business owners. ● Produces 8–12 services spanning admin, marketing, customer service, and niche-specific tasks ● Each service includes a clear benefit statement tied to time savings, productivity, or revenue impact ● Adapts to any solopreneur niche by incorporating industry context and VA skill sets ● Output is formatted as a clean bullet list, ready to use in proposals, onboarding docs, or service pages ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a virtual assistant services expert specializing in helping solopreneurs delegate effectively and scale their operations.

## Task
Create a comprehensive list of virtual assistant services tailored to {{solopreneur-niche}}. For each service, explain the specific benefit it provides to solopreneurs in this niche—how it saves time, improves productivity, or drives business growth.

## Context
Solopreneurs in {{solopreneur-niche}} face unique operational challenges and time constraints. The services you recommend should:
- Address the most time-consuming, repetitive, or specialized tasks in this niche
- Be practical to implement remotely
- Scale with the solopreneur's business
- Deliver measurable value quickly

Draw on {{va-expertise-and-strengths}} to ensure recommendations are grounded in real capability and market demand.

## Output
Format as a bullet point list:
- **[Service Name]**: [Specific benefit for solopreneurs in this niche, focusing on time saved, revenue impact, or growth enabled]

Include 8–12 services covering administrative, marketing, customer service, and specialized niche tasks.
```
