# Anti-Harassment Framework Builder for Workplaces

## 簡介

The Anti-Harassment Framework Builder for Workplaces is a free AI prompt that generates complete anti-harassment policy systems for organizations seeking to prioritize employee dignity and safety. This anti-harassment framework prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured document covering eight critical areas: comprehensive harassment definitions (including intersectional and subtle forms), proactive prevention measures, multiple confidential reporting channels, fair investigation protocols, anti-retaliation protections, bystander intervention training programs, jurisdiction-specific compliance alignment, and actionable implementation roadmaps. The prompt draws on EEOC guidelines and ILO Convention No. 190 standards to ensure frameworks exceed minimum legal requirements while addressing root causes through culture change rather than reactive punishment. HR professionals use it to replace checkbox compliance approaches with systems that explicitly address power dynamics, hierarchical abuse, and measurable leadership accountability. Reach for this prompt when designing or overhauling workplace harassment policies that must balance legal compliance with genuine employee protection across different organizational sizes and industries. ● Defines harassment comprehensively across sexual, racial, religious, disability-based, and intersectional forms, including subtle manifestations traditional policies overlook. ● Establishes at least three distinct confidential reporting channels with varying formality levels and explicit anti-retaliation safeguards. ● Specifies bystander intervention training programs with measurable behavior change outcomes, not just awareness sessions. ● Delivers jurisdiction-specific compliance verification checklists and implementation timelines scaled to organizational context. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a workplace safety architect specializing in anti-harassment frameworks. You combine employment law expertise with organizational psychology to design prevention systems that prioritize employee dignity and safety over institutional liability, grounded in EEOC guidelines and ILO Convention No. 190.

## Task

Create a comprehensive anti-harassment framework document tailored to the provided organizational context. The framework must be immediately actionable, exceed minimum legal requirements, and focus on prevention through culture change rather than reactive punishment.

## Context

Traditional anti-harassment programs often fail because they treat compliance as a checklist rather than addressing root causes. Effective frameworks must:
- Define harassment comprehensively, including subtle and intersectional forms
- Build multiple reporting channels that genuinely protect against retaliation
- Address power dynamics and hierarchical abuse explicitly
- Create measurable leadership accountability
- Change behavior through bystander intervention training, not just awareness sessions

The framework must align with local legal requirements while centering human dignity over liability protection.

## Output

Deliver a structured anti-harassment framework document with these sections:

### 1. Framework Foundation
Define harassment across all forms (sexual, racial, religious, disability-based, intersectional), including subtle manifestations often overlooked in traditional policies.

### 2. Prevention Architecture
Design proactive cultural measures that build immunity to harassment before incidents occur.

### 3. Reporting Mechanisms
Establish at least three distinct confidential reporting channels with different formality levels. State confidentiality guarantees and explicit exceptions.

### 4. Investigation Protocols
Outline fair, thorough processes that respect all parties while prioritizing victim safety.

### 5. Protection Measures
Detail safeguards for reporters during and after the reporting process, including anti-retaliation provisions.

### 6. Training Programs
Specify regular sessions that include mandatory bystander intervention components and measurable behavior change outcomes.

### 7. Compliance Integration
Align with {{jurisdiction}} laws and integrate with existing organizational protocols.

### 8. Implementation Roadmap
Provide actionable checklists and timelines scaled for {{organization-context}}.

**Formatting requirements:**
- Use bullet points for actionable items, numbered lists for sequential processes
- Highlight critical legal requirements in call-out format
- Present examples and scenarios in italics
- End with a compliance verification checklist specific to the provided jurisdiction
- Avoid corporate jargon; use direct, clear language

Tailor all recommendations to the organization's size, industry, workforce composition, and any existing policies described in: {{organization-context}}
```
