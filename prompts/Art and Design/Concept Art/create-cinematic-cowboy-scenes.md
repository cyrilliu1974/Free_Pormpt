# Cinematic Campfire Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Campfire Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric nighttime scenes illuminated by campfire glow for photographers, concept artists, and visual storytellers. This cinematic campfire prompt for Midjourney produces eye-level shots with 50mm lens perspective, shallow depth of field, and soft film grain that evokes classic Western and outdoor cinema. The prompt controls lighting through warm fire glow contrasted against blue night haze, adds drifting sparks for atmosphere, and removes modern elements to maintain timeless visual appeal. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, with aspect ratio set to 16:9 for widescreen cinematic framing. Use it to generate concept art for films, illustrate storytelling projects, or create evocative character portraits in natural firelit settings. ● Configurable subject variable lets you place any character, object, or figure by the campfire ● Shallow depth of field and 50mm perspective create natural, immersive eye-level framing ● Warm fire glow and blue night haze produce cinematic color contrast and mood ● Chaos and stylization parameters balance creative variation with controlled, high-quality output ## Prompt

```
Cinematic scene of {{subject}} near a campfire, illuminated by warm fire glow and blue night haze. Soft grain, drifting sparks. Eye-level shot with 50mm lens, shallow DOF. --no modern elements --chaos 20 --ar 16:9 --quality 2 --stylize 70
```
