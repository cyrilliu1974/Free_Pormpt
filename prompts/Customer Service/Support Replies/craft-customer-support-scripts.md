# Customer Support Script Builder for Multi-Channel Teams

## 簡介

The Customer Support Script Builder for Multi-Channel Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured support scripts across live chat, phone, and email channels for customer service teams. It produces nine distinct scripts - three tone variations (friendly, professional, apologetic) for each communication channel - with each script organized into opening, problem identification, solution presentation, and closing components. This customer support script prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok also embeds personalization points, transition phrases, and escalation triggers to help agents handle routine inquiries and complex situations. Support managers reach for it when standardizing communication across teams, training new agents, or ensuring brand voice consistency while maintaining the flexibility to match customer emotions and issue severity. ● Produces separate scripts for live chat, phone, and email with appropriate pacing and format for each channel. ● Delivers three tone variations per channel so agents can match customer mood and issue severity. ● Embeds labeled personalization points and escalation triggers within each script for consistent handling of edge cases. ● Structures every script with four clear components: opening to build rapport, problem identification to validate concerns, solution presentation with actionable steps, and closing to reinforce the relationship. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer service communication specialist designing support scripts for multi-channel teams.

## Task
Create a complete set of customer support scripts for the provided product and issues. Deliver separate scripts for live chat, phone, and email channels, each with three tone variations: friendly, professional, and apologetic.

## Context
{{product-and-issues}}

Structure each script with four components:
- **Opening/Greeting**: Establish rapport and gather initial information
- **Problem Identification**: Acknowledge and validate the customer's concern
- **Solution Presentation**: Provide clear resolution steps and next actions
- **Closing**: Reinforce the relationship and invite future engagement

Include transition phrases between sections, personalization points, and escalation triggers for complex situations. Scripts should de-escalate tension, build rapport, and guide customers to resolution while maintaining efficiency.

## Output
Organize your response with clear headings:

**Live Chat Scripts**
- Friendly tone
- Professional tone
- Apologetic tone

**Phone Scripts**
- Friendly tone
- Professional tone
- Apologetic tone

**Email Scripts**
- Friendly tone
- Professional tone
- Apologetic tone

Within each script, label the four structural components (Opening, Problem Identification, Solution, Closing) and mark suggested [personalization points] and [escalation triggers].
```
