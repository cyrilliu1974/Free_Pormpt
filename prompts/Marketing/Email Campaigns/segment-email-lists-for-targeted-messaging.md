# Email List Segmentation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Email List Segmentation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds detailed email segmentation plans with personalized campaign sequences for marketers and business owners. This email list segmentation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your audience criteria and business goals to create 3-5 distinct segments, each with complete profiles, tailored email sequences, content recommendations, send-time optimization, and performance frameworks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing step-by-step implementation guides that specify sequence cadence, messaging angles, personalization opportunities, and A/B testing strategies. Teams use it to move beyond one-size-fits-all campaigns and deliver messages that resonate with each audience group's unique pain points and preferences. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform a broad email list into targeted segments with actionable campaign blueprints, or when conversion rates signal that generic messaging is no longer working. ● Defines 3-5 audience segments with demographics, behaviors, pain points, and content preferences based on your criteria ● Designs tailored email sequences for each segment with specific cadence, themes, CTAs, and personalization hooks ● Recommends optimal send times, subject line formulas, content types, and A/B test opportunities per segment ● Establishes performance frameworks with key metrics and benchmarks to track engagement and conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in segmentation and personalized campaign design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive email segmentation strategy and targeted sequence plan, delivered as a step-by-step implementation guide.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

**Email Marketing Goals:**
{{goals}}

**Segmentation Criteria:**
{{segmentation-criteria}}

## Process
1. **Segment Analysis**: Evaluate the provided segmentation criteria and define 3-5 distinct audience segments with clear characteristics and needs.

2. **Segment Profiles**: For each segment, document demographics, behaviors, pain points, and content preferences.

3. **Email Sequence Design**: For each segment, outline a tailored email sequence including:
   - Sequence length and cadence
   - Content themes and messaging angles
   - Personalization opportunities
   - Calls-to-action aligned to segment goals

4. **Content Recommendations**: Specify content types (educational, promotional, testimonial, etc.) best suited to each segment.

5. **Optimization Tactics**: Recommend optimal send times, subject line formulas, and A/B test opportunities for each segment.

6. **Performance Framework**: Define key metrics (open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, revenue per email) and benchmarks to track for each segment.

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with clear section headings. Each segment should have its own subsection with actionable, specific recommendations ready for immediate implementation.
```
