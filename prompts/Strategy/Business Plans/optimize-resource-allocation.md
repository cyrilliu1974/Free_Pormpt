# Resource Allocation Strategy for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Resource Allocation Strategy for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a customized distribution plan for time, money, energy, and skills tailored to solo business owners. This resource allocation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, goals, available resources, challenges, and target market to create a structured allocation table spanning 5-8 critical areas. The prompt works step-by-step, first evaluating your business context, then identifying key allocation priorities for bootstrapping, determining percentage distributions, and providing clear justifications for every decision. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both strategic narrative and a markdown table that maps resources to activities like product development, marketing, customer acquisition, operations, and skill-building. Solopreneurs use it when launching ventures, pivoting business models, or recalibrating how they invest limited capacity. ● Produces a markdown table allocating resources across 5-8 areas specific to your business model and stage ● Accounts for time, money, energy, and skills in each allocation decision ● Provides justification grounded in your target market, bootstrapping realities, and stated objectives ● Delivers a 2-3 paragraph strategic introduction explaining allocation principles before the detailed breakdown ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in resource allocation for solopreneurs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive resource allocation strategy tailored to the solopreneur's specific business context. Work step-by-step:

1. Analyze the business type, goals, available resources, challenges, and target market
2. Identify key allocation areas crucial for bootstrapping this business
3. Determine appropriate percentage of resources for each area
4. Provide clear justification for each allocation decision

Consider time, money, energy, and skills when allocating resources. Ensure the strategy is realistic, efficient, and aligned with the stated goals.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide a brief introduction (2-3 paragraphs) explaining the overall strategy and key allocation principles for this solopreneur.

Then present the allocation plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Allocation Area | Percentage of Resources | Justification |
|----------------|------------------------|---------------|

Include 5-8 allocation areas relevant to this business.
```
