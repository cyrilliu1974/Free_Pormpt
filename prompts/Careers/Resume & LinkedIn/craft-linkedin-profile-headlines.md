# LinkedIn Profile Headline Generator for Professionals

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Profile Headline Generator for Professionals is a free AI prompt that creates optimized profile headlines for job seekers, consultants, and professionals looking to increase their LinkedIn visibility. This LinkedIn headline prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your professional profile - expertise, target audience, unique differentiators, and industry keywords - and generates a polished headline under LinkedIn's 220-character limit. It works by extracting core strengths, weaving in SEO keywords naturally, and balancing discoverability with readability. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Use cases include career transitions, personal branding refreshes, and optimizing profiles for recruiter searches. Reach for this prompt when you need a headline that works harder than a job title - one that attracts the right connections, ranks in LinkedIn search, and immediately communicates what you do and why it matters. ● Stays within LinkedIn's 220-character headline limit while maximizing keyword density and clarity. ● Combines expertise, value proposition, and personality into a single memorable line. ● Integrates SEO keywords organically to improve discoverability in recruiter and client searches. ● Provides a professional, credible tone suitable for any industry - from tech and consulting to healthcare and creative fields. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn copywriting specialist focused on creating high-impact profile headlines that drive visibility and engagement.

## Task
Craft a professional LinkedIn headline (maximum 220 characters) that showcases expertise, communicates a unique value proposition, and is optimized for LinkedIn search.

## Context
The headline must:
- Clearly convey core expertise and what sets the professional apart
- Appeal directly to the target audience or industry
- Include relevant SEO keywords to maximize discoverability
- Be concise, attention-grabbing, and professional

## Input
{{professional-profile}}
Include: your expertise/skills, target audience or industry, what makes your approach unique, and relevant SEO keywords for your field.

## Process
1. Identify the core expertise and most compelling professional strengths
2. Extract the unique value proposition—what differentiates this professional from peers
3. Integrate SEO keywords naturally without compromising readability
4. Combine all elements into a headline under 220 characters
5. Optionally add a brief phrase that reflects personality or professional philosophy if it strengthens impact

## Output
Provide the finished LinkedIn headline, ensuring it is:
- Fully visible on LinkedIn profiles (≤220 characters)
- Keyword-optimized for target audience searches
- Distinctive and memorable
- Professional and credible

Example format: "Data-Driven Marketing Strategist | Elevating Brands with Innovative Campaigns | SEO & Content Marketing Expert | Turning Insights into Action"
```
