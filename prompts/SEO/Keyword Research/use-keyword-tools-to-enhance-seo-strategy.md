# Keyword Research Report Generator for SEO Strategy

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Report Generator for SEO Strategy is a free AI prompt that produces prioritized keyword research reports with actionable recommendations for SEO strategists and content marketers. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes keyword candidates across relevance, search volume, and ranking difficulty to identify high-potential opportunities. It works with any keyword research tool data you provide, evaluating each keyword against your target audience characteristics, business niche, competitors, and domain authority. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured markdown table of keywords with search volume and difficulty metrics, followed by detailed analysis of the top five opportunities with justifications that cover user intent, strategic value, and content alignment. Real-world applications include planning content calendars, prioritizing SEO investments, identifying low-competition opportunities, and aligning organic search strategy with business objectives. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn raw keyword data into strategic direction, especially when balancing traffic potential against competitive reality for a specific domain authority level. ● Structures keyword evaluation around three critical dimensions: audience relevance, search volume, and competitive difficulty ● Outputs a scannable markdown table followed by strategic justifications for the top five keyword opportunities ● Balances traffic potential with ranking feasibility based on your current domain authority and content capabilities ● Connects each keyword recommendation to user intent and business objectives rather than raw metrics alone ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist conducting keyword research to optimize search visibility and organic traffic.

## Task
Deliver a prioritized keyword research report with actionable recommendations. Analyze keywords for relevance, search volume, and ranking difficulty, then select the most promising opportunities that align with the target audience and business objectives.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-context}}
(Include: target audience characteristics, business niche, main competitors, current SEO goals)

**Research Tools:**
{{keyword-research-tools}}

Evaluate each keyword candidate across three dimensions:
- **Relevance:** alignment with audience intent and business offerings
- **Search Volume:** monthly traffic potential
- **Difficulty:** competitive intensity and ranking feasibility

Prioritize keywords that balance high search volume with achievable difficulty levels for the business's current domain authority and content capabilities.

## Output
Deliver your findings as:

1. **Keyword Table** (markdown format):

| Keyword | Search Volume | Difficulty |
|---------|--------------|------------|

2. **Top 5 Analysis** (below the table):
For each of the five highest-priority keywords, provide a brief justification covering why it's valuable, the user intent it captures, and how it fits the SEO strategy.
```
