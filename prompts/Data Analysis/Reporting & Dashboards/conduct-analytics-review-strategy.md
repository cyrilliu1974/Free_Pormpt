# Engagement Analytics Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Engagement Analytics Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end audience engagement measurement plans tailored to your specific project context. This engagement analytics prompt for ChatGPT walks through platform selection (identifying which social and digital channels matter for your audience), metric collection methods (specific steps to extract engagement data), processing pipelines (cleaning and structuring raw data), and actionable recommendations tied to key insights. Each strategy includes credible citations to support the recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a multi-section text report with executive summary, platform justifications, collection steps, processing workflows, and insight-to-action pairs. Marketing teams use it to replace generic analytics templates with context-aware measurement frameworks; product managers use it to track feature engagement; content strategists use it to map audience behavior across channels. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, evidence-backed plan for measuring and interpreting engagement - not just a list of metrics, but a complete strategy from data capture to decision-making. ● Identifies relevant platforms with audience-specific justification instead of generic channel lists ● Breaks data collection into executable steps, not vague "track engagement" advice ● Maps insights directly to recommendations, connecting what the data shows to what action you should take ● Includes credible source citations to validate each strategic recommendation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an analytics strategist who designs audience engagement measurement plans: platform selection, data collection methods, processing pipelines, and actionable recommendations supported by credible sources.

## Context

{{project-context}}

## Task

Build a complete engagement analytics strategy covering:

1. **Platform selection** – which platforms matter for this audience and why
2. **Data collection** – specific steps to pull the right metrics from each platform
3. **Data processing** – steps to clean, structure, and interpret the collected data
4. **Insights and recommendations** – what the data reveals and what actions to take
5. **Citations** – credible sources supporting your recommendations

Stay specific to the context provided. Avoid generic advice.
```
