# Email Sequence Builder for Sales Funnels

## 簡介

The Email Sequence Builder for Sales Funnels is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, stage-by-stage email sequences for marketers and sales teams looking to guide prospects through their buyer journey. This email sequence prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, product positioning, and sales process to produce a complete drip campaign delivered in a markdown table format. Each email builds on the previous one, addressing pain points and motivations that match where the prospect is in the funnel - from initial awareness through consideration to final decision. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs subject lines, key content points, calls-to-action, and recommended send timing for every message in your sequence. Real use cases include onboarding new leads, nurturing trial users, re-engaging cold prospects, and accelerating enterprise sales cycles. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive email series that respects the natural pace of your buyer's journey and ties messaging to funnel position. ● Maps content and tone to each stage of the buyer journey so messaging evolves as prospects move through the funnel. ● Outputs a table with subject lines, content pillars, CTAs, and timing recommendations for every email. ● Identifies where prospects typically hesitate or drop off and addresses those friction points in sequence logic. ● Structures emails using dependency - each message assumes context from the prior one, building narrative momentum toward conversion. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in sequences that drive engagement and conversions.

## Task
Create a personalized email sequence for the sales process described below. Each email should build on the previous one, guiding recipients through the funnel from awareness to decision.

## Context
**Target Audience & Product:**
{{audience-and-product}}
(Include: who they are, their pain points, your product/service, and your unique selling proposition)

**Sales Process & Goals:**
{{sales-process-and-outcome}}
(Describe: your current sales stages, typical buyer journey, and the specific conversion outcome you want)

## Analysis
Before drafting, identify:
- Core pain points and motivations of this audience
- Where prospects typically hesitate or drop off
- How your USP addresses their specific needs
- Appropriate sequence length and email cadence for this sales cycle

## Output
Deliver the sequence as a markdown table:

| Email Number | Subject Line | Key Content Points | Call-to-Action |
|--------------|--------------|--------------------|-----------------|

**Requirements:**
- Use persuasive, audience-appropriate language
- Structure each email so it depends on insights from the prior one
- Match content and tone to the buyer journey stage (awareness → consideration → decision)
- Include optimal send timing recommendations (e.g., "Day 1", "3 days after Email 2")
- Make CTAs specific, actionable, and aligned with funnel position
```
