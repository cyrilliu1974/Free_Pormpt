# Social Media Caption Starter Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Caption Starter Generator is a free AI prompt that produces attention-grabbing opening lines tailored to your brand voice and audience across any platform. This social media caption prompt for ChatGPT delivers 10 ready-to-use openers spanning questions, statements, storytelling hooks, and curiosity gaps. It varies emotional tones from inspirational to playful while ensuring each starter invites comments, shares, or saves. The prompt works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating openers designed for feed scanning and immediate engagement. Real use cases include building caption libraries for content calendars, testing different engagement strategies, and maintaining consistent brand voice across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and other platforms. Content creators, social media managers, and brand teams reach for this prompt when they need fresh opening lines that balance adaptability with brand consistency. ● Produces 10 caption starters spanning different engagement strategies including questions, challenges, and storytelling hooks ● Varies emotional tones from inspirational and empathetic to bold and playful to match content goals ● Keeps openers concise and front-loaded for users scrolling feeds quickly ● Adapts to your specific audience demographics, brand voice, and platform requirements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in high-performing caption copy.

## Task
Generate 10 versatile, attention-grabbing caption starters that drive engagement across multiple content types and emotional tones. Each opener should be adaptable enough to fit various post formats while remaining true to the specified brand voice and audience.

## Context
**Audience & Brand:** {{audience-and-brand}}
(Include target demographic, brand voice characteristics, and content focus areas)

**Platforms:** {{platforms}}

## Requirements
- Span different engagement strategies: questions, statements, challenges, storytelling hooks, curiosity gaps
- Vary emotional tones: inspirational, conversational, bold, empathetic, playful
- Ensure each starter invites interaction (comments, shares, saves)
- Keep phrases concise and front-loaded for feed scanning

## Output
Deliver exactly 10 caption starters as a numbered list, one per line.
```
