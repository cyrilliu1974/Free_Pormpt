# Wellness-Integrated Lesson Plan Creator

## 簡介

The Wellness-Integrated Lesson Plan Creator is a free AI prompt that builds classroom-ready lesson plans embedding stress reduction techniques into academic content for K-12 educators and curriculum designers. This wellness lesson plan prompt for ChatGPT produces structured templates that show exactly where and how to weave mindfulness, breathing exercises, and calming techniques into existing subject instruction - complete with timing guides, age modifications, and dual assessment rubrics for both academic and wellness outcomes. It works within real constraints: no special equipment, limited class time, and teachers at any wellness expertise level. Use it when designing lessons for test-anxious students, high-pressure classes, or any environment where student stress interferes with learning. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Embeds evidence-based stress reduction techniques at natural transition points in lesson flow, with clear rationale connecting wellness activities to subject matter. ● Provides zero-cost implementation steps, prioritizing materials always available in classrooms and techniques requiring no specialized training. ● Includes age-group modifications and combined assessment rubrics that track both academic progress and wellness impact through observable behaviors and student self-report. ● Delivers opener routines, core instruction touchpoints, practice activities, and closure reflections in a bullet-and-numbered format for immediate classroom use. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert wellness educator and curriculum designer specializing in embedding evidence-based stress reduction techniques into academic lesson plans without disrupting learning objectives.

## Task
Create a comprehensive wellness-integrated lesson plan that naturally incorporates stress reduction techniques while maintaining academic rigor. The plan must work within real classroom constraints: limited time, no special equipment, and varying teacher wellness expertise.

## Context
{{teaching-context}}
*Include: subject area or grade level, typical class duration, main student stress triggers (test anxiety, social pressure, workload, etc.), your own wellness background (beginner to experienced), and any physical or time constraints.*

## Output
Provide a structured lesson plan template containing:

### Learning Objectives
- Academic learning goals aligned to standards
- Wellness outcomes (specific stress reduction skills students will practice)

### Wellness Integration Points
- Where and how stress reduction techniques naturally embed into the lesson flow
- Rationale connecting wellness activities to subject matter

### Step-by-Step Activities
1. Lesson opener (include embedded calming or centering technique)
2. Core instruction phases (mark stress reduction touchpoints)
3. Practice activities (integrate movement, breathing, or mindfulness)
4. Closure (reflection on both academic and wellness learning)

**For each activity provide:**
- Clear implementation steps an educator with any wellness experience level can follow
- Timing guide
- Modifications for different age groups and learning environments
- Equipment needed (prioritize zero-cost, always-available materials)

### Assessment Strategies
- Academic progress measures
- Wellness impact indicators (student self-report, observable behavior changes, engagement metrics)
- Combined rubric tracking both dimensions

Format with bullet points and numbered steps for immediate classroom implementation.
```
