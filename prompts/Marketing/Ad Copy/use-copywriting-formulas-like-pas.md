# PAS Copywriting Formula Sales Letter Generator

## 簡介

The PAS Copywriting Formula Sales Letter Generator is a free AI prompt that structures persuasive sales copy using the proven Problem-Agitate-Solution framework for marketers, business owners, and copywriters. This PAS copywriting prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a three-part structure: identifying the core problem your audience faces, agitating it by highlighting negative consequences and emotional costs, then presenting your product or service as the ideal solution with benefits and social proof. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure logical sentence flow and cause-effect clarity, making the letter easy to read and compelling. Use it when launching products, writing landing pages, crafting email campaigns, or building sales funnels that require high-converting copy. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Structures copy into Problem, Agitate, and Solution sections with clear headings and bullet points for readability ● Employs dependency grammar to create logical sentence progression and clear cause-effect relationships ● Incorporates emotional triggers, persuasive language, and social proof to drive decision-making ● Adapts tone and vocabulary to match your specific audience and offering details ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive sales letters.

## Task
Write a compelling sales letter using the Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS) framework. Structure the letter in three sections:

1. **Problem**: Identify the core problem your target audience faces. Make it specific and relatable.
2. **Agitate**: Amplify the problem by highlighting its negative consequences, emotional toll, and urgency. Show what happens if it remains unsolved.
3. **Solution**: Present the product or service as the ideal resolution. Explain how it addresses the problem, deliver the key benefits, and reinforce credibility with social proof.

## Context
{{offering-details}}

Use dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating clear cause-effect relationships. Employ persuasive language, emotional triggers, and concrete evidence. Tailor tone and vocabulary to resonate with the specific audience described above.

## Output
Deliver the sales letter with clear headings for Problem, Agitate, and Solution sections. Use bullet points or numbered lists within each section to enhance readability and impact.
```
