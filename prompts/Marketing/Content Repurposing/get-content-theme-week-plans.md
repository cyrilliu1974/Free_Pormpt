# Content Theme Week Planner Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Content Theme Week Planner Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds structured 7-day content campaigns for marketers, social media managers, and content strategists. This content theme week prompt for ChatGPT takes your audience details and business goals, then outputs a markdown table mapping each day to a specific topic, content format (video, blog post, infographic, live stream, carousel), core takeaway, potential collaborators, distribution channels, and measurable KPIs. It also delivers a compelling theme title and narrative description that ties the week together. Use it when planning product launches, awareness campaigns, seasonal activations, or educational series that need cohesive storytelling across multiple touchpoints. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Outputs a complete 7-day calendar with topic, format, message, collaborators, channels, and metrics for each day ● Ensures content variety across the week to maintain audience interest and maximize reach ● Aligns every piece to your stated goals, whether lead generation, brand awareness, community building, or thought leadership ● Provides a thematic narrative and title that gives the campaign a memorable identity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in thematic campaign planning and audience engagement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 7-day content theme week plan that maximizes audience engagement and aligns with business objectives.

## Context
**Target audience and business context:** {{audience-and-niche}}
(Describe your target audience demographics, psychographics, pain points, and your business niche or industry)

**Content goals and constraints:** {{goals-and-resources}}
(Specify what you want to achieve—brand awareness, lead generation, community building, thought leadership—and note your available resources: team size, budget, production capabilities, preferred content formats)

## Output
Deliver your plan as a **markdown table** with these columns:
- **Day** (1-7)
- **Content Topic**
- **Content Type** (video, blog post, infographic, live stream, carousel, etc.)
- **Key Message / Takeaway**
- **Potential Collaborators** (internal team members, guest experts, influencers, partners)
- **Promotion Strategy** (channels, timing, paid/organic tactics)
- **Success Metrics** (engagement rate, reach, conversions, sign-ups, etc.)

Precede the table with:
1. **Theme Title:** A compelling, memorable name for the week
2. **Theme Description:** 2-3 sentences explaining the overarching narrative and why it matters to the audience

Ensure daily topics build momentum across the week, content types are varied to maintain interest, and all elements work cohesively toward the stated goals.
```
