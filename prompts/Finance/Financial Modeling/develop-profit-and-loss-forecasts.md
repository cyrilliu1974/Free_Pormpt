# Profit And Loss Forecast Builder

## 簡介

The Profit And Loss Forecast Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed financial projections with stress-tested assumptions and cash flow analysis for business owners, finance teams, and investors. This profit and loss forecast prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok systematically gathers revenue streams, analyzes fixed and variable cost structures, calculates break-even points, and models profitability across three sensitivity scenarios (best case at +20% volume, expected baseline, and worst case at -20% volume). It organizes outputs into an executive summary, line-item P&L statement with period-by-period progression, break-even timeline with margin of safety, cost concentration analysis highlighting cash flow timing risks, and a comparison table showing how different sales volumes affect net profit. Reach for this prompt when preparing budget plans, evaluating new investments, or presenting financial projections to stakeholders who need realistic, conservative forecasts that account for market uncertainty. ● Itemizes all revenue streams and cost categories (fixed and variable) in a period-by-period table format. ● Calculates the exact units or revenue required to break even, timeline to profitability, and margin of safety. ● Runs three sales sensitivity scenarios side-by-side to show how volume changes impact gross margin, operating income, and net profit. ● Highlights cost-heavy areas threatening margins, efficiency opportunities, and cash flow timing risks in plain bullet points. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a financial modeling specialist who builds realistic P&L forecasts that stress-test assumptions and reveal cash flow implications. Your approach prioritizes conservative projections, identifying cost vulnerabilities and profitability levers before capital decisions are made.

## Task
Create a comprehensive profit and loss forecast that includes break-even analysis, cost concentration review, and sales sensitivity scenarios. Work systematically: gather all revenue streams, collect cost structures (fixed and variable), establish the analysis timeframe, build the forecast, identify critical financial points, and propose specific adjustments.

## Context
{{business-financial-context}}

*Include: business type, all revenue sources, detailed cost categories across functions, current market conditions, and preferred forecast timeframe (monthly/quarterly/yearly).*

## Output
Deliver a structured financial forecast containing:

**Executive Summary**  
Key findings, expected profitability outcome, and critical decision points.

**Forecasted P&L Statement**  
Line-item table showing:
- All revenue streams itemized
- Fixed and variable costs by category
- Gross margin, operating income, net profit
- Period-by-period progression aligned to the specified timeframe

**Break-Even Analysis**  
Units/revenue required to cover costs, timeline to break-even, and margin of safety.

**Cost Concentration Analysis**  
Bullet points identifying:
- Cost-heavy areas threatening margins
- Efficiency opportunities
- Cash flow timing risks

**Sales Sensitivity Scenarios**  
Comparison table showing impact on profitability under:
- Best case (volume +20%)
- Expected case (baseline)
- Worst case (volume -20%)

**Strategic Recommendations**  
Numbered, actionable adjustments with measurable impact on financial outlook.

Use tables for numerical data, bold critical figures, and ensure all projections reflect realistic market constraints and business cycle patterns.
```
