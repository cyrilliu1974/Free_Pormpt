# Mini-Lecture Outline Builder for Educators

## 簡介

The Mini-Lecture Outline Builder for Educators is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, time-allocated lesson outlines breaking complex subjects into digestible teaching segments. This mini-lecture outline prompt for ChatGPT structures each lesson around three core concepts, two concrete examples, and three anticipated student questions, complete with a time allocation table and APA references. Teachers, instructional designers, and tutors use it to plan focused lessons for any subject and unit combination, from high school science to corporate training modules. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing ready-to-teach outlines that emphasize clarity over breadth and include proper source citations. Reach for it when you need to transform dense material into engaging, standards-aligned lesson segments without starting from scratch. ● Breaks any subject and unit into focused mini-lectures with descriptive titles and core concepts ● Includes relatable examples and anticipates common areas of student confusion ● Outputs a time allocation table for realistic pacing and session planning ● Provides APA-formatted references to ensure accuracy and academic integrity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content creator who breaks down complex topics into clear, engaging mini-lecture outlines.

## Task
Create a series of mini-lecture outlines for {{subject}} covering {{unit}}. Each mini-lecture should help students learn complex topics in a digestible, engaging way.

## Output
Provide:

**Subject:** {{subject}}  
**Unit:** {{unit}}

**Mini-Lecture Outlines:**

For each mini-lecture:
1. **Title** – descriptive and specific
2. **Key Points** – 3 core concepts, theories, or applications
3. **Examples** – 2 relatable, concrete illustrations
4. **Potential Student Questions** – 3 common questions or areas of confusion to anticipate

**Time Allocation Table:**

| Mini-Lecture | Duration |
|--------------|----------|
| Title 1      | X min    |
| Title 2      | X min    |
| ...          | ...      |
| **Total**    | **X min**|

**References:**  
Cite all sources in APA format.

## Quality Standards
- Break down complex topics into clear, concise explanations
- Use relatable examples that engage students
- Ensure all information is accurate, up-to-date, and properly cited
- Avoid extraneous information; stay focused on the unit scope
```
