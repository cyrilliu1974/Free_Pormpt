# Brand Identity Generator for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Brand Identity Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete brand identity systems - logo concepts, color palettes, typography pairings, and usage guidelines - for businesses and founders who need cohesive visual branding. This brand identity prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and produces a structured brand system: a logo concept with rationale, a primary and secondary color palette grounded in color psychology, a two-font typography pairing with hierarchy rules, and actionable brand guidelines covering logo usage, color application, imagery style, and brand voice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, outputting text you can share with designers or use as a creative brief. Marketers, solopreneurs, and early-stage startups use it to define their visual identity before committing to design assets, ensuring every element aligns with target audience and brand personality. ● Produces a business overview analyzing niche, target market, personality, and differentiators before suggesting design elements. ● Delivers a logo concept with description, file format, and color-mode specifications ready for a designer. ● Recommends primary, secondary, and accent colors with explicit color psychology rationale tied to your audience. ● Pairs two fonts - primary and secondary - with usage rules and pairing rationale for typographic consistency. ● Outputs brand guidelines covering logo usage, color application, typography hierarchy, imagery style, and brand voice. ## Prompt
