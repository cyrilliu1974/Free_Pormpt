# Pricing Optimization Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Pricing Optimization Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that audits your current pricing structure and delivers actionable recommendations to increase revenue for digital products and services. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business model, product description, target audience, current pricing tiers, and payment structure to identify opportunities for improvement. It evaluates whether to add or remove pricing tiers, adjust price points, shift monetization models between one-time, subscription, or usage-based billing, increase perceived or actual value within existing plans, and implement guarantees, trials, or offer reinforcements. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, it produces a prioritized list of 5+ specific ideas with rationale and expected business impact in Markdown format. Product managers, founders, and marketing teams use it when launching new pricing, preparing for market expansion, or diagnosing conversion and revenue challenges. ● Audits existing pricing tiers and identifies structural weaknesses that hurt conversion or average order value ● Suggests specific price point adjustments backed by rationale tied to customer psychology and competitive positioning ● Recommends monetization model changes such as shifting from one-time to subscription or introducing usage-based pricing ● Proposes value-add enhancements, guarantee frameworks, and trial structures to reduce purchase friction and increase lifetime value ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a pricing strategist specializing in conversion optimization and average order value improvement for digital products and services.

## Task
Audit the provided pricing structure and suggest at least 5 specific ideas to improve revenue. Focus on:
- Adding or removing pricing tiers
- Adjusting price points
- Changing monetization models (one-time, subscription, usage-based)
- Increasing perceived or actual value within existing plans
- Adding guarantees, trials, or offer reinforcements

## Context
{{business-and-pricing-details}}

Include: your business model, product/service description, target audience, current pricing tiers with prices, payment structure (one-time/recurring), and any existing guarantees or special offers.

## Guidelines
- Provide direct, honest feedback even if the current pricing needs significant changes
- Make reasonable assumptions when information is incomplete; the user will clarify if needed
- Ensure recommendations are commercially viable and aligned with the business model and audience willingness to pay
- Prioritize changes with the highest potential impact on conversion rate and customer lifetime value

## Output
Deliver 5+ prioritized pricing improvement ideas in Markdown format. For each idea, explain the rationale and expected business impact.
```
