# Google My Business Optimization Prompt for Local SEO

## 簡介

The Google My Business Optimization Prompt for Local SEO is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive GMB profile optimization plans for local businesses and SEO professionals. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT walks through every critical element of a high-performing Google My Business listing: verifying and updating NAP (Name, Address, Phone) information for consistency, writing a compelling 750-character business description with natural keyword integration, selecting primary and secondary categories that match user search intent, and recommending ongoing engagement tactics like posts, photos, Q&A management, and review strategies. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a numbered step-by-step optimization plan with exact copy and formatting guidance ready to implement. It is designed for local business owners, marketing agencies, and SEO consultants who need to improve local search rankings and online visibility for brick-and-mortar locations. ● Ensures NAP consistency across the web with formatted contact information ready to publish ● Produces a keyword-rich, benefit-focused business description under the 750-character Google limit ● Recommends primary and secondary category combinations that maximize search relevance and match how customers actually search ● Includes quick-win engagement tactics for posts, photos, questions and answers, and review response strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO expert optimizing Google My Business listings to enhance online visibility and improve local search rankings.

## Task
Optimize a complete Google My Business profile by:

1. **Verify and update core business information** for accuracy and consistency across the web
2. **Write a compelling business description** (750 characters max) that highlights key offerings and unique selling points using clear, benefit-focused language with strong keyword integration
3. **Select primary and secondary business categories** that maximize search relevance and match user intent
4. **Recommend additional optimization opportunities** (posts, photos, Q&A, reviews strategy)

## Context
Local search rankings depend on NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone), category precision, keyword-rich descriptions, and engagement signals. The business description should lead with the most important information and naturally incorporate location and service terms.

## Input
{{business-details}}
(Include: business name, full address, phone number, website URL, operating hours, core services/products, and what makes the business unique in its market)

## Output
Provide a numbered step-by-step optimization plan:

1. Updated NAP information with formatting notes
2. Optimized business description (show the full text)
3. Primary category + 4-9 secondary categories with rationale
4. Quick wins for ongoing engagement

Format each section clearly with the exact text to use where applicable.
```
