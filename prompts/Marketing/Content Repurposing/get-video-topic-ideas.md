# Video Topic Ideas Generator for Content Creators

## 簡介

The Video Topic Ideas Generator for Content Creators is a free AI prompt that produces targeted video concepts complete with attention-grabbing titles and compelling descriptions for YouTube creators, social media marketers, and video producers. This video ideation prompt for ChatGPT delivers 10-15 numbered topic ideas that align with your specific audience, niche, and content strategy. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it covers diverse formats including tutorials, vlogs, interviews, how-tos, entertainment pieces, and behind-the-scenes content. Each suggestion includes a catchy title and a one-sentence hook designed to maximize engagement and shareability. Content creators use it to break through creative blocks, plan monthly upload calendars, test new content angles, and identify trending topics their viewers will actually watch. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh video concepts that balance audience interests with your production capabilities and strategic goals. ● Produces 10-15 video ideas per run, each with a catchy title and one-sentence description ● Tailors suggestions to your specified audience, niche, and content strategy ● Covers diverse formats from tutorials and interviews to entertainment and behind-the-scenes ● Balances trending topics with practical production feasibility and shareability potential ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in video content ideation and audience engagement.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive numbered list of 10-15 video topic ideas tailored to the specified audience and niche. Each idea must include:
- A catchy, attention-grabbing title
- A one-sentence compelling description

## Context
Target audience, niche, and strategy:
{{content-strategy}}

Ensure ideas:
- Align with current trends and audience interests
- Cover diverse content types (tutorials, vlogs, interviews, how-tos, entertainment, behind-the-scenes)
- Have strong potential for engagement, shareability, and discoverability
- Are practical to execute within the creator's typical production capabilities

## Output
Deliver as a clean numbered list:

1. **[Title]** – [One-sentence description]
2. **[Title]** – [One-sentence description]

(Continue through all ideas)
```
