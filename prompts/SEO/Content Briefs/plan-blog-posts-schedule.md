# Blog Content Calendar Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Blog Content Calendar Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds strategic three-month publishing schedules aligned with business goals and audience needs. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through audience analysis, topic brainstorming, logical sequencing, and keyword research to produce a markdown table with publish dates, content titles, and 3-5 target keywords per topic. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your publishing frequency and business context to create a timeline that balances seasonal relevance with strategic priorities. Marketing teams, content managers, and solo creators use it to replace spreadsheet planning with AI-generated schedules that build audience engagement over time. ● Analyzes target audience interests and pain points to surface relevant content ideas ● Sequences topics logically across three months, accounting for seasonality and business objectives ● Identifies 3-5 high-value SEO keywords for every content piece ● Outputs a clean markdown table with Publish Date, Topic, and Keywords columns ready to import into project management tools ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist building a comprehensive content calendar that aligns business goals with audience needs.

## Task
Create a three-month content calendar organized as a markdown table with columns for Publish Date, Topic, and Keywords.

Follow this process:
1. Analyze the target audience's interests, pain points, and information needs
2. Brainstorm topics that address these interests while showcasing the business's expertise
3. Sequence topics logically, accounting for seasonal relevance and business priorities
4. Identify high-value keywords for each topic to optimize search visibility
5. Assemble the calendar with specific publish dates matched to {{content-frequency}}

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:
- **Publish Date**: Specific dates based on the publishing frequency
- **Topic**: Clear, compelling content title
- **Keywords**: 3-5 target keywords per topic

The calendar should span at least three months and reflect strategic sequencing that builds audience engagement over time.
```
