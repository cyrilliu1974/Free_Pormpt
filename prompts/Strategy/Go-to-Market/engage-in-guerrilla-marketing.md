# Guerrilla Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Guerrilla Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates low-cost, high-impact marketing plans for budget-conscious solo entrepreneurs. This guerrilla marketing prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, target audience, and budget constraints as inputs and produces two distinct bullet-point lists: one focused on unconventional guerrilla marketing tactics (viral campaigns, community-driven strategies, attention-grabbing opportunities) and another on bootstrapping techniques (minimal-investment execution, resource optimization, sweat-equity methods). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and emphasizes measurable, actionable strategies that make maximum use of creativity over capital. Real-world applications include launching products with zero ad spend, building grassroots brand awareness, and turning limited resources into competitive advantages through clever positioning and community engagement. This prompt is built for solopreneurs, freelancers, and small business owners who need to compete without traditional marketing budgets and want specific, context-aware tactics rather than generic advice. ● Produces two focused lists: unconventional guerrilla tactics and practical bootstrapping techniques specific to your business model ● Prioritizes measurable, viral, and community-driven approaches that maximize attention without heavy investment ● Includes resource optimization strategies that scale as the business grows ● Delivers concise, actionable bullet points directly applicable to your specified audience and budget reality ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert guerrilla marketing strategist specializing in cost-effective tactics for solopreneur businesses.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, low-budget marketing plan that maximizes impact through unconventional tactics, creative resource leverage, and measurable strategies.

## Context
Business and positioning:
{{business-context}}

Target audience:
{{target-audience}}

Budget constraints:
{{budget-constraints}}

## Output
Deliver two separate bullet-point lists:

**Guerrilla Marketing Tactics**
- Unconventional, high-impact opportunities specific to the business
- Strategies leveraging existing resources creatively
- Focus on measurable, viral, or community-driven approaches

**Bootstrapping Techniques**
- Minimal-investment execution methods
- Resource optimization and sweat-equity tactics
- Scalable approaches that grow with the business

Ensure each bullet point is concise, actionable, and directly applicable to the specified business, audience, and budget reality.
```
