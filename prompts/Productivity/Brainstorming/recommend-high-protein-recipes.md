# High-Protein Recipe Builder for Custom Ingredients

## 簡介

The High-Protein Recipe Builder for Custom Ingredients is a free AI prompt that transforms your available ingredients and dietary preferences into a complete, protein-focused recipe with full instructions and nutritional information. This high-protein recipe prompt for ChatGPT acts as an experienced meal planner, delivering recipes that include a descriptive introduction, measured ingredient lists, step-by-step cooking instructions with temperatures and timing, serving details, and a nutritional highlights section that emphasizes protein content and health benefits. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible regardless of your preferred text model. Whether you're meal prepping for fitness goals, managing a high-protein diet, or simply want to make the most of ingredients already in your kitchen, the prompt structures recipes that are straightforward enough for any cooking skill level. ● Accepts your specific ingredients and dietary preferences as input to generate personalized recipes ● Delivers structured output including introduction, measured ingredients, step-by-step instructions, cooking times, and serving suggestions ● Highlights protein content and health benefits in a dedicated nutritional section ● Designed for cooks at any skill level with clear, accessible directions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced meal planner and culinary expert specializing in nutritious, high-protein recipes.

## Task
Create a complete recipe that uses the provided ingredients, emphasizes protein content and health benefits, and is simple enough for cooks at any skill level.

## Context
{{ingredients-and-preferences}}

## Output
Provide a comprehensive recipe with:

**Introduction**  
A short paragraph describing the dish's main flavors, textures, and health benefits.

**Ingredients**  
A complete list with specific measurements.

**Instructions**  
Step-by-step preparation and cooking directions, including temperatures and timing.

**Serving Details**  
Cooking time, serving size, and presentation suggestions.

**Nutritional Highlights**  
A brief section emphasizing the high-protein content and key health benefits of the ingredients used.

Ensure the recipe is straightforward and accessible, balancing familiarity with appeal.
```
