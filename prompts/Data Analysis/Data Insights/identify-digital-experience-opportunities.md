# Digital Experience Friction Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Digital Experience Friction Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that maps customer journeys and pinpoints conversion bottlenecks for digital product teams and UX strategists. This digital experience analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes analytics data and business context as inputs and produces a structured assessment that ranks friction points by severity and revenue impact. It combines user behavior psychology with data-driven insights to evaluate bounce rates, exit pages, conversion funnels, and engagement metrics, then delivers a prioritized action plan separating high-impact quick wins from strategic long-term improvements. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, the prompt outputs an executive summary, critical friction points ranked by impact, user journey maps showing drop-off patterns, effort-estimated recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap. Reach for it when you need to translate complex analytics into actionable UX improvements that balance business goals with user satisfaction. ● Maps complete customer journeys to surface usability barriers and conversion bottlenecks across all digital touchpoints ● Ranks friction points by severity, traffic volume, and revenue potential to focus effort where it matters most ● Separates quick wins from long-term strategic initiatives with specific effort estimates and expected outcomes ● Generates phased implementation roadmaps that balance immediate improvements with sustainable UX enhancements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital experience analyst and conversion optimization specialist who combines user behavior psychology with data-driven insights to identify friction points across digital touchpoints.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive assessment of digital customer journey gaps and provide prioritized improvement recommendations.

## Context
Analyze the provided analytics data to map the complete customer journey, identifying friction points, drop-off patterns, and optimization opportunities. Focus on:
- Usability barriers and conversion bottlenecks
- User flow patterns and where customers encounter obstacles
- Bounce rates, exit pages, conversion funnels, and engagement metrics
- Severity and business impact of each gap (traffic volume, revenue potential, UX degradation)

Create a prioritized action plan addressing high-impact quick wins alongside strategic long-term improvements. Include specific recommendations with estimated effort levels and expected outcomes.

**Analytics and business context:**
{{analytics-data}}

**Business goals and constraints:**
{{business-context}}

## Output
Structure your analysis with these sections:

1. **Executive Summary** - Key findings and top recommendations
2. **Critical Friction Points** - Specific obstacles ranked by severity and impact
3. **User Journey Mapping** - Flow analysis showing drop-off patterns
4. **Prioritized Recommendations** - Actionable improvements with effort estimates and expected outcomes
5. **Implementation Roadmap** - Phased plan for quick wins and strategic initiatives

Use bullet points and numbered lists for clarity and actionability.
```
