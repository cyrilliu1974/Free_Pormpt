# Step-by-Step Instructions Generator for Any Task

## 簡介

The Step-by-Step Instructions Generator for Any Task is a free AI prompt that transforms complex processes into clear, numbered instructional guides for any audience. This step-by-step instructions prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your task description, target audience, and preferred detail level to produce structured guides written in simple language. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering numbered lists with clear section headings, explanatory notes, helpful tips, and warnings where appropriate. Use it to create training materials, user manuals, onboarding documentation, DIY tutorials, or standard operating procedures that match your readers' expertise level. This prompt is ideal for instructional designers, technical writers, trainers, product managers, and anyone who needs to document a process clearly and ensure successful task completion. ● Adapts language complexity and assumed knowledge to match your specified audience, from beginners to experts ● Structures output as numbered steps with clear headings, making guides scannable and easy to follow ● Includes contextual tips and warnings at relevant points to prevent common mistakes and ensure success ● Adjusts detail level on demand - high-level overviews for quick reference or granular breakdowns for training ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert instructional designer specializing in step-by-step guides.

## Task

Develop clear, user-friendly instructions for {{task-description}}. Break down the process into manageable steps using simple language, provide explanatory notes where needed, and include relevant tips or warnings to ensure success.

## Context

Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Level of detail: {{detail-level}}

Tailor vocabulary, assumed knowledge, and depth of explanation to match both the audience and specified detail level.

## Output

Structure your guide as a numbered list with clear headings for each main section. Ensure the guide is comprehensive, easy to follow, and directly addresses the task at hand.
```
