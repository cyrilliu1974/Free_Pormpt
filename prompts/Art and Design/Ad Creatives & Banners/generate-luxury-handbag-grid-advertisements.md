# Luxury Handbag Grid Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Handbag Grid Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that produces elegant 3×2 panel advertisements for high-end leather handbags. This luxury handbag grid advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates six coordinated panels that combine studio product photography with lifestyle imagery: a hero shot highlighting the bag's silhouette and leather grain, macro texture details showing stitching precision, hardware close-ups capturing metal finishes, an everyday café or workspace scene, an interior view revealing compartments and lining, and an evening setting in an upscale restaurant or lounge. Each panel uses professional lighting - soft studio light for product shots, natural daylight for lifestyle contexts - and maintains a cohesive color palette you specify. Fashion brands, e-commerce teams, and marketing designers reach for this prompt when they need editorial-quality handbag advertising without arranging expensive photo shoots. ● Produces hero product shots with soft studio lighting that emphasize leather texture, stitching, and logo embossing. ● Includes macro detail panels capturing leather grain, stitching precision, and metal hardware finishes with controlled depth of field. ● Generates lifestyle scenes in modern café workspaces and upscale evening venues that communicate everyday elegance and timeless sophistication. ● Reveals interior functionality and organization through an open-bag panel showing compartments and lining quality. ## Prompt

```
Create an elegant 3×2 grid advertisement for luxury leather handbag, highlighting craftsmanship, material quality, and sophisticated everyday style.

**Canvas**: 3:2 landscape aspect ratio, 6 equal panels arranged in 3 columns × 2 rows.

**Panel 1 – Hero Bag Design**: Studio hero shot of the handbag positioned upright. Soft studio lighting with gentle highlights on a {{color-palette}} leather backdrop. Emphasize bag silhouette, leather grain, and logo embossing. Mood: luxury, craftsmanship, confidence.

**Panel 2 – Leather Texture Detail**: Macro close-up of handbag leather and stitching. Ultra-shallow depth of field capturing natural leather grain and stitching precision. Tactile clarity. Mood: quality, detail, artisan-made.

**Panel 3 – Hardware & Finish**: Close-up of metal hardware, zipper, or clasp. Controlled highlights on metal surfaces. Mood: refinement, durability.

**Panel 4 – Everyday Elegance**: Handbag on minimalist café table beside coffee and laptop, or draped over modern chair in bright workspace. Natural daylight. Mood: effortless style, modern living.

**Panel 5 – Interior Functionality**: Open handbag revealing interior compartments, organization, and lining texture. Mood: practical luxury, functionality.

**Panel 6 – Evening Sophistication**: Handbag in upscale restaurant or lounge setting, resting on velvet banquette or marble surface. Warm cinematic lighting. Mood: timeless, sophisticated, iconic.

**Visual Style**: Luxury fashion advertising, editorial photography, high realism, minimalist styling, timeless elegance. Professional fashion and product photography mixing soft studio lighting with natural daylight. Balanced exposure with warm highlights and soft sculpting shadows. {{color-palette}} color palette throughout. Minimal embossed or metallic logo presentation.

**Avoid**: Cheap faux leather look, uneven stitching, overly glossy materials, cluttered fashion scenes, unreadable branding, harsh lighting, CGI or cartoon style, oversaturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
