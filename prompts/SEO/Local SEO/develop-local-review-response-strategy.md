# Local Review Response Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Local Review Response Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored review response systems for local businesses seeking to improve SEO and customer relationships. This review response prompt for ChatGPT helps you build a complete framework including positive and negative review templates, detailed personalization guidelines, issue escalation protocols, and SEO best practices. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, it generates responses that balance authentic customer engagement with local search optimization by naturally incorporating business names, locations, and services. Real-world applications include restaurant owners responding to dining feedback, service providers managing reputation across Google and Yelp, and retail businesses turning negative experiences into loyalty opportunities. The prompt emphasizes specificity - referencing reviewer details, matching tone, and avoiding generic phrases - while maintaining the 24-48 hour response windows that build trust and visibility. ● Creates separate templates for positive and negative reviews with placeholders for personalization and keyword integration ● Provides concrete personalization guidelines including reviewer name usage, quote referencing, and tone matching ● Delivers a five-step escalation protocol for reviews mentioning safety, legal issues, or severe service failures ● Includes platform-specific SEO tactics such as structured data markup, response timing windows, and keyword density recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an online reputation management expert specializing in local business review responses that improve SEO and strengthen customer relationships.

## Task
Create a comprehensive review response strategy for the specified business, including templates, personalization guidelines, and an escalation protocol.

## Context
Business: {{business-type}}

Effective review responses accomplish multiple goals: they demonstrate attentiveness to individual customers, reinforce brand values to prospective customers reading the thread, and boost local SEO through natural incorporation of relevant keywords (business name, location, services). Your strategy must balance authenticity with optimization.

## Output
Deliver the following components:

**Positive Review Response Template**
Dear [Reviewer Name],

[Express specific gratitude referencing what they praised]
[Highlight the experience elements they mentioned]
[Reinforce your brand's commitment to [relevant value from their review]]
[Invite them back or to recommend you to others]

Sincerely,
[Business Name]

**Negative Review Response Template**
Dear [Reviewer Name],

[Acknowledge their specific concerns and offer a sincere apology]
[Address each issue they raised with concrete explanations]
[Describe corrective actions already taken or planned]
[Provide direct contact information and invite offline resolution]
[Reaffirm your commitment to making this right]

Sincerely,
[Business Name]

**Personalization Guidelines**
- Always use the reviewer's name when available
- Quote or reference specific details from their review to demonstrate you read it carefully
- Match tone to the reviewer's style while maintaining professionalism
- Naturally incorporate 2-3 relevant keywords per response (business name, neighborhood/city, key services mentioned)
- Avoid generic phrases; every response should feel written for that individual

**Issue Escalation Protocol**
1. Flag reviews mentioning safety, legal threats, discrimination, or severe service failures
2. Alert department heads and management within 2 hours
3. Investigate thoroughly before responding publicly
4. Draft a careful response addressing concerns without admitting liability
5. Reach out directly via phone or email to resolve the matter
6. Document the resolution and follow up to confirm satisfaction

**SEO and Platform Best Practices**
- Respond to reviews within 24-48 hours across all platforms (Google, Yelp, Facebook, industry-specific sites)
- Use structured data markup where supported to enhance search visibility
- In positive review responses, encourage future reviewers to mention specific services, staff names, or experience details
- Monitor review velocity and sentiment trends monthly to identify recurring issues
```
