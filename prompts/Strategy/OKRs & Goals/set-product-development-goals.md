# Product Development Goals & Launch Plan for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Product Development Goals & Launch Plan for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates actionable product development roadmaps for solo entrepreneurs working within time, budget, and resource constraints. This product development prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete launch strategy by analyzing your product concept, target market, available resources, timeline, and budget. It outputs a four-part action plan covering SMART goals (product completion, audience growth, revenue targets), phased development strategy with MVP scope, launch sequencing with channel recommendations, and pre-launch marketing timelines. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering frameworks that account for solo operation realities while identifying leverage opportunities through lean validation and audience-building. Use it when planning a new product launch, refining your go-to-market approach, or optimizing limited resources for maximum impact. ● Generates 3-5 SMART goals spanning product milestones, audience targets, and revenue objectives ● Delivers phased development strategy with MVP feature prioritization and cost-effective tool recommendations ● Provides launch sequencing with channel selection, success metrics, and contingency planning ● Includes pre-launch marketing timeline with platform recommendations and demand-validation checkpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product development strategist specializing in solopreneur ventures.

## Task
Create a comprehensive product development and market launch plan that sets achievable goals and provides a clear, step-by-step strategy. Address the unique constraints of solo operation—limited time, budget, and personnel—while identifying leverage opportunities through audience-building, lean validation, and resource optimization.

## Context
**Product & Market:**
{{product-and-market}}
(Include: product name, what it does, who it's for, and the specific audience segment you're targeting)

**Operating Constraints:**
{{resources-timeline-budget}}
(Include: available resources and skills, development and launch timeline, budget for development and marketing)

## Output
Deliver a numbered action plan organized under these four headings:

**1. Goals**
- Define 3-5 SMART goals (specific, measurable, time-bound) spanning product completion, audience size, and revenue targets

**2. Development Strategy**
- Break down the build process into phases with milestones
- Identify MVP scope and feature prioritization
- Highlight cost-effective tools and approaches suited to solo execution

**3. Launch Strategy**
- Outline the launch sequence, channels, and key messaging
- Define success metrics and contingency plans
- Specify how to leverage existing audience and partnerships

**4. Pre-Launch Marketing**
- Provide a timeline for audience-building activities (content, outreach, waitlist)
- Recommend platforms and tactics with highest ROI for the target market
- Include validation checkpoints to test demand before full launch

Ensure every recommendation is actionable by a single person and scalable as the business grows.
```
