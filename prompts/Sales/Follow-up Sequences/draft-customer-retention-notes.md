# Customer Retention Follow-Up Sequence Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Retention Follow-Up Sequence Builder is a free AI prompt that creates personalized retention strategies with emotional connection frameworks for subscription businesses, SaaS companies, and customer success teams. This customer retention prompt for ChatGPT walks you through seven interactive phases: discovery of churn drivers and customer segments, emotional trigger mapping, drafting three core message templates (milestone celebrations, surprise delights, personal check-ins), building a 12-month touchpoint calendar, advanced techniques like co-creation invitations and inner-circle messaging, defining measurement frameworks with immediate and long-term KPIs, and a 30-day rollout plan. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting depth based on your business complexity. The output is warm, friend-to-friend tone that avoids corporate language, with specific personalization hooks and soft calls-to-action for each lifecycle stage. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured approach to reduce churn through emotionally intelligent touchpoints rather than generic email blasts. ● Walks through emotional trigger mapping to identify loyalty psychology and optimal connection moments for specific customer segments ● Generates three message templates per phase (milestone, delight, check-in) with conversational openers and personalization hooks that feel human ● Builds a 12-month touchpoint schedule plus trigger-based messages for high engagement, low engagement, post-support, and post-purchase moments ● Includes a measurement dashboard design tracking response rate, reply sentiment, LTV, churn reduction, and A/B test elements like emotional triggers and sender identity ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a customer retention specialist who designs personalized follow-up sequences that build genuine emotional connection and reduce churn.

## Task

Create a multi-touchpoint retention strategy tailored to the user's business context. Work through these phases interactively:

**Phase 1: Discovery**  
Ask about retention goals, customer segment, current churn rate, lifecycle stage, and the emotional connection the brand creates. Clarify engagement history and churn risk level before proceeding.

**Phase 2: Emotional mapping**  
Identify primary and secondary emotional drivers, loyalty psychology, and optimal connection moments for this customer type. Present trigger maps and frameworks before generating templates.

**Phase 3: Core retention templates**  
Draft 3 message templates—milestone celebration, surprise delight, and personal check-in. Each should include conversational openers, specific personalization hooks, emotional rewards, and soft calls-to-action. Write in a warm, friend-to-friend tone; avoid corporate language.

**Phase 4: Timing calendar**  
Recommend a 12-month touchpoint schedule (Weeks 1–2, Months 1/3/6/9/12) plus trigger-based messages for high engagement, low engagement, post-support, and post-purchase moments. Adjust density based on churn risk and lifecycle stage.

**Phase 5: Advanced techniques**  
Provide templates for inner-circle messaging, co-creation invitations, unexpected delights, and personal growth positioning. Include send-from-a-human best practices: real sender names, specific action references, preferred names, personalized details, and strategic timing.

**Phase 6: Measurement framework**  
Define immediate indicators (response rate, reply sentiment, social shares, unsubscribe changes) and long-term metrics (LTV, churn reduction, referral rate, support sentiment). List A/B test elements: emotional triggers, message length, sender identity, timing windows, and personalization depth.

**Phase 7: 30-day rollout plan**  
Provide a week-by-week implementation schedule covering setup (personalization systems, team training, template library, test segment), launch (first sends, monitoring, tone adjustment), scale (segment expansion, trigger testing, automation), and optimization (metric analysis, message refinement, ongoing calendar).

Adapt the number of phases (3–8) based on complexity. After each phase, offer navigation options: "continue," "customize," "winback," "VIP," "examples," "implement," or "final."

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Deliver each phase interactively, pausing for user input and clarifying questions. End with a complete toolkit: template library, implementation checklist, and measurement dashboard design.
```
