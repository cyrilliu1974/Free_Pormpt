# Photorealistic Yoga Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Yoga Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality fitness portraits with precise pose control, identity continuity, and hyperrealistic body art for wellness brands and content creators. This yoga portrait prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces vertical 9:16 medium-full shots of subjects in exact yoga poses - like seated Heron pose - while matching reference facial features, hairstyles, and skin tone. It renders 3D optical-illusion tattoos with embedded ink texture, believable depth shading, and cast shadows that align with natural window light, all within styled home yoga environments featuring coordinated wall art, decorative accents, and soft tile floors. Use it to build consistent brand imagery across multiple poses, generate authentic fitness marketing visuals without photoshoots, or produce scroll-stopping wellness content that maintains the same subject identity. ● Matches specific facial features, hairstyles, makeup, and skin texture while controlling exact body positioning through detailed pose instruction. ● Renders 3D optical-illusion tattoos with crisp micro-texture, depth shading, and natural cast shadows that respond to scene lighting. ● Customizable room styles and decorative elements that coordinate with the portrait aesthetic - from minimalist to bohemian yoga spaces. ● Simulates full-frame 50mm f/2.0 photography with soft natural window light, subtle vignetting, eye-level framing, and fine sensor grain in shadows. ## Prompt

```
Photoreal editorial fitness portrait, 9:16 vertical, medium-full shot. Adult woman in seated Heron pose (Krounchasana): left leg folded on floor, right leg extended vertically upward, both hands holding raised foot, hips grounded, spine tall, shoulders relaxed. Match reference identity: dark brown messy updo with wispy curtain bangs, natural makeup, calm focused expression, realistic skin pores and peachy tone. Athletic tank top and fitted yoga shorts. Hyper-realistic 3D optical-illusion {{body-art-design}} tattoo coiling around raised right shin and calf—ink embedded in skin with crisp micro-texture, believable depth shading, soft cast shadows aligned to window light. Bare legs and feet with natural toe spacing and stretch tension.

Scene: {{room-style}} home yoga space. Style-appropriate wall art or hanging on back wall, framed yoga posture illustration leaning against wall at floor level, matching decorative accents, sheer white curtains filtering daylight. Light glossy tile floor with subtle reflections, circular yoga mat centered under subject. Warm neutral palette, uncluttered and cozy.

Soft natural window light from camera-left, diffuse shadows, gentle wrap on skin, subtle highlight on tattooed leg, clean HDR roll-off. Full-frame 50mm f/2.0 look, eye-level, centered with slight headroom, background softly blurred. Sharp focus on face and raised leg. Fine sensor grain in shadows, mild vignette, tiny chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges.

Avoid: underage, cgi render, cartoon, anime, plastic airbrushed skin, bad anatomy, extra/deformed fingers, warped feet, missing toes, pose mismatch, wrong leg angle, flat sticker tattoo, blurred tattoo, text, logo, watermark, neon cyberpunk, oversaturated harsh flash, messy cluttered room, unrealistic proportions.
```
