# Employee Offboarding Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Offboarding Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that creates systematic offboarding processes for HR professionals and organizational leaders managing workforce transitions. This employee offboarding prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete 10-step framework tailored to your organization's context and challenges. It covers the full departure lifecycle from resignation through post-departure follow-up, addressing knowledge transfer, access management, legal documentation, relationship preservation, and operational continuity. HR teams use it to reduce knowledge loss during departures, maintain employer brand reputation, and ensure compliance while treating exiting employees with respect. Reach for this prompt when you need a ready-to-implement offboarding process that balances tactical requirements with the human side of transitions. ● Produces 10 sequenced offboarding steps from pre-departure planning through post-exit follow-up, each with clear ownership and deadlines. ● Includes specific key actions for knowledge documentation, security protocols, compliance tasks, and relationship management at each stage. ● Defines success metrics so you can track completion and measure the effectiveness of your offboarding process. ● Adapts to your organizational context and current challenges through two input variables that shape the framework to your environment. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR strategist and organizational psychologist specializing in employee lifecycle management and workforce transitions.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 10-step offboarding framework that preserves organizational knowledge, maintains positive relationships, and protects company interests while treating departing employees with dignity and respect.

## Context
Effective offboarding prevents employer brand damage, legal risks, and costly knowledge loss. Each step must address both tactical requirements and human elements to ensure smooth transitions for all stakeholders.

Consider:
- Legal compliance and documentation requirements
- Knowledge transfer protocols
- Access and security management
- Relationship preservation strategies
- Operational continuity
- Pre-departure planning and post-departure follow-up

Organizational context: {{organizational-context}}

Current offboarding challenges: {{offboarding-challenges}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with 10 distinct steps. For each step, provide:

**[Step Number]. [Clear Step Title]**
- **Timeline:** When this step occurs in the offboarding process
- **Responsible parties:** Who owns execution
- **Key actions:** Specific tasks to complete (3-5 bullet points)
- **Success metrics:** How to measure effectiveness

Ensure steps are actionable, implementable immediately, and sequenced logically from resignation through post-departure.
```
