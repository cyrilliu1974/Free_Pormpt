# Motion Blur Portrait Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Motion Blur Portrait Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates fashion editorial-style portraits with motion blur effects and surreal lighting for photographers, designers, and content creators. This motion blur portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images where light streaks sweep across the subject's face, blending dynamic movement with soft, dreamy atmospheres and subtle psychedelic glows in the background. You control the subject, wardrobe, and color palette while the prompt handles the composition, contrast, and editorial styling. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models, delivering 2:3 aspect ratio photographs that resemble high-end fashion magazine spreads. Ideal for creating standout profile pictures, conceptual photography projects, or social media visuals that need an artistic, surreal edge. ● Produces editorial-style portraits with motion blur and light streaks layered across the face for a kinetic, artistic effect. ● Customizable subject, wardrobe, and color palette variables let you adapt the aesthetic to match your creative vision. ● Generates medium-contrast compositions with blurred backgrounds and soft psychedelic glows for a dreamy, surreal atmosphere. ● Outputs images in 2:3 aspect ratio, optimized for fashion magazine layouts, social media posts, and portfolio showcases. ## Prompt
