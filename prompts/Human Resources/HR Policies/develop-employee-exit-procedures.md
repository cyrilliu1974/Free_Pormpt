# Employee Exit Clearance Procedures Framework

## 簡介

The Employee Exit Clearance Procedures Framework is a free AI prompt that builds complete offboarding checklists and processes for HR teams managing employee departures across voluntary resignations, terminations, retirements, and emergency exits. This employee exit procedures prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a multi-section framework covering asset return, system access revocation, final settlement calculations, exit interviews, knowledge transfer, stakeholder communication, risk assessment, and audit trails. Each checklist item specifies the responsible party, timeline milestones (Day 1, final day, post-departure), and compliance verification methods. HR teams use it to ensure legal compliance, protect company assets, maintain business continuity, and preserve professional relationships during transitions, whether managing standard offboarding or high-risk departures involving sensitive roles. This prompt is designed for HR operations specialists, people operations managers, and compliance officers who need structured, adaptable exit procedures aligned with SHRM offboarding standards and CIPD exit management best practices. ● Structured checklists for physical and digital asset return, network access revocation, and confidential material recovery. ● Final settlement calculators covering salary, accrued leave, benefits, and expense reimbursements with employment law compliance. ● Exit interview templates with structured questions, feedback capture, and legal safeguards. ● Scenario-specific procedures for voluntary resignations, terminations, retirements, and high-access employee departures. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR operations specialist with expertise in employee offboarding, regulatory compliance, and organizational risk management.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive exit clearance framework that ensures legal compliance, protects organizational assets, maintains business continuity, and preserves professional relationships during employee departures. The framework must adapt to different departure scenarios (voluntary resignation, termination, retirement, emergency exit) and accommodate varying employee access levels and responsibilities.

## Context

{{organization-context}}

## Output

Provide a structured exit clearance framework organized into clear sections with detailed checklists. For each checklist item, specify:

- Responsible party or role
- Timeline (e.g., "Day 1," "Within 48 hours," "Final day," "Post-departure")
- Compliance verification method

Cover these core components:

1. **Asset Return Protocols** – physical equipment, digital resources, company property, confidential materials
2. **Access Revocation Procedures** – system credentials, building access, email, network permissions, third-party tools
3. **Final Settlement Calculations** – salary, accrued leave, benefits, expense reimbursements, compliance with employment law
4. **Exit Interview Process** – structured questions, feedback capture, legal safeguards
5. **Knowledge Transfer & Handover** – documentation requirements, transition meetings, project status updates
6. **Stakeholder Communication** – internal notifications, client transitions, external partner updates
7. **Risk Assessment** – tailored procedures for high-access employees, sensitive roles, or contentious departures
8. **Quality Assurance & Audit Trail** – verification checkpoints, documentation storage, compliance reporting

Ensure the framework aligns with SHRM offboarding standards and CIPD exit management best practices. Make procedures actionable and adaptable to the organization's specific context.
```
