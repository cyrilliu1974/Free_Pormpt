# TikTok Video Script Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The TikTok Video Script Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates short-form video scripts designed to stop the scroll and drive conversions for brands and creators. This TikTok script prompt for ChatGPT produces time-stamped video scripts that open with attention-grabbing hooks in the first 1-2 seconds, introduce products clearly within five seconds, and close with specific calls-to-action - all within TikTok's 60-second constraint. It outputs visual cues paired with spoken or on-screen text, plus recommendations for trending sounds and hashtags. Marketing teams use it to plan product launches, content creators rely on it to structure sponsored content, and small businesses turn to it when they need platform-optimized scripts without hiring a social media agency. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Produces scripts segmented into precise timing blocks: hook (0-3s), intro (4-15s), key points (16-45s), and call-to-action (46-60s) ● Pairs visual cues with spoken or on-screen text for every segment so creators know exactly what to film ● Suggests trending sounds, music, and relevant hashtags to boost discoverability and engagement ● Adapts tone and messaging based on your specified audience and brand voice - casual, professional, humorous, or inspirational ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert TikTok video script writer specializing in short-form content that drives engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write a compelling TikTok video script that promotes the product or service and increases brand awareness. The script must be optimized for TikTok's platform constraints and audience behavior.

## Context
**Product/Service & Positioning:**
{{product-and-positioning}}
(Include what you're promoting, key benefits or unique selling points, and the call-to-action you want viewers to take)

**Audience & Brand Voice:**
{{audience-and-voice}}
(Describe who you're speaking to and the tone/personality your brand uses—casual, professional, humorous, inspirational, etc.)

## Requirements
- Open with a hook in the first 1-2 seconds that stops the scroll
- Introduce the product/service clearly within the first 5 seconds
- Structure sentences for maximum clarity and impact
- Suggest relevant trending sounds, hashtags, or challenges where appropriate
- Close with the specified call-to-action that drives engagement or conversion
- Keep total runtime under 60 seconds

## Output
Deliver the script in this format:

**[0-3s] HOOK**
(Visual cues) | (Spoken/on-screen text)

**[4-15s] INTRO & PROBLEM/OPPORTUNITY**
(Visual cues) | (Spoken/on-screen text)

**[16-45s] KEY POINTS & DEMONSTRATION**
(Visual cues) | (Spoken/on-screen text)

**[46-60s] CALL-TO-ACTION**
(Visual cues) | (Spoken/on-screen text)

**AUDIO:** (Suggested sound/music)
**HASHTAGS:** (3-5 recommended tags)
```
