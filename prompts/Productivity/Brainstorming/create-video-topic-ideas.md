# Video Topic Ideas Generator for Content Creators

## 簡介

The Video Topic Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic, audience-focused video concepts for content creators, educators, and marketers looking to build engaging content calendars. This video topic ideas prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your main subject and target audience context to produce 10+ distinct video concepts spanning beginner to advanced levels. Each suggestion includes a clear title and a rationale explaining its relevance, audience appeal, and unique angle. The prompt considers current trends, content gaps, and audience psychology to deliver topics ranging from foundational overviews and practical tutorials to deep dives and trend analyses. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text AI platforms. Reach for this prompt when planning a YouTube channel, course curriculum, social media series, or any video content calendar where you need strategic topics that stand out and drive viewer engagement. ● Produces 10+ video topics with explanations of why each concept will resonate with your specific audience ● Covers multiple content formats including tutorials, trend analyses, case studies, and thought leadership pieces ● Identifies unique angles and underexplored aspects that help your videos stand out in crowded content spaces ● Suggests multimedia enhancement opportunities like infographics, interviews, and demonstrations for each topic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brainstorming facilitator specializing in video content strategy. You understand audience psychology, content trends, and how to identify topics with high engagement potential.

## Task
Generate a diverse list of at least 10 video topics centered around {{main-topic}}. Each topic should:
- Address specific interests, questions, or pain points within {{audience-context}}
- Offer a unique angle or fresh perspective that differentiates it from existing content
- Cover a range of approaches: foundational overviews, deep dives, practical tutorials, trend analyses, and forward-looking perspectives
- Have clear potential for multimedia enhancement (infographics, interviews, demonstrations, case studies)

## Process
1. Consider current conversations, trending questions, and gaps in existing content around the main topic
2. Identify what makes this subject valuable to the specific audience—their goals, challenges, and curiosity drivers
3. Develop topics that span beginner to advanced levels and theory to application
4. For each topic, find the distinctive hook—the uncommon angle, underexplored aspect, or connection to broader themes that will make viewers stop and watch

## Output
Present each video topic as:
**[Topic Title]**  
*Why it works:* [2-3 sentence explanation of relevance, audience appeal, and what makes this angle compelling]

Ensure the full list provides variety in scope, depth, and approach so the content calendar appeals to different viewer needs and viewing contexts.
```
