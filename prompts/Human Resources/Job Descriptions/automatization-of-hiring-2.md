# Automated Hiring Workflow for Marketing Roles

## 簡介

The Automated Hiring Workflow for Marketing Roles is a free AI prompt that designs end-to-end recruitment processes for HR teams hiring marketing talent. This hiring automation prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown table organizing eight hiring stages - from initial job posting through onboarding - with three parallel tracks: job requirements, screening criteria, and communication templates. You provide company context (size, marketing focus like B2B SaaS or consumer brand, timeline, budget) and your preferred communication style (formal or conversational, email or video), and the prompt tailors every stage to your marketing department's specific needs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, helping HR specialists create reusable workflows that balance technical marketing skills (SEO, analytics, content strategy) with soft skills like creativity and collaboration. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable, efficient hiring process for marketing roles that maintains candidate quality while saving time on job descriptions, screening rubrics, and outreach messages. ● Produces a complete eight-stage workflow table covering posting, acknowledgment, screening, assessment, interviews, follow-up, selection, and onboarding ● Tailors job requirements and screening criteria to marketing-specific competencies like growth marketing, analytics, and content creation ● Generates professional communication templates adapted to your tone and channel preferences ● Accounts for real-world constraints including hiring timelines, budget limits, and department size ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources specialist designing automated hiring workflows for marketing departments.

## Task
Create a comprehensive hiring workflow that streamlines job posting, resume screening, and candidate communication. Organize the workflow as a stage-by-stage table covering the complete process from initial posting through final selection.

## Context
Company and department details:
{{company-and-department-context}}

**Include:** company size, marketing department focus (e.g., B2B SaaS, consumer brand, growth marketing), hiring timeline, and budget constraints for the hiring process.

Communication approach: {{communication-style}}

**Specify:** preferred tone (formal/conversational/friendly), channel preferences (email/SMS/video), and any branding guidelines for candidate outreach.

## Requirements
- Tailor job requirements and screening criteria specifically to marketing roles
- Optimize each stage for efficiency without sacrificing candidate quality
- Design reusable communication templates that are professional yet personalized
- Ensure the workflow is practical given the stated budget and timeline
- Address both technical marketing skills (SEO, analytics, content) and soft skills (collaboration, creativity)

## Output
Deliver your workflow as a **markdown table with three columns:**

| Job Requirements | Screening Criteria | Communication Templates |
|-----------------|-------------------|-------------------------|

**Each row represents one hiring stage:**
- Initial job posting
- Application acknowledgment
- Resume screening
- First-round assessment
- Interview invitation
- Post-interview follow-up
- Final candidate selection
- Offer and onboarding

For each cell, provide specific, actionable content tailored to the company context and communication style provided.
```
