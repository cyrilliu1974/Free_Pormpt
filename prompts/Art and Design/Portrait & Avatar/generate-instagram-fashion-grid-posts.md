# Instagram Fashion Grid Generator for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Instagram Fashion Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cohesive 2x2 fashion photo grids for social media, styled with K-beauty influencer aesthetics and authentic smartphone camera realism. This fashion grid prompt for Midjourney produces a single 1:1 square image divided into four equal frames, each showing the same outfit styled across different poses and settings: a full-body café shot, a mirror selfie, a close-up portrait with soft bokeh, and a candid seated laugh. You specify the outfit ensemble and fashion style, and the prompt handles lighting, composition, color grading, and influencer-quality realism. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering the premium smartphone aesthetic (iPhone-style wide lens, dewy skin, soft pastel tones) that performs well on Instagram and TikTok. Reach for this prompt when you need feed-ready fashion content, influencer portfolio mockups, or brand lookbook grids that feel authentic rather than overly polished. ● Produces four distinct poses in a single cohesive grid: standing, mirror selfie, close-up portrait, and candid seated shot ● K-beauty soft glam makeup, dewy glass skin, and natural smartphone lighting across all frames ● Maintains outfit consistency and color-graded cohesion between frames for a polished feed aesthetic ● Includes subtle white borders and flattering composition that mirrors real influencer photography ## Prompt

```
2×2 Instagram fashion grid, single square image, 1:1 aspect ratio. Korean female fashion influencer, adult 21+, tall slim elegant hourglass figure, extremely beautiful with symmetrical features, radiant smooth skin, long silky dark brown hair softly styled, K-beauty soft glam makeup with dewy glass skin, subtle blush, gradient pink lips.

{{outfit-ensemble}} styled as {{fashion-style}}, same outfit across all four frames, emphasizing waistline and flattering silhouette, cute chic youthful Instagram-viral aesthetic.

Four equal square frames arranged 2×2:
• Top left: full-body standing pose against aesthetic café wall, soft cute smile
• Top right: mirror selfie pose indoors, playful wink or subtle pout
• Bottom left: close-up portrait with soft portrait mode blur, chic confident gaze, emphasizing dewy makeup
• Bottom right: seated pose on stairs, candid laugh looking away

Premium smartphone camera aesthetic (iPhone-style, ~26mm wide lens), natural daylight or soft café lighting, soft warm pastel color grading, slightly brightened exposure, cohesive tone across all frames. High-quality authentic influencer photo with ultra-realistic skin texture and fabric details. Subtle white borders between frames, clean trendy feed appearance.

Avoid: blurry image, over-smoothed skin, distorted anatomy, inconsistent outfit between frames, harsh flash lighting, low resolution.
```
