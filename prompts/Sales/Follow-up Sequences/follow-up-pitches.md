# Follow-Up Pitch Generator for Sales and Marketing

## 簡介

The Follow-Up Pitch Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, conversion-focused follow-up messages for sales and marketing professionals. This follow-up pitch prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar principles to craft messages where every sentence element connects logically, ensuring clarity and persuasive impact. It structures your pitch across five sections: an opening that references previous interactions, a problem statement that articulates your audience's pain points, a solution presentation tied to your unique selling proposition, proof using company track records and social proof, and a clear call-to-action with urgency. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, turning your product details, audience insights, and previous touchpoints into cohesive sales messages. Reach for this prompt when you need to re-engage leads who haven't converted yet, transforming cold follow-ups into structured pitches that demonstrate understanding and build credibility. ● References previous interactions to reestablish rapport and context ● Maps product features directly to audience pain points and benefits ● Incorporates social proof and company achievements to build trust ● Creates urgency with clear, specific next-step calls-to-action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing strategist crafting a follow-up pitch that converts leads into customers.

## Task
Write a structured follow-up pitch using dependency grammar principles—building sentences where each element clearly connects to and depends on others—to create clarity, coherence, and persuasive impact.

## Context
You are working with:

**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience & Pain Points:** {{audience-and-pain-points}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Previous Interaction:** {{previous-interaction}}

**Company Track Record:** {{company-track-record}}

Analyze how the product/service features address the audience's specific pain points. Incorporate urgency, social proof from the company track record, and a clear value proposition.

## Output
Deliver the pitch in this structure:

**Opening**
- Reference the previous interaction
- Reestablish rapport

**Problem Statement**
- Articulate the target audience's key pain points
- Demonstrate understanding of their situation

**Solution**
- Present the product/service as the answer
- Highlight the unique selling proposition
- Connect features directly to benefits that solve their problems

**Proof**
- Cite company track record and relevant achievements
- Use social proof to build credibility

**Call-to-Action**
- Create urgency
- Provide a clear, specific next step

Use bullet points for key details within each section. Apply dependency grammar throughout: ensure each clause and sentence builds logically on the previous one, with clear relationships between ideas.
```
