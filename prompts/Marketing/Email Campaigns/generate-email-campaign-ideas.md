# Email Campaign Strategy Builder for Conversions

## 簡介

The Email Campaign Strategy Builder for Conversions is a free AI prompt that creates structured, multi-email sequences designed to guide prospects from awareness to conversion for marketers and business owners. This email campaign prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your product's features, mapping customer pain points to messaging opportunities, and generating 3-5 sequenced emails complete with subject lines optimized for open rates, main content points that address customer motivations, and calls-to-action engineered to reduce friction. You provide your product or service details, ideal customer profile, unique selling proposition, campaign goal, and desired brand voice, and the prompt returns a complete nurture sequence ready to deploy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across all major text-generation platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need to build a cohesive email funnel that moves recipients through defined stages rather than sending one-off messages, or when you want a strategic framework that connects product benefits to customer outcomes in a logical progression. ● Generates 3-5 sequenced emails that logically progress from initial awareness to conversion action ● Maps product features and differentiators to customer pain points and motivations in each message ● Provides subject lines crafted for clarity and emotional resonance to improve open rates ● Includes specific, contextual calls-to-action designed to create urgency and minimize purchase friction ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in conversion-focused campaigns.

## Task
Design a complete email campaign strategy that drives conversions through a multi-email sequence. For each email in the sequence, provide:
- Subject line optimized for open rates
- Main content points structured to address customer needs and motivations
- Clear call-to-action designed to move recipients toward conversion

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}

Ideal customer profile: {{ideal-customer}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Campaign goal: {{campaign-goal}}

Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Approach
1. Identify the core features, benefits, and differentiators of the product/service
2. Map customer pain points, needs, and motivations to campaign messaging
3. Structure subject lines for clarity and emotional resonance
4. Write persuasive body copy that connects benefits to customer outcomes
5. Design CTAs that create urgency and reduce friction
6. Sequence emails logically to nurture leads from awareness to conversion

## Output
Present your campaign as a numbered sequence of emails. For each email, use this structure:

**Email [N]: [Purpose/Stage]**
- **Subject Line:** [text]
- **Main Content:**
  - [Key point 1]
  - [Key point 2]
  - [Key point 3]
- **Call-to-Action:** [specific CTA with context]

Include 3-5 emails that form a complete nurture sequence aligned with the campaign goal.
```
