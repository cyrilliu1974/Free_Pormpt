# Flash Fiction Story Generator for Strangers Meeting

## 簡介

The Flash Fiction Story Generator for Strangers Meeting is a free AI prompt that produces emotionally resonant short stories centered on chance encounters between two people who have never met. This flash fiction prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok guides the AI to write a complete story of 500 words or fewer, opening with scene-setting that establishes both strangers and the location, building through authentic dialogue that reveals personality and emotional state, and closing with a meaningful impression. Writers use it to explore human connection, practice economy of language, and create stories that fit literary journal flash fiction requirements. The prompt enforces sensory detail, emotional depth, and avoidance of clichés, making the location integral to the narrative rather than simply backdrop. Reach for this tool when you need publishable-length flash fiction quickly, want to practice character-driven micro-narratives, or are building a portfolio of short-short stories. ● Outputs a titled, complete story with exact word count, ready for workshopping or submission ● Enforces the 500-word maximum that defines flash fiction as a literary form ● Balances scene-setting, dialogue, and emotional arc within tight constraints ● Makes the setting an active story element rather than passive background ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an award-winning flash fiction writer who excels at capturing poignant moments between strangers, crafting vivid scenes, and evoking powerful emotions in 500 words or less.

## Task
Write a complete flash fiction story (maximum 500 words) centered on an unexpected encounter between two strangers in {{location}}. Focus on a single poignant moment rather than complex plot.

## Requirements
- Open with a brief introduction that establishes the scene and both strangers
- Use vivid sensory descriptions to bring {{location}} to life and make it integral to the story
- Include authentic dialogue that reveals each character's personality and emotional state
- Create palpable emotion that resonates with the reader
- Close with a lasting impression or meaningful takeaway
- Avoid clichés and overused tropes

## Output
Title: [your title]

[the complete story]

Word Count: [exact count] words
```
