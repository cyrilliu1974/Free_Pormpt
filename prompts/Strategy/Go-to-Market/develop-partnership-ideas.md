# Partnership Development Strategy Prompt for Business Growth

## 簡介

The Partnership Development Strategy Prompt for Business Growth is a free AI prompt that generates innovative collaboration opportunities tailored to your specific business context. This partnership development prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, market position, and goals to produce 8-10 concrete partnership ideas presented in a clean markdown table. Each recommendation identifies a potential partner, describes the collaboration concept, and outlines mutual benefits - with special attention to customer acquisition, retention impact, and feasibility. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it model-agnostic for teams working across platforms. Real use cases include SaaS companies finding distribution partners, ecommerce brands exploring co-marketing opportunities, and service providers building referral networks. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond obvious partnerships and discover creative, mutually beneficial collaborations that leverage your unique strengths and address current growth challenges. ● Produces a structured three-column table (Partner, Idea, Benefits) with 8-10 unique collaboration concepts ● Aligns each partnership suggestion with your stated business goals, target market, and core values ● Evaluates customer acquisition and retention impact alongside feasibility and resource requirements ● Prioritizes non-obvious opportunities that address current challenges while advancing strategic objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in partnership development and market expansion.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of innovative partnership ideas tailored to the business described below. Each collaboration should expand reach, grow the customer base, and offer mutual value to both parties.

## Context
{{business-context}}

For each partnership idea:
- Identify partners that align with the business's goals, values, and target market
- Ensure the collaboration offers clear mutual benefits
- Evaluate potential impact on customer acquisition and retention
- Consider feasibility and required resources
- Focus on creative, non-obvious opportunities that leverage the business's unique strengths

## Output
Present your partnership recommendations in a markdown table with three columns:

| Partner | Idea | Benefits |
|---------|------|----------|

Include 8-10 partnership ideas, each one unique and specifically tailored to the business context provided. Prioritize partnerships that address current challenges while advancing stated business goals.
```
