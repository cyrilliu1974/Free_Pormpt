# Fashion Character Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Character Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional studio fashion photos blending real faces with oversized 3D cartoon characters for content creators and brands. This fashion portrait prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces hyper-realistic images of a person in custom outfits standing casually beside or interacting with a giant photorealistic 3D character - think a polished studio shoot with playful toy or mascot elements. You control the outfit style, the character (animals, fictional figures, branded mascots), and the solid backdrop color. The prompt is engineered to preserve the exact face from your uploaded reference photo while rendering sharp fabric details, glossy character textures, and soft studio lighting. Use cases include influencer content, product mockups, brand campaigns, and social media visuals that need a bold, scroll-stopping aesthetic. Reach for this prompt when you want fashion-forward imagery with a whimsical twist but lack access to a physical studio or 3D artist. ● Preserves the exact face from your reference upload without morphing or identity drift. ● Customizes outfit, character type, and backdrop color through three simple variables. ● Delivers studio-quality lighting, hyper-realistic skin texture, and glossy 3D character rendering in a single generation. ● Produces scroll-stopping visuals for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn profiles, pitch decks, and marketing campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic professional fashion portrait of a stylish young woman wearing {{outfit}}, standing with one arm resting casually on a giant 3D photorealistic {{character}} figure. The character should be rendered in a clean, glossy, cartoon-accurate style with ultra-detailed texture. Natural, confident expression with playful energy. Seamless solid {{backdrop-color}} studio backdrop with soft, evenly diffused lighting creating clean highlights and minimal shadows. Modern fashion photography aesthetic with hyperrealistic skin texture, sharp fabric details, accurate lighting reflections on the 3D character, and smooth studio gradients.

**Face preservation**: Use the exact same face from the uploaded reference photo without altering any facial features or identity. Match the reference face precisely.
```
