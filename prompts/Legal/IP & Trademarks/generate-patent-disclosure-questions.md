# Patent Disclosure Questions Generator

## 簡介

The Patent Disclosure Questions Generator is a free AI prompt that creates thorough questionnaires to capture every technical detail, commercial application, and prior art reference inventors need before filing a patent application. This patent disclosure prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as a USPTO-experienced patent specialist, producing eight structured sections of questions tailored to your invention context: overview, technical specifications, development history, commercial applications, prior art analysis, advantages, variations, and inventor contributions. Each section includes numbered main questions, lettered sub-questions, bracketed guidance notes explaining why the information matters, examples showing required detail levels, and end-of-section checklists for supporting documents. Real-world use cases include inventors preparing first filings, patent attorneys conducting client interviews, R&D teams documenting innovations, and startups building IP portfolios before fundraising. Reach for this prompt when you need to avoid the costly mistake of incomplete disclosures that lead to rejection or allow competitors to file first. ● Produces questions designed to uncover information inventors consider "too obvious to mention" but examiners require ● Forces precision about components, materials, dimensions, operational parameters, and technical distinctions ● Explores development history to establish priority dates and document the invention's evolution ● Investigates commercial applications, target markets, and measurable advantages to demonstrate utility and non-obviousness ## Prompt

```
## Role

Patent disclosure specialist with USPTO examination experience. Generate comprehensive, structured questions that extract technical details, commercial insights, and prior art references needed for a complete patent application.

## Task

Create a thorough patent disclosure questionnaire tailored to:

{{invention-context}}

Before generating questions, analyze:
1. Invention category and industry context
2. Technical specifications requiring documentation
3. Commercial applications and market positioning
4. Prior art landscape
5. Possible variations and improvements

Patent examiners reject applications daily for missing information inventors assume is obvious. Incomplete disclosures cost inventors fortunes when crucial details go undocumented. This questionnaire ensures nothing is overlooked before competitors file or public disclosure destroys patentability.

## Output

Structure the questionnaire in these sections:

### 1. Invention Overview
Open-ended questions capturing the inventor's vision, core purpose, and problem being solved. Extract the essence before drilling into specifics.

### 2. Technical Specifications
Detailed questions forcing precision about components, materials, dimensions, operational parameters, and technical aspects that distinguish this invention. Include sub-questions probing areas inventors commonly overlook.

### 3. Development History
Questions exploring the invention's evolution, failed attempts, pivotal breakthroughs, conception date, and development timeline for establishing priority.

### 4. Commercial Applications
Investigate potential markets, use cases, business models, and target customers to demonstrate utility and commercial viability.

### 5. Prior Art Analysis
Probe for similar solutions, competitors, existing products, inspirations, and relevant patents to identify novelty and non-obviousness.

### 6. Advantages
Extract specific, measurable benefits over existing solutions. Focus on problems solved and quantifiable improvements, not just features.

### 7. Variations and Embodiments
Explore alternative implementations, materials, configurations, scales, and future improvements to maximize claim breadth.

### 8. Inventor Contributions
Document each inventor's specific technical contributions to establish proper inventorship.

**Format each section with:**
- Main questions numbered sequentially
- Sub-questions marked with letters (a, b, c)
- [Bracketed guidance notes] explaining why the information matters
- Examples showing the required level of detail
- End-of-section checklist of documents/evidence to gather

**Question criteria:**
- Specific enough to force concrete, detailed answers
- Designed to uncover information considered "too obvious to mention"
- Focused on factual information, not legal conclusions
- Emphasizing technical details distinguishing the invention from prior art
- Probing for measurable improvements and specific advantages
- Including prompts for sketches, diagrams, or supporting data where applicable

Use clear, direct language without legal jargon.
```
