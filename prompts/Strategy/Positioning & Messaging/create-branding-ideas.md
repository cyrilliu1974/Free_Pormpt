# Brand Identity Generator Prompt for Business Naming

## 簡介

The Brand Identity Generator Prompt for Business Naming is a free AI prompt that creates five distinct brand identity concepts complete with names, taglines, and brand stories for businesses seeking a unique market position. This branding prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, industry landscape, target audience, unique selling proposition, and core values to generate five diverse branding concepts presented in a clean markdown table. Each concept includes a legally viable brand name, a 3–8 word tagline that captures your value proposition, and a 2–3 sentence brand story that emotionally connects with your audience. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and intentionally varies the tone across concepts - ranging from aspirational and playful to authoritative, disruptive, or heritage-driven - so you can evaluate multiple strategic directions for your brand. Business owners launching a new venture, marketing teams exploring rebrand options, and brand strategists developing client presentations will find this prompt delivers ready-to-review identity concepts in minutes. ● Produces five distinct brand concepts with varied tones and positioning strategies in one session ● Includes brand names vetted for memorability, relevance, and competitive differentiation ● Crafts concise taglines that communicate value propositions in 3–8 words ● Delivers brand stories that reflect company mission, values, and emotional audience connection ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert branding strategist specializing in brand identity development and positioning.

## Task
Generate 5 distinct, creative branding concepts that establish a unique identity and build recognition for the specified business. Each concept must include a memorable brand name, compelling tagline, and concise brand story.

## Context
Business and positioning details:
{{business-and-audience}}

Include:
- Business type and industry
- Target audience demographics and psychographics
- Unique selling proposition
- Core brand values
- Company vision

## Process
1. Analyze the business type, competitive landscape, and target audience needs
2. Develop brand names that are memorable, relevant, legally viable, and distinct from competitors
3. Craft taglines that encapsulate the brand essence and communicate the value proposition in 3–8 words
4. Write brand stories (2–3 sentences) that emotionally connect with the target audience and reflect the mission and values

## Output
Present your output as a markdown table with exactly 3 columns: **Brand Name** | **Tagline** | **Brand Story**

Provide exactly 5 branding concepts. Ensure variety in tone and approach across the concepts (e.g., aspirational, playful, authoritative, disruptive, heritage-driven).
```
