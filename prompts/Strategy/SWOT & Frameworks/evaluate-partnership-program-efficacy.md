# Partnership Program Evaluation Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Partnership Program Evaluation Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive business analysis reports assessing partnership initiative performance and identifying growth opportunities. This partnership evaluation prompt for ChatGPT walks through every section of an executive report: a concise summary, evidence-backed key findings, comparative performance tables tracking partner KPIs, prioritized improvement areas with actionable recommendations, projected impact estimates, and an appendix of supporting references. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering structured insights grounded in data points and industry benchmarks rather than generic observations. Use cases include quarterly partnership reviews, board presentations, investor updates, and strategy planning sessions for B2B companies managing affiliate, reseller, or technology integration partners. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn partnership data into clear, decision-ready analysis formatted for senior leadership or when optimizing multi-partner ecosystems. ● Generates a complete report structure from executive summary through appendix, saving hours of formatting and outline work. ● Includes a comparative KPI table template that highlights performance gaps and top performers at a glance. ● Delivers three concrete optimization opportunities, each paired with two specific, implementable actions. ● Produces impact projections with estimated magnitude, helping prioritize which recommendations to act on first. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business analyst conducting a comprehensive evaluation of partnership initiatives. Deliver findings in an executive-ready report format.

## Task
Assess the efficacy of {{company-name}}'s partner programs and provide actionable recommendations for optimization and growth.

## Context
Draw on relevant data points and industry benchmarks to support your analysis. Focus on specific, measurable opportunities rather than generic observations.

## Output
Structure your report with the following sections:

**Executive Summary**  
Concise overview of key findings and recommendations (3-4 paragraphs)

**Key Findings**  
- Three most significant insights from the analysis
- Each supported by specific evidence or metrics

**Program Performance Metrics**  
Comparative table showing each partner's performance across three relevant KPIs:

| Partner | KPI 1 | KPI 2 | KPI 3 |
|---------|-------|-------|-------|
| Partner A | [value] | [value] | [value] |
| Partner B | [value] | [value] | [value] |
| Partner C | [value] | [value] | [value] |

**Areas for Improvement**  
Identify three optimization opportunities, each with two specific, actionable recommendations.

**Projected Impact of Recommended Changes**  
Three potential positive outcomes from implementing your recommendations, with estimated magnitude where possible.

**Appendix**  
3-5 references supporting your findings (industry reports, case studies, relevant research)
```
