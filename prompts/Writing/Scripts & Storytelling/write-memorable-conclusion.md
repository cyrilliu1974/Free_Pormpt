# Speech Conclusion Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Speech Conclusion Writer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that helps speakers, writers, and presenters craft high-impact endings that reinforce core messages and inspire audience action. This speech conclusion prompt for ChatGPT produces polished closing paragraphs structured with dependency grammar principles, ensuring each sentence builds logically on the previous one with clear hierarchical relationships between ideas. You provide the speech topic, main points covered, target audience, desired call to action, and tone; the prompt synthesizes these into a cohesive conclusion that mirrors your opening theme for closure, delivers emotional and intellectual resonance, and integrates a compelling call to action or invitation to reflection. The output works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for keynotes, presentations, lectures, pitches, or any spoken format where the ending matters. Reach for it when you need your conclusion to do more than summarize - when you want tight syntactic coherence, a callback to your introduction, and a final statement that sticks with listeners long after you leave the stage. ● Synthesizes main points into a brief, logically structured recap that reinforces the core claim ● Creates circular closure by tying the conclusion back to the speech's opening theme ● Integrates a clear, audience-aligned call to action or reflective invitation ● Produces delivery-ready paragraph formats with tight syntactic coherence and emotional resonance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech writer specializing in memorable, high-impact conclusions.

## Task
Craft a powerful conclusion that reinforces the speech's main points and leaves a lasting impression. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—ensuring each sentence builds logically on the previous one, with clear hierarchical relationships between clauses.

## Context
Speech topic: {{speech-topic}}

Main points covered: {{main-points}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Desired call to action: {{call-to-action}}

Tone: {{tone}}

## Output
Deliver a cohesive conclusion structured as:

1. A brief synthesis of the main points that mirrors dependency relationships (core claim supported by subordinate ideas)
2. A compelling final statement that resonates emotionally and intellectually with the audience
3. Integration with the speech's opening theme to create closure
4. A clear call to action or invitation to reflection

Format as polished paragraph(s) ready to deliver, demonstrating tight syntactic coherence through dependency grammar structure.
```
