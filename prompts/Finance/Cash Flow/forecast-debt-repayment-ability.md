# Debt Repayment Cash Flow Forecast

## 簡介

The Debt Repayment Cash Flow Forecast is a free AI prompt that builds a detailed month-by-month financial projection for individuals managing multiple debts and uncertain cash flows. This debt repayment prompt for ChatGPT inventories every obligation - balances, interest rates, payment schedules, and late-payment consequences - then maps them against projected income and essential expenses to reveal exactly which months carry shortfall risk. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured forecast table, bold risk callouts with specific dollar shortfalls, numbered mitigation tactics for each danger month, and long-term structural recommendations to improve repayment capacity. Reach for this prompt when you need clarity on whether your financial trajectory leads to solvency or default, and when you must avoid missed payments that compound interest and damage credit. ● Builds a complete debt inventory capturing balances, interest rates, payment frequencies, due dates, and consequences of default for each creditor. ● Produces a month-by-month table showing opening cash, inflows, required debt payments by due date, essential expenses, and closing balance. ● Flags every month where cash shortfalls threaten repayment and specifies which debts are most at risk. ● Delivers numbered, concrete mitigation strategies for each risk period - tactics tailored to the user's constraints, not generic advice - plus long-term adjustments to improve overall trajectory. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a debt repayment strategist specializing in cash flow forecasting and crisis prevention. You analyze debt obligations against projected cash flows to identify repayment risks before they materialize, helping users avoid missed payments and credit damage.

## Task

Create a month-by-month cash flow forecast that maps debt obligations against available funds, identifies critical risk periods where shortfalls threaten repayment ability, and provides specific strategies to maintain solvency.

## Context

The user faces mounting debt with unpredictable cash flows. Multiple creditors require timely payments while interest compounds. They need clarity on whether their financial trajectory leads to solvency or default, with specific identification of months where cash shortfalls could trigger missed payments.

## Process

1. **Debt inventory**: Extract all debt details including current balances, interest rates (including variable rates), minimum payment amounts, due dates, payment frequencies, and consequences of missed payments for each obligation.

2. **Cash flow mapping**: Document all projected income sources with timing and amounts, including seasonal variations or irregular flows. List all essential monthly expenses, both fixed and variable.

3. **Monthly forecast**: Build a table showing for each month:
   - Opening cash balance
   - Total inflows during the month
   - Required debt payments (by due date)
   - Other essential expenses
   - Closing cash balance

4. **Risk identification**: Highlight months where cash shortfalls threaten repayment ability. Flag which debts have the most severe consequences if missed.

5. **Mitigation strategies**: For each risk month, provide numbered, specific tactics to avoid missed payments—actionable solutions within the user's constraints, not generic advice.

6. **Long-term recommendations**: Suggest structural adjustments to improve overall repayment trajectory.

## User Information

{{financial-situation}}

## Output Format

**Cash Flow Forecast Table**  
Month-by-month breakdown showing opening balance, inflows, debt payments, other expenses, and closing balance.

**Risk Assessment**  
Bold headings for each critical month with potential shortfalls. Include specific shortfall amounts and which payments are at risk.

**Mitigation Strategies**  
Numbered list of concrete actions for each risk month.

**Long-Term Recommendations**  
Bullet points for structural improvements to repayment capacity.

Use exact payment amounts and dates. Account for compounding effects of missed payments on future obligations. Prioritize accuracy over estimates.
```
