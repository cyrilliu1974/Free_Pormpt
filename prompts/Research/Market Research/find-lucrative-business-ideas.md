# Find Lucrative Business Ideas for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Find Lucrative Business Ideas for Solopreneurs prompt is a free AI prompt that researches and presents tailored online business opportunities for individuals working independently. This business idea generator prompt for ChatGPT conducts market research to identify emerging trends and untapped niches, then analyzes each opportunity for feasibility, scalability, and profit potential. It evaluates initial investment requirements, required skills, target audience reach, revenue diversity, time commitment, and competitive landscape to surface business ideas that align with your specific profile. The output is a ranked markdown table showing the business idea, target market, and revenue streams for at least five promising opportunities, complete with explanations of why each fits your circumstances and what advantages or considerations apply. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is for solopreneurs, aspiring independent business owners, freelancers transitioning to product-based models, and anyone seeking data-driven business ideas that match their personal constraints and strengths. ● Surfaces emerging trends and untapped market niches based on current opportunity landscape ● Evaluates each business idea against budget, skills, time availability, and competitive factors ● Ranks opportunities by feasibility and match to your solopreneur profile ● Delivers structured output showing target markets and multiple revenue stream options for each idea ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in solopreneur ventures and online business models.

## Task
Research and present viable online business opportunities tailored to an individual working independently. Conduct market research to identify emerging trends and untapped niches, then analyze each idea for feasibility, scalability, and profit potential.

## Context
Consider the following when evaluating opportunities:
- Initial investment requirements and budget constraints
- Skills needed and existing expertise
- Target audience size and accessibility
- Revenue stream diversity and sustainability
- Time commitment and operational complexity
- Competitive landscape and market gaps

**Solopreneur Profile:**
{{solopreneur-profile}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns: **BUSINESS IDEA**, **TARGET MARKET**, and **REVENUE STREAMS**. Provide at least 5 unique and promising business ideas, ranked by viability given the solopreneur profile. For each idea, briefly explain why it fits the profile and note any particular advantages or considerations.
```
