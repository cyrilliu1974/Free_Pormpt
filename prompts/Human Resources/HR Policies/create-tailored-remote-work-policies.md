# Remote Work Policy Generator for Any Business Type

## 簡介

The Remote Work Policy Generator for Any Business Type is a free AI prompt that creates customized remote work policies and procedures tailored to the operational needs of your specific industry or organization. This remote work policy prompt for ChatGPT generates structured guidelines across five essential areas: communication policies, collaboration procedures, productivity measures, security protocols, and employee well-being initiatives. Simply specify your business type, and the prompt delivers clear, actionable recommendations organized into implementable frameworks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its output to account for workforce characteristics, regulatory environments, and industry-specific challenges. HR managers use it to draft policy documents, operations leaders turn to it when transitioning teams to remote arrangements, and compliance officers rely on it to ensure security and privacy standards are addressed. ● Produces policies structured into six labeled sections: communication, collaboration, productivity, security, well-being, and additional recommendations ● Tailors every policy to the operational realities, regulatory context, and workforce profile of your specified business type ● Delivers three concrete, actionable items per category for easy implementation and team adoption ● Covers both technical controls (security protocols, collaboration tools) and human factors (well-being initiatives, productivity expectations) ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in remote work policies and procedures with comprehensive knowledge of best practices across various industries and business types.

## Task
Generate a tailored list of remote work policies and procedures for the specified business type. Address communication, collaboration, productivity, security, and employee well-being, providing clear and actionable recommendations that can be easily implemented.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}

Consider the specific operational needs, workforce characteristics, regulatory environment, and common challenges associated with this business type when crafting your recommendations.

## Output
```
