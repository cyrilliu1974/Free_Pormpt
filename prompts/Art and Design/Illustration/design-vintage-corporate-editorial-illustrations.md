# Vintage Corporate Editorial Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Corporate Editorial Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hand-drawn narrative scenes in a timeless line art style for brands, marketers, and editorial designers. This corporate illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces multiple small vignettes arranged with generous negative space, each depicting story-driven scenes with expressive yet simplified characters. The output uses a limited palette of deep burgundy and dark red on warm cream backgrounds, featuring confident ink lines that feel hand-drawn rather than digital. It works for about us pages, service design communication, editorial blogs, annual reports, and brand culture storytelling where trust and human connection matter more than photorealism. Reach for this prompt when you need scalable visual assets that function as individual icons or cohesive illustration sets, readable at any size and adaptable across print and digital channels. ● Produces multiple narrative vignettes on a single canvas with asymmetrical yet cohesive layouts and ample negative space for flexible cropping. ● Delivers hand-drawn line art with medium detail, clean ink strokes, and a limited burgundy-cream color palette that conveys warmth and trust. ● Generates illustrations readable at small and large sizes, usable as standalone icons or complete visual sets for editorial and brand communication. ● Creates characters and scenes that communicate experience and human connection through expressive, simplified forms rather than realistic rendering. ## Prompt
