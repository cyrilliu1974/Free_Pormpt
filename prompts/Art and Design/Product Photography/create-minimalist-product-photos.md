The Minimalist Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional studio-quality product images with clean white backdrops and precise lighting control for e-commerce sellers, marketers, and designers. This minimalist product photography prompt for Midjourney produces centered compositions with sharp focus, detailed material textures, and soft shadows that add depth without distraction. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you specify any product description and aspect ratio to create branding-ready shots with an eye-level three-quarter angle and studio lighting that highlights form and finish. Reach for this prompt when you need catalog-ready images that put the product front and center, whether you're shooting tech accessories, consumer goods, fashion items, or physical products of any kind. ● Produces high-resolution images with visible material textures and clean silhouettes isolated on white. ● Controls lighting, shadow depth, and camera angle to match professional studio photography standards. ● Accepts any product description and custom aspect ratio for marketplace listings, social media, or print. ● Outputs branding-ready compositions with sharp focus and a three-quarter view that shows dimension. ## Prompt
