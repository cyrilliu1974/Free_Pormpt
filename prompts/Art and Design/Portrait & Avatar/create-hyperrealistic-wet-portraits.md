# Hyperrealistic Wet Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Wet Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates studio-quality close-up portraits with wet skin effects, dramatic lighting, and micro-textural detail for photographers and content creators. This wet portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images that replicate professional Hasselblad H6D-400c camera shots at 8K resolution. It captures the left side of the face with extremely shallow depth of field, water droplets running naturally down the skin, and cinematic lighting moods you customize. Real-world applications include beauty campaigns, fashion editorials, skincare brand visuals, and premium social media content where authentic texture and professional polish matter. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality portraits without organizing a studio shoot or investing in high-end camera equipment. ● Produces portraits with visible pores, freckles, natural skin texture, and authentic water droplet physics under side lighting. ● Simulates 100mm f/2.2 lens bokeh with the subject's eye in sharp focus and everything else falling into soft blur. ● Offers full control over lighting mood and subject description to match brand aesthetics or creative direction. ● Renders wet hair strands clinging to skin, moist lip reflections, and specular highlights that mimic gold reflector setups. ## Prompt

```
## Subject
Hyper-realistic close-up portrait of {{subject-description}}, capturing only the left side of the face. Faithfully maintain all facial features: thick well-defined eyebrow, long wet lashes, full slightly parted lips. Wet strands of hair fall along the right side of the face, clinging to the skin.

## Technical Setup
Shot on Hasselblad H6D-400c with 100mm f/2.2 lens at 8K resolution. Extremely shallow depth of field. Eye in absolute focus with crisp catchlight; everything else falls off into soft blur.

## Lighting
Simulated {{lighting-mood}} light from a large side softbox combined with a gold reflector. Soft yet intense glow creates vivid specular highlights and deep shadows, precisely illuminating micro-details with soft realistic contrast.

## Texture & Detail
Skin covered in water droplets running down naturally. Ultra-detailed micro-texture: visible pores, freckles, natural wet skin texture. Moist reflections on lips and nose. Vivid specular highlights on water droplets due to light angle. Cinematic effect with {{lighting-mood}} tones throughout.
```
