# Referral Partnership Strategy Guide

## 簡介

The Referral Partnership Strategy Guide is a free AI prompt that creates a step-by-step playbook for building and maintaining referral partnerships with complementary businesses in any target market. Acting as an expert business development consultant, the prompt produces a structured guide covering partner identification criteria, pitch frameworks, agreement terms, tracking systems, relationship nurturing tactics, and industry-relevant case studies. This referral partnership prompt for ChatGPT works on Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable strategies tailored to your specific market context rather than generic advice. Business development teams, growth marketers, and strategic partnership managers reach for this prompt when they need a complete roadmap for launching or scaling a referral partner program. The output addresses both tactical execution and strategic reasoning across six phases: identifying partners using complementary-offering and shared-customer filters, crafting ROI-focused pitches, negotiating fair referral agreements, implementing attribution and commission systems, maintaining partnerships through co-marketing and regular check-ins, and learning from real success stories with measurable results. ● Provides evaluation frameworks for assessing partner fit, strategic alignment, and non-competitive positioning ● Includes negotiation strategies and agreement templates covering referral fees, exclusivity, duration, and termination terms ● Delivers tracking system recommendations and commission calculation processes that ensure transparency and reduce disputes ● Supplies three tailored case studies with company names, partner details, measurable outcomes, and replicable tactics for your industry ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business development consultant specializing in referral partnerships and complementary business relationships across industries.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for developing and maintaining referral partnerships with complementary businesses in the user's market.

## Context
Target market and industry context: {{target-market}}

The guide should provide actionable strategies, concrete best practices, and relevant examples tailored to this market. Address both the tactical (how to execute) and strategic (why it matters) dimensions of each phase.

## Output
Deliver a structured guide covering these six phases:

### 1. Identifying Potential Referral Partners
- Criteria for evaluating potential partners (complementary offerings, shared customer profile, aligned values, non-competitive positioning)
- Research strategies and outreach methods
- Assessment frameworks for partnership fit and strategic alignment

### 2. Pitching the Referral Partnership
- Key benefits to emphasize (mutual customer value, revenue opportunities, market expansion)
- Methods for demonstrating partnership ROI and potential
- Techniques for customizing the pitch to each partner's priorities

### 3. Structuring the Referral Agreement
- Essential terms (referral fee structure, exclusivity clauses, duration, termination conditions)
- Negotiation strategies for win-win outcomes
- Clarity and fairness principles to prevent future disputes

### 4. Tracking Referrals and Commissions
- Tools and systems for accurate referral attribution and commission calculation
- Processes ensuring timely tracking and transparent reporting
- Optimization tactics to reduce friction and disputes

### 5. Nurturing the Referral Partnership
- Maintenance tactics (regular check-ins, shared wins, co-marketing initiatives)
- Communication and collaboration best practices
- Strategies for addressing challenges and scaling successful partnerships

### 6. Referral Partnership Success Stories
- Three industry-relevant case studies with companies, partners, and measurable results
- Key lessons and replicable tactics from each example
- Common pitfalls to avoid based on real-world experience

Provide specific, actionable guidance within each section rather than generic placeholders.
```
