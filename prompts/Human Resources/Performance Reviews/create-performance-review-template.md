# Performance Review Template Generator for Managers

## 簡介

The Performance Review Template Generator for Managers is a free AI prompt that creates a complete performance review framework tailored to your organization's HR practices and goal-setting methodology. This performance review prompt for ChatGPT takes your company's HR framework - whether you use OKRs, SMART goals, quarterly cycles, or annual reviews - and builds a ready-to-use template that covers pre-review preparation, meeting structure, and post-review actions. It produces checklists for both managers and employees, discussion guides for reviewing achievements and growth areas, goal-setting worksheets aligned with organizational priorities, and career development planning sections. The template is designed for live conversations, with clear headings and actionable steps that keep performance discussions focused and meaningful. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize your performance review process, train new managers on conducting development conversations, or refresh your existing review templates to better align with company values and strategic objectives. ● Creates pre-review checklists that prepare both managers and employees for productive discussions ● Builds meeting agendas that balance performance feedback, goal-setting, and career development in a single session ● Generates goal templates formatted to your methodology - OKRs, SMART goals, or custom frameworks ● Includes post-review documentation guidance and ongoing coaching schedules to maintain accountability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR consultant specializing in performance management, employee development, and goal-setting.

## Task
Create a comprehensive annual personal development review template that guides managers through effective performance discussions, goal-setting sessions, and career development planning. The template should facilitate meaningful dialogue, encourage employee participation, and align individual goals with organizational objectives.

## Context
{{company-hr-framework}}

Provide details about your HR practices:
- Performance review cycle (annual, semi-annual, quarterly)
- Goal-setting framework (OKRs, SMART goals, or other methodology)
- Career development initiatives (mentoring programs, training offerings, succession planning)
- Any specific organizational priorities or values to embed

## Output
Deliver a structured template organized into these sections:

**1. Pre-Review Preparation**
- Manager checklist (review prior feedback, gather performance data, prepare discussion points)
- Employee checklist (self-assessment, achievement documentation, goal ideas)

**2. Review Meeting Structure**
- Opening discussion (set tone, review agenda, establish dialogue)
- Performance review covering:
  - Key achievements and wins
  - Areas for improvement and growth opportunities
  - Manager observations and feedback
  - Employee self-assessment and perspective
- Goal setting including:
  - SMART/OKR formulation based on company framework
  - Alignment with organizational objectives
  - Employee input and ownership
  - Required resources and support mechanisms
- Career development discussion addressing:
  - Employee aspirations and interests
  - Training and development plan
  - Potential career paths within the organization
- Wrap-up with:
  - Key takeaways and agreements
  - Next steps and accountability
  - Follow-up schedule

**3. Post-Review Actions**
- Documentation requirements and storage
- Progress tracking mechanisms
- Ongoing feedback and coaching cadence

**4. Additional Resources**
- Performance review best practices
- Goal-setting templates tailored to company framework
- Career development plan examples

Use clear headings, bullet points, and numbered lists. Make the template actionable and easy for managers to follow during live conversations.
```
