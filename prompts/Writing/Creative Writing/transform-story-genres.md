# Story Genre Transformation Prompt

## 簡介

The Story Genre Transformation Prompt is a free AI prompt that adapts existing narratives into different genres while preserving their central conflict, character arcs, and emotional structure. This story genre transformation prompt for ChatGPT guides text models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) to rewrite any story through the lens of a new genre - turning a romance into a thriller, a fantasy into science fiction, or a drama into horror - while maintaining what made the original compelling. It produces a complete transformed narrative alongside structured analysis of setting, character, plot, and thematic changes. Writers use it to reimagine classic tales for modern audiences, explore alternate interpretations of their own work, or practice genre conventions by seeing how familiar stories translate across categories. Reach for this prompt when you need to repurpose a narrative for a different audience, experiment with genre mechanics, or breathe new life into existing story material without losing its emotional core. ● Preserves central conflict and character arcs while reshaping tone, setting, and plot details to fit target genre conventions ● Generates a full transformed story title and complete narrative text in the new genre style ● Documents specific changes to setting, character archetypes, plot structure, and thematic emphasis ● Structured output format makes it easy to compare original and adapted elements side by side ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a master storyteller who adapts narratives across genres while preserving their core essence.

## Task
Transform the given story into a different genre. Maintain the original's heart—its central conflict, character arcs, and emotional beats—while reshaping setting, tone, plot details, and themes to fit the conventions and expectations of the new genre.

## Context
{{story-and-genres}}
Provide: the original story title, its current genre, and the target genre for the transformation.

## Output

**Original Story Title:** [title]  
**Original Genre:** [genre]  

**New Genre:** [genre]  
**Transformed Story Title:** [new title]  

**Transformed Story:**  
[Write the full adapted narrative here, capturing the essence of the original through the lens of the new genre]

**Key Changes:**  
**Setting:**  
- [Describe how location, time period, or world-building shifted]

**Characters:**  
- [Describe role, archetype, or trait adaptations]

**Plot:**  
- [Describe structural or event changes to fit genre conventions]

**Themes:**  
- [Describe how core ideas were reframed or emphasized differently]
```
