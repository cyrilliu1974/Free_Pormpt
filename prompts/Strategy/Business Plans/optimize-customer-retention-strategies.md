# Customer Retention Strategy Generator for Business

## 簡介

The Customer Retention Strategy Generator for Business is a free AI prompt that produces targeted retention and upselling ideas for product-based businesses looking to maximize customer lifetime value. This customer retention prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product and customer base to deliver 10 unconventional ways to enhance your core offering - such as building community, increasing perceived value, or driving repeat engagement - plus 10 complementary product ideas for cross-selling and upselling. Each recommendation includes an impact-versus-effort score in a markdown table, helping you prioritize quick wins and high-value initiatives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing teams, founders, and product managers use it to identify revenue opportunities that stay within their product domain and minimize customer friction. ● Produces 10 core-product enhancements focused on perceived value, repeat purchases, and sustained engagement ● Identifies 10 complementary revenue opportunities that align with your primary offering and reduce context switching ● Scores every idea on a 0–10 impact and effort scale, surfacing high-ROI quick wins ● Accepts two variables - product name and customer description - to tailor all recommendations to your business ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative business strategist specializing in customer retention and lifetime value optimization.

## Task
Generate actionable strategies to increase customer lifetime value for {{product-name}} by enhancing the core offering and identifying complementary revenue opportunities. All recommendations must stay within the same product domain to minimize customer friction.

## Context
**Product:** {{product-name}}

**Customer base:** {{customer-description}}

Focus on ideas that:
- Increase perceived value without proportional cost increases
- Drive repeat purchases, subscriptions, or sustained engagement
- Build community or exclusivity
- Require moderate implementation effort relative to impact

## Output
Structure your response in three sections:

### 1. Core Product Value Enhancement
List **10 unconventional ideas** to increase the value of the existing product. Each idea should be concise (1-2 sentences) and target a specific aspect of the product experience or customer relationship.

### 2. Complementary Revenue Opportunities
List **10 product ideas** for up-selling or cross-selling to existing customers. Each should:
- Relate closely to the primary product
- Offer high perceived customer value
- Minimize context switching

### 3. Impact vs. Effort Analysis
Create a markdown table with all 20 ideas:

| Idea | Description | Impact (0-10) | Effort (0-10) |
|------|-------------|---------------|---------------|

- **Impact score:** Potential to increase customer lifetime value (10 = transformative)
- **Effort score:** Implementation difficulty (0 = trivial, 10 = major undertaking)

Prioritize quick wins (high impact, low effort) in your analysis.
```
