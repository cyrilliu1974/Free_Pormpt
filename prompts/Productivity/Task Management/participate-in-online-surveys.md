# Survey Platform Comparison Table Generator

## 簡介

The Survey Platform Comparison Table Generator is a free AI prompt that researches and compares paid online survey platforms for solopreneurs and side hustlers seeking passive income opportunities. This survey platform comparison prompt for ChatGPT analyzes legitimate survey sites based on payout rates, time efficiency, payment thresholds, and geographic availability. It produces a structured markdown table ranking at least five platforms by return on investment, complete with detailed columns for platform descriptions, payout structures, and time commitments. The output includes a summary paragraph highlighting the top recommendation and important caveats. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific niche, target audience, and constraints such as preferred payment methods or location restrictions. This prompt is ideal for entrepreneurs, freelancers, and researchers who need an objective, data-driven evaluation of survey monetization options without spending hours vetting individual platforms. ● Evaluates survey platforms on reliability, average earnings, time per survey, payment methods, and geographic reach ● Outputs a markdown table with at least five platforms ranked by return on investment ● Customizes analysis based on niche, target audience, time availability, payout preferences, and location ● Includes a summary paragraph with a top recommendation and important usage caveats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert survey analyst specializing in evaluating paid online survey platforms for reliability, payout rates, and time efficiency.

## Task
Research and compile a comprehensive comparison of survey platforms that offer paid opportunities in {{niche}}. Evaluate each platform's legitimacy, average earnings, time commitment per survey, payment methods, and geographic availability. Focus on platforms that provide the best return on investment for solopreneurs and side hustlers.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Key constraints: {{constraints}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Survey Platform** (name and brief description)
- **Payout** (rates, thresholds, and methods)
- **Time Commitment** (per survey and monthly potential)

Include at least 5 platforms, ranked by ROI. After the table, provide a summary paragraph highlighting the top recommendation and any important caveats.
```
