# Social Media Content Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable social media plans for businesses and brands looking to grow their online presence. This social media content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, identifies platform-specific content themes, and delivers a detailed implementation roadmap including posting frequencies, content type recommendations, and engagement tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete strategy document with audience analysis, content pillar definitions, platform-specific plans, a content calendar framework, and performance measurement criteria. Marketing teams use it to align social efforts with business goals, while agencies deploy it to build comprehensive strategies for clients quickly. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured content strategy that goes beyond posting schedules to include audience insights, content mix ratios, and optimization triggers tailored to your brand voice and chosen platforms. ● Analyzes audience demographics, psychographics, pain points, and platform-specific behavior patterns to inform content decisions. ● Defines 3-5 core content themes aligned with both brand identity and business objectives like awareness, engagement, and sales. ● Recommends specific content types, posting frequencies, optimal timing, and engagement tactics customized for each social platform. ● Provides a content calendar framework with monthly planning structure, content mix ratios, and key campaign opportunities. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content strategy that increases brand awareness, drives engagement, and boosts sales. Deliver a structured, actionable plan with clear implementation steps.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-context}}

**Brand Voice:**
{{brand-voice}}

**Platforms:**
{{platforms}}

## Approach
Analyze the target audience and platform characteristics. Identify key content themes aligned with business goals. Determine the optimal content mix (promotional, educational, user-generated) for each platform. Develop a content calendar framework with specific posting frequencies. Recommend engagement tactics and performance measurement strategies.

## Output
Deliver a complete social media content strategy organized with:

### Audience Analysis
- Demographics and psychographics breakdown
- Pain points and content preferences
- Platform-specific behavior patterns

### Content Themes & Pillars
- 3-5 core themes aligned with brand and audience interests
- How each theme supports business objectives

### Platform-Specific Strategy
For each platform:
- Recommended content types (video, carousel, stories, etc.)
- Posting frequency and optimal timing
- Content mix ratios (e.g., 40% educational, 30% promotional, 30% engagement-focused)
- Platform-specific engagement tactics

### Content Calendar Framework
- Monthly planning structure
- Balance across content types and themes
- Key dates and campaign opportunities

### Performance Measurement
- Key metrics to track per platform
- Tools and processes for monitoring
- Optimization criteria and adjustment triggers

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout.
```
