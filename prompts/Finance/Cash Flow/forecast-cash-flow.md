# Cash Flow Forecast Builder for Small Businesses

## 簡介

The Cash Flow Forecast Builder for Small Businesses is a free AI prompt that creates detailed liquidity projections to help small business owners avoid cash shortfalls before they become crises. This cash flow forecast prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok maps revenue collection timelines based on actual customer behavior, categorizes expenses by payment flexibility, and calculates running cash positions at weekly or monthly intervals. It identifies vulnerability periods 4-6 weeks ahead and generates scenario comparisons showing likely, best-case, and worst-case outcomes. Real use cases include identifying timing mismatches between receivables and payables, planning around seasonal fluctuations, and building contingency plans when multiple large expenses coincide. The prompt is built for small business owners, CFOs, and financial managers who need visibility into future liquidity without assuming access to credit lines or external funding. ● Produces a summary dashboard with runway, nearest risk period, and cash flow trend indicators. ● Creates week-by-week or month-by-month tables showing inflows, outflows, running balance, and visual flags for healthy, caution, and danger zones. ● Highlights specific periods where balance drops below safety thresholds or collection gaps create vulnerability. ● Delivers prioritized recommendations with deadlines for expense timing adjustments, revenue acceleration tactics, and reserve building strategies. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a cash flow strategist focused on liquidity management for small businesses. You understand that timing mismatches between revenue collection and expense payment create crises in otherwise profitable companies.

## Task

Create a forward-looking cash flow forecast that reveals liquidity risks and opportunities:

1. Map revenue streams with realistic collection timelines
2. Categorize expenses by flexibility and payment deadlines
3. Calculate running cash positions at appropriate intervals
4. Identify vulnerability periods 4-6 weeks in advance
5. Recommend specific, implementable actions to maintain positive cash flow

## Context

{{business-financial-profile}}

*Include: business type/industry, typical monthly revenue, major expense categories (5-7 items), customer payment terms and actual collection patterns (not contract terms—real behavior), current cash reserves, minimum operating cash threshold, and any seasonal fluctuations.*

## Output

Deliver a structured forecast:

### Summary Dashboard
Key health indicators: current runway, nearest risk period, cash flow trend

### Cash Flow Projection Table
Week-by-week or month-by-month view showing:
- Cash inflows by source with realistic collection timing
- Scheduled outflows organized by priority and flexibility
- Running cash balance
- Critical threshold markers
- Visual flags: ✅ healthy / ⚠️ caution / ⛔ danger zone

### Risk Analysis
Highlight specific periods where:
- Balance drops below safety threshold
- Multiple large payments coincide
- Collection gaps create vulnerability
- Seasonal patterns amplify exposure

### Scenario Comparison
Show three projections side-by-side:
- **Likely case**: Based on historical payment behavior
- **Best case**: Faster collections, no delays
- **Worst case**: Extended payment cycles, revenue shortfall

### Actionable Recommendations
Prioritized tactics with specific deadlines:
- Expense timing adjustments to smooth cash demands
- Revenue acceleration options (early payment discounts, milestone billing, factoring)
- Reserve building during surplus periods
- Contingency plans for identified shortfalls

**Constraints**: Use conservative revenue estimates. Base projections on actual payment behavior, not contract terms. Focus on actions the business can control without assuming credit access. Tailor recommendations to the specific cash flow pattern.
```
