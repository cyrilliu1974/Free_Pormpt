# Literature Review Generator for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Literature Review Generator for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, thesis-ready literature review chapters for graduate students, doctoral candidates, and academic researchers. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to synthesize relevant studies, critically evaluate methodologies and theoretical frameworks, identify gaps in existing scholarship, and build a clear narrative that positions your research as a meaningful contribution to the field. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting your thesis topic, scope, and preferred citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.) as inputs. The output includes structured sections - introduction, key thematic clusters, methodological and theoretical discussions, gap analysis, and a conclusion with implications - all formatted for direct incorporation into a thesis or dissertation chapter. Use it when you need to organize dozens of sources into a cohesive, academically rigorous review that meets committee standards. ● Organizes findings into thematic sections with supporting citations and critical evaluation of each study's strengths and limitations ● Compares methodological approaches and theoretical frameworks used across the literature, highlighting their applicability to your research question ● Identifies 3-5 substantive gaps in current knowledge, creating a logical foundation for your study's contribution ● Formats references in your chosen citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE) for immediate use in your manuscript ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and scholar writing a comprehensive, critical literature review chapter for a thesis.

## Task
Develop a structured literature review that:
- Synthesizes the most relevant and impactful studies in the field
- Critically evaluates the strengths and limitations of existing research
- Identifies overarching themes, debates, methodological approaches, and theoretical frameworks
- Highlights gaps in current knowledge
- Establishes a clear narrative showing how the proposed study will advance the field

## Context
Topic and scope: {{thesis-topic-and-scope}}

Citation style: {{citation-style}}

## Output
Structure the literature review with these sections:

# Introduction
## Background on the Topic
## Purpose of the Literature Review
## Scope of the Literature Review

# Key Themes
For each major theme (typically 3-5):
## Theme [N]
### Description of Theme
### Studies Supporting This Theme [with citations]
### Critical Evaluation

# Methodological Approaches
## Overview of Methodological Approaches
## Strengths and Limitations of Each Approach

# Theoretical Frameworks
## Overview of Theoretical Frameworks
## Strengths and Limitations of Each Framework

# Gaps in Existing Research
Identify and describe 3-5 significant gaps

# Conclusion
## Summary of Key Findings
## Implications for Current Study

# References
List all sources cited using the specified citation style
```
