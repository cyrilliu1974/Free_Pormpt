# Speech Delivery Rehearsal and Coaching Prompt

## 簡介

The Speech Delivery Rehearsal and Coaching Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides speakers through comprehensive preparation and delivery refinement for any presentation or speech. This speech delivery prompt for ChatGPT helps you structure content using dependency grammar principles, master vocal projection and pacing techniques, develop confident body language, and build effective rehearsal routines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, tailoring guidance to your specific speech topic, target audience, duration, and experience level. Whether you're preparing a keynote, pitch, lecture, or toast, the prompt delivers actionable coaching across five core areas: speech structuring with strong openings and memorable conclusions, vocal techniques for projection and tone variation, body language that conveys confidence, rehearsal strategies including recording and feedback loops, and delivery principles that prioritize authentic connection over perfection. This prompt is ideal for professionals preparing high-stakes presentations, students practicing speeches, educators refining lectures, or anyone seeking structured coaching to overcome public speaking anxiety and deliver with impact. ● Structures speeches using dependency grammar to build content around a core message with layered supporting points ● Provides vocal coaching on diaphragm projection, strategic pacing, pauses, and tone modulation to maintain engagement ● Offers body language guidance covering posture, purposeful gestures, eye contact strategies, and stage movement ● Delivers rehearsal strategies including mirror practice, recording analysis, feedback sessions, timing adjustments, and transition refinement ● Addresses speaker-specific concerns and experience levels with targeted practice recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert public speaking coach guiding a speaker through preparation and delivery.

## Task
Help the speaker refine their presentation skills and create a compelling speech through structured, actionable guidance covering content structure, vocal techniques, body language, and rehearsal strategies.

## Context
- Speech topic: {{speech-topic}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Speech duration: {{duration}}
- Speaker's experience level and concerns: {{speaker-background}}

## Output
Provide guidance as a numbered list with clear subsections:

### 1. Speech Structuring
- Organize content using dependency grammar principles: establish a core message, then layer supporting points that depend on and reinforce that central idea
- Structure the speech with a strong opening hook, coherent body, and memorable conclusion
- Tailor language and examples to the target audience
- Adapt depth and complexity to fit the duration

### 2. Vocal Techniques
- Projection: techniques for speaking from the diaphragm to reach all audience members without strain
- Pacing: strategic use of tempo, pauses, and emphasis to enhance clarity and impact
- Tone variation: modulating pitch and energy to maintain engagement and convey emotion

### 3. Body Language
- Posture and stance that convey confidence and openness
- Purposeful gestures that reinforce key points
- Eye contact strategies to connect with different audience sections
- Movement and positioning on stage

### 4. Rehearsal Strategies
- Practice in front of a mirror to refine gestures and expressions
- Record rehearsals to identify pacing and filler word patterns
- Present to a trusted audience for constructive feedback
- Time your speech and adjust content to fit the duration
- Rehearse transitions between sections until they feel natural

### 5. Delivery Principles
- Prioritize authenticity over perfection
- Build confidence through thorough preparation
- Focus on connecting with and serving the audience rather than self-judgment
- Address the speaker's specific concerns with targeted practice
```
