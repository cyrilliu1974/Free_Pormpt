# Audience Analysis Prompt for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Audience Analysis Prompt for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that builds detailed target audience profiles for writers, marketers, and content strategists planning messaging and tone. This audience analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table that breaks down your target audience across four research dimensions: demographics (age, location, education, income), psychographics (values, beliefs, interests, motivations), behaviors (media habits, purchasing patterns, content engagement), and needs/wants (pain points, goals, aspirations). You provide your writing project and industry niche as variables, and the prompt instructs ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to deliver specific, actionable insights that directly inform tone, style, and messaging decisions. Use it when launching a blog series, planning a product campaign, drafting white papers, or tailoring any content to resonate with a specific group. ● Organizes qualitative and quantitative audience data into four actionable categories that map directly to content decisions ● Surfaces pain points, aspirations, and information gaps that guide topic selection and messaging priorities ● Outputs a clean markdown table format that teams can share, annotate, and reference throughout the content lifecycle ● Adapts to any industry niche or writing project, from SaaS landing pages to nonprofit fundraising appeals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert audience analyst specializing in creating detailed target audience profiles for content strategy.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive audience analysis and deliver a structured profile that examines demographics, psychographics, behaviors, and needs/wants. Investigate how these factors influence content decisions including tone, style, and messaging approach.

## Context
Project: {{writing-project}}

Industry/Niche: {{industry-niche}}

## Analysis Framework
For the specified audience, research and document:

**Demographics** – Age ranges, geographic location, education level, income brackets, occupation, family status, and other statistical characteristics

**Psychographics** – Values, beliefs, interests, lifestyle preferences, attitudes, personality traits, and motivations

**Behaviors** – Media consumption habits, purchasing patterns, content engagement preferences, decision-making processes, and online/offline activities

**Needs/Wants** – Pain points, aspirations, goals, challenges, desired outcomes, information gaps, and unmet requirements

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with four columns (Demographics | Psychographics | Behaviors | Needs/Wants). Provide specific, actionable insights in each cell that will inform content creation decisions.
```
