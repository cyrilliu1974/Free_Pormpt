# Business Plan Writer Prompt for New Ventures

## 簡介

The Business Plan Writer Prompt for New Ventures is a free AI prompt that produces complete, investor-ready business plans for entrepreneurs launching new businesses. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through every critical section: executive summary with mission and objectives, market analysis identifying target customers and competitors, marketing and sales strategies, financial projections including income statements and cash flow forecasts, and an action plan with specific milestones and timelines. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by instructing the AI to act as an expert business strategist, ensuring the output covers all essential components in a structured markdown format with clear headings and bullet points. Real-world use cases include preparing pitch decks for investors, applying for small business loans, planning product launches, and creating internal strategic roadmaps. This prompt is designed for entrepreneurs, startup founders, and small business owners who need a professional business plan framework without hiring expensive consultants. ● Produces executive summaries that clearly articulate business concept, mission statement, and measurable objectives ● Delivers market analysis with target customer segmentation, competitive positioning, and industry trend assessment ● Generates realistic financial projections with income statements, cash flow forecasts, and break-even calculations tied to implementation timelines ● Creates actionable milestone roadmaps with specific deadlines and success metrics for each business phase ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in comprehensive business plan development for new ventures.

## Task
Create a detailed, well-structured business plan covering all essential aspects of a new venture. The plan must include:

- **Executive Summary**: Concise overview of the business concept, mission statement, and key objectives
- **Market Analysis**: Target customer segments, competitive landscape, and industry trends
- **Marketing & Sales Strategy**: Tactics and channels aligned with business goals and audience
- **Financial Projections**: Income statements, cash flow forecasts, and break-even analysis
- **Action Plan**: Specific milestones and implementation timelines

## Context
{{business-details}}

## Output
Deliver the business plan in structured markdown format with clear ## headings for each major section. Use bullet points for key details within sections. Ensure financial projections include realistic assumptions and are tied to the stated timeline. Tailor all strategies to the specific target market and unique selling proposition provided.
```
