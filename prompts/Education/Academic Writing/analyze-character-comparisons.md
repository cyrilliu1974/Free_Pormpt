# Character Comparison Analysis for Literary Study

## 簡介

The Character Comparison Analysis for Literary Study is a free AI prompt that produces detailed comparative analyses of two characters from any book, examining their individual traits, shared qualities, relationship dynamics, and thematic roles. This character comparison prompt for ChatGPT guides literary analysts through a systematic examination using a Venn diagram structure that maps unique and overlapping characteristics, then explores how each character functions within the narrative and what their similarities and differences reveal about the work's deeper themes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering psychological depth and narrative insight that goes beyond surface observations. Writers, students, educators, and book club facilitators reach for this prompt when preparing literary discussions, writing essays, or teaching character development and thematic interpretation. ● Maps unique and shared character traits in an organized Venn diagram format for clarity ● Examines relationship dynamics between characters and how they influence each other's arcs ● Analyzes each character's narrative role and function within the book's structure ● Interprets the thematic significance of both similarities and contrasts between characters ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literary analyst specializing in character comparison and thematic interpretation.

## Task
Compare and contrast two major characters from {{book-title}}, examining their traits, roles, relationship dynamics, and thematic significance. Provide nuanced insights that go beyond surface-level observations.

## Context
Characters to analyze:
- {{character-1}}
- {{character-2}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structured format:

**Venn Diagram: {{character-1}} vs. {{character-2}} from {{book-title}}**

{{character-1}} (Unique Traits)
- [Trait 1]
- [Trait 2]
- [Trait 3]

                        Shared Traits
                        - [Trait 1]
                        - [Trait 2]
                        - [Trait 3]

{{character-2}} (Unique Traits)
- [Trait 1]
- [Trait 2]
- [Trait 3]

**Relationship Dynamics:**
- [Key dynamic 1]
- [Key dynamic 2]
- [Key dynamic 3]

**Character Roles:**
- {{character-1}}'s role: [Description]
- {{character-2}}'s role: [Description]

**Thematic Significance:**
- Similarities: [What their shared traits reveal about the work's themes]
- Differences: [What their contrasts illuminate about the work's themes]

Focus on psychological depth, narrative function, and how each character serves the book's larger meaning.
```
