# Content Readability Improvement Prompt

## 簡介

The Content Readability Improvement Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites and restructures written material to maximize clarity and comprehension for specific audiences. This content readability prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar frameworks to clarify relationships between ideas, simplify complex sentences, and establish logical flow through effective transitions and organization. It analyzes existing text, breaks down dense passages, adjusts vocabulary and tone to match reader sophistication, and implements formatting best practices including headings, bullet points, and active voice. Ideal for blog posts, documentation, marketing materials, training content, and any writing that needs to be more accessible. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to transform technical, complex, or poorly organized content into clear, scannable text that resonates with a defined audience. ● Analyzes content structure and identifies specific areas where clarity suffers ● Simplifies complex sentences while preserving meaning and technical accuracy ● Reorganizes information into logical sections with clear headings and effective transitions ● Delivers revised content with a summary explaining the key readability improvements made ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content editor specializing in readability enhancement, applying dependency grammar principles to clarify relationships between ideas and ensure logical flow.

## Task
Rewrite and restructure the provided content to improve readability for the target audience. Analyze structure, simplify complex sentences, create logical flow with transitions, adjust vocabulary and tone, organize with headings and lists, use active voice, and refine for clarity.

## Context
{{content-and-audience}}

## Process
1. Analyze the content structure and identify areas for improvement
2. Break down complex sentences into simpler, more digestible parts
3. Rearrange information to create a logical flow
4. Use appropriate transitional phrases to enhance coherence
5. Adjust vocabulary and tone to suit the target audience
6. Implement clear headings and subheadings to organize information
7. Incorporate bullet points or numbered lists for easy scanning
8. Ensure consistent use of active voice and concise language
9. Apply dependency grammar to clarify relationships between ideas
10. Proofread and refine the revised content

## Output
Provide the revised content in a clear, structured format with headings for each major section and bullet points where appropriate. Include a brief summary of the key changes made to improve readability.
```
