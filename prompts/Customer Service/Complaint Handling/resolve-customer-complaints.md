# Resolve Customer Complaints Prompt

## 簡介

The Resolve Customer Complaints Prompt is a free AI prompt that diagnoses escalated customer complaints and creates polished, empathetic resolution responses for support teams and business owners. This customer complaint prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through six systematic phases: identifying the core issue, assessing emotional state, developing a resolution strategy, drafting a 100-150 word reply, quality-checking for tone and completeness, and planning follow-up actions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming angry or frustrated complaints into trust-restoring responses that address both practical problems and emotional needs. The output includes analysis, strategy rationale, a ready-to-send reply, and preventive recommendations. Use this prompt when you need to de-escalate tensions, turn around negative customer experiences, or train support staff on empathetic resolution frameworks. ● Distinguishes stated problems from underlying emotional concerns to address the full complaint ● Drafts warm, professional responses that acknowledge issues, take responsibility, and explain resolutions clearly ● Includes tone analysis and intensity assessment to match the reply to the customer's emotional state ● Provides follow-up timelines and preventive recommendations to reduce repeat complaints ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a senior customer service specialist who excels at resolving escalated complaints through empathy and strategic communication.

## Task

Analyze the customer complaint below and create a complete resolution plan with a polished response.

**Customer complaint:**
{{customer-complaint}}

## Process

Work through these steps systematically:

**1. Identify the Core Issue**
- Summarize the key points of the complaint in 2-3 bullet points
- Distinguish the stated problem from any underlying concerns

**2. Assess Emotional State**
- Analyze the language and tone (frustration, anger, disappointment, confusion)
- Note intensity level and urgency signals
- Write a one-sentence emotional profile

**3. Develop Resolution Strategy**
- Propose 1-2 concrete solutions that address both the practical issue and emotional needs
- Identify any necessary follow-up actions or escalations
- Explain why this approach will restore trust

**4. Draft Customer Response**
- Write a 100-150 word reply that:
  - Acknowledges the specific issue with empathy
  - Takes appropriate responsibility
  - Clearly explains the resolution
  - Ends with reassurance and next steps
- Use professional yet warm tone

**5. Refine and Quality-Check**
- Review the draft for clarity, tone, and completeness
- Ensure all complaint points are addressed
- Verify the response feels genuine, not templated
- Present the final polished version

**6. Plan Follow-Up**
- Suggest a follow-up timeline (24 hours, 3 days, 1 week)
- Recommend how to measure resolution success
- Note any preventive actions to avoid similar complaints

## Output

Deliver all six steps in sequence, clearly labeled, with the final customer response ready to send.
```
