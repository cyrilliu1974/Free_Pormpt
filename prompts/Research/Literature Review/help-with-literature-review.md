# Literature Review Summary Generator for Research

## 簡介

The Literature Review Summary Generator for Research is a free AI prompt that helps academic researchers and students produce thorough literature reviews by identifying, analyzing, and synthesizing recent studies on any research topic. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to search for relevant academic papers published within the last five years, extract key information about objectives and methodologies, compare findings across multiple studies, and deliver a structured document complete with proper citations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce reviews that include an introduction to the topic's significance, detailed study summaries, trend analysis, and suggestions for future research directions. Researchers use it to accelerate the early phases of thesis work, grant proposals, systematic reviews, and journal article preparation. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly understand the current state of research in a field, identify knowledge gaps, or build the foundation for new scholarly work without manually reading dozens of papers from scratch. ● Searches and selects relevant studies from the past five years across major academic databases ● Extracts and organizes research objectives, methodologies, main findings, and implications for each study ● Compares findings across studies to identify patterns, contradictions, and emerging themes in the field ● Delivers a complete literature review document with introduction, study summaries, trend analysis, future directions, and proper citations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced academic researcher conducting a comprehensive literature review.

## Task
Review and summarize key findings from recent studies on the specified research topic. Identify the most relevant and latest research papers, distill significant information, and synthesize these into a clear, comprehensive summary that includes main objectives, methodologies, findings, and implications. Provide an overview of the current state of research and cite all sources appropriately.

## Context
Research topic and scope:
{{research-topic}}

Focus on studies published within the last 5 years from academic databases such as PubMed, Google Scholar, and Scopus to ensure current findings.

## Process
1. **Identify and Select Relevant Studies**: Search for the most recent and relevant research papers using keywords related to the topic for comprehensive coverage.

2. **Read and Extract Key Information**: Thoroughly review selected studies, focusing on objectives, methodologies, results, and conclusions. Note main findings and significant approaches.

3. **Analyze and Synthesize Findings**: Compare and contrast findings across studies to identify patterns, discrepancies, and emerging themes. Evaluate methodologies to understand research strengths and limitations.

4. **Summarize the Research**: Structure your summary with:
   - Introduction providing topic overview and significance
   - For each study: research objectives, methodology, main findings, and implications
   - Discussion of overall trends and notable advancements in the field

5. **Conclude with Current State and Future Directions**: Summarize the current research landscape and suggest areas for future investigation or practical applications.

6. **Cite All Sources**: Provide proper citations for all studies, enabling readers to locate sources for further reading.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive literature review document that includes:
- Introduction to the topic and its significance
- Summaries of selected studies with their objectives, methodologies, findings, and implications
- Analysis of overall trends and advancements in the research
- Conclusion on the current state of research with suggestions for future directions
- Proper citations for all referenced studies
```
