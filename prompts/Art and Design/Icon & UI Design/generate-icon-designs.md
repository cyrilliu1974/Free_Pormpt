# Icon Design Generator for Minimalist Flat Style

## 簡介

The Icon Design Generator for Minimalist Flat Style is a free AI prompt that creates clean, two-color icon designs in a minimalist flat aesthetic for designers, developers, and brand teams. This icon design prompt for Midjourney and Flux produces square (1:1) icons with a white background, combining your chosen subject with a primary and secondary color. You specify the icon subject, select two colors, and the prompt outputs a minimalist flat-style icon suitable for app interfaces, websites, branding systems, and design libraries. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models that support aspect ratio and style parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, modern icon designs that fit minimalist UI kits, brand guidelines, or digital product interfaces. ● Produces minimalist flat-style icons with a subject of your choice and a two-color palette you define. ● Outputs square (1:1) icons on a white background, ideal for app UI, web design, and brand asset libraries. ● Works with any subject - objects, concepts, actions - and any primary and secondary color combination. ● Compatible with Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other AI image generators that accept style and ratio flags. ## Prompt

```
{{subject}} icon design, minimalist flat style, {{primary-color}} and {{secondary-color}}, white background, --ar 1:1
```
