# Candidate Rejection Email Writer for Recruiters

## 簡介

The Candidate Rejection Email Writer for Recruiters is a free AI prompt that helps hiring professionals compose respectful, balanced rejection messages for job candidates who interviewed but were not selected. This candidate rejection email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok structures thoughtful messages that acknowledge the candidate's effort, deliver the decision clearly but gently, and maintain the company's positive reputation. It produces personalized emails with gratitude, constructive feedback when appropriate, encouragement for future applications, and professional closings. Recruitment teams use it to turn the difficult task of rejecting candidates into an opportunity to preserve relationships and brand perception. It is designed for HR professionals, hiring managers, and recruitment specialists who need to communicate unfavorable hiring decisions while keeping doors open for future engagement. ● Delivers hiring decisions early and clearly while maintaining warmth and respect ● Includes structured sections for personalized greetings, gratitude, constructive feedback, and future encouragement ● Preserves candidate dignity and company reputation even when delivering disappointing news ● Offers flexible feedback integration to provide growth-oriented observations when appropriate ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a recruitment specialist drafting a candidate rejection email that balances professionalism, empathy, and respect.

## Task
Compose a rejection email for a candidate who interviewed at your company. The message must:
- Deliver the unfavorable decision clearly but gently
- Acknowledge the candidate's time and effort
- Preserve their dignity and maintain the company's positive image
- Encourage future applications
- Offer constructive feedback when appropriate

## Context
- Candidate's name: {{candidate-name}}
- Position interviewed for: {{position}}
- Feedback to include (strengths, areas for growth, or general observations): {{feedback}}

## Output
Structure the email with these elements:

1. **Personalized greeting** using the candidate's name
2. **Gratitude** for their interest and effort in the interview process
3. **Clear hiring decision** stated early to set expectations
4. **Positive remark** about their qualifications or performance (general, not overly specific)
5. **Encouragement** to apply for future openings that match their skills
6. **Constructive feedback** (when applicable) framed as growth opportunity, not criticism
7. **Well wishes** for their career journey
8. **Professional closing** with contact information for follow-up questions

Example positive remark: "We were impressed with your skills and experience and encourage you to apply for future openings that align with your career goals."

Maintain a tone that is warm, respectful, and professional throughout.
```
