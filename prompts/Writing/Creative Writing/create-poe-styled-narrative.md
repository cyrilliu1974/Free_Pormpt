# Poe-Style Gothic Narrative Generator

## 簡介

The Poe-Style Gothic Narrative Generator is a free AI prompt that produces complete fictional stories written in the distinctive voice and method of Edgar Allan Poe for creative writers, authors, and literature enthusiasts. This Poe-style narrative prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft original fiction that authentically mirrors Poe's psychological complexity, atmospheric tension, and poetic prose. The prompt instructs the model to build psychologically nuanced characters, establish dark and immersive settings through vivid sensory detail, employ strategic foreshadowing and suspenseful pacing, and explore themes of obsession, guilt, moral ambiguity, and inner darkness. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming a single theme or plot idea into a fully realized gothic narrative with a haunting, thought-provoking conclusion. Writers turn to this prompt when they want to experiment with classic literary horror techniques, study Poe's narrative structure through practice, or produce atmospheric short fiction for creative projects, anthologies, or portfolio pieces. ● Builds complete narratives with dark atmospheric openings, complex characters, and suspenseful pacing ● Incorporates symbolic imagery, rhetorical devices, and controlled poetic language characteristic of Poe's prose ● Explores psychological themes such as obsession, guilt, and moral ambiguity through character-driven plots ● Delivers ambiguous or unsettling endings that leave a lingering sense of unease ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert creative writer with deep knowledge of Edgar Allan Poe's literary style, narrative techniques, and thematic preoccupations.

## Task

Compose an original fictional narrative that authentically captures Poe's distinctive voice and method. Write a complete story based on the theme or plot provided below.

## Context

**Theme or plot to explore:**
{{theme-or-plot}}

## Output

Deliver a complete narrative that demonstrates:

- **Opening:** A dark, atmospheric beginning that immediately establishes mood and tone
- **Characters:** Psychologically complex figures whose inner conflicts drive the plot; prioritize depth over action
- **Atmosphere:** Vivid sensory details, symbolic imagery, and immersive descriptions that create a haunting environment
- **Pacing:** Suspenseful rhythm with strategic foreshadowing to build and sustain tension
- **Themes:** Exploration of psychological darkness, moral ambiguity, obsession, guilt, or other hallmarks of Poe's work
- **Language:** Poetic but controlled prose—employ rhetorical devices to enhance mood without overwhelming the narrative
- **Conclusion:** A haunting, ambiguous, or thought-provoking ending that resonates beyond the final line

Avoid overused gothic clichés. Favor inventive, psychologically nuanced approaches to the macabre. The story should feel unmistakably Poe-esque in voice, structure, and atmosphere, leaving the reader with a lingering sense of unease.
```
