# Social Media Impact on Sales Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Impact on Sales Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that quantifies the relationship between social media engagement and revenue outcomes for businesses and marketers. This social media analytics prompt for ChatGPT maps engagement metrics - likes, shares, comments, click-through rates, reach, and impressions - to actual sales performance across your platforms. It identifies which social signals show the strongest correlation with revenue, quantifies the relative impact of each platform, and accounts for audience behavior patterns and platform-specific dynamics. The output includes a structured data table, key findings highlighting the strongest correlations, and prioritized recommendations to optimize your social strategy for sales growth. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond vanity metrics and understand which social media activities actually drive revenue, or when you're allocating budget across platforms and need data-backed guidance. ● Maps platform-specific engagement signals (likes, shares, comments, CTR, reach, impressions) directly to sales outcomes ● Identifies leading versus lagging indicators so you know which metrics predict future revenue ● Quantifies the relative impact of each social platform on your bottom line ● Delivers prioritized, actionable recommendations based on the metrics with the highest sales correlation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media analytics expert specializing in quantifying the relationship between engagement metrics and revenue outcomes.

## Task
Analyze the correlation between social media engagement and sales performance. Examine how different platforms and their specific engagement signals drive revenue, then deliver actionable recommendations to optimize social strategy for sales growth.

## Context
Business and performance data:
{{business-and-audience-context}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Map engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments, click-through rates, reach, impressions) to sales outcomes for each platform
- Identify which signals show the strongest correlation with revenue
- Quantify the relative impact of each platform on sales
- Account for audience behavior patterns and platform-specific dynamics
- Highlight leading vs. lagging indicators

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

1. **Data table** in markdown format with three columns:
   - SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORM
   - ENGAGEMENT METRIC
   - SALES IMPACT

2. **Key Findings** (3-5 bullet points summarizing the strongest correlations and patterns)

3. **Recommendations** (prioritized action items to increase sales through social media, based on the metrics with highest impact)
```
