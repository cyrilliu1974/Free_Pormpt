# Content Channel Strategy Analyzer

## 簡介

The Content Channel Strategy Analyzer is a free AI prompt that evaluates and recommends the best content distribution channels for your specific business, audience, and goals. This content channel selection prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context, audience demographics, content objectives, and resource constraints to deliver a prioritized table of 5-7 channels - each with actionable pros and cons tailored to your situation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, assessing factors like audience behavior alignment, content format fit, resource requirements, competitive saturation, and ROI potential. Marketing teams use it to decide whether to invest in LinkedIn, YouTube, email newsletters, podcasts, or other platforms based on strategic fit rather than guesswork. This prompt is for content marketers, digital strategists, and business owners who need data-informed guidance on where to publish and promote their content for maximum reach and engagement. ● Delivers 5-7 prioritized channels with 3-4 specific pros and cons per channel, not generic advice ● Evaluates audience behavior alignment, content format suitability, and competitive opportunity gaps ● Accounts for your budget constraints, existing capabilities, and long-term scalability ● Produces a markdown table and strategic rationale ready to share with stakeholders or integrate into planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in content channel selection and audience reach optimization.

## Task
Analyze and recommend the most effective content channels for the business described below. Evaluate each channel based on audience demographics, reach potential, content format alignment, engagement patterns, and expected ROI.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

{{content-goals}}

{{constraints}}

## Analysis Framework
For each recommended channel, assess:
- Alignment with target audience behavior and preferences
- Suitability for the content formats and goals specified
- Resource requirements relative to budget and current capabilities
- Competitive saturation and opportunity gaps
- Scalability and long-term strategic fit

## Output
Provide a brief introduction (2-3 paragraphs) explaining the strategic rationale behind your channel recommendations and how they address the specific business context.

Then present your recommendations in a markdown table:

| Channel | Pros | Cons |
|---------|------|------|

Include 5-7 channels ranked by strategic priority. Each pros/cons entry should contain 3-4 specific, actionable points directly relevant to the business context provided.
```
