# 5-Day Email Course Builder for Engagement and Sales

## 簡介

The 5-Day Email Course Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured, high-engagement email sequence for coaches, course creators, and digital marketers who need to educate prospects while setting up a conversion. This email course prompt for ChatGPT walks you through designing five consecutive messages: a foundation email with a quick win, three core teaching emails that deliver strategies and mindset shifts, and a final integration email that recaps the journey and introduces your paid product as the logical next step. You supply a brief describing your audience, their desired transformation, your course topic, and the offer you want to soft-pitch; the prompt outputs subject lines, compelling hooks, 3-5 actionable points per email, recaps, key takeaways, and calls-to-action in a warm, conversational tone. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to nurture cold leads, onboard new subscribers with real value, or launch a product by teaching first and selling second. ● Outputs five complete emails (400-500 words each) with curiosity-driven subject lines, story-driven hooks, and skimmable formatting. ● Balances substantial teaching value in emails 1-4 with a soft-pitch framework in email 5 so the paid offer feels like an upgrade, not a surprise. ● Enforces a conversational, story-rich tone with concrete examples, short paragraphs, and markdown structure. ● Includes daily CTAs that build anticipation and a final integration roadmap that synthesizes all lessons into an implementation plan. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist specializing in educational course sequences.

## Task
Design a complete 5-day email course that educates the audience, builds engagement, and positions a paid offer as the natural next step.

## Context
{{course-brief}}

Include: target audience, the transformation or outcome they want, the course topic, and the paid offer you'll soft-pitch in email 5.

## Structure

**Email 1: Foundation**
- Set expectations for the 5-day journey
- Deliver one immediate, actionable quick win
- Build credibility and excitement

**Emails 2-4: Core Teaching**
Each email focuses on one key strategy, technique, or mindset shift with 3-5 actionable points.

**Email 5: Integration & Next Step**
- Synthesize all four lessons
- Provide an implementation roadmap
- Naturally introduce the paid offer as the logical continuation

## Output

For each email, provide:

**Subject Line:** Curiosity-driven, specific to the day's focus

**Introduction:** Compelling hook (story, question, or insight) that frames the day's topic

**Core Content:** 3-5 strategies, techniques, or insights with concrete examples

**Recap/Teaser:** Briefly tie to previous emails and hint at what's coming

**Key Takeaway:** One-sentence summary of the email's core lesson

**CTA:** Prompt one small action and build anticipation for the next email

## Style Requirements

- Warm, conversational tone with storytelling and analogies
- 400-500 words per email
- Use headings and short one-sentence paragraphs for skimmability
- No exclamation points or emojis
- Markdown formatting for structure and emphasis
- Deliver substantial value in every email; the paid offer should feel like a natural upgrade, not a bait-and-switch
```
