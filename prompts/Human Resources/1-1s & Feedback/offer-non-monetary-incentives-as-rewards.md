# Non-Monetary Sales Incentive Program Builder

## 簡介

The Non-Monetary Sales Incentive Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive motivation strategies for sales organizations without financial rewards. This non-monetary incentive prompt for ChatGPT takes your organizational context - company size, industry, sales structure, budget constraints, and culture - and produces a detailed markdown table mapping 6-8 distinct incentive types to their benefits and implementation steps. It covers recognition programs, professional development opportunities, autonomy grants, work-life balance initiatives, and team-building activities. The output is practical and immediately actionable, designed for HR leaders, sales managers, and people operations teams who need to boost performance within budget constraints. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces a three-column table showing incentive type, team benefits, and step-by-step implementation guidance ● Covers six to eight diverse motivation dimensions including recognition, autonomy, development, and work-life balance ● Aligns recommendations with your specific company culture, team structure, and budget realities ● Delivers actionable strategies HR and sales leaders can implement immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources strategist specializing in non-monetary motivation programs for sales organizations.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive non-monetary incentive program that boosts sales team motivation and performance without relying on financial rewards. Create a strategy that is innovative, practical, and aligned with the organization's context.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

(Include: company size, industry, sales team structure, budget constraints for implementation, and company culture)

## Output
Provide your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| INCENTIVE TYPE | BENEFIT TO SALES TEAM | IMPLEMENTATION STEPS |
|----------------|----------------------|---------------------|

Each row should present:
- A distinct non-monetary incentive
- Specific benefits it delivers to the sales team
- Clear, actionable implementation steps

Include at least 6-8 diverse incentive types covering recognition, development, autonomy, work-life balance, and team-building dimensions.
```
