# Market Trend Analysis Prompt for Strategic Insights

## 簡介

The Market Trend Analysis Prompt for Strategic Insights is a free AI prompt that transforms raw industry trends into a structured opportunity-challenge matrix for business strategists, market analysts, and decision-makers. This market trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT systematically evaluates 5-8 significant industry trends across five dimensions: consumer behavior shifts, technological advancements, economic indicators, competitive landscape changes, and regulatory impacts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a markdown table that pairs each trend with both the opportunity it unlocks (market expansion, efficiency gains, differentiation) and the challenge it presents (adoption barriers, cost pressures, competitive threats). Analysts use it to brief executives, inform strategic planning cycles, and identify where to invest or defend market position. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from trend observation to strategic action, when preparing board presentations, or when evaluating how macro forces will reshape your competitive position. ● Maps each trend to concrete business opportunities and competitive challenges in a scannable table format ● Evaluates trends across consumer behavior, technology, economics, competition, and regulation for comprehensive coverage ● Tailors insights to your specific industry focus and business profile for relevance ● Delivers 5-8 prioritized trends representing the most significant forces shaping your market currently ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in identifying strategic opportunities and competitive challenges from industry trends.

## Task
Analyze current market trends for the specified industry and deliver actionable insights in a structured format. For each significant trend, identify both the opportunity it creates and the challenge it poses for businesses operating in this space.

## Context
Industry and scope: {{industry-and-focus}}
Business profile: {{business-profile}}

Consider multiple dimensions when evaluating trends:
- Consumer behavior shifts and demand patterns
- Technological advancements and digital transformation
- Economic indicators and market conditions
- Competitive landscape and industry consolidation
- Regulatory changes and policy impacts

Evaluate how each trend translates into concrete opportunities (market expansion, efficiency gains, differentiation) and challenges (adoption barriers, cost pressures, competitive threats) relevant to the business profile provided.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Trend | Opportunity | Challenge |
|-------|-------------|----------|

Each row should cover one distinct trend with concise, actionable insights. Include 5-8 trends that represent the most significant forces shaping the industry currently.
```
