# Facebook Video Script Generator for Products

## 簡介

The Facebook Video Script Generator for Products is a free AI prompt that writes attention-grabbing social media video scripts optimized for Facebook's autoplay and mobile-first format. This Facebook video script prompt for ChatGPT structures your content using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow. It analyzes your product's unique selling points against your target audience's pain points, then crafts a hook designed to capture attention in the first 3 seconds - critical for Facebook's autoplay environment. The prompt delivers scripts in a three-column format (Visual | Spoken Dialogue | On-Screen Text) with timing cues, typically 30-60 seconds in length, and integrates storytelling elements, emotional appeals, and visual directions that align with mobile viewing behavior. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing teams, social media managers, and content creators use this prompt when they need conversion-focused video scripts that balance product promotion with audience engagement. ● Outputs three-column scripts (Visual, Spoken Dialogue, On-Screen Text) with timing cues for seamless handoff to video editors ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure every sentence flows logically and supports the core message ● Tailors hooks, benefits, and calls-to-action to your specific product, audience demographics, and brand voice ● Optimizes content for Facebook's autoplay behavior and the first 3-second attention window ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video script writer specializing in social media marketing content.

## Task
Write a compelling Facebook video script that promotes the product or service, captures attention immediately, and drives viewer action. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-or-service}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique selling points: {{usp}}

Brand voice and tone: {{brand-voice}}

Analyze the product features and how they address the target audience's preferences and pain points. Craft a hook that grabs attention in the first 3 seconds, develop a clear message highlighting tangible benefits, and close with this call-to-action: {{cta}}

Incorporate storytelling elements, emotional appeals, and visual cues optimized for Facebook's autoplay format and mobile-first viewing behavior.

## Output
Deliver the script in three-column format:

**VISUAL** | **SPOKEN DIALOGUE** | **ON-SCREEN TEXT**

Include timing cues where critical (e.g., "0:00-0:03"). Write 30-60 seconds of content unless the product complexity requires more.
```
