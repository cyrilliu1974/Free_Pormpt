# Streetwear Salon Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Streetwear Salon Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic fashion portraits of subjects styled in contemporary streetwear within professional salon environments for beauty brands, fashion marketers, and lifestyle businesses. This streetwear portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates medium-shot compositions featuring a seated subject in an oversized t-shirt with customizable text, distressed jeans, high-top sneakers, and accessories including sunglasses and jewelry, all set against a detailed salon backdrop with mirrors, product shelves, and warm lighting. The prompt delivers magazine-quality imagery with high-detail rendering of clothing textures, skin tones, and urban fashion aesthetics, making it ideal for social media content, website hero images, lookbook photography, and promotional campaigns that require authentic street-style visuals without studio costs.
