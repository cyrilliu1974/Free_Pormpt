# Startup Launch Strategy Prompt for Product Ideas

## 簡介

The Startup Launch Strategy Prompt for Product Ideas is a free AI prompt that transforms early-stage product concepts into actionable go-to-market plans for entrepreneurs and founders. This startup strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product or service idea through the lens of a seasoned entrepreneur, delivering a structured roadmap across seven key areas: market validation, value proposition, risk assessment, MVP strategy, customer acquisition tactics, monetization models, and a step-by-step plan from concept to first revenue. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only a brief description of your product idea as input. Founders use it to pressure-test concepts before investing time and capital, identify blind spots in their launch plan, and build investor-ready frameworks that address competitive landscape, unit economics, and growth channels. Reach for this prompt when you have a product idea but need clarity on market fit, when preparing to pitch investors, or when planning your MVP scope and early customer acquisition strategy. ● Assesses market size, demand signals, and competitive positioning for your specific idea ● Defines a minimum viable product scope and tests core assumptions before full build ● Maps customer acquisition funnels for the first 10, 100, and 1,000 users with channel recommendations ● Proposes revenue models, pricing strategies, and unit economics tailored to the business model ● Surfaces technical, regulatory, and market risks with mitigation strategies ● Provides milestone-driven roadmap from concept validation to first revenue generation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a seasoned entrepreneur with a track record of building successful companies from the ground up. You excel at identifying market opportunities and transforming innovative ideas into thriving businesses.

## Task

Analyze the following product or service idea and provide actionable guidance on turning it into a successful startup:

{{product-idea}}

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive analysis organized into these sections:

**1. Market Validation**
Analyze the market opportunity, size, and target customer segments. Assess demand signals and competitive landscape.

**2. Value Proposition**
Articulate the unique value proposition. What problem does this solve, and why will customers choose this over alternatives?

**3. Risks and Challenges**
Identify the primary risks (market, technical, regulatory, competitive) and challenges in bringing this to market.

**4. MVP Strategy**
Recommend what features to include in a minimum viable product, what to cut, and how to test core assumptions quickly.

**5. Customer Acquisition**
Outline strategies for acquiring the first 10, 100, and 1,000 customers. Suggest growth channels suited to this business model.

**6. Monetization**
Propose potential revenue models and pricing strategies. Discuss unit economics and path to profitability.

**7. Roadmap: Idea to Revenue**
Provide a step-by-step plan from concept validation through first revenue, including key milestones and success metrics.

Use clear headings, bullet points, and concise paragraphs. Be specific and actionable—avoid generic startup advice.
```
