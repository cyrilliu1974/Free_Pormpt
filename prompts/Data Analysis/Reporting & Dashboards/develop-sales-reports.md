# Sales Performance Report Generator

## 簡介

The Sales Performance Report Generator is a free AI prompt that analyzes sales data and produces structured performance reports with actionable insights for sales analysts, managers, and business owners. This sales performance report prompt for ChatGPT accepts a date range, product or service list, and business context, then outputs a markdown table showing revenue, units sold, and key insights for each offering, followed by strategic recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming raw sales figures into clear performance summaries that reveal patterns, identify top and underperforming products, and guide decision-making. Use cases include monthly sales reviews, quarterly business reporting, product portfolio analysis, and board-level performance summaries. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn transactional sales data into executive-ready reports that highlight trends, compare product performance, and deliver improvement opportunities without manual spreadsheet work. ● Outputs a markdown table with date range, product or service, revenue, units sold, and key insights columns for side-by-side performance comparison. ● Calculates critical financial metrics and surfaces trends and patterns that explain revenue changes or product momentum. ● Generates a bullet-point list of overall recommendations aligned with your specific business context and goals. ● Accepts custom date ranges, product or service names, and contextual details to tailor the analysis to any industry or sales cycle. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales analyst producing comprehensive performance reports with actionable insights.

## Task
Analyze sales data and deliver findings in a structured table format that reveals performance, trends, and improvement opportunities.

## Context
**Date range:** {{date-range}}

**Products/services:** {{products-services}}

**Business context:** {{business-context}}

Focus your analysis on identifying key performance metrics, calculating revenue and units sold for each offering, detecting significant trends and patterns, and formulating recommendations aligned with the business context.

## Output
Present your analysis as:

1. A markdown table with columns: DATE RANGE | PRODUCT/SERVICE | REVENUE | UNITS SOLD | KEY INSIGHTS
2. A bullet-point list of overall recommendations based on the analysis
```
