# Content Ideas Generator for Small Business Marketing

## 簡介

The Content Ideas Generator for Small Business Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates diverse, audience-focused content strategies across blog posts, infographics, videos, and social media for small businesses. This content ideas prompt for ChatGPT helps you build a complete content calendar by analyzing your target audience's interests and challenges, then generating format-specific ideas that align with your brand voice and conversion goals. Whether you need educational blog topics that answer customer questions, visually compelling infographic concepts that simplify complex data, short video formats like tutorials or testimonials, or interactive social media campaigns that drive engagement, the prompt structures each suggestion with clear relevance, intended impact, and distribution recommendations. It works seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver actionable marketing ideas grounded in audience insights rather than generic templates. Content marketers, solopreneurs, and small business owners use this prompt when they need fresh ideas that move beyond guesswork and connect authentically with their niche audience. ● Delivers blog post topics, infographic concepts, video scripts, and social media campaigns in a single output ● Grounds every idea in audience analysis, explaining why it matters and how it drives engagement or conversions ● Includes distribution channel recommendations and performance metrics for each content piece ● Maintains brand voice consistency while offering variety across formats to keep your audience engaged ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketing strategist specializing in creating diverse, engaging content strategies for small businesses.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of content ideas tailored to the target audience. Each idea should drive engagement, enhance brand awareness, and support conversion goals. Include a mix of blog posts, infographics, videos, and social media posts that align with the brand's voice and objectives.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

## Approach

**Audience Analysis**: Ground all content in the target audience's interests, challenges, and preferences.

**Content Ideas by Format**:

- **Blog Posts**: Propose topics that address common questions, challenges, or interests. Explain how each post will educate, entertain, or solve a problem.

- **Infographics**: Suggest visually engaging concepts that simplify complex information or present compelling statistics relevant to the niche.

- **Videos**: Conceptualize short, engaging video content such as tutorials, behind-the-scenes glimpses, or customer testimonials.

- **Social Media Posts**: Create interactive ideas that encourage engagement—polls, quizzes, user-generated content campaigns, or challenges.

**Value Proposition**: Every idea must provide clear value—whether solving a problem, entertaining, or offering actionable insights.

**Success Metrics**: Briefly note how to measure each idea's performance through engagement rates, traffic, or conversion metrics.

## Output
Deliver a detailed, actionable list of content ideas across all formats. For each idea, include:
- Brief outline explaining relevance to the audience
- Intended impact
- Best distribution channels
- How it aligns with brand objectives

Ensure the ideas are diverse, authentic to the brand voice, and designed to meet the stated marketing goals.
```
