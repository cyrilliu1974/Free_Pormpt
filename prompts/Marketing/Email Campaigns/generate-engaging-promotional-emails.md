# Promotional Email Generator for Sales and Product Launches

## 簡介

The Promotional Email Generator for Sales and Product Launches is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused marketing emails for e-commerce businesses, product marketers, and email campaign managers. This promotional email prompt for ChatGPT structures each message with a proven conversion framework: attention-grabbing subject lines, personalized greetings, three-benefit product introductions, detailed sale terms, strategic urgency through scarcity and time constraints, and action-driving CTAs. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your campaign details and outputting a complete, ready-to-send email with preview text, formatted discount lists, and placeholders for personalization tokens. Marketers use it to launch seasonal sales, announce new product drops, or re-engage dormant subscribers with limited-time offers. Reach for this prompt when you need to write promotional emails quickly without sacrificing persuasion principles or brand voice consistency. ● Outputs a complete email template with subject line, preview text, body copy, and CTA in a single generation ● Emphasizes three-benefit product introductions and bullet-pointed discount details for scannability ● Balances urgency tactics with authenticity to avoid spam filters and reader fatigue ● Provides structured placeholders for first-name personalization and dynamic campaign variables ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer crafting persuasive promotional emails that drive conversions through clear benefits and strategic urgency.

## Task
Write a promotional email for {{campaign-details}} that follows this proven structure:

**Subject line:** An engaging subject line to capture attention  
**Preview text:** Enticing preview text to encourage opening the email  
**Greeting:** Personalized greeting addressing the recipient by first name  
**Opener:** A strong hook to draw the reader in  
**Product intro:** Introduce the product, highlighting its 3 key benefits with supporting details  
**Sale details:** Outline the sale name, start date, and top 3 discounts or offers  
**Urgency:** Create urgency using scarcity (limited stock) and time constraints  
**Call-to-action:** Clear CTA button text with the shop link  
**Closing:** Sign off with sender's first name and company name

## Context
Use proven persuasion techniques to highlight benefits and create desire. Balance urgency with authenticity—avoid spammy overuse of scarcity tactics. Ensure all product claims and sale details are accurate. Make the email visually scannable with clear sections and bullet points.

## Output
Deliver the complete email in this format:

```
Subject: [engaging subject line]

Preview Text: [enticing preview text]

Hi [first name],

[hook opener]

[Product name] delivers [benefit 1], [benefit 2], and [benefit 3].

[Additional product details]

Our [sale name] starts [start date]! Don't miss: ● [discount 1] ● [discount 2] ● [discount 3]

[Stock scarcity statement]. [Time limit statement]. Act now!

[Shop Now]([CTA link])

[Sign-off], 
[Sender name] 
[Company name]
```
```
