# Target Audience Identification Prompt for Social Media

## 簡介

The Target Audience Identification Prompt for Social Media is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive audience profiles and tailored engagement strategies for marketers, strategists, and business owners. By feeding your topic and current situation into this target audience prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, you receive a full market research analysis, 2-4 detailed customer personas with psychographic and behavioral data, a platform-specific content strategy, and a measurement framework with KPIs. The prompt walks through competitor analysis, demographic mapping, content format preferences, optimal posting schedules, and advertising targeting parameters - translating raw data into an actionable social media plan. Use it when launching a campaign, entering a new market, or refining your content strategy to match real audience behaviors rather than assumptions. ● Conducts systematic market research by analyzing follower patterns, competitor tactics, and demographic distributions across age, geography, and income segments. ● Creates 2-4 distinct audience personas documenting interests, pain points, motivations, and preferred content formats for each segment. ● Designs a multi-channel engagement strategy with platform prioritization, content mix recommendations, posting schedules, and creative direction tailored to persona behavior. ● Outputs an advertising plan with targeting parameters, budget allocation guidance, and a monitoring dashboard defining engagement rate, reach, and conversion KPIs. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in audience identification and platform-specific engagement tactics.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive target audience profile and corresponding social media strategy for {{topic}}. Use market research methodologies, competitor analysis, and platform analytics to create actionable personas and a multi-channel engagement plan.

## Context
{{current-situation}}

## Process

**1. Conduct Market Research**
- Analyze social media analytics for current followers and engagement patterns around {{topic}}
- Review competitor profiles to identify their audience composition and successful tactics
- Map demographic patterns: age ranges, gender distribution, geographic concentration, income segments

**2. Build Audience Personas**
- Create 2-4 distinct personas representing key audience segments
- For each persona, document: core interests, content preferences, pain points, motivations for engaging with {{topic}}, and typical social media behavior
- Identify which content formats (video, carousel, long-form, stories) resonate with each persona

**3. Design Engagement Strategy**
- Develop a content mix tailored to each persona: educational resources, entertainment, product/service promotion, community-building
- Determine optimal posting schedules based on audience activity patterns
- Prioritize platforms where your target audience congregates for {{topic}}-related content

**4. Plan Targeted Advertising**
- Configure audience targeting parameters using platform advertising tools (demographics, interests, behaviors)
- Design ad creative and copy that addresses specific persona needs and desires
- Set budget allocation across platforms and audience segments

**5. Establish Monitoring Framework**
- Define key metrics: engagement rate, reach, click-through rate, conversion actions, audience growth
- Schedule monthly strategy reviews to incorporate new trends, platform changes, and performance data

## Output
Deliver:
1. **Audience Profile Document** – Demographics, psychographics, behavioral patterns, and 2-4 detailed personas
2. **Social Media Strategy** – Platform selection, content calendar framework, posting frequency, content type distribution
3. **Advertising Plan** – Targeting parameters, creative direction, budget recommendations
4. **Measurement Dashboard** – KPIs to track, reporting cadence, optimization triggers

Present findings in a clear, actionable format suitable for immediate implementation.
```
