# Hiring Email Templates for Business Management Roles

## 簡介

The Hiring Email Templates for Business Management Roles is a free AI prompt that generates professional email templates for HR teams and hiring managers recruiting Business Management candidates. This hiring email template prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown table with five essential templates: Initial Job Offer, Interview Invitation, Rejection Notice, Follow-up After Interview, and Onboarding Welcome. Each template includes subject lines, body copy, purpose statements, best practices, and optimal timing guidance. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, inserting your company name, hiring manager name, and HR contact information into ready-to-send messages. Templates feature personalization markers and highlight critical information like deadlines, interview times, and salary details. Recruitment coordinators at startups, HR departments at mid-size firms, and hiring managers filling Business Management roles will save hours customizing outreach at every candidate touchpoint. ● Covers all five critical hiring stages in one structured output table with subject lines, body copy, timing, and best practices for each ● Marks personalization points and critical details so you can quickly customize candidate names, dates, salaries, and interview logistics ● Maintains a welcoming yet professional tone appropriate for Business Management candidates while keeping each message concise at 150-200 words ● Outputs a markdown table format that HR teams can copy directly into their applicant tracking system or email client ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email template generator for Business Management hiring workflows.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive table of email templates covering five key stages of the hiring process: Initial Job Offer, Interview Invitation, Rejection Notice, Follow-up After Interview, and Onboarding Welcome.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Hiring manager: {{hiring-manager-name}}
HR contact (for onboarding questions): {{hr-contact-name}}

Each template should facilitate clear, professional communication tailored to Business Management positions. Mark personalization points with 🔄 and critical information with ⚡.

## Output
Return a markdown table with these columns:

**Email Type | Subject Line | Email Body | Purpose | Best Practices | Timing to Send**

For each of the five template types, populate:

- **Subject Line**: Include 🔄 before dynamic elements (company name, position, candidate name)
- **Email Body**: Use placeholders like [CANDIDATE_NAME], [POSITION], [DATE], [SALARY], [BENEFITS], [TIME], [LOCATION], [LINK], [DEADLINE], [ONBOARDING_CONTACT]. Mark key details with ⚡. Maintain a welcoming, professional tone.
- **Purpose**: One-sentence description of the email's function
- **Best Practices**: Bullet list of 3-4 guidelines (personalization, clarity, tone, actionable requests)
- **Timing to Send**: Recommended send window relative to the preceding hiring event

**Structure requirements per template:**

1. **Initial Job Offer**: Express enthusiasm, outline start date/salary/benefits, attach offer letter, set response deadline
2. **Interview Invitation**: Thank applicant, provide date/time/location or video link, request availability confirmation
3. **Rejection Notice**: Thank candidate, clearly state decision, acknowledge their qualifications, optionally keep door open for future roles
4. **Follow-up After Interview**: Thank candidate for their time, state decision timeline, invite further questions
5. **Onboarding Welcome**: Welcome new hire, specify first-day logistics (date/time/location/contact), list required documents

Avoid overly formal language. Keep each email body concise (150-200 words).
```
