# Watercolor Sticker Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Watercolor Sticker Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hand-drawn ink and watercolor sticker designs with clean die-cut outlines for lifestyle brands, product packaging, and visual content creators. This watercolor sticker prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces illustrations with loose but confident linework in thin to medium weight, soft watercolor washes with subtle paper-like grain, and a clean white die-cut border that mimics professional sticker printing. You specify any subject and color palette, and the prompt renders a single centered object with elegant minimal shading on a pure white background. Real use cases include food and beverage branding, social media graphics, product labels, and lifestyle brand assets that need a handcrafted, artisan feel without commissioning custom illustration work. Reach for this prompt when you need sticker-style visuals that balance artistic authenticity with the clean, isolated format required for digital and print production. ● Produces hand-drawn ink linework with natural weight variation and soft watercolor washes in your chosen color palette, complete with paper-like grain texture. ● Generates a clean white die-cut outline around the entire illustration, replicating the polished appearance of premium printed stickers. ● Delivers single centered objects with balanced proportions on pure white backgrounds, ready for direct use in branding, packaging, or social media. ● Maintains an elegant, minimal aesthetic with light shading that suits modern lifestyle, food, beverage, and creative brand identities. ## Prompt
