# Minimalist Fashion Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Fashion Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 3-panel vertical fashion advertisements for premium accessories and luxury goods. It produces a cohesive 3:4 aspect ratio grid featuring a hero product shot on a minimalist pedestal, an extreme close-up of craftsmanship details, and a lifestyle editorial scene - perfect for fashion brands, e-commerce catalogs, and social media campaigns. This fashion advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by structuring lighting direction, composition rules, and typography overlay instructions into a single coordinated output, and it accepts variables for accessory type and color palette so you can adapt it to handbags, wallets, belts, or any leather goods line. Use it when you need high-resolution editorial imagery that conveys premium craftsmanship without hiring a photographer or art director. ● Delivers hero, macro, and lifestyle shots in a single unified grid layout with precise panel composition rules ● Emphasizes natural leather texture, hand-stitched seams, and metal hardware through controlled lighting and depth of field ● Includes typography overlay guidelines for tagline placement in minimalist serif or sans-serif fonts ● Accepts color-palette and accessory-type variables so you can generate grids for different product lines and seasonal collections ## Prompt

```
3-panel vertical fashion advertisement grid (3:4 aspect ratio) for a premium {{accessory-type}} in deep espresso brown genuine textured leather. Luxury editorial photography style, sophisticated and minimal.

**Panel 1 (Top):** Hero product shot on minimalist stone pedestal. Soft directional studio lighting emphasizing the clean structured silhouette, hand-stitched seams, and natural leather texture. Shallow depth of field, elegant composition.

**Panel 2 (Middle):** Extreme close-up macro of leather grain, precise stitching detail, and minimal metal hardware. Soft highlight grazing across the surface to reveal premium craftsmanship and rich texture.

**Panel 3 (Bottom):** Lifestyle shot—elegant fashion model casually carrying the product while walking through an urban street setting. Natural daylight, editorial street fashion aesthetic, product styled in real-world use.

**Color palette:** {{color-palette}}

**Typography overlay:** "Carry Confidence." in elegant minimalist serif or modern sans-serif, white or subtle gold, centered between panels or bottom overlay.

High-resolution fashion poster quality. Soft directional lighting with gentle shadows throughout. Avoid blurry details, distorted product shape, cheap plastic texture, or cluttered backgrounds.
```
