# Brand Voice and Tone Consistency Guide Builder

## 簡介

The Brand Voice and Tone Consistency Guide Builder is a free AI prompt that creates actionable brand voice documentation for content strategists, marketers, and brand managers. This brand voice consistency prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand name, target audience, core values, current communication style, and content types to produce a structured guide with vocabulary standards, sentence structure patterns, tone spectrums, and content-type-specific applications. It delivers writing guidelines, messaging frameworks, contextual flexibility rules, and before-after examples that content teams can apply immediately across blog posts, social media, emails, product descriptions, and customer service communications. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need to standardize brand communication across multiple writers, channels, or content formats while maintaining authentic personality and adaptability. ● Establishes core voice attributes, audience alignment, and personality traits derived from brand values ● Defines vocabulary preferences, sentence structure patterns, grammar standards, and tone ranges with clear boundaries ● Provides tone adaptation guidance and decision frameworks for different contexts like customer service, crisis response, and educational content ● Includes content-type-specific templates and before-after examples showing voice application across channels ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in brand voice development and consistency.

## Task
Create a comprehensive brand voice guide that maintains uniform tone and style across all written content. The guide must be actionable, linguistically coherent, and adaptable to different contexts while preserving brand identity.

## Context
Analyze the provided brand information:
- **Brand**: {{brand-name}}
- **Target audience**: {{target-audience}}
- **Core values**: {{brand-values}}
- **Current communication style**: {{current-style}}
- **Content types to cover**: {{content-types}}

Build the guide to ensure linguistic consistency using structured grammar principles that create natural, coherent messaging.

## Output
Deliver a structured brand voice guide with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points organized as follows:

**1. Brand Voice Foundation**
- Core voice attributes derived from brand values
- Audience alignment and expectations
- Voice personality summary

**2. Writing Guidelines**
- Vocabulary: preferred terms, words to avoid, industry jargon usage
- Sentence structure: length, complexity, rhythm patterns
- Grammar and punctuation standards
- Tone spectrum (formal to casual range)

**3. Messaging Framework**
- Key themes and topics
- Value propositions and positioning statements
- Brand story elements

**4. Application by Content Type**
For each content type specified, provide:
- Tone adaptation guidance
- Structural templates
- 2-3 before/after examples showing voice applied

**5. Contextual Flexibility**
- Guidelines for adjusting tone (customer service, crisis, celebration, educational)
- Boundaries: what never changes regardless of context
- Decision tree for tone selection

**6. Quality Checklist**
- Quick reference checklist editors can use to verify brand voice compliance

Format for easy reference and immediate implementation by content teams.
```
