# Hyperrealistic Golden Hour Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Golden Hour Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic close-up portraits with professional Hasselblad camera aesthetics for photographers, content creators, and beauty brands. This golden hour portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 8K resolution images with ultra-shallow depth of field, capturing the left side of the face with razor-sharp eye focus and soft bokeh backgrounds. It simulates a Hasselblad H6D-400c with 100mm f/2.2 lens to render micro-textural details - pores, freckles, wet skin reflections, and individual lashes - while warm golden side lighting creates specular highlights on moisture-covered surfaces like lips, nose, and cheekbones. The prompt accepts variables for subject description and skin elements (water droplets, oil, perspiration) to tailor the aesthetic for fashion editorials, skincare campaigns, or artistic portfolios. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality portrait photography that replicates expensive studio setups with controlled lighting and high-end camera gear. ● Produces close-up portraits with one eye in absolute focus, crisp catchlight, and soft bokeh falloff across the rest of the face. ● Renders micro-textural skin details including pores, natural freckles, and wet surface reflections from directional golden hour lighting. ● Simulates professional Hasselblad medium-format camera quality with warm color grading, deep shadows, and soft realistic contrast. ● Accepts customizable subject descriptions and skin-element variables to adapt the look for water, sweat, oil, or other surface treatments. ## Prompt
