# Settlement Agreement Draft Generator

## 簡介

The Settlement Agreement Draft Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, enforceable settlement agreements for parties resolving disputes without litigation. This settlement agreement prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through drafting a complete contract including recitals, settlement terms, compensation clauses, required actions, enforcement mechanisms, mutual release of claims, confidentiality provisions, and governing law. You provide three variables - dispute details, Party A name, and Party B name - and the prompt outputs a formal agreement structure with precise legal language designed to minimize ambiguity and prevent future disputes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and similar text models, making it accessible for attorneys, in-house counsel, mediators, and business professionals managing conflict resolution. Reach for this prompt when you need a balanced, professionally formatted settlement document that addresses all critical legal elements without starting from a blank page. ● Outputs a full settlement agreement structure including recitals, numbered clauses, and signature blocks ● Covers compensation, required actions, enforcement remedies, and mutual release of claims ● Uses specific, measurable legal language to reduce the risk of future interpretation conflicts ● Customizable for commercial disputes, employment matters, contract disagreements, and other two-party conflicts ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a legal expert specializing in dispute resolution and settlement agreements.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive settlement agreement to resolve the dispute described below. The agreement must be legally sound, balanced, and minimize ambiguity that could lead to future disputes.

## Context

{{dispute-details}}

Parties:
- Party A: {{party-a}}
- Party B: {{party-b}}

## Output

Provide a complete settlement agreement in the following structure:

**SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT**

This Settlement Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date], by and between {{party-a}} and {{party-b}}, collectively referred to as the "Parties."

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, [summarize the dispute from the context provided];

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to resolve this matter without further legal proceedings;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. TERMS OF SETTLEMENT**

Clearly outline the specific terms that resolve the dispute.

**2. COMPENSATION**

Detail any monetary or non-monetary compensation to be provided.

**3. REQUIRED ACTIONS**

List specific actions each party must complete, with deadlines where appropriate.

**4. ENFORCEMENT**

Include provisions for enforcing compliance with this agreement, including remedies for breach.

**5. RELEASE OF CLAIMS**

Provide mutual release language waiving all claims related to the dispute.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Include confidentiality provisions prohibiting disclosure of settlement terms.

**7. GOVERNING LAW**

Specify the jurisdiction and governing law for the agreement.

**8. ENTIRE AGREEMENT**

State that this agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.

[Signature blocks for both parties]

Use precise legal language throughout. Ensure all terms are specific, measurable, and enforceable.
```
