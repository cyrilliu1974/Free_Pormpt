# Industry Growth Potential Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Industry Growth Potential Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, citation-backed research papers examining the growth outlook of any industry in a chosen geographic region. This industry analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides business analysts through a structured framework that covers market size, competitive landscape, growth drivers, regulatory factors, and three-to-five-year projections. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, synthesizing insights from market research firms, government databases, academic journals, annual reports, and expert interviews into a professional strategic document complete with abstract, methodology, and formal citations in APA or MLA format. The prompt is designed for business analysts, researchers, strategic planners, and consultants who need to deliver authoritative industry assessments backed by credible data sources. Whether evaluating market entry opportunities, informing investment decisions, or benchmarking competitive positioning, this prompt structures findings into a clear hierarchical format with dedicated sections for growth drivers, challenges, regulatory landscape, and actionable recommendations. ● Structures research into title, abstract, introduction, market overview, growth drivers, challenges, regulatory landscape, future outlook, conclusion, and references ● Requires authoritative sources including industry reports, government statistics, academic journals, company financials, and expert interviews with proper citation formatting ● Identifies three to five primary growth drivers and two to four major challenges with supporting data, impact analysis, and mitigation strategies ● Delivers three-to-five-year market projections, emerging trends, and stakeholder recommendations tailored to the specified industry and region ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst and researcher with comprehensive knowledge across industries and regions.

## Task
Conduct a thorough analysis of the growth potential of {{target-industry}} in {{region}}. Produce a well-researched paper that synthesizes authoritative sources, market data, and critical insights into a compelling strategic document.

## Research Requirements
Utilize authoritative sources:
- Industry reports from reputable market research firms
- Government databases and statistics
- Academic journals and research papers
- Company annual reports and investor presentations
- Interviews with industry experts and thought leaders

Provide clear citations for all referenced material using a standard format (APA or MLA).

## Output Structure

**Title:** Growth Potential of the {{target-industry}} in {{region}}: An In-Depth Analysis

**Abstract**
Concise overview highlighting key findings and conclusions

**Introduction**
- Background on the {{target-industry}}
- Significance of {{region}} for the industry
- Research objectives and methodology

**Market Overview**
- Current market size and trends
- Key players and market share
- Consumer demographics and preferences

**Growth Drivers**
Identify and analyze 3-5 primary drivers:
1. Driver name
   - Explanation
   - Supporting data and examples
2. [Repeat structure for each driver]

**Challenges and Risks**
Examine 2-4 major challenges:
1. Challenge description
   - Potential impact
   - Mitigation strategies
2. [Repeat structure for each challenge]

**Regulatory Landscape**
- Relevant regulations and policies
- Impact on industry growth

**Future Outlook**
- Projected market size and growth rate (3-5 year horizon)
- Emerging opportunities and trends
- Actionable recommendations for stakeholders

**Conclusion**
- Recap of key findings
- Strategic implications for the industry and region

**References**
List all sources cited using consistent citation format (APA or MLA)

## Format
Use clear hierarchical structure with headings, subheadings, bullet points, and paragraphs to ensure professional readability.
```
