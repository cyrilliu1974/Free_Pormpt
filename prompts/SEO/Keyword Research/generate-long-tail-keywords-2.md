# Long-Tail Keyword Research Generator for SEO

## 簡介

The Long-Tail Keyword Research Generator for SEO is a free AI prompt that produces strategic keyword lists with volume estimates, difficulty ratings, and audience-fit analysis for SEO specialists and content marketers. This long-tail keyword prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context - industry, target audience, products, services, and location - then generating keyword phrases that match search intent across informational, navigational, and transactional query types. It delivers findings in a markdown table with search volume and difficulty estimates, followed by detailed explanations of why each keyword matters, its conversion potential, and how to implement it through specific content types or page targeting. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic high-competition keywords and discover specific phrases your audience actually searches for - terms that drive qualified traffic because they align with precise user intent. ● Analyzes business context to identify keywords matching target audience search intent and commercial goals ● Estimates search volume and keyword difficulty for each phrase to help prioritize realistic opportunities ● Explains traffic quality, conversion potential, and recommended content types for each keyword ● Covers informational, navigational, and transactional query types to support full-funnel SEO strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist conducting long-tail keyword research to improve targeted organic traffic.

## Task
Generate a strategic list of long-tail keywords tailored to the business context below. For each keyword, assess its potential search volume, keyword difficulty, and relevance. Explain how each keyword can drive qualified traffic and support SEO goals.

## Context
{{business-context}}

*Include: business name or type, industry, target audience, primary products/services, and geographical location if relevant.*

## Analysis Requirements
- Identify keywords that match target audience search intent
- Balance search opportunity (volume) against competition (difficulty)
- Prioritize keywords with clear conversion potential
- Consider informational, navigational, and transactional query types

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Keyword Difficulty |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|
| [keyword phrase] | [estimate] | [estimate] |

Below the table, provide a brief explanation for each keyword covering:
- Why it matches the target audience
- Expected traffic quality and conversion potential
- Recommended implementation approach (content type, page targeting)
```
