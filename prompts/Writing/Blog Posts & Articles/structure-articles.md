# Article Structure Outline Prompt for Journalists

## 簡介

The Article Structure Outline Prompt for Journalists is a free AI prompt that creates structured, publication-ready article outlines for professional writers and newsrooms. This article structure prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok guides you through crafting in-depth pieces that meet journalistic standards of accuracy, integrity, and ethics. It produces a complete outline with an attention-grabbing introduction, coherent sections organized under compelling headlines, integration points for credible source quotes, and a thought-provoking conclusion. Real-world applications include feature stories, investigative pieces, news analysis, and long-form journalism for respected news outlets. The prompt walks you through identifying your core message, conducting thorough research, structuring background and context, analyzing multiple perspectives, examining societal impact, and incorporating human-centered case studies. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize complex reporting into a clear narrative framework that engages diverse readerships while maintaining editorial rigor. ● Establishes a central theme that guides research direction, interviews, and narrative arc ● Organizes content into focused sections covering background, analysis, impact, and human stories with strong headlines ● Builds in checkpoints for source verification, quote integration, fact-checking, and ethical review ● Delivers an outline format suited for respected news outlets and diverse readership demographics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert journalist crafting in-depth articles that meet professional standards of accuracy, integrity, and ethics for publication in respected news outlets.

## Task
Create a structured article outline on {{topic}} that engages a diverse readership through compelling narrative, rigorous research, and balanced perspectives.

## Context
{{article-context}} provides background on your angle, target readership demographics, available sources, and any unique insights that distinguish this piece.

## Process
1. **Identify the core message** – Clarify the central theme that will guide research, interviews, and narrative direction
2. **Research thoroughly** – Gather facts, statistics, and background from academic journals, reputable news sources, and expert interviews
3. **Craft an attention-grabbing introduction** – Open with context and significance, using a narrative hook or intriguing fact to draw readers in
4. **Organize into coherent sections** with compelling headlines:
   - **Background and Context**: Relevant history, developments, or controversies
   - **Analysis and Perspectives**: Factual analysis incorporating quotes from authoritative sources
   - **Impact and Implications**: Effects on society, industries, or global affairs; potential future developments
   - **Personal Stories or Case Studies**: Human-centered examples that illustrate key points (where applicable)
5. **Incorporate quotes from reputable sources** – Add credibility, perspective, and depth through authoritative voices
6. **Conclude with a thought-provoking summary** – Encapsulate key points, pose questions, or suggest ways readers can engage further
7. **Verify accuracy and ethics** – Double-check facts, credit sources, eliminate bias, and follow journalistic standards

## Output
Deliver a structured article outline including:
- A compelling introduction that establishes significance and hooks the reader
- Organized sections with focused content and strong headlines
- Integration points for quotes from credible sources
- A conclusion that encourages reflection or action

The outline should be publication-ready for a respected news outlet and suitable for a diverse readership.
```
