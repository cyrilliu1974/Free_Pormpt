# Job Search Networking Strategies by Field Prompt

## 簡介

The Job Search Networking Strategies by Field Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers targeted networking analysis for job seekers in any industry. This job search networking prompt for ChatGPT evaluates six field-specific strategies - three online and three offline - rating each on a 1-5 effectiveness scale and providing three actionable implementation tips per strategy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, drawing on credible research sources to ensure recommendations reflect proven approaches rather than generic advice. A software engineer will receive different strategies than a healthcare professional or marketing specialist, because the prompt tailors every recommendation to the {{field}} variable you provide. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-based networking tactics customized to your target industry, complete with implementation guidance you can act on immediately. ● Delivers six networking strategies split evenly between online and offline channels, each rated for effectiveness in your specific field ● Provides three concrete implementation tips per strategy so you know exactly how to execute each approach ● Cites a minimum of five credible research sources to ground recommendations in real-world data ● Avoids generic advice by requiring field-specific analysis tailored to industry norms and hiring patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert networking strategist specializing in job search techniques across diverse industries and career stages.

## Task

Analyze and present the most effective networking strategies for job seekers in {{field}}, covering both online and offline approaches. Evaluate each strategy's effectiveness and provide actionable implementation guidance.

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

**Most Effective Networking Strategies for Job Searching in [field name]**

**Online Strategies:**

For each of 3 online strategies, provide:
1. Strategy name
2. Effectiveness Rating (1-5 scale, where 5 is most effective)
3. Three specific implementation tips

**Offline Strategies:**

For each of 3 offline strategies, provide:
1. Strategy name
2. Effectiveness Rating (1-5 scale, where 5 is most effective)
3. Three specific implementation tips

**Sources:**

Cite all research sources used to support your analysis (minimum 5 credible sources).

Ensure all strategies are field-specific and avoid generic advice. Prioritize strategies with demonstrated impact in the target field.
```
