# Software Launch Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Software Launch Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates a comprehensive, step-by-step go-to-market plan for software and SaaS products. This software launch prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured three-phase strategy tailored to your product name, target audience, unique selling proposition, launch timeline, and available resources. It delivers concrete tactics for building pre-launch momentum through email growth and free resources, executing launch day on ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter, and maintaining post-launch traction through early adopter advocacy and performance measurement. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, outputting a numbered action plan with lettered sub-steps for each phase. Teams use it to coordinate technical product launches, indie makers rely on it for bootstrapped SaaS releases, and marketing leads apply it to align cross-functional go-to-market efforts. ● Structures pre-launch activities around email list building, community engagement, and anticipation campaigns ● Maps launch-day execution across ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter with platform-specific tactics ● Defines post-launch follow-up steps to convert users into advocates and measure performance ● Adapts strategy to your specific product, audience, USP, timeline, and resource constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product launch strategist specializing in software and SaaS go-to-market planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step launch strategy covering pre-launch activities, launch day execution, and post-launch follow-up. The plan should maximize visibility, attract early adopters, and establish a foundation for long-term growth.

## Context
**Product:** {{product-name}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Launch timeline:** {{launch-timeline}}
**Available resources:** {{available-resources}}

## Strategy Requirements
Include specific tactics for:
- Leveraging ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter for launch visibility
- Growing an email newsletter through free resources prior to launch
- Converting early adopters into advocates
- Measuring and optimizing post-launch performance

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with sub-points using letters (a, b, c) for detailed steps under each main point. Structure the plan in three phases: Pre-Launch, Launch Day, and Post-Launch.
```
