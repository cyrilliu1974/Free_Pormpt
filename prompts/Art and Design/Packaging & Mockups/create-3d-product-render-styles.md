# 3D Product Render Styles for Midjourney

## 簡介

The 3D Product Render Styles for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates premium floating product visualizations with gradient surfaces and studio lighting for designers, marketers, and creative professionals. This 3D product render prompt for Midjourney produces polished images of any product suspended in space with a gradient finish that transitions across the surface, clean sans-serif labels integrated into the design, and complementary abstract shapes with soft bokeh backgrounds. It works with Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to create professional-grade visuals for e-commerce listings, social media campaigns, packaging mockups, and portfolio presentations. Reach for this prompt when you need polished, studio-quality product images without a photoshoot or 3D modeling software. ● Renders any product floating in space with gradient color transitions and integrated typography ● Adds abstract background shapes and soft bokeh elements for depth and visual interest ● Applies studio lighting with rim highlights for a modern, premium aesthetic ● Outputs 1:1 square images at stylize 500 for polished, marketing-ready results ## Prompt
