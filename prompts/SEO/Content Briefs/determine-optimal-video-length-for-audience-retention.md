# Video Length Optimizer for Audience Retention and SEO

## 簡介

The Video Length Optimizer for Audience Retention and SEO is a free AI prompt that determines ideal video duration based on niche-specific engagement patterns and platform algorithm requirements. Acting as a specialized video content strategist, this video length prompt for ChatGPT analyzes how different duration segments affect viewer retention rates and search visibility, then delivers a structured markdown table mapping video lengths to retention performance and SEO impact. You provide your niche, target audience, and current performance metrics, and the prompt generates 5-7 duration segments with corresponding retention rates and ranking factors, plus actionable recommendations for improving watch time and click-through rates. Content creators, YouTube strategists, and digital marketers use this prompt when planning video content calendars, auditing underperforming videos, or entering new content verticals where optimal length is unknown. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, synthesizing platform best practices with niche engagement data to forecast retention improvements. ● Outputs a structured table comparing 5-7 video length ranges with audience retention rates and SEO consequences specific to your niche ● Calculates expected retention improvement when shifting from current video duration to the optimal range ● Identifies platform-specific ranking factors influenced by watch time, including CTR and algorithm signals ● Provides 2-3 implementation-ready recommendations grounded in current YouTube, TikTok, and social platform best practices ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a video content strategist specializing in SEO optimization.

## Task
Analyze the optimal video length for maximum audience retention in the specified niche to improve video SEO performance. Conduct a comprehensive analysis considering viewer engagement patterns, content type characteristics, and platform-specific algorithm requirements.

## Context
{{niche-and-audience}}

{{current-performance}}

## Output
Present your analysis as:

1. A markdown table with 3 columns: **Video Length** | **Audience Retention Rate** | **SEO Impact**. Include 5-7 length segments relevant to the niche (e.g., 0-2 min, 2-5 min, 5-10 min, etc.).

2. Following the table, provide:
   - The optimal video length range for this niche and why
   - Expected improvement in retention rate if shifting from current length
   - Specific SEO benefits (watch time, CTR, ranking factors)
   - 2-3 actionable recommendations for implementation

Base recommendations on current platform best practices and niche-specific engagement data.
```
