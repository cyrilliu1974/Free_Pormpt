# Luxury Beauty Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Beauty Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms product photos into 8K photorealistic beauty campaign images with a close-up model, professional studio lighting, and magazine-quality skin detail. This luxury beauty product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 4:5 aspect ratio images featuring a model in her mid-20s holding your product horizontally across one eye, with customizable model features, skin finish, and background tone. The prompt specifies soft diffused beauty lighting with a large frontal softbox, 85mm prime lens bokeh, ultra-detailed pores and micro-texture without over-smoothing, and realistic reflections on the product surface. Use it to create high-converting hero images for skincare, cosmetics, or fragrance e-commerce listings, social media ads, or pitch decks when you need professional product photography without a studio shoot. ● Produces 8K close-up portraits with realistic skin texture, natural subsurface scattering, and detailed pore structure ● Positions the product horizontally across one eye with natural hand grip, readable label, and moisture droplet reflections ● Specifies soft beauty lighting - large softbox with rim light for hair separation and controlled highlights on cheekbones ● Delivers 4:5 aspect ratio images optimized for Instagram, mobile storefronts, and vertical ad placements ## Prompt

```
High-end beauty product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio, 8K ultra high resolution. Tight close-up portrait of an extremely beautiful woman in her mid 20s with {{model-look}} features—symmetrical face, high cheekbones, soft jawline, striking natural eye color with crystal clear catchlights, naturally full lips with hydrated gloss finish. {{skin-finish}} skin with ultra detailed pores, realistic micro texture, subtle skin perfection without plastic smoothing, subsurface scattering. Wet slicked-back hair with natural shine. Soft kissy lips expression, relaxed but confident.

Product held horizontally covering one eye, label facing camera fully readable, natural hand grip with realistic finger pressure and shadow interaction, realistic reflections and moisture droplets on surface.

Soft diffused beauty lighting: large frontal softbox with subtle rim light for hair separation, controlled glossy highlights on cheekbones and forehead, very soft minimal shadows. {{background-tone}} smooth seamless studio backdrop with slight creamy blur.

85mm prime lens, f/2.0, sharp focus on face and product with shallow depth of field. Natural skin tones, commercial beauty grade color profile, luxury skincare campaign aesthetic. Extreme photorealism, avoid cartoon/CGI look, plastic skin, over-smoothing, artificial textures.
```
