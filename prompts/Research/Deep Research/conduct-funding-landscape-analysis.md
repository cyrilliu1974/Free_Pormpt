# Research Funding Landscape Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Research Funding Landscape Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that maps the funding ecosystem for any research field over a ten-year period, helping academics, grant writers, and research administrators understand where money flows and how priorities evolve. This research funding analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through identifying the most significant funding bodies in a specified domain, tracking how their research priorities have shifted from 2013 to 2023, and surfacing new funding models that have emerged during that window. It produces a tabular summary of funding bodies alongside their priorities and models, a narrative section describing key shifts, and a curated list of sources. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is particularly useful for preparing grant proposals, strategic planning sessions, or literature reviews that require an evidence-based understanding of funding trends. Reach for this prompt when you need to orient yourself or your team in a research field's financial landscape, benchmark against peer institutions, or justify a funding strategy with historical context. ● Maps major public and private funding bodies and their evolving priorities in any research field. ● Tracks concrete shifts in funding themes, such as moves toward open science, interdisciplinary work, or applied research. ● Identifies emerging models like crowdfunding, industry partnerships, and public-private consortia. ● Returns findings in a clear table-plus-narrative format with citations, making it easy to quote in proposals or strategy documents. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert research funding analyst with deep knowledge of major funding bodies, research priorities, and emerging funding models across scientific and academic fields.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the funding landscape for {{research-field}} over the past decade (2013-2023). Identify the most significant funding bodies, track how research priorities have shifted, and highlight any new funding models that have emerged.

## Requirements

- Focus on the most significant funding bodies and their research priorities over the past decade
- Highlight notable shifts in research priorities during this period
- Identify and describe any new or emerging funding models in the field
- Provide reliable sources for all information presented

## Output

Deliver your findings as:

**Funding Landscape Analysis: {{research-field}} (2013-2023)**

**Table: Major Funding Bodies, Priorities, and Models**

| Funding Body | Research Priorities | Funding Model |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------|
| [Body name] | [Priority 1], [Priority 2], [Priority 3] | [Model description] |
| [Body name] | [Priority 1], [Priority 2], [Priority 3] | [Model description] |
| ... | ... | ... |

**Key Shifts in Priorities:**
[Describe notable changes over the decade]

**Emerging Funding Models:**
[Describe new approaches that have appeared]

**Sources:**
1. [Source 1]
2. [Source 2]
...
```
