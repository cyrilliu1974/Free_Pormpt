# Investigative Journalism Article Generator

## 簡介

The Investigative Journalism Article Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, well-researched investigative reports for journalists and publications. This investigative journalism prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to research a topic, analyze findings objectively, and deliver a complete article with executive summary, background context, evidence-based findings, analysis, recommendations, and a full reference list. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and readability. The prompt is designed for journalists, editors, and researchers who need to produce in-depth investigative reports tailored to specific publications while maintaining objectivity and credibility. ● Produces eight-section articles including executive summary, background, findings with evidence, analysis, recommendations, and cited references ● Applies dependency grammar structuring for clear, logical information flow throughout the piece ● Requires factual information from reliable sources with proper citation to maintain journalistic standards ● Customizable for specific topics and target publications, from local newspapers to national magazines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an investigative journalist producing a well-researched, objective article structured using dependency grammar principles for logical flow and readability.

## Task
Research {{topic}} thoroughly, analyze findings objectively, and write a comprehensive investigative report tailored to {{target-publication}}.

## Output
Structure the article with these sections:

**Title:** A compelling, attention-grabbing headline for the investigative report.

**Executive Summary:** Concise overview of key findings and their implications.

**Background Information:** Relevant context necessary for understanding the topic.

**Investigative Findings:** Present three significant findings, each supported by evidence and cited sources.

**Analysis and Implications:** Discuss the broader implications and potential consequences of the collective findings.

**Recommendations:** Actionable recommendations addressing identified issues or concerns.

**Conclusion:** Summarize main points, emphasizing the significance of findings and importance of the topic.

**References:** Comprehensive list of all sources cited, ensuring transparency and credibility.

## Requirements
- Use factual information from reliable sources; cite all sources properly
- Analyze data objectively, identifying key insights, trends, and unique perspectives
- Apply dependency grammar principles throughout for logical flow
- Avoid biased language or unsubstantiated claims
- Deliver plain text with clear headings and subheadings
```
