# Video Editor Brief Generator for Social Media Ads

## 簡介

The Video Editor Brief Generator for Social Media Ads is a free AI prompt that creates structured creative briefs to guide video production for promotional content across social platforms. This video editor brief prompt for ChatGPT delivers a four-section creative document: a three-act story concept (hook, value delivery, resolution), prioritized key talking points that address audience pain points, detailed visual direction including shot lists and pacing recommendations, and platform-specific calls-to-action optimized for each network's conversion mechanics. It analyzes your target audience and platform requirements - whether TikTok's short-form vertical format, YouTube's longer watch times, or Instagram Reels - and tailors narrative structure, visual treatments, aspect ratios, and CTA timing accordingly. Marketing teams use it to translate campaign goals into actionable production briefs that video editors can execute without ambiguity. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to brief freelance editors, in-house production teams, or agencies on video ads that must perform within a specific platform's algorithm and user behavior patterns. ● Outputs a three-act narrative framework with hook, value delivery, and resolution tailored to platform norms ● Prioritizes key talking points that connect product benefits to audience pain points and brand positioning ● Specifies visual elements including shot lists, pacing, graphic overlays, text treatments, and aspect ratio requirements ● Delivers platform-optimized CTAs with strategic timing for swipe-up, link-in-bio, comment, share, or shop-now actions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video marketing strategist creating a comprehensive creative brief for a video editor.

## Task
Develop a structured video brief that outlines a compelling storytelling approach for {{video-type}} on {{platform}}. Analyze the target audience and platform requirements, identify key selling points, and craft a narrative that drives engagement and conversions.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-or-service}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Brand Voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver a complete creative brief organized under these headings:

**Story Concept**
A clear three-act narrative structure (hook, value delivery, resolution) that aligns with platform best practices and audience psychology.

**Key Talking Points**
Prioritized messages that highlight the product's unique value, address audience pain points, and reinforce brand positioning.

**Visual Elements**
Shot list, pacing recommendations, graphic overlays, text treatments, and aesthetic direction that matches the brand voice and platform format (aspect ratio, duration norms).

**Calls-to-Action**
Strategic CTAs timed for maximum impact, tailored to the platform's conversion mechanics (swipe up, link in bio, comment, share, shop now).

Ensure every element is optimized for {{platform}}'s algorithm, user behavior, and technical specifications.
```
