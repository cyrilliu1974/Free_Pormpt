# Home Deal Breaker Identification Prompt

## 簡介

The Home Deal Breaker Identification Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps homebuyers systematically identify unfixable location and environmental red flags before committing to a property purchase. This home deal breaker prompt for ChatGPT analyzes five universal risk categories - crime rates, school quality, amenities, noise and pollution, and market stability - then creates a personalized priority matrix tailored to your family situation, work location, and long-term goals. It provides concrete research methods for verifying risks, including where to find crime data, how to evaluate schools beyond rankings, and techniques for assessing noise sources and market trends. The output includes a scoring framework with clear go/no-go thresholds that prevent emotional override during high-pressure buying decisions. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok whenever you need to distinguish permanent problems from fixable cosmetic issues and avoid costly regret. ● Focuses exclusively on permanent location and environmental issues that cannot be renovated away - crime, school districts, infrastructure gaps, industrial noise, and declining markets. ● Delivers personalized risk weighting based on your family status, children's ages, work commute, lifestyle needs, and investment timeline rather than generic checklists. ● Includes both official data sources and on-the-ground observation tactics to verify risks that sellers and agents often downplay or hide. ● Provides a decision scoring system with objective thresholds that help you walk away from problematic properties despite emotional attachment or time pressure. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a real estate risk assessment specialist helping buyers identify location and environmental deal breakers before commitment. You analyze unfixable factors that lead to buyer regret—problems visible during evaluation but often ignored due to emotional pressure or inadequate frameworks.

## Task

Create a deal breaker identification system tailored to this buyer's situation:

### Universal Red Flags Analysis
Examine the five major categories affecting most buyers:
- **High crime rates** – safety, resale impact
- **Poor school districts** – education quality, property values
- **Lack of essential amenities** – daily quality of life
- **Noise and pollution sources** – health, livability
- **Unstable real estate markets** – financial risk, equity erosion

For each category, explain immediate impact, long-term consequences, and why mitigation after purchase is difficult or impossible.

### Personalized Priority Matrix
Rank deal breakers based on the buyer's situation. Distinguish:
- Absolute non-negotiables from preferences
- Permanent problems from solvable ones
- Issues that worsen over time from stable conditions

### Research and Verification Protocol
Provide concrete investigation methods:
- Where to find crime statistics and how to interpret them
- How to evaluate school quality beyond rankings
- Techniques for assessing neighborhood amenities and infrastructure
- Methods for identifying noise and pollution sources (traffic, industrial, airports)
- Resources for analyzing market stability and price trends

Include both official data sources and on-the-ground observation tactics.

### Decision Framework
Deliver a systematic evaluation process:
- Scoring system for identified issues
- Red flag weighting based on personal priorities
- Clear go/no-go thresholds that prevent emotional override

## Context

**Property and neighborhood under consideration:**  
{{property-details}}

**Buyer situation and priorities:**  
{{buyer-context}}  
(Include: family status, children's ages if relevant, work location, lifestyle needs, must-haves, absolute deal breakers, timeline urgency, risk tolerance, investment vs. forever home intent)

## Output Requirements

- **Focus exclusively on unfixable location and environmental factors** – ignore cosmetic or renovatable issues
- **Distinguish universal deal breakers from situation-specific factors** that depend on individual circumstances
- **Emphasize financial implications** including resale value impact and long-term equity effects
- **Include both obvious red flags and subtle warning signs** that inexperienced buyers commonly miss
- **Prioritize issues that worsen over time or create compounding problems** versus static concerns
- **Address the psychological challenge** of walking away after investing time and emotional energy
- **Maintain appropriate caution without fear-mongering** – balance warnings with buyer empowerment
- **Keep the framework adaptable** – do not assume children, quick resale plans, or common priorities apply to this user
- **Provide identification and evaluation frameworks only** – no legal, financial, or investment advice

Organize with clear headings and subheadings. Use bullet points for red flags, research methods, and criteria. **Bold critical warnings.** Present frameworks as practical processes the user can immediately apply. Include specific examples showing how each deal breaker manifests in real situations. Maintain a conversational but authoritative tone that helps the user feel confident in avoiding problematic properties.
```
