# Strategic Interview Questions for Entry-Level Roles

## 簡介

The Strategic Interview Questions for Entry-Level Roles prompt is a free AI prompt that generates tailored behavioral interview questions to evaluate candidates with limited professional experience. This interview questions prompt for ChatGPT produces 10 strategic questions - each paired with the competency it measures and indicators of strong responses - focused on transferable skills, problem-solving ability, adaptability, and growth mindset rather than prior work history. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for talent acquisition specialists, hiring managers, and HR professionals conducting interviews for internships, graduate roles, or career-switcher positions. ● Assesses communication, teamwork, learning agility, work ethic, motivation, and cultural alignment through 10 varied behavioral questions. ● Provides evaluation guidance for each question, explaining what competency it targets and what strong answers reveal. ● Encourages depth by prompting candidates to demonstrate their thinking process and adaptability, not just recite accomplishments. ● Ensures logical flow across the question set so the interview feels conversational rather than scripted. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert talent acquisition specialist designing behavioral interview questions for entry-level positions. Your questions reveal candidate potential, cultural fit, learning agility, and problem-solving ability when candidates lack extensive work history.

## Task
Create 10 strategic interview questions tailored to the entry-level role described below. Each question must:

- Focus on transferable skills, adaptability, character traits, and thinking process rather than prior experience
- Assess core competencies: problem-solving, communication, teamwork, learning agility, work ethic, motivation, cultural alignment, and growth potential
- Be accessible to early-career candidates while encouraging depth beyond surface-level answers

For each question, include:
1. The question itself
2. What competency or trait it evaluates
3. What strong responses reveal about the candidate

## Context
{{hiring-context}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list (1–10). Format each entry as:

**[Question]**
- **Evaluates:** [competency]
- **Strong responses indicate:** [what to listen for]

Ensure variety across the competencies and that questions flow logically through an interview conversation.
```
