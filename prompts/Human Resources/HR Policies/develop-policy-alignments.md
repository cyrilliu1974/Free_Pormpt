# Post-Restructure Policy Alignment Framework

## 簡介

The Post-Restructure Policy Alignment Framework is a free AI prompt that produces a detailed 5-step plan for aligning workplace policies with new organizational structures after company restructures. This policy alignment prompt for ChatGPT guides organizational change consultants and HR leaders through a systematic process: analyzing the current policy landscape against the new structure, identifying critical gaps and overlaps, prioritizing updates by legal requirements and business impact, establishing rollout communication channels, and creating sustainable maintenance processes. Each step includes specific actions, responsible stakeholders, timeline considerations, and potential challenges. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your restructure context and compliance requirements as inputs to deliver a numbered framework with sub-actions, responsible parties, and implementation timelines. Reach for this prompt when your organization faces role confusion, policy gaps, or compliance exposure following mergers, reorganizations, or departmental changes. ● Analyzes existing policies against new organizational charts to surface critical gaps, redundancies, and conflicts that threaten continuity. ● Prioritizes policy updates by legal urgency and business impact, involving department heads, legal counsel, and affected employee groups at the right moments. ● Establishes clear communication plans for policy rollout, addressing employee training needs and change management best practices. ● Creates repeatable maintenance processes so policies stay current as the organization evolves beyond the initial restructure. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational change management consultant specializing in post-restructure policy alignment.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive 5-step strategic framework for updating workplace policies after a company restructure. Each step must include:
- Specific actions to take
- Key stakeholders to involve
- Timeline considerations
- Potential challenges to anticipate

Address policy gaps, role confusion, and compliance risks to ensure operational continuity and legal protection.

## Context
**Restructure details:**
{{restructure-context}}

**Compliance landscape:**
{{compliance-requirements}}

The framework should cover: analyzing the current policy landscape against the new structure, identifying critical gaps and overlaps, prioritizing updates based on legal requirements and business impact, establishing communication channels for policy rollout, and creating sustainable processes for ongoing maintenance. Consider employee communication needs, training implications, and change management best practices.

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list with each of the 5 steps clearly defined. Under each step, include specific sub-actions, responsible parties, and implementation timelines for maximum clarity and actionability.
```
