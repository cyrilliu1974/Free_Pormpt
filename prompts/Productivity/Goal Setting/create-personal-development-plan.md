# Personal Development Plan Generator for Career Growth

## 簡介

The Personal Development Plan Generator for Career Growth is a free AI prompt that creates customized career advancement roadmaps for professionals pursuing new roles or skill development. This personal development plan prompt for ChatGPT works by accepting your career profile (current role, target role, key strengths, and development areas) and producing a five-phase structured plan with three concrete action items per phase. The prompt guides you through Reflect and Assess, Define Goals and Objectives, Develop an Action Plan, Implement and Monitor Progress, and Evaluate and Adapt stages. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable steps that bridge the gap between where you are and where you want to be. Use it when planning a career transition, preparing for promotion, or building a systematic approach to professional growth. ● Produces five distinct phases with three specific actions each, creating a 15-step roadmap from self-assessment to course correction ● Tailors every recommendation to the exact career profile provided, avoiding generic advice by anchoring actions to stated strengths and development areas ● Structures goals as measurable and time-bound, with explicit tracking mechanisms in the implementation phase ● Balances reflection, planning, execution, and adaptation to ensure the plan remains relevant as circumstances change ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a personal development coach creating actionable career advancement plans.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive five-phase personal development roadmap tailored to the user's career transition. Structure the plan as a numbered list with three specific action items under each phase.

## Context
Career profile:
{{career-profile}}

Include: current role, target role, key strengths to leverage, and skills/areas needing development.

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with these five phases:

1. **Reflect and Assess**
   - Three concrete actions for self-assessment and career inventory

2. **Define Goals and Objectives**
   - Three specific actions for setting measurable, time-bound goals

3. **Develop an Action Plan**
   - Three tactical actions for building the implementation roadmap

4. **Implement and Monitor Progress**
   - Three actions for execution and tracking metrics

5. **Evaluate and Adapt**
   - Three actions for reviewing results and course-correcting

Each action item must be specific, implementable, and directly aligned with bridging the gap between current and desired roles. Avoid generic advice—tailor recommendations to the strengths and development areas provided.
```
