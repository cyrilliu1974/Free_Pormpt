# Marketing Plan Outline Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Marketing Plan Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed, table-based marketing plan for businesses, startups, and marketing teams. This marketing plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, target audience, and goals to produce a structured plan organized into four columns: Objectives (measurable outcomes), Strategies (high-level approaches), Tactics (concrete actions and channels), and Metrics (KPIs and tracking methods). Each row delivers a cohesive marketing approach where all four elements work together. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, blending traditional and digital marketing channels with industry best practices tailored to your specific needs. Use it when launching a new product, entering a market, or formalizing your go-to-market strategy over a defined time period. ● Delivers a markdown table structure that makes objectives, strategies, tactics, and metrics easy to compare and present to stakeholders. ● Considers both traditional marketing channels and digital tactics to build a balanced, multi-channel approach. ● Tailors recommendations to your specific business type, audience demographics, and campaign duration. ● Includes KPIs and measurement methods so you can track progress and optimize as you execute. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist developing a comprehensive marketing plan.

## Task
Create a detailed marketing plan structured as a table with four columns: Objectives, Strategies, Tactics, and Metrics. Each row should represent a cohesive marketing approach addressing a specific objective.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Marketing goals: {{marketing-goals}}
Plan duration: {{duration}}

Analyze the business type, audience characteristics, and goals before developing the plan. Consider both traditional and digital marketing channels to create a well-rounded approach. Include innovative tactics and industry best practices that align with the specific business needs.

## Output
Deliver your marketing plan as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Objectives**: Specific, measurable outcomes tied to the marketing goals
- **Strategies**: High-level approaches to achieve each objective
- **Tactics**: Concrete actions and marketing channels to execute the strategy
- **Metrics**: KPIs and measurement methods to track success

Ensure each row presents a complete marketing approach where objectives, strategies, tactics, and metrics work together cohesively.
```
