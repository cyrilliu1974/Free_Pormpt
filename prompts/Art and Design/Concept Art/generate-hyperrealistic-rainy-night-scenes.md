# Hyperrealistic Rainy Night Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Rainy Night Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic 8K photographs of moody nighttime encounters between human subjects and oversized creatures in rain-soaked environments. This rainy night scene prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces wide-shot compositions featuring a person holding a red umbrella standing beside a gigantic, wet-furred creature at a bus stop, complete with atmospheric lamppost lighting, moon reflections on asphalt, and dense forest backgrounds. The prompt accepts three variables - location, attire, and creature type - allowing you to customize the setting, the person's clothing, and which fantastical being appears in the scene. Artists, concept designers, book cover creators, and visual storytellers reach for this prompt when they need emotionally charged fantasy scenes with photorealistic detail and cinematic atmosphere. ● Produces 8K ultra-realistic photographs with detailed rain reflections, wet surfaces, and atmospheric lighting from lampposts and moonlight. ● Places a normal-sized human character with sharp facial expressions beside a gigantic creature with wet fur and environmental details like leaves. ● Delivers wide-shot bus stop compositions in aged, forested locations with apprehensive, curious emotional tones perfect for storytelling. ● Accepts location, attire, and creature variables to generate unique fantasy encounters without CGI teams or photo shoots. ## Prompt

```
An ultra-realistic 8K UHD photograph of a rainy night scene at {{location}}. A person with a normal build stands holding a small red umbrella, wearing {{attire}}, looking to the side with an expression of apprehension and curiosity. Beside them, a gigantic, realistic {{creature}} with wet fur and a leaf on its head waits in the rain. The setting includes an aged bus stop sign, dense wet forest trees in the background, and lighting from a dim lamppost and moon, with realistic rain reflections on the wet asphalt. Wide shot composition with sharp, expressive facial features.
```
