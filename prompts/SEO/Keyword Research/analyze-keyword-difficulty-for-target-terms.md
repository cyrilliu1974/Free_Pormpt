# Keyword Difficulty Analysis for SEO Target Terms

## 簡介

The Keyword Difficulty Analysis for SEO Target Terms is a free AI prompt that evaluates the competitiveness and ranking potential of target keywords for SEO professionals and content strategists. This keyword difficulty prompt for ChatGPT generates a structured analysis table scoring each keyword on a 0-100 difficulty scale, estimating monthly search volume, and assessing ranking feasibility based on your website's domain authority, industry competition, and geographic focus. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable insights that account for SERP competition, content quality benchmarks, and competitive intensity. Real use cases include prioritizing keyword targets for new content campaigns, auditing existing keyword strategies against current domain authority, and identifying quick-win opportunities in competitive niches. The prompt is designed for SEO analysts, content marketers, and digital strategists who need data-driven keyword selection based on realistic ranking potential rather than search volume alone. ● Scores each keyword on a 0-100 difficulty scale considering SERP competition, domain authority requirements, and content quality benchmarks ● Estimates monthly search volume to help prioritize high-opportunity targets ● Provides keyword-by-keyword explanations of competitive factors and what influences each difficulty rating ● Accounts for industry-specific competition, geographic market dynamics, and your current website authority ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst specializing in keyword difficulty assessment and competitive analysis.

## Task
Evaluate the competitiveness and ranking potential of target keywords. For each keyword, assess:
- Difficulty score (0-100 scale, considering SERP competition, domain authority requirements, and content quality benchmarks)
- Estimated monthly search volume
- Ranking feasibility given the provided website authority and competitive landscape

## Context
**Target keywords:** {{target-keywords}}
**Industry & audience:** {{industry-and-audience}}
**Website authority score:** {{domain-authority}}
**Geographic focus:** {{geo-focus}}

Provide actionable insights that account for the website's current authority level and the competitive intensity in this industry and region.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table:

| KEYWORD | DIFFICULTY SCORE | SEARCH VOLUME |
|---------|------------------|---------------|

Below the table, provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) for each keyword's difficulty score, addressing why it received that rating and what factors most influence its competitiveness.
```
