# Technical Report Generator With Five Core Sections

## 簡介

The Technical Report Generator With Five Core Sections is a free AI prompt that produces formal technical reports structured around the five essential components of professional technical documentation. This technical report prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating a complete report covering Background (context and objectives), Methodology (procedures and experimental design), Results (data presentation with tables and figures), Analysis (interpretation and patterns), and Conclusions (implications and future directions). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, maintaining the formal tone and precision expected in academic, engineering, and research environments. The prompt enforces logical flow between sections, appropriate use of technical terminology, and evidence-based claims suitable for specialist audiences. Designed for researchers, engineers, technical writers, and professionals who need to document experiments, projects, or investigations with rigor and clarity. ● Structures reports with five distinct sections that mirror academic and industry standards for technical documentation ● Enforces reproducibility by requiring detailed methodology and sufficient procedural specificity ● Integrates data visualization guidance for tables, graphs, and figures that enhance comprehension ● Balances objective results presentation with interpretive analysis that connects findings to broader objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert technical writer with deep domain knowledge in the relevant field.

## Task
Write a comprehensive technical report structured around five core sections: Background, Methodology, Results, Analysis, and Conclusions. Each section serves a distinct purpose in communicating the project's context, execution, findings, interpretation, and implications.

## Context
Project/Experiment Details:
{{project-details}}

## Output
Structure the report with clear section headings:

**1. Background**
- Contextualize the project/experiment with relevant domain knowledge
- Establish the problem space, motivation, and objectives

**2. Methodology**
- Detail the methods, procedures, techniques, and tools employed
- Provide sufficient specificity for reproducibility
- Explain design choices and experimental setup

**3. Results**
- Present data, findings, and outcomes objectively
- Use tables, graphs, and figures where they enhance clarity
- Organize results logically (chronologically, by variable, by hypothesis)

**4. Analysis**
- Interpret the meaning and significance of results
- Identify patterns, trends, correlations, or anomalies
- Connect findings to the original objectives and existing literature

**5. Conclusions**
- Summarize key takeaways and their implications
- Acknowledge limitations and constraints
- Suggest directions for future investigation

Maintain a logical flow with smooth transitions between sections. Use technical terminology appropriate for a specialist audience. Support all claims with evidence from the data. Ensure accuracy, completeness, and analytical rigor throughout.
```
