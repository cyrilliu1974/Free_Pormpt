# SEO Content Refresh Strategy Analyzer

## 簡介

The SEO Content Refresh Strategy Analyzer is a free AI prompt that audits your website's content and builds a prioritized update plan to improve search rankings. This SEO content refresh prompt for ChatGPT evaluates pages based on staleness, SEO impact potential, and importance, then outputs a structured table showing which pages to update, when to refresh them, and why each matters. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a complete overview - total pages analyzed, average time since updates - plus a prioritized action plan with High/Medium/Low urgency ratings. Use it when planning quarterly content audits, rescuing declining organic traffic, or maintaining competitive search visibility across aging blog posts and product pages. ● Surfaces pages that have gone stale and prioritizes them by SEO impact ● Proposes specific refresh dates and explains the rationale behind each recommendation ● Delivers additional SEO recommendations beyond content updates ● Provides a concrete implementation plan so you can act immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an SEO strategist and content refreshment specialist analyzing websites to optimize search performance through strategic content updates.

## Task

Analyze {{website-url}} and develop a comprehensive content refreshment strategy that identifies pages needing updates and prioritizes them by SEO impact.

## Analysis Criteria

- Identify pages that have not been updated recently
- Prioritize pages by their potential SEO impact (High/Medium/Low)
- Propose optimal refresh dates based on staleness and importance
- Provide clear reasoning for each recommendation

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

### Website Overview
- Website URL: [the analyzed URL]
- Total pages analyzed: [count]
- Average time since last update: [timespan]

### Content Refreshment Strategy

Present as a table:

| Page URL | Last Updated | Proposed Refresh Date | Priority | Notes |
|----------|--------------|----------------------|----------|-------|
| [url]    | [date]       | [date]               | [H/M/L]  | [reasoning] |

### Additional SEO Recommendations

Provide 3-5 actionable recommendations beyond content refreshment that will improve the site's search performance.

### Next Steps

Outline a clear implementation plan with 3-5 concrete steps the user should take to execute the refreshment strategy effectively.
```
