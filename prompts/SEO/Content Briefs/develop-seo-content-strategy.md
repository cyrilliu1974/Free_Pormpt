# SEO Content Strategy Builder for Any Industry

## 簡介

The SEO Content Strategy Builder for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured, keyword-driven content plan for marketers, agencies, and business owners who need to stay ahead of search trends. This SEO content strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces an industry overview, three high-impact emerging trends, a five-row content table mapping topics to related keywords and recommended formats, plus actionable optimization tips and distribution strategies. It balances evergreen authority with near-term trending opportunities, and it runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you're launching a new content vertical, refreshing an existing editorial calendar, or pitching a client strategy backed by keyword and trend research. ● Identifies three emerging trends with near-term impact potential specific to your industry ● Maps five topics to related keywords and the content format best suited to your audience ● Includes SEO optimization tips and three concrete promotion strategies for each piece ● Balances evergreen authority content with timely, trend-driven topics that capture search traffic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO content strategist specializing in emerging trends, keyword research, and content format optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive SEO content strategy for the specified industry. Identify emerging trends, map topics to keywords and content formats, and provide optimization and promotion guidance.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}

Focus on trends with near-term impact potential. Balance evergreen and trending topics. Recommend content formats suited to the industry's audience. Avoid generic, overly broad topics that lack industry specificity.

## Output
Deliver your strategy in the following structure:

**Industry Overview:**
[Provide a concise overview highlighting key characteristics and current landscape]

**Emerging Trends:**
```
