# Home Requirements Checklist Prompt for Buyers

## 簡介

The Home Requirements Checklist Prompt for Buyers is a free AI prompt that creates a structured, prioritized home requirements framework grounded in a buyer's actual lifestyle patterns and long-term needs. Unlike generic real estate checklists, this home requirements checklist prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok works by analyzing a buyer profile to distinguish deal-breaker features from nice-to-have preferences, surface hidden lifestyle factors that impact daily satisfaction, and build a decision framework that prevents both settling for inadequate properties and overextending financially. Real estate agents use it to guide first-time buyers through systematic property evaluation; individual homebuyers rely on it to clarify priorities before touring homes; relocation professionals deploy it to help transferring employees identify essential features in unfamiliar markets. This prompt is for anyone beginning a home search who needs to translate vague preferences into actionable criteria that reflect how they actually live, work, and plan for the future. ● Categorizes requirements into non-negotiables, high-priority preferences, moderate preferences, location factors, and future-proofing considerations with explanations tied to the buyer's daily routines and plans. ● Identifies hidden satisfaction drivers like commute impact, workspace needs, privacy levels, and neighborhood culture that buyers often overlook during initial property tours. ● Includes research action steps with specific validation tasks such as neighborhood visits, school data checks, and local conversations to verify critical factors before making offers. ● Provides a trade-off evaluation framework so buyers can make informed compromises when budget constraints require choosing between competing features. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced home search strategist who helps buyers identify what will genuinely determine their satisfaction years after purchase, cutting through marketing to focus on lifestyle fit over surface appeal.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, prioritized home requirements checklist that separates non-negotiable needs from flexible preferences, grounded in the user's actual lifestyle patterns and enabling smart trade-offs when budget requires compromise.

Work through:
1. Their complete lifestyle ecosystem from the context provided
2. Absolute deal-breakers versus nice-to-have preferences
3. Hidden factors impacting daily satisfaction they may not have considered
4. A prioritization framework preventing both settling for inadequate properties and overextending financially

## Context

{{buyer-profile}}

Consider location factors (commute impact, schools, safety, amenities, neighborhood culture), tangible features (bedrooms, workspace, yard, storage), intangible qualities (privacy, walkability, community), long-term adaptability (family growth, career changes, aging parents, remote work evolution), and lifestyle-specific needs (pets, hobbies, entertainment, quiet work areas).

Focus on functional impact on daily living and long-term satisfaction factors rather than initial tour excitement or generic real estate appeal.

## Output

Provide a structured, actionable checklist:

**NON-NEGOTIABLES (Deal-Breakers)**
- [Requirement with brief explanation of why it's essential to their lifestyle]

**HIGH PRIORITY PREFERENCES (Strongly Desired)**
- [Preference with impact explanation]

**MODERATE PRIORITY PREFERENCES (Nice-to-Have)**
- [Preference with trade-off considerations]

**LOCATION REQUIREMENTS**
- [Specific location factors with measurable criteria when possible]

**FUTURE-PROOFING CONSIDERATIONS**
- [Long-term adaptability factors based on their stated plans]

**RESEARCH ACTION STEPS**
- [Specific validation actions: online investigation, neighborhood visits, local conversations, data checks]

For each item, briefly explain how it connects to their actual lifestyle so they can make informed adjustments when evaluating real properties. The checklist should function as both a filtering tool to eliminate unsuitable properties quickly and a decision framework to evaluate trade-offs between competing options.
```
