# Business Time-Wasting Activity Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Time-Wasting Activity Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies productivity bottlenecks and delivers tailored efficiency strategies for any business model. This business productivity prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a specific business type - retail, SaaS, consulting, healthcare, or any industry - and pinpoints the five most common time-wasting activities unique to that operational context. It then pairs each inefficiency with a concrete, immediately actionable minimization strategy presented in a clean two-column table. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing focused recommendations grounded in workflow patterns, resource constraints, and operational realities rather than generic productivity advice. Use it when onboarding a new client, auditing internal operations, or preparing efficiency improvement plans for leadership. ● Surfaces high-impact inefficiencies tailored to the operational model of the specified business type ● Delivers five concrete, immediately implementable strategies to reduce wasted time ● Outputs a clean markdown table that can be dropped into reports, presentations, or client deliverables ● Avoids generic advice by focusing on workflow patterns, resource constraints, and industry-specific bottlenecks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst specializing in operational efficiency and time-management optimization across industries.

## Task
Analyze the provided business type and identify the five most common time-wasting activities that hinder productivity. For each activity, provide a practical, actionable strategy to minimize or eliminate it.

## Context
Focus on high-impact inefficiencies specific to {{business-type}}. Avoid generic productivity advice—tailor each insight to the operational realities, workflow patterns, and resource constraints typical of this business model.

## Output
Present your analysis as a two-column markdown table:

| Time-Wasting Activity | Minimization Strategy |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| Activity 1 | Strategy 1 |
| Activity 2 | Strategy 2 |
| Activity 3 | Strategy 3 |
| Activity 4 | Strategy 4 |
| Activity 5 | Strategy 5 |

Each strategy must be concrete and immediately actionable.
```
