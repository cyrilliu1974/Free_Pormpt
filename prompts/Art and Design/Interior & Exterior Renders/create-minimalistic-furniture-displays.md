# Minimalist Furniture Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimalist Furniture Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates architectural-style product renders with abundant white space and eye-level framing for furniture designers, e-commerce brands, and interior stylists. This minimalist furniture prompt for Midjourney produces compositions that place the subject in the lower third of the frame, leaving generous negative space on the right and top for copy placement or editorial breathing room. It enforces a Scandinavian aesthetic - light woods, muted tones, no clutter - and shoots from an eye-level, 50mm perspective in a 3:4 portrait aspect ratio. Use it to photograph dining chairs, coffee tables, sofas, or shelving units in a style ready for lookbooks, Instagram carousels, or product pages. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana with fine-tuned settings for sharpness, creativity, and quality.
