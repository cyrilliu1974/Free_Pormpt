# Lifestyle Selfie Portrait Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Lifestyle Selfie Portrait Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic indoor selfie photography scenes with detailed composition, lighting, and styling direction for content creators and brands. This lifestyle selfie portrait prompt for Midjourney produces natural daylight scenes featuring a subject on elegant furniture with precise pose direction - torso twist, casual phone placement, relaxed limbs - plus wardrobe details like fabric textures, accessories, and color palettes. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, and is built for fashion brands, lifestyle influencers, and marketing teams who need relatable, aspirational imagery that feels candid rather than staged. The prompt includes two variables: subject-description and aesthetic-mood, letting you define who appears and the overall tone (soft morning light, golden hour, moody minimalism). Reach for this prompt when you need consistent lifestyle photography that showcases products, builds brand identity, or populates social feeds without scheduling shoots or hiring models. ● Specifies natural window light with soft shadows, fabric highlights, and warm diffused sunlight for authentic morning or afternoon aesthetics. ● Includes wardrobe detail down to fabric type, color, lace trim, jewelry, and manicure so every element aligns with brand identity. ● Directs body language and camera angle - sideways on sofa, knee up, torso twist, phone in hand - for relaxed, believable selfie compositions. ● Defines environment elements like wall color, curtains, plants, and furniture to create cohesive lifestyle backdrops. ## Prompt

```
Bright indoor setting, natural daylight from large window. {{subject-description}}, sitting sideways on a cream-colored velvet sofa, one knee up, torso slightly twisted toward the camera, taking a casual selfie with rose-gold iPhone held in right hand, left hand resting on thigh, soft playful smile. Wearing soft blush-pink satin cropped camisole with thin straps and matching high-waist satin shorts with delicate lace trim, small gold belly chain, thin gold anklet. Long almond-shaped nude-pink manicure. Warm diffused sunlight pouring in from the side, gentle highlights on skin and fabric. Light gray walls, flowing white curtains, hints of green plants near the window. {{aesthetic-mood}} morning selfie aesthetic.
```
