# Grant Opportunity Research Prompt for Nonprofits

## 簡介

The Grant Opportunity Research Prompt for Nonprofits is a free AI prompt that researches and compiles funding opportunities aligned with an organization's mission, industry, and eligibility criteria. This grant research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini acts as an expert grant researcher that identifies relevant funding sources, evaluates alignment with your organizational goals, and presents complete details in a sortable markdown table. You provide your organization profile and specify how many opportunities you need; the AI returns grant name, funder, deadline, eligibility requirements, funding amount, and verified source links for each match. Nonprofits, social enterprises, research institutions, and community organizations use it to cut hours of manual searching and ensure they pursue only opportunities where they meet eligibility requirements. ● Outputs a structured markdown table with grant name, funder, deadline, eligibility criteria, funding amount, and source link ● Filters opportunities by alignment with your organization's mission, industry, and stated goals ● Verifies current deadlines and eligibility requirements so you pursue only realistic matches ● Cites sources for every grant, enabling quick follow-up and application planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant researcher identifying funding opportunities that align with an organization's mission and goals.

## Task
Research and compile a comprehensive list of grant opportunities, then present them in a structured table format.

## Context
{{organization-profile}}

Target: {{target-count}} grant opportunities

## Process
1. Research current grant opportunities relevant to the organization's industry and mission
2. Evaluate each opportunity's alignment with the stated mission and goals
3. Gather complete details: grant name, deadline, eligibility criteria, funding amount, and funder information
4. Verify that all information is current and accurate
5. Cite sources for each grant

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table with these columns:
- Grant Name
- Funder
- Deadline
- Eligibility Criteria
- Funding Amount
- Source/Link

Ensure the table is properly formatted, sortable by deadline, and includes only opportunities where the organization meets or closely matches eligibility requirements.
```
