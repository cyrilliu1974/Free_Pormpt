# Product Development Roadmap Generator

## 簡介

The Product Development Roadmap Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive product roadmaps with phased timelines, milestones, and risk analysis for product managers and development teams. This product roadmap prompt for ChatGPT walks through your product description and outputs a complete development plan organized into logical phases - from discovery and planning through launch and post-launch optimization. It defines key deliverables for each phase, estimates realistic timelines, and identifies technical dependencies, resource constraints, vendor relationships, and market factors that could impact delivery. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured text roadmaps suitable for stakeholder alignment, sprint planning, and project tracking. Real use cases include SaaS launch planning, feature release sequencing, and cross-functional team coordination. Reach for this prompt when you need to communicate a product vision with clear phases and timelines, align engineering and business stakeholders around a shared schedule, or document dependencies before committing to delivery dates. ● Breaks development into 3-4 logical phases with descriptive names, timelines, and 3-5 deliverables per phase ● Surfaces 4-6 risks and dependencies - technical, resource, vendor, or market - that could shift your schedule ● Provides a total timeline estimate from kickoff to stable release based on product complexity ● Outputs a product overview section that defines purpose, target users, and strategic context for stakeholders ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced product manager creating a comprehensive product roadmap that outlines development timeline, key milestones, major phases, and deliverables.

## Task
Create a detailed product roadmap for the following product:

{{product-description}}

## Output
Structure your roadmap with these sections:

**Product Overview**
Provide a brief overview of the product, its purpose, and target users.

**Roadmap Phases**
Outline 3-4 major phases, each including:
- Phase Name
- Phase Description
- Key Deliverables (3-5 items)
- Estimated Timeline

Progress logically from discovery/planning through launch and post-launch optimization.

**Risks and Dependencies**
Identify 4-6 potential risks or dependencies that may impact the timeline, such as:
- Technical dependencies or infrastructure requirements
- Resource constraints or team availability
- External vendor or partner dependencies
- Market or regulatory factors

**Total Timeline**
Provide an overall estimate for the complete product development cycle from kickoff to stable release.

Ensure timeline estimates are realistic and account for the complexity described in the product details.
```
