# Character-Driven Story Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Character-Driven Story Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that produces detailed narrative frameworks centered on richly developed protagonists for novelists, screenwriters, and creative storytellers. This character-driven story prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models - including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok - to build a complete three-act story structure anchored by a multi-dimensional character. You specify an archetype (hero, mentor, trickster, etc.), genre, and tone, and the prompt generates a protagonist with background, motivations, fears, goals, and flaws, then maps their journey through inciting incident, rising action, midpoint, B-story, low point, climax, and resolution. Each story concept includes a thematic throughline that emerges organically from the character's arc. Reach for this prompt when you need a solid narrative blueprint that puts character transformation at the heart of your story, whether you're outlining a novel, screenplay, short story, or serial fiction. ● Creates protagonists with archetype, age, background, central trait, greatest fear, primary goal, and major flaw clearly defined. ● Maps every major story beat - inciting incident, midpoint twist, B-story subplot, low point, climax, and resolution - so your plot mirrors character growth. ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure linguistic clarity and narrative coherence throughout the output. ● Surfaces the central theme naturally from the character's journey, avoiding forced or preachy messaging. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a masterful storyteller skilled at crafting compelling narratives driven by richly developed characters.

## Task
Generate a detailed story concept centered on a character with distinct traits, background, and archetypal role. Explore how their motivations, strengths, weaknesses, and conflicts propel the plot forward through a complete three-act structure.

## Context
- Character archetype: {{archetype}}
- Story genre: {{genre}}
- Desired tone: {{tone}}

## Requirements
1. Create a multi-dimensional protagonist whose arc drives the narrative
2. Establish clear character motivations, fears, goals, and flaws
3. Structure the story with inciting incident, rising action, midpoint, B-story, low point, climax, and resolution
4. Identify conflicts and how the character overcomes them
5. Articulate the central theme that emerges from the character's journey
6. Use dependency grammar framework throughout

## Output Format

- Midpoint: [Major turning point]

**Act 2:**
- B-Story: [Subplot or relationship arc]

```
