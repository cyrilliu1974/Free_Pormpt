# SEO SWOT Analysis Prompt for Competitive Positioning

## 簡介

The SEO SWOT Analysis Prompt for Competitive Positioning is a free AI prompt that evaluates a company's search engine optimization standing against competitors and surfaces strategic opportunities for SEO analysts and digital marketers. This SEO SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an SEO analyst, researching current keyword rankings, backlink profiles, website performance, and competitor practices, then organizing findings into a structured markdown table with four columns: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. Each cell contains 3-5 evidence-based points that directly inform strategy decisions, such as identifying underutilized keywords, technical SEO gaps, content opportunities, algorithm risks, and competitive moves. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking a single variable describing your company and competitive landscape, then returning a strategic assessment you can act on immediately. Reach for this prompt when you need to benchmark your SEO positioning, prepare a quarterly strategy review, or identify quick wins and defensive priorities in a competitive market. ● Compares SEO strategies, backlink profiles, keyword rankings, and website performance against named competitors ● Delivers findings in a markdown table with Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats columns ● Surfaces specific, actionable insights rather than generic observations, such as content gaps and algorithm risks ● Accepts a single variable describing your company and competitive landscape, making setup fast ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO analyst conducting a competitive SWOT analysis to evaluate market positioning and identify strategic opportunities.

## Task
Deliver a comprehensive SEO SWOT analysis that compares the company against its top competitors. Research and analyze:

- Current SEO strategies, website performance, backlink profile, and keyword rankings
- Competitor SEO practices and points of differentiation
- Strategic gaps and opportunities in the competitive landscape

Provide actionable insights in each SWOT category that directly inform SEO strategy decisions.

## Context
{{company-and-competitive-landscape}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with four columns: **Strengths**, **Weaknesses**, **Opportunities**, and **Threats**.

Each cell should contain 3-5 concise, evidence-based points. Focus on:

- **Strengths**: What SEO advantages does the company hold versus competitors?
- **Weaknesses**: Where does the company fall behind in technical SEO, content, authority, or rankings?
- **Opportunities**: What underutilized keywords, content gaps, or market shifts can be exploited?
- **Threats**: What competitive moves, algorithm changes, or market dynamics pose risks?

Make each insight specific and actionable rather than generic.
```
