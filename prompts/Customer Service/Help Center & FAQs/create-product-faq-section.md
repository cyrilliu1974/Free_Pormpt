# FAQ Section Generator for Products and Services

## 簡介

The FAQ Section Generator for Products and Services is a free AI prompt that builds complete, audience-specific FAQ documentation for any product or service. This FAQ prompt for ChatGPT works by first creating a product overview and identifying the target audience, then organizing questions into five logical categories such as General, Features and Benefits, Pricing and Plans, Troubleshooting, and Customer Support. Each category contains at least three question-and-answer pairs written in plain language with direct, actionable answers that address real customer concerns. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured markdown output ready for help centers, product pages, or support documentation. Use it when launching a new product, refreshing support resources, or reducing repetitive customer inquiries. ● Organizes questions into five custom categories that match your product's actual structure and customer journey ● Ensures every answer is direct, actionable, and written in plain language appropriate for your audience ● Outputs complete markdown documentation including product overview, audience definition, and full category structure ● Eliminates filler and jargon, maintaining a friendly, trustworthy tone that builds customer confidence ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write FAQ sections for products and services. Your output is clear, organized, and written for the audience buying or evaluating the offering.

## Task

Build a complete FAQ section for {{product-or-service}}. Structure it as follows:

1. Write a brief product or service overview (2-4 sentences).
2. Identify the target audience for this FAQ and what they need to understand.
3. Organize questions into five logical categories that fit the offering (for example: General, Features and Benefits, Pricing and Plans, Troubleshooting, Customer Support).
4. Under each category, write at least three questions with full answers.

## Requirements

- Each question addresses a specific customer concern, inquiry, or pain point
- Answers are direct and actionable—no filler
- Language is plain; avoid jargon unless the audience expects it
- Tone is friendly and trustworthy throughout
- No duplicate questions or answers across categories
```
