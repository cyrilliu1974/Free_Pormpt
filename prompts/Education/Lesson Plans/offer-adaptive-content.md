# Adaptive Learning Plan Creator for Students

## 簡介

The Adaptive Learning Plan Creator for Students is a free AI prompt that builds personalized, scaffolded learning plans tailored to each learner's current understanding and specific challenges. This adaptive learning plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a student's baseline knowledge, defines measurable learning objectives, and sequences content into progressive modules with embedded checkpoints and interactive exercises. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured plans that include current-level assessments, concept breakdowns, reinforcement activities, progress milestones, and targeted support strategies for areas of difficulty. Teachers use it to differentiate instruction, tutors apply it to customize one-on-one sessions, and curriculum designers rely on it to scaffold complex topics for diverse learners. Reach for this prompt when you need to design a learning pathway that responds to individual student needs rather than delivering one-size-fits-all content. ● Assesses the learner's current understanding and prior knowledge to establish a baseline ● Breaks complex topics into logically sequenced modules that build on one another ● Embeds interactive elements and formative assessments at each stage to reinforce comprehension ● Identifies progress checkpoints and suggests adaptive support strategies for areas where students struggle ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content developer specializing in adaptive learning design.

## Task
Create a personalized learning plan that enhances student engagement and comprehension. Analyze the learner's current understanding, break complex concepts into manageable chunks, incorporate interactive reinforcement elements, and ensure logical progression of ideas. The plan should adapt to student progress and provide targeted support.

## Context
**Student & Subject:** {{student-subject-context}}
(Include: current level/grade, subject or topic area, and any relevant background)

**Learning Objectives & Challenges:** {{learning-objectives-challenges}}
(Include: specific goals to achieve, preferred learning style if applicable, and particular areas of difficulty or confusion)

## Output
Deliver a structured learning plan with:

- **Current Level Assessment** – summary of where the student is now
- **Learning Objectives** – clear, measurable goals
- **Content Breakdown** – complex concepts divided into progressive modules or lessons
- **Interactive Elements** – activities, questions, or exercises to reinforce each concept
- **Progress Checkpoints** – milestones to assess understanding and adapt the plan
- **Targeted Support** – specific resources or strategies for areas of difficulty

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout.
```
