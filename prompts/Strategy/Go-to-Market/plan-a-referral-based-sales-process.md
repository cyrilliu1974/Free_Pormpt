# Referral-Based Sales Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Referral-Based Sales Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds a complete, phased roadmap for launching and scaling referral partnerships and incentive programs for sales teams and business strategists. This referral-based sales strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed table with seven columns: Phase, Key Activities, Required Resources, Success Metrics, Timeline, Priority, and Estimated ROI Impact. It walks through six phases - Research & Planning, Program Design, Partner Outreach, Program Promotion, Monitoring & Optimization, and Expansion & Iteration - delivering specific activities, measurable targets, and realistic timelines for each stage. You input your target partner types, current referral program status, and strategic goals, and the prompt returns an immediately implementable plan with quantifiable success metrics, priority ratings, and resource requirements. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured action plan to build or revamp a referral channel, design partner incentive tiers, or scale an existing referral program with clear milestones. ● Covers the full lifecycle: partner identification, tiered reward structures, outreach training, tracking systems, and quarterly optimization. ● Outputs a seven-column table with timelines, priority ratings, and ROI impact levels for each phase. ● Includes concrete activities like SMART goal setting, A/B testing incentives, building referral portals, and partner recognition programs. ● Tailored to your target partners, current program maturity, and strategic objectives for immediate execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in referral-based sales and partnership development.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, phased referral-based sales strategy delivered as a detailed table. The strategy must cover the complete lifecycle from partner identification through implementation, monitoring, and scaling.

## Context
The user needs an actionable roadmap to boost partnerships and incentivize sales growth through referrals. The plan should address:
- Target partners: {{target-partners}}
- Current referral program status: {{current-program}}
- Strategic goals: {{strategic-goals}}

## Output
Deliver a table with these columns: **Phase | Key Activities | Required Resources | Success Metrics | Timeline | Priority (1-5) | Est. ROI Impact**

Include these phases with specific, actionable details:

1. **Research & Planning** – Identify ideal partner profiles, analyze competitor programs, establish SMART goals
2. **Program Design** – Create tiered rewards structure, implement referral tracking, develop marketing assets
3. **Partner Outreach** – Build partner list, conduct training, launch referral portal
4. **Program Promotion** – Execute email campaigns, social media outreach, and paid advertising
5. **Monitoring & Optimization** – Track metrics weekly, survey partners quarterly, A/B test incentives
6. **Expansion & Iteration** – Recruit additional partner tiers, launch recognition programs, explore new channels

For each phase, specify concrete key activities, required tools and resources, measurable success metrics (use quantifiable targets where applicable), realistic timelines, priority ratings, and ROI impact levels (Low/Medium/High). Ensure the plan is immediately implementable without further clarification.
```
