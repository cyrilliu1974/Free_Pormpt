# Trademark Coexistence Agreement Prompt

## 簡介

The Trademark Coexistence Agreement Prompt is a free AI prompt that drafts multi-jurisdictional trademark coexistence agreements for intellectual property attorneys and legal professionals. This trademark coexistence agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a comprehensive legal document with nine structured sections: preamble, definitions, territory allocation, channel restrictions, quality standards, dispute resolution, non-challenge provisions, modification and termination clauses, and mutual non-interference obligations. The prompt follows the INTA Model Forms framework and helps attorneys create enforceable agreements by analyzing party information, trademarks, potential conflict zones, and operational contexts to structure territorial and channel-based limitations that enable peaceful coexistence between trademark holders. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft agreements that protect both parties' trademark rights while preventing consumer confusion across different geographic territories, distribution channels, and product categories. ● Analyzes trademark landscapes to identify conflict zones and structures geographic territories with precision to prevent overlap and ensure mutual non-interference. ● Delineates distribution channels, product categories, and channel-specific boundaries with clarity for retail, online, wholesale, and other commercial contexts. ● Establishes quality control measures, inspection rights, and compliance monitoring procedures that protect both brands' reputation. ● Creates mediation and arbitration procedures with clear escalation paths, governing law specifications, and enforceable non-challenge provisions. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an intellectual property attorney specializing in trademark coexistence agreements, with expertise in multi-jurisdictional trademark disputes and the INTA Model Forms framework.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive trademark coexistence agreement that balances mutual rights, prevents consumer confusion, and establishes clear operational boundaries between two parties.

## Context

Analyze the trademark landscape using the provided party information, trademarks, potential conflict zones, and operational contexts. Structure territorial and channel-based limitations that enable peaceful coexistence.

**Party and trademark details:** {{parties-and-trademarks}}

**Operational boundaries and conflict areas:** {{operational-boundaries}}

## Output

Structure the agreement with the following sections, each containing specific clauses:

**Preamble**
- Identify both parties and their respective trademarks
- State the purpose and intent of the coexistence agreement

**Definitions**
- Define key terms: Territory, Channels, Products, Marks, and other relevant concepts

**Territory Allocation**
- Define each party's exclusive geographic territories with precision to prevent overlap
- Specify jurisdictions and any shared or neutral zones

**Channel Restrictions**
- Delineate distribution channels (retail, online, wholesale, etc.) for each party
- Clarify product categories and any category-specific boundaries

**Quality Standards**
- Establish quality control measures that protect both brands' reputation
- Define inspection rights and compliance monitoring procedures

**Dispute Resolution**
- Create mediation and arbitration procedures with clear escalation paths
- Specify governing law and venue

**Non-Challenge Provisions**
- Include clauses preventing either party from challenging the other's trademark rights within defined boundaries
- Address co-existence acknowledgment and good-faith obligations

**Modification and Termination**
- Define conditions for agreement modification
- Specify termination triggers and notice requirements
- Address successor and assign obligations

**Mutual Non-Interference**
- Establish affirmative duties to avoid encroachment
- Define prohibited conduct and remedies for breach

Ensure all provisions are enforceable and protect both parties' interests equally.
```
