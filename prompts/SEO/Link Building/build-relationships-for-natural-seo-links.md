# Natural SEO Link Building Outreach Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Natural SEO Link Building Outreach Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive backlink acquisition strategies for digital marketers and SEO professionals. This link building prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your industry, website, target audience, unique selling proposition, and content specialties to produce a complete outreach plan. It identifies relevant websites, blogs, and influencers who are natural fit partners, crafts personalized outreach message frameworks that emphasize genuine value exchange, and designs tracking systems to nurture long-term relationships. The output is delivered as a numbered action plan with lettered sub-steps, prioritizing prospects by domain authority, audience alignment, and partnership potential. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic link requests and build authentic relationships that yield natural, high-quality backlinks that improve search rankings. ● Identifies high-value link prospects within your specific industry and content niche ● Creates personalized outreach message templates that establish mutual value rather than one-sided requests ● Designs relationship tracking and nurturing systems to maintain ongoing partnerships ● Prioritizes targets based on domain authority, audience overlap, and long-term partnership potential ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in backlink acquisition and strategic relationship building.

## Task
Create a comprehensive outreach plan to acquire high-quality, natural backlinks that will improve SEO rankings. The plan must be structured, actionable, and tailored to the specific business context.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Website: {{website-url}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Content specialties: {{content-specialties}}

## Process
Develop the plan through these components:
- Identify relevant websites, blogs, and influencers within the industry who would be natural link partners
- Craft personalized outreach messages that establish genuine value exchange
- Design a system for tracking outreach efforts and nurturing ongoing relationships
- Prioritize prospects based on domain authority, audience alignment, and partnership potential

## Output
Provide the complete outreach plan as a numbered list. Use sub-points with letters (a, b, c) for detailed steps, explanations, or action items within each main point.
```
