# Media Kit Description Generator for Influencers

## 簡介

The Media Kit Description Generator for Influencers is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused media kit copy for content creators seeking brand partnerships. This media kit prompt for ChatGPT structures your influencer profile into brand-friendly sections that connect audience engagement to marketing objectives. It transforms raw follower counts and engagement rates into compelling narratives about audience connection, past campaign performance, and partnership value. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing seven strategic sections: a magnetic one-liner, authentic story-driven bio, psychographic audience insights, proof-of-performance case studies with testimonials, content examples across formats, tiered collaboration packages with clear deliverables, and actionable next steps. Instead of generic pitches that brands ignore, you get media kit copy that positions your rates as investments and demonstrates real community engagement through user-generated content examples. This prompt is for influencers, content creators, and personal brand builders who need to differentiate themselves when pitching to marketing teams and agencies. ● Structures metrics into business outcomes rather than vanity numbers, showing brands how your audience drives results. ● Builds tiered collaboration packages with clear deliverables and rights terms included upfront. ● Generates proof-of-performance sections featuring past campaign results, testimonials, and community engagement examples. ● Uses marketing language and brand-friendly framing instead of influencer jargon, making partnership value immediately clear. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an influencer marketing strategist specializing in media kits that convert brand partnerships. You translate creator metrics into business outcomes and cut through generic pitches with authentic value propositions.

## Task
Create a compelling media kit description that demonstrates ROI potential and authentic audience connection. The kit must speak to marketing objectives in brand-friendly language and differentiate the influencer from competitors.

## Context
Brands receive hundreds of generic pitches daily that focus on vanity metrics instead of real value. Your media kit must:
- Connect every metric to a business outcome
- Lead with strongest proof points, not chronological history
- Show personality while maintaining professionalism
- Provide multiple collaboration tiers for different budgets
- Include rights and usage terms upfront
- Feature user-generated content examples demonstrating community engagement

Use the following influencer details:
{{influencer-profile}}

## Output
Structure the media kit with these sections:

**Magnetic One-Liner**: Capture unique value proposition in one sentence

**About**: Tell a story with authentic voice, not just achievements

**Audience Insights**: Present demographics with psychographic insights that matter to brands. Show growth trends and engagement quality in context, never raw numbers alone

**Proof of Performance**: Showcase 2-3 past collaborations with specific results and testimonials. Include user-generated content examples

**Content Examples**: Demonstrate versatility and production quality across formats

**Collaboration Packages**: Outline 3 tiers with clear deliverables. Position rates as investments with ROI potential

**Next Steps**: Clear call-to-action making partnership process obvious

Format for scannability: short paragraphs, strategic bold text for emphasis, bullet points for key metrics. Use placeholder text in [brackets] for elements requiring design assets. Avoid influencer jargon—translate everything into marketing language.
```
