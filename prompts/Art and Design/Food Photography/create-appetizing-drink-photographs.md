# Appetizing Drink Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Appetizing Drink Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates commercial-quality beverage images with dessert styling, chocolate drizzle accents, and natural light on a clean white backdrop. This drink photography prompt for Midjourney produces tall-glass compositions shot from a side view in soft daylight, with minimal props like cocoa nibs or mint leaves and tack-sharp product focus enhanced by condensation highlights. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering 4:5 aspect ratio images ideal for menu design, social media marketing, and product catalogs. Reach for this prompt when you need professional drink photography that emphasizes creamy textures and dessert drink aesthetics without arranging a physical shoot. ● Produces wide-shot drink photographs in tall glasses with side-view angles and soft natural daylight. ● Highlights chocolate drizzle, creamy textures, and condensation details with tack-sharp product focus. ● Includes minimal styling props such as cocoa nibs and mint leaves against a clean white backdrop. ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images optimized for Instagram, menus, and product marketing materials. ## Prompt

```

```
