# Freelance Testimonial Collection System

## 簡介

The Freelance Testimonial Collection System is a free AI prompt that creates a complete strategy for gathering and presenting client testimonials to build your freelance credibility. This freelance testimonial prompt for ChatGPT walks you through identifying high-impact projects from your portfolio, drafting personalized outreach emails that make it easy for clients to respond, and formatting testimonials into a professional showcase. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by taking your professional profile - services offered, notable projects, target clients, and differentiators - and producing both a customizable outreach email template and a structured testimonial display format. Freelancers use it to transform past client relationships into social proof that wins new business, particularly when launching a portfolio site, updating LinkedIn, or preparing proposals. ● Identifies which past projects will yield the most impactful testimonials for your niche ● Creates polite, personalized outreach email templates that clients can respond to quickly ● Guides clients to mention specific challenges solved, measurable results, and working style ● Formats collected testimonials into a professional showcase organized by client type and skill demonstrated ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional freelancer building credibility through client testimonials.

## Task
Create a strategic plan to gather, format, and present compelling client testimonials that demonstrate your value and expertise.

## Context
**Your professional profile:**
{{professional-profile}}
(Include: freelance services offered, 3-5 notable projects with brief descriptions, target client base, years of experience, and what sets you apart from competitors)

## Process
1. **Identify high-impact projects** – Review your portfolio and select work with measurable outcomes, satisfied clients, and relevance to your target market
2. **Craft outreach requests** – Write polite, personalized messages that make it easy for clients to respond
3. **Provide testimonial guidance** – Suggest clients mention specific challenges solved, measurable results achieved, your working style, and what made the collaboration successful
4. **Compile and format** – Organize testimonials to highlight diverse skills and client types

## Output
Deliver two components:

**A. Draft outreach email template** tailored to your services and client relationships

**B. Formatted testimonial showcase** presenting collected testimonials as:

• Client Name, Company – Project Title
  "Testimonial text highlighting key outcomes and your expertise."

• Client Name, Company – Project Title
  "Testimonial text highlighting key outcomes and your expertise."
```
