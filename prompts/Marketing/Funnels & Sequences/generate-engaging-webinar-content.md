# Webinar Series Planner for Product Launches

## 簡介

The Webinar Series Planner for Product Launches is a free AI prompt that creates a structured three-webinar campaign framework for businesses launching new products. This webinar content prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete series that progresses audiences through awareness, consideration, and conversion stages. It outputs audience profiles, webinar titles and objectives, topic outlines, calls-to-action, multi-channel promotion strategies (email, social, influencer, paid ads), and measurable success metrics including registration, attendance, engagement, and conversion goals. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your product details, target audience, and business context to deliver a launch-ready webinar plan. Companies use it to replace scattered planning documents with a single cohesive series that aligns content with value propositions and audience pain points. This prompt is for product marketers, launch managers, and content strategists who need a repeatable framework to turn webinars into conversion engines rather than one-off presentations. ● Builds three sequenced webinars that move audiences from awareness to purchase decision ● Generates audience profiles with demographics, psychographics, pain points, and goals ● Creates promotion strategies across email, social media, influencer partnerships, and paid channels ● Defines measurable KPIs for registration, attendance, engagement, and conversion rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert webinar planner and content strategist creating a compelling webinar series to support a product launch.

## Task
Develop a complete three-webinar series framework that generates excitement, aligns with the product's unique value proposition, and drives conversions among the target audience.

## Context
**Product & Market:**
{{product-details}}

**Target Audience:**
{{target-audience}}

**Business Background:**
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with:

**Webinar Series Title:**
A captivating title reflecting both the product and audience.

**Target Audience Profile:**
- Demographics
- Psychographics
- Pain Points
- Goals

**Product Overview:**
- Name
- Description
- Unique Value Proposition

**Three Webinars** (for each provide):
- Title
- Objective
- Key Topics (3-5 points)
- Call to Action

**Promotion Strategy:**
- Email Marketing approach
- Social Media tactics
- Influencer Partnerships
- Paid Advertising channels

**Success Metrics:**
- Registration Goal (number)
- Attendance Rate (%)
- Engagement Rate (%)
- Conversion Rate (%)

Ensure each webinar builds momentum toward the launch, with clear CTAs that move attendees through awareness, consideration, and conversion stages.
```
