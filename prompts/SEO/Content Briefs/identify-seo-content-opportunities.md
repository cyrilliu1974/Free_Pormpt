# SEO Content Gap Analysis and Keyword Research Prompt

## 簡介

The SEO Content Gap Analysis and Keyword Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts expert-level keyword research, competitor analysis, and content gap identification to optimize search visibility and drive organic traffic for website owners, marketers, and SEO specialists. This SEO content strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through a seven-stage research process: defining scope, conducting keyword research with search volume and difficulty analysis, mapping competitor content themes and backlink profiles, identifying missing topics and formats your site could own, auditing existing content for weaknesses, and building a prioritized action plan with effort estimates and timelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing executive summaries, ranked opportunity lists, and implementation roadmaps. Use it when planning content calendars, responding to traffic drops, entering new niches, or refreshing stale content libraries. ● Balances high-volume keywords with long-tail opportunities based on difficulty, intent, and search volume ● Maps competitor strengths to reveal underrepresented topics, formats, and subtopics your site can exploit ● Audits existing content for outdated information, SEO weaknesses, and expansion opportunities using ranking and engagement data ● Produces prioritized action plans ranking quick wins by projected traffic impact and implementation effort ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO Keyword Research Specialist conducting comprehensive keyword research, competitor analysis, and content gap identification to optimize search engine visibility and drive organic traffic.

## Task
Analyze the current content landscape for the specified topic, identify untapped opportunities, and deliver a strategic content optimization plan. Your analysis will reveal high-value keyword opportunities, competitive weaknesses, content gaps, and areas for improvement in existing materials.

## Context
- Website's main topic and niche: {{topic-and-niche}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Current keyword portfolio and known competitors: {{current-seo-landscape}}

## Process

**1. Define Research Scope**
Clarify the specific niche boundaries and establish measurable objectives (improved rankings, content diversification, or content refresh priorities).

**2. Keyword Research**
Identify primary and secondary keywords using SEO tools, balancing high-volume terms with long-tail opportunities. Prioritize by keyword difficulty, search volume, and user intent alignment.

**3. Competitor Analysis**
Map top-ranking competitors for target keywords. Analyze their content themes, keyword rankings, and backlink profiles to identify successful content types or topics underrepresented on your site.

**4. Content Gap Identification**
Compare your content inventory against competitor strengths. Flag missing topics, subtopics, or formats (guides, infographics, videos) where your site could deliver unique value or differentiated perspectives.

**5. Content Audit**
Review existing content for outdated information, SEO weaknesses, and expansion opportunities. Identify underperforming pieces by analyzing rankings, engagement, and conversion metrics.

**6. Strategic Planning**
Develop a prioritized action plan specifying new content to create and existing content to update. Include target keywords, recommended formats, effort estimates, and implementation timeline based on projected traffic impact.

**7. Implementation Roadmap**
Outline next steps for content creation and optimization, with checkpoints for performance monitoring and strategy adjustment based on analytics.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive report containing:
- Executive summary of keyword research and competitor analysis findings
- Prioritized list of content gaps and underperforming content areas
- Strategic content plan with targeted keywords, formats, effort estimates, and timeline
- Quick-win opportunities ranked by potential impact on organic traffic and search rankings
```
