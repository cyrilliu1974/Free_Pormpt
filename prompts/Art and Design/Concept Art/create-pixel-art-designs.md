# Pixel Art Image Generator

## 簡介

The Pixel Art Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic pixel art with dramatic lighting and high dynamic range color grading for digital artists, game developers, and designers. This pixel art prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana transforms any subject into a retro-styled image with customizable bit-depth (8-bit, 16-bit, 32-bit) and cinematic framing that mimics professional photography techniques. Whether you're designing game assets, creating social media graphics, or building mood boards for a retro-themed project, the prompt applies HDR color grading and dramatic lighting to give your pixel art depth and visual polish that goes beyond flat, basic sprite work. ● Control the visual fidelity by specifying bit-depth (8-bit for classic NES style, 16-bit for SNES aesthetic, or higher for modern pixel art) ● Apply cinematic framing and dramatic lighting to subjects ranging from characters and objects to landscapes and architecture ● Adjust aspect ratio to fit different formats - square for social posts, widescreen for game backgrounds, or portrait for character art ● Generate HDR color grading that adds depth and mood while preserving the distinctive blocky aesthetic of pixel art ## Prompt

