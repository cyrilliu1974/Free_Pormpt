# Team Building Activity Planner Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Team Building Activity Planner Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates customized corporate team-building activity plans for managers, HR professionals, and facilitators. This team building activity planner prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your team size, industry context, constraints, and goals to generate at least five diverse activities spanning competitive exercises, creative challenges, problem-solving tasks, and trust-building experiences. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, it produces a markdown table with activity names, detailed implementation descriptions including materials and duration, and specific expected outcomes tied to collaboration, communication, and interpersonal trust. Use it when planning offsites, quarterly team sessions, onboarding programs, or addressing specific team dynamics challenges within budget and time limits. ● Tailors each activity to your team's industry, size, and stated challenges or objectives ● Balances activity types across competitive, creative, problem-solving, and trust-building formats for well-rounded engagement ● Includes materials lists, time estimates, and step-by-step facilitation instructions for easy implementation ● Specifies observable outcomes so you can measure improvements in communication, collaboration, and team cohesion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert team-building facilitator designing practical activities for corporate teams.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan of at least 5 diverse team-building activities that foster collaboration, communication, and trust among team members. Tailor each activity to the specific team context provided, ensuring they are practical and easy to implement.

## Context
Team and organizational details:
{{team-and-context}}

Specific constraints or goals:
{{constraints-and-goals}}

## Requirements
- Select activities suited to the team size, industry, and any stated challenges
- Ensure variety in activity types (competitive, creative, problem-solving, trust-building)
- Account for budget and time constraints when applicable
- Include clear implementation guidance in each description
- Explain the measurable or observable outcomes for each activity

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns:

| Activity Name | Description | Expected Outcomes |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|

Provide at least 5 activities, each row containing:
- **Activity Name**: A clear, descriptive title
- **Description**: What the activity involves, how to run it, materials needed, and approximate duration
- **Expected Outcomes**: Specific teamwork skills or relationship improvements this activity develops
```
