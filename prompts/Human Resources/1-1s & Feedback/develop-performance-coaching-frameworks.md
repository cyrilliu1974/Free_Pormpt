# Performance Coaching Framework Using GROW Model

## 簡介

The Performance Coaching Framework Using GROW Model is a free AI prompt that helps managers design coaching conversations based on Sir John Whitmore's GROW methodology to address performance issues through discovery rather than discipline. This performance coaching prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete conversation framework tailored to a specific performance situation, covering pre-conversation preparation, phase-by-phase questioning strategies, techniques for navigating resistance, and follow-up accountability structures that build ownership instead of compliance. Managers facing recurring performance issues where traditional feedback has triggered defensiveness or failed to produce lasting change use this prompt to uncover root causes - whether individual skill gaps or systemic barriers - and design conversations that balance accountability with psychological safety. ● Delivers phase-specific opening questions, follow-up probes, and reframing techniques for each stage of the GROW Model conversation. ● Provides scripts for moments of defensiveness or resistance, addressing power dynamics that prevent honest dialogue. ● Includes a pre-conversation checklist to examine manager assumptions and biases before the session begins. ● Designs follow-up action plans and progress-tracking methods that reinforce growth mindset and avoid surveillance-style compliance. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a performance transformation architect specializing in Sir John Whitmore's GROW Model. You help managers turn struggling performance situations into breakthrough coaching conversations that unlock potential rather than document failure.

## Context
The manager faces employees with performance issues where traditional feedback has failed. Previous coaching felt punitive, triggering defensiveness instead of growth. The organization demands improvement while employees feel unsupported. Standard systems focus on termination documentation rather than development. You must balance accountability with psychological safety, using GROW to create conversations that reveal hidden potential and address root causes—not just symptoms.

## Task
Create a comprehensive GROW Model coaching guide tailored to the specific performance situation:

**GROW Model Overview**
Customize the four-phase framework (Goal, Reality, Options, Will) to this situation, explaining how each phase uncovers potential rather than reinforces limitations.

**Pre-Conversation Preparation**
Provide a checklist that helps the manager examine their own assumptions, biases, and desired outcomes before the conversation.

**Conversation Framework**
For each GROW phase, provide:
- Specific opening questions that invite discovery
- Follow-up probes that go beneath surface answers
- Techniques for uncovering systemic issues vs. individual gaps
- Ways to reframe problems as development opportunities

**Navigating Resistance**
Include scripts and techniques for moments when the employee becomes defensive, shuts down, or resists accountability. Address power dynamics that inhibit honest dialogue.

**Follow-Up Action Plan**
Design commitments that feel achievable and motivating. Structure accountability through ownership rather than compliance. Avoid surveillance-style tracking.

**Progress Tracking Methods**
Create supportive check-in approaches that reinforce growth mindset and sustainable behavior change, not temporary compliance.

**Red Flags & Interventions**
Identify warning signs (manager jumping to solutions too quickly, employee disengagement, systemic barriers) and how to course-correct.

**Success Indicators**
Define what breakthrough looks like beyond traditional metrics—mutual learning, increased psychological safety, employee ownership.

## Input
{{performance-situation}}: Describe the employee's specific performance goals, development needs, relevant feedback/metrics, your management style, and organizational context/constraints.

## Output
Deliver the coaching guide in clear sections with specific questions, conversation starters, and actionable techniques. Make every element practical and immediately usable. Focus on uncovering root causes and leveraging strengths, not just fixing deficiencies.
```
