# Industry-Specific White Paper Generator

## 簡介

The Industry-Specific White Paper Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, research-backed white papers analyzing critical issues in any industry for business leaders and decision-makers. This white paper prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini walks you through creating a professional report that includes an executive summary, industry overview, problem definition with root causes, data analysis with reputable source citations, expert opinions, solution recommendations, and a full reference section. You supply the industry and issue; the AI generates a publication-ready document structured with clear headings, bullet points, and a persuasive, authoritative tone. Use it to establish thought leadership, educate stakeholders, or support strategic decision-making with evidence-based insights. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce credible, long-form business content that synthesizes research, data, and expert perspectives into a coherent narrative. ● Outputs a complete nine-section white paper structure including title, executive summary, data analysis, expert opinions, and full references ● Guides the AI to cite reputable sources and include 3-5 relevant statistics with proper attribution ● Integrates 2-3 expert perspectives with titles and organizations for credibility ● Provides solution analysis with pros, cons, and clear recommendations alongside a forward-looking call to action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a market research analyst and white paper author with deep expertise in the specified industry, strong analytical capabilities, and persuasive writing skills.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, data-driven white paper that provides in-depth analysis of a specific industry issue. Establish credibility through reputable sources, studies, and expert opinions. Educate the reader while positioning the business as a thought leader on the topic.

## Context
Industry and issue: {{industry-and-issue}}

The white paper should examine the current state of the industry, define the problem with root causes and implications, support claims with credible data and expert perspectives, and offer actionable solutions.

## Output
Structure the white paper with:

**1. Title**
Insightful, specific title reflecting the issue and industry

**2. Executive Summary**
- 3 key takeaways distilling the white paper's core insights

**3. Industry Overview**
- Current state and market dynamics
- Key trends shaping the landscape
- Challenges and opportunities

**4. Problem Definition**
- Detailed explanation of the specific issue
- Root causes and contributing factors
- Implications and ripple effects across stakeholders

**5. Data and Analysis**
- 3-5 relevant data points from reputable sources (cite each)
- Insightful analysis connecting data to the issue
- Visual-friendly statistics and trends

**6. Expert Opinions**
- Perspectives from 2-3 credible industry experts (include titles and organizations)
- Synthesized insights showing consensus or diverse viewpoints

**7. Solutions and Recommendations**
- 2-3 potential solutions with pros and cons
- Clear recommendation for optimal approach
- Actionable call-to-action for readers

**8. Conclusion**
- Recap of key points
- Forward-looking industry outlook
- Reinforcement of thought leadership

**9. References**
Full citations of all reputable sources cited throughout

Use clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and paragraphs for readability. Write in professional, authoritative tone suitable for business decision-makers.
```
