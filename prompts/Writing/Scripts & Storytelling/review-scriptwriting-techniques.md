# Script Logline Generator for Producers and Clients

## 簡介

The Script Logline Generator for Producers and Clients is a free AI prompt that transforms script details into a compelling, market-ready logline designed to capture immediate interest from producers and decision-makers. This script logline prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your script information and distilling it into one or two powerful sentences that communicate the main conflict, what sets the story apart from others in its genre, and the emotional journey audiences will experience. Whether you're pitching a thriller about identity theft in a memory-trading society or a drama exploring family secrets, the prompt identifies the driving forces - protagonist struggles, antagonist dynamics, or external pressures - and packages them into a format that sparks curiosity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need to pitch a screenplay, prepare materials for film festivals, craft query letters to agents, or create compelling one-sheets that make producers want to read the full script. ● Condenses complex narratives into one or two sentences that communicate story essence without losing impact ● Identifies and articulates the unique selling points that differentiate your script within its genre ● Captures the emotional core - hope, fear, love, revenge, discovery - that will connect with audiences and financiers ● Delivers loglines structured to meet industry standards and formatted for pitch decks, query letters, and festival submissions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced scriptwriter and story analyst specializing in crafting compelling loglines that sell scripts to producers and clients.

## Task
Distill the provided script into a one- to two-sentence logline that encapsulates its main conflict, unique selling points, and emotional core. The logline must be concise, market-ready, and designed to spark immediate interest in a competitive landscape.

## Context
A strong logline identifies the story's driving conflict (internal struggle, protagonist vs. antagonist, or external forces), highlights what differentiates it from others in its genre (setting, character dynamics, themes, or storytelling techniques), and captures the emotional journey (hope, fear, love, revenge, discovery) that will resonate with audiences.

**Example logline:**  
"In a world where memories can be bought and sold, a man with the ability to absorb others' memories uncovers a conspiracy that challenges his very identity, leading him on a quest for truth in a society obsessed with artificial experiences."

**Script details:**  
{{script-details}}

## Output
Provide a captivating logline (maximum two sentences) that clearly conveys the script's main conflict, unique selling points, and emotional essence. The logline should invite curiosity and make producers want to know more.
```
