This double exposure portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by merging two visual elements - your chosen subject and a scene overlay - into a single artistic composition, perfect for album covers, poster design, social media content, or portfolio pieces that require a dramatic, layered aesthetic.
