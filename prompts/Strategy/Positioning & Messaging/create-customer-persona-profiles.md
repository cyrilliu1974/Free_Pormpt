# Customer Persona Profile Generator for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Customer Persona Profile Generator for Marketing Teams is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive customer personas to guide product development and marketing strategy for businesses and agencies. This customer persona prompt for ChatGPT takes your client context - industry, target market, products, and brand positioning - and produces three distinct personas complete with demographics, psychographics, behavioral patterns, goals, challenges, and narrative stories. Each persona includes product and marketing implications with actionable recommendations for messaging, channel selection, and tactical approaches. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured profiles that blend quantitative data with qualitative insights to create authentic, empathy-building representations of your target segments. Marketing strategists, product managers, UX researchers, and agency teams reach for this prompt when they need to transform market assumptions into concrete personas that inform feature prioritization, positioning decisions, and campaign planning. ● Produces three complete personas with demographics, psychographics, behaviors, narratives, and strategic implications ● Includes separate product and marketing recommendation sections tailored to each persona's needs and preferences ● Emphasizes nuance and authenticity over stereotypes, ensuring personas reflect real market segments ● Delivers actionable insights for feature prioritization, messaging, channel selection, and positioning decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in data-driven customer persona development. Your task is to create comprehensive personas that will guide product development and marketing strategy.

## Task

Develop 3 distinct customer personas for the client's target market, followed by strategic recommendations. Each persona must represent an important market segment and provide actionable insights.

## Context

**Client Overview:**
{{client-context}}

Include: industry, target market description, products/services offered, and current brand positioning.

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

**Executive Summary**

Brief overview of the three personas and key strategic themes.

**Persona 1: [Name]**
- **Demographics:** Age, gender, income, location, family status
- **Psychographics:** Personality traits, core values, lifestyle
- **Behaviors:** Hobbies, media consumption, shopping habits, brand preferences
- **Goals, Challenges, and Needs:** What drives them and what obstacles they face
- **Preferred Features and Channels:** Product expectations and where they engage
- **Narrative:** A realistic 2-3 paragraph story that brings this persona to life

**Persona 2: [Name]**
[Same structure as Persona 1]

**Persona 3: [Name]**
[Same structure as Persona 1]

**Product Implications**
- Design considerations
- Feature priorities
- Positioning recommendations

**Marketing Implications**
- Key messages for each persona
- Recommended channels
- Tactical approaches

## Requirements

- Each persona must represent a distinct, valuable market segment
- Blend quantitative data with qualitative insights for vivid, realistic portraits
- Avoid stereotypes; prioritize nuance and authenticity
- Focus on details directly relevant to the client's offerings and marketing goals
- Ensure narratives build empathy and understanding
- All recommendations must be specific and actionable
```
