# Delivery Thank-You Note Email & SMS Template Generator

## 簡介

The Delivery Thank-You Note Email & SMS Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates post-purchase communication templates tailored to brand voice and customer segments for e-commerce businesses and customer experience teams. This delivery thank-you note prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your brand's emotional signature and customer post-purchase needs to produce email, SMS, and physical package insert templates. It applies behavioral psychology - specifically the Peak-End Rule and reciprocity - to design messages that include surprising value offers, personalized gratitude, and memorable closings. Use it when launching a new product line, refining your post-purchase experience, or segmenting thank-you messages for first-time buyers, repeat customers, and high-value orders. Reach for this prompt if you manage e-commerce operations, own a direct-to-consumer brand, or design customer journey touchpoints and need templates that feel personal rather than automated. ● Produces email subject lines, body copy, SMS versions, and package insert formats adapted to three customer segments. ● Embeds a reciprocity strategy - exclusive tips, bonus content, early access, or personalized recommendations - that aligns with brand personality. ● Includes an implementation guide covering automation setup, customer service briefing, and A/B testing frameworks with target metrics (40%+ open rate, 15%+ review rate, 10%+ referral activation). ● Crafts warm openings, sincere gratitude in authentic brand voice, quick support access, and soft review or referral requests with easy links. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer experience strategist specializing in post-purchase communication design. You apply behavioral psychology principles—the Peak-End Rule and reciprocity—to turn delivery thank-you notes into loyalty-building moments.

## Task
Create delivery thank-you note templates (email, SMS, and package insert) tailored to the brand voice and customer segment. Analyze the brand's emotional signature, identify customer needs at delivery, craft personal language, embed surprising value, and design memorable closings.

## Context
{{brand-context}}

**Include:**
- Brand personality traits and authentic voice
- Typical customer post-purchase concerns
- Customer segment characteristics (first-time, repeat, high-value)
- Product category or examples

{{value-offer}}

**Describe the gesture or value-add to include:**
- Exclusive tip, bonus content, early access, personal recommendation, or specific offer

## Output
Provide:

### 1. Reciprocity Strategy
Explain the chosen value exchange and why it aligns with the brand.

### 2. Multi-Channel Templates

**Email Version:**
- Subject line with personalization
- Warm opening acknowledging the customer's choice
- Sincere gratitude in brand voice
- Quick support access line
- Surprising value headline with actionable tip
- Optional perk with clear benefit
- Soft review/referral request with easy link
- Memorable sign-off

**SMS Version:**
- Mobile-optimized, concise version maintaining emotional impact

**Package Insert:**
- Physical format designed for tactile interaction

### 3. Segment Variations
Adapt templates for:
- First-time buyers (welcome and guidance)
- Repeat customers (recognition and deeper value)
- High-value orders (premium touches)

### 4. Implementation Guide
- Setup steps for email automation, customer service briefing, SMS sequences
- A/B testing framework (subject lines, review rates, referral clicks)
- Success metrics: 40%+ open rate, 15%+ review rate, 10%+ referral activation, positive sentiment

Ensure every template feels personal, not templated, and leverages the delivery moment as a peak experience.
```
