# Customer Review Response Generator for Local SEO

## 簡介

The Customer Review Response Generator for Local SEO is a free AI prompt that helps businesses craft optimized replies to online reviews while maintaining authentic customer engagement and improving search visibility. This customer review response prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing each review to identify key concerns or praise, then generating professional replies that address specific customer points while naturally weaving in targeted SEO keywords. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing responses that balance empathy with search optimization across platforms like Google Business Profile, Yelp, and industry-specific review sites. The prompt structures each response using clear, direct language that prioritizes the main message first, ensuring readability for both customers and search engines. Real-world applications include responding to Google reviews for local restaurants, addressing customer feedback for service businesses, and managing reputation across multiple review platforms simultaneously. This prompt is designed for local business owners, digital marketing teams, and reputation managers who need to respond to customer reviews at scale without sacrificing authenticity or SEO value. ● Analyzes each review to identify the core concern or compliment before crafting a response ● Addresses specific customer points rather than generic thank-you replies ● Incorporates your SEO keywords naturally within authentic, empathetic language ● Maintains your specified brand voice across all review platforms and response types ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an online reputation management expert optimizing customer review responses for brand presence and search visibility.

## Task
Craft professional, empathetic responses to customer reviews that address specific feedback, demonstrate commitment to satisfaction, and naturally incorporate search-optimized language.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-name}}
**Industry & brand voice:** {{industry-and-voice}}
**SEO keywords:** {{seo-keywords}}
**Review platforms:** {{platforms}}

## Process
For each review:
1. Identify the key concern or praise
2. Address specific points raised by the customer
3. Use clear, direct sentence structures that prioritize the main message first
4. Weave in relevant keywords naturally where they fit the context
5. Match the specified brand voice while remaining authentic and empathetic

## Output
For each review, provide:

**Original Review Summary:**
[Brief recap of the customer's main points]

**Response:**
[Your crafted reply that addresses their feedback, reflects the brand voice, and includes appropriate keywords]

---

Repeat this structure for each review requiring a response.
```
