# Contemporary Japanese Lounge Interior Render Prompt

## 簡介

The Contemporary Japanese Lounge Interior Render Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic architectural visualizations of serene, minimalist lounge spaces inspired by traditional Japanese design principles for architects, interior designers, and hospitality brands. This contemporary Japanese lounge prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates wide eye-level interior perspectives showcasing open-plan spaces with clearly defined calm zones, clean horizontal lines, and human-centered proportions rooted in ma (negative space) and wabi-sabi philosophy. You define the lounge program, material palette, and color scheme to generate high-resolution 1:1 renders featuring natural materials with matte, imperfect finishes, diffused lighting reminiscent of shoji screens, and sparse low-profile furnishings like floor-level seating, solid wood tables, and minimal ikebana arrangements. Use it to visualize hospitality interiors, create real estate marketing assets, or develop client presentations without expensive 3D rendering services. ● Produces ultra-realistic lounge interiors with customizable zones, material palettes (wood, stone, paper, fabric), and nature-derived color schemes in low contrast tones. ● Controls lighting atmosphere through natural diffused light and warm indirect fixtures that mimic traditional lanterns and hidden linear sources. ● Specifies low-profile furniture, sculptural indoor trees, stone arrangements, and minimal decor to maintain quiet luxury and avoid over-decoration. ● Enforces wabi-sabi aesthetics and ma philosophy for timeless, contemplative spaces grounded in traditional Japanese spatial design. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic architectural visualization of a contemporary Japanese lounge interior inspired by ryokan calmness and urban minimalism. Wide interior perspective at eye level, 1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution.

**Space & Architecture:**
Open-plan lounge with clearly defined calm zones: {{lounge-program}}. Clean lines, low horizontal emphasis, subtle curves following ma (negative space) and wabi-sabi philosophy. Human-centered proportions with slow, intuitive circulation encouraging pause and stillness.

**Materials & Finishes:**
{{material-palette}} with matte, natural, imperfect but refined finishes. Nature-integrated textures, high tactile detail.

**Color & Light:**
{{color-scheme}} using low contrast, nature-derived tones. Natural light filtered and diffused like shoji screens. Warm indirect artificial lighting from paper or fabric lantern-style fixtures and hidden linear lights emphasizing architecture. Soft shadows, intimate, tranquil mood with soft global illumination.

**Furnishings & Elements:**
Low-profile sofas, floor-level lounge seating, simple wooden stools. Solid wood or stone tables with minimal detailing. Sparse decor: bonsai or sculptural indoor trees, stone arrangements, minimal ikebana floral compositions—very few objects, each with purpose.

Quiet luxury aesthetic: zen calm, refined and contemplative, timeless and grounded. Avoid luxury hotel clichés, over-decoration, bright artificial colors, ornamental excess, western bar aesthetics.
```
