# Business Plan Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Plan Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive business plans with ten detailed sections including executive summaries, market analysis, financial projections, and implementation roadmaps. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT walks through every component founders, entrepreneurs, and business strategists need: company mission and vision, target market identification, competitive landscape analysis, product descriptions with unique selling propositions, marketing and sales strategies, operational workflows, multi-year financial forecasts with break-even analysis, phased implementation timelines, and risk mitigation plans. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking a single business-context variable and returning a professionally structured document ready for investor presentations, internal alignment, or strategic planning sessions. Use it when launching a startup, seeking funding, entering new markets, or refining an existing business strategy. ● Produces ten-section plans with executive summary, market analysis, competitive positioning, and 3-5 year financial forecasts ● Includes operational details like organizational structure, supply chain management, and production workflows ● Generates phased implementation roadmaps with milestones, timelines, and risk mitigation strategies ● Emphasizes realistic assumptions, credible data, and clear articulation of competitive advantages ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist developing a comprehensive, actionable business plan that outlines strategic goals, key initiatives, financial projections, and an operational roadmap.

## Task
Create a complete business plan that serves as a guiding blueprint for effective decision-making and resource allocation.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a well-structured business plan document with the following sections:

### 1. Executive Summary
Provide a high-level overview of the key points covered in the business plan.

### 2. Company Overview
- **Mission**: Define the company's mission statement
- **Vision**: Articulate the company's long-term vision
- **Core Values**: List the company's guiding principles

### 3. Market Analysis
- **Target Market**: Identify and describe the target market
- **Market Size**: Estimate the addressable market size
- **Market Trends**: Discuss relevant trends and their potential impact
- **Competitive Landscape**: Analyze main competitors and competitive positioning

### 4. Products and Services
For each offering, provide:
- Name and detailed description
- Unique selling proposition and differentiating features

### 5. Marketing and Sales Strategy
- **Marketing Objectives**: Define marketing goals
- **Marketing Channels**: Identify key channels and tactics
- **Sales Strategy**: Outline the sales approach
- **Pricing Strategy**: Describe pricing methodology

### 6. Operational Plan
- **Key Personnel**: Identify critical roles and team members
- **Organizational Structure**: Illustrate the company hierarchy
- **Production Process**: Detail service delivery or production workflow
- **Supply Chain Management**: Explain supply chain approach

### 7. Financial Projections
- **Revenue Model**: Describe revenue generation mechanisms
- **Sales Forecast**: Project sales for 3-5 years
- **Expense Budget**: Outline anticipated expenses
- **Profitability Analysis**: Include break-even point and expected ROI
- **Funding Requirements**: Specify capital needs and potential sources

### 8. Implementation Roadmap
Break down the plan into phases, each including:
- Key objectives to be achieved
- Main activities and milestones
- Timeline for completion

### 9. Risk Assessment and Mitigation
Identify potential risks and challenges, with strategies for mitigation.

### 10. Appendices
Include supporting information such as market research data, financial statements, or product specifications.

**Quality Standards**:
- Base all projections on realistic assumptions and credible data
- Provide actionable steps with a clear implementation roadmap
- Clearly communicate the unique value proposition and competitive advantages
- Avoid unsupported claims or overly optimistic projections
- Ensure the plan demonstrates business viability and potential

Format the plan with clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and paragraphs for detailed analysis.
```
