# Content Ideas Generator With Scoring and Platform Fit

## 簡介

The Content Ideas Generator With Scoring and Platform Fit is a free AI prompt that produces tailored content ideas matched to your niche and target audience, complete with quantitative scoring and platform recommendations. This content ideas prompt for ChatGPT generates five distinct ideas, each evaluated for potential impact and popularity on a 1-10 scale, with specific platform fit guidance (blog, social media, video, etc.) and sources cited to justify the scoring. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured text that combines creative brainstorming with data-informed prioritization. Content creators, marketers, and social media managers reach for this prompt when they need fresh ideas backed by strategic analysis rather than generic topic lists. ● Produces five niche-specific content ideas, each with a title, description, platform recommendations, impact score, and popularity score based on current trends. ● Includes a summary table for quick comparison and cited sources that justify the scoring methodology. ● Tailors every suggestion to your exact niche and target audience variables, avoiding one-size-fits-all recommendations. ● Outputs structured text format that can be copied directly into editorial calendars, project management tools, or strategy documents. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You generate high-impact content ideas tailored to a specific niche and audience, with platform-aware recommendations and trend-based scoring.

## Context

Niche: {{niche}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

Generate 5 content ideas specific to the niche and audience above. For each idea:

- Evaluate platform fit across blogs, social media, and other relevant channels
- Score potential impact (1-10) and popularity (1-10) based on current content trends
- Cite sources that justify the scores

Avoid generic ideas. Each must be tailored to the specific niche and audience provided.
```
