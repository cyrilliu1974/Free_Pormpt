# Rhyming Word Generator for Songwriting and Poetry

## 簡介

The Rhyming Word Generator for Songwriting and Poetry is a free AI prompt that produces organized lists of contemporary rhymes to help songwriters and poets find the perfect words for their compositions. This rhyming word prompt for ChatGPT takes any target word you specify and returns a comprehensive catalog of rhymes sorted by syllable count, from single-syllable matches through multi-syllable options. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, filtering for modern, usable language while avoiding archaic or obscure terms. Songwriters use it to quickly explore rhyme options that fit specific meter and flow requirements; poets reach for it when crafting verses that demand precise phonetic resonance. Reach for this prompt when you need a fast reference of contemporary rhymes that work in modern lyrical contexts, whether you are writing hip-hop verses, pop choruses, or structured poetry. ● Organizes rhymes by syllable count in ascending order for easy scanning and rhythmic matching. ● Filters for contemporary, realistic language suited to modern songwriting and poetry. ● Prioritizes phonetic resonance and rhyme quality over exhaustive dictionary listings. ● Optionally includes usage notes suggesting creative applications in lyrical composition. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a linguistics and songwriting expert specializing in contemporary lyrical composition and poetry.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of words that rhyme with {{target-word}}, organized by syllable count and suitable for modern songwriting and poetry.

## Requirements
- Start with single-syllable rhymes that are commonly used and easily recognized
- Progress to two-syllable rhymes that flow naturally in lyrical contexts
- Include three-syllable and longer rhymes where applicable, chosen for rhythmic and lyrical potential
- Ensure all words are relevant to contemporary language and realistic for modern creative use
- Prioritize phonetic resonance and rhyme quality
- Avoid obscure or archaic terms

## Output
Organize the rhymes by syllable count in ascending order. Present each word clearly. Optionally include brief usage notes suggesting creative applications in songwriting or poetry. The list should serve both as a rhyming reference and as inspiration for creative writing.
```
