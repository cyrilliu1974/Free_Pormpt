# Interview Preparation Research Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Interview Preparation Research Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive employer research frameworks and interview preparation guides tailored to specific industries. This interview preparation prompt for ChatGPT creates a structured guide that maps key research areas (company culture, financial performance, competitive landscape, recent initiatives, leadership, and industry trends) to reliable information sources and shows candidates exactly how to apply each insight during interviews. The output includes a three-column research framework table, properly formatted source citations, and tailored takeaways that help candidates demonstrate genuine interest and provide informed responses. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single industry input into a complete preparation roadmap. Job seekers preparing for company-specific interviews, career coaches building client resources, and recruiters guiding candidates through interview processes will find this prompt streamlines research and application strategy. ● Maps seven core research domains to the most reliable information sources for each industry ● Shows specific ways to leverage each research finding during actual interview conversations ● Generates properly formatted source citations for all recommended research materials ● Provides 3-5 actionable takeaways that help candidates tailor their interview approach based on findings ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert researcher and interview coach specializing in company-specific interview preparation across industries.

## Task

Create a comprehensive employer research and interview preparation guide for candidates in {{industry}}.

## Context

Thorough company research is essential for targeted interview success. Candidates who understand their potential employer's culture, business model, competitive position, and industry context can demonstrate genuine interest and provide relevant, informed responses.

## Output

Structure your guide as follows:

### Introduction
Brief overview (2-3 sentences) emphasizing why company-specific research matters for interview success.

### Research Framework Table
Create a table with three columns:

**Research Area** | **Information Sources** | **Application in Interview**

- **Research Area**: Key investigation domains (company culture, products/services, financial performance, competitive landscape, recent news/initiatives, leadership, industry trends)
- **Information Sources**: Most valuable and reliable sources for each area (company website, annual reports, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, industry publications, news outlets, financial filings, etc.)
- **Application in Interview**: Specific ways to leverage this knowledge during interviews (demonstrate cultural fit, discuss relevant trends, ask informed questions, reference specific initiatives, connect experience to company needs)

Use concise bullet points within each cell.

### Source Citations
Provide properly formatted citations for all sources mentioned:
[Number]. [Author/Organization]. "[Title]." [Publication/Website], [Date], [URL].

### Conclusion
Summarize key takeaways (3-5 points) emphasizing how to tailor interview approach based on research findings.
```
