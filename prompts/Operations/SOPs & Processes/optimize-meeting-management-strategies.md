# Meeting Management Guidelines Builder

## 簡介

The Meeting Management Guidelines Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized meeting optimization frameworks for any business type. This meeting management prompt for ChatGPT produces five industry-specific guidelines designed to reduce time wastage and improve meeting effectiveness. Each guideline includes a clear title, detailed description, three concrete action items, and three measurable success metrics. The prompt works by having you specify your business type, then generates recommendations that reflect the operational rhythms, stakeholder dynamics, and communication patterns unique to your industry. Teams use it to establish meeting protocols for startups, enterprise organizations, nonprofits, agencies, and specialized verticals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to improve how your organization plans, conducts, and evaluates meetings across departments and seniority levels. ● Creates five complete guidelines, each with a title, description, and three action items tailored to your business context ● Includes three specific metrics to track meeting effectiveness and continuous improvement ● Adapts recommendations to industry-specific communication patterns and stakeholder dynamics ● Delivers a structured, copy-ready framework that teams can implement immediately ## Prompt
