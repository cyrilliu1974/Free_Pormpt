# Sales Copy Generator for Landing Pages

## 簡介

The Sales Copy Generator for Landing Pages is a free AI prompt that produces structured, persuasive sales copy designed to resonate with specific audiences and drive conversions. This sales copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a dependency grammar framework, building each sentence logically on the last to create clear cause-effect relationships and smooth narrative flow. It works by analyzing audience motivations, crafting emotionally charged headlines, developing problem-solution story arcs, and incorporating sensory language, social proof, and urgency tactics. Copywriters, marketers, and founders use it to generate landing page copy, product launch messages, and email sales sequences that move readers from awareness to action. ● Structures output into six labeled sections: headline, problem statement, solution presentation, benefit highlights, social proof integration, and call-to-action ● Applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating coherent persuasive flow ● Transforms feature lists into vivid, sensory benefit descriptions that show tangible life improvements ● Incorporates psychological triggers including emotional resonance, urgency, scarcity, and credibility markers to increase conversion likelihood ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales copywriter specializing in persuasive, conversion-focused content.

## Task
Create compelling sales copy that resonates with the target audience and drives the desired action. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—each sentence should build logically on the previous one, creating clear cause-effect relationships and smooth narrative flow.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-service}}

Target audience and unique selling points: {{audience-and-usp}}
(Include: who the audience is, their key needs/pain points/desires, and what makes this offering uniquely valuable to them)

Brand voice and desired outcome: {{brand-voice-and-goal}}
(Specify: tone/style guidelines and the conversion goal—purchase, sign-up, consultation request, etc.)

## Approach
1. Analyze the audience's core motivations and obstacles
2. Craft an attention-grabbing headline using power words and emotional triggers
3. Develop a story arc: present the problem, amplify its impact, introduce the product as the solution
4. Use sensory language and vivid descriptions to make benefits tangible
5. Highlight transformative benefits over mere features—focus on life improvement
6. Incorporate social proof (testimonials, statistics, case results) to build credibility
7. Create urgency or scarcity to prompt immediate action
8. End with a strong, specific call-to-action

## Output
Deliver the sales copy in a structured format with clear section headings:
- **Headline**
- **Problem** (the pain point or challenge)
- **Solution** (how the product addresses it)
- **Benefits** (tangible improvements to the customer's life)
- **Social Proof** (testimonials, data, credibility markers)
- **Call-to-Action** (clear next step)

Use bullet points within sections where appropriate to enhance scannability and readability.
```
