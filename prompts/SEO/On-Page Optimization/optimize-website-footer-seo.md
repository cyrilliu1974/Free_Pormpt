# Website Footer SEO Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Website Footer SEO Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, actionable guides to improve footer design for better search visibility and user navigation. This website footer optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific URL and delivers customized recommendations across three critical areas: sitemap link structure, social media integration, and business information formatting. Whether you're redesigning an existing footer or auditing a client's site, the prompt structures its output with clear best practices, real-world examples tailored to your domain, and mobile-friendly design principles that balance SEO value with user experience. Web developers, SEO specialists, and digital marketers reach for this prompt when they need to transform a neglected footer into an asset that strengthens internal linking, reinforces brand consistency, and satisfies local search requirements. ● Produces numbered, step-by-step instructions with bolded key terms and practical examples for each footer element ● Covers sitemap link best practices including crawlable navigation, descriptive anchor text, and hierarchy for key pages ● Explains how to position social media icons for engagement without diluting link equity or appearing spammy ● Details business information formatting (company name, address, phone, copyright, privacy policy) for local SEO and trust signals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in website footer optimization for search engine visibility and user experience.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on optimizing the website footer for SEO. Focus on three essential elements: sitemap links, social media icons, and business information. Tailor all recommendations and examples to {{website-url}}.

## Guidelines

**1. Introduction**
- Explain the importance of website footer optimization for SEO and user experience

**2. Sitemap Links**
- **Importance:** Helps search engines and users navigate easily
- **Best Practices:**
  - Include links to key pages and categories
  - Use descriptive anchor text
  - Ensure links are crawlable and user-friendly
- **Example:** Show how {{website-url}} should structure sitemap links to Home, Products, Services, About Us, and Contact

**3. Social Media Icons**
- **Importance:** Boosts social signals and encourages user engagement
- **Best Practices:**
  - Link to active, relevant social media profiles
  - Use recognizable, high-quality icons
  - Position icons prominently for visibility
- **Example:** Demonstrate ideal social media icon placement for {{website-url}} linking to relevant platforms (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn)

**4. Business Information**
- **Importance:** Enhances trust, credibility, and local SEO
- **Best Practices:**
  - Include company name, address, and phone number (NAP)
  - Add copyright notice and privacy policy link
  - Ensure consistency with other online listings
- **Example:** Show proper formatting for {{website-url}} footer: "© 2024 Company Name | Address | Phone | Privacy Policy"

**5. Additional Tips**
- Keep footer design clean, organized, and mobile-friendly
- Avoid excessive links that may appear spammy
- Regularly test and update footer links for functionality

## Output
Use numbered lists and bullet points for clear organization. Bold key terms and phrases. Include specific examples for each footer element applied to the provided website. Separate sections with blank lines for readability.
```
