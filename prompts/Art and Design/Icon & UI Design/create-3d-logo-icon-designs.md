# 3D Logo Icon Design Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The 3D Logo Icon Design Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that transforms flat logos into polished three-dimensional icons with Cinema 4D-inspired styling for designers, marketers, and brand teams. This 3D logo prompt for Midjourney produces centered, brand-faithful iconography with moderate extrusion depth, rounded bevels, and glossy plastic finishes in your original brand colors - no redesign, just dimensional rendering. It applies three-point studio lighting with soft specular highlights and places the result on a pure white seamless background with subtle contact shadows, delivering production-ready assets at 1:1 aspect ratio. Use it to create app icons, presentation graphics, social media badges, or portfolio pieces on Midjourney, Flux, or Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need clean, professional 3D logo renders without modeling software or technical lighting knowledge. ● Preserves your original logo glyphs and brand colors while adding depth through extrusion and beveling. ● Applies Cinema 4D-inspired glossy plastic material and three-point lighting for a professional studio look. ● Outputs on a pure white seamless background with contact shadows, ready for web, print, or presentation use. ● Enforces crisp edges, centered composition, and artifact-free rendering at high quality settings. ## Prompt
