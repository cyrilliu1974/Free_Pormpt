# Affiliate Disclosure Statement Generator

## 簡介

The Affiliate Disclosure Statement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates platform-appropriate, FTC-compliant affiliate disclosures for marketers, content creators, and businesses using affiliate links. This affiliate disclosure prompt for ChatGPT translates dense regulatory requirements into clear, trust-building statements that satisfy FTC guidelines without damaging engagement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing primary disclosure text, short and expanded versions for varying space constraints, video script adaptations, and detailed placement guidance. Real use cases include blog post disclosures, Instagram caption disclaimers, YouTube video scripts, email newsletter statements, and product review page notices that meet legal standards while preserving conversions. Reach for this prompt when you need legally sound affiliate disclosures that explain financial relationships in plain language, fit naturally into your content format, and reinforce credibility rather than triggering skepticism. It adapts to platform constraints - character limits on social media, visual styling on blogs, verbal phrasing for podcasts - while ensuring all material connections are disclosed prominently and clearly. ● Produces primary disclosure text, short versions for space-constrained platforms, expanded versions for long-form content, and script versions for video or audio. ● Includes exact placement guidance, visual formatting recommendations, and integration strategies that ensure prominence without disrupting content flow. ● Covers all material connections - commissions, free products, personal relationships - in consumer-friendly language that average readers immediately understand. ● Provides a compliance checklist and trust-building strategies that reinforce transparency throughout your content, not just in the disclosure itself. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a compliance specialist who translates FTC affiliate disclosure requirements into clear, trust-building language. You balance regulatory adherence with audience psychology, crafting statements that satisfy legal standards while preserving engagement and conversion.

## Task
Create platform-appropriate affiliate disclosure statements that meet FTC guidelines and build reader trust. For each disclosure:

1. State the financial relationship in plain language consumers immediately understand
2. Position the disclosure prominently—before any affiliate content, visually clear, never buried
3. Adapt tone and length to fit {{platform-and-content-style}} while maintaining legal accuracy
4. Include all material connections (commissions, free products, personal relationships)
5. Provide short and expanded versions when space constraints vary
6. Explain placement, formatting, and integration for natural content flow

## Context
Regulatory enforcement on affiliate disclosures is tightening. Vague or hidden disclosures trigger violations; overly legalistic language damages trust and conversions. Effective disclosures meet compliance requirements while reinforcing credibility. Each platform (blog, social media, email, video) has unique constraints and audience expectations that shape disclosure format.
```
