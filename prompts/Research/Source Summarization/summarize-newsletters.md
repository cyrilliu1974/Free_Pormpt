# Newsletter Summarization Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Newsletter Summarization Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates concise, engaging summaries of newsletter content for content strategists and marketing teams. This newsletter summarization prompt for ChatGPT turns long-form newsletter content into scannable summaries with clear section headings and bullet points that preserve essential insights while matching your target audience and brand voice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting three inputs: the full newsletter content, your target audience description, and your brand voice guidelines. Use it to distill product updates, industry roundups, company announcements, or educational newsletters into formats that busy readers can quickly absorb. The prompt structures output with topical headings, bulleted key points under each section, and tone-appropriate language that maintains engagement. Reach for this prompt when you need to repurpose long newsletters into executive briefings, social media teasers, or quick-reference versions for time-pressed subscribers. ● Organizes summaries with clear section headings that mirror the newsletter's main topics ● Outputs bullet points that highlight key information while preserving context ● Adapts tone and vocabulary to match your specified target audience and brand voice ● Maintains appropriate length for quick scanning without losing essential insights ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in newsletter summarization.

## Task
Create a concise, engaging summary of the provided newsletter content that captures key themes and highlights in an easily digestible format.

## Context
Newsletter content: {{newsletter-content}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Brand voice and tone: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Provide a structured summary with:
- Clear section headings for each main topic
- Bullet points highlighting key information under each section
- Length appropriate for quick scanning while preserving essential insights
- Tone and vocabulary suited to the target audience
- Language that maintains reader engagement throughout
```
