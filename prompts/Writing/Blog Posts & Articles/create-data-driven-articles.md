# Data-Driven Article Writer With Citations

## 簡介

The Data-Driven Article Writer With Citations is a free AI prompt that produces research-backed articles grounded in statistical analysis and proper academic sourcing for writers, data scientists, and content professionals. This data-driven article prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to produce structured pieces that integrate quantitative evidence, peer-reviewed research, and dependency grammar frameworks - ensuring every claim is supported by verifiable data and correctly cited sources. The prompt walks through a full article structure: introduction with key statistics, three evidence-backed body sections, a synthesis conclusion, and a complete alphabetical references list. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it practical for anyone producing fact-based content, research summaries, industry reports, or academic-style blog posts. Reach for this prompt when you need credible, citation-heavy writing that balances rigor with readability - whether you're publishing thought leadership, translating research findings, or building authority content in technical domains. ● Enforces dependency grammar sentence construction for logical flow and readability ● Requires proper academic citation format (Author, Year) and a complete references section ● Structures articles into introduction, three data-supported sections, and conclusion ● Prevents unsupported claims by mandating verifiable statistics and peer-reviewed sources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert data scientist and writer specializing in statistical analysis, research methodologies, and dependency grammar frameworks for clear, evidence-based communication.

## Task
Write a comprehensive, data-driven article on {{topic}} that incorporates relevant statistics, trends, and peer-reviewed research. Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow. Cite all sources in proper academic citation format.

## Output Structure

**Title**
Create a compelling, data-focused title that captures the article's central insight.

**Introduction**
- Introduce {{topic}} and explain its significance
- Present key statistics or trends to be explored
- Preview the article's three-section structure

**Section 1**
- Examine the first major aspect of {{topic}}, supported by quantitative data and research findings
- Apply dependency grammar to construct clear, concise sentences with logical subject-verb-object relationships
- Cite sources using proper format: (Author, Year)

**Section 2**
- Analyze a second critical dimension of {{topic}}, backed by statistical evidence and documented trends
- Maintain dependency grammar structure to ensure coherent progression of ideas
- Continue citing all claims with appropriate academic references

**Section 3**
- Explore the final key aspect of {{topic}}, reinforced by empirical data
- Sustain dependency grammar principles throughout for maximum clarity
- Ensure every assertion is supported by properly cited sources

**Conclusion**
- Synthesize main findings and insights
- Emphasize the importance of {{topic}} and the role data plays in understanding it
- End with a thought-provoking statement or actionable recommendation

**References**
- List all cited sources in complete, accurate format
- Organize alphabetically by author surname
- Follow consistent citation style throughout

## Requirements
- Ground every claim in verifiable data or peer-reviewed research
- Avoid unsupported opinions or speculation
- Prioritize statistical rigor and methodological transparency
- Write for clarity and accessibility while maintaining academic standards
```
