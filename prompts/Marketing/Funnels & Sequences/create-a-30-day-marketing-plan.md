# 30-Day Marketing Plan Builder for Lead Generation

## 簡介

The 30-Day Marketing Plan Builder for Lead Generation is a free AI prompt that creates tactical, execution-ready marketing calendars for businesses with existing websites, email lists, and social media presence. This marketing plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured table spanning 30 days, with daily tasks, objectives, platform assignments, measurable KPIs, and implementation notes. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by taking your business context - product, target audience demographics, primary social platform, and competitors - and generating a content calendar that balances audience engagement, promotional campaigns, lead-generation initiatives, and performance tracking. Real-world applications include product launches, seasonal campaigns, event promotions, and quarterly growth sprints for e-commerce stores, SaaS companies, coaching practices, and local service businesses. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete activation plan rather than foundational strategy, and when your digital assets are already in place but require coordinated daily execution. ● Outputs a six-column table (Day, Task, Objective, Platform, KPIs, Notes) covering all 30 days with specific, actionable steps ● Integrates audience engagement tactics, content marketing themes, promotional campaigns, and lead-generation mechanisms into one cohesive timeline ● Tailors daily activities to your chosen social media platform, target demographics, and competitor landscape ● Includes weekly review checkpoints and KPI tracking to enable real-time optimization throughout the campaign ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced digital marketing manager developing a focused 30-day campaign for an established business with existing digital infrastructure (website, landing page, email list).

## Context

The business sells to a defined target audience and needs to maximize lead generation and engagement using one primary social media platform. All foundational assets are already optimized—this plan focuses on activation, not creation.

{{business-context}} should describe: the business type, the product/service being promoted, the target audience (including demographics like age), the chosen social media platform, and key competitors (URLs if available).

## Task

Create a detailed 30-day marketing strategy covering:

**1. Audience Engagement Strategy**
- Refine target personas using existing audience data for personalized marketing
- Plan daily engagement tactics: interactive posts, polls, live sessions tailored to the target audience to build community and brand loyalty

**2. Content Marketing**
- Develop a content calendar aligned with key themes, product launches, or industry trends relevant to the audience
- Mix content types (videos, blogs, infographics) for the social media platform and email newsletters

**3. Promotional Campaigns**
- Outline weekly campaigns highlighting different product/service aspects: special offers, customer testimonials, product demos
- Use targeted advertising on the social media platform, leveraging existing analytics for precision

**4. Lead Generation Initiatives**
- Embed strategic calls-to-action in content and posts to drive email sign-ups using existing offers or content as incentives
- Organize a virtual event or webinar requiring email registration, promoted via the social media platform and email

**5. Measurement and Optimization**
- Set specific, measurable goals per campaign: engagement rates, lead generation numbers, conversion rates
- Schedule weekly analysis sessions to review performance against KPIs and adjust tactics

## Output

Present the 30-day marketing plan as a table with these columns:

| Day | Task | Objective | Platform | KPIs | Notes |
|-----|------|-----------|----------|------|-------|

Tailor each row with specific daily tasks, objectives, platform usage, relevant KPIs, and implementation notes based on the strategy outlined above and the {{business-context}} provided.
```
