# Online Course Curriculum and Marketing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Online Course Curriculum and Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end course plans with module breakdowns, interactive elements, and audience-specific marketing tactics for educators and subject-matter experts. This course creation prompt for ChatGPT takes your topic, target audience, and unique features and outputs a four-module curriculum that progresses from foundational concepts to advanced practice, complete with quizzes, assignments, discussion forums, downloadable resources, and navigation architecture. It also delivers a marketing strategy tailored to your learner demographics, recommending promotional channels - social media, email campaigns, influencer partnerships - and messaging that highlights your course's differentiators. Works seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn expertise into a structured, sellable online course, whether you're launching on a learning platform, building a membership site, or packaging knowledge products for corporate training. ● Structures four progressive modules - basics, intermediate concepts, advanced techniques, and real-world practice - with clear learning objectives and interactive assessments. ● Embeds quizzes, case studies, projects, and expert-moderated forums to boost engagement and knowledge retention. ● Generates audience-specific marketing copy, channel recommendations, and messaging that emphasizes instructor credibility and learner success stories. ● Outputs downloadable resource lists, navigation design guidance, and formatting for immediate implementation on any learning management system. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced online course creator specializing in comprehensive curriculum design and course marketing.

## Task
Develop a complete curriculum and marketing strategy for an online course on {{topic}}. Structure the course to serve learners from beginner to advanced levels with clear progression, interactive elements (quizzes, assignments, discussion forums), intuitive navigation, and downloadable resources.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique course features: {{unique-features}}

## Output
Deliver a structured curriculum plan and marketing strategy covering:

### Course Introduction and Overview
- Captivating introduction explaining the importance and relevance of {{topic}}
- Clear learning objectives and expected outcomes

### Module Breakdown
**Module 1: Basics of {{topic}}**
- Foundational concepts with pop quizzes

**Module 2: Intermediate Concepts**
- Detailed information building on basics
- Case studies and real-world examples

**Module 3: Advanced Techniques**
- Complex aspects with applied projects or simulations

**Module 4: {{topic}} in Practice**
- Practical application with guest lectures, industry insights, and hands-on exercises

### Interactive Elements
- End-of-module quizzes
- Real-world application assignments/projects
- Weekly expert-moderated discussion forums

### Navigation and Resources
- Intuitive course navigation design
- Downloadable PDFs, cheat sheets, and reference materials

### Marketing Strategy
- Audience-tailored messaging based on {{target-audience}} needs
- Promotional channels: social media, email marketing, industry influencer partnerships
- Emphasis on {{unique-features}}, expert instructors, interactivity, and learner success stories

Format as a clear, implementation-ready plan.
```
