# Market Research Study Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Market Research Study Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive market analysis for businesses seeking strategic insights into trends, audiences, and competitors. This market research prompt for ChatGPT asks the AI to act as an expert analyst who synthesizes data from industry reports, consumer surveys, and competitive intelligence into a four-column markdown table: Key Findings, Implications, Recommendations, and Action Items. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming your business context into structured insights covering market size and growth, target audience demographics and pain points, competitive positioning and gaps, plus prioritized opportunities and threats. Real use cases include new market entry planning, competitive strategy development, product launch preparation, and quarterly business reviews. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform scattered market data into decision-ready analysis that aligns findings with business goals. ● Organizes findings into a four-column table that connects market data directly to business decisions ● Covers market trends, audience psychographics, competitive landscape, and growth opportunities in one cohesive analysis ● Delivers prioritized recommendations with specific, time-bound action items rather than generic observations ● Adapts to any business context, geographical focus, or competitive environment you specify ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market research analyst conducting a comprehensive market study.

## Task
Analyze market trends, target audience behavior, and competitive dynamics for the specified business. Gather data from industry reports, consumer surveys, and competitive intelligence sources. Synthesize findings to identify key trends, consumer preferences, and the competitive landscape. Deliver actionable insights structured for strategic decision-making.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Key Findings | Implications | Recommendations | Action Items |
|--------------|--------------|-----------------|-------------|

Each row should provide comprehensive insights directly relevant to the business type, target market, geographical focus, competitors, and stated goals. Cover:
- Market size, growth trajectory, and emerging trends
- Target audience demographics, psychographics, and pain points
- Competitive positioning, strengths, and gaps
- Opportunities and threats specific to the context

Ensure recommendations are prioritized and action items are specific and time-bound.
```
