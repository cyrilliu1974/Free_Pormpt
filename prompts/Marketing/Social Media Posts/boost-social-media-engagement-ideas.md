# Before-After Social Media Post Idea Generator

## 簡介

The Before-After Social Media Post Idea Generator is a free AI prompt that creates 10 high-engagement transformation post concepts for social media marketers and content creators. This before-after post prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your target audience, business niche, and chosen platform to generate visually striking transformation concepts that demonstrate concrete value and trigger curiosity. Each of the 10 ideas includes a clear transformation angle, a brief description of what changed and why it matters, and specific visual elements like shots, angles, or graphic treatments designed to stop the scroll. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering concepts that cover different customer pain points, timeframes, and types of transformation within your niche. Whether you're showcasing client results, product effectiveness, skill development, or process improvements, the output provides ready-to-produce content ideas optimized for engagement. This prompt is built for social media managers, content strategists, and business owners who need a steady stream of proven content formats that drive interaction and demonstrate value. ● Produces 10 distinct before-after concepts covering different angles, pain points, and transformation types ● Includes specific visual direction for each concept to maximize scroll-stopping impact ● Adapts output to your exact target audience, business niche, and social media platform ● Focuses on transformations that balance dramatic visual appeal with achievable, relatable outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in high-engagement content formats.

## Task
Generate 10 compelling before-after post concepts that showcase transformative results. Each concept should be visually striking, relatable, and optimized for stopping the scroll.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Business niche:** {{business-niche}}
**Platform:** {{platform}}

Before-after content performs exceptionally well because it demonstrates concrete value, triggers curiosity, and provides social proof. Focus on transformations that are dramatic enough to be visually obvious yet achievable enough to inspire action.

## Output
For each of the 10 concepts, provide:
1. **Transformation concept** – a clear before-after angle specific to the niche
2. **Brief description** – what changed and why it matters (2-3 sentences)
3. **Visual elements** – specific shots, angles, or graphic treatments to maximize impact

Present as a numbered list. Ensure variety across the 10 ideas—cover different customer pain points, timeframes, and types of transformation within the niche.
```
