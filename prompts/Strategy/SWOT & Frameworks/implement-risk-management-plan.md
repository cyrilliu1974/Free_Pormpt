# Risk Management Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Risk Management Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete risk register with identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring strategies for any business context. This risk management prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a four-column markdown table covering 8–12 priority risks. It walks through internal and external threats, evaluates likelihood and impact, designs actionable prevention controls, and establishes tracking mechanisms with review frequencies and ownership. Use it when launching a new venture, entering a regulated market, or auditing existing risk frameworks for compliance, insurance, or stakeholder reporting. ● Identifies significant internal and external threats across operations, compliance, finance, and reputation ● Assesses each risk by likelihood of occurrence and severity of business impact ● Proposes practical mitigation strategies focused on prevention and consequence reduction ● Defines ongoing monitoring workflows, review cycles, and accountable parties for every risk ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant developing a comprehensive risk management plan.

## Task
Create a detailed strategy that identifies, assesses, mitigates, and monitors risks for the business described below. Structure your analysis as a complete risk register covering:

1. **Risk Identification** – Enumerate all significant internal and external threats relevant to this business context
2. **Risk Assessment** – Evaluate each risk's likelihood (probability of occurrence) and impact (severity of consequences)
3. **Risk Mitigation Strategies** – Design practical controls focused on prevention and impact reduction
4. **Risk Monitoring** – Define ongoing tracking mechanisms, review frequencies, and responsible parties

## Context
{{business-context}}

Tailor the plan to the specific operational realities, regulatory environment, and risk appetite described above.

## Output
Present your risk management plan as a markdown table with four columns:

| Risk Identification | Risk Assessment | Risk Mitigation Strategies | Risk Monitoring |
|---------------------|-----------------|----------------------------|------------------|

Include 8–12 priority risks. For each risk, provide thorough analysis across all four columns. Ensure mitigation strategies are actionable and monitoring approaches are specific.
```
