# Annotated Bibliography Generator for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Annotated Bibliography Generator for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that produces formatted bibliographies with citations, summaries, and critical evaluations for any research topic. This annotated bibliography prompt for ChatGPT identifies 5-7 authoritative sources - prioritizing peer-reviewed scholarship, seminal works, and recent contributions - then structures each entry with a full citation, a 3-4 sentence summary of arguments and methodology, and a 2-3 sentence evaluation of strengths, limitations, and significance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it practical for literature reviews, thesis preparation, grant proposals, and coursework that demands critical engagement with academic sources. Researchers and students reach for it when they need to move beyond a reading list and produce annotated entries that demonstrate comprehension and critical assessment. ● Produces 5-7 entries, each with full citation, summary, and evaluative commentary. ● Prioritizes peer-reviewed and seminal works that represent key methodological approaches. ● Formats output in a clean, academic structure ready for inclusion in papers or proposals. ● Saves hours of manual synthesis by generating both descriptive summaries and critical assessments in one pass. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher skilled at identifying authoritative sources and creating annotated bibliographies that synthesize and critically evaluate academic literature.

## Task
Create a comprehensive annotated bibliography on the following research area:

{{research-topic}}

## Output Requirements
For each source, provide:

**Citation**: Full citation in appropriate academic style

**Summary**: 3-4 sentences covering the main arguments, methodologies, and conclusions

**Evaluation**: 2-3 sentences critically assessing the source's strengths, limitations, and significance to the research area

## Selection Criteria
- Identify 5-7 of the most pertinent and authoritative sources
- Prioritize peer-reviewed scholarship, seminal works, and recent contributions
- Ensure sources represent key perspectives and methodological approaches in the field
- Assess each source's relevance and contribution to understanding the research topic

## Format
Structure as:

**Annotated Bibliography: [Research Topic]**

**Source 1**  
Citation: [full citation]  
Summary: [3-4 sentences]  
Evaluation: [2-3 sentences]

**Source 2**  
[continue same structure]

[Continue for remaining sources]
```
