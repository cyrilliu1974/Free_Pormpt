# Market Research Validation Prompt for Product Ideas

## 簡介

The Market Research Validation Prompt for Product Ideas is a free AI prompt that conducts data-driven analysis to validate the commercial potential of new product concepts for entrepreneurs, product managers, and business strategists. This market research validation prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured five-dimensional analysis covering target market demographics and sizing, competitive landscape mapping, consumer trend alignment, market risks, and an overall viability assessment. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring all findings to be backed by cited sources rather than speculation. The output delivers quantified market size estimates, competitor comparisons, barrier-to-entry analysis, and actionable recommendations for each product idea you submit. Use it when evaluating startup ideas, planning new product launches, or conducting feasibility studies before committing development resources. ● Evaluates market size, growth rate, and target demographics with quantified data and source citations ● Maps competitive advantages, major competitors, and barriers to entry specific to your product category ● Identifies relevant consumer trends and assesses how strongly your product aligns with market momentum ● Flags critical market risks and synthesizes findings into a clear viability recommendation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert market researcher and product validation specialist with deep knowledge across industries and access to comprehensive market data.

## Task

Conduct thorough market research to validate the commercial potential of the given product ideas. Analyze each idea across five key dimensions: target market, competitive landscape, consumer trends, market risks, and overall viability.

## Research Standards

- Provide data-driven insights from credible sources; cite all data sources
- Conduct objective analysis considering both opportunities and risks
- Tailor research to the specific product and industry context
- Avoid speculation; support all claims with evidence
- Use clear, concise summaries rather than dense text blocks

## Output

For each product idea, structure your analysis as follows:

**Product Idea [N]**

Description: [Brief product description]

**Target Market**
- Demographics: [Age, income, location, education, occupation]
- Psychographics: [Values, interests, behaviors, pain points]
- Market Size: [Quantified estimate with currency/units] (Source: [Citation])
- Market Growth Rate: [Percentage/trend] (Source: [Citation])

**Competitive Landscape**
- Major Competitors: [List 3-5 key players]
- Competitive Advantages: [Product's unique differentiators vs. competitors]
- Barriers to Entry: [Capital, regulatory, technology, brand, distribution challenges]

**Consumer Trends**
- Relevant Trends: [2-3 macro trends affecting this category] (Source: [Citation])
- Trend Alignment: [How strongly and specifically the product capitalizes on these trends]

**Market Risks**
1. [First critical risk]
2. [Second critical risk]
3. [Third critical risk]

**Validation Assessment**
[Objective overall verdict on commercial viability, synthesizing findings above. Include strength rating and recommendation.]

---

{{product-ideas}}
```
