# Marketing Headline Generator With Grammar Analysis

## 簡介

The Marketing Headline Generator With Grammar Analysis is a free AI prompt that produces ten attention-grabbing marketing headlines, each accompanied by a dependency grammar breakdown showing how the headline's structure creates clarity and impact. This headline generator prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your campaign details, target audience, and unique selling proposition, then crafting headlines that evoke curiosity, create urgency, or spotlight concrete benefits. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure each headline is grammatically tight and emphasizes the core message through clear syntactic relationships. Each output includes the headline text plus an explanation identifying the head word and how dependent elements - modifiers, complements, adjuncts - attach to it and contribute to meaning. Marketers use it to generate campaign headlines, email subject lines, landing page titles, and ad copy that resonates with their specific audience while understanding the grammatical mechanics behind why certain phrasings work. ● Produces 10 unique headlines per run, each optimized for your campaign, audience, and value proposition ● Explains the dependency grammar structure of every headline so you understand why it works ● Adapts tone and vocabulary to match your brand voice and audience expectations ● Helps you spot patterns in high-performing headline structures for future campaigns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in high-impact marketing headlines.

## Task
Generate 10 attention-grabbing headlines for the marketing campaign described below. For each headline, include a brief explanation of its dependency grammar structure—showing how the head word governs its dependents to create clarity and impact.

## Context
Campaign and brand details:
{{campaign-details}}

Target audience:
{{target-audience}}

Unique selling proposition:
{{usp}}

Employ dependency grammar principles to ensure each headline is grammatically tight and emphasizes the core message through clear syntactic relationships. Craft headlines that evoke curiosity, create urgency, or spotlight concrete benefits. Adapt tone and vocabulary to match the brand voice and audience expectations embedded in the campaign details.

## Output
Provide a numbered list (1–10) in this format:

**Headline:** [the headline text]
**Grammar structure:** [identify the head word and explain how dependent elements (modifiers, complements, adjuncts) attach to it and contribute to meaning]

Repeat for all 10 headlines.
```
