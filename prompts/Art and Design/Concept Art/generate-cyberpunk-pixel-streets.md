# Cyberpunk Pixel Art Street Generator

## 簡介

The Cyberpunk Pixel Art Street Generator is a free AI prompt that creates 8-bit pixel art scenes of neon-lit cyberpunk city streets at night for digital artists, game designers, and pixel art enthusiasts. This cyberpunk pixel art prompt for Midjourney produces images in the iconic eBoy style, combining retro 8-bit aesthetics with futuristic cityscapes filled with glowing neon signs. The prompt uses cinematic HDR rendering, a chaos value of 33 for compositional variety, and a 3:4 aspect ratio ideal for vertical displays or social media. Style reference 759416501 and high stylization ensure consistent visual coherence across generations.
