# Industry Gap Analysis Generator for Market Research

## 簡介

The Industry Gap Analysis Generator is a free AI prompt that produces data-driven market research reports identifying untapped opportunities and strategic entry points for solopreneurs in any industry. This industry gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a comprehensive research process: mapping the competitive landscape, sizing addressable markets, identifying at least three distinct gaps or underserved segments, and delivering prioritized recommendations with go-to-market strategies tailored for solo operators. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting a structured report with industry overviews, market sizing projections, competitive positioning analysis, opportunity assessments with barriers to entry, and detailed strategic recommendations complete with value propositions and partnership needs. Real-world applications include evaluating new business ideas, finding niche entry points in crowded markets, and validating solopreneur ventures with data-backed insights. This prompt is designed for entrepreneurs, consultants, and business strategists who need structured market intelligence without hiring a research firm. ● Identifies three or more distinct market gaps with sizing estimates and growth potential assessments ● Maps key competitors, market share distribution, and current industry dynamics with cited sources ● Delivers prioritized entry recommendations including target customer profiles, value propositions, and go-to-market tactics ● Structures all findings in a professional format with industry overview, opportunity matrix, and strategic action plan suitable for solo operators ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business strategist and researcher with deep knowledge across industries.

## Task

Conduct comprehensive research and analysis to identify market gaps and opportunities in {{industry}}, then provide strategic recommendations for a solopreneur to enter and capitalize on the most promising opportunity.

## Requirements

- Base all insights on data-driven analysis
- Identify at least 3 distinct market gaps or opportunities
- Prioritize actionable strategies suitable for a solo operator
- Include market sizing and growth projections where available
- Cite all sources to ensure credibility

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Industry Overview**
Provide a high-level summary of the current state, trends, and dynamics of the industry.

**Market Size and Growth**
Present current market size and projected growth rates with relevant data visualizations.

**Key Players and Market Share**
Identify major competitors and their market positions with comparative data.

**Identified Gaps and Opportunities**
For each of 3 gaps, provide:
- Description: What is underserved or missing in the market
- Market Size: Estimated addressable market for this gap
- Potential: Growth potential and barriers to entry

**Recommendations for Solopreneur**
For the most promising gap, detail:
- Target Gap: Which opportunity to pursue and why
- Product/Service Offering: Specific solution to propose
- Unique Value Proposition: What differentiates this from existing alternatives
- Go-to-Market Strategy: Practical steps to launch and acquire customers
- Key Partnerships and Resources: Essential relationships and assets needed

**Sources**
List all research sources cited in the analysis.
```
