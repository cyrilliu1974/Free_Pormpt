# Photorealistic Portrait Variation Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Portrait Variation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic portrait photographs with customizable expressions, clothing, and environments while maintaining the exact facial features from a reference image. This photorealistic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces studio-quality close-up portraits using professional camera specifications: an 85mm lens at f/1.4 aperture, ISO 200, and 1/800s shutter speed. You provide a reference face and choose the expression (confident, relaxed, serious), wardrobe and accessories (business suit, casual sweater, statement jewelry), and setting and lighting (golden hour outdoors, studio softbox, office environment). The prompt enforces natural photography aesthetics and explicitly blocks illustration, 3D rendering, or artistic stylization, ensuring the output remains photorealistic. Marketing teams use it to generate multiple headshot variations without reshoots, content creators build character consistency across campaigns, and small businesses produce professional imagery without hiring photographers. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple portrait variations of the same person for A/B testing, seasonal campaigns, or brand refreshes but lack the budget or time for traditional photography sessions. ● Preserves exact facial features and identity from the reference image while transforming everything else around the subject. ● Enforces professional camera settings (85mm f/1.4, ISO 200, 1/800s) that mimic high-end portrait photography gear. ● Blocks artistic styles, illustrations, and 3D renders to guarantee natural, photorealistic output every time. ● Accepts three flexible variables for expression, wardrobe and accessories, and setting and lighting to cover dozens of scenarios. ## Prompt

```
Photo-realistic close-up portrait, 85mm lens at f/1.4, ISO 200, 1/800s shutter speed. Subject: man with exact face and facial features from reference image, {{expression}}, wearing {{wardrobe-and-accessories}}. {{setting-and-lighting}}. Natural photography only—no illustration, no 3D rendering, no artistic stylization.
```
