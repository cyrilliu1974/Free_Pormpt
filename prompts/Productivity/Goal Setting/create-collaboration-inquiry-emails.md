# 7-Day Meal Plan Generator With Recipes and Grocery List

## 簡介

The 7-Day Meal Plan Generator With Recipes and Grocery List is a free AI prompt that creates structured weekly meal plans with full recipes, nutritional breakdowns, and shopping lists for any dietary framework. This meal plan prompt for ChatGPT produces five meals per day across seven days - breakfast, lunch, dinner, and two snacks - each with ingredient lists, step-by-step cooking instructions, and per-serving nutritional data. It opens with an overview of the target diet's principles and restrictions, then delivers 35 unique recipes that respect those guidelines while incorporating variety through different cuisines and cooking methods. The prompt concludes with a categorized weekly grocery list and practical meal prep tips for batch cooking and storage. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting any diet type (keto, Mediterranean, vegan, paleo, gluten-free) and optional context about preferences or restrictions. Use this prompt when you need a complete, ready-to-execute meal plan that removes guesswork from dietary adherence and weekly shopping. ● Produces 35 distinct meals and snacks across a full week, each with precise ingredient measurements and cooking steps ● Includes per-serving nutritional information for every recipe to support macro tracking and calorie management ● Generates a categorized grocery list organized by produce, proteins, dairy, grains, and pantry staples ● Provides meal prep and storage guidance to streamline advance preparation and reduce daily cooking time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert meal planner with deep knowledge of dietary frameworks, recipe development, and nutritional balance.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 7-day meal plan that strictly adheres to {{diet-type}} principles. Each day must include breakfast, lunch, dinner, and two snacks—all balanced, nutritious, and aligned with the dietary guidelines. Provide detailed recipes with ingredients, measurements, step-by-step instructions, and nutritional information per serving. Include a categorized grocery list for the entire week and practical meal prep tips.

## Context
{{diet-context}}

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Diet Principles Overview**
Summarize the core guidelines, permitted foods, and restrictions for {{diet-type}}.

**7-Day Meal Plan**
For each day (Day 1 through Day 7), present:
- **Breakfast:** [Recipe Name] – [Brief Description]
- **Snack 1:** [Recipe Name] – [Brief Description]
- **Lunch:** [Recipe Name] – [Brief Description]
- **Snack 2:** [Recipe Name] – [Brief Description]
- **Dinner:** [Recipe Name] – [Brief Description]

**Detailed Recipes**
For each meal and snack across all seven days, provide:
- Ingredient list with precise measurements
- Step-by-step preparation and cooking instructions
- Nutritional information per serving (calories, macronutrients)

Ensure variety by incorporating different cuisines and cooking methods (grilling, baking, raw, slow-cooking, etc.).

**Weekly Grocery List**
Organize by category: produce, proteins, dairy/alternatives, grains, pantry staples, condiments.

**Meal Prep & Storage Tips**
Provide practical guidance for advance preparation, portioning, and refrigeration/freezing to streamline daily execution.
```
