The Luxury Perfume Bottle Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates hyper-realistic macro product photos of perfume bottles styled with crystal shards, orchid petals, and ethereal golden lighting for beauty brands and creative studios. This perfume photography prompt for Midjourney creates 8K close-ups featuring glass refraction, elegant lens flares, and smooth creamy bokeh that deliver a cinematic, ultra-luxury campaign aesthetic. You control the product name, scene styling details, and aspect ratio to match your brand's visual identity. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image synthesis models, making it ideal for e-commerce listings, social media campaigns, editorial lookbooks, and high-end brand storytelling.
