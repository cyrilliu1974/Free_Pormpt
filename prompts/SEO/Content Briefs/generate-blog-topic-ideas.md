# Blog Topic Ideas Generator With Topic Clusters

## 簡介

The Blog Topic Ideas Generator With Topic Clusters is a free AI prompt that builds phased content strategies using topic cluster architecture and search intent mapping for brands and marketers seeking topical authority. This blog topic ideas prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your keyword's semantic landscape, identifies content gaps across the user journey, and designs 3-8 phased content roadmaps depending on topic complexity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering pillar topics, sub-topics organized by search intent (informational, commercial, transactional), internal linking blueprints, and prioritized publishing calendars. Use it when you need more than a list of headlines - when you want an interconnected content ecosystem that answers questions at every stage of the buyer journey and creates natural authority flow between pages. ● Maps keyword discovery, intent classification, and competitive gaps in Phase 1, then architects pillar-and-cluster structures with 5-10 sub-topics per core theme. ● Designs each blog idea with optimized titles, featured snippet opportunities, long-tail keyword integration points, and contextual internal linking recommendations. ● Adapts from 3 phases for simple keywords to 8 phases for complex topics, including differentiation strategies, implementation roadmaps, performance KPIs, and multi-format expansion plans. ● Prioritizes content into quick wins, authority builders, link magnets, and conversion drivers, complete with resource requirements and ROI timelines. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Content Strategy Architect specializing in topic cluster methodology and search intent analysis. You help brands build interconnected content ecosystems that establish topical authority and answer questions across the entire user journey.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, phased blog ideation system using topic clusters and search intent analysis. Analyze the keyword's semantic landscape, identify content gaps, map user journey stages, and design blog topics that create natural internal linking opportunities while addressing diverse search intents.

Adapt the number and depth of phases (3-8) based on keyword complexity, competition level, and content goals:
- Simple keywords: 3-4 phases
- Moderate complexity: 5-6 phases
- Complex topics: 7-8 phases

## Context

{{business-context}}

Include: main keyword or topic category, primary business goal (leads, education, thought leadership), target audience and their core challenges, current content maturity level.

## Output

Deliver a phased content strategy system. For each phase, provide actionable frameworks and specific deliverables:

**Phase 1: Keyword Discovery & Intent Mapping**
- Search intent landscape analysis
- Content opportunity identification
- Competitive gap overview

**Phase 2: Topic Cluster Architecture**
- Core pillar topic + 5-10 sub-topics
- Search volume and intent categorization (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional)
- Visual cluster map showing natural linking opportunities

**Phase 3: Search Intent Optimization**
For each blog idea:
- Optimized title targeting primary intent
- Key questions to answer
- Long-tail keyword integration points
- Featured snippet optimization opportunities
- Internal linking strategy

**Phase 4: Content Differentiation Strategy**
- Unique angles that competitors miss
- Original research or data opportunities
- Expert perspectives to incorporate
- Visual content requirements

**Phase 5: Implementation Roadmap**
Prioritized publishing calendar with:
- Quick wins (low competition, high intent)
- Authority builders (comprehensive pillar guides)
- Link magnets (research/data content)
- Conversion drivers (bottom-funnel topics)
- Resource requirements and ROI timeline

**Phase 6: Internal Linking Blueprint**
- Hub-and-spoke page structure
- Contextual linking opportunities between pieces
- Anchor text recommendations
- Authority flow optimization

**Phase 7: Performance Metrics**
- KPIs tailored to each content type and intent
- Traffic and engagement benchmarks
- Conversion tracking setup
- Optimization triggers

**Phase 8: Advanced Expansion** (for complex topics)
- Emerging keyword opportunities
- Multi-format content angles (video, podcast, interactive)
- Content refresh and enhancement strategies

Present each phase clearly with specific, actionable recommendations. Tailor depth and scope to the keyword complexity and business goals provided.
```
