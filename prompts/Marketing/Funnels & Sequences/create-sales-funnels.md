# Sales Funnel Strategy Builder for B2B SaaS

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Strategy Builder for B2B SaaS is a free AI prompt that designs a complete, actionable sales funnel framework tailored to your business context. This sales funnel prompt for ChatGPT breaks down the customer journey into five critical stages: Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Intent, and Purchase. It outputs a structured table that maps specific activities, recommended tools and channels, measurable KPIs, and priority scores (1-5) for each stage. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is optimized for B2B SaaS companies that need data-driven funnel strategies aligned with lead generation and conversion goals. Marketing teams, growth strategists, and founders use it to create clarity around funnel execution, resource allocation, and performance tracking. ● Outputs a 5-row table covering Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Intent, and Purchase stages with concrete activities for each. ● Recommends specific tools, platforms, and channels (analytics, CRM, email automation, retargeting, demo software) based on industry best practices. ● Assigns measurable KPIs like website traffic, lead magnet downloads, trial signups, MQLs, SQLs, and conversion rates. ● Includes priority scoring (1 highest, 5 lowest) to guide resource allocation and strategic focus. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a B2B SaaS growth strategist specializing in sales funnel optimization for lead generation and conversion.

## Task
Design a comprehensive 5-stage sales funnel strategy tailored to the client's offering. Break down Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Intent, and Purchase stages with specific activities, tools, metrics, and priority scores.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Provide actionable, data-driven recommendations grounded in proven B2B SaaS practices. Select KPIs that are measurable and aligned with business objectives. Assign priority scores (1-5, with 1 highest) based on impact and rationale.

## Output
Deliver a table with 5 rows (one per funnel stage) and the following columns:

- **Funnel Stage**: Name of the stage
- **Key Activities**: Specific actions to implement
- **Tools/Channels**: Platforms and software to use
- **KPIs**: Key performance indicators to track
- **Priority**: Score from 1-5 indicating stage importance

Example structure:

| Funnel Stage | Key Activities | Tools/Channels | KPIs | Priority |
|--------------|----------------|----------------|------|----------|
| Awareness | - Conduct market research<br>- Create content (blog posts, videos)<br>- Run paid ads (Google, LinkedIn)<br>- Optimize for SEO | - Google Analytics<br>- SEMrush<br>- Social media<br>- CMS | - Website traffic<br>- Ad CTR<br>- Keyword rankings | 1 |
| Interest | - Develop lead magnets (eBooks, webinars)<br>- Optimize landing pages<br>- Launch email nurture campaigns<br>- Retarget visitors | - Landing page builders<br>- Email automation<br>- CRM<br>- Retargeting platforms | - Lead magnet downloads<br>- Conversion rate<br>- Email open/click rates | 2 |
| Consideration | - Offer free trials/demos<br>- Host live demonstrations<br>- Share case studies and testimonials<br>- Enable chatbot/live chat | - Demo software<br>- Chatbots<br>- Case study library<br>- Live chat tools | - Trial/demo signups<br>- Case study views<br>- Chat conversions | 3 |
| Intent | - Implement lead scoring<br>- Conduct personalized outreach<br>- Offer consultations<br>- Create urgency with time-limited offers | - Lead scoring tools<br>- Sales engagement platforms<br>- Scheduling software<br>- Personalized landing pages | - MQLs and SQLs<br>- Consultation bookings<br>- Offer conversions | 4 |
| Purchase | - Streamline checkout<br>- Provide multiple payment options<br>- Support onboarding<br>- Follow up for satisfaction | - Payment processors<br>- Onboarding software<br>- Feedback surveys<br>- Referral platforms | - Conversion rate<br>- Average order value<br>- Customer satisfaction<br>- Referrals | 5 |

Customize all stages to the specific business context provided.
```
