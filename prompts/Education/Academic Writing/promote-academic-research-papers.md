# Academic Research Paper Promotion Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Academic Research Paper Promotion Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive dissemination strategies for researchers seeking to maximize the reach and citation impact of their scholarly work. This academic research paper promotion prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your paper's subject matter, methodologies, and key findings to recommend high-impact journals, relevant conferences, digital platform optimization tactics, and content adaptation strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver an eight-part structured plan covering audience segmentation, scholarly database discoverability, professional social media engagement, industry media outreach, and performance metrics. Researchers use it when preparing to publish findings, launching preprints, or seeking broader interdisciplinary impact beyond traditional journal placement. ● Identifies 3-5 high-impact journals with impact factor tiers, audience demographics, and submission approaches tailored to your paper's methodology and findings ● Maps conference and symposia opportunities prioritized by thematic relevance, historical attendance, andPrestige to connect with key figures in your field ● Provides keyword optimization and profile completion tactics for Google Scholar, ResearchGate, Academia.edu, and discipline-specific repositories ● Recommends content adaptation formats including visual abstracts, blog summaries, video explainers, and podcast appearances with guidance on which findings translate best to each medium ● Defines success metrics such as citation counts, Altmetric scores, downloads, and social shares with a 3-month review cycle to optimize channel allocation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an academic research and marketing expert specializing in maximizing the visibility and impact of scholarly work.

## Task

Create a comprehensive promotion plan for the research paper "{{paper-title}}" that identifies optimal channels and actionable strategies to reach academics, scholars, and professionals in the relevant field.

## Context

{{research-context}}

Include: the paper's subject matter, methodologies, key findings, core audience, relevant subfields, and any preferred channels or constraints.

## Output

Deliver a structured promotion plan covering:

**1. Audience Analysis**
Identify the core audience segments and relevant subfields most interested in this research based on subject matter, methodologies, and findings.

**2. Academic Publication Venues**
Recommend 3-5 high-impact journals appropriate for the paper. For each, provide: impact factor tier, audience demographics, topical relevance, and submission approach.

**3. Conference & Event Strategy**
Suggest conferences, seminars, and symposia where presenting would maximize reach. Prioritize by: historical attendance of key figures, thematic relevance, prestige, and typical audience size.

**4. Scholarly Database Optimization**
Describe how to maximize discoverability on Google Scholar, ResearchGate, Academia.edu, and field-specific repositories through: keyword selection, abstract optimization, profile completion, and researcher engagement tactics.

**5. Professional Social Media**
Outline a strategy for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and relevant platforms including: posting cadence, hashtag strategy, engagement tactics, influencer outreach, and discussion participation.

**6. Industry Media Outreach**
Identify industry-specific websites, blogs, podcasts, and newsletters. Propose collaboration approaches: guest posts highlighting findings, expert commentary, or interviews.

**7. Content Adaptation**
Recommend accessible formats to broaden reach: blog summaries, visual abstracts, infographics, short video explainers, or podcast appearances. Specify which findings translate best to each format.

**8. Metrics & Iteration**
Define success metrics (citations, downloads, Altmetric score, social shares, media mentions) and a 3-month review cycle to reallocate effort toward highest-performing channels.

For each recommendation, include estimated audience reach, typical engagement rates for that channel, and why it suits this specific research.
```
