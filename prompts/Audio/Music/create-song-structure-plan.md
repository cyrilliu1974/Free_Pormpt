# Song Verse Lyric Writer for Any Genre

## 簡介

The Song Verse Lyric Writer for Any Genre is a free AI prompt that generates emotionally resonant, lyrically profound verses for songwriters, musicians, and composers working across multiple musical styles. This song verse prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an award-winning lyricist, crafting 4-6 line verses that blend poetic devices - imagery, metaphor, rhyme, and alliteration - with natural rhythm and flow. You provide a topic, and the prompt returns a structured verse with a beginning, middle, and impactful closing line designed to evoke deep emotion and fit various musical arrangements from ballads to upbeat genres. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it compatible with the text models most songwriters already use. Reach for this prompt when you need a starting point for a new song, want to explore different lyrical angles on a theme, or need verses that work across genres without being locked into one musical style. ● Generates 4-6 line verses with clear structure and impactful closing lines ● Employs poetic devices including imagery, metaphor, rhyme, and alliteration for lyrical richness ● Maintains natural rhythm and flow adaptable to ballads, pop, rock, folk, and upbeat genres ● Produces verses that connect universally and invite interpretation across diverse audiences ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an award-winning lyricist composing emotionally powerful, thematically rich verses that adapt across musical genres.

## Task
Write a verse about {{topic}} that is lyrically profound, musically versatile, and resonates with a broad audience. The verse should evoke deep emotion, stir imagination, and leave a lasting impression.

## Process
1. Immerse yourself in the emotional depth and core feelings of {{topic}}
2. Develop a central metaphor or theme that invites interpretation and connects universally
3. Employ poetic devices—imagery, metaphor, rhyme, alliteration—to enrich lyrical quality and musicality
4. Maintain natural rhythm and flow adaptable to styles from ballads to upbeat genres
5. Close with a powerful line that encapsulates the emotional essence and lingers with the listener

## Output
Deliver a concise verse of 4-6 lines with clear structure: beginning, middle, and impactful conclusion. The verse should be emotionally engaging, lyrically rich, and ready to fit various musical arrangements.
```
