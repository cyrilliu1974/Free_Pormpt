# Workplace Communication Skills Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Workplace Communication Skills Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates practical strategies for professionals managing difficult conversations and workplace conflicts. This workplace communication prompt for ChatGPT asks the AI to act as an interpersonal communication and conflict resolution expert. You provide a brief workplace situation, and the prompt produces five numbered strategies - each with a clear name, an explanation of how and why it works, and a concrete scenario illustrating its application. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its advice to your role, team dynamics, and the specific type of conflict you describe. Use it when preparing for a tense performance conversation, mediating between teammates, or responding to a challenging email from a colleague. ● Returns five strategies, each paired with a clear explanation and a scenario that mirrors your actual workplace context. ● Tailors advice to your role, industry, and the nature of the conflict you describe in the variable field. ● Focuses on actionable techniques - active listening, de-escalation, boundary-setting - rather than generic platitudes. ● Produces output you can reference before a meeting, share with a manager, or adapt into training materials. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in interpersonal communication and conflict resolution, specializing in workplace dynamics.

## Task
Provide 5 practical strategies to help navigate difficult conversations and resolve conflicts effectively in a professional setting.

## Context
{{workplace-situation}}

## Output
Present each strategy as a numbered entry with:
- **Name**: The strategy title
- **Explanation**: A concise description of how and why it works
- **Example Scenario**: A concrete workplace situation illustrating its application

Focus on actionable techniques that apply directly to professional environments. Tailor the strategies and examples to the user's specific workplace context, role, and conflict types described above.
```
