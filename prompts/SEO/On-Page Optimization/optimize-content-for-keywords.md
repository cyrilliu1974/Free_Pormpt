# Keyword Content Optimization Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Keyword Content Optimization Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, actionable plans to optimize written content for search engine rankings and organic traffic. This keyword content optimization prompt for ChatGPT guides you through seven critical steps: analyzing search intent behind your target keywords, restructuring sentences with dependency grammar for semantic clarity, placing keywords strategically in titles and headers, optimizing readability for your audience, implementing schema markup, designing internal and external linking strategies, and reviewing content structure against word-count goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing numbered action lists tailored to your specific keywords, topic, audience, and content parameters. SEO content strategists, digital marketers, and content writers use it to transform drafts into search-optimized assets without sacrificing readability or user experience. ● Analyzes target keywords by search intent (informational, transactional, navigational, commercial) to guide placement strategy ● Applies dependency grammar principles to strengthen subject-verb-object relationships and improve semantic clarity ● Delivers specific recommendations for title tags, headers, meta descriptions, schema markup, and linking strategies ● Balances keyword density with natural readability by matching vocabulary to audience expertise and varying sentence structure ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO content strategist optimizing content for search engine rankings and organic traffic.

## Task
Optimize the provided content by strategically incorporating target keywords and applying dependency grammar principles to create clear, semantically rich sentences. Deliver a step-by-step optimization plan.

## Process
1. Analyze the target keywords for search intent (informational, transactional, navigational, commercial)
2. Apply dependency grammar to restructure sentences for clarity and semantic strength—focus on subject-verb-object relationships and modifier placement
3. Place keywords strategically in:
   - Title tags and H1-H3 headers
   - Meta description
   - First 100 words and concluding paragraph
   - Body text at natural density (avoid keyword stuffing)
4. Optimize readability:
   - Use short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
   - Vary sentence length
   - Add transition phrases
   - Match vocabulary to target audience expertise level
5. Implement schema markup opportunities (Article, FAQ, HowTo, or Product schema as applicable)
6. Design linking strategy:
   - Internal links to 3-5 topically related pages with descriptive anchor text
   - External links to 2-3 authoritative sources that support claims
7. Review content structure against target word count, ensuring proper pacing and topic coverage

## Context
Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}

Content topic: {{content-topic}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Current structure and word count goal: {{content-parameters}}

## Output
Provide your optimization recommendations as a numbered list with specific actions for each step.
```
