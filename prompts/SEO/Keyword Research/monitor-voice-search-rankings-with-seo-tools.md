# Voice Search Ranking Monitor and Optimization Planner

## 簡介

The Voice Search Ranking Monitor and Optimization Planner is a free AI prompt that analyzes keyword performance for voice-driven queries and creates data-backed optimization strategies for SEO specialists and digital marketers. This voice search optimization prompt for ChatGPT helps you assess current rankings for conversational and question-based queries, identify featured snippet opportunities, and develop specific strategies tailored to how people actually speak to smart speakers and mobile assistants. You provide a target keyword, your preferred SEO tool, business context, and website URL, and the prompt generates an executive summary plus a structured table mapping current rankings to target positions with corresponding optimization tactics. It accounts for voice search nuances like local intent, position zero targets, and mobile-first factors that traditional SEO analysis often misses. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for SEO professionals tracking voice search performance, content strategists optimizing for conversational queries, and agencies managing local search visibility across smart speaker platforms. ● Analyzes ranking gaps between traditional and voice search results for your target keyword ● Generates 5-8 prioritized optimization actions mapped to specific rank improvement goals ● Identifies featured snippet and position zero opportunities tailored to voice query patterns ● Addresses conversational search behavior, question-based formats, and local intent signals ● Produces an executive summary plus structured table format ready to share with clients or stakeholders ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist focused on voice search optimization.

## Task
Analyze keyword performance for voice search queries and create a comprehensive optimization plan. Identify ranking opportunities and provide actionable strategies to improve voice search visibility.

## Context
Keyword: {{keyword}}
SEO tool: {{seo-tool}}
Industry and target audience: {{business-context}}
Website: {{website-url}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Assess current voice search rankings for the keyword
- Identify ranking gaps and optimization opportunities
- Develop specific strategies tailored to voice search behavior (conversational queries, question-based patterns, local intent)
- Account for featured snippet opportunities and position zero targets
- Consider mobile and smart speaker optimization factors

## Output
Provide a brief executive summary of key findings and recommendations (2-3 paragraphs), followed by a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Rank | Target Rank | Optimization Strategy |
|--------------|-------------|----------------------|

Include 5-8 rows covering the most impactful optimization actions.
```
