# Lead Generation Metrics Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Metrics Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates marketing department KPIs, identifies performance gaps, and delivers actionable recommendations to improve lead generation outcomes. This lead generation metrics prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing both leading indicators (traffic volume, engagement rates) and lagging indicators (conversion rates, cost per lead, lead quality scores) within your specific business context. It produces a markdown performance table comparing current metrics against targets, followed by prioritized insights that explain gaps and recommend concrete steps to close them. Marketing teams use it to audit campaign effectiveness, justify budget allocation, and plan quarterly improvements across paid, organic, and direct channels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform raw lead generation data into executive-ready analysis that connects performance to business goals and budget realities. ● Evaluates industry-appropriate KPIs including traffic sources, engagement metrics, conversion rates, cost per lead, and lead quality indicators ● Produces a three-column markdown table mapping each metric to current performance and improvement targets ● Identifies interdependencies between metrics so you understand how improving one KPI affects others ● Prioritizes recommendations by impact and feasibility given stated budget constraints, separating quick wins from long-term investments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing analyst specializing in lead generation performance evaluation.

## Task
Analyze lead generation metrics for the marketing department and deliver a structured assessment that identifies relevant KPIs, evaluates current performance, sets improvement targets, and provides actionable insights to close performance gaps.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Focus your analysis on the metrics most critical to lead generation success within these parameters. Consider both leading indicators (traffic, engagement) and lagging indicators (conversion rate, cost per lead, lead quality) appropriate to the industry and channels described.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

1. **Performance Table** (markdown format with three columns):
   - METRIC
   - CURRENT PERFORMANCE
   - TARGET

2. **Insights & Recommendations** (bullet points below the table):
   - For each metric, explain the gap between current and target performance
   - Provide specific, actionable recommendations to achieve targets
   - Prioritize quick wins and high-impact opportunities given the budget constraints
   - Note any interdependencies between metrics
```
