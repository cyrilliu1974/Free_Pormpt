# Lifestyle Photography Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Lifestyle Photography Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates intimate, candid lifestyle images of friends sharing moments in customizable indoor venues with professional editorial styling. This lifestyle photography prompt for Midjourney produces street-style candid shots with warm natural morning light, shallow depth of field, and 35mm lens aesthetics. You control the venue type (café, restaurant, lounge) and subject pair characteristics while the prompt handles cinematic framing, golden window highlights, relaxed body language, and environmental storytelling through blurred ambient details like barista counters, Edison bulbs, and rustic interiors. Output images feature soft bokeh, warm color grading, natural skin texture, and ultra-realistic detail suitable for editorial publications, brand campaigns, and portfolio work.
