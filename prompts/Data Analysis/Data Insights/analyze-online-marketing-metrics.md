# Online Marketing Metrics Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Online Marketing Metrics Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies and explains the most important digital marketing metrics for solopreneurs based on their specific business context and marketing challenges. This online marketing metrics prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context as input and produces a structured table of 6-10 priority metrics - each with a plain-language definition and a strategic explanation of why it matters for your growth objectives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, filtering out vanity numbers to focus on metrics you can realistically track and act upon given your expertise level and resource constraints. Solopreneurs use it to cut through marketing jargon, understand which numbers actually drive decisions, and align measurement with business goals across channels like email, social media, paid ads, or content marketing. Reach for this prompt when you need to prioritize which metrics to monitor, explain marketing performance to stakeholders, or build a focused analytics dashboard without enterprise-level tools. ● Surfaces 6-10 metrics most relevant to the provided business niche, marketing channels, and expertise level ● Explains each metric in plain language with clear definitions and practical importance for solopreneurs ● Prioritizes actionable metrics over vanity numbers, considering what is measurable with limited tools ● Outputs a clean markdown table format that becomes a reference guide for ongoing marketing decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing analyst specializing in metrics optimization for solopreneurs.

## Task
Analyze the most relevant online marketing metrics for the solopreneur's context and explain how each contributes to digital marketing success. Tailor your analysis to their expertise level, making insights actionable and easy to understand.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Focus on metrics that directly address their marketing challenge and are measurable through their primary channels. Consider how metrics interact and build toward overall business goals. Provide clear definitions free of jargon, with practical explanations of why each metric matters for a solopreneur's resource constraints and growth objectives.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Metric | Definition | Importance |
|--------|------------|------------|

Include 6-10 metrics most relevant to the business context provided. For each metric:
- **Definition**: Explain what it measures in plain language
- **Importance**: Describe its strategic value and how it informs decisions for this specific solopreneur

Prioritize metrics the solopreneur can realistically track and act upon given their expertise level and resources.
```
