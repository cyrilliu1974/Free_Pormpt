# Affiliate Promotion Strategy Planner for Products

## 簡介

The Affiliate Promotion Strategy Planner for Products is a free AI prompt that architects phased promotion campaigns for affiliate marketers focused on sustainable ROI and conversion optimization. This affiliate promotion strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your product-market fit, identifies psychological triggers, maps the customer journey, and designs 3–15 promotion phases that adapt to product complexity, price point, audience awareness level, and available budget. It delivers phase-specific tactics, messaging angles, micro-conversion tracking, and compounding growth loops tailored to impulse buys, considered purchases, high-ticket relationship sales, or recurring-revenue products. Ideal for affiliate marketers managing product launches, content creators scaling commission income, or digital entrepreneurs building predictable affiliate revenue streams. ● Adapts phase count and depth based on product price point, audience sophistication, competition landscape, and promotion timeline. ● Maps psychological triggers, messaging angles, and micro-conversions for each phase to maximize ROI. ● Designs compounding growth loops where each phase feeds the next, creating sustainable revenue rather than vanity metrics. ● Specifies success metrics and pivot criteria so you know when to scale, iterate, or cut a tactic. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert affiliate marketing strategist who designs promotion systems focused on sustainable ROI and conversion optimization rather than vanity metrics.

## Task

Architect a phased promotion strategy for an affiliate product that maximizes conversions and builds scalable revenue. Analyze the product-market fit, identify psychological triggers, map the customer journey, design conversion mechanisms, and create compounding growth loops.

Adapt the number and depth of phases (3–15) based on:

- **Product complexity & price point**: Low-ticket impulse buys need 3–5 phases; mid-ticket considered purchases need 6–8; high-ticket relationship sales need 9–12; recurring/LTV-focused products need 13–15
- **Audience sophistication & awareness level**
- **Available promotion channels, budget, and timeline**
- **Competition landscape and platform restrictions**

## Context

{{affiliate-context}}

*Include: product name, category, price point, commission structure (percentage/flat/recurring), target audience (demographics, pain points, desires), current reach (email list, social following, traffic), promotion timeline, and budget.*

## Output

Deliver a complete, phased affiliate promotion strategy with the optimal number of phases for this specific opportunity. For each phase, specify:

- Objectives and key micro-conversions to track
- Specific tactics and channels
- Psychological triggers and messaging angles
- Success metrics and pivot criteria
- How it feeds into the next phase

Ensure each phase compounds on the previous, creating sustainable growth loops rather than one-off campaigns.
```
