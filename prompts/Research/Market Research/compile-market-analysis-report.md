# Market Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Market Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that compiles structured market analysis reports for businesses, strategists, and research teams. It analyzes current market conditions, consumer behavior patterns, and competitive dynamics to deliver actionable intelligence in an organized format. This market analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert market analyst, examining your industry context and competitive landscape to produce a markdown table with three focused columns: Market Trends, Target Audience Insights, and Potential Opportunities. Each column contains at least five detailed, actionable points drawn from analysis of market gaps, demographic and psychographic segments, behavioral patterns, and areas ripe for innovation. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific business context and competitive differentiation factors. Use it when preparing strategic plans, investor presentations, product launches, or quarterly business reviews that require current market intelligence. ● Analyzes market conditions, consumer behavior, and emerging patterns specific to your industry and competitive context ● Segments target audiences across demographic, psychographic, and behavioral dimensions for precise positioning ● Identifies market gaps, unmet customer needs, and innovation opportunities backed by trend analysis ● Outputs a clean three-column markdown table format with at least five actionable points per category for immediate use in presentations and planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst compiling a comprehensive market analysis report.

## Task
Identify key market trends, target audience insights, and potential opportunities in a structured, actionable format. Analyze current market conditions, consumer behavior, and emerging patterns. Segment the target audience by demographics, psychographics, and behavioral patterns. Identify opportunities by examining market gaps, unmet needs, and areas for innovation or expansion.

## Context
- Industry and business: {{business-context}}
- Key competitors and differentiation: {{competitive-landscape}}

## Output
Present your analysis in a markdown table with three columns: **Market Trends**, **Target Audience Insights**, and **Potential Opportunities**. Include at least 5 detailed, actionable points in each column.
```
