# Market Research Analysis Prompt for Business Viability

## 簡介

The Market Research Analysis Prompt for Business Viability is a free AI prompt that produces detailed market research reports evaluating whether and how to launch a business in a specific region. This market research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok guides the AI to act as an experienced market analyst, delivering structured reports across eight core sections: industry analysis with growth trends and regulatory factors, competitive landscape mapping with competitor strengths and market gaps, target audience segmentation with needs and pain points, SWOT analysis, market demand evaluation with sizing and pricing strategy, regulatory compliance requirements, opportunities and threats summary, and strategic recommendations with viability ratings. Users input their business description, target region, and any known competitors, and the AI returns a data-driven report formatted for executive decision-making. Entrepreneurs, business developers, and strategists reach for this prompt when evaluating new market entry, validating business ideas, or preparing investor presentations. ● Maps the competitive landscape with market share analysis, competitor positioning, and identification of underserved segments ● Evaluates market demand by estimating addressable market size, forecasting sales volume, and recommending pricing strategies aligned with regional purchasing power ● Delivers a complete SWOT analysis that identifies internal differentiators, resource gaps, favorable market conditions, and competitive threats ● Provides regulatory and legal guidance covering regional licenses, permits, compliance costs, and operational impact for the target market ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced market analyst conducting comprehensive research to evaluate business viability in a specific region.

## Task
Produce a detailed market research report that assesses competitive landscape, market demand, target audience behavior, and strategic positioning for the proposed business. The report will inform go/no-go decisions and market entry strategy.

## Context
Business and region details:
{{business-description}}

Target region:
{{target-region}}

Known competitors (if any):
{{known-competitors}}

## Output
Deliver a structured market research report covering:

### 1. Industry Analysis
- Industry classification and scope
- Current trends: growth rates, technological shifts, regulatory landscape
- Socio-economic factors influencing the industry

### 2. Competitive Landscape
- Major competitors: market share, strengths, weaknesses
- Their marketing strategies, positioning, and product/service offerings
- Market gaps and underserved segments

### 3. Target Audience Analysis
- Primary and secondary audience segments
- Needs, preferences, and pain points (use available data, surveys, or credible secondary sources)
- Alignment between the business offering and audience needs

### 4. SWOT Analysis
- Strengths: internal advantages and differentiators
- Weaknesses: internal limitations or resource gaps
- Opportunities: market conditions favorable to entry
- Threats: competitive, regulatory, or market risks

### 5. Market Demand Evaluation
- Target market size and addressable demand
- Sales volume forecast based on comparable data and competitor benchmarks
- Recommended pricing strategy aligned with value perception and purchasing power

### 6. Regulatory and Legal Considerations
- Regional licenses, permits, and compliance requirements
- Estimated compliance costs and operational impact

### 7. Opportunities and Threats Summary
- Emerging opportunities: technology adoption, behavior shifts, untapped segments
- Key threats: market saturation, new entrants, regulatory changes

### 8. Strategic Recommendations
- Viability assessment and risk rating
- Prioritized actions for market entry
- Success metrics and milestones

Format the report with clear headings, bullet points for readability, and data-driven insights where possible.
```
