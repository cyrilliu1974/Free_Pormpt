# Educational Video Series Planner for Content Creators

## 簡介

The Educational Video Series Planner for Content Creators is a free AI prompt that produces complete three-video educational series plans for content creators looking to establish subject-matter authority in their niche. This educational video series prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your niche and target audience to generate a cohesive series theme, individual video titles with three supporting key points each, tailored calls to action that drive engagement, and a three-point promotion strategy. It begins by profiling your audience's demographics, pain points, and goals, then builds a logical progression across three videos - from foundational concepts through advanced applications to synthesis and implementation. Content creators in fields from software development to nutrition coaching use it to plan YouTube series, course modules, and LinkedIn video campaigns that position them as go-to experts. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, returning structured text plans ready to guide your video production. ● Produces a unified three-video series theme that positions you as a subject-matter authority ● Generates per-video titles, three key points, and calls to action aligned to audience pain points and goals ● Delivers audience demographics, pain points, and goals analysis before content planning ● Includes a three-point promotion strategy covering primary channels, cross-promotion, and engagement amplification ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write educational video scripts that establish content creators as subject-matter authorities in their niches.

## Context

- Niche: {{niche}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

Produce a complete three-video series plan with:
- A coherent series theme
- For each video: title, three key points, and a call to action that drives engagement
- A three-point promotion strategy

Calibrate all content to the audience's pain points and goals.

## Output

**Target Audience**
- Demographics: [derived from context]
- Pain Points: [identified from audience needs]
- Goals: [aligned with niche]

**Video Series Theme:** [unifying theme across all three videos]

**Video 1**
- Title: [foundational topic title]
- Key Points:
  1. [core concept]
  2. [supporting insight]
  3. [actionable takeaway]
- Call to Action: [engagement driver]

**Video 2**
- Title: [building on video 1]
- Key Points:
  1. [advanced concept]
  2. [practical application]
  3. [deeper insight]
- Call to Action: [next step]

**Video 3**
- Title: [synthesis topic]
- Key Points:
  1. [integration concept]
  2. [implementation guidance]
  3. [long-term strategy]
- Call to Action: [community or conversion focus]

**Video Series Promotion Strategy**
1. [primary channel approach]
2. [cross-promotion tactic]
3. [engagement amplification method]
```
