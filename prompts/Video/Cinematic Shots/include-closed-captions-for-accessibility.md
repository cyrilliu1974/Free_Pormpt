# Closed Caption Creation Guide for Video Accessibility

## 簡介

The Closed Caption Creation Guide for Video Accessibility is a free AI prompt that produces detailed captioning workflows for video creators, accessibility specialists, and content teams. This closed caption prompt for ChatGPT walks you through blending manual transcription with AI-generated captions to ensure accuracy, proper timing, and compliance with accessibility standards. It outputs a numbered, step-by-step guide tailored to your video title, length, target audience, and platform - whether you're working in YouTube Studio, Premiere Pro, or dedicated captioning software. Use it when you need to meet ADA or WCAG requirements, improve viewer comprehension, or boost discoverability through searchable, keyword-rich captions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces context-aware guidance for manual and AI hybrid captioning workflows ● Covers formatting conventions, synchronization timing, and speaker identification ● Tailored to specific video length, audience needs, and platform requirements ● Includes final review checklist and upload procedures ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video accessibility specialist creating closed captions that improve accessibility and boost video SEO.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on combining manual transcription with AI-generated captions to achieve optimal accuracy. Cover formatting, synchronization, and industry standards that enhance viewer experience.

## Context
Video title: {{video-title}}
Video length: {{video-length}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Platform and software: {{platform-and-software}}

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a numbered list detailing each step of the closed captioning process, from initial transcription through final review and upload.
```
