# Technical SEO Strategy Generator for Websites

## 簡介

The Technical SEO Strategy Generator for Websites is a free AI prompt that builds a complete technical SEO roadmap covering the three core foundations: sitemap optimization, robots.txt configuration, and redirect management. This technical SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your website and produces a structured strategy with actionable implementation steps for each pillar. It delivers clear guidance on crawl optimization, crawler access control, and redirect best practices, complete with prioritized action items and ongoing maintenance recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models.
