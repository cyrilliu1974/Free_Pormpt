# Customer Demographics Table Generator

## 簡介

The Customer Demographics Table Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored demographic tracking tables for businesses analyzing their customer data. This customer demographics prompt for ChatGPT produces a formatted markdown table with four essential columns: Age Range, Gender, Location, and Purchase History. Simply provide your company name, and the prompt generates 3-5 sample rows plus a detailed legend defining age segments, geographic markets, product categories, purchase frequency tiers, and average order value brackets - all customized to your specific business context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering clean, scannable tables that align with your data presentation needs without unnecessary complexity. Marketing analysts, CRM managers, and business strategists use this prompt when they need to structure demographic insights for reporting, strategy sessions, or customer segmentation planning. ● Produces a complete 4-column table with Age Range, Gender, Location, and Purchase History in markdown format ● Automatically tailors age brackets, geographic regions, product categories, and value tiers to your company's market ● Includes a comprehensive legend defining all demographic segments, frequency tiers, and order value ranges ● Generates 3-5 representative sample rows that reflect realistic customer profiles for your business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a table designer specializing in customer demographics tracking systems.

## Task
Design a 4-column customer demographics table for {{company-name}} with these columns:
- **Age Range**: relevant segments for the company's customer base
- **Gender**: M/F/Other
- **Location**: primary geographic markets
- **Purchase History**: product categories, purchase frequency, and average order value

## Requirements
1. Tailor age ranges, geographic regions, product categories, frequency tiers, and order value brackets to {{company-name}}'s specific market and offerings
2. Structure and format the table to align with the company's data presentation needs
3. Focus on essential demographic insights in a concise, scannable format
4. Avoid unnecessary complexity

## Output
Provide:
1. A formatted markdown table with the four columns and 3-5 representative sample rows
2. A legend below the table listing:
   - Age ranges used
   - Geographic regions/markets included
   - Product categories tracked
   - Purchase frequency tiers (e.g., Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly)
   - Average order value brackets (e.g., $0-50, $51-200, $201+)

Title the table "{{company-name}} Customer Demographics".
```
