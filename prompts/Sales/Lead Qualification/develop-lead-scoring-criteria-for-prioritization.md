# Lead Scoring Criteria Builder for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Lead Scoring Criteria Builder for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that creates customized prioritization frameworks to help sales and marketing teams identify and nurture high-quality leads. This lead scoring criteria prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - including sales cycle length, conversion patterns, and industry dynamics - to produce a complete markdown table with 8-12 lead attributes, their scoring criteria, and relative score weightings. It covers demographic fit (company size, industry, role), behavioral engagement (email opens, content downloads, website visits), and purchase intent signals (budget authority, timeline, pain point urgency). Use it when building or refining your lead qualification process, aligning sales and marketing on priorities, or launching a new product with different buyer profiles. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces a three-column table (Lead Attributes | Scoring Criteria | Score Weighting) in markdown format ready for immediate implementation ● Includes demographic, engagement, and buying-intent attributes shaped by your specific sales cycle and conversion patterns ● Assigns numeric weightings that reflect the relative importance of each attribute for your business ● Delivers 8-12 attributes for comprehensive lead evaluation without overwhelming your sales team ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lead scoring strategist designing a comprehensive prioritization framework.

## Task
Develop a structured lead scoring system that identifies and nurtures high-quality leads. Analyze the business context to determine key lead attributes, establish clear scoring criteria for each attribute (demographic information, engagement level, buying intent), and assign score weightings that reflect relative importance.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Consider how the sales cycle length, conversion patterns, and industry dynamics shape which lead behaviors and characteristics predict conversion.

## Output
Present the lead scoring system as a markdown table with three columns:

| Lead Attributes | Scoring Criteria | Score Weighting |
|-----------------|------------------|------------------|

The table must be comprehensive, immediately actionable, and tailored to the specified business context. Include 8-12 attributes covering demographic fit, behavioral engagement, and purchase intent signals.
```
