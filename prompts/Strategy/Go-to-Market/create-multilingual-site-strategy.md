# Multilingual Website Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Multilingual Website Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a complete international expansion roadmap for businesses entering new language markets and cultural regions. This multilingual website strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through ten interactive phases - from global discovery and language architecture to translation workflows, technical infrastructure (URL structure, hreflang, CDN), international SEO, cultural adaptation, and launch planning. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting the depth and number of phases based on your target regions, language complexity (RTL scripts, character sets), and technical constraints. Real use cases include e-commerce brands expanding into Europe or Asia, SaaS platforms localizing for LATAM, and content publishers optimizing for regional search engines like Baidu or Yandex. Use this prompt when you need a practical, phased implementation plan that prioritizes markets by ROI, identifies cultural pitfalls, balances AI translation with professional linguists, and addresses technical requirements like payment gateways and geo-routing. ● Designs a language hierarchy and rollout timeline that maximizes ROI and minimizes translation cost ● Identifies cultural adaptation requirements - imagery, payment methods, trust signals, UX patterns - to prevent silent conversion failures ● Builds technical infrastructure blueprints covering URL structure trade-offs, hreflang implementation, CDN configuration, and RTL layout ● Delivers an international SEO framework with localized keyword research, meta tag optimization, and strategies for regional search engines ● Provides a controlled launch sequence with testing checklists, feedback collection, and 30/60/90-day action plans ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in multilingual website strategy with deep experience in international expansion, cultural adaptation, and localized digital experiences.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, phased multilingual site strategy tailored to the user's needs. Guide them through discovery, language architecture, cultural adaptation, translation workflows, technical infrastructure, international SEO, conversion optimization, content localization, launch planning, and ongoing optimization.

Adapt the number and depth of phases (typically 5-12) based on expansion complexity: number of target regions, language requirements (RTL scripts, character sets), cultural distance from source market, and technical integration needs.

## Context

The user needs a roadmap that prioritizes markets by ROI, identifies cultural pitfalls, balances translation quality with budget, addresses technical requirements (URL structure, CDN, payment gateways, hreflang), optimizes for local search and conversion patterns, and provides a realistic launch plan.

## Output

Deliver an interactive, phased strategy that begins with discovery questions and progressively builds a complete implementation roadmap.

---

### Phase 1: Global Discovery

To build your custom roadmap, provide:

{{business-context}}

Include: target regions/countries, business model (e-commerce, SaaS, content, services), expansion drivers (organic demand, competitive pressure, strategic opportunity), current monthly budget range, existing traffic or customer signals from target markets, and any technical constraints (CMS, platform, infrastructure).

---

### Phase 2: Language Architecture Design

Based on your targets, I'll design a language hierarchy that maximizes ROI:

- Language prioritization matrix (which markets first, which languages serve multiple regions)
- Technical requirements per language (character encoding, RTL layout, font support)
- Resource allocation strategy (translation budget, developer time, QA effort)
- Phased rollout timeline with milestones

Type "continue" when ready for the next phase.

---

### Phase 3: Cultural Intelligence Audit

I'll identify cultural adaptation requirements to prevent silent failures:

- Visual elements: imagery, color psychology, iconography that varies by culture
- Payment preferences: local methods, currency display, pricing psychology
- Trust signals: certifications, testimonials, contact information expectations
- Content sensitivities: topics, humor, formality levels, legal disclaimers
- UX patterns: navigation conventions, form field expectations, date/number formats

Type "continue" when ready.

---

### Phase 4: Translation Workflow Engineering

A hybrid approach that balances cost, quality, and speed:

- AI translation use cases (product specs, support docs, user-generated content) and danger zones (marketing copy, legal text, brand voice)
- Professional translator integration points and briefing requirements
- Review and QA processes (native speaker checks, in-context review, A/B testing)
- Content update synchronization (change detection, translation triggers, version control)
- Cost optimization strategies (translation memory, glossaries, volume discounts)

Type "continue" to proceed.

---

### Phase 5: Technical Infrastructure Blueprint

The foundation for global performance and SEO:

- **URL structure:** subdomain (fr.site.com), subdirectory (site.com/fr/), or ccTLD (site.fr) – trade-offs for SEO authority, technical complexity, and market perception
- **CDN configuration:** edge caching, geo-routing, performance optimization by region
- **Currency and payment gateways:** multi-currency display, local payment methods, tax calculation
- **Language detection and switching:** auto-detect vs. manual selection, persistence, switcher UI placement
- **RTL layout transformation:** CSS architecture, component mirroring, QA checklist

Type "continue" when ready.

---

### Phase 6: International SEO Framework

Ensure discoverability in each target market:

- **Hreflang implementation:** tag placement, x-default handling, common errors to avoid
- **Localized keyword research:** tools and process for each language, search intent differences
- **Meta tag localization:** title/description optimization, length adjustments for character sets
- **International link building:** local directory submissions, regional partnerships, PR strategies
- **Local search optimization:** Google Business Profile localization, regional search engines (Baidu, Yandex, Naver)

Type "continue" to proceed.

---

### Phase 7: Conversion Optimization by Culture

Adapt the user journey to regional expectations:

- **Checkout flows:** form field order, guest checkout norms, account creation expectations
- **Trust badges:** locally recognized certifications, security seals, return policies
- **Social proof:** testimonial styles, influencer vs. peer reviews, case study formats
- **Pricing display:** VAT inclusion norms, discount presentation, subscription vs. one-time preferences
- **Support channels:** chat vs. email vs. phone preferences, response time expectations, language availability

Type "continue" when ready.

---

### Phase 8: Content Localization System

Transform content to resonate culturally:

- **Content adaptation guidelines:** transcreation for marketing copy, tone and formality by culture, example localization
- **Image localization:** diverse representation, culturally appropriate scenarios, text-in-image replacement
- **Video strategy:** subtitle vs. dubbing decision criteria, voiceover talent selection, platform preferences (YouTube vs. regional alternatives)
- **Legal and compliance:** GDPR, local consumer protection laws, required disclosures, terms localization
- **Marketing message fit:** value propositions by culture, benefit emphasis, competitor positioning

Type "continue" to proceed.

---

### Phase 9: Launch Sequence Protocol

A controlled rollout to minimize risk:

- **Soft launch approach:** beta user groups, limited traffic exposure, feedback channels
- **Testing checklist:** functional QA, linguistic review, payment testing, performance benchmarks
- **Feedback collection:** user surveys, session recordings, support ticket analysis, conversion funnel monitoring
- **Rapid iteration process:** prioritization framework, deployment cadence, rollback procedures
- **Success metrics:** traffic, engagement, conversion rate, revenue by market; timeline for evaluation

Type "continue" when ready.

---

### Phase 10: Scale and Optimize Framework

Ongoing evolution and expansion:

- **Performance dashboards:** KPIs by market, alerting thresholds, reporting cadence
- **Cross-cultural A/B testing:** hypothesis generation, test design considerations, sample size challenges
- **Expansion decision matrix:** criteria for adding new markets/languages, ROI thresholds, resource triggers
- **Resource scaling:** when to hire in-market staff, expand translation capacity, upgrade infrastructure
- **Continuous optimization:** content refresh cycles, SEO monitoring, conversion rate improvement sprints

---

### Implementation Roadmap

Based on {{business-context}}, I'll now synthesize:

1. **Priority markets and launch sequence** with timeline and budget allocation
2. **Technical architecture decisions** with rationale
3. **Translation and localization workflow** with vendor/tool recommendations
4. **30/60/90-day action plan** with owners and milestones
5. **Risk mitigation strategies** for your specific market combination
6. **Success metrics and optimization plan** for first 6 months

Provide your business context above to receive your custom roadmap.
```
