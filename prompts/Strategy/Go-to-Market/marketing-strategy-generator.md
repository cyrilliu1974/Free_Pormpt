# Go-to-Market Strategy Generator for New Products

## 簡介

The Go-to-Market Strategy Generator for New Products is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive marketing strategies tailored to your product, target audience, and unique selling points. This go-to-market strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through a two-phase process: brainstorming core marketing ideas, then refining them into an actionable strategy covering positioning and messaging, channel mix, campaign concepts, pricing and promotions, and strategic rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured marketing plan that connects every tactic back to your target audience and competitive differentiation. Product marketers use it to develop launch plans quickly, founders rely on it to map out their first campaigns, and marketing consultants deploy it to create client deliverables. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, end-to-end marketing strategy for a new product or feature launch and want to ensure every channel and message aligns with your unique selling proposition. ● Produces positioning statements, key messages, and competitive differentiation tailored to your target audience ● Recommends a prioritized channel mix across social media, content marketing, paid ads, events, and partnerships ● Generates high-level campaign concepts and content themes ready for creative development ● Includes pricing strategy and promotional tactics aligned with your unique selling points ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist developing a comprehensive go-to-market plan.

## Task
Create a complete marketing strategy for launching a new product based on:

**Product**: {{product-description}}

**Target audience**: {{target-audience}}

**Unique selling points**: {{usp}}

## Process

### Phase 1: Ideation
First, brainstorm initial ideas covering:
- Core value proposition and key messages
- Potential marketing channels and tactics
- Differentiation from competitors
- Campaign concepts and content themes
- Pricing and promotional approaches

Capture rough ideas without full development at this stage.

### Phase 2: Strategy Development
Refine your brainstorm into a cohesive marketing strategy that includes:

- **Positioning & Messaging**: Key marketing messages and how the product should be positioned in the market
- **Channel Mix**: Priority marketing channels and tactics (social media, content marketing, paid advertising, events, partnerships, etc.)
- **Campaign Concepts**: High-level ideas for marketing campaigns, content types, and creative executions
- **Pricing & Promotions**: Recommended pricing strategy and promotional tactics
- **Strategic Rationale**: Clear explanation of how each element connects to the target audience and unique selling points

Make reasonable assumptions for unstated details, but do not invent product features or selling points beyond those provided.

## Output
Present your brainstorm and final strategy using tables and bullet points for clarity.
```
