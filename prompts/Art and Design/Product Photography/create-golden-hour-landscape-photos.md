# Golden-Hour Landscape Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Golden-Hour Landscape Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic wide-angle landscape images with warm, dramatic golden-hour lighting for photographers, content creators, and designers. This golden-hour landscape prompt for Midjourney produces images of rolling hills bathed in soft, angled light with long shadows, rim-lit grass, and crisp foreground rocks. Built around a simulated 16–35mm f/4 wide-angle lens on a full-frame sensor, the prompt delivers dynamic contrast, soft atmospheric haze, and emotional warmth typical of magic-hour photography. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, outputting 16:9 images suitable for hero banners, environmental backdrops, and visual storytelling. Use it when you need cinematic landscape photography without a camera or location scout. ● Simulates a 16–35mm wide-angle lens with full-frame sensor characteristics for authentic landscape perspective ● Produces soft haze, rim lighting on grass, and crisp foreground detail that mimics real golden-hour conditions ● Outputs in 16:9 aspect ratio with high quality settings for web, presentation, and editorial use ● Delivers dynamic contrast and emotional warmth without requiring manual parameter tuning ## Prompt

```
Golden-hour landscape of rolling hills and long shadows, photographed with a 16–35mm f/4 wide-angle lens. Soft haze, rim-lit grass, crisp foreground rocks. Shot on full-frame sensor, dynamic contrast, emotional warmth. --ar 16:9 --q 2
```
