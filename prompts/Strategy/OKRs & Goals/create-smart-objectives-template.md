# SMART Objectives and OKR Template Generator

## 簡介

The SMART Objectives and OKR Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates goal-setting frameworks for teams and departments seeking to align performance with company strategy. This SMART objectives prompt for ChatGPT guides the creation of three objectives - each Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound - accompanied by three quantifiable key results per objective. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete template that includes progress tracking methods, alignment notes connecting team goals to organizational priorities, and a list of resources needed for execution. Teams use it for quarterly planning cycles, department kickoffs, and strategic reviews where clarity and measurability matter. It is built for managers, team leads, and strategy professionals who need a repeatable process for translating vision into trackable outcomes. ● Outputs three fully articulated SMART objectives, each with three measurable key results ● Includes dedicated sections for progress tracking methods and alignment with broader company goals ● Adapts to any team or department and any timeframe you specify ● Provides space for listing resources, tools, and support needed to achieve the objectives ## Prompt
