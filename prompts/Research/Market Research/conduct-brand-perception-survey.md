# Brand Perception Survey Builder for Market Research

## 簡介

The Brand Perception Survey Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive market research surveys to assess how your brand is perceived relative to competitors. This brand perception survey prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete questionnaire that covers four critical dimensions: brand awareness, brand image, brand loyalty, and competitive positioning. You input your brand name, target market, key competitors, unique selling proposition, and survey goal, and the prompt generates a logically structured survey that moves from general to specific questions. The output combines closed-ended questions (rating scales, multiple-choice) with open-ended free-text responses to capture both quantitative metrics and qualitative insights. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for market researchers, brand managers, and business strategists who need to understand consumer attitudes, measure brand health, or benchmark performance against competitors in a specific market segment. ● Creates questions organized into four core categories: brand awareness, brand image, brand loyalty, and competitive positioning ● Balances quantitative data collection (rating scales, multiple-choice) with qualitative insights (open-ended responses) ● Tailors every question to your specific brand, target market, competitors, and unique selling proposition ● Follows survey design best practices by structuring questions from general to specific for optimal respondent engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher designing a brand perception survey.

## Task
Create a comprehensive survey that assesses brand positioning in a specific target market. Develop questions covering brand awareness, brand image, brand loyalty, and competitive positioning.

## Context
**Brand & Market:**
{{brand-name}}

**Target Market:**
{{target-market}}

**Key Competitors:**
{{competitors}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:**
{{usp}}

**Survey Goal:**
{{survey-goal}}

## Output
Structure the survey logically, moving from general to specific questions. Include a mix of:
- Closed-ended questions (rating scales, multiple-choice)
- Open-ended questions (free-text responses)

This combination will capture both quantitative and qualitative data across all four perception dimensions.

Format your output as a numbered list of survey questions, grouped by category:
1. Brand Awareness
2. Brand Image
3. Brand Loyalty
4. Competitive Positioning
```
