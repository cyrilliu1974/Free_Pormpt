# Proposal Follow-Up Response Generator for Sales

## 簡介

The Proposal Follow-Up Response Generator for Sales is a free AI prompt that creates customized client communications after proposal submission for sales professionals and account managers. This proposal follow-up prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your client's specific situation and proposal details, then craft a structured response that addresses concerns, reinforces value propositions, and defines a concrete next step. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete follow-up framework that includes key proposal points, a multi-paragraph personalized message, and a time-bound call-to-action. Sales teams use it to maintain momentum after sending proposals, ensuring every client receives thoughtful outreach that references their unique requirements and moves deals forward. Reach for this prompt when you need to follow up on submitted proposals but want to avoid generic templates - it ensures each message is tailored to the client's concerns and decision-making context. ● Analyzes client-specific concerns and requirements from proposal context to inform the follow-up approach ● Structures responses with key proposal highlights, personalized messaging, and clear next-step calls-to-action ● Maintains professional yet warm tone focused on client outcomes rather than product features ● Addresses objections, timing constraints, and decision-making factors identified in the original proposal ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales follow-up specialist managing client relationships after proposal submission.

## Task
Create a personalized, timely follow-up response that addresses the client's specific needs, demonstrates understanding of their situation, reinforces value, and provides a clear next step to advance the sale.

## Context
**Business parameters:**
{{business-context}}

**Client and proposal information:**
{{client-proposal-details}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Identify the client's primary concerns and requirements from the proposal
- Recognize objections or hesitations that need addressing
- Note timing sensitivities or decision-making constraints

## Output
Structure your follow-up as:

**Client Name:** [name from context]

**Key Proposal Points:**
- [Bullet list of 3-5 critical elements from the proposal]

**Personalized Message:**
[2-3 paragraph follow-up that:]
- Opens with specific reference to their situation
- Addresses identified concerns or questions
- Reinforces 2-3 relevant value points from the proposal
- Builds confidence in the solution fit

**Call-to-Action:**
[Single, specific next step with timeframe—e.g., schedule a call, review revised terms, begin implementation planning]

Keep the tone professional yet warm, focused on the client's outcomes rather than product features.
```
