# Grant Application Writer for Nonprofits and Researchers

## 簡介

The Grant Application Writer for Nonprofits and Researchers is a free AI prompt that produces structured, persuasive grant proposals aligned with funder priorities. This grant application prompt for ChatGPT guides you through eight essential sections: executive summary, statement of need, SMART goals and objectives, detailed methodology with milestones, measurable impact and evaluation strategy, itemized budget with timeline, organizational capacity demonstration, and a sustainability plan. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, using dependency grammar principles to ensure every paragraph flows logically and builds a persuasive case. Organizations use it to write foundation grants, government proposals, research funding applications, and nonprofit program requests. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete grant narrative that connects your project to a funder's mission with evidence and clarity. ● Aligns project goals with the specific priorities and mission of the grant provider ● Generates SMART objectives, evidence-based needs statements, and measurable evaluation plans ● Produces itemized budgets with justifications and phased implementation timelines ● Includes organizational capacity proof and a sustainability plan for long-term impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer specializing in securing funding through persuasive, evidence-based proposals.

## Task
Craft a comprehensive grant application that aligns with the funder's priorities and demonstrates clear impact. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to maximize clarity and persuasion.

## Context
Grant provider and focus: {{grant-provider}}

Project details: {{project-description}}

Organization and beneficiaries: {{organization-context}}

## Output
Deliver a complete grant application with these sections:

**Executive Summary**
Concise overview connecting the project to the funder's mission

**Statement of Need**
Evidence-based case for why this project matters now

**Project Goals and Objectives**
SMART objectives that address the identified need

**Methodology and Approach**
Detailed implementation plan with milestones

**Impact and Evaluation**
Measurable outcomes and assessment strategy

**Budget and Timeline**
Itemized costs with justification and phased schedule

**Organizational Capacity**
Relevant experience, qualifications, and past successes

**Sustainability Plan**
How the project will continue beyond the grant period

Ensure each section builds logically on the previous one, with clear cause-effect relationships and no redundancy.
```
