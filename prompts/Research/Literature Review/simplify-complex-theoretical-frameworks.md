# Simplify Complex Theoretical Frameworks

## 簡介

The Simplify Complex Theoretical Frameworks prompt is a free AI prompt that helps researchers, students, and science communicators break down dense academic theories into accessible, well-cited overviews for general audiences. This theoretical framework prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to research and distill any theoretical framework - from epistemology to chaos theory - into a structured summary with historical context, core components, real-world applications, and key takeaways. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing bulleted outlines that maintain academic rigor while avoiding excessive jargon. Use it when preparing literature reviews, teaching materials, or grant proposals that require clear explanations of foundational theories. ● Structures output into historical origins, key principles, practical applications, and takeaways for easy comprehension ● Prompts citation of reputable sources for each claim, ensuring academic credibility ● Balances accessibility for non-specialists with the accuracy required for professional research contexts ● Applicable to any theoretical framework across disciplines - psychology, sociology, education, philosophy, or natural sciences ## Prompt
