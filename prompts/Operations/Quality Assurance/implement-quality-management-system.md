# Quality Management System Implementation Prompt

## 簡介

The Quality Management System Implementation Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds complete, customized quality management frameworks for organizations seeking ISO 9001 compliance and continuous improvement. This quality management system prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini generates seven structured components: quality policy statements, management responsibility matrices, resource allocation plans, product realization workflows, measurement and analysis frameworks, improvement initiatives, and tracked KPIs. Input your organization profile - industry sector, size, and current quality challenges - and receive a detailed QMS tailored to your operational context, incorporating Six Sigma methodologies and Kaizen continuous improvement principles. Organizations use it to establish new quality systems, upgrade existing frameworks for ISO certification, or address specific quality pain points in manufacturing, healthcare, professional services, and technology sectors. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, audit-ready quality management system that moves beyond generic templates to address your organization's actual challenges. ● Produces quality policy statements, management responsibilities, and resource plans customized to your industry and company size ● Integrates ISO 9001 structure with Six Sigma measurement rigor and Kaizen continuous improvement practices ● Defines actionable KPIs with measurement frequency, targets, and accountability assignments for every QMS component ● Delivers production controls, supplier management protocols, and nonconformance handling procedures specific to your operational challenges ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a quality management expert specializing in ISO 9001 standards, Six Sigma methodologies, and Kaizen continuous improvement principles.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive quality management system (QMS) tailored to the organization's context that ensures consistently high standards of product and service delivery across all operations.

## Context

**Organization profile:**
{{organization-profile}}

Include industry sector, organization size (employees/revenue/locations), and current quality challenges or pain points.

## Output

Structure your QMS framework with these components:

### 1. Introduction
Provide an overview of the QMS, its objectives, and how it addresses the organization's specific quality challenges.

### 2. Quality Policy
Draft a quality policy statement reflecting the organization's commitment to quality and continuous improvement.

### 3. Management Responsibility
Outline top management roles and responsibilities:
- Leadership commitment to the QMS
- Customer focus mechanisms
- Quality objectives and planning

### 4. Resource Management
Address:
- Human resource competencies and training
- Infrastructure requirements
- Work environment controls

### 5. Product Realization
Cover:
- Planning of product/service realization
- Customer-related processes
- Design and development controls
- Purchasing and supplier management
- Production and service provision

### 6. Measurement, Analysis, and Improvement
Detail:
- Monitoring and measurement processes
- Control of nonconforming products/services
- Data analysis methods
- Continuous improvement initiatives (Kaizen, Six Sigma)

### 7. KPIs and Metrics
Recommend specific key performance indicators with targets, measurement frequency, and responsible parties for each QMS area.

Present all recommendations with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Tailor every section to the organization's industry, size, and quality challenges rather than providing generic advice.
```
