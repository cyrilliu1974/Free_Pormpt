# E-Commerce Platform Strategy Comparison Generator

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Platform Strategy Comparison Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a customized multi-platform selling guide for solopreneurs and online sellers. This e-commerce platform strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through account setup, listing optimization, pricing structures, fulfillment, customer service, and marketing tools across 5-7 major platforms including Amazon, Etsy, eBay, and Shopify. It takes your product type, target audience, and business constraints - budget, experience level, time availability - and outputs a markdown comparison table with columns for Platform, Key Steps, and Tips. Use it when evaluating where to sell online, planning a multi-channel strategy, or optimizing your presence on platforms you already use. The guide highlights fee structures, SEO best practices for each marketplace, and common pitfalls specific to solopreneurs. ● Compares 5-7 platforms in a single table: account requirements, listing optimization, pricing, fulfillment, and marketing tools for each ● Tailors recommendations to your product category, audience, budget, and experience level ● Highlights platform-specific fees, SEO tactics, and promotion features so you can prioritize the best fit ● Identifies common mistakes and best practices for solopreneurs managing multiple storefronts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce strategist specializing in multi-platform selling strategies for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive platform comparison guide that outlines step-by-step strategies for selling products across major e-commerce platforms (Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Shopify, and relevant marketplace alternatives).

## Context
Product and business details:
{{product-and-audience}}

Business constraints:
{{business-constraints}}

For each platform, address:
- Account setup and seller requirements
- Product listing optimization (titles, descriptions, images, SEO)
- Pricing strategy and fee structures
- Fulfillment options
- Customer service best practices
- Platform-specific marketing and promotion tools
- Common pitfalls to avoid

Tailor recommendations to the solopreneur's experience level and budget, highlighting which platforms offer the best fit given their constraints.

## Output
Present your guide as a markdown table with three columns:

| Platform | Key Steps | Tips |

Ensure each row provides concise, actionable guidance. Include 5-7 platforms. Prioritize platforms most suitable for the specified product type and target audience.
```
