# Ethical Link Building Plan Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Ethical Link Building Plan Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a customized, actionable link building strategy for SEO consultants and website owners focused on compliance and quality. This link building prompt for ChatGPT takes your website URL, current domain authority, referring domain count, and target keywords, then outputs a comprehensive checklist covering backlink audits, content asset development, broken link building, guest posting, community engagement, digital PR, influencer collaboration, and progress tracking. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured plan with checkboxes and key considerations that prioritize relevance and authority over manipulative tactics. Use it when you need a clear roadmap for growing your backlink profile without risking search engine penalties. ● Produces a complete backlink strategy checklist customized to your domain authority, referring domains, and keyword targets ● Covers eight core activities from backlink audits and content creation to digital PR and influencer outreach ● Emphasizes quality over quantity, relevance, and adherence to search engine guidelines ● Includes ongoing monitoring steps to track domain authority changes and keyword ranking impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in ethical, white-hat link building strategies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable link building checklist tailored to the user's website. The checklist must focus on techniques that comply with search engine guidelines and prioritize quality, relevance, and authority.

## Context
**Website Overview:**
- Website URL: {{website-url}}
- Current domain authority: {{domain-authority}}
- Number of referring domains: {{referring-domains}}
- Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}

## Output
Deliver a structured checklist with checkboxes, organized as follows:

### Website Overview
- Website URL: {{website-url}}
- Current domain authority: {{domain-authority}}
- Number of referring domains: {{referring-domains}}
- Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}

### Link Building Checklist
1. **Conduct a thorough backlink audit**
   - [ ] Identify and analyze existing backlinks using tools like Ahrefs or SEMrush
   - [ ] Detect toxic or spammy links and disavow as needed
   - [ ] Benchmark competitors' backlink profiles

2. **Develop high-quality, linkable content assets**
   - [ ] Create original research, data studies, or industry reports
   - [ ] Produce comprehensive guides, tutorials, or resource pages
   - [ ] Design infographics, tools, or interactive content

3. **Leverage broken link building**
   - [ ] Identify broken links on relevant authority sites
   - [ ] Reach out with replacement content from your site

4. **Pursue guest posting opportunities**
   - [ ] Identify authoritative, relevant blogs in your niche
   - [ ] Pitch unique, valuable content ideas
   - [ ] Include natural, contextual links in author bios or content

5. **Engage in relevant online communities**
   - [ ] Participate authentically in industry forums, Reddit, and Quora
   - [ ] Share expertise and link to your content when genuinely helpful

6. **Implement a digital PR strategy**
   - [ ] Create newsworthy content or campaigns
   - [ ] Pitch stories to journalists and industry publications
   - [ ] Respond to journalist requests on platforms like HARO

7. **Collaborate with industry influencers**
   - [ ] Build relationships through social engagement and outreach
   - [ ] Co-create content or secure mentions and links

8. **Monitor and track progress**
   - [ ] Set up regular backlink monitoring (monthly minimum)
   - [ ] Track changes in domain authority and referring domains
   - [ ] Measure impact on target keyword rankings and organic traffic

### Key Considerations
- **Quality over quantity:** One link from a high-authority, relevant site outweighs dozens of low-quality links
- **Relevance and authority:** Target websites that align with your niche and have strong domain metrics
- **Guideline compliance:** Avoid paid links, link schemes, or manipulative tactics that violate search engine policies
- **Ongoing monitoring:** Regularly audit your backlink profile to identify and address toxic links or negative SEO attacks
```
