# Premium Fashion Advertisement Layout Generator

## 簡介

The Premium Fashion Advertisement Layout Generator is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-style fashion campaign layouts for apparel brands and marketers. This fashion advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a structured 3:4 portrait layout featuring an elegant slogan at the top, bold brand name, a large hero product shot of the garment in premium studio lighting, and a lower 2×2 grid showcasing fabric texture close-ups, embroidered emblem details, side-profile fit shots, and lifestyle scenes. The output mimics high-end editorial campaigns with clean minimalist aesthetics, soft diffused lighting, and balanced composition - perfect for Instagram posts, retail displays, lookbook covers, and campaign posters. Reach for this prompt when you need polished fashion imagery that showcases apparel from multiple angles without organizing a full studio shoot. ● Produces a complete ad layout with custom slogan, brand name, hero garment shot, and four detail panels in a clean 2×2 grid. ● Customize brand name and garment type to generate on-brand campaigns for shirts, jackets, dresses, or any apparel category. ● Delivers modern editorial aesthetics with soft studio lighting, premium fabric textures, and refined minimalist composition. ● Outputs 3:4 portrait format optimized for social media, print campaigns, and digital lookbooks. ## Prompt

```
Premium 3:4 portrait fashion advertisement for {{brand-name}} {{garment-type}}.

**Layout Structure:**
- Top center: Slogan "Timeless Style. Effortless Confidence." in elegant artistic serif with high-fashion spacing, subtle emboss effect, deep forest green or muted charcoal color
- Below slogan: {{brand-name}} in clean luxury sans-serif, bold weight, wide letter spacing, deep signature brand tone
- Upper half: Large hero product shot—modern tailored {{garment-type}} in classic white or forest green premium breathable cotton, signature embroidered brand emblem, soft structured collar, high-quality stitching. Garment centered against soft beige or light grey studio wall, straight-on or slight angle, soft diffused studio lighting with subtle grounding shadow
- Lower half: 2×2 grid showing (1) macro close-up of cotton texture and stitch precision, (2) detailed shot of embroidered brand emblem thread detail, (3) side profile on model or mannequin showing silhouette and fit, (4) lifestyle scene with model in modern minimal outdoor setting, effortless sporty elegance

**Visual style:** Modern fashion editorial, high-end minimalism, premium studio photography, balanced composition, clean layout design. Soft diffused daylight studio lighting, crisp balanced exposure, subtle refined shadows.

**Mood:** Refined, confident, sporty, elegant, timeless, modern.

**Avoid:** Wrinkled fabric, overly dramatic shadows, low-resolution logo, distorted garment proportions, cluttered background, cartoon style, watermarks, text artifacts.
```
