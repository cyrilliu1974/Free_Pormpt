# Grammar and Syntax Checker Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Grammar and Syntax Checker Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that reviews and corrects written text for grammar, punctuation, and syntax errors while maintaining the original tone and meaning. This grammar checker prompt for ChatGPT works systematically through any text to fix subject-verb agreement, verb tenses, pronoun usage, comma placement, semicolons, apostrophes, sentence fragments, run-ons, and awkward phrasing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to refine word choice, eliminate redundancy, improve flow and coherence, and deliver a polished final version with optional notes on significant improvements. Writers use it to prepare drafts for publication, polish business communications, clean up academic papers, or ensure professional presentation in any written material. Reach for this prompt when you need a thorough grammar review that goes beyond basic spell-check to address syntax, structure, and clarity without changing your original voice. ● Corrects complex grammatical errors including subject-verb agreement, verb tenses, and pronoun consistency ● Fixes punctuation mistakes and resolves syntactical issues like sentence fragments and run-ons ● Refines word choice, eliminates redundancy, and rearranges sentences to improve flow and coherence ● Provides optional feedback notes highlighting significant improvements made during the editing process ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grammar checker.

## Task
Meticulously review and correct the provided text for grammar, punctuation, and syntax errors. Maintain the original meaning and tone while enhancing clarity, conciseness, and structure.

## Process
1. Read the entire text to understand its context, tone, and purpose
2. Correct grammatical errors: subject-verb agreement, verb tenses, pronoun usage
3. Fix punctuation mistakes: commas, semicolons, apostrophes
4. Resolve syntactical issues: sentence fragments, run-ons, awkward phrasing
5. Ensure logical structure; rearrange sentences or paragraphs to improve flow and coherence
6. Refine word choice and eliminate redundancy
7. Provide brief feedback on significant improvements made
8. Verify all corrections flow smoothly

## Text to Review
{{text}}

## Output
Provide the polished, grammatically correct version of the text. Retain the original meaning and tone. Include brief notes on significant improvements where helpful.
```
