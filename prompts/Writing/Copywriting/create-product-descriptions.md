# Product Description Generator for SEO and Conversions

## 簡介

The Product Description Generator for SEO and Conversions is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, search-optimized product copy for ecommerce stores, landing pages, and digital catalogs. This product description prompt for ChatGPT translates technical specifications into tangible customer value, emphasizes unique selling propositions, and weaves 3-8 SEO keywords into natural, flowing sentences. You supply product details (features, specs, differentiators), target audience traits (needs, pain points, priorities), and priority keywords - the prompt outputs a polished 150-300 word description with an attention-grabbing hook, 3-5 benefit-focused features, clear competitive differentiation, and a strong call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, formatting output with short paragraphs and strategic bullet points for scannability. Use this when you need product copy that serves both human shoppers and search algorithms, whether you're writing for Shopify stores, Amazon listings, or SaaS product pages. ● Transforms technical features into customer value statements that answer "What's in it for me?" instead of listing dry specifications ● Integrates 3-8 SEO keywords naturally into readable prose without awkward stuffing or sacrificing persuasive flow ● Structures descriptions for scannability with short paragraphs, bullet points for key specs, and clear information hierarchy ● Applies proven copywriting techniques - action verbs, specificity, urgency cues, social proof hooks - to drive purchase decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in digital marketing and SEO-optimized product descriptions.

## Task
Write a compelling product description that converts browsers into buyers by clearly communicating features, benefits, and unique selling propositions while maximizing search visibility.

## Context
Product details: {{product-details}}
(Include the product name, category, key features, technical specifications, and what makes it unique compared to competitors)

Target audience: {{target-audience}}
(Describe who will buy this product, their needs, pain points, and what matters most to them)

SEO keywords: {{seo-keywords}}
(List 3-8 priority keywords and phrases to integrate naturally)

## Approach
1. **Lead with benefits, not just features** – Translate technical specifications into customer value ("What's in it for me?")
2. **Emphasize USPs** – Make clear why this product beats alternatives
3. **Integrate keywords naturally** – Weave SEO terms into flowing, readable sentences
4. **Use persuasive techniques** – Action verbs, specificity, urgency where appropriate, social proof if relevant
5. **Structure for scannability** – Short paragraphs, bullet points for key specs, clear hierarchy
6. **Close with a strong CTA** – Direct readers to purchase, learn more, or contact support

## Output
Deliver a polished product description (150-300 words) that:
- Opens with a hook that captures attention and states the core benefit
- Explains 3-5 key features as customer benefits
- Highlights what makes this product unique
- Integrates all provided SEO keywords seamlessly
- Ends with a clear, action-oriented CTA

Format with short paragraphs and bullet points where they enhance readability.
```
