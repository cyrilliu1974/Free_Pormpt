# Community Event and Contest Planning for Link Building

## 簡介

The Community Event and Contest Planning for Link Building is a free AI prompt that produces complete strategic plans for viral community events and contests that earn organic backlinks through shareability and participation. This community event prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating 3–5 distinct engagement campaigns, each with defined objectives, target audiences, promotional strategies, success metrics, timelines, and budgets. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, the prompt delivers structured project plans covering creative submission contests, challenge-based events, collaborative community projects, and gamified experiences. Content marketers, SEO specialists, and community managers use it to design events that naturally attract links from participants, media coverage, and industry mentions while building engaged communities around brands or content. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond standard content marketing and create interactive experiences that people want to share and link to organically. ● Produces 3–5 complete event plans tailored to different engagement styles and audience segments ● Includes detailed promotional strategies covering pre-launch, launch, ongoing amplification, and influencer outreach ● Defines clear success metrics for participation, engagement, link building, and reach ● Provides week-by-week timelines and itemized budgets for realistic execution planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content strategist and digital marketer specializing in community engagement, viral mechanics, and organic link acquisition.

## Task
Develop a series of community events and online contests that generate buzz, encourage social sharing, and drive natural link building for the provided content.

## Context
Content overview:
{{content-overview}}

## Output
For each proposed event or contest, provide a complete project plan using this structure:

**Event/Contest [Number]: [Name]**

**Objective:** Primary goal and expected outcome

**Target Audience:** Specific demographic, interest group, or community segment

**Event/Contest Details:**
- Core mechanic and participation method
- Unique value proposition for participants
- Prizes, recognition, or incentives
- Entry requirements and rules

**Promotional Strategy:**
1. Pre-launch awareness tactics
2. Launch activation channels
3. Ongoing engagement and amplification methods
4. Partner and influencer outreach approach

**Success Metrics:**
• Participation metrics (entries, registrations, submissions)
• Engagement metrics (shares, mentions, user-generated content)
• Link building metrics (backlinks acquired, referring domains, DA/DR)
• Reach metrics (impressions, unique visitors, social reach)

**Timeline:**
Week 1: [Planning and setup milestone]
Week 2: [Promotion and launch milestone]
Week 3: [Active engagement milestone]
Week 4: [Wrap-up and measurement milestone]

**Budget:** Itemized cost breakdown including platform fees, prizes, promotional spend, tools, and contingency

---

Provide 3–5 distinct events or contests, each tailored to different engagement styles (e.g., creative submission contest, challenge-based event, collaborative community project, gamified experience). Prioritize originality, shareability, and natural link-earning potential.
```
