# Business Presentation Slide Deck Builder

## 簡介

The Business Presentation Slide Deck Builder is a free AI prompt that transforms raw information into structured, visually-supported slide decks for professionals presenting to decision-makers. This business presentation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok converts unstructured content into a complete slide deck outline where every slide includes an action-oriented title, exactly three logically-sequenced talking points written for spoken delivery, and a specific visual recommendation that enhances comprehension rather than decoration. The prompt organizes material into a cohesive story arc with clear transitions, ensuring each slide builds on the previous one and maintains audience attention throughout. Marketing managers use it to turn campaign data into executive briefings, consultants transform findings into client recommendations, and product teams convert feature specs into stakeholder pitches. Reach for this prompt when you have information-dense content that must be distilled into a persuasive, visually-clear presentation for an audience with limited time. ● Converts raw content into slide outlines with action-oriented titles that focus on audience benefit, not generic topic labels. ● Delivers exactly three talking points per slide, organized to build logically and written in conversational language for spoken delivery. ● Provides one specific, practical visual recommendation per slide that clarifies the message rather than simply decorating it. ● Includes presentation flow notes that explain key transitions and narrative connections across the entire deck. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert presentation architect who transforms raw information into structured, visually-supported slide decks. You specialize in balancing comprehensive content with narrative flow and visual clarity for decision-maker audiences with limited attention spans.

## Task

Convert the provided content into a comprehensive slide deck outline. For each key point, create one slide containing:

- A compelling, action-oriented slide title (audience-focused, not generic topic headers)
- Exactly 3 supporting talking points that build logically and are conversational for delivery
- One specific, practical visual recommendation that enhances comprehension (not decorative)

Ensure a logical flow between slides that tells a cohesive story arc across the entire presentation.

- [Third supporting point that reinforces or concludes the slide message]

Visual Idea: [Specific, practical visual recommendation]

[Repeat for each slide]

### Presentation Flow Notes
[Key transitions and narrative connections between slides]

### Speaker Tips
[Practical delivery recommendations for maximum impact]
```
