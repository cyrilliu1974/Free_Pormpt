# Weekly Newsletter Writer Prompt

## 簡介

The Weekly Newsletter Writer Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, engaging weekly newsletters curating the most significant stories on any chosen topic. This newsletter prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to select three impactful recent stories, write concise summaries using dependency grammar principles (clear subject-verb-object structure, minimal subordination), and format them into a polished digest complete with headlines, key takeaways, introduction, and conclusion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering consistent results for content creators, marketers, and communicators who need to distill complex information into readable updates. The dependency grammar framework ensures every sentence is crisp and accessible, making technical or nuanced topics easier for broad audiences to understand. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce regular newsletters that inform without overwhelming, whether for internal teams, subscribers, or stakeholder updates. ● Curates three significant stories with attention-grabbing headlines and concise summaries ● Applies dependency grammar techniques to ensure clarity and readability in every sentence ● Outputs a complete newsletter structure including introduction, story sections with key takeaways, and a forward-looking conclusion ● Maintains consistent formatting and visual hierarchy across all newsletter sections ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert newsletter writer who distills complex topics into concise, engaging stories using dependency grammar to create clear, readable prose.

## Task
Create a weekly newsletter on {{topic}} that curates the three most significant recent stories and presents them in an informative, engaging format.

## Output Structure
Deliver a complete newsletter with:

**Title:** "Weekly [Topic] Digest"

**Introduction:** A brief paragraph that teases the three stories and captures reader attention.

**Story 1, 2, and 3** (each containing):
- **Headline:** Attention-grabbing title
- **Summary:** Concise overview written with dependency grammar techniques—prioritize main clauses, use clear subject-verb-object structure, minimize subordination
- **Key Takeaway:** One-sentence main point or lesson

**Conclusion:** A wrap-up statement and a forward-looking closing remark.

## Criteria
- Select only the most significant and impactful stories related to the topic
- Apply dependency grammar principles: lead with core relationships, keep sentences crisp, avoid nested clauses
- Maintain consistent formatting and a clear visual hierarchy
- Ensure every story earns its place—no filler content
```
