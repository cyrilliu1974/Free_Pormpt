# Frozen-Motion Product Splash Image Generator

## 簡介

The Frozen-Motion Product Splash Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-impact food photography with suspended splashes and airborne ingredients for brands, marketers, and content creators. This frozen-motion splash prompt for Midjourney produces commercial-grade visuals of yogurt erupting from bowls with fruits, granola, or toppings frozen mid-air against clean white backgrounds, delivering sharp texture detail and photorealistic composition in a 1:1 aspect ratio. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, using raw processing and controlled stylization to replicate the high-speed photography used in advertising and e-commerce. Reach for this prompt when you need standout product imagery for packaging, social media, or landing pages without a photo studio. ● Specify bowl materials, colors, and yogurt flavors to match your brand palette and product line. ● Control which ingredients fly through the frame - berries, nuts, granola, chocolate, or tropical fruits. ● Outputs photorealistic commercial-style images with sharp detail, clean backgrounds, and consistent 1:1 framing. ● Replicates expensive high-speed product photography using AI image generation at a fraction of the cost. ## Prompt
