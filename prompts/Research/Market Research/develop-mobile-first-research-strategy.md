# Mobile-First Research Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Mobile-First Research Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that designs research methodologies tailored to the behavioral patterns, attention spans, and technical constraints of mobile users. This mobile-first research strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured plan covering survey design for thumb navigation and progressive disclosure, testing protocols for varying network conditions and device capabilities, engagement tactics using push notifications and location awareness, and data collection methods optimized for small screens and touch interactions. It transforms traditional desktop research paradigms into mobile-native approaches that capture authentic handheld behaviors, addressing multitasking, environmental distractions, and interrupted sessions. UX researchers use it to boost mobile survey completion rates, product teams rely on it to design testing protocols that reflect real-world usage, and market research professionals apply it to gather reliable data from mobile-first audiences. ● Designs surveys with progressive disclosure patterns and thumb-friendly interfaces that respect micro-moment attention spans and reduce abandonment. ● Creates testing protocols accounting for network variability, device fragmentation, multitasking behaviors, and real-world environmental contexts. ● Develops engagement strategies using mobile-native features like sensors and location awareness while maintaining privacy boundaries. ● Provides measurable success metrics including completion rates, time-on-task, drop-off points, and engagement thresholds for continuous optimization. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a mobile UX research strategist specializing in mobile-first methodologies, micro-interactions, and behavioral patterns unique to handheld device usage.

## Task
Design a comprehensive mobile-first research strategy that accounts for the unique constraints and behaviors of mobile users. Address:

- Survey design optimized for thumb navigation, progressive disclosure, and micro-moment attention spans
- Testing protocols that accommodate real-world mobile contexts: varying network conditions, device capabilities, multitasking, environmental distractions, and interrupted sessions
- Engagement strategies leveraging mobile-native features (push notifications, location awareness, device sensors) while respecting privacy and attention boundaries
- Data collection methods that work within small screens and touch-based interactions
- Approaches that capture authentic mobile behaviors rather than adapting desktop paradigms

## Context
Mobile users exhibit different behavioral patterns, decision-making processes, and engagement thresholds than desktop users. Traditional desktop research methodologies fail on mobile: users abandon surveys quickly, cognitive load collapses attention spans, and screen limitations constrain conventional data collection.

**Research parameters:**
{{research-context}}

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear section headings. For each research component, provide:

- Actionable implementation steps in bullet points
- Specific mobile design recommendations
- Technical considerations
- Measurable success metrics (completion rates, time-on-task, drop-off points, engagement thresholds)

Prioritize practical, deployment-ready guidance.
```
