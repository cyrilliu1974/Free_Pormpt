# Podcast Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Podcast Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a structured launch and growth plan for podcasters tailored to their niche, target audience, budget, and available resources. This podcast plan prompt for ChatGPT delivers 11 sections covering every aspect of starting and scaling a show: niche viability, audience demographics and psychographics, a show premise, three content pillars, episode format and duration, publishing cadence, equipment recommendations, a detailed 3-month content calendar with 12 episode titles and descriptions, pre-launch and ongoing marketing tactics, monetization options, and actionable tips specific to your inputs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Whether you are planning a business podcast on a shoestring budget or a narrative true-crime series with studio resources, the prompt adapts its recommendations to your constraints and goals. ● Produces a 3-month content calendar with episode titles and descriptions aligned to your niche. ● Recommends equipment, recording software, and editing tools matched to your budget. ● Provides niche-specific marketing tactics for pre-launch, launch, and sustained growth. ● Suggests three monetization pathways tailored to your audience and resources. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You plan podcasts. Given a niche, target audience, budget, and available resources, you produce a structured launch and growth plan covering content, production, marketing, and monetization.

## Context

- Niche: {{niche}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Budget: {{budget}}
- Available resources: {{available-resources}}

## Task

Produce a complete podcast plan with the following sections, in order:

1. **Niche Overview:** summarize the niche, its size, and what makes it viable for a podcast
2. **Target Audience Analysis**
   - Demographics
   - Psychographics
   - Content Preferences
3. **Podcast Premise:** the show concept in 2-3 sentences
4. **Content Pillars**
   - Pillar 1
   - Pillar 2
   - Pillar 3
5. **Episode Format**
   - Duration
   - Segments
   - Delivery Style
6. **Publishing Schedule:** cadence and day/time recommendation
7. **Equipment and Software**
   - Microphone
   - Recording Software
   - Editing Software
8. **Monthly Content Calendar** (3 months, 4 episodes each)
   - Month 1: Episode 1–4 (Title | Description)
   - Month 2: Episode 1–4 (Title | Description)
   - Month 3: Episode 1–4 (Title | Description)
9. **Marketing Strategy**
   - Pre-launch Tactics
   - Launch Tactics
   - Ongoing Growth Tactics
10. **Monetization Options**
    - Option 1
    - Option 2
    - Option 3
11. **Additional Recommendations:** 3-5 actionable tips specific to this niche and budget

## Output

Tailor every section to the inputs above. Recommendations must be specific to the niche and budget. No generic advice. Keep each section concise and actionable.
```
