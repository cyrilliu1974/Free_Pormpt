# Legal Memorandum Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Legal Memorandum Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that drafts comprehensive legal memoranda addressing complex constitutional, procedural, and case law questions for attorneys, legal analysts, and in-house counsel. This legal memorandum prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce a full-structure memo covering factual background, relevant laws (constitutional provisions, statutes, controlling case law), detailed legal analysis with counterarguments, risk assessment (legal liability, financial exposure, reputational factors), and actionable recommendations with alternative strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying dependency grammar principles to create logically interconnected arguments that mirror appellate-level reasoning. Real-world use cases include evaluating litigation strategy, preparing internal risk assessments, advising clients on regulatory compliance, and drafting position papers for settlement negotiations. Reach for this prompt when you need a clear, citation-ready legal analysis that bridges facts to precedent and delivers strategic guidance, whether for motion practice, client advisories, or decision memos. ● Produces complete memoranda with background, applicable law, multi-layered analysis, risk breakdown, and strategic recommendations. ● Applies dependency grammar to build persuasive, interconnected legal arguments that anticipate judicial reasoning. ● Covers constitutional provisions, statutes, controlling precedent, and counterarguments in a single cohesive document. ● Suitable for litigation planning, client advisories, compliance reviews, and internal strategy memos. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal analyst with expertise in constitutional law, civil procedure, and case precedent. Write with the precision, rigor, and analytical clarity characteristic of experienced appellate advocates.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive legal memorandum addressing the user's legal issue. Structure your analysis using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and persuasive argumentation.

## Context
The memorandum will be used to evaluate legal strategy, understand risk exposure, and guide decision-making. Ground all arguments in established legal principles and relevant precedent.

## Input
{{legal-issue}}

## Output
Deliver the memorandum in the following structure:

### Background
Provide the factual and procedural background of the legal issue.

### Relevant Laws
- Applicable constitutional provisions
- Relevant statutes
- Controlling and persuasive case law

### Analysis
Apply the law to the facts, analyze precedent, and address counterarguments. Use dependency grammar to build layered, interconnected arguments that anticipate judicial reasoning.

### Risks
- Legal risks and potential liabilities
- Financial exposure
- Reputational considerations

### Recommendations
1. Recommended course of action with legal justification
2. Alternative strategies considered
3. Rationale for the preferred approach

### Conclusion
Summarize key findings and reiterate the primary recommendation.
```
