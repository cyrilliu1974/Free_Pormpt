# Twitter Ads Targeting Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Twitter Ads Targeting Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized audience targeting strategies for Twitter advertising campaigns. This Twitter Ads targeting prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and produces a structured markdown table containing distinct audience segments, relevant interests those audiences engage with on Twitter, and precise keywords - including hashtags, phrases, and industry terms - for reaching them. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering at least 10 viable targeting combinations that balance campaign reach with relevance. Digital marketers use it to move beyond generic demographics and discover specific interest clusters and keyword strategies that align with how their ideal customers actually behave on Twitter. Reach for this prompt when planning a new Twitter Ads campaign, expanding targeting options for existing ads, or researching audience segments you haven't yet tapped. ● Identifies 10+ distinct audience segments most likely to convert for your specific business ● Maps relevant topics, interests, and Twitter accounts each segment follows ● Generates keyword strategies including hashtags, phrases, and industry terms for precise ad delivery ● Outputs a clean markdown table format ready to inform your Twitter Ads campaign setup ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in Twitter Ads audience targeting and campaign optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of Twitter Ads targeting options tailored to the business described below. For each targeting idea, identify the specific audience segment, relevant interests they engage with, and keywords that would effectively reach them.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze this business to:
- Identify distinct audience segments most likely to convert
- Map interests, topics, and accounts these segments follow on Twitter
- Select keywords (including hashtags, phrases, and industry terms) for precise targeting

Ensure suggestions are specific, actionable, and diverse to maximize campaign reach and relevance.

## Output
Deliver your targeting ideas as a markdown table with three columns:

| Target Audience | Interests | Keywords |
|-----------------|-----------|----------|

Provide at least 10 distinct targeting combinations, each row representing a viable audience segment with corresponding interests and keyword strategies.
```
