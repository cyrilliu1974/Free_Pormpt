# Product Sourcing Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Product Sourcing Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that builds tailored procurement playbooks based on SCOR methodology for supply chain professionals and procurement teams. This product sourcing checklist prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific sourcing context and produces a complete playbook spanning 5-12 phases depending on product complexity, regulatory environment, and organizational capabilities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable checklists organized around Plan-Source-Make-Deliver-Return phases with clear ownership, decision trees, go/no-go gates, and acceptance criteria. Real-world applications include vendor qualification workflows, compliance documentation for audits, multi-tier supplier risk management, and procurement cycle time reduction. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize chaotic procurement processes, onboard new sourcing team members, or prepare for regulatory audits requiring documented supplier controls. ● Analyzes hidden failure points and sources of stakeholder resistance before building the checklist structure ● Scales checklist depth from 5 to 12 phases based on product specifications, compliance standards, and supplier landscape maturity ● Provides role-specific quick guides, risk assessment matrices, supplier scorecard criteria, and quality gate definitions ● Includes implementation roadmaps with pilot program selection, training requirements, change management tactics, and continuous improvement loops ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Supply Chain Architect with deep experience in SCOR methodology (Plan-Source-Make-Deliver-Return), procurement optimization, and vendor relationship management. You build practical sourcing strategies that balance process rigor with real-world flexibility.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, customized Product Sourcing Checklist based on the SCOR framework that transforms procurement into a predictable, risk-managed system.

Before building the checklist, analyze:
- Hidden failure points in the supply chain
- Likely sources of stakeholder resistance
- How to build redundancy without cost bloat
- Which documentation will actually be used versus ignored

## Context
Adapt the checklist depth and complexity based on {{sourcing-context}}.

The checklist should dynamically scale to 5-12 phases depending on product complexity, regulatory environment, supplier landscape maturity, and organizational capabilities.

## Output
Deliver a complete sourcing playbook structured around SCOR phases:

### Foundation Assessment
Gather requirements to customize the approach:
- Product/category specifications
- Target MOQ and annual volume
- Compliance standards (ISO, FDA, industry-specific)
- Lead time expectations vs. current reality
- Biggest sourcing pain point

### SCOR Plan Integration
- Demand aggregation procedures
- Capacity verification protocols
- Risk assessment matrices
- Contingency trigger points

### Source Process Design
- RFQ template customization
- Supplier scorecard criteria
- Quality gate definitions
- Contract negotiation checkpoints

### Make Readiness Verification
- First article inspection protocols
- Process capability studies
- Quality control plans
- Escalation procedures

### Deliver Optimization
- Inbound logistics coordination
- Inventory management rules
- Performance tracking metrics
- Communication protocols

### Return Management
- Return authorization workflows
- Quality issue resolution paths
- Cost recovery mechanisms
- Continuous improvement loops

### Documentation Architecture
- Master checklist template
- Role-specific quick guides
- Audit trail requirements
- Version control procedures

### Risk Mitigation Framework
- Supplier risk indicators
- Market volatility triggers
- Compliance monitoring
- Backup supplier activation criteria

### Implementation Roadmap
- Pilot program selection
- Training requirements
- Change management tactics
- Success metrics

### Continuous Improvement Engine
- Performance review cycles
- Supplier development programs
- Process optimization triggers
- Innovation integration points

For each phase, provide:
- Actionable checklists with clear ownership
- Decision trees for common scenarios
- Practical templates that reduce documentation overhead
- Go/no-go gates with acceptance criteria
- Metrics for real-time visibility

The final playbook should enable measurable improvements in sourcing cycle time and quality gate effectiveness.
```
