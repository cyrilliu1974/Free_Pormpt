# Photorealistic Product Photography Prompt Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Product Photography Prompt Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-end commercial product shots with professional studio lighting and luxury styling. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana structures each scene with pedestal staging, complementary botanical elements (petals, leaves), diamond-inspired cap detailing, and macro 100mm lens simulation with shallow depth of field. It produces eye-level three-quarter angles, ultra-sharp product focus with natural bokeh, and seamless color-matched backgrounds - ideal for e-commerce, advertising campaigns, cosmetics launches, and brand look-books. You simply describe your product, and the prompt handles composition, lighting ratios, and commercial-quality output flags. Reach for this prompt when you need catalog-ready visuals without a physical photo studio or when iterating on packaging concepts and hero shots. ● Pedestal staging with soft glow, botanical accents, and seamless color-matched backgrounds for cohesive luxury presentation. ● Macro 100mm lens effect with shallow depth of field - sharp product in focus, smooth bokeh behind. ● Professional studio lighting setup with controlled soft shadows and eye-level three-quarter camera angle. ● Commercial quality flags (--ar 1:1 --q 2 --style raw) tuned for print and digital advertising use. ## Prompt

