# Kirkpatrick Model Training Evaluation Template Generator

## 簡介

The Kirkpatrick Model Training Evaluation Template Generator is a free AI prompt that produces complete assessment frameworks for learning and development professionals measuring training program impact. This training evaluation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini builds four distinct template sections aligned to Donald Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Evaluation Model. It generates Likert-scale items, open-ended questions, behavioral observation checklists, and business impact metrics tailored to your specific training program and organizational context. Each template includes administrator deployment instructions, optimal timing recommendations, and analysis guidance for interpreting results and identifying improvement actions. Training coordinators, L&D managers, and HR specialists use it to replace generic feedback forms with rigorous, objective-aligned assessment instruments that capture meaningful data from immediate participant reactions through long-term business results. ● Produces separate templates for Reaction (immediate satisfaction), Learning (knowledge/skill gains), Behavior (on-the-job application), and Results (business impact) with deployment timing for each level. ● Combines quantitative rating scales, qualitative open-ended questions, and behavioral checklists that align directly to your stated learning objectives and organizational environment. ● Includes administrator instructions covering deployment methods, sample sizes, and optimal survey timing from post-training through 180 days. ● Provides analysis frameworks showing how to interpret data, calculate ROI where applicable, and translate findings into actionable continuous improvement steps. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a learning evaluation specialist with deep expertise in Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Evaluation Model, designing feedback systems that align assessment rigor with organizational needs.

## Task
Create a complete set of training evaluation templates covering all four Kirkpatrick levels (Reaction, Learning, Behavior, Results). Each template must be deployment-ready with specific questions, rating scales, and implementation instructions.

## Context
Effective training evaluation fails when questions are generic or misaligned with learning objectives. Your templates must:
- Progress logically through all four Kirkpatrick levels
- Combine quantitative scales, qualitative questions, and behavioral checklists
- Match the specific training context and organizational environment
- Provide actionable insights for continuous improvement

**Training program details:**
{{training-program-details}}

**Organizational context:**
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver four separate template sections with these specifications:

### Level 1: Reaction
- Likert-scale items (1-5 or 1-7) measuring satisfaction, engagement, and perceived relevance
- 3-5 open-ended questions capturing immediate participant feedback
- Optimal deployment: immediately post-training

### Level 2: Learning
- Pre/post assessment questions aligned to stated learning objectives
- Skill demonstration scenarios or knowledge checks
- Measures of attitude or confidence shifts
- Optimal deployment: end of training session

### Level 3: Behavior
- Observation checklists for managers or peers
- Self-assessment surveys on on-the-job application
- Questions identifying implementation barriers and support needs
- Optimal deployment: 30-90 days post-training

### Level 4: Results
- Business metrics tied directly to training outcomes
- ROI calculation framework (if applicable)
- Organizational impact indicators
- Optimal deployment: 90-180 days post-training

**For each level, include:**
- Ready-to-use question text with rating scales
- Administrator instructions (deployment method, timing, sample size)
- Analysis guidance (how to interpret results and identify action items)

Format all templates for immediate copy-paste into survey tools or observation forms.
```
