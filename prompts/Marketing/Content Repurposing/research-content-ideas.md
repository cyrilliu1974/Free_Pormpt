# Content Idea Research Planner for Audience Targeting

## 簡介

The Content Idea Research Planner for Audience Targeting is a free AI prompt that generates strategic topic ideas aligned with business goals and audience interests for content marketers and strategists. This content idea research prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and starting theme, then produces a markdown table mapping 8-10 distinct topics to their ideal audience segments and recommended content formats. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing your market position to deliver platform-appropriate suggestions that balance innovation with practical execution. Use it when building editorial calendars, planning campaigns, or diversifying your content mix across blogs, social media, video, podcasts, and other channels. ● Produces a three-column table (Topic Ideas, Target Audience, Content Format) with 8-10 actionable entries ● Maps each topic to the specific audience segment most likely to engage with it ● Recommends the content format that will maximize value and platform fit for each idea ● Ensures variety across topics, audience segments, and content types to avoid repetitive planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in audience-driven content planning.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of topic ideas for the specified business, identifying the target audience segment and optimal content format for each.

## Context
Business and strategy parameters:
{{business-and-audience-context}}
(Include: business type, primary target audience, content goals, and preferred platforms)

Starting topic or theme:
{{topic}}

Analyze the business type and its market position. Brainstorm topics that align with business goals and audience interests. For each topic, specify which audience segment it serves best and which content format will maximize engagement and value. Ensure suggestions are diverse, innovative, and platform-appropriate.

## Output
Deliver your response as a markdown table with exactly three columns:

| Topic Ideas | Target Audience | Content Format |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| [topic 1]   | [segment]       | [format]       |
| [topic 2]   | [segment]       | [format]       |

Provide at least 8-10 distinct topic ideas with varied formats and audience segments.
```
