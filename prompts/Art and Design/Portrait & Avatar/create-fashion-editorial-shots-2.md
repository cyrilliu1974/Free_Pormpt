# Fashion Editorial Photography Prompt for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Fashion Editorial Photography Prompt for AI Image Models is a free AI prompt that generates studio-quality fashion shots with customizable subjects and editorial styles for photographers, creative directors, and content creators. It produces high-resolution images with a grainy film texture, neutral color palettes, and Prada-inspired minimalist aesthetics against clean backdrops, controlling lighting, depth of field, and composition through variable shot types and subject descriptions. This fashion editorial prompt for Midjourney works across image generation models including Flux and Nano Banana, making it ideal for lookbooks, portfolio work, social media content, and commercial fashion photography. Use it when you need professional editorial shots that balance clean styling with artistic film grain, or when you want consistent branding across a fashion campaign without the cost of a full studio shoot. ● Configurable shot types and subject descriptions let you directpose, wardrobe, and accessories while maintaining editorial consistency. ● Built-in specifications for soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, and eye-level framing replicate professional fashion photography setups. ● Grainy film effect and neutral color palettes create an authentic editorial aesthetic inspired by high-fashion brands. ● 4:5 aspect ratio optimized for Instagram, digital lookbooks, and vertical portfolio presentations. ## Prompt
