# User-Generated Content SEO Strategy Optimizer

## 簡介

The User-Generated Content SEO Strategy Optimizer is a free AI prompt that develops actionable plans to integrate and optimize reviews, comments, and testimonials for better organic search performance and user engagement. This user-generated content prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your website and delivers implementation-ready recommendations across six critical dimensions: content moderation workflows that balance spam prevention with SEO value, structured data markup for Review and AggregateRating schema, internal linking tactics that distribute link equity through UGC, showcase strategies that surface high-value user contributions, measurement frameworks for tracking search impact, and specific KPIs tailored to your site's content model. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a multi-section strategy document with concrete steps rather than generic best practices. Digital marketers use it to turn existing user contributions into measurable SEO assets, e-commerce sites apply it to optimize product reviews for search visibility, and content platforms rely on it to structure comment sections for indexing. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured plan to make user-generated content work harder for organic search, not just a list of UGC benefits. ● Produces a three-step content moderation strategy that protects SEO value while filtering spam and low-quality contributions ● Provides schema markup implementation steps for Review, AggregateRating, and FAQ structured data that enhance SERP features ● Delivers three internal linking tactics that route authority through user comments and reviews to key conversion pages ● Offers three showcase ideas for surfacing high-value UGC in ways that maximize both user trust and crawlability ● Defines three measurement steps and three KPIs with descriptions so you can quantify how user content affects rankings and traffic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer and SEO strategist specializing in user-generated content optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategy to integrate and optimize user-generated content (reviews, comments, testimonials) on {{website-url}} that improves organic search performance and user engagement.

## Output
Provide specific, actionable recommendations across these dimensions:

**Content Moderation Strategy**
- 3 key steps for effective moderation that balances spam prevention with SEO value

**Structured Data Implementation**
1. 3 steps for implementing schema markup (Review, AggregateRating, FAQ) to enhance search visibility

**Internal Linking Tactics**
🔗 3 tactics to leverage UGC for internal link equity distribution

**UGC Showcase Ideas**
💡 3 ideas for surfacing high-value user content to maximize SEO impact

**Measuring UGC SEO Impact**
📈 3 steps for tracking how user-generated content affects search performance

**Key Performance Indicators**
- 3 specific KPIs with descriptions for monitoring UGC contribution to SEO goals

Focus on tactics applicable to the specific site type and content model. Avoid generic advice—provide implementation-ready recommendations.
```
