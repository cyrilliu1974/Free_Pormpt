# Employee Exit Survey Builder

## 簡介

The Employee Exit Survey Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive exit surveys using Likert scale methodology for HR teams and organizational leaders. It produces a complete survey instrument organized into themed sections covering job satisfaction, management effectiveness, career development, workplace culture, and organizational support, with strategic placement of scaled and open-ended questions to minimize response bias. This employee exit survey prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok delivers three components: the full survey with labeled Likert scales (5 or 7-point), implementation guidelines covering timing and confidentiality protocols, and a data analysis framework for translating responses into prioritized improvement initiatives. Reach for this prompt when you need to capture candid feedback from departing employees that reveals root causes rather than surface complaints. ● Builds Likert scale questions across five core themes with strategic sequencing to build respondent trust and reduce bias. ● Pairs quantitative scaled items with targeted open-ended questions that allow elaboration on ratings. ● Includes administration protocols specifying optimal timing, confidentiality measures, and delivery methods. ● Provides an analysis framework showing how to aggregate scaled data, identify patterns in qualitative responses, and prioritize action items. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in exit survey design using Likert scale methodology.

## Task
Create a comprehensive employee exit survey that balances quantitative and qualitative feedback. The survey must:

- Use Likert scale questions (5 or 7-point scales) covering job satisfaction, management effectiveness, career development, workplace culture, and organizational support
- Include targeted open-ended questions that allow elaboration on scaled responses
- Structure question sequences to build trust and minimize response bias
- Optimize survey length for completion rates while capturing essential data
- Align with the company's specific engagement challenges and improvement goals

## Context
{{company-context}}

Departing employees provide the most candid feedback. Design questions that uncover root causes rather than surface complaints, ensuring responses translate directly into organizational improvements.

## Output
Deliver the exit survey in three parts:

### 1. Survey Sections & Questions
Organize questions by theme (satisfaction, management, development, culture, support). Label each scaled question with its point scale. Place open-ended questions strategically after related scaled items.

### 2. Implementation Guidelines
Specify survey administration timing, confidentiality protocols, and optimal delivery method for your company context.

### 3. Data Analysis Framework
Provide a structure for translating responses into actionable insights: how to aggregate scaled data, identify patterns in open responses, and prioritize improvement initiatives based on results.
```
