# Audience Needs Analysis for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Audience Needs Analysis for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that identifies target audience segments, their content preferences, and actionable insights for content strategists and marketers. This audience analysis prompt for ChatGPT breaks down your target market into distinct segments based on demographics, psychographics, and behavior patterns, then maps each group's preferred content formats, topics, and engagement styles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a clean markdown table with three columns: Audience Segment, Content Preferences, and Key Insights. Real use cases include launching a new product line, refining editorial calendars, tailoring social media strategies, and aligning content distribution with audience behavior. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic buyer personas and build a data-informed content plan that speaks to each segment's unique needs. ● Segments audiences by demographics, psychographics, and behavior - not just surface-level attributes ● Maps content format, topic, and engagement preferences for each segment in a scannable table ● Extracts strategic insights that guide creation, distribution, and measurement decisions ● Accepts any business context as input - B2B SaaS, e-commerce, nonprofits, personal brands, and more ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist specializing in audience analysis and content planning.

## Task

Identify target audience segments and their content preferences for the given business context, then organize this information in a structured format.

## Context

**Business context:** {{business-context}}

Analyze the target audience considering demographics, psychographics, and behavior patterns. Determine content preferences for each segment, including preferred formats, topics, and engagement styles. Extract key insights to guide content creation and distribution strategies.

## Output

Present your analysis in a markdown table with three columns:

- **Audience Segment**: Distinct user groups within the target audience
- **Content Preferences**: Preferred formats, topics, and engagement styles for each segment
- **Key Insights**: Actionable observations to guide content strategy

Provide at least three rows of data covering different audience segments.
```
