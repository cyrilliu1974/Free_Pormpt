# Visual Design Specs for Multi-Platform Campaigns

## 簡介

The Visual Design Specs for Multi-Platform Campaigns is a free AI prompt that produces structured design specifications for cohesive digital marketing materials across social media, web, and email channels. This multi-platform campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert digital marketing designer and deliver platform-specific design specifications for social media graphics, website banners, and email templates. You provide your brand name, color scheme, target audience, campaign message, and logo description, and the prompt generates detailed layout, typography, dimension, and branding guidelines tailored to each platform. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for marketing teams, freelance designers, and brand managers who need to maintain visual consistency across multiple touchpoints without creating redundant briefs. Reach for this prompt when launching a new campaign, onboarding a design team, or standardizing brand assets across channels. ● Produces social media graphic specs with platform dimensions, composition rules, and CTA design for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter ● Delivers responsive website banner guidelines covering desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints with focal-point mapping ● Generates email template specifications including header, body, footer layout, mobile-responsive considerations, and CTA button styling ● Maintains brand consistency by weaving your color scheme, logo placement, and campaign message through every deliverable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing designer specializing in cohesive, multi-platform campaign materials.

## Task
Design a complete set of marketing materials for a digital campaign, including social media graphics, website banners, and email templates. Ensure all deliverables are visually unified, brand-consistent, and optimized for their respective platforms.

## Context
- Brand name: {{brand-name}}
- Primary color scheme: {{color-scheme}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Campaign message: {{campaign-message}}
- Brand logo: {{logo-description}}

## Output
Provide structured design specifications under these headings:

### Social Media Graphics
- Platform-specific dimensions and format recommendations
- Composition, typography, and visual hierarchy
- Branding element placement
- Call-to-action design

### Website Banners
- Responsive sizing guidelines (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- Layout structure and focal points
- Integration with existing site design
- Messaging hierarchy

### Email Templates
- Header, body, and footer design specifications
- Mobile-responsive layout considerations
- CTA button styling and placement
- Brand consistency elements

For each material type, use bullet points to detail specific design elements, color applications, typography choices, and technical specifications that maintain visual unity across the campaign.
```
