# Employee Disengagement Diagnostic Framework Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Disengagement Diagnostic Framework Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a multi-layered detection system to uncover root causes of employee turnover before it happens, designed for HR leaders and organizational diagnosticians. Unlike reactive exit interviews or surface-level pulse surveys, this employee disengagement prompt for ChatGPT develops a detective-style framework that correlates behavioral patterns, engagement survey data, and departure trends to reveal systemic issues traditional metrics miss. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete diagnostic methodology with data collection protocols, risk assessment matrices, and implementation roadmaps tailored to your organization's profile and available systems. Reach for this prompt when leadership lacks visibility into why talent is leaving, when standard HR metrics fail to predict turnover, or when you need a repeatable process to catch disengagement early and intervene before institutional knowledge walks out the door. ● Combines quantitative survey analysis with qualitative behavioral signal detection to capture disengagement patterns traditional tools overlook. ● Delivers a step-by-step data collection phase, integration methodology, and risk assessment matrix so you can prioritize interventions by severity and department. ● Produces a monitoring system for ongoing engagement health tracking, turning one-time diagnostics into a continuous organizational feedback loop. ● Outputs implementation roadmaps with clear timelines and responsibilities, making the framework practical to deploy even in complex, multi-department environments. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational diagnostician specializing in employee disengagement prediction. You identify at-risk talent by combining quantitative data with behavioral pattern recognition, focusing on early warning signals that traditional engagement surveys miss.

## Context

The organization is losing talent while leadership lacks visibility into root causes. Employee surveys show declining engagement, but standard HR metrics fail to reveal systemic issues. Exit interviews surface only complaints, not underlying problems. Different departments exhibit varied symptoms of the same core disease. Previous retention efforts failed by treating symptoms rather than causes. Institutional knowledge is at risk.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive diagnostic framework that identifies employee disengagement before turnover occurs. Combine survey data analysis, behavioral signal detection, and exit trend evaluation into a systematic, repeatable process. The framework must capture complex organizational dynamics while remaining practical to implement.

**Organization profile:** {{org-profile}}

**Available data sources and systems:** {{data-sources}}

## Output

Provide your framework using this structure:

**Diagnostic Overview**
Framework structure and methodology approach

**Data Collection Phase**
Step-by-step process for gathering survey data, behavioral signals, and exit information

**Behavioral Indicators**
Specific signals and patterns that indicate disengagement risk (early warning signs traditional surveys miss)

**Survey Analysis Framework**
Methods for analyzing engagement survey data and identifying trends; focus on predictive signals over lagging indicators

**Exit Trend Evaluation**
Process for analyzing departure patterns and extracting insights beyond surface complaints

**Integration Methodology**
System for correlating multiple data sources into a coherent diagnostic picture

**Risk Assessment Matrix**
Framework for categorizing disengagement levels and prioritizing interventions

**Implementation Roadmap**
Detailed deployment steps with clear timelines and responsibilities

**Monitoring System**
Ongoing process for tracking engagement health and updating diagnostics

Emphasize actionable methods, specific metrics, and practical tools. Avoid generic engagement advice. Create a detective-like approach that hunts for interconnected clues across data sources.
```
