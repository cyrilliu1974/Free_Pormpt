# Product Catalog Layout With Technical Drawings

## 簡介

The Product Catalog Layout With Technical Drawings is a free AI prompt that transforms an uploaded product photo into a two-section vertical design-catalog image for brands, designers, and marketing teams. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 3:4 editorial layout: the top half places your product in a customizable lifestyle environment (modern kitchen, minimalist living room, studio backdrop) with angled natural light and soft shadows, while the bottom section arranges orthographic technical line drawings (front, side, and cutaway views) alongside material swatches and understated captions. You control the interior environment, drawing line color, and canvas background through three variables, and the prompt preserves your product's actual design and proportions without inventing details or adding decoration. Reach for this prompt when you need premium catalog pages, design portfolios, press kits, e-commerce hero images, or pitch decks that show both aspiration and specification in a single frame. ● Splits the canvas into a lifestyle hero (top) and technical specification panel (bottom) with modular grid layout. ● Renders orthographic line drawings in your chosen color with measurement callouts and construction details. ● Includes 3–4 material swatches (fabric, leather, metal, wood, plastic) derived from the product with refined captions. ● Maintains calm, architectural, premium mood with warm muted color grading and generous whitespace - no clutter or bold graphics. ## Prompt

```
Create a vertical 3:4 editorial product catalog image with two distinct sections.

**Top section (lifestyle hero):**
Photograph the uploaded product in {{interior-environment}} under natural angled side light with soft, high-contrast shadows. Place the product dominant and top-center with generous whitespace. Include a subtle ground surface and wall texture consistent with the environment. Render as editorial lifestyle photography with high realism and warm, muted, premium color grading.

**Bottom section (technical specification panel):**
Arrange a clean, modular grid of technical content:
- **Left and center:** Three architectural line drawings (front view, side view, three-quarter cutaway or profile) in orthographic projection using {{drawing-line-color}} fine technical lines. Add minimal measurement and construction callouts in neutral editorial style.
- **Right:** 3–4 square or rectangular material swatches derived from the product (fabric, leather, metal, wood, or plastic as applicable), each with small refined technical captions.

Use minimal editorial typography in soft black or dark brown for subtle captions only. Overall canvas background: {{canvas-surface}}. Maintain an architectural, premium, calm mood—avoid clutter, bold colors, heavy branding, or decorative graphics.

**Preserve the uploaded product exactly:** do not change its design, proportions, materials, or identity. Do not invent materials, add logos unless present in the reference, or use perspective distortion in technical drawings.
```
