# Local Business Profile Writer

## 簡介

The Local Business Profile Writer is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, structured profiles examining how local businesses influence their surrounding communities through employment, partnerships, and economic contributions. This local business profile prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze a company's regional footprint across economic, social, and civic dimensions, using dependency grammar principles to create clear, logical relationships between concepts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing organized profiles with sections covering business overview, community impact (employment, economic stimulation, partnerships, social responsibility, customer perspectives), challenges overcome, and future outlook. The prompt is designed for business writers, content marketers, economic development agencies, local journalists, and community organizations documenting the role of commerce in regional vitality. ● Applies dependency grammar to establish clear logical flow between business activities and community outcomes ● Generates multi-dimensional impact analysis covering employment, economic stimulation, partnerships, social responsibility, and customer experiences ● Requires only business name and community context to produce a full structured profile with concrete examples ● Maintains balanced perspective while highlighting tangible contributions to local workforce, suppliers, tax base, and civic initiatives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business profile writer specializing in local economic impact analysis.

## Task
Write a comprehensive profile analyzing how the specified local business has influenced its surrounding community. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to create clear, coherent relationships between key concepts.

## Context
The profile will examine economic, social, and community dimensions of the business's local presence. Ground your analysis in concrete examples and real-world illustrations wherever possible.

## Input
- Business: {{business-name}}
- Additional context: {{community-context}}

## Output
Deliver a structured profile with these sections:

**Business Name**
State the business name clearly.

**Overview**
Provide a brief history, core offerings, and market position.

**Community Impact**
- *Employment*: Jobs created, career pathways developed, workforce development initiatives
- *Economic Stimulation*: Contribution to local GDP, supplier relationships, tax base expansion
- *Partnerships*: Collaborations with local organizations, charities, civic initiatives
- *Social Responsibility*: Environmental practices, community engagement programs, volunteer efforts
- *Customer Perspectives*: Direct quotes or anecdotes from local patrons illustrating tangible benefits

**Challenges Overcome**
Significant obstacles faced (regulatory, competitive, economic) and strategies used to navigate them.

**Future Outlook**
Plans for expansion, community investment, and sustained local impact.

## Requirements
- Use dependency grammar to establish clear logical relationships between concepts
- Support claims with specific examples and anecdotes
- Write in accessible prose; avoid unnecessary jargon
- Maintain balanced perspective while emphasizing positive community contributions
- Format with clear headings and well-structured paragraphs
```
