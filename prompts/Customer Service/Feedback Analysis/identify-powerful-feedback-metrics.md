# Customer Feedback Metrics That Drive Business Impact

## 簡介

The Customer Feedback Metrics That Drive Business Impact is a free AI prompt that identifies seven actionable metrics connecting customer feedback programs to measurable business outcomes for executives, product managers, and customer success leaders. This customer feedback metrics prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and current feedback setup to surface both leading indicators - like Net Promoter Score, Customer Satisfaction Score, and effort scores - and lagging indicators such as churn rates, revenue impact, and customer lifetime value shifts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured breakdown of each metric's calculation method, direct connection to revenue or retention goals, and implementation requirements. Reach for this prompt when you need to justify feedback program investment, build executive dashboards, or move beyond surface-level satisfaction scores to predictive insights about customer behavior and business sustainability. ● Surfaces seven metrics spanning leading indicators like NPS and CSAT, and lagging indicators like churn rate and lifetime value trends. ● Explains the calculation method, business impact connection, and implementation requirements for each metric. ● Ties every metric directly to revenue goals, retention outcomes, or operational efficiency improvements. ● Provides a structured analysis format that helps executives and product teams justify feedback program investments with data. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business metrics analyst specializing in customer feedback systems and their impact on revenue, retention, and operational efficiency.

## Task
Identify 7 powerful metrics that demonstrate the direct business impact of customer feedback programs. Go beyond surface-level satisfaction scores to reveal predictive insights about customer behavior, revenue trends, and business sustainability. Include both leading indicators (NPS, CSAT, effort scores) and lagging indicators (churn rates, revenue impact, lifetime value shifts).

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Current feedback setup: {{current-feedback-setup}}

## Output
For each metric, provide:

- **Metric name**
- **What it measures** – the specific data point and calculation method
- **Business impact connection** – how it ties to revenue, retention, or efficiency goals
- **Implementation considerations** – what's needed to track it effectively

Structure your response with clear headings for each metric. Use bullet points for clarity. Ensure all metrics are measurable, actionable, and directly tied to business outcomes.
```
