# Industry Webinar and Podcast Series Planner

## 簡介

The Industry Webinar and Podcast Series Planner is a free AI prompt that creates a structured content marketing plan for webinar or podcast series designed to attract audiences, position brands as thought leaders, and generate backlinks from authoritative industry sources. This webinar planning prompt for ChatGPT walks you through industry analysis, format selection, episode outlines (6-8 episodes minimum), promotion strategies, production timelines, and success metrics. It produces a complete plan tailored to your target industry, featuring episode titles, subtopics, key takeaways, suggested guest speaker profiles, channel-specific promotion tactics, and a Gantt chart timeline covering planning through launch. Real-world use cases include SaaS companies launching thought-leadership series, agencies building link-earning content assets for clients, and B2B marketers creating educational resources that attract backlinks from industry publications. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This planner is built for content marketers, SEO specialists, and brand strategists who need a repeatable framework to turn webinars or podcasts into backlink-generating assets while addressing real industry pain points. ● Delivers 6-8 episode outlines with titles, subtopics, takeaways, and guest speaker profiles tailored to your industry. ● Includes a promotion strategy covering platform selection, content calendar phases, influencer tactics, and visual content approach. ● Provides a Gantt chart production timeline tracking planning, recording, editing, promotion, and launch dates for each episode. ● Defines success metrics for registrations, attendance, engagement, backlinks, and lead attribution to measure ROI. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content marketing strategist specializing in webinar and podcast series that drive audience engagement, establish thought leadership, and earn backlinks from authoritative industry sources.

## Task
Develop a complete content marketing plan for a webinar or podcast series targeting {{target-industry}}. The plan must attract the right audience, position the brand as a thought leader, and generate backlinks by creating resources other sites want to reference.

## Context
Successful series in {{target-industry}} address real pain points, feature credible voices, and deliver actionable insights. The plan should reflect current industry challenges, emerging trends, and topics that resonate with practitioners, decision-makers, and influencers in the space.

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with these components:

**1. Industry Overview**
- Target industry context and audience profile
- Key challenges, trends, and opportunities
- High-value topics that drive engagement and shareability

**2. Content Format**
Recommend webinar or podcast format with rationale based on audience preferences, content complexity, and distribution goals.

**3. Episode Outline (6-8 episodes minimum)**
For each episode provide:
- Episode title
- 3-4 subtopics covered
- Key takeaways for the audience
- Suggested guest speakers or interviewees (role/expertise, not specific names)

**4. Promotion Strategy**
- Primary platforms and channels (social, email, partnerships)
- Content calendar structure for pre-launch, launch, and post-launch phases
- Influencer and partner leverage tactics
- Visual and teaser content approach

**5. Production Timeline**
Gantt chart format:

| Episode Title | Planning | Recording | Editing | Promotion | Launch Date |
|---------------|----------|-----------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| [per episode] | [dates]  | [dates]   | [dates] | [dates]   | [date]      |

**6. Success Metrics**
- Registrants/subscribers
- Attendance rate/listen-through rate
- Engagement (questions, comments, shares)
- Backlinks from reputable sources
- Leads and conversions attributed to the series
```
