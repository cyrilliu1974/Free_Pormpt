# Close-Up Portrait Photography Generator

## 簡介

The Close-Up Portrait Photography Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional-quality profile portraits with cinematic lighting and high contrast for photographers, designers, and content creators. This portrait photography prompt for Midjourney creates close-up profile shots with moody color grading, sharp focus, and 8K-quality detail. You define the subject and background, and the prompt applies dramatic lighting with deep shadows, high contrast, and ultra-detailed rendering. Works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models. Use it for character design, editorial photography, social media content, book covers, or any project requiring expressive, high-impact portraits with a cinematic feel. ● Produces close-up profile portraits with cinematic lighting, deep shadows, and high contrast for a dramatic, professional look. ● Customizable subject description and background let you create portraits of any person, character, or concept. ● Outputs 8K-quality images with ultra-detailed features, moody color grading, and sharp focus. ● 2:3 aspect ratio optimized for vertical formats, ideal for social media, print, and digital storytelling. ## Prompt

```
Close-up profile portrait of {{subject-description}} against {{background}}, cinematic lighting with deep shadows and high contrast, sharp focus with ultra-detailed features, 8K photography, moody color grading --ar 2:3
```
