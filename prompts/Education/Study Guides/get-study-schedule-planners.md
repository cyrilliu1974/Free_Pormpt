# Study Schedule Planner Prompt for Students

## 簡介

The Study Schedule Planner Prompt for Students is a free AI prompt that creates personalized weekly study timetables optimized for academic performance and sustainable learning habits. This study schedule planner prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your course load, learning style, and productivity patterns to generate a structured markdown table covering Monday through Sunday with hourly time slots. It applies cognitive load distribution and spaced repetition principles to balance core study blocks, revision sessions, breaks, and extracurricular activities. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing visual schedules with emoji indicators that make it easy to follow your personalized plan. Students use it to map out exam preparation periods, balance multiple courses, incorporate effective break schedules, and align study sessions with their peak focus hours. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize a demanding course load, prepare for finals week, transition to a new semester, or build consistent study habits that prevent burnout. ● Produces a complete weekly markdown table with time slots, subject blocks, breaks, and revision periods clearly marked with visual indicators ● Applies spaced repetition principles to distribute subjects effectively and maximize long-term retention ● Balances intensive study sessions with strategic break periods to maintain focus and prevent cognitive overload ● Includes tailored optimization notes based on your learning style, peak productivity hours, and specific course requirements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic advisor specializing in personalized study schedule optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive weekly study timetable that maximizes learning efficiency and academic performance. Analyze course load, balance subject distribution across the week, allocate study breaks and revision periods, and align the schedule with the student's natural productivity patterns.

## Context
**Student Profile:**
{{student-profile}}

**Academic Requirements:**
{{course-details}}

Consider cognitive load distribution, spaced repetition principles, and recovery time between intensive study sessions. Account for extracurricular commitments and ensure the schedule is sustainable long-term.

## Output
Deliver the study schedule as a **markdown table** with:
- **Columns:** Days of the week (Monday through Sunday)
- **Rows:** Hourly time slots covering the student's active study hours
- **Symbols/indicators:** Use emojis or symbols to differentiate:
  - 📚 Core subject study blocks
  - 📖 Revision/review sessions
  - ☕ Break periods
  - 🎯 Extracurricular activities
  - 💤 Recommended rest periods

Include a legend below the table explaining all symbols. Add 2-3 brief optimization notes tailored to the student's learning style and goals.
```
