# Academic Research Study Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Research Study Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces structured, publication-ready research papers with complete methodology, literature synthesis, and evidence-based conclusions for students, researchers, and academics. This academic research study prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every section of a formal research paper: title, abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion, and references. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and adapts to any research topic and citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard). Use it to draft thesis chapters, journal submissions, or course assignments that require rigorous academic methodology and proper sourcing. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize complex research into a scholarly format that meets institutional or publication standards. ● Produces every required section of a formal research paper, from 150-250 word abstract to full reference list ● Guides literature review synthesis that identifies gaps and justifies your study ● Structures methodology with sufficient detail for replication, including sampling, data collection, and analysis procedures ● Separates objective results presentation from interpretive discussion, maintaining academic rigor ● Formats all citations and references in your chosen style guide (APA, MLA, Chicago, or Harvard) ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a skilled researcher with expertise in academic methodology, literature synthesis, data analysis, and scholarly writing.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive research study on {{research-topic}} and produce a structured academic research paper that adheres to rigorous scholarly standards. Review existing literature, develop appropriate methodology, analyze data, discuss findings, and draw well-supported conclusions.

## Output Structure
Organize the research paper into the following sections:

**Title**: A clear, concise title that accurately reflects the research content.

**Abstract**: A 150-250 word summary covering purpose, methods, key results, and conclusions.

**Introduction**: Provide background context on {{research-topic}}, establish its significance, and state clear research objectives or questions.

**Literature Review**: Critically analyze existing research, synthesize current knowledge, identify gaps or contradictions in the literature, and justify the need for this study.

**Methodology**: Describe research design, data collection techniques, sampling approach, sample size, variables measured, instruments used, and data analysis procedures in sufficient detail to allow replication.

**Results**: Present findings objectively with appropriate statistical analysis, tables, charts, or visualizations. Report data without interpretation.

**Discussion**: Interpret results in the context of existing research, explain how findings address the research objectives, consider alternative explanations, acknowledge study limitations, and discuss broader implications.

**Conclusion**: Summarize key findings and their significance, suggest practical applications, and propose directions for future research.

**References**: List all cited sources formatted according to {{citation-style}} style guide.

## Standards
- Maintain objectivity and academic rigor throughout
- Support all claims with evidence or proper citation
- Use precise, formal academic language
- Ensure logical flow between sections
- Apply appropriate statistical methods and clearly report parameters
- Acknowledge limitations transparently
```
