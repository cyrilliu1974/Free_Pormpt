# Email Newsletter Content Calendar Planner

## 簡介

The Email Newsletter Content Calendar Planner is a free AI prompt that creates structured email campaign schedules for marketers, content teams, and business owners. It outputs a markdown table mapping 8–12 newsletters across your chosen timeframe, each with a strategic send date, compelling subject line, and actionable content summary. This email newsletter prompt for ChatGPT works by combining your business description, audience profile, and campaign duration with seasonal trends, industry events, and promotional cycles. The result is a balanced mix of educational, promotional, seasonal, and engagement-driven content designed to maintain subscriber interest and support business objectives. Marketers use it to plan quarterly campaigns, product launch sequences, and year-round nurture programs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to map out a cohesive email strategy quickly, ensure consistent send schedules, or hand off a ready-to-execute calendar to your copywriting team. ● Produces a markdown table with send dates, subject lines, and content summaries for 8–12 newsletters ● Balances content variety - educational, promotional, seasonal, and engagement-focused - to sustain subscriber interest ● Considers seasonal trends, industry events, and product cycles to time campaigns strategically ● Delivers actionable content briefs that copywriters can execute without further research ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in email marketing campaigns.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email newsletter content calendar that drives engagement and supports business objectives. Deliver the calendar as a markdown table with three columns: **Send Date**, **Subject Line**, and **Newsletter Content**.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

{{campaign-timeframe}}

Consider seasonal trends, industry events, product launch cycles, and promotional opportunities when planning content. Ensure subject lines are compelling and content ideas are actionable enough for a copywriter to execute.

## Output
Structure your calendar as:

| Send Date | Subject Line | Newsletter Content |
|-----------|--------------|--------------------|
| [date] | [engaging subject] | [concise content summary] |

Include 8–12 newsletters spread evenly across the timeframe. Vary content types (educational, promotional, seasonal, engagement-focused) to maintain subscriber interest.
```
