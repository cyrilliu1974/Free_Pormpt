# Employee Reward System Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Reward System Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds custom motivation frameworks for HR leaders, organizational psychologists, and people operations teams. It produces an 8-phase implementation blueprint - from baseline behavior analysis and diverse reward category design to reinforcement schedules, peer nomination frameworks, equity safeguards, rollout timelines, measurement systems, and long-term sustainability plans - all adapted to your organization's size, budget, industry, and culture. This employee reward system prompt for ChatGPT runs on text models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and grounds every recommendation in B.F. Skinner's behavioral reinforcement principles to ensure rewards drive lasting behavior change rather than short-term compliance. Reach for this prompt when you need to align recognition practices with core values, fix motivation gaps, or scale reward programs across diverse teams and demographics. ● Analyzes current versus desired behaviors and maps each to specific reinforcement strategies and reward categories. ● Designs diverse reward types - recognition-based, experience-based, development-based, peer-driven, achievement-based - with frequency, cost, and demographic considerations. ● Builds fairness and equity protocols to prevent bias, ensure accessibility, and respect cultural preferences across age ranges and remote or in-office splits. ● Delivers a 30-day pilot program, manager training materials, tracking metrics, ROI calculation methods, and quarterly review protocols for continuous improvement. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist specializing in employee motivation and reward system design, combining behavioral psychology with practical HR implementation.

## Task

Design a comprehensive employee reward system that creates sustainable motivation through strategic positive reinforcement. Build the system phase-by-phase, adapting to the organization's unique context, budget, and culture.

## Context

{{org-context}}
*Include: company size, industry, budget range for rewards, team demographics (age ranges, remote/in-office split, departments), current motivation challenges, and core values with behavioral examples*

{{existing-systems}}
*Describe: current recognition practices (formal and informal), communication platforms available (Slack, Teams, etc.), speed of current recognition (immediate to weeks), and any counterproductive behaviors currently being rewarded*

## Output

Deliver an 8-phase employee reward system blueprint:

**Phase 1: Organizational Discovery & Baseline Assessment**
- Behavior-value alignment matrix
- Current vs. desired behavior gap analysis
- Reinforcement readiness assessment

**Phase 2: Reward Category Design**
Create diverse reward categories within budget:
- Recognition-based (zero/low cost)
- Experience-based (moderate cost)
- Development-based (investment in growth)
- Peer-driven (community building)
- Achievement-based (milestone markers)

For each category provide: specific reward examples aligned to stated values, implementation methods, recommended frequency, and demographic considerations.

**Phase 3: Reinforcement Schedule Architecture**
- Immediate recognition triggers and methods
- Interval-based reward timing
- Variable reinforcement strategies to sustain engagement
- Behavior-reward pairing guidelines

**Phase 4: Peer Nomination & Social Recognition Framework**
- Nomination criteria tied to organizational values
- Selection/voting processes
- Public celebration methods (digital and physical)
- Integration with existing communication platforms

**Phase 5: Fairness & Equity Protocols**
- Demographic-specific preferences and accessibility
- Bias prevention mechanisms
- Equitable distribution safeguards
- Cultural sensitivity guidelines

**Phase 6: Implementation Roadmap**
- 30-day pilot program design
- Manager training materials and communication templates
- Rollout timeline with early-win milestones
- Risk mitigation strategies

**Phase 7: Measurement & Feedback Systems**
- Behavior change tracking metrics
- Participation and satisfaction measurement
- ROI calculation methodology
- Quarterly review protocols and adjustment triggers

**Phase 8: Long-Term Sustainability Plan**
- Culture integration checklist
- Leadership reinforcement practices
- Annual refresh protocols
- Scaling strategies for organizational growth
- Innovation pipeline for evolving rewards

Format each phase with clear headers, actionable deliverables, and specific examples. Ensure all recommendations are practical, budget-conscious, and tailored to the provided organizational context.
```
