# Joint Problem-Solving Activities Lesson Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Joint Problem-Solving Activities Lesson Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured collaborative learning activities for educators teaching any subject at any grade level. This joint problem-solving activities prompt for ChatGPT produces a set of five group exercises designed to build teamwork, critical thinking, and collaborative problem-solving skills. Each activity is delivered in a markdown table with columns for Activity Name, Description, Learning Objectives, and Materials Needed. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating age-appropriate activities that accommodate kinesthetic, visual, auditory, and logical learning styles. Teachers use it to create lesson plans that move beyond individual work and foster peer collaboration, whether teaching elementary math, high school science, middle school social studies, or any other subject. Reach for this prompt when you need ready-to-implement group activities that are matched to your students' developmental stage and your curriculum goals. ● Produces five distinct activities per request, each with a full description, measurable learning objectives, and a complete materials list for teacher preparation. ● Adapts to any subject and grade level through two simple variables, ensuring cognitive and social age-appropriateness. ● Designs activities that engage multiple learning styles - kinesthetic, visual, auditory, and logical - so every student can participate meaningfully. ● Outputs results in a clean markdown table format that teachers can copy directly into lesson plans, curriculum documents, or sharing platforms. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational curriculum designer specializing in collaborative learning activities that develop joint problem-solving skills.

## Task
Create a set of 5 engaging group activities tailored to the specified subject and grade level. Each activity should encourage teamwork, critical thinking, and accommodate diverse learning styles.

## Context
Subject: {{subject}}
Grade level: {{grade-level}}

Design activities that are age-appropriate, clearly define what students will learn, and list all materials needed for easy teacher preparation.

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with these columns: Activity Name, Description, Learning Objectives, and Materials Needed.

Ensure each activity:
- Promotes collaboration and problem-solving
- Suits the cognitive and social development of the grade level
- Has clear, measurable learning objectives
- Includes a complete materials list
- Varies in approach to engage different learning styles (kinesthetic, visual, auditory, logical)
```
