# Content Promotion Strategy SEO Planner

## 簡介

The Content Promotion Strategy SEO Planner is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive content promotion roadmaps for marketers, content strategists, and SEO specialists looking to increase organic visibility and engagement. This content promotion prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your content type, target audience, primary keywords, SEO challenges, and existing channels to deliver a multi-layered strategy. It combines on-page optimization tactics (keyword placement, internal linking architecture, meta description crafting) with off-page distribution methods (social media amplification, content syndication, outreach campaigns). The output is organized using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and hierarchical clarity, making it immediately actionable. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete promotion blueprint tailored to specific content types and audiences, not generic SEO checklists. ● Provides on-page optimization guidance including strategic keyword placement, internal linking schemes, and meta description templates ● Maps off-page promotion tactics across social platforms, content distribution networks, and outreach opportunities ● Identifies specific solutions to your stated SEO challenges rather than offering one-size-fits-all advice ● Delivers measurable action items organized by strategic pillar, making implementation and ROI tracking straightforward ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in SEO performance and organic traffic growth.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content promotion strategy that increases visibility, engagement, and search engine rankings through keyword optimization, internal linking, meta descriptions, and social media promotion.

## Context
Content type: {{content-type}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Primary keywords: {{primary-keywords}}
Current SEO challenges: {{seo-challenges}}
Existing promotion channels: {{promotion-channels}}

## Requirements
- Structure your strategy using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and hierarchical clarity
- Address on-page SEO tactics (keyword placement, meta descriptions, internal linking architecture)
- Include off-page promotion methods (social media, content distribution, outreach)
- Propose specific solutions to the stated SEO challenges
- Leverage existing promotion channels while identifying new opportunities
- Provide measurable tactics that can be tracked for ROI

## Output
Deliver your strategy in a structured format:
- Use main headings for strategic pillars (e.g., On-Page Optimization, Content Distribution, Technical SEO)
- Include subheadings for tactical categories
- Present specific action items as bullet points under each section
- Ensure recommendations are tailored to the content type and audience
```
