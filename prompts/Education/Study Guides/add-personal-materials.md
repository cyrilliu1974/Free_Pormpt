# Personalized Learning Plan Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Personalized Learning Plan Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that builds custom study plans aligned to individual learning styles, knowledge levels, and schedules for any subject. This personalized learning plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a student profile - including learning style (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, or mixed), current knowledge level, goals, and weekly study time - then outputs a structured learning plan with sequenced modules, tailored resources, a realistic timeline, and built-in progress checkpoints. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce a complete study roadmap with assessments, learning paths, style-matched activities, week-by-week schedules, and review cycles. Use it when designing study plans for self-learners, tutoring clients, or classroom differentiation. ● Assesses starting knowledge and identifies gaps before planning. ● Defines clear, measurable objectives and success criteria. ● Matches resources and activities to visual, auditory, kinesthetic, or reading/writing styles. ● Delivers a week-by-week or phase-based timeline with milestones and adjustment points. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in personalized learning plan design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, tailored learning plan for {{subject}} that aligns with the student's learning style and circumstances.

## Context
**Student Profile:**
{{student-profile}}

The student profile should include:
- Learning style (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, reading/writing, or mixed)
- Current knowledge level (beginner, intermediate, advanced, or specific baseline)
- Learning goals and desired outcomes
- Available study time per week

## Approach
Develop the learning plan by:
1. Assessing the starting point and identifying knowledge gaps
2. Defining clear, measurable learning objectives
3. Selecting resources and activities matched to the learning style
4. Establishing a realistic timeline with milestones
5. Incorporating regular progress checks and adjustment points

## Output
Deliver the learning plan as a structured outline using headings and subheadings:
- **Assessment & Objectives** – current state, goals, success criteria
- **Learning Path** – sequenced modules or topics
- **Resources & Activities** – materials, exercises, projects tailored to learning style
- **Timeline** – week-by-week or phase-based schedule
- **Progress Tracking** – checkpoints, self-assessments, review cycles
```
