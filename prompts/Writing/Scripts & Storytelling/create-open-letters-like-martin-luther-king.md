# Open Letter Generator in MLK's Voice

## 簡介

The Open Letter Generator in MLK's Voice is a free AI prompt that produces persuasive, morally grounded public letters addressing social injustice for activists, educators, and civic leaders. This open letter prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write in the tradition of Martin Luther King Jr.'s civil rights advocacy - combining historical context, personal stories, moral reasoning, and a concrete call to action. You supply the social issue, and the prompt structures an eight-part letter that opens with "Dear Fellow Americans," builds urgency through historical examples, humanizes the problem with anecdotes, frames the issue as a moral imperative, acknowledges opposition, paints a vision of a just future, and closes with actionable steps. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it delivers accessible yet powerful prose that mirrors King's oratory without academic jargon. Use it when you need to rally public opinion, inspire collective action, or make a moral case for systemic change. ● Structures letters in eight clear sections - from salutation and urgency through historical context, personal stories, moral argument, opposition, vision, call to action, and closing. ● Emulates Martin Luther King Jr.'s accessible yet passionate rhetorical style, grounding every argument in justice, morality, and shared humanity. ● Produces output suitable for publication, social media, or public speaking without requiring editing for tone or structure. ● Accepts any social issue as input and adapts the moral reasoning and historical references accordingly. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a civil rights advocate writing in the tradition of Martin Luther King Jr.—employing his signature passionate tone, moral reasoning, and persuasive oratory to address injustice.

## Task
Draft an open letter to the public addressing {{social-issue}}. The letter must inspire action by appealing to shared values of justice, morality, and humanity.

## Structure
Organize the letter as follows:

**Opening salutation:** "Dear Fellow Americans,"

1. **Introductory paragraph** – Establish the urgency and importance of the issue
2. **Historical context** – Provide examples showing the deep-rooted nature of the problem
3. **Personal dimension** – Include anecdotes or stories that humanize the issue and evoke empathy
4. **Moral argument** – Explain why change is a moral and ethical imperative, grounding your case in shared values
5. **Acknowledge opposition** – Recognize challenges and resistance, but emphasize why action cannot wait
6. **Vision** – Paint a picture of the better future possible if the issue is addressed
7. **Call to action** – Urge specific, concrete steps the public can take
8. **Closing** – Reinforce the issue's importance and the power of collective action

**Closing:** "Yours in the struggle for justice, Martin Luther King Jr."

## Output Criteria
- Write in a powerful, accessible style that reflects King's oratory—persuasive yet understandable to a wide audience
- Avoid jargon or overly complex language
- Convey urgency and the necessity for immediate action
- Ground every argument in justice, morality, and our common humanity
```
