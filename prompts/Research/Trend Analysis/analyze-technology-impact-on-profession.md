# Technology Impact on Profession Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Technology Impact on Profession Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces research-backed workforce transformation reports for professionals, analysts, and HR strategists planning for technological change. This technology impact prompt for ChatGPT takes any emerging technology and profession pair, then delivers a cited analysis covering new job roles likely to emerge, skills that will become critical, and existing competencies at risk of obsolescence. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only two variables: the technology you're researching and the profession you're examining. The output follows a structured format with introduction, role forecasts, skill evolution mapping, obsolescence assessment, strategic conclusions, and full source citations. Use cases include workforce planning for HR departments evaluating upskilling needs, career advisors guiding clients through industry transitions, and consultants preparing technology adoption roadmaps. This prompt is built for technology analysts, workforce strategists, career counselors, and business leaders who need evidence-based insights into how automation, AI, or other innovations will reshape specific occupations. ● Identifies at least three new job roles with descriptions of responsibilities and emergence drivers ● Maps minimum three evolving skills with explanations of criticality and application context ● Flags at least three skills facing obsolescence with reasoning tied to automation or transformation ● Requires credible source citations for every claim, ensuring research-grade reliability ● Delivers accessible language with technical term definitions for non-specialist audiences ● Produces actionable strategic implications professionals can use for training and career development ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a technology analyst researching the impact of emerging technologies on professions and workforce development.

## Task

Analyze how {{emerging-technology}} will transform {{profession}}, identifying new roles that may emerge, skills that will become critical, and existing competencies at risk of obsolescence. Support all findings with credible sources.

## Output

Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Impact of {{emerging-technology}} on {{profession}}**

**Introduction:**
Brief overview of the technology and its potential impact on the profession (2-3 paragraphs)

**New Job Roles:**
- Role name: Brief description of responsibilities and why this role emerges
- Role name: Brief description
- Role name: Brief description
(Minimum 3 roles)

**Evolving Skill Requirements:**
- Skill: Explanation of why this skill becomes critical and how it's applied
- Skill: Explanation
- Skill: Explanation
(Minimum 3 skills)

**Potentially Obsolete Skills:**
- Skill: Explanation of why automation or transformation reduces demand
- Skill: Explanation
- Skill: Explanation
(Minimum 3 skills)

**Conclusion:**
Summarize key findings and strategic implications for professionals in this field

**Sources:**
1. [Full citation]
2. [Full citation]
3. [Full citation]
(Include all sources referenced)

## Constraints

- Focus on high-probability impacts supported by current research, not speculative predictions
- Use accessible language; define technical terms when necessary
- Every claim must cite a credible source (academic research, industry reports, expert analysis)
- Provide actionable insights professionals can use for career planning
```
