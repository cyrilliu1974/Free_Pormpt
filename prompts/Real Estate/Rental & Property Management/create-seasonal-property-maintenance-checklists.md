# Seasonal Property Maintenance Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Seasonal Property Maintenance Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized four-season maintenance plans for property managers and landlords looking to protect asset value and prevent costly repairs. This seasonal property maintenance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your property details and climate factors to produce checklists covering spring, summer, fall, and winter tasks. Each maintenance item includes priority classification (critical, routine, or optional), recommended timing, estimated cost ranges, responsibility assignment, and the specific failure or expense it prevents. Property managers use it to schedule HVAC servicing, coordinate roof inspections, plan landscaping work, and document preventative care across their portfolio. Reach for this prompt when you manage rental properties and need a systematic approach to year-round maintenance that balances tenant satisfaction, budget constraints, and long-term asset protection. ● Identifies critical tasks that prevent structural damage and cascading failures, routine tasks that maintain tenant satisfaction, and optional improvements that boost competitive positioning. ● Specifies timing triggers, cost estimates, and whether each task belongs to the landlord, tenant, or requires professional service. ● Includes inspection protocols, professional service scheduling recommendations, tenant communication touchpoints, and documentation requirements. ● Adapts to property type, size, location, climate, current maintenance approach, and known issues you provide. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert property management consultant specializing in preventative maintenance systems that protect property value, reduce emergency repair costs, and improve tenant retention.

## Task
Create a comprehensive four-season maintenance checklist tailored to the property details provided. For each season (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter), identify:

- **Critical tasks** that prevent cascading failures and protect structural integrity
- **Routine tasks** that preserve property condition and tenant satisfaction
- **Optional improvements** that enhance tenant retention and competitive positioning

Include inspection protocols, professional service scheduling recommendations, tenant communication touchpoints, and documentation requirements.

## Context
Property type, size, and location with climate factors: {{property-details}}

Current maintenance approach, budget constraints, and any known issues: {{maintenance-context}}

## Output
Organize the checklist by season. For each maintenance item, specify:

- **Task Description** – what needs to be done
- **Priority Level** – Critical / Routine / Optional
- **Recommended Timing** – specific month or trigger condition
- **Estimated Cost Range** – approximate investment required
- **Responsibility** – Landlord / Tenant / Professional service
- **Prevention Impact** – what failure or expense this task prevents

Use bullet points within each seasonal section for clarity and ease of implementation.
```
