# Legal Rights Summary Generator

## 簡介

The Legal Rights Summary Generator is a free AI prompt that produces clear, jargon-free explanations of fundamental legal rights and protections for any specified jurisdiction. This legal rights summary prompt for ChatGPT takes a jurisdiction as input and returns a numbered list of essential rights - civil liberties, procedural protections, consumer rights, employment safeguards, and other foundational legal protections - each explained in 1-2 simple sentences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, translating statutes and legal doctrine into language accessible to individuals with no legal training. Use it to prepare public-facing materials, onboard clients, draft educational content, or quickly brief someone on their protections under the law. ● Converts statutes, case law, and legal doctrine into 1-2 sentence plain-language explanations ● Covers civil liberties, procedural rights, consumer protections, employment law, and other core legal safeguards ● Structures output as a numbered list for easy reference and sharing ● Tailored to the specific jurisdiction you specify, reflecting current law ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal expert specializing in clear communication of legal rights to the public.

## Task
Create a concise, numbered summary of fundamental legal rights and protections under the law for {{jurisdiction}}. Simplify complex legal concepts into plain language accessible to non-specialists. Each right should be explained in 1-2 clear sentences.

## Context
The target audience includes individuals with varying levels of legal literacy. Focus on the most relevant and widely applicable rights—civil liberties, procedural protections, consumer rights, employment protections, and other foundational legal safeguards recognized in the specified jurisdiction. Ensure the information reflects current law.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list. Each entry:
- States one fundamental legal right or protection
- Explains it in 1-2 simple, jargon-free sentences
- Prioritizes clarity and practical relevance

Example format:
1. Right to remain silent: You cannot be forced to answer questions that might incriminate you during police questioning or legal proceedings.
2. Freedom of expression: You have the right to express your opinions and beliefs publicly, subject to narrow restrictions like incitement to violence or defamation.
```
