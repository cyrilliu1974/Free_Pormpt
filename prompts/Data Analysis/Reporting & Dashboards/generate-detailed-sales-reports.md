# Sales Report Generator for Revenue and Profit Analysis

## 簡介

The Sales Report Generator for Revenue and Profit Analysis is a free AI prompt that creates structured sales reports with performance metrics and actionable insights for business decision-makers. This sales report prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table tracking date-by-date revenue, profit margins, customer acquisition costs, and sales growth percentages across any specified timeframe. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing industry-specific dynamics and delivering commentary tailored to your target audience - whether executives, investors, or operations teams. The prompt includes a follow-up analysis section that interprets trends, flags significant changes, and surfaces decision-ready insights without requiring manual spreadsheet work. Reach for this prompt when you need repeatable, professional sales reporting formatted for presentations, stakeholder updates, or strategic planning sessions. ● Outputs a five-column markdown table (Date, Revenue, Profit Margin, Customer Acquisition Cost, Sales Growth %) ready to paste into docs or slides. ● Includes narrative analysis below the table that explains what the numbers mean and what actions to consider. ● Accepts custom company name, timeframe, industry context, and target audience variables so every report fits your situation. ● Designed for financial analysts, business owners, and anyone responsible for tracking sales performance or preparing executive briefings. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial analyst creating comprehensive sales reports that deliver actionable insights into company performance.

## Task
Generate a detailed sales report for {{company-name}} covering {{timeframe}}. Analyze key metrics including revenue, profit margins, customer acquisition costs, and sales growth. Focus on {{industry-context}} dynamics and any additional performance indicators relevant to the business. Tailor the analysis and commentary for {{target-audience}}.

## Output
Present the report as a markdown table with 5 columns:
- Date
- Revenue
- Profit Margin
- Customer Acquisition Cost
- Sales Growth %

Below the table, provide a concise analysis that interprets trends, highlights significant changes, and identifies actionable insights for decision-making.
```
