# Job Search Strategy Analysis Prompt for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Job Search Strategy Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches and compares the most effective job search strategies for any profession in a given year. This job search strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured table that evaluates five distinct approaches to finding employment, rating each on a 1-5 effectiveness scale and providing three actionable best practices per strategy. The prompt explicitly instructs the AI to blend traditional methods (networking events, recruiter outreach, newspaper listings) with modern digital tactics (LinkedIn optimization, ATS keyword targeting, portfolio sites) and to cite five reputable sources supporting its recommendations. Career counselors use it to audit their advice against current trends, job seekers run it to identify high-ROI activities for their field, and HR professionals deploy it to understand candidate behavior. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-backed guidance tailored to a specific profession and timeframe, rather than generic job-hunting checklists. ● Compares five job search strategies side by side with numerical effectiveness ratings for easy comparison. ● Delivers three concrete best practices per strategy so users know exactly what actions to take. ● Includes both traditional and digital approaches, ensuring a balanced view of the job market landscape. ● Requires five numbered citations from reputable sources, grounding recommendations in real research and industry data. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert job search strategist with deep knowledge of effective employment search techniques across industries and professions.

## Task
Research and analyze the top 5 job search strategies for {{profession}} in {{year}}. Compare traditional job hunting methods with modern digital approaches. Organize your findings into a structured table with effectiveness ratings and actionable best practices.

## Output
Provide a table with 5 rows and 3 columns:

| Strategy | Effectiveness Rating (1-5) | Best Practices |
|----------|---------------------------|----------------|
| Strategy name | Rating | • Practice 1<br>• Practice 2<br>• Practice 3 |

**Requirements:**
- Each strategy receives an effectiveness rating from 1 (least effective) to 5 (most effective)
- List exactly 3 best practices per strategy
- Include a mix of traditional and digital approaches where applicable
- Follow the table with 5 numbered citations from reputable sources that support your ratings and recommendations

**Citations:**
1. [First citation]
2. [Second citation]
3. [Third citation]
4. [Fourth citation]
5. [Fifth citation]
```
