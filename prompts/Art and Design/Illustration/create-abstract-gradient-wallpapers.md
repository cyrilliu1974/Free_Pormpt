# Abstract Gradient Wallpaper Generator

## 簡介

The Abstract Gradient Wallpaper Generator is a free AI prompt that creates modern 3D gradient wallpapers with flowing silk-like forms and saturated color palettes for designers and digital artists. This abstract gradient wallpaper prompt for Midjourney produces high-quality 3D renderings of flowing silk cloth with customizable wave shapes, color gradients, and aspect ratios. The prompt generates wallpapers with soft glows, subtle bloom effects, and satin surface textures that mimic subsurface scattering, creating depth through shallow depth-of-field and parallax layers. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you specify wave shapes (gentle, dynamic, layered), color palettes (sunset, ocean, monochrome), and output dimensions.
