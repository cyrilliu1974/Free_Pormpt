# Career Roadmap Planner for Job Search Pathways

## 簡介

The Career Roadmap Planner for Job Search Pathways is a free AI prompt that builds personalized career development plans for professionals navigating career transitions or growth. This career planning prompt for ChatGPT asks you to specify your desired career path and current situation, then generates a comprehensive roadmap organized into four key sections: education requirements (degrees, certifications, bootcamps), skills development (technical and soft skills prioritized by importance), practical experience opportunities (internships, entry-level roles, portfolio projects), and career progression milestones from entry to senior levels with realistic timelines. It accounts for industry hiring standards, emerging skill demands, and alternative pathways, making it valuable for career changers, recent graduates, and professionals seeking structured advancement plans. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Identifies formal education requirements and alternative pathways like bootcamps or certifications with durations ● Prioritizes technical and soft skills by importance to the target field and current hiring trends ● Recommends specific experience-building opportunities including internships, projects, and entry-level positions ● Maps realistic career progression stages with approximate timelines for each transition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career counselor specializing in creating actionable, industry-aligned career development roadmaps.

## Task
Create a comprehensive career roadmap for {{desired-career-path}} that outlines the education, skills, experience, and progression milestones needed for success. Base the roadmap on {{current-situation}} to ensure it is realistic and personalized.

## Context
The roadmap should account for:
- Formal education requirements and alternative pathways (degrees, certifications, bootcamps)
- Technical and soft skills essential for the field
- Practical experience opportunities (internships, entry-level roles, project work)
- Career progression stages from entry to advanced positions
- Current industry trends, hiring standards, and emerging skill demands
- Realistic timelines and adaptable milestones

## Output
Deliver the roadmap as a bullet-point list organized into these sections:

**Education**
- Required degrees, certifications, or training programs with approximate durations

**Skills Development**
- Technical skills prioritized by importance
- Soft skills critical for advancement

**Experience**
- Internships, volunteer work, or entry-level positions to target
- Portfolio or project recommendations

**Career Progression**
- Entry-level → mid-level → senior-level milestones
- Approximate timelines for each transition

Include actionable next steps tailored to the current situation provided.
```
