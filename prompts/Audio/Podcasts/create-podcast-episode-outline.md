# Podcast Episode Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Podcast Episode Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, broadcast-ready episode structures for podcasters, content creators, and audio producers. This podcast outline prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every section of a professional episode - opening hook, background segments, deep-dive analysis, guest interview questions, conclusion summaries, and calls to action. You provide the topic and any episode context (audience interests, guest credentials, key angles), and the prompt returns a logically sequenced outline with narrative hooks, transition cues, and engagement prompts that keep listeners tuned in. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to draft compelling episode structures in minutes whether you host solo shows, interview series, or narrative podcasts. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to plan a new episode, brief a co-host or producer, or ensure your content flows smoothly from intro to outro. ● Crafts attention-grabbing opening hooks and episode roadmaps that set tone and expectations ● Breaks the body into logical segments covering background, deep-dive analysis, expert insights, and discussion ● Generates 4-6 guest interview questions when a subject-matter expert is involved ● Concludes with key takeaway summaries, future implications, and clear calls to action for subscription and sharing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert podcast episode writer specializing in structuring compelling, informative audio content.

## Task
Create a detailed podcast episode outline that flows logically from introduction through conclusion, maintaining listener engagement with narrative hooks, thought-provoking questions, and clear segment transitions.

## Context
The episode will cover: {{topic}}

Additional context: {{episode-context}}
(Include: key points or angles to address, typical audience interests or questions about this topic, any guest expert involvement and their credentials)

## Output
Deliver a complete outline with these sections:

**Introduction**
- Opening hook: an intriguing question or statement that captures attention
- Brief episode roadmap
- Establish relevance and set tone for the audience

**Segment 1: Background and Overview**
- History or origin of the topic
- Current significance and context
- Relatable story or anecdote

**Segment 2: Deep Dive into Key Aspects**
- Multiple angles or perspectives
- Controversies or debates (if applicable)
- Reflective questions for listeners

**Segment 3: Expert Insights** (if guest is included)
- Guest introduction and credentials
- 4-6 insightful interview questions
- How their perspective deepens understanding

**Segment 4: Analysis and Discussion**
- Relevant data, studies, or research
- Comparison of viewpoints
- Hypothetical scenarios or engagement questions

**Conclusion**
- Key takeaways summary
- Implications or future outlook
- Listener participation prompt

**Call to Action**
- Subscription/sharing request
- Additional resources or next steps

Format the outline with clear headers, bullet points, and specific questions/discussion prompts that facilitate smooth delivery.
```
