# Extreme Snowboarding POV Image Generator

## 簡介

The Extreme Snowboarding POV Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates first-person action shots of snowboarding down massive mountains for designers, content creators, and winter sports enthusiasts. This extreme snowboarding prompt for Midjourney produces dynamic images that place the viewer on the board, capturing the slope ahead and snowy pine forests far below in a 16:9 cinematic frame with raw style processing. The prompt uses first-person POV composition to immerse viewers in the descent, ideal for action sports marketing, outdoor brand campaigns, video game concept art, and editorial content about winter adventure. It works on Midjourney and similar image models like Flux and Nano Banana that support camera angles and atmospheric depth. Reach for this prompt when you need high-energy winter sports visuals that convey speed, altitude, and the adrenaline of backcountry snowboarding without organizing a mountain shoot. ● First-person board-and-slope framing that puts the viewer in the rider's boots ● Massive mountain scale with pine forests rendered far below to emphasize altitude and speed ● 16:9 aspect ratio and raw style flag for cinematic, unfiltered image quality ● Flexible additional-details variable for weather conditions, time of day, terrain features, or visual effects ## Prompt

```
Extreme snowboarding down a massive mountain, snowy pine forests far below, dynamic first-person POV showing the board and slope ahead, {{additional-details}} --ar 16:9 --style raw
```
