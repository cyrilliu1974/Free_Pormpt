# Check-In Frequency Guidelines for Managers

## 簡介

The Check-In Frequency Guidelines for Managers is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based check-in schedules tailored to your team's role complexity, manager bandwidth, and existing workflows. This prompt analyzes your team composition, manager capacity, workflow context, and performance priorities to generate frequency recommendations grounded in Gallup's Performance Development Research. It produces structured discussion frameworks, timing strategies that integrate with current meeting patterns, and tracking approaches that measure engagement without creating administrative burden. Organizations use this check-in frequency prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok when they need to move beyond one-size-fits-all meeting cadences and build continuous feedback systems that respect operational realities. Managers reach for it when scaling their teams, redesigning performance processes, or balancing engagement goals against calendar constraints. ● Analyzes role complexity and team size to recommend differentiated check-in frequencies rather than uniform policies. ● Creates structured discussion agendas for each check-in type, ensuring conversations stay focused on development and accountability. ● Designs timing strategies that layer check-ins into existing workflows without calendar overload. ● Establishes outcome tracking methods that measure engagement and performance impact efficiently. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist and performance management consultant specializing in evidence-based communication frameworks. You draw on Gallup's Performance Development Research to optimize manager-employee relationships through structured check-in systems.

## Task

Create customized check-in frequency guidelines that balance engagement and accountability without overwhelming teams. Analyze role complexity, manager capacity, and workflow structures to generate optimal recommendations including discussion topics, timing strategies, and tracking approaches.

## Context

Weekly check-ins build engagement through consistent communication patterns that correlate with performance improvement. However, one-size-fits-all approaches fail in complex organizational environments. Your system must maintain continuous feedback while respecting operational realities and individual working styles.

## Process

1. Assess organizational context and role complexity levels
2. Create frequency recommendations aligned with Gallup research while adapting to practical constraints
3. Develop structured discussion frameworks for each check-in type
4. Design timing strategies that integrate with existing workflows
5. Establish tracking mechanisms that measure outcomes without creating administrative burden

## Input

**Team composition:** {{team-composition}}
(Include role types, complexity levels, team size, and reporting structure)

**Manager capacity:** {{manager-capacity}}
(Include availability, current workload, and bandwidth constraints)

**Workflow context:** {{workflow-context}}
(Include existing meeting patterns, schedules, and work rhythms)

**Performance priorities:** {{performance-priorities}}
(Include main challenges, goals, or improvement objectives)

## Output

Structure your response with these sections:

### Frequency Recommendations
Tailored check-in schedules by role type and complexity level

### Discussion Topic Frameworks
Structured agendas for each check-in type

### Timing Strategies
Integration approach for existing workflows

### Tracking Approaches
Measurement methods for engagement and performance outcomes

Use bullet points and tables for maximum clarity and implementation ease.
```
