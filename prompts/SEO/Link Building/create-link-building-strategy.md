# Link Building Strategy Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Link Building Strategy Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive backlink acquisition plans tailored to your industry and website. This link building prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section guide covering everything from identifying high-authority websites (Domain Authority 40+) to crafting personalized outreach emails and monitoring placement success. It walks you through finding authoritative sites using tools like Ahrefs and SEMrush, analyzing linking opportunities such as guest posts and resource pages, developing 1,500+ word content pieces aligned with target site themes, and tracking outreach campaigns with detailed spreadsheet structures. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to any industry context and website URL you provide. Use it when you need a structured, repeatable process for earning editorial backlinks that improve search rankings and domain authority. ● Identifies industry-specific authoritative websites and provides tracking spreadsheet structures for Domain Authority, contact information, and outreach status. ● Delivers a customizable email template with personalization guidance for contacting editors, webmasters, and content managers. ● Includes content creation recommendations aligned with target site audiences and natural keyword integration strategies. ● Offers follow-up schedules, reciprocal value suggestions, and monthly backlink monitoring practices to refine your link acquisition efforts. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in link building strategies across industries.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for building high-quality backlinks in the user's industry, focusing on effective outreach strategies to secure link placements on authoritative websites. Include specific examples and actionable templates for each step.

## Context

**Industry & Scope:**
{{industry-context}}

**Website:**
{{website-url}}

## Output

Deliver a structured guide with the following sections:

### Industry Overview
- Industry name and positioning
- 3-5 key industry topics most relevant for link building
- Target audience description

### Step 1: Identify Relevant, High-Authority Websites
- Recommend tools (Ahrefs, Moz, SEMrush) to find websites with Domain Authority 40+ in the industry
- Identify websites covering the key industry topics identified above
- Provide a tracking spreadsheet structure including: Website URL, Domain Authority, Contact Information, Notes

### Step 2: Analyze Linking Opportunities
- Describe how to evaluate each website for link placement potential
- Identify opportunities: guest posts, resource pages, relevant existing content
- Explain how to document specific target page URLs

### Step 3: Create Valuable Content
- Guide content creation aligned with target website themes and audiences
- Recommend well-researched, informative pieces of 1,500+ words
- Include natural keyword integration strategies

### Step 4: Craft Personalized Outreach Emails
- Explain how to identify the right contact person (editor, webmaster, content manager)
- Provide this customizable email template:

**Subject:** [Personalized subject referencing their content]

Hello [Name],

I recently discovered [Website Name] and was impressed by your piece on [Specific Content Topic].

As someone working in [Industry], I've created a comprehensive guide on [Content Topic] covering [Brief Overview]. I believe it would add value to your readers interested in [Relevant Topic].

Would you be open to featuring this guide or linking to it from your related content? I'm happy to share it for your review.

Thank you for considering this.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

### Step 5: Follow Up and Monitor Progress
- Send follow-up emails after 7-10 days to non-responders
- Track: initial contact date, follow-up dates, responses, link placement status (successful/declined/pending)
- Regularly identify new opportunities and refine strategy

### Additional Success Tips
- Prioritize relationship-building over transactional requests
- Offer reciprocal value: social shares, relevant links back to their content
- Diversify content formats: infographics, videos, interactive tools
- Monitor backlink profile monthly using Ahrefs or Majestic to identify and disavow low-quality links

## Quality Criteria
- Focus on Domain Authority 40+ websites
- Ensure content alignment with target website themes
- Personalize every outreach message with specific references
- Build genuine industry relationships, not just links
```
