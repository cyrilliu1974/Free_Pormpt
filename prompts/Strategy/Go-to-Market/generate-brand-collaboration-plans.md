# Brand Collaboration Strategy Generator for Digital Businesses

## 簡介

The Brand Collaboration Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a complete partnership roadmap for digital businesses seeking to expand through strategic brand alliances. This brand collaboration prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business details and produces a multi-section strategy document covering niche positioning, target audience, collaboration goals, three vetted partner brands with synergy explanations, outreach methods, projected exposure and revenue outcomes, measurable KPIs, and a contingency plan if partnerships decline. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting a structured text plan ready to guide your partnership development. Use it when launching co-marketing campaigns, affiliate programs, joint product launches, content co-creation, or bundled offerings with complementary brands. ● Identifies three compatible partner brands with clear synergy analysis and collaboration type recommendations ● Defines realistic, quantified outcomes for brand exposure, audience growth, and revenue potential ● Provides 4-6 specific, measurable KPIs tied directly to your collaboration goals ● Includes a concrete contingency plan with alternative partnership tiers, formats, and outreach pivots ## Prompt

```
## Role

You plan brand collaborations and partnerships for digital businesses. Given details about a business, you identify suitable partner brands, design an outreach strategy, project outcomes, and define how to measure success.

## Context

Digital business: {{business-details}}

## Task

Produce a full collaboration strategy for the business above. Follow the structure in the Output section exactly.

### Partnership criteria

- Partner brands must align with the business's niche and target audience.
- Every collaboration must deliver mutual benefit to both parties.
- Outreach must be professional, personalized, and specific to each partner.
- Projected outcomes must be realistic and measurable.
- KPIs must be specific and tied directly to collaboration goals.
- The contingency plan must offer concrete alternative paths, not vague fallbacks.

## Output

**Niche:** [Analyze from business details]
**Target Audience:** [Analyze from business details]
**Unique Selling Proposition:** [Analyze from business details]

### Collaboration Goals

[Define 2-3 specific goals based on the business context]

---

### Partner 1

**Brand Name:** [Identify complementary brand]
**Brand Synergy:** [Explain alignment with niche and audience]
**Collaboration Type:** [e.g., Co-marketing campaign, affiliate program, joint product launch]

### Partner 2

**Brand Name:** [Identify complementary brand]
**Brand Synergy:** [Explain alignment with niche and audience]
**Collaboration Type:** [e.g., Content co-creation, event partnership, cross-promotion]

### Partner 3

**Brand Name:** [Identify complementary brand]
**Brand Synergy:** [Explain alignment with niche and audience]
**Collaboration Type:** [e.g., Bundled offerings, shared technology integration, influencer collaboration]

---

### Outreach Strategy

**Initial Contact Method:** [LinkedIn message, email to partnerships team, warm introduction, or social media DM]
**Partnership Proposal Highlights:** [3-4 bullet points covering mutual benefits, audience overlap, specific collaboration mechanism, and success metrics]
**Follow-up Approach:** [Timeline and escalation strategy if no initial response]

---

### Expected Outcomes

**Brand Exposure:** [Quantify reach, impressions, or visibility gains]
**Audience Growth:** [Project new followers, subscribers, or community members]
**Revenue Potential:** [Estimate direct revenue, cost savings, or lifetime value impact]

---

### KPIs

[List 4-6 specific, measurable KPIs tied to collaboration goals—e.g., "500+ co-branded content impressions within 60 days", "15% conversion rate on partner referrals", "$10K in attributed revenue within quarter one"]

---

### Contingency Plan

[If partnerships decline or underperform, outline 2-3 concrete alternatives: pivot to different partner tier, shift collaboration format, adjust outreach channels, or modify value proposition]
```
