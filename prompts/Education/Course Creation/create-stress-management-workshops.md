# Stress Management Workshop Template Builder

## 簡介

The Stress Management Workshop Template Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, adaptable workshop plans for trainers and HR professionals looking to address workplace stress. This stress management workshop prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete training template that incorporates evidence-based stress reduction techniques, interactive activities, and practical takeaways. By providing your team profile - including size, industry context, and specific stressors - and workshop parameters like duration and scheduling constraints, the prompt generates a structured plan with pre-workshop surveys, detailed agendas with timed activities, post-session evaluations, and follow-up resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver workshop designs that encourage active participation and provide immediately actionable strategies employees can apply in both professional and personal contexts. This prompt is ideal for corporate trainers, HR managers, employee wellness coordinators, and facilitators who need to design effective stress management sessions quickly without starting from scratch. ● Produces a complete workshop plan with introduction, learning objectives, timed agenda, and evaluation method ● Includes a pre-workshop survey design to assess baseline stress levels and tailor content to participant needs ● Generates five distinct interactive activities with clear descriptions and duration guidelines ● Provides three actionable follow-up resources to support ongoing stress management practice beyond the session ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional trainer and facilitator specializing in employee well-being and stress management. Create a comprehensive, adaptable template for conducting an engaging stress management workshop.

## Task
Develop a complete workshop template that incorporates proven stress reduction techniques, interactive activities, and practical takeaways to help employees manage stress effectively in both professional and personal contexts.

## Context
**Team Profile:** {{team-profile}}
*(Include team size, specific stress factors or challenges the team faces, industry context, and any particular stressors unique to this group)*

**Workshop Parameters:** {{workshop-parameters}}
*(Specify preferred duration, any scheduling constraints, available resources, and whether this is a one-time session or part of a series)*

## Output
Provide a structured workshop template with the following sections:

### 1. Introduction
- Overview of the stress management workshop
- Importance of addressing stress in this workplace context

### 2. Workshop Details
- Title
- Duration
- Three core learning objectives

### 3. Pre-Workshop Survey
- Design and purpose of survey to assess participants' baseline stress levels and specific needs

### 4. Workshop Agenda
Present activities in table format:

| Activity Name | Duration | Description |
|---------------|----------|-------------|
| [5 distinct activities with timing and clear descriptions] |

### 5. Post-Workshop Evaluation
- Method to assess workshop effectiveness and participant learning

### 6. Follow-Up Resources
- Three actionable resources participants can use to continue their stress management practice

### 7. Conclusion
- Key takeaways summary
- Implementation encouragement

**Requirements:**
- Make the template adaptable to different team sizes
- Focus on interactive, engaging activities that encourage active participation
- Ensure all techniques are evidence-based and feasible within a workshop setting
- Provide practical takeaways participants can immediately implement
```
