# Behavioral Interview Questions Generator for Problem-Solving

## 簡介

The Behavioral Interview Questions Generator for Problem-Solving is a free AI prompt that creates targeted interview questions to assess candidates' analytical thinking, decision-making under pressure, and collaborative problem-solving skills for hiring managers and talent acquisition specialists. This interview questions prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces 10 behavioral questions that elicit specific past examples rather than hypothetical answers, each with clear assessment criteria including targeted problem-solving dimensions (analytical thinking, creative constraint-solving, learning from failure) and concrete listening points like red flags, green flags, and follow-up areas. Use it when screening candidates for roles requiring strong analytical reasoning, building structured interview guides, or training hiring teams to conduct evidence-based behavioral interviews. ● Covers five core dimensions: analytical thinking, creative solutions under pressure, decision-making with incomplete information, learning from failure, and collaborative problem-solving. ● Every question includes the specific problem-solving skill it targets and what to listen for in responses. ● Encourages detailed storytelling that reveals candidates' decision-making frameworks and approach to ambiguity. ● Accepts hiring context as a variable to tailor questions to your specific role, team dynamics, or industry challenges. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a talent acquisition specialist with expertise in behavioral interviewing and problem-solving assessment.

## Task
Generate 10 behavioral interview questions that reveal genuine problem-solving abilities, critical thinking, and analytical reasoning. Each question must elicit specific past examples rather than theoretical responses, covering:

- Analytical thinking and systematic problem breakdown
- Creative solutions under constraint or pressure
- Decision-making with incomplete information
- Learning from failure and adaptation
- Collaborative problem-solving

Design questions that encourage detailed storytelling revealing the candidate's methodology, decision-making framework, and approach to ambiguity.

## Context
{{hiring-context}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 10 questions. After each question, include:
- Which problem-solving dimension it targets
- What to listen for in the response (red flags, green flags, follow-up areas)
```
