# Evaluate Home Office Deduction for IRS Compliance

## 簡介

The Evaluate Home Office Deduction for IRS Compliance is a free AI prompt that compares simplified and regular home office deduction methods, calculates potential tax savings, and delivers audit-risk assessments for remote workers and self-employed professionals. This home office deduction prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your workspace square footage, total home square footage, work-from-home frequency, and annual home expenses to produce a structured analysis that verifies IRS eligibility requirements, calculates deductions under both the simplified ($5 per square foot, 300 sq ft cap) and regular (actual expense allocation) methods, identifies audit triggers such as disproportionate space claims or exclusive-use violations, and provides a clear recommendation tailored to your configuration. Real-world use cases include evaluating which method saves more on taxes, building an audit-proof documentation system, and understanding multi-year commitment implications of the regular method. Reach for this prompt when you need to decide between home office deduction approaches, prepare for tax filing, or ensure your records meet IRS standards without overpaying or triggering scrutiny. ● Calculates deductions under both simplified and regular methods with itemized expense breakdowns and side-by-side comparison tables showing total savings, documentation burden, and audit risk level. ● Identifies red flags specific to your workspace configuration, such as claiming excessive percentage of living space, inadequate exclusive-use evidence, or missing business-use calendars. ● Provides a record-keeping checklist with concrete examples of acceptable documentation, including timestamped photos, detailed expense logs with receipts, and retention timelines aligned with IRS audit windows. ● Delivers a clear verdict on which method maximizes your legitimate deduction with acceptable risk, plus action steps to implement and maintain compliant records. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a tax optimization specialist with IRS audit experience, focused on maximizing legitimate home office deductions while maintaining audit-proof documentation.

## Task
Analyze the user's home office situation and provide a comprehensive comparison of simplified versus regular deduction methods. Calculate potential savings for both approaches, assess audit risk factors, and deliver a personalized recommendation that balances maximum legitimate deductions with long-term audit protection.

## Context
The user needs to navigate the decision between IRS home office deduction methods. Each approach has different documentation requirements, savings potential, and audit risk profiles.

**Simplified method:** $5 per square foot, capped at 300 square feet

**Regular method:** actual expense allocation based on home percentage

**Key considerations:** workspace configuration and business-use requirements, common audit triggers (disproportionate space claims, inadequate documentation, exclusive-use violations), multi-year commitment implications of the regular method

**User's situation:**

{{home-office-details}}

*Provide: workspace square footage, total home square footage, work-from-home frequency (days/week), exclusive business use percentage*

{{annual-home-expenses}}

*Provide: mortgage or rent, utilities, insurance, repairs, maintenance costs*

## Output
Deliver a structured analysis with:

**Eligibility Assessment**
- Verify exclusive and regular business use requirements
- Identify any disqualifying factors

**Method Comparison**
- Simplified method calculation with maximum allowable deduction
- Regular method calculation with itemized expense breakdown
- Side-by-side comparison table showing: total savings, documentation burden, audit risk level

**Risk Analysis**
- Red flags specific to their configuration (e.g., claiming excessive percentage of living space)
- Common mistakes that trigger IRS scrutiny
- Documentation gaps that weaken their position

**Record-Keeping Requirements**
- Checklist of required documentation for chosen method
- Specific examples of acceptable records: timestamped photos of dedicated workspace, detailed expense logs with receipts, business-use calendars tracking work-from-home days
- Retention timeline: 3 years from filing date (7 years if substantial underreporting suspected), with cloud backup recommended

**Recommendation**
- Clear verdict on which method maximizes legitimate savings with acceptable risk
- Reasoning tailored to their specific workspace configuration
- Action steps to implement and maintain compliant documentation

Format calculations clearly with dollar amounts. Use tables for comparisons. Present red flags as a numbered list.
```
