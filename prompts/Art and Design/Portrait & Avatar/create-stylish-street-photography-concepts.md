# Stylish Street Photography Portrait Prompt

## 簡介

The Stylish Street Photography Portrait Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality street portraits with cinematic lighting and motion-blurred urban environments for photographers, designers, and content creators. This street photography prompt for Midjourney produces medium shots with an 85mm perspective, placing your subject in tack-sharp focus while crowds and cityscapes blur into dynamic motion around them. You specify the subject and pose (motorcycle rider with helmet in hand, fashion model leaning against a wall, athlete in urban gear) and the urban setting (Tokyo intersections, New York financial district, London side streets), and the prompt renders a moody, high-contrast image with shallow depth of field. It works on Midjourney and compatible image models like Flux and delivers raw, cinematic color grading suitable for editorial portfolios, social media campaigns, and commercial lookbooks. Reach for this prompt when you need professional street portrait concepts without a location shoot or when exploring visual directions for client pitches and mood boards. ● Produces eye-level medium shots with 85mm perspective and shallow depth of field that isolate the subject from busy surroundings. ● Applies cinematic lighting, strong contrast, and moody color grading for a polished editorial aesthetic. ● Lets you control subject details (pose, wardrobe, props like helmets or bags) and urban backdrops (skyscrapers, intersections, specific city vibes). ● Renders motion-blurred crowds and environments while keeping the subject razor-sharp, creating energy and context without distraction. ## Prompt

```
Stylish editorial street photograph of a {{subject-and-pose}} standing still with helmet in hand, wearing a distinctive jacket; confident gaze into camera. {{urban-setting}} modern cityscape with towering skyscrapers; blurred motion crowd rushing around the tack-sharp subject. Cinematic lighting with strong contrast and moody color grading. Medium shot from eye level, 85mm perspective, shallow depth of field. --ar 2:3 --raw --q 2 --s 250
```
