# Business Brochure Copywriting Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Brochure Copywriting Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional brochure content and structure for businesses seeking to attract and convert potential clients. This business brochure prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every section of an effective marketing brochure: cover page headlines, about sections with unique selling propositions, three detailed service descriptions with benefit-focused language, authentic customer testimonials, and clear calls to action. It applies consumer psychology principles and design best practices - visual hierarchy, white space recommendations, color palettes, and persuasive copywriting - to produce brochure content that builds trust and drives engagement. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only your business context to tailor the output. Use it when you need a complete brochure framework that balances design guidance with conversion-focused copy, whether for print or digital distribution. ● Outputs a complete brochure outline from cover page to contact information, including color scheme and image recommendations ● Generates three service descriptions with client-focused benefits and outcome statements rather than feature lists ● Creates authentic-sounding customer testimonials that highlight specific results and positive experiences ● Applies benefit-focused, jargon-free language throughout to engage potential clients and differentiate from competitors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brochure designer with comprehensive knowledge in marketing, graphic design, and consumer psychology.

## Task
Create a detailed, visually appealing brochure structure and content that attracts and engages potential clients through persuasive copy, clear benefit statements, and credibility-building elements.

## Context
The brochure must be tailored to:
{{business-context}}

Apply brochure design best practices: effective white space, readable typography, clear visual hierarchy, benefit-focused language, and a consistent professional aesthetic.

## Output
Deliver a complete brochure outline in this format:

**Brochure Title:** [compelling title]

**Color Scheme:** [recommended palette]

**Cover Page Design:**
- Headline: [attention-grabbing headline emphasizing main benefit]
- Subheadline: [supporting headline]
- Main Image Description: [visual concept that represents the business/offerings]

**About Section:**
- Business Overview: [brief history, mission, and positioning]
- Unique Selling Proposition: [what differentiates this business from competitors]

**Services Section:**
For each of three key services, provide:
- Name: [service name]
- Description: [what it is and how it works]
- Key Benefits: [specific client outcomes and problem solutions]

**Testimonials Section:**
Three customer testimonials, each with:
- Customer Name: [name or role]
- Quote: [authentic statement highlighting positive experience and results]

**Call to Action:** [clear, compelling next step]

**Contact Information:** [phone, email, website, social media handles]

Ensure persuasive, jargon-free language throughout. Focus on benefits over features. Build trust through specificity and authenticity.
```
