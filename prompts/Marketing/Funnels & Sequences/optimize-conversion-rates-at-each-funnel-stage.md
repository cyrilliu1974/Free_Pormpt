# Conversion Rate Optimization by Funnel Stage

## 簡介

The Conversion Rate Optimization by Funnel Stage prompt is a free AI prompt that systematically audits your sales funnel and produces stage-specific strategies to increase conversions for marketers, growth teams, and funnel strategists. This conversion rate optimization prompt for ChatGPT takes your product description and funnel structure, then generates a detailed markdown table breaking down each funnel stage with tailored strategies grounded in customer psychology, industry benchmarks, and data-driven insights. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, identifying friction points at every step - from awareness through purchase - and recommending concrete implementation tactics alongside KPIs to track progress. Use it when launching a new funnel, diagnosing drop-off points, or planning quarterly optimization sprints. ● Produces a multi-column markdown table with one column per funnel stage, showing strategy, implementation tactics, and KPIs side by side. ● Applies customer psychology principles and decision-making patterns to surface why prospects drop off and how to reduce friction. ● Identifies bottlenecks and offers industry best practices tailored to each funnel position, from top-of-funnel awareness to final conversion. ● Defines measurable KPIs for every stage so you can A/B test changes and track ROI over time. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales funnel strategist specializing in conversion rate optimization.

## Task
Analyze the provided sales funnel and develop targeted strategies to improve conversion rates at each stage. Address potential bottlenecks, apply customer psychology principles, leverage data-driven insights, and implement industry best practices to maximize conversions throughout the entire funnel.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-service}}

Sales funnel structure: {{funnel-description}}

## Process
1. Identify the key stages and current conversion challenges in the funnel
2. For each stage, develop specific strategies considering:
   - Customer psychology and decision-making patterns
   - Marketing techniques appropriate to that funnel position
   - Industry benchmarks and best practices
   - Potential friction points and bottlenecks
3. Recommend implementation tactics for each strategy
4. Define measurable KPIs to track effectiveness

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with one column per funnel stage. Include these rows:
- **Strategy**: Core approach to improve conversions at this stage
- **Implementation**: Specific tactics and actions to execute the strategy
- **KPIs**: Key metrics to measure success and identify optimization opportunities
```
