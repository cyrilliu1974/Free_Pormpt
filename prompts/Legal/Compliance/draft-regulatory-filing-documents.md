# Regulatory Filing Process Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Regulatory Filing Process Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that produces customized, actionable workflows for compliance officers preparing regulatory submissions. This regulatory filing prompt for ChatGPT takes your industry context and experience level, then outputs a numbered guide covering regulatory research, information gathering, systematic data organization, content drafting, deadline management, submission procedures, accuracy checks, common pitfalls, and process optimization strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured guidance tailored to your specific regulatory environment - whether you're filing with the SEC, FDA, EPA, FCC, or industry-specific bodies. Use it when onboarding new compliance staff, standardizing filing procedures across teams, preparing for audits, or tackling unfamiliar regulatory frameworks. ● Outputs a complete numbered workflow from initial regulatory research through final submission and record-keeping. ● Tailors guidance to your specific regulatory context and adjusts complexity based on your team's experience level. ● Identifies common compliance pitfalls such as missed deadlines, incomplete documentation, and misinterpretation of requirements. ● Includes both foundational steps for accuracy and advanced strategies for streamlining multi-jurisdiction or recurring filings. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert regulatory compliance officer specializing in preparing accurate, comprehensive regulatory filings.

## Task
Create a step-by-step guide for preparing regulatory filings that includes:
- Research and identification of applicable regulations
- Information gathering and systematic data organization
- Drafting clear, concise content
- Deadline management and submission procedures
- Accuracy maintenance techniques
- Common pitfalls and how to avoid them
- Process streamlining strategies

## Context
Industry and regulatory environment: {{regulatory-context}}
Previous filing experience level: {{experience-level}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with clear headings for each main section of the regulatory filing process. Ensure the guide is actionable, specific to the regulatory context provided, and addresses both foundational steps and advanced optimization techniques.
```
