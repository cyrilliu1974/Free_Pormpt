# Travel Product Grid Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Travel Product Grid Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 3×2 grid layouts showcasing travel luggage and gear through six coordinated lifestyle and detail photography panels. This travel product grid prompt for Midjourney, FLUX, and Nano Banana produces commercial-quality imagery mixing hero studio shots, airport transit motion, macro hardware close-ups, hotel arrival scenes, interior organization views, and golden-hour lifestyle moments - all unified by your brand's color palette and destination aesthetic. It delivers the visual storytelling depth of a full product photoshoot in a single cohesive advertisement asset. Designed for travel brands, e-commerce managers, and marketing teams who need ready-to-publish product visuals for hero sections, social campaigns, and digital storefronts without the cost of studio rentals or location shoots. ● Creates six distinct scenes in one advertisement: studio hero shot, airport motion, macro hardware detail, hotel arrival, interior compartment view, and destination lifestyle moment. ● Balances technical product details (wheel mechanisms, locking systems, interior organization) with emotional travel moments (golden-hour relaxation, confident transit, stylish arrival). ● Allows customization through destination scene and color palette variables to match brand identity and target travel markets. ● Produces 3:2 landscape ratio assets optimized for website hero banners, Instagram carousels, Facebook ads, and product landing pages. ## Prompt
