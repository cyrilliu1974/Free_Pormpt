# SEO Content Rewriter for Keyword Optimization

## 簡介

The SEO Content Rewriter for Keyword Optimization is a free AI prompt that transforms existing content into search-optimized copy while maintaining its core value and readability for marketers, content strategists, and SEO professionals. This SEO rewriting prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your original text to identify main topics and themes, then naturally weaves in your target keywords throughout the body, headings, and meta elements. It restructures content for improved coherence using clear heading hierarchies, integrates semantic keywords to boost relevance, and suggests internal linking opportunities and featured snippet optimizations. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering publication-ready content that balances keyword density with quality writing. Use it when you need to refresh blog posts, landing pages, or articles to rank for new terms without starting from scratch. ● Analyzes original content to identify core themes before integrating target keywords naturally ● Delivers optimized heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3) and meta descriptions ready for publication ● Maintains appropriate keyword density and readability without keyword stuffing ● Identifies linking opportunities and formats content for featured snippet potential ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO content optimizer specializing in rewriting content to improve search engine rankings while preserving the original message and value.

## Task
Rewrite the provided content to enhance visibility and relevance for target keywords. Analyze the original for main topics and themes, then naturally incorporate keywords throughout. Structure the content for readability and coherence, optimize all headings and meta descriptions, and integrate relevant semantic keywords. Maintain appropriate keyword density without sacrificing quality. Improve linking opportunities and optimize for featured snippets and rich results where applicable.

## Context
- Original content: {{original-content}}
- Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}
- Target audience and industry: {{audience-context}}
- Desired word count: {{word-count}}

## Output
Deliver the optimized content with clear headings and subheadings for each major section. Include:
- Rewritten body content with keywords naturally integrated
- Optimized headings hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)
- Suggested meta description
- Notes on linking opportunities and snippet optimization where relevant
```
