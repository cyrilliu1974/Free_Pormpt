# Vintage Toy Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Toy Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic 1970s magazine print advertisements for toy brands and products. This vintage toy advertisement prompt for Midjourney recreates the warm amber and burnt orange color palettes, lens softness, chromatic aberration, and halftone dot textures that defined print advertising photography of the era. You specify your toy brand, the subject and scene, and aspect ratio, and the prompt generates images with period-accurate optical characteristics including on-camera flash glare, subtle film distortion, and paper grain texture. Toy designers, marketing teams, and collectors creating retro campaigns or nostalgic brand materials will find it particularly useful. ● Produces warm retro color palettes with amber and burnt orange tones characteristic of 1970s print advertising ● Applies authentic vintage optical effects including lens softness, chromatic aberration, motion blur, and on-camera flash glare ● Adds halftone dots and paper grain texture for realistic magazine print reproduction ● Lets you customize the toy brand name, scene composition, and output aspect ratio for different ad formats ## Prompt

```

```
