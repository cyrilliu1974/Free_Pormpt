# Compliance Risk Assessment Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Compliance Risk Assessment Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured risk management plans for compliance officers and business leaders navigating complex regulatory environments. This compliance risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - including industry sector, jurisdiction, operational scale, and business model - to deliver a detailed markdown table covering 10+ distinct risk categories. Each row maps a compliance domain (data protection, employment law, health and safety, financial reporting, licensing, environmental regulations, consumer protection, anti-corruption, and sector-specific requirements) to a likelihood rating (Low/Medium/High) and a concrete, actionable mitigation strategy. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to generate tailored risk plans without specialized compliance software. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured starting point for compliance audits, board presentations, regulatory filings, or internal risk workshops - especially in multi-jurisdictional or heavily regulated industries. ● Maps 10+ compliance risk categories - from data protection and employment law to anti-corruption and environmental standards - tailored to your industry and jurisdiction. ● Assigns a Low, Medium, or High likelihood rating to each risk based on operational scale, business model, and regulatory obligations. ● Proposes specific, proportionate mitigation strategies for every identified risk, so you can act immediately. ● Outputs a clean markdown table ready to paste into reports, presentations, or risk registers. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert compliance officer specializing in risk assessment and regulatory frameworks across industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive risk management plan that identifies, evaluates, and mitigates compliance risks for the specified business. Analyze the regulatory landscape, identify risk categories specific to the industry and jurisdiction, assess the likelihood of each risk materializing, and propose concrete mitigation strategies.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Consider the unique compliance obligations tied to the industry sector, geographic jurisdiction, operational scale, and business model. Cover risks across data protection, employment law, health and safety, financial reporting, licensing, environmental regulations, consumer protection, anti-corruption, and sector-specific requirements.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: **Risk Category**, **Likelihood** (Low/Medium/High), and **Mitigation Strategy**.

Include at least 10 distinct risk categories that reflect the full spectrum of compliance exposure for this business. Each mitigation strategy should be specific, actionable, and proportionate to the assessed likelihood.
```
