# TikTok Video Script Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The TikTok Video Script Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that writes scroll-stopping video scripts for brands marketing products or services on TikTok. This TikTok script prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating 25-second video frameworks built around the platform's fast-paced format. It structures each script into four sections: a 3-second hook to stop the scroll, a brief product introduction, a benefits section that addresses audience pain points, and a clear call-to-action. The prompt also suggests trending sounds, effects, and relevant hashtags to maximize organic reach. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your unique selling proposition and brand voice through customizable variables. Real-world applications include e-commerce product launches, service promotion, creator brand partnerships, and direct-response campaigns where concise, high-impact messaging is essential. Use this prompt when you need platform-native TikTok content that balances entertainment with conversion goals, whether you're a marketer, founder, or content creator building a TikTok presence. ● Produces time-stamped script sections aligned to TikTok's 25-second sweet spot for promotional content ● Incorporates product USP and brand voice variables so every script reflects your positioning ● Suggests trending platform elements like sounds, effects, and hashtags to boost discoverability ● Balances entertainment value with clear calls-to-action that drive clicks, follows, or purchases ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert TikTok content creator specializing in short-form video scripts that drive engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write a compelling TikTok video script that promotes {{product-or-service}} to {{target-audience}}. The script must be optimized for TikTok's fast-paced format and designed to stop the scroll, deliver value, and drive action.

## Context
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Brand voice and tone: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Structure the script with these sections:

**Hook** (0-3 seconds)  
An attention-grabbing opening that immediately stops viewers from scrolling.

**Introduction** (3-8 seconds)  
Present the product/service and its key features concisely.

**Benefits & Pain Points** (8-20 seconds)  
Highlight how it solves specific problems for the target audience. Use short, impactful sentences.

**Call-to-Action** (20-25 seconds)  
End with a clear, actionable message that encourages engagement, clicks, or conversions.

Incorporate platform-native elements: suggest trending sounds, effects, or challenge formats where relevant. Include 3-5 trending hashtags that align with the content and audience.
```
