# Document Retention Policy Generator

## 簡介

The Document Retention Policy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, compliance-ready retention policies for organizations managing records across regulatory environments. This document retention policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a formal policy document with executive summary, scope definitions, document classification matrices, retention schedule tables with triggering events and regulatory citations, access control frameworks, secure disposal procedures with audit trails, governance structures assigning clear roles, phased implementation timelines, and ongoing compliance monitoring protocols. Organizations use it when establishing or updating retention programs to meet ISO 15489 standards, legal discovery obligations, and industry-specific regulatory requirements while controlling storage costs and mitigating over-retention risks. Records managers, compliance officers, legal teams, and information governance professionals reach for this prompt when building defensible retention frameworks that balance legal obligations with operational efficiency. ● Builds a document classification matrix organizing all record types by business purpose, sensitivity level, and retention trigger events. ● Generates retention schedule tables specifying exact retention periods with regulatory citations and legal basis for each document category. ● Defines secure disposal procedures including destruction methods, certificate requirements, and audit trail retention for defensibility. ● Establishes governance structures with assigned roles for Records Manager, Department Owners, and Compliance Officer, plus implementation timelines and monitoring cycles. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a records management and compliance specialist with expertise in ISO 15489 standards, legal discovery requirements, and defensible information lifecycle management.

## Task
Create a comprehensive document retention policy that balances legal compliance, operational efficiency, and cost-effective storage. The policy must provide a complete framework for classifying, retaining, accessing, and disposing of organizational records with appropriate governance and audit controls.

## Context
Organization profile: {{organization-profile}}

Address applicable regulatory obligations, legal discovery standards, document taxonomy covering all business record types with specific retention periods, access classifications, storage locations, secure disposal procedures with audit trails, and governance structure with clear roles and compliance monitoring.

## Output
Deliver a professional policy document with these sections:

**Executive Summary** – Purpose, scope, and business rationale

**Scope** – Organizational units, document types, and systems covered

**Document Classification Matrix** – Taxonomy of record types with business purpose and sensitivity levels

**Retention Schedule Table** – Document categories with retention periods, triggering events, and regulatory basis

**Access Controls** – Authorization levels, access procedures, and security requirements by classification

**Disposal Procedures** – Methods for secure destruction, certificates of disposal, and retention of destruction logs

**Governance Structure** – Roles (Records Manager, Department Owners, Compliance Officer), responsibilities, and escalation paths

**Implementation Timeline** – Phased rollout plan, training requirements, and system configuration milestones

**Compliance Monitoring** – Audit procedures, reporting cycles, and policy review schedule

Format tables for schedules and matrices where appropriate. Use clear, enforceable language suitable for formal adoption.
```
