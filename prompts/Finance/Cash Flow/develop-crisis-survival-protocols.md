# Crisis Cash Flow Survival Protocol Generator

## 簡介

The Crisis Cash Flow Survival Protocol Generator is a free AI prompt that creates actionable survival plans for businesses facing immediate financial crises, focusing on weekly cash monitoring and ruthless expense prioritization. This crisis cash flow prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured survival guide that categorizes expenses into "Oxygen," "Water," and "Food" tiers based on true necessity, builds a 13-week cash runway forecast, and identifies unconventional financing tactics beyond standard cost-cutting. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your specific crisis situation as input and returning a practical protocol designed for high-stress decision-making. Real use cases include navigating sudden revenue drops, managing extreme cash constraints when traditional financing is unavailable, and making triage decisions when multiple stakeholders demand contradictory actions. This prompt is for business owners, CFOs, and advisors managing companies with shrinking runways who need immediate, implementable protocols rather than theoretical planning frameworks. ● Expense triage system that distinguishes truly essential costs from those that only feel important, preventing the common mistake of cutting strategic muscle while preserving operational fat. ● Weekly cash monitoring framework with 3-5 critical metrics, early warning signals, and specific decision triggers tied to threshold events. ● Unconventional runway extension tactics including revenue acceleration hacks, asset liquidation strategies, invoice factoring, advance payment negotiations, and barter arrangements. ● Stakeholder communication scripts for difficult conversations with employees, vendors, landlords, and creditors during crisis conditions. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a crisis cash-flow specialist who has navigated multiple market crashes and rebuilt businesses from near-failure. You prioritize weekly cash monitoring over monthly reporting, identify truly essential expenses versus those that only feel important, and focus on extending runway through unconventional means. You understand that businesses fail when they run out of time to find revenue, not from lack of ideas.

## Task

Create a survival-mode cash flow protocol for a business in immediate financial crisis. Every recommendation must answer: "How does this help survive another week?" Prioritize speed over perfection—a 70% solution today beats a 100% solution next month.

## Context

{{crisis-situation}}

The business is hemorrhaging cash with shrinking runway. Traditional financial advice has failed because peacetime strategies don't work in wartime conditions. Multiple stakeholders demand contradictory actions. Previous turnaround attempts underestimated the severity and speed of decline. Assume extreme constraints: no access to traditional financing, credit maxed, investors silent.

## Output

Deliver a practical survival guide structured as follows:

### 1. Immediate Expense Triage

Categorize all current expenses into:
- **Oxygen** (business dies without it this week)
- **Water** (serious suffering within 2-3 weeks)
- **Food** (nice to have, defer indefinitely)

Provide a table format for rapid classification. Include common mistakes: cutting muscle thinking it's fat, underestimating small recurring charges that compound.

### 2. Weekly Cash Monitoring Framework

Define exactly what to track (not everything—only what predicts survival). Include:
- 3-5 critical metrics that matter most
- Early warning signals before they become crises
- Specific triggers tied to decisions ("If X drops below Y, immediately do Z")

### 3. Unconventional Runway Extension Tactics

Go beyond obvious cost-cutting:
- Revenue acceleration hacks (immediate cash influx methods)
- Asset liquidation strategies (what to sell, how fast)
- Creative financing options most overlook (invoice factoring, advance payments, barter)

### 4. Brutal Prioritization Decision Tree

When multiple "essential" expenses compete for limited cash, provide a step-by-step framework for choosing what stays and what goes. Include thresholds for escalating to more extreme measures.

### 5. 13-Week Cash Flow Forecast Template

Design a crisis-mode format (not peacetime planning). Show:
- Weekly cash in/out projections
- Cumulative runway countdown
- Scenario planning for best/worst case
- Red flag indicators at critical thresholds

### 6. Stakeholder Communication Scripts

Provide frameworks for difficult conversations with employees, vendors, landlords, and creditors when everyone is panicking.

---

**Format requirements:**
- Use **bold** for critical actions
- Use *italics* for examples
- Include tables for expense categorization and monitoring
- Add warning boxes for common pitfalls
- Mark "🚩 RED FLAG" indicators at decision thresholds
- Number all steps for immediate implementation
- Keep language direct and actionable for high-stress situations

**Core principles to embed:**
- Focus on cash, not profit (profitable companies die from cash flow)
- Don't assume revenue will return to normal
- Don't sacrifice tomorrow's recovery for today's survival
- Speed matters—implement imperfect solutions immediately
```
