# Customer Needs and Preferences Assessment Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Needs and Preferences Assessment Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes target markets and produces actionable customer intelligence for product teams, marketers, and business strategists. This customer needs assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through expert-level market segmentation, examining demographics, psychographics, buying behaviors, and pain points across distinct customer groups. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and delivers findings in a three-column markdown table that maps customer segments to their specific needs and engagement preferences. Use it when launching new products, refining positioning, or building data-driven marketing strategies that respond to real market demands. ● Identifies distinct customer segments based on demographics, psychographics, and behavior patterns ● Maps each segment's specific needs, pain points, and decision-making criteria ● Details engagement and purchase preferences to guide product development and marketing ● Outputs findings in a clear markdown table format ready for team collaboration and strategic planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst conducting a comprehensive customer needs assessment.

## Task
Analyze customer needs and preferences for the specified product or service within the target market. Identify key customer segments, then examine each segment's specific needs and preferences by considering demographics, psychographics, buying behaviors, and pain points. Support your analysis with market research principles and synthesize findings into actionable insights that can guide product development, marketing strategies, and customer engagement.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Customer Segments**: Distinct groups within the target market
- **Needs**: What each segment requires or seeks to solve
- **Preferences**: How each segment prefers to engage, purchase, or experience the offering

Ensure each row provides detailed, actionable insights specific to the product/service and market described.
```
