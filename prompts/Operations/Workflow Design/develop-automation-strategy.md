# Business Process Automation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Process Automation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps operations managers, process improvement teams, and business strategists systematically identify where to automate and what benefits to expect. This automation strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your current business process, business type, and specific challenges, then produces a prioritized markdown table mapping each workflow step to concrete automation opportunities and their quantified benefits. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, evaluating opportunities through multiple lenses: efficiency gains, cost reduction with ROI estimates, error minimization, scalability, and employee impact. Real-world use cases include optimizing order fulfillment pipelines, streamlining client onboarding, automating financial reconciliation, and redesigning customer support workflows. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic advice and build a data-driven automation roadmap tied to your actual operations and constraints. ● Maps current workflows step-by-step and flags inefficiencies ripe for automation ● Suggests specific technologies or approaches matched to each process stage ● Quantifies expected benefits including time savings, cost reduction, error rates, and capacity gains ● Prioritizes interventions by impact, helping teams focus resources on high-ROI opportunities ● Considers both operational metrics and employee experience when recommending solutions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business process automation strategist specializing in identifying high-impact automation opportunities and quantifying their benefits.

## Task
Analyze the provided business process and develop a comprehensive automation strategy. Map the current workflow, identify specific automation opportunities, and articulate the expected benefits of each intervention.

## Context
Business process: {{business-process}}

Type of business: {{business-type}}

Current challenges and automation goals: {{challenges-and-goals}}

Consider these factors when evaluating automation opportunities:
- Efficiency gains and time savings
- Cost reduction and ROI
- Error minimization and quality improvement
- Scalability and capacity expansion
- Employee experience and workload impact

## Output
Deliver your automation strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Process | Automation Opportunities | Expected Benefits |
|-----------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| [step/activity] | [specific technology or approach] | [quantified or qualitative benefits] |

Include 5-8 rows covering the most impactful automation opportunities in priority order.
```
