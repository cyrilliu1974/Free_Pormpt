# Email Marketing Content Writer

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Content Writer is a free AI prompt that creates complete promotional emails designed to drive engagement and conversions for marketers and business owners. This email marketing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write structured email campaigns from subject line through call-to-action, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure every sentence flows logically and clearly. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing emails that match your brand voice while highlighting key selling points and incorporating emotional appeals that resonate with your target audience. Use it when launching products, running promotions, nurturing leads, or re-engaging subscribers - the prompt analyzes audience motivations before drafting copy optimized for open rates and click-throughs. ● Produces four distinct sections: attention-grabbing subject lines, engaging opening hooks, benefit-driven body paragraphs, and action-oriented CTAs ● Applies dependency grammar framework to create logical sentence structure that improves comprehension and reader engagement ● Accepts detailed inputs for product/service, target audience, brand voice, selling points, and desired call-to-action ● Structures content to address audience pain points first, then bridges to your solution with persuasive language that fits your brand personality ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist creating high-conversion email copy.

## Task
Write a complete promotional email that drives engagement and conversions for the specified offering.

## Context
Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—ensure each sentence element connects logically to create clarity and flow. Analyze the audience's motivations before writing.

**Offering:** {{product-or-service}}

**Audience & positioning:** {{target-audience-and-brand-voice}}
(Describe who you're writing to and the tone/personality to adopt—e.g., "B2B SaaS decision-makers, professional yet approachable" or "health-conscious millennials, friendly and energetic")

**Key selling points:** {{key-selling-points}}

**Call-to-action:** {{cta}}

## Output
Deliver the email in four clearly labeled sections:

**Subject Line:**
[Craft a compelling subject line optimized for open rates]

**Opening Paragraph:**
[Hook the reader immediately with relevance to their needs or pain points]

**Body Content:**
[2-3 concise paragraphs highlighting benefits and features, using persuasive language and emotional appeals that align with the brand voice]

**Call-to-Action:**
[Clear, action-oriented closing with the specified CTA prominently featured]
```
