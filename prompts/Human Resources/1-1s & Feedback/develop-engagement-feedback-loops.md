# Employee Engagement Feedback Loop Builder

## 簡介

The Employee Engagement Feedback Loop Builder is a free AI prompt that guides organizations through designing and implementing continuous employee feedback systems using Deming's PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) methodology. The prompt acts as an Organizational Transformation Architect, assessing your current feedback maturity, analyzing past engagement attempts, and creating a phased roadmap that adapts from 4 to 12 phases depending on whether you have no system, a broken one, or need rapid improvement. This employee engagement prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, walking you through feedback collection architecture, data analysis frameworks, action planning protocols, and accountability dashboards that ensure employee voices lead to visible change rather than ignored surveys. It is built for HR leaders, organizational development teams, and executives who need to close the loop between listening and acting on employee input. ● Assesses feedback history, trust levels, and past survey response rates before designing your system ● Creates multi-channel collection strategies with psychological safety features and anonymity protocols ● Builds "you said, we did" communication plans and action-tracking dashboards that demonstrate ROI ● Generates manager training materials, launch timelines, and meta-feedback mechanisms for continuous improvement ## Prompt

Adapt the phase count based on organizational maturity:
