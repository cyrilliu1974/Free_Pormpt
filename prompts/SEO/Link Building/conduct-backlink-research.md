# Backlink Research and Link Building Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Backlink Research and Link Building Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes backlink landscapes and delivers prioritized acquisition plans for SEO professionals and website owners. This backlink research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your current backlink profile, industry niche, and target goals to identify 10-15 high-authority websites ranked by domain authority, topical relevance, and acquisition feasibility. It outputs a structured markdown table with specific outreach strategies and concrete actions for each target, plus an implementation timeline and measurement framework tracking referral traffic, domain authority growth, and ranking improvements. Use it when you need to systematically expand your backlink portfolio, evaluate competitive positioning, or build a data-driven link acquisition roadmap rather than guessing at outreach targets. ● Evaluates domain authority, relevance scores, and traffic metrics to rank link opportunities by impact and feasibility ● Creates customized outreach strategies for each target website type, from guest posting to resource page inclusion ● Delivers actionable tables with specific next steps rather than generic advice ● Includes implementation timelines and KPI frameworks to measure referral traffic and ranking improvements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist conducting comprehensive backlink research to identify high-quality link building opportunities and create an actionable acquisition plan.

## Task
Analyze the backlink landscape for {{website-or-business}} and deliver a prioritized link building action plan with specific outreach strategies.

## Context
Industry/niche: {{industry-or-niche}}
Current backlink count: {{current-backlinks}}
Target new backlinks: {{target-backlinks}}
Preferred methods: {{preferred-methods}}

## Process
1. Analyze the current backlink profile and competitive positioning
2. Identify authoritative, relevant websites in the industry using domain authority, topical relevance, and traffic metrics
3. Evaluate link building opportunities and rank them by feasibility and impact
4. Develop customized outreach strategies tailored to each target website type
5. Define specific actions required to acquire each backlink
6. Propose a realistic implementation timeline
7. Recommend tracking methods and success metrics (referral traffic, domain authority growth, ranking improvements)

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table with these columns:

| Target Website | Domain Authority | Relevance Score | Outreach Strategy | Specific Actions |

Include 10-15 prioritized targets. After the table, provide a brief implementation timeline and measurement framework.
```
