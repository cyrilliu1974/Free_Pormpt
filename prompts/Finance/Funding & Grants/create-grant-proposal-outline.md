# Grant Proposal Outline Generator for Funding Applications

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Outline Generator for Funding Applications is a free AI prompt that builds persuasive, well-structured grant proposal frameworks for nonprofits, researchers, and organizations seeking funding. This grant proposal outline prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your project goal, problem description, and key details and produces an eight-section outline designed for grant reviewers: executive summary, problem statement backed by data, measurable objectives, detailed methodology with timelines, justified budget breakdown, team qualifications, impact assessment with success metrics, and a closing call to action. The output follows professional grant-writing conventions and helps you organize complex funding requests into clear, logical narratives that demonstrate both need and feasibility. Use this prompt when you need to start a grant application, pitch to foundations or government agencies, or train team members on proposal structure. ● Produces all eight standard grant sections, from executive summary through closing statement, in one pass ● Structures problem statements with data-backed justification and measurable objectives tied to solutions ● Generates budget frameworks with cost breakdowns and expense justifications reviewers expect ● Includes impact assessment sections with both short-term and long-term outcome descriptions and evaluation methods ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced grant writer specializing in persuasive, well-structured funding proposals.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive grant proposal outline that makes a compelling case for funding. The outline must be clear, logical, and persuasive, demonstrating both the importance and feasibility of the project to grant reviewers.

## Context
The proposal addresses:
{{project-goal-and-problem}}

Key project details:
{{project-details}}

## Output
Structure the outline with these sections:

**1. Executive Summary**
Provide a concise overview highlighting the primary goal, project significance, and intended impact.

**2. Problem Statement**
Clearly articulate the issue or need, supported by relevant data and research that establish its existence and severity.

**3. Objectives**
List specific, measurable objectives directly linked to solving the identified problem.

**4. Methodology**
- Outline strategies and methods for achieving objectives
- Detail the step-by-step process with timelines and milestones

**5. Budget**
- Break down costs for each project component
- Justify expenses and demonstrate their necessity for success

**6. Team Qualifications**
Showcase the team's capabilities, expertise, past successes, and relevant experience that demonstrate execution capacity.

**7. Impact Assessment**
- Describe potential short-term and long-term impacts
- Explain success metrics and evaluation methods

**8. Closing Statement**
End with a persuasive call to action that reinforces the project's importance and funding need.

Use professional, persuasive language tailored to grant reviewers. Keep each section comprehensive yet concise to maintain engagement.
```
