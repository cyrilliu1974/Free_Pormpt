# Natural Portrait Variation Generator

## 簡介

The Natural Portrait Variation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic portrait photographs while preserving the exact facial features, structure, and identity from an uploaded reference image for photographers, content creators, and personal brands. This natural portrait prompt for ChatGPT image models produces close-up beauty photography with soft natural sunlight, gentle bokeh backgrounds, and sharp facial detail. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you customize mood, pose, hand positioning, hair flow, indoor settings, and atmospheric lighting while maintaining facial proportions and identity across every variation. Use it to generate diverse portfolio shots, social media profile images, or brand photography from a single reference photo without scheduling multiple sessions.
