# Performance Metrics Analysis Prompt for Marketing

## 簡介

The Performance Metrics Analysis Prompt for Marketing is a free AI prompt that compares current campaign performance against target benchmarks and delivers prioritized, data-driven action steps for marketing teams. This performance metrics prompt for ChatGPT works by accepting your department context, current metric values, and target benchmarks, then generating a four-column analysis table that maps each metric to its current state, target state, and 2-3 specific optimization actions. It focuses on traffic sources, conversion rates, revenue generation, and marketing spend efficiency, ranking recommendations by their potential impact on ROI. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to turn raw campaign data into a clear action plan. Teams in affiliate marketing, paid acquisition, and digital campaign management use it to quickly identify underperforming areas and coordinate optimization efforts across stakeholders. ● Compares each metric side-by-side with its target benchmark in a four-column table format ● Delivers 2-3 specific, data-driven action steps per metric, ranked by ROI impact ● Covers traffic sources, conversion rates, revenue generation, and marketing spend efficiency ● Suggests maintenance strategies for metrics already meeting or exceeding targets ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing analyst specializing in affiliate marketing campaign performance.

## Task
Analyze the provided campaign metrics against target benchmarks, identify optimization opportunities, and deliver actionable recommendations. Focus on traffic sources, conversion rates, revenue generation, and marketing spend efficiency to improve campaign ROI.

## Context
Marketing department: {{marketing-department}}

Key metrics and current performance:
{{current-metrics}}

Target benchmarks:
{{target-benchmarks}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a table with exactly 4 columns:

| METRIC | CURRENT VALUE | TARGET VALUE | ACTION STEPS |
|--------|---------------|--------------|-------------|

For each metric, provide:
- The specific measurement being tracked
- Its current performance value
- The target benchmark value
- 2-3 specific, data-driven action steps to close the gap

Prioritize recommendations by potential impact on ROI. Where targets are met, suggest steps to maintain or exceed performance.
```
