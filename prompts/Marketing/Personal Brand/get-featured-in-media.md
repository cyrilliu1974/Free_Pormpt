# Media Pitch Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Media Pitch Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates tailored media pitch strategies to help independent entrepreneurs secure press coverage and brand visibility. This media pitch prompt for ChatGPT builds persuasive narratives that connect a solopreneur's personal story, industry expertise, and audience value into pitches designed to stand out in journalist inboxes. It analyzes the solopreneur's profile and target media outlets, then produces a complete pitch package including attention-grabbing hooks, credibility-building credentials, timely story angles, and a strategic follow-up sequence. Real applications include pitching podcast appearances, securing feature articles in trade publications, positioning yourself as an expert source for reporters, and building relationships with editors in your niche. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is for solopreneurs, consultants, and independent professionals who want to earn media attention but lack a PR team or agency budget. ● Creates a structured pitch narrative that connects personal story to editorial relevance ● Develops 3-4 timely story angles customized to specific target outlets and beats ● Includes a complete 3-touchpoint follow-up sequence with timing and variation strategy ● Positions credentials and expertise to establish authority without sounding promotional ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a media relations strategist specializing in personal brand visibility for solopreneurs.

## Task
Craft a compelling media pitch that positions the solopreneur as a credible, newsworthy source. Build the pitch using clear logical connections between the solopreneur's story, expertise, and value to create a persuasive narrative that appeals to journalists and editors.

## Context
Solopreneurs need to stand out in crowded media inboxes. A strong pitch connects personal narrative to audience benefit, demonstrates industry authority, and gives editors a clear reason to feature the story. The pitch should be tailored to the specific outlets and beats most relevant to the solopreneur's work.

**Solopreneur profile:**  
{{solopreneur-profile}}

**Target media outlets:**  
{{target-outlets}}

## Output
Deliver a complete media pitch strategy with the following sections:

### Introduction
A 2-3 sentence hook that immediately establishes relevance and captures attention.

### Unique Story
The compelling personal narrative that differentiates this solopreneur—what makes their journey noteworthy and relatable.

### Expertise & Authority
Credentials, accomplishments, and specific knowledge areas that establish the solopreneur as a go-to source.

### Value Proposition
What audiences will gain—actionable insights, fresh perspectives, or solutions to pressing problems.

### Media Angles
3-4 timely story angles tailored to the target outlets, each showing why this solopreneur is the right voice right now.

### Call to Action
A clear next step for the journalist (interview availability, content offers, exclusivity if applicable).

### Follow-Up Plan
A 3-touchpoint outreach sequence with timing and variation strategy to maximize response without being intrusive.
```
