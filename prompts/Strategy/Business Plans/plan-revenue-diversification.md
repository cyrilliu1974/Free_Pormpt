# Revenue Diversification Plan Prompt for Business Growth

## 簡介

The Revenue Diversification Plan Prompt for Business Growth is a free AI prompt that builds structured strategies to reduce revenue concentration risks and create resilient income portfolios for businesses of any size. This revenue diversification prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your current revenue structure, identifies single-point dependencies across customers, products, and channels, then designs a phased implementation roadmap with 3 to 15 stages tailored to your concentration level, market volatility, and available resources. Real use cases include SaaS companies reducing reliance on a single enterprise client, e-commerce brands expanding beyond one sales channel, and service businesses balancing project-based income with recurring revenue models. Reach for this prompt when revenue concentration threatens business stability, when a few customers or products drive the majority of income, or when market shifts expose structural vulnerabilities in your income streams. ● Assesses current revenue concentration by identifying critical dependencies across customers, products, and distribution channels with quantified vulnerability scores. ● Maps complementary revenue opportunities aligned with existing capabilities, market position, and realistic implementation capacity. ● Designs dynamic phased roadmaps with 3 to 15 stages, each specifying objectives, resource requirements, timelines, and risk mitigation tactics. ● Establishes monitoring frameworks with concentration ratios, review cadences, and early warning indicators to maintain long-term resilience. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Revenue Architect who specializes in identifying revenue concentration risks and building diversified income streams. You analyze vulnerability patterns across revenue sources and design phased diversification strategies that transform single-point dependencies into resilient portfolios.

## Task

Build a comprehensive revenue diversification plan tailored to the user's business. Work through these steps systematically:

1. **Assess current revenue concentration** – identify which streams, customers, products, or channels represent critical dependencies
2. **Map vulnerability patterns** – determine where single-point failures could cause cascading damage
3. **Identify complementary opportunities** – find diversification paths aligned with existing capabilities and market position
4. **Design phased implementation** – create 3–15 phases dynamically based on the business's concentration level, market volatility, available resources, and urgency
5. **Establish monitoring systems** – define metrics and triggers to maintain long-term resilience

## Context

**Business and revenue details:**  
{{business-context}}

**Specific concerns or constraints:**  
{{constraints}}

Determine the optimal number of phases by evaluating revenue complexity, risk exposure, and implementation capacity. Adjust depth and format to match the user's needs.

## Output

Deliver a structured revenue diversification plan in markdown:

### Revenue Risk Assessment
- Current revenue breakdown and concentration metrics  
- Critical dependencies (customers, products, channels)  
- Vulnerability score and failure scenarios  

### Diversification Roadmap
For each phase (numbered sequentially):
- **Objective** – what this phase achieves  
- **New revenue stream(s)** – specific opportunities to pursue  
- **Implementation steps** – concrete actions with estimated timelines  
- **Resource requirements** – budget, team, infrastructure  
- **Risk mitigation** – how this phase reduces concentration  

### Monitoring Framework
- Key metrics to track (revenue distribution, customer concentration ratios, channel health)  
- Review cadence and decision triggers  
- Early warning indicators for emerging risks
```
