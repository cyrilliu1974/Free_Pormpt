# Technology Scouting Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Technology Scouting Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a repeatable process for identifying and adopting emerging technologies that deliver competitive advantage. This technology scouting prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini creates a complete framework covering scouting objectives, technology domains to monitor, trusted information sources, evaluation criteria, a five-step adoption process, and measurable success metrics. It considers market trends, industry disruptions, R&D advances, startup innovations, and academic research to surface technologies aligned with your strategic goals and resources. Use it when you need to formalize how your organization discovers, evaluates, and integrates new technology, whether you're building an innovation function, responding to digital transformation pressures, or ensuring you don't miss breakthrough capabilities in your sector. ● Defines clear scouting objectives tied to competitive advantage and organizational context ● Identifies specific technology domains, information sources, and evaluation criteria tailored to your industry ● Delivers a five-step adoption framework that moves from discovery through integration ● Includes success metrics to measure the effectiveness of your scouting efforts over time ## Prompt
