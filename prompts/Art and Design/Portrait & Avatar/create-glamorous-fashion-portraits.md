# Glamorous Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Glamorous Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates upscale fashion photography with detailed styling, poses, and atmospheric lighting for content creators and designers. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces magazine-quality images set in opulent hotel corridors and elevator frames, complete with specified wardrobe details (floor-length satin gowns, feathered coats, luxury accessories), professional makeup direction (bold lips, dramatic eyes), and precise pose instructions (extended arms, tilted head angles, seductive expressions). The prompt includes warm cinematic lighting that highlights fabric textures, metallic surfaces, and creates depth through reflective elevator panels and gold-toned backgrounds. Use it when you need editorial-style fashion imagery for brand campaigns, social media content, lookbooks, or creative mood boards without organizing physical photo shoots. ● Specify your subject, desired mood (dramatic, romantic, confident), and photography style (editorial, cinematic, vintage) through three simple variables. ● Produces detailed fashion scenes with layered accessories, flowing fabrics, and precise pose direction that mimics professional fashion photography. ● Creates warm, dimensional lighting that emphasizes textures like feathers, satin sheen, and metallic accents naturally. ● Generates images framed within architectural elements (elevator doors, corridor perspectives) that add sophistication and context to portraits. ## Prompt

```
{{subject}} posing dramatically in a hotel corridor, framed inside an elevator. Light brown hair styled in soft waves, bold red lipstick with darker eye makeup. Layered pearl necklaces cascading over décolletage. Floor-length white satin slip dress with glossy finish, V-neckline, thin straps, form-fitting drape. Ankle-length luxurious white feathered coat worn open. Small structured black quilted handbag with gold chain strap.

Slightly angled posture, one foot stepping out of the elevator. Right arm extended upward resting on door frame. Head tilted down and to the right, eyes looking downward with seductive expression.

Dark muted gold reflective elevator panel walls with vertical lines and dark trim. She fills most of the frame. Warm overhead lighting illuminating hair, dress sheen, and feather texture, creating highlights on metallic surfaces. {{mood}} luxurious and dramatic atmosphere.

Photography style: {{style}}
```
