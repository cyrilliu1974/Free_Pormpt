# Value Ladder Product Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Value Ladder Product Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a cohesive five-tier product ecosystem for business owners and entrepreneurs seeking structured growth through strategic pricing and customer progression. This value ladder prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates ten distinct product ideas across Bait (free), Front End ($7–$97), Middle ($197–$997), Back End ($2,000–$10,000), and Peak ($15,000+) levels. Each product includes a name, problem-focused description, realistic pricing, and a progression rationale that explains how it naturally leads to the next tier. The prompt emphasizes creative, non-mainstream offerings that can be built through content creation, human expertise, or no-code tools - no capital-intensive development required. Real use cases include course creators mapping out membership tiers, consultants packaging their services into scalable offers, and coaches designing client journeys from free workshops to high-ticket transformation programs. This prompt is for business owners at growth inflection points who need a strategic framework to sequence offers, differentiate in saturated markets, and maximize revenue without overwhelming complexity. ● Produces 10 product ideas with specific pricing, positioning, and progression logic across all five value tiers. ● Focuses on realistic, buildable ideas using content, expertise, or no-code approaches rather than capital-heavy development. ● Includes an implementation strategy with recommended launch sequencing to build momentum without resource strain. ● Explains customer psychology at each tier so every purchase feels inevitable and aligned with their readiness to invest. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a product strategist specialized in building cohesive value ladder ecosystems. You understand how to sequence offerings so each purchase feels like a natural next step, creating progression from entry-level engagement to premium transformation.

## Task

Create a comprehensive Value Ladder strategy with 2 distinct product ideas for each of 5 levels: Bait (free), Front End ($7–$97), Middle ($197–$997), Back End ($2,000–$10,000), and Peak ($15,000+).

For each product idea, provide:
- Product name and description that solves a specific problem
- Realistic pricing based on market positioning and value delivery
- Brief rationale for how it fits the ladder progression

Focus on creative, non-mainstream ideas buildable through content creation, human expertise, or no-code tools—not capital-intensive development.

## Context

{{business-context}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Current Product Offering:** {{current-product}}

## Output

Structure your response as:

### Value Ladder Overview
Brief explanation of how this ladder serves the target audience's journey from discovery to transformation.

### Bait Level (Free)
Two high-value offers designed to attract and demonstrate expertise.

### Front End Level ($7–$97)
Two low-barrier offers to secure the first payment and build trust.

### Middle Level ($197–$997)
Two core products that form the business model foundation.

### Back End Level ($2,000–$10,000)
Two premium offerings providing significantly deeper value and outcomes.

### Peak Level ($15,000+)
Two ultra-premium products representing the highest transformation possible.

### Implementation Strategy
Recommended sequence and timeline for launching these products to build momentum without overwhelming resources.

Ensure each level stands alone as valuable while naturally leading to the next through increasing commitment and results.
```
