# Influencer Agreement Generator for Brand Campaigns

## 簡介

The Influencer Agreement Generator for Brand Campaigns is a free AI prompt that produces structured, legally sound contracts defining expectations, deliverables, and responsibilities for brand-influencer collaborations. This influencer agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini walks through seven core sections: scope of work with deliverable specifications and exclusivity terms, compensation structures and payment schedules, content ownership and usage rights, FTC disclosure and platform compliance requirements, contract duration and termination conditions, liability protections and indemnification clauses, and general provisions covering confidentiality and dispute resolution. You provide the campaign name, brand name, campaign brief, and budget; the model returns a complete agreement template ready for legal review. Marketing teams use it to standardize contracts across multiple influencer partnerships, agencies rely on it to draft initial agreements that protect client interests, and in-house legal departments use it as a starting framework to accelerate contract cycles. ● Covers all essential contract sections including scope of work, payment terms, content ownership, FTC compliance, termination rights, and indemnification ● Adapts to your specific campaign objectives, target audience, and budget constraints ● Produces agreements with precise legal language suitable for review and execution ● Includes exclusivity clauses, revision processes, and post-termination obligations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal consultant specializing in influencer marketing agreements.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive influencer agreement that clearly defines expectations, deliverables, rights, and responsibilities for both brand and influencer. Structure the agreement to be legally sound, protect both parties' interests, and align with current industry standards and regulations.

## Context
Campaign: {{campaign-name}}
Brand: {{brand-name}}
Campaign objectives and target audience: {{campaign-brief}}
Compensation budget: {{budget}}

## Output
Deliver a structured agreement with the following sections, each containing specific clauses and terms in bullet-point format:

**1. Scope of Work**
- Campaign overview and objectives
- Deliverables (content type, quantity, platforms, posting schedule)
- Approval and revision process
- Exclusivity and non-compete terms

**2. Compensation and Payment Terms**
- Payment amount and structure
- Payment schedule and milestones
- Expense reimbursement policy
- Late payment provisions

**3. Content Ownership and Usage Rights**
- Intellectual property ownership
- License grants and usage permissions
- Duration of usage rights
- Content modification rights

**4. Compliance and Disclosure**
- FTC disclosure requirements
- Platform-specific guidelines
- Brand guidelines and messaging standards
- Prohibited content and conduct

**5. Term and Termination**
- Agreement duration
- Termination conditions
- Notice requirements
- Post-termination obligations

**6. Liability and Indemnification**
- Warranties and representations
- Indemnification clauses
- Limitation of liability

**7. General Provisions**
- Confidentiality
- Governing law and dispute resolution
- Assignment and amendment terms
- Entire agreement clause

Use precise legal language appropriate for a binding contract while maintaining clarity.
```
