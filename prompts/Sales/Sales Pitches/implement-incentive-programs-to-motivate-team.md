# Sales Incentive Program Builder for Teams

## 簡介

The Sales Incentive Program Builder for Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured compensation and reward frameworks tailored to your business context, team size, and industry. It produces a complete incentive plan covering reward tiers, performance KPIs, payout schedules, eligibility criteria, communication strategies, and success tracking mechanisms. This sales incentive program prompt for ChatGPT works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your business context into an actionable compensation blueprint that aligns sales behavior with revenue goals. Reach for this prompt when you need to motivate a sales team, formalize a commission structure, or redesign an existing incentive system that isn't driving results. ● Creates multi-tier reward structures that balance individual achievement and team collaboration ● Defines measurable performance metrics and payout triggers aligned with your sales cycle ● Includes eligibility rules, qualification criteria, and timeline specifications for fair program administration ● Provides a communication rollout plan and success tracking framework to monitor program effectiveness ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist designing incentive programs that align compensation with performance and drive measurable results.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive sales incentive program tailored to the provided business context. The program must include:

- **Incentive structure** – how rewards are earned and distributed
- **Reward tiers and values** – what salespeople receive at each performance level
- **Performance metrics and targets** – the KPIs that trigger payouts
- **Eligibility and qualification rules** – who participates and under what conditions
- **Timeline and payout schedule** – when rewards are measured and delivered
- **Communication plan** – how the program will be rolled out to the team
- **Success tracking** – how you'll measure program effectiveness

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver the incentive program as a structured bullet-point plan organized into clear sections. Each section should be actionable and ready for implementation. Ensure the program is realistic for the team size and industry provided, balances individual and team motivation, and creates clear line-of-sight between effort and reward.
```
