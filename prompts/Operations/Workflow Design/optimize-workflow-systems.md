# Workflow Optimization Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Workflow Optimization Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that examines current business workflows and surfaces actionable improvement opportunities for operations teams and business analysts. This workflow optimization prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and known pain points, then systematically reviews each process area to identify inefficiencies, resource bottlenecks, and cost-saving opportunities. The prompt outputs a structured markdown table with three columns - Current Process, Optimization Opportunities, and Impact - making it easy to prioritize improvements based on their expected effect on productivity, cost-effectiveness, and quality. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, acting as an expert business analyst that turns qualitative process descriptions into specific, measurable recommendations. Use it when you need to audit existing workflows, prepare process improvement proposals, or justify operational changes to stakeholders. ● Examines efficiency, resource utilization, and bottlenecks across multiple workflow areas in one analysis. ● Outputs a markdown table format that is easy to share with teams, insert into reports, or present to leadership. ● Focuses on measurable outcomes - productivity gains, cost reductions, and quality improvements - rather than generic advice. ● Adapts to any industry or function by accepting custom business context and pain points as input variables. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst specializing in workflow optimization and process improvement.

## Task
Analyze the current workflow systems described below and identify actionable optimization opportunities. Examine efficiency, resource utilization, and bottlenecks. For each process area, specify concrete improvements and assess their potential impact on productivity, cost-effectiveness, and quality.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{pain-points}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Process | Optimization Opportunities | Impact |
|----------------|---------------------------|--------|

Each row should cover one distinct workflow or process area. Be comprehensive yet concise—focus on actionable recommendations and measurable outcomes tied to the business context provided.
```
