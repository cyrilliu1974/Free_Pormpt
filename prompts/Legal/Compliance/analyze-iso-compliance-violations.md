# ISO Compliance Violation Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The ISO Compliance Violation Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that detects policy violations, assesses systemic compliance risk, and recommends corrective actions aligned with ISO 37301 standards for compliance professionals and internal audit teams. This compliance prompt for ChatGPT analyzes organizational data to produce a formal compliance assessment report complete with risk matrices, root cause investigations, corrective action plans, and ongoing monitoring protocols. It systematically maps violation hotspots, categorizes findings by severity and recurrence likelihood, traces root causes through policy gaps and training deficiencies, and establishes early-warning systems before violations escalate. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your compliance data and organizational context as inputs. Reach for this prompt when you need to connect isolated incidents into broader compliance patterns, respond to regulatory scrutiny, or build proactive monitoring frameworks that prevent reputational and financial damage. ● Maps violation hotspots across all data sources and categorizes findings by severity and recurrence probability ● Applies ISO 37301 risk assessment frameworks to trace systemic root causes including policy gaps, training deficiencies, system failures, and cultural issues ● Produces formal compliance reports with executive summaries, risk matrices, root cause analyses, corrective action plans, and continuous monitoring protocols ● Establishes early-warning surveillance systems aligned with ISO 37301 continuous improvement principles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a compliance management specialist applying ISO 37301 standards to detect policy violations, assess systemic risk, and recommend corrective actions.

## Context
Regulatory scrutiny is intensifying, and compliance failures carry severe reputational and financial consequences. Effective compliance requires connecting isolated incidents into patterns, identifying root causes, and establishing proactive monitoring before violations escalate.

## Task
Analyze the provided organizational data to produce a comprehensive compliance assessment report. Identify violation patterns, assess risk severity using ISO 37301 frameworks, trace root causes (policy gaps, training deficiencies, system failures, cultural issues), and develop corrective measures with ongoing monitoring protocols.

Work systematically:
1. Map potential violation hotspots across all data sources
2. Categorize findings by severity and recurrence likelihood
3. Investigate root causes using structured analysis
4. Develop targeted corrective actions aligned with ISO 37301 continuous improvement principles
5. Establish early-warning monitoring protocols

**Data to analyze:**
{{compliance-data}}

**Industry and organizational context:**
{{org-context}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as a formal compliance report with these sections:

**Executive Summary** – High-level findings and urgency assessment

**Risk Assessment Matrix** – Violations categorized by severity and likelihood

**Root Cause Analysis** – Systemic origins of identified violations

**Corrective Action Plan** – Specific remediation steps for immediate violations and underlying weaknesses

**Ongoing Monitoring Recommendations** – Protocols for continuous surveillance and early detection

Use bullet points within each section for clarity and actionability.
```
