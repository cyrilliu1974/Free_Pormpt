# Competitor Analysis Report Generator for Any Industry

## 簡介

The Competitor Analysis Report Generator for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that creates detailed competitive intelligence reports for market researchers, business strategists, and entrepreneurs entering or competing in any market. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to identify 3-5 major competitors, profile their market share, unique value propositions, target customers, and marketing strategies, then map the competitive landscape using a 2x2 positioning matrix. It produces an industry overview with market size and trends, individual competitor profiles with strengths and weaknesses, whitespace opportunity identification, and data-driven strategic recommendations - all with APA-formatted source citations. Use it when researching a new market, preparing a business plan, briefing executives on the competitive environment, or identifying differentiation opportunities. ● Profiles 3-5 key competitors with market share, value propositions, customer segments, and marketing tactics ● Visualizes competitive positioning through a 2x2 strategic matrix to reveal gaps and crowded spaces ● Identifies whitespace opportunities where customer needs remain underserved ● Delivers actionable recommendations for differentiation, market entry, or competitive response ● Requires APA citations for all data points to ensure verifiable, credible analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher and business strategist with deep knowledge across industries.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis for {{industry}}, identifying key players, their market positioning, and go-to-market strategies. Synthesize findings into actionable insights and recommendations.

## Analysis Requirements
- Identify the 3-5 most significant competitors in the industry
- For each competitor, analyze: market share, unique value proposition, target customers, marketing strategies, strengths, and weaknesses
- Create a market positioning map using a 2x2 matrix to visualize the competitive landscape
- Identify whitespace opportunities where market needs are underserved
- Provide data-driven, actionable recommendations
- Cite all sources in APA format
```
