# Shareholder Agreement Template Generator

## 簡介

The Shareholder Agreement Template Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete, legally structured shareholder agreements for corporations seeking to formalize governance and ownership terms. This shareholder agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a formatted contract with recitals, numbered articles covering share ownership structure, voting rights (quorum and supermajority rules), dividend distribution policies, share transfer procedures (including right of first refusal, tag-along and drag-along rights), dispute resolution mechanisms, and standard miscellaneous provisions. You provide the company name, state of incorporation, and context about the business situation; the prompt returns a complete agreement draft with bracketed placeholders ready for review by counsel. It is designed for founders, general counsel, and business attorneys who need a thorough starting point that addresses the most common shareholder governance issues without starting from a blank page. ● Produces a full shareholder agreement with recitals, numbered articles, and signature blocks in proper legal format. ● Covers share classes, voting thresholds, dividend policy, right of first refusal, tag-along/drag-along rights, and buy-sell provisions. ● Includes bracketed placeholders for dates, names, and percentages so you can finalize specifics with your attorney. ● Adapts the agreement language to your company context, state of incorporation, and ownership structure. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business attorney specializing in shareholder agreements.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive shareholder agreement for {{company-name}}, a {{state-of-incorporation}} corporation. The agreement must cover:

- **Share ownership structure** – classes of shares, ownership percentages, rights and obligations of shareholders
- **Voting rights** – quorum requirements, majority/supermajority thresholds, matters requiring shareholder approval
- **Dividend distribution policies** – timing, calculation method, retained earnings provisions
- **Transfer and sale procedures** – right of first refusal, tag-along/drag-along rights, permitted transfers, valuation methodology
- **Dispute resolution** – deadlock provisions, buy-sell triggers
- **Miscellaneous provisions** – amendment procedures, governing law, confidentiality

## Context
Tailor the agreement to the company's specific situation:

{{company-context}}

## Output
Structure the agreement with:

**SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT**

Opening paragraph identifying the parties and effective date.

**RECITALS**  
WHEREAS clauses establishing background and intent.

**Numbered articles** (1. SHARE OWNERSHIP, 2. VOTING RIGHTS, etc.) with detailed provisions under each.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF** closing with signature blocks.

Use clear, precise legal language. Avoid unnecessary jargon. Include bracketed placeholders [DATE], [SHAREHOLDER NAMES] where specific information will be inserted. Ensure the agreement protects all shareholders' interests while supporting the company's governance needs.
```
