# LinkedIn Profile and Company Page Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Profile and Company Page Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive LinkedIn presence for professionals and businesses seeking to attract their ideal audience. This LinkedIn optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes three inputs - your target audience profile, professional background, and company details - and produces a full-package deliverable: a keyword-rich headline under 220 characters, a narrative summary that addresses audience pain points, achievement-focused experience bullets with quantifiable results, priority skills, a company About section optimized for search, mission and values statements, three sample post ideas, and a three-pillar content strategy table with formats and calls-to-action. It structures every element to showcase expertise, build credibility, and drive engagement, weaving SEO keywords and storytelling throughout. Use it when you need to refresh a stale profile, launch a company page, or align your LinkedIn presence with a new market position or service offering. ● Produces a target audience analysis (description, pain points, goals) so every content decision is anchored in real needs. ● Generates headline, summary, experience, and skills sections with achievement-focused language and quantifiable metrics. ● Delivers company page copy (About, mission, values) and three sample update ideas to jumpstart engagement. ● Includes a content strategy table mapping themes to formats and CTAs, plus optimization notes on SEO, storytelling, and goal alignment. ## Prompt
