# Video Script Generator for Persuasive Sales Videos

## 簡介

The Video Script Generator for Persuasive Sales Videos is a free AI prompt that writes conversion-focused video scripts for marketers, content creators, and business owners promoting products or services. This video script prompt for ChatGPT follows a seven-part waterfall structure: it opens with a click-worthy title and attention-grabbing hook, defines the audience's problem with emotional weight, introduces your solution with three key benefits, explains how it works in simple steps, adds social proof through testimonials and results, then closes with a clear call-to-action that creates urgency. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing conversational scripts optimized for YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, or any video platform where you need to turn viewers into customers. Reach for this prompt when you need a full sales video script that keeps viewers watching and guides them toward a purchase or signup. ● Produces all seven essential script sections - title, hook, problem, solution, how-it-works, social proof, and call-to-action - in one pass. ● Uses conversational language and grammar dependency techniques to build momentum and keep viewers engaged. ● Adapts to any product, service, audience demographic, or video platform you specify in the offering-and-audience variable. ● Incorporates emotional appeals, benefit-driven messaging, and urgency triggers proven to drive viewer action. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert scriptwriter specializing in persuasive, conversational video content that captures attention and drives action.

## Task
Write a complete video script following a proven "waterfall" structure where each section flows naturally into the next, keeping viewers engaged from open to close. Use simple, conversational language and grammar dependency techniques to build momentum.

## Context
Product/service and audience: {{offering-and-audience}}
(Describe what you're promoting and who it's for—include the product/service, target audience demographics/pain points, and video platform)

## Output
Deliver the script in these seven sections:

**Video Title**
Create a click-worthy title that directly addresses the target audience's problem or desire.

**Hook**
Open with a strong question or statement that connects to a core problem the audience faces and sparks immediate curiosity.

**Problem**
Highlight why this problem matters and evoke an emotional response. Make the audience feel the weight of not solving it.

**Solution**
Introduce the product/service as the answer. Emphasize three key benefits that directly counter the problem.

**How It Works**
Provide a simple 3-4 step explanation of how the product/service works. Keep it concrete and easy to visualize.

**Social Proof**
Include 1-2 brief customer testimonials and one impressive statistic or result to build trust and credibility.

**Call to Action**
Present a clear offer, provide simple signup/purchase instructions, and create urgency to drive immediate action.
```
