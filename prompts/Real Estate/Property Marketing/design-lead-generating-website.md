# Real Estate Lead-Generating Website Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Real Estate Lead-Generating Website Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused website blueprints for real estate agents competing in saturated local markets. This real estate website design prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete site architecture blueprint organized by page, including homepage foundation copy, navigation sequence, trust-building element placement, conversion architecture with strategic CTA positioning, lead capture form recommendations, and content strategy guidelines. Real estate professionals use it to transform generic template sites into authority-establishing lead machines that guide visitors from awareness to appointment booking through psychologically sequenced information architecture. The prompt outputs specific copy examples, form field recommendations (3-5 fields maximum), multiple contact pathways, and geographic authority positioning - all designed to answer the visitor's core questions within five seconds and make lead submission effortless. Reach for this prompt when building a new real estate site or redesigning an existing one that fails to convert traffic into qualified leads. ● Delivers exact navigation sequences (Home → About Us → Services → Gallery → Testimonials → Contact Us) that mirror the prospect's psychological journey from awareness to contact. ● Specifies strategic call-to-action placement at natural decision points, lead capture form types, optimal form field counts, and multiple contact pathways on every page. ● Provides example copy blocks demonstrating tone, clarity, and immediate relevance - answering "What services? Where located? Why the best choice?" within five seconds. ● Includes trust-building element strategies (professional photo/video placement, testimonial integration, local authority positioning) and clear guidelines on what to avoid (jargon, generic statements, excessive form fields, pages without conversion paths). ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused web strategist specializing in real estate lead generation. Effective real estate websites function as lead-capture machines, not digital brochures—every element must either build trust or drive action.

## Task
Design a complete website structure and content blueprint for a real estate agent that prioritizes lead generation over aesthetics. The blueprint should position the agent as the definitive local authority and guide visitors from awareness to contact through strategic information architecture.

## Context
The agent operates in a saturated market where templated websites fail to convert. Visitors decide within seconds whether to stay or bounce to competitors. The website must communicate credibility, locality, and superiority immediately while making it effortless to submit contact information or book appointments.

{{business-context}}

Provide: services offered and specializations; geographic service area and local market expertise; unique competitive advantages; target client profile; current website status (new build or redesign).

## Output
Deliver a structured website blueprint organized by page with these components:

**Homepage Foundation**
Opening statement that answers within 5 seconds: What services? Where located? Why the best choice? Include specific copy examples that establish immediate relevance and authority.

**Navigation Structure**
Present the exact top navigation sequence: Home → About Us → Services → Gallery → Testimonials → Contact Us. Explain how this mirrors the prospect's psychological journey.

**Trust-Building Elements**
- Professional photo and video placement recommendations
- Content that humanizes the agent and demonstrates expertise
- Testimonial integration strategy

**Conversion Architecture**
- Strategic call-to-action placement at natural decision points
- Lead capture form types (inquiry forms, appointment booking, information requests)
- Recommended form fields (minimal friction, maximum follow-up value)
- Multiple contact pathways on every page

**Content Strategy**
- Clear, jargon-free language guidelines
- Local authority positioning through geographic expertise
- Value proposition messaging for each key page
- Example copy demonstrating tone and clarity

**Must Include:**
- Multiple CTAs throughout all pages
- Direct appointment booking functionality
- Simple lead capture forms (3-5 fields maximum)
- Visual connection elements on homepage
- Geographic service area prominence
- Clear competitive differentiation

**Must Avoid:**
- Real estate jargon and complicated terminology
- Generic statements applicable to any agent
- Pages without conversion paths
- Excessive form fields
- Text-heavy sections without visual breaks
- Passive language that doesn't guide action

Format with bold headers for pages/sections, bullet points for elements, and example copy blocks where relevant.
```
