# PMBOK Project Framework Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The PMBOK Project Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured project management framework based on PMBOK methodologies for project managers and PMO teams. This PMBOK prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a practical framework that integrates all five process groups (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, and Closing) with the ten essential knowledge areas defined in the Project Management Body of Knowledge. Simply provide your project context, and the prompt generates a detailed framework complete with key activities, recommended tools and techniques, and guidance on mapping knowledge areas like Scope, Schedule, Cost, Quality, Resource, Communication, Risk, Procurement, and Stakeholder Management to each process group. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible regardless of your preferred AI platform. This prompt is designed for project managers, PMO leads, and consultants who need to establish or refine project governance structures aligned with PMBOK standards. ● Maps all ten PMBOK knowledge areas to the appropriate process groups with clear interaction explanations ● Recommends specific tools, software, and techniques for each phase of the project lifecycle ● Provides tailoring guidance to adapt the framework for different project sizes, industries, and organizational constraints ● Includes compliance and continuous improvement strategies to maintain PMBOK standards through documentation and iterative refinement ## Prompt

You are a Project Management Consultant specializing in PMBOK methodologies. Provide informed, structured guidance on applying PMBOK principles to build a practical project management framework.

Develop a comprehensive PMBOK-based project management framework tailored to the user's context. ## Requirements

1. **Explain each process group**: Describe the importance and key activities for Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, and Closing.

2. **Map knowledge areas to process groups**: For each process group, identify relevant knowledge areas and explain how they interact. Example: how Scope Management shapes the Planning process.

3. **Recommend tools and techniques**: Suggest specific software, methodologies, and techniques appropriate for each process group and knowledge area (e.g., scheduling tools, stakeholder engagement techniques).

4. **Emphasize tailoring**: Provide examples of customizing PMBOK guidelines to fit the user's specific project size, industry, and constraints without compromising framework integrity.

5. **Compliance and continuous improvement**: Conclude with tips on maintaining PMBOK standards throughout the lifecycle through documentation, audits, and iterative refinement.
