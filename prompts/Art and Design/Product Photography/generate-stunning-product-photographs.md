# Spirit Bottle Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Spirit Bottle Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional close-up shots of spirit bottles positioned on their side with editorial styling and precise focus. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and other AI image generators produces ultra-high resolution images with shallow depth of field, sharp label detail, and a glowing liquid effect that highlights the spirit's color and character. You control the spirit type, liquid color, scene styling with props like citrus or spices, and aspect ratio to match your brand guidelines or e-commerce requirements. Reach for this prompt when you need catalog-ready bottle shots, social media content, or marketing visuals that emphasize texture, label design, and the premium nature of the product. ● Centers the bottle laying on its side with customizable liquid glow in any color to match your spirit (amber, clear, golden, green). ● Adds scene styling with props scattered around the bottle - oranges, cinnamon sticks, botanicals, or ice - to tell a visual story. ● Delivers ultra-high resolution output with shallow depth of field and sharp focus on the label and liquid. ● Supports custom aspect ratios for different platforms, from square social posts to wide banner images. ## Prompt

```
Close-up product photograph of a {{spirit-type}} bottle with {{liquid-color}} glow, centered and laying on its side. {{scene-styling}} scattered around the bottle. Ultra-high resolution, shallow depth of field, editorial clean styling, sharp focus on label and liquid. --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
