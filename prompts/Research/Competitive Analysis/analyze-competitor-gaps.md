# Competitor Gap Analysis Prompt for Strategic Positioning

## 簡介

The Competitor Gap Analysis Prompt for Strategic Positioning is a free AI prompt that uncovers competitor messaging vulnerabilities, unaddressed customer pain points, and strategic positioning opportunities for product marketers and business strategists. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to analyze competitor product listings, messaging, and value propositions across multiple dimensions - from emotional triggers and trust gaps to keyword holes and pricing perception mismatches. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting three variables: your product details, competitor data, and market positioning context. The output is a structured strategic report with a gap analysis table, keyword opportunities, messaging vulnerabilities, and a tiered action plan (immediate, short-term, and long-term wins). Use it when you need to differentiate in crowded markets, refine your positioning strategy, or find white space competitors have missed. ● Identifies competitor strengths you cannot challenge and weaknesses in their messaging, value propositions, and emotional appeal. ● Maps high-value keyword gaps, long-tail search opportunities, and semantic SEO holes competitors ignore. ● Highlights overlooked customer needs, unaddressed objections, and underserved niche audiences. ● Delivers a tiered positioning strategy with immediate wins, short-term tactics, and sustainable long-term differentiation. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a competitive intelligence specialist focused on strategic positioning gaps. Your analysis uncovers not just what competitors offer, but vulnerabilities in their messaging and unspoken customer needs they fail to address.

## Task

Analyze competitor product listings to identify strategic gaps and generate actionable positioning recommendations:

1. Identify competitor strengths that cannot be directly challenged
2. Discover weaknesses in messaging, value propositions, and positioning
3. Find overlooked customer needs and pain points competitors acknowledge but don't solve
4. Map keyword and SEO gaps
5. Create positioning strategies that claim unique territory

## Context

**Your Product:** {{product-details}}

**Competitor Data:** {{competitor-data}}

**Market Context:** {{positioning-context}}

Analyze across these dimensions:

**Messaging:** Titles (keyword density, emotional triggers, clarity), bullet points (missing benefits, weak value propositions, overlooked use cases), descriptions (storytelling failures, trust gaps, missing social proof), visual communication weaknesses.

**Strategic:** Pricing vulnerabilities and value perception mismatches, unaddressed customer objections, ignored or underserved niche audiences, substitute products solving the same problem differently.

**Prioritization:** Immediate vs. long-term opportunities, sustainable advantages over temporary tactics, positioning opportunities not surface-level feature comparisons.

## Output

**Competitor Landscape Overview**
Summary of competitive dynamics and immediate opportunities

**Strategic Gap Analysis**
| Competitor Element | Gap Identified | Exploitation Opportunity | Priority |
|-------------------|----------------|--------------------------|----------|

**Keyword Gap Analysis**
- High-value keywords competitors miss
- Long-tail opportunities
- Semantic SEO gaps

**Messaging Vulnerabilities**
- Emotional triggers they ignore
- Trust elements they lack
- Value propositions they fail to communicate

**Actionable Positioning Strategy**
1. Immediate wins (implement within 24 hours)
2. Short-term advantages (1-2 weeks)
3. Sustainable differentiation (long-term)

**Recommended Messaging Framework**
Specific copy suggestions that exploit identified gaps without direct competitor attacks
```
