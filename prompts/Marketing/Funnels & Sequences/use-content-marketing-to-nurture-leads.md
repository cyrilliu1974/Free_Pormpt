# Lead Nurturing Content Marketing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Nurturing Content Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured content plan to move potential customers through the buyer's journey for marketers, growth teams, and content strategists. This lead nurturing content marketing prompt for ChatGPT walks you through defining your target audience, product, and business context, then outputs a three-stage strategy covering awareness, consideration, and decision phases. It specifies which content types to create at each touchpoint - blog posts, case studies, product demos - where to distribute them, and which metrics to track. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing an actionable blueprint you can hand to writers, designers, and campaign managers. Teams use it to align content calendars with funnel stages, avoid gaps in the buyer's journey, and set clear KPIs for each piece of content. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete content roadmap that connects top-of-funnel education to bottom-of-funnel conversion, or when onboarding a new product line and want a repeatable framework for planning nurture campaigns. ● Maps each stage of the buyer's journey to specific content types, distribution channels, and success metrics ● Tailors recommendations to your target audience, product offering, and business context ● Outputs an organized bullet-point strategy that teams can execute immediately ● Ensures logical progression from awareness through consideration to final decision ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in lead nurturing campaigns.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content marketing strategy that guides potential customers through the buyer's journey. Identify key touchpoints at each stage, recommend tailored content formats, suggest distribution channels, and establish success metrics.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Product/service:** {{product-service}}
**Business context:** {{business-context}}

The strategy should align content types and channels with each stage of the buyer's journey, ensuring a logical progression from awareness through consideration to decision.

## Output
Organize your strategy as a bullet point list structured by the three stages of the buyer's journey:

- **Awareness stage:** [content types, touchpoints, channels, metrics]
- **Consideration stage:** [content types, touchpoints, channels, metrics]
- **Decision stage:** [content types, touchpoints, channels, metrics]

For each stage, specify what content to create, where to distribute it, and how to measure effectiveness.
```
