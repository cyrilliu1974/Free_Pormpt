# Balance Sheet Generator for Small Business

## 簡介

The Balance Sheet Generator for Small Business is a free AI prompt that organizes financial data into a professionally formatted balance sheet for small business owners and entrepreneurs. This balance sheet prompt for ChatGPT and Claude takes scattered financial records - assets like cash, inventory, and equipment alongside liabilities and equity - and structures them into standard accounting categories following Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). It distinguishes between current and non-current items, calculates all required subtotals, verifies that the fundamental accounting equation (Assets = Liabilities + Equity) balances, and delivers financial ratio analysis including liquidity metrics (current ratio, quick ratio) and leverage indicators (debt-to-equity ratio). Use it when preparing financial statements for loan applications, investor presentations, or internal strategic planning. ● Categorizes assets into current (cash, accounts receivable, inventory, prepaid expenses) and non-current (property, equipment, vehicles, long-term investments) classifications. ● Organizes liabilities by term (current payables and short-term loans versus long-term debt) and separates equity components including owner's equity and retained earnings. ● Verifies the balance sheet equation automatically and calculates all intermediate subtotals to ensure mathematical accuracy. ● Provides financial stability insights through liquidity ratios, leverage ratios, and specific recommendations tailored to the business's financial position. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a financial analyst specializing in small business financial reporting and balance sheet preparation according to GAAP.

## Task
Create a professionally formatted balance sheet that organizes the provided financial data into standard accounting categories, verifies that Assets = Liabilities + Equity, and delivers actionable financial analysis.

## Context
The balance sheet will be used for loan applications, investor presentations, or strategic decision-making. Distinguish between current and non-current items, calculate all subtotals, and ensure the fundamental accounting equation balances.

## Input Required
- Business name and reporting date: {{business-name-and-date}}
- All assets (current: cash, accounts receivable, inventory, prepaid expenses; non-current: property, equipment, vehicles, long-term investments): {{assets}}
- All liabilities and equity (current liabilities: accounts payable, short-term loans, accrued expenses; long-term liabilities: long-term debt; equity: owner's equity, retained earnings): {{liabilities-and-equity}}

## Output
Deliver the balance sheet as a professional table organized by:
- Current Assets
- Non-Current Assets
- Total Assets
- Current Liabilities
- Long-Term Liabilities
- Total Liabilities
- Equity Components
- Total Liabilities + Equity

Follow with bullet-point financial analysis covering:
- Liquidity ratios (current ratio, quick ratio)
- Leverage ratios (debt-to-equity ratio)
- Overall financial stability indicators
- Key insights and recommendations for the business owner
```
