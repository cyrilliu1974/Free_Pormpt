# Sales Territory Planning Prompt for Teams

## 簡介

The Sales Territory Planning Prompt for Teams is a free AI prompt that creates optimized territory coverage plans for sales organizations looking to maximize revenue potential. This sales territory planning prompt for ChatGPT builds a markdown table mapping territories to sales representatives, target accounts, and revenue goals, then delivers customized recommendations for account management, prospecting techniques, and performance tracking methods. It works by analyzing market potential, customer demographics, and historical sales patterns to allocate resources strategically across territories. Sales leaders use it to replace manual spreadsheet planning, balance workloads across reps, and ensure high-value accounts receive appropriate coverage. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when restructuring sales territories, onboarding new reps, entering new markets, or conducting quarterly planning cycles. ● Outputs a customizable markdown table with territories, assigned reps, target accounts, and revenue targets ● Incorporates market demographics, sales history, and customer data you provide to inform assignments ● Recommends specific account management tactics and prospecting approaches for each territory segment ● Includes performance tracking methods to monitor quota attainment and plan execution ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist developing a comprehensive territory planning strategy that optimizes coverage and maximizes revenue potential.

## Task
Create a detailed territory plan presented as a structured markdown table. Include assigned sales representatives, target accounts, and revenue goals for each territory. Provide at least 5 sample rows illustrating the planning structure.

After the table, recommend:
- Account management strategies tailored to the territories
- Prospecting techniques suited to the market segments
- Performance tracking methods to monitor plan execution

## Context
Consider market potential, customer demographics, and historical sales patterns when allocating resources across territories.

{{sales-team-context}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these column headers:
- {{territory-column}}
- {{sales-rep-column}}
- {{target-accounts-column}}
- {{revenue-goals-column}}

Follow the table with a concise strategy section covering account management, prospecting, and performance tracking specific to the territories shown.
```
