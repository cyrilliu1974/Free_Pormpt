# Photogram Aesthetic Image Generator

## 簡介

The Photogram Aesthetic Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates striking edge-lit images with centered minimal compositions for designers and visual artists. This photogram aesthetic prompt for Midjourney produces images where your chosen subject - emoji, icon, object, or symbol - appears illuminated from the edges against a minimal background. The prompt applies a signature edge-lit glow effect that highlights contours and creates depth through light rather than detail. It works by centering your subject in a 1:1 square format with medium stylization (--s 250), high quality rendering (--q 2), and controlled variation (--c 10). Use it for app icons, social media graphics, logo concepts, or any design work where clean, glowing aesthetics matter. Reach for this prompt when you need polished, attention-grabbing visuals that balance simplicity with visual interest. ● Produces centered subjects with distinctive edge-lit glow effects that emphasize shape and silhouette. ● Applies a minimal composition style that keeps focus on the subject without distracting backgrounds. ● Uses fixed parameters (1:1 aspect ratio, stylization 250, quality 2, chaos 10) for reliable, repeatable results. ● Accepts any subject and style-details variables to customize materials, colors, finishes, and visual treatments. ## Prompt

```
Photogram aesthetic, {{subject}}, edge-lit glow, {{style-details}}, centered minimal composition, --ar 1:1 --s 250 --q 2 --c 10
```
