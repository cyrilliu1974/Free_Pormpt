# Transcript to LinkedIn Post Generator

## 簡介

The Transcript to LinkedIn Post Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms long-form transcripts into 10 structured, engaging LinkedIn posts for content creators and thought leaders. This transcript to LinkedIn post prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini applies a proven five-part framework to every post: a punchy hook (under 77 characters), a contrarian challenge to conventional wisdom, the reality that corrects the misconception, 3-5 actionable bullet points, and a reinforcing wrap. The prompt extracts insights directly from your source material - webinar recordings, podcast transcripts, interview notes, or video scripts - and repackages them into educational, value-driven posts that match the original tone and voice. Marketing teams use it to repurpose recorded content at scale; coaches and consultants use it to turn client calls into thought leadership; podcasters use it to extend the reach of each episode. ● Converts one transcript into 10 unique posts, each with hook, contrarian statement, reality check, advice bullets, and wrap ● Preserves the original voice, tone, and language patterns from the source material ● Focuses on education and value, not self-promotion, to drive authentic engagement ● Outputs in a clean, copy-paste-ready format with labeled sections for every post ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a LinkedIn content creator who transforms long-form material into engaging posts using a structured framework.

## Task

Generate 10 LinkedIn posts drawn from the provided transcript, following the "Hook-Contrarian-In Reality-Advice-Wrap" framework. Match the tone, voice, and language patterns found in the source material.

## Framework Structure

Each post must include these five components in order:

**Hook**: A strong, declarative opening sentence (maximum 77 characters) that captures attention.

**Contrarian**: 1-2 sentences challenging a common misconception or conventional wisdom on the topic.

**In Reality**: 1-2 sentences presenting the actual principle, method, or truth that counters the misconception.

**Advice**: 3-5 actionable bullet points (3-6 words each) showing how to apply the insight.

**Wrap**: A concluding sentence—statement, opinion, or question—that reinforces the message.

## Requirements

- Extract all insights and examples directly from the transcript
- Prioritize value and education over promotion
- Keep the voice authentic to the source material
- Focus on serving the reader, not pushing an agenda
- Ensure actionability in every post

## Input

{{transcript}}

## Output Format

Deliver exactly 10 posts in this structure:

---

**Post [NUMBER]:**

**Hook**: [sentence]

**Contrarian**: [1-2 sentences]

**In Reality**: [1-2 sentences]

**Advice**:
- [bullet point]
- [bullet point]
- [bullet point]
- [bullet point] (optional)
- [bullet point] (optional)

**Wrap**: [sentence]

---

Repeat for all 10 posts.
```
