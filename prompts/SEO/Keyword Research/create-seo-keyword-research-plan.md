# Google Keyword Planner Research Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Google Keyword Planner Research Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, step-by-step instructional guides for conducting keyword research using Google Keyword Planner, customized to any industry. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT produces a nine-section tutorial covering everything from initial tool access and interface navigation to advanced competitor analysis and keyword export workflows. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating markdown-formatted guides with clear headings, numbered steps, pro tips, and annotated screenshot placeholders. Real-world applications include training materials for SEO teams, client onboarding documentation for agencies, self-guided learning resources for marketers, and internal documentation for ad campaign specialists who need repeatable keyword research processes. This prompt is for SEO professionals, digital marketing agencies, content strategists, and Google Ads specialists who need to teach or document the keyword research process in a structured, industry-specific format. ● Generates nine structured sections covering tool access, metric interpretation, filtering techniques, competitor keyword analysis, and export workflows ● Includes placeholders for annotated screenshots with specific instructions on what interface elements to capture and highlight ● Produces industry-tailored pro tips and seed keyword selection strategies customized to your specific market ● Outputs markdown-formatted guides with numbered steps, bullet points, and callout boxes for immediate use in training materials or documentation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Google Ads and SEO strategist creating a comprehensive, step-by-step instructional guide for conducting keyword research using Google Keyword Planner.

## Task
Produce a detailed guide that walks through the complete keyword research process, from accessing the tool to exporting and integrating results. Break down each phase into clear, sequential steps that a user can follow to master keyword research for their specific industry.

## Context
Keyword research is fundamental to SEO success, and Google Keyword Planner remains one of the most powerful free tools available. This guide should cover:

**1. Introduction**
- Why keyword research matters for SEO performance
- Google Keyword Planner's role and capabilities

**2. Getting Started**
- How to access Google Keyword Planner (account requirements, navigation)
- Overview of the tool's interface and main sections

**3. Setting Up Your Search**
- Different keyword search options available
- How to enter and select effective seed keywords for {{industry}}
- Tips for choosing seed keywords that yield valuable opportunities in {{industry}}

**4. Interpreting Search Results**
- Key metrics explained: average monthly searches, competition level, bid ranges
- How to read and analyze the data tables
- Criteria for identifying high-potential keyword opportunities

**5. Refining Your Keyword List**
- Filtering and segmenting techniques (by volume, competition, relevance)
- Creating themed keyword groupings for campaign organization
- Best practices for narrowing to actionable keywords

**6. Expanding Your Research**
- Using "Broaden your search" and "Get ideas in bulk" features
- Strategies for uncovering long-tail and semantically related keywords
- Creative approaches to discover untapped opportunities in {{industry}}

**7. Analyzing Competitor Keywords**
- How to research keywords competitors rank for
- Using the competition feature to assess difficulty
- Identifying high-value competitor keywords to target

**8. Exporting & Integration**
- Step-by-step export process and format options
- Best practices for organizing exported keyword lists
- How to integrate keywords into content strategy, ad campaigns, and SEO planning

**9. Conclusion**
- Key takeaways and success factors
- Importance of ongoing, regular keyword research
- Encouragement to iterate and refine the approach

## Output
Format the guide with:
- Clear markdown headings (##, ###) for sections and subsections
- Numbered steps within each section for easy progression
- Bullet points for tips, best practices, and key insights
- Placeholders for screenshots with descriptions of what to capture and annotate (e.g., "[Screenshot: Keyword Planner landing page with 'Discover new keywords' and 'Get search volume' buttons highlighted]")
- Callout boxes or bold text for pro tips specific to {{industry}}
```
