The Golden Hour Car Mirror Selfie Generator is a free AI prompt that creates ultra-realistic mirror selfie photographs captured in car side mirrors during golden hour for content creators, lifestyle brands, and social media marketers. This golden hour mirror selfie prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 8k-resolution images of subjects leaning on car windows, phone in hand, with accurate mirror reflections and warm evening lighting. The prompt specifies optical correctness, natural bokeh effects, and cinematic composition to deliver Instagram-worthy portraits that capture the authentic feel of spontaneous outdoor photography.
