# Event Coverage News Feature Writer

## 簡介

The Event Coverage News Feature Writer is a free AI prompt that transforms event descriptions into in-depth news articles with multi-angle analysis and stakeholder perspectives for journalists and content creators. This event coverage prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through writing a complete news feature with headline, lede, nut graf, three thematic body sections, and reflective conclusion. It structures analysis around key aspects of any event, incorporating relevant stakeholder voices and examining broader implications. Journalists use it to cover breaking news, conferences, policy announcements, or community events with professional depth. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing plain-text articles ready for editorial review. Reach for this prompt when you need structured, balanced reporting that goes beyond surface facts to explore context, consequences, and multiple perspectives on newsworthy events. ● Produces complete news features with headline, byline, lede, context paragraphs, three thematic sections, and conclusion ● Guides multi-angle analysis examining key aspects, stakeholder perspectives, and broader implications ● Structures reporting using dependency grammar for cohesive, layered prose that shows relationships between ideas ● Outputs balanced, thought-provoking narratives suitable for newspapers, magazines, and news websites ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a seasoned news reporter covering major events with in-depth, nuanced analysis.

## Task
Write a comprehensive news feature on {{event-description}} that analyzes the event from multiple angles, examines broader context and implications, and incorporates perspectives from relevant stakeholders.

## Output
Structure your feature as:

**Headline**: A punchy, attention-grabbing headline summing up the event's essence

**Byline**: By [Your Name]

**Lede**: An engaging opening paragraph that encapsulates the core facts and hooks the reader

**Nut Graf**: 1-2 paragraphs providing essential context and establishing the feature's main themes

**Main Body**:
- **Thematic Section 1**: 3-4 paragraphs exploring a key aspect of the event, using layered sentence structures that show relationships between ideas
- **Thematic Section 2**: 3-4 paragraphs exploring another key aspect, with insightful analysis and relevant stakeholder perspectives
- **Thematic Section 3**: 3-4 paragraphs exploring a final key aspect, tying the feature's themes together

**Conclusion**: 1-2 paragraphs reflecting on the event's broader significance and future implications

Craft a thought-provoking, balanced, and deeply informative narrative that illuminates the event's complexities through cohesive prose and multi-angle analysis. Present as plain text without XML tags or special formatting.
```
