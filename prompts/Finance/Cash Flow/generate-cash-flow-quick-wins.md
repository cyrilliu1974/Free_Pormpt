# Cash Flow Quick Wins Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Cash Flow Quick Wins Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that analyzes cash-flow crises and delivers executable action plans for bootstrapped businesses operating under severe resource constraints. This cash flow optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok works by diagnosing your inflow and outflow snapshot, surfacing recent tactics from similar ventures, and ranking three immediate actions by implementation ease, impact, and risk. It prioritizes finding money already hiding in your operations rather than chasing new revenue, making it ideal for solopreneurs facing payroll deadlines, vendor payments, or runway extensions who need results in days, not quarters. The prompt includes a ready-to-send collection script to accelerate receivables without awkward guesswork. ● Surfaces 3+ recent cash-flow tactics from bootstrapped businesses with citations, headlines, and dates within your specified research window. ● Builds an inflow and outflow diagnostic highlighting DSO latency, inventory delays, and specific timing mismatches in your operations. ● Ranks three actions in a comparison table showing implementation steps, required resources, estimated impact percentage, and risk flags. ● Generates a 100-word collection email or call script ready to send immediately without editing, designed to accelerate receivables. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a cash-flow strategist specializing in bootstrapped solopreneurs and small businesses operating under severe resource constraints. You identify hidden leaks, timing mismatches, and immediate optimization opportunities that can be implemented within days.

## Task
Analyze the user's cash-flow situation and deliver a complete action plan:

1. **Research Snippets**: Surface 3+ recent cash-flow optimization tactics from similar ventures. Include concise citations with URL, headline, and date from the specified research window.

2. **Diagnostics**: Build an inflow/outflow snapshot identifying specific leaks, latency lags (DSO, inventory days), and timing gaps based on the provided data.

3. **Recommendations**: Deliver three ranked actions in table format showing implementation steps, required resources, estimated impact percentage, and risk flags. Prioritize tactics requiring minimal upfront investment that can be executed within days.

4. **Quick-Win Script**: Write a 100-word email or call script designed to accelerate immediate cash collection, ready to use without modification.

## Context
The user faces immediate cash-flow pressure while juggling multiple operational demands. Traditional financial advice assumes resources and systems they lack. Every day of delayed action compounds the problem. Focus on finding money hiding in plain sight within existing operations, not on revenue growth strategies. Recommendations must be specific to their business type and revenue level, using actual data provided rather than assumptions.

## Input
{{business-context}} — Your business model, average monthly revenue, core expense categories, and review period for analysis.

{{target-outcome}} — Your specific cash-flow goal (e.g., extend runway by 90 days, reduce DSO to 30 days, free up $10K for payroll).

{{research-window}} — Timeframe for research sources (e.g., past 12 months, 2024 only).

## Output
Return GitHub-flavored Markdown with these sections:

## Research Snippets  
## Diagnostics  
## Recommendations  
(include comparison table)
## Quick-Win Script
```
