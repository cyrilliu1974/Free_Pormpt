# Service Renewal Proposal Generator for Contracts

## 簡介

The Service Renewal Proposal Generator for Contracts is a free AI prompt that produces client-focused renewal proposals for businesses seeking to extend service agreements. This service renewal proposal prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete proposal document divided into seven sections: executive summary, accomplishments with measurable ROI, a four-column table of proposed terms (service offering, scope, KPIs, and investment), additional benefits with checkmarks, a numbered partnership roadmap with timelines, a call-to-action requesting a renewal meeting, and an appendix for supporting materials. You provide client name, previous contract period, key achievements, ROI data, and proposed renewal terms - pricing, duration, and any service changes - and the prompt structures this information into persuasive, client-centered copy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to demonstrate value delivered and present compelling reasons for contract continuation. Business development teams, account managers, and consultants reach for this prompt when a service contract is approaching expiration and they need a professional proposal that quantifies past impact and outlines future terms. ● Structures proposals with logical flow from executive summary through call-to-action and appendix. ● Highlights ROI and achievements using data, metrics, and concrete examples in a dedicated section. ● Presents renewal terms as a clear four-column table covering service scope, KPIs, and investment. ● Formats additional benefits with checkmarks and roadmap milestones as a numbered list for easy scanning. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business proposal writer specializing in service agreement renewals. Your task is to craft a persuasive renewal proposal that demonstrates value delivered during the previous contract period and presents compelling terms for continuation.

## Task

Write a comprehensive service agreement renewal proposal tailored to the client's history and future needs.

## Context

{{client-and-contract-details}}

Include: client name, previous contract period, key achievements and ROI delivered, and proposed renewal terms (pricing, duration, service changes).

## Output

Structure the proposal with logical flow using these sections:

**1. Executive Summary**
Provide a concise overview of the successful partnership and primary reasons for renewal.

**2. Accomplishments and Value Delivered**
- Highlight specific achievements and ROI from the previous contract
- Demonstrate measurable impact on the client's business
- Use data, metrics, and concrete examples to quantify value

**3. Proposed Terms for Renewal**
Present as a 4-column table:

| Service Offering | Scope of Work | Metrics and KPIs | Investment |
|-----------------|---------------|------------------|------------|

**4. Additional Benefits**
Use checkmarks (✓) to list enhanced benefits and their value to the client.

**5. Partnership Roadmap**
Use a numbered list outlining key milestones with timelines.

**6. Call-to-Action**
Reiterate the value proposition and request a meeting to finalize renewal.

**7. Appendix**
Include supporting case studies, testimonials, or additional data.

**Requirements:**
- Use persuasive, client-focused language
- Tailor content to the client's specific needs and goals
- Avoid unexplained jargon
- Ensure professional presentation and clarity throughout
```
