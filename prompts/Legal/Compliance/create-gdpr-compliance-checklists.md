# GDPR Compliance Checklist Generator for Organizations

## 簡介

The GDPR Compliance Checklist Generator for Organizations is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, role-specific checklists covering all core GDPR principles and multi-jurisdictional privacy requirements for businesses of any size. This GDPR compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms your organization context into structured checklists spanning lawful processing (Article 6), consent management (Article 7), data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation, security measures (Article 32), and accountability (Article 5(2)). It generates actionable verification steps for Executive Leadership, Data Protection Officers, IT Security, HR, Marketing, and Legal teams, covering cross-border data transfers (Chapter V), data subject rights (Chapter III), privacy impact assessment triggers (Article 35), third-party processor agreements, breach detection procedures, and retention schedules. Organizations use it to prepare for audits, onboard new team members to privacy protocols, and identify compliance gaps before they become regulatory issues. Reach for this prompt when you need role-based clarity on GDPR responsibilities, are integrating local privacy laws alongside European regulation, or must document your data protection processes for stakeholders. ● Organizes compliance tasks by role (DPO, IT Security, HR, Marketing, Legal, Executive Leadership) with role-specific verification steps. ● Covers all Chapter III data subject rights, Chapter V cross-border transfer rules, and Article 35 privacy impact assessment triggers. ● Includes breach detection and response procedures, third-party processor agreement checkpoints, and retention schedule verification. ● Integrates local privacy law requirements alongside GDPR to ensure complete regulatory coverage across jurisdictions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a data protection compliance specialist with deep expertise in GDPR implementation, breach response, and multi-jurisdictional privacy regulation.

## Task
Create comprehensive, role-based GDPR compliance checklists tailored to the organization described below. Cover all core principles: lawful processing (Article 6), consent management (Article 7), data minimization (Article 5), purpose limitation, accuracy, storage limitation, security measures (Article 32), and accountability (Article 5(2)). Include specific checkpoints for cross-border data transfers (Chapter V), data subject rights (Chapter III), and privacy impact assessment triggers (Article 35).

## Context
{{organization-context}}

Structure the checklists by organizational role: Executive Leadership, Data Protection Officer, IT Security, HR, Marketing, and Legal. For each role, provide actionable verification steps covering:
- Data handling processes and documentation requirements
- Third-party data transfers and processor agreements
- Storage methods and retention schedules
- Breach detection and response procedures
- Integration of local privacy laws alongside GDPR

Each checkpoint should include specific verification criteria to confirm compliance.

## Output
Deliver the checklists in detailed bullet-point format, organized by role. Prioritize clarity and actionability so each stakeholder understands their responsibilities and can verify completion. Flag any high-risk gaps given the organization's maturity level and processing activities.
```
