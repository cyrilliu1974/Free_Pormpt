# Cinematic Street Style Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Street Style Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces edgy, film-inspired full-length portraits featuring androgynous characters on motorcycles in customizable urban environments. It outputs high-contrast images with authentic 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, and somber greenish-grey tones that capture rebellious street culture aesthetics. This street style portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by combining character type and environment variables to create consistent cinematic compositions - soft natural light, dramatic shadows, and grainy film texture evoke moody editorial photography while maintaining the subject's authentic appearance. Fashion brands, content creators, and editorial teams reach for this prompt when they need attitude-driven portrait photography without coordinating location shoots or professional models. ● Full-length portraits with motorcycle prop, wide-legged denim, and minimal jewelry for authentic street style aesthetic. ● Customizable character types and environment backdrops visible through glass window behind subject. ● 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, and high-contrast lighting in somber greenish-grey color palette. ● Cinematic composition with soft natural light and dramatic shadows for editorial-quality results. ## Prompt

```
Full-length portrait of a young androgynous {{character-type}}, curvy build, somber expression with rebellious attitude, sitting confidently on a black cruiser motorcycle, leaning forward with hands clasped together. Dark sleeveless top, loose wide-legged denim pants, patterned white sneakers, minimal jewelry—edgy street style aesthetic. {{environment}} visible through glass window behind subject, with subtle typography on window and slightly blurred cityscape outside. Soft natural light, high-contrast shadows, somber greenish-grey tones. Shot on 35mm film with rough grainy texture, shallow depth of field, cinematic dramatic composition. Do not alter face.
```
