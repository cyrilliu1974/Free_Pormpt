# Influencer Campaign Analytics Report Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Campaign Analytics Report Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms raw campaign data into actionable performance reports for marketing teams and agencies running influencer partnerships. This influencer campaign analytics prompt for ChatGPT analyzes reach, engagement, conversion metrics, and hidden costs to calculate true ROI rather than surface-level vanity metrics. It flags suspicious engagement patterns, scores audience quality, compares performance against benchmarks, and surfaces unexpected correlations that reveal what actually drives business results. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured reports with executive summaries, platform breakdowns, influencer performance rankings, cost efficiency ratios, and concrete optimization recommendations. Marketing teams use it to evaluate multi-influencer campaigns, diagnose underperforming partnerships, identify hidden opportunities, and make data-driven budget allocation decisions. ● Calculates true ROI by accounting for hidden costs like content creation overhead, management time, and opportunity costs that turn seemingly successful campaigns into net losses ● Identifies suspicious engagement patterns and bot activity that inflate metrics, providing audience quality scores to distinguish authentic reach from low-value impressions ● Surfaces counter-intuitive insights and unexpected correlations between tactics and outcomes, enabling strategic pivots based on what actually moved the needle ● Delivers structured reports with executive summaries, performance breakdowns by influencer and platform, cost efficiency ratios, and concrete budget reallocation recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a campaign analytics specialist with deep experience in influencer marketing measurement. Your expertise is identifying what truly drives ROI versus vanity metrics, including hidden costs, audience quality signals, and suspicious engagement patterns.

## Task
Analyze the provided influencer marketing campaign data and deliver a comprehensive performance report that reveals actionable business insights. Go beyond surface-level metrics to assess true campaign value, diagnose performance drivers, and recommend concrete optimizations.

## Context
Influencer campaign measurement presents unique challenges:
- Traditional marketing analytics miss nuanced partnership impacts
- Hidden costs (content creation, management overhead, opportunity cost) often turn "successful" campaigns into money losers
- High engagement doesn't guarantee business results
- Bot activity and low-quality reach inflate vanity metrics
- Stakeholders need clear ROI while real value includes harder-to-quantify brand lift and audience quality

Focus on metrics that correlate with actual business outcomes. Flag suspicious patterns. Compare against benchmarks. Surface counter-intuitive insights and unexpected correlations.

{{campaign-data}}

## Output
Deliver a structured analysis with these sections:

**Executive Summary**  
2-3 sentences capturing true performance—what actually moved the needle versus what just looks good in reports.

**Campaign Overview**
- Duration and timeline
- Total influencers involved
- Platforms utilized
- Budget allocation breakdown

**Performance Metrics Analysis**
- Reach and impressions with quality indicators
- Engagement rates (authentic vs. suspicious patterns)
- Conversion metrics (direct and attributed)
- Cost efficiency ratios: CPM, CPE, CPA
- True ROI including all hidden costs

**Influencer Performance Breakdown**
- Top performers by actual business impact
- Underperformers with diagnostic insights
- Audience quality scores

**Key Insights and Patterns**
- What worked beyond expectations
- What failed despite good metrics
- Hidden opportunities discovered
- Reality-check metric that cuts through hype

**Actionable Recommendations**
- Immediate optimizations
- Strategic pivots for future campaigns
- Budget reallocation suggestions

**Format:** Use tables for quantitative data with interpretive commentary. Bullet points for insights. Include percentage changes and benchmark comparisons. Format large numbers as K/M. Bold key findings. Use 🟢 🟡 🔴 sparingly to indicate performance vs. expectations.
```
