# Sales Pipeline Metrics Health Check Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Pipeline Metrics Health Check Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates sales process performance and identifies concrete improvement opportunities for sales analysts and revenue operations teams. This sales pipeline metrics prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a structured evaluation of three critical dimensions: conversion rates at each funnel stage, lead velocity (the speed at which prospects move through your pipeline), and average deal size. You provide context about your sales process, industry, current performance data, team structure, and challenges; the prompt returns a metrics table comparing current performance against realistic targets, plus a prioritized list of actionable recommendations. Sales teams use it to diagnose bottlenecks, set quarterly goals, prepare board reports, and guide CRM optimization efforts. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from raw CRM data to strategic insight, whether you're preparing a pipeline review, onboarding a new sales leader, or investigating why deal velocity has slowed. ● Surfaces performance gaps across conversion rates, lead velocity, and deal size in a single structured output ● Sets evidence-based targets calibrated to your industry, team size, and sales cycle ● Delivers prioritized, actionable recommendations ranked by revenue impact ● Adapts to any B2B or B2C sales context when you describe your process and challenges ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales analyst evaluating pipeline performance and identifying improvement opportunities.

## Task
Analyze key sales metrics and deliver a structured health check of the sales process. Focus on conversion rates, lead velocity, and average deal size. Set realistic targets for each metric and provide actionable recommendations to optimize the pipeline and drive revenue growth.

## Context
{{sales-context}}

*Describe your sales process, industry, and current performance (include conversion rates, deal flow, revenue trends, team size, sales cycle length, and any challenges you're facing).*

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

1. **Metrics table** with three columns:
   - Metric
   - Current Performance
   - Target

2. **Recommendations** as a bullet-point list of specific, actionable improvements prioritized by impact.
```
