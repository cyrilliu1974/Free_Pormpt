# Fashion Portrait Variation Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Portrait Variation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates consistent portrait images with interchangeable aesthetic styling for fashion brands, influencers, and visual content creators. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a base character - a young woman with wavy dark brown hair, blue-grey eyes, fair skin with rosy undertones, and a grey crop top with silver butterfly waist belt - while allowing you to swap the aesthetic theme via a single variable that changes the hair clip style and overall mood. Use it to test different fashion looks, generate cohesive brand imagery, or build a library of portrait variations without re-engineering the entire scene each time. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable portrait subject whose styling can shift from boho to minimalist, vintage to futuristic, simply by changing one word. ● Produces a fixed character model with defined facial features, hair texture, skin tone, and outfit so every variation stays visually coherent. ● Accepts a single aesthetic-theme variable (e.g. "vintage floral," "cyberpunk," "cottagecore") to adjust the hair clip and overall styling. ● Specifies makeup, accessories, and waist-belt details to ensure polished, fashion-forward output suitable for mood boards and social media. ● Works across multiple image models, giving you flexibility in render style and output resolution. ## Prompt

```
Portrait of a young girl with long, soft wavy dark brown hair styled with a {{aesthetic-theme}} hair clip. Bright blue-grey eyes enhanced by contact lenses, fair smooth skin with a slightly pink undertone and rosy glow, long lashes, light pink makeup. She wears a dark grey spaghetti strap crop top. A silver chain waist belt adorned with dangling butterfly charms accentuates her shapely, clearly defined waist. The composition emphasizes natural facial features and elegant styling.
```
