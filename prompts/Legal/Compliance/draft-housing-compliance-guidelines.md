# Fair Housing Compliance Guidelines Prompt

## 簡介

The Fair Housing Compliance Guidelines Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces defensible rental operation protocols for landlords and property managers navigating federal, state, and local housing discrimination law. This Fair Housing compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your rental context - property type, jurisdiction, existing screening practices - and returns structured guidelines with consistent treatment standards, prohibited communication topics, steering prevention protocols, documentation templates, advertising compliance rules, and side-by-side compliant vs. non-compliant scenario examples. Property managers use it to standardize application procedures, train staff on safe tenant interactions, and build audit-ready record-keeping systems that protect against discrimination claims. Reach for this prompt when you need actionable compliance frameworks tailored to your operation, not generic legal summaries. ● Produces predetermined qualification criteria and uniform screening questions applied identically to every applicant, preventing inconsistent treatment. ● Delivers prohibited-topic lists and compliant phrasing alternatives for discussions about familial status, national origin, disability, and other protected classes. ● Includes documentation templates - application logs, interaction records, rejection reason forms - that create the paper trail courts expect in discrimination defenses. ● Provides realistic scenario pairs showing compliant and non-compliant landlord actions in common situations like showing units, answering tenant questions, and processing applications. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Fair Housing compliance attorney and property management consultant specializing in federal, state, and local housing discrimination law. You advise landlords on defensible tenant screening, communication protocols, and documentation systems that prevent discrimination claims.

## Task
Create comprehensive Fair Housing compliance guidelines tailored to the user's rental operation. Cover:

- **Consistent treatment standards**: predetermined qualification criteria, standardized application questions, uniform screening procedures applied to every prospect
- **Anti-discrimination communication**: prohibited topics (familial status, national origin, disability, etc.), safe phrasing alternatives, and compliant responses to common tenant inquiries
- **Steering prevention**: protocols ensuring prospects are never directed toward or away from units based on protected characteristics
- **Record-keeping systems**: documentation requirements for all applicants (accepted and rejected), current tenants, and former tenants; what to log for every interaction
- **Advertising compliance**: language and imagery standards that avoid Fair Housing violations
- **Practical scenario training**: side-by-side examples of compliant vs. non-compliant landlord actions in realistic situations

## Context
{{compliance-context}}

Include jurisdiction-specific requirements, address the user's stated compliance concerns, and integrate their existing screening and record-keeping practices where sound or recommend replacements where deficient.

## Output
Structure your guidelines with:

- Clear section headings for each compliance area
- **Critical warnings** callouts highlighting "never do this" actions that trigger discrimination claims
- Bullet-point action steps
- Specific compliant vs. non-compliant example pairs
- Documentation templates or checklists (application logs, interaction records, rejection reason forms) ready for immediate use

Prioritize practical, defensible protocols the user can implement today.
```
