# Automated Email Sequence Generator for Sales Funnels

## 簡介

The Automated Email Sequence Generator for Sales Funnels is a free AI prompt that creates stage-specific email sequences to move prospects through awareness, consideration, and decision phases of the sales funnel. This email sequence prompt for ChatGPT produces nine complete emails organized into three triggered sequences, each targeting a different funnel stage. You provide your product or service and target audience, and the prompt generates subject lines and body copy using dependency grammar framework for clear, persuasive messaging. It incorporates storytelling, social proof, and stage-appropriate calls to action while avoiding spam triggers. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for marketers building conversion-focused email automation. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete funnel email architecture - not just one-off messages, but coordinated sequences that address pain points, desires, and objections at each stage of the buyer journey. ● Creates three full sequences (awareness, consideration, decision) with defined triggers and conversion goals for each stage ● Generates nine emails total - three per sequence - with complete subject lines and body copy tailored to audience psychology ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure logical flow and persuasive structure in every message ● Addresses stage-specific objections, incorporates social proof, and avoids generic or spam-flagged language ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist and conversion copywriter specializing in automated funnel sequences.

## Task
Create three distinct automated email sequences that move the target audience through the sales funnel. Each sequence addresses a different funnel stage and includes three emails with subject lines and body copy.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

Structure all writing using dependency grammar framework to create clear, persuasive flow.

## Requirements
- Tailor each sequence to a specific funnel stage (awareness, consideration, or decision)
- Address audience pain points, desires, and objections appropriate to each stage
- Craft attention-grabbing, personalized subject lines
- Write concise, engaging body copy incorporating storytelling, social proof, and clear calls to action
- Avoid generic or spam-triggering language

## Output Format
For each of the three sequences, provide:

**Sequence [N]: [Name]**
Trigger: [What action or event initiates this sequence]
Goal: [Conversion objective for this sequence]

**Email 1:**
Subject: [Subject line]
Body: [Email copy]

**Email 2:**
Subject: [Subject line]
Body: [Email copy]

**Email 3:**
Subject: [Subject line]
Body: [Email copy]

---

Repeat this structure for all three sequences, ensuring progressive movement through the funnel from awareness to conversion.
```
