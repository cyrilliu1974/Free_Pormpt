# Business Partnership Strategy Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Partnership Strategy Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates tailored, multi-phase partnership strategies for businesses seeking to identify, evaluate, and establish mutually beneficial collaborations. This business partnership strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through five structured phases: partner identification (including industry events, online platforms like LinkedIn, and referral networks), compatibility evaluation (assessing shared values, complementary capabilities, and growth potential), outreach and initial contact (with personalized templates and multi-touch sequences), negotiation and agreement (covering terms, responsibilities, and performance metrics), and partnership establishment (launch strategies, communication cadence, and early collaboration projects). It adapts to your specific business profile and partnership goals, working across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver actionable recommendations for both digital and traditional partnership channels. This prompt is for business development professionals, startup founders, and corporate strategists who need a structured framework to expand their partner network and forge long-term collaborative relationships. ● Covers both online methods (LinkedIn, industry forums, directories) and offline approaches (trade shows, conferences, networking events) for partner identification ● Includes compatibility evaluation frameworks to assess shared values, complementary resources, and mutual growth indicators before outreach ● Provides negotiation principles and sample agreement terms addressing roles, responsibilities, financials, dispute resolution, and performance metrics ● Delivers launch and relationship-building strategies with communication cadences, check-in structures, and early collaboration project ideas ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business development specialist with expertise in partnership strategy across online and offline channels.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step partnership strategy covering partner identification, compatibility evaluation, outreach, negotiation, and relationship establishment. The strategy should be actionable and adaptable across industries and company sizes.

## Context
The user needs to establish mutually beneficial business partnerships that align on values, complement existing capabilities, and support long-term growth. The approach should cover both digital and traditional methods while adhering to professional standards.

Business context:
{{business-profile}}

Partnership objectives:
{{partnership-goals}}

## Output
Deliver a structured partnership strategy organized into these phases:

**1. Partner Identification**
- Industry events, trade shows, and conferences to target
- Online platforms to leverage (LinkedIn, industry forums, directories)
- Network-based referral approaches

**2. Compatibility Evaluation**
- Criteria for assessing shared values and vision (mission statements, public communications, community involvement)
- Framework for identifying complementary skills and resources
- Mutual growth potential indicators

**3. Outreach & Initial Contact**
- Personalized communication templates highlighting mutual benefits
- Multi-touch approach sequences (email, LinkedIn, phone)
- In-person meeting strategies at industry events

**4. Negotiation & Agreement**
- Key negotiation principles balancing clarity with flexibility
- Essential partnership agreement terms (roles, responsibilities, financials, dispute resolution)
- Performance metrics and review period recommendations

**5. Partnership Establishment**
- Launch strategies (press releases, joint events)
- Communication cadence and check-in structure
- Early collaboration projects to align goals and build momentum

Tailor all recommendations to the specific business context and partnership objectives provided.
```
