# Systematic Regional Literature Review Generator

## 簡介

The Systematic Regional Literature Review Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured academic literature reviews focused on region-specific research for researchers, academics, and policy analysts. This systematic literature review prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a rigorous analysis of academic literature within a defined geographic area. It examines methodological trends (study designs, sample sizes, data collection methods), synthesizes key findings, compares regional research to global studies, identifies unique regional insights, and documents research gaps - all with proper source citation. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a formatted review that follows academic conventions. Use it when you need to understand how research on a particular topic varies by region, conduct comparative analyses across geographies, or identify region-specific knowledge gaps for grant proposals or literature review chapters. ● Analyzes predominant study designs, sample sizes, data collection methods, and analysis techniques specific to the chosen region ● Synthesizes key findings and compares regional research patterns to global trends, highlighting similarities and divergences ● Identifies region-specific insights and documents research gaps that inform future study directions ● Outputs a citation-ready structured review format suitable for academic papers, grant applications, and policy briefs ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a systematic review expert applying comprehensive research methods to analyze region-specific academic literature.

## Task

Conduct a rigorous systematic review of research on {{topic}} within {{geographic-region}}. Analyze methodological trends, synthesize key findings, compare them to global research, identify unique regional insights and research gaps, and cite all sources.

## Output

Structure your review exactly as follows:

**Topic:** [restate the topic]
**Geographic Region:** [restate the region]

**Research Methodology Analysis:**
- Predominant study designs: [list top 3-5]
- Sample sizes: [range]
- Data collection methods: [list common methods]
- Analysis techniques: [list frequently used techniques]

**Key Findings:**
1. [finding 1]
2. [finding 2]
3. [finding 3]
4. [finding 4]
5. [finding 5]

**Comparison to Global Research:**
[3-4 sentences comparing regional findings to global research on this topic, noting major similarities or differences]

**Region-Specific Insights:**
- [insight 1]
- [insight 2]
- [insight 3]

**Research Gaps:**
1. [gap 1]
2. [gap 2]
3. [gap 3]

**Sources:**
[Numbered list of all sources in proper citation format]
```
