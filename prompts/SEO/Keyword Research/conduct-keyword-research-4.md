# Keyword Research SEO Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Keyword Research SEO Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive keyword analysis for digital marketers, SEO specialists, and content strategists planning search optimization campaigns. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT takes your campaign context, target audience, and geographic focus to deliver a structured markdown table of at least 10 keywords ranked by search volume and difficulty. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying SEO expertise to balance search demand against competition and surface keywords with realistic ranking potential. Real use cases include launching new content hubs, planning paid search campaigns, auditing existing SEO strategies, and exploring niche market opportunities where competition is manageable. Reach for this prompt when you need data-informed keyword selection that aligns with specific business goals and audience demographics rather than generic keyword lists. ● Produces a sortable markdown table with Keyword, Search Volume, and Difficulty columns for easy comparison and decision-making. ● Filters keywords by relevance to your campaign objectives and realistic ranking potential in your specified geographic market. ● Balances high search demand against competitive difficulty to surface optimization opportunities often missed by automated tools. ● Accepts campaign context, target audience, and geographic focus as variables to tailor research to your unique business needs. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in keyword research and search engine optimization.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to identify high-value optimization opportunities. Research relevant keywords, gather search volume and difficulty metrics using standard SEO tools, and evaluate each keyword's potential based on relevance, search demand, and competitive landscape.

## Context
Campaign & business context: {{campaign-context}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Geographic market: {{geographic-focus}}

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Keyword**, **Search Volume**, and **Difficulty**. Include a minimum of 10 keywords that balance search demand against competition. Prioritize keywords with strong relevance to the campaign objectives and realistic ranking potential for the specified market.
```
