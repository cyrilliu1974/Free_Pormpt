# Social Media Comment and Message Response Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Comment and Message Response Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic, on-brand replies to social media interactions for community managers and marketing teams. This social media response prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the sentiment and intent behind comments or messages, then drafts 3-5 reply options that address the customer's core point while maintaining your brand voice and weaving in relevant calls-to-action where appropriate. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, using dependency grammar principles to structure each response around a clear main verb with modifiers and clauses that flow logically - making replies easier to read and more persuasive. Real use cases include handling product questions on Instagram, responding to support inquiries on Facebook, engaging with mentions on Twitter, and converting interested commenters into leads. Reach for this prompt when you need to reply at scale without sacrificing quality, or when you want to train a team on response best practices across platforms. ● Analyzes sentiment and intent before drafting, so replies stay relevant and empathetic. ● Provides 3-5 response variations per interaction, giving you options that match different tones or business goals. ● Includes a linguistic breakdown for each reply, mapping which words depend on which to show how clarity and impact are built. ● Adapts to platform conventions - Instagram warmth, Twitter brevity, Facebook detail - and audience demographics you specify. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media manager optimizing engagement and conversion through strategic comment and message responses.

## Task
Craft quick, effective replies to social media interactions that address core sentiment, maintain brand voice, incorporate calls-to-action where natural, and use clear dependency structures for maximum impact.

## Context

{{sample-interactions}}

## Process
1. Identify the comment's sentiment and intent
2. Address main points while staying on-brand
3. Weave in relevant CTAs or product mentions when appropriate
4. Structure responses with clear grammatical dependencies—anchor each sentence to one main verb with modifiers and subordinate clauses radiating logically from it

## Output
Provide 3-5 sample responses, each followed by a brief analysis showing:
- Which words depend on which (main verb → objects, modifiers)
- How the dependency structure creates clarity and impact
- Why this approach fits the interaction type
```
