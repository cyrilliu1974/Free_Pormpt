# Hierarchical Study Guide Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Hierarchical Study Guide Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms course material into structured, scannable study outlines for students and educators. This study guide prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your course content, identifying the most important concepts and supporting details, then organizing everything into a multi-level outline with main topics, subtopics, and bullet points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a clear hierarchical structure that prioritizes brevity while retaining all critical information. Students use it to condense lecture notes and textbook chapters; educators use it to create review sheets and exam prep materials. Reach for this prompt when you need to distill dense academic content into an easy-to-scan format that supports efficient study sessions and information retention. ● Extracts core concepts, facts, and supporting details while filtering out non-essential information ● Organizes content into Roman numeral main topics, lettered subtopics, and bulleted key points ● Automatically identifies the course or subject from the source material ● Produces outlines optimized for quick review and long-term recall ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert summarizer who extracts key information from course material and organizes it into clear, hierarchical study guides.

## Task
Create a concise study guide from the provided course material by:
1. Identifying the most important concepts, facts, and supporting details
2. Organizing information into a hierarchical outline with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points
3. Prioritizing brevity while capturing all critical information
4. Excluding unnecessary details not directly relevant to core topics

## Input
Course material to summarize:
{{course-material}}

## Output
Deliver a hierarchical outline study guide using this structure:

**Course/Subject:** [Extract from material or state explicitly]

**I. Main Topic 1**
   A. Subtopic 1
      - Key fact or concept
      - Supporting detail
      - Additional information
   B. Subtopic 2
      - Key fact or concept
      - Supporting detail

**II. Main Topic 2**
   A. Subtopic 1
      - Key fact or concept
      - Supporting detail
   B. Subtopic 2
      - Key fact or concept
      - Supporting detail

**III. Main Topic 3**
   A. Subtopic 1
      - Key fact or concept
      - Supporting detail
   B. Subtopic 2
      - Key fact or concept
      - Supporting detail

[Continue with additional main topics as needed]

Ensure the outline is easy to scan and follow for efficient study and review.
```
