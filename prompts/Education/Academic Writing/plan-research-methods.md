# Educational Research Methods Planning Prompt

## 簡介

The Educational Research Methods Planning Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds systematic research plans for educators, graduate students, and instructional designers working on educational studies. This research methods prompt for ChatGPT accepts two variables - your educational topic and research context (audience, timeline, resources) - and returns a markdown table comparing 5–8 methodologies spanning qualitative, quantitative, and mixed approaches. Each entry names the method (survey, case study, experimental design, ethnography, etc.), explains how it works, and justifies its fit for your specific topic and constraints. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing methods that yield actionable insights while noting practical strengths and limitations. Use it when designing a thesis study, planning curriculum evaluation, or scoping a pedagogical intervention. ● Compares 5–8 research methods - surveys, interviews, case studies, experiments, action research, and more - in a three-column markdown table ● Provides concise descriptions of how each methodology works and what data it produces ● Justifies relevance by aligning method strengths and limitations with your specific educational topic, audience, timeline, and available resources ● Balances qualitative, quantitative, and mixed approaches to give you a complete methodological toolkit ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational research methodologist designing a systematic research plan.

## Task
Create a comprehensive research methods plan for the specified educational topic. Present multiple research methods—both qualitative and quantitative—that suit the context, prioritizing those most likely to yield actionable insights. Consider practical constraints and the strengths and limitations of each approach.

## Context
**Educational topic:** {{educational-topic}}

**Research context:** {{research-context}}
(Include target audience, time frame, and available resources)

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:

| Research Method | Description | Relevance |
|----------------|-------------|----------|

For each method:
- **Research Method:** Name the methodology (survey, case study, experimental design, etc.)
- **Description:** Concisely explain what the method entails and how it works
- **Relevance:** Justify why this method suits the educational topic and context, noting key strengths or limitations

Include 5–8 diverse methods spanning qualitative, quantitative, and mixed approaches where applicable.
```
