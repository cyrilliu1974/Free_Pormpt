# Grant Proposal Strategy & Workshop Prompt

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Strategy & Workshop Prompt is a free AI prompt that develops tailored grant writing strategies and detailed section outlines aligned to specific funding organizations' priorities. This grant proposal prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete strategy covering executive summaries, project descriptions, SMART objectives, methodology, budget justification, evaluation plans, funder alignment tactics, competitive differentiators, and common pitfalls to avoid. It accepts your project details, funding goal, target funder, and field of work, then delivers actionable guidance structured around the funder's language and priorities. Grant writers use it to map out winning proposals for foundations, government agencies, and corporate funders across nonprofit, academic, research, and social enterprise sectors. Reach for this prompt when you need strategic direction before drafting a full grant application, when entering a new funding relationship, or when tailoring a proposal template to a specific funder's requirements. ● Produces section-by-section strategy covering executive summary hooks, narrative structure, SMART objectives, evidence-based methodology, budget justification, and evaluation metrics ● Includes funder alignment tactics that mirror the funding organization's language, values, and stated priorities ● Identifies competitive differentiators and positions your proposal against likely competitors in the same funding cycle ● Highlights field-specific and funder-type pitfalls to avoid, reducing rejection risk before you draft ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer specializing in persuasive, fundable proposals.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive grant proposal strategy and outline tailored to the funder's priorities. Provide actionable guidance on each section and how to align the proposal with the funding organization's goals.

## Context
**Project/Organization:** {{project-organization}}
**Funding Goal:** {{funding-goal}}
**Target Funder:** {{target-funder}}
**Field of Work:** {{field-of-work}}

## Output
Provide a bullet-point structured response covering:

- **Executive Summary** – Key elements to include and how to hook the funder immediately
- **Project Description** – Narrative structure that connects the problem, solution, and impact
- **Objectives** – SMART goals framework and how to quantify success
- **Methodology** – Clear, logical sequence of activities with evidence-based approaches
- **Budget** – Cost justification strategies and alignment with funder caps/preferences
- **Evaluation Plan** – Metrics, data collection methods, and reporting timeline
- **Funder Alignment** – Specific tactics to mirror the funding organization's language, priorities, and values
- **Differentiators** – How to position this proposal against likely competition
- **Common Pitfalls** – What to avoid based on this funder type and field

Organize each section as a distinct bullet cluster with 3-5 concrete sub-points.
```
