# Career Progression Path Mapper for Any Role or Industry

## 簡介

The Career Progression Path Mapper for Any Role or Industry is a free AI prompt that charts detailed advancement roadmaps with stage-specific skills, milestones, and timeframes for professionals in any field. This career progression prompt for ChatGPT asks the AI to act as an expert career consultant and produce a multi-stage progression map tailored to your {{role}} and {{industry}}. The output includes 3–5 career stages, each listing the required skills, typical timeframe ranges, and credible sources that back the analysis. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when planning your own advancement, advising clients on realistic career paths, or building onboarding roadmaps for team members. ● Generates 3–5 career stages with specific skill requirements and realistic timeframe ranges for each level. ● Prompts the model to cite credible sources, ensuring the progression advice reflects current industry standards. ● Accepts any {{role}} and {{industry}} pair, making it reusable for technology, healthcare, finance, education, and beyond. ● Outputs a clean, consultant-ready format that can be shared with mentees, clients, or hiring managers. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career consultant analyzing progression paths across industries and roles.

## Task
Map the career progression path for {{role}} in {{industry}}. Identify key milestones, required skills at each stage, and typical timeframes based on industry standards.

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Career Progression Path for [role] in [industry]**

**Career Stage 1: [Stage Name]**
Required Skills:
- Skill 1
- Skill 2
- Skill 3
Typical Timeframe: X–Y years

**Career Stage 2: [Stage Name]**
Required Skills:
- Skill 1
- Skill 2
- Skill 3
Typical Timeframe: X–Y years

**Career Stage 3: [Stage Name]**
Required Skills:
- Skill 1
- Skill 2
- Skill 3
Typical Timeframe: X–Y years

**Career Stage 4: [Stage Name]**
Required Skills:
- Skill 1
- Skill 2
- Skill 3
Typical Timeframe: X–Y years

**Sources:**
1. [Source 1]
2. [Source 2]
3. [Source 3]

Ensure timeframes are realistic ranges and skills are specific to each stage. Include 3–5 credible sources to support your analysis.
```
