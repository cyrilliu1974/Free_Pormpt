# Research Proposal Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Research Proposal Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that produces structured, publication-ready research proposals for students, academics, and institutional researchers across all disciplines. This research proposal prompt for ChatGPT walks you through five core sections: a contextual introduction that frames your research question, a literature review synthesizing existing scholarship and identifying knowledge gaps, a methodology detailing data collection and analysis techniques with ethical considerations, expected outcomes articulating anticipated contributions to your field, and a properly formatted reference list. It adapts to any academic level - undergraduate, master's, or doctoral - and supports every major citation style including APA, MLA, Chicago, and Harvard. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible regardless of your preferred text model. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft a grant application, thesis proposal, or institutional research plan that meets rigorous academic standards. ● Automatically structures proposals with clear section headings and logical transitions between introduction, literature review, methodology, outcomes, and references. ● Tailors tone, depth, and argumentation style to your specified academic level and target audience - whether reviewers, ethics boards, or funding agencies. ● Identifies gaps in existing scholarship and positions your research within broader theoretical frameworks. ● Ensures methodological rigor by prompting you to justify data collection techniques, address validity and reliability, and consider ethical implications. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher specializing in proposal development across disciplines.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, well-structured research proposal that effectively communicates the research plan in clear academic format. The proposal must meet standards for {{academic-level}} work in {{discipline}} and address {{target-audience}}.

## Requirements

**Introduction**
- Provide context establishing the significance of {{research-topic}}
- State the research question or hypothesis clearly
- Articulate the purpose and scope of the investigation

**Literature Review**
- Synthesize relevant studies and theoretical frameworks
- Identify gaps in current knowledge that justify this research
- Demonstrate how this work builds on or challenges existing scholarship

**Methodology**
- Detail data collection methods appropriate for {{research-topic}}
- Specify analysis techniques with justification
- Address validity, reliability, and ethical considerations
- Explain the rationale connecting methods to research questions

**Expected Outcomes**
- Outline anticipated findings and contributions
- Discuss potential impact on {{discipline}}
- Consider practical applications or theoretical implications

**References**
- Compile a comprehensive bibliography in {{citation-style}} format
- Ensure all cited works are relevant and current

## Output
Structure the proposal with clear section headings (Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Expected Outcomes, References). Ensure logical flow between sections, rigorous argumentation, and adherence to academic writing conventions appropriate for {{academic-level}} scholarship.
```
