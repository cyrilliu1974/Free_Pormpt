# Urban Portrait Photography Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Urban Portrait Photography Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates xmiiru-style analog flash portraits with detailed styling, poses, and nighttime urban settings for fashion brands and creative projects. This urban portrait photography prompt for ChatGPT and Midjourney produces eye-level portraits of a subject sitting against a vehicle tire in a dark asphalt setting, captured with analog 35mm flash aesthetics. The prompt specifies athletic-inspired streetwear styling including a cropped hoodie with three-stripe detailing, customizable text, high-waist denim shorts, and chunky boots. It creates moody nighttime compositions with dramatic flash illumination that fashion photographers, streetwear brands, and social media marketers use for authentic urban campaign imagery. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent street-style portrait photography with specific wardrobe details, pose direction, and analog photography mood. ● Generates xmiiru-style analog 35mm flash photography with dramatic subject illumination against dark urban backgrounds. ● Specifies exact pose (sitting against vehicle tire, legs extended, one knee bent, holding head and cigarette) and eye-level camera framing. ● Creates detailed athletic streetwear styling with customizable hoodie text, three-stripe sleeve details, distressed denim, and chunky boots. ● Produces nighttime asphalt scenes with serious or tired subject expressions and direct camera eye contact for authentic street fashion mood. ## Prompt

```
Portrait in xmiiru style: {{subject-description}} sitting against the front tire of {{vehicle}}, legs extended with one knee bent, one hand holding their head, the other holding a cigarette. Serious or slightly tired expression, looking directly at camera. Eye-level framing. Wearing a charcoal crop hoodie with 3 thick Adidas-style stripes on both sleeves, {{text-on-hoodie}}, tight ribbing at cuffs and hem; black high-waist denim shorts with distressed frayed bottom; chunky black boots. Very dark night scene on gray asphalt. Analog 35mm flash photography illuminating the subject against darkness, tire visible in frame.
```
