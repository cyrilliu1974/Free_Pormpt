# Financial Forecast Model Generator for Five Years

## 簡介

The Financial Forecast Model Generator for Five Years is a free AI prompt that creates multi-scenario financial projections with historical analysis, market trends, and strategic planning insights for businesses and finance teams. This financial forecasting prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete five-year model that includes revenue, expense, and profitability projections across base case, optimistic, and conservative scenarios. It analyzes three years of historical performance, incorporates industry growth rates and competitive landscape data, and applies your strategic assumptions about pricing, cost structure, and investment plans. The output includes sensitivity analysis on critical variables, descriptions of data visualizations to communicate trends, and actionable strategic recommendations. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini when preparing investor presentations, annual strategic plans, budget cycles, or evaluating major business decisions that require quantified financial outcomes. ● Produces historical financial analysis, market trend assessments, and five-year annual projections for revenue, expenses, and profitability ● Generates three distinct scenario models with quantified assumptions and financial impact for each case ● Identifies and analyzes the three most critical variables affecting performance through sensitivity analysis ● Delivers descriptions of key data visualizations and actionable strategic recommendations aligned with company objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial analyst and forecasting specialist with deep knowledge of business modeling, accounting principles, and data-driven projections.

## Task
Create a comprehensive five-year financial forecast model that projects revenue, expenses, and profitability. Develop multiple scenarios, conduct sensitivity analysis, and provide data visualizations to support strategic decision-making.

## Context
Analyze the company's historical financial performance (past three years), incorporate relevant market trends (industry growth rate, market size, competitive landscape), and factor in strategic assumptions (pricing strategy, cost structure, investment plans).

## Input Required
{{company-financial-data}}

Include:
- Historical financial data: three years of revenue, expenses, and profitability figures
- Market context: industry growth rate, market size, competitive landscape
- Strategic assumptions: pricing strategy, cost structure, investment plans

## Output
Deliver a structured financial forecast model with:

**Historical Financial Analysis**
- Revenue, expenses, and profitability for the past three years

**Market Trends Assessment**
- Industry growth rate
- Market size
- Competitive landscape analysis

**Strategic Assumptions**
- Pricing strategy
- Cost structure
- Investment plans

**Five-Year Financial Projections**
- Annual revenue forecasts (Years 1-5)
- Annual expense forecasts (Years 1-5)
- Annual profitability forecasts (Years 1-5)

**Scenario Analysis**
Develop three distinct scenarios:
1. Base case scenario
2. Optimistic scenario
3. Conservative scenario

For each scenario, describe assumptions and quantify financial impact.

**Sensitivity Analysis**
Identify and analyze the three most critical variables that impact financial performance, quantifying their influence on outcomes.

**Data Visualizations**
Describe three key charts or graphs that effectively communicate:
- Revenue and profitability trends
- Scenario comparisons
- Sensitivity analysis results

**Key Insights and Recommendations**
Provide actionable strategic recommendations based on the forecast model, highlighting risks, opportunities, and decision points aligned with company objectives.
```
