# Hybrid Workforce Policy Generator

## 簡介

The Hybrid Workforce Policy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive hybrid work policy documents for organizations managing distributed teams. This hybrid workforce policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured, implementable document covering workspace allocation, schedule frameworks, communication standards, meeting protocols, performance measurement, and inclusion practices. You provide your organization context and collaboration technology stack, and the prompt delivers a complete policy with clear sections addressing office capacity utilization, desk booking systems, in-office and remote work patterns, core collaboration hours, response time expectations, hybrid meeting best practices, and location-independent evaluation criteria. HR leaders and organizational development teams use it to establish flexibility within clear boundaries that support both individual productivity and team cohesion. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete hybrid work framework that addresses the practical challenges of distributed collaboration while maintaining accountability and equity across work environments. ● Covers workspace allocation, work schedule frameworks, communication standards, and meeting protocols in a single coherent document. ● Establishes performance measurement criteria and accountability measures that work independently of employee location. ● Includes inclusion practices ensuring equity between remote and in-office team members. ● Provides an implementation plan with timelines, responsibility assignments, success metrics, and policy review cadence. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert organizational development consultant specializing in hybrid workforce policy design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive hybrid work policy that balances employee autonomy with organizational accountability, ensures seamless collaboration, and promotes inclusion across all work environments. Deliver the policy as a structured, implementable document.

## Context
Successful hybrid policies address autonomy, communication, and inclusion while maintaining clear performance standards and operational efficiency. The policy must cover in-office requirements, virtual collaboration protocols, workspace allocation, meeting standards, and measurable performance expectations. It should provide flexibility within clear boundaries that support both individual productivity and team cohesion.

Use the following organizational details:
{{organization-context}}

{{collaboration-technology}}

## Output
Structure the policy document with clear sections and subsections covering:

- **Workspace Allocation**: Office capacity utilization, desk booking systems, space constraints
- **Work Schedule Framework**: In-office/remote patterns, core collaboration hours, flexibility boundaries
- **Communication Standards**: Response time expectations, tool usage guidelines, asynchronous vs synchronous norms
- **Meeting Protocols**: Hybrid meeting best practices, inclusion safeguards, recording policies
- **Performance Measurement**: KPIs, accountability measures, evaluation criteria independent of location
- **Inclusion Practices**: Ensuring equity between remote and in-office employees
- **Implementation Plan**: Timelines, responsibility assignments, success metrics, review cadence

Format all guidelines as bullet points for clarity. Include provisions for policy adaptation based on organizational feedback and evolving needs.
```
