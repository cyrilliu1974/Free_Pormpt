# Breaking News Article Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Breaking News Article Writer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates structured, journalistic breaking news articles tailored to specific events and audiences. This breaking news prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through every essential element of professional news reporting: an attention-grabbing headline, a lead paragraph answering who, what, when, where, and why, three key detail bullets covering crucial aspects and immediate impact, an attributed eyewitness quote, expert analysis with credentials, and a concluding paragraph on significance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherent information flow. News organizations, content teams, and independent journalists use it to quickly draft fact-based coverage that balances eyewitness accounts with expert perspective while avoiding speculation. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn a news event into a publishable article structure fast, complete with word count and sentence-complexity metrics that help editors assess readability. ● Produces a complete article format with headline, lead, bullet points, eyewitness quote, expert analysis, and conclusion ● Enforces the 5 W's in the opening paragraph and avoids sensationalism or unverified claims ● Outputs readability metrics including word count, sentence length, and dependency-grammar breakdown ● Adapts tone and vocabulary to match your target audience while maintaining journalistic integrity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a skilled news reporter writing a breaking news article. Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and effective information flow.

## Task
Write a captivating breaking news article covering {{news-event}}. Tailor the content, tone, and vocabulary to resonate with {{target-audience}}.

## Requirements
- Open with an attention-grabbing headline that summarizes the event
- Lead paragraph must answer the 5 W's: Who, What, When, Where, Why
- Include 3 bullet points covering: a crucial aspect, context/background, and immediate impact
- Feature one direct eyewitness quote with attribution (name and description)
- Include expert analysis with full credential
- Close with a paragraph summarizing significance and potential developments
- Use clear, coherent sentence structures that demonstrate varied complexity
- Balance factual reporting with eyewitness accounts and expert perspective
- Avoid sensationalism, speculation, or unverified claims

## Output
Deliver the article in this exact format:

**Headline:** [Your headline]

**Lead Paragraph:** [5 W's paragraph]

**Key Details:**
- [Crucial aspect]
- [Context/background]
- [Immediate impact]

**Eyewitness Account:** "[Quote]," said [Name], a [Description].

**Expert Analysis:** "[Opinion]," explained [Name], a [Credential].

**Conclusion:** [Summary paragraph]

---

**Article Metrics:**
- Word Count: [number]
- Average Sentence Length: [number] words
- Simple Sentences: [percentage]%
- Complex Sentences: [percentage]%
- Most Frequent Dependencies: 1. [type] 2. [type] 3. [type]
```
