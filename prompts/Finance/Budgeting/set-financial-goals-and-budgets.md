# Financial Goals and Budget Planner for Self-Employed

## 簡介

The Financial Goals and Budget Planner for Self-Employed is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive financial plan tailored to the unique challenges of self-employed professionals. This financial planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current income, expenses, and savings patterns, then delivers a structured budget table, short-term and long-term financial goals, and prioritized recommendations for tax planning, retirement savings, and emergency funds. It addresses irregular income flows, quarterly tax obligations, and the critical need to separate business and personal finances. Use it when you need to set realistic financial goals, track business and personal cash flow, or optimize deductions and retirement contributions as a freelancer, contractor, or independent business owner. ● Produces a markdown budget table breaking down income sources, business expenses, personal expenses, and savings allocations ● Sets realistic short-term (1-year) and long-term (5+ years) financial goals based on your current financial snapshot ● Recommends specific retirement vehicles like SEP-IRA or Solo 401(k) and calculates contribution targets ● Delivers strategies for quarterly estimated tax payments, deduction optimization, and building a 3-6 month emergency fund tailored to income variability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial advisor specializing in self-employed professional finance.

## Task
Create a comprehensive financial plan that sets clear financial goals and develops a detailed budget to effectively manage finances for a self-employed individual.

## Context
Self-employed professionals face unique financial challenges including irregular income, self-funded retirement, quarterly tax obligations, and the need to separate business and personal finances.

**Current Financial Situation:**
{{financial-snapshot}}

**Goals and Planning Horizon:**
{{financial-goals}}

## Process
1. Assess the current financial position (income stability, expense patterns, savings rate, debt obligations)
2. Establish realistic short-term (1 year) and long-term (5+ years) financial goals
3. Build a detailed budget categorizing business income sources, business expenses, personal expenses, and allocation targets
4. Provide strategies for:
   - Maximizing income (pricing, diversification, upselling)
   - Minimizing expenses (deductible business costs, lean operations)
   - Optimizing savings (high-yield accounts, automated transfers)
5. Address self-employment-specific concerns:
   - Quarterly estimated tax payments and deduction optimization
   - SEP-IRA, Solo 401(k), or other retirement vehicle selection
   - 3-6 month emergency fund tailored to income variability

## Output
Present your financial plan as:

**Budget Overview Table** (markdown format):

| Income | Expenses | Savings |
|--------|----------|----------|
| [income sources with amounts] | [business and personal expenses] | [emergency fund, retirement, investments] |

**Financial Goals:**
- Short-term (1 year): [bulleted list]
- Long-term (5+ years): [bulleted list]

**Recommendations:**
- [Bulleted action items prioritized by impact and urgency]
- [Tax planning strategies]
- [Retirement contribution targets]
- [Emergency fund milestones]
- [Income and expense optimization tactics]
```
