# Restaurant Menu Design Prompt for AI Image Generators

## 簡介

The Restaurant Menu Design Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that creates polished menu layouts featuring illustrated food items, elegant serif typography, and hyper-realistic rendering for restaurants, cafés, and food businesses. This restaurant menu design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 4k resolution menu mockups with clean, legible text layouts and customizable food illustrations. You specify the food item (dish, dessert, or beverage), choose a style aesthetic (vintage, modern, rustic, minimalist), and define a color scheme that matches your brand identity. The prompt renders hyper-realistic illustrations paired with elegant serif fonts in a 2:3 aspect ratio, ideal for printed menus and digital displays. Use it to visualize menu redesigns, pitch concepts to clients, or generate portfolio pieces for hospitality design projects. ● Produces hyper-realistic food illustrations that showcase menu items in appetizing detail ● Pairs illustrated dishes with elegant serif typography for a polished, upscale presentation ● Supports any style aesthetic and color scheme to align with restaurant branding ● Outputs 4k resolution images in 2:3 aspect ratio, optimized for standard menu formats ## Prompt
