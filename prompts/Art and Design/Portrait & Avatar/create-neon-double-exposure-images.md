# Neon Double Exposure Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Neon Double Exposure Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates ethereal double-exposure portraits blending human subjects with neon-lit urban landscapes and flowing light trails. This neon double exposure portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images featuring a woman with curly dark hair and soft freckles merged with glowing cityscapes, using warm rim lighting, eye-level framing, and a 50mm lens effect for shallow depth. The prompt outputs emotionally charged, dreamlike compositions in 3:2 aspect ratio with controlled stylization and moderate chaos parameters, ideal for artists creating atmospheric character portraits, designers building mood boards for urban fantasy projects, or content creators producing striking visuals that marry human emotion with city energy. ● Blends portrait subjects with neon cityscapes and light trails in a single ethereal composition ● Produces warm rim lighting and shallow depth-of-field effects through 50mm lens simulation ● Delivers dreamy, emotional mood with eye-level framing at 3:2 aspect ratio ● Uses optimized Midjourney parameters (--chaos 15, --quality 2, --stylize 80) for consistent, artistic results ## Prompt
