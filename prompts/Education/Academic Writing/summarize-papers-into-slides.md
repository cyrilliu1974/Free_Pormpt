# Research Paper to Slides Converter Prompt

## 簡介

The Research Paper to Slides Converter Prompt is a free AI prompt that distills academic research papers into clear, logically structured presentation decks for researchers, educators, and students. Paste your paper content, specify the field of study and target audience, and the prompt generates a complete slide-by-slide breakdown - each with a title, main bullet points, suggested visuals (charts, diagrams, images), and speaker notes. This research paper to slides prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok typically produces 8-12 slides covering introduction, methodology, findings, and conclusions, with vocabulary and depth tailored to your audience's expertise level. Reach for this prompt when you need to present journal articles at conferences, explain thesis work to non-specialists, or create lecture materials from primary literature. ● Outputs slide number, title, bullet-point content, visual suggestions, and speaker notes for each slide. ● Organizes paper content into a logical narrative arc - introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusions. ● Adjusts technical depth and terminology to match your target audience, from undergraduates to domain experts. ● Recommends specific visual elements (flow charts, data graphs, concept diagrams) to reinforce each slide's message. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher and presentation specialist who transforms research papers into clear, engaging slide decks.

## Task
Create a comprehensive slide deck that distills the given academic paper into its core ideas, arguments, and findings. Organize the content logically, identify key points, and structure each slide for maximum clarity and impact.

## Context
**Paper content:** {{paper-content}}

**Field of study:** {{field-of-study}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Output
Provide a structured slide-by-slide breakdown. For each slide, specify:
- Slide number and title
- Main content points (bullet format where appropriate)
- Suggested visual elements (charts, diagrams, images)
- Speaker notes or key explanations

Aim for 8-12 slides unless otherwise needed. Ensure the deck flows logically from introduction through methodology, findings, and conclusions. Tailor vocabulary and depth to the target audience's expertise level.
```
