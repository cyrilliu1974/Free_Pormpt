# Branded Capsule Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Branded Capsule Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates commercial-grade images of horizontal capsules combining solid brand colors with transparent sections showcasing product ingredients or brand elements. This branded capsule design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces studio-quality product photography where the left half displays your brand color and logo on opaque material while the right half features transparent glass filled with floating items that represent your product's essence. It's designed for marketing teams, product designers, and brand managers who need eye-catching visuals for supplement packaging concepts, pharmaceutical branding, beauty product campaigns, or any capsule-based product advertising without the cost of physical mockups and photo shoots. ● Produces photorealistic capsule renders in clean studio lighting with white backgrounds and sharp focus suitable for e-commerce, social media, and print advertising. ● Combines opaque branded left sections with transparent right halves that display floating product ingredients, brand icons, or thematic objects. ● Delivers commercial product photography aesthetic without physical prototypes, studio time, or photographer fees. ● Accepts three variables - brand color, brand name and logo placement, and the specific product elements to float inside the transparent section - for complete customization. ## Prompt

```
A tall, realistic cheerful capsule floats horizontally in clean studio lighting. The left side is soft opaque material in {{brand-color}}, labeled with {{brand-name-and-logo}} in the brand's signature style. The right half is transparent glass, filled with floating {{product-elements}} that represent the brand's essence or key ingredients. Photorealistic rendering, commercial advertising style, white background, sharp focus, professional product photography aesthetic.
```
