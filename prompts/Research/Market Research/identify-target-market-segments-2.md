# Target Market Segmentation Analyzer

## 簡介

The Target Market Segmentation Analyzer is a free AI prompt that identifies and profiles distinct customer segments for businesses seeking strategic market positioning. This target market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert market analyst, researching your industry landscape, consumer behavior patterns, demographics, psychographics, and buying habits to output a structured markdown table. Each row describes a customer segment alongside its specific needs and pain points, enabling you to tailor product development and marketing campaigns. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context - your business type, goals, current customer base, known target markets, and main competitors - to produce actionable insights. Reach for this prompt when launching a new product, refining your positioning, or entering unfamiliar markets where clear segmentation drives competitive advantage. ● Identifies at least three distinct customer segments with demographic and psychographic profiles ● Maps specific needs and pain points for each segment to reveal unmet opportunities ● Outputs findings in a markdown table that supports immediate strategic planning and targeting ● Incorporates industry trends, competitive positioning, and consumer behavior patterns into every analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in customer segmentation and competitive positioning.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive market analysis to identify at least three distinct target market segments. For each segment, determine their specific needs and pain points. Base your analysis on industry landscape research, consumer behavior patterns, demographics, psychographics, buying habits, and current market trends.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

(Include your business type, goals, current customer base if applicable, known target markets, and main competitors)

## Output
Present your findings in a markdown table with three columns:

| Customer Segment | Needs | Pain Points |
|-----------------|-------|-------------|
| [segment name and demographic profile] | [specific requirements and desires] | [challenges and frustrations] |

Provide data-driven insights that support actionable business strategy decisions. Each segment should be clearly differentiated with specific characteristics that enable targeted marketing and product development.
```
