# Lifestyle Portrait Variation Generator

## 簡介

The Lifestyle Portrait Variation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multiple lifestyle images of the same person with identical facial features across different scenes and activities. This lifestyle portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana recreates a subject with exact facial accuracy, bone structure, skin tone, and hairstyle while changing clothing, poses, and environments. It produces smartphone-style vertical portraits with warm indoor lighting, realistic skin texture, and slight background blur - ideal for content creators building visual libraries, marketers developing campaigns with a consistent model, or brands needing multiple lifestyle shots without additional photo sessions. The prompt specifies eye-level composition, natural shadows, and authentic photography aesthetics to avoid artificial or cartoon-like results. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive set of lifestyle images featuring one recognizable individual in varied scenarios without scheduling multiple shoots. ● Preserves 100% facial accuracy, bone structure, and natural expression while changing subject description variables like outfit and activity. ● Produces vertical smartphone-style portraits with warm soft lighting, realistic skin texture, and authentic lifestyle photography quality. ● Customizes setting and subject description through two simple variables without losing identity consistency. ● Includes technical guardrails to avoid altered facial features, cartoon effects, plastic skin, and distorted anatomy. ## Prompt

```
Recreate the woman exactly as shown in the reference image with 100% identical facial features, bone structure, skin tone, and natural expression. Dark brown hair styled in a soft low updo with loose face-framing strands on each side.

{{subject-description}}

Environment: {{setting}}

Smartphone photo, vertical orientation, eye-level shot with slight forward tilt. Warm soft indoor lighting with natural shadows, sharp focus on the woman with slight background blur. Clean, realistic, high-detail portrait with authentic skin texture—warm, cheerful, natural lifestyle moment.

Avoid: altered facial features, different hairstyle, cartoon effects, plastic skin, unnatural lighting, extra limbs, distorted hands.
```
