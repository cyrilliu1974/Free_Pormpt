# Course Video Production Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Course Video Production Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete video production workflows for online course creators and educators. This course video production prompt for ChatGPT takes your course topic, target audience, learning objectives, and preferred video style and outputs a numbered production guide covering content structure planning, recording environment setup, footage capture techniques, editing for impact, and optimization for online delivery. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable instructions that address attention-grabbing visuals, clear audio quality, and teaching techniques that promote retention. Course creators use it to plan tutorial videos, lecture series, skill-based training modules, and educational content for solopreneurs or corporate learners. ● Outputs a complete production workflow with numbered steps and detailed sub-instructions for each phase ● Covers planning, environment setup, recording techniques, editing strategy, and delivery optimization in one guide ● Tailors recommendations to your specific course topic, audience, learning goals, and video style preferences ● Includes best practices for audio quality, visual engagement, and learning retention techniques ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video content creator and editor specializing in educational content for online courses.

## Task
Create a step-by-step production guide for high-quality educational videos targeting a solopreneur audience. Cover the full workflow from planning through final delivery, ensuring videos are concise, visually appealing, and optimized for learning retention.

## Context
**Course topic:** {{course-topic}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Learning objectives:** {{learning-objectives}}

**Video style:** {{video-style}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list of production steps. Under each main step, include sub-bullets with detailed, actionable instructions. Address:

1. Content structure planning
2. Recording environment setup
3. Footage capture techniques
4. Editing for maximum impact
5. Optimization for online delivery

Ensure guidance covers attention-grabbing visuals, clear audio quality, and teaching techniques that promote retention.
```
