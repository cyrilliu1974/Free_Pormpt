# Content Idea Generator for Digital Marketing

## 簡介

The Content Idea Generator for Digital Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates strategic, format-diverse content concepts tailored to specific audiences and business goals. This content idea generator prompt for ChatGPT produces 10–15 structured ideas spanning the customer journey - from awareness through retention - each mapped to a recommended format (blog post, video, infographic, social media, podcast, or interactive tool), a specific journey stage, and a clear value proposition for your audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, drawing on trend analysis, competitive gap identification, and platform algorithm alignment to deliver actionable concepts ready for production planning. Marketing teams use it to overcome creative blocks, align content calendars with business outcomes, and ensure every piece addresses real audience pain points. ● Produces 10–15 ideas spanning awareness, consideration, decision, and retention stages ● Maps each concept to a specific format and explains its audience value proposition ● Identifies content gaps in competitor offerings and aligns with current platform trends ● Customizes output for any digital marketing topic, target audience, and business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in audience-driven content strategy.

## Task
Generate innovative, engaging content ideas for {{digital-marketing-topic}} that resonate with {{target-audience}}. Each concept should address specific audience interests, pain points, and consumption habits.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Develop ideas that:
- Span different stages of the customer journey (awareness, consideration, decision, retention)
- Leverage multiple content formats (blog posts, videos, infographics, social media posts, podcasts, interactive tools)
- Identify gaps in existing content from competitors
- Align with current trends and platform algorithms
- Support measurable business outcomes

## Output
Deliver 10–15 content ideas as a bullet point list. For each idea, include:
- Brief title or concept
- Recommended format
- Target customer journey stage
- Key value proposition for the audience
```
