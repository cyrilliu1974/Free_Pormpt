# Regional Sales Workflow Template Generator

## 簡介

The Regional Sales Workflow Template Generator is a free AI prompt that builds customized sales process tables for five global markets - North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and Middle East and Africa. This sales workflow prompt for ChatGPT takes your product description and unique selling proposition, then outputs a markdown table mapping region-specific workflow steps, performance metrics, cultural factors, and implementation priorities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured tables that account for market-appropriate sales stages, regional business norms, and strategic urgency. Use it when expanding into new territories, standardizing multi-region sales operations, or training distributed teams on localized selling approaches. ● Outputs a structured five-region table with numbered workflow steps, bullet-pointed KPIs, and regional business considerations ● Tailors each workflow to cultural nuances - data-driven North American buyers, relationship-focused Asia-Pacific markets, compliance-heavy European processes ● Assigns 1-5 implementation priority scores to help you sequence rollout by strategic urgency ● Accepts two variables - product description and unique selling proposition - so every table reflects your actual offering ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sales process engineer specializing in designing optimized sales workflows across diverse geographical regions.

## Task
Create a comprehensive table of sales workflow templates tailored for five geographical regions: North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and Middle East and Africa.

## Context
{{product-service-description}}

{{unique-selling-proposition}}

## Output
Deliver a table with the following columns:

- **Region**: The geographical market area
- **Workflow Steps**: Numbered sequential stages of the sales process
- **Key Performance Metrics**: Bullet-pointed KPIs that measure success
- **Regional Considerations**: Bullet-pointed cultural, business, and market factors
- **Implementation Priority**: Scale of 1-5 (5 = highest priority)

For each region, ensure:
- Workflow steps reflect market-appropriate sales stages
- Performance metrics align with regional success factors
- Regional considerations capture cultural and business nuances
- Implementation priorities reflect strategic urgency

Format the output as a markdown table with clear, concise information. Use numbered lists for sequential steps and bullet points for multiple metrics or considerations.

**Example structure:**

Region | Workflow Steps | Key Performance Metrics | Regional Considerations | Implementation Priority (1-5)
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---
North America | 1. Prospecting<br>2. Qualification<br>3. Presentation<br>4. Negotiation<br>5. Closing | • Lead conversion rate<br>• Average deal size<br>• Sales cycle length | • Data-driven decision culture<br>• ROI-focused buyers<br>• Streamlined processes | 4
```
