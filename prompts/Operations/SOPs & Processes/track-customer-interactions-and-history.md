# CRM Strategy and Customer Data Management Framework

## 簡介

The CRM Strategy and Customer Data Management Framework is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end customer relationship management strategies for sales teams, customer success managers, and CRM specialists. This customer data management prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete CRM framework tailored to your business context and key performance indicators. It produces a strategic framework explaining how to organize customer data, implement personalization tactics, and design relationship-building processes, plus a structured markdown table with example customer records showing interaction dates, purchase history, and preferences. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your industry, team workflows, data capture processes, segmentation logic, and personalization triggers. Use it when launching a new CRM system, standardizing customer data practices across teams, or redesigning how your organization tracks and acts on customer information. ● Generates a 3-4 paragraph strategic framework covering data organization, personalization tactics, and relationship-building processes specific to your industry ● Creates a structured data table with realistic customer entries demonstrating interaction patterns, purchase behaviors, and preference categories ● Accounts for team workflows, segmentation logic, personalization triggers, and relationship touchpoints appropriate to your customer base ● Delivers actionable guidance for implementing data capture processes and designing effective CRM workflows ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Customer Relationship Management (CRM) specialist focused on optimizing customer data management and relationship building.

## Task
Create a comprehensive CRM strategy that organizes customer data, personalizes the sales experience, and builds long-term relationships. Develop the framework for tracking customer interactions, purchase history, and preferences.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{kpi}}

The strategy should account for team workflows, data capture processes, segmentation logic, personalization triggers, and relationship-building touchpoints appropriate to the industry and customer base.

## Output
Deliver your strategy in two parts:

1. **Strategic Framework** (3-4 paragraphs): Explain how to organize customer data, implement personalization tactics, and design relationship-building processes within the CRM system.

2. **Data Structure Example**: Present a markdown table with 4 columns:
   - Customer Name
   - Last Interaction Date
   - Purchase History
   - Preferences

Include at least 5 example entries that demonstrate realistic data types, detail level, and formatting appropriate to the industry and customer base described above. Entries should illustrate diverse interaction patterns, purchase behaviors, and preference categories that support effective personalization.
```
