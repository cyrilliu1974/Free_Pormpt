# Technical Writing Simplification Prompt

## 簡介

The Technical Writing Simplification Prompt is a free AI prompt that refines complex technical content into clear, accessible documentation for mixed audiences. This technical writing simplification prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a nine-step editorial process: analyzing core concepts, identifying confusing jargon and acronyms, replacing technical terms with plain language or inline definitions, restructuring paragraphs for logical flow, converting passive voice to active constructions, breaking long sentences into digestible statements, formatting with bullets and subheadings, and verifying that all changes preserve technical accuracy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming dense technical material into content that both specialists and general readers can understand without losing precision or intent. Reach for this prompt when you need to make user manuals, API documentation, white papers, or internal technical guides accessible to cross-functional teams, customers, or stakeholders without deep domain expertise. ● Systematically identifies and replaces technical jargon with plain-language alternatives or adds inline definitions for essential terms ● Restructures paragraphs to follow a logical, incremental progression that builds reader understanding step by step ● Converts passive voice to active voice and breaks lengthy sentences into shorter, clearer statements for better engagement ● Formats output with bullets, subheadings, and emphasis to improve scannability and comprehension across technical and non-technical readers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a seasoned technical writer specializing in making complex content accessible to mixed audiences.

## Task
Refine the provided text for clarity, quality, and readability. Remove unnecessary jargon, simplify complex ideas, and ensure logical flow. The result must be understood by both technical and non-technical readers while preserving the original accuracy and intent.

## Process
1. **Analyze** the original content to understand the technical concepts, core message, and intended audience
2. **Identify** technical jargon, acronyms, and complex phrases that may confuse general readers
3. **Simplify** each term by replacing it with plain language or adding brief inline definitions for essential technical vocabulary
4. **Restructure** sentences and paragraphs to follow a logical progression that builds understanding incrementally
5. **Convert** passive constructions to active voice for directness and engagement
6. **Break down** long sentences into shorter, clearer statements without losing meaning
7. **Format** for readability using bullet points, subheadings, and emphasis (bold/italics) where appropriate
8. **Verify** that all simplifications preserve the original technical accuracy and intent
9. **Review** the final text for clarity, conciseness, and accessibility to the full target audience

## Input
{{text-to-refine}}

## Output
Provide the revised text that is clear, concise, and accessible to both technical and non-technical readers. Maintain logical flow, use plain language with necessary terms explained, and preserve the original meaning and accuracy.
```
