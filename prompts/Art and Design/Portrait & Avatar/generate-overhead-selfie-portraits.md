The Overhead Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic casual selfie portraits with exaggerated overhead wide-angle perspective for content creators and personal brands. This overhead selfie prompt for Midjourney produces 1080×1350 iPhone-style images featuring subjects reclining on textured pillows, gazing directly at the camera with customizable expressions, while maintaining exact facial identity and proportions from reference images. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar AI image generators, delivering soft natural indoor lighting, gentle shadows, and the textural realism of smartphone photography.
