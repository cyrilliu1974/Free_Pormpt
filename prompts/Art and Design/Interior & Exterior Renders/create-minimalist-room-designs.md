# Minimalist Room Design Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimalist Room Design Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates architectural-grade interior visualizations with clean lines, natural textures, and contemporary residential styling. This minimalist room design prompt for Midjourney produces eye-level architectural photography of modern interiors featuring sculptural wall elements, integrated fireplaces, and user-defined material palettes. The prompt includes technical photography parameters - 24mm lens, 16:9 aspect ratio, and quality settings optimized for professional output - and draws on the aesthetic of spaces featured in architectural journals. Designers use it to visualize living rooms, bedrooms, offices, or any interior space with a refined, contemporary residential look. Reach for this prompt when you need publication-quality interior renders that balance minimalist restraint with striking architectural detail. ● Customize room type and material palette (wood, stone, concrete, plaster) to match your project vision. ● Produces eye-level architectural photography with 24mm lens perspective for natural spatial representation. ● Includes sculptural wall elements and integrated fireplace as signature design features in every render. ● Outputs 16:9 images suitable for portfolio presentations, client previews, and design exploration. ## Prompt

