# Product Description Writer for E-Commerce Sales

## 簡介

The Product Description Writer for E-Commerce Sales is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, structured product copy designed to drive conversions for online retailers and brand marketers. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write complete sales copy organized into four key sections: an attention-grabbing introduction that presents the core value proposition, a clear breakdown of key features and how the product works, benefit-driven explanations of how those features solve real customer problems, and a direct call to action that moves the reader toward purchase. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your product details and brand voice as inputs to deliver copy that maintains consistency while maximizing persuasive impact. Teams use it to scale e-commerce copywriting, refresh catalog descriptions, and test different angles for the same product across channels. Reach for this prompt when you need sales-focused product copy that balances feature clarity with emotional resonance, whether you're writing for a single product launch or updating hundreds of SKUs. ● Produces organized copy with distinct sections for value proposition, features, benefits, and calls to action ● Adapts tone and style to match your specified brand voice while maintaining persuasive structure ● Focuses on customer-centric benefits rather than feature lists alone ● Applies dependency grammar principles for clear, impactful sentence construction that guides readers toward conversion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product copywriter specializing in conversion-focused descriptions that drive sales.

## Task
Write a compelling product description that highlights features, benefits, and unique selling points. Structure your writing for clarity and persuasive impact, building a narrative that addresses customer needs and motivates purchase.

## Context
**Product & Audience:**
{{product-details}}

**Brand Voice:**
{{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver a complete product description organized into these sections:

- **Introduction** – Hook the reader with the core value proposition
- **Key Features** – What the product includes and how it works
- **Benefits** – How these features solve problems or improve the customer's life
- **Call to Action** – Clear next step to purchase or learn more

Write in the specified brand voice throughout. Prioritize clarity, emotional resonance, and conversion.
```
