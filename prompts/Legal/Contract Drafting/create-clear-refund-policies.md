# Refund Policy Generator for Business

## 簡介

The Refund Policy Generator for Business is a free AI prompt that drafts legally sound, customer-friendly return and refund policies for any business model. This refund policy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business details and produces a structured policy document with fixed sections covering returns eligibility and process, refund methods and timelines, exchange rules, customer and business responsibilities, special circumstances, and contact information. It balances consumer protection law compliance with clear, unambiguous language that protects your business while treating customers fairly. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is built for e-commerce stores, SaaS platforms, service providers, and any business that needs a transparent policy to reduce disputes and build buyer confidence. Use this prompt when launching a new product line, updating outdated terms, or establishing clear expectations that minimize chargebacks and support tickets. ● Covers all critical policy components including eligibility, timelines, item condition, refund methods, and exceptions ● Produces legally aware language that follows consumer protection principles without requiring a lawyer for every draft ● Balances transparency and customer trust with clear business safeguards against abuse ● Outputs a publish-ready document you can post to your website, include in checkout flows, or attach to terms of service ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write business policies. Given details about a business and its products or services, you produce a return and refund policy that is legally sound, clearly written, and fair to both sides.

## Context

{{business-details}}

## Task

Draft a complete return and refund policy for the business described above. The policy must:

- Follow relevant consumer protection laws
- Cover all key aspects of returns, refunds, and exchanges
- Be clear and unambiguous
- Balance business protection with fair customer treatment

## Output

Structure the policy with the sections below. Use plain, direct language throughout.

### Policy Overview

### Returns
- Eligibility
- Process
- Condition of Items
- Exceptions
- Timeline

### Refunds
- Eligibility
- Process
- Refund Method
- Timeline

### Exchanges
- Eligibility
- Process
- Exceptions

### Customer Responsibility

### Business Responsibility

### Special Circumstances

### Contact Information
```
