# HR Compliance Dashboard Design Prompt

## 簡介

The HR Compliance Dashboard Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates predictive compliance monitoring systems for HR teams and compliance officers. This HR compliance dashboard prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a hierarchical three-layer dashboard design with Executive Overview health scores, Risk Analysis predictive metrics by department, and Operational Details drill-down views. It transforms scattered HR data into visual early-warning systems that flag training gaps, policy violations, and regulatory risks before they trigger penalties. Real use cases include building ISO 30414-aligned dashboards for multi-system HR environments, designing mobile-responsive compliance monitors for leadership teams, and creating department-level peer comparison tools that emphasize leading indicators over lagging metrics. Reach for this prompt when your organization needs to move from reactive manual compliance checks to proactive risk intelligence, or when audit findings reveal the need for real-time visibility across multiple compliance areas. ● Component descriptions with widget placement, data relationships, and navigation flow for all three dashboard layers ● KPI definitions including specific formulas for compliance health scores, risk velocity metrics, and predictive risk indices ● Visual standards with color-coded severity thresholds, mobile-responsive layouts, and clear predictive vs. historical indicator distinctions ● Implementation roadmap with phased rollout prioritized by risk mitigation value and 12-month trend analysis for pattern recognition ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a compliance transformation architect specializing in HR analytics. You design predictive dashboards that surface compliance risks before they become violations, combining regulatory frameworks with data-driven early warning systems.

## Task

Design a three-layer HR compliance dashboard that transforms fragmented data into actionable intelligence:

**Executive Overview** – Enterprise-wide compliance health scores, trend indicators, and risk velocity metrics

**Risk Analysis** – Predictive risk scores by department with early warning signals and correlation patterns (e.g., training completion gaps vs. future policy violations)

**Operational Details** – Drill-down views into specific compliance areas with root-cause analysis

Translate complex compliance data into visual stories that non-technical managers can immediately act upon. Focus on leading indicators (what will happen) over lagging indicators (what already happened).

## Context

The organization faces mounting regulatory pressure with HR data scattered across multiple systems. Manual compliance monitoring has failed to prevent violations. Leadership needs real-time visibility into compliance health with enough advance warning to intervene before breaches trigger penalties.

{{organizational-context}}
(Include: employee count, industry/sector, current HR platforms, data refresh frequency, top 3-5 compliance areas requiring monitoring, any recent audit findings or known risk zones)

## Output

Provide a hierarchical dashboard design with:

1. **Component Descriptions** – Visual layout for each layer (widget placement, data relationships, navigation flow)
2. **KPI Definitions** – Specific formulas and calculation methods for compliance health scores, risk indices, and predictive metrics
3. **Visual Standards** – Color-coded severity levels (green/yellow/red with threshold definitions), mobile-responsive layouts, clear distinction between predictive vs. historical indicators
4. **Implementation Roadmap** – Phased rollout plan prioritized by risk mitigation value

Ensure:
- ISO 30414 alignment for credibility
- Department-level peer comparison without blame culture
- Real-time or near-real-time refresh (no static monthly reports)
- 12-month trend analysis for pattern recognition
- Each view answers one critical question clearly (avoid information overload)
- Actionable insights over raw data dumps
```
