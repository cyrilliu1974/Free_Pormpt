# Luxury Villa Grid Advertisement Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Villa Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates elegant 2×2 grid layouts for high-end property marketing campaigns. This luxury real estate prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces four coordinated panels in a single square canvas: a golden-hour architectural exterior, a refined interior living space with natural light, an outdoor lifestyle amenity scene, and a macro detail shot highlighting craftsmanship. Real estate developers, property marketers, and boutique agencies use it to create cohesive visual stories that position villas as premium investments without coordinating multi-day photo shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-quality property advertisements that combine architectural photography aesthetics with lifestyle appeal in one ready-to-publish asset. ● Produces four equal panels in 2×2 layout: hero exterior in natural setting, wide-angle interior with refined finishes, outdoor lifestyle amenity space, and material craftsmanship close-up. ● Customizable landscape-and-setting variable adapts the villa environment to coastal, mountain, desert, or forest contexts while maintaining cohesive visual identity. ● Delivers natural daylight photography with golden-hour exterior lighting, soft interior illumination, and editorial composition that avoids over-processed HDR or artificial rendering. ● Outputs professional architectural and lifestyle imagery with refined neutral palettes, clean modern lines, and minimal branding suitable for websites, social media, and digital advertising. ## Prompt

```
Create an elegant 2×2 grid advertisement showcasing a luxury villa, combining architectural excellence, interior refinement, natural surroundings, and elite lifestyle appeal.

**Grid Structure:** 1:1 square canvas, four equal panels in 2×2 layout.

**Panel 1 – Hero Architectural Exterior**
Wide-angle view of the villa set within {{landscape-and-setting}}. Golden-hour natural daylight illuminating contemporary architecture with clean lines and premium materials. Mood: prestige, scale, timeless luxury.

**Panel 2 – Interior Living Space**
Wide-angle interior showcasing open living area with natural stone, warm wood, and refined finishes. Natural daylight streaming through large windows with soft interior accents. Mood: comfort, refinement, calm.

**Panel 3 – Lifestyle Moment**
Bright daylight scene of villa's outdoor amenity space within {{landscape-and-setting}}—infinity pool terrace, landscaped garden pavilion, or panoramic vista deck. Mood: freedom, well-being, elite lifestyle.

**Panel 4 – Detail & Craftsmanship**
Macro close-up of architectural or interior detail highlighting material quality, clean joinery, and design precision. Shallow depth of field. Mood: craftsmanship, exclusivity.

**Branding:** Minimal, elegant logo placement. Color palette: refined neutrals, architectural tones, exclusive aesthetic.

**Visual Style:** Professional architectural and lifestyle photography. High realism, clean modern composition, editorial aesthetic. Balanced natural exposure with bright highlights and soft architectural shadows.

**Avoid:** Crowded interiors, cheap finishes, distorted architecture, over-processed HDR, cluttered decor, harsh lighting, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
