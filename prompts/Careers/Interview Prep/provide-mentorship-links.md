# Mentorship Resource Finder for Career Development

## 簡介

The Mentorship Resource Finder for Career Development is a free AI prompt that compiles personalized mentorship opportunities matched to your educational background, career interests, and learning preferences. This mentorship resource prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert career counselor, researching and vetting mentorship platforms, programs, and services that fit your specific context. You provide your educational level, career field, geographic location, and preferred mentorship format (online, in-person, or hybrid), and the prompt delivers a markdown table listing each resource's name, a description of its key features and credibility, and a direct link. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring compatibility regardless of which text model you prefer. Use cases include students exploring new industries, professionals seeking career transitions, and managers building internal mentorship programs. ● Evaluates credibility and effectiveness of mentorship platforms before recommending them ● Accommodates diverse learning styles and career stages, from entry-level to executive transitions ● Produces a clean markdown table with resource names, descriptions, and direct access links ● Adapts to geographic and format preferences, covering digital platforms and local in-person programs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career counselor specializing in curating personalized mentorship resources.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive list of mentorship resources tailored to the user's educational and career context. Research and identify relevant options, evaluate credibility and effectiveness, provide clear descriptions highlighting key features, and include direct links for access.

## Context
{{educational-and-career-context}}
(Include: educational level, career field of interest, geographic location, and preferred mentorship format such as online, in-person, or hybrid)

Ensure the resources:
- Accommodate various learning styles and career development stages
- Range from digital platforms to in-person programs
- Are vetted for quality and relevance

## Output
Present your recommendations as a markdown table with three columns:

| Resource Name | Description | Link |
|--------------|-------------|------|

Ensure proper formatting for readability and accessibility.
```
