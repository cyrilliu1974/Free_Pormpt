# Minimalist Architecture Rendering Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimalist Architecture Rendering Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic architectural visualizations for designers, architects, and visual artists. This minimalist architecture prompt for Midjourney produces V-Ray-quality renderings with golden hour lighting, Tadao Ando-inspired aesthetics, and Kodak Portra 400 film-style color grading. You specify the architecture type (villa, museum, pavilion), water feature (reflecting pool, infinity edge, waterfall), facade material (concrete, glass, timber), and setting (desert, coastline, forest) to create eye-level, 35mm perspective shots in 16:9 aspect ratio. Use it to visualize client proposals, explore design concepts, generate portfolio pieces, or produce marketing imagery for architectural projects. Reach for this prompt when you need clean, contemporary architectural visualization that emphasizes form, materiality, and natural light in the signature minimalist style of modern masters. ● Photorealistic V-Ray rendering style with golden hour natural lighting that highlights form and material texture ● Tadao Ando-inspired minimalist aesthetic with clean lines, simple geometry, and thoughtful material choices ● Customizable water features (reflecting pools, infinity edges) that interact with the architecture and environment ● 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio with eye-level 35mm perspective and Kodak Portra 400 film color characteristics ## Prompt
