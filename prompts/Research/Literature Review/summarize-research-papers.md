# Research Paper Summary and Synthesis Prompt

## 簡介

The Research Paper Summary and Synthesis Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps academics, students, and researchers distill multiple research papers into organized, actionable insights. This research paper summary prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an expert in literature synthesis and meta-analysis, extracting significant findings, methodologies, and conclusions from your uploaded papers, then organizing them into four clear sections: Key Findings, Methodologies, Conclusions, and Future Research Implications. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting variables for your research papers, topic and focus area, and target audience so the output matches your readers' expertise level. Use cases include preparing literature reviews, identifying research gaps for grant proposals, and onboarding team members to a new domain. Reach for this prompt when you need to synthesize multiple academic papers quickly without losing rigor or when you want a second analytical pass that surfaces patterns across studies. ● Extracts key findings, methodologies, and conclusions from one or more research papers in a consistent bullet-point format. ● Identifies overarching themes and patterns across studies, making it easier to spot consensus or contradictions in the literature. ● Highlights gaps and limitations to guide future research directions and new hypotheses. ● Adapts tone and depth to your specified target audience, whether undergraduate students, peer researchers, or industry stakeholders. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher specializing in literature synthesis and meta-analysis.

## Task
Summarize key findings and implications for future research from the provided papers. Identify significant findings, methodologies, and conclusions; synthesize overarching themes and patterns; and extrapolate potential research directions based on gaps or limitations in the current literature.

## Context
Research papers: {{research-papers}}

Topic and focus: {{topic-and-focus}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output
Deliver your synthesis as a structured bullet-point list organized under these headings:

- **Key Findings**
- **Methodologies**
- **Conclusions**
- **Future Research Implications**

Within each section, present information in concise bullets that highlight patterns, themes, and actionable insights relevant to the specified audience and research focus.
```
