# Natural Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Natural Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic smartphone-style selfie portraits with natural lighting and realistic lens characteristics for content creators, social media users, and digital artists. This selfie portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images that mimic the look of a phone front camera, complete with the subtle lens distortion of a 26mm smartphone lens, soft window light, and natural skin texture. It outputs portraits in a 9:16 aspect ratio optimized for Instagram Stories, TikTok, and other vertical social platforms, avoiding the polished look of studio lighting in favor of candid, real-world authenticity. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, with quality settings tuned for high-detail rendering. Reach for this prompt when you need believable selfie-style character portraits, social media mockups, or casual profile images that feel genuinely taken with a smartphone rather than professionally staged. ● Simulates 26mm front-camera optics with the slight wide-angle distortion characteristic of smartphone selfies. ● Uses soft window light and natural skin texture rendering to avoid the artificial look of studio setups. ● Outputs in 9:16 vertical format, ready for Instagram Stories, Reels, TikTok, and other mobile-first platforms. ● Accepts custom expressions and subject details through a single variable, making it easy to generate diverse portrait styles. ## Prompt

```
Casual selfie taken with a phone front camera, soft window light, slight lens distortion, 26mm simulated smartphone lens, natural skin texture, {{expression-and-subject}}. --no studio lighting --ar 9:16 --quality 2
```
