# Realistic T-Shirt Mockup Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Realistic T-Shirt Mockup Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional product photography of t-shirts in natural outdoor settings for apparel brands, print-on-demand sellers, and designers. This t-shirt mockup prompt for Midjourney produces photo-realistic images featuring an adult figure seated on grass with a golden retriever, facing away to display the entire back of the shirt. You customize the shirt color and scene setting while the prompt handles lighting, framing, and a Pacific Northwest backdrop of pine trees, a calm lake, and Cascade mountains. The 85mm three-quarter rear view with shallow depth of field delivers clean, product-ready mockups that work across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Use it when you need lifestyle mockups that show garment details in context without hiring a photographer or model. ● Produces centered, well-lit views of the shirt back with customizable color and scene details. ● Includes a golden retriever and wilderness setting for lifestyle context that appeals to outdoor and casual apparel audiences. ● Delivers medium-shot framing at 85mm equivalent with f/2.8 depth of field for product-photography clarity. ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images optimized for e-commerce, social media, and print-on-demand platforms. ## Prompt

```
Photo-realistic t-shirt mockup: adult figure facing away, sitting on grass, golden retriever beside them; entire back of {{shirt-color}} short-sleeve shirt fully visible and well-lit, centered in frame. {{scene-setting}}. Background: pine trees and calm lake with Pacific Northwest wilderness in the mid-ground, Cascade mountains in the distance. Three-quarter view from behind, medium shot 85mm equivalent, shallow depth of field f/2.8, clean product photography style with natural color grading --ar 4:5 --style raw --stylize 250
```
