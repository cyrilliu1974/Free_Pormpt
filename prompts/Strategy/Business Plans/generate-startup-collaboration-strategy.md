# Startup Partnership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Startup Partnership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive partnership plans for companies seeking innovation through strategic alliances with startups and established tech firms. This startup partnership prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company's industry and partnership goals to produce a detailed strategic framework. It identifies specific collaboration opportunities, evaluates potential partner candidates with clear value propositions for both parties, and maps a phased partnership roadmap complete with objectives, activities, timelines, and measurable success metrics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping business development teams move from vague collaboration ideas to concrete, actionable partnership strategies. Corporate innovation managers, startup founders exploring co-development opportunities, and BD professionals building their partnership pipeline will find this prompt particularly valuable when they need a structured approach to alliance planning. ● Identifies three key collaboration areas tailored to your industry and strategic goals ● Evaluates potential startup and tech company partners with mutual value propositions ● Delivers a three-phase partnership roadmap with concrete activities, timelines, and success metrics ● Includes challenge identification and mitigation strategies to anticipate common partnership obstacles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic partnership expert with deep knowledge in tech innovation and startup ecosystems.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan for building mutually beneficial partnerships with promising startups or established tech companies to drive innovation.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Partnership goals: {{partnership-goals}}

## Output
Provide your analysis in the following structure:

**Key Areas for Collaboration:**
1. [Area 1]
2. [Area 2]
3. [Area 3]

**Potential Partners:**

1. Startup:
   • Name: [Startup Name]
   • Value Proposition for Us: [What they bring]
   • Value Proposition for Them: [What we offer]

2. Tech Company:
   • Name: [Tech Company Name]
   • Value Proposition for Us: [What they bring]
   • Value Proposition for Them: [What we offer]

**Partnership Roadmap:**

Phase 1:
• Objective: [Objective]
• Key Activities:
  • [Activity 1]
  • [Activity 2]
• Timeline: [Timeline]

Phase 2:
• Objective: [Objective]
• Key Activities:
  • [Activity 1]
  • [Activity 2]
• Timeline: [Timeline]

Phase 3:
• Objective: [Objective]
• Key Activities:
  • [Activity 1]
  • [Activity 2]
• Timeline: [Timeline]

**Success Metrics:**
1. [Metric 1]
2. [Metric 2]
3. [Metric 3]

**Potential Challenges:**
1. [Challenge 1]
2. [Challenge 2]
3. [Challenge 3]

**Mitigation Strategies:**
1. [Strategy 1]
2. [Strategy 2]
3. [Strategy 3]

Ensure all recommendations are:
- Strategically aligned with the stated goals
- Specific and actionable rather than generic
- Focused on clear mutual value creation
- Realistic given typical partnership timelines and resource constraints
```
