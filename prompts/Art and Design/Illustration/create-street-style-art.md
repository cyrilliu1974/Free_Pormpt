# Street Style Art Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Street Style Art Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates street style fashion photography for designers, marketers, and content creators. It produces urban fashion imagery by combining a subject with styling details and a custom aspect ratio, delivering the raw energy and authenticity of street photography through AI image generation. This street style art prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana lets you create editorial-quality fashion shots, lookbook images, or social media content that captures contemporary urban aesthetics. Reach for it when you need fashion photography with a documentary, candid feel rather than studio-polished product shots. ● Define any subject (a model, outfit, or accessory) and pair it with styling details like location, mood, lighting, or garment specifics. ● Control the aspect ratio to fit Instagram posts, Pinterest pins, website banners, or print lookbooks. ● Produces candid, documentary-style fashion photography that feels authentic and culturally grounded. ● Works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana with consistent street photography aesthetics. ## Prompt

```
{{subject}}, street style fashion photography, {{styling-details}}, --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
