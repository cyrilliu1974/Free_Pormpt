# 3D Product Package Render Prompt

## 簡介

The 3D Product Package Render Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic luxury packaging visuals with studio-quality lighting and natural ingredient arrangements. This 3D package render prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces three-quarter view product shots with shallow depth of field, clean shadows, reflections, and soft gradient backgrounds that mimic professional product photography. You customize three variables: package details (shape, material, closure), brand identity (colors, logo placement, label design), and botanicals (fruits, herbs, nuts, or other natural ingredients arranged around the base). Perfect for skincare brands, food and beverage companies, supplement packaging, cosmetics lines, and any product category that benefits from showing natural ingredients alongside the final package. ● Produces luxury-grade 3D renders with premium material finishes, realistic reflections, and professional studio lighting that matches commercial photography standards. ● Includes natural ingredient styling with whole fruits, citrus slices showing juicy texture, scattered nuts or seeds, and herb leaves arranged organically in the foreground. ● Maintains shallow depth of field and three-quarter camera angle for editorial-quality product shots suitable for e-commerce, social media, and pitch decks. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images with light-gray gradient backgrounds and soft center glow, ready for web or print use without further compositing. ## Prompt

```
3D render of a luxury product package with premium finish, studio lighting with clean shadows and reflections, three-quarter view camera angle, shallow depth of field for photorealistic detail. Light-gray gradient background with soft center glow.

Package: {{package-details}}

Branding: {{brand-identity}}

Surrounding ingredients arranged naturally: {{botanicals}} — whole fruits, citrus slices with juicy texture, nuts or seeds scattered in foreground, herb leaves at the base.

--ar 16:9 --q 2 --no text artifacts, distortion
```
