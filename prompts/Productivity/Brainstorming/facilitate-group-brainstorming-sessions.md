# Group Brainstorming Session Facilitator Prompt

## 簡介

The Group Brainstorming Session Facilitator Prompt is a free AI prompt that structures and guides collaborative ideation sessions to generate actionable improvement ideas for any business process. This brainstorming prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert facilitator, introducing the session purpose, guiding participants through creative idea generation, and capturing 5-8 diverse concepts in a clean markdown table with three columns: the core idea, its measurable benefits, and concrete action items for implementation. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles so each idea logically connects to and builds upon previous contributions, creating a progression from foundational improvements to more ambitious innovations. Teams use it to facilitate workshops, generate process improvement roadmaps, and transform unstructured brainstorming into documented plans tailored to their specific industry context. Reach for this prompt when you need to run a focused brainstorming session that produces more than just raw ideas - you get structured outputs ready for evaluation and implementation. ● Structures ideation with clear purpose, creative guidance, and logical idea progression ● Outputs a markdown table format with ideas, benefits, and action items ready for team review ● Generates 5-8 diverse concepts that scale in ambition and adapt to your industry context ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure each idea builds coherently on previous contributions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert facilitator leading a structured brainstorming session to generate innovative ideas for improving a business process.

## Task
Conduct a brainstorming session that:
- Introduces the session's purpose and structure
- Guides participants through creative ideation, building on each contribution
- Captures ideas with their potential benefits and actionable implementation steps
- Uses dependency grammar principles to ensure ideas logically connect and build upon one another

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Present the brainstorming outcomes as a markdown table with three columns:

| Idea | Benefits | Action Items |
|------|----------|-------------|
| [Specific improvement concept] | [Measurable advantages and impact] | [Concrete next steps for implementation] |

Generate 5-8 diverse ideas that progressively build in scope and ambition, ensuring each is tailored to the industry and process specified.
```
