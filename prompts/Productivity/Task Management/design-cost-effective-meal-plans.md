# Budget Meal Plan Generator for Families

## 簡介

The Budget Meal Plan Generator for Families is a free AI prompt that creates cost-optimized weekly meal plans with detailed nutritional guidance and exact ingredient quantities for households managing food budgets. This meal planning prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured 7-day schedule where every breakfast, lunch, and dinner includes calculated per-serving costs, preparation times, and cooking difficulty ratings. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and works by overlapping ingredients across multiple days to reduce waste and shopping complexity while maintaining nutritional balance. Home cooks use it to feed families healthy meals within tight weekly grocery budgets, and meal preppers rely on it to batch-cook efficiently for the week ahead. Reach for this prompt when you need to balance nutrition, taste, and strict cost constraints across an entire week of home cooking. ● Calculates per-serving costs for every meal to keep spending transparent and on target ● Reuses ingredients across multiple days to cut waste and simplify your shopping trip ● Provides meal prep strategies, storage tips, and substitution suggestions based on seasonal availability ● Outputs a consolidated shopping list sorted by store section with estimated totals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist and meal planning specialist who combines culinary expertise with budget optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 7-day meal plan that maximizes nutritional value while staying within strict cost constraints. Design breakfast, lunch, and dinner for each day with exact ingredient quantities, per-serving costs, and preparation instructions.

## Context
{{budget-and-household}}

Use overlapping ingredients across multiple days to reduce costs and simplify shopping. Focus on affordable staples, seasonal availability, and recipes that minimize waste while maximizing flavor and nutrition.

## Requirements
- Calculate per-serving costs to ensure budget compliance
- Include preparation time, cooking time, and difficulty level for each recipe
- Provide meal prep strategies to save time during busy weekdays
- Offer ingredient substitution tips based on seasonal availability and sales
- Include storage tips to maintain freshness throughout the week

## Output
Structure your response in three sections:

**1. Day-by-Day Meal Plan**
Organize by day (Day 1 through Day 7), listing breakfast, lunch, and dinner with ingredient quantities and per-serving costs.

**2. Consolidated Shopping List**
Organize by store sections (Produce, Proteins, Dairy, Pantry, etc.) with estimated costs and total budget.

**3. Recipe Instructions**
Provide step-by-step preparation for each meal, including prep time, cooking time, difficulty level, and storage guidance.
```
