# Halloween Costume Selfie Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Halloween Costume Selfie Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates hyper-realistic smartphone mirror selfies featuring Halloween costumes in authentic home environments. This Halloween costume prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates natural-looking selfies with dramatic makeup, casual bedroom backdrops, and all the authentic details of real smartphone photography - partial face obstruction, mirror reflections, soft indoor lighting, and lived-in clutter. The prompt produces images that blend spooky aesthetics with modern social-media realism, capturing the confident, playful energy of Halloween costume photos without studio polish or artificial filters. Use it to create shareable Halloween content, test costume concepts, or generate seasonal visual assets that feel genuine and relatable. This prompt is perfect for content creators, social media marketers, or anyone who needs scroll-stopping Halloween imagery that looks like it came from a real phone camera rather than a professional shoot. ● Produces mirror selfies with realistic smartphone details like partial face coverage, screen glare, and natural hand positioning ● Captures dramatic Halloween makeup artistry including hollowed eyes, stitched-mouth illusions, and pale skin tones ● Renders authentic bedroom environments with casual clutter, unmade beds, and soft warm indoor lighting ● Generates images with natural imperfections and smartphone camera noise that enhance believability and social-media appeal ## Prompt
