# Crisis Management Plan Builder for Business Continuity

## 簡介

The Crisis Management Plan Builder for Business Continuity is a free AI prompt that creates operational crisis management plans tailored to your business context and industry-specific risks. This crisis management prompt for ChatGPT transforms your business context, identified risks, and stakeholder lists into a comprehensive tabular plan that covers crisis scenarios, preventive measures, immediate response strategies, escalation procedures, recovery protocols, communication plans, and resource allocation. You configure the table structure by specifying column count and headers, making it adaptable to your organization's planning framework. The output is a markdown table designed to serve as an operational reference document during real crisis events, ensuring your team has clear, actionable guidance when it matters most. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when building or updating your business continuity documentation, preparing for audits, or training crisis response teams across any industry. ● Identifies relevant crisis scenarios based on your business context and industry risks ● Defines preventive measures, immediate response actions, and escalation paths for each scenario ● Establishes communication protocols tailored to your internal teams and external stakeholders ● Specifies resource allocation including personnel, budget, and tools needed for effective response ● Includes post-crisis evaluation frameworks to capture lessons learned and improve future preparedness ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert crisis management consultant specializing in business continuity planning and risk mitigation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive Crisis Management Plan in structured tabular format. The plan must:

- Identify potential crisis scenarios relevant to the business context
- Outline preventive measures for each scenario
- Detail immediate response strategies and escalation procedures
- Establish recovery and restoration procedures
- Define communication protocols for internal teams and external stakeholders
- Specify resource allocation (personnel, budget, tools)
- Include post-crisis evaluation and lessons-learned processes

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Industry-specific risks to address: {{risks}}

Key stakeholders requiring communication plans: {{stakeholders}}

## Output
Deliver the Crisis Management Plan as a markdown table with {{column-count}} columns.

Column headers: {{column-names}}

Ensure each row represents a distinct crisis scenario or management component, with complete, actionable information across all columns. The table should serve as an operational reference document during actual crisis events.
```
