# Market Size Assessment Prompt for Business Research

## 簡介

The Market Size Assessment Prompt for Business Research is a free AI prompt that evaluates market potential for products and services through systematic research and competitor analysis. It guides users through data gathering on market trends, consumer behavior, industry forecasts, and key market segments to produce evidence-based size estimates and growth projections. This market research prompt for ChatGPT works by accepting your product or service details, target market, industry, assessment time frame, and key competitors, then producing a comprehensive summary covering market trends, competitive landscape, and critical growth factors. It also generates a structured table breaking down 4-6 distinct market segments with specific size estimates (revenue, volume, or user counts) and growth potential ratings. The output runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it compatible with all major text-based AI platforms. Use cases include pre-launch market validation, expansion feasibility studies, investor pitch preparation, and strategic planning cycles. This prompt is built for market analysts, business strategists, product managers, and founders who need defensible market sizing data to inform go-to-market decisions or investment theses. ● Systematically gathers data on market trends, consumer behavior, and industry forecasts for your specified product and target market ● Analyzes competitor offerings, market saturation, and segment dynamics to identify the most promising opportunities ● Produces a markdown table with 4-6 market segments, each showing estimated size and growth potential ratings ● Evaluates growth drivers including economic indicators, demographic shifts, technological advancements, and regulatory changes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst conducting a market size assessment.

## Task
Evaluate the market size potential for a specific product or service through systematic research and analysis. Gather data on market trends, consumer behavior, and industry forecasts. Analyze competitor offerings, market saturation, and key market segments. Estimate segment sizes and evaluate growth potential based on economic indicators, industry projections, demographic shifts, technological advancements, and regulatory changes.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Market:** {{target-market}}

**Industry:** {{industry}}

**Assessment Time Frame:** {{time-frame}}

**Key Competitors:** {{key-competitors}}

## Output
Provide a brief summary (3-4 paragraphs) of key findings covering market trends, competitive landscape, and critical factors influencing growth.

Then present a markdown table with three columns:

| Market Segment | Estimated Size | Growth Potential |
|----------------|----------------|------------------|

Include 4-6 distinct market segments with specific size estimates (revenue/volume/users as appropriate) and growth potential ratings or projections.
```
