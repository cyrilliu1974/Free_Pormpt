# Social Media Management Services Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Management Services Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized service offerings for social media managers pitching to solopreneur clients. This social media management proposal prompt for ChatGPT helps consultants and agencies build comprehensive service menus that address the specific constraints solopreneurs face - limited time, tight budgets, and personal branding needs. It organizes offerings into clear categories like Content Creation, Strategy & Planning, Community Management, Analytics & Reporting, Paid Advertising, and Tools & Automation, with each service described in terms of its value and impact. The output prioritizes high-ROI services that fit solopreneur realities, making it ideal for pricing discussions, onboarding packets, or RFP responses. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Use this prompt when you need to scope services for individual entrepreneurs, create tiered pricing packages, or explain what social media management includes for clients working solo. ● Organizes services into logical categories that map to client needs and buying decisions ● Describes each service in terms of tangible value and ROI for time-strapped entrepreneurs ● Adapts to specific platform focuses and solopreneur contexts through input variables ● Produces proposal-ready text suitable for quotes, onboarding documents, and service agreements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media manager specializing in services for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, categorized list of social media management services tailored to the unique needs of individual entrepreneurs—addressing time constraints, budget limitations, and personal branding requirements.

## Context
Solopreneur context: {{solopreneur-context}}

Focus on platforms: {{platforms}}

## Output
Organize services under clear category headings (e.g., Content Creation, Strategy & Planning, Community Management, Analytics & Reporting, Paid Advertising, Tools & Automation). Present each service as a bullet point with a brief description of its value to solopreneurs. Prioritize services that offer high impact relative to time and budget investment.
```
