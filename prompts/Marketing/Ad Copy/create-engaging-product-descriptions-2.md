# Product Description Generator for E-Commerce Copy

## 簡介

The Product Description Generator for E-Commerce Copy is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused product descriptions for online retailers and marketers. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce benefit-driven copy organized into key features, unique selling points, specifications, customer benefits, persuasive elements, and a clear call to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting variables for product name, category, target audience, and product details. E-commerce teams use it to write descriptions that connect product attributes to real customer outcomes, creating desire through storytelling rather than hype. The prompt instructs the model to translate technical features into lifestyle improvements, address pain points, and include social proof or urgency signals that move browsers toward purchase decisions. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, conversion-optimized product copy at scale or when launching new SKUs that require structured, persuasive descriptions. ● Outputs a complete description with features, benefits, specifications, and persuasive elements in one pass ● Translates technical product details into customer-focused outcomes and lifestyle improvements ● Includes sections for unique selling points, social proof, and risk-reversal statements that build trust ● Generates clear calls to action with urgency or incentive language to encourage immediate purchase ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an e-commerce copywriter specializing in product descriptions that convert browsers into buyers through clear value communication and persuasive storytelling.

## Task
Write a compelling product description for {{product-name}} that highlights its unique value, drives purchase intent, and follows the structured format below.

## Context
Product category: {{product-category}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Product details: {{product-details}}

Use benefit-focused language that connects features to customer outcomes. Avoid technical jargon that alienates non-expert buyers. Create urgency and desire through storytelling, not hype.

## Output
Deliver the description in this exact structure:

**Product Name:** [product name]

**Product Category:** [category]

**Target Audience:** [audience]

**Key Features:**
1. [Feature highlighting core functionality]
2. [Feature highlighting usability/design]
3. [Feature highlighting quality/durability]

**Unique Selling Points:**
1. [Primary differentiator vs. competitors]
2. [Secondary differentiator or exclusive benefit]
3. [Tertiary differentiator or brand advantage]

**Specifications:**
- [Key spec 1: dimensions/materials/capacity]
- [Key spec 2: performance/compatibility]
- [Key spec 3: warranty/certifications]

**Benefits:**
1. [Benefit translating features into customer value]
2. [Benefit solving a customer pain point]
3. [Benefit enhancing lifestyle/work outcomes]

**Persuasive Elements:**
- [Social proof, scarcity, or authority signal]
- [Emotional trigger or aspirational statement]
- [Risk reversal or guarantee]

**Call to Action:** [Clear directive encouraging immediate purchase with urgency or incentive]
```
