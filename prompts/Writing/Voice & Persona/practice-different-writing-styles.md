# Writing Style Practice Prompt for Multiple Styles

## 簡介

The Writing Style Practice Prompt for Multiple Styles is a free AI prompt that builds a complete training guide for writers developing versatility across persuasive, narrative, expository, and descriptive writing techniques. This writing style practice prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as an expert writing coach, analyzing the key characteristics of each major writing style and demonstrating how dependency grammar principles strengthen sentence structure and clarity. Writers specify their style focus, target audience, purpose, proficiency level, and topic, then receive tailored techniques with concrete examples, adaptation strategies for different contexts, and exercises matched to their current skill level. Content creators use it to master persuasive copywriting, novelists refine narrative voice, educators develop expository clarity, and marketers craft vivid descriptive content. This prompt serves writers at any skill level who want structured coaching in multiple writing styles without hiring a personal writing coach. ● Analyzes characteristics and use cases for persuasive, narrative, expository, and descriptive writing styles ● Demonstrates 3-4 high-impact techniques per style with concrete examples you can model ● Explains how dependency grammar framework improves clarity and sentence flow in each style ● Provides adaptation strategies for your specified audience, purpose, and proficiency level ● Includes targeted exercises and guidance on blending styles effectively for your topic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert writing coach specializing in style versatility and structural clarity.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide that develops proficiency across persuasive, narrative, expository, and descriptive writing styles. For each style, analyze its key characteristics, demonstrate effective techniques with examples, explain how dependency grammar principles strengthen sentence structure, and provide strategies for adapting content to different audiences and purposes.

## Context
Writing style focus: {{style-focus}}
Target audience: {{audience}}
Writing purpose: {{purpose}}
Current proficiency level: {{proficiency-level}}
Topic or theme: {{topic}}

## Output
Structure your guide with:

**For each relevant writing style:**
- Style characteristics and when to use it
- 3-4 high-impact techniques with concrete examples
- How dependency grammar framework improves clarity and flow in this style
- Adaptation strategies for the specified audience and purpose

**Tailored recommendations:**
- Exercises matched to the current proficiency level
- How to blend styles effectively for the given topic
- Common pitfalls to avoid

Use clear headings for each writing style, bullet points for techniques, and brief illustrative examples throughout.
```
