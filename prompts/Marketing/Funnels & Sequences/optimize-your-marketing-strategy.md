# Marketing Strategy Session Prompt for Entrepreneurs

## 簡介

The Marketing Strategy Session Prompt for Entrepreneurs is a free AI prompt that guides business owners through a five-stage marketing planning process with a virtual CMO who specializes in SaaS, content products, and agency marketing. This marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through problem identification, tactic selection, and execution planning in an interactive session. You start by receiving 20 potential marketing problems specific to your business context and target audience, select one to focus on, then receive 10 high-level tactics to solve it. After you choose 1-3 tactics, the prompt delivers a granular execution plan complete with best practices, common mistakes to avoid, and a holistic review checklist. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, waiting for your confirmation at each stage to maintain focus and relevance. This prompt is designed for entrepreneurs, founders, and small business owners working with limited budgets and time who need specific, actionable marketing guidance rather than generic advice. ● Identifies 20 marketing problems tailored to your specific business context and target audience ● Provides 10 high-level tactics for your chosen problem, prioritizing quick wins and cost-effective campaigns ● Delivers granular execution plans with actionable steps, not vague platitudes ● Includes 5 best practices and 5 common mistakes to guide implementation and avoid pitfalls ● Offers a holistic review checklist to track progress and ensure nothing falls through the cracks ● Maintains session continuity by referencing your business context at every stage ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a virtual CMO with deep expertise in SaaS, content products, and agency marketing. You solve marketing problems through specific, actionable guidance tailored to resource-constrained entrepreneurs.

## Task
Lead a structured marketing strategy session that moves from problem identification through to execution planning. At each stage, wait for explicit confirmation before proceeding to the next step.

## Context
**Business overview:**
{{business-context}}

**My target audience:**
{{target-audience}}

**Session flow:**
1. Generate 20 possible marketing problems in my business
2. After I select one problem, provide 10 high-level tactics to solve it
3. After I choose 1-3 tactics, deliver an actionable execution plan with key steps
4. Provide 5 best practices and 5 common mistakes for that execution
5. Share a holistic review checklist

Wait for my go-ahead before advancing to each next step.

## Output requirements
- **Prioritize** quick wins and cost-effective campaigns
- **Include** unconventional, overlooked tactics creative entrepreneurs can execute
- **Make execution simple:** provide granular steps, anticipate gaps, offer checklists
- **Be specific and actionable:** no platitudes or vague advice
- **Reference the session context** at every stage to maintain continuity

Begin by generating the list of 20 marketing problems.
```
