## 簡介

The Playful 3D Matte Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms flat images into volumetric 3D icons with soft polymer textures and smooth geometry for designers and UI artists. This 3D icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana analyzes the silhouette of your uploaded image and applies geometry-sensitive retexturing with matte plastic, rubberized coatings, and semi-matte finishes. The prompt preserves detail while smoothing contours, rounding edges, and adding soft shadow casting with ambient occlusion. Use it to create app icons, UI elements, product mockups, or playful brand assets that need a tactile, touchable feel with minimal reflection and consistent lighting.
