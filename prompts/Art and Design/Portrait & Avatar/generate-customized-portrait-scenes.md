# Portrait Scene Generator With Companion

## 簡介

The Portrait Scene Generator With Companion is a free AI prompt that creates realistic outdoor portrait photographs capturing bonding moments between people and their companion animals in natural settings. This portrait scene prompt for Midjourney guides the model through subject characteristics, companion animal details, environmental context, lighting, and composition to produce authentic, emotionally resonant imagery. You define the person's age, appearance, clothing, and expression; specify the companion animal's breed, eye color, and demeanor; and describe the outdoor environment including ground cover, vegetation, background structures, sky conditions, and seasonal mood. The prompt outputs medium close-up, eye-level portraits with shallow depth of field and 3:4 aspect ratio, suitable for lifestyle content, pet brand marketing, social media storytelling, and personal keepsakes. Designers, marketers, and content creators reach for it when they need authentic bonding imagery without coordinating live photo shoots. ● Customize person details (age, hair, clothing, expression) and companion animal traits (breed, eye color, demeanor) for personalized portraits. ● Define outdoor settings with ground cover, vegetation, background elements, sky, mood, color palette, and seasonal atmosphere. ● Produces medium close-up, eye-level compositions with soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field, and 3:4 aspect ratio. ● Ideal for pet brand visuals, lifestyle marketing, social media storytelling, and heartfelt personal content. ## Prompt

```
A realistic outdoor portrait capturing a bonding moment between a person and their companion animal in natural daylight with soft, even ambient lighting.

**Primary subject:** {{subject-description}} — describe the person's age range, hair, clothing, and facial expression toward the camera.

**Companion animal:** {{companion-description}} — specify breed or type, eye color, and expression.

**Setting:** {{environment}} — describe the outdoor location including ground cover, vegetation, background elements like vehicles or structures, sky conditions, overall mood (peaceful, adventurous, serene, etc.), dominant color palette, and seasonal tones.

Composition: medium close-up, eye-level angle, subjects positioned centrally with slight upward bias. Shallow depth of field with blurred background, standard lens perspective. Foreground subjects dominate; background minimal. 3:4 aspect ratio.
```
