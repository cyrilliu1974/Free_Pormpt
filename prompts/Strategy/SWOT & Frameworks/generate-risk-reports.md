# Risk Assessment Report Generator

## 簡介

The Risk Assessment Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive risk assessment reports for businesses analyzing potential threats and mitigation strategies. This risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT examines both internal and external risks by evaluating business operations, industry trends, regulatory factors, and economic conditions. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a structured markdown table covering 8-12 risks across operational, financial, strategic, compliance, and external categories. Each risk includes a likelihood rating, impact assessment, and specific mitigation strategy. Business analysts use it to identify vulnerabilities before they materialize, compliance teams rely on it for regulatory risk mapping, and executives use it to inform strategic planning decisions. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically evaluate risk exposure across multiple business dimensions or when preparing board presentations, strategic reviews, or due diligence documentation. ● Identifies 8-12 risks spanning operational, financial, strategic, compliance, and external threat categories ● Evaluates each risk by likelihood of occurrence and severity of potential impact ● Delivers mitigation strategies tailored to the specific business context provided ● Outputs a professional markdown table format with contextual introduction and executive summary ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk analyst specializing in comprehensive risk assessment and mitigation planning.

## Task
Generate a structured risk assessment report that identifies potential risks and develops actionable mitigation strategies for the specified business context.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Analyze both internal and external risks by examining:
- Business operations and vulnerabilities
- Industry trends and competitive pressures
- Regulatory, economic, and geopolitical factors
- Operational, financial, strategic, and reputational exposures

Evaluate each risk's likelihood of occurrence and severity of impact.

## Output
Provide a brief introduction (2-3 sentences) contextualizing the assessment.

Then present your findings in a markdown table with three columns:

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|---------------------|

Include 8-12 risks covering operational, financial, strategic, compliance, and external categories.

End with a concise conclusion (2-3 sentences) summarizing the overall risk profile and key recommendations.
```
