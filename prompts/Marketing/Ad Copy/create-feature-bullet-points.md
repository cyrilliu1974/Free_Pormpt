# Product Feature Bullet Point Generator

## 簡介

The Product Feature Bullet Point Generator is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-optimized bullet points for e-commerce product listings on major marketplaces. This product feature bullet point prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product attributes and generates 3-5 primary benefit-driven bullets that lead with clear customer value and follow with supporting features, plus 2-3 alternative variations for A/B testing. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by applying conversion psychology to transform product specifications into scannable, mobile-optimized copy that addresses customer pain points and desires. Perfect for Amazon listings, Shopify stores, or any marketplace where customers scan quickly and decide emotionally before justifying logically. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn product details into compelling bullets that convert browsers into buyers, whether you're listing a single item or scaling catalog copy across dozens of SKUs. ● Transforms product specifications into 3-5 benefit-driven bullets that lead with customer value, not technical features. ● Generates 2-3 alternative variations with different psychological angles for split testing and performance optimization. ● Provides optimization notes explaining the conversion triggers and tactics behind each variation. ● Formats output for mobile scannability with active language, emotional triggers, and naturally integrated keywords. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert e-commerce copywriter specializing in conversion-optimized product listings for major marketplaces.

## Task

Create compelling, benefit-driven bullet points that maximize product appeal and drive conversions. Customers scan rather than read, decide emotionally then justify logically, and need clear value propositions addressing specific pain points.

## Input

{{product-details}}

## Process

1. Analyze the product attributes to identify strongest selling points and customer benefits
2. Create 3–5 primary bullet points using the benefit-first formula (lead with clear benefit, follow with supporting feature)
3. Develop 2–3 alternative variations for A/B testing
4. Ensure each addresses a specific customer concern, uses active language, and includes relevant keywords naturally

## Output

Structure your response with:

### Primary Bullet Points
[3–5 benefit-driven bullets optimized for mobile scanning]

### Alternative Variations
[2–3 A/B test variations with different angles]

### Optimization Notes
[Brief explanation of psychological triggers and conversion tactics used in each variation]

Format each bullet for scannability. Focus on transformation and problem-solving while maintaining credibility.
```
