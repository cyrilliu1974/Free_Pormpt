# Anti-Inflammatory Meal Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Anti-Inflammatory Meal Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates a personalized 7-day meal plan designed to reduce inflammation and optimize gut microbiome health for anyone seeking dietary strategies to improve digestive function and overall vitality. This anti-inflammatory meal plan prompt for ChatGPT works by acting as an expert nutritionist and functional medicine practitioner who designs four eating occasions per day (breakfast, lunch, dinner, and one snack) featuring scientifically-backed ingredients like leafy greens, berries, nuts, olive oil, ginger, turmeric, and omega-3 rich foods. The prompt structures each meal to heal the gut lining, promote beneficial bacteria growth, and provide sustained energy while incorporating specific preparation methods such as soaking and sprouting to enhance nutrient bioavailability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and accommodates custom dietary constraints you specify through the included variable. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, science-based approach to meal planning that addresses inflammation and gut health through strategic food combinations rather than generic nutrition advice. ● Delivers seven full days of meal plans with complete ingredient lists, portion sizes, and step-by-step preparation instructions. ● Explains how each meal component reduces inflammation and supports gut microbiome health through specific mechanisms. ● Incorporates optimal preparation techniques like soaking and sprouting that enhance nutrient bioavailability and digestive ease. ● Accounts for your specific dietary constraints through the customizable variable field. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist and functional medicine practitioner specializing in anti-inflammatory nutrition and gut microbiome optimization.

## Task
Design a comprehensive 7-day anti-inflammatory meal plan that supports digestive health and reduces systemic inflammation through strategic food combinations and preparation methods.

## Context
Inflammation underlies many chronic conditions, and gut health directly impacts immune function, mental clarity, and overall vitality. Each meal should work synergistically to heal the gut lining, promote beneficial bacteria growth, and provide sustained energy without triggering inflammatory responses.

The plan should:
- Feature four eating occasions daily (breakfast, lunch, dinner, one snack)
- Incorporate scientifically-backed anti-inflammatory ingredients: leafy greens, berries, nuts, olive oil, ginger, turmeric, omega-3 rich foods
- Prioritize fiber-rich foods that feed beneficial gut bacteria while remaining gentle on digestion
- Include specific preparation methods that enhance nutrient bioavailability (soaking, sprouting, optimal cooking techniques)
- Provide timing recommendations for optimal digestion
- Explain how each meal component reduces inflammation and supports gut health

Account for the following: {{dietary-constraints}}

## Output
Structure your response with clear **Day 1** through **Day 7** headings. For each day, provide:

- **Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack** subheadings
- Complete ingredient lists with portion sizes
- Step-by-step preparation instructions in bullet points
- Brief explanations of how each meal supports anti-inflammatory and gut health goals

Ensure recipes match the user's cooking skill level, available prep time, and preferred complexity level.
```
