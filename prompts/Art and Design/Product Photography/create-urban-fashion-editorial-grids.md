# Urban Fashion Editorial Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Urban Fashion Editorial Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multi-pose street-fashion photo grids for designers, brands, and content creators. It produces a 2×2 layout in 3:4 aspect ratio with tight spacing, featuring the same model in four distinct candid poses on a concrete urban street beside a customizable lifestyle prop - rendered with natural daylight, handheld camera feel, and subtle cinematic grain. This urban fashion prompt for Midjourney (and compatible models like Flux and Nano Banana) accepts two variables: your outfit ensemble and a lifestyle prop (motorcycle, vintage car, bicycle), then outputs four coordinated frames with the model crouching, standing, sitting, and leaning in editorial poses that feel natural and confident. Reach for this prompt when you need cohesive multi-angle fashion content for lookbooks, Instagram carousels, e-commerce storytelling, or mood boards without booking a full photo shoot. ● Produces a 2×2 grid with minimal gaps, one model, four poses (crouching, standing, sitting, leaning), and consistent urban styling ● Customizable outfit ensemble and lifestyle prop (motorcycle, car, bike) to match your brand or campaign aesthetic ● Street editorial photography style with natural daylight, soft shadows, handheld motion blur, and neutral cinematic tones ● Outputs in 3:4 aspect ratio, ideal for Instagram posts, Pinterest pins, and vertical lookbook formats ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic 2×2 grid fashion editorial, 3:4 aspect ratio, tight spacing with minimal gaps between frames. Same young woman model in four distinct poses on an urban street, styled with effortless confidence.

**Model**: Long dark wavy hair, natural candid expressions alternating between soft smiles and confident looks, urban vibe.

**Wardrobe**: {{outfit-ensemble}}

**Setting**: Concrete pavement street with {{lifestyle-prop}} parked beside the model, subtle city elements (buildings, walls) in background, natural outdoor environment.

**Four poses, one per grid cell**:
- Top left: crouching near the {{lifestyle-prop}}, one arm resting on knee, candid street-style
- Top right: standing beside the {{lifestyle-prop}}, one hand touching it, relaxed confident stance
- Bottom left: sitting on ground next to the {{lifestyle-prop}}, legs extended, casual lifestyle pose
- Bottom right: mid-shot leaning slightly against the {{lifestyle-prop}}, smiling naturally

**Photography**: Street fashion editorial style, natural daylight with soft shadows, handheld camera feel, slight motion blur in background, natural light reflections, subtle grain for urban texture. Neutral color tones with slight cinematic contrast, high detail, photorealistic rendering.

--ar 3:4
```
