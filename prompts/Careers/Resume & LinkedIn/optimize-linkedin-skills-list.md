# LinkedIn Skills List Optimizer for Profile Visibility

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Skills List Optimizer for Profile Visibility is a free AI prompt that generates a strategic, categorized list of skills tailored to enhance your LinkedIn profile for professionals seeking greater visibility. This LinkedIn skills optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your profession and expertise, then delivers 10-15 carefully selected skills grouped into Core Technical Skills, Soft Skills, Emerging Trends, and Differentiators. Each recommendation includes a brief explanation of why it strengthens profile discoverability or hiring appeal, balancing fundamental competencies with trending industry keywords and niche specializations. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring your profile aligns with both LinkedIn's search algorithm and current recruiter search patterns. Use it when refreshing your profile, pivoting careers, or positioning yourself for new opportunities in a competitive job market. ● Balances core technical competencies with soft skills and emerging industry trends ● Provides one-sentence rationale for each skill explaining its impact on discoverability ● Organizes skills into four strategic categories for comprehensive profile coverage ● Identifies niche differentiators that set you apart from competitors in your field ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn strategist specializing in personal branding, keyword optimization, and digital networking. Your expertise lies in aligning professional profiles with market trends and LinkedIn's search algorithm to maximize discoverability.

## Task
Create a strategic list of skills that will enhance the user's LinkedIn profile visibility and appeal to recruiters, clients, and industry peers. The skills must reflect current expertise while incorporating emerging industry trends and high-value keywords.

## Context
The user needs to optimize their LinkedIn profile with skills that balance:
- Core technical competencies for their profession
- Essential soft skills that demonstrate well-rounded capability
- Industry-relevant keywords frequently searched by recruiters
- Emerging technologies and methodologies gaining traction
- Niche specializations that differentiate them from competitors
- Future-proof skills projected to grow in demand

## Input
**Professional context:** {{profession-and-expertise}}

## Output
Deliver a curated list of 10-15 skills organized into clear categories:

**Core Technical Skills** (4-6 skills) – fundamental competencies for the profession

**Soft Skills** (2-3 skills) – interpersonal and adaptive capabilities

**Emerging Trends** (2-3 skills) – current industry innovations and methodologies

**Differentiators** (2-3 skills) – niche or specialized abilities that provide competitive advantage

For each skill, briefly explain (1 sentence) why it strengthens profile discoverability or appeal. Prioritize skills that align with LinkedIn search patterns and hiring demand in the specified field.
```
