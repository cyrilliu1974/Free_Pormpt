# Minimalist Interior Design Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimalist Interior Design Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates architectural-quality renderings of clean, modern interior spaces with customizable material palettes and centerpiece elements. This minimalist interior design prompt for Midjourney produces photorealistic images of rooms featuring coordinated floors, walls, cabinets, and ceilings in your chosen material palette - wood, concrete, marble, or any combination - with accent inlays and a defined focal element as the room's centerpiece. The prompt delivers architectural photography aesthetics: natural lighting with soft shadows, eye-level perspective, and clean 24mm wide-angle composition at 16:9 aspect ratio. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image-generation models. Designers use it to visualize bedroom concepts, kitchen layouts, living rooms, bathrooms, or office spaces before committing to materials or furniture. Reach for this prompt when you need to communicate a minimalist aesthetic to clients, explore material combinations, or generate mood boards for residential or commercial projects. ● Specify any interior type - bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, office, living room - and see it rendered in clean minimalist style. ● Control the entire material palette across floors, walls, cabinets, and ceiling with accent inlays for visual interest. ● Define a focal element (fireplace, artwork, furniture piece, window view) that anchors the composition. ● Receive images shot in architectural photography style with natural light, soft shadows, and professional 24mm framing. ## Prompt

```
Minimalist {{interior-type}} interior with {{material-palette}} floors, walls, cabinets, and ceiling inlaid with accent materials. {{focal-element}} as the room's centerpiece. Shot in the style of architectural photography: clean composition, natural light with soft shadows, eye-level perspective, 24mm wide angle --ar 16:9 --s 250 --q 2
```
