# Industry Thought Leadership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Industry Thought Leadership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete roadmap to establish your business as a leading voice in your industry. This thought leadership strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes your business, audience, and industry context to produce an actionable plan covering target audience pain points, your unique value proposition, content formats that build authority, distribution channels, partnership opportunities, and measurable success metrics. Use it when you need to define how your business will earn credibility, grow an engaged following, and convert expertise into business impact - whether you're launching a new authority-building initiative or refining an existing approach. ● Identifies your target audience's pain points, content preferences, and consumption habits to guide content creation ● Defines three content types and three distribution channels, each with rationale and engagement strategies tailored to your industry ● Maps strategic partnership opportunities with media, influencers, and complementary businesses for amplification ● Establishes measurable KPIs and a phased timeline with quarterly milestones from launch through maturity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in thought leadership strategy, specializing in positioning businesses and individuals as industry authorities.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive thought leadership plan that establishes the business described below as a leading voice in its industry. The plan should provide a clear roadmap for building credibility, growing an audience, and driving business impact.

## Context
{{business-and-industry}}

Describe your business, industry, and target audience. Include what your business does, who you serve, their key pain points and preferences, and what makes your industry unique.

## Output
Deliver a strategic thought leadership plan with these components:

### 1. Target Audience Analysis
- Audience description
- Primary pain points
- Content preferences and consumption habits

### 2. Unique Value Proposition
What distinct perspective or expertise sets this business apart from competitors

### 3. Thought Leadership Goals
Three specific, measurable objectives (e.g., brand awareness, lead generation, industry influence)

### 4. Content Strategy
Three content types with descriptions:
- Format, topics, and how each builds authority
- Examples: long-form articles, video series, research reports, podcasts

### 5. Distribution Channels
Three channels with tailored strategies:
- Platform selection rationale
- Engagement approach for each
- Examples: LinkedIn, industry publications, conferences, owned media

### 6. Partnerships and Collaborations
Two strategic partnership opportunities:
- Type of partner (media, influencers, complementary businesses)
- Value exchange and amplification potential

### 7. Metrics and KPIs
Three measurable indicators:
- What to track and why it matters
- Examples: engagement rate, share of voice, qualified leads, speaking invitations

### 8. Timeline
Three key milestones with target dates:
- Phased rollout from launch through maturity
- Quarterly or milestone-based checkpoints

Format the plan using clear markdown with headings, bullet points, and organized sections.
```
