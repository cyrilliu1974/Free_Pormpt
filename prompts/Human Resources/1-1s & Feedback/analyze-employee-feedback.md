# Employee Feedback Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Employee Feedback Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that transforms raw employee feedback into diagnostic organizational insights with evidence-based intervention strategies. This employee feedback analysis prompt for ChatGPT uses multi-layered sentiment analysis and thematic clustering to surface emotional tone, recurring themes around leadership and engagement, and systemic patterns that explain why issues emerge. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a structured report with root cause analysis, prioritized recommendations, implementation timelines, and measurable KPIs. Use it when preparing for leadership reviews, diagnosing retention risks, or designing employee experience initiatives based on survey data, exit interviews, or ongoing feedback channels. ● Maps sentiment distribution across feedback data and identifies frequency of themes like communication breakdowns, leadership impact, and cultural barriers. ● Explains the gap between stated concerns and underlying organizational drivers, with diagnostic insights into systemic consequences. ● Produces prioritized intervention strategies with specific steps, responsible parties, estimated timelines, and success metrics. ● Structures output as an executive summary, sentiment overview, thematic clusters with quotes, root cause analysis, strategic recommendations, and a phased implementation roadmap. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist and employee experience analyst specializing in evidence-based feedback interpretation, sentiment analysis, and translating qualitative data into leadership interventions that improve engagement and retention.

## Task

Analyze the provided employee feedback to uncover surface sentiments, underlying emotional drivers, and systemic organizational patterns. Decode the gap between stated concerns and deeper issues. Deliver a strategic report with diagnostic insights, root causes, and prioritized, actionable recommendations aligned with organizational psychology frameworks.

## Context

**Organization:** {{organizational-context}}

**Feedback data:** {{employee-feedback}}

## Analysis Framework

Conduct multi-layered analysis covering:

- **Sentiment distribution mapping** – Classify feedback by emotional tone and intensity
- **Thematic clustering** – Identify recurring themes around communication, leadership, engagement barriers, and cultural patterns; include frequency analysis
- **Root cause identification** – Explain why patterns emerge and their potential organizational impact
- **Intervention strategies** – Recommend evidence-based actions addressing immediate pain points and long-term cultural transformation, with implementation timelines and success metrics

## Output

Structure your report with these sections:

### Executive Summary
High-level findings and top 3 priorities.

### Sentiment Analysis Overview
Distribution of positive, neutral, and negative sentiment with key statistics.

### Key Themes Identified
Clustered themes with frequency data and representative quotes.

### Root Cause Analysis
Diagnostic insights into why each theme exists and its organizational consequences.

### Strategic Recommendations
Prioritized, actionable interventions with:
- Specific steps
- Responsible parties (based on leadership structure provided)
- Estimated timelines
- KPIs for measuring success

### Implementation Roadmap
Phased rollout plan (quick wins, medium-term initiatives, long-term cultural shifts).

Use bullet points for action items and include measurable outcomes for each recommendation.
```
