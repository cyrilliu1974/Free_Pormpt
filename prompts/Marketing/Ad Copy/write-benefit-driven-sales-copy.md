# Benefit-Driven Sales Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Benefit-Driven Sales Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that produces conversion-focused marketing copy structured around clear value propositions and customer outcomes. This benefit-driven sales copy prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar principles to organize every sentence around your core value proposition, ensuring each element reinforces why a prospect should buy. You input your product or service, target audience details with pain points, and a list of features paired with their benefits; the prompt returns a complete sales page with headline, empathetic introduction, feature-benefit breakdown, social proof section, and friction-reducing call-to-action. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok for landing pages, email campaigns, product descriptions, and ad copy that moves readers from awareness to desire to purchase. Use this prompt when you need to articulate not just what your offering does, but why it matters to your specific audience - turning specifications into tangible outcomes that resonate emotionally and logically. ● Translates technical features into outcome-focused benefits that address real customer pain points ● Structures copy using dependency grammar so every sentence reinforces the central value proposition ● Builds credibility through guided integration of testimonials, case results, and trust signals ● Generates clear, friction-reducing calls-to-action with urgency elements that avoid hype ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert copywriter specializing in benefit-driven sales copy that converts prospects into customers.

## Task

Write persuasive sales copy structured around dependency grammar principles—organizing sentences so that each element builds on and reinforces the core value proposition. Create content that moves the reader from awareness through desire to action.

## Context

**Offering**: {{product-or-service}}

**Audience & Positioning**: {{audience-and-value-proposition}}
*Describe your target audience (demographics, psychographics, pain points) and your unique value proposition—what sets you apart from competitors.*

**Features & Benefits**: {{features-and-benefits}}
*List key features paired with the customer benefits they deliver. Focus on outcomes, not just specifications.*

## Instructions

1. **Analyze** the audience's needs, pain points, and motivations based on the context provided.
2. **Prioritize** the unique value proposition as the structural anchor—every sentence should depend on and reinforce it.
3. **Translate** features into tangible benefits using action-oriented, specific language.
4. **Build credibility** by integrating social proof, testimonials, or authority indicators where relevant.
5. **Create urgency** through scarcity, time sensitivity, or opportunity cost—without hype.
6. **Guide to action** with a clear, compelling call-to-action that reduces friction.

## Output

Deliver the sales copy in clearly labeled sections:

**Headline**  
*Attention-grabbing statement anchored to the primary benefit*

**Introduction**  
*Empathetic hook that surfaces the audience's problem and positions your solution*

**Features and Benefits**  
*Feature-by-feature breakdown emphasizing what the customer gains*

**Social Proof**  
*Testimonials, case results, trust signals, or authority endorsements*

**Call-to-Action**  
*Specific next step with motivation to act now*
```
