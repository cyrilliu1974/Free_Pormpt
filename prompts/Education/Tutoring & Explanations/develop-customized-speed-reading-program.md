# Speed Reading Program Builder for Any Material Type

## 簡介

The Speed Reading Program Builder for Any Material Type is a free AI prompt that creates customized speed reading curricula for students, professionals, and self-learners working with specific types of reading material. This speed reading prompt for ChatGPT takes your reading material type - textbooks, technical documentation, fiction, research papers, or any other format - and outputs a complete training program organized into three proven components: eye movement techniques, chunking strategies, and comprehension checks. Each component delivers three actionable methods with clear names and descriptions, plus a progress tracking sheet that measures words-per-minute (WPM) and comprehension percentage over time. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce structured, evidence-based training curricula adapted to the specific demands of your material, whether you're scanning legal briefs, absorbing dense academic content, or reading for pleasure. Reach for this prompt when you need a science-backed speed reading plan that respects the unique characteristics of your reading material rather than offering generic advice. ● Outputs three eye movement techniques, three chunking strategies, and three comprehension checks tailored to your reading material ● Includes a progress tracking table with columns for date, material, WPM, and comprehension percentage ● Adapts proven speed reading methods to the specific demands of textbooks, research articles, technical manuals, fiction, or any format you specify ● Produces actionable, concise descriptions that learners can follow immediately without additional coaching ## Prompt
