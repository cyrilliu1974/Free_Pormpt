# Social Media Scheduling Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Scheduling Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive, platform-specific social media plan for businesses and marketers looking to maximize reach and engagement. This social media scheduling prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your chosen platforms, evaluates audience demographics and peak engagement windows, and delivers a structured content calendar framework. It balances informative, promotional, and interactive post types while recommending optimal posting frequency and timing for each channel. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable strategies that maintain consistent branding while adapting format and tone to platform-specific best practices. Real-world applications include small business owners planning weekly content calendars, agencies managing multi-platform campaigns, and in-house marketers coordinating cross-channel engagement. Reach for this prompt when you need a detailed, business-specific social media roadmap that goes beyond generic advice and addresses your unique industry, offerings, and target audience. ● Evaluates each platform's audience, peak times, and native features to inform strategy ● Designs a balanced content mix with specific post types matched to platform strengths ● Recommends posting frequency, timing, and a weekly or monthly calendar structure ● Includes engagement tactics and performance metrics to track and improve results ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist developing a comprehensive scheduling and content strategy to maximize reach and engagement across multiple platforms.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable social media plan that includes:

- **Platform Analysis**: Evaluate each platform's audience demographics, peak engagement times, and native features
- **Content Strategy**: Design a balanced content mix of informative, promotional, and interactive posts tailored to each platform's strengths
- **Scheduling Plan**: Recommend optimal posting frequency and timing for each platform
- **Content Calendar Framework**: Provide a weekly/monthly structure that maintains consistent branding while adapting format and tone to each platform
- **Engagement Tactics**: Suggest platform-specific methods to encourage interaction and community building
- **Performance Metrics**: Identify key metrics to track success on each platform

## Context
Business: {{business-name}}
Platforms: {{platforms}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Key offerings: {{products-services}}

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with clear headings for each section. Use bullet points for detailed recommendations. Ensure all strategies are actionable and specific to the business context provided.
```
