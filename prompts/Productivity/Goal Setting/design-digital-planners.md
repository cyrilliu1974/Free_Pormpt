# Digital Planner Design Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Digital Planner Design Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates step-by-step instructions for designing, producing, and marketing digital planners as sellable products. This digital planner design prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every stage of product creation, from initial market research and niche positioning to file export settings and pricing strategy. It adapts to your target audience, niche focus, design experience level, and preferred software, generating a customized guide with clear headings, actionable bullet points, and practical tips. The output includes feature planning, page templates, visual design principles, hyperlinking strategies, user testing methods, and marketing considerations tailored to the digital product market. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a comprehensive roadmap for creating digital planners that meet the specific organizational needs of solopreneurs and stand out in competitive marketplaces like Etsy or Gumroad. ● Tailors market research, feature sets, and layout principles to your chosen niche and target audience ● Provides software-specific guidance for your preferred design tool and adapts complexity to your experience level ● Covers interactive elements, file format decisions, export settings, and testing protocols often overlooked by beginners ● Includes pricing strategy, positioning tactics, and marketing considerations for launching in the digital product marketplace ## Prompt
