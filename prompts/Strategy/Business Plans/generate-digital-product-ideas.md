# Digital Product Idea Generator With Budget Analysis

## 簡介

The Digital Product Idea Generator With Budget Analysis is a free AI prompt that helps entrepreneurs and product managers brainstorm viable digital products within specific budget constraints. This digital product ideation prompt for ChatGPT walks through structured market analysis, delivering three complete product concepts that include target market identification, unique value propositions, development effort scoring (1-5 scale), market potential assessment, estimated timelines, and detailed cost breakdowns. It then compiles all insights into a side-by-side comparison matrix and closes with a strategic recommendation on which idea offers the best return on investment. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across text-generation platforms. Reach for this prompt when you have a budget figure in mind and need to evaluate multiple product directions before committing resources. ● Scores each product idea on both development effort and market potential using a consistent 1-5 scale. ● Breaks down estimated costs by category so you can assess budget fit at a glance. ● Outputs a comparison matrix that places timeline, effort, and cost side by side for rapid evaluation. ● Includes a final strategic recommendation that weighs feasibility against expected return. ## Prompt
