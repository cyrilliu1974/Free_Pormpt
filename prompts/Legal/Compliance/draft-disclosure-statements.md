# Draft Disclosure Statements for Influencer Marketing

## 簡介

The Draft Disclosure Statements for Influencer Marketing prompt is a free AI prompt that generates legally compliant disclosure language for brands and influencers navigating sponsorship transparency requirements. This influencer marketing disclosure prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces platform-specific disclosure statements that meet FTC guidelines and international regulatory standards. It creates variations for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Twitter, with both beginning-of-content and in-content placement options, plus tone variations (formal, casual, creative) that maintain compliance while preserving authentic voice. Real-world use cases include brand partnerships, affiliate campaigns, gifted product posts, and sponsored content across video, story, and static formats. Reach for this prompt when you need disclosure statements that satisfy regulatory bodies in multiple jurisdictions (US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia) or when creating content templates that prevent costly compliance violations. ● Platform-specific formatting that accounts for character limits, visual placement, audio requirements, and story-frame disclosure rules ● International variations covering FTC (US), ASA (UK), EU regulations, Competition Bureau (Canada), and ACCC (Australia) requirements ● Side-by-side examples of compliant vs. non-compliant disclosures with clear explanations of what makes each pass or fail regulatory review ● Timing and placement guidance for video content, including when disclosures must appear audibly and visually, and how to handle temporary content formats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a compliance communication specialist with deep regulatory expertise in influencer marketing disclosure requirements across platforms and jurisdictions.

## Task
Create legally compliant disclosure statements for the influencer marketing campaign described below. Provide platform-specific variations, tone options, and international adaptations that satisfy FTC and equivalent regulatory bodies while maintaining authentic voice.

## Context
{{campaign-details}}

Regulatory bodies impose significant penalties for undisclosed sponsorships. Disclosure requirements vary by platform, region, and content format. Disclosures must use clear, unambiguous language and appear prominently—never buried in hashtags, behind "more" links, or using vague terms like #collab without clarification. Video content requires both audio and visual disclosure; stories need disclosure on every frame.

## Output
Provide:

**Primary Disclosure Statements**
- Clear, compliant disclosure meeting FTC guidelines
- Platform-specific variations (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter)
- Beginning-of-content and in-content options

**Tone Variations**
- Formal, casual, and creative versions—all compliant

**Platform Guidance**
- Visual placement and timing for video content
- Story/temporary content requirements

**International Variations**
- Adaptations for UK, EU, Canada, Australia markets

**Compliance Reference**
- Examples of non-compliant disclosures with explanations
- Dos and don'ts in comparison format

Use clear section headers, bullet points, and direct language. Focus on clarity over creativity—compliance first. Ensure disclosures are accessible to all audiences.
```
