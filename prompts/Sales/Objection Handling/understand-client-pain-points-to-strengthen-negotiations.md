# Client Pain Point Negotiation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Client Pain Point Negotiation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops hierarchical negotiation plans by identifying client challenges and connecting them to your offering. This negotiation strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a seven-step framework that identifies pain points, aligns your offering to client needs, structures arguments using dependency grammar (root goal, supporting dependencies, and strengthening modifiers), and prepares talking points with objection handling. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing numbered plans that sales professionals, account managers, and business development teams use before client negotiations, contract discussions, and strategic partnership meetings. The prompt asks for your target client, offering details, industry context, and negotiation goals, then outputs a prioritized strategy with a flexible negotiation flow. ● Systematically identifies client pain points and maps them to specific features of your offering ● Organizes arguments hierarchically with root goals, supporting dependencies, and evidence modifiers for logical clarity ● Generates prioritized talking points ranked by impact and relevance to client needs ● Anticipates objections and prepares strategic counterarguments before you enter the negotiation ● Provides a flexible negotiation flow sequence that adapts to real-time client responses ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert negotiation strategist building a client-focused negotiation plan.

## Task
Develop a structured negotiation strategy by identifying the target client's pain points and mapping how your offering addresses them. Organize your analysis using dependency grammar principles: establish a root goal, show how supporting arguments depend on that goal, and add strengthening evidence as modifiers.

## Context
**Target client:** {{target-client}}

**Your offering and position:** {{offering}}

**Industry context:** {{industry-context}}

**Negotiation goals:** {{negotiation-goals}}

## Output
Deliver a hierarchical negotiation plan with numbered sections:

1. **Pain Points** — list the client's key challenges, needs, and motivations.
2. **Offering Alignment** — explain how each pain point connects to your offering.
3. **Strategy Structure**
   - Root: state your main negotiation goal.
   - Dependencies: list the supporting arguments that connect to the root.
   - Modifiers: provide evidence or details that strengthen each argument.
4. **Talking Points** — develop specific talking points for each pain point, aligned with your strategy structure.
5. **Prioritization** — rank talking points by potential impact and relevance.
6. **Objection Handling** — anticipate objections and prepare counterarguments.
7. **Negotiation Flow** — outline a flexible sequence that adapts to client responses.

Use numbered lists and sub-points for clarity and quick reference.
```
