# Referral Marketing Strategy Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Referral Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized referral programs designed to generate leads and acquire new customers for businesses of any size. This referral marketing prompt for ChatGPT takes your business details, target audience characteristics, and budget constraints, then produces a structured three-column strategy table covering referral incentives, distribution channels, and success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, generating at least five distinct strategies in each category that work together as a cohesive system. Real use cases include B2B SaaS companies designing partner referral programs, e-commerce brands launching customer advocacy campaigns, and service businesses building word-of-mouth growth engines. The output is practical and actionable, designed to align with your existing resources and customer communication preferences. This prompt is for marketing strategists, growth teams, and business owners who need a structured framework to launch or improve referral acquisition programs without starting from scratch. ● Generates tailored referral incentive structures that balance customer motivation with budget realities. ● Identifies the most effective referral channels based on where your target audience already communicates. ● Establishes clear tracking metrics so you can measure program ROI and optimize over time. ● Delivers strategies as a clean markdown table, ready to share with your team or integrate into campaign planning documents. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in referral program design and customer acquisition.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive referral marketing strategy that generates leads and increases customer acquisition. Create a detailed plan outlining effective referral incentives, identifying appropriate referral channels, and establishing tracking metrics. Ensure the strategy is innovative, practical, and aligned with available resources.

## Context
Business and audience details:
{{business-and-audience}}
(Include: business name, what you offer, target audience characteristics, existing customer profile, budget available for referral incentives, and preferred communication channels)

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns: **Referral Incentives**, **Referral Channels**, and **Tracking Metrics**. Provide at least five distinct strategies in each column, ensuring they work together as a cohesive system tailored to the business context provided.
```
