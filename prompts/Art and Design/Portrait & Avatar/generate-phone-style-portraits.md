# Phone-Style Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Phone-Style Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, vertical portrait photographs with natural lighting and candid atmosphere for content creators, photographers, and social media professionals. This phone-style portrait prompt for Midjourney produces 9:16 aspect ratio images that mimic the look of casual smartphone photography - warm ambient lighting, laptop glow illuminating the subject, and a genuine late-night work scene aesthetic. You specify any subject and the prompt handles composition, lighting balance, and that coveted unposed feel that makes modern portrait photography relatable. Use it to generate profile pictures, social content, stock photography, or visual storytelling assets that feel immediate and human rather than staged. Reach for this prompt when you need vertical portraits optimized for Instagram stories, LinkedIn posts, TikTok thumbnails, or any platform where authenticity beats polish. ● Produces 9:16 vertical portraits ideal for mobile-first platforms and story formats ● Captures warm, low-light scenes with laptop glow as a key light source for authentic work-life imagery ● Applies controlled stylization (--s 30) and chaos (--c 5) for natural variation without over-processing ● Outputs high-quality renders (--q 2) suitable for professional use in marketing, editorial, and social content ## Prompt

```
Natural phone-style portrait of {{subject}} working late at desk with laptop glow, warm low light, candid mood --ar 9:16 --s 30 --q 2 --c 5
```
