# Novel Proofreading & Developmental Editing Prompt

## 簡介

The Novel Proofreading & Developmental Editing Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers multi-layered manuscript feedback for fiction authors seeking both line-level corrections and big-picture narrative analysis. This novel proofreading prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a fiction manuscript specialist, reviewing your text across six dimensions: grammar and mechanics (punctuation, syntax, spelling), flow and readability (transitions, pacing), structural coherence (plot consistency, narrative logic), character and theme development (arcs, thematic clarity), clarity (confusing passages), and reader engagement (tension, interest). It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by asking the model to provide category-organized feedback with specific examples, corrected passages, explanations of writing principles, and a prioritized summary of strengths and revision recommendations. Authors use it during manuscript revision to get actionable feedback that respects their voice while identifying weaknesses in both craft and storytelling. Reach for this prompt when you need comprehensive manuscript critique that goes beyond spell-checking to address narrative structure, character arcs, and reader investment. ● Catches grammar, punctuation, and syntax errors with corrected examples and explanations of common mistakes ● Evaluates sentence flow, paragraph transitions, and pacing to improve readability and narrative momentum ● Flags plot inconsistencies, pacing issues, and structural gaps that weaken story coherence ● Analyzes character development and thematic clarity, suggesting concrete ways to deepen arcs through dialogue and detail ● Highlights confusing or low-engagement passages with specific revision recommendations to increase reader investment ● Organizes all feedback by category with a summary that prioritizes key revisions and acknowledges manuscript strengths ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a proofreading expert specializing in fiction manuscripts, with expertise in grammar, narrative structure, and developmental editing.

## Task
Review the novel text for:
- **Grammar and mechanics:** Identify and correct errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling, and syntax. Explain common mistakes (subject-verb agreement, comma usage, fragments) with corrected examples.
- **Flow and readability:** Assess sentence and paragraph transitions. Recommend breaking up dense passages or restructuring for smoother reading.
- **Structure and coherence:** Evaluate plot consistency, pacing, and narrative logic. Flag gaps or discontinuities in storytelling.
- **Character and theme development:** Analyze character arcs and thematic clarity. Suggest strengthening through dialogue, action, or narrative detail.
- **Clarity:** Highlight confusing passages and propose specific revisions.
- **Engagement:** Identify sections lacking tension or interest. Recommend additions (scenes, dialogue, description) to increase reader investment.

## Context
The author seeks comprehensive feedback that addresses both line-level corrections and big-picture narrative elements. Preserve the author's voice and intent while elevating manuscript quality.

## Output
Provide structured feedback organized by category (grammar, flow, structure, character, clarity, engagement). Include:
- Specific examples from the text with suggested corrections or improvements
- Brief explanations of relevant rules or principles
- A concluding summary that highlights manuscript strengths and prioritizes key recommendations for revision

Novel text:
{{novel-text}}
```
