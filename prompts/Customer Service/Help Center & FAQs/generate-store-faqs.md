# Store FAQ Generator From Support Tickets

## 簡介

The Store FAQ Generator From Support Tickets is a free AI prompt that transforms common support inquiries into self-service FAQ content designed to resolve questions before customers need to ask. This store FAQ prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your top support tickets and brand voice guidelines, then delivers a six-phase transformation: support intelligence analysis, question psychology mapping (decoding the emotion behind "Where's my order?"), FAQ architecture design using customer mental models, answer engineering with a three-layer framework (answer first, explain second, anticipate third), optimization systems with search term mapping and A/B testing plans, and a complete implementation package with formatted content ready to deploy. Real use cases include e-commerce stores reducing ticket volume by addressing unstated customer concerns, B2B companies building efficiency-focused help centers, and international retailers proactively handling shipping and customs questions. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn support data into FAQs that actually prevent follow-up questions, not just document existing answers. ● Analyzes support ticket frequency and emotional triggers to identify underlying customer concerns behind surface-level questions ● Structures FAQ content using customer mental models with scannable formats, cross-linking strategy, and answer frameworks that prevent follow-ups ● Engineers three-layer answers (immediate response, essential details, anticipated next questions) aligned to your brand voice ● Delivers tracking systems with search term mapping, A/B testing plans, metrics dashboards, and a 30-day measurement plan for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Customer Experience Architect specializing in self-service content design. Your expertise is analyzing support patterns to create FAQ content that resolves questions before customers need to ask, reducing support volume while improving satisfaction.

## Task

Transform a store's most common support inquiries into high-performance FAQ content through a structured, phased approach. Each phase builds on the previous, moving from analysis to implementation.

## Context

Work with:

**{{support-data}}**  
(Include: top 10 most frequent support questions in order of frequency; current monthly ticket volume; industry and typical customer profile; any existing FAQ content and its performance)

**{{brand-voice}}**  
(Describe the brand's tone, communication style, and customer expectations)

Adapt your approach based on the context provided. For technical products, expand answer depth; for emotional purchases, emphasize reassurance; for B2B audiences, focus on efficiency; for policy-heavy inquiries, use decision trees; for international customers, address shipping and customs proactively.

## Output

Deliver a complete FAQ transformation across 6 phases:

**Phase 1: Support Intelligence Analysis**  
Analyze the support ecosystem to identify patterns, ticket triggers, and friction points. Map the relationship between stated questions and underlying customer concerns.

**Phase 2: Question Psychology Mapping**  
Decode the emotional drivers behind each question ("Where's my order?" = "Did you forget about me?"). Identify question clusters, journey friction points, and unstated concerns customers have but don't articulate.

**Phase 3: FAQ Architecture Design**  
Structure FAQs using proven information architecture principles:
- Logical category groupings based on customer mental models
- Scannable question formats optimized for quick navigation
- Answer frameworks that prevent follow-up questions
- Cross-linking strategy for related topics

**Phase 4: Answer Engineering**  
Craft each answer using the three-layer framework:
1. **Answer First**: Immediate answer in the opening sentence
2. **Explain Second**: Essential details in 2-3 concise sentences
3. **Anticipate Third**: Related information customers will need next

Match the brand voice while maintaining clarity and warmth.

**Phase 5: Optimization & Measurement**  
Build tracking and improvement systems:
- Search term mapping for each FAQ
- A/B testing variations for high-impact answers
- Metrics dashboard (support ticket reduction, FAQ engagement, search success rate)
- Feedback loop integration for continuous improvement

**Phase 6: Implementation Package**  
Deliver launch-ready materials:
- Formatted FAQ content for direct platform deployment
- Implementation checklist prioritized by impact
- Team training guide for maintaining FAQ quality
- 30-day measurement plan to track support volume reduction

For each phase, provide concrete deliverables formatted for immediate use. Explain your reasoning when making structural or content decisions.
```
