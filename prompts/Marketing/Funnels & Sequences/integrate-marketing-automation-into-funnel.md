# Marketing Automation Integration Plan for Sales Funnels

## 簡介

The Marketing Automation Integration Plan for Sales Funnels is a free AI prompt that designs a structured implementation roadmap for digital marketers seeking to automate lead nurturing and boost conversion rates. This marketing automation prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive, numbered action plan that addresses every stage of your sales funnel - awareness, consideration, decision, and retention. It specifies automation triggers, segmentation criteria, email campaign sequences, landing page requirements, and content assets tailored to your product, target audience, business metrics, and unique selling proposition. The output works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a coherent strategy that coordinates messaging across all touchpoints while including success metrics and optimization recommendations for each funnel stage. Use it when you need to map out how automation software will nurture leads from first contact through post-purchase engagement. ● Produces a multi-stage funnel plan with numbered steps and lettered sub-tasks for clarity ● Defines automation triggers, audience segments, and content types for each customer journey phase ● Specifies email campaign sequences, landing page elements, and coordinated messaging frameworks ● Includes KPIs, success metrics, and optimization tactics for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in marketing automation and conversion optimization.

## Task

Design a comprehensive step-by-step plan to integrate a marketing automation tool into the sales funnel. The plan must nurture leads and increase conversions through coordinated email campaigns, landing pages, and stage-specific content.

## Context

**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Business Metrics:** {{business-metrics}}
(Include current conversion rate, primary marketing channels, and any relevant KPIs)

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output Requirements

- Structure the plan as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps
- Address each stage of the funnel: awareness, consideration, decision, and retention
- Specify automation triggers, segment criteria, and content types for each touchpoint
- Ensure messaging is coherent and persuasive across email, landing pages, and content assets
- Include success metrics and optimization recommendations for each funnel stage
```
