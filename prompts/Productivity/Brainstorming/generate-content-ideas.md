# Content Idea Generator for Digital Marketing

## 簡介

The Content Idea Generator for Digital Marketing is a free AI prompt that produces actionable content ideas across formats for marketers, content teams, and business owners planning campaigns. This content ideas prompt for ChatGPT takes three inputs - business context, target audience, and brand voice - and returns at least 10 unique ideas in a markdown table with columns for topic, format, and target audience segment. It considers blog posts, video, social media, infographics, webinars, and case studies, weaving in trending topics, seasonal opportunities, audience pain points, and competitive differentiation. The output helps you balance educational, promotional, and engagement-focused content to drive both visibility and conversions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to fill an editorial calendar, pitch campaign concepts to clients, or break through creative block with ideas grounded in real audience needs and business goals. ● Outputs a structured markdown table with topic, format, and audience columns for easy handoff to designers and writers. ● Considers multiple content types - written, visual, interactive - so you cover every channel your audience uses. ● Factors in trending and seasonal angles to keep ideas timely and relevant. ● Balances pain-point solutions, aspirational messaging, and unique value propositions to differentiate from competitors. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in content planning and audience engagement.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of content ideas tailored to the specified business context. Each idea should be unique, actionable, and designed to drive engagement and conversions.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Brand voice and positioning: {{brand-voice}}

Consider:
- Multiple content formats (blog posts, video, social media, infographics, webinars, case studies, etc.)
- Trending topics and seasonal opportunities in the industry
- Content that addresses audience pain points and aspirations
- Ideas that differentiate from competitors and highlight unique value
- A mix of educational, promotional, and engagement-focused content

## Output
Present your output as a markdown table with three columns: **Topic**, **Format**, and **Target Audience**. Provide at least 10 distinct content ideas that align with the business goals and resonate with the intended audience.

Example structure:

| Topic | Format | Target Audience |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| [Specific topic] | [Content format] | [Audience segment] |
```
