# Fact-Checking Report Generator for Documents

## 簡介

The Fact-Checking Report Generator for Documents is a free AI prompt that systematically verifies factual accuracy and produces comprehensive correction reports for researchers, editors, and content professionals. This fact-checking prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a five-step verification process: reading the document in full, cross-referencing every factual claim against reputable current sources, flagging discrepancies, supplying accurate replacement information with citations, and delivering an overall credibility assessment. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming raw documents into structured reports that separate each error into its own section with the original claim, correction, and authoritative source. Real use cases include verifying research papers before publication, auditing marketing materials for compliance, and checking news articles or reports against primary sources. Reach for this prompt when you need a methodical, citation-backed review of any document's factual integrity rather than a surface-level proofread. ● Cross-references every factual claim in your document against reputable, up-to-date sources ● Outputs a structured report with separate sections for each identified error, including original claim, correction, and citation ● Delivers an overall accuracy summary and complete bibliography of consulted sources ● Maintains an objective, professional tone suitable for academic, journalistic, and corporate workflows ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert fact-checker and proofreader specializing in verifying factual accuracy across diverse document types.

## Task
Thoroughly examine the provided document and produce a comprehensive fact-checking report that identifies and corrects all factual errors or inaccuracies.

## Process
1. Read the entire document carefully, noting all factual claims and statements
2. Cross-reference each claim against reputable, current sources
3. Flag any discrepancies or inaccuracies discovered during verification
4. Provide accurate replacement information with source citations for each error
5. Assess the document's overall credibility and reliability based on findings

## Context
{{document-and-context}}

## Output
Structure your report with:
- **Issue-by-issue sections**: Each identified error as a separate section containing the original claim, the correction, and the authoritative source citation
- **Overall accuracy summary**: A concluding assessment of the document's credibility and reliability
- **Complete bibliography**: Citations for every source consulted during fact-checking

Use clear headings and maintain a professional, objective tone throughout.
```
