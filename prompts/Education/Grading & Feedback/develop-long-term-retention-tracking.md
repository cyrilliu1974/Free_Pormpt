# Graduate Retention Tracking Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Graduate Retention Tracking Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that generates a complete data analytics framework for measuring and analyzing long-term graduate outcomes in educational programs. This graduate retention tracking prompt for ChatGPT guides you through defining key retention metrics (employment rates, continued education, field alignment, satisfaction scores), identifying reliable data sources (alumni surveys, HR databases, LinkedIn, institutional records), and outlining analysis approaches tailored to your analytics tools and timeline. The output is a markdown table mapping each metric to its data sources and the actionable insights it yields, complete with visualization strategies and methods to address data limitations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting technical depth to your audience - whether executive leadership, academic boards, or funders. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or overhaul a graduate retention measurement system, prepare for accreditation reviews, or build data-driven improvement plans for educational programs. ● Generates 6–8 retention metrics that together provide a complete picture of graduate outcomes and program effectiveness. ● Maps each metric to specific data sources and collection methods, accounting for institutional resources and analytics tool capabilities. ● Defines analysis techniques and visualization strategies tailored to your report audience's technical level and decision-making needs. ● Identifies potential data limitations and proposes methods to address gaps in alumni tracking and survey response rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert data analyst specializing in educational program evaluation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan for tracking long-term retention rates of graduates using advanced data analytics. The plan should identify key metrics, data sources, collection methods, analysis approaches, and visualization strategies. Address potential challenges and propose how to derive actionable insights.

## Context
{{program-and-analytics-context}}
(Include: the educational program type, preferred data analytics tools, timeline for implementation, and available resources)

{{target-audience}}
(Specify who will receive the retention report—executive leadership, academic board, funders, etc.—to shape the level of technical detail and business focus)

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Metrics | Data Sources | Insights |
|---------|--------------|----------|

Each row should cover:
- **Metrics**: specific retention measures (employment rates, continued education, field alignment, satisfaction scores)
- **Data Sources**: where to collect each metric (alumni surveys, HR databases, LinkedIn, institutional records)
- **Insights**: analytical approach, visualization method, and the actionable conclusion each metric enables

Include 6-8 metrics that together provide a complete view of graduate retention. For each, note collection methods, analysis techniques suited to your tools, potential data limitations, and how findings should be visualized for your audience.
```
