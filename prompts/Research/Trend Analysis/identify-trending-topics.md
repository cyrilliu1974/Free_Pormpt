# Trending Topics Identifier for Influencer Campaigns

## 簡介

The Trending Topics Identifier for Influencer Campaigns is a free AI prompt that analyzes current trends and delivers ranked topic recommendations for digital marketers planning influencer campaigns. This trending topics prompt for ChatGPT evaluates search volume, social media sentiment, industry adoption signals, and audience alignment to produce a markdown table of 5-8 high-opportunity topics. Each entry includes the topic name, niche relevance justification, and reach assessment backed by platform performance data and audience metrics. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for strategists who need data-informed topic selection that fits campaign parameters and budget constraints. Use this prompt when planning influencer content calendars, launching niche campaigns, or evaluating which trends offer the strongest engagement potential for your target audience. ● Produces a structured table ranking 5-8 topics by opportunity, with clear justification for each ● Evaluates trends using search trajectory, social sentiment, expert commentary, and audience fit ● Assesses reach potential with supporting metrics from social platforms and industry data ● Filters topics by feasibility within your stated campaign constraints and niche focus ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in influencer marketing campaign planning.

## Task
Identify trending topics with high potential for reach and engagement in a specific niche. Analyze current trends, evaluate their relevance, and assess their potential impact to guide influencer marketing strategies and content creation.

## Context
**Campaign parameters:**
{{campaign-brief}}

**Research approach:**
Conduct research using reputable sources, social media platforms, and niche-specific forums. Evaluate each trend using:
- Search volume and trajectory
- Social media mention frequency and sentiment
- Industry expert commentary and adoption signals
- Alignment with the target audience's interests and behaviors
- Feasibility within stated constraints

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Topic**, **Relevance to Niche**, and **Potential Reach**.

For each entry, provide:
- Clear topic name
- Justification of relevance to the specified niche and audience
- Reach assessment with supporting evidence (metrics, platform performance, audience size)
- Brief rationale for its inclusion and potential campaign impact

Include 5-8 high-potential topics, ranked by overall opportunity.
```
