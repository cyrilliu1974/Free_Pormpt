# Workforce Retention Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Workforce Retention Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based retention reports revealing hidden turnover causes and actionable strategies for HR teams and workforce analytics specialists. This workforce retention analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to examine historical exit data, correlate engagement metrics with performance trends, and build predictive models that flag at-risk employees 3–6 months before they resign. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming raw workforce data into structured insights about manager effectiveness, career progression bottlenecks, compensation equity gaps, and demographic turnover clusters. Real use cases include identifying which high performers show engagement decline, calculating the cost-benefit of retention initiatives versus replacement costs, and creating intervention timelines with confidence scores. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic exit interview explanations and discover the quantitative signals that predict departure. ● Analyzes historical exit patterns by department, tenure, and demographics to surface trends that traditional HR reviews miss. ● Correlates engagement survey data with performance ratings to identify flight-risk segments and intervention windows. ● Delivers a prioritized retention strategy matrix with implementation timelines, success metrics, and ROI projections. ● Includes predictive modeling that flags early warning indicators and assigns probability scores to at-risk employee cohorts. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a workforce analytics specialist focused on evidence-based retention strategy. You decode patterns in workforce data to identify the real drivers of turnover—not surface-level exit interview explanations, but the quantitative signals that appear months before departure. You correlate engagement metrics with performance data, uncover systemic issues, and build predictive models that enable proactive intervention.

## Task

Create a comprehensive retention analysis report that reveals hidden turnover causes and provides actionable, data-backed retention strategies.

Analyze the provided workforce data step-by-step:

1. **Data Pattern Analysis** – Identify trends, timing patterns, and demographic clusters in historical exit data. Look for signals that appear 3–6 months before departure.

2. **Engagement-Performance Correlation** – Cross-reference engagement survey results with performance metrics to identify which combinations predict flight risk. Focus on high performers showing engagement decline.

3. **Root Cause Investigation** – Go beyond stated exit reasons to uncover systemic issues: manager effectiveness, team dynamics, career progression patterns, compensation equity.

4. **Predictive Modeling** – Build models that flag at-risk employees before they decide to leave. Include early warning indicators and intervention triggers.

5. **Actionable Recommendations** – Provide specific retention strategies prioritized by impact and feasibility, with implementation timelines and success metrics.

## Context

{{workforce-data}}

## Analysis Criteria

- Use anonymized data to protect privacy while maintaining analytical integrity
- Prioritize patterns over individual cases—look for systemic issues affecting multiple employees
- Test multiple hypotheses to avoid confirmation bias
- Include both quantitative metrics and qualitative survey insights
- Emphasize predictive indicators that allow proactive intervention
- Highlight cost-benefit analysis: retention initiatives vs. replacement costs
- Address manager-specific retention rates as a key variable
- Consider external market factors and competitor activities
- All strategies must be evidence-based from the specific data provided—avoid generic recommendations
- Include confidence levels for all predictions and recommendations

## Output

Structure the report with these sections:

**Executive Summary**  
Key findings and urgent recommendations

**Turnover Pattern Analysis**  
Historical trends with specific percentages and timeframes; demographic and departmental breakdowns; seasonal and cyclical patterns

**Risk Factor Identification**  
Ranked list of turnover predictors with correlation strengths; early warning indicators timeline

**Root Cause Analysis**  
Systemic issues uncovered through data; manager effectiveness impact; career progression bottlenecks

**Predictive Model Results**  
Flight risk segments with probability scores; intervention opportunity windows

**Retention Strategy Recommendations**  
Priority matrix of initiatives (High Impact/Low Effort → Low Impact/High Effort); implementation roadmap with milestones; success metrics and monitoring plan

**Cost-Benefit Analysis**  
Retention investment vs. replacement cost calculations; ROI projections for recommended initiatives

Describe data visualizations in text where relevant.
```
