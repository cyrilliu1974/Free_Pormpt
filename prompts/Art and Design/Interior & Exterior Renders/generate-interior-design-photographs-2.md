# Interior Design Photography Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Interior Design Photography Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic interior architectural photographs of styled apartment spaces with natural lighting and precise compositional control. This interior design prompt for Midjourney produces high-resolution images of apartment interiors with soft natural light filtering through floor-to-ceiling windows, casting gentle shadows and creating a serene atmosphere. You specify the design style (modern, Scandinavian, industrial, etc.) and featured wall shape, and the prompt renders a complete scene with complementary wall and flooring tones, minimalist furniture placement, and golden-hour lighting. The shot is framed from a low three-quarter angle with a 24mm lens perspective and deep depth of field, delivering professional-grade architectural photography. It runs on Midjourney with optimized parameters for quality and stylization.
