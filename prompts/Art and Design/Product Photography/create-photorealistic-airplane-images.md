# Photorealistic Airplane Takeoff Image Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Airplane Takeoff Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic aviation photography showing commercial aircraft departing major international airports during golden hour lighting. This airplane photography prompt for Midjourney produces high-detail images with a long telephoto perspective that mimics professional aviation photography shot on a Canon EOS R5. The prompt incorporates specific technical parameters including chaos variation, stylization strength, and a curated style reference to deliver consistent golden-hour lighting, runway detail, and that signature compressed telephoto look where the aircraft lifts dramatically against the warm sky. Use it for travel marketing, aviation content, airline presentations, or any project that needs professional-grade airplane imagery without coordinating airport access or waiting for perfect weather.
