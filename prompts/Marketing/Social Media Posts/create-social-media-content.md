# Social Media Content Calendar Creator

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Creator is a free AI prompt that builds structured, multi-platform content schedules for marketers and content strategists. This social media content calendar prompt for ChatGPT walks you through audience analysis, content theme development, platform-specific content mix planning, and performance tracking. It outputs a detailed calendar covering daily posts, weekly deep-dive content, and monthly anchor pieces across platforms like Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn, and TikTok. You provide product overview and marketing alignment details, and the prompt delivers a structured timeline that maps content types (blog posts, videos, infographics, stories, polls) to platform strengths, complete with KPIs for engagement, reach, and conversions. Ideal for marketing teams managing product launches, brands scaling their social presence, or agencies building repeatable content strategies for clients. ● Analyzes target audience demographics and behavior to tailor content themes ● Defines daily, weekly, and monthly content distribution across multiple platforms ● Aligns content with product launches, campaigns, and marketing events ● Specifies KPIs and review cadences for iterative performance optimization ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content creator and marketing strategist specializing in multi-platform audience engagement and strategic content planning.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive content calendar that educates, entertains, and engages the target audience, driving growth and maximizing interaction across platforms. The calendar will cover daily, weekly, and monthly content distribution with clear performance tracking.

## Context

{{product-overview}}

{{marketing-alignment}}

## Output

Deliver a detailed content calendar structured as follows:

### 1. Audience Analysis
Identify the target audience's demographics, interests, and online behavior to ensure content resonates with their preferences and needs.

### 2. Content Themes and Pillars
Establish 3-5 main content themes that align with the product's features, benefits, and the lifestyle or values it represents.

### 3. Content Mix Strategy
Define the blend of content types (blog posts, social media updates, videos, infographics) mapped to specific platforms (Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.). Match each content type to platform strengths and product messaging goals.

### 4. Calendar Planning

**Daily Plan**: Schedule social media updates and stories featuring timely topics, quick tips, user-generated content, polls, quizzes, and interactive elements.

**Weekly Plan**: Plan blog posts or videos providing deeper product insights—how-to guides, user testimonials, case studies, feature spotlights, and behind-the-scenes content.

**Monthly Plan**: Organize anchor content such as comprehensive infographics, influencer collaborations, monthly roundups, webinars, or campaign launches.

### 5. Marketing Campaign Alignment
Integrate upcoming product launches, campaigns, and events into the calendar. Create supporting content including teasers, countdowns, launch-day coverage, and post-event recaps.

### 6. Performance Tracking
Specify KPIs for each content type and platform: engagement rate, reach, impressions, click-through rate, conversion rate, audience growth, and sentiment. Include a review cadence (weekly check-ins, monthly deep dives) to iterate based on data.

Format the calendar as a table or timeline showing publication date, content type, platform, theme, objective, and assigned metrics.
```
