# Four-Week Meal Prep Roadmap Generator

## 簡介

The Four-Week Meal Prep Roadmap Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, week-by-week meal prep plans for busy individuals looking to streamline their cooking and eating habits. This meal prep roadmap prompt for ChatGPT walks you through an entire month of meals, delivering weekly grocery lists organized by store section, balanced breakfast-lunch-dinner schedules, batch cooking workflows optimized for efficiency, and detailed storage guidance to keep food fresh and safe. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to your dietary preferences, cooking skill level, and available weekly prep time. Use it when you want to eliminate daily cooking decisions, reduce food waste, and maintain nutritious eating without spending hours in the kitchen every night. ● Delivers four distinct weekly meal plans with breakfast, lunch, and dinner schedules to prevent meal fatigue ● Generates grocery lists organized by store section, minimizing shopping time and ingredient waste ● Provides step-by-step batch cooking workflows that optimize prep-day efficiency ● Includes container recommendations, shelf life estimates, and reheating instructions for every meal ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert meal prep specialist with deep knowledge of nutrition, cooking efficiency, and practical meal planning for busy schedules.

## Task
Create a comprehensive four-week meal prep roadmap that streamlines the user's cooking and eating habits. The plan should be realistic, time-efficient, and sustainable.

## Context
{{dietary-preferences-and-constraints}}
{{cooking-skill-level}}
{{weekly-prep-time-available}}

## Requirements

**Nutritional standards:**
- All meals balanced across protein, vegetables, whole grains, and healthy fats
- Variety across the four weeks to prevent meal fatigue
- Recipes that scale well for batch cooking and store safely

**Practical considerations:**
- Cost-effective ingredients with minimal waste
- Mix of cold-ready and reheat-friendly meals
- Proper food safety and storage guidance to maximize freshness
- Adaptation options for different dietary needs

## Output

**Introduction:** Brief overview of meal prep benefits and how this plan works.

**For each of the four weeks, provide:**

1. **Weekly Meal Plan Overview** (table format showing breakfast/lunch/dinner across 7 days)
2. **Complete Grocery List** organized by store section
3. **Meal Recipes** with ingredients and simple instructions for each dish
4. **Prep Day Workflow** - step-by-step batch cooking sequence optimized for efficiency
5. **Storage Instructions** - containers, shelf life, and reheating guidance for each meal
6. **Estimated Prep Time** for that week's batch cooking session

**Sustainability Tips:**
- How to maintain variety and prevent boredom
- Strategies for adapting the system long-term
- Troubleshooting common meal prep challenges

Use clear headings, bullet points for lists, numbered steps for instructions, and tables where helpful for quick reference.
```
