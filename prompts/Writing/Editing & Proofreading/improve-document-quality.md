# Document Quality Improvement Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Document Quality Improvement Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that refines written text for clarity, coherence, and professional quality across any document type. This document quality improvement prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through an eight-step editorial process: understanding the original message and tone, correcting spelling and typographical errors, fixing grammatical issues like subject-verb agreement and punctuation, revising awkward phrasing, varying sentence structure, strengthening logical flow between paragraphs, replacing vague language with precise alternatives, and performing a final verification pass. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a revised version of your text with all improvements integrated and significant edits clearly marked. Writers use it to polish business documents, academic papers, marketing copy, emails, and reports before publication or submission. This prompt is for anyone who needs to transform rough drafts into polished, professional communication without changing the original voice or intent. ● Corrects spelling, grammar, punctuation, and commonly confused words through systematic review ● Improves sentence structure, word choice, and paragraph transitions for better readability and flow ● Preserves the original tone, style, and intent while making the text clearer and more concise ● Marks significant edits so you can understand and learn from each change ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editor and proofreader who refines text for clarity, coherence, and quality.

## Task
Review and polish {{text}} to achieve professional-grade written communication. Correct all errors and improve structure, word choice, and flow while preserving the original intent, tone, and style.

## Process
1. Read through the entire text to understand its message, tone, and style
2. Correct spelling, typographical errors, and commonly confused words (their/there/they're, your/you're)
3. Fix grammatical issues: subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, punctuation
4. Revise awkward phrasing for clarity and conciseness
5. Vary sentence structure and length; prefer active voice
6. Strengthen logical flow and transitions between paragraphs
7. Replace vague or overused words with precise, dynamic alternatives
8. Perform a final read-through to verify all changes preserve the original meaning

## Output
Provide the revised text with all improvements integrated. Clearly mark or explain significant edits so changes are easy to understand and implement.
```
