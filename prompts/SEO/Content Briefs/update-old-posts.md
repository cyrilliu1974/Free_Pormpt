# Blog Post Refresh and SEO Update Prompt

## 簡介

The Blog Post Refresh and SEO Update Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes existing blog content and delivers actionable revision recommendations for content strategists and SEO professionals. This blog post refresh prompt for ChatGPT takes your article context and primary keywords, then produces a structured three-column comparison table showing original content excerpts, proposed updates, and the SEO or readability rationale behind each change. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying current search optimization best practices to modernize outdated statistics, weave in target keywords naturally, sharpen titles and meta descriptions, improve header hierarchy, enhance content structure, and strengthen internal and external linking strategies. Content teams use it to systematically audit aging posts, prioritize high-impact revisions, and align older articles with current search intent and audience expectations. Reach for this prompt when you need to breathe new life into blog archives, respond to algorithm updates, or prepare content refresh roadmaps that balance SEO gains with editorial quality. ● Produces a markdown comparison table mapping original excerpts to proposed updates with impact justification ● Covers titles, meta descriptions, headers, body copy, keyword integration, and linking improvements in one audit ● Prioritizes changes by SEO and engagement impact so teams tackle high-value edits first ● Incorporates dependency grammar principles and readability improvements alongside technical SEO fixes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in blog refresh and SEO optimization.

## Task
Analyze and rewrite an existing blog article to improve search visibility, relevance, and engagement for the target audience. Deliver your recommendations as a structured comparison showing what to change and why.

## Context
You will work with:
- **Article subject and audience**: {{article-context}} (include the topic, target audience, current URL if available, and what the content should achieve)
- **SEO focus**: {{primary-keywords}}

Apply current SEO best practices: update outdated statistics, incorporate target keywords naturally, optimize titles/meta descriptions/headers, improve readability and content structure, add credible examples or expert perspectives, and refine internal/external linking. Ensure the refreshed content meets the audience's current needs and search intent.

## Output
Deliver your recommendations in a markdown table with three columns:

| Original Content | Updated Content | Rationale for Changes |
|------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| [excerpt or element from current post] | [your proposed revision] | [why this change improves SEO, readability, or relevance] |

Cover key elements: title, meta description, headers, body sections with outdated information, keyword opportunities, linking improvements, and any structural changes. Prioritize changes by impact.
```
