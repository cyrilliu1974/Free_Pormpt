# Goal Communication Best Practices Generator

## 簡介

The Goal Communication Best Practices Generator is a free AI prompt that produces five structured, actionable strategies for leaders and managers to communicate objectives clearly to stakeholders and team members. This goal communication prompt for ChatGPT takes your organizational context, audience profile, and available channels, then outputs a numbered list of five best practices across four dimensions: clarity, alignment, engagement, and ongoing communication. Each practice includes two concrete sub-bullets with implementation examples or guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to tailor communication frameworks to any team size or industry. Use it when launching new initiatives, cascading OKRs, or improving stakeholder buy-in during strategic planning cycles. ● Creates five specific best practices, each with two implementation sub-bullets, ensuring guidance is concrete rather than generic ● Covers clarity, alignment, engagement, and ongoing communication to address the full lifecycle of goal-setting ● Adapts to your goals, audience, and channels for context-aware recommendations ● Outputs a clean, numbered format that can be shared directly with leadership or used in team meetings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert communicator and strategic advisor specializing in goal communication frameworks.

## Task
Create a numbered list of 5 specific, actionable best practices for communicating goals effectively to stakeholders and team members. Each practice must include 2 sub-bullets with concrete examples or implementation guidance.

## Context
Goals and organizational context: {{goals-and-context}}

Stakeholders and team: {{audience-description}}

Available communication channels: {{channels}}

## Requirements
- Focus on four core dimensions: clarity, alignment, engagement, and ongoing communication
- Provide actionable, universally applicable practices—avoid generic platitudes
- Examples should clearly illustrate implementation
- Ensure practices are specific enough to be immediately useful

## Output Format
1. Best Practice 1
   - Example or elaboration 1a
   - Example or elaboration 1b

2. Best Practice 2
   - Example or elaboration 2a
   - Example or elaboration 2b

3. Best Practice 3
   - Example or elaboration 3a
   - Example or elaboration 3b

4. Best Practice 4
   - Example or elaboration 4a
   - Example or elaboration 4b

5. Best Practice 5
   - Example or elaboration 5a
   - Example or elaboration 5b
```
