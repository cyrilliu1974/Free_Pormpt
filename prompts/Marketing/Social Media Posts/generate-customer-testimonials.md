# Testimonial Generator for Social Media Posts

## 簡介

The Testimonial Generator for Social Media Posts is a free AI prompt that creates realistic customer testimonials tailored to your product and target audience. This testimonial prompt for ChatGPT produces a set of 20 complete testimonials, each featuring a unique customer name, background, product experience quote, emotional impact statement, and recommendation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating text that sounds like real people sharing honest experiences rather than polished marketing copy. Marketers use it to populate social proof sections, test messaging angles, or draft testimonial templates before collecting real customer feedback. Each output uses plain, middle-school-level language and varied emotional angles to ensure the set feels diverse and genuine. Reach for this prompt when you need a batch of social-media-ready testimonials that reflect how your target audience actually speaks and what they care about. ● Outputs 20 full testimonials with customer name, background, experience quote, emotional impact, and recommendation in a consistent format ● Uses simple, conversational language that mirrors how real customers write online reviews and social media posts ● Accepts two variables - product or service and target audience - to tailor tone, benefits, and emotional angles ● Generates varied testimonials across the set, avoiding repetitive phrasing or cookie-cutter structure ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write authentic, relatable customer testimonials for social media. Your language stays simple and conversational, like a real person sharing their honest experience.

## Context

**Product / Service:** {{product-or-service}}  
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Task

Generate 20 customer testimonials that resonate with {{target-audience}}. Each testimonial must:

- Use plain, middle-school-level language (no jargon)
- Focus on a real benefit or emotional moment
- Feel like something a genuine customer would post online
- Vary the customer names, backgrounds, and emotional angles across the set

## Output

Produce all 20 testimonials in this format:

---

**Testimonial 1:**  
Customer Name: Sarah Martinez  
Customer Background: Busy mom of three, works part-time from home  
Product Experience: "This totally changed my mornings. I used to feel stressed getting everyone ready, but now I actually have time to breathe."  
Emotional Impact: Relief and confidence  
Recommendation: Would tell every parent friend about it

**Testimonial 2:**  
Customer Name: James Chen  
Customer Background: College student juggling classes and a campus job  
Product Experience: "I was skeptical at first, but after the first week I noticed a huge difference. Wish I'd found this sooner."  
Emotional Impact: Pleasant surprise and gratitude  
Recommendation: Already shared with his study group

**Testimonial 3:**  
Customer Name: Aisha Okafor  
Customer Background: Small business owner running a local bakery  
Product Experience: "Game changer. I can focus on baking instead of worrying about the million other things."  
Emotional Impact: Freedom and renewed passion  
Recommendation: Calls it essential for entrepreneurs

[Continue with Testimonials 4–20 following the same structure, each with distinct names, backgrounds, experiences, emotional impacts, and recommendations that authentically reflect {{target-audience}} needs and language.]
```
