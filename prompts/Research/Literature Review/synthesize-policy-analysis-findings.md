# Policy Analysis Synthesis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Policy Analysis Synthesis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that reviews recent government and NGO reports on any policy topic and produces a structured analysis with complete source citations for researchers and analysts. This policy analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by drawing exclusively from reports published within the past three years, extracting key findings, identifying where sources agree or contradict one another, highlighting gaps in current policy approaches, and compiling common recommendations across multiple organizations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing citation-backed syntheses ready for policy briefs, literature reviews, or stakeholder presentations. Policy researchers use it to quickly map the landscape of expert opinion on issues ranging from climate adaptation to healthcare reform, while advocacy groups apply it to ground their position papers in documented evidence from authoritative sources. Reach for this prompt when you need to synthesize multiple authoritative reports into a single coherent analysis that shows where the field stands, where experts disagree, and what gaps remain unaddressed. ● Extracts key findings from government and NGO reports published in the past three years with source-specific citations ● Maps areas of consensus and documents disagreements by linking opposing views to their exact sources ● Identifies policy gaps where current reports acknowledge missing data or unaddressed issues ● Compiles common recommendations and tracks which organizations proposed each solution ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert policy analyst specializing in synthesizing government and NGO reports.

## Task
Review and analyze reports from government agencies and NGOs published within the past three years on {{topic}}. Synthesize key findings, identify patterns of consensus and disagreement, highlight policy gaps, and extract common recommendations. Present your analysis in a structured format with complete citations.

## Requirements
- Draw exclusively from government and NGO reports (past 3 years)
- Cite specific sources for every finding, consensus point, disagreement, gap, and recommendation
- Stay faithful to what the reports state; do not add external analysis or opinion
- Link disagreements to the specific sources holding opposing views
- Provide full bibliographic citations for all sources referenced

## Output Format

**Topic:** {{topic}}

**Key Findings:**
1. [Finding 1] (Source: [Citation 1])
2. [Finding 2] (Source: [Citation 2])
3. [Finding 3] (Source: [Citation 3])

**Areas of Consensus:**
- [Consensus point 1]
- [Consensus point 2]

**Areas of Disagreement:**
- [Disagreement 1] ([Source A] vs. [Source B])
- [Disagreement 2] ([Source C] vs. [Source D])

**Policy Gaps:**
- [Gap 1]
- [Gap 2]
- [Gap 3]

**Common Recommendations:**
1. [Recommendation 1] (Proposed by: [Source E], [Source F])
2. [Recommendation 2] (Proposed by: [Source G])
3. [Recommendation 3] (Proposed by: [Source H], [Source I], [Source J])

**Sources:**
[1] [Full citation 1]
[2] [Full citation 2]
[3] [Full citation 3]
[Continue for all sources referenced]
```
