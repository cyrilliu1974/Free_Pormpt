# Short Story Generator With Narrative Structure

## 簡介

The Short Story Generator With Narrative Structure is a free AI prompt that writes complete fiction with strong character arcs, cohesive plots, and vivid sensory detail for authors, creative writers, and storytellers. This short story prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to follow classic five-act structure - introduction, rising action, climax, falling action, and resolution - while developing characters who evolve meaningfully through conflict. You provide a story premise variable, and the AI delivers a full narrative with dialogue that reveals personality, descriptive language that immerses readers, and a thematic conclusion that resonates. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, maintaining consistent tone and point of view throughout. Writers use it to draft original fiction, explore genre conventions, or generate story drafts that avoid clichés and stand alone without external context. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete, structured short story rather than fragments or outlines. ● Delivers five-act structure with clear scene breaks, dialogue, and sensory detail ● Develops multi-dimensional characters who face meaningful conflicts and grow through the plot ● Maintains consistent tone, style, and point of view from hook to resolution ● Generates original fiction that avoids clichés and ties thematic elements to a satisfying conclusion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an accomplished short story writer who crafts compelling narratives with strong character arcs, cohesive plots, and immersive prose.

## Task
Write an engaging short story that follows classic narrative structure: introduction, rising action, climax, falling action, and resolution. Develop multi-dimensional characters who evolve through the story, build tension that keeps readers invested, and deliver a satisfying conclusion that ties back to the central theme.

## Context
The story should:
- Follow the genre conventions and thematic direction specified in {{story-premise}}
- Create relatable characters who face meaningful conflicts
- Use vivid sensory details and dialogue to bring scenes to life
- Maintain consistent tone, style, and point of view throughout
- Avoid clichés and strive for originality
- Stand on its own without requiring external context

## Output
Deliver a complete short story formatted with:
- Clear paragraph breaks indicating scene, time, or perspective shifts
- Descriptive language that immerses without overwhelming
- Dialogue that reveals personality and advances plot
- Proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation
- A coherent arc from hook to resolution that leaves readers with something to ponder
```
