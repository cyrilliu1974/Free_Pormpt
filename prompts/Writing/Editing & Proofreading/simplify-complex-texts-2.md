# Complex Text Simplification Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Complex Text Simplification Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that rewrites technical or intricate passages into plain language accessible to general audiences. This text simplification prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to break down complicated concepts, replace jargon with everyday words, and restructure sentences for clarity without losing the original message. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it practical for editing technical documentation, customer-facing materials, educational content, or internal communications that need to reach non-specialist readers. The prompt follows a seven-step process: identifying key concepts, segmenting ideas, replacing technical terms, using relatable examples, writing in active voice, ensuring logical flow, and verifying accuracy. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate specialized content for broad audiences, improve readability scores, or ensure compliance materials are understandable to employees without domain expertise. ● Transforms technical jargon and industry-specific terminology into plain, everyday language ● Preserves the original meaning and critical details while restructuring sentences for readability ● Uses a structured seven-step process including analogies, active voice, and logical flow checks ● Outputs only the rewritten text, ready to publish or share without further editing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert at rewriting complex texts into clear, simple language that anyone can understand.

## Task
Rewrite the provided passage to make it accessible to a general audience. Preserve the original meaning while eliminating jargon, technical terms, and unnecessarily complex phrasing. Use a professional yet friendly tone that works for readers of any background.

## Process
1. Identify the key concepts and main message in the original text
2. Break the text into smaller segments, each focused on one main idea
3. Replace technical terms and jargon with plain, everyday words
4. Explain complex concepts using analogies or relatable examples
5. Write short, direct sentences in active voice
6. Ensure logical flow and coherent structure throughout
7. Verify the rewritten version accurately reflects the original meaning without losing important details

## Example
**Original:**
"Utilizing advanced algorithms, the software systematically analyzes vast datasets to identify patterns and anomalies indicative of potential cybersecurity threats."

**Rewritten:**
"The software uses special computer programs to look through huge amounts of data. It looks for patterns or unusual things that might show there are risks to computer security."

## Input
{{complex-text}}

## Output
Provide the rewritten version only—clear, concise, jargon-free, and accessible to a wide audience while preserving the original message.
```
