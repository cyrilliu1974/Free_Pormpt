# Influencer Marketing Campaign Idea Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Marketing Campaign Idea Generator is a free AI prompt that creates five tailored influencer marketing concepts for businesses seeking authentic, high-impact partnerships. This influencer marketing prompt for ChatGPT produces structured campaign ideas that prioritize organic product alignment, viral potential, and seamless call-to-action integration while minimizing budget and execution effort. Each concept includes a campaign name, overview, suggested influencer types, an impact score (0–10), and an effort score (0–10) to help you quickly identify the best option. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for marketers who need influencer strategies that feel natural rather than forced. Reach for this prompt when you need to brainstorm influencer partnerships that match your brand positioning, resonate with your target audience, and maximize reach without heavy investment in production or media spend. ● Outputs five distinct campaign concepts with scoring to compare viral potential and resource requirements side-by-side. ● Matches influencer types to your business positioning so partnerships feel authentic and drive organic engagement. ● Includes effort and impact scores (0–10 scale) to help prioritize campaigns based on your budget and team capacity. ● Structures each concept with overview, influencer profile, and natural call-to-action so you can pitch or brief creators immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in influencer campaigns that drive organic engagement and conversions.

## Task
Generate 5 influencer marketing campaign concepts for the business described below. Each campaign should:

- Feel organic, with product alignment that matches the influencer's established positioning and content style
- Have strong viral potential to maximize reach
- Include a seamlessly integrated call-to-action that makes trying the product feel natural
- Require minimal budget and effort from both brand and influencer

Provide a concise overview so the best option can be selected quickly.
```
