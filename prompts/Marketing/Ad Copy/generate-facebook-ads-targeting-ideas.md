# Facebook Ad Targeting Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Facebook Ad Targeting Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces complete Facebook ad concepts with headlines, copy, and audience targeting recommendations for businesses and marketers. This Facebook ad targeting prompt for ChatGPT takes a single business-context input - your business type, product or service, unique selling proposition, target demographics, and advertising objective - and returns at least five distinct ad ideas in a structured markdown table. Each row delivers a ready-to-test headline, persuasive ad copy, and specific audience targeting parameters, ensuring every concept aligns with your goals and reaches different angles or segments within your market. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it a versatile planning tool for digital marketers, agency teams, and small-business owners managing their own Facebook campaigns. Use this prompt when you need a fast creative sprint for Facebook Ads Manager, want to explore multiple audience segments at once, or need inspiration that ties messaging to targeting strategy in one view. ● Produces at least five unique ad concepts per run, each exploring a different creative angle or audience segment. ● Outputs a three-column markdown table (Ad Headline, Ad Copy, Target Audience) that is easy to review, share, and import into campaign briefs. ● Adapts to any business context - e-commerce, SaaS, local services, lead generation, or brand awareness. ● Saves hours of brainstorming by combining creative ideation and targeting strategy in one prompt. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Facebook advertising strategist specializing in audience-targeted campaign development.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of Facebook ad concepts tailored to the business context provided. For each ad idea, create:
- A compelling ad headline that captures attention
- Persuasive ad copy that drives action
- Precise audience targeting recommendations

Ensure each concept is unique, engaging, and aligned with the business goals and audience preferences.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
(Include: business type, key product/service, unique selling proposition, target audience demographics and psychographics, primary advertising objective)

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns: **Ad Headline** | **Ad Copy** | **Target Audience**. Provide at least 5 distinct ad ideas, each optimized for different angles or audience segments within the overall target market.
```
