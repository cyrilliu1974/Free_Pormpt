# Literary Agent Research Table Generator

## 簡介

The Literary Agent Research Table Generator is a free AI prompt that compiles verified, up-to-date tables of literary agents actively acquiring manuscripts in your chosen genre. This literary agent research prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table with agent names, their agencies, notable clients they represent, current submission guidelines, and accurate contact information. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, asking the AI to act as an expert literary agent researcher who verifies credentials, reviews current submission policies, and prioritizes agents open to queries with recent sales in your target genre. Authors use it to streamline the querying process when seeking representation, turning hours of manual research into a single organized output that can be filtered by genre and customized to specific research requirements. This prompt is for novelists, nonfiction writers, and debut authors preparing to query agents, or for writing coaches and publishing consultants who guide clients through the submission process. ● Produces a 10-15 row table with agent name, agency, notable clients, submission guidelines, and contact information ● Filters for agents currently open to queries and those with recent sales in the target genre ● Verifies agent credentials and client lists to ensure accuracy and relevance ● Accepts custom research requirements to tailor the output to manuscript type, geographic focus, or other criteria ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literary agent researcher specializing in the publishing industry.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive, well-organized table of literary agents with proven track records in {{genre}}. Research reputable agencies, verify agent credentials and client lists, review current submission guidelines, and gather accurate contact information.

## Context
Authors seeking representation need reliable, up-to-date information to target the right agents for their work. Your research should focus on agents actively acquiring manuscripts in the specified genre, with demonstrated success placing books with publishers.

{{research-requirements}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with these columns:
- Agent Name
- Agency
- Notable Clients
- Submission Guidelines
- Contact Information

Include 10-15 agents unless otherwise specified. Prioritize agents currently open to queries and those with recent sales in the genre.
```
