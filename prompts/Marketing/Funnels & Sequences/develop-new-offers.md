# Affiliate Marketing Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Affiliate Marketing Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds complete affiliate program strategies for businesses launching or scaling partner-driven sales channels. This affiliate marketing prompt for ChatGPT takes your product details, target audience, unique selling proposition, and campaign goals to produce a markdown table organizing potential affiliate partners by segment, competitive commission structures tailored to incentivize promotion, and specific promotional materials needed for each partner type. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering 4-6 rows that cover distinct partner categories - from niche bloggers and YouTube influencers to deal sites and email list owners - with actionable guidance on monitoring and optimizing campaign performance. Marketing managers use it to map out affiliate programs before launch, affiliate coordinators rely on it to expand into new partner categories, and founders turn to it when exploring performance-based growth channels without upfront ad spend. ● Identifies 4-6 affiliate partner types aligned to your target audience and niche, from micro-influencers to comparison sites ● Proposes commission structures (percentage, flat-rate, tiered, or hybrid) matched to each partner segment's promotional effort ● Specifies promotional materials - banners, product copy, email templates - customized to showcase your unique selling proposition ● Includes campaign monitoring and optimization recommendations to maximize affiliate ROI within your budget constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert affiliate marketing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive affiliate marketing plan to promote {{product-or-service}} and drive sales through partner websites and influencers.

## Context
**Offering:** {{product-or-service}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Campaign goals & budget constraints:** {{goals-and-budget}}

Identify potential affiliate partners that align with the target audience and have proven success in the relevant niche. Develop a competitive commission structure that incentivizes active promotion. Create engaging promotional materials (banners, product descriptions, email templates) that effectively showcase the offering's benefits. Include guidance for monitoring and optimizing campaigns to maximize ROI.

## Output
Deliver the plan as a markdown table with exactly 3 columns:

| Affiliate Partners | Commission Structure | Promotional Materials |

Each row should represent a distinct affiliate strategy segment. Provide 4-6 rows covering different partner types or audience channels.
```
