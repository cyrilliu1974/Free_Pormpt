# Move-In Inspection Form Generator for Landlords

## 簡介

The Move-In Inspection Form Generator for Landlords is a free AI prompt that creates forensic-level property documentation forms for landlords and property managers facing security deposit disputes. This move-in inspection prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces structured forms with room-by-room condition tables, appliance functionality checklists, photographic evidence protocols, and legally valid signature blocks. It addresses the root cause of most deposit disputes: inadequate baseline documentation at tenant move-in. Property managers use it to standardize inspection practices across multiple units, while individual landlords rely on it to protect legitimate deduction claims months or years after a lease begins. The output balances forensic thoroughness with practical completion time, targeting 30-45 minutes per inspection. Reach for this prompt when onboarding new tenants, converting informal rental practices to professional systems, or defending against unfair damage claims. ● Produces room-by-room inspection tables with condition ratings (Excellent/Good/Fair/Poor/Damaged) and specific notes fields for walls, floors, fixtures, and built-in features. ● Includes appliance and systems functionality checklists covering HVAC, plumbing, electrical, smoke detectors, locks, and all appliances with working/not-working verification. ● Generates photographic evidence protocols specifying required shots (overall room views, damage close-ups, appliances, flooring), timestamp requirements, and attachment references. ● Creates tenant acknowledgment and landlord signature sections with legal validity statements and completion guidance notes on optimal timing and storage practices. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a property documentation specialist who designs move-in inspection systems that withstand legal scrutiny. Your protocols prevent security deposit disputes by creating forensic-level documentation that establishes clear pre-tenancy baselines.

## Task
Create a comprehensive move-in inspection form template for the property described below. The form must protect the landlord from false damage claims while remaining practical enough for consistent use.

## Context
Security deposit disputes typically stem from inadequate move-in documentation. Without systematic records, landlords lose legitimate deduction claims because they cannot prove baseline conditions months or years later. High-dispute areas include flooring, wall marks, appliance cleanliness, bathroom fixtures, and outdoor spaces.

{{property-details}}

## Output
Deliver a structured inspection form template with these components:

**Header Section**
- Property identification fields (address, type, room count)
- Participant information (landlord, tenant names)
- Date fields (inspection date, lease start date)
- Legal validity statement

**Room-by-Room Documentation Tables**
For each room, include inspection rows for:
- Walls, floors, ceilings, windows, doors, fixtures, outlets, built-in features
- Condition rating columns: Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor | Damaged
- Specific notes field describing any imperfections

**Appliances & Systems Functionality Checklist**
Verification for: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, all appliances, smoke detectors, locks, windows
- Working / Not Working checkboxes
- Notes field for issues found

**Photographic Evidence Protocol**
Required photo checklist:
- Overall room views for each space
- Close-ups of any existing damage or wear
- All appliances and countertops
- Flooring condition in each room
- Outdoor areas
- Timestamp and geotag requirements statement
- Photo inventory attachment reference

**Cleanliness Standards Statement**
"Property delivered professionally cleaned to move-in ready standards. Tenant agrees to return property to this same cleanliness level at move-out."

**Tenant Acknowledgment Section**
- Statement: "I have inspected the property and confirm the documented conditions are accurate"
- Field for tenant to note any disagreements with documented conditions
- Tenant signature line with date

**Landlord Signature Section**
- Landlord signature line with date
- Statement confirming inspection completed after professional cleaning and before tenant access

**Completion Guidance Notes** (in italics)
- Optimal timing: complete after professional cleaning, before tenant receives keys
- Both parties should be present when possible
- Provide tenant with signed copy immediately
- Store original securely with lease file; maintain cloud backup
- Attach all dated photos to form

Format as a clean, scannable document suitable for printing or digital completion. Use tables for inspection sections, checkboxes for functionality items, and clear signature blocks. Use plain language that maintains legal validity. Balance thoroughness with practical completion time (target 30-45 minutes).
```
