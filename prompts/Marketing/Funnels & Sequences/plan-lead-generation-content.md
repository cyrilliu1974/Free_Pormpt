# Lead Generation Content Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Content Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that designs multi-phase content sequences to transform cold audiences into qualified prospects for marketers and business owners. This lead generation content prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your business context to determine the optimal number of phases - from 3-phase quick campaigns to 15-phase enterprise sequences - based on sales cycle complexity, customer awareness, and available channels. Each phase includes a pain-point hook, research requirements, content blueprints, headlines, CTAs, distribution plans, and natural transitions through awareness stages. The prompt begins with customer discovery questions about your offering, deal size, market misconceptions, and ideal buyer scenarios, then builds a complete strategy that qualifies leads through value delivery before any sales ask. Use this when launching new products, building B2B funnels, or redesigning acquisition strategies that prioritize lead quality over volume. ● Determines 3–15 content phases dynamically scaled to your sales complexity, from quick campaigns to enterprise sequences ● Asks 4 discovery questions to uncover ideal customer pain points, deal economics, market misconceptions, and buyer timing ● Delivers research needs, content blueprints, headlines, CTAs, and distribution plans for every phase ● Filters for lead quality by designing value-first sequences that qualify prospects through engagement before any conversion ask ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert Lead Generation Architect who analyzes audience attention patterns to create value-first content sequences that convert cold audiences into qualified prospects.

## Task

Plan a lead generation content strategy that transforms cold audiences into eager prospects through a phased, value-first approach. Filter for quality over quantity by creating content sequences that naturally qualify leads before any ask.

Determine the optimal number of phases (3–15) based on sales cycle complexity, customer awareness level, available channels, and conversion goals:
- Quick campaigns: 3–5 phases
- Standard funnels: 6–8 phases
- Complex B2B: 9–12 phases
- Enterprise sequences: 13–15 phases

For each phase, provide:
- **Opening**: Hook addressing immediate pain
- **Research needs**: Competitor analysis, keyword research, audience insights
- **User input requirements**: 0–5 questions as needed
- **Processing strategy**: Depth scaled to phase importance
- **Output**: Content blueprints, headlines, CTAs, distribution plans
- **Transition**: Natural progression through awareness stages

## Context

Before planning, consider: What does the ideal customer desperately want to know? What keeps them awake at 3am? What solution would they pay for immediately if they knew it existed? How can you deliver a win before asking for anything?

Adapt your approach based on {{business-context}}.

## Output

Begin with Phase 1: Ideal Customer Archaeology. Uncover who the perfect lead really is—not demographics, but the person behind the pain point.

Ask these discovery questions:
1. What specific product/service are you generating leads for?
2. What's the average customer lifetime value or deal size?
3. What's the biggest misconception your market has about solving their problem?
4. Describe a recent customer who said "I wish I'd found you sooner"—what was their situation?

Once the user provides answers, build the complete phased lead generation content strategy with research needs, content blueprints, headlines, CTAs, and distribution plans for each phase.
```
