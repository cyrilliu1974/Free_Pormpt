# Email Subscriber List Builder

## 簡介

The Email Subscriber List Builder is a free AI prompt that creates complete subscriber acquisition and engagement campaigns for businesses looking to grow their email lists. This email list building prompt for ChatGPT delivers opt-in form strategies with headline and copy variations, multi-email welcome sequences that establish immediate connection, and segmented nurture flows designed to drive conversions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and structures each component using dependency grammar principles so your forms, welcome messages, and ongoing sequences build logically on one another. Use it when you need a full-funnel email strategy - from first visit to repeat engagement - customized to your product, audience demographics, and brand voice. ● Opt-in form placement, design, and value-proposition copy tailored to minimize friction and maximize sign-ups ● Welcome email series with subject lines, preview text, timing, and relationship-building messaging ● Nurture sequence architecture including segmentation logic, content cadence, engagement triggers, and conversion pathways ● Audience analysis that maps motivations, preferences, and objections to every campaign element ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist building subscriber acquisition and engagement campaigns.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email marketing campaign including opt-in forms, welcome email series, and nurture sequences that grow and engage a subscriber list. Structure your strategy using dependency grammar principles—each element should build logically on the previous, with clear relationships between components.

## Context
Business/Product: {{business-product}}

Target Audience & Goals: {{audience-and-goals}}
(Include demographics, psychographics, behaviors, pain points, and the primary marketing objectives this campaign should achieve)

Brand Identity: {{brand-voice-and-usp}}
(Describe the unique value proposition, brand voice, tone, and key differentiators)

## Approach
1. Analyze the target audience's motivations, preferences, and objections
2. Design opt-in forms that clearly communicate value and minimize friction
3. Develop welcome emails that build immediate connection and set expectations
4. Create nurture sequences that progressively deepen engagement and guide toward conversion

## Output
Deliver the complete campaign strategy organized under these headings:

**Opt-in Forms**
- Form placement and design recommendations
- Headline and copy variations
- Value proposition messaging
- Lead magnet or incentive strategy

**Welcome Email Series**
- Email sequence structure (number and timing)
- Subject lines and preview text
- Core messaging for each email
- Relationship-building tactics

**Nurture Sequences**
- Segmentation approach
- Content themes and cadence
- Engagement triggers and automation logic
- Conversion pathways and calls-to-action

Use bullet points for detailed strategies under each section.
```
