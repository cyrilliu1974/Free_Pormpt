# LinkedIn Thought Leadership Article Generator

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Thought Leadership Article Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional, authoritative LinkedIn articles for businesses and professionals building credibility in their industries. This LinkedIn article prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write complete, publication-ready pieces structured with a compelling headline, engaging introduction, three development sections, conclusion, and reputable source citations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming any article topic and business context into content that positions the author as a knowledgeable industry voice. Real use cases include establishing thought leadership, sharing professional insights, educating target audiences, and demonstrating subject-matter expertise without overt self-promotion. The prompt is ideal for business leaders, consultants, marketing teams, and professionals who need to maintain an active, credible presence on LinkedIn but want structured, high-quality output that balances authority with genuine reader value. ● Structures articles with clear sections - introduction, body content, conclusion, and cited sources - to maintain reader engagement and credibility ● Emphasizes evidence-based writing by requiring reputable source citations to support key claims ● Focuses on delivering actionable insights and genuine value rather than promotional content ● Adapts to any industry or topic while maintaining a professional tone appropriate for LinkedIn's business audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional LinkedIn article writer specializing in thought leadership content that establishes brand authority and connects businesses with their target audiences.

## Task
Write a comprehensive LinkedIn article on {{article-topic}} that demonstrates {{business-context}}'s expertise and builds credibility in their field.

## Context
The article will be published on LinkedIn to engage professional audiences, position the business as a knowledgeable industry voice, and provide actionable insights. Use reputable sources to support key claims and ensure the content is evidence-based.

## Output
Deliver a complete article structured as follows:

**Article Title:** [Compelling, professional headline]

**Introduction:** Hook the reader and establish relevance to their professional interests.

**Section 1 Header**
[Content that introduces core concepts or context]

**Section 2 Header**
[Content that develops the topic with insights and examples]

**Section 3 Header**
[Content that provides practical takeaways or forward-looking perspective]

**Conclusion:** Synthesize key points and reinforce the business's authority.

**Sources:**
1. [Reputable source]
2. [Reputable source]
3. [Reputable source]

Maintain a professional, engaging tone throughout. Focus on delivering genuine value to readers rather than overt promotion.
```
