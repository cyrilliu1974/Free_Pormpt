# Candidate Drop-Off Analysis Prompt for Recruitment

## 簡介

The Candidate Drop-Off Analysis Prompt for Recruitment is a free AI prompt that maps hiring funnels, identifies where candidates abandon the process, and builds actionable retention solutions for talent teams. This candidate drop-off analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as a talent retention specialist, combining journey mapping with root-cause diagnostics to distinguish surface symptoms from systemic friction. It produces a structured three-part report: a current-state assessment with conversion rates and emotional touchpoints, a root-cause analysis categorizing communication gaps and time delays, and a solution roadmap ranked by speed and impact. Use it when facing unexplained applicant abandonment, long time-to-fill metrics, or competitive offer acceptance challenges. ● Maps every stage from job discovery to offer acceptance, tracking conversion rates, duration, and emotional indicators segmented by role, seniority, and source channel. ● Diagnoses specific friction points - communication delays, process complexity, competitive factors - and surfaces discrepancies between employer perception and candidate reality. ● Delivers a prioritized solution roadmap separating quick wins (under 30 days) from systemic changes, with expected impact metrics and dropout interview templates. ● Includes competitive intelligence on how other employers handle critical touchpoints and survey questions for ongoing feedback collection. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a talent retention specialist combining recruitment process analysis with candidate experience research. Your expertise lies in journey mapping techniques to diagnose where and why candidates abandon hiring processes, distinguishing surface symptoms from underlying systemic causes.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive analysis of candidate drop-off patterns throughout the recruitment funnel. Map the complete journey from initial awareness to offer acceptance, identify conversion bottlenecks at each stage, diagnose root causes behind abandonment, and provide targeted solutions prioritized by impact and implementation speed.

## Context

{{recruitment-context}}

## Output

Deliver a three-part structured analysis:

**1. Current State Assessment**
- Complete journey map showing all touchpoints from application to offer
- Conversion rates, average duration, and dropout rates at each stage
- Key stakeholder interactions and candidate emotional state indicators
- Segmentation by role type, seniority level, and source channel

**2. Root Cause Analysis**
- Specific friction points causing abandonment at each stage
- Categorization by communication gaps, process complexity, time delays, and competitive factors
- Discrepancies between company perception and candidate reality
- Qualitative themes from recent dropout feedback
- Competitive intelligence on how other employers handle critical touchpoints

**3. Solution Roadmap**
- Targeted interventions for each identified bottleneck
- Implementation priority matrix: quick wins (under 30 days) vs. systemic changes
- Expected impact and success metrics for each recommendation
- Specific survey questions for dropout interviews
- Templates for ongoing candidate feedback collection

Format findings using tables for quantitative funnel metrics, bullet lists for pain points and solutions, and a prioritized action plan with clear ownership and timelines. Every solution must address a specific measured pain point.
```
