# Side Letter Agreement Drafting Prompt

## 簡介

The Side Letter Agreement Drafting Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates surgical contract modifications for transactional attorneys and business professionals who need to change specific terms without reopening full negotiations. This side letter prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete, ready-to-execute agreement that references the main contract with precision, states exact modifications to specific provisions, explicitly preserves all other terms, and establishes clear conflict resolution hierarchy between documents. It delivers proper legal formatting including headers, recitals, operative provisions, preservation clauses, integration language, and signature blocks that mirror the main agreement's defined terms and formality level. Real use cases include modifying pricing schedules in commercial agreements, adjusting vesting terms in investment documents, amending employment conditions, and updating delivery timelines in real estate transactions without triggering full renegotiation cycles. Reach for this prompt when you need to modify an existing agreement precisely and efficiently while maintaining the integrity of the original deal structure and ensuring the modification survives due diligence reviews, regulatory audits, and potential disputes. ● Creates complete side letters with precise main agreement references (title, date, parties' legal names) and exact modification language for specific provisions or sections. ● Includes explicit preservation clauses that protect all unmodified terms and establish document hierarchy to resolve conflicts between the side letter and main agreement. ● Addresses effective date requirements (immediate, retroactive, or future), confidentiality provisions when needed, and proper integration language. ● Delivers implementation guidance covering execution steps, delivery procedures, recordkeeping practices, and notice requirements under the main agreement. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert transactional attorney specializing in side letter agreements that surgically modify existing contracts without requiring full amendments or renegotiation. Your side letters survive due diligence reviews, regulatory audits, and disputes by creating absolute clarity about what they modify and what they preserve.

## Task

Draft a complete, executable side letter agreement that:

- References the main agreement with precision (title, date, parties' legal names)
- States the exact modification to specific provisions or sections
- Explicitly preserves all other terms of the main agreement
- Establishes clear conflict resolution hierarchy between documents
- Includes proper legal formatting with header, recitals, operative provisions, preservation clause, integration language, and signature blocks
- Mirrors the main agreement's defined terms and formality level
- Addresses effective date requirements (immediate, retroactive, or future)
- Incorporates confidentiality provisions if required

## Context

{{agreement-details}}

Side letters are used when the main agreement represents months of negotiation and reopening it would be impractical, yet a precise modification is necessary. The document must maintain the integrity of the original deal structure while creating zero ambiguity about the change.

## Output

Provide your response in four clear sections:

### 1. Side Letter Overview
Brief summary of the modification strategy and document hierarchy.

### 2. Complete Side Letter Document
Full executable text with proper legal formatting, spacing, and structure ready for signature.

### 3. Key Provisions Explanation
Clear explanation of critical clauses: the modification language, preservation clause, conflict resolution statement, and integration provisions.

### 4. Implementation Steps
Practical guidance for execution, delivery, recordkeeping, and any notice requirements under the main agreement.
```
