# Hooked Model Implementation Plan for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Hooked Model Implementation Plan for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a custom habit-formation strategy based on Nir Eyal's proven framework for consumer psychology and engagement. This Hooked model prompt for ChatGPT walks you through each of the four phases - Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, and Investment - with clear definitions, actionable strategies tailored to your business, and explanations of the psychological principles that drive habit formation. Whether you are launching a newsletter, a coaching practice, a SaaS product, or a content platform, the prompt generates concrete tactics to attract users, simplify interaction, deliver compelling rewards, and encourage ongoing commitment. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing an organized, numbered plan with headings, bullet points, and real-world examples in a conversational tone. This prompt is designed for solopreneurs, founders, and product designers who want to integrate behavioral psychology into their offerings without wading through academic theory or hiring a consultant. ● Identifies triggers - external and internal cues - that bring users back to your product or content. ● Simplifies the action phase with friction-reducing strategies that make engagement easy and rewarding. ● Designs variable reward systems that tap into curiosity, social validation, and the hunt for novelty. ● Builds investment mechanisms that increase switching costs and deepen user commitment over time. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in consumer psychology and habit formation, specializing in Nir Eyal's "Hooked" model.

## Task
Create a step-by-step plan to implement the "Hooked" model (Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, Investment) for a solopreneur's business. For each of the four phases, provide:

1. A clear definition of the phase and its importance in the cycle
2. 2-3 specific, actionable strategies tailored to the business context below
3. An explanation of the psychological principles at play and why this phase drives habit formation

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your plan in a numbered list format with clear headings for each phase (Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, Investment). Use bullet points and subheadings to organize the content.

Write in an engaging, conversational tone that speaks directly to the solopreneur. Use tangible, real-world examples to illustrate concepts. Avoid overly technical jargon or complex explanations—keep strategies clear and immediately actionable.
```
