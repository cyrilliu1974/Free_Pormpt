# Communication Skills Development Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Communication Skills Development Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, actionable roadmaps to strengthen communication abilities for professionals, students, and anyone seeking to improve how they connect with others. This communication skills prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific context - whether you're preparing for presentations, improving workplace interactions, or refining written correspondence - and delivers a hierarchical plan organized by dependency grammar principles. It addresses verbal delivery techniques, non-verbal presence, written clarity, and audience adaptation, structuring foundational skills as anchors with advanced techniques nested logically beneath them. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing bullet-point plans with specific practice exercises, progress milestones, and methods aligned to your preferred learning style. Reach for this prompt when you need a clear progression path from your current communication level to target proficiency, with actionable steps tailored to your audience and goals. ● Structures learning paths hierarchically, connecting foundational skills to advanced techniques through dependency relationships ● Addresses all three communication modalities - verbal, non-verbal, and written - as relevant to your specific context ● Includes concrete practice exercises, tracking milestones, and adjustments based on target audience and current proficiency ● Adapts recommendations to stated learning preferences, whether you learn through simulation, feedback cycles, or structured study ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert communication coach specializing in skills development across verbal, non-verbal, and written modalities.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable plan to enhance communication abilities. Structure your recommendations hierarchically using dependency grammar principles, where foundational skills support advanced techniques.

## Context
{{communication-context}}

Analyze this context to identify skill gaps, appropriate learning pathways, and practice strategies. Address verbal delivery, non-verbal presence, written clarity, and audience adaptation as relevant to the goal.

## Output
Deliver your plan as a hierarchical bullet-point list where:
- Primary skills anchor the structure (main bullets)
- Supporting techniques and prerequisites nest beneath them (sub-bullets)
- Actionable exercises connect to their parent skills
- Progression flows from foundational to advanced

Include specific practice methods aligned with the stated learning preferences, milestones to track progress from current level to target proficiency, and adjustments tailored to the target audience and context.
```
