# Luxury Product Showcase Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Product Showcase Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates split-screen product photography with studio-quality lighting, macro detail shots, and interactive material configurator interfaces for brands and designers. This luxury product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a three-panel layout: a centered overhead shot of your product on textured surfaces with ambient lighting, two cinematic macro close-ups highlighting material details and metallic accents, and a frosted-glass UI panel displaying four material pairing options. The output mimics high-end editorial catalog photography with photoreal textures, controlled shadows, and 8K detail - perfect for e-commerce hero images, brand lookbooks, pitch decks, and social media campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need aspirational product visuals without a photography studio or when exploring multiple material finishes for client presentations. ● Full overhead product shot with textured wood or stone backgrounds, soft vignette, and warm ambient reflections ● Two macro close-up panels with shallow depth of field, rim lighting halos, and jewel-tone gradient backgrounds ● Material configurator UI rendered as a floating frosted-glass panel with four pairing options and metallic edge lighting ● 8K photoreal textures showing brushed metal grain, polished reflections, and beveled edge details ## Prompt

```
Split-screen luxury product showcase in hyper-realistic 8K photography style.

**Left Panel:** Full {{product}} centered in top-down overhead view with elegant breathing space. Product features dark primary material body with light material accents and metallic-trimmed beveled edges. Background: textured surface (wood grain or stone) with soft out-of-focus luxury textile drapery, warm ambient glow reflections, and subtle floor texture at edges. Soft directional spotlight from above with gentle vignette. Mood: aspirational interior setting—sophisticated lounge, executive office, or boutique display.

**Right Panel — Upper Section:** Two macro cinematic close-ups. Detail A: light material body with metallic detailing on soft gradient background with warm rim lighting halo. Detail B: side-profile macro of dark material with secondary accent metal surface treatment, dark jewel-tone-to-black luxury gradient background.

**Right Panel — Lower Section:** Premium material configurator UI as floating frosted glass panel with soft metallic edge lighting. Display four material pairing options: Option 1 (dark + light contrast), Option 2 (industrial metals), Option 3 (warm natural tones), Option 4 (contemporary minimalist).

**Visual treatment:** Soft cinematic studio lighting with metallic reflections and controlled shadows for depth. Photoreal textures showing material grain, veins, brushed metal detail, and polished reflections. Deep focus on full product, shallow depth of field for close-ups. Color palette: neutrals with one warm metallic accent and one jewel-tone for depth. Ultra-luxury editorial catalog aesthetic—exclusive, refined, aspirational.
```
