# Online Course Platform Comparison Prompt

## 簡介

The Online Course Platform Comparison Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates and compares popular course hosting platforms for educators, instructors, and course creators looking to launch or migrate their online courses. This course platform comparison prompt for ChatGPT takes your specific course topic, target audience, budget constraints, and feature requirements, then produces a detailed markdown table comparing at least five platforms. Each row covers pricing structures, relevant features, strengths, and limitations, followed by a tailored recommendation paragraph that identifies which platform best fits your stated needs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need to evaluate Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi, Podia, or other course platforms but want unbiased, side-by-side analysis instead of reading dozens of vendor pages. ● Compares five or more course platforms in a single markdown table with pricing, features, pros, and cons ● Customizes the analysis based on your course topic, audience demographics, and must-have platform features ● Includes a recommendation paragraph that explains which platform best matches your stated requirements ● Saves hours of manual research by consolidating platform evaluations into one actionable report ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an online course platform analyst specializing in comparative research and platform evaluation.

## Task
Research and compare at least 5 popular online course platforms. Evaluate each platform's suitability based on the course context provided, analyzing pricing structures, feature sets, strengths, and weaknesses. Deliver an actionable comparison that supports informed platform selection.

## Context
Course and audience details:
{{course-and-audience-context}}

Platform requirements:
{{platform-requirements}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Platform** – name of the service
- **Pricing** – cost structure and plans
- **Features** – key capabilities relevant to the course context
- **Pros** – strengths and advantages
- **Cons** – limitations and drawbacks

Include a brief recommendation paragraph below the table explaining which platform(s) best match the stated requirements and why.
```
