# Affiliate Program Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Affiliate Program Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds structured affiliate marketing programs for businesses launching or optimizing partner incentive strategies. This affiliate program prompt for ChatGPT creates a five-part plan covering commission structures, promotional materials, tracking systems, compliance guidelines, and ongoing affiliate support. It designs tiered commission frameworks that reward sales volume and customer retention, specifies brand-consistent marketing assets like banners and email templates, and outlines real-time tracking systems for conversions and attribution. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing organized bullet-point plans tailored to your product, target audience, and ideal affiliate profile. Use it when you need to document a repeatable system for recruiting, equipping, and incentivizing affiliate partners who align with your brand values. ● Tiered commission structures that reward high performers based on sales, retention, and promotional effort ● Ready-to-deploy promotional material lists including banners, videos, email templates, and social content aligned with your brand ● Real-time tracking and reporting specifications for click-through rates, conversions, and revenue per affiliate ● Clear policy frameworks covering promotional methods, content restrictions, payment terms, and compliance requirements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert affiliate marketing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive affiliate program plan that incentivizes partners and drives promotion. Structure the plan with clear headings covering:

**Commission Structure**
Design a tiered commission system that rewards top performers and encourages long-term partnerships. Factor in sale volume, customer retention, and promotional effort when setting rates.

**Promotional Materials**
Specify engaging, brand-consistent materials for affiliates: banners, product images, videos, email templates, and social media content. Ensure materials communicate the value proposition and unique selling points effectively.

**Tracking System**
Outline a reliable tracking implementation that monitors affiliate performance, conversions, and attribution. Include real-time reporting on click-through rates, conversion rates, and revenue per affiliate.

**Guidelines and Policies**
Define clear rules covering permitted promotional methods, content restrictions, payment terms, and legal/ethical compliance requirements.

**Support and Training**
Describe ongoing support mechanisms: webinars, Q&A sessions, newsletters, and dedicated affiliate manager access to help affiliates optimize their efforts.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Brand Guidelines:** {{brand-guidelines}}

**Ideal Affiliate Profile:** {{affiliate-profile}}

## Output
Deliver the affiliate program plan as a bullet-point list with clear headings for each main section.
```
