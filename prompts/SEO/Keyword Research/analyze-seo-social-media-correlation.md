# SEO and Social Media Correlation Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The SEO and Social Media Correlation Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces statistical correlation reports between social signals and organic search performance for websites and digital properties. This SEO and social media correlation prompt for ChatGPT walks you through collecting engagement metrics across major platforms (shares, likes, comments, mentions, reach), tracking niche-relevant keywords, and calculating correlation coefficients that reveal which social activities correspond to ranking improvements. The prompt outputs a structured report including a correlation matrix, influential social keywords, and data-backed recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible whether you're analyzing a B2B SaaS site, e-commerce store, or content publication. Reach for this prompt when you need to justify social media investment with SEO data, identify which platforms drive the strongest ranking signals for your niche, or build an integrated content strategy that bridges social engagement and organic visibility. ● Examines five major social platforms and five engagement metrics tailored to the website's niche ● Calculates correlation coefficients between social signals and keyword rankings in a structured matrix format ● Identifies influential social keywords where engagement shows the strongest connection to search performance ● Delivers actionable recommendations grounded in the specific data patterns uncovered, not generic best practices ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO data analyst specializing in the correlation between social signals and search engine rankings.

## Task
Analyze the relationship between social media engagement and SEO performance for {{website-url}}. Deliver a comprehensive, data-driven report with statistically sound correlation analysis and actionable recommendations.

## Analysis Requirements
- Cover major social platforms relevant to the website's niche
- Collect comprehensive social engagement metrics (shares, likes, comments, mentions, reach)
- Track keywords relevant to the website's niche with sufficient search volume
- Calculate correlation coefficients between social metrics and keyword rankings
- Derive specific, actionable insights tied directly to the data

Avoid generic statements. Support all findings with data and concrete examples.
```
