# Hooked Framework Audience Retention Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Hooked Framework Audience Retention Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a customized engagement plan for solopreneurs using Nir Eyal's Hooked cycle to increase audience retention and authority. This audience retention prompt for ChatGPT walks through the four behavioral psychology phases - Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, and Investment - and delivers 2-3 concrete strategies per phase tailored to your business model, target audience, and value proposition. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a structured guide with psychological explanations, real-world examples, and immediately actionable steps. Solopreneurs use it to transform one-time visitors into repeat customers by designing habit-forming engagement loops grounded in proven behavioral science. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand why audiences disengage and how to systematically build retention into your content, product, or service touchpoints. ● Explains each Hooked cycle phase - Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, Investment - with psychological principles in plain language ● Generates 2-3 context-specific strategies per phase based on your business model and audience ● Includes tangible examples so you can see what each tactic looks like in practice ● Structures output as a complete guide with introduction, four detailed phase sections, and a conclusion summarizing compound engagement effects ## Prompt
