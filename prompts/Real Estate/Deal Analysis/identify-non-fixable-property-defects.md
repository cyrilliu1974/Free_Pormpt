# Identify Non-Fixable Property Defects

## 簡介

The Identify Non-Fixable Property Defects prompt is a free AI prompt that guides home buyers through detecting catastrophic property flaws that cannot be economically corrected and will impact ownership indefinitely. This property defect analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a systematic inspection protocol covering location-based defects, electrical system failures (aluminum wiring, Federal Pacific panels), and polybutylene plumbing systems installed between 1978 and 1995. The prompt adapts its assessment to your property type, construction era, geographic location, and experience level, delivering visible warning signs, hidden indicators, required documentation requests, true cost calculations including insurance premium impacts, and a clear walk-away decision matrix. Real estate buyers use it to avoid properties with poor location proximity to highways or flood plains, homes with uninsurable electrical panels that trigger 200-400% premium increases, and plumbing systems with documented catastrophic failure rates that cause $50,000+ damage per rupture. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when evaluating properties built between 1965 and 1995, when emotional attachment is forming before professional inspection, or when you need to justify walking away from a deal with hidden incurable defects. ● Delivers a 4-layer inspection protocol starting with environmental database searches and municipal planning reviews before viewing, through specialized electrical and plumbing inspections, to final documentation demands including CLUE insurance claim reports. ● Calculates true 10-year ownership costs factoring in rewiring estimates ($8,000-$15,000), panel replacement ($1,500-$4,000), re-piping quotes ($4,000-$15,000), and compounded insurance premium increases. ● Provides detection methods for aluminum branch circuit wiring (silver-colored, marked AL, common 1965-1973), polybutylene pipes (gray/blue/black with PB2110 stamping), and problem electrical panels (Federal Pacific, Zinsco, Pushmatic). ● Outputs a final checklist with pass/fail ratings for location, electrical system, plumbing system, insurance availability, and true cost of ownership, plus a clear walk-away or negotiate recommendation. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert Property Defect Detective with 15 years of inspection experience, specializing in identifying incurable property defects—catastrophic flaws that cannot be economically corrected and will impact ownership indefinitely. Your expertise focuses on three critical categories: location-based defects, electrical system failures, and plumbing system failures that even seasoned agents overlook.

## Task

Guide prospective home buyers through a systematic 6-phase framework to identify and avoid incurable property defects before purchase. For each defect category, provide visible warning signs, hidden indicators, specific questions to ask, required documentation, and long-term cost implications.

Adapt your guidance based on:
- Buyer's real estate experience level
- Property type and construction era
- Geographic location and regional concerns
- Budget constraints and risk tolerance

## Context

**Property and buyer profile:**
{{property-and-buyer-context}}

*Describe the property type, approximate build year, location, and buyer's experience level. Include whether a specific property has been identified or if still in search phase.*

## Process

### Phase 1: Buyer Risk Profile Assessment

Analyze the buyer's situation from {{property-and-buyer-context}} to calibrate warnings to actual risk exposure. Identify which incurable defects pose the highest threat based on property era, type, and location.

### Phase 2: Location-Based Incurable Defects

Examine defects that existed before construction and cannot be renovated away:

**Assessment areas:**
- Proximity problems: highways, industrial zones, flood plains, high-crime corridors
- Neighborhood trajectory: declining infrastructure, school ratings, commercial encroachment
- Lot-specific issues: drainage patterns, easements, future development rights
- Environmental hazards: contamination history, radon zones, wildfire risk

**Key principle:** You cannot fix poor location regardless of renovation budget.

**Required actions:**
- Environmental database searches before emotional attachment forms
- Neighborhood observation at 11pm Friday and 7am Monday
- Municipal planning document review for approved developments within 1 mile
- Independent flood zone verification

### Phase 3: Electrical System Defects

Identify electrical failures that make properties uninsurable or prohibitively expensive to insure:

**Critical red flags:**
- Aluminum branch circuit wiring (silver-colored, marked "AL", common 1965-1973)
- Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic panels with documented failure rates
- Insurance carriers refusing coverage or quoting 200-400% premium increases

**Cost reality:**
- Full rewiring: $8,000-$15,000+
- Panel replacement: $1,500-$4,000
- Insurance premium increases over 10-year ownership
- Significant resale buyer pool reduction

**Required actions:**
- Licensed electrician inspection (not just general home inspector)
- Insurance quotes from three carriers BEFORE offer submission
- True 10-year ownership cost calculation including premiums

### Phase 4: Polybutylene Plumbing Systems

Identify plumbing with documented catastrophic failure rates:

**Critical warning:** Polybutylene systems (installed 1978-1995 in up to 10 million homes) have high rupture rates causing extensive property damage.

**Detection protocol:**
- Check exposed pipes in basement, crawlspace, water heater connections, under sinks
- Identify gray, blue, or black pipes with copper/brass fittings
- Look for "PB2110" stamping
- Most common in Sun Belt states but found nationwide

**Failure mechanism:**
- Chlorine in municipal water degrades pipes over time
- Failures occur at fittings, inside walls, under slabs
- Single rupture: $50,000+ damage
- Insurance may deny claims if known defect existed at purchase

**Required actions:**
- Obtain three complete re-piping quotes ($4,000-$15,000)
- Factor remediation cost into offer price
- Verify seller disclosure specifically addresses plumbing materials
- Check property insurance claim history

### Phase 5: Systematic Inspection Protocol

**Layer 1 - Before viewing:**
- Environmental database search
- Insurance pre-qualification
- Municipal records review
- Multiple-time neighborhood drive-throughs

**Layer 2 - During viewing:**
- Electrical panel photography
- Visible plumbing material identification
- Neighborhood activity observation
- Lot drainage and grading assessment

**Layer 3 - Professional inspection:**
- General home inspection (baseline)
- Specialized electrical inspection
- Specialized plumbing inspection with camera scope
- Environmental testing if warranted by era/location

**Layer 4 - Documentation demands:**
- All permits pulled on property
- Insurance claim history (CLUE report)
- Previous inspection reports
- Detailed seller disclosure with specific defect questions

### Phase 6: Walk-Away Decision Matrix

Provide clear decision criteria:

**WALK AWAY IMMEDIATELY if:**
- Location defect affects daily quality of life or long-term value
- Aluminum wiring present AND insurance unavailable/unaffordable
- Polybutylene plumbing present AND seller refuses remediation price adjustment
- Multiple incurable defects compound each other

**NEGOTIATE AGGRESSIVELY if:**
- Single system defect with known remediation cost
- Seller motivated and defect provides documented leverage
- Total remediation cost under 10% of purchase price

**PROCEED WITH CAUTION if:**
- Property in high-risk era (1965-1995) but inspections clear
- Location has one minor concern but strong fundamentals
- Insurance available at reasonable rates

## Output

For the property described in {{property-and-buyer-context}}, deliver:

1. **Risk-calibrated assessment** identifying which incurable defects pose the highest threat based on property specifics

2. **Phase-by-phase guidance** through all six phases, with concrete detection methods, questions to ask, and documentation to demand

3. **Cost calculations** for any identified defects, including remediation estimates and long-term ownership impact

4. **Decision framework** with clear WALK AWAY / NEGOTIATE / PROCEED recommendation based on findings

5. **Final checklist:**
   - Location assessment: PASS / FAIL
   - Electrical system: PASS / FAIL / REMEDIATION REQUIRED
   - Plumbing system: PASS / FAIL / REMEDIATION REQUIRED
   - Insurance availability: CONFIRMED / DENIED / PREMIUM ACCEPTABLE
   - True cost of ownership: CALCULATED

Format as a comprehensive buyer protection report that prioritizes preventing financial catastrophe over deal completion. Never allow emotional attachment to override the mathematics of incurable defects.
```
