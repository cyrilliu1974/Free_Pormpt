# Headline Generator for SEO and Engagement

## 簡介

The Headline Generator for SEO and Engagement is a free AI prompt that creates multiple headline options tailored to your content type, target audience, and desired tone. This headline prompt for ChatGPT produces 5–8 variations with built-in dependency grammar analysis that explains how each word anchors and modifies the core message. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, letting you specify content type (article, email, ad, video), topic, target audience, tone, and character limits. Real use cases include blog post titles, email subject lines, social media hooks, landing page headers, and ad copy that needs to balance SEO keywords with emotional impact. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple tested options quickly, want grammar-backed reasoning for why a headline works, or are optimizing for both search visibility and click-through rates. ● Creates 5–8 headline variations per request, each with dependency grammar breakdown ● Incorporates power words, emotional triggers, and SEO-optimized keywords automatically ● Adapts to specific content types, audiences, tones, and character constraints ● Explains which word anchors each headline and how modifiers build clarity and impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in headline creation.

## Task
Craft compelling headlines that captivate readers and drive engagement. Use dependency grammar principles to structure headlines with maximum clarity and impact—organize words so that each element's relationship to the core message is immediately clear.

## Context
**Content type:** {{content-type}}
**Topic:** {{topic}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Desired tone:** {{tone}}
**Character limit (if any):** {{character-limit}}

## Guidelines
- Identify key elements that will resonate with the target audience
- Use power words and emotional triggers to enhance impact
- Craft multiple headline variations to provide options
- Keep each headline concise, clear, and attention-grabbing
- Incorporate relevant keywords for SEO optimization when applicable
- Ensure correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 5–8 headline options. For each headline, include a brief explanation of the dependency grammar structure used (which word anchors the headline, what modifies what, and how the relationships create clarity and impact).
```
