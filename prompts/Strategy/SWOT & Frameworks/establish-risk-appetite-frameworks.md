# Risk Appetite Framework Builder for Organizations

## 簡介

The Risk Appetite Framework Builder for Organizations is a free AI prompt that creates structured risk management frameworks defining how much risk an organization is willing to accept across different categories. This risk appetite prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a five-part framework including risk categories with appetite levels (averse to hungry), quantitative tolerance thresholds, measurable key risk indicators with trigger points, a visual tolerance matrix mapping functions to risk types, and implementation guidance for governance and stakeholder communication. Organizations use it to align risk strategy with business objectives, establish monitoring cadences, and create clear escalation protocols when risk thresholds are breached. Reach for this prompt when building or refreshing enterprise risk management programs, preparing for board-level risk discussions, or standardizing how different departments understand and report on risk exposure. ● Produces risk appetite statements for each category that connect risk tolerance to strategic objectives in plain language ● Defines 3-5 key risk indicators per category with target ranges, trigger points, and recommended monitoring frequency ● Generates a color-coded tolerance matrix mapping business functions to risk categories with escalation thresholds ● Includes governance guidance on review cadence, adaptation protocols, and communication strategies for different stakeholder groups ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a risk management expert specializing in developing risk appetite and tolerance frameworks for organizations.

## Task
Create a comprehensive risk appetite and tolerance framework that enables the organization to effectively manage and communicate its risk strategy.

## Context
Organization profile and risk landscape:
{{organization-and-risk-profile}}

(Include: organization size, industry, primary risk concerns, strategic objectives, regulatory environment, and any specific risk management priorities.)

## Output
Deliver a complete framework with:

### 1. Risk Categories Overview
Structured table listing:
- Major risk categories relevant to the organization
- Appetite level for each category (e.g., averse, minimal, cautious, open, hungry)
- Tolerance thresholds (quantitative ranges where applicable)

### 2. Risk Appetite Statements
Clear, concise statement for each major risk category that:
- Aligns with strategic objectives
- Defines the amount and type of risk the organization is willing to accept
- Uses accessible language for all stakeholders

### 3. Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
For each risk category, identify:
- 3-5 measurable, relevant KRIs
- Target ranges and trigger points
- Monitoring frequency

### 4. Risk Tolerance Matrix
Visual matrix showing:
- Business activities or functions (rows)
- Risk categories (columns)
- Acceptable risk ranges color-coded or clearly marked
- Escalation thresholds

### 5. Implementation Guidance
Brief notes on:
- How to adapt the framework to organizational changes
- Governance and review cadence
- Communication approach for different stakeholder groups

Format the entire framework using markdown with tables, bullet points, and clear headings. Ensure it is visually scannable, avoids technical jargon, and remains flexible for customization across different organizational contexts.
```
