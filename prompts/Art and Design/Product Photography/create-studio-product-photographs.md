# Studio Product Photography Prompt for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Studio Product Photography Prompt for AI Image Models is a free AI prompt that generates commercial-grade product photographs with center composition, professional lighting, and hyper-detailed rendering for brands and marketers. This studio product photography prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models produces polished visuals of boxed product kits - open packaging revealing gradient ampoules, precision droppers, and metallic caps - against clean white backgrounds with subtle geometric accents and floating benefit icons. The prompt specifies elevated camera angles, soft professional lighting with gentle shadows, clear branding visibility, and 8K resolution output. Use it to create scroll-stopping e-commerce hero images, social media assets, or pitchdecks that showcase physical products with the polish of a commercial photoshoot. Reach for this prompt when you need product photography that looks professionally shot in a studio but can be generated and iterated in minutes instead of days. ● Center-composed, slightly elevated views that mirror traditional studio photography conventions ● Open-box staging with visible product details, branding, and packaging elements in sharp focus ● Floating icon overlays (droplet, leaf, sparkle) to visually communicate product benefits ● Soft professional lighting with 8K resolution and hyper-realistic material rendering ## Prompt
