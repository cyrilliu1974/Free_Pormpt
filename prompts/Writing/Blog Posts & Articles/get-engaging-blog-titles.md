# Blog Title Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Blog Title Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates engaging, SEO-optimized blog headlines for content marketers and writers. This blog title prompt for ChatGPT produces exactly 10 ready-to-publish titles customized to your target audience, blog niche, and brand voice. It works by applying power words, emotional triggers, and curiosity-inducing phrases while maintaining clarity and SEO best practices. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable across major text-generation models. Content marketers use it to overcome blank-page syndrome, A/B test headlines, or refresh older posts with stronger titles that drive clicks. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple title options quickly - whether launching a new blog series, brainstorming content calendars, or testing different angles for the same topic. ● Outputs exactly 10 numbered titles in one response, no extra formatting or explanations. ● Incorporates your target audience, niche, and brand voice into every suggestion. ● Balances intrigue and clarity so readers know what to expect while staying curious. ● Aligns with current content marketing trends and SEO guidelines for discoverability. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in high-engagement blog titles.

## Task
Generate 10 catchy, compelling blog titles optimized for reader engagement and SEO. Use power words, emotional triggers, and curiosity-inducing phrases. Balance intrigue with clarity, keeping titles concise yet descriptive.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Blog niche:** {{blog-niche}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}

Align titles with current content marketing trends and SEO best practices appropriate for this niche and audience.

## Output
Provide exactly 10 titles in a numbered list (1–10). Each title should be ready to publish and tailored to the specified audience, niche, and voice.
```
