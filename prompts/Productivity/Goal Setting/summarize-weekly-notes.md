# Weekly Summary Generator for Business Owners

## 簡介

The Weekly Summary Generator for Business Owners is a free AI prompt that transforms a week of daily notes into an executive summary for entrepreneurs and business leaders. This weekly summary prompt for ChatGPT reads your daily business logs - activities, decisions, wins, friction points - and extracts the most significant patterns into a five-section report: week overview, key accomplishments, challenges faced, insights gained, and areas for improvement with concrete action steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and requires only a single input: your daily notes. Business owners use it every Friday to consolidate what happened, spot recurring bottlenecks, and plan the next week without re-reading pages of raw notes. It replaces manual journaling review with structured synthesis. Reach for this prompt when you keep daily logs but struggle to step back and see the bigger picture - or when you need a repeatable end-of-week ritual that turns reflection into action. ● Extracts accomplishments, challenges, and insights directly from your notes without padding or vague generalities. ● Delivers a five-part structure that business owners can archive, share with advisors, or feed into quarterly reviews. ● Generates concrete action steps for each improvement area, not aspirational intentions. ● Includes a motivational quote tailored to the week's theme to close the summary on a reflective note. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a performance analyst synthesizing weekly business activity into an executive summary.

## Context

The user is a business owner reviewing their week through daily notes that capture activities, decisions, wins, and friction points.

## Task

Review the daily notes below. Extract the most significant accomplishments, challenges, and insights. Synthesize them into the structured summary format defined in Output. Keep each point specific and grounded in the notes—do not pad or generalize. For Areas for Improvement, write concrete action steps, not vague intentions.

## Input

**Daily notes for the week:**  
{{weekly-daily-notes}}

## Output

Produce the following sections:

**Week Overview**  
Brief recap of the week's overall trajectory and theme.

**Key Accomplishments**  
- Accomplishment 1  
- Accomplishment 2  
- Accomplishment 3

**Challenges Faced**  
- Challenge 1  
- Challenge 2  
- Challenge 3

**Insights Gained**  
- Insight 1  
- Insight 2  
- Insight 3

**Areas for Improvement**  
1. Improvement Area 1  
   Specific action steps  
2. Improvement Area 2  
   Specific action steps  
3. Improvement Area 3  
   Specific action steps

**Motivational Quote**  
"Relevant motivational quote" — Author
```
