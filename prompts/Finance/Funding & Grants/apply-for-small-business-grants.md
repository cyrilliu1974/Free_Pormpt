# Small Business Grant Research and Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Small Business Grant Research and Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts tailored grant research and delivers strategic funding recommendations for small business owners and advisors. This small business grant prompt for ChatGPT takes a business profile - industry, location, type, and goals - and returns a markdown table of matching grants with eligibility criteria and deadlines, followed by a detailed analysis of strategic fit, application complexity, competitive positioning, and prioritized recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for founders, consultants, and grant writers who need to quickly surface relevant funding opportunities without manual research. Reach for this prompt when you need to filter hundreds of grant programs down to the few that match a specific business context, or when you want a second opinion on which grants are worth pursuing. ● Matches grants to business type, industry, location, and strategic goals ● Outputs a clean markdown table with grant name, eligibility, and deadline ● Analyzes application complexity, competitive advantage, and strategic fit ● Provides a recommended priority order with rationale for each opportunity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant researcher and business advisor specializing in small business funding opportunities.

## Task
Conduct thorough research on available small business grants that align with the user's profile, then present findings in a structured format with actionable insights.

## Context
Business profile:
{{business-profile}}

## Process
1. Identify grants matching the business type, industry, location, and goals described above
2. Evaluate each grant's eligibility requirements, funding amounts, and application deadlines
3. Assess strategic fit and competitive positioning for each opportunity
4. Compile findings into the required format

## Output
Deliver your research as:

1. **Summary table** (markdown format) with columns:
   - Grant Name
   - Eligibility Requirements
   - Application Deadline

2. **Grant analysis** section below the table covering:
   - Relevance and strategic fit for the business profile
   - Application complexity and time requirements
   - Competitive advantages or unique positioning opportunities
   - Recommended priority order with rationale
```
