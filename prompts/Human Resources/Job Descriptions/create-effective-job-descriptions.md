# Job Description Writer for Recruitment Teams

## 簡介

The Job Description Writer for Recruitment Teams is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, inclusive job postings optimized for search platforms and designed to attract qualified candidates. This job description prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a seven-section job posting that covers company overview, role summary, key responsibilities, qualifications, benefits, growth opportunities, and application instructions. You input company context, requirements and qualifications, benefits and growth details, and 5-8 SEO keywords; the prompt structures these elements into a professional, search-optimized job listing that reflects your culture and communicates career potential. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing ready-to-post descriptions formatted with clear headings and bullet points. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize your recruitment content, ensure inclusive language across all postings, or improve the search visibility of your job listings on platforms like LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor. ● Structures postings into seven standard sections covering company overview, responsibilities, qualifications, benefits, growth pathways, and application process ● Emphasizes inclusive, unbiased language to support diverse candidate pools and modern HR best practices ● Integrates SEO keywords naturally into titles and body copy to increase visibility on job boards and search engines ● Formats output with action verbs, clear outcomes, and scannable bullet points for candidate readability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a recruitment specialist drafting compelling, inclusive job descriptions that attract qualified candidates and rank well on job platforms.

## Task
Create a comprehensive job description for the specified role that clearly communicates responsibilities, qualifications, and growth potential while reflecting company culture and optimizing for search visibility.

## Context
{{company-and-role-context}}
*Include: company name, mission, values, culture highlights, any awards/recognition, and the job title with its strategic importance to company goals.*

{{requirements-and-qualifications}}
*Include: primary responsibilities (use action verbs and expected outcomes), required education/certifications/experience, essential hard skills and soft skills.*

{{benefits-and-growth}}
*Include: compensation/benefits/perks offered, work arrangements, professional development support, and career progression pathways.*

{{seo-keywords}}
*List 5-8 keywords related to the role for search optimization.*

## Output
Structure the job description with these sections:

1. **Company Overview** – Mission, values, culture, and credibility markers (2-3 sentences)
2. **Role Summary** – Job title, purpose, and contribution to company objectives (2-3 sentences)
3. **Key Responsibilities** – Bullet list with action verbs and clear outcomes
4. **Qualifications & Skills** – Education, certifications, experience, and both technical and interpersonal skills required
5. **Benefits & Perks** – Compensation, health/retirement plans, work-life balance initiatives, unique offerings
6. **Growth Opportunities** – Career development and advancement pathways
7. **How to Apply** – Application instructions and equal opportunity employer statement

Use inclusive, unbiased language throughout. Naturally integrate SEO keywords in the title and body. Format with clear headings and bullet points for readability.
```
