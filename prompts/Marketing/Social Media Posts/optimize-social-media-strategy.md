# Social Media Sharing Widget Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Sharing Widget Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes any website and produces a detailed plan for integrating social sharing widgets to drive referral traffic and boost user engagement. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT accepts a brief description of your website and its goals, then delivers a structured implementation plan covering widget placement, design specifications, platform selection, brand customization, analytics tracking, and A/B testing recommendations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, identifying your three most shareable content types and tailoring widget strategies to your audience's behavior. Marketing teams, web developers, and social media managers use it to make data-driven decisions about where to place sharing buttons, which platforms to include, and how to measure success. ● Identifies the 3 most shareable content types on your site and matches widget placement to audience behavior ● Specifies exact design requirements including mobile responsiveness, call-to-action wording, and brand alignment ● Recommends 3-5 social platforms with inclusion rationale and explains which to exclude based on your audience ● Delivers 3 trackable metrics with implementation methods and 3 A/B tests with clear hypotheses and success criteria ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist specializing in website-integrated sharing strategies that drive referral traffic and engagement.

## Task
Analyze the provided website and create a comprehensive social sharing widget implementation plan. Determine optimal placement, design, and functionality to maximize content visibility, social media referrals, and user engagement.

## Context
Website & Goals:
{{website-context}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy organized into these sections:

**Website Overview**
- URL and core purpose
- Target audience profile
- Primary business goals

**Key Content Types**
Identify the 3 most shareable content types on the site (e.g., blog posts, product pages, case studies)

**Recommended Sharing Widgets**
For each of 3 widget types, specify:
- Exact placement location and reasoning
- Design specifications (size, style, mobile behavior)
- Functionality features (one-click vs. customizable sharing, copy link, etc.)

**Social Media Platform Selection**
✅ Included platforms: List 3-5 with specific reasons tied to audience behavior
❌ Excluded platforms: Note 1-2 and why they don't fit

**Widget Customization**
- Brand alignment (colors, fonts, tone)
- Call-to-action wording that drives clicks
- Visual elements (icons, counters, hover effects)

**Tracking and Analytics**
Recommend 3 metrics with implementation details:
1. Metric name, tracking method, and success benchmark
2. Metric name, tracking method, and success benchmark
3. Metric name, tracking method, and success benchmark

**Testing and Optimization**
Propose 3 A/B tests:
1. What to test, hypothesis, and success criteria
2. What to test, hypothesis, and success criteria
3. What to test, hypothesis, and success criteria

**Expected Results**
Project 3 measurable outcomes with timeframes and explanations of how the strategy achieves them.

Provide specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the website's unique context. Avoid generic advice.
```
