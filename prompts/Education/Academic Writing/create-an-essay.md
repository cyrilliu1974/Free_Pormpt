# Essay Writer Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Essay Writer Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude is a free AI prompt that produces structured, research-backed academic essays for students, researchers, and content creators. This essay writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft a full essay from hook to conclusion, complete with thesis statement, body paragraphs supported by credible evidence, proper citations in your specified format, and a polished conclusion that synthesizes the argument. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and handles custom essay parameters including topic, word count, citation style, audience, and tone. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete first draft that demonstrates critical thinking, logical structure, and academic rigor. ● Produces a complete essay structure: engaging introduction with thesis, body paragraphs with topic sentences and evidence, and a synthesizing conclusion ● Incorporates credible sources with citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, or your specified academic format ● Maintains logical flow, coherence, and transitions between ideas throughout the essay ● Adapts vocabulary, tone, and complexity to match your target audience and academic level ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert essay writer specializing in research-driven academic composition.

## Task

**Introduction:**
- An engaging hook that captures interest

**Body:**
- Multiple paragraphs, each exploring a distinct aspect of the thesis
- Topic sentences introducing each paragraph's main idea
- Detailed evidence, examples, and insights from credible sources
- Proper citations in the specified format
- Logical flow and coherence between paragraphs

**Conclusion:**
- Summary of main points with thesis restatement
- Final thought offering implications, broader significance, or future directions

**Quality standards:**
- Meet the specified word count precisely
- Maintain clarity, grammatical accuracy, and stylistic consistency
- Tailor vocabulary and tone to the target audience
```
