# Cease and Desist Letter Generator for IP Infringement

## 簡介

The Cease and Desist Letter Generator for IP Infringement is a free AI prompt that drafts professional legal letters to address intellectual property violations and protect client rights. This cease and desist prompt for ChatGPT structures a complete formal letter that includes infringement description, legal basis citing relevant statutes and precedents, specific resolution demands with deadlines, and warnings of potential legal action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing attorney-ready correspondence that establishes legal grounds, communicates the seriousness of the violation, and demands corrective action within a specified timeframe (typically 10-14 business days). Legal consultants and intellectual property attorneys use it to accelerate the drafting process when clients face trademark infringement, copyright violations, or other IP disputes. The prompt is designed for legal professionals and in-house counsel who need to produce formal cease and desist correspondence quickly while maintaining appropriate legal language and structure. ● Structures letters with law firm letterhead, detailed infringement description, legal basis with statute citations, resolution demands, and consequences ● Maintains formal, authoritative tone that asserts legal rights without being hostile or ambiguous ● Incorporates three customizable variables: infringement details, recipient information, and law firm details ● Produces complete letters ready for attorney review and transmission to infringing parties ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal consultant specializing in intellectual property law, drafting a formal cease and desist letter to address infringement and protect the client's legal rights.

## Task
Draft a persuasive, professional cease and desist letter that clearly communicates the infringement, establishes legal grounds, demands resolution, and warns of consequences if the recipient fails to comply.

## Context
{{infringement-details}} should specify: the infringing activity, how it violates the client's rights, the impact on the client, and any relevant dates or evidence.

{{recipient-info}} should include: recipient name, company name, and address.

{{law-firm-details}} should provide: law firm name, attorney name, and any letterhead information.

## Output
Structure the letter with these components:

**[Law Firm Letterhead]**

[Date]

{{recipient-info}}

Re: Cease and Desist - Intellectual Property Infringement

Dear [Recipient Name],

**Introduction**
State the purpose of this letter and identify yourself as counsel representing the aggrieved party.

**Infringement Description**
Provide a detailed account of the infringing activity based on {{infringement-details}}, establishing the nature and extent of the violation.

**Legal Basis**
Cite relevant intellectual property laws, statutes, and legal precedents that support the claim and demonstrate the validity of the client's position.

**Resolution Demands**
Specify the actions the recipient must take to remedy the infringement, including a reasonable deadline (typically 10-14 business days from receipt).

**Consequences**
Outline potential legal action if demands are not met, including injunctive relief, monetary damages, attorney's fees, and court costs.

**Closing**
Conclude by reiterating the expectation of prompt compliance and the seriousness of the matter.

Sincerely,
{{law-firm-details}}

---

**Tone and Style:**
- Formal and professional throughout
- Firm and authoritative without being hostile
- Clear, specific language avoiding ambiguity
- Confident assertion of legal rights
```
