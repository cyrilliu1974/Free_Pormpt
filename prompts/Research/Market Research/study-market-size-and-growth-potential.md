# Market Size and Growth Potential Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Size and Growth Potential Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers comprehensive market research and growth forecasts for strategic decision-making. Built for business strategists, product managers, and analysts, this market research prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models to evaluate addressable market size, competitive dynamics, demographic trends, and economic indicators within your specified industry and geography. The prompt synthesizes both quantitative metrics - market penetration rates, revenue projections, and TAM/SAM estimates - and qualitative factors such as regulatory environment, buying behavior, and emerging technology shifts. You provide five variables: the product or service being analyzed, target market scope, industry vertical, geographic focus, and analysis time frame. The AI returns a markdown table with three columns (Market Size, Growth Rate, Key Insights) followed by a 150–250 word summary that highlights strategic implications, recommended focus areas, and any risks or data limitations. This structure makes findings immediately actionable for pitch decks, investment memos, and go-to-market planning. ● Evaluates addressable market size, penetration potential, and revenue forecasts across custom geographies and time horizons ● Analyzes competitive landscape, demographic shifts, and economic indicators that drive or constrain growth ● Outputs findings in a three-column table plus executive summary, ready for stakeholder presentations ● Balances quantitative projections with qualitative context on regulation, buyer behavior, and emerging risks ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert market analyst specializing in sizing markets, forecasting growth, and identifying sales opportunities.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive market analysis for {{product-or-service}} to inform strategic decision-making. Analyze industry trends, evaluate competitive dynamics, and synthesize data on target demographics, economic indicators, and technological shifts. Deliver actionable insights on market opportunity and growth potential.

## Context

**Market scope:** {{target-market}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Geographic focus:** {{geographic-focus}}
**Analysis time frame:** {{time-frame}}

Consider both quantitative metrics (addressable market, penetration rates, revenue projections) and qualitative factors (regulatory environment, buying behavior, emerging threats and opportunities).

## Output

Present your analysis in a markdown table with three columns:

| Market Size | Growth Rate | Key Insights |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Below the table, provide a concise summary (150–250 words) highlighting:

- The most significant findings
- Strategic implications and recommended focus areas
- Risks or limitations to the analysis
```
