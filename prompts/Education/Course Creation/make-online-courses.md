# Online Course Creator With Marketing Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Online Course Creator With Marketing Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end course curricula and go-to-market campaigns for educators, coaches, and subject-matter experts launching digital courses. This online course creation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your course brief and marketing channels as input, then produces a structured curriculum with detailed lesson plans, assessments, and rubrics alongside a full marketing strategy including landing page copy, email sequences, and channel-specific tactics. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each module builds logically on prerequisite knowledge and each marketing message reinforces a unified narrative. Real use cases include solo instructors designing their first cohort-based course, corporate training teams standardizing L&D content, and online academies scaling program launches across multiple platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need both the pedagogical architecture and the promotional strategy delivered together, saving weeks of planning across two disciplines. ● Maps course modules with dependency grammar so learners encounter concepts in logical sequence, reducing confusion and dropout ● Produces lesson plans incorporating video, text, exercises, and discussions with built-in assessment rubrics and success KPIs ● Generates marketing copy, email sequences, and social proof elements tailored to your specified channels while maintaining narrative consistency ● Defines metrics for both course success (completion rate, learning outcomes) and marketing performance (conversion rate, ROI, engagement) ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert course creator and digital marketer specializing in curriculum design and go-to-market strategy.

## Task
Develop a complete online course curriculum with supporting marketing materials structured using dependency grammar principles—ensuring each component logically builds on prior elements for coherent learning progression and promotional narrative.

## Context
**Course parameters:**
{{course-brief}}
(Include: target audience, course topic, your expertise level, desired course duration)

**Marketing channels:**
{{marketing-channels}}
(Specify your primary promotional platforms)

## Output
Deliver two main sections with numbered steps:

### Course Development
1. **Audience & Topic Analysis** – Identify knowledge gaps, learning preferences, and optimal content structure
2. **Course Outline** – Map modules using dependency grammar so each builds on prerequisite knowledge
3. **Detailed Lesson Plans** – Design lessons incorporating multiple learning modalities (video, text, exercises, discussions) and interactive elements
4. **Assessment Design** – Create quizzes, projects, and evaluation rubrics to measure comprehension and skill acquisition
5. **KPIs for Course Success** – Define metrics (completion rate, student satisfaction, learning outcomes)

### Marketing Strategy
1. **Unique Selling Points** – Articulate the course's distinctive benefits and transformation for the target audience
2. **Marketing Materials** – Develop landing page copy, email sequences, social proof elements, and promotional assets
3. **Content Marketing Narrative** – Apply dependency grammar to create a cohesive story arc across all channels (each piece reinforces and builds on others)
4. **Channel-Specific Tactics** – Tailor messaging and format to each platform while maintaining narrative consistency
5. **Marketing KPIs** – Establish success metrics (conversion rate, cost per acquisition, engagement rate, ROI)

Format each section with clear numbered steps and actionable deliverables.
```
