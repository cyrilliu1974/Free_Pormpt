# Economic Trends Analysis Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Economic Trends Analysis Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed sector analysis reports for financial advisors, analysts, and business strategists. This economic trends analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through building a structured 10-section report that examines current market conditions, historical performance, regulatory changes, technological advancements, consumer behavior shifts, competitive dynamics, SWOT analysis, future projections, and actionable recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting two variables: the sector and scope you want to analyze, plus the data sources available to you. The output is a stakeholder-ready document that translates complex economic data into clear investment and business strategy guidance. Reach for this prompt when you need to deliver a thorough sector analysis that balances quantitative data with qualitative insights, or when clients require evidence-based recommendations grounded in multi-factor trend evaluation. ● Structures reports into 10 dedicated sections from market overview through actionable recommendations ● Incorporates SWOT framework to surface strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats systematically ● Guides analysis of regulatory environment, technological developments, and consumer behavior as distinct trend drivers ● Delivers future projections and risk mitigation strategies tailored to the specified sector and data sources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced financial advisor conducting a thorough economic trend analysis.

## Task
Deliver a comprehensive, data-backed report analyzing economic trends within the specified sector. Examine current market conditions, historical performance, and future projections to identify key influencing factors including regulatory changes, technological advancements, and consumer behavior shifts. Provide stakeholders with detailed insights and actionable recommendations to inform investment and business strategies.

## Context
Sector and scope:
{{sector-and-scope}}

Available data sources:
{{data-sources}}

## Output
Structure your report with these sections:

**1. Market Overview**
Provide current market conditions: market size, growth rate, and key players. Support with recent data.

**2. Historical Analysis**
Examine sector performance over the specified historical period. Highlight significant trends, events, market shifts, regulatory changes, or economic impacts.

**3. Regulatory Environment**
Analyze current and upcoming legislation affecting the sector and its potential impact on businesses and investors.

**4. Technological Advancements**
Explore relevant technological developments driving innovation, efficiency improvements, or new market opportunities.

**5. Consumer Behavior**
Study shifts in consumer preferences, demographic trends, and spending habits influencing the sector.

**6. Competitive Landscape**
Analyze key competitors, market share distribution, barriers to entry, and potential disruptors.

**7. SWOT Analysis**
Identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats facing the sector. Derive actionable insights from this framework.

**8. Future Projections**
Offer projections for growth areas, emerging trends, and challenges that could impact sector performance.

**9. Actionable Recommendations**
Provide specific advice on investment opportunities, risk mitigation strategies, and areas for innovation.

**10. Conclusion**
Summarize key findings and emphasize the value of informed decision-making based on comprehensive analysis.

Present all findings clearly and concisely, backed by data and structured for stakeholder decision-making.
```
