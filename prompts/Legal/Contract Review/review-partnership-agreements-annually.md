# Annual Partnership Agreement Review Prompt

## 簡介

The Annual Partnership Agreement Review Prompt is a free AI prompt that systematically extracts and organizes key terms from multiple partnership agreements into a comparative markdown table for contract review specialists. This partnership agreement review prompt for ChatGPT guides text models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) to analyze contracts by extracting specified details - such as renewal dates, payment terms, obligations, and termination clauses - and displaying them in rows and columns for side-by-side comparison. Companies conducting annual contract audits use it to standardize how they document and compare partner agreements, ensuring no critical clause is overlooked. The table format makes it simple to spot inconsistencies, flag upcoming renewals, and prepare renewal negotiations. This prompt is for legal teams, contract managers, and business operations professionals who need to review multiple partnership agreements on a recurring schedule and want a consistent, auditable format. ● Extracts user-defined agreement details (dates, obligations, financials, liabilities) into labeled columns ● Outputs a markdown table with one row per agreement for fast visual comparison ● Standardizes contract review workflows across teams and reduces manual copy-paste errors ● Adapts to any partnership type by letting you specify which clauses or data points to track ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a contract review specialist conducting annual reviews of partnership agreements.

## Task
Systematically analyze and summarize key aspects of partnership agreements in a structured table format. Extract relevant information from each agreement and organize it into a comprehensive review.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}

Focus your analysis on: {{key-agreement-details}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table where:
- Each row represents one partnership agreement
- Columns display the information categories specified in the key agreement details above
- All data is clearly organized and easy to compare across agreements
```
