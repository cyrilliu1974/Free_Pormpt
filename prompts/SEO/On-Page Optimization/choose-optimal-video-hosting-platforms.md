# Video Hosting Platform Comparison and Selection

## 簡介

The Video Hosting Platform Comparison and Selection is a free AI prompt that analyzes and recommends the optimal video hosting platform for your specific business needs and audience. This video hosting platform prompt for ChatGPT evaluates 5-7 major platforms including YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, Vidyard, and Brightcove across critical dimensions: SEO capabilities, audience reach, analytics, branding options, marketing tool integrations, pricing, and technical requirements. The prompt produces a structured markdown comparison table with pros and cons for each platform, followed by a detailed recommendation paragraph that explains which platform best fits your business type, target audience, budget, and content strategy. Digital marketing strategists, content managers, and business owners use it to make data-informed decisions about video content distribution without hours of manual research. Reach for this prompt when evaluating video hosting solutions, planning a video content strategy, or migrating from one platform to another. ● Compares platforms across SEO discoverability, audience reach, analytics depth, and branding flexibility ● Evaluates integration capabilities with CRM systems and marketing automation tools ● Weighs pricing models against budget constraints and technical implementation complexity ● Delivers a clear recommendation with implementation considerations tailored to your business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in video content distribution and platform selection.

## Task
Analyze and recommend the optimal video hosting platform for the specified business. Conduct a thorough comparison of top video hosting platforms (YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, Vidyard, Brightcove, and others relevant to the business context), evaluating:

- SEO capabilities and discoverability
- Audience reach and distribution potential
- Analytics and performance tracking
- Customization and branding options
- Integration with marketing tools and CRM systems
- Pricing relative to budget
- Technical requirements and ease of use

Evaluate each platform's strengths and weaknesses in relation to the specific business needs, target audience, and content strategy.

## Context
{{business-and-audience-context}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: **Platform**, **Pros**, and **Cons**. Include 5-7 platforms in the comparison.

After the table, provide a **Recommendation** section (2-3 paragraphs) that:
- Identifies the best platform for this specific business
- Explains why it's the optimal choice given the business context
- Notes any important implementation considerations
```
