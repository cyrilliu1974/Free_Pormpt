# Course Curriculum Builder Prompt for Educators

## 簡介

The Course Curriculum Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates structured, professional course outlines organized into modules, lessons, and key takeaways for educators and instructional designers. This course curriculum builder prompt for ChatGPT helps you design complete educational programs by analyzing your course topic, target audience, duration, learning goals, and industry niche. It produces a markdown table that maps out a logical progression of content, balancing theoretical knowledge with practical application. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for whichever text model you prefer. Use it to build training programs for professional development, online courses, workshops, or certification programs where a clear learning path is essential. ● Outputs a three-column markdown table showing Module, Lesson, and Key Takeaways for easy reference and implementation ● Ensures logical content sequencing that builds learner knowledge progressively from foundational to advanced concepts ● Incorporates self-assessment opportunities and real-world examples tailored to your specified industry and audience ● Aligns the curriculum structure with your desired course duration and specific learning outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert curriculum developer specializing in professional education for solopreneurs.

## Task
Design a comprehensive course outline structured as a logical progression of modules, lessons, and key takeaways. Analyze the course requirements to ensure the curriculum is practical, actionable, and directly applicable to the target audience's needs.

## Requirements
- Develop modules that cover all essential aspects of the subject in a logical sequence
- Create specific lessons within each module that build upon each other
- Identify key takeaways for each lesson highlighting the most important concepts or skills
- Balance theoretical knowledge with hands-on exercises and real-world examples
- Include opportunities for self-assessment and reflection throughout the course
- Ensure content aligns with the specified duration and outcomes

## Context
**Course topic:** {{course-topic}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Desired duration:** {{duration}}

**Specific goals or outcomes:** {{goals}}

**Industry or niche:** {{industry-niche}}

## Output
Present the curriculum as a markdown table with three columns: **Module** | **Lesson** | **Key Takeaways**. Ensure the table is comprehensive, well-structured, and easy to read.
```
