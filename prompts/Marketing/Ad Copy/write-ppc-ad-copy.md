# PPC Ad Copy Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The PPC Ad Copy Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused ad variations tailored to Google Ads, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok character limits. This PPC ad copy prompt for ChatGPT delivers 5-10 distinct variations, each testing a specific psychological trigger - pain points, curiosity, social proof, urgency, or contrarian angles. You provide a campaign brief, and the prompt outputs complete ad sets with headlines, descriptions, the trigger being tested, a conversion rationale, and a prioritized testing roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying direct-response principles like leading with immediate benefits, avoiding spam triggers, and matching ad copy to landing pages for better Quality Scores. Use it when you need fast, testable ad copy for paid campaigns without sacrificing platform compliance or conversion psychology. ● Outputs 5-10 ad variations, each testing a distinct psychological angle with structured headlines and descriptions. ● Respects Google Ads, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok character limits automatically. ● Includes a testing priority guide showing which variations to run first and why. ● Applies direct-response principles: specific numbers, tangible benefits, no spam triggers, and 2-second attention optimization. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused PPC copywriter with extensive direct-response and affiliate marketing experience. Your specialty is writing short-form ad copy that stops the scroll and drives immediate clicks within tight character limits and strict platform compliance rules.

## Task
Generate 5-10 variations of PPC ad copy optimized for the specified platform. Each variation should test a different psychological angle (pain/desire, curiosity, social proof, urgency, contrarian) and match the audience's awareness stage.

## Context
{{campaign-brief}}

Platform character limits:
- Google Ads: 30/30/90 (headline/headline/description)
- Facebook/Instagram: 40 primary text / 125 description
- TikTok: 100 characters

## Output
For each variation, provide:

**Ad Variation #[X]**  
Headline 1: [Text]  
Headline 2: [Text]  
Description: [Text]  
*Angle:* [Psychological trigger used]  
*Why it works:* [1-2 sentence rationale]

---

[Repeat for 5-10 variations]

**Testing Priority:**  
1. [Top 2-3 variations to test first and why]  
2. [Secondary tests based on initial data]  
3. [Advanced optimization opportunities]

**Copy Principles Applied:**
- Lead with immediate pain point or desire
- Use specific numbers and tangible benefits over vague claims
- Avoid spam triggers (all caps, excessive punctuation, unsubstantiated superlatives)
- Match ad messaging to landing page for Quality Score
- Stay compliant with platform advertising policies
- Optimize for 2-second attention threshold
```
