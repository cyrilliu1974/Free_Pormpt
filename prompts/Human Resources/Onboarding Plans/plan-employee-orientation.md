# Employee First-Day Orientation Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee First-Day Orientation Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive first-day schedules for HR professionals, hiring managers, and onboarding teams. This employee orientation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a detailed hour-by-hour schedule organized around three research-backed pillars: Purpose (connecting the role to company mission), Clarity (defining expectations and success measures), and Belonging (building relationships and cultural fit). You provide company context, values, team structure, and role details; the prompt returns a markdown table with timing, activities, responsible people, and measurable outcomes. Real use cases include scaling onboarding for fast-growing startups, standardizing orientation experiences across departments, and helping remote teams create structured virtual first days. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform ad-hoc first days into repeatable, high-impact experiences that reduce new-hire anxiety and accelerate productivity. ● Produces hour-by-hour schedules with strategic introduction sequences that prioritize relationship-building over information dumps. ● Includes manager conversation scripts, check-in points, and preparation checklists to ensure every stakeholder knows their role. ● Maps each activity to Purpose, Clarity, or Belonging so you understand the psychological foundation behind every scheduled block. ● Provides success metrics for each session, turning orientation from a compliance checklist into a measurable employee experience. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an Employee Experience Designer and organizational psychologist specializing in first-day orientation programs grounded in employee experience design principles.

## Task
Design a comprehensive day-one orientation schedule that balances emotional engagement with practical onboarding. Structure the day around three pillars:

- **Purpose**: Connect the new hire's role to the company mission
- **Clarity**: Set clear expectations, processes, and success measures
- **Belonging**: Foster meaningful relationships and cultural integration

The schedule should gradually build confidence and connection, moving from welcome activities through essential knowledge transfer to forward-looking goal setting.

## Context
{{company-and-role-context}}

Include in your context: company culture and core values, team structure and key personnel, meeting norms and communication style, the new hire's specific role and department, and how this role contributes to the company mission.

## Output
Deliver an hour-by-hour schedule in markdown table format with these columns:

| Time | Activity | Purpose | Key People Involved | Success Metrics |

Within the schedule, include:
- Strategic introduction sequences that prioritize relationship-building over information overload
- Workspace setup guidance and practical first tasks
- Regular check-in points throughout the day
- Specific talking points and conversation scripts for managers at critical moments
- Actionable preparation checklists for managers and team members
- Clear connections between each activity and the three core pillars

Ensure the new hire finishes day one understanding their role's impact, feeling supported and valued, and equipped with the practical knowledge needed to begin contributing.
```
