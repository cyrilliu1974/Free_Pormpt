# Macro Miniature Advertising Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Macro Miniature Advertising Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic product photography featuring miniature workers around a beverage in a glass. The prompt produces studio-quality images where 8-12 tiny figures in high-visibility orange and yellow uniforms climb ladders, operate cranes, spray hoses, and scale ropes attached to a drink-filled cup set on a wooden table. This miniature advertising scene prompt for Midjourney works on Flux, Nano Banana, and similar AI image models, rendering sharp focus on the glass and workers with soft background blur at a 45-degree camera angle. Designers, marketers, and creative directors use it to produce scroll-stopping product visuals that combine whimsy with hyperrealism. ● Specifies 8-12 tiny workers performing realistic actions - climbing, pouring, spraying, adjusting foam - around a beverage glass for dynamic storytelling. ● Defines exact lighting (soft diffused overhead), camera angle (elevated 45 degrees), and depth of field (sharp foreground, soft background blur) for professional product photography style. ● Accepts custom beverage details and aspect ratio variables, adapting to beer foam, coffee, cocktails, or any drink-based campaign. ● Produces clean studio compositions with wooden table surface and neutral background, ready for commercial use in ads, social media, and packaging mockups. ## Prompt

