# Pregnancy Meal Planning Prompt for Nutritionists

## 簡介

The Pregnancy Meal Planning Prompt for Nutritionists is a free AI prompt that creates detailed weekly meal guides addressing the critical nutritional demands of pregnancy for maternal nutrition professionals and dietitians. This pregnancy meal planning prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured markdown table covering every meal of the week with portion sizes, key nutrients, vegetarian alternatives, and leftover integration strategies. It accounts for trimester-specific iron, folate, calcium, and omega-3 requirements while managing common pregnancy symptoms like nausea, food aversions, and heartburn. Nutritionists use it to build practical meal rotation systems that maximize nutrient density through iron-rich combinations paired with vitamin C enhancers, provide protein-complete vegetarian options, and adapt to fluctuating energy levels and time constraints. The output includes weekly iron intake targets, meal prep efficiency tips, and symptom management strategies tailored to individual pregnancy profiles. This prompt is ideal for registered dietitians, maternal nutritionists, prenatal health coaches, and healthcare providers who need to deliver evidence-based, personalized meal plans that support optimal fetal development. ● Develops meal plans emphasizing iron-rich food combinations with vitamin C enhancers for optimal absorption during pregnancy. ● Includes protein-complete vegetarian alternatives that match or exceed the nutritional profile of omnivorous options. ● Creates leftover integration strategies transforming previous meals into new dishes while maintaining nutritional integrity. ● Accounts for fluctuating energy levels, changing food preferences, and common pregnancy symptoms throughout all trimesters. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert maternal nutritionist and registered dietitian specializing in pregnancy nutrition across diverse dietary preferences and cultural backgrounds.

## Task
Create a comprehensive weekly meal planning guide tailored to the unique nutritional demands of pregnancy. Address critical nutrient requirements—iron, folate, calcium, omega-3 fatty acids—while managing common pregnancy symptoms and accommodating practical meal preparation constraints.

## Context
Pregnancy nutrition strategically supports fetal development beyond increased caloric needs. Design a practical meal rotation system that:
- Maximizes nutrient density through iron-rich combinations with vitamin C enhancers for optimal absorption
- Provides protein-complete vegetarian alternatives matching or exceeding the nutritional profile of omnivorous options
- Incorporates leftover integration strategies that transform previous meals into new dishes while maintaining nutritional integrity
- Accounts for fluctuating energy levels, changing food preferences, and common symptoms throughout pregnancy
- Scales portion guidance appropriately with maternal weight and trimester-specific needs

{{pregnancy-profile}}: current trimester, pre-pregnancy weight, dietary preferences or restrictions, pregnancy symptoms affecting eating (nausea, food aversions, heartburn, etc.), and meal preparation time availability.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive markdown table with these columns: **Day | Meal Type | Food Item | Portion Size | Key Nutrients | Vegetarian Alternative | Leftover Integration Strategy**.

Include a summary section with:
- Weekly iron intake targets based on trimester
- Meal prep efficiency tips suited to the stated time availability
- Symptom management strategies relevant to the reported symptoms
```
