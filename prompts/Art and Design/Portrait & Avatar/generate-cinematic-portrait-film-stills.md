# Cinematic Portrait Film Still Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait Film Still Generator is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric portrait photography with vintage film aesthetics and professional arthouse composition for content creators, brands, and visual artists. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images in a 3:4 aspect ratio with subjects positioned in the lower third, generous negative space, and rule-of-thirds framing reminiscent of Wong Kar-wai films. It specifies detailed subject styling including natural makeup, wind-tousled hair, relaxed posture, and vintage props, combined with outdoor settings featuring wild grasses, morning mist, and atmospheric haze. The output features soft golden backlighting, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, and a lo-fi analog color grade with lifted blacks, desaturated tones, warm amber-sepia cast, and visible grain that evokes sun-bleached 1970s cinema. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial portraits, lifestyle marketing visuals, or gallery-quality images that convey quiet nostalgia and poetic mood without expensive film photography equipment or location shoots. ● Produces long-shot portraits with off-center composition, negative space, and cinematic framing inspired by arthouse cinema. ● Specifies vintage 50mm anamorphic lens characteristics including soft flare, shallow depth of field, and foreground bokeh. ● Delivers authentic analog film color grading with lifted blacks, flat contrast, warm sepia tones, hazy softness, and visible grain. ● Customizable subject descriptions and natural settings while maintaining consistent atmospheric mood and 1970s film aesthetic. ## Prompt

```
Cinematic arthouse film still, 3:4 aspect ratio. Long shot, subject occupying lower third with generous negative space above, composed off-center left per rule of thirds.

**Subject:** {{subject-description}}. Relaxed stance, one hand holding a vintage prop, the other resting on garment. Graceful posture, soft gaze toward camera. Serene expression, warm eyes, gentle closed-lip smile. Warm brown hair, long and straight, wind-tousled. Natural makeup, dewy skin, glossy nude lips.

**Accessories:** Gold kanzashi hairpin with floral ornament, simple pearl drop earrings.

**Setting:** {{setting}}. Tall wild grasses, scattered wildflowers, morning mist on slopes, atmospheric haze creating depth layers. Natural landmark anchoring the right side of frame.

**Lighting & Photography:** Soft golden backlight, gentle rim light on hair, blooming highlights, no harsh shadows. Shallow depth of field, foreground grass blurred with creamy bokeh. Vintage 50mm anamorphic lens with subtle flare and soft vignette.

**Color Grade:** Lo-fi analog film aesthetic — lifted blacks, desaturated muted tones, warm amber-sepia cast, flat contrast, hazy softness, visible grain, sun-bleached 1970s warmth.

**Mood:** Quiet nostalgia, contemplative, poetic, Wong Kar-wai stillness.
```
