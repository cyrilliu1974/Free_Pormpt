# Writing Foreshadowing Revision Prompt

## 簡介

The Writing Foreshadowing Revision Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes prose to surface foreshadowing opportunities and revises text while preserving tone and style for fiction writers and creative authors. This foreshadowing prompt for ChatGPT accepts any writing sample and returns three identified opportunities for subtle anticipation, a full revised version that weaves them in naturally, and a dependency grammar analysis explaining how sentence structure supports narrative flow. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it practical for novelists revising chapters, short-story writers tightening plot hints, or creative writing students learning to layer meaning without telegraphing twists. The prompt maintains the original voice while deepening thematic resonance through grammatical precision. Reach for this prompt when you want to add narrative depth without rewriting from scratch or when beta readers say your twists feel sudden rather than earned. ● Surfaces three specific foreshadowing opportunities with clear rationale tied to your actual text ● Revises your sample to incorporate hints naturally, keeping your original tone intact ● Provides dependency grammar analysis showing how clause structure and modifiers support narrative coherence ● Teaches literary technique by example, so every revision becomes a lesson in craft ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert writing coach specializing in literary techniques and dependency grammar frameworks.

## Task
Analyze the provided writing sample, identify three subtle foreshadowing opportunities, revise the text to incorporate them seamlessly, and provide a dependency grammar analysis of the revision.

## Context
Foreshadowing should:
- Create anticipation without being overt
- Hint at future events or plot twists naturally
- Preserve the original tone and style

The dependency grammar analysis demonstrates how sentence structure supports the narrative flow.

## Input
{{writing-sample}}

## Output
**Original Text:**
[Present the provided sample]

**Foreshadowing Opportunities:**
1. [First opportunity with brief rationale]
2. [Second opportunity with brief rationale]
3. [Third opportunity with brief rationale]

**Revised Text with Foreshadowing:**
[Full revision incorporating all three elements]

**Dependency Grammar Analysis:**
[Breakdown showing how dependent clauses, modifiers, and sentence structures support the foreshadowing and overall narrative coherence]
```
