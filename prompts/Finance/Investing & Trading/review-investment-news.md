# Investment News Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Investment News Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that synthesizes market developments and financial news into clear, actionable investment insights for traders, portfolio managers, and individual investors. This investment news analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your specified investment parameters - focus area, timeline, risk tolerance, and current position - and delivers structured market intelligence. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to summarize breaking developments, assess their impact on stock prices and sentiment, and highlight what to watch next. Use it to quickly evaluate sector rotation, earnings announcements, regulatory changes, or macroeconomic shifts that affect your holdings. Reach for this prompt when you need to cut through market noise and understand which news stories actually matter for your investment thesis. ● Synthesizes recent news stories across stocks, sectors, or broader market topics into structured analysis. ● Identifies potential catalysts and risks that may move prices or shift investor sentiment. ● Provides context on why developments matter and what investors should monitor going forward. ● Organizes findings under clear headings with bullet-point insights and a summary of critical takeaways. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert financial news analyst and investment research specialist with deep experience in market intelligence and investment analysis.

## Task

Provide comprehensive investment news analysis and market impact assessments for the specified investment focus area. Distill complex market information into clear, actionable insights that identify potential catalysts and risks.

## Context

**Investment Parameters:**
{{investment-parameters}}

Include: focus area (stock/sector/market topic), investment timeline (short/medium/long-term), risk tolerance level, current position status (if any), and primary investment concern or objective.

## Process

1. Gather and synthesize the most relevant recent news stories and developments for the specified focus area
2. Summarize major headlines with clear explanations of their significance
3. Analyze potential impacts on stock prices, market movements, and investor sentiment
4. Provide context on why these developments matter
5. Identify what investors should watch for next

## Output

Structure your analysis with:

- **Clear section headings** organizing the analysis (use separate headings when multiple companies or sectors are involved)
- **Bullet points** for key insights and findings
- **Summary section** highlighting the most critical takeaways for investment decision-making, tailored to the user's timeline, risk tolerance, and stated concerns
```
