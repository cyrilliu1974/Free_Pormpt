# Local SEO Content Calendar & Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Local SEO Content Calendar & Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven plans to update local landing pages and content around seasonal changes and community events for businesses targeting specific geographic markets. This local SEO strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a 12-month content calendar tied to local search trends, complete with event-specific keywords, content formats, publish timing, on-page optimization checklists, and off-page tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. You provide your target cities, website URL, and business type, and the prompt returns a structured plan that maps seasonal peaks and local events to content opportunities, helping businesses stay visible during high-intent search windows in their service areas. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond static local pages and create a recurring content rhythm that captures seasonal demand, community happenings, and location-specific search behavior. ● Produces a 12-month content calendar mapping local events and seasons to target keywords and content types ● Delivers location-specific on-page optimization checklists and off-page link-building tactics ● Identifies high-impact seasonal keywords using local search trend logic ● Includes measurable KPIs and tracking methods to monitor local ranking improvements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in local SEO and content creation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan to consistently update local content and landing pages based on seasonal changes and local events. Leverage local search trend data to identify relevant keywords and topics that will improve local search rankings and visibility.

## Context
- Target locations: {{target-locations}}
- Website: {{website-url}}
- Business type: {{business-type}}

## Output
Provide a structured local SEO content plan in the following format:

**Objective:**
[State the main goal of this local content update plan]

**Target Locations:**
- [List each city and state]

**Local Events and Seasons to Target:**
For each seasonal period or major local event, include:
1. Event/Season name and timing (months)
   - Relevant local search keywords
   - Content ideas tailored to the location and business

**Content Calendar:**
| Month | Event/Season | Content Type | Target Keywords | Publish Date |
|-------|--------------|--------------|-----------------|-------------|
| [Populate 12-month calendar with specific entries] |

**On-Page Optimization Checklist:**
☑️ [List 5-7 specific on-page SEO tactics]

**Off-Page Optimization Tactics:**
- [List 4-6 actionable off-page strategies]

**Measuring Success:**
- [List 4-6 specific KPIs and tracking methods]

Focus on tactics with the highest impact on local search performance. Avoid generic recommendations—tailor all suggestions to the specific locations and business type provided.
```
