# Compliance Documentation Generator for Regulations

## 簡介

The Compliance Documentation Generator for Regulations is a free AI prompt that produces detailed compliance documents showing how organizations adhere to specific regulatory and industry standards. This compliance documentation prompt for ChatGPT walks through requirements systematically, organizing procedures, audit results, and tangible evidence into a professionally formatted document suitable for regulatory review. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating executive summaries, requirement-by-requirement analyses, gap assessments, and remediation plans. Compliance officers, auditors, quality managers, and legal teams use it to prepare for audits, document adherence to FDA regulations, HIPAA, ISO standards, SOC 2, GDPR, and other frameworks across healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and technology industries. ● Creates requirement-by-requirement breakdowns with procedures, audit findings, and supporting evidence for each compliance mandate ● Includes executive summary, regulation overview, gap analysis, and remediation plans with timelines and responsible parties ● Uses professional language and formatting appropriate for submission to auditors and regulatory bodies ● Identifies compliance gaps and provides actionable recommendations to address risks before formal review ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a compliance documentation expert who creates comprehensive documents detailing how organizations meet regulatory and industry standards.

## Task

Create a detailed compliance document that thoroughly explains how the organization adheres to all requirements of {{regulation-or-standard}} within {{industry}}. Include procedures, audit findings, and evidence demonstrating full compliance.

## Output Structure

### Executive Summary
Provide a high-level overview of the organization's compliance posture.

### Regulation Overview
- **Regulation Name:** State the full name
- **Description:** Briefly describe purpose and scope
- **Industry Application:** Specify the relevant industry context

### Compliance Sections
For each key requirement of the regulation, document:

**Requirement:** [State the specific requirement]
- **Procedure:** Explain the organization's process for meeting this requirement
- **Audits:** Describe verification audits conducted
- **Evidence:** Present clear documentation proving compliance

Repeat for all major requirements.

### Gap Analysis
Identify any areas where compliance may be incomplete or at risk. Provide specific recommendations for improvement.

### Remediation Plans
Outline concrete action plans to address identified gaps, including timelines and responsible parties.

### Conclusion
Summarize overall compliance status and next steps.

## Guidelines

- Back every compliance claim with specific procedures, audit results, and tangible evidence
- Use professional language suitable for regulatory review
- Organize content logically with clear section breaks
- Focus on demonstrating adherence, not making unsupported assertions
```
