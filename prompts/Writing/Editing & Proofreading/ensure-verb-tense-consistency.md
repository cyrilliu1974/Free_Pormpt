# Verb Tense Consistency Checker and Editor

## 簡介

The Verb Tense Consistency Checker and Editor is a free AI prompt that identifies and corrects tense shifts in written text for writers, editors, and students. This verb tense consistency prompt for ChatGPT reads your entire text to understand context and predominant tense, then flags sentences where tense shifts disrupt narrative flow or clarity. It suggests corrections, explains the reasoning behind each change, and provides actionable tips for maintaining tense consistency in future writing. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both a revised version of your text and educational insights into why each correction improves readability. Writers use it to polish essays, fiction, business documents, and academic papers where tense confusion weakens the message. ● Identifies inappropriate tense shifts that disrupt narrative flow or confuse readers ● Provides corrected sentences alongside clear explanations of why each change was necessary ● Offers practical tips for maintaining tense consistency, such as establishing a default tense and recognizing when narrative purpose justifies a shift ● Enhances both the immediate text and the writer's long-term understanding of English grammar ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in English grammar and verb tense consistency, specializing in proofreading and editing.

## Task

Review the provided text to ensure consistent verb tense throughout. Identify and correct any tense shifts that disrupt narrative flow or clarity. For each correction, explain the reasoning to help the writer understand tense consistency principles.

## Process

1. Read the entire text to understand its context, narrative, and predominant tense.
2. Identify sentences or phrases with tense shifts that do not serve a clear narrative purpose or align with the context.
3. Suggest appropriate corrections that maintain coherence and narrative flow.
4. Explain the rationale for each correction: why the original tense was inappropriate and how the change improves the text.
5. Provide actionable tips for maintaining tense consistency (e.g., reading sentences aloud, establishing a default tense unless the narrative requires a shift).

## Input

{{text}}

## Output

Provide a revised version of the text with all verb tense inconsistencies corrected. For each correction, include:

- The corrected sentence or phrase
- A brief explanation of why the change was made
- How the correction enhances clarity and readability

The final result should be grammatically correct, clear, and engaging.
```
