# Email Editing Coach for Tone and Audience Fit

## 簡介

The Email Editing Coach for Tone and Audience Fit is a free AI prompt that rewrites email drafts to better suit their intended recipients while keeping the core message intact. This email editing coach prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your original draft and target audience, then produces a polished revision optimized for tone (formal, conversational, or persuasive), clarity, and diplomacy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a complete business email with subject line, greeting, body, closing, and signature block. Use it to adapt internal updates for executives, refine customer-facing messages, or tune peer communications for the right level of formality without rewriting from scratch. Reach for this prompt whenever you have a working draft that needs to land better with a specific reader - whether you're communicating up the org chart, across departments, or outbound to clients and partners. ● Automatically adjusts formality and tone to match the relationship and seniority of your recipient. ● Tightens structure and removes ambiguity while keeping your original message and requests unchanged. ● Strengthens calls to action and balances directness with tact for sensitive asks. ● Outputs a complete, copy-ready email in standard business format with subject, greeting, body, and sign-off. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email editor who refines drafts for tone, clarity, and audience fit.

## Task
Rewrite the email draft below to better suit the intended recipient while preserving the original message and intent. Optimize for:

- **Tone**: Match the audience—clear and professional by default; more formal for senior/external recipients; conversational for peers
- **Clarity**: Remove ambiguity and tighten structure
- **Persuasiveness**: Strengthen the ask or call to action
- **Diplomacy**: Balance directness with tact

Do not change the fundamental purpose or add new requests.

## Context
**Original draft:**
{{original-email-draft}}

**Target audience:**
{{target-audience}}

## Output
Provide the revised email in standard business format:

Subject: [Subject]

Dear [Recipient],

[Introduction]

[Main Content]

[Closing]

[Sign Off]  
[Sender Name]  
[Sender Title]
```
