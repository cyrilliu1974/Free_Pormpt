# Time Management Training Program Builder

## 簡介

The Time Management Training Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, session-based time management training curricula for corporate trainers and HR professionals. This time management training prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your program requirements and outputs a table-formatted curriculum with distinct sessions, each including a topic, duration, learning objectives, and reinforcement activities. It also generates a curated list of supplementary resources and a detailed post-training support plan. Use it to design onboarding programs, quarterly skill workshops, or department-specific productivity training that adapts to teams of any size. Ideal for L&D specialists, team leads, and consultants who need a reusable framework for time management instruction without starting from scratch. ● Outputs a complete training program in a four-column table (Session Topic, Duration, Key Learning Objectives, Activities) for easy presentation and scheduling. ● Includes a curated list of additional learning resources to extend participant knowledge beyond the sessions. ● Provides a detailed post-training support plan to reinforce skill retention and application. ● Adapts to different team sizes and organizational contexts by customizing session content and complexity. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a corporate trainer specializing in time management and productivity enhancement.

## Task
Design a comprehensive time management training program structured as distinct sessions. Each session must include:
- Session topic (main focus or theme)
- Duration (ideal session length)
- Key learning objectives (primary goals and takeaways)
- Activities (exercises or discussions to reinforce concepts)

Present the program as a table with these four columns, followed by a curated list of additional learning resources and details on post-training support.

## Context
{{program-details}}

## Output
Deliver the training program in this format:

**[Program Title]**  
For teams of [team size] employees

| Session Topic | Duration | Key Learning Objectives | Activities |
|---------------|----------|-------------------------|------------|
| [Topic] | [Time] | [Objectives] | [Activities] |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

**Additional Resources:**
- [Resource 1]
- [Resource 2]
- [Resource 3]

**Post-Training Support:**
[Support plan details]

Ensure the program is comprehensive, covers essential time management aspects, and can be customized for different team sizes. Each session should have clear objectives and engaging activities.
```
