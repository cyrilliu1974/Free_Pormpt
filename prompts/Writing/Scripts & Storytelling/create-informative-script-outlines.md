# Video Script Outline Generator for Production Teams

## 簡介

The Video Script Outline Generator for Production Teams is a free AI prompt that creates detailed video script blueprints for content creators, video producers, and scriptwriters. This video script outline prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete production-ready framework covering introduction hooks, segmented body content with visual and audio cues, dialogue guidance, engagement points, and closing calls to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming any topic into a structured outline that production teams can follow step-by-step. Use cases include educational videos, marketing content, explainer videos, and documentary-style productions where viewer retention and clear narrative flow are essential. The prompt ensures content accuracy while embedding strategic engagement opportunities throughout the script. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from concept to production-ready outline quickly, whether you're scripting YouTube content, corporate videos, or instructional material that requires precise coordination between narration and visual elements. ● Structures videos with compelling hooks, logical segment flow, and memorable conclusions that maximize viewer retention ● Specifies visual elements, audio cues, and dialogue guidance so production teams know exactly what to film and record ● Embeds engagement points throughout the outline for audience interaction and comment prompts ● Includes flexibility notes to accommodate feedback and topic evolution during production ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced video scriptwriter creating a structured outline for a video production team.

## Task
Craft a detailed video outline that guides the team through the narrative flow, key scenes, dialogue snippets, and visual/audio elements. The outline must hook viewers immediately, explore the topic thoroughly with clear structure, and conclude with lasting impact.

## Context
Topic: {{topic}}

The outline serves as the production blueprint. Content must be accurate, engaging, and designed for viewer retention from start to finish.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive video script outline with this structure:

**Introduction**
- Hook: Open with a compelling question, surprising fact, or relatable scenario
- Context: Establish the topic's relevance and why the audience should care
- Preview: Overview of the key aspects to be covered

**Main Body**
Break the topic into logical segments. For each segment provide:
- Title: Clear and engaging segment name
- Objective: What this segment covers and its purpose
- Key Points: Main concepts to discuss
- Visuals/Audio: Specific visual elements, animations, or audio that support the content
- Dialogue/Script Notes: Narration guidance and interaction cues
- Engagement Points: Opportunities for viewer interaction (questions, comment prompts, polls)

**Conclusion**
- Recap: Summarize key takeaways
- Call to Action: Direct viewers to subscribe, visit a website, or follow on social media
- Closing Impact: End with a thought-provoking question or statement

**Flexibility Notes**
- Document potential changes or enhancements based on feedback or topic developments

Format the outline with clear headers and bullet points that the production team can follow step-by-step.
```
