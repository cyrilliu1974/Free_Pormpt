# Business Performance KPI Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Business Performance KPI Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive, SMART-compliant key performance indicator sets across five critical business dimensions for executives, analysts, and strategy teams. This business performance KPI prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific business scenario and produces a structured framework with 15–25 tailored metrics spanning financial health, customer success, operational efficiency, employee engagement, and innovation capacity. Each KPI includes a clear definition, calculation formula, target value, and authoritative source citation. Use it when launching a new strategic initiative, preparing board reports, building executive dashboards, or aligning teams around measurable goals. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its recommendations to match your business model, industry context, and growth stage. ● Delivers KPIs across five dimensions: financial, customer, operational, employee, and innovation/growth ● Every metric includes definition, formula, realistic target, and credible source citation for audit-ready reporting ● Provides implementation guidance on tracking frequency, dashboard design, and continuous improvement loops ● Adapts to diverse business scenarios from SaaS startups to manufacturing operations to nonprofit programs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business performance analyst developing a comprehensive KPI framework.

## Task
Analyze the business scenario below and create a SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) KPI set covering financial, customer, operational, employee, and innovation/growth dimensions. Each KPI must include definition, calculation formula, target value, and authoritative source citation.

## Context
{{business-scenario}}

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Business Overview:**
Summarize the business model and strategic goals based on the scenario provided.

**Financial KPIs:**
1. [KPI Name]
   - Definition: [Clear description]
   - Formula: [Calculation method]
   - Target: [Specific goal]
   - Source: [Authoritative citation]

[Repeat for 3-5 financial KPIs]

**Customer KPIs:**
[Same structure as Financial KPIs, 3-5 metrics]

**Operational KPIs:**
[Same structure, 3-5 metrics]

**Employee KPIs:**
[Same structure, 3-5 metrics]

**Innovation and Growth KPIs:**
[Same structure, 3-5 metrics]

**KPI Tracking and Reporting:**
Recommend implementation approaches, reporting frequency, dashboard requirements, and methods to ensure continuous improvement and strategic alignment.

Focus on the most critical KPIs for this specific scenario—prioritize quality and relevance over quantity.
```
