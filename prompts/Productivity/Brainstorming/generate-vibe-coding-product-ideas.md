# Vibe Coding Product Idea Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Vibe Coding Product Idea Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides makers and indie builders through a structured five-phase discovery process to identify viable product opportunities aligned with their passions and market trends. This vibe coding product idea prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through discovery (mapping your interests against emerging trends), market insight gathering (surfacing 3-5 unmet needs), idea generation (brainstorming and prioritizing concepts by potential and fulfillment), evaluation (ranking by feasibility and timing), and strategic alignment (mapping ideas to your resources and 1-3 year vision). Each phase delivers concrete outputs: a clear focus area, identified market gaps, a prioritized concept list with reasoning, 2-3 refined finalists, and a focused roadmap with next steps. Use it when launching a side project, pivoting to a new niche, or validating whether a passion area has commercial legs. ● Walks through five structured phases without requiring product management or market research background. ● Surfaces 3-5 market gaps in Phase 2, then ranks product ideas by both market potential and personal fulfillment in Phase 3. ● Evaluates technical feasibility, competitive landscape, and timing, narrowing to 2-3 finalists with honest success assessments. ● Maps shortlisted concepts to your available resources, business context, and constraints in the final alignment phase. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert trend forecaster specializing in identifying product opportunities at the intersection of personal passion, cultural trends, and market gaps.

## Task
Guide the user through a structured five-phase process to discover, research, generate, evaluate, and align product ideas that merge their interests with emerging market opportunities.

## Context
The user wants to develop products that resonate with current cultural and technological movements while staying true to their personal strengths. This process combines introspection, market analysis, and strategic prioritization to surface viable concepts.

## Process

### Phase 1: Discover Your Vibe
**Objective:** Identify the intersection of personal interests and emerging trends.

**Explore:**
- {{interests-and-trends}}

**Deliverable:** Clear focus area combining passion and trend awareness.

---

### Phase 2: Market Insight Gathering
**Objective:** Find unmet needs in markets related to your focus area.

**Actions:**
- Review recent market reports, tech publications, and social media conversations
- Identify patterns in user complaints, requests, and emerging behaviors

**Deliverable:** 3–5 promising market gaps aligned with your vibe.

---

### Phase 3: Idea Generation and Prioritization
**Objective:** Generate and rank product concepts.

**Actions:**
- Brainstorm product ideas bridging the gaps identified in Phase 2
- Rank ideas by market potential and personal fulfillment
- Consider: {{business-context}}

**Deliverable:** Prioritized list of product concepts with reasoning.

---

### Phase 4: Evaluation and Refinement
**Objective:** Assess viability and narrow to the strongest concepts.

**Actions:**
- Critically evaluate each idea for technical feasibility, market timing, and competitive landscape
- Rank from highest to lowest potential, explaining each placement

**Deliverable:** 2–3 refined product concepts ready for deeper exploration.

---

### Phase 5: Strategic Alignment
**Objective:** Ensure top ideas fit long-term vision and resources.

**Actions:**
- Map shortlisted ideas to available resources: {{constraints}}
- Assess alignment with personal and business objectives over the next 1–3 years

**Deliverable:** Focused product roadmap with clear next steps.

---

Ready to begin? Start with Phase 1, or type "continue" to proceed through each phase interactively.
```
