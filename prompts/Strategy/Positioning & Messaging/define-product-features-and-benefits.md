# Product Features and Benefits Mapper

## 簡介

The Product Features and Benefits Mapper is a free AI prompt that translates product features into customer-focused benefits and builds a prioritized value proposition for product marketing strategists. This product features and benefits prompt for ChatGPT takes your product and market context - including target audience, competitors, and unique selling points - and generates a two-column markdown table that directly links each feature to its corresponding customer benefit. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and helps product marketers identify unique selling points, connect features to both practical outcomes and emotional value, and prioritize the most impactful elements to craft a persuasive product narrative. Real use cases include preparing product launch materials, refining positioning documents, creating sales enablement assets, and aligning cross-functional teams around a shared value proposition. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond feature lists and articulate why your product matters to your target audience in clear, benefit-driven language. ● Maps each product feature to a tangible customer benefit with both practical and emotional dimensions ● Prioritizes features by market impact, surfacing the strongest differentiators first ● Incorporates competitive context and target audience psychographics to ensure relevance ● Outputs a clean markdown table ready for pitch decks, one-pagers, and sales training materials ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a product marketing strategist defining key features and benefits to create a compelling value proposition.

## Task
Analyze the product and translate its features into tangible customer benefits that resonate with the target audience. Identify unique selling points, connect features to both practical and emotional value, and prioritize the most impactful elements to build a persuasive product narrative.

## Context
{{product-and-market-context}}

Include: product name and description, target audience demographics and psychographics, industry, main competitors, and the product's unique selling proposition.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with two columns:

| Features | Benefits |
|----------|----------|

Each row must directly link one feature to its corresponding customer benefit. Prioritize features by impact, leading with the strongest differentiators. Benefits should address both practical outcomes and emotional appeal relevant to the target audience.
```
