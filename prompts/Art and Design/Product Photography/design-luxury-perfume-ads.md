# Luxury Perfume Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Perfume Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates six-panel high-fashion fragrance campaign imagery for perfume brands, creative directors, and marketing teams. The prompt produces a complete 3:2 landscape grid featuring hero bottle photography on reflective marble, macro glass detail with shallow depth of field, a model spraying fragrance in cinematic glow, evening glamour portrait with sculpted beauty lighting, ingredient-inspired still life arrangements, and a silhouetted signature brand finish. This luxury perfume ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers the controlled studio lighting, warm highlights, deep shadows, and ultra-high realism expected in prestige fragrance advertising. Reach for it when you need campaign-ready visuals that convey mystery, elegance, and high-fashion prestige without a photographer or studio budget. ● Combines hero bottle shot, macro glass detail, fragrance mist moment, evening portrait, ingredient still life, and backlit signature panel in one cohesive grid ● Controls lighting to preserve glass refraction, metallic cap reflections, and deep sculpted shadows without overexposure or flat rendering ● Customizable via product name, fragrance profile (rose, oud, amber), and color palette variables to match brand identity ● Produces high-fashion editorial realism with moody contrast, elegant serif typography, and cinematic spacing suitable for print and digital campaigns ## Prompt
