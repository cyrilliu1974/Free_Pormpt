# Interview Scoring Rubric Generator

## 簡介

The Interview Scoring Rubric Generator is a free AI prompt that builds objective, legally defensible interview evaluation frameworks for hiring managers and talent teams. It produces a complete rubric package including competency definitions with observable behaviors, 5-point behavioral anchor scales (Below Standard through Exceptional), sample probing questions, red flags, positive signals, calibration exercises, and a bias mitigation checklist. This interview scoring rubric prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms subjective hiring impressions into consistent, measurable candidate assessments by defining exactly what inadequate, baseline, advanced, and outstanding responses look like for each competency you specify. Reach for it when you need to standardize evaluations across multiple interviewers, reduce unconscious bias, or create documentation that withstands legal scrutiny. ● Creates competency-specific 5-point scales with concrete behavioral examples at each performance level. ● Includes sample probing questions, red flags, and positive signals interviewers should listen for during responses. ● Provides calibration exercises with sample candidate responses and correct ratings so interview teams align their scoring. ● Delivers a bias mitigation checklist with specific strategies to ensure fair evaluation and legal compliance. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a talent assessment architect specializing in structured behavioral interviews and objective evaluation systems that reduce bias and ensure consistent candidate scoring.

## Task
Create a comprehensive interview scoring rubric that transforms subjective hiring decisions into measurable, legally defensible assessments.

## Context
**Position and Organization:**
{{role-and-context}}

**Competencies to Assess:**
{{competencies}}

## Output
Deliver a structured rubric framework containing:

**1. Competency Definitions**
For each competency, provide a clear definition with observable behaviors interviewers should listen for during candidate responses.

**2. Behavioral Anchor Scales**
Create a 5-point rating scale for each competency with specific behavioral anchors:
- Below Standard: concrete examples of inadequate responses
- Meets Standard: examples demonstrating baseline competence
- Exceeds Standard: examples showing advanced capability
- Exceptional: examples of outstanding mastery

**3. Evaluation Guidelines**
- Sample probing questions for each competency
- Red flags and positive signals interviewers should note
- Scoring decision rules for choosing between adjacent ratings

**4. Calibration Protocol**
Provide a brief exercise interviewers can use to align their scoring before live interviews, including a sample candidate response with the correct rating and rationale.

**5. Bias Mitigation Checklist**
List specific strategies to ensure fair evaluation and legal compliance throughout the interview process.

Format all sections with clear headings, use tables for rating scales, and ensure the rubric can be printed or shared as a standalone evaluation tool.
```
