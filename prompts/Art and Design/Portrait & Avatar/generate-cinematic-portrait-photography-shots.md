# Cinematic Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates editorial-quality portrait images with natural lighting, realistic textures, and cinematic atmosphere for photographers, brands, and content creators. This portrait photography prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and other image generators produces 1:1 aspect ratio portraits (1200×1200px) featuring natural sunlight streaming from controlled angles, creating dramatic highlights and shadow contrast. It delivers dreamy yet confident expressions with natural makeup styling - rosy cheeks, subtle eyeliner, and soft lip gloss - while maintaining sharp, hyper-realistic detail in skin textures, hair, and facial features. Use it for social media profiles, editorial features, brand imagery, or any project requiring professional portrait photography without a studio shoot. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality portraits with cinematic lighting and a soft, elegant aesthetic that looks authentically human rather than over-processed. ● Natural sunlight lighting from the left and front with sharp highlights on hair, cheeks, and nose, plus darkened backgrounds for depth and contrast. ● Dreamy, confident expressions paired with natural makeup looks - thin eyeliner, mascara, rosy cheeks, and pink-orange gloss. ● Ultra HD output with hyper-realistic skin textures, sharp detail, and soft cinematic elegance throughout the frame. ● 1:1 square format (1200×1200px) ideal for Instagram, LinkedIn, portfolio sites, and editorial layouts. ## Prompt

```
Editorial portrait photography, 1:1 aspect ratio, 1200×1200px. Preserve facial features from reference image strictly.

{{subject-description}}

Expression: Dreamy, confident, slightly cheerful. Natural makeup with rosy cheeks, thin eyeliner, mascara, and pink-orange lip gloss.

Lighting: Natural sunlight streaming from the left and front, creating sharp highlights on hair, cheeks, and nose tip, with slightly darkened background for contrast. Cinematic quality with stunning light and shadow interplay.

Style: Realistic portrait with dreamy, gentle elegance and soft cinematic feel. Ultra HD, hyper-realistic textures, sharp detail throughout.
```
