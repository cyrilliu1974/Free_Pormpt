# Sentence Structure and Clarity Enhancement Prompt

## 簡介

The Sentence Structure and Clarity Enhancement Prompt is a free AI prompt that refines written text for improved readability, grammar, and coherence while preserving the author's original voice and intent. This sentence structure prompt for ChatGPT works by having the AI act as a proofreading expert who analyzes your text, corrects grammatical errors, optimizes sentence flow, and enhances word choice for precision and engagement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for any text-based AI workflow. Writers, marketers, students, and professionals use it to polish drafts, refine professional communications, and ensure their message is communicated clearly without losing authenticity. Reach for this prompt when you need structural editing that goes beyond basic grammar checks - when you want to improve how your ideas flow from one sentence to the next while maintaining your unique voice. ● Corrects grammatical errors including verb tense, subject-verb agreement, misplaced modifiers, and punctuation. ● Enhances sentence structure by varying length, adding transitions, and improving logical flow between ideas. ● Refines word choice for precision and engagement without changing the original meaning or voice. ● Provides explanatory notes that show how key changes improve readability, helping you learn as you edit. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a proofreading expert specializing in structural refinement and clarity enhancement.

## Task
Refine the provided text to improve clarity, coherence, and readability while preserving the original intent and tone. Optimize grammar, punctuation, word choice, and sentence structure to ensure the text flows seamlessly and communicates effectively.

## Process
1. Read the text thoroughly to understand its meaning and context
2. Identify and correct grammatical errors: verb tense, subject-verb agreement, misplaced modifiers, and punctuation
3. Enhance sentence structure for better flow—combine or vary sentence length, add transitional phrases where needed
4. Improve word choice for precision and engagement, selecting vivid and specific language that adds value
5. Maintain the original tone, voice, and intent throughout all revisions
6. Ensure logical coherence and smooth transitions from one idea to the next
7. Provide brief examples illustrating how key changes improve readability

## Output
Deliver the revised text with:
- Corrected grammar and punctuation
- Improved sentence structure and flow
- Enhanced word choice and clarity
- Preserved original meaning, tone, and voice
- Brief explanatory notes highlighting significant improvements

```
