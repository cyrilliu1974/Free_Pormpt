# Ride-Share Driver Launch Guide Prompt

## 簡介

The Ride-Share Driver Launch Guide Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces a complete onboarding and optimization roadmap for anyone starting a ride-share driving career. This ride-share career prompt for ChatGPT walks aspiring drivers through every phase - initial platform sign-up, vehicle preparation, regulatory compliance, peak-time strategy, and performance optimization - tailored to their specific platform (Uber, Lyft, or others), vehicle type, availability window, location, and driving experience. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a chronological bullet-point action plan with sub-steps, earning tips, and compliance checks. Real-world use cases include side-hustle planning for part-time drivers, full-time solopreneur launches, and vehicle acquisition decisions informed by local market conditions. This prompt is for anyone exploring ride-share driving as income - whether transitioning careers, supplementing existing work, or testing the gig economy - and who needs a structured, personalized roadmap rather than generic platform FAQs. ● Tailors sign-up steps and vehicle requirements to the chosen platform and local regulations ● Identifies peak earning windows and demand zones based on the driver's availability and location ● Provides compliance checklists for insurance, background checks, vehicle inspections, and platform policies ● Includes safety practices and optimization tactics scaled to the driver's experience level ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert ride-share advisor helping aspiring drivers launch successful solopreneur or side-hustle careers.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step action plan for becoming a successful ride-share driver on {{platform}}. Cover the complete journey from initial sign-up through optimizing performance and maximizing earnings, ensuring the driver meets all platform compliance requirements.

## Context
Driver profile:
- Platform: {{platform}}
- Vehicle: {{vehicle-type}}
- Availability: {{availability}}
- Location: {{location}}
- Driving experience: {{driving-experience}}

Tailor recommendations to this specific setup, addressing local regulations, peak earning windows for their schedule, vehicle optimization opportunities, and experience-appropriate safety practices.

## Output
Deliver the action plan as a chronological bullet-point list. Each major step should include relevant sub-points with detailed instructions, tips for maximizing earnings, compliance checks, and optimization tactics specific to their profile.
```
