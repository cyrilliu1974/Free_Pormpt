# Contrarian Content Ideas Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Contrarian Content Ideas Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces thought-provoking content angles by systematically challenging conventional wisdom in any field. This contrarian content prompt for ChatGPT walks through a four-step process: it first identifies dominant narratives and widely held beliefs in your topic, then questions the foundations and evidence supporting those beliefs, explores alternative viewpoints that contradict the mainstream, and finally delivers 20 contrarian content ideas with explanations of why each challenges the status quo. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, showing its reasoning at each step so you understand the logic behind every contrarian angle. Writers, marketers, and thought leaders use it to break through content sameness and develop perspectives that spark debate and engagement. Reach for this prompt when you need to stand out in a crowded content landscape or when your audience has grown tired of echo-chamber thinking. ● Systematically identifies mainstream beliefs and accepted best practices in any topic area ● Questions the foundations of conventional wisdom with structured reasoning ● Explores data, scenarios, and perspectives that contradict popular consensus ● Delivers 20 numbered contrarian ideas, each with a clear explanation of its provocative angle ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic content ideation specialist who identifies unconventional angles and challenges conventional wisdom.

## Task
Generate 20 contrarian content ideas about {{topic-and-context}} by systematically questioning mainstream beliefs and exploring alternative viewpoints.

## Process
1. **Identify widely held beliefs or trends** in this area. List the dominant narratives, popular opinions, and accepted best practices.
2. **Question the foundations** of these beliefs. Examine why they are held, what evidence supports them, and whether that justification is sound or simply inherited momentum.
3. **Explore alternative viewpoints** that challenge the consensus. Consider scenarios, data, or perspectives that contradict the mainstream position.
4. **Propose 20 contrarian content ideas** that could be written about this topic, each offering a fresh or provocative angle.

## Output
For each step above, show your reasoning explicitly. Present the final 20 ideas as a numbered list with a one-sentence explanation of why each is contrarian.
```
