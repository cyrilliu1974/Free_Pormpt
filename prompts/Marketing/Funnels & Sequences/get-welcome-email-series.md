# Welcome Email Series Builder for New Subscribers

## 簡介

The Welcome Email Series Builder for New Subscribers is a free AI prompt that designs multi-email onboarding sequences to introduce your brand and engage new subscribers from their first interaction. This welcome email series prompt for ChatGPT generates 3-5 structured emails that form a cohesive subscriber journey. Each email includes a compelling subject line, key content points, optimal send timing relative to the previous message, and a specific call-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your business context and brand voice to create sequences that deliver value while building trust. Common use cases include SaaS product onboarding, e-commerce first-purchase nurturing, newsletter subscription kickoffs, and course enrollment sequences. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework for turning new subscribers into engaged community members through strategic automated touchpoints. ● Produces 3-5 emails with subject lines engineered to maintain open rates across the sequence ● Maps send intervals that balance consistent touchpoints with avoiding inbox fatigue ● Tailors content to your specific business context and matches your established brand voice ● Includes strategic calls-to-action that guide subscribers toward meaningful next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in subscriber onboarding and engagement.

## Task
Design a comprehensive welcome email series that introduces new subscribers to the brand, delivers value, and drives engagement. For each email in the sequence, provide:
- Compelling subject line
- Key content points
- Optimal send interval (timing from previous email)
- Effective call-to-action

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{brand-voice}}

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings for each email in the series (Email 1, Email 2, etc.). Use bullet points for key content points. Format:

**Email [Number]: [Purpose]**
- **Subject Line:** [text]
- **Send Interval:** [timing]
- **Key Content:**
  - [point]
  - [point]
  - [point]
- **Call-to-Action:** [specific action]

Include 3-5 emails that form a cohesive onboarding journey.
```
