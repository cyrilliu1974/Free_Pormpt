# 21-Day Creative Thinking Challenge Builder

## 簡介

The 21-Day Creative Thinking Challenge Builder is a free AI prompt that designs personalized three-week creativity programs for professionals, students, and innovators seeking systematic creative development. This creative thinking prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete 21-day roadmap featuring daily creative prompts, reflection questions, and targeted exercises in brainstorming, lateral thinking, and mental block elimination. Each day builds progressively on the last, creating cumulative momentum. Writers use it to break through blocks, product teams deploy it for innovation sprints, and educators adapt it for student workshops. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, the prompt accepts any purpose - from product innovation to content creation - and tailors every element accordingly. Reach for this prompt when you need structured, progressive creative development rather than one-off brainstorming sessions. ● Delivers four complete sample days (Day 1, 7, 14, 21) with themes, prompts, questions, and exercises matched to your declared purpose ● Structures each day with three components: a creative prompt, a reflection question, and a specific technique (brainstorming, lateral thinking, or mental block busting) ● Includes supplemental resources - recommended reading, inspiration sources, and community links - all tailored to your field ● Produces output in a clear, reusable format suitable for personal use, team workshops, or educational curriculum ## Prompt
