# Proofread Marketing Materials Prompt

## 簡介

The Proofread Marketing Materials Prompt is a free AI prompt that reviews marketing copy for errors, style consistency, and brand voice alignment. It takes your marketing materials and brand guidelines, then returns a detailed, numbered list of corrections and suggestions across grammar, tone, formatting, and messaging. This marketing proofreading prompt for ChatGPT works by having the AI act as an expert marketing proofreader who scans for spelling and grammar errors, tone inconsistencies, brand voice mismatches, unclear sentence structures, factual inaccuracies, formatting issues, and opportunities to strengthen impact. Each correction pinpoints the location, identifies the issue, provides a recommended change, and explains the rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when preparing campaign emails, landing pages, ad copy, blog posts, social media content, or any customer-facing material that needs to reflect your brand standards before publication. ● Catches spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors across all marketing materials ● Flags tone and style inconsistencies that weaken brand voice ● Verifies facts, figures, and claims for accuracy and proper support ● Suggests stronger phrasing and word choices to increase persuasive impact ● Ensures formatting consistency across headings, bullets, and section structure ● Reviews industry terminology for appropriateness and audience fit ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert proofreader specializing in marketing content.

## Task
Review the provided marketing materials for errors, consistency, and brand alignment. Deliver corrections and suggestions as a detailed, numbered list with each issue clearly separated.

## Context
**Materials to review:** {{marketing-materials}}

**Business & brand context:** {{brand-context}}

Ensure the content:
- Is free of spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors
- Maintains consistent tone, style, and messaging throughout
- Adheres to the brand voice and style guidelines described
- Uses clear, well-structured sentences that improve readability
- Contains accurate facts, figures, and properly cited claims
- Follows consistent formatting (headings, subheadings, bullets)
- Applies industry terminology appropriately for the target audience
- Maximizes impact without altering core messages

Where improvements are possible, suggest alternative phrasings or word choices that strengthen effectiveness.

## Output
Provide a numbered list where each item addresses one correction or suggestion. For each entry, specify:
1. Location (section/paragraph)
2. Issue found
3. Recommended change
4. Rationale
```
