# Audience Feedback Survey Builder for Speeches

## 簡介

The Audience Feedback Survey Builder for Speeches is a free AI prompt that generates structured feedback collection systems for speakers, presenters, and speech analysts who need data-driven insights on their delivery and content. This audience feedback prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete three-part framework: a question bank covering clarity, relevance, impact, delivery style, and engagement; recommended distribution methods and timing to maximize response rates; and both quantitative scoring techniques and qualitative thematic analysis approaches. You supply speech details - topic, audience, context like conference keynote or classroom lecture - and your preferred feedback approach, including survey length, rating scales versus open comments, and anonymous or attributed responses. The prompt structures questions using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and minimize bias, then maps analysis techniques that turn raw feedback into actionable improvement priorities. Real use cases span corporate town halls, academic lectures, sales presentations, and public speaking competitions. Reach for it whenever you need systematic audience insights beyond anecdotal comments. ● Generates question banks balancing numeric scales, multiple choice, and open-ended prompts across content and delivery dimensions ● Recommends distribution channels, timing windows, and platform tools matched to your speaking context ● Outlines quantitative scoring, correlation analysis, and thematic coding techniques to synthesize findings into improvement priorities ● Applies dependency grammar structure to minimize response bias and ensure coherent question flow ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech analyst specializing in audience feedback systems and data-driven presentation improvement.

## Task
Design a comprehensive survey to collect audience insights on speech content and delivery, then outline methods for analyzing the results to identify strengths, weaknesses, and improvement opportunities.

## Context
**Speech details:**
{{speech-details}}
(Include: topic, target audience, speaking context—conference keynote, classroom lecture, corporate town hall, etc.—and any specific aspects you're concerned about, such as pacing, technical clarity, storytelling, or visual aids.)

**Feedback approach:**
{{feedback-approach}}
(Specify: preferred style—numeric scales, open-ended comments, anonymous vs. attributed, post-event timing, and format constraints like survey length or delivery platform.)

## Output
Provide a structured deliverable with these sections:

### 1. Survey Design
- Question bank covering clarity, relevance, impact, delivery style, and audience engagement
- Mix of quantitative (rating scales, multiple choice) and qualitative (open-response) items
- Questions structured using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and minimize response bias

### 2. Data Collection Methods
- Recommended distribution channels and timing
- Strategies to maximize response rate and quality
- Tools or platforms suited to the speaking context

### 3. Analysis Techniques
- Quantitative analysis: scoring, distribution patterns, correlation between dimensions
- Qualitative analysis: thematic coding of open responses, sentiment trends
- Synthesis framework to translate findings into actionable improvement priorities
```
