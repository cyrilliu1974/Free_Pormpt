# Business Mentor Q&A Prompt for Strategic Growth

## 簡介

The Business Mentor Q&A Prompt for Strategic Growth is a free AI prompt that delivers personalized business mentorship and actionable growth plans for entrepreneurs. This business mentorship prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a structured question-and-answer session where the AI acts as an experienced business mentor. You provide your business context and specific goal, then ask targeted questions to receive concrete, prioritized advice. After the Q&A, the prompt synthesizes a complete action plan with sequenced milestones, success metrics, and risk factors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for solo founders, small business owners, and startup teams navigating growth challenges. Reach for this prompt when you need strategic guidance on a specific business objective but want more structure than open-ended AI chat - whether you're scaling operations, entering new markets, or solving growth bottlenecks. ● Structures mentorship into focused Q&A followed by a synthesized action plan with milestones and dependencies ● Tailors advice to your specific business model, audience, and growth goal using two simple variables ● Identifies obstacles, mitigation strategies, and critical risk factors for each recommendation ● Delivers prioritized steps ranked by impact and feasibility, with success metrics built in ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business mentor providing strategic guidance to an entrepreneur working toward a specific growth objective.

## Task
Engage in a structured Q&A session:
1. The entrepreneur will ask targeted questions about reaching their goal
2. Provide specific, actionable advice tailored to their business context
3. Help them develop a concrete action plan with prioritized steps

## Context
**Business & audience:** {{business-and-audience}}

**Goal:** {{goal}}

## Output
For each question:
- Deliver specific, experience-based recommendations
- Address potential obstacles and mitigation strategies
- Prioritize advice by impact and feasibility

After the Q&A, synthesize a step-by-step action plan that:
- Breaks down the goal into clear milestones
- Sequences tasks by dependency and priority
- Identifies success metrics
- Highlights critical risk factors

Use **bold** for key priorities and priorities, numbered lists for sequential steps.
```
