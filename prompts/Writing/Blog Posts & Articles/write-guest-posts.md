# Guest Post Outline Generator for Link Building

## 簡介

The Guest Post Outline Generator for Link Building is a free AI prompt that creates structured, pitch-ready guest post outlines designed to earn backlinks and expand audience reach for content marketers and SEO professionals. This guest post prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your topic, niche, and brand expertise to produce a complete outline with attention-grabbing hooks, 3-5 main sections with subpoints, and strategic placement for statistics, case studies, and backlinks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating outlines that align with the content style and editorial standards of blogs in your space. Real use cases include pitching to industry publications, preparing content briefs for writers, and mapping out link-building campaigns that deliver genuine value to host audiences while establishing author credibility. This prompt is ideal for content marketers, SEO specialists, and digital PR professionals who need repeatable frameworks for guest blogging outreach and execution. ● Creates complete outlines with introduction hooks, 3-5 main sections, subpoints, and conclusion with natural backlink placement ● Identifies where to integrate statistics, case studies, and expert quotes to boost credibility ● Tailors content angle and structure to match the audience preferences and editorial style of target blogs ● Includes author bio section and call-to-action framework to maximize engagement and link value ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in guest blogging strategy and execution.

## Task
Craft a structured guest post outline that will expand reach and build backlinks for {{topic-and-niche}}. The outline should be ready to pitch to or write for relevant blogs in this space.

## Context
Guest posting succeeds when it delivers genuine value to the host blog's audience while establishing the author's credibility. Research the content style and audience preferences common in this niche. Develop a unique angle that differentiates this piece from existing coverage while aligning with typical blog themes in the space.

**Brand positioning**: {{brand-expertise}}
**Target backlink**: {{backlink-url}}

Incorporate 2-3 relevant statistics, case studies, or expert quotes to enhance credibility. Ensure the content provides actionable value while naturally showcasing your expertise.

## Output
Deliver a detailed outline with:

**I. Introduction**  
- Hook that grabs attention immediately  
- Problem or question the post addresses  
- Preview of value readers will gain  

**II. Main Points** (3-5 sections)  
- Each point as a clear heading  
- 2-4 subpoints per section showing key arguments, examples, or steps  
- Note where statistics, case studies, or quotes will be integrated  

**III. Conclusion**  
- Summary of key takeaways  
- Call-to-action that encourages engagement  
- Natural placement for author bio and backlink

Write with clarity and coherence, ensuring each section flows logically into the next.
```
