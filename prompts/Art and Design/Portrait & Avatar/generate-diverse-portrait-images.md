# Split-Face Portrait Generator for Skin Conditions

## 簡介

The Split-Face Portrait Generator for Skin Conditions is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic dual-condition portraits for medical education, dermatology practices, and awareness campaigns. It produces close-up portrait photographs with a split composition: the left side shows healthy, clear skin while the right side displays a visible skin condition, all captured in soft natural window light against an indoor kitchen background. This split-face portrait prompt for Midjourney works equally well on Flux, Nano Banana, and other modern image generation models, delivering professional 85mm lens aesthetics with shallow depth of field. Use it to generate training materials for healthcare professionals, patient education resources, or advocacy visuals that require side-by-side anatomical comparison. The prompt accepts variables for subject description and facial expression, giving you control over demographics and emotional tone while maintaining medical accuracy and photographic realism. ● Produces split-face portraits with one side showing healthy skin and the other displaying a specified skin condition for clear visual comparison. ● Renders subjects in soft, natural window daylight within a kitchen setting, creating approachable, non-clinical imagery suitable for patient-facing materials. ● Mimics professional 85mm portrait photography with shallow depth of field and photorealistic texture detail. ● Accepts customizable subject descriptions and facial expressions to match your demographic needs and emotional context. ## Prompt

```
Close-up photo portrait of {{subject-description}} with {{facial-expression}}; indoor kitchen background; shot on professional camera, soft window daylight; split-face dual portrait: left side healthy clear skin, right side showing visible skin condition; photorealistic, 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field --ar 3:4
```
