The Smartphone Fashion Portrait Collage Generator is a free AI prompt that produces ultra-realistic 4-photo grid layouts in a minimalist editorial style for fashion brands, content creators, and social media campaigns. This smartphone fashion portrait collage prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers four distinct poses in a single 1:1 composition: full-body standing, crouching, casual seated, and upper-body portrait, all captured with natural smartphone photography aesthetics and consistent lighting across frames. It renders a young female model with customizable hair, outfit, and background variables, applying dewy natural makeup, realistic skin and fabric textures, and a color palette of white, denim blue, and soft gray.
