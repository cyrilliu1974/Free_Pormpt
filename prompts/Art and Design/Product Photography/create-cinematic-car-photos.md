# Cinematic Car Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Car Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces dramatic, low-angle vehicle shots with rain-soaked street reflections and professional lighting for automotive photographers, marketers, and digital artists. This cinematic car photography prompt for Midjourney produces moody, high-impact images featuring your chosen vehicle positioned under street lights with wet pavement reflections and rim lighting that highlights body curves. The prompt specifies a 24mm f/2.8 lens with a 1/30 shutter speed to capture light streaks, delivering the atmospheric quality of professional automotive night photography. Use it for product showcases, social media content, portfolio pieces, or any project that demands visually striking car imagery with filmic depth.
