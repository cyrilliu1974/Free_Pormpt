# Late Rent Policy Generator for Landlords

## 簡介

The Late Rent Policy Generator for Landlords is a free AI prompt that produces professional, legally sound late rent policy documents for property managers and landlords who need consistent rental payment enforcement procedures. This late rent policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates a structured document covering payment deadlines, tiered late fee schedules, grace periods, tenant extension request procedures, enforcement escalation steps, and tenant acknowledgment sections. It helps landlords establish universal payment standards that apply equally to all tenants while incorporating state and local rental law requirements. Property managers use it to draft clear policies for single-family homes, multi-unit buildings, or full portfolios that balance firm enforcement with professional tenant communication. Reach for this prompt when you need a defensible policy framework that protects cash flow, motivates timely rent payment, and reduces favoritism or inconsistent application across tenants. ● Produces six core policy sections including payment due dates, late fee structures, extension request protocols, enforcement escalation, and tenant acknowledgment language. ● Incorporates tiered consequence structures with specific dates, dollar amounts, and escalation triggers that encourage tenants to prioritize housing costs. ● Includes proactive extension request procedures that let tenants communicate before the due date with reduced penalties, balancing flexibility and accountability. ● Ensures compliance with state and local rental laws governing grace periods, late fee caps, and eviction triggers you provide in the rental-laws variable. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert property management consultant specializing in tenant policy design that balances consistent enforcement with operational clarity.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Late Rent Policy Document that establishes clear payment expectations, consequences, and procedures. The policy protects landlord interests while maintaining professional tenant relationships through consistent, legally sound rules.

## Context
Effective rent collection relies on universal standards applied to all tenants equally. Late fees function as priority-setting incentives that encourage timely payment by creating meaningful consequences. The policy must be firm, professional, legally defensible, and easy for tenants to understand.

Incorporate:
- Universal payment standards (no customized due dates or exceptions)
- Tiered consequence structure with specific dates, fees, and escalation steps
- Proactive communication option: tenants may request extensions *before* the due date with reduced penalties
- Clear language explaining late fees as tools that help tenants prioritize housing costs
- Payment methods, communication procedures, eviction triggers, and record-keeping requirements

Comply with all applicable regulations in {{rental-laws}}.

## Input
**{{property-details}}** — Property type, number of units, standard rent due date, grace period (if any), late fee amounts, and when fees apply.

**{{rental-laws}}** — State and local rental regulations that govern late fees, grace periods, and eviction procedures.

## Output
Deliver a complete, ready-to-use policy document structured with these sections:

**1. Policy Overview**  
**2. Payment Due Dates**  
**3. Late Fee Structure**  
**4. Extension Request Procedures**  
**5. Enforcement Protocol**  
**6. Tenant Acknowledgment**

Use professional formatting: headers, bullet points, and numbered lists for maximum clarity and legal defensibility.
```
