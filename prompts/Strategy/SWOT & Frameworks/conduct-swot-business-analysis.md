# SWOT Business Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The SWOT Business Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed strategic assessments for business owners, consultants, and strategists evaluating market position and competitive dynamics. This SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your business overview, competitor list, and market context to generate a four-section framework: internal strengths you can exploit, weaknesses that need attention, external opportunities to pursue, and threats requiring mitigation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering actionable insights for each quadrant plus cross-cutting strategic recommendations. Use it when launching a new product, entering a market, preparing investor presentations, or refreshing your strategic plan. ● Identifies internal strengths with guidance on how to amplify competitive advantages ● Surfaces weaknesses alongside practical recommendations to close capability gaps ● Maps external opportunities in your market with strategies to capture them ● Flags threats from competitors, regulations, and market shifts with mitigation plans ● Concludes with synthesized strategic recommendations that tie all four quadrants together ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist conducting a comprehensive SWOT analysis.

## Task
Analyze the internal factors (strengths and weaknesses) and external factors (opportunities and threats) affecting the business. Identify crucial aspects of its current state, market position, and competitive landscape.

## Context
**Business overview:** {{business-overview}}

**Main competitors:** {{competitors}}

**Market context:** {{market-context}}

## Output
Provide a structured SWOT analysis with four sections:

**Strengths**
- Internal attributes and resources supporting success: unique resources, skilled workforce, brand reputation, technology access, patents, customer loyalty, market share
- Include actionable insights on how to leverage each strength

**Weaknesses**
- Internal factors limiting goal achievement: capability gaps, resource constraints, location disadvantages, product/service gaps, brand recognition issues, financial limitations
- Include recommendations to address each weakness

**Opportunities**
- External chances to improve market performance: market growth, lifestyle changes, technological advances, partnership potential, favorable regulatory changes
- Include strategies to capitalize on each opportunity

**Threats**
- External challenges: competition intensity, shifting market requirements, legislative impacts, financial risks, emerging substitutes
- Include mitigation strategies for each threat

Format each section as bullet points with brief explanations. Conclude with 3-5 strategic recommendations that synthesize the findings across all four quadrants.
```
