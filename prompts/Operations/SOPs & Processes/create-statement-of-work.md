# Statement of Work Builder Prompt for Any Industry

## 簡介

The Statement of Work Builder Prompt for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that generates step-by-step guides for creating detailed, industry-tailored Statements of Work for business consultants and project managers. This statement of work prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive seven-step framework covering industry overview, scope definition, pricing and budget models, trade-off analysis, contract terms, stakeholder alignment, and finalization protocols. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its output to any industry context you specify - from software development to construction to healthcare services. The prompt walks you through defining clear deliverables with acceptance criteria, choosing appropriate pricing models (fixed-price, time-and-materials, value-based), creating trade-off comparison sheets for common project tensions, drafting RACI matrices for role clarity, and establishing stakeholder mapping by influence and interest. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft a professional SOW document but want structured guidance on all the critical components, terminology, and best practices specific to your industry. ● Produces industry-specific SOW guides with tailored examples, terminology, and regulatory considerations ● Covers seven essential stages from industry overview through finalization, approval workflows, and version control ● Includes practical templates for deliverable breakdowns, cost estimation, trade-off comparison sheets, and RACI matrices ● Addresses real-world challenges like reconciling conflicting stakeholder inputs, setting realistic contingencies, and crafting balanced termination clauses ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specialized in creating Statements of Work (SOWs) across industries. Your deliverables are known for clarity, comprehensiveness, and stakeholder alignment.

## Task
Create a detailed, step-by-step guide for developing a Statement of Work tailored to {{industry}}. The guide must cover pricing models, deliverables, trade-off analysis, contract details, and stakeholder alignment processes.

## Context
{{project-context}} should inform the specificity and examples throughout the guide.

## Output
Structure the guide using these seven steps:

### 1. Industry Overview
- Key characteristics: List 3-5 defining traits of {{industry}}
- Major players: Identify 3-5 leading organizations or competitors
- Regulatory landscape: Describe compliance requirements and constraints

### 2. Defining Scope and Deliverables
- Identify project objectives: Explain how to translate business goals into clear, measurable outcomes
- Break down deliverables: Provide a template for itemizing tangible outputs with acceptance criteria
- Set milestones and timelines: Offer guidance on creating realistic schedules with dependencies

### 3. Pricing and Budget
- Determine pricing model: Discuss common models (fixed-price, time-and-materials, value-based) with pros/cons for {{industry}}
- Estimate costs: Explain accurate cost estimation techniques including labor, materials, and overhead
- Build in contingencies: Advise on appropriate reserve percentages for risk mitigation

### 4. Trade-off Analysis
- Identify potential trade-offs: List common tensions (speed vs. quality, cost vs. scope, flexibility vs. certainty)
- Create trade-off sheets: Provide a comparison template showing options, impacts, and recommendations
- Prioritize trade-offs: Offer a framework for ranking based on business impact and stakeholder needs

### 5. Contract Details
- Define roles and responsibilities: Explain how to assign clear ownership using RACI or similar matrices
- Specify payment terms: Discuss milestone-based payments, invoicing schedules, and late payment provisions
- Include termination clauses: Advise on crafting balanced exit terms covering notice periods, deliverable handoff, and final compensation

### 6. Stakeholder Alignment
- Identify key stakeholders: Explain stakeholder mapping by influence and interest level
- Set up reference calls: Provide tips for conducting effective discovery sessions and expectation-setting meetings
- Incorporate feedback: Offer guidance on reconciling conflicting inputs and documenting consensus

### 7. Finalizing the SOW
- Review and refine: Emphasize cross-functional review for accuracy, completeness, and legal compliance
- Obtain approvals: Outline signature authority requirements and approval workflow
- Distribute and archive: Advise on version control, secure storage, and access protocols

Use bullet points and clear subheadings. Tailor all examples, terminology, and trade-offs to {{industry}}.
```
