# Linkable Asset Strategy for SEO & Link Building

## 簡介

The Linkable Asset Strategy for SEO & Link Building is a free AI prompt that develops complete content strategies for attracting authoritative backlinks and improving organic search rankings. This linkable asset prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a multi-format content strategy tailored to your industry and competitive landscape. It outputs specific asset ideas (infographics, videos, original research, interactive tools), production workflows, distribution plans, and measurable success metrics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text AI platforms. Real-world applications include B2B companies creating industry reports that earn hundreds of backlinks, niche publishers developing data visualizations that become citation sources, and SaaS platforms building free tools that naturally attract editorial links. This prompt is built for SEO specialists, content strategists, and digital marketers who need a systematic approach to link-earning content that goes beyond guest posting and outreach templates. ● Produces 3–5 specific linkable asset concepts with rationale for why each will earn backlinks in your industry ● Maps out complete production workflows including timelines, required resources, and design specifications ● Identifies target publications and platforms with tailored outreach messaging and social amplification tactics ● Defines measurable KPIs for link acquisition, traffic impact, and ranking improvements with realistic timelines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in SEO and linkable asset development.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategy for creating linkable assets—infographics, videos, original research, or interactive tools—that will attract backlinks and improve search rankings.

## Context
Industry and audience: {{industry-and-audience}}
Competitive landscape: {{competitive-context}}

The strategy should account for current industry trends, content formats that resonate with the target audience, and realistic distribution channels. Focus on assets with high shareability and genuine utility that naturally attract links from authoritative sources.

## Output
Provide a structured strategy with clear headings and bullet points covering:

**Asset Concepts**
- 3–5 specific linkable asset ideas tailored to the industry
- Rationale for why each will attract backlinks

**Creation Process**
- Production workflow and timeline
- Required resources and skills
- Design principles and format specifications

**Distribution & Outreach**
- Target publications, blogs, and platforms
- Outreach tactics and messaging approach
- Social amplification strategy

**Success Metrics**
- KPIs to track link acquisition
- Traffic and ranking impact measurement
- Timeline for expected results
```
