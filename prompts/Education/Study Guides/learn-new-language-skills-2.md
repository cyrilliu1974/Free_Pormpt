# Language Learning Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Language Learning Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized, step-by-step language study plans tailored to your proficiency level, learning style, goals, and available time. This language learning prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete roadmap organized into skill-specific activities (reading, writing, speaking, listening), resource recommendations, study techniques, milestone timelines, and progress checkpoints. It works by analyzing your current proficiency, preferred learning style (visual, auditory, kinesthetic), personal goals, and weekly study time to build a logical, actionable curriculum. Run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to receive a bullet-point plan that balances all core linguistic competencies while respecting your schedule and motivation. Use this prompt when you need a structured approach to language acquisition that goes beyond generic advice - whether you're starting from scratch, breaking through a plateau, or preparing for professional certification. ● Assesses your starting proficiency and aligns activities with your sensory learning style ● Recommends specific resources and materials matched to your target language and level ● Designs a realistic timeline with milestones and built-in progress checkpoints ● Balances all four core skills - reading, writing, speaking, listening - in a cohesive plan ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert language learning specialist creating personalized, comprehensive learning plans.

## Task
Develop a structured language learning plan that addresses all core skills (reading, writing, speaking, listening) and includes resources, study techniques, milestones, and progress checkpoints. Ensure each element builds logically on the others.

## Context
**Target language:** {{target-language}}
**Current proficiency:** {{current-proficiency}}
**Learning style:** {{learning-style}}
**Goals and motivations:** {{goals}}
**Available study time per week:** {{available-time}}

## Output
Deliver the plan as a bullet-point list organized into clear, actionable steps and recommendations covering:
- Assessment of starting point and learning approach alignment
- Skill-specific activities (reading, writing, speaking, listening)
- Recommended resources and materials
- Effective study techniques and strategies
- Realistic timeline with milestones
- Progress assessment and plan adjustment methods
```
