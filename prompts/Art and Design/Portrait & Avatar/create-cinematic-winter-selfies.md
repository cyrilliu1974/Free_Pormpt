# Cinematic Winter Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Winter Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric nighttime selfie portraits featuring realistic snowfall, natural lighting, and raw winter energy for content creators and visual artists. This winter portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces close-up outdoor selfies shot from a confident upward angle during heavy snowfall. The prompt generates realistic details like snowflakes on lashes and hair, flushed cheeks from cold, visible skin texture, and snow-dusted winter clothing against dark night sky backgrounds. Each image captures the dramatic atmosphere of a nighttime winter shoot with soft cold lighting that creates natural highlights and a cinematic mood. Use it for social media content, lifestyle photography concepts, seasonal marketing visuals, or personal avatar creation. Reach for this prompt when you need authentic winter selfie aesthetics without organizing an outdoor night shoot in actual snowfall conditions. ● Produces close-up selfies with realistic snowfall effects including snowflakes on eyelashes, hair, and winter clothing. ● Captures natural skin texture with cold-flushed cheeks and realistic facial features lit by soft overhead night lighting. ● Generates dark winter night backgrounds with faintly visible frosted tree branches for dramatic atmospheric depth. ● Includes comprehensive negative prompts to avoid blurry faces, distorted proportions, plastic skin, and artificial lighting artifacts. ## Prompt

```
A close-up outdoor selfie taken at night during heavy snowfall. {{subject-description}}, holding a camera with her left hand positioned slightly below eye level, capturing her face from a confident upward angle. Lips softly parted, expression calm yet intense. Natural skin tone with visible, realistic texture; cheeks slightly flushed from the cold. Snowflakes rest on her lashes and hair. She wears winter clothing—{{winter-outfit}}—dusted and covered with fresh snow across the shoulders, collar, and headwear.

Background: dark winter night sky with faint, lightly frosted tree branches barely visible, creating dramatic atmosphere. Soft, cold night lighting from above and slightly in front, creating a subtle glow on her cheeks and gentle highlights on the jacket. Cold, cinematic, and raw winter energy; stylish modern snowy portrait with natural texture and detail.

Avoid: blurry face, altered facial features, incorrect facial structure, distorted proportions, extra limbs, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed eyes, inaccurate expression, unrealistic skin texture, plastic skin, oversoftened skin, unnatural lighting, harsh shadows, artifacts, pixelation, low resolution.
```
