# Brand Identity Story Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Identity Story Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cohesive brand narratives for companies, founders, and marketing teams who need to articulate their brand's essence in story form. This brand identity story prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) through building a five-section narrative: an attention-grabbing introduction, origin story, core values exposition, unique selling proposition articulation, and a compelling conclusion. You provide the company name, target audience, brand foundation (values and USP), and company history; the AI analyzes what resonates with your audience and structures a narrative where each section builds naturally on the last. Marketing agencies use it to draft brand guidelines, startups use it to define positioning before launch, and established companies use it to refresh messaging across channels. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured brand story that goes beyond taglines - one that connects history, values, and differentiation in a single coherent document ready for pitch decks, About pages, or internal alignment. ● Produces five logically sequenced sections (introduction, history, values, USP, conclusion) that flow as a unified story ● Analyzes which brand elements will resonate most with the specified target audience ● Structures narrative using dependency grammar so each section reinforces the next ● Delivers language refined for both emotional connection and clear differentiation in the market ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in narrative identity development.

## Task
Create a compelling brand identity story that captures the company's essence, values, and unique selling proposition. Develop a cohesive narrative that builds logically from the company's origins through its current market position, ensuring each element reinforces the next.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Core values and unique selling proposition: {{brand-foundation}}

Company history and background: {{company-history}}

## Process
1. Analyze the company's mission, values, and market position
2. Identify what resonates most with the target audience
3. Weave together history, values, and unique offerings into a unified narrative
4. Structure each section to build naturally on the previous one
5. Refine language to create emotional resonance while clearly communicating differentiation

## Output
Deliver a structured narrative with these sections:

**Introduction** – Hook the audience and establish brand promise

**Company History** – Origin story and evolution

**Core Values** – Foundational beliefs and principles

**Unique Selling Proposition** – What sets this brand apart

**Conclusion** – Compelling call to understanding and connection

Each section should flow seamlessly into the next, creating a complete and emotionally engaging brand story.
```
