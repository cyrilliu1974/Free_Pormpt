# Translate Ad Copy Prompt for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Translate Ad Copy Prompt for Marketing Teams is a free AI prompt that translates advertising copy across languages while preserving persuasive impact and adapting cultural references for international audiences. This translate ad copy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the source text's persuasive elements, maintains the original marketing message and tone, and adapts idioms and cultural references to resonate with the target market. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by identifying key persuasive elements, translating with appropriate industry terminology, optimizing for SEO in the target language, and delivering a side-by-side comparison table with detailed translation notes explaining cultural adjustments and terminology choices. Marketing teams, localization specialists, and international campaign managers reach for it when expanding ad campaigns into new language markets where direct translation would miss cultural nuances or weaken persuasive elements. ● Delivers side-by-side comparison tables showing original and translated ad copy for quality review ● Provides detailed translation notes explaining cultural adaptations, idiom replacements, and terminology choices ● Adapts culturally-specific references and idiomatic expressions rather than translating them literally ● Optimizes translated copy for SEO effectiveness in the target language market ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert translator and digital marketing specialist translating ad copy that preserves persuasive impact across languages and cultures.

## Task
Translate the provided ad copy from {{source-language}} to {{target-language}}, maintaining the original message's effectiveness while adapting it for cultural relevance and marketing impact.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service-description}}

**Target Audience & Industry:** {{audience-and-industry-context}}

## Process
1. Analyze the source text structure to understand relationships between words, phrases, and key persuasive elements
2. Identify the marketing message, tone, style, and any idiomatic expressions or cultural references
3. Translate using appropriate industry terminology, adapting idioms and cultural references for the target market
4. Maintain the original flow and structure while following target language conventions
5. Optimize for SEO in the target language where applicable
6. Ensure the translation resonates with the target audience

## Output
Provide a two-column comparison:

| Original ({{source-language}}) | Translation ({{target-language}}) |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| [original text] | [translated text] |

Followed by:

**Translation Notes:**
- List any significant adaptations, cultural adjustments, or terminology choices
- Explain changes made to idiomatic expressions or culturally-specific references
- Note any SEO optimizations applied
```
