# Digital Detox Schedule Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Digital Detox Schedule Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured weekly disconnection plans to prevent burnout and improve productivity for anyone seeking healthier technology habits. This digital detox prompt for ChatGPT designs day-by-day schedules organized by time blocks, suggesting when to step away from screens and what offline activities to pursue instead. It balances work requirements with meaningful disconnection periods, recommending specific strategies for implementation and accountability mechanisms to maintain consistency. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your occupation, interests, and daily routine into account to build a realistic detox framework that fits your life. Use this prompt when you feel overwhelmed by constant connectivity, need structure around device usage, or want to reclaim time for offline hobbies and relationships. ● Creates day-by-day detox windows that respect occupational technology needs while maximizing offline periods ● Suggests alternative activities aligned with personal interests, from outdoor exercise to creative hobbies ● Provides concrete strategies for overcoming digital distractions and staying accountable to your detox goals ● Outputs a bullet-point schedule organized by weekday and time block for easy implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital wellness expert creating a personalized digital detox plan.

## Task
Design a structured weekly schedule that incorporates regular disconnection periods from digital devices to prevent burnout and enhance productivity. Balance technology use with dedicated offline time, tailored to the individual's situation.

## Context
{{user-profile}}

## Requirements
- Organize the schedule by days of the week and time blocks
- Provide specific strategies for implementing detox periods
- Suggest alternative offline activities aligned with the user's interests
- Include tips for maintaining consistency and overcoming common obstacles
- Account for occupational technology requirements while maximizing meaningful disconnection

## Output
Deliver the plan as a bullet-point list structured by:
- Day of the week
- Time blocks with recommended detox windows
- Specific offline activities and implementation strategies
- Accountability mechanisms
```
