# Tweet Writing Coach

## 簡介

The Tweet Writing Coach is a free AI prompt that transforms content ideas into actionable, structured tweets for social media managers, creators, and marketers. This tweet writing prompt for ChatGPT applies the What-Why-How framework to produce three distinct tweets from any topic you provide. Each tweet opens with a clear principle (7 words or fewer), explains why it matters without restating the obvious, and delivers three concrete implementation steps. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, enforcing quality rules that eliminate jargon and vague advice. Real-world use cases include turning blog concepts into tweetable lessons, repurposing newsletter insights for Twitter, and building consistent content streams that teach rather than tell. Reach for this prompt when you need Twitter content that drives engagement through specificity and immediate value, not inspirational platitudes. ● Enforces the What-Why-How structure for clarity and actionable advice ● Produces three tweet variants per content idea for A/B testing or scheduling ● Eliminates generic statements with built-in word limits and specificity rules ● Outputs plain text ready to paste directly into Twitter or scheduling tools ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write actionable, concise tweets that follow the What-Why-How framework and prioritize specific, implementable advice over generic statements.

## Task

Write 3 tweets about the content idea below. Each tweet must use the What-Why-How structure.

**Content idea:**  
{{content-idea}}

## Structure

- **What** — one clear principle, 7 words or fewer  
- **Why** — why the What matters, 7 words or fewer; do not start with "To"  
- **How** — 3 steps, each 2–5 words, each on its own line starting with a hyphen; make every step concrete and specific

## Quality criteria

1. One focused idea per tweet  
2. Plain language; no jargon  
3. Actionable advice a reader can use immediately  
4. No vague or generic statements — be specific  
5. Why must logically support What, not restate it

## Output

Tweet 1:  
"[What]

[Why]

How:
- [Step 1]
- [Step 2]
- [Step 3]"

Tweet 2:  
"[What]

[Why]

How:
- [Step 1]
- [Step 2]
- [Step 3]"

Tweet 3:  
"[What]

[Why]

How:
- [Step 1]
- [Step 2]
- [Step 3]"
```
