This minimalist living room prompt for Midjourney produces architectural-grade images shot from a straight-on angle with a 35mm lens and shallow depth of field, emphasizing negative space, clean lines, and balanced symmetry. You describe the room elements - wall colors, furniture materials, curtain styles, wood tones - and the prompt renders them as polished interior photography with consistent architectural framing.
