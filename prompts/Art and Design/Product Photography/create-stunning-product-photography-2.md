# Flat Lay Product Photography Prompt for AI

## 簡介

The Flat Lay Product Photography Prompt for AI is a free AI prompt that generates professional centered flat lay compositions for brands and product marketers. This product photography prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces tack-sharp 1:1 close-ups with soft directional lighting, textured surfaces, subtle atmospheric haze, and diffused highlights on packaging. You control the product and brand alongside the theme and mood - whether minimalist and serene, bold and energetic, or vintage and nostalgic - and the prompt builds a cohesive scene with complementary abstract elements, gentle shadows with soft falloff, and a color palette that matches your brand identity. Real use cases include e-commerce hero images, social media content, email campaign visuals, and pitch decks that demand commercial-quality product photography without a studio shoot. Reach for this prompt whenever you need scroll-stopping flat lay imagery that frames your product's character in a clean, aspirational visual tone. ● Outputs perfectly framed 1:1 flat lay shots with tack-sharp focus and soft shadow falloff for Instagram, Amazon listings, and print catalogs. ● Lets you specify any product, brand, theme, and mood to match your campaign or creative brief. ● Produces scenes with textured surfaces, diffused package highlights, and abstract elements that echo your product's identity. ● Delivers clean, aspirational visuals at high resolution commercial quality without studio lighting, props, or a physical set. ## Prompt

```
Centered flat lay close-up of {{product-and-brand}} styled in a {{theme-and-mood}} setup, placed over textured surface with soft directional lighting, subtle atmospheric haze, packaging reflecting diffused highlights, scene designed in a cohesive color palette that matches brand identity, surrounded by complementary abstract elements that visually echo the product's character, perfectly framed for 1:1 composition, tack-sharp focus, gentle shadows with soft falloff, high resolution commercial quality, clean and aspirational visual tone
```
