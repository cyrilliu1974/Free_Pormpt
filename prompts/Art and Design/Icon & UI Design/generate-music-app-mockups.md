# Music Streaming App Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Music Streaming App Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates polished mobile app UI designs for designers, developers, and product teams. The prompt produces a 3:4 aspect ratio front-view mockup featuring a black background, glowing purple accent highlights, fullscreen album art hero section at the top, a floating play button, and a clean tab bar navigation at the bottom. This music app mockup prompt for Midjourney works equally well in Flux, DALL·E, and Nano Banana, generating immersive yet minimal UI aesthetics ready for portfolio presentations, client pitches, or design exploration. Reach for it when you need a contemporary music player interface concept without manually arranging every layer in Figma. ● Produces a front-facing mobile screen mockup in 3:4 portrait format with a dark, glowing purple color scheme. ● Includes fullscreen album art at the top, a floating play button overlay, and a minimal tab bar for navigation. ● Works across Midjourney (with --raw style), Flux, DALL·E, and Nano Banana for consistent UI design output. ● Ideal for app designers prototyping music streaming interfaces or showcasing UI concepts in portfolios. ## Prompt

