# UV Tanline Brand Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The UV Tanline Brand Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyperrealistic beach advertisement imagery with brand text rendered as an authentic sun-tan line effect. This tanline mockup prompt for Midjourney produces a close-up, 50mm top-down shot of a figure lying on a beach towel under harsh midday sunlight, where your brand name appears as lighter, untanned skin with razor-sharp edges - mimicking the effect of sunscreen stencil application. The prompt specifies deep bronze skin with pore-level texture, natural freckles, subtle sunburn redness, and high-contrast tan lines that look photographically real, not overlaid. You control the towel pattern, brand text, and swimwear style through three variables. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other photorealistic image models. Ideal for beauty brands, suncare product campaigns, swimwear labels, and creative agencies pitching bold summer advertising concepts. ● Photorealistic skin texture with pore detail, freckles, sweat sheen, and natural sunburn gradient ● Brand text rendered as crisp, high-contrast UV tanline integrated into the skin, not a graphic overlay ● Customizable beach towel pattern, brand copy, and swimwear style via prompt variables ● Top-down 50mm shallow depth-of-field composition with harsh sunlight and hard shadows for luxury ad aesthetics ## Prompt

```
Top-down close shot, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, extreme sharpness. Young adult female lying face down on a {{towel-pattern}} cotton beach towel, sandy beach, midday harsh direct sunlight at 90 degrees casting hard crisp shadows.

Hyperrealistic render, ultra-high resolution, extreme detail, vibrant summer color palette, luxury beach advertisement mood. Tight crop centered on back.
```
