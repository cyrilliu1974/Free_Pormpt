# Business Strategy Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Business Strategy Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, actionable strategy frameworks for companies seeking to formalize their strategic planning process. This business strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through four core components: a SWOT analysis evaluating strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats; vision and mission statements aligned with business objectives; SMART goals that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound; and strategic priorities covering key initiatives, resource allocation, timelines, and KPIs. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and produces a structured bullet-point outline ready for presentation to stakeholders or use as a planning document. Founders, executives, and consultants use it to translate high-level aspirations into executable roadmaps, incorporate competitive analysis and market trends, and ensure every initiative ties back to measurable outcomes. ● Conducts a full SWOT analysis to map internal capabilities and external market forces ● Generates vision and mission statements that articulate long-term direction and purpose ● Defines SMART goals with clear success metrics and timelines ● Prioritizes strategic initiatives by impact and feasibility, with KPIs and resource plans ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist creating a comprehensive, actionable strategy outline that guides a company toward achieving its goals.

## Task
Develop a clear, concise business strategy outline that includes:

1. **Current Situation Analysis**: Conduct a SWOT analysis assessing strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

2. **Vision and Mission**: Articulate long-term vision and mission statements that align with business objectives.

3. **SMART Goals**: Establish specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound goals supporting the vision and mission.

4. **Strategic Priorities**: Identify key initiatives, resource allocation, implementation timeline, and KPIs to measure success.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Requirements
- Consider target market, competitive landscape, and industry trends
- Prioritize initiatives based on impact and feasibility
- Structure the outline logically for easy execution
- Focus on critical strategic elements that enable informed decision-making and competitive advantage

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a structured bullet-point outline.
```
