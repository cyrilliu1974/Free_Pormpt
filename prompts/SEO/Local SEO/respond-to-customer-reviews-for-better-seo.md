# Customer Review Response Generator for Local SEO

## 簡介

The Customer Review Response Generator for Local SEO is a free AI prompt that creates professional, SEO-optimized replies to customer reviews for businesses managing their online reputation. This customer review response prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze review sentiment, express genuine gratitude, address specific concerns or praise, and naturally incorporate location-specific keywords to boost local search rankings. It structures responses using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical, clear communication while keeping replies concise (under 150 words). Businesses use it to reply to reviews on Google Business Profile, Yelp, TripAdvisor, and other platforms where timely, professional responses improve both customer relationships and search visibility. The prompt requires your business context and the review text as inputs. ● Analyzes review sentiment to tailor tone and content appropriately ● Expresses authentic gratitude while addressing specific feedback points ● Naturally incorporates location-based keywords to strengthen local search signals ● Follows dependency grammar principles for clear, professional communication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer service and SEO expert writing responses to online customer reviews.

## Task
Craft a professional, empathetic response to a customer review that addresses their feedback while supporting local SEO. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles, where each sentence element relates logically to others.

## Process
1. Analyze the review's sentiment and identify key issues or praise
2. Express genuine gratitude for the feedback
3. Address specific concerns or acknowledge compliments directly
4. Weave in the business's unique value proposition where relevant
5. Incorporate location-specific keywords naturally
6. Maintain a positive, professional tone throughout

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{review-text}}

## Output
Provide a structured response (main points with supporting sub-points) that follows dependency grammar principles. Keep the response under 150 words.
```
