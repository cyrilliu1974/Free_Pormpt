# Investor Pitch Deck Builder for Startups

## 簡介

The Investor Pitch Deck Builder for Startups is a free AI prompt that creates structured, investment-ready pitch deck outlines for entrepreneurs seeking funding. This investor pitch deck prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a comprehensive presentation covering eight critical sections: problem statement, solution, market opportunity, competitive landscape, business model, financial projections, team credentials, and funding ask with ROI potential. You provide your business context - including your business idea, target market, unique selling proposition, development stage, and funding requirements - and the prompt generates a complete outline with main sections, detailed bullet points, and recommendations for visual elements like charts and infographics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a logical narrative flow using dependency grammar principles to ensure each section builds naturally toward your investment thesis. Entrepreneurs preparing for seed rounds, Series A fundraising, or accelerator pitches use it to organize their story, validate they have covered investor expectations, and identify gaps in their narrative before designing slides. ● Structures all eight essential pitch deck sections with subsection guidance and visual element recommendations ● Emphasizes quantifiable market data, competitive differentiation, and clear financial milestones investors expect ● Ensures logical information flow from problem to solution, market validation, business model, and funding ask ● Adapts to any business context, from SaaS and consumer apps to hardware, biotech, and service businesses ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist creating a compelling investor pitch deck.

## Task
Craft a persuasive, comprehensive presentation that effectively communicates the value proposition and investment opportunity to potential investors. Develop a structured outline with main sections and bullet points for subsections that ensures logical information flow.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include: the business idea, target market, unique selling proposition, current stage of development, and funding requirements.

## Output
Provide a complete pitch deck outline covering:

**Problem Statement**
- Clearly articulate the market problem or pain point
- Quantify the impact and urgency

**Solution**
- Present the innovative product or approach
- Explain how it addresses the problem effectively

**Market Opportunity**
- Total addressable market size with data-driven insights
- Market growth trends and potential
- Target customer segments

**Competitive Landscape**
- Key competitors analysis
- Competitive advantages and differentiation
- Barriers to entry

**Business Model**
- Revenue streams and pricing strategy
- Unit economics
- Path to profitability

**Financial Projections**
- 3-5 year revenue and expense forecasts
- Key financial metrics and milestones
- Use of funds breakdown

**Team**
- Founder and key team member qualifications
- Relevant expertise and track record
- Advisory board (if applicable)

**Ask & ROI Potential**
- Specific funding amount and use
- Expected milestones and timeline
- Projected return on investment

For each section, include recommendations for visual elements (charts, graphs, infographics) that would strengthen the presentation. Ensure the narrative emphasizes growth potential and investment opportunity throughout.
```
