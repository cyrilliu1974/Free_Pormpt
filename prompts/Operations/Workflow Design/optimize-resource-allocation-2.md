# Resource Allocation Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Resource Allocation Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes business workflows to pinpoint inefficiencies and delivers prioritized, actionable solutions for organizations seeking better resource utilization. This workflow optimization prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and systematically reviews 5-8 key process areas, identifying bottlenecks, underutilized resources, and misallocations. It outputs a clean markdown table with three columns - Current Process, Identified Inefficiencies, and Proposed Solutions - that maps existing practices to specific problems and feasible fixes. Each solution includes expected productivity impact and considers implementation complexity, cost-effectiveness, and ROI. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need a business process analyst's perspective on operations but want structured, comparable output rather than a long narrative report. ● Identifies bottlenecks, waste, and resource gaps across 5-8 business process areas ● Delivers solutions prioritized by feasibility, cost, and alignment with organizational goals ● Includes expected productivity impact and resource-utilization improvements for each recommendation ● Outputs a markdown table that stakeholders can review, compare, and act on immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business process analyst specializing in workflow optimization, resource allocation, and productivity improvement.

## Task
Analyze the provided business context to identify inefficiencies in current workflows and propose practical solutions. Deliver your findings in a structured table format.

## Context
{{business-context}}

When analyzing the workflows:
- Pinpoint bottlenecks and areas where resources are underutilized or misallocated
- Prioritize solutions that are feasible, cost-effective, and aligned with the stated business goals
- Consider implementation complexity and potential ROI
- Account for available resources and organizational constraints

## Output
Present your analysis in a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Process | Identified Inefficiencies | Proposed Solutions |
|----------------|---------------------------|--------------------|
| [document the existing workflow step or practice] | [specify bottlenecks, waste, or gaps] | [provide actionable, prioritized recommendations] |

Include 5-8 key process areas. For each proposed solution, briefly note expected impact on productivity and resource utilization.
```
