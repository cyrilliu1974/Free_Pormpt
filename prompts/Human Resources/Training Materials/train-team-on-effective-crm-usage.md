# CRM Training Program Builder for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The CRM Training Program Builder for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that creates customized training curricula to help sales teams master their CRM platform and improve performance. This CRM training prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your CRM platform (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.), team context, and training duration to generate a complete learning program. It produces progressive modules covering contact management, pipeline tracking, activity logging, reporting, and automation - each with hands-on exercises, industry-specific scenarios, and time allocations. Sales managers, training coordinators, and consultants use it to design onboarding programs, improve CRM adoption rates, and address common resistance to new systems. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Builds progressive learning modules with clear objectives, activities, and assessment checkpoints for each phase ● Includes practical exercises tied to real workflow tasks like pipeline reviews, data hygiene protocols, and follow-up discipline ● Generates industry-relevant scenarios that demonstrate CRM applications in your specific sales context ● Provides implementation roadmaps with milestones, performance metrics, and strategies to overcome adoption obstacles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales training consultant specializing in CRM adoption and sales performance improvement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step training curriculum that teaches a sales team to leverage their CRM platform to streamline processes, strengthen customer relationships, and boost sales performance.

## Context
**CRM Platform:** {{crm-platform}}

**Team & Business Context:** {{team-and-business-context}}
(Include: team size, industry, primary sales challenges, and any relevant process complexity or customer lifecycle details)

**Training Duration:** {{training-duration}}

## Requirements
- Design the curriculum as a progressive learning path with clear phases
- Include hands-on exercises tied to real workflow tasks
- Provide realistic scenarios drawn from common sales situations in the industry
- Cover the CRM's core features: contact management, pipeline tracking, activity logging, reporting, and automation
- Integrate best practices for data hygiene, follow-up discipline, and collaboration
- Define measurable success criteria and performance metrics
- Address common adoption obstacles and change management

## Output
Deliver the training program in a structured format with:
- **Module headings** (e.g., Module 1: CRM Fundamentals, Module 2: Pipeline Management)
- **Session breakdowns** with objectives, activities, and time allocation
- **Practical exercises** for each key feature
- **Real-world scenarios** relevant to the industry and sales challenges
- **Assessment checkpoints** to validate mastery
- **Implementation roadmap** with milestones aligned to the training duration

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout for readability and actionability.
```
