# Irresistible Product Offer Builder

## 簡介

The Irresistible Product Offer Builder is a free AI prompt that generates complete, conversion-focused product offers using Dan Kennedy's proven 7-step copywriting framework for marketers, founders, and sales professionals. This irresistible product offer prompt for ChatGPT walks you through seven sequential sections that move prospects toward purchase: target market identification, pain-point agitation, benefit articulation, social proof integration, urgency creation, tiered pricing presentation, and frictionless calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing emotionally resonant, story-driven sales copy tailored to your specific product, audience, and unique selling proposition. Use it to craft launch offers, funnel pages, sales emails, or proposal packages that systematically address objections and amplify desire. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, psychology-backed offer framework that moves beyond feature lists into emotional transformation and tangible proof. ● Generates seven complete offer sections - target market, pain points, benefits, credibility elements, urgency triggers, three-tier pricing, and step-by-step calls-to-action. ● Applies emotional storytelling and vivid language to surface the cost of inaction and the reward of immediate commitment. ● Structures tiered packages (core, mid-tier upsell, premium) that give prospects choice while anchoring perceived value. ● Embeds social proof placeholders and urgency mechanisms that reinforce credibility and accelerate decision-making. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter and marketing strategist who builds high-converting offers using Dan Kennedy's 7-step framework.

## Task
Create a complete, irresistible offer for the product/service described below. Structure the offer in seven sequential sections that guide the prospect from awareness to action.

## Context
Dan Kennedy's framework walks a prospect through: understanding who the offer is for, recognizing their pain, seeing the benefits, trusting the source, feeling urgency, evaluating the value, and taking the next step. Each section builds momentum toward the purchase decision.

**Product/service:** {{product-service}}  
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}  
**Key benefit and unique selling proposition:** {{benefit-and-usp}}

## Output
Deliver seven clearly labeled sections:

**1. Target Market**  
Describe who this offer is for—their role, situation, and the transformation they want. Make it specific enough that the right person immediately recognizes themselves.

**2. Problem / Pain Points**  
Identify the 2–3 most pressing frustrations or obstacles this audience faces. Agitate each pain point to surface the cost of inaction.

**3. Benefits**  
List the specific, tangible outcomes the product/service delivers. Focus on what changes in the customer's life or business and how they will feel after buying.

**4. Social Proof / Credibility**  
Provide 2–3 testimonials, case studies, credentials, or results that prove the offer works. Each piece of proof should reinforce a benefit from section 3 and feel relatable to the target market.

**5. Reason to Act Now**  
Introduce a limited-time bonus, discount, or extra available only to those who respond immediately. Clarify what they lose by waiting.

**6. Irresistible Offer (Tiered)**  
Present three tiers:  
– **Core:** the foundational product/service with its features, benefits, and any included bonuses.  
– **Mid-tier upsell:** additional features or support that multiply value.  
– **Premium:** the top-tier option with maximum support, extras, or exclusivity.

For each tier, explain what is included and why it represents exceptional value.

**7. Call to Action**  
Tell the prospect exactly what to do next—step by step—to accept the offer. Remove friction, emphasize the positive outcome, and reinforce urgency.

**Tone and formatting:**  
Write as if speaking directly to one ideal customer. Use emotional language, vivid descriptions, and storytelling. Format with line breaks, bullet points, and **bold** or *italic* text for emphasis. Stay conversational, persuasive, and authentic—no hype or false promises.
```
