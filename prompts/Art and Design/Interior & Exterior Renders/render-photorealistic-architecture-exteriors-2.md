# Photorealistic Architecture Exterior Rendering Prompt

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Architecture Exterior Rendering Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces professional-grade architectural visualizations of contemporary civic-commercial buildings with detailed materials, lighting, landscaping, and urban context for architects, urban planners, and developers. This architecture rendering prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates ultra-realistic exterior views of low-to-mid-rise pavilions featuring fluid geometry, floor-to-ceiling glass facades, floating canopy roofs, and open urban plazas with natural pedestrian activity. The prompt allows you to specify building program (retail, café, civic lounge), urban context (streetscape, park edge, waterfront), and time of day to control lighting mood. Output is a 4:5 high-resolution image (2048x2560) shot from eye level with a 28mm architectural lens perspective, physically based rendering with accurate textures, surface imperfections, and minimal color grading. Reach for this prompt when you need portfolio-ready architectural visualizations for client presentations, planning approvals, competition boards, or marketing materials without expensive rendering software or multi-hour 3D modeling workflows. ● Produces cinema-quality exterior views with transparent facades, timber and concrete materials, aluminum accents, integrated skylights, and warm interior glow at dusk or daylight. ● Includes realistic urban plaza elements - seating steps, native landscape planters, mature trees, water features, stone and timber paving, architectural lighting - populated by 10–25 people in natural candid poses. ● Generates physically based rendering with sharp architectural focus, subtle background softness, accurate shadows, atmospheric depth, and no cartoon style, distortion, or oversaturation. ● Customizable variables let you adapt the building program, surrounding urban fabric, and time of day to match your project brief or design intent. ## Prompt
