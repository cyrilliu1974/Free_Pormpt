# SEO Blog Post Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The SEO Blog Post Writer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates fully structured, search-optimized blog articles for content marketers, strategists, and business owners. This blog post prompt for ChatGPT takes your topic, target audience, and key message as inputs and produces a complete article formatted in markdown with headline, introduction, body sections with hierarchical headings, bullet points, and a conclusion with call-to-action. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, guiding the model to research relevant information, structure sentences for logical flow, incorporate keywords naturally, and support claims with examples or statistics. Use it when you need publication-ready blog content that balances reader engagement with search visibility, whether for company blogs, thought leadership, or content marketing campaigns. ● Accepts custom topic, length, target audience, key message, and CTA variables for each article ● Produces markdown-formatted output with headline, introduction, hierarchical headings, and conclusion ● Instructs the model to incorporate SEO keywords naturally while maintaining readability ● Ensures logical content flow, practical examples, and consistent tone throughout ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in digital marketing blog posts that drive engagement and results.

## Task
Write a compelling, SEO-optimized blog article structured for clarity and reader engagement.

## Context
The article should:
- Research and synthesize relevant information from the digital marketing domain
- Structure sentences and paragraphs for logical flow and coherence
- Incorporate relevant keywords naturally for search optimization
- Open with a captivating introduction that hooks readers and establishes purpose
- Organize content with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points
- Support claims with practical examples, case studies, or statistics
- Close with a strong call-to-action that drives the desired reader behavior
- Maintain consistent tone, style, and grammatical accuracy throughout

**Topic & Scope:**
{{topic-and-length}}

**Audience & Positioning:**
{{target-audience}}

**Strategic Goals:**
{{key-message-and-cta}}

## Output
Deliver the complete blog post with:
- Headline
- Introduction
- Body sections with ## headings and ### subheadings
- Bullet points where appropriate
- Conclusion with call-to-action

Format in markdown for maximum readability.
```
