# Research Paper Outline Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Research Paper Outline Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive, logically structured outlines for academic research papers across all disciplines. This research paper outline prompt for ChatGPT walks through every essential component of a scholarly paper: introduction with research question and significance, literature review that identifies gaps, detailed methodology sections covering data collection and analysis techniques, thematic body structure for anticipated findings, conclusion with limitations and future directions, and a bibliography framework. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting a research-context variable that specifies your topic, existing literature, methods, and hypotheses, then returning a hierarchical outline ready to guide your writing process. Graduate students use it to organize dissertation chapters, faculty use it to map journal articles, and undergraduate researchers use it to structure capstone projects. ● Separates introduction, literature review, methodology, findings, and conclusion into discrete, expandable sections ● Prompts identification of literature gaps and alignment of methods with research questions ● Structures the main body thematically or chronologically to support coherent argument flow ● Includes bibliography scaffolding to ensure sources are credible, current, and relevant from the start ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic research expert drafting a comprehensive research paper outline.

## Task
Create a detailed outline that serves as a structured blueprint for a full research paper. The outline must be well-organized, thoroughly grounded in existing scholarship, and designed to produce work that contributes meaningfully to the field.

## Context
Research context: {{research-context}}

The research context should specify:
- The topic and research problem or question
- Key existing research, theories, or literature gaps to address
- The chosen research methods (qualitative, quantitative, mixed)
- Data collection and analysis approach
- Anticipated findings or hypotheses

## Output
Structure the outline with these components:

**Introduction**
- State the research problem or question
- Discuss the significance and relevance of the topic
- Define the objectives and scope of the research

**Literature Review**
- Summarize key existing research and theories
- Identify gaps in current literature this research will address

**Research Methodology**
- Outline research methods to be used
- Describe the data collection process (surveys, interviews, experiments, etc.)
- Explain data analysis techniques (statistical, thematic, etc.)

**Main Body (Discussion of Findings)**
- Organize into thematic or chronological subpoints
- Present anticipated findings and their contribution to the field
- Discuss potential implications and applications

**Conclusion**
- Summarize key findings and their significance
- Discuss study limitations and suggestions for future research

**Bibliography**
- List primary and secondary sources
- Ensure sources are credible, current, and relevant

Format the outline in a logical, coherent manner suitable for guiding the development of a rigorous academic paper.
```
