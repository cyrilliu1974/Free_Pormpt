# Product Pricing Strategy Optimizer

## 簡介

The Product Pricing Strategy Optimizer is a free AI prompt that builds data-driven tiered pricing structures for businesses seeking to maximize both profitability and market penetration. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product's unique value proposition, competitive environment, cost economics, and target market characteristics to generate a multi-tier pricing framework. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured table that maps pricing tiers to specific feature sets and customer segments, complete with strategic rationale. Real use cases include SaaS companies launching subscription models, consumer product brands entering new markets, and B2B services defining package offerings that capture value across different buyer personas. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond cost-plus pricing and align your price points with perceived customer value, competitive positioning, and segment willingness to pay. ● Produces a markdown table mapping pricing tiers to feature sets and target customer segments ● Explains the strategic rationale behind each tier's price point and how tiers work together to capture diverse buyers ● Factors in cost structure, demand elasticity, competitive positioning, and customer willingness to pay ● Balances market penetration goals with sustainable margin requirements across segments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert pricing strategist developing a comprehensive pricing strategy that maximizes profitability and market share.

## Task
Create a tiered pricing structure for the specified product and market. Analyze the product's value proposition, target market characteristics, and competitive landscape, then develop pricing tiers that align with different customer segments and their perceived value.

## Context
**Product & Market:**
{{product-and-market}}
(Include: product name, description, target market segment characteristics, and key customer needs)

**Competitive Environment:**
{{competitive-landscape}}
(Include: main competitors, their pricing approaches, and how your product differentiates)

**Economics:**
{{cost-structure}}
(Include: approximate production costs, cost drivers, and margin requirements)

Consider cost structure, market demand elasticity, competitive positioning, and customer willingness to pay. Each tier should offer a distinct feature set and value proposition that justifies its price point. Balance market penetration objectives with long-term profitability.

## Output
Deliver your pricing strategy as a markdown table with three columns: **Pricing Tier**, **Features**, **Target Customer**.

After the table, provide a concise rationale explaining:
- Why each tier is priced at its level
- How the tiers work together to capture different segments
- How the strategy positions against competitors
- Expected impact on market share and profitability
```
