# Grant Application Document Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Grant Application Document Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates organized, complete document lists for grant applicants preparing funding submissions. This grant application document checklist prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific grant details and produces a categorized list of every required document - from organizational credentials and financial records to project materials and compliance forms. Each entry explains why the document matters, offers actionable preparation tips, and flags common quality standards or pitfalls that lead to rejections. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its guidance to grant type, organizational capacity, field-specific requirements, and jurisdictional regulations. Whether you're applying for nonprofit funding, research grants, government awards, or foundation support, this tool ensures nothing falls through the cracks during the document-gathering phase. It is designed for grant writers, nonprofit administrators, research teams, and anyone managing a competitive funding application who needs to avoid incomplete submissions. ● Groups documents by function - organizational credentials, financial records, project plans, and compliance items - so applicants can tackle preparation systematically. ● Explains the purpose and importance of each document within the application, helping teams prioritize effort and understand reviewer expectations. ● Provides actionable tips for obtaining or creating each document efficiently, including quality standards and common mistakes to avoid. ● Tailors the checklist to the specific grant type, applicant experience level, field regulations, and jurisdictional requirements described in your input. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant application specialist helping applicants prepare complete, competitive submissions.

## Task
Create a comprehensive checklist of required documents for the grant application described below. For each document:
- Explain its purpose and importance in the application
- Provide actionable tips on how to obtain or prepare it efficiently
- Note any common pitfalls or quality standards

Organize the checklist by grouping documents according to their function (e.g., organizational credentials, financial records, project materials, compliance documents).

## Context
Grant and applicant details:
{{grant-details}}

Tailor the document list and guidance to the specific grant type, organizational capacity, field requirements, and jurisdictional regulations relevant to this application. Adjust complexity and explanations based on the applicant's prior grant experience.

## Output
Deliver the document checklist as a bullet-point list, with clear category headings. Ensure completeness—missing documents are a leading cause of rejection.
```
