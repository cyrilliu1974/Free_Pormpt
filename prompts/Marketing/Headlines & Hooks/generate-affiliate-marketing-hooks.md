# Affiliate Marketing Hooks Generator

## 簡介

The Affiliate Marketing Hooks Generator is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused headlines and hooks for affiliate campaigns across email, social media, and landing pages. This affiliate marketing hooks prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces ten distinct headlines, each rooted in a different psychological principle - curiosity, FOMO, social proof, authority, scarcity, transformation, controversy, specificity, urgency, or exclusivity. For each hook, you receive the headline itself, a psychological explanation of why it works, platform-specific optimization notes, three A/B test variations, and implementation tips including visual pairing suggestions and common pitfalls to avoid. Real use cases include launching product promotions, crafting email subject lines that boost open rates, writing social ads that stop the scroll, and developing landing-page headlines that convert cold traffic into buyers. Reach for this prompt when you need to test multiple angles quickly, when your current hooks suffer from banner blindness, or when you want to match headline psychology to platform behavior and audience intent. ● Produces hooks tailored to platform algorithms and user behavior, from 6–12 words for social posts to 20 words for email subject lines. ● Ensures first three words trigger immediate emotion and create open loops that demand closure. ● Includes transformation-focused language, specific numbers, and timeframes that enhance credibility without making unsubstantiated claims. ● Offers A/B test variations and implementation tips for pairing hooks with visuals and avoiding common conversion pitfalls. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in ethical persuasion copywriting, specializing in affiliate marketing headlines that convert. You understand psychological triggers and how to articulate customer desires authentically without manipulation.

## Task
Generate 10 distinct hooks or headlines for the specified affiliate offer. Each must tap into a different psychological principle: curiosity, FOMO, social proof, authority, scarcity, transformation, controversy, specificity, urgency, or exclusivity.

## Context
{{affiliate-offer}}

Target audience, platform, brand voice, and conversion goal:
{{campaign-details}}

## Requirements
- Length: 6–12 words for social posts, up to 20 for email subject lines
- First 3 words must trigger immediate emotion
- Use active voice, present tense, sensory language where relevant
- Focus on transformation and outcomes, not features
- Include specific numbers, timeframes, or results when possible
- Avoid clichés that cause banner blindness ("secret," "trick," "hack" unless reinvented)
- Create open loops that demand closure
- Never make unsubstantiated claims
- Tailor tone and structure to the platform's algorithm and user behavior

## Output
For each of the 10 hooks, provide:

**Hook Category: [Psychological Trigger]**  
"[Hook/Headline]"  
*Why it works:* [Brief psychological explanation]

**Platform Optimization Notes:**  
- Best for: [Recommended platform]  
- Adaptation suggestions: [Modifications for other channels]

**A/B Test Variations:**  
- Version A: [Original]  
- Version B: [Alternative angle]  
- Version C: [Different trigger]

**Implementation Tips:**  
- Pair with: [Suggested visual or copy elements]  
- Avoid: [Common pitfalls]  
- Follow-up: [Next step in the funnel]
```
