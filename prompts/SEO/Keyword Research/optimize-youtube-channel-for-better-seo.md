# YouTube Channel SEO Optimization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The YouTube Channel SEO Optimization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that delivers a complete, actionable SEO strategy for YouTube creators seeking better search rankings and audience reach. This YouTube SEO prompt for ChatGPT guides you through four critical pillars: keyword research to uncover high-value search terms in your niche, video metadata optimization that refines titles, descriptions, and tags using dependency grammar principles for semantic clarity, thumbnail strategy recommendations based on color contrast and click-through psychology, and performance analysis frameworks to track impressions, watch time, and traffic sources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its recommendations to your specific channel context. Content creators use it to audit existing channels, launch new ones with SEO-first workflows, or diagnose why videos aren't surfacing in search and suggested feeds. ● Identifies primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities aligned with audience intent and content themes ● Applies dependency grammar techniques to video descriptions for improved readability and search-engine parsing ● Provides thumbnail design principles addressing contrast, text legibility, focal points, and brand consistency ● Outlines metrics-tracking methods and iteration workflows based on click-through rate, watch time, and traffic-source data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert YouTube SEO strategist optimizing channels for discoverability, search rankings, and audience engagement.

## Task
Deliver a comprehensive, step-by-step YouTube SEO optimization plan covering:

1. **Keyword Research** – Identify high-value search terms and phrases aligned with the channel niche, audience intent, and content themes. Include primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities.

2. **Video Metadata Optimization** – Refine titles, descriptions, and tags using researched keywords. Structure titles for both search algorithms and human click-through. Write descriptions using dependency grammar principles (establish clear subject-verb-object relationships, anchor modifiers close to their referents) to improve readability and semantic clarity for both viewers and search engines.

3. **Thumbnail Strategy** – Recommend design principles for visually distinctive thumbnails that increase click-through rates in search results and suggested video feeds. Address color contrast, text legibility, facial expressions or focal points, and brand consistency.

4. **Performance Analysis & Iteration** – Outline methods to track key metrics (impressions, click-through rate, watch time, traffic sources) and adjust the SEO strategy based on data patterns.

## Context
{{channel-context}}

## Output
Present your recommendations as a numbered list (one per major step above). Use bullet points under each number for detailed explanations, specific techniques, and actionable recommendations tailored to the provided channel context.
```
