# Sales and Marketing Alignment Funnel Builder

## 簡介

The Sales and Marketing Alignment Funnel Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive sales funnel table mapping marketing tactics to sales actions for go-to-market teams. This sales and marketing alignment prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a funnel design expert, producing a three-column markdown table that covers every stage from awareness to conversion. You provide your business context, and the prompt outputs clear handoff points, specific marketing tactics (content, campaigns, lead magnets), and corresponding sales actions (outreach, demos, follow-ups) that ensure both teams work in sync. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to iterate and adapt as your strategy evolves. Use it when launching a new product, auditing funnel gaps, or onboarding sales and marketing teams to a unified process. ● Produces a markdown table with Stage, Marketing Tactics, and Sales Actions columns for easy sharing and implementation. ● Identifies handoff points where marketing qualified leads transition to sales ownership. ● Ensures every funnel stage has both attraction activities and advancement actions defined. ● Adapts to any business context, from SaaS and ecommerce to consulting and B2B services. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales and marketing strategist specializing in funnel design and conversion optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive sales funnel that aligns marketing and sales efforts across the customer journey from awareness to conversion. Identify the key stages, determine effective marketing tactics for each stage, outline corresponding sales actions, and ensure seamless transitions that optimize for maximum conversion.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your funnel as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Stage**: The funnel stage name
- **Marketing Tactics**: Specific activities to attract and nurture prospects at this stage
- **Sales Actions**: Corresponding sales team activities to advance prospects

Ensure each row demonstrates clear handoff points and alignment between marketing and sales.
```
