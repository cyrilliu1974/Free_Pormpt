# Hyper-Local SEO Content Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Hyper-Local SEO Content Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds a customized content plan to improve local search rankings and community engagement for businesses serving specific geographic markets. This hyper-local SEO prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business type, target audience, and local market to produce a structured content strategy. It delivers blog post ideas, area-specific landing pages, local news update schedules, reusable content templates, distribution tactics, and performance metrics - all tailored to your community. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible regardless of your preferred text model. Real-world use cases include independent retailers building neighborhood authority, service providers targeting multiple zip codes, and franchise locations differentiating themselves through local content. This prompt is built for local business owners, marketing managers, and SEO consultants who need a practical roadmap for creating content that resonates with a specific geographic audience and ranks in local search results. ● Produces blog post ideas that address the specific interests and pain points of your local audience ● Outlines area-specific landing pages optimized for neighborhood and district search queries ● Provides content templates and local news update schedules to maintain consistent publishing ● Includes promotion strategies and performance metrics tailored to your market and business type ## Prompt

