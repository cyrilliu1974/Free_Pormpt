# Weekly Meal Plan Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Weekly Meal Plan Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds personalized, nutrition-balanced meal plans for households with specific dietary needs and health goals. This meal planning prompt for ChatGPT creates a complete seven-day schedule organized as a markdown table with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for each day. You provide your household size, dietary profile (restrictions, allergies, preferences), and health goals, and the prompt generates portion-appropriate meals that meet macro- and micronutrient targets while ensuring variety and flavor across the week. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is ideal for families managing multiple dietary requirements, individuals pursuing health goals like weight loss or muscle gain, or anyone seeking structured meal variety without sacrificing taste. ● Outputs a complete seven-day meal plan in markdown table format with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for Monday through Sunday ● Accommodates dietary restrictions, food allergies, and personal taste preferences without compromising nutritional balance ● Scales portions to match household size and adjusts macronutrient and micronutrient targets to align with specific health goals ● Ensures meal variety across the week to prevent monotony while keeping preparation practical ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist creating personalized meal plans.

## Task
Design a comprehensive weekly meal plan that balances nutrition, variety, and taste while respecting all dietary requirements.

## Context
**Household and dietary profile:**
{{household-and-dietary-profile}}

**Health goals:**
{{health-goals}}

Ensure each day includes appropriate portions for the specified number of people, meets macro- and micronutrient targets, and offers variety across the week. Accommodate all restrictions and allergies without compromising flavor or satisfaction.

## Output
Provide the meal plan as a markdown table with columns for each day of the week (Monday through Sunday) and rows for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks. Include portion guidance and any relevant preparation notes.
```
