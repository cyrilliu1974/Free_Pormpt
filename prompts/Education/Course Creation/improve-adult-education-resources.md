# Adult Education Resource Improvement Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Adult Education Resource Improvement Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps education consultants, administrators, and program designers develop actionable strategies to enhance lifelong learning opportunities. This adult education prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a systematic analysis of your current resources, identifies gaps in accessibility and programming, and generates prioritized solutions that fit your budget and local context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured three-part plan that catalogs existing assets, pinpoints underserved learner segments and barriers, and recommends specific strategies that account for technology integration, diverse learning styles, and implementation constraints. Use it when launching new adult education initiatives, auditing existing programs, or writing grant proposals that require detailed needs assessments. ● Catalogs existing educational assets and their current utilization rates to establish a baseline ● Identifies accessibility barriers, underserved audiences, and unmet learning needs in your local context ● Proposes specific, budget-aware solutions prioritized by impact and feasibility ● Structures output as a ready-to-present plan with distinct sections for resources, gaps, and solutions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert education consultant specializing in adult and lifelong learning program design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan to improve adult education resources for the specified context. Analyze the current landscape, identify gaps, and propose innovative, implementable solutions that enhance learning opportunities.

## Context
**Subject area:** {{subject-area}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Local education landscape, available resources, and budget constraints:** {{local-context}}

Consider accessibility, technology integration, diverse learning styles, and practical constraints when crafting recommendations. Solutions must be actionable and appropriate for the stated budget and resources.

## Output
Deliver your plan as a structured bullet-point list organized under three headings:

**Available Resources**
- Catalog existing assets and their current utilization

**Gaps in Current Offerings**
- Identify unmet needs, accessibility barriers, and underserved learner segments

**Potential Solutions**
- Propose specific, prioritized strategies to address each gap with implementation considerations
```
