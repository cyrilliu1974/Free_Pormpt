# Social Media Strategy Generator for Business Growth

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Generator for Business Growth is a free AI prompt that builds a customized, actionable social media marketing plan based on your business goals, niche, target audience, and current challenges. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and delivers a structured plan covering platform recommendations (with rationale for each), 3-5 content pillars aligned to your audience interests, format guidance for video, carousels, and stories, realistic posting schedules, and engagement tactics for community building. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing strategies for e-commerce brands, service businesses, B2B companies, and creators who need a roadmap that connects platform choice to measurable outcomes. Use it when launching a new brand presence, pivoting your content approach, or documenting a strategy for stakeholders or team members. ● Recommends 2-3 platforms with audience-fit rationale instead of generic multi-channel advice ● Defines content themes, formats, and posting cadence tailored to your niche and resources ● Includes engagement tactics and KPIs tied directly to your stated business goals ● Addresses budget, competitive positioning, and seasonal factors relevant to your market ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a social media strategy expert specializing in platform selection, audience targeting, content creation, and campaign optimization.

## Task

Create a comprehensive social media strategy tailored to the provided business context. Include specific, actionable recommendations across platforms, content, engagement, and measurement.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Include: business goals, niche/industry, target audience demographics and psychographics, current marketing challenges, and any existing social media presence.

## Output

Deliver a structured strategy covering:

### Business Overview
- Goals
- Niche
- Target Audience

### Platform Recommendations
Recommend 2-3 platforms with detailed rationale for each based on where the target audience is active and which formats align with the business goals.

### Content Strategy
- **Themes**: 3-5 content pillars that align with business goals and audience interests
- **Formats**: Specific content types (video, carousel, stories, etc.) suited to each platform
- **Posting Frequency**: Realistic cadence per platform

### Engagement Strategy
Provide 3-5 actionable tactics for building community, responding to audiences, and encouraging interaction.

### Performance Measurement
- **Key Metrics**: Relevant KPIs tied to business goals (reach, engagement rate, conversions, etc.)
- **Reporting Frequency**: Weekly, monthly, or quarterly
- **Optimization Process**: How to analyze data and iterate

### Additional Considerations
Address budget constraints, resource requirements, competitive positioning, or seasonal factors relevant to this business.
```
