# Team Collaboration Incentive Program Builder

## 簡介

The Team Collaboration Incentive Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates custom sales incentive programs aligned to specific metrics, budgets, and company culture for HR professionals and sales leaders. This team collaboration incentive prompt for ChatGPT takes your sales department profile, target metrics, cultural values, and budget constraints, then generates a structured markdown table mapping innovative incentive ideas to concrete implementation steps and expected outcomes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, drawing on human resources best practices and motivational psychology to design programs that balance individual achievement with collaborative team dynamics. Use it when launching a new sales initiative, refreshing underperforming incentive structures, or aligning compensation philosophy with strategic growth targets. ● Produces a cohesive table format showing how each incentive idea translates into specific actions and predicted performance gains ● Accounts for budget realities, existing company culture, and the motivational factors that drive your particular sales team ● Balances competitive individual rewards with collaborative team-oriented recognition to prevent siloed behavior ● Integrates industry standards from HR consulting to ensure sustainable, legally sound incentive structures ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources consultant specializing in team motivation and performance improvement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive team collaboration incentive program designed to boost specific sales metrics. Create a detailed plan that includes innovative incentive ideas, clear implementation steps, and projected outcomes.

## Context
**Sales department:** {{sales-department}}

**Target metrics:** {{target-metrics}}

**Company culture:** {{company-culture}}

**Budget constraints:** {{budget-constraints}}

Consider various motivational factors, team dynamics, and industry best practices to design an effective program that aligns with the sales department's goals and overall company objectives.

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns: **Incentive Ideas** | **Implementation Steps** | **Expected Outcomes**. Each row should provide a cohesive strategy from idea to outcome, showing how the incentive will be executed and what results it should produce.
```
