# Email Campaign Ideas Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Email Campaign Ideas Generator for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email marketing campaign strategies tailored to any business context. This email campaign prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured seven-part strategy covering subject line concepts, content themes, call-to-action approaches, design and layout suggestions, personalization and segmentation techniques, timing and frequency recommendations, and A/B testing roadmaps. You provide your business context as a variable, and the prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver actionable campaign blueprints that address open rates, click-through rates, and conversions. Email marketers use it to quickly develop complete campaign frameworks for product launches, seasonal promotions, nurture sequences, and customer retention initiatives. This prompt is for email marketing strategists, growth marketers, and business owners who need end-to-end campaign planning without starting from scratch. ● Produces 5-7 optimized subject line concepts designed to improve open rates ● Suggests 3-5 content themes aligned with business goals and audience interests ● Recommends specific CTA wording, placement tactics, and urgency strategies to drive clicks ● Outlines personalization and segmentation approaches to increase message relevance ● Identifies 3-4 testable campaign elements with clear hypotheses and success metrics ● Delivers timing, frequency, and design recommendations based on your business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist generating campaign ideas that boost open rates, click-through rates, and conversions.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive email marketing campaign strategy tailored to the business context provided. Cover all elements needed to execute high-performing campaigns:

1. **Subject Line Concepts** – Generate 5-7 attention-grabbing subject lines optimized for open rates
2. **Content Themes** – Propose 3-5 campaign content ideas that align with the business goals and resonate with the target audience
3. **Call-to-Action Strategy** – Recommend specific CTA approaches (wording, placement, urgency tactics) to maximize click-through
4. **Design & Layout** – Suggest visual structures, imagery styles, and layout innovations to enhance engagement
5. **Personalization & Segmentation** – Outline techniques to increase relevance through dynamic content and audience segmentation
6. **Timing & Frequency** – Advise on optimal send schedules and campaign cadence
7. **A/B Testing Roadmap** – Identify 3-4 testable elements with hypothesis and success metrics

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Present your strategy in a structured format with main ideas as headings (use **bold**) and supporting details as bullet points. Ensure recommendations are specific, actionable, and directly tied to the business context provided.
```
