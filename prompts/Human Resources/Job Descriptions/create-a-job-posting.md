# Job Posting Generator for Hiring and Recruitment

## 簡介

The Job Posting Generator for Hiring and Recruitment is a free AI prompt that creates professional, compelling job postings for companies looking to attract top talent. This job posting prompt for ChatGPT takes your company name, position details, and contact information, then generates a complete, structured job advertisement across eight essential sections: a tabular summary of job details (title, department, location, employment type, experience level, salary range), an engaging role overview, key responsibilities, required and preferred qualifications, benefits and perks, company culture description, and clear application instructions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible for HR teams, recruiters, startup founders, and hiring managers who need to create professional job listings quickly. The prompt follows recruitment best practices by balancing informative content with engaging language that resonates with highly qualified candidates while avoiding jargon or generic phrasing. ● Outputs a formatted job posting with tabular details, responsibilities, qualifications, benefits, culture, and application steps ● Tailors language to attract highly qualified candidates while maintaining readability and visual appeal ● Separates required qualifications from preferred ones, helping candidates self-assess fit ● Includes sections on company culture and benefits to showcase employer brand and work environment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert job posting writer with deep knowledge of effective hiring strategies and techniques for attracting top talent.

## Task
Craft a detailed, compelling job posting that will stand out to highly qualified candidates. Structure the posting to be informative, engaging, and aligned with recruitment best practices.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Position details: {{job-description}}
Contact information: {{contact-details}}

## Output
Format the job posting with the following sections:

### Job Details
Create a table with these columns:
- Job Title
- Department
- Location
- Employment Type
- Experience Level
- Salary Range

### Role Overview
Provide a brief, engaging summary of the role and its significance within the organization.

### Key Responsibilities
List the main responsibilities and tasks using bullet points.

### Required Qualifications
List the essential qualifications and skills using bullet points.

### Preferred Qualifications
List additional advantageous qualifications or skills using bullet points.

### Benefits & Perks
Highlight attractive company benefits and perks using bullet points.

### Company Culture
Describe the company culture, values, and work environment.

### How to Apply
Provide clear application instructions, required documents, and submission deadline.

**Guidelines:**
- Tailor content to attract highly qualified candidates
- Avoid overly technical jargon or generic language
- Focus on key responsibilities, qualifications, and benefits
- Ensure readability and visual appeal throughout
```
