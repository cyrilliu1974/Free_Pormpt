# Landscape Photography Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Landscape Photography Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic 8K landscape photographs featuring grassy fields, individual trees, and clear blue skies in golden hour lighting for photographers, designers, and content creators. This landscape photography prompt for Midjourney produces wide-angle shots at 16:9 aspect ratio with customizable field colors, tree species, and landscape types. It works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to create professional-grade nature scenes with authentic golden hour warmth and depth. The prompt is designed for projects requiring natural outdoor imagery, from website headers and presentation backgrounds to portfolio pieces and social media content. Reach for this prompt when you need photorealistic landscape images with consistent lighting and composition without location scouting or waiting for perfect weather conditions. ● Specify field color (green, golden, amber) and tree type (oak, pine, willow) to match your creative vision ● Outputs 8K resolution images in landscape photography style with golden hour lighting automatically applied ● Wide-angle composition at 16:9 aspect ratio ideal for web banners, presentations, and widescreen displays ● Clear blue sky and single-tree focal point create clean, uncluttered compositions that work across design contexts ## Prompt
