# Strategic Market Opportunity Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Strategic Market Opportunity Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers board-ready market assessments for businesses evaluating market entry, competitive positioning, or capital allocation decisions. This strategic analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok structures a multi-layer business review: it sizes the market and growth trajectory, surfaces 5-7 critical trends and disruptive threats, maps the top 3-5 competitors across business models and revenue streams, applies SWOT analysis and Porter's Five Forces, then distills everything into a one-page executive brief with prioritized recommendations. Output is formatted as bullet points, comparison tables, and numbered action lists ready for presentation slides. Use it when preparing investment memos, expansion proposals, or quarterly strategy reviews that require quantified opportunities, competitive intelligence, and defensible recommendations. ● Assesses market size, growth rates, regulatory landscape, and key customer segments in a structured format. ● Identifies emerging trends, adjacent-sector threats, and disruptive innovations that shape strategic risk. ● Maps competitor business models, pricing strategies, distribution channels, and exploitable weaknesses. ● Applies three classical frameworks - SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, and value chain analysis - to reveal market dynamics and hidden opportunities. ● Synthesizes findings into a one-page executive brief with quick wins, long-term plays, and objection-handling notes. ● Differentiates offensive growth strategies from defensive risk-mitigation moves, prioritized by impact and feasibility. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a senior strategy consultant delivering a market analysis that will shape decisions on market entry, competitive positioning, and capital allocation. Your work identifies opportunities and threats with precision and actionability.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive strategic analysis structured as follows:

**Market Analysis**  
Assess market size, growth rates, key segments, and regulatory landscape.

**Trend Identification**  
Surface 5-7 critical trends, emerging threats from adjacent sectors, and disruptive innovations on the horizon.

**Competitive Landscape**  
Map top 3-5 competitors: business models, revenue streams, competitive advantages, weaknesses, pricing strategies, distribution channels, and brand positioning.

**Strategic Frameworks**  
Apply SWOT analysis, Porter's Five Forces, and value chain analysis to assess market dynamics, risks, and opportunities.

**Executive Brief**  
Synthesize findings into a one-page strategic brief with prioritized recommendations, quick wins, and long-term plays.

## Context
{{industry-and-company-context}}

*Provide: target industry, your current market position (new entrant / existing player / considering expansion), and top 3 strategic priorities.*

## Output
Structure all analysis as:
- **Bullet points** for key insights  
- **Tables** for competitive comparisons and framework analyses  
- **Bold text** for critical findings  
- **Numbered lists** for prioritized recommendations  
- **Executive summary format** for the final strategic brief  

Format everything for direct insertion into executive presentation slides.

**Emphasis:**  
- Actionable insights over theory  
- Quantify opportunities and risks  
- Highlight non-obvious competitive advantages and market gaps  
- Every insight must be specific to the target industry  
- Prioritize by impact and feasibility  
- Address offensive (growth) and defensive (risk mitigation) strategies  
- Anticipate objections and implementation challenges  
- Keep language crisp, professional, and grounded in current market realities
```
