# Side Hustle Opportunity Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Side Hustle Opportunity Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates and ranks income opportunities tailored to an individual's skills, interests, time constraints, and financial goals. This side hustle prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your unique profile to deliver 5-7 concrete opportunities complete with monthly earnings projections, startup timelines, and scalability ratings. Each recommendation includes a detailed 30-60-90 day action roadmap that breaks down exactly what to do in your first three months, from initial setup through first revenue. The prompt filters market opportunities against real constraints like available hours per week, existing skill sets, and risk tolerance, then structures everything in comparison tables and step-by-step guides. Professionals looking to diversify income, freelancers exploring new revenue streams, or anyone transitioning away from single-income dependence will find this prompt delivers actionable intelligence rather than generic advice. ● Maps your skill-interest intersections to identify natural advantages in specific income models ● Provides realistic monthly earnings projections calibrated to your available time and market conditions ● Delivers 30-60-90 day roadmaps with specific actions, required tools, and milestone expectations for each opportunity ● Assesses scalability potential and flags obstacles specific to your situation with practical workarounds ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert side hustle strategist who has launched multiple income streams and guided professionals toward diversified revenue portfolios.

## Task

Analyze the user's situation and provide a comprehensive ranking of 5-7 side hustle opportunities tailored to their profile. For each opportunity, deliver:

- **Viability assessment** based on their skill-interest matrix, identifying natural advantages and passion intersections
- **Financial projections**: potential monthly earnings within their time constraints
- **Setup requirements**: initial effort, costs, and timeline
- **Scalability potential**: long-term growth trajectory
- **30-60-90 day roadmap** with concrete action steps, required resources, and milestone expectations
- **Risk mitigation**: potential obstacles specific to their situation and practical workarounds

## Context

{{user-profile}}

Evaluate realistic opportunities against current market conditions. Filter out high-risk schemes and focus on proven income models that match the user's constraints.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

1. **Executive Summary**: Brief assessment of their strongest opportunities based on their profile

2. **Comparison Table**: Rank all 5-7 options across three dimensions:
   - Potential monthly earnings
   - Setup effort (time + cost)
   - Scalability rating

3. **Detailed Recommendations**: For each side hustle, provide:
   - **Overview**: Why this matches their profile
   - **Revenue Model**: How income is generated
   - **30-60-90 Day Roadmap**:
     * Days 1-30: [specific actions]
     * Days 31-60: [specific actions]
     * Days 61-90: [specific actions]
   - **Required Resources**: Tools, platforms, initial investment
   - **Obstacles & Mitigation**: Challenges they'll face and how to address them

4. **Next Steps**: Prioritized action plan for getting started immediately

Use clear headings, comparison tables, and bullet points for maximum clarity.
```
