# Business Update Email Template Generator

## 簡介

The Business Update Email Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured announcement emails for communicating changes, launches, and developments to stakeholders. This update email prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your update type, target audience, desired outcome, business context, and key message to produce a complete email framework. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering attention-grabbing subject lines, concise opening summaries, detailed body sections covering background and impact, and clear calls-to-action. Marketing teams use it to announce product launches, HR departments deploy it for policy changes, and project managers rely on it to communicate timeline shifts while maintaining a consistent professional voice across all stakeholder communications. Reach for this prompt when you need to announce organizational changes, product updates, policy revisions, or any development that requires clear stakeholder notification with built-in customization points. ● Produces complete email templates with subject lines, opening summaries, detailed body sections, and closing CTAs ● Includes [CUSTOMIZABLE] markers throughout to guide personalization for different scenarios ● Structures content for scannability with brief paragraphs and logical information flow ● Balances background context, impact explanation, and next-step clarity in professional business tone ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert communications strategist specializing in stakeholder update announcements.

## Task
Create a polished, customizable email template that effectively communicates {{update-type}} to {{target-audience}}. The email must be clear, engaging, and drive toward {{desired-outcome}}.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Key message to convey: {{key-message}}

The template should work across multiple update scenarios while maintaining a consistent professional tone. Stakeholders need to quickly grasp what changed, why it matters, and what happens next.

## Output
Deliver a complete email template with:

**Subject Line:** Attention-grabbing, specific to the update type

**Opening Paragraph:** Concise summary that states the key update upfront

**Body Sections:** 
- Background/context for the update
- Detailed explanation of changes or developments
- Impact on the audience
- Timeline or implementation details (if applicable)

**Closing:** Clear call-to-action or next steps

**Signature Block:** Placeholder for sender details

Format each section with [CUSTOMIZABLE] markers where content should be tailored. Use professional business tone with active voice. Keep paragraphs brief for scannability.
```
