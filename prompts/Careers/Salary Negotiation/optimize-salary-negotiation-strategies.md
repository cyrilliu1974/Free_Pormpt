# Salary Negotiation Guide Builder for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Salary Negotiation Guide Builder for Any Profession is a free AI prompt that creates tailored compensation negotiation strategies for professionals in any field. This salary negotiation prompt for ChatGPT takes your profession as input and produces a complete negotiation guide that includes industry-specific salary ranges, standard benefits packages, concrete negotiation scripts for opening, counter-offering, and closing, plus a scenario-response table for common situations. It delivers research-backed tactics calibrated to your field's compensation norms, hiring cycles, and workplace culture. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, the prompt produces actionable dos and don'ts, insider intelligence unique to your industry, and proper source citations to back every recommendation. Use it when preparing for a job offer discussion, annual review, or promotion conversation where compensation is on the table. ● Delivers industry overview with current trends, typical salary ranges, and standard benefits packages for your profession ● Provides three concrete negotiation scripts - opening, counter-offer, and closing - worded for real conversations ● Lists profession-specific dos and don'ts to avoid common pitfalls and strengthen your bargaining position ● Includes a scenario-response table mapping common negotiation situations to optimal replies ## Prompt
