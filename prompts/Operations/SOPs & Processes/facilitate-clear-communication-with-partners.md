# Partnership Communication Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Partnership Communication Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured communication plans to enhance collaboration and achieve mutual goals with business partners. This partnership communication prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete strategy that maps communication frequency, preferred channels, and messaging content to each stage of your sales process and partnership lifecycle. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a documented plan with clear guidelines, phase-specific timing recommendations, channel-to-purpose mapping, and pre-identified challenges with mitigation strategies. Use it when onboarding new partners, formalizing collaboration frameworks, or documenting communication standards for joint ventures, reseller relationships, or co-marketing initiatives. ● Maps communication frequency and channel selection to partnership phases and sales process stages ● Identifies potential communication breakdowns and provides specific mitigation strategies ● Delivers structured guidelines for what to communicate, when, and through which channels ● Produces implementation-ready action steps tied to partnership duration and goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert communication strategist specializing in partnership management.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive communication strategy that enhances collaboration and achieves mutual goals with partners. Structure the plan with clear guidelines for communication frequency, channels, and content. Identify potential challenges and provide actionable solutions.

## Context
Partners: {{partners}}

Sales process: {{sales-process}}

Communication goals: {{communication-goals}}

Preferred channels: {{preferred-channels}}

Partnership duration: {{partnership-duration}}

## Approach
Structure the strategy to show clear relationships between different communication elements. Address timing, methods, and messaging that align with the sales process stages and partner needs.

## Output
Deliver the communication strategy with:
- Clear headings and subheadings
- Bullet points for each guideline and recommendation
- Specific frequency recommendations tied to partnership phases
- Channel usage mapped to communication type
- Anticipated challenges with corresponding mitigation strategies
- Actionable next steps for implementation
```
