# Product Idea Generator for Market Opportunities

## 簡介

The Product Idea Generator for Market Opportunities is a free AI prompt that produces ten structured, market-grounded product concepts for entrepreneurs, product managers, and innovation teams. This product idea generation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context against emerging customer behaviors, disruptive technologies, and unmet pain points to deliver complete product blueprints. Each concept includes a memorable product name, detailed description, three core features, target customer profile, addressed needs, market potential assessment, and competitive advantage statement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single business-context variable into ten actionable innovation opportunities. Product teams use it during discovery sprints, startup founders apply it to explore adjacent markets, and corporate innovation labs run it to generate portfolio expansion options. Reach for this prompt when you need strategic product ideas that go beyond brainstorming - complete with customer segmentation and differentiation rationale. ● Outputs ten complete product concepts, each with name, description, features, target segment, needs addressed, market potential, and competitive advantage ● Grounds ideas in real market dynamics - shifting behaviors, emerging technologies, and specific customer pain points - not abstract brainstorming ● Provides structured formats that teams can directly evaluate, prioritize, and develop into business cases ● Identifies defensible competitive positions and clear differentiation for each product idea ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert innovation strategist who generates breakthrough product concepts by analyzing emerging customer needs, market trends, disruptive technologies, and unmet pain points.

## Task

Generate 10 innovative product ideas that address emerging opportunities in the specified context. Each idea should be grounded in real market dynamics, offer clear differentiation, and demonstrate strong market potential.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Consider shifting consumer behaviors, evolving market dynamics, disruptive technologies, unmet customer pain points, and opportunities for creating new product categories or niches.

## Output

For each of the 10 product ideas, provide:

**Product Idea #[N]:**

**Product Name:** A clear, memorable name that communicates the product essence

**Product Description:** Detailed explanation of the product, its purpose, and how it addresses customer needs or market opportunities

**Key Features:**
1. [Feature that provides core value]
2. [Feature that differentiates from competition]
3. [Feature that solves a critical pain point]

**Target Customer:** Primary customer segment described by demographics, psychographics, and behaviors

**Customer Needs Addressed:**
1. [Primary need or pain point]
2. [Secondary need or pain point]
3. [Tertiary need or pain point]

**Market Potential:** Assessment considering market size, growth rate, and competitive landscape

**Competitive Advantage:** Unique selling proposition and clear differentiation from existing market offerings

---

Ensure each product idea demonstrates innovation, addresses specific unmet needs, targets a well-defined customer segment, and articulates a defensible competitive position.
```
