# Home Appraisal Preparation Checklist Prompt

## 簡介

The Home Appraisal Preparation Checklist Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive action plan to help homeowners prepare their property for appraisal and maximize valuation outcomes. This home appraisal preparation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your property details and specific concerns, then produces a multi-section plan covering improvements documentation, room measurement templates, comparable property analysis strategies, appraiser vetting criteria, presentation checklists for curb appeal and interiors, and a timeline organizing every task relative to your appraisal date. It addresses both the documentation appraisers need and the strategic presentation decisions that influence property assessment. Use this prompt when you have an upcoming appraisal and want to ensure you present complete information, identify favorable comparable sales, and make decisions that support accurate valuation. ● Organizes property improvements with dates and costs in a format ready to present to appraisers ● Provides measurement templates and comparable property research strategies that support strong valuations ● Creates presentation checklists covering curb appeal, interior staging, and decluttering priorities ● Delivers a countdown timeline scheduling each preparation task relative to the appraisal date ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert real estate preparation consultant who has guided hundreds of homeowners through successful appraisals, combining deep knowledge of appraisal methodology with practical home presentation strategies.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, actionable home appraisal preparation plan that maximizes property valuation and ensures a smooth appraisal process. Your plan must address documentation preparation, property presentation, comparative market analysis, appraiser vetting, and strategic communication.

## Context

**Property details:** {{property-details}}

**Appraisal concerns:** {{appraisal-concerns}}

Appraisals are based on recently sold comparable properties, and proper preparation can significantly influence the appraiser's assessment. The plan should be tailored to the specific property type, improvements made, and timeline available before the appraisal date.

## Output

Deliver a structured preparation plan organized into the following sections:

### 1. Improvements Documentation List
Compile all property enhancements mentioned in the property details with dates and costs in a format ready to present to the appraiser.

### 2. Room Measurement Cheat Sheet
Create a comprehensive template that captures accurate measurements to save the appraiser time and ensure precision.

### 3. Comparative Market Analysis (Comps) Strategy
Develop guidance for identifying and highlighting favorable recently sold properties and current market listings that support a strong valuation.

### 4. Appraiser Qualification Criteria
Establish vetting criteria focusing on local market expertise, neighborhood familiarity, and relevant experience with similar property types.

### 5. Property Presentation Checklist
Design an actionable checklist covering:
- Curb appeal enhancements
- Interior cleanliness standards
- Decluttering priorities
- Strategic staging recommendations

### 6. Timeline & Task Schedule
Provide a countdown timeline indicating when each preparation task should be completed relative to the appraisal date (e.g., "4 weeks before," "1 week before," "day before").

Format all action items as clear bullet points with specific, measurable steps the homeowner can execute immediately.
```
