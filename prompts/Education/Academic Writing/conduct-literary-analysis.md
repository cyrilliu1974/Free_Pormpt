# Literary Analysis Prompt for Academic Critique

## 簡介

The Literary Analysis Prompt for Academic Critique is a free AI prompt that produces rigorous scholarly assessments of literary works and authors for students, academics, and critics. This literary analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to examine textual evidence, apply critical theory, and evaluate a work's contribution to its genre and field - structured as a formal academic critique complete with contextualization, balanced evaluation, and synthesis. You provide a {{work-or-author}} variable, and the prompt directs ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to deliver an overview, documented strengths and weaknesses, two applied theoretical frameworks (feminist, post-colonial, psychoanalytic, or others relevant to the text), and a conclusion that places the work in literary history. Real use cases include writing thesis chapters, preparing seminar presentations, developing book reviews for journals, and teaching students how to construct evidence-based arguments about literature. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, citation-ready analysis that goes beyond summary to engage with critical discourse and scholarly reception. ● Delivers a formal structure - overview, strengths, weaknesses, two critical theories, field contribution, and conclusion - that mirrors scholarly standards. ● Requires textual evidence and references to scholarship, ensuring claims are supported rather than impressionistic. ● Applies two relevant critical theories (e.g., Marxist, structuralist, queer theory) to deepen interpretation and reveal multiple layers of meaning. ● Evaluates impact on genre, influence on later works, and ongoing scholarly debates, situating the text in its broader literary context. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a literary critic conducting a rigorous scholarly analysis of a work or author.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive critical assessment that examines strengths, weaknesses, theoretical frameworks, and broader literary impact. Support all claims with textual evidence and reference relevant scholarship.

## Context
Analyze: {{work-or-author}}

Consider the work's literary merit, its contribution to the genre, influence on subsequent literature, and scholarly reception. Apply two relevant critical theories to deepen the analysis.

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Overview**
[Contextual summary of the work or author, including historical period, genre, and primary themes]

**Strengths**
- [Strength 1 with supporting evidence]
- [Strength 2 with supporting evidence]
- [Strength 3 with supporting evidence]

**Weaknesses**
- [Weakness 1 with supporting evidence]
- [Weakness 2 with supporting evidence]
- [Weakness 3 with supporting evidence]

**Theoretical Frameworks**

1. [Theory Name]
   - Key Concepts: [Core principles of the theory]
   - Application: [How this lens illuminates the work]

2. [Theory Name]
   - Key Concepts: [Core principles of the theory]
   - Application: [How this lens illuminates the work]

**Contribution to the Field**
- Impact on Genre: [How the work shaped or challenged genre conventions]
- Influence on Other Works: [Direct and indirect influence on later literature]
- Scholarly Reception: [Critical consensus and ongoing debates]

**Conclusion**
[Synthesize your assessment, articulating the work's lasting significance and place in literary history]
```
