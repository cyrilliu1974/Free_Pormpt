# Interdisciplinary Research Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Interdisciplinary Research Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that synthesizes insights from multiple academic disciplines to address complex problems for researchers, academics, and analysts. This interdisciplinary research prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to investigate how different fields contribute to solving a specific problem, examine integration challenges between disciplines, and present real-world case studies with proper citations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured research report that covers 3-5 relevant disciplines, identifies methodological conflicts and communication barriers, and provides measurable impact data from successful implementations. Use it when writing literature reviews, preparing grant proposals, or conducting systematic analyses that require bridging multiple academic or professional fields. ● Structures analysis around each discipline's unique contribution to the problem ● Identifies integration challenges including methodological conflicts and conceptual incompatibilities ● Requires 3 real-world case studies with measurable impact and source citations ● Produces properly formatted research reports suitable for academic and professional use ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an interdisciplinary research analyst synthesizing insights from multiple fields to solve complex problems.

## Task
Investigate how {{interdisciplinary-approach}} can be applied to solve {{problem-statement}}. Analyze each discipline's unique contribution, discuss integration challenges, and present successful real-world case studies with proper citations.

## Output
Provide a structured research report:

**Interdisciplinary Approach:** {{interdisciplinary-approach}}
**Problem Statement:** {{problem-statement}}

**Disciplines Involved:**
• List 3-5 relevant disciplines
• For each, explain its unique contribution to solving the problem

**Integration Challenges:**
• Identify 3-4 key difficulties in combining diverse disciplinary perspectives
• Address methodological conflicts, communication barriers, or conceptual incompatibilities

**Successful Case Studies:**
• Present 3 real-world examples where this approach was effectively implemented
• For each case study include:
  - Key takeaways
  - Measurable impact
  - Proper source citation

**Conclusion:**
• Summarize findings on the approach's effectiveness
• Discuss future implications and potential applications

Ensure all claims are supported by evidence and properly cited.
```
