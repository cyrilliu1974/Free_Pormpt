# Executive Summary Writer for Grant Proposals

## 簡介

The Executive Summary Writer for Grant Proposals is a free AI prompt that generates professional, persuasive executive summaries for businesses seeking grant funding. This grant proposal prompt for ChatGPT takes your proposal details - business name, grant purpose, funding amount, timeline, and key outcomes - and structures them into a compelling one-page summary organized with clear section headings: Business Overview, Problem/Opportunity, Proposed Solution, Expected Outcomes, and Funding Request. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering approximately 500 words of concise, scannable content tailored to grant review committees. Use it when applying for business grants, foundation funding, or government programs where a stand-alone executive summary must communicate your entire value proposition. ● Structures the summary into five essential sections grant reviewers expect to see ● Emphasizes measurable outcomes, unique value proposition, and transformative potential ● Formats deliverables and outcomes as bullet points for quick scanning ● Maintains a professional, persuasive tone that positions your proposal as a high-impact opportunity ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert grant writer specializing in business funding proposals. Your task is to craft an executive summary that is compelling, concise, and immediately persuasive to grant reviewers.

## Task

Write a professional executive summary for a business grant proposal that captures the essential elements in approximately 500 words (one page). The summary must:

- Introduce the business and articulate its mission
- Define the specific problem or opportunity the grant addresses
- Present the proposed solution with clarity
- Outline expected outcomes and measurable impact
- State the financial requirements and justify the investment
- Emphasize the unique value proposition and transformative potential

## Context

{{proposal-details}}

Include: business name and description, grant purpose, funding amount requested, project timeline, and 3-5 key expected outcomes.

## Output Requirements

Structure the executive summary using:

- **Clear section headings** (Business Overview, Problem/Opportunity, Proposed Solution, Expected Outcomes, Funding Request)
- **Bullet points** for outcomes and key deliverables
- **Professional, persuasive tone** suited to grant review committees
- **Concise language** that respects the one-page constraint

Ensure the summary is scannable, logically organized, and positions the proposal as a high-impact investment opportunity.
```
