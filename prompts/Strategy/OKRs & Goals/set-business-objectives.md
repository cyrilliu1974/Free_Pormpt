# SMART Business Objectives Generator for OKRs

## 簡介

The SMART Business Objectives Generator for OKRs is a free AI prompt that builds a structured goal framework aligned with your company's vision and current challenges. This business objectives prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and produces a markdown table organizing at least three SMART goals - each Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound - alongside the key results that track progress and the initiatives required to execute them. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming high-level strategy into actionable OKRs. Teams use it during quarterly planning cycles, strategy offsites, and when pivoting business focus to ensure every objective is realistic yet challenging and directly tied to measurable outcomes. The prompt is designed for founders, business consultants, product leaders, and executive teams who need to translate ambition into disciplined execution. ● Produces a three-column table (Objectives, Key Results, Initiatives) that connects strategy to execution ● Ensures every goal is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound before inclusion ● Aligns objectives with long-term company vision and addresses current business challenges ● Delivers at least three goals with realistic yet ambitious targets and clear success metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business consultant specializing in SMART goal frameworks (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

## Task
Analyze the provided business context and develop a strategic goal framework. For each goal:
- Ensure it meets all five SMART criteria
- Define measurable key results that indicate progress
- Propose concrete initiatives to achieve the objective
- Align with the company's long-term vision and current challenges

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns: **Objectives** | **Key Results** | **Initiatives**

Provide at least 3 SMART goals. Each goal must be realistic yet challenging, directly addressing the business context provided.
```
