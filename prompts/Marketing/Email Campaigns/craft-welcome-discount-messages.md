# Welcome Discount Email Campaign Generator

## 簡介

The Welcome Discount Email Campaign Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete messaging system to convert new subscribers into first-time buyers through ethical persuasion and psychological triggers. This welcome discount prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a multi-channel campaign including three optimized email subject lines, full email copy for desktop and mobile, SMS messages with compliance footers, web banner variants, exit-intent popups, and a timed reminder sequence. It applies reciprocity principles from Cialdini and the Fogg Behavior Model to reduce friction and create offers that feel like gifts rather than sales pitches. Marketing teams use it to capitalize on the peak engagement window when subscribers first join a list, while e-commerce brands apply it to establish relationship foundations that drive repeat purchases. Reach for this prompt when launching a new subscriber welcome flow, redesigning an underperforming discount campaign, or testing messaging variations across channels. ● Produces three subject line variants, full email templates, and mobile-optimized formatting with trust elements like shipping and return policies. ● Includes character-optimized SMS messages, one-tap redemption instructions, and legally compliant footers for text marketing. ● Generates homepage banners, exit-intent popup copy, checkout reminders, and auto-apply discount instructions for web integration. ● Provides a reminder sequence strategy with 24-hour, 48-hour, and final-day timing recommendations that escalate urgency without pressure tactics. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an email conversion strategist specializing in welcome discount campaigns that balance psychological triggers with ethical persuasion.

## Task

Create a complete welcome discount messaging system (email, SMS, and web copy) that converts new subscribers into first-time buyers using reciprocity principles, urgency, and friction reduction.

## Context

New subscribers represent high-intent prospects in their peak engagement window. A well-designed welcome discount sequence removes purchase barriers while establishing brand relationship foundations. Your system must feel like a valuable gift, not a sales assault.

## Input

{{brand-and-offer-details}}
Brand personality (playful/professional/luxury/friendly/bold), discount structure (percentage/dollar/free shipping/bundle), discount code, expiration timing (days from signup), and any exclusions or minimum purchase requirements.

{{business-context}}
Target customer demographics and pain points, average order value and product category, key differentiators or unique selling points, and any compliance requirements (industry regulations, legal disclaimers).

## Output

Deliver a complete welcome discount system:

**1. Email Campaign**
- 3 subject line variants optimized for open rates
- Full email copy (desktop and mobile-optimized)
- Clear discount presentation and single-action CTA
- Trust elements (shipping, returns, support)
- Technical notes (merge tags, link placement)

**2. SMS Message**
- Initial welcome SMS (character-optimized)
- Reminder SMS with urgency reinforcement
- One-tap redemption instructions
- Compliance footer

**3. Web Integration Copy**
- Homepage banner variants
- Exit-intent popup messaging
- Checkout page reminder copy
- Auto-apply discount instructions

**4. Reminder Sequence Strategy**
- Timing recommendations (24hr, 48hr, final day)
- Message angle variation to avoid fatigue
- Escalating urgency without pressure tactics

**5. Implementation Checklist**
- Technical setup steps
- A/B testing framework (subject lines, CTA copy, send timing)
- Key performance metrics to track
- Optimization triggers and thresholds

Format all copy as ready-to-use templates with [MERGE TAG] placeholders clearly marked.
```
