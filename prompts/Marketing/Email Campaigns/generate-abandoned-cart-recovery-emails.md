# Abandoned Cart Recovery Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Abandoned Cart Recovery Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete multi-email campaigns designed to bring shoppers back and drive purchase completion for e-commerce businesses. This abandoned cart recovery prompt for ChatGPT walks through a strategic 3-4 email sequence with escalating urgency and value. Each email includes a subject line, preview text, body copy, and a clear call-to-action, all structured using dependency grammar principles so every sentence builds logically on the last. The prompt applies proven consumer behavior psychology - loss aversion, scarcity, reciprocity, and personalization - and adjusts tone based on your store context variable. You can run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to produce email copy that feels personal, not templated. Use cases include reminder emails sent 2-4 hours after abandonment, follow-ups with social proof or incentives at 24 hours, and final urgency pushes at 48-72 hours. Reach for this prompt when you need a turnkey recovery campaign that balances persuasion with clarity, whether you're a solo founder, an e-commerce marketer, or an agency building campaigns for clients. ● Creates a timed 3-4 email sequence with escalating urgency, social proof, and incentive strategies ● Structures every email with subject line, preview text, body copy, and specific CTA button text ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure logical flow and persuasive momentum in every message ● Adapts tone, language, and psychology triggers to match your store context and target demographic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in e-commerce conversion optimization and abandoned cart recovery.

## Task
Develop a complete abandoned cart recovery email sequence (3-4 emails) that re-engages customers and drives purchase completion. Each email must include a subject line, body copy, and clear call-to-action. Use dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating persuasive momentum.

## Context
Store and offer details:
{{store-context}}

Structure the sequence with escalating urgency and value:
- Email 1 (send 2-4 hours after abandonment): friendly reminder, highlight the abandoned items, emphasize benefits
- Email 2 (send 24 hours later): add social proof or scarcity elements, introduce incentive if appropriate
- Email 3 (send 48-72 hours later): final urgency push, strongest offer, last-chance framing

Apply consumer behavior psychology: loss aversion, reciprocity, scarcity, and personalization. Tailor tone and language to the target demographic described in your store context.

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list with this structure for each email:

**Email [Number] – [Timing]**
- **Subject Line:** [compelling subject]
- **Preview Text:** [secondary hook]
- **Body:**
  [Opening]
  [Product reminder with benefits]
  [Persuasive element: social proof/urgency/offer]
  [Clear CTA]
- **Call-to-Action:** [specific button/link text]

Use **bold** for emphasis on key benefit statements and offers. Keep email bodies concise (150-250 words each) and mobile-friendly.
```
