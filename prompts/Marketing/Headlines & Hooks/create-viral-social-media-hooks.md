# Viral Social Media Hook Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Viral Social Media Hook Generator is a free AI prompt that creates scroll-stopping opening lines for social media posts across Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. This social media hook prompt for ChatGPT takes your post context and produces five distinct opening hooks, each under 280 characters, using proven viral content techniques like emotional triggers, curiosity gaps, and audience pain points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying copywriting psychology to craft punchy openers that encourage engagement, comments, and shares. Whether you're launching a product, sharing insights, or building thought leadership, each hook is tailored to your specific message while avoiding generic promotional language. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple high-performing hook options for the same post, want to A/B test different emotional angles, or need to cut through crowded social feeds with stronger opening lines. ● Produces five unique hooks per request, each tailored to your post context and under the 280-character limit ● Applies viral content structures including curiosity triggers, emotional resonance, and audience desire mapping ● Avoids promotional clichés and generic phrases in favor of strong vocabulary and persuasive copywriting ● Works for any social platform or post type - product launches, educational threads, personal stories, or industry commentary ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter and social media strategist specializing in viral content.

## Task
Generate 5 original, attention-grabbing hooks for a social media post. Each hook must be 280 characters or less, optimized for maximum engagement and shareability.

## Context
Post details:
{{post-context}}

Apply proven viral hook techniques:
- Concise, punchy opening that stops the scroll
- Tap into audience emotions, pain points, or desires
- Encourage engagement and sharing through curiosity, controversy, or value
- Avoid overly promotional language or generic phrases
- Use strong vocabulary choices and persuasive copywriting structures

## Output
Provide 5 hooks in a numbered list:

1. [Hook 1]
2. [Hook 2]
3. [Hook 3]
4. [Hook 4]
5. [Hook 5]

Ensure each hook is tailored to the specific post context and stands alone as a compelling opener.
```
