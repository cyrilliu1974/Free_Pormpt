# Video Sales Script Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Video Sales Script Generator is a free AI prompt that produces conversion-focused video scripts for marketers, content creators, and sales teams. This video sales script prompt for ChatGPT guides you through crafting structured video content that moves viewers from awareness to action. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, it uses dependency grammar principles to ensure each idea builds logically from the previous one, creating a persuasive narrative arc. You input your target audience, product or service, unique selling proposition, brand voice, and desired call-to-action; the AI delivers a complete script with five key sections: an attention-grabbing introduction, a problem statement that surfaces audience pain points, a solution explanation, tangible benefits, and a clear call-to-action. Real use cases include product launch videos, explainer content, social media ads, landing page videos, and sales presentations. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, conversion-optimized video script that speaks directly to your audience's challenges and motivations. ● Analyzes target audience motivations and pain points to craft relevant messaging ● Structures scripts with dependency grammar so each section flows naturally into the next ● Adapts tone and persuasive language to match your specified brand voice ● Delivers organized sections with bullet points for easy filming and teleprompter use ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in video marketing scripts that drive conversions.

## Task
Write a complete video script structured to boost sales by addressing audience pain points and clearly communicating value. Organize the script using dependency grammar principles—each idea builds logically from the previous one, creating a persuasive narrative arc.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Product/service:** {{product-service}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}
**Call-to-action:** {{cta}}

Analyze what motivates this audience, their key challenges, and how the offering solves their specific problems. Use storytelling techniques and persuasive language appropriate to the brand voice.

## Output
Deliver the script in structured sections with clear headings:

**Introduction** – Hook that captures attention and establishes relevance

**Problem Statement** – Pain points the audience experiences

**Solution** – How the product/service addresses these problems

**Benefits** – Tangible outcomes and value delivered

**Call-to-Action** – Clear directive that encourages immediate engagement

Use bullet points within each section to highlight key talking points and ensure the script flows logically from awareness to action.
```
