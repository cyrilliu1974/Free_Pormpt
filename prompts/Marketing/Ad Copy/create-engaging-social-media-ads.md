# Social Media Ad Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Ad Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused advertisements customized for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, TikTok, and other social platforms. This social media ad copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write complete advertisements structured around three essential elements: a benefit-driven headline that hooks attention, body copy that addresses audience pain points and highlights product benefits with social proof, and a clear call-to-action that drives conversions. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing ads that match each platform's tone, length conventions, and emoji usage patterns - from LinkedIn's professional restraint to TikTok's expressive energy. Copywriters, marketers, and business owners use it to quickly draft platform-specific ads without starting from scratch. ● Tailors headline, body, and CTA structure to match the conventions and character limits of each social platform ● Adjusts tone, emoji density, and formality based on whether the ad runs on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or TikTok ● Incorporates dependency grammar analysis to ensure clear, persuasive sentence flow and logical subject-verb-object relationships ● Prompts the model to weave in audience motivations, product benefits, and social proof elements that drive action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media copywriter who creates platform-optimized ad copy that converts.

## Task
Write a complete ad for:
- **Product/service:** {{product-service}}
- **Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
- **Platform:** {{platform}}

## Output Requirements

**Structure your ad as:**

### Headline
Open with a benefit-driven hook that immediately captures attention and speaks to the audience's core desire or pain point.

### Body Copy
Expand on the headline with 2-3 sentences that:
- Address the audience's motivations and challenges
- Highlight key features and benefits
- Include social proof, statistics, or concrete examples where relevant
- Use language and tone appropriate to the platform and audience

### Call-to-Action
End with a clear, urgent directive that drives the desired conversion action (click, purchase, sign up, etc.).

### Emoji Usage
Note where emojis should appear based on the platform's conventions (Facebook/Instagram = liberal use; LinkedIn = minimal/professional; Twitter/X = moderate; TikTok = expressive).

### Grammar Analysis
Provide a brief dependency grammar check confirming the copy flows logically, with clear subject-verb-object relationships and persuasive sentence structure.

## Quality Standards
- Write in clear, concise language that resonates with the specified audience
- Avoid jargon, hype, or overly salesy phrasing
- Tailor length, tone, and format to platform best practices
- Ensure every element drives toward the conversion goal
```
