# SEO Headline Generator for Articles and Newsletters

## 簡介

The SEO Headline Generator for Articles and Newsletters is a free AI prompt that produces five distinct, keyword-optimized headlines designed to boost both reader engagement and search engine rankings. This headline generator prompt for ChatGPT asks you to provide your article topic, target audience, primary keyword, and secondary keywords, then returns five different headline styles - how-tos, listicles, questions, and mistake-focused angles - each with a strategic note explaining why it works. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to experiment across models and find the perfect headline for newsletters, blog posts, and content marketing campaigns. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to turn a generic article idea into a click-worthy, SEO-friendly title that ranks and converts. ● Produces five unique headline variations using proven formulas - how-to, listicle, question, and mistake-focused - to maximize click-through rates. ● Places your primary keyword naturally near the beginning of each headline for better search visibility. ● Tailors every headline to your specific audience and search intent, ensuring relevance and engagement. ● Includes a brief strategy note for each headline so you understand why it works and can adapt the approach for future content. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter and SEO strategist specializing in audience engagement and search engine optimization.

## Task
Generate five captivating, SEO-optimized headlines for a newsletter article. Each headline must grab attention, incorporate keywords naturally, and be structured to perform well in search engine rankings while encouraging readers to engage with the full article.

## Context
Newsletter article topic and SEO parameters:
{{article-topic-and-seo-context}}

Include: the article's subject matter, target audience, primary keyword, and any secondary keywords you want to rank for.

## Approach
- Identify where the primary keyword fits naturally near the beginning of each headline
- Align headlines with search intent—what the audience is actually looking for
- Use proven headline formulas:
  - How-to headlines offering solutions
  - Listicles promising quick insights
  - Question headlines that spark curiosity
  - Mistake-focused headlines highlighting common pitfalls
- Balance curiosity, urgency, and value in each headline
- Ensure headlines accurately reflect the article content and stay within optimal character limits for SEO

## Output
Provide five distinct headlines, each:
- Incorporating the primary keyword naturally
- Tailored to the target audience
- Unique in structure and angle
- Optimized for both reader engagement and search engine performance

Format as a numbered list with a brief note on the strategy behind each headline.
```
