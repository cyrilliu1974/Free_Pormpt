# Phone Selfie AI Image Prompt Generator

## 簡介

The Phone Selfie AI Image Prompt Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic-looking smartphone selfie images with natural lighting artifacts, handheld camera imperfections, and street photography aesthetics for photographers, content creators, and digital artists. This phone selfie prompt for Midjourney produces images that mimic real iPhone front-camera shots, complete with uneven amber color casts, natural skin texture, slight diagonal tilt, low-light grain, and environmental motion blur. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, accepting two variables - subject description and setting - to generate medium close-up selfies with cluttered backgrounds, auto white balance drift, and the candid feel of golden-hour street photography. Use it to create portfolio samples, visual references for film pre-production, social media content mockups, or character design studies that require believable smartphone photography realism. Reach for this prompt when you need images that look like they were taken on a phone in real-world conditions, not studio-perfect renders. ● Simulates iPhone 13 front camera characteristics including warm color cast, handheld grain, and auto white balance inaccuracies ● Adds environmental realism with cluttered backgrounds, motion blur on passersby, and slight diagonal tilt from handheld shooting ● Produces natural skin texture with uneven lighting typical of golden-hour street conditions ● Outputs 9:16 vertical aspect ratio with raw style processing and medium close-up framing ## Prompt
