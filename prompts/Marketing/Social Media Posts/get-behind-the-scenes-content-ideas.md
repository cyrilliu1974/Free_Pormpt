# Behind-the-Scenes Content Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Behind-the-Scenes Content Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces authentic, engaging content concepts revealing brand operations, culture, and creative processes for content strategists and social media managers. This behind-the-scenes content prompt for ChatGPT delivers 8-12 diverse concepts, each with a compelling title, audience-focused description explaining what the content reveals and why it resonates, plus a practical execution strategy covering production approach, format recommendation, and resource requirements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring variety across formats like video, photo series, live streams, written pieces, and interactive content while covering themes from people and process to product development, workspace culture, decision-making, and challenges overcome. Content teams use it to plan authentic storytelling that builds trust and connection without generic stock content. ● Produces 8-12 tailored behind-the-scenes concepts spanning video, photo, live stream, written, and interactive formats ● Includes execution strategies detailing production approach, format choice, and resource needs for each idea ● Covers diverse themes from team culture and creative process to workspace insights and challenges overcome ● Aligns every concept with your specific brand identity, audience interests, and content goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in behind-the-scenes content that reveals authentic brand stories and builds audience connection.

## Task
Brainstorm innovative behind-the-scenes content concepts tailored to the brand and audience described below. Each concept should offer genuine insight into operations, culture, or creative processes while aligning with strategic content goals.

## Context
{{brand-and-audience}}

{{content-goals-and-resources}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of 8-12 diverse behind-the-scenes content ideas. For each:

**Title** — A clear, compelling concept name

**Description** — What the content reveals and why it engages this audience (2-3 sentences)

**Execution Strategy** — Practical production approach, format recommendation, and resource requirements (2-3 sentences)

Ensure variety across formats (video, photo series, live streams, written pieces, interactive content) and themes (people, process, product development, workspace, decision-making, challenges overcome).
```
