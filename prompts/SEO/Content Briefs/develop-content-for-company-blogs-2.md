# Company Blog Content Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Company Blog Content Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds a complete blog content calendar with SEO-optimized topics, target keywords, and publication scheduling for businesses looking to grow organic traffic and brand awareness. This blog content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, industry trends, and competitive landscape to generate a structured content plan delivered as a markdown table. You provide your company name, target audience, industry, and the number of posts you want to plan, and the prompt returns publication dates, blog post topics, target keywords, and strategic descriptions for each piece of content. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes strategic rationale explaining how the plan aligns with audience needs and market opportunities. Marketing teams use it to plan quarterly content sprints, content strategists rely on it to map customer journey touchpoints, and startups use it to launch consistent publishing schedules without hiring agencies. Reach for this prompt when you need a data-informed content calendar that connects business goals to search visibility and audience engagement, not just a random list of blog ideas. ● Produces a markdown table with publication dates, blog topics, target keywords, and content descriptions that map to the customer journey ● Includes strategic rationale explaining how the plan addresses audience behavior, competitive gaps, and market opportunities ● Customizes the calendar based on your company name, target audience, industry, and desired post count ● Outputs a publication schedule designed for consistent delivery and search discoverability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist developing a comprehensive blog content strategy to boost brand awareness and drive organic traffic.

## Task
Create a strategic content plan that includes:
- Analysis of target audience behavior, industry trends, and competitive landscape
- A content calendar aligned with business goals and audience interests
- Specific blog post topics with SEO optimization
- Publication scheduling for consistent delivery

## Context
**Company:** {{company-name}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Number of posts to plan:** {{post-count}}

Ensure each piece of content serves a clear purpose in the customer journey and supports discoverability through search.

## Output
Present your content strategy as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Publication Date**
- **Blog Post Topic**
- **Target Keywords**
- **Description** (brief overview of the post's content and strategic purpose)

Include strategic rationale before the table explaining how the plan addresses audience needs and market opportunities.
```
