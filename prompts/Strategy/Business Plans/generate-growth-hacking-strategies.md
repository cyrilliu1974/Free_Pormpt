# Growth Hacking Strategy Generator for Businesses

## 簡介

The Growth Hacking Strategy Generator for Businesses is a free AI prompt that identifies effective, budget-conscious growth tactics tailored to your specific business, product, or service. This growth hacking prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your niche, target audience demographics, competitor landscape, and industry trends to surface 5+ high-impact strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured niche overview followed by a comparison table that breaks down each strategy's description, cost tier, potential impact rating, and concrete implementation steps. Startups testing market fit, bootstrapped SaaS teams seeking viral loops, and e-commerce brands exploring referral programs use it to prioritize experiments that balance affordability with reach. ● Receives your business context, stage, niche, audience, and resource constraints, then researches competitor tactics and emerging channels. ● Outputs a niche overview plus a five-column table ranking strategies by cost, potential impact, and step-by-step implementation guidance. ● Includes cited sources so you can verify findings and dig deeper into industry benchmarks or case studies. ● Designed for founders, growth marketers, and product teams who need fast, data-informed experiments without expensive consultants. ## Prompt
