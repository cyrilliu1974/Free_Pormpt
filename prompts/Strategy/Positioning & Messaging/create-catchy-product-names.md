# Product Naming Generator for Brands

## 簡介

The Product Naming Generator for Brands is a free AI prompt that creates innovative, memorable product names for branding professionals and marketing teams. This product naming prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured process: analyzing your product's features and target audience, generating names through creative ideation and linguistic techniques, screening for pronunciation and cultural sensitivity, performing preliminary trademark and domain checks, and delivering five shortlisted names with detailed rationale. Each suggestion explains how the name connects to product benefits, why it resonates with your audience, and its linguistic accessibility. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text-generation platform. Reach for this prompt when you need brandable names that balance creativity with market viability - whether launching a new product, rebranding an existing offering, or exploring naming directions for client projects. ● Analyzes product features, audience demographics, and cultural nuances before generating names ● Screens every candidate for ease of spelling, pronunciation, and cross-cultural appropriateness ● Includes preliminary trademark and domain-availability considerations in the selection criteria ● Delivers five finalized names with rationale covering uniqueness, relevance, and brandability ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in branding and creative naming strategies with deep knowledge of market trends, consumer psychology, and linguistic appeal.

## Task

Generate five catchy, innovative product names that are unique, memorable, easy to spell and pronounce, and free from obvious trademark conflicts. Each name should capture the product's essence and resonate with the target audience.

## Context

**Product and audience:**
{{product-brief}}

## Process

1. **Understand the Product**: Identify the product's features, benefits, unique selling points, and competitive differentiation.
2. **Target Audience Analysis**: Consider demographics, interests, preferences, and cultural/linguistic nuances that affect brand name perception.
3. **Creative Ideation**: Use word association, metaphorical thinking, and linguistic play to generate names that reflect key features, benefits, and emotional appeal.
4. **Preliminary Screening**: Eliminate names that are difficult to pronounce, spell, or remember, misaligned with brand image, or potentially offensive across cultures.
5. **Trademark and Domain Check**: Conduct basic searches to verify names are not already trademarked in the same industry and that corresponding domains are likely available.
6. **Final Selection**: Select the top five names based on uniqueness, relevance, linguistic appeal, and availability.

## Output

Provide five product name suggestions in a clear format. For each name, include:
- **The name**
- **Brief rationale** explaining how it connects to the product's features, benefits, and target audience
- **Why it works**: linguistic accessibility, memorability, and brandability

Example format:
1. **EcoQuench** – Combines "eco" (environmental responsibility) with "quench" (satisfying thirst), conveying sustainability and core function in a memorable two-syllable name that's easy to pronounce globally.
```
