# Brand History Narrative Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand History Narrative Generator is a free AI prompt that builds cohesive brand stories for marketers, founders, and brand strategists looking to articulate their company's journey. This brand history prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft a three-section narrative covering your brand's origin story and milestones, values demonstrated through concrete actions, and unique selling points backed by customer outcomes. You supply four variables (brand name, industry, target audience, and key founding details), and the prompt structures a narrative arc that builds naturally from founding motivations through pivotal moments to present-day differentiation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing clearly formatted sections with vivid, emotionally resonant language calibrated to your audience. Reach for this prompt when you need website About pages, pitch decks, investor narratives, or onboarding materials that go beyond bullet points to tell a story people remember. ● Structures the narrative into three interdependent sections that build a cohesive story arc from founding to present day ● Grounds values in specific actions and decisions rather than abstract mission statements ● Supports unique selling points with concrete examples and customer outcomes, not unsupported claims ● Adapts tone and sensory language to match your target audience and brand identity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand storyteller.

## Task
Craft a compelling brand narrative that showcases history, values, and unique selling points. Structure the story so each section builds naturally on the previous one, creating a cohesive arc from founding to present-day differentiation.

## Context
Brand: {{brand-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Key values & founding details: {{brand-essentials}}

## Approach
- Open with the brand's origin story, highlighting founding motivations and early challenges
- Identify 2-3 pivotal milestones that shaped the brand's trajectory
- Articulate core values through concrete actions and decisions, not abstract claims
- Emphasize unique selling points with specific examples and customer outcomes
- Use vivid, sensory language and emotional hooks appropriate to the target audience
- Maintain a tone consistent with the brand's identity throughout

## Output
Deliver the narrative in clearly structured sections:

**Brand History**  
[Origin story and key milestones]

**Core Values**  
[Values demonstrated through actions]

**What Sets Us Apart**  
[Unique selling points with evidence]

Use bullet points or numbered lists within sections to enhance readability.
```
