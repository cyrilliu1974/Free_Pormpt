# NAP Consistency Audit for Local SEO

## 簡介

The NAP Consistency Audit for Local SEO is a free AI prompt that creates a complete citation audit and correction strategy for local businesses seeking to improve search rankings and customer trust. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT produces a prioritized list of 15-25 relevant directories and review sites, a step-by-step audit process to identify discrepancies, an implementation plan for corrections, and ongoing maintenance recommendations. It works by analyzing your business details, industry, and target area to identify high-authority platforms like Google Business Profile and Yelp, industry-specific directories, major data aggregators such as Neustar and Factual, and local chambers of commerce. The output includes a structured markdown table with existing NAP information placeholders and corrected entries, making it easy to track and execute updates across all platforms. Use cases include launching a new location, recovering from a business move or rebrand, and routine citation hygiene for multi-location businesses. This prompt is ideal for local SEO specialists, digital marketing agencies managing local clients, and small business owners who need to maintain accurate business information across the web. ● Produces a prioritized directory list ranked by authority and relevance to your industry and location ● Delivers a systematic audit process with verification steps for each citation source ● Generates a markdown table tracking existing NAP data, discrepancies, and corrected information across 15-25 platforms ● Includes maintenance recommendations to preserve citation accuracy over time and prevent future inconsistencies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local business optimization specialist focused on ensuring accurate, consistent Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP) citations across online directories and review platforms.

## Task
Create a comprehensive NAP audit and update strategy for the business. Deliver:

1. A prioritized list of relevant local directories and review sites for the industry and geography
2. A step-by-step audit process to identify NAP discrepancies across platforms
3. An implementation plan for correcting inconsistencies
4. Ongoing maintenance recommendations to preserve citation accuracy

## Context
Business details:
{{business-nap}}

Industry: {{industry}}

Target local area: {{target-area}}

NAP consistency directly impacts local search rankings and customer trust. Focus on high-authority directories (Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places), industry-specific platforms, major data aggregators (Neustar/Localeze, Factual, Foursquare, Infogroup), and local chambers or municipal listings.

## Output
Provide the audit process as numbered steps, followed by a markdown table:

| Website | Existing NAP | Updated NAP |
|---------|--------------|-------------|

Include 15-25 platforms ranked by priority. In the "Existing NAP" column, note "[Verify]" as a placeholder for audit findings. In "Updated NAP," show the corrected information or "[No change needed]" if accurate.
```
