# Investigative Report Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Investigative Report Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that builds structured, balanced investigative report frameworks for journalists, researchers, and content creators. This investigative report prompt for ChatGPT produces a hierarchical outline with main sections, subsections, key investigative questions, evidence and source placeholders, and integrated counterarguments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, guiding you through a logical narrative from introduction to findings and conclusion while weaving opposing perspectives throughout the report rather than isolating them. Real use cases include investigative journalism pieces, corporate research reports, academic investigations, and long-form exposés that demand rigorous fact-checking and unbiased coverage. This prompt is ideal for journalists working under deadline constraints, researchers structuring multi-source investigations, and content teams tackling complex topics that require balanced reporting. ● Creates hierarchical outlines with main sections, subsections, and specific investigative questions for each area of inquiry ● Includes embedded placeholders for evidence, sources, and fact-checking notes to maintain research rigor ● Integrates counterarguments and alternative perspectives throughout the structure, not in isolated sections ● Adapts to your investigation topic, resource constraints, deadline, and target audience for realistic planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert investigative journalist developing a balanced, comprehensive report outline.

## Task
Create a structured investigative report outline that ensures unbiased coverage, logical flow, and rigorous fact-checking. The outline must include:

- Main sections with relevant subsections
- Key points and questions to address under each subsection
- Placeholders for evidence, sources, and fact-checking notes
- Counterarguments and alternative viewpoints to maintain balance
- A clear narrative progression from introduction through findings to conclusion

## Context
**Investigation:** {{investigation-topic}}

**Constraints and resources:** {{deadline-resources-access}}

**Audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Output
Deliver a hierarchical outline using this structure:

**I. Main Section**
   A. Subsection
      • Key point / question to investigate
      • [Evidence placeholder]
      • [Source placeholder]
   B. Subsection
      • Key point with counterargument consideration

Ensure every major claim has a corresponding fact-check marker and that opposing perspectives are woven throughout, not isolated in a single "other side" section.
```
