# Email Marketing Automation Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Automation Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email campaign systems for businesses looking to nurture leads and drive engagement through automated sequences. This email marketing automation prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a 10-step implementation plan that covers audience analysis, segmentation strategies, content development, automated email sequences mapped to customer journey stages, personalization tactics using dynamic content and conditional logic, KPI frameworks, A/B testing protocols, list hygiene practices, CRM integration workflows, and continuous optimization loops. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context and campaign objectives as inputs. Use it when you need to design a complete automated email system that moves subscribers through welcome series, onboarding flows, engagement campaigns, re-engagement sequences, conversion funnels, and retention programs. ● Produces audience segmentation frameworks based on demographics, behavior, purchase history, and engagement patterns ● Maps automated email sequences to every customer lifecycle stage from first contact through retention ● Defines personalization approaches using merge tags, conditional content blocks, and segment-specific messaging ● Establishes KPI tracking for open rates, click-through rates, conversions, revenue per email, list growth, and churn metrics ● Creates A/B testing roadmaps for subject lines, preview text, content variations, CTA elements, and send-time optimization ● Details CRM integration requirements including data sync points, trigger conditions, field mapping, and workflow automation ● Builds performance review cadences with dashboard specifications and strategy adjustment protocols ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist designing an automated campaign system to nurture leads and drive customer engagement.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable plan for implementing email marketing automation. Cover audience analysis, segmentation, content creation, automated sequences, personalization, performance measurement, optimization, list hygiene, CRM integration, and continuous improvement.

## Context
**Business overview:**
{{business-context}}
(Include: business type, target audience demographics and psychographics, current email list size, primary business goal, and key product or service offering.)

**Campaign objectives:**
{{campaign-objectives}}
(Specify: lead nurture goals, desired engagement outcomes, conversion targets, and customer lifecycle stages you want to address.)

## Output
Deliver a numbered action plan covering:

1. **Audience analysis** – Determine optimal email content types, messaging themes, and send frequency based on the business type and target audience.
2. **Segmentation strategy** – Define customer segments by demographics, behavioral signals, purchase history, and engagement level.
3. **Content development** – Write clear, persuasive email copy with strong value propositions and logical message flow.
4. **Automated sequences** – Map email series to customer journey stages (welcome, onboarding, engagement, re-engagement, conversion, retention).
5. **Personalization tactics** – Specify dynamic content, merge tags, conditional logic, and segment-specific messaging approaches.
6. **KPIs and metrics** – Identify success measures including open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, revenue per email, list growth rate, and unsubscribe rate.
7. **A/B testing plan** – Design experiments for subject lines, preview text, content variations, CTA placement and wording, and send time optimization.
8. **List maintenance** – Establish protocols for removing inactive subscribers, validating email addresses, managing bounces, and re-engagement campaigns.
9. **CRM integration** – Detail data synchronization points, trigger conditions, field mapping, and workflow automation between email platform and CRM.
10. **Optimization loop** – Create a review cadence with performance dashboards, insight generation, and strategy adjustment protocols.

For each step, provide specific strategies, recommended tools or techniques, and measurable outcomes.
```
