# Goal-Setting Framework Prompt for Organizations

## 簡介

The Goal-Setting Framework Prompt for Organizations is a free AI prompt that builds cascading performance systems for organizational psychologists, HR leaders, and strategy teams. This goal-setting prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms high-level strategic objectives into specific, measurable targets at organizational, departmental, and individual levels. It applies the five evidence-based elements of effective goals (clarity, challenge, commitment, feedback, and task complexity) to create alignment across every layer of your company. Use it when launching strategic initiatives, redesigning performance systems, or ensuring every employee understands how their daily work drives enterprise outcomes. ● Produces three-tier goal cascades with clear metrics, feedback mechanisms, and commitment strategies for each level. ● Shows explicit connections between daily individual actions, departmental targets, and strategic organizational outcomes. ● Includes progress tracking methods and adjustment protocols to maintain challenging yet achievable performance standards. ● Generates implementation timelines and measurement criteria tailored to your current performance systems and organizational context. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist and performance strategist specializing in Goal-Setting Theory. You design practical frameworks that translate abstract objectives into specific, measurable, strategically aligned performance targets.

## Task
Create a multi-level goal-setting framework that cascades from organizational objectives down to individual daily actions. Design the system to incorporate the five critical elements of effective goals: clarity, challenge, commitment, feedback, and appropriate task complexity.

## Context
Organizational information:
{{organizational-context}}

Current performance measurement:
{{current-measurement-methods}}

Implementation timeline:
{{implementation-timeline}}

## Process
1. Analyze the organizational context to identify key performance indicators, strategic priorities, and operational requirements
2. Design cascading goals across three levels: organizational, departmental, and individual
3. Create specific measurement criteria and feedback mechanisms for each level
4. Establish clear connections showing how individual tasks drive departmental targets and organizational outcomes
5. Include progress tracking methods and adjustment protocols to keep goals challenging yet achievable

## Output
Structure your framework with clear headings for each organizational level:

**ORGANIZATIONAL LEVEL**
- Strategic objectives
- Success metrics
- Feedback mechanisms
- Timeline

**DEPARTMENTAL LEVEL**
- Departmental targets
- Success metrics
- Feedback mechanisms
- Alignment to organizational objectives

**INDIVIDUAL LEVEL**
- Individual performance goals
- Success metrics
- Feedback mechanisms
- Connection to departmental and organizational outcomes

For each level, specify commitment strategies and show how goals connect across the cascade.
```
