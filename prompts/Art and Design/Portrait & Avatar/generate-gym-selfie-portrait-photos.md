# Gym Selfie Portrait Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Gym Selfie Portrait Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates realistic vertical fitness selfies with authentic post-workout details for content creators and fitness brands. This gym selfie portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 9:16 smartphone-style images featuring subjects seated on gym floors with natural breathlessness, dewy skin, messy ponytails, and visible sweat on activewear. The prompt specifies compression leggings with mesh details, sports bras with strappy backs, fitness trackers, towels, water bottles with condensation, and background gym equipment reflected in mirrors. It captures the authentic slightly-above eye-level angle and LED gym lighting that defines real workout selfies shared on Instagram and TikTok. Reach for this prompt when you need relatable fitness content that mirrors how real people photograph themselves after training sessions - no studio setup required. ● Produces smartphone front-camera framing with natural lighting, flyaway hair, flushed cheeks, and visible moisture on skin and clothing. ● Includes customizable subject details and gym settings while maintaining post-workout realism - high ponytails, scrunchies, diamond studs, fitness trackers, and towels. ● Generates props like insulated water bottles with stickers, dumbbells, yoga mats, and mirrored backgrounds that ground the image in real gym environments. ● Delivers vertical 9:16 composition optimized for Instagram Stories, Reels, and TikTok without the cost or coordination of on-location photo shoots. ## Prompt
