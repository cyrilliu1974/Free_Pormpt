# Affiliate Program Planning Prompt for Digital Business

## 簡介

The Affiliate Program Planning Prompt for Digital Business is a free AI prompt that creates a tailored affiliate marketing program plan for digital product and service businesses. This affiliate program planning prompt for ChatGPT walks through platform evaluation, commission design, affiliate recruitment, and management workflows. It outputs a structured plan with a comparison table of major affiliate networks (ShareASale, Impact, Awin) including setup costs, efficiency ratings, and feature breakdowns, all cited from authoritative sources. The prompt analyzes your specific business description to recommend commission tiers, cookie durations, target affiliate profiles, and a three-phase rollout timeline. Reach for this prompt when you need a documented roadmap to launch or restructure an affiliate channel - whether you run SaaS subscriptions, online courses, digital downloads, or membership sites. ● Compares affiliate network platforms with real pricing, features, and recommendations specific to your business model. ● Defines commission structures, payment schedules, and cookie windows aligned with your margins and goals. ● Maps recruitment channels, affiliate personas, onboarding steps, and ongoing management practices. ● Delivers a week-by-week implementation timeline divided into foundation, growth, and optimization phases. ## Prompt
