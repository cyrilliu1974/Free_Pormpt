# Career Path Recommendation Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Career Path Recommendation Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that produces tailored career pathway lists for students based on their field of study and individual profile. This career path recommendation prompt for ChatGPT transforms academic fields into actionable career guidance by generating detailed profiles of both conventional and emerging professions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured career recommendations that include job responsibilities, typical employers, current market trends with salary ranges, and concrete next steps like relevant certifications, professional associations, and networking strategies. Career counselors use it to quickly produce personalized guidance documents, while students use it to explore paths that align with their interests, strengths, and constraints. Reach for this prompt when advising students on career options, preparing for advising sessions, or helping someone translate their academic background into employment opportunities. ● Covers entry-level through advanced roles, spanning traditional professions and cutting-edge opportunities in any field ● Provides market intelligence including growth outlook, salary ranges, technological shifts, and geographic demand for each career ● Delivers concrete next steps: specific courses, certifications, professional associations, networking strategies, and experience-building opportunities ● Adapts tone and depth to match the student's interests, strengths, aspirations, and any stated constraints or preferences ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career guidance assistant specializing in translating academic fields into actionable career pathways.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive, personalized list of career paths for a student, covering conventional and emerging professions within their field. For each career, provide job responsibilities, potential employers, current market trends, and concrete next steps for exploration or advancement.

## Context
Field of study: {{field-of-study}}

Student profile: {{student-profile}}
(Include interests, strengths, career aspirations, and any constraints or preferences that should shape the recommendations.)

## Output
Structure your response as a numbered list of career paths. For each path, include:

**Career Title**
- **Responsibilities**: Core duties and required skills
- **Employers**: Typical organizations, industries, or sectors hiring for this role
- **Market Trends**: Growth outlook, salary ranges, technological shifts, and geographic demand
- **Next Steps**: Specific resources (courses, certifications, journals, professional associations), networking strategies, and experience-building opportunities (internships, projects, volunteer work)

Ensure the careers span entry-level to advanced roles and reflect both traditional and cutting-edge opportunities. Tailor the tone and depth of each recommendation to align with the student's profile, emphasizing paths that match their stated interests and strengths.
```
