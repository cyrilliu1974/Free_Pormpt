# Customer Segmentation by Purchase Behavior Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Segmentation by Purchase Behavior Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps market analysts and business strategists identify and categorize customers into actionable segments based on their buying patterns and demographic profiles. This customer segmentation prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an expert market analyst, examining purchase frequency, average order value, product preferences, and demographic data to reveal distinct customer groups. It produces a clean markdown table that names each segment, describes key characteristics, documents purchase frequency, and specifies average order value ranges. Use it for retail businesses, e-commerce platforms, subscription services, or any operation that needs to turn raw transaction data into targeted marketing strategies. The prompt adapts to your specific business type and segmentation criteria through two simple variables. ● Generates a markdown table with segment names, key characteristics, purchase frequency, and average order value tiers ● Differentiates customer groups based on behavioral and demographic patterns for precise targeting ● Adapts to any business type and custom segmentation criteria through two flexible variables ● Delivers concise, actionable output designed for immediate use in marketing strategy and business planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in customer segmentation.

## Task
Analyze purchase data to identify distinct customer segments based on buying behavior, preferences, and demographics. Develop a segmentation strategy that considers purchase frequency, average order value, product preferences, and demographic patterns.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}
Segmentation criteria: {{segmentation-criteria}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with the following structure:

| Segment Name | Key Characteristics | Purchase Frequency | Average Order Value |
|--------------|--------------------|--------------------|---------------------|

For each identified segment:
- Provide a descriptive name that captures the segment's essence
- List 3-5 key demographic and behavioral characteristics
- Specify typical purchase frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- Include average order value range or tier

Ensure each segment is clearly differentiated and the information is concise yet actionable for marketing and business strategy.
```
