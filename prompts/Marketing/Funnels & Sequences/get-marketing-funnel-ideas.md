# Marketing Funnel Ideas Generator (AARRR Framework)

## 簡介

The Marketing Funnel Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a complete AARRR funnel strategy with 15 concrete, immediately actionable marketing tactics tailored to solo founders and bootstrapped teams. This marketing funnel prompt for ChatGPT walks you through Acquisition, Activation, Revenue, Retention, and Referral stages, delivering three specific ideas per stage with impact scores. Instead of generic advice like "use social media," you receive detailed tactics such as "Post a 60-second LinkedIn video showing before/after customer results with a controversial industry take in the caption." The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a structured markdown table that prioritizes zero-to-low budget ideas one person can execute. It is designed for entrepreneurs who need quick-win marketing strategies that stand out from standard competitor tactics without requiring large budgets or teams. ● Delivers 15 concrete marketing ideas across all five AARRR funnel stages with actionable specificity ● Prioritizes low-budget, high-impact tactics that solo founders can execute immediately ● Includes impact scoring (0-10) to help prioritize which tactics to implement first ● Emphasizes creative, non-obvious strategies differentiated from standard competitor approaches ## Prompt

## Requirements
- **Specificity**: Every idea must be concrete and immediately actionable. Instead of "post on social media," specify the exact content type, platform, and hook (e.g., "Post a 60-second LinkedIn video showing before/after customer results with a controversial industry take in the caption").

- **Constraints**: Focus on zero-to-low budget tactics one person can execute.
- **Quick wins**: Prioritize ideas likely to deliver results quickly over high-effort, uncertain experiments.
