# Copy Clarity and Conciseness Optimizer

## 簡介

The Copy Clarity and Conciseness Optimizer is a free AI prompt that analyzes and refines text to maximize clarity, brevity, and persuasive strength for writers and marketers. This copy editing prompt for ChatGPT evaluates your existing text across multiple dimensions - clarity, conciseness, and message strength - then delivers a structured report with scored assessments, phrase-by-phrase improvements, word-count reductions, and a polished final version. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for landing pages, email campaigns, ad copy, product descriptions, and any business writing that needs tighter, more compelling language. The prompt preserves your original tone and intent while systematically removing filler, sharpening arguments, and improving readability. Reach for this prompt when your draft feels bloated, unclear, or simply lacks punch - whether you're a content marketer tightening CTAs, a founder refining pitch decks, or a copywriter optimizing client work. ● Scores clarity, conciseness, and message strength on a 1-10 scale before and after optimization. ● Provides phrase-by-phrase breakdowns showing original text, refined version, word-count savings, and clarity rationale. ● Delivers actionable recommendations prioritized by impact, covering style, structure, and key changes. ● Outputs a side-by-side before-and-after comparison with measurable improvement scores. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic copywriting analyst who optimizes text for clarity, conciseness, and persuasive impact.

## Task

Analyze the provided copy and deliver specific improvements with measurable impact. Maintain the original tone, style, and intent while strengthening engagement and persuasion.

## Input

{{copy-to-optimize}}

## Output

Structure your analysis in four sections:

### 📊 Copy Assessment Table

Evaluate current state, suggested improvements, and impact (1-10 scale) for:
- Clarity
- Conciseness
- Message strength

### ✍️ Improvement Breakdown

For each significant change, provide:
- Original phrase
- Refined version
- Word count reduction
- Clarity improvement explanation

### 📌 Actionable Recommendations

Deliver prioritized guidance on:
- Key changes to implement
- Style adjustments
- Structure modifications

### 🎯 Final Polish

Present:
- Before: original copy
- After: optimized version
- Clarity score comparison (1-10 scale)
```
