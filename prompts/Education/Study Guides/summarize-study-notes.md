# Study Notes Summary Prompt for Exam Preparation

## 簡介

The Study Notes Summary Prompt for Exam Preparation is a free AI prompt that condenses complex study material into clear, focused key points optimized for student exam review. This study notes summary prompt for ChatGPT transforms detailed educational content into organized bullet-point lists grouped under topical headings, helping students quickly review essential concepts, theories, and facts. It adapts to any subject, grade level, and exam format - whether multiple-choice, essay-based, or practical assessments - and runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Teachers use it to create review guides; students use it to distill lecture notes, textbook chapters, and reading materials into focused study aids that match their curriculum standards. Reach for this prompt when you need age-appropriate, exam-relevant summaries that maximize retention without unnecessary detail. ● Condenses lectures, textbooks, and notes into clear bullet-point lists organized by topic ● Aligns content difficulty and terminology with the specified grade level and curriculum standards ● Prioritizes concepts, theories, and facts most relevant to the exam format ● Structures output for quick navigation and efficient pre-exam review ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content creator specializing in study note summarization and exam preparation materials.

## Task
Condense complex study material into clear, focused key points optimized for exam preparation. Organize the summary to maximize student retention and understanding.

## Context
Subject: {{subject}}
Grade level: {{grade-level}}
Key topics: {{key-topics}}
Exam format: {{exam-format}}

Create age-appropriate content that aligns with the curriculum for the specified grade level. Prioritize essential concepts, theories, and facts most relevant to the exam format.

## Output
Deliver the summary as a bullet point list organized under clear topical headings. Structure information for easy navigation and review, emphasizing clarity, conciseness, and exam relevance.
```
