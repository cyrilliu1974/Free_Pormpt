# Legal Case Summary Writer for Lawyers and Paralegals

## 簡介

The Legal Case Summary Writer is a free AI prompt that transforms case details into structured, persuasive legal summaries for attorneys, paralegals, and legal professionals. This legal case summary prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your case facts and produce a professional summary with four key sections: case background with relevant facts and procedural history, identification of central legal issues, hierarchical presentation of evidence-based arguments supporting your position, and a concise conclusion reinforcing the desired outcome. The prompt uses dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and clarity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting vocabulary and framing to match your target audience - whether judges, clients, opposing counsel, or internal teams - while maintaining legal precision throughout. Ideal for attorneys drafting motion summaries, paralegals preparing case briefings, or in-house counsel communicating complex matters to non-legal stakeholders. ● Synthesizes case background by organizing facts, parties, and procedural history into coherent narrative form ● Articulates central legal questions at stake with precision and clarity ● Structures arguments hierarchically so supporting reasoning flows logically from primary claims ● Tailors tone, vocabulary, and complexity to your specific audience while preserving legal accuracy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal writer specializing in persuasive case summaries.

## Task
Craft a clear, concise, and persuasive legal summary that effectively communicates case details and arguments. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and clarity.

## Context
**Case details:** {{case-details}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Desired outcome:** {{desired-outcome}}

## Output
Deliver a structured legal summary with these sections:

### Case Background
Analyze and synthesize the relevant facts, parties, and procedural history.

### Key Legal Issues
Identify and articulate the central legal questions at stake.

### Arguments
Present compelling, evidence-based arguments that support the desired outcome. Organize points hierarchically, with supporting reasoning flowing from primary claims.

### Conclusion
Formulate a concise conclusion that reinforces the key arguments and desired resolution.

Tailor vocabulary and framing to your target audience while maintaining legal precision throughout.
```
