# Lead Nurturing Workflow Builder for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Lead Nurturing Workflow Builder for Marketing Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured, multi-stage nurturing workflows tailored to your sales cycle and conversion goals. This lead nurturing prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete workflow table with 5-8 stages covering initial lead capture through conversion and post-purchase engagement. Each stage specifies the action (email send, content offer, sales alert), content type (educational email, case study, demo invitation), trigger condition (behavioral or time-based), and measurable goal. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your business context and current conversion rate as inputs to recommend timing, content depth, and progression logic that fits your audience and product complexity. Marketing teams use it to map out automated sequences that respond to lead behavior and engagement metrics rather than relying on generic drip campaigns. Reach for this workflow builder when you need to design or document a lead nurturing system that balances automation with personalization, or when you want to optimize follow-up timing and content selection based on where leads are in the buying journey. ● Generates 5-8 nurturing stages with specific actions, content formats, triggers, and success metrics ● Incorporates behavioral triggers and engagement thresholds so follow-ups respond to lead activity ● Accounts for sales cycle length, product complexity, and baseline conversion rates ● Outputs a markdown table format that serves as both documentation and implementation guide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing automation specialist designing lead nurturing workflows that streamline sales processes and improve conversion rates.

## Task
Create a comprehensive lead nurturing workflow presented as a structured stage-by-stage plan. Each stage should specify the action, content type, trigger condition, and goal. The workflow must incorporate personalized email sequences, targeted content distribution, and timely follow-ups based on lead behavior and engagement metrics.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Current baseline conversion rate: {{current-conversion-rate}}

Consider the sales cycle length, product/service complexity, and audience characteristics when determining stage timing, content depth, and progression triggers.

## Output
Deliver the workflow as a markdown table with these columns:

| Stage | Action | Content Type | Trigger | Goal |
|-------|--------|--------------|---------|------|

Include 5-8 stages that cover the full journey from initial lead capture through conversion and post-purchase nurturing. For each stage, specify concrete actions (email send, content offer, sales alert), appropriate content formats (educational email, case study, demo invitation), behavioral or time-based triggers, and measurable goals (open rate threshold, meeting booked, purchase completed).
```
