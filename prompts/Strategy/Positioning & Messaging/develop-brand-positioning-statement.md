# Brand Positioning Statement Generator

## 簡介

The Brand Positioning Statement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clear, memorable positioning statements for companies and brand strategists. This brand positioning statement prompt for ChatGPT structures your unique value proposition using dependency grammar - a framework that arranges ideas hierarchically with your core differentiator as the root and supporting benefits branching logically from it. It analyzes your company name, target audience, products or services, unique selling points, and mission statement to craft a 1-3 sentence positioning statement that immediately communicates why your brand matters. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both the final statement and a brief explanation of how the grammatical structure reinforces your value hierarchy. Use it when launching a new brand, refining market positioning, or preparing strategy documents that require a crystal-clear articulation of what makes your offering distinct. ● Identifies your primary competitive differentiator from the unique selling points you provide ● Maps target audience pain points to the core value proposition in hierarchical format ● Produces a concise, memorable statement (1-3 sentences) designed for immediate clarity ● Includes an explanation of how the dependency grammar structure reinforces brand hierarchy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in positioning statements that clearly communicate unique value to target audiences.

## Task
Develop a compelling brand positioning statement structured with dependency grammar—a framework that arranges ideas hierarchically, with the core value as the root and supporting elements branching logically from it.

## Context
Use the following information to inform your positioning statement:

**Company:** {{company-name}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Products/Services:** {{products-services}}

**Unique Selling Points:** {{unique-selling-points}}

**Mission Statement:** {{mission-statement}}

## Process
1. Identify the primary differentiator that sets this company apart from competitors
2. Analyze the target audience's core needs and pain points that this differentiator addresses
3. Structure the statement with the main value proposition as the root, with benefits and emotional resonance branching from it
4. Ensure the statement is concise (1-3 sentences), memorable, and immediately communicates why this brand matters to the target audience

## Output
Deliver a final positioning statement in dependency grammar format, followed by a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) of how the grammatical structure reinforces the brand's core value hierarchy.
```
