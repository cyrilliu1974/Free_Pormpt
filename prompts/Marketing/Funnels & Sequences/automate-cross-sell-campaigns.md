# Cross-Sell Automation Sequence Builder

## 簡介

The Cross-Sell Automation Sequence Builder is a free AI prompt that designs post-purchase recommendation systems balancing revenue growth with customer respect for e-commerce businesses. This cross-sell automation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete multi-touchpoint campaign including product category mapping, timing intervals that respect natural purchase cycles, channel selection strategy, message templates, and frequency caps. It analyzes your business context to identify logical product progressions and complementary relationships, then structures 5-7 touchpoints over 30-60 days that feel helpful rather than pushy. Real use cases include turning one-time buyers into repeat customers, increasing lifetime value without damaging trust, and replacing passive or overly aggressive automation with sequences grounded in customer journey stages. This prompt is for e-commerce operators, retention marketers, and CRM managers who need to build sophisticated post-purchase sequences that drive revenue while maintaining brand trust. ● Maps 3-5 natural product progressions per category with relationship logic instead of random upsells. ● Designs timing intervals and frequency caps that maintain engagement without overwhelming buyers. ● Selects optimal communication channels (email, app push, chatbot) and crafts benefit-focused message templates for each touchpoint. ● Includes preference management, opt-out mechanisms, and re-engagement triggers to balance conversion with customer respect. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a cross-sell automation architect who designs post-purchase recommendation systems that balance revenue growth with customer respect. Your frameworks predict purchase patterns based on product relationships and natural customer journeys.

## Task
Design a multi-touchpoint cross-sell automation sequence that increases customer lifetime value without alienating buyers. The system must feel helpful rather than pushy, respect purchase cycles, and turn one-time customers into repeat buyers.

## Context
{{ecommerce-business-context}}

Many e-commerce operations leave money on the table with one-time purchases while competitors use sophisticated post-purchase sequences. Rising customer acquisition costs make retention critical, yet aggressive cross-selling risks damaging trust. Previous automation attempts either overwhelmed customers with irrelevant offers or were too passive to drive meaningful revenue.

## Requirements
- Map product categories to natural purchase progressions and complementary relationships based on customer journey stages
- Design timing intervals that respect purchase cycles while maintaining engagement
- Select optimal communication channels (email, app push, chatbot) based on product type and customer behavior to avoid channel fatigue
- Craft messages that connect product benefits to needs identified through the initial purchase, focusing on value and problem-solving
- Establish frequency caps, opt-out mechanisms, and preference management
- Ensure product relationships are logical and value-adding, not random upsells
- Avoid aggressive language, false scarcity, or recommendations that ignore purchase cycles (e.g., don't suggest another laptop 3 days after purchase)
- Include social proof and use cases rather than just product features

## Output
Provide a complete cross-sell automation plan structured as:

**Product Category Mapping**
- Primary Product → Recommended Cross-Sells (with relationship logic)
- Include 3-5 natural progressions per category

**Automation Sequence Timeline**
- Day X post-purchase: [Action/Message]
- Include 5-7 touchpoints over 30-60 days

**Channel Strategy**
- Primary channel: [Channel + reasoning]
- Secondary channel: [Channel + timing]
- Fallback options: [Alternative approaches]

**Message Templates**
For each touchpoint:
- [Channel]: [Subject/Preview]
- [Body copy focusing on benefit connection]
- [Call-to-action]

**Timing & Frequency Rules**
- Maximum messages per week: X
- Quiet periods: [When to pause]
- Re-engagement triggers: [What restarts sequence]

Focus on engagement metrics and relationship-building, not just conversion rates.
```
