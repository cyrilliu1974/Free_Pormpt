# SEO Competitor Benchmarking Analysis Report Builder

## 簡介

The SEO Competitor Benchmarking Analysis Report Builder is a free AI prompt that examines competitors' search rankings, backlink profiles, and content strategies to surface improvement opportunities for your company. This competitor benchmarking prompt for ChatGPT analyzes target keywords, backlink quality and sources, content formats and publishing frequency, technical SEO indicators, and on-page optimization patterns across your specified competitors. It works with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce a markdown table summarizing each competitor's top keywords, backlink metrics, and content highlights, followed by prioritized action items including keyword gaps, link-building tactics, content topic recommendations, and both quick wins and strategic moves. Marketing teams use it to inform quarterly SEO planning, agencies deploy it for client audits, and in-house strategists run it before launching new campaigns. ● Structures findings into a comparative table showing keyword positions, backlink strength, and content approach side by side ● Identifies keyword gaps where competitors rank but your company does not, surfacing untapped search demand ● Recommends link-building tactics drawn from successful patterns in competitor backlink portfolios ● Delivers specific, measurable actions ranked by impact and effort, not generic advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in competitive analysis and performance benchmarking.

## Task
Analyze competitors' SEO strategies to identify opportunities for improvement in {{company-name}}'s search engine optimization efforts. Conduct comprehensive research using {{seo-tools}} to gather data on {{competitors}}' keywords, backlinks, and content strategies.

## Analysis Scope
For each competitor, examine:
- Target keywords and ranking positions
- Backlink profile (quantity, quality, and sources)
- Content strategies (topics, formats, publishing frequency, engagement)
- Technical SEO indicators
- On-page optimization patterns

## Output
Deliver a structured analysis report containing:

### Competitive Landscape Table
Present findings in a markdown table with columns for:
- Competitor name
- Top keywords
- Backlink metrics
- Content strategy highlights

### Actionable Recommendations
Below the table, provide prioritized recommendations as bullet points that:
- Identify keyword gaps and opportunities
- Suggest link-building tactics based on competitor success
- Recommend content topics and formats to pursue
- Highlight quick wins and long-term strategic moves
- Include specific, measurable actions {{company-name}} can implement
```
