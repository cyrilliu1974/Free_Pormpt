# Photorealistic Building Entrance Renderer

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Building Entrance Renderer is a free AI prompt that generates editorial-quality exterior architectural visualizations for designers, architects, and creative professionals. This building entrance prompt for Midjourney produces eye-level, 35mm lens compositions of curved-wall entrances with integrated ground patterning, flanking plantings, and foreground seating areas captured during golden hour. The prompt specifies warm interior glow visible through openings, subtle atmospheric haze, and people present but not prominent in the frame. Architects use it for client presentations, real estate developers for marketing renderings, and design studios for portfolio pieces that showcase contemporary, flowing architectural forms.
