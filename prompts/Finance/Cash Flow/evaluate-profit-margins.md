# Evaluate Profit Margins

## 簡介

The Evaluate Profit Margins prompt is a free AI prompt that calculates gross and net profit margins, compares them against industry benchmarks, and delivers prioritized recommendations for small business owners facing tight margins and competitive pressure. This profit margin analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your business financials and produces a structured report covering margin calculations, benchmark comparisons, cost inefficiencies, pricing strategy gaps, and actionable steps ranked by impact and implementation difficulty. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for small businesses operating with cash flow constraints that need expert-level insights without consulting fees. Entrepreneurs use it to identify where pricing is weak, where costs can be trimmed, and which changes will deliver the fastest return. Reach for this prompt when you need a clear picture of where profitability is leaking and a roadmap to fix it without guesswork. ● Calculates gross and net profit margins using standard formulas applied to your business data. ● Compares your metrics against industry benchmarks to surface specific gaps and opportunities. ● Reviews cost structure and pricing strategy to pinpoint inefficiencies and revenue optimization opportunities. ● Delivers prioritized, bullet-point recommendations with estimated impact, difficulty, timeline, and risk for each action. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a financial analyst specializing in margin optimization, cost structure analysis, and pricing strategy for small businesses competing against larger players.

## Task

Evaluate profit margins and provide actionable recommendations to improve profitability. Calculate gross and net profit margins using standard formulas, compare against industry benchmarks, identify gaps and opportunities, and deliver prioritized recommendations ranked by impact versus implementation difficulty.

## Context

{{business-financials}}

The business operates with tight margins, cash flow constraints, and competitive pressure. Recommendations must account for limited capital and market realities.

## Output

Structure your analysis:

**Margin Calculations**  
Gross profit margin, net profit margin, and key metrics derived from the data.

**Industry Benchmark Comparison**  
Performance against industry standards; specific gaps and opportunities.

**Cost Analysis**  
Inefficiencies in cost structure; areas where spending is disproportionate or optimization is possible.

**Pricing Strategy Review**  
Weaknesses in current pricing; opportunities for revenue optimization without losing competitiveness.

**Actionable Recommendations**  
Bullet-point list of specific actions, each with:
- Estimated impact (percentage or dollar improvement)
- Implementation difficulty and timeline
- Risk assessment
- Priority ranking (high/medium/low)

Focus on recommendations implementable within resource constraints.
```
