# Transcript to LinkedIn Post Generator

## 簡介

The Transcript to LinkedIn Post Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms spoken insights from transcripts into high-performing LinkedIn posts for content creators and thought leaders. This transcript to LinkedIn post prompt for ChatGPT extracts 10 unique posts from a single transcript, applying the proven Hook-Contrarian-In Reality-Advice-Wrap structure. It preserves the speaker's tone and vocabulary while reformatting ideas into attention-grabbing hooks under 77 characters, contrarian takes that challenge misconceptions, reality-based principles, 3-5 actionable bullet points, and conclusive wrap statements. Each post pulls different themes so your content library stays fresh. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for fast content repurposing. Use this prompt when you have video transcripts, podcast recordings, webinar notes, or interview content that needs to reach your LinkedIn audience in bite-sized, engagement-ready formats. ● Applies the Hook-Contrarian-In Reality-Advice-Wrap framework to every post for consistent engagement ● Extracts 10 distinct insights from one transcript to maximize content output ● Preserves the speaker's tone, vocabulary, and style across all posts ● Formats actionable advice as concise 3-6 word bullet points ● Labels hooks, sections, and post numbers for easy editing and scheduling ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn content strategist who transforms spoken insights into high-performing posts.

## Task
Write 10 LinkedIn posts using the Hook-Contrarian-In Reality-Advice-Wrap framework. Each post must draw its ideas, tone, and language directly from the transcript.

## Context
Transcript:
{{transcript}}

## Framework Structure

**Hook**: One strong, declarative sentence under 77 characters that grabs attention.

**Contrarian**: 1–2 sentences calling out a common misconception on the topic and why it's wrong, plus 1 transition sentence into "In Reality."

**In Reality**: 1–2 sentences explaining the principle, concept, or truth that counters the misconception.

**Advice**: 3–5 actionable bullet points, each 3–6 words, showing how to implement the "In Reality" insight.

**Wrap**: One conclusive sentence—statement, opinion, or question—that sums up the post.

## Output
For each of the 10 posts:
- Match the tone, vocabulary, and style present in the transcript
- Extract different insights or themes so posts don't repeat
- Follow the framework exactly as specified
- Keep hooks punchy and under the character limit
- Make bullet points concrete and actionable

Number each post (Post 1, Post 2, etc.) and label each section clearly.
```
