# Home Inspection Checklist Prompt for Property Assessments

## 簡介

The Home Inspection Checklist Prompt for Property Assessments is a free AI prompt that generates customized residential inspection checklists tailored to specific property characteristics and inspection purposes for real estate professionals, home buyers, property managers, and inspectors. This home inspection checklist prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured assessment workflows organized by major systems: foundation and structural integrity, exterior components like roofing and windows, interior systems including plumbing and electrical, HVAC, and critical safety features such as fire alarms and carbon monoxide detectors. Each checklist includes checkboxes for actionable inspection points, specific guidance on what to evaluate, explanations of why items matter, and red flags that require immediate specialist attention. The prompt adapts to property age and type, highlighting vintage-specific concerns for older homes or modern system considerations for newer construction. Reach for this tool when preparing for pre-purchase inspections, rental property turnovers, seasonal maintenance reviews, or insurance documentation. ● Foundation and structural analysis covering load-bearing elements, cracks, settlement, and integrity issues ● Exterior evaluation including roof condition, siding, gutters, windows, doors, and drainage systems ● Interior systems assessment of plumbing fixtures and pipes, electrical panels and outlets, HVAC function and maintenance needs ● Safety inspection points for fire detection, carbon monoxide protection, hazardous materials, and code compliance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert home inspection consultant specializing in residential property assessment across all construction types and eras.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, customized home inspection checklist tailored to the property's characteristics. Cover all critical areas systematically:

- Foundation and structural integrity
- Exterior components (roof, walls, windows, doors)
- Interior systems (plumbing, electrical, HVAC)
- Safety features (fire alarms, carbon monoxide detectors, hazards)
- Age-specific concerns common to properties of this vintage

Prioritize safety-critical items first, followed by major systems, then maintenance and cosmetic concerns.

## Context
**Property Details:**
{{property-details}}

**Inspection Purpose:**
{{inspection-purpose}}

## Output
Structure the checklist with:

- Clear category headings for each major area
- Checkboxes (- [ ]) for each inspection point to make it actionable and printable
- Specific inspection points: what to look for in each area
- Brief explanations of why each item matters
- Potential red flags that require immediate attention or specialist evaluation

Organize as a printable, step-by-step inspection workflow.
```
