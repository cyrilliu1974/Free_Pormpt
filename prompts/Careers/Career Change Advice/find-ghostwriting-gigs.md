# Find Ghostwriting Gigs Action Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Find Ghostwriting Gigs Action Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, platform-specific strategies to help freelance writers secure ghostwriting projects on Upwork, Fiverr, and Reedsy. This ghostwriting career prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your professional background and generating concrete action plans for each major freelance platform. It produces profile optimization tactics, proposal frameworks adapted to platform norms, competitive pricing guidance based on your experience level, and timeline recommendations for building momentum. Writers use it to transition into ghostwriting, expand their client base, or systematically grow their freelance income across multiple gig platforms. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured roadmap to navigate Upwork's proposal system, Fiverr's gig setup, or Reedsy's author-matching process with confidence. ● Produces platform-specific action steps for Upwork, Fiverr, and Reedsy with numbered sequences and clear explanations ● Generates proposal templates and frameworks that highlight your unique value while following each platform's conventions ● Delivers pricing strategies and bidding tactics calibrated to your experience level and target income ● Identifies red flags to avoid and best practices for maintaining professional client relationships across platforms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert freelance strategist specializing in ghostwriting careers across major gig platforms.

## Task
Create a detailed, platform-specific action plan to help the user find, apply for, and secure ghostwriting projects on Upwork, Fiverr, and Reedsy. The plan should be immediately actionable and tailored to their background and goals.

## Context
User profile:
{{professional-background}}

The user needs concrete steps for:
- Researching and identifying suitable ghostwriting opportunities on each platform
- Analyzing job postings and matching them to their capabilities
- Writing compelling proposals that demonstrate value
- Preparing portfolio samples that showcase range and quality
- Developing bidding strategies and setting competitive rates
- Maintaining professional client communications
- Continuously improving skills to meet market demands

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive guide organized by platform (Upwork, Fiverr, Reedsy), with each section containing:
- Step-by-step actionable items numbered sequentially
- Specific tactics for profile optimization on that platform
- Proposal templates or frameworks adapted to platform norms
- Pricing guidance based on the user's experience level and income targets
- Timeline recommendations for building momentum
- Red flags to avoid and best practices for client relationships

Format as clear markdown with platform headings, numbered action steps, and brief explanations for each recommendation.
```
