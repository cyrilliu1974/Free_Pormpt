# Interior Design Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Interior Design Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic interior design renderings for architects, designers, and homeowners visualizing space transformations. This interior design prompt for Midjourney produces detailed room concepts featuring natural lighting, curated furniture arrangements, textured materials, and cohesive color schemes in any architectural style - from minimalist Scandinavian to industrial loft or classical French. You describe the space (living room, open kitchen, bedroom suite) and specify the design style; the prompt renders a 16:9 composition with attention to material finishes, textile textures, and decorative details. It works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana for client presentations, mood boards, or renovation planning. Reach for this prompt when you need to communicate spatial concepts quickly, explore style directions before committing to purchases, or present design options to stakeholders without full CAD renders. ● Produces wide-angle interior views with natural light behavior, shadow play, and realistic material reflections. ● Maintains design coherence across furniture selection, textile patterns, wall treatments, and accent pieces. ● Supports any room type and design movement - mid-century modern, coastal, maximalist, Japandi, or custom hybrid styles. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images ideal for presentation decks, Pinterest boards, or client approval workflows. ## Prompt

```
Interior design of {{space-description}}, {{design-style}} style, natural lighting, curated furniture and decor, harmonious color palette, attention to texture and materials, --ar 16:9
```
