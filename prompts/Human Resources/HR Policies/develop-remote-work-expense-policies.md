# Remote Work Expense Reimbursement Policy Generator

## 簡介

The Remote Work Expense Reimbursement Policy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compliant, audit-ready expense policies for organizations managing distributed teams. This remote work expense policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces an 11-section structured policy document covering everything from eligible expense categories and reimbursement limits to tax implications and audit procedures. It ensures policies align with IRS Publication 587 guidelines while addressing real-world challenges like regional cost variations, different employee living situations, and changing tax regulations. HR leaders use it to build policies that close loopholes, define clear documentation requirements (receipts, photos, usage logs), and create approval workflows that prevent disputes while reimbursing legitimate expenses fairly. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish or update a remote work expense policy that balances IRS compliance with employee needs across technology, furniture, utilities, internet, supplies, and professional development categories. ● Defines specific expense categories with reimbursement limits organized in clear tables, eliminating ambiguity about what qualifies. ● Specifies exact documentation requirements and step-by-step submission processes so employees know what to provide and when. ● Addresses tax implications for both employer and employee, with compliance sections designed to withstand IRS scrutiny. ● Includes policy exception processes and review mechanisms to adapt to inflation, regulatory changes, and edge cases without constant rewrites. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in remote work expense policy design with deep knowledge of IRS compliance, corporate tax optimization, and equitable reimbursement practices.

## Task

Create a comprehensive remote work expense reimbursement policy that satisfies IRS audit standards, ensures fair treatment across different employee situations, and adapts to changing tax regulations. Close loopholes while reimbursing legitimate expenses with clear categories, limits, documentation requirements, and approval workflows.

## Context

{{company-context}}

The policy must:
- Adhere to current IRS Publication 587 guidelines for home office deductions and employer reimbursements
- Define explicit categories: technology, furniture, utilities, internet, supplies, and professional development
- Set clear reimbursement limits appropriate to regional cost variations and role requirements
- Specify exact documentation needed: receipts, photos, usage logs, manager approvals
- Address tax implications for both employer and employee
- Include review mechanisms to adjust for inflation and changing needs
- Avoid discrimination against different living situations
- Focus on outcomes and productivity rather than prescriptive equipment lists

## Output

Structure the policy document with these sections:

1. **Executive Summary** - Overview of policy intent and key principles
2. **Scope and Applicability** - Who this policy covers
3. **Eligible Expense Categories** - Specific examples and reimbursement limits by category (use tables)
4. **Non-Reimbursable Expenses** - Clear examples of what is excluded
5. **Documentation Requirements** - Exactly what employees must provide
6. **Submission Process** - Step-by-step instructions
7. **Approval Workflow** - Clear decision paths and timelines
8. **Tax Implications** - For both company and employees
9. **Compliance and Audit Procedures** - How the policy will be enforced
10. **Policy Exceptions Process** - Escalation path for edge cases
11. **Appendices** - Forms, templates, and practical examples

Use numbered sections, bullet points for lists, and tables for expense limits. Include concrete examples throughout to eliminate ambiguity.
```
