# Note-Taking Guide Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Note-Taking Guide Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that creates customized study frameworks for learners at any education level. This note-taking guide prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your subject matter and builds a complete study aid with hierarchical headings, bulleted key concepts, visual placeholders for diagrams and charts, and dedicated space for personal reflections. You specify the subject and learning objectives, education level, and preferred note-taking style - whether Cornell, outline, or mind-mapping - and the prompt produces a ready-to-use guide that works for both lecture capture and independent review. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes a techniques section explaining how to maximize retention with the guide. Educators, instructional designers, and students preparing for exams or organizing coursework will find this prompt especially useful when building reusable templates that match specific curriculum needs. ● Organizes content into scannable hierarchies with headings, subheadings, and bulleted lists for rapid review. ● Embeds visual placeholders and simple ASCII diagrams to clarify complex relationships and processes. ● Includes annotation sections so students can add personal examples, questions, and connections during lectures or self-study. ● Provides actionable summarization and recall techniques tailored to the education level, from middle school to graduate coursework. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content developer specializing in note-taking systems and study aids that improve learning outcomes and information retention.

## Task
Design a comprehensive note-taking guide tailored to the specified subject and education level. The guide should:

- Analyze the subject matter and organize information hierarchically with clear headings and subheadings
- Present key concepts, definitions, and examples as concise bullet points
- Include designated space for additional notes and personal reflections
- Incorporate visual elements (diagrams, charts, tables) where they clarify complex topics
- Support both lecture note-taking and independent study workflows
- Provide actionable note-taking techniques and summarization strategies appropriate to the education level

## Context
Subject and scope: {{subject-and-learning-objectives}}

Education level: {{education-level}}

Preferred note-taking style: {{note-taking-style}}

## Output
Deliver the guide in a structured format with:
- Clear hierarchical headings (##, ###)
- Bulleted lists for scannable content
- Designated sections for user annotations
- Visual placeholders or simple ASCII diagrams where helpful
- A techniques section explaining how to use the guide effectively
```
