# Speech Outline Generator for Persuasive Writing

## 簡介

The Speech Outline Generator for Persuasive Writing is a free AI prompt that creates structured, hierarchical speech outlines for speakers, executives, educators, and communicators. This speech outline prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your speech context - whether a keynote, sales pitch, commencement address, or policy briefing - and produces a complete outline with an engaging introduction, well-organized main points supported by evidence and examples, acknowledgment of counterarguments, audience-appropriate persuasive techniques, and a powerful conclusion with a call to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and coherence between sections. Real-world use cases include preparing board presentations, drafting political speeches, building academic lectures, and scripting fundraising pitches where clarity and persuasion are critical. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from a rough idea or topic to a polished, speaker-ready outline that guides your audience through a clear argument. ● Produces hierarchical outlines with Roman numerals, capital letters, and numbered sub-points for easy navigation. ● Analyzes speech context to determine appropriate tone, style, and persuasive techniques. ● Includes transitional elements and counterargument handling to strengthen credibility. ● Generates engaging hooks, credibility-building introductions, and memorable conclusions with calls to action. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech writer specializing in persuasive and impactful communication.

## Task
Create a structured speech outline that effectively communicates the main points and achieves the desired impact. Analyze the speech context to determine appropriate tone, style, and structure, then develop a comprehensive outline with:

- An engaging introduction that hooks the audience and establishes credibility
- Well-organized main points with supporting evidence and examples
- Acknowledgment of potential counterarguments where relevant
- Persuasive techniques tailored to the audience
- A powerful conclusion that reinforces key messages and calls to action

Ensure logical flow and coherence between all sections, using transitional elements that guide the audience through your argument.

## Context
{{speech-context}}

## Output
Deliver the outline using hierarchical formatting:
- Roman numerals (I, II, III) for major sections
- Capital letters (A, B, C) for main points within sections
- Numbers (1, 2, 3) for supporting details and sub-points
```
