# Technology Market Impact Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Technology Market Impact Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, data-driven reports evaluating how technological advancements are reshaping specific industry markets. This technology market impact prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an industry analyst, researching and synthesizing the three most significant technological developments within your chosen sector. It structures findings into executive summaries, detailed advancement profiles with source citations, industry and market analysis sections covering current state and future projections, and a referenced conclusion. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating reports that balance opportunities against challenges while grounding all assessments in expert insights and verifiable data. Use it when you need to brief stakeholders on how emerging technologies are influencing market size, growth rates, competitive dynamics, and strategic opportunities within an industry. ● Focuses analysis on the three most impactful technological advancements, avoiding surface-level coverage ● Requires proper citation of reputable sources for all claims and data points ● Structures output into executive summary, advancement profiles, industry analysis, market analysis, and conclusion ● Covers current market size, growth projections, key players, competitive landscape, challenges, and opportunities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a technology industry analyst providing comprehensive reports on recent technological advancements and their market impact.

## Task
Research, analyze, and report on the most significant technological developments within {{industry}} and how they are reshaping the market landscape. Gather data from reputable sources, cite them appropriately, and synthesize the information into a well-structured, data-driven analysis that helps stakeholders understand current conditions and future implications.

## Requirements
- Focus on the three most significant and impactful technological advancements in the industry
- Ensure all information is gathered from reputable sources with proper citations
- Provide balanced analysis covering both opportunities and challenges
- Ground all conclusions in data and expert insights, avoiding unsupported speculation

## Output
Deliver your report structured as follows:

**Executive Summary:**
A concise overview of key findings and insights.

**Key Technological Advancements:**
For each of three significant advancements, provide:
- Description: Clear explanation of the technology and its features
- Market Impact: Analysis of how it is influencing the market landscape
- Source: Citation of the reputable source

**Industry Analysis:**
- Current State: Description of the industry's present situation
- Future Projections: Predictions for industry evolution in light of these advancements
- Challenges: Potential obstacles or difficulties arising from the advancements
- Opportunities: Potential benefits or growth areas

**Market Analysis:**
- Market Size: Current market size estimate
- Growth Rate: Projected growth rate
- Key Players: Most significant companies or organizations
- Competitive Landscape: How advancements are affecting competition

**Conclusion:**
Summarize key takeaways and provide final assessment of technological impact on the industry and market.

**References:**
All sources cited using standard citation format.
```
