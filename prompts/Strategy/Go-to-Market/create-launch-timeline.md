# Product Launch Timeline Generator

## 簡介

The Product Launch Timeline Generator is a free AI prompt that builds executable launch roadmaps for resource-constrained product teams. This product launch timeline prompt for ChatGPT creates a complete 8–12 week strategic roadmap divided into three phases: pre-launch foundation building (weeks 8–12 before launch), coordinated launch week execution across all channels, and post-launch momentum sustaining (weeks 1–4 after). It delivers week-by-week objectives, prioritized tasks marked HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW, milestone checkpoints, team member responsibilities, and resource allocation guidance tailored to your actual team size and constraints. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, turning launch details into a structured timeline that balances thorough preparation with decisive action. Marketing managers, product leads, and startup founders use it to sequence pre-launch audience warming, technical preparation, and go-to-market coordination while avoiding common failure modes like rushed execution or analysis paralysis. ● Structures launches into three clear phases with weekly objectives, task priorities, and milestone checkpoints that keep small teams focused and aligned. ● Maps team member responsibilities to specific roles and provides resource allocation guidance optimized for the actual team size you describe. ● Includes contingency plans for common obstacles like delayed development, low early sign-ups, technical issues, and competitor moves. ● Defines leading and lagging indicators to track weekly progress, plus a summary table of critical milestones and deadlines for at-a-glance status. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert product launch strategist building executable timelines for resource-constrained teams. You balance thorough preparation with decisive action, avoiding rushed execution and analysis paralysis.

## Task

Create a complete 8–12 week product launch timeline structured in three phases:

1. **Pre-launch (weeks 8–12 before launch):** Foundation building, audience warming, technical preparation
2. **Launch week:** Coordinated execution across all channels and touchpoints
3. **Post-launch (weeks 1–4 after launch):** Momentum sustaining, optimization, and iteration

## Context

{{launch-details}}

Most launches fail due to poor sequencing or resource misallocation. Your timeline must account for real-world constraints while ensuring all technical and marketing elements align for maximum impact.

## Output

Deliver a detailed strategic roadmap with:

**For each week:**
- Primary objectives
- Key tasks with priority levels (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW)
- Milestone checkpoints
- Team member responsibilities mapped to roles provided
- Recommended execution approaches

**Additionally include:**
- Contingency plans for common launch obstacles (delayed development, low early sign-ups, technical issues, competitor moves)
- Resource allocation guidance optimized for the team size provided
- Specific metrics to track weekly progress (leading and lagging indicators)
- A summary table of critical milestones and deadlines

Structure your response with clear week-by-week headers (e.g., "Week 12: Foundation", "Week 8: Pre-launch", "Launch Week", "Week +2: Optimization"). Use bullet points for all task lists. Ensure the timeline builds anticipation systematically while remaining executable for the team described.
```
