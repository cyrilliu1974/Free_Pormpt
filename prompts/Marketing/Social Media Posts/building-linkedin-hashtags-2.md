# LinkedIn Hashtag Generator for Business Visibility

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Hashtag Generator for Business Visibility is a free AI prompt that creates strategic hashtag lists tailored to any business context for B2B marketers and LinkedIn content creators. This LinkedIn hashtag prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and generates 15+ hashtags balanced between broad-reach tags and niche-specific options. The output is a structured markdown table showing each hashtag alongside its relevance to your business and popularity metrics (follower counts and engagement potential). Use it to research trending industry conversations, target professional audiences by pain points, and align hashtag strategy with seasonal or emerging sector trends. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when planning LinkedIn content campaigns, entering new markets, or auditing your current hashtag performance against industry benchmarks. ● Balances high-volume hashtags (500K+ followers) with targeted niche tags for qualified engagement ● Explains the strategic relevance of each hashtag to your specific business context ● Provides popularity assessments and reach potential so you can prioritize by campaign goals ● Delivers 15+ hashtags ordered from broadest appeal to most niche, ready to copy into posts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn marketing expert specializing in hashtag strategy for B2B visibility and engagement.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of 15+ trending and effective LinkedIn hashtags tailored to the specified business context. Balance broad-reach hashtags with niche-specific tags to maximize visibility while targeting the right professional audience.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Research current LinkedIn trends relevant to this business. Consider:
- Industry-specific conversations and topics
- Audience professional interests and pain points
- Seasonal or emerging trends in the sector
- Mix of high-volume general hashtags and targeted niche tags

Evaluate each hashtag for reach potential, engagement likelihood, and strategic alignment with business goals.

## Output
Present as a markdown table with three columns:

| Hashtag | Relevance | Popularity |
|---------|-----------|------------|

**Relevance column**: Explain why this hashtag aligns with the business (1-2 sentences).
**Popularity column**: Assess reach potential (e.g., "High - 500K+ followers" or "Niche - 50K engaged professionals").

Include minimum 15 hashtags ordered from broadest reach to most niche.
```
