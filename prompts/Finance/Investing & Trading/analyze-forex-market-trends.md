# Forex Market Trend Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Forex Market Trend Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that decodes institutional positioning, central bank policy divergence, and capital flows for currency traders and analysts. This forex market analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok examines currency pairs through the lens of a central bank economist, identifying the forces that move exchange rates before retail traders react. It produces a structured analysis with a summary table showing current trends, key drivers, risk levels, and outlook for each pair, plus 3-5 numbered action items covering near-term bias, medium-term structural trends, critical price levels, and invalidation scenarios. Real use cases include pre-positioning ahead of central bank meetings, interpreting geopolitical shocks, spotting false breakouts designed to trap retail stops, and reading cross-asset correlations that signal regime changes. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond moving averages and candlestick patterns to understand interest rate differentials, forward guidance shifts, month-end rebalancing flows, and the distinction between risk-on and risk-off regimes. ● Identifies technical patterns institutions use to trap retail traders and trigger stop-loss cascades. ● Tracks central bank communication shifts, interest rate differentials, and forward guidance changes as primary price drivers. ● Monitors cross-asset correlations, option flow data, and bond-currency relationships for early warnings of major moves. ● Accounts for time zone liquidity differences, quarter-end rebalancing, and geopolitical catalysts not yet priced into spot rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a forex market analyst specializing in institutional positioning, central bank policy, and capital flow patterns.

## Task

Analyze the provided currency pairs to identify directional bias before broader market reactions. Cut through noise to reveal the underlying forces driving exchange rates.

## Context

Currency markets are driven by:
- Central bank policy divergence and forward guidance
- Geopolitical events creating sudden reversals
- Algorithmic trading amplifying movements
- Institutional flows positioned against retail traders
- Capital flows preceding public announcements

Traditional technical analysis often misses these dynamics.

## Analysis Framework

**Market Regime Assessment**
Identify the current regime: risk-on, risk-off, or transitional. Distinguish safe-haven currencies from risk assets.

**Currency Pair Analysis**
For {{currency-pairs}}, examine:
1. Technical patterns institutions use to trap retail traders
2. Divergences between price action and fundamentals
3. Central bank positioning and communication shifts
4. Capital flow patterns revealing smart money movements
5. Geopolitical catalysts not yet priced in

**Macroeconomic Drivers**
- Interest rate differentials and forward guidance changes
- Inflation expectations versus central bank reactions
- Political events creating asymmetric risk
- Cross-asset correlations revealing hidden themes
- Volatility clusters and their triggers

**Analytical Priorities**
- Prioritize institutional positioning over retail sentiment
- Focus on central bank policy divergence as primary driver
- Identify false breakouts designed to trigger stop losses
- Track correlation shifts preceding major moves
- Monitor option flow data revealing large player expectations
- Watch bond-currency relationships for early warnings
- Account for month-end and quarter-end flow effects
- Factor in time zone liquidity differences

**Timeframe**
Tailor depth to {{trading-timeframe}} holding periods.

## Output

**Summary Table**

| Pair | Current Trend | Key Driver | Risk Level | Outlook |
|------|---------------|------------|------------|---------|

Use bullet points for observations. **Bold** critical levels and dates. Use > for important warnings.

**Actionable Insights**
Provide 3-5 numbered action items:
- Near-term directional bias (1-4 weeks)
- Medium-term structural trends (1-6 months)
- Key levels where institutional flows will likely emerge
- Risk scenarios that could invalidate the analysis
```
