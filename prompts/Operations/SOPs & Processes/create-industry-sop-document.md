# Industry SOP Document Generator

## 簡介

The Industry SOP Document Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive Standard Operating Procedure documents for any task or operation across industries. This SOP prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a fully structured procedure document that includes purpose statements, scope definitions, role-based responsibilities, integrated safety guidelines with PPE requirements and hazard identification, sequential procedural steps with verification checkpoints, quality assurance measures, required documentation, and regulatory references. Whether you're documenting chemical handling in manufacturing, patient intake workflows in healthcare, equipment maintenance in facilities management, or food preparation in hospitality, the prompt produces clear, actionable SOPs written in imperative language that frontline personnel can follow. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to the specific regulatory landscape and terminology of your industry. This prompt is for operations managers, safety officers, quality assurance leads, and compliance teams who need to standardize procedures, train new staff, meet audit requirements, or document best practices in a format that satisfies both operational needs and regulatory standards. ● Structures every critical SOP section - purpose, scope, responsibilities, safety, procedures, quality checks, documentation, and references - in a consistent, auditable format. ● Integrates safety throughout the document rather than isolating it, with specific PPE requirements and hazard mitigation tied directly to procedural steps. ● Uses imperative voice and actionable checkpoints so operators know exactly what to do, what to verify, and when to escalate. ● Prompts for industry-specific regulations and standards to ensure compliance references are accurate and version-specific. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert technical writer specializing in operational procedures and safety standards. Create a comprehensive Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) document that is clear, actionable, and compliant with industry requirements.

## Task
Develop a complete SOP document for: {{task-and-industry}}

Include the task/operation name and the industry context (e.g., "Chemical tank cleaning in pharmaceutical manufacturing" or "Patient intake in urgent care clinic").

## Output
Structure the SOP document with these required sections:

# Standard Operating Procedure: [Task/Operation] in [Industry]

## Purpose
State why this SOP exists and what it achieves.

## Scope
Define what this SOP covers and any boundaries or exclusions.

## Responsibilities
List each role involved and their specific duties:
- [Role]: [Responsibilities]

## Safety Guidelines
### General Safety
[Key safety principles and precautions]

### PPE Requirements
[Required personal protective equipment]

### Potential Hazards
[Identified risks and mitigation strategies]

## Procedure
Provide detailed sequential steps:

### Step 1
- Description: [What to do]
- Checkpoint: [Verification or decision point]

[Continue for all necessary steps]

## Quality Assurance
1. [Quality control measure]
2. [Verification method]
3. [Compliance check]

## Documentation
[Forms, logs, or records required]

## References
[Applicable industry standards, regulations, and internal policies]

---

**Writing Guidelines:**
- Use clear, direct language accessible to all personnel who will follow this procedure
- Write steps as imperative statements ("Check the gauge" not "The gauge should be checked")
- Define technical terms when first introduced
- Ensure safety considerations are integrated throughout, not isolated
- Make checkpoints actionable (specific measurements, visual confirmations, sign-offs)
- Cite specific regulation numbers and standard versions where applicable
```
