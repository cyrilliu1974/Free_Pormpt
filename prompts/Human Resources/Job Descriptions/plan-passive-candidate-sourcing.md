# Passive Candidate Sourcing Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Passive Candidate Sourcing Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds targeted sourcing plans to identify and engage hidden talent across non-traditional channels for recruiters and talent acquisition teams. This passive candidate sourcing prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok maps where high-value professionals congregate online beyond job boards - from GitHub and Stack Overflow to Slack communities, conference rosters, and open-source contribution graphs - then delivers platform-specific Boolean search strings, access methods, and relationship-first outreach frameworks. It performs competitive intelligence to understand where top performers in your target role engage professionally, then creates engagement tactics that provide value and respect passive status. Use it when building a talent pipeline for hard-to-fill technical, specialized, or executive roles where active candidates are scarce. ● Identifies professional communities, technical forums, academic platforms, and industry-specific channels where top talent is active but not job-seeking. ● Provides Boolean search strings tailored to each platform that surface candidates by skills, experience markers, and digital behaviors rather than job titles. ● Delivers engagement frameworks and outreach messaging that build authentic relationships and respect professional boundaries. ● Structures output with clear headings and bullet points for each channel, making every tactic immediately actionable for your recruiting team. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert talent sourcing strategist specializing in passive candidate identification, Boolean search techniques, and non-traditional talent channel mapping.

## Task
Create a comprehensive passive candidate sourcing strategy that uncovers hidden talent through digital footprint analysis and targeted engagement. Deliver a detailed action plan identifying where high-value professionals congregate online beyond traditional job boards, with specific search strings and outreach tactics.

## Context
Target role, industry, and sourcing landscape:
{{sourcing-context}}

## Approach
Begin with competitive intelligence to understand where top performers engage professionally. Map relevant channels including:
- Professional communities (Slack, Discord groups)
- Technical forums (Stack Overflow, GitHub, GitLab)
- Academic publications and preprint servers
- Conference circuits and speaking rosters
- Industry-specific platforms and newsletters
- Open source contribution graphs

For each channel, develop Boolean search strings that identify candidates based on skills, experience markers, and digital behaviors rather than active job seeking. Create engagement frameworks that provide value first, respect professional boundaries, and build long-term pipeline relationships.

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings for each channel category. For each channel provide:
- Specific Boolean search strings tailored to the platform
- Platform recommendations and access methods
- Engagement tactics that respect passive candidate status
- Outreach messaging frameworks
- Pipeline relationship-building strategies

Format all tactical elements as bullet points for immediate implementation.
```
