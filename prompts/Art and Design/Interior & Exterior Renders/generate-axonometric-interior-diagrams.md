# Axonometric Interior Diagram Generator

## 簡介

The Axonometric Interior Diagram Generator is a free AI prompt that produces technical architectural diagrams of complete dwelling interiors viewed from above in true axonometric projection for architects, interior designers, and design students. This axonometric interior diagram prompt for Midjourney creates CAD-quality line drawings that show all rooms with walls at full height and the roof removed, maintaining a 45-degree angle with no perspective distortion or vanishing points. You specify the dwelling type (apartment, townhouse, single-family home), describe the room layout and adjacencies, and choose an accent color to highlight select elements. The output is a clean, monochrome technical diagram with clear line-weight hierarchy - thick outer walls, medium interior partitions, and thin furniture - suitable for portfolio presentations, client reviews, and design documentation. Architects use it to visualize spatial relationships and circulation patterns without manual drafting, while interior designers apply it to communicate furniture arrangements and room programs in early-phase work. ● True axonometric projection with uniform wall thickness and no perspective distortion, maintaining architectural accuracy across the entire drawing. ● Customizable room layouts, dwelling types, and accent colors to match specific design projects and client requirements. ● CAD-quality line hierarchy and minimal flat fills that produce readable, professional diagrams without clutter or unnecessary detail. ● White background with generous margins, ready for insertion into presentation decks, reports, and design portfolios. ## Prompt

```
Create a clean axonometric architectural diagram of a complete {{dwelling-type}} interior viewed from above at a 45-degree angle. Show all rooms with walls at full height, roof removed. Use true axonometric projection with no perspective distortion or vanishing points.

**Spatial Layout**: {{room-layout}}

**Drawing Style**: CAD-quality line drawing with clear hierarchy—outer walls thick, interior partitions medium weight, furniture and fixtures thin. Monochrome palette with {{accent-color}} highlights on select elements. Minimal flat fills, no textures or shading. White background with generous margins.

**Technical Requirements**: Uniform wall thickness, furniture at realistic residential scale, clear spatial separation between rooms. Medium precision linework—readable and clean, not cluttered. Portfolio-quality technical diagram suitable for architectural presentation.

--ar 3:4 --style raw --q 2

Avoid: perspective view, photorealistic rendering, heavy shading, textures, dramatic lighting, sketchy linework, clutter, text, logos, watermarks
```
