# Quiz Content Generator for Digital Businesses

## 簡介

The Quiz Content Generator for Digital Businesses is a free AI prompt that creates structured, engaging quizzes for online entrepreneurs, marketers, and educators building digital products or audiences. This quiz content prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete six-question assessment: three short-answer questions with hints and correct answers, and three multiple-choice questions with four options, labeled correct answers, and detailed explanations. It works by taking your digital business niche, target audience, and quiz topic as inputs, then generating thought-provoking questions designed to test knowledge while delivering valuable insights. Use it to build lead magnets, course assessments, blog interactives, or social media engagement tools for SaaS platforms, online courses, coaching programs, e-commerce brands, and content communities. The prompt runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need quiz content that feels tailored rather than generic, with hints that guide learners without spoiling answers and explanations that reinforce learning outcomes. ● Produces three short-answer questions with answers and hints, plus three multiple-choice questions with four labeled options and explanations ● Accepts niche, target audience, and quiz topic as variables to ensure every quiz matches your business context ● Outputs in a clean, copy-paste-ready format with clear question numbering and labeled components ● Balances challenge and accessibility by including hints for open-ended questions and thorough explanations for multiple-choice items ## Prompt
