# Feature Story Outline Generator for Magazines

## 簡介

The Feature Story Outline Generator for Magazines is a free AI prompt that creates structured, publication-ready narrative outlines for journalists, writers, and editors working on long-form feature articles. This feature story outline prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to build a six-section narrative framework following classic story structure: hook, background, rising action, climax, falling action, and resolution. You provide the subject and target magazine, and the prompt generates a complete outline with title, subtitle, attention-grabbing opening, character background, conflict development, pivotal moments, and satisfying conclusion. It emphasizes vivid descriptions, dialogue placement, emotional depth, and thematic coherence. The output works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for professional storytelling needs. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize complex human-interest stories, profile pieces, or investigative narratives before drafting. ● Structures stories around classic six-part narrative arc from hook through resolution ● Prompts for dialogue placement, character motivation, and thematic depth ● Includes title and subtitle generation tailored to your target publication ● Maintains narrative coherence by tracking relationships between story elements throughout each section ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert feature story writer skilled in crafting compelling narratives with strong structural coherence.

## Task
Create a detailed feature story outline for {{subject}} to be published in {{magazine}}. Structure the outline as a classic narrative arc with vivid descriptions, dialogue, and insight into the subject's thoughts and motivations.

## Output
Deliver the outline in the following format:

**Title:** An engaging, relevant title that captures the essence of the story

**Subtitle:** A brief, compelling subtitle that further entices the reader

**Section 1: Hook**
- Attention-grabbing opening line or anecdote
- Establish the main theme or conflict
- Introduce the subject and their unique circumstances

**Section 2: Background**
- Relevant background information on the subject
- Origins, upbringing, and formative experiences
- Key moments or turning points that shaped their path

**Section 3: Rising Action**
- Main challenges, obstacles, or opportunities faced
- Vivid descriptions and dialogue that bring the story to life
- Build tension and anticipation leading to the climax

**Section 4: Climax**
- The pivotal moment or decision that defines the story
- Emotional and practical implications of this turning point
- Insight into the subject's thoughts, feelings, and motivations

**Section 5: Falling Action**
- Consequences and aftermath of the climactic event
- How the subject adapts, grows, or changes as a result
- Relevant themes or lessons learned

**Section 6: Resolution**
- Satisfying conclusion to the story
- Reflection on the subject's journey and impact
- Lasting impression or thought-provoking message for the reader

Focus on creating cohesive narrative flow, maintaining the relationship between story elements throughout.
```
