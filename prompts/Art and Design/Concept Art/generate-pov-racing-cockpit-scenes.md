# POV Racing Cockpit Scene Generator

## 簡介

The POV Racing Cockpit Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-realistic first-person racing photography from the driver's seat for concept artists, game designers, and motorsport content creators. This racing cockpit prompt for Midjourney produces immersive driver-perspective images showing gloved hands gripping a steering wheel, LED-lit dashboards with telemetry data, carbon fiber cockpit details, and motion-blurred race tracks stretching toward dramatic sunset horizons. You configure the vehicle type (Formula 1, rally car, GT racer), driving environment (circuit, desert, mountain pass), and lighting condition to generate high-speed action shots with cinematic composition and professional motorsport photography aesthetics. It runs on Midjourney with optimized chaos, stylization, and style-reference parameters for consistent ultra-detailed output.
