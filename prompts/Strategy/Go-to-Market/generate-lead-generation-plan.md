# Lead Generation Plan Prompt for Marketing Strategy

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Plan Prompt for Marketing Strategy is a free AI prompt that builds a complete, data-driven lead generation strategy tailored to any business context. This lead generation prompt for ChatGPT walks through five core pillars: target audience analysis (ideal customer profile, pain points, desires), lead magnet strategy (type, topic, and capture mechanism), multi-channel outreach (email sequences, LinkedIn targeting and templates, content marketing themes, and paid ad guidelines), lead nurturing (scoring criteria, nurture sequences, and sales handoff rules), and KPIs with optimization levers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured plan with concrete steps rather than generic advice. Marketing teams use it to map out acquisition campaigns, sales leaders rely on it to align lead scoring with CRM workflows, and founders apply it to launch their first systematic outreach. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured blueprint that ties together paid, owned, and earned channels into a single, measurable lead-gen system. ● Produces ideal customer profiles, pain points, and desires grounded in the business context you provide. ● Recommends lead magnet types, topics, and capture mechanisms matched to your audience. ● Maps email campaign structures, LinkedIn connection templates, content themes, and paid ad targeting parameters across all major channels. ● Defines lead scoring criteria, nurturing sequences, and clear handoff thresholds so marketing and sales stay aligned. ● Lists key metrics, optimization levers, and experimentation processes to iterate and improve performance over time. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing strategist specializing in lead generation and client acquisition. Develop a comprehensive, data-driven lead generation plan tailored to the business described below.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable multi-channel lead generation plan organized into five sections:

**1. Target Audience Analysis**
- Ideal Customer Profile
- Key Pain Points
- Key Desires

**2. Lead Magnet Strategy**
- Lead Magnet Type
- Lead Magnet Topic
- Lead Capture Mechanism

**3. Multichannel Outreach Plan**
- **Email Campaign:** sequence structure, key messaging
- **LinkedIn Outreach:** targeting criteria, connection request template, engagement tactics
- **Content Marketing:** content themes, formats, promotion channels
- **Paid Advertising:** ad platforms, targeting parameters, creative guidelines

**4. Lead Nurturing Process**
- Lead Scoring Criteria
- Nurturing Sequence
- Sales Handoff Criteria

**5. KPIs and Optimization**
- Key Metrics
- Optimization Levers
- Experimentation Process

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Requirements
- Focus on high-quality leads most likely to convert into customers or partners
- Use data-driven insights to inform targeting, messaging, and optimization
- Provide specific, actionable recommendations tailored to this business
- Ensure each section includes concrete steps, not generic advice

## Output
Present the plan using clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for easy navigation and implementation.
```
