# Outdoor Fashion Editorial Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Outdoor Fashion Editorial Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates sun-drenched, cinematic fashion images styled after editorial photography with film-grain texture and vibrant warm color palettes. Built for image models like Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, this outdoor fashion prompt for ChatGPT and AI image generators produces shots that combine specific wardrobe details (orange jackets, pink hats, tie-dye dresses, statement accessories) with shallow depth-of-field, Kodak Gold 400 film emulation, and Guy Aroch editorial framing. You control the shot type (close-up, full-body, three-quarter), subject description, pose and expression, and background setting (palm-lined streets, residential backdrops, urban summer scenes). Use it when you need fashion content that looks editorially composed rather than snapshot-casual, or when a client brief calls for warm-toned, sun-soaked looks with recognizable film-photography aesthetics. ● Produces images with warm wardrobe palettes (orange, pink, red, tie-dye) and statement accessories (sunglasses, hats, bold lipstick) that read as intentionally styled. ● Emulates Kodak Gold 400 film grain and shallow depth-of-field for a professional editorial finish. ● Lets you specify shot type, subject details, pose and expression, and outdoor setting variables to match creative briefs. ● References Guy Aroch's editorial photography style for color-saturated, sunlit, fashion-forward composition. ## Prompt

```

```
