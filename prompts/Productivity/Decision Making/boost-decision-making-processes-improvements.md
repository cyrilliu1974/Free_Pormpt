# Decision-Making Process Improvement Prompt

## 簡介

The Decision-Making Process Improvement Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers actionable, evidence-based recommendations to strengthen decision-making processes for organizations of any size or industry. This decision-making prompt for ChatGPT asks the model to act as an expert consultant specializing in organizational behavior and strategic planning. You provide your organizational context, and the prompt returns 8-12 specific recommendations covering process frameworks, cognitive bias mitigation, data and technology integration, team communication, and documentation systems. Each recommendation includes clear implementation guidance and anticipated challenges. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured output with an introduction, detailed recommendations, and a summary of cumulative impact. Reach for this prompt when you need to audit and improve how your team or organization makes decisions, whether at the individual contributor level or for group strategic planning. ● Addresses both individual and group decision-making scenarios with process frameworks and structural improvements. ● Identifies cognitive biases and provides specific mitigation strategies rooted in organizational behavior research. ● Recommends data-driven and technology integration approaches that fit your organizational context. ● Includes implementation guidance and highlights common barriers for each recommendation. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert decision-making consultant specializing in organizational behavior, strategic planning, and process improvement.

## Task
Provide actionable, evidence-based recommendations to improve decision-making processes within the user's organization. Address both individual and group decision-making, leveraging technology and data-driven approaches where applicable.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

Consider common decision-making biases, implementation barriers, and recommendations that work across different timescales (short-term quick wins and long-term structural improvements).

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

1. **Introduction** (2-3 sentences): Explain why effective decision-making processes matter for organizational performance.

2. **Recommendations** (bullet list): Provide 8-12 specific, implementable recommendations covering:
   - Process frameworks and structures
   - Cognitive bias mitigation
   - Data and technology integration
   - Team dynamics and communication
   - Documentation and learning systems

   For each recommendation:
   - State the action clearly
   - Explain its benefit and implementation approach (1-2 sentences)
   - Note any key challenges to anticipate

3. **Summary** (2-3 sentences): Emphasize the cumulative impact of these improvements on organizational outcomes.

Ensure all recommendations are evidence-based, avoid generic advice, and tailor guidance to the organizational context provided.
```
