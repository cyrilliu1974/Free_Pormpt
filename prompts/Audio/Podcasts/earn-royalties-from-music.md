# Music Royalty Earnings Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Music Royalty Earnings Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, actionable roadmaps for artists to maximize their streaming and licensing income. This music royalty prompt for ChatGPT works by taking an artist's genre, career stage, location, and revenue goals and producing a structured guide that covers performing rights organization (PRO) registration, mechanical and performance royalties, neighboring rights, streaming platform optimization, sync licensing pitches, and emerging digital revenue streams. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering platform-specific tactics for Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and licensing opportunities in film, TV, advertising, and games. Musicians use it to build registration checklists, understand which royalties they're missing, craft playlist pitching strategies, and identify sync opportunities aligned with their genre and market. Reach for this prompt when you need a concrete, step-by-step plan to grow music income beyond album sales-whether you're an independent artist navigating PRO registration for the first time or an established act exploring new licensing revenue. ● Produces registration checklists for performing rights organizations, mechanical rights agencies, and neighboring rights societies ● Breaks down mechanical, performance, synchronization, and neighboring rights royalties with collection instructions ● Delivers streaming optimization tactics including playlist pitching, metadata strategies, and platform-specific release scheduling ● Identifies sync licensing opportunities across film, TV, advertising, podcasts, video games, and user-generated content platforms ● Highlights emerging revenue streams like NFTs, direct fan platforms, and micro-licensing ● Organizes all guidance into immediately actionable steps tailored to the artist's genre, career stage, and geographic market ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert music industry consultant specializing in royalty structures, streaming economics, and music licensing.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for artists to earn royalties through streaming platforms and music licensing. Cover registration with performing rights organizations, different royalty types (mechanical, performance, synchronization, neighboring rights), streaming optimization strategies, and licensing opportunities across traditional and emerging digital revenue streams.

## Context
Artist profile:
- Genre and career stage: {{artist-profile}}
- Geographic location: {{location}}
- Primary streaming platforms and licensing goals: {{revenue-targets}}

The guide should address both foundational steps (PRO registration, rights management, distribution setup) and growth strategies (playlist placement, licensing pitches, royalty collection optimization). Include platform-specific tactics where relevant and highlight opportunities specific to the artist's genre and market.

## Output
Structure the guide with clear headings, subheadings, and actionable bullet points. Organize by:
1. Rights registration and administration
2. Understanding royalty types and collection
3. Streaming platform optimization
4. Licensing opportunities (sync, film/TV, advertising, games)
5. Emerging revenue streams
6. Monitoring and maximizing collections

Make every recommendation concrete and immediately actionable.
```
