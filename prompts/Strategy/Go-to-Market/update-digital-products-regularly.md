# Digital Product Update and Launch Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Digital Product Update and Launch Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates detailed go-to-market plans for digital product managers preparing to update and launch products. This digital product launch prompt for ChatGPT walks you through the complete lifecycle: pre-launch preparation and product readiness, customer communication strategies, multi-channel marketing tactics, launch execution and monitoring, and post-launch optimization with feedback loops. Built for text models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it takes your product details and business parameters as input and returns a structured, numbered plan tailored to maximize customer satisfaction and market penetration. Real teams use it to coordinate cross-functional launches, align stakeholder messaging, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks during critical update cycles. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework for product launches or when onboarding new product managers who need to understand the full scope of a coordinated release. ● Structures your plan into five major phases with clear headings and numbered steps for execution clarity ● Covers customer engagement tactics, multi-channel promotion, and resource allocation within your marketing budget ● Incorporates post-launch monitoring and feedback loops to iterate and improve after release ● Customizes output based on your specific product, target audience, and business constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital product manager specializing in product updates and launches.

## Task
Create a comprehensive strategy for updating and launching a digital product. Develop a detailed plan that covers the entire process from product update to market launch, focusing on maximizing customer satisfaction and market penetration. Include strategies for leveraging various platforms and resources to promote the updated product effectively.

## Context
{{product-and-audience}}

{{business-parameters}}

## Output
Provide your strategy as a numbered list with clear headings for each major step in the process. Structure the plan to cover:
- Pre-launch preparation and product readiness
- Customer communication and engagement
- Multi-channel marketing and promotion tactics
- Launch execution and monitoring
- Post-launch optimization and feedback loops
```
