# Course Discount Campaign Planner for Online Education

## 簡介

The Course Discount Campaign Planner for Online Education is a free AI prompt that builds conversion-focused promotional campaigns to drive enrollment and urgency for online courses. This course discount campaign prompt for ChatGPT takes your course details, target audience, campaign parameters (discount percentage, duration, enrollment caps), and unique selling points, then outputs a complete multi-channel marketing strategy organized by platform. Each channel receives tailored messaging, specific content formats, urgency mechanisms, calls-to-action, and timing recommendations prioritized by expected ROI. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable campaign plans covering email marketing, social media, paid advertising, landing pages, partnerships, and retargeting. Course creators, online education marketers, and digital strategists use this prompt when launching limited-time offers that need to convert browsers into enrolled students across multiple touchpoints. ● Develops platform-specific value propositions and messaging tailored to each marketing channel's audience and format ● Creates urgency mechanisms and conversion-focused calls-to-action that drive immediate enrollment decisions ● Prioritizes channels and tactics by expected return on investment and implementation difficulty ● Structures output as an actionable bullet-point campaign plan covering email, social media, paid ads, landing pages, partnerships, and retargeting ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in conversion-focused promotional campaigns for online education.

## Task
Create a limited-time discount campaign that drives enrollment and urgency for an online course. Develop a clear value proposition, attention-grabbing promotional content, an enticing offer structure, and a multi-channel marketing strategy.

## Context
**Course & Audience:**
{{course-and-audience}}
(Include: course topic, target audience demographics/psychographics, what problem it solves)

**Campaign Parameters:**
{{campaign-offer}}
(Include: discount percentage, campaign duration, any enrollment caps or bonus incentives)

**Differentiation:**
{{unique-selling-points}}
(List 3-5 factors that set this course apart from competitors—instructor credentials, outcomes, methodology, community, etc.)

## Output
Provide a comprehensive campaign plan organized by marketing channel. For each channel, include:
- Core messaging and value proposition tailored to that platform
- Specific content formats and creative angles
- Urgency mechanisms and calls-to-action
- Timing and frequency recommendations

Structure your response as a bullet-point list grouped by channel:
- Email Marketing
- Social Media (specify platforms)
- Paid Advertising
- Website/Landing Page
- Partnerships/Affiliates
- Retargeting

Prioritize channels and tactics by expected ROI and ease of implementation.
```
