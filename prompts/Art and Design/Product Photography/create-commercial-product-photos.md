# Commercial Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Commercial Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates studio-quality product images with professional lighting, clean composition, and accurate color rendering for brands, e-commerce sellers, and marketers. This product photography prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces commercial-grade images by combining your product specification with custom scene and styling variables - delivering sharp focus on the subject, shallow depth of field on background elements, balanced framing, and vibrant accurate colors that match real-world studio shoots. Whether you're preparing hero images for an online store, building ad creative, or mocking up packaging concepts, the prompt handles lighting and composition so you can iterate faster than traditional photography workflows. Reach for this prompt when you need repeatable, on-brand product visuals without booking studio time or hiring a photographer. ● Produces sharp, focused product shots with shallow depth of field that highlights the subject and softly blurs background styling elements. ● Applies professional studio lighting and balanced framing automatically, replicating the look of commercial photography without manual setup. ● Accepts any product type and scene-and-styling combination, from cosmetics on marble pedestals to electronics in minimalist environments. ● Outputs vibrant, color-accurate images suitable for e-commerce listings, social media ads, pitch decks, and print catalogs. ## Prompt

```
Product photography of {{product}}, {{scene-and-styling}}. Clean commercial composition with balanced framing, professional studio lighting, vibrant accurate colors, sharp focus on product with shallow depth of field on background elements.
```
