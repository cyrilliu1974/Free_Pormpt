# Affiliate Partner Research and Evaluation Prompt

## 簡介

The Affiliate Partner Research and Evaluation Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies, analyzes, and prioritizes potential affiliate partners based on brand alignment, audience overlap, and engagement metrics for digital marketers and affiliate program managers. This affiliate research prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured five-step evaluation process: compiling active affiliates in your niche, analyzing content quality and audience demographics, gathering contact information, scoring brand-value alignment, and ranking partners by partnership potential. It outputs a spreadsheet-ready table with affiliate names, websites, contact details, and detailed notes including alignment scores, audience size indicators, personalized outreach angles, and priority rankings. Use it when launching an affiliate program, expanding partner networks, or vetting potential collaborators to ensure strategic fit before outreach. ● Produces a prioritized table of 10-15 qualified affiliates sorted by partnership potential and brand alignment ● Includes alignment scores with justification, audience engagement data, and priority rankings for each candidate ● Generates personalized outreach angles based on content themes and audience interests for higher conversion rates ● Delivers contact information and partnership-ready insights in a spreadsheet format for immediate action ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert digital marketer specializing in affiliate marketing and partner identification.

## Task

Identify and evaluate potential affiliates who align with the specified brand values and target audience. Provide a prioritized list with actionable outreach insights formatted as a spreadsheet.

## Context

Niche: {{niche}}

Brand values: {{brand-values}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Process

1. Research and compile potential affiliates active in the niche
2. Analyze each affiliate's website, content quality, audience demographics, and engagement to assess brand alignment
3. Gather contact information (email, social handles, partnership pages)
4. Evaluate partnership fit based on audience overlap, content authenticity, and brand value alignment
5. Prioritize affiliates by partnership potential (reach, engagement, alignment score)
6. Note personalized outreach angles for each affiliate based on their content themes and audience interests

## Output

Deliver a spreadsheet-formatted table with these columns:

**AFFILIATE NAME** | **WEBSITE** | **CONTACT INFORMATION** | **NOTES**

In the NOTES column, include:
- Alignment score (High/Medium/Low) with justification
- Audience size and engagement indicators
- Personalized outreach angle
- Partnership priority ranking

List at least 10-15 qualified affiliates, sorted by priority (highest first).
```
