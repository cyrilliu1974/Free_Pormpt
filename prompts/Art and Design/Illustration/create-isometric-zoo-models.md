# Isometric Zoo Model Generator for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Isometric Zoo Model Generator is a free AI prompt that creates miniature zoo scenes rendered in isometric perspective for digital illustrators, game designers, and 3D artists. This isometric zoo prompt for Midjourney produces a floating square platform populated with individual animal enclosures, trees, walking paths, and tiny visitors, all rendered with clean geometry and subtle shadows. You specify the animals to feature, choose a color palette that sets the visual tone, and define the atmosphere - whether cozy, mysterious, vibrant, or serene. The prompt applies a chaos value of 33 for organic variation, outputs at 3:4 aspect ratio, and uses stylization 400 to balance artistic interpretation with structural clarity.
