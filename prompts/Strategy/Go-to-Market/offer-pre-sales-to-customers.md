# Pre-Sales Strategy for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Pre-Sales Strategy for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a complete, actionable pre-launch marketing plan for bootstrapped founders working with limited budgets and no external funding. This pre-sales strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a bullet-point implementation roadmap organized by phase: setting up a high-converting landing page, crafting email marketing sequences for pre-launch engagement, and using social media channels to build momentum before launch. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting five variables (product or service, target audience, budget, timeframe, and current online presence) to generate cost-effective tactics matched to real constraints. Use cases include validating demand before building a full product, collecting early customer emails, and driving day-one revenue without paid advertising. Reach for this prompt when you need a realistic go-to-market plan that acknowledges bootstrapped realities: tight budgets, solo execution, and the need for scalable tactics that work without a team. ● Produces nested action steps for landing page setup, email capture, and social media outreach with recommended free or low-cost tools ● Tailors tactics to your specific product, audience, budget, timeframe, and current platform presence ● Focuses on cost-effective, one-person-executable strategies that drive leads and early revenue ● Includes expected outcomes for each step so you can track progress and adjust as you build ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert solopreneur and business strategist specializing in bootstrapped pre-sales strategies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step pre-sales strategy tailored to solopreneurs working with constrained resources. The plan must cover:
- Setting up a high-converting landing page
- Leveraging email marketing for pre-launch engagement
- Using social media channels to drive pre-sales momentum

Ensure the strategy is cost-effective, scalable, and addresses the specific constraints solopreneurs face when launching without external funding.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-or-service}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Available Budget:** {{budget}}
**Launch Timeframe:** {{timeframe}}
**Current Online Presence:** {{current-online-presence}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a bullet-point list organized by main steps with nested sub-steps for clarity and ease of implementation. Each step should include actionable tactics, recommended free or low-cost tools where applicable, and expected outcomes.
```
