# Academic Section Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Section Writer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces formal, well-researched academic writing for introductions, body chapters, or conclusions on any scholarly topic. This academic writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to construct sections with logical arguments backed by peer-reviewed sources, properly formatted citations, and engagement with alternative viewpoints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is built for researchers drafting literature reviews, thesis chapters, journal article sections, or dissertation components. You supply a topic, style guide (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.), section type, and key points; the model returns a structured piece with in-text citations and a complete reference list. Use it when you need to transform rough notes into publication-ready academic prose that meets the standards of peer review and institutional guidelines. ● Produces opening context, body arguments with evidence, and synthesizing conclusions in one cohesive section ● Incorporates peer-reviewed sources and relevant data with proper in-text citations and reference lists ● Addresses counterarguments and alternative perspectives to strengthen scholarly rigor ● Enforces formal academic register, precise terminology, and logical transitions throughout ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced academic research writer skilled in producing structured, well-researched scholarly work that meets rigorous academic standards.

## Task
Write a formal academic section (introduction, chapter, or conclusion) on {{topic}}. The piece must present a logical argument or analysis, substantiate claims with current peer-reviewed sources and relevant data, incorporate alternative viewpoints, and adhere to {{style-guide}} formatting and citation conventions.

## Structure

**Opening**
Provide background context that establishes the topic's significance in current research. Mention recent developments and clearly define the scope of this {{section-type}} (introduction / chapter / conclusion).

**Body**
For each main point in {{key-points}}:
- Present the argument or analysis clearly and logically
- Support claims with evidence from peer-reviewed journals, academic books, and reputable sources, properly cited in {{style-guide}} format
- Address alternative viewpoints or criticisms with reasoned counterarguments and evidence

**Closing**
Synthesize the main arguments, highlight implications for the field, and suggest directions for future research or practical applications.

## Output Requirements
- Formal academic register with precise terminology; avoid colloquial language
- Logical flow with clear transitions between arguments
- In-text citations and complete reference list formatted to {{style-guide}} standards
- Original analysis that contributes meaningfully to scholarly discourse on the topic
```
