# Long-Form Sales Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Long-Form Sales Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates narrative-driven sales copy for brands looking to convert readers into customers through storytelling. This long-form sales copy prompt for ChatGPT structures your copy using dependency grammar principles - where each sentence builds logically from the previous one to create narrative momentum. You provide your brand overview, target audience, and desired call-to-action, and the prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok through five distinct sections: an emotional opening hook, brand introduction with core values, a transformation journey addressing pain points, social proof woven naturally into the narrative, and a compelling invitation to act. The result is coherent, emotionally engaging sales copy that maintains consistent voice while building toward conversion. Use this prompt when you need persuasive website copy, landing pages, sales letters, or email sequences that tell your brand story rather than simply listing features. ● Structures copy in five sections - hook, brand intro, transformation journey, proof, and call-to-action - for logical narrative flow ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure each paragraph builds coherently from the last with clear hierarchical relationships ● Integrates brand values, audience pain points, product benefits, and trust signals within a unified story ● Maintains consistent tone and uses emotive, sensory language to foster emotional connection and drive action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in long-form sales narratives that convert readers into customers.

## Task
Craft a compelling brand story in narrative format that introduces core values and unique selling proposition, addresses audience pain points, highlights benefits, and builds emotional connection leading to action.

## Context
Use dependency grammar principles to structure the copy—establish clear hierarchical relationships between ideas, with each sentence building logically from the previous one. Main clauses carry primary brand messages; dependent clauses add supporting detail, emotion, and proof. This creates coherent flow and narrative momentum.

## Output
Deliver the sales copy as a structured narrative with clear sections:

1. **Opening Hook** – Establish emotional connection by reflecting the audience's current situation or aspiration
2. **Brand Introduction** – Present core values and unique positioning in story form
3. **The Journey** – Show how the product/service transforms the customer's experience, addressing specific pain points and delivering concrete benefits
4. **Social Proof & Credibility** – Weave in trust signals naturally within the narrative
5. **The Invitation** – Clear, compelling call-to-action that feels like the natural next step

Maintain consistent voice and tone aligned with the brand identity throughout. Use emotive, sensory language. Show dependency relationships by ensuring each paragraph flows logically from the last, with transitional elements that create narrative coherence.
```
