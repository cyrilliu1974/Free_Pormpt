# Content Refresh Prompt for SEO and Article Updates

## 簡介

The Content Refresh Prompt for SEO and Article Updates is a free AI prompt that audits existing articles, integrates fresh data, and delivers structured revision plans for content strategists and SEO professionals. This content refresh prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a five-step process: auditing the current structure for gaps, researching the latest statistics and trends, weaving new information into the existing narrative, optimizing readability and keyword placement, and recommending expansions that boost comprehensiveness. It outputs a complete markdown article structure with refreshed introduction, updated body sections enriched with current case studies and data points, a revised conclusion, and a practical recommendations list covering content gaps and SEO opportunities. Runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when published content has aged, search rankings have declined, or you need to integrate breaking trends without rewriting from scratch. It preserves brand voice while injecting relevance. ● Audits existing article structure to identify outdated sections, gaps, and expansion opportunities ● Researches and integrates the latest statistics, case studies, and industry trends relevant to your topic ● Optimizes sentence structure, header hierarchy, keyword placement, and semantic relevance for search engines ● Outputs a fully revised article in markdown format plus a recommendations section for further improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in article refresh and SEO optimization.

## Task
Update an existing article with current data, trends, and insights to improve search rankings and maintain relevance. Deliver a fully revised article structure with actionable recommendations.

## Context
Article topic and scope: {{article-topic}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
SEO and length requirements: {{seo-requirements}}

## Process
1. **Audit the existing structure** – Identify gaps, outdated sections, and opportunities for deeper coverage
2. **Research current data** – Gather the latest statistics, case studies, trends, and examples relevant to the topic
3. **Integrate new information** – Weave updates into the existing content while preserving tone and voice consistency
4. **Optimize for search** – Incorporate target keywords naturally, improve readability (sentence length, formatting, headers), and enhance semantic relevance
5. **Recommend expansions** – Suggest additional sections, subtopics, or angles that increase comprehensiveness and user value

## Output
Deliver the updated article in a structured format with clear markdown headings:

### Introduction
[Refreshed hook and overview]

### Main Body
#### [Subsection 1]
[Updated content with new data and insights]

#### [Subsection 2]
[Updated content with new data and insights]

[Additional subsections as needed]

### Conclusion
[Synthesized takeaways and call-to-action]

### Recommendations
- Additional sections to consider
- SEO improvement opportunities
- Content gaps to address
```
