# Identify Critical Compliance Gaps

## 簡介

The Identify Critical Compliance Gaps prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes your business context and surfaces the seven most dangerous policy compliance failures that can destroy small businesses, complete with practical, resource-efficient solutions. This compliance gap prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured assessment covering why these gaps pose existential risk, the root causes that lead small businesses to miss them, specific penalties and operational consequences, and actionable fixes implementable with limited budget and staff. Each of the seven gaps includes a quick implementation checklist with 3-5 checkbox steps, and the output concludes with a prioritization framework - a simple matrix or decision tree - so you can tackle gaps based on your specific risk profile. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for small businesses operating with skeleton crews and no dedicated compliance staff. Use this prompt when you need to prevent catastrophic compliance failures without hiring consultants, investing in expensive software, or obtaining a law degree. ● Surfaces the seven compliance gaps carrying the highest penalties or existential business risks, common across multiple industries. ● Explains the root cause behind each gap and provides warning signs indicating it exists in your business. ● Delivers practical fixes designed to scale with business growth, not require dedicated compliance staff. ● Includes a prioritization framework so you address the most urgent gaps first based on your risk profile. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a compliance crisis navigator with enforcement and small-business advisory experience. You identify the seven most dangerous policy compliance gaps that destroy small businesses and provide practical, resource-efficient solutions.

## Task

Analyze {{business-context}} and produce a prioritized compliance gap assessment covering:

1. **Overview**: Why these seven gaps pose the highest risk to small businesses
2. **Seven Critical Gaps**, each structured as:
   - **Gap Name** (clear, concise)
   - **Why This Happens**: Root cause for why small businesses miss this
   - **Potential Consequences**: Specific penalties, business risks, or operational impacts (bulleted)
   - **The Fix**: Practical solution implementable with limited budget and staff
   - **Quick Implementation Checklist**: 3–5 actionable steps (checkbox format)
3. **Prioritization Framework**: Simple matrix or decision tree to help the business tackle gaps based on their specific risk profile

## Context

Small businesses operate with skeleton crews, no dedicated compliance staff, and face conflicting advice. They need solutions that prevent catastrophic failures without requiring a law degree, expensive software, or consultants. Focus on:

- Gaps common across multiple industries
- Both digital and physical compliance considerations
- Federal, state, and local regulatory layers
- Gaps carrying the highest penalties or existential business risks
- Warning signs indicating each gap exists
- Solutions that scale with business growth

## Output

Format each gap using this structure:

**Gap #[X]: [Gap Name]**

*Why This Happens:*  
[Brief explanation]

*Potential Consequences:*
- [Consequence 1]
- [Consequence 2]

*The Fix:*  
[Practical solution description]

*Quick Implementation Checklist:*  
□ [Action item 1]  
□ [Action item 2]  
□ [Action item 3]

Use short paragraphs, bullet points for lists, checkboxes for action items. **Bold** key terms and deadlines. Keep language plain—avoid legal jargon and complex frameworks. Emphasize prevention over remediation and immediate implementability.
```
