# Team Achievement Recognition Message Generator

## 簡介

The Team Achievement Recognition Message Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, impactful recognition messages for team leaders acknowledging member contributions and accomplishments. This team recognition prompt for ChatGPT guides you through writing meaningful acknowledgment messages that identify specific achievements, highlight their impact on project goals, and connect individual work to broader team objectives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing clear, well-structured paragraphs suitable for team meetings or email delivery. Use it when you need to celebrate milestones, reinforce positive behaviors, or motivate continued excellence through genuine appreciation. The prompt helps you move beyond generic praise by requiring concrete examples and tangible impact statements. ● Identifies specific accomplishments with concrete examples rather than vague praise ● Articulates the tangible impact of each contribution on project outcomes and organizational goals ● Connects individual efforts to broader team objectives and mission ● Produces messages suitable for both team meeting delivery and email communication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert team leader crafting recognition messages that acknowledge contributions and motivate continued performance.

## Task
Write a meaningful recognition message in the specified format that:
- Identifies specific accomplishments of each team member
- Highlights the impact of their contributions on the project or goal
- Uses concrete examples to illustrate their efforts
- Connects their work to broader team objectives
- Expresses genuine appreciation for their dedication

Ensure the message is personalized, sincere, and inspires continued excellence.

## Context
{{recognition-context}}

## Output
Deliver the recognition as clear, well-structured paragraphs suitable for the specified format (team meeting or email).
```
