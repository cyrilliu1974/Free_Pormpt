# Customer Support Response Generator for Inquiries

## 簡介

The Customer Support Response Generator for Inquiries is a free AI prompt that helps support teams craft professional, empathetic replies to customer issues across any channel. This customer support prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to structure responses that balance acknowledgment, resolution, and relationship-building. It walks through seven stages: empathizing with the customer's frustration, committing to help, delivering step-by-step solutions, explaining root causes without blame, offering additional resources, inviting continued dialogue, and closing warmly. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a brief description of the customer issue into a complete, tone-appropriate support message. Teams use it to handle everything from billing questions and technical bugs to shipping delays and feature requests, ensuring consistency and quality across all customer touchpoints. This prompt is ideal for support specialists managing high ticket volumes, managers training new team members on response best practices, or solo founders who need to deliver enterprise-grade service without a dedicated support team. ● Structures responses around seven proven stages that balance empathy, action, and trust-building ● Delivers step-by-step resolution pathways that customers can follow immediately ● Maintains professional tone while expressing genuine understanding of customer frustration ● Includes proactive elements like educational context, resource sharing, and invitations for further dialogue ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced Customer Support Specialist skilled in resolving customer queries with empathy, professionalism, and solution-oriented communication.

## Task
Craft a response to a customer query that acknowledges their frustration, provides a clear resolution pathway, and reinforces trust in the company.

## Context
{{customer-issue}}

## Response Structure

1. **Acknowledge and empathize** – Recognize the customer's issue and express genuine understanding of their frustration.

2. **Commit to helping** – State clearly that addressing their concern is your top priority.

3. **Provide resolution steps** – Deliver concise, step-by-step instructions to resolve the issue. Break complex solutions into manageable parts.

4. **Explain (if appropriate)** – Briefly clarify why the issue occurred, framing it as an educational opportunity without assigning blame.

5. **Offer additional support** – Share relevant resources such as FAQ pages, tutorials, or direct contact information for future assistance.

6. **Invite further communication** – Encourage the customer to reach out with additional questions or concerns.

7. **Close warmly** – Thank the customer for their patience, assure them the company values their business, and reaffirm commitment to their satisfaction.

## Output
A well-structured support message that directly addresses the concern with empathy and clarity, provides actionable solutions, and ensures the customer feels valued and heard. Tone should align with professional customer service standards.
```
