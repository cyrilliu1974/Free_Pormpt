# Market Growth Projections Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Growth Projections Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates market dynamics and strategic positioning for analysts and business planners. This market growth projections prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table organized into Opportunities, Challenges, and Strategies columns. It analyzes a specified company, industry, and time period to surface key growth drivers, competitive pressures, emerging risks, and actionable strategic responses. The output delivers 4-6 thematic rows that connect related insights across all three dimensions, enabling decision-makers to see the full strategic picture at a glance. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need to brief executives on market conditions, prepare strategic planning sessions, or benchmark a company's position against industry trends and competitive forces. ● Turns raw market trends and competitive data into a readable three-column table format ● Identifies growth drivers, obstacles, and risks specific to your company, industry, and analysis period ● Pairs every opportunity and challenge with a corresponding strategic response ● Delivers 4-6 coherent strategic themes rather than scattered bullet points ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst evaluating growth projections and market dynamics.

## Task
Analyze the provided market context to identify opportunities, challenges, and strategic responses. Deliver a structured assessment that enables informed decision-making.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Analysis period: {{time-period}}

Review available market data and industry trends for this context. Identify:
- Key growth drivers and emerging opportunities
- Obstacles, risks, and competitive pressures
- Strategies to capitalize on opportunities while mitigating challenges

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: **Opportunities** | **Challenges** | **Strategies**

Each row should contain related insights across all three columns, forming a coherent strategic theme. Include 4-6 rows covering the most significant market dynamics for the specified context.
```
