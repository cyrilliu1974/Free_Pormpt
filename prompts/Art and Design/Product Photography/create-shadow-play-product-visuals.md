# Shadow Play Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Shadow Play Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms uploaded product images into ultra-minimal editorial shadow compositions for beauty, skincare, and luxury brands. This shadow play product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates a scene where warm afternoon sunlight casts sharp human and product shadows onto a smooth plaster wall, then overlays the product's label text in crisp white typography directly onto the shadow form. You control the wall tone, shadow pose, and campaign aesthetic through three variables, generating hyperreal product silhouettes that match the uploaded image's exact proportions, cap shape, and edges. Designers and marketers use it to produce editorial-quality visuals for social media, websites, and advertising without studio lighting setups. Reach for this prompt when you need artistic, atmosphere-driven product imagery that stands out in feeds and hero banners. ● Casts hyperreal product shadows with exact silhouette fidelity extracted from your uploaded image, preserving cap, bottle, and edge details. ● Projects the product's label text in clean white typography onto the shadow itself, maintaining original spacing and alignment. ● Combines human shadow poses with product shadows in a single composition for storytelling depth. ● Outputs ultra-high-resolution visuals with sharp shadow edges, soft film grain, and restricted color palettes for a refined editorial finish. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-minimal shadow play product visual: a {{wall-tone}} smooth plaster wall with subtle natural imperfections and soft film grain, lit by warm afternoon sunlight from a side angle that creates elongated, sharp-edged shadows in a soft dreamy atmosphere.

Centered composition featuring a human shadow in {{shadow-pose}} alongside the product's shadow. The product shadow must be hyperreal and precisely recreated from the uploaded image—exact silhouette, cap shape, proportions, and edges locked to the actual product form.

The product label text (extracted from the uploaded image) is projected in clean crisp white typography directly onto the product shadow, preserving exact placement, spacing, size, and alignment from the original label.

Style: {{campaign-aesthetic}}, editorial luxury skincare finish. Ultra high resolution with sharp shadow edges and wall texture fidelity. No extra objects. Color palette restricted to the natural wall tone and white typography only. Gently diffused lighting with precise shadow definition.
```
