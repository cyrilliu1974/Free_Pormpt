# Modern Editorial Portrait Generator

The Modern Editorial Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates striking editorial-style portraits with bold color palettes and dramatic lighting for photographers, art directors, and content creators. This modern editorial portrait prompt for Midjourney produces close-up shots with intense color saturation, heavy shadows, and glossy skin textures inspired by photographer Harley Weir's distinctive aesthetic. The prompt simulates a 50mm f/1.2 lens at 3:4 aspect ratio, delivering professional editorial looks without requiring a physical shoot. Use it to generate fashion magazine covers, artist press photos, creative portfolio pieces, or social media content that demands attention.
