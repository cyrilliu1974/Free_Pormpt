The Cinematic 3D Product Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI image prompt that generates nine-frame product grids with unified studio lighting, photorealistic materials, and bold brand integration for advertisers and creative teams. This 3D advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 3×3 grid in 3:4 aspect ratio, with each frame showcasing a different dynamic product moment - ice explosions, carbonation bursts, macro condensation, liquid splashes, floating hero shots, slow-motion rotations, bubble close-ups, vapor crashes, and iconic final frames. Every scene shares consistent color grading, dramatic key and rim lighting, and natural logo placement through reflections and camera angles. Use it to create scroll-stopping social media content, digital billboard visuals, or pitch-ready advertising mockups for beverages, cosmetics, electronics, or any premium packaged good.
