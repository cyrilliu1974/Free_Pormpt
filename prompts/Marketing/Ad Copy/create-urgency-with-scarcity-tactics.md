# Scarcity-Driven Marketing Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Scarcity-Driven Marketing Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates urgency-based marketing copy designed to motivate immediate customer action through scarcity signals and time-sensitive offers. This scarcity marketing prompt for ChatGPT produces structured ad copy across four essential sections: attention-grabbing headlines with urgency hooks, value-reinforcing subheadlines, benefit-driven body paragraphs with social proof, and deadline-focused calls-to-action. It works by building a cohesive narrative where each sentence escalates the sense of limited availability, weaving together your unique selling proposition, target audience insights, and specific offer deadlines. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable for email campaigns, landing pages, social ads, and promotional materials where conversion speed matters. Marketing professionals reach for this prompt when launching flash sales, product drops with limited inventory, early-bird promotions, or exclusive access campaigns that require persuasive, urgency-driven messaging. ● Structures copy into four tactical sections - headline, subheadline, body, and CTA - each calibrated for urgency ● Incorporates multiple scarcity signals like limited stock, expiring offers, and exclusive access into a cohesive narrative ● Builds emotional resonance by connecting audience desires to concrete benefits and time-sensitive opportunities ● Outputs copy that balances persuasive intensity with clarity, avoiding generic hype in favor of specific, actionable messaging ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in urgency-driven marketing campaigns.

## Task
Write compelling scarcity-based copy that drives immediate action for {{product-or-service}}. Structure your writing so each sentence builds on the previous one, creating a cohesive, persuasive narrative that emphasizes limited availability and prompts swift decision-making.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Offer and deadline: {{offer-and-deadline}}

## Approach
- Open by highlighting the core benefit that solves the audience's pressing need
- Layer in scarcity signals (limited stock, expiring offer, exclusive access)
- Use concrete, emotionally resonant language that reflects the audience's desires
- Build momentum toward the call-to-action through escalating urgency

## Output
Deliver the copy in four clearly labeled sections:

**Headline**  
[attention-grabbing statement with urgency hook]

**Subheadline**  
[reinforce value + scarcity element]

**Body Copy**  
[2–3 tight paragraphs: benefit-driven narrative, social proof or specifics, urgency reinforcement]

**Call-to-Action**  
[direct, action-oriented instruction with deadline emphasis]
```
