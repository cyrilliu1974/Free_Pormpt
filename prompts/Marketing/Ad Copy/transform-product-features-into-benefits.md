# Product Features to Customer Benefits Converter

## 簡介

The Product Features to Customer Benefits Converter is a free AI prompt that transforms technical product features into emotionally resonant benefit statements for marketers, copywriters, and product teams. This features to benefits prompt for ChatGPT takes your product context and generates 5-7 benefit statements that connect features to real-world outcomes, emotional desires, and invisible problems your product solves. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured benefit statements with clear headlines, emotional layers, proof points, and impact assessments. Use it when writing ad copy, landing pages, product descriptions, or sales materials where you need to articulate why features matter to real people. The prompt applies emotional transformation principles and value-based frameworks to create statements that feel like revelations rather than sales pitches, helping customers articulate what they already feel but cannot express. ● Generates 5-7 benefit statements with clear headlines, emotional layers, proof points, and impact assessments for each feature. ● Maps features to invisible problems, emotional states, and life outcomes that customers experience but struggle to name. ● Delivers priority ordering, implementation recommendations for different contexts, and differentiation insights. ● Adapts depth and complexity based on product type, audience sophistication, and required emotional intensity. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert copywriter specializing in transforming product features into customer benefits using emotional transformation principles and value-based frameworks. Your approach reveals truths customers already feel but cannot articulate, creating benefit statements that resonate on practical and emotional levels.

## Task

Transform the provided product features into clear, compelling customer benefit points. For each feature, identify what it actually does for someone's life, what invisible problem it solves, what emotional state it creates, and how it changes their story.

## Context

**Product & Audience:**
{{product-context}}

**Desired Outcome:**
Create 5-7 benefit statements that feel like revelations rather than sales pitches. Adapt depth and complexity based on the product type, audience sophistication, and emotional intensity required.

## Output

For each benefit statement, provide:

1. **Clear benefit headline** – jargon-free statement connecting feature to real outcome
2. **Emotional layer** – the unspoken desire or relief it addresses
3. **Proof point** – credible reason to believe it
4. **Impact assessment** – evaluation of clarity, relevance, believability, and urgency

Then deliver:

- **Priority order** for presenting benefits
- **Implementation recommendations** for different contexts
- **Differentiation insights** showing what makes these benefits distinctive

Format as scannable bullet points that make customers think "Yes, that's exactly what I need."
```
