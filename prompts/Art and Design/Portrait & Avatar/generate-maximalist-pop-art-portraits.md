# Maximalist Pop-Art Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Maximalist Pop-Art Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic urban portraits overwhelmed by dense neon illustrated overlays for designers, content creators, and brands seeking eye-catching visual assets. This maximalist pop-art portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana combines pastel street photography with chaotic graphic design layers. It outputs a photorealistic subject in urban fashion - cropped hoodies, sneakers, playful poses - surrounded and partially covered by hundreds of flat-color creatures, blob shapes, whimsical monsters, and neon graphic elements like stars, hearts, zigzags, and drips. The face and skin stay clean and photographic while clothing, background, and negative space burst with thick-outlined cartoon overlays in pink, cyan, lime, yellow, and purple. Use it for social media content, album art, youth-brand campaigns, or any project that demands scroll-stopping contrast between real and illustrated. ● Photorealistic subject with clean face, arms, and legs contrasted against hundreds of neon creatures and graphic shapes. ● Customizable subject description and overlay theme (monsters, food, plants, cosmic elements) via two simple variables. ● Layered depth with creatures in front of and behind the subject, creating stacked, chaotic visual interest. ● Pastel street-corner setting with diffused afternoon light, chunky sneakers, cropped hoodies, and playful poses that feel contemporary and relatable. ## Prompt

```
Placement: entire background packed with overlapping {{overlay-theme}} creatures; creatures peeking near shoulders, feet, and around hair silhouette; some creatures rest on hoodie, skirt, and shoes with small clothing shadows. Face, legs, and arms remain clean and photorealistic. Layers in front and behind create stacked chaotic depth. Glowing rim lines, white spark dots, motion lines surrounding her.

Mid to low camera angle, wide lens with mild depth warp, subject centered with plenty of room for decoration layers. Pastel street photography overwhelmed by neon {{overlay-theme}} pop-art aesthetic—cute, overloaded, vibrant, surreal.
```
