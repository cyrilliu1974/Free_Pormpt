# Thesis Introduction Chapter Writer for Academic Papers

## 簡介

The Thesis Introduction Chapter Writer for Academic Papers is a free AI prompt that produces structured, compelling introduction chapters for graduate and doctoral theses across any academic discipline. This thesis introduction prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to write a five-section introduction that provides essential background context, clearly articulates the research problem, lists specific research objectives, explains the study's significance and potential impact, and outlines the complete thesis structure with chapter descriptions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting a single variable for the thesis topic and field, then generating a ready-to-edit introduction that follows academic conventions. Graduate students use it to overcome blank-page syndrome when starting their thesis, while researchers adapt the output to match institutional formatting requirements and disciplinary norms. ● Produces five standard sections: background information, research problem statement, numbered research objectives, significance of the study, and annotated thesis structure ● Maintains academic tone and logical flow between sections, avoiding unsupported claims or jargon ● Outputs a complete chapter draft that students can customize to match advisor feedback and institutional style guides ● Works across all academic disciplines by adapting language and structure to the specified field ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert thesis introduction chapter writer with deep knowledge across academic disciplines.

## Task
Write a compelling introduction chapter for an academic thesis that sets the stage for the research. The introduction must provide essential background, clearly state the research problem and objectives, explain the study's significance, and outline the thesis structure.

## Context
Thesis topic and field: {{thesis-topic-and-field}}

The introduction should be concise yet comprehensive, using clear and accessible language. Ensure logical flow between sections and avoid unsupported claims. Focus on guiding the reader smoothly from background context through to the thesis roadmap.

## Output
Structure the introduction chapter in five sections:

**Background Information:**
[Provide essential context and background information related to the thesis topic]

**Research Problem:**
[Clearly state the research problem being addressed]

**Research Objectives:**
- [Objective 1]
- [Objective 2]
- [Objective 3]

**Significance of the Study:**
[Explain the importance and potential impact of the research]

**Thesis Structure:**
1. [Chapter 1 title]: [Brief description]
2. [Chapter 2 title]: [Brief description]
3. [Chapter 3 title]: [Brief description]
4. [Chapter 4 title]: [Brief description]
5. [Chapter 5 title]: [Brief description]
```
