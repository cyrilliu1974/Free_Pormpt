# Product Description Writer for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Product Description Writer for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, SEO-optimized product copy for online retailers, marketers, and copywriters. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce structured 300-word descriptions that open with a compelling hook, showcase 3-5 key features using sensory language, connect each feature to specific customer pain points, and weave a lifestyle narrative that helps shoppers envision themselves using the product. The prompt ensures SEO keywords are integrated naturally into the copy without disrupting readability, and every description closes with a clear call-to-action. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone to match your audience - whether professional, casual, aspirational, or technical - based on the product details you provide. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, conversion-focused product copy at scale or want to train AI to match your brand voice across catalog pages, marketing materials, and social media. ● Transforms product specs into benefit-driven narratives that address real customer needs ● Enforces a six-part structure: hook, features, benefits, lifestyle context, SEO integration, and CTA ● Maintains 300-word limit for versatile use across product pages, emails, and ads ● Adapts tone and language style to match target audience expectations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive, SEO-optimized product descriptions.

## Task
Write a compelling 300-word product description that highlights features, articulates benefits, and resonates with the target audience. The description must integrate SEO keywords naturally and conclude with a strong call-to-action.

## Context
{{product-details}}

Include the product name, unique features, pain points it solves, target audience characteristics, and SEO keywords to incorporate.

## Output
Structure the description as follows:

1. **Opening hook** – One compelling sentence that captures the product's core appeal to the target audience
2. **Feature showcase** – Describe 3-5 key features using vivid, sensory language
3. **Benefit focus** – Connect each feature to specific audience needs, desires, or pain points; show the tangible impact on their life
4. **Lifestyle narrative** – Weave a brief story that helps the audience envision themselves using the product in a way that aligns with their values or aspirations
5. **SEO integration** – Naturally incorporate provided keywords into headlines, body copy, and product mentions without disrupting readability
6. **Call-to-action** – End with a clear, persuasive CTA (purchase, learn more, sign up)

Keep the total length at or under 300 words for optimal use across product pages, marketing materials, and social media. The tone should match the audience's expectations—whether professional, casual, aspirational, or technical—based on the context provided.
```
