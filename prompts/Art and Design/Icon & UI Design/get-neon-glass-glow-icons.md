## 簡介

The Neon Glass Glow Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms any image into a futuristic 3D neon glass icon with volumetric glow and translucent materials for designers and digital artists. This neon glass icon prompt for Midjourney and Flux analyzes the silhouette and geometry of your uploaded image, then retextures it with transparent acrylic, crystalized resin, and light-bending glass materials while preserving sharp edges and stylized outlines. It applies high-intensity neon backlighting in vivid cyan (#00FFC3), electric green (#33FF00), and bright yellow accents, renders the object centered on a pure black background, and adds inner and outer rim glow with bloom effects for a holographic 3D look.
