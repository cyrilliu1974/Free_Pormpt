# Customer Satisfaction Survey Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Satisfaction Survey Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides you through designing surveys, analyzing results, and building implementation plans to improve service quality and product offerings. This customer satisfaction analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every stage: defining your target audience and survey method, crafting multiple-choice, Likert scale, and open-ended questions, applying statistical analysis to responses, identifying trends and patterns, and translating findings into prioritized recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a structured report covering survey design, data analysis, service and product improvement recommendations, timelines, resource requirements, and KPIs. Marketing teams use it to understand customer pain points, product managers use it to prioritize feature updates, and customer-success teams use it to track satisfaction over time. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from raw feedback to an actionable roadmap that increases customer loyalty and retention. ● Generates survey questions across multiple formats - multiple choice, Likert scale, and open-ended - to capture both quantitative scores and qualitative insights. ● Applies statistical methods to raw response data, surfacing key trends, patterns, and correlations that reveal what drives satisfaction. ● Delivers prioritized service and product recommendations tied directly to survey findings, so every insight leads to a concrete next step. ● Includes a full implementation plan with timelines, resource requirements, and KPIs, turning analysis into executable strategy. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer satisfaction expert and data analyst skilled in designing surveys, analyzing results, and deriving actionable insights to enhance service quality and product offerings.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive customer satisfaction survey, analyze the results, and provide data-driven recommendations with an implementation plan.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
Survey parameters: {{survey-parameters}}
Analysis methods: {{analysis-methods}}

The survey should cover all key aspects of the business using clear, concise questions without bias. Include multiple choice, Likert scale, and open-ended questions to gather both quantitative and qualitative data. Focus on actionable insights that drive meaningful improvements in customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## Output
Deliver a structured report with these sections:

### Customer Satisfaction Survey Design
- Target audience, sample size, and survey method
- Question types: multiple choice, Likert scale, and open-ended questions

### Data Analysis
- Data sources used
- Statistical methods applied
- Key findings, trends, and patterns identified

### Recommendations
- Service improvements based on insights
- Product enhancements based on insights
- All recommendations should maximize customer satisfaction and loyalty

### Implementation Plan
- Timeline for implementing recommendations
- Resources required for successful implementation
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure success
```
