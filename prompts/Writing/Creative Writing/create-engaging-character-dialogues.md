# Character Dialogue Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Character Dialogue Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compelling fiction dialogue grounded in sensory detail and character truth for novelists, screenwriters, and creative writers. This character dialogue prompt for ChatGPT takes your character backstories, setting details, and plot context and produces complete prose scenes where conversation reveals emotion, motivation, and conflict without exposition. It opens with atmospheric scene-setting, weaves subtext into exchanges, maintains distinct character voices, and closes with a story-driving moment. Writers use it to draft emotionally resonant conversations for novels, short stories, scripts, and serialized fiction on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. The prompt enforces show-don't-tell principles, balances dialogue tags with action beats, and ensures every line serves character or plot. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn a plot outline or character profile into living, breathing dialogue that feels authentic and moves your story forward. ● Opens every scene with sensory detail that immerses readers in atmosphere and context before the first line of dialogue ● Builds subtext into exchanges so meaning emerges through tone, pauses, and what characters leave unsaid ● Maintains consistency with each character's established voice, backstory, and relationships throughout the scene ● Closes with a revelation, decision, or action that propels the narrative and hooks the reader into the next beat ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a fiction writer skilled in crafting dialogue that reveals character and advances story.

## Task
Write a compelling dialogue scene between the specified characters. The dialogue must develop character depth, convey emotions and motivations, and move the plot forward. Ground the exchange in vivid sensory detail that immerses the reader in the moment.

## Context
{{character-and-plot-context}}

Include: the characters involved, their backstories and personalities, the scene setting (location, atmosphere, sensory details), and how this moment fits into the larger plot.

## Output
Deliver a dialogue scene that:

- Opens with vivid scene-setting using sensory details (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste) to establish atmosphere and context
- Begins dialogue with a hook—a compelling line or action that immediately draws the reader in
- Reveals character traits, emotions, and motivations through what characters say and do (show, don't tell)
- Incorporates subtext; let meaning emerge through tone, word choice, pauses, and what remains unsaid
- Uses dialogue tags and action beats sparingly to convey tone and maintain dynamic pacing
- Stays consistent with each character's established voice, backstory, and relationships
- Concludes with a significant moment—a revelation, decision, or action—that propels the story forward and leaves the reader wanting more

Format the scene as prose with proper dialogue formatting (quotation marks, paragraph breaks for speaker changes).
```
