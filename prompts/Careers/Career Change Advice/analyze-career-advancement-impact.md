# Cross-Functional Career Advancement Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Cross-Functional Career Advancement Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed research reports on how diverse functional experience shapes career growth within a specified industry. This career advancement prompt for ChatGPT delivers a complete analysis including an industry overview, a three-column comparison table mapping functional areas to advancement benefits and acquisition strategies, a list of common challenges, actionable recommendations, and citations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by adopting the role of a career-development researcher who synthesizes organizational dynamics, skill-transfer patterns, and progression data into a presentation-ready format. Use it when advising professionals on lateral moves, building workforce-development playbooks, or evaluating the ROI of rotation programs. ● Outputs a structured table linking cross-functional areas to specific career benefits and practical acquisition strategies. ● Identifies real challenges professionals face when seeking diverse experience and offers mitigation tactics. ● Includes an industry-contextualized overview and reference section for credibility. ● Adapts to any target industry through a single variable, making it reusable for consulting engagements or internal HR planning. ## Prompt

