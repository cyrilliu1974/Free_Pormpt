# International SEO Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The International SEO Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, market-specific SEO guidebooks for businesses expanding into international markets. This international SEO prompt for ChatGPT walks through six core implementation steps: conducting keyword research across target regions, implementing hreflang tags and language-specific URL structures, localizing content beyond simple translation, building local backlinks and partnerships, optimizing for regional search engines like Baidu and Yandex, and monitoring market-specific performance trends. The prompt generates actionable guidebooks tailored to your website URL, target markets, and industry, with practical examples and tool recommendations throughout. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured documentation that addresses technical requirements, cultural nuances, and local search behavior patterns. This prompt is for marketing teams, SEO specialists, and business owners preparing to launch or optimize websites in new geographic markets where language, search behavior, and dominant platforms differ significantly from their home region. ● Produces market-specific keyword research instructions with attention to language variations and local search intent ● Explains hreflang tag implementation and URL structure options (ccTLDs, subdirectories, subdomains) with common pitfall warnings ● Details content localization that goes beyond translation to address cultural adaptation, local currencies, date formats, and measurements ● Outlines strategies for acquiring local backlinks, forming regional partnerships, and optimizing for search engines like Baidu, Yandex, and Naver ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in international SEO practices.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guidebook that details actionable methods for enhancing a website's international SEO to expand global reach.

## Context
The guidebook will cover:
- **Website:** {{website-url}}
- **Target markets:** {{target-markets}}
- **Industry:** {{industry}}

Consider market-specific factors including local search behavior, technical requirements, cultural nuances, and regional search engines.

## Output
Structure the guidebook with these sections:

**Introduction**
Brief overview of international SEO importance and the guidebook's purpose.

**Step 1: Conduct thorough keyword research for target markets**
Provide instructions, practical examples, and tool recommendations for international keyword research. Address language variations, search intent differences, and local keyword volume.

**Step 2: Implement hreflang tags and language-specific URLs**
Explain technical setup with clear examples for hreflang implementation and URL structure options (ccTLDs, subdirectories, subdomains). Include common pitfalls to avoid.

**Step 3: Localize and optimize website content**
Detail content localization beyond translation—cultural adaptation, local currency, measurements, date formats, and region-specific examples. Include on-page optimization for each market.

**Step 4: Build local backlinks and partnerships**
Outline strategies for acquiring authoritative local backlinks, forming regional partnerships, and building market-specific domain authority.

**Step 5: Optimize for local search engines and directories**
Cover optimization for region-dominant search engines (Baidu, Yandex, Naver) and local business directories. Include submission guidelines and profile optimization.

**Step 6: Monitor and adapt to market-specific trends and algorithms**
Describe monitoring tools, KPIs to track per market, and how to respond to regional algorithm updates and emerging trends.

**Conclusion**
Recap key points and emphasize the importance of consistently applying international SEO best practices.

Use bullet points, subheadings, and clear formatting throughout for readability. Focus on proven, actionable methods with specific examples rather than generic advice.
```
