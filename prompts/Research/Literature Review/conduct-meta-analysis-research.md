# Meta-Analysis Research Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Meta-Analysis Research Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured meta-analysis research designs for academics, graduate students, and evidence-based practitioners. This meta-analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the synthesis of quantitative findings from multiple studies, walking through literature search strategies, inclusion criteria, data extraction protocols, statistical pooling methods, and evidence interpretation. It outputs a complete research framework covering introduction, methods, results with effect sizes and heterogeneity statistics, discussion of limitations and implications, and references. Researchers use it to design systematic reviews, prepare grant proposals, or structure evidence syntheses in medicine, psychology, education, and social sciences. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to design a rigorous quantitative synthesis or communicate complex meta-analytic methodology clearly. ● Defines literature search strategies, inclusion criteria, and data extraction protocols aligned with PRISMA standards ● Outlines statistical approaches including effect size pooling, heterogeneity assessment, subgroup analysis, and publication bias testing ● Structures results sections with study characteristics tables, forest plot descriptions, and confidence interval reporting ● Provides discussion templates addressing limitations, theoretical implications, and recommendations for future primary research ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert meta-analysis researcher skilled at synthesizing data from multiple studies to draw meaningful conclusions and evidence-based implications.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive meta-analysis on {{topic-or-field}}, following systematic review methodology to pool findings from existing research and determine overall effect sizes and clinical or practical significance.

## Context
A meta-analysis combines quantitative results from independent studies addressing a common research question. Your synthesis will identify patterns, contradictions, and gaps in the evidence base to guide future research and inform evidence-based practice.

## Output
Structure your meta-analysis with these sections:

**1. Introduction**
- Background on {{topic-or-field}} and its significance
- Research question and objectives of this meta-analysis

**2. Methods**
- Literature search strategy (databases, keywords, date range)
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria for study selection
- Data extraction protocol (effect sizes, sample characteristics, study quality)
- Statistical analysis approach (fixed vs. random effects model, heterogeneity assessment)

**3. Results**
- Characteristics of included studies (sample sizes, designs, populations)
- Overall pooled effect size with confidence intervals and significance level
- Heterogeneity statistics (I², Q-test)
- Subgroup analyses by relevant moderators
- Sensitivity analyses
- Publication bias assessment (funnel plot, Egger's test)

**4. Discussion**
- Summary of main findings and their magnitude
- Comparison with prior reviews or individual landmark studies
- Methodological limitations (search scope, study quality, heterogeneity)
- Theoretical and practical implications for {{topic-or-field}}
- Specific recommendations for future primary research

**5. Conclusion**
- Concise synthesis of key takeaways and confidence in findings

**6. References**
- Complete citation list in standard academic format

Include tables summarizing study characteristics and forest plots visualizing effect sizes where appropriate. Use clear headings and maintain academic rigor throughout.
```
