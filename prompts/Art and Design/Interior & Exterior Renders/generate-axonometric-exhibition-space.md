# Axonometric Exhibition Space Generator

## 簡介

The Axonometric Exhibition Space Generator is a free AI prompt that produces precise technical architectural drawings of contemporary exhibition spaces for architects, designers, and spatial planners. This axonometric exhibition space prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates clean, line-weight architectural diagrams showing double-height gallery halls with exposed structural grids, circulation loops, and configurable display elements - all rendered in true axonometric projection without perspective distortion. You specify a center feature (stage, sculpture court, cafe zone) and display objects (pedestals, vitrines, screens), and the prompt delivers publication-ready technical drawings in monochrome with architectural line hierarchy: thick structural lines, medium partition walls, and thin furniture detail. Reach for this prompt when you need portfolio-quality spatial diagrams for client presentations, competition boards, or design documentation without launching CAD or 3D modeling software. ● Produces true axonometric projections with uniform angles and no perspective distortion for accurate spatial reading. ● Shows structural column grids, exposed ceiling systems, lighting tracks, and circulation paths with proper line weight hierarchy. ● Includes customizable center features and display object arrangements to match specific exhibition briefs. ● Outputs high-resolution 1:1 images suitable for architectural portfolios, competition panels, and stakeholder presentations. ## Prompt

```
## Subject
A contemporary exhibition hall rendered as a precise axonometric architectural drawing with roof removed, showing spatial organization, structural systems, and exhibition layout in clean technical diagram style.

## Spatial Organization
Open double-height exhibition hall with {{center-feature}} as focal point. Perimeter features movable exhibition wall panels, integrated storage niches, and secondary gallery zones. Clear loop circulation with defined entry axis through center. Slightly raised platform zones for exhibition areas.

## Architectural Systems
Regular column grid and perimeter load-bearing walls. Exposed structural ceiling grid with integrated lighting tracks, acoustic panels, and suspended mechanical lines. Continuous smooth floor plate.

## Interior Elements
{{display-objects}}. Minimal simplified human silhouettes for scale reference only.

## Drawing Style
Clean CAD-like architectural line drawing in monochrome dark gray on white background. Line weight hierarchy: structural lines thickest, partitions medium, furniture and objects thin. Very subtle flat tonal highlight for exhibition panels only—no gradients, no dramatic shadows, no texture.

## Composition
Top-corner axonometric view (true axonometric projection, no perspective distortion). Uniform projection angle maintaining architectural precision and spatial clarity. Generous white margins. High diagrammatic strength focused on system visibility.

## Technical Requirements
1:1 aspect ratio, very high resolution, portfolio and publication ready quality.

## Exclude
Perspective view, photorealistic rendering, dramatic lighting, heavy surface textures, messy sketch lines, ornamental decoration, complex backgrounds, text, logos, watermarks, AI artifacts.
```
