# Feedback Request Email Template Generator

## 簡介

The Feedback Request Email Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, structured email templates designed to drive response rates and collect actionable feedback from your audience. This feedback request prompt for ChatGPT produces complete email templates with customized subject lines, personalized body copy, and clearly defined feedback areas tailored to your business context. It works by taking your business and audience details, the specific topics where you need input, and your response deadline and submission method, then generating a professional yet approachable template with placeholders ready to fill and send. Use it for customer surveys, product feedback loops, stakeholder consultations, or any scenario where you need structured, respectful requests for honest opinions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this template when you want to gather meaningful insights without starting from scratch or when response rates matter and you need language that emphasizes the value of recipient input. ● Produces subject lines and body copy that explain why the recipient's feedback matters and how it will be used ● Organizes specific feedback areas as bullet points so recipients know exactly what input you need ● Includes placeholders for names, deadlines, and submission methods so the template is customizable in seconds ● Balances professional tone with warmth to encourage honest, detailed responses and improve completion rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert communications specialist who writes compelling feedback request emails that drive response rates and gather actionable insights.

## Task
Create a customizable email template that requests feedback from recipients. The template should include:

- A personalized greeting
- Clear explanation of why their feedback matters
- Specific areas where input is needed
- The response deadline and submission method
- Persuasive language that emphasizes how their feedback will drive meaningful improvements

## Context
Business and audience: {{business-and-audience}}

Feedback focus: {{feedback-areas}}

Response details: {{response-deadline-and-method}}

## Output
Deliver a complete email template with:
- Subject line
- Body copy in structured paragraphs
- Bullet points for the specific feedback areas
- Clear placeholders (e.g., [Recipient Name], [Your Name]) for easy personalization
- Professional yet warm tone that encourages honest, detailed responses

Format the template so it's immediately usable—the user should only need to fill in the placeholders and send.
```
