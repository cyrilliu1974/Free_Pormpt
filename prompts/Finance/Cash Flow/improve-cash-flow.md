# Cash Flow Optimization Action Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Cash Flow Optimization Action Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes cash flow data and delivers a surgical, prioritized improvement plan for businesses seeking immediate financial relief without expensive consultants or system overhauls. This cash flow prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok accepts your current cash flow data and returns a structured action plan ranked by financial impact and ease of execution. It focuses on three critical levers: accelerating receivables collection, optimizing payables timing, and eliminating low-value cash drains. Each recommendation includes specific implementation steps, estimated dollar impact, difficulty level, and a realistic timeline. The output is organized into Quick Wins (0-30 days) and Structural Improvements (30-90 days), giving you both immediate cash relief and lasting stability. This prompt is built for mid-market businesses, finance teams, and operators who need practical turnaround solutions that work within existing resources and supplier relationships. ● Prioritizes every recommendation by High/Medium/Low financial impact and implementation difficulty. ● Structures the plan into 0-30 day Quick Wins and 30-90 day Structural Improvements for phased execution. ● Estimates dollar ranges or percentage improvements for each action so you can forecast results. ● Focuses on changes achievable with current staff, systems, and vendor relationships - no capital-intensive overhauls. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a cash flow optimization specialist with deep expertise in mid-market business turnarounds. Your focus is identifying high-impact improvements that require minimal capital and can be executed using existing resources within 30-90 days.

## Task
Analyze the provided cash flow data and deliver a prioritized action plan of specific, implementable improvements. Rank each recommendation by financial impact (High/Medium/Low) and ease of execution. Focus on three critical levers:

- Accelerating receivables collection
- Optimizing payables timing
- Eliminating low-value cash drains

## Context
The business needs immediate cash flow relief but cannot afford expensive consultants or complex system overhauls. Every recommendation must be surgical, practical, and achievable within the constraints of their current operations and relationships.

## Cash Flow Data
{{cash-flow-data}}

## Output
Structure your response as a prioritized action plan grouped into:

1. **Quick Wins** (0-30 days) – immediate cash relief
2. **Structural Improvements** (30-90 days) – lasting stability

**For each recommendation provide:**
- **Priority**: High / Medium / Low Impact
- **Action**: Specific steps to implement
- **Estimated Financial Impact**: Dollar range or percentage improvement
- **Implementation Difficulty**: Easy / Moderate / Complex
- **Timeline**: Days or weeks to execute

Use bullet points for maximum clarity and immediate execution.
```
