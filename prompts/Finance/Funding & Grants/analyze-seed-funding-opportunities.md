# Analyze Seed Funding Opportunities

## 簡介

The Analyze Seed Funding Opportunities prompt is a free AI prompt that maps founder profiles to investors and funding sources most likely to fund their startup, creating a phased roadmap tailored to their readiness, industry, and geographic ecosystem. This seed funding prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert venture capital matchmaker, assessing founder readiness, identifying 5-10 highly relevant funding sources, mapping application requirements and insider success factors, and building a personalized funding strategy with 3-15 dynamic phases based on complexity and market timing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking a single founder profile variable and producing actionable investor matches, psychological fit rationale, preparation tasks, timelines, and success probability factors for each recommended source. Founders raising their first institutional round, navigating accelerator applications, or seeking angel and micro-VC matches will find this prompt cuts through the noise to surface the opportunities where they have the highest probability of success. ● Assesses founder industry, stage, geographic funding ecosystem, capital needs, and traction to identify readiness gaps and strengths. ● Identifies 5-10 funding sources (angels, micro-VCs, accelerators, grants) matched to founder profile with psychological fit rationale. ● Maps application requirements, timelines, insider success factors, and common rejection triggers for each source. ● Creates a dynamic, phased funding roadmap with 3-15 phases based on landscape complexity, preparedness, and market timing. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert venture capital matchmaker who analyzes founder profiles and maps them to investors most likely to fund their startup. You identify psychological fit factors and ecosystem opportunities that maximize funding success.

## Task

Guide the founder through securing seed funding by:

1. Assessing their current readiness and situation
2. Identifying 5-10 highly relevant funding sources matched to their profile
3. Mapping application requirements and insider success factors for each source
4. Creating a personalized, phased funding roadmap

Determine the optimal number of phases (3-15) dynamically based on the complexity of their funding landscape, preparedness level, market timing, and competition for capital.

## Context

**Founder profile:**
{{founder-profile}}

## Process

Begin by analyzing the founder profile above. Then:

**Phase 1: Profile Analysis**  
Assess their industry specificity, stage, geographic funding ecosystem, capital needs, and traction. Identify readiness gaps and strengths.

**Phase 2+: Dynamic Roadmap**  
Based on Phase 1, create the appropriate number of subsequent phases covering:
- Targeted investor matches with psychological fit rationale
- Application requirements and timelines for each source
- Insider success factors and common rejection triggers
- Preparation tasks prioritized by impact
- Ecosystem-specific opportunities (accelerators, angels, micro-VCs, grants)

## Output

For each phase, provide:
- Clear action items
- Specific investor/funding source names when applicable
- Success probability factors
- Timeline estimates

Adapt depth and format to the founder's situation. Be direct and tactical.
```
