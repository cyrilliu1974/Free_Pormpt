# Neon Fashion Collage Generator

## 簡介

The Neon Fashion Collage Generator is a free AI prompt that produces 4-panel fashion lifestyle grids with consistent characters, cinematic nighttime photography, and flash-and-neon lighting for fashion brands and content creators. This fashion collage prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates a 2×2 grid of four equal-sized frames showing the same subject in different poses - full-body flash shot against a metallic door, over-the-shoulder view walking toward city lights, seated profile with mocktail, and artistic neon-lit portrait. You control the subject appearance, urban setting details, and evening outfit style (sequin dress, sleek jumpsuit, cocktail attire) through three variables. The output mimics professional flash photography mixed with ambient neon signs in pink, blue, and cool white, delivering high-contrast, edgy night-luxe visuals with slight grain and 8k resolution. Fashion brands, e-commerce sellers, and Instagram content creators use it to produce magazine-quality lookbook grids, product showcases, and editorial-style marketing assets without coordinating photo shoots or hiring models. ● Produces four distinct poses in one cohesive grid - standing flash shot, over-the-shoulder walk, seated candid, and neon-lit portrait - with the same subject and outfit across all panels. ● Specifies flash photography technique blended with neon ambient light (pink, blue, cool white) for authentic nighttime urban glamour and high contrast. ● Delivers 8k resolution output with slight film grain, maintaining a cinematic party vibe and edgy night-luxe style suitable for fashion marketing and social media grids. ● Allows customization of subject appearance, setting (neon signs, city bokeh, metallic doors), and outfit type to match your brand or creative direction. ## Prompt

```
A cohesive 4-panel fashion lifestyle collage in a 2×2 grid layout, featuring the same subject across all frames in a glamorous evening outfit under city lights.

**Subject:** {{subject-appearance}} — woman in her early 20s with glamorous waves, evening makeup with red lip, sparkly clutch and statement earrings.

**Setting:** {{setting}} — nighttime urban scene with neon signs, city bokeh, and dramatic shadows.

**Outfit:** {{outfit}} — glamorous evening wear (sequin dress, sleek jumpsuit, or elegant cocktail attire).

**Panel Layout (2×2 grid, equal-sized frames):**

- **Top Left:** Full-body flash shot. Subject standing against a metallic door or shutter, posing with one leg forward, fierce expression. Flash photography style with high contrast.

- **Top Right:** Over-the-shoulder view. Subject walking away toward neon city lights, looking back at camera, showcasing the back of the outfit.

- **Bottom Left:** Seated profile. Subject on a high bar stool or velvet booth, holding a mocktail, laughing candidly toward someone off-camera.

- **Bottom Right:** Artistic portrait. Subject standing near a neon sign, face illuminated by pink and blue light, looking dreamily upward.

**Lighting:** Flash photography mixed with neon signs. Camera flash and ambient neon in cool white, vibrant pink, and blue creating high contrast, edgy night-luxe vibe.

**Style:** Flash photography with film aesthetic, cinematic party vibe, edgy and glamorous. 8k resolution with slight grain for authentic night photography feel.
```
