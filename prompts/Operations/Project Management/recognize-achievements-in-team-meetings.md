# Recognition Framework for Sales Team Achievements

## 簡介

The Recognition Framework for Sales Team Achievements is a free AI prompt that builds a complete recognition and incentive system for sales managers who need to systematically acknowledge individual performance and drive motivation. This recognition framework prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your team context, performance metrics, and preferred incentive types and produces a five-part system: a KPI alignment and tracking method, an incentive structure tied to achievement thresholds, a recognition schedule matched to your sales cycle, a communication plan for announcements, and a feedback loop for continuous improvement. Sales managers use it to replace ad-hoc recognition with a repeatable process that ties rewards directly to measurable results - whether you're managing a three-person inside sales team with monthly quotas or a regional enterprise team with quarterly targets. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize how achievements are measured, celebrated, and rewarded, or when team motivation has become inconsistent because recognition feels arbitrary. ● Aligns key performance indicators with sales goals and establishes how each metric will be measured and recorded ● Maps specific incentives (bonuses, PTO, development opportunities) to achievement levels and thresholds ● Defines a recognition schedule appropriate for your sales cycle, from weekly shout-outs to quarterly awards ● Includes a communication plan specifying channels and messaging for announcing achievements ● Provides a feedback mechanism to gather team input and refine the recognition process over time ● Outputs a sample recognition table ready to populate with team member names, achievements, and corresponding rewards ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sales team manager designing a structured recognition process that acknowledges individual achievements and motivates continued success.

## Task
Create a comprehensive recognition and incentive framework by:

1. Reviewing the provided KPIs and aligning them with sales goals
2. Designing a tracking and measurement system for these KPIs
3. Recommending specific incentives matched to achievement levels
4. Establishing a recognition schedule appropriate for the sales cycle
5. Outlining a communication strategy for announcing recognitions
6. Proposing a feedback mechanism to refine the process over time

## Context
**Team & Environment:**
{{team-and-sales-context}}
(Include: team size, industry, typical sales cycle length)

**Performance Metrics:**
{{kpi}}
(Specify the key performance indicators you track)

**Incentive Approach:**
{{incentive-type}}
(Describe preferred incentive types: monetary bonuses, gift cards, extra PTO, public recognition, professional development opportunities, etc.)

## Output
Deliver your recognition framework as:

1. **KPI Alignment & Tracking System** – how each KPI will be measured and recorded
2. **Incentive Structure** – what rewards correspond to which achievement thresholds
3. **Recognition Schedule** – frequency and timing of acknowledgments
4. **Communication Plan** – channels and messaging for announcements
5. **Feedback Loop** – method for gathering team input and iterating

Conclude with a **sample recognition table** in this markdown format:

| Team Member Name | Achievement | Incentive |
|------------------|-------------|-----------|
```
