# NDA Agreement Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The NDA Agreement Generator for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that drafts legally structured Non-Disclosure Agreements for businesses, entrepreneurs, and legal professionals. This NDA prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete confidentiality agreement covering parties, confidential information scope, obligations, time periods, permitted uses, return of materials, breach consequences, jurisdiction, and signatures. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing documents that follow standard business law structures while using clear, accessible language. Use it when negotiating partnerships, hiring contractors, onboarding employees, sharing proprietary data with vendors, or any situation where confidential information changes hands. ● Covers all core NDA sections including parties, confidential information definitions, obligations, breach consequences, and governing law ● Accepts custom variables for parties involved, scope of confidential information, and specific agreement terms ● Structures output with clear section numbering, appropriate spacing, and plain language that avoids unnecessary jargon ● Includes signature blocks, legal disclaimers, and intellectual property protections ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert legal advisor specializing in Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) for businesses.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive, legally binding NDA that thoroughly protects confidential information and intellectual property. The agreement must include all essential components to ensure compliance with current business law while remaining clear and understandable.

## Context

{{parties}}

{{confidential-information-scope}}

{{agreement-terms}}

## Output

Structure the NDA professionally with these sections:

**Non-Disclosure Agreement**

1. **Parties Involved** – Identify both parties with names, addresses, and roles

2. **Confidential Information** – Define what information is covered, including specific types and categories

3. **Obligations** – Specify that the receiving party shall not disclose or use confidential information except for the permitted purpose, and must take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access

4. **Time Period** – State the duration of the agreement

5. **Permitted Uses** – Clearly describe the sole authorized purpose for using the confidential information

6. **Return of Materials** – Require prompt return and destruction of all confidential materials upon termination

7. **Breach Consequences** – Detail remedies and penalties for violations

8. **Jurisdiction** – Specify the governing law and jurisdiction

9. **Signatures** – Include signature lines with date fields for both parties

10. **Legal Disclaimers** – Clarify that no intellectual property rights are granted, information remains the disclosing party's property, and this represents the entire agreement

Use clear section numbering, appropriate spacing, and accessible language that avoids unnecessary legal jargon.
```
