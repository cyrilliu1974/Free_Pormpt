# Beverage Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Beverage Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 6-panel beverage ad layouts for soft drink brands and marketers. This beverage advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 16:9 landscape grid showcasing a slim aluminum can across six distinct compositions: a bold hero shot with condensation, an energetic fizz-and-pour moment, a high-speed splash with floating ice cubes, a lifestyle scene with a young adult on a rooftop, a macro close-up of aluminum texture and water droplets, and a clean brand-finish frame against a sunset gradient. Every panel follows a cohesive golden-hour aesthetic on a modern urban rooftop with concrete surfaces, ice, and soft city skyline silhouettes. The prompt ensures professional product photography standards with realistic lighting, dynamic motion capture, and vibrant color balance tailored to your product name and color palette. Designers launching soft drink campaigns, beverage brand managers preparing pitch decks, and creative agencies needing realistic mockups will find this prompt ready to deploy. ● Produces a complete 3×2 grid with hero, open-fizz, splash, lifestyle, macro, and brand panels in one 16:9 output ● Applies golden-hour lighting, urban rooftop atmosphere, and professional condensation and ice detail across all shots ● Accepts custom product name and color palette variables to match your brand identity ● Avoids common pitfalls like flat soda, dented cans, overexposed highlights, and cartoon aesthetics through explicit negative guidance ## Prompt

```
Create a vibrant 3×2 grid advertisement in 16:9 landscape format showcasing {{product-name}} carbonated soft drink in a slim aluminum can with bold graphic design and {{color-palette}}.

**Setting:** Modern urban rooftop with concrete slab surface, scattered ice cubes, light water droplets, soft sunset sky gradient, minimal city skyline silhouette. Golden hour sunlight with cool reflective highlights. Mood: cool, refreshing, modern.

**Panel 1 – Hero Can Shot:** Can centered upright with condensation droplets. Low-angle shot for bold presence.

**Panel 2 – Open & Fizz Moment:** Close-up of can being opened with visible fizz and carbonation. Backlit to highlight bubbles. Dynamic, crisp mood.

**Panel 3 – Ice & Splash Detail:** Soda splash with ice cubes frozen mid-air. High-speed capture lighting. Energetic, cold mood.

**Panel 4 – Lifestyle Moment:** Young adult holding the soda during casual rooftop hangout. Warm sunset glow. Relaxed, social mood.

**Panel 5 – Can Texture Close-Up:** Macro shot of matte-finish aluminum texture and condensation. Ultra-shallow depth of field. Fresh, cold mood.

**Panel 6 – Graphic Brand Finish:** Can centered against bold color-block sunset gradient. Clean studio lighting. Modern, eye-catching mood.

**Visual Style:** Professional product and lifestyle photography with high realism, dynamic splash capture, bold color composition. Bold modern sans-serif typography. Vibrant balanced exposure with soft but defined shadows.

**Avoid:** Flat soda, dented can, messy background, overexposed highlights, plastic-looking liquid, cartoon style, low-resolution detail, watermarks, text overlays.
```
