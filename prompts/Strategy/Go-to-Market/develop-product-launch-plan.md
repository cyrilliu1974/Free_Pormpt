# Product Launch Plan for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Product Launch Plan for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a full-lifecycle go-to-market strategy tailored for individual founders with limited budgets and time. This product launch prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered, step-by-step playbook covering pre-launch preparation, audience building through email newsletters and content marketing, tactical channel guidance for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter/X, a day-by-day launch week execution plan, and post-launch growth tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming three inputs - your product and audience, unique selling proposition, and current presence and budget - into a solopreneur-optimized roadmap that emphasizes high-leverage, low-cost tactics. Whether you're validating positioning, creating launch assets, or planning your first 90 days of visibility, the prompt organizes each phase with actionable sub-steps and resource recommendations. Reach for this prompt when you need a realistic, resource-conscious launch plan that accounts for the constraints and advantages of being a solo operator. ● Structures the launch into five clear phases, each with indented sub-steps and specific platform tactics. ● Focuses on free and low-cost channels like content marketing, lead magnets, ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter/X. ● Includes a day-by-day launch week playbook with messaging cadence and community engagement tips. ● Provides feedback loops and sustained visibility tactics to maintain momentum after launch day. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product launch strategist specializing in solopreneur go-to-market plans.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step product launch strategy tailored for a solo entrepreneur. Cover the full lifecycle from pre-launch preparation through post-launch growth, with emphasis on high-leverage, low-cost tactics suited to individual founders.

## Context
**Product & Market:**
{{product-and-audience}}

**Positioning:**
{{usp}}

**Resources:**
{{current-presence-and-budget}}

## Output
Deliver the launch plan as a numbered list with clearly labeled main sections and indented sub-steps. Include:

1. **Pre-Launch Preparation** – product readiness, positioning validation, asset creation
2. **Audience Building** – strategies for growing an email newsletter using free resources (content marketing, lead magnets, community engagement)
3. **Launch Channels** – tactical guidance for ProductHunt, HackerNews, Twitter/X, and other high-impact platforms for solopreneurs
4. **Launch Week Execution** – day-by-day playbook, messaging cadence, community engagement
5. **Post-Launch Growth** – feedback loops, iteration priorities, sustained visibility tactics

For each section, provide actionable steps, specific resource recommendations, and solopreneur-friendly tips that maximize impact within tight budget and time constraints.
```
