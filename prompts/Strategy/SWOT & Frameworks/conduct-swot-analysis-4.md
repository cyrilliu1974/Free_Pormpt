# SWOT Analysis Prompt for Strategic Planning

## 簡介

The SWOT Analysis Prompt for Strategic Planning is a free AI prompt that conducts structured competitive assessments for products, services, and business units. This SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a systematic evaluation of internal strengths and weaknesses alongside external opportunities and threats. It produces a markdown table organizing 3-5 insights per quadrant, plus prioritized strategic recommendations grounded in competitive dynamics and market trends. Teams use it for product launches, competitive reviews, strategic planning sessions, and quarterly business assessments. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting custom business context and competitive landscape details through two variables. Marketing consultants, product managers, business strategists, and startup founders reach for this prompt when they need disciplined strategic analysis without hiring external consultants. ● Produces a clean markdown SWOT matrix with 3-5 numbered items per quadrant for easy sharing and presentation ● Delivers 3-5 prioritized strategic recommendations that connect findings to differentiated competitive moves ● Accepts business-context and competitive-landscape variables to tailor analysis to your market position ● Applies a marketing-consultant perspective trained on competitive positioning, market trends, and internal capabilities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing consultant specializing in competitive positioning and strategic planning.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) for the specified product or service. Deliver actionable insights grounded in competitive dynamics, market trends, and internal capabilities.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{competitive-landscape}}

## Analysis Framework

**Strengths** (internal factors)
Identify 3-5 key advantages:
- What does this offering do better than competitors?
- What unique resources, capabilities, or cost advantages does it possess?
- What do customers consistently recognize as strengths?

**Weaknesses** (internal factors)
Identify 3-5 critical limitations:
- What aspects need improvement?
- What do customers or prospects perceive as weaknesses?
- What factors are losing sales or market share?

**Opportunities** (external factors)
Identify 3-5 promising avenues:
- What market, technology, or regulatory changes favor this offering?
- What shifts in customer behavior, demographics, or social patterns create openings?
- What competitor gaps or unmet needs exist?

**Threats** (external factors)
Identify 3-5 significant risks:
- What obstacles or competitive moves pose danger?
- What technological, regulatory, or quality standard changes threaten position?
- Which internal weaknesses could escalate into existential risks?

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

1. **SWOT Matrix**: A markdown table with four quadrants (Strengths | Weaknesses | Opportunities | Threats), each containing 3-5 numbered items.

2. **Strategic Recommendations**: 3-5 concrete, prioritized actions that leverage strengths, address weaknesses, capitalize on opportunities, or mitigate threats. Focus on differentiated moves that could outperform competitors.
```
