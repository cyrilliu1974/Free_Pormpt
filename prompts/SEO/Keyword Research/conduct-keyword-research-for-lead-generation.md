# Keyword Research for Lead Generation Prompt

## 簡介

The Keyword Research for Lead Generation Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes industry-specific search terms and delivers a prioritized list of keywords optimized for attracting qualified business leads. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT helps digital marketers identify 15-25 high-potential keywords by balancing search volume against difficulty, then organizes them in a markdown table sorted by lead generation potential. It works by taking your business context - industry, target audience pain points, products, geographic focus, and competition level - and producing a detailed report that includes search metrics and a strategic analysis highlighting quick wins and long-term targets. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need data-driven keyword selection for SEO campaigns, paid search, or content planning focused on converting search traffic into leads. ● Produces a sortable markdown table of 15-25 keywords with search volume and difficulty ratings ● Identifies quick-win opportunities (high volume, low competition) and strategic long-term targets ● Includes a priority analysis explaining why the top 3-5 keywords offer the strongest lead generation potential ● Adapts to any industry, niche, or geographic market when you provide business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in keyword research and lead generation.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to identify high-potential lead generation opportunities. Analyze industry-specific keywords, their search volumes, and difficulty levels to prioritize terms that will attract qualified leads.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Provide specific details about:
- Industry and niche
- Target audience demographics and pain points
- Primary products or services offered
- Geographic focus (local, regional, national, global)
- Competition level (high, medium, or low)

## Output
Deliver a detailed keyword research report in markdown table format with three columns:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Keyword Difficulty |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|

Sort the table by the most promising keywords for lead generation, balancing high search volume with manageable keyword difficulty. Include 15-25 keywords that represent the best opportunities.

After the table, provide a brief analysis section that:
- Highlights the top 3-5 priority keywords and why they offer the strongest lead generation potential
- Notes any patterns or insights from the research
- Suggests quick wins (high volume, low difficulty) and long-term targets (high volume, high difficulty)
```
