# Email Newsletter Writing Prompt for Marketing Campaigns

## 簡介

The Email Newsletter Writing Prompt for Marketing Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused email newsletters for businesses promoting products or services to specific audiences. This email newsletter prompt for ChatGPT guides text models through crafting every component of a promotional email: attention-grabbing subject lines, opening hooks that speak to reader pain points, body sections that weave features with benefits through storytelling, integrated social proof elements like testimonials or statistics, and closing calls-to-action that drive conversions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing newsletters formatted for readability with short paragraphs, subheadings, and strategic white space. Marketing teams use it to maintain consistent brand voice across campaigns, product launches, and customer retention efforts. Reach for this prompt when you need audience-tailored newsletter copy that balances persuasion with authenticity, or when you want to standardize your email content production process across multiple campaigns. ● Produces complete newsletter drafts including subject line, opening hook, body narrative, social proof integration, and explicit next-step CTAs ● Structures content using logical flow principles so each section builds momentum toward the conversion goal ● Adapts tone and messaging based on your target audience, product specifications, and brand positioning variables ● Formats output for scanability with short paragraphs, subheadings, and white space that improve email engagement metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing specialist crafting newsletters that drive engagement and build brand loyalty.

## Task
Write a complete email newsletter that promotes the product or service while resonating with the target audience. Structure each section to build logically on the previous one, creating a coherent narrative from subject line through final call-to-action.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Product/service:** {{product-service}}
**Brand positioning:** {{brand-positioning}}

## Output
Deliver the newsletter with these components:

**Subject line:** Create an attention-grabbing line that speaks directly to audience pain points or desires.

**Opening:** Hook the reader immediately with a statement or question that resonates with their situation.

**Body:** 
- Present the product/service by highlighting key features and their concrete benefits
- Incorporate a brief story or scenario that creates emotional connection
- Use persuasive language that emphasizes value and urgency
- Include social proof (testimonial, statistic, or case study result) to build credibility
- Guide the reader toward the desired action with clear, compelling calls-to-action

**Closing:** Reinforce the main benefit and provide explicit next steps.

Format for easy scanning: short paragraphs, subheadings where appropriate, and strategic white space. Ensure each section flows naturally into the next, building momentum toward conversion.
```
