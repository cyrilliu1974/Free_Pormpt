# Food Delivery App UI Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Food Delivery App UI Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that produces polished mobile app interface designs for food delivery platforms, UX designers, and product teams. This food delivery app UI mockup prompt for Midjourney generates a straight-on phone screen view featuring a hero section with your chosen dish photography, a multi-item product grid displaying dishes with visible prices, a bold "Order Now" call-to-action button, and clean bottom tab navigation - all rendered in a fresh green and orange color palette. The prompt works with Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to create interface designs that balance appetizing visuals with functional layout conventions. Designers use it to prototype ordering flows, pitch client concepts, build portfolio pieces, or explore color-and-layout variations without launching design tools.
