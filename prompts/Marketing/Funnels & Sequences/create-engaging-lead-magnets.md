# Lead Magnet Strategy Builder for Content Marketing

## 簡介

The Lead Magnet Strategy Builder for Content Marketing is a free AI prompt that designs complete lead magnet strategies to attract qualified leads and drive contact information exchange for content marketers and business owners. This lead magnet prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's core challenges, creates benefit-driven titles and descriptions, structures logical content progressions from problem to solution, and delivers conversion-focused calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting a structured markdown table with content sections, key points, and value propositions. Real-world use cases include building ebook outlines, designing checklist resources, planning webinar content, and structuring guide downloads that convert visitors into subscribers. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive lead magnet framework that connects audience pain points to actionable solutions, or when you want to ensure every element of your content offer reinforces your conversion goals. ● Identifies the three core pain points your target audience faces and maps them to content solutions ● Creates compelling titles and descriptions that immediately communicate tangible value to prospects ● Structures 4-6 logical content sections that guide readers from problem awareness to solution implementation ● Delivers clear, conversion-focused CTAs designed specifically for contact information exchange ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in lead magnet development and conversion optimization.

## Task
Create a complete lead magnet strategy that attracts qualified leads and drives contact information exchange. Deliver:

1. **Audience pain point analysis** — identify the 3 core challenges your target audience faces
2. **Compelling title and description** — craft a benefit-driven headline and 2-3 sentence overview that immediately communicates value
3. **Content outline** — structure 4-6 main sections/chapters that logically progress from problem to solution
4. **Section summaries** — write a brief, enticing description for each section that highlights the takeaway
5. **Call-to-action** — design a clear, conversion-focused CTA for lead capture

## Context
{{business-context}}

Ensure each element reinforces the previous one, building a cohesive narrative from pain point through solution to action.

## Output
Deliver as a markdown table:

| Content Section | Key Points | Value Proposition |
|----------------|------------|-------------------|
| [section name] | [2-3 bullets] | [why it matters] |

Include the title, description, and CTA above the table.
```
