# Social Media Customer Service Protocol Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Customer Service Protocol Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive support guidelines for businesses managing customer service across multiple social channels. This social media customer service prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete protocol covering response time standards, brand voice alignment, escalation procedures, crisis management strategies, and platform-specific best practices. You provide your company profile - including active platforms, brand voice characteristics, target audience, and coverage model - and receive a structured implementation guide organized into six core sections: Response Standards, Communication Guidelines, Escalation Framework, Crisis Protocols, Platform Playbooks, and Quality & Training. Real use cases include training new support teams, standardizing multi-platform responses, and preparing crisis communication workflows. This prompt is built for customer service managers, social media teams, and operations leads who need documented protocols to ensure every team member delivers consistent, on-brand support across Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, and other channels. ● Defines response time standards by platform and urgency level to set clear team expectations ● Provides tone and voice guidelines with scenario-specific examples for complaints, praise, and inquiries ● Structures escalation triggers and chain-of-command workflows for moving conversations private or involving management ● Includes crisis detection protocols with coordinated response and approval workflows for sensitive situations ● Delivers platform-specific playbooks addressing character limits, DM versus comment protocols, and native feature utilization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer service strategist specializing in social media support operations.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social media customer service protocol that ensures consistent, effective support across all platforms. The protocol must cover:

- Response time standards (by platform and urgency level)
- Tone and voice guidelines that align with brand identity
- Escalation procedures (when to move conversations private, when to involve management)
- Crisis management strategies (identifying emerging issues, coordinated response)
- Platform-specific best practices (character limits, DM vs. comment protocols, native features)
- Quality assurance and team accountability measures

## Context
{{company-profile}}

Include the company name, primary social media platforms (Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.), brand voice characteristics (formal/casual, playful/serious, technical/accessible), target audience demographics and preferences, and business hours/coverage model.

## Output
Deliver the protocol as a structured implementation guide with:

**I. Response Standards**
- Target response times by platform and query type
- Availability and coverage expectations

**II. Communication Guidelines**
- Brand voice application in support contexts
- Tone adjustments for different scenarios (complaints, praise, inquiries)
- Do's and don'ts with examples

**III. Escalation Framework**
- Triggers for escalation
- Chain of command
- Private messaging thresholds

**IV. Crisis Protocols**
- Early warning signs
- Response coordination
- Approval workflows for sensitive situations

**V. Platform Playbooks**
- Tailored tactics for each active platform
- Feature utilization (stories, polls, quick replies)

**VI. Quality & Training**
- Performance metrics
- Onboarding checklist

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout for immediate reference and team training.
```
