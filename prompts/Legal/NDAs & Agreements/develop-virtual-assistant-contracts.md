# Virtual Assistant Contract Template Generator

## 簡介

The Virtual Assistant Contract Template Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts comprehensive service agreements for freelancers and solopreneurs hiring or working as virtual assistants. This virtual assistant contract prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured eight-section agreement covering parties and effective dates, scope of work with clear deliverables and exclusions, payment terms including invoicing schedules and late fees, confidentiality and data protection obligations, intellectual property ownership and licensing, term and termination provisions with notice periods, liability limitations and dispute resolution, and general provisions establishing independent contractor status. Each section includes 3-5 specific clauses written in plain language that remains legally appropriate, with bracketed guidance showing where to customize rates, hours, jurisdiction, and service details. Use it when onboarding a VA, formalizing an ongoing relationship, or establishing clear expectations before work begins. ● Defines scope of work with specific deliverables, hours, and exclusions to prevent scope creep and misunderstandings ● Establishes clear payment terms, invoicing schedules, accepted methods, and late fee policies for smooth financial operations ● Includes confidentiality clauses and data protection obligations to safeguard sensitive client information ● Clarifies intellectual property ownership, licensing rights, and treatment of pre-existing materials ● Provides termination clauses with notice periods, grounds for immediate termination, and transition obligations for both parties ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal consultant specializing in service contracts for freelancers and solopreneurs.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive contract template for virtual assistant services that protects both parties' interests and establishes clear expectations. The contract must be legally sound, practical, and adaptable across different VA service types.

## Context
This template will be used by:
{{service-description}}

{{contract-requirements}}

## Output
Structure the contract with these sections, using clear headings and bullet points for individual clauses:

1. **Parties & Effective Date** – Identify service provider and client
2. **Scope of Work** – Detailed description of services, deliverables, hours/availability, and exclusions
3. **Payment Terms** – Rates, invoicing schedule, payment methods, late fees, expenses
4. **Confidentiality & Data Protection** – Non-disclosure obligations, handling of sensitive information, data retention
5. **Intellectual Property Rights** – Ownership of work product, licensing, pre-existing materials
6. **Term & Termination** – Contract duration, notice periods, grounds for immediate termination, transition obligations
7. **Liability & Indemnification** – Limitations of liability, warranties, dispute resolution
8. **General Provisions** – Independent contractor status, amendments, governing law, severability

For each section, provide 3-5 specific clauses that address common scenarios and protect the solopreneur's interests. Use plain language that is legally appropriate but accessible to non-lawyers. Include bracketed guidance [like this] where the user should customize specific details.
```
