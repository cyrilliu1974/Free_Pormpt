# Elevator Pitch Generator for Business Presentations

## 簡介

The Elevator Pitch Generator for Business Presentations is a free AI prompt that creates concise, compelling elevator pitches for entrepreneurs, marketers, and sales professionals. The prompt produces a five-part pitch structure - hook, problem, solution, benefits, and call-to-action - designed to be delivered in 30 to 60 seconds. This elevator pitch prompt for ChatGPT runs on text models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing your business context to craft a conversational narrative that captures attention and drives action. Use it when preparing for networking events, investor meetings, sales calls, or any situation where you need to communicate your value proposition quickly and memorably. ● Produces a hook that immediately grabs listener attention and sets the context. ● Identifies the specific pain point your target audience faces and positions your solution as uniquely suited to solve it. ● Emphasizes tangible benefits and outcomes rather than feature lists. ● Closes with a clear, actionable next step that moves the conversation forward. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in elevator pitches that capture attention and communicate value propositions clearly.

## Task
Craft a concise, compelling elevator pitch (30–60 seconds when spoken) that hooks the audience, identifies a problem, presents a unique solution, emphasizes benefits, and closes with a strong call-to-action.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze the target audience's needs, identify differentiators against competitors, and structure the pitch for maximum impact.

## Output
Deliver the elevator pitch in the following format:

**Hook**  
[Opening statement that immediately grabs attention]

**Problem**  
[The specific pain point or challenge the target audience faces]

**Solution**  
[How the business uniquely addresses this problem]

**Benefits**  
[The tangible value and outcomes customers gain]

**Call-to-Action**  
[Clear next step for the listener to take]

Each section should be 1–2 sentences. The complete pitch should feel conversational and be deliverable in under one minute.
```
