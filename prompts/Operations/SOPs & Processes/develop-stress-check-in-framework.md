# Stress Check-In Framework for Team Leaders

## 簡介

The Stress Check-In Framework for Team Leaders is a free AI prompt that creates a structured, adaptable system for monitoring and supporting team member well-being through regular stress check-ins. This stress check-in framework prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete guide covering frequency, duration, question prompts, active listening techniques, and follow-up strategies, all customized to your team size, industry, and work environment. Team leaders use it to establish consistent well-being practices that foster psychological safety and open dialogue. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a practical playbook that emphasizes empathy, confidentiality, and actionable next steps. This prompt is designed for managers, team leads, and HR professionals who want to implement regular emotional health check-ins without relying on generic scripts or external consultants. ● Outputs detailed guidance on scheduling, format, and setting for stress check-ins across different team sizes and work environments ● Provides opening, work-related, personal well-being, and closing question prompts that encourage honest conversation ● Includes active listening techniques, empathy guidelines, and confidentiality protocols to build trust ● Recommends follow-up actions and best practices for creating psychological safety and consistency ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced team leader with expertise in conducting stress check-ins, emotional intelligence, and team dynamics.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, customizable framework for conducting regular stress check-ins with team members. The framework must be practical, adaptable, and actionable for real-world application.

## Context
The user leads a team and needs a structured approach to monitor and support team member well-being through regular check-ins. Tailor the framework to:
- Team size: {{team-size}}
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Work environment: {{work-environment}}

Emphasize active listening, empathy, and psychological safety. Avoid jargon; provide clear, concise guidance.

## Output
Organize the framework using this structure:

### 1. Framework Overview
Provide a brief overview of the stress check-in framework, its purpose, and how it adapts to different team sizes, industries, and work environments.

### 2. Frequency and Duration
**Frequency:** Recommend how often stress check-ins should be conducted.

**Duration:** Suggest the ideal duration for each check-in session.

**Scheduling Considerations:** Discuss factors to consider when scheduling check-ins, such as team schedules and workload.

### 3. Format and Setting
**Format:** Describe the format of the check-ins (e.g., one-on-one meetings, small group discussions, or a combination).

**Setting:** Recommend the most appropriate setting (e.g., in-person, virtual, or hybrid).

**Communication Channels:** Discuss suitable communication channels (e.g., video conferencing, phone calls, in-person meetings).

### 4. Question Prompts
**Opening Questions:** Provide examples to start the check-in and create a comfortable atmosphere.

**Work-Related Questions:** Suggest questions focused on work-related stress, challenges, and successes.

**Personal Well-being Questions:** Offer questions addressing personal well-being and work-life balance.

**Closing Questions:** Include questions to wrap up and set action items or follow-up steps.

### 5. Active Listening and Empathy Guidelines
**Active Listening Tips:** Provide tips such as paraphrasing, asking clarifying questions, and maintaining eye contact.

**Demonstrating Empathy:** Offer guidance on validating team members' feelings and experiences.

### 6. Addressing Concerns and Follow-up
**Addressing Concerns:** Discuss strategies for offering support, resources, or solutions.

**Follow-up Actions:** Recommend steps for following up on action items or concerns.

**Confidentiality and Exceptions:** Discuss maintaining confidentiality and exceptions (e.g., risk of harm).

### 7. Best Practices and Tips
**Creating a Safe Space:** Offer tips for creating a comfortable environment for sharing.

**Adapting to Individual Needs:** Provide guidance on tailoring check-ins to individual preferences.

**Maintaining Consistency and Fairness:** Discuss conducting check-ins consistently across the team.

**Leading by Example:** Encourage leaders to model self-care, stress management, and open communication.
```
