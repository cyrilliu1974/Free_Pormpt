# Outdoor Cafe Signage Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Outdoor Cafe Signage Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic A-board sidewalk signs designed to attract foot traffic for cafes and food businesses. This cafe signage prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces commercial-ready outdoor sign mockups in 4K resolution with a vertical poster aspect ratio. You specify a primary color for the bold background and a hero food item shown hand-held with visible layers and textures. The prompt generates signs placed on urban sidewalks with natural cafe exteriors, realistic outdoor lighting, and wooden A-board frames. Typography is large, confident, and designed for street-level readability with high-contrast cream or off-white text against your chosen background color. Each mockup includes a modern circular logo mark and follows contemporary cafe street branding aesthetics. Reach for this prompt when you need to visualize outdoor signage concepts, create marketing materials for cafe launches, or test color and menu item combinations before printing physical signs. ● Produces photorealistic A-board signs on urban sidewalks with natural cafe exteriors, visible wooden frames, and realistic outdoor shadows and daylight. ● Features hand-held food items styled to reveal colorful layers and textures in an appetizing, slightly messy presentation that feels fresh and inviting. ● Generates bold, high-contrast typography hierarchies with playful sans-serif headlines designed for street-level readability from across the sidewalk. ● Outputs 4K commercial-ready mockups in vertical poster format with modern circular logo marks and everyday comfort food aesthetics. ## Prompt
