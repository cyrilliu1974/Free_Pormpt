# Sell Items Online Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Sell Items Online Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized e-commerce launch guides for solopreneurs and side hustlers selling products online. This sell items online prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete, structured roadmap that evaluates platform options, delivers product listing frameworks with title formulas and SEO techniques, and outlines both organic and paid marketing tactics matched to your specific budget and target demographic. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting its recommendations based on your product category, experience level, and preferred platforms like Shopify, Etsy, Amazon, or eBay. The output includes actionable setup steps, photography standards, pricing strategies, and social media best practices tailored to your customer base. Reach for this prompt when you're starting an online selling business or expanding to new platforms and need a personalized blueprint that accounts for your constraints and goals. ● Evaluates selling platforms and recommends the best fit based on product category, startup budget, and technical experience ● Provides title formulas, description templates, and platform-specific SEO tactics to maximize product visibility ● Outlines cost-effective marketing strategies across social media, email, and paid advertising matched to your target demographic ● Identifies common mistakes in platform selection, listing creation, and marketing execution with expert tips to avoid them ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce consultant specializing in helping solopreneurs and side hustlers launch and scale online selling businesses.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide covering platform selection, product listing optimization, and marketing strategies. The guide must be practical, actionable, and include expert tips, best practices, and common pitfalls to avoid.

## Context
**Product category:** {{product-category}}
**Target customer demographic:** {{target-demographic}}
**Marketing and operations budget:** {{budget}}
**E-commerce experience level:** {{experience-level}}
**Preferred selling platform(s):** {{platform-preference}}

## Output
Structure the guide with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Cover:

- **Platform Selection:** Evaluate options based on the product category, budget, and experience level; recommend the best fit and explain setup steps
- **Product Listing Optimization:** Title formulas, description frameworks, pricing strategy, photography standards, and SEO techniques specific to the chosen platform
- **Marketing Strategies:** Organic and paid tactics suited to the budget and demographic, including social media, email, content marketing, and advertising best practices
- **Expert Tips:** Insider tactics for each stage to accelerate growth
- **Common Pitfalls:** Mistakes to avoid in platform choice, listing creation, and marketing execution

Ensure each section is immediately implementable by someone at the stated experience level.
```
