# First Customer Acquisition Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The First Customer Acquisition Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces 10 tactical, ready-to-execute strategies for solopreneurs and bootstrapped founders launching a product with zero paying customers. This first customer acquisition prompt for ChatGPT creates ideas designed to be set up in hours and produce results in days, not months. Each tactic includes numbered steps, alignment rationale for your pricing model (B2B, B2C, freemium, or subscription), and is optimized for businesses with no existing audience or customer base. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your business description, target audience, and pricing model as inputs. Real use cases include validating a new SaaS, launching a consulting service, or testing product-market fit before investing in long-term marketing infrastructure. Reach for this prompt when you need practical, manual, high-touch tactics that skip the overused playbook and prioritize free or low-budget approaches aligned to your specific audience and revenue model. ● Generates 10 named tactics, each with 3+ concrete steps and reasoning for why it fits your pricing model and audience. ● Prioritizes speed: ideas executable in a few hours with results expected in days, not weeks. ● Works with zero existing customers - no referral schemes or testimonial-dependent tactics. ● Balances free manual methods with a few budget-conscious paid options, tailored to B2B or B2C contexts. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You generate first-customer acquisition ideas for solopreneurs. You know which tactics produce results in days, not months, and which ones require zero audience to start.

## Context

{{business-description}} is a new business with 0 paying customers. The target audience is {{target-audience}}. The pricing model is {{pricing-model}}.

## Task

Generate 10 user acquisition ideas tailored to this exact situation.

Rules for every idea:
- Can be set up and executed in a few hours
- Produces results in days, not months
- Works with 0 existing customers (no referral programs)
- Specific: include the exact steps to take
- Creative: skip the overused playbook
- Free ideas take priority, but include a few that require a budget
- Align the tactic to the pricing model (e.g. cold outreach for B2B, Reddit ads for B2C)
- Manual and high-touch tactics are fine at this stage

## Output

For each idea:

**[Idea name]**

Steps:
1. [First concrete action]
2. [Second concrete action]
3. [Third concrete action]

Include a brief note on why it fits this pricing model and audience.
```
