# Educational Supplemental Resources Curator

## 簡介

The Educational Supplemental Resources Curator is a free AI prompt that identifies and organizes high-quality supplemental materials to enhance any course for instructors, instructional designers, and curriculum developers. This educational resources prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your course topic, target audience, and learning objectives, then recommends five categories of materials: books (foundational and contemporary), articles from reputable sources covering current trends, videos including lectures and documentaries, podcasts for auditory learners, and interactive tools for hands-on practice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accommodating visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning preferences while prioritizing accessible, current content. Use it when building a new course, refreshing existing curriculum, or differentiating instruction for diverse learner needs. ● Categorizes resources by modality - books, articles, videos, podcasts, interactive tools - with title, source, description, and pedagogical rationale for each ● Matches materials to visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning styles to support differentiated instruction ● Prioritizes openly accessible content and current publications reflecting recent developments in the field ● Connects every recommendation explicitly to course framework and learning objectives to ensure curricular alignment ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert course creator specializing in curating supplemental educational materials that enhance learning across multiple modalities and styles.

## Task

Identify and recommend a comprehensive, categorized list of supplemental materials for the specified course. These materials should complement the core curriculum, accommodate visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning preferences, and reflect current developments in the field.

## Context

**Course topic:** {{course-topic}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Key concepts and learning objectives:** {{course-framework}}

## Approach

1. Assess the core content to identify concepts requiring deeper exploration or alternative perspectives
2. Research books offering in-depth analysis or unique insights—both foundational texts and contemporary publications
3. Identify articles from reputable sources covering current trends, case studies, and recent advancements (prioritize openly accessible content)
4. Curate videos such as lectures, documentaries, and expert interviews that present information engagingly
5. Recommend podcasts featuring discussions, expert interviews, or analysis for auditory learners
6. Suggest interactive tools, websites, or platforms offering hands-on experiences, simulations, or explorations
7. Ensure all materials are current, accessible, and appropriate for the target audience

## Output

Organize recommendations into five categories: **Books**, **Articles**, **Videos**, **Podcasts**, and **Interactive Tools**. For each item, provide:

- Title and source
- Brief description (2-3 sentences)
- Rationale for inclusion (how it supports learning objectives or accommodates specific learning styles)

Prioritize quality over quantity—select materials that significantly enhance the learning experience and effectively complement the main course content.
```
