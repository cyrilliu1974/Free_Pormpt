# Customer Journey Story Prompt for Personal Branding

## 簡介

The Customer Journey Story Prompt for Personal Branding is a free AI prompt that helps marketers and entrepreneurs craft emotionally resonant narratives mapping how their brand transforms customer lives. This customer journey story prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a five-stage narrative: problem recognition, search, discovery, engagement, and transformation. You provide your brand context and target audience, and the prompt outputs a structured story with headings for each stage, bullet points detailing pain points and brand touchpoints, and a transformation section showing measurable and emotional impact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, using concrete details and cause-and-effect relationships to make each story feel authentic yet representative of the broader audience. Marketing professionals, personal brand builders, and content strategists use it to communicate value propositions through relatable storytelling rather than feature lists. ● Maps the complete journey from pain point to transformation with clear stage headings ● Integrates emotional shifts alongside functional benefits at each customer touchpoint ● Uses dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow between problem, intervention, and outcome ● Outputs bullet-point structure ideal for presentations, website copy, and pitch decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert storyteller and marketing strategist specializing in customer journey narratives.

## Task
Craft a compelling story that shows how {{brand-context}}'s products or services solve customer pain points and transform their lives. Structure the narrative as a customer journey from problem recognition through solution discovery to outcome.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

At each stage of the journey:
- Identify the customer's pain points and needs
- Show how the brand's unique value proposition addresses them
- Use concrete details and emotional elements to make the story relatable
- Demonstrate clear cause-and-effect relationships between the customer's situation, the brand's intervention, and the results

## Output
Structure your narrative with:
- **Heading for each journey stage** (problem recognition, search, discovery, engagement, transformation)
- **Bullet points** under each heading capturing key pain points, brand touchpoints, features in action, and emotional shifts
- **Concluding transformation section** that demonstrates measurable and emotional impact on the customer's life

Make the story specific enough to feel real while remaining representative of the broader target audience experience.
```
