# Vocabulary Expansion Plan With Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The Vocabulary Expansion Plan With Dependency Grammar is a free AI prompt that creates a structured learning plan to help writers acquire and integrate new words using linguistic frameworks. This vocabulary expansion prompt for ChatGPT guides you through selecting genre-appropriate words, understanding their syntactic roles through dependency grammar, and practicing them with exercises tailored to your learning style. It delivers a five-part numbered plan covering word selection strategy, integration techniques that map head-dependent relationships, daily practice exercises, retention methods with spaced repetition, and progress tracking milestones. Writers working in any genre - from academic essays to creative fiction - use it to systematically grow their active vocabulary while maintaining grammatical precision. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Tailors word selection to your specific writing genre, current level, and daily acquisition targets ● Uses dependency grammar to show syntactic roles and head-dependent relationships for each new word ● Includes practice exercises aligned with visual, auditory, or kinesthetic learning preferences ● Provides spaced repetition schedules and self-assessment criteria to track vocabulary retention ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert linguist and writing coach specializing in vocabulary development and dependency grammar frameworks.

## Task
Create a personalized vocabulary expansion plan that helps the writer acquire new words, use them in proper grammatical context, and integrate them naturally into their writing practice.

## Context
**Writer profile:**
{{writer-profile}}
(Include: writing genre, current vocabulary level, specific writing goals, target number of new words per day, and preferred learning style)

**Dependency grammar framework:** Use dependency grammar principles to demonstrate how new words function within sentence structures—showing head-dependent relationships, syntactic roles, and contextual positioning.

## Output
Deliver your vocabulary expansion plan as a numbered list. Each main point should include:

1. **Word selection strategy** tailored to the writer's genre and level
   a. Criteria for choosing high-impact vocabulary
   b. Sources and resources specific to their genre
   c. Examples of target words with dependency relationships illustrated

2. **Integration techniques** using dependency grammar
   a. How to identify the syntactic role of each new word
   b. Sentence construction patterns that accommodate the vocabulary
   c. Concrete examples showing head-dependent relationships

3. **Daily practice exercises** aligned with their learning style
   a. Structured writing prompts incorporating new words
   b. Dependency tree analysis activities
   c. Progressive difficulty levels

4. **Retention and reinforcement strategies**
   a. Spaced repetition schedule
   b. Contextual review methods
   c. Genre-specific application techniques

5. **Progress tracking and adjustment**
   a. Milestones aligned with their writing goals
   b. Self-assessment criteria
   c. Plan modification triggers

For each numbered point, provide lettered sub-points with detailed explanations and concrete examples relevant to the writer's profile.
```
