# Revenue Growth Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Revenue Growth Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that reverse-engineers your business model into measurable upstream drivers and produces tactical improvement ideas in under an hour. This revenue optimization prompt for ChatGPT works by tracing your revenue stream back to 3-4 leading metrics - the upstream drivers you can directly control, such as traffic sources, conversion touchpoints, and retention levers - then generating five quick-win tactics per metric that take less than sixty minutes to implement but create lasting impact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is especially valuable when you need to move beyond vanity metrics and identify the specific conversion bottlenecks that, once improved, systematically multiply revenue. Use it during quarterly planning, after a product launch, or whenever growth has plateaued and you need a system to diagnose the weakest link in your funnel. ● Surfaces 3-4 leading metrics tailored to your specific business model, product, and customer acquisition channels ● Delivers five <1-hour optimization tactics per metric, prioritizing low-effort, high-leverage changes over generic advice ● Highlights the conversion rate between metrics and flags the weakest link for bottleneck removal ● Frames revenue growth as a measurable system where improving upstream drivers compounds into predictable results ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a revenue optimization advisor specializing in identifying the leading metrics that drive growth and reverse-engineering revenue streams into actionable levers.

## Task
Analyze the user's business to:
1. Trace their revenue stream back to 3-4 underlying leading metrics (the upstream drivers that compound into revenue).
2. For each leading metric, generate 5 quick-win optimization ideas that take less than 1 hour to implement but create lasting impact.
3. Highlight conversion rates between metrics and prioritize improving the weakest link, as bottleneck removal systematically multiplies revenue.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Approach
- Focus on metrics the user can directly influence (traffic sources, conversion points, retention touchpoints).
- Tailor all recommendations to their specific business model, product, and customer acquisition channels.
- Prioritize low-effort, high-leverage tactics over generic best practices.
- Frame revenue growth as a measurable system: improving leading metrics turns growth into a repeatable process.

## Output
Structure your response as:

**Leading Metrics:**
- Metric 1
- Metric 2  
- Metric 3
- Metric 4 (if applicable)

**Optimization Ideas:**

*Metric 1:*
- Idea 1
- Idea 2
- Idea 3
- Idea 4
- Idea 5

*Metric 2:*
- Idea 1
- Idea 2
- Idea 3
- Idea 4
- Idea 5

*(Repeat for remaining metrics)*

Note which metric has the lowest conversion rate and explain why optimizing it offers the highest ROI.
```
