# Farewell Communication Plan Prompt for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Farewell Communication Plan Prompt for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured employee departure communications for human resources professionals, internal communications managers, and people operations teams. This farewell communication prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes departure context and organizational guidelines to produce audience-specific messaging templates (direct reports, peers, leadership, clients, external stakeholders), a sequenced rollout timeline coordinating who receives what message when, and protocols for handling sensitive situations or confidentiality requirements. HR teams use it when an employee resigns or retires and the organization needs to communicate the transition internally and externally while maintaining goodwill, celebrating contributions authentically, and protecting employer brand reputation. ● Produces audience-tailored message templates that balance appreciation with professionalism and adapt tone for different stakeholder groups while maintaining consistency. ● Generates a sequenced communication timeline showing the order and timing of announcements across channels to avoid surprises and coordinate messaging. ● Provides sensitive-situation guidance for managing confidentiality, addressing questions, and handling departures with reputational or morale implications. ● Highlights genuine contributions and positions transitions positively without generic platitudes, rooted in the departing employee's actual tenure and impact. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational communication strategist specializing in employee departure communications that maintain goodwill, celebrate contributions, and strengthen employer reputation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive farewell communication plan with stakeholder-specific messaging templates and a clear rollout timeline.

## Context
Employee departures shape organizational culture, morale, and brand perception. Effective farewell communications balance appreciation and professionalism, honor genuine contributions, position transitions positively, and adapt tone for different audiences while maintaining message consistency.

## Input
**Departure context:** {{departure-context}}  
(Include: employee name, role, tenure, key contributions, departure circumstances, sensitivity or confidentiality requirements, and departure date)

**Communication guidelines:** {{communication-guidelines}}  
(Include: organizational tone, cultural values, brand voice principles, specific audience segments requiring communication, and any relevant team dynamics)

## Output
Structure your response with these sections:

**1. Departure Analysis & Key Messages**  
Core themes, genuine contributions to highlight, and tone recommendations

**2. Audience-Specific Messaging Templates**  
Ready-to-adapt templates for direct reports, peers, leadership, clients, and external stakeholders, with rationale for each approach

**3. Communication Timeline & Rollout Plan**  
Sequenced schedule showing who receives what message when, coordinated across all channels

**4. Sensitive Situation Guidance**  
Protocols for handling concerns, maintaining confidentiality, and managing questions

Provide practical, immediately implementable materials that leadership can use with minimal adaptation.
```
