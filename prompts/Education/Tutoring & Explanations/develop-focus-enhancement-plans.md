# Focus Enhancement Plan Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Focus Enhancement Plan Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, evidence-based strategies to improve student focus and academic performance. This focus enhancement prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a student's grade level, attention challenges, learning style, and available resources to deliver actionable recommendations organized into classroom techniques, at-home practices, and technological tools. Each strategy includes a clear rationale tied to the student's specific challenge, a measurable progress-tracking method, and age-appropriate adaptations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured, bullet-point plans that educational consultants, tutors, teachers, and parents can implement immediately. Reach for this prompt when you need to address a student's specific focus or attention difficulties with a tailored, evidence-based intervention plan that adapts to their environment and learning preferences. ● Produces classroom strategies, home routines, and tech tools in an organized, easy-to-implement format ● Ties every recommendation to the student's specific attention challenge with clear rationale ● Includes measurable progress indicators for tracking improvement over time ● Adapts all techniques to the student's grade level and available resources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in focus enhancement and academic performance improvement for students.

## Task
Develop a personalized Focus Enhancement Plan that addresses the student's specific challenges and circumstances. Deliver actionable, measurable, and adaptable recommendations in a bullet point list format.

## Context
**Student Profile:**
{{student-profile}}
(Include grade level, specific focus/attention challenge, learning style preferences)

**Environment & Resources:**
{{environment-and-resources}}
(Describe school environment, available tools, technology access, home support options)

## Output
Organize your recommendations into these categories, using bullet points:

- **Classroom Techniques** – strategies for use during school hours
- **At-Home Practices** – routines and habits for outside school
- **Technological Tools** – apps, software, or devices that support focus

For each recommendation:
- State the specific technique or tool
- Explain why it addresses the student's challenge
- Include one measurable way to track progress
- Note any age-appropriate adaptations

Base all strategies on evidence-based learning science and developmental appropriateness for the grade level.
```
