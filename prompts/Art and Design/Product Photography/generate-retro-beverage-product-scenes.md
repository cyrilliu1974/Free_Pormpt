# Retro Beverage Product Photography Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Retro Beverage Product Photography Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates stylized lifestyle product shots featuring a beverage can in a nostalgic kitchen setting with a model, open refrigerator, and vintage props. The prompt produces a specific composition: a young woman seated on the floor holding your product can beside a retro-style refrigerator stocked with matching beverages, Ramune bottles, and colorful accessories, all lit with warm, muted tones that evoke analog film photography. This retro beverage product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana allows you to customize the product can and aesthetic style through two variables, making it ideal for drink brands creating social media content, e-commerce visuals, or campaign hero images without a studio shoot.
