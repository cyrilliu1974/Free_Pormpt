# Urban Night Portrait Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Urban Night Portrait Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates realistic street photography scenes featuring your subject in vibrant nighttime cityscapes with authentic iPhone-style aesthetics. This urban portrait prompt for ChatGPT image models (Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana) places your uploaded subject near the curb in customizable locations during your chosen time conditions, holding a specified object while wearing detailed styled clothing. The prompt generates scenes with vertical LED billboards, city traffic with glowing brake lights, wet asphalt reflecting neon magenta and icy blue lights in puddles, and layered urban details like storefront signs and scaffolding. It explicitly preserves 100% facial accuracy from your reference photo, making it ideal for personal branding, social media content, and creative portfolios that require consistent identity across atmospheric city scenes. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-looking urban portraits without coordinating night shoots in busy metropolitan areas. ● Maintains exact facial features from uploaded reference photos while integrating subjects into detailed urban nightscapes with neon lighting and wet reflections. ● Customizable location, time condition, and held object variables let you adapt the scene while preserving the core atmospheric urban aesthetic. ● Generates layered city details including LED billboards with legible text, moving traffic with realistic lighting, puddle reflections, and authentic street debris. ● Produces iPhone-style photography with natural color grading, intimate framing, and the electric energy of nighttime metropolitan environments. ## Prompt
