# Brand Positioning Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Positioning Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that delivers a multi-dimensional strategic analysis of a company's market position for brand strategists, marketers, and business consultants. This brand positioning analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine five core dimensions: company overview, unique value proposition, target market segments, competitive landscape with relative strengths and weaknesses, and brand perception insights. It structures findings using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical clarity between concepts, producing a complete positioning statement and actionable recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is ideal for competitive analysis projects, rebranding initiatives, market entry planning, and strategic reviews where understanding how a brand is perceived and positioned relative to competitors is essential. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic framework to evaluate brand positioning, compare competitive dynamics, or prepare strategic recommendations grounded in available market data rather than speculation. ● Structures analysis across company overview, value proposition, target markets, competitive landscape, and brand perception in a logical, dependency-grammar format ● Produces a formal positioning statement and actionable recommendations to strengthen market differentiation ● Compares competitors with specific strengths and weaknesses relative to the analyzed company ● Bases all findings on available information, avoiding speculation or unsupported claims ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a brand strategist conducting a comprehensive positioning analysis using dependency grammar principles to ensure clear, logical relationships between concepts.

## Task

Analyze {{company-name}}'s brand positioning across five dimensions: company overview, value proposition, target markets, competitive landscape, and brand perception. Provide specific, actionable recommendations for strengthening positioning. Base all findings on available information; do not speculate.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Company Overview**
- Founded in [year], {{company-name}} is a [company type] that [description of products/services].
- Key facts:
  - Headquarters: [location]
  - Number of employees: [number]
  - Revenue (latest fiscal year): [revenue]

**Value Proposition**
{{company-name}}'s unique value proposition centers on:
1. [Value prop 1]
2. [Value prop 2]
3. [Value prop 3]

**Target Markets**
{{company-name}} primarily targets the following market segments:
- [Segment 1]: [description]
- [Segment 2]: [description]
- [Segment 3]: [description]

**Competitive Landscape**
Key competitors in {{company-name}}'s space include:
1. [Competitor 1]: [positioning analysis and strengths/weaknesses relative to {{company-name}}]
2. [Competitor 2]: [positioning analysis and strengths/weaknesses relative to {{company-name}}]
3. [Competitor 3]: [positioning analysis and strengths/weaknesses relative to {{company-name}}]

**Brand Perception Survey**
A survey of [number] consumers in {{company-name}}'s target markets revealed:
- [X]% associate the brand with [attribute 1]
- [X]% perceive {{company-name}} as [attribute 2] compared to competitors
- [X]% would consider purchasing from {{company-name}} based on [factor]

**Positioning Statement**
For [target market], {{company-name}} is the [category] that [key benefit/promise] because [reason to believe].

**Positioning Analysis Summary**
In summary, {{company-name}}'s current brand positioning is [describe positioning] within the [category], driven by its [key differentiators]. To strengthen and clarify its positioning, {{company-name}} should focus on [recommendations].
```
