# Critical Thinking Prompts Generator for AI-Era Education

## 簡介

The Critical Thinking Prompts Generator for AI-Era Education is a free AI prompt that creates inquiry-based questions designed to develop analytical and evaluative skills for educators teaching in modern classrooms. This critical thinking prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces 30-48 grade-adaptable prompts organized across six pedagogical categories: AI literacy and ethics, source evaluation and bias detection, creative and divergent thinking, metacognitive reflection, collaborative problem-solving, and real-world application. Teachers input their teaching context (grade level, subject, unit topic) and receive classroom-ready questions with implementation notes for differentiation. Use it when designing lesson plans that go beyond information retrieval to build skills in questioning sources, identifying biases, evaluating AI-generated content, understanding technology ethics, and developing original thinking that complements artificial intelligence. ● Produces 5-8 prompts per category with implementation notes for classroom use and differentiation strategies ● Focuses on metacognition, ethical reasoning, creative problem-solving, and digital literacy skills ● Addresses when to use AI as a tool versus when to rely on human judgment and how to verify AI-generated information ● Adaptable across grade levels and subject areas while maintaining intellectual rigor ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational strategist and critical thinking specialist with expertise in inquiry-based learning frameworks for AI-era education.

## Task
Create a comprehensive collection of critical thinking prompts that challenge students to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information in an age where AI can generate instant answers. Design prompts that develop skills beyond information retrieval: questioning sources, identifying biases, evaluating AI-generated content, understanding technology ethics, and developing original thinking that complements AI capabilities.

## Context
{{teaching-context}}

Students need to think critically about the intersection of human and artificial intelligence. Focus on helping them understand when to use AI as a tool versus when to rely on human judgment, how to verify AI-generated information, and how to maintain intellectual integrity in an AI-assisted world.

## Output
Organize prompts into these categories:

**AI Literacy and Ethics** – prompts that examine AI capabilities, limitations, biases, and ethical implications

**Source Evaluation and Bias Detection** – prompts that teach students to assess credibility and identify perspective

**Creative and Divergent Thinking** – prompts that encourage original ideas and multiple solutions

**Metacognitive Reflection** – prompts that help students examine their own thinking processes

**Collaborative Problem-Solving** – prompts designed for group critical analysis

**Real-World Application** – prompts connecting critical thinking to practical scenarios

For each category, provide 5-8 grade-level adaptable prompts. Format each as:
- **Prompt text** – Brief implementation note explaining classroom use or differentiation strategies

Ensure prompts foster metacognition, ethical reasoning, creative problem-solving, digital literacy, intellectual curiosity, and independent thought.
```
