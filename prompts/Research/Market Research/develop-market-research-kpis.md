# Market Research KPI Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Market Research KPI Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds structured performance measurement systems for market research analysts and strategy teams. This market research KPI prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini creates a six-category framework covering data quality and representativeness, actionable insights, stakeholder satisfaction, return on investment, timeliness and efficiency, and competitive advantage. It produces industry-tailored metrics with measurable benchmarks, SMART criteria, and prioritized recommendations that connect research outputs to strategic business outcomes. Marketing teams use it to justify research budgets, research analysts use it to demonstrate impact, and executives use it to evaluate vendor performance. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish evaluation standards for market research projects, prove research ROI to stakeholders, or align research activities with business strategy in a specific industry context. ● Organizes KPIs into six categories with clear descriptions and quantifiable metrics like response rates, insight impact scores, and ROI percentages ● Applies SMART criteria to ensure every indicator is specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time-bound for the given industry ● Identifies the 3-5 most critical KPIs for the industry with strategic rationale explaining why they matter for research effectiveness ● Includes measurement scales, tables, and implementation guidance that make the framework immediately actionable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market research analyst defining key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the effectiveness of market research initiatives.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, industry-specific framework of KPIs that assess market research success across data quality, actionable insights, stakeholder satisfaction, return on investment, timeliness, and competitive advantage. Each KPI should include a clear description and measurable metric.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}

{{baseline-metrics}}

Effective market research KPIs must be SMART (specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, time-bound) and span the full lifecycle from data collection quality to business impact. Prioritize KPIs that directly influence strategic decisions in the given industry.

## Output
Provide a structured KPI framework organized into six categories:

**1. Data Quality and Representativeness**
- Sample size and statistical validity
- Response rate
- Margin of error
- Demographic representation score (1-5 scale)

**2. Insights and Actionability**
- Number of key insights identified
- Percentage of insights acted upon
- Impact score on business decisions (1-5 scale)

**3. Stakeholder Satisfaction**
- Overall satisfaction score (1-5 scale)
- Relevance to stakeholder needs (1-5 scale)
- Clarity and presentation quality (1-5 scale)

**4. Return on Investment**
- Total research cost
- Estimated revenue generated from insights
- ROI percentage

**5. Timeliness and Efficiency**
- Average project completion time
- On-time delivery rate
- Cost per actionable insight

**6. Competitive Advantage**
- Unique insights vs. competitors
- Market share gain attributable to research

For each category, specify relevant metrics for the industry context. Use tables or impact scores to enhance readability. Include emojis strategically to highlight critical KPIs. Conclude with the 3-5 most critical KPIs for this industry and explain why they matter most for research effectiveness.
```
