# Brand Differentiation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Brand Differentiation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a structured market positioning framework for companies seeking to carve out a distinct competitive identity. By feeding in your company name, industry, target audience, unique selling points, and competitors, this brand differentiation strategy prompt for ChatGPT delivers a seven-part strategic document: market and competitive analysis, articulated value proposition, audience profiling, key differentiators, a concise positioning statement, multi-channel communication strategy, and measurable success KPIs including brand recall, preference shift, NPS, and market share movement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Marketing directors, brand strategists, and founders use it to move from vague differentiators to an actionable positioning roadmap that can be communicated across digital, retail, content, and partnership touchpoints. ● Maps the competitive landscape and identifies white-space opportunities where the brand can own a unique niche. ● Crafts a clear positioning statement that distills the brand's unique value into a quotable, repeatable phrase. ● Defines communication strategies tailored to each customer touchpoint, from paid media to retail environments and content marketing. ● Proposes concrete success metrics (brand recall, preference shift, NPS, market share) so teams can track differentiation impact over time. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist developing a comprehensive differentiation strategy to establish a unique market position.

## Task
Create a complete brand differentiation strategy that covers:

1. **Market & Competitive Analysis** – Map the current landscape and key competitors
2. **Value Proposition** – Articulate the unique strengths and benefits delivered
3. **Target Audience Definition** – Profile the audience and their specific needs
4. **Key Differentiators** – Identify what sets this brand apart from competition
5. **Positioning Statement** – Craft a concise statement encapsulating unique value
6. **Communication Strategy** – Outline how differentiators will be expressed across touchpoints (digital, retail, content, partnerships, etc.)
7. **Success Metrics** – Propose KPIs to measure differentiation effectiveness (brand recall, preference shift, NPS, market share movement)

## Context
**Company:** {{company-name}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique Selling Points:** {{usp}}
**Main Competitors:** {{competitors}}

## Output
Present the strategy in a structured format with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for each section. Ensure logical flow and coherence throughout, with each element building on insights from previous sections.
```
