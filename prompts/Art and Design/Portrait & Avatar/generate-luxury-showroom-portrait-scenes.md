# Luxury Showroom Portrait With Custom Vehicle

## 簡介

The Luxury Showroom Portrait With Custom Vehicle is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic lifestyle portraits placing you in a premium automotive showroom environment while preserving your exact appearance from a reference image. This luxury portrait prompt for ChatGPT-assisted image generators produces detailed scenes with you seated on a beanbag chair beside a high-end vehicle, complete with customizable fashion styling, showroom décor, and natural lighting that mimics iPhone photography. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, generating images with speckled concrete floors, mirrored walls, racing-themed accents, and micro-details like dust particles and leather reflections. Use it to create aspirational personal branding content, social media visuals, or lifestyle marketing assets without organizing an expensive photoshoot. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-quality lifestyle imagery that features you authentically in a luxury setting with full control over the vehicle, outfit, and showroom design. ● Preserves your exact facial features from an uploaded reference photo across all generated showroom portraits. ● Customizes three variables: showroom setting, outfit description, and hero vehicle object for full creative control. ● Renders photorealistic details including leather textures, vehicle reflections, concrete floor speckles, and natural shadow falloff. ● Produces natural HD aesthetics with authentic iPhone camera qualities, avoiding the artificial look of typical AI-generated images. ## Prompt

```
Use the uploaded reference photo. Do not change the subject's facial features — they must look identical to the uploaded image.

{{setting}}, speckled concrete floor with diffused light reflections. Subject seated slightly off-center on a bright red beanbag chair, arms crossed in a relaxed confident pose. Wearing {{outfit-description}} jacket with structured seams and folded cuffs, oversized black leather trousers pooling at the ankles, chunky black platform shoes with high-shine surfaces. Subtle reflections emphasizing leather textures, dramatic shadow falloff around shoes.

Midground: {{hero-object}} with angular front bumper, sharp air intakes, glossy black detailing, sculpted hood shifting from deep shadows to bright reflections, black rims under aerodynamic fenders, polished windshield capturing faint interior reflections.

Background: bold linear black slats running diagonally across ceiling creating depth with soft reflections. Right side: glossy black croc-embossed duffel bag with faint fingerprints on its texture. Left side: another red beanbag and subtle racing-themed decor providing controlled pops of color. Mirrored wall showing reversed silhouettes of showroom signage, racing helmet displays, and stylized circuit-track outlines.

Micro-details: dust speckles on concrete, tiny reflections across leather surfaces. Natural authentic HD look as if photographed with an iPhone camera.
```
