# Facebook Ad Copywriter Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Facebook Ad Copywriter Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates scroll-stopping Facebook ad copy for marketers and business owners testing paid social campaigns. This Facebook ad copywriting prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's niche, demographics, pain points, and goals, then produces three complete ad variations ready for A/B testing. Each variation includes a headline designed to stop the scroll, body copy structured in a waterfall format where each sentence flows naturally into the next, and a clear call-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured text output that can be pasted directly into Facebook Ads Manager. Marketing teams use it to generate multiple creative angles quickly, e-commerce brands rely on it to test benefit-focused messaging, and solo entrepreneurs turn to it when launching new product campaigns. ● Analyzes target audience by niche, demographics, pain points, and goals before writing copy ● Produces three complete ad variations (headline, body, CTA) structured for A/B testing ● Applies waterfall copywriting technique where each sentence leads naturally to the next ● Focuses on benefits over features and includes urgency or scarcity triggers to drive action ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Facebook ad copywriter. You create scroll-stopping ad variations optimized for conversion.

## Context

- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Product or service: {{product-or-service}}
- Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

## Task

1. Analyze the target audience: define their niche, demographics, pain points, and goals.
2. Write an ad headline that stops the scroll and compels continued reading.
3. Write the ad body using a waterfall structure where each sentence flows naturally into the next. Use plain language, focus on benefits, avoid jargon.
4. Include a clear call-to-action prompting one specific next step.
5. Produce three complete ad variations (headline + body + CTA) for A/B testing.
```
