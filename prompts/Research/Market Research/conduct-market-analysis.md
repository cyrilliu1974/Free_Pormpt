# Market Analysis Report Generator for Products

## 簡介

The Market Analysis Report Generator for Products is a free AI prompt that delivers in-depth market research reports for any business or product, complete with competitive intelligence, audience profiling, and strategic recommendations. This market analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section report that includes executive summary, market overview with size and growth metrics, a three-competitor landscape analysis, detailed target audience demographics and psychographics, opportunity and threat assessments, synthesized insights, and actionable recommendations - all grounded in credible sources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single business or product description into a professional analyst-grade document. Use it when launching a product, entering a new market, preparing investor decks, or briefing stakeholders on competitive positioning. ● Analyzes three key competitors by market share, strengths, and weaknesses with evidence-based assessments ● Profiles target audiences across demographics, psychographics, purchasing behaviors, and product preferences ● Identifies market opportunities, threats, and strategic gaps to inform positioning decisions ● Requires cited sources for all findings, ensuring credibility and verifiability of market intelligence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a seasoned market analyst with deep expertise in conducting comprehensive market research and competitive analysis.

## Task
Conduct an in-depth market analysis for the following business or product:

{{business-product-description}}

## Output
Deliver a structured market analysis report covering:

**Executive Summary**
Provide an overview of key findings and strategic insights.

**Market Overview**
- Market size and valuation
- Growth rate and trajectory
- Current trends shaping the market

**Competitive Landscape**
Identify and analyze three key competitors, covering for each:
- Company name
- Market share
- Core strengths
- Key weaknesses

**Target Audience Profile**
- **Demographics**: age range, gender distribution, income level, education, geographic location
- **Psychographics**: personality traits, values, interests, lifestyle patterns
- **Behaviors**: purchasing habits, brand loyalty, media consumption patterns
- **Preferences**: desired product features, price sensitivity, customer service expectations

**Market Opportunities**
Identify gaps, emerging segments, or favorable conditions to exploit.

**Market Threats**
Highlight competitive pressures, regulatory challenges, or adverse trends.

**Key Insights**
Synthesize the most important strategic takeaways from the analysis.

**Recommendations**
Provide specific, actionable strategies for positioning and growth.

**Sources**
Cite reputable industry reports, databases, and publications used in the analysis.

## Requirements
- Ground all findings in credible, current sources
- Focus on actionable intelligence rather than generic observations
- Ensure competitor analysis is balanced and evidence-based
- Develop a nuanced understanding of the target customer
```
