# Referral Traffic Analysis and SEO Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Referral Traffic Analysis and SEO Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes referral traffic sources and develops targeted action plans to increase both volume and quality of incoming traffic. This referral traffic analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines your website's top 3-5 referring domains, identifies the specific pages and keywords driving visitors from each source, and generates tailored SEO tactics matched to each domain's context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured analysis complete with percentage breakdowns and pie chart visualization. Use cases include auditing link partner performance, discovering content collaboration opportunities, and prioritizing outreach efforts based on actual traffic contribution. This prompt is built for SEO professionals, web analytics consultants, and marketing teams who need data-driven referral strategies rather than generic link-building advice. ● Identifies key referring pages and target keywords from each traffic source ● Generates domain-specific SEO action plans tailored to each referrer's audience and context ● Provides pie chart visualization of referral traffic distribution by domain ● Prioritizes tactics by potential impact on referral traffic metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert web analytics consultant specializing in referral traffic analysis, SEO strategy, and data visualization.

## Task

Analyze referral traffic sources for the provided website, identify top contributing domains, and develop actionable SEO strategies to increase referral traffic volume and quality. Present findings with a pie chart visualization.

## Context

Website to analyze: {{website-url}}

Focus on tactics that will yield measurable improvements in both the quantity and quality of referral traffic from identified sources.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Top Referral Traffic Sources for {{website-url}}:**

For each of the top 3-5 referring domains, provide:

1. **[Domain Name]: [Percentage]%**
   - Key Referring Pages:
     - [URL 1]
     - [URL 2]
   - Target Keywords: [Keyword 1], [Keyword 2], [Keyword 3]
   - **SEO Action Plan:**
     • [Specific tactic targeting this domain's audience]
     • [Content optimization recommendation for identified keywords]
     • [Link building or relationship strategy]

**Analysis Criteria:**

- Identify specific pages and keywords driving traffic from each source
- Provide tailored, actionable tactics—not generic advice—matched to each referring domain's context
- Prioritize strategies with the highest potential impact on referral traffic metrics

**Visualization:**

[Insert pie chart showing referral traffic breakdown by domain with percentages]
```
