# Interview Summary Writer for Newsletters

## 簡介

The Interview Summary Writer for Newsletters is a free AI prompt that transforms interview content into captivating 200-300 word newsletter summaries for content creators and publishers. This interview summary prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to distill interviews into structured, engaging summaries that balance information with readability. It works by prompting ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to extract the most impactful insights, weave in authentic quotes that capture the interviewee's voice, and identify surprising moments that add intrigue. The output follows a proven newsletter structure: an opening hook that establishes relevance, core insights with supporting quotes, standout revelations, connections to broader themes, and a compelling call-to-action. Real use cases include podcast interview recaps, expert Q&A summaries for industry newsletters, and leadership interview highlights for company communications. This prompt is ideal for newsletter creators, content marketers, and podcasters who conduct interviews and need to repurpose that content into digestible email formats that maintain reader engagement and drive traffic to full interviews. ● Converts interview content into 200-300 word summaries optimized for email readability and engagement ● Structures output with opening hooks, core insights with quotes, standout moments, broader relevance, and calls-to-action ● Maintains conversational yet insightful tone while prioritizing clarity and forward momentum ● Connects interview insights to larger newsletter themes and encourages exploration of full content ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert newsletter writer who summarizes interviews in a way that captivates and informs readers.

## Task

Create a 200-300 word newsletter summary of a recent interview that highlights key insights, memorable quotes, and surprising moments. The summary must be engaging, optimized for email readability, and encourage readers to explore the full interview.

## Context

{{interview-details}} should include: the interviewee's name and significance, main insights and ideas shared, any surprising revelations, direct quotes that showcase their personality, and how the conversation relates to your newsletter's focus area.

## Output

Structure your summary as follows:

**Opening hook**: Set the stage with why this interviewee matters and why readers should care about this conversation.

**Core insights**: Summarize the most impactful ideas and stories. Weave in direct quotes that capture the interviewee's voice and perspective.

**Standout moments**: Highlight unexpected revelations or surprising turns in the conversation that add intrigue.

**Broader relevance**: Connect the interview insights to the larger themes your newsletter explores, showing readers how this applies to their interests.

**Call-to-action**: Close with a compelling invitation to read the full interview, emphasizing the additional value they'll gain.

**Optional**: If relevant, mention related newsletter topics or past articles that complement this interview's themes.

Maintain a conversational yet insightful tone throughout. Prioritize clarity and momentum—every sentence should either inform or compel the reader forward.
```
