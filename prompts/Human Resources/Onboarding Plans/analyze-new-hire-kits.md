# New Hire Welcome Kit Planner

## 簡介

The New Hire Welcome Kit Planner is a free AI prompt that creates a ranked list of must-have onboarding items tailored to your company culture and work environment. The prompt acts as an HR strategist, analyzing each proposed item for employee engagement impact, practical utility, cost-effectiveness, and cultural alignment, then delivering a numbered list where every entry explains why it's essential, what specific benefit it provides, and how it reduces first-day anxiety or strengthens company connection. This new hire welcome kit prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok helps HR teams move beyond generic swag to curate kits that balance functional necessities with meaningful touches employees will actually use, whether they work remotely, hybrid, or in-office. Reach for this prompt when you need to design an onboarding kit that makes new hires feel valued from day one while serving multiple purposes: building brand loyalty, providing immediate practical value, and showcasing your culture. ● Prioritizes items based on engagement impact, practical utility, and cost-effectiveness ● Explains the specific benefit and necessity of each recommended item in 2-3 sentences ● Accounts for remote, hybrid, and in-office work environments in recommendations ● Focuses on items new hires will actually use rather than decorative branded merchandise ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR strategist and employee experience designer specializing in onboarding programs that drive engagement and retention.

## Task
Create a prioritized list of 10 must-have items for a new hire welcome kit. Each item should include:
- A clear explanation of why it's essential
- Its specific benefit to the new hire experience
- How it addresses practical needs, reduces first-day anxiety, or strengthens company connection

Analyze each item for employee engagement impact, practical utility, cost-effectiveness, and cultural alignment.

## Context
{{company-context}}

Balance functional necessities with meaningful touches that make employees feel valued from day one. Prioritize items new hires will actually use over generic swag. Consider versatility across different work environments (remote, hybrid, in-office). Focus on items that serve multiple purposes: building brand loyalty, providing immediate practical value, and showcasing company culture.

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list (1-10), each entry containing:
1. **Item name**
2. Why it's essential (2-3 sentences)
3. Specific benefit to the new hire
```
