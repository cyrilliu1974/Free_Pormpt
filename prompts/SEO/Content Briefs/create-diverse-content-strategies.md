# Content Strategy Generator for Niche Websites

## 簡介

The Content Strategy Generator for Niche Websites is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, SEO-driven content strategies tailored to any website niche and target audience. This content strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to identify 3-5 core themes relevant to your niche, research primary and secondary keywords, and generate multiple unique content ideas per theme. Each idea includes an SEO-optimized headline, a brief description, format recommendations (listicles, how-to guides, case studies), meta description suggestions, and audience engagement tactics like polls or discussion prompts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured document organized by theme that balances search visibility with user engagement. This prompt is built for content marketers, SEO specialists, and website owners who need a repeatable system to plan editorial calendars, diversify content angles, and maximize organic traffic. ● Identifies 3-5 core themes aligned with your niche and audience, ensuring topical breadth and relevance ● Integrates keyword research to guide SEO optimization for headlines, meta descriptions, and alt tags ● Generates multiple content ideas per theme with catchy headlines, audience-benefit descriptions, and format recommendations ● Suggests engagement tactics like discussion questions, comment prompts, and polls to boost interaction and dwell time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in SEO and content marketing.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive content strategy with diverse, SEO-optimized content ideas tailored to the website's niche. Each idea must include a catchy headline and brief description that explores different angles within the subject matter.

## Context
The content strategy should enhance search visibility, drive traffic, and generate user engagement. Ideas must align with core themes relevant to the target audience and incorporate keyword research to ensure discoverability.

## Process
1. **Identify Core Themes**: Outline 3-5 core themes most relevant to {{niche-and-audience}}, ensuring comprehensive subject coverage and content diversity.

2. **Keyword Integration**: Identify primary and secondary keywords for each theme based on search volume and relevance. Use these to guide content creation and SEO optimization.

3. **Generate Content Ideas**: Develop multiple content ideas per theme that offer unique value—informational, educational, or entertaining—incorporating the identified keywords naturally.

4. **Craft Headlines and Descriptions**:
   - **Headline**: Create SEO-optimized, curiosity-driven headlines that include primary keywords and promise clear value
   - **Description**: Draft brief descriptions outlining key points, unique angles, and audience benefits

5. **SEO Elements**: Include recommendations for meta descriptions, image alt tags, and internal linking opportunities for each content idea.

6. **Content Formats**: Suggest optimal formats (listicles, how-to guides, case studies, interviews) that suit each topic and maximize engagement.

7. **Engagement Tactics**: Recommend audience interaction strategies such as discussion questions, comment prompts, polls, or quizzes to boost participation.

## Output
Deliver a structured content strategy document organized by core themes. Each section should contain multiple content ideas with sample headlines, descriptions, keyword targets, format recommendations, and engagement tactics designed to increase traffic and user interaction.
```
