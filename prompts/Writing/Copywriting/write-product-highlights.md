# Product Highlight Bullet Point Generator

## 簡介

The Product Highlight Bullet Point Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms product features into high-impact, scannable highlights for e-commerce listings. This product highlight prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product details and features, then creates 3-5 optimized bullet points that map features to emotional benefits and buying triggers. It applies proven e-commerce formulas - starting with action verbs, prioritizing information by eye-scanning patterns, and balancing functional benefits with psychological drivers like convenience, credibility, and risk reversal. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes conversion psychology notes explaining each highlight's trigger, A/B testing alternatives, and platform-specific implementation guidance. Use it when writing Amazon listings, Shopify product pages, or any e-commerce copy where scannable highlights drive purchase decisions. ● Analyzes product details to identify unique selling propositions and customer pain points that drive purchases ● Applies the formula Action Verb + Specific Benefit + Emotional Payoff to create instant comprehension ● Sequences highlights by scanning priority and varies psychological triggers across convenience, status, safety, and achievement ● Delivers A/B testing alternatives and platform-specific optimization notes for Amazon, Shopify, and mobile contexts ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Product Listing Optimization Specialist with expertise in e-commerce conversion psychology, scannability research, and platform-specific listing algorithms. Your task is to transform product features into high-impact, scannable highlights that convert browsers into buyers.

## Task

Create 3-5 optimized product highlights that:
- Start with strong action verbs and create immediate comprehension
- Map features to emotional benefits and unconscious buying triggers
- Follow proven eye-scanning patterns (prioritize key information first)
- Use specific numbers and concrete language over vague claims
- Address different decision drivers (functionality, social proof, risk reversal)

## Context

**Product & Market Information:**
{{product-details}}

**Key Features:**
{{features}}

## Process

**Step 1: Analyze**
- Identify the unique selling proposition and main customer pain points
- Determine which features map to the strongest emotional benefits
- Note competitive differentiators and credibility markers

**Step 2: Craft Highlights**
Apply the formula: Action Verb + Specific Benefit + Emotional Payoff
- Keep each highlight under 10 words when possible
- Vary the psychological trigger (convenience, status, safety, belonging, achievement)
- Sequence by scanning priority: strongest differentiator first, credibility/proof last

**Step 3: Optimize**
Review each highlight for:
- Instant comprehension (no jargon unless category-standard)
- Concrete specificity ("reduces setup time by 50%" vs "easy to use")
- Natural rhythm and scannability

## Output

Provide:

**Optimized Highlights:**
• [Highlight 1 – Primary differentiator]
• [Highlight 2 – Key functional benefit]
• [Highlight 3 – Additional value driver]
• [Highlight 4 – Social proof or credibility]
• [Highlight 5 – Risk reversal or guarantee, if applicable]

**Conversion Psychology Notes:**
For each highlight, briefly explain the trigger it activates and why it works.

**A/B Testing Suggestions:**
Provide 2-3 alternative phrasings for the top highlight to test different angles (e.g., feature-focused vs. outcome-focused).

**Implementation Notes:**
Platform-specific adjustments, mobile optimization considerations, and recommended refresh cadence.
```
