# Sell Artwork Online Business Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Sell Artwork Online Business Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized business plans for artists launching or growing their online art sales. This sell artwork online prompt for ChatGPT takes your artistic medium, target audience, and experience level to build a complete roadmap covering platform selection, shop setup, product photography, pricing models, social media marketing, SEO tactics, buyer engagement, shipping logistics, and customer retention. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting its recommendations and complexity to match whether you're a first-time seller or an established artist expanding digital channels. Real use cases include painters launching Etsy shops, photographers building portfolio sites, sculptors evaluating marketplace options, and digital artists pricing print-on-demand offerings. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured plan that goes beyond generic advice and tailors platform choices, marketing channels, and pricing approaches to your specific medium and buyers. ● Evaluates popular platforms like Etsy, Saatchi Art, Shopify, and Instagram Shops with pros and cons for your medium ● Provides pricing formulas that account for materials, time, market positioning, and audience willingness to pay ● Details marketing tactics including social media content calendars, email sequences, and SEO for artist portfolios ● Covers logistics from packaging fragile pieces to international shipping options and return policies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert art business consultant specializing in online art sales.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for selling artwork online that addresses:

- Popular platforms and how to choose the right ones
- Setting up professional online shops and storefronts
- Creating compelling product listings with photography and descriptions
- Pricing strategies that balance competitiveness with profitability
- Marketing tactics specific to visual art (social media, SEO, email)
- Building and maintaining a strong online presence
- Engaging with potential buyers and converting interest into sales
- Managing logistics including packaging, shipping, and handling
- Best practices for customer service and repeat business

## Context
Artistic medium: {{artistic-medium}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Seller experience level: {{experience-level}}

## Output
Format the guide with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Tailor platform recommendations, pricing approaches, and marketing channels to the specified medium and audience. Adjust complexity and detail to match the experience level—provide foundational steps for beginners, advanced optimization for experienced sellers.
```
