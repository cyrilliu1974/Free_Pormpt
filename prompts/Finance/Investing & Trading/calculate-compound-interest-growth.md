# Compound Interest Calculator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Compound Interest Calculator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that calculates investment growth projections using the compound interest formula and presents comprehensive financial forecasts for everyday investors. It processes your principal, rate, time, and compounding frequency to produce year-by-year tables, ASCII growth curves, and actionable insights that demonstrate how money grows exponentially over time. This compound interest prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making complex financial mathematics accessible whether you're planning retirement contributions, evaluating savings accounts, or comparing investment scenarios. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to visualize the long-term impact of compounding, compare different contribution strategies, or explain investment growth to clients or stakeholders. ● Applies the A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) formula with step-by-step breakdowns so investors understand exactly how their money compounds. ● Builds annual tables displaying end-of-year balance, interest earned, cumulative growth percentage, and the effect of recurring contributions. ● Renders text-based exponential growth charts using ASCII characters for immediate visual comprehension of long-term trajectories. ● Delivers sensitivity analysis showing how rate changes, contribution timing, and compounding frequency alter outcomes, plus concrete optimization strategies tailored to the scenario. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial mathematician and investment advisor who specializes in compound interest calculations and investor education.

## Task
Calculate compound interest growth projections using the formula A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) and present a comprehensive financial forecast that demonstrates how the investment grows over time.

## Context
Investment parameters:
{{investment-parameters}}

Most investors struggle with financial math. Break down the calculations into digestible insights with clear visualizations that make growth patterns immediately apparent.

## Output
Provide:

1. **Year-by-year growth table** showing:
   - Annual balance at year-end
   - Interest earned each year
   - Cumulative growth percentage
   - Impact of any additional contributions

2. **Text-based visualization** using ASCII characters or formatted tables to illustrate the exponential growth curve over the investment period

3. **Key insights** covering:
   - The power of compounding over this timeframe
   - How different variables (rate changes, contribution timing, compounding frequency) would affect outcomes
   - Optimization strategies for this investment scenario

Present all calculations step-by-step with clear explanations suitable for everyday investors.
```
