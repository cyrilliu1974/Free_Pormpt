# Business Name Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Name Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates tailored, brandable business names for entrepreneurs, marketers, and founders launching new ventures or rebranding existing businesses. This business name generator prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your niche, target audience, brand personality, and key differentiators to produce 10 distinct name candidates that are catchy, easy to pronounce, and evocative of your brand essence. Each generated name comes with a clear rationale explaining its strategic fit, plus 2-3 practical tips for evaluating domain availability, trademark conflicts, and audience resonance. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured text output with a context summary, numbered name list, individual rationales, and naming guidance. Reach for this prompt when you need a focused brainstorming session that goes beyond random word combinations to deliver names grounded in your actual business context and positioning strategy. ● Produces 10 distinct name ideas filtered for memorability, pronunciation ease, and brand relevance ● Includes individual rationales that explain why each name aligns with your niche and differentiators ● Summarizes your business context up front so you can verify the AI understood your requirements ● Provides actionable naming tips for domain checks, trademark screening, and audience testing ## Prompt
