# Research Guide Builder for Any Topic or Field

## 簡介

The Research Guide Builder for Any Topic or Field is a free AI prompt that produces a comprehensive six-part research guide tailored to any subject, discipline, or area of study. This research guide prompt for ChatGPT walks users through the entire research lifecycle: finding credible sources, evaluating their reliability, taking efficient notes, synthesizing insights from multiple references, and organizing findings into a coherent framework. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a complete instructional document that includes research strategies with substeps, a source evaluation checklist covering authority and bias, note-taking tips, synthesis techniques, a text-based process flowchart, and a fill-in-the-blank findings template. Students use it to structure literature reviews, educators to design research curricula, and professionals to onboard teams into unfamiliar domains. ● Delivers research strategies with concrete substeps customized to the specified topic or field. ● Includes a 5–7 point source evaluation checklist addressing authority, currency, accuracy, bias, and relevance. ● Provides a text-based research process flowchart mapping every stage from topic selection to documentation. ● Supplies a fill-in-the-blank findings organization template with sections for key points, evidence, counterarguments, and implications. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research skills instructor specializing in teaching effective information literacy and synthesis techniques.

## Task
Create a comprehensive research guide for {{topic-or-field}} that equips the user to locate, evaluate, and synthesize high-quality information. Structure the guide in six parts:

**Part I: Research Strategies**  
Provide 3-4 effective research strategies tailored to {{topic-or-field}}, each with 2-3 concrete substeps.

**Part II: Source Evaluation Checklist**  
List 5-7 criteria for assessing source quality and reliability (authority, currency, accuracy, bias, relevance).

**Part III: Note-Taking Tips**  
Offer 4-5 practical tips for efficient note-taking during research.

**Part IV: Synthesis Techniques**  
Describe 3 techniques for integrating information from multiple sources into cohesive understanding.

**Part V: Research Process Flowchart**  
Create a text-based flowchart showing the research process from topic selection through synthesis and documentation.

**Part VI: Findings Organization Template**  
Provide a fill-in-the-blank template with sections: Topic Overview, Key Points, Supporting Evidence, Counterarguments, Implications, and Areas for Further Exploration.

## Output Format
Title: **Research Guide: Mastering [Topic/Field]**

Use numbered lists for strategies and techniques, checkbox items (▢) for the evaluation checklist, and bullet points (•) for note-taking tips. Present the flowchart using simple arrows and boxes in plain text. Format the template with clear section headers and blank lines for user input.
```
