# Cinematic Environmental Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Environmental Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces hyper-realistic fashion and lifestyle portraits with professional bokeh, natural lighting, and magazine-quality composition for content creators and marketers. This environmental portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates 8k-resolution images of stylish subjects posed confidently in natural settings, complete with soft dappled sunlight, cinematic depth of field, and the technical precision of a Sony A7R IV shot at f/1.8. You customize two core variables: the environment (urban alley, forest clearing, desert landscape) and the outfit style (casual streetwear, elegant evening wear, bohemian layers), and the prompt renders a portrait with professional composition, sharp subject focus, and rich bokeh backgrounds. Use it for fashion lookbooks, lifestyle brand campaigns, social media content, editorial mockups, or any project that needs authentic-looking portrait photography without a shoot. Reach for this prompt when you need fashion or lifestyle imagery that feels professionally shot but requires creative control over wardrobe and location that would be expensive or impractical to photograph in real life. ● Produces 8k-resolution portraits with cinematic bokeh, dappled natural light, and professional depth of field mimicking a 35mm f/1.8 lens. ● Customizable environment and outfit-style variables let you match brand aesthetics, seasonal campaigns, or mood boards. ● Includes a detailed negative prompt to prevent cartoon rendering, distorted anatomy, harsh lighting, and watermarks. ● Outputs sharp, matte-finish portraits suitable for e-commerce, editorial layouts, Instagram grids, and brand storytelling. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic environmental portrait of a stylish young woman standing in {{environment}}. She wears {{outfit-style}}. She stands with a confident posture, leaning slightly forward, with her right hand resting naturally on a nearby vertical element in the scene. The lighting is soft and diffused with dappled sunlight filtering through the surroundings, highlighting the texture of the environment and her clothing. The background features a deep cinematic depth of field (bokeh) with rich natural tones and organic undergrowth. Shot on Sony A7R IV, 35mm lens at f/1.8, high contrast, matte finish, sharp focus on subject, 8k resolution.

Negative prompt: cartoon, anime, 3d render, illustration, painting, low quality, blurry, distorted face, bad anatomy, extra fingers, missing limbs, text, watermark, neon colors, overexposed, harsh lighting, bright artificial light, makeup smudge, messy hair
```
