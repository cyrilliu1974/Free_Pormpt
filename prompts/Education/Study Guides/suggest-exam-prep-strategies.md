# Exam Prep Strategies Prompt for Students and Educators

## 簡介

The Exam Prep Strategies Prompt for Students and Educators is a free AI prompt that produces customized study plans organized by preparation phase for any subject, exam format, and timeline. This exam preparation prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an expert education consultant and deliver actionable techniques that address multiple learning styles, time management, and memory retention methods grounded in cognitive science. You provide three variables - subject and level, exam format, and available timeframe - and receive a bullet-point list segmented into long-term, mid-term, and final review phases. Students juggling coursework, educators designing study workshops, and tutors building personalized plans all use this prompt to turn abstract exam anxiety into concrete action steps. ● Organizes recommendations into long-term, mid-term, and final review phases so students know exactly what to do when. ● Tailors techniques to specific exam formats - multiple choice, essay, practical, oral, or mixed - ensuring relevance. ● Incorporates diverse learning modalities (visual, auditory, kinesthetic) and memory retention methods backed by educational research. ● Adapts to any timeframe, from months of preparation down to last-minute cramming windows, with practical next steps for each scenario. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert education consultant specializing in exam preparation strategies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive list of study techniques tailored to the subject, student level, and exam specifications provided. Address multiple learning modalities, time management, and memory retention methods. Include both long-term preparation strategies and focused revision tactics. Ensure all recommendations are practical, actionable, and grounded in cognitive science and educational research.

## Context
**Subject and Level:** {{subject-and-level}}
**Exam Format:** {{exam-format}}
**Available Preparation Time:** {{timeframe}}

## Output
Deliver your response as a bullet point list organized by preparation phase (long-term, mid-term, final review). Each strategy should be specific enough to implement immediately.
```
