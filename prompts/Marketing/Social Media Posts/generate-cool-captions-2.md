# Social Media Caption Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The Social Media Caption Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates five platform-optimized captions designed to drive engagement and conversions for marketers and brand managers. This social media caption prompt for ChatGPT takes four inputs - your target platform, product or service, audience, and brand voice - and produces five distinct captions, each crafted with persuasive language, emotional hooks, and clear calls-to-action. Every caption includes a structural breakdown explaining how its sentence construction creates clarity and emphasis, helping you understand what makes effective copy work. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone, hashtag strategy, and length to match platform conventions and audience expectations. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, conversion-focused caption copy that reflects your brand voice across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, or Twitter without starting from a blank page every time. ● Produces five complete captions per run, each with a different angle or hook to test what resonates with your audience ● Explains the sentence structure behind each caption so you learn what drives clarity and persuasive impact ● Adapts hashtag usage, tone, and length to fit the conventions of your chosen platform and audience behavior ● Matches your specified brand voice - whether casual, professional, playful, or authoritative - across all five outputs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in high-performing caption writing.

## Task
Create 5 engaging captions for {{platform}} that promote {{product-or-service}} to {{target-audience}}. Each caption should drive engagement and conversions through persuasive language, emotional appeal, and clear calls-to-action. Match the {{brand-voice}} throughout.

## Context
Strong captions balance clarity with impact. Structure each caption to lead with the most compelling element—whether that's a benefit, question, or statement—then support it with proof points from the product's key features. Tailor tone, hashtag strategy, and length to {{platform}} conventions and current best practices for {{target-audience}}.

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 5 captions. After each caption, include a brief explanation of how its sentence structure creates clarity and emphasis (identifying the main clause, dependent elements, and how they build persuasive flow).
```
