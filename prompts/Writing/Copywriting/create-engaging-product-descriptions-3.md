# Product Description Generator for E-Commerce SEO

## 簡介

The Product Description Generator for E-Commerce SEO is a free AI prompt that creates high-converting, search-optimized product copy for online retailers and marketing teams. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write structured 300-word descriptions that combine features, benefits, emotional triggers, and natural keyword placement. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your product overview and target audience details, then generating copy that follows a proven eight-step formula: headline hook, problem statement, feature-benefit bullets, emotional connection, woven SEO keywords, persuasive language, clear call-to-action, and optional social proof placement. Real-world applications include launching new products on Shopify or WooCommerce stores, refreshing underperforming listings on marketplaces, and scaling content production for large catalogs while maintaining brand voice consistency. E-commerce managers, digital marketers, and freelance copywriters reach for this prompt when they need descriptions that serve both human shoppers and search algorithms without sounding robotic or keyword-stuffed. ● Enforces a 300-word limit to keep descriptions concise and scannable for mobile shoppers ● Structures output with headline, hook, feature-benefit bullets, emotional appeal, and CTA in a logical purchase-decision flow ● Integrates SEO keywords naturally throughout the copy rather than forcing awkward placements that hurt readability ● Adapts tone and voice to match brand guidelines, from professional B2B language to playful direct-to-consumer styles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in high-converting, SEO-optimized product descriptions that drive purchase decisions.

## Task
Write a compelling product description (maximum 300 words) that highlights features, benefits, and value while naturally incorporating SEO keywords and maintaining strong brand voice.

## Context
Product details and positioning:
{{product-overview}}

Target audience and SEO strategy:
{{audience-and-seo}}

## Output
Structure the description following this flow:

1. **Headline**: Lead with the product name and primary benefit or unique selling proposition

2. **Opening hook**: Create intrigue or present the problem this product solves (2-3 sentences)

3. **Key features and benefits**: Use bullet points showing how each feature delivers user value

4. **Emotional connection**: Describe how the product improves the customer's life or solves their specific pain points

5. **SEO integration**: Weave the provided keywords naturally throughout—never force placement

6. **Persuasive elements**: Use power words to create appropriate urgency or exclusivity without being pushy

7. **Call-to-action**: End with a clear, compelling CTA that drives the desired next step

8. **Optional enhancement**: Suggest placement for customer testimonials or reviews to add social proof

Maintain the specified brand voice (professional, casual, playful, etc.) throughout. Ensure the description flows naturally while staying within the 300-word limit and optimizing for both human readers and search visibility.
```
