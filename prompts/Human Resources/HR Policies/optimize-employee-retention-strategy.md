# Employee Retention Strategy Analyzer and Planner

## 簡介

The Employee Retention Strategy Analyzer and Planner is a free AI prompt that transforms company workforce data into actionable retention strategies for HR leaders and organizational development teams. This employee retention strategy prompt for ChatGPT applies industrial-organizational psychology principles to analyze engagement metrics, satisfaction scores, and turnover patterns, then delivers a complete strategic plan with phased initiatives, KPIs, and expected ROI. You provide company information - industry, size, demographics, existing survey data, cultural attributes, and pain points - and the prompt structures a response covering current state analysis, root cause identification, strategic recommendations with measurable outcomes, and a timeline-based implementation roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for HR directors, people operations teams, and executives tackling attrition or engagement challenges. ● Structures engagement analysis around satisfaction scores, turnover rates, and exit interview themes ● Identifies multiple drivers of disengagement with evidence-based root cause frameworks ● Generates three prioritized retention initiatives, each with expected impact and KPIs ● Delivers a three-phase implementation roadmap with activities, timelines, and resource requirements ● Forecasts outcomes for engagement, retention, productivity, and return on investment ## Prompt
