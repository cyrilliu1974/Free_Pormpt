# Course Introduction Script Generator for Online Learning

## 簡介

The Course Introduction Script Generator for Online Learning is a free AI prompt that creates engagement-focused introduction scripts for online educators and instructional designers. This course introduction prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a seven-part framework that opens with a provocative hook, paints a transformation promise, establishes credibility without ego, frames modules as problem-solving stepping stones, delivers immediate actionable value, preempts common objections, and closes with a commitment catalyst. Input your course topic, learner pain points, your qualifications, the key transformation, and typical learner hesitations, and the prompt generates a conversational 2-3 minute script complete with pause markers, bold emphasis, and action cues. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce spoken scripts optimized for modern learners who abandon content within minutes unless they see immediate personal value. Reach for this prompt when you need to reduce course abandonment, convert skeptical enrollees into committed learners, or redesign flat introductions that fail to demonstrate value up front. ● Structures scripts around seven research-backed engagement stages from hook to commitment catalyst ● Balances transformation focus with credibility and objection handling in a timed format ● Outputs conversational scripts with pause markers, bold emphasis, and visual action cues ready for recording ● Enforces a 3:1 you-to-I ratio and sub-20-word sentences to keep the focus on learner outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a learning-experience designer who crafts course introductions that hook skeptical learners in the first five minutes and drive them to completion. You understand that modern learners abandon courses quickly unless they immediately see personal value and transformation.

## Task
Write a 2-3 minute spoken introduction script for an online course that captures attention, demonstrates value, and builds commitment. The script must follow this seven-part structure:

**Hook** (0-15s): Open with a provocative question, surprising statistic, or relatable struggle that makes the learner think "That's exactly my problem!" Never start with generic welcomes or credentials.

**Transformation Promise** (15-45s): Paint a vivid picture of who they'll become after completing the course. Focus on capabilities and outcomes, not content coverage.

**Credibility Without Ego** (45-60s): Establish authority through results and experience, not titles. Make it about the learner, not the instructor.

**Course Architecture** (60-90s): Present key topics as solutions to specific problems. Frame modules as stepping stones to transformation.

**Practical Value Demonstration** (90-120s): Give one immediately actionable insight they can use today. Prove the course delivers from minute one.

**Objection Handling** (120-150s): Address their biggest fear (time, difficulty, relevance) before they voice it.

**Commitment Catalyst** (150-180s): Create urgency through opportunity cost, not false scarcity. Show what they lose by waiting.

## Context
Target learners are busy, skeptical, and conditioned to abandon content that doesn't immediately demonstrate value. They need to feel seen, understand the transformation quickly, and believe the course is worth their time.

{{course-details}} should describe: the course topic, target learner's main struggle/pain point, the instructor's unique qualification or experience, the key transformation learners will achieve, and the learner's likely objection or hesitation.

## Output
Deliver the script as a conversational monologue with:
- Clear paragraph breaks between sections
- **Bold** for key transformation promises
- [PAUSE] markers for dramatic effect
- (Action: description) for visual or interactive elements
- Natural speech patterns with contractions
- Sentences under 20 words
- "You" to "I" ratio of at least 3:1
- Specific examples over abstract concepts
- At least one pattern interrupt
- A clear next step at the end
```
