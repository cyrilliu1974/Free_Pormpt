# Sales Contest Planning Prompt for Team Motivation

## 簡介

The Sales Contest Planning Prompt for Team Motivation is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, revenue-driving sales contest plans for managers looking to boost team performance and engagement. This sales contest prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini transforms your team details, target metrics, prize budget, timeline, and company goals into a six-part contest framework covering structure and rules, real-time leaderboard design, prize distribution across multiple categories, communication cadence from kickoff through winner announcements, contingency planning for common challenges like tied scores or low participation, and post-contest analysis methods to sustain momentum. Sales managers use it to design fair, motivating competitions that align daily activity with quarterly revenue targets while maintaining team morale. Reach for this prompt when you need to launch a sales contest quickly, ensure your rules are clear and equitable, or want a structured approach to recognition that goes beyond a simple top-performer prize. ● Produces a dynamic leaderboard system with real-time tracking mechanisms customized to your chosen metrics ● Designs multi-tier prize structures that reward top performers, category winners, and participation to maximize engagement across skill levels ● Includes kickoff announcements, regular update schedules, and motivational touchpoints to maintain contest energy throughout the duration ● Provides contingency protocols for disputes, tied scores, and engagement drops so you can adapt without losing momentum ● Delivers post-contest analysis frameworks to extract performance insights and identify team development opportunities from contest data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales manager designing a motivating, revenue-driving sales contest.

## Task
Create a comprehensive contest plan that includes:

1. **Contest Structure & Rules**
   - Clear, fair rules aligned with company goals
   - Timeline and milestones for the contest period
   - Eligibility criteria and participation guidelines

2. **Leaderboard & Tracking System**
   - Dynamic leaderboard design for real-time visibility
   - Tracking mechanisms for the specified metrics
   - Frequency and format of updates

3. **Prizes & Recognition**
   - Prize structure and distribution (top performers, categories, participation)
   - Recognition milestones throughout the contest
   - Celebration and announcement plan for winners

4. **Communication Strategy**
   - Kickoff announcement to generate excitement
   - Regular updates and motivational touchpoints
   - Channels and cadence for participant engagement

5. **Contingency Planning**
   - Potential challenges (low participation, tied scores, disputes)
   - Solutions to maintain momentum if engagement drops
   - Adjustment protocols for unforeseen circumstances

6. **Post-Contest Analysis & Development**
   - How to analyze results for performance insights
   - Strategies to sustain momentum after the contest
   - Team development opportunities identified from contest data

## Context
**Sales Team:** {{sales-team-description}}
**Metrics:** {{metrics}}
**Prizes:** {{prizes}}
**Duration:** {{duration}}
**Company Sales Goals:** {{company-sales-goals}}

## Output
Deliver the plan as a numbered list with clear headings for each section. Ensure all elements are actionable, specific to the team and metrics provided, and designed to maximize engagement and results.
```
