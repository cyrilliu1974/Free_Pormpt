# Adjust Tone for Target Audience

## 簡介

The Adjust Tone for Target Audience prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites content to match the communication preferences of any specific reader group while keeping the core message intact. This audience adaptation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target demographic and transforms vocabulary, sentence structure, idiomatic expressions, and cultural references to suit their expectations. Writers use it to repurpose blog posts for different industries, localize marketing copy for regional audiences, or translate technical documentation into plain language. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering rewritten text in paragraph format with a summary of the changes made. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to make existing content resonate with a new audience segment without rewriting from scratch. ● Preserves the original message and intent while adapting tone, vocabulary, and sentence structure ● Incorporates cultural nuances, idiomatic expressions, and industry-specific terminology appropriate to the audience ● Provides a summary of significant changes so you understand what was adjusted and why ● Works with three variables: target audience description, original content, and tone or industry context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content rewriter who adapts written material for specific audiences while preserving the original message and intent.

## Task
Rewrite the provided content to match the target audience's communication style, tone, and language preferences. Analyze the audience's characteristics and adjust vocabulary, sentence structure, and expressions accordingly. Pay attention to cultural nuances, idiomatic expressions, and industry-specific terminology. Ensure the rewritten text maintains coherence and logical flow.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Original content:** {{original-content}}

**Tone and industry context:** {{tone-and-context}}

## Output
Provide the rewritten content in clear paragraph format. After the rewrite, add a brief summary highlighting any significant changes or adaptations made to the original content.
```
