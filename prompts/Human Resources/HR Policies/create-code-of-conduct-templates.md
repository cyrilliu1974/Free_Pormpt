# Code of Conduct Template Generator for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Code of Conduct Template Generator for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive workplace conduct policies tailored to your organization's values, industry, and regulatory environment. This code of conduct prompt for ChatGPT and similar text models (Claude, Gemini, Grok) produces a ready-to-review document with numbered sections on ethical behavior, professional conduct, respect and inclusion, confidentiality, conflict of interest, legal compliance, and reporting mechanisms. You supply your organization profile - size, sector, culture - and the prompt delivers clear policy statements with specific behavioral examples, practical guidance for enforcement, implementation recommendations, and bracketed customization notes showing where to adapt language for different industries or regulatory frameworks. HR departments use it to draft board-ready policies that balance SHRM best practices with real-world enforceability. Reach for this prompt when launching a new organization, updating outdated policies, navigating regulatory changes, or harmonizing standards across merged entities. ● Outputs a complete document structure with section headings, policy language, and behavioral examples suitable for immediate board review. ● Covers essential compliance areas - ethics, confidentiality, data protection, conflicts of interest, and whistleblower reporting - in one cohesive template. ● Includes bracketed guidance showing exactly where to insert industry-specific risks, local labor law references, or organizational value statements. ● Balances legal rigor with plain-language readability so every employee can understand expectations and consequences. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR compliance specialist and organizational development consultant with expertise in code of conduct frameworks for corporate, nonprofit, and startup environments.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, legally-sound code of conduct template that balances regulatory compliance with practical implementation. Provide clear behavioral guidelines that are enforceable and accessible to all employees.

## Context
Organizations face regulatory scrutiny, diverse workforce dynamics, and the need for enforceable standards. The code of conduct must protect the organization legally while remaining practical for daily use.

**Organization profile:**
{{organization-profile}}

## Output
Deliver a complete code of conduct template structured as a professional document with:

- Numbered sections covering: ethical behavior, professional conduct, respect and inclusion, confidentiality and data protection, conflict of interest, legal compliance, and reporting mechanisms
- Clear policy statements with specific behavioral examples for each section
- Practical guidance for real-world application
- Implementation recommendations
- Customization notes in [brackets] indicating where to adapt language for different organizational contexts, industries, or regulatory requirements

Format as a ready-to-use document with clear section headings and professional structure suitable for board review and employee distribution.
```
