# SEO Tool Research and Comparison Prompt

## 簡介

The SEO Tool Research and Comparison Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches, analyzes, and compares SEO tools with a focus on keyword research capabilities for website owners and digital marketers. This SEO tool research prompt for ChatGPT delivers three essential outputs: a structured comparison table of at least five SEO platforms covering features, pricing, and user ratings; a ranked list of the top three keyword research tools with their key benefits; and five actionable SEO strategy tips customized to your specific website URL. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming a single website input into expert-level tool recommendations and strategic guidance. Real use cases include agencies evaluating tools for client projects, in-house marketers building their SEO stack, and consultants needing quick competitive analysis of current software options. Reach for this prompt when you need to cut through SEO tool marketing noise and get an objective, structured comparison that prioritizes keyword research strength while addressing your site's unique needs. ● Produces a scannable comparison table with at least five SEO tools, including features, pricing models, user ratings, and ideal use cases ● Identifies and ranks the three strongest keyword research platforms with specific benefits for each ● Generates five actionable, website-specific SEO strategy tips based on the URL you provide ● Structures all output for immediate decision-making, eliminating hours of manual tool research and comparison ## Prompt
