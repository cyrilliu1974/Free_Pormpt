# Colorful Product Photography Generator

## 簡介

The Colorful Product Photography Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional bokeh-style product images with controlled lighting and texture emphasis for marketers, e-commerce teams, and content creators. This product photography prompt for Midjourney lets you specify the medium (digital, film, macro), the product itself, the surface and setting it rests on, lighting direction and temperature, and overall aesthetic style. It renders images with shallow depth of field that softly blur the background while keeping your subject sharp, perfect for hero images, social media posts, catalog photography, and advertising mockups. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other diffusion models that support aspect ratio and quality parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need styled product shots without a physical studio setup or when iterating quickly on composition and lighting ideas. ● Adjustable medium (photograph, film scan, macro shot) to match your brand's visual tone ● Surface and setting variables let you place products on wood, fabric, stone, or custom props ● Lighting control highlights form and texture with warm, cool, or dramatic directional light ● Style parameter adapts the aesthetic from minimalist and clean to vintage, moody, or vibrant ## Prompt

```
{{medium}} photograph of a {{product}} resting on {{surface-and-setting}}, shot with shallow depth of field creating soft bokeh in the background, {{lighting}} highlights emphasizing form and texture, {{style}} aesthetic --ar 16:9 --q 2
```
