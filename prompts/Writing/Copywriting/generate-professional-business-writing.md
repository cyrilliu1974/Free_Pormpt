# Business Writing Guide Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Writing Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, ready-to-use guides on professional written communication for managers, teams, and training departments. This business writing prompt for ChatGPT produces an eight-section reference document that includes audience analysis frameworks, content organization strategies, tone and style guidelines, grammar tips, and common pitfall examples. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, generating structured guides with clear headings, bullet-point strategies, and before-and-after writing samples. Use it to standardize communication standards across teams, onboard new hires, or create training materials that improve clarity and professionalism in emails, reports, and proposals. The prompt is ideal for HR professionals building training curricula, managers seeking to improve team communication, or anyone tasked with documenting writing standards. ● Produces eight structured sections from introduction through additional resources, each with concrete examples ● Includes audience analysis tips, organizational frameworks, and tone guidelines with appropriate vs. inappropriate comparisons ● Generates proofreading checklists and common error lists with correction examples ● Delivers before-and-after writing samples that illustrate pitfall avoidance strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business writing expert creating a comprehensive, practical guide on professional written communication best practices.

## Task
Produce a complete, standalone business writing guide structured with the following sections:

1. **Introduction** – Establish the importance of effective business writing and preview the key areas covered
2. **Understanding Your Audience** – Explain audience analysis, provide identification tips, and include a tailored writing example
3. **Organizing Your Content** – Discuss clear structure principles, present logical organization strategies, and show an effective outline example
4. **Tone and Style** – Define their role, provide professional tone guidelines, and contrast appropriate vs. inappropriate choices with examples
5. **Grammar, Mechanics, and Proofreading** – Emphasize correctness, offer proofreading tips, and highlight common errors to avoid with examples
6. **Common Pitfalls** – Identify frequent mistakes, provide avoidance strategies, and show before/after improvement examples
7. **Conclusion** – Summarize key takeaways and encourage practical application
8. **Additional Resources** – List books, websites, or templates for further learning

## Context
{{guide-context}}

## Output Requirements
- Use clear headings (##) and subheadings (###) to organize each section
- Present tips and strategies as bullet points for readability
- Include concrete, actionable examples in every section
- Maintain a professional yet engaging tone throughout
- Ensure the guide is comprehensive and can stand alone as a reference document
- Format for easy sharing and navigation
```
