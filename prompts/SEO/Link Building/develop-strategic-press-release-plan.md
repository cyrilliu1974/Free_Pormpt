# Strategic Press Release Calendar Generator

## 簡介

The Strategic Press Release Calendar Generator is a free AI prompt that builds year-long press release plans for PR professionals seeking consistent media coverage and high-quality backlinks. This press release planning prompt for ChatGPT creates a structured 12-month calendar that balances product announcements, company milestones, thought leadership, and industry insights. It assigns each release a newsworthy topic, a differentiated angle, specific media outlet targets, and desired backlink domains. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your company name, industry, target audience, and unique selling proposition to generate a quarter-by-quarter schedule complete with KPIs and strategic recommendations. Real use cases include SaaS companies coordinating product launches with industry events, B2B brands establishing thought leadership, and agencies managing multi-client PR calendars. Reach for this prompt when you need a strategic roadmap that aligns announcements with business goals, avoids generic promotional content, and identifies the exact publications and domains you want coverage from. ● Produces a quarter-by-quarter calendar with 8-12 press releases distributed across product, company, and thought-leadership categories ● Identifies specific media outlets and high-authority domains for each announcement to guide outreach and backlink acquisition ● Includes KPI targets for media mentions, backlinks secured, referral traffic, and lead generation ● Delivers strategic recommendations on timing, angle differentiation, and alignment with your unique selling proposition ## Prompt
