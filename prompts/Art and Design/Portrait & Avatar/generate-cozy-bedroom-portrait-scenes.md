# Cozy Bedroom Portrait Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Cozy Bedroom Portrait Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic lifestyle portraits in intimate bedroom settings for brands and marketers. This cozy bedroom portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces warm, authentic images of a young woman in satin pajamas hugging a plush teddy bear on a bed, surrounded by coordinated bedding and soft lamplight. The prompt lets you customize the color scheme across outfit, bear, and decor, and adjust the mood to match brand tone. Use it for sleepwear catalogs, bedding e-commerce, social media content, or lifestyle marketing campaigns that need relatable, emotion-driven visuals without arranging photoshoots. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, brand-aligned lifestyle imagery that conveys comfort, warmth, and approachability. ● Photorealistic portrait style with soft focus and intimate bedroom photography framing. ● Fully customizable color scheme variable that unifies pajamas, teddy bear, bedding, and curtains. ● Adjustable mood variable to shift atmosphere from playful and cheerful to calm and serene. ● Warm ambient lighting from a bedside lamp creates a cozy, inviting glow perfect for comfort-focused branding. ## Prompt
