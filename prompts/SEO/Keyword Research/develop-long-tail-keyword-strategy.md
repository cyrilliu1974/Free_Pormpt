# Long-Tail Keyword Research Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Long-Tail Keyword Research Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a structured, metrics-driven list of low-competition long-tail keywords for SEO professionals and content marketers. This long-tail keyword research prompt for ChatGPT walks through scope definition, candidate generation using tools like Ahrefs and SEMrush, search intent classification (informational, transactional, navigational, commercial), and multi-factor evaluation of search volume, competition, and ranking difficulty. It outputs a prioritized table of 15-25 three-or-more-word keyword opportunities, ordered by strategic value, so you can target phrases your audience actually searches while avoiding high-competition terms. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific topic input. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable research process that balances relevance, search demand, and ranking feasibility in a single pass. ● Outputs a table with search volume, competition level, ranking difficulty, and intent for each keyword ● Classifies intent as informational, navigational, transactional, or commercial investigation ● Prioritizes 15-25 three-plus-word phrases that balance opportunity and feasibility ● Guides integration of data from Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Ubersuggest ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in keyword research.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of long-tail keywords (3+ words) for {{topic}} that have lower competition, high specificity, and strong potential to attract targeted organic traffic. Include key metrics for each keyword to support content optimization and SEO strategy decisions.

## Process
1. **Define scope**: Clarify the core topic and its relevance to content strategy and business goals.
2. **Generate candidates**: Use SEO tools (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, SEMrush, Ubersuggest) to identify long-tail keyword opportunities.
3. **Analyze search intent**: Classify each keyword by intent (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial investigation) to ensure alignment with content purpose.
4. **Evaluate metrics**:
   - Search volume: Identify keywords with healthy interest levels
   - Competition level: Prioritize lower-competition keywords
   - Ranking difficulty: Focus on keywords with lower difficulty scores
5. **Select strategically**: Choose keywords that balance relevance, competition, intent alignment, and search volume.
6. **Structure output**: Organize findings into an actionable reference.

## Output
Provide a structured table with columns:
- Long-tail keyword
- Search volume
- Competition level (low/medium/high)
- Ranking difficulty score
- Search intent classification

Order the list by strategic priority, placing the most promising opportunities first. Include 15-25 keywords that collectively support a comprehensive content optimization strategy for the topic.
```
