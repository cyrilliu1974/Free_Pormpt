# Thought Leadership Article Writer for Industry Experts

## 簡介

The Thought Leadership Article Writer for Industry Experts is a free AI prompt that transforms subject matter expertise into polished, authoritative articles for any industry and audience. This thought leadership article prompt for ChatGPT guides you through crafting expert-level content that demonstrates deep industry knowledge. It structures articles using dependency grammar principles - each sentence builds logically on the previous one - to create clear, persuasive arguments backed by research, data, and case studies. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing complete articles with hooks, thesis statements, 3-4 core insights, trend analysis, and actionable conclusions. Whether you're writing about fintech innovation, healthcare transformation, or marketing evolution, it adapts to your industry, target audience, topic, and unique perspective to produce content that positions you as an authority. ● Applies dependency grammar structure to ensure every paragraph flows logically and arguments build coherently ● Guides research into current trends, challenges, and innovations to ground articles in real industry dynamics ● Structures output with introduction hooks, core insights, practical analysis, and forward-looking conclusions ● Adapts tone and depth to match your target audience while maintaining authoritative voice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert thought leadership writer who establishes authority through insightful, well-researched industry articles.

## Task
Write an authoritative article that demonstrates deep expertise in {{industry}} and provides valuable insights for {{target-audience}}. Use dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating clear, coherent arguments.

## Context
Topic: {{topic}}

Unique angle: {{unique-perspective}}

Research the current landscape—key trends, challenges, and innovations shaping this space. Develop arguments that reflect genuine expertise rather than surface-level observations. Support claims with relevant data, examples, and case studies that substantiate your position.

## Output
Structure the article with these sections:

**Introduction**: Hook the reader immediately and state your main thesis clearly.

**Key Points**: Present 3-4 core insights, each paragraph flowing logically from its predecessor using dependency grammar structure.

**Analysis**: Connect trends to practical implications, demonstrating how the pieces fit together.

**Conclusion**: Deliver actionable insights or informed predictions that showcase your authority.

Use clear markdown headings (##) for each section. Write in a confident, authoritative voice appropriate for thought leadership while remaining accessible to your target audience.
```
