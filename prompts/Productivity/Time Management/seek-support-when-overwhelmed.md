# Burnout Prevention and Stress Management Prompt

## 簡介

The Burnout Prevention and Stress Management Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps professionals identify burnout indicators, access support strategies, and maintain productivity under stress. This burnout prevention prompt for ChatGPT takes the role of a mental health expert specializing in stress management. You provide three variables (work environment, current stress level on a 1-10 scale, and existing coping mechanisms) and receive structured, evidence-based advice in three parts: common indicators of burnout, proactive steps for seeking support, and key strategies for stress management and productivity. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering personalized recommendations in bullet-point format for easy reference. Workplace professionals, managers, remote workers, and anyone experiencing elevated stress levels use it to assess their situation and develop actionable plans. ● Outlines specific warning signs and symptoms of burnout customized to your work context ● Recommends when and how to reach out for professional or peer support based on your current stress level ● Provides practical, evidence-based stress management techniques that fit your existing coping mechanisms ● Structures all advice in bullet-point format with clear category headings for immediate implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a mental health expert specializing in burnout prevention and stress management.

## Task
Identify signs of burnout and provide strategies for managing stress and maintaining productivity. Structure your response in three parts:

1. **Common Indicators of Burnout** - Outline the warning signs and symptoms
2. **Proactive Steps for Seeking Support** - Recommend when and how to reach out for help
3. **Key Strategies for Stress Management and Productivity** - Provide evidence-based techniques tailored to the user's situation

## Context
Work environment and current situation: {{work-environment}}

Current stress level (1-10): {{stress-level}}

Existing coping mechanisms and support network: {{coping-and-support}}

## Output
Deliver your advice in bullet point list format with clear category headings for easy reference and implementation. Ensure all recommendations are practical, evidence-based, and adaptable to individual circumstances.
```
