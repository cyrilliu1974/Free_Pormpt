# Language Immersion Plan Generator for Daily Practice

## 簡介

The Language Immersion Plan Generator for Daily Practice is a free AI prompt that builds personalized immersion schedules for learners committed to acquiring fluency through daily exposure and practice. This language immersion prompt for ChatGPT creates a complete weekly plan tailored to your target language, including five daily activities across listening, reading, speaking, writing, and thinking skills; three categories of media (podcasts, YouTube channels, films, books, news sites, or apps) with nine specific recommendations; four conversation practice strategies covering online and offline partners; and a morning-afternoon-evening schedule template distributed across seven days. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting every recommendation to the media landscape, cultural context, and learning resources available for the specific language you want to learn. Learners use it to replace generic study advice with actionable, language-specific routines that fit into real schedules. Reach for this prompt when you need a roadmap that moves beyond textbooks - when you want to surround yourself with authentic content and speaking opportunities every day of the week. ● Outputs five daily immersion activities spanning all core language skills ● Provides nine specific, named media resources across three categories relevant to the target language ● Includes four conversation practice strategies with online and offline options ● Delivers a weekly schedule template with morning, afternoon, and evening time blocks, plus emoji-based progress tracking ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert language learning coach specializing in immersive acquisition techniques.

## Task
Create a comprehensive daily immersion plan for learning {{target-language}}. The plan must include:

1. **Daily Immersion Activities** (5 activities)
   - Practical activities the learner can integrate into their routine
   - Activities should span different skill areas (listening, reading, speaking, writing, thinking)

2. **Media Recommendations** (3 media types, 3 specific recommendations each)
   - Choose media types appropriate for {{target-language}} (podcasts, YouTube channels, TV shows, films, books, news sites, music, apps, etc.)
   - Provide specific titles or names, not generic categories

3. **Conversation Practice Strategies** (4 strategies)
   - Methods for finding conversation partners and practicing speaking
   - Include both online and offline options where applicable

4. **Weekly Immersion Schedule Template**
   - Distribute the activities, media, and strategies across a 7-day schedule
   - Structure: Morning / Afternoon / Evening time blocks
   - Ensure variety and balance across the week

5. **Progress Tracking System**
   - Use emoji indicators: ✅ Completed / ⏳ Partial / ❌ Missed

6. **Phrase of the Day**
   - Provide one useful phrase in {{target-language}} with translation
   - Choose a phrase relevant for daily conversation practice

## Context
Tailor all recommendations specifically to {{target-language}}—consider its media landscape, learning resources, cultural context, and available conversation opportunities. Avoid generic advice that could apply to any language.

## Output Format
Present the plan using clear headings, numbered lists, and a markdown table for the weekly schedule. Begin with "Target Language: [name]" and end with the phrase of the day.
```
