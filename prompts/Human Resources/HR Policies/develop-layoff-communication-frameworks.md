# Layoff Communication Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Layoff Communication Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured workforce transition communication guides for HR leaders, executives, and organizational strategists managing difficult staffing changes. This layoff communication prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces executive messaging frameworks, manager conversation scripts tailored to different employee scenarios, team announcement templates, individual meeting structures, timing plans with legal checkpoints, emotional preparation guidance for leaders, and post-conversation support resources. It addresses the full communication lifecycle from pre-announcement preparation through long-term relationship management with both departing and remaining employees. Use it when you need to communicate workforce reductions while maintaining organizational trust, meeting legal requirements, and preserving morale among team members who stay. ● Creates one-on-one conversation scripts for managers delivering news to high performers, long-tenured staff, and team leads with specific guidance for each scenario. ● Develops executive messaging that communicates organizational rationale clearly while maintaining credibility with remaining employees. ● Provides timing and sequencing plans that coordinate notification order with legal compliance checkpoints and communication cascades. ● Includes trust preservation strategies and post-conversation support resources for both affected employees and team members continuing with the organization. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational communication strategist specializing in workforce transitions. Develop layoff communication guides that balance transparency, empathy, legal compliance, and organizational integrity.

## Task
Create a comprehensive layoff communication framework including:

- **Executive messaging frameworks** – organization-wide announcements and rationale
- **Manager conversation scripts** – one-on-one delivery guidance for different scenarios (high performers, long-tenured staff, team leads)
- **Team announcement templates** – addressing remaining employees
- **Individual meeting structures** – step-by-step conversation flow
- **Timing and sequencing plan** – order of notifications, legal checkpoints
- **Emotional preparation guidance** – leader readiness techniques
- **Post-conversation support resources** – what affected employees receive, ongoing support for remaining staff
- **Trust preservation strategies** – maintaining credibility with survivors while honoring departing employees

## Context
**Company:** {{company-context}}

**Layoff Details:** {{layoff-scope}}

**Compliance Requirements:** {{legal-and-policy-constraints}}

Address the full lifecycle: pre-communication preparation, delivery, immediate follow-up, and long-term relationship management. Account for high-stakes risks including legal exposure, reputation damage, and morale impact.

## Output
Structure all guidance with clear section headings. Provide templates, scripts, and checklists in bullet-point format ready for immediate customization and implementation.
```
