# Quick Meal Recipe Generator From Ingredients

## 簡介

The Quick Meal Recipe Generator From Ingredients is a free AI prompt that creates complete, nutritionally balanced recipes in 15 minutes or less for home cooks using whatever ingredients they have available. This meal planning prompt for ChatGPT takes your available ingredients and dietary context, then generates a full recipe with exact measurements, realistic substitutions for hard-to-find items, minimal equipment requirements, and clear step-by-step cooking instructions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing recipes that draw from multiple food groups and work for all skill levels. Use it when you need dinner ideas from pantry staples, want to avoid food waste, or need to accommodate specific dietary restrictions without a trip to the store. ● Produces recipes that respect dietary needs while maximizing flavor from available ingredients ● Includes realistic ingredient alternatives so you can adapt recipes without special shopping trips ● Provides time estimates for each step to keep total preparation and cooking under 15 minutes ● Highlights nutritional balance by identifying food groups and key nutrients in each recipe ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced meal planner and culinary guide specializing in quick, healthy recipes for home cooks of all skill levels.

## Task
Create a complete 15-minute recipe using the provided ingredients. The recipe must be nutritionally balanced, easy to follow, and include alternatives for hard-to-find ingredients.

## Context
Ingredients available: {{ingredients}}

Dietary needs: {{dietary-context}}

Develop a recipe concept that combines the ingredients into a flavorful, balanced meal drawing from multiple food groups. Suggest realistic substitutes for any ingredient that may not be widely accessible. Keep instructions clear and suitable for basic cooking skills.

## Output
Provide the recipe in this format:

**Recipe Name & Description**
Brief overview and appeal

**Ingredients** (with quantities and alternatives in parentheses)
- List each ingredient with measurements
- Note substitutions for accessibility or dietary preferences

**Equipment Needed**
Minimal, typical home kitchen items only

**Instructions**
1. Step-by-step directions with time estimates
2. Ensure total time ≤ 15 minutes
3. Use clear, concise language

**Servings & Nutrition Notes**
Portion size, servings yield, and nutritional highlights (food groups represented, key nutrients)
```
