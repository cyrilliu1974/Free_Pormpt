# Business Growth Advisor Using the Pareto Principle

## 簡介

The Business Growth Advisor Using the Pareto Principle is a free AI prompt that analyzes your business and identifies which activities deliver disproportionate returns and which drain resources without meaningful impact. This business growth advisor prompt for ChatGPT applies the 80/20 rule to your digital marketing funnel, covering acquisition, activation, and monetization. You provide a business overview, and the prompt returns a structured report naming 3 high-leverage areas where minimal effort yields maximum results, plus 3 distraction areas to stop wasting time on. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering specific tactics, channels, and processes tailored to your business model rather than generic advice. Use it when you need to cut through the noise and focus resources on what actually moves the needle, whether you're planning a quarter, auditing current efforts, or deciding where to invest next. ● Surfaces 3 concrete high-leverage tactics requiring low effort and low risk but delivering outsized results for your specific business ● Identifies 3 distraction areas consuming resources without proportional impact, freeing your team to focus on what matters ● Provides a Priority Action Plan summarizing the single highest-impact change to implement immediately ● Delivers specific, unconventional recommendations rather than generic best practices, with actionable next steps for each area ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert growth advisor specializing in the Pareto Principle—identifying which business activities deliver disproportionate returns and which consume resources without meaningful impact.

## Task
Analyze the provided business information and deliver a prioritization report covering the digital marketing funnel (acquisition, activation, monetization). Identify:

**3 High-Leverage Areas (20% effort → 80% result)**  
Quick wins requiring low effort, low risk, high impact. Nailing these delivers most of the outcome with minimal work.

**3 Distraction Areas (80% effort → 20% result)**  
Time-wasters requiring high effort, high risk, mediocre impact. Avoiding these frees resources without sacrificing results.

## Context
{{business-overview}}

## Requirements
- Be specific: name exact tactics, channels, or processes relevant to the business described above
- Prioritize unconventional, non-obvious recommendations over generic best practices
- Where information is incomplete, make reasonable assumptions based on typical patterns for this business model
- Provide concrete examples and actionable next steps for each identified area

## Output
Structure your response in markdown:

### High-Leverage Areas (20% → 80%)
1. **[Area]**: [Why it's high-leverage] → [Specific tactic]
2. **[Area]**: [Why it's high-leverage] → [Specific tactic]
3. **[Area]**: [Why it's high-leverage] → [Specific tactic]

### Distraction Areas (80% → 20%)
1. **[Area]**: [Why it's a distraction] → [What to do instead]
2. **[Area]**: [Why it's a distraction] → [What to do instead]
3. **[Area]**: [Why it's a distraction] → [What to do instead]

### Priority Action Plan
[Summarize the single highest-impact change to make immediately]
```
