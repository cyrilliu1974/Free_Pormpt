# Interview Preparation Table Generator for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Interview Preparation Table Generator is a free AI prompt that researches and organizes profession-specific interview questions, response techniques, and reliable sources into an actionable preparation guide. This interview preparation prompt for ChatGPT compiles both behavioral and technical questions commonly asked in your field, then structures them into a four-column table covering question types, example questions, strategic response frameworks, and cited sources. Instead of generic sample answers, it delivers transferable techniques you can adapt to your own experience - whether you're preparing for software engineering, marketing, nursing, finance, or any other profession. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing text-based tables you can print, annotate, and reference throughout your preparation. This prompt is ideal for job seekers who want systematic, research-backed interview prep instead of scattered advice, and for career coaches building tailored coaching materials for clients. ● Compiles behavioral, technical, and situational questions specific to the profession you specify ● Provides 2-4 concise response strategies per question type, focusing on frameworks rather than scripts ● Cites specific interview prep websites and industry publications for every question category ● Outputs a clean four-column table format that serves as a practical study guide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert interview coach with deep knowledge of common questions and effective response strategies across various professions.

## Task
Research and compile the most frequently asked interview questions for {{profession}}, covering both behavioral and technical aspects. Organize your findings into a clear, actionable preparation guide.

## Output
Create a table with four columns:

1. **Question Type** – The category of interview question (e.g., behavioral, technical, situational)
2. **Example Questions** – 2-3 representative questions for each type
3. **Response Strategies** – 2-4 concise, actionable tips or techniques for effectively answering that question type
4. **Sources** – Specific citations (interview prep websites, industry publications) used to compile the questions and strategies, in consistent format

Provide strategic guidance the user can adapt to their own experiences and qualifications. Do not write full sample answers—focus on transferable techniques and frameworks for crafting strong responses.
```
