# Street Taco Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Street Taco Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates realistic close-up food photographs with an authentic street-food aesthetic for content creators and food brands. This street taco photo prompt for Midjourney produces images with warm color grading, a shallow 50mm f/2 depth of field, and a gritty documentary style that captures the raw appeal of street food. The prompt simulates professional food photography with natural lighting and texture-rich details. Use it to generate marketing visuals for restaurants, social media posts for food blogs, or menu photography that conveys authenticity and appetite appeal. This prompt is ideal for food photographers, restaurant marketers, and content creators who need quick, convincing street-food imagery without a full photo shoot. ● Produces close-up food shots with shallow depth of field that highlights texture, garnishes, and char marks ● Applies warm tones and a gritty street-food aesthetic for authentic, documentary-style imagery ● Outputs in 3:2 aspect ratio at high quality, suitable for web, print, and social media ● Accepts custom dish descriptions so you can generate tacos, tortas, elotes, or any street-food subject ## Prompt

```
Close-up food photograph of {{dish-description}}, gritty street-food vibe, warm tones, 50mm f/2. --ar 3:2 --quality 2
```
