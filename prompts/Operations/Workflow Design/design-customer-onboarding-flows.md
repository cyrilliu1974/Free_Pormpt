# Customer Onboarding Flow Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Onboarding Flow Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured onboarding experiences to reduce early-stage churn and build user habits for product teams and customer success leaders. This customer onboarding flow prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your product and customer context as input and outputs a tailored 5–12 phase onboarding sequence. Each phase includes timing, objectives, specific touchpoints (email, in-app messages, calls), messaging strategy, success metrics, and the trigger that moves customers forward. It adapts complexity and touchpoint density based on your product type, delivery method, and customer sophistication, balancing excitement with cognitive load to systematically build adoption habits without overwhelming new users. Teams use it to map first-day welcome sequences, week-one education paths, milestone celebrations, and re-engagement triggers that guide customers from signup to active use. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable onboarding blueprint grounded in customer success principles, whether you're launching a SaaS product, a course, a service, or any offering where early engagement predicts long-term retention. ● Outputs 5–12 onboarding phases dynamically adjusted to product complexity and customer journey stage. ● Specifies timing, objectives, key touchpoints, messaging tone, success metrics, and phase-transition triggers for each step. ● Balances educational content with self-directed exploration to reduce cognitive overload and prevent abandonment. ● Helps identify what causes customers to succeed versus churn during the critical first days and weeks. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a Customer Success Architect specializing in onboarding system design. You focus on reducing early-stage churn by creating frictionless adoption experiences that build user habits without overwhelming customers.

## Task

Design a comprehensive customer onboarding flow that reduces churn, accelerates product adoption, and maximizes lifetime value. The flow should consist of 5-12 phases tailored to the product's complexity and the customer's journey stage.

## Context

{{product-and-customer-context}}

Before designing each phase, consider:
- What causes customers to succeed versus abandon during onboarding
- How to balance excitement with cognitive load
- When to provide education versus allowing self-directed exploration
- How to make each touchpoint feel valuable rather than transactional

## Output

For each phase of the onboarding flow, provide:

1. **Phase name and timing** – When it occurs in the customer journey
2. **Primary objective** – What this phase accomplishes
3. **Key touchpoints** – Specific interactions (emails, in-app messages, calls, etc.)
4. **Messaging strategy** – Tone, focus, and content approach
5. **Success metrics** – How to measure phase effectiveness
6. **Transition trigger** – What moves the customer to the next phase

Adapt the number of phases, touchpoint density, and complexity based on the product type, delivery method, customer sophistication, and learning curve described in the context. Ensure the flow feels effortless while systematically engineering habit formation.
```
