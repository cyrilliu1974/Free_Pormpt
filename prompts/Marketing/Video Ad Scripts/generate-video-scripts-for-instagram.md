# Instagram Video Script Generator for Brand Promotion

## 簡介

The Instagram Video Script Generator for Brand Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, platform-optimized video scripts for marketers and content creators promoting products or services on Instagram. This video script prompt for ChatGPT produces three-column scripts with synchronized visuals, audio/voiceover, and on-screen text elements, using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and narrative flow. It tailors the hook, storyline, and call-to-action to Instagram's format conventions while weaving in your key selling points and matching your brand voice. Content teams use it to move from product brief to production-ready script in one pass, saving hours of iteration. Works seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt whenever you need Instagram video content that converts viewers into followers or customers - whether you're launching a product, running a campaign, or building ongoing brand awareness. ● Outputs a three-column format (visuals, audio, on-screen text) ready for production handoff ● Opens with platform-appropriate hooks designed for Instagram's scroll-stop dynamics ● Integrates your key selling points naturally into a persuasive narrative arc ● Includes timing cues and a call-to-action suited to Instagram engagement patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video script writer specializing in social media promotion content.

## Task
Create a compelling video script that increases brand awareness and drives engagement on {{platform}}. Structure the script using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherence.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}

**Key Selling Points:** {{key-selling-points}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the product/service to identify core features and benefits
2. Open with an attention-grabbing hook suited to the platform's conventions
3. Develop a clear narrative arc that maintains engagement
4. Integrate the key selling points naturally throughout
5. Match language and tone to both the target audience and brand voice
6. Close with a persuasive call-to-action appropriate for the platform

## Output
Deliver the script in three synchronized columns:

**VISUALS** | **AUDIO/VOICEOVER** | **ON-SCREEN TEXT**

Include timing cues where relevant and ensure all three elements work together to reinforce the message.
```
