# Profession-Specific Home Office Setup Checklist

## 簡介

The Profession-Specific Home Office Setup Checklist is a free AI prompt that generates customized workspace recommendations for professionals across any industry. This home office setup prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the unique demands of your profession and produces a four-part checklist spanning furniture, technology, ergonomics, and environmental considerations. Unlike generic office advice, every recommendation reflects the actual work patterns, physical requirements, and tools specific to your field - whether you're a graphic designer needing dual monitors and wrist support, a podcaster requiring acoustic treatment, or a financial analyst demanding secure document storage. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and delivers a structured checklist you can use immediately to audit or build your workspace. It is built for remote workers, freelancers, and anyone designing a home office who wants profession-specific guidance rather than one-size-fits-all lists. ● Produces a four-category checklist (furniture, technology, ergonomics, additional considerations) customized to any profession or industry you specify. ● Prioritizes ergonomic health and productivity by addressing posture, repetitive strain risks, lighting, acoustics, and workflow efficiency unique to your field. ● Avoids generic advice - every item reflects the real tools, challenges, and physical demands of the profession entered. ● Outputs a clean, scannable format ready to share with colleagues, use as a shopping guide, or adapt for office budgeting. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a home office setup consultant specializing in ergonomics, productivity optimization, and profession-specific workspace design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive home office setup checklist tailored to {{profession-or-industry}}. The checklist must address the unique demands, workflows, and physical requirements of this field.

## Output
Organize the checklist into four categories:

**Furniture**
List essential furniture prioritizing ergonomics and functional needs specific to {{profession-or-industry}}. Focus on items that directly support the work performed.

**Technology**
Include equipment and technology required to optimize productivity and efficiency in {{profession-or-industry}}.

**Ergonomics**
Provide ergonomic recommendations addressing the physical demands and health considerations particular to {{profession-or-industry}}—posture, repetitive strain risks, viewing distances, and comfort during extended work sessions.

**Additional Considerations**
Cover lighting requirements, storage solutions, acoustic needs, and any industry-specific environmental factors that enhance effectiveness.

Avoid generic office advice. Every recommendation should reflect the actual work patterns, tools, and challenges inherent to {{profession-or-industry}}.
```
