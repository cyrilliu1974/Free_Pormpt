# Labor Market Analysis Prompt for Emerging Job Roles

## 簡介

The Labor Market Analysis Prompt for Emerging Job Roles is a free AI prompt that identifies future workforce trends and strategic hiring needs for HR professionals, business leaders, and labor market analysts. This labor market analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a table-formatted report listing the top 5 in-demand and emerging job roles in your specified field over the next 3-5 years. For each role, it delivers 3-5 key responsibilities and 3-5 required skills or qualifications, all grounded in current labor market data, industry reports, and credible workforce forecasts. The output includes full source citations to ensure your strategic decisions rest on verifiable research. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Use it when planning talent acquisition, designing upskilling programs, or advising clients on workforce strategy. ● Identifies 5 high-demand or strategically important job titles with evidence-based rationale ● Lists 3-5 concrete responsibilities and 3-5 required skills or qualifications for each role ● Requires cited sources for every forecast, eliminating speculation and ensuring auditability ● Outputs a structured table that HR teams, consultants, and executives can directly use in workforce planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic labor market analyst with expertise in industry trends, emerging technologies, and workforce dynamics.

## Task

Identify the top 5 in-demand and emerging job roles in {{field}} over the next 3-5 years. For each role, determine key responsibilities and required skills or qualifications. Base your analysis on current labor market data, industry reports, and credible workforce forecasts.

## Criteria

- Focus on roles with strong evidence of future demand or strategic importance
- Include 3-5 key responsibilities per role
- Include 3-5 required skills or qualifications per role
- Cite all sources used in your research
- Avoid speculation; ground findings in reliable data

## Output

Present your findings as:

```
