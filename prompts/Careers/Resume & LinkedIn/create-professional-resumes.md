# Cover Letter Writer for Job Applications

## 簡介

The Cover Letter Writer for Job Applications is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, interview-winning cover letters tailored to specific job postings and companies. This cover letter prompt for ChatGPT takes your applicant details, target job information, and employer contact data to produce a complete one-page business letter formatted to professional standards. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, opening with a compelling hook, weaving in keywords from the job description, demonstrating authentic knowledge of the company's mission and values, and closing with a confident call to action. Job seekers use it when applying for positions that require personalized outreach rather than generic template letters, ensuring each application speaks directly to the employer's needs. ● Produces a complete cover letter with proper business formatting including date, contact blocks, salutation, body paragraphs, and signature line ● Incorporates keywords naturally from the job description while highlighting your most relevant qualifications with specific examples ● Demonstrates authentic knowledge of the target company by connecting your experience to their mission, values, or current work ● Avoids clichés and generic phrasing in favor of confident, professional language that captures attention and prompts interview requests ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert job application writer who creates tailored, persuasive cover letters that secure interviews.

## Task
Write a complete cover letter in standard business format that:
- Opens with genuine enthusiasm and a strong hook that captures attention
- Highlights the most relevant qualifications using specific examples and keywords from the job description
- Demonstrates knowledge of the company and explains authentic alignment with its mission, values, or work
- Closes with a confident call to action expressing interest in an interview
- Uses professional tone throughout, avoiding clichés and generic phrases

## Context
{{applicant-details}}
(Include: name, contact information, relevant skills, key experiences, and notable achievements)

{{job-and-company}}
(Include: job title, company name, why you're interested in this specific company, and how you learned about the role)

{{employer-contact}}
(Include: hiring manager's name if known, their title, company address; use "Hiring Manager" if name is unavailable)

## Output
Deliver a complete, one-page cover letter formatted as:
- Date and full contact blocks (applicant's and employer's)
- Professional salutation
- 3-4 concise paragraphs (introduction, 1-2 body paragraphs, closing)
- Professional signature line
- Single-spaced with double spacing between paragraphs
- Business letter conventions throughout

Incorporate keywords naturally from the job description. Proofread to eliminate all errors.
```
