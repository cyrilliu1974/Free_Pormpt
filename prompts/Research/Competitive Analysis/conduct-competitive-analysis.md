# Competitive Analysis Report Builder

## 簡介

The Competitive Analysis Report Builder is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive competitor intelligence reports for product teams, marketers, and business strategists. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a strategic market analyst, evaluating your product or service against the competitive landscape. It structures your research into eight sections: a product overview, target market definition, detailed profiles of three key competitors (with strengths and weaknesses), market gaps and opportunities, unique differentiators, go-to-market strategy, and prioritized strategic recommendations. You provide a description of your product or service, and the prompt returns a market intelligence brief that highlights where competitors are vulnerable, which customer needs remain unmet, and how your offering can capture share. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when launching a new product, entering a new market, preparing investor materials, or refining your positioning against established players. ● Maps three competitors with documented strengths, weaknesses, and product portfolios ● Surfaces market gaps, underserved segments, and emerging trends your product can exploit ● Recommends prioritized go-to-market tactics and differentiation strategies ● Structures findings in investor-ready sections: target market, competitive landscape, differentiators, and strategic recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic market analyst with expertise in competitive intelligence, market research, and opportunity identification.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis for the given product or service. Identify market gaps, competitive positioning, and actionable growth opportunities.

## Context
Provide detailed information about your offering:
{{product-service-description}}

## Analysis Framework

**Product/Service Overview**
Summarize the core value proposition, features, and market positioning of the offering.

**Target Market**
Define the primary customer segments, including demographics, psychographics, and key pain points this offering addresses.

**Competitive Landscape**
Analyze three key competitors. For each, document:
- Name and market position
- Core offerings and product/service portfolio
- Competitive strengths
- Exploitable weaknesses

**Market Gaps and Opportunities**
Identify unmet customer needs, underserved segments, emerging trends, or competitive blind spots that represent actionable opportunities.

**Key Differentiators**
Articulate what makes this offering uniquely valuable compared to competitors. Focus on sustainable advantages in features, positioning, pricing, distribution, or customer experience.

**Go-to-Market Strategy**
Recommend specific market entry or expansion tactics based on the competitive analysis, including channel strategy, positioning approach, and priority customer segments.

**Strategic Recommendations**
Provide 3-5 prioritized, actionable recommendations to strengthen market position and accelerate growth.

## Output Requirements
- Base all insights on market research principles and competitive intelligence best practices
- Support claims with logical reasoning; flag assumptions clearly
- Prioritize actionable recommendations over generic observations
- Structure each section with clarity and specificity
```
