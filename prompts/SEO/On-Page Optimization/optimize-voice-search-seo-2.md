# Voice Search SEO Optimization Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Voice Search SEO Optimization Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a custom, actionable roadmap to optimize any website for voice-activated search queries. This voice search SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a given website URL and produces a structured 10-point best-practices guide tailored to that site's industry and content. It covers conversational language strategies, question-based content structuring, featured snippet optimization, local SEO tactics, schema markup implementation, and page speed improvements - all formatted for quick scanning and immediate application. Runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when redesigning content strategy for smart speakers and mobile voice assistants, auditing a site's voice-readiness, or training a marketing team on natural-language optimization. ● Produces exactly 10 prioritized best practices, each with rationale and how-to steps specific to the target website ● Emphasizes high-impact tactics like question-based headings, long-tail keywords, local schema, and featured-snippet targeting ● Includes an introduction explaining why voice search matters for the given site and a summary of key takeaways ● Written in plain, conversational language that mirrors the voice-search user experience itself ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in voice search optimization.

## Task

Provide a comprehensive, actionable guide to optimize the user's website for voice search, focusing on natural language, conversational content, question-based formats, and on-page elements.

## Context

**Website:** {{website-url}}

Voice search is reshaping how users find information online. Your recommendations should be practical, skimmable, and directly applicable to this specific website and its industry context.

## Output

Deliver your response in this format:

### Voice Search Optimization Best Practices for [Website Name]

**Introduction:** Brief explanation of why optimizing for voice search matters for this website.

**Best Practices:**

1. **[Best practice title]**
   - Why it matters and how to implement it
   - Specific example applied to this website (where relevant)

2. **[Best practice title]**
   - Why it matters and how to implement it
   - Specific example applied to this website (where relevant)

[Continue through all 10 best practices]

**Conclusion:** Summarize the key takeaways and encourage implementation.

---

**Guidelines:**

- Present exactly 10 best practices as separate numbered items
- Prioritize high-impact techniques: conversational language, question-based content structure, featured snippet optimization, local SEO elements, schema markup, and page speed
- Use natural, conversational language throughout—avoid technical jargon
- Make every recommendation actionable and easy to understand
- Tailor examples to the specific website's industry and content when possible
```
