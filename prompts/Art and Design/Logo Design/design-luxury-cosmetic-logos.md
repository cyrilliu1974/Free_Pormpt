# Luxury Cosmetic Logo Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Cosmetic Logo Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates flat vector logos conveying elegance, sophistication, and sensory indulgence for premium beauty and cosmetics brands. This cosmetic logo design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces either refined wordmarks in elegant modern serif typography or abstract geometric symbols derived from letterforms, droplets, botanical curves, or feminine geometry. It outputs minimalist contemporary luxury designs with flat design principles - no gradients or textures - ensuring the logo remains iconic and memorable at small scales, from product packaging to social media avatars. Beauty entrepreneurs, cosmetics startups, and brand designers use it to create timeless visual identities that communicate premium quality without hiring expensive agencies or learning complex design software. ● Customizable brand name, logo type (wordmark or symbol), and color palette variables for tailored output ● Solid-fill color application ensuring recognition in single-color versions across all media ● Pure white or transparent backgrounds for versatility on packaging, websites, and social platforms ● Crisp edges and balanced proportions designed to scale from tiny social avatars to large-format print ## Prompt

```
Flat vector logo design for {{brand-name}}, a clean luxury beauty and cosmetics brand. The design conveys elegance, sophistication, and sensory indulgence.

**Style:** Minimalist contemporary luxury with flat design principles, no gradients or textures.

**Form:** {{logo-type}} – either a refined wordmark in elegant modern serif typography, or an abstract geometric symbol derived from letterforms or beauty-related shapes (droplet, botanical curve, feminine geometry). Design must be iconic and memorable at small scales.

**Color:** {{color-palette}}, applied in solid fills only. Design should remain recognizable in single-color versions.

**Background:** Pure white or transparent, ensuring versatility across packaging, digital, and print applications.

**Requirements:** Scalable from product packaging to social media avatars. Crisp edges, balanced proportions, timeless aesthetic suitable for premium cosmetics positioning.
```
