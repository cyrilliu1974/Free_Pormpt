# Live Chat Support Implementation Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Live Chat Support Implementation Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed roadmap for integrating live chat on your website to boost customer engagement and sales. This live chat support prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your website URL, business context, and current support channels to produce a structured implementation plan. It delivers chat script examples tailored to your products - opening greetings, inquiry responses, objection handling, and hand-off protocols - plus customer service best practices including tone guidelines, response templates, escalation procedures, and quality standards. The prompt also covers technical implementation (widget placement, platform selection, mobile responsiveness, channel integration), response time optimization (staffing models, automated routing, peak coverage), and performance metrics (response time, resolution rate, CSAT, conversion impact). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Ideal for e-commerce businesses, SaaS companies, and service providers launching or upgrading live chat support without starting from scratch. ● Produces chat scripts with opening greetings, product inquiry responses, objection handling, and escalation hand-offs specific to your business ● Defines customer service tone guidelines, response templates, and quality standards aligned with your brand ● Recommends chat widget placement, platform selection criteria, mobile responsiveness requirements, and integration with existing channels ● Outlines staffing models, automated routing logic, and peak-hour coverage strategies to optimize response times ● Specifies performance metrics to track - response time, resolution rate, customer satisfaction, conversion impact - with reporting cadence and improvement benchmarks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer support strategist specializing in live chat implementation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan for integrating live chat support on a website to enhance customer engagement and increase sales. Cover chat script development, customer service best practices, technical implementation, and performance tracking.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Business context: {{business-context}}
Current support channels: {{current-support-channels}}

The business context should describe your business type, target audience, and key products or services being supported.

## Output
Deliver a detailed live chat integration roadmap in numbered list format with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps. Include:

1. Chat script examples tailored to the business context—opening greetings, product inquiry responses, objection handling, and hand-off protocols
2. Customer service best practices—tone guidelines, response templates, escalation procedures, and quality standards
3. Technical implementation guidelines—chat widget placement recommendations, platform selection criteria, mobile responsiveness requirements, and integration with existing support channels
4. Response time optimization strategies—staffing models, automated vs. human chat routing, peak hour coverage
5. Performance metrics and tracking—key metrics to monitor (response time, resolution rate, customer satisfaction, conversion impact), reporting cadence, and improvement benchmarks

Ensure all recommendations are actionable and specific to the provided business context.
```
