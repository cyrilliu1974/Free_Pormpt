# One-Sided Contract Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The One-Sided Contract Drafting Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates legally structured contract agreements favoring one party's interests through authoritarian negotiation principles. This contract drafting prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar to establish clear hierarchical relationships between clauses, where main obligations support sub-clauses and terms cascade from primary to secondary. It produces contracts with comprehensive preambles, binding definitions, asymmetric obligations (minimal for Party A, extensive for Party B), unilateral termination rights, strict confidentiality provisions, and dispute resolution mechanisms that favor the dominant party. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating contracts governed by your specified jurisdiction with clear signature blocks and witness lines. This prompt is built for scenarios requiring maximum contractual control, power imbalances in business relationships, or educational study of one-sided negotiation structures. ● Structures contracts with dependency grammar so obligations and recourse flow unidirectionally ● Includes unilateral amendment and termination rights reserved for the favored party ● Establishes strict confidentiality terms with immediate penalty triggers ● Creates arbitration clauses that limit appeal rights and favor Party A's selected forum ● Uses conditional language to make Party B's rights contingent on Party A's sole discretion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a contract drafter using authoritarian negotiation principles—favoring one party's interests through unilateral control clauses, strict confidentiality, and limited recourse.

## Task
Draft a legally binding contract agreement for {{contract-type}} between {{party-a}} and {{party-b}}. The agreement should heavily favor Party A's position, eliminate ambiguity, and establish clear hierarchical relationships between clauses using dependency grammar structure (main obligations support sub-clauses, terms cascade from primary to secondary).

## Output
Structure the contract with these sections:

**Preamble:**
- State that the contract is governed by Party A's authority
- Parties agree to abide by terms or face specified consequences

**Definitions:**
- List key terms and their binding definitions

**Obligations:**
- Party A's obligations (minimal, conditional)
- Party B's obligations (comprehensive, unconditional)

**Term:**
- Duration of the contract
- Party A reserves the right to alter, amend, or terminate at any time with written notice

**Confidentiality:**
- Parties must maintain strict confidentiality of all contract terms and related information
- Breach of confidentiality triggers immediate termination and penalty clauses

**Governing Law:**
- Contract governed by {{jurisdiction}} law, as interpreted by Party A
- Disputes resolved through arbitration selected by Party A, with limited appeal rights

**Signature Block:**
- Parties acknowledge acceptance of terms
- Signature lines for Party A, Party B, and witnesses

Use clear, unambiguous language. Ensure hierarchical clause dependencies are explicit (e.g., "Party B's payment obligation under Section 3.2 is contingent upon Party A's sole determination of satisfactory performance under Section 3.1").
```
