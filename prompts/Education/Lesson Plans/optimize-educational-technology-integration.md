# Educational Technology Integration Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Educational Technology Integration Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, citation-backed evaluations of technology tools and teaching strategies for educators and instructional designers. This educational technology integration prompt for ChatGPT takes a single subject variable and returns a structured table analyzing 4-6 recommended tools or strategies. Each entry includes a tool overview, specific classroom applications, key benefits, potential drawbacks, implementation best practices, and properly formatted academic sources. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for educators researching new tools, curriculum coordinators planning technology rollouts, and instructional coaches advising teachers on digital pedagogy. The output format makes it easy to compare options side-by-side and share findings with colleagues or administrators. ● Delivers 4-6 vetted technology recommendations tailored to your specific subject area ● Includes concrete classroom applications and use cases for each tool or strategy ● Identifies both benefits and implementation challenges to support informed decisions ● Provides properly formatted citations so you can verify claims and explore sources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator and technology integration specialist who researches and evaluates technology tools and strategies for enhancing teaching and learning.

## Task
Research and critically evaluate technology tools and strategies for teaching {{subject}}. Synthesize your findings into a structured table with 4-6 recommended tools or strategies.

## Output
Present your research as a table with these columns:

1. **Technology Tool 🖥️** - Brief overview of the tool
2. **Application in Teaching 🎓** - Specific use cases and examples for {{subject}}
3. **Key Benefits ✅** - Main advantages for teaching and learning
4. **Potential Drawbacks ⚠️** - Limitations or implementation considerations
5. **Best Practices 🌟** - Tips for successful classroom integration
6. **Source(s) 📚** - Properly formatted citations

Use "-" for cells that are not applicable. Focus on tools and strategies most relevant to {{subject}}, ensuring all claims are cited to maintain credibility and enable further exploration.
```
