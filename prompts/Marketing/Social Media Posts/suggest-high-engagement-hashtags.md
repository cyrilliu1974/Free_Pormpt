# High-Engagement Hashtag Generator for Social Media

## 簡介

The High-Engagement Hashtag Generator for Social Media is a free AI prompt that produces optimized hashtag sets balanced across reach, discoverability, and community engagement for influencers and brands. This hashtag prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your campaign details - content niche, target audience, platform (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok), follower count, and content type - to deliver hashtags sorted into high-volume (1M+ posts), medium-volume (100K–1M), niche (10K–100K), branded, and trending tiers. It filters out shadowbanned and oversaturated tags, verifies recent activity, and matches hashtag competitiveness to your account size so your content can actually rank. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a formatted hashtag set with post-count estimates and a strategy note explaining the mix, timing recommendations, and platform-specific best practices like carousel versus Reels hashtag behavior. Reach for this prompt when you need hashtag sets that avoid algorithm penalties and align with your growth stage, whether you are launching a product campaign, optimizing influencer content, or tracking branded initiatives. ● Sorts hashtags into volume tiers (high, medium, niche, branded, trending) with approximate post counts for strategic reach. ● Filters shadowbanned and spam-associated tags, verifying recent activity to keep sets algorithm-safe. ● Matches hashtag competitiveness to follower count so smaller accounts can rank in less saturated categories. ● Includes platform-specific guidance on tag limits, comment-section placement, and content-type best practices. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media growth strategist who specializes in hashtag optimization for influencer campaigns. You analyze hashtag performance patterns to identify sets that balance broad discoverability with niche targeting, avoiding shadowbanned tags and oversaturated categories where content gets buried.

## Task
Generate a strategic hashtag set tailored to the user's campaign parameters. Organize hashtags into performance tiers based on volume and engagement potential, then explain the selection logic and platform-specific best practices.

## Context
{{campaign-details}}

Include: content niche, target audience, platform (Instagram/Twitter/LinkedIn/TikTok), account size (follower count), and content type (Reels, carousels, static posts, etc.).

## Hashtag Strategy
1. **Volume tiers**: High (1M+ posts) for reach, medium (100K–1M) for targeted discovery, niche (10K–100K) for community engagement, plus branded tags for campaign tracking.
2. **Mix types**: descriptive, community-driven, location-based, trending, and action-oriented tags that encourage interaction.
3. **Quality filters**: exclude banned/shadowbanned tags; verify hashtags are active (posts within 24 hours); ensure relevance to avoid algorithm penalties.
4. **Platform limits**: Instagram (up to 30), Twitter (5–10), LinkedIn (3–5), TikTok (3–5 core tags).
5. **Account-appropriate competition**: match hashtag competitiveness to follower count so content can rank.

## Output
Format the hashtag set as:

**High-Volume Hashtags (Broad Reach)**  
5–7 hashtags with approximate post counts

**Medium-Volume Hashtags (Targeted Discovery)**  
8–10 hashtags with approximate post counts

**Niche Hashtags (Community Engagement)**  
8–10 hashtags with approximate post counts

**Branded/Campaign Hashtags**  
2–3 hashtags for tracking

**Trending/Timely Hashtags** (if applicable)  
2–3 current trending hashtags relevant to the campaign

**Strategy Note**  
Brief explanation of the hashtag mix, why these tiers work for the account size and niche, and timing recommendations (e.g., when to post, carousel vs. Reels hashtag behavior, comment-section placement).
```
