# Customer Follow-Up Automation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Follow-Up Automation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates detailed implementation plans for automating lead nurturing and customer follow-up workflows tailored to your business and CRM platform. This customer follow-up automation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a six-part strategy covering email sequence architecture, customer segmentation criteria, interaction tracking setup, behavior-based personalization rules, KPI measurement frameworks, and phased implementation timelines. You provide your business context and current CRM system, and the prompt delivers actionable sub-tasks grounded in email marketing and data analysis best practices. Marketing teams use it to design automated nurture campaigns that convert leads into customers, while CRM managers apply it to map out scalable follow-up systems that respond to customer behavior in real time. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from manual follow-up processes to a structured, automated system that maintains engagement without overwhelming your team. ● Designs email sequence cadences with triggers and content themes aligned to your sales cycle ● Defines customer segmentation criteria based on behavior, demographics, and engagement levels ● Specifies interaction tracking touchpoints including email opens, clicks, form submissions, and page visits ● Creates personalization rules that dynamically adjust content based on customer data and behavior patterns ● Establishes KPI measurement frameworks with reporting dashboards and optimization triggers ● Delivers phased implementation timelines with milestones and resource requirements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) expert specializing in lead nurturing automation and conversion optimization.

## Task
Design a comprehensive strategy to automate customer follow-up processes that nurture leads and increase conversions. Develop a detailed implementation plan covering automated email sequences, interaction tracking, and behavior-based personalization.

## Context
**Business context:** {{business-context}}

**Current CRM system:** {{crm-system}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list with clear headings for each major implementation step. Include:

1. **Email Sequence Architecture** – cadence, triggers, and content themes aligned with the sales cycle
2. **Customer Segmentation Strategy** – criteria for grouping leads based on behavior, demographics, and engagement
3. **Interaction Tracking Setup** – touchpoints to monitor (email opens, link clicks, form submissions, page visits)
4. **Personalization Rules** – dynamic content logic tied to customer data and behavior patterns
5. **KPI Measurement Framework** – metrics, reporting dashboards, and optimization triggers
6. **Implementation Timeline** – phased rollout with milestones and resource requirements

For each step, provide actionable sub-tasks grounded in email marketing and data analysis best practices. Tailor recommendations to the specified business context and CRM platform capabilities.
```
