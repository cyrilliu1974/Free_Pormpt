# Wet Portrait Close-Up Generator

## 簡介

The Wet Portrait Close-Up Generator is a free AI prompt that produces cinematic extreme close-up portraits with realistic water and moisture effects for photographers, content creators, and brand marketers. This wet portrait prompt for Midjourney creates side-profile portraits featuring dark wet hair naturally adhering to skin, fine water droplets, realistic facial moisture, and bright natural eyes with authentic reflections. It specifies soft cool lighting, customizable background bokeh tones, and photorealistic 8K output quality. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, and includes negative prompts to avoid cartoon styles, excessive gloss, or plastic skin textures. Photographers shooting beauty campaigns, skincare brands needing hero imagery, and social media managers building fashion content reach for this when studio sessions are impractical. ● Produces side-profile portraits with dark wet hair, fine water droplets, and natural skin glistening that mimics post-shower or rain photography. ● Customizable subject features and background tones while maintaining soft cool lighting, realistic eye reflections, and moist lip texture. ● Includes negative prompts to eliminate cartoon rendering, excessive eye shine, plastic skin, and other common AI artifacts. ● Supports image reference settings at 0.75 strength with Canny or Depth ControlNet to preserve specific facial features across generations. ## Prompt

```
Extreme wet close-up portrait, side profile view. {{subject-description}} with dark wet hair naturally glued to the skin. Fine droplets of water and thin sweat glistening realistically on the face. Bright natural eyes with realistic reflection, avoiding excessive shine. Moist shiny lips, soft natural texture. Soft lighting with cool shades, {{background-tone}} bokeh background. Photorealistic, cinematic, 8k resolution, high fidelity.

**Negative prompt:** cartoon, illustration, 3d render, drawing, painting, blurry, low quality, distorted features, dry skin, matte finish, excessive gloss on eyes, warm tones, harsh contrast, bad anatomy, plastic skin

**Image reference settings:** Use uploaded image at 0.75 strength with Canny or Depth ControlNet to maintain facial features.
```
