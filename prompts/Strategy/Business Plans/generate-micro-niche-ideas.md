# Micro-Niche Business Idea Generator

## 簡介

The Micro-Niche Business Idea Generator is a free AI prompt that identifies three profitable sub-niches within broader markets for entrepreneurs and strategists seeking underserved segments with clear commercial potential. This micro-niche business prompt for ChatGPT analyzes consumer trends and market gaps to surface highly specific opportunities that are narrow enough to dominate yet profitable enough to sustain. For each idea, it delivers a niche description explaining why the segment is underserved, detailed target demographics (age, gender, income, geography), key pain points, a proposed product or service solution, and a complete go-to-market strategy covering marketing channels, customer acquisition tactics, and pricing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Entrepreneurs use it to escape crowded markets, product developers reach for it when validating new concepts, and consultants deploy it to uncover white-space opportunities for clients. ● Identifies three distinct micro-niches per market category, each with a specific underserved demographic and clear differentiation from mainstream competitors ● Provides structured demographic profiles including age range, gender distribution, income level, and geographic targeting for precise audience definition ● Maps pain points to proposed solutions, ensuring every business idea directly addresses real unmet needs ● Delivers actionable go-to-market strategies with concrete marketing channels, customer acquisition tactics, and pricing guidance for faster launch ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a micro-niche business strategist specializing in identifying profitable sub-niches within broader markets by analyzing consumer trends and underserved customer needs.

## Task

Generate three highly targeted micro-niche business ideas within the specified market category. Each idea must address a specific underserved segment with clear commercial potential.

## Context

Broad market category: {{market-category}}

## Output

For each of the three micro-niche ideas, provide:

**Micro-Niche Idea [1/2/3]:**

● **Niche Description:** A concise explanation of the specific sub-niche and why it's underserved

● **Target Demographic:**
  ● Age range
  ● Gender distribution
  ● Income level
  ● Geographic location or market

● **Key Pain Points:** The specific problems or unmet needs this demographic experiences

● **Proposed Solution:** A product or service that directly addresses these pain points

● **Go-to-Market Strategy:** Specific marketing channels, customer acquisition tactics, and pricing strategy

Ensure each micro-niche is narrow enough to dominate, profitable enough to sustain a business, and differentiated from mainstream offerings in the broader market.
```
