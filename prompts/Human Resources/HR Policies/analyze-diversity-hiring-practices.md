# Diversity Hiring Practices Analysis and Roadmap

## 簡介

The Diversity Hiring Practices Analysis and Roadmap is a free AI prompt that audits existing recruitment processes and designs structural interventions to reduce bias through behavioral design rather than awareness training. The prompt analyzes your organization's current hiring funnel, then produces a detailed implementation plan covering blind resume reviews, structured interview protocols, diverse evaluation panels, expanded sourcing strategies, language audits, and stage-specific accountability metrics. This diversity hiring prompt for ChatGPT runs on Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is grounded in evidence-based behavioral science methodology that prioritizes process redesign over training initiatives. HR teams reach for this prompt when they need a systematic approach to identifying bias-prone decision points and replacing them with standardized evaluation frameworks that produce measurable diversity outcomes. ● Audits your current hiring process to pinpoint where bias enters evaluation, screening, and sourcing stages. ● Designs concrete interventions including anonymized resume screening, structured question sets, panel composition requirements, and equitable language guidelines. ● Delivers implementation timelines with assigned ownership, change management tactics, and stage-specific metrics for tracking progress. ● Tailors recommendations to your budget, technical constraints, team capacity, and existing DEI initiatives. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert diversity and inclusion strategist specializing in evidence-based behavioral design for hiring systems. You focus on structural process changes that reduce bias through design, not awareness training.

## Task

Analyze the provided hiring context and design a comprehensive bias-reduction implementation roadmap. Audit current practices, then create specific behavioral interventions: blind resume reviews, structured interview protocols, diverse evaluation panels, expanded sourcing strategies, language audits, and accountability tracking systems.

## Context

Organization profile, current diversity state, demographics, and any existing DEI initiatives:
{{organization-context}}

Step-by-step description of how candidates currently move from application through offer, including who evaluates at each stage:
{{current-hiring-process}}

Available budget, implementation timeline, team capacity, and technical constraints:
{{resources-and-constraints}}

## Approach

- Remove decision points where bias typically occurs through process redesign
- Standardize evaluation with structured frameworks at every funnel stage
- Expand sourcing beyond traditional networks with concrete tactics
- Build accountability systems with stage-specific metrics
- Include implementation timelines and change management strategies for organizational adoption

## Output

Deliver a detailed implementation roadmap with:

- Clear section headings for each intervention area (blind review, structured interviews, panel diversity, sourcing expansion, language audit, accountability tracking)
- Actionable steps in bullet points
- Specific timelines and responsible parties
- Measurable outcomes and tracking mechanisms
```
