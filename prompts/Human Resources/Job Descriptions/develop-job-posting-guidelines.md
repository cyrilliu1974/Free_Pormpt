# Job Posting Guidelines Builder

## 簡介

The Job Posting Guidelines Builder is a free AI prompt that creates actionable job posting standards by applying Nielsen's web usability principles to recruitment content for HR teams and hiring managers. This job posting guidelines prompt for ChatGPT produces complete frameworks covering optimal length, formatting, keyword placement, visual hierarchy, reusable templates, and compliance checklists. It translates web usability research - scannability, inverted pyramid writing, and mobile-first design - into practical rules that balance speed, legal compliance, job board algorithms, and candidate behavior. Teams use it to standardize how they write job descriptions, reduce time-to-post, and improve application conversion rates across multiple roles and departments. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish or refresh organization-wide standards for writing job postings that attract top talent and perform well on job boards. ● Applies Nielsen's scannability, inverted pyramid, and visual hierarchy principles to job posting structure and formatting. ● Produces SEO and keyword strategies tailored to job board algorithms and mobile candidate behavior. ● Includes reusable templates and compliance checklists that prevent legal issues while maintaining readability. ● Delivers section headings, bullet-point guidelines, and practical checklists ready for immediate rollout. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert HR strategist specializing in job posting optimization using web usability principles to maximize candidate engagement and application conversion rates.

## Task

Create comprehensive, actionable job posting guidelines that apply Nielsen's web usability principles—scannability, inverted pyramid writing, and visual hierarchy—to transform how the organization attracts talent. The guidelines must balance hiring manager speed requirements, legal compliance, job board algorithm optimization, and mobile-first candidate behavior.

## Context

{{posting-context}}

## Output

Deliver structured guidelines covering:

- **Optimal length and formatting strategies** for maximum scannability and mobile readability
- **Strategic keyword placement** and SEO best practices tailored to job board algorithms
- **Visual hierarchy techniques** that accelerate reading comprehension and highlight unique selling points
- **Reusable templates** with consistent structure that improve conversion rates
- **Compliance checklists** that prevent legal issues while maintaining readability

Format your response with:
- Clear section headings (##)
- Actionable guidelines in bullet points
- Ready-to-use templates
- Practical checklists for immediate implementation
```
