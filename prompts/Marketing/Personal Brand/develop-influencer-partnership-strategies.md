# Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven influencer marketing plans for brands, agencies, and marketing teams seeking authentic partnerships. This influencer partnership strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, target audience, and budget to identify suitable influencer niches and recommend specific collaboration formats. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured markdown table that maps influencer profiles to partnership ideas optimized for your marketing goals - whether awareness, engagement, or conversions. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond guesswork and build a diversified influencer strategy that spans micro to macro creators across multiple content categories. ● Identifies influencer niches that align with your brand values and target audience demographics ● Recommends specific creators across different follower tiers (micro, mid, macro) to diversify reach and budget allocation ● Suggests concrete partnership formats including sponsored posts, product reviews, giveaways, and long-term ambassador programs ● Delivers a markdown table with influencer profiles, niche categories, and detailed collaboration ideas tied to your marketing objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert influencer marketing strategist developing partnership strategies that expand brand reach and drive conversions.

## Task
Create a tailored influencer partnership strategy by:
1. Analyzing the business context and target audience to identify suitable influencer niches
2. Selecting potential influencers who align with brand values and demonstrate authentic audience engagement
3. Developing creative partnership ideas that resonate with each influencer's community while advancing marketing goals
4. Recommending collaboration formats (sponsored posts, product reviews, giveaways, ambassador programs) optimized for impact and budget

## Context
{{business-context}}
(Include: business type, target audience demographics and psychographics, brand values, specific marketing goals such as awareness/engagement/conversions, and budget range for influencer partnerships)

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Influencer Name**: Real or realistic profile name with follower range
- **Niche**: Their content category and audience focus
- **Partnership Idea**: Detailed collaboration format, deliverables, and how it advances the stated marketing goals

Provide at least 5 entries that span different niches, follower tiers (micro to macro), and partnership types to create a diversified strategy.
```
