# Google Ads Keyword Research Generator

## 簡介

The Google Ads Keyword Research Generator is a free AI prompt that produces detailed keyword research reports with volume estimates, competition analysis, and strategic campaign groupings for paid search marketers. This Google Ads keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context to identify high-performing search terms across broad commercial intent, long-tail variations, industry-specific phrases, and local modifiers. It outputs structured markdown tables showing each keyword's estimated monthly search volume and advertiser competition level (Low/Medium/High), then organizes findings into 3-5 thematic campaign clusters with bid strategy recommendations and priority targeting guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to handle everything from e-commerce stores to B2B service providers. Reach for this prompt when planning a new Google Ads campaign, expanding into new markets, or auditing existing keyword portfolios for untapped opportunities. ● Produces keyword tables with search volume estimates and competition levels based on industry analysis ● Organizes results into logical campaign groups with thematic clustering for easier ad group structure ● Delivers bid strategy insights tailored to competition levels and search intent patterns ● Identifies priority keywords to target first based on value potential and budget constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in Google Ads keyword research and campaign planning.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive keyword research report that identifies high-performing Google Ads keywords for the specified business. Analyze search intent, provide volume estimates, assess competition levels, and organize keywords into logical campaign groups.

## Context
Business and market details:
{{business-context}}

## Process
1. Analyze the business type, industry, and target audience to identify core search intents
2. Generate relevant keywords including:
   - Broad commercial intent terms
   - Long-tail variations
   - Industry-specific phrases
   - Local modifiers (if applicable)
3. Estimate monthly search volume based on industry trends and market size
4. Assess competition level (Low/Medium/High) by considering ad saturation and CPC indicators
5. Group keywords by theme or campaign structure
6. Provide bid strategy recommendations for high-value keyword clusters

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Competition |
|---------|---------------|-------------|

**Column definitions:**
- **Keyword**: The exact search term or phrase
- **Search Volume**: Estimated average monthly searches
- **Competition**: Advertiser competition level (Low/Medium/High)

After the table, include:
- **Keyword Groups**: Organize keywords into 3-5 thematic campaign clusters
- **Bid Strategy Insights**: Recommended approaches for high-value keywords based on competition and intent
- **Priority Recommendations**: Top 5 keywords to target first given the budget constraints
```
