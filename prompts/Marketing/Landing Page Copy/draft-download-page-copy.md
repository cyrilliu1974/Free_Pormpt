# Download Page Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Download Page Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customer-focused post-download page copy for businesses and marketers using the StoryBrand framework. This download page copy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your product details, target audience, and desired next action to produce a complete page structure with hero headlines, gratitude messaging, benefit-driven content, usage instructions, trust reinforcement, and empowerment messaging. The prompt automatically scales from 3-5 sections for simple products to 6-8 sections for complex solutions, weaving in social proof and risk-reversal elements throughout. Reach for it when you need to turn a download moment into a conversion opportunity that positions the customer as the hero of their transformation journey. ● Delivers messaging architecture with hero success vision, guide positioning, clear plan, call-to-action sequence, and success stakes. ● Produces seven core copy sections including hero headline, gratitude affirmation, benefits overview, usage steps, next-action path, trust reinforcement, and final empowerment message. ● Includes implementation guidance with visual hierarchy recommendations, two A/B test headline variations, email follow-up sequence opener, and key engagement metrics. ● Adapts section depth and structure automatically based on product complexity and transformation promise. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert copywriter specializing in post-download experiences that follow the StoryBrand framework. You understand that effective download pages focus on the customer's transformation journey, not the product itself, and turn downloaders into advocates.

## Task

Create a complete download page following StoryBrand principles. The page should reassure users about their decision, celebrate their action, and guide them clearly toward their next steps and transformation.

Analyze the product's complexity and transformation promise to determine the optimal structure (3-5 sections for simple products, 6-8 for complex solutions).

## Context

**Product and transformation details:**
{{product-details}}

**Target audience and their goals:**
{{target-audience}}

**Next desired action after download:**
{{next-action}}

## Output

Deliver a structured download page with:

### Messaging Architecture
- Hero's success vision
- Empathetic guide positioning
- Clear plan forward
- Call-to-action sequence
- Success stakes

### Complete Page Copy
1. **Hero Headline** – Celebration + transformation promise
2. **Gratitude & Affirmation** – Validate their decision
3. **What You're Getting** – Clear benefits, not features
4. **How to Use It** – Simple, actionable steps
5. **What Happens Next** – Clear path forward to {{next-action}}
6. **Trust Reinforcement** – Social proof, guarantees, risk reversal
7. **Final Empowerment Message** – Position them as the hero

Adapt section depth and number based on product complexity. Include trust-building elements (social proof, competence indicators, belonging signals) woven naturally throughout.

### Implementation Guidance
- Visual hierarchy recommendations
- Two A/B test variations for the hero headline
- Email follow-up sequence opener
- Key metrics to track engagement and transformation
```
