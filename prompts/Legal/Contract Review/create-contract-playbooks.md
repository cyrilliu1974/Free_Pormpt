# Contract Playbook Generator for Legal Teams

## 簡介

The Contract Playbook Generator for Legal Teams is a free AI prompt that builds structured, actionable contract playbooks to accelerate review cycles and standardize negotiation positions for in-house counsel and law firms. This contract playbook prompt for ChatGPT takes your contract type and deal parameters and produces a living reference document complete with three-tier risk classifications (GREEN/YELLOW/RED), position matrices showing negotiation ranges, pre-written email templates, red flag detection guides, and a library of approved model clauses ready to deploy. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming institutional knowledge into a fast-lookup tool that junior attorneys can use during live negotiations. Real-world applications include creating playbooks for SaaS agreements, vendor contracts, licensing deals, and international partnerships with tailored strategies for each business context. Reach for this prompt when your legal team needs to scale contract reviews without sacrificing consistency, reduce escalations, or onboard new attorneys with documented negotiation guidance. ● Produces clause-by-clause breakdowns with risk ratings, business impact explanations, and counterparty pushback responses ● Includes decision flowcharts for approval routing based on deal size, risk tier, and deviations from standard terms ● Delivers scenario playbooks for common contexts like high-value enterprise deals, startup vendors, and cross-border agreements ● Incorporates modern contracting standards: plain language principles, ESG provisions, remote work clauses, and AI/data usage terms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert contracts attorney specializing in commercial agreements, translating institutional knowledge into clear, actionable playbooks that accelerate review cycles.

## Task
Create a comprehensive contract playbook for {{contract-type}} that enables faster, more consistent reviews by providing:

- **Three-tier risk classification** (GREEN / YELLOW / RED) for each major clause
- **Position matrices** showing negotiation ranges and fallback positions
- **Negotiation scripts** including email templates and talking points
- **Red flag detection** with concrete examples
- **Approval workflows** tied to risk level and deal parameters
- **Approved language library** with model clauses ready to deploy

## Context
{{deal-parameters}}

Include modern contracting considerations: plain language principles, risk-tiered frameworks, ESG provisions where relevant, remote work clauses, and AI/data usage terms.

## Output
Structure the playbook as a living reference document:

1. **Executive summary** – one-page overview of the contract type, typical issues, and when to escalate
2. **Quick reference card** – risk ratings and approval thresholds at a glance
3. **Clause-by-clause breakdown** – for each standard clause, provide:
   - Risk rating and rationale
   - Business impact in plain English
   - Negotiation position (must-have / prefer / concede-if)
   - Model language with inline annotations
   - Common counterparty pushback and responses
4. **Decision flowcharts** – visual guides for approval routing based on deal size, risk, and deviation from standard
5. **Scenario playbooks** – 3–5 common business contexts (e.g., high-value enterprise deal, startup vendor, international counterparty) with tailored strategies
6. **Appendices** – glossary, stakeholder contact matrix, template repository

Use clear section headers, conversational tone, and practical examples throughout. Organize for fast lookup during live negotiations.
```
