# Business Funding Plan Generator for Strategic Growth

## 簡介

The Business Funding Plan Generator for Strategic Growth is a free AI prompt that creates customized financial roadmaps identifying suitable funding sources, amounts, and realistic timelines for businesses seeking capital. This funding strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - industry, stage, and growth trajectory - then evaluates funding options from bootstrapping and angel investment to venture capital, bank loans, grants, and revenue-based financing. It explains why each source fits your situation, assesses strategic trade-offs, and sequences funding milestones aligned with cash flow needs and growth projections. The output includes detailed reasoning for each recommendation plus a clear markdown table mapping sources to amounts and timelines. Founders preparing pitch decks, finance teams modeling capital raises, and advisors building client funding strategies all use this prompt to translate business goals into actionable financial plans. ● Evaluates multiple funding sources - equity, debt, grants, revenue-based - and matches them to business type, industry, and growth stage ● Provides 2-3 sentence explanations for each recommended source, covering strategic fit and key trade-offs ● Outputs a markdown table with funding source, amount, and timeline columns for clear presentation ● Sequences funding milestones to align with cash flow requirements and growth objectives, creating a path to financial sustainability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial strategist developing comprehensive funding plans for businesses.

## Task
Create a detailed financial roadmap that identifies suitable funding sources, evaluates their strategic fit, and establishes a realistic timeline for securing and deploying capital.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Analyze this business's funding needs by:
1. Identifying funding sources appropriate for this business type, industry, and growth stage
2. Evaluating the strategic advantages and trade-offs of each option
3. Determining realistic funding amounts from each source
4. Sequencing funding milestones aligned with growth projections and cash flow requirements
5. Accounting for industry-specific opportunities or constraints

## Output
For each recommended funding source, provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) covering why it's appropriate and key considerations.

Then present the complete funding plan as a markdown table:

| Funding Source | Amount | Timeline |
|----------------|--------|----------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Ensure the plan creates a clear path to financial sustainability and supports the stated growth objectives.
```
