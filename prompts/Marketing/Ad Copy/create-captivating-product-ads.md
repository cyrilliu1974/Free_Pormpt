# Product Ad Copy Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Product Ad Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive advertising copy designed to capture attention, communicate value, and motivate target audiences to take action. This product ad copy prompt for ChatGPT works by guiding you through a benefit-first approach: it opens with attention-grabbing headlines, emphasizes how your product solves real problems, differentiates your offering from competitors, and closes with clear calls to action. You provide product details and supporting marketing assets (social proof, urgency triggers, brand voice guidelines), and the prompt generates copy suitable for digital ads, social media campaigns, landing pages, and promotional emails. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing professionals, small business owners, and e-commerce teams reach for this prompt when they need persuasive ad text that converts without spending hours on multiple drafts. ● Produces attention-grabbing headlines that signal immediate value or evoke curiosity ● Prioritizes customer benefits over feature lists, showing how the product improves lives or solves problems ● Incorporates urgency, scarcity, and social proof elements to strengthen persuasion ● Delivers platform-ready copy with clear, action-oriented CTAs tailored to audience needs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive advertising.

## Task
Write compelling ad copy that captures attention, communicates value, and drives the target audience to take action. The copy must highlight benefits, differentiate the product from competitors, and include a clear call to action that aligns with the brand voice.

## Context
Product and audience details:
{{product-overview}}

Supporting elements (include any relevant social proof, urgency triggers, or brand voice guidelines):
{{marketing-assets}}

## Approach
1. Open with an attention-grabbing headline that signals immediate value or evokes curiosity
2. Focus on benefits over features—show how the product solves problems or improves the customer's life
3. Emphasize what makes this product unique compared to alternatives
4. Incorporate urgency elements (limited-time offers, scarcity) where appropriate
5. Weave in social proof (testimonials, ratings, endorsements) if available
6. Close with a specific, action-oriented CTA that tells the audience exactly what to do next
7. Match the brand's established voice and tone throughout

## Output
Deliver ready-to-deploy ad copy that is:
- Concise and persuasive
- Tailored to the target audience's needs and preferences
- Suitable for the intended platform and format
- Aligned with brand guidelines and marketing objectives
```
