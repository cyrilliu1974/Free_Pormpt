# SEO Content Plan Generator for Organic Growth

## 簡介

The SEO Content Plan Generator for Organic Growth is a free AI prompt that builds quarterly content calendars aligned to organic search performance for marketers, agencies, and in-house SEO teams. This SEO content planning prompt for ChatGPT takes your industry and business goals as input, then produces a structured 3-month roadmap with two pieces of content per month. It surfaces high-impact topics, assigns target keywords, maps content types to SEO objectives, and includes technical and distribution recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering an industry overview, a tabular monthly schedule, content-type definitions, and actionable supplementary tactics for link-building and amplification. Teams use it to balance keyword targeting with funnel strategy, ensuring every content piece serves a clear role in driving organic acquisition. Marketing directors, SEO managers, and content strategists reach for this prompt when launching quarterly planning cycles or pitching data-backed content roadmaps to stakeholders. ● Produces a six-piece, three-month content calendar with target keywords and SEO objectives for each topic. ● Explains the strategic role of each content type - blog posts, guides, comparison pages - so teams understand format choices. ● Surfaces three supplementary recommendations covering technical SEO, link acquisition, and distribution channels. ● Balances content variety and keyword coverage to support both awareness and conversion goals across the organic funnel. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO content strategist specializing in aligning content plans with organic traffic and conversion goals across industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive 3-month SEO content plan for the specified industry. Deliver a monthly schedule with 2 pieces of content per month, prioritizing high-impact topics that drive organic search performance.

## Context
Industry and business context: {{industry-and-goals}}

The plan should balance content variety, keyword targeting, and strategic SEO objectives. Each piece of content must serve a clear purpose in the organic acquisition funnel.

## Output
Structure your response in four sections:

**1. Industry Overview**
- Industry: [state the industry]
- Key SEO goals: [list 3 primary goals derived from the context]

**2. Content Plan**
Present as a table:

| Month | Topic | Target Keyword | Content Type | SEO Objective |
|-------|-------|----------------|--------------|---------------|
| [populate 6 rows: 2 per month across 3 months] |

**3. Content Type Overview**
- [Content Type 1]: [brief description of format and use case]
- [Content Type 2]: [brief description of format and use case]
- [repeat for each unique type used in the plan]

**4. Supplementary Recommendations**
Provide 3 actionable recommendations to amplify the content plan's SEO impact (e.g., technical optimizations, link-building tactics, distribution strategies).
```
