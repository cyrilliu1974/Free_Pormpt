# Product Advertisement Hero Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Product Advertisement Hero Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that creates commercial-quality beverage photography with studio lighting, natural surroundings, and professional composition for brands and marketers. This product advertisement prompt for Midjourney produces photorealistic hero shots featuring your beverage in its vessel at center frame, complete with condensation droplets, cream foam, sculptured ice, and fresh botanical blossoms. The prompt orchestrates golden hour lighting, soft-focus natural landscapes, rich gradient contrasts, and elegant serif typography placement in the top third. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering cinematic commercial-quality images with rule-of-thirds composition, shallow depth of field (f/2.8), and 85mm lens perspective. Use it to generate scroll-stopping advertisement concepts for social media campaigns, packaging mockups, pitch decks, or e-commerce listings.
