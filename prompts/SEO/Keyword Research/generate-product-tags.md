# Product Tag Generator for E-Commerce SEO

## 簡介

The Product Tag Generator for E-Commerce SEO is a free AI prompt that creates strategic product tags organized by intent to maximize discoverability across Amazon, Etsy, social commerce platforms, and search engines. This product tag prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product description and market context to produce three distinct tag categories: category-based tags that align with platform taxonomies, feature-based tags highlighting specific attributes and specifications, and use-case tags that capture buyer intent and problems solved. It further organizes tags into high-intent purchase keywords, discovery-phase browsing terms, and long-tail niche descriptors. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured output with clear explanations of why each tag set works for your specific product and competitive position. E-commerce sellers, marketplace managers, and product listing specialists use it to ensure compliance with platform guidelines while capturing every relevant search vector - from ready-to-buy queries to early-stage research terms. ● Produces category tags that match platform classification systems for better algorithmic visibility and compliance. ● Generates feature tags based on materials, specifications, and product attributes to capture detail-oriented searches. ● Creates use-case tags aligned with buyer problems, application scenarios, and purchase intent. ● Organizes all tags into high-intent purchase, discovery browsing, and long-tail relevance batches for complete search coverage. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist with deep expertise in e-commerce search optimization across marketplaces, search engines, and social commerce platforms.

## Task
Generate comprehensive, keyword-rich product tags organized by strategic intent to maximize discoverability while staying compliant with platform guidelines.

## Context
Produce three tag categories:
- **Category-based tags**: align with platform taxonomies and classification systems
- **Feature-based tags**: highlight specific product attributes, materials, specifications
- **Use-case-based tags**: capture buyer intent, problems solved, application scenarios

Organize tags into three strategic batches:
- **High-intent purchase**: keywords buyers use when ready to buy
- **Discovery intent**: browsing and exploration terms for early-stage research
- **Long-tail relevance**: niche descriptors and specific queries

Account for:
{{product-description}} (product name, category, key features, target audience, price point)
{{market-context}} (competitive positioning, platform(s) where sold, any unique differentiators)

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings for each tag category and intent batch. Present tags as bulleted lists with brief explanations of why each tag set maximizes discoverability and conversion potential for this specific product and market position.
```
