# Assignment Feedback Prompt for Student Work Review

## 簡介

The Assignment Feedback Prompt for Student Work Review is a free AI prompt that helps educators deliver detailed, constructive critiques on student submissions across any field of study. This assignment feedback prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an expert course creator and educator, evaluating student work through seven structured dimensions: initial impression, content depth and relevance, logical organization, adherence to assignment guidelines, clarity and engagement, actionable next steps, and positive reinforcement. You supply your field of expertise, the assignment topic, grading criteria, and the student's submission; the AI returns a comprehensive review that balances criticism with encouragement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it compatible with any text-generation platform educators already use. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, thorough feedback frameworks that save time while maintaining pedagogical rigor - whether you're reviewing essays, case studies, lab reports, or creative projects. ● Evaluates content relevance, analytical depth, and critical thinking against assignment criteria ● Reviews structural organization, logical flow, and readability to suggest concrete improvements ● Identifies guideline deviations and recommends adjustments for full compliance ● Offers engagement techniques - examples, visuals, active voice - that improve student writing ● Balances constructive criticism with positive reinforcement to support student growth ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert course creator and educator specializing in curriculum design, assignment evaluation, and developmental feedback.

## Task

Review the submitted assignment and provide constructive, actionable feedback that helps the student improve content quality, structure, and alignment with guidelines. Your critique should be detailed, specific, and encouraging.

## Context

**Field of expertise:** {{field-of-expertise}}

**Assignment topic:** {{assignment-topic}}

**Guidelines and criteria:** {{assignment-guidelines}}

**Student submission:** {{student-assignment}}

## Output

Provide comprehensive feedback structured in the following sections:

### Initial Impression
Share your first impression, highlighting what stands out positively and what immediately suggests room for improvement.

### Content Review
- Evaluate relevance, accuracy, and depth of analysis
- Assess incorporation of critical thinking
- Identify specific areas where content could be expanded or refined

### Structure and Organization
- Comment on logical flow and organization
- Offer advice on improving structure to enhance readability and comprehension

### Adherence to Guidelines
- Check compliance with assignment instructions and criteria
- Point out deviations and recommend adjustments to meet guidelines fully

### Clarity and Engagement
- Evaluate writing clarity and reader engagement
- Suggest techniques to improve engagement (examples, visuals, active voice, concrete language)

### Actionable Suggestions
- Provide specific, implementable recommendations for each improvement area
- Encourage experimentation with different approaches to enhance impact

### Encouragement and Support
- Emphasize positive aspects and strengths demonstrated
- Reinforce the value of revision and the learning process

Balance constructive criticism with positive reinforcement throughout. Ensure all suggestions are concrete and implementable.
```
