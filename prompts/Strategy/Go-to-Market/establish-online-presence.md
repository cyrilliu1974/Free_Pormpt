# Online Presence Strategy Builder for Self-Employed

## 簡介

The Online Presence Strategy Builder for Self-Employed is a free AI prompt that creates a customized digital marketing roadmap for solo practitioners and independent professionals. This online presence strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed markdown table covering at least five platforms, each with tailored content strategies, posting schedules, and engagement tactics. You provide your professional profile (skills, services, target audience) and current state (existing platforms, follower counts, growth objectives), and the prompt returns a prioritized plan designed for execution within 5-10 hours per week. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is particularly effective for consultants, freelancers, coaches, and service providers who need to market themselves while delivering client work. Each platform recommendation includes a quick-win tactic for immediate implementation. ● Justifies each platform choice based on audience fit and ROI potential ● Specifies content types, themes, and frequencies that establish expertise without overwhelming limited time ● Provides concrete engagement tactics that convert visibility into client relationships ● Balances quick wins with sustainable long-term growth strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in online presence for self-employed professionals.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive online presence strategy that maximizes visibility, attracts clients, and establishes authority. The plan must account for the constraints self-employed professionals face: limited budget, time scarcity, and the need to handle marketing alongside service delivery.

## Context
**Professional profile:**  
{{professional-profile}}  
_(Include: your profession, primary skills/services offered, target audience demographics and pain points)_

**Current state and goals:**  
{{current-presence-and-goals}}  
_(Describe: existing online presence (platforms, follower counts, engagement levels), specific objectives for growth (client acquisition targets, authority milestones, revenue goals))_

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Platform | Content Strategy | Engagement Tactics |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------|

Cover at least 5 platforms relevant to the professional's field. For each platform:

- **Platform:** Name the channel and justify its fit for this audience
- **Content Strategy:** Specify content types, posting frequency, themes that showcase expertise and address audience needs
- **Engagement Tactics:** Provide specific actions to build relationships, convert followers to clients, and sustain momentum

Prioritize strategies that deliver ROI quickly and can be executed within 5-10 hours per week. Include one quick-win tactic per platform that can be implemented immediately.
```
