# Demand Letter Template Generator for Unpaid Debts

## 簡介

The Demand Letter Template Generator for Unpaid Debts is a free AI prompt that creates formal demand letters for creditors seeking payment of outstanding debts or fulfillment of legal obligations. This demand letter prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete business letter formatted on creditor letterhead, covering all essential elements: party identification, detailed debt specifications, legal basis with citations, required actions with deadlines, and consequences of non-compliance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming your party details, debt information, and payment deadline into a professionally structured letter that references previous collection attempts, cites relevant laws or contractual provisions, and outlines potential legal action including interest, legal fees, and collection costs. The tone remains firm yet professional, focused on objective facts rather than emotional language. This prompt is designed for legal professionals, business owners, accounts receivable teams, and collection agencies who need to formalize payment demands before initiating litigation. ● Produces complete demand letters with introduction, debt details, legal basis, required actions, consequences, and professional closing ● Incorporates custom party details, debt amounts, outstanding periods, and payment deadlines into a formal business letter format ● Cites legal grounds for the claim and warns of litigation, additional interest, and collection costs for non-payment ● Maintains a firm but professional tone that avoids personal attacks while asserting the creditor's legal position ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal expert specializing in demand letters for unpaid debts and unfulfilled obligations. Draft a formal, legally sound demand letter that clearly communicates the creditor's position and encourages prompt resolution.

## Task
Create a demand letter that includes:

**1. Introduction**
- State the purpose as a formal demand for payment
- Identify creditor and debtor

**2. Debt Details**
- Specify total amount due and basis for the debt
- Note how long the amount has been outstanding
- Reference previous collection attempts

**3. Legal Basis**
- Explain how non-payment breaches legal obligations
- Cite relevant laws, regulations, or contractual provisions
- Assert the creditor's right to legal action

**4. Required Actions**
- Demand full payment by the specified deadline
- Provide payment methods and instructions
- Request written confirmation of payment

**5. Consequences**
- Warn of potential legal consequences for non-compliance
- Mention the right to file a lawsuit
- Highlight additional costs (interest, legal fees, collection expenses)

**6. Conclusion**
- Encourage prompt resolution
- Provide contact information for questions or payment plans
- Close with a professional, firm tone

## Context
{{party-details}}

{{debt-information}}

{{deadline}}

## Output
Format as a formal business letter on creditor letterhead:

[CREDITOR LETTERHEAD]
[CREDITOR ADDRESS]
[DATE]

[DEBTOR NAME]
[DEBTOR ADDRESS]

Re: Demand for Payment of Outstanding [Debt/Obligation]

Dear [Debtor],

[Body following the structure above]

Sincerely,

[Legal Representative Name]
[Law Firm Name]

Maintain a firm but professional tone throughout. Avoid emotional language or personal attacks. Focus on objective facts and legal requirements.
```
