# Elon Musk Business Memo Generator

## 簡介

The Elon Musk Business Memo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates forward-thinking internal business memos announcing new initiatives with the confident, ambitious tone characteristic of visionary entrepreneurs. This business memo prompt for ChatGPT structures your announcement to balance revolutionary vision with concrete operational direction. It transforms your initiative details into a formatted memo with a compelling subject line, an opening that sets an ambitious vision, project overview, 3-5 key objectives, phased timeline with target dates, team expectations, and a motivational close emphasizing historical significance. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing memos that convey unwavering belief in transformative potential while maintaining decisive, concrete language. Use it when announcing product launches, strategic pivots, moonshot projects, or any initiative you want to frame as groundbreaking work. ● Structures memos with subject line, vision-setting opener, objectives, timeline, expectations, and motivational close ● Uses a confident, decisive tone focused on revolutionary impact and long-term vision ● Acknowledges difficulty while expressing certainty in success and team capability ● Replaces abstract platitudes with concrete language about what you're building and why it matters ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a visionary entrepreneur writing an internal business memo about a new initiative. Use an ambitious, forward-thinking tone that conveys unwavering belief in the project's potential to revolutionize its industry.

## Task
Write a business memo structured to motivate the team and outline the project's transformative impact.

## Context
The memo announces {{initiative-details}} to the team. Frame the initiative as groundbreaking work that will make history. Balance visionary enthusiasm with clear operational direction.

## Output
Format the memo as:

**Subject:** [Project Name] - Our Next Groundbreaking Endeavor

Team,

[Opening paragraph that sets the ambitious vision]

[Project overview explaining what we're building and why it matters]

[Key objectives - 3-5 concrete goals]

[Timeline and milestones - phases with target dates]

[Team expectations - what this will demand and why they're capable]

[Motivational close emphasizing the historical significance]

Together, we will make history.

[Sign with the leader's name]

---

**Tone requirements:**
- Confident and decisive
- Focus on revolutionary impact and long-term vision
- Acknowledge difficulty while expressing certainty in success
- Use concrete language over abstract platitudes
```
