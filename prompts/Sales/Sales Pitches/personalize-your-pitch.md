# Personalized Sales Pitch Generator

## 簡介

The Personalized Sales Pitch Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored sales messaging for copywriters, marketers, and sales professionals. This sales pitch prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar principles to build logically structured narratives that flow from problem recognition through solution presentation to call-to-action. You provide your product or service details, target audience, their pain points, your unique selling points, and desired outcome - the prompt then generates a complete pitch organized into introduction, problem statement, solution, benefits, and call-to-action sections. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing persuasive copy that balances emotional resonance with clarity. Use it when you need to create customized pitches for specific audience segments, launch campaigns, or train sales teams on effective messaging frameworks. ● Connects product features directly to audience pain points through logical narrative flow ● Structures pitches into five clear sections that guide prospects from problem awareness to action ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure each sentence builds coherently on the previous ● Balances persuasive techniques with empathy and clarity for authentic audience connection ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter and marketing strategist specializing in persuasive sales messaging.

## Task
Craft a compelling, personalized sales pitch that connects {{product-or-service}} to {{target-audience}} by addressing their specific needs and demonstrating clear value.

## Context
Use dependency grammar principles to structure your writing: each sentence should build logically on the previous one, creating coherent relationships between problem, solution, features, benefits, and action. Analyze how the audience's challenges connect to the product's strengths, then construct a narrative flow that guides the reader from recognition of their pain point to the desired resolution.

{{audience-pain-points}}

{{unique-selling-points}}

{{desired-outcome}}

## Output
Deliver a structured sales pitch organized into clearly flowing paragraphs:

- **Introduction** – establish relevance and capture attention
- **Problem statement** – articulate the audience's pain points with empathy
- **Solution** – introduce the product/service as the answer
- **Benefits** – connect features to tangible outcomes for the audience
- **Call-to-action** – guide toward the desired outcome

Ensure logical progression between sections, using persuasive language and emotional resonance while maintaining clarity throughout.
```
