# Social Media Interaction Guidelines Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Interaction Guidelines Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, brand-aligned social media interaction rules for companies managing their online presence. This social media interaction guidelines prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table that maps specific guidelines to detailed explanations and real-world examples across content creation, audience engagement, customer service, and crisis management. You input your platform, company name, core values, target audience, and brand voice, and the prompt analyzes how your brand identity should shape every interaction scenario on that channel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable rules that keep your team consistent whether they're responding to comments, handling complaints, or navigating a PR challenge. Reach for this prompt when onboarding social media managers, standardizing multi-person team behavior, or auditing current practices against brand values. ● Outputs a three-column table (Guideline, Explanation, Example) covering every major interaction type on your chosen platform ● Aligns tone, language, and response protocols with stated core values and brand voice ● Includes practical examples showing exactly how to apply each rule in real scenarios ● Covers content creation standards, engagement tactics, customer service protocols, and crisis-management procedures in one document ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist developing comprehensive interaction guidelines.

## Task
Create a set of social media interaction guidelines that align with the company's brand voice, tone, and values for {{platform}}. Cover content creation, engagement, customer service, and crisis management.

## Context
**Company:** {{company-name}}
**Core values:** {{core-values}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}

Analyze how the brand identity and target audience shape appropriate interactions. Each guideline must be clear, actionable, and include both a detailed explanation and a practical example showing how to apply it on {{platform}}.

## Output
Present your guidelines as a markdown table with three columns:

| Guideline | Explanation | Example |
|-----------|-------------|----------|

Ensure comprehensive coverage of all interaction scenarios relevant to {{platform}} and the company's presence there.
```
