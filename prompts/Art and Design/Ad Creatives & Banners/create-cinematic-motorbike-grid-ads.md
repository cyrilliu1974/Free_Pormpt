# Cinematic Motorbike Grid Ad Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Motorbike Grid Ad Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a six-panel 3×2 advertisement layout showcasing performance motorcycles through hero studio shots, engineering close-ups, action scenes, rider lifestyle portraits, and journey imagery for brands and marketers. This motorbike ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates a complete visual campaign in a single generation: a studio three-quarter hero shot with dramatic lighting on tank and chrome, a macro engine detail highlighting mechanical craftsmanship, a motion-blur highway scene conveying speed, a golden-hour rider portrait for aspirational branding, a sharp handlebar cockpit close-up showing digital controls, and a wide coastal-road dusk shot evoking freedom and endurance. Each panel is composed with cinematic automotive photography techniques - balanced exposure, strong highlights on metal, defined shadows, and high realism - so the output works immediately as social media content, website hero sections, or digital ad creatives without post-production. Motorcycle brands, digital marketers, and creative agencies reach for this prompt when they need cohesive campaign assets that tell a complete story of power, precision, and lifestyle in one grid. ● Produces six distinct compositions in one 3×2 grid: studio hero, engine macro, highway motion, rider lifestyle, cockpit detail, and open-road journey. ● Applies cinematic automotive photography lighting with directional studio setups, shallow depth of field for detail shots, natural golden-hour tones, and motion-blur dynamics. ● Outputs high-realism imagery with correct bike geometry, metallic finishes, chrome accents, and modern performance branding aesthetics - no toy proportions or CGI cartoon styles. ● Accepts campaign-details variable to tailor bike model, color palette, rider gear, or environment to match specific brand identity and marketing goals. ## Prompt
