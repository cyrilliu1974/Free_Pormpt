# Product Photography Style Generator

## 簡介

The Product Photography Style Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-resolution, studio-lit product images with clean backgrounds and sharp detail for e-commerce sellers, marketers, and designers. This product photography prompt for Midjourney produces commercial-grade shots with customizable photography styles, soft shadow rendering, and neutral backdrops that put full focus on your product. You specify the product and the desired photography style - minimalist, lifestyle, dramatic, vintage, or any aesthetic - and the prompt renders a professional studio setup with balanced highlights, sharp focus, and a clean presentation ready for catalogs, online stores, or ad campaigns. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar diffusion models that respect aspect-ratio and style-weight parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need catalog-ready product shots without a physical studio or when you want to test multiple visual styles before a shoot. ● Specify any product and photography style to generate clean, professional studio shots with soft lighting and sharp detail. ● Outputs square 1:1 aspect ratio images optimized for product catalogs, online marketplaces, and social media. ● High style-weight parameter (750) ensures strong adherence to the chosen aesthetic while maintaining product clarity. ● Neutral background and focused composition eliminate distractions and highlight product features. ## Prompt

```
Professional product photography of {{product}}, {{photography-style}}, studio lighting setup with soft shadows and highlights, high resolution commercial quality, clean neutral background, sharp focus on product details --s 750 --ar 1:1
```
