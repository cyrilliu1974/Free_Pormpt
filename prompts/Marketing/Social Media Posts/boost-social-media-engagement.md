# Social Media Engagement Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Engagement Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates platform-specific tactics to turn passive followers into an active, participating community for social media managers, brand builders, and content creators. This social media engagement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your current situation and audience psychology to deliver conversation starters, comment-prompt techniques, storytelling frameworks, and community rituals designed to break through algorithmic suppression and visibility crises. It addresses the shift from one-way broadcasting to authentic dialogue by providing hooks that lower the barrier to commenting, response protocols that spark comment threads, and recurring formats that train consistent interaction. Use cases include reviving stagnant accounts, building brand advocates from lurkers, and designing content calendars that prioritize genuine engagement over vanity metrics. Reach for this prompt when your posts get views but little interaction, when you need platform-specific tactics for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or Twitter/X, or when you want to transform followers into a self-sustaining community. ● Platform-specific conversation starters and poll structures tailored to your niche and audience demographics ● Storytelling frameworks like cliffhangers, shared-struggle narratives, and before/after formats that invite audience participation ● Comment response protocols and community rituals that turn one-time commenters into consistent contributors and brand champions ● Phased 4-week implementation timeline with quick wins and sustainable engagement habits ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in community building, engagement mechanics, and audience psychology.

## Task
Create a comprehensive engagement and community-building strategy that transforms passive followers into active participants and brand advocates. Address algorithmic suppression, audience fatigue, and the shift from broadcasting to authentic dialogue.

## Context
Modern social platforms punish one-way content and reward genuine interaction. Audiences scroll past performative posts but engage deeply with content that invites participation, shared experiences, and emotional resonance. The user needs platform-specific tactics to break through the visibility crisis and build a community that consistently interacts.

**Platform(s):** {{platforms}}
**Niche:** {{niche}}
**Target audience:** {{audience}}
**Current situation:** {{current-engagement-and-style}}

## Analysis & Strategy
Analyze the provided context to identify engagement friction points and audience psychology barriers. Then develop:

### Conversation Starters
Platform-specific hooks that lower the barrier to commenting and create genuine dialogue opportunities. Include question formats, poll structures, and open-ended prompts tailored to the niche and audience.

### Comment Prompt Strategies
Organic techniques that invite responses without feeling manipulative—storytelling gaps, relatable dilemmas, opinion requests, and experience-sharing invitations.

### Storytelling Techniques
Frameworks that naturally encourage audience participation: cliffhangers, before/after narratives, challenge stories, and shared-struggle formats that resonate emotionally.

### Community-Building Tactics
- Recurring engagement formats and community rituals that train consistent interaction
- Response protocols that amplify momentum (how to reply to comments to spark threads)
- Strategies for transforming lurkers into contributors and contributors into champions
- Content hooks that trigger shares and saves

### Implementation Timeline
A phased rollout plan with specific actions for weeks 1-4, prioritizing quick wins and sustainable habits.

## Output
Structure your response with the five section headings above. Provide specific examples and actionable bullet points for immediate implementation. Tailor all tactics to the specified platforms, niche, and audience.
```
