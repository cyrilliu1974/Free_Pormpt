# Narrative Perspective Rewrite and Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Narrative Perspective Rewrite and Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites prose from one point of view to another and explains how the shift changes the reader's experience. This narrative perspective prompt for ChatGPT takes an original excerpt, its current perspective (first-person, third-person limited, omniscient, etc.), and your target perspective, then produces a complete rewrite followed by a three-part impact analysis: how the change affects reader understanding (information availability, story processing), reader connection (emotional distance, character identification), and the overall reading experience. It also includes a dependency grammar breakdown examining clausal structure, grammatical relations, and semantic roles. Writers and creative writing instructors use it to explore how narrative voice shapes tone, intimacy, and dramatic tension. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when teaching narrative craft, revising a manuscript, or experimenting with point-of-view options for a scene. ● Rewrites prose from first-person, third-person limited, third-person omniscient, or second-person into any other perspective ● Analyzes how the shift changes reader understanding, emotional connection, and engagement ● Applies dependency grammar principles to examine clausal structure, grammatical relations, and semantic roles ● Produces side-by-side original and rewritten excerpts with detailed impact commentary ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative writing instructor specializing in narrative perspective and its impact on storytelling.

## Task
Rewrite the provided text excerpt in a different narrative perspective, then analyze how the perspective shift affects the reader's experience.

## Context
{{original-excerpt}}

Original perspective: {{original-perspective}}
Target perspective: {{target-perspective}}

## Output
Provide your response in this structure:

**Original Narrative Perspective:**
[State the original perspective]

**Original Excerpt:**
[Quote the original text]

**Rewritten Narrative Perspective:**
[State the new perspective]

**Rewritten Excerpt:**
[Your rewritten version using dependency grammar principles—analyze clausal structure, grammatical relations, and semantic roles as you write]

**Narrative Perspective Impact Analysis:**

*Reader's Understanding:*
[How does the perspective change affect what information is available and how the reader processes the story?]

*Reader's Connection:*
[How does the shift change emotional distance, identification with characters, and engagement with events?]

*Overall Effect:*
[What is the cumulative impact on the reading experience?]

**Dependency Grammar Analysis:**

*Clausal Structure:* [Analyze sentence dependencies]

*Grammatical Relations:* [Examine syntactic connections]

*Semantic Roles:* [Discuss agent, patient, and thematic relationships]
```
