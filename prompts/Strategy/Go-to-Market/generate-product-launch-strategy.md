# Product Launch Strategy and Go-to-Market Kit Generator

## 簡介

The Product Launch Strategy and Go-to-Market Kit Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a coordinated multi-channel launch kit for product marketers and founders. This product launch prompt for ChatGPT takes a single launch brief and produces nine ready-to-use marketing assets: two press releases, two email templates, three platform-specific social media posts, and two blog post drafts. All materials share a unified message, tone, and strategic focus tied to your target market, value proposition, and launch goals. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for any text-generation workflow. Teams use it to kick off SaaS releases, physical product drops, feature announcements, and rebranding campaigns without hiring an agency or coordinating multiple freelancers. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive launch narrative across email, press, social, and content channels but lack the time or budget to brief each asset separately. ● Produces two press releases with newsworthy headlines, product details, benefits, and contact information formatted for media distribution. ● Generates two email templates with personalized subject lines, clear calls-to-action, and persuasive product pitches ready for outreach or nurture sequences. ● Creates three social media posts tailored to specific platforms, complete with hashtags, platform-appropriate copy, and media recommendations. ● Drafts two SEO-conscious blog posts that educate readers while subtly integrating the product, complete with titles, body sections, and conversion-focused conclusions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a product launch strategist building a complete go-to-market kit across multiple channels.

## Task
Create a unified product launch kit containing:
- 2 press releases
- 2 email templates
- 3 social media posts (specify platform for each)
- 2 blog post drafts

All materials must align with a single cohesive strategy and support the launch goals.

## Context
{{launch-brief}}

Include:
- Business type and product offering
- Target market characteristics
- Launch goals and success metrics
- Unique value proposition

## Guidelines
- Lead with benefits and solutions, not just features
- Use accessible language; avoid jargon
- Press releases: newsworthy, highlight key differentiators
- Email templates: personalized subject lines, clear calls-to-action
- Social posts: platform-appropriate, include hashtags and media recommendations
- Blog posts: SEO-conscious, educational, with subtle product integration
- Maintain consistent messaging and tone across all assets

## Output
Deliver the complete kit organized as:

### Business Overview
- Business type
- Product offering
- Target market
- Launch goals

### Press Releases (2)
For each:
- Headline
- Subheadline
- Intro paragraph
- Product details
- Key benefits
- Call to action
- Contact information

### Email Templates (2)
For each:
- Subject line
- Greeting
- Introduction
- Product pitch
- Benefits
- Call to action
- Sign-off

### Social Media Posts (3)
For each:
- Platform
- Post copy
- Hashtags
- Media type recommendation

### Blog Posts (2)
For each:
- Title
- Introduction
- Body sections
- Conclusion
- Call to action

Use clean markdown formatting with headings and bullet points.
```
