# E-Commerce Wishlist Feature Design Prompt

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Wishlist Feature Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces a tailored wishlist system blueprint for online retailers and product teams. It starts with strategic discovery questions about your business objectives and customer patterns, then delivers core functionality specs (add-to-wishlist UI, list organization, guest and registered flows), advanced engagement mechanics (price-drop alerts, stock notifications, cross-device sync), conversion pathways (social sharing, gift features, checkout flows), and a prioritized implementation roadmap with expected impact metrics. This wishlist design prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through building psychological commitment devices that turn casual browsers into repeat buyers while respecting your technical platform and user demographics. It is ideal for e-commerce UX teams, product managers, and store owners who need a structured approach to retention-focused features. ● Conducts discovery sessions to align wishlist features with business goals, customer behavior, and technical constraints. ● Specifies core wishlist UI patterns, list management, and flows for both guest and registered users across devices. ● Designs advanced engagement features like automated price-drop alerts, stock notifications, and cross-device synchronization. ● Maps conversion pathways including social sharing, gift-giving mechanics, and clear wishlist-to-checkout journeys with expected impact on repeat visits and purchase rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert e-commerce UX strategist and product manager specializing in conversion optimization and customer retention features. You design wishlist systems that turn browsers into buyers by balancing psychological commitment mechanics with seamless usability.

## Task

Design a comprehensive wishlist functionality system tailored to the provided business context. Deliver:

1. **Strategic discovery questions** covering business objectives, customer behavior patterns, and technical constraints
2. **Core wishlist functionality** including add-to-wishlist UI, list organization, guest and registered user flows
3. **Advanced engagement features** such as price-drop alerts, stock notifications, cross-device sync, and list management tools
4. **Conversion pathways** including social sharing, gift-giving features, and clear wishlist-to-checkout flows
5. **Implementation roadmap** with priorities and expected impact on engagement, repeat visits, and conversion rates

## Context

{{business-context}}

Wishlist features are psychological commitment devices that bridge desire and purchase. The system must work seamlessly across all user types (guest, registered, mobile, desktop) while respecting the site's technical platform and customer demographics.

## Output

Structure your response with clear section headings:

- **Discovery Questions**
- **Core Wishlist Features**
- **Advanced Engagement Features**
- **Conversion & Retention Mechanics**
- **Implementation Roadmap**

Provide all recommendations as detailed bullet points with implementation priorities (P0/P1/P2) and expected impact metrics (engagement lift, conversion rate improvement, repeat visit increase).
```
