# Peer Teaching Strategy Builder for Any Subject

## 簡介

The Peer Teaching Strategy Builder for Any Subject is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive peer teaching frameworks for educators across all disciplines. This peer teaching prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a complete strategy including learning objectives, participant role definitions, explanation guidelines, questioning techniques, feedback principles, and a time-structured session template. Educators input their subject area and receive a ready-to-implement plan grounded in learning science principles. Use it to design collaborative learning sessions where students take turns teaching one another, reinforcing their own understanding while helping peers master new material. This prompt is for K-12 teachers, university instructors, corporate trainers, and curriculum designers who want to incorporate active learning and peer-to-peer instruction into their lessons. ● Defines clear peer teacher and peer learner roles with specific responsibilities for each session phase ● Provides evidence-based explanation guidelines and questioning techniques that promote deep understanding ● Includes a structured session template with time blocks mapping activities for both participants ● Offers post-session reflection prompts to help students metacognitively assess what they learned and taught ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a learning scientist specializing in peer teaching strategies grounded in evidence-based learning principles.

## Task
Create a comprehensive peer teaching strategy for {{subject}} that promotes active engagement and deep understanding. The strategy must be detailed, adaptable, and practical to implement.

## Output
Deliver a structured peer teaching strategy in the following format:

**Subject:** {{subject}}

**Peer Teaching Strategy Overview:**
- Key learning objectives:
  1. [Objective 1]
  2. [Objective 2]
  3. [Objective 3]
- Session duration: [Duration in minutes]
- Participant roles:
  - Peer Teacher: [Role description]
  - Peer Learner: [Role description]

**Explanation Guidelines:**
1. [Guideline 1 - detailed]
2. [Guideline 2 - detailed]
3. [Guideline 3 - detailed]

**Questioning Techniques:**
1. [Technique 1]: [Brief description]
2. [Technique 2]: [Brief description]
3. [Technique 3]: [Brief description]

**Constructive Feedback Principles:**
1. [Principle 1]
2. [Principle 2]
3. [Principle 3]

**Peer Teaching Session Template:**

| Time | Peer Teacher Role | Peer Learner Role |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| [Time block 1] | [Activity description] | [Activity description] |
| [Time block 2] | [Activity description] | [Activity description] |
| [Time block 3] | [Activity description] | [Activity description] |

**Post-Session Reflection Prompts:**
1. [Reflection prompt 1]
2. [Reflection prompt 2]
3. [Reflection prompt 3]
```
