# Study Checklist Plan Generator for Exams and Courses

## 簡介

The Study Checklist Plan Generator for Exams and Courses is a free AI prompt that transforms course descriptions into organized, actionable study plans for students and educators. This study checklist prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes exam or course details and produces a complete study framework: a list of topics to master, key skills to develop, recommended study resources with descriptions, and a progress-tracking table with status checkboxes. Students use it to map out semester courses, certification exams, or self-paced learning programs; teachers use it to scaffold curricula and guide student preparation. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to break down complex subject matter into clear learning objectives and trackable study milestones. ● Outputs three organized sections: topics to master, essential skills, and curated study resources ● Includes a visual progress-tracking table with Not Started, In Progress, and Mastered checkboxes ● Adds notes and resource links for each topic or skill row to centralize study materials ● Works for any subject level, from high school courses to professional certifications ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert study guide creator who breaks down courses into clear, actionable study plans.

## Task
Create a comprehensive yet concise study checklist for the exam or course described below. Identify key topics, essential skills to develop, and the most valuable study resources. Organize everything in an easy-to-follow format with visual indicators for tracking progress.

## Context
Exam or course: {{exam-or-course-details}}

## Output
Deliver the study checklist in this structure:

**Study Checklist for [Exam/Course Name]**

📚 **Topics to Master:**
1. [Topic 1]
2. [Topic 2]
3. [Topic 3]
...

🎯 **Key Skills to Develop:**
- [Skill 1]
- [Skill 2]
- [Skill 3]
...

📖 **Recommended Study Resources:**
• [Resource 1] - [Brief description]
• [Resource 2] - [Brief description]
• [Resource 3] - [Brief description]
...

| Topic/Skill | Status | Notes/Resources |
|-------------|--------|------------------|
| [Topic/Skill 1] | ⬜ Not Started<br>⬜ In Progress<br>✅ Mastered | • [Notes/Resources for Topic/Skill 1] |
| [Topic/Skill 2] | ⬜ Not Started<br>⬜ In Progress<br>✅ Mastered | • [Notes/Resources for Topic/Skill 2] |
| [Topic/Skill 3] | ⬜ Not Started<br>⬜ In Progress<br>✅ Mastered | • [Notes/Resources for Topic/Skill 3] |
...
```
