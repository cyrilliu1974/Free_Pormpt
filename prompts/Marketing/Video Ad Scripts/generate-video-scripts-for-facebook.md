# Video Script Generator for Facebook Ads

## 簡介

The Video Script Generator for Facebook Ads is a free AI prompt that creates structured, conversion-optimized video scripts for Facebook marketing campaigns. It analyzes your product, target audience, and brand voice to produce scripts with attention-grabbing hooks, clear value propositions, and strong calls-to-action. This Facebook video script prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft scripts following platform-specific best practices - analyzing Facebook's content preferences, typical video length conventions, and pacing requirements. It structures each script with a 3-second hook, contextual introduction, core benefit statement, supporting key points, and actionable CTA, plus optional visual and transition notes for production teams. Works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok for text-based script generation. Marketing teams, social media managers, and content creators use it to produce ad scripts that align brand messaging with platform dynamics and audience expectations. ● Produces scripts with platform-optimized hooks designed for Facebook's autoplay and mobile-first viewing environment ● Builds narratives using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and viewer comprehension ● Incorporates brand voice and unique selling points into every script element, from introduction to CTA ● Outputs production-ready structure with visual cues and pacing notes that streamline handoff to video editors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video script writer specializing in social media content that drives conversions.

## Task
Write a compelling video script optimized for {{platform}} that promotes the product or service and converts viewers into customers.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-or-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Brand Voice & Key Selling Points:** {{brand-voice-and-usp}}

Analyze the platform's content preferences and typical video length. Develop an attention-grabbing hook tailored to the platform's format. Craft a clear value proposition and build the narrative with strong coherence. Optimize pacing for the platform's conventions (e.g., TikTok favors fast cuts in under 60 seconds; YouTube allows longer storytelling). Include visual cues and transitions where they enhance flow. End with a strong call-to-action that encourages engagement or purchase.

## Output
Deliver the script in this structure:

**Hook** (first 3 seconds)
[Attention-grabbing opening line or question]

**Introduction** (establishing context)
[Brief setup that resonates with the target audience]

**Value Proposition** (core benefit)
[Clear statement of what the product/service does and why it matters]

**Key Points** (supporting details)
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

**Call-to-Action**
[Specific instruction for what the viewer should do next]

**Visual/Transition Notes**
[Optional: key visual cues or pacing notes for production]
```
