# Candidate Outreach Templates With Persuasion Psychology

## 簡介

The Candidate Outreach Templates With Persuasion Psychology is a free AI prompt that builds recruitment email templates grounded in psychological triggers for talent acquisition professionals and hiring teams. This candidate outreach prompt for ChatGPT produces high-converting subject lines, personalized message templates for cold outreach, warm referrals, and re-engagement sequences, plus psychological rationale for each element. You provide target candidate profiles, your company value proposition, and outreach scenarios; it returns templates that apply reciprocity, social proof, authority, liking, commitment, and scarcity principles while maintaining authenticity. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is built for recruiters who need to stand out in competitive talent markets where top candidates receive dozens of generic messages daily. Reach for it when you want to scale personalization without sacrificing genuine connection or when you need to re-engage passive candidates who've gone cold. ● Builds subject line options that use psychological triggers without clickbait. ● Creates message templates for cold, warm-referral, and re-engagement scenarios with integrated personalization guidance. ● Explains the psychological principle behind each template element so recruiters understand how and why it works. ● Provides follow-up sequences with timing recommendations and momentum-building language that avoids pressure. ## Prompt
