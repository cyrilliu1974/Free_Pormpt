# Trade Journal Entry Template Generator

## 簡介

The Trade Journal Entry Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive trade journaling system for active traders seeking disciplined record-keeping and performance analysis. This trade journal prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete framework for documenting every trade with essential details: entry and exit times, prices, position sizing, stop-loss and take-profit levels, strategy rationale, and profit/loss outcomes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific trading style - whether day trading, swing trading, or scalping - and generating structured guidelines that encourage consistent documentation, bias-free analysis, and pattern recognition across market conditions. Traders use it to create journals that separate objective facts from emotional decision-making, enabling honest post-trade reviews that prevent rationalization and identify recurring mistakes. This prompt is ideal for traders committed to continuous improvement who need a systematic approach to analyzing their performance, identifying profitable setups, and eliminating costly behavioral patterns. ● Creates core trade record sections including date/time stamps, trade direction, instrument, prices, position sizing, risk levels, strategy rationale, and outcomes ● Provides journaling guidelines that enforce consistency, mitigate bias by recording plan criteria before execution, and separate facts from emotional interpretation ● Builds a performance analysis framework tracking win rate, risk-reward ratios, average profit/loss, maximum drawdown, and equity curve trajectory ● Includes weekly, monthly, and quarterly review protocols for identifying patterns, quantifying the impact of impulsive trades, and refining strategy parameters ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced day trader specializing in meticulous record-keeping and performance analysis.

## Task
Create a comprehensive trade journal format that captures essential trade details and serves as a tool for ongoing improvement in trading strategies and decision-making.

## Context
The journal must support {{trading-style}} and accommodate the specific instruments and strategies the trader uses. It should balance thoroughness with usability, enabling pattern recognition and bias-free analysis over time.

## Output
Deliver a structured trade journal format with:

**Core Trade Record Sections:**
- Date and time (entry and exit)
- Trade direction (long/short)
- Instrument traded
- Entry and exit prices
- Trade size (position sizing)
- Risk and target levels (stop-loss, take-profit)
- Strategy rationale (setup, trigger, thesis)
- Profit/loss outcome
- Observations and execution notes

**Journaling Guidelines:**
- **Consistency:** Update immediately after each trade closes to capture authentic decision-making context
- **Bias mitigation:** Record planned entry/exit criteria *before* trade execution; note any deviations from the plan without justification
- **Post-trade rationalization prevention:** Separate objective facts (prices, times, outcomes) from subjective interpretation; flag emotional states that influenced decisions

**Performance Analysis Framework:**
- Win rate percentage
- Risk-reward ratio (average and per setup type)
- Average profit/loss per trade
- Maximum drawdown
- Overall profitability and equity curve trajectory

**Review Protocol:**
- Weekly: Identify recurring patterns in winning and losing trades
- Monthly: Assess deviations from trading plan; quantify impact of emotional or impulsive trades
- Quarterly: Evaluate strategy effectiveness across market conditions; adjust risk parameters and position sizing rules

**Evolution Principles:**
Treat the journal as a living document. As market conditions shift and your skill develops, refine entry criteria, add context fields relevant to new strategies, and archive outdated approaches with notes on why they were retired.
```
