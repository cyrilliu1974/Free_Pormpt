# Grant Proposal Workflow and Collaboration Protocol

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Workflow and Collaboration Protocol is a free AI prompt that creates structured coordination systems for teams preparing high-stakes grant applications. Built to prevent common failure modes like siloed writing, missed compliance requirements, late feedback, and narrative inconsistencies, this prompt delivers a phase-by-phase collaboration framework tailored to your proposal details. This grant proposal workflow prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces role assignments, compliance matrices, review schedules, and day-by-day timelines formatted as checklists, tables, and numbered action steps. Use it when coordinating multi-author proposals where one oversight can disqualify an application, or when past attempts suffered from poor version control and conflicting stakeholder input. ● Assigns clear decision-making authority and backup roles for Principal Investigator, Technical Writers, Budget Analyst, Compliance Officer, and external reviewers. ● Establishes a pre-writing phase with proposal architecture, win themes, evaluation criteria mapping, and shared funder priority understanding. ● Designs multi-stage review cycles (technical, compliance, readability, mock panel) with timing buffers for meaningful revision, not just proofreading. ● Creates redundant compliance verification covering solicitation requirements, page limits, formatting rules, attachments, and certifications. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert grant proposal orchestrator who designs collaboration frameworks that prevent common failure modes: siloed writing, missed compliance requirements, late conflicting feedback, and narrative inconsistencies.

## Task

Create a comprehensive step-by-step collaboration protocol for preparing a high-stakes grant proposal. The protocol must prevent single points of failure, ensure compliance verification at multiple stages, maintain narrative coherence across all sections, and provide clear role assignments, decision-making authority, version control, and review cycles with sufficient time for meaningful revision.

## Context

{{proposal-details}}

The team faces competing priorities, varying writing abilities, and different interpretations of funder priorities. One compliance miss or inconsistent narrative thread can eliminate the proposal. Previous attempts have failed due to inadequate coordination systems.

## Output

Deliver the protocol organized by phase:

**1. Team Structure & Role Assignments**  
Define responsibilities for Principal Investigator, Technical Writers, Budget Analyst, Compliance Officer, Graphics Designer, and External Reviewers. Specify decision-making authority, escalation paths, and backup assignments for every critical role.

**2. Pre-Writing Phase**  
Establish proposal architecture: win themes, evaluation criteria mapping, compliance matrix, section ownership assignments, and shared understanding of funder priorities.

**3. Collaborative Writing Protocol**  
Detail parallel drafting processes, version control systems, style guide enforcement, integration checkpoints, and specific collaboration tools/platforms.

**4. Review Cycle Framework**  
Design multiple review stages (internal technical review, compliance review, readability review, mock panel review). Specify timing, participants, and feedback integration methods that allow meaningful revisions, not just proofreading.

**5. Compliance Verification System**  
Create multi-layer checks covering solicitation requirements, page limits, formatting rules, required attachments, and certifications. Build redundancy into the verification process.

**6. Quality Control & Finalization**  
Implement consistency checks across sections, fact verification, budget-narrative alignment validation, and final production procedures.

**7. Day-by-Day Milestone Schedule**  
Provide a backward-planned timeline from the submission deadline with contingency buffers for unexpected issues.

Format using numbered lists for sequential steps, tables for role assignments and timelines, and checklists for compliance verification. Ensure quick-reference usability during the intense preparation period.
```
