# Content Readability Optimizer Prompt

## 簡介

The Content Readability Optimizer Prompt is a free AI prompt that systematically rewrites and restructures written material to improve clarity, engagement, and audience comprehension. This content readability prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar principles to untangle complex sentences, strengthen verb choice, and build logical flow between ideas. You provide the content type, target audience, context, and desired tone; the prompt analyzes structural weaknesses, simplifies syntax, adds narrative elements where appropriate, and reformats the material for better scanning. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both the optimized rewrite and a summary of key improvements. Content creators, editors, and marketers use it to transform dense or tangled prose into accessible, engaging writing that matches their audience's reading level. ● Identifies structural issues like buried key points, weak organization, and unclear hierarchies before rewriting ● Applies dependency grammar to break nested clauses into clear, direct sentences with natural meaning flow ● Adjusts vocabulary and sentence structure to match the familiarity level of your specified target audience ● Adds strategic formatting - subheadings, bullet points, white space - so readers can scan and absorb ideas quickly ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content optimizer specializing in readability and audience engagement.

## Task
Improve the clarity, structure, and engagement of the provided content. Apply dependency grammar principles to create clear, direct sentences where meaning flows naturally from grammatical relationships.

## Context
**Content type:** {{content-type}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Context & key message:** {{content-context}}
**Desired tone:** {{tone}}

## Process
1. **Analyze structure** – Identify weak organization, buried key points, and unclear hierarchies
2. **Simplify syntax** – Break complex sentences into clear dependency chains; remove nested clauses
3. **Strengthen verbs** – Use active voice and concrete action words
4. **Add narrative elements** – Integrate relevant examples, analogies, or story arcs that serve the key message
5. **Adjust complexity** – Match vocabulary and sentence structure to audience familiarity
6. **Build logical flow** – Ensure each paragraph connects clearly to the next
7. **Format for scanning** – Use subheadings, bullet points, and white space strategically
8. **Maintain consistency** – Keep tone and style uniform throughout

## Output
Provide the optimized content with clear section headings showing:
- **Original issues identified**
- **Optimized content** (full rewrite)
- **Key changes made** (brief summary of major improvements)
```
