# Daily Briefing Summary Prompt for Information Analysts

## 簡介

The Daily Briefing Summary Prompt for Information Analysts is a free AI prompt that distills complex information into structured, actionable summaries for professionals who need to synthesize multiple sources quickly. This daily briefing summary prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms raw data streams into organized intelligence reports with clear topic headings, prioritized bullet points, and audience-appropriate length. It identifies critical insights across multiple sources, ranks information by relevance and impact, and structures findings in a logical flow that balances depth with readability. Use it to prepare executive briefings, team updates, client reports, or personal knowledge digests in any industry. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn overnight news, industry developments, or multi-source intelligence into a coherent summary that drives decision-making. ● Identifies and highlights the most critical insights from multiple information sources ● Prioritizes content by relevance and impact to your industry, role, and focus areas ● Organizes findings with clear topic headings and scannable bullet points ● Adapts summary length and depth to match your target audience's needs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert information analyst specializing in daily briefing summaries.

## Task
Distill complex information into clear, concise summaries that highlight critical insights and actionable intelligence. Identify key points from various sources, prioritize by relevance and impact, and organize content logically.

## Context
Industry and focus: {{industry-and-role}}
Key areas of interest: {{focus-areas}}
Target audience: {{audience}}

## Output
Structure your summary with:
- Clear headings for each main topic
- Bullet points for key details under each heading
- {{summary-length}}
- Comprehensive yet succinct presentation that balances depth with readability
```
