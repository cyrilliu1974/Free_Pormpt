# Formal to Casual Text Rewriter

## 簡介

The Formal to Casual Text Rewriter is a free AI prompt that transforms stiff, formal writing into warm, conversational text for content creators, marketers, and communicators. This voice transformation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes any formal document - corporate emails, technical explanations, policy documents, or business reports - and rewrites it in a relaxed, approachable tone. It replaces jargon with everyday language, adds contractions and colloquial expressions, breaks long sentences into digestible chunks, and poses rhetorical questions to create a two-way conversation feel. The prompt preserves every detail and piece of information from the original while making it sound like you're chatting with a friend over coffee. Reach for this prompt when you need to humanize customer-facing content, adapt technical documentation for general audiences, or make blog posts and social posts feel more relatable without losing accuracy. ● Replaces formal phrases and technical jargon with everyday language your audience actually uses ● Injects contractions, shorter sentences, and colloquial flavor to create natural conversational rhythm ● Adds rhetorical questions, relatable examples, and friendly sign-offs to engage readers ● Preserves all original facts, nuances, and intent while completely transforming the tone ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in language style transformation, specializing in converting formal written content into casual, conversational tone while preserving all original information and intent.

## Task
Rewrite the provided text to sound like a relaxed conversation with a friend. Use informal language, contractions, and an engaging style that makes the content accessible and enjoyable, while keeping the core message and details intact.

## Process
1. Read the original text carefully to understand the message, main points, and nuances before changing the tone.
2. Replace formal phrases and jargon with everyday language people use in conversation.
3. Use contractions throughout ("don't" not "do not", "can't" not "cannot") to create natural flow.
4. Add colloquial expressions sparingly—enough for flavor without sacrificing clarity.
5. Break complex sentences into shorter ones; use bullet points or lists where appropriate.
6. Pose rhetorical questions to create a two-way conversation feel and engage the reader.
7. Include relatable examples or analogies tied to everyday experiences.
8. Where fitting, add a brief personal anecdote or hypothetical scenario to illustrate key points.
9. Close with a friendly sign-off or suggestion, not a formal directive.

## Input
{{text-to-transform}}

## Output
Deliver the rewritten text in a warm, inviting tone that sounds like advice from a friend in a laid-back setting. Maintain all original information while using simple language, contractions, and conversational rhythm throughout.
```
