# Negotiation Checklist Builder for Sales Meetings

## 簡介

The Negotiation Checklist Builder for Sales Meetings is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, print-ready negotiation checklists for sales teams preparing for client discussions. This negotiation checklist prompt for ChatGPT helps sales representatives systematically organize their strategy by generating bullet-point sections covering objectives, non-negotiable boundaries, strategic concessions, fallback positions, value propositions, objection responses, and success metrics. You provide the business context, and the prompt produces a structured checklist organized under clear headings with 4-8 specific items per section. The output is designed to be printed and referenced during actual meetings, ensuring no critical element is overlooked. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for sales managers preparing their teams for high-stakes negotiations, individual representatives facing complex client meetings, or anyone who needs to transform negotiation strategy into a practical, step-by-step reference tool. ● Organizes negotiation strategy into logical sections from pre-meeting preparation through closing tactics ● Identifies clear boundaries, concessions, and fallback positions before entering the room ● Includes objection-handling scripts and value differentiation points tailored to your business context ● Produces print-ready, actionable checklists that sales reps can reference during live negotiations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert negotiation strategist preparing sales representatives for high-stakes client meetings.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable negotiation checklist that sales representatives can follow to systematically prepare for and navigate client negotiations. Organize the checklist into logical sections that flow from pre-meeting preparation through to closing strategies.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include these critical negotiation elements:
- Clear objectives and desired outcomes
- Non-negotiable terms and boundaries
- Strategic concessions (what can be offered and under what conditions)
- Fallback positions and alternative approaches
- Value propositions and differentiation points
- Common objections and responses
- Decision criteria and success metrics

## Output
Deliver the negotiation checklist as a bullet-point list organized under clear section headings. Each section should contain 4-8 specific, actionable items. Make the checklist practical enough to print and reference during actual meetings.

Format example:
**Section Name**
- Specific actionable item
- Specific actionable item
```
