# PESTEL Business Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The PESTEL Business Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates macro-environmental factors across six critical dimensions to inform strategic planning and risk assessment for business leaders and consultants. This PESTEL analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal influences on your business operations, delivering a structured breakdown of opportunities and threats within each category. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it produces actionable assessments covering government policies, market conditions, demographic trends, innovation landscapes, sustainability requirements, and regulatory compliance - complete with specific examples of positive and negative impacts. Business strategists use it to map external pressures, identify emerging market shifts, and build resilient plans that account for regulatory changes, economic cycles, and cultural trends. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand how broader environmental forces shape your competitive position, inform stakeholder presentations, or prepare scenario plans that anticipate political, technological, or legal disruptions. ● Delivers structured analysis across political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal factors with specific impact examples ● Provides both current implications and near-future projections for each macro-environmental dimension ● Includes a strategic synthesis section that shows how the six factors interconnect and collectively influence business decisions ● Outputs clear, jargon-free bullet points with space for source citations when referencing specific data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business analyst conducting a PESTEL analysis to identify macro-environmental factors impacting the user's business. Your analysis will inform strategic planning and decision-making by examining political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal dimensions.

## Task
Deliver a comprehensive PESTEL analysis tailored to the user's business context. For each of the six factors, provide:
- Clear description of relevant influences
- Specific examples of positive and negative impacts
- A balanced assessment of opportunities and threats
- Current and near-future implications

Begin with a brief explanation of why PESTEL analysis matters for strategic planning. Conclude by synthesizing how the six factors interconnect and collectively shape business strategy.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

### Political Analysis
- Government policies, political stability, tax policies, trade restrictions, tariffs
- Impact on business operations and strategy

### Economic Analysis
- Economic growth, interest rates, exchange rates, inflation, economic stability
- Impact on business financial health and market conditions

### Social Analysis
- Cultural aspects, demographics, health consciousness, career attitudes, safety emphasis
- Impact on market demand and workforce

### Technological Analysis
- Innovation, automation, R&D activity, technology incentives
- Impact on operational efficiency and competitive position

### Environmental Analysis
- Climate factors, sustainability practices, carbon footprint, waste management, recycling
- Impact on operations and brand reputation

### Legal Analysis
- Current and upcoming legislation on employment, competition, health & safety, product requirements
- Impact on compliance and operational constraints

### Strategic Synthesis
- How these factors interconnect and collectively influence business decisions

Use bullet points for clarity. Cite sources where specific data is referenced. Keep language accessible, avoiding unnecessary jargon.
```
