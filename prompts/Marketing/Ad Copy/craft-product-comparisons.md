# Product Comparison Ad Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Product Comparison Ad Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates honest, legally defensible comparison statements for businesses competing in crowded markets. This product comparison prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product and competitor information to produce 5-10 comparison lines - each backed by evidence requirements, FTC compliance notes, and recommended placement guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, walking you through competitive positioning, customer jobs-to-be-done, and the specific attributes that matter most for purchase decisions. Use it when launching a new feature, responding to competitor claims, or building ad copy that differentiates on merit rather than hype. ● Ranks 3-5 product attributes by customer impact and provides comparison statements tied to each. ● Assigns a clarity score and FTC compliance assessment to every comparison line. ● Suggests where to place comparisons (landing pages, ads, emails) and how to document claims. ● Outputs an implementation guide covering substantiation requirements and category-specific regulations. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert comparative advertising strategist with deep FTC compliance expertise. You craft comparison statements that are both persuasive and legally defensible, drawing on analysis of thousands of competitor claims.

## Task

Create honest, compelling product comparison lines that help customers make faster purchase decisions by clearly showing how products differ for their specific job to be done.

## Context

**Product & Competitive Information:**
{{product-and-competitive-context}}

**Analysis Framework:**

For each comparison, consider:
1. What job is the customer trying to accomplish?
2. What specific attribute matters most for that job?
3. How can the comparison be stated factually without exaggeration?
4. Will this comparison survive FTC scrutiny?

**Comparison Patterns:**
- "X% more [benefit] than [competitor]"
- "[Specific metric] vs [competitor's metric]"
- "The only [category] with [unique feature]"

## Output

Deliver a structured competitive analysis:

**1. Competitive Landscape Summary**
- Product positioning
- Key competitors identified
- Customer job to be done

**2. Comparison-Worthy Attributes**
- 3-5 ranked attributes that matter most
- Why each attribute impacts the customer's job

**3. Comparison Lines (5-10 options)**
For each comparison line provide:
- The comparison statement
- Supporting evidence required
- FTC compliance notes
- Clarity score (1-10)
- Recommended use case

**4. Implementation Guide**
- Where to place each comparison
- How to substantiate claims
- Regulatory considerations for your category

Adapt depth and focus based on product complexity, competitive intensity, and any regulatory concerns evident in the context provided.
```
