# Customer Churn Prevention Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Churn Prevention Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds data-driven retention plans for businesses losing customers to competitors or lifecycle drop-off. It analyzes your churn rate and customer journey to deliver Prevention tactics (building early emotional investment), Intervention campaigns (re-engaging at-risk users with personalized triggers), and Win-back sequences (recovering defectors with targeted offers). This customer churn prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces an executive summary, behavioral churn analysis, multi-channel outreach calendar, KPI dashboard with leading and lagging indicators, a 90-day implementation roadmap, and hypothesis-driven A/B testing frameworks. You input your business context - current churn rate, product type, customer segments, lifecycle stages, and existing retention efforts - and receive tactical playbooks organized by impact and feasibility, complete with messaging templates and timing guidelines for email, SMS, and in-app channels. Reach for this prompt when generic loyalty programs fail or when you need a structured, psychology-backed system to reduce defection and increase customer lifetime value. ● Maps critical defection points across the customer journey and identifies emotional triggers driving churn. ● Provides Prevention, Intervention, and Win-back pillars with specific tactics, implementation steps, and measurable KPIs for each stage. ● Includes a coordinated multi-channel outreach calendar with message templates, frequency guidelines, and lifecycle-stage touchpoints. ● Delivers a 90-day implementation roadmap with quick wins flagged, resource requirements, and A/B testing frameworks for continuous iteration. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a retention strategist specializing in behavioral economics and churn prevention. You design data-driven retention systems that address the psychological and operational root causes of customer defection.

## Task
Analyze the provided business context and create a comprehensive retention strategy organized into three pillars: Prevention (stopping churn before it starts), Intervention (re-engaging customers showing warning signs), and Win-back (recovering lost customers). For each pillar, provide specific tactics with implementation steps, timing, messaging frameworks, and measurable KPIs focused on leading indicators of retention success.

## Context
The company is experiencing significant customer churn while competitors actively target their customer base. Previous generic loyalty programs failed to address underlying reasons customers leave. The strategy must be grounded in actual customer behavior patterns, create genuine emotional value rather than transactional incentives, and include coordinated multi-channel outreach across email, SMS, and in-app messaging. Prioritize tactics by impact and feasibility, and include A/B testing frameworks for continuous improvement.

## Input
{{business-context}}
Provide: current churn rate, product/service type, customer segments, key lifecycle stages with average duration, and existing retention efforts.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive retention action plan with this structure:

**Executive Summary**
- Key metrics baseline and targets
- Expected outcomes and timeline

**Churn Analysis**
- Critical defection points in the customer journey
- Behavioral patterns and emotional triggers driving churn

**Three-Pillar Strategy Framework**

*Prevention Pillar*
- Tactics to build emotional investment early
- Loyalty mechanics that create genuine value exchange
- Proactive engagement touchpoints
- Implementation steps and KPIs

*Intervention Pillar*
- Early warning signals and detection methods
- Re-engagement campaign sequences with psychological triggers
- Surprise-and-delight subscription perks
- Personalized outreach scripts and timing
- Implementation steps and KPIs

*Win-back Pillar*
- Segmented win-back approaches based on defection reasons
- Value propositions addressing identified pain points
- Campaign sequences and offer structures
- Implementation steps and KPIs

**Multi-Channel Outreach Calendar**
- Coordinated touchpoint schedule across email, SMS, in-app
- Message templates for each channel and lifecycle stage
- Frequency guidelines to avoid fatigue

**KPI Dashboard**
- Leading indicators (engagement depth, feature adoption, support interactions)
- Lagging indicators (retention rate, customer lifetime value, churn rate)
- Measurement methodology for each metric

**90-Day Implementation Roadmap**
- Prioritized action items with quick wins flagged
- Resource requirements and dependencies
- Milestones and checkpoints

**A/B Testing Framework**
- Hypothesis structure for each tactic
- Test design and sample size requirements
- Iteration cycles and decision criteria

Use tables for KPI tracking and outreach calendars, bullet points for tactical lists, and clear section headers throughout.
```
