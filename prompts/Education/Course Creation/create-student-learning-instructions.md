# Student Learning Community Instructions Generator

## 簡介

The Student Learning Community Instructions Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed operational frameworks for establishing peer-led learning organizations. This student learning community prompt for ChatGPT helps educators and student leaders design self-governing study groups with built-in accountability, leadership rotation, and sustainable engagement mechanisms that function independently without constant administrative oversight. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing phased implementation plans that cover foundation setup, operational systems, conflict resolution, and growth strategies. The prompt is ideal for educators launching collaborative learning initiatives, student affairs professionals supporting peer mentorship programs, and student leaders building study communities around shared academic goals. ● Produces a four-phase implementation roadmap with timelines, from initial setup through long-term sustainability and leadership transitions. ● Generates governance models including role definitions, leadership rotation schedules, facilitation protocols, and decision-making processes. ● Creates ready-to-use templates for study sessions, peer teaching workshops, accountability check-ins, and knowledge-sharing events. ● Provides troubleshooting guides addressing engagement decline, exclusionary dynamics, diverse learning styles, and inclusive participation across skill levels. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational community architect and peer learning specialist with expertise in designing self-sustaining student organizations that operate independently without constant administrative oversight.

## Task
Create comprehensive, actionable instructions for establishing and maintaining a student-run learning community that fosters intellectual growth, peer accountability, and collaborative knowledge building.

## Context
Effective peer learning communities emerge when students take ownership of their educational journey and build supportive ecosystems around shared goals. Your framework must address:

{{community-parameters}}

Design a sustainable structure covering:
- Community governance and leadership rotation systems
- Meeting formats and facilitation protocols
- Accountability mechanisms and progress tracking
- Conflict resolution processes
- Growth and recruitment strategies
- Templates for study sessions, peer teaching workshops, project collaborations, and knowledge-sharing events

Address common challenges including maintaining engagement over time, preventing exclusionary cliques, accommodating diverse learning styles, and ensuring inclusive participation across different skill levels and backgrounds.

## Output
Structure your response with clear section headings (##). Provide all actionable steps in bullet point format with specific timelines and implementation guidelines. Include:

1. **Foundation Phase** (Weeks 1-4): Initial setup, governance model, role definitions
2. **Operational Systems** (Ongoing): Meeting structures, accountability tools, communication channels
3. **Sustainability Mechanisms**: Leadership transition plans, onboarding processes, engagement tactics
4. **Templates & Tools**: Ready-to-use formats for sessions and events
5. **Troubleshooting Guide**: Solutions for common challenges with concrete intervention steps
```
