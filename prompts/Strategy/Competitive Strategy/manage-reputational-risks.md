# Reputation Management Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Reputation Management Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that develops comprehensive reputation protection frameworks for businesses and organizations facing potential reputational threats. This reputation management prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a five-part strategy: identifying reputational risks specific to your industry, designing an online sentiment monitoring system, creating crisis response protocols with communication templates and escalation paths, recommending proactive reputation-building initiatives, and establishing a continuous improvement framework. You provide your business context and current reputation baseline, and the prompt returns a structured strategy document with actionable steps, clear ownership assignments, and realistic timelines. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver tactical plans ready for implementation. This prompt is for communications directors, brand managers, C-suite executives, and consultants who need to protect organizational reputation before, during, and after critical incidents. ● Conducts risk assessment mapping reputational threats across competitive, regulatory, customer, and stakeholder dimensions ● Designs sentiment monitoring frameworks specifying platforms, metrics, alert thresholds, and responsible parties ● Creates crisis response plans with trigger criteria, team structures, communication templates, and escalation procedures ● Recommends proactive reputation strategies including thought leadership, community engagement, and transparency measures ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert reputation management strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive reputation management strategy that identifies potential threats, monitors online sentiment, and establishes a crisis response protocol.

## Context
Business and stakeholder context:
{{business-context}}

Current reputation baseline:
{{reputation-status}}

## Approach
Your strategy must address:

1. **Risk Assessment** – Identify reputational threats specific to this business type and industry, including competitive risks, regulatory exposure, customer sentiment vulnerabilities, and stakeholder concerns.

2. **Online Sentiment Monitoring System** – Design a monitoring framework covering relevant platforms, key metrics, alert thresholds, reporting cadence, and responsible parties.

3. **Crisis Response Protocol** – Create a detailed plan including trigger criteria, response team structure, communication templates, escalation paths, and stakeholder notification procedures.

4. **Proactive Reputation Strategies** – Recommend initiatives to strengthen reputation before issues arise, such as thought leadership, community engagement, transparency measures, and positive brand building.

5. **Continuous Improvement Framework** – Establish how the strategy will be reviewed, updated, and adapted based on emerging threats and performance data.

## Output
Deliver your strategy in a structured document with:
- Main section headings for each strategic component
- Subheadings breaking down key elements
- Bullet points detailing specific, actionable steps
- Clear ownership and timelines where applicable

Ensure logical flow and dependencies between sections are explicit.
```
