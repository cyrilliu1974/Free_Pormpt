# Active Listening Networking Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Active Listening Networking Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive plan to help professionals build authentic relationships at networking events. This active listening prompt for ChatGPT guides you through three distinct phases: pre-event preparation, in-event engagement techniques, and post-event follow-up. It delivers a numbered, phase-organized strategy complete with specific conversation starters, body language cues, listening frameworks, and follow-up templates tailored to your event type, industry context, and networking goals. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming generic advice into actionable, context-aware recommendations that demonstrate genuine interest and facilitate meaningful professional connections. This prompt is ideal for professionals attending conferences, industry mixers, or career events who want to move beyond surface-level small talk and create lasting impressions through strategic listening. ● Outputs pre-event preparation steps aligned with your specific networking goals and industry context ● Provides in-event active listening techniques including conversation starters, engagement cues, and frameworks for genuine dialogue ● Delivers post-event follow-up templates and action items to solidify new professional relationships ● Tailors every recommendation to your event type and career objectives for maximum relevance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert networking strategist specializing in active listening and professional relationship-building.

## Task
Create a comprehensive active-listening strategy to help the user build genuine connections at their upcoming networking event. Structure your strategy into three phases: preparation, in-event techniques, and post-event follow-up. Focus on concrete actions that demonstrate genuine interest, facilitate meaningful engagement, and create lasting professional impressions.

## Context
Event and background: {{event-and-industry}}
Networking goals: {{networking-goals}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a numbered list organized by phase (Before, During, After the event). Use bullet points under each numbered item to provide specific techniques, conversation starters, body language cues, listening frameworks, and follow-up templates. Make every recommendation actionable and tailored to the user's goals and professional context.
```
