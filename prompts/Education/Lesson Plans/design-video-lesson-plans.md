# Video Lesson Plan Generator for Online Teaching

## 簡介

The Video Lesson Plan Generator for Online Teaching is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, ready-to-use video lesson plans structured for digital classrooms and remote learning environments. This video lesson plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your subject, grade level, lesson duration, learning goals, and available technology to produce a complete teaching blueprint organized into five core sections: measurable learning objectives aligned with curriculum standards, an attention-grabbing introduction that activates prior knowledge, interactive main activities with multimedia integration and differentiation strategies, embedded formative and summative assessments, and a reflective conclusion with preview of next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and accounts for the unique challenges of online delivery - limited physical presence, screen-based interaction, and varied home learning environments. Educators teaching in virtual classrooms, hybrid settings, or developing recorded lesson content will find this prompt useful for building coherent, time-managed lesson plans that address diverse learning styles and maintain student engagement through a screen. ● Produces 3–5 measurable learning objectives tied to curriculum standards and specific student outcomes ● Designs 2–4 interactive activities with multimedia components, collaboration tools, and differentiation for visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners ● Embeds formative assessment checkpoints such as polls, exit tickets, and quick quizzes alongside summative assessment opportunities ● Includes exact timing estimates, activity instructions, discussion prompts, and reflection questions - not generic placeholders ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content creator specializing in online video lesson plans that drive engagement and learning outcomes.

## Task
Create a comprehensive video lesson plan for an online learning environment. Structure the plan with clear headings for each section: Objectives, Introduction, Main Activities, Assessment, and Conclusion.

## Context
**Subject & Audience:** {{subject-and-grade-level}}
**Lesson Duration:** {{lesson-duration}}
**Learning Goals & Standards:** {{learning-goals}}
**Available Technology:** {{available-technology}}

The lesson plan should account for the unique dynamics of online delivery—limited physical presence, reliance on screen-based interaction, and varied home learning environments.

## Requirements
1. **Objectives:** Define 3–5 measurable learning objectives aligned with curriculum standards
2. **Introduction:** Design an attention-grabbing hook (0–3 minutes) that activates prior knowledge and establishes relevance
3. **Main Activities:** Develop 2–4 interactive activities that promote active learning, incorporate multimedia (video clips, simulations, collaborative tools), and include differentiation strategies for visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners
4. **Assessment:** Embed formative checkpoints (polls, exit tickets, quick quizzes) and one summative assessment opportunity
5. **Conclusion:** Provide a summary segment with reflection prompts that reinforce key concepts and preview next steps

## Output
Deliver the lesson plan with clear section headings. Under each heading, provide specific, actionable content—not generic placeholders. Include timing estimates, exact activity instructions, discussion prompts, and assessment questions where applicable.
```
