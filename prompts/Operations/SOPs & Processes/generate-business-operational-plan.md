# Business Operational Plan Generator for Digital Teams

## 簡介

The Business Operational Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured operational plans for digital business owners and operations managers. This business operational plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a ready-to-implement blueprint that covers team roles, daily workflows, internal and external communication strategies, customer support protocols, KPI tracking, and contingency planning. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming a high-level business overview into an actionable operational framework with executive summaries, role definitions, process documentation, and implementation roadmaps. Use it when launching a new digital business, restructuring operations, onboarding an operations team, or documenting workflows for consistent execution across remote or distributed teams. ● Creates customized team structures with defined roles and responsibilities tailored to your business model ● Documents daily operational tasks and processes with clear ownership and execution steps ● Designs both internal communication workflows and external customer-facing channel strategies ● Establishes measurable performance metrics and KPI tracking methods aligned with business goals ● Builds contingency plans for operational risks with mitigation strategies and escalation processes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an operations strategist and business consultant developing a comprehensive operational plan for a digital business.

## Task
Create a detailed operational plan covering team structure, daily operations, communication, customer support, performance tracking, and risk management. The plan must be actionable and tailored to the specific business context provided.

## Context
Digital business overview:
{{business-overview}}

## Output
Deliver the operational plan using this structure:

**Executive Summary:**
[Overview of the operational plan and its key components]

**Team Structure and Roles:**
● Role 1: [Description and responsibilities]
● Role 2: [Description and responsibilities]
● Role 3: [Description and responsibilities]
[Additional roles as needed]

**Daily Operations:**
● Task 1: [Specific process description]
● Task 2: [Specific process description]
● Task 3: [Specific process description]
[Additional tasks as needed]

**Communication Plan:**

Internal Communication:
● Tool 1: [Purpose and usage]
● Tool 2: [Purpose and usage]

External Communication:
● Channel 1: [Strategy and application]
● Channel 2: [Strategy and application]

**Customer Support:**
● Support Channels: [List of channels]
● Response Time Goals: [Specific targets]
● Escalation Process: [Step-by-step workflow]

**Performance Metrics:**
● Metric 1: [KPI description and tracking method]
● Metric 2: [KPI description and tracking method]
● Metric 3: [KPI description and tracking method]

**Contingency Plans:**
● Scenario 1: [Risk description and mitigation strategy]
● Scenario 2: [Risk description and mitigation strategy]

**Next Steps:**
[Prioritized implementation roadmap with clear action items]
```
