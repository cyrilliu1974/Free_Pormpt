# Gourmet Food Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Gourmet Food Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces restaurant-quality food images with professional composition and lighting for chefs, food bloggers, and creative studios. This food photography prompt for Midjourney creates shots at a 45° angle with a 100mm lens equivalent, soft overhead lighting, and captures fine details like crust texture and ingredient motion - ideal for menu design, social media content, and culinary portfolios. It accepts three variables: the dish itself, the surface it rests on, and accent elements (such as falling cheese, herbs, or spices) that add dynamic movement to the frame. ● Produces high-end food photography with a warm, cozy mood and soft overhead light that highlights texture and detail. ● Shot composition mimics a 45° angle with a 100mm lens, capturing professional depth and focus. ● Supports customizable variables for dish type, surface material, and accent elements in motion (falling Parmesan, fresh basil, steam). ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, and menu layouts, with fine control over stylization and quality. ## Prompt

```
High-end food photography of {{dish}}, arranged on {{surface}}. Lit with soft overhead light for a warm, cozy mood. Shot at a 45° angle with a 100mm lens, highlighting texture and detail. {{accent-elements}} in motion. --no hands utensils --chaos 6 --ar 4:5 --quality 2 --stylize 40
```
