# Technical Assembly Hologram Image Generator

## 簡介

The Technical Assembly Hologram Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates realistic workshop scenes with futuristic holographic 3D exploded diagrams floating above workbenches for manufacturers, educators, and technical content creators. This technical assembly hologram prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces close-up product photography showing hands assembling a device while a bright cyan holographic overlay displays part numbers, directional arrows, torque specifications, and 3D component placement. The 16:9 composition combines shallow depth-of-field workshop realism (pegboard tool walls, organized parts containers, soldering stations) with high-contrast vector hologram elements that appear spatially anchored above the work surface. Use it for instruction manuals, training materials, product documentation, or marketing visuals that need to show both real-world assembly action and technical assembly information in a single frame. Reach for this prompt when you need instructional imagery that bridges hands-on work with digital augmented-reality aesthetics, or when static exploded diagrams feel too abstract and plain photography lacks technical detail. ● Produces 16:9 overhead-angle workshop photography with hands holding components, safety glasses, and organized tool backgrounds in sharp focus with creamy bokeh blur. ● Overlays a bright cyan holographic 3D exploded assembly diagram with part numbers, torque specs, directional arrows, and spatially correct component placement above the workspace. ● Customizable device type variable lets you adapt the scene for electronics, mechanical assemblies, drones, robotics, or any product requiring step-by-step assembly visualization. ● Combines cool fluorescent shop lighting with warm holographic glow for industrial color grading in grey, deep red, metallic silver, and neon cyan palettes. ## Prompt

```
Close-up product photography shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, slightly overhead angle. A person wearing safety glasses assembles {{device-type}} at a cluttered but organized home workshop bench, holding a small component with tweezers or screwdriver. Hands, component, and hologram are razor-sharp; background blurs into creamy bokeh.

Workshop details: pegboard tool wall, organized parts containers, soldering iron station, wood grain bench, subtle metal shavings. The person is deeply concentrated, leaning over disassembled parts spread on the workbench.

CENTRAL FEATURE: A bright, high-contrast cyan holographic 3D exploded assembly diagram floats directly above the workspace. The hologram shows a fully rendered, rotating 3D model of the device with vector lines, directional arrows, part numbers (P/N: M24B), and assembly instructions ("Attach to Main Hub, Torque to 1.5 Nm"). The diagram clearly indicates where the held component fits. Spatially correct placement.

Lighting: Cool, bright fluorescent shop light from above contrasted by warm directional glow from the cyan hologram. Color palette: greys, deep reds, metallic silver, neon cyan holographic overlay. Industrial, high-detail, technical action shot style. Extremely shallow depth of field.
```
