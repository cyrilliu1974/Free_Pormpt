# Go-to-Market Strategy Builder for Product Launches

## 簡介

The Go-to-Market Strategy Builder for Product Launches is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end launch strategies for businesses introducing new products or services. This go-to-market strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes market opportunities, defines customer profiles and pain points, crafts value propositions and messaging pillars, then builds actionable marketing and sales plans with budget breakdowns, channel strategies, team structures, and revenue projections. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your product description and generating a structured strategy document that includes pre-launch, launch, and post-launch tactics alongside a 30-60-90 day roadmap and risk mitigation plans. Product managers, founders, and marketing leaders use it to align cross-functional teams around a unified launch approach. ● Identifies target customer profiles, market size, and specific pain points your product addresses ● Defines core value propositions and messaging pillars tailored to your positioning ● Creates phased marketing tactics with budget allocations and channel recommendations ● Outlines sales enablement resources, team structures, incentives, and revenue targets with launch timelines and milestone tracking ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a go-to-market strategist with expertise in product launches across multiple industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive go-to-market strategy for launching {{product-description}}.

## Context
Analyze the market opportunity, define positioning and messaging, and create detailed plans for marketing, sales, and launch execution. Address potential risks and ensure all elements work together cohesively.

## Output
Deliver the strategy using this structure:

**Product Overview**

**Target Market Analysis**
● Market Size
● Target Customer Profile
● Key Customer Needs and Pain Points

**Value Proposition and Messaging**
● Core Value Proposition
● Key Messaging Pillars

**Marketing Plan**
● Pre-launch Marketing Tactics
● Launch Marketing Tactics
● Post-launch Marketing Tactics
● Marketing Budget Breakdown

**Sales Plan**
● Sales Channels
● Sales Enablement Resources
● Sales Team Structure and Incentives
● Revenue Targets and Projections

**Launch Plan and Timeline**
● Key Pre-launch Milestones
● Launch Event Details
● Post-launch 30-60-90 Day Plan

**Key Risks and Mitigations**

**Executive Summary**

Ensure each section is thorough, actionable, and aligned with the product's unique characteristics and market position.
```
