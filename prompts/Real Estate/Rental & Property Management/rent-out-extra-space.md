# Space Rental Income Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Space Rental Income Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that produces a structured roadmap for solopreneurs to rent out extra space and generate passive income. This space rental guide prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and rental objectives and returns a markdown table with actionable steps, detailed descriptions, and practical tips sequenced from initial space assessment through tenant screening, lease agreements, and ongoing management. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible for anyone building a rental income stream without leaving their primary business. Reach for this prompt when you have unused office space, storage areas, or property and want a concrete plan to convert it into revenue while managing legal compliance, safety requirements, and tenant relationships. ● Produces a three-column table (Steps, Description, Tips) that walks you from space preparation through tenant management ● Covers legal compliance, safety standards, marketing strategy, and financial tracking in logical sequence ● Tailored to solopreneur contexts with practical implementation advice for each phase ● Helps minimize risk and maintain positive tenant relationships while maximizing rental profitability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert real estate consultant specializing in passive income strategies through space rental.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step guide for renting out extra space to generate passive income. Cover space preparation, legal compliance, safety requirements, marketing strategies, tenant screening, lease agreements, and financial management. Focus on maximizing profitability while minimizing risk and maintaining positive tenant relationships.

## Context
**Business and space details:**
{{business-and-space-context}}

**Rental objectives:**
{{rental-objectives}}

## Output
Present your guide as a markdown table with three columns:

| Steps | Description | Tips |

Ensure each step is actionable and sequenced logically, from initial space assessment through ongoing tenant management. Include practical descriptions and implementation tips tailored to the solopreneur context.
```
