# Exit Risk Assessment Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Exit Risk Assessment Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive workforce continuity frameworks for HR teams and risk managers. This exit risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete methodology covering risk identification matrices, scoring systems for role criticality and knowledge loss, mitigation strategies, early warning protocols, and implementation roadmaps. Built on ISO 31000 Risk Management principles, it runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver scoring tables, intervention plans, and monitoring processes tailored to your organization's size, structure, and historical turnover patterns. HR teams use it to systematically evaluate departure risks, prioritize succession planning, and protect institutional knowledge. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured approach to workforce continuity planning, especially in organizations where critical role departures create operational vulnerability. ● Creates weighted scoring systems that evaluate role criticality, knowledge uniqueness, succession readiness, and team dependencies ● Produces risk identification matrices for knowledge loss, operational gaps, and replacement challenges specific to your organization ● Delivers prioritized mitigation strategies with implementation timelines and resource requirements ● Generates early warning indicators and monitoring protocols HR teams can deploy immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert risk management consultant specializing in organizational resilience and workforce continuity planning, with deep knowledge of ISO 31000 Risk Management principles and human capital analytics.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive exit risk assessment methodology that identifies, evaluates, and mitigates potential disruptions caused by employee departures. Provide scoring matrices, actionable recommendations, and early warning protocols HR teams can implement.

## Context

**Organization details:**
{{organization-context}}

*Include: organization size, departments, reporting structure, industry, business model, role types, specializations, interdependencies, available workforce data systems, historical turnover patterns, seasonal trends, and known departure triggers.*

## Output

Structure your exit risk assessment model with these five components:

**1. Risk Identification Framework**
- Matrices for knowledge loss, operational gaps, and replacement challenges
- Assessment criteria based on ISO 31000 principles

**2. Scoring Methodology**
- Weight factors: role criticality, knowledge uniqueness, succession readiness, institutional memory concentration, team dependencies, skill transferability
- Scoring tables with clear numerical scales and interpretation guidance

**3. Mitigation Strategies**
- Specific interventions ranked by implementation priority and resource requirements
- Address knowledge transfer, succession planning, and cross-training needs

**4. Monitoring Protocols**
- Early warning indicators to detect elevated departure risks
- Practical monitoring processes for HR teams

**5. Implementation Roadmap**
- Phased approach with quick wins and longer-term initiatives

Present the framework using narrative explanations, scoring matrices in table format, and actionable recommendations in bullet lists for clarity and ease of implementation.
```
