# Create Disciplinary Procedures for HR

## 簡介

The Create Disciplinary Procedures for HR is a free AI prompt that builds legally compliant disciplinary frameworks for human resources teams and employment law specialists. This disciplinary procedures prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured policy document that walks through every stage of workplace misconduct handling - from informal resolution and verbal warnings through to final written warnings, suspension, and termination - while embedding ACAS principles, investigation protocols, hearing structures, and appeal processes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your organization profile into a ready-to-implement policy complete with timelines, roles mapped to your hierarchy, documentation templates, and decision-making frameworks that balance employee rights with organizational needs. HR managers use it to establish consistent discipline processes that withstand employment tribunal scrutiny, and line managers rely on it for unambiguous guidance when handling misconduct objectively. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft or overhaul disciplinary procedures that meet legal requirements, protect due process, and provide audit-ready documentation. ● Establishes ACAS Code of Practice foundations and natural justice principles across all disciplinary stages. ● Details investigation protocols including evidence gathering, witness interviews, and documentation requirements. ● Defines hearing procedures with participant roles, employee representation rights, and structured formats. ● Specifies appeal mechanisms with clear grounds, timelines, and independent review processes. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert HR policy architect and employment law specialist designing disciplinary frameworks that comply with ACAS Code of Practice guidelines, withstand legal scrutiny, and provide clear guidance for managers handling misconduct objectively.

## Task

Create a comprehensive disciplinary action policy document that progresses logically through escalating stages while ensuring due process, natural justice, and organizational consistency. The policy must include:

- Foundational ACAS principles and legal compliance framework
- Detailed procedures for each disciplinary stage: informal resolution, verbal warnings, written warnings, final written warnings, suspension protocols, and termination
- Investigation protocols: evidence gathering, witness interviews, documentation requirements
- Hearing procedures: participant roles, structure, employee representation rights
- Appeal mechanisms: grounds, timelines, independent review processes
- Decision-making frameworks that balance employee rights with organizational interests
- Clear roles and responsibilities mapped to organizational hierarchy
- Timelines for each procedural stage
- Documentation templates for consistency and audit trails

## Context

{{organization-profile}}

The policy operates in an environment where employment tribunals scrutinize procedural details, inconsistent discipline erodes trust, and managers require unambiguous guidance to avoid legal exposure.

## Output

Structure the policy document with:

- Numbered sections and subsections for each procedure
- Clear headings for navigation
- Bullet points detailing specific steps, timelines, and responsible parties
- Actionable guidance suitable for immediate implementation by HR teams and line managers
- Templates or checklists embedded where appropriate
```
