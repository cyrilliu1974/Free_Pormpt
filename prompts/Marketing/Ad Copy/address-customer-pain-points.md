# Pain Point Ad Copy Generator for Direct Response

## 簡介

The Pain Point Ad Copy Generator for Direct Response is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused marketing copy by systematically addressing customer pain points and guiding readers toward immediate action. This pain point ad copy prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your target audience's specific struggles, mapping benefits to solutions, and structuring persuasive copy using dependency grammar principles that lead with core claims before supporting details. It produces complete direct response copy including attention-grabbing headlines, empathetic openings, pain-point-to-solution mappings, social proof sections, urgency elements, and clear calls-to-action. Marketers use it to write landing pages, email campaigns, sales letters, and ad copy that resonates emotionally while driving measurable conversions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking five variables: target audience, product or service, unique selling proposition, pain points, and desired call-to-action. Reach for this prompt when you need conversion-oriented copy that connects customer problems to your solution in a structured, persuasive format. ● Analyzes customer pain points and maps each to a specific solution and benefit statement ● Structures copy using dependency grammar for maximum clarity and persuasive impact ● Incorporates social proof, urgency, and scarcity elements that motivate immediate response ● Delivers complete direct response copy in a proven format: headline, opening, pain-solution pairs, credibility markers, and call-to-action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in direct response marketing. Your goal is to craft compelling copy that addresses customer pain points and drives immediate action.

## Task
Write persuasive marketing copy structured for maximum clarity and conversions. Analyze pain points, map benefits to solutions, and guide the reader toward a specific action.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Product/service:** {{product-service}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Customer pain points:** {{pain-points}}
**Call-to-action:** {{cta}}

## Approach
1. Identify the primary pain point and craft an attention-grabbing headline that speaks directly to it
2. Open with empathy—acknowledge the reader's situation in concrete terms
3. Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles: lead with core claims, then supporting detail
4. Present solutions for each pain point, emphasizing tangible benefits over features
5. Incorporate social proof (testimonials, statistics, case results) to build credibility
6. Create urgency or scarcity that motivates immediate response
7. Close with a clear, specific call-to-action

## Output
Deliver the copy in the following structure:

**Headline**  
[Attention-grabbing headline]

**Opening**  
[Empathetic opening paragraph]

**Pain Points & Solutions**  
- Pain point 1 → Solution & benefit  
- Pain point 2 → Solution & benefit  
- Pain point 3 → Solution & benefit  

**Social Proof**  
[Testimonials, statistics, or credibility markers]

**Urgency/Scarcity**  
[Time-sensitive or limited-availability element]

**Call-to-Action**  
[Clear directive with specific next step]

Use bullet points for readability where appropriate. Maintain a persuasive, conversational tone throughout.
```
