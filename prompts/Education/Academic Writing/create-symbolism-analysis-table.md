# Symbolism Analysis Table Generator for Literature

## 簡介

The Symbolism Analysis Table Generator for Literature is a free AI prompt that helps students, educators, and literary analysts systematically identify and interpret symbolic elements in novels, plays, and other literary texts. This symbolism analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through a methodical process: it identifies recurring symbols and motifs, interprets their deeper meanings in relation to themes and characters, and locates 2-3 direct quotes with chapter and page references to support each interpretation. The output is a clean markdown table that organizes symbols, meanings, and textual evidence in an accessible format perfect for essays, study guides, or classroom discussion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it suitable for any text-based AI platform. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level reading and produce a rigorous, citation-backed analysis of how symbols function within a narrative. It is ideal for high school and college literature courses, independent literary research, or preparing teaching materials. ● Identifies 3+ key symbols with thematic weight and narrative significance ● Provides 2-3 direct textual quotes per symbol with chapter and page numbers ● Outputs a markdown table format ready for academic papers or study guides ● Focuses analysis on symbols most central to understanding the work's meaning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literary analyst specializing in symbolism analysis.

## Task
Analyze the symbolism in the specified book and present your findings in a structured table format.

## Context
Symbols are literary devices that carry deeper meaning beyond their literal appearance. Your analysis should identify key symbols, explain their significance in relation to the book's themes, characters, and plot, and support interpretations with textual evidence.

## Process
1. Read through the book carefully, identifying symbols that recur or carry thematic weight
2. For each symbol, determine its deeper meaning and how it functions within the narrative
3. Locate 2-3 direct quotes that illustrate each symbol's usage and significance
4. Document chapter and page numbers for all citations
5. Focus on symbols that are most central to understanding the work

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with this structure:

| Symbol | Meaning | Textual Examples |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| Name of symbol | Interpretation of the symbol's meaning and significance (2-3 sentences) | 1. "Relevant quote" (Chapter X, page Y)<br>2. "Relevant quote" (Chapter X, page Y)<br>3. "Relevant quote" (Chapter X, page Y) |

Include at least 3 key symbols, adding additional rows for other important symbols as needed.

---

**Book to analyze:**
{{book-text}}
```
