# Promotional Poster With Transparent Device Design

## 簡介

The Promotional Poster With Transparent Device Design is a free AI prompt that generates futuristic marketing visuals featuring a transparent, holographic device as the hero element against a modern skyline backdrop. This promotional poster prompt for Midjourney produces 3D photoreal renders with a centered composition, blue-white gradient glow, and clean negative space designed for product launches, tech campaigns, and brand announcements. You customize two variables: the device type (laptop, tablet, phone) and your headline text, which appears in bold sans-serif typography with wide tracking. The output follows a 3:4 aspect ratio with minimal aesthetic, bright sky lighting, and elegant futuristic styling that works across print and digital channels. ● Produces centered hero compositions with transparent device rendering, holographic texture, and blue-white gradient glow effects ● Includes customizable headline text in bold sans-serif with wide tracking and space for logo placement in the top-right ● Outputs 3D photoreal style with clean margins, generous negative space, and minimal aesthetic free of clutter or busy patterns ● Excludes unwanted elements like extra text, watermarks, multiple devices, and heavy textures through built-in negative prompts ## Prompt
