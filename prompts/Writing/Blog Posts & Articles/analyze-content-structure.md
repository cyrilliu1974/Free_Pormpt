# Content Structure Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Content Structure Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that dissects existing written content and generates fresh article outlines for content strategists, bloggers, and marketing teams. This content analysis prompt for ChatGPT breaks down any piece of writing across five dimensions: summary of key arguments and themes, structural flow and organization, tone and writing voice, reader engagement factors, and specific techniques like storytelling or data use. After analysis, it produces three high-level content ideas and three fully detailed outlines, each with a working title, 3-5 key points, illustrative examples, and a unique perspective that distinguishes the new piece from the source. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Writers and content teams use it to study competitor posts, reverse-engineer successful articles, and rapidly ideate follow-up content that builds on proven themes without duplicating the original. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand why a piece of content works and want to spin its core ideas into new angles for your editorial calendar. ● Deconstructs content into summary, structure, voice, engagement hooks, and writing techniques. ● Generates three high-level concepts and three detailed outlines with titles, key points, examples, and unique angles. ● Ensures new content ideas honor the original themes while remaining clearly distinct. ● Ideal for content teams studying successful posts, planning follow-ups, or training writers on what makes content work. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist and writer who analyzes content deeply and generates new piece outlines inspired by the original.

## Task

Analyze the provided content, then create three distinct outlines for new pieces that build upon its core ideas with fresh angles.

## Analysis Framework

1. **Summary**: Identify key points, arguments, and themes explored
2. **Structure**: Break down sections, components, and content flow
3. **Voice**: Characterize tone, style, and writing voice
4. **Engagement**: Pinpoint what makes the piece compelling to readers
5. **Techniques**: Highlight specific methods used (storytelling, data, humor, analogies, etc.)

## Content to Analyze

{{content}}

## Output

Provide your analysis following the framework above, then deliver:

**Three High-Level Ideas**
Brief concepts for new pieces that riff on the same themes but explore different angles or applications.

**Three Detailed Outlines**
For each new piece, include:
- Working title
- 3-5 key points or arguments to explore
- Examples or stories to illustrate those points
- A unique insight, perspective, or opinion that distinguishes this piece from the original

Ensure each new piece is clearly distinct from the source material while honoring its core themes.
```
