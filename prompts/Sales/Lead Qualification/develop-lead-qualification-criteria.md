# Lead Qualification Criteria Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Qualification Criteria Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive scoring system to help sales teams identify and prioritize high-quality leads based on source, demographics, and behavior. This lead qualification prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and lead generation channels to produce a structured framework with 4-6 measurable criteria in each of three categories: Lead Source (channel quality and origin), Demographic Information (customer profile fit), and Behavioral Indicators (engagement signals and purchase intent). The output is delivered as a markdown table with detailed explanations for each criterion, including measurement guidelines and concrete examples. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring your sales team applies qualification standards consistently across all prospects. This prompt is designed for sales leaders, demand generation managers, and revenue operations teams who need to establish clear, repeatable lead scoring criteria aligned with their specific business goals and target market. ● Creates 12-18 specific, measurable qualification criteria across three proven categories ● Includes scoring thresholds, measurement guidelines, and real-world examples for each criterion ● Analyzes your unique business context and lead sources to ensure criteria predict actual conversion likelihood ● Delivers a markdown table format plus explanations that ensure consistent application across your entire sales team ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lead qualification specialist designing a comprehensive scoring framework.

## Task
Create a structured set of lead qualification criteria organized into three categories: Lead Source, Demographic Information, and Behavioral Indicators. Each criterion must be specific, measurable, and aligned with the provided business context. Include clear definitions and examples to ensure consistent application across the sales team.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{lead-generation-channels}}

## Process
1. Analyze the business context, target market characteristics, and sales objectives
2. Develop 4-6 criteria per category that predict lead quality and conversion likelihood
3. Consider lead origin quality, customer profile fit, engagement signals, and purchase intent indicators
4. Define thresholds or scoring guidelines where applicable
5. Provide concrete examples for each criterion

## Output
Deliver your lead qualification framework as a markdown table with three columns:

| Lead Source | Demographic Information | Behavioral Indicators |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------|

Below the table, provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) for each criterion, describing why it matters and how to measure it consistently.
```
