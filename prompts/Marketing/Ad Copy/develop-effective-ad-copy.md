# Social Media Ad Copy Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Social Media Ad Copy Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates targeted, conversion-focused advertising copy tailored to the unique requirements of each social media channel. This ad copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through building complete advertisements that balance platform-specific constraints with persuasive messaging. You provide the platform (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.), product details and unique selling points, plus audience demographics and format requirements - the prompt returns structured copy with an engaging hook, benefit-focused body text, integrated social proof when available, and a direct call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone and length to match character limits, visual formats, and user behavior patterns across different networks. Marketing teams, social media managers, and independent creators reach for this prompt when they need ready-to-publish ad copy that respects platform policies while driving clicks and conversions. ● Crafts attention-grabbing hooks tailored to audience pain points and desires on each platform. ● Emphasizes product benefits through concise, emotionally resonant messaging that fits character and format limits. ● Integrates testimonials, ratings, or user metrics naturally to build credibility. ● Delivers clear, action-oriented CTAs that make the conversion path obvious. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in social media advertising across multiple platforms.

## Task
Create a compelling ad copy optimized for the specific platform, audience demographics, and format constraints provided. The copy must engage viewers, highlight product benefits, and drive immediate action through a strong call-to-action.

## Context
Different social media platforms have distinct audiences, content formats, and character limits. Effective ad copy speaks directly to platform-specific user behavior while showcasing what makes the product valuable and trustworthy.

## Output
Deliver ad copy that includes:

1. **Engaging Hook**: Open with a compelling question, statement, or attention-grabber that resonates with the target audience's desires or challenges.

2. **Benefit-Focused Body**: Concisely articulate how the product solves problems or enhances lives. Use emotional appeals or brief storytelling where appropriate. Emphasize what makes it stand out.

3. **Social Proof** (if available): Weave in testimonials, ratings, or user metrics to build credibility and trust.

4. **Clear Call-to-Action**: End with a direct, action-oriented CTA ("Shop Now", "Sign Up", "Learn More") that makes the next step obvious and easy.

5. **Platform Optimization**: Ensure the copy adheres to character limits, format requirements, and content policies while maximizing visual and emotional impact.

---

**Required Information:**
- Platform: {{platform}}
- Product/service and unique selling points: {{product-details}}
- Target audience and platform-specific considerations (character limits, visual requirements, etc.): {{audience-and-constraints}}
```
