# Doodle Notebook Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Doodle Notebook Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hand-colored marker and pen sketch portraits on lined notebook paper for creators, professionals, and anyone building a distinctive visual identity. This doodle portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana transforms a reference photo into an illustrated waist-up portrait with bold ink outlines, cross-hatch shading, and energetic red and yellow marker strokes that create a neon-like glow. The prompt preserves 100% of your facial features, skin tone, hair texture, and expression while rendering you in a playful, slightly messy notebook aesthetic complete with scattered annotations, speech bubbles, hand-lettered keywords related to your profession, and decorative doodles like stars, arrows, and lightning bolts. The result mimics the texture of real notebook paper with visible grain, marker bleed, smudges, and faint pencil construction lines underneath. Reach for this prompt when you need personality-rich profile pictures, social media visuals, or personal branding assets that stand out from polished photography. ● Preserves exact facial proportions, skin tone, hair texture, and expression from your reference photo in sketch form. ● Adds custom text bubbles, hand-lettered profession keywords, and scattered doodles (stars, arrows, chat bubbles) around the portrait. ● Renders on ruled notebook paper with authentic textures: visible grain, marker bleed, smudges, and faint pencil construction lines. ● Outputs waist-up portraits in a three-quarter pose with energetic red and yellow marker highlights and bold ink outlines. ## Prompt
