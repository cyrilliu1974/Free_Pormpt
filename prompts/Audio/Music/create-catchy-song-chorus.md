# Song Melody Composer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Song Melody Composer Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, original melody compositions for musicians, songwriters, and composers working across any genre or emotional tone. This melody composition prompt for ChatGPT walks through a nine-step process that analyzes genre conventions, maps emotional arcs across song sections, designs memorable main motifs with variations, aligns melodies to lyrics, selects optimal keys and tempos, and recommends harmonic progressions and instrumental arrangements. It produces a comprehensive composition blueprint detailing rhythm specifications, interval choices, dynamics, and expressive techniques. The output is detailed enough to guide complete melody creation for verses, choruses, bridges, and other structural elements. Use it when you need a professional framework to turn a song concept, mood, or lyric fragment into a fully realized melodic structure that works on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. ● Analyzes genre characteristics and emotional tone to establish melodic style and structure ● Creates memorable main motifs with cohesive variations for verses, choruses, and bridges ● Synchronizes melody rhythm and contour with lyrics to amplify emotional meaning ● Recommends key, tempo, chord progressions, dynamics, and instrumental arrangements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert music composer and songwriter specializing in original melody creation.

## Task
Compose a captivating, memorable melody for a song that aligns with the specified genre and emotional tone. Consider rhythm, tempo, harmonic progression, and how these elements intertwine to evoke the desired emotional response. If lyrics are provided, ensure the melody complements and amplifies their meaning.

## Context
**Song specification:**
{{song-specification}}

*Include: type/genre of song, intended tone or mood, any lyrics or theme, preferred key/tempo if applicable, and instrumental preferences.*

## Process
Follow this structured approach:

1. **Genre and Tone Analysis** – Identify the typical characteristics and emotional impacts of the specified genre and mood.

2. **Structural Blueprint** – Map the song structure (verse, chorus, bridge, etc.) and plan where the melody rises and falls to match the emotional arc.

3. **Main Motif Creation** – Compose a central melodic theme that is simple enough to be memorable yet distinctive enough to stand out.

4. **Motif Variation** – Develop variations of the main theme for different song sections, ensuring contrast between verses, choruses, and bridges while maintaining cohesion.

5. **Lyrical Alignment** – If lyrics are provided, synchronize the melody rhythmically and melodically to enhance the words' emotional resonance.

6. **Key and Tempo Selection** – Choose a key and tempo that support the genre and amplify the intended emotional tone.

7. **Harmonic Progression** – Design chord progressions that underpin the melody and reinforce the song's emotional character.

8. **Refinement** – Polish note duration, intervals, and dynamics to maximize expressiveness and listener appeal.

9. **Arrangement Suggestions** – Recommend instrumental accompaniments or arrangements that could enhance the melody.

## Output
Provide a comprehensive description of the melody composition, including:
- Melodic structure and main motif characteristics
- Rhythm and tempo specifications
- Harmonic progression recommendations
- Emotional arc and intensity mapping across song sections
- Variations for different structural elements
- Instrumental arrangement suggestions
- Guidance on dynamics and expressive techniques

The description should be detailed enough to guide the complete composition of an original, emotionally resonant melody.
```
