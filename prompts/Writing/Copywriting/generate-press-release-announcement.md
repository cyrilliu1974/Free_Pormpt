# Press Release Announcement Generator

## 簡介

The Press Release Announcement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, professional press releases announcing significant business milestones for corporate communications teams and PR professionals. This press release prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce complete press release documents with all standard components: attention-grabbing headline, lead paragraph with essential facts, body paragraphs with context and executive quotes, company background, and media contact information. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to structure sentences that clearly show relationships between ideas, creating announcements that flow logically and highlight the significance of achievements. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only the business details and milestone information. Reach for this prompt when you need to announce customer milestones, new locations, revenue targets, product launches, or other significant corporate achievements in a format that journalists and stakeholders expect. ● Creates full press releases with headline, lead, body, boilerplate, and contact section in standard industry format ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure logical sentence structure and clear relationships between ideas ● Prompts for executive quotes and forward-looking statements that add credibility and strategic context ● Produces professional, jargon-free language appropriate for media distribution and stakeholder communication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced press release writer specializing in corporate milestone announcements.

## Task
Write a professional press release announcing a significant business milestone. Use dependency grammar principles—structuring sentences to clearly show relationships between words and phrases—to create a compelling, well-organized announcement.

## Context
Business and milestone details:
{{business-and-milestone}}

Include:
- Type of business and industry position
- The specific milestone achieved (customer count, new location, revenue target, etc.)
- Why this milestone matters to the company's growth
- A quote from a company spokesperson
- Future plans or next steps
- Company background and mission

## Output
Structure the press release with these standard components:

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

1. **Headline**: Attention-grabbing title highlighting the achievement

2. **Lead paragraph**: Company name, milestone achieved, date, and location—the essential facts up front

3. **Body paragraphs**:
   - Context and details about the achievement
   - Executive or spokesperson quote emphasizing significance and impact
   - How this milestone positions the company for future success
   - Closing statement on commitment to stakeholders

4. **About [Company]**: Brief company history, mission, and description

5. **Contact**: Name, title, company, phone, email, and website

Use clear, professional language. Avoid jargon. Apply dependency grammar throughout to create sentences that flow logically and highlight key relationships between ideas.
```
