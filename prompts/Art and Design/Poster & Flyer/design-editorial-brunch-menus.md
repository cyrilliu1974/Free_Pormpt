# Editorial Brunch Menu Design Prompt for AI

## 簡介

The Editorial Brunch Menu Design Prompt for AI is a free AI prompt that creates sophisticated, print-ready brunch menu posters with modern editorial layouts for restaurants, cafes, and hospitality venues. This brunch menu design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces clean two-column grid layouts with generous white space, aligned item names and prices, and a relaxed urban cafe aesthetic. It combines modern sans-serif typography with neutral serif accents, muted color palettes of off-white, charcoal, and soft beige or sage green, and organizes content into structured sections like Eggs & Plates, Brunch Classics, Light & Fresh, and Brunch Drinks. Restaurant owners, graphic designers, and hospitality marketers use it to create high-end visual menus that reflect contemporary brunch culture without design software or hiring specialists. This prompt is for anyone building brand imagery for upscale dining venues, cafes, or food service businesses that want polished, professional menu visuals. ● Produces two-column editorial grid layouts with left-aligned dish names and right-aligned pricing for easy readability. ● Incorporates customizable venue details and brand elements while maintaining a cohesive minimal, modern design language. ● Structures menu content into clear sections with bold titles, medium-weight item names, and light descriptive text. ● Outputs muted neutral color palettes and generous white space that convey a confident, upscale brunch atmosphere. ## Prompt

```
Design a cool, modern editorial brunch menu poster with an urban cafe aesthetic. Clean two-column grid layout with generous white space, item names aligned left and prices aligned right. High-end minimal composition with relaxed, confident, premium mood.

{{venue-details}}

Typography: Modern sans-serif primary font with neutral serif accents. Section titles large and bold, item names medium weight, descriptions small and light. Muted neutral palette—off-white background, charcoal text, soft beige or sage green accents. {{brand-elements}}
```
