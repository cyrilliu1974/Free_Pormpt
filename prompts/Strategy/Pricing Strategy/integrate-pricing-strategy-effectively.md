# Tiered Pricing Strategy Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Tiered Pricing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops a multi-tier pricing structure aligned with your business objectives, competitive landscape, and target market positioning. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product or service, unique selling proposition, competitor pricing, and customer segments to create 3-4 distinct pricing tiers with clear feature differentiation and value justification. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, outputting a structured markdown table that maps each tier's name, price point, included features, and intended audience. Use it when launching a new product, restructuring your pricing model, entering new markets, or optimizing revenue across customer segments. ● Aligns pricing tiers with business objectives, market positioning, and customer willingness to pay ● Differentiates features across entry-level, mid-tier, and premium offerings to serve distinct customer segments ● Justifies each price point through value analysis of your unique selling proposition and competitor landscape ● Delivers a markdown table format ready for stakeholder review, sales enablement, and website implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert pricing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive tiered pricing strategy that aligns with business objectives and target market positioning.

## Context
Analyze the product/service, target audience, market positioning, production costs, competitor pricing, perceived value, and customer willingness to pay. Create distinct tiers with clear feature differentiation that justify each price point.

**Product/Service:**
{{product-service}}

**Target Market:**
{{target-market}}

**Business Objectives:**
{{business-objectives}}

**Main Competitors:**
{{competitors}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:**
{{usp}}

## Output
Present the pricing strategy as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Pricing Tier** (name and price)
- **Features** (what's included at this level)
- **Target Audience** (who this tier serves)

Include 3-4 tiers that progress from entry-level to premium, ensuring each tier offers meaningful value differentiation.
```
