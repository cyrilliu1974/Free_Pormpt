# Minimalist Architecture Image Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Architecture Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photographic architectural renderings combining clean modernist structures with serene garden landscapes. This minimalist architecture prompt for Midjourney produces eye-level, 35mm perspective images styled after legendary architectural photographer Julius Shulman, featuring native flowering plants, reflecting pools, and a human figure for scale reference. The prompt controls facade expression, materials, lighting quality, and garden composition while maintaining the crisp lines and intentional simplicity that define minimalist design. Architects, designers, real estate developers, and creative professionals reach for this prompt when they need presentation-ready visuals that communicate spatial harmony and material honesty. ● Produces 16:9 architectural renderings with eye-level perspective and 35mm focal length that mirrors professional photography standards. ● Incorporates reflecting pools, native flowering plants, and a positioned human figure to establish scale and invite viewer engagement. ● Applies Julius Shulman's documentary style - emphasizing natural light, material texture, and the relationship between structure and landscape. ● Excludes visual clutter and overcast conditions through negative prompts, ensuring bright, focused imagery that highlights architectural intent. ## Prompt
