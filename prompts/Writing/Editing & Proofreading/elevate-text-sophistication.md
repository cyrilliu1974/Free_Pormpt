# Text Sophistication & Vocabulary Enhancement Prompt

## 簡介

The Text Sophistication & Vocabulary Enhancement Prompt is a free AI prompt that refines written content by upgrading vocabulary for editors, academics, and professionals seeking more polished prose. This text sophistication prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a systematic five-step process: analyzing the original text's meaning and tone, substituting simplistic words with intellectually engaging synonyms, verifying contextual accuracy, checking flow and cohesion, and performing a final review to ensure fidelity to the source material. It works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to transform everyday language into more refined expression without introducing ambiguity or forced formality. Writers use it to prepare manuscripts for academic journals, polish business communications, or adapt content for discerning audiences. Reach for this prompt when you need to elevate writing quality without rewriting from scratch, or when client-facing documents require a more sophisticated register. ● Identifies and replaces simplistic or overused words with contextually appropriate synonyms that fit naturally ● Maintains the original message, tone, and intent throughout the enhancement process ● Verifies each substitution for contextual accuracy and checks that enhanced vocabulary creates smooth, cohesive flow ● Delivers a complete enhanced version that reads fluently without awkward phrasing or forced formality ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert editor specializing in vocabulary refinement and linguistic sophistication.

## Task

Elevate the given text by replacing simple or overused words with contextually appropriate, intellectually engaging synonyms. Preserve the original meaning, tone, and intent while making the text more compelling for a sophisticated audience.

## Input

{{text}}

## Process

1. **Analyze**: Read the text thoroughly to identify its core meaning, tone, and key themes that must remain unchanged.

2. **Replace**: Substitute simplistic or commonly used words with sophisticated synonyms. Ensure each replacement fits the context naturally and does not distort meaning.

3. **Verify Context**: Review each modified sentence to confirm the new vocabulary maintains accuracy and introduces no ambiguity.

4. **Check Flow**: Ensure enhanced vocabulary integrates smoothly, creating natural cohesion rather than forced formality.

5. **Final Review**: Confirm the enhanced version stays true to the original message, reads fluently, and contains no awkward phrasing.

**Enhancement example:**
- Original: "This book is very good and teaches you a lot."
- Enhanced: "This tome is exceptionally insightful, imparting copious knowledge."

## Output

Provide the complete enhanced version of the text with refined vocabulary that adds intellectual depth while maintaining contextual accuracy, cohesive flow, and alignment with the original purpose.
```
