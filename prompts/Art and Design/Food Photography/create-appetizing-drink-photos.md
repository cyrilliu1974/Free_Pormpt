# Appetizing Drink Photo Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Appetizing Drink Photo Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that produces commercial-grade beverage photography for food brands, marketers, and content creators. It outputs high-resolution images of drinks and shakes with professional styling cues - creamy textures, drizzle toppings, visible condensation, and side-angle composition in soft natural daylight against a clean backdrop. This drink photography prompt for Midjourney runs on any version of the platform and accepts a single variable for the drink description, making it simple to generate tack-sharp product shots for menus, social media, advertising, or e-commerce listings. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping beverage visuals without a studio shoot or when prototyping packaging and marketing concepts for regional dessert drinks, smoothies, milkshakes, or specialty beverages. ● Outputs side-angle wide shots in 4:5 aspect ratio with product tack-sharp focus and soft natural daylight. ● Includes condensation highlights, drizzle toppings, and creamy texture rendering for realistic beverage appeal. ● Clean white backdrop with minimal props and regional dessert aesthetic influences for versatile commercial use. ● Accepts any drink description - from mango lassi to matcha shake - and applies consistent professional food styling. ## Prompt

```
Appetizing commercial photograph of a {{drink-description}} shake in a tall glass; creamy texture with drizzle topping and visible condensation highlights. Side-angle wide shot in soft natural daylight. Clean white backdrop with minimal props nearby. Product tack-sharp focus, professional food styling with regional dessert aesthetic influences. --ar 4:5 --q 2 --s 250
```
