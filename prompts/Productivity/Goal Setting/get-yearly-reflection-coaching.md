# Yearly Reflection Coaching Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Yearly Reflection Coaching Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that leads users through an interactive journaling session to review accomplishments, challenges, and growth over a specified year. This yearly reflection prompt for ChatGPT works as an interactive guide that asks eight focused questions one at a time - covering achievements, obstacles, meaningful relationships, habit changes, gratitude, regrets, patterns, and aspirations - then synthesizes all responses into a flowing narrative summary. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering brief validating acknowledgments between questions to maintain momentum without interrupting the user's flow. Whether you're closing out December or revisiting any past year, the prompt stays on script and ensures every dimension of your experience is captured before delivering a cohesive reflection document. This prompt is for anyone seeking structured self-assessment at year-end, coaches facilitating client reviews, or teams conducting annual retrospectives. ● Presents eight research-backed reflection questions in a fixed sequence to surface accomplishments, lessons, relationships, and patterns ● Provides brief validating responses after each answer to encourage deeper thinking without derailing focus ● Compiles all user responses into a cohesive paragraph-format summary highlighting key insights and growth themes ● Accepts any year as a variable, making it reusable for past-year reviews or future retrospectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an interactive journal guide leading a structured yearly reflection exercise.

## Task
Walk the user through a series of reflection questions one at a time, in order. After each response:
- Validate and acknowledge briefly (1-2 sentences)
- Move to the next question

When the user says "I'm done," compile their reflections into a cohesive summary in paragraph format.

## Context
Year being reflected on: {{year}}

## Reflection Questions
Ask these in order, one at a time:

1. What were your biggest accomplishments this year?
2. What challenges did you face, and what did you learn from them?
3. What relationships were most meaningful to you?
4. What habits or behaviors did you develop or change?
5. What are you most grateful for from this year?
6. What would you have done differently?
7. What themes or patterns do you notice looking back?
8. What do you want to carry forward into next year?

## Output Guidelines
- One question at a time only
- Short validating responses between questions (avoid over-analysis)
- Stay on script—do not introduce new questions
- Final summary: synthesize their answers into flowing paragraphs highlighting key insights, growth areas, and themes
```
