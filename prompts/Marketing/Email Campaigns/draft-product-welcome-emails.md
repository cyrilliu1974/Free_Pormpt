# Product Welcome Email Generator With StoryBrand

## 簡介

The Product Welcome Email Generator With StoryBrand is a free AI prompt that creates onboarding emails using the StoryBrand narrative framework for SaaS companies, e-commerce brands, and product marketers. This product welcome email prompt for ChatGPT walks you through six StoryBrand principles: identifying the user's core problem, positioning your product as the guide (not the hero), demonstrating empathy and authority, presenting a clear three-step plan, issuing a single call to action, and painting both success and failure stakes. It outputs a mobile-optimized email body, a benefit-focused subject line, three subject line alternatives for A/B testing, and two CTA button text variations. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when launching a new user flow, redesigning onboarding sequences, or aligning customer communication with narrative marketing principles. ● Structures every email around the six core StoryBrand elements so the customer sees themselves as the hero. ● Outputs a complete email with subject line, empathy hook, authority statement, three-step plan, CTA, and success-versus-failure framing. ● Includes three alternative subject lines and two CTA button variations ready for split testing. ● Formats copy for mobile readability with short paragraphs, visual breaks, and scannable structure. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert email copywriter specializing in the StoryBrand framework. You structure welcome emails that position the user as the hero and the product as their guide.

## Task

Create a product welcome email using the StoryBrand framework that:

1. Positions the user as the hero with a problem or desire
2. Introduces the product as the guide (not the hero)
3. Demonstrates empathy and authority
4. Presents a clear 3-step plan
5. Issues one specific call to action
6. Paints success and failure stakes

## Context

**Product & audience:**
{{product-and-audience}}

**Brand voice & constraints:**
{{brand-voice}}

**Primary call to action:**
{{cta}}

## Output

Deliver a complete welcome email with:

**Subject line** – StoryBrand-aligned, benefit-focused

**Email body** structured as:
- Opening that recognizes the user's challenge
- Empathy statement acknowledging their struggle
- Authority statement establishing credibility
- Clear 3-step plan for success
- Single, specific call to action
- Success vision (what they'll achieve) and failure stakes (what they'll avoid)
- Warm sign-off

**Format for mobile:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Scannable structure with visual breaks
- Clear CTA placement

Include 3 subject line alternatives for A/B testing and 2 CTA button text variations.
```
