# Customer Referral Program Builder for Growth Teams

## 簡介

The Customer Referral Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored referral program strategies for growth teams, marketers, and business leaders looking to drive customer-led acquisition. This customer referral program prompt for ChatGPT takes your company context and produces a complete implementation plan including dual-sided incentive structures for both referrers and referees, tracking methodologies, multi-channel marketing strategies, and measurable performance metrics. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, extracting your industry, target audience, and product details to build a program that fits your specific business model rather than delivering generic templates. Use it when launching a new referral initiative, revamping an underperforming program, or pitching a word-of-mouth growth strategy to stakeholders. ● Designs dual-sided incentive frameworks that reward both the referring customer and the new customer they bring in ● Delivers a phased implementation timeline with tasks, durations, and milestones for program rollout ● Includes tracking methodologies to attribute referrals accurately and measure conversion rates at each stage ● Anticipates common challenges like low participation or incentive abuse and provides mitigation strategies ## Prompt
