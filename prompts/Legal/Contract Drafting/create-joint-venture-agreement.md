# Joint Venture Agreement Drafting Prompt

## 簡介

The Joint Venture Agreement Drafting Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates complete, legally structured joint venture contracts for business partners and legal professionals. This joint venture agreement prompt for ChatGPT produces a formal contract organized into eight standard sections: preamble, purpose, contributions, management, profit-sharing, dispute resolution, term and termination, and general provisions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting detailed inputs about both partners (legal entity information, registered addresses), the joint venture project description, each party's contributions and management committee structure, and financial terms including profit distribution percentages and dispute resolution methods. It outputs a contract using precise legal terminology with numbered subsections and formal language designed to prevent ambiguity. This prompt is built for attorneys drafting partnership agreements, business owners forming collaborative ventures, and corporate legal teams who need a structured starting point for joint venture negotiations. ● Produces contracts with eight core sections covering purpose, contributions, management committees, profit-sharing percentages, and dispute resolution procedures ● Accepts detailed partner information including legal entity types, capital contributions, management voting thresholds, and applicable jurisdiction ● Outputs formal legal language with numbered subsections, signature blocks, and entire agreement clauses ready for review and customization ● Tailors the agreement structure to match the specific project description, contribution types, and termination conditions you provide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal expert specializing in joint venture agreements, with deep knowledge of business law, corporate structures, and contract negotiation.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive joint venture agreement covering partner contributions, management structure, profit-sharing terms, and dispute resolution processes. Use clear legal language and organize the contract into standard sections.

## Context
The agreement must be tailored to the specific partners and project while ensuring all key aspects are adequately addressed. Avoid ambiguous language that could lead to disputes. The structure should be easy to navigate with clearly defined sections.

## Input Required
**Partner Details:**
{{partner-a-details}} — Name, legal entity type, and registered address of Partner A
{{partner-b-details}} — Name, legal entity type, and registered address of Partner B

**Business Terms:**
{{project-description}} — Purpose and description of the joint venture project
{{contributions-and-management}} — What each partner will contribute (capital, resources, expertise); management committee composition (number of representatives per partner, voting thresholds)
{{financial-and-term-details}} — Profit-sharing percentages, agreement duration or termination conditions, dispute resolution method (arbitration, mediation, etc.), applicable law and jurisdiction

## Output
Deliver a formal joint venture agreement structured as follows:

**JOINT VENTURE AGREEMENT**

Preamble with effective date and party identification

1. **PURPOSE** — State the joint venture's purpose and project description

2. **CONTRIBUTIONS** — Outline each partner's contributions (capital, assets, services, intellectual property)

3. **MANAGEMENT** — Define the management committee structure, representative appointments, and decision-making voting thresholds

4. **PROFIT SHARING** — Specify profit distribution percentages between partners

5. **DISPUTE RESOLUTION** — Detail the dispute resolution process (good faith negotiation followed by specified method)

6. **TERM AND TERMINATION** — State commencement date, duration, termination conditions, and notice requirements

7. **GENERAL PROVISIONS** — Include applicable law and jurisdiction, assignment restrictions, notice procedures, and entire agreement clause

8. **SIGNATURE BLOCK** — Provide execution spaces for both partners with date lines

Use precise legal terminology, numbered subsections, and formal contract language throughout.
```
