# Affiliate Marketing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Affiliate Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that generates a complete, actionable affiliate marketing plan for solopreneurs and businesses looking to create passive income streams. This affiliate marketing prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and outputs a numbered, step-by-step strategy covering content development (product reviews, comparisons, tutorials), affiliate network selection, partnership opportunities, tracking and performance measurement tools, conversion rate optimization tactics, and budget allocation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a customized roadmap that balances revenue goals with audience trust and value delivery. Whether you're launching your first affiliate program or refining an existing one, this prompt generates strategies grounded in your specific niche, audience, and business model. Use it when you need a structured plan to monetize content, evaluate which affiliate networks align with your offerings, or optimize existing affiliate efforts for better conversion rates. ● Creates actionable content strategies for product reviews, comparisons, and tutorials that drive affiliate revenue ● Identifies suitable affiliate networks and partnership opportunities based on your business profile and audience ● Recommends tracking tools and performance metrics to measure ROI and optimize campaigns ● Provides conversion rate optimization tactics and budget allocation guidance specific to your resources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert affiliate marketing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step affiliate marketing strategy that generates passive income through product or service promotion. The plan must maximize revenue potential while maintaining authenticity and delivering genuine value to the audience.

## Context
Business and audience details:
{{business-context}}

Create a strategy tailored to this business profile that addresses:
- Content development (product reviews, comparisons, tutorials)
- Affiliate network selection and partnership opportunities
- Tracking and performance measurement tools
- Conversion rate optimization tactics
- Budget allocation and resource prioritization

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with main steps and relevant sub-steps. Each step should be actionable and specific to the provided business context.
```
