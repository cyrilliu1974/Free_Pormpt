# F1 Action Shot Generator for Midjourney

## 簡介

The F1 Action Shot Generator for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that creates dynamic racing photography with professional camera simulation and motion effects. This motorsport photography prompt for Midjourney produces images of vehicles cornering at speed with tire smoke, background motion blur, and crisp subject focus. It simulates a 200mm f/2.8 telephoto lens at 1/4000s shutter speed, replicating the technical setup professional sports photographers use at racetracks. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, and accepts two variables: {{vehicle}} for the car or motorcycle model, and {{lighting}} for track conditions like golden hour, floodlit night racing, or overcast daylight. Use it to generate poster artwork, social media content for racing teams, editorial illustrations, or concept visuals for motorsport marketing campaigns. ● Simulates professional telephoto lens settings (200mm f/2.8, 1/4000s) for authentic sports photography aesthetics ● Produces motion blur in the background while maintaining sharp focus on the vehicle, mimicking panning technique ● Includes tire smoke and cornering dynamics that convey speed and g-force ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio suitable for posters, YouTube thumbnails, and landscape displays ## Prompt

```
High-speed action shot of {{vehicle}} cornering at full speed, tire smoke, motion blur in background, crisp focus on the vehicle itself. 200mm f/2.8 telephoto, 1/4000s shutter, {{lighting}}. --chaos 10 --ar 16:9 --exp 15 --quality 2 --stylize 400
```
