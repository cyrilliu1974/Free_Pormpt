# Marketing Automation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Marketing Automation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates scalable marketing automation plans tailored to your business, audience, and KPIs. This marketing automation strategy prompt for ChatGPT helps you build a complete plan that covers every stage of the customer lifecycle - from awareness and lead generation through nurturing, conversion, retention, and advocacy. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, the prompt produces a markdown table that pairs each funnel stage with specific objectives, proven tactics, and recommended automation tools. You provide your business context, current challenges, and key performance indicators, and the AI returns 6-10 cohesive strategy components that address lead management, lifecycle optimization, and ROI improvement. Real-world use cases include SaaS onboarding sequences, e-commerce cart-abandonment workflows, and B2B lead-scoring systems. Marketing leaders, growth teams, and automation specialists use this prompt when they need a structured, scalable plan that aligns tools and tactics with business goals and budget constraints. ● Maps objectives, tactics, and tools across awareness, consideration, conversion, retention, and advocacy stages ● Incorporates modern automation platforms while respecting budget and technical constraints ● Ensures every strategy component is measurable, scalable, and aligned with industry best practices ● Produces a clean markdown table format that teams can immediately use for planning and implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in marketing automation and customer lifecycle optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive marketing automation strategy that streamlines lead generation, nurturing, and conversion processes. Create a scalable, measurable plan that maximizes efficiency and ROI across the entire customer journey—from initial awareness through post-purchase engagement.

## Context
Business and audience: {{business-and-audience}}

Current challenges and constraints: {{challenges-and-constraints}}

Key performance indicators: {{kpi}}

## Requirements
- Address each stage of the marketing funnel
- Incorporate modern automation tools and tactics
- Ensure strategies are measurable and scalable
- Align with current industry best practices
- Consider budget parameters when recommending tools

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Objectives | Tactics | Tools |
|------------|---------|-------|

Each row should represent one cohesive strategy component addressing a specific funnel stage or business need. Include 6-10 comprehensive rows covering awareness, consideration, conversion, retention, and advocacy phases.
```
