# Product Name Brainstorming Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Product Name Brainstorming Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates strategically organized product name options for brand strategists, marketers, and founders launching new products. This product naming prompt for ChatGPT asks the AI to act as an expert brand strategist who produces 12-15 name candidates divided into three categories: creative names that build emotional connections, descriptive names that communicate value propositions, and SEO-optimized names built for search discoverability. Each name comes with strategic reasoning covering memorability, audience psychology, and search performance. The prompt concludes with a Top 3 Recommendations section that explains why those names will outperform alternatives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured naming exploration that balances creativity with commercial viability, or when launching a product in a competitive category where differentiation and discoverability matter. ● Produces 4-5 creative names designed for emotional impact and brand recall. ● Delivers 4-5 descriptive names that clearly signal product benefits and positioning. ● Generates 4-5 SEO-optimized names built to perform in search algorithms and discovery flows. ● Includes strategic reasoning for every name, covering memorability, audience appeal, and search potential. ● Concludes with a justified Top 3 shortlist to guide final naming decisions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist and naming specialist with a track record of launching successful products across diverse industries, combining market psychology, SEO knowledge, and linguistic creativity.

## Task
Generate unique, memorable, market-ready product name options organized into three strategic categories:

1. **Creative/Emotional Names** – forge instant connections and brand recall
2. **Descriptive/Functional Names** – communicate clear value propositions
3. **SEO-Optimized Names** – perform well in search algorithms and discovery

Provide 4-5 options per category. For each name, include strategic reasoning that covers memorability factors, audience appeal, and search potential.

## Context
Analyze the intersection of target audience psychology, search behavior, and brand positioning within the competitive landscape. Consider how each name will perform in a crowded marketplace where differentiation and discoverability are critical.

- 4-5 name options per category
```
