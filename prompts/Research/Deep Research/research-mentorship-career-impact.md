# Research Mentorship Career Impact Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Research Mentorship Career Impact Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces evidence-based reports on how mentorship drives career progression in any professional field. This mentorship research prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to investigate the measurable effects of mentor-mentee relationships on career advancement, compile actionable best practices for both parties, and organize findings into a clear report with a side-by-side comparison table and proper source citations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is particularly useful for HR professionals designing mentorship programs, academic researchers studying professional development, career coaches building evidence-based frameworks, and organizational leaders evaluating the ROI of mentorship initiatives. ● Produces key findings summarizing mentorship's measurable impact on career growth and advancement timelines ● Generates a three-column table organizing best practices by mentorship aspect, with separate guidance for mentors and mentees ● Includes properly formatted source citations to support academic integrity and credibility ● Adapts to any field variable, from healthcare and engineering to finance and education ## Prompt

```

You are an expert researcher and analyst who investigates complex topics, identifies key insights, and presents findings in clear, well-organized formats.

## Task
Research the impact of mentorship on career advancement in {{field}}, identify best practices for both mentors and mentees, and present your findings in a structured report with proper source citations.

## Output Requirements

**Structure your response as follows:**

### Mentorship Impact on Career Advancement in [field]

**Key Findings:**
- [Finding 1]
- [Finding 2]
- [Finding 3]
- [Continue as needed]

**Mentorship Best Practices:**

| Mentorship Aspect | Best Practices for Mentors | Best Practices for Mentees |
|-------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| [Aspect 1] | - [Practice 1]<br>- [Practice 2] | - [Practice 1]<br>- [Practice 2] |
| [Aspect 2] | - [Practice 1]<br>- [Practice 2] | - [Practice 1]<br>- [Practice 2] |
| [Aspect 3] | - [Practice 1]<br>- [Practice 2] | - [Practice 1]<br>- [Practice 2] |

**Sources:**
1. [Full citation]
2. [Full citation]
3. [Full citation]

**Guidelines:**
- Base all findings on thorough research and analysis
- Focus on actionable insights and recommendations
- Use proper citation format for all sources
- Ensure the table clearly organizes best practices by mentorship aspect
- Present information in an easy-to-understand overview suitable for both mentors and mentees
```
