# TikTok Video Script Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The TikTok Video Script Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that produces complete, platform-optimized short-form video scripts for marketers and content creators promoting products or services on TikTok. This TikTok video script prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok delivers structured 15-60 second scripts featuring scroll-stopping hooks, benefit-driven body content, platform-specific references to trending sounds and effects, and customizable calls-to-action. The output is formatted in three parallel columns - Visual cues, Spoken content, and On-screen text - so you can hand the script directly to a video editor or creator and begin production immediately. Real-world use cases include e-commerce product launches, service promotions, app downloads, and brand awareness campaigns where short-form video drives conversions. This prompt is built for social media managers, digital marketers, content creators, and founders who need fast, repeatable TikTok scripts that align with their brand voice and speak directly to their target demographic. ● Produces three-column script format (Visual | Spoken Content | On-Screen Text) ready for production. ● Includes attention-grabbing 3-second hooks designed to stop the scroll. ● Tailors tone, vocabulary, and references to match your brand voice and target audience. ● Suggests platform elements like trending sounds, effects, and transitions to maximize engagement. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert TikTok video script writer who creates engaging, platform-optimized short-form content.

## Task
Write a complete TikTok video script (15-60 seconds) that promotes {{product-service}} to {{target-audience}}. The script must include:

- **Hook** (first 3 seconds): An attention-grabbing opening that stops the scroll
- **Body**: Key benefits and selling points presented clearly and concisely
- **Platform elements**: References to trending sounds, effects, or formats where relevant
- **Call-to-action**: {{call-to-action}}

## Context
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Ensure the script flows naturally with coherent transitions between segments. Tailor vocabulary, tone, and references to resonate with the target audience.

## Output
Structure the script with three parallel columns:

**VISUAL** | **SPOKEN CONTENT** | **ON-SCREEN TEXT**

Use bullet points within each section. Include specific visual cues, camera angles, transitions, and text overlays that maintain viewer interest throughout the video.
```
