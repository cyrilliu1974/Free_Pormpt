# Feature Article Analysis Generator for Innovations

## 簡介

The Feature Article Analysis Generator for Innovations is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive feature articles examining breakthrough innovations and their industry effects for journalists, content strategists, and business analysts. This feature article prompt for ChatGPT walks you through creating structured analysis pieces that trace an innovation's development timeline, explain its disruption of existing practices, and project future applications. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, guiding the model to write with analytical rigor and narrative clarity using dependency grammar principles to ensure coherent, logically flowing prose. Real use cases include technology journalism, industry reports, thought leadership content, and market analysis pieces that require both depth and accessibility. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform technical innovation details into engaging feature stories that balance expert insight with readability for a general business audience. ● Produces complete feature articles with headline, lead paragraph, development timeline, industry impact analysis, and future outlook sections ● Structures analysis around the innovation's problem-solution fit, disruption of incumbent practices, and economic implications ● Guides placement of expert and innovator quotes throughout the narrative to validate key points ● Applies dependency grammar principles to maintain sentence clarity when explaining technical concepts to non-specialist readers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert feature article writer producing in-depth analysis of innovations and their industry impact. Write with clarity, analytical rigor, and narrative engagement.

## Task
Write a comprehensive feature article tracing the development, impact, and future outlook of a specific innovation. Structure your prose using dependency grammar principles to ensure clear, coherent sentences that guide the reader logically through complex ideas.

## Context
{{innovation-and-industry}}

Provide the innovation name, the industry it operates within, the problem it solves, and any key context about market conditions or incumbent practices it disrupts.

## Output
Deliver a feature article with the following structure:

**Headline**  
Attention-grabbing statement highlighting the innovation's breakthrough nature.

**Lead Paragraph**  
Engaging summary capturing the innovation, its significance, and why it matters now.

**Innovation Overview**  
- Clear description of what the innovation is and its purpose  
- Industry context and the specific problem it addresses  

**Development Process**  
- Timeline showing key milestones and breakthroughs  
- Challenges encountered and how they were overcome  
- Quote(s) from innovators reflecting on the development journey  

**Industry Impact**  
- How it disrupts existing practices  
- Benefits for consumers and businesses  
- Economic implications and market projections  
- Quote(s) from industry experts on its significance  

**Future Prospects**  
- Planned enhancements and next iterations  
- Potential applications in adjacent industries  
- Long-term vision  
- Quote(s) from innovators or experts on future direction  

**Conclusion**  
Recap the innovation's importance and close with a thought-provoking question or forward-looking statement.

**Article Requirements:**
- Use standard prose paragraphs with **bold section headings** and *italics for quotes*
- Explain technical terms clearly for a general business audience
- Base analysis on verifiable information and credible expert perspectives
- Maintain analytical depth while remaining accessible
```
