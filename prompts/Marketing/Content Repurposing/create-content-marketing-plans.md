# Content Marketing Plan Generator With Strategy Framework

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, actionable content plans for entrepreneurs and marketers who need differentiated angles on any topic. This content marketing prompt for ChatGPT accepts three variables - your content idea, target marketing channel, and desired complexity level - and returns a hierarchical outline formatted as a table of contents with 2–4 sub-headings under each main heading, plus brief starter ideas to accelerate execution. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Unlike generic outlines, the prompt instructs the model to prioritize unconventional, often-overlooked angles that differentiate your content from typical industry voices, and it adapts structure and topic selection to the marketing channel you specify (short-form insights for Twitter, long-form essays for Medium, video scripts for YouTube). Whether you're planning a blog series, social media campaign, or newsletter sequence, the output gives you a clear roadmap so you can move straight into drafting instead of staring at a blank page. Reach for this prompt whenever you have a content idea but need a strategic structure that stands out. ● Outputs a hierarchical table of contents with main headings, sub-headings, and starter ideas for each section. ● Tailors format and topic selection to the marketing channel - blog, Twitter, LinkedIn, Medium, email, or video. ● Prioritizes unconventional angles and overlooked perspectives to differentiate your content from competitors. ● Accepts three variables: content idea, marketing channel, and complexity level (beginner, intermediate, advanced). ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional content strategist who creates structured, differentiated content plans for entrepreneurs.

## Task
Generate a detailed content plan for the supplied content idea and marketing channel. The plan will serve as an actionable structure to simplify content creation.

## Context
**Content idea:** {{content-idea}}

**Marketing channel:** {{marketing-channel}}

**Desired complexity level:** {{complexity-level}}

## Requirements
- Organize as a table of contents with headings and sub-headings
- Prioritize unconventional, often-overlooked angles that differentiate the content from typical industry voices
- Tailor format and topics to the specified marketing channel (e.g., short-form insights for Twitter, long-form essays for Medium; do not mix channel-specific topics unless the content idea explicitly addresses that channel)
- Include 2–4 sub-headings under each heading
- Provide a brief starter idea for each sub-heading to accelerate execution
- Use concise, clear language

## Output
Return a structured content plan formatted in Markdown using this pattern:

**Heading topic 1**
- Subheading topic 1
  - Subheading topic 1 starter ideas
- Subheading topic 2
  - Subheading topic 2 starter ideas

**Heading topic 2**
- Subheading topic 1
  - ...
```
