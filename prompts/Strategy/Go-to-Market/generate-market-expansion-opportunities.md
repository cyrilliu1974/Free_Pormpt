# Market Expansion Opportunity Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Expansion Opportunity Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers data-informed growth strategies, market opportunity assessments, and detailed go-to-market plans for businesses evaluating new markets, customer segments, or geographic expansion. This market expansion prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through a structured strategic analysis that examines emerging trends, competitive landscapes, and untapped market opportunities. It produces a multi-section report covering current business assessment, market sizing (TAM/SAM), growth forecasts, entry strategies, risk mitigation plans, and phased implementation timelines. Business strategists, growth teams, and executives use it to evaluate expansion into new geographic regions, customer segments, or industry verticals with specific recommendations on positioning, pricing, distribution channels, and marketing tactics tailored to their unique value proposition. The prompt runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring three variables: business context (model, products, market position), target markets under consideration, and the company's unique value proposition. ● Identifies up to three high-potential markets with sizing, growth rates, key drivers, and recommended entry strategies ● Delivers market prioritization frameworks, product-market fit assessments, and complete go-to-market plans covering positioning, pricing, sales channels, and marketing tactics ● Provides risk analysis with specific mitigation strategies for regulatory, competitive, and operational challenges ● Produces phased action plans with milestones, ownership assignments, and 6-12 month timelines plus expected revenue impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business expansion expert specializing in market analysis, trend forecasting, and growth strategy development.

## Task
Analyze the business landscape, identify high-potential growth opportunities, and develop a detailed, actionable go-to-market strategy to drive expansion and revenue growth.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include: current business model, products/services offered, existing market position, competitive advantages, and any constraints (budget, timeline, resources).

{{target-markets}}

Specify: geographic regions, customer segments, or industry verticals under consideration for expansion.

{{unique-value-proposition}}

Describe: what differentiates this business from competitors and creates customer value.

## Output
Structure your analysis in the following sections:

### 1. Current Business Overview
Summarize the business's present state, core strengths, and market position.

### 2. Market Analysis
- Emerging trends affecting the target markets
- Consumer behavior insights and unmet needs
- Competitive landscape and white space opportunities

### 3. Opportunity Identification
For up to 3 high-potential markets, provide:
- **Market name and segment**
- **Market size** (TAM/SAM)
- **Growth rate** (CAGR, trajectory)
- **Key drivers** (what's fueling growth)
- **Recommended entry strategy** (partnerships, direct sales, digital-first, etc.)

### 4. Go-to-Market Strategy
- **Market prioritization** (rank by attractiveness and feasibility)
- **Positioning and differentiation** (how to stand out)
- **Product-market fit** (adaptations needed)
- **Pricing strategy** (model and rationale)
- **Sales and distribution** (channels and partnerships)
- **Marketing and promotion** (acquisition tactics and messaging)

### 5. Key Risks and Mitigations
Identify 3-5 critical risks (regulatory, competitive, operational) with specific mitigation strategies.

### 6. Expected Business Impact
Estimate potential revenue contribution, customer acquisition, and timeline to profitability for each opportunity.

### 7. Next Steps and Timeline
Provide a phased action plan with milestones, owners, and target dates for the next 6-12 months.

---

**Format:** Use markdown headers, bullet points, and tables where helpful (e.g., market comparison matrices, priority scoring).

**Quality bar:** Deliver specific, data-informed insights. Avoid generic advice—tailor every recommendation to the business context provided.
```
