# Landing Page Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Landing Page Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates narrative-driven landing pages designed to convert browsers into customers through strategic storytelling. This landing page copy prompt for ChatGPT structures each variation as a customer transformation story with clear beats: relatable challenge, brand as guide, transformation through product, vivid new reality, and story-driven call to action. You provide business context - product category, brand origin, ideal customer profile, and unique positioning - and the prompt produces three complete landing page variations using distinct psychological approaches: emotional appeal, authority building, and aspirational positioning. Each variation includes headline, subheadline, opening scene, discovery moment, transformation narrative, social proof positioned as mini-stories, vision of customer's new reality, and calls to action that complete the narrative arc. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is built for conversion copywriters, marketing teams, founders, and agencies who need landing pages that move beyond feature lists into emotional resonance. ● Produces three complete landing page variations, each with a different psychological strategy - emotional appeal, authority building, and aspirational positioning - while maintaining authentic narrative flow. ● Structures every page as a customer journey with specific story beats: visceral challenge, brand as mentor, product as transformation catalyst, social proof as plot points, and calls to action that feel like natural next chapters. ● Ensures all copy avoids generic marketing language by requiring specific, sensory details and authentic brand origin stories that connect to the customer's emotional arc. ● Integrates testimonials and social proof as mini-stories that advance the narrative rather than interrupt it, building trust without breaking the storytelling flow. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert conversion copywriter specializing in narrative-driven landing pages. You apply story structure principles to transform features into emotional journeys that guide visitors from awareness to action.

## Task
Create three distinct landing page copy variations that use strategic storytelling to convert browsers into customers. Each variation employs a different psychological approach while maintaining authentic narrative flow.

## Context
Effective landing pages treat the customer as the protagonist in a transformation story. Generic benefit statements and feature lists fail because they lack emotional resonance. Your copy will weave the brand's authentic story with the customer's journey, creating a narrative arc from problem to solution. Structure each page as a story with clear beats: relatable challenge, brand as guide, transformation through product, vivid new reality, and story-driven call to action.

{{business-context}} should include: product/service category, brand origin and mission, ideal customer profile (demographics, psychographics, desires, challenges), and any unique positioning.

## Output
Deliver three complete landing page variations, each following this structure:

**[VARIATION TYPE] - [STORY TITLE]**

*Headline:* [Story-driven hook]

*Subheadline:* [Deepen intrigue]

*Opening Scene:* Customer challenge narrative with specific, visceral details (2-3 paragraphs)

*The Discovery:* How the brand enters as guide/mentor (2 paragraphs)

*The Transformation:* Product as catalyst for believable yet aspirational change (2-3 paragraphs)

*Social Proof Moments:* Testimonials positioned as mini-stories that advance the narrative

*The New Chapter:* Vision of the transformed customer reality (1-2 paragraphs)

*Call to Action:* Button text and supporting copy that complete the narrative arc

---

### The Three Variations:

1. **Emotional Appeal Version**: Focus on feelings, desires, and psychological transformation
2. **Authority Building Version**: Emphasize expertise and trust woven into the narrative
3. **Aspirational Positioning Version**: Highlight status, achievement, and identity transformation

### Quality Standards:
- Every element serves the story—no generic marketing language
- Customer challenges are specific and visceral, not vague
- Brand origin feels authentic and connects to customer journey
- Social proof integrated as plot points, not interruptions
- CTAs feel like natural next chapters, not pressure tactics
- Voice: conversational storyteller, never hard-selling
- Use sensory details and specific moments over abstractions
```
