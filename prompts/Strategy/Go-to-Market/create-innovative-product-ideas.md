# Product Innovation and Launch Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Product Innovation and Launch Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates novel product concepts and detailed go-to-market plans for entrepreneurs, product managers, and market strategists. This product innovation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through systematic ideation and strategic planning by generating 3-5 unique product solutions tailored to your target market, recommending the strongest concept based on market fit, and building a complete launch strategy covering positioning, pricing, distribution channels, marketing tactics, platform recommendations, and measurable success metrics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured output that transforms market problems and launch constraints into actionable product roadmaps. Whether you're validating a new business idea, entering an unfamiliar market, or planning a product launch under budget and timeline constraints, this prompt produces concrete guidance that bridges creativity and execution. ● Produces 3-5 product concepts with feature descriptions and value propositions tailored to your specific market and problem statement ● Recommends the strongest concept based on market fit analysis and provides justification for prioritization ● Delivers a detailed go-to-market strategy covering positioning, pricing approach, distribution channels, marketing tactics, and platform leverage ● Identifies specific tools, partnerships, and resources to maximize visibility and adoption within stated budget and timeline constraints ● Defines measurable success metrics and KPIs to track launch effectiveness and market traction ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert product innovator and market strategist.

## Task

Generate innovative product ideas and a comprehensive launch strategy for a specific market opportunity. Work through the problem systematically to create novel solutions, outline a detailed go-to-market plan, and provide actionable guidance on maximizing product visibility and adoption.

## Context

**Market & Problem**
{{market-and-problem}}
(Describe your target market, the specific problem you're solving, and what type of product you envision)

**Launch Parameters**
{{launch-constraints}}
(Include your budget constraints, timeline, and any other critical requirements or limitations)

## Output

Provide your response as a numbered list with:

1. **Product Concepts** – 3-5 unique solutions addressing the stated problem, with brief descriptions of key features and value propositions
2. **Recommended Concept** – Which product idea offers the strongest market fit and why
3. **Go-to-Market Strategy** – Detailed launch plan including positioning, pricing approach, distribution channels, and marketing tactics
4. **Platform & Resource Leverage** – Specific platforms, tools, and partnerships to maximize visibility and drive adoption within budget and timeline
5. **Success Metrics** – Key performance indicators to track launch effectiveness

Use clear main sections with indented sub-points for readability.
```
