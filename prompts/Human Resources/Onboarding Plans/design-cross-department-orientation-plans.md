# Cross-Department Orientation Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Cross-Department Orientation Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates sequenced onboarding programs to break down silos and accelerate new hire integration across organizational departments. This cross-department orientation prompt for ChatGPT applies Tuckman's Team Development Model - Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing - to structure every department interaction so new employees build progressively deeper relationships and collaborative skills. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok and produces a complete orientation schedule with department profiles, interactive sessions, timing recommendations, facilitation methods, and success metrics. HR teams and organizational development specialists use it to replace ad-hoc introductions with a cohesive plan that addresses departmental silos, reduces new hire overwhelm, and delivers measurable improvements in cross-team communication. It is designed for organizations onboarding multiple employees across complex structures where integration speed and collaboration quality directly impact retention and productivity. ● Maps every department interaction to Forming, Storming, Norming, and Performing stages with specific activities and expected outcomes. ● Includes department profiles that highlight goals, functions, cultural dynamics, interdependencies, and collaboration friction points. ● Outputs an executive summary, detailed interaction schedule with timing and facilitation notes, cross-departmental integration activities, and a follow-up measurement framework. ● Tailors the plan to your organizational context, orientation timeline, and new hire volume for immediate implementation. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational development specialist designing cross-department onboarding programs that accelerate integration, break down silos, and build collaborative relationships.

## Task

Create a comprehensive orientation plan that guides new hires through structured interactions with all departments. Use Tuckman's Team Development Model (Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing) to sequence activities that progressively deepen understanding and collaboration.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

The plan must address: departmental silos that hinder collaboration, new hire overwhelm from organizational complexity, and leadership expectations for measurable improvements in cross-departmental communication and trust.

Align the plan to {{orientation-timeline}} and account for {{new-hire-volume}}.

## Requirements

**Framework Application**

Sequence each department interaction through Tuckman's four stages:
- **Forming**: Initial introductions and relationship building
- **Storming**: Addressing potential conflicts, misunderstandings, or friction points
- **Norming**: Establishing shared communication patterns and working norms
- **Performing**: Collaborative activities demonstrating effective cross-departmental work

**Content for Each Department**
- Brief department profile (goals, functions, cultural dynamics)
- Interactive sessions and joint activities that encourage authentic dialogue and problem-solving
- Specific timing and facilitation methods
- Success metrics and follow-up mechanisms
- How the department's value proposition, working style, challenges, and interdependencies connect to other teams

## Output

Provide a complete orientation schedule organized by Tuckman stage:

1. **Executive Summary**: Overview of the plan's philosophy and structure
2. **Department Interaction Schedule**: For each department, specify:
   - Activities mapped to each Tuckman stage
   - Duration and timing
   - Facilitation approach
   - Expected outcomes and success indicators
3. **Cross-Departmental Integration Points**: Activities involving multiple teams simultaneously
4. **Follow-Up and Measurement Framework**: How progress will be tracked and reinforced

Format with clear headings, tables for schedules, and actionable detail suitable for immediate implementation.
```
