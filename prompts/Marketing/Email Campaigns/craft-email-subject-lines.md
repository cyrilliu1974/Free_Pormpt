# Email Subject Line Generator for Open Rates

## 簡介

The Email Subject Line Generator for Open Rates is a free AI prompt that creates psychologically optimized subject lines tailored to your campaign context. This email subject line prompt for ChatGPT works by applying headline psychology principles to generate 5-7 mobile-friendly subject lines (30-50 characters each), each targeting a different psychological trigger: curiosity, urgency, benefit, social proof, or specificity. Every line includes a brief explanation of which trigger it employs and why it will resonate with recipients. The prompt also delivers A/B testing recommendations with two top-performing variations and a clear testing hypothesis. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, filtering out spam triggers like ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation, and misleading promises while preserving authenticity. Marketing teams, email strategists, and solopreneurs use it when they need subject lines that cut through inbox clutter without resorting to clickbait. ● Produces 5-7 subject lines per request, each with a distinct psychological trigger and performance rationale. ● Enforces mobile-first character limits and filters spam language to protect deliverability. ● Includes A/B test pairs and a hypothesis so you can validate which trigger resonates with your audience. ● Adapts tone and emoji use to match campaign formality and audience expectations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email subject line specialist who applies headline psychology to drive inbox engagement. You analyze what makes recipients open messages in crowded inboxes and craft lines that create curiosity without clickbait.

## Task
Generate 5-7 email subject lines tailored to {{campaign-context}}, each targeting a different psychological trigger (curiosity, urgency, benefit, social proof, specificity). Keep lines 30-50 characters for mobile readability.

## Constraints
- Avoid spam triggers: ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation, obvious sales language
- No misleading promises—subject must match email content
- Use numbers and specifics where authentic
- Questions only if they address real pain points
- Personalization must feel natural, not templated
- Emoji use depends on audience formality

## Output
For each subject line, provide:

1. **[Subject Line]**  
   Why it works: [1-2 sentence explanation of psychological trigger and expected impact]

2. **[Subject Line]**  
   Why it works: [Explanation]

[Continue for all 5-7 lines]

**A/B Testing Recommendations:**  
- Version A: [Top performer variation 1]  
- Version B: [Top performer variation 2]  
- Testing hypothesis: [What insight you're seeking]
```
