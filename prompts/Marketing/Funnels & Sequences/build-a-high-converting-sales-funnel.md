# Sales Funnel Builder Prompt for Conversion Copy

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Builder Prompt for Conversion Copy is a free AI prompt that designs complete multi-stage sales funnels with strategically crafted copy for businesses seeking higher conversion rates. This sales funnel prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your target customer, market position, and unique selling proposition to build a four-stage funnel - awareness, interest, consideration, and decision - where each piece of copy depends on and reinforces previous messaging to create persuasive momentum. You provide your product or service details, ideal customer profile with industry context and competitors, and your USP; the prompt returns strategic approaches, key messaging, recommended channels, engagement tactics, differentiation strategies, proof elements, objection handling, and conversion mechanisms tailored to each stage. Marketing teams use it to map customer journey touchpoints, copywriters use it to ensure logical progression in campaigns, and founders use it to structure launch sequences that guide cold prospects to purchase decisions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Analyzes target audience pain points and decision triggers to align funnel stages with customer readiness ● Produces copy for all four funnel stages - awareness messaging and channels, interest engagement tactics, consideration proof elements and objection handling, decision calls-to-action with urgency mechanisms ● Applies dependency grammar principles so each sentence and claim rests on established context, creating a cohesive persuasive arc ● Integrates your unique selling proposition into stage-appropriate messaging that differentiates against named competitors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales funnel strategist specializing in conversion optimization.

## Task
Design a complete sales funnel that guides prospects from awareness through decision. Structure copy for each stage using dependency grammar principles—ensuring each element builds logically on the previous to create persuasive momentum toward conversion.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Customer & Market:** {{customer-and-market}}
(Include ideal customer profile, industry context, and main competitors)

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Process
1. Analyze the target audience's core needs, pain points, and decision triggers
2. Map each funnel stage to customer readiness
3. Craft copy where each sentence/claim depends on and reinforces prior context

## Output
Deliver a structured sales funnel with four stages:

### Awareness Stage
- Strategic approach
- Key messaging and copy
- Recommended channels/formats

### Interest Stage
- Engagement tactics
- Copy that deepens relevance
- Content or offer type

### Consideration Stage
- Differentiation strategy
- Proof elements and copy
- Objection handling

### Decision Stage
- Conversion mechanism
- Closing copy and calls-to-action
- Urgency or incentive elements

For each stage, show how the copy builds dependency: later claims rest on earlier established context, creating a cohesive persuasive arc.
```
