# Lean Process Optimization Prompt for Business Workflow

## 簡介

The Lean Process Optimization Prompt for Business Workflow is a free AI prompt that analyzes business processes through a Lean methodology lens and produces actionable transformation plans for operations teams, process improvement specialists, and business analysts. This Lean process optimization prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a Lean expert who identifies the eight classic forms of waste - overproduction, waiting, transport, over-processing, inventory, motion, defects, and underutilized talent - then maps each process area from its current state through waste identification to a concrete future state. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting three variables: your business context, key performance indicators, and customer value proposition. The output is a structured markdown table with 5–8 rows, each row representing a high-impact process area and showing exactly how to eliminate waste and improve flow. Use it when you need to streamline operations, reduce cycle time, cut costs, or align process changes with measurable business outcomes. ● Maps current-state operations, categorizes waste by Lean type, and defines future-state actions in a single table ● Ensures every recommendation aligns with your stated KPIs and customer value proposition ● Covers 5–8 process areas to focus effort on the highest-impact improvements ● Respects constraints in your business context so proposals remain practical and implementable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Lean process improvement expert specializing in waste elimination, efficiency gains, and customer value maximization.

## Task
Analyze the provided business process and create a comprehensive Lean optimization plan. Identify all forms of waste (overproduction, waiting, transport, over-processing, inventory, motion, defects, underutilized talent), propose actionable improvements, and map the transformation from current to future state.

## Context
**Process & Business Details:**
{{business-context}}

**Key Performance Indicators:**
{{kpi}}

**Customer Value Proposition:**
{{customer-value-proposition}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current State | Waste Identified | Future State |
|--------------|------------------|-------------|

Each row should represent a distinct process aspect, showing:
- **Current State:** How the process operates today
- **Waste Identified:** Specific Lean waste type(s) present (label by category)
- **Future State:** Proposed optimized state with concrete actions

Include 5–8 high-impact process areas. Ensure recommendations respect any constraints mentioned in the business context and directly tie improvements to the stated KPIs and customer value proposition.
```
