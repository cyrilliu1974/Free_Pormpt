# Luxury Packaging Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Packaging Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates campaign-quality jewelry box visuals for premium brands and creative professionals. This luxury packaging mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces photorealistic still life scenes featuring geometric jewelry boxes with brushed metal exteriors, velvet-lined interiors, and natural warm sunlight casting crisp shadows - perfect for website hero sections, lookbooks, and brand campaigns without expensive studio shoots. The prompt specifies mechanical precision in hinged or sliding lids, muted interior textiles in olive or sand beige, and Mediterranean-inspired color palettes of sand gold, ocean blue, and seafoam green. Use it when you need editorial-quality packaging imagery that communicates sophistication through composition, light, and material detail. ● Produces geometric jewelry boxes with brushed metal, ceramic, or matte lacquer finishes and textured velvet interiors in muted earth tones. ● Captures natural low-angle sunlight with specular highlights on metal and soft interior glow, mimicking high-end jewelry campaigns. ● Specifies top-down composition with generous negative space, asymmetrical placement, and crisp shadow detail for editorial impact. ● Outputs high-resolution images suitable for web, print, and marketing collateral with no people, no external logos, and clean minimalist styling. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic editorial still life of a luxury jewelry packaging concept for {{brand-name}}. Geometric jewelry box with soft-edged rectangle or rounded cube shape, brushed metal exterior with ceramic or matte lacquer interior, sliding lid or hinged top with clean mechanical precision. Interior lined with textured velvet or microfiber in muted olive, sand beige, or seafoam green. A ring or small luxury object is centered inside the open box, slightly angled to catch light. Minimal brand name or initials engraved inside lid only.

Scene: {{scene-description}} surface with natural props creating a calm, refined, sun-warmed mood inspired by high-end jewelry campaigns and Mediterranean summer minimalism.

Lighting: natural low-angle warm sunlight casting crisp shadows on the surface, subtle specular highlights on metal, soft glow illuminating the box interior.

Color palette: sand gold, deep ocean blue, warm brass as dominant tones; seafoam green and soft ivory accents. Earthy luxury tones only, no bright or neon colors.

Composition: top-down camera angle with slight perspective, generous negative space around the hero box, asymmetrical but intentional object placement.

High resolution, campaign-quality image. No people, no visible brand logos outside the box, no literal themed decorations on packaging.
```
