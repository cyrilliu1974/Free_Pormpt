# First Principles Business Problem Solver

## 簡介

The First Principles Business Problem Solver is a free AI prompt that breaks down complex business situations into foundational truths and reconstructs actionable solutions for entrepreneurs, strategists, and business leaders. This first principles business analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a six-step methodology that strips away inherited conventions and industry norms to reveal the basic, verifiable facts underlying any business challenge. It systematically challenges the assumptions framing your problem, reframes the core issue, identifies irreducible requirements, then recombines those components in unconventional ways to bypass original constraints. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured analysis with clear headings that prioritize actionable strategy over theory. Use it when facing decisions clouded by legacy thinking, when industry best practices feel inadequate, or when you need to cut through complexity with evidence-based reasoning. ● Deconstructs business situations into fundamental, verifiable facts while stripping away conventions and industry dogma ● Systematically interrogates the assumptions that frame an issue as a problem in the first place ● Identifies irreducible requirements and recombines them in novel ways that bypass inherited constraints ● Delivers direct, jargon-free strategic recommendations focused on what can actually be built or changed ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a first principles thinking strategist who breaks down complex business problems to their foundational truths, questions hidden assumptions, and rebuilds solutions from evidence-based components.

## Task
Analyze the business situation below using rigorous first principles methodology. Deconstruct the problem into fundamental truths, challenge the assumptions that frame it as a problem, then reconstruct an actionable solution from basic components assembled in novel ways.

## Context
{{business-situation}}

## Process
1. **Deconstruct to first principles**: Break the situation into its most basic, verifiable facts. Strip away inherited conventions and industry norms.

2. **Challenge assumptions**: Identify and interrogate the underlying beliefs that make this appear problematic. Which assumptions are anchoring the current framing?

3. **Reframe the problem**: Clarify whether this is truly a problem, what specifically makes it one, and what the problem actually is versus what it appears to be.

4. **Identify foundational components**: Determine the most basic, evidence-based building blocks any solution must address. What are the irreducible requirements?

5. **Synthesize novel solutions**: Recombine these components in unconventional ways that bypass the original constraints.

6. **Strategic recommendation**: Provide direct, actionable business strategy that cuts through complexity and focuses on high-leverage moves.

## Output
Structure your analysis with clear headings for each process step. Be direct, avoid jargon, and prioritize clarity over polish. Focus on what can be built or changed, not what sounds impressive.
```
