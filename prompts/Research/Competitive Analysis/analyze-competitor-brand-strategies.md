# Competitor Brand Positioning Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor Brand Positioning Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches competitor brand strategies and surfaces concrete differentiation opportunities for brand strategists and marketers. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines how rival brands position themselves within your industry, documenting their target segments, value propositions, brand voice, and marketing channels in a structured markdown table. For each competitor, the prompt identifies specific gaps, underserved needs, and angles your brand can exploit to stand out. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and closes with a summary of the most promising differentiation opportunities. Real use cases include pre-launch positioning research, rebrand planning, and quarterly competitive reviews. Reach for this prompt when you need to map the competitive landscape and find white space for your brand to own a distinct market position. ● Maps each competitor's target audience, core value proposition, brand voice, and primary marketing channels in one view ● Identifies specific market gaps, underserved customer needs, and strategic angles your brand can claim ● Outputs a markdown table for easy comparison plus a summary of top differentiation opportunities ● Customizable by industry, competitor list, your target audience, and your unique selling proposition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a brand strategist conducting a competitive positioning analysis.

## Task
Analyze competitor positioning strategies within {{industry}} to identify concrete differentiation opportunities for the client brand. Research the specified competitors' current market positions, examining their target audiences, unique selling propositions, brand voice, and primary marketing channels. Map gaps in the market where the client can establish a distinct position.

## Context
**Industry:** {{industry}}

**Competitor brands to analyze:** {{competitor-brands}}

**Client's target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Client's unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Competitor | Positioning Strategy | Opportunities for Differentiation |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|

For each competitor, document:
- **Positioning Strategy:** Their target segment, core value proposition, brand voice/tone, and key marketing channels
- **Opportunities for Differentiation:** Specific gaps, underserved needs, or angles the client brand can exploit to stand out

After the table, provide a brief summary highlighting the most promising differentiation opportunities across all competitors.
```
