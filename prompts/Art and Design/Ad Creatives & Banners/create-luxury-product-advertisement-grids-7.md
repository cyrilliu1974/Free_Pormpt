# Luxury Product Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Product Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates polished 2×2 square grid layouts for premium products across four photography-style panels. Built for image models like Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, this luxury product ad grid prompt for Midjourney constructs a single 1:1 composition with a hero shot in a minimalist interior, a comfort or lifestyle moment with natural light, a macro close-up revealing material texture and craftsmanship, and a styled interior panel integrating the product into a modern design setting. The prompt specifies professional photography lighting, neutral color palettes, high realism, and anti-clutter guardrails to ensure each panel conveys elegance and quality. Use this prompt when you need a cohesive, multi-angle advertisement that communicates both function and aspirational design in one shareable image. ● Produces a 2×2 square grid with hero shot, lifestyle scene, macro material detail, and interior styling in one image. ● Specifies natural daylight, shallow depth of field, and high-realism rendering to match editorial product photography standards. ● Accepts variables for product name, product type, and color palette to adapt the composition to any premium item. ● Includes anti-clutter and quality guardrails to avoid harsh lighting, low resolution, text overlays, and cheap textures. ## Prompt

```
2×2 square grid luxury advertisement for premium pet bed, 1:1 aspect ratio, professional interior photography style.

**Panel 1 (top-left):** {{product-name}} {{product-type}} centered in minimalist living room, soft natural daylight, modern neutral-toned interior with clean lines, elegant and design-forward mood.

**Panel 2 (top-right):** Well-groomed dog or cat resting peacefully on the bed, warm natural sunlight, cozy and loving atmosphere emphasizing comfort.

**Panel 3 (bottom-left):** Macro close-up of Italian boucle fabric texture and precision stitching, shallow depth of field, tactile and detailed, showcasing craftsmanship and quality.

**Panel 4 (bottom-right):** Pet bed styled beside designer sofa and coffee table, soft diffused daylight, modern lifestyle integration, cohesive interior styling.

Product features: Memory foam cushion, boucle exterior, orthopedic support, removable washable cover, anti-slip bottom, minimal sculptural design. {{color-palette}} palette. Refined, warm, design-conscious brand personality. Bright balanced exposure, gentle natural shadows, high realism.

Avoid: messy fur, cluttered backgrounds, cheap fabric texture, harsh flash lighting, low-resolution details, cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
