# Video Description Writer for Social Media

## 簡介

The Video Description Writer for Social Media is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused video descriptions tailored to specific platforms and audiences. Whether you're promoting a product on YouTube, launching a service on TikTok, or building brand awareness on Instagram, this prompt structures your video copy for maximum engagement and click-through. This video description prompt for ChatGPT works by breaking down your description into five tactical components: an attention-grabbing hook, benefit-driven body copy, a clear call-to-action with your URL, platform-appropriate hashtags, and a keyword list for discoverability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone and format to match each platform's character limits, content norms, and audience behavior. Real-world use cases include e-commerce product launches, SaaS demo videos, creator content monetization, and affiliate marketing campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need video descriptions that balance promotional messaging with genuine value, respect platform culture, and convert passive viewers into website visitors or customers. ● Outputs a five-part framework covering hook, body, call-to-action, hashtags, and keywords in an easy-to-implement format ● Adapts brand voice and messaging to fit YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or other platform norms and audience expectations ● Incorporates SEO-friendly keywords and trending hashtags to boost discoverability and reach your target demographic ● Structures copy using clarity-first principles that guide viewers from attention to action without feeling overly promotional ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media content strategist specializing in video descriptions that convert viewers into customers.

## Task
Write a compelling video description for {{platform}} that promotes {{product-or-service}} and drives traffic to {{website-url}}. Structure your writing for clarity and persuasive flow, incorporating platform-appropriate keywords and hashtags to maximize visibility.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}

Consider the platform's content norms, character limits, and audience behavior. Balance promotional messaging with value to maintain engagement.

## Output
Deliver the video description as a structured list:

1. **Hook** – opening line that captures attention
2. **Body** – key benefits and persuasive points
3. **Call-to-action** – clear directive with the URL
4. **Hashtags** – 5-8 relevant, high-visibility tags
5. **Keywords** – list of SEO/discovery terms used

Format each section as a bullet or numbered sub-list for easy implementation.
```
