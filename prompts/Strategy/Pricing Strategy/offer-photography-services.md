# Photography Service Package & Pricing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Photography Service Package & Pricing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive photography business offerings tailored to specific target audiences and market contexts. This photography service design prompt for ChatGPT analyzes client needs and market positioning to generate 3-5 distinct service packages, each with detailed inclusions and ideal customer profiles. It develops a justified pricing structure that balances competitiveness with profitability, suggests optional add-ons with their costs, and identifies 3-5 concrete unique selling points that differentiate your photography services from competitors. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering organized, bullet-point recommendations across three core sections: packages, pricing, and market differentiators. Photography business owners, studio managers, and freelance photographers building or refining their service offerings will find this prompt essential for strategic positioning and revenue planning. ● Generates 3-5 named photography packages with detailed service inclusions and target client descriptions ● Creates justified pricing for each package tier based on market competitiveness and profitability goals ● Produces optional add-on services with individual pricing to maximize revenue flexibility ● Identifies concrete unique selling points that distinguish your photography business from local and online competitors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert photography business consultant specializing in service packaging, competitive pricing strategy, and market positioning.

## Task
Develop a complete photography service offering that includes attractive packages, a pricing structure, and unique selling points tailored to the specified target audience.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze the target audience and potential client needs. Create a range of photography packages that offer variety and flexibility while addressing these needs. Design a competitive yet profitable pricing structure for each package. Identify unique selling points that differentiate these services from competitors in the market.

## Output
Organize your recommendations in bullet-point format with three clear sections:

**Photography Packages**
- List 3-5 distinct packages with names, what's included, and ideal client fit

**Pricing Structure**
- Price point for each package with justification
- Optional add-ons and their costs

**Unique Selling Points**
- 3-5 differentiators that set these services apart from competitors
```
