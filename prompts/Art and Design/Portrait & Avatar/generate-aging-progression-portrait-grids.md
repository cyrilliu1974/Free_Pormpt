# Aging Progression Portrait Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Aging Progression Portrait Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a sixteen-stage photographic study of human aging for artists, filmmakers, educators, and storytellers. It creates a single 4×4 grid showing the same individual at ages 1, 11, 21, 31, 41, 51, 61, 71, 81, 91, 101, 111, 121, 131, 141, and 151, maintaining extreme facial consistency across every portrait so the subject remains immediately recognizable throughout their life journey. This aging progression prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana uses realistic photographic rendering, neutral studio lighting, and a simple backdrop to focus attention on the clear, progressive shift in physical detail across infancy, youth, middle age, elder years, and hypothetical extreme longevity. Reach for this prompt when you need a visual timeline of aging for concept art, character development, educational materials about human biology, or narrative projects that span decades. ● Produces sixteen high-resolution photographic portraits in a single 4×4 grid layout, from toddler to extreme hypothetical age. ● Enforces consistent core facial structure, skin tone, and distinguishing features across all sixteen life stages for immediate recognition. ● Uses timeless clothing and uniform studio lighting to remove distractions and emphasize the aging process itself. ● Generates output suitable for character design, storytelling campaigns, biology education, and conceptual art projects exploring mortality and time. ## Prompt

```
A single 4×4 grid containing sixteen clean photographic portraits of the same {{subject}}, depicting their life from age 1 to age 151. Realistic, high-detail photographic study with steady, neutral lighting and a simple {{backdrop}}. The focus is strictly on the clear, progressive shift in age and physical detail across the life stages. MAINTAIN EXTREME CONSISTENCY in the individual's core facial structure, skin tone, and distinguishing general features across all 16 portraits for immediate recognition.

Portrait sequence (left to right, top to bottom):
Row 1: Age 1 (infant/toddler with soft skin, minimal hair) | Age 11 (pre-teen with bright eyes, youthful expression) | Age 21 (young adult at peak, smooth skin, clear definition) | Age 31 (slight maturing, defined features)

Row 2: Age 41 (early lines around eyes/mouth, robust look) | Age 51 (more pronounced lines, subtle facial volume change) | Age 61 (grey or white hair begins, visible lines, kind expression) | Age 71 (significant lines, thinner skin, deep-set eyes)

Row 3: Age 81 (sagging skin, numerous wrinkles, frail but clear-eyed) | Age 91 (thinning hair, distinct bone structure visible, deep character lines) | Age 101 (extreme aged features, delicate appearance, peaceful expression) | Age 111 (advanced aging signs, very gentle features)

Row 4: Age 121 (maximum visual effects of long lifespan, unique textures) | Age 131 (hypothetical extreme age, focus on skin texture and feature endurance) | Age 141 (maximum artistic representation of very long life) | Age 151 (preserving core identity despite time's passage)

Visual constraints: Timeless, simple clothing consistent across all squares to avoid period-specific details. Studio lighting, uniform, no harsh shadows. Natural, calm expression with direct gaze. Hyper-realistic photography, high-resolution detail.
```
