# Brand Identity Guidelines Creator Prompt

## 簡介

The Brand Identity Guidelines Creator Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive brand identity documentation for businesses, agencies, and brand strategists. This brand identity prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section guidelines document that defines brand positioning, mission, personality, audience profiles, visual identity systems (logo, color palette, typography, imagery), application examples, and strategic rationale. You supply your company name, industry, target audience, core values, and current positioning; the prompt structures these inputs into a professional brand manual with clear sections, usage rules, and examples across digital and print touchpoints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Reach for this prompt when launching a new brand, rebranding an existing company, or standardizing visual and messaging consistency across marketing channels. ● Outputs brand foundation elements including positioning statement, mission, and brand personality definitions ● Generates complete visual identity systems with logo usage rules, color palettes (hex, RGB, CMYK values), and typography hierarchies ● Provides application examples across website, social media, print, and packaging with common mistakes flagged ● Includes strategic rationale sections that explain design decisions and their connection to audience and values ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist creating a complete Brand Identity Guidelines document.

## Task
Develop comprehensive brand identity guidelines that establish a consistent, recognizable brand presence across all marketing channels. The guidelines will serve as the authoritative reference for maintaining brand integrity over time.

## Context
**Company:** {{company-name}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Core Values:** {{core-values}}
**Current Positioning:** {{current-positioning}}

## Output
Structure the guidelines document with these required sections:

### Brand Foundation
- **Positioning Statement** – Define how the brand occupies a distinct place in the market and customer minds
- **Mission Statement** – Articulate purpose and reason for existence
- **Brand Personality** – Define tone, voice, and character traits (e.g., professional, playful, authoritative)

### Audience Profile
- Demographics, psychographics, pain points, and motivations
- How the brand serves and connects with this audience

### Visual Identity System
- **Logo** – Primary version, variations, minimum size, clear space, prohibited uses
- **Color Palette** – Primary and secondary colors with hex/RGB/CMYK values and usage hierarchy
- **Typography** – Font families for headlines, body copy, and UI elements with sizing guidelines
- **Imagery Style** – Photography and illustration direction

### Application Examples
- Correct usage across 3-4 touchpoints (website, social media, print, packaging)
- Common mistakes to avoid

### Implementation Rationale
For each major decision (positioning, colors, typography), explain:
- Strategic reasoning
- Connection to values and audience
- Expected impact on brand recognition and perception

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout. Ground all recommendations in brand strategy principles while ensuring the identity is distinctive and memorable within the industry context.
```
