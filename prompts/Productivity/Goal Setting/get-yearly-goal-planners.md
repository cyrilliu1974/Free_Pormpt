# Yearly Goal Planner Template Generator

## 簡介

The Yearly Goal Planner Template Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms personal and professional objectives into an actionable 12-month roadmap with built-in accountability mechanisms. This goal setting prompt for ChatGPT takes your stated goals, target completion date, and anticipated challenges, then produces a ready-to-use template organized into four core sections: goal articulation with success criteria, concrete action steps divided into manageable milestones, progress-tracking mechanisms to measure outcomes, and scheduled reflection checkpoints to assess what's working and pivot when needed. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a formatted planner you can start using immediately. Use it when launching a new year, starting a career transition, or committing to a major personal initiative that requires sustained focus over months. ● Breaks ambitious objectives into quarterly and monthly action steps that prevent overwhelm ● Builds in scheduled reflection checkpoints so you can course-correct before falling off track ● Tailors the planner to your specific challenges, ensuring obstacles are addressed upfront ● Outputs a clean, hierarchical template with headings and bullet points you can adapt in any document tool ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert goal-setting strategist designing a comprehensive yearly goal planner.

## Task
Create a detailed, structured template that transforms the user's personal and professional objectives into an actionable year-long plan. The template must include:

- **Goal Setting** – Clear articulation of objectives with success criteria
- **Action Steps** – Concrete tasks broken into manageable milestones
- **Progress Tracking** – Mechanisms to monitor advancement and measure outcomes
- **Reflection** – Scheduled checkpoints to assess what's working, what isn't, and necessary pivots

The planner should enhance motivation, clarity, and accountability throughout the year.

## Context
**Goals:**
{{goals}}

**Target completion date:** {{completion-date}}

**Biggest challenges:** {{challenges}}

## Output
Provide a structured template format with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for each section. Make it immediately usable and adaptable to the user's specific circumstances.
```
