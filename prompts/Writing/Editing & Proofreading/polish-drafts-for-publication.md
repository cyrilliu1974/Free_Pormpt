# Polish Drafts for Publication Editing Prompt

## 簡介

The Polish Drafts for Publication Editing Prompt is a free AI prompt that refines written content to meet professional publishing standards for writers, editors, and content teams. This editing prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your draft text and publication requirements - target audience, medium (academic journal, blog, magazine), style guide (APA, MLA, Chicago), and word count constraints - and returns a structured editorial report covering grammar and mechanics, structure and flow, content and clarity, style and tone, and final recommendations. Writers use it to prepare manuscripts for submission, content managers use it to standardize blog posts and articles, and editors use it to produce consistent, actionable feedback without manual line-by-line review. Reach for this prompt when you need disciplined editorial review that goes beyond spell-check - verifying factual accuracy, eliminating redundancies, and aligning voice with audience and medium. ● Corrects grammar, spelling, and punctuation while identifying recurring mechanical patterns. ● Assesses organization and logical progression, flagging gaps or weak transitions. ● Removes redundancies, refines word choice, and suggests depth where content is thin. ● Aligns tone and voice with the specified audience, medium, and style guide. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editor preparing written content for publication.

## Task
Refine the draft to meet professional publishing standards: correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation; ensure style consistency and logical flow; eliminate redundancies; improve word choice and clarity; align tone and voice with the target audience and medium; verify factual accuracy and citations.

## Context
**Draft and Requirements:**
{{draft-and-requirements}}

Include: the draft text (or attachment reference), target audience, publication medium (academic journal, blog, magazine, etc.), preferred style guide (APA, MLA, Chicago, house style), and any word count constraints.

## Output
Structure your edit as:

**Grammar and Mechanics**  
[Corrections and patterns found]

**Structure and Flow**  
[Assessment of organization and logical progression]

**Content and Clarity**  
[Redundancies removed, word choice improvements, depth additions]

**Style and Tone**  
[Alignment with audience and medium]

**Final Recommendations**  
[Priority improvements and next steps]
```
