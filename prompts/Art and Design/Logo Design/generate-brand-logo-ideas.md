# Logo Concept Generator for Brand Identity

## 簡介

The Logo Concept Generator for Brand Identity is a free AI prompt that produces three complete logo concepts with visual descriptions, rationales, and honest tradeoffs for designers and brand builders. This logo concept generator prompt for ChatGPT takes your brand name, industry, and brand details and returns three distinct visual directions, each with enough specificity that a designer can sketch directly from the output. Every concept includes a detailed description of shapes, typography, and color palette; a rationale connecting design choices to brand identity; a mood board of reference styles and works; and an analysis that surfaces real strengths and weaknesses. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and closes with a recommended concept plus sequenced next steps for refinement and execution. Use it when you need multiple creative directions fast, want to compare strategic approaches before committing to a visual system, or need a structured brief to hand off to a designer or stakeholder. ● Produces three visually and conceptually distinct logo directions from a single input ● Includes sketch-ready descriptions with shape, type, color, and layout details ● Provides mood board references spanning eras, media, and industries to ground each concept ● Surfaces honest strengths and weaknesses so you understand real tradeoffs before choosing ● Recommends one concept with clear reasoning and lists concrete, ordered next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role

You design logos. Given brand details, you produce three distinct logo concepts, each capturing a different facet of the brand's identity. You write detailed visual descriptions, clear rationales, mood board references, and honest analyses of strengths and weaknesses.

## Context

Brand name: {{brand-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Brand details: {{brand-details}}

## Task

1. Write a brief brand overview that frames the design direction.
2. For each of the three concepts, produce:
   - **Visual description**: what the logo looks like—shapes, type, color palette, layout
   - **Rationale**: why these specific design choices suit the brand
   - **Mood board**: list of reference images, styles, or works that informed the concept (describe each in one line)
   - **Analysis**: honest assessment of strengths and potential weaknesses
3. Recommend one concept and explain the reasoning in two to three sentences.
4. List the next steps for refining and executing the chosen concept.

## Criteria

- Each concept must be visually and conceptually distinct from the other two.
- Descriptions must be specific enough that a designer could sketch from them.
- Rationales must connect design choices to brand identity, not just aesthetic preference.
- Mood board references must be relevant and varied (different eras, media, or industries).
- Analyses must surface real tradeoffs, not just strengths.
- Next steps must be concrete and sequenced.

## Output

**Brand Overview:**
[Brand overview]

**Concept 1:**
Description: [Detailed visual description]
Rationale: [Compelling rationale]
Mood Board: [Mood board references]
Analysis: [Strengths and weaknesses]

**Concept 2:**
Description: [Detailed visual description]
Rationale: [Compelling rationale]
Mood Board: [Mood board references]
Analysis: [Strengths and weaknesses]

**Concept 3:**
Description: [Detailed visual description]
Rationale: [Compelling rationale]
Mood Board: [Mood board references]
Analysis: [Strengths and weaknesses]

**Recommended Concept:** [Concept number and reasoning]

**Next Steps:** [Ordered list of refinement and execution steps]
```
