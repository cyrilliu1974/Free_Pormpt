# Twitter Thread Hook Generator

## 簡介

The Twitter Thread Hook Generator is a free AI prompt that creates 10 unique opening tweets designed to stop the scroll and drive thread engagement for content marketers and social media creators. This Twitter thread hook prompt for ChatGPT produces ten different hook styles - bold statements, metaphors, counterintuitive claims, personal story openings, data-driven leads, and more - each formatted to maximize click-through into your full thread. Every hook stays under 280 characters, uses 2–4 short paragraphs (max 10 words per sentence), and opens with an ultra-short first line to reduce reading friction. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and accepts variables for your thread topic and desired content style. Marketers use it to quickly test multiple angles for the same thread idea, while creators deploy it to maintain variety across weekly content calendars. ● Delivers 10 hooks in different patterns - no repeated formats - so you can A/B test or choose the strongest angle ● Enforces short, punchy structure: 2–4 one-sentence paragraphs, each under 10 words, with a very brief opening line ● Matches your specified content style (professional, casual, provocative, educational, etc.) and stays under Twitter's 280-character limit ● Bans hashtags and minimizes rhetorical questions to keep hooks declarative and action-focused ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in high-performing Twitter hooks that stop the scroll and drive thread engagement.

## Task
Generate 10 distinct hook tweets for a Twitter thread about {{thread-topic}}. Each hook must use a different pattern (bold statement, metaphor, counterintuitive claim, personal story opening, data-driven lead, etc.). Do not repeat the same hook type.

## Hook Requirements
- **Goal**: grab attention and compel the reader to open the thread
- **Length**: under 280 characters total
- **Structure**: 2–4 paragraphs, each paragraph is one sentence, each sentence max 10 words
- **Opening**: first sentence must be very short to lower reading friction
- **Style**: match {{content-style}}
- **Prohibitions**: no hashtags, minimize rhetorical questions

## Output Format
For each hook, present:

**[Hook type name]**  
[Hook tweet text with proper line breaks]

---

**[Hook type name]**  
[Hook tweet text with proper line breaks]

_(repeat for all 10 hooks)_
```
