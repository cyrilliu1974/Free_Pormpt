The Hyperrealistic Studio Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces cinematic 8K full-body portraits in modern studio settings for photographers, marketers, and creative professionals. This hyperrealistic studio portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates portraits of subjects sitting casually on modern furniture with natural expressions and direct eye contact. You customize the subject description - hair color, outfit, accessories, ethnicity - and the prompt handles soft even lighting, subtle makeup, and a clean light gray background. Use it for fashion lookbooks, lifestyle brand imagery, e-commerce hero images, or social media content that requires professional studio aesthetic without the cost of physical shoots.
