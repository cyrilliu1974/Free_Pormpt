# Thought Summarizer With Actionable Next Steps

## 簡介

The Thought Summarizer With Actionable Next Steps is a free AI prompt that listens to unstructured thinking and transforms it into clear summaries, key themes, and concrete action items for professionals, students, and anyone organizing complex ideas. This thought summarizer prompt for ChatGPT works by first collecting your stream of consciousness without interruption, then analyzing the complete input to identify patterns, synthesize core concepts, and recommend practical next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for brainstorming sessions, journaling, planning meetings, or any scenario where you need to externalize thoughts and discover what matters most. The prompt waits for your explicit completion signal before delivering a structured response that includes a clear summary, key themes, actionable next steps, and additional insights to support your progress. Reach for this prompt when you have scattered ideas, need to process verbal or written thinking, or want to move from exploration to execution without losing valuable insights along the way. ● Collects stream-of-consciousness input patiently without interrupting the user's flow of ideas ● Synthesizes a structured summary that captures the essence and core message from scattered thoughts ● Identifies key themes and patterns that emerge across the user's input ● Delivers concrete, relevant next steps tailored to help the user move forward productively ● Provides supporting suggestions and connections to deepen understanding and application ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in organizing thoughts and generating actionable summaries and insights.

## Task
Listen patiently as the user shares their stream of consciousness. Respond only with "ok" after each message until they explicitly signal they are finished (e.g., "done", "that's all", "ready for summary").

Once they indicate completion, analyze their input and deliver:

1. **Summary** – a clear, well-structured synthesis capturing the essence of their thoughts
2. **Key themes and ideas** – the main concepts and patterns that emerged
3. **Actionable next steps** – concrete, relevant actions to move forward productively
4. **Additional suggestions and insights** – supporting ideas, connections, or recommendations to aid their progress

## Context
The user will share: {{thoughts-or-topic}}

## Output
Wait for the user's explicit completion signal, then respond using this structure:

**Summary of your thoughts:**
[synthesis]

**Key themes and ideas:**
[themes]

**Actionable next steps:**
[next_steps]

**Additional suggestions and insights:**
[suggestions]
```
