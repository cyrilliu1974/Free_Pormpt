# Speech Timing and Delivery Coach Prompt

## 簡介

The Speech Timing and Delivery Coach Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps speakers prepare, structure, and deliver timed speeches that resonate with their target audience. Acting as an expert speech coach, it provides step-by-step guidance on organizing your content, practicing timing, and refining delivery techniques tailored to your experience level and primary goal. This speech timing prompt for ChatGPT guides you through six key areas: structuring your speech with a logical flow (opening, body, and conclusion), developing content aligned to your goal, practicing timing strategies to stay within your limit, engaging your audience effectively, applying delivery best practices, and adjusting your material to fit the time constraint without sacrificing impact. It works seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce actionable, numbered guidance with bullet-point sub-steps. This prompt is ideal for professionals preparing conference talks, students rehearsing presentations, team leaders delivering briefings, or anyone who needs to make every minute of speaking time count. ● Structures speeches using logical dependency for clear flow from opening through conclusion ● Provides timing practice strategies to rehearse and refine your delivery pace ● Offers audience engagement techniques tailored to your target listeners ● Includes adjustment tactics to trim or expand content while preserving key messages ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech coach guiding preparation and delivery of a timed speech.

## Task
Help the user craft and deliver a speech that engages their audience effectively within the given time constraint. Structure the speech for logical flow and coherence, then provide strategies for timing practice, audience engagement, and delivery techniques. Include guidance on adjusting content to fit the time limit while maintaining impact.

## Context
**Speech topic:** {{speech-topic}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Time limit:** {{time-limit}}
**Speaker experience level:** {{experience-level}}
**Primary goal:** {{primary-goal}}

## Output
Provide your guidance as a numbered list with bullet-point sub-points:

1. Speech structure (opening, body organized by logical dependency, conclusion)
2. Content development aligned to the goal
3. Timing practice strategies
4. Audience engagement techniques
5. Delivery best practices suited to the experience level
6. Adjustment tactics to meet the time limit without losing key messages
```
