# Local Landing Page SEO Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Local Landing Page SEO Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops complete local search optimization strategies for businesses targeting specific geographic markets. This local landing page SEO prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building an actionable plan that addresses keyword research, on-page elements, content creation, and local ranking factors like NAP consistency and geo-targeted content. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing your business context and competitive landscape to produce a sequenced, prioritized roadmap formatted for immediate execution by marketing teams. Real-world applications include launching city-specific service pages, optimizing multi-location business sites, and improving visibility for brick-and-mortar stores in local search results. This prompt is built for SEO specialists, digital marketers, and business owners who need to improve local search rankings and drive geographically relevant traffic to landing pages. ● Produces numbered implementation phases with specific tactics, rationale, and priority sequencing for marketing teams ● Addresses core local ranking signals including location-specific keywords, NAP consistency, and geo-targeted content ● Analyzes competitive landscape and business context to tailor recommendations to your market position ● Structures output using dependency grammar principles for logical flow and immediate applicability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist focused on local search optimization and landing page strategy.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive local SEO strategy to improve search visibility and relevance for a geographically-targeted landing page. Deliver a complete action plan covering keyword research, on-page optimization, content creation, and local SEO implementation.

## Context
Business and location details:
{{business-context}}

Competitive landscape:
{{competitor-analysis}}

## Approach
- Structure content using clear dependency relationships for natural readability
- Prioritize local ranking factors (NAP consistency, local keywords, location-specific content)
- Balance search engine requirements with user intent and conversion goals
- Ensure all recommendations are actionable and sequenced logically

## Output
Present your strategy as a detailed, numbered action plan with:
1. Each major optimization phase as a numbered step
2. Specific tactics and sub-tasks as bullet points beneath each step
3. Clear rationale for local SEO techniques recommended
4. Priority indicators where sequencing matters

Format for immediate implementation by a marketing team.
```
