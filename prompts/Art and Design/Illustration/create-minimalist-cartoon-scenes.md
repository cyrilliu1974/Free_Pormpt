# Minimalist Cartoon Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Cartoon Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates surreal, clean-lined illustrations of doors floating in serene water settings. The prompt produces hyper-realistic cartoon imagery with cinematic composition and sharp focus, combining dreamlike subject matter with precise minimalist aesthetics. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, making it a versatile minimalist cartoon prompt for ChatGPT-assisted workflows or direct image generation. You control the door style (white door, wooden door, etc.), the water environment (calm ocean, misty lake, turquoise sea), and the aspect ratio to fit your canvas. Reach for this prompt when you need striking, contemplative visuals for editorial illustrations, social media graphics, album artwork, or conceptual design mockups that balance whimsy with photorealistic rendering. ● Adjustable door types and water environments let you create endless variations of the floating-door motif. ● Hyper-realistic rendering with clean lines ensures each scene feels both stylized and photorealistic. ● Cinematic composition and sharp focus produce portfolio-ready images suitable for print and digital use. ● Customizable aspect ratios adapt the scene to square social posts, widescreen banners, or vertical mobile formats. ## Prompt

```
Minimalist cartoon scene of a white {{door-type}} floating in the middle of {{water-environment}}, hyper-realistic rendering with clean lines, cinematic composition, sharp focus --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
