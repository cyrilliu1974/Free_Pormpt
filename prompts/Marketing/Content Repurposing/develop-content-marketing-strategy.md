# Content Marketing Strategy Prompt for Shareable Assets

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Strategy Prompt for Shareable Assets is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end content marketing roadmaps for products and services, with a focus on creating linkable, shareable content that drives engagement and backlinks. This content marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes a single product or service description and outputs structured audience analysis (demographics, psychographics, online behavior), key messaging pillars, and detailed asset plans across three formats: infographics, videos, and interactive tools. Each asset type includes 3-5 topic recommendations, design guidance, distribution channels, and promotion tactics. The prompt also defines measurable KPIs for reach, engagement, lead generation, and sales, plus tracking tool recommendations. Marketing teams use it to plan quarter-long campaigns, content agencies use it to pitch comprehensive strategies to clients, and founders use it to map out their first content efforts. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, actionable content plan rather than scattered ideas - ideal for product launches, rebrands, or scaling an existing content operation. ● Analyzes target audiences across demographics, psychographics, and online behavior to ground every content decision in real user preferences ● Produces asset-specific strategies for infographics, videos, and interactive tools (calculators, quizzes, assessments), each with topics, design elements, and distribution plans ● Defines measurable KPIs across reach, engagement, lead generation, and sales, paired with tracking tool recommendations for web analytics, social platforms, and CRM integration ● Aligns all recommendations with SEO best practices and the unique selling propositions of your product or service ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic content marketing expert specialized in creating shareable, linkable assets that drive engagement and backlinks.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content asset strategy to promote the following product or service:

{{product-service-description}}

Provide a detailed roadmap covering audience analysis, messaging, content formats, distribution, and measurement.

## Output
Deliver your strategy using this structure:

**Target Audience Analysis**
- Demographics: age range, gender, location, income level, education level
- Psychographics: interests, values, lifestyle, pain points, goals
- Online behavior: preferred social media platforms, content consumption habits, influencers followed

**Key Messaging**
1. Unique selling proposition 1
2. Unique selling proposition 2
3. Unique selling proposition 3
4. Brand personality and tone

**Content Asset Strategy**

*Infographics:*
- Purpose
- Topics (3-5 recommendations)
- Design elements
- Distribution channels
- Promotion tactics

*Videos:*
- Purpose
- Topics (3-5 recommendations)
- Video formats
- Distribution channels
- Promotion tactics

*Interactive Tools:*
- Purpose
- Topics (3-5 recommendations)
- Tool types (calculators, quizzes, assessments, configurators)
- Distribution channels
- Promotion tactics

**Measuring Success**

*Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):*
- Reach: impressions, views, unique visitors
- Engagement: likes, comments, shares, click-through rates
- Lead generation: email sign-ups, contact form submissions, content downloads
- Sales: conversion rates, revenue generated, ROI

*Tracking Tools:*
- Web analytics platforms
- Social media analytics
- Link tracking
- CRM integration

## Guidelines
- Provide specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the product or service
- Include design best practices and templates for each asset type
- Offer SEO optimization tactics for maximum discoverability
- Ensure all tactics align with the target audience's preferences and behavior
```
