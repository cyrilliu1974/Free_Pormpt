# Purple Pastel Icon Retexture Prompt

## 簡介

The Purple Pastel Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that applies a smooth, matte-plastic 3D style overlay to any image while preserving its original silhouette and shape. This purple pastel icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by analyzing the geometry of your uploaded image and mapping a studio-lit, soft-textured treatment with lavender (#A78BFA) and light purple (#B794F4) tones over a dark solid background. Designers use it to convert photos, logos, or illustrations into cohesive icon sets with rounded edges, gentle glossy highlights, and soft contact shadows. It is ideal for UI designers, brand teams, and content creators who need consistent, modern icon assets without manual 3D modeling. ● Preserves the original silhouette and applies geometry-sensitive mapping for high detail retention and smooth forms. ● Uses studio soft light with medium intensity to produce soft contact shadows and gentle glossy highlights on edges. ● Enforces a harmonious color palette of lavender, pale gray, light purple, and deep violet against a solid dark background. ● Specifies smooth matte plastic material with rounded edges, subtle reflections on curves, and no visible seams or harsh outlines. ## Prompt
