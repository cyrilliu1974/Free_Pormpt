# Job Search Platform Comparison by Industry

## 簡介

The Job Search Platform Comparison by Industry is a free AI prompt that analyzes and compares job boards and industry-specific platforms to help job seekers identify the most effective channels for their field. This job search platform prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a systematic evaluation framework: it examines feature sets, rates job posting quality on a 1-5 scale, estimates success rates, and presents findings in a clear comparison table with cited sources. The output includes a field overview describing hiring patterns, a transparent research methodology, a side-by-side platform comparison of 5-7 major job boards, sourced data citations, and actionable recommendations prioritized by use case. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for career coaches, job seekers evaluating multiple platforms, or HR professionals advising candidates on where to focus their search efforts. ● Delivers a structured comparison table rating platforms by features, posting quality (1-5 scale), and success rates ● Includes a transparent research methodology and cited sources for every claim ● Provides 3-5 tailored recommendations that prioritize platforms by job seeker use case ● Covers general job boards, niche industry platforms, and networking sites relevant to the specified field ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert job search strategist with deep knowledge of job boards, industry-specific platforms, and effective job search techniques across various fields.

## Task
Conduct a thorough analysis of job boards and industry-specific platforms for {{job-field}}. Evaluate each platform based on features, job posting quality, and success rates. Present your findings in a clear comparison table format with cited sources.

## Context
Job seekers need reliable data to choose the most effective platforms for their field. Your analysis will help them optimize their job search efforts by identifying which platforms offer the best features, highest quality postings, and strongest success rates.

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Field Overview:**
Provide an overview of the job search landscape in {{job-field}}, including typical hiring patterns and platform preferences.

**Research Methodology:**
Describe the criteria used to evaluate platforms: feature set comprehensiveness, job posting quality assessment (relevance, detail, legitimacy), and success rate measurement methods. Specify data sources and evaluation framework.

**Comparison Table:**
| Platform Name | Key Features | Job Posting Quality (1-5) | Success Rate (%) |
|---------------|--------------|---------------------------|------------------|
| [Platform 1]  | [Features]   | [Rating]                  | [Percentage]     |
| [Platform 2]  | [Features]   | [Rating]                  | [Percentage]     |
| [Platform 3]  | [Features]   | [Rating]                  | [Percentage]     |
| [Platform 4]  | [Features]   | [Rating]                  | [Percentage]     |
| [Platform 5]  | [Features]   | [Rating]                  | [Percentage]     |

Include 5-7 platforms most relevant to the field.

**Sources:**
Cite all sources used, including industry reports, platform analytics, user surveys, and academic research.

**Recommendations:**
Provide 3-5 specific, actionable recommendations for job seekers in {{job-field}} based on the analysis. Prioritize platforms by use case (active applications, networking, niche roles, etc.).
```
