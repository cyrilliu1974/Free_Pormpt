# Ad Script Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Ad Script Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates platform-optimized advertising scripts tailored to your product, audience, and brand voice. This ad script prompt for ChatGPT produces three distinct variations of complete ad copy, each containing an attention-grabbing headline, benefit-focused body copy, and compelling call-to-action. The prompt analyzes your product's core benefits against audience pain points, then generates scripts that comply with platform-specific character limits and formatting requirements for channels like Facebook, Google Ads, Instagram, LinkedIn, or TikTok. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting persuasive language and emotional triggers to match your specified brand voice while testing different angles across the three variations. Use this prompt when you need multiple ready-to-test ad concepts quickly, or when launching campaigns across platforms with different creative requirements. Marketing teams, advertising professionals, and business owners will save hours of copywriting iteration while ensuring every element - from hook to CTA - is strategically aligned with conversion goals. ● Produces three complete ad scripts per run, each with headline, body, and CTA structured for immediate use ● Tailors copy to platform specifications - character counts, tone conventions, and format rules for your chosen channel ● Incorporates your unique selling proposition and brand voice consistently across all variations ● Tests different persuasive angles and emotional triggers in each script to identify top performers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert advertising copywriter specializing in platform-optimized ad scripts that drive engagement and conversions.

## Task
Create three complete ad script variations, each containing a headline, body copy, and call-to-action. Each variation should be persuasive, platform-appropriate, and tailored to the target audience.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Advertising Platform:** {{platform}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}

Analyze the product's core benefits and how they solve the target audience's pain points. Research platform-specific requirements (character limits, formatting rules, best practices). Use persuasive language and emotional triggers that resonate with the audience while maintaining the specified brand voice.

## Output
Provide three distinct ad script variations in this structure:

**Variation 1**
- **Headline:** [attention-grabbing headline]
- **Body Copy:** [concise, benefit-focused copy]
- **Call-to-Action:** [clear, compelling CTA]

**Variation 2**
- **Headline:** [attention-grabbing headline]
- **Body Copy:** [concise, benefit-focused copy]
- **Call-to-Action:** [clear, compelling CTA]

**Variation 3**
- **Headline:** [attention-grabbing headline]
- **Body Copy:** [concise, benefit-focused copy]
- **Call-to-Action:** [clear, compelling CTA]

Ensure all variations comply with platform character limits and formatting guidelines. Each variation should test a different angle or emotional appeal.
```
