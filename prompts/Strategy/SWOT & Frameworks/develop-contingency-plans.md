# Contingency Plan Generator for Business Risk Management

## 簡介

The Contingency Plan Generator for Business Risk Management is a free AI prompt that systematically identifies risks, evaluates their probability and consequences, and recommends practical mitigation strategies for any business context. This contingency planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific business vulnerabilities across operational, financial, regulatory, and market dimensions. By providing your business context, the prompt generates a structured markdown table that categorizes each risk, rates its likelihood and impact on a three-tier scale, and pairs it with an actionable mitigation strategy aligned to industry best practices. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it suitable for consultants, business owners, and operations managers who need to document risk exposure and preparedness plans quickly. Common use cases include annual risk assessments, investor readiness reports, compliance documentation, and strategic planning sessions. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic framework to surface hidden vulnerabilities, prioritize mitigation efforts, or communicate risk posture to stakeholders in a clear, tabular format. ● Systematically categorizes risks across financial, operational, regulatory, and market domains relevant to the business context ● Rates each risk by likelihood and impact, enabling prioritization of mitigation resources and management attention ● Proposes practical, actionable mitigation strategies tailored to the specific risk and industry best practices ● Outputs a clean markdown table format ready for reports, slide decks, or stakeholder review ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant developing a comprehensive contingency plan.

## Task
Identify potential risks, assess their likelihood and impact, and propose effective mitigation strategies tailored to the specific business context provided.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Analyze the business type and its specific vulnerabilities. Consider industry regulations, market conditions, operational complexities, and business size when identifying risks. Categorize risks systematically, evaluate their probability of occurrence and potential consequences, and devise practical mitigation measures aligned with industry best practices.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:
- Risk Category
- Likelihood (Low/Medium/High)
- Impact (Low/Medium/High)
- Mitigation Strategy

Provide a comprehensive list of potential risks covering the main risk categories relevant to this business. Ensure each mitigation strategy is practical, actionable, and specific to the identified risk.
```
