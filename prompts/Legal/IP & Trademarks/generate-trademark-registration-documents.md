# Trademark Registration Document Generator

## 簡介

The Trademark Registration Document Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts comprehensive trademark application packages for attorneys, in-house counsel, and business owners filing brand protection paperwork. This trademark registration prompt for ChatGPT takes your trademark details - brand name, logo, goods and services classification, and jurisdiction - and produces a structured application set that includes applicant information, detailed mark descriptions with class codes, a checklist of required supporting documents (proof of use, power of attorney, priority filings), and step-by-step filing instructions tailored to your jurisdiction's office, fees, and timelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring compliance with legal standards and reducing preparation time for trademark filings. Reach for this prompt when you need to prepare registration paperwork for a new mark, respond to office actions, or coordinate multi-jurisdiction filings without starting from scratch each time. ● Outputs applicant details, mark descriptions, and goods/services classifications with appropriate class codes ● Lists supporting documents required by the target jurisdiction (specimens, assignments, declarations) ● Provides filing office details, fee schedules, and expected timelines ● Structures every section to meet legal standards and minimize processing delays ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert trademark attorney experienced in legal requirements and processes for trademark registration across multiple jurisdictions.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive set of documents necessary for trademark registration based on the provided details. Ensure all documents meet legal standards for the specified jurisdiction, with complete and unambiguous information throughout.

## Context

Trademark registration requires:
- Detailed description of the mark and its intended usage
- Applicant information and supporting documentation
- Compliance with jurisdiction-specific legal requirements
- Clear filing procedures

{{trademark-details}}

## Output

Provide a complete trademark registration package structured as follows:

### Trademark Details
- Brand name
- Logo description
- Goods and services classification
- First use date
- Jurisdiction

### Trademark Application
- Applicant information (name, address, entity type)
- Mark description (detailed visual and textual characteristics)
- Goods and services description (with appropriate class codes)
- Specimen details
- Declaration and signature block

### Supporting Documents
List 3-5 relevant supporting documents required for this jurisdiction (e.g., proof of use, priority documents, power of attorney, assignments).

### Filing Instructions
Provide step-by-step filing instructions specific to the jurisdiction, including:
- Filing office and method (online/paper)
- Required fees
- Timeline expectations
- Post-filing procedures
```
