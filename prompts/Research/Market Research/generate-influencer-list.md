# Influencer Research and Brand Partnership Prompt

## 簡介

The Influencer Research and Brand Partnership Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps brands identify and evaluate social media influencers for strategic collaborations based on content relevance, engagement data, and audience demographics. This influencer research prompt for ChatGPT systematically analyzes candidates on any specified platform - Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn - by examining content quality, engagement rates, follower demographics, authenticity, and brand safety. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a markdown comparison table ranking 5-10 top influencer candidates by strategic fit. Marketing teams use it to vet creators for campaigns, partnerships use it to assess alignment with brand values, and agencies use it to build shortlists for client pitches. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, data-driven evaluation of influencer candidates that goes beyond follower counts to measure real engagement and audience match. ● Analyzes content relevance, engagement rate, follower demographics, and authenticity across any social platform ● Produces a markdown table ranking 5-10 influencers by strategic fit with explanations of brand alignment ● Evaluates brand safety and controversy history to surface partnership risks ● Customizable by platform, industry, brand values, and target audience to match your campaign goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media influencer analyst specializing in brand partnership strategy.

## Task
Identify and evaluate key influencers who align with the specified brand for potential partnerships. Research candidates on {{platform}}, analyze their content quality, engagement metrics, and audience demographics, then compile a structured comparison to support partnership decisions.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Platform: {{platform}}
Brand values: {{brand-values}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Analysis Criteria
- Content relevance to industry and brand values
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares relative to follower count)
- Follower demographics (age, location, interests)
- Authenticity and audience trust
- Brand safety and controversy history

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table:

| Name | Follower Count | Engagement Rate | Brand Alignment |
|------|----------------|-----------------|------------------|

In the "Brand Alignment" column, briefly explain how each influencer's content, audience, and values match the brand requirements. Include 5-10 top candidates ranked by strategic fit.
```
