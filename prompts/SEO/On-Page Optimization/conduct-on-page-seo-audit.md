# On-Page SEO Audit Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The On-Page SEO Audit Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive website audits for SEO professionals, content strategists, and digital marketers. This on-page SEO audit prompt for ChatGPT analyzes critical ranking factors including title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy, content quality, keyword usage, internal linking, URL structure, image alt text, page speed, mobile responsiveness, and schema markup implementation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce a structured report complete with an overall SEO score, documented strengths and weaknesses, critical improvement areas with specific recommendations, and a prioritized action list tagged by urgency level. Real-world use cases include pre-launch website reviews, competitive analysis benchmarking, client onboarding audits, and ongoing performance monitoring. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic evaluation framework that goes beyond surface-level checks and delivers actionable next steps ranked by potential impact. ● Evaluates all major on-page SEO elements in a single audit pass, from metadata and headers to structured data and page speed signals ● Outputs an overall SEO score out of 100 alongside categorized strengths, weaknesses, and critical focus areas ● Delivers a prioritized action list where each recommendation is tagged with High, Medium, or Low urgency ● Includes additional quick-win suggestions and advanced optimization opportunities tailored to the specific website audited ## Prompt
