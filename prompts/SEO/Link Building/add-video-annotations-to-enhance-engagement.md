# Video Annotation Strategy for SEO and Engagement

## 簡介

The Video Annotation Strategy for SEO and Engagement is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive annotation plan tailored to your audience, content type, and retention goals. This video annotation prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed framework covering audience analysis, keyword integration, placement timing, style guidelines, and success metrics - all designed to boost both viewer interaction and search visibility. Whether you're optimizing YouTube tutorials, marketing videos, or educational content, the prompt analyzes your target audience's viewing behaviors, weaves SEO keywords into annotation copy, and recommends precise timing and positioning based on your retention data. It's ideal for video marketers, content creators, and SEO specialists who need a data-informed approach to annotations that serve both the viewer experience and search algorithms. ● Analyzes target audience preferences and viewing patterns to inform annotation content and timing ● Integrates primary SEO keywords into annotations to improve discoverability on YouTube, Vimeo, and other video platforms ● Provides a placement framework with timing, frequency, and positioning recommendations based on retention rate data ● Establishes tone, format, and messaging guidelines to maintain consistent annotation style ● Defines measurable KPIs and tracking methods to evaluate annotation impact on engagement and search performance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video content strategist specializing in annotation-driven engagement and search optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive annotation strategy that improves SEO, video discoverability, and viewer engagement for the specified video content.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Video content type:** {{content-type}}
**Primary SEO keywords:** {{seo-keywords}}
**Distribution platform(s):** {{platforms}}
**Current average viewer retention rate:** {{retention-rate}}

## Strategy Components
Your annotation strategy must address:

1. **Audience Analysis** – Preferences, viewing behaviors, and engagement patterns specific to this audience
2. **SEO Integration** – How to incorporate keywords and metadata into annotations for search visibility
3. **Placement Framework** – Timing, frequency, and positioning guidelines based on retention data
4. **Content & Style Guidelines** – Tone, format, and messaging standards for annotations
5. **Success Metrics** – KPIs and measurement methods to track annotation impact on engagement and discoverability

## Output
Present your strategy as a numbered list with sub-points, using clear headings for each main section. Make every recommendation actionable and tailored to the provided context.
```
