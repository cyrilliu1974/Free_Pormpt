# Follow-Up Email After Support Resolution

## 簡介

The Follow-Up Email After Support Resolution is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, empathetic follow-up emails for support teams to send after resolving customer issues. This support follow-up prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete email with a clear subject line, a warm greeting using the customer's name, a check-in about the resolved issue, an invitation for additional questions, a feedback request, and a professional signature. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming four simple variables - customer name, resolved issue, agent name, and company name - into human-sounding, non-templated messages. Support teams use it to maintain consistent post-resolution communication, gauge customer satisfaction, and collect actionable feedback without spending time drafting individual emails from scratch. Reach for this prompt when you want to automate follow-up outreach while preserving the warm, personal tone that strengthens customer relationships and encourages honest feedback. ● Produces a subject line, personalized greeting, resolution check, support offer, feedback request, and branded signature in one output. ● Guides the AI to avoid generic or boilerplate phrasing, ensuring each message feels genuinely caring. ● Structured to make it easy for customers to reply with concerns or praise, improving response rates. ● Works for any resolved issue, from technical troubleshooting to billing questions, across industries. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced customer support representative skilled in crafting personalized, empathetic follow-up emails.

## Task
Write a follow-up email to check in with a customer after their support issue has been resolved. The email must feel personalized and genuinely caring, not generic or templated. Keep the tone warm, professional, and human.

## Context
The goal is to ensure customer satisfaction, build rapport, and encourage them to reach out with any further concerns or feedback.

## Guidelines
1. **Subject line**: Clear and concise, reflecting the purpose of the follow-up
2. **Opening**: Warm greeting that addresses the customer by name
3. **Issue resolution check**: Reference the resolved issue and ask whether the customer is satisfied with the outcome
4. **Additional support offer**: Let the customer know they can reach out anytime with questions or concerns
5. **Feedback request**: Ask for their feedback on the support experience and the resolution provided
6. **Closing**: Friendly and professional sign-off
7. **Signature**: Include the agent name and company name

Personalize everything to the specific customer and their situation. Avoid impersonal or boilerplate language. Make it easy and comfortable for the customer to reply.

## Information
- Customer name: {{customer-name}}
- Resolved issue: {{resolved-issue}}
- Agent name: {{agent-name}}
- Company name: {{company-name}}

## Output
Provide the complete email in this format:

Subject: [Subject Line]

Hi {{customer-name}},

[Opening Line]

[Issue Resolution Check]

[Additional Support Offer]

[Feedback Request]

[Closing Line]

Best regards,
{{agent-name}}
{{company-name}} Customer Support
```
