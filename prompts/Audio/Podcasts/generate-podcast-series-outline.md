# Podcast Series Generator With Episode Scripts

## 簡介

The Podcast Series Generator With Episode Scripts is a free AI prompt that designs complete podcast series with full episode scripts for content creators, producers, and subject-matter experts launching narrative-driven audio shows. This podcast series prompt for ChatGPT builds a cohesive multi-episode arc by applying dependency grammar principles to ensure spoken clarity and listener engagement. It produces a series title, a narrative description mapping the journey listeners will take, a recommended total episode count, and three fully scripted episodes complete with storytelling beats, actionable insights, key takeaways, and a closing call to action. Each episode builds on the previous one, creating a progression that positions the series as a definitive resource in its niche. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and accepts three variables: target audience, niche, and series goal. Reach for this prompt when you need more than a topic list - when you want structured, ready-to-record scripts that blend expert insights with narrative flow and give listeners something they can act on immediately. ● Outputs a series title, description, episode count, and three complete episode scripts with titles, descriptions, and key takeaways. ● Applies dependency grammar throughout scripts to optimize for spoken delivery and listener comprehension. ● Builds a narrative arc across episodes so each installment advances the series and deepens audience engagement. ● Includes a call to action that drives subscription, community participation, or listener response. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert podcast producer crafting engaging audio series. You build cohesive narrative arcs, write compelling scripts using dependency grammar principles, and deliver content that positions the series as the definitive resource in its space.

## Context

- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Niche: {{niche}}
- Series goal: {{series-goal}}

## Task

Design a complete podcast series for the audience and niche above. Apply dependency grammar throughout all scripts to ensure spoken clarity. Weave storytelling techniques with expert insights—nothing generic.

Deliver:

1. **Series title** that captures the narrative arc
2. **Series description** mapping key themes and the journey listeners will take
3. **Total episode count** for the full series
4. **First three episodes**, each containing:
   - Engaging episode title
   - Episode description (main focus and hook)
   - Three concrete listener takeaways
   - Full episode script with storytelling beats and immediately actionable insights
5. **Call to action** that drives listener engagement and series momentum

Each episode must build on the previous one. Scripts should give listeners something they can act on immediately. Avoid surface-level content.
```
