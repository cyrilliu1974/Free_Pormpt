# Product Development Timeline Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Product Development Timeline Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured launch roadmaps for solopreneurs bringing new products to market. This product development timeline prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every phase from concept to post-launch, delivering a markdown table that maps 8–12 critical milestones against resources, deadlines, and tactical launch actions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your product-and-market-context into a phase-by-phase plan covering Research, Design, Build, Test, Launch, and Post-Launch stages. Each milestone identifies the tools, skills, and budget required, assigns realistic timelines, and flags potential risks with mitigation strategies. Use it when you need to transform a product idea into an actionable roadmap that adapts to shifting market conditions or resource constraints. ● Breaks product development into distinct phases with specific milestones and deliverables ● Identifies required resources - tools, skills, budget, external help - for every stage ● Assigns realistic timelines and target dates adaptable to market or resource changes ● Integrates launch strategy and risk mitigation directly into each milestone row ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product development strategist specializing in solopreneur launch planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive product launch timeline that outlines key milestones, identifies necessary resources, and develops a launch strategy. Break down the product development process into distinct phases, determine realistic timelines, and consider potential risks and mitigation strategies. Ensure the timeline is adaptable to changes in market conditions or resource availability.

## Context
{{product-and-market-context}}

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with the following columns:
- **Phase** – major stage of development (e.g., Research, Design, Build, Test, Launch, Post-Launch)
- **Milestone** – specific achievement or deliverable within that phase
- **Resources Needed** – tools, skills, budget allocation, or external help required
- **Timeline** – estimated duration or target date
- **Launch Strategy** – tactical actions aligned with this milestone

Each row should represent one key milestone. Include 8–12 milestones spanning the full journey from concept to post-launch. For each milestone, note any significant risks and brief mitigation approaches where relevant.
```
