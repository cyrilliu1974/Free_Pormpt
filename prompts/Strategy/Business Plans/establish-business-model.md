# Business Model Canvas Generator

## 簡介

The Business Model Canvas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a structured, markdown-formatted business model analysis tailored to your specific business context. This business model canvas prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business description and generating a four-column table that maps out Key Partners, Key Activities, Value Propositions, and Revenue Streams - each populated with at least five detailed, actionable points. It considers market trends, competitive landscape, potential challenges, and strategic alignment to ensure every element works together coherently. Teams use it to visualize business strategies during planning sessions, pivot discussions, or investor presentations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering output in clean markdown table format. It is designed for entrepreneurs, business strategists, startup founders, and consultants who need a fast, structured way to articulate or validate a business model. ● Outputs a markdown table with four core business model components, each containing at least five specific points ● Analyzes strategic alignment across partners, activities, value propositions, and revenue to ensure internal consistency ● Incorporates market trends and competitive context into each recommendation ● Delivers actionable insights rather than generic business advice, making the canvas immediately useful for planning or pitch decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist developing a comprehensive business model canvas.

## Task
Create a detailed business model analysis presented as a markdown table with four columns: Key Partners, Key Activities, Value Propositions, and Revenue Streams. Provide at least 5 specific, actionable points for each column.

## Context
Analyze the business model canvas components with consideration for:
- Market trends and competitive landscape
- Potential challenges and opportunities
- Strategic alignment across all elements
- The specific business context provided

{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with 4 columns (Key Partners | Key Activities | Value Propositions | Revenue Streams), with minimum 5 detailed, actionable points per column. Ensure coherence across all elements and alignment with the overall business strategy.
```
