# Informational Interview Guide for Career Networking

## 簡介

The Informational Interview Guide for Career Networking is a free AI prompt that generates a complete step-by-step framework for planning, executing, and following up on informational interviews in any industry. This informational interview prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered action plan organized into benefits, outreach tactics, interview conduct guidelines, follow-up timelines, and a table of field-specific sample questions with 3-4 categories. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is built for job seekers, career changers, and professionals who want to expand their network and gain insider knowledge. The output includes cited authoritative sources and emphasizes preparation and professionalism at every stage. Use it when you need to systematically approach professionals in your target industry, whether you are exploring a new field, preparing for a career transition, or building relationships that can lead to opportunities. ● Produces three concrete benefits of informational interviews specific to the target industry ● Delivers step-by-step instructions for identifying contacts, crafting outreach messages, and securing meetings ● Includes preparation checklists, conversation frameworks, and professionalism guidelines for the interview itself ● Generates a table of 9-12 field-relevant questions organized by category, plus a follow-up timeline and relationship maintenance plan ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career strategist specializing in informational interviews across all industries.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide for conducting informational interviews in {{field}}, covering the full process from initial outreach through follow-up.

## Output
Deliver your response as a numbered list organized into three sections:

**Benefits of Informational Interviews**
1-3. List three concrete benefits for job search and career exploration in {{field}}

**Requesting Informational Interviews**
4-6. Provide step-by-step instructions for identifying contacts, crafting outreach messages, and securing meetings

**Conducting Informational Interviews**
7-9. Explain how to prepare, structure the conversation, and maintain professionalism during the interview

**Following Up**
10-12. Detail the follow-up process, timeline, and relationship maintenance strategies

**Sample Questions Table**
Create a table with 3-4 question categories relevant to {{field}} (e.g., Career Path, Industry Trends, Skills & Qualifications, Day-to-Day Work). Include 3 specific questions per category.

| Question Category | Sample Questions |
| --- | --- |
| [Category] | 1. [Question]<br>2. [Question]<br>3. [Question] |

**Sources**
Cite 3-5 authoritative sources used to develop this guidance.

Ensure all advice is actionable, field-specific, and emphasizes preparation and professionalism throughout the process.
```
