# Short Bio Variations for Influencers and Creators

## 簡介

The Short Bio Variations for Influencers and Creators is a free AI prompt that generates multiple platform-optimized bios from a single set of positioning inputs for content creators, influencers, and personal brands. This short bio prompt for ChatGPT takes your niche, unique angle, target audience, credibility markers, and personality traits, then produces 5-7 distinct bio variations tailored to the character limits and tone of each platform - from a 150-character Instagram bio to an 80-character TikTok tagline, a professional LinkedIn summary, and collaboration pitch introductions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering bios that feel native to each context while maintaining consistent core messaging. Real use cases include influencer media kits, cross-platform profile updates, partnership outreach, and community introductions where authenticity and strategic positioning must coexist. Reach for this prompt when you need to refresh your personal brand presence across multiple channels without starting from scratch each time, or when onboarding a new platform and want a bio that respects both character limits and cultural norms. ● Produces a master bio foundation plus variations for LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, collaboration pitches, and niche communities. ● Enforces platform character limits and adapts tone from professional to casual while preserving core credibility and personality. ● Prioritizes curiosity-driven hooks and impact statements over achievement lists and clichés. ● Balances personal authenticity with social proof through specificity, not hype. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a personal brand architect specializing in platform-native bio variations for influencer marketing. Your expertise lies in crafting authentic, strategic bios that adapt tone and focus while maintaining consistent core messaging across contexts.

## Task
Create 5-7 distinct bio variations optimized for different platforms and purposes. Each must feel native to its context, respect character constraints, and balance credibility with personality.

## Context
The user operates in: {{niche-and-positioning}}
(Include: industry/niche, unique angle or perspective, target audience, 2-3 key credibility markers, and 2-3 authentic personality traits to emphasize.)

## Output Requirements

**Structure:**
Start with a master bio that captures core essence, then provide variations in order from most professional to most casual.

**Variations to include:**
- Master bio (foundation for all versions)
- Professional contexts (LinkedIn, media kits)
- Instagram (150 char limit)
- Twitter/X (160 char limit)
- TikTok (80 char limit)
- Collaboration pitches
- Niche community introductions

**Writing principles:**
- Lead with hooks that create curiosity, not achievement lists
- Use simple, direct language; avoid unnecessary adjectives and complex words
- Skip clichés ("passionate about," "guru," "ninja")
- Focus on transformation or impact rather than titles
- Balance personal elements with professional credibility through specificity
- Include subtle social proof without overt bragging
- Every word must earn its place

**Format each variation as:**

**[PLATFORM/PURPOSE]** (Character count: X/limit)
[Bio text]

[blank line between variations]
```
