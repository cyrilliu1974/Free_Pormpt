# Email Automation Workflow Blueprint Generator

## 簡介

The Email Automation Workflow Blueprint Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed email automation strategies for businesses looking to nurture leads, onboard customers, and re-engage inactive subscribers. This email automation prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured blueprint for three critical workflows: lead nurturing sequences that guide prospects toward conversion, customer onboarding series that educate and activate new users, and re-engagement campaigns that win back dormant subscribers. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by generating numbered workflows with lettered sub-points that cover email sequencing, timing triggers, content themes, segmentation criteria, and key performance indicators. Marketing teams use it to map out complete automation strategies before building campaigns in their email service provider. This prompt is for email marketers, growth teams, and business owners who need a complete automation framework tailored to their business context and audience. ● Specifies email cadence, triggers, and timing for each workflow stage ● Defines content themes that progress logically from awareness to action ● Includes segmentation criteria and list-cleaning decision points for deliverability ● Provides conversion goals, calls-to-action, and KPIs for each sequence type ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in automated workflows and conversion optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email automation blueprint covering three workflows: lead nurturing, customer onboarding, and re-engagement of inactive subscribers. Design targeted sequences with compelling content and conversion-focused strategies for each workflow.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your blueprint as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) detailing:

1. **Lead Nurturing Workflow**
   - Email sequence structure (number of emails, timing, triggers)
   - Content themes and progression for each email
   - Conversion goals and calls-to-action
   - Segmentation criteria

2. **Customer Onboarding Workflow**
   - Welcome series architecture
   - Educational content delivery schedule
   - Milestone-based triggers
   - Success metrics and checkpoints

3. **Re-engagement Workflow**
   - Inactive subscriber identification criteria
   - Win-back sequence structure
   - Incentive and messaging strategy
   - List cleaning decision points

For each workflow, specify email cadence, subject line approaches, content structure using clear dependency relationships between ideas, and key performance indicators to track.
```
