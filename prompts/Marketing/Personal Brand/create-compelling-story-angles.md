# Brand Story Angles Generator for Marketing Campaigns

## 簡介

The Brand Story Angles Generator for Marketing Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates multiple narrative approaches for brands looking to engage their audience through emotionally resonant storytelling. This brand story angles prompt for ChatGPT produces 5–7 distinct narrative frameworks, each with an evocative title and a 2–3 sentence explanation of its emotional hook, cultural relevance, and how it differentiates your brand from competitors. You provide your brand name, target audience, unique value proposition, industry context, and key competitors, and the prompt generates story angles that work across multiple marketing channels while maintaining message consistency. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible for content marketers, brand strategists, and campaign planners working on narrative-driven campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh storytelling directions for a product launch, rebrand, or ongoing content calendar and want each angle grounded in both emotional appeal and competitive differentiation. ● Outputs 5–7 titled story angles, each with a clear emotional hook and explanation of how it communicates your unique value proposition ● Considers competitive landscape to ensure your narratives create contrast with rival brands in your industry ● Emphasizes cultural relevance and current trends so stories feel timely and authentic to your audience ● Designed for cross-channel adaptability, ensuring narrative consistency whether you deploy on social, email, video, or paid media ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in narrative-driven campaign development.

## Task
Generate compelling story angles for a brand's marketing campaign. Each angle must engage the target audience, communicate the brand's unique value proposition, and remain adaptable across multiple marketing channels.

## Context
**Brand:** {{brand-name}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique Value Proposition:** {{unique-value-proposition}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Key Competitors:** {{key-competitors}}

Develop story angles that:
- Resonate emotionally with the target audience
- Highlight the brand's key attributes and differentiators
- Reflect cultural relevance and current trends
- Maintain message consistency across channels
- Create clear contrast with competitor positioning

## Output
Provide 5–7 distinct story angles in bullet point format. For each angle:
- **Title:** A clear, evocative name for the narrative approach
- **Brief Explanation:** 2–3 sentences describing the angle, its emotional hook, and how it communicates the unique value proposition
```
