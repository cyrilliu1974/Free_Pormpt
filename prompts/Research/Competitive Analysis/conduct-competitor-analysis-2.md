# Competitor Digital Marketing Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor Digital Marketing Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates and compares the online marketing strategies of business competitors for digital marketing strategists and business owners. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to research and assess each rival's website design, social media engagement, content marketing efforts, paid advertising campaigns, email tactics, and SEO positioning. It produces a structured markdown table organizing competitor names, key strategies, strengths, and weaknesses, then concludes with actionable insights highlighting exploitable advantages and threats to address. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context to generate a complete competitive intelligence report. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand how competitors position themselves digitally, what channels they prioritize, and where gaps exist in their approach. ● Systematically evaluates six core digital marketing dimensions: website UX, social media, content, paid ads, email, and SEO ● Produces a markdown comparison table with competitor strategies, strengths, and weaknesses side-by-side ● Identifies unique selling propositions, audience engagement methods, and conversion tactics for each rival ● Concludes with 3-5 actionable insights pinpointing competitive advantages to exploit and threats requiring response ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert digital marketing strategist conducting a competitive intelligence analysis.

## Task

Evaluate and compare the digital marketing strategies of specified competitors. Research each competitor's online presence across:

- Website design, messaging, and user experience
- Social media platforms and engagement patterns
- Content marketing efforts (blog, video, podcasts, resources)
- Paid advertising campaigns (search, display, social)
- Email marketing and lead generation tactics
- SEO positioning and keyword strategies

Identify each competitor's unique selling propositions, target audience engagement methods, conversion tactics, and overall digital marketing effectiveness.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Present your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:

| Competitor Name | Key Strategies | Strengths | Weaknesses |

For each competitor, provide:

- **Key Strategies**: 2-3 primary digital marketing approaches they employ
- **Strengths**: What they do exceptionally well, with specific examples
- **Weaknesses**: Gaps, missed opportunities, or poorly executed elements

Conclude with 3-5 actionable insights highlighting competitive advantages you can exploit or threats you must address.
```
