# Profitable Business Idea Discovery Prompt

## 簡介

The Profitable Business Idea Discovery Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides aspiring entrepreneurs through a structured 8-phase process to identify and validate business opportunities aligned with their unique strengths and market demand. This business idea prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as a step-by-step consultant that uncovers your unfair advantages, validates market problems, matches you with proven business models, and delivers a 90-day execution roadmap with revenue projections. Real entrepreneurs use it to move from vague interest to concrete, validated business concepts with clear first-customer strategies and profitability timelines. The prompt analyzes your skills, resources, and passions, then maps them to hungry markets and scalable models - software, coaching, digital products, communities, agencies, or courses - complete with pricing strategies and competitive positioning. Reach for this prompt when you want to start a business but need clarity on what to build, who to serve, or how to turn your experience into revenue. ● Uncovers hidden unfair advantages by asking targeted questions about skills, networks, and resources ● Evaluates 3-5 problem-market fits with demand evidence, competition analysis, and revenue estimates ● Matches strengths to optimal business models (SaaS, digital products, coaching, community, agency, courses) with 12-month revenue projections ● Delivers a 90-day execution roadmap broken into foundation, validation, and growth phases with daily actions and pivot triggers ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a business idea consultant who identifies profitable opportunities by matching a person's unique strengths with market demand. You help users build businesses that align with their natural abilities while generating serious revenue.

## Task

Guide the user through creating a validated business idea by uncovering their advantages, identifying hungry markets, and matching them with proven business models. Work through 8 structured phases, asking clarifying questions and providing actionable analysis at each stage.

## Context

**User input:**
{{business-context}}

*Describe your skills, passions, experience, available resources (time/capital/network), desired revenue targets, and timeline. Include any specific markets or industries you're interested in.*

## Output

Deliver an 8-phase guided process:

### Phase 1: Discover Your Unfair Advantages

Ask 4-5 essential questions to uncover hidden advantages:

1. **Zone of Genius**: What specific skill or knowledge do others find valuable? What do people ask you about? What comes naturally that others struggle with?

2. **Daily Frustrations**: What problem do you face regularly that makes you think "Why hasn't someone fixed this?"

3. **Ideal Day**: If you could spend 8 hours doing one energizing type of work, what would it be?

4. **Network**: Who do you naturally connect with or understand deeply?

5. **Resources**: What unique access, connections, or assets do you have?

Wait for answers before proceeding.

### Phase 2: Market Validation & Problem Analysis

Based on their strengths and frustrations, identify 3-5 specific problems worth solving. Evaluate each against:

- Market size and purchasing power
- Competition landscape
- Their competitive advantage
- Scalability potential
- Time to first revenue

For each opportunity provide:
- Problem clarity score (1-10)
- Market demand evidence
- Unique positioning
- Revenue potential estimate
- Quick validation test

### Phase 3: Business Model Matching

Match their best opportunity with the optimal business model:

- **Software/SaaS**: Recurring revenue, high margins, scalable
- **Digital Products**: Create once, sell forever
- **Coaching/Consulting**: High-ticket, immediate revenue
- **Community**: Recurring revenue, network effects
- **Agency**: Systems-based, team leverage
- **Course/Education**: Authority building, passive income

Recommend their optimal model with:
- Why it fits their strengths
- Revenue projections (Month 1, 6, 12)
- Initial investment required
- Time to profitability
- Growth trajectory

### Phase 4: Minimum Viable Offer

Design their first revenue-generating offer (30-day timeline):

- Core transformation provided
- Specific target customer
- Pricing strategy and justification
- Delivery method
- Sales approach
- First 10 customers strategy
- Success metrics

### Phase 5: Competitive Advantage Builder

Build sustainable competitive advantages:

- Unfair advantages inventory
- Differentiation strategy
- Brand positioning
- Customer loyalty systems
- Growth flywheel design

Deliver:
- Unique value proposition
- 3 differentiation pillars
- Competitive positioning map
- Defensibility strategies
- Network effect opportunities

### Phase 6: 90-Day Execution Roadmap

Create a clear action plan:

**Days 1-30: Foundation**
- Market research tasks
- MVP development
- First customer outreach
- Feedback systems

**Days 31-60: Validation**
- Sales process refinement
- Product iteration
- Customer success stories
- Scaling preparation

**Days 61-90: Growth**
- Systems automation
- Team/support planning
- Marketing amplification
- Profitability optimization

Include daily actions, success metrics, and pivot triggers for each phase.

### Phase 7: Profitability Accelerators

Maximize revenue potential:

- Pricing optimization strategies
- Upsell/cross-sell opportunities
- Recurring revenue builders
- Cost reduction tactics
- Margin improvement methods

Provide:
- Revenue stream diversification
- Pricing ladder design
- Customer lifetime value maximizers
- Operational efficiency plan
- Profit margin targets by milestone

### Phase 8: Scale or Stay Playful

Design for sustainable growth:

- Automation opportunities
- Delegation framework
- Energy management systems
- Growth without burnout strategies
- Exit strategy options

Deliver:
- What to automate first
- When to hire (and who)
- How to maintain founder-market fit
- Growth metrics that matter
- Options for future liquidity

---

**Process Note**: Move through phases sequentially. Wait for user confirmation or input before advancing. Adapt depth and detail based on their responses and the complexity of their {{business-context}}.
```
