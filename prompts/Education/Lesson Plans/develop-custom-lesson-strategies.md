# Custom Lesson Strategy Generator by Learning Style

## 簡介

The Custom Lesson Strategy Generator by Learning Style is a free AI prompt that creates differentiated lesson plans organized by learning modality for educators teaching any subject to a specific age group. This learning style lesson plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table mapping each major learning style - visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and reading/writing - to concrete teaching methods and classroom resources tailored to the developmental stage of your students. It works by first analyzing the cognitive and social characteristics typical of the target age group, then matching instructional strategies to how students at that stage best acquire and retain information, while building in differentiation for varied ability levels. Teachers use it to plan units in math, science, language arts, social studies, and elective subjects for elementary, middle, and high school classrooms. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to design inclusive lessons that engage every type of learner in your classroom, or when you want to quickly generate a scaffolded plan that addresses multiple intelligences without starting from scratch. ● Produces a three-column table (Learning Style | Teaching Method | Resources) with one row per modality, making lesson differentiation visual and actionable. ● Tailors teaching methods and resource recommendations to the cognitive and social characteristics of your specific student age group. ● Addresses visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and reading/writing learners in a single plan, ensuring no student is left without an entry point. ● Outputs age-appropriate, classroom-ready strategies and accessible resources aligned to each instructional method. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert education specialist developing personalized lesson strategies that accommodate diverse learning styles and abilities.

## Task
Create an engaging, tailored lesson plan for {{subject}} targeting {{student-age-group}}. Address visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and reading/writing learning styles with specific teaching methods and resources suited to this age group's developmental stage.

## Context
{{teaching-context}}

Analyze the cognitive and social characteristics typical of this age group, then match teaching methods to how students at this stage best acquire and retain information. Consider differentiation strategies for varied ability levels within the classroom.

## Output
Deliver your lesson plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Learning Style | Teaching Method | Resources |
|----------------|-----------------|------------|

Include one row for each major learning style (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, reading/writing). Each teaching method should be actionable and age-appropriate. Resources should be specific, accessible, and aligned to the method.
```
