# Influencer Outreach Email Writer

## 簡介

The Influencer Outreach Email Writer is a free AI prompt that generates personalized partnership pitch emails for businesses seeking influencer collaborations. It structures complete outreach messages that reference the influencer's actual work, propose tailored content ideas, and articulate mutual benefits to maximize response rates. This influencer outreach prompt for ChatGPT takes two inputs - an influencer profile (name, niche, audience, recent work) and business details (mission, products, target market) - and produces a ready-to-send email with an attention-grabbing subject line, genuine personalized opening, clear pitch, three co-branded content concepts, three concrete partnership benefits, and a simple call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Marketing teams use it to streamline influencer prospecting, agencies use it to pitch brand partnerships at scale, and solopreneurs use it to land their first creator collaborations. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic cold emails and craft outreach that demonstrates you've researched the influencer and understand their audience. ● Structures every email with subject line, personalized intro, pitch, three tailored content ideas, three partnership benefits, and call-to-action ● Emphasizes what the influencer gains - compensation, creative freedom, audience growth, exclusive access - not just brand needs ● Maintains a professional yet approachable tone that respects the creator's time and creative identity ● Produces concise, scannable emails optimized for busy influencers who receive dozens of partnership requests ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert outreach copywriter specializing in influencer collaboration emails.

## Task
Write a personalized outreach email pitching a mutually beneficial partnership between a business and an influencer.

## Context
Influencer: {{influencer-profile}}
(Include name, niche/content focus, audience demographics, recent notable work, and engagement style)

Business: {{business-details}}
(Include company name, mission, products/services, target market, and what makes this collaboration valuable)

## Output
Structure the email with:

**Subject:** Attention-grabbing line that references their work or audience

**Introduction:**
- Personalized greeting referencing specific content they've created
- Genuine compliment on their impact and audience engagement

**Pitch:**
- Brief business introduction and mission
- Clear explanation of mutual benefits and shared values

**Collaboration Ideas:**
Three specific co-branded content concepts tailored to their platform and audience

**Partnership Benefits:**
Three concrete benefits for the influencer (compensation, creative freedom, audience growth, exclusive access, etc.)

**Call-to-Action:**
- Gauge interest in exploring further
- Propose clear next step (call, meeting, or detailed proposal)

**Closing:**
Professional signature

## Requirements
- Personalize based on the influencer's actual work and audience
- Highlight the unique value proposition of this specific collaboration
- Maintain a professional yet approachable tone
- Keep the email concise and scannable
- Focus on what the influencer gains, not just what the business needs
```
