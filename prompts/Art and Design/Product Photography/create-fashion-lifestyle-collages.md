# Fashion Lifestyle Collage Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Lifestyle Collage Generator is a free AI prompt that creates 4-panel street fashion photography grids featuring a consistent character across multiple poses and angles for fashion brands, social media creators, and lookbook designers. This fashion lifestyle collage prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a professional 2×2 grid where each panel captures the same subject in different urban poses - walking confidently on a crosswalk, sitting peacefully on concrete steps, standing with her back to the camera showcasing outfit details, and leaning casually against a brick wall. The prompt controls subject styling (outfit, hair, accessories), urban environment details, and maintains consistent overcast lighting and editorial street photography aesthetics across all four panels. Fashion brands use it to create Instagram grid content, lookbook layouts, and campaign visuals without scheduling photoshoots or hiring models. Reach for this prompt when you need cohesive multi-angle fashion content that showcases an outfit from multiple perspectives in a single shareable image, ideal for streetwear brands, social media campaigns, and Gen Z-focused fashion marketing. ● Produces equal-sized panels in a 2×2 grid with the same subject, lighting, and urban environment maintained across all four shots. ● Captures front full-body, side profile seated, back view standing, and mid-shot leaning poses that showcase outfit details from every angle. ● Customizable subject styling and urban setting variables let you adapt clothing, accessories, and background environments to match brand aesthetics. ● Delivers 8k resolution editorial street photography with overcast natural lighting and clean urban Gen Z candid style. ## Prompt

```
A cohesive 4-panel street fashion collage in 2×2 grid layout, featuring the same subject across four dynamic poses and angles. Each panel is equal size with consistent lighting and environment throughout.

**Subject:** Woman in early 20s with high ponytail or messy bun, fresh urban makeup. Wearing oversized graphic hoodie, biker shorts, high socks, chunky sneakers, cross-body bag, sunglasses on head. {{subject-styling}}

**Four Panels:**
- Top Left: Front full-body walking confidently toward camera on city crosswalk, hair moving in wind, looking slightly to the side
- Top Right: Side profile sitting on concrete steps, knees pulled up, chin resting on knees, gazing peacefully at street view
- Bottom Left: Back view full-body standing and looking away at city billboard, hands in hoodie pockets, highlighting back graphic
- Bottom Right: Mid-shot leaning casually against brick wall, one leg up on wall, looking directly at camera with cool expression

**Environment:** {{urban-setting}} with brick textures, street signs, city depth.

**Lighting:** Overcast soft natural light, cool white tone, even exposure ideal for street fashion.

**Style:** Clean editorial street photography aesthetic, urban Gen Z candid vibe, 8k resolution, sharp focus on subject throughout all panels.
```
