# Flash Editorial Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Flash Editorial Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates magazine-quality fashion photography with dramatic paparazzi-style flash lighting for designers, brands, and content creators. This flash editorial fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney produces striking 1:1 images (1200x1200px) featuring hard flash lighting that creates sharp shadows and high contrast against dark backgrounds. The prompt guides the AI to pose subjects elegantly - perched on armchairs with a confident over-the-shoulder gaze - while you customize subject appearance, wardrobe styling, and nightlife-inspired interior settings. Fashion brands use it to create lookbook imagery, social media campaigns use it for attention-grabbing posts, and editorial teams generate mood boards without organizing physical shoots. It works with Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image models, and includes strict facial-feature preservation when working from reference images. Reach for this prompt when you need polished, nightclub-glamour visuals that mimic the energy of celebrity paparazzi shots but with full creative control over styling, setting, and composition. ● Direct flash photography aesthetic with hard shadows, high contrast, and dark moody backgrounds that replicate paparazzi night-scene energy. ● Elegant seated poses with over-the-shoulder camera engagement, creating confident and alluring editorial compositions. ● Full control over subject styling, wardrobe details, accessories, and luxurious interior settings with designer props. ● 1:1 square format optimized for Instagram, portfolio grids, and editorial layouts, with facial-feature preservation from reference images. ## Prompt

```
High-fashion editorial portrait, direct flash photography with paparazzi aesthetic. Hard flash creating sharp shadows and high contrast against dark background, nightlife glamour.

{{subject-appearance}}: hair, expression, styling details

Pose: perched elegantly on armchair, twisting torso to look back over shoulder, confident and alluring gaze directly at camera

{{wardrobe}}: outfit, footwear, accessories

{{setting}}: interior type and style, primary furniture piece, designer props beside subject, dimly lit background out of focus

Night scene. 1:1 aspect ratio, 1200x1200px. Preserve facial features from reference image strictly.
```
