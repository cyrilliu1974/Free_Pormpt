# IP Representations and Warranties Clause Generator

## 簡介

The IP Representations and Warranties Clause Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete IP representations and warranties provisions for mergers and acquisitions attorneys and corporate counsel. It produces professional stock purchase agreement sections covering ownership, title, non-infringement warranties, validity assurances, enforcement history, survival periods, indemnification triggers, and negotiated caps and baskets - all formatted with section headings, numbered paragraphs, and cross-references standard in M&A practice. This IP representations prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is built around ABA Model Stock Purchase Agreement standards. Reach for it when you need to allocate IP risk between parties in an acquisition, divestiture, or corporate transaction and must establish clear chain of title while addressing infringement exposure. ● Analyzes transaction details, deal structure, party dynamics, and asset composition to determine the appropriate level of protection and disclosure. ● Drafts ownership and title representations that establish clear chain of title and confirm absence of encumbrances on intellectual property assets. ● Includes non-infringement warranties addressing both existing claims and potential disputes, validity assurances for registered IP with qualifications, and enforcement history disclosures. ● Formats output as professional stock purchase agreement provisions with section headings, numbered paragraphs, subsections, and cross-references matching M&A best practices. ## Prompt
