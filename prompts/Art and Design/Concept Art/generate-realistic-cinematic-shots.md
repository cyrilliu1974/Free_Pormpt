# Realistic Cinematic Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Realistic Cinematic Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that produces film-quality images with dramatic compositions and professional color grading for photographers, filmmakers, and visual artists. This cinematic shot prompt for Midjourney creates images featuring your chosen subject atop ancient pyramids under vibrant pink skies, styled with synthwave and vaporwave color palettes mixing neon pinks and electric blues. The prompt delivers golden hour lighting, hero angle compositions, and film-grade color toning that mimics professional cinematography. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, giving you control over shot type (wide, close-up, overhead), subject details, and aspect ratio to match your project needs.
