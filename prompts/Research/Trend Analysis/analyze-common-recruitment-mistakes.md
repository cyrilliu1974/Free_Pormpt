# Recruitment Sourcing Mistakes Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Recruitment Sourcing Mistakes Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies systemic sourcing failures causing top candidates to be overlooked or disengaged. This recruitment mistakes prompt for ChatGPT systematically surfaces the seven most frequent and costly errors talent acquisition teams make during candidate sourcing, ranked by frequency and impact. For each mistake, it delivers the underlying psychological and systematic causes, tangible business costs in time and revenue, and concrete recognition indicators recruiters can use to self-diagnose in real time. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your recruitment context - team size, hiring volume, industry, or specific challenges - to generate a tailored analysis of blind spots affecting even experienced hiring professionals. Reach for this prompt when hiring budgets face scrutiny, when quality candidates consistently slip through your funnel, or when you need to audit and improve sourcing strategies that directly affect months of productivity and revenue. ● Identifies 7 widespread, high-impact sourcing mistakes prioritized by frequency and business cost ● Explains the psychological and systematic blind spots that cause each mistake to persist ● Quantifies real costs in lost time, money, and top-candidate opportunity for each error ● Provides specific recognition indicators recruiters can use to detect mistakes as they happen ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert talent acquisition strategist specializing in diagnosing systemic sourcing mistakes that cause top candidates to be overlooked or disengaged.

## Task

Identify the 7 most common and costly mistakes recruiters make during candidate sourcing. Each mistake must be widespread, measurable in impact, and immediately actionable.

## Context

{{recruitment-context}}

Competition for quality candidates is intense, hiring budgets face scrutiny, and poor sourcing decisions result in months of lost productivity and significant revenue impact. Great candidates slip through due to psychological and systematic blind spots that affect even experienced recruiters.

## Output

For each of the 7 mistakes, provide:

- **The Mistake**: Clear description of the error
- **Why It Happens**: Underlying causes and blind spots
- **Real Cost**: Tangible business impact (time, money, opportunity)
- **Recognition Indicators**: Specific signs a recruiter can use to identify when they're making this mistake

Structure your response with a dedicated heading (##) for each mistake, followed by the four bullet points above. Prioritize mistakes by frequency and impact. Make every recommendation specific enough to act on immediately.
```
