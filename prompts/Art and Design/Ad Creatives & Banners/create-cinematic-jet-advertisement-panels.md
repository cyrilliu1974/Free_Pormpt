# Cinematic Jet Advertisement Panel Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Jet Advertisement Panel Generator is a free AI prompt that produces three-panel luxury aviation banners for private jet brands, charter services, and premium travel marketers. This private jet advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates an ultra-wide 21:9 horizontal composition divided into three cinematic scenes: a hero exterior shot of the jet in golden-hour light, a sophisticated cabin interior featuring custom materials and ambient lighting, and an elite arrival moment that conveys status and effortless luxury. You control the jet exterior scene, cabin materials palette, arrival setting, and brand name, allowing you to tailor every visual element to match your aviation brand identity. Use it for website hero banners, high-end digital campaigns, editorial spreads, or investor presentations that demand photorealistic quality without the cost of full aviation photography shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive, editorial-grade visual asset that communicates exclusivity and prestige across exterior, interior, and lifestyle touchpoints in a single composition. ● Produces a single ultra-wide 21:9 banner divided into three panels - jet exterior, cabin interior, and arrival lifestyle scene - in one cohesive layout. ● Lets you specify jet exterior conditions, cabin material palettes (leather, wood, metal finishes), arrival scenes, and brand name for full creative control. ● Delivers editorial-quality, photorealistic imagery with natural lighting, balanced highlights, and cinematic color grading suitable for premium marketing. ● Outputs a horizontal format optimized for website headers, LinkedIn banners, presentation decks, and digital ad placements. ## Prompt

```
Cinematic 3×1 horizontal advertisement banner (21:9 aspect ratio, landscape orientation) for an ultra-luxury private jet, showcasing elite travel and refined global mobility.

Style: Luxury aviation advertising, cinematic realism, editorial lifestyle photography, ultra-premium composition. Professional aviation and lifestyle photography with natural daylight and cinematic enhancement, balanced highlights, soft elegant shadows.

Negative prompt: commercial airline appearance, crowded terminals, cheap materials, distorted proportions, oversaturated skies, unreadable branding, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays
```
