# Social Media Strategy Builder for Brand Growth

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Builder for Brand Growth is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable social media plans for businesses seeking to increase brand awareness and audience engagement. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, recommends the most suitable platforms, and develops tailored content approaches for each channel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a markdown table that maps platform choices to content strategies and key performance metrics. Real use cases include launching a new brand presence, revitalizing stagnant accounts, entering new markets, and coordinating multi-platform campaigns. The prompt incorporates your brand voice, competitive landscape, and current social media presence to deliver content calendar recommendations, user-generated content tactics, paid advertising strategies, and customer feedback protocols. This prompt is for marketers, founders, and brand managers who need a complete social media roadmap rather than piecemeal tactics. ● Analyzes target audience preferences and matches them to the most effective social media platforms for your business ● Builds platform-specific content strategies with posting schedules, content types, and community engagement tactics ● Defines key metrics and tracking methods to measure brand awareness growth and audience engagement ● Recommends user-generated content approaches, paid advertising strategies, and customer feedback monitoring systems ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist developing a comprehensive plan to increase brand awareness and engagement.

## Task
Create a structured, actionable social media strategy that covers:

1. Target audience analysis and their social media preferences
2. Most suitable platforms for the business
3. Tailored content strategies for each platform
4. Key metrics to measure success and engagement
5. Content calendar and posting schedule recommendations
6. User-generated content and community building tactics
7. Paid social media advertising strategies (where applicable)
8. Methods for monitoring and responding to customer feedback

## Context
**Business & Audience:** {{business-and-audience}}

**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}

**Competitive Landscape:** {{competitors}}

**Current Social Media Presence:** {{current-presence}}

## Output
Provide a brief introduction (2-3 sentences) summarizing the strategic approach.

Then present the core strategy in a markdown table with 3 columns:

| Platform | Content Strategy | Key Metrics |
|----------|------------------|-------------|

Include 3-5 platforms most relevant to the business.

Conclude with a concise paragraph (2-3 sentences) on implementation priorities and next steps.
```
