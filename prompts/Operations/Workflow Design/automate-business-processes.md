# Business Process Automation Analyzer for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Business Process Automation Analyzer for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that evaluates one workflow in your one-person business and delivers implementation-ready automation solutions with named tools and measurable time savings. This business process automation prompt for ChatGPT works by ingesting your current process steps and pain points, then recommending specific platforms, integrations, and AI tools you can configure immediately. It quantifies the impact of each suggestion in concrete terms (minutes saved per run, manual handoffs removed) and identifies which steps can be eliminated or combined outright. Solo business owners use it to transform repetitive workflows like client onboarding, content publishing, invoicing, or lead follow-up into streamlined, tool-assisted sequences. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt whenever you feel stuck repeating the same manual tasks or suspect a process could be faster but lack the time to research automation options yourself. ● Names exact automation platforms, integrations, and AI tools for each solution ● Provides step-by-step setup instructions that are implementation-ready ● Quantifies time savings in measurable terms (minutes per instance, handoffs eliminated) ● Identifies steps that can be removed entirely to simplify the workflow ## Prompt

```
## Role

You specialize in business process automation for one-person businesses. Your job is to help the user cut time and effort from a specific process by combining AI tools and automation platforms.

## Context

- Process name: {{process-name}}
- Current steps: {{current-process-steps}}
- Inefficiencies or pain points: {{current-inefficiencies}}

## Task

Analyze the process above and provide specific, actionable automation solutions the user can implement without additional research. Where steps can be removed entirely to simplify the process, say so.

## Output

### Automation Solutions

For each solution:
1. Name the specific tools, platforms, or integrations to use
2. Provide step-by-step setup instructions
3. Quantify time savings in concrete terms (e.g., "saves 15 minutes per instance" or "eliminates 3 manual handoffs")

### Process Streamlining

List steps that can be:
- Eliminated entirely
- Combined with other steps
- Simplified through different approaches

## Constraints

- Be specific: name exact tools, settings, and sequences
- Make solutions implementation-ready: no vague advice that requires further research
- Quantify impact: explain time savings in measurable terms, not generalities
```
