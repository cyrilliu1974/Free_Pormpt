# Industry Case Study Writer for Project Documentation

## 簡介

The Industry Case Study Writer for Project Documentation is a free AI prompt that transforms project details into professional case studies for business analysts, consultants, and technical writers. This case study prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce a complete analysis following a proven structure: executive summary, problem definition, solution architecture, implementation timeline, quantitative and qualitative outcomes, lessons learned, and strategic conclusions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only your project details as input to generate a polished case study that documents challenges, solutions, and measurable results. Whether you're capturing a software implementation, operational transformation, or technology deployment, the prompt ensures both data-driven metrics and narrative context are woven together in a format ready for stakeholder presentations, marketing collateral, or internal knowledge management. Reach for this prompt when you need to document a completed project with professional rigor, substantiate ROI claims with evidence, or build a portfolio of validated implementations. ● Enforces a complete case study structure with executive summary, problem/solution/implementation sections, and dual quantitative-qualitative outcome reporting ● Separates measurable results from qualitative benefits to satisfy both financial and strategic stakeholders ● Includes lessons-learned documentation to capture organizational knowledge and repeatable best practices ● Maintains analytical rigor by requiring all claims to be grounded in the project details you provide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert case study writer with deep knowledge in business, technology, and industry-specific challenges and solutions.

## Task
Write a comprehensive, evidence-based case study documenting the problem, solution, implementation, and outcomes of the project described below.

## Context
{{project-details}}

## Output
Deliver the case study in this structure:

**Case Study: [Project/Implementation] in [Industry]**

**Executive Summary**
Provide a high-level overview of the case study in 3-4 sentences.

**Problem**
- Detailed description of the problem faced
- Impact on the organization or stakeholders

**Solution**
- Overview of the solution implemented
- Key components and architecture
- Innovative or unique aspects

**Implementation**
- Timeline and phases
- Challenges encountered and how they were addressed
- Best practices followed

**Outcomes**

*Quantitative Outcomes:*
1. [Measurable result with specific metrics]
2. [Measurable result with specific metrics]
3. [Measurable result with specific metrics]

*Qualitative Outcomes:*
1. [Non-numeric benefit or improvement]
2. [Non-numeric benefit or improvement]
3. [Non-numeric benefit or improvement]

**Lessons Learned**
1. [Key lesson from the project]
2. [Key lesson from the project]
3. [Key lesson from the project]

**Conclusion**
Summarize the main points and reinforce the project's success and broader implications.

## Requirements
- Use only verified information from the project details provided
- Include specific data points to substantiate outcomes
- Maintain a professional, analytical tone appropriate for business audiences
- Ensure all claims are supported by evidence from the implementation
```
