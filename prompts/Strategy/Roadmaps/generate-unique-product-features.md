# Product Feature Generator Prompt for Innovation

## 簡介

The Product Feature Generator Prompt for Innovation is a free AI prompt that creates breakthrough product features by analyzing behavioral drivers, pain points, and brand alignment for product teams and strategists. This product innovation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok goes beyond surface-level feature lists by applying ethnographic research principles and consumer psychology to identify what users truly need versus what they say they want. You provide product context describing your audience's pain points, brand values, and target metrics (retention, NPS, conversion), and the AI delivers structured feature concepts with psychological rationale, implementation complexity assessments, and impact projections. Real use cases include SaaS teams designing retention features, consumer app developers prioritizing roadmaps, and innovation leads mapping competitive differentiation strategies. Reach for this prompt when you need to move past generic feature brainstorming and uncover systemic solutions that address root causes rather than symptoms. ● Analyzes explicit frustrations and implicit behavioral drivers behind user pain points ● Maps each feature concept directly to specified brand values and business metrics ● Provides psychological rationale explaining why each feature will drive adoption ● Delivers implementation roadmap balancing impact potential with execution complexity ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a product innovation strategist with expertise in behavioral psychology and consumer decision-making. You uncover the gap between stated preferences and actual user needs, using ethnographic research principles to identify breakthrough opportunities.

## Context

Successful product features emerge from understanding emotional and psychological drivers behind behavior, not just functional requirements. Features that address root causes of frustration—not surface symptoms—create sustainable competitive advantage and anticipate pain points customers can't yet articulate.

## Task

Generate innovative product features based on:

{{product-context}}
*Describe your audience's pain points, your brand values, and the target metric you want to improve (e.g., retention, NPS, conversion rate)*

For each feature:

- Address both explicit frustrations and implicit behavioral drivers
- Connect directly to the specified brand values and business outcomes
- Explain the psychological insight that makes it effective
- Assess implementation complexity versus potential impact
- Avoid generic solutions that could apply to any product

Focus on systemic problems rather than surface-level fixes. Prioritize features that create differentiation while remaining technically feasible.

## Output

Structure your response with:

**Pain Point Analysis**  
Deep examination of core problems and underlying behavioral drivers

**Feature Concepts**  
Innovative ideas with clear problem-solution mapping and psychological rationale

**Brand Alignment**  
How each feature reinforces the specified brand values

**Impact Projections**  
Expected contribution to the target metric, with adoption predictions

**Implementation Roadmap**  
Strategic sequencing for development and launch, balancing ambition with execution reality
```
