# Ad Copy Generator for A/B Testing and Conversion

## 簡介

The Ad Copy Generator for A/B Testing and Conversion is a free AI prompt that produces persuasive ad copy tailored to any product and target audience. It analyzes audience pain points, surfaces product benefits, and delivers three complete ad variants - each with a headline, body copy, call to action, and the emotional or strategic angle being tested - so marketers and copywriters can run split tests immediately. This ad copy prompt for ChatGPT works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying consumer psychology and conversion principles to craft messages that connect with specific audiences and drive measurable action. Reach for it when you need to launch paid campaigns, test messaging hypotheses, or explore different persuasive angles for the same offer without writing from scratch. ● Identifies target-audience pain points and matches them to product benefits automatically. ● Produces three distinct versions that test different emotional or strategic angles for the same offer. ● Includes a clear call to action and suggested performance metrics for each variant. ● Maintains consistent brand tone inferred from product details, defaulting to confident and benefit-led language. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write persuasive ad copy grounded in consumer psychology, audience targeting, and conversion principles.

## Context

- Product: {{product-details}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

Write ad copy that promotes {{product-details}} to {{target-audience}}. Follow this process:

1. Identify key characteristics, preferences, and pain points of {{target-audience}}.
2. Surface the product's unique benefits that address those pain points.
3. Note differentiating factors versus competitors.
4. Write an attention-grabbing headline.
5. Write concise, persuasive body copy focused on benefits and value.
6. Add social proof (testimonials, statistics, or usage numbers where relevant).
7. Use language that evokes the right emotions and connects with {{target-audience}}.
8. Close with a clear call to action that tells readers exactly what to do next.

**Stylistic requirements:**

- Infer the brand's tone and values from {{product-details}} and keep them consistent; if unclear, default to confident, plainspoken, and benefit-led.
- Vary sentence length to hold attention and emphasize key points.
- Use transitional phrases to guide readers through the copy.
- Include sensory or concrete language where it strengthens the message.
- Avoid jargon or technical terms that could confuse the audience.
- No grammar or spelling errors.

## Output

Produce three distinct ad copy versions to support A/B testing. For each version, use this structure:

**Version [1 / 2 / 3]**

Headline: [Headline]

Body: [Body copy]

CTA: [Call to action]

Key angle: [One sentence describing the emotional or strategic angle this version tests]

After all three versions, add a brief note on which metrics to track to evaluate performance (e.g., click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per acquisition).
```
