# Study Environment Design Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Study Environment Design Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates tailored guides for optimizing physical and digital learning spaces for students, professionals, and lifelong learners. This study environment prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive, structured guide that addresses ergonomic considerations, organization strategies, digital productivity tools, and multiple physical setup configurations based on your specific audience, learning goals, and current space constraints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming simple inputs about your learning context into actionable recommendations for desk height, chair selection, lighting, monitor positioning, clutter management systems, and tool recommendations across note-taking, focus management, and collaboration categories. Use it when designing a new study space, optimizing an existing environment, or advising students and remote workers on productivity-enhancing setups. ● Delivers specific ergonomic recommendations for desk height, seating, monitor placement, and lighting tailored to the learner's context ● Provides actionable organization strategies for managing study materials, reducing clutter, and maintaining focus over time ● Suggests curated digital productivity tools across note-taking, time management, and collaboration categories ● Outputs multiple physical setup configurations for different space constraints, budgets, and learner types with detailed layout descriptions ## Prompt
