# Editorial Content Calendar Builder for Strategic Planning

## 簡介

The Editorial Content Calendar Builder for Strategic Planning is a free AI prompt that creates organized, multi-entry editorial calendars aligned with business goals and audience needs. This editorial content calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and target audience to produce a markdown table with at least 10 calendar entries, each containing publication date, content type, topic, target audience segment, and recommended channel. It balances consistent content flow with seasonal trends, product launches, and industry events, ensuring every piece has a clear purpose and audience target. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams using different text-generation models. Use it when planning quarterly campaigns, aligning content with business objectives, or maintaining steady publishing rhythms across blogs, social media, email newsletters, and other channels. ● Produces a minimum of 10 calendar entries in a clean markdown table format with date, content type, topic, audience, and channel columns ● Analyzes business type and audience context to recommend content that serves both engagement and strategic goals ● Balances publishing cadence to maintain consistent output while adapting to seasonal opportunities and key business moments ● Assigns distinct audience segments and appropriate channels to each entry, ensuring targeted distribution and maximum relevance ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist building an editorial calendar that balances audience needs, business goals, and publication cadence.

## Task

Create a structured editorial content calendar with at least 10 entries. Each entry must specify date, content type, topic, target audience segment, and publication channel.

## Context

{{business-and-audience-context}}

Analyze this context to:
- Identify content types and topics that resonate with the audience and support business goals
- Recommend the most effective channels for each piece
- Balance the schedule to maintain consistent flow while accommodating seasonal trends, product launches, or industry events
- Assign clear purpose and audience segment to every entry

{{content-cadence}}

## Output

Present the calendar as a markdown table:

| Date | Content Type | Topic | Target Audience | Publication Channel |
|------|--------------|-------|-----------------|---------------------|

Include at least 10 entries spanning the cadence period. Ensure variety in content types and topics.
```
