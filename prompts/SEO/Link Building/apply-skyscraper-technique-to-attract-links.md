# Skyscraper Technique Link Building Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Skyscraper Technique Link Building Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a structured, actionable roadmap for earning high-quality backlinks by identifying top-performing content, building something better, and reaching out to relevant sites. This Skyscraper Technique prompt for ChatGPT walks you through three complete phases: researching the 10–15 best-ranking pieces in your niche and analyzing what makes them successful; outlining how your new content will exceed the originals in depth, data, visuals, and utility; and building a targeted outreach campaign to websites that linked to the competitor content. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to your niche, target audience, and available resources. Use it when you want to systematically replicate Brian Dean's proven link-building framework rather than guessing which content ideas will attract links. ● Identifies benchmark content by search rankings, backlink counts, and social shares, then documents specific gaps and improvement opportunities ● Specifies exactly how to surpass originals - additional research, original data, better structure, multimedia, actionable frameworks - and defines format and timeline ● Compiles backlink prospect lists, segments targets by authority and relevance, and provides personalized outreach templates with follow-up sequences ● Defines measurable success metrics including response rates, backlink conversion, and domain authority of acquired links ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in SEO and content strategy, with deep experience executing link-building campaigns.

## Task
Implement the Skyscraper Technique to attract high-quality backlinks. Deliver a structured, step-by-step plan covering research, content development, and outreach.

## Context
Niche: {{niche}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Website expertise and resources: {{website-context}}

## Output
Provide a complete implementation plan organized into three phases:

### Phase 1: Content Research & Gap Analysis
- Identify the top 10-15 performing pieces of content in the niche (search rankings, backlink counts, social shares)
- Analyze what makes each piece successful (depth, structure, visuals, data, utility)
- Document gaps, outdated information, and opportunities for improvement

### Phase 2: Superior Content Development
- Outline how the new content will surpass the originals in depth, quality, and value
- Specify enhancements: additional research, original data, better visuals, improved structure, multimedia elements, actionable frameworks
- Define content format (long-form guide, interactive tool, visual resource, case study compilation)
- Map production timeline and required resources

### Phase 3: Strategic Outreach Campaign
- Compile list of websites linking to the original content (use backlink analysis tools)
- Segment targets by domain authority, relevance, and relationship potential
- Draft personalized outreach templates emphasizing the concrete value-add of the new resource
- Create follow-up sequence and tracking system
- Define success metrics (response rate, conversion to backlink, domain authority of acquired links)

For each phase, provide actionable steps tailored to the specified expertise level and available resources.
```
