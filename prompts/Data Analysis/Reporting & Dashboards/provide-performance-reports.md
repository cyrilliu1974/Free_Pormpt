# Marketing Channel Performance Report Generator

## 簡介

The Marketing Channel Performance Report Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive performance analyses for marketing channels, comparing two time periods to reveal trends and actionable opportunities. This marketing performance report prompt for ChatGPT evaluates key metrics including conversions, ROI, customer acquisition cost, and campaign-specific KPIs. It structures output as a three-column comparison table (Metric, Previous Period, Current Period), followed by an insights summary that highlights significant changes and explains what the data reveals about campaign effectiveness. The prompt then delivers 3-5 prioritized, data-driven recommendations for optimization. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to different marketing channels including paid search, social media, email, content marketing, and affiliate programs. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform raw marketing data into executive-ready reports that clearly communicate performance shifts and next steps. ● Compares key metrics across two time periods in a clean, tabular format that highlights changes at a glance ● Delivers an insights summary that explains the story behind the numbers, identifying improvements and concerns ● Provides prioritized, actionable recommendations ranked by potential impact on channel performance ● Adapts to any marketing channel context by incorporating campaign objectives, audience characteristics, and custom metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert data analyst specializing in marketing performance measurement.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive performance report that evaluates the effectiveness of a specific marketing channel. Compare two time periods to identify trends, improvements, and opportunities.

## Context
{{marketing-channel-context}}
(Include: the channel being analyzed, the two time periods for comparison, target audience demographics/characteristics, and campaign objectives)

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

1. **Performance Table** with three columns:
   - Metric
   - Previous Period
   - Current Period

   Include these key performance metrics:
   - Conversions
   - ROI (Return on Investment)
   - Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
   - Other relevant metrics based on campaign objectives

2. **Insights Summary** below the table:
   - Highlight significant changes or trends for each metric
   - Identify improvements and areas of concern
   - Explain what the data reveals about campaign effectiveness

3. **Recommendations**:
   - Provide 3-5 data-driven recommendations to optimize channel performance
   - Prioritize actions with the highest potential impact
```
