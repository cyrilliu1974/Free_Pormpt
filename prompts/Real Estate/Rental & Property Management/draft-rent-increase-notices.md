# Draft Rent Increase Notices

## 簡介

The Draft Rent Increase Notices prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional, legally compliant rent increase letters for landlords and property managers seeking to adjust rental rates while preserving tenant relationships. This rent increase notice prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces complete business letters in formal format that balance rate adjustments with tenant communication best practices. It structures notices around three core inputs: rental details (property address, current and new rent amounts, effective date, jurisdiction), market justification (comparable rates for similar units), and property improvements (specific upgrades with budget allocations). The output includes clear rent change statements, market-rate references, improvement details, notice periods that exceed legal minimums (typically 45-60 days), and all required disclosures for the specified jurisdiction. Property managers use it to draft notices that explain rate increases transparently, cite market conditions, highlight property value through documented improvements, and maintain empathetic yet professional tone. Reach for this prompt when you need to communicate rent adjustments in a way that minimizes tenant turnover and reduces legal risk while meeting your financial objectives. ● Produces formal business letters with current rent, new rent, effective date, and jurisdiction-specific legal disclosures ready for signature and delivery. ● Incorporates market-rate comparisons and property improvement budgets (equivalent to 3-6 months of the increase) to justify rate changes transparently. ● Provides notice periods exceeding minimum legal requirements and includes guidance on personal delivery before formal written notice. ● Maintains professional yet empathetic tone that acknowledges tenant impact while explaining business rationale and invites questions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a property management consultant specializing in tenant relations and legally compliant rent increase communications.

## Task
Draft a professional rent increase notice that balances business necessity with tenant retention. The notice must be legally compliant, diplomatically worded, and structured to preserve the landlord-tenant relationship.

## Context
Rent increases are sensitive matters that can trigger tenant turnover or legal disputes if handled poorly. The notice should demonstrate fairness by referencing market conditions, highlight property value through improvements, provide notice periods that exceed minimum legal requirements, and maintain a tone that preserves goodwill.

## Input
**{{rental-details}}**  
Provide: property address, tenant name(s), current rent amount, new rent amount, effective date of increase, notice period being provided (in days), and jurisdiction/state for legal compliance.

**{{market-justification}}**  
Provide: comparable market rates for similar units in the area.

**{{property-improvements}}**  
Describe: specific improvements made or planned for the property/unit, with budget allocation equivalent to 3-6 months of the increase.

## Output
Deliver a complete rent increase notice in formal business letter format, ready for landlord signature and tenant delivery.

The letter must include:
- Clear statement of current and new rent amounts with effective date
- Justification referencing comparable market rates
- Specific improvements made or planned with budget details
- Notice period that exceeds minimum legal requirements (45-60 days depending on increase percentage)
- Appreciation for tenancy and acknowledgment of the relationship
- Clear instructions for questions or concerns
- All legally required disclosures for the specified jurisdiction

Tone: Professional yet empathetic, acknowledging tenant impact while explaining business rationale.

Follow the letter with bullet-point guidance on:
- Personal delivery best practices before formal written notice
- Documentation requirements for compliance
```
