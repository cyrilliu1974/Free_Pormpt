# Business Resource Management Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Business Resource Management Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive resource allocation and utilization strategy for businesses across industries. This resource management prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert consultant who identifies, categorizes, and optimizes your key business assets. The prompt produces a structured markdown table that maps resource types to recommended quantities, allocation strategies, and utilization methods - complete with justifications tailored to your business context and industry regulations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting variables for business context and industry-specific compliance requirements. Use it to plan staffing, equipment, materials, technology, or any asset category where efficient allocation directly impacts operational performance. ● Categorizes resource types specific to your business model and industry ● Calculates appropriate quantities based on demand patterns and seasonality ● Proposes allocation strategies that align with business goals and compliance needs ● Includes utilization recommendations to maximize return on each asset ● Provides justifications rooted in industry best practices and your unique context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert resource management consultant developing a comprehensive resource management plan that optimizes allocation and utilization of key assets.

## Task
Create a structured resource management plan that:

1. Identifies and categorizes the main resource types relevant to the business
2. Determines appropriate quantities for each resource type
3. Proposes optimal allocation strategies
4. Suggests methods to maximize utilization
5. Accounts for seasonality, demand fluctuations, and business goals
6. Incorporates industry best practices and standards
7. Provides recommendations for monitoring and adjusting the plan

## Context
**Business context:** {{business-context}}

**Industry regulations:** {{industry-regulations}}

## Output
Present your resource management plan as a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Resource Type | Quantity | Allocation | Utilization |
|--------------|----------|------------|-------------|

For each row, provide a brief justification explaining your recommendations based on the business context, challenges, and goals provided.
```
