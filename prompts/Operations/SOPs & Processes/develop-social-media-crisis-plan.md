# Social Media Crisis Management Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Crisis Management Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured crisis response frameworks for businesses and organizations facing potential online reputation issues. This social media crisis management prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete plan that covers every phase of a crisis - from detection and assessment through resolution and post-crisis evaluation. It produces a multi-section document that includes step-by-step response protocols for the first hour and first 24 hours, a roles and responsibilities matrix defining your crisis team structure, pre-approved messaging templates for scenarios like product defects, data breaches, employee misconduct, viral complaints, and misinformation, plus long-term reputation management strategies and social media monitoring workflows. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, tailoring the output to your business context and any prior crisis experience. Reach for this prompt when you need to prepare your organization for potential social media incidents, establish clear escalation triggers and stakeholder communication workflows, or update an existing crisis plan with current best practices. ● Produces a complete response protocol covering detection, assessment, activation, response, and resolution phases with specific actions for each stage. ● Defines a roles and responsibilities matrix assigning spokesperson, social media lead, legal liaison, executive decision-maker, and customer service coordinator functions. ● Generates pre-approved messaging templates for five common crisis scenarios, ensuring consistent and rapid communication. ● Includes social media monitoring protocols with tool recommendations, alert thresholds, escalation triggers, and brand message consistency guidelines throughout the crisis lifecycle. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media crisis manager developing a comprehensive crisis management plan.

## Task
Create a robust strategy for handling online reputation issues. The plan must include:

- **Step-by-step response protocol** covering detection, assessment, activation, response, and resolution phases
- **Roles and responsibilities matrix** defining who does what (spokesperson, social media lead, legal liaison, executive decision-maker, customer service coordinator)
- **Pre-approved messaging templates** for common scenarios (product defect, data breach, employee misconduct, customer complaint viral spread, misinformation)
- **Immediate response tactics** (first 1 hour, first 24 hours)
- **Long-term reputation management strategies** (weeks 1-4 and beyond)
- **Post-crisis evaluation procedures** (metrics, debriefing, plan updates)
- **Social media monitoring protocols** (tools, alert thresholds, escalation triggers)
- **Stakeholder communication workflows** (internal and external)
- **Brand message consistency guidelines** throughout the crisis lifecycle

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{prior-crisis-experience}}

## Output
Deliver the crisis management plan in a structured format with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points optimized for quick reference during active crisis situations. Make all sections immediately actionable.
```
