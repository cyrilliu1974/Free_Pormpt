# Elegant Earring Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Elegant Earring Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates polished close-up photographs of earrings in a professional studio setting for jewelry designers, e-commerce sellers, and product photographers. This earring photography prompt for Midjourney creates images of earrings resting on ivory linen with soft warm lighting, shallow depth of field, and sharp focus on gemstones in a 4:5 aspect ratio. You specify the earring details - metal type, gemstone cut, color, setting style - and the prompt produces an elegant, sophisticated studio shot suitable for product listings, lookbooks, or marketing materials. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana image generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need high-end jewelry photography without a physical studio or when iterating quickly on different earring styles and presentations. ● Produces close-up shots with ivory linen backdrop, soft warm studio lighting, and shallow depth of field that isolates the jewelry ● Maintains sharp focus on gemstones and metal details while creating an elegant, sophisticated atmosphere ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images optimized for vertical product displays and social media ● Accepts custom earring specifications including metal type, stone cut, color palette, and setting details ## Prompt

```
Close-up photograph of {{earring-details}} resting on ivory linen; studio setup with soft warm lighting, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on gemstones — elegant, sophisticated atmosphere --ar 4:5
```
