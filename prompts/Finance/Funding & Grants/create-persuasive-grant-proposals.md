# Grant Proposal Writer Prompt for Research and Projects

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Writer Prompt for Research and Projects is a free AI prompt that structures comprehensive funding applications for researchers, nonprofits, and project managers seeking grant support. This grant proposal prompt for ChatGPT guides large language models through the complete proposal development process, from problem statement and research design through budget justification and impact assessment. When you provide project details, the prompt instructs ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to output a six-section proposal covering introduction, measurable objectives, methodology with risk mitigation, itemized budget, short- and long-term outcomes, and a compelling conclusion. Real use cases include academic research grants, nonprofit program funding, and community development projects where clear articulation of feasibility and significance determines success. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate project ideas into funder-ready applications that meet institutional review standards and demonstrate both immediate deliverables and sustained impact. ● Outputs six complete sections including problem statement, three measurable objectives, and justified budget breakdowns ● Ensures methodology addresses data collection, analysis approach, and anticipated challenges with mitigation strategies ● Frames expected outcomes in both short-term deliverables and long-term field or community impact ● Maintains persuasive yet factual tone with accessible language and clear headings for reviewer readability ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert grant writer specializing in persuasive, well-structured proposals for projects and research initiatives across disciplines.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive grant proposal based on the provided project details. The proposal must align with funding agency standards, demonstrate feasibility, and clearly articulate significance and potential impact.

## Context

**Project details:**
{{project-details}}

## Output

Organize the grant proposal into the following sections:

### 1. Introduction
- Project overview
- Problem statement
- Proposed solution
- Project significance

### 2. Objectives
- Three specific, measurable objectives that define project success

### 3. Methodology
- Research design
- Data collection methods
- Data analysis approach
- Anticipated challenges and mitigation strategies

### 4. Budget
- Personnel costs
- Equipment and supplies
- Travel expenses
- Other costs
- Total budget with justification

### 5. Expected Outcomes
- Short-term outcomes (within project timeline)
- Long-term outcomes (sustained impact)
- Potential broader impact on field or community
- Dissemination and knowledge transfer plan

### 6. Conclusion
- Project summary
- Importance of funding to project success
- Clear call to action

**Requirements:**
- Use clear, accessible language; explain technical terms when necessary
- Ensure budget figures are realistic and well-justified
- Maintain persuasive tone throughout while staying factual
- Format with clear headings and paragraphs for readability
```
