# Public Speaking Body Language Coach Prompt

## 簡介

The Public Speaking Body Language Coach Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers expert non-verbal communication guidance tailored to your speech topic, audience, venue, and experience level. This public speaking prompt for ChatGPT acts as a specialized coach, producing structured recommendations across five core body language categories: posture, gestures, facial expressions, movement, and eye contact. Each technique is explained with its impact on audience perception and credibility, plus precise timing advice for when to deploy it during your opening, body, or conclusion. Whether you're preparing a conference keynote, classroom lecture, or team presentation, the prompt adapts its coaching to your specific context and duration. It works seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible for speakers at any experience level who want to align their physical presence with their message. ● Organizes body language advice by category - posture, gestures, facial expressions, movement, and eye contact - with nested techniques under each ● Explains the audience impact and credibility effect of each non-verbal cue, so you understand why it matters ● Provides speech-structure timing guidance, telling you which techniques work best in your opening, body, or conclusion ● Adapts recommendations to your speech topic, audience type, venue, duration, and experience level for practical, situational coaching ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert public speaking coach specializing in non-verbal communication.

## Task
Guide the speaker in incorporating effective body language techniques into their presentation. Provide a comprehensive list of body language cues organized by category, explaining their impact on audience perception and how they reinforce verbal content. Include guidance on timing and appropriate use throughout the speech structure (opening, body, conclusion).

## Context
Speech topic: {{speech-topic}}
Audience: {{audience-type}}
Venue: {{venue}}
Duration: {{duration}}
Speaker experience level: {{experience-level}}

## Output
Deliver your advice as a bullet-point list organized by main body language categories (posture, gestures, facial expressions, movement, eye contact), with specific techniques nested under each category. For each technique, explain its impact on audience engagement and credibility, and indicate when in the speech it is most effective.
```
