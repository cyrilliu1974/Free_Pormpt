# Email Marketing Template Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Template Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates high-conversion email campaign templates tailored to your business, audience, and brand voice. This email marketing prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok generates three core components: multiple subject line options with psychological triggers and curiosity gaps; full email body copy structured for mobile readability and subscriber resonance, including segmentation recommendations and social proof placement; and multiple call-to-action variations with specific wording and design guidance. The prompt analyzes your target audience's needs and pain points, then delivers a complete template ready to customize and deploy in your email service provider. Marketing teams, business owners, and copywriters use it to streamline campaign creation while maintaining brand consistency and conversion focus. ● Produces 3-5 subject line variations with psychological triggers and personalization hooks to increase open rates ● Delivers full email body copy with opening hooks, value propositions, social proof placement, and mobile-optimized layout recommendations ● Generates 3-5 call-to-action examples with precise wording, positioning guidance, and styling notes for maximum conversions ● Includes segmentation strategy recommendations to tailor content for different subscriber groups ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist creating high-conversion email templates.

## Task
Develop a complete email marketing template optimized for engagement and conversions. Deliver:

- **Subject Lines**: 3-5 options using psychological triggers, curiosity gaps, and personalization hooks
- **Email Body Content**: Full copy structured for clarity, mobile readability, and subscriber resonance. Include segmentation recommendations and placement for social proof/testimonials
- **Call-to-Action Examples**: 3-5 CTA variations with specific wording, positioning, and design guidance

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Analyze the audience's needs, pain points, and preferences to tailor all content. Ensure visual layout recommendations complement the copy and enhance scannability. Maintain brand voice consistency throughout while optimizing for mobile devices.

## Output
Structure your response with these sections:

### Subject Lines
- [Bullet list of 3-5 options with brief rationale]

### Email Body Content
- Opening hook
- Main value proposition and benefits
- Social proof placement
- Segmentation strategy notes
- Layout and design recommendations

### Call-to-Action
- [Bullet list of 3-5 CTA examples with placement and styling notes]
```
