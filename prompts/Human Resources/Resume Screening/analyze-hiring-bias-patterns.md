# Hiring Bias Audit and Reduction Program Prompt

## 簡介

The Hiring Bias Audit and Reduction Program Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes hiring practices and designs evidence-based interventions to reduce unconscious bias in talent acquisition for HR teams and organizational leaders. This hiring bias analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an organizational psychologist who examines your hiring data, identifies specific bias patterns in demographic outcomes and decision-making, then builds a tailored program including anonymous resume screening, standardized interview protocols, diverse evaluation panels, and scoring criteria. It produces a structured roadmap with measurement metrics, timeline recommendations, and both quick wins and long-term initiatives. Organizations use it to interrupt automatic judgments in recruiting, implement name-blind applications, require written justifications for hiring decisions, and track flow-through rates across demographic groups. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to diagnose where bias enters your hiring funnel and build a practical, measurable program to improve equity and decision quality. ● Conducts a bias audit analyzing current hiring data, demographic outcomes, and decision patterns to pinpoint where bias manifests ● Designs structured interventions including anonymous screening, standardized behavioral interviews, predetermined scoring rubrics, and diverse evaluation panels ● Creates measurement systems tracking demographic flow-through rates, interviewer consistency scores, and decision pattern analysis ● Delivers an implementation roadmap with specific metrics, timelines, ROI indicators for leadership, and actionable steps prioritized for practical adoption ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert organizational psychologist and bias mitigation specialist combining behavioral economics research with practical HR systems design.

## Task

Analyze the hiring practices below and design a comprehensive bias reduction program covering:

1. **Bias Audit**: Assess hiring data, demographic outcomes, and decision patterns to identify specific bias manifestations
2. **Intervention Design**: Create structured interventions including anonymous resume screening, standardized interview protocols, diverse evaluation panels, and predetermined scoring criteria
3. **Process Modifications**: Implement techniques that interrupt automatic judgments—name-blind applications, structured behavioral interviews, mandatory written justifications for hiring decisions
4. **Measurement Systems**: Define metrics to track bias indicators including demographic flow-through rates, interviewer consistency scores, and decision pattern analysis
5. **Implementation Roadmap**: Provide actionable recommendations tailored to the organization's patterns, constraints, and culture

## Context

{{organization-hiring-context}}

## Output

Structure your response with clear section headings and actionable implementation steps in bullet format, including:

- Specific measurement metrics
- Timeline recommendations
- ROI indicators leadership can track
- Quick wins and longer-term initiatives

Prioritize practical adoption within the stated constraints.
```
