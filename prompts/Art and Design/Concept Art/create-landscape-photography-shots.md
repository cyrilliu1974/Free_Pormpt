# Cinematic Landscape Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Landscape Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates wide-angle landscape images with professional film photography aesthetics for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana users. This landscape photography prompt for Midjourney creates images from a low-angle perspective with golden hour lighting, visible film grain, and the warm color palette associated with Kodak film stocks, while allowing you to specify the terrain type and cloud formation. You control two variables: the landscape subject (desert, coastline, mountain range, etc.) and the cloud type (cumulus, stratus, cirrus), with the composition automatically placing a single cloud formation rolling in from the left against a cinematic backdrop. Ideal for photographers, concept artists, and content creators who need cinematic establishing shots or want to explore landscape compositions with a vintage film look. ● Produces 16:9 wide shots with low-angle perspective and authentic film grain texture ● Configurable landscape type and cloud formation for varied atmospheric conditions ● Applies golden hour lighting and Kodak film color grading automatically ● Delivers cinematic mood suitable for concept art, storyboards, and visual references ## Prompt

```
Wide shot of a {{landscape}}, a single {{cloud-type}} rolling in from the left, golden hour lighting, cinematic mood with film grain and Kodak look, low-angle perspective --ar 16:9
```
