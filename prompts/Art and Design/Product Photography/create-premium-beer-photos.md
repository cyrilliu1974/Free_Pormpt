# Premium Beer Bottle Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Premium Beer Bottle Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional commercial studio shots of beer bottles with hard rim lighting, reflective surfaces, and advertising-grade symmetry. This beer bottle photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images with clean highlights from softbox top-lighting, textured detail from hard rim lights, and a reflective black surface that mimics high-end product photography studios. The prompt simulates a 100mm f/11 product lens with crisp edges and a 4:5 aspect ratio ideal for packaging mockups, social media ads, and print campaigns. It's built for breweries, beverage brands, marketing agencies, and designers who need commercial-quality product visuals without a studio rental or professional photographer. ● Produces hard rim lighting that reveals bottle texture, condensation, and frosted glass detail with precision. ● Creates softbox top-light for clean highlights and a premium advertising aesthetic matching commercial campaigns. ● Uses a reflective black surface to anchor the product and add depth, simulating professional studio setups. ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images with perfect symmetry and crisp edges, optimized for Instagram, packaging, and print. ## Prompt
