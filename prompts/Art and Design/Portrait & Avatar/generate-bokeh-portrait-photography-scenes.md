The Bokeh Portrait Photography Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates serene portrait scenes with warm bokeh lighting, blurred urban backgrounds, and customizable text overlays for lifestyle brands and content creators. This bokeh portrait photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces atmospheric images of subjects holding mugs while gazing at city views, with fairy lights and soft background blur creating a contemplative, peaceful mood. You define the subject description, location setting, and overlay text to match your brand aesthetic. Use it for coffee brand campaigns, wellness marketing, lifestyle blog headers, or any project needing intimate, relatable visual content that feels authentic and cinematic.
