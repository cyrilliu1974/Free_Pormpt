# 4-Week Content Calendar Builder for Social Media

## 簡介

The 4-Week Content Calendar Builder for Social Media is a free AI prompt that generates a structured content plan organized around four strategic pillars for content creators, marketers, and businesses managing social presence. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT produces 16 complete content ideas across four weeks, with each week featuring exactly one piece from each pillar: Educational (teaching value), Entertaining (engagement-focused), Thought-Provoking (challenging assumptions), and Empathetic (emotional connection). Every content idea includes a core message, recommended format (carousel, video, text post, infographic, or story), a compelling headline, and a three-point outline. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to any niche and target audience you specify. Marketing teams use it to plan consistent campaigns, solo creators use it to maintain posting momentum without creative burnout, and agencies use it to present clients with ready-to-execute strategies. Reach for this prompt when you need a balanced content mix that addresses audience pain points and interests through varied angles and formats, or when you want to eliminate guesswork from content planning. ● Cycles through Educational, Entertaining, Thought-Provoking, and Empathetic pillars each week to maintain audience interest and strategic balance ● Outputs headline, format recommendation, message takeaway, and three-point outline for every content idea ● Adapts to any niche and target audience through two simple variables ● Prevents content repetition by ensuring no pillar appears twice in the same week ## Prompt

## Context
