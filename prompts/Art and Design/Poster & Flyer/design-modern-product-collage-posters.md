# Product Collage Poster Generator for Multi-Angle Layouts

## 簡介

The Product Collage Poster Generator for Multi-Angle Layouts is a free AI prompt that creates catalog-quality grid posters featuring products photographed from side, rear, top-down, and close-up perspectives with professional studio lighting and brand typography. This product collage poster prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces photorealistic images with warm sunset-to-black gradient backgrounds, centered brand headlines, taglines, and lifestyle descriptor text blocks. The structured layout mimics 85mm–100mm product photography, rendering sharp material textures, soft shadows, and balanced exposure across all product angles. Real-world use cases include e-commerce hero images, social media ad creatives, print catalogs, and brand lookbooks that need to communicate design details, build quality, and brand identity in a single cohesive visual. Reach for this prompt when you need multi-perspective product visuals without booking a studio shoot or owning professional lighting gear. ● Renders products from side profile, rear branding view, top-down interior texture, close-up logo detail, and hero stacked foreground with realistic shadows. ● Applies warm amber and terracotta color palettes with soft black accents and clean studio lighting for approachable, casual-confidence mood. ● Embeds custom brand name headlines and taglines with clean typography and subtle lifestyle descriptor text in the background. ● Outputs catalog-lifestyle hybrid compositions with photorealistic material accuracy, sharp focus, and balanced exposure - no harsh reflections or CGI appearance. ## Prompt

```
Modern product collage poster in structured grid layout featuring {{product}} from multiple angles: side profile showing silhouette and design details, rear view with branding, top-down view of interior texture and build quality, close-up of base and logo, hero image in foreground with stacked depth and soft shadows. Warm amber and terracotta tones with soft black accents, soft studio lighting, photorealistic material-accurate texture. 85mm–100mm product photography style, sharp focus on product, balanced exposure, no overexposure.
```
