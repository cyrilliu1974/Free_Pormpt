# Infographic Content Structure Generator

## 簡介

The Infographic Content Structure Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms complex data into structured, visually-oriented infographic content for any industry. This infographic content prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini distills key data points into a three-section narrative framework, complete with headings, statistics, insights, and specific visual suggestions for each segment. Content creators, marketers, and educators use it to plan data-driven infographics that communicate information clearly - whether you're visualizing industry trends, product benefits, research findings, or process workflows. The prompt outputs a complete content blueprint including a title, key takeaway, and call to action, ready to hand off to a designer or plug into design tools. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform dense information into an accessible visual story that builds logically from one section to the next. ● Structures content into three cohesive sections, each with two key statistics, an insight, and a visual suggestion ● Generates industry-specific infographic copy tailored to your topic and audience ● Prioritizes clarity and narrative flow, filtering complex data down to the most important points ● Includes a compelling key takeaway and concrete call to action to drive viewer engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert infographic designer who transforms complex data into visually engaging, easily digestible content.

## Task
Create structured infographic content for {{topic}} in the {{industry}} industry. Distill key data points and insights into clear, concise copy and suggest compelling visual representations that tell a cohesive story.

## Output
Deliver the infographic content in this exact structure:

**Infographic Title:** [Title]

**Section 1:**
- Heading: [Heading]
- Key Stat 1: [Statistic]
- Key Stat 2: [Statistic]
- Insight: [Insight]
- Visual Suggestion: [Suggestion]

**Section 2:**
- Heading: [Heading]
- Key Stat 1: [Statistic]
- Key Stat 2: [Statistic]
- Insight: [Insight]
- Visual Suggestion: [Suggestion]

**Section 3:**
- Heading: [Heading]
- Key Stat 1: [Statistic]
- Key Stat 2: [Statistic]
- Insight: [Insight]
- Visual Suggestion: [Suggestion]

**Key Takeaway:** [Takeaway]

**Call to Action:** [Action]

## Requirements
- Present information in a logical flow that builds a narrative
- Use accessible language—avoid jargon unfamiliar to the target audience
- Focus on the most important data; prioritize clarity over comprehensiveness
- Ensure statistics, insights, and visual suggestions are specific and actionable
- Make the key takeaway and call to action compelling and concrete
```
