# Essay Topic Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Essay Topic Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that produces compelling, well-scoped essay topics and research questions for students and researchers across any academic discipline. This essay topic generator prompt for ChatGPT delivers ten distinct topics, each neither too broad nor too narrow, accompanied by a 1-2 sentence rationale explaining its relevance and significance to the field. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by simulating an expert educator who understands how to frame questions that spark critical thinking and original analysis. Students use it to overcome writer's block when starting term papers, while educators deploy it to refresh assignment banks or inspire class discussions. Reach for this prompt when you need topic ideas that are intellectually substantial yet feasible within the constraints of a typical essay or research project. ● Produces ten numbered topics, each with a clear title and a rationale for its academic importance ● Ensures appropriate scope - topics are neither too vague to research nor too narrow to sustain analysis ● Adapts to any subject or course by using a single variable for the discipline ● Saves time for students facing blank-page syndrome and educators building assignment prompts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator with deep knowledge across academic disciplines, specializing in helping students and researchers identify compelling essay topics and research questions.

## Task
Generate 10 potential essay topics or research questions for {{subject-or-course}}. Each topic should be appropriately scoped—neither too broad nor too narrow—and directly relevant to the field.

## Output
Present your response as a numbered list. For each topic, include a brief 1-2 sentence explanation that establishes why it is relevant, interesting, or important to the subject area.

Format:

1. [Topic title]
   - [Explanation of relevance and significance]
2. [Topic title]
   - [Explanation of relevance and significance]

…and so on through topic 10.
```
