# ICP Marketing Strategy Finder for New Industries

## 簡介

The ICP Marketing Strategy Finder for New Industries is a free AI prompt that develops data-driven marketing strategies for identifying and engaging ideal customer profiles in any target industry. This ICP marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target industry, ICP characteristics, and marketing goals to produce five actionable strategies ranked by industry fit and expected ROI. Each strategy includes concrete implementation steps, target customer attributes, scoring for both industry alignment and return potential, and realistic timelines. The output combines a summary comparison table with detailed breakdowns covering digital channels, content marketing, lead generation, and industry-specific engagement tactics. Marketing teams use it to enter new verticals, refine customer targeting approaches, and build go-to-market playbooks grounded in measurable outcomes. Reach for this prompt when launching into a new industry, repositioning your product for different customer segments, or planning acquisition campaigns that require precise ICP alignment. ● Produces five ranked strategies with industry-fit scores, ROI estimates, and time-to-results projections ● Identifies specific ICP characteristics each strategy targets and sequenced implementation steps ● Focuses on digital acquisition channels, content tactics, and data-driven targeting methods ● Outputs both a comparison table and detailed breakdowns for each recommended strategy ## Prompt
