# Fashion Portrait Transformation Prompt

## 簡介

The Fashion Portrait Transformation Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality fashion photography while maintaining exact facial features and body proportions from a reference image. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces studio portraits with contrapposto poses, refined hair styling, and sculptural lighting that defines bone structure and fabric detail. Fashion brands, e-commerce teams, and creative studios use it to generate consistent model imagery across campaigns without arranging new photo shoots, controlling garment aesthetic and setting details through two simple variables. Reach for it when you need magazine-quality portraits that match a specific model's appearance but require different styling, poses, or environments. ● Maintains reference facial features and body proportions while adding refined hair volume, glossy definition, and enhanced styling details. ● Produces full-body studio portraits in contrapposto alignment with hands clasped low, head tilted, and torso gently twisted toward camera. ● Renders customizable fashion garments with ornate metallic accessories, structured bodice elements, and detailed fabric folds. ● Applies sculptural studio lighting that creates an ethereal yet commanding mood and defines bone structure with professional precision. ## Prompt
