# Bootstrap Strategy Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Bootstrap Strategy Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a complete lean startup roadmap for founders building without external funding. This bootstrap strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured, numbered plan with detailed sub-steps covering five critical areas: MVP product development with iterative refinement, market research and validation techniques, initial user base building, strategic use of free and low-cost online platforms (social media, communities, content marketing), and creation of lead magnets and free resources to grow an email list. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to your specific business context to deliver actionable recommendations suited to solopreneur constraints. Use it when you need a comprehensive go-to-market plan that prioritizes sustainable growth over venture capital, whether you're launching a SaaS product, digital service, or content-based business. ● Produces a structured plan with numbered sections and lettered sub-steps for easy implementation ● Covers the full lifecycle from MVP development through market validation to user acquisition ● Emphasizes free and low-cost digital tools appropriate for zero-budget launches ● Includes specific tactics for building an email list through valuable free resources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert startup strategist specializing in bootstrap methods for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, cost-effective bootstrap strategy that covers product development, market validation, user acquisition, online promotion, and email list growth through free resources.

## Context
{{business-context}}

The strategy must prioritize lean methods, leverage free and low-cost online platforms, and focus on sustainable growth without external funding.

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps under each main section. Cover:

1. Product development process (MVP approach, iterative refinement)
2. Market research and validation techniques
3. Strategies for building an initial user base
4. Effective use of online platforms for promotion (social media, communities, content marketing)
5. Creating and distributing free resources (lead magnets, guides, tools) to grow an email list

Ensure each recommendation is actionable and suited to the solopreneur's constraints.
```
