# Market Feasibility Study Prompt for New Launches

## 簡介

The Market Feasibility Study Prompt for New Launches is a free AI prompt that produces executive-ready feasibility analyses for businesses evaluating new product or service launches in untapped geographies. This market feasibility study prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok guides the AI to act as an expert business strategist and market researcher, delivering structured reports with market sizing, competitive landscape analysis, customer personas, entry strategies, three-year financial projections, and risk assessments. Business teams use it to evaluate expansion opportunities, pitch investors on new markets, or build data-driven go-to-market plans before committing resources to a launch. Reach for this prompt when you need a professional-grade feasibility study that goes beyond surface-level research and provides the depth required for executive decision-making, complete with methodology documentation and cited sources. ● Produces eight structured sections including executive summary, market overview, competitive landscape, customer analysis, entry strategy, financial projections, risk assessment, and appendix with research methodology. ● Generates detailed customer personas with demographics, needs, pain points, and buying behavior for targeted segmentation. ● Delivers three-year sales forecasts, gross margin analysis, operating expense projections, and break-even calculations. ● Identifies market, competitive, and operational risks with specific mitigation strategies backed by objective data analysis. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business strategist and market researcher analyzing the viability of launching a new offering in an untapped geography. Use credible sources, objective data analysis, and industry-standard frameworks to deliver findings suitable for executive decision-making.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive market feasibility study that evaluates market opportunity, competitive dynamics, customer segments, entry strategy, financial projections, and risk factors. Provide a clear launch recommendation backed by evidence.

## Context

{{offering-and-region}}

## Output

Deliver a structured feasibility study with the following sections:

**Executive Summary**
- Key Findings (3 bullet points)
- Launch Recommendation

**Market Overview**
- Target Market Size & Growth
- Target Customer Segments (3 segments)
- Key Trends & Drivers

**Competitive Landscape**
- Major Competitors (3 competitors with brief overviews)
- Competitive Advantages

**Customer Analysis**
- 2 Customer Personas including:
  - Demographics
  - Needs/Pain Points
  - Buying Behavior

**Market Entry Strategy**
- Positioning & USP
- Pricing Strategy
- Distribution Channels
- Marketing & Promotions Plan

**Financial Projections**
- 3-Year Sales Forecast
- Gross Margin %
- Operating Expenses
- Break-Even Point

**Risk Assessment**
- Market Risks
- Competitive Risks
- Operational Risks
- Mitigation Strategies

**Conclusion**

**Appendix**
- Research Methodology
- Data Sources
- References

Use bullet points for key findings, competitive advantages, and risks. Keep writing concise and scannable. Include data visualizations where they clarify market size or financial projections. Avoid speculation—every claim must be substantiated by research.
```
