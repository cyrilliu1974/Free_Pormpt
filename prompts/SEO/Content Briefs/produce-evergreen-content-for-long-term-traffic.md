# Evergreen Content Generator for Long-Term SEO Traffic

## 簡介

The Evergreen Content Generator for Long-Term SEO Traffic is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, SEO-optimized articles focused on timeless value and sustained organic visibility for content strategists and marketing teams. This evergreen content prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write full-length articles structured around enduring principles rather than fleeting trends. It incorporates your target keywords naturally, applies dependency grammar for logical flow, and delivers a complete piece with meta descriptions, proper header hierarchy, practical examples, internal linking suggestions, and calls-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, asking the model to act as an expert content strategist who understands both SEO fundamentals and the principles of creating content that remains valuable for years. Reach for this prompt when you need content that will continue attracting search traffic and engaging readers long after publication, whether you're building a knowledge base, launching a content marketing initiative, or establishing topical authority in your niche. ● Structures articles using dependency grammar principles to ensure coherent, logical flow between ideas and sentences ● Incorporates target keywords naturally throughout headers, body content, and meta descriptions without keyword stuffing ● Focuses on timeless principles and enduring aspects of topics rather than time-sensitive trends or news ● Delivers complete articles with proper markdown formatting, internal linking opportunities, examples, and calls-to-action ready to publish ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in evergreen SEO content that drives sustained organic traffic.

## Task
Write a comprehensive, SEO-optimized article on {{topic}} that will remain valuable and relevant for years. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure coherence and logical flow between sentences and ideas.

## Context
**SEO Parameters:**
- Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Content goal: {{content-goal}}

Incorporate the target keywords naturally throughout the content. Focus on timeless aspects and enduring principles rather than trends or time-sensitive information. Address questions and concerns this audience typically has about the topic.

## Output
Deliver a complete article with:

**Meta description** (at the top): 150-160 characters summarizing the content and including primary keywords

**Article structure:**
- Compelling header tags (H1, H2, H3) incorporating target keywords naturally
- In-depth sections providing lasting value
- Practical examples, case studies, or supporting data
- Strategic internal linking opportunities marked as [link: anchor text → suggested destination]
- Concluding summary of key takeaways
- Call-to-action encouraging reader engagement

Format as clean markdown with proper heading hierarchy and paragraph breaks.
```
