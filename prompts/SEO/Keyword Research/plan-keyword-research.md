# Keyword Research Plan Prompt for Revenue-Focused SEO

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Plan Prompt for Revenue-Focused SEO is a free AI prompt that builds strategic keyword research plans for businesses prioritizing commercial and transactional search intent over vanity metrics. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context to produce five core sections: keyword clusters organized by intent (foundation terms, commercial investigation phrases, transactional keywords, and long-tail opportunities), competitor gap analysis highlighting ranking opportunities, a prioritization framework that ranks keywords by conversion potential rather than volume alone, tool recommendations matched to your budget (both free and premium options), and an implementation roadmap with sequenced action steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable plans that identify the search terms customers use when ready to buy. Marketing teams use it to shift focus from traffic metrics to revenue-generating keywords, content strategists use it to discover competitor content gaps, and SEO consultants use it to build intent-based keyword strategies for clients. ● Organizes keywords into intent-based clusters (informational, commercial investigation, transactional) to align content with buyer journey stages. ● Analyzes competitor content gaps and ranking opportunities to uncover high-converting keywords others overlook. ● Delivers a prioritization framework that evaluates keywords by conversion potential, difficulty, and competitive landscape - not volume alone. ● Recommends specific research tools (free and premium) with guidance on interpreting keyword difficulty and intent signals for your budget. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an SEO strategist specializing in revenue-focused keyword research. You prioritize commercial and transactional intent over volume metrics, identifying search terms customers use when ready to buy.

## Task

Create a comprehensive keyword research plan for the provided business. Organize keywords into clusters based on search intent, analyze competitor gaps, and build a prioritization framework centered on conversion potential.

## Context

**Business Information:**
{{business-context}}

Your plan should:

- Map short-tail foundation terms, long-tail opportunity phrases, and transactional keywords indicating purchase readiness
- Classify keywords by search intent (informational, commercial, transactional)
- Identify competitor content gaps and ranking opportunities
- Recommend both free and premium research tools appropriate for the stated budget
- Provide guidance on interpreting keyword difficulty, intent signals, and competitive landscape
- Rank opportunities by conversion potential, not volume alone

## Output

Structure your keyword research plan with these sections:

**1. Keyword Clusters by Intent**
- Foundation (short-tail) keywords
- Commercial investigation phrases
- Transactional (high-intent) terms
- Long-tail opportunities

**2. Competitor Gap Analysis**
- Terms competitors rank for that represent opportunities
- Content gaps in the current market

**3. Prioritization Framework**
- Priority tier assignments (high/medium/low)
- Rationale based on intent + difficulty + opportunity

**4. Tool Recommendations**
- Specific tools matched to the budget
- How to use each for intent analysis and difficulty assessment

**5. Implementation Roadmap**
- Sequenced action steps
- Quick wins vs. long-term plays

Provide specific, actionable detail in each section for immediate implementation.
```
