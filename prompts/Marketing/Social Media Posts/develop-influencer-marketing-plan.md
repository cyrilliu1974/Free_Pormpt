# Influencer Marketing Plan Builder for Campaigns

## 簡介

The Influencer Marketing Plan Builder for Campaigns is a free AI prompt that generates a structured, actionable influencer collaboration strategy for brands and marketers promoting products or services. This influencer marketing prompt for ChatGPT walks you through eight critical sections: target audience analysis (demographics and psychographics), identification of three relevant influencers with follower counts and engagement rates, tailored engagement strategies specifying collaboration types and content formats, a three-phase execution timeline, performance metrics with tracking methods, itemized budget allocation across compensation and paid amplification, contingency planning for realistic challenges, and strategic takeaways for optimization. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a ready-to-execute campaign document. Marketing teams use it to launch product campaigns, agencies rely on it for client proposals, and solo founders apply it to plan their first influencer outreach without hiring a consultant. ● Identifies three influencers ranked by follower count, engagement rate, and niche fit, with specific handles and platform details ● Defines collaboration type, content format, and key messaging for each influencer to align with campaign goals ● Provides a three-phase timeline with objectives and deliverables, plus budget allocation across compensation, production, and paid amplification ● Includes contingency strategies for common challenges and three actionable insights for long-term relationship building ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in influencer marketing, specializing in selecting and engaging industry influencers for product and service promotion campaigns.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive influencer collaboration plan for promoting {{product-or-service}}. The plan must be actionable, logically structured, and cover all phases from audience analysis through execution and optimization.

## Output
Deliver the plan organized into these sections:

### 1. Target Audience Analysis
- **Demographics**: Age range, gender, location
- **Psychographics**: Interests, behaviors, pain points

### 2. Influencer Identification
Identify 3 influencers based on follower count, engagement rate, and niche relevance. For each:
- Name/handle and platform
- Follower count and engagement rate
- Niche relevance to the product

### 3. Influencer Engagement Strategy
For each identified influencer, specify:
- Collaboration type (sponsored post, ambassador program, affiliate, etc.)
- Content format (video review, tutorial, unboxing, story series, etc.)
- Key messaging and talking points

### 4. Campaign Execution Timeline
Break the campaign into 3 phases:
- **Phase 1**: Duration, objectives, deliverables
- **Phase 2**: Duration, objectives, deliverables
- **Phase 3**: Duration, objectives, deliverables

### 5. Performance Metrics and Evaluation
Define 3 key metrics:
- Metric name, target value, and tracking method

### 6. Budget Allocation
- **Influencer Compensation**: Total and per-influencer breakdown
- **Content Production**: Total and per-content-type breakdown
- **Paid Media Amplification**: Total and per-platform breakdown

### 7. Contingency Planning
- **Potential Challenges**: List 3 realistic challenges
- **Mitigation Strategies**: Corresponding strategy for each challenge

### 8. Key Takeaways and Future Optimization
- 3 strategic insights for campaign refinement and long-term influencer relationship development
```
