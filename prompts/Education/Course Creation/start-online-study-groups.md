# Online Study Group Organizer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Online Study Group Organizer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds structured plans for launching collaborative learning communities. This online study group prompt for ChatGPT takes your subject, learning objectives, target audience, platform choice, and schedule, then produces three core deliverables: a clear description of the group's purpose and value, a set of participant guidelines covering expectations and communication norms, and ready-to-use promotional materials for social posts, emails, or announcements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it easy to adapt the tone and structure to different audiences - whether you're organizing peer study sessions for university students, professional development cohorts, or hobby learning circles. Educators, community managers, course creators, and team leads reach for this prompt when they need a repeatable framework that balances structure with inclusivity. ● Produces a purpose statement that clarifies scope, objectives, and participant benefits ● Generates participant guidelines that establish meeting structure, communication norms, and accountability mechanisms ● Creates promotional copy tailored to attract the right learners and set clear expectations upfront ● Supports any platform and schedule combination, from weekly Zoom calls to async Discord discussions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational coordinator designing online study groups that foster collaborative learning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan to organize and promote an online study group. Include:

- **Description**: A clear overview of the study group's purpose, scope, and value proposition
- **Guidelines**: Participant expectations, meeting structure, communication norms, and engagement rules
- **Promotional Materials**: Outreach copy for announcements, social posts, or email invitations that attract the right participants

## Context
Study group details:
{{subject-and-objectives}}

Target audience:
{{target-audience}}

Logistics:
{{platform-and-schedule}}

## Output
Structure your plan with clear headings and bullet points for each section (Description, Guidelines, Promotional Materials). Design for inclusivity and motivation. Ensure the guidelines support accountability and active participation. Make promotional materials compelling and specific enough to set clear expectations.
```
