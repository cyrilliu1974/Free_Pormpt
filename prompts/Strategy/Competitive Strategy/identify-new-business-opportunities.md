# Business Innovation Opportunity Finder Using Google's 8 Principles

## 簡介

The Business Innovation Opportunity Finder Using Google's 8 Principles is a free AI prompt that systematically uncovers growth opportunities and strategic blind spots for business leaders and innovation teams. This business innovation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a structured framework that applies Google's proven innovation methodology to any business context. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, it analyzes your company through eight distinct lenses: reframing mission and purpose, pursuing moonshot ideas, embracing experimentation, seeking diverse inspiration, sharing knowledge openly, balancing imagination with data, opening up capabilities to partners, and taking smart calculated risks. For each principle, the prompt generates 2-3 concrete, actionable ideas that push beyond incremental thinking, then ranks the top three opportunities by impact and feasibility with detailed implementation roadmaps. Strategic planners use it during annual planning cycles, product managers reach for it when exploring new market segments, and consultants apply it to client discovery engagements. ● Systematically explores all 8 Google innovation principles to reveal blind spots your team may have missed ● Delivers a comparison table scoring opportunities on both transformative impact (0-10) and achievability (0-10) ● Generates specific action plans for your top three opportunities with concrete next steps ● Encourages bold, moonshot thinking while grounding recommendations in data-driven feasibility analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an innovation consultant applying Google's 8 principles of innovation to identify growth opportunities and strategic blind spots.

## Task
Analyze the business context provided below and generate actionable innovation opportunities across all 8 Google principles:

1. **Reframe the mission** – find broader purpose and customer pain points
2. **Pursue moonshots** – identify bold, ambitious ideas
3. **Embrace experimentation** – suggest areas to test and learn
4. **Seek diverse inspiration** – highlight neglected sources of insight
5. **Share knowledge** – find opportunities to open up expertise
6. **Balance imagination with data** – propose creative ideas worth pressure-testing
7. **Open up capabilities** – identify assets others could build on
8. **Take smart risks** – recommend calculated bets worth taking

For each principle, brainstorm 2–3 specific, actionable ideas with concrete examples. Push outside the comfort zone; avoid safe or incremental suggestions.

Then prioritize the top 3 opportunities in a table with:
- Impact score (0–10, where 10 is transformative)
- Feasibility score (0–10, where 10 is highly achievable)
- Brief rationale

Finally, provide a concise action plan for each of the top 3 opportunities.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output Format
- Organize by the 8 principles as section headings
- Use bullet points for ideas and action plans
- Include a comparison table for the top 3 opportunities
- Use emojis to highlight key points: 💡 creative ideas, 🎯 high-impact opportunities, ✅ feasible to implement
```
