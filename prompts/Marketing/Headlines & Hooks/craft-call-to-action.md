# Call-to-Action Generator for High Conversion Copy

## 簡介

The Call-to-Action Generator for High Conversion Copy is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive CTA messages designed to drive immediate action from your target audience. This call-to-action prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your product features, unique selling proposition, and customer pain points to produce structured conversion copy in three parts: an attention-grabbing headline that leads with core benefits, body copy that addresses pain points and builds urgency around your USP, and a specific action statement with clear next steps tied to your deadline. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it useful for landing pages, email campaigns, sales pages, and promotional materials where you need to turn interest into action. Marketers, copywriters, and business owners use it to craft CTAs that balance persuasion with clarity. ● Produces three-part CTA structure: headline, body, and action statement ● Incorporates your unique selling proposition and customer pain points into the message ● Builds time-sensitivity using your offer deadline to motivate immediate action ● Generates action-oriented language with specific next steps instead of vague requests ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in high-conversion calls-to-action.

## Task
Craft a persuasive call-to-action message that motivates the target audience to take immediate action. Analyze the product/service features, identify the unique selling proposition, understand customer pain points and desires, then create a clear, urgent message using action-oriented language.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Ideal Customer:** {{ideal-customer}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Desired Action:** {{desired-action}}

**Offer Deadline:** {{deadline}}

## Output
Deliver your call-to-action in three clearly separated sections:

**HEADLINE:**
[Attention-grabbing headline that leads with the core benefit]

**BODY:**
[2-3 sentences addressing customer pain points, emphasizing the USP, and building urgency]

**CALL-TO-ACTION:**
[Specific, action-oriented statement with clear next steps and time sensitivity]
```
