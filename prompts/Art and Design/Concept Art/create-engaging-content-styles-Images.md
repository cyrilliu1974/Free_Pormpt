# Content Style Image Generator

## 簡介

The Content Style Image Generator is a free AI prompt that produces styled images by specifying content type, subject, artistic style, and compositional structure for creators and designers. This content style image prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works through four variables: content-type (photo, illustration, rendering, etc.), subject (what you're depicting), style (artistic approach or aesthetic), and composition (framing, angle, or layout). A photographer might generate "photograph of mountain landscape, cinematic golden hour lighting, wide-angle panoramic view" while a product designer could create "3D rendering of smartwatch, minimalist Scandinavian style, centered on gradient background." The template adapts to concept art, marketing visuals, mood boards, and creative exploration across any image generation model. Reach for this prompt when you need precise control over both the aesthetic direction and structural arrangement of generated images, from editorial photography to stylized illustrations. ● Separate style and composition variables let you independently control aesthetic and layout decisions. ● Works across photography, illustration, 3D rendering, and other content types by changing one parameter. ● Four-variable structure keeps prompts organized and repeatable for consistent visual series. ● Compatible with all major image generation models without model-specific syntax. ## Prompt

```
{{content-type}} of {{subject}}, {{style}}, {{composition}}
```
