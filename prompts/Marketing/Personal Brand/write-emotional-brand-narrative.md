# Emotional Brand Narrative Writer

## 簡介

The Emotional Brand Narrative Writer is a free AI prompt that transforms brand details into a structured, emotionally resonant story for personal brands and businesses. This brand narrative prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your brand's value proposition, mission, and customer impact, then weave them into a cohesive story using dependency grammar principles - where each sentence builds logically and emotionally on the previous one. The prompt produces four labeled sections: an Introduction that establishes your brand's world, a Brand Journey tracing purpose and evolution, a Customer Impact section showing tangible transformation, and a Conclusion with a strong call-to-action. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, instructing the model to use sensory details, metaphors, and relatable scenarios rather than generic marketing language. Strategists, founders, and personal brand builders use it when they need a narrative that connects on an emotional level, not just a list of features. ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure each sentence builds momentum toward an emotional crescendo ● Outputs four distinct sections - Introduction, Brand Journey, Customer Impact, Conclusion - with bullet points highlighting key narrative elements ● Instructs the AI to integrate sensory details, metaphors, and relatable scenarios that bring brand essence to life ● Ends with a call-to-action that reinforces brand significance and drives audience engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in emotionally resonant narratives.

## Task
Craft a compelling brand narrative that evokes strong emotion and connects deeply with the target audience. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—each sentence should build logically and emotionally on the previous one, creating momentum toward a powerful conclusion.

## Context
{{brand-details}}

Analyze the brand's unique value proposition, mission, and customer impact. Weave these elements into a cohesive story that brings the brand's essence to life through sensory details, metaphors, and relatable scenarios.

## Output
Deliver the narrative in four clearly labeled sections:

**Introduction** – Establish the brand's world and the audience's entry point  
**Brand Journey** – Trace the brand's purpose and evolution  
**Customer Impact** – Show tangible transformation in customers' lives  
**Conclusion** – End with a strong call-to-action that reinforces the brand's significance

Use bullet points within each section to highlight key narrative elements. Ensure the story flows logically and builds to an emotional crescendo.
```
