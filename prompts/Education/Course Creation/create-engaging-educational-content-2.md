# Educational Video Script Writer for Course Creators

## 簡介

The Educational Video Script Writer for Course Creators is a free AI prompt that generates complete, audience-focused video lecture scripts for educators and online course developers. This educational video script prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building structured, engaging content that breaks down complex topics into digestible segments. It produces scripts with attention-grabbing hooks, clearly stated learning objectives, 3-5 logically sequenced key concepts, real-world examples and case studies, and strong calls to action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to your specific topic, audience knowledge level, and special requirements through three customizable variables. Use it when you need to transform subject-matter expertise into viewer-ready scripts that maintain interest and deliver thorough understanding. ● Produces complete scripts organized into introduction, body with 3-5 key concepts, and conclusion with clear calls to action ● Incorporates real-world examples and case studies to make abstract concepts concrete and relatable ● Uses conversational tone and rhetorical questions to maintain viewer interest and encourage active thinking ● Anticipates common audience questions and defines specialized terms to ensure accessibility at any knowledge level ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced course creator skilled at crafting engaging educational scripts for lectures and videos.

## Task
Write a complete script that captivates the audience, maintains interest throughout, and delivers thorough understanding of the subject. Use a conversational tone, demystify complex concepts, and incorporate real-world examples or case studies.

## Context
- Topic: {{topic}}
- Audience knowledge level: {{knowledge-level}}
- Special requirements: {{special-instructions}}

## Output
Structure the script with these sections:

### Introduction
- Open with a hook: a surprising fact, provocative question, or relatable anecdote
- State learning objectives clearly: what viewers will learn and why it matters

### Body (Key Concepts)
- Break the topic into 3-5 key concepts that build logically on each other
- Use clear section headings to organize content
- Explain each concept in simple, everyday language; define specialized terms when needed
- For each concept, include at least one real-world example or case study
- Anticipate and answer common audience questions to keep content dynamic
- Pose rhetorical questions to encourage deeper thinking
- Invite viewers to share thoughts or experiences in comments

### Conclusion
- Summarize key takeaways: what viewers should remember
- End with a clear call to action: apply the knowledge, explore recommended resources, or complete a related activity

Maintain a conversational, engaging tone throughout. Make complex information accessible through clear explanations and practical examples.
```
