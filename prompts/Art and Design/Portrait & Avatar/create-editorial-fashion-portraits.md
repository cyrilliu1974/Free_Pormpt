# Editorial Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Editorial Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic close-up fashion photography styled with bohemian summer aesthetics and intimate expressions. This editorial fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates high-contrast images featuring a young woman in textured oversized cardigans, bright patterned headscarves tied under the chin, and large tortoise-shell sunglasses, with an animal companion resting on her shoulder. The prompt controls backlit sunlight flares, wind-blown hair details, soft natural makeup, and vibrant summer color palettes against bright blue skies. Fashion brands, lifestyle bloggers, and social media managers use it to create magazine-quality visuals without coordinating models, stylists, or outdoor shoots. ● Customizable fashion aesthetic and animal companion variables let you theme the portrait to match brand identity or seasonal campaigns. ● Photorealistic rendering of fabric textures, wind-blown hair strands, skin tones, and high-contrast backlit sunlight with natural lens flares. ● Close-up framing and intimate expressions create engaging hero images for editorial spreads, product lookbooks, and social content. ● Vibrant summery color palettes and cozy layered styling evoke warmth and authenticity in every generated portrait. ## Prompt
