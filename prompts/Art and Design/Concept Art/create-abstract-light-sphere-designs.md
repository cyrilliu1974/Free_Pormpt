The Abstract Light Sphere Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates symmetrical, futuristic scenes with glowing spheres and dramatic cinematic lighting for concept artists and visual designers. This abstract light sphere prompt for Midjourney produces high-detail compositions featuring two semi-transparent spheres - one above, one below - suspended against a customizable background. The prompt specifies rim glow, soft bloom effects, caustics, and atmospheric haze, rendered from a low-angle camera perspective with dark blue and orange color accents. The result is a balanced, symmetrical sci-fi aesthetic with glossy reflective materials and cinematic depth, ideal for concept art portfolios, title screens, and speculative design work. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other generative image models.
