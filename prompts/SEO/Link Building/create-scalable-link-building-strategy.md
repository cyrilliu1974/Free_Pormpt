# Link Building Strategy Blueprint Generator

## 簡介

The Link Building Strategy Blueprint Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a phased, scalable link building roadmap for SEO professionals and digital marketers. This link building prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini transforms your campaign details into a comprehensive five-phase blueprint covering Foundation, Prospecting, Outreach, Relationship Building, and Maintenance & Growth. Each phase includes three specific white-hat tactics, two measurable PASS/FAIL checkpoints, and a final set of 5-7 KPIs to track campaign success. The structured output ensures every recommendation complies with Google Webmaster Guidelines and aligns with your competitive landscape and target audience. Use this prompt when you need a clear, at-a-glance roadmap for executing a sustainable link acquisition campaign that scales without risking penalties. ● Breaks complex link acquisition into five manageable phases with clear tactical steps for each stage ● Provides PASS/FAIL criteria at every checkpoint so you know when to advance or iterate ● Includes actionable KPIs tailored to your campaign context for tracking domain authority, referral traffic, and backlink quality ● Ensures all tactics are white-hat and aligned with current Google Webmaster Guidelines to protect site rankings ## Prompt

```

**Phase 2: Prospecting**

**Phase 4: Relationship Building**

**Phase 5: Maintenance & Growth**
```
