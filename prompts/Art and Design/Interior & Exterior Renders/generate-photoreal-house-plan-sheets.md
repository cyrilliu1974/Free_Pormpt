# Photoreal House Plan Sheet Generator

## 簡介

The Photoreal House Plan Sheet Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional architectural presentation boards combining photorealistic exterior renders with technically accurate floor plans in one cohesive layout for architects, developers, and real estate professionals. This house plan sheet prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates a single 4:5 presentation board divided into two coordinated panels: the top half renders an ultra-photorealistic exterior with golden hour lighting, black-framed glazing, and refined landscaping, while the bottom half displays clean architectural floor plans with labeled rooms, correct dimensions in square feet, and wall thicknesses that match the building shown above. The prompt ensures logical consistency - double-height windows align with living areas, outdoor features like pools or courtyards appear in both views, and multi-level buildings display ground and upper floor plans side-by-side with matching geometry. Reach for this prompt when you need client-ready architectural marketing materials, real estate listing visuals, or design presentations that communicate both aesthetic intent and functional layout without juggling separate rendering and drafting tools. ● Produces photorealistic exterior renders with correct perspective, ray-traced lighting, PBR materials, and buildable geometry alongside technical floor plans with proper line weights and room labels. ● Accepts custom variables for building design style and site context, enabling you to specify modern farmhouse, contemporary villa, or any architectural typology with appropriate landscape settings. ● Ensures spatial logic between views - stair cores, window placement, and outdoor elements align across the 3D render and plan drawings so clients see one coherent design. ● Outputs clean presentation boards with white margins, readable English labels, and no watermarks or decorative clutter, ready for client meetings, brochures, or listing portfolios. ## Prompt

```
Architectural presentation board, 4:5 aspect ratio, clean white margins. Professional sheet layout divided into two panels:

**TOP HALF – EXTERIOR RENDER:**
Ultra-photorealistic visualization of {{building-design}} set in {{site-context}}. Rectilinear volumes, strong horizontal rooflines, deep window reveals, large black-framed glazing. Refined landscaping with calm yard composition. Golden hour lighting with warm interior glow through windows, soft sky, crisp shadows, ray-traced reflections, PBR materials, high dynamic range. Camera: slightly elevated 3/4 view, 28–35 mm equivalent, straight verticals, no distortion. Buildable structure with detailed facade joints and correct scale.

**BOTTOM HALF – FLOOR PLAN DRAWINGS:**
Clean architectural floor plans matching the exterior exactly. Black linework on white background, consistent stroke weights. Doors with swing arcs, windows, wall thickness, stairs where applicable. Every room labeled in English with sizes in sq ft. Multi-level buildings show plans side-by-side (Ground Floor left, Upper Floor right); single-level centered. Logical arrangement consistent with exterior: double-height glazing aligns with living areas, outdoor features (pool/courtyard/terrace) match plan edges, core/stair placement corresponds across views.

Minimal captions only, no logos or watermarks. Crisp, readable linework and labels.

Avoid: mismatched exterior and plan, warped geometry, unreadable or gibberish text, decorative plan styles, watermarks, fisheye distortion, bent verticals, cartoon style, overlapping labels.
```
