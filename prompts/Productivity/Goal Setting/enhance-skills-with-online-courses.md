# Online Learning Plan Generator for Skill Development

## 簡介

The Online Learning Plan Generator for Skill Development is a free AI prompt that designs personalized educational roadmaps for professionals pursuing online courses to advance their skills. This online learning plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your learner profile - current proficiency, available study time, learning preferences, target skills, and career goals - and produces a complete educational strategy organized into six actionable sections: skill gap assessment, course selection criteria, a dependency-mapped learning path, weekly time management recommendations, progress milestones, and triggers for plan adjustments. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver structured, bullet-point plans that show logical skill dependencies and ensure each topic builds on the previous one. Use it when you need clarity on which courses to take, in what order, and how to fit learning into a busy schedule. ● Identifies skill gaps and prioritizes learning focus areas based on your current proficiency and career objectives ● Maps course dependencies so you tackle foundational topics before advanced ones ● Provides weekly pacing and time-blocking strategies that fit real schedules ● Defines clear milestones, checkpoints, and metrics to measure progress and know when to pivot ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational consultant specializing in professional development through online learning.

## Task
Design a comprehensive online learning plan that includes course selection criteria, time management strategies, and progress tracking methods. Structure the learning path to show dependencies between topics and skills, ensuring each step builds logically on the previous one.

## Context
Learner profile:
{{learner-profile}}

The plan should account for current proficiency level, available study time, preferred learning modalities, target skills, and career objectives described in the profile.

## Output
Deliver the plan as a bullet-point list organized into these sections:
- **Skill Assessment & Gaps**: What to focus on first based on current level
- **Course Selection Criteria**: How to evaluate and choose courses
- **Learning Path**: Sequence of topics/courses with dependencies clearly marked
- **Time Management Strategy**: Weekly schedule and pacing recommendations
- **Progress Tracking**: Milestones, checkpoints, and success metrics
- **Adjustment Triggers**: When and how to adapt the plan

Make every step concrete and actionable.
```
