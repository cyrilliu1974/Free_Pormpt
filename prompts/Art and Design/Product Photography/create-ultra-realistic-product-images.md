# Ultra-Realistic Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Ultra-Realistic Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates commercial-grade product images with professional studio lighting and composed scenes for brands, marketers, and content creators. This product photography prompt for Midjourney produces centered hero shots with clean backgrounds, f/2.8 depth-of-field simulation, softbox key lighting with diffused highlights, and customizable scene props that add refractions and visual depth. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image models, delivering the polished aesthetic of commercial advertising photography. Use it when you need e-commerce listings, social media content, ad creatives, or pitch decks that require high-end product visuals without a physical shoot. ● Specify any product and packaging type along with props (glassware, botanicals, fabric, water) to build a scene with light refractions and texture. ● Produces centered, upright hero composition with clean studio background, mimicking professional commercial shoots. ● Includes camera settings (f/2.8 aperture) and lighting setup (softbox key light, diffused highlights) for realistic depth and shine. ● Outputs square 1:1 aspect ratio images optimized for social feeds, product pages, and paid ad placements. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic product photography of a {{product-and-packaging}} (centered upright hero composition), surrounded by {{scene-props}} creating light refractions and visual interest. Clean studio background with bright, radiant lighting. Captured with professional camera at f/2.8, softbox key light with diffused highlights, commercial advertising style --ar 1:1 --s 750 --q 2
```
