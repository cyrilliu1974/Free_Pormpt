# Product Visual Concept Generator for Ad Creatives

## 簡介

The Product Visual Concept Generator for Ad Creatives is a free AI prompt that creates professional product photography concepts with integrated tagline typography for marketers and designers. This product visual prompt for Midjourney produces commercial-grade images that combine your product in a custom setting with bold, all-caps tagline text overlaid in a modern typographic style. The prompt specifies professional studio lighting, dynamic camera angles that emphasize the product, and a 16:9 aspect ratio ideal for digital ads, social media banners, and presentation decks. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models that support style parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need to visualize ad concepts quickly, present campaign ideas to clients, or generate multiple creative directions without a full photo shoot. ● Combines product placement, environmental context, and tagline typography in a single cohesive image. ● Applies professional studio lighting and dynamic camera angles that highlight product features. ● Outputs in 16:9 format suited for digital advertising, YouTube thumbnails, and presentation slides. ● Adjustable stylization parameter lets you control the artistic interpretation versus photorealism. ## Prompt

```
{{product}} in {{setting}} with stylized words "{{tagline}}" in bold all-caps typography, modern commercial photography style, professional studio lighting, dynamic angle that emphasizes the product, --ar 16:9 --s 750
```
