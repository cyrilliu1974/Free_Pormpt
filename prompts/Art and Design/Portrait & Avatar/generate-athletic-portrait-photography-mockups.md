# Athletic Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Athletic Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates candid, golden-hour sports portraits styled like authentic iPhone Instagram photos for photographers, content creators, and marketing teams. This athletic portrait prompt for Midjourney produces realistic smartphone-style portraits of athletes or active subjects positioned at the edge of sports fields, stadiums, or urban park settings. The prompt specifies golden-hour lighting, shallow depth-of-field bokeh that mimics iPhone 15 Pro portrait mode, chain-link fences and stadium lights in the background, and natural wind-blown hair with realistic skin texture. You customize three variables: the subject description, the sports environment (basketball court, soccer field, track), and the outfit details. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image models, delivering high-detail portraits with the casual, candid aesthetic of street-sports Instagram photography. Reach for this prompt when you need realistic athletic lifestyle imagery for social media campaigns, athlete profiles, fitness branding, or any project requiring authentic sports photography that looks like it was shot on a modern smartphone rather than a professional DSLR. ● Produces smartphone-style portraits with iPhone 15 Pro portrait mode shallow depth of field and HDR processing ● Includes stadium lights, chain-link fences, and athletic environment details for authentic street-sports atmosphere ● Specifies golden-hour lighting, natural skin texture, wind-blown hair, and candid confident poses ● Customizable subject, sports environment (court, field, track), and outfit for diverse athlete portraits ## Prompt
