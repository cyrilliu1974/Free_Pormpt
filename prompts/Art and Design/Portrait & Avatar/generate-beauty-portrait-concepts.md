# Beauty Portrait Concept Generator for Product Shots

## 簡介

The Beauty Portrait Concept Generator for Product Shots is a free AI prompt that creates professional close-up beauty photography concepts for skincare and cosmetics brands. This beauty portrait prompt for Midjourney produces 8K-resolution images of models applying products to the back of their hand against soft pastel backgrounds, with shallow depth-of-field, 85mm lens simulation, and studio-quality diffused lighting. It specifies serene expressions, relaxed hand poses, and a high-end commercial aesthetic ideal for e-commerce hero images, social media campaigns, and product launch materials. The 4:5 aspect ratio is optimized for Instagram and vertical formats. Reach for this prompt when you need polished product application shots without a studio setup or when prototyping visual concepts for beauty clients. ● Specifies luminous, even skin tones and product application on the back of the hand for authentic beauty campaign realism. ● Controls technical parameters including 8K resolution, 85mm f/1.8 lens simulation, and shallow depth of field for professional bokeh. ● Sets soft pastel pink backgrounds and diffused lighting that match high-end skincare commercial standards. ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images ideal for Instagram, Pinterest, and vertical ad placements. ## Prompt

```
Close-up beauty portrait of {{model-description}} with luminous, even skin, gently applying {{product-details}} to the back of the left hand; serene expression, fingers relaxed. Soft pastel pink background, high-end skincare commercial aesthetic, 8K resolution, soft diffused lighting, 85mm f/1.8, shallow depth of field. --ar 4:5 --style raw --s 250
```
