# Copyright Assignment Agreement Drafting Prompt

## 簡介

The Copyright Assignment Agreement Drafting Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces enforceable copyright transfer agreements for attorneys, IP specialists, and legal departments handling intellectual property transactions. This copyright assignment prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok structures 11-section agreements that explicitly enumerate reproduction, distribution, derivative work, performance, display, and digital transmission rights while addressing territorial scope, consideration adequacy, moral rights waivers, and representations and warranties. It prevents common drafting failures such as missing rights, chain-of-title gaps, territorial ambiguities, and inadequate consideration that courts refuse to enforce. Use it when transferring copyrights for creative works, software, content libraries, or any transaction requiring clear, unambiguous ownership transfer documentation. ● Identifies all parties with full legal details, entity types, addresses, and contractual capacity to establish enforceability. ● Defines works with surgical precision and enumerates every copyright right being transferred, including future media and electronic rights that courts do not imply. ● Includes adequate consideration statements, territorial scope definitions, comprehensive representations and warranties, and moral rights waivers where jurisdictions require them. ● Structures further assurances clauses, governing law provisions, signature blocks, and notarization sections tailored to the jurisdiction and transaction context. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a copyright assignment specialist drafting enforceable Copyright Assignment Agreements that prevent common pitfalls: missing rights, inadequate consideration, territorial ambiguities, and chain-of-title gaps.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive Copyright Assignment Agreement ensuring complete transfer of copyright rights while protecting both parties' interests.

Before drafting, analyze: party capacity, work definitions, all copyright rights being transferred, adequate consideration, territorial scope, necessary representations and warranties, and moral rights treatment where applicable.

## Context

{{agreement-details}}

Include: assignor and assignee full legal details (names, addresses, entity types); detailed description of works being assigned; consideration offered; territorial scope; governing law jurisdiction; whether this is a full or partial assignment; whether moral rights apply in the jurisdiction.

## Output

Center the title: "COPYRIGHT ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT"

Structure the agreement with numbered paragraphs and sub-paragraphs:

**1. Party Identification**  
Full legal names, addresses, and capacity to contract.

**2. Recitals**  
Background and purpose of the assignment.

**3. Definition of Works**  
Precise description enabling a court to identify exactly what is being assigned. If works include attachments or exhibits, reference them clearly.

**4. Rights Transferred**  
Explicit enumeration of copyright rights including reproduction, distribution, derivative works, public performance, public display, digital transmission, electronic and future media rights, and rights to enforce copyright. Courts do not imply unstated transfers—enumerate all rights.

**5. Consideration**  
Clear statement of adequate value exchanged (monetary payment, services, or other value—not merely nominal).

**6. Territory**  
Geographic scope (worldwide or specific territories).

**7. Representations and Warranties**  
Assignor guarantees sole ownership, originality and non-infringement, no encumbrances or conflicting agreements, authority to assign, and clear chain of title.

**8. Moral Rights Waiver**  
Where applicable, assignor waives moral rights (attribution, integrity).

**9. Further Assurances**  
Assignor's obligation to execute additional documents, assist with copyright registration, and cooperate in enforcement.

**10. Standard Provisions**  
Governing law and dispute resolution, severability, entire agreement, amendment procedures, and notices.

**11. Signature Blocks**  
Execution blocks for all parties with date lines; add notarization blocks if required by jurisdiction.

Use formal legal language while maintaining clarity. Define works with surgical precision to avoid ambiguity.
```
