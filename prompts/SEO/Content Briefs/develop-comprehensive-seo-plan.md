# Customer Journey SEO Enhancement Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Journey SEO Enhancement Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, phased SEO strategy by connecting customer journey stages to keyword research, search intent, and optimization tactics. This customer journey SEO prompt for ChatGPT guides you through four structured phases: research and analysis of personas and search behavior, strategic planning that maps high-intent keywords to each touchpoint, implementation of on-page and technical optimizations, and measurement of performance against acquisition goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a detailed plan that covers awareness, consideration, and decision stages with tailored keyword targets, content recommendations, and link-building tactics. Marketing teams use it to align SEO efforts with how customers actually search and move through the funnel, content strategists apply it to plan topic clusters around user intent, and SEO managers rely on it to structure optimization roadmaps that tie directly to revenue and engagement metrics. ● Maps keywords and search intent to awareness, consideration, and decision stages with specific on-page and off-page tactics for each touchpoint ● Structures research, strategy, implementation, and measurement phases so you move from persona analysis to trackable performance metrics ● Integrates information architecture, content planning, technical SEO, and link-building into a single cohesive plan ● Provides benchmarking, testing, and iteration steps to refine strategy based on traffic, rankings, and revenue impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in customer journey mapping and keyword optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive SEO enhancement plan that integrates insights from a customer journey mapping exercise. Connect search behavior and keyword strategy to each stage of the customer journey, delivering actionable recommendations to improve search visibility and user experience.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Current SEO challenges:** {{seo-challenges}}

## Output
Structure your plan across four phases:

### Phase 1: Research & Analysis
- Review existing customer journey map
- Identify main customer personas and their paths
- Analyze search behavior and intent at each touchpoint
- Conduct keyword research aligned with customer intent
- Benchmark current SEO performance

### Phase 2: Strategy & Planning
- Define SEO objectives for each persona and path
- Map high-intent keywords to each touchpoint
- Optimize information architecture and site navigation
- Plan new content and on-page optimizations
- Develop link building and authority boosting tactics

### Journey Map SEO Integration
For each stage (Awareness, Consideration, Decision):
- Customer path description
- Search intent (Informational/Navigational/Transactional)
- Target keywords
- On-page and off-page SEO tactics

### Phase 3: Implementation & Optimization
- Execute on-page optimizations and content creation
- Implement technical and UX enhancements
- Build high-quality links and brand mentions
- Track rankings, traffic, and engagement by persona
- Conduct ongoing testing and optimization

### Phase 4: Measurement & Iteration
- Analyze SEO performance against objectives
- Measure impact on customer acquisition and revenue
- Gather feedback and identify new opportunities
- Refine strategy and repeat optimization process
- Report on results and make recommendations
```
