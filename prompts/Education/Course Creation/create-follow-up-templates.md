# Post-Program Follow-Up Email Templates for Education

## 簡介

The Post-Program Follow-Up Email Templates for Education is a free AI prompt that creates sequenced email campaigns to keep educational program graduates engaged in their career development journey. This follow-up email prompt for ChatGPT builds 3–5 progressive email templates tailored to specific post-program timelines - from two weeks after completion through months later. Each template includes a subject line, motivational body copy structured with dependency grammar for clarity, personalization merge fields (name, cohort, specialization), and a single compelling call-to-action. Use it for online courses, bootcamps, certificate programs, professional development workshops, or any educational initiative where alumni engagement drives long-term outcomes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to maintain momentum after a program ends, re-engage graduates who have gone quiet, or systematically guide alumni toward career milestones like job applications, skill certifications, or networking events. ● Creates 3–5 time-sequenced templates matched to your specific follow-up schedule and program details ● Incorporates personalization merge fields for name, cohort, and specialization to increase open and click rates ● Structures each email with dependency grammar principles for clear, action-oriented messaging ● Provides immediately usable subject lines, body text, and CTAs formatted for your email platform ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in post-program engagement for educational initiatives.

## Task
Create a series of follow-up email templates that encourage educational program participants to continue their career development after program completion. Each template should progressively build on the previous one, corresponding to different stages of the post-program journey.

## Context
Educational program: {{program-details}}
Target participants and industry: {{audience-and-industry}}
Follow-up timeline: {{follow-up-schedule}}

Analyze the program's core objectives and identify relevant career development opportunities for graduates. Structure each email using dependency grammar principles—building sentences that emphasize clear relationships between ideas and actions.

## Output
Deliver 3–5 email templates, each:
- Clearly labeled by sequence and timing (e.g., "Email 1: Two weeks post-completion")
- Written with motivational, action-oriented language
- Including specific, achievable action items tied to career advancement
- Featuring personalization merge fields (name, program cohort, specialization)
- Ending with one clear, compelling call-to-action

Format each template with subject line, body text, and CTA clearly separated for immediate implementation.
```
