# Business Expansion Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Business Expansion Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a multi-section growth plan for companies exploring new markets, product lines, or geographic territories. This business expansion strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your company context and expansion goals as inputs and produces four core deliverables: a market analysis profiling 2-3 target segments with competitive positioning, a ranked list of the top three expansion opportunities with impact and feasibility assessments, a phased expansion plan detailing strategic objectives, resource requirements, timelines, and KPIs, and actionable recommendations split into 90-day quick wins and 12-24 month strategic moves. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing directors, strategy consultants, and founders use it to replace weeks of analysis with a single structured output they can refine, present to stakeholders, and execute against. Reach for this prompt when you need a blueprint that connects market research to concrete next steps, not just high-level vision statements. ● Profiles target markets with competitive landscape, customer needs, and unmet gaps ● Ranks expansion opportunities by revenue potential, strategic value, and feasibility ● Delivers a phased timeline with resource requirements, milestones, and success metrics ● Includes 90-day action items and a risk-mitigation plan for the top 3-5 threats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business expansion expert specializing in market analysis, opportunity identification, and growth planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive business expansion strategy that includes market analysis, prioritized opportunities, an actionable expansion plan, and concrete recommendations.

## Context
Company and Current Situation:
{{company-context}}

Expansion Goals:
{{expansion-goals}}

## Analysis Framework

**Market Analysis:**
- Identify and profile 2-3 viable target markets based on fit and attractiveness
- Analyze prevailing market trends and trajectory
- Map core customer needs and unmet gaps
- Assess the competitive landscape and positioning opportunities

**Opportunity Identification:**
Identify the top 3 expansion opportunities. For each, provide:
- Clear description of the opportunity
- Potential business impact (revenue, market share, strategic value)
- Feasibility assessment (resources, timeline, barriers)

**Expansion Plan:**
- Strategic objectives aligned with company capabilities
- Key initiatives required to capture identified opportunities
- Resource requirements (capital, talent, technology, partnerships)
- Phased timeline with milestones
- Success metrics and KPIs to track progress

**Recommendations:**
- Immediate actions to initiate in the next 90 days
- Long-term strategic considerations (12-24 months)
- Top 3-5 risks with specific mitigation strategies

## Output
Deliver a structured expansion strategy organized by: Market Analysis → Opportunity Identification (ranked 1-3) → Expansion Plan → Recommendations. Prioritize actionable insights over theoretical frameworks. Be specific with timelines, resources, and metrics.
```
