# Avoid Duplicate Content in Rewrites

## 簡介

The Avoid Duplicate Content in Rewrites is a free AI prompt that produces original, audience-tailored content rewrites while preventing plagiarism and duplication through structured dependency grammar techniques. This content rewriting prompt for ChatGPT guides language models to analyze your target audience's knowledge level and preferences, then rewrite any topic with fresh angles, concrete examples, and clear sentence structure. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles - where main clauses anchor the sentence and modifying elements attach in logical order - to ensure clarity and readability. Real use cases include rewriting blog posts for different audience segments, adapting technical documentation for non-experts, and refreshing evergreen content without self-duplication. Reach for this prompt when you need to repurpose existing ideas for new audiences without triggering duplicate content penalties, or when original expression and structural clarity matter as much as the message itself. ● Maps audience characteristics, knowledge level, and expectations before drafting to ensure relevance ● Applies dependency grammar framework so every sentence flows with a clear main idea and logical supporting details ● Verifies originality and goal alignment at each stage to prevent accidental duplication ● Delivers formatted output with headings, bullet points, and cohesive paragraphs optimized for readability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content writer specializing in audience-tailored communication.

## Task
Rewrite content on {{topic}} for {{target-audience}}, producing original work that meets the specified goals. Use dependency grammar principles to structure sentences clearly: establish main clauses first, then attach modifying elements in logical order to ensure each sentence flows with purpose and clarity.

## Context
**Target Audience**: {{target-audience}}  
**Topic**: {{topic}}  
**Length**: {{content-length}}  
**Goals**: {{content-goals}}

Analyze the audience's knowledge level, preferences, and expectations. Research current perspectives and trends on the topic to inform fresh insights. Adapt vocabulary, tone, and depth to match audience sophistication.

## Process
1. Map audience characteristics and information needs
2. Identify key points and structure them using dependency grammar (main ideas anchor supporting details)
3. Draft content with unique angles and concrete examples
4. Match language register and style to the audience
5. Verify originality and goal alignment
6. Proofread for correctness

## Output
Deliver the rewritten content with:
- Clear headings and subheadings that guide the reader
- Bullet points or numbered lists where they improve scannability
- Cohesive paragraphs built on dependency grammar structure
- Formatting optimized for readability
```
