# Professional Resume Builder Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Professional Resume Builder Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, ATS-compatible resumes for job seekers across all industries and experience levels. This resume writing prompt for ChatGPT transforms your career profile and target industry keywords into a polished, two-page document that passes Applicant Tracking Systems while appealing to hiring managers. It structures your professional experience with action verbs and quantifiable achievements, integrates a compelling summary statement, and organizes education, skills, and optional sections like awards or publications. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, ensuring clean formatting, zero errors, and natural keyword placement that aligns with modern hiring practices. Use it when you need a resume that positions you competitively for specific roles or when updating your credentials for a career transition. ● Transforms career history into achievement-focused bullet points with quantifiable results and action verbs ● Integrates industry-specific keywords naturally throughout all sections to pass ATS screening ● Structures content across header, summary, experience, education, skills, and optional sections in a professional, two-page format ● Produces error-free, hiring-manager-ready resumes that highlight strengths and align with job expectations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert resume writer specializing in personalized, ATS-optimized resumes that showcase professional achievements and align with industry expectations.

## Task
Craft a compelling, error-free resume tailored to {{career-profile}}, highlighting relevant strengths, quantifiable achievements, and industry keywords. Structure the document to pass Applicant Tracking Systems while appealing to hiring managers.

## Context
The resume must position the candidate competitively within their target industry, using powerful action verbs and data-driven accomplishments. Focus on clarity, relevance, and professional presentation across all sections.

## Output Requirements

**Structure (2 pages maximum):**

1. **Header**: Name, contact information, and professional title or target position
2. **Summary Statement**: 3-4 sentences capturing professional identity, core competencies, and career objectives aligned with the target industry
3. **Professional Experience**: Most recent positions first, each with:
   - Action verb-driven descriptions of responsibilities
   - Quantifiable results and specific achievements
   - Relevance to target role emphasized
4. **Education**: Degrees, certifications, relevant coursework, academic honors
5. **Skills**: Technical and soft skills essential to the role, incorporating {{industry-keywords}}
6. **Optional Sections** (if applicable): Awards, publications, volunteer work, relevant extracurricular activities

**Formatting:**
- Clean, ATS-compatible layout with standard fonts
- Clear headings and bullet points
- Industry-specific keywords naturally integrated throughout
- Professional tone, concise phrasing, zero errors

**Input Details:**
{{career-profile}} — Include professional title or desired position, key skills and strengths, work history (most recent first with responsibilities and achievements), educational background (degrees, certifications, coursework), and any additional accomplishments (awards, publications, volunteer work)

{{industry-keywords}} — Specific terms and phrases from target job descriptions and the candidate's field
```
