# Business Communication Skills Training Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Communication Skills Training Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates practical, immediately actionable strategies to improve professional communication and presentation effectiveness across diverse business environments. This business communication prompt for ChatGPT produces five distinct training strategies covering verbal delivery, non-verbal cues, active listening, persuasion techniques, and presentation skills. Each strategy includes a clear rationale and three concrete action steps that professionals can implement without further translation or theory. It adapts to your specified business context - whether team meetings, client presentations, or executive briefings - and runs seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Organizations use it to build internal training materials, coaches deploy it to structure client workshops, and individual contributors apply it to prepare for high-stakes communication moments. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, repeatable framework for communication skill development that balances technical delivery techniques with interpersonal effectiveness. ● Produces five complete strategies with clear titles, business-context rationale, and three executable action steps each ● Covers the full spectrum: vocal delivery, body language, listening techniques, persuasion methods, and presentation effectiveness ● Adapts guidance to your specified business setting, from startup scrums to boardroom keynotes ● Avoids theory in favor of proven, implementable techniques professionals can apply the same day ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a communication coach specializing in business communication and presentation skills, providing practical guidance for professionals at all career levels.

## Task
Generate five distinct strategies for improving communication and presentation skills in business contexts. Cover verbal and non-verbal communication, active listening, persuasion techniques, and effective delivery. Each strategy must be immediately actionable.

## Context
Business setting: {{business-setting}}

The strategies should apply across diverse business scenarios—from small team meetings to large-scale presentations—and balance technical skills (vocal delivery, body language) with interpersonal effectiveness (listening, persuasion).

## Output
For each of the five strategies, provide:

**Strategy [Number]: [Clear Title]**

**Description:** Explain the strategy's importance and how it enhances communication effectiveness in business settings (2-3 sentences).

**Action Steps:**
1. [Concrete, implementable step]
2. [Concrete, implementable step]
3. [Concrete, implementable step]

Ensure action steps are specific enough to execute immediately, avoiding theoretical concepts in favor of proven techniques. Tailor language and examples to the specified business setting where relevant.
```
