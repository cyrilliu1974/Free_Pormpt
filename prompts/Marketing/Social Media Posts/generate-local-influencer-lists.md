# Local Influencer List Generator for Brand Partnerships

## 簡介

The Local Influencer List Generator is a free AI prompt that identifies location-specific influencers and content creators who align with your product or service, complete with engagement metrics and partnership strategies. This local influencer marketing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product positioning and target audience, then delivers five researched influencer profiles with follower counts, engagement rates, content styles, and specific collaboration ideas tailored to each creator's authentic voice. It also provides a step-by-step outreach strategy and success metrics to measure reach, engagement, conversions, and ROI. Marketing teams, brand managers, and small business owners reach for this prompt when launching location-based campaigns or seeking authentic partnerships with creators who have real influence in specific geographic markets. ● Surfaces five influencer profiles prioritizing engagement rate over vanity metrics, ensuring authentic audience connections ● Delivers platform-specific collaboration ideas that respect each creator's content style and audience expectations ● Includes outreach strategy with value proposition framing and relationship-building tactics for cold or warm contact ● Defines measurable KPIs tied to reach, engagement, and conversions so you can track campaign performance from day one ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local marketing specialist with expertise in influencer marketing and deep networks in the specified geography.

## Task
Generate a targeted list of 5 local influencers and content creators who align with the given product or service and can boost its visibility through collaborations. Provide actionable outreach guidance and measurement criteria.

## Context
Location: {{location}}

Product/Service: {{product-service}}

## Output
Provide your response in this structure:

**Product/Service Overview:**
[Brief positioning statement]

**Target Audience:**
[Demographics, interests, and behaviors]

**Local Influencers:**

Influencer 1:
- Name: [name]
- Platform: [platform]
- Followers: [count]
- Engagement Rate: [percentage]
- Content Style: [description]
- Collaboration Idea: [specific partnership approach]

[Repeat structure for Influencers 2-5]

**Outreach Strategy:**
[Step-by-step approach for initial contact, value proposition framing, and relationship building]

**Success Metrics:**
[Specific KPIs to measure campaign impact: reach, engagement, conversions, ROI]

---

**Selection Criteria:**
- Prioritize influencers whose audience demographics and content themes align closely with the product/service
- Focus on engagement rate over raw follower count
- Ensure collaboration ideas are specific, mutually beneficial, and authentic to each influencer's style
- Include a mix of platform types and audience sizes for diversified reach
```
