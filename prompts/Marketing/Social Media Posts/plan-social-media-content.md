# Social Media Content Calendar Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates strategic, multi-platform content calendars for businesses looking to drive website traffic and audience engagement. This social media content calendar prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates 20+ actionable content ideas distributed across platforms like Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter, and Facebook. It produces a structured markdown table with four columns - Platform, Content Type, Topic, and Call-to-Action - tailored to each platform's unique strengths. The prompt balances educational posts, behind-the-scenes content, user-generated content, product showcases, testimonials, how-tos, polls, and stories while aligning with your business goals and audience interests. Real use cases include e-commerce brands planning monthly content, B2B companies coordinating LinkedIn campaigns, and content creators scheduling cross-platform launches. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete content strategy that goes beyond generic ideas and delivers platform-appropriate tactics with traffic-driving calls-to-action already written. ● Creates 20+ content ideas in a clean four-column table format ready to schedule ● Distributes content strategically across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter, Facebook, and other platforms based on their strengths ● Includes diverse content types from educational and community-building to promotional and user-generated ● Provides platform-specific calls-to-action that drive clicks, comments, shares, and website traffic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in content planning and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive 20+ item content calendar that drives website traffic and engagement across multiple social media platforms.

## Context
Business and audience details:
{{business-and-audience}}
(Include: business type, target audience demographics and interests, key products/services, brand voice and tone, website URL)

## Requirements
- Distribute content across platforms based on their unique strengths (Instagram for visuals, LinkedIn for professional content, TikTok for short video, Twitter for news/conversation, Facebook for community, etc.)
- Include diverse content types: educational posts, behind-the-scenes, user-generated content, product showcases, testimonials, industry news, how-tos, polls, and stories
- Craft platform-appropriate calls-to-action that drive traffic ("Link in bio," "Swipe up," "Read more at [link]," "Comment below," "Share your experience")
- Ensure content aligns with business goals while resonating with audience preferences
- Balance promotional content with value-driven and community-building posts

## Output
Present as a markdown table with exactly 4 columns:

| Platform | Content Type | Topic | Call-to-Action |

Provide at least 20 rows of actionable content ideas ready to schedule.
```
