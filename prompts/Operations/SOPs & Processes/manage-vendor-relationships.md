# Vendor Relationship Management Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Vendor Relationship Management Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end vendor management frameworks for procurement teams, supply chain managers, and business operations leaders. This vendor management prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured strategy covering the full vendor lifecycle: current-state landscape analysis, stakeholder mapping, vendor evaluation and selection criteria, onboarding procedures, performance KPIs, review governance, communication protocols, conflict resolution frameworks, contract negotiation guidelines, risk management protocols, and continuous improvement initiatives. The output includes an executive summary, operational playbooks, and a vendor tracking table ready for immediate use. Real-world applications include onboarding multiple suppliers, standardizing procurement processes across departments, improving contract compliance, resolving service quality issues, and building strategic partnerships with key vendors. This prompt is for procurement managers rebuilding vendor programs, operations leaders standardizing supplier governance, or finance teams seeking better contract visibility and risk controls. ● Produces a full vendor lifecycle playbook from evaluation criteria through continuous improvement ● Includes stakeholder maps, performance metrics, review cadences, and conflict resolution procedures ● Delivers contract negotiation guidelines and risk management protocols tailored to your business context ● Outputs a vendor tracking table template for immediate operational use ## Prompt
