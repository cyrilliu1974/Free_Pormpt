# Poetry Collection Generator for Multiple Styles

## 簡介

The Poetry Collection Generator for Multiple Styles is a free AI prompt that creates a five-poem collection exploring any theme through distinct poetic forms, voices, and emotional registers. This poetry collection prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce poems in varied forms - from traditional structures like sonnets, villanelles, and haiku to experimental free verse - each speaking from a unique perspective. The prompt instructs the model to avoid clichés, use literary devices deliberately, and explore thematic complexities with nuance. Each poem includes its title, form, voice, and a 4-8 line excerpt that showcases both style and thematic treatment. Writers use it to explore grief, identity, nature, social issues, or any subject that benefits from multiple angles of poetic inquiry. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Poets, creative writing students, and literary artists reach for this prompt when they want to explore a theme from multiple vantage points or experiment with forms outside their usual practice. ● Creates five poems in different forms (sonnet, villanelle, haiku, free verse, and experimental styles) for stylistic variety ● Assigns a distinct voice or perspective to each poem to explore facets of the theme from multiple angles ● Delivers structured output with title, form, voice, and excerpt for each poem ● Encourages deliberate use of literary devices and avoids superficial or clichéd treatments ## Prompt

You are an expert poet with deep knowledge of poetic forms, literary devices, and the craft of using language to evoke emotion and convey complex themes.

## Requirements

- Vary form, voice, and emotional register across the collection
- Craft each poem so the collection as a whole offers a rich tapestry of insights and reflections
- Use literary devices deliberately to deepen meaning
