# Industry Social and Cultural Trends Analysis Report

## 簡介

The Industry Social and Cultural Trends Analysis Report is a free AI prompt that produces structured research reports examining how societal and cultural shifts are shaping specific industries for business professionals and decision-makers. This social trends analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks the AI through building a multi-section report that identifies three major social trends and three cultural trends, evaluates their positive, negative, or neutral impact on your chosen industry, and illustrates each with real examples and case studies. The output includes an executive summary, introduction, trend-by-trend analysis with impact markers (✓ ✗ -), future outlook, strategic recommendations, and a full APA-formatted reference list. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it suitable for market research teams, consultants, and corporate strategists who need evidence-based industry intelligence. Use this prompt when you need a professional-grade report that connects macro-level societal changes to tangible business implications, whether for internal planning, client deliverables, or investment analysis. ● Identifies six distinct trends (three social, three cultural) with clear explanations and industry-specific impact assessments ● Includes case studies and real-world examples to illustrate how each trend manifests in the target industry ● Provides strategic recommendations for adaptation and innovation based on identified trend trajectories ● Enforces academic rigor with APA citation requirements for all sources referenced in the analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and report writer with deep knowledge of business, social trends, and cultural influences across industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, well-researched report analyzing how social and cultural trends are impacting {{industry}}. The report must cite all sources in APA format and provide actionable insights for industry professionals and decision-makers.

## Output Structure

**Title:** The Impact of Social and Cultural Trends on {{industry}}: An In-Depth Analysis

**Executive Summary**
- 3-5 key findings as bullet points

**Introduction**
- Brief overview of {{industry}}
- Importance of understanding social and cultural trends

**Social Trends Influencing {{industry}}**

For each of 3 major social trends:
1. Explanation of the trend
2. Impact on the industry (mark as Positive: ✓, Negative: ✗, or Neutral: -)
3. Specific examples and case studies

**Cultural Trends Influencing {{industry}}**

For each of 3 major cultural trends:
1. Explanation of the trend
2. Impact on the industry (mark as Positive: ✓, Negative: ✗, or Neutral: -)
3. Specific examples and case studies

**Future Outlook**
- Potential long-term effects of identified trends
- Recommendations for industry adaptation and innovation

**Conclusion**
- Recap of key findings
- Importance of staying attuned to social and cultural shifts

**References**
- APA-formatted citations for all sources used
```
