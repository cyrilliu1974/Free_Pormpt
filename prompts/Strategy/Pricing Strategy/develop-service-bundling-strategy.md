# Service Bundling Strategy Prompt for Business Growth

## 簡介

The Service Bundling Strategy Prompt for Business Growth is a free AI prompt that creates tailored service package strategies to increase proposal win rates for consultants and business leaders. This service bundling prompt for ChatGPT walks through market analysis, competitive positioning, customer segmentation, and bundle design to produce a complete strategy document. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs an executive summary, market analysis with trend identification, three distinct service bundles with pricing strategies, a phased implementation roadmap, KPIs for tracking success, and a risk mitigation plan. Real use cases include professional service firms combining consulting services into packages, SaaS companies creating tiered offerings, and agencies bundling creative services to improve close rates. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from selling individual services to strategic packages that address specific customer pain points and differentiate from competitors. ● Produces three distinct service bundles with clear value propositions, target segments, and pricing models tailored to your market ● Includes competitive landscape analysis and customer needs mapping to identify differentiation opportunities ● Delivers a five-phase implementation roadmap from market validation through full rollout with specific action items ● Provides measurable KPIs like bundle adoption rate, proposal win rate improvement, and average deal size to track performance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business consultant specializing in market research, service bundling, and sales optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive service bundling strategy that increases proposal win rates by combining offerings into value-added packages tailored to the target market. Analyze market trends, customer behavior, and competitive positioning to identify optimal bundling opportunities. Design a phased implementation approach for testing and refining packages.

## Context
Target market: {{target-market}}

Current service portfolio: {{available-services}}

## Output
Provide a detailed strategy document with these sections:

**Executive Summary**
- Key market insights
- Bundling strategy overview
- Expected impact on proposal win rate

**Market Analysis**
- Target market definition and segmentation
- Key trends and demand drivers
- Customer needs and pain points
- Competitor landscape and bundle offerings

**Service Bundling Strategy**

For each of three bundles, define:
- Services included
- Value proposition
- Target customer segment(s)
- Pricing strategy

**Implementation Roadmap**
1. Market validation and bundle design
2. Pricing model development
3. Pilot program launch
4. Feedback collection and refinement
5. Full-scale rollout

**KPIs and Success Metrics**
- Bundle adoption rate
- Proposal win rate improvement
- Average deal size
- Customer satisfaction scores

**Risk Mitigation Plan**

Identify three key risks with mitigation strategies:
- Risk 1: [Description] → Mitigation approach
- Risk 2: [Description] → Mitigation approach
- Risk 3: [Description] → Mitigation approach

**Next Steps and Recommendations**
1. Immediate action item
2. Short-term priority
3. Long-term strategic initiative

Avoid generic solutions; ensure all recommendations are specific to the stated target market and service portfolio.
```
