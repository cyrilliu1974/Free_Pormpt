# Forum Engagement Plan for Link Building

## 簡介

The Forum Engagement Plan for Link Building is a free AI prompt that creates a structured strategy for growing brand presence through authentic community participation that earns organic backlinks. This forum engagement prompt for ChatGPT maps out platform selection, engagement tactics, content themes, and a weekly activity schedule tailored to your brand, target audience, and expertise areas. It works by prioritizing substantive, helpful contributions over promotional tactics, building credibility that naturally generates referral traffic and links. Marketing teams use it to enter Reddit communities, niche forums, and Q&A platforms with a disciplined approach that respects community norms while demonstrating expertise. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a complete plan with platform rationale, relationship-building strategies, themed content pillars, a five-day activity calendar with measurable goals, and a monthly review framework tracking engagement rates, referral traffic, and backlink acquisition. ● Identifies three high-relevance platforms with selection criteria based on audience fit and community activity ● Delivers three engagement strategies centered on substantive answers, consistent presence, and organic backlink earning ● Provides a structured weekly calendar with platform-specific activities and concrete metrics like response targets ● Includes a monthly review framework analyzing engagement rates, referral traffic, and backlink data to refine the approach ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert SEO strategist and online community manager specializing in growing brand presence through authentic engagement that attracts natural backlinks.

## Task

Create a strategic forum and discussion group participation plan for {{brand}}. The plan must prioritize genuine value delivery over promotional tactics, building relationships that naturally generate backlinks through helpful, expert contributions.

## Context

**Brand:** {{brand}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Core Expertise:** {{expertise-areas}}

## Output

Deliver the plan in this structured format:

### Target Platforms
Identify 3 platforms most relevant to the brand and audience, with selection rationale for each.

### Engagement Strategy
Outline 3 specific strategies focused on:
- Providing substantive answers and insights
- Building credibility through consistent, helpful presence
- Earning backlinks organically (no self-promotion)

### Content Themes
Define 3 content themes that align the brand's expertise with community interests and common questions.

### Weekly Schedule
Create a 5-day activity calendar in table format:

| Day | Platform | Activity |
|-----|----------|----------|
| Monday | [Platform] | [Specific activity with measurable goal] |
| Tuesday | [Platform] | [Specific activity with measurable goal] |
| Wednesday | [Platform] | [Specific activity with measurable goal] |
| Thursday | [Platform] | [Specific activity with measurable goal] |
| Friday | [Platform] | [Specific activity with measurable goal] |

Each activity should include a concrete metric or goal (e.g., "Answer 5 questions in r/[subreddit] with 200+ word responses").

### Monthly Review Framework
- **Analyze:** 3 key performance metrics (engagement rates, referral traffic, backlink acquisition)
- **Adjust:** Strategy modifications based on 2 factors (performance data and community feedback)
- **Set Targets:** 3 specific goals for the next month
```
