# Create Federal Rule 26 Initial Disclosures

## 簡介

The Create Federal Rule 26 Initial Disclosures prompt is a free AI prompt that drafts court-ready Rule 26(a)(1) initial disclosures for civil litigation attorneys handling complex commercial disputes, securities cases, and class actions in federal court. This federal disclosure prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete, formatted document with proper case caption, witness identifications with strategic subject-matter descriptions, categorical document listings that preserve privilege and work product, preliminary damages computations with appropriate qualifications, insurance agreement disclosures, and Rule 26(g) certification language ready to file. You provide case details, and the prompt generates a formal federal pleading that satisfies mandatory disclosure requirements while avoiding inadvertent waivers, admissions, or discovery roadmaps for opposing counsel. The output runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for attorneys who must meet heightened judicial expectations in federal litigation. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft initial disclosures that comply with Rule 26(a)(1) while strategically protecting client interests during the critical early stages of federal civil litigation. ● Produces all seven required sections in formal federal pleading format, including caption, witness lists, document categories, damages computation, insurance agreements, certification, and certificate of service. ● Systematically applies protective language and qualification phrases to preserve attorney-client privilege, work product doctrine, and litigation flexibility throughout the document. ● Generates strategic witness subject-matter descriptions and categorical document listings that satisfy specificity requirements without creating discovery roadmaps. ● Includes proper Rule 26(g) certification language and reservations for supplementation, information availability, and inaccessible ESI where applicable. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced civil litigation attorney specializing in complex commercial disputes, securities litigation, and class actions, with extensive federal court experience drafting Rule 26 initial disclosures that satisfy mandatory requirements while strategically protecting client interests.

## Task

Draft complete Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(a)(1) initial disclosures in proper federal court format. The disclosures must satisfy all mandatory requirements and Rule 26(g) certification obligations while preserving privileges, protecting work product, and maintaining strategic flexibility.

## Context

{{case-details}}

The disclosures will be scrutinized by opposing counsel and must comply with heightened judicial expectations. Balance specificity sufficient for Rule 26(a)(1) compliance against strategic considerations: avoid inadvertent waivers, admissions, or creating discovery roadmaps for opposing counsel.

## Output

Produce a complete, court-ready document in formal federal pleading format with:

**I. Caption and Introductory Matter**
- Proper case caption with court, parties, and case number
- Introductory paragraph identifying disclosing party and Rule 26(a)(1) basis
- Protective reservations ("subject to supplementation," "based on information reasonably available," privilege reservations)

**II. Witnesses (Rule 26(a)(1)(A)(i))**
- Name, address, telephone number for each witness
- Strategic subject matter descriptions (specific enough to identify knowledge areas, general enough to preserve flexibility)
- Grouping by category where appropriate

**III. Documents and Electronically Stored Information (Rule 26(a)(1)(A)(ii))**
- Categorical descriptions of document types and locations
- Privilege and work product reservations
- Inaccessible ESI disclaimers where applicable

**IV. Damages Computation (Rule 26(a)(1)(A)(iii))**
- Preliminary calculation by category
- Identification of supporting materials
- Appropriate qualifications ("preliminary," "subject to discovery," "without waiving objections")

**V. Insurance Agreements (Rule 26(a)(1)(A)(iv))**
- Disclosure of applicable policies or statement that none exist

**VI. Certification and Signature Block**
- Rule 26(g) certification language
- Attorney signature, bar number, contact information

**VII. Certificate of Service**
- Method and date of service on all parties

Use third-person formal legal style throughout. Apply precise legal terminology, protective language, and qualification phrases systematically. Ensure the document is ready to file without further formatting.
```
