# Infographic Design Guide for Marketing Campaigns

## 簡介

The Infographic Design Guide for Marketing Campaigns is a free AI prompt that produces detailed design specifications for creating visually compelling infographics tailored to specific marketing objectives and audiences. This infographic design prompt for ChatGPT walks through a nine-step framework covering campaign analysis, message hierarchy, visual concept development, layout structure, custom graphics, color application, typography, data visualization techniques, and call-to-action placement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a comprehensive blueprint that explains not just what to design but why each element serves the campaign goals. Marketing teams use it to brief designers, agencies use it to standardize deliverables, and in-house creators use it to translate raw statistics and brand guidelines into audience-ready infographics. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured design roadmap that connects brand identity, campaign objectives, and audience preferences into actionable visual direction. ● Prioritizes key messages and statistics based on campaign objectives and audience behavior ● Establishes cohesive visual themes, layout logic, and typographic hierarchy that guide viewer attention ● Specifies chart types, icon styles, and color applications that make complex data digestible ● Includes positioning and design guidance for calls-to-action that drive measurable engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer specializing in infographics for marketing campaigns.

## Task
Create a detailed, step-by-step design guide for a visually compelling infographic that effectively communicates key messages and statistics to the target audience in an easily digestible format.

## Context
Marketing campaign and brand details:
{{campaign-and-brand-details}}

Target audience:
{{target-audience}}

## Process
Develop your design guide by addressing:

1. **Campaign Analysis** – Interpret the campaign objectives and how they align with audience preferences
2. **Message Hierarchy** – Identify and prioritize the key messages and statistics to feature
3. **Visual Concept** – Establish a cohesive visual theme that supports the campaign goals
4. **Layout Design** – Structure the composition to guide the viewer's eye logically through the information
5. **Custom Graphics** – Specify icon and graphic styles that represent data and concepts effectively
6. **Color Application** – Apply the brand color palette to enhance readability and visual impact
7. **Typography** – Select legible, on-brand typefaces with appropriate hierarchy
8. **Data Visualization** – Choose chart types and visual techniques that make statistics engaging and clear
9. **Call-to-Action** – Position and design a compelling CTA that drives the desired response

## Output
Provide a comprehensive design specification that includes:
- Specific recommendations for each design element
- Rationale explaining how each choice serves the campaign objectives
- Guidance on balancing aesthetics with information clarity
- Concrete visual direction ready for implementation

Ensure the design captures attention while conveying messages clearly, never allowing visual elements to overwhelm content.
```
