# Fashion Editorial Low-Angle Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Editorial Low-Angle Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic fashion photography with analog film aesthetics and dynamic upward angles for photographers, designers, and content creators. This fashion editorial prompt for Midjourney produces images shot from below with ambient daylight, blue-sky backgrounds, and shallow depth-of-field foregrounds featuring blurred green trees. It applies Kodak Gold film grain and color grading to every frame, letting you specify the subject, outfit details, and motion concept - such as a model falling through glass or leaping upward - to build narrative tension. The 3:4 aspect ratio suits Instagram, lookbooks, and portfolio presentations. Use it on Midjourney, Flux, or Nano Banana when you need fashion imagery that feels editorial-ready without a physical shoot. Reach for this prompt when you want film-inspired fashion visuals with a distinct low-angle point of view that adds drama and scale to any outfit or styling concept. ● Ambient daylight lighting with blue-sky backgrounds and shallow depth-of-field green foliage in the foreground ● Low-angle composition that photographs subjects from below, adding height and editorial impact ● Kodak Gold analog film grain and color palette for a warm, vintage finish ● Customizable subject, outfit, and motion concept variables to match your creative brief ## Prompt

```
Fashion editorial, ambient daylight, low-angle shot of {{subject-description}} wearing {{outfit-details}}, photographed from below as if {{motion-concept}}, blue-sky background, blurred green trees in the foreground (shallow depth), analog film grain, Kodak Gold aesthetic --ar 3:4
```
