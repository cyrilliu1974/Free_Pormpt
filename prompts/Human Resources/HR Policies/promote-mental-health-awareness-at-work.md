# Mental Health Awareness Campaign Builder for Work

## 簡介

The Mental Health Awareness Campaign Builder for Work is a free AI prompt that designs customized workplace mental health initiatives for HR professionals and business leaders. This workplace mental health prompt for ChatGPT takes your industry, company size, workplace culture, budget, and timeline and produces a bullet-pointed campaign plan with concrete implementation steps for each strategy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering strategies that address burnout prevention, employee well-being, and productivity enhancement specific to your organizational context. Real use cases include rolling out mental health resources at mid-sized tech companies, launching awareness months at healthcare organizations, and embedding wellness practices into remote-team cultures. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, budget-aligned mental health campaign that fits your company's unique environment and resource constraints. ● Produces campaign strategies with clear purpose, impact, and step-by-step implementation guidance. ● Adapts to your industry, company size, culture, budget, and rollout schedule. ● Prioritizes initiatives that address the specific mental health challenges in your workplace context. ● Balances employee support, burnout prevention, and productivity goals in one cohesive plan. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources professional specializing in employee well-being and mental health initiatives.

## Task
Design a comprehensive Mental Health Awareness Campaign tailored to the workplace described below. The campaign must promote employee well-being, prevent burnout, and boost productivity through actionable strategies and initiatives.

## Context
{{workplace-context}}

Include: industry, company size, current workplace culture, available budget, and implementation timeline.

## Output
Provide a bullet-pointed list of key strategies. For each strategy:
- Brief explanation of its purpose and impact
- Concrete implementation steps
- Alignment with the budget and timeline constraints

Prioritize strategies that address the specific challenges and opportunities present in the workplace context provided.
```
