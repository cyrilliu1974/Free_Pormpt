# Time Tracking System Prompt for Task Management

## 簡介

The Time Tracking System Prompt for Task Management is a free AI prompt that builds a complete time tracking framework for individuals and teams looking to identify productivity bottlenecks and optimize their workflow. This time tracking prompt for ChatGPT walks you through the importance and benefits of monitoring task duration, provides step-by-step setup instructions for your chosen tracking method (apps or spreadsheets), and delivers a markdown table with Task Name, Time Spent, and Notes columns - complete with five example entries demonstrating proper usage. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific tasks, tracking method, time-tracking goal, and current workflow challenges. Real use cases include freelancers billing by the hour, project managers diagnosing team bottlenecks, and knowledge workers auditing where their day actually goes. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured system to categorize tasks, record time accurately, and analyze the data to refine your workflow - not just a blank spreadsheet, but expert-level guidance on what to track and why. ● Explains the importance and benefits of time tracking before jumping into mechanics ● Provides detailed setup and usage instructions tailored to your chosen tracking method and workflow challenges ● Delivers a markdown table template with five example entries showing proper categorization and notation ● Includes guidance on analyzing collected data to identify bottlenecks and optimize task allocation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert designing an efficient time tracking system.

## Task
Create a structured approach for monitoring time spent on tasks to identify productivity bottlenecks and optimize workflow. Explain the importance and benefits of time tracking, provide detailed setup and usage instructions, and include guidance on categorizing tasks, recording time accurately, and analyzing collected data.

## Context
Tasks to track: {{tasks}}
Tracking method: {{tracking-method}}
Time tracking goal: {{goal}}
Current workflow challenges: {{workflow-challenges}}

## Output
Deliver your response as a markdown table with three columns: Task Name, Time Spent, and Notes. Include at least five example entries demonstrating proper usage of the time tracking system.
```
