# Customer Feedback Questions for Support Teams

## 簡介

The Customer Feedback Questions for Support Teams is a free AI prompt that creates post-support survey questions designed to maximize response rates and capture actionable insights for customer experience teams. This customer feedback questions prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a numbered list of 10 strategically designed questions, each annotated with its diagnostic purpose and the type of insights it captures. The prompt instructs the model to balance brevity with depth, avoid leading language, and measure both rational metrics (resolution effectiveness, communication quality) and emotional dimensions (satisfaction, effort, loyalty indicators). Use cases include post-ticket surveys, live chat follow-ups, phone support assessments, and cross-channel feedback programs where you need questions that work consistently across email, chat, phone, and self-service interactions. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or refresh a customer feedback program that goes beyond generic satisfaction scores to reveal specific service delivery gaps and improvement opportunities. ● Produces questions structured for specific diagnostic purposes: satisfaction, effort assessment, emotional impact, resolution quality, and loyalty prediction. ● Annotates each question with its strategic intent and insight type, so your team understands what each question measures and why. ● Designs conversational phrasing that respects customer time and works across email, chat, phone, and self-service channels. ● Outputs questions suitable for both quantitative scoring and qualitative pattern analysis when responses are reviewed collectively. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer experience strategist specializing in post-support feedback design that maximizes response rates and captures actionable insights.

## Task
Create 10 psychologically-informed feedback questions that balance brevity with depth, avoid leading language, and capture both rational and emotional dimensions of the support experience. Structure questions to serve specific diagnostic purposes: satisfaction measurement, effort assessment, emotional impact, resolution effectiveness, communication quality, and future loyalty indicators.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Questions should feel conversational rather than interrogative, work across multiple support channels, and provide clear pathways for service optimization. Design for both quantitative measurement and qualitative insights that reveal patterns when analyzed collectively.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list where each question is followed by:
- Its strategic purpose
- The type of insights it captures

Ensure questions are applicable across the support channels and customer demographics described in your business context.
```
