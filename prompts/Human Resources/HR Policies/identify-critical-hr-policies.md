# Critical HR Policies for Small Business Compliance

## 簡介

The Critical HR Policies for Small Business Compliance is a free AI prompt that identifies the five essential workplace policies small businesses must implement to protect themselves legally and support employee satisfaction. It produces a structured breakdown of each policy's purpose, the specific legal risks it mitigates, the consequences of operating without it, and practical implementation steps designed for companies without dedicated HR departments. This HR policies prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok focuses on maximum legal protection with minimal administrative burden, making it ideal for resource-constrained environments. Small business owners, founders, and managers who need to establish legally compliant workplace policies quickly will find this prompt particularly valuable. ● Prioritizes policies by legal risk and operational impact, focusing on high-exposure areas like employment law compliance and workplace safety. ● Explains both the legal protections each policy provides and the specific liabilities a business faces without it. ● Delivers actionable implementation steps suited to small teams and limited budgets. ● Structures output with clear headings and organized bullet points covering purpose, legal protection, compliance risks, and practical next steps. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR consultant and employment law specialist helping small businesses implement essential workplace policies that ensure legal compliance and support employee satisfaction.

## Task

Identify and explain the 5 most critical HR policies that small businesses must implement. For each policy, provide:

- **Policy name and primary purpose** – what it accomplishes for legal compliance and business operations
- **Key legal protections and risks mitigated** – what liability or problems it prevents
- **Consequences of not having it** – specific exposures the business faces without this policy
- **Practical implementation steps** – actionable guidance tailored for resource-constrained environments

Prioritize policies that offer maximum legal protection with minimal administrative overhead, suitable for businesses without dedicated HR departments.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Structure your response with a clear heading for each of the 5 policies. Under each heading, use detailed bullet points organized by: Purpose, Legal Protection, Risks of Non-Compliance, and Implementation Steps.
```
