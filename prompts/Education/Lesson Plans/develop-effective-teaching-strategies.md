# Evidence-Based Teaching Strategies Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Evidence-Based Teaching Strategies Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches and summarizes scientifically-backed instructional methods for teaching any skill within any subject area. This teaching strategies prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete instructional guide organized into four sections: a contextual introduction to the skill's importance, 4-6 numbered teaching strategies with detailed implementation steps and pitfalls to avoid, a troubleshooting table mapping common student difficulties to their causes and solutions, and APA-formatted references for all sources cited. Teachers use it to design lesson plans grounded in educational research, curriculum designers use it to evaluate instructional approaches, and school administrators use it to train staff on best practices. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-based instructional design for a specific skill or subject rather than generic teaching advice. ● Delivers 4-6 evidence-based strategies with step-by-step classroom implementation instructions ● Identifies common pitfalls for each strategy so educators avoid ineffective execution ● Provides a structured troubleshooting table linking student difficulties to root causes and targeted solutions ● Includes APA-formatted citations for all educational research sources referenced ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator and instructional designer specializing in evidence-based teaching strategies across subjects and skills.

## Task
Research and summarize the most effective, evidence-based strategies for teaching {{skill}} within {{subject}}. Provide actionable implementation guidance, common pitfalls, troubleshooting for student difficulties, and APA-formatted citations for all sources.

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Introduction**
Briefly contextualize {{skill}} within {{subject}} and its learning importance.

**Evidence-Based Teaching Strategies**
Provide a numbered list (aim for 4-6 strategies):

1. **[Strategy Name]: Brief description**
   - Step 1: [Detailed implementation instruction]
   - Step 2: [Detailed implementation instruction]
   - Step 3: [Continue as needed]
   - **Pitfalls to avoid:**
     - [Pitfall 1]
     - [Pitfall 2]

2. **[Strategy Name]: Brief description**
   - [Continue same format]

[Continue for all strategies]

**Troubleshooting Common Student Difficulties**

| Difficulty | Possible Causes | Solutions |
|------------|-----------------|------------|
| [Specific difficulty students face] | • [Cause 1]<br>• [Cause 2]<br>• [Cause 3] | • [Solution 1]<br>• [Solution 2]<br>• [Solution 3] |
| [Next difficulty] | • [Causes] | • [Solutions] |

[Include 4-5 common difficulties]

**References**
[List all sources in APA format]
```
