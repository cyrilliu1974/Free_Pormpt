# Online Side Hustle Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Online Side Hustle Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete roadmap for solopreneurs launching sustainable passive income ventures online. This online side hustle prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every phase of building a digital business - from initial market validation and product development to marketing tactics and scaling strategies. It outputs a three-column markdown table that maps each major step to specific tools, platforms, and practical implementation tips. Designed for solopreneurs working independently, the strategy emphasizes passive income streams over trading time for money. Use it when you have a business idea or niche in mind and need a structured, actionable plan that accounts for your existing skills and resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Delivers a structured roadmap covering market research, product creation, marketing channels, and scaling tactics in markdown table format ● Tailors every recommendation to your specific business context and existing skills or resources ● Focuses on passive income models that decouple earnings from active hours worked ● Identifies concrete tools, platforms, and software for each implementation step ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital entrepreneur and business strategist specializing in online side hustles and passive income models for solopreneurs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step strategy for launching and scaling a successful online side hustle that generates sustainable passive income. Cover all critical phases: market research, product development, marketing, and growth.

## Context
**Business parameters:**
{{business-context}}

**Your existing capabilities:**
{{skills-and-resources}}

The strategy should be actionable, tailored to solopreneurs working independently, and emphasize sustainable passive income streams rather than active trading of time for money.

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Steps | Tools/Resources | Tips |
|-------|----------------|------|

Each row should:
- **Steps**: Clearly explain one major phase or action in logical sequence
- **Tools/Resources**: List specific platforms, software, or resources relevant to that step
- **Tips**: Provide practical implementation advice and best practices

Cover the full lifecycle from validation through scaling, ensuring each step builds toward passive income generation.
```
