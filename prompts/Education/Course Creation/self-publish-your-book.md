# Self-Publishing Guide Prompt for Authors

## 簡介

The Self-Publishing Guide Prompt for Authors is a free AI prompt that produces a complete, personalized roadmap for writers navigating the entire self-publishing process from manuscript to market. This self-publishing prompt for ChatGPT walks authors through every critical phase: manuscript development, editing and quality assurance, print and digital formatting, cover design principles, pricing strategy, marketing and promotion, and distribution platform selection. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking an author profile as input and returning a hierarchical guide with clearly marked sections, action items, best practices, and common pitfalls to avoid at each stage. Writers use it to create a personalized publishing plan that addresses their specific genre, budget, timeline, and marketing capabilities. The prompt is ideal for first-time authors who need structured guidance, experienced writers exploring self-publishing for the first time, and independent publishers building repeatable launch processes. ● Structures the publishing journey into logical phases that build sequentially from manuscript to market. ● Addresses all production elements - editing workflows, formatting for print and ebook, cover design essentials - with practical best practices. ● Includes pricing models, marketing strategies, and distribution channel guidance specific to independent authors. ● Flags common mistakes and industry insights that materially affect launch success and reader reach. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert self-publishing consultant creating a comprehensive guide tailored to an author's specific circumstances.

## Task
Produce a detailed, step-by-step roadmap covering the entire self-publishing process from manuscript to market. Structure each step so it builds logically on the previous one, ensuring clarity and actionable direction.

## Context
The author needs guidance across:
- Writing and manuscript development
- Editing and quality assurance
- Formatting for print and digital
- Cover design principles
- Pricing strategy
- Marketing and promotion
- Distribution channels and platforms

For each phase, include best practices, common pitfalls to avoid, and industry insights that materially affect success.

**Author profile:**
{{author-profile}}

## Output
Deliver the guide using a hierarchical structure:
- Primary sections as ## headings (Writing, Editing, Production, Launch, Marketing, Distribution)
- Subsections as ### subheadings
- Action items and key points as bullet lists
- Warnings and pitfalls clearly flagged

Ensure each section flows logically into the next, creating a cohesive roadmap the author can follow sequentially.
```
