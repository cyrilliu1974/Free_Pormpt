# High-End Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The High-End Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic editorial fashion photography with cinematic lighting and sophisticated modern glamour for designers, marketers, and creative professionals. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney produces magazine-quality imagery featuring elegantly posed subjects on luxurious black leather chairs with reflective surfaces and plaid-patterned backrests. Soft directional lighting highlights clothing textures, skin tones, and material details, while a color palette of black and neutral tones with natural warmth creates a sophisticated, alluring mood. The prompt delivers sharp focus and professional retouching quality suitable for editorial campaigns, brand lookbooks, and social media content. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana image generators. Reach for this prompt when you need polished fashion photography without studio costs or model bookings, or when building visual assets that convey luxury and modern elegance. ● Produces photorealistic editorial portraits with cinematic lighting that accentuates fabric textures, accessories, and skin tones. ● Customizable subject description variable lets you specify attire, styling, poses, and model characteristics to match brand identity. ● Delivers 3:2 aspect ratio images optimized for fashion editorials, lookbooks, and digital campaigns. ● Generates consistent mood and aesthetic - sophisticated, alluring, modern glamour - across multiple variations. ## Prompt
