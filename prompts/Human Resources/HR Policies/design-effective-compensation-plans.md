# Sales Compensation Plan Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Compensation Plan Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds balanced, competitive pay structures for sales teams. It creates a comprehensive three-component framework - base salary, commission structure, and benefits - tailored to your company's performance goals, sales cycle, and market position. This compensation plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context to recommend fixed and variable pay ratios, commission tiers, and benefit packages that motivate individual and team performance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured markdown table with justifications for each component based on industry benchmarks, budget constraints, product complexity, and competitive pressures. HR leaders and compensation strategists use it to design scalable plans that attract top talent while controlling costs and aligning incentives with revenue targets. ● Balances base salary, commission, and benefits into a cohesive structure that supports sales goals ● Considers sales cycle length, deal size, and product complexity when recommending fixed versus variable pay ratios ● Provides industry-aligned benchmarks and justifications for each compensation component ● Outputs a clear markdown table format with explanations that stakeholders can review and approve ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert compensation strategist designing a comprehensive sales representative compensation plan.

## Task
Create a balanced, three-tiered pay structure (base salary, commission, benefits) that aligns with company goals and industry standards. The plan must be competitive, scalable, and promote both individual and team performance.

## Context
Analyze the following business parameters:

{{business-context}}

Consider these factors when balancing fixed versus variable pay:
- Industry compensation benchmarks and trends
- Company performance and budget constraints
- Sales cycle length and its impact on cash flow timing
- Product/service complexity and deal size
- Market position and competitive pressures
- Mix of individual versus team-based targets

## Output
Deliver your compensation plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Base Salary | Commission Structure | Benefits |
|-------------|---------------------|----------|
| [Your recommendations] | [Your recommendations] | [Your recommendations] |

Below the table, provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences per component) justifying each element and how it supports the sales goals outlined in the business context.
```
