# Performance Metrics Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Performance Metrics Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds customized SMART performance metrics for any position, helping HR professionals and managers create fair, data-driven evaluation systems. This performance metrics prompt for ChatGPT takes a job title and role details, then produces a complete framework of outcome-based and behavior-based metrics organized by category - core responsibilities, process and behavior, collaboration, customer satisfaction, and innovation. Each metric includes a clear definition, measurement method, target benchmark, review frequency, and SMART alignment confirmation. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for both formal performance reviews and continuous employee development conversations. Real-world applications include onboarding new managers who need consistent evaluation criteria, aligning individual KPIs with organizational goals, and ensuring appraisals measure not just what employees achieve but how they achieve it - teamwork, adaptability, problem-solving, and innovation. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize performance expectations, reduce subjectivity in reviews, or give employees transparent growth targets. ● Produces grouped metrics for core duties, behaviors, teamwork, customer satisfaction, and innovation ● Specifies measurement methods, target benchmarks, and review frequencies for every metric ● Balances quantifiable outcomes with qualitative process indicators like adaptability and collaboration ● Ensures every metric meets all five SMART criteria: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in performance management and human resources specializing in SMART metrics frameworks (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

## Task
Develop a comprehensive set of performance metrics for {{position}} that align with team and organizational goals. Create both outcome-based and behavior-based metrics that provide a holistic view of employee performance, supporting tracking, feedback, and development.

## Context
{{role-details}}

The metrics should:
- Cover key responsibilities, daily tasks, projects, and overall role objectives
- Define what successful completion looks like for each responsibility
- Use quantifiable criteria such as completion rates, quality scores, time efficiency, or impact measures
- Balance individual and team performance indicators where collaboration is significant
- Measure both outcomes (what is achieved) and processes (how it's achieved: problem-solving, adaptability, teamwork, innovation)
- For customer-facing positions, include satisfaction rates and feedback
- For sales positions, include revenue, acquisition costs, and retention rates
- Reward innovation and continuous improvement

## Output
Provide a structured framework with:

**Performance Metrics for [Position Title]**

For each metric:
1. **Metric Name** – clear, descriptive title
2. **Definition** – what is being measured
3. **Measurement Method** – how to quantify (specific numbers, percentages, scores, ratings)
4. **Target/Benchmark** – expected performance level
5. **Review Frequency** – timeframe for assessment (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually)
6. **SMART Alignment** – brief note confirming it meets all five criteria

Group metrics into categories:
- **Core Responsibilities** (outcome metrics)
- **Process & Behavior** (how work is done)
- **Collaboration & Team Impact** (if applicable)
- **Customer/Stakeholder Satisfaction** (if applicable)
- **Innovation & Growth** (improvement contributions)

Ensure the framework is practical for both performance reviews and ongoing employee development.
```
