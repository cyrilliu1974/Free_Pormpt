# Transactional Keyword Product Description Generator

## 簡介

The Transactional Keyword Product Description Generator is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused product content for e-commerce stores, marketplace sellers, and online retailers. This transactional keyword prompt for ChatGPT produces structured product descriptions across four sections: a compelling overview with primary search terms, scannable feature lists, customer-focused benefit statements, and formatted technical specifications. It works by identifying high-intent transactional keywords for your product category, then building content using dependency grammar principles to create a logical flow from attributes to value propositions to unique selling points. The prompt adapts to your target audience and brand voice while balancing search engine optimization with persuasive copywriting. You can run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to generate descriptions for individual products, product lines, or category pages. Reach for this prompt when you need to write or rewrite product content that must both rank in search results and convert visitors into buyers. ● Identifies transactional keywords specific to your product category and audience intent ● Structures descriptions with clear information hierarchy from overview through specifications ● Formats features as scannable bullets and translates them into customer-focused benefits ● Maintains your brand voice while optimizing for search visibility and purchase decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert e-commerce content strategist specializing in SEO-optimized product descriptions that drive conversions.

## Task

Create compelling product descriptions that integrate transactional keywords, rank well in search engines, and persuade customers to purchase. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow from features to benefits to unique selling points.

## Context

Product category: {{product-category}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Approach:
1. Identify high-intent transactional keywords relevant to the product category
2. Structure content with clear information hierarchy that guides the reader toward conversion
3. Emphasize differentiation and value proposition
4. Balance search optimization with persuasive copywriting

## Output

Provide structured product descriptions with the following sections:

**Overview** - Compelling introduction with primary transactional keywords

**Features** - Key product attributes in scannable bullet points

**Benefits** - Customer-focused value statements showing how features solve problems

**Specifications** - Technical details formatted for easy reference

Use bullet points throughout for readability and incorporate transactional keywords naturally within each section.
```
