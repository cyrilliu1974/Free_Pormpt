# Hyperrealistic Architecture Visualization Prompt

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Architecture Visualization Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic three-quarter perspective renderings of modern hybrid workspace buildings for architects, developers, and marketers. This architecture visualization prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates images showing modular volumes stacked and shifted to create shared outdoor working terraces, ground-floor cafes and event spaces, and dynamic material detail across customizable facade materials and urban contexts. The prompt captures both bright daylight interiors and nighttime interior glow visible through the building envelope, with people interacting on terraces and at street level to convey an inclusive, innovative atmosphere. Use it for architectural presentations, real estate marketing, urban development proposals, or design portfolio work. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade architectural imagery without expensive rendering services or lengthy timelines. ● Produces three-quarter exterior perspective views with realistic facade materials, lighting conditions, and human scale. ● Generates modular workspace buildings with outdoor terraces, public ground-floor areas, and collaboration-focused layouts. ● Captures both daytime brightness flooding interiors and nighttime interior lighting glowing through the facade. ● Accepts variables for facade materials and urban context to fit diverse project requirements and site conditions. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic architectural visualization of a collaboration-driven hybrid workspace. Three-quarter exterior perspective showing modular volumes stacked and shifted to create shared outdoor working terraces. {{facade-materials}} form the building envelope with strong material detail. Ground floor features public-facing cafes and event spaces integrated into {{urban-context}}. Bright daylight floods the interior during day; at night, lively interior lighting glows through the facade. People interact on terraces and at street level. Dynamic, inclusive, and innovative atmosphere. Avoid corporate stiffness or isolated layouts.
```
