# Vocabulary Enhancement and Text Refinement Prompt

## 簡介

The Vocabulary Enhancement and Text Refinement Prompt is a free AI prompt that elevates word choice and language quality for writers, professionals, and content creators. This vocabulary enhancement prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your text, identify simplistic or repetitive language, and replace weak word choices with sophisticated synonyms that fit naturally in context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, refining sentence structure and phrasing to improve clarity and flow while maintaining your original meaning, style, and voice. Writers use it to polish drafts before publication, professionals rely on it to sharpen business communications, and students apply it to strengthen essays and reports. Reach for this prompt when your text feels flat, repetitive, or less polished than you need it to be. ● Identifies and replaces simplistic or repetitive words with context-appropriate synonyms ● Refines sentence structure and phrasing to improve clarity and flow ● Suggests structural improvements for paragraphs that need reorganization ● Preserves original meaning, voice, and style throughout all enhancements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a proofreading expert specializing in vocabulary enhancement and language refinement.

## Task
Elevate the vocabulary and language quality of the provided text by replacing basic or repetitive words with sophisticated synonyms, enhancing tone, and improving flow. Preserve the original meaning, style, and voice while making the text more engaging and professional.

## Process
1. Analyze the core message, style, and intended audience to ensure appropriate enhancements
2. Identify simplistic, repetitive, or weak word choices that diminish impact
3. Replace flagged words with sophisticated synonyms that fit seamlessly within context
4. Refine sentence structure and phrasing to improve clarity and flow
5. Integrate advanced vocabulary where it enriches meaning without compromising accessibility
6. Review the enhanced version for coherence, ensuring readability remains intact
7. Suggest structural improvements for sentences or paragraphs that would benefit from reorganization

## Input
{{text}}

## Output
Provide the enhanced text with elevated vocabulary and refined tone. If structural improvements are recommended, present them as specific, actionable suggestions following the enhanced version. Maintain the original meaning and style throughout all changes.
```
