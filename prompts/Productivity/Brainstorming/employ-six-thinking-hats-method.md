# Six Thinking Hats Brainstorming Facilitation Prompt

## 簡介

The Six Thinking Hats Brainstorming Facilitation Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides teams through structured, multi-perspective problem-solving using Edward de Bono's proven framework. This Six Thinking Hats prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete facilitation guide that systematically explores any project challenge through six lenses: White (facts and data), Red (emotions and intuition), Black (risks and concerns), Yellow (benefits and opportunities), Green (creative alternatives), and Blue (process organization). Simply provide your project challenge, and the prompt generates hat assignments, tailored discussion questions for each perspective, and a synthesis of insights into actionable recommendations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible for teams using any major text model. Product managers use it to evaluate feature decisions, consultants apply it to client problems, and workshop facilitators rely on it to ensure every angle of a complex issue receives attention. Reach for this prompt when you need to avoid groupthink, surface hidden concerns, or ensure balanced input from team members with different thinking styles. ● Produces a full facilitation guide with role assignments, discussion prompts, and synthesis steps ● Ensures systematic coverage of facts, emotions, risks, benefits, creativity, and process ● Captures contributions from each perspective and synthesizes them into actionable next steps ● Adapts to any project challenge, from product launches to organizational change initiatives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert facilitator skilled in the Six Thinking Hats Method, a structured brainstorming framework that examines problems from six distinct perspectives (White: facts and data; Red: emotions and intuition; Black: risks and critical judgment; Yellow: benefits and optimism; Green: creativity and alternatives; Blue: process and organization).

## Task
Guide a team through a complete Six Thinking Hats session to generate innovative solutions for the following:

{{project-challenge}}

## Process
1. Open by briefly explaining the Six Thinking Hats framework and assign each perspective to team members (multiple members may share a hat if needed).
2. Facilitate discussion sequentially by hat color, prompting each perspective to contribute:
   - **White Hat**: What facts, data, and information do we have or need?
   - **Red Hat**: What are our gut feelings, emotions, and hunches about this?
   - **Black Hat**: What are the risks, obstacles, and potential weaknesses?
   - **Yellow Hat**: What are the benefits, opportunities, and best-case scenarios?
   - **Green Hat**: What creative alternatives, new ideas, and possibilities exist?
   - **Blue Hat**: How do we organize our thinking and next steps?
3. Encourage full participation within each hat's perspective; remind participants to stay in their assigned mindset.
4. Capture and summarize the key contributions from each hat.
5. Synthesize insights across all six perspectives into actionable recommendations.

## Output
Structure your facilitation guide as:
- Brief method introduction
- Hat assignments
- Facilitation prompts for each hat color
- Summary of ideas per hat (use bullet points)
- Synthesized solutions and recommended next steps
```
