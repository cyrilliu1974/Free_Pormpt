# House Cleaning Service Business Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The House Cleaning Service Business Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a comprehensive launch plan for solopreneurs and side hustlers starting a house cleaning business. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured, bullet-point roadmap covering licensing and insurance requirements, itemized equipment and supply lists with cost guidance, competitive pricing strategies, client acquisition tactics, and operational scaling considerations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and adapts every recommendation to your specific location, target market, budget, availability, and experience level. Service entrepreneurs use it to move from idea to launch with clarity on what to buy, what to charge, and how to find their first clients. Reach for this prompt when you need a concrete action plan for a cleaning service startup rather than generic business advice. ● Produces an organized plan with five core sections: Getting Started, Essential Supplies & Equipment, Pricing Strategy, Marketing & Client Acquisition, and Operations & Growth ● Adapts recommendations to your location, target market, budget, time availability, and prior cleaning experience ● Provides itemized supply lists with cost guidance and competitive pricing structures including package options ● Delivers specific marketing tactics across online and offline channels with messaging guidance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in solopreneur ventures and service-based side hustles.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable plan for launching and growing a house cleaning service. Cover startup logistics, equipment and supplies, pricing strategy, client acquisition, and marketing tactics. Organize the plan into clear sections with bullet points that can be implemented immediately.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver the plan as a bullet-point list organized under these headings:
- **Getting Started** (licensing, insurance, business structure)
- **Essential Supplies & Equipment** (itemized list with cost guidance)
- **Pricing Strategy** (rate structures, competitive positioning, package options)
- **Marketing & Client Acquisition** (online and offline channels, messaging)
- **Operations & Growth** (scheduling, quality control, scaling considerations)

Tailor all recommendations to the location, target market, budget, availability, and experience level provided in the context.
```
