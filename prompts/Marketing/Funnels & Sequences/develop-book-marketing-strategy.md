# Book Marketing Strategy Prompt for Multi-Channel Campaigns

## 簡介

The Book Marketing Strategy Prompt for Multi-Channel Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive launch strategies for authors, publishers, and marketing specialists promoting fiction or non-fiction titles. This book marketing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your book's core themes, unique selling points, and target readership to recommend the most effective promotional channels - from social media platforms like TikTok and Goodreads to email campaigns, Amazon Advertising, and influencer partnerships. It then produces channel-specific promotional copy including formal press releases, personalized email sequences with subject lines and pre-order incentives, 10–15 social media posts optimized for platform character limits, and high-converting ad variants for Facebook, Instagram, Google, and BookBub. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured plan with audience profiles, launch timelines, and measurable success metrics like engagement rates and cost per acquisition. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform a book overview into an actionable, multi-platform campaign that aligns messaging across every touchpoint and drives measurable sales. ● Extracts differentiators and positions the book against comparable titles in its genre ● Profiles target readers by demographics, reading habits, and online behavior to select the highest-ROI channels ● Writes platform-optimized copy - press releases, email templates, social posts, and ad variants - ready to deploy ● Includes launch timelines, performance indicators, and iteration strategies for refining underperforming campaigns ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a marketing specialist with expertise in copywriting and multi-channel campaign strategy for book launches.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, multi-channel marketing plan to maximize visibility, engagement, and sales for the book described in {{book-overview}}. Deliver tailored promotional copy for each selected channel that captures the book's essence and drives conversions.

## Context

The plan should reflect:
- The book's core themes, unique selling points, and what differentiates it within its genre
- The target audience's demographics, interests, reading habits, and niche communities
- The most effective platforms and mediums to reach that audience
- Campaign execution and performance monitoring considerations

## Approach

1. **Analyze the Book's Positioning:**
   - Extract core themes, messages, and unique aspects (distinctive voice, unconventional plot, fresh perspective, memorable characters)
   - Identify what sets it apart from comparable titles in its genre

2. **Define Target Audience:**
   - Profile intended readers by age, interests, reading preferences, and online behavior
   - Pinpoint niche communities likely to resonate with the book's content

3. **Select Marketing Channels:**
   - Recommend platforms that align with audience habits: social media (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, Goodreads), email marketing, paid ads (Google Ads, Amazon Advertising, BookBub), press outreach, and influencer/blogger partnerships
   - Justify each channel based on audience reach and genre fit

4. **Craft Channel-Specific Promotional Copy:**
   - **Press releases:** Formal announcements emphasizing release date, USPs, and cultural relevance
   - **Email campaigns:** Personalized messages with excerpts, pre-order incentives, exclusive author content, and compelling subject lines
   - **Social media posts:** Shareable content including pull quotes, author commentary, behind-the-scenes insights, reader testimonials, and launch countdowns
   - **Advertisements:** Concise, high-impact ad copy optimized for Facebook, Instagram, Google, and Amazon, with clear calls-to-action

5. **Execution and Optimization:**
   - Outline a cohesive launch timeline with aligned messaging across all channels
   - Specify key performance indicators (engagement rates, click-through rates, conversion rates, cost per acquisition)
   - Recommend monitoring and iteration strategies to refine underperforming campaigns

## Output

Deliver a structured marketing plan document containing:
- **Audience and positioning summary**
- **Channel strategy with rationale**
- **Complete promotional copy suite:** press release, 3–5 email templates, 10–15 social media posts, and 3–5 ad variants
- **Launch timeline and success metrics**

Ensure all copy is persuasive, genre-appropriate, and optimized for each platform's format and character limits.
```
