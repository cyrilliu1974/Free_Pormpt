# Social Media Content Calendar Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Generator is a free AI prompt that builds strategic posting schedules for businesses and creators looking to maintain consistent, engaging social media presence. This social media content calendar prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown table spanning at least two weeks, with columns for date, time, post type, content description, and hashtags or mentions. It applies platform-specific best practices - whether you're planning for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or Twitter - and considers optimal posting times for your target demographic. The prompt balances promotional messages about your product or service with value-driven content that builds community and sustains follower interest. Use cases include product launch campaigns, seasonal promotions, brand awareness initiatives, and ongoing content management for agencies or in-house marketing teams. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework that moves beyond ad-hoc posting and establishes a rhythm your audience can rely on. ● Outputs a markdown table with date, time, post type, content description, and hashtags for easy import into scheduling tools. ● Balances promotional posts with engagement-focused content to avoid audience fatigue and maintain authentic brand voice. ● Tailors posting times and content mix to platform-specific algorithms and your target demographic's behavior. ● Includes a strategic rationale paragraph explaining the timing and variety decisions behind the calendar. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in content calendar development and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media posting schedule that promotes {{product-or-service}} while maximizing audience engagement and reach. Create a detailed content calendar that balances promotional posts with valuable content to maintain sustained interest.

## Context
- Platform: {{platform}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Business context: {{business-context}}

Apply platform-specific best practices, optimal posting times for the target demographic, and content mix strategies. Ensure the schedule reflects authentic brand communication that serves both promotional and community-building objectives.

## Output
Deliver the content calendar as a markdown table with these columns:
- Date
- Time
- Post Type
- Content Description
- Hashtags/Mentions

Include at minimum 2 weeks of scheduled posts. Specify the strategic rationale for post timing and content variety in a brief paragraph below the table.
```
