# Error Page Copywriting Generator

## 簡介

The Error Page Copywriting Generator is a free AI prompt that creates empathetic, brand-aligned content for 404 and 500 error pages designed to retain users during technical failures. This error page prompt for ChatGPT produces six complete variations - three tone options (witty/playful, balanced/friendly, professional/reassuring) for both 404 and 500 errors. Each variation includes a headline, body copy, visual suggestions, 3-5 navigation links, and a primary CTA that guides users toward valuable next actions instead of abandonment. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your brand context as input to ensure every element matches your established voice and serves your target audience. UX writers, conversion specialists, and product teams use it to turn error pages from dead ends into engagement opportunities that acknowledge frustration, provide clear next steps, and subtly reinforce brand personality. ● Creates six complete error page variations covering both 404 (page not found) and 500 (server error) scenarios with distinct tonal approaches. ● Includes all essential elements for each variation: headline, body copy, visual direction, helpful navigation links, and conversion-focused CTAs. ● Delivers implementation notes with technical and design guidance for developers and designers. ● Balances empathy and problem acknowledgment with actionable solutions that feel helpful rather than sales-driven. ## Prompt

## Task

- Acknowledges the problem without dwelling on negativity
- Provides clear, actionable next steps (navigation links, search, contact)
- Subtly reinforces brand personality
- Includes conversion-focused CTAs that feel helpful rather than pushy
- Matches the brand's established voice and serves its target audience
