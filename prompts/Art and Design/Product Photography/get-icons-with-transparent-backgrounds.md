# Transparent Background Product Icon Generator

## 簡介

The Transparent Background Product Icon Generator is a free AI prompt that retextures images into clean studio product renders with transparent backgrounds for designers, marketers, and e-commerce teams. This transparent background icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana preserves the original object shape while applying semi-matte or satin shaders that mimic printed plastic or coated materials. It uses 3-point studio lighting and soft drop shadows to ground the object visually, delivering centered front views or slightly elevated angles with neutral depth of field. Real use cases include creating app icons, product catalog imagery, marketing visuals, and web assets that require alpha-channel transparency and consistent studio lighting. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn product photos or concept sketches into polished, icon-ready renders without manual compositing or background removal. ● Preserves original object geometry with smooth bevels and transitions, no harsh outlines or distortion. ● Applies semi-matte plastic or coated finishes with subtle specular highlights and clean shader setups. ● Renders branding as bold, high-contrast printed decals or sticker-style labels for clear visual hierarchy. ● Outputs fully transparent backgrounds with optional soft drop shadows to ground the object on translucent floor planes. ## Prompt

```
Retexture the attached image as a clean studio product render with transparent background.

**Form & Geometry**
Preserve the original object shape. Use smooth geometry transitions and bevels; no outlines.

**Material & Surface**
Apply a semi-matte or satin shader mimicking printed plastic or coated materials. Surface should be smooth or lightly textured based on the object, with a clean shader setup and minimal noise. Add subtle specular highlights for a semi-matte plastic or coated finish.

**Lighting**
Studio-style 3-point lighting or soft HDRI setup. Cast a soft drop shadow directly beneath the object on a translucent floor plane to ground it visually.

**Color & Branding**
Make the object's primary color the focal point with slightly boosted saturation and contrast for clean product visualization. Render any branding as bold, high-contrast printed decals or sticker-style labels.

**Camera & Background**
Centered front view or slightly elevated angle for dimensional clarity. Neutral or slightly shallow depth of field to keep the object fully sharp. Fully transparent background (alpha channel), with optional soft drop shadow beneath the object.

**Style**: {{render-style}}
```
