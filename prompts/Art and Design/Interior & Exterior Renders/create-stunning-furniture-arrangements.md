# Furniture Arrangement Interior Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Furniture Arrangement Interior Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic interior photography featuring custom furniture compositions in any design style you specify. This furniture arrangement prompt for Midjourney produces architectural-quality images with natural window lighting, minimalist staging, and warm color palettes. You customize the primary furniture piece, secondary seating or accent furniture, interior style (Scandinavian, mid-century modern, industrial, etc.), and wall backdrop, while the prompt automatically includes curated styling elements like decorative vases, warm linen curtains, light oak herringbone flooring, and cream wool rugs. It renders every scene with eye-level framing, 50mm focal length perspective, high detail, and a serene wabi-sabi aesthetic that highlights the furniture without visual clutter. Interior designers use it to mock up client proposals, furniture brands generate product lifestyle shots, and home stagers visualize arrangements before physical setup. ● Produces architectural photography-style interiors with natural light, warm tones, and minimalist staging that keeps focus on the furniture ● Includes fixed quality parameters (--ar 3:2 --s 250 --q 2 --c 10) and negative prompts to eliminate clutter and harsh shadows ● Combines wabi-sabi influence with serene aesthetics for timeless, calming interior compositions ● Works with any furniture type and style combination while maintaining cohesive flooring, textiles, and backdrop elements ## Prompt
