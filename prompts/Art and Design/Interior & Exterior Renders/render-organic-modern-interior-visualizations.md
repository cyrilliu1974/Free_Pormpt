# Organic Modern Interior Visualization Prompt

## 簡介

The Organic Modern Interior Visualization Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic contemporary living room renderings for interior designers, architects, and creative studios. This organic modern interior prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces high-ceiling living spaces blending architectural minimalism with soft brutalism and mid-century influence. It creates eye-level wide shots with dramatic natural daylight streaming through geometric windows, sculptural wooden furniture, raw stone tables, and intentional negative space. Variables control material palettes, sculptural decor placement, and color atmosphere to match your design vision. Use it for design portfolios, architecture magazine submissions, luxury brand campaigns, or client pitch decks. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-quality interior visuals without expensive photoshoots or 3D rendering software. ● Produces 2:3 or 4:5 ultra-high-resolution renders with sharp architectural focus, rule-of-thirds composition, and tactile surface textures. ● Controls material palette across flooring, walls, and furniture - from natural wood to raw stone and soft textiles. ● Delivers striking light and shadow patterns through large circular or geometric windows with minimal warm accent lighting. ● Generates custom sculptural furniture pieces, organic coffee tables, and curated decor that avoid generic or cluttered aesthetics. ## Prompt

```
Cool contemporary organic modernism interior visualization. Living room with high ceilings, open breathable layout with sculptural composition and intentional negative space. Architectural minimalism meets soft brutalism with mid-century influence, gallery-like atmosphere.

Eye-level wide shot, 35mm architectural lens, rule of thirds composition with strong verticals, sharp focus throughout. High-end photorealistic render with tactile detailed textures, soft defined shadows, subtle realistic reflections. Calm, refined, innovative, artistic mood suitable for interior design portfolios, architecture magazines, luxury campaigns, editorial features.

Avoid: cluttered or over-decorated spaces, cheap materials, harsh artificial lighting, cold corporate look, generic furniture, cartoonish rendering

2:3 or 4:5 aspect ratio, ultra high resolution, cohesive architectural visual
```
