# SEO Trend Analysis Prompt for Historical Performance

## 簡介

The SEO Trend Analysis Prompt for Historical Performance is a free AI prompt that conducts a comprehensive multi-year SEO performance review for any website URL and analysis period you specify. This SEO trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT works equally well on Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a year-by-year timeline chart that tracks organic traffic growth percentages, identifies the top three performing and underperforming keywords for each year, and surfaces persistent keyword gaps that have gone unaddressed across multiple years. Digital marketers use it to prepare annual SEO audits, content strategists rely on it to spot long-term content gaps, and agency teams turn to it when onboarding new clients who need historical context before planning future campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, birds-eye view of SEO performance over time rather than a snapshot of current rankings. ● Tracks organic traffic growth percentage year-over-year across a five-year window to reveal momentum or decline patterns ● Identifies the top three performing and underperforming keywords for each year, making it easy to spot winners and recurring weak points ● Highlights persistent keyword gaps that span multiple years, so you can prioritize fixes that address systemic content or targeting problems ● Delivers a structured timeline chart format and a dedicated recommendations section, making the output immediately usable in client reports or internal strategy decks ## Prompt
