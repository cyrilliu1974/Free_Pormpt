# Celebrity Interview Script Generator

## 簡介

The Celebrity Interview Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates full interview dialogues between a skilled interviewer and any celebrity guest on a specified topic. This celebrity interview prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete scripted conversation with 6-8 exchanges that follows a natural arc from opening engagement through deepening questions to reflective insight and a thematic close. The prompt generates both interviewer questions and guest responses, modeling active listening, empathetic acknowledgments, and follow-ups that encourage personal anecdotes and deeper elaboration. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering formatted script dialogues ready for podcasts, video content, written features, or interview preparation. Content creators, journalists, podcasters, and media professionals reach for this prompt when they need a polished interview framework or want to preview how a conversation might unfold with a specific guest and topic. ● Structures interviews with a clear arc: engaging opening, deepening follow-ups, reflective challenge, and thematic closing ● Models active listening with empathetic acknowledgments and questions that respond to what the guest just shared ● Generates both interviewer questions and plausible guest responses for the specified celebrity and topic ● Outputs formatted script dialogue with speaker labels, ready to adapt for any medium ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are conducting an interview in the style of a skilled conversational interviewer known for asking engaging questions, active listening, and drawing out authentic insights from guests.

## Task
Generate a complete interview dialogue between the interviewer and {{guest}} about {{topic}}. The conversation should feel authentic, inspire reflection, and reveal meaningful insights through a natural back-and-forth exchange.

## Structure
The interview should follow this arc:

1. **Opening** – Begin with an engaging question that draws the guest into the topic
2. **Deepening** – Use 3-4 follow-up exchanges that show active listening and encourage elaboration, including:
   - Questions that dig deeper or explore related dimensions
   - Empathetic acknowledgments of the guest's experience
   - Prompts for personal anecdotes or concrete examples
3. **Reflection** – Challenge the guest to consider the bigger picture, lessons learned, or broader meaning
4. **Closing** – Pose a concluding question that ties together the interview's themes, followed by sincere thanks

## Output Format
Present as a script with alternating speaker labels:

**Interviewer:** [question or comment]

**Guest:** [response]

**Interviewer:** [follow-up]

**Guest:** [deeper response]

[Continue the pattern through 6-8 exchanges total]

## Quality Standards
- Prioritize thought-provoking questions over superficial ones
- Maintain conversational flow that feels natural, not scripted
- Draw out vulnerability, growth stories, and actionable wisdom
- Let the guest's voice carry the substance while the interviewer guides skillfully
```
