# Vocal Delivery Skills Training Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Vocal Delivery Skills Training Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized vocal coaching guides tailored to specific speaking situations, audiences, and skill levels. This vocal delivery prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive training plan covering articulation, pacing, inflection, and vocal variety with practical exercises, real-world examples, and troubleshooting advice customized to your exact speaking context - whether you're preparing for presentations, podcasting, teaching, public speaking, or any other vocal performance scenario. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting the guide to match your current abilities and address the vocal challenges you face. The prompt is ideal for speakers, educators, podcasters, coaches, and anyone seeking structured, actionable guidance to improve their vocal delivery through deliberate practice. ● Creates custom guides organized by vocal delivery dimension with clear headings, bullet-point techniques, and numbered step-by-step exercises ● Tailors all advice, examples, and troubleshooting strategies to your specific audience, speaking context, current skill level, and vocal challenges ● Builds content using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical progression from foundational concepts to advanced techniques ● Includes a personalized progression plan aligned with your stated vocal delivery goals and practical timelines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert vocal coach developing a comprehensive guide to enhance vocal delivery skills.

## Task
Create a structured guide on improving articulation, pacing, inflection, and vocal variety. Tailor all advice, exercises, and examples to the specific audience and context provided. Address the skill level, challenges, and goals described in {{speaking-situation}}.

## Context
Use dependency grammar principles to ensure logical information flow—build from foundational concepts to advanced techniques. Focus on practical application: every technique should include concrete exercises and examples the reader can immediately practice. Address common obstacles and provide strategies for continuous improvement.

## Speaking Situation
{{speaking-situation}}
(Include: target audience, speaking context (e.g., presentations, podcasting, teaching), current skill level, specific vocal challenges you face, and your vocal delivery goals.)

## Output
Structure the guide with:
- Clear headings and subheadings organized by vocal delivery dimension (articulation, pacing, inflection, vocal variety)
- Bullet points for techniques and tips
- Numbered practical exercises with step-by-step instructions
- Real-world examples relevant to the speaking context
- A troubleshooting section for the specific challenges mentioned
- A progression plan aligned with the stated goals and skill level
```
