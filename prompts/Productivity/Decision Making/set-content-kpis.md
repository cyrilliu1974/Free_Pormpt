# Content KPI Definition and Targeting Prompt

## 簡介

The Content KPI Definition and Targeting Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps content strategists, marketers, and analysts define measurable performance indicators directly tied to business objectives. This content KPI prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the relationship between your content strategy and business goals to produce a markdown table of at least five KPIs, each with a realistic target and a specific measurement method. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting performance indicators that can be tracked through standard analytics tools. Teams use it to align editorial calendars with revenue targets, set benchmarks for blog traffic or video engagement, and establish data-driven accountability across content programs. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond vanity metrics and tie content performance directly to business outcomes like lead generation, customer retention, or brand awareness. ● Produces a markdown table with KPI name, target value, and precise measurement method for each indicator ● Aligns performance metrics to the specific content type, audience, and strategic goals you provide ● Bases targets on industry benchmarks and realistic capabilities, avoiding arbitrary numbers ● Specifies measurement methods that rely on implementable analytics tools and produce reliable data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic content analyst specializing in performance measurement and business alignment.

## Task
Define key performance indicators (KPIs) for content that directly support business objectives. For each KPI, establish realistic targets and precise measurement methods.

## Context
**Content type:** {{content-type}}

**Business goals and target audience:** {{business-goals}}

**Content strategy:** {{content-strategy}}

Analyze the relationship between the content strategy and business goals to identify the most relevant performance indicators. Set targets based on industry benchmarks and the company's current capabilities. Specify measurement methods that are implementable and produce reliable data.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns: **KPI**, **Target**, and **Measurement**. Include at least 5 KPIs that are directly relevant to the content type and measurable through standard analytics tools.
```
