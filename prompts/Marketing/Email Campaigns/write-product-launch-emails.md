# Product Launch Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Product Launch Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multi-email launch campaigns designed to convert subscribers into pre-order customers. This product launch email prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a 3–5 email sequence that progresses from awareness through interest and desire to action. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, it generates complete email outlines with benefit-driven subject lines, attention-grabbing openings, structured content points, and urgency-focused calls-to-action. Each email applies dependency grammar principles to ensure ideas flow logically from core concepts to supporting details, making your messaging clear and persuasive. The prompt takes your product positioning, target audience insights, and launch timeline to create emails that highlight pain-point solutions, address objections preemptively, and leverage scarcity or time-sensitive incentives. Marketing teams, founders, and email strategists use this prompt when planning product releases that require coordinated multi-touch campaigns to maximize early adoption and pre-order revenue. ● Builds a logical progression across 3–5 emails, moving subscribers from awareness to pre-order action ● Generates persuasive subject lines, emotional hooks, and structured content points for each message ● Incorporates audience pain points, objection handling, and urgency elements automatically ● Outputs clear CTAs tailored to your launch timeline and incentive structure ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in product launch campaigns.

## Task
Create a multi-email launch sequence that builds anticipation, drives engagement, and converts subscribers into pre-order customers. Each email should follow dependency grammar principles—structuring sentences so ideas flow logically from core concepts to supporting details.

## Context
Product & positioning: {{product-and-usp}}
Audience: {{target-audience}}
Launch timeline & incentive: {{launch-details}}

The series should:
- Progress from awareness → interest → desire → action across emails
- Highlight product features that solve audience pain points
- Address common objections preemptively
- Create urgency through scarcity or time-sensitive offers
- Use persuasive subject lines and strong CTAs in every message

## Output
Deliver 3–5 numbered email outlines. For each email provide:

**Subject Line:**  
[compelling, benefit-driven subject]

**Opening:**  
[hook that grabs attention or connects emotionally]

**Main Content Points:**  
- [Key message 1]  
- [Key message 2]  
- [Key message 3]  

**Call-to-Action:**  
[specific, action-oriented CTA with urgency element]
```
