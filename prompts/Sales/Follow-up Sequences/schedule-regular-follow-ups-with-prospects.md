# Sales Follow-Up Schedule Builder for Lead Nurturing

## 簡介

The Sales Follow-Up Schedule Builder for Lead Nurturing is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive follow-up plan to guide sales professionals from first contact through conversion and relationship building. This lead nurturing prompt for ChatGPT generates a markdown table mapping every follow-up stage in your sales process - defining the specific actions (emails, calls, demos, content shares), the timing between touchpoints, the objectives for each stage, measurable KPIs, and personalization tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need a systematic framework to prevent leads from going cold, standardize your team's outreach cadence, or diagnose where prospects drop out of your pipeline. Sales teams, SDRs, account executives, and revenue operations professionals rely on it to build consistency into their nurturing process while maintaining flexibility for different buyer personas and sales cycle lengths. ● Defines timing and actions for every follow-up stage in your sales cycle ● Maps key objectives, success metrics, and personalization tactics per touchpoint ● Provides decision criteria for advancing leads or recycling them back through the funnel ● Adapts to your industry dynamics, buyer personas, and average sales cycle length ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sales strategist specializing in lead nurturing and conversion optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive follow-up schedule that maximizes conversion rates and builds long-term client relationships. Develop a structured plan with specific touchpoints, personalized communication strategies, and measurable KPIs that guide sales professionals through the complete nurturing journey.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include in your business context:
- Prospect profile (buyer personas, pain points, decision-making authority)
- Industry dynamics and competitive landscape
- Average sales cycle length
- Number of follow-up stages and what each stage represents
- Current challenges in lead conversion

## Output
Present the follow-up schedule as a markdown table with:
- **Columns**: Each follow-up stage
- **Rows**: 
  - Specific actions (email, call, demo, content sharing, etc.)
  - Timing (days/weeks from previous stage)
  - Key objectives for that stage
  - Success metrics/KPIs
  - Personalization tactics

Ensure the schedule is actionable, with clear transitions between stages and decision criteria for advancing or recycling leads.
```
