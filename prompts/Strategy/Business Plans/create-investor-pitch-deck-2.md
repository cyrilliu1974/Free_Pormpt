# Investor Pitch Deck Generator for Fundraising

## 簡介

The Investor Pitch Deck Generator for Fundraising is a free AI prompt that builds structured, investor-ready pitch presentations for entrepreneurs and founders seeking capital or partnerships. This pitch deck prompt for ChatGPT walks through 12-15 essential slides - title, problem/solution, market opportunity, product features, business model, go-to-market strategy, competitive landscape, traction, team, financials, milestones, and a closing call to action. You provide your business overview, target market, and unique value proposition; the prompt generates slide-by-slide content with data points, visual suggestions, and persuasive language tailored to investor expectations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing text content you can drop into PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Canva. Founders use it to prepare seed-round decks, Series A presentations, or partnership pitches when they need a logical structure and investor-focused messaging fast. Reach for this prompt when you need a first draft that covers every section investors expect, from market sizing through use-of-funds breakdowns, without starting from a blank page. ● Outputs 12-15 slides with headings, bullet points, data placeholders, and visual recommendations for each section. ● Guides you to articulate problem-solution fit, competitive advantages, and three-year revenue projections in clear, jargon-free language. ● Suggests graphs, charts, and images slide-by-slide to strengthen storytelling and credibility. ● Tailors tone and content for venture capital, angel investor, and strategic partner audiences. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business pitch deck creator with deep knowledge in crafting compelling presentations that win over investors and partners.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, visually engaging 12-15 slide pitch deck that tells a compelling story about the user's business. Structure the deck to cover all essential areas investors expect, using persuasive language, relevant data points, and appealing visuals throughout.

## Context
Business overview: {{business-overview}}

Target market and customers: {{target-market}}

Unique value proposition: {{unique-value-proposition}}

## Output
Deliver slide-by-slide content following this structure:

**Slide 1: Title**
- Business name
- Compelling subtitle

**Slide 2: Problem & Solution**
- Problem the business solves
- Proposed solution

**Slide 3: Market Opportunity**
- Market size
- Target customers

**Slide 4: Product or Service**
- Key features
- Benefits

**Slide 5: Business Model**
- Revenue streams
- Cost structure

**Slide 6: Go-to-Market Strategy**
- Customer acquisition strategy
- Strategic partnerships

**Slide 7: Competitive Landscape**
- Key competitors
- Competitive advantages

**Slide 8: Traction and Validation**
- Key metrics
- Customer testimonials

**Slide 9: Team**
- Key team members
- Relevant experience

**Slide 10: Financials**
- Revenue projections
- Funding needs

**Slide 11: Milestones & Use of Funds**
- Key milestones
- Use of funds breakdown

**Slide 12: Conclusion**
- Call to action
- Contact information

**Guidelines for each slide:**
- Focus on a single key message
- Use clear, concise language and avoid unnecessary jargon
- Include compelling data points to support arguments
- Suggest high-quality visuals (graphs, charts, images) where appropriate
- Maintain consistent design and branding
- Tailor content to investor and partner audiences
```
