# Commission-Only Employment Contract Generator

## 簡介

The Commission-Only Employment Contract Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete commission-based employment agreements for business management positions. This commission-only employment contract prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured legal framework including contract section tables, commission structure matrices, performance thresholds, payment terms, and jurisdiction-specific compliance provisions. You provide your business context, location, and specific requirements, and the prompt builds a contract outline with parties, scope of duties, compensation basis, term and termination clauses, confidentiality, non-compete provisions, and dispute resolution mechanisms. It addresses worker classification, tax withholding clarity, expense reimbursement policies, and record-keeping obligations relevant to your jurisdiction. This prompt is designed for HR professionals, business owners, legal consultants, and managers who need to create commission-based employment agreements that balance clear compensation incentives with legal compliance. ● Outputs organized contract section tables prioritized by importance with legal considerations for each component ● Generates detailed commission structure matrices linking performance metrics to rates, thresholds, and payment schedules ● Includes jurisdiction-specific employment law compliance items, tax classification guidance, and anti-discrimination provisions ● Provides clear formatting for compensation terms, confidentiality clauses, dispute resolution, and termination conditions ## Prompt

