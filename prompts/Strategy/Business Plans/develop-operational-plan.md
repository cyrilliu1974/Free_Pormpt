# Operational Plan Builder for Business Strategy

## 簡介

The Operational Plan Builder for Business Strategy is a free AI prompt that creates detailed operational plans for businesses seeking to align objectives with actionable execution strategies. This operational plan prompt for ChatGPT transforms business context into a structured markdown table that maps specific objectives to their corresponding strategies, realistic timelines, and required resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking into account market trends, competitive pressures, and operational challenges to ensure every strategy is measurable and aligned with business goals. Use it to develop quarterly rollout plans, expansion strategies, product launch roadmaps, or internal process optimization initiatives. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from high-level business goals to concrete execution plans with clearly defined timelines and resource allocation. ● Produces a minimum of five detailed objective-strategy pairs with execution timelines and allocated resources ● Accounts for market dynamics, competitive landscape, and operational constraints in strategy formulation ● Delivers output in markdown table format for easy sharing, presentation, and implementation tracking ● Ensures strategies are actionable, measurable, and directly tied to stated business objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist developing a comprehensive operational plan.

## Task
Create a structured operational plan that outlines key strategies, processes, and resources required to achieve the specified business objectives. For each objective, develop actionable strategies with realistic timelines and allocated resources.

## Context
{{business-context}}

When developing strategies, account for market trends, competitive pressures, and potential operational challenges. Ensure each strategy is actionable, measurable, and aligned with overall business goals.

## Output
Present the operational plan as a markdown table with 4 columns:
- **Objective**: The specific business goal to be achieved
- **Strategy**: The actionable approach to reach that objective
- **Timeline**: Realistic time frame for execution
- **Resources**: Required assets, budget, personnel, or tools

Provide at least 5 detailed rows covering the primary objectives and their execution plans.
```
