# Candid Coffee Photography Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Candid Coffee Photography Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates realistic café and coffee shop scenes with documentary-style realism and natural lighting for photographers, content creators, and visual storytellers. This candid coffee photography prompt for Midjourney produces images that mimic 35mm film photography with natural morning window light, warm color tones, and genuine human expressions. You customize two variables - subjects (friends, coworkers, solo drinkers) and setting (cozy café corner, minimalist coffee bar, busy morning spot) - and Midjourney renders a 3:2 aspect ratio photograph that captures authentic moments rather than posed portraits. Use it for blog headers, lifestyle editorial content, restaurant marketing materials, or mood boards that require believable human connection in café environments. Reach for this prompt when you need authentic coffee shop imagery without staged poses or artificial lighting. ● Specifies 35mm f/1.8 lens aesthetic for shallow depth-of-field and professional bokeh effects. ● Uses natural morning window light and warm tones to avoid harsh or artificial-looking results. ● Delivers candid, unposed expressions that feel genuine rather than stock-photo stiff. ● Outputs in 3:2 aspect ratio at quality level 2 for high-detail renders suitable for print and web. ## Prompt

```
Realistic photograph of {{subjects}} in {{setting}}, natural morning window light, warm tones, genuine candid expression, 35mm f/1.8 lens, documentary realism. --ar 3:2 --quality 2
```
