# Edgy Flash Photography Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Edgy Flash Photography Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates dramatic urban nighttime portraits with analog film aesthetics for photographers, designers, and content creators. This flash photography prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a specific scene: a high-angle view of a young woman lying relaxed in an open car trunk at night, captured with hard direct flash that mimics 35mm film grain and texture. You control the outfit styling and car color through two variables, while the prompt handles complex composition details - the subject's wolf-cut hair, dreamy side-glance expression, bent-crossed leg positioning, cigarette, and the dark urban environment with barely visible building silhouettes. Fashion brands use it for lookbook imagery that feels candid rather than staged; social media creators deploy it for moody editorial content; graphic designers pull it into campaigns that need raw street-style energy. Reach for this prompt when you want the gritty authenticity of analog flash photography without coordinating a night shoot, hiring a model, or renting a vehicle. ● Produces high-angle car-trunk portraits with hard flash lighting, grainy 35mm film texture, and dark ambient backgrounds that evoke street photography. ● Customizable outfit description and car color variables let you adapt the aesthetic to match brand guidelines or creative direction. ● Generates a full scene with specific pose details, props (cigarette, transparent box, yellow bottle), and environmental context for narrative depth. ● Delivers the edgy, mysterious, candid atmosphere of analog night photography without the logistical complexity of a live shoot. ## Prompt

```
High-angle flash photography portrait of a young woman lying relaxed inside an open car trunk. Long ash-brown textured wolf cut hair, dreamy expression looking to the side. {{outfit-description}} Body position: legs bent and crossed, right arm stretched upward, left hand holding a lit cigarette near mouth. Trunk of a bright {{car-color}} sports car containing transparent box and yellow bottle. Dark outdoor nighttime setting with faint building silhouette and partial wheel of another vehicle visible on left. Hard direct flash illumination creating 35mm analog film aesthetic with grainy texture, dark ambient background. Edgy, mysterious, candid atmosphere.
```
