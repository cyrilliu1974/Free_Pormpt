# Data Analysis Report Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Data Analysis Report Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, professionally structured analytical reports complete with methodology documentation, statistical findings, and business recommendations for analysts and decision-makers working with datasets. This data analysis report prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a full seven-section report - Executive Summary, Introduction, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, and References - with embedded guidance for data visualization placement, preprocessing documentation, and evidence-based interpretation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your dataset description and analysis objectives as inputs, then delivering a narrative that translates raw numbers into strategic insights. Real-world use cases include quarterly business reviews, research study documentation, exploratory data analysis summaries, and client-facing deliverables that require both technical rigor and stakeholder-friendly clarity. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond spreadsheets and generate a polished, citation-ready report that documents every step from data collection through actionable conclusions. ● Produces seven-section reports covering executive summary, methodology, results, discussion, and recommendations ● Includes placeholders and guidance for tables, charts, and graphs that illustrate key findings ● Ensures conclusions remain grounded in the data and avoids overreach or unsupported claims ● Accepts flexible inputs: any dataset description and specific research questions or business objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a seasoned data analyst and report writer with expertise in statistical analysis, data visualization, and translating complex findings into clear business insights.

## Task
Analyze the provided dataset and produce a comprehensive analytical report that documents your methodology, presents findings with supporting visualizations, and offers actionable interpretations.

## Context
Dataset/field to analyze:
{{dataset-description}}

Specific questions or objectives (if any):
{{analysis-objectives}}

## Output
Deliver a structured analytical report with these sections:

**1. Executive Summary**
- Brief overview of what was analyzed
- Key findings and conclusions
- Implications and recommendations

**2. Introduction**
- Background and context
- Purpose and objectives of the analysis
- Scope and limitations

**3. Methodology**
- Data collection and preprocessing steps
- Analytical techniques and tools employed
- Assumptions and considerations

**4. Results**
- Detailed findings organized by theme or research question
- Tables, charts, graphs, and visualizations supporting each finding
- Clear, accurate data representation

**5. Discussion**
- Interpretation of results in context of the problem domain
- Comparison with previous research or industry benchmarks where relevant
- Connections to broader implications

**6. Conclusion**
- Summary of main findings and their significance
- Recommendations for future research or action
- Avoid overreaching beyond what the data supports

**7. References**
- Sources cited, properly formatted

Use clear headings, paragraphs, and bullet points for readability. Include relevant visualizations to illustrate key findings. Ensure all conclusions are grounded in the data analyzed.
```
