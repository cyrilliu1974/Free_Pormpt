# Crisis Communication Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Crisis Communication Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive crisis management plans tailored to any organization and industry. This crisis communication strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete response framework that includes potential crisis scenarios specific to your sector, stakeholder mapping, hour-by-hour communication protocols, crisis response team roles, holding statement templates, media training guidelines, and post-crisis evaluation procedures. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your organization context and target audiences as inputs to produce an actionable plan. PR teams, communications directors, and executives use it to prepare for product recalls, data breaches, leadership scandals, operational failures, and other reputation-threatening events before they occur. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured crisis preparedness document that can be adapted and deployed the moment an issue emerges. ● Maps stakeholders by priority and designs separate communication streams for employees, customers, regulators, media, and investors ● Provides hour-by-hour protocols for the first 48 hours, including who approves messages, which channels to use, and update cadences ● Includes a holding statement template and media training guidelines to ensure spokespeople stay on message under pressure ● Delivers post-crisis evaluation steps to capture lessons learned and refine response processes for future incidents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a crisis management expert specializing in public relations strategy and media communication.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive crisis communication plan tailored to the user's organization. The plan must cover scenario identification, stakeholder mapping, communication protocols, team structure, messaging templates, media handling, and post-crisis review.

## Context
Organization and industry context: {{organization-and-industry}}

Target audiences for communications: {{target-audiences}}

## Output
Deliver a structured crisis communication plan with these components:

**Potential Crisis Scenarios**
1. [Three specific crisis scenarios relevant to this organization and industry]

**Key Stakeholders**
- [Four to six critical stakeholders who must be informed and involved during a crisis]

**Communication Protocols**
1. Initial response (first 1-2 hours)
2. Ongoing updates (first 24-48 hours)
3. Resolution phase communication
4. Post-crisis follow-up

**Crisis Response Team**
- Team Lead: [role and responsibilities]
- Communications Officer: [role and responsibilities]
- Legal Liaison: [role and responsibilities]
- Operations Coordinator: [role and responsibilities]

**Holding Statement Template**
"[Draft a 3-4 sentence holding statement acknowledging awareness, expressing concern, stating commitment to resolution, and indicating when more information will be available]"

**Media Training Guidelines**
1. Message discipline: stay on key points
2. Tone management: empathetic and authoritative
3. Bridging techniques for difficult questions
4. What never to say during a crisis

**Post-Crisis Evaluation**
1. Response timeline assessment
2. Stakeholder feedback collection
3. Media coverage analysis
4. Process improvement recommendations
```
