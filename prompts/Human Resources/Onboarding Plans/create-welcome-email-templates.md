# Welcome Email Templates for New Hires

## 簡介

The Welcome Email Templates for New Hires is a free AI prompt that creates five distinct onboarding email templates tailored to specific hiring scenarios for HR teams and people operations professionals. This welcome email prompt for ChatGPT produces role-specific templates covering executive hires, individual contributors, remote employees, interns, and department-specific welcomes. Each template includes a subject line, greeting, essential information, clear next steps, contact details, and usage notes that explain when and how to customize it. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone and content to each audience while maintaining brand consistency - executives receive strategic context, remote employees get logistics and connection points, and interns receive reassurance and guidance. It works from your company profile to ensure every template reflects your organizational voice. Reach for this prompt when you need scalable, personalized onboarding emails that make new hires feel valued without overwhelming them or sacrificing professionalism. ● Creates five scenario-specific templates in one generation - executive leadership, individual contributors, remote workers, interns, and department-focused welcomes. ● Includes subject lines, formatted email bodies, next steps, and practical usage notes for each template. ● Balances warmth with professionalism and tailors tone to audience - strategic for executives, logistical for remote hires, supportive for interns. ● Maintains brand voice consistency across all templates while adapting content to role requirements and seniority levels. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR communications specialist creating onboarding welcome emails that balance professionalism with warmth, deliver essential information clearly, and make new hires feel valued from day one.

## Task
Create 5 distinct welcome email templates, each tailored to a specific hiring scenario:
1. Executive/leadership roles
2. Individual contributors
3. Remote employees
4. Interns/entry-level positions
5. Department-specific welcomes

Each template must include:
- Subject line
- Greeting and welcome message
- Key information delivery
- Clear next steps
- Closing with contact information
- Brief usage notes

## Context
{{company-profile}}

Tailor the tone and content to each audience—executives need strategic context, remote employees need logistics and connection points, interns need reassurance and guidance—while keeping a consistent brand voice across all five templates.

## Output
For each of the 5 templates, provide:

**Template [Number]: [Scenario Name]**

**Subject Line:** [compelling subject]

**Email Body:**
[Formatted email with appropriate greeting, welcome message, essential information, next steps, and warm closing]

**Usage Notes:**
- When to use this template
- Key customization points
- Tone/approach guidance
```
