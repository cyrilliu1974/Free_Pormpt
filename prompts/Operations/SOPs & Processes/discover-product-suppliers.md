# Product Supplier Sourcing Strategy & Evaluation Report

## 簡介

The Product Supplier Sourcing Strategy & Evaluation Report is a free AI prompt that builds a complete supplier discovery and vetting framework for businesses establishing reliable, cost-effective supply chains. This supplier sourcing prompt for ChatGPT walks you through six structured phases: market research across B2B platforms and trade networks, initial screening for responsiveness and capability, in-depth evaluation of production capacity and quality certifications, RFP preparation and price negotiation, contract finalization with compliance clauses, and ongoing performance monitoring systems. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a comprehensive report with supplier profiles that detail strengths, weaknesses, production capabilities, quality control standards, pricing structures, and risk mitigation strategies. Procurement managers, supply chain directors, and sourcing teams use it to replace ad-hoc vendor searches with a repeatable, audit-ready process. ● Compiles candidate suppliers from online marketplaces, industry directories, trade shows, and professional networks ● Evaluates shortlisted vendors through sample reviews, facility audits, quality certifications, and financial stability checks ● Guides RFP creation and negotiation of pricing, payment terms, delivery schedules, and after-sales support ● Outputs supplier profiles with risk assessments and a performance management framework for ongoing monitoring ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in global sourcing and supply chain management.

## Task
Create a structured, actionable supplier sourcing strategy and deliver a detailed report recommending suppliers that meet quality, quantity, cost, and delivery requirements.

## Context
The company needs to establish a steady, reliable, and cost-effective supply chain for {{product-and-requirements}}. The sourcing process must include market research, rigorous supplier evaluation across reputation, production capacity, quality control, cost structure, and compliance capabilities, plus negotiation strategies to secure favorable terms balancing cost, quality, and reliability.

## Process

1. **Conduct market research to identify potential suppliers:**
   - Utilize online B2B marketplaces, industry directories, and trade shows to compile a candidate list.
   - Leverage professional networks and industry associations for recommendations.

2. **Initial supplier assessment:**
   - Review each supplier's website and online presence for business scope, product range, and market reputation.
   - Reach out for initial contact to gauge responsiveness and willingness to provide detailed information.

3. **Detailed evaluation of shortlisted suppliers:**
   - Request and review product samples to assess quality.
   - Evaluate production capacity through facility audits (virtual or in-person) to ensure quantity demands can be met.
   - Assess quality control processes and certifications for alignment with requirements.
   - Investigate financial stability and business practices through credit reports and references.

4. **Price and terms negotiation:**
   - Prepare a comprehensive Request for Proposal (RFP) outlining product specifications, quality standards, and volume requirements.
   - Negotiate pricing, payment terms, delivery schedules, and after-sales support. Consider long-term partnerships for better terms.

5. **Finalize the supplier agreement:**
   - Draft a contract detailing all negotiated terms, including quality specifications, delivery timelines, pricing, and penalties for non-compliance.
   - Establish a regular review process to ensure ongoing compliance.

6. **Continuously monitor and evaluate supplier performance:**
   - Implement a supplier performance management system to track delivery, quality, and responsiveness.
   - Schedule regular reviews and audits to ensure continuous improvement and adherence to standards.

## Output
Deliver a detailed report on recommended suppliers including comprehensive profiles that cover:
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Production capabilities and capacity
- Quality control standards and certifications
- Pricing structure and negotiated terms
- Identified risks and mitigation strategies

The report should enable informed decision-making for establishing a resilient and cost-effective supply chain.
```
