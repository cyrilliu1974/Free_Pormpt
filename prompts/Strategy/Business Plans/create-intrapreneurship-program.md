# Intrapreneurship Program Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Intrapreneurship Program Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates comprehensive internal innovation programs tailored to any organization's structure, culture, and innovation goals. This intrapreneurship program prompt for ChatGPT walks through nine structured components: defining program objectives, mapping the ideation process, allocating resources, managing risk, designing employee incentives, setting success metrics, planning implementation timelines, building communication strategies, and establishing evaluation mechanisms. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for HR leaders, innovation managers, and executives who need to formalize employee-driven innovation within their companies. Organizations use it to move from ad-hoc suggestion boxes to structured programs that turn employee creativity into measurable business value. ● Produces a phased rollout timeline with milestones for launching the program in stages ● Defines financial, human, and technology resource allocation frameworks for participant projects ● Identifies cultural, operational, and financial risks with practical mitigation strategies ● Creates KPIs that tie innovation output to employee engagement and business impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic consultant specializing in intrapreneurship program design that drives innovation within organizations.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive intrapreneurship program tailored to the organization described below. The program should enable employees to generate, develop, and implement innovative ideas with maximum engagement and practical output.

## Context
{{organization-context}}

*Include: organization type and culture, industry sector, approximate employee count, current innovation maturity, and any specific innovation challenges or opportunities.*

## Output
Structure your program design with these sections:

### Program Objectives
Clearly state the goals and measurable objectives of the intrapreneurship program.

### Ideation Process
Outline a step-by-step process for employees to generate, refine, and pitch new ideas.

### Resource Allocation
Describe how financial, human, and technological resources will be allocated to support participant projects.

### Risk Management
Identify potential risks (financial, operational, cultural) and provide mitigation strategies.

### Employee Incentives
List tangible and intangible incentives that will drive participation and sustained engagement.

### Success Metrics
Define key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure program impact on innovation output, employee engagement, and business value.

### Implementation Timeline
Provide a phased rollout approach with clear milestones, phase descriptions, and realistic durations.

### Communication Plan
Describe how the program will be announced, promoted, and reinforced across employee and stakeholder groups.

### Program Evaluation
Explain mechanisms for ongoing evaluation, feedback collection, and continuous improvement.

Format your response in clear markdown with headings and bullet points for readability.
```
