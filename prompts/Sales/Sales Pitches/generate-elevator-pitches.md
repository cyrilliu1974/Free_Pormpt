# Elevator Pitch Generator for Conversational Sales

## 簡介

The Elevator Pitch Generator for Conversational Sales is a free AI prompt that creates short, engaging pitches designed to introduce your business and spark genuine interest in brief encounters. This elevator pitch prompt for ChatGPT produces five distinct pitches, each under 280 characters, written in a friendly, conversational tone that appeals to a stranger's self-interest without pushing for a sale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. You provide your business context, and the prompt returns five numbered pitches that use plain language, ultra-specific benefits, and curiosity-building phrasing. Entrepreneurs, sales professionals, and founders use it to prepare for networking events, conferences, chance meetings, or any situation where a brief introduction matters. ● Produces exactly five pitches under 280 characters, perfect for quick introductions or social media bios ● Focuses on tangible, specific benefits that appeal to listener self-interest instead of generic value propositions ● Uses conversational, friend-to-friend language that feels natural in casual encounters ● Avoids sales pressure and calls-to-action, making pitches suitable for social settings where hard-selling would backfire ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a skilled salesperson crafting conversational elevator pitches that spark curiosity without hard-selling.

## Task
Write 5 distinct elevator pitches (under 280 characters each) that subtly introduce the user and their product in a way that makes a stranger want to continue the conversation later.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Constraints
- Each pitch must be under 280 characters
- Short, punchy, conversational—like talking to a good friend
- Appeal to the listener's self-interest
- Build curiosity through ultra-specific, tangible benefits
- Introduce, don't sell; no call-to-action
- Use plain, simple language

## Output
Return exactly 5 numbered elevator pitches with no additional commentary.
```
