# In-Demand Professional Skills Research Prompt

## 簡介

The In-Demand Professional Skills Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies and analyzes the most valuable skills for any profession or industry in a specified year, complete with learning resources and market context. This professional skills research prompt for ChatGPT uses labor market data, emerging technology trends, and industry-specific requirements to produce a structured table ranking 10 critical skills. Each entry explains why the skill matters in the current job market and lists 3-5 high-quality courses, certifications, books, and websites for acquiring it. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes IEEE-formatted citations for all sources. Career changers use it to identify skill gaps before transitioning roles; HR teams use it to design training programs aligned with real hiring demand; consultants use it to advise clients on workforce development priorities. Reach for this prompt when you need data-driven clarity on which skills will differentiate candidates and drive career advancement in a specific field and timeframe. ● Produces a 10-row table with skill name, 2-4 sentence importance rationale, and 3-5 vetted learning resources per skill ● Reflects actual labor market demand, emerging tech adoption, and industry-specific hiring criteria for the target year ● Includes full IEEE-style reference list of all cited sources for credibility and follow-up research ● Adapts to any profession or industry variable, from software engineering to nursing to supply chain management ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional skills analyst and career guidance expert with deep knowledge of job market trends across industries.

## Task
Research and identify the top 10 most in-demand skills for {{profession-or-industry}} in {{target-year}}. For each skill, explain its value in the current job market and recommend high-quality learning resources.

## Context
The analysis should reflect labor market demands, emerging technologies, and industry-specific requirements relevant to the specified profession and timeframe. Focus on skills that meaningfully differentiate candidates and drive career advancement.

## Output
Present findings in a table with three columns: **Skill | Importance | Learning Resources**

For each of the 10 skills:
- **Skill**: Name of the skill
- **Importance**: 2-4 sentences describing what the skill entails and why it is valuable and in-demand for this profession in this year
- **Learning Resources**: 3-5 high-quality resources (courses, books, websites, certifications) with titles and URLs where applicable

After the table, provide a numbered reference list of all sources cited, formatted in IEEE citation style.
```
