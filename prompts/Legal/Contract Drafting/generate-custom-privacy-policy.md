# Privacy Policy Generator for Business Compliance

## 簡介

The Privacy Policy Generator for Business Compliance is a free AI prompt that creates legally sound privacy policies tailored to your business's jurisdiction, services, and data handling practices. This privacy policy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business details - type, location, services offered, and data collection methods - and generates a complete policy document that addresses the specific data protection regulations in your jurisdiction. Unlike generic templates, it customizes every section to reflect how your business collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects customer data. After delivering the full policy text, the prompt generates three targeted follow-up questions designed to surface missing details and strengthen compliance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need a first draft that reflects real regulatory requirements and your specific business context, or when updating an existing policy to match new services or jurisdictions. ● Generates full privacy policy text aligned with the data protection laws of your stated jurisdiction ● Tailors every clause to your business type, services, and actual data collection practices ● Provides three follow-up questions to uncover missing details and improve accuracy ● Outputs a structured draft with business overview, complete policy, and refinement questions ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write legally sound privacy policies tailored to the business's jurisdiction, services, and data practices.

## Context

{{business-details}}

*Provide: business type, location (jurisdiction), services or products offered, what data is collected and why.*

## Task

1. Draft a complete privacy policy covering all data protection regulations relevant to the stated jurisdiction, addressing how data is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected.
2. Tailor every section to the specific business type, location, services, and data practices. Do not use generic boilerplate that ignores these details.
3. After the draft, write three follow-up questions that would surface details needed to make the policy more accurate, compliant, or protective for this business.

## Output

### Business Overview

[Restate the key details from the context]

### Privacy Policy Draft

[Full privacy policy text]

### Follow-up Questions

1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]
3. [Question 3]
```
