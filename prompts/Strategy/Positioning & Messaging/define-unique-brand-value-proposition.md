# Brand Value Proposition Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Brand Value Proposition Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates clear, differentiated brand positioning statements for businesses seeking to clarify their market position. This brand value proposition prompt for ChatGPT guides you through competitive analysis, customer insight mapping, and strategic positioning to produce a complete brand positioning framework. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured positioning statement, strategic rationale, value pillars with proof points, emotional benefit articulation, and consistency guidelines for maintaining the proposition across customer touchpoints. Brand strategists, marketing leaders, and founders use it when entering new markets, repositioning existing brands, or clarifying messaging for competitive differentiation. ● Analyzes competitive white space to identify strategic positioning opportunities that set your brand apart ● Maps target customer needs and pain points to your brand's core strengths and capabilities ● Structures output using dependency grammar so each positioning claim builds logically from supporting evidence ● Delivers consistency guidelines to ensure the value proposition translates coherently across all customer touchpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in competitive positioning and value proposition development.

## Task
Create a differentiated brand value proposition that attracts the target audience and sets the business apart from competitors. Deliver this as a clear, concise brand positioning statement.

## Context
Business and market context:
{{business-context}}

Target customer profile:
{{target-customer}}

Competitive differentiation:
{{differentiation-and-value}}

## Process
1. Analyze the competitive landscape to identify white space and strategic positioning opportunities
2. Map the target customer's core needs, desires, and pain points that align with the brand's strengths
3. Synthesize insights into a compelling brand promise that communicates tangible and emotional value
4. Ensure the proposition is authentic, relevant, and can be consistently delivered across all customer touchpoints

## Output
Structure your response using dependency grammar principles—each claim should build logically from supporting evidence, with clear relationships between ideas:

**Brand Positioning Statement** (1-2 sentences capturing the essence)

**Strategic Rationale** (explain the competitive logic and customer alignment)

**Value Pillars** (3-4 core differentiators with supporting proof points)

**Emotional Resonance** (the deeper customer benefit and desired brand perception)

**Consistency Guidelines** (how to maintain this positioning across touchpoints)
```
