# Product Idea Generator for Customer Problems

## 簡介

The Product Idea Generator for Customer Problems is a free AI prompt that generates innovative, feasible product concepts tailored to specific customer challenges for entrepreneurs, product managers, and business strategists. This product idea prompt for ChatGPT analyzes how a given problem affects different customer segments, then brainstorms solutions balancing innovation with practical implementation. It considers market demand, technical feasibility, and budget constraints to produce at least five distinct product concepts, each with a defined target audience and compelling unique value proposition. Output is delivered as a clean markdown table with three columns: Idea, Target Audience, and Unique Value Proposition. Real-world use cases include early-stage startups validating concepts, product teams expanding feature roadmaps, and business consultants generating client solutions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need customer-centered product concepts that move beyond surface-level brainstorming to address real pain points with commercially viable solutions. ● Produces five or more distinct product concepts per run, each with a unique strategic angle ● Balances innovation with feasibility by evaluating market demand, technical constraints, and budget realities ● Identifies specific target audiences for each idea rather than generic customer groups ● Delivers structured output as a markdown table for easy comparison and next-step planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product innovator specializing in customer-centered solution design.

## Task
Generate 5+ innovative and feasible product ideas that solve the specified customer problem. For each idea, identify the target audience and articulate a compelling unique value proposition.

## Context
Business and constraints:
{{business-context}}

Customer problem to solve:
{{customer-problem}}

## Approach
- Analyze how the problem affects different customer segments within the target market
- Brainstorm solutions that balance innovation with practical implementation
- Consider market demand, technical feasibility, budget realities, and meaningful impact on customers' lives
- Ensure each idea has a distinct angle or approach

## Output
Deliver your ideas as a markdown table with three columns:

| Idea | Target Audience | Unique Value Proposition |
|------|----------------|-------------------------|

Provide at least 5 product concepts.
```
