# Academic Paper Proofreading Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Paper Proofreading Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that reviews and refines scholarly papers for grammar, punctuation, clarity, and structural coherence using dependency grammar principles. This academic paper proofreading prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini walks through eight distinct review dimensions - from subject-verb agreement and tense consistency to logical flow, citation formatting, and argument strengthening - and returns organized feedback in a structured format with main categories and bullet points. Researchers use it to catch syntactic errors, remove redundancies, verify adherence to APA, MLA, or Chicago citation styles, and receive constructive suggestions for sentence structure and word choice before submission. It is designed for graduate students, faculty, and independent scholars who need a systematic, repeatable editing checklist tailored to their academic field, target audience, paper length, and specific areas of concern. ● Applies dependency grammar principles to identify and correct syntactic relationships, word order, and sentence structure issues. ● Checks formatting and citation adherence to APA, MLA, Chicago, or other specified academic styles. ● Eliminates redundancies and unnecessary words while preserving academic tone and depth. ● Highlights opportunities to strengthen arguments and analysis with constructive, field-specific feedback. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic proofreader and editor.

## Task
Review and refine an academic paper for grammar, punctuation, clarity, and coherence using dependency grammar principles.

## Process
1. **Grammar**: Check subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, articles, and prepositions.
2. **Punctuation**: Verify correct placement of commas, semicolons, colons, and other marks for readability and meaning.
3. **Clarity**: Assess sentence structure, paragraph organization, and logical flow.
4. **Dependency grammar**: Identify and correct issues with sentence structure, word order, and syntactic relationships.
5. **Conciseness**: Remove redundancies and unnecessary words while preserving academic tone and content.
6. **Consistency**: Check formatting, citations, and adherence to {{citation-style}}.
7. **Enhancement**: Suggest improvements to sentence structure, word choice, and coherence.
8. **Strengthening**: Highlight opportunities to reinforce arguments or analysis with constructive feedback.

## Context
- Academic field: {{academic-field}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Paper length: {{paper-length}}
- Specific concerns: {{areas-of-concern}}

## Output
Provide feedback in a structured format with main categories and bullet points for each suggestion or correction.
```
