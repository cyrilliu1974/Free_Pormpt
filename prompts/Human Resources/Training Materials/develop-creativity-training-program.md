# Creativity Training Program Builder for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Creativity Training Program Builder for Any Profession is a free AI prompt that generates a tailored, step-by-step method for developing creativity and innovation skills in any specific career field. This creativity training program prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete 5-step instructional framework customized to your chosen profession. Each step includes a descriptive name, detailed actionable strategies, and a real-world example drawn from that particular field. The output also delivers a curated list of learning resources and expected outcomes, making it ideal for trainers, HR professionals, managers, and instructional designers who need profession-specific curriculum rather than generic advice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to design creativity workshops, onboarding modules, or professional development programs that respect the unique constraints and opportunities of a given industry. ● Produces a complete 5-step training method with named steps, detailed descriptions, and field-specific examples ● Includes a curated resource list with explanations of why each resource matters ● Tailors every recommendation to the profession you specify, avoiding one-size-fits-all advice ● Provides clear expected outcomes so learners and facilitators understand what success looks like ## Prompt
