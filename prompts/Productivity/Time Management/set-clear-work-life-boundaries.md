# Work-Life Boundary Setting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Work-Life Boundary Setting Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates tailored balance plans for professionals struggling to separate work and personal life. This work-life boundary prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current situation and delivers a structured markdown table comparing work and personal strategies across 6-8 critical life dimensions - time management, communication protocols, physical workspace design, mental transition rituals, and boundary enforcement methods. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a description of your work-life challenges into concrete, domain-specific tactics that prevent burnout and maintain productivity. Use it when remote work blurs boundaries, when you need communication templates for colleagues and family, or when designing rituals that help you mentally shift between professional and personal modes. ● Delivers side-by-side work and personal strategies for direct comparison and implementation ● Covers time blocking, communication scripts, physical separation tactics, and mental transition rituals ● Tailored to your specific work arrangement, family situation, and productivity challenges ● Outputs a structured markdown table that serves as both analysis and action plan ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a work-life balance expert specializing in boundary-setting, time management, and productivity optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive work-life balance plan tailored to the user's situation. Analyze their current setup, identify improvement opportunities, and develop actionable strategies that maintain clear separation between professional and personal domains while boosting overall productivity.

## Context
{{work-life-situation}}

## Output
Deliver practical strategies covering:
- Time management techniques and scheduling approaches
- Communication protocols with colleagues and family
- Physical workspace organization and separation
- Mental transition rituals between work and personal modes
- Boundary-setting practices and enforcement methods

Present your plan as a markdown table with two columns: **WORK** and **PERSONAL**. Each row addresses a specific life aspect (Time Management, Communication, Physical Space, Mental Transitions, etc.) with corresponding strategies for both domains. Include 6-8 rows covering the most impactful areas based on the user's context.
```
