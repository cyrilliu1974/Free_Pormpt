# Photorealistic Health Awareness Outdoor Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Health Awareness Outdoor Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates empathetic, natural outdoor imagery showing subjects in pain with visual anatomical highlights for health campaigns and medical marketing materials. This health awareness prompt for ChatGPT image models produces scenes set in park environments with soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field backgrounds, and customizable pain-area overlays such as soft glows or heatmap effects. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to deliver medium-shot compositions with an empathetic, clean aesthetic suitable for patient education materials, pharmaceutical advertising, physical therapy campaigns, and wellness app visuals. You control subject demographics, wardrobe styles (athleisure, casual), affected body areas (neck, back, joints), and visual treatment of pain indicators through two variables. Reach for this prompt when you need authentic, non-clinical health imagery that balances realism with clear communication of discomfort or symptoms. ● Photorealistic park scenes with natural morning daylight, blurred greenery backgrounds, and shallow depth of field for professional health-campaign aesthetics. ● Customizable pain-area overlays including soft red glows, heatmap effects, or other visual indicators that communicate discomfort without graphic imagery. ● Eye-level medium shots with natural poses (touching neck, holding lower back) that convey authentic patient experiences. ● Clean, empathetic mood suitable for pharmaceutical ads, physical therapy materials, telehealth platforms, and wellness brand campaigns. ## Prompt
