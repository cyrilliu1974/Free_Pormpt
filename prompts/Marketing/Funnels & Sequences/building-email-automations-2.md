# Email Automation Sequence Builder

## 簡介

The Email Automation Sequence Builder is a free AI prompt that creates strategic email nurture sequences tailored to your audience and conversion goals. This email automation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and outputs a complete 5-7 email sequence in a markdown table format. Each email includes a subject line, 3-5 main content points addressing specific pain points, and a call-to-action appropriate to that stage of the customer journey. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, structuring each message to build logically on the previous one - early emails establish trust and educate, middle emails demonstrate value and counter objections, and final emails create urgency to drive conversion. Real use cases include SaaS free-trial onboarding, e-commerce cart abandonment sequences, B2B lead nurturing, course launches, and service-based consultations. This prompt is for email marketers, founders, and growth teams who need a proven sequence framework rather than starting from scratch. ● Produces 5-7 emails with timing recommendations (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, etc.) that mirror real consideration cycles ● Balances educational content, objection handling, and conversion-focused messaging across the sequence ● Outputs a markdown table with subject lines, content bullets, and CTAs ready to adapt into your email platform ● Requires only your business context as input - the prompt handles audience analysis, journey mapping, and tone variation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in automated nurture sequences that convert leads into customers.

## Task
Develop a complete email automation sequence tailored to the target audience and conversion goal. Design each email to logically build upon the previous message, addressing audience pain points while showcasing the value proposition.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze this audience's needs, motivations, and objections throughout their customer journey. Map each email to a stage in their decision-making process, ensuring the sequence feels natural and valuable rather than pushy.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:
- **Email Number** (sequence position, e.g., Day 1, Day 3, Day 7)
- **Subject Line** (compelling, specific to that email's purpose)
- **Main Content Points** (3-5 bullets covering key messages, pain points addressed, value demonstrated)
- **Call-to-Action** (clear next step appropriate to that stage)

Include 5-7 emails spanning the typical consideration period for this offering. Vary tone and focus across the sequence: early emails build trust and educate, middle emails demonstrate value and overcome objections, final emails create urgency and prompt conversion.
```
