# AI Retention Strategy Builder for Churn Prevention

## 簡介

The AI Retention Strategy Builder for Churn Prevention is a free AI prompt that generates targeted retention playbooks for support teams managing AI-detected customer attrition risks. This customer retention prompt for ChatGPT produces 10 concrete strategies, each with trigger conditions tied to early warning signals - decreased engagement, support ticket anomalies, usage changes, or sentiment shifts - along with implementation steps, success metrics, and escalation protocols. Built for businesses running AI-enhanced support operations on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, it bridges predictive analytics and human intervention by translating machine learning signals into immediate team actions. Use it when your support infrastructure detects churn risks and you need a structured playbook to guide proactive outreach, personalized engagement, and escalation decisions. ● Pairs AI-detected triggers with human touchpoints, ensuring retention efforts feel personal rather than automated. ● Delivers both reactive interventions for immediate risks and proactive engagement tactics for medium-term retention. ● Includes escalation protocols so teams know when to intensify outreach or involve account managers. ● Provides clear success metrics for each strategy, enabling continuous refinement of retention playbooks. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert customer retention strategist specializing in AI-enhanced support operations and predictive churn prevention.

## Task

Develop 10 highly targeted retention strategies that leverage early churn signal detection to proactively prevent customer attrition in AI-supported environments. Each strategy must be immediately actionable by support teams when AI systems detect warning signals such as decreased engagement, support ticket patterns, usage anomalies, or sentiment shifts.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Design interventions that combine AI-generated insights with personalized human touchpoints. Address the unique challenges of modern support ecosystems where automation and human expertise work in harmony. Include both reactive interventions for immediate churn risks and proactive engagement strategies for medium-term retention optimization.

## Output

For each of the 10 retention strategies, provide:

1. **Strategy title** – clear, descriptive name
2. **Trigger conditions** – specific AI-detected signals that activate this strategy
3. **Implementation steps** – concrete actions support teams execute immediately
4. **Success metrics** – measurable KPIs to track effectiveness
5. **Escalation protocols** – when and how to intensify intervention

Format as a numbered list with each strategy clearly separated and all five components included.
```
