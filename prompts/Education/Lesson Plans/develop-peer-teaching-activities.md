# Peer Teaching Activities Generator for Any Subject

## 簡介

The Peer Teaching Activities Generator is a free AI prompt that creates three complete peer teaching activities for any subject and topic, designed for educators and instructional designers. This peer teaching prompt for ChatGPT produces fully structured activities that include detailed descriptions, clearly defined student roles, assessment strategies, and a comprehensive implementation table covering learning objectives, timing, materials, setup, procedures, and evaluation methods. Each activity is formatted with step-by-step guidance and includes reference placeholders for evidence-based sources. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for lesson planning, curriculum development, or professional learning community workshops where collaborative learning strategies are needed. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly design peer-led learning experiences that go beyond basic group work, complete with role differentiation and assessment built in. ● Creates three distinct peer teaching activities per request, each with unique student roles and learning objectives ● Includes a structured implementation table with time estimates, materials lists, setup instructions, and step-by-step procedures ● Defines multiple assessment strategies per activity to evaluate both peer teaching effectiveness and content mastery ● Outputs in a consistent, organized format that educators can immediately incorporate into lesson plans or share with colleagues ## Prompt

