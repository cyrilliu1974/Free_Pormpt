# Employee Performance Review System Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Performance Review System Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive, fair, and objective performance review framework tailored to your organization's profile. This performance review prompt for ChatGPT walks you through creating a multi-stage evaluation process complete with measurable metrics, employee development plans, and clear communication protocols for employees, managers, and HR teams. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your organization's size, industry, culture, and objectives as input and returning a structured framework with three distinct review stages, five performance metrics, goal-setting approaches, training resources, mentoring structures, and a continuous improvement process. HR leaders and consultants use it to replace inconsistent review practices with standardized systems that deliver actionable feedback and support career growth. This prompt is for HR professionals, organizational leaders, and consultants who need to establish or redesign a performance management system that balances accountability with employee development. ● Generates three distinct review stages with defined frequencies, participants, and deliverables that create regular feedback loops ● Identifies five objective, measurable performance metrics customized to your industry and organizational goals ● Structures employee development plans including goal-setting frameworks, training methods, and mentoring pairing strategies ● Defines communication protocols that clarify what employees receive, how managers are supported, and what HR oversees ● Includes a continuous improvement process for collecting system feedback and iterating on effectiveness ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR consultant specializing in performance review system design.

## Task
Design a comprehensive, multi-stage performance review process that delivers regular, meaningful feedback and targeted development opportunities. The process must be fair, objective, and aligned with current HR best practices.

## Context
{{organization-profile}}

Include: company size, industry, culture attributes, and primary performance review objectives.

## Output
Deliver a structured performance review framework with:

**Three Review Stages**
For each stage provide:
- Stage name and purpose
- Frequency (timing/cadence)
- Participants (roles involved)
- Key activities and deliverables

**Performance Metrics**
List five objective, measurable metrics relevant to employee roles and organizational goals.

**Employee Development Plan**
- Goal Setting: approach and framework
- Training and Development: methods and resources
- Mentoring and Coaching: structure and pairing strategy

**Communication Plan**
Define communication protocols for:
- Employees: what they receive, when, and how
- Managers: guidance, training, and support provided
- HR: oversight, documentation, and facilitation responsibilities

**Continuous Improvement**
Describe the process for collecting feedback, analyzing effectiveness, and iterating on the review system.

Ensure all components are tailored to the organization profile and interconnect to form a cohesive, actionable system.
```
