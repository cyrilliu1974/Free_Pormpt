# Persuasive Speech Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Persuasive Speech Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compelling speech frameworks designed to convince specific audiences through credibility, emotion, and logic. This persuasive speech prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert speechwriter and build a complete hierarchical outline - from attention-grabbing openings and thesis statements through main arguments, counterargument rebuttals, and memorable calls to action. You supply a speech brief (topic, target audience, desired outcome, main arguments, and situational context), and the prompt structures a multi-level outline using Roman numerals, capital letters, and Arabic numerals that balances ethos, pathos, and logos. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for public speakers, executives, activists, educators, and marketing professionals who need to draft persuasive presentations quickly. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to build a logically organized, emotionally resonant speech that anticipates objections and drives your audience toward a specific decision or belief. ● Balances ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), and logos (logic) across every section of the outline ● Includes dedicated counterargument and rebuttal sections to strengthen your position ● Outputs multi-level hierarchical structure with Roman numerals, capital letters, and Arabic numerals for easy navigation ● Generates introduction hooks, smooth transitions, synthesized conclusions, and audience-aligned calls to action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech writer specializing in persuasive communication.

## Task
Create a compelling persuasive speech outline that effectively convinces the target audience through strategic use of ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), and logos (logic). Structure the outline with clear hierarchical connections between ideas.

## Context
{{speech-brief}} should include: the topic, target audience, desired outcome/persuasive goal, 2-3 main supporting arguments, and any relevant situational context (venue, timing, constraints, audience concerns).

## Output
Deliver a structured outline using:
- Roman numerals (I, II, III) for main sections
- Capital letters (A, B, C) for subpoints
- Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3) for supporting details

Include:

**I. Introduction**
- Attention-grabbing opening
- Credibility establishment
- Thesis statement
- Preview of main points

**II-IV. Main Points** (typically 2-3)
- Supporting evidence and reasoning
- Appeals to logic, emotion, and credibility
- Smooth transitions

**V. Counterarguments & Rebuttals**
- Acknowledge opposing views
- Provide respectful refutations

**VI. Conclusion**
- Synthesize key arguments
- Reinforce main message
- Call to action aligned with persuasive goal
- Memorable closing statement
```
