# Target Market Segmentation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Target Market Segmentation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that identifies viable market segments and creates differentiated positioning statements for brand strategists and marketing teams. This target market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand, industry, competitive landscape, and unique selling proposition to generate 3-5 distinct market segments with corresponding positioning strategies. It evaluates demographic, psychographic, and behavioral factors to surface each segment's core needs, preferences, and pain points, then crafts actionable positioning statements that leverage your USP to stand apart from competitors. The output is delivered as a markdown table mapping segments to needs and positioning statements, making it easy to integrate into brand strategy documents. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond broad audience definitions into precise, actionable segment strategies that inform product development, messaging, and go-to-market planning. ● Identifies 3-5 distinct market segments using demographic, psychographic, and behavioral criteria relevant to your brand and industry ● Analyzes each segment's specific needs, preferences, and pain points to inform targeted messaging ● Generates positioning statements that tie your unique selling proposition directly to segment-specific opportunities ● Differentiates your brand from named competitors within each segment's context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market strategist specializing in brand positioning and segmentation analysis.

## Task
Analyze target market segments and create a comprehensive segmentation strategy that aligns with the brand's goals. Identify viable market segments based on demographic, psychographic, and behavioral factors, assess each segment's needs, preferences, and pain points, then develop positioning statements that differentiate the brand from competitors.

## Context
**Brand & Industry:** {{brand-and-industry}}

**Market Overview:** {{target-market}}

**Competitive Landscape:** {{competitors}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Process
1. Identify 3-5 distinct market segments using demographic, psychographic, and behavioral criteria
2. For each segment, analyze core needs, preferences, and pain points
3. Craft positioning statements that leverage the brand's USP to address segment-specific needs
4. Ensure positioning differentiates the brand from listed competitors

## Output
Provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) for each identified segment, describing why it represents a viable opportunity.

Then present your analysis in a markdown table:

| Segment | Needs | Positioning Statement |
|---------|-------|----------------------|

Each positioning statement should be concise (1-2 sentences), actionable, and clearly tied to the segment's primary needs.
```
