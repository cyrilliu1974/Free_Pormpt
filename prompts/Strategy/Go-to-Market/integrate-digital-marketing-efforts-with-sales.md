# Sales-Aligned Digital Marketing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Sales-Aligned Digital Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven integration plans connecting digital marketing channels to sales objectives for go-to-market teams. This sales-aligned digital marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, active marketing channels, and revenue goals, then outputs a structured table mapping each channel to specific sales targets, 3-5 executable tactics, and quantifiable KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both leading indicators (pipeline metrics) and lagging indicators (revenue outcomes) so teams can track performance across email, social, content, paid media, and other channels. Marketing leaders use it to align campaigns with quarterly sales targets, while revenue operations teams apply it to unify attribution models and optimize spend allocation. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate marketing activity into pipeline contribution, justify channel budgets with sales impact, or build cross-functional alignment between marketing and revenue teams. ● Maps every marketing channel to a specific sales goal, eliminating ambiguity about revenue contribution ● Prescribes 3-5 concrete tactics per channel tailored to your business context, industry, and maturity stage ● Defines leading and lagging KPIs so you can track pipeline velocity and closed revenue in parallel ● Outputs a markdown table format that serves as a ready-to-use planning document for cross-functional teams ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in sales-aligned campaign planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategy that integrates marketing channels with sales objectives. Create a detailed plan outlining specific tactics for each channel and the metrics that will measure success.

## Context
**Business context:** {{business-context}}

**Marketing channels:** {{marketing-channels}}

**Sales goals:** {{sales-goals}}

Ensure the strategy is data-driven, customer-centric, and scalable. Tailor tactics to the target audience, industry vertical, and business maturity level described in the business context.

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with four columns:

| Marketing Channel | Sales Goal | Tactics | Metrics |

Provide at least one row for each marketing channel listed. Each row should include:
- **Marketing Channel:** The specific channel being deployed
- **Sales Goal:** The aligned revenue or pipeline objective
- **Tactics:** 3-5 concrete actions to execute in that channel
- **Metrics:** Quantifiable KPIs to track performance (leading and lagging indicators)
```
