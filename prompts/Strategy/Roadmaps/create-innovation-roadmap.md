# Innovation Roadmap Generator for Product Development

## 簡介

The Innovation Roadmap Generator for Product Development is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive strategic plan for developing and launching new products and services tailored to your company profile. This innovation roadmap prompt for ChatGPT walks through nine structured phases: innovation vision, market analysis with competitive positioning, emerging technology evaluation, ideation with evaluation criteria, concept development including prototyping and user testing plans, a prioritized product roadmap with timelines, go-to-market strategy with segmentation and pricing, measurable success metrics, and concrete next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a company profile into actionable guidance that balances short-term wins with long-term strategic objectives. Product managers use it to align cross-functional teams around data-driven innovation priorities; strategists use it to map customer needs to technology opportunities; executives use it to ensure new initiatives support business goals. ● Delivers market analysis that identifies customer pain points, competitive gaps, and industry trends. ● Provides structured ideation frameworks with evaluation criteria and prioritization rationale for top concepts. ● Builds a product roadmap with feature prioritization, development milestones, and resource allocation. ● Outlines a go-to-market strategy including target segments, value propositions, pricing rationale, and KPIs. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert innovation strategist with deep knowledge in product development, market research, and business strategy.

## Task

Create a comprehensive innovation roadmap to guide the development of new products and services. The roadmap must align with business goals while considering market trends, emerging technologies, and customer needs. Provide specific, actionable recommendations for each phase from ideation through commercialization.

## Context

**Company Profile:**
{{company-profile}}

*Provide: industry, target market, unique value proposition, and key business objectives.*

## Requirements

- Balance short-term wins with long-term strategic objectives
- Ground all recommendations in data-driven insights
- Prioritize customer-centric innovation with continuous feedback loops
- Ensure alignment with stated business objectives throughout

## Output

Deliver the innovation roadmap using this structure:

**1. Innovation Vision**
Overall strategic direction and aspirational goal

**2. Market Analysis**
- Key trends shaping the industry
- Customer needs and pain points
- Competitive landscape and positioning gaps

**3. Emerging Technologies**
Relevant technologies to leverage for competitive advantage

**4. Ideation Phase**
- Recommended brainstorming techniques
- Idea evaluation criteria
- Top 3-5 ideas with rationale

**5. Concept Development**
- Prototyping strategy (approach and fidelity)
- User testing plan (methods and sample size)
- Iteration process and decision gates

**6. Product Roadmap**
- Prioritized features (with rationale)
- Development timeline with key milestones
- Resource allocation requirements

**7. Go-to-Market Strategy**
- Target market segments
- Value proposition for each segment
- Pricing strategy and rationale
- Marketing and sales plan overview

**8. Success Metrics**
- Key performance indicators (leading and lagging)
- Monitoring and evaluation plan

**9. Next Steps**
Immediate actions to initiate roadmap implementation
```
