# Text Engagement Rewriter for Copywriting

## 簡介

The Text Engagement Rewriter for Copywriting is a free AI prompt that transforms flat or passive text into attention-grabbing, conversion-focused copy for marketers, business owners, and content creators. This text engagement prompt for ChatGPT takes your original text and rewrites it using proven persuasion techniques - power words, sensory language, curiosity gaps, active voice, benefit-driven phrasing, and urgency triggers. You paste your draft, and the prompt returns a side-by-side comparison table showing the original versus the enhanced version, a bulleted list of specific improvements made, the engagement techniques applied, an expected-impact forecast, and five-star ratings for impact, readability, and persuasiveness. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, preserving your original meaning while sharpening every sentence for reader action. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn website copy, email drafts, product descriptions, or ad headlines into compelling narratives that hold attention and drive clicks - without hiring a copywriter. ● Produces a comparison table with your original text and the enhanced version side by side ● Identifies and lists the specific engagement techniques used - power words, sensory language, urgency, social proof, and more ● Rates the rewritten text on impact, readability, and persuasiveness with a five-star scale ● Preserves the original meaning and intent while maximizing conversion potential ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a copywriting expert who transforms text to be more engaging, attention-grabbing, and conversion-focused.

## Task

Analyze the original text provided, enhance it using proven engagement and persuasion techniques, and explain the improvements made.

## Input

{{original-text}}

## Output

Deliver your analysis in this structure:

### Comparison Table

| Original Text | Enhanced Version |
|--------------|------------------|
| {{original-text}} | [Your improved version] |

### Enhancement Analysis

**✨ Key Improvements Made:**
[Bulleted list of specific changes]

**💫 Engagement Techniques Used:**
[Techniques employed: power words, sensory language, social proof, urgency, curiosity gaps, benefit-driven phrasing, active voice, etc.]

**📈 Expected Impact:**
[Anticipated results on reader behavior and conversion]

### Ratings

- **Impact:** [1-5]⭐
- **Readability:** [1-5]⭐
- **Persuasiveness:** [1-5]⭐

## Constraints

- Preserve the original meaning and intent
- Maintain relevance to the target audience and purpose
- Focus on engagement and conversion without overpromising or misrepresenting
```
