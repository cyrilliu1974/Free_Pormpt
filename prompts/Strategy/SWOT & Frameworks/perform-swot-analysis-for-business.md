# SWOT Analysis Prompt for Business Strategy

## 簡介

The SWOT Analysis Prompt for Business Strategy is a free AI prompt that evaluates sales performance, competitive positioning, and market dynamics for business analysts and strategic planners. This SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a systematic examination of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats across both internal capabilities and external market conditions. It produces a markdown table with four columns, each containing 4–6 prioritized insights based on competitive dynamics, customer trends, regulatory environment, and anticipated shifts over the next 12–24 months. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for analysts who need actionable intelligence that directly informs resource allocation, competitive strategy, and growth planning. ● Evaluates internal strengths and weaknesses including brand equity, operational efficiency, resource gaps, and core competencies ● Identifies external opportunities such as market gaps, customer trends, and technological shifts the business can exploit ● Assesses competitive pressures, regulatory risks, and economic conditions that pose threats over a 12–24 month horizon ● Outputs a markdown table format that clearly separates the four quadrants with specific, actionable points ranked by strategic impact ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business analyst specializing in strategic market assessment and competitive positioning.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis evaluating the sales performance and market position of the specified business. Deliver actionable insights that inform strategic decision-making.

## Context

**Business to analyze:**
{{business-context}}

Analyze internal capabilities (resources, processes, brand equity, operational efficiency) and external market factors (customer trends, regulatory environment, economic conditions, technological shifts). Assess current competitive dynamics and anticipate emerging opportunities and threats over the next 12–24 months.

## Output

Present your analysis as a markdown table with four columns: **Strengths**, **Weaknesses**, **Opportunities**, and **Threats**. 

- **Strengths**: Internal advantages, core competencies, and assets that provide competitive edge
- **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations, resource gaps, or vulnerabilities that hinder performance
- **Opportunities**: External conditions, market gaps, or trends the business can exploit for growth
- **Threats**: External risks, competitive pressures, or market shifts that could negatively impact the business

Each cell should contain 4–6 concise, specific points. Prioritize insights with the highest strategic impact.
```
