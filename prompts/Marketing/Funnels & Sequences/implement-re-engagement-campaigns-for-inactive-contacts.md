# Re-Engagement Campaign Planner for Inactive Contacts

## 簡介

The Re-Engagement Campaign Planner for Inactive Contacts is a free AI prompt that creates structured re-engagement strategies to reactivate dormant customer relationships. This re-engagement campaign prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete plan: it segments your inactive contacts by activity patterns, designs multi-touch email sequences tailored to each segment, recommends optimal timing and cadence, and defines the metrics that matter. The prompt integrates with your sales tool context and business details to produce audience-appropriate messaging themes, subject line strategies, and clear calls-to-action for every touchpoint. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing teams, sales operations professionals, and CRM managers reach for this prompt when subscriber engagement drops, customer lists go cold, or churn signals appear in the data. ● Segments inactive contacts by recency, past behavior, and engagement history to personalize outreach ● Generates multi-touch email sequences with progression logic, subject lines, messaging themes, and CTAs for each segment ● Recommends send schedules, intervals, and optimal timing based on your audience and industry norms ● Defines KPIs, success benchmarks, and optimization triggers so you can track open rates, click-throughs, and conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in re-engagement campaigns for inactive contacts.

## Task
Create a comprehensive re-engagement campaign plan that includes:
- Contact segmentation strategy based on last activity patterns
- Personalized email sequences tailored to each segment
- Content strategies and messaging approaches
- Timing and cadence recommendations
- Performance metrics and success criteria

## Context
{{business-context}}

The campaign will run in {{sales-tool}} and should reflect our industry norms, customer expectations, and competitive positioning.

## Output
Structure your plan with clear headings and bullet points covering:

**Segmentation Framework**
- Criteria for grouping inactive contacts
- Segment definitions and characteristics

**Email Sequences**
- Sequence structure for each segment (number of touches, progression)
- Subject line strategies
- Email copy guidelines and messaging themes
- Clear calls-to-action for each touchpoint

**Timing & Deployment**
- Send schedule and intervals between emails
- Optimal days/times based on the audience

**Measurement Plan**
- Key performance indicators to track
- Benchmarks for success
- Optimization triggers
```
