# Luxury Tech Advertisement Panel Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Tech Advertisement Panel Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic three-panel horizontal ad campaigns for premium technology products. It produces a 16:9 landscape layout featuring a floating hero device shot with edge lighting, a macro close-up of material craftsmanship and engineering detail, and a lifestyle scene placing the product in modern architectural environments. This luxury tech advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers showroom-quality visuals suitable for website banners, digital billboards, product launches, and brand campaigns without requiring expensive photo shoots. Designers, marketers, and brand teams working on premium product positioning rely on it to generate cohesive visual assets that communicate innovation, precision engineering, and minimalist elegance. ● Produces floating hero device shots with directional edge lighting, glowing displays, and gradient-to-black backgrounds that highlight product contours and premium materials. ● Generates ultra-sharp macro photography of structural frames, buttons, seams, and machining details with shallow depth of field to showcase engineering precision. ● Creates lifestyle integration scenes in modern architectural settings with natural daylight, concrete, glass, and minimal environments that convey confident, effortless sophistication. ● Delivers 16:9 horizontal layouts with three equal panels optimized for web banners, digital billboards, and multi-channel product launch campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Create a refined 1×3 horizontal luxury advertisement emphasizing precision engineering, minimalist elegance, and next-generation technology.

**Format**: 16:9 landscape, three equal horizontal panels for luxury tech campaigns, hero banners, digital billboards, and product launch visuals.

**Panel 1 – Hero Device Shot**: {{product-name}} floating against deep gradient-to-black background with glowing active display. Directional edge lighting highlights contours and materials. Futuristic, elite, precision mood.

**Panel 2 – Material & Edge Detail**: Macro close-up of structural frame, button craftsmanship, and seam precision. Ultra-shallow depth of field with crisp metallic detail showing machining tolerance and seamless transitions. Engineering excellence and minimalism.

**Panel 3 – Luxury Lifestyle Integration**: Professional adult using {{product-name}} in modern architectural setting with minimal concrete, glass, and premium materials. Soft natural daylight. Confident, modern, effortless power mood.

**Visual Style**: Luxury tech advertising, high realism, minimalist composition, cinematic product lighting, premium futuristic aesthetic. Professional tech product photography with high-contrast edge lighting, subtle reflections, balanced exposure, sharp highlights, deep controlled shadows. Ultra-clean geometric sans-serif typography. {{color-palette}} throughout.

Avoid: cheap plastic appearance, overly glossy reflections, fingerprints, smudges, cluttered backgrounds, oversaturated colors, cartoon style, CGI look, watermarks, text overlays.
```
