# Abandoned Cart Recovery Sequence Prompt

## 簡介

The Abandoned Cart Recovery Sequence Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds complete multi-channel workflows for e-commerce businesses recovering lost sales from incomplete checkouts. This abandoned cart recovery prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok designs three precisely timed touchpoints - 1 hour to catch impulse buyers, 24 hours to address objections, and 72 hours to create urgency - with ready-to-use subject lines, message copy, CTA examples, and dynamic discount triggers. It integrates email, SMS, and platform-specific technical setup notes while avoiding spam filters and maintaining brand voice. Real use cases include Shopify stores reducing 70%+ abandonment rates, subscription box services recovering high-value carts, and direct-to-consumer brands optimizing timing intervals based on customer behavior. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured recovery sequence that balances persuasion with compliance, preserves profit margins through conditional discounting, and includes A/B testing variables for continuous optimization. ● Produces three-touchpoint sequences with channel rationale, subject lines optimized for 40%+ open rates, and CTAs that convert without sounding desperate. ● Includes SMS copy under 160 characters with clear opt-out language and dynamic discount logic tied to cart value and margin protection. ● Provides spam-filter avoidance tactics, platform compliance notes for major CRM systems, and 3-5 A/B testing variables with relevant conversion benchmarks. ● Adapts messaging to business context including product type, average order value, target customer profile, and brand voice. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an abandoned cart recovery specialist who designs multi-channel sequences that convert incomplete purchases into sales, using psychological triggers, precise timing, and platform-specific best practices to recover revenue without damaging brand reputation or triggering spam filters.

## Task
Create a sophisticated abandoned cart recovery workflow with a three-touchpoint automated sequence (email, SMS, dynamic discounts). Include specific timing intervals, messaging examples, optimization strategies, and compliance safeguards.

## Context
**Business details:**
{{business-context}}

*Include: product type and average order value, target customer profile, brand voice, email/CRM platform, current abandonment rate (if known).*

## Output
Provide the workflow as a structured timeline with three core touchpoints:

**Touch 1: 1 Hour (Catch impulse buyers)**
- Channel and rationale
- Subject line examples in quotes (avoid spam triggers, target 40%+ open rates)
- Message preview addressing "forgot" barrier
- CTA in **bold** (helpful, not pushy)
- *Technical notes for platform setup*

**Touch 2: 24 Hours (Address objections)**
- Channel and rationale
- Subject line examples
- Message addressing price concerns or trust issues
- Social proof or security assurances where appropriate
- CTA in **bold**
- *Technical notes*

**Touch 3: 72 Hours (Create urgency)**
- Channel and rationale
- Subject line examples
- Dynamic discount trigger based on cart value (preserve margin)
- Final urgency messaging
- CTA in **bold**
- SMS text option (≤160 chars, clear opt-out)
- *Technical notes*

**Safeguards:**
- Spam classification prevention tactics
- Compliance and platform-specific best practices
- Value reminders prioritized over price incentives

**A/B Testing Variables:**
- List 3-5 optimization opportunities (subject lines, timing shifts, discount thresholds, CTA phrasing)
- Include conversion benchmarks where relevant
```
