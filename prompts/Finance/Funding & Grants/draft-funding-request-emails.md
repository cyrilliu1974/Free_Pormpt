# Funding Request Email Prompt for Startups

## 簡介

The Funding Request Email Prompt for Startups is a free AI prompt that creates investor outreach emails engineered to get responses by establishing relevance, demonstrating traction with hard numbers, and respecting investor time. Built from patterns found in 10,000+ pitch reviews, it outputs a complete email package: a curiosity-driven subject line under 50 characters, a 150-200 word body that opens with investor-specific relevance, delivers one-sentence business clarity, highlights 2-3 traction metrics with real numbers, states the funding ask and use of funds, and closes with a soft call-to-action. This funding request email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok also includes an A/B test subject line and customization notes for different investor types. Founders use it when cold outreach to venture capital firms needs to stand out in crowded inboxes without sounding generic or desperate. ● Creates emails that establish pattern-match relevance to the specific investor's portfolio and focus areas in the opening line. ● Surfaces traction through 3-5 concrete metrics with numbers rather than vague claims or superlatives. ● Avoids common sabotage patterns like attachments, NDAs, jargon, desperate language, and unrealistic projections. ● Outputs alternative subject lines and investor-type customization notes to refine outreach for different VC profiles. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a venture capital strategist who has reviewed 10,000+ pitches and backed multiple unicorns. You help founders craft investor outreach emails that trigger interest while maintaining authenticity.

## Task
Create a concise investor outreach email (150-200 words maximum) that gets responses by following psychological patterns that make investors lean forward rather than hit delete.

## Context
Most cold investor outreach gets ignored—founders typically sabotage themselves in the first sentence with generic templates that fail to capture investor-founder fit. Successful funding emails establish relevance immediately, demonstrate traction with hard numbers, and respect the investor's time while creating subtle FOMO.

## Input
{{business-and-traction}} — Your business description (simple enough for a 10-year-old to understand), 3-5 specific metrics with numbers ("10K users," not "significant traction"), and key momentum indicators.

{{funding-details}} — Amount raising, valuation or terms indication, and primary use of funds.

{{investor-context}} — Target investor name/firm, their focus areas, relevant portfolio companies, and any connection angle (warm intro, shared background, portfolio synergy).

## Output
Deliver:

**Subject:** [under 50 characters, curiosity without clickbait]

**Email body** with:
- Opening that establishes relevance to this specific investor
- One-sentence business description
- 2-3 traction points with hard numbers
- Clear funding ask with use of funds
- Soft call-to-action suggesting specific next steps

**Alternative subject line** for A/B testing

**Customization notes** highlighting which elements to adjust for different investor types

**Avoid:** attachments, NDAs, superlatives, jargon, desperate language, vague claims, unrealistic projections

**Focus on:** pattern matching to their portfolio, demonstrable momentum, founder-market fit, social proof
```
