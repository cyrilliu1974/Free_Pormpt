# Social Media Engagement Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Engagement Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates structured social media strategies for brands and businesses looking to build consistent, measurable engagement across platforms. This social media engagement plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a full strategy document including platform-specific best practices, content mix recommendations (educational, entertaining, promotional), weekly posting schedules, and performance measurement frameworks tied to your KPIs. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by accepting inputs for your platforms, product or service, target audience, brand voice, and key performance indicators. Use it when you need to move from ad-hoc posting to a coordinated, metrics-driven social media presence. ● Analyzes each social platform for optimal posting frequency, timing, and native features to use ● Recommends content mix ratios and themes aligned to audience interests and business goals ● Provides a weekly posting schedule template and content rotation framework ● Defines success benchmarks and reporting cadence tied to your specific KPIs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in brand presence and conversion optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social media engagement plan that includes platform-specific strategies, content mix recommendations, a content calendar framework, and measurement guidelines.

## Context
**Platforms:** {{platforms}}
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}
**Key Performance Indicators:** {{kpis}}

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with these sections:

**Platform Analysis**
- Platform-specific best practices and content strategies for each channel
- Optimal posting frequency and timing
- Native features to leverage (Stories, Reels, polls, etc.)

**Content Strategy**
- Content mix ratios (educational, entertaining, promotional)
- Content themes aligned to audience interests and product positioning
- Engagement tactics specific to each platform

**Content Calendar**
- Weekly posting schedule template
- Content rotation framework ensuring variety
- Campaign and seasonal opportunity planning

**Performance Metrics**
- Measurement framework tied to the specified KPIs
- Success benchmarks for each platform
- Reporting cadence and optimization recommendations

Format each section with clear headings and bullet points for immediate implementation.
```
