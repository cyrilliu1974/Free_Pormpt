# Black-and-White Forest Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Black-and-White Forest Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates minimalist, high-contrast forest scenes wrapped in fog with a cinematic film camera look. This black-and-white forest photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates images with early morning diffused lighting, subtle film grain, and deep atmosphere in a 16:9 aspect ratio. You specify the forest scene (dense pines, birch grove, redwood canopy) and fog density (light mist, thick fog, patchy haze) to control mood and visibility, producing award-winning compositions that evoke the timeless quality of medium-format analog photography.
