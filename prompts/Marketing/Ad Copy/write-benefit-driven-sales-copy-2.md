# Benefit-Driven Sales Copy Writer

## 簡介

The Benefit-Driven Sales Copy Writer is a free AI prompt that transforms product features and pain points into conversion-focused sales messaging for marketers, founders, and copywriters. This sales copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft complete sales messages structured in four key sections: an attention-grabbing headline tied to core benefits, a relevance-building opening hook, a features-to-benefits translation that addresses specific pain points, and a clear call to action. You provide your product or service details, target audience, unique value proposition, and feature list; the prompt returns ready-to-use copy that moves logically from capturing attention through demonstrating value to prompting action. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need sales copy that speaks to customer needs rather than just listing features, whether you're writing landing pages, email campaigns, product descriptions, or ad copy. ● Structures output into headline, opening, features-benefits section, and call-to-action for immediate use ● Translates technical features into tangible customer benefits tied to real pain points ● Maintains logical flow from attention capture through value demonstration to action prompt ● Accepts detailed inputs for product, audience, value proposition, and feature lists to ensure relevance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in benefit-driven sales copy.

## Task
Write compelling sales copy that resonates with the target audience and drives conversions. Structure your writing to ensure logical flow, moving from attention-grabbing headline through value demonstration to action.

## Context
**Offer:** {{product-service}}

**Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Value proposition:** {{unique-value-proposition}}

**Features and pain points:** {{features-and-pain-points}}

## Output
Deliver the sales copy in these sections:

- **Headline**: Attention-grabbing statement that speaks to the core benefit or pain point
- **Opening**: Hook that builds on the headline and establishes relevance
- **Features and Benefits**: Key features translated into concrete benefits, addressing specific pain points
- **Call to Action**: Clear, compelling next step

Use bullet points within sections where they enhance readability.
```
