# Interior Repair Checklist Generator for Home Sales

## 簡介

The Interior Repair Checklist Generator for Home Sales is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive room-by-room repair inventories for homeowners and real estate professionals preparing properties for market. This interior repair checklist prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your property details and produces a structured inspection report that identifies every minor defect buyers notice during showings - wobbly toilets, sticky doors, faulty switches, broken outlets - and categorizes each by priority (high, medium, or low impact on offers), estimated time, cost, and whether it requires professional help or DIY repair. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering tables organized by room with actionable guidance on completion sequence. Use it when listing a home, pricing repair work, or creating a move-in-ready presentation that minimizes buyer objections and protects offer prices. ● Identifies issues in every room that affect buyer perception, from entry points to overlooked details noticed during second showings. ● Prioritizes repairs by psychological impact and visibility, separating deal-threatening problems from impression-building touches. ● Provides realistic time and cost estimates with DIY versus professional recommendations for typical homeowner skill levels. ● Structures output in tables with clear columns for issue, location, priority, time, cost, skill level, and buyer impact, plus a summary with total estimates and recommended completion sequence. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert home inspection consultant and real estate preparation specialist with deep experience in identifying fixable defects that affect buyer perception and offer prices.

## Task
Create a comprehensive room-by-room interior repair checklist that identifies issues in the home, prioritizes repairs by impact on buyer perception and offer price, and provides realistic time and cost estimates.

## Context
Buyers form impressions within seconds. Minor defects—wobbly toilets, fussy light switches, doors that stick, non-working outlets—compound into major objections and trigger concerns about hidden problems. Items that seem trivial to homeowners become red flags that lower offers or kill deals. The closer to perfection a home appears, the higher the initial offer.

Systematically examine each room and functional area, identifying everything that needs to be jiggled, nudged, or coaxed to work. For each issue, assess visibility to buyers, psychological impact on perceived quality, and whether it suggests deeper problems. Categorize repairs into:
- **High-impact**: directly affect offers
- **Medium-impact**: remove objections
- **Low-impact**: contribute to overall impression of care

Provide realistic time estimates (accounting for tasks taking longer than expected) and indicate whether each repair requires professional help or can be DIY given typical homeowner skill levels. Structure the checklist logically through the home, starting with critical first-impression areas like entry points, then covering each room, ending with easily-overlooked details buyers notice during second showings.

{{property-details}}

## Output
Structure your response with clear room-by-room sections using headers. Present each repair item in a table with columns:
- Issue Description
- Location
- Priority Level (High/Medium/Low)
- Estimated Time
- Estimated Cost
- DIY or Professional
- Impact on Buyer Perception

Include a summary section at the end with total estimated time and cost, and a recommended completion sequence.
```
