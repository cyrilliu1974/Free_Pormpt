# Employee Feedback Benchmarking Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Feedback Benchmarking Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive cross-departmental feedback analysis for HR leaders, organizational development teams, and performance management professionals. This employee feedback prompt for ChatGPT collects and examines feedback data across job satisfaction, professional development, work-life balance, and management effectiveness, then benchmarks results against industry standards to surface strengths and improvement opportunities. It produces a multi-section performance report with executive summaries, methodology documentation, detailed findings with visual data representations, and prioritized recommendations complete with feasibility assessments. Works seamlessly with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to transform raw feedback into actionable intelligence for decision-makers. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically analyze employee sentiment, compare departmental performance, or build data-driven engagement strategies that executives can act on immediately. ● Systematically collects and examines feedback using both statistical analysis and qualitative methods across customizable organizational scopes ● Benchmarks departmental and organizational performance against industry standards to identify competitive positioning ● Generates visual data representations including charts, graphs, and tables that make patterns immediately visible ● Delivers prioritized, specific recommendations with impact and feasibility scores for each proposed intervention ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a performance review expert specializing in organizational behavior and employee engagement.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive benchmarking analysis of employee feedback across departments. Collect, examine, and compare feedback data to identify patterns, strengths, and areas for improvement using data-driven methodologies and performance management best practices.

## Context
**Scope of Analysis:**
{{organizational-scope}}

**Key Performance Indicators:**
{{kpi}}

The analysis should cover job satisfaction, professional development, work-life balance, and management effectiveness. Benchmark findings against industry standards and best practices to determine where the organization excels and where gaps exist.

## Output
Generate a structured performance report containing:

**Executive Summary**
- High-level findings and priority recommendations

**Methodology**
- Data collection approach (surveys, interviews, performance metrics)
- Statistical and qualitative analysis techniques applied

**Detailed Findings**
- Patterns, trends, and anomalies at departmental and organizational levels
- Visual data representations (charts, graphs, tables)
- Benchmarking comparison against industry standards

**Insights & Recommendations**
- Meaningful interpretation of findings
- Specific, prioritized strategies for improvement (enhanced communication, professional development programs, refined management practices)
- Impact and feasibility assessment for each recommendation

Maintain an unbiased, data-driven approach. Write in clear, professional language suitable for executive decision-makers. Focus on actionable insights that support employee engagement and development planning.
```
