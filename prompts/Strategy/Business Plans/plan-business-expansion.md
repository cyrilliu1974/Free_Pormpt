# Business Expansion Plan Generator for New Markets

## 簡介

The Business Expansion Plan Generator for New Markets is a free AI prompt that builds a complete roadmap for entering new markets or locations for business strategists, entrepreneurs, and growth planners. This business expansion prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current business model, identifies market opportunities and challenges, and delivers a structured expansion plan organized across four dimensions: Objectives, Strategies, Tactics, and Timeline. It covers every critical phase - market entry, operational setup, competitive positioning, customer acquisition, and scale-up - and outputs a markdown table that aligns each goal with actionable steps and realistic timelines. Use it when planning geographic expansion, launching in new verticals, or opening additional locations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Analyzes your existing business model and expansion context to tailor the plan to your specific industry, market, and resources ● Covers market research, competitive analysis, financial projections, operational requirements, and marketing strategies in a single framework ● Organizes the plan into coherent phases from market entry through scale-up, with each row aligning objectives to tactics and timelines ● Outputs a markdown table format that's easy to share with stakeholders, refine iteratively, or integrate into business planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in market expansion and growth planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive business expansion plan that provides a detailed roadmap for entering a new market or location. Analyze the current business model, identify opportunities and challenges, and outline strategies for successful expansion across all critical dimensions: market research, competitive positioning, financial projections, operational requirements, and marketing strategies.

## Context
{{expansion-context}}
(Include: type of business, target market or location, current business size, available resources, and desired expansion timeline)

## Output
Present the expansion plan as a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Objectives | Strategies | Tactics | Timeline |
|------------|------------|---------|----------|

Each row should represent one coherent aspect of the expansion plan, with clear alignment between the objective, supporting strategies, specific tactics, and implementation timeline. Cover all phases of expansion: market entry, operational setup, competitive positioning, customer acquisition, and scale-up.
```
