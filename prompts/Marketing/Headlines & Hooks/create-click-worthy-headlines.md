# Headline Rewriter for Specificity and Curiosity

## 簡介

The Headline Rewriter for Specificity and Curiosity is a free AI prompt that transforms weak, generic headlines into three distinct, click-worthy alternatives optimized for engagement. This headline rewriter prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your existing title and produces three rewritten options - each under 20 words - that weave together specificity (concrete pain points or outcomes), curiosity (unexpected insights or contrarian angles), actionability (clear reader benefits), and emotion (power words and sensory details). Every rewritten headline includes a one-line explanation of which elements drive its effectiveness. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it a versatile tool for blog posts, email subject lines, landing pages, and social media content. Content marketers, copywriters, and newsletter editors rely on it to test multiple angles quickly without sacrificing clarity or falling into clichéd hype. ● Rewrites any generic headline into three distinct options in under 20 words each. ● Applies four proven copywriting levers - specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion - simultaneously. ● Provides a one-line explanation for each rewrite, teaching you what makes a headline effective. ● Filters out clichés, hype, and sensational language to keep your brand voice credible. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in transforming weak headlines into compelling, click-worthy alternatives using specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion.

## Task
Rewrite the provided headline into three distinct, engaging options. For each:
- Keep it under 20 words
- Make it specific (concrete pain point, benefit, strategy, or outcome)
- Add curiosity (unexpected insight, contrarian view, or insider angle)
- Imply actionability (clear result the reader will gain)
- Incorporate emotion (power words, sensory detail, or story element)
- Avoid clichés, hype, and sensational language

## Input
Generic headline: {{generic-headline}}

## Output
For each of the three options:

**Headline:** [your rewritten headline]

**Why it works:** [one-line explanation of which elements—specificity, curiosity, actionability, emotion—drive its effectiveness]
```
