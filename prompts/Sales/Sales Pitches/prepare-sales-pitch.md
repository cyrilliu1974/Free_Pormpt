# Sales Pitch Generator for Products and Services

## 簡介

The Sales Pitch Generator for Products and Services is a free AI prompt that builds complete, persuasive sales pitches tailored to your product, target customer, and brand voice. This sales pitch prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to create a five-section pitch: an attention-grabbing introduction, a problem statement that articulates customer pain points, a solution section presenting your unique selling proposition, a benefits breakdown using emotional triggers and logical arguments, and a motivating call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and uses dependency grammar principles to ensure every sentence flows logically. Sales teams use it to prepare pitch decks, email campaigns, and investor presentations; marketers use it to craft landing page copy and product launch messaging. Reach for this prompt when you need a persuasive, customer-centric sales message quickly, whether you're pitching a SaaS product, consulting service, physical goods, or B2B solution. ● Produces a complete five-part pitch structure from hook to call-to-action ● Incorporates your unique selling proposition, key benefits, and brand voice into every section ● Uses dependency grammar to ensure logical flow and readability ● Includes placeholders for social proof, testimonials, and statistics to boost credibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive sales messaging.

## Task
Create a compelling sales pitch that communicates value, addresses customer pain points, and motivates action. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically and clearly from the previous one.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-service}}

Target customer and their pain points: {{ideal-customer}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Key benefits: {{key-benefits}}

Brand voice and tone: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver a structured sales pitch with the following sections:

**Introduction** – Hook the reader with a compelling opening that acknowledges their situation

**Problem Statement** – Articulate the pain points and challenges your ideal customer faces

**Solution** – Present your product/service as the answer, highlighting the unique selling proposition

**Benefits** – Detail the key benefits using persuasive language, emotional triggers, and logical arguments. Incorporate social proof, testimonials, or relevant statistics where applicable

**Call-to-Action** – End with a clear, motivating instruction that guides the customer to take the next step

Use bullet points for key details within each section. Maintain the specified brand voice throughout.
```
