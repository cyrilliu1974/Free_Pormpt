# Concept Art Style Generator

## 簡介

The Concept Art Style Generator is a free AI prompt that creates concept art images styled after specific art movements for designers, artists, and creative professionals. This concept art prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana takes three variables - subject, shot type, and art movement - and renders them as professional concept art in a 16:9 aspect ratio. You might generate a futuristic cityscape in the style of Art Deco, a character portrait inspired by Impressionism, or an environment study that echoes Brutalist architecture. The prompt works across image-generation models that accept style and composition parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need quick visual exploration of ideas, mood boards, or portfolio pieces that reflect a particular artistic tradition. ● Specify any subject - characters, environments, objects, or abstract concepts - and pair it with a shot type like close-up, wide angle, or isometric view. ● Apply any art movement style, from Renaissance and Baroque to Cyberpunk and Synthwave, to give your concept art historical or thematic grounding. ● Output is locked to 16:9 aspect ratio, ideal for presentation decks, video pre-production, and widescreen displays. ● Works consistently across major image models without requiring deep prompt-engineering knowledge. ## Prompt

```
{{subject}}, {{shot-type}}, in the style of {{art-movement}}, concept art, --ar 16:9
```
