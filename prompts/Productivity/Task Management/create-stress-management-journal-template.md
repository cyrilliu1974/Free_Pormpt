# Stress Management Journal Template Builder

## 簡介

The Stress Management Journal Template Builder is a free AI prompt that creates personalized journaling frameworks designed to address workplace stress through cognitive-behavioral therapy, mindfulness, and evidence-based techniques. This stress management journal prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete template featuring three distinct exercises, each with a descriptive name, clear instructions, three guided writing prompts, and reflection questions that encourage introspection, problem-solving, and positive reframing. The prompt adapts to your specific work stressors and journaling preferences, whether you face deadline pressure, interpersonal conflict, burnout, or role ambiguity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering structured output that connects your unique challenges to therapeutic journaling practices backed by workplace wellness research. This prompt is ideal for professionals experiencing work-related stress, HR teams building employee wellness resources, coaches supporting clients through workplace challenges, or anyone seeking a systematic approach to processing difficult emotions and situations at work. ● Produces three complete journaling exercises with names, descriptions, guided prompts, and reflection questions tailored to specific work stressors ● Adapts to individual journaling experience levels and preferred styles, from beginners to experienced practitioners ● Incorporates evidence-based stress management strategies including cognitive reframing, mindfulness techniques, and problem-solving frameworks ● Includes an introduction linking stressors to journaling benefits and a conclusion with guidance on sustainable practice and additional resources ## Prompt
