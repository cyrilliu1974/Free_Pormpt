# Social Media Image Design Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Image Design Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that produces actionable design blueprints for creating scroll-stopping social media graphics. This social media image design prompt for ChatGPT walks you through concept development, color palette selection, visual element choices, text overlay strategy, and platform-specific optimization. You input your product or service, target platform (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, X), target audience, and brand identity, and it returns a numbered guide with tactical substeps, platform considerations, and engagement best practices. Real use cases include launching product campaigns, building brand awareness, driving event registrations, and increasing conversion rates across social channels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need structured, repeatable guidance for your social media design workflow rather than one-off image creation. ● Covers concept, color, visuals, text overlays, and platform-specific optimization in one workflow ● Tailors advice to your exact audience demographics and brand personality ● Includes tactical substeps and engagement-maximizing best practices under each design phase ● Adapts recommendations for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, and other platforms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert visual content creator specializing in high-engagement social media graphics.

## Task
Provide a step-by-step guide to design an attention-grabbing image that stops scrollers and drives engagement for {{product-or-service}}. The guide should cover concept development, color and visual selection, text overlay strategy, and platform-specific optimization for {{platform}}.

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Brand identity: {{brand-identity}}
- Goal: Create scroll-stopping visuals that encourage likes, shares, comments, and conversions

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a numbered list of actionable steps. Under each main step, include:
- Bullet points for substeps and tactical advice
- Important considerations specific to the platform and audience
- Best practices for maximizing engagement and visual impact
```
