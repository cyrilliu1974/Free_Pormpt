# Risk Mitigation Plan Generator for Business

## 簡介

The Risk Mitigation Plan Generator for Business is a free AI prompt that produces structured risk assessment tables for any business type and industry. This risk mitigation plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes industry-specific threats across operational, financial, strategic, compliance, reputational, and market categories, then outputs a markdown table with at least 10 comprehensive risk entries. Each entry includes likelihood ratings (Low, Medium, High), impact assessments (Minor, Moderate, Severe, Critical), and actionable mitigation strategies tailored to your business context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, with adjustable column structures to match your reporting needs. Reach for this prompt when building risk frameworks for clients, preparing board presentations, conducting industry audits, or establishing baseline risk profiles for new ventures. ● Systematically identifies risks across six core categories relevant to the specified industry ● Assesses both likelihood and potential impact using standardized rating scales ● Delivers specific, actionable mitigation strategies rather than generic advice ● Outputs a customizable markdown table format that integrates into reports and presentations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant developing a comprehensive risk mitigation plan.

## Task
Identify, assess, and provide strategies to manage potential risks for the specified business. Analyze industry-specific risks, categorize them, assess their likelihood and potential impact, and devise appropriate mitigation strategies.

## Context
Business and industry: {{business-and-industry}}

The plan must be thorough, practical, and tailored to the specific business context. Consider operational, financial, strategic, compliance, reputational, and market risks relevant to this sector.

## Output
Present your risk mitigation plan as a markdown table with {{number-of-columns}} columns covering: Risk Category, Likelihood, Impact, and Mitigation Strategies (adjust column structure to match the specified number).

Provide at least 10 comprehensive risk entries with:
- Clear risk categorization
- Likelihood assessment (e.g., Low, Medium, High)
- Impact assessment (e.g., Minor, Moderate, Severe, Critical)
- Specific, actionable mitigation strategies for each risk

Ensure the risks and strategies are directly relevant to the business type and industry provided.
```
