# Customer Re-Engagement Campaign Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Re-Engagement Campaign Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds systematic win-back strategies for businesses looking to reactivate dormant customers and drive repeat sales. This customer re-engagement campaign prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok delivers a complete action plan that includes inactivity thresholds aligned with your purchase cycles, progressive workflows that escalate from gentle outreach to compelling offers, and coordinated multi-channel sequences across email, SMS, and social media. Marketing teams use it to design campaigns that acknowledge the relationship gap, provide genuine value, and include message templates ready for immediate deployment. The prompt tailors timing intervals and escalation logic to your business context, ensuring outreach feels personal rather than automated while driving measurable reactivation. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to reconnect with inactive customers, rebuild emotional connection, and convert dormant accounts into revenue-generating relationships. ● Defines inactivity thresholds based on purchase cycles and customer lifetime value specific to your business model. ● Builds progressive escalation sequences that balance persistence with respect, moving from soft touches to stronger incentives. ● Coordinates multi-channel outreach across email, SMS, and social platforms with optimal timing intervals grounded in customer psychology. ● Provides message templates that acknowledge the gap in the relationship, offer genuine value, and include clear calls to action. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer retention strategist specializing in re-engagement campaigns that convert inactive customers into active buyers through systematic, multi-channel outreach.

## Task
Design a comprehensive customer re-engagement campaign tailored to {{business-context}}. Deliver a detailed action plan that includes:

- Inactivity threshold frameworks aligned with purchase cycles and customer lifetime value
- Progressive re-engagement workflows that escalate from soft touches to compelling offers
- Multi-channel outreach sequences coordinating email, SMS, and social media
- Optimal timing intervals that balance persistence with customer psychology
- Message templates that acknowledge the relationship gap, provide genuine value, and include clear calls to action

## Context
Customer inactivity often signals relationship breakdown, not just purchase timing. Your approach must feel personal rather than automated, rebuilding emotional connection while driving measurable action.

## Output
Structure your campaign plan with clear section headings. Provide all recommendations as detailed bullet points with specific timelines, channel strategies, and ready-to-use message templates that can be implemented immediately.
```
