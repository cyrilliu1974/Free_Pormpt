# SEO Keyword Alignment Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword Alignment Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates tailored content optimization strategies for businesses looking to improve organic search visibility and align their website with target keywords. This keyword alignment prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, existing content, and target keywords to produce a complete SEO strategy covering keyword analysis, content optimization, on-page technical improvements, and implementation priorities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable recommendations including search volume analysis, competitor gap identification, title tag and meta description updates, internal linking architecture, schema markup suggestions, and content cluster opportunities ranked by expected impact. Marketing teams use it to bridge the gap between keyword research and execution, while SEO specialists rely on it to create audit-ready optimization roadmaps. This prompt is for content marketers, SEO strategists, and business owners who need a structured approach to align existing website content with search intent and close keyword gaps against competitors. ● Performs keyword intent mapping and competitive gap analysis against your specific target keywords and business type ● Delivers an on-page SEO checklist covering title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, internal linking, and schema markup ● Identifies new content opportunities organized into topic clusters and pillar page strategies ● Prioritizes recommendations into quick wins versus long-term initiatives based on expected ranking impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO content strategist specializing in keyword alignment and organic search optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive SEO content strategy that aligns website content with target keywords to improve search engine rankings and attract qualified traffic. Deliver actionable recommendations covering keyword research, content optimization, and on-page SEO techniques.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}
(Include: business type, target audience demographics/psychographics, and main competitors)

**Current State:**
{{current-content-overview}}
(Briefly describe existing website content, structure, and any known performance issues)

**Target Keywords:**
{{target-keywords}}
(List primary and secondary keywords you want to rank for)

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings and bullet points covering:

1. **Keyword Analysis** - Search volume, competition, intent mapping, and gap analysis vs. competitors
2. **Content Optimization Plan** - Specific recommendations for aligning existing content with target keywords
3. **On-Page SEO Checklist** - Title tags, meta descriptions, headers, internal linking, schema markup
4. **New Content Opportunities** - Topic clusters, pillar pages, and supporting content to create
5. **Implementation Priority** - Quick wins vs. long-term initiatives, ranked by expected impact

Ensure all recommendations are specific, actionable, and tailored to the business context provided.
```
