# Landing Page Copywriting Prompt for High Conversions

## 簡介

The Landing Page Copywriting Prompt for High Conversions is a free AI prompt that generates complete, conversion-optimized landing page copy for marketers, founders, and copywriters who need to turn visitors into customers. This landing page copywriting prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as a direct-response copywriter that structures your entire page - from attention-grabbing headlines and problem agitation to benefit-driven body copy, social proof placement, objection handling, and calls-to-action with genuine urgency. You supply your product or service details, target audience, and unique value proposition; the AI delivers a ready-to-publish landing page built around visitor psychology, the 3-second attention test, and ethical persuasion principles. Real-world use cases include product launch pages, lead-magnet opt-ins, SaaS signup flows, course enrollment pages, and service booking funnels. Reach for this prompt when you need landing page copy that speaks to one person rather than a demographic, addresses objections before they arise, and prioritizes transformation over information. ● Delivers every essential landing page section in sequence: headline, subheadline, opening hook, problem agitation, solution introduction, benefits, social proof, objection handling, calls-to-action, urgency, guarantee, and final CTA. ● Applies direct-response writing standards - 8th-grade readability, short mobile-friendly paragraphs, outcome-focused benefits, and authentic urgency instead of hype. ● Mirrors the visitor's internal reasoning and inner monologue to create immediate engagement and guide decision-making. ● Tailors tone, pain points, and proof elements to your specific product, audience, and unique value proposition through three customizable variables. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused copywriter with direct response experience. You write landing pages that mirror the visitor's internal reasoning, use psychological triggers ethically, and prioritize authentic urgency over manipulation.

## Task
Write complete landing page copy optimized for conversion. Address the visitor's immediate pain or desire, guide them through objections, build trust with social proof, and close with a compelling call-to-action.

## Context
The landing page must capture attention in seconds and convert today's skeptical, scroll-fast visitors. Avoid generic content and corporate jargon. Every element should feel like the natural next step in a one-to-one conversation.

{{product-service-details}}

{{target-audience}}

{{unique-value-proposition}}

## Output
Deliver the landing page in this structure:

**HEADLINE**  
[Pass the 3-second test—instantly communicate value and address immediate pain or desire]

**SUBHEADLINE**  
[Promise a specific transformation or outcome]

**OPENING HOOK**  
[First paragraph that creates immediate engagement]

**PROBLEM AGITATION**  
[2-3 paragraphs exploring their pain points]

**SOLUTION INTRODUCTION**  
[How your offer solves their problem]

**BENEFITS SECTION**  
• Benefit 1: [Outcome-focused explanation]  
• Benefit 2: [Outcome-focused explanation]  
• Benefit 3: [Outcome-focused explanation]

**SOCIAL PROOF**  
[Specific, verifiable testimonials, statistics, or case studies]

**OBJECTION HANDLING**  
[Address top 3 concerns before they arise]

**CALL TO ACTION**  
[Action-verb CTA button text + supporting copy that creates momentum]

**URGENCY/SCARCITY**  
[Genuine, time-sensitive reason to act now]

**GUARANTEE/RISK REVERSAL**  
[Remove purchase anxiety]

**FINAL CTA**  
[Reinforced action step]

---

**Writing standards:**  
- Write at 8th-grade reading level  
- Use short paragraphs and bullet points for mobile  
- Focus on transformation, not information  
- Write to one person, not a demographic  
- Every sentence must earn its place
```
