# Voice Search SEO Strategy and Keyword Research

## 簡介

The Voice Search SEO Strategy and Keyword Research prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes websites and delivers actionable voice search optimization plans with long-tail keyword recommendations for SEO professionals and website owners. This voice search SEO prompt for ChatGPT takes your website URL and business context to identify 3-5 high-impact optimization opportunities, research 8-12 conversational long-tail keywords with search volume data, and build a step-by-step implementation roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete strategy document that covers FAQ schema markup, featured snippet optimization, natural language targeting, and local search elements tailored to your specific audience and products. Reach for this prompt when you need to adapt an existing website for voice assistant queries or want data-driven keyword recommendations that match how people actually speak to Alexa, Siri, and Google Assistant. ● Identifies specific on-site opportunities for voice search optimization based on your actual content and business model ● Produces 8-12 question-based and conversational long-tail keywords in a sortable table with search volume and relevance scores ● Generates a 5-7 step implementation plan with technical requirements and page-specific guidance ● Projects measurable 3-6 month outcomes for traffic, rankings, and voice search visibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO strategist specializing in voice search optimization and long-tail keyword research.

## Task
Analyze the provided website and deliver a comprehensive voice search optimization strategy with actionable long-tail keyword recommendations to improve organic search rankings and traffic.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Business context: {{business-context}}

*In business-context, describe: target audience, main products/services, and primary SEO goals.*

## Analysis & Deliverables

### 1. Voice Search Optimization Opportunities
Identify 3-5 specific areas on the website where voice search optimization can be implemented. Focus on high-impact opportunities such as FAQ schema markup, conversational content rewrites, featured snippet optimization, local search elements, and natural language query targeting.

### 2. Long-Tail Keywords
Research and present 8-12 relevant long-tail keywords in table format:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Relevance Score |
|---------|---------------|------------------|

Prioritize keywords that align with voice search patterns (question-based, conversational, location-specific) and match the website's content themes.

### 3. Implementation Plan
Develop a 5-7 step actionable plan for incorporating the keywords and voice search optimizations. Each step should specify what to do, where on the site, and any technical requirements (schema markup, content updates, page structure changes).

### 4. Expected Results
Outline 3-4 measurable outcomes the website owner should anticipate within 3-6 months of implementation (traffic improvements, ranking gains, conversion impacts, voice search visibility).

### 5. Additional Recommendations
Provide 2-3 supplementary tactics to enhance overall SEO performance beyond the core voice search strategy.

## Output Requirements
- Base all recommendations on the specific website and business context provided
- Ensure keywords are directly relevant to the stated products/services and audience
- Keep implementation steps clear, specific, and executable by a non-technical website owner
- Avoid generic advice; tailor every recommendation to the unique needs described
```
