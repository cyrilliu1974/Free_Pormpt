# SEO Content Optimization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The SEO Content Optimization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive optimization strategies for existing website content to improve search engine rankings and organic traffic. This SEO content optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target keywords, competitive landscape, and business context to deliver a structured action plan covering keyword research, meta tag refinements, content gaps, technical SEO considerations, and competitive differentiation opportunities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a prioritized roadmap with specific recommendations for each optimization area. Real use cases include auditing underperforming blog posts, refreshing product pages for seasonal keywords, and identifying quick-win opportunities to outrank competitors in your niche. This prompt is for SEO specialists, content marketers, and site owners who need a systematic approach to improving existing content rather than starting from scratch. ● Conducts keyword research with search volume, difficulty, and intent mapping for your target terms ● Provides specific on-page SEO improvements including meta titles, descriptions, header tags, and URL structure ● Identifies content gaps and internal linking opportunities by analyzing competitor strategies ● Delivers a prioritized implementation roadmap with expected impact for each recommendation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist optimizing existing website content to improve search engine rankings, visibility, and organic traffic.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive SEO optimization plan for the provided website. Conduct keyword research, update meta tags, refine content structure, and integrate strategic keywords to align with search intent and improve performance.

## Context
**Target keywords:** {{target-keywords}}
**Website URL:** {{website-url}}
**Competitive landscape:** {{competitors}}
**Business context:** {{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a structured optimization plan using clear headings and bullet points for each major step. Include:

- **Keyword Research & Strategy** – Analysis of target keywords, search volume, difficulty, and intent mapping
- **On-Page SEO Improvements** – Meta titles, descriptions, header tag optimization, URL structure
- **Content Enhancement** – Recommendations for content gaps, keyword integration points, readability improvements, internal linking
- **Technical Considerations** – Page speed, mobile optimization, schema markup opportunities
- **Competitive Differentiation** – Gaps in competitor content that present ranking opportunities
- **Implementation Roadmap** – Prioritized action items with expected impact

Each section should contain specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the provided website and industry context.
```
