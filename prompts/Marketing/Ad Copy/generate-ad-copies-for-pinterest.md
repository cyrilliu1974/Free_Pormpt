# Pinterest Ad Copy Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Pinterest Ad Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused ad packages specifically optimized for Pinterest's visual-first platform. This Pinterest ad copy prompt for ChatGPT delivers a complete ad package including an attention-grabbing headline (80 characters or fewer), persuasive 2-3 sentence descriptions, bullet-pointed key benefits, action-oriented CTAs, 5-7 strategic hashtags, and detailed visual recommendations. It structures writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and impact while weaving in power words and emotional triggers tailored to your target audience. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your product, audience, unique selling proposition, website URL, and brand voice. Marketing teams, e-commerce brands, and Pinterest advertisers will reach for this when launching campaigns that need cohesive copy and creative direction for Pinterest's scroll-stopping environment. ● Writes concise headlines under 80 characters that capture attention in crowded Pinterest feeds ● Produces persuasive descriptions that communicate value propositions clearly in 2-3 sentences ● Generates bullet lists of specific benefits and unique selling points that differentiate your offering ● Provides action-oriented CTAs designed to drive clicks to your website ● Recommends 5-7 high-performing hashtags to boost discoverability ● Suggests detailed imagery and video concepts that complement the copy and stop users mid-scroll ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in Pinterest advertising.

## Task
Create compelling Pinterest ad copy that drives traffic and conversions. Deliver a complete ad package with all necessary components optimized for the platform.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Website URL: {{website-url}}
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Requirements
- Structure writing using dependency grammar principles for maximum clarity and impact
- Use power words and emotional triggers appropriate to the audience
- Optimize all elements for Pinterest's visual-first environment
- Ensure cohesion between copy and visual recommendations

## Output
Deliver your response with these labeled sections:

**Headline**
One attention-grabbing headline (80 characters or fewer)

**Description**
Concise, persuasive copy (2-3 sentences) that highlights the core value proposition

**Key Benefits**
Bullet list of 3-4 specific benefits or unique selling points

**Call-to-Action**
One strong, action-oriented CTA that drives clicks to the website

**Hashtags**
5-7 relevant, high-performing hashtags to maximize discoverability

**Visual Recommendations**
Detailed suggestions for imagery or video concepts that complement the copy and stop the scroll
```
