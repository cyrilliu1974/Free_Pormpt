# Early Performance Decline Warning Signs Prompt

## 簡介

The Early Performance Decline Warning Signs Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps managers identify and address performance issues before they escalate through structured early intervention strategies. This performance management prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces ten detailed warning signs, each with observable indicators managers can track, explanations of their significance, immediate and long-term response strategies, documentation guidance, and criteria for when to escalate to HR. It translates subtle behavioral and productivity shifts into actionable management steps that preserve working relationships while addressing concerns directly. Use it when you need to move from reactive discipline to proactive support, when training new managers on performance observation, or when building a consistent early-intervention framework across your organization. ● Recognizes ten specific early warning signs with concrete behavioral indicators managers can observe and document in daily work. ● Provides immediate response strategies and long-term management actions for each sign that support employee success rather than punitive measures. ● Includes documentation best practices and conversation guidance that protect both the manager and employee while addressing performance concerns. ● Tailors all recommendations to your specific management context through the management-context variable, adapting advice to team size, industry, and organizational culture. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources consultant and organizational psychologist specializing in performance management and early intervention strategies.

## Task
Identify 10 critical early warning signs of declining employee performance. For each warning sign, provide:

- **Observable indicators**: Specific behaviors and patterns managers can directly observe
- **Significance**: Why this sign predicts performance decline and what deeper issues it may signal
- **Response strategies**: Immediate and long-term management actions that support employee success
- **Documentation and communication guidance**: How to record observations and approach conversations
- **Escalation criteria**: When to involve HR

## Context
Managers often miss subtle behavioral and productivity shifts that signal performance problems before they escalate. Focus on early intervention techniques that preserve working relationships and enable employee success rather than punitive measures.

Consider this management context:
{{management-context}}

## Output
Structure your response as a numbered list (1-10) with clear headings for each warning sign. Use bullet points to organize indicators, significance, and response strategies for easy reference and action.
```
