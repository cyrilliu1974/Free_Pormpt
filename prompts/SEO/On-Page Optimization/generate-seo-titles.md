# SEO Title Generator for Product Pages

## 簡介

The SEO Title Generator for Product Pages is a free AI prompt that transforms product descriptions into search-optimized, click-worthy titles for e-commerce and content marketers. This SEO title prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product type, keyword competitiveness, and audience intent to create 5–7 title variations that front-load primary keywords, stay under the 60-character limit, and weave in emotional triggers and power words. It then ranks the top three by predicted balance of search performance and human appeal, complete with rationale and A/B testing guidance on which title elements to experiment with - word order, emotional hooks, number inclusion - and which metrics to track (CTR, bounce rate, conversions). The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for product managers, SEO specialists, and digital marketers who need to satisfy both search algorithms and buyer psychology. Reach for this prompt whenever you launch a new product page, refresh underperforming listings, or need data-backed title alternatives fast. ● Outputs 5–7 title variations under 60 characters with front-loaded primary keywords ● Ranks the top 3 titles by SEO strength and human appeal, with clear rationale ● Maps optimal keyword placement and integrates emotional triggers relevant to your industry ● Includes A/B testing guidance specifying which title elements to test and which metrics to monitor ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an SEO title specialist who balances keyword optimization with human appeal.

## Task

Transform the product description into SEO-optimized titles that rank well in search results and compel users to click.

## Context

Effective SEO titles front-load primary keywords, stay under 60 characters, include emotional triggers, and clearly communicate unique value. Your titles must satisfy both search algorithms and human intent.

Use this information:

**Product & Keywords**
{{product-and-keywords}}

**Target Audience & Differentiator**
{{audience-and-value}}

## Process

1. **Analyze** the product type, keyword competitiveness, and audience intent
2. **Map** optimal keyword placement (front-load primary keywords)
3. **Integrate** emotional triggers and power words relevant to the industry
4. **Generate** 5–7 title variations optimized for search and click-through
5. **Rank** the top 3 by predicted performance

## Output

Provide:

### Title Variations (5–7)
Each under 60 characters, with primary keyword front-loaded.

### Top 3 Recommended Titles
Ranked by balance of SEO strength and human appeal, with brief rationale for each.

### A/B Testing Guidance
Which elements to test (word order, emotional hooks, number inclusion) and success metrics to track (CTR, bounce rate, conversions).
```
