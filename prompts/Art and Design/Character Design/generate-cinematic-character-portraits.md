# Cinematic Character Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Character Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces atmospheric medium close-up film stills featuring two characters in introspective, layered scenes with professional cinematography techniques. This cinematic character portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates medium close-up compositions with a foreground man and mid-ground woman separated by environmental elements like windows, using customizable variables for setting (interior location), accent color (clothing highlights), and mood (lighting tone). The prompt specifies eye-level framing, diffused natural daylight, muted cool color palettes with strategic color pops, layered depth of field, and weathered character styling that evokes introspective narrative moments. Directors, concept artists, storyboard designers, and visual storytellers use it to generate film-quality character interaction scenes without location shoots or casting. ● Produces medium close-up compositions with foreground and mid-ground character layering separated by environmental elements like windows. ● Customizable setting interiors, accent color for wardrobe highlights, and mood-based diffused lighting (natural daylight tones). ● Specifies authentic cinematography: eye-level angle, depth of field variation, muted cool palettes, atmospheric exterior blur. ● Generates introspective character interactions with weathered styling, pensive gazes, and film-still realism for storyboards and concept development. ## Prompt

```
A medium close-up cinematic still. Inside {{setting}}, a man and a woman are seated. The man is in the foreground, wearing dark, heavy layered clothing. He has a weathered look and gazes pensively to the side. The woman is in the mid-ground, looking towards the man. She wears distinctive clothing with {{accent-color}} tones that catch the light. The interior is dark and utilitarian. Environmental elements are visible on the window between them, blurring the grey landscape outside. The lighting is diffused and {{mood}}.

Eye-level angle. Soft, diffused natural daylight from outside. Muted tones, cool color palette with pops of {{accent-color}}. Atmospheric exterior visible through window. Introspective tone. Sharp focus on the man in foreground, slightly softer focus on the woman through the window.
```
