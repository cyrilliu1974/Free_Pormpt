# Content SEO Implementation Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Content SEO Implementation Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a complete, actionable SEO roadmap tailored to a specific website, audience, and competitive landscape. This content SEO prompt for ChatGPT accepts your website URL, target audience, primary keywords, industry niche, and competitors, then delivers a numbered implementation plan with lettered sub-points covering keyword research, on-page optimization, content quality standards, internal linking architecture, and technical SEO requirements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured document that SEO specialists, content strategists, and digital marketers can execute immediately. Each recommendation is contextualized to your industry and competitive positioning, not generic advice. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive SEO strategy document that bridges keyword targeting, technical foundations, and content best practices in one output. ● Generates a numbered action plan with sub-steps for every major SEO pillar: keyword research, on-page elements, content quality, internal linking, and technical health. ● Tailors recommendations to your website URL, target audience, primary keywords, industry niche, and direct competitors for contextually relevant advice. ● Structures output for immediate execution by SEO teams, content writers, and developers with clear, actionable sub-tasks. ● Works across all major text-generation models - ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok - so you can run it in your preferred environment. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist focused on improving search engine rankings and driving organic traffic through comprehensive, actionable content optimization strategies.

## Task
Develop a detailed SEO implementation plan that covers keyword research, on-page optimization, content quality standards, internal linking architecture, and technical SEO requirements. Structure your writing to maximize readability, relevance, and keyword integration throughout.

## Context
**Website/Blog:** {{website-url}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Primary Keywords:** {{primary-keywords}}
**Industry/Niche:** {{industry-niche}}
**Main Competitors:** {{competitors}}

## Output
Provide your recommendations as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) detailing specific steps under each main recommendation. Each recommendation should be immediately actionable and tailored to the website, audience, keywords, industry, and competitive landscape provided.
```
