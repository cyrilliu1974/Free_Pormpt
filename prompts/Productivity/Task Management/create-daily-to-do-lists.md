# Daily To-Do List Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Daily To-Do List Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates structured, prioritized task lists tailored to solopreneur workflows and business goals. This daily to-do list prompt for ChatGPT builds markdown-formatted task lists with priority levels (High, Medium, Low), time estimates, and checkboxes for tracking completion. It applies SMART criteria to every task and sequences work based on your energy patterns, work rhythm, and stated business objectives. You provide context about your business focus and goals, and the prompt returns a ready-to-use list that balances urgent actions with progress toward longer-term milestones. Solopreneurs use it to transform vague intentions into concrete, time-boxed actions that fit within realistic daily capacity. Reach for this prompt when you need to plan a focused work day, avoid decision fatigue, or ensure your daily tasks ladder up to your business strategy. ● Applies SMART criteria to ensure every task is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound ● Groups tasks by priority tier and surfaces high-impact work first ● Includes time estimates so you can fit tasks into available work hours ● Outputs markdown with checkboxes for simple tracking and completion logs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in solopreneur workflows and time management.

## Task
Create a personalized daily to-do list that prioritizes tasks, estimates durations, and supports completion tracking. Design the list to balance immediate actions with progress toward longer-term business objectives.

## Context
{{solopreneur-context}}

Ensure all tasks follow SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound). Account for the solopreneur's work rhythm, energy patterns, and common obstacles when sequencing tasks.

## Output
Format as a structured markdown to-do list with:
- [ ] Checkboxes for each task
- **Priority level** (High / Medium / Low) clearly marked
- Estimated duration in minutes or hours
- Tasks grouped by priority, with High-priority items first
- Brief rationale connecting tasks to stated business goals where helpful

Example structure:
### High Priority
- [ ] **[Task name]** – 45 min – [Brief context]

### Medium Priority
- [ ] **[Task name]** – 30 min – [Brief context]

### Low Priority
- [ ] **[Task name]** – 20 min – [Brief context]
```
