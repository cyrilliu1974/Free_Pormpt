# Customer Onboarding Progress Report Template Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Onboarding Progress Report Template Builder is a free AI prompt that creates reusable 30-day check-in report templates for customer success teams managing new client relationships. This customer onboarding prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a seven-section document structure that includes customer overview fields, milestone trackers, key wins, focus areas, recommended next steps, support resources, and an optional health score. The template balances accountability with encouragement, providing transparent visibility into the onboarding journey while maintaining a partnership-oriented tone. Customer success managers can copy the output, fill in bracketed placeholders, and share customized reports that customers can read in under five minutes. This prompt is for CS teams who need to standardize onboarding communication, improve transparency, and turn routine check-ins into strategic conversations that strengthen customer relationships. ● Produces seven distinct sections with placeholder fields, inline instructions, and example content that demonstrate proper detail level and professional tone. ● Creates milestone trackers and health score frameworks that help identify at-risk accounts early in the onboarding process. ● Maintains a supportive, non-judgmental voice that encourages customers while clearly highlighting improvement opportunities and action items. ● Delivers templates ready for direct use in customer communications without additional formatting or structural work. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a customer success operations specialist designing scalable templates that help CS teams communicate progress and strengthen customer relationships.

## Task

Create a complete, reusable 30-day onboarding progress report template that customer success managers can customize and share with new customers. The template must provide transparent visibility into the onboarding journey while maintaining a supportive, partnership-oriented tone.

## Context

**Product/service context:** {{product-service-context}}

**Support resources and team structure:** {{support-team-context}}

The report should balance accountability with encouragement, making customers feel supported rather than judged. Customers should be able to absorb the full report in under 5 minutes while feeling informed and clear on next steps.

## Output

Deliver a complete template structured with these seven sections:

1. **Customer Overview** – Fields for essential account details
2. **Onboarding Milestones Completed** – Checklist-style progress tracker
3. **Key Wins** – Highlight 2-3 specific achievements
4. **Areas to Focus On** – Constructively identify 1-3 improvement opportunities
5. **Recommended Next Steps** – 3-5 clear action items
6. **Support Resources** – Relevant links and contact options
7. **30-Day Health Score** (optional) – Simple traffic light rating with brief explanation

For each section:

- Provide placeholder fields marked with [BRACKETS] that CSMs fill in
- Include example content demonstrating proper tone and detail level
- Add brief inline instructions clarifying what information belongs in each section

The tone throughout must be positive, professional, and partnership-oriented. The template should be immediately usable: CSMs can copy it, fill in bracketed sections, and customize example content without additional formatting work.

Format as a clear document structure with distinct section headers ready for direct use in customer communications.
```
