# Brand Tagline Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Tagline Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, market-ready taglines tailored to your brand's unique positioning and audience. This brand tagline prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand brief to produce 10 or more tagline options, each 7-10 words maximum, designed for versatility across digital ads, social media, print, and product packaging. The prompt works by mapping your brand's core differentiators, target audience motivations, and competitive landscape to generate taglines that balance memorability with strategic intent. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering each tagline with a strategic rationale that explains its emotional appeal and market positioning. Marketing teams use it to refresh brand messaging, launch new products, or test multiple creative directions before committing to a campaign. This prompt is built for brand strategists, copywriters, and founders who need professional-grade taglines without the agency timeline or cost. ● Produces 10+ tagline variations per run, each with strategic reasoning tied to brand positioning and audience psychology ● Analyzes brand briefs to identify differentiators, emotional triggers, and competitive gaps before generating options ● Ensures taglines stay concise (7-10 words) and adaptable across all marketing channels and future brand evolution ● Formats output for immediate testing or deployment, eliminating the need for additional refinement rounds ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist and copywriter specializing in tagline development.

## Task
Generate at least 10 compelling, memorable taglines for {{business-name}} that encapsulate the brand's identity, unique value proposition, and emotional resonance. Each tagline must be concise (7-10 words maximum), versatile across marketing channels (digital ads, social media, print, packaging), and aligned with long-term brand vision.

## Context
Use the following brand intelligence to inform your creative direction:

{{brand-brief}}

Analyze this information to:
- Identify the brand's core differentiators and market positioning
- Understand what emotional or rational appeals resonate with the target audience
- Recognize competitive landscape patterns to ensure distinction
- Extract keywords and phrases that capture brand essence

## Process
1. Define the brand's identity, ethos, and what makes it stand out in its industry
2. Map target audience demographics, psychographics, and motivational triggers
3. Review competitor taglines (if provided) to identify gaps and opportunities for differentiation
4. Brainstorm aligned keywords and phrases
5. Draft initial options prioritizing brevity, memorability, and impact
6. Refine each tagline for clarity, emotional resonance, and brand alignment
7. Validate versatility by envisioning each tagline across diverse marketing contexts
8. Ensure scalability—taglines should remain relevant as the brand evolves

## Output
Present 10+ tagline options in a numbered list. For each tagline, include:
- The tagline itself (bolded)
- One-sentence rationale explaining its strategic intent and emotional appeal

Format taglines ready for immediate testing or deployment in marketing materials.
```
