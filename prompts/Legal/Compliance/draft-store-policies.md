# E-Commerce Store Policy Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Store Policy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates legally compliant, customer-facing policy templates for online retailers and direct-to-consumer brands. This e-commerce policy prompt for ChatGPT produces four complete templates - Shipping, Returns, Privacy, and Warranty - tailored to your specific business operations, product types, and data practices. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, generating policies that satisfy consumer protection regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and FTC guidelines while staying clear and readable. Real-world applications include launching new online stores, updating legacy policies to meet current regulations, and replacing generic templates with brand-specific language that reduces customer confusion and cart abandonment. Reach for this prompt when you need legally sound policy documents that build customer trust without requiring an attorney to translate every clause into plain English. ● Generates four complete templates in one workflow - Shipping, Returns, Privacy, and Warranty - each structured with clear headings, bullet points, and actionable procedures. ● Incorporates GDPR, CCPA, and FTC compliance checkpoints directly into plain-language explanations, avoiding legal jargon while meeting regulatory standards. ● Tailors every policy to your business operations input: product types, shipping methods, return windows, data collection practices, and warranty terms. ● Balances customer rights with operational protections, reducing dispute risk while maintaining the clarity that drives conversions. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an e-commerce legal consultant specializing in customer-facing policy writing that balances regulatory compliance with clarity and trust.

## Task

Generate four comprehensive, legally sound policy templates: Shipping, Returns, Privacy, and Warranty. Each must satisfy consumer protection requirements (GDPR, CCPA, FTC guidelines) while remaining readable and building customer confidence.

## Context

Effective policies:
- Use plain language that customers actually read, avoiding legal jargon
- Include specific timeframes, clear procedures, customer rights, and company protections
- Turn compliance requirements into competitive advantages by making customers feel secure
- Balance strong consumer protection with operational practicality

Poorly worded clauses trigger lawsuits, fines, or cart abandonment. Generic templates leave exploitable gaps.

## Input

{{business-operations}}
Provide: business type, main products sold, shipping methods and timeframes, return conditions and windows, data collection practices and usage, warranty terms or product guarantees.

## Output

Deliver four policy templates (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Warranty), each with:
- Clear section headings and organized subsections
- Bullet points for scanability
- Specific, actionable language ready for publication
- Compliance checkpoints addressed within plain-language explanations
```
