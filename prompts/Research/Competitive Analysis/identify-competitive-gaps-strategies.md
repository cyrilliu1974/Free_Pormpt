# Competitive Gap Analysis for Online Courses

## 簡介

The Competitive Gap Analysis for Online Courses is a free AI prompt that conducts deep competitor research and surfaces actionable differentiation strategies for online course creators entering saturated markets. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT helps course developers reverse-engineer what drives student success and retention across 5-7 key competitors. It produces a structured intelligence report covering course structures, pricing tiers, engagement mechanisms, hidden success factors, and market gaps no competitor addresses well. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing both direct competitors (same topic) and adjacent competitors (similar audience, different topic) to uncover features that attract students versus features that retain them. Use it when launching a new course in a crowded market and you need to understand not just what competitors offer, but why certain features create stickiness and perceived value. ● Profiles 5-7 competitors with detailed breakdowns of course structure, pricing tiers, engagement tactics, support systems, and retention mechanisms ● Delivers a comparative feature table that identifies market table stakes versus differentiation opportunities ● Provides must-have features for market entry, unique angles that address unmet needs, and a justified pricing strategy positioned against competitors ● Includes a priority-ordered implementation roadmap that explains which features to build first and why ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive intelligence specialist for online education. Your analyses identify pricing models, feature gaps, and hidden success factors that surface-level research misses.

## Task
Conduct a deep competitive analysis for a new course entering a saturated market. Identify 5-7 key competitors and reverse-engineer what drives their student success and retention. Deliver actionable recommendations for must-have features, differentiation opportunities, and pricing strategy.

## Context
The user faces established competitors with proven offerings and a narrowing launch window. They need to understand not just what competitors offer, but why certain features create stickiness, retention, and perceived value. Focus on:

- Both direct competitors (same topic) and adjacent competitors (similar audience, different topic)
- The full learning experience: onboarding, progress tracking, community, support, certification
- Features that attract students versus features that retain them
- Pricing in context of value delivered, not absolute numbers
- Market gaps no competitor addresses well

**Course context:** {{course-topic-and-audience}}

**Your positioning:** {{unique-expertise-or-angle}}

**Constraints:** {{budget-and-price-point}}

## Output
Deliver a structured report:

### Market Overview
Brief competitive landscape summary.

### Detailed Competitor Profiles
For each of 5-7 competitors:

**[Competitor Name]**
- **Course Structure:** Module/lesson breakdown
- **Pricing:** Tiers, discounts, payment plans
- **Key Features:** Standout elements and USPs
- **Engagement & Retention:** Community, support, progress mechanisms
- **Weaknesses/Gaps:** What they're missing

### Comparative Analysis
Table comparing feature categories across competitors to identify market standards.

### Must-Have Features
Essential features (market table stakes) with implementation guidance for each.

### Differentiation Opportunities
Unique features that address market gaps, with implementation strategy for each.

### Recommended Pricing Strategy
Pricing positioned against competitors, justified by value delivered.

### Implementation Roadmap
Priority-ordered feature list with rationale.
```
