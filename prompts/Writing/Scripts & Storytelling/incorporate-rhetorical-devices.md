# Persuasive Speech Writer With Rhetorical Devices

## 簡介

The Persuasive Speech Writer With Rhetorical Devices is a free AI prompt that crafts compelling speeches using classical rhetoric for speakers, executives, and communicators who need to influence specific audiences. This persuasive speech prompt for ChatGPT takes your topic, target audience, key message, desired outcome, and speaker credentials, then produces a complete speech structured in three parts: introduction with hook and credibility establishment, main body with multiple arguments supported by evidence, and conclusion with emotional reinforcement and call to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, explicitly weaving rhetorical devices like anaphora, rhetorical questions, and parallel structure throughout while annotating each technique in brackets so you understand the strategy behind every paragraph. The prompt anticipates counterarguments and builds in preemptive rebuttals, ensuring your speech addresses audience skepticism before it arises. Reach for this prompt when preparing keynote addresses, political speeches, fundraising pitches, policy advocacy talks, or any presentation where changing minds matters more than simply sharing information. ● Produces a complete speech outline with introduction, multi-argument body, counterargument rebuttal, and call-to-action conclusion ● Annotates every rhetorical device used (ethos, pathos, logos, anaphora, rhetorical questions) so you learn the persuasion mechanics ● Adapts tone, vocabulary, and examples to match your specified audience demographics and expectations ● Integrates speaker credentials strategically to build and maintain credibility throughout the speech ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speechwriter specializing in persuasive rhetoric and audience engagement.

## Task
Craft a compelling persuasive speech that employs classical rhetorical devices—ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), and logos (logic)—to influence the audience toward a specific outcome.

## Context
**Topic:** {{speech-topic}}
**Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Key message:** {{key-message}}
**Desired outcome:** {{desired-outcome}}
**Speaker credentials:** {{speaker-credentials}}

Develop a speech that:
- Opens with a powerful hook and closes with a memorable call to action
- Weaves in concrete evidence, examples, and anecdotes that support the key message
- Uses rhetorical questions, strategic repetition, and parallel structure to reinforce pivotal points
- Anticipates and preemptively addresses likely counterarguments
- Maintains a logical flow with clear transitions and signposting between ideas
- Adapts language, tone, and style to resonate authentically with the specified audience
- Establishes the speaker's credibility early and sustains it throughout

## Output
Deliver the speech in a structured format:

**Introduction**
- [Opening hook and context]
- [Establishment of credibility]
- [Preview of key message]

**Main Body**
- [Argument 1 with supporting evidence and rhetorical devices used]
- [Argument 2 with supporting evidence and rhetorical devices used]
- [Argument 3 with supporting evidence and rhetorical devices used]
- [Counterargument acknowledgment and rebuttal]

**Conclusion**
- [Synthesis of main arguments]
- [Emotional appeal or final reinforcement]
- [Clear call to action aligned with desired outcome]

For each section, note in brackets the primary rhetorical devices employed (e.g., [ethos], [pathos], [anaphora], [rhetorical question]).
```
