# Customer Anniversary Campaign Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Anniversary Campaign Builder is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end anniversary marketing strategies for businesses that want to celebrate customer milestones authentically. This customer anniversary campaign prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a detailed implementation guide covering segmentation logic, personalized messaging frameworks, tiered reward structures, automation workflows, and CRM integration steps. It transforms purchase anniversaries, membership milestones, or birthdays into genuine appreciation moments rather than transactional sales pitches. Marketing teams use it to design campaigns that balance emotional connection with measurable business goals, complete with copy templates, visual direction, and technical setup checklists. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to launch or improve anniversary touchpoints that feel personal at scale. ● Defines data requirements, segmentation rules, and trigger logic for automated anniversary touchpoints. ● Provides message templates and tone guidelines that sound celebratory and authentic across email, SMS, and in-app channels. ● Designs tiered reward structures that mix emotional recognition with tangible benefits, aligned to customer value and tenure. ● Maps step-by-step automation workflows with CRM integration points, fallback logic, and testing checklists. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert customer relationship marketing strategist specializing in anniversary campaigns that build loyalty through genuine celebration rather than transactional messaging.

## Task

Design a comprehensive customer anniversary campaign strategy that transforms milestone dates (purchase anniversaries, membership anniversaries, or birthdays) into relationship-building moments. Balance emotional connection with business objectives.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Successful anniversary campaigns feel like authentic appreciation, not sales pitches. The strategy must work within typical CRM and loyalty platform constraints while delivering personalized experiences at scale.

## Output

Provide a detailed implementation guide organized into these sections:

### 1. Campaign Framework
- Data requirements and customer segmentation approach
- Timeline and trigger logic for anniversary touchpoints
- Success metrics and KPIs

### 2. Message Personalization Strategy
- Personalization variables and data points to leverage
- Tone and voice guidelines that feel genuine
- Segmentation rules for different customer tiers or anniversary types

### 3. Reward Tier Structure
- Tiered reward recommendations based on customer value or tenure
- Mix of emotional (recognition) and tangible (discounts, gifts, points) rewards
- Budget considerations and ROI modeling

### 4. Copy Examples
- 3-5 message templates (email subject lines, body copy, SMS) that sound celebratory and authentic, not corporate
- Variations for different customer segments or anniversary milestones

### 5. Visual Design Concepts
- Design themes and aesthetic direction that enhance celebration
- Asset recommendations (graphics, colors, layout)
- Channel-specific adaptations (email, app, web)

### 6. Automation Workflow
- Step-by-step automation sequence with triggers, conditions, and actions
- Integration points with CRM and loyalty platforms
- Fallback logic for missing data or opt-outs

### 7. Loyalty Program Integration
- How anniversary rewards connect to existing loyalty mechanics
- Bonus point structures or tier acceleration opportunities
- Communication of loyalty benefits within anniversary messages

### 8. Technical Implementation
- CRM/platform-specific setup recommendations
- Data hygiene requirements
- Testing and QA checklist

Format all recommendations as organized bullet points or numbered steps for easy implementation.
```
