# Social Media Content Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, platform-specific content strategies for social media marketers and brand managers. This social media content strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces 8-10 executable content ideas tailored to your chosen platform, target audience, and product or service. Each idea includes a clear concept, engagement mechanism (polls, challenges, user-generated content campaigns), step-by-step execution guidance, and alignment with current platform trends. The output also delivers a content calendar overview showing post diversity and thematic flow, plus cross-cutting tactics for hashtags, comment engagement, and shareability optimization. Real use cases include planning Instagram campaigns for fashion brands, building TikTok challenge series for consumer products, or designing LinkedIn thought-leadership content for B2B services. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete content strategy that balances interactive posts, educational tutorials, behind-the-scenes content, influencer collaborations, and user testimonials across a cohesive posting schedule. ● Produces 8-10 diverse content ideas spanning polls, quizzes, challenges, user-generated campaigns, live sessions, and collaborations ● Includes execution steps, scheduling considerations, and engagement tactics for each concept ● Delivers a content calendar overview to visualize posting rhythm and thematic variety ● Provides hashtag strategy, comment response frameworks, and shareability optimization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in platform-specific content strategy and audience engagement.

## Task
Generate a detailed content strategy comprising 8-10 innovative, platform-specific content ideas designed to drive engagement (likes, comments, shares) and enhance brand visibility. Each idea should be original, aligned with current platform trends, and executable within the platform's format and guidelines.

## Context
Platform: {{platform}}
Audience: {{target-audience}}
Offering: {{product-service}}

The content must resonate with the audience's interests and behaviors while leveraging trending topics, hashtags, and formats currently gaining traction on the platform.

## Approach
For each content idea, provide:
- **Concept**: A clear description of the content type and theme
- **Engagement mechanism**: How it prompts interaction (polls, user participation, challenges, etc.)
- **Execution steps**: Creation requirements, scheduling considerations, and engagement tactics
- **Trend alignment**: How it connects to current platform trends

Include a diverse mix of content formats:
- Interactive content (polls, quizzes, stories)
- User-generated content campaigns
- Behind-the-scenes posts
- Educational content and tutorials
- Challenges and contests
- Influencer or brand collaborations
- Live Q&A sessions
- Testimonials and success stories

## Output
Deliver a content strategy document structured as:
1. **Content Calendar Overview**: A suggested posting schedule showing content type diversity and thematic flow
2. **Detailed Content Ideas**: 8-10 specific ideas, each with concept, engagement mechanism, execution steps, and trend alignment
3. **Cross-Cutting Engagement Tactics**: Hashtag strategy, comment response approach, and shareability optimization

Ensure all ideas are feasible within typical resource constraints and platform-specific technical limitations.
```
