# Lead Generation Survey Question Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Survey Question Builder is a free AI prompt that creates high-quality survey questionnaires designed to identify, qualify, and engage prospective customers. This lead generation survey prompt for ChatGPT produces 8–12 strategically sequenced questions that mix closed-ended items for segmentation and scoring with open-ended questions for deeper insight into pain points and buying intent. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and each generated question includes a parenthetical explanation of its purpose - whether for qualification, lead scoring, personalization triggers, or discovery. Marketing teams use it to build surveys that respect respondent time while capturing actionable data on product fit, urgency, budget authority, and competitive context. Reach for this prompt when you need a concise, logically flowing questionnaire that turns anonymous visitors into qualified pipeline opportunities. ● Generates 8–12 questions that flow logically, each building context for the next ● Balances closed-ended questions for scoring and segmentation with open-ended questions for qualitative insight ● Labels every question by purpose - qualification, pain-point discovery, lead scoring, or personalization trigger ● Tailors the survey to your specific business offering, ideal customer profile, and unique selling proposition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher specializing in lead generation surveys.

## Task
Create a survey questionnaire that generates high-quality leads by engaging the target audience and revealing purchase intent, pain points, and qualification criteria. Structure questions so they flow logically, with each building context for the next. Mix closed-ended questions (for segmentation and scoring) with open-ended questions (for deeper insight). Keep the survey concise—respect respondent time while gathering actionable data.

## Context
**Business overview:** {{business-and-offering}}

**Target audience and ideal customer:** {{audience-and-icp}}

**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 8–12 survey questions. After each question, include a brief parenthetical explanation of its purpose (e.g., qualification, pain-point discovery, lead scoring, personalization trigger).
```
