# Automated Drip Campaign Builder for Email Marketing

## 簡介

The Automated Drip Campaign Builder for Email Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates structured email nurture sequences for marketers and growth teams looking to convert leads across awareness, consideration, and decision stages. This drip campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a full email sequence strategy, complete with subject lines, body templates, personalization hooks, CTAs, and behavioral triggers that adapt based on recipient engagement. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete campaign blueprint that includes send intervals, progression logic, segmentation paths for openers vs. non-responders, and A/B testing priorities for subject lines, CTAs, and timing. Real use cases include SaaS trial nurturing, e-commerce cart recovery sequences, webinar follow-up campaigns, and B2B lead qualification funnels. Reach for this prompt when you need a ready-made drip campaign framework that accounts for audience behavior, conversion goals, and your unique selling proposition - without starting from scratch. ● Generates a complete email sequence with stage mapping, send timing, and progression logic tied to your offer and campaign duration ● Outputs customizable templates for each email including subject lines, body structure, personalization variables, and calls-to-action ● Defines segmentation rules that route engaged leads faster and adjust follow-ups for non-openers based on behavioral triggers ● Recommends A/B testing priorities, KPIs to track, and adjustment thresholds so you can optimize performance in real time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist building an automated drip campaign that nurtures leads through awareness, consideration, and decision stages.

## Task
Develop a complete email drip campaign plan including:

- Email sequence strategy (number of emails, intervals, progression logic)
- Template for each email: subject line, body structure, personalization hooks, and CTA
- Engagement tracking and campaign adjustment triggers
- Audience segmentation rules based on opens, clicks, and conversions
- A/B testing recommendations for subject lines, CTAs, and send times

## Context
**Target audience & offer:** {{audience-and-offer}}

**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

**Campaign duration:** {{campaign-duration}}

**Conversion goals:** {{conversion-goals}}

Ensure each email builds on the previous one, addressing evolving customer awareness and objections. Incorporate behavioral triggers so non-openers receive adjusted follow-ups and engaged leads progress faster.

## Output
Present the plan as a structured bullet list:

- Campaign overview (total emails, timeline, funnel stage map)
- Email 1: [Stage] – subject line, key message, CTA, send timing
- Email 2: [Stage] – subject line, key message, CTA, send timing
- (Continue for full sequence)
- Segmentation logic (criteria and resulting paths)
- A/B test priorities (what to test first, success metrics)
- Tracking dashboard essentials (KPIs to monitor, adjustment thresholds)
```
