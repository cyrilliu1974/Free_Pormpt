# Book Outline Generator With Chapter Summaries

## 簡介

The Book Outline Generator With Chapter Summaries is a free AI prompt that creates structured tables of contents with chapters, subchapters, and descriptive summaries for authors planning their manuscripts. This book outline prompt for ChatGPT takes your book specifications - genre, target audience, main theme, desired length, and unique selling point - and produces a hierarchical table of contents with logical progression. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, organizing major topics into main chapters and breaking them down into coherent subchapters, each with a brief summary that explains its role in the overall narrative. Writers use it to map out non-fiction guides, novels, memoirs, and instructional books before drafting. ● Creates hierarchical chapter numbering (1, 1.1, 1.2, 2, 2.1) for clear nesting and flow ● Produces descriptive titles and concise summaries for every chapter and subchapter ● Ensures logical thematic progression aligned with genre, audience, and book goals ● Outputs a markdown table ready to paste into your manuscript planning document ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert book outliner specializing in creating comprehensive, well-structured tables of contents.

## Task
Develop a complete table of contents with chapters, subchapters, and summaries for a book based on the provided specifications.

## Context
**Book specifications:**
{{book-specifications}}

Include: genre, target audience, main theme, desired length (page count or word count), and unique selling point or key differentiator.

## Process
1. Design an overarching structure that ensures logical flow and progression of key themes
2. Create main chapters that encompass major topics or sections
3. Break down each chapter into relevant subchapters with coherent idea progression
4. Write concise, descriptive titles for all chapters and subchapters
5. Compose brief summaries for each chapter highlighting main points and relevance to the overall book

## Output
Present the table of contents as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Chapter Number** (use hierarchical numbering: 1, 1.1, 1.2, 2, 2.1, etc.)
- **Chapter Title**
- **Chapter Summary**

Ensure subchapters are clearly nested under their respective main chapters.
```
