# Vintage Japanese Animal Painting Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Japanese Animal Painting Generator is a free AI prompt that creates full-body animal portraits styled after early 18th-century Japanese paintings with symmetrical composition on white paper backgrounds. This vintage Japanese painting prompt for Midjourney produces classical animal illustrations - tigers, cranes, dragons, or any subject you specify - rendered in authentic period aesthetics. The prompt uses controlled chaos, a 3:4 aspect ratio, and specific style reference parameters to ensure historically-inspired results with artistic variation. Designers use it for editorial illustration, wall art mockups, book covers, and branding projects that need traditional Japanese art direction without digital polish.
