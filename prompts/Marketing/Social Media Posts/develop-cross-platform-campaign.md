# Cross-Platform Campaign Builder for Social Media

## 簡介

The Cross-Platform Campaign Builder for Social Media is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive marketing campaigns with platform-optimized content while maintaining consistent brand identity across all channels. This cross-platform campaign prompt for ChatGPT analyzes audience demographics and algorithmic behavior for each social network, then produces tailored content strategies, specific post ideas, and a unified content calendar. Marketing teams use it to launch campaigns that span Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, and other platforms simultaneously while ensuring each piece of content follows native best practices. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your product details, target platforms, audience profile, brand guidelines, and campaign goals to output a structured campaign blueprint with platform-by-platform breakdowns, posting schedules, and performance tracking frameworks. Reach for this prompt when you need to coordinate a brand campaign across multiple social channels without losing consistency or platform relevance. ● Produces platform-specific content strategies aligned with each channel's native formats, audience preferences, and algorithmic signals ● Establishes a unified brand message and visual identity that adapts consistently across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and other platforms ● Generates a content calendar framework with posting frequency, strategic timing, and cross-platform coordination ● Defines measurable KPIs and tracking mechanisms tailored to each platform's performance metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in cross-platform social media campaigns.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive marketing campaign that delivers engaging, platform-optimized content while maintaining consistent brand identity across all channels.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}
Target Platforms: {{platforms}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Brand Guidelines: {{brand-guidelines}}
Campaign Goals: {{campaign-goals}}

## Approach
1. Analyze each platform's audience demographics, content preferences, and algorithmic behavior
2. Develop platform-specific content strategies aligned with each platform's best practices and native formats
3. Establish a unified brand message and visual identity adaptable across all channels
4. Design engaging content formats optimized for each platform (short-form video, static posts, carousels, stories, etc.)
5. Create a content calendar ensuring consistent posting frequency and strategic timing
6. Define KPIs and tracking mechanisms to measure campaign performance on each platform

## Output
Structure your response with:
- **Cross-Platform Brand Strategy**: unified messaging, visual identity, and tone
- **[Platform Name]**: audience analysis, content strategy, specific content ideas (bulleted), posting frequency, and success metrics
- Repeat platform section for each channel
- **Content Calendar Framework**: weekly posting schedule across all platforms
- **Performance Tracking**: key metrics and measurement approach for each platform
```
