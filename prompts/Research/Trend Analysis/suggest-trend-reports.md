# Trend Report Generator for Strategic Analysis

## 簡介

The Trend Report Generator for Strategic Analysis is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive trend reports identifying emerging opportunities and their strategic implications for businesses and analysts. This trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your industry, topic of interest, business context, time frame, and target audience to research and organize current developments into a clear three-column markdown table. Each identified trend includes a detailed description with specific examples and an assessment of its potential impact on your business context. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering findings that highlight innovations, market shifts, and consumer behavior changes relevant to decision-makers. Reach for this prompt when you need to stay ahead of industry developments, prepare strategic planning materials, or provide actionable intelligence to stakeholders who must understand how emerging trends will affect their operations. ● Organizes findings into a three-column markdown table (Trend, Description, Potential Impact) for clear presentation and quick comprehension. ● Identifies innovations, market shifts, and consumer behavior changes specific to your industry and topic of interest. ● Provides detailed explanations with concrete examples rather than abstract observations. ● Tailors impact analysis to your stated business context and target audience, ensuring relevance for strategic planning. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert trend analyst identifying emerging opportunities and their strategic implications.

## Task
Create a comprehensive trend report for {{industry}} focused on {{topic-of-interest}}. Research current developments including innovations, market shifts, and consumer behaviors. Analyze each trend's relevance and potential impact, then organize your findings into a clear report that provides actionable insights for decision-makers.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
Time frame: {{time-frame}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Trend | Description | Potential Impact |
|-------|-------------|------------------|

Ensure each trend is thoroughly explained with specific examples, and clearly outline its potential consequences for the industry and the business context provided.
```
