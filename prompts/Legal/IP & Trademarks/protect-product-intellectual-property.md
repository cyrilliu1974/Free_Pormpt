# Product IP Protection Strategy for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Product IP Protection Strategy for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a practical, budget-conscious intellectual property roadmap for solo business owners working with limited resources. This IP protection prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and delivers a prioritized action plan that covers patent filings, trademark registration, copyright protections, non-disclosure agreements for collaborators, and digital asset security through encryption and access controls. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a numbered list of steps ranked by impact and feasibility. Real-world use cases include protecting SaaS products, creative content, proprietary methods, brand identities, and customer data for consultants, makers, creators, and indie developers who lack dedicated legal teams. This prompt is built for solopreneurs who need to prioritize IP protections strategically when time and budget are tight, offering cost-effective alternatives and clear execution guidance at each step. ● Identifies which IP areas (patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets) matter most for your specific offering and industry ● Orders protection steps by business impact, so you act on high-value safeguards first ● Includes practical how-to guidance for filing registrations, drafting NDAs, and implementing digital security measures ● Suggests budget-friendly alternatives and DIY options for solopreneurs without in-house counsel ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert intellectual property strategist specializing in practical IP protection for resource-constrained solo businesses.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step IP protection strategy tailored to the solopreneur's specific context. The strategy must address:

- Key areas of IP protection relevant to their offering and industry
- Processes for filing patents, trademarks, or copyrights appropriate to their assets
- Implementation of NDAs with collaborators
- Methods for securing digital assets through encryption and access controls
- Prioritization based on business stage and available budget

## Context

{{business-context}}

Consider the unique challenges solopreneurs face: limited budget, time constraints, lack of legal staff, and the need to prioritize protection efforts strategically.

## Output

Present the strategy as a numbered list of actionable steps. Under each main step, include sub-bullets with:
- Detailed explanations of what to do
- Why it matters for this specific situation
- Practical guidance on execution within the stated constraints
- Cost-effective alternatives where applicable

Order steps by priority, placing highest-impact protections first.
```
