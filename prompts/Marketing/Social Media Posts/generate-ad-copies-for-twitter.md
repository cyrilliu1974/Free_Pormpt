# Twitter Ad Copy Generator Prompt for High Conversions

## 簡介

The Twitter Ad Copy Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates 5-10 optimized, click-driving Twitter ad copies for any product or service. This Twitter ad copy prompt for ChatGPT structures each tweet using dependency grammar principles to maximize clarity and engagement within the 280-character limit. You provide your product details, destination URL, target audience pain points, unique selling proposition, and brand voice, and the prompt generates a numbered list of tweet-length ads - each with an attention-grabbing hook, clear value proposition, and strong call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across text-generation platforms. Digital marketers, social media managers, and entrepreneurs use it to quickly produce multiple ad variations for A/B testing and campaign optimization. ● Produces 5-10 tweet-length ad variations in one run, ideal for split testing ● Uses dependency grammar to ensure every word contributes to clarity and persuasion ● Customizes messaging to audience interests, pain points, and your unique selling proposition ● Maintains strict 280-character compliance while including hook, benefit, and call-to-action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media copywriter specializing in high-conversion Twitter ad copy.

## Task
Generate 5-10 compelling Twitter ad copies that promote the product or service, drive clicks to the destination URL, and maximize engagement. Each tweet must be 280 characters or less.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-service}}
Destination URL: {{url}}
Target audience and their interests or pain points: {{audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Brand voice and tone: {{brand-voice}}

Structure each tweet using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and impact. Each ad should include:
- An attention-grabbing hook that resonates with the target audience
- A clear benefit or value proposition
- A strong call-to-action that encourages clicks

Tailor language, tone, and messaging to the audience's needs and the brand voice provided.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of 5-10 tweet-length ad copies. Each must be exactly 280 characters or less and optimized for engagement and clickthrough rate.
```
