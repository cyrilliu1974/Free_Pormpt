# Macro Action Ads Prompt for Image AI

## 簡介

The Macro Action Ads Prompt for Image AI is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic miniature scenes of athletes performing dynamic actions on chocolate bars for advertising and creative visuals. This macro action ads prompt for Midjourney produces cinematic food-sport hybrid imagery by positioning customizable athletes along segmented chocolate bars with flying crumbs and shavings captured in slow motion. The prompt uses low-angle hero perspectives, shallow depth of field, and dramatic macro lighting to create scroll-stopping product ads that blend sports energy with food appeal. Users specify the athlete and action, chocolate bar type, and background environment to generate unique advertisement concepts. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image generation models that support photorealistic rendering and aspect ratio control. This prompt is for creative directors, advertising agencies, food brands, and social media marketers who need high-impact product visuals that combine action sports aesthetics with food photography. ● Photorealistic miniature athletes performing tricks on chocolate bars with debris and shavings in slow motion ● Cinematic macro lighting with dramatic shadows and shallow depth of field for professional ad quality ● Customizable athlete actions, chocolate bar descriptions, and background environments ● Low-angle hero perspective with 16:9 aspect ratio optimized for social media and display ads ## Prompt
