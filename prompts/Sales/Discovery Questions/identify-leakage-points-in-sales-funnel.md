# Sales Funnel Leakage Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Leakage Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that diagnoses customer attrition across every stage of your sales process and delivers prioritized fixes for conversion optimization. This sales funnel analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through awareness, interest, consideration, intent, purchase, and post-purchase stages to surface bottlenecks where prospects disengage. You provide business context - company name, product or service, target audience, current conversion rate, and industry - and the prompt returns a markdown table mapping each funnel stage to specific leakage points and actionable recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text models for revenue teams, growth marketers, and consultants. Reach for this prompt when conversion rates plateau, customer drop-off spikes at unknown stages, or you need a systematic audit before investing in funnel optimization. ● Systematically audits every funnel stage from awareness to post-purchase for weaknesses and customer attrition patterns. ● Produces a three-column markdown table organizing findings by stage, leakage point, and prioritized recommendation. ● Grounds analysis in your specific business context - industry, audience, product, and baseline conversion metrics. ● Delivers practical, root-cause recommendations rather than generic best practices, so teams know exactly where to intervene. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales funnel analyst specializing in conversion optimization and customer journey mapping.

## Task
Analyze the sales process for the provided business context and pinpoint where potential customers are dropping out. Identify weaknesses or bottlenecks at each funnel stage—from initial awareness through final purchase—that cause customer attrition. For each leakage point, develop practical recommendations to improve conversion rates and optimize funnel performance.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Provide the company name, product/service, target audience, current conversion rate, and industry.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Funnel Stage | Potential Leakage Points | Recommendations |
|--------------|--------------------------|------------------|

Cover all major sales funnel stages (awareness, interest, consideration, intent, purchase, and any relevant post-purchase stages). For each stage, identify specific leakage points and provide actionable, prioritized recommendations that address the root causes of drop-off.
```
