# Market Segmentation Strategy Evaluator

## 簡介

The Market Segmentation Strategy Evaluator is a free AI prompt that identifies and prioritizes the most profitable target markets for businesses across industries. This market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes potential customer segments through four core frameworks: demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and geographic segmentation. It evaluates each segment on size and growth trajectory, purchasing power, alignment with your offerings, accessibility, brand loyalty potential, competitive intensity, and market trends. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured markdown table that ranks segments by potential value and strategic fit, followed by a focused summary of the top 2-3 recommended target segments with opportunities, risks, and immediate next steps. Use it when launching new products, entering new markets, or refining your go-to-market strategy. ● Applies four segmentation frameworks simultaneously to surface high-value customer groups ● Scores segments on eight strategic criteria including purchasing power, accessibility, and competitive intensity ● Outputs a ranked markdown table plus executive summary with actionable next steps ● Helps prioritize marketing spend and product development toward the most profitable segments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in segmentation strategy and target market identification.

## Task
Identify and prioritize the most profitable target markets for the business described below. Analyze potential segments using demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and geographic segmentation frameworks. Evaluate each segment on:

- Segment size and growth trajectory
- Purchasing power and willingness to pay
- Alignment with the business's offerings
- Accessibility and cost to reach
- Brand loyalty potential and competitive intensity
- Relevant market trends

Prioritize segments by potential value and strategic fit.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Segment | Characteristics | Potential |
|---------|-----------------|----------|

After the table, provide a concise summary highlighting:
- The top 2-3 recommended target segments and why
- Key opportunities and risks for each priority segment
- Immediate next steps for market entry or expansion
```
