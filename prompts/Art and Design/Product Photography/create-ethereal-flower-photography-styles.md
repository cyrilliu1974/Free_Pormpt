The Ethereal Flower Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates dreamy, close-up floral images with double-exposure techniques and soft pastel color palettes for photographers, designers, and botanical artists. This flower photography prompt for Midjourney creates images with translucent petals, subtle light veins, dewdrops, and glowing edges against soft gradient backgrounds. The prompt produces a 100mm macro look with shallow depth of field and creamy bokeh, applying fantasy-inspired styling and soft diffused lighting. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you specify any flower type and aspect ratio to match your project needs. Use it for botanical art prints, product packaging, wedding invitations, or social media visuals that require a delicate, otherworldly aesthetic.
