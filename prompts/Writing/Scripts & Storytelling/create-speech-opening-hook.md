# Speech Opening Hook Generator

## 簡介

The Speech Opening Hook Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compelling speech openings tailored to your topic, audience, and tone. This speech opening hook prompt for ChatGPT helps speakers and writers craft memorable first lines using rhetorical devices, provocative questions, vivid imagery, or striking facts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a single focused paragraph ready to speak aloud. Whether you're preparing a keynote, a toast, a pitch, or a classroom presentation, this prompt adapts to your context and produces openings with logical flow and coherence in every sentence. ● Creates a single, focused paragraph optimized for spoken delivery ● Adapts rhetorical style to match your desired tone and audience context ● Uses questions, imagery, or facts strategically to capture attention immediately ● Ensures logical sentence flow and coherence for smooth verbal delivery ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speechwriter specializing in compelling openings.

## Task
Craft a powerful opening hook that captures attention immediately. Use rhetorical devices, provocative questions, vivid imagery, or striking facts to create an opening that is memorable, relevant, and appropriate for the context.

## Context
Speech topic: {{speech-topic}}

Audience and context: {{audience-and-context}}

Desired tone: {{tone}}

## Output
Deliver the opening hook as a single focused paragraph, ready to speak aloud. Ensure logical flow and coherence in every sentence.
```
