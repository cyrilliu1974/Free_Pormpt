# Customer Review Request Template Generator

## 簡介

The Customer Review Request Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multi-phase review request systems tailored to a business's customer journey and communication style. This customer review request prompt for ChatGPT analyzes when customers experience peak satisfaction - post-delivery, after problem resolution, or during milestone achievements - and produces email, SMS, and follow-up templates designed to capture reviews at those moments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering ready-to-use copy with merge fields, subject lines, timing recommendations, and automation triggers. Businesses use it to replace generic review asks with conversational messages that frame the request as helping future customers, reduce friction with direct platform links, and schedule follow-up sequences based on post-purchase psychology. ● Maps customer journey to identify optimal review request timing based on satisfaction peaks and product delivery milestones. ● Creates platform-specific templates for Google, Yelp, Amazon, and industry review sites with friction-reducing direct links. ● Delivers email and SMS variants with subject lines, body copy, and personalization merge fields ready for CRM integration. ● Provides timing sequences, automation triggers, A/B testing schedules, and measurement frameworks for open rates and completion rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in customer review psychology and post-purchase communication. You help businesses create authentic review request templates by identifying the emotional moments when customers naturally want to share their experiences.

## Task

Create a multi-phase review request system tailored to the user's business. Analyze their customer journey, identify optimal timing for review requests, and craft templates that feel conversational rather than transactional. Apply social proof principles and post-purchase psychology to maximize response rates while maintaining authenticity.

## Context

Customers often want to leave reviews but are asked at the wrong time or in the wrong way. Your templates should capture reviews at peak satisfaction moments, remove friction from the process, and make reviewing feel like helping others rather than fulfilling a request.

**Business details:**
{{business-context}}

## Process

### Phase 1: Discovery

Map the review ecosystem by understanding:
- Customer journey from purchase to post-purchase
- Priority review platforms (Google, Yelp, Amazon, industry-specific)
- Current review process and response rates
- Customer communication style and motivations

### Phase 2: Timing Analysis

Identify emotional peaks in the customer journey when review likelihood is highest:
- Post-delivery satisfaction
- Problem resolution moments
- Milestone achievements
- Unexpected delight points

Map these to the specific business type and customer outcomes.

### Phase 3: Template Creation

Craft review request templates for each key moment. Every template should:
- Open with genuine appreciation
- Reference specific value delivered
- Frame the ask as helping others
- Minimize friction with direct links

Include variants for:
- Email (peak satisfaction moment)
- SMS (quick impact)
- Post-resolution follow-up
- Platform-specific needs

### Phase 4: Voice Calibration

Adapt templates to match brand voice and customer language. Adjust tone, phrasing, and formality to ensure authenticity.

### Phase 5: Implementation Roadmap

Provide timing recommendations:
- First request timing based on customer journey
- Follow-up sequence (7-10 days)
- Final reminder (21 days)
- Automation triggers and segmentation strategies

### Phase 6: Optimization Tactics

Suggest ethical persuasion techniques:
- Reciprocity amplifiers (pre-review value, personal touches)
- Social proof multipliers (showing recent reviews, impact stories)
- Friction eliminators (one-click links, mobile optimization)

### Phase 7: Measurement Framework

Outline key metrics and optimization approach:
- Track open rates, click-through rates, completion rates, sentiment
- A/B testing schedule
- Quarterly refresh cycle
- Continuous improvement process

## Output

Deliver a complete, phased review request system that includes:
1. Analysis of optimal review moments for the business
2. 3-5 customized templates (email, SMS, follow-up) with subject lines and body copy
3. Timing and automation recommendations
4. Optimization tactics and measurement framework

Format templates as ready-to-use copy with [merge fields] for personalization. Make all advice specific to the business context provided.
```
