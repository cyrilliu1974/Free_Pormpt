# Etsy Product Keyword Research Tool

## 簡介

The Etsy Product Keyword Research Tool is a free AI prompt that identifies high-performing keywords to maximize visibility and sales for Etsy sellers. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product's materials, target audience, uses, and differentiators to generate both short-tail keywords (broader terms like "handmade jewelry" with higher search volume) and long-tail keywords (specific buyer-intent phrases like "handmade silver leaf necklace" with lower competition). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying SEO methodologies comparable to Google Keyword Planner, Etsy Rank, and Marmalead to assess search volume, competition level, and relevance. The output includes structured keyword lists plus integration recommendations for naturally incorporating terms into your product title, tags, and description to drive organic traffic from buyers ready to purchase. Etsy sellers preparing new listings or refreshing existing products will find this tool essential for competitive market positioning. ● Delivers both broad-reach short-tail keywords and conversion-focused long-tail phrases based on product attributes ● Evaluates search volume and competition metrics to prioritize keywords with the best visibility-to-difficulty ratio ● Provides practical guidance on where and how to place keywords in titles, tags, and descriptions for natural readability ● Adapts to any Etsy product category by analyzing materials, use cases, occasions, and unique selling points ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in Etsy e-commerce optimization.

## Task
Perform keyword research for an Etsy product listing. Identify both short-tail and long-tail keywords with high search volume and low to moderate competition to maximize visibility, drive organic traffic, and increase sales.

## Context
The product is: {{product-name}}

Core characteristics: {{product-details}}

Analyze the product's uses, materials, target audience, purchase occasions, and unique differentiators. Use keyword research methodologies comparable to Google Keyword Planner, Etsy Rank, or Marmalead to assess search volume, competition level, and relevance.

## Process
1. Identify the product's core attributes and what makes it distinct from competitors
2. Gather data on keyword metrics (search volume, competition, relevance)
3. Compile short-tail keywords—broader descriptive terms (e.g., "handmade jewelry", "natural skincare") with higher search volume and competition
4. Develop long-tail keywords—specific phrases used by buyers close to purchase (e.g., "handmade silver leaf necklace", "organic aloe vera face cream") with lower competition and higher conversion potential
5. Prioritize keywords that balance search volume with manageable competition
6. Provide integration guidance for title, tags, and product description ensuring natural, buyer-appealing keyword usage

## Output
Deliver a structured list organized into two sections:
- **Short-tail keywords**: Broader terms with high search volume
- **Long-tail keywords**: Specific phrases with lower competition and higher conversion rates

Include brief integration recommendations for optimizing the Etsy listing's title, tags, and description.
```
