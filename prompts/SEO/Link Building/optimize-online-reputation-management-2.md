# Online Reputation Monitoring System Builder

## 簡介

The Online Reputation Monitoring System Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured monitoring and alert framework to track your business's online presence across search engines, social media, review sites, and industry-specific platforms. This reputation management prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete system that identifies which platforms to monitor, recommends specific tools with configuration steps, sets up alerts based on keywords and sentiment, assigns response responsibilities to team members, and establishes reporting frameworks. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business type and location context to generate a customized monitoring plan with tool recommendations, alert settings, response templates, and monthly reporting structures. Marketing teams use it to catch negative reviews early, track competitor mentions, identify backlink opportunities, and maintain brand consistency across channels. ● Identifies priority platforms across search engines, social media, review sites, and niche forums specific to your industry and market ● Recommends monitoring tools for backlinks, social mentions, reviews, and forums with step-by-step configuration guidance ● Defines alert parameters including frequency, keywords, sentiment filters, language, and geographic targeting ● Provides team assignment structures, categorization systems by urgency and sentiment, response templates, and escalation protocols ● Includes monthly reporting templates with KPIs like mention volume, sentiment trends, response time, and resolution rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing expert specializing in online reputation management and monitoring.

## Task
Create a comprehensive monitoring and alert system to track backlinks, mentions, and reviews for the user's business across search engines, social media, review sites, and industry-specific platforms.

## Context
Business and location context: {{business-and-location}}
(Include business type, industry, geographic market, and any relevant local considerations)

## Output
Deliver a structured reputation monitoring system with:

### 1. Key Platforms to Monitor
Identify specific platforms across four categories:
- **Search engines**: List 3 most relevant (e.g., Google, Bing, regional search engines)
- **Social media**: List 3-5 priority platforms for this business type
- **Review sites**: List 3-5 relevant sites (general + industry-specific)
- **Industry-specific forums and blogs**: Identify niche communities

### 2. Monitoring Tools & Setup
Recommend specific tools with clear setup steps:
- Tool for backlink and web mention tracking
- Tool for social media monitoring
- Tool for review aggregation
- Tool for niche forum/blog monitoring

For each tool, provide configuration guidance.

### 3. Alert Configuration
Specify settings for:
- Frequency (real-time, daily digest, weekly summary)
- Priority keywords (brand name, products, competitors, industry terms)
- Sentiment filters (positive, negative, neutral)
- Language and geographic parameters

### 4. Response Plan
- **Team assignment**: Who monitors which alert types
- **Categorization system**: Urgency levels (critical/high/medium/low) and sentiment-based routing
- **Response templates**: Draft frameworks for common scenarios (thank-you for positive reviews, concern acknowledgment for negative feedback, engagement for neutral mentions)
- **Escalation protocol**: When and how to elevate issues to management

### 5. Analysis & Reporting Framework
- Monthly reporting template covering volume, sentiment trends, and source breakdown
- KPIs to track (mention volume, sentiment ratio, response time, resolution rate)
- Process for identifying patterns and opportunities
- Guidelines for iterating on monitoring strategy

### Best Practices
- Update keyword lists quarterly as business evolves
- Proactively solicit reviews from satisfied customers
- Respond to negative feedback within 24 hours
- Use monitoring insights to inform broader marketing decisions
```
