The Bi-Fold Brochure Design Prompt for AI Image Tools is a free AI prompt that produces print-ready bi-fold A4 brochure mockups tailored to any business topic and visual style. This bi-fold brochure prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates a flat-design layout that shows both the cover and inside pages in a single 1:1 aspect-ratio image. The cover highlights your main offer, while the inside panels display services, benefits, and contact information with professional lighting and a cohesive brand color palette. Marketing teams, freelance designers, and small-business owners use it to visualize brochure concepts before committing to print, adapt templates for different industries, or generate client mockups in minutes.
