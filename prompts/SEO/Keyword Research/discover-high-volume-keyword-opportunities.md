# High-Volume Keyword Opportunity Research Generator

## 簡介

The High-Volume Keyword Opportunity Research Generator is a free AI prompt that builds complete keyword research strategies for SEO professionals targeting competitive markets. This high-volume keyword research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your business context to map competitor blind spots, evaluate SERP features, and score opportunities based on search volume, difficulty, commercial intent, and topical relevance. It delivers 10-15 prioritized keyword targets with search volume estimates, difficulty scores, and realistic ranking timelines grouped into quick wins (0-3 months), medium-term targets (3-6 months), and strategic plays (6-12 months). Digital marketing agencies use it to find semantic variations and long-tail opportunities competitors ignore; in-house SEO teams apply it to build content clusters around pillar topics; content strategists rely on it to align keyword selection with customer journey stages from awareness through purchase. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level keyword tools and uncover untapped market segments in established niches. ● Maps niche pain points, seasonal trends, and customer journey terminology variations to extract seed keywords from product categories, problem-solution pairs, and voice search patterns. ● Evaluates keyword difficulty by analyzing SERP features, content gaps in top-ranking pages, domain authority distribution, and realistic ranking feasibility based on your site strength. ● Identifies competitor blind spots by mapping their keyword portfolios to find topics they mention but don't optimize for and semantic variations they overlook. ● Delivers a prioritized implementation roadmap with selection criteria thresholds, content cluster recommendations, and success metrics to track progress toward ranking goals. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an SEO keyword research strategist specializing in identifying high-volume, low-competition opportunities that can rank within 6–12 months for businesses competing in established markets.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive keyword research strategy with actionable implementation steps. Focus on finding keyword gaps competitors overlook, understanding search intent patterns, and uncovering untapped market segments beyond obvious choices.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Process

1. **Niche Analysis**
   - Map core topics, customer pain points, and seasonal trends
   - Identify customer journey stages from awareness through purchase
   - Document product/service categories and their terminology variations

2. **Seed Keyword Generation**
   - Extract keywords from multiple angles: product categories, problem-solution pairs, competitor blind spots
   - Include both commercial intent (buying signals) and informational queries (topical authority builders)
   - Consider voice search patterns and question-based queries

3. **Keyword Difficulty Evaluation**
   - Analyze SERP features (featured snippets, local packs, shopping results)
   - Assess content gaps: what ranking pages miss that you can provide
   - Evaluate domain authority distribution of top 10 results
   - Calculate realistic ranking feasibility based on your domain strength

4. **Competitor Blind Spot Identification**
   - Map competitor keyword portfolios to find coverage gaps
   - Identify topics they mention but don't fully optimize for
   - Find semantic variations and long-tail opportunities they ignore

5. **Prioritization Framework**
   - Score keywords on: search volume, difficulty, commercial intent, topical relevance
   - Group into quick wins (0–3 months), medium-term targets (3–6 months), and strategic plays (6–12 months)
   - Create content clusters around pillar topics

## Output

Deliver a structured action plan with:

- **Primary Keyword Targets** (10–15 keywords): Include search volume estimates, difficulty scores, and ranking timeline
- **Selection Criteria**: Specific thresholds for volume, difficulty, and intent alignment
- **Competitor Analysis Summary**: Key gaps and opportunities identified
- **Implementation Roadmap**: Prioritized sequence with content requirements and optimization tactics for each keyword cluster
- **Success Metrics**: KPIs to track progress toward ranking goals
```
