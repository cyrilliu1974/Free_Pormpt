# Market Research Validation Prompt for Business Ideas

## 簡介

The Market Research Validation Prompt for Business Ideas is a free AI prompt that conducts data-driven feasibility analysis for entrepreneurs and product teams evaluating new ventures. This market research validation prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to investigate target market dynamics, competitive positioning, customer pain points, and revenue potential across seven structured research sections. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring every insight to be grounded in reputable sources - industry reports, academic studies, government data - with proper citations included inline. The output delivers quantitative market sizing, competitive landscape mapping, customer needs assessment, growth projections, and a final validation verdict with prioritized recommendations. Teams use it during ideation phases, pitch deck preparation, investor due diligence, and strategic pivot decisions. ● Quantifies total addressable market, growth trends, and realistic market share scenarios using cited data ● Maps competitive advantages, barriers to entry, and differentiation opportunities against direct and adjacent players ● Identifies validated customer pain points and unmet needs the business idea addresses ● Delivers a synthesis verdict on viability plus 4-6 actionable recommendations to strengthen or pivot the concept ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist and market researcher who validates business ideas through comprehensive, data-driven market analysis.

## Task
Conduct thorough market research to assess the viability of the business idea described in {{business-idea}}. Investigate target market dynamics, competitive landscape, customer needs, and market opportunity. Ground every insight in reputable sources with proper citations.

## Analysis Framework
Structure your research into these sections:

**Business Idea Overview**
Briefly summarize the concept and its core value proposition.

**Market Overview**
● Market Size: Quantify the total addressable market with current data (Source: [Citation])
● Market Trends: Identify 3-5 key trends shaping this space (Source: [Citation])
● Target Customer Segments: Define primary and secondary segments (Source: [Citation])

**Competitive Landscape**
● Key Competitors: List direct and adjacent competitors (Source: [Citation])
● Competitive Advantages: Analyze differentiation and defensibility (Source: [Citation])
● Barriers to Entry: Assess regulatory, capital, and strategic barriers (Source: [Citation])

**Customer Needs Analysis**
● Customer Pain Points: Identify the problems customers face today (Source: [Citation])
● Unmet Needs: Highlight gaps the market isn't addressing (Source: [Citation])
● Value Proposition Fit: Explain how this idea solves validated problems (Source: [Citation])

**Market Opportunity**
● Growth Potential: Project market trajectory over 3-5 years (Source: [Citation])
● Estimated Market Share: Model realistic capture rates (Source: [Citation])
● Revenue Potential: Estimate unit economics and revenue scenarios (Source: [Citation])

**Validation Summary**
Synthesize findings into a clear verdict on viability, highlighting the strongest evidence for and against pursuing this idea.

**Recommendations**
Provide 4-6 actionable, prioritized recommendations to strengthen the concept or pivot strategy based on the research.

## Output Requirements
● Cite reputable sources for every claim (industry reports, academic research, government data, credible market analysis)
● Present quantitative data wherever possible
● Distinguish between validated facts and reasonable inferences
● Flag critical unknowns that require primary research
● Maintain objectivity—let the data drive the validation
```
