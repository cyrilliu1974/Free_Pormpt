# Social Media Brand Strategy Builder for Business

## 簡介

The Social Media Brand Strategy Builder for Business is a free AI prompt that creates a step-by-step marketing roadmap to establish a strong, recognizable brand presence on any social platform. This brand strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed strategic document covering five core areas: brand foundation (value proposition, audience mapping, SMART objectives), content strategy (editorial calendar, creation guidelines, engagement tactics), platform optimization (feature utilization, format recommendations), growth and amplification (paid promotion, influencer partnerships, organic reach), and performance management (KPIs, analytics reviews, crisis protocols). It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific platform, brand context, and budget constraints. Teams use it to align messaging across departments, agencies use it to pitch clients, and solopreneurs use it to structure their first formal social presence. Reach for this prompt when you need a practical, actionable marketing plan rather than generic advice - it outputs timelines, specific tactics, and measurable success criteria. ● Defines brand value proposition, audience demographics, pain points, and content consumption patterns before any content is created ● Outputs a content calendar with post types, frequency, thematic pillars, voice guidelines, and platform-specific format recommendations ● Includes growth tactics for paid promotion, influencer collaboration, and organic reach aligned to budget constraints ● Establishes measurable KPIs, analytics review schedules, and crisis management protocols for reputation issues ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in brand building.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step social media marketing strategy to establish a strong, recognizable brand presence on {{platform}}.

## Context
The strategy must address {{brand-context}} and create consistent messaging that resonates with the target audience while leveraging platform-specific capabilities for maximum visibility and engagement.

## Output
Deliver a detailed strategy document covering:

### 1. Brand Foundation
- Define the brand's unique value proposition and how it differentiates in the market
- Map target audience demographics, preferences, pain points, and content consumption patterns
- Establish SMART objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

### 2. Content Strategy
- **Content calendar:** Specify content types (posts, videos, stories, reels), publishing frequency, and thematic pillars
- **Content creation guidelines:** Define brand voice, visual identity standards, and incorporate relevant hashtags and keywords
- **Engagement tactics:** Outline response protocols for comments and messages, plus interactive content formats (polls, Q&As, live sessions)

### 3. Platform Optimization
- Identify and detail how to leverage platform-specific features (Stories, Reels, Spaces, Live, Shopping, etc.)
- Recommend content formats that perform best on the chosen platform

### 4. Growth & Amplification
- Plan paid promotion strategies within {{budget-and-goals}}
- Identify collaboration opportunities with influencers or complementary brands
- Outline tactics for organic reach expansion

### 5. Performance Management
- Define key metrics and KPIs to track
- Establish analytics review cadence and optimization process
- Create crisis management protocols for negative feedback or brand reputation issues

Format the strategy as a practical roadmap with specific actions, timelines, and success measurements.
```
