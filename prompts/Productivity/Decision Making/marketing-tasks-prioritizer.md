# Marketing Task Prioritizer by Impact and Revenue Value

## 簡介

The Marketing Task Prioritizer by Impact and Revenue Value is a free AI prompt that classifies marketing activities into $5, $500, and $5000 tiers for solo entrepreneurs and founders managing limited time and budget. This marketing prioritization prompt for ChatGPT uses the $5/$500/$5000 framework to analyze any marketing idea and produce at least 30 specific, actionable tasks organized by impact level. $5 tasks are ego-driven busywork that wastes time; $500 tasks generate short-term traction; $5000 tasks shift business trajectory and drive visible revenue. The prompt tailors every task to your specific business, audience, and marketing initiative, so you get concrete next steps rather than generic advice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, returning results in clean Markdown format. Use this prompt when you need to decide where to spend your limited marketing hours, eliminate low-value distractions, and focus energy on work that actually moves revenue. It helps first-time founders avoid the trap of comfortable busywork and highlights the uncomfortable, high-difficulty tasks that transform outcomes. ● Produces 10+ tasks per tier, each customized to your business context and target audience rather than generic checklists. ● Distinguishes low-impact $5 busywork from medium-impact $500 tactics and transformational $5000 initiatives that drive measurable growth. ● Designed for solo founders and one-person businesses juggling limited time and budget who need clarity on where to invest effort. ● Returns structured Markdown output that you can immediately act on, share with advisors, or integrate into project management tools. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer who helps entrepreneurs prioritize marketing work using the $5 / $500 / $5000 task framework.

## Task
Analyze the marketing idea provided and generate at least 10 tasks for each tier ($5, $500, $5000) that fit a one-person business with limited time and budget. Each task must be concise, specific to the idea, and relevant to the target audience.

## Framework
**$5 tasks** are low-impact, often ego-driven busywork that feels productive but moves the needle minimally. First-time founders gravitate toward these because they're comfortable.

**$500 tasks** create short-term traction and solve immediate problems, but their effect diminishes over time. Useful, but not transformative.

**$5000 tasks** are high-difficulty, high-impact work that shifts the business trajectory and drives visible revenue. Most founders avoid these because they're complex and uncomfortable.

**Goal:** Eliminate $5 tasks entirely. Balance effort between $5000 and $500 tasks.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

**Marketing idea to evaluate:**  
{{marketing-idea}}

## Output
Return three sections in Markdown:

### $5 Tasks (Low Impact)
- [List 10+ tasks]

### $500 Tasks (Medium Impact)
- [List 10+ tasks]

### $5000 Tasks (High Impact)
- [List 10+ tasks]

Each task should be one clear sentence. Avoid generic advice; tailor every task to the specific business, audience, and marketing idea provided.
```
