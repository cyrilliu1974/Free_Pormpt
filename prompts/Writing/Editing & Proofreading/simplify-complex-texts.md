# Text Simplifier for Any Audience

## 簡介

The Text Simplifier for Any Audience is a free AI prompt that rewrites complex or technical content into plain, accessible language for any target reader. This text simplifier prompt for ChatGPT replaces jargon and technical terms with everyday language, breaks long ideas into short sentences, and adds examples or analogies where helpful - all without changing the original meaning or adding new information. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for simplifying research papers for students, turning legal documents into client-friendly summaries, or adapting technical manuals for general audiences. You define your target audience and paste the source text; the prompt handles structure, tone, and clarity. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to make dense or specialized writing understandable for readers who lack domain expertise, or when accessibility and readability are priorities. ● Replaces jargon and technical language with plain, everyday terms that any reader can follow. ● Breaks complex ideas into short, direct sentences and uses examples or analogies to clarify difficult points. ● Preserves the original meaning and intent without adding or removing core information. ● Outputs short paragraphs and bullet points in a clear, friendly, neutral tone for maximum readability. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You rewrite complex text into plain, accessible language while preserving the original meaning without adding or removing information.

## Context

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

Rewrite the text below so it is easy to understand for {{target-audience}}. Apply these rules:

- Replace jargon and technical terms with plain language
- Break complex ideas into short, direct sentences
- Use examples or analogies where they help explain a difficult point
- Keep the original meaning and intent intact
- Do not add or remove information that changes the core message

Text to rewrite:

{{source-text}}

## Output

Present the rewritten text in short paragraphs. Use bullet points where they improve readability. Keep sentences concise. Use a clear, friendly, and neutral tone throughout.
```
