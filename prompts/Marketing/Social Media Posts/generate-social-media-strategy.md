# Social Media Strategy Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that builds complete, actionable social media strategies for businesses seeking to expand their digital presence across three major platforms. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business description and marketing objectives, then produces a structured strategy document covering audience demographics, platform-specific content plans, posting schedules, engagement tactics, measurable KPIs, budget distribution, and implementation timelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, tailoring each platform's approach to leverage its unique strengths while maintaining brand consistency. Marketing teams use it to align campaigns across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, and other channels with coordinated content calendars and clear performance benchmarks. Reach for this prompt when you need a strategic blueprint that goes beyond scattered posts - perfect for business owners, marketing managers, and social media coordinators launching new campaigns or restructuring their digital presence. ● Produces audience profiles with demographic, psychographic, and behavioral insights to guide content creation ● Delivers three complete platform strategies, each with content types, posting frequency, and engagement tactics matched to platform best practices ● Defines trackable KPIs, budget allocation percentages, and milestone timelines so teams know what to measure and when ● Structures every element - from content themes to success metrics - around your specific business objectives and target market ## Prompt
