# Audience Segmentation for Marketing Automation

## 簡介

The Audience Segmentation for Marketing Automation is a free AI prompt that creates detailed customer segments organized into actionable profiles for personalized marketing campaigns. This audience segmentation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target market and produces a structured markdown table with distinct segments, each defined by demographics, interests, and behaviors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming raw customer data and marketing goals into mutually exclusive audience profiles ready for campaign activation. Marketing teams use it to map out customer personas for email sequences, ad targeting, content personalization, and automated funnel workflows. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize diverse audience data into clear, campaign-ready segments that align with specific business objectives and available customer information. ● Combines demographic factors, interests, and behaviors into cohesive customer profiles that support precise targeting ● Outputs a clean markdown table format that integrates directly into campaign planning tools and documentation ● Ensures segments are mutually exclusive and aligned with your stated marketing goals and available data ● Produces actionable profiles ready for activation across email automation, ad platforms, and content personalization systems ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in audience segmentation for personalized marketing automation.

## Task
Create detailed audience segments in a structured table that combines demographics, interests, and behaviors into distinct customer profiles. Each segment must be well-defined, mutually exclusive, and actionable for targeted campaigns.

## Context
**Business & Industry:** {{business-and-industry}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Marketing Goals:** {{marketing-goals}}

**Available Customer Data:** {{customer-data}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the target audience to identify key demographic factors, relevant interests, and significant behaviors
2. Combine these elements into meaningful segments that align with your marketing goals and campaign strategies
3. Ensure segments are distinct and practical for activation

## Output
Provide a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Segment Name | Demographics | Interests | Behaviors |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|------------|

Each row should represent one distinct, actionable audience segment.
```
