# Keyword Search Volume and Competition Analysis

## 簡介

The Keyword Search Volume and Competition Analysis is a free AI prompt that evaluates target keywords by search demand and ranking difficulty for SEO strategists and content marketers. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business niche, target audience, and geographic focus to identify 10-15 search terms aligned with customer intent, then outputs a structured markdown table showing monthly search volume estimates and competition levels (Low/Medium/High) for each keyword. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable data that helps you prioritize keywords balancing search demand with ranking feasibility. Use it when planning content strategy, launching new campaigns, or entering new markets where you need data-driven keyword selection. ● Evaluates 10-15 keywords per analysis with realistic monthly search volume estimates ● Classifies competition level for each term based on market saturation and ranking difficulty ● Identifies high-opportunity keywords that balance search demand with feasibility ● Includes strategic recommendations highlighting the most promising terms for your SEO plan ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in keyword research and SEO strategy.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to assess search volume and competition for target keywords. Analyze the business context to identify relevant search terms potential customers would use, then evaluate each keyword's search volume and competition level.

## Context
Business context:
{{business-context}}

Target geography: {{geographic-focus}}

## Process
1. Analyze the business niche, audience, and core offerings
2. Identify 10-15 relevant keywords and phrases aligned with customer search intent
3. Assess each keyword's monthly search volume (provide realistic estimates or ranges)
4. Evaluate competition level (Low/Medium/High) based on market saturation and ranking difficulty
5. Prioritize keywords that balance search demand with ranking feasibility

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| KEYWORD | SEARCH VOLUME | COMPETITION LEVEL |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|

Include at least 10 relevant keywords. After the table, provide 2-3 sentences highlighting the most promising keyword opportunities for the SEO strategy.
```
