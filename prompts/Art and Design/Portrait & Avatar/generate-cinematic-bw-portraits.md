# Cinematic Black and White Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Black and White Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates dramatic, high-contrast monochrome portraits with Peter Lindbergh-inspired natural lighting and raw expression. This black and white portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images that mimic the look of 35mm film shot on Kodak Tri-X stock, complete with grain, deep shadows, and authentic emotion. The prompt specifies natural light only, a 35mm f/2 lens aesthetic, and a 3:2 aspect ratio that mirrors classic portrait photography. Use it to generate headshots, character studies, or editorial-style portraits with cinematic realism and texture. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need expressive, film-quality portraits that stand out from polished digital photography. ● Produces grainy Kodak Tri-X film texture with deep blacks and natural highlights ● Captures raw, authentic facial expressions with dramatic shadow play ● Mimics the 35mm f/2 lens depth and bokeh for a classic portrait look ● Outputs in 3:2 aspect ratio with quality and stylization parameters pre-tuned ## Prompt

```
High-contrast black-and-white portrait of {{subject}}, photographed in the style of Peter Lindbergh. Natural light only, dramatic shadows, raw authentic expression. Shot on a 35mm f/2 lens, grainy Kodak Tri-X vibe, deep texture, cinematic realism. --ar 3:2 --quality 2 --stylize 40
```
