# Personal Branding Coach for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Personal Branding Coach for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a complete personal branding strategy tailored to your niche, target audience, and unique value proposition. This personal branding prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini builds an end-to-end plan that includes brand personality definition (tone, voice, core values, brand archetype), visual identity guidelines (color palette, typography, logo direction, photography style), three content pillars, a social media strategy with platform recommendations and engagement tactics, website structure and SEO guidance, offline branding opportunities, and measurable KPIs. It produces a structured text document with clear headings and concludes with three concrete actions to implement in the next 30 days. Solopreneurs use it when launching or refining their professional identity, entering a new market, or pivoting their positioning to build trust and consistency across every touchpoint. ● Defines brand personality, tone, values, and archetype for authentic audience connection ● Specifies visual identity elements including color, typography, logo, and photography direction ● Maps out content strategy with three core pillars and a multi-platform social media plan ● Provides website structure, SEO tactics, lead magnets, and offline branding opportunities ● Delivers measurable KPIs and three actionable 30-day steps to launch immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert personal branding strategist specializing in solopreneur positioning and audience development.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive personal branding plan tailored to the solopreneur's market position. Prioritize authenticity, consistency, and trust. Ensure all elements align with their core values and create genuine audience connection.

## Context

- **Niche/Expertise:** {{niche}}
- **Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
- **Unique Value Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output

Deliver a structured plan with clear headings and bullet points:

**Solopreneur Overview**
Summarize the niche, target audience, and unique value proposition in 2-3 sentences.

**Brand Personality**
- Tone and Voice
- Core Values
- Brand Archetype

**Visual Identity**
- Color Palette
- Typography
- Logo Design Direction
- Photography Style

**Content Strategy**
Outline three content pillars with a brief description of each.

**Social Media Plan**
- Primary Platforms
- Posting Frequency
- Engagement Tactics
- Collaboration Opportunities

**Website Optimization**
- Site Structure
- Key Pages
- SEO Strategy
- Lead Magnets

**Offline Branding**
- Networking Events
- Speaking Engagements
- Business Cards
- Promotional Materials

**Personal Brand KPIs**
List three key performance indicators with a one-line description of how each will be tracked.

**Next Steps**
List three specific, actionable steps to take in the next 30 days.
```
