# Empty Cart Message Generator for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Empty Cart Message Generator for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that creates emotionally intelligent empty cart messaging systems for online retailers and UX teams. This empty cart message prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a structured discovery process that analyzes your brand voice, maps the emotional journey of cart abandonment, and delivers complete message architecture with category-specific variations. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it applies Don Norman's Emotional Design principles and Nielsen Norman Group tone guidelines to craft messages that acknowledge disappointment, pivot toward possibility, and provide clear re-engagement paths. Real use cases include reducing cart abandonment rates for Shopify stores, improving browse-back metrics for marketplace platforms, and aligning empty-state messaging with brand personality for direct-to-consumer brands. This prompt is for e-commerce managers, UX copywriters, conversion rate optimizers, and product teams who need to turn empty cart moments into browsing opportunities. ● Extracts emotional blueprint from brand context to identify tone anchors, customer mindset, and voice guardrails for authentic messaging. ● Maps the psychological journey from cart abandonment to empty-state discovery, pinpointing re-engagement triggers like curiosity and delight. ● Delivers structured message architecture with opening hooks, emotional pivots, and actionable CTAs in multiple variations for A/B testing. ● Creates category-specific messaging tailored to your product range with link structure recommendations and popular product showcase strategies. ## Prompt

```

You are an expert UX copywriter specializing in empty-state messaging and emotional design, applying Don Norman's Emotional Design principles and Nielsen Norman Group tone guidelines to transform user disappointment into re-engagement.

## Context

{{brand-context}} — Include: brand name, desired customer feeling, brand voice in 3-5 words (e.g., playful, sophisticated, caring), top 3-5 product categories, and brief typical customer description.

### Phase 1: Brand Voice Discovery

**Output:** Clear emotional blueprint for messaging.
```
