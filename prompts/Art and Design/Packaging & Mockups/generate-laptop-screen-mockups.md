# Laptop Screen Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Laptop Screen Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic lifestyle scenes showing custom content integrated into laptop screens for designers, marketers, and product teams. This laptop screen mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana transforms uploaded images into commercial-quality product photography. The prompt handles perspective warping to match the laptop's tilt angle, adds realistic glass reflections and LCD light emission, and places the scene in authentic work environments with accurate hand anatomy and keyboard details. Use it to showcase web apps, dashboards, presentations, or any digital interface in believable real-world contexts without scheduling photoshoots or hiring product photographers. Reach for this prompt when you need portfolio pieces, landing page hero images, client presentation visuals, or social media content that demonstrates software in use. ● Warps uploaded screen content to match laptop display angle and respects bezel boundaries for realistic integration. ● Renders natural hand positions on keyboard with correct finger count and accurate anatomy. ● Applies soft diffused window lighting from the left with warm-neutral color temperature and subtle background depth of field. ● Produces high-resolution output with precise laptop proportions, material textures, and no overlaid text or UI elements. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic lifestyle product mockup: person typing on a laptop placed on {{surface-and-environment}}, hands visible on keyboard. Slightly elevated front-left perspective, laptop centered in frame.

The uploaded image {{screen-content}} is seamlessly integrated into the laptop screen with realistic perspective warp matching the screen tilt angle, respecting bezel boundaries. Soft glass reflections and simulated LCD light emission create authentic screen glow. The content appears naturally embedded in the display, not as a flat overlay.

Soft natural indoor lighting: diffused window light from left as key source, subtle ambient bounce fill. Warm-neutral color temperature (4500-5500K). Soft realistic shadows cast by keyboard and hands. Subtle background depth of field (f/2.8-f/4 equivalent) keeping focus on laptop and hands.

Accurate hand anatomy with correct finger count and natural positioning. Precise keyboard details, accurate laptop proportions, and realistic material textures (aluminum, plastic, glass). Commercial-grade quality, high resolution, no added text or UI overlays.
```
