# Subscription Service Launch Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Subscription Service Launch Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive launch strategies for solopreneurs building subscription-based businesses. This subscription business prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, budget, and timeline to produce an eight-part action plan covering market positioning, pricing models, platform selection, marketing tactics, customer acquisition channels, retention strategies, solo operational workflows, and 12-month success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, optimizing for scalability within limited resources and long-term profitability. This prompt is designed for independent entrepreneurs establishing passive income streams through subscription services, whether you're launching a SaaS tool, membership community, content library, or recurring service. ● Outputs numbered launch plans with tiered pricing recommendations, trial period structures, and annual versus monthly billing analysis ● Prioritizes customer acquisition channels by ROI and includes churn reduction techniques specific to solo operations ● Delivers platform and technology stack recommendations matched to your budget and technical skill level ● Provides 12-month milestone tracking with key metrics for measuring scalability and sustainability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in subscription-based business models and passive income strategies for independent entrepreneurs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step launch strategy for a subscription-based service. The plan must address pricing strategy, platform selection, marketing tactics, and customer retention techniques. Structure the output as a numbered list with clearly labeled main sections and indented subsections.

## Context
The strategy targets solopreneurs establishing passive income streams. Focus on:
- Scalability within limited resources
- Sustainability and long-term profitability
- Practical implementation given solo operator constraints
- Low-overhead operational models

**Business Context:**
{{business-context}}

**Budget & Timeline:**
{{budget-and-timeframe}}

## Output
Deliver a detailed launch plan in numbered list format with:
1. Market positioning and differentiation strategy
2. Pricing model recommendations (tiered structures, trial periods, annual vs. monthly)
3. Platform and technology stack selection
4. Pre-launch and launch marketing tactics
5. Customer acquisition channels prioritized by ROI
6. Retention and churn reduction techniques
7. Operational workflow for solo management
8. Key metrics and milestones for the first 12 months

Ensure all recommendations are actionable for a single operator with the specified constraints.
```
