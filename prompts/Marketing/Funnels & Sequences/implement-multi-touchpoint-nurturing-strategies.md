# Multi-Touchpoint Lead Nurturing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Multi-Touchpoint Lead Nurturing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven lead nurturing plans tailored to your business context and customer journey. This lead nurturing prompt for ChatGPT generates a comprehensive, actionable strategy covering six core components: audience segmentation based on behavior and funnel stage, personalized email sequences with subject lines and send triggers, targeted content assets mapped to each stage, behavioral workflows triggered by opens and clicks, marketing automation recommendations, and a complete measurement framework. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your business context as input and returning a step-by-step implementation roadmap. Marketing teams use it to design multi-channel campaigns that move prospects from awareness through decision, while ensuring every touchpoint is relevant and timely. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, scalable approach to guide leads through your sales funnel without starting from scratch. ● Segments audiences by behavior, preferences, and position in the funnel, ensuring relevant messaging for every group. ● Generates personalized email sequences with specific subject lines, key messages, and behavioral send triggers. ● Maps targeted content assets like guides, case studies, and webinars to each stage of the customer journey. ● Designs behavioral workflows that automatically respond to opens, clicks, page visits, and downloads. ● Provides marketing automation setup guidance, KPIs, timelines, and resource requirements for execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in lead nurturing and marketing automation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive multi-touchpoint lead nurturing strategy that guides prospects through the sales funnel. Create a detailed, actionable plan covering:

- Audience segmentation framework based on behavior, preferences, and funnel stage
- Personalized email sequences for each segment with subject lines, key messages, and send triggers
- Targeted content assets (guides, case studies, webinars, etc.) mapped to each funnel stage
- Behavioral triggers and follow-up workflows (e.g., email opens, link clicks, page visits, content downloads)
- Marketing automation setup and integration recommendations
- Key performance indicators and measurement framework
- Timeline and resource requirements for implementation

## Context
{{business-context}}

The strategy must be data-driven, scalable, and aligned with overall business objectives. Consider the complete customer journey from awareness through decision.

## Output
Deliver the strategy in step-by-step format with clear headings for:
1. Audience Segmentation Strategy
2. Email Nurture Sequences by Segment
3. Content Asset Plan
4. Behavioral Triggers & Automation Workflows
5. Implementation Roadmap
6. Success Metrics & Optimization

For each component, provide specific, actionable recommendations ready for execution.
```
