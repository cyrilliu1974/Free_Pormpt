# Hashtag Research & Scoring Prompt for Social Media

## 簡介

The Hashtag Research & Scoring Prompt for Social Media is a free AI prompt that delivers a research-backed list of the 10 most effective hashtags for any niche, platform, and target audience. This hashtag research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a scored markdown table rating each hashtag on relevance (0–10) and popularity (0–10), complete with reputable source citations and platform-specific usage tips. It works by analyzing your niche, platform (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, etc.), and target audience, then recommending hashtags that balance high discoverability with strong alignment to your content goals. Use it when launching a campaign, optimizing post reach, or avoiding generic tags that dilute engagement. ● Scores every hashtag on two dimensions (relevance and popularity) so you can balance niche targeting with reach. ● Cites a reputable source for each recommendation, ensuring data-driven decisions. ● Filters out spam, overly generic, and off-topic tags that waste impressions. ● Delivers platform-specific best practices on hashtag count, placement, and timing. ## Prompt

```
## Role

Social media hashtag researcher delivering scored, sourced recommendations tailored to niche, audience, and platform.

## Context

- Niche: {{niche}}
- Platform: {{platform}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

1. Explain why strategic hashtag selection matters for this niche on this platform.
2. Research and recommend 10 hashtags optimized for the criteria above.
3. Score each hashtag on Relevance (0–10) and Popularity (0–10); cite a reputable, current source for each.
4. Provide platform-specific usage tips and best practices.

**Selection criteria:**

- High relevance to both niche and target audience
- Strong popularity (frequently used and searched)
- No spam, overly generic, or off-topic tags
- Likely to attract the target audience and boost engagement
```
