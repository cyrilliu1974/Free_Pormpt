# Video SEO Optimization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Video SEO Optimization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that analyzes video metadata and builds a comprehensive, step-by-step optimization strategy for content creators and marketers. This video SEO prompt for ChatGPT accepts your video title, target keywords, current description, tags, and audience, then produces a seven-part action plan covering transcription strategy, keyword placement, title rewrites, description optimization, tag expansion, thumbnail considerations, and platform-specific tweaks for YouTube, Vimeo, and similar services. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, balancing search-algorithm requirements with natural language flow so your content ranks higher without sacrificing viewer engagement. Use it when launching new video content, refreshing underperforming uploads, or scaling a content library across multiple platforms. ● Produces transcription analysis and keyword-density recommendations tailored to your target search terms ● Rewrites titles and descriptions following SEO best practices while preserving brand voice and audience appeal ● Expands and refines tag sets to capture long-tail queries and related topics ● Includes thumbnail guidance and platform-specific optimizations for YouTube, Vimeo, and other video hosts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist optimizing video content for search engine discoverability and ranking.

## Task
Analyze the provided video metadata and create a comprehensive optimization plan that enhances search visibility while maintaining natural language flow and user engagement. Structure your recommendations as actionable steps covering transcription strategy, keyword integration, and metadata enhancement.

## Context
**Video Title:** {{video-title}}

**Target Keywords:** {{target-keywords}}

**Current Description:** {{current-description}}

**Current Tags:** {{current-tags}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Output
Deliver your optimization plan as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps. Cover:

1. Transcription and content analysis
2. Strategic keyword placement and density
3. Title optimization recommendations
4. Description rewrite with SEO best practices
5. Tag refinement and expansion
6. Thumbnail and engagement considerations
7. Platform-specific optimizations (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.)

Ensure each recommendation is specific to the provided video data and balances search algorithm requirements with viewer experience.
```
