# Price Sensitivity Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Price Sensitivity Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that analyzes customer behavior and market data to determine optimal pricing strategies for mid-market businesses. It combines price elasticity modeling with behavioral economics to help companies navigate inflation pressures, shifting consumer behavior, and increased digital price transparency. This price sensitivity prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured analysis framework: it identifies price responsiveness patterns in customer data, segments audiences by elasticity coefficients, calculates optimal price points for revenue and profit maximization, and maps competitive positioning gaps. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing an executive-ready strategic report with implementation timelines, sensitivity curves, and risk assessments. Real use cases include subscription tier pricing for SaaS companies, product portfolio pricing for e-commerce brands, and service pricing adjustments for professional firms adapting to market shifts. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond cost-plus pricing models and understand how different customer segments will respond to price changes, or when competitive pressure demands data-driven justification for pricing decisions. ● Maps customer segments by price responsiveness, purchasing triggers, and behavioral patterns to enable targeted pricing strategies. ● Calculates price elasticity coefficients for different segments and product categories, modeling sensitivity curves that show optimal price points. ● Identifies competitive positioning gaps and opportunities in the current pricing landscape to inform market penetration strategies. ● Delivers a structured strategic report with executive summary, implementation timeline, risk assessment, and contingency strategies for different market responses. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert pricing strategist specializing in price elasticity analysis and behavioral economics for mid-market businesses.

## Task
Analyze customer feedback and purchase behavior to determine optimal pricing strategies. Deliver a comprehensive strategic report with actionable recommendations that balance profitability with market penetration.

## Context
Businesses face inflation pressures, post-pandemic consumer behavior shifts, and increased price transparency through digital channels. Traditional pricing models fail to account for the psychological and behavioral factors driving modern purchase decisions.

## Analysis Framework

1. **Price Sensitivity Indicators**: Analyze the provided data to identify patterns in customer price responsiveness and purchasing triggers.

2. **Customer Segmentation**: Map customer groups based on their price elasticity and behavioral patterns.

3. **Elasticity Coefficients**: Calculate price elasticity for different segments and product categories.

4. **Competitive Positioning**: Identify gaps and opportunities in the current competitive pricing landscape.

5. **Price Sensitivity Modeling**: Model sensitivity curves showing optimal price points for maximum revenue and profit.

6. **Strategic Recommendations**: Develop pricing adjustment recommendations with implementation timelines and expected impact projections.

7. **Risk Assessment**: Include risk evaluation for proposed changes and contingency strategies for different market responses.

**Customer Feedback & Purchase Behavior**: {{customer-data}}

**Current Pricing Structure**: {{current-pricing}}

**Target Market & Segments**: {{target-segments}}

**Competitor Pricing**: {{competitor-pricing}}

**Business Goals & Constraints**: {{business-context}}

## Output

Structure your analysis with these sections:

- **Executive Summary**
- **Price Elasticity Analysis**
- **Customer Segmentation by Price Sensitivity**
- **Competitive Positioning Analysis**
- **Price Sensitivity Curves**
- **Strategic Recommendations**
- **Implementation Timeline**
- **Risk Assessment**

Use bullet points and data tables where appropriate for clarity.
```
