# Gender-Swapped Story Rewrite and Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Gender-Swapped Story Rewrite and Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites stories with all character genders reversed and delivers a structured analysis of how gender expectations shape narrative dynamics for writers, educators, and cultural critics. This gender-swapped story prompt for ChatGPT takes your original story text and produces two outputs: a complete rewritten version with every character's gender flipped, and a detailed breakdown comparing character interactions, relationships, and societal roles before and after the swap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, maintaining the core plot and themes while revealing how deeply gender norms influence reader perception, character authority, and relationship interpretation. Writers use it to test whether their narratives rely on gendered stereotypes; educators employ it to teach critical reading of gender in literature; cultural analysts apply it to classic texts to surface hidden assumptions about power and social position. Reach for this prompt when you need to examine how gender shapes storytelling, challenge traditional character archetypes, or prepare inclusive narratives that avoid reductive gender roles. ● Produces a full story rewrite with every character gender reversed, preserving plot and themes ● Delivers side-by-side analysis of character dynamics, relationship meanings, and societal role shifts ● Includes a reflective section on what the exercise reveals about cultural gender norms and audience expectations ● Designed to avoid reinforcing stereotypes while surfacing nuanced insights into gendered storytelling patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative writing expert specializing in literature, gender dynamics, and societal roles across cultures and time periods.

## Task
Rewrite the provided story with all character genders swapped, then analyze how this reversal impacts character dynamics, relationships, and societal roles within the narrative.

## Context
Gender-swap analysis reveals how deeply gender expectations shape storytelling. The rewritten version should maintain the core plot and themes while exposing assumptions about gender roles. Focus on nuanced exploration without reinforcing stereotypes or making reductive gender-based assumptions.

## Input
{{original-story}}

## Output
**Original Story Overview:**
[Summarize the original story's plot, characters, and setting]

**Gender-Swapped Story:**
[Full rewrite with all character genders reversed]

**Character Dynamics Analysis:**
- Original Dynamic: [Describe key character interactions and power relationships]
- Gender-Swapped Dynamic: [Describe how these interactions shift with reversed genders]
- Impact: [Explain what the reversal reveals about gendered expectations in character behavior]

**Relationship Analysis:**
- Original Relationships: [Describe romantic, familial, or professional bonds]
- Gender-Swapped Relationships: [Describe how these bonds read differently with reversed genders]
- Impact: [Explain what changes in perception or meaning]

**Societal Role Analysis:**
- Original Societal Roles: [Describe characters' positions, authority, and social functions]
- Gender-Swapped Societal Roles: [Describe how credibility, expectations, or constraints shift]
- Impact: [Explain what this reveals about gendered social structures]

**Gender Norm Reflection:**
[Provide a thought-provoking reflection on what the exercise reveals about embedded gender norms, audience expectations, and cultural assumptions about gender roles]
```
