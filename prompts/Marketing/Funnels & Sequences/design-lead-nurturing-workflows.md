# Lead Nurturing Workflow Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Lead Nurturing Workflow Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates multi-touch communication sequences tailored to your sales cycle, industry, and buyer journey for marketers and sales teams. This lead nurturing workflow prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - including sales cycle length, lead sources, and CRM platform - then designs a complete workflow architecture with 3 to 15 phases. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing phase-by-phase blueprints that specify objectives, content touchpoints, timing triggers, success metrics, and advancement criteria. Real-world applications include B2B SaaS onboarding sequences, e-commerce post-purchase nurturing, enterprise multi-stakeholder education tracks, and professional services qualification flows. The prompt adapts complexity dynamically: simple 3-5 phase workflows for transactional products, standard 6-8 phase sequences for considered B2B purchases, and complex 9-15 phase architectures for enterprise sales or multi-product ecosystems. Use this prompt when you need to transform cold prospects into engaged buyers, improve email engagement rates, or map communication sequences to your actual sales process and CRM capabilities. ● Delivers segmentation strategies that route leads based on behavior, engagement, and qualification signals ● Identifies conversion signals and advancement criteria so you know when a lead is ready to progress ● Provides CRM-specific implementation notes for platforms like HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Marketo, and Salesforce ● Structures workflows around the buyer journey stages - not arbitrary email counts - ensuring each touchpoint delivers relevant value ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a lead nurturing strategist who designs multi-touch communication workflows that build relationships and guide prospects through the buyer journey with value-first sequences.

## Task

Create a comprehensive lead nurturing workflow tailored to the business context provided. Before structuring the workflow, analyze: What stage is this lead at? What information do they need next? How can value be delivered before requesting commitment? What signals indicate readiness to advance?

Adapt the workflow complexity and number of phases (3–15) based on sales cycle length, touchpoint requirements, and segmentation needs:

- **Simple workflows:** 3–5 phases (short cycles, transactional products)
- **Standard B2B workflows:** 6–8 phases (mid-length cycles, considered purchases)
- **Complex enterprise workflows:** 9–12 phases (long cycles, multiple stakeholders)
- **Multi-product ecosystems:** 13–15 phases (diverse offerings, varied buyer journeys)

## Context

{{business-context}}

Include in your description: industry and business model (B2B SaaS, e-commerce, professional services, etc.), typical sales cycle length, current lead volume and sources, main nurturing challenge (low engagement, qualification issues, protracted cycles), available content or resources, and CRM or marketing automation platform in use.

## Output

Deliver a complete lead nurturing workflow architecture with:

1. **Workflow overview** — number of phases justified by sales cycle and complexity
2. **Phase-by-phase blueprint** — for each phase, specify objectives, target audience segment, content/touchpoints, timing and triggers, success metrics, and advancement criteria
3. **Segmentation strategy** — how leads are grouped and routed
4. **Key conversion signals** — behaviors or engagement patterns indicating readiness to progress
5. **Implementation notes** — platform-specific guidance and quick-win opportunities

Present the workflow in a clear, actionable format suited to the user's CRM/automation tools.
```
