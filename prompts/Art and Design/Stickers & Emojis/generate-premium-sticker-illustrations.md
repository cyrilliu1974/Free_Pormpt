# Premium Sticker Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Premium Sticker Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that converts any uploaded photo into a polished sticker-style illustration with clean white outlines and customizable moods for designers, marketers, and content creators. This sticker illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana preserves the subject's identity and pose while adding playful modern attitude through three variables: mood (such as cheerful, edgy, or calm), enhancement (personality tweaks that boost character without altering identity), and accent-color (to match brand palettes). The output is a 1024×1024 image with a smooth white die-cut outline on a solid white background, ready for print or digital use. Use it to create brand mascots, product packaging graphics, social media assets, or merchandise designs without needing illustration software. Designers and marketers reach for this prompt when they need sticker art that maintains recognizable subject features while injecting personality and polish. ● Preserves subject identity and pose while adding personality through mood and enhancement variables. ● Generates a clean, smooth white die-cut outline with medium-thick borders and smooth edges. ● Outputs on a solid pure white background with no transparency, gradients, or texture - ready for immediate use. ● Accepts accent-color input to align illustrations with brand color schemes across campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Transform the uploaded image into a premium sticker-style illustration with a {{mood}} feel. Preserve the subject's identity and pose while adding playful modern attitude and {{enhancement}} to boost personality without changing who the character is. Use {{accent-color}} as the primary accent throughout. Apply a clean, smooth white die-cut sticker outline (medium-thick) around the subject with smooth edges.

Background: solid pure white (#FFFFFF), fully filled, no transparency, no checkerboard, no texture, no gradient. Final image must look like a finished sticker placed on white canvas.

Composition: single centered subject, clean silhouette, modern sticker illustration style, 1024×1024.

Avoid: transparent background, checkerboard background, grid pattern, PNG transparency preview, textured background, gradients, shadows outside sticker, text, logos.
```
