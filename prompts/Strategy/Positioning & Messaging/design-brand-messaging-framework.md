# Brand Messaging Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Brand Messaging Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete brand positioning strategy for marketers, founders, and brand strategists. This brand messaging framework prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company context to produce four interconnected brand elements: a Brand Promise that captures what you stand for, Brand Personality traits that define your character, Brand Voice guidelines for consistent communication, and Key Messaging Pillars that articulate your unique value. It works by evaluating your competitive landscape, customer pain points, and target audience preferences to deliver actionable, specific guidance in a clean markdown table format. The framework runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable across platforms. Use it when launching a new brand, refreshing existing messaging, or aligning teams around a unified voice. ● Produces a four-column markdown table mapping Brand Promise, Personality, Voice, and Messaging Pillars in one structured output ● Analyzes competitive positioning gaps and customer needs to ensure differentiation ● Includes specific tone descriptors, communication examples, and messaging themes for each component ● Delivers immediately actionable guidelines that ensure consistency across all brand touchpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist designing a comprehensive brand messaging framework.

## Task
Establish a strong brand positioning in the target market by creating a structured framework that encompasses Brand Promise, Brand Personality, Brand Voice, and Key Messaging Pillars. Ensure each component is clearly defined and aligned with company values and target audience expectations.

## Context
Develop the framework by analyzing:
- Unique selling propositions that differentiate the brand
- Competitive landscape and positioning gaps
- Customer pain points and unmet needs
- Target audience preferences and communication styles

Provide specific examples and actionable guidelines for each element to ensure consistency across all brand communications.

{{company-context}}

## Output
Present your framework as a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Brand Promise | Brand Personality | Brand Voice | Key Messaging Pillars |
|---------------|-------------------|-------------|----------------------|

Each cell must contain detailed, actionable information that can be immediately applied to brand communications. Include specific examples, tone descriptors, and messaging themes that reflect the company's positioning in its competitive landscape.
```
