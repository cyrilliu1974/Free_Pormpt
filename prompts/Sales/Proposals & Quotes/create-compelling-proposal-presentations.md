# Proposal Presentation Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Proposal Presentation Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that generates a complete seven-step roadmap for crafting winning proposal presentations tailored to any target industry. This proposal presentation prompt for ChatGPT walks you through the full lifecycle of presentation development, from researching stakeholder pain points and articulating differentiated value propositions to designing impactful visuals, applying persuasion techniques, rehearsing delivery, and preparing for Q&A sessions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing industry-specific guidance rather than generic advice. Use it when you need to win new business, pitch a project to decision-makers, or deliver a high-stakes proposal that addresses the competitive landscape and priorities unique to your client's industry. ● Generates a structured seven-step framework covering audience research, value articulation, narrative design, visual strategy, persuasion tactics, rehearsal protocols, and follow-up planning. ● Tailors every recommendation to the target industry's decision-making context, competitive dynamics, and stakeholder expectations. ● Provides concrete techniques and best practices instead of generic templates, ensuring each step reflects real-world proposal success factors. ● Helps anticipate objections, prepare supplementary materials, and plan immediate next steps to move deals forward. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert proposal presentation creator with deep knowledge across industries and presentation best practices.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, step-by-step process for crafting compelling proposal presentations tailored to {{target-industry}}. Provide a clear roadmap that guides from initial research to final delivery, covering all critical elements that contribute to proposal success.

## Process Requirements

Deliver a 7-step framework covering:

1. **Audience Analysis** - Research decision-makers, influencers, and stakeholders; identify pain points, goals, and preferences; tailor content and messaging to resonate with their needs

2. **Value Proposition** - Articulate unique value; highlight benefits and outcomes for the client; differentiate from competitors

3. **Compelling Narrative** - Craft an engaging story arc with clear structure (problem, solution, benefits, call-to-action); incorporate relevant case studies, testimonials, and examples

4. **Impactful Visuals** - Use charts, graphs, and infographics for digestible data; ensure consistent, professional look and feel; leverage visual metaphors and imagery to reinforce key points

5. **Persuasion Techniques** - Apply social proof, scarcity, and other persuasive techniques; anticipate and address potential objections; use confident, action-oriented language

6. **Rehearsal and Refinement** - Practice delivery multiple times; gather feedback from colleagues or test audience; iterate and polish based on insights

7. **Q&A and Follow-Up Preparation** - Anticipate likely questions and prepare responses; have supplementary materials ready; plan immediate next steps

## Output Format

Present each step as:

**Step [#]: [Step Name]**
- [Key Action 1]
- [Key Action 2]
- [Key Action 3]

Provide actionable, industry-specific insights and best practices for {{target-industry}} in each step. Avoid generic advice—offer concrete techniques relevant to this industry's decision-making context, competitive landscape, and stakeholder priorities.
```
