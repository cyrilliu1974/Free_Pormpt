# Minimal Restaurant Logo Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimal Restaurant Logo Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates flat vector combination-mark logos with abstract symbols and refined typography for restaurants, cafés, and dining brands. This restaurant logo prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces timeless, scalable designs that pair cuisine-inspired abstract icons with clean serif or script typography. It outputs pure flat vector logos with no gradients or shadows, optimized for readability at any size - from menu inserts to large-format signage. Restaurant owners input their establishment name, cuisine type, and brand colors to generate warm, authentic marks that communicate elegance and personality across menus, storefronts, packaging, and social media. Ideal for food entrepreneurs, café owners, and brand designers who need professional visual identity assets without custom illustration costs. ● Produces flat vector logos with abstract symbols drawn from restaurant name or cuisine heritage, paired with legible serif or script type. ● Accepts custom restaurant name, cuisine type, and brand color palette to generate unique, ownable visual identity. ● Outputs designs with transparent or white backgrounds, optimized for small-scale menu use and large-format signage alike. ● Delivers timeless, minimal aesthetic with warm, authentic, and elegant brand personality - no trends, no unnecessary ornament. ## Prompt

```
Flat vector logo design for {{restaurant-name}}, a {{cuisine-type}} restaurant. Combination mark featuring an abstract symbol inspired by the restaurant name or cuisine identity, paired with clean serif or refined typography. Brand personality: warm, authentic, elegant. Color palette: {{brand-colors}}. Minimal, timeless style with no gradients, no shadows, pure flat design. White or transparent background. Optimized for scalability—crisp and readable at small sizes for menu, signage, and social media use.
```
