# Studio Portrait Variations Generator

## 簡介

The Studio Portrait Variations Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional studio portrait photography with pixel-perfect facial consistency across multiple variations for fashion designers, e-commerce brands, and creative directors. This studio portrait prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates 32k HD resolution images of a subject in elegant studio poses against clean white backgrounds with professional lighting, realistic shadows, and natural highlights. The prompt locks facial proportions - ears, nose, mouth, and bone structure - while allowing you to vary expression, hair color, and outfit color through three simple variables. Fashion brands use it to build cohesive lookbooks without reshooting; e-commerce teams generate consistent model imagery across product lines; creative studios prototype styling directions before booking expensive photo sessions. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality portrait variations that maintain the same face but swap styling details, or when building a visual identity system that demands model consistency across dozens of images. ● Locks 100% facial consistency - exact ear, nose, mouth, and skeletal proportions - across every generated variation. ● Produces 32k HD studio photography with professional lighting, natural skin texture, and realistic shadow rendering. ● Customizes expression, hair color, and outfit color through three variables while preserving the subject's identity. ● Outputs fashion-ready portraits with detailed styling: corset tops, floral embellishments, diamond accessories, and specific makeup references. ## Prompt
