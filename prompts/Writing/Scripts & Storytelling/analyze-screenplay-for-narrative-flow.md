# Screenplay Scene-by-Scene Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Screenplay Scene-by-Scene Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers detailed narrative structure evaluation and pacing optimization for screenwriters and script editors. This screenplay analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by examining each scene's purpose, impact, and contribution to overall story flow, then providing specific recommendations for reordering, rewriting, or tightening scenes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a structured report with pacing scores, transition diagnostics, and concrete fixes grounded in industry-standard principles of audience engagement and narrative coherence. Writers use it during revision to identify redundant scenes, fix jarring cuts, strengthen emotional beats, and ensure every scene earns its place in the final draft. Reach for this prompt when you have a complete or near-complete screenplay draft and need an objective structural audit before production or submission. ● Assigns a 1-10 pacing score to every scene and flags redundant or weak moments. ● Identifies transition problems - jarring cuts, missing bridges, and confusing time shifts. ● Recommends specific scene reordering with clear rationale tied to suspense and coherence. ● Provides rewrite objectives for individual scenes that need content changes to serve the story. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert screenplay analyst specializing in narrative structure, pacing optimization, and story flow.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive scene-by-scene analysis of the screenplay, evaluating each scene's purpose, impact, and pacing. Identify what works, diagnose structural problems, and recommend specific improvements to scene order, content, and transitions.

## Context
**Screenplay:** {{screenplay-title}}
**Genre:** {{genre}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Key themes and desired emotional impact:** {{themes-and-impact}}

Base all recommendations on industry-standard principles: audience engagement, suspense maintenance, and narrative coherence.

## Output
Structure your analysis in this format:

### Scene Structure Analysis
Create a table with columns:
- Scene Number/Name
- Current Position
- Purpose/Impact
- Pacing Score (1-10)
- Recommended Position
- Suggested Changes

### Flow Assessment
**✅ Working Elements:** List scenes, sequences, and structural choices that effectively serve the story.

**❌ Problem Areas:** Identify pacing lags, redundant scenes, unclear motivations, or weak payoffs.

**⚡ Transition Issues:** Pinpoint jarring cuts, missing bridging moments, or confusing time/location shifts.

### Optimization Recommendations
**🎯 Scene Order Adjustments:** Specific resequencing suggestions with rationale.

**📝 Rewrite Suggestions:** Scenes requiring content changes, with clear objectives for each rewrite.

**🔄 Transition Improvements:** Concrete fixes for scene-to-scene flow problems.
```
