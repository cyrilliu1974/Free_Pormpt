# Partner Incentive Program Builder for Product Launches

## 簡介

The Partner Incentive Program Builder for Product Launches is a free AI prompt that creates structured partner incentive strategies for businesses launching new products. This partner incentive program prompt for ChatGPT walks you through designing a tiered reward system that maximizes partner engagement and product adoption. Enter your launch context - industry, product description, target partner types like resellers or affiliates, and existing relationships - and receive an actionable 8-step plan covering partner selection, tiered incentive structures (premium rewards for top partners, standard benefits for others), timeline mapping across pre-launch, launch, and post-launch phases, clear terms and conditions, tracking metrics like sales volume and ROI, communication strategies including partner webinars and dashboards, execution monitoring, and program closeout with feedback loops. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a complete incentive framework ready to present to leadership or roll out to your channel partners. ● Selects and segments partners into three tiers with differentiated reward levels based on reach, influence, and past performance ● Maps incentives across three launch phases with time-specific rewards to build anticipation, maximize launch buzz, and sustain post-launch momentum ● Defines qualification criteria, payout schedules, and exclusions so partners understand exactly how to earn rewards ● Establishes tracking metrics - sales volume, lead generation, conversion rates, CAC, program ROI - and real-time reporting dashboards ● Includes communication playbooks, partner webinars, program guides, and feedback collection for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business strategist specializing in partner incentive programs that drive product adoption and launch success.

## Task
Create a comprehensive partner incentive strategy for a new product launch. Develop a tiered program with clear qualification criteria, timeline, and measurement framework that will maximize partner engagement and product adoption.

## Context
{{launch-context}}

Include: industry, product description, target partner types (resellers, distributors, affiliates, etc.), and any existing partner relationships or constraints.

## Output
Deliver an 8-step incentive program plan:

**Step 1: Identify Top Partners**
Select 5-10 partners based on reach, influence, past performance, alignment with product positioning, and capacity to drive adoption.

**Step 2: Design Tiered Incentive Structure**
- Tier 1 (Top 1-3 partners): Premium rewards (exclusive access, highest commission rates, co-marketing budget, dedicated support)
- Tier 2 (Next 2-5 partners): Strong incentives (enhanced commissions, marketing materials, priority training)
- Tier 3 (Remaining partners): Foundational benefits (standard commissions, product samples, launch kit)

Recommended incentive types: revenue share bonuses, volume discounts, exclusive territories, early access, co-branded marketing campaigns, performance bonuses, training and certification programs.

**Step 3: Set Incentive Timeline**
- Pre-launch (4-6 weeks): Build anticipation with early access, training sessions, preview materials
- Launch window (2-4 weeks): Maximum rewards for early sales and promotional activity
- Post-launch (8-12 weeks): Sustain momentum with performance milestones and follow-on bonuses

**Step 4: Define Clear Terms and Conditions**
- Qualification criteria (sales targets, marketing activities, certification requirements)
- Payout schedule and calculation methods
- Program duration and key dates
- Exclusions and limitations

**Step 5: Establish Tracking and Reporting**
Measure: partner sales volume, lead generation, conversion rates, time-to-first-sale, customer acquisition cost, program ROI, partner engagement levels.

**Step 6: Communicate Plan to Partners**
- Launch partner webinar explaining program details
- Provide comprehensive program guide and FAQ
- Assign dedicated contact for questions
- Create partner portal with real-time performance dashboards

**Step 7: Execute and Monitor**
- Launch program with email and call campaigns
- Track weekly performance against targets
- Conduct bi-weekly partner check-ins
- Adjust rewards or terms based on early results

**Step 8: Close Out and Report**
- Distribute final rewards and recognition
- Gather partner feedback via survey
- Document results, learnings, and ROI for future programs
- Share success stories internally and with top partners
```
