# Winter Vehicle Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Winter Vehicle Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic images of vehicles on snowy terrain with atmospheric winter lighting and fine surface detail. This winter vehicle photography prompt for Midjourney produces wide-angle, low-angle compositions with shallow depth of field, soft overcast lighting, and atmospheric fog effects that bring out the drama of cold-weather automotive scenes. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image models, making it ideal for automotive marketers, product photographers, and creative directors who need striking winter vehicle visuals without arranging costly outdoor shoots. Use it to create promotional imagery for SUVs, trucks, winter tire campaigns, or adventure vehicle branding. ● Produces wide-angle shots from a low camera angle that emphasize vehicle presence against winter terrain. ● Captures fine surface detail on accessories, grilles, wheels, and body features even in overcast conditions. ● Renders soft lighting breaking through overcast winter skies with natural atmospheric fog for depth. ● Applies shallow depth of field and cinematic photography style for professional-grade automotive visuals. ## Prompt

```
{{medium}} of {{vehicle-scene}} aligned on a snowy terrain structure, fine surface detail on accessories, soft lighting peeking through overcast winter sky, atmospheric fog, wide-angle shot from low camera angle, shallow depth of field, cinematic photography style --ar 16:9
```
