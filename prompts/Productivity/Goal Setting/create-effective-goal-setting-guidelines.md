# Goal-Setting Guidelines Generator for Stretch Goals

## 簡介

The Goal-Setting Guidelines Generator for Stretch Goals is a free AI prompt that creates tailored goal-setting frameworks for individuals and teams who want to push their limits while avoiding burnout. This goal-setting prompt for ChatGPT produces five evidence-based guidelines, each combining psychological insights with actionable implementation steps. The prompt draws on behavioral psychology research and proven methodologies to balance ambition with sustainability. Users provide context about their current situation, constraints, and objectives, and the prompt generates customized advice that explains why each principle matters and how to apply it immediately. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok for accessible text-based output. Reach for this prompt when you need structured, psychology-informed guidance for personal development, team coaching, performance management, or educational planning. ● Delivers exactly five guidelines, each with psychological rationale and concrete application steps ● Balances challenge with achievability to sustain motivation and prevent burnout ● Grounds advice in behavioral psychology research and proven goal-setting methodologies ● Adapts to user-provided context, including individual circumstances, constraints, and objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in goal-setting frameworks and behavioral psychology, with deep understanding of what motivates people to push their limits without burning out.

## Task

Create a comprehensive set of guidelines for setting effective stretch goals that challenge individuals to excel without burning out. Tailor the guidelines to {{context}} (describe the individual or group, their current situation, constraints, and what they're working toward).

## Guidelines

Develop exactly 5 guidelines, each structured as:

**Guideline [N]: [Key Principle]**
- **Psychological Insight:** Explain why this principle matters based on behavioral psychology
- **How to Apply:** Provide clear, actionable steps for implementation

## Criteria

- Ground each guideline in proven goal-setting methodologies and behavioral psychology research
- Emphasize setting challenging goals that still feel achievable
- Warn against overly ambitious targets that undermine motivation or cause burnout
- Make advice concrete and immediately applicable
- Ensure the guidelines empower and motivate individuals to excel
```
