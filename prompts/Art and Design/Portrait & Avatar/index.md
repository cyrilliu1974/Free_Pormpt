A photorealistic direct-flash portrait in the style of 80s–90s club photography with warm film tones and documentary candid aesthetic. {{subject-description}} sits centered on a worn retro couch in a relaxed cross-leg position, holding a gaming controller naturally with both hands. Sharp focus on face and upper torso, background clearly readable but secondary. {{mood}}
