# Instagram Ad Ideas Prompt for Startups

## 簡介

The Instagram Ad Ideas Prompt for Startups is a free AI prompt that generates five unconventional, actionable Instagram ad campaigns for early-stage businesses and solopreneurs. This Instagram ad prompt for ChatGPT produces complete campaign blueprints: each idea includes a scroll-stopping visual concept (video or static), a compelling offer (discount, freebie, or organic hook), and full campaign settings covering objective, audience interests, placement, and configuration. Designed for founders executing their own marketing, it emphasizes pattern-interruption techniques that break through feed fatigue without requiring a design team or agency. Works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh ad angles fast, when budget constraints rule out creative agencies, or when you want tested performance-marketing frameworks adapted to your specific business model and pricing. ● Produces five distinct ad concepts, each with visual format, creative direction, offer structure, and campaign configuration ● Focuses on pattern-interruption tactics that stop mid-scroll without expensive production budgets ● Tailors offers and targeting recommendations to your business model and pricing structure ● Written in beginner-friendly language so non-marketers can execute every step without external help ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a performance marketer specializing in Instagram ad campaigns for early-stage businesses.

## Task
Generate 5 unconventional Instagram ad ideas that use pattern interruption and are simple enough for a solopreneur to execute independently. Each idea must include:

- **Visual**: Specify format (video or static image) and describe a scroll-stopping creative concept
- **Offer**: A compelling hook—limited-time deal, freebie, discount, or organic nudge—that aligns with the visual and business model
- **Campaign Settings**: Campaign objective, target audience interests, placement recommendations, and any critical configuration notes

## Context
{{business-and-pricing}}

## Output
Present all 5 ideas in markdown. Use clear, beginner-friendly language. Make each idea actionable without requiring a design team or agency support.
```
