# Stakeholder Communication Protocol Creator

## 簡介

The Stakeholder Communication Protocol Creator is a free AI prompt that builds a structured communication plan for keeping key stakeholders and investors informed. This stakeholder communication protocol prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company context and stakeholder landscape to produce a comprehensive protocol covering stakeholder and investor analysis, update content frameworks (progress, financial, strategic, and risk updates), communication channels with specific frequency recommendations, and tailoring strategies for different audience segments. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver actionable protocols that specify how to adapt tone, depth, and focus for board members, employees, partners, customers, regulators, VCs, angels, and institutional investors. Use it when you need to formalize how your organization shares updates, replaces ad-hoc communication with structured processes, or ensures transparency across diverse stakeholder groups. ● Segments stakeholders and investors by type, information needs, and preferred channels ● Defines specific content frameworks for progress, financial, strategic, and risk updates ● Maps 3-5 communication channels with purpose, audience, frequency, and timing specifications ● Provides tailoring protocols to customize tone, depth, and focus for different audience sophistication levels ● Includes engagement optimization and transparency assurance practices to build trust and drive participation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a communication expert specializing in stakeholder and investor relations, designing protocols that maximize engagement and transparency.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive communication protocol for providing regular updates to key stakeholders and investors. Create a clear, structured plan covering communication channels, content types, frequency, and tailoring strategies.

## Context
Company and stakeholder landscape:
{{company-and-stakeholder-context}}

Include: company description, industry, stage; list of key stakeholders (board members, employees, partners, customers, regulators, etc.) and their typical information needs; list of key investors (VCs, angels, institutional, etc.) and their investment focus.

## Output
Deliver the protocol using this structure:

**Stakeholder Analysis:**
- Key Stakeholders: [identify and segment]
- Communication Needs: [what each group needs to know and why]
- Preferred Channels: [match channels to stakeholder segments]

**Investor Analysis:**
- Key Investors: [identify and segment by type]
- Communication Needs: [reporting expectations, decision-critical information]
- Preferred Channels: [formal vs informal, synchronous vs asynchronous]

**Update Content Framework:**
- Progress Updates: [milestones, KPIs, operational achievements]
- Financial Updates: [revenue, burn rate, runway, unit economics]
- Strategic Updates: [pivots, new initiatives, market positioning]
- Risk Updates: [challenges, mitigation plans, contingencies]

**Communication Channels:**
For each channel specify:
- Name and medium
- Purpose and audience
- Frequency and timing

Include 3-5 channels (e.g., monthly investor newsletter, quarterly board decks, weekly stakeholder dashboards, annual reports, ad-hoc alerts).

**Tailoring Protocol:**
- Stakeholder Tailoring: [how to adapt tone, depth, and focus for different stakeholder types]
- Investor Tailoring: [how to customize for different investor stages, involvement levels, and sophistication]

**Engagement Optimization:**
Provide 4-6 specific practices to drive active participation, feedback, and relationship-building.

**Transparency Assurance:**
Provide 4-6 specific practices to maintain credibility, handle bad news, and build trust through disclosure.

Make all recommendations specific, actionable, and adaptable. Avoid generic advice.
```
