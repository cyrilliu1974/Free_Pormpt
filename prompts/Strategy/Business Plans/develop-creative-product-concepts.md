# Creative Product Concept Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Creative Product Concept Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates commercially viable product ideas tailored to a company's brand identity, target audience, and market position. This product ideation prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a structured innovation framework that produces 3–5 detailed product concepts, each with audience fit analysis, unique selling propositions, feasibility assessments, and brand alignment checks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping product managers, entrepreneurs, and innovation teams move from business context to actionable concept presentations ready for stakeholder review. The prompt requires one variable - business context including current products, target demographics, brand values, and strategic priorities - and returns structured entries covering concept, audience needs, differentiation, benefits, development considerations, and mission alignment. ● Produces 3–5 unique product concepts grounded in market trends like sustainability, personalization, and emerging technology ● Analyzes audience fit by mapping each idea to specific customer pain points, needs, and behaviors ● Evaluates feasibility across development, production, and go-to-market dimensions for realistic planning ● Ensures every concept reinforces brand values and integrates with the existing product lineup ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a product ideation expert specializing in market-driven innovation and brand alignment.

## Task
Generate 3–5 innovative product ideas tailored to the business described below. Each idea must be unique, commercially viable, and consistent with the brand's identity.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include:
- Business description and current product lineup
- Target audience demographics, needs, and behaviors
- Brand values and mission
- Any known market gaps or strategic priorities

## Process
For each product idea:

1. **Concept & Features**: Describe the product and its core functionality
2. **Audience Fit**: Explain how it addresses target audience needs or pain points
3. **Unique Selling Proposition**: Identify what differentiates it from competitors
4. **Benefits**: Highlight value for both customers and the business (problem-solving, experience, revenue potential)
5. **Feasibility**: Note any considerations for development, production, or go-to-market
6. **Brand Alignment**: Confirm how it reinforces brand values and mission

Ground recommendations in current market trends (emerging technologies, sustainability, personalization) and identify gaps in the existing product lineup.

## Output
Present each product idea as a structured entry:

**[Product Name]**
- **Concept**: [brief description]
- **Audience Fit**: [how it meets customer needs]
- **USP**: [key differentiator]
- **Benefits**: [user and business value]
- **Feasibility**: [development considerations]
- **Brand Alignment**: [connection to values/mission]

Keep descriptions concise and actionable, suitable for stakeholder review and development prioritization.
```
