# Product Market Fit Research Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Product Market Fit Research Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, executable market research plans for businesses validating their product-market fit. This product market fit prompt for ChatGPT walks you through designing research objectives, identifying target segments, building survey instruments, structuring customer interviews, analyzing competitors, and synthesizing findings into actionable product development recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a numbered strategy with lettered sub-steps that cover everything from sample size calculations to qualitative coding frameworks. Teams use it when launching new products, entering new markets, or refining positioning based on customer discovery research. ● Builds complete research strategies covering surveys, interviews, and competitive analysis in one cohesive plan ● Provides specific guidance on sample sizes, question types, recruitment criteria, and distribution channels ● Includes analysis frameworks for synthesizing both quantitative survey data and qualitative interview findings ● Outputs actionable recommendations structured for product development and go-to-market decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher specializing in product-market fit validation and customer discovery.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step market research strategy to validate product-market fit and inform product development decisions for {{product-and-industry}}. The strategy must cover research design, target market identification, data collection methods (surveys, interviews, competitor analysis), analysis frameworks, and actionable recommendations.

## Context
{{business-context}}

The research must be practical and executable within the constraints above, focusing on identifying market trends, understanding customer needs and preferences, assessing competitive landscape, and evaluating market potential.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with detailed sub-steps (using letters a, b, c) covering:

1. Research objectives and key questions
2. Target market identification and segmentation approach
3. Survey design (question types, distribution channels, sample size)
4. Interview protocol (recruitment criteria, question framework, documentation)
5. Competitor analysis framework (who to analyze, what dimensions, data sources)
6. Data collection timeline and execution plan
7. Analysis methodology (quantitative and qualitative)
8. Synthesis framework for drawing actionable conclusions
9. Reporting structure and decision-making recommendations

Each main point should include practical, specific guidance tailored to the business context provided.
```
