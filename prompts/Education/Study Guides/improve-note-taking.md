# Note-Taking System Builder for Students

## 簡介

The Note-Taking System Builder for Students is a free AI prompt that creates customized study frameworks designed to enhance information retention and active learning for students at any grade level. This note-taking prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the cognitive demands of a specific subject and grade, then recommends proven techniques - Cornell notes, mind mapping, outlining, or split-page methods - alongside organizational strategies and review cycles that reduce cognitive load. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured bullet-point system that covers setup and materials, during-class capture methods, post-class organization, and spaced review routines. Teachers, tutors, and educational consultants use it to build personalized study systems that match individual learning styles, address specific challenges like working memory or attention difficulties, and leverage each student's strengths. ● Recommends note-taking techniques matched to subject demands and grade-level cognitive abilities ● Provides organizational methods that simplify review and reduce study friction ● Integrates self-testing and spaced-repetition strategies proven to strengthen long-term retention ● Adapts the system to address student context such as learning differences, attention challenges, or specific academic goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in learning systems and study strategies.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive note-taking system for {{subject-and-grade}}. The system should enhance information retention, promote active learning, and facilitate efficient review.

## Context
Consider the cognitive and developmental characteristics typical of this grade level, the specific demands of the subject matter, and these student factors: {{student-context}}.

Incorporate:
- Note-taking techniques suited to the subject (e.g., Cornell, mapping, outlining, split-page)
- Organizational methods that reduce cognitive load
- Review and self-testing strategies that strengthen retention
- Adaptations that address the student's challenges and leverage their strengths

## Output
Provide the note-taking system as a bullet-point list with clear, actionable components. Structure it to cover: setup/materials, during-class capture methods, post-class organization, and review cycle.
```
