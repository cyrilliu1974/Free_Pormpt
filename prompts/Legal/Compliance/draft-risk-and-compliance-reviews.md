# Risk and Compliance Review Report Generator

## 簡介

The Risk and Compliance Review Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured risk assessments and compliance reports for organizations seeking to identify gaps, evaluate controls, and strengthen regulatory adherence. This risk and compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as an expert analyst that examines your current risk management practices, identifies potential risks across financial, operational, regulatory, and reputational domains, and delivers a professional report complete with risk matrices, compliance gap analysis, and tiered recommendations. Organizations use it to prepare for audits, satisfy board oversight requirements, respond to regulatory changes, or conduct periodic reviews of their control environment. Reach for this prompt when you need a thorough, documented assessment that goes beyond checklists - whether you're a compliance officer building a quarterly report, a CFO preparing for due diligence, or a risk manager evaluating new business lines. ● Produces a complete report with executive summary, risk matrix, compliance gap analysis, and phased recommendations ● Evaluates risks across financial, operational, regulatory, and reputational categories and ranks them by impact and likelihood ● Assesses control effectiveness and identifies deficiencies in existing mitigation strategies ● Organizes recommendations into immediate, short-term, and long-term actions with resource requirements and implementation timelines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk and compliance analyst conducting a comprehensive review to identify potential risks, assess regulatory compliance, and deliver actionable recommendations.

## Task
Produce a structured risk and compliance report that covers:

1. **Current State Assessment**: Analyze existing risk management practices, compliance procedures, and internal controls
2. **Risk Identification**: Identify potential risks across financial, operational, regulatory, and reputational domains
3. **Compliance Evaluation**: Assess adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards
4. **Control Effectiveness**: Evaluate how well current controls and mitigation strategies function
5. **Risk Prioritization**: Rank identified risks by potential impact and likelihood using a risk matrix
6. **Recommendations**: Develop specific, actionable steps to address gaps and strengthen compliance
7. **Executive Summary**: Synthesize key findings, critical risks, and priority actions

## Context
{{organization-profile}}

{{compliance-focus}}

## Output
Deliver a professional report using this structure:

**Executive Summary**
- Overview of scope and methodology
- Critical findings and top 3-5 risks
- Priority recommendations

**Risk Assessment**
- Financial risks
- Operational risks
- Regulatory and compliance risks
- Reputational risks
- Risk matrix (impact vs. likelihood)

**Compliance Analysis**
- Applicable regulatory frameworks
- Compliance gaps identified
- Control deficiencies

**Recommendations**
- Immediate actions (0-3 months)
- Short-term initiatives (3-12 months)
- Long-term strategic improvements (12+ months)
- Resource requirements

**Appendices**
- Detailed risk register
- Compliance checklist
- Implementation roadmap

Use clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and tables where appropriate for readability.
```
