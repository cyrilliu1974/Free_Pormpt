# Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven influencer marketing strategies for brands seeking to expand reach and drive sales through creator collaborations. This influencer partnership prompt for ChatGPT guides you through identifying relevant creators, analyzing audience demographics and engagement metrics, and developing customized partnership proposals that outline mutual benefits and collaboration ideas. It delivers an executive summary, a structured table of at least five influencer partnerships with detailed proposals, a timeline-based outreach plan with communication templates, and measurable KPIs to track ROI. Designed for brand managers, marketing teams, and founders building creator programs, it works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your brand details, industry, target audience, and budget range as input. Use this prompt when you need a repeatable framework for evaluating creators, crafting partnership pitches, and managing outreach at scale. ● Generates a table of five or more influencer partnership recommendations with platform, audience fit, and collaboration proposals ● Produces ready-to-send outreach templates and follow-up cadences tailored to each partnership tier ● Defines clear KPIs and success metrics to measure engagement, reach, and return on investment ● Delivers an executive summary that ties partnership strategy to brand values and business outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an influencer marketing strategist developing partnership strategies for brand expansion.

## Task
Create a comprehensive influencer partnership strategy that expands brand reach and drives sales. Research relevant influencers, analyze their audience demographics, engagement rates, and content style. Develop tailored partnership proposals outlining mutual benefits and collaboration ideas. Include a structured outreach plan with clear communication strategies and follow-up procedures.

## Context
**Brand & Market:**
{{brand-and-market}}
(Include: business name, industry, target audience demographics, brand values, and budget range for partnerships)

## Output
Deliver your strategy as:

1. **Executive Summary** — Overview of the partnership strategy and expected outcomes

2. **Influencer Partnership Table** — Minimum 5 partnerships in this format:

| Influencer Name | Social Media Platform | Partnership Proposal |
|----------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| [Name/Handle] | [Platform] | [Detailed proposal with collaboration ideas, mutual benefits, and estimated reach] |

3. **Outreach Plan** — Timeline, communication templates, and follow-up cadence for each partnership tier

4. **Success Metrics** — KPIs to track partnership performance and ROI
```
