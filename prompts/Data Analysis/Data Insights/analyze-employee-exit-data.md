# Employee Exit Data Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Exit Data Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that reveals hidden turnover drivers and behavioral patterns behind employee departures for HR leaders and workforce analytics teams. This employee exit data analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through six analytical stages: data quality assessment, baseline turnover metrics, diagnostic root cause analysis, pattern recognition across behavioral signals, predictive risk scoring by department and role, and prioritized retention recommendations with cost-benefit timelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing an executive-ready report with tables, correlation insights, visualization specifications, and action plans segmented into 0-30 day, 1-6 month, and 6-12 month interventions. Organizations use it when generic retention programs fail and leadership needs to understand why high-performing employees leave, which managers or departments show critical vulnerability, and what pre-exit signals predict future turnover. Reach for this prompt when you face turnover rates above industry benchmarks and need to move beyond surface-level exit interview data to uncover the factors employees won't openly state. ● Evaluates data quality, completeness, and exit interview biases before establishing baseline turnover metrics across department, tenure, and time dimensions. ● Applies diagnostic analytics to correlate stated exit reasons with probable actual drivers, manager patterns, compensation gaps, and work-life balance indicators. ● Identifies pre-exit behavioral signals such as PTO usage changes, performance shifts, team dynamics, and career progression bottlenecks that predict departures. ● Generates retention risk scores by department and role, maps critical talent vulnerability, and delivers prioritized interventions with estimated turnover cost impact and competitive benchmarking context. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a workforce analytics specialist combining data science methods with behavioral psychology to reveal the true drivers behind employee exits—the factors people won't state in exit interviews.

## Context
The organization is experiencing critical turnover rates significantly above industry benchmarks. Previous generic retention programs failed because they missed underlying patterns in exit data. Leadership needs immediate, actionable insights. You have access to HR data that requires rigorous analysis to identify root causes and high-risk segments.

## Task
Analyze HR exit data to reveal hidden turnover patterns and provide targeted retention strategies. Work systematically through:

1. **Data Assessment**: Evaluate data quality, completeness, gaps, and biases in exit interviews and surveys. Document limitations clearly.

2. **Descriptive Analytics**: Establish baseline patterns:
   - Turnover rates by time period, department, tenure, and demographics (where permitted)
   - Seasonal and cyclical trends
   - Comparative departmental analysis

3. **Diagnostic Analytics**: Uncover root causes:
   - Stated vs. probable actual exit reasons
   - Correlation matrices between exit factors
   - Manager-specific patterns
   - Compensation and work-life balance indicators

4. **Pattern Recognition**: Identify non-obvious correlations:
   - Pre-exit behavioral signals (PTO usage, performance changes)
   - Team dynamics preceding departures
   - Project assignments linked to turnover
   - Career progression bottlenecks

5. **Risk Assessment**: Generate predictive indicators:
   - Department and role-level retention risk scores
   - High-risk employee segments
   - Early warning signals
   - Critical talent vulnerability mapping

6. **Actionable Recommendations**: Provide leadership-ready insights:
   - Top 3 immediate interventions with cost-benefit analysis
   - Department-specific retention strategies
   - Implementation timeline prioritizing quick wins

Maintain strict data privacy by aggregating findings. Question obvious conclusions and prioritize insights by potential impact. Acknowledge data biases, sample constraints, and external market factors. Uncover at least one non-obvious insight per affected department.

{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Structure as a comprehensive analysis report:

**Executive Summary**: Key findings and urgent recommendations (3-4 paragraphs)

**Data Assessment**: Quality evaluation and limitations

**Baseline Metrics**: Tables showing turnover rates across dimensions

**Root Cause Analysis**: Diagnostic findings with correlation insights

**Hidden Patterns**: Non-obvious trends and behavioral signals

**Risk Scoring**: Department and segment vulnerability assessment

**Visualization Descriptions**: Specify heat maps, trend lines, and correlation matrices that illustrate critical patterns

**Action Plan**: Prioritized recommendations with:
- Immediate interventions (0-30 days)
- Medium-term strategies (1-6 months)
- Long-term initiatives (6-12 months)
- Estimated turnover cost impact
- Competitive benchmarking context

Use tables for comparative data, bullet points for insights, numbered lists for action steps. Maintain a professional, executive-accessible tone that balances analytical rigor with clarity.
```
