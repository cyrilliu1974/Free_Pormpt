# Natural Portrait Photography With Facial Preservation

## 簡介

The Natural Portrait Photography With Facial Preservation prompt is a free AI prompt that creates professional outdoor portraits while maintaining the exact facial structure and identity from an uploaded reference image. This portrait photography prompt for ChatGPT image models (Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana) transforms a single reference photo into multiple variations by changing clothing, architectural backgrounds, window lighting, and mood while keeping facial features, proportions, and expression identical. The prompt produces standing poses near windows with natural sunlight, soft bokeh backgrounds, and close-up framing that highlights authentic skin texture and confident expressions. Content creators use it to build consistent personal brand imagery across different settings without expensive photo shoots or location scooning. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple professional portraits that look naturally shot but share the same recognizable face - ideal for LinkedIn profiles, author bios, speaker pages, or brand imagery that requires visual consistency across platforms. ● Preserves exact facial structure, proportions, and expression from the reference image without any alteration to identity. ● Generates standing window poses with natural sunlight streaming through, creating soft highlights and calm sophisticated lighting. ● Produces sharp subject focus with soft background bokeh featuring architectural elements and outdoor scenery. ● Customizes clothing description, location and background, and mood variables to create multiple brand-consistent variations. ## Prompt
