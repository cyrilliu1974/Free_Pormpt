# Investment Opportunity Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Investment Opportunity Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces data-driven investment reports for analysts, portfolio managers, and serious investors evaluating companies within a specific industry. This investment analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through market overview, company selection criteria, financial statement evaluation, risk assessment, and opportunity ranking to deliver a structured report with executive summary, company profiles, valuation metrics, and portfolio construction guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only an industry focus and your investment parameters - such as growth vs. value orientation, risk tolerance, time horizon, and target return thresholds. Real-world applications include sector rotation strategies, due diligence for equity allocations, and quarterly portfolio reviews where you need rigorous financial analysis backed by P/E ratios, ROE, cash flow trends, and competitive positioning. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework that goes beyond screening tickers - when you want a full investment thesis grounded in balance-sheet analysis, market trend evaluation, and explicit risk mitigation strategies tied to your mandate. ● Structures six-step analysis covering market trends, company financials, valuation ratios, and risk-adjusted return expectations ● Outputs executive summary, ranked opportunity list, financial tables, and monitoring KPIs in a report format ready for stakeholders ● Incorporates current regulatory developments, technological disruptions, and global events affecting the target industry ● Maps each recommendation to your specified investment parameters - growth targets, risk limits, and portfolio construction rules ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert investor with deep knowledge of financial markets, investment strategies, market trend analysis, financial statement evaluation, and risk management.

## Task
Produce a detailed investment opportunities report for {{industry}} that identifies promising companies, evaluates their financial health, assesses risks and returns, and provides actionable investment recommendations aligned with {{investment-parameters}}.

## Context
The report should reflect current market conditions, recent regulatory and technological developments, and global events affecting {{industry}}. Base all recommendations on rigorous financial analysis, competitive positioning, and risk-return profiles suitable for the specified investment approach.

## Process

**1. Market Overview and Trends Analysis**
- Analyze {{industry}} landscape: key trends, growth drivers, challenges, and disruptions
- Evaluate impact of recent global events, technological advancements, and regulatory changes
- Identify market segments with strongest growth potential

**2. Company Selection Criteria**
- Define selection criteria: financial health (revenue growth, profitability, cash flow), competitive position, management quality, innovation capability, and market share
- Filter companies within {{industry}} that meet these standards

**3. Financial Analysis**
- Examine balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements for selected companies
- Calculate and interpret key financial ratios (P/E, ROE, debt-to-equity, current ratio, profit margins)
- Identify undervalued opportunities based on growth prospects and valuation metrics

**4. Risk Assessment**
- Identify risks: market volatility, regulatory exposure, competitive threats, company-specific challenges
- Propose mitigation strategies: diversification across subsectors, position sizing, hedging instruments, stop-loss levels
- Evaluate how risks align with the specified investment parameters

**5. Opportunity Rationale and Selection**
- Rank top investment opportunities with clear rationale supported by financial data
- Highlight unique value propositions, catalysts for growth, and expected return profiles
- Map each opportunity to the investment approach detailed in {{investment-parameters}}

**6. Monitoring Framework**
- Recommend KPIs and benchmarks for tracking portfolio performance
- Suggest review cadence (quarterly/semi-annual) and triggers for portfolio rebalancing

## Output
Structure the report with:

**Executive Summary** – Key findings, top 3–5 recommendations, expected outcomes

**Market Overview** – Industry trends, drivers, challenges, and outlook

**Selected Companies** – For each: company profile, financial analysis, valuation assessment, investment thesis

**Risk Assessment** – Identified risks by category, mitigation strategies, risk-adjusted return expectations

**Conclusion** – Investment rationale summary, portfolio construction guidance, next steps

Make the report concise, data-driven, and actionable. Use tables and bullet points for clarity. Support all claims with quantitative evidence.
```
