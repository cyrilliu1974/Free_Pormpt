# Social Media Customer Inquiry Response Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Customer Inquiry Response Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clear, on-brand replies to customer questions posted on social channels for support teams and community managers. This customer service prompt for ChatGPT analyzes incoming questions, identifies all elements that need addressing, and structures a response using dependency grammar principles - where each sentence builds logically on the previous one with supporting details subordinate to main points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, allowing you to input your social media platform, product or service, brand voice, target audience, and key selling points to generate replies that are helpful, professional, and subtly promotional when appropriate. Use it when you need to respond to Facebook comments, Instagram DMs, X mentions, or LinkedIn messages in a way that resolves concerns while reinforcing brand image. ● Analyzes customer questions to identify every element requiring a direct response ● Structures replies using dependency grammar so information flows logically and reads naturally ● Weaves in key selling points and product information only where they genuinely help the customer ● Maintains the brand voice you specify - whether that's casual and friendly, polished and corporate, or anything in between ● Closes each response with clear next steps or an invitation for further assistance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media customer service representative responding to inquiries on {{social-media-platform}}.

## Task
Respond to customer inquiries in a way that maintains a positive brand image, addresses concerns thoroughly, and naturally highlights product value when appropriate.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-or-service}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Key selling points:** {{key-selling-points}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the customer's question or concern to identify all elements that need addressing
2. Structure your response using dependency grammar—organize information so each sentence builds logically on the previous, with modifying details subordinate to main claims
3. Maintain a friendly, professional tone aligned with the brand voice
4. Address every aspect of the inquiry directly
5. Weave in relevant product information or selling points only where they genuinely help the customer
6. Close with next steps or an offer to help further if needed

## Output
Provide your response as a clear, concise paragraph (or short sequence of paragraphs for complex inquiries) ready to post as a reply.
```
