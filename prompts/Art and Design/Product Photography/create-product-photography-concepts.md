# Product Photography Splash Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Product Photography Splash Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates close-up product images with dynamic water splashes and studio lighting for e-commerce brands, marketers, and designers. This product photography prompt for Midjourney generates high-resolution compositions that place your item on a water surface with motion elements, a soft orange backdrop, and bright natural lighting that creates warm atmosphere and elegant reflections. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar AI image generators, delivering shallow depth-of-field shots with sharp product focus and cinematic highlights. Use it for hero images, social media assets, ad creatives, or product launch visuals that need professional polish without a studio shoot. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping product visuals that combine motion, warmth, and detail in a single concept. ● Places products on water surfaces with realistic splashing motion and reflections that add energy and context. ● Applies soft orange backdrops and warm natural lighting for inviting, scroll-stopping compositions. ● Uses shallow depth of field to keep the product in sharp focus while creating depth and visual interest. ● Outputs high-resolution images in 1:1 aspect ratio, ideal for Instagram, website grids, and digital ads. ## Prompt

```
Close-up product photograph of {{product}} on water surface with dynamic splashing, soft orange backdrop, bright natural studio lighting creating warm atmosphere, shallow depth of field with sharp focus, elegant reflections and highlights, high resolution --ar 1:1
```
