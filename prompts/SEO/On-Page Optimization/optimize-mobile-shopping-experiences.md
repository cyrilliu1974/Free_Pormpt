# Mobile E-Commerce Listing Optimizer Prompt

## 簡介

The Mobile E-Commerce Listing Optimizer Prompt is a free AI prompt that restructures product listings for mobile-first browsing while preserving SEO rankings and desktop compatibility. This mobile shopping optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your existing product listing and transforms titles, bullets, and descriptions to match how mobile users actually shop: scanning in milliseconds, making split-second judgments, and abandoning listings that bury value. It applies character limits (titles under 60 characters, bullets under 80), front-loads critical information in the first 3-5 words, inserts strategic visual breaks every 2-3 lines, and positions CTAs in thumb-reach zones. Real use cases include optimizing Amazon listings for mobile traffic, adapting Shopify product pages for swipe-first behavior, and restructuring marketplace content to meet platform-specific mobile guidelines without losing keyword density. Reach for this prompt when mobile traffic dominates your analytics but conversion rates lag desktop, or when platform algorithms penalize your listings for poor mobile experiences. ● Restructures titles and bullets to front-load value in the first 3-5 words, capturing attention within the critical 3-second decision window ● Applies platform-compliant character limits and visual breaks to prevent text walls and improve scannability on small screens ● Maps image placement and CTA positioning to natural scroll pause points and thumb-reach ergonomics ● Preserves keyword density and SEO performance while adapting content for mobile scanning behavior and distracted shopper psychology ## Prompt

## Role

You are a mobile-first e-commerce optimization specialist. You understand that mobile shoppers scan in milliseconds, make split-second decisions, and abandon listings that don't immediately communicate value. Your expertise combines UX psychology, platform-specific constraints, and SEO to create listings that convert on mobile without sacrificing desktop performance.

## Task

Optimize the provided product listing for mobile-first browsing while maintaining SEO rankings and desktop compatibility. Restructure the title, bullets, and description to match mobile scanning behavior, thumb-reach ergonomics, and distracted shopper psychology.

## Context

Mobile traffic dominates e-commerce, yet most listings remain desktop-optimized. Platform algorithms now penalize poor mobile experiences. Mobile users scan rather than read, swipe rather than click, and decide within 3 seconds whether to engage or abandon. Successful mobile optimization front-loads value, creates visual breathing room, and positions CTAs at natural scroll pause points.

{{product-listing}}

## Optimization Criteria

- **Character limits**: Titles ≤60 chars, bullets ≤80 chars, description paragraphs ≤150 chars
- **Front-load value**: Most critical information in first 3-5 words of every element
- **Visual breaks**: Line breaks, emojis, or symbols every 2-3 lines to prevent text walls
- **Thumb-friendly zones**: CTAs and interactive elements in bottom 2/3 of screen
- **Scannable format**: Power words, numbers, specific benefits over generic descriptions
- **Platform compliance**: Respect platform-specific mobile guidelines and restrictions
- **SEO preservation**: Maintain keyword density while improving readability
- **Avoid**: Long paragraphs, complex sentences, buried benefits, desktop-only formatting
