# Email Marketing Campaign Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Campaign Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email marketing strategies designed to increase online sales for digital marketers and e-commerce businesses. This email marketing campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a multi-touch nurture sequence tailored to your product, audience, and unique selling proposition. It delivers a structured strategy covering audience analysis (demographics, pain points, motivations), email sequence architecture (timing, purpose, touchpoints), content strategy (subject lines, body copy, CTAs), personalization tactics, A/B testing recommendations, and success metrics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable marketing plans that address real customer needs while moving leads systematically through the sales funnel. Reach for this prompt when you need to develop or refine an email campaign strategy from scratch, especially when launching new products or targeting specific audience segments with defined conversion goals. ● Generates detailed audience analysis including demographics, behavioral patterns, pain points, and purchase motivations ● Designs multi-email nurture sequences with specific timing, purpose, and conversion objectives for each touchpoint ● Creates content strategies with subject line themes, body copy approaches, and calls-to-action aligned to funnel stages ● Delivers personalization variables, segmentation logic, A/B testing plans, and measurable KPIs for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist developing a campaign to increase online sales.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email marketing strategy that converts the target audience through a structured nurture sequence.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-or-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Campaign Goals:** {{campaign-goals}}

Analyze the target audience's demographics, preferences, and pain points. Craft email content that addresses these factors while emphasizing the unique value proposition. Design a multi-email sequence that nurtures leads through the sales funnel.

## Output
Deliver your strategy with clear headings and bullet points covering:

- **Audience Analysis** – demographics, behavioral patterns, pain points, and motivations
- **Email Sequence Architecture** – number of emails, timing, purpose of each touchpoint
- **Content Strategy** – subject lines, body copy themes, calls-to-action for each email
- **Personalization & Segmentation** – variables to personalize, audience segments to target differently
- **Optimization Plan** – A/B testing recommendations (subject lines, send times, content variants)
- **Success Metrics** – KPIs to track, expected improvements over baseline performance

Ensure all copy is clear, persuasive, and structured for maximum readability and conversion impact.
```
