# Delivery Service Business Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Delivery Service Business Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a structured, actionable guide for launching and operating a delivery service as a solopreneur or side hustle. This delivery service business plan prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every decision in logical order: market research and competitive analysis come first, followed by vehicle selection and maintenance planning, then delivery area optimization, pricing strategies, customer acquisition tactics, and time management systems. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a hierarchical guide with clear headings, bullet-point action steps, and recommendations tailored to your specific location, budget, target market, available resources, and time constraints. Use it when you need a roadmap that respects real-world limitations and prioritizes what matters most for a part-time or solo delivery operation. ● Produces market research frameworks and competitive positioning strategies specific to your local delivery landscape ● Recommends vehicle options, maintenance schedules, and delivery area routing that match your budget and time availability ● Delivers pricing models that balance market competitiveness with profitability for solo operators ● Outlines customer acquisition channels and retention techniques suited to small-scale delivery businesses ● Includes time management systems designed for balancing delivery work with other professional or personal commitments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in delivery services, with deep knowledge of solopreneur operations and side-hustle economics.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable guide for launching and operating a successful delivery service as a solopreneur or side hustle. Structure the guide using clear dependency relationships: foundational decisions (market research, vehicle selection) must precede tactical execution (pricing, customer acquisition), which in turn enables operational optimization (time management, area routing).

## Context
{{business-context}}

The guide must address:
- Market research and competitive positioning
- Vehicle selection and maintenance considerations
- Delivery area optimization and route planning
- Pricing strategies that balance competitiveness with profitability
- Customer acquisition and retention techniques
- Time management systems for balancing delivery work with other commitments

## Output
Deliver the guide with:
- Clear hierarchical headings (##) and subheadings (###)
- Bullet points for actionable steps and key insights
- Logical progression from research and planning through launch to ongoing operations
- Specific recommendations tailored to the location, budget, target market, available resources, and time constraints provided
- Implementation priorities that respect resource and time limitations
```
