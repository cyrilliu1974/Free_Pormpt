# Cinematic Office Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Office Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional executive portraits with golden hour lighting and high-rise city backdrops for entrepreneurs, coaches, and corporate brands. The prompt produces images of a subject at a sleek dark marble desk with a MacBook Pro, leather notebook, and golden pen, framed by tall windows and warm bokeh lighting effects that mimic high-end commercial photography. This cinematic office portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by specifying camera angle, depth-of-field, lighting conditions, wardrobe details, and environmental elements to deliver empowering, success-focused compositions. Photographers, marketers, and professionals building LinkedIn profiles or corporate websites reach for it when they need polished visual assets without studio costs. ● Specifies golden hour lighting through tall office windows with soft bokeh and warm circular reflections for a visionary, cinematic look. ● Includes detailed desk setup with dark marble surface, silver MacBook Pro, leather notebook, and golden pen to communicate professionalism. ● Uses slightly low camera angle and depth-of-field control to create empowering, inspiring compositions. ● Accepts a subject-description variable so you can tailor wardrobe, demographics, and branding to your exact needs. ## Prompt

```
A cinematic, professionally photographed office scene featuring {{subject-description}}, sitting at a sleek dark marble desk. The subject is wearing a tailored charcoal blazer over a white collared shirt. Warm golden hour lighting pours through tall office windows offering a high-rise city view with softly blurred buildings in the background. The subject lightly interacts with an open silver MacBook Pro displaying a graph. A dark leather notebook and a stylish golden pen are neatly placed on the desk. Strong depth-of-field bokeh with large, warm, circular light reflections creates a visionary, movie-like aesthetic. Camera angle is slightly low and empowering. The overall mood is inspiring, focused, and successful. Shot in cinematic professional photography style with golden hour lighting.
```
