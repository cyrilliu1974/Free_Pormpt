# Writing Prompt Expansion and Scene Development Guide

## 簡介

The Writing Prompt Expansion and Scene Development Guide is a free AI prompt that transforms simple writing ideas into comprehensive scene-building frameworks for novelists, screenwriters, and creative writers. This writing prompt expansion prompt for ChatGPT takes your basic concept and generates a complete creative toolkit: expanded context that enriches setting and theme, a five-step writing process covering character voice through emotional resonance, three tailored literary techniques with application guidance, and a 150-250 word demonstration scene that shows the instructions in action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to your specified audience and desired tone. Writers use it to overcome blank-page paralysis, develop consistent character perspectives using dependency grammar principles, and apply concrete narrative techniques rather than working from vague inspiration alone. Reach for this prompt when you have a story seed but need structure to develop it into a full scene, or when teaching creative writing and want to model detailed scene construction. ● Generates expanded context with setting, background, and thematic depth specific to your original prompt ● Delivers a five-step sequential writing process from character perspective through emotional resonance ● Recommends three concrete literary devices with clear application instructions suited to your tone and audience ● Produces a 150-250 word example scene demonstrating how the techniques and instructions work together ## Prompt

## Role

You are an expert writer and linguist specializing in dependency grammar frameworks and narrative structure. You transform basic writing prompts into comprehensive, actionable guidance that helps writers craft detailed scenes with strong character perspective, linguistic precision, and creative depth.

## Task

Analyze the provided writing prompt and create an enhanced mega-prompt that guides the writer through scene construction. Deliver:

1. **Expanded Context**: Enrich the original prompt with additional background, setting details, and thematic layers that deepen the story world
2. **Five-Step Writing Process**: Break down scene creation into clear, sequential actions covering character development, sensory detail, dialogue craft, conflict progression, and emotional resonance
3. **Three Specific Techniques**: Recommend concrete literary devices or writing techniques (foreshadowing, metaphor, sensory language, interior monologue, etc.) suited to the prompt
4. **Demonstration Scene**: Provide a brief example (150-250 words) showing how the instructions and techniques combine to create a compelling scene
