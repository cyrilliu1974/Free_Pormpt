# Luxury Automotive Grid Advertisement Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Automotive Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates nine-panel grid layouts for premium sedan marketing campaigns. It produces a cohesive square 1×1 advertisement split into equal tiles, each highlighting a different facet of the vehicle: front hero shot, signature grille and LED headlight details, sculpted side profile, luxury cabin interior, digital dashboard technology, leather stitching macro, driving experience in golden-hour light, rear taillight design, and aspirational lifestyle context. This luxury automotive grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana lets you specify brand name, color palette, and location setting to match your campaign identity. Ideal for car brands, dealerships, and marketing agencies creating social media grid takeovers, showroom displays, or editorial-style brand content without the cost of a full production shoot. ● Produces nine distinct panels in a single square composition - exterior hero angles, interior craftsmanship, technology UI, material close-ups, and lifestyle scenes - all styled with professional automotive photography lighting and cinematic realism. ● Customizable variables for brand name, color palette, and location setting ensure each grid aligns with your visual identity and campaign goals. ● Includes detailed negative prompts to avoid distorted proportions, oversaturated colors, CGI artifacts, and cluttered compositions, ensuring clean, high-end results. ● Delivers editorial-grade assets suitable for Instagram grid posts, digital showroom displays, print brochures, and luxury brand storytelling. ## Prompt

```
Premium 3×3 grid advertisement for {{brand-name}} luxury sedan. Square 1×1 aspect ratio, nine equal panels showcasing refined design, advanced technology, and elite lifestyle appeal.

**Panel 1 – Hero Exterior Presence:** Front three-quarter hero shot, cinematic directional lighting, {{color-palette}} architectural backdrop, authority and prestige.

**Panel 2 – Signature Front Detail:** Close-up of grille, emblem, and LED signature headlights emphasizing brand identity and craftsmanship.

**Panel 3 – Body Line Elegance:** Side profile with soft highlight tracing sculpted silhouette, flowing elegant lines.

**Panel 4 – Luxury Interior Cabin:** Wide interior shot of seats, dashboard, ambient lighting, {{color-palette}} materials, comfort and refinement.

**Panel 5 – Technology & Control:** Close-up of high-resolution digital dashboard and infotainment system, intuitive UI, innovation and precision.

**Panel 6 – Seat & Material Detail:** Macro shot of leather stitching and seat contours, shallow depth of field, tactile luxury craftsmanship.

**Panel 7 – Driving Experience:** {{location-setting}} backdrop, golden-hour daylight, power and effortless motion.

**Panel 8 – Rear Design & Lighting:** Rear view highlighting LED taillights and confident stance, low-light cinematic glow.

**Panel 9 – Lifestyle Prestige:** {{location-setting}} environment, status and exclusivity.

Style: Professional luxury automotive photography, cinematic realism, editorial clean composition, ultra-premium branding. Natural daylight combined with cinematic highlights, balanced exposure with rich highlights and deep shadows. Brand personality: elegant, authoritative, refined. Minimal chrome or illuminated emblem presentation.

Negative prompt: Cheap materials, distorted car proportions, overly glossy paint, unreadable screens, cluttered scenes, harsh reflections, CGI or cartoon style, oversaturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
