# Strategic Risk Vulnerability Assessment Prompt

## 簡介

The Strategic Risk Vulnerability Assessment Prompt is a free AI prompt that uncovers hidden failure points and cascading risks in strategic plans for business leaders, project managers, and strategic planners. This strategic risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT systematically examines five critical vulnerability areas: operational weaknesses, resource blind spots, timing dependencies, human factors, and external threats. It generates a structured analysis that highlights not only individual risks but also interconnected failure points where one problem could trigger multiple cascading issues. The prompt requires your plan details as input and produces a comprehensive risk profile with exactly two actionable mitigation strategies for each identified vulnerability. It runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text AI platforms. Reach for this prompt when reviewing strategic initiatives, business plans, product launches, or organizational changes where optimistic assumptions may hide critical vulnerabilities. It is especially valuable for stress-testing plans before resource commitments or when stakeholder alignment depends on demonstrating thorough risk management. ● Examines five distinct risk categories - operational, resource, timing, human factors, and external threats - to ensure comprehensive coverage ● Delivers exactly two concrete, actionable mitigation strategies per identified risk, focused on implementation rather than abstraction ● Highlights cascading failure points where interconnected risks could amplify damage across multiple areas ● Structures output in a standardized format that supports prioritization based on probability and impact ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic risk assessment specialist identifying failure points that optimistic planning overlooks. You analyze operational, financial, human, and external factors that create cascading failures.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive risk assessment of the provided plan to identify potential failure points and blind spots. For each identified risk, provide exactly two specific, actionable mitigation strategies. Focus on practical, implementable solutions. Prioritize risks based on probability and potential impact.

## Context

{{plan-details}}

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Risk Assessment Overview**  
Summary of the plan's overall risk profile and vulnerability areas

**Operational Risks**  
● Risk: [Specific operational vulnerability]  
  - Mitigation Strategy 1: [Concrete action to prevent/reduce risk]  
  - Mitigation Strategy 2: [Alternative concrete action to prevent/reduce risk]

**Resource Blind Spots**  
● Blind Spot: [Hidden resource-related vulnerability]  
  - Avoidance Strategy 1: [Specific action to address blind spot]  
  - Avoidance Strategy 2: [Alternative specific action to address blind spot]

**Timing Vulnerabilities**  
● Vulnerability: [Time-sensitive risk or scheduling blind spot]  
  - Prevention Strategy 1: [Concrete timing-related solution]  
  - Prevention Strategy 2: [Alternative concrete timing-related solution]

**Human Factor Risks**  
● Risk: [People-related vulnerability or behavioral blind spot]  
  - Management Strategy 1: [Specific approach to address human factors]  
  - Management Strategy 2: [Alternative specific approach to address human factors]

**External Threats**  
● Threat: [Market, competitive, or environmental risk]  
  - Response Strategy 1: [Concrete external risk mitigation]  
  - Response Strategy 2: [Alternative concrete external risk mitigation]

**Cascading Failure Points**  
Critical interconnected risks where one failure could trigger multiple problems and recommended safeguards

Use clear, direct language emphasizing practical prevention. Provide realistic evaluation of potential challenges.
```
