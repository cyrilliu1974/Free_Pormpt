# Automated Email Sequence Builder for Lead Nurturing

## 簡介

The Automated Email Sequence Builder for Lead Nurturing is a free AI prompt that creates complete drip campaign structures for businesses looking to convert prospects into customers through systematic outreach. This automated email sequence prompt for ChatGPT generates 5-8 strategically timed emails that build on each other, with each message assuming knowledge from the previous one. You provide your business context, product USP, and customer journey; the prompt returns a numbered sequence with subject lines, core messages, value offers, CTAs, and timing triggers tailored to each funnel stage (awareness, consideration, decision). Marketers use it to design onboarding campaigns, product launch sequences, abandoned-cart recovery flows, and nurture tracks that systematically address pain points and objections. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok for text-based campaign planning. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework for multi-touch email campaigns that move leads through a defined journey rather than sending one-off blasts. ● Generates complete sequences with subject lines, message content, offers, CTAs, and send timing for each email ● Applies dependency grammar so later emails reference and build upon earlier touchpoints ● Maps email content to specific funnel stages with appropriate calls-to-action for awareness, consideration, and decision phases ● Customizes sequences based on your unique customer journey, product positioning, and audience pain points ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in automated sequence design that nurtures leads and drives conversions.

## Task
Create a comprehensive automated email sequence for {{business-and-audience}}. The sequence must guide recipients from awareness through conversion, with each email building logically on the previous message.

## Context
Product/service and unique selling proposition:
{{product-and-usp}}

Ideal customer journey:
{{customer-journey}}

## Requirements
- Structure each email with clear dependency logic: later messages assume knowledge and actions from earlier ones
- Personalize content to address audience pain points, aspirations, and objections at each funnel stage
- Include relevant offers that deliver genuine value (educational content, limited-time incentives, social proof)
- Embed clear, action-oriented CTAs appropriate to each stage (awareness: learn more; consideration: try/demo; decision: buy now)
- Specify timing between emails and trigger conditions where applicable

## Output
Deliver the sequence as a numbered list:

1. **Email [number]** – [Stage: Awareness/Consideration/Decision]
   a. Subject line
   b. Core message and value proposition
   c. Offer or content included
   d. Call-to-action
   e. Timing/trigger

2. **Email [number]** – [Stage]
   ...

Continue through the complete funnel, typically 5-8 emails depending on complexity of the customer journey provided.
```
