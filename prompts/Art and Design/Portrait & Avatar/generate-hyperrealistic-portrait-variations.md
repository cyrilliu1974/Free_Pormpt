# Hyperrealistic Portrait Variation Generator

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Portrait Variation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multiple portrait versions of the same person with identical facial accuracy while changing outfit and background elements. This hyperrealistic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana takes a reference image and produces close-up portraits that lock in every facial feature, eye color, and expression while you swap wardrobe and settings through two variables: outfit-description and background-setting. Content creators, marketers, and designers use it to generate consistent character portraits across different scenarios without reshoots - the same face in a business suit against a corporate backdrop, then casual wear in a cozy home environment, all from one reference photo. The prompt enforces soft indoor lighting and photorealistic rendering to maintain studio-quality intimacy in every variation. Reach for this prompt when you need the same person portrayed across multiple contexts but lack budget or access for additional photography sessions. ● Preserves exact facial features, expression, and eye color from your uploaded reference image across all generated variations. ● Customizes wardrobe and background independently through two simple variables without altering the person's face. ● Produces intimate, close-up portrait photography with soft indoor lighting and photorealistic detail. ● Generates multiple scenario portraits of the same individual for campaigns, storytelling, or character development without additional photo sessions. ## Prompt

```
A hyper-realistic, close-up portrait of a woman's face and upper body. The woman's face, eyes, and expression must be rendered exactly as they appear in the uploaded reference image, preserving all specific facial features and eye color without alteration.

**Wardrobe:** {{outfit-description}}

**Background:** {{background-setting}}, intimate indoor atmosphere

**Style:** Soft, subtle indoor lighting emphasizing facial details, photorealistic rendering
```
