# Research Paper Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Research Paper Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete research paper structures with thesis statements and detailed outlines for academic writers and students. This research paper outline prompt for ChatGPT produces a formatted academic outline that includes a specific, arguable thesis statement, an introduction section, three to four body sections with three key discussion points each, and a conclusion. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each section builds logically on the previous one, creating a coherent roadmap for writing the full paper. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it adaptable to your preferred AI writing assistant. Use it when starting a new research project, organizing existing research notes, or restructuring a paper that lacks clear direction. ● Creates thesis statements that encapsulate a paper's central argument in one specific, arguable sentence ● Structures outlines with introduction, body sections, and conclusion, each containing three supporting discussion points ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure logical progression and coherent argumentation between sections ● Outputs a clear, hierarchical format that serves as an actionable roadmap for drafting the complete research paper ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic writer specializing in research paper structure and thesis development.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive thesis statement and detailed outline for the research topic below. Apply dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and coherent argumentation throughout the structure.

## Context
Research topic: {{research-topic}}

## Requirements
- Craft a specific, arguable thesis statement that encapsulates the paper's central argument
- Structure the outline with Introduction, 3-4 body sections, and Conclusion
- Include 3 key discussion points per section that directly support the thesis
- Ensure each section builds logically on the previous one using dependency grammar framework
- Provide sufficient detail to serve as a clear roadmap for writing the full paper

## Output
Deliver the outline in this format:

**Thesis Statement:**
[Thesis statement]

**Outline:**

I. Introduction
   A. [Key point 1]
   B. [Key point 2]
   C. [Key point 3]

II. [Section 1 Title]
    A. [Key point 1]
    B. [Key point 2]
    C. [Key point 3]

III. [Section 2 Title]
     A. [Key point 1]
     B. [Key point 2]
     C. [Key point 3]

IV. [Section 3 Title]
    A. [Key point 1]
    B. [Key point 2]
    C. [Key point 3]

V. [Additional Section Title if needed]
   A. [Key point 1]
   B. [Key point 2]
   C. [Key point 3]

VI. Conclusion
    A. [Key point 1]
    B. [Key point 2]
    C. [Key point 3]
```
