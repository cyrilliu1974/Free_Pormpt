# Audience-Centric Tagline Generator for Brand Strategy

## 簡介

The Audience-Centric Tagline Generator for Brand Strategy is a free AI prompt that creates multiple taglines aligned with business objectives and tailored to specific audience segments. This tagline prompt for ChatGPT produces a strategic table of five distinct taglines, each crafted for a different target audience within your business context. The prompt evaluates each option across four dimensions: the tagline itself (maximum 8 words), its intended audience segment, how it supports your business goals, and a 1-10 impact score reflecting brand positioning potential. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring each tagline addresses specific customer needs while avoiding generic or cliché phrasing. Marketing teams use it to develop emotionally compelling brand messages that balance creative appeal with measurable strategic value. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple positioning options for diverse customer segments or when launching campaigns that require audience-specific messaging variations. ● Creates five distinct taglines, each optimized for a different customer segment within your business context. ● Evaluates strategic alignment by linking each tagline to specific business objectives and assigning a market impact score. ● Enforces an 8-word maximum to ensure concise, memorable phrasing that avoids generic language. ● Delivers results in a comparison table format that simplifies decision-making for marketing teams and brand managers. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing strategist and brand storyteller creating taglines that align with business objectives and resonate with target audiences.

## Task
Generate 5 distinct taglines, each tailored to a different audience segment within the provided context. Evaluate each tagline's strategic alignment and market impact potential.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Each tagline must:
- Be concise (8 words maximum)
- Address the specific needs, desires, or pain points of its target audience
- Support one or more stated business objectives
- Be memorable, emotionally compelling, and original
- Avoid generic or cliché phrasing

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with 5 rows (one per tagline) and 4 columns:

| Tagline | Target Audience | Strategic Value | Impact Score (1-10) |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| [Your tagline] | [Specific audience segment] | [How it supports business objectives] | [1-10 rating] |

The Impact Score (1-10) reflects the tagline's potential effect on brand positioning and market performance, with 10 representing highest impact. Prioritize taglines that balance audience resonance with measurable business impact.
```
