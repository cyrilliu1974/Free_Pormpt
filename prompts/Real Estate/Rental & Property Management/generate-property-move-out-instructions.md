# Property Move-Out Instructions Generator

## 簡介

The Property Move-Out Instructions Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clear, enforceable move-out documentation for landlords and property managers protecting their rental investments. This property move-out instructions prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured guides that eliminate ambiguity around tenant responsibilities at lease end. It generates room-by-room cleaning standards, timeline checklists starting two weeks before move-out, utility transfer procedures, and self-verification lists tenants can use before final inspection. Property managers use it to standardize expectations across portfolios, while individual landlords rely on it to prevent the disputes that arise from vague language like "broom clean" or "normal wear and tear." Reach for this prompt whenever you need move-out documentation that balances legal enforceability with tenant fairness, reducing conflict while protecting your security deposit deductions. ● Defines precise cleaning requirements for every appliance, surface, and area with no vague terms, making standards legally defensible. ● Creates day-by-day timelines with specific tasks, deadlines, and consequences tied to security deposit deductions. ● Generates printable checklists tenants can follow during their cleaning process to self-verify compliance before inspection. ● Covers exterior responsibilities, utility transfers, key return, forwarding address submission, and final walk-through scheduling in one document. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert property management consultant and tenant relations specialist with deep experience overseeing successful lease transitions across residential properties.

## Task

Create comprehensive, legally sound move-out instructions that protect the landlord's interests while maintaining positive tenant relationships. The instructions must be specific enough to be legally enforceable yet reasonable enough that tenants can realistically comply.

## Context

Ambiguous move-out expectations are the primary source of security deposit disputes and tenant conflicts. These instructions will eliminate interpretation gaps and provide clear, actionable guidance tenants can follow.

**Property Details:**
{{property-details}}

**Legal & Financial Framework:**
{{legal-financial-context}}

## Requirements

- Establish exact condition standards for property return, covering every area of the unit with specific cleaning and maintenance requirements
- Define what "clean" means for each appliance, surface, and area—no vague terms
- Include exterior responsibilities (lawn care, snow removal, trash disposal) where applicable
- Specify whether professional cleaning is required or if tenant cleaning to specific standards is acceptable
- Create a timeline starting two weeks before move-out with specific tasks and deadlines
- Address utility transfer procedures, key return protocols, forwarding address requirements, and final walk-through scheduling
- Include consequences for non-compliance tied directly to security deposit deductions with specific dollar amounts where possible
- Provide a checklist format tenants can use to self-verify completion before final inspection

## Output

Structure the move-out instructions with:

- Clear section headings for each area and responsibility
- Numbered lists for sequential tasks and timelines
- Bullet points for requirements within each area
- A final printable move-out checklist tenants can use during their cleaning process

Ensure the tone is professional, clear, and fair—designed to prevent disputes rather than create adversarial relationships.
```
