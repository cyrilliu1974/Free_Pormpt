# Interview Question Generator for Deep Conversations

## 簡介

The Interview Question Generator for Deep Conversations is a free AI prompt that produces structured, open-ended interview questionnaires designed to reveal how an interviewee's ideas connect and depend on one another. This interview questions prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar principles to craft questions that expose relationships between concepts rather than surface facts. It generates five subtopics with three progressively deeper questions each, moving from primary observations to contextual factors to implications. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is ideal for journalists, researchers, podcast hosts, HR professionals, and anyone conducting interviews where depth matters more than checklists. ● Produces 15 open-ended questions organized into five thematic subtopics for logical flow ● Applies dependency grammar to frame questions that uncover how concepts influence and shape one another ● Sequences questions to progress from surface observations to underlying mechanisms within each subtopic ● Outputs a ready-to-use questionnaire formatted with subtopic headers and numbered questions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert interviewer who crafts open-ended questions that elicit detailed, nuanced responses. You apply dependency grammar principles—structuring questions around core predicates and their semantic arguments—to reveal how the interviewee's ideas connect and depend on one another.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive interview questionnaire for {{interviewee-name}} on {{topic}}. Organize the questionnaire into five subtopics, each containing three questions. Questions should flow logically within each subtopic and cover a wide range of angles related to the main topic.

## Question Design Criteria
- Open-ended: require detailed, thoughtful responses rather than yes/no answers
- Dependency-focused: structure questions to expose relationships between concepts (e.g., "How does X influence Y?" "What factors shape your approach to Z?")
- Progressively deeper: move from surface observations to underlying mechanisms within each subtopic
- Logically sequenced: each question builds on or complements the previous one
```
