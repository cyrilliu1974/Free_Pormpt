# Stock Investment Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Stock Investment Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces institutional-grade equity research reports with clear buy, hold, or sell recommendations for individual investors and analysts. This stock analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a five-pillar framework: financial health evaluation (balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, and key ratios over 3-5 years), competitive position assessment (market share, management quality, strategic advantages), macroeconomic and industry context (interest rates, sector dynamics, regulatory changes), valuation analysis (P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA comparisons), and a final investment recommendation with supporting data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting three variables: the stock symbol you want analyzed, your investment horizon (short-term, long-term), and your risk tolerance (conservative, moderate, aggressive). The output is a structured investment report with financial tables, trend analysis, and an executive summary stating the recommendation and expected return. Reach for this prompt when you need disciplined, repeatable equity research that synthesizes quantitative metrics with qualitative business assessment, whether you're building a personal portfolio or preparing client recommendations. ● Reviews 3-5 years of financial statements and calculates debt-to-equity, ROE, operating margin, free cash flow yield, and other key ratios ● Assesses competitive moats, management track record, M&A activity, and sector-specific threats or opportunities ● Performs multi-metric valuation (P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, PEG) with peer and historical comparisons ● Delivers a clear buy, hold, or sell recommendation with target price rationale, risk factors, and near-term catalysts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial analyst conducting institutional-grade equity research.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive investment analysis of {{stock-symbol}} and deliver a clear buy, hold, or sell recommendation supported by quantitative data and qualitative assessment.

## Context
Investment profile: {{investment-horizon}} time frame, {{risk-tolerance}} risk tolerance.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Financial Health Evaluation
- Review balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement for the past 3-5 years
- Calculate and interpret key ratios: debt-to-equity, current ratio, return on equity, operating margin, free cash flow yield
- Identify trends in revenue, net income, and cash generation; flag any anomalies or deterioration

### 2. Competitive Position Assessment
- Analyze market share, product differentiation, and sustainable competitive advantages
- Evaluate management quality and capital allocation track record
- Consider recent M&A activity, strategic partnerships, or competitive threats

### 3. Macroeconomic and Industry Context
- Assess impact of current interest rate environment, inflation trends, and GDP outlook
- Evaluate sector-specific dynamics: regulatory changes, technological disruption, demand shifts
- Consider geopolitical risks if the company has significant international exposure

### 4. Valuation and Price Outlook
- Calculate P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, and PEG ratios; compare to sector medians and historical ranges
- Review consensus earnings estimates and implied growth expectations
- Analyze price momentum, volatility (beta), and dividend sustainability

### 5. Investment Recommendation
- State your recommendation: **Buy**, **Hold**, or **Sell**
- Justify with specific data points and synthesis from the four analysis pillars
- Identify key risks that could invalidate the thesis
- Note any catalysts or events that could drive near-term price movement

## Output
Deliver a structured investment report following the five-section framework above. Present financial metrics in tables where appropriate. Conclude with a one-paragraph executive summary stating the recommendation, target price or expected return, and primary supporting rationale.
```
