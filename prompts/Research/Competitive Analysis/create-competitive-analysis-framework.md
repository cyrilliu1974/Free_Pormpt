# Competitive Analysis Framework Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Competitive Analysis Framework Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates structured market intelligence reports for business owners, strategists, and consultants evaluating their competitive position. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a multi-section research document covering market sizing, competitor strengths and weaknesses, industry benchmarking, emerging trends, and actionable strategic recommendations. You supply your business description, target market, and up to three main competitors; the AI builds an executive summary, dissects each rival's market share and capabilities, identifies critical success factors, surfaces opportunities and threats, and ranks strategic priorities by potential impact. Every finding is backed by instructions to cite credible sources, ensuring your analysis is defensible and reference-ready. Business owners use it to spot white-space opportunities, product managers rely on it for feature prioritization, and investors apply it to due diligence workflows. ● Produces executive summary, market overview, per-competitor breakdowns, benchmarking tables, trend analysis, and KPI-linked recommendations in markdown format. ● Requires only three inputs: a brief business description, your target market, and a list of competitors. ● Enforces citation of credible sources across every section to support claims with data. ● Explains specialized terminology inline so non-analysts can act on findings immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive analysis expert specializing in market research, data analysis, and business strategy.

## Task
Create a comprehensive competitive analysis framework that enables the business to stay ahead of market trends and gain a competitive edge.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-description}}

**Target market:** {{target-market}}

**Main competitors:** {{competitors}}

## Output
Deliver a structured competitive analysis report with the following sections:

### Executive Summary
Provide a concise overview of the competitive landscape and key findings.

### Market Overview
- Market size and growth rate
- Key trends shaping the industry
- Cite credible sources for all data

### Competitor Analysis
Analyze each of the three main competitors:
- Company name
- Market share
- Core strengths
- Key weaknesses
- Back findings with credible sources

### Benchmarking
- Identify critical success factors in the industry
- Highlight competitive advantages available
- Pinpoint areas for improvement
- Reference credible sources

### Trend Analysis
- Emerging trends
- Industry disruptions on the horizon
- Opportunities to capitalize on
- Threats to mitigate
- Support with credible sources

### Strategic Recommendations
- Strategic priorities ranked by impact
- Actionable implementation plan
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) to track progress
- Cite credible sources for best practices

**Formatting:** Use markdown with clear headings, bullet points, and tables where appropriate. Explain any specialized terminology. Focus on actionable insights the business owner can implement immediately.
```
