# Contemporary Spa Interior Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Contemporary Spa Interior Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates professional architectural renderings of boutique wellness spaces for interior designers, spa owners, and hospitality brands. This spa interior design prompt for Midjourney and Flux produces wide-angle editorial visualizations of sculptural open-flow spa layouts with distinct functional zones, soft curved pathways, and Mediterranean modernist influences. It balances calm, immersive atmospheres with visually striking color-blocked architecture, filtered daylight through textured openings, and tactile natural materials. Real use cases include branding presentations, investor pitch decks, portfolio imagery, and social media content for wellness hospitality projects. Designed for interior designers, architects, spa developers, and hospitality marketers who need high-resolution concept visuals without photoshoots or completed construction. ● Produces sculptural spa interiors with curved architectural forms, color-blocked surfaces, and soft ambient lighting that avoids traditional spa clichés. ● Customizable material palettes, color schemes, and spa elements (treatment pods, meditation corners, water areas) let you match specific brand aesthetics. ● Generates wide-angle editorial compositions with high-detail tactile surfaces, accurate global illumination, and asymmetrical framing focused on materials and spatial relationships. ● Outputs high-resolution 1:1 aspect ratio images suitable for branding decks, design portfolios, social media, and client presentations. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic architectural visualization of a modern wellness spa interior. Wide-angle perspective (24–35mm lens), editorial composition showcasing sculptural open-flow layout with soft curved pathways and distinct zones: relax lounge, treatment pods, meditation corner, water & steam area.

**Design Identity:** Cool, contemporary, artistic, immersive—boutique hotel spa meets art gallery meets high-end fashion retail with Mediterranean modernist influences. Luxury without stiffness, calm yet visually exciting.

**Materials & Surfaces:** {{material-palette}} with matte, tactile, natural finishes. Custom stone or terrazzo side tables, color-blocked architectural surfaces (not decoration).

**Color:** {{color-scheme}} applied as architectural color-blocking—base tones on primary surfaces, accents integrated structurally.

**Furniture & Forms:** Low sculptural lounge sofas, organic curved benches, rounded edges, bold silhouettes, minimal detailing.

**Lighting:** Filtered daylight through textured or circular openings. Indirect LED strips hidden in curves, soft ambient wall washing, glowing sculptural light objects. Warm, dreamy, shadow-rich but soft atmosphere with accurate global illumination.

**Features:** {{spa-elements}}. Semi-open treatment pods with fabric or glass separation. Abstract wall art integrated into architecture, large indoor plants as spatial dividers, ceramic and stone art objects—all architectural, not decorative.

**Render Quality:** High-detail tactile surfaces, editorial color grading, asymmetrical but balanced framing focusing on materials, curves, and color relationships.

**Avoid:** Traditional spa clichés, bamboo decor, zen symbols, white sterile interiors, cheap candles, overly minimal empty spaces.

High resolution, 1:1 aspect ratio. Cool, modern, unforgettable mood.
```
