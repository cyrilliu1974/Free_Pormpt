# Animated Film Poster Generator

## 簡介

The Animated Film Poster Generator is a free AI prompt that creates Pixar-style movie posters featuring joyful children in customizable rural settings. This animated film poster prompt for Midjourney produces cinematic images of children in traditional rural clothing running through landscapes while flying colorful kites, set against misty mountains and vibrant vegetation. The prompt uses Midjourney's quality and stylization parameters (--q 2 --s 750) to render ultra-detailed textures, warm lighting, and expressive character designs in a 2:3 aspect ratio ideal for poster formats. You control the landscape type and lighting atmosphere through two variables, making it adaptable for different moods and environments - from golden-hour rice paddies to twilight meadows. This prompt is perfect for animators, illustrators, marketers, and storytellers who need evocative poster artwork that captures the heart of family-friendly narratives. ● Produces posters with cinematic warm lighting and cute character designs that evoke Pixar's signature animation style. ● Customizable landscape variable lets you specify rice fields, meadows, valleys, or other rural settings to match your story. ● Adjustable lighting-atmosphere variable controls mood - golden hour, soft dawn, dramatic sunset, or misty morning. ● Includes optimized Midjourney parameters for ultra-detailed textures, joyful expressions, and 2:3 poster aspect ratio. ## Prompt

```
Animated film poster in Pixar-like style, a group of children in rural traditional clothes running through {{landscape}}, flying a giant colorful kite; distant misty mountains, lush green vegetation. {{lighting-atmosphere}}. Cinematic warm lighting, cute character design, ultra-detailed textures, joyful expressions. --ar 2:3 --q 2 --s 750
```
