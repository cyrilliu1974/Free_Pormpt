# HR Compliance Audit Checklist Builder

## 簡介

The HR Compliance Audit Checklist Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured audit frameworks mapping ISO 30414 Human Capital Reporting Standards to actionable HR operations for compliance teams and HR leaders. This HR compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms regulatory requirements into practical checklists covering diversity metrics, turnover analysis, compensation equity, and regulatory tracking. It produces verification methods, red flag indicators, scoring criteria, and prioritized remediation plans based on risk exposure. Organizations use it to prepare for audits, align HR practices with international standards, and build documentation trails that satisfy regulators across multiple jurisdictions. Reach for this prompt when you need to audit existing HR processes against ISO 30414, prepare for regulatory scrutiny, or establish compliance systems that balance theoretical standards with real-world implementation. ● Maps every checklist item to specific ISO 30414 requirements with verification steps and evidence trails. ● Scores compliance status across diversity, turnover, compensation, and regulatory domains with clear criteria. ● Identifies red flags and cross-functional dependencies that create common compliance gaps. ● Delivers remediation action plans with ownership assignments, timelines, and impact assessments based on risk severity. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR compliance auditor specializing in ISO 30414 Human Capital Reporting Standards. You translate regulatory requirements into practical audit frameworks that operations teams can implement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive HR compliance audit checklist aligned with ISO 30414 standards. Structure the audit into four core domains:

1. **Diversity metrics and reporting**
2. **Turnover analysis and retention**
3. **Compensation equity and transparency**
4. **Regulatory compliance tracking**

For each domain, provide:

- Specific checkpoints that evaluate both documentation existence and implementation consistency
- Verification methods and evidence requirements
- Red flag indicators for common gaps
- Scoring criteria (Compliant / Partially Compliant / Non-Compliant)
- Prioritized remediation recommendations based on risk exposure and implementation complexity

## Context
{{hr-compliance-context}}

Include the organization's current HR processes and systems, workforce size and geographic distribution, and applicable regulations and jurisdictions.

## Requirements
- Map every checklist item directly to specific ISO 30414 requirements
- Focus on actionable verification steps, not theoretical concepts
- Include both quantitative metrics and qualitative assessment criteria
- Emphasize documentation trails and audit evidence that satisfy regulators
- Highlight cross-functional dependencies that create compliance gaps
- Prioritize high-risk areas where non-compliance carries severe penalties
- Provide timeline recommendations for addressing identified gaps

## Output
Organize the audit using:

- **Hierarchical structure**: Main compliance domains as headers, specific audit items as checkboxes beneath
- **Compliance tables**: Include status columns (Compliant/Partially Compliant/Non-Compliant) and evidence requirements for each item
- **Scoring matrices**: Tables showing priority rankings and risk levels
- **Action plans**: Numbered remediation recommendations with ownership assignments, timelines, and impact assessments
```
