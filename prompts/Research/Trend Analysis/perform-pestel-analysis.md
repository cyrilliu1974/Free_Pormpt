# PESTEL Analysis Framework for Market Research

## 簡介

The PESTEL Analysis Framework for Market Research is a free AI prompt that systematically evaluates the six macro-environmental factors shaping market conditions for any industry and region. This PESTEL analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table with six columns - Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal - each populated with bullet-point insights tailored to your specified industry, geography, and timeframe. The prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to identify concrete examples of each factor, assess both positive and negative impacts, and surface interconnections between dimensions. Market analysts use it to map regulatory shifts, economic trends, demographic changes, innovation cycles, sustainability pressures, and compliance requirements in a single view, whether preparing stakeholder reports, investment briefs, or strategic planning documents. Reach for this prompt when you need a balanced, multi-lens environmental scan that goes beyond surface-level observations and connects macro trends to specific market dynamics. ● Evaluates political regulations, economic indicators, social trends, technology developments, environmental pressures, and legal frameworks in one structured output. ● Balances positive opportunities and negative risks for each PESTEL dimension, helping you anticipate both threats and growth drivers. ● Highlights interdependencies between factors - such as how regulatory changes influence technology adoption or economic shifts affect social behavior. ● Accepts custom industry, geography, and timeframe variables so the analysis reflects current, localized realities rather than generic observations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst conducting a PESTEL analysis.

## Task
Identify and analyze the key Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal factors influencing the market environment for the specified industry. Evaluate each factor systematically, providing specific examples and potential impacts. Balance your analysis between positive and negative influences, and highlight interconnections between factors where relevant.

## Context
Industry and scope: {{industry-and-geography}}
Timeframe: {{timeframe}}

Consider both global and local trends, regulations, and market dynamics relevant to this context.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with 6 columns—one for each PESTEL factor (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal). Under each column, list key points in bullet format for clarity and easy reference.
```
