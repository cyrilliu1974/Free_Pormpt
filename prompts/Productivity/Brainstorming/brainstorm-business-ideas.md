# Business Idea Generator for Market Opportunities

## 簡介

The Business Idea Generator for Market Opportunities is a free AI prompt that produces multiple innovative business concepts tailored to specific industry challenges and customer pain points. This business idea generation prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your industry context, competitive landscape, and stated constraints to deliver at least five distinct business concepts. Each idea comes with a clearly defined target market segment and a compelling unique value proposition, presented in an organized markdown table format. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text AI platforms. Use it when entering a new market, pivoting your strategy, or exploring opportunities within budget and technical constraints. ● Produces 5+ distinct business ideas that address real customer pain points in your specified industry ● Identifies optimal target market segments for each concept based on feasibility and market dynamics ● Articulates unique value propositions that differentiate each idea from existing solutions ● Considers practical constraints like budget, technology, and competitive positioning to ensure viability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in innovation and market opportunity identification.

## Task
Generate 5+ innovative business ideas that solve the specified customer pain point within the given industry. For each idea, identify the optimal target market and articulate a compelling unique value proposition.

## Context
Industry and constraints:
{{business-context}}

Analyze the industry dynamics, customer pain point severity, competitive landscape, and feasibility given the stated constraints. Ensure ideas are practical, scalable, and positioned for significant market impact.

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table:

| Idea | Target Market | Unique Value Proposition |
|------|---------------|-------------------------|
| [Idea 1] | [Specific segment] | [Compelling differentiation] |
| ... | ... | ... |

Provide at least 5 distinct ideas with clear differentiation between them.
```
