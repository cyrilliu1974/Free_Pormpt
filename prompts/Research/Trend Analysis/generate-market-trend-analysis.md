# Market Trend Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Market Trend Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive industry intelligence reports for business analysts, strategists, and decision-makers. This market trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT delivers an eight-section report covering industry overview, market size and growth projections, consumer behavior patterns, emerging technologies with impact assessments, competitive landscape mapping, opportunity and risk identification, future outlook, and actionable strategic recommendations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by guiding the model to synthesize current market data, identify both incremental trends and disruptive forces, and provide depth beyond surface-level observations. Use it when preparing strategic plans, investor briefings, competitive intelligence documents, or quarterly market reviews for any industry or sector. ● Structures reports into eight comprehensive sections from industry overview through actionable recommendations ● Identifies consumer behavior patterns, emerging technologies, and their potential market impact ● Maps competitive landscape including key players, market share distribution, and recent developments ● Balances opportunity identification with risk assessment for complete strategic perspective ● Produces substantive, data-grounded analysis that prioritizes strategic relevance over generic observations ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic business analyst specializing in market intelligence and trend analysis to inform decision-making.

## Task

Research and analyze the latest developments in {{industry-or-sector}}. Cover market trends, consumer behavior patterns, emerging technologies, competitive dynamics, and strategic implications.

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive market analysis structured as follows:

### 1. Industry Overview
Provide a synthesis of the current market landscape and potential future directions.

### 2. Market Size and Growth
- Current Market Size
- Projected Growth Rate
- Key Growth Drivers

### 3. Consumer Trends
- Trend 1
- Trend 2
- Trend 3

### 4. Emerging Technologies
For each technology:
- Technology 1:
  - Name
  - Potential Impact
- Technology 2:
  - Name
  - Potential Impact
- Technology 3:
  - Name
  - Potential Impact

### 5. Competitive Landscape
- Key Players
- Market Share Distribution
- Recent Developments

### 6. Opportunities and Risks
**Opportunities:**
- Opportunity 1
- Opportunity 2

**Risks:**
- Risk 1
- Risk 2

### 7. Future Outlook
Synthesize insights on short-term and long-term trajectories, including potential disruptive forces.

### 8. Recommendations
Provide actionable strategic guidance based on the analysis.

## Requirements

- Ensure thorough coverage of all sections with substantive, specific insights
- Ground findings in current data and credible market intelligence
- Identify both incremental trends and potential disruptions
- Avoid superficial observations; prioritize depth and strategic relevance
```
