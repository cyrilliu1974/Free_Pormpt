# Travel Article Generator With Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The Travel Article Generator With Dependency Grammar is a free AI prompt that produces engaging destination articles for travel writers, bloggers, and content creators. This travel article prompt for ChatGPT applies linguistic dependency grammar principles to craft concise, logically flowing sentences that guide readers from known to unknown information while painting vivid sensory pictures of places. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to generate complete travel narratives with an intriguing introduction, detailed coverage of three key attractions with sensory details and personal impressions, two signature experiences with practical insights, and an inspiring conclusion with a call-to-action. Writers working on destination guides, travel blogs, tourism content, or editorial features reach for this prompt when they need structured, publication-ready articles that balance clarity with evocative description. ● Structures articles around three attractions and two experiences, each with sensory details and reader takeaways ● Applies dependency grammar rules for concise sentences with clear core arguments and relevant supporting clauses ● Outputs clean markdown-formatted articles ready for blogs, magazines, or content management systems ● Prompts for specific destination input (name, attractions, experiences) to generate tailored, authentic narratives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert travel writer who applies dependency grammar principles to create precise, flowing sentences where core arguments are clearly expressed and supporting clauses add relevant detail without superfluity.

## Task
Compose an engaging travel article that vividly depicts the destination and evokes a strong sense of place.

## Structure

**Introduction**
- Open with an intriguing hook about the destination
- Preview the key attractions and experiences
- Establish tone and style

**Three Key Attractions** (cover each):
- Name and significance (1-2 sentences)
- Sensory details: sights, sounds, smells, tastes, textures
- Brief personal anecdote or impression

**Two Experiences** (cover each):
- Name and essence (1-2 sentences)
- Main takeaways or insights
- Why readers should try it

**Conclusion**
- Summarize overall impression and appeal
- Reiterate most memorable highlights
- Close with an inspiring call-to-action to visit

## Output Requirements
- Apply dependency grammar: sentences must be concise and logically structured, following "known to unknown" information flow
- Use determiners and auxiliaries properly; avoid superfluous clauses
- Paint vivid pictures through sensory language
- Return in clean markdown format without XML tags

## Input
{{destination-and-highlights}}

*Provide: destination name, 3 key attractions, and 2 signature experiences*
```
