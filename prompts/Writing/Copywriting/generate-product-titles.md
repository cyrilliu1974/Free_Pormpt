# Generate Product Titles for Marketplace SEO

## 簡介

The Generate Product Titles for Marketplace SEO prompt is a free AI prompt that creates algorithm-optimized product titles for e-commerce sellers listing on Amazon, Etsy, and eBay. It analyzes your product details and target marketplace, then generates 3-5 title variations designed for A/B testing, each emphasizing different keyword strategies - front-loaded exact matches for Amazon A9, long-tail conversational phrases for Etsy search, or technical compatibility terms for eBay Best Match. This product title prompt for ChatGPT works equally well on Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing titles that respect platform-specific character limits (Amazon 200, Etsy 140, eBay 80) while avoiding suppression triggers like keyword stuffing, ALL CAPS, and restricted promotional language. Reach for this prompt when you need to balance search visibility with click-through rate, ensuring your listings comply with marketplace rules while standing out to human buyers. ● Produces 3-5 title variations per request, each annotated with character count, strategic focus (keyword angle, benefit-driven, niche positioning), and compliance notes. ● Includes a product analysis section that identifies keyword opportunities and competitive positioning based on the details you provide. ● Provides A/B testing recommendations specifying which two titles to compare, which metrics to track (click-through rate, conversion rate, search rank), and evaluation timeframes. ● Adapts output to the algorithm priorities of your chosen marketplace - Amazon A9 keyword weighting, Etsy handmade emphasis, or eBay technical specification requirements. ## Prompt

```
## Role
Marketplace optimization specialist with expertise in e-commerce search algorithms (Amazon A9, Etsy search, eBay Best Match) and platform-specific compliance requirements.

## Task
Generate optimized product titles for the specified marketplace that maximize search visibility and click-through rate. Provide 3-5 title variations emphasizing different keyword strategies or psychological angles for A/B testing.

## Context
Marketplace algorithm priorities:
- **Amazon**: Front-loaded exact-match keywords, 200-char limit, no promotional language
- **Etsy**: Long-tail conversational phrases, handmade/unique emphasis, 140-char limit
- **eBay**: Technical specs and compatibility terms, 80-char limit

Titles must avoid suppression triggers (keyword stuffing, ALL CAPS, trademark violations, restricted terms like "best" or "cheapest") while incorporating 2-3 primary keywords naturally.

**Product & Business Context:**
{{product-details}}

**Target Marketplace:**
{{marketplace}}

## Output
**Product Analysis:**
Brief assessment of keyword opportunities and competitive positioning based on the product details.

**Optimized Title Variations:**

For the specified marketplace, generate 3-5 title options:

1. [Title] (XX chars) – Focus: [keyword/psychological strategy]
2. [Title] (XX chars) – Focus: [alternative angle]
3. [Title] (XX chars) – Focus: [feature emphasis]
4. [Title] (XX chars) – Focus: [benefit-driven]
5. [Title] (XX chars) – Focus: [niche positioning]

**A/B Testing Recommendation:**
- Primary test: Compare top 2 titles with strongest strategic contrast
- Track: Click-through rate, conversion rate, search ranking position
- Evaluate after: 7-14 days or 100+ impressions per variation
```
