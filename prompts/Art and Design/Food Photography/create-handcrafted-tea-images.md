# Handcrafted Tea Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Handcrafted Tea Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates commercial-quality beverage images for food photographers, café owners, and marketing teams. It produces front-view shots of tea in transparent glass cups, complete with condensation droplets, reusable straws, vibrant liquid textures, and fresh garnishes against minimalist café backgrounds. This tea photography prompt for Midjourney works equally well on Flux and Nano Banana, delivering professional food photography with soft natural window lighting, shallow depth of field, and rule-of-thirds composition. Reach for it when you need appetizing tea visuals for menus, social media, or marketing campaigns that require commercial product photography standards. ● Produces front-view tea photos with transparent cups showing condensation, pulp texture, and liquid clarity at eye-level angle with 85mm lens simulation. ● Specifies natural window lighting, warm color palettes, and shallow depth of field for appetizing commercial food photography mood. ● Includes customizable tea flavor and aspect ratio variables to match your specific beverage type and platform requirements. ● Delivers sharp focus on the cup with rule-of-thirds composition, fresh garnish placement, and minimalist café backgrounds that keep attention on the product. ## Prompt
