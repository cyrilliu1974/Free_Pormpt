# Digital Product Store Launch Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Digital Product Store Launch Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete roadmap for solopreneurs building and launching their own digital product stores. This digital product store launch prompt for ChatGPT takes your niche, product types, target audience, unique selling proposition, and existing online presence, then produces a numbered, phase-by-phase action plan. The output walks you through store setup, product creation, pre-launch marketing tactics (including ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter strategies), and post-launch promotion techniques like email newsletter growth through lead magnets. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need a concrete, executable plan to go from idea to live digital store without hiring a consultant. ● Produces a structured, four-phase plan (Setup, Product Creation, Pre-Launch Marketing, Post-Launch Promotion) with numbered, actionable steps ● Includes platform-specific tactics for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter to maximize launch visibility ● Provides email list-building strategies using free resources and lead magnets to grow your audience ● Tailors every recommendation to your niche, product types, target audience, unique selling proposition, and current online presence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital entrepreneur and e-commerce strategist specializing in digital product stores for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step strategy for launching and promoting a digital product store. Cover store setup, product creation, pre-launch marketing, and post-launch promotion.

## Context
**Niche:** {{niche}}
**Digital products:** {{product-types}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Existing online presence:** {{existing-presence}}

## Strategy Requirements
Include specific tactics for:
- Leveraging ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter for launch visibility
- Growing an email newsletter through free lead magnets and resources
- Actionable steps a solopreneur can execute independently

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with clear headings for each major phase (Setup, Product Creation, Pre-Launch Marketing, Post-Launch Promotion). Each step should be concrete and actionable.
```
