# Google Ads Retargeting Campaign Builder for Real Estate

## 簡介

The Google Ads Retargeting Campaign Builder for Real Estate is a free AI prompt that creates precision remarketing systems for real estate professionals who need to recapture high-intent prospects through targeted advertising. This real estate retargeting prompt for ChatGPT generates a seven-section implementation plan covering audience segmentation based on search keywords and competitor site visits, technical setup instructions for Google Ads Campaign dashboard, targeting list creation organized by intent level, ad copy frameworks tailored to different buyer and seller segments, campaign structure recommendations balanced for your monthly budget, landing page requirements matched to each audience tier, and performance tracking tables with real estate-specific benchmarks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your business context, monthly budget, unique selling proposition, and primary goal as inputs to produce a campaign that focuses exclusively on people already demonstrating buying or selling intent. Real estate agents, brokers, and marketing teams reach for this prompt when they need to move beyond generic display advertising and build remarketing campaigns that target prospects who have searched for terms like "sell house," "buy Realtor," or visited competitor listings and MLS sites. ● Segments audiences by intent level using search keyword behavior, competitor website visits, and MLS listing views with clear criteria for each tier. ● Provides numbered technical setup instructions for creating remarketing lists inside the Google Ads Campaign dashboard, including website URL and keyword targeting configuration. ● Generates 3-5 ad copy templates with headlines and descriptions tailored to different audience segments, aligned with decision journey stage and unique selling proposition. ● Delivers campaign structure hierarchy that balances audience granularity with budget efficiency, plus landing page requirements and performance tracking tables with metric definitions and target ranges. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a real estate digital advertising strategist specializing in Google Ads remarketing campaigns. You design precision targeting systems that recapture prospects who have demonstrated intent through search behavior and competitor site visits.

## Task
Create a complete Google Ads remarketing campaign structure that targets high-intent real estate prospects and drives them back to dedicated landing pages.

## Context
The campaign operates in a competitive real estate market where prospects research across multiple platforms before making contact. Traditional broad display advertising wastes budget on cold audiences. This campaign focuses exclusively on remarketing to people who have actively searched for real estate terms ("sell house," "buy house," "Realtor," related terms) or visited competitor sites—audiences already in buying or selling mode.

Business context: {{business-context}}

Monthly budget: {{monthly-budget}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Primary goal: {{primary-goal}}

## Requirements
- Target only audiences demonstrating active real estate search behavior
- Structure campaigns to account for different intent levels (early research vs. ready to contact)
- Prioritize qualified traffic generation over vanity metrics
- All ads must direct to dedicated landing pages matched to audience segment
- Avoid generic display approaches

## Output
Provide a step-by-step implementation plan using this structure:

**SECTION 1: AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION STRATEGY**
- Define targeting lists based on search keywords, competitor website visits, and MLS listing views
- Segment by intent level with clear criteria for each tier

**SECTION 2: TECHNICAL SETUP WALKTHROUGH**
- Numbered step-by-step instructions for creating remarketing lists in Google Ads Campaign dashboard
- Include how to configure website URLs and keyword targeting

**SECTION 3: TARGETING LIST CREATION**
- Specific keywords organized by intent level
- Competitor URLs categorized by priority
- Informed by the business context provided

**SECTION 4: AD COPY FRAMEWORKS**
- 3-5 ad templates (headlines + descriptions) tailored to different audience segments
- Copy should emphasize the USP and direct traffic to dedicated landing pages, not the homepage
- Align with where prospects are in the decision journey

**SECTION 5: CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE**
- Visual hierarchy showing how campaigns and ad groups should be organized
- Balance granularity with budget efficiency for the stated monthly budget

**SECTION 6: LANDING PAGE STRATEGY**
- Requirements for dedicated landing pages that convert each audience segment
- Align with the primary goal

**SECTION 7: PERFORMANCE TRACKING**
- Key metrics and target benchmarks specific to real estate remarketing
- Present as a table with metric names, definitions, and target ranges
```
