# Risk Management Plan Generator for Projects

## 簡介

The Risk Management Plan Generator for Projects is a free AI prompt that creates tailored risk management plans covering identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring for project managers and business leaders. This risk management prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete plan with project overview, risk categories, detailed assessments of critical risks (with likelihood and impact ratings), mitigation strategies, monitoring protocols, contingency plans, and stakeholder communication frameworks. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by analyzing your project details and generating context-specific risk assessments rather than generic checklists. Project managers use it to systematically document risks for IT implementations, product launches, organizational changes, construction projects, and strategic initiatives. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to identify vulnerabilities, prioritize threats, and establish clear response protocols before risks materialize into project failures. ● Identifies five relevant risk categories and assesses five critical risks with likelihood, impact, and mitigation strategies specific to your project ● Provides practical monitoring and control plans with clear frequency, responsibility assignments, and escalation triggers ● Includes contingency planning for highest-impact scenarios and a stakeholder communication framework ● Delivers structured output with headings and lists for immediate use in project documentation and team briefings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced risk management consultant with expertise in identifying potential risks and developing effective mitigation strategies across various industries and project types.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive risk management plan for the following project:

{{project-details}}

## Output
Deliver a structured risk management plan with these components:

**Project Overview:**
Summarize the key project details, scope, and objectives.

**Risk Categories:**
Identify 5 risk categories most relevant to this project (e.g., technical, financial, operational, regulatory, reputational).

**Risk Assessment:**
For 5 specific critical risks, provide:
- Description: What the risk is and how it could materialize
- Category: Which risk category it belongs to
- Likelihood: Low / Medium / High
- Impact: Low / Medium / High
- Mitigation Strategy: Clear, actionable steps to reduce likelihood or impact

Prioritize high-impact risks that are most critical to project success. Tailor each risk to the specific project context—avoid generic assessments.

**Risk Monitoring and Control:**
Outline a practical, feasible plan for ongoing tracking of identified risks, including monitoring frequency, responsibility assignment, and triggers for escalation.

**Contingency Planning:**
Describe backup plans and response protocols for the highest-impact risks, should mitigation strategies prove insufficient.

**Risk Communication Plan:**
Define how risk information will be shared with stakeholders, including communication channels, frequency, and level of detail appropriate for different audiences.

Format each section clearly with headings and structured lists for easy reference.
```
