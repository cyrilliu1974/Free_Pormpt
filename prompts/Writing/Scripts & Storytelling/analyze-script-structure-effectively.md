# Script Structure Analysis Prompt for Screenwriters

## 簡介

The Script Structure Analysis Prompt for Screenwriters is a free AI prompt that evaluates scene effectiveness, pacing issues, and narrative flow for scriptwriters and storytellers. This script structure prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your screenplay scene by scene, scoring each for pacing and narrative impact on a 1-5 scale, then delivers targeted recommendations for reordering, editing, or rewriting. It works by applying established story structure principles - three-act structure, Save the Cat beats, or the hero's journey - to your specific genre and target audience. Writers use it to diagnose slow scenes, identify weak transitions, and build stronger climax sequences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your script details and genre conventions. Reach for this prompt when you need an objective structural audit of a draft, especially before production or pitch meetings, or whenever your screenplay feels uneven but you cannot pinpoint why. ● Builds a scene evaluation table scoring purpose, pacing, and narrative impact with specific actions required for each scene. ● Assesses story flow across transitions, suspense maintenance, and climax build-up to reveal rhythm problems. ● Flags problem areas - slow scenes, disrupted rhythm, weak transitions, redundant content - with clear explanations. ● Provides strategic rationale backed by industry structure principles, explaining how each recommended change strengthens emotional resonance and forward momentum. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert scriptwriter specializing in story structure, pacing, and narrative flow optimization.

## Task
Analyze the provided script to evaluate scene effectiveness, identify pacing issues, and recommend structural improvements that enhance audience engagement and narrative impact.

## Context
- Genre: {{genre}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Script details: {{script-details}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structured format:

### 📊 Scene Evaluation Table
| Scene # | Purpose | Pacing (1-5) | Narrative Impact (1-5) | Action Required |
|---------|---------|--------------|------------------------|----------------|

### 🔄 Flow Assessment
- Transitions (Smooth/Choppy)
- Story progression
- Suspense maintenance
- Climax build-up

### ⚠️ Problem Areas
- Scenes slowing narrative
- Disrupted story rhythm
- Weak transitions
- Redundant content

### ✅ Recommendations
- Scene reordering suggestions
- Editing recommendations
- Rewriting proposals
- Transition improvements

### 💡 Strategic Rationale
Provide industry-backed justification for each major change, explaining how it enhances narrative impact and audience engagement.

Base your analysis on established story structure principles (three-act structure, save-the-cat beats, or hero's journey as applicable to the genre). Prioritize recommendations that maximize emotional resonance and maintain forward momentum.
```
