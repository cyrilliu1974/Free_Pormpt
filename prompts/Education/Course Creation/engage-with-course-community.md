# Course Community Engagement Content Generator

## 簡介

The Course Community Engagement Content Generator is a free AI prompt that creates discussion prompts, announcements, and model responses to drive engagement and peer-to-peer networking in solopreneur course communities. This course community engagement prompt for ChatGPT produces three types of content: engaging discussion questions that spark conversation and experience-sharing, clear announcements that inform members about milestones and events, and thoughtful response examples that model how to deepen relationships. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure every sentence leads with its core message and layers in supporting details for maximum clarity. Course creators use it to maintain active, collaborative communities on platforms like Slack, Circle, Discord, or custom forums, tailoring content to specific course topics, audience needs, and engagement goals. This prompt is for course creators, community managers, and educators who want to transform passive student groups into thriving peer networks where solopreneurs support one another. ● Produces 3–5 discussion prompts that invite members to share experiences, advice, and resources relevant to the course topic ● Generates 2–3 motivating announcements for milestones, feature launches, community wins, or upcoming events ● Creates 2–3 model responses to common member posts - questions, introductions, achievements - that demonstrate thoughtful engagement ● Structures all content using dependency grammar for clear, action-oriented communication that encourages participation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert online community manager specializing in fostering collaboration and peer-to-peer networking among solopreneurs.

## Task
Create community content that drives engagement and connection: discussion prompts that spark conversations, announcements that inform and motivate, and responses that deepen relationships. Structure all writing using dependency grammar principles—prioritizing main clauses with subordinate details that enhance clarity and flow.

## Context
Course: {{course-topic}}
Audience: {{target-audience}}
Platform: {{community-platform}}
Engagement goals: {{engagement-goals}}

## Output
Organize your content under these headings:

### Discussion Prompts
- Bullet-point 3–5 engaging questions or scenarios that encourage solopreneurs to share experiences, advice, or resources

### Announcements
- Bullet-point 2–3 clear, motivating announcements (course milestones, feature launches, community wins, upcoming events)

### Response Examples
- Bullet-point 2–3 model replies to common member posts (questions, introductions, achievements) that demonstrate thoughtful engagement and encourage further dialogue

For each item, apply dependency grammar: lead with the core message, then layer in supporting context. Keep sentences clear and action-oriented.
```
