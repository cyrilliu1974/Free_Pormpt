# Instagram Ad Copy Generator for Product Campaigns

## 簡介

The Instagram Ad Copy Generator for Product Campaigns is a free AI prompt that produces structured, conversion-focused ad packages for any product and target audience. This Instagram ad copy prompt for ChatGPT takes two inputs - your product or service and target audience - and returns a complete ad framework: headline, body copy, call-to-action, targeting context (demographics, pain points, goals), key benefits, social proof elements, and offer details. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying benefit-led writing principles and plain, scannable language designed for mobile feeds. The prompt emphasizes leading with the strongest benefit, matching tone to audience, and building urgency around time-sensitive offers when relevant. Use it when launching product campaigns, testing new audience segments, or scaling ad creative production for Instagram. ● Produces eight distinct ad components in one pass: headline, body, CTA, targeting, benefits, proof, and offer ● Structured output format makes it easy to hand off to designers or paste directly into Ads Manager ● Adapts tone and benefit emphasis based on the audience you specify ● Includes targeting context fields that double as creative brief documentation for your team ## Prompt
