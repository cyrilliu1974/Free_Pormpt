# Chalkboard Anime Art Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Chalkboard Anime Art Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces documentary-style photographs of intricate anime chalk drawings on green classroom blackboards for content creators and visual storytellers. This chalkboard anime art prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates medium-shot compositions that capture the dusty, matte texture of chalk with visible hatching strokes, hand-blended shading, and authentic classroom environments including teacher's desks, colored chalk boxes, and erasers. The prompt specifies 35mm camera settings, ambient classroom lighting at 5000K color temperature, and f/5.6 aperture to render crisp chalk detail against dark green slate while maintaining documentary realism. Use it when you need nostalgic educational imagery, marketing visuals celebrating traditional art forms, or texture-rich content that evokes the ephemeral beauty of chalkboard art without requiring classroom access. ● Captures the physical properties of chalk medium with cross-hatching, smudging, and dust accumulation on green slate surfaces. ● Includes customizable variables for character scenes and Japanese text rendered in crisp white chalk. ● Specifies full camera technical details (35mm lens, ISO 400, 1/60s shutter) and diffuse fluorescent-plus-daylight lighting for reproducible results. ● Frames compositions with foreground desk elements for scale and documentary context, avoiding hyper-saturated or stylized filters. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic documentary photograph of a classroom chalkboard mural, 4:3 aspect ratio, centered medium shot.

**Subject:** Large-scale intricate chalk drawing on standard green classroom blackboard: {{character-and-scene}}. Preserve the dusty, matte quality of chalk medium with visible hatching and cross-hatching strokes creating shading, smudged areas where colors have been hand-blended on the green slate. Vertical Japanese text reading "{{board-text}}" in crisp white chalk to the right of the character.

**Foreground:** Wooden teacher's desk in lower frame for scale. Scattered across surface: yellow box of colored chalks, loose sticks of red, white, and blue pastel chalk, dust-covered black felt eraser.

**Environment:** Standard Japanese school classroom. Green chalkboard spans width of frame, bordered by metallic chalk tray containing accumulated chalk dust. Plain off-white plaster wall above with small mounted speaker box. Quiet, academic atmosphere suggesting unoccupied room.

**Lighting:** Diffuse ambient classroom lighting—soft, nondirectional illumination from overhead fluorescent fixtures mixed with daylight from windows on left. Even light prevents glare on chalkboard surface while highlighting chalk texture. Neutral white color temperature (5000K) for accurate chalk color rendition against dark green board. Overhead and slightly frontal direction.

**Camera:** 35mm full-frame digital sensor, 35mm prime lens, f/5.6 aperture, 1/60s shutter, ISO 400. Moderate depth of field keeps chalkboard drawing in sharp focus while foreground desk elements soften slightly. Eye-level standing position set back to frame entire drawing and desk.

**Style:** Documentary realism, texture-focused, ambient naturalism. Composition includes desk in immediate foreground, artwork dominating background space.

Negative prompt: Multiple characters, digital art overlay, vector graphics, paper texture, oil painting, messy composition, extreme low angle, fisheye lens, hyper-saturation, soft focus filters, heavy vignetting.
```
