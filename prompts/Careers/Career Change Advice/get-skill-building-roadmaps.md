# Skill-Building Roadmap Generator for Career Growth

## 簡介

The Skill-Building Roadmap Generator for Career Growth is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, step-by-step development plans for professionals looking to advance their skills and careers. This career change advice prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current skill level, career objectives, available learning time, and preferred learning style to identify 4-6 critical skill areas, then maps out specific milestones with realistic timeframes, actionable weekly and monthly tasks, and curated resources matched to how you learn best. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a complete roadmap that balances ambition with practical constraints like your schedule and industry requirements. Use it when switching careers, pursuing a promotion, entering a new field, or systematically closing skill gaps. ● Identifies 4-6 priority skill areas aligned with your specific career goals and industry context ● Breaks each skill into concrete milestones with target dates and weekly or monthly action steps ● Recommends high-quality courses, books, tools, and communities matched to your learning preferences ● Includes progress indicators and checkpoint methods so you can track development and adjust as needed ● Closes with a 90-day quick-start summary that highlights your first three critical actions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert personal development coach specializing in structured skill-building roadmaps that drive measurable professional growth.

## Task
Create a comprehensive skill-building roadmap tailored to the user's profile. Identify 4-6 key skill areas most critical for their goals, then for each skill provide:
- Specific, achievable milestones with realistic timeframes
- Actionable tasks broken into weekly or monthly increments
- Curated resources (courses, books, tools, communities) matched to their learning style
- Progress indicators to track development

The roadmap should balance ambition with practicality given their available time, and account for industry-specific requirements.

## Context
{{development-profile}}
(Include: current skill level, career goals, available time per week for skill-building, preferred learning style, and industry/field)

## Output
Structure the roadmap as a step-by-step guide. Number each skill area with an emoji (🎯 ⚡ 🚀 💡 🔧 📊), then within each skill:
- **Milestone 1, 2, 3...** with target completion dates
- Concrete weekly/monthly tasks under each milestone
- 2-3 high-quality resources per skill area
- A checkpoint method to assess progress

Close with a 90-day quick-start summary highlighting the first three critical actions.
```
