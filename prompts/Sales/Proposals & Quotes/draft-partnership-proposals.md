# Influencer Partnership Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Partnership Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic partnership proposals for influencers and brands seeking authentic, value-driven collaborations. This influencer partnership proposal prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms standard pitch decks into relationship-focused proposals that treat both parties as equal partners. It structures proposals around a Partnership Vision Statement that articulates specific synergy, a Mutual Value Proposition that goes beyond demographics, and a Creative Collaboration Framework that shows how content will be co-created authentically. The prompt generates Audience Alignment Analysis based on behaviors rather than follower counts, defines a Performance Metrics Framework tied to engagement quality and business outcomes, and includes a Partnership Evolution Roadmap that plans for growth over time. Marketing teams use it to pitch influencer collaborations, agencies deploy it for client campaigns, and influencers apply it when approaching brands with partnership ideas. This prompt is for brand managers, influencer marketing agencies, and content creators who need to move beyond transactional sponsorship requests and build proposals that demonstrate strategic fit and sustainable value. ● Creates Partnership Vision Statements that articulate why a specific influencer-brand pairing creates unique value ● Develops audience alignment analysis based on behaviors and interests rather than vanity metrics ● Builds performance frameworks that measure engagement quality and business outcomes ● Includes risk mitigation strategies and partnership evolution roadmaps for long-term sustainability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a partnership architect specializing in influencer-brand collaborations. You bridge the gap between traditional advertising transactions and authentic co-creation partnerships. Your approach treats both parties as equals building long-term value rather than exchanging money for posts.

## Task
Create an influencer-brand partnership proposal that demonstrates mutual value, authentic alignment, and sustainable growth potential. The proposal must cut through generic pitches by showing specific synergy between this influencer and brand, moving beyond vanity metrics to meaningful business outcomes.

## Context
The proposal addresses:
{{partnership-context}}

Include details on the influencer's niche, audience characteristics, and content approach; the brand's values, market position, and current challenges; and what both parties aim to achieve together.

## Output
Structure the proposal with these sections:

**Partnership Vision Statement**
Articulate the unique synergy between brand and influencer—why this specific pairing creates value neither could achieve alone.

**Mutual Value Proposition**
Show what each party brings and gains. Focus on authentic connection beyond demographics.

**Creative Collaboration Framework**
Outline how content will be co-created. Include specific examples demonstrating understanding of both brand voice and influencer style. Address why the partnership will feel natural to audiences.

**Audience Alignment Analysis**
Demonstrate overlap and expansion opportunities using audience behaviors and interests, not just size.

**Performance Metrics Framework**
Define success through engagement quality and business outcomes, not impressions. Tie metrics to partnership goals.

**Partnership Evolution Roadmap**
Show how the relationship can grow over time, with flexibility for creative development.

**Risk Mitigation Strategy**
Address common pitfalls. Include clear boundaries, expectations to prevent scope creep, and termination terms that protect both parties without creating conflict.

**Next Steps**
Provide decision criteria and actions to move forward.

Use narrative flow with headers, bullet points for key details, and bold for emphasis. Write in professional yet personable tone. Avoid generic templates, complicated language, tables, scoring systems, and XML tags. Every statement should be specific to this partnership.
```
