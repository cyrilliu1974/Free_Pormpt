# Personalized B2B Email Outreach Template Generator

## 簡介

The Personalized B2B Email Outreach Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates relationship-focused cold emails for business-to-business sales and marketing teams. This B2B email outreach prompt for ChatGPT helps you craft professional email templates under 200 words that open with recipient-specific context, identify relevant pain points, position your product or service as the solution, and include social proof from similar industries. The prompt structures each element - subject line, personalized opening, problem statement, solution presentation, proof points, and low-friction call-to-action - using dependency grammar principles to keep core messages clear and supporting details subordinate. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs templates with bracketed placeholders for easy customization per recipient. Reach for this prompt when launching targeted campaigns, personalizing outreach at scale, or training sales teams to write emails that emphasize unique selling propositions and align with campaign goals. ● Outputs complete email templates with subject line, opening hook, pain point identification, solution positioning, proof element, and call-to-action ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure clarity, logical flow, and reader-friendly sentence structure ● Marks personalization points with brackets so you can quickly customize for each prospect ● Keeps tone professional yet conversational and length under 200 words for higher read-through rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist creating personalized B2B outreach emails that build relationships and drive engagement.

## Task
Write a tailored email template that positions {{product-service}} as the solution to the recipient's challenges. The email must:

1. Open with a personalized introduction referencing the recipient's business context or recent developments
2. Identify a specific pain point or opportunity relevant to {{target-customers}}
3. Present {{product-service}} as the solution, emphasizing {{usp}}
4. Include social proof or case studies from similar industries
5. Close with a clear, low-friction call-to-action
6. Use dependency grammar principles: lead with core information in each sentence, subordinate supporting details, and maintain clear logical connections between ideas

## Context
**Target customers:** {{target-customers}}
**Product/service:** {{product-service}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Campaign goals:** {{campaign-goals}}

## Output
Structure the email template with:
- **Subject line:** [personalization hook + value proposition]
- **Opening:** [recipient-specific reference]
- **Problem statement:** [their challenge]
- **Solution:** [how {{product-service}} addresses it]
- **Proof:** [relevant case study or metric]
- **Call-to-action:** [next step]

Mark sections requiring personalization with [brackets]. Keep tone professional yet conversational, length under 200 words.
```
