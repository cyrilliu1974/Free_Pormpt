# Social Proof Strategy Generator for Brands

## 簡介

The Social Proof Strategy Generator for Brands is a free AI prompt that builds a custom social proof playbook for businesses looking to increase trust and drive conversions through customer validation. This social proof strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your brand name, offering, and target audience and produces a structured plan covering customer reviews, video testimonials, case studies, influencer endorsements, expert validation, and user-generated content. It maps out how to acquire each proof type, where to display it (homepage, product pages, checkout, email, social media), and how to use it to influence purchasing decisions. The output runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for marketers, founders, and growth teams building credibility with skeptical audiences. ● Covers customer reviews, video testimonials, case studies, influencer endorsements, and expert validation with acquisition and display plans for each ● Includes post-purchase email sequences, incentivized feedback programs, partnership outreach, and social listening campaigns ● Maps social proof to high-intent touchpoints like pricing pages, checkout flows, exit-intent popups, and retargeting ads ● Provides credibility boosters such as aggregate scores, logo walls, third-party badges, media mentions, and before/after metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a social proof strategist. You help brands build, showcase, and activate social influence to drive awareness, trust, and sales.

## Context

Brand: {{brand-name}}
Offering: {{offering}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

Generate a complete social proof strategy tailored to the brand above. Cover the full lifecycle from acquisition to conversion influence. Keep all recommendations specific to this brand's offering and audience. Avoid generic advice that could apply to any business.

Address a variety of social proof formats: customer reviews, testimonials, case studies, influencer endorsements, social media mentions, and expert validation. For each type, include how to acquire it, where to display it, and how to use it to influence purchasing decisions. Prioritize tactics that build credibility and trust with {{target-audience}}.

## Output

### Social Proof Types
- Customer reviews and ratings
- Video testimonials
- Case studies with measurable results
- Influencer and creator endorsements
- Expert validation and certifications

### Acquisition Strategies
1. Post-purchase email sequences with review requests
2. Incentivized feedback programs (discounts, early access)
3. Partnership outreach to industry experts and influencers
4. Customer success interviews for case studies
5. Social listening and user-generated content campaigns

### Showcase Tactics
1. Homepage hero section with rotating testimonials
2. Product pages with contextual reviews and star ratings
3. Dedicated case study library with filters by industry/use case
4. Social media highlights and story pins
5. Email signature and sales deck integration

### Promotion Channels
- Website (homepage, product pages, checkout)
- Email marketing campaigns
- Social media (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- Paid advertising creative
- Sales enablement materials

### Credibility Boosters
1. Aggregate review scores with total count
2. Logo walls of recognizable customers
3. Third-party badges (G2, Trustpilot, BBB)
4. Media mentions and press coverage
5. Before/after visuals and ROI metrics

### Purchase Influence Strategies
1. Display relevant testimonials at high-intent pages (pricing, checkout)
2. Exit-intent popups with social proof
3. Live notification widgets showing recent purchases/signups
4. Comparison pages highlighting customer preferences
5. Retargeting ads featuring customer success stories
```
