# Virtual Field Trip Planner for K-12 Educators

## 簡介

The Virtual Field Trip Planner for K-12 Educators is a free AI prompt that designs immersive virtual learning experiences tailored to any subject area for elementary through high school students. This virtual field trip prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces three fully developed trip concepts, each featuring a destination description that brings the location to life, three specific learning objectives aligned to curriculum standards, and three follow-up activities to reinforce concepts. It also generates a planning table that maps preparation steps, technology requirements, and assessment methods for each destination, plus curated source lists to support implementation. Teachers use it to create engaging remote experiences for science (virtual lab tours, ecosystem exploration), social studies (historical sites, cultural institutions), arts (museum collections, performance venues), and any other subject where place-based learning adds depth. This prompt is for K-12 educators, curriculum coordinators, and homeschool parents who want to expand learning beyond the classroom without the cost or logistics of physical trips. ● Designs three distinct virtual destinations per subject with rich descriptions that set context and spark curiosity ● Provides clear learning objectives and follow-up activities for each trip to ensure educational outcomes ● Includes a planning table that covers preparation, technology needs, and assessment strategies for seamless execution ● Generates credible source lists to validate destination information and support further research ## Prompt
