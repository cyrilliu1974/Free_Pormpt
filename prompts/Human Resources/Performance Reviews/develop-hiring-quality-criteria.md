# Quality of Hire Measurement System Builder

## 簡介

The Quality of Hire Measurement System Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven frameworks to track hiring effectiveness over time for talent acquisition teams and HR analytics leaders. This quality of hire prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok generates a complete measurement system with scoring formulas, multi-checkpoint tracking schedules (30, 60, 90, 180, and 365 days post-hire), dashboard specifications, and comparative analysis frameworks that identify patterns across recruitment sources, recruiters, and hiring managers. It applies Dr. John Sullivan's Quality of Hire Index methodology - combining performance metrics, retention data, and manager satisfaction - to move beyond traditional speed-and-cost hiring KPIs. Use it when you need to prove recruitment ROI to stakeholders, diagnose high turnover, or establish statistically valid benchmarks that predict long-term employee success. ● Executive summary explaining why longitudinal measurement outperforms single-point assessment and the three-component Index methodology ● Data collection framework specifying metrics, measurement intervals, and system integration points for performance, retention, and manager satisfaction ● Step-by-step scoring formulas with weighting factors, mathematical notation, and sample calculations ● Baseline establishment methodology using historical data, statistically valid target-setting, and bias-free metric design ● Dashboard layouts, visualization requirements, alert thresholds, and multi-checkpoint tracking schedules ● Comparative analysis tables to detect statistically significant differences (p<0.05) and root cause analysis for voluntary versus involuntary turnover ● Phased implementation roadmap with resource requirements, stakeholder engagement strategy, and scaling best practices ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a talent acquisition analytics specialist who designs measurement systems that track hiring quality over time, combining data-driven metrics with behavioral science to predict long-term employee success.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive Quality of Hire measurement system based on Dr. John Sullivan's Quality of Hire Index methodology. Create actionable benchmarks, tracking mechanisms, and comparative analysis frameworks that prove recruitment effectiveness to stakeholders.

## Context
Traditional hiring metrics focus on speed and cost while missing quality dimensions. Single-point assessments fail to capture the true impact of hiring decisions. The organization experiences high talent turnover while competitors successfully retain top performers, and previous measurement attempts failed because they didn't track outcomes over time or identify patterns across recruitment sources, recruiters, and hiring managers.

**Business Context**: {{org-and-hiring-context}}
*Include: organization size, industry, annual hiring volume, current HRIS/ATS platforms, and top 3 hiring pain points*

## Output
Deliver a complete Quality of Hire measurement system structured as follows:

### 1. Executive Summary
- Explain the Quality of Hire Index methodology and its three core components: performance, retention, and manager satisfaction
- Justify why longitudinal measurement outperforms single-point assessment

### 2. Data Collection Framework
For each component (performance, retention, manager satisfaction):
- Specific metrics to capture
- Measurement intervals and data sources
- Integration points with existing HR systems

### 3. Scoring & Calculation
- Step-by-step formulas for composite Quality of Hire scores
- Weighting factors customizable by role criticality and organizational priorities
- Mathematical notation with worked examples

### 4. Baseline & Targets
- Methodology for establishing baseline using minimum 6-month historical data
- Process for setting statistically valid improvement targets
- Approach for ensuring legal defensibility and bias-free metrics

### 5. Tracking System Design
- Multi-checkpoint measurement schedule: 30, 60, 90, 180, and 365 days post-hire
- Dashboard requirements (describe layouts, key visualizations, alert thresholds)
- HRIS/performance management platform integration specifications

### 6. Comparative Analysis Frameworks
Create frameworks to identify patterns across:
- Recruitment sources (job boards, referrals, agencies, etc.)
- Individual recruiters
- Hiring managers

Include tables showing how to detect statistically significant differences (p<0.05) and root cause analysis for retention data (voluntary vs involuntary turnover).

### 7. Implementation Roadmap
- Phased rollout plan with milestones
- Resource requirements and stakeholder engagement strategy
- Scaling best practices as hiring volume grows

**Requirements**:
- Focus on leading indicators that predict future performance, not just lagging measures
- Performance metrics must include quantitative outputs and qualitative assessments from multiple stakeholders
- Avoid vanity metrics; ensure all metrics correlate with business outcomes
- Use bullet points for lists, tables for comparisons, numbered steps for processes
- Provide concrete examples and sample calculations throughout
```
