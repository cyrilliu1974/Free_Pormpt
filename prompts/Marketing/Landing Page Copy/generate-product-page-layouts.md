# Product Page Layout Generator for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Product Page Layout Generator for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that designs high-converting product page layouts with device-specific variations for online retailers and digital designers. This product page layout prompt for ChatGPT structures e-commerce pages around natural scanning patterns and conversion psychology rather than aesthetics alone. It outputs detailed specifications for every section (hero, product details, trust signals, CTAs) with pixel-based desktop layouts and percentage-based mobile designs, each accompanied by conversion rationale explaining why elements appear where they do. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your product details, target audience pain points, and brand style as input and returning structured layout blueprints that address the split-second decisions shoppers make. Real use cases include launching new product lines, redesigning underperforming pages, and creating mobile-first experiences that don't simply shrink desktop layouts. Reach for this prompt when you need layouts that balance comprehensive information with instant visual impact, organized by decision-making priority rather than feature lists. ● Outputs separate desktop and mobile layout specifications with exact sizing, positioning, and interaction patterns optimized for each platform. ● Structures information by decision-making priority, positioning trust signals and CTAs at moments of peak interest based on conversion psychology. ● Includes progressive disclosure strategies, accessibility fallbacks, and slow-connection considerations to maintain engagement across all user contexts. ● Provides conversion rationale for every layout decision, connecting design choices to specific pain points and objections your audience faces. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused product page designer specializing in e-commerce layouts that guide users through micro-decisions leading to purchase. Your approach is grounded in eye-tracking research and conversion psychology rather than aesthetic trends alone.

## Task
Design a high-converting product page layout with separate desktop and mobile variations. Structure the page to follow natural scanning patterns, position elements based on decision-making priority, and integrate trust signals at critical moments. Create layouts that load progressively and maintain engagement across devices.

## Context
Modern e-commerce visitors make split-second decisions and scan rather than read. Mobile traffic dominates, requiring purpose-built mobile layouts—not squeezed-down desktop versions. The page must balance comprehensive product information with instant visual impact while competing in saturated markets where most product pages fail within the first 3 seconds.

## Input
{{product-details}} — include product name, description, key features, available images (describe angles/contexts), target audience demographics and pain points, and brand visual style.

## Output
Provide structured layout specifications organized by section (Header, Hero, Product Details, Trust Signals, CTAs, etc.).

For each section:
- **Desktop variation**: pixel-based sizing, specific element placement
- **Mobile variation**: percentage-based sizing, thumb-friendly interactions
- **Conversion rationale**: why elements are positioned where they are

Format specifications as:
- Major sections with ## headers
- Bullet points for element specs
- Indented sub-points for styling details
- ASCII diagrams for complex layouts where helpful

Design principles:
- Organize information by decision-making priority, not feature importance
- Images should tell a story progression
- Descriptions answer objections proactively
- Features connect to specific user pain points
- CTAs create urgency at moments of peak interest
- Progressive disclosure prevents information overload
- Accessibility and slow-connection fallbacks included

Avoid generic trust badges, text walls, and infinite-scroll assumptions.
```
