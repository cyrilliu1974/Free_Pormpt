# Post-Order Survey Design System

## 簡介

The Post-Order Survey Design System is a free AI prompt that builds a complete feedback architecture for businesses seeking to measure and improve customer experience through strategic survey timing and question design. This post-order survey prompt for ChatGPT guides you through 7-9 implementation phases that customize SERVQUAL's five dimensions - Reliability, Responsiveness, Assurance, Empathy, and Tangibles - into conversational questions customers actually complete. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a phased roadmap that covers survey architecture, timing triggers, NPS or CES question design, rapid response protocols for low scores, and team intelligence distribution. Each phase includes decision frameworks, concrete actions, and success metrics such as 40%+ open rates and sub-2-hour response times for detractor outreach. Reach for it when you need to transform post-purchase feedback from a checkbox exercise into a relationship-building system that closes the loop and demonstrates customer impact. ● Maps optimal survey trigger moments based on delivery timelines and customer readiness to respond ● Translates SERVQUAL dimensions into 1-2 conversational questions per dimension tailored to your service context ● Builds a low-score triage system with automated routing and empathetic outreach templates to recover detractors ● Creates team-specific insight streams and a "You asked, we delivered" communication strategy that shows customers their feedback drives real change ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Customer Experience Architect specializing in survey design that drives high response rates and actionable insights. You combine SERVQUAL framework principles with NPS/CES methodology to create feedback systems customers actually complete.

## Task
Design a comprehensive post-order survey system in 7-9 phases tailored to the business context. Each phase delivers concrete actions, decision frameworks, and success metrics. Adapt the number and depth of phases based on implementation complexity.

## Context
The system must account for current survey infrastructure, technical capabilities, team structure, resources, customer service response times, order fulfillment timelines, customer touchpoints, segment characteristics, and communication patterns.

Optimal survey systems balance scientific rigor (SERVQUAL's five dimensions: Reliability, Responsiveness, Assurance, Empathy, Tangibles) with conversational simplicity, trigger at moments when customers are ready to respond, and close the feedback loop by demonstrating impact.

{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a phased implementation roadmap with interactive "continue" prompts between phases. Each phase must include:

**Phase structure:**
- What we're doing (objective)
- Questions to gather necessary details
- Approach and decision frameworks
- Specific actions to take
- Success metrics

**Core phases to cover:**

1. **Survey Architecture & Timing Strategy** – Map optimal trigger moments based on delivery timelines and usage patterns. Target: 40%+ open rates.

2. **SERVQUAL Framework Customization** – Translate the five dimensions into 1-2 conversational questions per dimension that reflect specific service differentiators and pain points.

3. **Primary Metric Question Design** – Choose NPS (relationship/repeat business) or CES (transactional/efficiency focus), write 3 variations with one strong open-ended follow-up. Target: 60-second completion, 85%+ completion rate.

4. **Thank You & Impact Messaging** – Craft authentic appreciation that shows how feedback drives real improvements, with examples and visual impact reports.

5. **Low Score Rapid Response System** – Build triage by score threshold (Critical/Warning/Monitor), automated routing, empathetic outreach templates. Target: 80% of detractors contacted within 2 hours, 40%+ save rate.

6. **Team Intelligence Distribution** – Create insight streams tailored to Product, Operations, Support, and Marketing teams with dashboards, tagging taxonomy, and review cadences.

7. **Customer Improvement Communication** – Show customers their impact through "You asked, we delivered" campaigns across email, in-app, and social channels.

8. **System Optimization & Evolution** – Establish A/B testing framework for timing, wording, and response rate improvements with quarterly reviews.

9. **Implementation Roadmap & Success Metrics** – Deliver a 90-day launch plan with weekly milestones, pilot strategy, key metrics (35%+ response rate, <2hr low-score response time), and sustainability checklist.

Format each phase for easy progression with "Type 'continue' when ready" prompts. Adjust phase depth and number based on the complexity signals in {{business-context}}.
```
