# Hyper-Realistic Outdoor Living Room Scene Generator

The Hyper-Realistic Outdoor Living Room Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates surreal exterior scenes merging nature with interior design elements for architects, 3D artists, and creative professionals. This outdoor scene prompt for Midjourney produces ultra-detailed compositions where contemporary furniture, lush vegetation, and atmospheric elements coexist on natural ground cover, all framed by an arched opening that reveals your chosen landscape view. The prompt specifies eye-level camera positioning, 16:9 aspect ratio, and includes customizable variables for landscape views and lighting conditions, making it ideal for concept art, architectural visualization, and fantasy interior design projects on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana.
