# Google Ads Keyword Research Generator

## 簡介

The Google Ads Keyword Research Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive keyword lists for digital marketers planning paid search campaigns. This Google Ads keyword research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes industry trends, customer search behavior, and competitor strategies to deliver at least 20 targeted keywords organized in a markdown table with search volume estimates and competition ratings. The output includes broad match, phrase match, and long-tail keyword variations aligned to your business context, target audience, and geographic location. Marketing teams use it to quickly build keyword foundations for new campaigns, identify untapped search opportunities, and evaluate competitive intensity before budget allocation. Reach for this prompt when launching Google Ads campaigns, expanding into new markets, or auditing existing keyword portfolios for businesses of any size or industry. ● Produces 20+ keywords across match types, ranked by estimated monthly search volume and competition level ● Incorporates business-specific context, audience demographics, and geographic targeting into keyword selection ● Delivers results in a sortable markdown table format ready for campaign planning tools ● Identifies long-tail variations that balance search intent with competitive opportunity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing specialist focused on Google Ads keyword research and campaign optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of at least 20 high-performing Google Ads keywords tailored to the specified business. Research relevant industry trends, analyze customer search behavior and competitor strategies, then identify keywords across match types including long-tail variations. Evaluate each keyword for relevance, estimated search volume, and competition level.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Geographic focus: {{geographic-location}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Competition |
|---------|--------------|-------------|

For each keyword, provide:
- **Keyword**: The exact search term or phrase
- **Search Volume**: Estimated monthly searches (High/Medium/Low or approximate range)
- **Competition**: Competitive intensity (High/Medium/Low)

Include a mix of broad, phrase, and long-tail keywords that align with the business's unique selling proposition and geographic market.
```
