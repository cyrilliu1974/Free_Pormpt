# Blue Matte 3D Icon Generator

## 簡介

The Blue Matte 3D Icon Generator is a free AI prompt that retextures uploaded images into stylized 3D iconography with soft-touch matte rubber materials and pastel cyan coloring for designers and UI creators. This blue matte icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana transforms any source image into clean, professional 3D icons with smooth surfaces, slightly rounded edges, and studio lighting. It produces isometric or front-centered renders with pastel cyan as the primary color, off-white accents, and light gray-to-white gradient backgrounds - perfect for app interfaces, design systems, marketing assets, and SaaS branding. The prompt specifies no reflections, bloom, or depth-of-field effects, ensuring a consistent, polished look across all generated icons. Reach for this prompt when you need cohesive icon sets, branded UI elements, or product illustrations with a modern, tactile aesthetic. ● Retextures uploaded images with soft-touch matte rubber material, pastel cyan base, and off-white text or logos ● Applies even soft studio lighting with no reflections, refractions, or glow effects for a clean, consistent finish ● Outputs isometric or front-centered angles with the entire object in focus and medium-blur soft shadows ● Supports customizable text treatment with bold sans-serif fonts, engraved or extruded styles, and centered or top alignment ## Prompt

```
Retexture the attached image as stylized 3D iconography with soft-touch matte rubber material. Apply pastel cyan as the primary color with off-white text and logos as accents. Surface should be smooth and clean with slightly rounded edges.

**Lighting:** Soft studio lighting at medium intensity, even top and front light. Use pastel cyan and light gray as accent colors. No reflections, refractions, dispersion effects, or bloom.

**Background:** Light gray to white gradient, no vignette, no texture.

**Camera:** Isometric or front-centered angle, entire object in focus, no depth of field.

**Text:** {{text-treatment}} font in bold sans-serif, color slightly darker or lighter than base, centered or top-aligned.

**Shadows:** Soft, low intensity, downward direction, medium blur.

**Post-processing:** No chromatic aberration, glow, or high contrast. Emphasize cyan and white colors. Medium sharpness, clean clarity.
```
