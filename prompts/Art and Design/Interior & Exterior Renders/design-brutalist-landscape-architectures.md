# Brutalist Landform Architecture Visualization Prompt

## 簡介

The Brutalist Landform Architecture Visualization Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates competition-grade architectural visualizations of terrain-integrated structures for architects, landscape designers, and environmental planners. This landform architecture prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces ultra-realistic images where the ground itself becomes the built structure - sunken courtyards carved into sloping terrain, elevated platforms connected by wide ramps, and pedestrian pathways emerging organically from natural topography. You specify the site landscape (coastal cliffs, desert mesa, forested hillside), ground geometry (interconnected courtyards, layered planes, carved volumes), and time of day to control shadow drama. The output features exposed fair-faced concrete with weathered matte finishes, native vegetation clustering around architectural voids, and small human figures providing scale reference in contemplative, monumental spatial environments.
