# Instagram Video Script Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The Instagram Video Script Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates short-form promotional video scripts optimized for Instagram's 15-60 second format. This Instagram video script prompt for ChatGPT helps marketers and content creators structure compelling narratives that stop scrolling and drive action. It generates complete scripts with attention-grabbing opening hooks, naturally woven product features and benefits, persuasive calls-to-action, and visual cues for on-screen elements. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity - leading with core ideas and layering supporting details logically. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, it accepts three variables: your product or service, target audience, and brand voice to produce customized scripts. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn product information into scroll-stopping Instagram content that balances entertainment with conversion goals. ● Structures scripts into four clear sections: opening hook, key product points, call-to-action, and visual cues for editors and creators ● Optimizes specifically for Instagram's short-form video constraints and viewer behavior patterns ● Uses dependency grammar framework to ensure every sentence builds logically and maintains clarity under tight time limits ● Generates visual direction alongside spoken copy so creators know what to show on screen ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Instagram video script writer specializing in short-form promotional content.

## Task
Write a compelling Instagram video script that promotes {{product-or-service}} and drives brand awareness for {{target-audience}}.

## Context
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

The script must:
- Open with an attention-grabbing hook that stops scrolling
- Weave product features and benefits naturally into the narrative
- Use persuasive language and a clear call-to-action
- Optimize for Instagram's short-form video format (15-60 seconds typical)
- Follow dependency grammar principles for clarity: lead with core ideas, layer supporting details logically

## Output
Structure your script with these sections:

**Opening Hook**
- [bullet points for the first 2-3 seconds]

**Key Points**
- [bullet points covering product benefits and features]

**Call-to-Action**
- [clear next step for viewers]

**Visual Cues**
- [bullet points suggesting on-screen actions, text overlays, transitions]
```
