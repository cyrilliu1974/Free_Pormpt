# Chargeback Prevention Strategy Prompt for Business

## 簡介

The Chargeback Prevention Strategy Prompt for Business is a free AI prompt that creates tailored, multi-phase prevention plans to stop disputes before they occur for e-commerce, SaaS, and service businesses. This chargeback prevention prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product category's unique dispute triggers - whether digital or physical, subscription or one-time - maps the complete customer journey from purchase through post-delivery, and identifies psychological friction points where confusion or dissatisfaction breed chargebacks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, dynamically scaling from 3 to 15 phases based on your product complexity, risk level, and business maturity. Ideal for payment operations teams, customer success managers, and business owners facing chargeback losses who need an actionable roadmap with proactive touchpoints, communication scripts, and measurable risk reduction at every stage. ● Analyzes dispute triggers specific to your product category (digital vs. physical goods, instant vs. delayed fulfillment, subscription vs. one-time transactions) and customer journey stages. ● Delivers phase-by-phase prevention actions including communication scripts, policy clarifications, automation triggers, and success metrics tailored to your business profile and prevention goals. ● Determines optimal phase count (3-5 for simple products, 6-8 for services, 9-12 for high-risk categories, 13-15 for complex ecosystems) with implementation timelines and risk reduction estimates. ● Provides an immediately actionable roadmap that addresses psychological friction points before they escalate into disputes, chargebacks, or lost customer relationships. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert Chargeback Prevention Strategist who analyzes transaction friction points and designs proactive communication strategies that prevent disputes before they occur.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, adaptive chargeback prevention plan tailored to the user's business profile. The plan will dynamically scale from 3 to 15 phases based on product complexity, risk level, and business maturity.

## Context

Chargebacks often stem from preventable customer friction—unclear expectations, poor communication, or unresolved concerns. Your prevention plan transforms potential disputes into loyalty opportunities by addressing psychological triggers at each stage of the customer journey.

**Business Profile:**
{{business-profile}}

**Prevention Goals:**
{{prevention-goals}}

## Process

1. **Analyze** the product category's unique dispute triggers (digital vs. physical, instant vs. delayed delivery, subscription vs. one-time)
2. **Map** the customer journey from purchase through post-delivery, identifying moments where confusion or dissatisfaction arise
3. **Identify** psychological friction points that create dispute mindsets (unclear policies, communication gaps, unmet expectations)
4. **Determine** the optimal number of phases:
   - Simple products: 3–5 phases
   - Service-based businesses: 6–8 phases
   - High-risk categories: 9–12 phases
   - Complex ecosystems: 13–15 phases
5. **Engineer** proactive touchpoints and clarity mechanisms for each phase

## Output

Deliver a structured chargeback prevention plan with:

- **Phase count and rationale** based on the business profile
- **Phase-by-phase breakdown**, each containing:
  - Dispute triggers specific to that stage
  - Preventive actions (communication scripts, policy clarifications, automation triggers)
  - Success metrics
- **Implementation timeline** with quick wins and long-term strategies
- **Risk reduction estimate** for each phase

Format the plan as a clear, actionable roadmap that can be implemented immediately.
```
