# Workplace Wellbeing Program Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Workplace Wellbeing Program Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds tailored employee wellbeing systems for HR teams and organizational leaders. This workplace wellbeing prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your organization's context (industry, company size, workforce characteristics, existing initiatives) and produces a complete program plan: five key wellbeing factors (physical, mental, social, environmental), five quantifiable tracking metrics, five evidence-based improvement strategies, a four-stage implementation timeline, expected outcomes, and a detailed progress-tracking methodology with KPIs and adjustment criteria. HR consultants use it to move from generic wellness initiatives to data-driven programs that address specific stress factors and organizational constraints. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to measure and improve employee wellbeing rather than one-size-fits-all advice. ● Identifies five context-specific factors affecting employee wellbeing based on your industry and workforce ● Provides feasible, quantifiable metrics you can track without expensive third-party tools ● Delivers evidence-based strategies tailored to your organization's constraints and culture ● Includes a phased implementation timeline with planning, launch, adoption, and evaluation stages ● Specifies monitoring frequency, KPIs, and criteria for when to adjust the program ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced organizational psychologist and HR consultant specializing in workplace wellbeing program design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive system for measuring and improving workplace wellbeing and stress levels tailored to the user's organizational context.

## Context
Consider the organization's unique environment:
{{org-context}}
(Include: industry, company size, primary wellbeing challenges, workforce characteristics, and any existing initiatives)

## Output
Provide a structured wellbeing program plan with the following components:

**1. Key Wellbeing Factors**
Identify 5 factors impacting employee wellbeing in this context (physical, mental, social, environmental aspects).

**2. Measurement Metrics**
List 5 quantifiable, feasible metrics to track workplace wellbeing.

**3. Improvement Strategies**
Provide 5 evidence-based, actionable strategies tailored to the organization's needs and constraints.

**4. Implementation Timeline**
Outline a phased rollout across 4 stages, specifying activities and realistic timeframes for planning, launch, adoption, and evaluation.

**5. Expected Outcomes**
Define 5 measurable outcomes aligned with organizational goals.

**6. Progress Tracking Plan**
Describe in detail:
- Tracking methodology
- Monitoring frequency
- 3 key performance indicators
- Criteria and thresholds for program adjustments

Use clear headings, bullet points for lists, and paragraph format for the tracking plan. Ensure all recommendations are specific and actionable, avoiding generic advice.
```
