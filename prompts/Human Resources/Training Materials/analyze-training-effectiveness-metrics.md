# Training Effectiveness Analysis With Kirkpatrick Model

## 簡介

The Training Effectiveness Analysis With Kirkpatrick Model is a free AI prompt that transforms raw training data into a comprehensive evaluation framework across Reaction, Learning, Behavior, and Results levels for L&D professionals and training specialists. This training effectiveness prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze your program data, organizational goals, and stakeholder requirements, then output a structured assessment with current-state findings, specific KPIs for each Kirkpatrick level, practical measurement methods, realistic timelines, and improvement recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping you justify training investments by connecting participant reactions and skill development to on-the-job behavior changes and measurable business outcomes. Use it when you need to prove ROI, identify measurement gaps, or transform activity-based metrics into impact-driven insights. ● Assesses engagement patterns, satisfaction trends, knowledge acquisition, and skill development evidence at Levels 1 and 2. ● Tracks transfer of learning to workplace behavior and investigates connections between training outcomes and business results at Levels 3 and 4. ● Recommends data collection approaches, evaluation tools, and measurement timelines that capture meaningful insights without participant burden. ● Provides improvement recommendations for training design, delivery, and follow-up based on identified effectiveness gaps. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert training evaluation specialist with deep expertise in implementing the Kirkpatrick Four-Level Evaluation Model to connect learning outcomes to measurable business impact.

## Task
Analyze training effectiveness data and develop a comprehensive evaluation framework across all four Kirkpatrick levels (Reaction, Learning, Behavior, Results). Transform raw training data into strategic insights that justify investment and guide program improvements.

## Context
Training program and data:
{{training-program-and-data}}

Business objectives the training should support:
{{organizational-goals}}

Stakeholder requirements and current measurement challenges:
{{stakeholder-requirements-and-challenges}}

You operate in an environment where training budgets face constant scrutiny, stakeholders demand ROI proof, and previous evaluations failed by measuring activities instead of impact.

## Analysis Framework
For each Kirkpatrick level:

**Level 1 – Reaction**
- Examine engagement patterns, satisfaction trends, and immediate participant responses
- Identify current measurement gaps and missed opportunities

**Level 2 – Learning**
- Evaluate evidence of knowledge acquisition and skill development
- Assess whether learning objectives were achieved

**Level 3 – Behavior**
- Assess indicators of on-the-job application and performance changes
- Track transfer of learning to workplace behavior

**Level 4 – Results**
- Investigate connections between training outcomes and business results
- Link learning impact to organizational KPIs

## Output
Structure your analysis with clear **Level 1**, **Level 2**, **Level 3**, and **Level 4** headings. For each level provide:

- **Current State Assessment**: What the data reveals
- **Specific KPIs**: Measurable indicators aligned with organizational goals
- **Measurement Methods**: Data collection approaches and evaluation tools that capture meaningful insights without overwhelming participants
- **Realistic Timelines**: When to measure each level
- **Improvement Recommendations**: Actionable steps for enhancing training design, delivery, and follow-up based on identified effectiveness gaps

Present all findings in bullet-point format for clarity and actionability.
```
