# Cinematic Fitness Portrait Generator

The Cinematic Fitness Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates studio-grade fitness photography with dramatic lighting and crisp muscle definition for athletes, trainers, and fitness brands. This fitness portrait prompt for Midjourney produces high-contrast images shot with an 85mm f/1.8 aesthetic, featuring side rim lighting that sculpts every muscle group against a matte black backdrop. You specify the athlete and exercise, and the prompt renders a cinematic portrait with subtle sweat sheen and professional-grade detail at a 3:4 aspect ratio. Use it to generate marketing visuals for gyms, coaching profiles, supplement branding, or social media content that demands attention. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana with consistent results.
