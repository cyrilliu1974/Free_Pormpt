# Local SEO Citation Optimization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Local SEO Citation Optimization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed, actionable strategy to improve local search visibility through accurate and consistent business listings across major directories. This local SEO citation prompt for ChatGPT takes your business name, address, phone number, website, and hours (NAP details) and produces a comprehensive optimization plan covering Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and industry-specific directories. The prompt acts as a local SEO expert, walking through citation auditing with tools like Moz Local or BrightLocal, verification procedures, NAP standardization techniques, content enhancement strategies, platform-specific feature recommendations, review generation tactics, and ongoing maintenance schedules. Marketing teams and local business owners use it to eliminate duplicate listings, fix inconsistencies, and build a stronger local search presence that drives foot traffic and leads. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured roadmap to clean up and optimize your local citations, whether launching a new location, rebranding, or fixing existing listing problems across multiple platforms. ● Audits existing citations across platforms to identify duplicates, inconsistencies, and gaps in coverage ● Provides claiming and verification instructions for Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, and Apple Maps ● Ensures NAP information is standardized with zero formatting variation across all directories ● Recommends platform-specific optimizations like Google Posts, Yelp check-in offers, and booking integrations ● Includes review generation tactics and professional response strategies to build credibility ● Establishes monitoring schedules and quarterly audit processes to maintain listing accuracy over time ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a local SEO expert specializing in optimizing business citations for improved online visibility and local search rankings.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, step-by-step local citation optimization plan that covers auditing, claiming, standardizing, and maintaining business listings across key directories and platforms.

## Context

Business details:
{{business-nap-details}}

## Process

### Objective
Improve local search visibility, attract more customers, and drive business growth through consistent, accurate, and optimized local citations.

### Key Platforms
1. Google Business Profile
2. Yelp
3. Bing Places
4. Apple Maps
5. Industry-specific directories

### Optimization Steps

**1. Audit Existing Citations**
- Use tools like Moz Local, Whitespark, or BrightLocal to identify current listings
- Document inconsistencies, duplicates, or missing information
- Prioritize platforms for cleanup and optimization

**2. Claim and Verify Listings**
- Complete verification process for each unclaimed listing (phone, email, or postcard)
- Secure direct ownership of all available listings

**3. Standardize NAP Information**
- Apply the exact business name, address, phone, website, and hours from {{business-nap-details}} consistently across ALL platforms
- Ensure zero variation in formatting

**4. Enhance Listing Content**
- Write compelling, keyword-rich business descriptions
- Upload high-quality photos of the business, products, and services
- Select relevant business categories
- Complete additional fields (payment types, parking, accessibility, etc.)

**5. Leverage Platform-Specific Features**
- Google Business Profile: Posts, Q&A, Products, Services, Booking
- Yelp: Check-in offers, Call-to-Action buttons, Business highlights
- Bing Places: Business photos, Services, Amenities
- Maximize all available fields to create robust listings

**6. Build Review Strategy**
- Implement review generation tactics: email campaigns, in-store signage, post-purchase follow-ups
- Respond professionally to all reviews, both positive and negative
- Focus on platforms most relevant to the industry

**7. Monitor and Maintain**
- Set up alerts for listing changes or edits
- Schedule quarterly audits to catch inconsistencies
- Update information promptly when business details change
- Refresh content regularly to maintain listing quality

## Output

Deliver the complete plan structured as above, with specific tactical recommendations for each step tailored to the business information provided.
```
