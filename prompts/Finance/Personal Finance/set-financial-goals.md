# Financial Goal-Setting Prompt for Self-Employed

## 簡介

The Financial Goal-Setting Prompt for Self-Employed is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable financial plans for solopreneurs and independent business owners. This financial goal-setting prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a SMART objectives framework - Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound - designed around your unique business profile. You supply details about your self-employment type, current finances, aspirations, challenges, and risk tolerance; the prompt returns a markdown table listing at least five financial goals, each paired with realistic target dates and concrete action steps covering revenue growth, expense control, profit margin improvement, and cash flow optimization. It also explains how to track progress and when to revisit your plan. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need strategic financial clarity beyond generic advice - when your freelance income fluctuates, when quarterly tax planning feels overwhelming, or when you want a growth roadmap that respects your risk tolerance. ● Produces at least five SMART financial goals aligned with your business type and current financial situation. ● Covers four core pillars: revenue growth strategies, expense management tactics, profit margin targets, and cash flow optimization. ● Includes realistic target dates and step-by-step action plans for each objective. ● Adds a progress-tracking section with guidance on when and how to adjust goals as your business evolves. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial advisor specializing in self-employment business growth and financial planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive financial goal-setting plan with SMART objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) tailored to the user's self-employment business. Address revenue growth, expense management, profit margins, and cash flow optimization. Include guidance on tracking progress and adjusting goals over time.

## Context
Business and financial profile:
{{business-and-financial-profile}}

(Include: type of self-employment business, current financial situation, long-term financial aspirations, main financial challenges, and risk tolerance level)

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Financial Goal | Target Date | Action Steps |
|---|---|---|

Provide at least 5 SMART financial goals, each with:
- **Financial Goal**: A specific, measurable objective aligned with the business profile
- **Target Date**: A realistic timeline for achievement
- **Action Steps**: Concrete, actionable tasks to reach the goal

After the table, add a brief section on how to track progress and when to revisit and adjust these goals.
```
