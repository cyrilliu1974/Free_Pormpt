# SEO-Optimized FAQ Section Generator

## 簡介

The SEO-Optimized FAQ Section Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a complete, search-friendly FAQ section for websites in any industry. This FAQ prompt for ChatGPT conducts keyword research for your specific website and industry, identifying 5-10 high-value search terms with volume and difficulty metrics. It then crafts questions that reflect real user search intent and delivers concise, informative answers (2-4 sentences each) that incorporate keywords naturally without stuffing. The prompt also provides 3-5 actionable optimization recommendations to improve the FAQ section's performance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only your website URL and industry as inputs. Website owners, content marketers, and SEO specialists use this prompt when they need to create or refresh FAQ sections that serve both search engines and real visitors. ● Performs keyword research with search volume and difficulty analysis specific to your industry ● Creates questions that match actual user search intent rather than forced keyword phrases ● Writes clear, helpful answers that prioritize readability and value over keyword density ● Includes actionable recommendations to further enhance FAQ performance and visibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO expert and content strategist developing a comprehensive, search-optimized FAQ section that addresses common customer concerns, provides genuine value, and incorporates relevant keywords naturally.

## Task
Conduct keyword research for {{website-url}} in the {{industry}} sector, then develop a complete FAQ section that ranks well in search engines while serving visitor needs.

## Process
1. **Keyword Research**: Identify 5-10 high-value keywords relevant to the industry and website, noting search volume and difficulty for each
2. **FAQ Development**: For each keyword, craft:
   - A clear question that naturally incorporates the targeted keyword
   - An informative answer (2-4 sentences) that addresses the question and uses the keyword organically
3. **Optimization Recommendations**: Provide 3-5 actionable tactics to further enhance the FAQ section's performance

## Requirements
- Prioritize readability and user value over keyword density
- Avoid keyword stuffing or manipulative SEO tactics
- Ensure answers are concise, accurate, and genuinely helpful
- Questions should reflect real user search intent

## Output Format
**Target Website**: {{website-url}}  
**Target Industry**: {{industry}}

**Keyword Research Results**:
- Keyword 1 (Search Volume: X, Difficulty: Y)
- Keyword 2 (Search Volume: X, Difficulty: Y)
- [Continue for 5-10 keywords]

**FAQ Section**:

**Q1**: [Question incorporating Keyword 1]?  
**A1**: [Clear, informative answer naturally using Keyword 1]

**Q2**: [Question incorporating Keyword 2]?  
**A2**: [Clear, informative answer naturally using Keyword 2]

[Continue for each keyword]

**Additional Recommendations**:
- [Recommendation 1 to further optimize the FAQ section]
- [Recommendation 2 to further optimize the FAQ section]
- [Continue for 3-5 recommendations]
```
