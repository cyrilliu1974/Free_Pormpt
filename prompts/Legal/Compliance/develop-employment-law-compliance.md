# Employment Law Compliance Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Employment Law Compliance Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive legal risk mitigation strategies for businesses navigating hiring, performance management, and termination processes. This employment law compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates structured checklists, documentation templates, red-flag warnings, and step-by-step protocols that cover the full employment lifecycle. It addresses common litigation triggers including discrimination claims under Title VII, ADA and FMLA violations, wrongful termination suits, and wage and hour disputes by building consistent policy application and legally sound documentation practices. The framework includes jurisdiction-specific state law considerations, disability accommodation protocols, performance improvement plan templates, and termination procedures designed to withstand legal scrutiny. HR professionals, business owners, and compliance officers reach for this prompt when they need practical, implementation-ready guidance to reduce exposure to costly employment litigation and maintain workplace fairness. ● Pre-hiring legal foundations and compliant hiring practices with discrimination-prevention checklists ● Documentation templates and performance management protocols that ensure consistent policy enforcement ● Legally defensible termination procedures with timeline recommendations and red-flag alerts for when to consult counsel ● Jurisdiction-specific state law guidance and accommodation request protocols for ADA, FMLA, and disability situations ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an employment law attorney and HR risk management consultant specializing in wrongful termination defense, discrimination claims, and compliance strategy.

## Task

Provide a comprehensive, legally defensible framework for hiring and termination that protects against litigation while ensuring compliance with federal and state employment law. Cover the full employment lifecycle: pre-hiring foundations, compliant hiring practices, documentation requirements, performance management protocols, and termination procedures.

## Context

Most employment litigation stems from poor documentation, inconsistent policy application, and procedural failures during hiring or termination. Address common legal risks including discrimination claims, wrongful termination, wage and hour violations, and failure to accommodate disabilities under Title VII, ADA, FMLA, and applicable state laws.

**Business profile:**  
{{business-context}}

## Output

Structure your response with clear section headings and actionable bullet points. For each phase of the employment lifecycle, provide:

- **Checklists** with specific steps and timeline recommendations
- **Documentation templates** with recommended language for policies and records
- **Red flags** that signal when to consult legal counsel
- **Step-by-step protocols** for high-risk situations (disability accommodation requests, performance improvement plans, termination)
- **Jurisdiction-specific considerations** that vary by state law

Include concrete examples and template language where applicable. Prioritize practical implementation over theory.
```
