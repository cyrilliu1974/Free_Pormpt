# Cold Email Template Coach

## 簡介

The Cold Email Template Coach is a free AI prompt that writes concise, benefit-driven cold outreach emails tailored to your target audience, their pain points, your offer, and social proof. This cold email prompt for ChatGPT helps you craft personalized outreach messages that demonstrate industry understanding, present clear solutions, and include credible testimonials or statistics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing ready-to-send emails with attention-grabbing subject lines, 150–200 word bodies that emphasize benefits over features, and professional closings. Sales teams, founders, and marketers use it to generate cold emails that resonate with prospects in specific industries, turning template creation from guesswork into a structured, repeatable process. Reach for this prompt when you need a proven outreach structure that balances personalization with scalability, especially when launching campaigns to new segments or testing messaging angles. ● Produces a complete email structure: subject line, personalized opening, pain-point acknowledgment, two key benefits, social proof placement, and specific call to action. ● Keeps the body between 150 and 200 words to respect recipient time while delivering a complete value proposition. ● Guides you to input target audience, pain points, product or service details, and a statistic or testimonial, then weaves them into a conversational, benefit-focused message. ● Outputs a format ready to personalize with recipient names and send, reducing the friction between drafting and deploying outreach campaigns. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer specializing in cold outreach that converts prospects into clients.

## Task
Write a concise, persuasive cold email template tailored to the provided audience and offering.

## Context
The email must:
- Open with an attention-grabbing subject line
- Demonstrate understanding of the recipient's industry and challenges
- Present the solution and its two most compelling benefits
- Build credibility through social proof
- Close with a clear, specific call to action
- Maintain 150–200 words in the body
- Use a conversational tone that emphasizes benefits over features

**Input:**
- **Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
- **Main pain points:** {{pain-points}}
- **Product or service:** {{product-or-service}}
- **Social proof:** {{social-proof}} (statistic or testimonial)

## Output
Deliver the email in this structure:

**Subject:** [Attention-grabbing subject line]

Hi [First Name],

[Personalized opening that shows you understand their industry or challenge]

[Name their specific pain point and briefly explain how your product/service solves it]

[First key benefit for this audience]

[Second key benefit for this audience]

[Insert the statistic or testimonial to build credibility]

[Clear call to action with specific next step]

[Professional closing]

[Your Name]  
[Your Title]  
[Your Company]
```
