# Study Schedule Builder Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Study Schedule Builder Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates optimized, personalized study plans for students and lifelong learners preparing for exams or mastering new subjects. This study schedule prompt for ChatGPT takes your subject, available daily study hours, learning style, current knowledge level, and deadline, then produces a structured weekly or daily timetable that breaks complex material into manageable topics, assigns time blocks, and weaves in active recall, spaced repetition, and interleaving techniques. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering schedules with clear headings for time periods, bullet-point activities, topic progression from foundational to advanced, designated review sessions, and strategic breaks to prevent burnout. Real use cases include college exam prep, professional certification study, language learning roadmaps, and skills acquisition timelines. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to organize self-study around a deadline, balance multiple topics, or apply evidence-based learning science without hiring a tutor. ● Breaks subjects into logical topic sequences, moving from foundational concepts to advanced material. ● Allocates time blocks that fit your daily availability and adapts pacing to your current knowledge level. ● Incorporates spaced repetition intervals and active recall sessions to maximize long-term retention. ● Schedules regular breaks and active recovery periods to sustain focus and prevent cognitive fatigue. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in evidence-based study schedules.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, optimized study schedule that maximizes retention and efficiency through active recall, spaced repetition, and strategic breaks.

## Context
Subject: {{subject}}
Available study time per day: {{daily-study-hours}}
Learning style preference: {{learning-style}}
Current knowledge level: {{knowledge-level}}
Exam or deadline: {{deadline}}

Break the subject into manageable topics, allocate appropriate time blocks for each, incorporate proven study techniques (active recall, spaced repetition, interleaving), and include strategic breaks to maintain focus and prevent burnout. Make the schedule adaptable to the stated learning style and time constraints while prioritizing high-impact study methods.

## Output
Provide the schedule in a clear, structured format with:
- Headings for different time blocks (e.g., Week 1, Day 1-3)
- Bullet points for specific activities within each block
- Clear topic progression from foundational to advanced concepts
- Designated review sessions using spaced repetition intervals
- Break times and active recovery periods
```
