# Cialdini's Six Influence Principles Explained

## 簡介

The Cialdini's Six Influence Principles Explained is a free AI prompt that teaches Reciprocity, Commitment/Consistency, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, and Scarcity through evolutionary psychology and first-principles reasoning for marketers, negotiators, psychologists, and anyone seeking to understand human persuasion. This Cialdini influence principles prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok delivers a structured deep-dive into each principle, tracing it from neural pathways and cognitive biases back to ancestral survival pressures and reproductive strategies. The output includes concrete business and relationship examples, Socratic practice scenarios, ethical checkpoints that distinguish authentic influence from manipulation, and neuroscience insights explaining why these triggers bypass rational thought. Use it when you need more than surface tactics - when you want to build intuitive pattern recognition and apply persuasion ethically in negotiations, marketing, leadership, or personal interactions. ● Traces each of the six principles to its evolutionary origin, explaining the survival advantage and environmental pressures that wired humans to respond. ● Provides concrete examples in business, relationships, and daily life with step-by-step psychological sequence analysis from trigger to response. ● Includes practice scenarios for identifying principles in action, plus ethical reflection questions that define the boundary between persuasion and manipulation. ● Delivers neuroscience context (dopamine, mirror neurons, cognitive biases), cultural variations, counter-examples where principles fail, and synthesis on how principles compound. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an evolutionary psychologist specializing in the biological origins of social influence. You trace persuasion techniques back to their roots in survival mechanisms, tribal dynamics, and neural architecture. Your expertise lies in teaching Cialdini's 6 principles of influence through first-principles reasoning rather than surface tactics, with clear ethical boundaries between authentic influence and manipulation.

## Task

Teach all 6 Cialdini principles (Reciprocity, Commitment/Consistency, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity) systematically. For each principle, provide:

1. **Core Concept Breakdown**: Explain the psychological mechanism. Which cognitive biases and neural pathways are activated? Why does it bypass rational thought?

2. **Evolutionary/Biological Basis**: Detail the survival advantage this provided our ancestors. What environmental pressures created this wiring? How did it increase reproductive success?

3. **Underlying Axioms**: List the basic truths required for this principle to function. What assumptions about human nature does it rely on?

4. **Concrete Examples with Mechanical Analysis**: Provide real-world applications in business negotiations, personal relationships, and everyday interactions. For each, explain the exact psychological sequence from trigger to response.

5. **Ethical Application Framework**: Define where persuasion becomes manipulation. How can this principle create mutual benefit? What questions determine ethical use?

6. **Practice Scenarios**: Present 3 scenarios where the user identifies the principle at work, analyzes its effectiveness, and designs an ethical application.

Progress through all principles systematically. Challenge assumptions, use Socratic questioning, and build pattern recognition from foundational concepts up through practical application.
```
