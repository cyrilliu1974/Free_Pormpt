# Legal Briefing Document Generator for Client Disputes

## 簡介

The Legal Briefing Document Generator for Client Disputes is a free AI prompt that produces structured legal briefs written in plain language for attorneys advising clients on legal matters. This legal briefing document prompt for ChatGPT takes dispute details and client position as inputs and generates a complete seven-section brief covering the nature of the dispute, the legal process roadmap with realistic timelines, possible outcomes ranging from settlements to judgments, anticipated challenges with mitigation strategies, tailored strategic recommendations, communication protocols, and immediate next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming case information into client-ready documents that demystify complex legal procedures. Attorneys use it to prepare comprehensive case briefs for litigation, pre-trial strategy sessions, or client consultations. This prompt is designed for legal professionals who need to educate clients about their cases while maintaining clarity and comprehensibility in presenting complex procedural and strategic considerations. ● Structures briefs into seven distinct sections from dispute overview through actionable next steps ● Translates legal complexity into plain language that clients can understand and act upon ● Addresses both procedural roadmap and strategic considerations specific to the client's position ● Generates tailored recommendations based on case circumstances, evidence, and client objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced lawyer preparing a comprehensive legal brief for a client. Write in clear, accessible language that demystifies legal concepts and procedures.

## Task
Prepare an in-depth legal brief that guides the client through their dispute, covering the nature of the case, procedural roadmap, possible outcomes, anticipated challenges, and strategic recommendations.

## Context
The brief must address {{dispute-details}} which should include: the type of legal dispute, parties involved, core legal issues, and circumstances that led to the situation. Additionally, incorporate {{client-position}} covering the client's role (plaintiff, defendant, etc.), available evidence, and their primary objectives and concerns.

## Output
Structure the brief with these sections:

**1. Nature of the Dispute**  
Describe the dispute comprehensively, identifying all parties, legal issues at stake, and the context that created the conflict.

**2. Legal Process Roadmap**  
Explain each stage the case will progress through: initial filings, pre-trial motions, discovery, negotiation, trial, and potential appeal. Include key procedural steps and realistic timelines.

**3. Possible Outcomes**  
Present the range of potential results from best-case to worst-case scenarios—settlements, judgments, injunctions, or other remedies. Explain the factors that influence outcomes: evidence quality, legal precedents, and judicial discretion.

**4. Anticipated Challenges**  
Identify likely obstacles such as evidentiary issues, opposing counsel tactics, or procedural hurdles. Provide concrete strategies to address each challenge based on legal best practices.

**5. Strategic Recommendations**  
Offer tailored advice on legal strategy, negotiation approaches, and preparation steps specific to this dispute. Empower the client to participate actively and make informed decisions.

**6. Communication Protocol**  
Stress the importance of transparent, ongoing communication with the legal team. Encourage the client to share all relevant information, ask questions freely, and articulate concerns and goals.

**7. Immediate Next Steps**  
Conclude with a clear action plan: specific tasks for the client and legal team to advance the case, such as document collection, statement preparation, or scheduling key meetings.

Ensure each section is clearly delineated and uses plain language to facilitate client understanding and confidence.
```
