# Product Category Tree Generator for E-Commerce SEO

## 簡介

The Product Category Tree Generator for E-Commerce SEO is a free AI prompt that builds hierarchical product taxonomies balancing customer navigation patterns with search engine optimization for online retailers. This product category tree prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - product range, catalog size, target demographics, and competitive landscape - to design multi-level category structures that mirror how customers naturally search and shop. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it delivers both visual tree diagrams and indented list formats, complete with filter and facet recommendations (price, color, size, brand, material) tailored to each category branch. Real-world applications include launching new e-commerce platforms, restructuring existing catalogs for better conversion rates, and aligning product organization with SEO-friendly naming conventions that improve organic search visibility. Reach for this prompt when you need to scale your product catalog logically, reduce bounce rates through intuitive navigation, or align category architecture with customer mental models and shopping behaviors. ● Builds parent-child-grandchild category relationships that reflect natural customer search patterns and shopping journeys. ● Suggests filter attributes (price ranges, colors, sizes, brands, materials) specific to each major category branch to enhance findability. ● Outputs both visual tree structures with formatting symbols and clean indented lists for immediate implementation. ● Uses SEO-friendly, customer-intuitive naming conventions with consistent terminology across all hierarchy levels. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an e-commerce taxonomy architect specializing in product categorization systems that balance intuitive navigation with search discoverability.

## Task
Design a comprehensive hierarchical product category structure with parent-child-grandchild relationships that reflect how customers naturally search and shop.

## Context
Effective product categorization directly impacts conversion rates, SEO performance, and customer satisfaction. The taxonomy should:

- Mirror customer mental models and shopping behaviors
- Use SEO-friendly, customer-intuitive naming conventions
- Maintain consistent terminology across all levels
- Include relevant filtering options (price, color, size, brand, material, etc.) for each major category
- Scale logically as the catalog grows

Analyze the business context below to identify natural product groupings, determine the optimal depth of hierarchy, and suggest filter attributes that enhance navigation at each level.

{{business-context}}
*Include: business type, product range, catalog size (~SKUs), target customer demographics, main competitors (2-3 URLs for reference), and priorities (SEO, UX, conversion, etc.)*

## Output
Deliver the taxonomy in two formats:

1. **Visual tree structure** using text formatting (├── └── │ symbols)
2. **Indented list** with clear parent > child > grandchild relationships

For each major category branch, specify recommended filter/facet options that improve findability.
```
