# Terms of Service Agreement Generator

## 簡介

The Terms of Service Agreement Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts legally structured terms of service documents for digital platforms and applications. This terms of service prompt for ChatGPT produces a 12-section agreement covering user rights, responsibilities, acceptable use policies, intellectual property, privacy, termination procedures, disclaimers, liability limitations, and governing law. It works by taking your platform details and generating a structured document written in plain language that protects both users and platform owners. Legal consultants, startup founders, app developers, and SaaS businesses use it to create foundational legal documents that balance legal soundness with readability. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a starting framework for terms of service that covers essential legal bases while remaining accessible to average users. ● Produces 12 structured sections including user rights, responsibilities, acceptable use, intellectual property, privacy, disclaimers, and liability limitations ● Written in plain language with brief explanations of technical terms for user comprehension ● Includes termination procedures, governing law clauses, and amendment processes ● Formats output with clear headings, bullet points, and numbered lists for readability ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert legal consultant specializing in drafting terms of service agreements for digital platforms and applications.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive terms of service document that clearly outlines user rights, responsibilities, acceptable use policies, disclaimers, and limitations of liability. The document must be legally sound, written in plain language accessible to average users, and protect both users and platform owners.

## Context

Platform or application: {{platform-details}}

## Output

Structure the terms of service using the following sections with clear headings, bullet points, and numbered lists:

**1. Introduction**
- Brief overview of the platform/application
- Purpose of these terms of service

**2. User Rights**
- Key rights granted to users

**3. User Responsibilities**
- Key obligations and duties of users

**4. Acceptable Use Policies**
- Permitted and prohibited uses of the platform

**5. Intellectual Property Rights**
- Ownership statements for platform content and user-generated content

**6. Privacy Policy**
- Data collection and usage practices
- Data sharing and disclosure policies
- Data security measures

**7. Termination and Suspension**
- Conditions triggering account termination or suspension
- Procedures for account termination

**8. Disclaimers**
- Key disclaimers regarding service availability, warranties, and platform liability

**9. Limitation of Liability**
- Scope and extent of platform liability limitations

**10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**
- Applicable governing law
- Designated jurisdiction for disputes

**11. Changes to Terms of Service**
- Process for updating terms
- User notification procedures

**12. Contact Information**
- Platform contact details for terms-related inquiries

Write in plain language, avoiding complex legal jargon where possible. When technical terms are necessary, provide brief explanations. Ensure disclaimers and liability limitations are prominently featured.
```
