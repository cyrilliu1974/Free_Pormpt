# Final Settlement Checklist Generator for HR Compliance

## 簡介

The Final Settlement Checklist Generator for HR Compliance is a free AI prompt that creates systematic employee termination checklists covering financial obligations, benefits closeout, and documentation requirements for HR departments and compliance teams. This final settlement checklist prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured checklists organized across five categories: payroll settlement calculations, benefits termination procedures, expense reimbursements, legal compliance documentation, and verification workflows. Each checklist item includes the responsible party, timeline aligned with statutory deadlines, calculation formulas, and references to applicable ILO Employment Termination Standards or local labor regulations. The prompt adapts to different separation scenarios including voluntary resignation, involuntary termination, and retirement, and accepts company-specific context to tailor outputs to your organization's structure and jurisdictions. Reach for this prompt when designing or auditing termination processes, onboarding HR staff across multiple offices, or standardizing settlement procedures to reduce exposure from documentation gaps and calculation errors. ● Outputs executive summaries explaining why each checklist category prevents legal exposure and ensures fair treatment. ● Includes calculation tables with formulas for final pay, accrued leave, severance, and pro-rated allowances with verification steps. ● Provides warning flags for common pitfalls such as items requiring legal review, missed documentation, or jurisdictional exceptions. ● Delivers implementation guidance for HR teams on executing checklists, managing approvals, and handling dispute escalation paths. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a compliance architect specializing in employment termination processes. You design systematic checklists that satisfy legal requirements, prevent settlement oversights, and protect organizations from costly disputes during employee separations.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive final settlement checklist based on ILO Employment Termination Standards and applicable local labor law. The checklist must cover all financial obligations, documentation requirements, and verification procedures for employee terminations.

## Context
Organizations face significant legal and financial risk from inconsistent termination processes. Settlement disputes typically arise from procedural failures—missing documentation, calculation errors, and incomplete benefit termination create exposure. Your checklist must accommodate different termination scenarios (voluntary resignation, involuntary termination, retirement) while ensuring transparency and compliance across:

{{company-context}}

## Output
Structure the checklist in these categories:

### Executive Summary
Explain why standardized settlement checklists prevent legal exposure and ensure fairness.

### 1. Payroll Settlement
- Final pay calculation (regular wages, overtime, bonuses)
- Accrued leave payouts
- Severance entitlements
- Pro-rated allowances

### 2. Benefits Termination
- Health insurance continuation procedures
- Retirement plan distributions
- Stock options and equity vesting
- Other benefit closeouts

### 3. Expense Reimbursements
- Outstanding expense claims
- Corporate asset returns
- Final settlements

### 4. Legal Compliance Documentation
- Required employee acknowledgments
- Regulatory filings
- Record retention requirements
- Confidentiality and non-disclosure provisions

### 5. Verification Procedures
- Internal approval requirements
- Payment accuracy checks
- Documentation completeness review
- Dispute escalation paths

### Implementation Guide
Provide HR team guidance on executing the checklist.

**For each checklist item include:**
- ☐ Specific actionable task
- Responsible party
- Timeline (aligned with statutory requirements)
- Calculation method or documentation requirement
- Relevant ILO standard or local law reference
- ⚠️ Warning flags for common pitfalls or items requiring legal review

Use tables for financial calculations showing formulas and verification steps. Include sample documentation templates where applicable. Format for both printing and digital use. Ensure all content is accessible to HR professionals and departing employees. Every item must be measurable and actionable—avoid generic statements.
```
