# Partnership Communication Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Partnership Communication Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured communication strategies for B2B partnership managers and relationship teams. This partnership communication plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your partnership context and communication goals, then produces a markdown table mapping channels (email, quarterly business reviews, webinars, Slack, executive touchpoints) to optimal contact frequencies and core messaging themes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, helping you organize partner touchpoints across onboarding, growth, and renewal stages with clear cadences - weekly check-ins, monthly syncs, quarterly reviews, or ad-hoc updates - and value propositions tailored to each medium. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize partner engagement, align internal teams on communication rhythm, or document your partner success playbook. ● Specifies communication channels from email and Slack to executive touchpoints and quarterly business reviews ● Defines appropriate contact cadences for each channel to avoid over-communication or neglect ● Articulates key messages and value propositions aligned with mutual partnership objectives ● Outputs a clean markdown table you can drop into Notion, Confluence, or partner success documentation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert communication strategist specializing in B2B partnership management.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive Partnership Communication Plan that manages and nurtures partner relationships effectively. The plan should specify communication channels, appropriate contact frequencies, and key messages aligned with partnership objectives.

## Context
Partnership context:
{{partnership-context}}

Communication goals:
{{communication-goals}}

## Output
Deliver the plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Communication Channels | Frequency | Key Messages |
|------------------------|-----------|---------------|

For each row:
- **Communication Channels**: Specify the medium (email, quarterly business reviews, webinars, Slack channels, executive touchpoints, etc.)
- **Frequency**: Define cadence (weekly, monthly, quarterly, ad-hoc, etc.)
- **Key Messages**: Articulate the core themes and value propositions to communicate through that channel

Ensure the plan enhances partner engagement, strengthens relationships, and drives mutual success across all partnership stages.
```
