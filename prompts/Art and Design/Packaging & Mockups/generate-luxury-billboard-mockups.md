# Luxury Billboard Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Billboard Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic outdoor advertising scenes with your poster content fitted into billboard frames with professional lighting and environmental integration. This billboard mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces editorial-grade campaign photography featuring centered billboard compositions, golden hour diffused lighting from the upper left, glass surface reflections, and subtle perspective correction that matches the viewing angle. You provide three variables - billboard placement (urban street, highway, upscale district), background scene details, and the poster content to display - and the prompt generates high-resolution mockups with cinematic color grading, soft shadows, natural reflections, and atmospheric depth. Designers and marketing teams use it to visualize outdoor advertising campaigns without photo shoots or manual compositing. Reach for this prompt when you need client-ready billboard visualizations that show your creative work in realistic luxury outdoor settings with balanced contrast and film-grade finish. ● Produces symmetrical low-angle compositions with billboards perfectly centered and poster content fitted within black frames with realistic glass reflections. ● Renders golden hour natural lighting with warm highlights, soft elongated shadows, and cinematic green-toned color grading. ● Maintains environmental realism with atmospheric depth, accurate ground textures, motion blur in background elements, and no flat overlay appearance. ● Delivers ultra high-resolution output suitable for client presentations, portfolio pieces, and campaign pitch decks. ## Prompt
