# Interview Preparation Guide Builder for Career Coaches

## 簡介

The Interview Preparation Guide Builder for Career Coaches is a free AI prompt that generates tailored interview prep guides for any career field, addressing research strategies, common questions, competencies, and presentation techniques. This interview preparation prompt for ChatGPT takes three inputs - the target career field and role, the candidate's skill profile and experience level, and their primary interview challenge - and produces a structured guide with clear sections on industry research, answer frameworks for common questions, technical and behavioral competencies, mock interview methods, delivery techniques, and day-of logistics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable for career coaches working with clients across industries from software engineering to healthcare to finance. The output includes actionable advice that scales across entry-level to senior roles, helping candidates articulate their value and navigate the interview process with confidence. Career coaches, HR professionals, and mentors will find this prompt valuable when preparing clients for high-stakes interviews or when building reusable prep materials for specific fields. ● Generates structured guides with sections on company research, question frameworks, competencies, practice methods, and follow-up ● Tailors advice to the specific career field, candidate experience level, and stated interview challenge ● Covers both technical and behavioral interview components with actionable answer strategies ● Scales across experience levels, from early-career job seekers to senior executives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career coach specializing in interview preparation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive interview preparation guide tailored to a specific career field. Cover research strategies, practice techniques, and presentation skills. Address common interview questions, current industry trends, and essential competencies. Provide practical, actionable advice that scales across experience levels.

## Context
Career field and target role: {{career-field-and-role}}
Key skills and experience level: {{candidate-profile}}
Primary interview challenge: {{interview-challenge}}

## Output
Structure the guide with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points:
- Industry research and company preparation
- Common interview questions and strong answer frameworks
- Technical and behavioral competencies to demonstrate
- Practice methods (mock interviews, answer rehearsal)
- Presentation and delivery techniques
- Day-of logistics and follow-up best practices

Tailor all advice to the specified career field and address the stated challenge directly.
```
