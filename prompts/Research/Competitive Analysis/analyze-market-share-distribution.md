# Market Share Distribution Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Share Distribution Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed competitive landscape reports for market analysts, business strategists, and research teams. This market share analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks you through identifying major companies in a specified industry and region, calculating their market share percentages, and assessing each player's competitive position, strengths, and weaknesses. It outputs a structured markdown table showing company names, market share percentages, and key insights, followed by a growth opportunities summary that highlights 3-5 actionable opportunities based on current trends, underserved segments, and market gaps. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and accepts three variables: industry, region, and timeframe, making it adaptable to any market scope from SaaS in North America to consumer electronics in Southeast Asia. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly assess a competitive landscape, prepare investor briefings, or identify white-space opportunities in a new market. ● Produces a markdown table with company names, market share percentages, and competitive insights ● Evaluates each player's strengths, weaknesses, and strategic position in the market ● Identifies 3-5 specific growth opportunities based on trends, gaps, and emerging segments ● Customizable by industry, geographic region, and timeframe to fit any market scope ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive landscape assessment and market share distribution.

## Task
Produce a comprehensive market share analysis report that identifies key players, quantifies their market positions, and highlights growth opportunities.

## Context
Analyze the market defined by:
- **Industry and scope**: {{industry}}
- **Geographic region**: {{region}}
- **Analysis timeframe**: {{timeframe}}

Identify the major companies operating in this market and calculate their market share percentages. Examine each company's competitive position, strengths, and weaknesses. Uncover potential growth opportunities based on current trends, underserved segments, and market gaps.

## Output
Present your findings as:

1. **Market Share Analysis Table** (markdown format) with three columns:
   - Company Name
   - Market Share Percentage
   - Key Insights (position, strengths, weaknesses)

2. **Growth Opportunities Summary**: A concise paragraph identifying 3-5 specific opportunities in the market based on your analysis, including trends, gaps, and emerging segments.
```
