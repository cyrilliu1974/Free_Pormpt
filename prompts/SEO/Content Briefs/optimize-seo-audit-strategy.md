# SEO Audit Plan Builder for Website Performance

## 簡介

The SEO Audit Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized, actionable SEO audit strategies for websites across any industry. This SEO audit prompt for ChatGPT accepts your website URL, industry context, and target keywords, then produces a complete audit framework covering the metrics that matter most, tool recommendations with specific purposes, optimal audit cadence, and prioritized implementation steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering output structured for immediate use by marketing teams, agencies, and site owners who need to systematically monitor and improve search performance. The prompt accounts for industry-specific competitive dynamics and search behavior patterns, ensuring recommendations align with the realities of your vertical. ● Identifies five critical SEO metrics with explanations of why each matters for your site ● Recommends four specific tools matched to audit needs, with clarity on what each reveals ● Establishes audit frequency with rationale tied to industry dynamics and keyword competition ● Delivers prioritized action items ranked by implementation impact, not generic checklists ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in website performance auditing and search engine optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan for regularly auditing SEO performance for {{website-url}}. The plan must be actionable, specific to {{industry}}, and aligned with ranking for {{target-keywords}}.

## Context
The audit plan should identify what to measure, which tools to use, how often to audit, and concrete steps to execute. Tailor recommendations to the competitive landscape and search patterns typical in {{industry}}.

## Output
Deliver the plan in this structure:

**SEO Audit Plan**

**Metrics to Track:**
- Metric 1 [Description and why it matters]
- Metric 2 [Description and why it matters]
- Metric 3 [Description and why it matters]
- Metric 4 [Description and why it matters]
- Metric 5 [Description and why it matters]

**Tools to Use:**
1. Tool 1 – Purpose and what it reveals
2. Tool 2 – Purpose and what it reveals
3. Tool 3 – Purpose and what it reveals
4. Tool 4 – Purpose and what it reveals

**Audit Frequency:**
[Specify cadence with rationale: monthly, quarterly, or event-triggered]

**Action Items:**
• Step 1
• Step 2
• Step 3
• Step 4
• Step 5

Ensure every recommendation is implementable and prioritized by impact.
```
