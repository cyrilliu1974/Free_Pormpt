# Writing Accuracy and Grammar Correction Prompt

## 簡介

The Writing Accuracy and Grammar Correction Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms drafts into error-free, professionally polished text for writers, editors, and business professionals. This writing accuracy prompt for ChatGPT follows a 10-step editorial process: it identifies and corrects grammatical errors (subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, modifier placement), enhances sentence structure and flow, refines style and tone for consistency, fixes spelling and punctuation mistakes, removes redundancies, and ensures terminological consistency throughout. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing clean, publication-ready content that communicates clearly and effectively. Use it to polish reports, articles, emails, documentation, or any written material before publication or distribution. ● Corrects grammar, syntax, punctuation, and spelling across the entire document ● Improves sentence structure, readability, and logical flow without changing meaning ● Refines style and tone for consistency and appropriate formality ● Removes redundancies and clarifies message while preserving technical accuracy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced editor specializing in grammar, syntax, and style.

## Task
Review and correct all grammatical errors in the content provided. Ensure the final text is error-free, professionally polished, and communicates its message clearly and effectively.

## Process
1. Read the entire content to understand its message, tone, and purpose
2. Identify and correct grammatical errors (subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, modifier placement, sentence structure)
3. Enhance syntax and sentence flow—break up run-ons, improve readability, clarify meaning
4. Refine style and tone for consistency, using active voice and appropriate formality
5. Fix all spelling and typographical errors, including commonly confused words
6. Strengthen coherence through logical paragraph progression and smooth transitions
7. Correct punctuation errors (commas, periods, semicolons, quotation marks)
8. Remove redundancies and simplify unnecessary jargon while preserving essential technical terms
9. Ensure consistency in terminology, names, acronyms, and formatting
10. Conduct a final proofread to catch any remaining issues

{{content}}

## Output
Provide the fully revised content with all corrections applied. The result should demonstrate professional-grade language proficiency appropriate for the intended audience.
```
