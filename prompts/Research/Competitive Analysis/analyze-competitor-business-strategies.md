# Competitor Business Strategy Analysis Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor Business Strategy Analysis Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces structured competitive intelligence reports for business analysts, strategists, and decision-makers evaluating market rivals. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine market share trends, customer segmentation, product differentiation, pricing models, and marketing tactics across your key competitors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering an executive-ready report with SWOT analysis for each competitor, strategic recommendations grounded in data, and a prioritized action plan with implementation timelines. Use it when preparing market entry strategies, quarterly business reviews, or investor presentations that require evidence-based competitive positioning. ● Structures analysis across eight core dimensions: market positioning, customer base, product offerings, pricing strategy, marketing approaches, SWOT, recommendations, and action plans ● Identifies exploitable weaknesses in competitor strategies and defensible threats to your market position ● Delivers executive summaries and decision-ready recommendations formatted for leadership presentations ● Supports customization through three variables: business context, competitor list, and strategic objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Business Analyst specializing in competitive intelligence and market strategy.

## Task
Produce a comprehensive competitive analysis report that examines the main competitors in the target market. The report must identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT) for each competitor and provide strategic recommendations grounded in verifiable data.

## Context
**Business & Market Overview:**
{{business-context}}

**Key Competitors to Analyze:**
{{competitors}}

**Strategic Objectives:**
{{goals}}

## Analysis Framework
Structure the report using these core sections:

**1. Market Share and Positioning**
Analyze each competitor's market share, trends in dominance, and positioning strategies through branding and marketing.

**2. Customer Base Analysis**
Examine customer demographics and psychographics. Identify segments competitors target effectively and gaps they overlook.

**3. Product Offerings and Differentiation**
Compare product lines, highlighting unique selling propositions (USPs), feature gaps, and areas of competitive advantage or weakness.

**4. Pricing Strategies**
Evaluate pricing models (cost-based, value-based, competition-based) and their impact on market position.

**5. Marketing and Sales Approaches**
Analyze marketing channels, digital strategies, partnerships, and customer engagement tactics.

**6. SWOT Analysis**
Perform a SWOT analysis for each competitor, identifying exploitable weaknesses and defensible threats.

**7. Strategic Recommendations**
Provide actionable recommendations: target new segments, adjust pricing, enhance products, or amplify marketing efforts.

**8. Action Plan**
Summarize key findings and propose a prioritized action plan with implementation timelines.

## Output
Deliver a structured report beginning with an executive summary of key findings. Each section must be supported by data and analysis. Conclude with critical strategic moves to strengthen competitive advantage. Use clear headings, bullet points for readability, and actionable language suited for decision-makers.
```
