# Ethical Risk Assessment Prompt for Organizations

## 簡介

The Ethical Risk Assessment Prompt for Organizations is a free AI prompt that evaluates ethical vulnerabilities and produces actionable preventive measures for compliance officers, risk managers, and business ethics consultants. This ethical risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies the Institute of Business Ethics (IBE) three-pillar framework to examine organizational culture through leadership behavior and policy effectiveness, analyze decision-making structures for overlooked ethical considerations, and map misconduct exposure by reviewing historical incidents and near-misses. The output is a complete assessment report with an executive summary, current state analysis, risk identification matrix with likelihood and impact ratings, high-priority vulnerabilities, and a detailed preventive action plan specifying interventions, timelines, responsible parties, and success metrics. Organizations use it to strengthen integrity systems before audits, after incidents, or as part of annual governance reviews. ● Evaluates ethical culture by examining leadership patterns, employee feedback mechanisms, and policy effectiveness across the organization. ● Produces a risk matrix categorizing threats by likelihood and impact, highlighting critical exposure areas in business functions and stakeholder relationships. ● Delivers specific preventive interventions including policy updates, training programs, monitoring systems, and governance improvements with assigned ownership. ● Structures findings in a report format ready for board presentations, compliance documentation, or internal action planning. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business ethics consultant and risk management specialist with deep knowledge of the Institute of Business Ethics (IBE) framework and practical corporate governance experience.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive ethical risk assessment that identifies vulnerabilities, evaluates organizational culture, and provides actionable preventive measures. Systematically analyze the organization's ethical landscape using the IBE's three-pillar approach:

1. **Organizational culture evaluation** – Examine leadership behavior patterns, employee feedback mechanisms, and existing policy effectiveness
2. **Decision-making process assessment** – Identify where ethical considerations may be overlooked or compromised in governance structures
3. **Misconduct exposure analysis** – Review historical incidents and near-misses to understand recurring vulnerability patterns across business functions and stakeholder relationships

Create a risk matrix prioritizing threats by likelihood and impact. Develop specific preventive actions including policy updates, training programs, monitoring systems, and governance improvements. Focus on practical, implementable solutions.

## Context
**Organization profile:** {{organization-profile}}

**Assessment scope:** {{assessment-scope}}

## Output
Deliver a structured assessment report with:

- **Executive Summary** – Key findings and priority recommendations
- **Current State Analysis** – Evaluation of culture, policies, and leadership practices
- **Risk Identification Matrix** – Categorized threats with likelihood and impact ratings
- **High-Priority Vulnerabilities** – Critical exposure areas requiring immediate attention
- **Detailed Preventive Action Plan** – Specific interventions with timelines, responsible parties, and success metrics
```
