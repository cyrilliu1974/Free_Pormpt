# Social Media Strategy Prompt for Business Goals

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Prompt for Business Goals is a free AI prompt that builds data-driven social media marketing strategies tailored to specific business objectives and target audiences. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through creating a seven-section strategy document that includes audience analysis with demographics and behavior patterns, justified platform recommendations, a content calendar framework with post types and frequency, measurable KPIs tied to your goals, engagement tactics like contests and influencer collaborations, platform-specific optimization guidelines, and a monitoring system for performance tracking. You provide your business goal, target audience, unique value proposition, and current social media presence, and the prompt generates an actionable roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured strategy documents that marketing teams can implement immediately. This prompt is for social media managers, marketing strategists, brand teams, and business owners who need a systematic approach to planning campaigns that drive engagement and achieve measurable outcomes. ● Analyzes target audiences by demographics, behavior, pain points, and platform preferences to inform content decisions ● Recommends specific social media platforms with justification based on where your audience is most active ● Creates a content calendar framework with post types, themes, formats, and frequency tailored to each platform ● Defines measurable KPIs like engagement rate, conversion rate, and reach that connect directly to your business goal ● Provides engagement tactics, community management practices, optimization guidelines, and monitoring processes for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in data-driven campaign development and execution.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media marketing strategy that reaches the target audience, drives engagement, and achieves the specified business goal. The strategy must be actionable, adaptable, and optimized for measurable results.

## Context
Goal: {{goal}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Brand unique value proposition: {{usp}}
Current social media presence: {{current-presence}}

## Output
Deliver a structured strategy document with these sections:

**1. Audience Analysis**
- Demographics, interests, online behavior, and platform preferences
- Pain points, motivations, and decision-making factors
- Audience segmentation if applicable

**2. Platform Selection**
- Recommended social media platforms with justification based on audience presence and content fit
- Platform-specific opportunities aligned with the goal

**3. Content Strategy**
- Content calendar framework: post types (educational, promotional, interactive, user-generated), themes, and posting frequency
- Content pillars aligned with audience interests, brand messaging, and the goal
- Mix of formats (posts, videos, stories, live sessions) tailored to each platform

**4. KPIs and Measurement**
- Specific metrics (engagement rate, conversion rate, follower growth, reach, click-through rate)
- How each KPI connects to the overarching goal
- Benchmarks for success

**5. Engagement and Community Building**
- Tactics: contests, Q&A sessions, polls, user-generated content campaigns, influencer collaborations
- Community management practices to foster conversations and build loyalty
- Response protocols and tone guidelines

**6. Content Optimization**
- Platform-specific best practices (optimal posting times, hashtag strategies, algorithm considerations)
- A/B testing recommendations for content formats and messaging
- Multimedia usage guidelines (images, video length, captions)

**7. Monitoring and Adaptation**
- Analytics tools and dashboards for tracking performance
- Review cadence (weekly/monthly)
- Process for adapting strategy based on performance data and emerging trends

Format the strategy as a clear, actionable roadmap with specific recommendations tied to the goal.
```
