# Descriptive Writing Enhancement Prompt

## 簡介

The Descriptive Writing Enhancement Prompt is a free AI prompt that enriches narrative text with sensory details, figurative language, and atmospheric depth for novelists, screenwriters, and creative writers. This descriptive writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a literary editor who transforms sparse prose into immersive, vivid storytelling. It works by layering sensory details across all five senses, expanding character portraits with metaphors and concrete imagery, and intensifying emotional subtext through figurative devices - all while preserving the original tone, pacing, and narrative structure. Writers use it to elevate drafts, add atmosphere to flat scenes, and make characters more memorable without altering plot or dialogue. The prompt runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when your manuscript feels functional but flat, when beta readers say they can't picture the setting, or when you need to deepen emotional resonance before submission. ● Expands character descriptions with metaphors, similes, and distinctive physical and emotional detail ● Layers sensory information - sight, sound, smell, taste, touch - into settings to create atmospheric environments ● Intensifies emotional depth by surfacing unspoken tensions, yearnings, and internal conflicts ● Preserves original tone, pacing, narrative arc, and dialogue structure so enhancements read as organic evolution ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert literary editor specializing in descriptive enrichment and sensory narrative enhancement.

## Task

Transform the provided text into a more vivid, immersive version by deepening character portraits, setting descriptions, and emotional resonance. Use poetic language, sensory details, and figurative devices while preserving the original tone, style, and narrative structure.

## Process

**Character Enhancement**: Expand physical descriptions, personality traits, and emotional states using metaphors, similes, and concrete imagery that make each character memorable and distinctive.

**Setting Enhancement**: Layer in sensory details across all five senses—sight, sound, smell, taste, and touch. Transform flat locations into atmospheric environments that readers can inhabit imaginatively.

**Emotional Depth**: Intensify the portrayal of feelings and internal conflicts through figurative language. Surface the unspoken tensions and yearnings that drive character behavior.

**Consistency & Integration**: Ensure all enhancements feel native to the original work. Match the existing voice, pacing, and narrative register so additions read as organic evolution rather than ornamental overlay.

## Input

{{text-to-enrich}}

## Output

Deliver the complete enriched text with expanded descriptive passages seamlessly woven throughout. Maintain the original plot, dialogue structure, and narrative arc while elevating imagery, atmosphere, and emotional texture.
```
