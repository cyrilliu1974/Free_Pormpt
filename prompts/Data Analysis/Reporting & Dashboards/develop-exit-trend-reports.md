# Exit Trend Report Generator for Workforce Analytics

## 簡介

The Exit Trend Report Generator for Workforce Analytics is a free AI prompt that transforms exit interview data into visual, executive-ready reports revealing the root causes of employee turnover for HR teams and workforce analysts. This exit trend report prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok structures a multi-section analysis that includes an executive dashboard with traffic-light metrics, department heatmaps, root cause clustering, predictive risk scoring, financial impact assessments, and prioritized retention recommendations. You provide organizational context and exit data sources; the AI outputs a comprehensive report with trend arrows, correlation matrices, text analytics themes, cost-of-turnover calculations, and actionable intervention strategies tied directly to data patterns. Real use cases include monthly turnover reviews, C-suite briefings on talent retention, and identifying early warning signals before attrition accelerates. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond raw exit interview tallies and surface the strategic patterns that standard HR dashboards miss - especially when presenting to executives who require visual clarity and quantified business impact. ● Produces a one-page executive dashboard with top three turnover drivers, trend arrows for three-, six-, and twelve-month periods, and traffic-light indicators. ● Includes root cause analysis with exit reason clustering, correlation matrices for compensation and management factors, and text analytics of open-ended responses. ● Delivers predictive risk scoring by department, role, and tenure alongside financial impact assessments covering cost of turnover, productivity loss, and recruitment investment. ● Outputs a priority matrix plotting retention interventions by impact versus effort, with success metrics and a monitoring plan. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a workforce analytics specialist who transforms exit interview data into strategic intelligence, combining rigorous data analysis with visual storytelling to expose turnover patterns that standard HR metrics miss.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Exit Trend Report that reveals root causes of employee turnover and translates complex workforce data into executive-ready insights. Connect exit patterns to business impact and provide data-backed retention recommendations.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

{{exit-data-sources}}

## Output
Structure the Exit Trend Report with these components:

**EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD**
- One-page visual summary with traffic light indicators for key metrics
- Top 3 turnover drivers with quantified business impact
- Trend arrows showing 3-month, 6-month, and 12-month directional movement

**DETAILED ANALYSIS SECTIONS**

1. **Turnover Overview**
   - Department heatmap highlighting concentration areas
   - Tenure distribution analysis
   - Voluntary vs involuntary breakdown

2. **Root Cause Analysis**
   - Exit reason clustering with frequency charts
   - Correlation matrices examining compensation, management, workload, and career development factors
   - Text analytics revealing themes from open-ended responses

3. **Predictive Indicators**
   - Early warning signals identified through pattern analysis
   - Risk scoring segmented by department, role, and tenure

4. **Financial Impact Assessment**
   - Cost of turnover calculations by role type
   - Productivity loss estimates during vacancy and ramp-up periods
   - Recruitment and training investment analysis

5. **Actionable Recommendations**
   - Priority matrix plotting interventions by impact vs effort
   - Specific retention strategies tied directly to data findings
   - Success metrics and ongoing monitoring plan

**Analysis Principles:**
- Prioritize visual clarity—executives should grasp key insights within 30 seconds
- Focus on trends over point-in-time snapshots to reveal momentum and direction
- Include statistical significance indicators to prevent overreaction to normal variance
- Maintain employee confidentiality through anonymization protocols
- Distinguish correlation from causation in all interpretations
- Design visualizations for monthly updates without manual reconstruction
- Quantify cost implications to ensure C-suite engagement

Include methodology notes and data limitations in an appendix.
```
