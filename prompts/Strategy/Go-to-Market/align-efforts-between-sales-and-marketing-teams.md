# Sales and Marketing Alignment Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Sales and Marketing Alignment Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete lead nurturing framework aligning sales and marketing efforts across every funnel stage for go-to-market teams. This sales and marketing alignment prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context - team structures, target audience, industry, and current funnel stages - and outputs a detailed markdown table mapping specific, actionable responsibilities for each team at every stage. It defines handoff points, lead scoring criteria, escalation triggers, communication cadence, and the right mix of automation and personalized outreach. Revenue operations leaders use it to eliminate the gaps between demand generation and sales execution that cost deals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when silos between sales and marketing are causing leads to fall through the cracks, handoffs are unclear, or you need a single source of truth for who does what at each funnel stage. ● Defines stage-specific actions for both sales and marketing, eliminating ambiguity about ownership and timing ● Specifies lead scoring, qualification criteria, and handoff triggers to ensure only sales-ready leads are passed ● Recommends communication channels, content types, cadence, and success metrics tailored to your business context ● Balances automated nurture sequences with high-touch personalized outreach to maximize engagement without waste ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales and marketing strategist specializing in lead nurturing and revenue operations alignment.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive lead nurturing strategy that aligns sales and marketing efforts across the entire funnel. Define specific, actionable responsibilities for each team at every stage to maximize engagement, smooth handoffs, and conversion rates.

## Context
**Business context:** {{business-context}}
(Include: sales team structure and capabilities, marketing team structure and capabilities, target audience profile, industry, and your current sales funnel stages)

**Strategy requirements:**
- Ensure clear handoff points between marketing and sales
- Specify tactics appropriate to each funnel stage
- Address lead scoring, qualification criteria, and escalation triggers
- Include communication cadence and channel recommendations
- Balance automation with personalized outreach

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Lead Stage | Sales Team Actions | Marketing Team Actions |
|------------|-------------------|------------------------|

Cover every stage in the provided sales funnel. Each cell must contain clear, executable actions tailored to the business context. Include timing, tools, content types, and success metrics where relevant.
```
