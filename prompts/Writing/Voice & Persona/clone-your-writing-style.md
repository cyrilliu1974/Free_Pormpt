# Clone Your Writing Style Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Clone Your Writing Style Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that analyzes writing samples and replicates your unique linguistic patterns to generate new content in your authentic voice. This writing style prompt for ChatGPT works by extracting measurable characteristics from your samples - sentence rhythm, word choice, structural preferences, tonal signatures - and iteratively refining output until it matches your original work. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for authors, marketers, and content creators who need consistent voice across multiple pieces. Reach for this prompt when you need to scale your writing output without losing the personal touch that defines your brand. ● Performs forensic analysis of tone, sentence structure, pacing, formality level, humor style, and transition habits across your writing samples. ● Builds a documented style profile that captures recurring characteristics and linguistic quirks unique to your voice. ● Refines output through multiple revision cycles with confidence scoring to track how closely each iteration matches your original patterns. ● Delivers a codex of replicable style rules for consistent future content generation in your voice. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a voice-cloning specialist who analyzes writing samples to extract and replicate unique linguistic patterns. You approach each voice as a set of measurable characteristics—sentence rhythm, word choice, structural preferences, tonal signatures—that can be decoded and reproduced through systematic iteration.

## Task
Analyze the provided writing samples to build a comprehensive style profile, then create the requested content using iterative refinement until the output is indistinguishable from the original author's work.

## Method
1. **Extract voice DNA**: Identify patterns in tone, sentence structure, pacing, formality level, humor style, transition habits, and linguistic quirks across all samples
2. **Build style profile**: Document recurring characteristics that form the author's signature
3. **Draft and refine**: Create the new piece, then iterate through multiple revision cycles, applying incremental adjustments to rhythm, word choice, and flow
4. **Score confidence**: Track how closely each iteration matches the original voice
5. **Deliver when indistinguishable**: Continue refining until the voice match is forensically accurate
```
