# Welcome Book Outline Generator for Vacation Rentals

## 簡介

The Welcome Book Outline Generator for Vacation Rentals is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive guest guide outlines for Airbnb hosts, property managers, and vacation rental operators. This welcome book prompt for ChatGPT produces a ready-to-populate 12-section framework that balances practical property information with curated local insights. You provide your property details, and the prompt builds a structured outline covering welcome letters, house rules, safety protocols, appliance instructions, dining recommendations, local attractions, activity suggestions, emergency contacts, checkout procedures, and insider tips. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a hospitality-focused outline that transforms basic property information into an engaging guest resource. Hosts use it to standardize their welcome materials, ensure nothing critical is overlooked, and present their rental as both a well-run property and a gateway to the local experience. ● Produces a complete structural outline for a Welcome Book in under a minute, covering property guides, local recommendations, and safety information ● Includes all essential sections hosts need - from Wi-Fi instructions and house rules to hidden local gems and emergency contacts ● Designed by hospitality experts to balance practical property details with personal, welcoming touches that improve guest satisfaction ● Works for any property type - urban apartments, beach houses, mountain cabins, or countryside estates - by adapting to the property details you provide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Airbnb host and content creator specializing in guest experience design through comprehensive welcome materials.

## Task
Create a structured outline for a Welcome Book that serves as both a property guide and local travel resource. The outline should cover all essential information guests need for a seamless, enriched stay—from property details and house rules to curated local recommendations and emergency contacts.

## Context
{{property-details}}

The Welcome Book should blend practical information with personal hospitality touches, reflecting insider knowledge of both the property and the surrounding area. Structure the content to be easy to navigate and engaging to read.

## Output
Develop a 12-section Welcome Book outline following this structure:

1. **Welcome Letter**: Personalized greeting expressing excitement about their stay and your availability to assist

2. **Property Overview**: Detailed description including history, unique features, and special amenities

3. **House Rules**: Clear list of expectations and guidelines to ensure a pleasant stay

4. **Safety Information**: Emergency exits, fire extinguisher locations, and critical safety protocols

5. **Guide to Property Amenities**: Step-by-step instructions for Wi-Fi, appliances, HVAC, outdoor features (BBQ, hot tub, etc.)

6. **Local Attractions**: Curated list of parks, historical sites, museums, and family activities with addresses, hours, and visit rationale

7. **Dining Recommendations**: Personal favorite restaurants, cafes, and bars with signature dishes and optimal visit times

8. **Activity Suggestions**: Interest-based recommendations (hiking, biking, shopping, arts/theater) with insider tips

9. **Emergency Contacts**: Local emergency services, nearest hospital, and your direct contact information

10. **Checkout Instructions**: Key return, lock-up procedures, and cleaning/waste disposal guidelines

11. **Personal Recommendations and Insider Tips**: Hidden gems, favorite coffee shops, local events, and farmer's markets

12. **Guest Feedback and Contact Information**: Invitation for reviews and your details for future bookings or referrals

Present the outline with clear section headings, brief content descriptions, and a welcoming tone throughout.
```
