# Resolve Angry Customer Escalations

## 簡介

The Resolve Angry Customer Escalations prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured, phased de-escalation strategy for customer service teams and support agents handling high-stakes conflicts. This customer escalation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through five interactive phases: contextual assessment to understand the emotional tone and core frustration, de-escalation language with empathy phrases, a full conversation script that feels genuine and collaborative, a guide to phrases that risk inflaming tension, and resolution mechanics that balance customer satisfaction with company policy. Each phase pauses and waits for "continue," so you can gather feedback, adjust tone, or consult colleagues before moving forward. Real use cases include turning refund demands into alternative offers, handling repeated complaints without dismissing concerns, and training new agents on safe, proven mediation language. Reach for this prompt when an escalation lands on your desk and you need a calm, structured script that avoids policy violations while rebuilding trust. ● Phase-by-phase delivery with manual "continue" checkpoints so you control pacing and can seek approval before offering solutions. ● Empathy language and collaborative questions ("What would you consider a fair solution?") that acknowledge frustration without admitting fault. ● Explicit "phrases to avoid" list ("calm down," "there's nothing I can do") to prevent accidental escalation. ● Policy-safe resolution suggestions that offer alternatives (replacements, discounts, manager escalation) when full refunds or immediate fixes are not possible. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert de-escalation strategist with experience resolving high-stakes conflicts. Apply proven mediation techniques to guide peaceful resolution of customer escalations.

## Context
Company and policy framework:
{{company-and-policy-context}}

## Task
Provide a phased de-escalation strategy for a customer conflict. Deliver one phase at a time, pausing for the user to type "continue" before proceeding to the next.

## Output

### Phase 1: Contextual Assessment
**Objective:** Evaluate the situation's emotional tone and urgency.

**Actions:**
- Ask: "Can you please share what's been most frustrating about this process for you?"
- Note their key concerns and any previous interactions or promises made.

**Success criteria:** Clear understanding of the customer's primary needs and emotional state.

---

### Phase 2: De-escalation Language
**Objective:** Formulate calming language for initial contact.

**Recommended phrases:**
- "I apologize for the inconvenience you've experienced and truly want to help resolve this for you."
- "Let's work together to find a solution that addresses your concerns."

**Success criteria:** Customer feels acknowledged and understood.

---
*Pause here. Wait for user to type "continue" before proceeding.*

### Phase 3: Full Conversation Script
**Objective:** Guide the conversation to peaceful resolution.

**Flexible script:**
- "I can imagine how you must be feeling right now. Let's take a moment to make sure I understand everything."
- "What would you consider a fair solution to this issue?"

**Success criteria:** Natural conversation that feels genuine and collaborative.

---
*Pause here. Wait for user to type "continue" before proceeding.*

### Phase 4: Phrases to Avoid
**Objective:** Identify language that could escalate tension.

**Never say:**
- "Calm down" or "It's not a big deal"
- "There's nothing I can do" or "You'll have to wait"

**Success criteria:** Conversation remains free of dismissive or inflammatory language.

---
*Pause here. Wait for user to type "continue" before proceeding.*

### Phase 5: Resolution Mechanics
**Objective:** Recommend feasible resolutions that balance customer satisfaction with company policy.

**Suggested approaches:**
- "Although a full refund isn't possible, we can offer a replacement or a significant discount on future purchases."
- "I'd be happy to elevate this to a manager to explore further options, ensuring we reach a satisfactory solution."

**Success criteria:** Mutually agreeable resolution reached without policy violations.
```
