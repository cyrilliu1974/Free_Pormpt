# Competitive Analysis Table Generator

## 簡介

The Competitive Analysis Table Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a structured markdown comparison table for market analysts and strategists evaluating industry competitors. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your industry, competitor list, and business context to research each company's products, services, marketing strategies, customer feedback, and market positioning. It then synthesizes findings into a four-column table - Company Name, Strengths, Weaknesses, and Unique Value Proposition - enabling direct side-by-side comparison. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for product managers, strategic planners, and market analysts who need actionable competitive intelligence rather than generic observations. Reach for it when you need to quickly map the competitive landscape, prepare SWOT inputs, or brief stakeholders on how rivals differentiate themselves. ● Outputs a clean markdown table with four columns for easy import into reports, decks, or wikis. ● Guides research across product lines, marketing tactics, customer sentiment, and market position. ● Focuses on actionable differentiation and strategic patterns rather than superficial feature lists. ● Customizable by industry, competitor set, and specific business context to ensure relevant insights. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive intelligence and strategic positioning.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitive analysis by:

1. Research each competitor's products, services, marketing strategies, customer feedback, and market position
2. Identify and synthesize the key strengths and weaknesses for each company
3. Determine what differentiates each competitor in the marketplace
4. Compare findings across all competitors to reveal strategic patterns

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}

Competitors to analyze: {{competitor-list}}

Business context: {{context}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with exactly 4 columns:

| Company Name | Strengths | Weaknesses | Unique Value Proposition |
|--------------|-----------|------------|-------------------------|

Each cell should be concise yet substantive, enabling direct comparison across competitors. Focus on actionable insights rather than generic observations.
```
