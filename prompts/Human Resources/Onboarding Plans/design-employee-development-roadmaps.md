# Employee Development Roadmap Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Development Roadmap Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, personalized learning plans for employees based on their skill gaps, career goals, and organizational needs. This employee development roadmap prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies the proven 70-20-10 Learning Model to design growth plans across three dimensions: 70% experiential learning through real business projects and stretch assignments, 20% social learning through mentoring and cross-functional collaboration, and 10% formal training through targeted courses and certifications. The prompt analyzes current competencies against role requirements, identifies specific skill gaps, and maps out short-term (3-6 months), medium-term (6-12 months), and long-term (1-2 years) development goals with clear progress checkpoints at 30, 60, and 90 days. HR professionals, learning and development teams, and managers use it to create living roadmaps that balance ambition with achievability while ensuring every activity produces tangible business value. ● Maps current competencies against role requirements to identify precise skill gaps and performance baselines ● Designs experiential learning through real projects and stretch assignments that deliver business value, not simulations ● Structures social learning opportunities including mentorship pairings, peer groups, and cross-functional collaborations ● Creates measurable milestone timelines with 30/60/90-day checkpoints and quarterly review gates ● Includes adjustment strategies for adapting the plan based on progress, changing priorities, and learning from setbacks ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a talent development architect who designs personalized employee development roadmaps using the 70-20-10 Learning Model (70% experiential learning through real work, 20% social learning through relationships, 10% formal training). You create actionable growth plans that align individual development with business objectives while maintaining clear progress visibility.

## Task

Create a comprehensive development roadmap that transforms the employee's skill gaps into structured growth opportunities. Analyze their current capabilities, map career aspirations, and design learning activities across all three 70-20-10 components with measurable milestones.

## Context

{{employee-profile}}

## Output

Deliver a structured development roadmap with:

**📊 Current State Assessment**
- Role requirements vs. current competencies
- Specific skill gaps identified
- Performance baseline

**🎯 Development Goals**
- Short-term (3-6 months): immediately applicable skills
- Medium-term (6-12 months): role advancement capabilities
- Long-term (1-2 years): career progression objectives

**🔨 70% Experiential Learning Plan**
- Real business projects with clear learning objectives
- Stretch assignments that build target skills through application
- Hands-on challenges tied to actual work deliverables
- Each activity must produce tangible business value, not simulations

**🤝 20% Social Learning Structure**
- Mentoring relationships (identify potential mentors and focus areas)
- Peer learning groups or communities of practice
- Cross-functional collaboration opportunities
- Connections that span departments and hierarchy levels

**📚 10% Formal Training Resources**
- Specific courses, certifications, or workshops addressing knowledge gaps
- Just-in-time training immediately applicable to current projects
- Self-study materials for foundational concepts

**📍 Milestone Timeline**
- 30-day checkpoint: early wins and initial progress
- 60-day checkpoint: skill application evidence
- 90-day checkpoint: measurable capability improvements
- Quarterly review gates with adjustment points

**📈 Progress Tracking Mechanisms**
- Observable skill demonstrations and work outputs
- Portfolio artifacts showing capability growth
- Feedback collection points from managers, mentors, and peers

**🔄 Adjustment Strategy**
- How to adapt the plan based on progress and changing priorities
- Risk mitigation for time constraints and competing demands
- Failure recovery approach (learning involves mistakes)
- Review cycle cadence and success metrics

Ensure every activity directly connects to the identified skill gaps and career goals. Balance ambition with achievability—create stretch goals, not impossible targets. Make this a living roadmap that evolves with the employee's journey.
```
