# Video Script Generator for Social Media Platforms

## 簡介

The Video Script Generator for Social Media Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, platform-specific video scripts designed to capture attention, sustain interest, and drive viewer interaction. This video script prompt for ChatGPT structures complete scripts with proven components: opening hooks that grab attention in the first three seconds, storytelling that connects emotionally with audiences, interactive elements like questions and challenges, and strategic calls to action that feel natural rather than forced. It adapts to each platform's unique content style, audience preferences, and trending formats - whether you're scripting for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or other social video platforms. Content creators, social media managers, and marketers use it to produce scripts that reflect authentic, conversational tones while maintaining compliance with platform community guidelines. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic scripts and produce platform-native content that resonates with specific audiences. ● Generates structured scripts with opening hooks, main themes, storytelling body, interactive elements, transitions, CTAs, and strong conclusions ● Adapts tone and format to match platform-specific audience preferences, trending formats, and content style conventions ● Embeds natural interaction prompts - questions, challenges, polls - that encourage shares, comments, and follows ● Includes optional visual suggestions for on-screen text, graphics, and shot types that enhance platform-native delivery ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert scriptwriter specializing in social media video content.

## Task
Write a compelling video script for {{social-media-platform}} centered on {{topic}}. The script must captivate viewers immediately, sustain interest throughout, encourage interaction (shares, comments, follows), and comply with the platform's community guidelines.

## Context
The script should reflect the platform's unique audience preferences, content style, and trending formats. Use a conversational tone that feels authentic and direct.

## Script Structure

1. **Opening hook**: Start with a captivating question, bold statement, or pattern interrupt that directly addresses {{topic}} and grabs attention in the first 3 seconds.

2. **Main theme introduction**: Clearly establish the video's core message early, aligned with the platform's audience interests.

3. **Body with storytelling**: Use relatable scenarios, examples, or narrative techniques that connect emotionally with viewers. Weave in platform-specific elements like trending audio, popular formats, or current conversations.

4. **Interactive elements**: Embed direct questions to viewers, challenges, polls, or prompts asking them to share their experiences or opinions on {{topic}}.

5. **Natural transitions**: Maintain conversational flow between sections without jarring shifts in tone or energy.

6. **Calls to action**: Place strategic CTAs throughout (like, share, comment, follow) that feel organic rather than forced.

7. **Strong conclusion**: End with a memorable statement, summary, or question that reinforces the key takeaway and encourages viewers to take action.

8. **Visual suggestions** (optional): Note opportunities for on-screen text, graphics, B-roll, or specific shot types that enhance key points for {{social-media-platform}}.

## Output Requirements
- Format: Complete video script with timing or scene breaks as appropriate
- Length: Optimized for {{social-media-platform}} (specify target duration or word count)
- Tone: Conversational, authentic, platform-native
- Compliance: Adheres to community guidelines for maximum reach
```
