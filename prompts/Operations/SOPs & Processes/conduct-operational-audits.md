# Operational Audit Prompt for Business Efficiency Review

## 簡介

The Operational Audit Prompt for Business Efficiency Review is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic operational audits to identify inefficiencies and improvement opportunities for business leaders and process consultants. This operational audit prompt for ChatGPT walks through a four-step audit framework: documenting current processes, identifying bottlenecks and resource waste, developing root-cause solutions, and prioritizing recommendations by impact and feasibility. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a markdown table that maps each process to its inefficiencies and corresponding actionable solutions. Real use cases include manufacturing workflow reviews, SaaS onboarding optimization, supply-chain diagnostics, and service-delivery process mapping. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, repeatable method to audit operations across departments, benchmark current performance, or prepare efficiency improvement plans for stakeholders. ● Maps current processes to specific inefficiencies and recommended solutions in a three-column markdown table ● Identifies bottlenecks, resource waste, and performance gaps using systematic review criteria ● Prioritizes solutions by implementation feasibility and expected business impact ● Tailors findings to your industry, company size, operational challenges, and KPIs through a single business-context variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business auditor specializing in operational efficiency analysis.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive operational audit to identify inefficiencies, bottlenecks, and areas for improvement across business workflows. Review current processes systematically, analyze performance gaps, and develop practical, innovative solutions tailored to the specific operational context.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
(Include industry, company size, main operational challenges, and key performance indicators)

## Process
1. Review and document current processes and workflows
2. Identify inefficiencies, bottlenecks, resource waste, and performance gaps
3. Develop actionable recommendations addressing root causes
4. Prioritize solutions by impact and feasibility

## Output
Present audit findings in a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Processes | Inefficiencies | Recommended Solutions |
|-------------------|----------------|----------------------|
| [Process description] | [Specific inefficiency identified] | [Actionable solution with expected impact] |

Ensure findings are comprehensive, evidence-based, and directly applicable to the business context provided.
```
