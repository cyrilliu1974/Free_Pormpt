# Agile Project Management Implementation Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Agile Project Management Implementation Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized Agile transition strategies for teams and organizations moving from traditional project management approaches. This Agile project management prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current workflows, team structure, and organizational constraints to recommend specific Agile practices - whether Scrum ceremonies, Kanban systems, or XP techniques - mapped directly to your existing pain points. It produces a structured markdown table comparing current processes against recommended Agile practices and their measurable benefits, plus a phased implementation timeline and change management guidance. Use it when planning an Agile transformation, addressing productivity bottlenecks, or tailoring Agile frameworks to your industry and team dynamics. The prompt runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Maps 6-10 current processes or challenges to targeted Agile practices with clear rationale ● Addresses team readiness, change management, and transition risks specific to your context ● Delivers measurable expected benefits across velocity, quality, transparency, and delivery timelines ● Includes implementation phasing and rollout recommendations tailored to team size and industry ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an Agile project management consultant designing a tailored implementation plan.

## Task
Analyze the current state, recommend specific Agile practices, and create a transition roadmap that addresses team dynamics, project characteristics, and organizational culture.

## Context
{{current-state}}

Include: current project management approach, team size, industry, main challenges (productivity bottlenecks, communication gaps, delivery timelines, quality issues, etc.), and any relevant organizational constraints.

## Requirements
- Map current processes to appropriate Agile practices (Scrum, Kanban, XP techniques, ceremonies, artifacts)
- Identify transition challenges and provide mitigation strategies
- Account for team readiness and change management needs
- Tailor recommendations to the industry and project types described

## Output
Deliver your implementation plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Processes | Agile Practices | Expected Benefits |
|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|

Each row should:
- Describe one existing process or pain point
- Recommend a specific Agile practice or ceremony to address it
- Explain the measurable benefit (velocity, quality, transparency, morale, time-to-market, etc.)

Include 6-10 rows covering planning, execution, communication, and delivery aspects. After the table, provide a brief implementation timeline and change management recommendations.
```
