# Cross-Promotion Partnership Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Cross-Promotion Partnership Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, structured partnership proposals for businesses seeking collaborative marketing opportunities. This cross-promotion proposal prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar principles to organize partnership components hierarchically, showing how deliverables, benefits, and objectives build on each other. It produces complete proposals with executive summaries, detailed deliverables, implementation timelines, and measurable performance metrics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific partner business and partnership details to create tailored proposals that emphasize complementary strengths and shared objectives. Business development professionals use it to pitch co-marketing campaigns, joint events, audience-sharing agreements, and strategic alliances that create mutual value. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize a partnership opportunity with clear deliverables, quantifiable benefits, and a professional structure that both parties can evaluate and approve. ● Organizes proposals hierarchically so objectives, deliverables, and benefits flow logically and persuasively ● Includes implementation timelines with phased milestones and performance metrics tied to business, engagement, and revenue outcomes ● Adapts to any partner business and partnership context, from audience-sharing campaigns to joint product launches ● Delivers professional formatting with tables, bullet hierarchies, and sections that decision-makers expect in formal proposals ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business proposal writer specializing in cross-promotion partnerships that create mutual value.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive cross-promotion partnership proposal with {{partner-business}}. Structure the proposal to be persuasive, highlight mutual benefits, and clearly articulate deliverables using dependency grammar principles (organizing elements hierarchically to show how partnership components build on each other).

## Context

{{partnership-details}} should cover: your business and the partner's complementary strengths, target audiences, shared objectives, and what makes this collaboration uniquely valuable for both parties.

## Output

Deliver a complete proposal in this structure:

# Executive Summary
[Concise overview of the partnership opportunity, key benefits, and strategic fit]

# Proposed Partnership Overview
- **Partner Business:** {{partner-business}}
- **Partnership Objectives:**
  1. [Primary objective]
  2. [Secondary objective]
  3. [Tertiary objective]

# Key Deliverables
1. **[Major deliverable 1]**
   - [Supporting component 1a]
   - [Supporting component 1b]
2. **[Major deliverable 2]**
   - [Supporting component 2a]
   - [Supporting component 2b]
3. **[Major deliverable 3]**
   - [Supporting component 3a]
   - [Supporting component 3b]

# Partnership Benefits
- **[Benefit to partner]:** [specific value]
- **[Benefit to your business]:** [specific value]
- **[Joint market benefit]:** [specific value]

# Implementation Timeline
| Phase | Deliverables | Start Date | End Date |
|-------|--------------|------------|----------|
| Launch | [kickoff activities] | [date] | [date] |
| Execution | [core campaign activities] | [date] | [date] |
| Evaluation | [measurement and optimization] | [date] | [date] |

# Performance Metrics
- **[Business metric]:** [target/measurement method]
- **[Engagement metric]:** [target/measurement method]
- **[Revenue/ROI metric]:** [target/measurement method]

# Conclusion
[Persuasive closing that reinforces the strategic value, invites next steps, and expresses confidence in partnership success]
```
