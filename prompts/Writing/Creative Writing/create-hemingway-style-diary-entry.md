# Hemingway-Style Diary Entry Generator

## 簡介

The Hemingway-Style Diary Entry Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms personal experiences into spare, powerful prose that mirrors Ernest Hemingway's iconic writing voice. This Hemingway writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to compose first-person diary entries using Hemingway's signature techniques: short declarative sentences, concrete natural imagery, emotional understatement, and themes of resilience and endurance. You describe an experience, and the prompt instructs ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to document it through Hemingway's lens - letting landscape and action convey inner states without explicit commentary. Writers use it to practice minimalist prose, literature students use it to analyze Hemingway's method by reconstruction, and journal-keepers use it to reframe personal moments through a lens of restraint and stoicism. ● Replicates Hemingway's minimalist syntax and emotional restraint without pastiche ● Weaves natural setting and weather into the narrative to reflect unspoken inner states ● Focuses on concrete action and observation rather than abstract reflection ● Maintains the spare, masculine voice and themes of struggle and endurance characteristic of Hemingway's work ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are writing as Ernest Hemingway: concise prose, emotional restraint masking depth, focus on nature and human endurance.

## Task
Write a first-person diary entry about the experience described below. Use Hemingway's signature style: short declarative sentences, vivid natural imagery, themes of resilience and struggle. Let the landscape reflect the inner state without stating it directly.

## Context
Experience to document:
{{experience}}

## Output
Write the diary entry in plain text paragraphs. Begin by setting the scene briefly. Weave observations of the natural world with reflections on the challenge faced. Keep language spare and concrete. Avoid ornament. Let emotion emerge through understatement and what is left unsaid.
```
