# Modern Product Grid Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Modern Product Grid Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 2×2 grid product advertisements for brands and marketers showcasing ergonomic furniture, workspace products, and lifestyle goods. This product grid advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces four coordinated panels in a single square canvas: a studio hero shot, a standard-use lifestyle scene, an alternate-configuration view, and a detail close-up of controls or materials. It delivers modern workspace advertising with natural daylight, minimalist interiors, and premium branding aesthetics ideal for LinkedIn campaigns, e-commerce banners, website hero sections, and digital marketing. Designers, furniture brands, product marketers, and agencies reach for this prompt when they need polished, multi-angle visual assets without studio photography costs. ● Produces a complete 2×2 grid with hero product shot, professional lifestyle use, alternate ergonomic configuration, and control mechanism detail in one cohesive visual. ● Customizes product name, workspace environment, color palette, and branding to match furniture lines, office equipment, or productivity tools. ● Delivers natural daylight photography style with soft shadows, minimal interiors, and premium office lifestyle mood for conversion-focused campaigns. ● Outputs high-resolution visuals ready for LinkedIn ads, social media marketing, e-commerce listings, and website hero sections without expensive photo shoots. ## Prompt

```
Create a clean, modern 2×2 grid advertisement for {{product-name}}, emphasizing ergonomics, productivity, and elevated work lifestyle.

**Grid Structure:** 1:1 square canvas divided into four equal panels.

**Panel 1 – Hero Product:** Studio hero shot of the {{product-name}} in primary configuration. Soft directional daylight, minimal interior with neutral tones. Focus on structural frame, control interface, and logo. Premium, precise, stable aesthetic.

**Panel 2 – Standard Use:** Professional using the {{product-name}} in standard configuration within a modern office environment. Natural daylight from side window. Convey focus, comfort, and efficiency.

**Panel 3 – Alternate Mode:** User working with the {{product-name}} adjusted to alternate ergonomic configuration. Bright, balanced daylight highlighting healthy posture. Energy, wellness, balance.

**Panel 4 – Control Detail:** Close-up of the primary control or adjustment mechanism. Shallow depth of field on technology, controls, and material finish. Innovation, control, reliability.

**Visual Style:** Modern workspace advertising, minimalist interior photography, high realism, clean composition, premium office lifestyle branding. Natural daylight with soft diffusion. Balanced exposure, soft natural shadows.

**Brand Presentation:** Minimal logo, etched or flush-mounted. Modern, ergonomic, productivity-driven personality.

**Mood:** Focused, healthy, modern, productive, clean, empowering.

**Avoid:** Cluttered surfaces, cheap materials, unstable appearance, unreadable controls, harsh lighting, oversaturation, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
