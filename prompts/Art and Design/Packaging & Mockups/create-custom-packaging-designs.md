# Custom Packaging Design Prompt for AI Image Generators

## 簡介

The Custom Packaging Design Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that produces tailored packaging visuals for any product category and aesthetic direction. This packaging design prompt for Midjourney works by letting you define three core inputs: the physical package format (bottle, box, pouch, can), the product and brand context, and the desired visual style or aesthetic treatment. The prompt includes Midjourney-compatible parameters (--s 50 for moderate stylization, --ar 1:1 for square output) that balance creative interpretation with design control. Use it to explore packaging concepts for beverage brands, cosmetics, food products, or consumer goods across minimalist, vintage, modern, or experimental visual languages. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and any image model that honors parameter syntax. Designers, brand strategists, and product teams reach for this prompt when prototyping packaging directions, pitching visual identities, or testing shelf appeal before committing to production artwork. ● Specify any package type - bottles, boxes, tubes, sachets, cans - and pair it with your product category for relevant output. ● Control the aesthetic by describing visual styles: clean and minimal, ornate vintage, bold geometric, hand-drawn organic, or luxury premium. ● Generates square 1:1 packaging mockups ideal for social media grids, portfolio presentations, and client concept reviews. ● Works across industries: skincare, beverages, snacks, supplements, candles, pet products, and more. ## Prompt

```
{{package-type}} packaging for {{product-context}}, featuring {{visual-style}}, --s 50, --ar 1:1
```
