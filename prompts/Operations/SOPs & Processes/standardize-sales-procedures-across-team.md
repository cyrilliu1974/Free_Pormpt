# Sales Process Flowchart Builder for Teams

## 簡介

The Sales Process Flowchart Builder for Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured, scalable sales methodologies from lead generation through closing and post-sale follow-up. This sales process prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed flowchart that defines stage transitions, measurable objectives, KPIs, and contingency paths tailored to your sales organization. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok and transforms your sales context into a visual, actionable workflow that standardizes operations across your entire team. Whether you're aligning a remote sales force, onboarding new reps, or refining an existing pipeline, this prompt analyzes current operations, integrates industry best practices, and maps decision points with alternative paths for different scenarios. ● Maps the complete customer journey with clear stage names, transitions, and decision diamonds for branching scenarios ● Assigns specific, measurable objectives and KPIs to each stage so progress and bottlenecks are trackable ● Identifies improvement opportunities and potential roadblocks with contingency plans baked into the flow ● Outputs in structured markdown or visual flowchart notation ready for diagramming tools or team handbooks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales process consultant specializing in designing standardized, scalable sales methodologies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step sales process flowchart that guides the team from lead generation through closing and follow-up. The flowchart should define clear stages, measurable objectives, and include KPIs for tracking effectiveness at each phase.

## Context
**Sales organization details:**
{{sales-context}}

*In your response, address:*
- Current operations and improvement opportunities
- A logical sequence through the entire customer journey
- Industry best practices relevant to this market
- Potential roadblocks with contingency plans
- Scalability across different sales scenarios
- Key performance indicators for each stage

## Output
Deliver a detailed flowchart structure with:
- Clear stage names and transitions
- Specific, measurable objectives for each stage
- Concise instructions for sales team implementation
- Decision points and alternative paths where appropriate
- Recommended KPIs tied to each stage

Format the flowchart using clear visual indicators (arrows, decision diamonds, process boxes) or structured markdown that can be easily translated into a visual diagram.
```
