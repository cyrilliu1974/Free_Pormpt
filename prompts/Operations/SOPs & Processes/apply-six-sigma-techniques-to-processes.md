# Six Sigma DMAIC Process Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Six Sigma DMAIC Process Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that applies the full Define-Measure-Analyze-Improve-Control methodology to any business workflow you need to refine. This Six Sigma prompt for ChatGPT takes your process description, industry constraints, and current bottlenecks, then delivers a five-column markdown table with at least five detailed, actionable strategies per DMAIC phase. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce a structured roadmap covering goal-setting, KPI selection, root-cause analysis, improvement tactics, and control mechanisms. Teams use it to document process-improvement initiatives, prepare for kaizen events, and build data-driven optimization plans without hiring external consultants. It is ideal for operations managers, continuous-improvement specialists, and quality leads who need a rigorous, repeatable framework quickly. ● Covers all five DMAIC phases in a single table with measurable, resource-aware action items ● Tailors strategies to your specific industry context and documented bottlenecks ● Outputs markdown format that drops directly into project documentation and presentation decks ● Provides at least 25 concrete strategies total, ensuring depth across Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Six Sigma expert specializing in business process optimization.

## Task
Apply the DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control) methodology to optimize the specified business process. Develop detailed strategies and action items for each phase that enhance productivity and streamline workflow.

## Context
**Business process:** {{process-to-optimize}}

**Industry and constraints:** {{industry-context}}

**Current bottlenecks:** {{bottlenecks}}

## Output
Present your recommendations in a markdown table with 5 columns—one for each DMAIC phase (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control). Each column must contain at least 5 detailed, actionable strategies or action items that directly address the process, bottlenecks, and industry context provided. Ensure strategies are specific, measurable, and implementable with typical organizational resources.
```
