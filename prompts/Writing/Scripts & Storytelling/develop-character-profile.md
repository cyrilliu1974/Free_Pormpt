# Character Dialogue Script Generator for Video

## 簡介

The Character Dialogue Script Generator for Video is a free AI prompt that creates character-driven dialogue scripts for video projects, exploring specific topics through meaningful exchanges that reveal personality depth and advance story arcs. This character dialogue prompt for ChatGPT produces a full script framework including setting establishment, character profiles, dialogue objectives, and the exchange itself - complete with spoken lines, body language cues in brackets, and reflective analysis of how the conversation shifts relationships. It works by taking your dialogue setup (characters, traits, topic, setting, and objectives) and structuring a conversation that alternates distinct perspectives, builds tension through conflict, and reaches a resolution or turning point. Scriptwriters, content creators, and filmmakers use it to craft scenes for short films, web series, narrative podcasts, and character-focused video content on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need dialogue that sounds authentic to each character's voice and serves both emotional engagement and narrative momentum. ● Produces dialogue that reflects unique personality traits, backgrounds, and emotional states for each character ● Includes non-verbal cues and body language directions to guide actors and enhance performance ● Builds dramatic tension through conflict or disagreement that exposes character strengths and vulnerabilities ● Concludes with a turning point or resolution that propels the story forward and signals future character dynamics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced video script writer specializing in character-driven storytelling.

## Task
Write a dialogue script between characters exploring a specified topic. The conversation must reveal character depth, showcase distinct perspectives, advance the narrative, and engage the audience emotionally and intellectually.

## Context
{{dialogue-setup}}
Provide: the characters involved (names, personality traits, backgrounds, current emotional states), the topic they're discussing, the setting and situation leading to the conversation, and what each character hopes to achieve through this exchange.

## Output
Deliver a dialogue script that includes:

**Setting & Situation**  
Briefly establish where and why this conversation is happening.

**Character Profiles**  
Summarize each character's traits, motivations, and state of mind relevant to this exchange.

**Dialogue Objective**  
State the purpose of the conversation and how it serves the larger story.

**The Dialogue**  
- Open with a line that immediately engages and sets the tone
- Alternate dialogue that reflects each character's unique voice and viewpoint
- Include body language and non-verbal cues in [brackets] to convey unspoken emotions
- Build tension through conflict or disagreement that reveals character strengths or flaws
- Conclude with a resolution or turning point that propels future story developments

**Reflection**  
Analyze how this conversation has shifted the relationship between characters and what it signals for their future interactions.

Format clearly with character names, spoken lines, and stage directions so actors can interpret and audiences can follow naturally.
```
