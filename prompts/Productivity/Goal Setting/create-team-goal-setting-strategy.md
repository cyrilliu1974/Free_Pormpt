# Team Goal-Setting Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Team Goal-Setting Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates structured SMART goals for teams across any function or industry. It acts as an expert performance management consultant, translating your team and organizational context into clearly defined, trackable objectives. This team goal-setting prompt for ChatGPT walks through each dimension of the SMART framework: specificity, measurability, achievability, relevance to broader mission, concrete timelines, and a monitoring approach for progress reviews. You input your team's function, constraints, and organizational priorities; the prompt returns 3–5 fully formed goals complete with KPIs, deadlines, and review cadences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible for managers, HR professionals, and team leads working in any text-based AI environment. Real-world use cases include quarterly planning cycles, post-restructure alignment, onboarding new managers, and cascading company OKRs down to department level. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from vague aspirations to actionable, time-bound objectives that your team can rally behind and you can measure. ● Produces 3–5 complete SMART goals, each with specific outcomes, measurable KPIs, achievability rationale, relevance to company mission, deadlines, and monitoring plans. ● Adapts to any team size, function, or industry by accepting freeform context about your organization, resources, and strategic priorities. ● Structures output for immediate use in performance reviews, project kickoffs, and stakeholder presentations. ● Saves hours of workshop facilitation by applying performance-management best practices in seconds. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in performance management and human resources specializing in SMART goal frameworks.

## Task

Develop 3–5 SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) for the team described below. Each goal must include:

- **Specific objective**: Clear, detailed outcome with no ambiguity
- **Measurable criteria**: Concrete metrics or KPIs that can be tracked
- **Achievability assessment**: Confirmation the goal fits the team's resources, skills, and constraints
- **Relevance**: Alignment with both the team's function and the organization's broader mission
- **Timeline**: Realistic deadline for completion
- **Monitoring approach**: How progress will be reviewed (e.g., weekly check-ins, monthly reports, quarterly reviews)

## Context

{{team-and-org-context}}

## Output

Provide a numbered list of SMART goals. For each goal, use this structure:

**Goal [N]: [Title]**  
- **Specific**: [What exactly will be achieved]  
- **Measurable**: [Metrics/KPIs and target numbers]  
- **Achievable**: [Why this is realistic given current capabilities]  
- **Relevant**: [How this supports organizational objectives]  
- **Time-bound**: [Deadline]  
- **Monitoring**: [Review cadence and method]

Example:

**Goal 1: Improve Customer Satisfaction**  
- **Specific**: Increase customer satisfaction ratings through a new feedback system and enhanced training  
- **Measurable**: Achieve a 15% increase in CSAT score  
- **Achievable**: Training resources allocated; feedback platform selected  
- **Relevant**: Aligns with company mission to deliver exceptional service  
- **Time-bound**: End of Q2 2024  
- **Monitoring**: Bi-weekly team reviews of feedback data and training completion rates
```
