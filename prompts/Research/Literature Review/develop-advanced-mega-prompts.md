# Cross-Disciplinary Theory Comparison Prompt

## 簡介

The Cross-Disciplinary Theory Comparison Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates structured comparative analyses of theories or models across multiple academic and professional fields for researchers, students, and interdisciplinary practitioners. This cross-disciplinary theory comparison prompt for ChatGPT examines how a single theory manifests and adapts in different disciplinary contexts - from psychology to business, economics to education - then synthesizes cross-cutting insights and concrete integration opportunities. The prompt runs on text-generation models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing disciplinary breakdowns (key principles, unique adaptations, limitations), a synthesis of 3-5 emergent themes or contradictions, and actionable recommendations for cross-pollinating insights between fields. Use it when writing literature reviews, preparing interdisciplinary research proposals, or exploring how a framework behaves under different epistemological lenses. ● Systematically breaks down how any theory or model operates in each specified discipline, highlighting principles, adaptations, and critiques ● Identifies 3-5 patterns, contradictions, or emergent themes visible only through cross-disciplinary comparison ● Proposes concrete integration opportunities where insights from one field strengthen understanding or application in another ● Requires citation of 5 authoritative sources distributed across the disciplines examined, ensuring evidence-based analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in cross-disciplinary analysis with deep knowledge across multiple academic and professional fields.

## Task
Conduct a systematic comparative analysis of {{theory-or-model}} across {{disciplines}}. Examine how the theory manifests, adapts, and performs in each disciplinary context, then synthesize cross-disciplinary insights and integration opportunities.

## Output Structure

**Disciplinary Analysis**

For each discipline in {{disciplines}}, provide:

1. **Key Principles**: Which aspects of {{theory-or-model}} are most relevant and emphasized
2. **Unique Adaptations**: How the discipline modifies or applies the theory in field-specific ways
3. **Limitations & Critiques**: Discipline-specific challenges, criticisms, or boundary conditions

**Cross-Disciplinary Insights**

Identify 3-5 patterns, contradictions, or emergent themes that appear when viewing {{theory-or-model}} across all disciplines.

**Integration Opportunities**

Propose 3-5 concrete ways insights from one discipline could strengthen application or understanding in another.

**Sources**

Cite 5 authoritative sources (academic papers, seminal texts, or recognized practitioners) that informed the analysis, distributed across the disciplines examined.
```
