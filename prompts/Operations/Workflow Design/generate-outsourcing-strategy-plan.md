# Outsourcing Strategy Plan Generator for Business Tasks

## 簡介

The Outsourcing Strategy Plan Generator for Business Tasks is a free AI prompt that analyzes your operations and creates a structured outsourcing roadmap for business owners, consultants, and operations managers. This outsourcing strategy prompt for ChatGPT evaluates each task in your workflow, scores its suitability for delegation (1-5 scale), and delivers a phased implementation plan complete with vendor selection criteria, quality controls, risk mitigation strategies, budget estimates, and expected ROI calculations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Use it when you need to identify which tasks drain internal resources, compare the cost-effectiveness of outsourcing versus in-house execution, or present a data-backed delegation plan to stakeholders. ● Scores every task for outsourcing suitability with clear rationale tied to efficiency and cost impact ● Generates vendor selection criteria, communication plans, and quality control checkpoints specific to your context ● Provides a phased timeline so you implement outsourcing incrementally rather than all at once ● Calculates estimated budget and expected ROI so you can justify the investment and track results ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in outsourcing strategies and optimization.

## Task
Analyze the provided task list and create a comprehensive outsourcing plan that optimizes business operations through strategic delegation.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Provide specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the user's unique situation. Focus on tasks that improve efficiency and cost-effectiveness when outsourced.

## Output
Deliver your analysis in the following structure:

**Task List Analysis:**
Thorough analysis of the provided task list, identifying outsourcing opportunities.

**Outsourcing Suitability Table:**
| Task | Outsourcing Suitability (1-5) | Rationale |
|------|-------------------------------|-----------|
| [Task 1] | [Score] | [Clear reasoning for score] |
| [Task 2] | [Score] | [Clear reasoning for score] |

**Outsourcing Recommendations:**
Specific, actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

**Vendor Selection Criteria:**
Criteria for selecting best-fit vendors for outsourced tasks.

**Communication and Monitoring Plan:**
Plan for effectively managing and monitoring outsourced work.

**Quality Control Measures:**
Measures to ensure high-quality output from outsourced tasks.

**Risk Mitigation Strategies:**
Strategies to address potential outsourcing risks.

**Outsourcing Timeline:**
Phased implementation timeline for the outsourcing plan.

**Outsourcing Budget:**
Estimated budget required for the outsourcing initiative.

**Expected ROI:**
Calculation of the expected return on investment from outsourcing.
```
