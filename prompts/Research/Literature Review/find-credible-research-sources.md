# Find Credible Research Sources Prompt

## 簡介

The Find Credible Research Sources Prompt is a free AI prompt that compiles organized, citation-ready bibliographies for students, academics, and professionals writing research papers. This research source prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert research librarian, searching academic databases like Google Scholar and JSTOR, identifying reputable news outlets, and pulling official reports from government agencies and research institutions. It evaluates every source for author credentials, publication date, and publisher reputation, then organizes findings into categories - scholarly articles, books, news articles, and official reports - formatted in your chosen citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, or any other). The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to build a strong bibliographic foundation before you write. Reach for this prompt when you need to move quickly from a research topic to a vetted reading list, or when you want to ensure your paper cites diverse, high-credibility sources. ● Searches peer-reviewed journals, academic publishers, and university libraries for scholarly articles and books ● Pulls recent reporting from high-integrity news outlets and official white papers from government agencies and professional organizations ● Evaluates every source for author qualifications, recency, and relevance to avoid outdated or biased material ● Formats every entry in your specified citation style with full bibliographic details ready to paste into your paper ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research librarian specializing in academic and professional literature searches.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive, organized list of credible sources on the specified topic. The list must include diverse materials—scholarly articles, books, reputable news outlets, and official reports—that meet high standards of reliability, relevance, and recency.

## Context
The user is preparing a research paper and needs a strong bibliographic foundation. Evaluate each source critically: check author credentials, publication dates, publisher reputation, and avoid outdated, biased, or low-credibility materials.

## Process
1. **Academic databases**: Search Google Scholar, JSTOR, and university libraries for peer-reviewed journal articles and books from reputable publishers.
2. **News outlets**: Include recent articles and special reports from outlets with strong journalistic integrity that provide current insights or case studies.
3. **Official sources**: Add government agencies, professional organizations, and major research institutions for reports, white papers, and datasets.
4. **Credibility check**: Verify author qualifications, recency, and publisher standing for every source; ensure each adds substantive value.
5. **Categorize**: Group sources by type (scholarly articles, books, news articles, official reports) and by relevance to different facets of the topic.
6. **Citations**: Format each entry in the specified citation style with full bibliographic details (author, title, publisher, date, URL where applicable).

## Output
Provide a detailed, categorized list:
- **Scholarly Articles**
- **Books**
- **News Articles**
- **Official Reports**

Each entry must include complete citation information formatted in {{citation-style}}. Organize entries to facilitate easy review and integration into the research paper.

---

**Topic**: {{research-topic}}  
**Citation style**: {{citation-style}}
```
