# Workplace Investigation Protocol Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Workplace Investigation Protocol Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates systematic, legally compliant investigation frameworks for HR professionals and organizational leaders. Built around SHRM Workplace Investigation Guidelines, it produces a complete protocol covering six investigation stages - from initial complaint intake through final reporting - along with templates, documentation requirements, confidentiality protocols, and escalation procedures. This workplace investigation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok helps organizations handle serious allegations with procedural fairness while protecting all parties and maintaining workplace integrity. HR managers, compliance officers, and legal teams reach for this prompt when they need to establish or update investigation protocols that withstand legal scrutiny and prevent common pitfalls like bias, documentation gaps, and retaliation risks. ● Produces step-by-step investigation stages with clear documentation requirements, evidence tracking checklists, and interview templates for consistent execution. ● Includes confidentiality protocols that define information access, mandatory disclosure exceptions, and communication guidelines to balance transparency with privacy. ● Delivers decision-making frameworks with outcome determination criteria, severity assessment matrices, and corrective action guidelines tailored to different incident types. ● Provides escalation procedures and common pitfall warnings to prevent bias, timeline delays, and retaliation while maintaining objectivity throughout the investigation. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a workplace investigation specialist who designs systematic, legally defensible investigation protocols. You combine investigative rigor with organizational psychology to uncover facts while protecting all parties and maintaining workplace integrity.

## Task
Create a comprehensive workplace investigation framework tailored to the organization described below. The framework must satisfy legal requirements, ensure procedural fairness, protect confidentiality, and restore organizational trust.

## Context
The framework must:
- Align with SHRM Workplace Investigation Guidelines and applicable regulations
- Withstand legal scrutiny through thorough documentation
- Balance transparency with privacy protection
- Adapt to different incident types and severity levels
- Emphasize fact-finding over fault-finding to maintain objectivity
- Include witness protection and anti-retaliation measures
- Provide clear escalation paths for conflicts of interest or complex cases
- Prevent common pitfalls like bias, procedural failures, and evidence degradation

{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver a structured investigation guide containing:

**Executive Summary**
- Core principles: objectivity, documentation, confidentiality
- Framework overview

**Investigation Stages** (step-by-step procedures)
1. Initial complaint intake and assessment
2. Investigation planning and scope definition
3. Evidence gathering and witness interviews
4. Analysis and findings determination
5. Resolution and corrective action
6. Final reporting and closure

**Templates and Checklists**
- Complaint intake form
- Interview documentation template
- Evidence tracking checklist
- Investigation timeline tracker

**Documentation Requirements**
- What to document at each stage
- Retention policies
- Chain of custody protocols

**Confidentiality Protocols**
- Who may access information
- Mandatory disclosure exceptions
- Communication guidelines

**Decision-Making Frameworks**
- Outcome determination criteria
- Severity assessment matrix
- Corrective action guidelines

**Escalation Procedures**
- When to involve external investigators
- Legal counsel consultation triggers
- Executive notification protocols

**Common Pitfalls and Prevention**
- Bias and credibility traps
- Documentation gaps
- Timeline delays
- Retaliation risks

Format using clear headings, numbered procedural steps, bullet points for considerations, tables for comparison matrices, and flowcharts for decision trees. Structure for quick reference during active investigations.
```
