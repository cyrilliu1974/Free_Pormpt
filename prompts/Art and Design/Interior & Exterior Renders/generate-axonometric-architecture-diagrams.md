# Exploded Axonometric Architecture Diagram Generator

## 簡介

The Exploded Axonometric Architecture Diagram Generator is a free AI prompt that produces technical architectural diagrams showing building systems separated into precise vertical layers for architects, designers, and educators. This architecture diagram prompt for Midjourney and Flux creates publication-ready axonometric views that deconstruct buildings into eight exploded layers: foundation platforms, structural skeletons, interior cores, floor plates, facade panels, partitions, and roof assemblies. Each component floats along a shared vertical axis with consistent spacing, rendered in clean CAD-style linework with customizable ink colors and true axonometric projection. The prompt outputs 1:1 aspect-ratio diagrams suitable for portfolios, client presentations, construction documentation, and academic materials without requiring 3D modeling software. Reach for this prompt when you need to explain complex building systems clearly, communicate construction logic visually, or produce professional technical drawings that separate structure from envelope and partitions. ● Produces eight-layer exploded assemblies showing foundation, structure, core, floors, envelope panels, partitions, and roof with controlled vertical spacing and alignment guides. ● Customizable building types, spatial programs, and single-color ink rendering on white backgrounds while maintaining true axonometric geometry. ● Outputs clean technical linework with differentiated line weights for structure, envelope, and partitions, plus optional transparency for glazing and numeric layer labels. ● Excludes perspective distortion, photorealistic rendering, textures, heavy shadows, and cluttered annotations to maintain diagram clarity. ## Prompt

```
## Subject
Exploded axonometric architectural diagram of a contemporary {{building-type}}, separated into eight vertical layers with wall panels fanned outward. True axonometric projection (no perspective), all elements aligned to a single vertical axis, consistent exploded spacing, clean technical linework, no photoreal rendering or textures.

## Layers (Bottom to Top)
1. Foundation + Site Platform: pier footings or shallow pads, raised platform plinth, entry steps
2. Structural Skeleton: primary columns, ring beams, bracing frames, cantilever supports
3. Interior Core + Stair: stair volume, service core, minimal built-ins for scale
4. Ground Floor Plate: structural deck, edge beams, terrace connection
5. Facade/Envelope Panels: large sliding glass panels, solid insulated wall panels, optional sun-shading screens—explode outward symmetrically left and right
6. Upper Partitions: bedroom panels, bathroom core, corridor/guardrail panels—float outward to show assembly
7. Upper Floor Plate: structural deck with beams and joists, cutout opening for double-height space
8. Roof Assembly: pitched or mono-slope roof plane, deck panels, thin overhang edges, ridge beams

## Architectural Logic
{{spatial-program}}

## Drawing Style
Precise CAD-like linework: structure lines slightly heavier, envelope medium, partitions light, dashed vertical guide lines connecting layers. Single ink color ({{ink-color}}) on pure white background. Subtle transparency for glazing only. Thin dashed alignment guides with small numeric labels (1–8) near each layer.

## Composition
Vertical exploded stack with generous whitespace, clean separation distances, no cluttered annotations. 1:1 aspect ratio, publication-ready quality.

## Exclude
Perspective view, photoreal rendering, material textures, heavy shadows, dramatic lighting, hand-drawn sketchiness, messy linework, text paragraphs, logos, watermarks.
```
