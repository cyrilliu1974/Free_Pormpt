# News Backgrounder Generator for Journalism

## 簡介

The News Backgrounder Generator for Journalism is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive contextual briefings for journalists preparing to cover breaking or ongoing news stories. This news backgrounder prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to compile a chronological timeline of key events, identify central figures and their roles, analyze the topic's impact across multiple domains, and distill the essential context into a concise summary. Journalists provide a topic variable, and the prompt structures the output into four clear sections: a key events timeline with dates, a list of central figures with their relevance, an impact-and-implications breakdown by affected area, and a summary statement explaining why the background matters for upcoming coverage. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to brief your newsroom, prepare reporters for live coverage, or build contextual sidebars that accompany feature articles. ● Builds a chronological timeline of 3–5 significant events to anchor reader understanding. ● Identifies central figures by name and relevance so audiences know who drives the story. ● Breaks down impact and implications across affected sectors, communities, or policy areas. ● Closes with a 1–2 sentence summary that explains why the background is essential for interpreting the news. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert journalist creating a comprehensive backgrounder that provides essential context for upcoming news stories.

## Task
Produce a structured backgrounder on {{topic}} that equips readers with the foundational knowledge needed to fully understand forthcoming news coverage. Organize information chronologically and thematically to highlight crucial contextual elements.

## Output
Deliver the backgrounder in this format:

**Background on {{topic}}**

**Key Events Timeline:**
• [Year]: [Significant event]
• [Year]: [Significant event]
• [Year]: [Significant event]

**Central Figures:**
• [Name], [Role/Relevance]
• [Name], [Role/Relevance]
• [Name], [Role/Relevance]

**Impact and Implications:**
• [Area of Impact]: [Brief explanation of effect]
• [Area of Impact]: [Brief explanation of effect]
• [Area of Impact]: [Brief explanation of effect]

**In Summary:** [1-2 sentence distillation of the essential context and why it matters for understanding the forthcoming news]

## Guidelines
- Cover all essential aspects comprehensively: relevant historical information, key events, important people or entities, and broader significance
- Structure information in a clear, logical flow that builds understanding progressively
- Focus on what is directly relevant to upcoming news; omit tangential details
- Include 3-5 items per section depending on topic complexity
```
