# Cinematic Sci-Fi Concept Art Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Sci-Fi Concept Art Generator is a free AI prompt that produces wide-angle, low-perspective science fiction scenes with dramatic lighting and rich atmospheric detail. This sci-fi concept art prompt for Midjourney creates compositions where your chosen subject stands on an alien landscape, gazing at distant celestial bodies encircled by glowing rings of fire. The prompt applies cinematic techniques including rim lighting, backlighting, swirling dust, and nebula haze across a color palette of deep blues and fiery oranges. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to deliver highly detailed, fantastical imagery in 16:9 aspect ratio. Use it for sci-fi book covers, concept art portfolios, worldbuilding reference, or visual development for games and films. ● Produces wide-angle, low-perspective shots that emphasize scale and otherworldly terrain ● Applies dramatic rim and backlighting for cinematic mood and subject separation ● Incorporates atmospheric effects like dust, nebula haze, and celestial phenomena with glowing rings ● Delivers rich color palettes balancing deep blues with fiery oranges for visual contrast ## Prompt
