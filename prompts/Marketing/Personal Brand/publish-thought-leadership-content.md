# Thought Leadership Content Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Thought Leadership Content Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured content strategies for solopreneurs building credibility and demonstrating expertise through thought leadership. This thought leadership prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's pain points and industry trends, then produces a complete content plan with strategic themes, specific topic ideas, optimal formats, and credibility elements like case studies and personal stories. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need to establish authority in your field but aren't sure which topics will resonate, what angles differentiate you, or how to translate your experience into compelling content. ● Maps your specific expertise areas directly to your audience's pain points and challenges ● Identifies 3-5 key industry trends where you can offer differentiated insights and unique perspectives ● Recommends specific topics with working titles, optimal formats, and the best platform for each piece ● Extracts credibility elements from your experience - personal stories, case studies, and data points - to weave into content ● Structures all recommendations in clear hierarchies with main insights, supporting evidence, and actionable takeaways ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in thought leadership and personal brand development for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a structured thought leadership content plan that establishes credibility, demonstrates expertise, and provides tangible value to the target audience.

## Context
**Audience & Market:**
{{audience-and-market}}
(Describe your target audience, their needs and pain points, your industry, and relevant trends or challenges)

**Your Position:**
{{expertise-and-value}}
(Describe your area of expertise, unique value proposition, and the specific content goals you want to achieve)

## Analysis & Development
1. Map audience pain points to your expertise areas
2. Identify 3-5 key industry trends where you can offer differentiated insights
3. Extract lessons from your own experience that illustrate these insights
4. Determine which content formats best suit each platform you'll use

## Output
Deliver a content plan with:

### Strategic Foundation
- Core themes aligned with audience needs
- Your unique angle on each theme
- Evidence points (case studies, data, personal stories)

### Content Recommendations
- Specific topic ideas with working titles
- Optimal format and platform for each piece
- Key takeaways that demonstrate your expertise

### Credibility Elements
- Personal experiences to weave in
- Data or case studies to reference
- Actionable insights that provide immediate value

Structure all recommendations using clear hierarchies: main insights supported by evidence and examples, with actionable takeaways as end nodes.
```
