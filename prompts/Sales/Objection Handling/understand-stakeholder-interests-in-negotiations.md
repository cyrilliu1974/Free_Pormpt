# Stakeholder Analysis for Sales Negotiations

## 簡介

The Stakeholder Analysis for Sales Negotiations is a free AI prompt that identifies each party's interests and motivations in complex sales deals, then generates tailored engagement strategies for negotiators and sales professionals. This stakeholder analysis prompt for ChatGPT asks the model to adopt the role of an expert negotiation strategist and produce a four-column markdown table covering every key player: their explicit interests (desired outcomes, terms, conditions), their underlying motivations (business drivers, personal goals, organizational pressures, risk factors), and a concise strategic recommendation for engaging that stakeholder. After the table, the prompt generates a synthesis paragraph highlighting conflicting interests or alignment opportunities across the group. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for sales leaders, account executives, procurement negotiators, and deal teams preparing for multi-party discussions. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to map the political and business landscape before a negotiation, especially when multiple decision-makers or influencers are involved. ● Uncovers implicit motivations and organizational pressures that shape each stakeholder's position, not just the demands they state publicly. ● Delivers a structured markdown table that sales teams can share, annotate, and reference throughout the negotiation cycle. ● Produces a cross-stakeholder synthesis identifying where interests conflict and where natural alliances or trade-offs exist. ● Accepts a single variable - your sales negotiation context - so you can run the analysis with minimal setup and immediately apply the strategic recommendations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert negotiation strategist specializing in stakeholder analysis for complex sales negotiations.

## Task
Analyze all stakeholders involved in the negotiation, uncovering their interests (what they want) and motivations (why they want it). Consider both explicit stated positions and implicit underlying factors. Based on this analysis, provide tailored strategic recommendations for engaging each stakeholder.

## Context
{{sales-negotiation-context}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with four columns:

| Stakeholder | Interests | Motivations | Strategic Recommendation |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|-------------------------|

Include all key stakeholders identified in the context. For each, provide:
- **Interests**: What they seek from this negotiation (outcomes, terms, conditions)
- **Motivations**: Why they hold these interests (business drivers, personal goals, organizational pressures, risk factors)
- **Strategic Recommendation**: A concise approach for engaging this stakeholder effectively

After the table, provide a brief synthesis highlighting any conflicting interests or alignment opportunities across stakeholders.
```
