# Editorial Product Photoshoot With Model Prompt

## 簡介

The Editorial Product Photoshoot With Model Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates high-end studio fashion photography staging a model with your product in a coordinated, professionally lit scene. This editorial product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models produces 8k ultra-high-resolution imagery with a female model in a pose that highlights the product - lying on her stomach, elbows propped, holding the item naturally near her face. The prompt specifies tight wardrobe coordination (a form-fitting bodysuit that exactly matches your product color), seamless backdrop tones, softbox studio lighting, 50mm f/2.8 shallow depth-of-field camera settings, and realistic product detail including logos, proportions, and materials. Use it to create hero imagery for e-commerce, social media campaigns, or pitch decks when you need commercial-grade product shots without a physical photoshoot. Reach for this prompt when you want catalog-ready editorial photography that pairs lifestyle appeal with clear product visibility - ideal for beverage containers, beauty tools, accessories, or any handheld consumer goods. ● Specifies exact pose, grip, and eye-line so the product remains unobstructed and naturally integrated into the composition ● Enforces color coordination between model wardrobe, product finish, and studio backdrop for cohesive brand aesthetics ● Includes technical camera parameters (50mm prime, f/2.8, shallow DOF) and three-point studio lighting setup for realism ● Calls out common AI image pitfalls to avoid - distorted anatomy, cartoon skin, wrong logo placement, color mismatches, extra fingers ## Prompt

```
Ultra photorealistic studio fashion editorial product photography, 8k ultra high resolution, extremely sharp detail.

Wearing form-fitting one-piece bodysuit in {{product-color}} matte stretch fabric, matching the product exactly. No glasses, no jewelry. Model holding {{product}} {{variant}} with both hands, straw near lips, natural ergonomic grip, product clearly visible and unobstructed.

Product: {{product}} {{variant}} in {{product-color}}, powder-coated stainless steel with visible brand logo at accurate placement and scale, realistic lid, straw, and handle proportions.

Background: {{product-color}} toned smooth seamless paper backdrop, minimal clean fashion editorial style.

Lighting: Soft studio setup with large softbox key light from front-left, gentle fill from right, soft subtle shadows, natural even skin tone.

Camera: 50mm prime lens, f/2.8, slightly elevated eye-level angle, shallow depth of field with sharp subject and background separation.

Style: High-end commercial fashion editorial, natural skin texture, realistic fabric folds, accurate product proportions, clean minimal modern aesthetic.

Avoid: glasses, sunglasses, color mismatch between outfit and product, incorrect logo, distorted anatomy, plastic skin, cartoon style, low resolution, blur, oversaturation, harsh shadows, extra fingers, warped product proportions.
```
