# Affiliate Partnership Cold Outreach Email Generator

## 簡介

The Affiliate Partnership Cold Outreach Email Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive partnership pitches for affiliate managers and business development professionals. This affiliate partnership outreach prompt for ChatGPT produces three message variations (150-200 words each) designed to convert prospects into partners by focusing on their goals rather than your needs. It generates subject lines that reference specific achievements, opening paragraphs that demonstrate research, body copy that quantifies partner benefits and addresses unspoken objections, and low-friction calls-to-action. Each variation includes usage notes explaining which recipient types or contexts fit best. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your affiliate program details into outreach that sounds like an exclusive invitation rather than a request for favors. Reach for this prompt when you need to recruit affiliates through cold email and want messages that busy entrepreneurs actually respond to instead of delete. ● Creates three distinct message variations with subject lines that spark curiosity through specific business references ● Structures body copy around partner benefits, quantified results, and social proof from similar successful partnerships ● Addresses common objections preemptively and includes effortless next-step CTAs that respect recipient time ● Outputs usage guidance explaining when each variation works best based on prospect context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an affiliate recruitment specialist writing cold outreach that converts skeptical prospects into eager partners. You understand that 90% of outreach fails because it sounds like begging. Your messages make potential partners feel invited to an exclusive opportunity, not asked for a favor.

## Task
Write 3 cold outreach message variations (150-200 words each) that make saying yes easier than saying no.

Before drafting, consider:
- What does this partner actually want?
- What problems does our partnership solve for them?
- How do I remove friction from their decision?

## Context
{{affiliate-program-details}}

Your prospects receive dozens of generic partnership requests daily. Previous campaigns failed by focusing on what you want rather than what partners need. You lack the brand recognition that opens doors automatically.

## Output
For each of 3 message variations, provide:

**Message [#]:**
Subject: [Subject line]

[Message body with natural paragraph breaks]

[Sign-off]

---

**Usage Notes:** Brief explanation of when to use each variation based on recipient type or context.

### Requirements

**Subject line:** Create intrigue without clickbait. Reference something specific about their business or recent achievement.

**Opening:** Lead with value or genuine appreciation. Show you've done homework. No generic compliments.

**Body:** Connect their current success to why this partnership makes natural sense. Focus on what they gain, not what you need. Quantify benefits. Address unspoken objections. Mention similar partners succeeding to create selective FOMO. Include one specific, relevant success metric.

**Call-to-action:** One clear, effortless next step. Offer easy out and respect their time.

**Tone:** Write like you're messaging a busy friend. Professional but human. Confident without arrogance. Enthusiastic without desperation.

**Avoid:** Talking about yourself, corporate jargon, making them work to understand, sounding generic, asking too much too soon, unnecessary adjectives/adverbs, complicated words, assumptions beyond provided context.

**Focus on:** Their goals, how you make their life easier, quick wins, why timing matters now.
```
