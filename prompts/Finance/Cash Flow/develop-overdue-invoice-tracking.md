# Overdue Invoice Tracking System Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Overdue Invoice Tracking System Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a complete receivables management framework integrated with real-time cash flow forecasting for growing businesses. This overdue invoice tracking prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured implementation guide that transforms reactive invoice collection into proactive cash flow intelligence. It designs multi-tiered categorization by risk level and payment history, automated escalation workflows based on aging periods, and probability-weighted collection scenarios that feed directly into cash projections. Financial operations teams use it to replace manual spreadsheets with systematic tracking that connects receivables data to strategic cash planning. It is built for finance managers, operations consultants, and business owners who need to convert unpredictable collections into forecasted cash positions with measurable outcomes and clear timelines. ● Categorizes overdue invoices by risk tier, customer payment history, and relationship value to prioritize collection efforts. ● Automates escalation workflows and early warning signals based on invoice aging periods and customer behavior patterns. ● Integrates real-time receivables data into cash flow projections with scenario planning for varying collection success rates. ● Delivers dashboards, contingency frameworks, resource allocation guidance, and measurable success metrics for each implementation phase. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a financial operations consultant specializing in receivables management and cash flow forecasting for growing businesses.

## Task

Design a comprehensive overdue invoice tracking system integrated with cash flow projections. Deliver a structured implementation guide with clear phases, actionable steps, specific timelines, and measurable outcomes.

## Context

The business currently operates with:

{{business-operations}}

Cash flow visibility is critical to survival. Traditional accounting methods fail to capture real-time money movement, and every day of collection delay creates strategic disadvantage. The solution must transform overdue invoices from reactive fire-fighting into proactive cash flow intelligence.

## Requirements

**Tracking System Design**
- Multi-tiered categorization by risk level, payment history, and customer relationship value
- Automated escalation workflows based on aging periods and customer profiles
- Pattern recognition for customer payment behavior
- Early warning signals for collection issues

**Cash Flow Integration**
- Real-time receivables data feeding into cash flow models
- Probability-weighted collection scenarios for different outcomes
- Scenario planning capabilities based on collection success rates
- Direct connection between invoice aging data and cash projections

**Reporting & Intelligence**
- Dashboards showing both operational collection metrics and strategic cash flow insights
- Contingency planning frameworks for varying collection rates
- Impact analysis on projected cash positions

## Output

Structure your implementation guide in clear phases with:
- Actionable bullet-point steps for each phase
- Specific timelines for implementation
- Measurable outcomes and success metrics
- Integration protocols suited to the existing systems described
- Resource allocation recommendations based on available team capacity
```
