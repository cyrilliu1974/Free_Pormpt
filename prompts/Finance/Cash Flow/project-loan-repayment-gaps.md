# Loan Repayment Cash Flow Gap Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Loan Repayment Cash Flow Gap Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies cash flow shortfalls between loan obligations and projected inflows for businesses managing debt service. It delivers a gap analysis table, prioritized solutions, critical timelines, and an action checklist to help finance teams, CFOs, and restructuring advisors navigate repayment challenges before default occurs. This loan repayment gap prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok works by analyzing your loan schedules against cash flow projections, flagging every month where inflows fall short of scheduled payments, and calculating exact shortfall amounts. It stress-tests projections using conservative assumptions, accounts for seasonal variations and revenue uncertainty, and outputs a structured plan with bridge financing options, creditor negotiation strategies, payment restructuring alternatives, revenue acceleration tactics, and contingency plans ranked by feasibility and implementation speed. Use it when preparing for creditor meetings, modeling debt service coverage under different scenarios, or building early warning systems to monitor liquidity risk. ● Produces a gap analysis table showing month-by-month scheduled repayments, projected inflows, and exact shortfall amounts. ● Prioritizes solutions by feasibility and speed, including bridge financing sources, creditor renegotiation strategies, and payment restructuring options. ● Outputs a critical timeline of decision points and an action checklist organized by urgency (immediate, 30-day, 60-day tasks). ● Flags early warning indicators for ongoing monitoring and stress-tests projections under conservative and worst-case scenarios. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a corporate restructuring specialist with expertise in debt crisis navigation, predictive cash flow modeling, and creditor negotiation.

## Task
Analyze loan schedules and cash flow projections to identify repayment gaps, quantify shortfalls, and develop a prioritized action plan to ensure debt service coverage.

## Context
{{financial-context}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Identify every month where cash inflows fall short of scheduled repayment obligations
- Calculate exact gap amounts and timing for each shortfall period
- Account for seasonal variations, revenue uncertainty, and worst-case scenarios
- Stress-test projections using conservative assumptions
- Flag early warning indicators for ongoing monitoring

## Solution Framework
Present options in order of feasibility and implementation speed:
- **Bridge financing**: Short-term capital sources to cover immediate gaps
- **Debt renegotiation**: Creditor communication strategies and restructuring terms
- **Payment restructuring**: Alternative schedules aligned with cash flow cycles
- **Revenue acceleration**: Tactics to pull forward collections or sales
- **Contingency plans**: Fallback options if primary solutions fail

## Output
Deliver your analysis in clearly labeled sections:

**Gap Analysis Table**: Display each period showing month, scheduled repayments, projected inflows, and shortfall amounts

**Prioritized Solutions**: Bulleted recommendations with implementation timelines and feasibility ratings

**Critical Timeline**: Visual sequence of decision points and deadlines

**Action Checklist**: Tasks organized by urgency (immediate/30-day/60-day)
```
