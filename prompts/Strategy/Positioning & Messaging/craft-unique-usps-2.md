# Unique Selling Proposition Generator for Brands

## 簡介

The Unique Selling Proposition Generator for Brands is a free AI prompt that develops differentiated USPs for marketing strategists and brand managers. This unique selling proposition prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand, market context, and target audience to produce a markdown table with three columns: Features, Benefits, and Unique Value. It works by identifying core differentiators, articulating customer benefits, and explaining why each combination matters to your specific audience. Use it when launching a new brand, repositioning against competitors, or clarifying your value proposition for sales and marketing materials. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Delivers a markdown table mapping features to benefits to unique value for each USP ● Ensures propositions are compelling, relevant, defensible, and aligned with brand positioning ● Incorporates target audience pain points, demographics, and purchase motivations into the analysis ● Produces output that can be used directly in pitch decks, marketing plans, and sales enablement materials ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in competitive differentiation and value proposition development.

## Task
Develop a set of unique selling propositions (USPs) that differentiate the brand from its competitors and articulate its core strengths. Deliver the analysis as a structured markdown table.

## Context
**Brand & Market:**
{{brand-and-market-context}}
(Include: brand name, industry, key competitors, and current brand positioning)

**Target Audience:**
{{target-audience}}
(Describe demographics, psychographics, pain points, and purchase motivations)

## Analysis Framework
For each USP:
1. Identify a core feature that distinguishes this brand from competitors
2. Articulate the concrete benefit that feature delivers to customers
3. Explain the unique value—why this combination matters specifically to the target audience and cannot be easily replicated

## Output
Present 3–5 USPs in a markdown table with three columns:

| Features | Benefits | Unique Value |
|----------|----------|-------------|

Ensure each USP is:
- **Compelling**: Emotionally resonant and rationally defensible
- **Relevant**: Directly addresses target audience needs
- **Defensible**: Rooted in genuine brand capabilities
- **Aligned**: Consistent with the stated brand positioning strategy
```
