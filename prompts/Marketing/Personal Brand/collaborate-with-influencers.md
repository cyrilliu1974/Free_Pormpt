# Influencer Collaboration Strategy Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Influencer Collaboration Strategy Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates actionable partnership roadmaps for solopreneurs, coaches, and content creators building personal brand authority. This influencer collaboration prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section strategy covering influencer profiling, collaboration formats (guest posts, webinars, podcast interviews, social media takeovers), tailored content ideas, personalized outreach sequences, and measurable KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, mapping every recommendation back to your niche expertise, target audience, and brand values. Use it when you need a clear framework to connect with aligned voices in your industry and co-create content that builds credibility without guesswork. ● Profiles 3–5 influencers by reach, audience overlap, content style, and collaboration history ● Outlines 4–6 partnership formats - from guest posts and co-hosted webinars to video collaborations and joint resources ● Develops 5–7 content ideas that showcase your expertise while serving both audiences ● Details a research-backed outreach strategy with personalized pitch structure, value propositions, and follow-up cadence ● Defines KPIs for engagement, audience growth, referral traffic, lead generation, and brand sentiment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in influencer collaboration and personal brand development.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive influencer collaboration plan that showcases expertise and builds personal brand authority. The plan must be structured, actionable, and aligned with brand values.

## Context
**Niche & Expertise:** {{niche-and-expertise}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Brand Values & Goals:** {{brand-values-and-goals}}

## Approach
Create the plan with clear hierarchical relationships between strategy components:

1. **Influencer Identification** – Profile 3-5 suitable influencers (reach, audience overlap, content style, collaboration history)
2. **Collaboration Formats** – Outline 4-6 viable formats (guest posts, co-hosted webinars, podcast interviews, social media takeovers, joint resources, video collaborations)
3. **Content Ideas** – Develop 5-7 concepts that demonstrate expertise while serving both audiences
4. **Outreach Strategy** – Detail the approach (research phase, personalized pitch structure, value proposition, follow-up cadence)
5. **Success Metrics** – Define KPIs for each collaboration type (engagement rates, audience growth, referral traffic, lead generation, brand sentiment)

## Output
Deliver the plan using:
- **Main headings** for each strategic component
- **Subheadings** for categories within components
- **Bullet points** for actionable steps, specific ideas, and tactical details

Ensure every recommendation connects logically to the stated brand values and audience needs.
```
