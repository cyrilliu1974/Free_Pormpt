# Study Summary Generator for Students

## 簡介

The Study Summary Generator for Students is a free AI prompt that distills complex academic material into clear, structured summaries tailored to specific grade levels and learning objectives. This study summary prompt for ChatGPT transforms dense textbook chapters, lecture notes, and educational content into concise bullet-point lists using proven learning techniques like chunking, analogies, and real-world examples. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing your subject matter to identify key concepts and themes, then presenting them in grade-appropriate language that builds understanding logically. Teachers use it to prepare review materials before exams, students use it to consolidate notes, and tutors use it to create focused study aids that match their learners' comprehension levels. This prompt is for educators, students, and instructional designers who need to make academic content more accessible without losing accuracy or depth. ● Accepts subject matter, grade level, and learning objectives as variables to tailor every summary ● Structures output as bullet points that are concise, logically sequenced, and free of extraneous detail ● Applies chunking and analogy techniques to make difficult concepts easier to grasp ● Ensures language and examples remain appropriate for the specified grade level ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content creator specializing in academic summaries that enhance comprehension and retention.

## Task
Distill complex academic material into a clear, structured summary tailored to the specified grade level. Identify key concepts and themes, then present them using effective learning techniques: chunking information into manageable pieces, employing analogies, and providing real-world examples where relevant.

## Context
**Subject & Material:** {{subject-and-material}}

**Grade Level:** {{grade-level}}

**Learning Objectives:** {{learning-objectives}}

## Output
Deliver the summary as a bullet-point list. Each point should be:
- Concise and grade-appropriate in language
- Clearly connected to the learning objectives
- Logically sequenced to build understanding
- Relevant to the core concepts without extraneous detail
```
