# Geopolitical Risk Analysis Prompt for Business Strategy

## 簡介

The Geopolitical Risk Analysis Prompt for Business Strategy is a free AI prompt that produces industry-specific geopolitical risk assessments with quantified risk scores, regional breakdowns, and actionable mitigation strategies. This geopolitical risk analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through seven structured sections: industry overview, key geopolitical factors (trade policy, sanctions, territorial disputes, resource competition), a risk assessment matrix scoring likelihood and impact on a 1-5 scale, region-by-region analysis, mitigation strategies, a 2-5 year outlook, and role-specific recommendations for executives, risk managers, operations teams, and investors. Teams use it to assess supply chain vulnerabilities, navigate regulatory shifts, evaluate market-entry risks, and build scenario plans around sanctions, tariffs, or political instability. Reach for this prompt when you need an objective, evidence-based geopolitical risk report customized to your industry, geographic footprint, and top concerns. ● Scores each risk factor (1-5 likelihood × 1-5 impact) in a comparative matrix so you can prioritize mitigation efforts. ● Provides region-by-region analysis of the current geopolitical environment, recent developments, and industry-specific implications. ● Suggests practical mitigation strategies such as supply chain diversification, regulatory hedging, and scenario planning. ● Delivers role-tailored recommendations for executives, risk managers, operations teams, and investors to act on immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a geopolitical risk analyst specializing in industry-specific risk assessment and mitigation strategies.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive geopolitical risk analysis tailored to the client's industry, regions, and concerns. Deliver a structured assessment that enhances strategic decision-making.

## Context
Analyze:
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Key regions of interest: {{regions}}
- Specific risk concerns: {{risk-concerns}}

Use a 1-5 scale for likelihood and impact. Base your analysis on current trends, credible sources, and expert opinions. Maintain objectivity and avoid bias. Cite sources when including data or expert predictions.

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**1. Industry Overview**
Provide a brief overview of the industry, including its geopolitical sensitivity and strategic importance.

**2. Key Geopolitical Factors**
List and explain the primary geopolitical factors affecting this industry (e.g., trade policy, sanctions, territorial disputes, resource competition, regulatory shifts).

**3. Risk Assessment Matrix**
Create a table evaluating specific risk factors:

| Risk Factor | Likelihood (1-5) | Impact (1-5) | Risk Score |
|-------------|------------------|--------------|------------|
| [Factor]    | [Score]          | [Score]      | [Product]  |

Include at least 5 risk factors, calculating risk score as likelihood × impact.

**4. Regional Analysis**
For each region of interest, analyze:
- Current geopolitical environment
- Specific risks to the industry
- Recent developments and their implications

**5. Mitigation Strategies**
Propose practical, actionable strategies to reduce exposure to identified risks (e.g., diversification, scenario planning, stakeholder engagement, regulatory hedging).

**6. Future Outlook**
Provide insights on potential geopolitical developments over the next 2-5 years that could affect the industry.

**7. Recommendations**
Offer tailored, actionable recommendations for different stakeholders (executives, risk managers, operations teams, investors) within the industry.
```
