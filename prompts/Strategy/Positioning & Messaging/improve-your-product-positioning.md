# Product Positioning Transformation Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Product Positioning Transformation Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that helps marketers and founders reframe their offerings around the customer journey from problem to desired outcome. This product positioning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business and audience, then delivers a structured Point A to Point B framework that shows the struggle customers want to escape, the transformation they're buying, and the rational, emotional, and social benefits they receive. Instead of listing features, it maps the actual job-to-be-done your product accomplishes. The output is a markdown-formatted analytical report ready to inform landing pages, sales decks, email campaigns, and messaging guides. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing strategists, product managers, founders, and copywriters use it when repositioning an offering, launching a new product, or clarifying messaging that feels too feature-focused. ● Maps the customer's current struggle and negative consequences alongside the desired state and multi-layered benefits ● Structures positioning as a clear before-and-after story that sales and marketing teams can immediately use ● Avoids hype and vague claims in favor of specific, balanced, analytical language ● Outputs a markdown report that serves as the foundation for landing pages, ad copy, and pitch decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in transformation-based positioning for products and services.

## Task
Analyze the provided business and develop a transformation framework that repositions the offering from features to customer outcomes. Structure the transformation as a clear before-and-after narrative that shows the journey from problem state (Point A) to desired state (Point B).

## Context
Effective positioning sells the transition customers buy, not the mechanics of how the product works. The framework must be:
- Specific and concise, avoiding vague or wordy claims
- Balanced in tone—persuasive but not aggressive or hyperbolic
- Immediately usable in marketing copy without requiring interpretation
- Presented as an analytical report, not as sales pitch

**Business and audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

## Transformation Framework Structure

**Point A (Current Situation):**
- Main struggle: the core problem customers want to escape
- Negative consequences: what happens if they don't solve it

**Point B (Desired Situation):**
- The change: the job-to-be-done customers are hiring the product to accomplish
- Rational benefits: tangible, measurable results
- Emotional benefits: how customers will feel
- Social benefits: how others will perceive them

## Output
Deliver the transformation framework as a structured analytical report using markdown formatting. Present Point A and Point B with their components clearly labeled. Keep language accessible to average users.
```
