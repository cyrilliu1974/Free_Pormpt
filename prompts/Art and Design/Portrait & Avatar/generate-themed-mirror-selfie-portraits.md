# Themed Mirror Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Themed Mirror Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed mirror selfie portraits with themed styling, retail backdrops, and authentic smartphone photography composition for content creators and designers. This mirror selfie prompt for Midjourney produces 8k-resolution images with customizable outfit details and store environments, capturing the look of real in-store smartphone photography with wide-angle perspective, lens flare effects, and cool-toned lighting. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar AI image generators, specifying character styling (hair, makeup, pose), attire with accessories, themed retail settings, and professional lighting setups that simulate softbox and backlight conditions. Use it for social media content, fashion lookbooks, character design references, or marketing visuals that require authentic selfie-style imagery without a physical shoot. ● Customize two key variables: outfit-details (clothing, accessories, color palette) and store-environment (retail theme, props, background merchandise). ● Generates images with authentic mirror reflection composition, lens characteristics (24mm wide-angle feel, f/1.8 aperture simulation), and texture-rich details on fabrics and surfaces. ● Produces cool-toned, high-key lighting with sparkle effects, bokeh, and star-filter lens flares that mimic real sequin and glitter reflections. ● Includes detailed prompting for character features, pose, makeup, accessories, foreground/background layers, and color grading to ensure consistent output quality. ## Prompt
