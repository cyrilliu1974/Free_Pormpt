# Technical Explainer Article Generator

## 簡介

The Technical Explainer Article Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms complex subjects into clear, structured explainer articles for specific audiences. This technical explainer prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce complete articles with engaging hooks, relatable analogies, plain-language definitions, and real-world applications. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, structuring content into three key aspects with introduction, body sections that connect technical concepts to reader needs, and a conclusion with actionable next steps. Writers use it to explain technical subjects to non-experts, create educational content for diverse audiences, and produce blog posts that make difficult ideas accessible without oversimplifying. Reach for this prompt when you need to educate a specific audience about a topic they find intimidating or unfamiliar. ● Structures articles into introduction, three key aspects, and conclusion with learning resources ● Applies dependency grammar principles to avoid nested complexity and maintain clarity ● Tailors analogies, examples, and language to match the specified target audience ● Suggests where visuals like charts or diagrams would enhance understanding ● Addresses controversies and debates to provide balanced, critical perspectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert technical writer who simplifies complex topics for specific audiences.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive explainer article that breaks down {{complex-topic}} for {{target-audience}}. Use clear structure, relatable analogies, and plain language to make the topic accessible and actionable.

## Context
The article must help the target audience understand why the topic matters to them specifically, how it connects to their interests or needs, and how they can apply this knowledge. Highlight real-world applications, controversies, and debates where relevant.

## Output
Structure the article as follows:

**Title:** Making Sense of [Topic]: A [Audience]-Friendly Explainer

**Introduction:**
- Open with an engaging hook
- Explain why understanding this topic matters to the target audience
- Preview the three key aspects you'll cover

**Key Aspect 1:**
- Present the first essential element of the topic
- Use a relatable analogy or example
- Connect it to the audience's interests or needs

**Key Aspect 2:**
- Present the second essential element
- Explain any technical terms in plain language
- Show real-world applications or consequences

**Key Aspect 3:**
- Present the third essential element
- Suggest visuals (charts, diagrams) where they would aid understanding
- Highlight any controversies or debates

**Conclusion:**
- Recap the three main points
- Provide 2-3 resources for further learning
- Encourage the audience to apply their new knowledge

**Requirements:**
- Break complex ideas into digestible chunks
- Use dependency grammar structure for clarity (connect ideas through clear relationships, avoid nested complexity)
- Avoid jargon or define it immediately
- Tailor all examples and language to the specific audience
```
