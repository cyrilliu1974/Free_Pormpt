# Balanced Meal Recipe Generator From Ingredients

## 簡介

The Balanced Meal Recipe Generator From Ingredients is a free AI prompt that transforms any ingredient list into a complete, healthy recipe with detailed nutrition information and easy-to-follow cooking steps. This balanced meal recipe prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your supplied ingredients and designing a recipe optimized for nutritional balance and simplicity. It generates structured output including measured ingredient quantities, preparation and cooking times, step-by-step instructions, and comprehensive nutrition facts per serving. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for your preferred text AI platform. Use it when you have groceries on hand and need meal inspiration, want to reduce food waste, or need to plan healthy dishes that fit dietary goals. ● Converts any ingredient list into a fully formatted recipe with measurements, timing, and nutritional breakdown ● Optimizes for nutritional balance while keeping preparation simple and accessible ● Includes detailed nutrition facts per serving (calories, macros, fat types, cholesterol, sodium, fiber, sugars) ● Provides clear step-by-step instructions that anyone can follow, regardless of cooking experience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert meal planner specializing in healthy, balanced, and easy-to-prepare recipes.

## Task
Create a nutritious and delicious recipe using only the ingredients provided. Optimize for nutritional balance and simplicity of preparation.

## Context
The recipe must:
- Use only the ingredients from the supplied list
- Focus on balanced nutrition and ease of cooking
- Include clear, step-by-step instructions anyone can follow

## Input
{{ingredient-list}}

## Output
Provide the complete recipe in this format:

**[Recipe Name]**

**Ingredients:**
- [List each ingredient with measurements]

**Timing:**
- Preparation Time: [time]
- Cooking Time: [time]
- Total Time: [time]
- Servings: [number]

**Instructions:**
1. [Detailed step-by-step directions]
2. [Continue for all steps]

**Nutrition Facts (per serving):**
- Calories: [amount]
- Total Fat: [amount]
- Saturated Fat: [amount]
- Trans Fat: [amount]
- Cholesterol: [amount]
- Sodium: [amount]
- Total Carbohydrate: [amount]
- Dietary Fiber: [amount]
- Total Sugars: [amount]
- Protein: [amount]
```
