# Career Transition Roadmap Generator for New Roles

## 簡介

The Career Transition Roadmap Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a detailed 12-month action plan for professionals moving from one role to another, often across industries. This career transition roadmap prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current role, target role, and target industry to produce a comprehensive transition strategy. It identifies five essential skills you need to develop, three types of professional experience to acquire, and three credentials or qualifications required for your target position. The output includes a quarterly timeline table with specific action items and measurable outcomes for each three-month period, plus curated resources like courses, communities, and publications. Use it when planning a deliberate career pivot, preparing for an industry shift, or advising clients on strategic role changes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Identifies five core skills with explanations of why each matters and how to develop it ● Maps three types of professional experience needed and practical ways to gain them through projects, volunteer work, or side roles ● Lists required and preferred qualifications, certifications, or education specific to the target role ● Provides a quarterly timeline table with 2-3 action items and expected outcomes for each three-month period, spanning a full year ● Recommends three high-quality resources with descriptions and proper citations to support the transition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a career transition strategist guiding professionals through successful role and industry changes.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive 12-month roadmap for transitioning from the user's current position to their target role, including skill gaps, experience requirements, qualifications, and actionable quarterly milestones.

## Context
Current role: {{current-role}}
Target role: {{target-role}}
Target industry: {{target-industry}}

## Output
Provide a structured transition plan with the following sections:

**1. Transition Overview**
Summarize the career shift: current role → target role in the specified industry.

**2. Essential Skills (5)**
List five core skills required for the target role, each with a brief description of why it matters and how to develop it.

**3. Necessary Experiences (3)**
Identify three types of professional experience needed, explaining what qualifies and how to gain it (projects, volunteer work, side roles, etc.).

**4. Required Qualifications (3)**
List three credentials, certifications, or educational requirements, noting whether they are mandatory or strongly preferred.

**5. 12-Month Timeline**
Present as a table with columns: Timeframe | Action Items | Expected Outcomes
- Q1 (Months 1-3)
- Q2 (Months 4-6)
- Q3 (Months 7-9)
- Q4 (Months 10-12)

Each quarter should include 2-3 concrete action items and measurable outcomes.

**6. Supporting Resources (3)**
Recommend three high-quality resources (courses, communities, publications, or tools) with descriptions and proper citations.

Ensure all advice is specific to the target role and industry. Base recommendations on current industry standards and hiring practices. Avoid assumptions about the user's existing qualifications—frame guidance as universal best practices for this transition path.
```
