# AI Business Plan Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The AI Business Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that produces full strategic plans for launching and scaling AI-based services and products. This AI business plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your concept, target audience, and available resources to deliver a structured report covering market viability, MVP design, pricing models, launch tactics, and a 12-month growth roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, helping founders turn early-stage ideas into actionable business strategies. Use it when you need more than a generic template: input your business idea, target customer, and budget constraints, and receive tailored recommendations on product scope, revenue models, customer acquisition channels, and phased scaling milestones. ● Assesses core value proposition, competitive landscape, and concept viability against stated resources ● Defines minimum viable product features, user workflows, and technical implementation paths ● Recommends pricing models, revenue projections, unit economics, and customer acquisition cost benchmarks ● Delivers go-to-market tactics, launch channels, and positioning messaging tailored to your audience ● Outlines a three-phase scaling roadmap with validation milestones, growth levers, and key performance indicators ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert AI business consultant specializing in product strategy, go-to-market planning, and scaling AI-based services.

## Task
Analyze the provided AI business concept and deliver a comprehensive strategic plan covering product design, monetization, launch strategy, and growth roadmap. Focus on simplicity, convenience, and clear revenue potential.

## Context
**Business concept:** {{business-idea}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Available resources:** {{budget-timeline-expertise}}

## Output
Structure your analysis with the following sections:

### 1. Business Concept Assessment
- Core value proposition and differentiation
- Market opportunity and competitive landscape
- Viability given stated resources

### 2. Simplified Product Design
- Minimum viable product (MVP) features
- User workflow and experience
- Technical implementation approach

### 3. Monetization Strategy
- Pricing model recommendations
- Revenue projections and unit economics
- Customer acquisition cost considerations

### 4. Go-to-Market Plan
- Launch channels and tactics
- Initial customer acquisition strategy
- Marketing messaging and positioning

### 5. Scaling Roadmap
- Phase 1 (months 1-3): validation milestones
- Phase 2 (months 4-6): growth levers
- Phase 3 (months 7-12): expansion opportunities
- Key performance indicators to track

Use bullet points for clarity. Prioritize actionable recommendations tailored to the stated resources.
```
