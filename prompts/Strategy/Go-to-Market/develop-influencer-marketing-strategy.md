# Influencer Marketing Strategy Builder for Brands

## 簡介

The Influencer Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven influencer campaign plans for brands looking to increase awareness and drive sales. This influencer marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through the entire campaign lifecycle: identifying 8-12 platform-specific influencers (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn), analyzing engagement rates and audience fit, designing personalized outreach messaging, defining content themes and collaboration types, and establishing KPIs with measurement frameworks. It delivers both a structured recommendation table with estimated costs and a detailed strategic breakdown covering platform rationale, budget allocation, timelines, and risk mitigation. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and you tailor it with four variables: brand name, target audience, industry, and campaign parameters. Marketing teams, brand managers, and growth strategists use this prompt when launching influencer partnerships or expanding existing creator programs. ● Generates a recommendation table of 8-12 vetted influencers with engagement rates, audience fit scores, and cost estimates ● Maps platform strategy to audience behavior across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn ● Provides outreach templates, negotiation guidelines, and content collaboration frameworks ● Defines campaign KPIs, budget splits, timelines, and reporting cadence for measurable ROI ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert influencer marketing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive influencer marketing strategy to increase brand awareness and drive sales for {{brand-name}}.

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Campaign parameters: {{campaign-parameters}}

The strategy must be actionable and data-driven, covering the full campaign lifecycle from influencer discovery through performance measurement.

## Process
1. **Influencer identification**: Research and select 8-12 influencers across relevant platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn) whose audiences align with the target demographic
2. **Engagement analysis**: Evaluate each influencer's engagement rate, audience authenticity, content quality, and brand fit
3. **Platform strategy**: Recommend optimal platform mix based on where the target audience is most active and engaged
4. **Outreach plan**: Design a personalized outreach approach including messaging templates, value propositions, and negotiation guidelines
5. **Content strategy**: Define content themes, formats, posting cadence, brand guidelines, and collaboration types (sponsored posts, product reviews, takeovers, affiliate partnerships)
6. **Performance framework**: Establish KPIs (reach, engagement, conversions, ROI) and measurement methods

## Output
Deliver your strategy in two parts:

**Part 1: Influencer Recommendation Table**

| Influencer Name | Platform | Engagement Rate | Audience Fit | Estimated Cost | Notes |
|-----------------|----------|-----------------|--------------|----------------|-------|

(Include 8-12 rows with specific recommendations)

**Part 2: Comprehensive Strategy**

Provide a detailed bullet-point breakdown covering:
- Platform strategy and rationale
- Outreach and negotiation approach
- Content creation guidelines and campaign themes
- Timeline and campaign phases
- Budget allocation across influencers and content types
- Success metrics and reporting cadence
- Risk mitigation and contingency plans
```
