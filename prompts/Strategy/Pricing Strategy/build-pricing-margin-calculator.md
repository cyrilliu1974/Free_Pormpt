# Pricing Margin Calculator Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Pricing Margin Calculator Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete pricing analysis and calculation system for product-based businesses seeking margin optimization and strategic pricing control. This pricing margin calculator prompt for ChatGPT generates a multi-section framework covering cost analysis, pricing formulas, competitive positioning strategy, dynamic pricing automation recommendations, and risk management guidelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a brief business context into spreadsheet-ready formulas, step-by-step calculation examples, and actionable checklists for implementing automated price adjustments based on cost fluctuations, competitor movements, and demand signals. The output identifies hidden expenses like storage, returns, payment processing, and customer acquisition costs that many businesses overlook when setting prices. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic approach to pricing that balances profitability with competitive positioning, whether you operate in e-commerce, wholesale, or direct-to-consumer channels. ● Identifies all cost components including frequently missed expenses and shows exactly how each impacts final margins. ● Provides clear mathematical formulas for retail, wholesale, and promotional pricing with worked examples ready for spreadsheet implementation. ● Recommends positioning strategy (premium, competitive parity, value) based on competitor analysis and market landscape. ● Delivers automation checklists for dynamic price adjustments triggered by cost changes, competitor moves, inventory levels, and seasonal cycles. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a pricing strategist and financial analyst specializing in margin optimization and dynamic pricing systems for product-based businesses.

## Task
Build a comprehensive pricing margin calculator framework that accounts for all cost variables, competitive positioning, and profit optimization. Provide formulas, strategic insights, and automation recommendations the user can implement immediately.

## Context
Sustainable pricing balances profitability with competitive positioning while allowing flexibility for market changes. Many businesses overlook hidden costs or fail to systematically adjust prices as conditions shift.

**Business details:**
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your calculator framework with:

### 1. Complete Cost Analysis
- Identify all cost components including frequently overlooked expenses (storage, returns, payment processing, customer acquisition)
- Break down how each cost category impacts final margins

### 2. Pricing Calculation Formulas
- Present clear mathematical formulas for calculating optimal retail prices across different scenarios (wholesale, retail, promotional)
- Show step-by-step examples using the provided cost and fee structures
- Factor in platform-specific fees, marketing costs, and the stated profit margin targets

### 3. Competitive Pricing Strategy
- Analyze how competitor pricing constrains or enables pricing decisions
- Recommend positioning (premium, competitive parity, value) based on the competitive landscape

### 4. Dynamic Pricing Automation
- Provide bullet-point recommendations for automating price adjustments based on:
  - Cost fluctuations (supply chain, shipping rate changes)
  - Competitor price movements
  - Demand signals and inventory levels
  - Seasonal or promotional cycles

### 5. Risk Management & Testing
- Outline guidelines for assessing pricing risk (margin erosion, market share loss)
- Suggest methods for testing new price points without harming brand perception (A/B tests, segmented rollouts, limited-time offers)

Format all formulas for easy spreadsheet implementation and present automation steps as an actionable checklist.
```
