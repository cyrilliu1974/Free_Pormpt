# Hyper-Realistic Anatomical Muscle Study Prompt

## 簡介

The Hyper-Realistic Anatomical Muscle Study Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces medically accurate, photorealistic renderings of human musculature for educators, artists, and medical professionals. This anatomical study prompt for Midjourney creates high-resolution images with precise muscle insertions, natural human proportions, realistic tissue depth, and subsurface scattering that mimics light penetration through skin and tissue. Soft studio lighting and a neutral background ensure every anatomical detail remains visible and clinically useful. Medical illustrators, anatomy instructors, fitness trainers, and concept artists reach for this prompt when they need publication-quality muscle references without hiring photographers or 3D modelers. ● Specify any body region (chest, shoulders, arms, legs, back) through a single variable to generate targeted anatomical studies. ● Produces 3:4 aspect ratio images at high resolution suitable for print textbooks, digital courses, and clinical presentations. ● Includes chaos and stylize parameters calibrated for anatomical accuracy rather than artistic abstraction. ● Renders subsurface scattering and tissue depth that show how light interacts with real human skin and muscle layers. ## Prompt

```

```
