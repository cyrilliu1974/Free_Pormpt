# Modern Still-Life Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Modern Still-Life Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates contemporary product scenes with studio-quality lighting and composition for e-commerce brands, designers, and content creators. This modern still-life prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and other image models arranges your products on a light wood table over polished concrete flooring, with a warm off-white wall featuring a subtle grey grid pattern as the backdrop. Diffused natural sunlight casts soft diagonal shadows that emphasize shape and texture, while a red hemispherical accent object adds visual interest. The prompt specifies materials like terrazzo, concrete, glass, and matte plastics in a muted pastel palette with vibrant color accents, shot with a 50mm lens at f/2.8 using rule-of-thirds composition and generous negative space for a clean, minimalist aesthetic. Designers use it to create product mockups, e-commerce teams generate hero images for landing pages, and social media managers produce scroll-stopping visuals without a photo studio. Reach for this prompt when you need contemporary product photography that balances warmth, texture, and negative space in a single coherent scene. ● Specifies lighting direction, shadow quality, and material finishes (terrazzo, concrete, glass, matte plastics) for photorealistic texture rendering. ● Controls composition with 50mm lens focal length, f/2.8 aperture, rule-of-thirds framing, and negative space for professional-grade layouts. ● Includes a red accent object and grey grid pattern background for visual interest without overwhelming the product. ● Outputs clean, minimalist scenes suitable for e-commerce hero images, social media, and brand lookbooks. ## Prompt
