# Churn Analysis and Retention Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Churn Analysis and Retention Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers statistical churn analysis and builds evidence-based retention plans for data scientists, customer success teams, and business strategists. Upload your customer dataset and this churn analysis prompt for ChatGPT identifies the key drivers of attrition, quantifies each factor's percentage impact, segments your customer base by churn risk probability, and produces three detailed retention strategies with measurable expected outcomes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying statistical techniques to surface patterns in customer behavior and turning raw churn data into implementation-ready plans complete with step-by-step actions, revenue retention projections, and lifetime value increases. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from spreadsheet observations to board-ready retention initiatives backed by your actual data. ● Identifies and ranks churn drivers with percentage contribution to attrition ● Segments customers into risk tiers with calculated churn probabilities ● Delivers three retention strategies, each with expected impact metrics and implementation steps ● Projects churn rate reduction, revenue retention, and customer lifetime value increase in quantifiable terms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a data scientist and business strategist specializing in churn analysis and customer retention.

## Task
Perform a comprehensive churn analysis using the provided dataset. Identify key drivers of customer attrition, at-risk segments, and develop actionable retention strategies with measurable outcomes.

## Context
{{churn-dataset}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Apply statistical techniques to uncover patterns and causal factors in customer churn
- Quantify the impact of each churn driver with percentage contribution
- Define customer segments by churn risk level with associated probabilities
- Ground all findings in the data provided and industry best practices

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Data Sources:**
● List all datasets and sources used in the analysis

**Churn Analysis:**

Key Churn Drivers:
1. Driver name (percentage impact%)
2. Driver name (percentage impact%)
3. Driver name (percentage impact%)

At-Risk Segments:
1. Segment name: Clear definition
   Churn risk: percentage%
2. Segment name: Clear definition
   Churn risk: percentage%
3. Segment name: Clear definition
   Churn risk: percentage%

**Retention Strategies:**

For each of three strategies, provide:

1. Strategy name: Description of approach
   Expected impact: Quantified outcome
   Implementation steps:
   ● Step 1
   ● Step 2
   ● Step 3

2. Strategy name: Description of approach
   Expected impact: Quantified outcome
   Implementation steps:
   ● Step 1
   ● Step 2
   ● Step 3

3. Strategy name: Description of approach
   Expected impact: Quantified outcome
   Implementation steps:
   ● Step 1
   ● Step 2
   ● Step 3

**Projected Outcomes:**
● Churn rate reduction: percentage%
● Revenue retention: dollar amount or percentage
● Customer lifetime value increase: percentage%
```
