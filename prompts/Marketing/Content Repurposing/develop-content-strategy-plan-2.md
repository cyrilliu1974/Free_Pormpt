# Content Strategy Plan Generator for Business Goals

## 簡介

The Content Strategy Plan Generator for Business Goals is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive content roadmap aligned with your business context, competitive landscape, and performance baseline. This content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business goals and audience needs to produce a strategic overview, a detailed table mapping content types to audience segments and key messages, plus distribution recommendations, publication frequency guidelines, and measurable KPIs. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by synthesizing your business context, competitive environment, and existing performance data into a cohesive plan that differentiates your content approach from competitors. Marketing teams, content leads, and strategists use it to shift from ad-hoc publishing to intentional, goal-driven content planning. ● Produces a markdown table linking content types, target audiences, and key messages in one view ● Includes distribution channel recommendations and publication cadence tailored to your context ● Defines measurable KPIs and adaptability considerations for evolving market conditions ● Synthesizes competitive landscape and baseline performance into strategic differentiation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist developing a comprehensive content strategy plan that aligns with business goals and target audience needs.

## Task
Create a strategic content plan that includes:
- Analysis of the business context and content objectives
- Recommended content types mapped to specific audience segments
- Key messages tailored to each audience
- Distribution channel recommendations
- Content frequency guidelines
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Adaptability considerations for market trends and audience shifts

## Context
{{business-and-goals}}

{{competitive-landscape}}

{{baseline-performance}}

## Output
Provide a brief strategic overview (2-3 paragraphs) explaining how the plan aligns with business goals, addresses audience needs, and differentiates from competitors.

Then present the core plan as a markdown table with 3 columns:

| Content Types | Target Audiences | Key Messages |
|---------------|------------------|-------------|

Include 5-8 rows covering your recommended content mix. After the table, add brief sections on distribution strategy, publication frequency, and measurement approach.
```
