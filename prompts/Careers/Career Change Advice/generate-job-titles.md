# Business Job Title and Career Path Generator

## 簡介

The Business Job Title and Career Path Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a detailed career opportunities table tailored to your business management experience, interests, and salary goals. This career path prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your profile and generates at least 20 diverse business job titles across all seniority levels - entry, mid-level, senior, and executive - spanning departments like operations, finance, marketing, HR, strategy, and sales. Each row includes the role's key responsibilities, current average salary range, and a five-star growth potential rating. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for professionals exploring new business functions, planning career transitions, or benchmarking their current position against the market. This prompt is built for business professionals at any stage who need a comprehensive view of where their skills and ambitions can take them across the corporate landscape. ● Covers diverse business functions and all seniority levels in a single, scannable table format. ● Provides current average salary ranges and clear responsibility descriptions for each role. ● Includes a five-star growth potential rating to help prioritize high-trajectory positions. ● Tailors recommendations based on your stated experience level, desired specialization, and target salary range. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business management career guide with deep knowledge of job titles, responsibilities, and career progression across all business functions.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive career opportunity table with at least 20 diverse business job titles spanning entry to executive levels. Tailor the selection to align with the user's experience, interests, and salary goals.

## Context
User profile:
{{career-profile}}

(Include: current experience level in business management, desired business function or specialization if any, and target salary range)

## Output
Present results as a well-formatted table with these columns:

- **Job Title**
- **Seniority Level** (Entry, Mid, Senior, Executive)
- **Department/Specialization**
- **Key Responsibilities**
- **Average Salary Range**
- **Growth Potential** (⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)

**Growth Potential Key:**
- ⭐ Limited growth
- ⭐⭐ Some growth potential
- ⭐⭐⭐ Good growth potential
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Strong growth potential
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Excellent growth potential

Ensure all information reflects current industry standards. Cover diverse business functions (operations, finance, marketing, HR, strategy, sales, etc.) and all seniority levels. Provide clear, concise responsibility descriptions and accurate salary ranges.
```
