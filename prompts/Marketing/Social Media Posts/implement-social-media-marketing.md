# Social Media Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Social Media Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed, actionable social media plan to increase brand awareness, engage your target audience, and drive website traffic. This social media marketing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business description, target audience, primary platforms, and unique selling proposition to generate platform-specific tactics including content calendars, post formats, hashtag strategies, and community engagement techniques. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce a structured strategy covering content pillars, messaging frameworks, visual identity recommendations, KPIs, analytics setup, and a 30-60-90 day implementation roadmap. Solopreneurs use it to establish consistent posting schedules, identify quick-win opportunities, and plan paid advertising without hiring an agency. This prompt is built for solo business owners who need a complete social media strategy but lack the time or budget for a full-time marketing team. ● Generates platform-specific content calendar frameworks with recommended post formats, frequency, and timing ● Provides hashtag research strategies and community engagement techniques tailored to your platforms ● Defines measurable KPIs, analytics tracking setup, and A/B testing recommendations for continuous improvement ● Delivers a phased 30-60-90 day action plan with resource requirements and quick-win opportunities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketing strategist building comprehensive platform strategies for solopreneurs.

## Task
Develop a detailed, actionable social media marketing plan that increases brand awareness, engages the target audience, and drives website traffic. Analyze the business and audience, then create a strategy tailored to the specified platforms.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-description}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Primary Platforms:** {{platforms}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output
Deliver a structured strategy with these sections:

### Platform-Specific Tactics
- Content calendar framework for each platform
- Recommended post formats and frequency
- Hashtag research and usage strategy
- Community engagement techniques
- Paid advertising opportunities

### Content Strategy
- Core content pillars aligned with the USP
- Content themes and messaging frameworks
- Post templates and copywriting guidelines
- Visual identity recommendations

### Measurement & Optimization
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) to track
- Analytics tools and tracking setup
- Reporting cadence and benchmarks
- A/B testing recommendations
- Continuous improvement process

### Implementation Roadmap
- 30-60-90 day action plan
- Resource requirements
- Quick-win opportunities

Format the strategy with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for easy implementation.
```
