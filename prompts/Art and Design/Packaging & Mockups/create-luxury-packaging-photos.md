# Luxury Fashion Packaging Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Fashion Packaging Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-ready product photographs of high-end fashion packaging boxes for brands, designers, and marketers. This luxury packaging prompt for Midjourney produces wide-angle side-view shots featuring custom box text, minimal geometric patterns with colorful shapes, and professional studio lighting on a backdrop color of your choice. The prompt outputs images with a matte soft-touch finish, stacked arrangement, and shallow depth of field at 16:9 aspect ratio, making it ideal for e-commerce listings, social media campaigns, pitch decks, and brand lookbooks. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image generation models that support detailed product photography parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need polished product visuals without a physical photoshoot or when iterating on packaging concepts before manufacturing. ● Specify any quantity of boxes, custom brand or product text, and backdrop color to match your visual identity. ● Produces soft studio lighting, matte finishes, and shallow depth of field that mimic professional product photography. ● Outputs 16:9 high-resolution images suitable for hero sections, presentations, and social media posts. ● Eliminates the cost and logistics of physical shoots while maintaining the polished aesthetic of luxury fashion brands. ## Prompt

```
Wide-angle side-view product photograph of {{quantity}} luxury fashion packaging boxes with the text "{{box-text}}"; minimal geometric pattern with colorful shapes on a {{backdrop-color}} backdrop; editorial-ready, high resolution, soft studio lighting, matte soft-touch finish, stacked arrangement, shallow depth of field --ar 16:9
```
