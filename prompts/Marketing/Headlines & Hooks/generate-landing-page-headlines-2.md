# Landing Page Headline Generator for High Conversions

## 簡介

The Landing Page Headline Generator for High Conversions is a free AI prompt that produces 10–15 concise, benefit-focused headline options tailored to your product, audience pain points, unique selling proposition, and brand tone. This landing page headline prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar principles to structure each option around a strong core verb or benefit noun, with modifiers that add clarity, urgency, and emotional resonance. Each headline stays under 12 words and incorporates power words and emotional triggers matched to your audience's desires or frustrations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a clean numbered list with no commentary - ready to test and deploy. Copywriters, marketers, and founders use it to quickly generate multiple headline variations for A/B testing, product launches, and campaign optimization. ● Applies dependency grammar to anchor headlines around strong verbs and benefit nouns for maximum clarity and impact. ● Tailors every headline to your specific product, audience pain point, unique selling proposition, and brand tone. ● Generates 10–15 concise options (under 12 words each) in a clean, numbered format for fast review and testing. ● Incorporates power words and emotional triggers that resonate with audience desires and frustrations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert landing-page copywriter specializing in high-conversion headlines.

## Task
Generate 10–15 compelling headline options for a landing page. Structure each headline using dependency grammar principles—anchor each phrase around a strong core (verb or benefit noun) with modifiers that clarify value, urgency, or emotion.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}

Target audience & pain point: {{audience-and-pain-point}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Brand tone: {{brand-tone}}

Each headline must be concise (under 12 words), attention-grabbing, and benefit-driven. Use power words and emotional triggers that resonate with the audience's desires or frustrations. Ensure the tone aligns with the brand voice throughout.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of 10–15 headline options, each on its own line. No explanations or commentary—only the headlines.
```
