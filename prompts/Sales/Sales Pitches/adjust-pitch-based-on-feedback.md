# Adjust Pitch Based on Feedback

## 簡介

The Adjust Pitch Based on Feedback is a free AI prompt that refines sales presentations by analyzing audience reactions and restructuring messaging for maximum persuasive impact. This sales pitch refinement prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing specific feedback from your target audience and applying dependency grammar principles to restructure your messaging. The prompt identifies clarity issues, unaddressed objections, weak value communication, and structural problems in your current pitch, then reorganizes sentences around core verbs and anchors modifiers to their head words to increase impact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a detailed breakdown of each adjustment with the rationale and linguistic technique applied. Sales professionals use it to transform flat presentations into compelling pitches that directly address prospect concerns and emphasize unique value propositions through strategic clause hierarchy. Reach for this prompt when you have received audience feedback on a pitch and need a systematic method to incorporate those insights while improving the linguistic structure of your messaging. ● Analyzes audience feedback to pinpoint specific weaknesses in clarity, objection handling, and value communication ● Applies dependency grammar techniques to restructure sentences with key selling points as syntactic anchors ● Delivers a bullet-point breakdown of each refinement with the feedback addressed and linguistic method used ● Ensures benefits and differentiators receive proper emphasis through strategic clause positioning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist specializing in pitch refinement and persuasive communication.

## Task
Refine and optimize the provided sales pitch based on audience feedback. Restructure sentences using dependency grammar principles—organizing clauses around core verbs and anchoring modifiers to their head words—to increase persuasive impact and clarity.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Feedback Received:** {{feedback}}

Analyze the feedback to identify specific areas requiring adjustment: clarity issues, unaddressed objections, weak value communication, or structural problems. Apply dependency grammar to make key selling points the syntactic anchors of your sentences, ensuring benefits and differentiators receive proper emphasis through clause hierarchy.

## Output
Present the refined pitch as a bullet point list. For each major adjustment:
- State the change made
- Explain the rationale (which feedback point it addresses)
- Note the dependency grammar technique applied (e.g., "repositioned benefit as main clause," "converted weak passive to active verb anchor," "elevated unique value proposition to sentence head")

Ensure the refined pitch directly addresses audience concerns, clearly communicates the unique value proposition, and emphasizes concrete benefits.
```
