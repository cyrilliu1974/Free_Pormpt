# Social Media Trend Research Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Trend Research Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that identifies and evaluates emerging topics and hashtags across Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok for marketers, social media managers, and content creators. This social media trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by scanning current platform activity within your specified industry, timeframe, and geographical focus. It measures engagement signals like shares, comments, and views, then delivers a markdown table ranking 10-15 significant trends by engagement level alongside relevance notes. The output includes a summary of the top 3 cross-platform trends, helping you understand which topics resonate across multiple channels. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it adaptable to your preferred text AI model. Reach for this prompt when you need data-driven trend insights to inform content calendars, campaign planning, or competitive analysis in a specific market or audience segment. ● Analyzes Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok simultaneously to capture platform-specific and cross-platform trends ● Sorts findings by engagement level so you prioritize topics with proven audience traction ● Evaluates both rising and sustained trends within your chosen timeframe and geographical region ● Outputs a clean markdown table and executive summary ready to share with teams or clients ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media trends analyst specializing in identifying and evaluating emerging topics across major platforms.

## Task
Research current trending topics and hashtags on Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok relevant to {{industry}}. Analyze each trend's popularity and relevance, then compile your findings into a structured report.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Research timeframe: {{timeframe}}
Geographical focus: {{geographical-focus}}

## Research Criteria
- Prioritize trends with measurable engagement (shares, comments, views)
- Assess relevance to the specified industry and audience
- Note platform-specific variations in how trends manifest
- Identify both rising and sustained trends within the timeframe

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with the following columns:

| Topic | Hashtag | Platform | Engagement Level | Relevance Notes |

Sort by engagement level (highest first). Include 10-15 of the most significant trends. Add a brief summary paragraph above the table highlighting the top 3 cross-platform trends.
```
