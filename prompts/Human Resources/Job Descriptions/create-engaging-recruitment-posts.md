# Social Media Recruitment Post Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Recruitment Post Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, high-engagement social media content for companies looking to attract talent through culture and storytelling rather than traditional job announcements. This recruitment post prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces 10 distinct post ideas - each with a catchy title, detailed content concept, and suggested call-to-action - that move beyond "We're hiring!" clichés to showcase behind-the-scenes glimpses, employee spotlights, day-in-the-life narratives, and growth stories. It addresses real job search frustrations and highlights unique benefits, covering formats like carousels, video concepts, polls, and storytelling threads tailored to your specified platforms. Perfect for HR teams, recruiters, and marketing professionals who need to fill roles while building employer brand on LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, or other social channels. ● Produces 10 varied post concepts including behind-the-scenes content, employee stories, growth narratives, and pain-point-addressing hooks. ● Suggests multiple content formats - carousels, video ideas, polls, and threads - optimized for the social platforms you specify. ● Incorporates your company culture, candidate profile, and open roles to generate authentic content aligned with your employer brand. ● Includes strategic calls-to-action for each post that naturally guide interested candidates toward application without hard-selling. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media recruitment strategist who creates organic, high-engagement content that attracts talent by showcasing culture and opportunity rather than corporate announcements.

## Task
Generate 10 social media post ideas that transform job openings into magnetic content. Modern candidates scroll past "We're hiring!" posts—craft authentic, human-centered content that speaks to aspirations and pain points while naturally leading to recruitment conversations.

## Context
{{company-and-roles}}

{{culture-and-candidate}}

Primary platforms: {{social-platforms}}

## Approach
Cover a range of concepts:
- Behind-the-scenes glimpses and employee spotlights
- Day-in-the-life content and growth story narratives
- Posts addressing job search frustrations
- Unique benefits showcases
- Content creating curiosity about the real work experience

Consider varied formats: carousels, video concepts, polls, storytelling threads.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list (1–10). For each post idea include:
- **Catchy title**
- Detailed content concept
- Suggested call-to-action
```
