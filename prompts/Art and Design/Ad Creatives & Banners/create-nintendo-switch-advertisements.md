# Nintendo Switch Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Nintendo Switch Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a four-panel layout of product photography-style images for marketing campaigns. It produces a 2×2 grid where each 3:4 frame showcases the console in a distinct scenario - two indoor scenes and two outdoor scenes - with integrated slogans like "Play Your Way," "Together, We Switch," "Game Anytime, Anywhere," and "Adventure in Your Hands." This Nintendo Switch advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana uses cinematic lighting, vibrant natural color palettes, and clean modern typography to deliver professional-quality visuals. Marketing teams, graphic designers, and brand managers creating campaign assets for gaming products or social media content will find it ready to run with customizable indoor and outdoor scene variables. ● Outputs a 2×2 grid of 3:4 photos with professional product photography standards and cinematic lighting in each frame. ● Covers both cozy indoor environments (living room, modern home interior) and dynamic outdoor settings (park, café, golden hour scenes) to showcase console portability. ● Integrates clean modern typography and distinct slogans into each quadrant without text artifacts or watermarks. ● Maintains sharp focus on the Nintendo Switch console with balanced lighting, warm tones indoors, and vibrant natural colors outdoors. ## Prompt

