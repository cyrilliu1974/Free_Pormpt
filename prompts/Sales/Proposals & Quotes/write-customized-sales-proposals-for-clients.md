# Customized Sales Proposal Writer for B2B Clients

## 簡介

The Customized Sales Proposal Writer for B2B Clients is a free AI prompt that creates strategic sales proposals tailored to each prospect's industry context, pain points, and business goals. This sales proposal prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target client profile - industry dynamics, competitive pressures, operational challenges - and builds a persuasive proposal around your product offering, unique selling points, and company track record. It structures every proposal with an executive summary, situation analysis that mirrors the client's context, a solution section emphasizing outcomes over features, credibility-building evidence such as case studies and benchmarks, implementation timelines, and a clear call-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing professional documents ready for client review. Sales teams use it to respond to RFPs, pitch new accounts, or expand existing relationships with data-driven, client-centric proposals. ● Analyzes client industry trends, pain points, and decision criteria to ground every proposal in real business context ● Structures proposals with executive summary, situation analysis, value proposition, evidence, implementation plan, and call-to-action ● Emphasizes outcomes and ROI over feature lists, weaving in your unique selling points as differentiators tied to client needs ● Incorporates case studies, testimonials, and quantified results to build credibility and support claims ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist specializing in B2B proposals that align product value with client business outcomes.

## Task
Create a compelling, customized sales proposal that demonstrates how the offering solves the target client's specific challenges and advances their goals.

## Context
Analyze the {{target-client-profile}} to identify:
- Industry context, competitive pressures, and market trends
- Key pain points, operational challenges, and strategic priorities
- Decision-making criteria and success metrics

Then develop a proposal built around {{product-offering}} that directly addresses these findings.

## Approach
Structure the proposal with:

**Executive Summary**: Open with the client's core challenge and your solution thesis in 2-3 sentences.

**Situation Analysis**: Mirror back the client's context to demonstrate understanding—reference their industry dynamics, growth stage, or documented pain points.

**Solution & Value Proposition**: Explain how {{product-offering}} solves their specific problems. Emphasize outcomes over features. Weave in {{unique-selling-points}} as differentiators tied to client needs, not generic claims.

**Evidence & Credibility**: Support claims with relevant case studies (similar client size/industry), quantified results, testimonials, or benchmark data. Include {{company-track-record}} where it builds confidence.

**Implementation & Next Steps**: Outline the pathway forward—timeline, key milestones, and what the client can expect.

**Call-to-Action**: Close with a clear, specific next step (discovery call date, pilot program, contract review) that moves the deal forward.

## Output
Deliver the proposal using:
- Clear heading hierarchy (##, ###)
- Bullet points for benefits, features, and key details
- Bold text for critical value statements
- Concise, client-centric language throughout
```
