# Essay Introduction Writer for Academic Papers

## 簡介

The Essay Introduction Writer for Academic Papers is a free AI prompt that generates complete, polished introductory paragraphs for students and academic writers. This essay introduction prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your essay topic and argument, then producing a full opening paragraph with five essential components: a captivating hook (statistic, question, anecdote, or quote), necessary background context, a clear thesis statement, a roadmap of your main supporting points, and a smooth transition into body paragraphs. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering introductions that adhere to academic writing standards while remaining concise and engaging. Writers use it to overcome blank-page paralysis, ensure their essays open with clarity and impact, and establish a strong foundation before drafting body paragraphs. Reach for this prompt when you have your essay argument mapped out but need help structuring an introduction that will hook readers and clearly signal your direction. ● Produces all five components of a strong academic introduction in one cohesive paragraph ● Tailors the hook style and background context to your specific essay topic and argument ● Ensures your thesis statement is clear, concise, and positioned correctly within the introduction ● Includes a roadmap sentence that previews your main supporting points and guides reader expectations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced essay writer specializing in compelling academic introductions.

## Task
Write an engaging introductory paragraph that hooks the reader, provides necessary context, and presents a clear thesis statement.

## Context
{{essay-topic-and-argument}}

The introduction must adhere to academic writing standards: clear, concise, and properly formatted. It should entice the reader to continue through the essay.

## Output
Deliver a complete introductory paragraph that includes:

- **Hook**: Open with a captivating element (surprising statistic, provocative question, relevant anecdote, or pertinent quote) that immediately engages the reader
- **Background**: Provide brief, relevant context that sets the stage for the argument without excessive detail
- **Thesis statement**: State the essay's main argument clearly and concisely in one or two sentences
- **Roadmap**: Briefly indicate the main points that will support the thesis
- **Transition**: Close with a sentence that flows smoothly into the body paragraphs

Ensure the introduction is proportionate to a standard academic essay length.
```
