# Competitor-Driven Risk Management Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor-Driven Risk Management Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps businesses identify competitor threats, assess vulnerabilities, and build defensive and offensive strategies to strengthen market position. This competitor risk management prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your competitive landscape and produces a structured risk framework including probability-impact matrices, mitigation strategy tables, KPI tracking systems, and phased action plans spanning immediate moves through long-term positioning shifts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming competitor intelligence into concrete risk management protocols. Use it when launching competitive responses, entering contested markets, facing disruptive threats, or building enterprise risk frameworks that account for market share erosion, pricing pressure, innovation displacement, talent competition, and supply chain conflicts. ● Produces a Risk Identification Matrix that categorizes competitor threats by type and scores each on probability and impact scales from 1 to 5. ● Generates a detailed Mitigation Strategies table mapping each risk to specific implementation steps and resource requirements. ● Delivers a Monitoring and Review Process defining competitive KPIs, reassessment schedules, and surveillance tools for tracking rival activities. ● Provides a three-horizon Action Plan separating immediate defensive moves, capability-building initiatives, and strategic positioning shifts across 0-6, 6-18, and 18+ month timeframes. ● Includes a SWOT analysis evaluating the risk management approach itself and an Executive Summary highlighting the 3-5 most critical threats with priority responses. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Risk Management Consultant specializing in competitor analysis and strategic planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive competitor-driven risk management strategy that enhances business resilience and competitive advantage.

## Context
{{competitive-landscape}}

Provide: industry sector, main competitors (name 2-5), current market position (leader/challenger/niche player/etc.), and key products or services offered.

## Output
Deliver your analysis in the following structure:

### 1. Risk Identification Matrix
List potential competitor-related risks, categorize them (market share erosion, pricing pressure, disruptive innovation, talent poaching, supply chain competition, etc.), and assign probability and impact scores on a 1-5 scale for each.

### 2. Mitigation Strategies
Present as a table:

| Risk | Strategy | Implementation Steps | Resources Required |
|------|----------|----------------------|--------------------|

### 3. Monitoring and Review Process
- Define KPIs to track competitive threats
- Recommend frequency of risk reassessments
- Suggest tools and methods for ongoing competitor surveillance

### 4. Action Plan
📊 **Short-term (0-6 months):** Immediate defensive and offensive moves

📈 **Medium-term (6-18 months):** Capability-building initiatives

🚀 **Long-term (18+ months):** Strategic positioning shifts

### 5. SWOT Analysis of the Risk Management Approach
Present as a table:

| Strengths | Weaknesses | Opportunities | Threats |
|-----------|------------|---------------|---------|

### 6. Executive Summary
Highlight the 3-5 most critical risks and their corresponding priority strategies.

## Criteria
- All risks must stem directly from competitor actions or market dynamics
- Prioritize by impact on competitive advantage
- Strategies must be actionable, measurable, and aligned with business objectives
- Emphasize innovative approaches that convert risks into growth opportunities
- Tailor all recommendations to the specific industry and competitive context
- Balance defensive protection with offensive positioning
- Ensure ethical compliance with all relevant regulations
- Avoid generic frameworks; provide specific, contextual guidance
```
