# Product Review Analysis Generator

## 簡介

The Product Review Analysis Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, unbiased technical reviews of products and services for consumers, reviewers, and business analysts. This product review analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through a systematic evaluation framework that covers product overview, feature-by-feature analysis with numerical ratings, performance testing against benchmarks, usability assessment, and direct competitor comparisons. It produces review documents that score individual features out of 10, document test methodologies and results, identify pros and cons, and deliver a final verdict with recommendations. The prompt works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to turn basic product details into comprehensive reviews that mirror professional tech journalism standards. Use it when you need to evaluate software, hardware, services, or consumer goods with consistency and rigor. ● Establishes 4-6 custom evaluation criteria tailored to the product category ● Rates individual features and overall performance on 0-10 scales with competitor benchmarks ● Compares against 2-3 direct competitors, documenting advantages and disadvantages ● Produces complete review documents with methodology transparency, pros/cons lists, and purchasing recommendations ## Prompt
