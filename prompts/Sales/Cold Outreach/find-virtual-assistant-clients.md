# Find Virtual Assistant Clients Prompt

## 簡介

The Find Virtual Assistant Clients Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches and identifies high-fit solopreneurs for virtual assistant service providers. It analyzes business models within a specified niche, surfaces operational pain points, and matches them to relevant VA services, delivering findings in a structured markdown table. This virtual assistant client research prompt for ChatGPT works by taking three inputs - your target niche, the number of prospects to find, and your specialized VA services - then returning a detailed table with prospect names, business descriptions, identified pain points, and specific service recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for VA businesses conducting cold outreach, building prospect lists, or preparing personalized service pitches. Each row in the output connects a real prospect to actionable reasons why they need your help and which of your offerings solve their challenges. Use this prompt when launching outreach campaigns, expanding into new niches, or qualifying leads before first contact. ● Identifies solopreneurs in any specified niche with business-model analysis ● Surfaces operational pain points based on how each prospect runs their business ● Maps pain points to your specific VA service offerings for tailored pitches ● Outputs a clean markdown table ready for CRM import or outreach sequencing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a virtual assistant services expert identifying potential clients.

## Task
Research and compile a list of solopreneurs who would benefit from virtual assistant services. For each prospect:

1. Identify solopreneurs operating in the specified niche
2. Analyze their business models to surface likely operational pain points
3. Match pain points to relevant virtual assistant services that would address them
4. Organize findings into a structured table

## Context
Target niche: {{target-niche}}
Number of prospects to identify: {{prospect-count}}
Your specialized VA services: {{va-services}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:

| PROSPECT NAME | BUSINESS | PAIN POINTS | SERVICES TO OFFER |
|---------------|----------|-------------|-------------------|

Ensure each row provides actionable insight into why the prospect is a good fit and which specific services from your offering would solve their challenges.
```
