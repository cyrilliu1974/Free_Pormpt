# Ad Script Generator for Digital Platforms

## 簡介

The Ad Script Generator for Digital Platforms is a free AI prompt that produces platform-optimized ad copy variations for marketers running campaigns across social media, search, and display channels. This ad script prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok translates product features into audience benefits and delivers nine distinct copy assets: three headline variations, three body copy blocks, and three calls-to-action, each testing different angles, emotional triggers, or benefit emphasis. It respects character limits, formatting rules, and creative best practices for the specified platform while addressing target audience pain points directly. Marketing teams use it to generate A/B test-ready ad sets for Facebook, Google Ads, LinkedIn, TikTok, and other paid channels without starting from a blank page. This prompt is for performance marketers, media buyers, and copywriters who need multiple platform-compliant ad variations quickly and want each option grounded in audience psychology and conversion principles. ● Produces three headlines, three body copy blocks, and three CTAs in a single output, ready for split testing ● Applies dependency grammar for clear, scannable sentence structures that perform well in feed environments ● Adapts tone, length, and formatting to match the requirements of Facebook Ads, Google Search, LinkedIn Sponsored Content, and other platforms ● Incorporates emotional triggers and urgency while aligning each call-to-action with user intent and campaign goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing copywriter specializing in platform-optimized ad scripts that drive conversions.

## Task
Create persuasive, concise ad copy variations tailored to the platform's best practices and character limits. Deliver three variations each of headlines, body copy, and calls-to-action.

## Context
Product/service and positioning:
{{product-and-usp}}

Target audience (include demographics, pain points, desires, and motivations):
{{target-audience}}

Digital marketing platform:
{{platform}}

Desired call-to-action:
{{cta}}

## Approach
- Analyze product features and translate them into audience-relevant benefits
- Address target audience pain points and desires directly
- Apply the platform's character limits, formatting rules, and creative best practices
- Use dependency grammar to create clear, scannable sentence structures
- Incorporate emotional triggers and persuasive language that resonate with the audience
- Ensure headlines grab attention within platform constraints
- Make the call-to-action specific, urgent, and aligned with user intent

## Output
Deliver in this structure:

**Headlines** (3 variations)
1. [headline]
2. [headline]
3. [headline]

**Body Copy** (3 variations)
1. [body copy]
2. [body copy]
3. [body copy]

**Calls-to-Action** (3 variations)
1. [CTA]
2. [CTA]
3. [CTA]

Each variation should test different angles, tones, or benefit emphasis while staying true to the product positioning and audience needs.
```
