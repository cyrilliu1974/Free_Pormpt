# Research Emerging Market Trends Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Research Emerging Market Trends prompt is a free AI prompt that helps market research analysts and strategists identify emerging trends, evaluate opportunities, and assess business impact within any industry. This market research prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown table analyzing 5–7 high-priority trends, each paired with a specific actionable opportunity and an assessment of its potential impact on revenue, market share, or operational efficiency. You configure three variables - industry, company context, and area of focus - and the prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to deliver findings in a three-column format (Trend, Opportunity, Impact) that transforms qualitative research into strategic decisions. It prioritizes developments with meaningful potential for sales growth and competitive advantage within a 12–24 month action horizon. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn broad market intelligence into a concise, boardroom-ready summary that connects external trends to internal strategy. ● Surfaces 5–7 emerging trends tailored to your specified industry and business context ● Identifies specific, actionable opportunities tied to each trend rather than generic observations ● Assesses potential impact on revenue, market position, and operational efficiency in concise entries ● Outputs findings in a markdown table format that integrates directly into reports, decks, and strategy documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a market research analyst identifying emerging trends and actionable opportunities.

## Task
Conduct research on market trends in {{industry}} and analyze their implications for {{company-context}}. Identify specific opportunities each trend creates and assess their potential impact on operations, revenue, or market position.

## Context
Focus on trends with meaningful potential for sales growth and competitive advantage. Prioritize developments that align with {{area-of-focus}} and can be acted upon within a 12–24 month horizon.

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Trend | Opportunity | Impact |
|-------|-------------|--------|

Each row should:
- **Trend**: Describe the emerging development or shift (2–3 sentences)
- **Opportunity**: Specify the actionable business opportunity it creates
- **Impact**: Assess the potential effect on revenue, market share, operational efficiency, or strategic positioning

Include 5–7 high-priority trends. Keep entries concise but substantive enough to inform strategic decisions.
```
