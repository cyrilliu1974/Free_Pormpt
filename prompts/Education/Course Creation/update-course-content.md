# Course Content Update and Revision Prompt

## 簡介

The Course Content Update and Revision Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps course creators and educators refine existing online course material by identifying improvement opportunities and integrating current best practices. This course content update prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your existing structure, tailors content to your target audience, and delivers a revised outline that aligns with specific learning objectives and desired course duration. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow in every module and lesson. Whether you're refreshing a solopreneur bootcamp, updating professional development training, or modernizing educational content, this prompt transforms outdated material into engaging, actionable lessons. ● Analyzes current course structure to pinpoint gaps, outdated sections, and improvement opportunities ● Incorporates latest trends and proven methods in online education and course design ● Structures content with hierarchical headings, bullet points, and logical progression aligned to learning objectives ● Tailors material to audience needs with actionable steps, concrete examples, and immediately practical guidance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert course content developer specializing in online education for solopreneurs.

## Task
Revise and enhance existing online course material. Analyze the current content, identify improvement opportunities, and incorporate current best practices in online course design. Structure all writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Course topic: {{course-topic}}
Current structure: {{current-structure}}
Desired duration: {{course-duration}}
Key learning objectives: {{learning-objectives}}

## Requirements
- Tailor content specifically to solopreneur needs and constraints
- Use clear, concise language accessible to self-directed learners
- Include actionable steps with concrete examples throughout
- Make material engaging and immediately practical
- Reflect latest trends and proven methods in online course creation

## Output
Deliver a structured course outline using:
- Hierarchical headings and subheadings
- Bullet points for clarity
- Logical progression aligned with the learning objectives
- Clear module/lesson boundaries optimized for the specified duration
```
