# SEO Competitor Gap Analysis and Opportunity Finder

## 簡介

The SEO Competitor Gap Analysis and Opportunity Finder is a free AI prompt that delivers actionable competitive intelligence and ranking improvement strategies for SEO professionals and digital marketers. This SEO competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT conducts structured research across your company's current search performance and each competitor's strategy, comparing keyword rankings, backlink profiles, on-page optimization, and technical health. It outputs a comparative strengths-and-weaknesses table followed by prioritized, implementable recommendations spanning keyword targeting, content development, technical fixes, and link acquisition. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to whatever company and market context you provide. Reach for this prompt when launching a new SEO initiative, onboarding a client, or needing to justify resource allocation with data-backed opportunity identification. ● Produces a structured competitor comparison table isolating what each rival does well and where they are weak ● Generates prioritized, specific recommendations rather than generic SEO advice ● Covers all major ranking factors: keywords, content, backlinks, and technical health ● Accepts any company and market context, making it reusable across industries and client engagements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst conducting competitive intelligence to identify opportunities for ranking improvement and market advantage.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis and deliver actionable SEO recommendations. Your analysis should:

1. Research the company's current SEO standing: keyword rankings, backlink profile, on-page optimization, and technical health
2. Analyze each listed competitor's SEO strategy, identifying their strengths (what they do well that drives rankings) and weaknesses (gaps or vulnerabilities to exploit)
3. Compare performance metrics to pinpoint opportunities where the company can gain ground
4. Develop specific, prioritized recommendations across keyword targeting, content strategy, technical SEO, and link acquisition

## Context
{{company-and-market-context}}

## Output
Deliver your findings in two parts:

**Part 1: Competitor Analysis Table**

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| [One row per competitor] | [What they excel at in SEO] | [Where they are vulnerable] |

**Part 2: Actionable SEO Improvement Strategies**

A prioritized bullet-point list of specific, implementable recommendations tailored to the company's current position and goals.
```
