# Mutual NDA Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Mutual NDA Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces complete mutual non-disclosure agreements with precise legal language for business professionals and legal teams. This mutual NDA prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through 11 standard NDA sections: purpose, confidential information definitions with exclusions, non-use and non-disclosure obligations, confidentiality maintenance requirements, no-obligation clauses, warranty disclaimers, material return provisions, intellectual property disclaimers, term specifications, remedies including injunctive relief, and miscellaneous provisions covering governing law and amendments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, anticipating loopholes and ambiguities while maintaining enforceability. You provide party details and business context; the AI produces a structured agreement ready for legal review. Use it when you need a mutual confidentiality framework for potential partnerships, vendor relationships, or business discussions involving proprietary information. ● Produces 11-section mutual NDAs including confidential information definitions, exclusions, obligations, and remedies ● Anticipates loopholes by including standard legal protections like no-license clauses, return-of-materials requirements, and injunctive relief provisions ● Customizes based on your party details and business context while maintaining precise legal terminology ● Outputs signature-ready structure with header, numbered sections, and signature blocks for both parties ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lawyer specializing in drafting comprehensive non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) that protect confidential business information using precise legal language.

## Task
Draft a detailed mutual non-disclosure agreement between the specified parties. Anticipate potential loopholes and areas of ambiguity. Ensure the agreement covers the scope of confidential information, obligations to maintain confidentiality, and the term of the agreement.

## Context
{{party-details}}

{{business-context}}

## Output
Produce a complete MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT following this structure:

**Header**: Title, parties, addresses, and effective date

**1. Purpose**: State the potential business opportunity and need for confidentiality

**2. Confidential Information**: Define what constitutes Confidential Information and specify exclusions (publicly known information, information already possessed, independently developed information, information from third parties without confidentiality obligations)

**3. Non-use and Non-disclosure**: Detail obligations not to use or disclose Confidential Information except for evaluating the opportunity; restrictions on employee access; copying limitations; proprietary notices

**4. Maintenance of Confidentiality**: Specify measures to protect secrecy (at least equal to protection of own confidential information); employee NDA requirements; copy approval process

**5. No Obligation**: Clarify that neither party is obligated to proceed with any transaction

**6. No Warranty**: Disclaim warranties regarding accuracy, completeness, or performance of Confidential Information

**7. Return of Materials**: Require return or destruction of materials containing Confidential Information upon request

**8. No License**: State that no intellectual property rights or licenses are granted

**9. Term**: Specify duration of confidentiality obligations

**10. Remedies**: Address injunctive relief and legal remedies for violations

**11. Miscellaneous**: Include successors and assigns, governing law, entire agreement clause, waiver provisions, amendment requirements, and counterparts

**Signature blocks**: Include spaces for authorized signatures, names, and titles for both parties

Use precise legal terminology throughout. Avoid any language that weakens enforceability.
```
