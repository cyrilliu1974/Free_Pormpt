# Grant Proposal Narrative Generator

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Narrative Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive, funder-aligned grant narratives for nonprofits, researchers, and organizations seeking funding. This grant writing prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete proposal structure with four core sections: a quantified problem statement, an evidence-based solution, SMART objectives, and measurable impact indicators. It uses dependency grammar principles to build logical connections between claims, ensuring each section flows naturally from the previous one. You provide your project details and the funder's stated priorities, and the prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to craft a narrative that demonstrates clear need, innovative approach, and mission alignment. Ideal for grant writers drafting foundation applications, government proposals, or corporate sponsorship requests. ● Articulates the problem scope with quantitative data and urgency framing ● Connects your proposed solution directly to both the root problem and funder mission ● Generates 3-5 SMART objectives with clear measurement criteria ● Balances emotional storytelling with rigorous evidence and impact metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer who crafts compelling, evidence-based narratives that secure funding.

## Task
Write a persuasive grant proposal narrative that aligns with the funder's priorities and demonstrates clear need, solution, and impact.

## Context
Project and organization details:
{{project-details}}

Funding organization priorities:
{{funder-priorities}}

Use dependency grammar principles to structure your argument: establish the core problem as the root, then branch logically through solution, objectives, and impact. Each section should build on and depend upon the previous claims. Support all assertions with data and evidence.

## Output
Deliver a structured grant narrative with these sections:

**Problem Statement**
- Define the specific problem or need
- Quantify the scope and urgency
- Explain why current approaches are insufficient

**Proposed Solution**
- Present your intervention or initiative
- Explain how it addresses the root problem
- Highlight innovative or evidence-based approaches

**Objectives**
- List 3-5 SMART objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Connect each objective directly to the problem and solution

**Expected Impact**
- Describe short-term and long-term outcomes
- Provide measurable indicators of success
- Demonstrate alignment with the funder's mission and priorities

Use bullet points within each section. Write in clear, persuasive prose that balances emotional appeal with rigorous evidence.
```
