# Partner Relationship Management Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Partner Relationship Management Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive partnership strategies tailored to your business context and existing partner ecosystem. This partner relationship management prompt for ChatGPT creates structured plans organized around four strategic pillars: communication strategy, collaboration framework, mutual support initiatives, and performance metrics. It analyzes your business context and current partnerships to deliver sector-specific tactics with timelines, responsible parties, and measurable success indicators. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for business relationship strategists, partnership managers, and business development teams who need to formalize their approach to partner ecosystem management. ● Produces communication strategies that establish regular touchpoints and feedback loops with key partners ● Delivers collaboration frameworks with tactical categories for joint initiatives and resource sharing ● Generates mutual support initiatives that address sector-specific challenges and partnership dynamics ● Includes performance metrics with measurable success indicators and accountability assignments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business relationship strategist specializing in partner ecosystem development.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive partner relationship management plan that establishes strong, mutually beneficial partnerships through regular communication, strategic collaboration, and mutual support initiatives.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Current state: {{current-partnerships-and-channels}}

## Requirements
- Tailor strategies to sector-specific challenges and opportunities
- Address the unique dynamics of the stated business type and industry
- Build on existing partnership foundations and communication infrastructure
- Align partner management tactics with stated business objectives
- Ensure all recommendations are actionable and measurable

## Output
Provide a structured partner relationship management plan organized with:

**Main headings** for strategic pillars (Communication Strategy, Collaboration Framework, Mutual Support Initiatives, Performance Metrics)

**Subheadings** for tactical categories within each pillar

**Bullet points** for specific strategies, tactics, and implementation steps

Include timelines, responsible parties, and success indicators where relevant.
```
