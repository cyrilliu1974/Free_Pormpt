# Team Productivity Gamification System Designer

## 簡介

The Team Productivity Gamification System Designer is a free AI prompt that creates structured gamification frameworks tailored to your team's specific tasks, size, and productivity challenges. This productivity gamification prompt for ChatGPT takes your team context and outputs a full system design: three core game mechanics calibrated to different player types, a balanced points economy, individual and team leaderboards, achievements and badges, collaborative challenges, virtual and real-world rewards, progress dashboards, feedback loops, a cohesive narrative theme, and a practical rollout plan. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need to increase task completion rates, address motivation gaps, or introduce structured engagement systems without starting from scratch. ● Outputs three core game mechanics that balance individual competition with team collaboration, appealing to diverse motivational profiles ● Includes a full points economy showing how points are earned through desired behaviors and what they can be redeemed for ● Designs leaderboards, achievement badges, team challenges, and both virtual and tangible reward structures ● Applies behavioral psychology principles explicitly, explaining how each mechanic drives sustained engagement and task completion ● Provides implementation guidance covering rollout strategy, technical requirements, potential obstacles, and change management ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a gamification expert specializing in game mechanics, behavioral psychology, and team dynamics to design systems that increase task completion and productivity.

## Task
Design a comprehensive gamification system tailored to the team context provided. The system should motivate participants through engaging mechanics while fostering collaboration and sustained engagement.

## Context
{{team-context}}

*Provide: team size, primary tasks and objectives, current productivity challenges*

## Requirements
- Include at least 3 core game mechanics that appeal to different player types
- Balance individual competition with team collaboration
- Tie rewards directly to desired behaviors and outcomes
- Ensure mechanics are intuitive and avoid unnecessary complexity
- Provide clear feedback loops and progress visibility
- Address practical implementation considerations

## Output
Deliver your design using this structure:

**Gamification System Overview:**
[Brief description of the system and how it addresses the team's needs]

**Core Game Mechanics:**
1. [Mechanic 1 with explanation]
2. [Mechanic 2 with explanation]
3. [Mechanic 3 with explanation]

**Points System:**
- Earning Points: [How points are earned through task completion and behaviors]
- Redeeming Points: [What points can be exchanged for]

**Leaderboards and Achievements:**
- Leaderboards: [Individual and team leaderboard structures]
- Achievements: [Badge/milestone system]

**Team Challenges and Collaboration:**
- Team Challenges: [Cooperative challenge design]
- Collaboration Mechanics: [How the system encourages teamwork]

**Rewards and Incentives:**
- Virtual Rewards: [In-system rewards]
- Real-World Incentives: [Tangible rewards and recognition]

**Progress Tracking and Feedback:**
- Progress Visualization: [Dashboards, charts, status indicators]
- Feedback Mechanisms: [How and when feedback is delivered]

**Narrative and Theme:**
[Overarching story or metaphor that gives the system cohesion]

**Key Behavioral Principles:**
1. [Principle 1 and application]
2. [Principle 2 and application]
3. [Principle 3 and application]

**Implementation Considerations:**
[Rollout strategy, potential obstacles, technical requirements, change management]
```
