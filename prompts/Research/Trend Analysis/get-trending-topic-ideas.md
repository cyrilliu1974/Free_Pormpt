# Trending Topic Ideas Generator for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Trending Topic Ideas Generator for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that identifies and scores current trending topics for content creators, marketers, and strategists looking to boost audience engagement. This trending topic ideas prompt for ChatGPT analyzes social media conversations, viral content patterns, industry news, and platform-specific algorithms to deliver a numbered list of relevant topics. Each entry includes a popularity score (1-10), the topic name, and a 2-3 sentence explanation of why it's trending and how it connects to your specific audience and content goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to whichever text model you prefer. Real-world use cases include planning editorial calendars, identifying timely blog post angles, and selecting social media content themes that align with current audience interests. Reach for this prompt when you need to stay ahead of content trends, validate topic ideas with popularity metrics, or align your content strategy with what's capturing attention on your preferred platforms. ● Produces a structured numbered list with popularity scores (1-10) for each trending topic, making prioritization straightforward. ● Draws on social media signals, viral content patterns, industry news, and platform-specific algorithms to ensure relevance. ● Explains why each topic is trending and how it connects to your target audience and content goals. ● Accepts four variables - target audience, industry, content goals, and preferred platforms - so results match your exact content strategy needs. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in trend identification and audience engagement.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive numbered list of trending topics that will boost content engagement for the specified audience and industry. For each topic, include:
- The topic name
- A popularity score (1-10, where 10 is highest)
- A brief explanation of why it's trending and its potential impact

## Context
Analyze current trends by drawing on:
- Social media conversations and viral content patterns
- Industry-specific news and emerging developments
- Platform-specific trending features and algorithms
- Audience behavior and engagement signals

Evaluate each topic's relevance to the content strategy and its potential to resonate with the target audience.

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Industry:** {{industry}}

**Content Goals:** {{content-goals}}

**Preferred Platforms:** {{preferred-platforms}}

## Output
Present as a numbered list. Each entry must include:
1. **[Topic Name]** – Popularity: [X/10] – [2-3 sentence explanation of why it's trending and how it connects to the audience/goals]
```
