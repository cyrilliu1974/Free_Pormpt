# Cinematic Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces striking over-the-shoulder fashion photography featuring subjects in elegant evening wear against refined interior settings. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates dramatic images of a subject looking back over their shoulder, wearing a backless gown with crisscross spaghetti straps in luxurious satin or silk. The scene unfolds in a formal entrance hall with dark wooden doors, ornate trim, and warm cinematic lighting that evokes a vintage, filmic aesthetic. You customize the subject description and garment type through two simple variables, making it perfect for lookbook photography, luxury brand campaigns, editorial fashion content, and boutique marketing materials. Reach for this prompt when you need high-end fashion photography aesthetics without studio costs or professional models. ● Produces over-the-shoulder poses with dramatic backlighting in formal interior settings featuring dark wood doors and ornate architectural details. ● Specifies backless gown construction with crisscross strap details, flowing fabric drape, and elegant styling including updo hair and statement jewelry. ● Delivers moody, warm, soft-focus lighting with a vintage filmic quality suited to evening wear and luxury fashion contexts. ● Generates consistent formal entrance hall environments with glass-paneled doors, gold-framed artwork, and refined molding for sophisticated brand imagery. ## Prompt
