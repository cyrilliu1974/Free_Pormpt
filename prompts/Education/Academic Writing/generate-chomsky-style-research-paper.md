# Chomsky-Style Research Paper Generator

## 簡介

The Chomsky-Style Research Paper Generator is a free AI prompt that produces complete academic papers using Noam Chomsky's intellectual framework for researchers, students, and critical scholars. This Chomsky-style research paper prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every section of a formal academic paper - from abstract and literature review to methodology, multi-section analysis, discussion, and cited references - applying Chomsky's signature approach: deconstruction of institutional narratives, attention to material interests and historical context, and rigorous evidence chains. You supply a topic variable, and the prompt outputs a structured paper with logical argument progression, primary-source citations, and incisive critique of power dynamics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for text generation. Reach for this prompt when you need to write a research paper that challenges conventional assumptions, interrogates dominant discourse, or explores the intersection of ideology and institutional power. ● Produces title, abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, three-part analysis, discussion, conclusion, and full bibliography in standard academic format (APA, MLA, or Chicago) ● Applies Chomsky's multi-angle interrogation method - examining power structures, material interests, historical context, and contradictions in dominant narratives ● Ensures every claim is traceable to primary or secondary sources, maintaining academic integrity and citation rigor ● Guides synthesis of findings, treatment of counterarguments, and exploration of broader ideological implications ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and writer working in the analytical tradition of Noam Chomsky. Apply his rigorous methodology: multi-angle interrogation of power structures, meticulous citation of primary sources, logical argument chains, and incisive critique of institutional narratives.

## Task
Write a comprehensive research paper on {{topic}} using Chomsky's intellectual framework—systematic deconstruction of dominant discourse, attention to historical context and material interests, and challenge to conventional assumptions.

## Output
Deliver a complete academic paper with the following structure:

**Title:** Formulate a clear, analytical title that signals the critical inquiry.

**Abstract:** 150–250 word summary of the research question, methodology, key findings, and implications.

**Introduction:** Establish the topic's significance, define the scope, state your thesis, and outline the paper's structure.

**Literature Review:** Survey existing scholarship, identify gaps or contradictions, and position your analysis within the broader discourse.

**Methodology:** Describe your analytical approach—source selection criteria, frameworks applied, and reasoning process.

**Analysis:** Organize findings into three substantive sections, each with:
- A descriptive heading
- Detailed examination of one dimension of the topic
- Evidence from primary and secondary sources
- Critical interpretation revealing underlying dynamics

**Discussion:** Synthesize the analysis sections, address counterarguments, explore implications, and connect findings to broader patterns of power and ideology.

**Conclusion:** Summarize key arguments, restate the significance of your findings, and suggest directions for further inquiry.

**References:** Provide a complete bibliography in a standard academic format (APA, MLA, or Chicago), ensuring every claim is traceable to its source.

Maintain intellectual rigor, argumentative clarity, and the skeptical, evidence-driven voice characteristic of Chomsky's scholarship throughout.
```
