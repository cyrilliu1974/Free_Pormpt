The Comparative Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude is a free AI prompt that produces structured, in-depth comparisons between any two topics, concepts, or approaches for researchers, analysts, and decision-makers. This comparative analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine similarities, differences, and broader implications using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical clarity. It works by prompting the model to identify high-level and nuanced comparison points, elaborate each with specific examples, and analyze implications from multiple angles. Whether you're comparing business strategies, technologies, methodologies, or theories, the prompt delivers an organized report with an introduction, similarity section, difference section, implication analysis, and actionable conclusion. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.
