# LinkedIn Post Generator for Product Marketing

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Post Generator for Product Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates engaging, strategic LinkedIn content for professionals promoting products or services while building their personal brand. This LinkedIn post prompt for ChatGPT produces five standalone posts (150-250 words each) designed to drive engagement and establish domain expertise. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure logical sentence flow and coherent narratives. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single product or service description into varied content formats including thought leadership pieces, customer stories, industry trend analyses, and behind-the-scenes insights. Each post opens with audience-relevant hooks, weaves in unique selling points naturally, and closes with questions or calls-to-action that encourage meaningful responses. This prompt is ideal for B2B marketers, founders, consultants, and professionals who need consistent, high-quality LinkedIn content that balances promotion with value delivery. ● Produces five complete posts with varied angles: thought leadership, customer stories, trends, and insights ● Structures content using dependency grammar for clarity and logical flow from sentence to sentence ● Includes 3-5 relevant hashtags per post and strategic calls-to-action that invite substantive discussion ● Outputs posts in the optimal 150-250 word range for LinkedIn engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert LinkedIn content strategist specializing in B2B thought leadership and product marketing.

## Task
Create a series of engaging LinkedIn posts that promote the offering while establishing credibility and expertise in the industry. Each post should highlight unique value, reflect core positioning, and drive meaningful conversation.

## Context
**Offering & Market:**
{{product-service-description}}
(Include: what the product/service is, industry context, target audience, unique selling proposition, and company core values)

**Content Requirements:**
- Apply dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically from the previous, building coherent narratives
- Open with hooks that resonate with the target audience's pain points or aspirations
- Weave in unique selling points naturally through stories, insights, or data
- Demonstrate domain expertise through original observations, not generic advice
- Include 3-5 relevant industry hashtags per post
- End with open-ended questions or calls-to-action that invite substantive responses
- Vary post formats: mix thought leadership, customer stories, industry trends, and behind-the-scenes insights

## Output
Provide 5 LinkedIn posts formatted as a numbered list. Each post should:
- Be 150-250 words (optimal LinkedIn length)
- Stand alone as a complete piece of content
- Be clearly labeled (Post 1, Post 2, etc.)
- Include suggested hashtags at the end
```
