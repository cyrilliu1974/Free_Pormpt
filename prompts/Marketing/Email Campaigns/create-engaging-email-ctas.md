# Email CTA Generator for Newsletters

## 簡介

The Email CTA Generator for Newsletters is a free AI prompt that produces compelling call-to-action phrases designed to drive immediate subscriber engagement and conversions. This newsletter CTA prompt for ChatGPT takes your newsletter context and generates 5-10 action-oriented phrases that align with your core message, match your audience's interests, and incorporate proven conversion techniques like urgency and exclusivity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering CTAs that range from direct product pitches to soft-touch engagement asks, giving you a variety of options for A/B testing. Email marketers use it to refresh tired CTAs, test multiple framing approaches in a single campaign, and ensure every newsletter issue includes conversion-optimized action prompts. Reach for this prompt when you need a set of CTAs for an upcoming newsletter send, want to test different urgency levels and tones, or need fresh phrases that speak directly to your subscriber base. ● Produces 5-10 CTAs per run, each one sentence or less and immediately usable in your newsletter layout ● Varies urgency, tone, and framing across the set so you can A/B test and identify your highest-performing option ● Uses strong action verbs and clear language that prompts specific behaviors like clicking, replying, signing up, or purchasing ● Aligns every CTA with your newsletter's theme and the vocabulary that resonates with your target subscribers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing copywriter specializing in high-converting call-to-action phrases.

## Task
Generate 5-10 compelling call-to-action (CTA) phrases for a newsletter. Each CTA must be one sentence or less, action-oriented, and designed to drive immediate subscriber engagement.

## Context
**Newsletter details:**
{{newsletter-context}}

Craft CTAs that:
- Align tightly with the newsletter's core message and theme
- Prompt the specific action you want subscribers to take (clicking links, replying, signing up, purchasing, etc.)
- Use strong action verbs and clear, concise language
- Incorporate urgency or scarcity where appropriate ("limited time," "exclusive," "act now")
- Match the tone and vocabulary that resonates with your target audience's interests and needs
- Are versatile enough to use at multiple points in the newsletter or across related issues

## Output
Deliver 5-10 CTAs as a numbered list. Each CTA should be immediately usable, specific to the newsletter topic, and optimized to maximize click-through and conversion rates.

Variations in urgency, tone, and framing across the set will allow for A/B testing to identify the highest-performing options.
```
