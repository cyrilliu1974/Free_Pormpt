# Press Release Writer in Richard Branson Style

## 簡介

The Press Release Writer in Richard Branson Style is a free AI prompt that generates media-ready announcements in the distinctive voice and strategic style of entrepreneur Richard Branson. This press release prompt for ChatGPT guides you through crafting announcements that mirror Branson's approach: accessible language, compelling narrative structure, and a strategic focus on benefits rather than technical details. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing complete press releases with attention-grabbing headlines, structured details, stakeholder quotes, company background, and clear calls-to-action. Marketing teams use it to announce product launches, partnerships, and company milestones in a voice that generates excitement while maintaining credibility. PR professionals turn to it when they need announcements that appeal to broad audiences without sacrificing strategic messaging. ● Structures every element of a professional press release from headline through contact information ● Applies Branson's signature communication principles: clarity over jargon, benefits over features, excitement with strategic intent ● Includes stakeholder quotes, company background, and actionable next steps formatted for journalists ● Works with any announcement type by accepting custom details through a single variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are crafting a press release in Richard Branson's communication style: clear, concise, strategically messaged to generate excitement and broad appeal.

## Task
Write a media-ready press release that announces {{announcement-details}} following Branson's signature approach—accessible language, compelling narrative, and strategic focus on benefits and implications.

## Output
Structure the press release with these components:

**Headline:** Attention-grabbing, immediate interest

**Subheadline:** Enticing follow-up that deepens intrigue

**Lead Paragraph:** Concise summary answering who, what, when, where, and why

**Key Details:**
1. [Expand on lead with additional context]
2. [Second supporting detail]
3. [Third supporting detail]

**Quote:** Relevant, engaging statement from a key stakeholder that adds credibility and human dimension

**About Section:** Brief background on the company or individuals involved

**Call-to-Action:** Clear next step for readers (visit website, contact for information, etc.)

**Contact Info:**
- Website: [URL]
- Press Contact: [Name]
- Phone: [Number]
- Email: [Address]

## Guidelines
- Avoid jargon and technical language; write for a general audience
- Highlight the most compelling aspects and key benefits
- Maintain logical flow and clear section structure
- Generate excitement while staying strategically focused
```
