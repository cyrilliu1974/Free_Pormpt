# Sales Letter Writer for Direct Response Copy

## 簡介

The Sales Letter Writer for Direct Response Copy is a free AI prompt that produces conversion-focused sales letters for marketers, founders, and copywriters selling products or services. This sales letter prompt for ChatGPT guides Claude, Gemini, or Grok to craft a complete direct-response letter structured around eight essential sections: headline, opening, problem, solution, benefits, proof, offer, and call-to-action. You provide your product or service, target audience, unique selling proposition, primary benefit, and offer details; the AI delivers a logically sequenced sales letter where each sentence builds persuasive momentum using dependency grammar principles. Real-world uses include landing pages, email campaigns, direct mail, webinar scripts, and product launch copy. Reach for this prompt when you need a proven framework to turn features into benefits, address objections, and close with urgency and clarity. ● Delivers eight clearly labeled sections that flow into one another for maximum readability and conversion impact. ● Incorporates credibility markers, social proof, scarcity, and urgency in the appropriate sections of the letter. ● Uses dependency grammar to ensure each sentence logically extends the previous one, making arguments easy to follow. ● Guides the AI to preemptively answer objections and tie benefits directly to the audience's pain points or desires. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert direct response copywriter specializing in persuasive sales letters that drive conversions.

## Task
Write a compelling sales letter for {{product-service}} targeting {{target-audience}}. Structure the letter using dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one for maximum clarity and persuasive impact.

## Context
**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Primary Benefit:** {{main-benefit}}

**Offer Details:** {{offer}}

The sales letter must:
- Open with a powerful headline that immediately captures attention and speaks to the audience's core desire or pain point
- Establish credibility and rapport in the opening paragraphs
- Present a clear value proposition tied to the target audience's specific needs
- Preemptively address common objections
- Incorporate social proof, scarcity, and urgency where appropriate
- Close with a strong, action-oriented call-to-action

## Output
Deliver the sales letter with these clearly labeled sections:

**Headline**

**Opening**

**Problem**

**Solution**

**Benefits**

**Proof**

**Offer**

**Call-to-Action**

Each section should flow naturally into the next, with sentences structured to build persuasive momentum throughout the letter.
```
