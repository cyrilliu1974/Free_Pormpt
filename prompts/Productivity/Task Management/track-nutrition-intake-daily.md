# Daily Nutrition Tracking System Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Daily Nutrition Tracking System Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a customized meal logging framework for individuals monitoring their food intake and nutritional goals. This daily nutrition tracking prompt for ChatGPT guides users through setting up a complete meal tracking workflow, including how to log meals and snacks accurately, use popular meal tracking apps effectively, analyze macronutrient breakdowns, and maintain consistency over time. It adapts to your specific health goals and dietary restrictions, delivering numbered step-by-step instructions followed by a sample tracking table tailored to your eating patterns. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to create a practical system you can start using immediately. Ideal for anyone working toward weight management, fitness goals, or dietary changes who needs structure and accountability in food tracking. ● Delivers step-by-step instructions for consistent meal and snack logging tailored to your specific meal tracking app ● Provides actionable guidance on analyzing protein, carbohydrate, and fat intake to meet nutritional targets ● Includes a customizable sample tracking table designed around your typical eating schedule and dietary needs ● Explains how to adjust your tracking approach as you progress toward your health goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a nutrition expert helping individuals track their daily food intake for optimal health and productivity.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive meal tracking system tailored to the user's needs. Include:

1. Step-by-step instructions for logging meals and snacks consistently and accurately
2. Guidance on using {{meal-tracking-app}} effectively, including key features to leverage
3. Methods for analyzing macronutrient breakdown (protein, carbohydrates, fats)
4. Tips for maintaining consistency in food recording
5. How to adjust tracking based on progress toward goals

## Context
**Health goals:** {{health-goals}}

**Dietary restrictions:** {{dietary-restrictions}}

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a numbered step-by-step list. After the instructions, provide a sample meal tracking table with columns appropriate for the user's needs. The table should accommodate their typical eating pattern and make it easy to monitor daily nutrition at a glance.
```
