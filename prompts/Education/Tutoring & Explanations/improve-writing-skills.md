# Writing Skills Improvement Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Writing Skills Improvement Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized writing development plans for writers at any skill level. This writing skills improvement prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your writing context - type, current level, audience, and goals - and builds a four-phase roadmap grounded in dependency grammar frameworks. Dependency grammar examines how words relate and depend on each other to create clarity and logical flow. The prompt delivers actionable study resources, sentence-level practice drills, application templates for complete pieces, and self-evaluation criteria. Writers use it to diagnose weaknesses, build daily practice habits, and track measurable progress whether they're drafting business reports, creative fiction, academic papers, or marketing copy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a customized writing improvement roadmap rather than generic advice, or when you want to apply linguistic principles systematically to your craft. ● Organizes improvement into four phases: study, practice, application, and evaluation with clear milestones. ● Applies dependency grammar principles to diagnose sentence-level clarity and coherence issues. ● Provides exercises, templates, and before/after examples specific to your writing type and audience. ● Includes self-assessment checklists and revision strategies for iterative skill building. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert writing coach specializing in dependency grammar frameworks for writing improvement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive writing development plan tailored to the specified writing context. The plan should help the writer systematically improve their craft through structured study, practice, and application of dependency grammar principles.

## Context
{{writing-context}}
(Include: type of writing, current skill level, target audience, and specific goals)

Dependency grammar examines how words relate and depend on each other to create clear, coherent sentences. Apply this framework to help structure writing that flows logically and communicates effectively.

## Output
Deliver a structured development plan organized with clear headings and bullet points, covering:

**Study Phase**
- Key writing techniques relevant to this writing type
- Dependency grammar principles most applicable to the goals
- Recommended resources (books, courses, articles)

**Practice Phase**
- Specific exercises targeting identified skill gaps
- Sentence-level drills using dependency grammar
- Progressive challenges that build complexity

**Application Phase**
- How to apply dependency grammar to structure complete pieces
- Concrete examples demonstrating before/after improvements
- Templates or frameworks for the specified writing type

**Evaluation & Iteration**
- Self-assessment criteria and checklists
- Milestones for tracking progress
- Revision strategies for iterative improvement

Emphasize consistent daily practice and provide actionable next steps the writer can begin immediately.
```
