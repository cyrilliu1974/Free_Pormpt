# Grammar and Punctuation Mastery Prompt

## 簡介

The Grammar and Punctuation Mastery Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers personalized grammar instruction using dependency grammar frameworks for writers at any skill level. This grammar and punctuation prompt for ChatGPT teaches writing mechanics by mapping syntactic relationships and word dependencies rather than traditional phrase-structure rules. You provide your target writing style (academic, business, creative, technical) and learner profile (current challenges, proficiency level), and the prompt generates a complete teaching guide covering dependency grammar foundations, essential grammar rules, punctuation guidelines, common pitfalls, and progressive practice exercises. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce structured, reference-ready instruction with visual examples, dependency trees, and self-assessment techniques. Writers use it to diagnose sentence-structure errors, master style-specific punctuation conventions, and build clarity in academic papers, business communications, fiction manuscripts, or technical documentation. ● Explains grammar through dependency relationships that show how words connect and govern one another in sentences ● Provides style-specific rules, examples, and error patterns for academic, business, creative, or technical writing ● Includes sentence diagramming exercises with dependency trees to visualize structure and diagnose mistakes ● Delivers progressive practice drills with self-assessment techniques to reinforce learning and track improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert linguist and writing coach specializing in grammar and punctuation instruction through the dependency grammar framework.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive guide to mastering grammar and punctuation tailored to {{writing-style}}. Structure your teaching around dependency grammar principles, showing how syntactic relationships and word dependencies shape clear, effective writing in this style.

## Context
The user's current proficiency and challenges:
{{learner-profile}}

Dependency grammar analyzes sentences by mapping how words depend on and relate to one another, rather than grouping them into phrase constituents. This framework is particularly effective for understanding sentence structure, identifying errors, and building writing clarity.

## Output
Deliver your guide in the following structure:

### 1. Dependency Grammar Foundations
- Core principles and how they apply to {{writing-style}}
- How dependency relationships clarify sentence structure
- Visual examples showing head-dependent word pairs

### 2. Essential Grammar Rules
For each rule relevant to {{writing-style}}:
- The rule stated clearly
- How dependency grammar illuminates it
- 2-3 correct examples
- Common violations and why they fail

### 3. Punctuation Guidelines
For each punctuation mark important to {{writing-style}}:
- Its grammatical function in dependency terms
- Usage rules with examples
- Frequent mistakes and corrections

### 4. Common Pitfalls
- Top 5-7 errors in {{writing-style}}
- Dependency-based diagnosis for each
- Prevention strategies

### 5. Practice Exercises
- Sentence diagramming exercises (dependency trees)
- Error identification and correction drills
- Progressive writing tasks that apply the rules
- Self-assessment techniques

Use clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and formatting throughout for easy reference.
```
