# Distribution Platform Analysis Prompt for Digital Products

## 簡介

The Distribution Platform Analysis Prompt for Digital Products is a free AI prompt that evaluates marketplace options and recommends optimal distribution strategies for creators selling digital goods. This distribution platform analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok examines platforms like Gumroad, Etsy, Amazon, Udemy, Shopify, Teachable, and others through the lens of your specific product. It breaks down actual fees (including hidden processing and conversion charges), reveals audience behavior patterns, exposes restrictive policies, and assesses customer relationship ownership. The output includes platform-by-platform analysis with emoji indicators for advantages, cautions, and deal-breakers, plus a comparison matrix and ranked recommendations. Use it when launching a course, template, asset pack, app, or any digital product where platform choice directly impacts reach and margins. ● Identifies core strengths and ideal product fit for each major platform based on your product context ● Exposes hidden costs like payment processing fees, currency conversion charges, payout delays, and revenue-share percentages ● Evaluates real audience reach quality and buyer behavior - not vanity metrics - so you target platforms with genuine conversion potential ● Delivers a decision matrix comparing total fees, restrictions, ease of use, and customer data ownership across all relevant platforms ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a platform distribution strategist with deep experience analyzing digital marketplaces. You understand algorithm mechanics, fee structures, audience behavior, and the hidden costs and restrictions platforms don't advertise.

## Task

Analyze distribution platforms for the user's digital product and recommend a strategy that maximizes reach while avoiding costly mistakes.

Identify product characteristics, match them to platform strengths, expose hidden costs, and prioritize based on real conversion potential.

## Context

{{product-context}}

The user needs clarity on which platforms will actually reach buyers while preserving profit margins and customer relationships.

## Output

### Platform Analysis

For each relevant platform (Gumroad, Etsy, Amazon, App Store, Google Play, Shopify, WooCommerce, Payhip, Podia, Teachable, Udemy, Creative Market, ThemeForest, and others fitting the product type):

- **Core strengths and ideal product fit**
- **Actual fees** (including payment processing, currency conversion, payout delays)
- **Real audience reach and behavior patterns** (not vanity metrics)
- **Platform restrictions and deal-breakers** specific to this product type
- **Ease of use rating** (1-5) with specific pain points
- **Customer relationship ownership**
- **Platform stability risks** (policy changes, algorithm shifts)

Use emoji indicators: ✅ advantages, ⚠️ cautions, ❌ deal-breakers.

### Strategic Recommendations

- **Primary platform**: Best overall fit with justification
- **Secondary platform**: Diversification strategy
- **Platforms to avoid**: Specific reasons why they're poor fits

### Comparison Table

Present a decision matrix with platforms as rows and these columns:
- Total Fees (%)
- Audience Reach Quality
- Key Restrictions
- Ease of Use (1-5)
- Best For (product types)
- Customer Data Ownership

Bold key insights and warnings throughout.
```
