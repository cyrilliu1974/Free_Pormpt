# Monthly Newsletter Email Template Generator

## 簡介

The Monthly Newsletter Email Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates fully structured, customer-centric newsletters for any business type and audience segment. This monthly newsletter prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete email structure including an attention-grabbing subject line, warm introduction, three industry-relevant news items, two special promotions with value propositions, two customer testimonials, three expert tips, a clear call-to-action, and a professional closing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and follows best practices for customer engagement and conversion. Whether you're a SaaS company writing to enterprise decision-makers or a local gym reaching fitness enthusiasts, the prompt adapts tone, content themes, and advice to your specific business context and audience pain points. ● Outputs eight distinct newsletter sections from subject line through sign-off, ready to customize and send ● Adapts voice, content themes, and expert advice to match your business type and customer demographics ● Includes social proof and actionable tips that build credibility and keep readers engaged month after month ● Maintains a conversational, value-driven tone that encourages interaction rather than sounding like a sales pitch ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a newsletter copywriter and content strategist specializing in customer-centric business communications.

## Task
Write an engaging monthly newsletter for {{business-type}} targeting {{customer-segment}}. The newsletter should build connection with readers through relevant news, promotions, social proof, and actionable advice.

## Context
The newsletter will be sent to existing and prospective customers. Content should be conversational, value-driven, and encourage interaction with the business.

## Output
Deliver a complete newsletter with the following structure:

**Subject Line:** An attention-grabbing subject line

**Intro Paragraph:**
- Warm greeting
- Brief business update or seasonal message
- Preview of what's inside

**Latest News:**
- 3 relevant news items (industry trends, company updates, community involvement)

**Special Promotions:**
- 2 current offers with clear details and value proposition

**Customer Testimonials:**
- 2 authentic testimonials that demonstrate results or satisfaction

**Expert Tips:**
- 3 actionable tips relevant to {{customer-segment}} needs

**Call to Action:**
- Clear CTA message
- Specific button text

**Outro Paragraph:**
- Closing message that reinforces connection
- Professional sign-off

Use a conversational tone throughout. Ensure all content aligns with {{business-type}} and speaks directly to the interests and pain points of {{customer-segment}}.
```
