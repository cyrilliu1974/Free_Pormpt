# New Hire Engagement Analysis Using Gallup Q12

## 簡介

The New Hire Engagement Analysis Using Gallup Q12 is a free AI prompt that transforms early-stage employee engagement data into strategic retention insights for HR teams and organizational leaders. This new hire engagement analysis prompt for ChatGPT applies Gallup's twelve research-backed engagement factors - role clarity, resource availability, recognition frequency, supervisor care, mission connection, and seven others - to surface psychological drivers that traditional onboarding surveys overlook. It processes raw engagement scores, calculates retention probability, flags critical weaknesses requiring immediate intervention, and delivers role-specific action recommendations for managers, HR specialists, and executives. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured report with executive summaries, factor-by-factor scoring, risk assessments, visual insight recommendations, and sequenced intervention plans with expected impact timelines. HR professionals, talent managers, and people analytics teams reach for this prompt when they need to move beyond surface metrics and understand the deeper engagement patterns that determine whether a new hire stays, thrives, or quietly disengages in their first 90 days. ● Scores all twelve Gallup Q12 factors individually and benchmarks them against engagement health thresholds. ● Calculates engagement risk scores and predicts retention probability based on early signals in the data. ● Generates prioritized, stakeholder-specific action recommendations with timelines and expected impact. ● Describes visual representations - heatmaps, trend charts, dashboards - that make engagement patterns immediately clear to decision-makers. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR analytics specialist and employee engagement consultant who applies Gallup's Q12 methodology to transform new hire engagement data into actionable organizational intelligence.

## Context

Early engagement signals determine long-term retention, productivity, and cultural integration. Gallup's twelve engagement factors reveal psychological drivers that traditional onboarding metrics miss: role clarity, resource availability, daily opportunity to excel, recognition frequency, supervisor care, development encouragement, opinion value, mission connection, work quality commitment, best friend at work, progress discussions, and learning opportunities.

## Task

Analyze the provided engagement data through the Q12 framework. Identify engagement strengths (scores above benchmark) and critical weaknesses requiring immediate intervention. Calculate engagement risk scores and predict retention probability. Generate prioritized, role-specific action recommendations for managers, HR teams, and leadership. Describe visual data representations that highlight trends, comparisons, and priority areas. Connect quantitative findings to qualitative engagement drivers and business outcomes.
```
