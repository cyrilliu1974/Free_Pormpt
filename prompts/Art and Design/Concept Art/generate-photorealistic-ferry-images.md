# Photorealistic Cinematic Establishing Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Cinematic Establishing Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that produces film-quality wide establishing shots with early morning lighting and cinematic depth for visual storytellers and concept artists. This photorealistic image prompt for Midjourney uses ARRI Alexa 65 camera simulation parameters to render any subject in any setting with the production value of a feature film establishing shot. The prompt combines high photorealism settings (--stylize 400, --sw 500) with controlled variation (--chaos 33) and a locked style reference to maintain consistent cinematic color grading and mood across generations. Real use cases include pre-visualization for film and television, concept art for game environments, architectural visualization in narrative context, and portfolio pieces for cinematographers exploring location aesthetics. Reach for this prompt when you need broadcast-quality wide shots that convey time, place, and atmosphere in a single frame, whether you're storyboarding a feature, pitching a location concept, or building a visual narrative. ● Two variables (subject, setting) let you generate any scene from passenger ferries in open sea to urban skylines or wilderness landscapes ● ARRI Alexa 65 simulation with locked style reference produces consistent film color science and dynamic range across all outputs ● 3:4 aspect ratio and wide establishing shot framing optimized for vertical cinematic composition and environmental storytelling ● Early morning lighting baked into the prompt ensures golden hour warmth, soft shadows, and atmospheric depth in every generation ## Prompt
