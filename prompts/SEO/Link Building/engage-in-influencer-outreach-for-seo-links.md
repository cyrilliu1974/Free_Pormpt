# Influencer Outreach Email Template for SEO Backlinks

## 簡介

The Influencer Outreach Email Template for SEO Backlinks is a free AI prompt that writes personalized, response-optimized outreach emails for SEO professionals seeking backlink collaborations. This influencer outreach prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete email template with a personalized subject line, research-backed opening hook, clear value proposition, specific collaboration proposal, and actionable call-to-action. It uses dependency grammar principles to ensure every sentence flows logically without nested clauses that dilute your core offer. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, keeping emails under 200 words while maximizing clarity and response rates. Real use cases include guest post pitches, resource page mentions, content partnerships, and expert roundup requests in any industry. Reach for this template when you need to move beyond generic link-building emails and craft outreach that demonstrates genuine familiarity with the recipient's audience and content. ● Produces five distinct email sections: subject line, personalization hook, value proposition, collaboration proposal, and call-to-action ● Enforces clear dependency-focused sentence structure that avoids confusing nested clauses ● Guides users to articulate mutual benefits and concrete deliverables rather than vague partnership requests ● Keeps total email length under 200 words to respect recipient time and improve engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert outreach specialist focused on securing high-quality backlinks through strategic influencer partnerships.

## Task
Write a compelling outreach email template using clear, direct sentence structure that maximizes response rates. The email should identify mutual benefits, demonstrate genuine familiarity with the recipient's work, and propose a concrete backlink collaboration opportunity.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include:
- Your industry and website
- What makes your content/offering valuable and link-worthy
- The type of influencer or site owner you're targeting
- Your preferred collaboration approach (guest post, resource mention, content partnership, etc.)

## Output
Structure the email template with these sections:

**Subject Line:** [Attention-grabbing, personalized]

**Personalization Hook:** Show you've researched their content/audience (2-3 sentences)

**Value Proposition:** Explain why collaboration benefits them and their audience (2-3 sentences)

**Collaboration Proposal:** Specific backlink opportunity with clear deliverables (3-4 sentences)

**Call-to-Action:** Simple next step (1-2 sentences)

Keep the full email under 200 words. Use clear, dependency-focused syntax—each sentence should build logically on the previous one, avoiding nested clauses that obscure your core offer.
```
