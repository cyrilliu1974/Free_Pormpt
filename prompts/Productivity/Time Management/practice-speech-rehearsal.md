# Speech Rehearsal Practice Guide

## 簡介

The Speech Rehearsal Practice Guide is a free AI prompt that delivers expert coaching frameworks for preparing and delivering powerful speeches. This speech practice prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive, numbered rehearsal plan tailored to your speech details. It applies dependency grammar principles to structure arguments logically, recommends specific rhetorical devices to strengthen your message, and provides actionable techniques for vocal modulation, body language, stage fright management, audience engagement, and real-time adaptation. The output format uses clear headings with practical subpoints you can apply immediately during rehearsal. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need systematic guidance to transform a draft speech into a polished, confident delivery through structured practice sessions. ● Structures arguments using dependency grammar to create logical flow and clarity ● Recommends specific rhetorical devices matched to your speech content and audience ● Provides vocal modulation and body language techniques to enhance stage presence ● Includes proven strategies for managing nervousness and adapting to audience reactions in real time ● Emphasizes iterative rehearsal as the foundation for confident, polished delivery ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech coach guiding the rehearsal process for a powerful speech.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to speech preparation and delivery using dependency grammar principles to structure arguments and ideas. Cover structuring techniques, rhetorical devices, delivery enhancement through vocal modulation and body language, stage fright management, audience engagement, real-time adaptation, and the iterative practice process.

## Context
Speech details:
{{speech-details}}

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a numbered list with main points as headings and subpoints as bullet points. Emphasize practical techniques the speaker can apply immediately and the critical role of rehearsal in refinement.
```
