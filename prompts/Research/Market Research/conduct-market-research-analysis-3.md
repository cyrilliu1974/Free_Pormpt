# Market Research Analysis Prompt for Product Launch

## 簡介

The Market Research Analysis Prompt for Product Launch is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive feasibility reports for businesses evaluating new product opportunities. This market research prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through an eight-step analytical framework covering product positioning, demand assessment, customer segmentation, competitive landscape mapping, pricing strategy justification, and profitability projections. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to generate executive summaries, market analysis sections, competitive positioning maps, financial viability assessments, and SWOT analyses. Product managers, entrepreneurs, and marketing strategists use it to evaluate whether a product concept has sufficient market demand, understand target customer profiles, identify competitive threats and opportunities, and forecast revenue potential before committing resources to development. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured market viability assessment that moves beyond intuition to deliver evidence-based launch recommendations. ● Evaluates market demand using trend indicators, growth trajectory analysis, and addressable market sizing ● Profiles target customer segments with demographic, psychographic, and behavioral pattern breakdowns ● Maps competitive positioning against direct and indirect rivals to clarify differentiation opportunities ● Projects profitability with break-even analysis, margin estimates, and ROI calculations based on realistic sales assumptions ● Delivers a clear feasibility verdict with prioritized recommendations for product development and marketing strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a market researcher conducting a comprehensive feasibility analysis for a new product launch.

## Task
Analyze the market viability of the specified product by evaluating demand, customer segments, competitive positioning, and profitability potential. Deliver a structured report with data-driven conclusions and actionable recommendations.

## Context
Product and market details:
{{product-and-market-context}}

Include: product description and unique selling proposition, target market segments, production costs, pricing strategy, key direct and indirect competitors, and relevant market trends.

## Method
1. **Product positioning**: Define the product's features, USP, and the problem it solves for customers
2. **Demand analysis**: Assess market size, growth trajectory, and current demand using available market data and trend indicators
3. **Customer segmentation**: Identify and profile target customer segments by demographics, psychographics, and behavioral patterns
4. **Market trends**: Evaluate technological shifts, consumer behavior changes, and industry dynamics affecting demand
5. **Competitive landscape**: Map direct and indirect competitors, their market positions, strengths, weaknesses, and your product's differentiation
6. **Pricing strategy**: Justify the proposed pricing based on costs, perceived value, competitive benchmarks, and customer willingness to pay
7. **Profitability projection**: Estimate revenue potential, margins, break-even analysis, and ROI based on production costs, pricing, and realistic sales volume assumptions
8. **SWOT analysis**: Systematically identify Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats in the current market context

## Output
Deliver a structured market research report containing:

**Executive Summary**
- Product overview and USP
- Key feasibility findings

**Market Analysis**
- Demand assessment and market size
- Target customer profiles
- Market trends and drivers

**Competitive Analysis**
- Competitor mapping and positioning
- Differentiation strategy

**Financial Viability**
- Pricing rationale
- Profitability projections with key assumptions

**SWOT Analysis**
- Comprehensive four-quadrant assessment

**Conclusions and Recommendations**
- Clear feasibility verdict
- Prioritized actions for product development, go-to-market strategy, and marketing approach
```
