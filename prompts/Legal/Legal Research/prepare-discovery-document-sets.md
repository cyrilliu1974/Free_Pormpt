# Discovery Document Preparation Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Discovery Document Preparation Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, legally sound procedures for preparing discovery materials in litigation matters. This discovery document preparation prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured, six-part guide covering document review and categorization, labeling and indexing protocols, redaction of privileged or sensitive information, inventory creation, cover letter drafting, and quality control procedures. Legal professionals input their specific case details - including jurisdiction, case type, timeline constraints, and evidence types - and receive actionable steps tailored to both electronic and physical document handling. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring that chain of custody, confidentiality, and procedural compliance are addressed at every stage. Reach for this prompt when preparing discovery responses, organizing document productions, or establishing internal workflows for litigation teams that handle complex or high-volume evidence. ● Produces numbered, structured guidance across document review, labeling, redaction, indexing, inventory, and cover letters ● Addresses chain of custody, privilege logs, confidentiality protocols, and timeline management ● Adapts output to the jurisdiction, case type, and evidence format specified in case details ● Ensures compliance with legal requirements and best practices for both paper and electronic discovery ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal document specialist preparing discovery materials for litigation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for preparing discovery documents that covers organization, labeling, redaction, indexing, and presentation. The guide must be practical, legally sound, and address both electronic and physical evidence handling.

## Context
**Case details:**
{{case-details}}

The guide should account for legal requirements, confidentiality and privilege concerns, chain of custody protocols, and efficient document management practices. Address the specific needs of the jurisdiction and case type, and ensure the process can be completed within the timeline constraints.

## Output
Deliver your response as a numbered list with clear headings for each main section:
1. Document Review and Categorization
2. Labeling and Indexing
3. Redaction of Sensitive Information
4. Inventory Creation
5. Cover Letter Preparation
6. Quality Control and Final Review

For each section, provide actionable steps, best practices, and specific guidance relevant to the case details provided.
```
