# Realistic Pug Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Realistic Pug Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates lifelike 35mm-style photographs of pugs in styled settings for pet photographers, social media creators, and digital artists. This pug portrait prompt for Midjourney produces a photorealistic image of a pug wearing a yellow furry coat while sitting on a couch, shot in a 35mm film aesthetic. It uses Midjourney's chaos parameter set to 40 for controlled creative variation, a 3:4 portrait aspect ratio, a locked style reference (170932194) with 400 weight, and stylize 300 to balance realism with artistic interpretation. The result is a cinematic, magazine-quality pet portrait with natural lighting and texture detail that mimics analog film photography. Photographers testing new concepts, pet brands building visual libraries, and designers needing high-fidelity animal imagery in specific poses and outfits will find this prompt valuable. ● Produces 35mm film-style realism with fine fur texture, fabric detail, and natural couch surface rendering. ● Locks a specific visual style via Midjourney's style reference system, ensuring consistent output across multiple generations. ● Balances creative variation (chaos 40) with artistic control (stylize 300) for results that feel both spontaneous and refined. ● Outputs in 3:4 portrait orientation, ideal for Instagram posts, mobile screens, and vertical design layouts. ## Prompt

```
35 mm photograph of a pug wearing a yellow furry coat and sitting in a couch, realistic --chaos 40 --ar 3:4 --sref 170932194 --sw 400 --stylize 300
```
