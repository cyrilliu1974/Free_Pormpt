This high-speed drink photography prompt for Midjourney produces ultra-detailed images featuring cream splashes frozen mid-air, flying toppings, and sci-fi-inspired neon glow effects under dramatic studio lighting. The prompt delivers professional food photography aesthetics in 16:9 aspect ratio with sharp focus and commercial-grade detail.
