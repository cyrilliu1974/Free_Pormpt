# Social Media Hook Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Hook Generator is a free AI prompt that creates ten attention-grabbing opening lines for any completed social media post. This social media hook prompt for ChatGPT reads your finished post copy and returns ten drastically different hooks, each designed to stop the scroll and earn the next sentence. It alternates between one-line zingers and three-line openers, all written in simple conversational English a middle schooler can understand. Content creators, social media managers, and marketers use it to test multiple angles, emotions, and structures before publishing on LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, or any text-based platform. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a clean numbered list with no jargon, no questions, no emojis, and no title case. Reach for this prompt whenever you have a strong post but know the first line will make or break its performance. ● Produces ten hooks with drastically different angles, structures, and emotional tones so you can A/B test what resonates. ● Alternates one-liners with three-line openers, giving you format variety for platform algorithms and reader preferences. ● Enforces plain conversational English with no jargon, questions, emojis, or fancy words that dilute impact. ● Outputs a clean numbered list you can copy, paste, and swap into your post without reformatting. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write social media hooks that stop the scroll.

## Context

The user has a finished post and needs a hook—the opening line or opening three lines—that earns the next sentence.

## Task

Read the post below and write 10 hooks for it.

**Post:**
{{post-copy}}

## Rules

- Simple, conversational English a middle schooler can read
- No jargon, fancy words, questions, or emojis
- No title case—write as normal sentences
- Alternate between one-liners and three-liners (three-liners use line breaks between each line)
- Keep hooks short and punchy; split long sentences into two lines
- Make all 10 drastically different in angle, structure, and emotion
- Each hook should create a feeling or reaction that stops the scroll

## Output Format

10 numbered hooks, each on its own line. Three-liners have a blank line between each of their three lines. No XML tags, no special formatting, no labels.
```
