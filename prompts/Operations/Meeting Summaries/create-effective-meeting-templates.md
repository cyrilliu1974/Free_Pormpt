# Daily Stand-Up Meeting Template Generator

## 簡介

The Daily Stand-Up Meeting Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized stand-up meeting frameworks for project managers and team leads. This stand-up meeting template prompt for ChatGPT takes your team and project context and produces a complete, reusable template covering meeting overview, pre-meeting preparation, structured agenda with individual updates, challenge discussion, priority alignment, and post-meeting follow-up. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to design templates that enable teams to concisely review progress, identify blockers, and align on next steps. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish a consistent structure for daily team check-ins that maximize productivity and accountability while respecting everyone's time. ● Produces complete templates with meeting overview, preparation checklist, agenda structure, and post-meeting follow-up workflows ● Includes a framework for individual updates covering progress, blockers, support needs, and next steps with suggested time-boxes ● Provides collaborative troubleshooting processes for identifying common challenges and defining action items with clear owners ● Delivers templates formatted for immediate copying, customization, and reuse across recurring team meetings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced project manager who designs effective meeting templates that maximize team productivity and accountability.

## Task
Create a reusable daily stand-up meeting template tailored to the following context:

{{team-and-project-context}}

The template should enable the team to concisely review progress, identify and troubleshoot obstacles, and align on priorities and next steps.

## Output
Provide a complete stand-up meeting template with the following structure:

**Meeting Overview**
- Meeting name, recommended duration, and purpose statement

**Pre-Meeting Preparation**
- What individual team members should prepare before the meeting
- Key data or metrics to review in advance

**Agenda Structure**
1. **Opening** – How to start the meeting effectively
2. **Individual Updates** – Framework for each team member to share:
   - Progress since last meeting
   - Current challenges or blockers
   - Support needed from others
   - Planned next steps
   - Suggested time-box per person
3. **Challenges Discussion** – Process for:
   - Identifying common themes across updates
   - Collaborative troubleshooting
   - Defining clear action items with owners
4. **Priorities Alignment** – Method to:
   - Summarize top priorities for the day/sprint
   - Confirm team alignment and understanding
5. **Closing** – How to wrap up and confirm next meeting

**Post-Meeting Follow-Up**
- How to communicate action items to the team
- Process for updating project tracking tools or documentation

Format the template so it can be easily copied, customized, and reused. Focus on clarity, efficiency, and accountability.
```
