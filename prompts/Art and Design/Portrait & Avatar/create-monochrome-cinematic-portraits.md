# Monochrome Cinematic Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Monochrome Cinematic Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-contrast black-and-white portrait photography with dramatic lighting and atmospheric depth for photographers, content creators, and visual artists. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney produces side-profile close-ups with soft directional lighting, visible water droplets on skin and hair, and deep bokeh backgrounds. It accepts three variables - subject description, weather condition, and mood - to generate editorial-quality monochrome images that mimic film photography aesthetics. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering sharp facial focus with blurred backgrounds and gentle highlight-shadow interplay ideal for magazine spreads, portfolio work, and brand storytelling. Reach for this prompt when you need evocative, story-driven portrait imagery without arranging studio shoots or hiring models. ● Produces side-profile close-ups with wet hair, glistening skin, and visible water droplets for authentic weather-worn realism. ● Applies soft moody directional lighting that creates gentle highlights and shadows across the subject's face and features. ● Delivers sharp focus on facial details with deep bokeh backgrounds, mimicking shallow depth-of-field from professional prime lenses. ● Customizable subject, weather condition, and mood variables let you tailor the emotional tone and narrative context of each portrait. ## Prompt

```
A dramatic black-and-white close-up portrait of {{subject-description}} standing in {{weather-condition}}. Side profile facing left, calm and pensive expression. Long wavy wet hair falling forward, skin glistening with visible droplets, soft natural makeup with defined lashes, sleeveless top minimal visible. Soft moody directional lighting creating gentle highlights and shadows. Dark cinematic high-contrast monochrome aesthetic. Sharp focus on the subject's face, blurred dark background with deep bokeh. {{mood}} atmosphere.
```
