# Target Audience Fit Analysis Prompt for Product Strategy

## 簡介

The Target Audience Fit Analysis Prompt for Product Strategy is a free AI prompt that helps product strategists assess whether a proposed product or service aligns with a specific target market and delivers actionable go/no-go recommendations. This target audience fit analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a four-part evaluation framework: identifying key audience characteristics and pain points, analyzing how well your offering addresses their needs, surfacing optimization opportunities to improve fit, and providing a clear recommendation on whether to pursue development. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured markdown reports with bulleted insights and section headings. Teams use it before committing resources to new products, when considering pivots, or when refining positioning for an existing service. The prompt is designed for product managers, strategists, founders, and market researchers who need a systematic way to validate product-market fit before development begins. ● Identifies audience characteristics, needs, and pain points most relevant to fit decisions ● Analyzes alignment gaps between your offering and real market requirements ● Brainstorms concrete optimization or pivot strategies to improve market match ● Delivers a justified go/no-go recommendation in clean markdown format ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a product strategist evaluating whether a proposed offering fits a target market.

## Task
Assess the fit between {{product-or-service}} and {{target-audience}}, then recommend whether to pursue development.

## Context
Your analysis will inform a go/no-go decision. Focus on characteristics, needs, and pain points most relevant to product-market fit.

## Output
Deliver your assessment in four sections:

### 1. Target Audience Analysis
Bulleted list of key characteristics, needs, and pain points relevant to this offering.

### 2. Product/Service Alignment
Analyze how well the proposed offering serves the audience. Identify where it matches their needs and where it may fall short.

### 3. Optimization Opportunities
Brainstorm ways to improve, modify, or pivot the offering to better address audience needs and pain points.

### 4. Recommendation
Provide a clear go/no-go recommendation with justification based on the analysis above.

Use markdown formatting with headings and bullet points for readability.
```
