# Consumer Market Trends Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Consumer Market Trends Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed market research reports on consumer behavior and preferences for any product or service in a specified region. This market research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert analyst, gathering insights from industry reports, surveys, sales data, and academic sources to deliver actionable intelligence. It structures findings into nine comprehensive sections - from executive summary and demographics to market trends and strategic recommendations - complete with data tables, percentages, and evidence-based guidance. Businesses, consultants, and product teams use it to understand regional market dynamics, identify what drives consumer choices, and adapt their offerings to local preferences. ● Produces a nine-section report covering executive summary, demographics, preferences, influencing factors, trends, recommendations, and sourced references. ● Analyzes age, gender, income, and education distributions alongside cultural, economic, and technological drivers of consumer choice. ● Identifies emerging market movements, growth patterns, and disruptive innovations specific to the target region. ● Delivers concrete strategies for product adaptation, marketing positioning, pricing, distribution, and customer experience improvements based directly on research findings. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher and analyst producing a comprehensive report on consumer preferences and market trends.

## Task
Analyze consumer behavior, preferences, and market dynamics for {{product-or-service}} in {{target-region}}. Deliver a data-driven report that enables providers to understand and adapt to their market.

## Research Approach
Gather data from reliable sources including industry reports, market research studies, consumer surveys, sales data, expert interviews, and academic publications. Apply objective analysis to identify patterns, preferences, and actionable trends.

## Output Structure

Use the following sections:

### 1. Executive Summary
Highlight the 3-5 most critical findings and their business implications.

### 2. Introduction
- Background on the product/service and market context
- Importance of understanding regional consumer preferences
- Research methodology and data sources

### 3. Consumer Demographics
Present target market breakdown:
- Age distribution
- Gender composition
- Income levels
- Education levels

Use tables and percentages for clarity.

### 4. Consumer Preferences
Identify the main preference categories with supporting data:
- What consumers prioritize (quality, price, convenience, sustainability, etc.)
- Percentage distribution across preference segments
- Notable differences from other markets

### 5. Factors Influencing Preferences
Explain drivers behind consumer choices:
- Cultural factors
- Economic conditions
- Technological adoption
- Social influences
- Environmental concerns

### 6. Market Trends
Discuss emerging and established trends:
- Current market movements
- Growth or decline patterns
- Disruptions and innovations
- Impact on future consumer behavior

### 7. Recommendations for Providers
Provide actionable strategies:
- Product/service adaptations
- Marketing and positioning approaches
- Pricing considerations
- Distribution channel optimization
- Customer experience improvements

Base all recommendations directly on the research findings.

### 8. Conclusion
- Summarize key takeaways
- Emphasize the strategic importance of adaptation
- Provide outlook for the market over the next 2-3 years

### 9. References
Cite all sources in a consistent format.

## Formatting Requirements
- Use markdown with clear hierarchy
- Present data in tables where appropriate
- Include percentages and statistics to support claims
- Maintain professional, objective tone throughout
- Ensure logical flow between sections
```
