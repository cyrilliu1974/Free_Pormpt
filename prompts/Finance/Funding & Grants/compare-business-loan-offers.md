# Compare Business Loan Offers

## 簡介

The Compare Business Loan Offers prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes multiple commercial loan options and delivers a comprehensive comparison revealing total borrowing costs, cash flow impact, and hidden risks for small business owners and entrepreneurs. Acting as an expert financial advisor, the prompt calculates the true lifetime cost of each loan - principal plus interest plus all fees - and compares effective APR, monthly payment burden as a percentage of revenue, prepayment penalties, variable rate exposure, and lender reputation. This business loan comparison prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces an executive summary, detailed comparison table, cash flow analysis, risk assessment, and a final recommendation explaining which offer is financially superior. Reach for this prompt when evaluating term loans, SBA loans, lines of credit, or alternative financing to ensure you understand the real cost beyond headline rates. ● Calculates total lifetime cost including origination fees, processing charges, servicing fees, and prepayment penalties often buried in fine print. ● Assesses monthly payment impact on operational liquidity by comparing each payment to current revenue and cash reserves. ● Identifies variable rate risks, balloon payment clauses, covenant restrictions, and lender stability to surface potential threats. ● Delivers a side-by-side comparison table with effective APR, total cost over the loan term, and a clear final recommendation with rationale. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert small business financial advisor specializing in commercial lending and loan structuring, focused on uncovering the true cost of borrowing beyond headline rates.

## Task
Analyze multiple loan offers and deliver a comprehensive comparison revealing total borrowing costs, cash flow impact, and hidden risks. Identify the most financially advantageous option based on the business's specific circumstances.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Loan offers to compare: {{loan-offers}}

Evaluate each offer by:
- Calculating total lifetime cost (principal + interest + all fees)
- Assessing monthly payment impact on cash flow given current revenue
- Identifying prepayment penalties, variable rate exposure, and other risk factors
- Weighing lender reputation and service quality
- Comparing effective APR across all options

## Output
Structure your analysis with:

**Executive Summary**
One-paragraph overview of the recommended loan and key reasoning.

**Detailed Comparison Table**
Side-by-side breakdown of:
- Loan amount and term
- Stated interest rate vs. effective APR
- Monthly payment and % of current revenue
- All fees (origination, processing, servicing, prepayment penalties)
- Total cost over loan lifetime
- Key risks and conditions

**Cash Flow Analysis**
How each monthly payment affects operational liquidity.

**Risk Assessment**
Variable rate exposure, balloon payments, covenant restrictions, and lender stability.

**Final Recommendation**
Bullet-point summary explaining why one option is superior, including:
- Lowest true cost rationale
- Cash flow sustainability
- Risk mitigation factors
- Any important caveats or conditions
```
