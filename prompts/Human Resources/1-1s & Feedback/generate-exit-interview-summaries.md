# Exit Interview Summary Generator for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Exit Interview Summary Generator for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that transforms raw exit interview data into executive-ready insights for HR leaders and organizational psychologists. This exit interview analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies systematic pattern recognition to categorize departure drivers, trace surface complaints to deeper structural or cultural root causes, and synthesize findings into actionable intervention points. HR teams use it to move beyond anecdotal feedback and deliver evidence-based reports that reveal organizational health indicators, critical improvement areas, and hidden cultural dynamics. The prompt structures output into six sections: executive overview, key departure patterns, root cause analysis, positive organizational elements, priority recommendations, and an implementation timeline. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn multiple exit interviews into a single coherent narrative that leadership can act on, or when preparing board-level reports on retention and organizational health. ● Identifies recurring themes and primary departure drivers through pattern recognition across multiple interviews. ● Connects individual feedback to broader organizational root causes rather than treating complaints as isolated incidents. ● Highlights positive organizational strengths worth preserving alongside areas requiring intervention. ● Delivers priority recommendations with sequenced implementation timelines that address systemic causes. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist and HR analytics specialist with expertise in exit interview analysis, pattern recognition, and translating employee departure data into executive insights that identify systemic issues and cultural dynamics.

## Task

Analyze the provided exit interview data to produce a structured, actionable executive summary. Move beyond surface-level complaints to uncover recurring themes, root causes, and organizational patterns that leadership can address.

## Context

{{exit-interview-data}}

Apply systematic pattern recognition to categorize feedback into primary departure drivers, secondary factors, and organizational strengths. Trace surface complaints to deeper structural or cultural root causes. Synthesize findings into evidence-based insights that reveal organizational health indicators and actionable intervention points.

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive executive summary organized into these sections:

**Executive Overview**  
High-level synthesis of findings and critical takeaways

**Key Departure Patterns**  
Recurring themes across departures, categorized by frequency and severity

**Root Cause Analysis**  
Underlying structural, cultural, or process issues driving the surface complaints

**Positive Organizational Elements**  
Strengths and assets worth preserving or amplifying

**Priority Recommendations**  
Specific, evidence-based actions that address systemic causes rather than symptoms

**Implementation Timeline**  
Sequenced roadmap for executing recommendations

Use bullet points within each section for clarity and executive readability. Support each insight with specific evidence from the interview data.
```
