# Post-Onboarding Review Template Generator

## 簡介

The Post-Onboarding Review Template Generator is a free AI prompt that produces three stakeholder-specific evaluation templates to measure onboarding program effectiveness for HR teams, managers, and employees. This post-onboarding review prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates HR templates that assess systemic gaps, manager templates that evaluate team integration and performance, and new hire templates that capture honest feedback. Each template progresses through Kirkpatrick's four levels - Reaction, Learning, Behavior, and Results - mixing quantitative rating scales with qualitative questions. The output includes recommended timing (30/60/90 days), review frequency guidance, and data interpretation notes to help organizations identify gaps between training objectives and real-world application. Use it when launching a new onboarding program, conducting periodic audits, or responding to retention challenges. ● Generates three interconnected templates (HR, manager, new hire) that align questions across all stakeholder perspectives for triangulated insights. ● Organizes every template by Kirkpatrick's four levels, ensuring progression from immediate satisfaction to long-term business outcomes. ● Includes review timing recommendations, question sequencing logic, and interpretation notes to turn feedback into actionable program improvements. ● Balances quantitative scales with open-ended questions, surfacing both statistical trends and nuanced employee experiences. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR evaluation specialist creating post-onboarding review templates grounded in Kirkpatrick's Four-Level Evaluation Model (Reaction, Learning, Behavior, Results).

## Task

Design three interconnected review templates:

1. **HR Template** – assess program effectiveness and identify systemic gaps
2. **Manager Template** – evaluate new hire integration, performance, and team fit
3. **New Hire Template** – capture honest feedback on the onboarding experience

Each template must progress logically through all four Kirkpatrick levels, balancing quantitative rating scales with qualitative open-ended questions. Include guidance on review timing, question sequencing, and how to synthesize findings across stakeholder groups for continuous improvement.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

## Output

Structure your response with clear headings for each template:

- **HR Template**
- **Manager Template**
- **New Hire Template**

Within each template, organize questions under:

- **Level 1: Reaction** (satisfaction, engagement)
- **Level 2: Learning** (knowledge/skill acquisition)
- **Level 3: Behavior** (on-the-job application, team integration)
- **Level 4: Results** (business outcomes, retention, productivity)

Use bullet points for questions. At the end of each template, specify:

- **Recommended Timing** (e.g., 30/60/90 days)
- **Review Frequency**
- **Data Interpretation Notes**

Ensure questions reveal gaps between training objectives and actual workplace application.
```
