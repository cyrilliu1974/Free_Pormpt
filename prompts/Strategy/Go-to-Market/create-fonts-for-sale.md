# Font Design and Marketing Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Font Design and Marketing Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates both a detailed typeface design plan and a full go-to-market campaign for font creators and type designers. This font design prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive deliverable covering font collection concepts, 3-5 named variations with use cases, technical specifications, and a multi-channel marketing playbook spanning social media, email sequences, and landing page structure. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, it takes your target audience, design style, marketing budget, and unique selling proposition as inputs and returns a structured plan that bridges creative design and commercial promotion. Real-world applications include launching a new typeface family for solopreneurs, positioning a font collection for specific industries, or building a cohesive campaign that turns a creative product into sales. This prompt is for independent type designers, font foundries, and creative entrepreneurs who need both the design rationale and the marketing execution mapped out in one workflow. ● Defines a font collection concept with 3-5 variations, names, industry applications, and licensing details ● Builds platform-specific social media content calendars for Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Pinterest ● Designs email campaign sequences with subject lines, body copy, CTAs, and segmentation logic ● Structures a conversion-focused landing page with hero messaging, interactive font previews, pricing, and FAQ copy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert font designer and marketing strategist specializing in type design for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a distinctive font collection and a complete promotional strategy that drives sales. Deliver both the design rationale and marketing execution plan.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Design approach:** {{design-style}}
**Marketing resources:** {{marketing-budget}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output
Provide a structured deliverable with clear headings and bullet points covering:

### Font Design
- Collection concept and aesthetic direction
- 3-5 font variations with names and use cases
- Industry applications and versatility notes
- Technical specifications (format, character sets, licensing)

### Social Media Strategy
- Platform-specific content plans (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Pinterest)
- Post types: showcases, tutorials, customer examples
- Posting cadence and campaign timeline
- Hashtag and engagement tactics

### Email Marketing
- Campaign sequence (launch, nurture, conversion)
- Subject lines and preview text
- Email body structure with CTAs
- Segmentation approach

### Landing Page Design
- Hero section with value proposition
- Font showcase with interactive previews
- Social proof elements
- Pricing structure and purchase flow
- FAQ addressing common objections
```
