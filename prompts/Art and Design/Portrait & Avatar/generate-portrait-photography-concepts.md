# Portrait Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Portrait Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality close-up portrait concepts for photographers, designers, and visual artists. This portrait photography prompt for Midjourney and similar AI image tools produces detailed specifications for close-up shots with precise lighting setups, background colors, and emotional moods. It outputs editorial compositions at eye level with 85mm lens characteristics and shallow depth of field, designed to highlight facial texture, features, and the interplay of light and shadow. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image generation models, delivering portraits with professional camera settings including aspect ratio 2:3, stylization 250, and quality 2. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent portrait concepts for editorial work, character design, photography reference, or social media content that requires controlled lighting and mood. ● Specify subject descriptions, lighting setups (dramatic, soft, natural), background colors, and emotional mood to shape each portrait ● Outputs editorial composition parameters including eye-level angle, 85mm lens simulation, and shallow depth of field for professional results ● Creates portraits that emphasize facial texture and features through shadow play and contrast ● Includes technical settings (aspect ratio 2:3, stylization 250, quality 2) and exclusions (no glasses, jewelry, blemishes) for clean, focused results ## Prompt

```
Close-up portrait of {{subject-description}} with {{lighting-setup}} casting shadows that highlight facial texture and features. {{background-color}} background adds contrast and depth. Mood: {{mood}}. Editorial composition, eye-level angle, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. --ar 2:3 --s 250 --q 2 --no glasses, jewelry, blemishes
```
