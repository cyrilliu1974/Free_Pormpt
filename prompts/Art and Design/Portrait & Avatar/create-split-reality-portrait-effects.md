# Split Reality Portrait Effect Generator

## 簡介

The Split Reality Portrait Effect Generator is a free AI prompt that creates chest-up portraits divided into photo-realistic and cartoon halves with a crisp torn-paper transition for digital artists and content creators. This split reality portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces ultra-high-resolution images where the left side renders soft diffused lighting, analog grain, and film texture overlaid with stylized stickers and symbols, while the right side bursts with vibrant pop-art colors, kawaii decorations, and bold digital illustration. Real use cases include social media avatars, brand identity visuals, profile headers, and creative portfolio pieces that demand attention through contrasting aesthetics. The prompt maintains perfect facial consistency across both halves, preserving proportions, skin texture, and eye shape while rendering one side in realistic photography and the other in exaggerated cartoon style.
