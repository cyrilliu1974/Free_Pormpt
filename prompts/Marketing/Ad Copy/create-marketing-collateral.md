# Marketing Collateral Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Marketing Collateral Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that produces professional brochure, flyer, and business card copy tailored to digital solopreneur businesses. This marketing collateral prompt for ChatGPT takes a simple business description and returns three ready-to-use pieces: a brochure with headline, benefits, and call to action; a flyer with key points and a special offer; and a business card layout with tagline and contact details. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to generate persuasive, structured copy without hiring a designer or copywriter. Use it when you need brand-aligned marketing materials quickly - whether you're launching a new service, attending a networking event, or refreshing your print collateral. ● Outputs brochure copy with benefit bullets and a clear call to action ● Generates flyer text with attention-grabbing headlines and special offers ● Provides business card layout including tagline, name, title, and contact info ● Uses plain language and persuasive structure that highlights your unique value proposition ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write professional marketing collateral for solopreneur digital businesses. Your output is structured, persuasive, and ready for immediate use across print and digital formats.

## Context

Business: {{business-description}}

## Task

Using the business details above, produce three pieces of marketing collateral tailored to the business and its target audience. Highlight the unique value proposition, use plain language, and drive action throughout.

## Output

### Brochure

Engage with a compelling headline and subheading, followed by a concise business overview that establishes credibility.

**Key Benefits:**
- First major benefit to the customer
- Second compelling advantage
- Third differentiating strength

Close with a strong call to action that drives the next step.

---

### Flyer

Open with an attention-grabbing headline that stops the reader.

**Key Points:**
- Most important service or product feature
- Secondary value proposition
- Social proof or credibility marker

Include an irresistible special offer (discount, limited-time bonus, or exclusive access).

End with an urgent call to action.

---

### Business Card

Business Name
Memorable Tagline

Your Name
Your Title

**Contact:**
Website URL
Email Address
Phone Number
```
