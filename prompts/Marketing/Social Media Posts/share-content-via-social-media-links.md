# Social Media Post Generator for Website Traffic

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Generator for Website Traffic is a free AI prompt that creates optimized social media content across LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook to drive clicks and improve SEO through strategic backlinking. This social media prompt for ChatGPT takes your content type, website URL, target audience, and SEO keywords, then produces tailored posts for each major platform with platform-appropriate tone, length, hashtags, visual recommendations, calls-to-action, and optimal posting times. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing teams use it to promote blog posts, product launches, case studies, and thought leadership content while maintaining consistency across channels and maximizing click-through rates. Reach for this prompt when you need to distribute a single piece of content across multiple social platforms without manually rewriting for each channel's audience expectations and format constraints. ● Produces three platform-specific versions of your content - LinkedIn (professional, 150-300 words), Twitter (concise, under 280 characters), and Facebook (conversational, 100-250 words) - each with audience-appropriate tone and messaging. ● Suggests relevant hashtags tailored to each platform's discovery algorithms and provides visual content recommendations that align with your brand and message. ● Includes clear calls-to-action designed to drive click-throughs to your website and recommends optimal posting times based on platform engagement patterns. ● Explains the strategic reasoning behind each post's approach, helping you understand why specific tactics maximize engagement and backlink potential for your target audience. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in content that drives website traffic and builds backlinks for SEO.

## Task
Create platform-specific social media posts optimized for LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook that promote the content, incorporate relevant hashtags, include visual recommendations, feature compelling calls-to-action, and provide optimal posting times.

## Context
- Content type: {{content-type}}
- Website URL: {{website-url}}
- Target audience and industry: {{audience-and-industry}}
- Key SEO keywords: {{seo-keywords}}

Each platform has distinct audience behaviors and format constraints. Content should encourage sharing, interactions, and click-throughs while maintaining platform-appropriate tone and length.

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings for each platform:

### LinkedIn
- Post copy (professional tone, 150-300 words)
- Relevant hashtags (3-5)
- Visual recommendation
- Call-to-action
- Optimal posting time

### Twitter
- Tweet text (concise, under 280 characters)
- Relevant hashtags (2-3)
- Visual recommendation
- Call-to-action
- Optimal posting time

### Facebook
- Post copy (conversational tone, 100-250 words)
- Relevant hashtags (2-4)
- Visual recommendation
- Call-to-action
- Optimal posting time

For each platform, explain briefly why the approach maximizes engagement and backlink potential for the given audience.
```
