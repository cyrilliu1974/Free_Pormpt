# Product Description Copywriting Prompt

## 簡介

The Product Description Copywriting Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates multi-channel product copy optimized to convert browsers into buyers for e-commerce brands, marketers, and online retailers. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a structured analysis of customer problems, product solutions, and purchase objections before generating five distinct formats: a short 50-100 word version for marketplace titles and ads, a long 200-300 word description for product pages, 5-7 benefit-driven bullet points, a complete email campaign with subject line and body, and platform-specific social media variations for Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter/X. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring two variables - product details (features, specifications, competitive differentiation, and pricing) and target customer profile (demographics, pain points, and decision triggers). Reach for this prompt when you need persuasive product copy that speaks to emotional outcomes rather than technical specs, or when you must adapt a single product story across marketplace listings, website pages, email, and social platforms simultaneously. ● Analyzes customer problems and product fit before writing, ensuring copy addresses real pain points and purchase hesitations. ● Delivers five distinct formats in one pass - short marketplace copy, long website descriptions, feature-to-benefit bullets, email campaigns, and social media adaptations. ● Applies conversion principles like sensory language, specific outcome promises, 8th-grade readability, and proactive objection handling. ● Translates technical specifications into tangible life improvements ("never worry about rain ruining your day" instead of "waterproof"). ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a conversion copywriter who transforms product features into compelling benefits that drive purchase decisions.

## Task

Create product descriptions optimized for multiple channels (marketplace listings, website pages, email, social media) that convert browsers into buyers by focusing on customer transformation.

Before writing, analyze:
1. What specific problem does the target customer face?
2. How does this product uniquely solve it?
3. What transformation will they experience?
4. What purchase objections exist and how can copy address them?

## Context

{{product-details}}

Include key features and specifications, primary benefits and outcomes, what makes it different from competitors, and price positioning.

{{target-customer}}

Describe demographics, psychographics, pain points, and decision-making triggers.

## Output

Provide:

**SHORT VERSION (50-100 words)**  
For marketplace titles, email subjects, and ad copy. Lead with the primary benefit and create urgency.

**LONG VERSION (200-300 words)**  
For website product pages and full listings. Structure: problem identification → solution presentation → benefit amplification → social proof → clear call to action.

**BULLET POINTS (5-7 items)**  
Translate each feature into a tangible customer outcome. Not "waterproof" but "never worry about rain ruining your day."

**EMAIL CAMPAIGN**  
- Subject line (emotional hook)
- Preview text (first 50 characters)
- Body (150-200 words with clear CTA)

**SOCIAL MEDIA VARIATIONS**  
- Instagram: visual-focused with emoji
- Facebook: story-driven with engagement hook
- Twitter/X: ultra-concise value proposition

### Copy Principles

✓ Benefits over features—always explain what specifications mean for the user's life  
✓ Specific outcomes—"get 2 hours back every week" not "saves time"  
✓ Sensory language and emotional outcomes—help customers visualize their improved life  
✓ 8th-grade reading level—clarity over cleverness  
✓ Address objections proactively (price, quality, compatibility)

✗ Avoid: technical jargon without translation, unsupported superlatives, generic phrases ("high quality", "best in class"), feature lists without benefit explanations
```
