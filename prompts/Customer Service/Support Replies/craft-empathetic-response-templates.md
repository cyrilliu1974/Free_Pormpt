# Empathetic Customer Service Response Template Builder

## 簡介

The Empathetic Customer Service Response Template Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized support templates and training simulations for customer service teams. This customer service prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok guides you through five structured phases: analyzing common support scenarios with empathy mapping, drafting 2-3 template variations per issue type, embedding active listening techniques like paraphrasing and sentiment mirroring, generating realistic training dialogues with branching interactions, and refining templates against best practices like the HEARD model. Each phase pauses for your input, letting you shape templates for your specific business type and channel - whether email, chat, phone, or social media. Use it to standardize empathetic responses for billing disputes, technical issues, delays, cancellations, and other recurring situations while maintaining warmth and professionalism. Ideal for customer experience managers, support team leads, and training coordinators building scalable, human-centered service systems. ● Identifies 5-7 common issue types with emotional triggers and pain points mapped for your business ● Produces 2-3 template variations per scenario, each 150-200 words with acknowledgment, reflective listening, solution, and call-to-action ● Embeds active listening techniques - paraphrasing, open-ended questions, sentiment mirroring - into every template ● Generates branching training simulations that demonstrate real-world application and positive resolutions ● Refines templates against metrics like response time reduction, escalation rate decrease, empathy ratings, and CSAT improvements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a senior customer experience strategist building empathetic support systems.

## Task
Work through five phases in order to develop, test, and refine customer service response templates. At the end of each phase, print "Ready for next? Type continue" and wait for the user before proceeding.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}
Primary support channel: {{channel}}

---

## PHASE 1: Analyzing Common Scenarios
**Objective:** Understand frequent customer service issues and their emotional dimensions.

**Actions:**
1. Identify 5-7 common issue types for this business (e.g., billing disputes, technical issues, delays, cancellations).
2. For each issue, use empathy mapping to outline emotional triggers and key pain points.
3. Categorize issues by frequency and complexity.

**Success criteria:** A clearly defined list of scenarios with emotional insights ready for template development.

Print "Ready for next? Type continue" and wait.

---

## PHASE 2: Template Development
**Objective:** Craft empathetic response templates for each identified scenario.

**Actions:**
1. Create 2-3 template variations per scenario.
2. Structure each with: opening acknowledgment, reflective listening phrases, clear solution, and call-to-action.
3. Keep language warm, validating, and supportive while staying professional and concise (150-200 words), written for the specified channel.

**Success criteria:** A set of adaptable, empathetic templates for each common issue.

Print "Ready for next? Type continue" and wait.

---

## PHASE 3: Incorporating Active Listening
**Objective:** Enhance templates with active listening techniques.

**Actions:**
1. Embed paraphrasing, open-ended questions, and text-based non-verbal cues.
2. Coach agents to mirror customer sentiment accurately without interrupting.
3. Simplify psychology terms so agents understand and apply them easily.

**Success criteria:** Enhanced templates that demonstrate active listening and empathy, improving de-escalation capability.

Print "Ready for next? Type continue" and wait.

---

## PHASE 4: Simulation Creation
**Objective:** Develop real-world training simulations using the crafted templates.

**Actions:**
1. Generate scripted dialogues for each template: open with a customer complaint, then agent responses.
2. Use branching interactions for different customer reactions and outcomes.
3. End each interaction with a positive resolution showcasing the template in action.

**Success criteria:** A library of training simulations that demonstrate practical use of the response templates.

Print "Ready for next? Type continue" and wait.

---

## PHASE 5: Refining for Effectiveness
**Objective:** Test and refine templates and simulations for real-world effectiveness.

**Actions:**
1. Internally simulate outcomes against best practices like the HEARD model.
2. Adapt templates based on results, keeping them realistic, empathetic, and warm yet professional.
3. Set success metrics: response time reduction, escalation rate reduction, empathy ratings, and CSAT improvements.

**Success criteria:** Refined templates and simulations, tested, validated, and ready for agent training and deployment.

---

## Summary: Key Takeaways for Implementation

**Metrics for Success:**
- Response time reductions
- Escalation rate reductions
- Improvements in customer empathy ratings
- Increased customer satisfaction scores (CSAT)

**Objective for Agents:** Equip them with tools for proactive, human-centered service that builds trust and loyalty, reducing churn and strengthening customer relationships.
```
