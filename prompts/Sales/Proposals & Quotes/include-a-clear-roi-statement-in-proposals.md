# ROI-Focused Sales Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The ROI-Focused Sales Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive sales proposals centered on return on investment for sales professionals, consultants, and business developers. This sales proposal prompt for ChatGPT transforms your product overview and value propositions into a complete investment case with five core sections: an executive summary stating the ROI promise, a value analysis mapping features to measurable business outcomes, financial projections quantifying cost savings and revenue gains, an investment case with payback period calculations, and actionable next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, structuring each proposal to flow logically from problem identification through solution presentation to quantified financial benefits. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate technical features into business value, justify pricing with hard numbers, or build a client-facing document that speaks the language of finance and strategic investment. ● Maps product features directly to measurable business outcomes and revenue impact ● Calculates cost savings, efficiency improvements, and projected payback periods ● Structures proposals with clear headings and bullet formatting for executive readability ● Includes an investment case section comparing total cost against projected returns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist specializing in ROI-focused proposals.

## Task
Create a clear, persuasive ROI statement that demonstrates the financial value and return on investment for the product or service described below.

## Context
**Product/Service & Market:**
{{product-service-overview}}

**Key Value Propositions:**
{{value-propositions}}

Structure your proposal to flow logically from problem to solution to quantified benefits. Connect each feature directly to measurable business outcomes.

## Output
Deliver a professional sales proposal with:

- **Executive Summary** – High-level ROI promise and investment rationale
- **Value Analysis** – Feature-to-benefit mapping with business impact
- **Financial Projections** – Quantified gains, cost savings, revenue uplift, or efficiency improvements
- **Investment Case** – Total cost vs. projected return, payback period, and risk mitigation
- **Next Steps** – Clear call-to-action

Use clear headings, bullet points, and specific numbers to maximize readability and persuasive impact.
```
