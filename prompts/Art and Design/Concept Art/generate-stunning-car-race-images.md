# Car Race Image Generator for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Car Race Image Generator for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that creates dynamic racing visuals featuring any vehicle on a custom track with a specified art style. This car race image prompt for Midjourney takes four inputs - vehicle type, track description, visual style, and aspect ratio - and outputs a high-quality racing scene rendered with motion, drama, and detail. It runs on Midjourney with fixed settings (stylize 250, chaos 20, quality 2) tuned for speed, energy, and photographic impact. Use it to generate concept art for racing games, automotive marketing visuals, motorsport illustrations, or personal vehicle renders in motion. Reach for this prompt whenever you need a polished racing image without manually tweaking dozens of Midjourney parameters. ● Specify any vehicle - sports car, rally car, formula racer, motorcycle, or futuristic concept - and place it on any track environment. ● Choose the visual style, from photorealistic to illustrated, cinematic, retro poster, or cel-shaded animation. ● Control composition with custom aspect ratios while the prompt handles stylization, chaos, and quality for consistent results. ● Delivers motion-filled scenes with strong lighting, shadows, and atmospheric effects that convey speed and excitement. ## Prompt
