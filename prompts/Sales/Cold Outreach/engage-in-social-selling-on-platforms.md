# Social Selling Strategy Generator for LinkedIn & Twitter

## 簡介

The Social Selling Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds complete social selling strategies across LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook for marketers and sales teams. This social selling prompt for ChatGPT takes your industry, product, target audience, unique selling proposition, and key performance indicators, then produces a structured strategy organized by platform. The output includes platform-specific content formats and themes, engagement tactics for initiating and sustaining conversations, recommended posting schedules, messaging approaches that emphasize your value proposition, and concrete methods for tracking performance metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need a structured social selling plan that adapts your offering to the unique behaviors and content preferences of audiences on LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook. ● Delivers content ideas tailored to how your target audience behaves on each platform ● Provides engagement tactics for building genuine connections that lead to conversions ● Includes performance tracking methods aligned with your specific KPIs ● Suggests a consistent posting schedule to maintain visibility and momentum ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in social selling strategies across LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social selling strategy that maximizes reach, engagement, and conversions. Organize the strategy by platform and include:
- Platform-specific content ideas tailored to audience behavior
- Engagement tactics for building meaningful connections
- Performance tracking methods tied to key metrics
- A consistent posting schedule recommendation
- Messaging approaches that highlight the unique value proposition

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Product/Service: {{product-service}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique Selling Proposition: {{usp}}
Key Performance Indicators: {{kpi}}

## Output
Provide your strategy as a bullet point list organized by platform (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook). For each platform include:
- Specific content formats and themes
- Tactics for initiating and sustaining engagement
- Metrics to track and methods for measuring them
```
