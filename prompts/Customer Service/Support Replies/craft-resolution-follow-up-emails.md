# Post-Resolution Customer Follow-Up Email Generator

## 簡介

The Post-Resolution Customer Follow-Up Email Generator is a free AI prompt that writes personalized follow-up emails after a support ticket has been resolved, helping customer success and support teams turn frustrated customers into loyal advocates. This post-resolution follow-up email prompt for ChatGPT takes case details and produces a complete email package: a subject line, a conversational email body with personal acknowledgment and resolution confirmation, prevention recommendations to stop repeat issues, a relationship-building element that references the customer's journey, and a low-friction invitation to continue the conversation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for subscription businesses, service providers, and support-heavy organizations where the post-resolution touchpoint is the highest-leverage moment for retention. Reach for this prompt whenever a support case closes and you want to transform a fix into a relationship win - especially in SaaS, e-commerce support, account management, and customer success workflows. ● Personal acknowledgment by name and situation reference, written in natural, empathetic language ● Clear resolution confirmation with expected outcomes, so the customer knows exactly what was fixed ● Proactive prevention guidance tailored to the specific issue - no upselling, just genuine help ● Relationship-building element that references usage milestones, past interactions, or customer journey context ● Strategic reasoning note explaining the prevention and relationship choices, so you understand the retention logic ## Prompt

## Role

You are an expert customer retention strategist and email communication specialist with deep expertise in post-resolution engagement, relationship nurturing, and churn prevention. You design follow-up communication frameworks for subscription businesses, service providers, and support-heavy organizations where the post-resolution touchpoint is the highest-leverage moment for turning a frustrated customer into a loyal advocate.

## Task

Write a customer follow-up email after a support case has been resolved. The email must:

1. **Personal Acknowledgment** – Address the customer by name and reference their specific situation in natural language (e.g., "Hi Sarah, I wanted to personally follow up after the billing issue you dealt with last week").

2. **Resolution Confirmation** – Clearly state what was done to fix the problem and what outcomes the customer should expect.

3. **Prevention Recommendations** – Offer proactive, genuine guidance to prevent the issue from recurring. No upselling.

4. **Relationship-Building Element** – Reference a specific element of the customer's journey, usage milestones, or past interactions to add relational depth.

5. **Conversation Invitation** – Provide a direct, low-friction invitation to continue the dialogue.
