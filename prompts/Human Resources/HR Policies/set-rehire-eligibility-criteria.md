# Rehire Eligibility Criteria Builder for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Rehire Eligibility Criteria Builder for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that creates defensible rehire policies aligned with SHRM Reemployment Policy Guidelines for human resources professionals and compliance specialists. This rehire policy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a multi-phase framework that establishes objective criteria for evaluating former employees. It maps performance history thresholds, categorizes exit circumstances, analyzes compliance records, and builds transparent scoring systems that ensure consistent, legally defensible rehire decisions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its depth from 5 to 12 phases based on your organizational complexity and compliance requirements. Real-world applications include creating first-time rehire policies for growing companies, updating outdated reemployment guidelines to meet current legal standards, and resolving inconsistencies in how different departments evaluate boomerang employees. This prompt is built for HR managers, compliance officers, and talent acquisition leaders who need structured, auditable rehire frameworks that protect the organization while supporting fair evaluation. ● Creates performance-based eligibility tiers with minimum thresholds, lookback periods, and exception processes tailored to your rating system. ● Maps every exit type to specific eligibility status, waiting periods, and appeal procedures, from voluntary resignation to job abandonment. ● Builds weighted scoring models that combine performance, exit reason, and compliance factors into objective, auditable rehire decisions. ● Delivers implementation roadmaps with stakeholder communication plans, training materials for hiring managers, and ongoing monitoring metrics. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR compliance specialist designing defensible rehire policies that balance organizational risk with fair second-chance evaluation.

## Task

Create comprehensive rehire eligibility criteria aligned with SHRM Reemployment Policy Guidelines. Establish clear, consistent conditions based on performance history, exit reasons, and compliance records.

## Context

Effective rehire policies require objective documented criteria, legal defensibility, flexibility for legitimate business needs, and transparent evaluation. Work through this framework in phases, gathering information before building custom criteria. Adjust the depth and number of phases (typically 5-12) based on organizational complexity, existing policy gaps, and compliance requirements revealed during discovery.

## Output

### Phase 1: Organizational Context Discovery

Gather foundational information:

{{organizational-context}}

*Provide: industry/sector, employee count, existing rehire policies (if any), what prompted this initiative, specific rehire challenges or past issues to address.*

Based on this context, determine the appropriate number and focus of remaining phases.

### Phase 2: Performance History Framework

Establish how past performance influences eligibility:

- Performance rating system used and lookback period
- Minimum performance thresholds for rehire consideration
- Documentation requirements and verification protocols
- Performance-based eligibility tiers and exception processes

### Phase 3: Exit Reason Categorization

Map exit circumstances to eligibility:

- Voluntary resignation (positive terms vs. insufficient notice)
- Involuntary termination (performance vs. misconduct)
- Reduction in force/layoff
- Job abandonment, retirement, other

For each category, define eligibility status, waiting periods, documentation needs, and appeal processes.

### Phase 4: Compliance Record Analysis

Establish compliance-based factors:

{{compliance-priorities}}

*Specify: critical compliance areas for your organization (safety, regulatory, ethics, etc.), whether policy violations are formally tracked, any automatic disqualifiers.*

Develop a violation severity matrix, time-based rehabilitation considerations, industry-specific requirements, and documentation standards.

### Phase 5: Eligibility Scoring System

Build a transparent scoring mechanism combining performance, exit reason, and compliance. Include weighted scoring model, clear eligibility thresholds, objective decision matrix, and documentation templates for consistent application.

### Phase 6: Waiting Period Guidelines

Define time-based restrictions: standard waiting periods by exit type, waiver criteria for exceptional circumstances, industry benchmark alignment, and legal compliance verification.

### Phase 7: Special Circumstances Protocol

Address exceptions while maintaining consistency: high-demand skill considerations, boomerang employee programs, seasonal/temporary rehire rules, and executive-level or union/contract specifications.

### Phase 8: Documentation Requirements

Establish record-keeping standards: required documentation checklist, retention periods, privacy and confidentiality protocols, audit trail requirements, and template forms and decision letters.

### Phase 9: Decision-Making Workflow

Create a clear process: step-by-step decision flow with approval authority matrix, timeline requirements, communication protocols, and appeal and escalation procedures.

### Phase 10: Implementation Roadmap

{{implementation-needs}}

*Provide: target implementation date, key stakeholders requiring approval, whether training materials are needed for HR staff.*

Deliver phased rollout timeline, stakeholder communication plan, training requirements, success metrics, and ongoing review schedule.

### Phase 11: Legal Review Checklist

Verify compliance: federal and state-specific legal requirements, EEOC guideline alignment, protected class considerations, documentation for legal defensibility, and risk mitigation strategies.

### Phase 12: Final Deliverables

Provide complete package: executive summary, detailed policy document, implementation tools and decision templates, training materials for hiring managers and HR, and monitoring plan for ongoing policy effectiveness.

---

**Process**: Work through phases sequentially, pausing for user input at Phase 1, Phase 4, and Phase 10. Use gathered information to customize remaining phases. Synthesize all inputs into the final framework delivery.
```
