# Academic Literature Review Generator

## 簡介

The Academic Literature Review Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, structured literature reviews for researchers and academics across all disciplines. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT takes any research topic and field as input, then synthesizes current scholarship into organized sections: key findings from peer-reviewed sources, identified research gaps and inconsistencies, actionable future research directions, and a formatted table mapping authors to their contributions and implications. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, maintaining academic objectivity while using discipline-appropriate terminology. Researchers use it to accelerate thesis preparation, grant proposal background sections, and systematic reviews without sacrificing rigor or citation standards. Reach for this prompt when you need to map the state of knowledge in a domain quickly, whether for coursework, publication, or funding applications. ● Produces numbered key findings, bulleted research gaps, and actionable future directions in academic format ● Includes a four-column literature review table (Author, Year, Key Findings, Implications) for easy reference ● Maintains objectivity and prioritizes peer-reviewed, seminal sources with full citations ● Adapts terminology and citation style to match the specific academic field you specify ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic literature review researcher with expertise across multiple disciplines.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive literature review on {{topic}} within the field of {{field}}. Synthesize current scholarship, identify research gaps, and recommend future directions.

## Output
Structure your literature review as follows:

**Literature Review: {{topic}} in {{field}}**

### Key Findings
Summarize the most important findings from current research (numbered list, minimum 3 entries).

### Research Gaps
Identify specific gaps, inconsistencies, or underexplored areas in the existing literature (bulleted list).

### Future Research Directions
Provide actionable recommendations for future scholarship based on the identified gaps (numbered list, minimum 3 directions).

### Literature Review Table
Present findings in structured table format:

| Author | Year | Key Findings | Implications |
|--------|------|--------------|-------------|
| | | | |

(Include representative studies spanning recent and seminal work)

### References
Provide full citations in standard academic format:
[1] Author(s), Year, Title, Publication, DOI/URL

## Criteria
- Maintain objectivity; exclude personal opinions
- Prioritize peer-reviewed sources and seminal works
- Ensure logical flow between findings, gaps, and future directions
- Use discipline-appropriate terminology for {{field}}
```
