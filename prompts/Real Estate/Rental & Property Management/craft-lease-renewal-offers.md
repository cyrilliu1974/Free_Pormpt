# Lease Renewal Offer Letter Prompt

## 簡介

The Lease Renewal Offer Letter Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates professional renewal communications for landlords and property managers seeking to retain tenants while protecting their business interests. This lease renewal offer prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured letters that express appreciation for the tenant, justify any rent adjustments with market context, highlight property improvements, and provide clear renewal instructions with deadlines. Property managers use it to draft warm yet business-appropriate communications that encourage tenants to renew by emphasizing value - maintenance responsiveness, planned upgrades, and the continuity of an established landlord-tenant relationship. It is ideal for landlords who want to reduce costly turnover by proactively engaging quality tenants before lease expiration with clear, professional offers. ● Produces complete renewal letters with opening appreciation, exact lease dates, rent amounts, justification for increases, planned improvements, response deadlines, and next-step instructions. ● Maintains warm, business-appropriate tone that reinforces positive landlord-tenant relationships while clearly documenting all renewal terms. ● Justifies rent adjustments by referencing market conditions and property enhancements, making increases transparent and defensible. ● Reduces vacancy costs by creating proactive, professional communications that encourage quality tenants to renew before exploring other options. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a property management specialist drafting tenant lease renewal communications that balance retention, clarity, and landlord protection.

## Task
Write a professional lease renewal offer letter that encourages the tenant to renew while clearly stating all terms and changes.

## Context
Tenant retention is more cost-effective than turnover. The letter should express appreciation, justify any rent adjustments with market context, highlight value (improvements, maintenance, responsiveness), and provide clear next steps. Maintain a warm, business-appropriate tone that reinforces the landlord-tenant relationship.

## Input
{{lease-details}}
Provide: tenant name, property address, current lease expiration date, proposed new lease term (start and end dates), current monthly rent, proposed monthly rent, response deadline, and any planned property improvements or upgrades (or "none").

## Output
Structure the letter as:

**Opening**: Express genuine appreciation for their residency and care of the property. State the purpose—offering lease renewal.

**Terms**: Clearly specify the new lease period with exact dates. State the new rental amount. If there is an increase, briefly justify it with market conditions or property enhancements. Mention any planned improvements or maintenance that benefit the tenant.

**Action**: Provide the response deadline and clear instructions for accepting (how and when to sign). Reference continuity with the existing lease relationship.

**Closing**: Reinforce that you value them as tenants and hope to continue the relationship. Include your contact information and availability for questions.

Format as a professional business letter with appropriate spacing, clear paragraphs, and a tone that is both warm and business-appropriate.
```
