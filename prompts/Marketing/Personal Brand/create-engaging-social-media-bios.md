# Social Media Bio Generator for Personal Branding

## 簡介

The Social Media Bio Generator for Personal Branding is a free AI prompt that creates tailored, ready-to-paste bios for X (Twitter), Instagram, and LinkedIn based on your professional profile and goals. This social media bio prompt for ChatGPT produces three platform-adapted versions in a single run: a punchy Twitter bio with strategic hashtags, a visual Instagram bio with emoji suggestions, and a credibility-focused LinkedIn headline. It respects each platform's character limits and audience expectations while weaving in keywords for discoverability and highlighting your unique value proposition. Use it when launching a new account, refreshing your personal brand, pivoting your professional focus, or testing different positioning strategies across networks. ● Produces three format-compliant bios in one pass - Twitter, Instagram, and LinkedIn - each adapted to platform tone and audience. ● Includes character counts, relevant hashtags, emoji recommendations, and three actionable optimization tips for ongoing improvement. ● Transforms profile details (name, occupation, interests, goals) into concise, keyword-rich copy that attracts the right followers or clients. ● Avoids generic buzzwords in favor of concrete details that differentiate your voice and expertise. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist specializing in personal branding across X (Twitter), Instagram, and LinkedIn.

## Task
Create three platform-specific bios that capture the user's professional identity and goals while respecting each platform's character limits and audience expectations.

## Context
{{profile-details}}

Include: name, occupation, key interests or specialties, and what the user wants their bio to achieve (e.g., attract clients, showcase expertise, grow network).

## Requirements

**All platforms:**
- Keep bios concise, specific, and true to the user's voice
- Integrate strategic keywords for discoverability
- Highlight the most compelling attributes and value proposition
- Avoid generic phrases; prioritize concrete details over buzzwords

**Platform adaptation:**
- **X (Twitter):** Punchy and conversational; use 1-2 relevant hashtags
- **Instagram:** Visual and approachable; suggest 2-3 complementary emojis
- **LinkedIn:** Professional and credibility-focused; emphasize expertise and impact

## Output

### X (Twitter) Bio
**Character count:** [count]  
**Bio:** [text]  
**Hashtags:** [hashtags]

### Instagram Bio
**Character count:** [count]  
**Bio:** [text]  
**Emojis:** [emojis]

### LinkedIn Headline
**Character count:** [count]  
**Bio:** [text]

### Optimization Tips
Provide 3 actionable recommendations for strengthening bio performance across platforms (e.g., A/B testing CTAs, updating seasonally, linking to current work).
```
