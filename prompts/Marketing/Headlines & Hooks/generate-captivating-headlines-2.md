# Headline Rewrite Generator for High-Engagement Content

## 簡介

The Headline Rewrite Generator for High-Engagement Content is a free AI prompt that transforms bland headlines into specific, curiosity-driven alternatives designed to increase click-through rates and reader engagement. This headline prompt for ChatGPT takes a single generic headline and produces three rewritten options, each analyzed for how it incorporates specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotional resonance. The prompt works by asking the AI to act as an expert copywriter, zooming in on concrete pain points or benefits, hinting at unexpected insights, and using power words that drive action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it a versatile tool for content creators, marketers, and bloggers who need multiple headline variations quickly. Each output includes not just the rewritten headline but also an explanation of why it works, helping you learn the principles of effective headline writing. Use this prompt when you have a topic or working title that feels flat and need to test stronger variations before publishing articles, email campaigns, social posts, or landing pages. ● Transforms one generic headline into three specific, curiosity-sparking alternatives under 20 words each ● Explains the copywriting strategy behind each option - specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion ● Keeps headlines free of clichés and hyperbole while maximizing engagement potential ● Ideal for A/B testing headlines across blog posts, emails, ads, and social media ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in high-performing, click-worthy headlines that drive traffic and engagement.

## Task
Transform the user's generic headline into three captivating alternatives. Each headline must:

- **Be specific**: zoom in on a concrete pain point, benefit, strategy, or outcome
- **Spark curiosity**: hint at an unexpected insight, contrarian view, or insider secret
- **Be actionable**: allude to a clear, achievable result the reader will gain
- **Evoke emotion**: use power words, sensory details, or storytelling elements
- Stay under 20 words and avoid clichés or hyperbole

## Input
Generic headline:
{{generic-headline}}

## Output
Provide three headline options in this format:

**Headline Option 1:**
[Headline]

*Explanation:* [Brief analysis of why it works—how it incorporates specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion]

**Headline Option 2:**
[Headline]

*Explanation:* [Brief analysis of why it works—how it incorporates specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion]

**Headline Option 3:**
[Headline]

*Explanation:* [Brief analysis of why it works—how it incorporates specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion]
```
