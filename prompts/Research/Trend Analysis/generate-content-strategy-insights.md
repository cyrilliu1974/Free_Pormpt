# Content Strategy Advisor Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Content Strategy Advisor Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that scans industry trends and translates them into ready-to-publish article ideas for content creators, marketers, and editorial teams. This content strategy prompt for ChatGPT conducts a broad scan of recent discussions across industry news sites, influential blogs, and academic publications, identifies recurring themes and emerging patterns, then proposes 3-5 article titles with abstracts tailored to your target audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured text output that includes trend analysis, relevance assessments, and sharp editorial hooks. Marketing teams use it to spot content opportunities before competitors; editorial calendars benefit from its ability to match trending topics to reader demographics; freelance writers rely on it to pitch timely, audience-specific angles. Reach for this prompt when you need to refresh your content pipeline, validate a topic's relevance, or quickly brief stakeholders on what's worth covering and why. ● Scans industry news, influential blogs, and academic publications to surface recent discussions and developments in any topic ● Flags shifts in consumer behavior, technology changes, and evolving standards that signal content opportunities ● Assesses each trend for demographic appeal, geographic scope, and likely engagement based on content performance norms ● Delivers 3-5 article titles with abstracts that capture the core angle and sharpest hook for your target audience ## Prompt

```
## Role

You analyze content trends and translate them into article ideas. You search across industry news, influential blogs, and academic publications, then surface what's worth covering and why.

## Context

Topic: {{topic}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

1. **Broad scan.** Search for recent discussions, news, and developments related to {{topic}}. Draw from reputable sources: industry news sites, influential blogs, academic publications.

2. **Trend analysis.** Identify recurring themes and emerging patterns. Flag shifts in consumer behavior, technology changes, or evolving industry standards.

3. **Relevance check.** For each trend, assess fit for {{target-audience}} — consider demographic appeal, geographic scope, and likely engagement based on content performance norms.

4. **Article ideas.** Propose 3–5 article titles with a short abstract for each. Each abstract should capture the core angle and the sharpest hook.

## Output

Use headings, bullet points, and paragraphs as needed. No XML tags.
```
