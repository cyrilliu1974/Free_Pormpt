# High-End Fashion Magazine Cover Generator

## 簡介

The High-End Fashion Magazine Cover Generator is a free AI prompt that produces editorial-quality fashion covers with professional beauty lighting, medium-format camera aesthetics, and magazine-ready color grading for designers, stylists, and creative directors. This fashion magazine cover prompt for Midjourney renders photorealistic editorial portraits with dramatic directional lighting using octabox and rim setups, sculpted couture silhouettes, and the sharp detail of a Hasselblad 80mm lens at f/8. It accepts two variables - model subject and eyewear plus wardrobe - then applies confident power poses, aloof high-fashion expressions, glossy retouched skin texture, and a muted tonal studio backdrop. The output matches Vogue-level production values with rich blacks, clean highlights, and crisp specular catchlights that carve jawlines and cheekbones.
