# Content Marketing Strategy Builder for Sales Growth

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Strategy Builder for Sales Growth is a free AI prompt that generates a structured content marketing plan designed to boost sales and customer engagement for businesses. This content marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, identifies target audience pain points and preferences, and produces a markdown table with 5-7 strategic recommendations. Each row connects a specific content type to a defined audience segment and the optimal distribution channels to reach them. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a cohesive roadmap that aligns content creation with conversion goals. Marketing teams use it to move beyond scattered tactics into integrated campaigns, product managers apply it to launch strategies, and founders rely on it to build their first go-to-market content plans. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured content strategy that ties audience insights directly to actionable content and channel decisions, especially in go-to-market planning. ● Delivers 5-7 strategic recommendations in a three-column markdown table format (Content Types, Target Audience, Distribution Channels) ● Analyzes audience pain points and preferences from your business context to recommend content that resonates and converts ● Identifies the most effective distribution channels for each audience segment and content type pairing ● Creates a cohesive roadmap that aligns content creation, audience targeting, and channel strategy into one actionable plan ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in conversion-driven marketing plans.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content marketing strategy that boosts sales and customer engagement. Analyze the target audience's preferences and pain points, recommend content types that will resonate, identify effective distribution channels, and align all elements into a cohesive roadmap.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns: **Content Types**, **Target Audience**, and **Distribution Channels**. Each row must represent a cohesive strategy linking these three elements. Include 5-7 strategic recommendations that directly address the audience pain points and marketing goals described above.
```
