# Weekly Meal Plan Generator for Specific Diets

## 簡介

The Weekly Meal Plan Generator for Specific Diets is a free AI prompt that creates structured seven-day meal plans tailored to any dietary approach and personal cooking constraints. This meal plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your chosen diet type and specific needs - allergies, cuisine preferences, skill level, time availability - and produces a complete week of breakfast, lunch, dinner, and two daily snacks. Each meal includes a full recipe with ingredient quantities, step-by-step instructions with cooking times and temperatures, and nutritional breakdowns covering calories and macronutrients. The prompt also generates a consolidated grocery list organized by category and offers meal prep strategies, leftover tips, and storage guidelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Use this prompt when you need to follow a specific diet - keto, vegan, Mediterranean, paleo, or any other approach - without spending hours researching recipes and building shopping lists. It works for individuals managing health conditions, families coordinating dietary needs, or anyone wanting structured, balanced meal planning that respects their time and skill level. ● Produces seven full days of meals with recipes, ingredient lists, and preparation instructions that align with any diet type ● Generates a categorized grocery list organized by produce, proteins, dairy, and pantry staples for efficient shopping ● Includes detailed nutritional information for every meal and snack, covering calorie counts and macro breakdowns ● Provides meal prep tips, advance preparation strategies, and storage guidelines to save time and reduce waste ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced meal planner specializing in diet-specific nutrition and recipe development.

## Task
Create a comprehensive seven-day meal plan that adheres to the principles of {{diet-type}}. Provide breakfast, lunch, dinner, and two snacks for each day, complete with recipes, preparation instructions, nutritional information, and a consolidated grocery list.

## Context
The meal plan must:
- Align with the core nutritional guidelines and restrictions of {{diet-type}}
- Incorporate variety across the week to maintain interest and cover diverse tastes
- Balance macronutrients and food groups as permitted by the diet
- Account for {{user-constraints}}: dietary restrictions or allergies, preferred cuisines or flavors, cooking skill level, and available meal preparation time
- Be practical and easy to follow

## Output
Deliver a structured seven-day meal plan organized by day, containing:

**Daily Meal Schedule** (for each of 7 days):
- Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack 1, Snack 2

**Recipes** (for each meal):
- Ingredient list with quantities
- Step-by-step preparation instructions with cooking times, temperatures, and techniques
- Nutritional information: calorie count and macro/micronutrient breakdown

**Weekly Grocery List**:
- Categorized by food type (produce, dairy, proteins, pantry staples, etc.) for efficient shopping

**Meal Prep & Storage Tips**:
- Advance preparation strategies
- Leftover utilization suggestions
- Storage guidelines to maintain freshness
```
