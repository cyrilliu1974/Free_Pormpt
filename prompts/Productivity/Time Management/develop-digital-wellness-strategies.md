# Digital Wellness Strategy Builder for Technology Stress

## 簡介

The Digital Wellness Strategy Builder for Technology Stress is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based action plans to help individuals and consultants address technology-related stress and digital overwhelm. This digital wellness prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a structured framework that identifies root causes of tech stress from psychological, neurological, and human-computer interaction perspectives, then delivers five concrete strategies with step-by-step implementation plans and expected benefits grounded in scientific research. You provide your specific context, and the prompt returns a complete analysis covering problem definition, root causes, detailed strategies with implementation steps, progress measurement methods, and long-term sustainability approaches. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text AI platforms for wellness consultants, HR professionals, therapists, and individuals seeking to improve their relationship with technology. ● Analyzes technology stress through psychological, neurological, and behavioral lenses to identify true root causes ● Delivers five evidence-based strategies prioritized for high impact and low implementation barriers ● Provides concrete metrics and assessment methods to track progress over time ● Includes sustainability recommendations to maintain healthy technology habits long-term ## Prompt
