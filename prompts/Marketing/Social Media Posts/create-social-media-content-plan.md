# Social Media Content Calendar Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates a strategic week-long posting schedule tailored to your product and target audience. This social media content plan prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a balanced content mix across seven days, alternating between educational posts, promotional updates, entertaining content, user-generated stories, behind-the-scenes glimpses, interactive elements, and inspirational messages. Each day's entry includes a complete caption with call-to-action, visual suggestions, optimal posting time based on audience behavior, and 5-10 relevant hashtags combining popular and niche tags. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to your specific product positioning and audience platform habits. Social media managers, marketing teams, and small business owners use it to maintain consistent brand visibility while avoiding content fatigue. ● Creates a structured 7-day calendar with varied content themes to keep audiences engaged across the week ● Provides complete post specifications including captions, CTAs, visual descriptions, timing, and hashtag sets for each day ● Balances promotional material with educational value, entertainment, and community interaction to prevent audience burnout ● Includes guidance on performance monitoring and strategy adjustment based on engagement metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in multi-platform content creation and audience engagement.

## Task
Create a 7-day social media content calendar for a product. Each day should include:
- Content type and theme (educational, promotional, entertaining, user-generated, behind-the-scenes, interactive, or inspirational)
- Captivating caption with a clear call-to-action where appropriate
- Image description or visual suggestion
- Optimal posting time
- Relevant hashtags (mix of popular and niche)

The calendar should balance promotional, educational, and entertaining content to increase brand visibility, foster audience connection, and drive product interest.

## Context
**Product and positioning:**
{{product-and-usp}}

**Audience and platform behavior:**
{{audience-preferences}}

## Approach
Use this content structure across the week:
- **Monday (Educational):** Share product usage tips, industry insights, or relevant statistics. Include a question to drive comments.
- **Tuesday (Promotional):** Highlight a key product feature or current offer. Use a strong CTA directing to your website or product page.
- **Wednesday (Entertaining):** Post a relatable meme, GIF, or video tied to your product or industry. Keep it shareable and light-hearted.
- **Thursday (User-Generated Content):** Feature a customer review, testimonial, or user post. Tag the customer and express gratitude.
- **Friday (Behind-the-Scenes):** Show product creation, team culture, or company values to humanize your brand.
- **Saturday (Interactive):** Run a quiz, poll, or Q&A related to your product or industry. Encourage participation and sharing.
- **Sunday (Inspirational):** Share a quote or story aligned with your brand mission to strengthen emotional connection.

Schedule posts when your target audience is most active on each platform. Select high-quality visuals that align with your brand aesthetic and complement each post's message.

## Output
Format the calendar as a table or daily breakdown with these columns:
- Day
- Content Type/Theme
- Caption (including CTA where relevant)
- Image Description
- Posting Time
- Hashtags (5-10 per post)

Include brief notes on how to monitor performance and adjust the strategy based on engagement metrics.
```
