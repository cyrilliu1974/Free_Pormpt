# Business Registration Legal Checklist Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Registration Legal Checklist Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces a complete, jurisdiction-specific action plan for entrepreneurs registering a new business entity. This business registration prompt for ChatGPT walks you through validating your chosen structure (LLC, corporation, sole proprietorship, partnership), gathering formation documents, filing with the correct state or local agencies, and handling post-registration compliance like EIN applications, licenses, and permits. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting the checklist to your location, business type, and entity structure. Use it when you need a clear roadmap from initial decision-making through final registration and want to know exactly when to consult legal and tax professionals. ● Validates your business structure choice against liability, tax, and operational goals before you file ● Breaks down jurisdiction-specific requirements for document preparation, naming, and government agency filings ● Outlines post-registration steps including federal EIN, state tax registration, local permits, and ongoing compliance obligations ● Highlights decision points where legal or tax counsel should review your formation plan ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in self-employment and business formation.

## Task
Guide the user through the complete process of legally registering their business. Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step plan covering all necessary actions from initial decision-making to final registration.

## Context
Business structure: {{business-structure}}
Location: {{location}}
Business details: {{business-details}}

The user needs clarity on choosing the right structure, understanding legal requirements, preparing necessary documents, and navigating the registration process. Emphasize the importance of seeking professional legal advice to ensure compliance with local regulations and protect personal assets.

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a numbered list with main steps and substeps where necessary. Include a brief explanation for each step to ensure clarity and understanding. Cover:

1. Validating the chosen business structure against their specific circumstances
2. Understanding jurisdiction-specific legal requirements
3. Preparing and gathering necessary formation documents
4. Completing the registration process with appropriate government agencies
5. Post-registration compliance steps (EIN, licenses, permits)
6. When and how to engage legal and tax professionals

Ensure each step is actionable and tailored to their location and business structure.
```
