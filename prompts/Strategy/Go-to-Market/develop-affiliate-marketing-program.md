# Affiliate Marketing Program Setup Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Affiliate Marketing Program Setup Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, actionable roadmap for building an affiliate marketing program customized to any product, service, and industry. This affiliate marketing program prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured 10-section guide covering every phase of program development - from defining objectives and identifying ideal affiliate partners to designing commission structures, creating promotional assets, implementing tracking systems, and establishing ongoing optimization processes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a logically sequenced plan that moves from strategic foundation through tactical execution. Marketing managers use it to launch partner programs for SaaS products, e-commerce stores, digital courses, B2B services, and physical goods across industries from fintech to fitness. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete blueprint that accounts for your specific market dynamics, competitive landscape, and business model rather than generic affiliate advice. ● Produces a numbered guide with introduction, goal-setting, audience targeting, commission planning, recruitment strategy, promotional materials spec, tracking implementation, support infrastructure, optimization framework, and launch checklist ● Tailors every section to your specific product characteristics, price point, industry norms, and target affiliate demographics ● Structures recommendations using dependency-based logic so each step builds naturally on the previous phase ● Addresses both strategic decisions (performance tiers, bonus incentives) and tactical execution (outreach templates, reporting cadence) ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced affiliate marketing manager who develops programs across industries.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for setting up an affiliate marketing program for {{product-service}} in {{industry}}. Structure the guide using clear, dependency-based writing that flows logically from one step to the next.

## Output
Deliver a numbered 10-section guide:

1. **Introduction**: Explain affiliate marketing and its specific benefits for this product/service and industry.

2. **Program Goals**: Define key objectives—brand awareness, lead generation, sales growth, and market expansion.

3. **Target Audience**: Identify ideal affiliates by niche relevance, audience demographics, engagement levels, and promotional methods.

4. **Commission Structure**: Design a competitive commission plan considering price point, industry standards, performance tiers, and bonus incentives.

5. **Affiliate Recruitment**: Build a recruitment strategy covering outreach methods (email, social media, events), application criteria, and onboarding materials.

6. **Promotional Materials**: Specify assets to provide—banners, graphics, product descriptions, email templates, and social media examples.

7. **Tracking and Reporting**: Implement tracking systems with unique affiliate links/codes, conversion tracking (sales, leads, clicks), and regular performance reports.

8. **Communication and Support**: Establish support channels—dedicated affiliate manager, newsletters, updates, FAQ resources, and knowledge base.

9. **Program Optimization**: Create a continuous improvement process using affiliate feedback, performance data, and industry innovations.

10. **Conclusion**: Summarize key points and provide clear next steps for launching the program.

Each section should contain actionable steps tailored to the specific product/service and industry context.
```
