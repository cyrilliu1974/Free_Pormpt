# Anime Class Photo Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Anime Class Photo Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates vibrant group illustrations of anime characters posed in traditional school photo formations. This anime illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana arranges your chosen characters in rows with straight-on framing, soft even lighting, and clean anime line work that captures the nostalgic feel of real class pictures. You customize the character lineup and setting, and the prompt handles composition, spacing, and that friendly group energy that makes class photos memorable. Perfect for fan artists assembling crossover casts, social media creators building character rosters, or anyone who wants to celebrate a favorite anime ensemble in one polished illustration. ● Arrange multiple characters in traditional school photo rows with everyone visible and properly framed. ● Apply soft, even lighting and wide-angle composition that mimics real class photography. ● Deliver clean anime line art with bright, cheerful tones and a fun group atmosphere. ● Customize character lists and settings to match fandoms, original casts, or creative projects. ## Prompt

```
Colorful anime illustration of a class photo with {{characters}} standing together in rows like a traditional school picture. {{setting}}. Straight-on group shot, wide angle, everyone visible and well-framed. Soft even lighting like a real school photo. Clean lines, bright tones. Fun, friendly, energetic group atmosphere.
```
