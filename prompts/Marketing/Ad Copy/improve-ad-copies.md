# Ad Copy Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Ad Copy Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces persuasive advertising copy tailored to your product, audience, and key benefits. This ad copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an expert copywriter who analyzes your target audience's needs and motivations, then structures compelling copy around emotional appeal and conversion triggers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting ready-to-use ad copy organized into headlines, body text, and calls-to-action. Marketing teams use it to quickly generate multiple ad variations for A/B testing, while solo entrepreneurs rely on it to write professional copy without hiring an agency. Reach for this prompt when you need conversion-focused ad copy that balances emotional resonance with clear benefit communication, whether you're writing Facebook ads, Google campaigns, or landing page copy. ● Analyzes target audience psychology to craft messages that resonate with specific customer needs and pain points ● Structures output into actionable sections - headline, body copy, and call-to-action - ready to deploy across ad platforms ● Balances emotional triggers with benefit-driven language to move prospects through the conversion funnel ● Accepts three variables (product/service, target audience, key benefits) to generate customized copy for any offering ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive advertising that drives conversions.

## Task
Create compelling ad copy that resonates with the target audience and highlights the key benefits of the offering. Use clear, direct language structured for maximum impact.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Key Benefits:** {{key-benefits}}

Analyze the audience's needs and motivations, then craft copy that speaks directly to those needs. Use persuasive language, emotional appeal, and strong calls-to-action.

## Output
Structure your ad copy with clear headings and bullet points:

- **Headline:** [attention-grabbing hook]
- **Body Copy:** [benefit-driven narrative with emotional appeal]
- **Call-to-Action:** [clear, compelling directive]

Include key elements as bullet points within each section where appropriate.
```
