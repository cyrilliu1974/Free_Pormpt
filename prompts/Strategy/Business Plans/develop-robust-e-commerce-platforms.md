# E-commerce Strategy Builder Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The E-commerce Strategy Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven, actionable e-commerce plans tailored to your product, target customer, and budget. This e-commerce strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured go-to-market plan that addresses platform selection, marketing channel mix, sales funnel optimization, and customer retention tactics. You provide three variables - your product or service, ideal customer profile, and current online presence and budget - and the prompt outputs an executive summary, platform recommendations with technical requirements, prioritized marketing channels with budget allocation, conversion optimization tactics at each funnel stage, loyalty and lifecycle programs, competitive positioning, and a phased implementation roadmap. Marketing teams, founders launching direct-to-consumer brands, and business consultants use it to build scalable strategies that show how each component supports the others. ● Analyzes product-market fit against your target demographic and current competitive landscape ● Recommends e-commerce platforms with rationale, technical requirements, and integration needs ● Prioritizes high-impact marketing channels with budget allocation and campaign frameworks ● Maps sales funnel optimization tactics from awareness through post-purchase flow ● Designs customer retention programs, loyalty mechanisms, and upsell opportunities ● Provides an implementation roadmap with quick wins, long-term plays, and key metrics to track ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce strategist developing a comprehensive go-to-market plan.

## Task
Create a detailed, actionable e-commerce strategy that covers platform selection, marketing channels, sales funnel optimization, and customer retention. Structure the strategy to show how each component supports the others.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-or-service}}

**Target Customer:** {{ideal-customer-profile}}

**Current State:** {{current-presence-and-budget}}
(Describe your existing online presence, available budget, and main competitors)

Your strategy should be data-driven, scalable, and aligned with current e-commerce best practices. Address:

1. Product-market fit analysis for the target demographic
2. Recommended e-commerce platforms and rationale
3. High-impact online marketing channels
4. Sales funnel optimization tactics
5. Customer retention and lifetime value strategies

## Output
Deliver the strategy using this structure:

**Executive Summary**
- Key recommendations
- Expected outcomes

**Platform Strategy**
- Primary platform(s)
- Technical requirements
- Integration needs

**Marketing Channel Mix**
- Channel priorities
- Budget allocation
- Campaign frameworks

**Sales Funnel Optimization**
- Awareness tactics
- Consideration triggers
- Conversion optimizations
- Post-purchase flow

**Retention & Growth**
- Customer lifecycle programs
- Loyalty mechanisms
- Upsell/cross-sell opportunities

**Competitive Positioning**
- Differentiation strategy
- Competitive advantages to leverage

**Implementation Roadmap**
- Priority phases
- Quick wins vs. long-term plays
- Key metrics to track

Use bullet points for detailed tactics under each section. Show dependencies between strategy components where relevant.
```
