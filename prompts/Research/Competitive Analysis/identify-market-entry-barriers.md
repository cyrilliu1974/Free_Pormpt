# Market Entry Barriers Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Entry Barriers Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that systematically identifies and evaluates obstacles facing businesses entering new markets. This market entry barriers prompt for ChatGPT analyzes competitive intensity, regulatory requirements, capital needs, technological infrastructure, customer acquisition challenges, and distribution access across any business context. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a prioritized markdown table that ranks 5-7 barriers by significance, assesses their impact severity, and delivers tailored mitigation strategies. Strategic planners, business analysts, and entrepreneurs use it to conduct due diligence before market entry, prepare investor pitches, or evaluate expansion opportunities. ● Evaluates six critical dimensions including regulatory compliance costs, incumbent competitive advantages, and supply chain access ● Ranks barriers by significance with High/Medium/Low impact ratings and specific consequence explanations ● Delivers practical, resource-appropriate mitigation strategies tailored to your timeframe and capabilities ● Outputs a clean markdown table format ready for strategic presentations and planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive landscape assessment and market entry strategy.

## Task
Analyze the market landscape and identify the most significant barriers to entry for the specified business. For each barrier, assess its potential impact and develop a practical mitigation strategy.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Consider the following dimensions in your analysis:
- Competitive intensity and incumbent advantages
- Regulatory requirements and compliance costs
- Economic conditions and capital requirements
- Technological infrastructure and expertise needs
- Customer acquisition challenges and brand loyalty
- Distribution channels and supply chain access

## Output
Present your analysis in a markdown table with three columns:

| Barrier | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---------|--------|--------------------|

For each barrier:
- **Barrier**: Clearly name and briefly describe the entry obstacle
- **Impact**: Assess severity (High/Medium/Low) and explain the specific consequences for this business
- **Mitigation Strategy**: Provide actionable, practical steps to overcome or minimize the barrier's effects

Identify 5-7 barriers ranked by significance. Ensure each mitigation strategy is tailored to the available resources and timeframe specified in the business context.
```
