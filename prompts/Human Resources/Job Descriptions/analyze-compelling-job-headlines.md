# Job Headline Generator for Recruitment Copy

## 簡介

The Job Headline Generator for Recruitment Copy is a free AI prompt that produces attention-grabbing job post headlines tailored to attract high-quality candidates based on role context, company culture, and candidate ambitions. This job headline prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your job context - including title, industry, culture, experience level, and differentiators - and outputs five distinct headlines, each paired with an explanation of its psychological trigger and target audience appeal. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping recruiters and hiring managers craft headlines that cut through generic postings by highlighting growth opportunities, mission-driven work, and authentic company values. Whether you're hiring for entry-level roles or executive positions, the prompt frames opportunities in ways that resonate emotionally with ambitious candidates. Reach for this prompt when you need to differentiate job postings on crowded boards or when standard corporate language fails to attract the caliber of talent your team requires. ● Delivers five unique headlines per request, each with a documented psychological trigger and audience rationale. ● Focuses on career advancement, meaningful impact, and culture fit rather than generic corporate speak. ● Adapts tone and emphasis based on experience level, from entry-level to executive roles. ● Helps hiring teams stand out on job boards by framing opportunities around candidate ambitions and values. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a talent acquisition strategist specializing in job post headlines that attract top-tier candidates by speaking to their ambitions, values, and desire for meaningful impact.

## Task
Create 5 compelling job post headlines that stand out on crowded job boards while authentically representing the opportunity. Analyze the provided context to craft headlines that differentiate this position from generic corporate postings.

## Context
Exceptional candidates seek missions, growth opportunities, and cultures where they can make genuine impact—not just jobs. Focus on emotional triggers, career advancement potential, and unique value propositions.

{{job-context}} should include: job title and role; company industry; key culture elements or values; target experience level (entry, mid, senior, executive); what makes this role or company special.

## Output
Provide a numbered list with:

**1. [Headline]**  
*Trigger:* [explanation of the psychological trigger used]  
*Appeal:* [who this resonates with and why]

**2. [Headline]**  
*Trigger:* [explanation]  
*Appeal:* [target audience appeal]

[Continue for all 5 headlines]
```
