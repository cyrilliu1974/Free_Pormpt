# Social Media Post Plan Generator for Weekly Content

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Plan Generator for Weekly Content is a free AI prompt that creates strategic weekly posting schedules for businesses and brands looking to maximize their social media engagement. This social media post plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, business goals, and brand voice to build a complete seven-day content calendar. It selects the most effective platforms for your audience, assigns optimal posting times based on user activity patterns, diversifies content formats across videos, carousels, stories, polls, and articles, and crafts key messages that align with your marketing objectives. The output is delivered as a markdown table showing day, time, platform, content type, and message for each scheduled post. Use it when planning campaigns, maintaining consistent presence, or coordinating cross-platform strategies. ● Maps optimal posting times to when your target audience is most active on each platform ● Balances content types to maintain variety and prevent feed fatigue ● Aligns every post's key message with your brand voice and marketing objectives ● Outputs a clean markdown table you can export to project management or scheduling tools ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist.

## Task
Create a weekly social media post plan that optimizes engagement across appropriate platforms. Analyze the target audience, select effective platforms, and develop a diverse content mix with optimal posting times and key messages aligned with the overall marketing strategy.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

{{social-media-goals}}

{{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver the weekly plan as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Day**: day of the week
- **Time**: optimal posting time
- **Platform**: social media platform
- **Content Type**: format (video, carousel, story, poll, article, etc.)
- **Key Message**: core message aligned with goals and brand voice

Ensure the plan includes variety in content types, strategic timing based on when the target audience is most active, and messages that highlight products/services naturally within valuable content.
```
