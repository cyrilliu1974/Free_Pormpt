# LinkedIn Hashtag Research & Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Hashtag Research & Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a comprehensive, organized table of hashtags designed to maximize post visibility and engagement for any industry or audience on LinkedIn. This LinkedIn hashtag prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific industry context and content focus, then delivers at least 15 hashtags in a three-column markdown table that explains each tag's relevance to your audience and popularity level on LinkedIn. The output includes a strategic mix of broad industry terms, niche-specific tags, and emerging trends, with clear justifications for why each hashtag fits your content and estimated reach metrics (high, medium, or emerging engagement). Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when planning LinkedIn posts, building social media calendars, or auditing your current hashtag strategy to ensure you're capturing both established and rising conversations in your space. ● Provides minimum 15 hashtags with relevance and popularity ratings for each ● Balances broad industry terms, niche-specific tags, and trending topics in one table ● Explains why each hashtag fits your content and audience, not just what it is ● Estimates LinkedIn-specific reach and engagement levels to guide prioritization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing expert specializing in LinkedIn hashtag strategy.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of hashtags that will maximize post visibility and engagement for the specified industry context. Research popular and relevant hashtags, evaluate each based on content relevance and user engagement on LinkedIn, then compile your findings into an organized table.

## Context
Industry and audience context:
{{industry-and-audience}}

Content focus:
{{content-focus}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns: Hashtag, Relevance, and Popularity.

- Provide minimum 15 hashtags
- Include a strategic mix: broad industry terms, niche-specific tags, and trending topics
- Explain Relevance: why this hashtag fits the content and audience
- Explain Popularity: estimated reach and engagement level on LinkedIn (high/medium/emerging)
```
