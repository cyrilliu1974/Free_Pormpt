# Grant Needs Statement Generator for Funding Proposals

## 簡介

The Grant Needs Statement Generator for Funding Proposals is a free AI prompt that helps nonprofits, researchers, and organizations craft compelling needs statements for grant applications. This grant writing prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a structured narrative that opens with the core problem, describes the affected population and their vulnerability, presents your proposed solution, and weaves in statistical evidence throughout. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing needs statements where each sentence builds logically on the previous one to create cohesive forward momentum. Use it when applying for foundation grants, government funding, research awards, or social impact investments where you need to clearly connect a community need to your proposed intervention. ● Structures your needs statement in logical sections that flow from problem identification through solution presentation ● Balances urgency with credibility using strong active verbs and evidence-based claims ● Integrates your statistics and data seamlessly throughout the narrative to substantiate each claim ● Ensures each sentence depends on and extends the meaning of the previous one for maximum coherence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer specializing in compelling needs statements.

## Task
Craft a persuasive needs statement for a grant proposal that articulates the problem, target population, and proposed solution with clarity and urgency. Structure each sentence to build logically upon the previous one, creating cohesive forward momentum throughout the narrative.

## Context
Grant topic and scope: {{grant-topic}}

Target population details: {{target-population}}

Proposed solution: {{proposed-solution}}

Supporting data: {{key-statistics}}

## Output
Write the needs statement as a structured series of paragraphs where:
- The opening establishes the core problem with urgency and specificity
- The next section describes who is affected and why this population is particularly vulnerable
- The following section presents the proposed solution and its approach
- Statistical evidence is woven throughout to substantiate claims
- The conclusion connects the solution directly back to the identified need

Use strong, active verbs. Ensure each sentence depends on and extends the meaning of the preceding sentence. Maintain a tone that balances urgency with credibility.
```
