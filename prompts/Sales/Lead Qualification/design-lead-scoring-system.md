# Lead Scoring System Design Prompt for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Lead Scoring System Design Prompt for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that builds a structured lead qualification framework with scoring criteria, point values, and prioritization thresholds for marketing and sales professionals. This lead scoring prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and conversion factors to produce a complete scoring system with 8-12 weighted criteria covering demographic fit, behavioral signals, and engagement levels. The output is a markdown table mapping each criterion to point values that reflect its importance to conversion, plus recommended score ranges for segmenting leads into cold, warm, and hot categories. Sales teams use it to standardize lead qualification, align marketing and sales on priority definitions, and focus outreach on high-conversion prospects. ● Produces 8-12 weighted scoring criteria based on your unique conversion factors and sales cycle ● Assigns point values that create meaningful segmentation thresholds for pipeline management ● Outputs a structured markdown table with criterion descriptions and recommended score ranges ● Adapts to any business type by analyzing the relationship between lead attributes and conversion likelihood ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in lead qualification and scoring systems.

## Task
Design a comprehensive lead scoring system that prioritizes and segments leads based on their likelihood to convert. Develop scoring criteria tailored to the business context, assign point values that reflect each criterion's relative importance to conversion, and structure the system as a clear, actionable table.

## Context
Business and sales context: {{business-and-sales-context}}

Key factors that predict conversion: {{conversion-factors}}

## Process
1. Analyze the business type, sales cycle, and conversion factors to identify what distinguishes high-value leads
2. Define 8-12 scoring criteria that capture demographic fit, behavioral signals, and engagement level
3. Assign point values to each criterion, weighting them according to their correlation with conversion
4. Ensure the total possible score creates meaningful segmentation thresholds (e.g., cold/warm/hot)

## Output
Present the lead scoring system as a markdown table with these columns: **Criterion** | **Description** | **Point Value**

Include a brief summary above the table explaining the scoring philosophy and suggested score ranges for lead prioritization (e.g., 0-30 = low priority, 31-60 = medium, 61+ = high).
```
