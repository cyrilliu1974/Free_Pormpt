# Video Series Planning Prompt for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Video Series Planning Prompt for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end video series plans for content creators, strategists, and video producers. This video series planning prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every stage of series development: audience analysis, episode arc and topics, production requirements, channel-specific distribution, and engagement tactics. You provide your series concept, target audience and niche, and available resources and channels, and the prompt returns a structured outline with numbered sections and lettered sub-points covering overview, episode planning, production framework, distribution strategy, and growth tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to generate a professional content plan whether you are launching a YouTube series, LinkedIn video campaign, or multi-platform educational content. Use this prompt when you need a roadmap that covers both creative and logistical dimensions of a video series, ensuring audience retention and measurable success from episode one through the finale. ● Analyzes target audience and niche to shape episode topics and series arc ● Defines production format, style guidelines, resource needs, and timeline ● Maps distribution strategy with channel optimization and publishing schedule ● Includes engagement tactics, calls-to-action, and performance tracking methods ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in video series development and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive video series plan that maximizes audience engagement and content effectiveness. Structure the plan as a detailed outline covering all phases from concept to distribution.

## Context
Series concept: {{series-concept}}
Target audience & niche: {{audience-and-niche}}
Production & distribution resources: {{resources-and-channels}}

## Output
Provide a structured outline using this format:

1. Series Overview
   a. Core concept and value proposition
   b. Target audience analysis
   c. Success metrics

2. Episode Planning
   a. Episode topics and titles
   b. Content progression and arc
   c. Episode length and frequency

3. Production Framework
   a. Format and style guidelines
   b. Resource requirements per episode
   c. Production workflow and timeline

4. Distribution Strategy
   a. Channel-specific optimization
   b. Publishing schedule
   c. Cross-promotion tactics

5. Engagement & Growth
   a. Audience retention tactics
   b. Call-to-action strategy
   c. Performance tracking

Use numbered main points and lettered sub-points throughout. Include clear section headings for readability.
```
