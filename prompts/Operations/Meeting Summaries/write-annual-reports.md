# Annual Report Compiler Prompt for Business Analysis

## 簡介

The Annual Report Compiler Prompt for Business Analysis is a free AI prompt that generates structured annual reports summarizing company performance, achievements, and strategic direction for business analysts and executives. This annual report prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a three-column markdown table that organizes financial performance metrics, key milestones achieved during the year, and future strategic goals. You provide your business context and financial year, and the AI compiles a clear, executive-ready overview that highlights essential KPIs, summarizes major achievements, and outlines strategic objectives. Business analysts use it to transform raw performance data into board-ready summaries; finance teams use it to prepare year-end stakeholder communications; executives use it to align leadership on annual outcomes and next-year priorities. Reach for this prompt when you need to consolidate a year's worth of business activity into a concise, structured format that decision-makers can quickly scan and understand. ● Organizes financial KPIs, milestones, and goals into a scannable three-column table ● Tailors output to your specific business context and reporting period ● Produces markdown-formatted reports ready for slides, documents, or stakeholder emails ● Works across all major text AI models for flexible deployment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst preparing a clear, executive-ready annual report.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive overview of the company's performance, achievements, and future direction. Structure the report as a three-column markdown table covering Financial Performance, Key Milestones, and Future Goals.

## Context
The report must:
- Highlight key financial metrics and performance indicators
- Summarize the year's significant achievements and milestones
- Outline strategic objectives for the upcoming year
- Be concise, informative, and tailored to the business context

**Business context:**
{{business-context}}

**Financial year:**
{{financial-year}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table:

| Financial Performance | Key Milestones | Future Goals |
|----------------------|----------------|---------------|
| (KPIs and metrics analysis) | (Major achievements) | (Strategic objectives) |

Each column should provide a comprehensive yet concise overview. Use bullet points within cells for clarity where appropriate.
```
