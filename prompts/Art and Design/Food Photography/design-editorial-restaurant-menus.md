# Editorial Restaurant Menu Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Editorial Restaurant Menu Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates magazine-quality restaurant menus with professional food photography and refined layout structures for chefs, restaurateurs, and designers. This restaurant menu design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces two-column editorial layouts pairing large-format natural-light food photography with clean menu text, complete with dish names, descriptions, and right-aligned pricing. The prompt specifies authentic plating shot from top-down and 45-degree angles on neutral stone or linen backdrops, generous white space, and typography that blends elegant serifs with modern sans-serif typefaces. Real-world applications include print menus for fine dining establishments, seasonal menu updates, pop-up concept boards, and culinary brand development materials. Reach for this prompt when you need print-ready menu visuals that communicate culinary sophistication without commissioning custom photography or hiring a graphic designer. ● Produces two-column grid layouts with natural-light food photography on one side and left-aligned menu text with right-aligned prices on the other. ● Customizable by cuisine style variable to match Mediterranean, Japanese, French, or any culinary tradition with appropriate color palettes and plating aesthetics. ● Specifies authentic dish presentations with olive oil drizzle, fresh herb garnish, and delicate preparations shot on stone tables or neutral linen backdrops. ● Delivers print-ready, high-resolution output with elegant serif headings, clean sans-serif descriptions, uppercase section titles with letter spacing, and generous airy white space throughout. ## Prompt

```
Editorial restaurant menu design with high-end food photography. Two-column grid layout: large natural-light food photography on one side, menu text on the other. Text left-aligned, prices right-aligned, generous airy white space throughout.

{{cuisine-style}}

Food photography style: natural light, top-down and 45-degree angles, shot on stone table or neutral linen backdrop. Show signature dishes with olive oil drizzle, delicate preparations, fresh presentations, herbs as garnish. Authentic plating, no overstyling or fake garnish.

Menu sections:

**COLD STARTERS**
Octopus Carpaccio — Lemon, herbs — €18
Oysters — Fresh daily selection — €22

**MAINS**
Grilled Sea Bass — Olive oil, seasonal greens — €32
Butter Shrimp — Garlic, parsley — €29

Typography: elegant serif for dish names, clean sans-serif for descriptions, section titles in uppercase with letter spacing. Color palette derived from the cuisine mood: soft neutral background, deep dark text, muted accent tone. Premium, airy, refined aesthetic. Modern Mediterranean fine dining feel. Print-ready, high resolution, editorial quality—not a mockup.
```
