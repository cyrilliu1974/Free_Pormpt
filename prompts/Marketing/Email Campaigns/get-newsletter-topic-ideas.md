# Newsletter Topic Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Newsletter Topic Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces diverse, audience-aligned topic ideas for email newsletter creators and content strategists. This newsletter topic ideas prompt for ChatGPT delivers 8–10 curated suggestions, each with a clear title and a 2–3 sentence description explaining its value and relevance. It balances educational pieces, trend commentary, actionable advice, and community-building themes to keep your email content calendar fresh and engaging. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and requires three variables: your target audience, industry, and newsletter focus. Reach for this prompt when planning your next email campaign, quarterly content calendar, or when subscriber engagement starts to plateau. ● Outputs 8–10 formatted topic ideas with titles and value-focused descriptions ● Ensures variety across educational, trend-based, actionable, and community themes ● Customizes suggestions to your specific audience, industry, and newsletter focus ● Saves brainstorming time while maintaining strategic alignment with subscriber interests ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert newsletter strategist.

## Task
Generate a diverse range of engaging newsletter topic ideas that boost subscriber engagement. Each idea should align with current trends, audience interests, and industry-specific relevance.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Newsletter focus:** {{newsletter-focus}}

## Output
Present 8–10 topic ideas as a numbered list. For each item, provide:
- A clear, compelling topic title
- A brief description (2–3 sentences) that highlights the value to readers and explains why it will resonate with the audience

Ensure variety across the list—mix educational content, trend commentary, actionable advice, and community-building themes.
```
