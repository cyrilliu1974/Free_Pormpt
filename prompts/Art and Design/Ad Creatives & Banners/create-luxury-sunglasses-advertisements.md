# Luxury Sunglasses Advertisement Grid Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Sunglasses Advertisement Grid Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates ultra-premium three-panel fashion advertisements for sunglasses brands and eyewear campaigns. This luxury sunglasses advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 1×3 horizontal grid composition featuring a tight portrait with visible branding, a confident street-style lifestyle shot, and a studio product close-up - all with high-fashion editorial lighting, cinematic contrast, and commercial-grade polish. Fashion marketers, brand designers, and creative agencies use it to create campaign visuals that mirror glossy magazine spreads and global advertising standards. ● Portrait frame with close-up face, refined features, and clearly readable sunglasses logo captured in soft natural daylight with shallow depth of field. ● Street-style lifestyle shot showing the subject walking confidently through a modern urban setting, product clearly visible as the focal fashion item. ● Product-only close-up on a clean surface with studio lighting, sharp focus on materials and craftsmanship, and prominent brand name conveying exclusivity. ● Visual output styled for ultra-realistic rendering, rich color grading, realistic reflections, and high-end commercial finish suitable for global fashion campaigns. ## Prompt
