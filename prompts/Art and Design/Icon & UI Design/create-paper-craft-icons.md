# Paper Craft Icon Generator

## 簡介

The Paper Craft Icon Generator is a free AI prompt that creates handcrafted, textured paper-style icons centered on a pure white background for designers and brand creators. This paper craft icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces icons made from colorful cut paper with visible creases, layered shapes, and soft drop shadows that give depth and a floating effect. The prompt specifies soft studio lighting to emphasize paper edges and texture, while maintaining a minimal, playful composition with generous negative space around the subject. Use it to design logos, emoji-style graphics, app icons, or brand assets that need a tactile, handmade aesthetic without the expense of physical craft production. Reach for this prompt when you need icon designs that stand out with warmth and personality, or when clients want a modern-minimal look with organic, approachable textures. ● Produces centered icons with soft shadows and layered paper textures that mimic real craft work. ● Maintains clean negative space and minimal composition for modern, versatile designs. ● Highlights paper edges and creases with studio lighting for realistic tactile detail. ● Works with any subject variable to create custom emoji, logo, or UI icons in seconds. ## Prompt

```
A paper craft-style {{subject}} floating on a pure white background. The subject is handcrafted from colorful cut paper with visible textures, creases, and layered shapes. It casts a soft drop shadow beneath, giving a sense of lightness and depth. The design is minimal, playful, and clean — centered in the frame with lots of negative space. Use soft studio lighting to highlight the paper texture and edges.
```
