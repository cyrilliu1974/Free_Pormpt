# Social Media Post Generator for Instagram & Facebook

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Generator for Instagram & Facebook is a free AI prompt that creates complete, ready-to-publish social media content tailored to your product, audience, and brand voice. This social media post prompt for ChatGPT produces structured posts that include platform-optimized captions (respecting character limits for Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest), strategic hashtag mixes blending popular and niche terms, emojis for readability, clear calls-to-action, and detailed visual descriptions to guide your design or photography. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your product details, target audience, key features, platform choice, and brand voice to output content that follows each platform's best practices. Marketing teams, social media managers, and small business owners use it to streamline content creation while maintaining consistency and engagement across channels. ● Outputs platform-specific captions that respect character limits: 50 words for Instagram, 80 for Facebook, 30 for Pinterest ● Combines 8-15 hashtags mixing high-traffic and niche tags to maximize discoverability without spam ● Includes visual composition briefs so designers and photographers know exactly what image will pair with the copy ● Embeds emojis and calls-to-action strategically to drive comments, shares, and conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist specializing in high-converting posts for Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest.

## Task
Create a complete social media post optimized for {{platform}} that showcases the product, drives engagement, and encourages action. The post must include:

- A caption that highlights key features and benefits in the brand's voice (50 words max for Instagram, 80 for Facebook, 30 for Pinterest)
- A strategic mix of popular and niche hashtags for discoverability
- Relevant emojis that enhance readability and appeal
- A clear call-to-action
- A brief description of the ideal accompanying visual (image or graphic style, composition, mood)
- Optional: A question or prompt to encourage comments

## Context
**Product:** {{product}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Key features/benefits:** {{features-benefits}}

**Platform:** {{platform}} (Instagram/Facebook/Pinterest)

**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver the post as:

**Visual description:**  
[Describe the image/graphic]

**Caption:**  
[The post copy with emojis]

**Hashtags:**  
[List 8-15 hashtags]

**CTA:**  
[Clear action statement]

Ensure all elements follow platform best practices and character limits.
```
