# Interactive Webinar Concept Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Interactive Webinar Concept Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured webinar concepts tailored to your target audience, subject area, and platform capabilities. This interactive webinar prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces five complete webinar concepts in a markdown table format. Each concept includes a benefit-driven title under ten words, a persuasive 2-3 sentence description explaining value and takeaways, specific interactive elements matched to your platform's features (polls, breakout rooms, live demos, Q&A segments, whiteboards), and a concrete call-to-action designed to drive registration. You provide your audience and subject area, list your webinar platform's interactive capabilities, and share any past successful topics or duration constraints; the prompt then generates concepts that build on what works while incorporating engagement tactics proven to retain attention and encourage participation. Content strategists, marketing teams, and educators planning webinar campaigns use this prompt to rapidly develop multiple concepts for testing and audience feedback. It is especially useful when you need to brainstorm fresh angles on familiar topics or match webinar formats to specific platform strengths. ● Produces five distinct webinar concepts in a clean table format with titles, descriptions, interactive tactics, and calls-to-action ● Tailors each concept to your specific audience, subject matter, and the interactive features your webinar platform supports ● Incorporates lessons from your past webinar successes and respects duration or format constraints you specify ● Focuses on engagement and conversion by pairing each concept with concrete interactive elements and registration hooks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert webinar content strategist specializing in interactive audience engagement and conversion-focused programming.

## Task
Generate 5 compelling webinar concepts tailored to the target audience and subject area. Each concept must include an attention-grabbing title, a persuasive description, specific interactive elements, and a clear call-to-action that drives registration.

## Context
**Audience & Subject**: {{audience-and-subject}}
(Describe your target audience and the subject area you want to cover)

**Platform Capabilities**: {{platform-features}}
(List any unique interactive features your webinar platform offers—polls, breakout rooms, Q&A, whiteboards, etc.)

**Past Success & Constraints**: {{webinar-history}}
(Note previous successful topics to build on and your ideal webinar duration)

## Output
Present your webinar concepts in a markdown table with these columns:

| Webinar Title | Description | Interactive Elements | Call-to-Action |
|---------------|-------------|---------------------|----------------|

Each row should contain:
- **Webinar Title**: Clear, benefit-driven, under 10 words
- **Description**: 2-3 sentences explaining value and key takeaways
- **Interactive Elements**: Specific engagement tactics (polls, live demos, Q&A segments, breakouts, worksheets)
- **Call-to-Action**: Concrete registration hook with urgency or incentive
```
