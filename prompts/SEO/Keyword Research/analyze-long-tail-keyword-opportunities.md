# Long-Tail Keyword Research and Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Long-Tail Keyword Research and Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates organized lists of 45-60 long-tail keywords segmented by search intent, geographic targeting, and voice search optimization for SEO strategists and digital marketers. This long-tail keyword research prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your business context and returns three strategic categories: intent-based keywords mapping to awareness, consideration, and decision stages; location-specific keywords with geographic modifiers and proximity patterns; and voice search keywords framed as natural questions. Each keyword includes search volume estimates, competition analysis, conversion potential scoring, and ranking difficulty assessment in a structured markdown table. Use it when planning content strategies, optimizing local SEO campaigns, or identifying quick-win opportunities in competitive niches. ● Organizes 15-20 keywords per category with clear intent signals, geographic modifiers, and conversational question formats. ● Evaluates each keyword across five dimensions: search volume range, competition level, priority ranking, conversion potential, and strategic rationale. ● Identifies buyer journey stages from awareness through decision, matching keywords to customer search psychology. ● Highlights quick-win opportunities where low competition meets meaningful search volume and strong conversion intent. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an SEO keyword strategist specializing in long-tail keyword research, search intent analysis, and local market optimization.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive long-tail keyword list organized into three strategic categories:

1. **Intent-based keywords** – capturing awareness, consideration, and decision stages of the buyer journey
2. **Location-specific keywords** – incorporating geographic modifiers, neighborhood names, and proximity search patterns
3. **Voice search keywords** – using natural language, question formats, and conversational phrasing

For each keyword, assess search volume range, competition level, conversion potential, and ranking difficulty.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Provide 15–20 keywords per category in a markdown table:

| Keyword | Category | Search Volume Estimate | Competition Level | Priority Level | Strategic Notes |
|---------|----------|------------------------|-------------------|----------------|------------------|

**Categories:** Intent-Based / Local / Voice Search  
**Competition & Priority Levels:** Low / Medium / High

Organize with clear section headers for each category. Include strategic notes on why each keyword matters, expected user intent, and quick-win opportunities.
```
