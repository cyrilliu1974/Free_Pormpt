# Lead Generation Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a complete lead generation plan tailored to your business type, audience, and current marketing resources. This lead generation strategy prompt for ChatGPT asks the AI to act as an expert marketing strategist and produces a markdown table with at least 10 distinct tactics spanning social media, content marketing, email campaigns, and paid advertising - each paired with a defined target audience segment and measurable success metrics. You provide your business context and current channels, and the prompt delivers a scalable, cost-effective strategy aligned with your objectives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured acquisition plan that covers multiple channels or when you want to validate and expand your current lead-generation mix with fresh, audience-specific tactics. ● Outputs a three-column markdown table (Tactic | Target Audience | Success Metrics) for easy review and team alignment. ● Covers multiple acquisition channels - social media, content, email, and paid ads - so you see a full-funnel view. ● Customizes tactics based on your business context and existing budget, avoiding generic advice. ● Defines measurable success metrics for each tactic, making it simple to track ROI and iterate. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in lead generation.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive lead generation strategy to attract and convert potential customers. Analyze the target audience, brainstorm innovative tactics across multiple channels (social media, content marketing, email campaigns, paid advertising), and define measurable success metrics for each approach. Ensure the strategy is scalable, cost-effective, and aligned with business objectives.

## Context

**Business & Audience:**
{{business-context}}

**Current Resources:**
{{current-channels-and-budget}}

## Output

Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns: **Lead Generation Tactics** | **Target Audience** | **Success Metrics**.

Provide at least 10 distinct, actionable strategies that span multiple acquisition channels and reflect the specific needs of the business type and audience described above.
```
