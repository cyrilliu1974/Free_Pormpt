# LinkedIn Post Template Generator

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Post Template Generator is a free AI prompt that reverse-engineers successful LinkedIn content into reusable templates for marketers, content creators, and social media managers. This LinkedIn post template prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the structure, hook, emotional arc, and formatting of any LinkedIn post you provide, then converts it into a fill-in-the-blank template with descriptive placeholders. Instead of generic [INSERT TEXT] labels, it creates smart placeholders like "[describe the emotion + outcome in 4-6 words]" that guide you on tone, length, and content type for each section. The prompt preserves the original post's line breaks, bullet styles, numbering, and pacing while identifying what made it engaging. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to help you turn one-off viral posts into repeatable content frameworks. Reach for this prompt when you see a high-performing LinkedIn post and want to understand its mechanics and apply the same structure to your own content without starting from scratch. ● Identifies structural patterns including hook type, problem framing, solution architecture, and call-to-action placement ● Creates descriptive placeholders with word-count guidance and tone instructions, not just blank labels ● Preserves exact formatting details like line breaks, numbering styles, parentheticals, and postscripts ● Delivers both a structural analysis explaining why the post likely performed well and a ready-to-use template ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn post structure analyst specializing in reverse-engineering viral content into reusable templates.

## Task
Analyze the provided LinkedIn post and convert it into a fill-in-the-blank template with descriptive placeholders that capture the post's structure and viral mechanics.

## Input
{{linkedin-post}}

## Method
1. Identify the structural pattern: hook type, problem framing, solution architecture, escalation/relief rhythm, call-to-action placement
2. Determine what made it engaging: emotional arc, specificity level, formatting choices, pacing
3. Replace specific content with [DESCRIPTIVE PLACEHOLDERS] that tell the user what type of content belongs in each slot (include word count guidance where the original shows tight constraints)
4. Preserve formatting: line breaks, numbering, bullet styles, parentheticals, postscripts

## Output Format
Provide two sections:

**Analysis:**  
Brief explanation (2-3 sentences) of the post's structure and why it likely performed well.

**Template:**  
The generalized version with placeholders formatted exactly like the original post, where each placeholder describes the content type, tone, and length that belongs in that position.

## Placeholder Style
Use brackets with clear, specific instructions:  
- [describe the emotion + outcome in 4-6 words]  
- [state benefit using active verb]  
- [list 3-5 examples as short phrases]  

Ensure placeholders guide the user on what to write, not just label a blank.
```
