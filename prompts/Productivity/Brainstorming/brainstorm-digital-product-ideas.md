# Digital Product Idea Generator for Market Opportunities

## 簡介

The Digital Product Idea Generator for Market Opportunities is a free AI prompt that produces commercially viable digital product concepts with detailed market and development analysis for entrepreneurs, strategists, and creators exploring new revenue streams. This digital product brainstorming prompt for ChatGPT generates 8-12 structured product ideas based on your specified niche and audience, complete with development difficulty ratings (1-5 scale), market demand assessments, target use cases, and suggested pricing ranges with rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing market gaps, competition levels, and monetization potential while considering current trends and emerging technologies. Each idea includes a value proposition, the primary problem it solves, and the customer segment it serves, organized by product type and complexity to help you compare time investment against revenue potential. Reach for this prompt when you need to explore product opportunities in a specific market segment, validate concepts before investing development resources, or span multiple price points and complexity levels in your product portfolio planning. ● Produces 8-12 product ideas with genuine problem-solution fit and clear value propositions for your specified niche and audience. ● Rates each concept by development difficulty (1-5 scale), market demand level, and suggested price range with supporting rationale. ● Incorporates your product preferences and constraints (technology stack, time horizon, budget limits) into every recommendation. ● Organizes ideas by product type and complexity so you can filter by resource availability and strategic priorities. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital product strategist and market researcher specializing in identifying untapped market opportunities and scalable revenue streams.

## Task
Generate 8–12 commercially viable digital product ideas based on the provided market context. Each idea must solve a genuine problem, offer a clear value proposition, and be developable with reasonable resources while maintaining high profit margins.

## Context
Analyze market gaps, competition levels, and monetization potential for {{niche-and-audience}}. Consider current market trends, emerging technologies, and underserved customer needs. Span different complexity levels and price points to enable strategic comparison.

Additional constraints: {{product-preferences-and-constraints}}

## Output
Group ideas by product type and development complexity. Present each idea in this format:

**Product Name**
- **Description**: Compelling overview and value proposition
- **Target Use Case**: Primary problem solved and customer segment served
- **Development Difficulty**: 1–5 scale
- **Market Demand**: High / Medium / Low with key demand indicators
- **Suggested Price Range**: Specific dollar range with rationale

Organize products to facilitate comparison by complexity, time investment, and revenue potential.
```
