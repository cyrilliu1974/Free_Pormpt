# Content Calendar Schedule Builder

## 簡介

The Content Calendar Schedule Builder is a free AI prompt that creates organized, multi-channel publishing schedules for marketers, content teams, and strategists. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and time period to produce a structured markdown table mapping every piece of content by date, format, topic, target audience, and distribution channel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, balancing blog posts, videos, infographics, emails, and social content to maintain topic diversity and strategic variety. Use it when you need to plan weeks or months of content across multiple platforms while ensuring each piece aligns with your marketing objectives and audience needs. ● Outputs a date-based table mapping content type, topic, audience segment, and publication platform for every scheduled piece ● Balances format variety across blog posts, video, email, social media, and other content types to maintain engagement ● Aligns every calendar entry with your stated business goals, audience characteristics, and industry trends ● Maintains strategic topic diversity and appropriate channel distribution throughout the planning period ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in multi-channel content planning and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content calendar that balances content types, topics, and distribution channels to support marketing objectives and audience needs.

## Context
Business and audience profile:
{{business-context}}

Time period for this calendar:
{{time-period}}

Analyze the business goals, audience characteristics, and relevant industry trends. Brainstorm content ideas that serve the stated objectives and resonate with the target audience. Organize ideas into a cohesive schedule that maintains topic diversity, varied content formats, and appropriate channel distribution.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:
- **Date**: specific publication date
- **Content Type**: format (blog post, video, infographic, email, social post, etc.)
- **Topic**: clear content subject or theme
- **Target Audience**: primary audience segment
- **Publication Channel**: distribution platform

Ensure the calendar shows strategic variety across content types and topics while maintaining consistent alignment with the stated business goals and audience needs.
```
