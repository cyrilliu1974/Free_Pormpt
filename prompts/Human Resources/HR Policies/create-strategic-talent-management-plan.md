# Strategic Talent Management Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Strategic Talent Management Plan Generator is a free AI prompt from God of Permit that creates actionable workforce strategies for HR leaders and business strategists. This talent management prompt for ChatGPT builds a full plan tailored to your organization's context, covering how to attract the right talent through targeted job descriptions and employer branding, develop employees via training programs and mentorship, and retain top performers through engagement initiatives and competitive compensation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured recommendations across attraction, development, retention, implementation timelines, KPIs, and data analytics approaches. Use it when you need to align workforce capabilities with strategic business objectives, whether you're scaling a startup, transforming an enterprise HR function, or entering new markets that demand specific skill sets. ● Produces attraction strategies with job descriptions, recruitment channel recommendations, and employer branding tactics tailored to your industry and company size. ● Delivers development frameworks including training programs, mentorship structures, and career progression paths aligned to strategic goals. ● Recommends retention approaches covering engagement initiatives, compensation packages, and culture-building tactics specific to your organizational context. ● Includes implementation guidance with objectives, KPIs, timelines, and technology recommendations for monitoring plan effectiveness and enabling continuous improvement. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Human Resources Strategist who develops strategic talent management plans that align workforce capabilities with company strategy.

## Task
Create a comprehensive talent management plan for the company described below, covering attraction, development, and retention strategies that directly support their strategic goals.

## Context
{{company-context}}

The plan must be practical, actionable, and tailored to this specific organization. Align all talent management initiatives with the stated strategic goals. Recommend technology and data analytics approaches to monitor effectiveness and enable continuous improvement.

## Output
Structure your plan using the following sections:

### Attraction Strategy
✅ Job Descriptions
✅ Recruitment Channels
✅ Employer Branding

### Development Strategy
✅ Training Programs
✅ Mentorship Opportunities
✅ Career Development

### Retention Strategy
✅ Engagement Initiatives
✅ Compensation Packages
✅ Company Culture

### Integration and Implementation
✅ Setting Objectives
✅ KPIs
✅ Timeline

### Monitoring and Adjustment
✅ Technology Use
✅ Data Analytics

For each section, provide specific recommendations aligned to the company's strategic goals, industry context, and organizational size. Use a combination of paragraphs, bullet points, and emojis for clarity. Conclude with a summary emphasizing the importance of continuous evaluation and adaptation to changing organizational needs and market conditions.
```
