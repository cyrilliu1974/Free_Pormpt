# Content Curation Strategy Builder for Social Media

## 簡介

The Content Curation Strategy Builder for Social Media is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed content strategy framework for brands and marketers looking to boost awareness and engagement on specific platforms. This content curation prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your chosen social media platform, audience demographics, and brand context to produce a markdown table with at least five distinct content types, each paired with recommended posting frequency and measurable goals. It then provides 2-3 paragraphs explaining how the strategy adapts to platform-specific features and audience behavior patterns. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for any text-generation workflow. Marketing teams use it to plan Instagram story sequences, LinkedIn thought leadership calendars, TikTok video schedules, and cross-platform campaigns grounded in trending formats and engagement data. Use this prompt when you need a documented content plan that balances variety, consistency, and strategic goals without starting from a blank page. ● Generates a three-column table (Content Types, Frequency, Goals) with at least five rows for diverse formats. ● Ties every content type to measurable brand-awareness or engagement objectives. ● Includes follow-up analysis paragraphs that explain how the strategy aligns with platform algorithms and audience behavior. ● Accepts variables for platform, audience demographics, brand identity, and industry so the strategy fits your specific context. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content strategist building a content curation strategy to boost brand awareness and engagement.

## Task
Develop a detailed content strategy that outlines content types, posting frequency, and goals for each format. Work systematically through the platform's unique features, audience behavior patterns, and trending formats to maximize engagement and reach.

## Context
{{social-media-platform-and-audience}}

{{brand-and-industry-context}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with three columns: **Content Types**, **Frequency**, and **Goals**. Include at least 5 distinct content types, each with specific posting frequency and measurable goals tied to brand awareness and audience engagement. After the table, provide 2-3 paragraphs explaining how the strategy leverages platform-specific features and audience behavior.
```
