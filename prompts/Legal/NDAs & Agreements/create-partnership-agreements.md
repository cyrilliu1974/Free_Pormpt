# Partnership Agreement Template Generator

## 簡介

The Partnership Agreement Template Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts formal partnership agreements for business attorneys and entrepreneurs establishing multi-partner ventures. This partnership agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a seven-section legal document that defines partner contributions, profit and loss sharing percentages, decision-making authority, dispute resolution pathways, and termination procedures. You provide the partnership details - business purpose, partner names, capital and expertise contributions, profit-sharing ratios, and management structure - and the prompt returns a formatted agreement with a preamble, numbered clauses, and signature section. Law firms use it to accelerate client drafting; startups and small businesses use it to establish clear operating terms before launch. Reach for this prompt when forming an LLC partnership, joint venture, or general partnership that requires written governance terms and equitable allocation of profits, losses, and decision rights. ● Creates a complete seven-section agreement including partner contributions, profit-sharing, decision-making processes, dispute resolution, and termination provisions. ● Uses clear legal terminology and defines key terms to minimize ambiguity and future disputes. ● Includes a formal signature section with witness language and date fields compliant with contract execution standards. ● Structures dispute resolution as a tiered process: good faith negotiation, mediation, and binding arbitration. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a seasoned business attorney specializing in drafting comprehensive partnership agreements.

## Task

Draft a detailed partnership agreement that covers all essential legal and operational aspects of the business relationship. Use clear legal language, define all key terms, and ensure the agreement is fair and equitable to all parties.

## Context

{{partnership-details}} should include: the business venture and its purpose, names of all partners, each partner's contributions (capital, assets, expertise, time), profit/loss sharing percentages, frequency of distributions, who handles day-to-day operations, and any other material terms.

## Output

Structure the agreement with these sections:

**PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT**

Preamble stating the effective date, partner names, and business venture.

**1. PARTNERSHIP NAME AND PURPOSE**
- Legal name of the partnership
- Stated purpose and scope of business activities

**2. PARTNER CONTRIBUTIONS**
- Itemized list of each partner's contributions (cash, property, services, expertise)

**3. PROFIT-SHARING ARRANGEMENTS**
- Profit and loss sharing proportions for each partner
- Distribution frequency (quarterly, annually, etc.)

**4. DECISION-MAKING PROCESSES**
- Major business decisions requiring unanimous consent
- Authority for day-to-day operational decisions

**5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION**
- Sequential process: good faith negotiations → mediation → binding arbitration

**6. TERMINATION**
- Circumstances permitting termination
- Asset distribution method upon dissolution

**7. AMENDMENTS**
- Requirement for written consent of all partners to modify the agreement

**SIGNATURE SECTION**

Include signature lines with date fields for all partners, preceded by "IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Partners have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written."
```
