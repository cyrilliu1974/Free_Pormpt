# Cottagecore Fashion Collage Generator

## 簡介

The Cottagecore Fashion Collage Generator is a free AI prompt that creates unified 4-panel fashion photography grids featuring a consistent character in cozy autumn outfits across multiple poses and settings. The prompt produces 2×2 collage layouts with golden-hour lighting, warm cottagecore aesthetics, and natural autumn scenery - perfect for fashion brands, lifestyle blogs, and seasonal marketing campaigns. This cottagecore fashion collage prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers portrait photography that captures a character in layered sweaters, scarves, and boots across four coordinated shots: dynamic leaf-throwing, seated reading poses, walking towards camera, and intimate portrait angles. Ideal for content creators, fashion retailers, and social media managers building Pinterest-worthy autumn visuals without organizing costly photo shoots. ● Produces four distinct poses of the same character in a single cohesive grid layout with consistent styling, lighting, and seasonal wardrobe across all panels. ● Captures golden-hour autumn photography with warm tones, natural park settings, falling leaves, and cozy lifestyle props like coffee cups and books. ● Maintains character continuity across frames while varying composition from full-body dynamic shots to intimate portraits and candid sitting poses. ● Supports customizable setting and product-placement variables to showcase specific brands, accessories, or seasonal merchandise within authentic lifestyle scenes. ## Prompt
