# Black-and-White Coloring Page Generator

## 簡介

The Black-and-White Coloring Page Generator is a free AI prompt that creates print-ready line art coloring pages for educators, parents, publishers, and artists. This coloring page prompt for Midjourney produces clean black-and-white line drawings with no shading or color fills, featuring your chosen subject and action with customizable line styles and aspect ratios. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to deliver simple, clear backgrounds optimized for coloring with crayons, markers, or digital tools. Reach for this prompt whenever you need original coloring pages for classroom activities, children's books, therapy materials, or personal projects without the hassle of hand-drawing or licensing stock art. ● Outputs black-and-white line art with customizable line styles (bold, delicate, varied weight) and no shading or gradients. ● Accepts any subject and action combination, from animals and vehicles to fantasy characters and everyday scenes. ● Supports custom aspect ratios to fit standard paper sizes, book layouts, or digital canvases. ● Produces simple, clear backgrounds that keep the focus on the subject and make coloring intuitive for all ages. ## Prompt
