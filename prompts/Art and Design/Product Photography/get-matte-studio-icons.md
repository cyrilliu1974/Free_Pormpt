# Matte Studio Icon Retexture Prompt

## 簡介

The Matte Studio Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms uploaded images into polished product-style renders with professional matte finishes for designers, marketers, and content creators. This matte studio retexture prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana preserves the original silhouette and geometry while applying soft-touch matte plastic, brushed metal, and composite shell materials with diffused softbox lighting, light drop shadows, and catalog-grade clarity. It works by analyzing the object's shape and mapping new materials to seams, contours, and curves without distorting the form, producing high-fidelity renders suitable for product catalogs, marketing assets, and portfolio presentations. Reach for this prompt when you need to elevate product photography, create consistent icon sets, or prototype packaging visuals with a professional studio aesthetic. ● Locks object shapes and silhouettes while retexturing with materials like matte plastic, brushed metal, perforated mesh, and rubberized surfaces in graphite, beige, and cool gray palettes. ● Applies diffused softbox lighting with soft highlights along curves, light drop shadows, and clean studio gradients for catalog-ready realism. ● Preserves fine details including seams, surface transitions, and contours with geometry-sensitive mapping and sharp object clarity. ● Outputs front-facing or slightly top-down product renders with smooth background fades and high-fidelity separation for marketing and portfolio use. ## Prompt
