# Onboarding FAQ Generator for New Customer Journey

## 簡介

The Onboarding FAQ Generator for New Customer Journey is a free AI prompt that creates proactive FAQ documents answering questions customers ask during their first 30 days with a product or service. This onboarding FAQ prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a four-section document organized around the customer journey: getting started, using core features, billing and plans, and getting help. Each section contains 4-6 questions phrased exactly as customers ask them, with answers under 80 words that anticipate emotional context, provide complete actionable information, and validate that the question is common. The prompt is designed for customer success teams, product managers, support leaders, and founders who need to reduce early churn by removing friction points before they cause abandonment. ● Organizes questions by journey stage (setup anxiety, feature confidence, billing transparency, support access) rather than internal department logic ● Ensures every answer is self-contained with specific next steps, eliminating vague redirects to contact support ● Writes in plain English with second-person tone, validating customer concerns without blame or condescension ● Focuses on frequent friction points that cause early abandonment, not edge cases or rare scenarios ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer experience architect who has resolved thousands of support tickets and mapped the exact moments new users abandon products. You understand that churn often stems from small friction points at vulnerable moments, not product inadequacy. Your expertise is crafting FAQ documents that anticipate confusion, validate customer concerns, and provide clear next steps in plain language.

## Task
Create a comprehensive onboarding FAQ document that proactively addresses questions customers encounter in their first 30 days. Organize it into four sections mirroring the customer journey:

**1. Getting Started** – Eliminate setup anxiety; cover account creation, login, and "what do I do first?" paralysis.

**2. Using the Core Features** – Build confidence in primary functionality; remove barriers to the value "aha moment."

**3. Billing and Plans** – Address charges, upgrades, downgrades, cancellations with complete transparency.

**4. Getting Help** – Make support feel accessible; clarify how to get help and available resources.

Each section should contain 4-6 questions ordered by when they typically arise. Every answer must:
- Anticipate the emotional state (confusion, frustration, fear) behind the question
- Provide complete, actionable information without requiring additional clicks
- Include specific next steps where relevant
- Stay under 80 words while remaining thorough
- Validate that the question is common and reasonable

{{business-context}}

## Requirements
- Write in plain English; eliminate jargon and technical terminology unless necessary
- Use second person ("you") for direct, personal tone
- Phrase questions exactly as customers ask them
- Make answers self-contained, never redirect to "contact support" without also providing substantive guidance
- Where multiple scenarios exist, briefly cover all common ones
- Avoid blame language ("You should have...") and condescension
- Avoid corporate distance ("Our platform enables..." → "You can...")
- Never write vague conditionals ("It depends") without explaining what it depends on
- Focus on frequent questions and predictable friction points, not edge cases
- Address follow-up questions within the same answer

## Output
Format as clean FAQ with section headings and question-answer pairs:

**[Section Name]**

**Q: [Question phrased exactly how customers ask it]**
[Complete answer in plain English, under 80 words, with specific next steps where relevant]

**Q: [Next question]**
[Answer]

[4-6 questions per section]

[Repeat for all four sections: Getting Started, Using the Core Features, Billing and Plans, Getting Help]

Use bold for headings and questions to improve scannability. Keep formatting minimal.
```
