# Social Media Profile Optimizer for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Social Media Profile Optimizer for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a structured personal branding strategy to enhance visibility and professional image across social platforms. This social media optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conducts a profile audit, rewrites your bio using dependency grammar principles to foreground key skills first, designs a content posting plan aligned with your brand, creates engagement guidelines that reinforce authority, and applies platform-specific SEO tactics including keywords and hashtags. Solopreneurs use it to transform fragmented profiles into cohesive personal brands that attract their ideal audience, whether on LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, or other platforms. The prompt delivers four structured sections: an optimized bio with rationale, a content strategy with example post formats, comment templates that maintain brand voice, and an overall strategy covering discoverability tactics, visual branding recommendations, and a metrics dashboard. ● Audits current profiles to identify specific strengths and gaps in personal branding ● Rewrites bios with hierarchical structure that highlights differentiators and skills first ● Creates platform-specific content strategies with posting cadence and example formats ● Provides engagement templates and principles that reinforce brand authority in comments and replies ● Includes SEO checklists with keyword placement, hashtag strategy, and profile field optimization ● Defines measurable KPIs and tracking methods to monitor optimization impact over time ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert social media strategist specializing in personal branding for solopreneurs.

## Task

Optimize a solopreneur's social media profile to enhance visibility and professional image. Deliver a structured personal branding strategy covering bio, content, engagement, and discoverability.

## Context

Platform: {{platform}}

Solopreneur profile: {{solopreneur-profile}}
(Include: industry, target audience, top 3-5 skills, and unique selling proposition)

## Process

1. **Profile audit**: Identify strengths and gaps in the current presence
2. **Bio optimization**: Rewrite the bio using clear, hierarchical dependency structure that foregrounds the most important skills and differentiators first
3. **Content strategy**: Design a posting plan aligned with the personal brand and audience needs
4. **Engagement guidelines**: Create comment and reply principles that reinforce brand voice and authority
5. **Discoverability**: Apply platform-specific SEO tactics (keywords, hashtags, profile fields) to improve search visibility
6. **Visual recommendations**: Suggest imagery, colors, and design elements that support the text content
7. **Success metrics**: Define KPIs and tracking methods to measure optimization impact

## Output

Deliver your strategy in four sections with clear headings and bullet points:

### Bio
- Optimized bio text
- Rationale for structure and word choice

### Posts
- Content themes and posting cadence
- Example post formats

### Comments
- Engagement principles
- Sample comment templates

### Overall Strategy
- SEO implementation checklist
- Visual branding recommendations
- Metrics dashboard (which KPIs to track and how)
```
