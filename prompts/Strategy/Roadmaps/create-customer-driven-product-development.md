# Customer Co-Creation Process Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Co-Creation Process Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a structured framework for involving customers throughout the entire product development lifecycle. This customer co-creation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through designing an 8–10 step process tailored to your product domain, target audience, and experience level. It generates a detailed roadmap covering ideation, development, launch, and post-launch phases, complete with governance structures, employee enablement strategies, and alignment considerations. Product managers, innovation teams, and UX researchers use it to transform customers from passive buyers into active collaborators who shape features, validate assumptions, and champion the final product. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a ready-to-implement co-creation framework that balances methodological rigor with practical action steps. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically integrate customer insights across the innovation cycle, whether you're launching a new co-creation initiative or refining an existing collaborative development practice. ● Generates 8–10 actionable steps covering ideation, design, development, launch, and feedback loops ● Includes governance models, training plans, and strategic alignment considerations ● Adapts output based on product domain, target customer profile, and organizational maturity ● Emphasizes empathy-building tactics and shared ownership mechanisms between company and customers ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in customer co-creation and collaborative product development, skilled at designing processes that involve customers throughout the innovation lifecycle.

## Task

Design a comprehensive customer co-creation process that engages customers and incorporates their insights from ideation through launch and beyond. The process should foster collaboration, empathy, and shared ownership between the company and its customers.

## Context

**Product domain:** {{product-domain}}

**Target customers:** {{target-customers}}

**Company's co-creation experience level:** {{experience-level}}

## Output

Provide your response in the following structure:

# Customer Co-Creation Process

## Introduction
Briefly explain the importance and benefits of customer co-creation in product development for this specific context.

## Step-by-Step Process
Outline 8-10 detailed, actionable steps that cover the entire product development lifecycle. Each step should include specific actions to engage customers effectively and build collaboration, empathy, and shared ownership.

## Key Considerations
Highlight 3-5 critical factors for successful implementation, including:
- Governance structures
- Employee training and enablement
- Alignment with overall product strategy
- Other relevant organizational or operational factors

## Conclusion
Summarize the key points and emphasize the potential impact of this co-creation process on product success and customer relationships.

---

**Approach:** Focus on principles and methodology rather than specific tools or technologies. Ensure all guidance is practical and tailored to the product domain, customer base, and experience level provided.
```
