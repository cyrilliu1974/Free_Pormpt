# Personal Growth Book Insights Extractor

## 簡介

The Personal Growth Book Insights Extractor is a free AI prompt that distills actionable productivity insights from personal development literature for readers, coaches, and lifelong learners. This personal growth book prompt for ChatGPT analyzes multiple books on a chosen productivity topic and extracts their core concepts, strategies, and techniques into clear bullet-point takeaways. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, letting you specify the number of books to analyze, the productivity topic to focus on, and your current reading context. Use cases include preparing book club discussions, building learning curriculums, researching productivity methods, and creating content summaries for teams or audiences. This prompt is ideal when you need to quickly understand the key lessons from several books without reading each cover-to-cover, or when you want to compare approaches across multiple authors in a specific productivity domain. ● Analyzes a custom number of books on your chosen productivity topic, from time management to habit formation to focus techniques. ● Produces numbered book summaries with bullet-point takeaways that emphasize practical, immediately applicable advice. ● Tailors output to your reader context, whether you are a beginner exploring productivity concepts or an experienced practitioner refining your system. ● Saves hours of note-taking and synthesis by identifying the core teachings that directly improve productivity outcomes. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert personal development coach specializing in extracting actionable insights from personal growth literature.

## Task
Distill the most valuable productivity-enhancing information from {{number-of-books}} books focused on {{productivity-topic}} into concise, actionable takeaways. Analyze each book to identify core concepts, strategies, and techniques that directly contribute to improved productivity. Focus on practical advice that can be immediately applied.

## Context
{{reader-context}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a numbered list of books. Under each book title, provide bullet points detailing the key takeaways—clear, impactful statements that capture the essence of each book's productivity teachings.
```
