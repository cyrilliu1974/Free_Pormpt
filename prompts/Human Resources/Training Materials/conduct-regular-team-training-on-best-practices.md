# Sales Operations Training Program Builder

## 簡介

The Sales Operations Training Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a customized sales training curriculum for teams of any size or experience level. This sales operations training prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured program organized into main topics and subtopics, complete with practical exercises, role-playing scenarios, and real-world case studies. You provide your sales team's profile - size, experience levels, skill gaps, and roles - along with your industry, target market, current sales challenges, and desired outcomes. The prompt then generates a bullet-point curriculum addressing four core competencies: sales process optimization, lead management techniques, sales forecasting methods, and performance tracking systems. Each topic includes recommended activities to reinforce learning and translate theory into practice. ● Creates a complete training program covering the four pillars of sales operations: process, leads, forecasting, and performance ● Incorporates hands-on learning components including exercises, role-plays, and case studies relevant to your industry ● Organizes content into hierarchical topics and subtopics for clear learning progression ● Adapts to teams at different experience levels and addresses identified skill gaps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales trainer designing a comprehensive training curriculum for a sales team.

## Task
Create a structured training program covering best practices in sales operations. Address:
- Sales process optimization
- Lead management
- Sales forecasting
- Performance tracking

Incorporate practical exercises, role-playing scenarios, and real-world case studies to reinforce learning.

## Context
{{sales-team-profile}}
(Describe your sales team: size, experience levels, current skill gaps, and roles)

{{business-context}}
(Include your industry, target market, current sales challenges, and desired training outcomes)

## Output
Deliver the training program as a bullet-point list organized into main topics and subtopics. Each topic should include recommended exercises or activities where appropriate.
```
