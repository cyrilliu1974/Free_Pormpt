# Guest Post Pitch Generator for Digital Marketing Blogs

## 簡介

The Guest Post Pitch Generator for Digital Marketing Blogs is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, editor-ready guest post pitches tailored to specific blogs and niches. This guest post pitch prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target blogs and generates 3-5 complete pitch packages, each with an attention-grabbing headline, a structured 3-4 point outline, and a clear value proposition explaining reader benefits. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and clarity in every pitch. Content strategists use it to streamline outreach campaigns, match editorial standards of target publications, and position themselves as credible contributors who understand audience expectations. The prompt takes your target blogs and niche as input and returns pitches that balance topical relevance with fresh perspectives. This tool is for content marketers, SEO professionals, and digital marketing consultants building backlinks and expanding publication reach through guest posting. ● Analyzes target blog content style, audience, and editorial focus to tailor pitches that resonate ● Generates multiple pitch options with specific headlines, detailed outlines, and reader-benefit statements ● Structures pitches using dependency grammar for maximum clarity and persuasive impact ● Positions you as a credible contributor with actionable insights relevant to each blog's readership ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in guest post outreach for digital marketing publications.

## Task
Create compelling guest post pitches for target blogs that will expand reach and build backlinks. Structure each pitch using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow.

## Context
{{target-blogs-and-niche}}

Analyze each target blog's content style, audience expectations, and editorial focus. Develop pitch ideas that align with their existing content while introducing fresh perspectives from your expertise. Each pitch should position you as a credible contributor who understands the blog's readership and can deliver actionable insights.

## Output
Present 3-5 numbered pitches, each containing:

1. **Headline**: Attention-grabbing, specific, and benefit-driven
2. **Brief Outline**: 3-4 bullet points covering the main points or sections
3. **Value Proposition**: 2-3 sentences explaining how this content will benefit the blog's readers and contribute to the digital marketing conversation

Ensure each pitch demonstrates topical relevance, practical value, and alignment with the target blog's editorial standards.
```
