# Cross-Selling and Upselling Strategy Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Cross-Selling and Upselling Strategy Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds actionable revenue-optimization strategies for any business and audience. This cross-selling and upselling prompt for ChatGPT takes your business description and produces a minimum of six scored strategies: at least three cross-selling tactics and three upselling approaches. Each strategy includes a detailed description, three or more implementation steps, a defined target customer segment, an expected ROI score (1–10), and an ease-of-implementation score (1–10). The output is formatted as a structured table and also as expanded entries, making it easy to prioritize and execute. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is tailored to your specific business context rather than offering generic advice. Use this prompt when you need a clear roadmap to increase average order value, deepen customer relationships, or identify new revenue streams without acquiring more leads. ● Produces at least six strategies split evenly between cross-selling and upselling, each with three concrete implementation steps. ● Scores every strategy on expected ROI and ease of implementation so you can prioritize fast wins and high-return initiatives. ● Defines specific target customer segments for each tactic, ensuring your sales and marketing teams know exactly who to approach. ● Returns results both as a table for quick comparison and as detailed entries for planning and execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic sales expert specializing in cross-selling, upselling techniques, and revenue optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive cross-selling and upselling strategy plan to maximize sales opportunities and revenue growth for the business described below. Provide at least 3 strategies for cross-selling and at least 3 strategies for upselling.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

For each strategy, provide:
- Detailed description of the strategy
- At least 3 actionable implementation steps
- Specific target customer segment
- Expected ROI score (1-10 scale, 10 = highest return)
- Ease of Implementation score (1-10 scale, 10 = easiest to execute)

Ensure strategies are specific and tailored to the business context provided—avoid generic recommendations.

## Output
Return a table with 6 columns: **Strategy Type** | **Description** | **Implementation Steps** | **Target Customer Segment** | **Expected ROI (1-10)** | **Ease of Implementation (1-10)**

For each strategy, present the information in this format:

**[Strategy Type: Cross-selling or Upselling]**
- **Description:** [Detailed description]
- **Implementation Steps:**
  1. [Step 1]
  2. [Step 2]
  3. [Step 3]
- **Target Customer Segment:** [Specific segment]
- **Expected ROI (1-10):** [Score]
- **Ease of Implementation (1-10):** [Score]

Provide a minimum of 6 total strategies (3 cross-selling + 3 upselling).
```
