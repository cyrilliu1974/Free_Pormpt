# Gaming Product Advertisement Banner Generator

## 簡介

The Gaming Product Advertisement Banner Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional three-panel product banners for gaming hardware brands and e-sports companies. This gaming product advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers studio-quality 16:9 horizontal layouts combining hero photography with dramatic lighting, extreme macro shots of engineering features, and lifestyle context scenes of gamers in RGB-lit setups. Each banner balances dark premium aesthetics with precise technical detail, creating cohesive visual assets for social media headers, e-commerce listings, and digital campaigns without requiring professional photo shoots. Marketers and brand designers reach for this prompt when they need competitive gaming visuals that communicate power, precision, and performance across all three narrative beats: product beauty, technical credibility, and authentic use. ● Hero panel with studio lighting, angled premium presentation, and controlled RGB accent glow in custom brand colors. ● Macro detail panel showcasing core technology, build quality, and surface texture with ultra-shallow depth of field. ● Lifestyle panel featuring gamers in low-light competitive setups with soft RGB ambiance and immersive focus. ● Dark gradient backgrounds, sharp product focus, and e-sports inspired aesthetics that avoid cheap plastic appearance and overexposed lighting. ## Prompt

```
Professional commercial product photography for a high-performance gaming hardware advertisement, 16:9 horizontal banner with three distinct panels in a 3×1 grid layout.

**Panel 1 – Hero Shot:** Studio photograph of {{product-name}} angled for aggressive premium presentation. Controlled studio lighting with subtle RGB accent glow in {{brand-color-palette}}. Dark gradient or matte black background. Sharp focus on product silhouette, primary interaction point, and logo clarity. Mood: power, precision, premium.

**Panel 2 – Engineering Detail:** Extreme macro close-up of {{key-feature}} showing core technology component, construction quality, and surface texture. Ultra-shallow depth of field with technical sharpness. Mood: accuracy, engineering, control.

**Panel 3 – Lifestyle Context:** Gamer using {{product-name}} in modern gaming setup. Low-light environment with {{brand-color-palette}} RGB accents from monitor and peripherals softly visible in background. Mood: immersion, speed, competitive focus.

Overall aesthetic: dark premium gaming hardware style, minimalist tech composition, e-sports inspired, high realism. Balanced exposure with crisp edges and soft dramatic shadows. Fast, precise, competitive, modern, powerful, focused.

Avoid: cheap plastic appearance, blurry technical details, unreadable branding, overexposed RGB lighting, cluttered desk setup, CGI or cartoon style, oversaturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
