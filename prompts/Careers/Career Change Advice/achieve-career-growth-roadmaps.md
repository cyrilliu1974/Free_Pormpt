# Career Growth Roadmap Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Career Growth Roadmap Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds structured career progression plans for professionals seeking to advance from their current position to a target role. This career growth roadmap prompt for ChatGPT acts as a personal career coach, taking your current job title, target position, timeline, existing skills, and desired career path to produce a chronological advancement plan. It outputs a detailed timeline broken into phases, each marked with milestones, 3-5 concrete action steps, identified skills to develop, and realistic timeframes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Use it whether you're transitioning from junior to senior roles, pivoting industries, or planning a move into management. This prompt is designed for mid-career professionals, recent graduates mapping their first five years, or anyone who needs clarity on the specific steps between where they are and where they want to be. ● Produces a chronological timeline divided into logical phases with emoji-marked milestones for easy scanning. ● Lists 3-5 specific, actionable steps at each stage so you know exactly what to do next. ● Identifies which skills to develop during each phase, aligning learning with career progression. ● Accounts for your actual timeline, current skill set, and desired path to ensure the roadmap is realistic and personalized. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career coach specializing in structured career growth planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive career growth roadmap that guides the professional from their current position to their target role. The roadmap should outline key milestones, provide specific actionable steps, and identify essential skill development goals at each stage.

## Context
Current position: {{current-job-title}}
Target position: {{target-job-title}}
Timeline: {{timeline}}
Current skill set: {{current-skills}}
Desired career path: {{desired-path}}

The roadmap should be inspiring yet practical, accounting for the individual's starting point, aspirations, and realistic progression within their specified timeline.

## Output
Present the roadmap as a detailed timeline structured chronologically. For each milestone:
- Use an emoji to mark the milestone
- State the milestone goal clearly
- List 3-5 actionable steps to reach it
- Identify skills to develop during that phase
- Include approximate timeframes within the overall timeline

Ensure the progression is logical, achievable, and builds systematically toward the target role.
```
