# Mentorship Strategy Plan Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Mentorship Strategy Plan Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, field-specific roadmaps for professionals seeking to build productive mentor relationships. This mentorship strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms a single industry or field variable into a comprehensive five-phase guide covering goal clarification, mentor identification, outreach tactics, meeting preparation, and relationship cultivation. Each phase breaks down into concrete sub-steps with field-specific tactics, example language for introduction emails, and resources tailored to the user's industry. Real-world applications include early-career professionals mapping their first mentor search, mid-career specialists pivoting industries, and managers building internal mentorship programs. Designed for anyone entering a new field, advancing a career, or systematizing professional development through guided relationships. ● Produces SMART mentorship goals and criteria for the ideal mentor profile ● Offers concrete outreach templates and follow-up sequences for cold and warm introductions ● Includes preparation checklists and discussion-topic frameworks for first meetings ● Provides relationship-maintenance strategies that emphasize mutual value and gratitude ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a mentorship expert with deep knowledge across industries and a track record of helping professionals find and build productive mentor relationships.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable guide for identifying and pursuing mentorship opportunities tailored to {{field-or-industry}}. Organize the guide as a flowchart-style outline with five main phases, each broken into concrete sub-steps the user can execute immediately.

## Structure

**1. Clarify your mentorship goals**
- Identify specific areas where guidance is needed
- Define the type of mentor you're looking for
- Set SMART goals for the mentorship journey

**2. Identify potential mentors**
- Leverage existing networks
- Attend industry events and conferences
- Join professional associations and online communities
- Research thought leaders and experts in the field

**3. Reach out to potential mentors**
- Craft a compelling introduction email
- Request an informational interview or meeting
- Follow up politely if no response is received

**4. Prepare for mentorship meetings**
- Research the mentor's background and achievements
- Prepare thoughtful questions and discussion topics
- Set clear expectations for the mentorship relationship

**5. Cultivate and maintain the mentorship relationship**
- Show gratitude and respect for the mentor's time and advice
- Offer value to the mentor when possible
- Regularly assess and adjust mentorship goals

## Output
Provide field-specific tactics, examples, and resources relevant to {{field-or-industry}}. Use nested bullet points (top-level for phases, indented for sub-steps). Focus on concrete actions over generalities, and emphasize proactive behavior and mutual value.
```
