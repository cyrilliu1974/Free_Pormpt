# Sales Funnel Content Strategy Mapping Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Content Strategy Mapping Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a structured content plan aligned to each stage of the customer journey for marketing professionals and strategists. This sales funnel content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product, target audience, unique selling proposition, and competitive landscape to deliver a markdown table mapping Awareness, Consideration, Decision, and Retention stages to specific content types and key messages. It considers audience pain points, decision-making behavior, and how prospects move through the funnel, ensuring each stage delivers the right information at the right time. Use it when planning campaigns, auditing existing content programs, or building go-to-market strategies that guide leads smoothly from first touch to conversion and beyond. ● Maps four funnel stages (Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Retention) to actionable content formats and channels ● Articulates key messages that resonate with audience needs and pain points at each stage of the buying journey ● Integrates your unique selling proposition and competitive context into a cohesive narrative arc ● Outputs a clean markdown table for easy sharing with teams, clients, or stakeholders ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in sales funnel content alignment.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content strategy that maps to each stage of the sales funnel, nurturing leads from awareness through to conversion. For each funnel stage, specify the most effective content types and the key messages that should be communicated.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique Selling Proposition: {{usp}}
Industry & Competitive Landscape: {{industry-context}}

Analyze the target audience's needs, pain points, and decision-making process at each stage. Consider how awareness builds into consideration, and consideration into purchase intent. Ensure the strategy creates a seamless journey from first touch to conversion.

## Output
Deliver your content strategy as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Funnel Stage** (Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Retention)
- **Content Type** (specific formats and channels)
- **Key Message** (what to communicate and why it matters at this stage)

Each row should represent one stage, with multiple content types and messages clearly articulated.
```
