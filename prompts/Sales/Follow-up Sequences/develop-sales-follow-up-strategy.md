# Sales Follow-Up Strategy Prompt for Unresponsive Proposals

## 簡介

The Sales Follow-Up Strategy Prompt for Unresponsive Proposals is a free AI prompt that builds a complete three-stage outreach plan for sales professionals whose proposals have gone unanswered. This sales follow-up prompt for ChatGPT takes your proposal details - client name, submission date, deal value, key decision-maker, and time elapsed - and produces a structured escalation plan spanning 15–21 days. Each stage includes a persuasive email with a subject line, body copy that highlights benefits and creates urgency, and a clear call to action. Stage 1 offers a gentle reminder and value reinforcement; Stage 2 introduces social proof and proposes a meeting; Stage 3 expresses professional concern, presents a time-sensitive incentive, and requests a definitive answer. The prompt also delivers supplementary tactics - multi-channel outreach, leveraging mutual connections, sharing industry insights - and a closing strategy for re-engagement at 30, 60, and 90 days if no response comes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text models. Reach for this prompt when a high-value proposal sits in limbo and you need a disciplined, non-pushy framework to reclaim attention and move the deal forward. ● Outputs three complete follow-up emails with subject lines, body copy, and timing intervals calibrated to maintain professionalism while escalating urgency. ● Includes case-study references, testimonials, time-sensitive incentives, and alternative contact strategies to maximize response probability. ● Provides a multi-channel outreach blueprint - LinkedIn, phone, mutual connections - so you can pivot beyond email if needed. ● Delivers a 30/60/90-day re-engagement plan for proposals that remain dormant, preserving the relationship for future opportunities. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business development consultant specializing in sales proposal follow-up strategies that maximize response rates and deal closure.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive three-stage follow-up plan for a sales proposal that has not received a response. The strategy should escalate appropriately through each stage, using persuasive communication that highlights value, creates urgency, and maintains professionalism.

## Context
Proposal details:
{{proposal-details}}

Include: client name, proposal submission date, deal value, primary contact name/title, and the timeframe since submission without response.

## Output
Provide a structured follow-up plan in this format:

**Proposal Overview**
- Client name: [from input]
- Proposal date: [from input]
- Proposal value: [from input]
- Key decision-maker: [from input]

**Stage 1 Follow-up** (5-7 days after submission)
Subject: [Engaging, value-focused subject line]
Content:
1. Gentle reminder referencing the proposal
2. Highlight 2-3 key benefits and unique selling points
3. Offer additional information or clarification
4. Request specific response date or meeting

**Stage 2 Follow-up** (5-7 days after Stage 1)
Subject: [Compelling, benefit-driven subject line]
Content:
1. Reaffirm interest in partnership
2. Share relevant case study or testimonial
3. Emphasize time-sensitivity or market context
4. Propose specific call date/time

**Stage 3 Follow-up** (5-7 days after Stage 2)
Subject: [Urgent but professional subject line]
Content:
1. Express concern about lack of response
2. Reiterate core competitive advantages
3. Offer time-sensitive incentive or value-add
4. Request definitive answer by specific date

**Additional Tactics**
Provide 3 supplementary approaches such as:
- Multi-channel outreach (LinkedIn, phone)
- Leveraging mutual connections
- Sharing industry insights or relevant news
- Offering limited-time value additions

**Closing Strategy**
If no response after Stage 3:
1. Analyze potential reasons (budget cycle, internal priorities, decision-maker change)
2. Identify alternative contacts or approaches
3. Design a 30/60/90-day re-engagement plan that maintains the relationship

Ensure all messaging is concise, client-focused, and avoids generic or pushy language.
```
