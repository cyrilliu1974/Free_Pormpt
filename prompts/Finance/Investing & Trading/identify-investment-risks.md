# Investment Risk Analysis Prompt for Industries

## 簡介

The Investment Risk Analysis Prompt for Industries is a free AI prompt that produces structured, expert-level risk assessments for investors evaluating opportunities in specific sectors. This investment risk analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to adopt the role of an expert investment analyst and deliver a ranked report covering five critical risk categories: market volatility and economic factors, regulatory and policy changes, technological advancements and disruptions, ESG considerations, and competitive landscape dynamics. Each risk receives a detailed description, impact assessment, and supporting evidence drawn from market data and historical precedents. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting three variables - industry, investment focus, and risk tolerance - to tailor the analysis to your portfolio strategy. Use it when conducting due diligence on a new sector, reassessing existing holdings, or preparing client briefings that demand defensible, data-backed risk insights. ● Delivers a prioritized, five-category framework covering market, regulatory, technological, ESG, and competitive risks ● Adapts output based on your specified industry, investment focus, and risk tolerance ● Includes impact assessments and supporting evidence for each identified risk ● Produces investor-ready reports suitable for portfolio reviews, client presentations, and strategic planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert investment analyst with deep knowledge of market trends, economic indicators, regulatory frameworks, and industry-specific challenges.

## Task
Analyze and identify the top 5 investment risks in {{industry}}, producing a structured report that explains each risk's significance and potential impact. Use current market data, historical precedents, and relevant examples to support your analysis.

## Context
The analysis should account for:
- Investment focus: {{investment-focus}}
- Risk tolerance: {{risk-tolerance}}

Consider both current conditions and forecasted challenges that could affect the investment landscape.

## Analysis Framework
Structure your analysis around these five risk categories:

**1. Market Volatility and Economic Factors**
Examine how inflation rates, interest rates, geopolitical tensions, and broader economic conditions impact investment stability and growth prospects in this industry. Explain both direct and indirect effects.

**2. Regulatory and Policy Changes**
Analyze the regulatory environment, including recent and anticipated changes in laws, regulations, and policies. Discuss risks from compliance costs, operational constraints, and profitability impacts.

**3. Technological Advancements and Disruptions**
Address how innovation cycles could render existing products obsolete, erode market share, or require costly adaptations.

**4. Environmental and Social Governance (ESG) Factors**
Examine ESG considerations affecting investment decisions, including environmental sustainability, social responsibility, and governance practices that influence investor sentiment and regulatory scrutiny.

**5. Competition and Market Entry Barriers**
Assess the competitive landscape, market saturation, barriers to entry, and disruption potential from new entrants or innovative technologies.

## Output
For each of the 5 risks, provide:
- **Risk Title**: Clear, descriptive heading
- **Description**: Comprehensive explanation of the risk's nature
- **Impact Assessment**: How this risk affects investments in the industry
- **Supporting Evidence**: Relevant data, case studies, or examples

Format the report as a prioritized list ranked by significance and potential impact on investments.
```
