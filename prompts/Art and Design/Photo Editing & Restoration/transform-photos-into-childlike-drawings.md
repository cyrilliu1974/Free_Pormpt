# Photo to Childlike Drawing Converter

## 簡介

The Photo to Childlike Drawing Converter is a free AI prompt that transforms photos into hand-drawn colored pencil portraits rendered in the style of naive art on lined notebook paper. This childlike drawing prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana preserves exact facial resemblance and hairstyle while applying vibrant but uneven coloring, simplistic shading, and the earnest imperfections of a treasured childhood sketch. You customize the subject's outfit and background elements, both rendered in the same charmingly amateur style, making it ideal for social media content, greeting cards, personalized gifts, or nostalgic marketing visuals. Reach for this prompt when you want to create emotionally resonant imagery that evokes childhood memories without requiring any drawing skill or design software. ● Preserves 1:1 facial resemblance and hairstyle from reference photos with no beautification or unwanted alterations. ● Applies vibrant colored pencil strokes with intentionally uneven coloring and simplistic shading for authentic childlike execution. ● Renders on lined notebook paper background with customizable outfit and background elements in matching naive style. ● Creates nostalgic, shareable visuals perfect for personal keepsakes, content marketing, or emotionally engaging social posts. ## Prompt

```
A hand-drawn colored pencil portrait on lined notebook paper in the style of naive art or a child's drawing. The subject is a woman with exact 1:1 facial resemblance to the reference photo—preserve her specific face, hairstyle, and identity without beautification or alterations. She wears {{outfit}}. Vibrant but slightly uneven coloring with simplistic shading on the face. Background: {{background-elements}}, rendered in the same childlike, amateur style. ```
```
