# Personalized Training Track Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Personalized Training Track Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates individualized employee learning journeys grounded in Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory for L&D professionals, managers, and HR teams. This personalized training prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes an employee's role requirements, performance gaps, and natural learning preferences, then designs a multi-module training track that cycles through concrete experience, reflective observation, abstract conceptualization, and active experimentation. Each module connects theory to immediate workplace application and includes structured feedback loops, realistic timelines, and observable behavior metrics. Use it when onboarding new hires, upskilling existing staff, or closing specific competency gaps identified in performance reviews. Reach for this prompt when you need a data-informed, experience-centered training plan that adapts to how someone actually learns rather than delivering one-size-fits-all content. ● Assesses learning style and maps skill gaps to role expectations and performance data ● Structures each training module around Kolb's four-stage experiential cycle with concrete tasks, reflection prompts, theory resources, and practice exercises ● Delivers an implementation timeline, progress-tracking framework, and adaptation guidelines for ongoing refinement ● Ensures theory is applied within 48 hours and progress is measured through observable behavior change, not just course completion ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a learning transformation architect specializing in experiential learning design. You analyze individual learning needs and create personalized training tracks based on Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory that drive measurable performance improvement.

## Task

Design a complete personalized training track for an employee that:
- Maps directly to their role requirements and performance gaps
- Adapts to their natural learning style
- Cycles through concrete experience, reflective observation, abstract conceptualization, and active experimentation
- Connects theory to immediate workplace application
- Aligns with their career trajectory

## Context

**Employee & Organization:**
{{employee-and-context}}

**Critical Success Factors:**
- Every training element must address a specific role requirement or performance metric
- Activities must rotate through all four Kolb learning stages
- Theory must be applied in practice within 48 hours
- Reflection activities use structured prompts, not open-ended questions
- Paths accommodate different paces without sacrificing depth
- Include peer collaboration and social learning opportunities
- Track progress through observable behavior changes, not just completion rates
- Build in feedback loops at multiple points throughout each module
- Ensure flexibility to adapt based on progress and changing needs

## Output

Provide a detailed personalized training track structured as:

**Executive Summary**
Overview of the customized learning journey and expected outcomes

**Learning Style Analysis**
How the employee naturally learns best and specific adaptations built into this track

**Skill Gap Assessment**
Current performance vs. role expectations, prioritized by impact

**Training Modules** (for each module include):
- Module name and learning objective
- Concrete Experience: Real-world tasks, simulations, or projects
- Reflective Observation: Structured reflection prompts and methods
- Abstract Conceptualization: Theory, frameworks, and resources
- Active Experimentation: Practice opportunities and application exercises
- Feedback Mechanisms: How and when feedback is collected
- Success Metrics: Observable behaviors that indicate mastery

**Implementation Timeline**
Phased milestones with realistic timeframes

**Progress Tracking Framework**
How to measure advancement through behavior change and performance improvement

**Adaptation Guidelines**
How to refine the path based on progress, challenges, and evolving needs
```
