# Seasonal Keyword Trend Analysis for SEO

## 簡介

The Seasonal Keyword Trend Analysis for SEO is a free AI prompt that analyzes seasonal search patterns and keyword performance fluctuations for digital marketers and SEO professionals. This seasonal keyword trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to identify 5-7 high-impact keywords for your product type, map their popularity across 12 months, describe seasonal patterns, and deliver actionable recommendations to improve online visibility. The prompt structures output into discrete sections: keyword lists, trend descriptions suitable for graph visualization, seasonal pattern analysis, and specific optimization tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing teams use it to anticipate search volume shifts, content planners rely on it to schedule campaigns around peak interest periods, and ecommerce businesses apply it to align inventory and ad spend with seasonal demand. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand how search interest in your product category changes throughout the year and want data-informed recommendations to capitalize on those patterns. ● Identifies 5-7 relevant keywords tailored to your specific product type ● Maps 12-month popularity trends and describes seasonal fluctuations in search behavior ● Delivers insight-recommendation pairs that connect observed patterns to specific actions ● Suggests complementary analysis tools to deepen your keyword research ## Prompt
