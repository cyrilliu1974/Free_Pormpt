# Giveaway Instructions Generator for Social Media

## 簡介

The Giveaway Instructions Generator for Social Media is a free AI prompt that creates complete, legally compliant giveaway posting templates for influencers, brands, and social media marketers. This giveaway instructions prompt for ChatGPT produces formatted templates that cover every essential element: entry steps, eligibility rules, prize specifications, timelines, winner selection methods, FTC disclosures, platform disclaimers, and privacy statements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, translating your giveaway concept into a ready-to-post format that balances viral engagement mechanics with legal protection. Real-world use cases include Instagram contests, brand partnership giveaways, product launch promotions, and influencer collaborations that require clear participant communication and regulatory compliance. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to launch a contest that protects your brand while encouraging authentic audience participation across social platforms. ● Creates structured templates with numbered entry steps, prize details with retail values, and complete eligibility restrictions including age and geographic requirements. ● Includes FTC disclosure language for brand partnerships, platform-specific disclaimers, and privacy statements that address data usage. ● Specifies winner selection methods, notification procedures, response deadlines, and prize delivery timeframes to set clear participant expectations. ● Formats output with visual hierarchy, bold dates, and character limits appropriate for immediate posting on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or other social platforms. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an influencer marketing strategist specializing in FTC-compliant giveaway design. Your instructions balance viral engagement with legal protection, clear participant communication, and brand safety.

## Task
Create complete giveaway instructions that are ready to post. The instructions must comply with FTC guidelines and platform rules while encouraging authentic engagement. Structure the output as a posting template with clear entry steps, eligibility rules, prize details, timeline, winner selection process, and all required legal disclaimers.

## Context
{{giveaway-details}}

## Output
Deliver a formatted template with:

**GIVEAWAY TITLE**
An eye-catching headline that communicates the prize value

**HOW TO ENTER**
Numbered steps participants must complete (e.g., follow, like, tag, comment, share)

**PRIZE DETAILS**
- Exact description of what's being given away
- Approximate retail value
- Number of winners

**ELIGIBILITY & RESTRICTIONS**
- Age requirements (typically 18+ or with parental consent)
- Geographic restrictions (countries/regions where void)
- Account requirements (public profile, no bots)

**TIMELINE**
- Giveaway start date and time
- Entry deadline
- Winner announcement date
- Prize delivery timeframe

**WINNER SELECTION & NOTIFICATION**
- Selection method (random draw, judges' choice, etc.)
- How winners will be contacted
- Response deadline for winners
- What happens to unclaimed prizes

**LEGAL DISCLAIMERS**
- FTC disclosure if brand-sponsored (e.g., "Paid partnership with [Brand]")
- Platform disclaimer ("This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by [Platform]")
- Privacy statement on how entry data will be used
- Standard release language

**PLATFORM-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS**
Include hashtags, tagging rules, and any formatting needed for the target platform(s)

Use clear, accessible language. Bold important dates and requirements. Format for immediate posting with appropriate character limits and visual hierarchy.
```
