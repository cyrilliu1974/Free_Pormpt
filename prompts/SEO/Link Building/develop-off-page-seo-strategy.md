# Off-Page SEO Brand Messaging Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Off-Page SEO Brand Messaging Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive brand consistency frameworks for marketers managing link building and off-page content strategies. This off-page SEO prompt for ChatGPT takes your brand overview and produces a complete messaging guide covering voice and tone rules, context-specific content guidelines for guest posts, social media interactions, forum participation, and influencer partnerships, plus concrete examples for each channel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured document that aligns your external content with your brand's unique value proposition and target audience perception goals. Use it when you need to scale off-page SEO efforts across guest blogging, community engagement, and link-building campaigns while maintaining a unified brand identity. ● Extracts brand name, value proposition, audience, and desired perception from your overview ● Delivers 5-7 specific voice and tone guidelines covering personality, language style, and prohibited patterns ● Provides 3-4 context-specific content rules for guest posts, social media, forums, and influencer collaborations ● Includes real-world examples and compliant vs. non-compliant scenarios for each channel ● Outputs a 6-section quick-reference document with compliance checklist for internal distribution ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a brand strategist specializing in off-page SEO content and brand identity management.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive brand messaging guide that ensures consistency across all off-page SEO touchpoints—guest posts, social media, forums, community participation, and influencer partnerships.

## Context
Brand details:
{{brand-overview}}

The guide must align voice, tone, and messaging with the brand's unique value proposition, target audience, and desired market perception. Provide actionable guidelines with concrete examples for each off-page SEO context.

## Output
Deliver a structured brand messaging guide in this format:

**Brand Overview**
- Extract and present: brand name, unique value proposition, target audience, desired perception

**Brand Voice and Tone Guidelines**
- 5-7 specific, actionable guidelines (numbered list)
- Cover personality, language style, emotional tone, and prohibited patterns

**Off-Page SEO Content Guidelines by Context**

1. **Guest Posts**
   - 3-4 guidelines specific to long-form contributed content
   - Example: Show how guidelines apply in a sample guest post scenario

2. **Social Media Interactions**
   - 3-4 guidelines for comments, replies, and posts
   - Example: Demonstrate compliant vs. non-compliant social engagement

3. **Forum and Community Participation**
   - 3-4 guidelines for Q&A, discussions, and community contributions
   - Example: Illustrate proper community voice in action

4. **Influencer Partnerships**
   - 3-4 guidelines for collaboration briefs and co-created content
   - Example: Show partnership messaging framework

**Quick-Reference Document Outline**
Provide a 6-section outline structure suitable for internal distribution:
1. Introduction
2. Brand Overview
3. Voice and Tone Guidelines
4. Context-Specific Off-Page SEO Guidelines
5. Examples and Use Cases
6. Compliance Checklist

Make all guidelines specific enough to guide execution while flexible enough to adapt to emerging platforms.
```
