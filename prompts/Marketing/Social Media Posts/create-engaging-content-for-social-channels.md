# Social Media Content Suite Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Suite Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored content strategies across multiple social platforms for businesses seeking to drive sales and brand awareness. This social media content prompt for ChatGPT produces platform-specific post ideas, story concepts, and campaign frameworks complete with researched hashtags, visual direction, and conversion-optimized calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts its output to respect the unique format, audience behavior, and content conventions of each channel you specify. Real-world applications include planning monthly content calendars for e-commerce brands, developing launch campaigns across Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok simultaneously, and creating consistent brand messaging that converts followers into customers. Content creators, social media managers, and small business owners will reach for this prompt when they need a structured approach to multi-platform posting that balances creative ideation with tactical execution. ● Produces complete content blueprints for each specified social channel, eliminating guesswork about format and tone ● Includes curated hashtag sets (5-10 per post) to expand organic reach and discoverability ● Provides concrete visual recommendations aligned to brand identity and platform best practices ● Delivers clear, conversion-focused calls-to-action that guide audiences from engagement to purchase ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media content strategist focused on driving sales and brand awareness through platform-optimized content.

## Task
Develop a suite of engaging social media content ideas tailored to {{social-channels}}. For each platform, provide specific posts, stories, or campaign concepts with accompanying hashtags, visual recommendations, and calls-to-action that convert.

## Context
- Business: {{business-type}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}
- Key offerings: {{key-products-services}}

Consider each platform's unique format, audience behavior, and content conventions. Structure your writing for clarity and impact.

## Output
Organize as follows:

### [Platform Name]
- **Content idea**: [specific post/story/campaign concept]
- **Hashtags**: [5-10 relevant tags]
- **Visual**: [image/video/graphic recommendation]
- **CTA**: [clear conversion action]

Repeat this structure for each platform in {{social-channels}}.
```
