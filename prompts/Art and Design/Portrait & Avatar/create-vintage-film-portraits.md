# Vintage Film Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Film Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic vintage film photographs with motion blur, soft focus, and melancholic nighttime atmospheres for photographers and visual artists. This vintage film portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 1200x1200px square photographs that simulate classic film stock aesthetics - grainy texture, heavy motion blur, and atmospheric haze. The prompt captures subjects leaning from moving car windows at night, hair blown by wind, illuminated by warm vintage flash mixed with streaking streetlights. You control the film stock type and subject appearance through two variables, while the prompt handles authentic motion effects, passenger-seat perspective, and the dreamy quality of genuine analog photography. Reach for this prompt when you need moody, nostalgic imagery for album covers, editorial spreads, fashion portfolios, or any project requiring emotional depth and retro authenticity without a physical film camera or nighttime shoots. ● Produces grainy, dark film photography with heavy motion blur, soft focus, and atmospheric haze that replicates actual vintage camera limitations and charm. ● Customizable film stock and subject appearance variables while maintaining authentic windswept hair motion, natural facial features, and passenger-seat framing. ● Generates streaking city lights and illegible background shapes from motion and darkness, creating genuine sense of speed and melancholic mood. ● Outputs 1200x1200px square format with warm vintage flash mixed with ambient streetlight illumination, ideal for social media, print, and editorial use. ## Prompt

```
A dark, grainy vintage film photograph with a dreamy, melancholic atmosphere. Shot on {{film-stock}} with heavy motion blur, soft focus, and atmospheric haze.

Subject leaning upper back out of an open car window while the vehicle moves at night. {{subject-appearance}}. Hair heavily blown about by wind. Natural facial features and head positioning.

Captured from passenger seat perspective inside a vintage car. Completely blurred streaks of dark city and road lights in background, illegible shapes due to motion and darkness. Extremely windy conditions.

Low light illumination from warm, soft vintage camera flash mixed with ambient streaking streetlights. 1200x1200px square format.
```
