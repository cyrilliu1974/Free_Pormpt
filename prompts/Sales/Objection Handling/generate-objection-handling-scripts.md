# Sales Objection Response Script Generator

## 簡介

The Sales Objection Response Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates three persuasive, ready-to-use responses to any sales objection for sales teams, account executives, and customer success professionals. This objection handling prompt for ChatGPT takes a single objection - whether about pricing, product features, service terms, timing, or competition - and produces three distinct response variations. Each follows a proven structure: acknowledge the customer's concern, counter with a compelling benefit or value proposition, and close with a forward-moving question or call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs responses formatted by objection category with three full response scripts ready to adapt for email, phone calls, or live conversations. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, professional language to overcome common sales obstacles without sounding defensive or dismissive. ● Creates three unique response variations for every objection, giving reps flexibility and choice ● Structures each response to acknowledge, counter, and advance the conversation toward close ● Covers all common objection types: pricing, product capabilities, contracts, timing, and competitors ● Outputs formatted scripts that sales teams can copy, customize, and deploy immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in crafting persuasive, diplomatic responses to common sales objections.

## Task
Generate three carefully worded responses to address the specific objection provided. Each response should acknowledge the concern, counter it with a benefit or value proposition, and steer the conversation forward with a relevant question or call-to-action.

## Context
The objection falls within one of these common categories: pricing and budget, product features and capabilities, service terms and contracts, timing and urgency, or competition and alternatives. Your responses must handle the objection diplomatically while moving toward a successful close.

## Input
{{objection-details}}
(Include the objection category and the specific objection you're addressing)

## Output
Provide three distinct response variations, each following this structure:
- Acknowledge the validity of the objection
- Counter with a compelling benefit or value proposition
- Close with a forward-moving question or call-to-action

Format as:

**Objection Category:** [category name]

**Specific Objection:** [objection statement]

**Response 1:**
[Full response text]

**Response 2:**
[Full response text]

**Response 3:**
[Full response text]
```
