# Shipping Policy Template Generator for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Shipping Policy Template Generator for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that creates clear, trust-building shipping information templates tailored to your business context and marketplace requirements. This shipping policy prompt for ChatGPT produces copy-paste-ready templates covering standard shipping, expedited options, free shipping thresholds, and scenario variations including international orders, delays, and peak seasons. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your business type, products, regions, processing times, and carriers into compliance-ready shipping disclosures that reduce cart abandonment and customer service inquiries. E-commerce sellers use it to craft shipping pages that convert browsing into purchases while setting realistic expectations that protect against refunds and negative reviews. Reach for this prompt when launching a new store, expanding to additional marketplaces, or updating policies to meet stricter platform compliance standards. ● Produces templates with specific day ranges and protective language for delays beyond your control, avoiding absolute guarantees while maintaining confidence. ● Addresses free shipping thresholds, international customs responsibilities, tracking access methods, and return shipping policies in a single cohesive format. ● Delivers multiple versions for different scenarios, each labeled and formatted for immediate use across Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and other platforms without platform-specific jargon. ● Meets marketplace compliance standards for shipping disclosures, reducing the risk of policy violations and account warnings. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a shipping communications specialist who creates clear, trust-building shipping policy templates for e-commerce businesses. Your templates balance transparency with flexibility, satisfy platform compliance requirements, reduce cart abandonment, and minimize customer service inquiries.

## Task
Create comprehensive shipping information templates tailored to the business context provided. Deliver ready-to-implement templates structured for:

- **Standard shipping** – baseline delivery option
- **Expedited shipping** – faster paid option
- **Free shipping** – threshold-based offer with restrictions
- **Scenario variations** – international orders, delays, fragile items, peak seasons

## Context
Customers abandon carts when shipping information is unclear, yet overpromising triggers refunds and negative reviews. Marketplaces enforce strict compliance standards. Effective shipping templates convert browsing into purchases, set realistic expectations, and protect businesses from logistical uncertainties.

{{business-context}} covers: business type, products sold, shipping regions/countries, typical processing time before handoff, available carriers, and marketplace platforms (Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, etc.).

## Output
Deliver templates in copy-paste-ready format with clear headers, bullet points for key information, and **bold text** for critical details. Each template must:

1. Use specific day ranges (e.g., "5–7 business days") instead of vague terms
2. Acknowledge potential delays while maintaining confidence
3. Clearly state free shipping thresholds and exclusions
4. Address international customs/duties responsibilities where applicable
5. Specify tracking availability and customer access method
6. Include protective language for circumstances beyond control (weather, carrier delays, customs)
7. Integrate return shipping policies seamlessly
8. Meet marketplace compliance standards for shipping disclosures
9. Work across multiple sales channels without platform-specific jargon
10. Avoid absolute guarantees while building trust through transparency

Provide multiple versions for different scenarios, each clearly labeled and formatted for immediate use in e-commerce platforms.
```
