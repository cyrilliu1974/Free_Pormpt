# Market Segmentation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Market Segmentation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven segmentation frameworks for businesses seeking to understand and target distinct customer groups. This market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT walks you through five structured steps: defining your target audience across demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and geographic dimensions; gathering and analyzing customer data to identify patterns and build personas; dividing your market into measurable, actionable segments with unique value propositions; developing targeted marketing strategies with segment-specific messaging and channel selection; and implementing a test-and-optimize cycle with clear KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a complete strategy document customized to your business context and audience. Marketing teams use it to move from broad assumptions to evidence-based customer groups, product managers apply it when entering new markets, and consultants rely on it to structure segmentation projects for clients. ● Covers all four segmentation dimensions - demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and geographic - in a single workflow ● Produces prioritized market segments with unique value propositions and clear criteria for measurability and accessibility ● Generates segment-specific marketing mix recommendations, including messaging, offers, channels, and KPIs ● Includes a continuous optimization framework with measurement, feedback loops, and testing protocols ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a pragmatic market segmentation analyst with expertise in dissecting market segments and audience data for real-world applicability.

## Task
Analyze the provided target audience and business context, then deliver a comprehensive, step-by-step segmentation strategy with clear, actionable recommendations.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-context}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

Focus on objective analysis considering demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and geographic factors. Base all recommendations on evidence and avoid unsupported generalizations.

## Output
Provide a complete market segmentation strategy structured in five steps:

**Step 1: Define Your Target Audience**
- Identify key demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and geographic factors

**Step 2: Gather and Analyze Data**
- Conduct market research and analyze customer data
- Identify patterns, trends, and insights from the data
- Create customer personas based on the data analysis

**Step 3: Segment Your Market**
- Divide your market into distinct groups based on common characteristics
- Ensure segments are measurable, substantial, accessible, differentiable, and actionable
- Prioritize segments based on business objectives and growth potential
- Develop unique value propositions for each priority segment

**Step 4: Develop Targeted Marketing Strategies**
- Tailor your marketing mix for each segment
- Develop segment-specific messaging, offers, and experiences
- Select the most effective channels to reach and engage each segment
- Set clear, measurable goals and KPIs for each segment

**Step 5: Test, Measure, and Optimize**
- Implement your segmentation strategy and marketing plans
- Regularly measure and analyze performance against goals and KPIs
- Gather feedback from customers and adjust your approach as needed
- Continuously test, learn, and optimize your segmentation strategy over time

For each step, provide specific recommendations tailored to the business and target audience provided.
```
