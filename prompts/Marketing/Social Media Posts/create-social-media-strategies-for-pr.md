# Social Media Strategy Builder for PR and Marketing

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Builder for PR and Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, platform-specific social media strategies for companies looking to boost brand awareness, engage audiences, and drive conversions. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company's current presence across Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn, then delivers tailored content themes, posting schedules, engagement tactics, and a 30-day content calendar complete with post concepts and hashtags. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your company name, target audience, industry, current social media presence, and marketing goals as inputs, then producing a structured strategy document with platform-specific recommendations, success metrics, and performance adaptation guidelines. Use it when you need a full-scale social media plan that aligns with your brand voice and converts audience insights into actionable campaigns. ● Audits your existing social media presence and identifies opportunities for growth across four major platforms ● Delivers platform-specific content themes, optimal posting frequencies, and community-building tactics tailored to your target audience ● Provides a 30-day content calendar with concrete post ideas, relevant hashtags, and campaign tie-ins ● Includes success metrics (reach, engagement rate, conversions, follower growth) and a framework for adapting your strategy based on performance data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist developing a comprehensive, actionable plan to boost brand awareness, engage audiences, and drive conversions.

## Task
Analyze the company's current social media landscape and create a tailored multi-platform strategy covering Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn. Deliver specific content ideas, posting schedules, engagement tactics, a content calendar with post concepts and hashtags, campaign ideas, success metrics, and adaptation guidelines.

## Context
**Company:** {{company-name}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Current social media presence:** {{current-presence}}
**Marketing goals:** {{marketing-goals}}

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points:

1. **Current State Analysis** – assess existing presence and audience insights
2. **Platform-Specific Strategies** – for each platform (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn):
   - Content themes and post ideas
   - Posting frequency and optimal schedule
   - Engagement and community-building tactics
3. **Content Calendar** – 30-day sample with specific post concepts, hashtags, and campaign tie-ins per platform
4. **Brand Voice Alignment** – how strategy supports overall marketing goals and maintains consistent tone
5. **Success Metrics** – KPIs to track (reach, engagement rate, conversions, follower growth)
6. **Performance Adaptation Framework** – guidelines for iterating based on analytics

Ensure all recommendations are actionable, aligned with the stated marketing goals, and reflect the target audience's preferences.
```
