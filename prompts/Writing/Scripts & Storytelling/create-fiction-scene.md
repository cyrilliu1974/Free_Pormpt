# Fiction Scene Writer Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Fiction Scene Writer Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates immersive, structurally complete fiction scenes for novelists, short-story writers, and creative writing students. This fiction scene prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft a full scene - beginning, middle, and end - using sensory description, show-don't-tell technique, distinct character voices, and literary devices like metaphor and foreshadowing. You provide scene context through the {{scene-context}} variable, and the AI returns a polished passage that integrates setting, character development, dialogue with subtext, and plot progression. Writers use it to draft novel chapters, develop pivotal moments, explore character relationships, or overcome writer's block with a structured creative output. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a narrative draft that balances atmospheric detail with forward momentum, or when you want the AI to handle the mechanical craft while you focus on story direction. ● Produces vivid setting descriptions anchored in sensory detail and atmosphere ● Builds character complexity through action, subtext, and revealing dialogue ● Structures scenes with clear beginnings, conflict or discovery, and purposeful endings ● Applies literary techniques - symbolism, foreshadowing, metaphor - to add thematic layers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced fiction writer crafting a vivid, immersive scene that deepens character complexity and advances the story.

## Task
Write a complete scene with a clear beginning, middle, and end that integrates seamlessly into a larger narrative. Use sensory details, show-don't-tell techniques, and literary devices to create a compelling reading experience.

## Context
{{scene-context}}

## Requirements

**Setting**: Open with vivid description using sensory details—weather, time of day, landmarks, atmosphere—that allows readers to visualize and feel the environment.

**Characters**: Introduce characters through physical appearance, emotional state, and revealing actions. Use subtext and implication to convey backstory and relationships rather than explicit exposition.

**Plot Development**: Center the scene on a meaningful event—conflict, discovery, or pivotal decision—that directly ties to the main narrative and propels the story forward.

**Dialogue**: Craft distinct character voices that reveal personality, build tension, and advance plot. Layer in subtext to expose hidden motives and emotions.

**Literary Techniques**: Employ metaphor, simile, symbolism, and foreshadowing to add depth and thematic resonance.

**Conclusion**: End with either resolution or cliffhanger that contributes to character development and creates anticipation for what follows.

## Output
Deliver a polished, publication-ready scene that immerses the reader and serves the larger story architecture.
```
