# Grant Proposal Writer for Non-Profit Funding

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Writer for Non-Profit Funding is a free AI prompt that produces structured, persuasive grant applications for organizations seeking funding from foundations, government agencies, and grant-making bodies. This grant proposal prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete application with five essential components: an executive summary that distills your request, a statement of need that establishes urgency and significance, a detailed project description with timeline and expected outcomes, a comprehensive budget aligned to activities, and an evaluation plan with measurable success metrics. Whether you run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, it tailors the narrative to match your funder's mission and priorities while keeping your organization's capacity and beneficiaries front and center. Non-profit program directors, development officers, and fundraising consultants use it to streamline proposal writing while maintaining the clarity and specificity grant reviewers demand. ● Produces executive summaries that capture project essence and funding request in a concise overview ● Articulates compelling statements of need with evidence of the problem's urgency and significance ● Details project implementation with timeline, activities, outcomes, and beneficiary impact ● Generates itemized budgets that map financial requirements to project activities and justify costs ● Designs evaluation frameworks with clear metrics, measurement methods, and reporting timelines ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert grant writer specializing in persuasive, well-structured proposals that secure funding.

## Task

Create a complete grant proposal with five core sections:

1. **Executive Summary** – Concise overview highlighting key aspects and requested funding
2. **Statement of Need** – Compelling articulation of the problem, its significance, and urgency
3. **Project Description** – Detailed solution, implementation timeline, activities, and expected outcomes
4. **Budget** – Comprehensive financial breakdown aligned with project activities
5. **Evaluation Plan** – Clear metrics, measurement methods, and reporting framework to demonstrate success

## Context

**Grant amount:** {{grant-amount}}

**Project details:** {{project-details}}
(Include: non-profit organization name, mission statement, project description, target beneficiaries, and any relevant organizational history or capacity)

**Funder alignment:** {{funder-profile}}
(Include: grant-giving organization name, their mission, funding priorities, and any specific requirements or preferences)

## Output

Structure your response with clear ## headings for each of the five sections. Write in clear, direct prose that emphasizes the project's alignment with the funder's mission and priorities. Use concrete details, measurable outcomes, and logical connections between problem, solution, and impact.
```
