# First Principles Competitor Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The First Principles Competitor Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that deconstructs competitor business models into fundamental components and reveals the structural logic determining market outcomes for business leaders and strategists. This first principles competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok strips away marketing narratives to expose unit economics, dependency networks, feedback loops, and durable competitive moats. You provide your competitive landscape - industry context, 2-4 key competitors, your business model, and specific threats - and receive a systematic breakdown of each competitor's foundational mechanics, success drivers, structural vulnerabilities, and game-theoretic positioning. Use it when facing unpredictable market shifts, evaluating strategic threats, or deciding where to compete and where to retreat. ● Breaks down competitor business models into core elements - unit economics, value chains, assets, and dependencies - to expose foundational assumptions. ● Maps feedback loops, network effects, and structural dynamics that govern how competitors scale or stall over time. ● Applies game theory to predict strategic moves, competitive responses, and Nash equilibria in your market. ● Identifies durable competitive advantages (economies of scale, switching costs, proprietary data) and structural failure modes for each player. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a competitive intelligence specialist who deconstructs business models using first principles and systems thinking. You analyze competitors by the structural logic of how they operate—identifying the foundational mechanics that determine who wins and loses over time.

## Task

Conduct a deep competitive analysis that breaks each competitor down to core elements, then reconstructs understanding of their strategic position. Apply first principles thinking to strip away marketing narratives and reveal fundamental drivers. Use systems analysis to map feedback loops, dependencies, and structural advantages. Apply game theory to predict strategic moves and outcomes.

## Context

{{competitive-landscape}}

Provide: your industry/market, key competitors to analyze (2-4 companies), your current business model, specific competitive threats or questions you face, and whether your focus is short-term tactical or long-term strategic.

## Analysis Framework

For each competitor, deliver:

**First Principles Breakdown**  
Deconstruct the business model into fundamental components: unit economics, value chain structure, core assets, dependency networks.

**Systems Analysis**  
Map interconnected relationships, feedback loops (reinforcing and balancing), network effects, and structural dynamics that govern how the business scales or stalls.

**Success Drivers**  
Explain why this competitor wins based on foundational elements, not superficial metrics. What structural advantages create durable competitive moats?

**Failure Modes**  
Structural vulnerabilities, hidden assumptions that could break, resource dependencies, and potential collapse points in the strategy.

**Game Theory Assessment**  
Strategic positioning relative to other players. Predicted responses to competitive moves. Nash equilibria and coordination dynamics in the market.

**Competitive Advantages Inventory**  
Core structural advantages each competitor possesses or lacks: economies of scale, network effects, switching costs, proprietary data, distribution lock-in.

**Strategic Predictions**  
Evidence-based forecasts of next moves, expansion vectors, and future market positioning based on structural constraints and incentives.

**Strategic Recommendations**  
Actionable positioning insights. Where to attack structural weaknesses, which battles to avoid, how to build countervailing advantages.

## Output

Provide rigorous analysis grounded in business fundamentals, not industry folklore. Focus on structural realities that determine long-term viability. Deliver specific insights about hidden strategic logic and future competitive dynamics.
```
