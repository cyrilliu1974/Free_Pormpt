# Long-Tail Keyword Research Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Long-Tail Keyword Research Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that analyzes your business context and produces a strategic list of rankable, high-intent keywords for organic traffic growth. This long-tail keyword research prompt for ChatGPT works by examining your niche, audience, and competitive landscape to identify 3+ word keyword phrases that balance search opportunity with attainability. It outputs a markdown table with search volume estimates and difficulty scores for 15-25 keywords, sorted by volume, so you can prioritize content creation and optimization efforts around terms that attract qualified visitors. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, the prompt evaluates both commercial and informational intent to surface keywords aligned with your business goals rather than chasing vanity metrics. SEO specialists, content strategists, and marketing teams will reach for this prompt when building a content roadmap, entering a new market vertical, or auditing an existing keyword strategy for untapped opportunities. ● Produces a structured markdown table with keyword, search volume, and difficulty columns for easy filtering and decision-making. ● Focuses on 3+ word long-tail phrases that reflect real user intent and match your specific industry, geography, and offerings. ● Evaluates ranking feasibility alongside traffic potential, helping smaller sites compete without chasing impossibly competitive terms. ● Delivers 15-25 keyword recommendations in a single run, giving you a substantive foundation for content planning and on-page optimization. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist conducting long-tail keyword research to drive targeted organic traffic.

## Task
Analyze the business niche and compile a prioritized list of long-tail keywords that balance search opportunity with attainability. For each keyword, provide search volume estimates and difficulty scores.

## Context
Business and audience details:
{{business-context}}

Focus on long-tail keywords (3+ words) that:
- Align with the products, services, and content offerings
- Match the search intent of the target audience
- Reflect the geographic and industry focus
- Offer realistic ranking potential given competitive landscape

Evaluate keywords on their ability to attract qualified traffic, not just volume. Prioritize those with commercial or informational intent relevant to the business goals.

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Keyword**, **Search Volume**, and **Keyword Difficulty**. Sort by search volume in descending order. Include 15-25 keywords.

Example format:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Keyword Difficulty |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|
| best organic dog food for puppies | 2,400 | 35 |
| how to train a rescue dog at home | 1,800 | 28 |
```
