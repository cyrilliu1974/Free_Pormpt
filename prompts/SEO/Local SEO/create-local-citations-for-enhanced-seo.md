# Local Citation Building Strategy for SEO

## 簡介

The Local Citation Building Strategy for SEO is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive citation strategies to improve local search rankings for businesses seeking better visibility in their geographic markets. This local citation building prompt for ChatGPT takes your business profile and priority citation sites, then produces a complete six-stage strategy covering NAP audit, site identification, listing creation, optimization, verification, and ongoing maintenance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable instructions for ensuring NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across directories, handling duplicate listings, and optimizing each citation with categories, descriptions, images, and business hours. Real-world use cases include multi-location businesses standardizing citations, local service providers entering new markets, and agencies managing client visibility across Google Business Profile, Yelp, and industry-specific directories. This prompt is designed for local SEO specialists, digital marketing agencies, small business owners, and anyone responsible for improving local search rankings through accurate business listings. ● Conducts pre-launch NAP audits to establish consistent business information across all platforms ● Identifies relevant citation opportunities beyond initial priority sites based on business category and location ● Provides clear claim and creation workflows for both new listings and existing duplicates ● Includes optimization techniques for categories, descriptions, images, hours, and other listing attributes ● Establishes verification procedures and ongoing monitoring protocols to maintain citation quality ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert local SEO specialist focused on building high-quality local citations that improve search visibility and rankings.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step citation building strategy for the business described below. Cover the complete process from identifying relevant citation sites through creating, claiming, and optimizing listings for maximum local SEO impact.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-profile}}

Priority citation sites:
{{citation-sites}}

Ensure strict NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all platforms. Address how to identify additional relevant citation opportunities beyond the listed sites, handle duplicate listings, verify citations, and optimize each listing with categories, descriptions, images, and business hours where applicable.

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a numbered list, with each step containing clear, actionable instructions. Include:
1. Pre-launch NAP audit and preparation
2. Citation site identification and prioritization
3. Listing creation or claim process
4. Optimization techniques for each citation
5. Verification and quality control
6. Ongoing monitoring and maintenance
```
