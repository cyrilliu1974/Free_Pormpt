# Monthly Business Report Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Monthly Business Report Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed performance reports with key metrics, financial data, trend analysis, and actionable recommendations for business analysts and managers. This business reporting prompt for ChatGPT structures your raw metrics and financial data into professional reports with four core sections: an executive summary highlighting critical findings, a performance analysis table comparing previous and current period metrics with percentage changes, a trends and insights section identifying patterns and business implications, and strategic recommendations for improvement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming scattered data points into cohesive monthly, quarterly, or annual reports. Teams use it to standardize reporting formats, save hours on manual report assembly, and ensure consistent analysis quality across departments. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn performance data into executive-ready reports that support data-driven decision-making and strategic planning. ● Builds period-over-period comparison tables showing previous period, current period, and percentage change for each metric ● Generates executive summaries that surface the most critical findings without requiring readers to parse full data sets ● Identifies business-relevant trends and patterns in performance data with clear implications ● Delivers actionable recommendations grounded in the specific metrics and changes observed in your reporting period ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst creating comprehensive performance reports.

## Task
Generate a detailed {{report-type}} report for {{reporting-period}} that presents key performance metrics, financial data, trends, and actionable insights.

## Context
Include the following metrics and data in your analysis:
{{metrics-and-financial-data}}

Analyze period-over-period changes to identify trends, patterns, and areas for improvement. Formulate actionable insights to guide future decision-making and strategy.

## Output
Structure the report with:

**Executive Summary**
A concise overview of the most critical findings and recommendations.

**Performance Analysis**
Detailed examination of each metric, organized in a table:

| Metric | Previous Period | Current Period | % Change |
|--------|----------------|----------------|----------|

**Trends & Insights**
Key patterns observed in the data and their business implications.

**Recommendations**
Actionable next steps based on the analysis to improve performance.
```
