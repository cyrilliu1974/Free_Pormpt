# Weekly Meal Plan Generator for Households

## 簡介

The Weekly Meal Plan Generator for Households is a free AI prompt that creates customized 7-day meal plans tailored to dietary preferences, family size, cooking ability, time constraints, and budget. This meal planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your household details and nutritional requirements to generate a balanced, varied weekly menu. It delivers three meals per day for seven days in a structured markdown table format, complete with corresponding ingredient lists. Nutrition professionals use it to design client meal plans, busy parents rely on it to organize family dinners, and individuals turn to it when they need practical meal ideas that fit their lifestyle and dietary restrictions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is for anyone who needs consistent, practical meal planning - whether you're feeding a family, managing dietary requirements, working within a tight grocery budget, or simply want to eat more intentionally without spending hours planning. ● Creates 7 full days of meal ideas (breakfast, lunch, dinner) in one structured table ● Accounts for dietary preferences, allergies, family size, and cooking skill level ● Includes complete ingredient lists for each day to simplify grocery shopping ● Balances variety, nutrition, and practicality based on time and budget limits ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist and meal planner.

## Task
Create a personalized weekly meal plan (7 days) that is balanced, diverse, and practical for the household described below.

## Context
{{household-and-dietary-details}}

Analyze the dietary preferences and nutritional needs, then design a varied menu that incorporates different foods and flavors. Ensure the plan is realistic given the cooking skill level, time constraints, and budget. Each day should include breakfast, lunch, and dinner suggestions.

## Output
Provide the meal plan as a markdown table with two columns:

| Day | Meal Ideas | Ingredients |

Include one row per day (7 rows total). In the "Meal Ideas" column, list breakfast, lunch, and dinner. In the "Ingredients" column, list all ingredients needed for that day's meals.
```
