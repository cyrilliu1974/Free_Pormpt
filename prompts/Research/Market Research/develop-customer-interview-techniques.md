# Customer Interview Guide for Market Research

## 簡介

The Customer Interview Guide for Market Research is a free AI prompt that builds a structured, seven-phase framework for conducting effective customer interviews and extracting actionable insights from any target market. This customer interview prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every stage of primary research: defining research objectives tied to business goals, identifying and recruiting your ideal participants, crafting open-ended interview questions that flow logically, conducting interviews that balance structure with natural conversation, analyzing transcripts to surface themes and patterns, building customer personas, and presenting findings to stakeholders in a visually engaging report. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for market researchers, product managers, UX researchers, and founders who need to understand customer motivations, pain points, and decision-making processes. The output is a ready-to-implement guide with numbered steps, sub-task bullets, and best practices you can apply immediately to your next round of interviews. ● Covers all seven phases of customer interview research in a logical, dependency-driven sequence ● Provides practical techniques for crafting open-ended questions, probing for deeper responses, and identifying themes in qualitative data ● Includes guidance on creating screening questionnaires, scheduling logistics, and developing customer personas from interview findings ● Delivers a framework for translating raw interview notes into stakeholder-ready reports and action plans ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert market researcher specializing in customer interview methodologies and primary research strategy.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for conducting customer interviews to gather primary market research insights for {{target-market}}.

## Guidelines

### 1. Define Research Objectives
- List key research questions
- Ensure objectives align with business goals

### 2. Identify Target Audience
- Describe ideal customer profile
- Specify demographic, psychographic, and behavioral characteristics
- Determine sample size and recruitment criteria

### 3. Develop Interview Guide
- Outline interview structure and flow
- Craft open-ended questions that build logically on each other
- Include probing questions to deepen responses
- Allocate time for each section

### 4. Recruit Participants
- Identify channels to reach target audience
- Create screening questionnaire
- Offer incentives if necessary
- Schedule interviews

### 5. Conduct Interviews
- Introduce purpose and establish rapport
- Ask questions from guide while allowing natural conversation
- Actively listen, take notes, and probe for details
- Conclude by thanking participant and discussing next steps

### 6. Analyze and Synthesize Findings
- Transcribe recordings and organize notes
- Identify themes, patterns, and insights
- Map relationships between findings
- Develop customer personas
- Summarize findings and recommendations

### 7. Report and Communicate Results
- Create visually engaging report
- Use storytelling techniques to convey insights
- Present results to stakeholders
- Develop action plan based on insights

## Output

Provide the guide using numbered steps with bullet points for sub-tasks. Focus on practical techniques and actionable best practices that can be implemented immediately. Ensure logical flow and coherence throughout.
```
