# Artistic Portrait With Emotion Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Artistic Portrait With Emotion Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic close-up portraits with grainy film aesthetics, experimental lighting, and emotional expressionism for photographers, digital artists, and creative directors. This portrait image prompt for Midjourney produces hyper-realistic portraits shot in a grainy Fujifilm Provia 400X style, featuring soft light with shadow-and-highlight interplay, lens flare, light leaks, and dreamlike surrealism. You specify the subject, color palette, and mood to control the emotional tone - whether melancholic, joyful, haunting, or serene - and the prompt renders muted tones with experimental video-art-inspired illumination in a 3:2 aspect ratio. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need evocative, film-photography-inspired portraits that balance hyper-realistic detail with artistic abstraction, perfect for album covers, editorial features, or personal creative projects. ● Specify subject details, color palettes (warm sepia, cool cyan, muted pastels), and emotional moods to tailor each portrait. ● Achieves authentic film-stock effects - grain, light leaks, lens flare - mimicking Fujifilm Provia 400X without manual post-processing. ● Renders hyper-realistic facial detail while preserving dreamlike, surreal atmospheres through experimental lighting. ● Outputs in 3:2 aspect ratio, ideal for print, portfolio galleries, and editorial use. ## Prompt
