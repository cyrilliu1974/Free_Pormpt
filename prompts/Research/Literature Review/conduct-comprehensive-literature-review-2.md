# Literature Review Prompt for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Literature Review Prompt for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that produces organized, citation-rich literature reviews for students, researchers, and analysts across any discipline. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT walks through a systematic review process: it identifies 3-5 leading researchers and their contributions, organizes major breakthroughs into thematic sub-topics with full citations, and surfaces real-world applications of the research. You supply a topic and field, and the prompt structures findings into six sections (Overview, Key Researchers, Major Breakthroughs & Findings, Potential Applications, and References) formatted as clear bullet points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to synthesize complex academic literature without starting from scratch. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly understand the state of research in an unfamiliar area, prepare background for a thesis or grant proposal, or build a foundation for your own scholarly work. ● Surfaces 3-5 leading researchers and their specific contributions to the field ● Groups major findings into 3-4 thematic sub-topics with 2-3 key discoveries each, all cited ● Identifies 3-5 potential real-world applications of the research ● Produces a complete references section with full citations for every source mentioned ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a research analyst conducting a comprehensive literature review, skilled at identifying key researchers, breakthroughs, and applications across diverse fields.

## Task
Conduct a thorough literature review on {{topic-and-field}} and present findings in a clear, organized structure.

## Output
Organize your review into these sections:

**Overview**
- Brief introduction to the topic and its significance in the field

**Key Researchers**
- List 3-5 leading researchers with their primary contributions

**Major Breakthroughs & Findings**
- Group by relevant sub-topics (3-4 sub-topics)
- Include 2-3 key findings per sub-topic
- Provide citations for each finding

**Potential Applications**
- Identify 3-5 real-world applications with brief descriptions

**References**
- Full citations for all sources used

Format as a bullet-point structure grouped by sub-topics. Do not use XML tags.
```
