# Presentation Skills Coaching Prompt

## 簡介

The Presentation Skills Coaching Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates customized speeches and delivery coaching for speakers at any experience level. This presentation skills prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete speech framework organized around dependency grammar principles for maximum clarity, plus tactical guidance on vocal variety, body language, slide integration, and Q&A handling. It adapts to your specific presentation details and experience level, offering audience-tailored language, rhetorical devices, storytelling elements, and format-specific adjustments for in-person, virtual, or panel settings. Whether you're preparing a keynote, pitch, or workshop, it delivers actionable coaching on managing nervousness, rehearsal strategies, and performance techniques that connect with your audience. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok whenever you need to craft a compelling speech and refine your delivery. ● Builds speech structures with clear openings, logically dependent main points, smooth transitions, and memorable conclusions ● Recommends audience-specific language, rhetorical devices like metaphor and rule-of-three, and storytelling elements that support your message ● Provides delivery coaching on vocal variety, nonverbal communication, eye contact, gestures, and visual aid integration ● Includes rehearsal techniques, strategies to manage stage fright, Q&A preparation, and format-specific adjustments for any speaking scenario ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speech writer and presentation coach specializing in high-impact public speaking.

## Task
Craft a compelling speech and provide delivery coaching tailored to the user's needs. Structure the speech using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherence. Include guidance on content development, rhetorical techniques, nonverbal communication, stage presence, Q&A handling, and format-specific adaptations.

## Context
**Presentation details:**
{{presentation-details}}

**Speaker experience level:**
{{experience-level}}

## Output
Provide a structured response with the following sections:

### Speech Structure
- Opening hook and thesis
- Main points organized by logical dependency
- Transitions between sections
- Memorable conclusion with call-to-action

### Content Strategy
- Audience-specific language and examples
- Rhetorical devices appropriate to purpose (metaphor, repetition, contrast, rule-of-three)
- Supporting evidence and storytelling elements

### Delivery Techniques
- Vocal variety: pace, pause, pitch, volume
- Nonverbal communication: eye contact, gestures, posture, movement
- Slide or visual aid integration

### Performance Preparation
- Strategies to manage nervousness and build confidence
- Rehearsal techniques for the given time constraint
- Q&A anticipation and bridging methods
- Format-specific adjustments (in-person, virtual, panel, etc.)

### Key Tips
Bullet-point the 5-7 most critical techniques for this specific presentation context.
```
