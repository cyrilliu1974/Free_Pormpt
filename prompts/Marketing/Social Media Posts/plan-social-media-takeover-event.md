# Social Media Takeover Event Planner

## 簡介

The Social Media Takeover Event Planner is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive execution plans for brands and marketers looking to maximize reach and engagement through platform takeover campaigns. This social media takeover prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every strategic component needed to launch a successful event: from defining measurable goals and profiling your target audience to developing day-by-day content timelines, platform-specific tactics, and post-event analysis frameworks. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce structured plans that cover content themes, posting cadence, native feature usage (Stories, Reels, Lives, polls), influencer partnership opportunities, engagement drivers, and contingency protocols for common challenges like low participation or technical hiccups. Use it when planning a takeover to boost brand awareness, launch a product, or collaborate with creators. ● Profiles your target audience with demographics, behaviors, pain points, and content preferences to guide creative decisions ● Builds a content timeline customized to your event duration, unique selling proposition, and chosen platform ● Identifies platform-specific features and engagement tactics to drive comments, shares, and user-generated content ● Includes success metrics, tracking methods, contingency plans, and post-event analysis frameworks for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist planning a Social Media Takeover Event.

## Task
Create a comprehensive event plan to increase brand awareness and engagement on {{platform}}. The plan must include:

- **Goals & Success Metrics**: Define measurable objectives (reach, engagement rate, conversions) and how they will be tracked
- **Target Audience Analysis**: Profile {{target-audience}}, including demographics, behaviors, pain points, and content preferences
- **Content Strategy**: Develop a timeline for {{event-duration}} with specific post types, themes, and cadence that showcase {{usp}}
- **Platform-Specific Tactics**: Leverage native features (Stories, Reels, Lives, polls, hashtags) to maximize visibility and interaction
- **Collaboration Opportunities**: Suggest influencer partnerships or brand collaborations aligned with the audience
- **Engagement Tactics**: Provide strategies to drive comments, shares, and user-generated content during the takeover
- **Contingency Plans**: Address potential challenges (low engagement, technical issues, negative feedback) with mitigation strategies
- **Post-Event Analysis**: Outline methods to evaluate performance against goals and extract actionable insights

## Context
Brand: {{brand}}

Structure the plan with clear headings and subheadings. Use bullet points for actionable details under each section.

## Output
Deliver a complete, implementation-ready takeover plan organized by the sections above.
```
