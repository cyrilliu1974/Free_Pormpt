# Product Bundle Ideas Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Product Bundle Ideas Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that generates strategic product bundle concepts to increase customer lifetime value for independent business owners. This product bundle prompt for ChatGPT produces 10 distinct product ideas that naturally connect to your existing offerings and audience. It works by analyzing your business context and creating synergistic bundle options across diverse formats - digital products, services, communities, SaaS tools, and mobile apps - all structured around a one-time payment model priced above $99. Each recommendation includes the product name, type, a clear description, connection to your core business, and unique value proposition. Solopreneurs use it to expand their revenue streams without adding recurring operational costs, exploring fresh angles that avoid commodity markets and overdone concepts. This prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to diversify your product offerings strategically while maintaining alignment with your current business model and audience needs. ● Creates 10 diverse product concepts spanning digital downloads, premium services, SaaS platforms, mobile applications, and community offerings ● Ensures each bundle idea connects organically to your existing business and audience for natural cross-selling opportunities ● Focuses on one-time payment models above $99 to maximize lifetime value without ongoing operational overhead ● Delivers formatted output with product names, descriptions, business synergies, and unique value propositions in clean Markdown ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer and expert in product bundle strategy for solopreneurs.

## Task
Generate 10 creative product ideas for a bundle that will increase customer lifetime value. Each idea should:

- Connect naturally to the user's current product and audience, creating clear synergies with their existing business
- Use a one-time payment model priced above $99, avoiding products with significant recurring operational costs
- Span diverse formats: digital products, services, communities, SaaS tools, and mobile apps
- Avoid commodity markets and overdone concepts; prioritize fresh angles that stand out

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

## Output
Present each product idea with:
- Product name and type
- Brief description (2-3 sentences)
- How it connects to the existing business
- Why it delivers unique value

Format your response in Markdown with clear numbering.
```
