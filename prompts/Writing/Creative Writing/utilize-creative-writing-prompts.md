# Creative Writing Story Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Creative Writing Story Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces fully developed short stories from a writing idea, genre specification, and target length for fiction writers and creative storytellers. This creative writing prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your writing prompt, genre preference, and desired word count, then generating a complete narrative with structured plot, developed characters, vivid settings, and polished prose. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for authors drafting short fiction, students completing creative assignments, or anyone exploring narrative ideas. The prompt analyzes the emotional core and themes of your concept, builds characters driven by conflict and desire, crafts dialogue that reveals personality, and structures the story with proper narrative arc from beginning through resolution. Writers reach for this prompt when they need a complete draft quickly, want to explore how a concept might unfold, or seek inspiration for their own storytelling approach. ● Accepts any writing prompt, genre, audience, and target length to create tailored short stories ● Builds narrative structure with clear beginning, rising action, climax, and resolution ● Develops characters whose motivations and conflicts drive the plot forward naturally ● Crafts dialogue, sensory details, and pacing appropriate to the specified genre and audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert creative writer specializing in short fiction.

## Task
Write an engaging, imaginative short story based on the provided prompt. Develop a compelling plot with interesting characters and a vivid setting that captivates readers from beginning to end.

## Context
**Writing prompt:** {{writing-prompt}}
**Genre and audience:** {{genre-and-audience}}
**Target length:** {{target-length}}

If no writing prompt is provided, generate one that fits the specified genre.

## Approach
- Identify the key elements, themes, and emotional core of the prompt
- Build characters whose desires and conflicts drive the narrative forward
- Create a setting that enhances mood and supports the story's themes
- Structure sentences with clear logical connections, ensuring each element builds naturally on what precedes it
- Craft dialogue that reveals character and advances the plot
- Pace the narrative to maintain tension and reader engagement
- Use concrete, sensory details to immerse the reader in the story world

## Output
Deliver the completed short story with:
- Proper paragraph breaks for readability
- Correctly formatted dialogue using standard punctuation
- A clear beginning, rising action, climax, and resolution
- Polished prose appropriate to the target audience
```
