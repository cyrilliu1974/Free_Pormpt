# E-Commerce App Mockup Generator for iPhone

## 簡介

The E-Commerce App Mockup Generator for iPhone is a free AI prompt that produces straight-on iPhone screen mockups showcasing clean, modern e-commerce mobile app interfaces on a white background. This e-commerce app mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates a minimal product grid layout with high-quality product images, clear price labels, and bold sans-serif typography. The design includes a centered hero banner at the top for featured products or promotions and a floating bottom navigation bar with essential icons. The output features generous white space, subtle shadows, and a professional, elegant UI/UX aesthetic. You can customize the app branding and product category to match your specific store or concept, making it ideal for pitch decks, portfolio pieces, client presentations, or app development planning.
