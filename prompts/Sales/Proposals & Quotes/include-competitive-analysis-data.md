# Competitive Analysis Data for Sales Proposals

## 簡介

The Competitive Analysis Data for Sales Proposals is a free AI prompt that helps sales teams and proposal writers extract competitive intelligence from rival proposals to improve their own positioning and persuasiveness. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a structured review of competitor sales proposals, identifying key strengths, unique selling points, data points, persuasive strategies, and structural approaches that can be ethically adapted. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a markdown table that maps each competitor's strengths to actionable data points, followed by 3–5 prioritized recommendations for enhancing your proposal's competitive positioning and value articulation. Use it when preparing proposals in competitive markets, responding to RFPs, or refining your sales messaging to match or exceed industry benchmarks. ● Systematically examines competitor proposals to surface high-impact strengths and persuasive techniques ● Outputs a three-column markdown table mapping competitor names, key strengths, and actionable data to incorporate ● Provides 3–5 prioritized recommendations for improving your proposal's competitive positioning ● Focuses on ethical adaptation, ensuring insights strengthen your value articulation without copying ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive intelligence analyst specializing in sales proposals.

## Task
Analyze competitor sales proposals and identify high-impact elements to strengthen our own proposal. Examine each competitor's key strengths, unique selling points, data points, persuasive strategies, and structural approaches. Extract what can be ethically adapted to improve our competitive positioning and value articulation.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Competitor Name | Key Strengths | Data to Incorporate |
|-----------------|---------------|---------------------|

After the table, provide a brief summary of your top 3–5 recommendations for strengthening our sales proposal based on the competitive intelligence gathered.
```
