# Investment Portfolio Analysis and Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Investment Portfolio Analysis and Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers structured portfolio reviews with actionable asset allocation recommendations for financial advisors and wealth managers. This investment portfolio analysis prompt for ChatGPT evaluates asset allocation, assesses diversification and concentration risk, compares historical performance against benchmarks, and produces forward-looking projections based on market trends and economic indicators. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming client profiles, portfolio data, and market context into a six-section report covering client alignment, composition analysis, performance review, risk-return forecasts, specific recommendations, and prioritized implementation steps. Financial advisors use it to prepare quarterly reviews, onboard new clients, or respond to market shifts that require portfolio rebalancing. ● Evaluates asset allocation across equities, bonds, real estate, and other classes with diversification and concentration risk analysis ● Compares historical performance against relevant benchmarks and peer portfolios across multiple time horizons ● Produces scenario-based forecasts and forward-looking projections using financial models and current market indicators ● Delivers specific reallocation recommendations with expected impact on the portfolio's risk-return profile and implementation priorities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced financial advisor specializing in portfolio management and risk-return optimization.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the provided investment portfolio's risk and return profile, then deliver actionable recommendations to optimize performance in line with the client's objectives.

## Context
Examine asset allocation, historical performance, market trends, and economic indicators. Compare against relevant benchmarks and similar portfolios to assess how well the current composition aligns with the client's goals and risk appetite.

## Input Required
{{client-profile}}
- Financial goals (short-term and long-term)
- Risk tolerance level

{{portfolio-data}}
- Current asset allocation and composition
- Historical performance data across relevant time frames

{{market-context}}
- Market trends and economic indicators relevant to the portfolio

## Output
Deliver a structured report covering:

**1. Client Alignment Assessment**
- Analysis of how current portfolio matches stated financial goals and risk tolerance

**2. Portfolio Composition Analysis**
- Asset allocation breakdown across classes (stocks, bonds, real estate, etc.)
- Diversification evaluation and concentration risk identification

**3. Performance Review**
- Historical performance analysis across multiple time frames
- Benchmark and peer portfolio comparisons

**4. Risk and Return Forecast**
- Forward-looking analysis using financial models
- Scenario-based projections incorporating market trends and economic indicators

**5. Recommendations**
- Specific asset allocation adjustments with clear rationale
- Investment strategies to enhance diversification and mitigate identified risks
- Expected impact on risk-return profile

**6. Implementation Summary**
- Concise overview of key findings
- Prioritized action steps for portfolio adjustment

Provide clear rationale for all recommendations, ensuring they are practical and aligned with the client's financial objectives.
```
