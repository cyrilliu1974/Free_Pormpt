# Workflow Process Analysis and Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Workflow Process Analysis and Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that systematically evaluates business workflows to uncover inefficiencies and recommend specific improvements for analysts, operations managers, and process consultants. This workflow analysis prompt for ChatGPT breaks down each step of a process to identify bottlenecks, redundancies, underutilized resources, and automation opportunities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a three-column markdown table that maps current process steps against identified inefficiencies and concrete optimization opportunities. Real-world applications include auditing customer service workflows, streamlining manufacturing operations, redesigning approval chains, and evaluating resource allocation across departments. The output prioritizes recommendations by impact and feasibility, making it immediately actionable for teams. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond gut feelings and apply structured analysis to any workflow - whether onboarding, order fulfillment, content production, or financial close processes. ● Examines each workflow step for bottlenecks, redundancies, and automation potential ● Produces a structured markdown table that clearly links process steps to problems and solutions ● Considers automation, resource reallocation, process redesign, and industry best practices ● Prioritizes recommendations by impact and feasibility for immediate implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business process analyst specializing in workflow efficiency optimization.

## Task
Analyze the provided business process to identify inefficiencies and propose concrete optimization opportunities. Examine each step for bottlenecks, redundancies, underutilized resources, and automation potential. For every inefficiency identified, recommend specific solutions that streamline operations and boost productivity.

## Context
{{process-details}}

Consider factors including:
- Automation and technology integration
- Resource reallocation
- Process redesign and elimination of redundant steps
- Industry-specific best practices

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Process | Inefficiencies | Optimization Opportunities |
|----------------|----------------|---------------------------|
| [Step description] | [Identified problems] | [Recommended solutions] |

Ensure each row addresses a discrete process step or workflow segment. Prioritize recommendations by impact and feasibility.
```
