# Sales Funnel Conversion Rate Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Conversion Rate Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps marketers, growth teams, and business owners diagnose conversion bottlenecks and optimize each stage of their sales funnel. This sales funnel analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your funnel stages, business context, and current overall conversion rate, then produces a markdown table breaking down conversion performance at every step. The AI examines user experience friction, messaging alignment, pricing strategy, customer support gaps, and technical barriers, delivering 2-4 specific, actionable recommendations per stage with prioritization guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, giving you a consultant-grade funnel audit in minutes. Reach for this prompt when you need to identify where prospects drop off, understand why conversion rates lag at specific stages, or build a prioritized optimization roadmap without hiring an external analyst. ● Identifies bottlenecks by analyzing conversion rates at each funnel stage ● Assesses user experience friction, messaging clarity, pricing perception, and support quality ● Provides 2-4 specific, prioritized recommendations per stage in a structured markdown table ● Adapts to any funnel structure, from lead generation to checkout, across B2B and B2C contexts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales funnel analyst specializing in conversion rate optimization.

## Task
Analyze the effectiveness of each stage in the provided sales funnel and deliver specific, actionable recommendations to improve conversion rates at every stage.

## Context
**Sales funnel stages:** {{funnel-stages}}

**Business context:** {{business-context}}

**Current overall conversion rate:** {{overall-conversion-rate}}

Examine conversion rates at each funnel stage to identify bottlenecks and underperformance. Consider:
- User experience and friction points
- Messaging clarity and alignment
- Pricing strategy and perceived value
- Customer support availability and quality
- Technical or process barriers

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Funnel Stage | Conversion Rate | Recommendations |
|--------------|-----------------|------------------|

For each stage, provide:
- Clear conversion rate assessment
- 2-4 specific, actionable recommendations
- Prioritization guidance where appropriate
```
