# Sales Recognition Rewards Program Builder

## 簡介

The Sales Recognition Rewards Program Builder is a free AI prompt that designs tailored incentive programs for sales teams that balance motivation, fairness, and company goals. This sales recognition prompt for ChatGPT helps HR managers and sales leaders create complete rewards frameworks with at least five distinct tiers - from individual achievements to team-level excellence - presented in a clear markdown table format. It incorporates monetary, experiential, developmental, and recognition-based rewards alongside measurable performance criteria and multiple recognition methods that appeal to different employee motivations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and produces output aligned with your specific sales-team-context variable, ensuring scalability as teams grow and sustainability within stated budgets. Reach for it when launching a new incentive program, refreshing outdated recognition systems, or aligning sales performance with strategic objectives. ● Balances four reward categories - monetary bonuses, experiential perks, professional development, and public recognition - to address diverse motivations ● Establishes clear, measurable performance criteria that connect individual effort to company goals and foster a culture of excellence ● Outputs a structured markdown table with Reward, Criteria, and Recognition Method columns for immediate implementation ● Ensures sustainability and scalability by embedding budget considerations and growth planning into the program design ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Human Resources manager specializing in employee recognition and sales incentive programs.

## Task
Design a comprehensive rewards program for top-performing sales team members. The program must align with company goals, motivate sales staff, and foster a culture of excellence. Ensure the plan is fair, transparent, and scalable across the organization.

## Context
{{sales-team-context}}

## Requirements
- Include diverse reward types (monetary, experiential, developmental, recognition-based)
- Establish clear, measurable, and achievable performance criteria
- Incorporate multiple recognition methods to appeal to different motivations
- Ensure the program is sustainable within the stated budget
- Design for scalability as the team grows

## Output
Present your rewards program as a markdown table with 3 columns: **Reward** | **Criteria** | **Recognition Method**

Provide at least 5 distinct reward tiers or categories, progressing from individual achievements to team-level excellence.
```
