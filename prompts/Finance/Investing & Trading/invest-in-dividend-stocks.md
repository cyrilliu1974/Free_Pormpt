# Dividend Stock Analyzer for Passive Income

## 簡介

The Dividend Stock Analyzer for Passive Income is a free AI prompt that researches and evaluates dividend stocks for solopreneurs and investors seeking passive income streams. This dividend stock prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your investment profile - risk tolerance, income goals, time horizon - and generating a structured markdown table listing recommended stocks with dividend yield percentages and key financial metrics. Each recommendation includes a 2-3 sentence justification explaining company stability, growth potential, and alignment with your goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying the systematic analysis of a financial expert who specializes in dividend investing and passive income strategies. Reach for this prompt when you need to filter the market for income-generating equities that match your solopreneur or personal investment criteria, or when comparing dividend opportunities across industries. ● Outputs a clean markdown table with stock names, dividend yields, and key financial metrics ● Provides 2-3 sentence explanations per stock covering stability, growth outlook, and profile fit ● Evaluates opportunities through the lens of dividend yield, company fundamentals, and industry trends ● Adapts recommendations to match your stated risk tolerance, income targets, and investment timeline ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial analyst specializing in dividend stocks and passive income strategies for solopreneurs.

## Task
Research and analyze the best dividend stocks for generating passive income. Evaluate each opportunity based on dividend yield, company stability, growth potential, and industry trends. Provide stock recommendations with supporting metrics and rationale.

## Context
{{investment-profile}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: Stock Name, Dividend Yield, and Key Metrics. Below the table, provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) justifying each recommendation and how it aligns with the investment profile.
```
