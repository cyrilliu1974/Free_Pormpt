# Course Syllabus Builder for Educators and Trainers

## 簡介

The Course Syllabus Builder for Educators and Trainers is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, week-by-week course syllabi for instructors, curriculum designers, and corporate trainers. This course syllabus prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete educational roadmap that includes a course overview, weekly breakdowns with specific learning objectives and topics, planned teaching methodologies (lectures, discussions, hands-on activities), required resources, assignments, and clear evaluation methods with grading policies. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your high-level course concept and audience details into a professional syllabus ready for distribution. Whether you are designing a university course, a professional certification program, or a corporate training module, the prompt structures your content into a sequential learning pathway that accommodates diverse learning styles and clearly communicates expectations to students. Use this prompt when you need to formalize a course idea into a teaching document that balances theory, critical thinking, and practical application. ● Produces weekly module breakdowns with learning objectives, topics, methodologies, and assignments ● Includes evaluation methods and grading policies that clarify how students will be assessed ● Covers essential syllabus sections like course policies, instructor information, and prerequisites ● Adapts to any subject, duration, or audience by using your course-details variable as input ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced course creator and educator specializing in designing structured, engaging learning experiences that balance theory, critical thinking, and practical application.

## Task
Create a comprehensive course syllabus that provides a clear, sequential learning pathway. The syllabus should guide students through weekly modules that build systematically on each other, incorporating lectures, discussions, and hands-on activities to support diverse learning styles.

## Context
{{course-details}} should specify: the subject/topic, course duration, expected weekly time commitment, any prerequisites, and the target audience.

## Output
Deliver a complete syllabus structured as follows:

**Course Overview**
- Introduction explaining the topic's relevance and value
- Duration, time commitment, and prerequisites

**Weekly Breakdown** (for each week):
- Week number and descriptive title
- Learning objectives: specific skills and knowledge to be gained
- Topics covered: key concepts and theories
- Teaching methodologies: planned mix of lectures, discussions, and activities
- Required readings/resources: textbooks, articles, videos, tools
- Assignments and projects: homework, quizzes, group work

**Evaluation Methods**
- Assessment types (assignments, quizzes, projects, participation) with grade weights
- Peer evaluation or self-assessment components if applicable

**Grading Policy**
- Clear breakdown of final grade calculation

**Course Policies**
- Attendance, late submissions, academic integrity, and disability accommodations

**Instructor Information**
- Contact details and office hours

Format the syllabus for readability with clear headings and structure. Ensure it motivates students by clearly articulating what they will learn and how the course will support their success.
```
