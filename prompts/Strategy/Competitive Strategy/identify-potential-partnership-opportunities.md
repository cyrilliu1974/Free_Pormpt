# Partnership Opportunity Identification Prompt

## 簡介

The Partnership Opportunity Identification Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes companies in a target industry and identifies high-potential partnership opportunities aligned with your product or service offerings. This partnership opportunity prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your industry, product or service details, and partnership context to research and evaluate 5–8 qualified prospects. It delivers a structured markdown table showing each company name, specific partnership benefits (revenue potential, market access, capability gaps, customer overlap), and concrete next steps such as key contacts to reach, pitch angles, proof-of-concept ideas, or relevant events to attend. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for business development professionals who need to prioritize partnerships with the strongest strategic fit and clearest engagement path. Reach for this prompt when you're expanding into a new market, seeking distribution partners, looking for technology integrations, or building a strategic alliance pipeline. ● Surfaces 5–8 partnership candidates with clear mutual value creation potential based on your specific industry and offerings ● Evaluates synergies across revenue potential, market access, capability alignment, and customer base overlap ● Provides concrete initial actions for each prospect, including key contacts, pitch angles, and engagement opportunities ● Delivers results in a clean markdown table format ready to share with stakeholders or integrate into CRM systems ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business development strategist specializing in partnership identification and opportunity assessment.

## Task
Analyze companies in {{industry}} and identify high-potential partnership opportunities that align with our offerings. Research candidates, evaluate synergies, and propose actionable next steps for each prospect.

## Context
**Our offering:** {{product-service}}

**Partnership context:** {{partnership-context}}

Focus on companies where mutual value creation is clear—where their capabilities, market position, or customer base create natural alignment with our strengths and goals.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Company Name | Partnership Benefit | Next Steps |
|--------------|---------------------|------------|

Include 5–8 qualified prospects. For each:
- **Partnership Benefit:** Specific synergies and mutual value (revenue potential, market access, capability gaps filled, customer overlap)
- **Next Steps:** Concrete initial actions (key contact to reach, pitch angle, proof-of-concept idea, event to attend)

Prioritize partnerships with the strongest strategic fit and clearest path to engagement.
```
