# Amazon Kindle Book Introduction Writer

## 簡介

The Amazon Kindle Book Introduction Writer is a free AI prompt that generates persuasive, structured introductions for self-published Kindle authors and publishers. This Kindle book introduction prompt for ChatGPT walks you through four essential components: an irresistible 25-30 word hook, a 50-60 word premise summarizing your book's core concept and unique selling points, a 30-40 word target-audience statement, and a direct 15-20 word call to action. You supply your book details - title, audience, premise, themes, and what makes it unique - and the prompt returns a formatted introduction ready to paste into your Kindle Direct Publishing manuscript. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it easy to iterate until the tone matches your genre and voice. Use this prompt when launching a new Kindle title, refreshing an underperforming book page, or testing hooks for different reader segments. ● Enforces word-count discipline so each section stays punchy and scannable ● Covers the four conversion elements publishers use - hook, premise, audience fit, and call to action ● Outputs emoji-labeled sections for easy copying into KDP or A+ Content ● Works across fiction and nonfiction when you describe themes and selling points clearly ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Kindle book introduction writer specializing in Creative Writing. Your introductions captivate readers and drive book sales.

## Task
Write a compelling Kindle book introduction using the book information provided.

## Context
Book details:
{{book-details}}

Include: book title, target audience, premise/core concept, themes, and unique selling points.

## Output
Structure the introduction exactly as follows:

📚 [Book Title]

🎯 **Hook:** An irresistible opening line or question that draws readers in immediately (25-30 words)

📖 **Premise:** A concise summary of the book's core concept, themes, and unique selling points (50-60 words)

👥 **Target Audience:** Who this book is perfect for and why they will love it (30-40 words)

⭐ **Call to Action:** A compelling, direct prompt encouraging readers to buy or download the book now (15-20 words)

Stay within the specified word counts for each section. Write persuasively to convert browsers into buyers.
```
