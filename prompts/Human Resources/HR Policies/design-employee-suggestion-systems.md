# Employee Suggestion System Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Employee Suggestion System Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that designs end-to-end employee suggestion programs tailored to your organization's context and operations. This employee suggestion system prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates a complete framework covering submission templates, transparent evaluation methodologies, tiered recognition structures, communication workflows, and phased implementation roadmaps. It addresses the most common failure points in suggestion programs - unclear processes, opaque decisions, and poor follow-through - by delivering ready-to-use forms, quantifiable scoring rubrics, and realistic timelines that integrate with existing operations. Organizations use it to launch pilot programs, redesign stalled initiatives, or scale successful systems while minimizing administrative overhead. This prompt is built for HR leaders, organizational development consultants, and operations managers who need a structured, sustainable approach to capturing and acting on employee ideas. ● Generates copy-paste-ready submission templates with fields and guidance that help employees articulate feasible, well-scoped ideas. ● Provides transparent evaluation criteria with scoring rubrics, decision workflows, and review cycles that ensure fair, consistent assessment. ● Designs tiered recognition systems that reward both implemented suggestions and quality participation, fostering sustained engagement. ● Delivers phased implementation roadmaps with pilot phases, success metrics, and scale-up milestones tailored to your company context. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational development consultant specializing in continuous improvement systems. Design a structured employee suggestion program that drives engagement and measurable operational gains.

## Context
Most suggestion systems fail due to unclear submission processes, opaque evaluation criteria, and inadequate recognition. A successful program requires end-to-end workflows—from idea capture through evaluation, implementation tracking, and employee feedback—that integrate with existing operations and demonstrate ROI.

## Task
Create a complete employee suggestion system framework tailored to {{company-context}}. The framework must include:

- **Submission template**: Fields and guidance that help employees articulate actionable, well-scoped ideas
- **Evaluation methodology**: Transparent scoring criteria (feasibility, impact, strategic alignment) and decision workflow
- **Recognition system**: Tiered rewards for both implemented ideas and quality participation
- **Communication workflows**: Status updates, feedback loops, and reporting dashboards that keep contributors informed
- **Implementation roadmap**: Phased rollout plan with pilot phase, success metrics, and scale-up milestones

Emphasize sustainability: design for minimal administrative overhead and easy integration into daily operations.

## Output
Deliver the framework with clear section headings:

1. **Submission Template** (ready-to-use form)
2. **Evaluation Criteria & Workflow** (scoring rubric, review cycle, decision tree)
3. **Recognition & Incentive Structure** (tiered rewards, celebration rituals)
4. **Communication Plan** (channels, cadence, status taxonomy)
5. **Implementation Timeline** (phases, owners, milestones, KPIs)

Provide actionable detail: templates should be copy-paste ready; criteria quantifiable; timelines realistic.
```
