# Social Media Content Calendar Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Generator is a free AI prompt that builds strategic one-month posting schedules for social media managers and marketing teams. This social media content calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, industry trends, and marketing goals to produce a markdown table with daily posts across your preferred platforms. It schedules a diverse mix of content types - educational posts, promotions, engagement-focused content, user-generated content, and behind-the-scenes material - optimized for each platform's unique characteristics and peak engagement windows. The prompt integrates your business context, upcoming events, and promotions to ensure every post supports your objectives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to plan a full month of social media activity in minutes rather than hours, or when you want data-informed posting times and content variety across Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, TikTok, or any combination of platforms. ● Delivers a complete 30-day posting schedule in a structured markdown table with date, platform, content type, and topic columns ● Balances content types to prevent repetition and maintain audience interest across educational, promotional, and engagement-driven posts ● Accounts for platform-specific best practices and optimal posting times based on audience behavior patterns ● Incorporates your business events, product launches, and promotional campaigns into the content flow ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist developing a strategic content calendar.

## Task
Create a one-month content calendar that schedules posts across multiple social media platforms. Analyze the target audience, industry trends, and marketing goals to inform your strategy. Design a diverse mix of content types optimized for each platform's unique characteristics and peak engagement times. Integrate key dates, events, and promotions to support business objectives.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{upcoming-events-promotions}}

{{preferred-platforms}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns: Date | Platform | Content Type | Topic

Include daily posts for one full month across all specified platforms. Ensure balanced distribution and variety in content types (educational, promotional, engagement-focused, user-generated content, behind-the-scenes, etc.). Note optimal posting times where relevant to platform and audience behavior.
```
