# Social Media Thought Leadership Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Thought Leadership Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds complete content strategies for professionals looking to showcase expertise and grow their audience on any platform. This thought leadership prompt for ChatGPT takes your platform, industry, target audience, area of expertise, and content goals, then produces an eight-part strategy covering audience analysis, networking tactics, a balanced content calendar mixing educational and promotional posts, signature content pillars, hashtag and discovery strategies, engagement protocols, performance metrics, and systems for consistent output. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is used by consultants, coaches, agency owners, and subject-matter experts who need a structured approach to building credibility and attracting clients through social content. ● Analyzes target audience preferences, pain points, and content consumption habits specific to the chosen platform ● Designs a content calendar with the right balance of educational, inspirational, and promotional posts plus recommended frequency ● Maps out discovery tactics including hashtag strategies, mention opportunities, and platform-specific visibility features ● Delivers engagement protocols and performance frameworks with metrics to track and refine over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media content strategist focused on establishing thought leadership and attracting potential clients through high-value content.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social media strategy that showcases industry expertise, engages the target audience, and achieves specific content goals on {{platform}}.

## Context
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Area of expertise:** {{area-of-expertise}}
**Content goals:** {{content-goals}}

## Strategy Components
Develop a complete approach covering:

1. **Audience analysis** – preferences, pain points, and content consumption habits on the platform
2. **Networking strategies** – relevant tactics for building industry connections and visibility
3. **Content calendar** – balanced mix of educational, inspirational, and promotional posts with recommended posting frequency
4. **Content pillars** – signature post types that demonstrate deep expertise and deliver actionable insights
5. **Discovery tactics** – hashtag strategy, mention opportunities, and platform-specific visibility features
6. **Engagement protocols** – how to respond to followers and interact with industry peers to expand reach
7. **Performance framework** – key metrics to track and how to use analytics to refine the strategy
8. **Consistency plan** – systems to maintain regular, high-quality output

## Output
Provide your strategy as a bullet point list. Each main point should include 2-3 subpoints that elaborate on specific tactics or implementation steps.
```
