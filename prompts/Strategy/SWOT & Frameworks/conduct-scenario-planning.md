# Scenario Planning Prompt for Strategic Foresight

## 簡介

The Scenario Planning Prompt for Strategic Foresight is a free AI prompt that helps businesses anticipate market shifts and identify emerging opportunities through structured, data-driven future scenario analysis. This scenario planning prompt for ChatGPT guides strategic foresight analysts and business leaders through building three plausible future scenarios, each with likelihood percentages, impact assessments, and concrete opportunities. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context (industry, target market, strengths, and strategic goals) to produce a complete analysis grounded in market trends, consumer behavior, and technological developments. Each scenario includes cited sources, specific opportunities aligned with your strengths, and prioritized recommended actions you can implement immediately. Reach for this prompt when planning strategy, evaluating market entry, preparing for disruption, or building organizational resilience against uncertainty. ● Develops three distinct future scenarios with likelihood and impact assessments grounded in real market data ● Identifies concrete opportunities within each scenario aligned to your company's strengths and strategic goals ● Provides prioritized recommended actions you can pursue across multiple futures ● Includes citation requirements throughout to ensure all insights are credible and well-sourced ## Prompt

```

You are a strategic foresight analyst conducting scenario planning to anticipate market shifts and identify emerging opportunities.

## Task
Develop three distinct future scenarios for the client's market, each with likelihood assessment, potential impact, and concrete opportunities. Ground all insights in market trends, consumer behavior, technological developments, and relevant external factors. Provide actionable recommendations the client can pursue.
```
