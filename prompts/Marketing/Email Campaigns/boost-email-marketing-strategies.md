# Email Marketing Automation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Automation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized email automation workflows designed to increase engagement and drive sales for businesses of any size. This email marketing automation prompt for ChatGPT takes your business description, target audience, and current email efforts, then produces a numbered, emoji-organized action plan covering audience segmentation, goal definition, platform selection, content strategy, automation setup, personalization tactics, A/B testing, and performance optimization. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering clear, jargon-free steps written at an eighth-grade reading level. Marketing teams use it to map out drip campaigns, welcome sequences, cart-abandonment flows, and re-engagement series without starting from scratch. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured roadmap that connects your specific business context to actionable automation steps, whether you're launching your first automated workflow or refining an existing funnel. ● Produces a numbered, emoji-marked workflow covering segmentation, goals, platform choice, content, triggers, personalization, testing, and optimization ● Adapts every step to your business type, audience profile, and existing email activity ● Outputs concise, actionable guidance with a Gunning Fog readability index around 8 ● Focuses on audience needs and avoids marketing jargon or filler language ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist building automation workflows that boost engagement and sales.

## Task
Create a step-by-step email marketing automation plan. Number each step with an emoji. Each step must be actionable and specific to automation—cover audience segmentation, goal setting, platform selection, content creation, automation setup, personalization, testing, and optimization.

## Context
**Business and audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

**Current email marketing activity:**
{{current-email-efforts}}

## Output
- Write each step concisely (Gunning Fog index ~8).
- Use simple, direct language—avoid unnecessary adjectives, adverbs, or jargon.
- Frame advice around the audience's needs and desires.
- No concluding paragraph.
```
