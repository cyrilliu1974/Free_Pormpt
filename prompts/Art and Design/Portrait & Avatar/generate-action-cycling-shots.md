# Dynamic Cycling Action Shot Prompt

## 簡介

The Dynamic Cycling Action Shot Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic sports portraits of cyclists descending trails with dramatic backlighting and motion-focused composition. This cycling action photography prompt for Midjourney produces 16:9 images shot from a low tracking angle with a 70mm lens equivalent, golden-hour backlight illuminating dust clouds, and focused athlete expressions captured at high shutter speeds. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other advanced image models by combining technical camera parameters with environmental storytelling - ideal for sports marketing, editorial content, cycling brand campaigns, and adventure portfolio work. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade action sports imagery that conveys speed, terrain challenge, and athletic intensity without staging a real photo shoot. ● Produces images with golden-hour backlighting that catches airborne dust and creates depth through natural atmospheric effects. ● Uses a low tracking angle and 70mm focal length to emphasize speed and place the viewer in the action. ● Captures focused athlete expressions and body language that convey concentration and effort. ● Flexible variables let you specify any cycling discipline, terrain type, and environmental setting while maintaining consistent photographic quality. ## Prompt

```

```
