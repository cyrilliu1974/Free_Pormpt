# Financial Projections Builder for Business Planning

## 簡介

The Financial Projections Builder for Business Planning is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive multi-year financial forecasts for entrepreneurs, analysts, and finance teams. This financial projections prompt for ChatGPT produces detailed markdown tables with columns for each year and rows for revenue streams, expense categories, profitability metrics, and financial ratios (gross margin, EBITDA margin, net margin, growth rates). The prompt guides the AI to analyze historical data, industry trends, market conditions, and competitive dynamics, then deliver line-by-line explanations of the assumptions and reasoning behind each projection. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for business plan drafting, investor decks, budget planning, and strategic reviews. Reach for this prompt when you need to model financial performance over multiple years, justify assumptions to stakeholders, or explore how different revenue and cost scenarios impact profitability. ● Outputs a markdown table with years as columns and financial metrics as rows, ready for reports and presentations ● Includes explanations for each metric row, documenting assumptions, growth drivers, and risks ● Calculates key financial ratios (gross margin, EBITDA margin, net margin) alongside raw revenue and expense figures ● Considers historical data, industry benchmarks, market conditions, and competitive positioning to ground projections in reality ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial analyst creating comprehensive financial projections.

## Task
Forecast revenue, expenses, and profitability over a specified time period. Present projections in a detailed markdown table with columns for each year and rows for each financial metric. Include a brief explanation for each row clarifying the assumptions and reasoning behind the projections.

## Context
Business and scope: {{business-context}}

Analyze historical data and industry trends, identify key revenue streams and expense categories, consider market conditions and competitive dynamics, and apply appropriate financial modeling techniques. Calculate relevant financial ratios (gross margin, EBITDA margin, net margin, growth rates) and highlight material risks and opportunities that could impact the forecast.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table structured as:
- **Columns**: Each year in the projection period
- **Rows**: Each specified financial metric (revenue lines, expense categories, profitability measures, and ratios)
- **Explanations**: Below the table, provide a concise rationale for each metric row, noting significant assumptions, growth drivers, or expected variations
```
