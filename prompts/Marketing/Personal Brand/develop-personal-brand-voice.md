# Personal Brand Voice Style Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Personal Brand Voice Style Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a detailed brand voice framework for solopreneurs building consistent, authentic communication across all channels. This personal brand voice prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes your solopreneur profile - including industry context, target audience, and unique positioning - to develop a complete style guide covering voice pillars, tone adjustments by context, language patterns, signature phrases, and practical do's and don'ts. The output includes a tone matrix showing how your voice adapts across social media, client proposals, email, and presentations, plus side-by-side examples comparing on-brand and off-brand communication. Solopreneurs building a consultancy, coaching practice, or personal brand use this prompt to document their authentic voice and ensure every touchpoint reflects their expertise and values. ● Defines 3-4 core voice attributes and a brand personality summary grounded in your industry and audience ● Creates a tone matrix showing how your voice shifts across contexts while maintaining consistency ● Provides vocabulary preferences, sentence patterns, and technical depth guidelines tailored to your market ● Delivers practical side-by-side examples showing on-brand versus off-brand communication ● Includes a channel playbook with adaptation notes for email, social media, website copy, presentations, and client communication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in personal brand voice development for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive personal brand voice style guide that ensures consistent, authentic communication across all channels. The guide must include:

- **Voice pillars**: Core attributes that define how the brand sounds (e.g., authoritative yet approachable, technical yet accessible)
- **Tone guidelines**: How voice adapts by context (social media, client proposals, thought leadership, email)
- **Language patterns**: Vocabulary preferences, sentence structure, pronouns, complexity level
- **Key phrases & messaging**: Signature expressions, value propositions, taglines
- **Do's and Don'ts**: Concrete examples showing on-brand vs. off-brand communication
- **Channel-specific applications**: How the voice translates to different platforms while maintaining consistency

## Context
{{solopreneur-profile}}

Analyze the industry landscape, audience expectations, and competitive positioning. Identify the authentic intersection between the solopreneur's natural communication style and what resonates with their target market.

## Output
Deliver the style guide using this structure:

### Brand Voice Overview
- Core voice attributes (3-4 defining characteristics)
- Brand personality summary

### Tone Matrix
Table showing tone adjustments across contexts

### Language Guidelines
- Vocabulary (preferred/avoided terms)
- Sentence patterns and rhythm
- Technical depth and jargon usage

### Signature Elements
- Key phrases that embody the brand
- Unique selling point messaging
- Value-driven statements

### Practical Examples
Side-by-side comparisons: ✓ On-brand | ✗ Off-brand

### Channel Playbook
Brief adaptation notes for email, social media, website, presentations, and client communication.

Format with clear headings, bullet points, and tables for immediate reference and team implementation.
```
