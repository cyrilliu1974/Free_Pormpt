# Cinematic Light Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Light Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces film-grade portrait images with signature blue-gold practical lighting and emotional depth. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney applies the visual language of cinematographer Roger Deakins - soft volumetric beams, subtle haze backlighting, and an 85mm f/1.8 shallow depth of field that isolates your subject with dynamic color contrast. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image generators that support quality and stylization parameters.
