# Gamified Learning Experience Designer

## 簡介

The Gamified Learning Experience Designer is a free AI prompt that builds structured gamification strategies for educational programs, courses, and training environments. This gamified learning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your subject matter, target audience, and learning objectives to produce a four-part strategy: a rewards system with points, badges, and unlockables; task design with difficulty tiers and scaffolding; a progression structure with clear advancement criteria; and an implementation roadmap with success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for course creators, instructional designers, and educators building engagement into online learning, corporate training, or classroom environments. The prompt calibrates difficulty curves to maintain motivation without frustration and maps game mechanics directly to knowledge retention goals. ● Produces a point economy, badge taxonomy, and unlockable content aligned with learning milestones ● Designs challenge types with scaffolding and failure-recovery mechanics to sustain momentum ● Delivers a level-by-level progression structure with clear feedback loops and engagement hooks ● Includes an implementation roadmap with phasing, resource allocation, and measurable success criteria ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert gamification designer specializing in educational systems that drive engagement and knowledge retention.

## Task
Design a complete gamified learning experience that includes:
- A rewards system aligned with learning milestones
- Task challenges calibrated to maintain motivation without frustration
- A progression structure that sustains momentum and encourages mastery

## Context
Subject matter: {{subject}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Learning objectives: {{learning-objectives}}

Constraints: {{implementation-context}}

## Approach
Analyze the subject complexity and audience capabilities to set appropriate difficulty curves. Map learning objectives to game mechanics that reinforce the desired knowledge and skills. Balance extrinsic rewards (points, badges, levels) with intrinsic motivators (autonomy, mastery, progress visibility). Ensure the progression structure provides clear feedback loops and celebrates incremental wins.

## Output
Deliver a structured gamification strategy with these sections:

### Rewards System
- Point economy and earning triggers
- Badge/achievement taxonomy
- Unlockable content or privileges

### Task Design
- Challenge types and difficulty tiers
- Scaffolding approach for skill-building
- Failure recovery mechanics

### Progression Structure
- Level/phase breakdown with learning goals per stage
- Advancement criteria
- Engagement hooks for retention

### Implementation Roadmap
- Phasing and timeline
- Resource allocation
- Success metrics
```
