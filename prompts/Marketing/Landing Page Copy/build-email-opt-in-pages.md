# Email Opt-In Page Copy and Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Email Opt-In Page Copy and Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused opt-in pages designed to turn skeptical visitors into engaged email subscribers. This email opt-in page prompt for ChatGPT produces ready-to-implement copy and strategy across six key areas: multiple headline and subheadline combinations that address visitor pain points, benefit-driven value propositions with credibility indicators, compelling lead magnet descriptions that emphasize transformation, frictionless form design with psychological friction removal, channel-specific promotion tactics tailored to your traffic sources, and a prioritized implementation checklist. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and handles audiences who are banner-blind, skeptical of free offers, and overwhelmed by content. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete opt-in strategy that addresses modern visitor skepticism while clearly communicating the value of your lead magnet. ● Produces 3-4 headline and subheadline combinations that speak directly to audience desires and pain points. ● Writes value proposition copy focused on transformation rather than features, with social proof integration. ● Delivers frictionless form design recommendations including field requirements, visual hierarchy, and call-to-action wording. ● Creates channel-specific promotion strategies for blog integration, social campaigns, webinars, paid ads, and other traffic sources you specify. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert conversion copywriter specializing in email opt-in pages that attract engaged subscribers through value-first positioning and tested strategies.

## Task
Create a complete email opt-in page strategy that converts cold traffic into subscribers. Address audiences who are banner-blind, skeptical of "free" offers, and overwhelmed by content. Design around a compelling lead magnet that solves a specific, urgent problem while building immediate trust.

## Context
**Business and audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

**Lead magnet:**
{{lead-magnet}}

**Traffic sources:**
{{traffic-sources}}

## Output
Provide implementation-ready strategy with these sections, using bullet points for immediate execution:

### Opt-In Page Headline Options
Deliver 3–4 headline and subheadline combinations that speak directly to the audience's pain point or desire.

### Value Proposition Copy
Write benefit-focused copy emphasizing transformation rather than features. Include social proof or credibility indicators.

### Freebie Description
Craft a compelling description of the lead magnet that highlights the specific problem it solves and the outcome it delivers.

### Form Design Recommendations
Outline a frictionless sign-up process: field requirements, visual hierarchy, psychological friction removal, and call-to-action wording.

### Promotion Strategy by Channel
Develop channel-specific tactics for the traffic sources provided: blog integration, social media campaigns, webinar/event funnels, paid ads, or others relevant to the business.

### Implementation Checklist
Provide a prioritized, step-by-step checklist covering page elements in order: headline, value proposition, social proof, opt-in form, call-to-action, and promotion deployment.
```
