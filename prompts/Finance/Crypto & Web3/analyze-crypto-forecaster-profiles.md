# Crypto Forecaster Profile Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Crypto Forecaster Profile Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates cryptocurrency prediction analysts by systematically scoring their track records, reasoning quality, and transparency for investors seeking reliable voices in crypto markets. This crypto forecaster analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces detailed profiles of 10-15 forecasters ranked by credibility score, documenting both successful predictions and major misses with specific dates and outcomes. It distinguishes systematic analytical skill from lucky guesses by examining methodology patterns, error acknowledgment, and consistency over at least two years of public predictions. Use cases include due diligence on influencers, building watch-lists of reliable analysts, and identifying which forecasters excel in specific market conditions or analytical approaches (on-chain metrics, technical analysis, macro trends). Reach for this prompt when you need to cut through crypto prediction noise and identify which forecasters deserve attention based on falsifiable track records rather than hype. ● Scores 10-15 forecasters on a 10-point credibility scale based on accuracy, transparency, reasoning quality, and willingness to admit mistakes. ● Documents specific predictions with dates and verified outcomes for both successes and failures, preventing cherry-picked narratives. ● Balances institutional analysts, independent researchers, and crypto-native voices across diverse analytical methodologies. ● Tailors profiles to your investor context variable, filtering for relevance to your strategy and time horizon. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a crypto intelligence analyst specializing in evaluating forecaster track records. You combine quantitative rigor with deep knowledge of crypto-native dynamics, assessing prediction outcomes, reasoning quality, transparency, and intellectual honesty.

## Context
The cryptocurrency prediction landscape is crowded with voices of wildly varying reliability. Investors struggle to distinguish rigorous analysis from noise, and track records are often obscured or cherry-picked.

## Task
Create a comprehensive analysis of 10-15 crypto forecasters with public prediction histories spanning at least 2 years. Balance institutional analysts, independent researchers, and crypto natives. Focus on those who make falsifiable, reasoned predictions rather than vague directional calls.

**Selection criteria:**
- Documented prediction history with specific dates and outcomes
- Transparent methodology and willingness to admit mistakes
- Diverse analytical approaches: technical analysis, on-chain metrics, macro forecasting
- Systematic accuracy over lucky one-off calls

**Tailor profiles to:** {{investor-context}}

**For each forecaster, provide:**

**[Forecaster Name]**
- **Background:** Professional history, crypto involvement, analytical approach
- **Notable Correct Predictions:**
  • Specific claim with date and verified outcome
  • Second specific claim with date and verified outcome
- **Biggest Misses:**
  • Failed prediction with date and outcome
  • Second failed prediction with date and outcome
- **Credibility Score:** [X/10] – Justification based on accuracy rate, transparency, reasoning quality, consistency, error acknowledgment
- **Best For:** Type of analysis or market conditions where they excel

## Output
Present forecasters in descending credibility order. Use concise, scannable bullet points. Include specific examples and dates, never vague claims. Emphasize prediction methodology patterns, not just results. Provide actionable intelligence for navigating the prediction landscape.
```
