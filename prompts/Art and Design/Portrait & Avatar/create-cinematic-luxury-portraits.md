# Cinematic Luxury Car Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Luxury Car Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates sophisticated night photography portraits staged in opulent vehicle interiors for brands, marketers, and creative professionals. This luxury portrait prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces high-end lifestyle imagery featuring subjects seated in the back of premium cars, framed by soft leather, polished wood trim, and brushed metal accents. The prompt specifies elegant poses, dim cinematic lighting from ambient LED strips and instrument panels, and a soft-focus background that delivers the polished aesthetic of expensive automotive and fashion campaigns. Real-world use cases include premium brand lookbooks, automotive marketing visuals, lifestyle editorial content, and social media assets that communicate exclusivity and sophistication. This prompt is ideal when you need aspirational lifestyle photography without the logistics and budget of a traditional studio shoot. ● Produces 1200x1200px images with cinematic lighting, ambient LED glow, and soft focus on background details for authentic luxury aesthetics. ● Allows customization of subject description, pose, and styling to match brand identity and campaign goals. ● Delivers the visual language of high-end automotive and fashion photography with contrast stitching, wood trim, and metal accent details. ● Creates composed, elegant atmospheres that communicate premium quality and exclusivity to upscale audiences. ## Prompt
