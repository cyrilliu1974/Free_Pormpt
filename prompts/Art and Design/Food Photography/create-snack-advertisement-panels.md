# Snack Advertisement Grid Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Snack Advertisement Grid Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a bold 3×2 panel layout of modern food photography for packaged snack brands and marketing teams. This snack advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces six coordinated panels in a 4:5 portrait grid: a hero product shot, macro chip texture close-up, high-speed floating crunch moment, casual snack-time lifestyle scene, fresh ingredient detail, and a bold graphic finish panel. Each frame uses bright studio lighting, vibrant color blocking, and professional food photography techniques to create appetizing, modern visuals ready for social media, packaging mockups, or pitch decks. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive multi-panel snack ad campaign that balances product beauty shots with dynamic action and relatable lifestyle context. ● Six distinct panel compositions (hero bag, macro texture, floating action, lifestyle scene, ingredient detail, bold graphic) in a single 3×2 grid ● Bright studio food photography aesthetic with diffused lighting, clean shadows, and vibrant color-blocking backgrounds ● Customizable product name and color palette variables to match any snack brand identity ● 4:5 portrait aspect ratio optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, and mobile-first social advertising ## Prompt

```
Create a visually bold 3×2 grid advertisement in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio with six equal panels showcasing a packaged snack with modern, high-quality food photography.

**Product:** {{product-name}} classic salted crispy chips in matte colorful resealable packaging with golden crisp texture.

**Overall Style:** Professional studio food photography with bright diffused lighting, clean soft shadows, vibrant color blocking, minimal graphic composition. Modern snack photography aesthetic—playful, crisp, energetic, appetizing.

**Background & Environment:** Modern graphic studio setup with matte pastel or lemon yellow backdrops. Accent with floating chips frozen mid-air, subtle salt crystals scattered, minimal geometric shapes.

**Six Panels:**

1. **Hero Bag Shot** — Product bag standing upright centered on vibrant background, straight-on camera angle, bold and clean.

2. **Chip Texture Close-Up** — Macro shot of golden crispy chip texture with ultra-shallow depth of field for texture clarity, crunchy and appetizing.

3. **Floating Crunch Moment** — Chips suspended mid-air as if tossed, high-speed capture lighting, dynamic and fun.

4. **Snack Time Scene** — Adult casually grabbing chips from bowl in stylish living room, soft natural indoor light, relaxed everyday enjoyment.

5. **Ingredient Detail** — Fresh potato slices and salt crystals styled minimally, simple and authentic.

6. **Bold Graphic Finish** — Bag centered with graphic color-block background, clean studio lighting, modern and eye-catching.

**Brand Personality:** Fun, modern, energetic with bold rounded sans-serif typography and {{color-palette}}.

Crisp balanced exposure, high realism, bright and fresh mood.

Avoid: stale chips, greasy appearance, wrinkled packaging, cluttered composition, harsh shadows, cartoon style, low-resolution textures, watermarks, text overlays.
```
