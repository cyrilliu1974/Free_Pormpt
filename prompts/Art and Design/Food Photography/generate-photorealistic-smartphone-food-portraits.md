# Photorealistic Smartphone Food Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Smartphone Food Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic iPhone-style dining photographs with direct flash lighting for restaurants, food brands, and lifestyle marketers. This smartphone food photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana simulates the exact look of raw iPhone 16 Pro Max captures taken at night with on-camera flash: high-contrast lighting with rapid falloff, sharp foreground detail on both subject and food, warm bokeh from string lights in the background, and the natural depth-of-field blur of a wide smartphone aperture. It produces medium-shot portraits of a diner enjoying dishes like sizzling bone marrow, fried rice with egg, and iced milk tea in semi-outdoor night market or street food settings, complete with checkered tablecloths and tent canopies. Ideal for restaurants creating relatable social media content, food delivery platforms needing authentic hero images, and brands that want the spontaneous feel of real smartphone photography without a shoot. ● Simulates iPhone 16 Pro Max camera characteristics including f/1.8 aperture effect, sharp focus plane, and natural depth-of-field blur. ● Customizable subject demographics, outfit details, and venue types from night markets to street food tents. ● Produces direct flash lighting with high contrast, cool foreground illumination, warm ambient background bokeh, and defined shadows. ● Includes detailed food props like sizzling cast iron dishes, fried rice with egg, and Southeast Asian street food staples. ## Prompt

```
Generate a photorealistic smartphone photograph simulating a raw night-time capture with on-camera flash.

**Subject:** {{subject-description}}. Long, voluminous dark wavy hair styled with a center part and polished finish. {{outfit-description}} — fabric shows specular highlights typical under flash. Thick gold hoop earrings, layered gold necklaces including a textured chain and pendant. Seated at a table facing camera directly, right hand holding a clear plastic cup with a drink, left hand resting on the table holding a smartphone. Soft, confident smile with direct eye contact.

**Setting:** Semi-outdoor dining tent at {{venue-type}}. White canopy tent structure visible above. Background is dark with bokeh circles from warm string lights and blurred silhouettes of a crowd. Table covered with classic red and white checkered tablecloth.

**Props:** Sizzling cast iron plate containing a large roasted bone marrow or rib topped with crispy fried garlic and oil. Paper bowl with white rice topped with sunny-side-up fried egg and a calamansi lime. Iced milk tea in a clear plastic disposable cup with lid and black straw.

**Lighting:** Direct on-camera smartphone flash. High contrast with rapid falloff. Subject and foreground food brightly illuminated with cool/neutral flash light, background drops into darkness with warm ambient tungsten tones. Sharp, defined shadows behind subject and objects, typical of a small direct light source.

**Camera:** Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max aesthetic. Medium shot, eye-level. Wide aperture (f/1.8 equivalent) effect with sharp focus on subject and foreground food, slight depth of field blurring the background. High definition, sharp details, vibrant colors, slightly glossy finish.
```
