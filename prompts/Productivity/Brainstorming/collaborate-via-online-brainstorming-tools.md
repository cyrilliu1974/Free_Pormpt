# Online Brainstorming Facilitator Prompt for Projects

## 簡介

The Online Brainstorming Facilitator Prompt for Projects is a free AI prompt that generates and evaluates innovative project ideas in a structured table format for teams and project managers. This brainstorming prompt for ChatGPT works by having the AI assume the role of an expert project manager who produces 8-12 distinct ideas tailored to your specific project and collaboration tools. Each idea is presented in a markdown table with three columns: the concept itself, 2-4 key benefits (pros), and 2-4 potential challenges (cons). Ideas are automatically ranked from highest to lowest potential impact, making it easy to identify the most promising directions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your named project and the collaboration tools your team already uses. Real use cases include kicking off new initiatives, exploring alternative approaches when projects stall, and facilitating remote team ideation sessions where a neutral structure keeps discussion focused. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond unstructured idea lists and want a balanced assessment of benefits and trade-offs before committing resources. ● Produces 8-12 evaluated ideas per session, each with concrete benefits and challenges listed ● Automatically ranks ideas by potential impact so high-value concepts surface first ● Accepts any project name and collaboration-tool stack, tailoring suggestions to your context ● Outputs clean markdown tables that paste directly into Notion, Confluence, or meeting notes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert project manager facilitating a collaborative brainstorming session.

## Task
Generate innovative ideas for the specified project and evaluate each idea's potential benefits and challenges. Guide creative exploration while maintaining structured analysis to support decision-making.

## Context
Project: {{project-name}}

Collaboration tools available: {{collaboration-tools}}

Consider how these tools can enable idea generation, team coordination, and implementation. Focus on ideas that are actionable within the constraints and opportunities of the project context.

## Output
Present your brainstormed ideas in a markdown table with three columns:

| Idea | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|

Generate 8-12 distinct ideas. For each:
- **Idea**: Clear, concise description of the concept
- **Pros**: 2-4 key benefits or advantages
- **Cons**: 2-4 potential challenges or drawbacks

Organize ideas from highest to lowest potential impact.
```
