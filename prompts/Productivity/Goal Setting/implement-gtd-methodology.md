# GTD Implementation Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The GTD Implementation Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates personalized Getting Things Done frameworks for independent business owners working without teams. This GTD methodology prompt for ChatGPT translates the five core principles - capture, clarify, organize, reflect, and engage - into concrete steps tailored to solo work environments. It accounts for limited delegation, multiple role-switching, and the tension between strategic thinking and daily operations. When you provide your solopreneur context, the prompt builds a structured guide with implementation steps, profession-specific examples, and integration points for your existing task management tools. Real use cases include consultants managing client work alongside business development, content creators balancing production with administration, and service providers handling operations without support staff. The output works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from reactive firefighting to systematic productivity as a solopreneur, or when generic GTD advice fails to address the realities of working independently. ● Adapts all five GTD principles to solo contexts where delegation is limited or impossible ● Provides profession-specific examples rather than generic office scenarios ● Includes quick wins for immediate productivity gains alongside long-term system building ● Integrates with existing tools instead of prescribing a complete workflow overhaul ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in task management systems for independent business owners.

## Task
Create a tailored implementation guide that translates Getting Things Done (GTD) principles into actionable steps for a solopreneur. Analyze the core GTD concepts—capture, clarify, organize, reflect, and engage—and adapt them to address the unique challenges of working independently without a team.

## Context
Solopreneur context: {{solopreneur-context}}

Provide specific adaptations that account for:
- Limited delegation options and the need to wear multiple hats
- Balancing strategic and operational work
- Managing interruptions and context-switching
- The mentioned productivity challenge and current organizational baseline

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive, actionable guide structured as:
- **Main GTD principles** as bold headings (Capture, Clarify, Organize, Reflect, Engage)
- Implementation steps as sub-bullets under each principle
- Practical examples tied to the solopreneur's profession and work environment
- Integration points with their preferred task management tools

Each point must be clear, concise, and immediately applicable. Prioritize quick wins alongside systemic improvements.
```
