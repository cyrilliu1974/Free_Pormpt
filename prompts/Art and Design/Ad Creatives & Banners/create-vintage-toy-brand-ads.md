# Vintage Toy Brand Ad Image Generator

## 簡介

The Vintage Toy Brand Ad Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic 1970s magazine print advertisements for toy building brick brands. This vintage toy ad prompt for Midjourney produces images with warm amber and burnt orange 70s color palettes, vintage lens softness, flash photo glare, motion blur, chromatic aberration, and film grain distortion that replicate the look of period product photography. You customize two variables - the subject (typically a minifigure character) and the brick landscape setting - and the prompt renders a high-detail nostalgic scene in 3:2 aspect ratio. Designers creating retro brand campaigns, collectors building vintage-style catalogs, and hobbyists celebrating classic toy aesthetics will find this prompt ready to use on Midjourney, Flux, or Nano Banana. ● Renders warm 70s color tones (amber, burnt orange) with vintage lens characteristics including softness and flash glare. ● Applies motion blur, chromatic aberration, and film grain distortion for authentic period photography effects. ● Outputs high-detail product photography style images in 3:2 magazine print format. ● Accepts custom subject and brick-landscape variables to tailor each advertisement scene. ## Prompt

```
1970s magazine print advertisement for toy building bricks: {{subject}} flying a hang glider over {{brick-landscape}}, warm amber and burnt orange 70s tones, vintage lens softness with flash photo glare, motion blur on the wings, chromatic aberration and film grain distortion, high-detail product photography style --ar 3:2
```
