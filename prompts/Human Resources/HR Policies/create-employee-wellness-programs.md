# Employee Wellness Program Builder for Physical Activity

## 簡介

The Employee Wellness Program Builder for Physical Activity is a free AI prompt that creates structured workplace wellness initiatives tailored to diverse employee needs and organizational constraints. This corporate wellness prompt for ChatGPT takes a company profile and generates a complete program framework: three distinct employee personas with individualized activity recommendations, phased implementation timelines, measurable KPIs, and support resources that fit into existing work schedules without disrupting productivity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for HR professionals, wellness coordinators, and leadership teams planning physical activity initiatives that address real barriers like sedentary job roles, time constraints, and varying fitness levels. ● Generates three employee personas with job roles, current activity levels, primary barriers, and tailored exercise recommendations ● Delivers a phased rollout plan with specific timelines and activities for pilot, expansion, and sustainment stages ● Provides measurable success metrics and support resources to maintain long-term engagement ● Adapts recommendations to different work environments, schedules, and organizational cultures ## Prompt
