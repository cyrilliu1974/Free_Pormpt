# Healthy School Lunch Meal Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Healthy School Lunch Meal Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates nutritionally balanced 5-day lunch plans tailored to primary school children's dietary needs and preparation realities. This school lunch planner prompt for ChatGPT generates five distinct daily menus that combine protein, whole grains, fruits, vegetables, and healthy fats while accommodating allergies, preferences, and time constraints. Each plan includes portion guidelines for ages 5-7 and 8-11, preparation instructions matched to your schedule (morning prep, night before, or weekend batch cooking), and a categorized shopping list with quantities. Parents packing daily lunches, school nutrition coordinators designing cafeteria programs, and caregivers managing dietary restrictions use it to plan meals that satisfy nutritional standards without repetitive ingredients. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this tool when you need structured meal planning that balances nutrition science with the practical constraints of budget, time, and children's taste preferences. ● Creates five non-repetitive daily menus with colorful, kid-friendly presentations and familiar flavors enhanced for nutrition. ● Provides portion sizes calibrated to two age brackets (5-7 and 8-11 years) to match developmental needs. ● Generates a shopping list organized by grocery store section with exact quantities and substitution notes for common allergies and dietary restrictions. ● Adapts meal complexity to your available prep time and budget level, ensuring practical execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a pediatric nutritionist and meal planning specialist designing school lunch programs that meet nutritional guidelines while appealing to children's tastes.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 5-day healthy lunch plan for primary school children. Each day must include a balanced combination of protein, whole grains, fruits, vegetables, and healthy fats. The meals should feature colorful presentations, familiar flavors with nutritional upgrades, and accommodate common preparation and storage constraints for school settings.

## Context
Work with these parameters:
- {{child-details}}: age range, food preferences, dislikes, allergies, intolerances, or special dietary needs
- {{prep-constraints}}: available meal preparation time (morning of, night before, or weekend batch prep) and budget preference (budget-conscious, moderate, or flexible)

Design five distinct lunch options that avoid repetitive ingredients while maintaining nutritional balance. Include portion guidelines for ages 5-7 and 8-11. Provide preparation tips that work within the stated time constraints. Consider seasonal availability and offer budget-appropriate ingredient choices. Include simple substitution options for common dietary restrictions.

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Day 1 through Day 5**: For each day, provide the complete lunch menu with age-appropriate portion sizes, brief preparation instructions, and presentation tips that increase appeal.

**Shopping List**: Organize by grocery store section (produce, dairy, protein, pantry, etc.) with specific quantities needed for all five lunches. Include substitution notes where relevant.
```
