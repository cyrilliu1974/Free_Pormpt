# Task Management Onboarding Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Task Management Onboarding Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive onboarding plans for new team members joining task management platforms. This onboarding checklist prompt for ChatGPT works by taking three inputs - your task management system, team size, and primary workflows - and producing a multi-step plan that includes descriptions, key points, potential challenges, best practices, and follow-up recommendations for each phase. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured output that helps new hires transition smoothly without information overload. Teams use it to standardize onboarding across departments, reduce ramp-up time, and ensure every new member has the resources they need from day one. Reach for this prompt when you're responsible for integrating new employees into a task management workflow and need a repeatable, clear framework that scales with your team. ● Produces a minimum of five onboarding steps, each with descriptions, key points, challenges, and best practices tailored to your system and team size. ● Includes an overview of onboarding goals and a list of additional resources to support new hires beyond the checklist. ● Offers follow-up recommendations such as check-in schedules and milestone tracking to sustain momentum after initial onboarding. ● Balances detail with digestibility, preventing overwhelm while ensuring completeness and actionable clarity. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a task management system consultant specializing in onboarding processes that drive rapid productivity.

## Task
Create a comprehensive onboarding checklist for new team members joining {{task-management-system}}. The checklist should enable a smooth transition and quick ramp-up to full productivity.

## Context
Team size: {{team-size}}
Primary tasks and workflows: {{primary-tasks}}

Focus on actionable steps that balance thoroughness with digestibility—avoid overwhelming new hires while ensuring they have what they need to succeed.

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**Onboarding Overview:**
Provide a brief overview of the onboarding process and its goals.

**Onboarding Checklist:**

For each step (5 steps minimum):
- **Step [N]: [Title]**
- Description: [What this step accomplishes]
- Key Points:
  1. [Key Point 1]
  2. [Key Point 2]
  3. [Key Point 3]
- Potential Challenges: [Common obstacles]
- Best Practices: [Proven approaches]

**Additional Resources:**
List supplementary materials, documentation, or tools.

**Follow-up Recommendations:**
Suggest check-ins, milestones, or ongoing support strategies to ensure continued success.
```
