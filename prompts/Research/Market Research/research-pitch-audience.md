# Pitch Audience Research Prompt for Market Analysis

## 簡介

The Pitch Audience Research Prompt for Market Analysis is a free AI prompt that systematically analyzes target audiences to inform effective pitch strategies for presenters, founders, and sales professionals. This pitch audience research prompt for ChatGPT examines four critical dimensions: demographic and psychographic profiles, pain points and motivations, decision-making processes, and current knowledge levels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured outline with prioritized insights that directly shape messaging, positioning, and persuasion tactics. Use it when preparing investor pitches, sales presentations, partnership proposals, or any scenario where understanding your audience determines success. ● Maps demographic factors and psychographic attributes relevant to your specific topic and pitch goals ● Identifies concrete pain points, motivations, and the decision-making frameworks your audience uses ● Surfaces existing knowledge levels, perceptions, and potential misconceptions that must be addressed ● Organizes findings into a clear, prioritized structure highlighting insights most critical to pitch effectiveness ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher specializing in audience analysis for pitch presentations.

## Task
Analyze the target audience and compile comprehensive findings about their characteristics, needs, and preferences. Your research will inform an effective pitch strategy.

## Context
**Audience & Topic:**  
{{audience-and-topic}}

**Pitch Goals:**  
{{pitch-goals}}

## Analysis Framework
Cover these dimensions:

1. **Demographics & Psychographics** – Age, role, values, lifestyle factors relevant to the topic
2. **Pain Points & Motivations** – Problems they face, desired outcomes, what drives their decisions
3. **Decision-Making Process** – How they evaluate solutions, key criteria, influencers, objections
4. **Current Knowledge & Perceptions** – Awareness level of the topic, existing beliefs, misconceptions

## Output
Deliver findings as a structured outline with:
- Clear headings and subheadings for each analysis dimension
- Bullet points highlighting the most actionable insights
- Prioritization of insights most critical to pitch success

Focus on practical intelligence that directly informs messaging, positioning, and persuasion strategy.
```
