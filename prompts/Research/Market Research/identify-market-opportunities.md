# Market Opportunity Identification Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Opportunity Identification Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive opportunity assessments for businesses seeking growth and competitive advantage. This market research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business profile against the current market landscape, mapping industry trends, consumer behavior patterns, and competitive positioning to surface high-potential segments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, evaluating each market segment for growth and profitability potential, then matching opportunities to your existing strengths and capabilities. The output is a structured markdown table with three columns - Market Segment, Opportunity, and Potential Strategy - delivering at least five detailed rows of actionable insights with resource allocation considerations and expected timelines. Use it when entering new markets, planning expansion, or reassessing your competitive position. ● Maps industry trends, consumer behavior, and competitive dynamics to reveal untapped segments ● Evaluates growth and profitability potential across multiple market segments simultaneously ● Matches identified opportunities to your business's existing strengths and operational capabilities ● Delivers a structured table format with specific strategies, resource needs, and expected outcomes for each opportunity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst conducting a comprehensive opportunity assessment.

## Task
Identify and analyze potential market opportunities, then present findings in a structured format showing which segments offer the best growth potential and how to pursue them.

## Context
Business and market profile:
{{business-profile}}

Perform your analysis by:
1. Mapping the current market landscape: industry trends, consumer behavior patterns, and competitive positioning
2. Identifying key market segments and evaluating their growth and profitability potential
3. Matching opportunities to the business's existing strengths and capabilities
4. Proposing actionable strategies with consideration for resource allocation, timeline, and expected outcomes

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: **Market Segment** | **Opportunity** | **Potential Strategy**

Provide at least five rows with detailed, specific insights for each segment-opportunity-strategy combination.
```
