# Black-and-White Coloring Page Generator

## 簡介

The Black-and-White Coloring Page Generator is a free AI prompt that creates printable line-art illustrations with bold outlines and plain white backgrounds for educators, parents, and designers. This coloring page prompt for Midjourney produces vector-style artwork with customizable subjects, actions, and complexity levels - ideal for activity books, classroom handouts, therapy materials, or personal creative projects. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering clean black outlines on white with no shading, gradients, or background clutter. Reach for this prompt whenever you need print-ready coloring sheets tailored to toddlers, kids, teens, or adults without the hassle of hand-drawing or hiring an illustrator. ● Specify age group, subject, and action to generate on-theme illustrations - from simple animals for toddlers to intricate mandalas for adults. ● Control complexity level to match fine-motor skills and attention span, ensuring appropriate line density and shape detail. ● Produces pure line art with bold outlines and a plain white background, eliminating color fills, shading, textures, and photorealistic noise. ● Ready to print or import into design software for coloring books, worksheets, party activities, and therapeutic use. ## Prompt

```
Black-and-white line-art coloring page for {{age-group}}; {{subject-and-action}} with bold outlines, {{complexity-level}} shapes, plain white background, simple clear style --no color, shading, gray fill, textures, background clutter, gradients, shadows, photorealism
```
