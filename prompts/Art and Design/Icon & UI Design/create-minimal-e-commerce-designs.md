# Minimal E-Commerce Landing Page Design

## 簡介

The Minimal E-Commerce Landing Page Design is a free AI prompt that generates clean, modern online store interfaces with professional product layouts and conversion-focused elements. This minimal e-commerce design prompt for Midjourney produces landing pages built around a crisp white background, oversized sans-serif headlines, and simple grid layouts that display multiple products with subtle drop shadows and interactive hover states. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to create high-contrast designs with generous whitespace, professional commercial photography styling, and a prominent "Shop Now" call-to-action button in a 16:9 responsive format. Use it when building mockups for fashion stores, product launches, or any retail brand that needs a contemporary, distraction-free shopping experience. ● Oversized sans-serif typography and bold "Shop Now" buttons that guide visitors toward purchase decisions ● Simple grid layout displaying multiple products with subtle shadows and hover state effects for interactivity ● Professional commercial photography style with high contrast, generous whitespace, and responsive 16:9 aspect ratio ● Customizable product focus variable lets you tailor the design to any category, from apparel to electronics ## Prompt
