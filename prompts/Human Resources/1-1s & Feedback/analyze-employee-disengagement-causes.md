# Analyze Employee Disengagement Causes

## 簡介

The Analyze Employee Disengagement Causes prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies leadership failures, cultural breakdowns, and systemic workflow issues driving employee turnover for managers, HR professionals, and organizational leaders. This employee disengagement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok surfaces 10 root causes of attrition that leadership directly controls, focusing on structural dysfunction rather than compensation or external market forces. For each cause, it explains the psychological mechanism behind disengagement, describes how it manifests in daily workplace behaviors, and outlines downstream effects on motivation, performance, and retention. Use it during retention crises, after exit interview patterns emerge, or when preparing leadership development initiatives. ● Identifies 10 high-impact causes of disengagement that leadership teams commonly overlook, prioritized by their influence on departure decisions. ● Explains the psychological mechanisms that turn leadership behaviors into disengagement, linking structural issues to employee motivation loss. ● Provides concrete workplace manifestations for each cause, such as meeting patterns, decision-making breakdowns, and communication failures. ● Delivers downstream effect analysis showing how each dysfunction cascades through performance, morale, and attrition rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in leadership dysfunction, workplace culture, and employee retention. Analyze the root causes of disengagement and turnover that stem from leadership failures, cultural breakdowns, and systemic workflow issues.

## Task
Identify 10 specific causes of employee disengagement and turnover within the organization described below. Focus on root causes that leadership directly controls or influences—not external market factors or compensation.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

Prioritize causes that:
- Have the highest impact on departure decisions
- Are commonly overlooked by leadership teams
- Represent structural issues rather than surface-level complaints
- Are actionable for leadership to address

## Output
For each of the 10 causes, provide:
1. **Clear description** of the leadership or systemic failure
2. **Psychological mechanism** that creates the disengagement
3. **Workplace manifestations** – specific examples of behaviors and decision-making patterns
4. **Downstream effects** on employee motivation, performance, and retention

Format as a numbered list with each cause fully explained.
```
