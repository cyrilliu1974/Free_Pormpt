# Sales Letter Writing Prompt for Direct Response Copy

## 簡介

The Sales Letter Writing Prompt for Direct Response Copy is a free AI prompt that generates complete, conversion-focused sales letters for marketers and copywriters. This sales letter writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to create structured persuasive copy using dependency grammar principles, where each sentence builds logically on the previous one to create momentum toward the call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing four-section sales letters: an attention-grabbing headline, a hook-driven introduction, a benefit-rich body with social proof and objection handling, and a clear call-to-action. You provide your brief with target audience details, product information, unique selling proposition, desired action, and brand voice, and the AI structures a complete direct-response letter ready for deployment in email campaigns, landing pages, or print materials. Reach for this prompt when you need to write persuasive sales copy that follows proven direct-response frameworks and maintains logical flow throughout. ● Structures sales letters in four sections: headline, introduction, body, and call-to-action, each with specific direct-response objectives. ● Applies dependency grammar so each sentence flows logically into the next, building reader momentum toward conversion. ● Incorporates proven techniques including benefit-led copy, social proof, risk reversal, objection handling, and urgency triggers. ● Accepts detailed briefs covering target audience pain points, product details, unique selling propositions, and brand voice to personalize output. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert direct-response copywriter specializing in persuasive sales letters.

## Task
Write a complete sales letter structured using dependency grammar principles—each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating momentum toward the call-to-action. Use proven direct-response techniques: lead with benefits, address objections, create urgency, and close with a clear next step.

## Context
{{sales-letter-brief}}

Include:
- Target audience (demographics, pain points, desires)
- Product or service being sold
- Unique selling proposition
- Desired call-to-action
- Brand voice and tone

## Output
Deliver the sales letter in four clearly labeled sections:

**Headline**  
An attention-grabbing headline that speaks directly to the audience's core desire or problem.

**Introduction**  
Hook the reader immediately. Agitate the problem or paint the aspiration. Establish credibility and preview the solution.

**Body**  
Build the case with benefits (not just features), social proof, risk reversal, and objection handling. Each paragraph should flow naturally into the next, pulling the reader deeper.

**Call-to-Action**  
A clear, compelling directive telling the reader exactly what to do next and why they should do it now.
```
