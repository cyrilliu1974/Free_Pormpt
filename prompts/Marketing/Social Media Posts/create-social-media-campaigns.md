# Obama-Style Social Media Campaign Generator

## 簡介

The Obama-Style Social Media Campaign Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic social media content in Barack Obama's communication style for political campaigns, advocacy groups, and public figures. This social media campaign prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces five distinct posts tailored to platforms like Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Each post includes platform-optimized text, strategic hashtags, and media recommendations (images, videos, or links with preview text). The prompt guides the AI to capture Obama's trademark tone - inspirational, policy-grounded, accessible, and unifying - while avoiding divisive language. Political strategists use it to draft cohesive campaign messaging; nonprofit communicators rely on it to craft uplifting advocacy content; and communications teams adapt it to maintain a consistent, hope-centered voice across channels. ● Produces 5 complete posts with platform assignments, text, hashtags, and media descriptions ● Mirrors Obama's communication style: hopeful, accessible, and grounded in policy ● Varies content format across image posts, video posts, and link shares for engagement ● Ensures unity-focused messaging that resonates with diverse audiences ## Prompt
