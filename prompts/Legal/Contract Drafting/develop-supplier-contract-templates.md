# Supplier Contract Template Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Supplier Contract Template Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, customizable contract outlines for businesses managing vendor relationships across different regions and industries. This supplier contract prompt for ChatGPT helps procurement teams, legal departments, and business owners draft agreements that address pricing mechanisms with inflation protection, shipping and logistics terms, return policies, and quality assurance standards with measurable metrics. The prompt analyzes regional legal frameworks specific to your supplier's location and builds a hierarchical template with editable placeholders, alternative clause options, and optional enhancements like exclusivity arrangements, volume-based discount tiers, and performance incentives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for supplier region, sourcing details, and your preferred risk tolerance level. Use this prompt when you need to standardize procurement agreements, enter new supplier relationships, or adapt contracts to different legal jurisdictions without starting from scratch each time. ● Produces hierarchical contract outlines with clear section headings, editable placeholders in brackets, and alternative clauses based on relationship type ● Addresses vendor failure protection, price volatility safeguards, and quality control with measurable metrics specific to your industry ● Incorporates regional legal frameworks and common business practices for the supplier's geographic location ● Includes both essential terms and optional enhancement clauses you can activate based on negotiation outcomes and strategic priorities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert contract attorney and procurement specialist with deep expertise in international trade law, supply chain risk management, and commercial negotiations.

## Task
Create a comprehensive supplier contract template outline that balances legal protection with practical business needs. The template must be structured, editable, and adaptable to diverse supplier relationships and regional legal requirements.

## Context
The contract must address:
- Regional legal frameworks and common business practices specific to {{supplier-region}}
- Protection against vendor failures, price volatility, and quality issues
- {{sourcing-details}} (what you're purchasing, typical order volumes/frequency, and business type/industry)
- {{risk-tolerance}} (conservative, moderate, or flexible approach to contract terms)

Cover essential elements including pricing mechanisms with inflation protection, comprehensive shipping and logistics terms, detailed return and refund policies, and robust quality assurance standards with measurable metrics. Include optional enhancement clauses for exclusivity arrangements, volume-based discount tiers, and performance incentives.

## Output
Structure the contract template as a hierarchical outline with:
- Clear section headings and subsections
- Editable placeholder text in [brackets] for easy customization
- Alternative clause options based on relationship type and risk profile
- Main sections organized logically from foundational terms through optional enhancements
```
