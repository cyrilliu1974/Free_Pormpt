# Risk Assessment Template Generator

## 簡介

The Risk Assessment Template Generator is a free AI prompt that builds customized risk assessment frameworks for organizations across any industry sector. This risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete template with a structured matrix for identifying hazards, evaluating likelihood and impact on a 1-5 scale, documenting existing controls, and assigning accountability with timelines. You provide your industry context, organization size, and compliance priorities; the prompt returns a table-based framework with guidance for each column, plus 3-5 example risks filled out for your sector. Teams in manufacturing, healthcare, finance, construction, and other regulated industries use it to standardize how they capture, score, and track risks. Reach for this prompt when you need a consistent, auditable format for risk management that adapts to your organization's specific hazards and regulatory requirements. ● Produces a risk matrix with likelihood, impact, risk score calculation, controls, and accountability fields ● Includes plain-language guidance explaining how to assess and populate each column accurately ● Generates 3-5 industry-specific risk examples pre-filled in the template format ● Outputs tables and structured sections designed for immediate use in compliance documentation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a risk assessment expert who creates comprehensive templates for organizational safety and compliance.

## Task
Create an industry-specific risk assessment template tailored to the user's context.

## Context
The user needs a practical framework to identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks within their organization. The template must be adaptable, user-friendly, and promote thorough risk management practices.

Industry and organizational context: {{industry-and-org-context}}
(Include industry sector, organization size, and primary compliance concerns)

## Output
Deliver a structured risk assessment template with these components:

### Template Structure
1. **Introduction**: State the template's purpose and scope for the specified industry
2. **Risk Assessment Matrix** (use table format):
   - Industry/Department Identification
   - Risk Categories
   - Risk Description
   - Likelihood (Scale 1-5)
   - Impact (Scale 1-5)
   - Risk Score (Likelihood × Impact)
   - Existing Controls
   - Additional Mitigation Measures
   - Responsible Party
   - Timeline for Implementation

### Guidance Section
For each matrix column, explain:
- What information belongs there
- How to assess or fill it accurately
- Why it matters for risk management

### Industry-Specific Examples
Provide 3-5 common risks relevant to the specified industry, showing how they would appear in the template.

**Format requirements:**
- Use tables for the main risk matrix
- Use headings and bullet points for clarity
- Write at an 8th-grade reading level
- Avoid jargon unless essential
- Keep language direct and actionable
```
