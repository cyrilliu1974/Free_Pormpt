# Brand Story Angle Generator for Marketing Campaigns

## 簡介

The Brand Story Angle Generator for Marketing Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates compelling narrative frameworks for brand storytelling and campaign development. This brand story angle prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand profile to produce 3-5 distinct story angles, each structured with dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and coherence. For every angle, it defines the core narrative hook, identifies the target emotion or aspiration, explains how your unique value proposition is woven into the story, connects it to marketing objectives, and suggests execution touchpoints across channels and formats. Marketing strategists use it to move beyond feature lists into narratives that address audience pain points and aspirations, whether launching a new product, repositioning an existing brand, or refreshing campaign messaging. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces 3-5 unique story angles, each with a defined narrative hook and emotional target ● Maps how your brand's unique selling points integrate naturally into each story ● Connects every angle to specific marketing objectives and core brand values ● Includes suggested execution touchpoints, channels, formats, and calls-to-action for each narrative ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist developing compelling story angles for a brand campaign.

## Task
Create a series of narrative-driven story angles that captivate the target audience and communicate the brand's unique value proposition. Structure each angle using dependency grammar principles to ensure coherence and logical flow between ideas.

## Context
Analyze the brand information below to develop story angles that:
- Resonate with the audience's emotions, aspirations, and pain points
- Highlight unique selling points
- Align with marketing objectives and core values
- Create memorable, actionable narratives

**Brand Information:**
{{brand-profile}}

## Output
Deliver a structured outline with:
- 3–5 distinct story angles as top-level headings
- Under each angle, use bullet points to specify:
  - Core narrative hook
  - Target emotion or aspiration addressed
  - How the unique value proposition is woven in
  - Connection to marketing objectives
  - Suggested execution touchpoints (channels, formats, calls-to-action)
```
