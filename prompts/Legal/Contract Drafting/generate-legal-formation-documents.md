# Legal Entity Formation Documents Generator

## 簡介

The Legal Entity Formation Documents Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete, jurisdiction-compliant formation documents for businesses and legal professionals establishing new entities. This legal entity formation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces three essential components: Articles of Incorporation with all required provisions and clauses, a comprehensive Operating Agreement detailing management structure and ownership terms, and any additional filings mandated by your specific jurisdiction. By accepting entity type and jurisdiction as inputs, it reviews applicable laws and generates legally binding documents that meet local regulatory requirements. Attorneys, paralegals, and entrepreneurs use it to prepare formation filings for LLCs, corporations, partnerships, and other entities across different states and countries. ● Drafts Articles of Incorporation with provisions required by the target jurisdiction ● Creates Operating Agreements covering management structure, ownership rights, and operational procedures ● Identifies and prepares supplementary filings mandated by state or country regulations ● Ensures all documents comply with local laws and contain legally binding language ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal document drafter specializing in entity formation filings.

## Task
Draft the complete set of legal documents required to form the specified entity in the given jurisdiction.

## Context
The user needs formation documents tailored to their specific entity type and jurisdiction. Review the entity structure and applicable laws to ensure compliance.

## Requirements
1. Draft Articles of Incorporation with all provisions and clauses required for the entity type and jurisdiction
2. Create an Operating Agreement outlining management structure, ownership, and operational procedures in compliance with local laws
3. Identify and draft any additional filings mandated by the jurisdiction
4. Ensure all documents are legally binding and compliant with relevant state or country regulations

## Input
- Entity type: {{entity-type}}
- Jurisdiction: {{jurisdiction}}

## Output
Provide three sections:

**Articles of Incorporation:**
[Complete articles with all required provisions]

**Operating Agreement:**
[Complete agreement with management, ownership, and operational terms]

**Additional Filings:**
[Any supplementary documents required by the jurisdiction]
```
