# Competitor Research Prompt for Direct Market Analysis

## 簡介

The Competitor Research Prompt for Direct Market Analysis is a free AI prompt that identifies real, active competitors in your market and delivers actionable positioning insights for entrepreneurs and business strategists. This competitor research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business description and target audience to surface three direct competitors - companies solving the same problem for the same customer base. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured analysis that includes each competitor's core offering, why they qualify as a direct threat, their key strengths, and specific differentiation opportunities for your business. The prompt focuses on companies your audience will recognize, ensuring the research is relevant and immediately actionable for refining your value proposition and go-to-market strategy. Use this prompt when launching a product, preparing for investor meetings, refining positioning, or building competitive battle cards. It is built for founders, product managers, marketers, and consultants who need fast, structured competitor intelligence without manual market scanning. ● Surfaces three real, active competitors with current market presence and named companies ● Explains why each competitor qualifies as a direct threat based on shared problem and audience ● Provides actionable differentiation opportunities to sharpen your value proposition ● Outputs clear markdown analysis with company names, core offerings, strengths, and positioning insights ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive intelligence researcher specializing in direct competitor identification for entrepreneurs.

## Task
Identify 3 direct competitors for my business by researching active companies in my market. Direct competitors are solutions that solve the same problem for the same target audience—they may differ in product format or value proposition, but compete for the same customer attention and budget.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

Provide the business description (product/service offering) and target audience details.

## Requirements
- Name 3 specific, real companies with active operations
- Select competitors my audience will recognize and find relevant
- Focus on actionable insights that sharpen my value proposition
- Explain why each competitor is a meaningful threat and what makes the competitive dynamic compelling
- Research current market presence to ensure accuracy

## Output
For each competitor, provide:
- Company name and core offering
- Why they qualify as a direct competitor
- Key strengths and positioning
- Differentiation opportunities for my business

Format your analysis in clear markdown with headings for each competitor.
```
