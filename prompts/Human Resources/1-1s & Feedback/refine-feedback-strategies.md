# Feedback Strategies Guide for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Feedback Strategies Guide for Any Profession is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, industry-specific feedback guides for managers, team leads, and professionals who want to improve their workplace communication. This feedback strategies prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section guide that includes an industry-contextualized introduction, 5-7 evidence-based best practices, three established feedback frameworks with concrete examples, and a scenario table mapping realistic situations to effective giver approaches and receiver responses. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts its recommendations to the unique dynamics, hierarchies, and challenges of the profession or industry you specify. Use it when preparing for performance reviews, training managers on feedback delivery, designing team workshops, or building internal communication resources. ● Tailors feedback principles and frameworks to the specific context of any profession or industry ● Provides three established feedback frameworks with concrete application examples ● Includes a scenario table mapping realistic workplace situations to effective approaches ● Cites authoritative sources and research to support recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in workplace communication and professional development, specializing in feedback methodologies across industries.

## Task
Create a comprehensive feedback guide tailored to {{profession-or-industry}}. Structure your response to cover foundational principles, practical frameworks, and real-world applications that professionals can immediately use to improve their feedback interactions.

## Output
Organize your guide using this structure:

# Introduction
Provide a brief overview of why effective feedback is critical in {{profession-or-industry}}, highlighting its impact on performance, relationships, and outcomes specific to this field.

# Best Practices
List 5-7 evidence-based best practices for giving and receiving feedback in {{profession-or-industry}}. Focus on principles that address common challenges in this context.

# Feedback Frameworks
Present three established feedback frameworks particularly relevant to {{profession-or-industry}}. For each framework:
- Explain its core methodology
- Provide a concrete example demonstrating its application in this field

# Feedback Scenarios
Create a table with 4-5 realistic scenarios professionals in {{profession-or-industry}} commonly encounter:

| Scenario | Giver's Approach | Receiver's Response |
|----------|------------------|---------------------|
| [Describe situation] | [Effective approach] | [Constructive response] |

Ensure scenarios reflect the specific dynamics, hierarchies, and challenges of {{profession-or-industry}}.

# Sources
Cite 4-6 authoritative sources (research, frameworks, or expert resources) that informed this guide.
```
