# Miniature Food Scene Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Miniature Food Scene Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates macro photography scenes of desserts being built by tiny workers for food photographers, content creators, and digital artists. This miniature food photography prompt for Midjourney produces whimsical images where tiny bakers in uniforms apply frosting with brushes and spatulas while standing on miniature ladders, push ingredient carts, and carry sprinkles across a dessert slice under construction. The prompt delivers warm golden tones and cartoon-like cuteness combined with photorealistic detail, using shallow depth of field and eye-level perspective. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models, with customizable variables for the dessert scene and background lighting. Reach for this prompt when you need playful, imaginative food photography that combines fantasy storytelling with realistic textures and lighting. ● Creates macro views of desserts under construction with tiny workers applying frosting, pushing carts, and carrying decorations ● Delivers warm golden tones and whimsical cartoon-style characters rendered with photorealistic food textures ● Uses shallow depth of field and eye-level perspective to enhance the miniature-world illusion ● Supports customization of dessert type and background lighting to match your creative vision ## Prompt
