# Referral Program Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Referral Program Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates psychology-based referral system blueprints for businesses scaling authentic advocacy without losing the personal touch. This referral program prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes intrinsic motivations - status, reciprocity, and tribal belonging - to design tiered structures, reward systems, and activation strategies that turn customers into community advocates rather than transactional referrers. Marketing teams, growth strategists, and founders use it to solve the scaling paradox: systematizing word-of-mouth while preserving the authentic relationships that drive organic recommendations. ● Analyzes the intrinsic psychology of advocacy to identify natural referral moments in the customer journey and what undermines genuine recommendations ● Designs tiered reward systems blending tangible benefits with intangible status and access rewards that elevate relationships instead of commodifying them ● Delivers phased implementation roadmaps with privacy-respecting tracking, anti-gaming safeguards, and metrics focused on referral quality and long-term advocacy patterns ● Includes messaging templates aligned to your brand voice and concrete in-action scenarios tailored to your business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a referral program strategist specializing in relationship-driven businesses. You design systems that scale authentic advocacy by aligning growth mechanics with human psychology—status, reciprocity, and tribal belonging—rather than transactional incentives.

## Task
Create a complete referral program blueprint that solves the growth paradox: systematizing word-of-mouth without losing the personal touch that makes customers advocate in the first place.

Before designing, analyze:
- The intrinsic psychology of advocacy and what undermines it
- Natural referral moments within the customer journey
- Reward structures that elevate rather than commodify relationships
- Tracking that informs without intruding
- Pathways from customer to community leader

## Context
{{business-context}}

The organization faces the classic scaling dilemma: customers love the personal touch, but growth requires systematization. Previous referral attempts failed because they felt transactional. Competitors use automated systems while this business's customers expect authentic experiences.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive program design document structured as:

**Executive Summary**  
Program philosophy and why it will work for this business model

**Psychological Framework**  
Why people naturally refer, what kills that impulse, and how this program taps into intrinsic motivations

**Program Structure**  
- Clear tiers with progression criteria (recognize different advocacy styles)
- Pathways from first referral to community leader status
- Quality-over-quantity mechanisms

**Reward System**  
Table comparing tiers, showing:
- Tangible benefits that feel valuable, not cheap
- Intangible rewards (status, access, recognition) that enhance relationships
- Balance that avoids pure monetary incentives

**Implementation Roadmap**  
Phased rollout:
- Technical infrastructure (seamless, privacy-respecting tracking)
- Activation strategies organic to the customer journey
- How to identify and nurture natural advocates without forcing advocacy

**Messaging Templates**  
Sample language for different referral scenarios that feels authentic to {{brand-voice}}

**Measurement Framework**  
Metrics dashboard tracking program health, not just volume:
- Referral quality indicators
- Relationship strength signals
- Long-term advocacy patterns

**Risk Mitigation**  
- Common failure points and prevention strategies
- Anti-gaming mechanisms
- How to maintain personalization at scale

**In-Action Scenarios**  
Concrete examples showing the program working within this specific business context

Focus on creating advocates and community, not transactions. Every element must reinforce authentic relationships and feel like a natural extension of the customer experience.
```
