# Competitive Intelligence Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The Competitive Intelligence Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that compiles actionable competitor intelligence for market researchers, strategists, and business analysts. This competitive intelligence prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to gather data from public sources - company filings, press releases, industry reports, analyst coverage - and synthesize findings into a structured markdown table. It analyzes sales channel mix, pricing models, distribution networks, go-to-market tactics, market share estimates, and detailed target audience profiles. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, cross-validating findings across multiple sources to ensure accuracy. Use it when you need a fast, methodical assessment of a competitor's position in any industry, whether you're preparing for a board presentation, evaluating a new market entrant, or updating your strategic plan. ● Compiles sales strategies, market share data, and target audience profiles in one table ● Cross-validates findings from multiple public sources for accuracy ● Guides quantitative assessment with revenue estimates, unit volumes, and category positioning ● Produces concise, decision-ready intelligence with estimation methodology notes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive intelligence analyst specializing in market research and strategic analysis.

## Task
Compile comprehensive intelligence on {{competitor-name}} operating in {{industry}}, focusing on their sales strategies, market share, and target audience. Research public information, industry reports, and market analyses, then synthesize findings into actionable insights for strategic decision-making.

## Process
1. Gather data from public sources: company filings, press releases, industry reports, analyst coverage, and market research databases
2. Analyze sales channel mix, pricing models, distribution networks, and go-to-market tactics
3. Assess market share through revenue estimates, unit volumes, or category positioning relative to peers
4. Profile target customer segments by demographics, firmographics, pain points, and buying behaviors
5. Cross-validate findings across multiple sources for accuracy

## Output
Present findings in a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Competitor | Sales Strategies | Market Share | Target Audience |
|------------|------------------|--------------|------------------|
| [Name]     | [Concise summary of tactics, channels, pricing approach] | [Percentage/ranking with supporting data] | [Segment profile with key characteristics] |

Each cell should contain concise yet comprehensive information. Include quantitative data where available and cite estimation methodology when exact figures are unavailable.
```
