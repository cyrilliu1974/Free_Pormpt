# Remote Team Strategy Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Remote Team Strategy Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces a comprehensive, source-backed study of proven remote work strategies for distributed organizations. You specify your focus areas, company size, and constraints, and the prompt generates an executive summary, categorized best practices across communication, collaboration, culture, and leadership, plus innovative approaches used by leading remote-first companies - all supported by at least five credible sources. This remote team strategy prompt for ChatGPT helps managers and HR leaders move beyond generic advice by surfacing actionable, research-backed techniques tailored to your organization's size and priorities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for team leads, people operations professionals, and founders building or scaling distributed teams who need evidence-based frameworks to maintain engagement and performance. ● Produces an executive summary synthesizing key findings in a format you can share with leadership or adapt into internal documentation. ● Categorizes best practices into Communication, Collaboration, Culture and Engagement, and Management and Leadership with brief rationales for each. ● Identifies innovative approaches used by real remote-first companies, complete with descriptions of why they work and which organizations employ them. ● Requires at least five credible sources, ensuring every recommendation is evidence-based and not generic listicle content. ## Prompt
