# News Report Generator for Any Outlet Style

## 簡介

The News Report Generator for Any Outlet Style is a free AI prompt that produces publication-ready news reports tailored to match the editorial voice and audience of any specified news outlet. This news report prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to research a given topic extensively, identify key narratives and insights, then synthesize findings into a structured article complete with headline, lede, body sections with supporting details, conclusion, and cited sources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting the writing style to fit outlets ranging from national newspapers to niche trade publications. Journalists, content teams, and communications professionals use it to accelerate the reporting process while maintaining editorial standards and source credibility. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce a news story quickly without sacrificing depth, research quality, or stylistic consistency with your target publication. ● Produces a complete report structure: headline, lede, multi-level body points with supporting details, conclusion, and numbered source citations. ● Adapts tone, vocabulary, and narrative style to match the specified news outlet's audience and editorial standards. ● Ensures readability and logical flow by emphasizing clear relationships between ideas and facts. ● Requires only two inputs - the topic to cover and the target news outlet - making it fast to customize and run. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert journalist and news writer skilled at conducting research, formulating insightful analyses, and crafting compelling reports tailored to specific outlets and audiences.

## Task
Create a well-researched, engaging news report on {{topic}} written in the style of {{news-outlet}}. Research the topic extensively from reputable sources, identify key insights and narratives, and synthesize findings into a structured report that matches the outlet's editorial voice and audience expectations.

## Output
Deliver the report in this structure:

**Title:** [Eye-catching headline that captures the story's essence]

**Lede:** [Engaging opening paragraph that draws readers in and establishes the narrative]

**Body:**
- [Key point 1]
  - [Supporting detail 1a]
  - [Supporting detail 1b]
- [Key point 2]
  - [Supporting detail 2a]
  - [Supporting detail 2b]
- [Key point 3]
  - [Supporting detail 3a]
  - [Supporting detail 3b]

**Conclusion:** [Strong closing paragraph summarizing main findings and implications]

**Sources:**
1. [Source 1]
2. [Source 2]
3. [Source 3]
4. [Source 4]
5. [Source 5]

Ensure clear relationships between words and phrases for readability and coherence. Cite all sources to maintain credibility and transparency.
```
