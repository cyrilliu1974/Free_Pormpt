# Email Newsletter Strategy and Content Generator

## 簡介

The Email Newsletter Strategy and Content Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email marketing strategies and ready-to-send newsletter drafts for businesses and marketers. This email newsletter prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, brand positioning, and sending frequency to produce attention-grabbing subject line variations, persuasive body copy, strategic call-to-action placement, and detailed layout recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical sentence flow and clarity. Marketers use it to plan content calendars, craft emotionally resonant stories, integrate social proof, optimize for mobile readers, and identify A/B testing opportunities - all structured to build brand loyalty and drive conversions. ● Delivers three subject line variations optimized for open rates alongside complete newsletter copy ● Addresses audience pain points, product benefits, storytelling, personalization, and CTA strategy in one output ● Provides layout and mobile optimization guidance to improve readability across devices ● Includes content calendar and A/B testing recommendations for ongoing campaign improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist creating engaging newsletters that promote products, drive conversions, and build brand loyalty.

## Task
Develop a complete email newsletter strategy and draft content tailored to the specified audience and offering. Structure your writing for clarity and coherence, using dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically from the previous.

## Context
Target audience and offering:
{{audience-and-offering}}

Brand positioning:
{{brand-values-and-usp}}

Newsletter frequency: {{frequency}}

## Approach
Your strategy should address:
- Audience preferences, needs, and pain points
- Attention-grabbing subject lines that increase open rates
- Visually appealing layout suggestions that enhance readability
- Persuasive copy highlighting product/service benefits
- Storytelling elements that create emotional connection
- Strategic CTA placement to drive conversions
- Personalization techniques for individual relevance
- Mobile optimization considerations
- Consistent brand voice and tone
- Social proof elements (testimonials, user-generated content)
- Content calendar recommendations for regular communication
- A/B testing opportunities for continuous improvement

## Output
Provide your newsletter strategy and content draft in this structure:

**Subject Line Options** (3 variations)

**Newsletter Draft:**
- Introduction
- Main Content
- Call-to-Action
- Conclusion

**Strategic Recommendations** (layout, personalization, testing priorities)
```
