# Political Issue Multi-Perspective Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Political Issue Multi-Perspective Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces structured, objective analyses of complex political issues from at least three distinct perspectives for researchers, students, and policy professionals. This political issue analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine any political topic through multiple ideological, stakeholder, or theoretical lenses, presenting arguments and evidence objectively with proper citations. The output includes an introduction, historical background, three or more distinct perspectives with bullet-pointed arguments and supporting evidence, a comparative analysis highlighting strengths and weaknesses, and a full reference list in standard academic format. Whether you're researching policy debates, understanding ideological divides, or preparing a balanced briefing, this prompt structures the AI's response using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherence across complex political discussions. The prompt is designed for political analysts, students, journalists, policy advisors, and anyone needing an even-handed examination of contentious topics without editorial bias. ● Examines at least three distinct perspectives on any political issue, ensuring balanced representation of ideological, stakeholder, or theoretical viewpoints ● Structures output with clear sections for background, individual perspectives with key arguments and evidence, comparative analysis, and full citations ● Enforces objectivity and evidence-based reasoning, preventing biased or opinion-driven analysis ● Applies dependency grammar principles to maintain clarity and coherence across complex political discussions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a political analyst conducting objective, multi-perspective research on complex political issues.

## Task
Analyze {{political-issue}} from at least three distinct perspectives. Present arguments and evidence objectively, avoiding bias. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles for clarity and coherence.

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Introduction**
Briefly introduce the issue and its significance.

**Background**
Provide relevant context and historical background.

**Perspective 1**
- Key arguments: Use bullet points for main arguments
- Evidence: Present supporting evidence with proper citations

**Perspective 2**
- Key arguments: List contrasting arguments as bullet points
- Evidence: Provide backing evidence with citations

**Perspective 3**
- Key arguments: Outline distinct arguments using bullet points
- Evidence: Give supporting evidence with citations

**Comparative Analysis**
Compare and contrast the perspectives, highlighting strengths and weaknesses of each.

**Conclusion**
Summarize key findings and insights.

**References**
List all cited sources using a standard academic citation format (APA, MLA, or Chicago).

## Requirements
- Examine at least three distinct ideological, stakeholder, or theoretical perspectives
- Cite all sources properly throughout
- Maintain objectivity; present evidence-based arguments rather than opinions
- Ensure balanced treatment across all perspectives
```
