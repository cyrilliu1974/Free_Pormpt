# Market Segment Value Proposition Generator

## 簡介

The Market Segment Value Proposition Generator is a free AI prompt that creates targeted value propositions for businesses exploring new customer segments and market opportunities. This market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product or service and identifies three distinct, profitable market segments, then crafts a unique value proposition for each. For every segment, it maps key characteristics, customer needs, primary benefits, differentiators, and competitive positioning - then synthesizes everything into a go-to-market strategy. Marketing teams use it to expand into adjacent markets, product managers rely on it to validate segment-market fit, and consultants deploy it to accelerate client positioning work. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond one-size-fits-all messaging and test how your offering resonates across different customer groups, each with distinct pain points and purchase drivers. ● Identifies three high-potential segments with clear demographics, behaviors, or firmographics ● Crafts a tailored value proposition for each segment highlighting the most relevant benefits and differentiators ● Provides competitive positioning recommendations specific to each segment's landscape ● Delivers a go-to-market strategy covering channels, tactics, messaging, and resource allocation across all three segments ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic marketing consultant specializing in market segmentation, value proposition design, and go-to-market strategies.

## Task

Develop a compelling value proposition for the given product or service, tailored to three distinct new market segments. Identify segments with significant revenue potential that are large enough to be profitable yet specific enough for targeted marketing.

## Context

Product or service details:
{{product-service-details}}

## Requirements

For each of three new market segments:

- **Segment Name**: Choose a clear, descriptive name
- **Key Characteristics**: Describe shared demographics, behaviors, or firmographics
- **Needs and Preferences**: Identify what drives purchase decisions and usage patterns
- **Tailored Value Proposition**: Craft a clear, concise statement highlighting the most relevant benefits and differentiators (2-3 key benefits, primary differentiator, resonating message)
- **Positioning Recommendation**: Specify how to position the offering within this segment's competitive landscape

Ensure value propositions address specific segment needs rather than using generic messaging. Focus on segments that align with available business resources.
```
