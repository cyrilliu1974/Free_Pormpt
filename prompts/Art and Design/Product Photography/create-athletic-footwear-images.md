# Athletic Footwear Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Athletic Footwear Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic, levitating sneaker images with professional lighting and atmospheric effects for product designers, marketers, and e-commerce teams. This athletic footwear prompt for Midjourney produces photorealistic renders of sneakers hovering above a dimly lit charcoal running track, wrapped in dynamic smoke from a low three-quarter camera angle with shallow depth of field. You control the sneaker description (material, colors, design details), background and lighting conditions, and aspect ratio to match your creative brief or marketing requirements. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image synthesis models that support detailed scene composition.
