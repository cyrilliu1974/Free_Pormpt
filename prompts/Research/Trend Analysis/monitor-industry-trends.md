# Industry Trend Analysis and Opportunity Finder

## 簡介

The Industry Trend Analysis and Opportunity Finder is a free AI prompt that analyzes current developments in any industry to uncover productivity improvements and self-employment prospects for professionals. This industry trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by examining technological advancements, market shifts, regulatory changes, and consumer behavior patterns to identify 5-7 significant trends shaping your field. It evaluates each trend through two lenses: how it can enhance productivity and what self-employment opportunities it creates. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and delivers a clean markdown table with three columns (Trend, Productivity Implications, Self-Employment Opportunities) that professionals can immediately act upon. Use it when entering a new market, planning a career pivot, or researching competitive advantages in your domain. ● Maps emerging trends to concrete productivity gains and business opportunities ● Customizes analysis based on your professional background and specific focus areas ● Outputs a structured markdown table for easy comparison and decision-making ● Covers technological, regulatory, market, and behavioral factors in one cohesive analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert industry analyst specializing in trend monitoring and opportunity identification.

## Task

Analyze current developments in the specified industry to uncover productivity improvements and self-employment prospects. Research recent trends, then evaluate their impact through the lens of technological advancements, market shifts, regulatory changes, and consumer behavior patterns.

## Context

Industry: {{industry}}

Professional context: {{professional-background}}

Focus areas: {{focus-areas}}

## Analysis Framework

1. Identify 5-7 significant trends currently shaping the industry
2. Assess each trend's implications for productivity enhancement
3. Evaluate self-employment opportunities each trend creates
4. Consider feasibility given the professional background and focus areas provided

## Output

Present findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Trend | Productivity Implications | Self-Employment Opportunities |
|-------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|

Each row should provide clear, concise, and actionable insights that guide decision-making for professionals seeking to enhance productivity or explore self-employment within the industry.
```
