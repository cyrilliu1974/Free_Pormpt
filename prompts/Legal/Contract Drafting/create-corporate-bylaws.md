# Corporate Bylaws Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Corporate Bylaws Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates complete corporate bylaws covering all essential governance provisions and operational procedures for companies. This corporate bylaws prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a corporate lawyer, drafting a full set of bylaws organized into five core articles: governance structure (board composition, powers, officer roles), meeting protocols (annual and special meetings, notice requirements, quorum rules), voting procedures (shareholder rights, proxies, written consent), amendment processes, and indemnification provisions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing legally structured bylaws written in precise legal language while maintaining clarity for company executives and board members. Real use cases include establishing governance frameworks for new corporations, updating outdated bylaws, and ensuring legal compliance across different industries and organizational stages. This prompt is for startup founders, corporate secretaries, in-house counsel, and business executives who need to establish or revise internal company rules without starting from scratch. ● Outputs five structured articles covering governance, meetings, voting, amendments, and indemnification with specific subsections for board composition, officer roles, quorum requirements, and proxy procedures. ● Tailors the bylaws to the company name you provide while following best practices for corporate law compliance and internal rule-setting. ● Generates precise legal language appropriate for formal corporate documents while keeping provisions clear enough for non-lawyers to understand and implement. ● Covers both routine operational procedures (meeting notices, voting protocols) and critical protections (indemnification, amendment requirements) in a single comprehensive document. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a corporate lawyer specializing in drafting comprehensive bylaws for companies across industries and organizational stages.

## Task
Draft a complete set of corporate bylaws for {{company-name}}, covering all necessary governance provisions and operational procedures required for legal compliance and effective corporate management.

## Context
Corporate bylaws establish the internal rules and procedures that govern a company's operations, including board structure, meeting protocols, voting rights, and amendment processes. The bylaws must be legally sound, comprehensive, and tailored to the company's specific needs while remaining clear and accessible.

## Output
Deliver the bylaws using this structure:

**{{company-name}} Corporate Bylaws**

**Article 1: Governance**
1.1 Corporate Purpose
1.2 Board of Directors
  1.2.1 Composition
  1.2.2 Powers
  1.2.3 Meetings
1.3 Officers

**Article 2: Meetings**
2.1 Annual Meetings
2.2 Special Meetings
2.3 Notice Requirements
2.4 Quorum

**Article 3: Voting**
3.1 Voting Rights
3.2 Proxies
3.3 Action by Written Consent

**Article 4: Amendments**

**Article 5: Indemnification**

For each section, provide specific regulations, procedures, and requirements. Use precise legal language while maintaining clarity. Ensure all provisions comply with applicable corporate law and reflect best practices for corporate governance.
```
