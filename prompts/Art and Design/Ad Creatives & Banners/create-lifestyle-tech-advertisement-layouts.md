# Lifestyle Tech Advertisement Layout Generator

## 簡介

The Lifestyle Tech Advertisement Layout Generator is a free AI prompt that creates natural, authentic lifestyle tech ads for consumer electronics brands and marketers. This lifestyle tech ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 3:4 portrait layout split into two sections: a hero image showing someone using your product in a cozy living room with soft window light, and a 2×2 grid below with four detail shots - macro textures, hands-on usage, product on a table, and a candid lifestyle moment. The entire composition uses natural daylight photography with minimal branding and modern typography. Marketing teams use it to create social media ads, e-commerce hero images, and product launch visuals that feel genuine rather than overly commercial. Reach for this prompt when you need lifestyle tech photography that looks like real everyday use, not a studio shoot. ● Hero section with over-the-shoulder or elevated angle of product in use, soft window light, authentic unposed grip ● 2×2 grid with macro texture detail, hands-on usage shot, product on coffee table, and environmental lifestyle panel ● Modern minimal typography with product name and tagline in neutral sans-serif ● Avoids harsh studio lighting, neon accents, excessive branding, and artificial commercial styling ## Prompt

```
Natural lifestyle tech advertisement in 3:4 portrait layout. Top half: large hero image; bottom half: 2×2 equal grid.

**Hero Section (top)**
Person relaxing on a couch using {{product-name}}, cozy living room with large window, soft natural daylight streaming from side window, over-the-shoulder or slightly elevated camera angle, product held in hands with natural unposed grip, soft blurred living room background, relaxed casual authentic mood.

**Grid Panels (bottom 2×2)**
Panel 1: Macro close-up of product's signature surface texture and controls, material detail focus.
Panel 2: Natural shot of hands using product mid-moment, real usage and comfort.
Panel 3: Product resting on wooden coffee table beside companion device, natural lifestyle setting.
Panel 4: Person sitting comfortably with sunlight entering room, casual everyday environment.

**Typography**
{{product-name}} centered at top in minimal modern sans-serif, neutral black or soft gray. "Live Naturally." tagline below in thin clean sans-serif.

**Style**
Natural lifestyle tech photography, authentic everyday moment, soft window daylight, minimal composition, high realism, gentle natural shadows, soft balanced exposure. Relaxed, cozy, modern mood.

Avoid: overly commercial studio lighting, harsh artificial spotlights, neon or RGB accent lighting, excessive branding, cartoon style, low-resolution textures, watermarks, text artifacts.
```
