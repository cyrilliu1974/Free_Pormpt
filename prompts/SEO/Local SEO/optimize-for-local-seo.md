# Local SEO Strategy Builder for Google Business Profile

## 簡介

The Local SEO Strategy Builder for Google Business Profile is a free AI prompt that creates a white-hat local search optimization roadmap tailored to your business context and competitive landscape. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business information and produces a structured, three-phase plan: immediate quick-win tasks (days 1-7), foundational optimization work (weeks 2-4), and advanced long-term tactics (month 2+). It covers Google's core local ranking signals - proximity, relevance, prominence, and entity associations - and prioritizes every recommendation by impact-to-effort ratio. The prompt walks through Google Business Profile completeness (categories, attributes, posts, Q&A, photos, hours), location-specific content creation that solves real community problems, citation consistency across directories, schema markup, and relationship-driven local link building. Each phase includes implementation steps, timelines for Google to process changes, and common mistakes to avoid. The output also provides monitoring frameworks, sample copy that avoids keyword stuffing, and a prioritized 10-task action checklist with dependencies. Marketing agencies, local business owners, and in-house SEO teams reach for this prompt when they need a systematic plan to climb from invisibility into the Google map pack. ● Outputs immediate-win tasks, foundation-building actions, and advanced tactics in a clear timeline with expected Google processing windows. ● Covers Google Business Profile optimization (categories, posts, Q&A, photos, hours, attributes), citation NAP consistency, schema markup, and local link acquisition. ● Includes location-content templates that match user intent and establish entity associations without template city-name swaps or keyword stuffing. ● Provides monitoring KPIs, tools for tracking map pack position and GBP insights, sample service-page copy, and a prioritized 10-task checklist. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a local SEO strategist specializing in Google Business Profile optimization and map pack rankings. You understand Google's local search signals—proximity, relevance, prominence, behavioral factors, and entity associations.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, white-hat local SEO strategy that moves the business from invisibility to map pack prominence. Prioritize by impact-to-effort ratio and organize actions into immediate wins (0-7 days), foundation building (weeks 2-4), and advanced long-term tactics (month 2+).

## Context

{{business-context}}

The business is currently invisible in local search while competitors dominate. 76% of local searches occur on mobile.

## Strategy Requirements

**Google Business Profile**: Cover critical ranking factors—complete business information, categories, attributes, posts, Q&A, photos, service areas, hours including special hours, booking links, product catalogs, and review response patterns.

**Location Content**: Build pages and posts that solve actual local problems, not template city-name swaps. Match user intent for local queries and establish entity associations with local landmarks, events, and organizations.

**Citations & NAP**: Ensure consistency across directories, schema markup, and high-authority local sources. Focus on citation quality and velocity, not volume.

**Local Link Building**: Prioritize relationship-driven backlinks from local chambers, news sites, community organizations, and complementary businesses.

**Avoid**: Keyword stuffing, fake reviews, duplicate location content, citation spam, black-hat tactics.

## Output

Provide a structured plan with:

### Immediate Actions (Days 1-7)
- Specific tasks with implementation steps
- Expected timeline for Google to process changes
- Common mistakes to avoid

### Foundation Building (Weeks 2-4)
- Core optimization priorities
- Content templates demonstrating local relevance
- Schema markup examples

### Advanced Tactics (Month 2+)
- Sustainable growth strategies
- Scaling frameworks for multi-location businesses
- Competitive moat-building

### Monitoring & Metrics
- KPIs to track weekly and monthly
- Tools and methods for measuring map pack position, GBP insights, and local pack triggers

### Sample Copy
- Location-specific content examples that avoid keyword stuffing
- Service page templates
- GBP post examples

### Action Checklist
- Prioritized first 10 tasks for immediate implementation
- Dependencies and sequencing

Use headers, bullet points, tables for timelines/comparisons, and quoted blocks for copywriting examples. Make all recommendations actionable and specific.
```
