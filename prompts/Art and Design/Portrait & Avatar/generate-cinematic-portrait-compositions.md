# Cinematic Bedroom Portrait Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Bedroom Portrait Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates intimate, magazine-style portrait photographs with subjects seated on beds in softly lit bedroom environments. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces photographs with a signature aesthetic: subjects elegantly posed on the edge of a bed, holding a smartphone that obscures their face, dressed in customizable outfits, surrounded by warm dim lighting and a grainy, film-inspired texture. The prompt accepts three variables - subject description, outfit and footwear, and bedroom setting details - allowing creators to generate lifestyle imagery that feels authentic and personal. Use it for fashion lookbooks, social media content, editorial projects, or brand storytelling that requires a moody, relatable atmosphere. This prompt is ideal for content creators, fashion brands, and visual marketers who need consistent, high-quality lifestyle photography without coordinating photo shoots. ● Produces intimate bedroom portraits with subjects holding smartphones, legs crossed, in stylish outfits you define. ● Delivers a moody, cinematic aesthetic with soft warm lighting, grainy texture, and muted color palettes. ● Accepts three customizable inputs: subject characteristics, complete outfit with shoes, and bedroom decor or ambiance. ● Generates magazine-quality imagery suitable for fashion campaigns, lifestyle blogs, Instagram content, and editorial features. ## Prompt

```
{{subject-description}} sitting on the edge of a neatly made bed, holding a smartphone in front of her face, obscuring it. Legs crossed elegantly. She wears {{outfit-and-shoes}}. ```
```
