# Instagram Video Script Generator for Ads and Reels

## 簡介

The Instagram Video Script Generator for Ads and Reels is a free AI prompt that writes compelling short-form video scripts optimized for Instagram's vertical video format and engagement-driven algorithm. This Instagram video script prompt for ChatGPT structures your promotional content using six core sections: a scroll-stopping hook for the first three seconds, a clear introduction that highlights your unique selling proposition, a problem statement that names your audience's pain point, a solution presentation, proof elements like testimonials or results, and a direct call-to-action. The prompt includes visual cue annotations throughout to guide transitions, text overlays, product shots, and b-roll that work within Instagram's constraints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to your specific product, target audience, brand voice, and desired action. Use this prompt when you need to turn product details into a ready-to-shoot Instagram Reel or video ad that balances persuasion with platform best practices. ● Structures scripts around dependency grammar principles for clarity and logical flow that keeps viewers watching ● Provides labeled sections - hook, introduction, problem, solution, proof, call-to-action - so every script element has a clear purpose ● Includes visual cue annotations to indicate when to show product shots, overlays, transitions, or b-roll footage ● Adapts to your brand voice, unique selling proposition, target audience, and specific call-to-action for personalized output ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Instagram video script writer specializing in short-form promotional content that drives engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write a compelling Instagram video script structured using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherent flow. The script must work within Instagram's short-form constraints and visual-first format.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique Selling Proposition: {{usp}}
Brand Voice: {{brand-voice}}
Call-to-Action: {{cta}}

## Output
Deliver the script with these clearly labeled sections:

**Hook** (first 3 seconds)
Open with an attention-grabbing statement or question that stops scrolling.

**Introduction**
Introduce the product/service and spotlight the unique selling proposition.

**Problem**
Name the specific pain point or desire your target audience experiences.

**Solution**
Show how the product/service resolves that problem or fulfills the desire.

**Proof**
Include evidence, testimonials, social proof, or results that build credibility.

**Call-to-Action**
End with a clear, compelling directive that encourages immediate engagement or purchase.

Include [visual cue] annotations throughout to indicate transitions, text overlays, product shots, or b-roll suitable for Instagram's vertical video format.
```
