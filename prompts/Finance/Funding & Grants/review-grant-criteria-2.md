# Grant Eligibility Criteria Review Prompt

## 簡介

The Grant Eligibility Criteria Review Prompt is a free AI prompt that extracts and summarizes key eligibility requirements for any grant type, tailored to your organization's profile, field, and location. This grant eligibility prompt for ChatGPT takes a specific grant type and produces a structured bullet-point analysis covering applicant qualifications, project requirements, funding limits, submission deadlines, and other critical eligibility factors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping nonprofits, research institutions, and grant-seeking organizations quickly understand whether a funding opportunity matches their capabilities before investing time in a full application. You provide the grant type, your organization profile, field of interest, and location, and the prompt returns a clear, digestible summary of who qualifies, what projects are eligible, how much funding is available, and when deadlines fall. ● Organizes complex grant guidelines into applicant qualifications, project scope, funding caps, and deadlines ● Accepts organization profile, field, and location context to surface relevant regional or sector-specific requirements ● Identifies critical eligibility factors that might disqualify an application before submission ● Outputs a scannable bullet-point format that teams can share and reference during proposal planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant reviewer specializing in eligibility analysis.

## Task
Review and summarize the key eligibility requirements for {{grant-type}}. Carefully examine all aspects of the eligibility criteria, including applicant qualifications, project requirements, funding limits, and submission deadlines. Capture all essential information while presenting it in an easily digestible format.

## Context
- Organization: {{organization-profile}}
- Field of interest: {{field}}
- Location: {{location}}

## Output
Provide your analysis as a bullet point list covering:
- Applicant qualifications
- Project requirements
- Funding limits
- Submission deadlines
- Any other critical eligibility factors
```
