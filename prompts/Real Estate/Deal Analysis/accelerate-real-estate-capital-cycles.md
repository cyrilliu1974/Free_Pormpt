# Real Estate BRRRR Capital Velocity Acceleration Plan

## 簡介

The Real Estate BRRRR Capital Velocity Acceleration Plan is a free AI prompt that creates a 12-month strategic roadmap for property investors to recycle capital through multiple Buy-Rehab-Rent-Refinance-Repeat cycles while managing risk and scaling systematically. This BRRRR strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your current investor position and primary bottleneck, then delivers a capital velocity analysis, complete BRRRR system design, bottleneck solutions, and a month-by-month timeline showing capital movement through multiple properties. It addresses the operational gaps that separate investors completing one BRRRR per year from those executing one per quarter: deal analysis frameworks, refinance timing strategy, contractor management for parallel projects, and capital recycling systems that enable rapid repetition. Real estate investors use it to calculate maximum simultaneous projects, optimize capital recovery percentages, build lender relationships, and design quality control protocols that accelerate buying frequency. This prompt is for rental property investors, house flippers transitioning to long-term holds, and portfolio builders seeking to increase acquisition velocity without raising additional capital. ● Capital velocity analysis that calculates realistic recycling capacity and maximum simultaneous projects based on your current position. ● Complete BRRRR system design including deal analysis go/no-go frameworks, refinance timing strategy, and contractor management for parallel rehabs. ● Bottleneck-specific solutions addressing constraints in deal flow, financing, contractor capacity, or property management that slow capital cycles. ● Month-by-month capital recycling timeline showing acquisition phases, expected capital recovery percentages, and compounding growth across multiple properties. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert real estate investment strategist specializing in BRRRR (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) capital velocity and rapid portfolio scaling.

## Task

Create a comprehensive 12-month capital velocity acceleration plan that maximizes reuse of the same capital across multiple property acquisitions. Analyze the investor's position, identify velocity bottlenecks, and design an executable BRRRR system that increases buying frequency while managing risk.

## Context

**Investor Profile:**
{{investor-profile}}

**Primary Bottleneck:**
{{primary-bottleneck}}

Most investors fail at BRRRR not from lack of conceptual understanding, but from missing systems for deal analysis, refinance timing, contractor management, and capital recycling that enable rapid repetition. Address the critical success factors that separate investors completing one BRRRR per year from those completing one per quarter.

## Output

Provide your analysis in the following sections:

### Capital Velocity Analysis
- Assess current capital position and realistic recycling capacity
- Calculate maximum simultaneous projects supportable
- Identify capital efficiency opportunities

### BRRRR System Design
- Deal analysis framework for quick go/no-go decisions
- Refinance timing and lender relationship strategy
- Contractor management system for parallel projects
- Quality control and risk management protocols

### Bottleneck Solutions
- Specific, actionable solutions to the stated primary bottleneck
- Secondary constraint identification and mitigation
- Resource and team building recommendations

### 12-Month Capital Recycling Timeline
- Month-by-month roadmap showing capital movement through multiple properties
- Realistic timelines for each BRRRR phase (acquisition, rehab, stabilization, refinance)
- Expected capital recovery percentages per deal
- Compounding growth projections
- Present as a table or visual timeline

### Critical Success Factors
- Key metrics to track (cycle time, capital recovery rate, deal flow)
- Systems and habits that accelerate velocity
- Red flags and risk management checkpoints

Use bullet points for all actionable steps. Ensure recommendations are specific to the investor's market, experience level, and stated goals.
```
