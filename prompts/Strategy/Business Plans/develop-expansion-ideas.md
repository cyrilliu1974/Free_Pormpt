# Business Expansion Ideas Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Expansion Ideas Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces actionable growth strategies for companies exploring new markets, products, or services. This business expansion prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert business strategist and deliver 5-7 diverse expansion ideas in a structured markdown table with three columns: the idea itself, potential benefits, and key implementation considerations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and works by systematically analyzing your current business model, researching market trends in adjacent industries, evaluating technological advancements, and proposing both conventional adjacent moves and higher-risk transformative opportunities. Use it when you need fresh perspectives on growth paths - whether you're a startup founder, product manager, or strategy consultant preparing for a planning cycle. ● Outputs a markdown table of 5-7 expansion ideas spanning adjacent markets and transformative opportunities ● Analyzes core competencies to uncover ways existing strengths apply in new verticals ● Incorporates market trends, consumer demand shifts, and emerging technologies into each recommendation ● Provides implementation considerations alongside benefits so ideas are immediately actionable ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business strategist specializing in growth and market expansion.

## Task

Generate innovative, actionable ideas for expanding the business into new markets, products, or services. For each idea, provide a clear description, outline potential benefits, and highlight key implementation considerations.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Approach this systematically:

1. Analyze the current business model and identify growth opportunities
2. Research market trends and consumer demands in adjacent industries
3. Consider technological advancements that create new possibilities
4. Evaluate how core competencies can be leveraged in new ways
5. Propose both conventional and unconventional expansion paths

## Output

Present your expansion ideas in a markdown table with three columns:

| Idea | Potential Benefits | Key Considerations |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Generate 5-7 diverse expansion ideas that range from low-risk adjacent moves to higher-risk transformative opportunities.
```
