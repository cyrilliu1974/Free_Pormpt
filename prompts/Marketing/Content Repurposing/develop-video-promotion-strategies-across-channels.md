# Video Promotion Strategy Builder for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Video Promotion Strategy Builder for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates channel-specific video marketing plans for content creators and marketers. This video promotion prompt for ChatGPT takes your platforms, video content type, and target audience, then outputs a structured markdown table with three columns: Channel, SEO Tactics, and Video SEO Tips. Each row delivers actionable strategies aligned with that platform's unique algorithm, ranking factors, metadata requirements (titles, descriptions, tags, hashtags), content format trends, and audience engagement patterns. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for marketers who need platform-specific guidance rather than generic advice. The prompt ensures your video content is optimized for discoverability and engagement on each channel you target, from YouTube and TikTok to LinkedIn and Instagram. ● Delivers platform-specific metadata optimization guidance including titles, descriptions, tags, and hashtags tailored to each channel's algorithm ● Recommends content formats and posting strategies aligned with current platform trends and audience behavior patterns ● Produces implementation-ready tactics organized in a markdown table for easy reference and execution ● Adapts to your specific video content type and target audience, ensuring relevance across all channels you select ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video marketing strategist specializing in cross-platform promotion and search optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive video promotion strategy that maximizes reach and engagement across three platforms. For each platform, provide specific SEO tactics and video optimization tips tailored to its unique algorithm, audience behavior, and content formats.

## Context
**Platforms:** {{platforms}}
**Video content type:** {{video-content-type}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Requirements
- Address platform-specific features, algorithms, and ranking factors
- Include metadata optimization (titles, descriptions, tags, hashtags)
- Recommend content formats and posting strategies aligned with each platform's trends
- Consider audience behavior and engagement patterns unique to each channel
- Provide actionable, implementation-ready tactics

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Channel | SEO Tactics | Video SEO Tips |
|---------|-------------|----------------|

Each row should contain platform-specific strategies with clear, actionable insights.
```
