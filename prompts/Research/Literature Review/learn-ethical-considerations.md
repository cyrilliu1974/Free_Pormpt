# Research Ethics Review Prompt for Academic Papers

## 簡介

The Research Ethics Review Prompt for Academic Papers is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive ethical analyses of scholarly research for academics, researchers, and ethics review boards. This research ethics prompt for ChatGPT systematically evaluates research papers across four critical dimensions: methodology appropriateness and participant risk, data collection procedures including informed consent and confidentiality protections, interpretation and presentation objectivity, and compliance with established guidelines like the APA Ethics Code and Declaration of Helsinki. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured report that identifies specific ethical concerns, compares practices against academic standards, and provides actionable recommendations for strengthening research integrity. Use it when preparing manuscripts for submission, conducting peer review, teaching research ethics courses, or ensuring institutional compliance with ethical guidelines. ● Identifies ethical issues across research design, participant protections, data handling, and results presentation with clear reasoning for each concern ● Benchmarks research practices against APA Ethics Code, Declaration of Helsinki, and field-specific standards to pinpoint compliance gaps ● Delivers actionable recommendations including methodology revisions, enhanced consent procedures, improved confidentiality measures, and presentation corrections ● Structures output as a professional ethics review report suitable for IRB submissions, manuscript revisions, or educational purposes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic research ethics expert conducting a comprehensive ethical review of a research paper.

## Task
Analyze the research paper for ethical concerns across its research methods, data collection, interpretation of results, and presentation. Compare the paper's approach against established academic ethics guidelines (APA, Declaration of Helsinki) and provide actionable recommendations for improvement.

## Context
Research paper details:
{{paper-details}}

The analysis should examine:
- **Research methods**: study design appropriateness, participant risk, potential deception, justification for methodology
- **Data collection**: informed consent procedures, transparency about purpose and data use, participant freedom to withdraw, confidentiality and anonymity protections
- **Interpretation and presentation**: objectivity in analysis, freedom from bias, accuracy of results reporting, appropriate representation of data significance
- **Compliance**: adherence to standard ethical guidelines and best practices in research integrity

## Output
Deliver a structured ethical analysis report containing:

**1. Ethical Concerns Identified**
List specific ethical issues found in each area (methods, data collection, interpretation, presentation), with clear reasoning

**2. Comparison to Standards**
Reference specific applicable guidelines (APA, Declaration of Helsinki, or field-specific standards) and explain where the paper deviates from or meets these benchmarks

**3. Recommendations**
Provide concrete, actionable advice for addressing each identified issue:
- Revisions to study design or methodology
- Enhanced informed consent procedures
- Improved data confidentiality measures
- Corrections to analysis or presentation
- Steps to increase transparency and accountability

Ensure the report is logically structured, clearly reasoned, and emphasizes how improvements will strengthen the research's ethical integrity and contribution to scholarship.
```
