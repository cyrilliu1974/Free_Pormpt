# Business Problem Solving Prompt for Strategic Ideas

## 簡介

The Business Problem Solving Prompt for Strategic Ideas is a free AI prompt that generates innovative, actionable business solutions tailored to specific industry challenges and organizational contexts. This business problem solving prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert business strategist, producing at least five distinct ideas that address your challenge while critically evaluating each option's feasibility, potential impact, advantages, and implementation barriers. The output arrives as a clean markdown table with three columns - Idea, Pros, and Cons - making it simple to compare options side by side. You provide your business context, target audience, market constraints, and available resources through a single variable, and the prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver diverse solutions grounded in real-world practicality. Teams use it for product development brainstorming, strategic planning sessions, market-entry analysis, and operational improvement initiatives. ● Produces at least five diverse business ideas per query, each with clear pros and cons ● Evaluates feasibility, potential impact, and implementation barriers for every solution ● Delivers output in a markdown table format for easy comparison and presentation ● Adapts to any industry, market constraint, or resource limitation you specify in the business-context variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in innovative solution design and feasibility analysis.

## Task
Generate at least 5 innovative business ideas that address the specified problem. For each idea, critically evaluate its feasibility, potential impact, pros, and cons.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze this situation thoroughly, generating diverse solutions that account for the target audience, market constraints, and available resources described above.

## Output
Present your ideas in a markdown table with three columns:

| Idea | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|

For each idea:
- **Idea column**: Briefly describe the business concept and how it addresses the problem
- **Pros column**: List key advantages, including feasibility factors and potential impact
- **Cons column**: List challenges, risks, and implementation barriers

Provide clear, actionable insights that enable informed decision-making.
```
