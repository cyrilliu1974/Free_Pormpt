# Customer Feedback System Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Feedback System Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds tailored feedback architectures for businesses looking to capture authentic customer insights and drive product improvements. It produces a complete feedback ecosystem including collection points mapped to your user journey, recommended channels (passive, active, and community-driven), a specific tool stack matched to your budget and technical capabilities, prioritization frameworks, closed-loop communication methods, and success metrics. This customer feedback system prompt for ChatGPT works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your business context and generating a layered approach that avoids survey fatigue while balancing automation with personal connection at scale. Use it when you need to design or overhaul a feedback system that transforms user input into product changes and strengthens customer loyalty. ● Maps optimal feedback collection points to your specific product type and user journey stages. ● Recommends passive feedback methods, strategic survey deployment timing, community engagement tactics, and behavioral analytics approaches. ● Suggests specific tools and platforms with budget considerations, technical requirements, and phased implementation timelines. ● Provides frameworks for analyzing feedback volume, prioritizing requests, and building action plans that demonstrate customer impact. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer experience strategist specializing in feedback systems that capture authentic insights, drive product improvements, and strengthen loyalty.

## Task
Design a comprehensive, multi-channel customer feedback system tailored to the business context provided. The system should:
- Capture both explicit feedback and behavioral signals across multiple touchpoints
- Create closed-loop processes that show customers how their input drives change
- Balance automation with personal connection at scale
- Avoid survey fatigue by meeting customers where they are
- Include frameworks for analyzing, prioritizing, and acting on feedback
- Provide methods for measuring system effectiveness

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Most feedback systems fail because they prioritize company convenience over customer psychology. Design a layered approach combining passive feedback collection, strategic survey deployment, community engagement, and behavioral analytics. Recommend specific tools and platforms matched to the budget and technical capabilities described in the business context. Include implementation timelines and success metrics.

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings:

1. **Optimal Feedback Collection Points** – based on the product type and user journey
2. **Recommended Feedback Channels** – passive, active, and community-driven methods
3. **Tool Stack & Implementation** – specific platforms with budget considerations and rollout timeline
4. **Analysis & Action Framework** – how to prioritize and act on feedback
5. **Closed-Loop Communication** – methods for showing customers their impact
6. **Success Metrics** – KPIs for system performance and customer satisfaction with the feedback process

Provide actionable recommendations in bullet points with specific tool suggestions where appropriate.
```
