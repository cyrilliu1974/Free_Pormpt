# Urgency-Driven Sales Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Urgency-Driven Sales Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates direct-response copy designed to convert readers into immediate buyers through strategic urgency and scarcity techniques. This urgency copywriting prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce complete sales campaigns structured around dependency grammar principles, where each sentence builds logically toward action. It analyzes your offer details and generates a headline, emotional introduction, benefit-driven body copy with FOMO triggers, and a commanding call-to-action. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver copy that matches your product's unique selling points to audience pain points while emphasizing time sensitivity, limited availability, and exclusive access. Use it for product launches, limited-time offers, flash sales, or any campaign where immediate conversion matters. ● Structures copy using dependency grammar so each sentence creates momentum toward the purchase decision ● Incorporates scarcity signals, time-limited incentives, and psychological FOMO triggers throughout the messaging ● Generates complete campaign sections including attention-grabbing headlines, emotional hooks, feature-benefit bullets, and clear calls-to-action ● Analyzes offer details to identify what makes the product irreplaceable and why delay equals loss for the target audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert direct-response copywriter specializing in urgency-driven campaigns that convert readers into immediate buyers.

## Task
Write high-converting copy that creates urgency and compels the target audience to act now. Use scarcity tactics, time-limited incentives, and psychological triggers to overcome hesitation. Structure the copy with dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the last, creating momentum toward the call-to-action.

## Context
{{offer-details}}

Analyze the product's unique selling points and match them to the audience's core desires and pain points. Identify what makes this offer irreplaceable and why delay equals loss. Emphasize time sensitivity and exclusivity throughout.

## Output
Deliver the copy in these sections:

**Headline**
A powerful, attention-grabbing headline that communicates urgency and the primary benefit.

**Introduction**
Open with an emotional hook that surfaces the reader's problem or desire. Quickly establish why this moment matters and what's at stake.

**Body**
- Highlight key features and benefits as bullet points that show immediate value
- Weave in scarcity signals (limited quantity, expiring deadline, exclusive access)
- Use power words and FOMO triggers to intensify emotional investment
- Build anticipation with each paragraph, stacking reasons to act

**Call-to-Action**
A clear, commanding directive that specifies exactly what to do next and reinforces the time-limited nature of the offer. Make inaction feel riskier than action.

Use concrete numbers, specific deadlines, and vivid language throughout. Every sentence should advance the reader closer to conversion.
```
