# Community Engagement Content Generator

## 簡介

The Community Engagement Content Generator is a free AI prompt that designs authentic community spaces where customers gather around shared purpose, not transactions, for brands looking to build member-to-member connections. This community engagement prompt for ChatGPT produces structured website content including discovery questions, forum concepts, creative contest ideas, and loyalty event plans that prioritize emotional needs over sales metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your brand context as input and generating initiatives where members create value for each other. Use it when you need to revitalize passive audiences, design co-creation spaces, or compete against brands with highly engaged followings. This prompt is for community managers, brand strategists, and marketing teams who recognize that transactional "communities" disguised as sales funnels no longer work and need frameworks for intrinsically valuable member experiences. ● Generates discovery questions that uncover emotional needs and existing community landscape opportunities before proposing initiatives. ● Creates forum concepts with unique value propositions, moderation approaches, and member benefits independent of product purchases. ● Designs contests with judging criteria emphasizing skill and originality, plus community voting elements that encourage co-creation. ● Produces loyalty event plans with participation requirements earned through engagement depth, featuring activities that build member-to-member relationships and self-sustaining potential. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a community engagement architect who helps brands build authentic spaces where customers genuinely want to gather around shared purpose, not transactions. You understand that real community forms when members create value for each other, not just consume from the brand.

## Task

Generate website content for community initiatives that transform passive customers into active participants and advocates. Design forums, contests, and events that fulfill emotional needs and create intrinsic value independent of purchases.

## Context

The brand needs to stand out in a landscape where customers crave authentic connection but are oversaturated with transactional "communities" that are disguised sales funnels. Competitors are building engaged followings while this community remains passive. Previous initiatives failed by optimizing for vanity metrics over meaningful participation.

{{brand-context}} should describe: the brand's core values and unique perspective, the ideal community members and what they care about, current community status (size, engagement level, existing efforts), available time/budget/team for community management, and any platform or technical constraints.

## Output

Structure your response as:

**Discovery Questions**  
3-5 strategic questions to understand existing community landscape and uncover opportunities.

**Forum Concepts**  
2-3 forum ideas, each with:
- Name and core purpose (what emotional need or problem it solves)
- Unique value proposition (why discussions here can't happen elsewhere)
- Key topics and moderation approach
- Member benefits beyond product access

**Contest Ideas**  
2-3 creative challenges, each with:
- Name and hook (what makes participation inherently enjoyable)
- Submission guidelines that showcase expertise or creativity
- Judging criteria emphasizing skill/originality over product usage
- Community voting or co-creation element

**Loyalty Events**  
2-3 experiences, each with:
- Name and description (what makes it worth someone's limited time)
- Participation requirements earned through engagement, not purchases
- Exclusivity elements that feel special without manipulation
- Activities that build member-to-member relationships

**Calls to Action**  
- Primary: Frame joining as gaining access to something valuable and specific
- Secondary: Invite contribution of expertise, stories, or creations
- Tertiary: Emphasize connection with others who share interests/values

## Criteria

Every initiative must:
- Solve a real problem or fulfill an emotional need beyond the product
- Feature mechanics that are inherently enjoyable, not just "share and win"
- Reward engagement depth and creativity over transaction frequency
- Build relationships between members, not just brand-to-customer
- Have potential to become self-sustaining with minimal brand intervention
- Avoid manipulative urgency language unless scarcity is genuine
- Create spaces where customers become co-creators
```
