# Academic Journal Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Journal Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that systematically reviews top-tier journals in any scholarly field to surface research patterns, methodologies, and emerging areas of interest. This academic journal analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine the five leading journals in your specified field over a custom time period, extracting impact factors, dominant research topics, preferred methodological approaches, and newly emerging themes. It produces a structured comparison table with proper academic citations in a standardized format, plus a summary paragraph that synthesizes cross-journal trends. Researchers use it to quickly map a field's current state, prepare literature reviews, or benchmark their own work against prevailing scholarship. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a bird's-eye view of a research domain before diving into a systematic review, grant proposal, or dissertation chapter. ● Compares impact factors, top topics, and methodologies across five leading journals in any field ● Outputs a clearly formatted table with six data columns plus full academic citations ● Identifies emerging research areas based on recent publication patterns ● Includes a cross-journal synthesis paragraph highlighting similarities and divergences in research direction ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced academic researcher conducting systematic literature reviews and trend analyses across scholarly fields.

## Task
Analyze the top 5 academic journals in the specified field over the given time period. Identify patterns in research topics, methodologies, and emerging areas of interest. Present findings in a structured table with proper citations.

## Context
Field of study: {{field-of-study}}
Time period: {{analysis-period}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

**Table** with these columns:
- Journal Name (full title)
- Impact Factor (most recent)
- Top Research Topics (3-5 most frequent)
- Preferred Methodologies (2-3 most common)
- Emerging Research Areas (2-3 based on recent publications)

**Citations** in this format:
[Author(s), "Article Title," Journal Name, Volume(Issue), Pages, Year, DOI/URL]

**Summary paragraph** highlighting overall trends across the journals, noting key similarities and differences in research direction, methodological preferences, and emerging themes.

Ensure the table is clearly formatted with proper alignment for readability.
```
