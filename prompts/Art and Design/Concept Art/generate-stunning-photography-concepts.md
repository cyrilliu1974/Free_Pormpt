# Professional Product Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Professional Product Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates studio-lit, high-resolution product images for e-commerce brands, marketers, and creatives. This product photography prompt for Midjourney takes a single product variable and renders it with professional studio lighting, sharp focus, and modern DSLR aesthetics in a 16:9 aspect ratio with raw styling - ideal for catalog shots, Amazon listings, social media ads, or pitch decks. It runs on image generation models including Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering commercial-grade results in seconds. Reach for this prompt when you need clean, professional product visuals without a physical shoot or when testing packaging and positioning concepts before production. ● Renders any product with professional studio lighting and sharp focus in a 16:9 format ● Uses raw style processing to preserve detail and texture for commercial applications ● Simulates modern DSLR camera output for realistic, high-resolution product shots ● Requires only a product name or description - no complex technical parameters ## Prompt

```
{{product}}, professional product photography, studio lighting, sharp focus, high resolution, shot on modern DSLR, --ar 16:9 --style raw
```
