# Keyword Trend Analysis and Content Planning Prompt

## 簡介

The Keyword Trend Analysis and Content Planning Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts predictive keyword research and builds strategic content calendars for marketers, content strategists, and SEO professionals. This keyword trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT identifies the top 5 keywords in your industry, forecasts their popularity over the next 6 months with scored predictions, and explains the specific factors driving each trend. It surfaces 3 emerging keywords with growth potential, recommends 5 data-driven content strategies aligned to the trends, and outputs a quarterly content calendar mapping target keywords to themes, tactics, and content types. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured analysis you can act on immediately. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from reactive to predictive content planning, validate new topic investments, or align editorial calendars with search demand shifts. ● Outputs current popularity scores and 6-month projections for the top 5 industry keywords, each with three trend-driving factors ● Identifies three emerging keywords to monitor, with rationale for their growth potential ● Delivers five content strategies explicitly tied to keyword trend data, not generic advice ● Provides a quarterly content calendar linking timeframes to target keywords, themes, tactics, and formats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a predictive analytics and content strategy expert identifying emerging keyword trends to optimize content planning.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive keyword trend analysis for {{industry}} and develop a strategic content plan based on predicted shifts in keyword popularity over the next 6 months.

## Analysis Requirements

**Industry Overview:**
- Identify the current top 5 keywords in {{industry}}
- For each keyword, provide:
  - Current popularity score (out of 100)
  - 6-month forecast (increasing/decreasing, with projected score)
  - 3 specific factors influencing the trend

**Emerging Opportunities:**
- List 3 emerging keywords to monitor that show growth potential

**Strategic Recommendations:**
- Recommend 5 content strategies that directly align with the keyword trend analysis
- Ensure strategies are data-driven and actionable, not speculative

**Content Calendar:**
- Provide 3 calendar suggestions spanning different timeframes (e.g., Q1, Q2, H2)
- For each timeframe, specify target keywords, content themes, tactics, and content types

## Output Format

**Industry Overview:**
- Industry: [name]
- Current top keywords: [list 5]

**AI Keyword Trend Predictions:**

For each of the 5 keywords:
- Keyword: [name]
- Current popularity: [score]/100
- 6-month forecast: [increasing/decreasing] to [score]/100
- Influencing factors: [factor 1], [factor 2], [factor 3]

**Emerging Keywords to Monitor:**
1. [keyword + brief rationale]
2. [keyword + brief rationale]
3. [keyword + brief rationale]

**Recommended Content Strategies:**
1. [strategy with specific tie to keyword trends]
2. [strategy with specific tie to keyword trends]
3. [strategy with specific tie to keyword trends]
4. [strategy with specific tie to keyword trends]
5. [strategy with specific tie to keyword trends]

**Content Calendar Suggestions:**
- [Timeframe]: Focus on [keywords] with content themed around [theme], using [tactic/content type]
- [Timeframe]: Prioritize [keywords] with content themed around [theme], using [tactic/content type]
- [Timeframe]: Transition to [keywords] with content themed around [theme], using [tactic/content type]
```
