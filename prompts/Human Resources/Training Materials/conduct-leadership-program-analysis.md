# Leadership Program Analysis and Comparison Prompt

## 簡介

The Leadership Program Analysis and Comparison Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts evidence-based comparative research on leadership development programs tailored to specific industries or roles. This leadership program analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to evaluate three top programs across four critical dimensions: structure and delivery methods, duration and time commitment, cost and additional expenses, and quantifiable outcomes with supporting data. The prompt enforces rigorous research standards by requiring citations for every finding and produces a structured report that includes individual program profiles, a side-by-side comparison table, synthesized insights on cost-effectiveness and differential outcomes, and a final recommendation with evidence-based rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for L&D professionals, HR leaders, and organizational consultants who need to make informed program selection decisions. ● Evaluates programs across structure, duration, cost, and outcomes with quantitative and qualitative data ● Requires full citations and research-backed findings for every claim ● Produces a comparison table and synthesis of insights on cost-effectiveness and ROI patterns ● Delivers a final recommendation with evidence-based rationale and implementation considerations ## Prompt
