# Photorealistic Packaging Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Packaging Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms flat dieline designs into high-fidelity 3D box visualizations with perfect structural detail and professional studio presentation for packaging designers and brand teams. This packaging mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 8K resolution product visuals featuring accurate panel placement, sharp folded edges, and undistorted typography rendered on customizable box types and material finishes. It delivers soft commercial lighting with global illumination, minimalist studio backgrounds, and subtle shadow work that anchors the product in space. Use it to validate packaging concepts, present client mockups, or create marketing assets without physical prototypes or photography shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need print-ready packaging visualization that shows structural integrity, material texture, and brand artwork in realistic context before manufacturing begins. ● Renders boxes in refined three-quarter perspective showing front and side panels with perfectly folded structure and sharp edges. ● Applies customizable material finishes with physically based rendering that reveals surface grain and texture under even studio lighting. ● Preserves original design artwork with zero distortion, maintaining typography legibility and color accuracy across all panels. ● Delivers minimalist high-end aesthetic with soft neutral backdrops, clean surfaces, and gentle shadows for premium editorial presentation. ## Prompt
