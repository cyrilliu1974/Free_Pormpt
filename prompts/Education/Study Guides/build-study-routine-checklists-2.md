# Study Routine Checklist Builder for Students

## 簡介

The Study Routine Checklist Builder for Students is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, formatted study checklists for any subject and grade level. This study routine prompt for ChatGPT uses evidence-based learning science - spaced repetition, active recall, and interleaving - to produce organized markdown checklists with category headings, actionable checkbox items, time estimates, and brief technique explanations. It adapts cognitive load and study duration recommendations to match the student's grade level and learning context. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering scannable, realistic routines that students can follow daily. Teachers, tutors, parents, and students themselves use it to turn vague "study more" intentions into concrete habits, whether preparing for exams, mastering new topics, or maintaining consistent practice across semesters. ● Tailors study sessions to specific subjects, grade levels, and available time, ensuring realistic expectations. ● Incorporates proven retention strategies like spaced repetition and active recall with plain-language explanations. ● Outputs markdown checklists with checkboxes, subheadings, and time estimates for easy daily tracking. ● Encourages consistent learning habits through structured, motivating routines that reduce procrastination. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational consultant specializing in study routine design and learning habits.

## Task
Create a comprehensive study routine checklist tailored to the subject, grade level, and learning context provided. The checklist must be organized, actionable, and designed to build consistent learning habits that improve retention and academic performance.

## Context
**Subject & Grade:** {{subject-and-grade}}

**Learning Context:** {{learning-context}}

Consider age-appropriate cognitive load, recommended study duration patterns for this grade level, evidence-based retention techniques (spaced repetition, active recall, interleaving), and motivational elements that sustain engagement.

## Output
Deliver the study routine as a formatted checklist with:
- **Main category headings** (e.g., Daily Preparation, Active Study Session, Review & Consolidation)
- **Subheadings** for specific activities
- **Checkboxes** (use `- [ ]` markdown syntax) for each actionable item
- **Time estimates** where helpful
- **Brief explanations** (1 sentence) for techniques that may be unfamiliar

Ensure the structure is scannable, the language is encouraging, and the routine is realistic for the available study time.
```
