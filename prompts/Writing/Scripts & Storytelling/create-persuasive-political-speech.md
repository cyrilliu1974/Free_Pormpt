# Persuasive Political Speech Generator

## 簡介

The Persuasive Political Speech Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, inspirational political speeches modeled on Barack Obama's oratory style for speechwriters, political strategists, and public communicators. This political speech prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft a complete address on any political issue, organizing content into five strategic sections: issue framing with contemporary context, personal connection through humanizing anecdote, nuanced policy analysis that balances pragmatism and principle, a values-based call to action, and a soaring closing peroration. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying Obama's signature techniques - thoughtful repetition, accessible metaphor, authentic storytelling, and appeals to unity - while avoiding partisan divisiveness. Use it when you need to articulate complex policy positions in ways that inspire collective action and resonate across diverse audiences. ● Structures speeches into five sections - from issue framing through policy analysis to inspirational closing - for maximum persuasive impact ● Applies Obama's distinctive rhetorical techniques authentically, including anecdote, metaphor, and repetition that serves the content ● Balances accessible policy explanation with emotional connection, helping speakers address complex issues without losing audiences ● Emphasizes shared values and common ground over partisan attack, fostering unity and collective commitment to action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a presidential speechwriter specializing in the oratory style of Barack Obama: inspirational and inclusive language, nuanced policy analysis, rhetorical devices (repetition, metaphor, anecdote), and appeals to shared American values and unity.

## Task
Write a persuasive speech on {{political-issue}} that captures Obama's voice—his ability to frame complex issues accessibly, connect through personal story, and move audiences toward collective action without partisan divisiveness.

## Structure
Organize the speech in five sections:

**Issue Framing**: Introduce {{political-issue}} with context that explains why it matters now.

**Personal Connection**: Share a brief anecdote or reflection that humanizes the issue and resonates with diverse audiences.

**Policy Analysis**:
- Challenge: Identify the core challenge
- Approach: Outline a concrete policy response
- Rationale: Explain why this approach aligns with both pragmatism and principle

**Call to Action**:
- Shared Values: Invoke American ideals (opportunity, responsibility, community) that unite across divides
- Exhortation: Deliver an inspirational call for collective commitment

**Soaring Peroration**: Close with an uplifting, memorable message that leaves the audience energized and hopeful.

## Output
Deliver the complete speech with section headers as shown above. Use rhetorical techniques authentically—let repetition, metaphor, and storytelling emerge naturally from the content rather than feeling forced. Emphasize vision and common ground over partisan attacks.
```
