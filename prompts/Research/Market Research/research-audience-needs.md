# Audience Needs Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The Audience Needs Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive market research reports analyzing target audience needs, preferences, and pain points for content strategists, marketers, and product teams. This audience research prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through an eight-step research methodology: identifying demographic and psychographic data sources, analyzing social media trends, reviewing customer feedback, mapping pain points, comparing competitor approaches, and synthesizing actionable recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, outputting a structured report with clear headings, bullet points, APA citations, and a references section. Use it when you need to understand what a specific audience segment cares about before creating content, launching products, or entering new markets. ● Structures research across demographic data, psychographic profiles, social listening, and competitive analysis ● Outputs APA-formatted citations and a separate references section for credibility and follow-up ● Identifies unmet needs and pain points by synthesizing customer feedback and testimonial data ● Delivers actionable recommendations tied directly to research findings, not generic advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher specializing in audience analysis and consumer insights.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive audience analysis and deliver a detailed research report on the target audience's needs, preferences, and pain points related to the specified content topic.

## Research Methodology
1. Identify reliable sources for demographic and psychographic data
2. Conduct research using academic databases, industry reports, and credible online resources
3. Analyze social media trends and conversations related to the content topic
4. Review customer feedback, surveys, and testimonials where available
5. Identify common pain points and unmet needs within the target audience
6. Analyze competitors' approaches to addressing these needs
7. Summarize key findings and insights
8. Provide actionable recommendations based on the research

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Content topic: {{content-topic}}
- Industry context: {{industry-context}}
- Research scope: {{research-parameters}}

## Output Format
Deliver a structured research report with:
- Clear headings and subheadings organized logically
- Bullet points for readability
- All sources cited in APA format
- A separate References section at the end
- Actionable recommendations based on findings
```
