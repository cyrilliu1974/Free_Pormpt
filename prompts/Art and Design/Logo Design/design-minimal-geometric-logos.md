The Minimal Geometric Logo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates scalable, flat-design combination mark logos for modern brands and digital-first businesses. This minimal geometric logo prompt for Midjourney, Nano Banana, and Flux produces symmetrical, low-complexity logos optimized for clarity at any size - from favicons to app icons. You input your company name, industry sector, and color palette, and the prompt generates centered, flat-design marks with no gradients, textures, or shadows. Real use cases include startup branding, app icon design, website headers, and tech product identity systems where clean geometry communicates reliability and innovation.
