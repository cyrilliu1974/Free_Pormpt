# Interior Photography Concept Generator for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Interior Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that produces editorial-quality interior architectural photographs for designers, real estate professionals, and content creators. This interior photography prompt for Midjourney and other AI image models generates magazine-style compositions featuring clean lines, balanced symmetry, and natural light. You specify the interior style (modern, minimalist, Scandinavian, etc.), room details (furniture, materials, colors, layout), and camera setup (angle, focal length, perspective), and the prompt produces professional architectural photography with negative space and editorial framing. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, using parameters optimized for high-quality architectural visualization. Designers use it to visualize client spaces before production, real estate marketers generate aspirational property imagery, and interior stylists create portfolio pieces that showcase specific design aesthetics. ● Produces architectural photographs with clean lines, negative space, and balanced symmetry that match editorial interior magazine standards. ● Accepts detailed room specifications including furniture type, materials, finishes, wall colors, and decorative elements for precise scene control. ● Generates compositions with natural window lighting and camera setups defined by your chosen angle, perspective, and focal approach. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images optimized for stylization, quality, and creative variation through Midjourney parameters. ## Prompt

```
Interior architectural photograph of a {{interior-style}} with {{room-details}}, shot as editorial interior-magazine composition from a {{camera-setup}}. Clean lines, negative space, balanced symmetry. Natural light from large windows. --ar 16:9 --s 250 --q 2 --c 10
```
