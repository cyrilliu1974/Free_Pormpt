# Question Keyword Research for Content Ideas

## 簡介

The Question Keyword Research for Content Ideas is a free AI prompt that researches search trends and generates SEO-optimized editorial ideas for content strategists, bloggers, and marketing teams. This question keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes search patterns and audience questions around any target keyword, then produces a categorized list of blog post and article ideas organized by content type - how-to guides, listicles, comparison articles, expert interviews, case studies, and beginner tutorials. Each idea includes a working title optimized for search intent and a rationale explaining its SEO and audience value. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking three variables: your target keyword, niche and audience description, and content goals. Reach for this prompt when planning editorial calendars, filling content gaps, or building a topic cluster strategy that balances search demand with audience needs. ● Identifies trending questions, search patterns, and content gaps for any keyword and niche ● Organizes ideas by content type for strategic editorial planning and production workflows ● Provides working titles optimized for search intent alongside SEO and audience appeal rationale ● Balances search demand with audience value to guide prioritization and resource allocation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in SEO-driven editorial planning.

## Task
Research and generate a comprehensive list of SEO-optimized blog post and article ideas for the given niche. Identify popular questions, search trends, and high-intent topics that align with the target audience's needs and the specified content goals.

## Context
**Keyword:** {{keyword}}
**Niche & Audience:** {{niche-and-audience}}
**Content Goals:** {{content-goals}}

## Process
1. Analyze search trends and question patterns around the keyword
2. Identify content gaps and high-opportunity topics
3. Develop ideas that balance search demand with audience value
4. Organize by content type for strategic planning

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of content ideas grouped by content type (how-to guides, listicles, comparison articles, expert interviews, case studies, beginner tutorials, etc.). For each idea, include:
- Working title optimized for search intent
- Brief rationale explaining its SEO and audience appeal

Format each category with a clear heading, then list ideas beneath it.
```
