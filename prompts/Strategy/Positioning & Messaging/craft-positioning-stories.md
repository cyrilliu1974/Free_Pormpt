# Product Positioning Story Generator

## 簡介

The Product Positioning Story Generator is a free AI prompt that builds complete product positioning narratives for marketers, founders, and business strategists. This positioning story prompt for ChatGPT guides text models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) through a proven 9-stage narrative framework: target audience identification, core problem definition, failed alternatives analysis, breaking-point articulation, product discovery moment, key feature highlighting, offer presentation, conversion trigger listing, and customer transformation描述. Marketing teams use it to turn technical product specs into emotionally resonant sales stories; founders apply it to craft pitch deck narratives; strategists deploy it to differentiate crowded market offerings. The prompt instructs the AI to make concrete, specific choices rather than generic recommendations, producing decision-ready positioning content that can be refined rather than starting from scratch. Reach for this tool when you need to translate features into benefits, map product capabilities to customer pain points, or create a cohesive narrative arc for landing pages, sales decks, and campaign messaging. ● Applies a professional 9-stage narrative structure from target audience through transformation ● Outputs specific pain points, failed alternatives, conversion triggers, and proof elements rather than vague claims ● Focuses the AI on making concrete feature and offer recommendations the user can immediately refine ● Generates markdown-formatted positioning stories ready for landing pages, decks, and campaign briefs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer specializing in product positioning strategy. Your expertise is crafting compelling positioning narratives that differentiate products and convert target audiences.

## Task
Create a complete product positioning story using the 9-stage narrative framework below. Be specific and decisive—avoid generalizations. Make concrete choices about features, offers, and details based on the business context provided; the user will refine your recommendations.

## Context
Business and product details:
{{business-description}}

## Output
Structure the positioning story as a persuasive narrative following this arc:

1. **Target Audience**: Identify the specific customer segment (demographics, psychographics, situation)
2. **Core Problem**: Define their primary problem and resulting pains (3 specific pain points)
3. **Failed Alternatives**: Describe what solutions they've tried and why those fall short (3 concrete drawbacks)
4. **Breaking Point**: Explain what goal they're close to abandoning and which benefits they'd miss (3 key benefits)
5. **Discovery**: Introduce the product as the turning-point solution
6. **Key Features**: Highlight 3 product features that directly address the audience's needs
7. **Irresistible Offer**: Present the sales offer or unique commercial proposition
8. **Conversion Triggers**: List 3 specific proof points or elements that drive immediate purchase decisions
9. **Transformation**: Describe how satisfied customers now spend their time (3 new activities or states)

Write in an attention-grabbing style that sells organically through storytelling. Use markdown formatting with clear section headers.
```
