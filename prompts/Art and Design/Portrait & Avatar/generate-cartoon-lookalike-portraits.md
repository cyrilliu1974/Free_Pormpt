# Cartoon Lookalike Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cartoon Lookalike Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates dual portraits showing a realistic photograph next to a stylized cartoon version of the same subject with identical features, clothing, and pose. This cartoon lookalike prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces side-by-side images where a photorealistic figure stands alongside their animated counterpart rendered in your chosen cartoon style - both striking the same exaggerated pose with matching haircuts, facial features, and outfits against a soft neutral background. The result is a before-and-after style visual that highlights personality through contrasting art styles while maintaining character consistency. Content creators use it for social media profile images, brand mascots, and eye-catching visual marketing that showcases both realism and creative stylization in one frame. Reach for this prompt when you want character art that bridges photography and illustration, or when you need comparison visuals that demonstrate artistic transformation while preserving recognizable identity. ● Produces photorealistic and cartoon versions of the same subject with identical facial features, hairstyle, and clothing in one image. ● Customize the subject, outfit, and cartoon style - from vibrant anime to bold illustration - while maintaining pose consistency across both figures. ● Delivers high-detail imagery with exaggerated animated stances, colorful stylization, and soft neutral backgrounds that emphasize both characters. ● Outputs cinematic lighting and professional composition suitable for social media avatars, marketing materials, and creative branding. ## Prompt

```
A realistic photograph of {{subject}} wearing {{outfit}}, striking an exaggerated, animated pose. Next to them is their exact lookalike, rendered in a lively {{cartoon-style}} with the same haircut, same facial features, and same exaggerated stance. The cartoon figure should be highly stylized, colorful, and dynamically rendered. The background is soft, minimal, and neutral to emphasize both figures. Cinematic lighting, high detail.
```
