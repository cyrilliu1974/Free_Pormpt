The Cinematic Portrait Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that produces candid, photorealistic desk portraits with professional film-inspired lighting and composition for content creators and visual marketers. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates an 8K-resolution image of a woman resting her cheek on her hand at a desk, gazing into space with a contemplative or custom mood. The prompt specifies 35mm lens characteristics, shallow depth of field, subtle grainy film texture, and a 9:16 mobile-optimized aspect ratio. Warm key light illuminates the subject's face while cool blue ambient light creates depth in the background, and the desk is scattered with notebooks and colorful stationery for organic visual storytelling. Variables let you adjust the mood (thoughtful, melancholic, inspired) and setting (home office, creative studio, cozy workspace) while maintaining professional photographic quality.
