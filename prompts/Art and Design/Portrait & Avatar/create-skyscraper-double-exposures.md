# Double Exposure Portrait Generator for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Double Exposure Portrait Generator for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that creates artistic double exposure images blending human profiles with cityscapes, nature, or abstract scenes. The prompt merges a subject silhouette with a secondary scene layer, adding bright bokeh lights and dramatic tone for depth and mood. This double exposure prompt for Midjourney works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, producing 3:4 aspect ratio portraits ideal for album covers, social media avatars, editorial illustrations, or creative portfolio pieces.
