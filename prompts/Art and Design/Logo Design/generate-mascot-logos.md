# Mascot Logo Generator for Sports Teams

## 簡介

The Mascot Logo Generator for Sports Teams is a free AI prompt that creates vector illustration mascot logos with custom character designs in a navy blue, crimson red, and white color scheme. This mascot logo prompt for Midjourney produces clean vector-style sports team logos featuring animals, characters, or creatures with specific emotions and actions you define. You simply replace the {{character-description}} variable with your desired mascot concept - such as "fierce eagle with spread wings" or "playful bear holding a basketball" - and Midjourney renders a sports-ready logo in a coordinated three-color palette. The prompt uses quality parameter optimization (--q 1) to balance rendering speed with output clarity, making it practical for iterating through multiple mascot concepts quickly. Sports teams, school athletic departments, esports organizations, and recreational leagues use it to explore mascot identities before commissioning final artwork. ● Accepts any character description - animals, mythical creatures, anthropomorphic figures - with emotion and action keywords to shape personality and pose. ● Outputs vector illustration style suitable for scaling to uniforms, banners, merchandise, and digital media without quality loss. ● Fixed three-color palette (navy blue, crimson red, white) ensures visual cohesion and simplifies adaptation to team branding guidelines. ● Quality flag set to balance iteration speed with detail, letting you test multiple mascot concepts in a single design session. ## Prompt

```
vector illustration logo, sports team mascot logo, {{character-description}}, navy blue, crimson red, and white colors --q 1
```
