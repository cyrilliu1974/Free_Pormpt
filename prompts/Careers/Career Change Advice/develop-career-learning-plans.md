# Career Learning Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Career Learning Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates personalized learning and development frameworks for professionals at any career stage. This career development prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target career path and outputs a structured four-section plan covering essential skills, knowledge areas, experiences, and continuous learning strategies. Each section includes focused subheadings with three specific, actionable bullet points tailored to the demands of your chosen field. Whether you're mapping out growth as a software engineer, transitioning into product management, or building expertise in data science, the prompt generates career-specific guidance rather than generic advice. It runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. This prompt is ideal for career coaches, HR professionals, learning and development consultants, and individuals designing their own professional growth roadmap. ● Produces a four-section framework covering key development areas specific to the career path you specify ● Generates two focused subheadings per section, each with three concrete action items or insights ● Emphasizes targeted, career-specific strategies rather than one-size-fits-all advice ● Formats output in a clear hierarchical structure ready for immediate implementation or client presentation ## Prompt
