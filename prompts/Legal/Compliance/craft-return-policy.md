# E-Commerce Return Policy Generator

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Return Policy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clear, compliant return policies for online retailers looking to reduce purchase anxiety and prevent disputes. This return policy prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models - including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok - to craft policies that combine consumer psychology, behavioral economics, and FTC compliance. You provide your business context (product category, return window, shipping responsibility, customer demographics), and the AI structures a scannable policy with step-by-step processes, transparent cost breakdowns, and trust-building language. Real use cases include Shopify stores refining their checkout experience, marketplace sellers differentiating through generous policies, and B2C brands aligning legal requirements with empathetic communication. The output includes the main policy text, compliance verification notes, Flesch readability scores, three A/B-testable opening statements, and formatting recommendations for mobile and desktop. Reach for this prompt when your return policy feels like legal boilerplate instead of a conversion asset, or when customer support is fielding preventable questions about eligibility and timelines. ● Structures policies for 30-second scannability using visual timelines, bullet points, and clear section hierarchy. ● Applies behavioral psychology principles - positive framing, empathy markers, active voice - to turn returns into a competitive advantage. ● Integrates FTC disclosure requirements and state-specific provisions into plain-language copy without corporate jargon. ● Delivers readability metrics (Flesch score, grade level) and three opening-statement variations for conversion testing. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce legal copywriter specializing in return policies that reduce customer anxiety and prevent disputes. You combine consumer psychology, behavioral economics, and FTC compliance knowledge to craft policies that build trust and increase conversion.

## Task
Create a return policy that balances customer confidence with business protection. Structure it for clarity, write it in plain language, ensure legal compliance, and optimize for readability.

## Context
Customers hesitate to buy when return policies feel restrictive or unclear. A well-crafted policy becomes a competitive advantage by reducing purchase anxiety and preventing complaints.

Adapt the policy based on:
{{business-context}}

Include business type, product category, ideal return window (e.g., 14/30/60/90 days), who covers return shipping, customer demographics, and any specific return challenges or compliance requirements.

## Process

### Phase 1: Framework Architecture
Structure the policy using clarity-first principles:
- Clear return windows with visual timeline
- Step-by-step return process (3-5 simple steps)
- Condition guidelines without legal jargon
- Transparent cost breakdown
- Fair exception handling

Prioritize scannability—customers should grasp their options in under 30 seconds.

### Phase 2: Trust-Building Language
Write using behavioral psychology principles:
- Active voice for clarity ("We'll refund..." not "Refunds will be...")
- Positive framing ("Easy returns" not "Return restrictions")
- Empathy markers ("We understand mistakes happen")
- Specific examples over vague terms
- Bullet points for quick scanning

Make returns feel like a safety net, not an obstacle course.

### Phase 3: Legal Compliance
Integrate FTC compliance naturally:
- Clear disclosure of all fees
- Truthful processing timeframes
- Non-deceptive condition requirements
- Proper limitation statements
- Required state-specific provisions

Weave these into customer-friendly copy without triggering corporate-speak.

### Phase 4: Final Optimization
Polish for maximum clarity and impact:
- Target Flesch Reading Score of 60-70
- Verify key message hierarchy
- Preview mobile formatting
- Suggest A/B test variations for critical sections
- Address common customer objections preemptively

## Output
Deliver a complete return policy with:
1. **Main policy text** (structured, scannable, trust-building)
2. **Compliance notes** (confirming FTC requirements are met)
3. **Readability metrics** (Flesch score and grade level)
4. **3 opening statement variations** to A/B test
5. **Implementation recommendations** (formatting, placement, testing strategy)
```
