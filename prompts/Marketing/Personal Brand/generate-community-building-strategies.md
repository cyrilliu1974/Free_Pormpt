# Community-Building Strategy Prompt for Personal Brands

## 簡介

The Community-Building Strategy Prompt for Personal Brands is a free AI prompt that creates tailored plans for building and growing engaged online communities around your brand. This community-building strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) to act as an expert community manager, producing a structured strategy document that covers six core pillars: community overview with audience profiling, content strategy with calendar frameworks, engagement tactics including rituals and live events, moderation guidelines with crisis protocols, growth initiatives from onboarding to referral programs, and measurable KPIs. Real use cases include launching a membership community for a coaching brand, revitalizing engagement in a SaaS user forum, or planning expansion for a creator's Discord server. The prompt emphasizes actionable, specific tactics over generic advice and delivers outputs formatted for immediate implementation. ● Produces a six-section strategy document with brand positioning, niche analysis, and target audience profiles ● Delivers content calendars, user-generated content tactics, and engagement rituals tailored to your specific brand and niche ● Includes moderation frameworks with community guidelines, team structure, and crisis management protocols ● Provides growth blueprints covering onboarding flows, referral programs, cross-promotion tactics, and trackable KPIs for membership, engagement, and retention ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert community manager specializing in building engaged online communities around brands.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive community strategy tailored to the brand and niche provided. Cover all aspects of building, engaging, moderating, and growing an active, loyal online community.

## Context
Brand and niche: {{brand-and-niche}}

Provide specific, actionable tactics—not generic advice. Focus on innovative approaches with clear objectives and measurable outcomes. Prioritize high-impact tactics for growth and engagement.

## Output
Return a structured strategy plan organized into the following sections:

**1. Community Overview**
- Brand positioning
- Niche analysis
- Target audience profile
- Community goals

**2. Content Strategy**
- Content themes (aligned to audience interests)
- Content formats (text, video, polls, AMAs, etc.)
- Content calendar framework
- User-generated content tactics

**3. Engagement Strategy**
- Conversation starters and prompts
- Community rituals (weekly threads, challenges, recognition)
- Influencer and brand partnerships
- Live events (webinars, Q&As, meetups)
- Engagement metrics (comments per post, response rate, active members)

**4. Moderation Strategy**
- Community guidelines
- Moderation team structure
- Moderation tools and automation
- Crisis management protocol

**5. Growth Strategy**
- Member onboarding process
- Referral and advocacy programs
- Social media cross-promotion
- Paid acquisition tactics
- Growth metrics (new members, activation rate, referral rate)

**6. Key Performance Indicators**
- Membership growth
- Engagement rate
- Retention rate
- Brand sentiment score

Use **bold** for key terms and metrics. Structure with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for easy scanning.
```
