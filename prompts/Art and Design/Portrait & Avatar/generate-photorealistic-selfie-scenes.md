# Photorealistic Selfie Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Selfie Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates ultra-detailed 8K selfie scenes pairing real people with fictional characters in custom locations. The prompt produces cinematic compositions where both subjects stand side-by-side as if taking a selfie together, complete with detailed appearances, professional lighting, and environmental context such as a movie theater backdrop. This photorealistic selfie prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by accepting three variables: a real person (with age, ethnicity, hair, clothing, and features), a fictional character (described the same way), and a location setting. Content creators, fan communities, and social media marketers use it to generate impossible photo moments that blend reality with fantasy without green screens or complex editing software. ● Produces side-by-side selfie compositions with one real person and one fictional character in the same frame. ● Includes cinematic lighting, 8K resolution, and environmental details like movie theater screens in the background. ● Accepts detailed appearance inputs for both subjects (age, ethnicity, hair, clothing, distinctive features) and custom location settings. ● Generates shareable visual content for fan art, social media posts, creative storytelling, and marketing campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic selfie scene, ultra-detailed 8K quality, cinematic lighting. {{real-person}} and {{fictional-character}} standing side-by-side taking a selfie together in {{location}}, large movie screen in the background showing an action scene with multiple characters in dynamic poses.

Provide each character as: name, appearance details (age, ethnicity, hair, clothing, distinctive features).
```
