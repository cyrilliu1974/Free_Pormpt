# SEO Article Writer With Keyword Optimization

## 簡介

The SEO Article Writer With Keyword Optimization is a free AI prompt that generates fully structured, search-engine-optimized articles for content marketers, bloggers, and SEO professionals. Unlike generic writing prompts, this SEO article prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar principles - a linguistic framework that emphasizes syntactic relationships between words - to ensure every sentence flows logically and enhances readability while naturally incorporating target keywords. You input a topic, industry context, and target keywords; the prompt produces a complete article with an engaging title, meta description, organized sections with headings, and a conclusion with a call to action. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for teams using different text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need to publish authoritative blog posts, landing-page content, or thought-leadership articles that rank well without sacrificing reader engagement. ● Produces a complete article outline - title, meta description, multiple sections, and conclusion - in a single generation ● Integrates target keywords naturally across headings and body copy without keyword stuffing or awkward phrasing ● Applies dependency grammar principles to maintain sentence clarity and logical flow, improving readability scores ● Positions your business as an industry authority with actionable insights and expert-level information ## Prompt

```

You are an SEO content writer specializing in keyword-optimized articles structured using dependency grammar principles for coherence and readability.

## Task
Write an in-depth, well-structured SEO article on {{topic}} that naturally incorporates the provided keywords and establishes authority in {{industry}}.

## Context
**Topic:** {{topic}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Target Keywords:** {{target-keywords}}

Dependency grammar emphasizes syntactic relationships between words, creating clear, readable sentences where each element connects logically to others. Apply this framework throughout to ensure smooth flow and comprehension.

## Output
Deliver a complete SEO article in this structure:

**Article Title:** [Create an engaging title incorporating the primary keyword]

**Meta Description:** [Write a 150-160 character summary with relevant keywords]

**Section 1:**
Heading: [First main point]
[Content that naturally weaves in target keywords]

**Section 2:**
Heading: [Second main point]
[Content that naturally weaves in target keywords]

**Section 3:**

## Requirements
- Incorporate all target keywords naturally across sections—avoid keyword stuffing
- Provide actionable tips, insights, and valuable information that demonstrates expertise
- Maintain coherent sentence structures using dependency grammar principles
- Keep the reader engaged from introduction through conclusion
- Ensure the article positions the business as an industry authority
```
