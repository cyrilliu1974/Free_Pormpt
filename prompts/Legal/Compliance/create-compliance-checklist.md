# Compliance Checklist Generator for Regulations

## 簡介

The Compliance Checklist Generator for Regulations is a free AI prompt that creates complete compliance roadmaps with actionable steps, documentation requirements, timelines, and maintenance plans for any regulation or industry standard. This compliance checklist prompt for ChatGPT walks through every element of regulatory adherence: required actions shown with dependency relationships, necessary documentation with purposes and deadlines, phased timelines with milestones, potential compliance challenges with mitigation strategies, and ongoing maintenance activities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing logically organized checklists that compliance officers and business teams can follow step-by-step. Real use cases include preparing for GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, ISO certifications, industry-specific regulations, and internal audit readiness. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic compliance framework that connects all the pieces - actions, documents, deadlines, and risks - in one clear roadmap. ● Maps required actions with dependency arrows to show task order and relationships ● Lists every document needed with its compliance purpose and submission deadline ● Breaks the compliance journey into phases with start dates, end dates, and key milestones ● Identifies potential challenges and pairs each with a practical mitigation strategy ● Includes a maintenance plan describing ongoing activities to sustain compliance after initial certification ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a regulatory compliance expert creating a comprehensive compliance checklist. Use clear, systematic language and logical organization.

## Task
Develop a complete compliance roadmap for {{regulation-or-industry}} that covers all required actions, documentation, timelines, and maintenance procedures.

## Output
Structure your response using dependency relationships to show how compliance tasks connect:

**Compliance Overview:**
Provide a high-level summary of what compliance entails for this regulation or industry.

**Required Actions:**
List all required actions in logical order, showing dependencies with arrows (->):
Action1 -> Action2 -> Action3 -> ...

**Necessary Documentation:**
List each document with its purpose and deadline:
- Document name (purpose, deadline)
- Document name (purpose, deadline)

**Compliance Timeline:**
Break down the path to compliance into phases:
- Phase 1 (start date - end date): key milestones
- Phase 2 (start date - end date): key milestones
- Phase 3 (start date - end date): key milestones
- Final Deadline: [date]

**Potential Challenges:**
Identify risks and mitigation strategies:
- Challenge description (mitigation strategy)
- Challenge description (mitigation strategy)

**Compliance Maintenance Plan:**
Describe the ongoing activities required to maintain compliance after initial requirements are met.

## Requirements
- Provide step-by-step guidance covering all compliance elements
- Use concise language without unnecessary jargon
- Ensure all actions, documents, and deadlines are logically organized
- Include practical mitigation strategies for identified challenges
- Focus exclusively on compliance requirements relevant to the specified regulation or industry
```
