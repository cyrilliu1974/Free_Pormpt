# Fiction Plot Outline Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Fiction Plot Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive story blueprints for fiction writers working in any genre. This fiction plot outline prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to build a structured narrative framework covering setting introduction, main characters with motivations and backgrounds, inciting incident, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution, thematic messages, and character development arcs. Writers provide story parameters - genre, setting, protagonist background, central conflict, and target audience - and receive a complete outline ready for drafting. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is designed for novelists, screenwriters, and storytellers who need a narrative architecture that balances pacing, suspense, and character growth. It works equally well for literary fiction, genre novels, short stories, and serialized narratives. ● Structures every essential plot element from inciting incident through resolution, ensuring no narrative gaps ● Develops protagonist, antagonist, and supporting characters with clear motivations, backgrounds, and transformation arcs ● Balances external conflicts (antagonist, environment) with internal struggles (moral dilemmas, personal growth) ● Identifies and weaves thematic messages throughout the narrative for cohesive storytelling ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced fiction writer and storyteller who crafts captivating narratives with strong pacing, suspense, and character growth.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plot outline for a novel or story that engrosses readers from beginning to end. The outline should include pivotal plot points, character arcs, thematic elements, and a vivid setting that enriches the narrative.

## Context
Genre and story parameters:
{{story-parameters}}

Provide: genre, setting (time period and location), main character background, central conflict or theme, and target audience.

## Output
Deliver a structured plot outline containing:

**Setting Introduction**: Describe the unique setting where the story unfolds, including relevant historical or cultural context that enhances the narrative and provides backdrop for character actions.

**Main Characters**: Introduce the protagonist, antagonist, and key supporting characters with their personalities, motivations, backgrounds, and roles. Show how their desires and histories set up conflict and development.

**Inciting Incident**: Define the event or realization that disrupts the status quo and propels the protagonist into the main narrative conflict, challenging their goals or beliefs.

**Rising Action and Conflicts**: Outline the series of escalating challenges the protagonist faces—both external (against antagonist or environment) and internal (personal growth, moral dilemmas)—as they pursue their goals.

**Climax**: Detail the moment of highest tension where the protagonist confronts the main conflict directly, creating a turning point that leads to significant change or realization.

**Falling Action**: Describe how the aftermath of the climax begins resolving the story's tensions and conflicts, leading toward resolution.

**Resolution**: Explain how conflicts are finally resolved and the state of characters and world at story's end. Include any open questions if part of a larger series.

**Themes and Messages**: Highlight the underlying themes explored through the narrative (redemption, courage, identity, power, human connection, etc.).

**Character Development**: Elaborate on how the protagonist and key characters transform throughout the story—their growth, lessons learned, and evolution of initial desires and goals.

The outline should serve as a complete blueprint for writing an engaging, memorable story that resonates with the target audience.
```
