# Frosted Glass Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Frosted Glass Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates premium editorial-style product images with hands artistically blurred behind frosted glass while keeping the product in razor-sharp focus. This frosted glass product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces studio-quality visuals where a single product floats perfectly centered in frame with ultra-sharp detail, while human hands behind a frosted glass surface create an emotional, human connection without distraction. The prompt controls every aspect of the composition: soft diffused studio lighting, minimal shadows, layered depth of field, and intentional blur applied only to background elements. It's designed for e-commerce brands, marketing teams, and designers who need luxury commercial imagery that balances technical precision with poetic visual storytelling. ● Centers the product with ultra-sharp micro-contrast while diffusing hands behind frosted glass for artistic depth. ● Applies soft frontal studio lighting with gentle top wash, minimal shadows, and controlled highlights on product surfaces only. ● Produces airy, minimal, modern visuals with high negative space and layered depth of field for luxury brand aesthetics. ● Allows customization of studio environment, background style, and aesthetic mood through three simple variables. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic high-end editorial product photography. A single product object perfectly centered and floating in frame, captured at natural scale in an upright orientation. Ultra-sharp focus with high micro-contrast on the product only; everything else intentionally diffused.

Environment: {{studio-environment}}
Background: {{background-style}}
Mood: {{aesthetic-mood}}

Two human hands positioned behind a frosted glass surface, creating a milky diffusion blur with softened edges and subtle light bloom. Hands are semi-opaque, intentionally out of focus, making a slow delicate gesture with no contact with the product. The frosted glass effect applies only to the hands.

Soft diffused studio lighting from frontal with gentle top wash. Extremely soft minimal shadows. Clean controlled highlights on the product surface only. Low overall contrast, high contrast on product. Premium tactile material rendering with clean intentional reflections, no imperfections.

Visual language: minimal, airy, modern, poetic. Emotion: care, purity, precision. Strong subject isolation with layered depth of field and very high negative space.

Camera: 85mm lens, f/2.0 aperture, eye-level angle, no motion. Luxury commercial retouching polish, no grain. Color grading consistent with the aesthetic mood and background style. Ultra-high resolution output with razor-sharp product and diffused background. No artifacts.
```
