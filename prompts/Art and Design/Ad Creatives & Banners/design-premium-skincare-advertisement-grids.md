# Premium Skincare Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Premium Skincare Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cohesive four-panel commercial layouts for beauty and wellness brands. This skincare advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 1:1 aspect ratio grids with four coordinated panels: a hero studio product shot with clean natural lighting, an outdoor lifestyle application scene, a macro texture detail of the product being dispensed, and an active moment showing the product in use within customizable settings like beaches, mountains, urban spas, or botanical gardens. Each panel maintains consistent color grading, bright natural light, and minimalist composition that communicates trust and modern wellness branding. Brands use it to generate campaign-ready visual assets for Instagram carousels, Facebook ads, website hero sections, and digital marketing materials without coordinating multi-location photo shoots. This prompt is ideal for skincare brands, beauty marketers, social media managers, and creative teams who need polished, professional product photography across multiple contexts in a single cohesive layout. ● Produces four equal panels showcasing product packaging, real-world application, texture close-ups, and aspirational lifestyle moments in a unified 2×2 layout. ● Customizable by product name and setting context (beach, mountain, urban spa, botanical garden) while maintaining premium wellness aesthetics and natural lighting. ● Outputs high-resolution grids suitable for social media carousels, paid ad campaigns, e-commerce banners, and brand storytelling content. ● Avoids common AI pitfalls like distorted labels, harsh shadows, greasy skin appearance, and cluttered backgrounds through specific composition and lighting guidance. ## Prompt

```
Professional commercial skincare advertisement in 2×2 grid format, 1:1 aspect ratio. Four equal panels showcasing {{product-name}} in {{setting}} (beach, mountain, urban spa, or botanical garden).

**Panel 1 – Hero Product Shot**
Studio shot of {{product-name}} bottle or tube on clean surface. Strong side natural light with soft shadows. Minimalist background in white or setting-appropriate neutral tones. Clear, readable product label. Sharp focus. Mood: trust, protection, clarity.

**Panel 2 – Lifestyle Application**
Outdoor scene of healthy-skinned adult applying product to skin in {{setting}} environment. Bright natural daylight. Relaxed expression. Aspirational and confident mood.

**Panel 3 – Texture & Detail**
Macro shot of product being dispensed onto skin or fingers. Shallow depth of field highlighting texture and consistency. Clinical yet luxurious, care-focused aesthetic.

**Panel 4 – Active Lifestyle Moment**
Cinematic shot of adult enjoying {{setting}} activity with product visible in hand or bag. Warm natural highlights. Fresh energy. Protected and confident mood.

**Visual Style**: Modern skincare advertising. Bright, clean, fresh natural light. Minimalist composition. High realism. Premium wellness branding. Professional commercial photography with balanced exposure and sharp clarity on product labels. Color grading consistent with {{setting}}—natural, fresh tones.

**Mood**: Fresh, protective, healthy, clean, trustworthy, modern.

**Avoid**: Harsh shadows, greasy skin appearance, distorted packaging, unreadable labels, cluttered background, CGI look, cartoon style, oversaturation, watermarks, text overlays, fake branding.
```
