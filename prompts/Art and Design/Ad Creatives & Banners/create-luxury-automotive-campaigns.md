# Luxury Automotive Campaign Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Automotive Campaign Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic three-panel automotive advertisements for luxury car brands and creative agencies. This luxury car ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a horizontal 1×3 grid in 16:9 aspect ratio, each frame in 3:4 format, with ultra-realistic rendering, high contrast, shallow depth of field, and premium commercial color grading. The prompt builds three distinct compositions: a fashion-editorial hero shot of a model beside the vehicle in a desert setting at golden hour, a dynamic low-angle driving scene showcasing interior details and motion, and an ultra-detailed macro close-up of the brand emblem with cinematic lighting. Use this prompt when you need blockbuster-quality visuals for automotive marketing campaigns, social media ads, or high-end brand presentations. ● Frame 1 delivers a power-and-elegance hero pose with a model in black leather beside the vehicle in a golden-hour desert landscape. ● Frame 2 captures dynamic motion with a low-angle driving shot showing steering wheel, dashboard, leather interior, and motion blur on the road. ● Frame 3 provides an iconic brand moment with a razor-sharp macro close-up of the vehicle logo, reflecting light and environment with cinematic depth of field. ● All three frames maintain consistent lighting, color grading, and visual storytelling that emphasizes modern luxury, bold femininity, and timeless automotive elegance. ## Prompt

```
Create a 1×3 horizontal grid luxury automotive advertisement in 16:9 aspect ratio. Each frame is 3:4. Ultra-realistic cinematic rendering with high contrast, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, and premium commercial color grading.

**Frame 1 – Hero Pose**
A fashion-editorial shot in a vast desert at golden hour. An extremely beautiful brunette woman with long, flowing wavy hair stands confidently beside a {{vehicle}}, wearing a sensual black leather dress with sleek, glossy finish. She wears stylish sunglasses, one hand on her hip, the other holding the car key at her side. Warm sunlight creates dramatic highlights and shadows across her figure and the vehicle. Distant mountains and dusty road softly blurred in background. Mood: power, elegance, independence, premium luxury.

**Frame 2 – Dynamic Drive**
A cinematic shot of the same woman driving the {{vehicle}}, captured from a low front-side angle. Her hands grip the steering wheel confidently, sunglasses on, hair subtly moving with the wind through the open window or sunroof. Visible interior details: leather seats, metallic accents, digital dashboard. Motion blur on the road adds speed and energy while the car remains sharp and dominant. Mood: freedom, control, modern luxury, cinematic motion.

**Frame 3 – Brand Detail**
Ultra-detailed macro close-up of the {{vehicle}} logo on the grille or hood. The chrome emblem is razor-sharp, reflecting warm desert light and environment. Shallow depth of field isolates the logo. Subtle dust particles and reflections add realism. Mood: prestige, craftsmanship, iconic branding.

The {{vehicle}} brand is clearly visible and dominant, seamlessly integrated into every frame. Aesthetic: modern luxury, bold femininity, timeless automotive elegance, blockbuster-level production quality.
```
