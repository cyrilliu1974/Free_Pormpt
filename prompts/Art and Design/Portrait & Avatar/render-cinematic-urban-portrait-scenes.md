# Cinematic Urban Night Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Urban Night Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-realistic night photography scenes featuring a subject against vehicles in moody urban environments with dramatic lighting and wet pavement reflections. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney produces 8k resolution images with smartphone computational photography aesthetics - natural skin tones, accurate facial proportions, and HDR glow from neon signs and colorful storefronts reflecting across rain-soaked streets. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you customize the vehicle type, urban location, and outfit details while maintaining photorealistic textures and authentic color science.
