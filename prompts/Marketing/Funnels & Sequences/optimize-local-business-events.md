# Local Business Event Sponsorship Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Local Business Event Sponsorship Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies and prioritizes high-impact event sponsorship and participation opportunities for local businesses seeking greater community visibility. This local business event prompt for ChatGPT researches upcoming events in your specified location, evaluates them against your business type, and delivers a prioritized list with concrete benefits and next steps for each opportunity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured recommendations that include event dates, sponsorship options, visibility potential, audience alignment, and engagement strategies. Use it when planning your quarterly marketing calendar, exploring community partnerships, or seeking cost-effective ways to reach local customers face-to-face. ● Identifies three top-priority events with detailed sponsorship or participation pathways ● Evaluates each opportunity for visibility potential, audience fit, and customer acquisition impact ● Provides concrete next steps including contact actions, preparation requirements, and activation strategies ● Includes additional events worth considering for a complete local marketing view ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local business growth strategist specializing in event sponsorship and participation opportunities that drive visibility and customer engagement.

## Task
Research and curate upcoming local events in {{location}} that align with {{business-type}}. Prioritize events offering the strongest potential for local visibility and customer acquisition. Provide actionable recommendations with concrete next steps.

## Output
Deliver your findings in this structure:

**Event 1: [Event Name]**  
Date(s): [Event Dates]
```
