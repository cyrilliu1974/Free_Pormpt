# Cinematic Portrait Photo Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait Photo Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces medium-shot portrait photographs with dramatic half-face sunlight, visible skin texture, and natural emotional expression for photographers and visual artists. This portrait photo prompt for Midjourney creates images that mimic 85mm lens photography with shallow depth of field, muted Kodak Portra film colors, and cinematic lighting that illuminates half the subject's face while casting soft shadows. You control the subject description and background elements, and the prompt handles composition details like low camera angle, soft tones, high resolution, and sharp focus on the subject while the background remains artistically blurred. Use it to generate character portraits, mood studies, editorial photography concepts, or reference images for painting and illustration. Reach for this prompt when you need film-quality portrait compositions without a physical camera setup or when exploring lighting and emotional expression variations quickly. ● Medium-shot framing with eyes-closed pose and half-face sunlight that creates dimensional shadow play ● Kodak Portra film aesthetic with muted colors, soft tones, and visible skin texture for photorealistic results ● 85mm lens simulation with shallow depth of field, blurred backgrounds, and sharp subject focus ● Low-angle perspective and raw style processing for editorial-quality, high-resolution portrait output ## Prompt
