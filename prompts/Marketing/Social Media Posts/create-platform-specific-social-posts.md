# Platform-Specific Social Media Post Generator

## 簡介

The Platform-Specific Social Media Post Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete social media launch campaigns tailored to Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram for digital marketers and product teams. This social media post prompt for ChatGPT takes your product details and target audience information, then generates three unique posts per platform - each optimized for that platform's format, character limits, and best practices. It delivers engaging copy that connects product features to customer benefits, recommends appropriate visual formats (images, videos, carousels), includes platform-specific hashtags for Twitter and Instagram, and crafts compelling calls-to-action that drive clicks and conversions. The prompt runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible regardless of your preferred text model. Use it when launching a new product, running seasonal campaigns, or coordinating messaging across multiple social channels while maintaining brand consistency. ● Produces three posts each for Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram with platform-appropriate formatting and character counts ● Recommends specific visual formats (image, video, carousel) matched to each post's content and platform ● Generates relevant hashtags for discoverability on Twitter and Instagram while maintaining brand voice ● Includes measurable KPIs - reach, engagement rate, click-through rate, and conversion goals - to track campaign performance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing expert specializing in multi-platform social media campaigns and product launches.

## Task
Develop a complete social media launch campaign with platform-specific posts for Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram. Create engaging content that highlights product features, addresses audience needs, and drives conversions through compelling calls-to-action.

## Context
Product & Market:
{{product-details}}
(Include: product name, description, key features, and industry context)

Target Audience:
{{target-audience}}
(Include: demographics—age, gender, location—and psychographics—interests, pain points, goals)

## Requirements
- Tailor each post to its platform's format, character limits, and best practices
- Use engaging copy that connects features to customer benefits
- Recommend high-quality visual formats (image, video, carousel) appropriate to each post
- Include strong CTAs that encourage engagement, clicks, or purchases
- Add relevant hashtags for Twitter and Instagram to maximize discoverability
- Maintain consistent messaging and visual identity across all platforms
- Avoid overly promotional language; ensure claims are accurate
- Follow platform advertising guidelines

## Output
Structure your campaign as follows:

**Product Overview:**
[Product Name]: [Brief Description]
Key Features:
- [Feature 1]
- [Feature 2]
- [Feature 3]

**Target Audience:**
Demographics: [Summary]
Psychographics: [Summary]

**Facebook Posts (3):**
1. Copy: [Post text]
   Visual: [Image/Video description]
   CTA: [Call-to-action]

2. [Repeat structure]

3. [Repeat structure]

**Twitter Posts (3):**
1. Copy: [Tweet text with hashtags]
   Visual: [Image/Video/Link description]

2. [Repeat structure]

3. [Repeat structure]

**Instagram Posts (3):**
1. Copy: [Post caption]
   Visual: [Image/Video/Carousel description]
   Hashtags: [List relevant hashtags]

2. [Repeat structure]

3. [Repeat structure]

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Reach Goal: [Target number]
- Engagement Rate Goal: [Target percentage]
- Click-Through Rate Goal: [Target percentage]
- Conversion Goal: [Target number/percentage]
```
