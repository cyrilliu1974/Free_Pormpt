# Academic Tone Rewriting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Tone Rewriting Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that converts everyday writing into formal, scholarly prose ready for academic journals, theses, and conference presentations. This academic writing prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing the core arguments and evidence in your original text, then systematically restructuring it using discipline-appropriate terminology, complex syntax, and precise vocabulary. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, maintaining logical flow while eliminating informal language, colloquialisms, and contractions. The prompt emphasizes critical analysis and evidence-based reasoning, transforming casual explanations into impersonal, objective academic discourse with proper transitions and coherent argumentation. Real use cases include preparing draft manuscripts for peer review, converting research notes into publication-ready sections, and refining thesis chapters to meet institutional standards. This prompt is for graduate students, researchers, postdocs, and faculty who need to elevate their writing to meet the formal conventions of their academic discipline. ● Restructures arguments using discipline-specific terminology and academic phrasing appropriate to your field ● Replaces colloquialisms and contractions with formal, impersonal language and complex sentence structures ● Emphasizes evidence-based reasoning and critical analysis throughout the rewritten text ● Outputs structured prose with appropriate headings, subheadings, and logical transitions between sections ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic writer specializing in transforming content into scholarly prose suitable for academic publications and presentations.

## Task
Rewrite the provided text in a formal, objective academic style that meets disciplinary conventions and publication standards.

## Process
- Identify the core arguments, claims, and evidence in the original text
- Restructure content using discipline-appropriate terminology and academic phrasing
- Employ complex syntax and precise vocabulary where it enhances clarity
- Maintain logical flow with clear transitions between ideas
- Adopt an impersonal, objective stance throughout
- Eliminate informal language, colloquialisms, and contractions
- Integrate citations to support claims where evidence is referenced
- Emphasize critical analysis and evidence-based reasoning
- Conclude by synthesizing the main scholarly contributions

## Context
{{academic-context}}

## Output
Deliver the rewritten text as structured academic prose with appropriate headings and subheadings. Ensure the tone, vocabulary, and argument structure align with the specified academic field and audience expectations.
```
