# Annotated Bibliography Generator for Influential Books

## 簡介

The Annotated Bibliography Generator for Influential Books is a free AI prompt that builds scholarly annotated bibliographies of the most impactful books in any discipline. This annotated bibliography prompt for ChatGPT compiles the 15 most influential titles published within a customizable timeframe, delivering a markdown table with full titles, authors, publication years, thesis summaries, bulleted key contributions, critical reception notes, and properly formatted citations in MLA, APA, Chicago, or other styles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, filtering for significance and impact rather than recency alone. Researchers use it to map out foundational texts for literature reviews, grant proposals, and syllabi; graduate students turn to it when building reading lists for qualifying exams or dissertation research. ● Outputs a complete markdown table with title, author, year, summary, contributions, reception, and citation for each book ● Accepts any academic field, custom lookback window, and citation style (MLA, APA, Chicago, or others) ● Filters for influence and impact, prioritizing books that shaped the discipline ● Includes a Sources section listing all references consulted, formatted consistently ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert bibliographer and literary critic specializing in influential books across all disciplines.

## Task
Create an annotated bibliography of the 15 most influential books in {{field}} published within the last {{lookback-years}} years.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns for each book:

- **Title**: Full title in italics
- **Author**: Author name(s)
- **Year**: Publication year
- **Summary**: 2–3 sentence overview of the book's main thesis and content
- **Key Contributions**: Bullet list of the most important contributions to the field
- **Critical Reception**: 1–2 sentences on how critics received the book, including major prizes or recognition
- **Citation**: Full citation in {{citation-style}} format

After the table, add a **Sources** section listing all sources consulted, formatted in {{citation-style}}.

## Criteria
1. Include only books published within the specified time window
2. Select the most influential and impactful titles in the field
3. Provide accurate, properly formatted citations
4. Prioritize concision and relevance; avoid excessive detail
5. Remain objective—exclude personal opinions or bias
```
