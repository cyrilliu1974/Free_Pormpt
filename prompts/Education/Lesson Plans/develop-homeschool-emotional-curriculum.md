# Homeschool Social-Emotional Learning Curriculum Builder

## 簡介

The Homeschool Social-Emotional Learning Curriculum Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured lesson plans teaching emotional intelligence and social skills for homeschool educators. This social-emotional learning curriculum prompt for ChatGPT produces a three-column table mapping specific skills (empathy, conflict resolution, self-awareness) to concrete activities (role-playing exercises, discussion strategies, reflection tasks) and measurable assessment methods. You provide the student age range and homeschool context, and the prompt generates age-appropriate activities feasible for home-based learning. Homeschool parents use it to systematically integrate emotional intelligence instruction alongside academic subjects, ensuring children develop communication, relationship, and self-regulation skills. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a clear framework for teaching social skills at home without a traditional classroom structure. ● Organizes emotional intelligence instruction into skill, activity, and assessment columns for easy implementation ● Includes role-playing scenarios, conflict resolution techniques, and empathy exercises tailored to your student's age ● Adapts to different homeschool contexts, from single-child households to co-op learning environments ● Provides practical evaluation methods so parents can track social-emotional growth over time ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert homeschool educator specializing in social-emotional learning curricula.

## Task

Design a comprehensive strategy for teaching emotional intelligence and social skills in a homeschool setting. Create a table with three columns:

1. **Skill** - The specific emotional intelligence or social skill being taught
2. **Activity** - A detailed description of the exercise, strategy, or activity used to teach the skill
3. **Assessment Method** - How the student's mastery of the skill will be evaluated

For each skill, provide:
- At least one engaging role-playing exercise
- One practical conflict resolution strategy
- One empathy-building activity

## Context

{{student-age-range}}

{{homeschool-context}}

## Output

Deliver a well-organized table without XML tags or additional formatting. Ensure all activities are age-appropriate, engaging, and feasible to implement in the described homeschool environment.
```
