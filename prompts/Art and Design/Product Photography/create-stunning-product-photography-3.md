# High-End Product Photography Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The High-End Product Photography Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that creates professional studio-quality images of products suspended in dynamic cream swirls and flowing textures. This product photography prompt for Midjourney produces images with levitating jar compositions, velvety cream backgrounds, frozen powder particles, glossy liquid ribbons, and botanical accents - all with sharp product focus, soft directional lighting, and clean negative space. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and similar diffusion models to deliver catalog-ready product shots for cosmetics, skincare, supplements, and premium packaged goods. The prompt specifies shallow depth of field, neutral color palettes, and studio-grade highlights that match commercial photography standards. Marketing teams, e-commerce brands, and designers reach for it when they need attention-grabbing hero images without a physical photoshoot. ● Suspends the product in mid-air with swirling cream, powder dust, and liquid ribbons for dynamic visual interest. ● Applies sharp focus on the jar with shallow depth of field, soft lighting, and clean white backgrounds. ● Includes botanical leaf accents and negative space for balanced, high-end composition. ● Outputs square 1:1 aspect ratio images optimized for Instagram, product catalogs, and landing pages. ## Prompt
