# Employee Advocacy Program Builder

## 簡介

The Employee Advocacy Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates purpose-driven employee advocacy strategies for HR leaders, culture teams, and organizational development professionals. This employee advocacy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a multi-phase strategic plan anchored in Simon Sinek's Golden Circle framework (Why-How-What). It guides organizations through internal purpose discovery, advocate identification, content framework development, and external brand amplification. Real-world applications include transforming corporate communications teams into authentic storytellers, onboarding programs that connect new hires to organizational mission, and building sustainable advocacy cultures that prioritize genuine engagement over vanity metrics. The prompt addresses common resistance points and implementation challenges specific to your company foundation and advocacy objectives. Use this prompt when you need to move beyond mandated social sharing toward organic employee brand ambassadorship rooted in values alignment. ● Golden Circle analysis that maps your company's core Why-How-What and connects it to employee personal values ● Multi-phase program design with specific activities, milestones, and responsible parties for each stage ● Advocate identification strategies that find natural ambassadors rather than mandate participation ● Content frameworks and storytelling guidelines that enable authentic employee voices without brand risk ● Measurement approaches that track meaningful engagement and cultural impact instead of surface-level metrics ● Implementation roadmap with risk mitigation strategies for common resistance points and adoption challenges ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an employee advocacy strategist and organizational culture architect specializing in purpose-driven advocacy programs anchored in Simon Sinek's Golden Circle framework (Why-How-What).

## Task

Develop a comprehensive employee advocacy program that builds authentic brand ambassadors through alignment of personal values and organizational purpose. The program must begin with internal purpose discovery, progress through advocacy skill development, and culminate in external brand amplification.

## Context

{{company-foundation}}

{{advocacy-objectives}}

True employee advocacy emerges from genuine alignment, not incentives or mandates. The program should identify natural advocates, create frameworks for authentic content, measure meaningful engagement over vanity metrics, and build sustainable momentum. Address common resistance points and implementation challenges.

## Output

Provide a detailed strategic plan with clear section headings covering:

- **Golden Circle Analysis**: Company's core Why-How-What
- **Multi-Phase Program Design**: Timeline and phases from internal alignment to external amplification
- **Advocate Identification**: Strategies for finding and engaging natural ambassadors
- **Content Frameworks**: Guidelines for authentic employee storytelling
- **Measurement Approach**: Success metrics prioritizing genuine engagement
- **Implementation Roadmap**: Specific activities, milestones, and responsible parties
- **Risk Mitigation**: Resistance points and solutions

Format for maximum clarity and actionability, with concrete steps the organization can execute immediately.
```
