# Dessert Slice Presentation Image Generator

## 簡介

The Dessert Slice Presentation Image Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional food photography of dessert slices with dramatic mid-air garnishes and splash effects for pastry chefs, food bloggers, and marketing teams. This dessert photography prompt for Midjourney creates ultra-detailed macro shots featuring frosting peaks, cream textures, and suspended elements like nuts, chocolate shards, and seeds captured in motion around the central slice. The prompt templates three variables - dessert type, splash effect, and backdrop mood - so you can generate everything from a levitating tiramisu slice with espresso splashes on a moody dark background to a cheesecake wedge surrounded by berry juice droplets in bright studio light. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, producing 4:5 ratio images optimized for Instagram, menu design, and packaging mockups. Reach for this prompt when you need commercial-grade dessert visuals without a physical shoot: recipe developers illustrating new creations, restaurant marketers building seasonal campaigns, or culinary students assembling portfolio work. ● Produces macro photography with dramatic highlights that emphasize texture - cream peaks, glaze shine, crumb structure - and movement through suspended ingredients. ● Three template variables let you swap dessert type (cheesecake, layer cake, tart), splash liquid (chocolate, caramel, fruit juice), and backdrop mood (elegant minimalist, rustic wood, vibrant color). ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images ideal for vertical social feeds, print menus, and e-commerce product pages. ● Studio lighting and ultra-detailed rendering capture the appetizing qualities that drive engagement in food marketing and editorial content. ## Prompt

