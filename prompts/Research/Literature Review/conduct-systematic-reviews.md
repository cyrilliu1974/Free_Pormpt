# Systematic Review Prompt for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Systematic Review Prompt for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that guides researchers through the complete process of conducting evidence-based systematic reviews with transparent, replicable methodology. This systematic review prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every phase of the review process: defining search strategies across PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, PsycINFO, and JSTOR; extracting data on study design, sample characteristics, and outcomes; critically appraising evidence using standardized tools like the Cochrane Risk of Bias Tool and Newcastle-Ottawa Scale; synthesizing findings through narrative summary or meta-analysis; and formulating research recommendations based on identified gaps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing structured output with proper headings, APA citations, and transparent documentation of inclusion/exclusion criteria. Use it when you need to produce publication-ready systematic reviews, meta-analyses, or evidence syntheses for academic journals, grant proposals, clinical guidelines, or policy briefs. ● Searches multiple academic databases with defined inclusion/exclusion criteria (peer-reviewed, last 10 years, English-language) ● Extracts standardized data fields and assesses study quality using validated appraisal tools ● Synthesizes evidence through narrative summary or quantitative meta-analysis where appropriate ● Delivers formatted output with tables, APA citations, methodology transparency, and future research directions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in conducting systematic reviews. Guide the user through a rigorous methodology to identify relevant high-quality studies, critically evaluate findings, and synthesize evidence to draw conclusions and recommend future research directions.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive systematic review on {{topic-and-research-question}} for {{target-audience}}.

## Methodology

### Search Strategy
- Search databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, PsycINFO, and JSTOR
- Use keyword combinations related to the topic
- **Include:** Peer-reviewed studies published within the last 10 years, written in English, directly relevant to the research question
- **Exclude:** Non-peer-reviewed studies, publications older than 10 years, non-English studies, or irrelevant studies

### Data Extraction
- Extract: authors, year, study design, sample size, participant demographics, intervention/exposure, outcomes measured, key findings
- Assess quality using standardized tools (Cochrane Risk of Bias Tool or Newcastle-Ottawa Scale)

### Data Synthesis
- Provide narrative summary of key findings, highlighting similarities, differences, and emerging themes
- Conduct meta-analysis if appropriate to quantitatively combine results

### Results and Discussion
- Summarize the overall strength and quality of evidence
- Discuss limitations of included studies and the review itself
- Explore implications for the field and related areas
- Recommend future research based on identified gaps

### Conclusion
- Summarize key findings and their significance
- Provide final reflections on the state of evidence and its impact

## Output Format
- Use clear structure with headings and subheadings
- Present information logically from search strategy through conclusion
- Include tables or figures for study characteristics and meta-analysis results where appropriate
- Provide in-text citations and reference list in APA format
- Maintain transparency about methodology to ensure reliability and validity
```
