# Real Estate Crowdfunding Platform Comparison Prompt

## 簡介

The Real Estate Crowdfunding Platform Comparison Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes three major real estate crowdfunding platforms and delivers a customized investment comparison for solopreneurs and individual investors. This real estate crowdfunding prompt for ChatGPT takes your investor profile - including financial goals, risk tolerance, and available capital - and produces a markdown comparison table covering Fundrise, RealtyMogul, and CrowdStreet. It evaluates minimum investment thresholds, expected returns, fee structures, liquidity terms, investment types, and risk levels, then closes with a tailored recommendation paragraph. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across leading text AI models. Use it when you need to evaluate passive income opportunities in alternative real estate investments or when comparing crowdfunding platforms side-by-side. ● Compares Fundrise, RealtyMogul, and CrowdStreet across eight decision-critical metrics in one table ● Adapts analysis to your specific investor profile, risk tolerance, and capital availability ● Outputs markdown-formatted results ready to save, share, or integrate into investment research ● Includes a personalized recommendation paragraph that aligns platform features with your financial goals ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert financial advisor specializing in alternative investments and real estate crowdfunding.

## Task

Guide a solopreneur in diversifying their investment portfolio through real estate crowdfunding platforms. Provide a comprehensive analysis of Fundrise, RealtyMogul, and CrowdStreet, focusing on their potential for generating passive income streams.

## Context

Investor profile:
{{investor-profile}}

Research and compile key information about each platform, including minimum investment requirements, expected returns, fee structures, liquidity terms, and investment types offered. Create a detailed comparison that helps the solopreneur make an informed decision aligned with their financial goals and risk tolerance.

## Output

Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with the following columns:
- Platform
- Minimum Investment
- Expected Returns
- Investment Types
- Fees
- Liquidity
- Risk Level
- Best For

Follow the table with a brief recommendation paragraph tailored to the investor profile provided.
```
