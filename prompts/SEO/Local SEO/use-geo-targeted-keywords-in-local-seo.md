# Local SEO Geo-Targeted Keyword Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Local SEO Geo-Targeted Keyword Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored local search optimization plans for businesses aiming to attract nearby customers. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT transforms your business profile, target keywords, and unique selling proposition into a complete six-part action plan covering Google Business Profile optimization, citation building, on-page elements, review management, local content strategy, and performance metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to craft readable business descriptions while naturally weaving geo-targeted keywords throughout every recommendation. Real businesses use it to audit NAP consistency across directories, generate platform-specific optimization checklists, and build content calendars that drive qualified local traffic. This prompt is ideal for local business owners, digital marketing agencies managing multi-location clients, and SEO specialists building repeatable local search strategies. ● Produces a six-section strategy with numbered steps and lettered sub-actions for immediate implementation ● Incorporates geo-targeted keywords organically across Google Business Profile, citations, and on-page content ● Audits and enforces NAP consistency to prevent local ranking penalties ● Includes review generation tactics and local content ideas tied to your unique selling proposition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert local SEO specialist optimizing online business listings to improve local search visibility and attract nearby customers.

## Task
Create a comprehensive local SEO optimization strategy that maximizes visibility in local search results and drives qualified traffic from the surrounding area.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-profile}}

Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

## Approach
- Incorporate geo-targeted keywords naturally throughout all listing elements
- Use dependency grammar principles to craft compelling, readable business descriptions that emphasize key attributes and relationships
- Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all platforms
- Prioritize actionable, platform-specific recommendations

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps. Include:

1. Google Business Profile optimization
2. Citation building and NAP consistency audit
3. On-page local SEO elements
4. Review generation and management
5. Local content strategy
6. Performance tracking metrics

For each section, provide specific, implementable actions tailored to the business profile.
```
