# Micro Ad Copywriting Prompt Using A1B3C1 Framework

## 簡介

The Micro Ad Copywriting Prompt Using A1B3C1 Framework is a free AI prompt that generates concise, benefit-driven ad variations for any product or target audience. This micro ad prompt for ChatGPT applies the A1B3C1 copywriting structure - Attention (one headline), Benefits (three tangible outcomes), and Call-to-Action (one clear instruction) - to create 10 ready-to-test variations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing what your target customer cares about most before drafting headlines and benefits that speak directly to their desires or pain points. Marketers use it to quickly generate ad copy for social media, email subject lines, landing pages, and paid campaigns without starting from a blank page. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple ad angles fast, or when you want a systematic way to turn features into outcome-focused messaging. ● Produces 10 complete ad variations, each with a headline, three benefits, and a call-to-action ● Focuses on tangible outcomes and desirability rather than feature lists ● Addresses objections parenthetically within benefits to preempt hesitation ● Guides the model to analyze audience psychology before writing, ensuring relevance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in concise, benefit-driven advertising.

## Task
Write 10 micro ad variations using the A1B3C1 framework:

- **A1 (Attention):** One headline that speaks directly to the biggest desire or pain point of the target customer
- **B3 (Benefits):** Three tangible benefits focusing on outcomes and desirability
- **C1 (Call-to-Action):** One clear instruction telling the reader what to do next

## Context
**Target customer:**
{{target-customer}}

**Product/service:**
{{product}}

Before writing, analyze what this audience cares about most and what grabs their attention. Let that insight guide every headline and benefit.

## Output
Deliver 10 variations, each structured as:

[Attention-Grabbing Headline]  
• [Big Benefit 1]  
• [Big Benefit 2]  
• [Big Benefit 3]  
[Call-to-Action]

**Guidelines:**
- Lead with benefits over features
- Be straightforward, not overly salesy
- Make benefits tangible and quantifiable where possible
- Address objections parenthetically within benefits when relevant (e.g., "Create a 2-hour course 10× faster—without sounding generic or compromising quality")
```
