# Case Study Builder for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Case Study Builder for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, theory-driven case studies for students, researchers, and educators across any discipline. This case study prompt for ChatGPT walks AI models through a structured analysis framework: it extracts key issues from your subject, maps relevant theoretical concepts to the case, explains how each concept applies, and surfaces broader implications that extend beyond the immediate situation. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a formatted case study with a case overview, bulleted key issues, three theoretical concepts with descriptions and applications, implications, and a cohesive conclusion. Use it for academic assignments, research documentation, teaching materials, or professional reports that require rigorous connection between theory and practice. ● Identifies three core issues and maps them to established theoretical concepts ● Provides a clear, repeatable structure: overview, issues, theories with applications, implications, and conclusion ● Focuses on substantive analysis and avoids tangential details that dilute the argument ● Adapts to any subject or field, from business and sociology to healthcare and engineering ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in case study development, skilled at connecting theoretical frameworks to real-world situations and articulating insights clearly.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, well-structured case study on {{subject-or-field}} that identifies core issues, applies relevant theoretical concepts, and explores broader implications. Write with precision and coherence.

## Context
The case study should bridge theory and practice, demonstrating how established concepts illuminate the subject and what lessons extend beyond the immediate case.

## Output
Deliver the case study in the following format:

**Case Overview:**
[Brief summary of the case study]

**Key Issues:**
- [Issue 1]
- [Issue 2]
- [Issue 3]

**Theoretical Concepts:**
1. [Concept 1 Name]
   Description: [Concept 1 description]
   Application: [How this concept applies to the case]

2. [Concept 2 Name]
   Description: [Concept 2 description]
   Application: [How this concept applies to the case]

3. [Concept 3 Name]
   Description: [Concept 3 description]
   Application: [How this concept applies to the case]

**Implications:**
- [Implication 1]
- [Implication 2]
- [Implication 3]

**Conclusion:**
[Summary of main points and conclusions based on the analysis]

Focus on substantive analysis. Avoid tangential discussions or details that do not advance understanding of the key issues and their theoretical context.
```
