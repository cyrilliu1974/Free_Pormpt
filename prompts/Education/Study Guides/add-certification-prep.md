# Certification Exam Study Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Certification Exam Study Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds personalized, evidence-based study schedules for professionals preparing for certification exams. This certification prep prompt for ChatGPT analyzes official exam objectives, recommends specific study materials, and creates a realistic week-by-week timeline tailored to your current knowledge level, weekly study hours, target exam date, and preferred learning style. It integrates proven techniques like spaced repetition, active recall, and interleaving, then defines milestones with practice tests and self-assessments to track progress. The output includes an executive summary, organized study units, recommended resources for each topic, a detailed weekly schedule, concrete learning strategies, progress checkpoints, and a final exam simulation phase. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured roadmap for any professional certification - from cloud computing and project management to healthcare, finance, or IT security credentials. ● Breaks down official exam objectives into manageable study units aligned with your timeline ● Recommends specific textbooks, online courses, practice exams, and documentation for each topic ● Creates a week-by-week schedule accounting for your knowledge level and available hours per week ● Embeds learning techniques like spaced repetition and active recall directly into the schedule ● Defines clear milestones with progress checks, practice tests, and a final exam simulation period ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in professional certification preparation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, structured study plan that prepares the candidate for their target certification exam. The plan must be realistic, evidence-based, and tailored to the candidate's constraints.

## Context
**Certification:** {{certification-name}}
**Current knowledge level:** {{knowledge-level}}
**Available study time:** {{study-hours-per-week}} hours per week
**Target exam date:** {{target-exam-date}}
**Preferred learning style:** {{learning-style}}

## Process
1. Analyze the certification's official exam objectives and break them into manageable study units
2. Recommend specific study materials (textbooks, online courses, video tutorials, practice exams, official documentation)
3. Create a week-by-week timeline from today until the target exam date, accounting for the candidate's knowledge level and available time
4. Integrate effective learning techniques including spaced repetition, active recall, and interleaving
5. Define clear milestones (end of each major topic or study phase) with progress check activities (practice tests, self-assessments, project work)
6. Include a final review and exam simulation period

## Output
Structure the study plan with:
- **Executive Summary** - overview of the plan and key dates
- **Study Units** - organized breakdown of all exam topics
- **Recommended Resources** - specific materials for each unit
- **Weekly Schedule** - timeline with topics, activities, and hours allocated
- **Learning Strategies** - concrete techniques to apply during study sessions
- **Milestones & Checkpoints** - dates and assessment methods to track progress
- **Final Preparation Phase** - review strategy and exam-day readiness plan

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout for maximum readability.
```
