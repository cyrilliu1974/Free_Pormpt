# Financial Risk Assessment and Mitigation Prompt

## 簡介

The Financial Risk Assessment and Mitigation Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps financial analysts and business leaders identify, evaluate, and develop strategies to mitigate financial risks within specific business processes. This financial risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a systematic evaluation of potential financial threats tied to any business activity. You provide the business process and organizational context - including industry, company size, risk tolerance, and operational constraints - and the prompt produces a comprehensive analysis with a markdown table showing each risk, its potential impact, and tailored mitigation strategies. The output runs seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering an executive-ready risk assessment complete with introduction and conclusion. Use this prompt when you need to present risk findings to stakeholders, prepare for audits, evaluate new initiatives, or strengthen enterprise risk management frameworks. It adapts to any industry and scales from startups to large enterprises. ● Systematically identifies financial risks across liquidity, credit, market, operational, and compliance domains ● Assesses impact severity to prioritize resources and attention on the most critical threats ● Delivers practical, context-aware mitigation strategies that respect your company's risk appetite and constraints ● Outputs findings in a clean markdown table format ideal for reports, presentations, and stakeholder communication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial risk analyst conducting a comprehensive risk assessment.

## Task
Analyze the financial risks associated with the specified business process and develop effective mitigation strategies. Your analysis should:

1. Identify potential financial risks related to the business process
2. Assess the potential impact of each risk on the organization
3. Develop practical mitigation strategies for each risk
4. Present findings in a clear, organized manner

## Context
Business process: {{business-process}}

Organizational context: {{organizational-context}}
(Include industry, company size, risk tolerance level, and any relevant operational constraints)

## Output
Provide a brief introduction (2-3 sentences) summarizing the scope of your assessment.

Then present your analysis in a markdown table with three columns:

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|--------|--------------------|

After the table, provide a concise conclusion (2-3 sentences) highlighting the most critical risks and overall risk posture.
```
