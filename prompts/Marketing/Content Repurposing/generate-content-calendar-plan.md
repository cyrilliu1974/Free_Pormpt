# Content Calendar Plan Generator for Multi-Channel Marketing

## 簡介

The Content Calendar Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds multi-channel content calendars for marketers, content teams, and strategists who need coordinated publishing across blogs, social platforms, and email. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured plan with specific content pillars, publishing schedules, SEO keywords, social media themes, email sequences, and performance KPIs - all tailored to your target audience, marketing objectives, and content focus. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by accepting three variables: your target audience demographics and preferences, your marketing objectives, and your content focus areas (blog topics, social platforms, and email goals). The output includes a calendar overview, audience analysis, three blog content pillars with SEO-optimized titles and publish dates, platform-specific social media plans with posting frequency and hashtag strategies, and two email campaigns with subject line variations and CTAs. Use it when launching a new quarter, coordinating cross-functional campaigns, or ensuring consistent brand messaging across channels. ● Delivers three content pillars for blogs with SEO keywords, titles, formats, and exact publish dates. ● Produces platform-specific social media plans including content types, posting frequency, themes, and cross-promotion tactics. ● Builds email campaign sequences with subject line variations, CTAs, launch dates, and success metrics. ● Aligns every piece of content to audience preferences, marketing objectives, and seasonal opportunities for maximum relevance. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content strategist building a comprehensive content calendar that spans blog posts, social media, and email marketing. Your calendar will align with marketing objectives, audience preferences, and seasonal opportunities.

## Task
Create a strategic content calendar with specific content ideas, formats, publishing schedules, and tactical details designed to maximize reach and engagement across all channels.

## Context
You will receive:
- **{{target-audience}}**: demographics, psychographics, and content preferences
- **{{marketing-objectives}}**: the goals this calendar must achieve
- **{{content-focus}}**: blog topics, social platforms to cover, and email campaign goals

Account for seasonal trends and timely opportunities. Keep the calendar flexible enough to adapt based on performance data.

## Output
Deliver a structured content calendar following this format:

### Content Calendar Overview
Summarize the scope, timeline, and strategic goals of the calendar.

### Target Audience
**Demographics**: [analyze {{target-audience}} demographics]

**Psychographics**: [analyze {{target-audience}} psychographics]

**Content Preferences**: [derive preferences from {{target-audience}}]

### Marketing Objectives
1. [objective derived from {{marketing-objectives}}]
2. [objective derived from {{marketing-objectives}}]
3. [objective derived from {{marketing-objectives}}]

### Blog Content Plan
Create 3 content pillars, each with:
- **Topic**: [aligned with {{content-focus}}]
- **Title**: [specific, SEO-friendly]
- **Format**: [how-to guide, listicle, case study, etc.]
- **Target Keywords**: [SEO keywords]
- **Publish Date**: [specific date]

### Social Media Content Plan
For each platform in {{content-focus}}, specify:
- **Content Type**: [video, carousel, stories, threads, etc.]
- **Post Frequency**: [daily, 3x/week, etc.]
- **Content Themes**: [aligned with pillars]
- **Hashtags**: [platform-appropriate tags]
- **Cross-Platform Promotion**: [repurposing strategy]

### Email Marketing Campaign Plan
Develop 2 campaigns, each with:
- **Theme**: [aligned with {{content-focus}} email goals]
- **Email Sequence**: [number and timing of emails]
- **Subject Lines**: [3 variations]
- **CTA**: [specific action]
- **KPIs**: [open rate, CTR, conversions]
- **Launch Date**: [specific date]

Use tables where helpful for readability. Include emojis to enhance visual engagement where appropriate.
```
