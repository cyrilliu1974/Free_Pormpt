# Unlinked Brand Mention Recovery and Link Building Guide

## 簡介

The Unlinked Brand Mention Recovery and Link Building Guide is a free AI prompt that creates a complete system for discovering brand mentions without links and converting them into valuable backlinks for SEO professionals and digital marketers. This link building prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered, actionable roadmap covering monitoring setup, opportunity evaluation criteria, personalized outreach templates, follow-up cadences, relationship management techniques, and tracking metrics. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting each section to your specific brand name, website URL, industry context, and target audience. Teams use it to systematically recover lost link equity, build long-term relationships with publishers, and improve domain authority without cold pitching new sites. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured approach to unlinked mention recovery that balances conversion rates with brand reputation. ● Six-step framework from initial discovery tools through success metrics and optimization loops ● Opportunity evaluation criteria tailored to your industry positioning and audience ● Professional outreach and follow-up templates that preserve relationships while securing backlinks ● Relationship management tactics for turning one-time link conversions into ongoing partnerships ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in SEO and link building through unlinked brand mention recovery.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for identifying unlinked brand mentions across the web and converting them into high-quality backlinks. The strategy must be systematic, professional, and focused on maintaining positive relationships with site owners and content creators.

## Context

- Brand: {{brand-name}}
- Website: {{website-url}}
- Industry and positioning: {{industry-context}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output

Deliver a numbered list with clear subheadings for each main step. Cover:

1. **Monitoring Setup** – Tools and methods for discovering unlinked brand mentions
2. **Opportunity Evaluation** – Criteria for assessing link quality and relevance
3. **Outreach Strategy** – Templates and best practices for personalized contact
4. **Follow-Up Process** – Timing and tone for subsequent messages
5. **Relationship Management** – Techniques for building long-term partnerships
6. **Tracking & Optimization** – Metrics to measure success and refine the approach

Each step should be actionable, specific to the brand context provided, and designed to maximize conversion rates while preserving brand reputation.
```
