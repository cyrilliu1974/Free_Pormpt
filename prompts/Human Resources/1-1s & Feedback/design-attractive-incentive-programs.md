# Sales Incentive Program Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Incentive Program Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates fair, measurable, and sustainable incentive structures for sales teams facing specific performance challenges. This sales incentive program prompt for ChatGPT helps HR professionals and sales leaders design a balanced mix of rewards - monetary bonuses, commission tiers, recognition programs, professional development opportunities, team rewards, and non-monetary perks - that align with organizational goals and drive desired behaviors without unintended consequences. You provide details about your sales team and context (industry, company size, current challenges), and the prompt produces a markdown table with 5–8 distinct incentives, each with implementation details, eligibility criteria, payout structures, and measurable expected outcomes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a ready-to-present incentive framework. Reach for this prompt when you need to address specific sales performance gaps, redesign an outdated compensation plan, or build a motivating program that remains practical to administer and track. ● Produces 5–8 distinct incentives spanning monetary, recognition, development, and team-based rewards ● Includes eligibility criteria, payout structures, and implementation steps for each incentive type ● Maps every incentive to specific behavioral outcomes and measurable performance metrics ● Ensures the program aligns with organizational goals and fits operational realities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources consultant specializing in sales incentive program design.

## Task
Design a comprehensive, motivating incentive program for a sales team that boosts performance while remaining fair, measurable, and sustainable.

## Context
{{sales-team-and-context}}

Consider a balanced mix of incentive types (monetary bonuses, commission structures, recognition programs, professional development, team rewards, non-monetary perks). Ensure each incentive aligns with organizational goals, drives desired behaviors without unintended consequences, and fits within operational realities. The program should address the specific performance challenges described while being practical to administer and track.

## Output
Present your incentive program as a markdown table with three columns:

| Incentive Type | Description | Expected Impact |
|----------------|-------------|------------------|

Include 5–8 distinct incentives that together form a complete program. For each:
- **Incentive Type**: Name and category (e.g., "Quarterly Performance Bonus" or "President's Club Trip")
- **Description**: How it works, eligibility criteria, payout/reward structure, and implementation details
- **Expected Impact**: Specific behavioral and performance outcomes, including how success will be measured
```
