# Landing Page Copywriting Prompt for Website Conversion

## 簡介

The Landing Page Copywriting Prompt for Website Conversion is a free AI prompt that generates strategically structured website copy designed to guide visitors from awareness to signup. This landing page copy prompt for ChatGPT produces four interconnected sections: a headline that captures attention, a description that builds desire, demo video copy that demonstrates value, and a signup call-to-action that converts interest into commitment. It works by applying the Problem-Agitate-Solution sequence while preempting objections and maintaining logical flow throughout the customer journey. Built for text models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it transforms your product details, customer pain points, and desired outcomes into persuasive copy that prioritizes benefits over features. Marketing teams use it to create product launch pages, SaaS companies deploy it for feature announcements, and founders rely on it to test messaging before committing design resources. This prompt is for marketers, founders, and growth teams who need conversion-focused landing pages without hiring a dedicated copywriter or spending weeks on iterative drafts. ● Produces four distinct sections (headline, description, demo video, signup CTA) that flow logically from awareness to action ● Applies Problem-Agitate-Solution methodology to build emotional engagement and urgency ● Incorporates multiple conversion points to capture readers at different stages of readiness ● Uses sensory language, specific details, and short sentences to maintain clarity and momentum ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write high-converting website copy that guides readers through a strategic sales journey, addressing their questions and concerns at each stage.

## Context

- Product: {{product-details}}
- Current customer situation (pain points): {{current-situation}}
- Desired customer outcome (transformation): {{desired-outcome}}

## Task

Write website copy for {{product-details}} that moves a visitor from awareness to action. Structure the copy in this exact order:

1. Headline
2. Description
3. Demo Video
4. Signup CTA

Apply these principles throughout:

- Progress logically from one section to the next so the reader never loses the thread
- Preempt and address likely customer objections before they surface
- Use short, punchy sentences and generous white space
- Use vivid, sensory language and specific details—no vague generalities
- Apply the Problem-Agitate-Solution sequence to build desire and drive action
- Place calls-to-action at multiple points to capture readers at different stages of interest
- Every word earns its place—no filler
- Lead with benefits and outcomes, not features

## Output

**Headline**

[Your headline here]

**Description**

[Your description here]

**Demo Video**

[Your demo video copy here]

**Signup CTA**

[Your CTA here]
```
