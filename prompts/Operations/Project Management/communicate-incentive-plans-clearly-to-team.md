# Sales Incentive Plan Communication Template

## 簡介

The Sales Incentive Plan Communication Template is a free AI prompt that transforms raw incentive details into a motivating, easy-to-understand table for sales teams. This sales incentive plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your sales context - team size, goals, industry, cycle length - and your incentive plan details, then outputs a three-column markdown table showing Incentive Type, Qualifying Criteria, and Payout Structure. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, turning vague reward descriptions into concrete numbers and unambiguous language. Sales managers use it to communicate commission tiers, bonus thresholds, recognition programs, and tiered rewards in a format that eliminates confusion and drives performance. Reach for this template when you need to roll out a new incentive structure, align compensation with quarterly goals, or onboard reps who need to understand exactly what they'll earn. ● Structures complex commission plans, bonuses, and recognition programs into a single markdown table ● Specifies qualifying thresholds, timeframes, and tier breakpoints in unambiguous terms ● Outputs concrete dollar amounts, percentages, or benefit descriptions so every rep knows what they'll earn ● Adapts to different sales contexts - team size, industry, cycle length - to ensure relevance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced sales manager communicating an incentive plan to your team.

## Task
Present the incentive plan in a clear, motivating way that drives performance. Define each incentive type, outline qualifying criteria, and detail payout structures.

## Context
{{sales-context}}

Provide relevant details: sales team size, key company sales goals, industry, typical sales cycle length, and any other factors that shape how incentives should work.

## Incentive Plan
{{incentive-plan}}

Describe the incentives you want to offer: types of rewards, thresholds, timing, tier structures, bonuses, commissions, or recognition programs.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:
- **Incentive Type**: Name and brief description of each incentive
- **Qualifying Criteria**: Clear requirements to earn it
- **Payout Structure**: Specific reward amounts, percentages, or benefits

Make the table easy to scan and motivating. Use concrete numbers and unambiguous language so every team member knows exactly what they need to do and what they'll earn.
```
