# Video Script Outline Generator for Engaging Content

## 簡介

The Video Script Outline Generator for Engaging Content is a free AI prompt that creates complete video script structures for content creators, marketers, and educators. Built around five essential sections - hook, introduction, main content, summary, and call-to-action - this video script prompt for ChatGPT produces dialogue suggestions, timing estimates, visual direction notes, and pacing guidance tailored to your video title, target audience, main message, desired length, and specific call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your creative brief into a ready-to-shoot outline that balances information delivery with viewer retention. Whether you're producing YouTube tutorials, marketing explainers, training videos, or social media content, this prompt ensures your script opens with a strong hook, maintains momentum through logical transitions, and closes with a clear next step. ● Outputs a five-section framework - hook, introduction, main content (2-4 segments), summary, and call-to-action - each with timing and tone guidance ● Tailors dialogue and pacing to your specified audience, message, video length, and desired action ● Includes scene direction and visual notes so you can coordinate narration with on-screen elements ● Accepts any video format or platform: explainer videos, tutorials, product demos, social media shorts, or training modules ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video scriptwriter who creates engaging, audience-focused scripts.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive video script outline with all essential structural elements: opening hook, key message delivery, supporting points, transitions, and closing call-to-action.

## Context
Video title: {{video-title}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Main message and key points: {{main-message}}

Desired length: {{video-length}}

Call-to-action: {{call-to-action}}

## Output
Structure the script outline with clear section headings:
- **Hook/Opening** (first 5-10 seconds)
- **Introduction** (establish credibility and preview value)
- **Main Content** (organized into 2-4 key segments with transitions)
- **Summary** (reinforce the main message)
- **Call-to-Action** (clear next step)

For each section, provide:
- Suggested dialogue/narration
- Timing estimates
- Visual/scene direction notes
- Tone and pacing guidance
```
