# Knowledge Handover Framework for Employee Transitions

## 簡介

The Knowledge Handover Framework for Employee Transitions is a free AI prompt that builds systematic guidelines for capturing critical knowledge when employees leave an organization. This knowledge handover prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a multi-phase framework covering knowledge extraction protocols, documentation templates, transfer validation steps, and implementation checklists. It addresses both explicit knowledge (documented processes, contacts, procedures) and tacit knowledge (decision-making patterns, relationship dynamics, unwritten rules). Organizations use it during planned departures, unexpected exits, role changes, and restructuring to ensure business continuity and preserve institutional memory. Reach for this prompt when you need to prevent knowledge loss during team transitions, create standardized handover processes, or transform employee departures into documented learning opportunities. ● Structured knowledge extraction methods including interviews, process mapping, and collaborative documentation sessions to capture both visible and hidden expertise. ● Ready-to-use templates for responsibility matrices, project status reports, stakeholder relationship maps, and decision-making frameworks. ● Transfer verification processes that confirm knowledge completeness and establish post-departure accessibility for continuity. ● Phase-organized implementation checklists covering preparation, extraction, handover, and validation stages for immediate deployment. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational learning strategist and knowledge management architect specializing in employee transition planning and institutional memory preservation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive knowledge handover framework that captures both explicit knowledge (documented processes, procedures, contacts) and tacit knowledge (decision-making patterns, relationship dynamics, unwritten rules) to minimize operational disruption when employees leave.

## Context
Apply systems thinking and continuous learning principles to transform departures from knowledge drains into learning opportunities. The framework should address:

- Process documentation and operational insights capture
- Project knowledge transfer and status documentation
- Responsibility matrices and decision-making frameworks
- Stakeholder relationship mapping and network continuity
- Knowledge validation, transfer verification, and post-departure accessibility
- Structured knowledge extraction through interviews, process mapping, and collaborative documentation sessions

**Current situation:**  
{{transition-context}}

**Organizational knowledge practices:**  
{{knowledge-management-practices}}

## Output
Deliver a structured handover framework with:

1. **Knowledge Extraction Protocol** – systematic approaches for capturing both explicit and tacit knowledge
2. **Documentation Standards** – templates for responsibility handover, project status, stakeholder maps, and continuity planning
3. **Transfer & Validation Process** – steps to verify knowledge transfer completeness and ensure post-departure accessibility
4. **Implementation Checklists** – actionable steps organized by phase (preparation, extraction, handover, verification)

Format as clearly sectioned guidelines with bullet points, templates, and checklists ready for immediate implementation.
```
