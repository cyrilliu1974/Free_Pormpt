# Operational Efficiency Improvement Prompt

## 簡介

The Operational Efficiency Improvement Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes business operations to identify inefficiencies and recommend concrete improvements for teams and business analysts. This operational efficiency prompt for ChatGPT examines resource allocation, workflow bottlenecks, technology gaps, employee productivity friction, and cost drivers within your provided business context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering findings in a clean markdown table with three columns: Current Process, Inefficiencies, and Proposed Solutions. Each row addresses a distinct operational area with specific problems and actionable recommendations. Use cases include process audits, cost reduction initiatives, workflow optimization projects, and preparing efficiency roadmaps that balance quick wins with strategic long-term value. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic framework to evaluate operations, whether you're conducting a quarterly review, onboarding a new operations manager, or diagnosing performance issues across departments. ● Examines resource utilization, time management, and workflow bottlenecks across business processes ● Identifies technology integration opportunities and employee productivity friction points ● Highlights cost drivers and waste, balancing immediate operational gains with sustainable improvements ● Outputs findings as a markdown table that pairs each process area with its inefficiencies and concrete solutions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst specializing in operational efficiency and cost reduction.

## Task
Analyze the provided business operations to identify inefficiencies and propose actionable improvements. Balance quick wins with long-term strategic value.

## Context
Business context:
{{business-context}}

Examine:
- Resource allocation and utilization
- Time management and workflow bottlenecks
- Technology integration opportunities
- Employee productivity and process friction
- Cost drivers and waste points

Consider both immediate operational gains and sustainable strategic improvements.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Process | Inefficiencies | Proposed Solutions |
|-----------------|----------------|--------------------|

For each row, describe one process area, its specific inefficiencies, and concrete improvement recommendations with expected impact.
```
