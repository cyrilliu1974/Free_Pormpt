# Hashtag Strategy Planner for Social Media

## 簡介

The Hashtag Strategy Planner for Social Media is a free AI prompt that builds a complete hashtag roadmap tailored to your platform, audience, and business goals. This hashtag strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand voice, key offerings, and target audience to recommend 12–15 categorized hashtags with usage frequency guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a markdown table that separates branded tags, high-volume industry terms, and trending conversation hooks. Use it when launching a campaign, refining your posting cadence, or optimizing discoverability without saturating your feed with irrelevant tags. ● Combines 2–3 branded hashtags, 5–7 industry-specific tags, and 3–5 trending terms in one cohesive plan ● Explains relevance for each hashtag so you understand its connection to your audience or business goals ● Recommends usage cadence (every post, weekly, campaign-specific, seasonal) to avoid oversaturation ● Includes a strategic summary that contextualizes the approach for your platform and brand voice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in hashtag research and campaign planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive hashtag strategy to increase brand visibility and audience engagement on {{platform}}. Analyze audience behavior, industry trends, and competitor approaches to create an effective mix of branded, industry-specific, and trending hashtags aligned with business objectives.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-type}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Brand voice:** {{brand-voice}}
**Key offerings:** {{key-products-services}}

Consider the optimal frequency of use for each hashtag to maintain relevance without oversaturation. Balance evergreen branded tags with timely trending tags and niche industry terms.

## Output
Present your hashtag strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Hashtag | Relevance | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|----------|

**Relevance:** Explain why this hashtag connects to the brand, audience, or business goals.
**Frequency:** Recommend usage cadence (every post / weekly / campaign-specific / seasonal).

Include 12–15 hashtags spanning:
- 2–3 branded hashtags unique to the business
- 5–7 industry-specific hashtags with strong search volume
- 3–5 trending or topical hashtags relevant to current conversations

Provide a brief strategic summary before the table explaining the overall approach.
```
