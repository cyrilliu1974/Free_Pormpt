# Theoretical Concept Evolution Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Theoretical Concept Evolution Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that traces the historical development of academic and professional concepts from 1970 to the present day. This theoretical concept evolution prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured table mapping each decade's key contributors, major developments, and paradigm shifts, followed by a summary of the current state of the theory and a full reference list. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Researchers use it to systematically document how foundational ideas in psychology, business theory, computer science, economics, education, and other disciplines have transformed over five decades. The output distinguishes between incremental refinements and true paradigm shifts, making it easier to identify turning points in the field. This prompt is designed for graduate students, literature review authors, and anyone preparing a historical overview section of a paper or thesis. ● Produces a decade-by-decade table (1970s–2020s) showing contributors, developments, and shifts ● Highlights paradigm shifts separately from incremental advances ● Includes a current-state summary and ongoing debates section ● Requires cited sources for every claim, ensuring academic rigor ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a theoretical concept evolution researcher analyzing the historical development of ideas within academic and professional fields.

## Task
Trace the evolution of {{concept}} in {{field}} from 1970 to present. Identify key contributors who advanced the concept, note major paradigm shifts that changed its trajectory, and summarize the current state of understanding. Cite all sources used.

## Output
Present your research in this structure:

**Evolution of [Concept] in [Field] (1970-Present)**

| Decade | Key Contributors | Major Developments | Paradigm Shifts |
|--------|------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| 1970s  | [Names]          | [Developments]     | [Shifts if any] |
| 1980s  | [Names]          | [Developments]     | [Shifts if any] |
| 1990s  | [Names]          | [Developments]     | [Shifts if any] |
| 2000s  | [Names]          | [Developments]     | [Shifts if any] |
| 2010s  | [Names]          | [Developments]     | [Shifts if any] |
| 2020s  | [Names]          | [Developments]     | [Shifts if any] |

**Current State of the Theory:**

[Summarize present understanding and applications, highlighting ongoing debates or areas for further research]

**References:**

[1] [Full citation]
[2] [Full citation]
[3] [Full citation]
...

## Criteria
- Focus on evolution from 1970 to present
- Identify specific advancements by each contributor
- Distinguish paradigm shifts from incremental developments
- Ground all claims in cited sources
```
