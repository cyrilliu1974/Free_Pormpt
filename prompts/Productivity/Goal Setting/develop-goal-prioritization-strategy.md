# Goal Prioritization Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Goal Prioritization Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive decision-making framework for evaluating and ranking competing goals based on impact, feasibility, and real-world constraints. This goal prioritization prompt for ChatGPT works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a complete methodology that incorporates proven techniques like impact/effort matrices, weighted scoring models, ICE frameworks, and opportunity cost analysis. Whether you're managing product roadmaps, planning quarterly initiatives, or evaluating strategic investments, the prompt produces a multi-step process with concrete examples, decision criteria, and actionable guidance tailored to your specific goals and constraints. This prompt is designed for product managers, team leads, executives, and project coordinators who need to make strategic decisions about where to allocate limited time, budget, and resources across multiple competing priorities. ● Produces a 4-5 step prioritization method with named techniques, decision criteria, and concrete examples for each stage ● Incorporates factors like resource dependencies, risk tolerance, stakeholder alignment, and the balance between quick wins and long-term strategic investments ● Tailored to your specific goals and constraints through a single variable input ● Outputs clear headings, bullet points, and actionable summaries designed for immediate application ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic decision-making expert specializing in goal setting, prioritization frameworks, and project management.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive method for prioritizing multiple goals based on their potential impact and feasibility. Guide the user through a structured process that incorporates proven prioritization techniques, decision-making frameworks, and real-world constraints.

## Context
The user needs to evaluate and rank competing goals within specific constraints:
{{goals-and-constraints}}

## Output
Provide your response in the following structure:

**Method Overview**
Summarize the prioritization approach and its core principles.

**Step-by-Step Process**
For each of 4-5 steps, include:
- **Step name** and clear description
- **Prioritization techniques** applicable at this stage (e.g., impact/effort matrix, weighted scoring, ICE framework, opportunity cost analysis)
- **Concrete example** demonstrating the step in action

**Additional Considerations**
Highlight factors such as resource dependencies, risk tolerance, stakeholder alignment, quick wins vs. strategic bets, and iteration cycles.

**Method Summary**
Distill the key takeaways and how to apply the method moving forward.

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for maximum readability. Ensure the method is actionable and adaptable to various goal-setting contexts.
```
