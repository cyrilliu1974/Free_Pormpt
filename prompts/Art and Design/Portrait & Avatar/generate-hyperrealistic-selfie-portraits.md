# Hyperrealistic Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic high-angle selfie photographs of a young woman with professional lighting and styling details. The prompt produces 8k resolution portraits featuring soft rosy-pink makeup, glossy lips, natural eye highlights, and accessories like pink-gold frame glasses and silver jewelry, all shot against a clean white wall with customizable lighting direction and aesthetic styles. This hyperrealistic selfie prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers sharp facial focus with shallow depth of field and avoids common AI artifacts like distorted anatomy or cartoon styling.
