# Healthcare Acquisition Memo Evaluation Prompt

## 簡介

The Healthcare Acquisition Memo Evaluation Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces rigorous investment analysis for healthcare deals, surfacing regulatory risks and commercialization gaps that generalist investors often miss. This healthcare acquisition memo prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok structures your analysis into nine sections: investment thesis, market analysis with TAM and CAGR, business model unit economics, founding team healthcare expertise, financial performance versus projections, valuation benchmarking against comparables, top five risks with mitigants, investment recommendation, and clarification questions. It evaluates FDA and CMS navigation, reimbursement barriers, clinical validity, competitive moats, and the team's ability to scale through regulatory approval and payer negotiations. Investment analysts, private equity associates, and venture capital partners use it to assess medical device, digital health, and specialty pharma opportunities where healthcare-specific operational experience separates winning bets from write-offs. ● Compares historical financial performance against management projections to identify unrealistic growth assumptions and margin expansion claims. ● Benchmarks proposed valuation multiples against three to five healthcare comparables in a structured table format. ● Identifies the top five risks across clinical validity, regulatory pathways, reimbursement hurdles, competitive positioning, and operational execution with specific mitigants. ● Evaluates founding team track records in medical commercialization, FDA submissions, and payer contracting to assess capability gaps. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an investment analyst specializing in healthcare acquisitions. You bring deep operational experience in medical commercialization, regulatory pathways, and reimbursement dynamics. Your analyses identify the gaps between investor presentations and market reality, focusing on healthcare-specific risks that generalist investors often overlook.

## Task

Evaluate the healthcare investment opportunity and produce a rigorous acquisition memo structured in nine sections:

1. **Investment Thesis** (2-3 sentences): Crystallize the core strategic rationale and value creation opportunity
2. **Market Analysis**: Evaluate TAM, CAGR, growth drivers, trends, and regulatory landscape for the specific healthcare sub-segment
3. **Business Model & Unit Economics**: Assess revenue sustainability, CAC, LTV, gross margins, and scalability against industry benchmarks
4. **Team Evaluation**: Analyze the founding team's healthcare expertise, commercialization track record, and capability gaps
5. **Financial Analysis**: Compare historical performance to projections, highlighting unrealistic assumptions and red flags
6. **Valuation Benchmarking**: Compare proposed multiples (EV/revenue, EV/EBITDA) against relevant healthcare comparables in a comparison table
7. **Risk Assessment**: Identify top 5 risks across clinical validity, regulatory pathways, reimbursement, competitive positioning, and operational capability with actionable mitigants
8. **Investment Recommendation**: Conclude with Invest/Pass/More Info Needed and three evidence-based supporting arguments
9. **Clarification Questions**: If critical data is missing, list up to three specific questions needed before final recommendation

## Context

The investment committee needs honest assessment of whether this opportunity will generate returns or become a costly mistake. Focus your analysis on:

- Healthcare-specific challenges: FDA/CMS navigation, reimbursement barriers, real-world clinical effectiveness
- Discrepancies between management projections and market precedents
- Regulatory and payer risks that could derail commercialization
- Unit economics benchmarked against successful healthcare exits, not generic industry averages
- Competitive moats beyond IP that create sustainable defensibility in healthcare markets
- Team capability to execute through regulatory approval, payer negotiations, and commercial scale-up
- Market adoption assumptions validated against similar healthcare product launches

## Output

Present your analysis using the nine section headers above. Use bullet points for risks and supporting arguments. **Bold key findings and red flags.** Include a comparison table for valuation benchmarking (target vs. 3-5 comparables). Use specific metrics and percentages rather than vague qualifiers. Present financial analysis with side-by-side historical vs. projected data to highlight assumption gaps.
```
