# Employee Offboarding Timeline Template Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Offboarding Timeline Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, phase-based offboarding schedules for HR teams, project managers, and operations leaders managing employee transitions. This employee offboarding prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies PMBOK scheduling principles to structure departures across four phases: notice period management, documentation and knowledge capture, security and access clearance, and knowledge transfer completion. It produces milestone-driven templates with task dependencies, accountability assignments, duration estimates, and risk mitigation strategies tailored to different departure scenarios - voluntary resignations, terminations, or role transitions. Organizations use it to prevent knowledge loss, security gaps, and operational disruptions when team members leave. Reach for this prompt when you need a reusable framework that adapts to your organizational context, role complexity, and handover requirements. ● Builds phase-by-phase task sequences with parallel and sequential activity identification, realistic time buffers, and clear dependencies. ● Defines milestone deliverables, success criteria, and assigns primary and secondary responsible parties for every task. ● Includes a summary timeline table showing critical path activities and milestone dates. ● Provides a risk mitigation checklist and escalation procedures for common offboarding delays or deviations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert project management consultant specializing in organizational transitions and PMBOK-based offboarding frameworks.

## Task
Design a detailed offboarding timeline template that ensures seamless employee departures while protecting organizational knowledge and maintaining operational continuity. The template must accommodate different departure scenarios (voluntary resignations, terminations, role transitions) and prevent security risks, knowledge loss, and operational disruptions.

## Context
Structure the timeline around four critical phases:

1. **Notice period management** – initial communication, departure logistics, and role coverage planning
2. **Documentation and knowledge capture** – process documentation, project handover files, and institutional knowledge recording
3. **Security and access clearance** – credential revocation, equipment return, and data access termination
4. **Knowledge transfer completion** – successor training, final Q&A sessions, and handover sign-off

For each phase, define:
- Specific milestones with realistic duration estimates
- Responsible parties (primary and secondary)
- Task dependencies and which activities can run in parallel
- Deliverables and success criteria
- Buffer time for complex handovers
- Risk mitigation strategies for common delays
- Escalation procedures for timeline deviations

Account for these organizational factors:
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Provide:

1. **Phase-by-phase breakdown** with clear task sequences, accountability assignments, and time allocations
2. **Summary timeline table** showing critical path activities, milestone dates, and dependencies
3. **Accountability matrix** mapping tasks to responsible parties
4. **Risk mitigation checklist** for common offboarding delays

Format with clear phase headers (##), detailed task breakdowns in bullet points, and tables where appropriate.
```
