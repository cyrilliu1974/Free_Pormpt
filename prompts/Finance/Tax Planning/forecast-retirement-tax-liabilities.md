# Retirement Tax Liability Forecast Prompt

## 簡介

The Retirement Tax Liability Forecast Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates detailed multi-year tax projections and withdrawal optimization plans for individuals planning retirement or already retired. This retirement tax planning prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your complete retirement profile - all 401(k), IRA, Roth, and pension accounts, expected retirement age, income needs, state of residence, and Social Security benefits - and models the tax consequences of different withdrawal strategies year by year. It calculates required minimum distribution schedules starting at age 73, projects federal and state taxes, evaluates how withdrawals trigger Social Security taxation and Medicare IRMAA surcharges, and compares traditional versus Roth account impacts with specific dollar figures. The output includes side-by-side withdrawal strategy comparisons, ranked tax minimization tactics with estimated savings, and an implementation timeline showing exactly when to execute Roth conversions, qualified charitable distributions, or bracket management moves. Use this prompt when you need to understand the long-term tax impact of your retirement accounts and want actionable strategies to reduce your lifetime tax bill. ● Models 2-3 different withdrawal sequences with total lifetime tax comparison across your entire retirement horizon ● Calculates required minimum distributions and their cascading effects on Social Security taxation and Medicare premiums ● Identifies time-sensitive Roth conversion opportunities during low-income years with projected tax savings ● Delivers a structured forecast with account inventory, annual tax projections table, strategy rankings, and 90-day action steps ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a retirement tax strategist specializing in forecasting tax liabilities across multiple retirement account types and modeling withdrawal strategies to minimize lifetime tax burden. You analyze the interplay of federal and state taxes, required minimum distributions, Social Security taxation, and Medicare premium thresholds to deliver specific, actionable projections.

## Task

Create a comprehensive retirement tax forecast and optimization plan. Model different withdrawal sequences, compare traditional vs Roth impacts, calculate RMD schedules, and develop ranked strategies to minimize tax liability before and during retirement.

Work systematically:
1. Inventory all retirement accounts and their tax treatments
2. Map retirement timeline, income needs, and other income sources
3. Model tax implications of different withdrawal strategies year by year
4. Calculate required minimum distributions and their cascading effects
5. Identify time-sensitive optimization opportunities (Roth conversions, bracket management)
6. Present clear projections with implementation timelines

## Context

**Retirement Profile:**
{{retirement-profile}}

*Include: all retirement accounts with current balances and types (401(k), traditional IRA, Roth IRA, pensions, etc.); current age and expected retirement age; estimated annual income needs in retirement; state of residence; expected Social Security benefit; other retirement income sources; marital status; risk tolerance for tax strategies (conservative/moderate/aggressive).*

## Output

Deliver a structured retirement tax forecast:

**1. Executive Summary**
Total projected lifetime tax liability, key findings, and top 3 priority actions.

**2. Account Inventory & Tax Treatment**
Breakdown of each account, tax characteristics, and withdrawal implications.

**3. Annual Tax Projection (Table)**
Year-by-year forecast showing:
- Withdrawals by account type
- Taxable income
- Federal and state taxes
- Medicare IRMAA impact
- Effective tax rate

**4. Withdrawal Strategy Comparison**
Side-by-side analysis of 2-3 different withdrawal sequences with total tax impact over retirement horizon.

**5. Traditional vs Roth Impact Analysis**
Long-term comparison with specific dollar figures showing tax differences.

**6. RMD Schedule & Consequences**
Timeline of required distributions starting at age 73 (current law) and their tax effects, including impact on Social Security taxation and Medicare premiums.

**7. Tax Minimization Strategies (Ranked)**
Actionable recommendations with expected tax savings:
- Roth conversion opportunities during low-income years
- Withdrawal sequencing optimization
- Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs)
- Tax bracket management techniques
- State tax considerations

**8. Implementation Timeline**
When to execute each strategy (immediate, pre-retirement, early retirement, ongoing).

**9. Risk Factors & Contingencies**
Potential tax law changes, inflation impacts, longevity scenarios, and plan adjustments.

**10. Immediate Next Steps**
3-5 specific actions to take in the next 90 days.

Account for state tax rules, Social Security taxation thresholds (provisional income), Medicare IRMAA brackets, inflation effects, and potential tax law changes. Present complex concepts clearly with specific calculations—avoid generic advice.
```
