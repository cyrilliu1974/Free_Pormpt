# Spot Bonus Program for Sales Team Performance

## 簡介

The Spot Bonus Program for Sales Team Performance is a free AI prompt that creates structured, tiered incentive tables for HR managers and sales leaders looking to reward exceptional sales results. This sales incentive prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table with three columns: performance thresholds expressed as percentages above target, corresponding bonus amounts, and additional non-monetary rewards such as recognition or time off. You provide context about your current sales targets, team size, available budget, industry norms, and desired performance thresholds, and the prompt generates at least three escalating reward tiers designed to motivate progressive improvements while staying within financial constraints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or refine a transparent, fair spot bonus system that aligns individual achievement with company sales strategy and budgetary realities. ● Outputs a clear three-column table showing performance thresholds, bonus amounts, and additional incentives ● Builds at least three escalating tiers to reward incremental achievement above sales targets ● Ensures the total program cost fits within your stated budget when applied across the team ● Balances monetary rewards with recognition, PTO, and development opportunities for holistic motivation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Human Resources manager designing a spot bonus program for a sales team.

## Task
Create a structured incentive table that rewards exceptional sales performance and motivates the team to exceed targets. The bonus structure must be fair, transparent, and aligned with the company's sales strategy and financial constraints.

## Context
{{sales-team-context}}

Include information about:
- Current sales targets and typical performance ranges
- Total budget available for the bonus program
- Sales team size
- Industry context and competitive compensation norms
- Desired performance thresholds that trigger bonuses

## Output
Present your bonus program as a markdown table with 3 columns:
- **Percentage Above Target**: The performance threshold (e.g., 10% above target, 25% above target)
- **Bonus Amount**: The monetary reward or percentage of base/commission
- **Additional Incentives**: Non-monetary rewards or recognition (e.g., public recognition, extra PTO, leadership opportunities)

Include at least 3 tiers of escalating rewards that incentivize progressive performance improvements. Ensure the total program cost remains within budget when applied across the team.
```
