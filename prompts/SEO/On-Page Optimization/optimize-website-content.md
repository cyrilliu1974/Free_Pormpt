# Website Content Optimization Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Website Content Optimization Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds actionable content plans to improve engagement and conversion rates for digital marketers and business owners. This website content optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - including current conversion rates, target audience behaviors, and product offerings - to deliver a structured strategy covering keyword research, content formats, distribution channels, and compelling calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing prioritized recommendations organized by strategic pillars and tactical areas. Marketing teams use it to identify audience pain points, align messaging with user preferences, and apply SEO best practices without starting from scratch. Reach for this prompt when you need a data-informed content roadmap tailored to a specific business and audience rather than generic advice. ● Conducts keyword research and integrates SEO best practices into every recommendation ● Recommends specific content types, formats, and distribution channels matched to audience behavior ● Designs calls-to-action tailored to the audience's stage in the buyer journey ● Structures all recommendations with clear dependencies between content elements for logical implementation ● Addresses audience pain points with targeted messaging that resonates and converts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in website content optimization for engagement and conversions.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, actionable content optimization plan tailored to the specified business context. Analyze audience preferences, behaviors, and pain points to create targeted content strategies.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-name}}  
**Website:** {{website-url}}  
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}  
**Primary Products/Services:** {{products-services}}  
**Current Conversion Rate:** {{current-conversion-rate}}  

## Requirements
- Conduct keyword research and apply SEO best practices
- Recommend specific content types, formats, and distribution channels
- Design compelling calls-to-action suited to the audience
- Structure recommendations using clear dependency relationships between content elements
- Address audience pain points with tailored messaging

## Output
Deliver your strategy in a structured format with:
- Main headings for each strategic pillar
- Subheadings for tactical areas
- Bullet points for specific, actionable recommendations
- Prioritization guidance where relevant
```
