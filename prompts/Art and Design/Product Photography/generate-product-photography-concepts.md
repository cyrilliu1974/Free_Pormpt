# Product Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Product Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional close-up product photographs with minimal studio aesthetics for designers, marketers, and e-commerce teams. This product photography prompt for Midjourney produces studio-quality images featuring soft natural lighting from above, eye-level perspective, and shallow depth of field that captures fine details. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to generate clean, professional shots suitable for catalogs, websites, and marketing materials. Users customize two variables - product description and surface type - to render photos that highlight texture, color, and build quality in a 16:9 aspect ratio. Ideal for hardware manufacturers, online retailers, and content creators who need polished product visuals without a physical photoshoot. ● Produces eye-level close-ups with shallow depth of field to emphasize product details and texture. ● Applies soft natural overhead lighting and minimal backgrounds for a clean, professional studio look. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images suitable for web banners, social media, and e-commerce listings. ● Accepts any product description and surface material, making it adaptable across categories. ## Prompt

```
Close-up product photograph of {{keyboard-description}} keyboard on {{surface}} tabletop; soft natural light from above; eye-level perspective, sharp focus with shallow depth of field capturing fine details — minimal, clean studio aesthetic --ar 16:9
```
