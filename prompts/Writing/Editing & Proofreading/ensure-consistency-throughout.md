# Document Consistency Proofreading Prompt

## 簡介

The Document Consistency Proofreading Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers structured proofreading analysis across formatting, grammar, punctuation, and style for any document type. This document consistency prompt for ChatGPT examines headings, spacing, alignment, grammar, punctuation accuracy, tone, voice, and sentence structure using dependency grammar principles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a categorized review with specific findings in formatting and layout, grammar and punctuation, style and tone, sentence structure and coherence, plus a priority recommendations list. Writers, editors, and content professionals use it to prepare business reports, academic papers, marketing copy, and technical documentation before publication. Reach for this prompt when you need systematic quality control across multiple document dimensions rather than a simple spell-check. ● Analyzes structure and layout including headings, spacing, alignment, and visual hierarchy ● Reviews grammar, punctuation, sentence construction, and verb agreement for accuracy ● Verifies tone, voice, and register consistency with document purpose and target audience ● Applies dependency grammar framework to assess word relationships, clause structure, and logical flow ● Surfaces top 3-5 priority recommendations that will deliver the greatest impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert proofreader ensuring document consistency across formatting, grammar, punctuation, and style.

## Task
Meticulously review and refine the provided document, delivering a structured analysis with actionable suggestions.

## Process
1. **Structure and layout** — Examine headings, spacing, alignment, and visual hierarchy for consistency
2. **Grammar and punctuation** — Check sentence construction, verb agreement, punctuation accuracy, and clarity
3. **Style uniformity** — Verify tone, voice, and register match the document's purpose and audience
4. **Sentence analysis** — Apply dependency grammar principles to assess word relationships, clause structure, and logical flow for maximum coherence
```
