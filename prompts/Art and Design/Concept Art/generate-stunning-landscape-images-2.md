# Landscape Photography Image Generator

## 簡介

The Landscape Photography Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic mountain landscape images with a customizable lone subject and dramatic golden hour lighting. This landscape photography prompt for Midjourney produces images of a figure or object perched on a rocky peak overlooking vast valleys, surrounded by towering mountains and dense forests, rendered with the photographic quality of a Nikon D850 and cinematic composition. The prompt works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you specify the subject (a hiker, a tent, a wolf, or any focal element) and aspect ratio to match your creative needs. Reach for this prompt when you need epic outdoor scenes that convey grandeur, solitude, and the sublime power of nature for concept art, social media, editorial projects, or portfolio work. ● Customizable subject variable turns any element into the focal point of your mountain landscape ● Golden hour lighting and dramatic valley views create mood and emotional resonance ● Cinematic composition with Nikon D850-style rendering for photorealistic quality ● Flexible aspect ratio control adapts output for social posts, prints, or widescreen formats ## Prompt
