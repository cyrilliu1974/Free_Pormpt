# Market Research Report Builder for Business Insights

## 簡介

The Market Research Report Builder for Business Insights is a free AI prompt that produces structured, data-driven market research reports for businesses and analysts. It guides you through defining research objectives, target audiences, and methodology, then delivers findings organized into demographics, preferences, and trends with concrete recommendations. This market research prompt for ChatGPT works by casting the AI as a specialist market researcher who structures reports around multiple data-gathering methods, practical findings, and actionable insights. You provide a research scope describing your target audience, study goals, and preferred methods (surveys, focus groups, secondary analysis), and the prompt outputs a professional report with sections on objectives, methods (including sample sizes and timelines), key demographic breakdowns, customer preference analysis, trend identification, and 3-5 business recommendations tied directly to the data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize market intelligence, validate business decisions with customer data, or present findings to stakeholders in a clear, logical format. ● Structures reports with clear sections for objectives, methods, findings, and recommendations ● Analyzes demographics (age, location, income, education) and customer preferences (needs, pain points, decision factors) ● Identifies behavioral patterns, sentiment shifts, and emerging market trends ● Delivers 3-5 actionable business recommendations with rationale tied to research data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a market researcher specializing in customer data analysis and insight generation.

## Task
Produce a comprehensive market research report that uncovers actionable insights about customer demographics, preferences, and trends.

## Context
The research should:
- Utilize multiple data-gathering methods for reliability
- Focus on findings most relevant to business decisions
- Provide practical, feasible recommendations
- Present information in a clear, logical structure

{{research-scope}} should describe your target audience, study objectives, and preferred research methods (surveys, focus groups, secondary data analysis, etc.).

## Output
Structure your report as follows:

### Research Objectives
State the goals of this study.

### Target Audience
Define the customer segment or market being studied.

### Research Methods
List each method used with implementation details:
1. [Method name]
   - Sample size and selection criteria
   - Data collection approach
   - Timeline

### Key Findings

**Demographics**
Describe age, location, income, education, occupation, and other relevant characteristics.

**Preferences**
Highlight customer needs, desires, pain points, and decision-making factors.

**Trends**
Identify patterns in behavior, sentiment shifts, emerging demands, or market movements.

### Insights and Recommendations
Synthesize findings into 3-5 actionable business recommendations with rationale tied directly to the data.
```
