# Brand Messaging Framework Generator for PR

## 簡介

The Brand Messaging Framework Generator for PR is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive brand messaging strategies for companies seeking consistent, compelling communication across all PR and marketing channels. This brand messaging prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete framework anchored in your company name, target audience, industry context, core values, and brand personality. It performs competitive research, identifies differentiators, and outputs a structured strategy with a unique value proposition, prioritized messaging hierarchy, brand voice guidelines, and channel-specific applications for website copy, social media, advertising, press releases, and more. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across text-generation platforms. Marketing teams use it to align internal communications, PR agencies deploy it to onboard new clients quickly, and startup founders rely on it to articulate their positioning before a launch. ● Outputs a clear value proposition statement that highlights unique selling points and resonates with your specified target audience ● Builds a prioritized messaging hierarchy with supporting proof points, organizing themes from primary to secondary for channel consistency ● Defines brand voice and tone guidelines with specific vocabulary patterns and communication style aligned to your brand personality ● Provides touchpoint applications with concrete copy direction for websites, social media, ad campaigns, press releases, and other channels ● Includes an adaptation framework so messaging stays consistent while flexing to address market shifts and customer needs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist developing a comprehensive brand messaging strategy.

## Task
Create a clear, compelling brand messaging framework that communicates the company's unique value proposition, mission, and vision to target audiences.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Core values: {{company-values}}
Brand personality: {{brand-personality}}

Conduct research on the target audience, competitors, and industry trends to inform your strategy. Identify key differentiators and unique selling points. Develop a consistent brand voice and tone aligned with the company's values and personality.

## Output
Deliver a structured brand messaging strategy that includes:

**Value Proposition**
A clear, concise, memorable statement that resonates with the target audience and highlights unique selling points.

**Messaging Hierarchy**
Prioritized core messages with supporting proof points, organized from primary to secondary themes to ensure consistency across all channels.

**Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines**
Specific characteristics, vocabulary patterns, and communication style that reflect the desired brand personality.

**Touchpoint Applications**
Concrete guidelines for implementing the messaging across:
- Website copy
- Social media content
- Advertising campaigns
- Press releases
- Other key communication channels

**Adaptation Framework**
Guidance for maintaining brand consistency while flexing messaging to address changing market conditions and evolving customer needs.

Structure your strategy with clear logical relationships between elements, ensuring each component builds coherently on the previous ones.
```
