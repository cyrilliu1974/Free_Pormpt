# Multi-Platform Marketing Campaign Builder

## 簡介

The Multi-Platform Marketing Campaign Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive content-driven growth campaigns for marketers and business owners seeking to generate user engagement and website traffic. This multi-platform marketing campaign prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every stage of campaign planning: you provide your website URL, and the prompt returns a complete marketing blueprint with platform selection, five content creation tactics, three incentivization strategies to encourage user participation, tracking tools with specific purposes, a campaign flowchart showing phases and decision points, a four-week execution timeline, percentage-based budget allocation across categories, and success evaluation criteria tied to your KPIs. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, formatting output into clearly labeled sections ready for team handoff or client presentation. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework for launching coordinated campaigns across social media, email, and web channels, or when a client or stakeholder demands a data-informed roadmap before greenlighting spend. ● Generates five distinct content creation tactics tailored to multi-platform distribution and user participation. ● Proposes three incentivization strategies that motivate audiences to contribute content and share your message. ● Specifies tracking tools by name and purpose so you can measure engagement, traffic, and conversions from day one. ● Provides a four-week timeline and percentage-based budget allocation to keep execution on schedule and on budget. ## Prompt
