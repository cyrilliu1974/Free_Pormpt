# Solopreneur Blog Monetization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Solopreneur Blog Monetization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a customized, stage-by-stage guide for solopreneurs launching or scaling a blog to hit specific monthly revenue goals. This blog planning prompt for ChatGPT takes three inputs - your niche, target monthly income, and current experience level - and produces a structured roadmap covering niche validation, content foundation, audience growth, monetization tactics, and sustainability strategies. Each step explains its objective, breaks down actionable sub-tasks, and identifies dependencies so you know exactly what to complete before advancing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure every recommendation builds logically on the last. Reach for this prompt when you need a personalized blogging business plan that adapts to your available hours per week and skill level, whether you're a first-time blogger or pivoting an existing site toward revenue. ● Covers the full lifecycle from niche validation and positioning through content creation, audience building, and revenue implementation. ● Structures each step with clear objectives, bulleted sub-tasks, and dependencies so you complete foundational work before moving forward. ● Tailors timelines and complexity to match your stated experience level and weekly availability. ● Outputs a numbered, logically sequenced guide you can follow week by week to turn blogging effort into sustainable income. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist and blogger specializing in solopreneur ventures.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for building a profitable blog that generates the target monthly income. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles: each idea should build logically on the previous one, with clear connections between concepts.

## Context
Niche: {{niche}}
Desired monthly income: {{desired-monthly-income}}
Current experience and time commitment: {{blogger-profile}}

## Output
Deliver the guide as a numbered list. Each main step should include:
- The objective and why it matters at this stage
- Bulleted sub-steps with specific, actionable tasks
- Clear dependencies (what must be completed before moving to the next step)

Cover the full lifecycle: niche validation and positioning, content foundation, audience building, monetization implementation, and sustainable growth strategies. Tailor the timeline and complexity to the experience level and time availability provided.
```
