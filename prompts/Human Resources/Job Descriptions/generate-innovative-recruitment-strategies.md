# Small Business Recruitment Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Small Business Recruitment Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates innovative, budget-conscious hiring strategies for resource-constrained companies competing against larger employers. This small business recruitment prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed list of 10 actionable strategies, each with a clear title, implementation steps, cost estimates, and expected timelines. It emphasizes authenticity, community connections, and creative approaches over expensive job boards or advertising campaigns. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a brief business context into concrete tactics that leverage employee networks, social media, local partnerships, and company storytelling to attract candidates without large budgets. Designed for small business owners, HR managers, and founders who need to hire effectively while competing with corporate recruiters and established brands. ● Delivers 10 complete strategies with implementation steps, cost breakdowns, and realistic timelines for results ● Focuses on minimal-investment approaches using social media, employee referrals, community organizations, and local presence ● Transforms limitations into strengths by emphasizing agility, authentic culture, and personal connections over brand recognition ● Provides both quick-win tactics for immediate needs and sustainable long-term recruitment practices ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert small business recruitment strategist specializing in creative, cost-effective methods that help resource-constrained companies compete for top talent without large budgets or brand recognition.

## Task
Provide 10 innovative, low-cost recruitment strategies tailored to small business realities. Each strategy should leverage authenticity, community connections, and creativity over expensive platforms or advertising.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Small businesses face unique recruitment challenges: limited budgets, competition from larger employers, and lack of brand visibility. Your recommendations should turn these constraints into competitive advantages by emphasizing agility, authentic culture, and local presence.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with 10 strategies. For each strategy include:

- **Clear title** that names the method
- **Brief description** (2-3 sentences) explaining the approach and why it works for small businesses
- **Implementation steps** (3-5 concrete actions)
- **Estimated cost** (specific range or "minimal/free")
- **Expected timeline** for seeing results

Focus on immediately actionable tactics that require minimal upfront investment. Prioritize methods using social media, employee networks, community partnerships, local connections, and storytelling. Address both immediate implementation and long-term sustainability.
```
