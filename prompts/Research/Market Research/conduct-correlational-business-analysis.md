# Goal-Setting and Business Success Correlation Analysis

## 簡介

The Goal-Setting and Business Success Correlation Analysis is a free AI prompt that produces empirical research reports connecting goal-setting practices to business outcomes for researchers, strategists, and analysts. This goal-setting correlation prompt for ChatGPT walks you through defining measurable metrics, calculating correlation coefficients, determining statistical significance, and translating raw data into actionable business recommendations. You supply your target industry and data sources, and the prompt structures a complete correlational study that includes citation formatting, optimal goal-setting intervals backed by evidence, and best practices grounded in your dataset. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond anecdotal advice and quantify whether goal-setting frequency actually predicts success in a given market or vertical. ● Defines both goal-setting frequency and business success as measurable variables, ensuring your study rests on objective data rather than opinion. ● Calculates correlation coefficients and p-values so you can report statistical significance and confidence intervals. ● Identifies the optimal goal-setting interval supported by your dataset, giving you a clear, evidence-based recommendation. ● Synthesizes findings into a concise recommendation paragraph that executives and stakeholders can act on immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and analyst specializing in correlational studies between goal-setting practices and business success.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive study analyzing the relationship between goal-setting frequency and business success rates. Base your analysis on empirical data and statistical methods. Identify specific, measurable metrics for both goal-setting frequency and business success. Provide actionable recommendations supported by the data. Cite all sources throughout your analysis.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}

Data sources: {{data-sources}}

## Output
Structure your study as follows:

**Industry:** [State the industry]

**Data Sources:**
[List each source with full citations]

**Correlation Analysis:**
- Goal-setting frequency metric: [Define how frequency was measured]
- Business success metric: [Define success measurement]
- Correlation coefficient: [Provide coefficient value]
- P-value: [Provide statistical significance]

**Optimal Goal-Setting Interval:** [Specify the interval supported by data]

**Goal-Setting Best Practices:**
[List 3-5 practices identified from the study]

**Recommendation:**
Synthesize the correlation findings, optimal interval, and best practices into a concise, data-driven recommendation explaining what businesses in this industry should do to maximize success through goal-setting.
```
