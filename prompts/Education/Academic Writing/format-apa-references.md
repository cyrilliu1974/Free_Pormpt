# APA Reference Formatter for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The APA Reference Formatter for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that converts references in any text to conform with American Psychological Association (APA) style standards for students, researchers, and academics. This APA formatting prompt for ChatGPT takes a document containing citations and references and systematically corrects in-text citations to proper parenthetical and narrative formats, rebuilds the reference list with hanging indents and sentence-case titles, and formats footnotes with superscript numbering. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, handling books, journal articles, online sources, and other reference types according to current APA guidelines. Use it to ensure publication dates, author names, punctuation, italicization, DOIs, and URLs meet the stringent requirements of academic journals and university submissions. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to clean up references before submitting a manuscript, dissertation chapter, or course paper that requires APA style. ● Corrects in-text citations to include author, year, and page numbers in the proper format ● Rebuilds reference lists with alphabetization, hanging indents, and sentence-case titles ● Verifies punctuation, italics, DOIs, and URLs against APA standards ● Formats footnotes with superscript numbering and complete citation details ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in academic writing and APA (American Psychological Association) style formatting, specializing in reference accuracy and consistency.

## Task
Format all references in the provided text to conform perfectly to APA style guidelines. This includes in-text citations, the reference list, and footnotes.

## Guidelines

**In-text citations:**
- Include author's last name and year of publication: (Author, Year)
- For direct quotes, add page numbers: (Author, Year, p. Page)
- Use "&" for multiple authors in parentheses, "and" in narrative citations

**Reference list:**
- Alphabetize entries by author's last name
- Apply hanging indent to each entry
- Use sentence case for titles (capitalize only the first word and proper nouns)

*Books:* Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book*. Publisher.

*Journal articles:* Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Journal*, *volume*(issue), pages.

*Online sources:* Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Website*. URL

**Footnotes:**
- Use superscript numbers in text
- Match numbered notes at page bottom with full citation details

**Formatting standards:**
- Verify accuracy of all publication dates and author name spelling
- Ensure consistent punctuation and italicization
- Double-check DOIs and URLs for online sources

## Input
{{text-with-references}}

## Output
Provide the text with all references formatted to APA standards, including:
- Corrected in-text citations throughout the body
- A complete, alphabetized reference list
- Properly formatted footnotes (if present)

Present the formatted text ready for academic submission.
```
