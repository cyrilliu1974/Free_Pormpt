This sticker design prompt for ChatGPT and Midjourney takes two inputs - your subject and desired art style - and produces print-ready sticker designs with strong outlines, saturated colors, and a clean white die-cut border isolated on white background. The 1:1 square aspect ratio makes output immediately usable for digital stickers, print-on-demand products, emoji packs, or brand assets. Works reliably across Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image models.
