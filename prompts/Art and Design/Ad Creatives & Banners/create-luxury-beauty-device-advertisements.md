# Luxury Beauty Device Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Beauty Device Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that produces dual-panel vertical ad layouts blending clinical product shots with aspirational lifestyle visuals for premium skincare brands. This luxury beauty device advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates 2:3 portrait compositions featuring a studio hero shot of your device alongside a real-world application scene. The first panel captures the product on a clean surface with soft diffused lighting that highlights material finishes and brand details, while the second panel shows the device in use within your chosen ritual environment - bathroom vanity, spa setting, or bedroom - bathed in natural window light. Beauty marketers, wellness brands, and e-commerce teams use it to produce photorealistic ad creative for Instagram, product pages, and launch campaigns without coordinating expensive studio sessions. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade beauty advertising assets that balance scientific credibility with emotional appeal, all controlled through three simple variables: product name, color palette, and ritual environment. ● Produces dual-panel vertical layouts optimized for social media feeds and mobile e-commerce browsing. ● Renders photorealistic skin tones and natural lighting that convey wellness and authenticity. ● Controls mood through your specified color palette and ritual environment variables. ● Delivers minimalist, high-end compositions that avoid harsh shadows, over-retouched skin, and cluttered backgrounds. ## Prompt

```
Create a 2:3 portrait advertisement in 1×2 vertical panels for a luxury beauty device, combining clinical credibility with aspirational lifestyle visuals.

**Panel 1 – Hero Technology & Design**

**Panel 2 – Luxury Ritual**
Adult using the {{product-name}} in {{ritual-environment}}. Natural window light with warm undertones. Healthy, natural skin glow. Mood: self-care, confidence, wellness.

**Visual Style**
Luxury beauty advertising, clinical-meets-lifestyle aesthetic, high realism, minimalist composition, premium wellness branding. Professional beauty and product photography. Soft, diffused, skin-friendly lighting. Balanced exposure, clean highlights, minimal and flattering shadows. Refined, trustworthy, calm, luxurious, modern, empowering.

**Brand Presentation**
Refined, science-backed, elegant personality. Logo minimal, embossed, or softly printed.

Avoid: harsh shadows, over-processed skin, cheap-looking device materials, unreadable branding, cluttered environments, oversaturated colors, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
