# Retro Film Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Retro Film Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates sunlit profile portraits with an authentic 1970s analog film aesthetic for photographers, designers, and creative professionals. This retro portrait photography prompt for Midjourney recreates the look of Kodak Portra 400 film shot on a Nikon FM2 with an 85mm lens, producing editorial-style portraits with soft diffused light, gentle shadow fall-off, and luminous highlights on the subject's face. The prompt specifies shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, natural grain, and warm color temperature that evokes vintage analog photography. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, allowing you to customize subject details while maintaining the characteristic film aesthetic. Use it for editorial portraiture, character design, social media content, or any project requiring authentic vintage portrait photography. This prompt is designed for photographers transitioning to AI image generation, graphic designers creating retro-themed campaigns, and content creators who need period-accurate 1970s portrait aesthetics without accessing physical film cameras or darkroom equipment. ● Produces profile portraits with sunlight illumination on face, soft diffused lighting, and gentle shadow transitions that mimic professional film photography ● Specifies Kodak Portra 400 film stock characteristics, Nikon FM2 camera body, 85mm lens focal length, and editorial portraiture framing conventions ● Generates natural film grain, warm brown tones, neutral cream backgrounds, and shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh typical of 1970s analog photography ● Excludes harsh shadows, digital artifacts, and oversaturation through negative prompting to maintain period-accurate film aesthetic ## Prompt
