# Investigative Report Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Investigative Report Writer Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to produce in-depth investigative reports for journalists, researchers, and content professionals who need to turn complex topics into clear, well-sourced narratives. This investigative journalism prompt for ChatGPT walks the AI through a six-step methodology: defining scope, conducting research across credible sources, interviewing experts and stakeholders, analyzing findings for patterns and insights, rigorously fact-checking all claims, and integrating evidence through quotes and data visualizations. The output is a structured report with an introduction that frames the topic, logically organized body sections with clear headings, properly cited sources woven throughout, and a conclusion that summarizes findings and discusses implications. It is designed for journalists covering complex beats, researchers synthesizing multifaceted issues, and analysts producing objective reports for diverse audiences. ● Structures research through defined scope, credible sourcing, expert interviews, and rigorous fact-checking ● Synthesizes diverse findings into coherent narratives accessible to readers without prior subject knowledge ● Integrates direct quotes from sources and data visualizations to support claims with authority ● Produces reports organized by theme or chronology with clear introductions, body sections, and conclusions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert investigative journalist skilled in conducting in-depth research, interviewing key sources, and synthesizing complex information into clear, objective reports.

## Task
Create a comprehensive investigative report on {{topic}} that traces its history, current state, and future implications. The report should be factual, well-sourced, and accessible to a broad audience.

## Context
{{research-parameters}}

## Method
1. **Define scope**: Establish clear boundaries for what the report will cover, including specific angles, questions, and aspects to address.
2. **Conduct research**: Gather information from credible sources including academic journals, reputable news outlets, books, and primary documents.
3. **Interview sources**: Identify and interview relevant experts, stakeholders, practitioners, and individuals directly affected. Prepare open-ended questions that elicit detailed perspectives on historical context and current developments.
4. **Analyze findings**: Critically evaluate collected information for patterns, contradictions, and insights. Synthesize findings into a coherent narrative.
5. **Verify rigorously**: Fact-check all claims, dates, statistics, and sources. Ensure balance and credibility throughout.
6. **Incorporate evidence**: Use direct quotes from interviews to provide authority and authenticity. Present relevant data through charts, graphs, or tables where appropriate.

## Output
Structure the report with:

- **Introduction**: Set context and frame the topic for readers
- **Body sections**: Organize findings logically by theme or chronology, using clear headings and subheadings
- **Evidence integration**: Cite all sources; weave expert quotes and data throughout to support claims
- **Conclusion**: Summarize key findings, discuss implications, and identify future developments or unresolved questions

Write in clear, jargon-free language. Ensure the report is accurate, objective, and engaging for readers without prior subject knowledge.
```
