# Pomodoro Technique Planner for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Pomodoro Technique Planner for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates customized time-blocking schedules using the Pomodoro method for independent workers and small business owners. This Pomodoro technique prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific work context - including the task at hand, your environment, typical distractions, and preferred session length - then produces a structured plan that divides your work into manageable intervals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a markdown table with 4-6 sequenced subtasks, custom session durations, and tailored break periods, plus focus strategies that directly counter your stated distractions. Solopreneurs juggling client work, content creation, or product development use it to maintain deep focus without burning out, while freelancers and consultants apply it to billable project work that demands sustained concentration. ● Breaks complex projects into 4-6 concrete subtasks with sequenced Pomodoro intervals ● Adjusts session and break lengths based on your stated preferences and work environment ● Provides focus tips targeted at your specific distractions (notifications, noise, context-switching) ● Includes practical break strategies that fit your workspace and energy patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in time management for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a personalized Pomodoro Technique plan that breaks down the user's work into focused sessions with strategic breaks.

## Context
The Pomodoro Technique uses timed work intervals (traditionally 25 minutes) followed by short breaks to maintain focus and prevent burnout. Adapt the session length, break timing, and task breakdown to fit the user's specific work context and distraction patterns.

{{work-context}} should include: the specific task to accomplish, the work environment, common distractions, and preferred session duration.

## Output
First, provide a concise explanation of the Pomodoro Technique tailored to the user's situation.

Then present the plan as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Pomodoro Number** (sequential numbering)
- **Task** (specific subtask to complete)
- **Duration** (work session length)
- **Break Duration** (rest period after the session)

After the table, include:
- 3-4 focus tips customized to counter the user's specific distractions
- 2-3 strategies for using breaks effectively in their work environment

Break the main task into 4-6 concrete subtasks that build toward completion. Adjust session and break lengths based on stated preferences while maintaining productive rhythm.
```
