The Lifestyle Food Advertisement Visuals is a free AI prompt that generates authentic social dining scenes featuring friends sharing food, designed for restaurants, food brands, and delivery apps seeking engaging visual marketing content. This lifestyle food photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 3:4 portrait-format image of two friends laughing together at a casual restaurant table while sharing a loaded dish - complete with warm golden lighting, detailed food textures like cheese melt stretch, and customizable brand typography overlays. The prompt balances eye-level 50mm lifestyle photography framing with shallow depth of field, sharp focus on appetizing food details, and a soft-blurred restaurant background that creates an inviting, comforting atmosphere. Food brands can replace {{brand-name}} to integrate their own logo and tagline seamlessly into the composition.
