# Luxury Product Advertisement Panel Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Product Advertisement Panel Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that produces cohesive multi-panel advertisement layouts for high-end wellness and technology products. This luxury product advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates six coordinated panels in a 4:5 portrait grid format, combining architectural hero photography, macro material close-ups, air-quality visualization, lifestyle wellness scenes, smart interface details, and ambient night-mode shots. The prompt structures each panel with specific camera angles, lighting direction, and compositional guidelines to maintain visual consistency across the campaign while highlighting different product features and use contexts. Brand managers, product marketers, and creative teams working on smart home devices, air purifiers, wellness technology, or premium consumer products use this when they need magazine-quality advertisement assets without coordinating expensive multi-location photo shoots. ● Structures six distinct panel compositions including sculptural hero shots in minimalist interiors, macro material texture details, functional airflow visualization, lifestyle wellness context, smart display interface close-ups, and ambient night lighting modes. ● Specifies professional architectural photography techniques with soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field for detail shots, and low-key ambient lighting for evening scenes to create a refined, high-end aesthetic. ● Accepts customizable color palette and product name variables to adapt the grid layout for different brands, product lines, and campaign identities while maintaining consistent visual language. ● Produces 4:5 portrait format optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, mobile screens, and vertical digital displays where multi-panel storytelling performs effectively. ## Prompt
