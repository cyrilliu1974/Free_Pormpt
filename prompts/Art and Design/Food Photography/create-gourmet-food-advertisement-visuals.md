# Gourmet Food Advertisement Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Gourmet Food Advertisement Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional hero shots of branded dishes with studio-quality lighting, composition, and integrated typography for restaurants, food brands, and marketing teams. This food photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces portrait 3:4 images with shallow depth of field, warm directional lighting, and razor-sharp focus on texture details like cheese melts and grill marks. It outputs a centered composition on a rustic surface with a softly blurred restaurant background, plus brand-name and tagline overlays in specified fonts and colors. Use it to create menu hero images, social media ads, app banners, or print posters without a photoshoot. Reach for this prompt when you need appetite-appeal visuals that look like they came from a professional food photographer's studio, but generated in seconds with full control over dish components, branding, and mood. ● Photorealistic gourmet dish rendering with 85mm lens perspective, shallow depth of field, and eye-level or slight low-angle framing ● Warm directional side lighting that highlights texture, creates soft shadows, and enhances the juicy, savory appeal of ingredients ● Integrated typography overlay with customizable brand name in bold modern serif and tagline in thin sans-serif, positioned and color-coded for readability ● Portrait 3:4 aspect ratio optimized for mobile screens, Instagram Stories, app interfaces, and vertical print formats ## Prompt
