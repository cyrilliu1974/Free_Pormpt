# Informal to Formal Text Rewriter

## 簡介

The Informal to Formal Text Rewriter is a free AI prompt that transforms everyday casual language into professionally polished compositions for business, academic, and official contexts. This informal to formal text prompt for ChatGPT systematically replaces slang and colloquialisms with formal equivalents, expands contractions, restructures fragmented or run-on sentences, and elevates vocabulary while preserving your original meaning. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it practical whether you need to formalize an email draft, polish a report, or convert meeting notes into professional correspondence. The prompt follows a six-step process that addresses tone, grammar, sentence structure, and logical flow without adding unnecessary jargon. This prompt is ideal for professionals preparing official communications, students converting draft notes into academic prose, or anyone needing to present information with appropriate formality. ● Replaces slang, idioms, and contractions with formal language standards ● Restructures sentences for clarity, coherence, and logical progression ● Elevates vocabulary appropriately without introducing unnecessary complexity ● Delivers rewritten text that adheres to business, academic, and official writing conventions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in formal writing and composition.

## Task
Transform the provided informal text into a formal, professional composition suitable for business, academic, or official contexts.

## Process
1. Replace slang, idiomatic phrases, and colloquial language with formal equivalents while preserving the original meaning.
2. Expand all contractions (can't → cannot, won't → will not, it's → it is).
3. Restructure run-on sentences, fragments, and awkward constructions for clarity and coherence.
4. Elevate vocabulary where appropriate, ensuring precision without unnecessary complexity.
5. Ensure smooth logical flow between ideas using appropriate transitional phrases.
6. Verify grammatical accuracy, punctuation, and adherence to formal writing conventions.

## Input
{{informal-text}}

## Output
Provide the formally rewritten text that maintains the original message and intent but is grammatically correct, professionally toned, and free from informal language. Present only the rewritten composition without commentary.
```
