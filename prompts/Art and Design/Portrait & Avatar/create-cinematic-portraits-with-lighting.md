# Cinematic Portrait Lighting Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait Lighting Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that produces professional studio-style portraits with dramatic side lighting and precise technical controls for photographers, character designers, and visual artists. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney generates portraits with a single colored light source illuminating the subject from the left, creating high-contrast shadows against a clean white gradient background. The prompt includes pre-configured camera settings - 85mm lens, f/1.8 aperture, shallow depth of field, ISO 400, and 1/200s shutter speed - that replicate the look of professional portrait photography. You customize the subject description and lighting color, and Midjourney renders the shot at eye level with the subject looking slightly off-camera. Works on Midjourney v5 and later, as well as compatible generators like Flux and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need polished portrait work for character concepts, editorial visuals, or social media content that demands a cinematic look without manually tuning technical parameters. ● Produces portraits with cinematic side lighting in any color you specify - red, blue, amber, green - for mood and visual impact. ● Pre-built camera settings (85mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field) replicate professional portrait photography techniques. ● Clean white gradient background isolates the subject, making the image easy to composite or use in design layouts. ● Excludes glasses and jewelry by default for a streamlined, distraction-free portrait. ## Prompt

```
Portrait of {{subject-description}} illuminated by {{lighting}} light on the left side, looking slightly off-camera, against a white gradient background; cinematic lighting, high contrast, shot from eye level. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, ISO 400, f/1.8, 1/200s. --ar 4:5 --s 750 --q 2 --c 15 --no glasses, jewelry
```
