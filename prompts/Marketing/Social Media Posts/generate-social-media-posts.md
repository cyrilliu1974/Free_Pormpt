# Social Media Post Generator for AI Topics

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Generator for AI Topics is a free AI prompt that creates educational and engaging social media content about artificial intelligence for digital marketers and content creators. This social media post prompt for ChatGPT generates five complete posts that educate audiences about AI technology, developments, and trends. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce platform-optimized content that balances informative and entertaining elements. Each post includes an attention-grabbing headline, tailored copy, relevant hashtags, and a media recommendation. Real-world applications include launching AI product campaigns, sharing industry news, sparking discussions about AI ethics, and building thought leadership around emerging technologies. Reach for this prompt when you need to maintain consistent social media presence around AI topics but want to save hours of brainstorming and formatting. It transforms a single campaign context variable into five distinct, publication-ready posts. ● Produces five complete posts with headlines, optimized copy, hashtags, and media suggestions in one generation ● Adapts tone and length for different platforms while maintaining accessible language that avoids heavy jargon ● Mixes content types including news updates, opinion pieces, and thought-provoking questions to drive interaction ● Structures output consistently so posts are ready to schedule or publish with minimal editing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketer and social media expert creating content about AI developments, trends, and news.

## Task
Generate 5 social media posts that educate and engage audiences about AI technology. Each post should spark interest and discussion about AI's potential and implications.

## Context
{{campaign-context}}

## Requirements
- Write for a wide audience: accessible language, minimal jargon
- Mix content types: news updates, opinion pieces, and thought-provoking questions
- Optimize content length and style for the specified platforms
- Balance informative and entertaining elements to encourage interaction and sharing

## Output
For each of the 5 posts, provide:

**Post [N]:**
- **Headline:** [concise, attention-grabbing title]
- **Content:** [platform-optimized copy that highlights the AI topic]
- **Hashtags:** [mix of industry-specific and trending hashtags]
- **Media Suggestion:** [recommended visual—image, video, or infographic]

Format each post clearly and maintain consistent structure across all five.
```
