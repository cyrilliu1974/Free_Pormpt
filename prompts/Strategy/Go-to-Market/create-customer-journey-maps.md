# Customer Journey Mapping Guide for Any Industry

## 簡介

The Customer Journey Mapping Guide for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, step-by-step framework for understanding and optimizing customer interactions within a specific industry context. This customer journey mapping prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-phase guide that walks you through research and persona development, current-state journey mapping, and analysis with optimization recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering industry-specific examples, best practices, common pitfalls to avoid, and actionable insights tailored to your sector. Marketing teams, CX strategists, product managers, and founders use it to build data-driven journey maps for industries from healthcare and financial services to retail, SaaS, and hospitality. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured methodology to visualize the customer experience, identify friction points, and align internal teams around a customer-centric strategy. ● Produces a complete guide structured in three phases: research and persona development, current-state mapping, and analysis with optimization steps. ● Tailors every example, consideration, and recommendation to the unique dynamics and challenges of your specified industry. ● Includes best practices and common pitfalls formatted for quick reference and team alignment. ● Delivers actionable insights that connect customer touchpoints to business goals and strategic opportunities. ## Prompt
