# Marketing Strategy Plan Generator for Product Launches

## 簡介

The Marketing Strategy Plan Generator for Product Launches is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive go-to-market strategies for new products by analyzing your product details, target audience, and competitive advantages. This marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT works through a two-phase process: brainstorming followed by strategy formulation. You provide three inputs - product description, target market definition, and unique selling points - and the prompt returns a structured plan covering key marketing messages, positioning, priority channels and tactics, creative campaign ideas, and pricing recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Marketing managers use it when launching consumer products, SaaS tools, or B2B services to move quickly from concept to actionable strategy without starting from a blank page. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured marketing plan that ties every tactical choice back to your target audience and competitive differentiation. ● Produces brainstormed ideas followed by a cohesive strategy with clear rationale for each recommendation ● Identifies priority marketing channels and explains why they fit the target market ● Generates campaign concepts and content ideas tailored to the product's unique selling points ● Includes pricing and promotional tactics aligned with competitive positioning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist developing a comprehensive launch strategy for a new product.

## Task
Analyze the provided product information and create a tailored marketing strategy that addresses positioning, channels, campaigns, pricing, and promotion. Begin with brainstorming, then develop ideas into a cohesive strategy with clear rationale for each recommendation.

## Context
Product description: {{product-description}}

Target market: {{target-market}}

Unique selling points: {{usp}}

## Requirements
- Make reasonable assumptions for missing details, but do not invent product features or selling points beyond what is provided
- Ensure all strategic recommendations tie directly back to the target market and unique selling points
- Provide clear, logical rationale for each strategic choice

## Output
**Brainstorming:**
- Initial ideas covering potential marketing channels, tactics, key messages, and campaign concepts

**Marketing Strategy:**

**Key Marketing Messages and Product Positioning**
[Well-organized paragraphs explaining positioning and core messages]

**Priority Marketing Channels and Tactics**
[Detailed recommendations for which channels to use and how]

**Marketing Campaign, Content, and Creative Ideas**
[High-level campaign concepts and content strategies]

**Pricing and Promotional Strategies**
[Approach to pricing and promotional tactics]

**Strategic Alignment**
[Explanation of how the strategy connects to the target market and unique selling points]
```
