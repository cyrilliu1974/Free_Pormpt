# Fashion Photography Side-View Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Photography Side-View Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality side-view images of walking subjects with precise control over outfit, accessories, and styling details. This fashion photography prompt for Midjourney produces high-resolution commercial shots featuring soft natural lighting, eye-level camera angles, and shallow depth of field that keeps the subject in sharp focus against a clean minimal backdrop. You specify the subject's outfit, bag description (including color, material, and style), and aspect ratio to generate images suited for lookbooks, e-commerce, editorial spreads, or social media campaigns. Designers, creative directors, e-commerce brands, and content creators reach for this prompt when they need consistent, professional fashion photography without a physical shoot. ● Produces side-view walking shots with soft natural lighting, eye-level perspective, and shallow depth of field for professional editorial quality. ● Accepts detailed specifications for subject outfit, bag type, material, and color, along with customizable aspect ratios. ● Delivers high-resolution images with sharp subject focus and clean minimal backdrops suitable for commercial use. ● Works across major AI image models including Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana for flexible deployment. ## Prompt

