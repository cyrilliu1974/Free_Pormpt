# Fashion Portrait Variation Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Portrait Variation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-fashion editorial portrait descriptions for content creators and fashion brands. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces consistent model imagery with a specific K-pop-inspired aesthetic - slender silhouette, Hollywood waves, clean-girl makeup, and sultry expression - while letting you customize outfit details and color schemes through two variables. The prompt specifies hair styling, makeup details, accessories, and poses, making it easy to generate a cohesive series of fashion portraits that maintain the same model look across different outfits and palettes. Use it for e-commerce lookbooks, social media content, fashion blog headers, or brand mood boards. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-quality fashion photography with a consistent model aesthetic but want to experiment with different styling directions without managing multiple detailed prompts. ● Produces a specific K-pop idol aesthetic with defined facial features, Hollywood wave hair, and clean-girl makeup that stays consistent across generations. ● Includes pre-specified luxury accessories - layered rhinestone chokers, pearl drop earrings, and jeweled butterfly clips - for a complete editorial look. ● Two variables let you swap outfit details and color schemes while keeping the model's appearance, pose, and styling framework intact. ● Works across major image generation platforms to create fashion editorial content without photoshoots or professional models. ## Prompt

```

```
