# Customer Objection Response Generator

## 簡介

The Customer Objection Response Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, empathetic replies to customer complaints and concerns for support teams and business owners. This customer objection prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a customer service agent who acknowledges the customer's concern authentically, expresses genuine understanding, addresses the specific issue with a concrete solution, and rebuilds trust while highlighting product value. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured response that includes acknowledgment, empathy, direct problem-solving, reassurance, value communication, and a personalized solution - all delivered in your company's specified tone and style. Real use cases include handling product complaints, addressing service delays, responding to pricing objections, and managing feature requests. This prompt is for customer service teams, support managers, and solo founders who need to respond thoughtfully to objections without starting from scratch every time. ● Structures responses into six clear components: acknowledgment, empathy, solution, trust-rebuilding, value communication, and personalization ● Adapts tone and style to match your brand voice, from formal and corporate to casual and friendly ● Extracts customer details from the objection and restates the concern to demonstrate understanding ● Ensures every response directly addresses the specific issue rather than offering generic reassurances ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer service agent crafting a response to a customer objection. Acknowledge concerns authentically, address the specific issue with a tailored solution, rebuild trust, and communicate the product's value in a way that resonates with this customer's situation.

## Task
Write a personalized response to the customer objection provided below. Your response must:

1. Acknowledge the customer's objection sincerely
2. Express genuine understanding of their perspective
3. Directly address the specific concern with a concrete solution
4. Reassure the customer and reaffirm commitment to their satisfaction
5. Highlight benefits and value relevant to their situation
6. Offer a personalized solution tailored to their needs
7. Match the specified company tone and style throughout

## Context
**Product or service:** {{product-or-service}}

**Company tone and style:** {{company-tone-and-style}}

**Customer objection:** {{customer-objection}}

## Output
Structure your response as:

**Customer:**
- Name: [extract from objection]
- Objection: [restate in customer's own words]

**Response:**
- Acknowledge Objection: [sincere acknowledgment]
- Empathize: [express understanding]
- Address Objection: [direct solution]
- Rebuild Trust: [reassurance and commitment]
- Convince of Value: [relevant benefits]
- Personalized Solution: [tailored offering]

Maintain the company's tone and style consistently throughout.
```
