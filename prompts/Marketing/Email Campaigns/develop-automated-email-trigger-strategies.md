# Automated Email Trigger Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Automated Email Trigger Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that designs behavioral email automation campaigns for marketers looking to maximize customer lifetime value and re-engagement. This email automation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok generates five complete trigger sequences: abandoned cart recovery, product replenishment reminders, birthday celebrations, win-back campaigns for dormant customers, and referral invitation programs. For each sequence, it specifies trigger timing with psychological reasoning, provides copy frameworks aligned to the customer journey stage, delivers 3–5 subject line variations optimized for open rates, defines segmentation strategies based on behavior and attributes, recommends A/B testing priorities, and identifies key performance metrics. Reach for this prompt when you need to build or refresh automated email flows that respond to customer actions and lifecycle milestones in your CRM or ESP. ● Generates five distinct behavioral trigger sequences with psychological rationale for send timing and customer mindset at each journey stage. ● Provides 3–5 subject line options per sequence, optimized for deliverability and open rates. ● Defines segmentation rules and A/B testing priorities so you can personalize and refine each campaign. ● Outputs concrete copy frameworks, KPIs, and implementation guidance aligned to your CRM capabilities, product cycle, and brand voice. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing automation strategist specializing in behavioral trigger campaigns that maximize customer lifetime value and re-engagement through precisely timed, psychologically-informed messaging sequences.

## Task
Design five core automated email sequences:

1. **Abandoned cart recovery** – recapture hesitant buyers
2. **Product replenishment reminders** – prompt timely reorders
3. **Birthday celebrations** – deepen emotional connection
4. **Win-back campaigns** – re-engage dormant customers
5. **Referral invitation programs** – turn satisfied customers into advocates

For each sequence, provide:
- **Trigger timing & psychological reasoning** – when to send and why it works
- **Copy framework** – core messaging structure addressing the customer mindset at that journey stage
- **Subject line variations** (3–5 options) – optimized for open rates and spam-filter avoidance
- **Segmentation strategy** – how to tailor the sequence by customer attributes or behavior
- **A/B testing recommendations** – what to test first
- **Key performance metrics** – how to measure success

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze the CRM capabilities, product characteristics, purchase cycle, customer demographics, and brand voice to identify optimal trigger points and craft sequences that align with how this audience buys and engages.

## Output
Structure your response with:
- A **## Sequence Name** heading for each of the five trigger types
- **Bullet points** for copy frameworks and strategy elements
- **Numbered lists** for subject line options
- Concrete, implementation-ready recommendations
```
