# Business Concept First Principles Breakdown Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Concept First Principles Breakdown Prompt is a free AI prompt that deconstructs business concepts by revealing hidden assumptions, examining how beliefs form, and showing how information asymmetry drives real-world outcomes. It applies Theory of Knowledge and first-principles reasoning to strip away surface-level definitions and expose the gap between what people think they know and what actually drives results in practice. This business concept prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured analysis that moves from common beliefs through evidence quality assessment to fundamental truths, complete with a Theory of Mind mapping of what different stakeholders know or misunderstand. Use it when you need to move beyond textbook definitions and understand the psychological and informational dynamics that make a concept succeed or fail in the real world. ● Distinguishes between perceived knowledge and operational reality by assessing evidence quality and separating correlation from causation. ● Identifies 3-4 unspoken premises people hold about the concept and contrasts each with what close examination reveals. ● Maps what customers, employees, competitors, and partners know, believe, or misunderstand to reveal how perspective-taking changes strategic decisions. ● Synthesizes how understanding human knowledge dynamics transforms effectiveness with the concept in real business situations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in first-principles reasoning and epistemology applied to business. You deconstruct concepts by exposing hidden assumptions, examining knowledge sources, and revealing how psychology and information asymmetry shape outcomes.

## Task
Analyze {{business-concept}} using Theory of Knowledge and first-principles thinking. Strip away surface-level definitions to reveal what people actually believe, why they believe it, how those beliefs form, and where assumptions break down. Connect this analysis to concrete business outcomes, emphasizing how perspective-taking and information gaps drive success or failure.

## Context
The user wants to move beyond superficial understanding into the foundational dynamics that make this concept work in practice. Focus on exposing gaps between perceived knowledge and operational reality.

## Output
Structure your response as:

**The Concept**  
Brief acknowledgment of the topic.

**What We Think We Know**  
Common beliefs, standard definitions, and widely accepted frameworks around this concept.

**How We Actually Know It**  
Sources of this knowledge (case studies, social proof, theory, empirical data). Assess evidence quality and distinguish correlation from causation.

**Hidden Assumptions Exposed**  
3-4 unspoken premises people hold about this concept, each paired with what's actually true when examined closely.

**First Principles Breakdown**  
3 fundamental truths that remain when all assumptions are stripped away.

**Theory of Mind Application**  
Map what different stakeholders (customers, employees, competitors, partners) know, believe, or misunderstand. Identify information gaps and show how perspective-taking changes strategic decisions.

**The Real Insight**  
Synthesize how understanding the human knowledge dynamics—what people know, assume, and communicate—transforms effectiveness with this concept in practice.
```
