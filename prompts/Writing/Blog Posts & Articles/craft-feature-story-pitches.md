# Feature Story Pitch Generator for Journalists

## 簡介

The Feature Story Pitch Generator for Journalists is a free AI prompt that creates diverse, newsworthy feature story pitches tailored to a specific publication and audience. This feature story pitch prompt for ChatGPT produces 5-7 complete pitches, each containing a sharp headline, a 2-3 sentence story summary, potential sources or reporting angles, and an explanation of why it resonates with the target readership. Writers supply the publication-and-audience context and their preferred story themes, and the prompt returns pitches grounded in current trends, human interest angles, and fresh perspectives on familiar topics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured, pitch-ready output that editors expect. Freelance journalists, magazine writers, and editorial teams reach for it when they need to quickly generate story ideas that match editorial calendars and reader interests. ● Produces 5-7 pitches per run, each with a catchy, specific headline and concise story summary ● Suggests potential interviewees, sources, or reporting angles to deepen each story ● Explains why each pitch resonates with the target publication's audience ● Prioritizes timeliness, human interest, and unique perspectives to increase acceptance rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert feature story writer who develops compelling, newsworthy pitches.

## Task
Generate a diverse range of feature story pitches tailored to the specified publication and audience. Each pitch must include:
- A catchy headline
- A brief story summary (2-3 sentences)
- Potential sources, interviewees, or reporting angles
- Why it resonates with the target audience

Consider current trends, human interest angles, and fresh perspectives on familiar topics. Prioritize timeliness and reader engagement.

## Context
Publication & audience: {{publication-and-audience}}
Preferred story themes: {{story-themes}}

## Output
Deliver 5-7 pitches as a numbered list. Separate each pitch clearly with white space. Make headlines sharp and specific, not generic.
```
