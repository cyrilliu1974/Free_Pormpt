# Complementary Business Partnership Finder Prompt

## 簡介

The Complementary Business Partnership Finder Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes market landscapes and identifies strategic partnership opportunities for businesses seeking to expand their reach and enhance offerings. This business partnership prompt for ChatGPT takes your company profile - industry, core offerings, target market, geographical focus, and size - and delivers a structured markdown table of 5-8 vetted partnership candidates. Each recommendation includes the strategic rationale, synergy areas where collaboration creates mutual value, and 2-3 actionable next steps to initiate contact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying a four-stage analysis framework that evaluates industry ecosystem players, assesses their strengths and unique value propositions, identifies mutual growth opportunities, and prioritizes by strategic fit and feasibility. Business development teams use it to streamline partnership scouting, sales leaders deploy it to find channel partners, and market expansion strategists rely on it to map alliance opportunities in new territories. ● Outputs a clean three-column table (Company Name, Partnership Opportunity, Next Steps) optimized for executive review and team collaboration ● Focuses exclusively on partnerships that demonstrably expand market reach or enhance product and service offerings ● Delivers realistic, implementable recommendations aligned with your company size, industry position, and geographical focus ● Provides concrete next steps - introductory email angles, mutual value talking points, and relationship-building tactics - so you move from analysis to outreach immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business development expert specializing in partnership identification and market expansion.

## Task
Analyze the market landscape and propose complementary businesses for strategic partnerships that will expand market reach and enhance product/service offerings.

## Context
**Business Profile:**
{{business-profile}}

(Include: industry, core offerings, target market, geographical focus, and company size)

**Analysis Framework:**
1. Research companies within the relevant industry ecosystem
2. Evaluate their strengths, weaknesses, and unique value propositions
3. Identify synergy areas where partnerships create mutual growth opportunities
4. Prioritize partnerships by strategic fit and implementation feasibility

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Company Name | Partnership Opportunity | Next Steps |
|--------------|------------------------|------------|

**Requirements:**
- Include 5-8 partnership candidates
- Partnership Opportunity: describe the strategic rationale, synergies, and mutual value creation potential
- Next Steps: provide 2-3 concrete, actionable steps to initiate contact and build the relationship
- Prioritize partnerships that demonstrably expand market reach or enhance offerings
- Ensure all recommendations are realistic and aligned with the business profile provided
```
