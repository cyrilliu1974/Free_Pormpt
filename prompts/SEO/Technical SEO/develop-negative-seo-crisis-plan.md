# Negative SEO Attack Crisis Management Plan

## 簡介

The Negative SEO Attack Crisis Management Plan is a free AI prompt that builds a tailored, actionable checklist to respond to and recover from negative SEO attacks targeting a specific website. This negative SEO crisis plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured four-section response: immediate priority actions to stop the attack, ongoing monitoring and prevention measures, reputation management tactics, and long-term SEO strategy adjustments to strengthen resilience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting a single website URL and delivering a concise, checklist-style plan that SEO teams can execute immediately. Real use cases include responding to toxic backlink campaigns, combating content scraping or duplication attacks, addressing fake negative reviews, and recovering from sudden ranking drops caused by malicious activity. This prompt is for in-house SEO managers, agency teams, and technical SEO consultants who need a rapid, structured response framework when a client or property comes under negative SEO attack. ● Produces immediate-action checklists prioritized by impact and urgency to stop ongoing attacks quickly ● Includes proactive monitoring steps and prevention measures to catch future threats early ● Delivers reputation management tactics that address both technical SEO issues and public perception ● Provides long-term strategy adjustments designed to harden the site against subsequent attacks and improve overall SEO health ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in crisis management for negative SEO attacks.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, actionable crisis management plan to mitigate the impact of a negative SEO attack on {{website-url}}. Deliver the plan as a concise, easy-to-follow checklist.

## Output
Structure your plan with these four sections:

**Crisis Management Plan for Negative SEO Attack**

Website: {{website-url}}

**Immediate Actions:**
1. [First priority action]
2. [Second priority action]
3. [Third priority action]

**Ongoing Monitoring and Prevention:**
- [Monitoring step 1]
- [Monitoring step 2]
- [Prevention measure 1]
- [Prevention measure 2]

**Reputation Management:**
• [Reputation tactic 1]
• [Reputation tactic 2]
• [Reputation tactic 3]

**Long-term SEO Strategy Adjustments:**
→ [Strategy adjustment 1]
→ [Strategy adjustment 2]
→ [Strategy adjustment 3]

## Requirements
- Tailor all recommendations specifically to the provided website URL
- Prioritize immediate actions that deliver rapid impact mitigation
- Include detection mechanisms to identify future attacks early
- Address online reputation repair alongside technical SEO fixes
- Provide recovery-focused long-term adjustments to improve overall SEO health
```
