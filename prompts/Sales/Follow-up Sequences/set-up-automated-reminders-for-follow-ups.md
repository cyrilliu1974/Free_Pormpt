# Automated Follow-Up System for Inactive Leads

## 簡介

The Automated Follow-Up System for Inactive Leads is a free AI prompt that creates detailed implementation guides for re-engaging dormant prospects through structured email sequences. This follow-up sequences prompt for ChatGPT helps email marketers design complete reactivation systems tailored to specific lead types, inactivity periods, CRM platforms, industries, and value propositions. It produces actionable guides covering lead data analysis, segmentation strategies, email template creation, automation workflows, tracking mechanisms, A/B testing protocols, and continuous optimization loops. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering concrete recommendations with numbered steps, tactical bullet points, and sentence structure examples that enhance readability and engagement. Use it when you need to systematically convert cold leads back into active prospects rather than simply drafting individual follow-up emails. ● Analyzes lead inactivity patterns and segments dormant contacts by characteristics and duration ● Creates multi-stage email sequences with progressively persuasive messaging and clear sentence structures ● Designs automation rules and scheduling logic for timely dispatch based on lead behavior ● Establishes tracking frameworks for open rates, click-through rates, conversions, and A/B test results ● Builds feedback loops and optimization protocols for continuous campaign improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing specialist designing an automated follow-up system to re-engage inactive leads.

## Task
Create a detailed step-by-step guide for building a personalized email sequence that converts dormant leads back into active prospects. Structure your recommendations around these core activities:

1. Analyze lead data and inactivity patterns within the CRM
2. Develop a segmentation strategy based on lead characteristics and inactivity duration
3. Create email templates with clear, engaging sentence structures
4. Design a follow-up sequence with progressively persuasive messaging
5. Implement automation rules for timely email dispatch
6. Establish tracking mechanisms for open rates, click-through rates, and conversions
7. Develop A/B testing protocols to optimize content and timing
8. Create a feedback loop for continuous system improvement

## Context
Lead type: {{lead-type}}
Inactivity period: {{inactivity-period}}
CRM platform: {{crm-platform}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

## Output
Provide a comprehensive implementation guide using:
- Numbered lists for main steps
- Bullet points for sub-steps and tactical details
- Concrete examples of effective email sentence structures that enhance readability and engagement
- Specific recommendations tailored to the lead type, inactivity period, CRM platform, industry, and unique selling proposition provided
```
