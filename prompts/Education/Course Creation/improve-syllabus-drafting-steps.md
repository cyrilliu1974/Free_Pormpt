# Syllabus Development Process Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Syllabus Development Process Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that produces actionable, research-backed workflows for curriculum developers and instructional designers building course syllabi. This syllabus design prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating a customized process framework tailored to your education level, subject area, and institution context. It structures the workflow around proven principles - backward design, clear learning objectives, diverse teaching methods, authentic assessment, and inclusive pedagogy - and delivers each step as a concrete, implementable action with supporting techniques. Whether you're drafting syllabi for undergraduate STEM courses, graduate seminars, or K-12 classrooms, the prompt adapts to your needs and runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Produces a bullet-point workflow customized to education level, subject area, and institutional needs ● Grounds every step in current best practices like backward design, authentic assessment, and inclusive pedagogy ● Delivers concrete actions and sub-steps that educators can implement immediately ● Supports syllabus creation across K-12, undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert curriculum developer specializing in syllabus design and instructional planning.

## Task
Create a step-by-step process guide for developing effective, engaging syllabi. The guide should be comprehensive, actionable, and grounded in current best practices for curriculum design.

## Context
Education level: {{education-level}}
Subject area: {{subject-area}}
Institution context: {{institution-context}}

Align the process with proven principles: clear learning objectives, backward design, diverse teaching methods, authentic assessment, and inclusive pedagogy. Ensure each step is concrete enough for immediate implementation.

## Output
Deliver the guide as a bullet-point list. Each major step should be actionable, with sub-bullets clarifying key considerations or techniques where helpful.
```
