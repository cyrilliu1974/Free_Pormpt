# Guest Post Generator for Target Blogs

## 簡介

The Guest Post Generator for Target Blogs is a free AI prompt that produces publication-ready guest post drafts matched to a target blog's niche, voice, and audience. Whether you pitch tech publications, lifestyle magazines, or industry journals, this guest post prompt for ChatGPT helps you craft original, well-researched articles that editors want to publish. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured draft with title, introduction, three subheaded body sections backed by examples and data, conclusion, call-to-action author bio, and a full set of on-page optimization notes including target keywords, meta description, internal and external link opportunities, and image suggestions with alt text. Reach for this prompt when you need to write guest content that mirrors the editorial tone and depth of a specific blog without spending hours analyzing past articles. ● Matches target blog voice and conventions by inferring tone, structure, and content depth from the niche and audience you provide. ● Produces 100% original, scannable content with short paragraphs, descriptive subheadings, and specific examples or data supporting every claim. ● Reserves all promotional language for the call-to-action section, keeping the body educational and editor-friendly. ● Includes SEO optimization notes covering keywords, meta description, internal and external link suggestions, and image recommendations with alt-text ideas. ## Prompt

```
## Role

Guest-post copywriter producing publication-ready drafts and SEO guidance tailored to a specific blog's voice and editorial standards.

## Context

{{target-blog}}

*Format: blog name, URL, niche, and target audience in 2–4 sentences.*

## Task

Write one complete guest post draft that:

1. **Matches the blog's voice and depth** – infer tone, structure, and content conventions from the niche and audience; mirror the standards of well-edited blogs in that category.
2. **Follows guest-post editorial norms** – 100 % original content, short scannable paragraphs, descriptive subheadings (H2/H3), specific examples or data supporting every major claim, zero promotional language in the body.
3. **Delivers genuine value** – well-researched, informative, demonstrates subject-matter expertise.
4. **Reserves promotion for the CTA only** – author bio and link belong at the end.

## Output

### Guest Post Draft

**Title:** *[working headline]*

**Introduction**  
*[hook, context, preview of key points – 2–3 short paragraphs]*

**[Subheading 1]**  
*[body section with examples/data – 2–4 paragraphs]*

**[Subheading 2]**  
*[body section with examples/data – 2–4 paragraphs]*

**[Subheading 3]**  
*[body section with examples/data – 2–4 paragraphs]*

**Conclusion**  
*[summary, key takeaway, transition to CTA – 1–2 paragraphs]*

**Call to Action**  
*[author bio, link, soft invitation – 1–2 sentences]*

---

### On-Page Optimization Notes

- **Target keywords** – primary + 2–3 secondary terms  
- **Meta description** – 150–160 characters, benefit-driven  
- **Internal link opportunities** – 2–3 relevant anchor suggestions  
- **External links** – 2–3 authoritative sources cited  
- **Image suggestions** – featured image + 1–2 in-body visuals with alt-text ideas
```
