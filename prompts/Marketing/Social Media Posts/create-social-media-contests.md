# Social Media Contest Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Contest Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end social media contest campaigns for businesses looking to increase brand awareness and follower engagement. This social media contest prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and budget, then produces a full strategy including contest mechanics, legally compliant rules, prize descriptions aligned with your brand, and ready-to-publish promotional posts optimized for platform-specific features like Stories, Reels, hashtags, and tagging. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver viral contest elements that encourage sharing and organic reach. Use it when launching giveaways, product launches, seasonal campaigns, or any initiative where you need structured, goal-oriented contest design that resonates with your target audience. ● Develops contest ideas with clear participation mechanics and viral engagement elements ● Writes unambiguous, legally compliant rules covering eligibility, entry methods, and winner selection ● Crafts prize descriptions that connect brand values to audience interests and motivations ● Generates promotional post templates with platform-optimized formatting, hashtags, and calls-to-action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing expert specializing in contest design for brand awareness and follower engagement.

## Task
Create a complete social media contest strategy tailored to the specified business context. Deliver contest ideas, clear rules, prize descriptions, and ready-to-use promotional post templates.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
Contest budget: {{contest-budget}}

Design the contest to:
- Align with the brand's values and marketing objectives described in the business context
- Leverage platform-specific features (Stories, Reels, hashtags, tagging mechanics) appropriate to the platforms mentioned
- Include viral elements that encourage sharing and organic reach
- Ensure rules are unambiguous and legally compliant
- Target the audience demographics and psychographics provided

## Output
Provide your strategy using these headings with bullet points under each:

**Contest Idea**
- Core concept and participation mechanics
- Expected engagement outcomes

**Rules**
- Eligibility requirements
- Entry method and submission guidelines
- Duration and deadline
- Winner selection criteria
- Terms and conditions highlights

**Prize Description**
- Main prize details and value proposition
- Runner-up prizes (if applicable)
- How prizes connect to brand and audience interests

**Promotional Post Template**
- Attention-grabbing opening
- Clear call-to-action
- Hashtags and tagging instructions
- Urgency or incentive messaging
- Platform-optimized formatting
```
