# Pool Landscape Architecture Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Pool Landscape Architecture Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates editorial-quality ultra-wide shots of outdoor pools set in natural Mediterranean landscapes with mountain backdrops. The prompt produces cinematic architectural photography in the style of Julius Shulman, featuring minimalist modern design elements, natural materials like teak and terracotta, and carefully specified camera settings including 24mm wide-angle perspective, elevated three-quarter viewpoints, and deep depth of field. This pool landscape prompt for Midjourney and other AI image generators lets you customize the pool environment, surrounding plantings, and time-of-day lighting while maintaining professional architectural composition standards. It works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana with consistent editorial quality. Designers, architects, landscape professionals, and real estate marketers reach for it when they need studio-grade outdoor photography without a location shoot. ● Produces ultra-wide 16:9 cinematic shots with specified 24mm focal length, elevated three-quarter angles, and deep depth of field for full environmental context. ● Defines complete material and color direction: natural teak furniture, concrete tables, textured terracotta planters, warm earth tones with sage green Mediterranean accents. ● Includes technical camera settings (ISO 100, 1/250s shutter) and atmospheric effects (soft haze) modeled on Julius Shulman's architectural photography style. ● Variables let you swap pool types, landscape features, and lighting conditions (golden hour, midday, dusk) while preserving the editorial composition. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-wide cinematic shot of an {{pool-and-landscape}} with mountain views at {{time-and-lighting}}. Shot in the style of Julius Shulman architectural photography. --ar 16:9 --s 300 --q 2 --c 10
```
