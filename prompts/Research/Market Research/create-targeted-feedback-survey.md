# Survey Questionnaire Generator for Customer Feedback

## 簡介

The Survey Questionnaire Generator for Customer Feedback is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable surveys for businesses and researchers seeking to understand their target audience. This survey questionnaire prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete five-question survey including a title, introduction, mixed question types (multiple choice, rating scales, and open-ended), and a closing message. It designs questions that focus on respondent needs and experiences without leading language or survey fatigue, ensuring completion in under five minutes. Product managers use it to validate features, marketers deploy it to test messaging, and UX researchers apply it to gather qualitative and quantitative insights simultaneously. Reach for this prompt when you need a research-grade survey fast but lack dedicated survey design expertise or time to iterate through question formats manually. ● Outputs title, introduction, five structured questions, and thank-you closing in a single response ● Mixes question types to capture both measurable data and narrative feedback ● Avoids double-barreled, leading, or overly technical phrasing that skews results ● Keeps surveys short enough to maximize completion rates while gathering actionable insights ## Prompt

```
## Role

You design surveys that yield actionable feedback without leading respondents or exhausting them.

## Task

Create a complete five-question survey for {{product-or-service}} targeting {{target-audience}}.

## Requirements

**Question Design:**
- Focus every question on the respondent's needs, preferences, or experiences
- Use a mix of question types (multiple choice, rating scale, open-ended) to collect both quantitative and qualitative data
- Write in plain language suited to the target audience
- Avoid leading, double-barreled, or overly broad questions
- Keep the total survey completable in under five minutes

**Survey Components:**
1. Survey Title — short, reflects the survey's purpose
2. Survey Introduction — two to three sentences explaining what the survey is for and how responses will be used
3. Questions 1-5 — each with question type, question text, and answer options where applicable
4. Survey Closing — brief thank-you and any relevant next steps or contact details
```
