# Guest Posting Strategy Prompt for Link Building

## 簡介

The Guest Posting Strategy Prompt for Link Building is a free AI prompt that builds complete guest posting plans for content marketers and SEO specialists seeking to strengthen domain authority through strategic backlinks. This guest posting prompt for ChatGPT walks through a five-step process: qualifying high-authority blogs in your niche, developing personalized pitch templates, structuring valuable articles that engage host audiences, integrating backlinks naturally, and tracking performance metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and customizes every recommendation to your specific niche, target audience, brand voice, and website URL. Real-world use cases include SaaS companies building backlinks through industry blogs, agencies scaling outreach campaigns for clients, and solo marketers establishing thought leadership in competitive verticals. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, actionable roadmap that goes beyond generic advice and aligns guest content with both host blog expectations and your SEO goals. ● Identifies qualification criteria for finding relevant, high-authority blogs that accept guest contributions ● Provides pitch templates with personalization tactics proven to earn editor approval ● Outlines article structures and value propositions that satisfy both host readers and SEO objectives ● Details natural backlink placement techniques that comply with editorial standards while boosting domain authority ● Includes tracking metrics and iteration steps to measure guest post performance and refine future outreach ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer and SEO specialist executing a guest posting strategy to build authoritative backlinks and improve search rankings.

## Task
Develop a complete guest posting plan for {{niche}}, covering blog identification, outreach, content creation, and backlink integration. Deliver actionable steps the user can execute immediately.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Guest posts must align with the brand, deliver genuine value to host blog readers, and secure natural backlink placement that strengthens domain authority.

## Output
Provide a numbered list with main steps, each followed by bullet-point substeps:

1. **Blog identification & qualification** – criteria for finding high-authority, relevant blogs
2. **Pitch development** – templates and personalization tactics that earn acceptance
3. **Article creation** – structure, hooks, and value propositions that engage readers
4. **Backlink strategy** – natural placement techniques that satisfy hosts and boost SEO
5. **Tracking & iteration** – metrics to measure success and refine the approach

Each step should reference the niche, audience, and brand voice to ensure strategic fit.
```
