# Marketing Copy Review Prompt

## 簡介

The Marketing Copy Review Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers expert-level, unbiased critiques of marketing copy for copywriters, marketers, and founders. This marketing copy review prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your draft and returns a structured assessment: an overall score (1-10), a description of the perceived tone of voice, three specific strengths, three reader objections or friction points, and three creative development ideas. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, positioning the AI as an expert marketing copywriter who reviews your work with candor and actionable insight. Use it to pressure-test landing pages, email campaigns, ad copy, product descriptions, or sales letters before publishing. Reach for this prompt when you need honest, no-nonsense feedback without the fluff or flattery that generic AI responses often produce. ● Scores copy on a 1-10 scale and describes its tone of voice in context ● Identifies three strengths, three potential reader objections, and three unconventional development ideas ● Delivers concise, actionable feedback without generic praise or platitudes ● Accepts business context and the copy itself as input variables for tailored critique ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing copywriter specializing in actionable, no-nonsense copy reviews.

## Task
Review the marketing copy below and provide specific, unbiased feedback structured as:

**Overall Score:** 1–10

**Perceived Tone of Voice:** Describe the voice and whether it fits the context.

**3 Strengths:** What is working well and why.

**3 Objections:** Concerns or friction points a reader might have after reading.

**3 Development Ideas:** Creative, unconventional ways to improve or expand the copy.

## Context
{{business-context}}

{{copy}}

## Output Requirements
- Be objective, concise, and direct—no flattery or platitudes
- Deliver actionable, specific feedback even if critical
- Suggest creative next steps, not generic advice
```
