# Before After Food Infographic Generator

## 簡介

The Before After Food Infographic Generator is a free AI prompt that creates split-panel visual recipe breakdowns for food creators, fitness bloggers, and nutrition influencers. This before after food infographic prompt for Midjourney produces a 3:4 vertical image with two panels: the left displays raw ingredients arranged in a flat-lay composition on a neutral stone surface, while the right shows the finished plated dish with garnish and realistic texture. A calorie breakdown and ingredient list overlay the bottom section, along with the dish name and a short tagline at the top. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, and is ideal for Instagram posts, recipe blogs, meal-prep guides, and fitness content that needs to show nutritional transparency alongside appetizing presentation. Reach for this prompt when you need clean, Instagram-ready recipe visuals that combine aesthetic appeal with nutritional information in a single shareable graphic. ● Produces 3:4 aspect ratio split-panel layouts optimized for Instagram and Pinterest ● Left panel stages raw ingredients as separated flat-lay components on neutral stone ● Right panel renders the finished dish with realistic plating, garnish, and food texture ● Includes customizable text overlays for dish name, tagline, total calories, and per-ingredient calorie breakdown ## Prompt

```
Split-panel "Before vs After" food infographic, 3:4 aspect ratio, top-down flat lay photography.

LEFT PANEL ("BEFORE"): Raw ingredients for {{dish-name}} arranged and visually separated on a light neutral stone surface — {{ingredient-list}} displayed as whole, uncooked components (e.g. whole bananas above a bowl of oats and cacao powder).

RIGHT PANEL ("AFTER"): Hero shot of the finished {{dish-name}}, plated in a styled bowl with garnish and spoon beside it, showing realistic texture and any caramelization or finishing touches.

TEXT OVERLAY: Top corner displays "{{dish-name}}" with a short playful tagline. Bottom section shows total calories ({{total-calories}}) and a clean infographic-style breakdown listing each ingredient's calorie contribution.

STYLE: Clean minimal food infographic aesthetic, soft natural daylight, sharp food texture detail, light neutral stone background, fitness recipe / Instagram-ready presentation.

--ar 3:4
```
