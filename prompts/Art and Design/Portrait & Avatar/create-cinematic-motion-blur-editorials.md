# Cinematic Motion Blur Fashion Portrait Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Motion Blur Fashion Portrait Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces freeze-frame editorial shots with razor-sharp faces set against dynamic, motion-blurred environments for photographers, fashion creators, and brands. This motion blur portrait prompt for Midjourney and Flux places your subject in sharp focus while chaos swirls around them - wind-swept streets, exploding powder, crowding paparazzi - creating Vogue-caliber fashion editorials at 8k resolution with hyper-realistic skin texture and dramatic lighting. You define the chaos scenario and outfit; the prompt handles depth of field, face restoration, and cinematic framing in a 2:3 aspect ratio. Ideal for social content, lookbooks, and editorial campaigns that demand high-impact visuals without studio rental or post-production specialists. ● Medium-shot composition with face in sharp focus and environment motion blur for editorial drama. ● Hyper-realistic skin rendering and depth-of-field effects that mimic professional DSLR photography. ● Customizable chaos scenarios (windstorm, confetti, crowd) and outfit variables for brand alignment. ● Negative prompt and face-restoration settings included to prevent distortion and preserve identity. ## Prompt
