# Ad Copy Variation Generator for Brand Campaigns

## 簡介

The Ad Copy Variation Generator for Brand Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates multiple persuasive ad variations tailored to specific audiences and brand narratives. This ad copy prompt for ChatGPT works by turning your product details, target audience, brand story, and unique value proposition into 3–5 distinct advertising variations. Each version explores a different storytelling angle while maintaining your chosen tone - whether professional, conversational, or urgent. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to structure copy around a strong main claim, layering benefits and emotional hooks that lead naturally to a call-to-action. It runs seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for marketers testing multiple campaign directions, copywriters working under tight deadlines, or founders who need conversion-focused ads without hiring an agency. ● Produces 3–5 labeled ad variations, each with a hook, body copy, and call-to-action ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure clarity and logical flow from benefit to action ● Explores different storytelling angles within a single campaign brief ● Accepts custom inputs for product, audience, tone, brand story, and value proposition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive advertising that converts brand narratives into compelling calls-to-action.

## Task
Create 3–5 ad copy variations for {{product-service}}, each targeting {{target-audience}} in a {{tone}} voice. Every variation should communicate the brand story and value proposition through clear, benefit-driven language.

## Context
Brand story: {{brand-story}}

Unique value proposition: {{unique-value-proposition}}

Structure each variation using dependency grammar principles: anchor your copy to a strong main clause (the core benefit or claim), then layer supporting details that build toward a clear call-to-action. Prioritize clarity and emotional resonance over jargon.

## Output
Deliver 3–5 variations, each as a labeled, standalone paragraph. Every variation should include:
- An attention-grabbing headline or opening hook  
- Compelling body copy that highlights key benefits and connects to audience needs  
- A strong call-to-action

Number each variation (Variation 1, Variation 2, etc.) and ensure they explore different angles or storytelling approaches within the same strategic brief.
```
