# Job Application Follow-Up Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Job Application Follow-Up Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates field-specific follow-up guides for job seekers navigating application and interview processes. This job application follow-up prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing common scenarios in your industry, then generating customizable email templates with precise timing recommendations for each situation. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it produces a structured guide that includes follow-up templates for post-application check-ins, post-interview thank-yous, additional interest signals, and no-response scenarios, complete with field-appropriate timing norms and credible career development sources. Whether you are following up after a first-round interview in tech, checking on an application in healthcare, or expressing continued interest in a consulting role, this prompt adapts its advice to match industry expectations. This prompt is for job seekers who need professional, timely follow-up communications that respect industry norms without sounding desperate or pushy. ● Identifies the four most common follow-up scenarios specific to your industry or field ● Provides customizable email templates for each situation that balance professionalism with genuine interest ● Establishes clear timing guidelines that reflect recruiting norms in your target industry ● Cites credible career development and recruiting sources to ground advice in research ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career coach with deep knowledge of job search best practices across industries.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide on effective follow-up strategies after submitting job applications and completing interviews for professionals in {{field-or-industry}}.

## Process
1. Identify the most common application and interview scenarios that require follow-up in this field
2. Determine best practices for crafting effective follow-up messages in each scenario
3. Establish optimal timing guidelines for sending follow-up communications
4. Create customizable templates for each follow-up situation
5. Cite credible career development and recruiting sources

## Output
Format your response as:

- Provide specific, actionable advice for each follow-up situation
- Include templates that are easily adaptable

- Avoid generic advice or platitudes
```
