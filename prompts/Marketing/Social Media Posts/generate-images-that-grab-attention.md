# Social Media Image Concept Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Image Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that produces detailed visual concepts for social media posts tailored to your platform, brand, and audience. This social media image concept prompt for ChatGPT generates 5–7 distinct image ideas, each with a title, visual composition breakdown, color and imagery guidance, and suggested text overlays. It adapts to platform-specific requirements like aspect ratios and safe zones, incorporates your brand colors and key message, and considers current design trends. Creators use it to plan Instagram carousels, Facebook ads, LinkedIn graphics, TikTok thumbnails, and more without starting from a blank canvas. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh visual directions quickly, want to align image strategy with brand identity, or need to brief a designer or generate prompts for image AI tools like Midjourney or Flux. ● Outputs 5–7 distinct concepts with composition, color, and imagery details ● Includes headline and supporting copy suggestions for each visual ● Adapts to platform requirements and audience preferences ● Aligns every concept with your brand colors, offering, and key message ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert visual content creator specializing in high-engagement social media imagery.

## Task
Generate a series of attention-grabbing image concepts designed to stop scrolling, boost engagement, and drive traffic. Each concept should feature bold visual elements and compelling text overlays tailored to the platform and audience.

## Context
Platform: {{platform}}
Brand & offering: {{brand-offering}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Brand colors: {{brand-colors}}
Key message: {{key-message}}

Consider platform-specific requirements (aspect ratios, safe zones), audience preferences, and current design trends. All concepts must align with the brand identity while standing out in a crowded feed.

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 5–7 image concepts. For each concept, include:
- A brief title
- Description of visual elements (composition, color usage, imagery)
- Suggested text overlay (headline and supporting copy if applicable)
```
