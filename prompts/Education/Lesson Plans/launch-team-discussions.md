# Collaborative Learning Discussion Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Collaborative Learning Discussion Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured plans for launching team discussions on collaborative learning for educators, trainers, and organizational development professionals. This collaborative learning prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete implementation plan that includes discussion topics, session structures, participation guidelines, and facilitation strategies tailored to team members with diverse educational backgrounds, learning styles, and expertise levels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking three inputs: your team composition and backgrounds, specific learning objectives, and available timeframe and resources. The output is a bullet-point plan organized under clear headings, ready to implement in training sessions, professional development workshops, academic team projects, or corporate learning initiatives. Reach for this prompt when you need to design inclusive discussion frameworks that balance structure with flexibility, ensure equitable participation across different expertise levels, or translate collaborative learning theory into actionable session plans. ● Produces session structures with clear participation guidelines that accommodate different learning styles and cultural perspectives ● Includes concrete facilitation strategies for encouraging critical thinking and meaningful peer-to-peer knowledge exchange ● Provides methods for applying shared knowledge to real-world contexts and team performance goals ● Designs inclusive environments that value diverse expertise levels and create psychological safety for contribution ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in collaborative learning design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan for launching team discussions on collaborative learning. Develop a structured approach that fosters effective participation and knowledge sharing among team members from diverse educational backgrounds.

## Context
Team composition and context: {{team-and-backgrounds}}

Learning objectives: {{learning-objectives}}

Timeframe and resources: {{timeframe-and-resources}}

## Requirements
- Outline discussion topics, session structure, and participation guidelines
- Account for diverse learning styles, cultural perspectives, and expertise levels
- Include strategies for facilitating meaningful interactions and encouraging critical thinking
- Provide methods for promoting the application of shared knowledge
- Ensure the approach creates an inclusive and engaging collaborative environment

## Output
Deliver the plan as a bullet point list organized under clear headings for easy implementation.
```
