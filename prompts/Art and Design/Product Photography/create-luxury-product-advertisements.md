# Luxury Product Advertisement Image Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Product Advertisement Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates single-frame hero product ads with cinematic lighting, editorial composition, and custom brand typography for e-commerce and social campaigns. This luxury product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana renders your product centered on a dark marble slab with soft gradient backdrop, dramatic spotlight and rim lighting, shallow depth of field, and a 45-degree angle that highlights material texture and craftsmanship. It layers brand name and tagline overlays in elegant serif and minimalist sans-serif fonts, delivering a polished, high-fashion aesthetic ready for launch. Use it when you need premium ad creative for fashion, accessories, or lifestyle goods without a full studio shoot. ● Portrait 3:4 composition with centered hero product on matte marble and gradient backdrop ● Cinematic soft spotlight and rim light that accentuates material texture and embossed details ● Branded typography overlays with elegant serif brand name and minimalist tagline in gold or ivory ● Premium editorial style with shallow depth of field, 85mm lens look, and balanced exposure ## Prompt

```
Premium product advertisement photograph for {{brand-name}} {{product-type}}, single hero layout, portrait 3:4 aspect ratio.

Product: {{product-description}} — full-grain premium material, rich saturated color, hand-finished edges, slim modern profile, subtle embossed logo detail. Centered composition on matte dark marble slab against soft gradient charcoal-to-warm brown backdrop.

Cinematic soft spotlight from upper left with subtle rim light accentuating material texture. Shallow depth of field isolating product. Slight 45-degree camera angle showing depth, 85mm product photography lens look, sharp focus on logo and stitching details. Soft grounding shadow beneath.

Typography overlays: Brand name "{{brand-name}}" in elegant high-fashion serif with wide letter spacing, soft gold or warm ivory color, positioned top center. Tagline "Crafted for the Modern Gentleman" in thin minimalist sans-serif, muted beige, placed between brand and product.

Style: Luxury product photography, minimal editorial aesthetic, high realism, premium material focus, timeless and sophisticated mood. Balanced exposure with rich shadows.

Avoid: cheap synthetic material look, overexposed highlights, flat lighting, cluttered background, low-resolution texture, cartoon style, watermarks, text artifacts.
```
