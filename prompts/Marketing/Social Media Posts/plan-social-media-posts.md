# Social Media Content Calendar Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a structured week-long posting schedule tailored to your brand's voice and audience. This social media prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok generates seven days of platform-specific content ideas, each with format recommendations (images, videos, text, polls, stories), thematic angles, and engagement tactics. The output balances educational content, behind-the-scenes glimpses, and product mentions while maintaining an approachable tone. Use it to plan Instagram carousels, LinkedIn thought leadership, TikTok trends, Twitter threads, or multi-platform campaigns that drive interaction without aggressive selling. Ideal for social media managers, content strategists, small business owners, and marketing teams who need consistent, varied posting plans that align with brand guidelines and platform best practices. ● Produces a Monday-through-Sunday calendar with distinct platforms, formats, and themes for each day ● Tailors every post idea to platform strengths like Instagram Reels, LinkedIn articles, or Twitter polls ● Includes hashtag suggestions, calls-to-action, and interactive elements (questions, swipe-ups) to boost engagement ● Balances educational tips, industry insights, and brand storytelling with minimal hard-sell language ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media content strategist planning a week of platform-specific posts.

## Task
Create a seven-day content calendar (Monday through Sunday) with varied platforms, content types, and themes. Each day's post should drive engagement and align with the brand.

## Context
**Brand & Audience:**
{{brand-context}}
(Include: brand name, industry, target audience demographics/interests, company tone of voice)

**Content Principles:**
- Vary formats (images, videos, text, polls, stories) throughout the week
- Tailor each post to platform strengths and audience behavior
- Balance value-driven content (tips, industry news, behind-the-scenes) with product showcases
- Use concise, conversational language at an 8th-grade reading level
- Avoid hard-sell; focus on relationships and utility

## Output
For each day, provide:

**[Day]:**
- **Platform:** [specify which social channel]
- **Content Type:** [format/post style]
- **Theme:** [topic or angle]
- **Additional Elements:** [hashtags, engagement hooks, CTAs, platform-specific features like polls or swipe-ups]

Ensure variety across the week in platforms, formats, and themes. Include specific engagement strategies (questions, polls, interactive elements) and clear calls-to-action where appropriate.
```
