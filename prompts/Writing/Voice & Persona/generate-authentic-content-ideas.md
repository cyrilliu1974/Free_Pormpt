# Content Vulnerability Coach Prompt for Creators

## 簡介

The Content Vulnerability Coach Prompt for Creators is a free AI prompt that helps content creators uncover authentic, relatable story angles by exploring personal challenges, fears, and growth moments. This vulnerability coach prompt for ChatGPT guides you through brainstorming 10 unique content angles rooted in genuine experience - embarrassing moments, impostor syndrome, unconventional beliefs, and personal impacts of your topic. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering each angle as a titled breakdown with a one-sentence summary and a sample opening hook line. Ideal for writers, coaches, educators, and entrepreneurs who want to move beyond surface-level content and build deeper connections with their audience through storytelling that feels human and earned. ● Surfaces personal challenges, fears, and growth moments tied directly to your content topic and audience ● Explores impostor syndrome, humbling lessons, and unconventional beliefs that humanize your perspective ● Provides 10 titled angles, each with a summary and a ready-to-use opening hook line ● Ensures every angle is specific, genuine, and avoids generic or superficial storytelling ## Prompt

```
## Role

Vulnerability and authenticity expert helping creators connect deeply with their audience through personal narrative.

## Context

You will brainstorm angles and personal stories that add authenticity and vulnerability to content. These angles should humanize the creator and let the audience see themselves in the experiences shared.

**Content topic:** {{content-topic}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Creator's background and experience:** {{background-and-experience}}

## Task

Generate 10 unique angles or personal stories, drawing from:

- Challenges or fears faced and how they were overcome
- Embarrassing or humbling moments that taught key lessons
- Impostor syndrome experiences and working through self-doubt
- Unconventional beliefs or quirks shaping perspective
- Personal impacts of the topic on life or work

**Criteria:**

1. Humanize the creator; ensure relatability to the audience
2. Avoid generic or superficial ideas—prioritize genuine vulnerability
3. Showcase challenges overcome, lessons learned, and growth
4. Tie each angle clearly back to the content topic

## Output

For each of the 10 angles:

### Angle [NUMBER]: [TITLE]

[One-sentence summary]

**Sample opening line:** [Engaging first line to hook the reader]
```
