# Peer-to-Peer Learning Plan for Collaborative Classrooms

## 簡介

The Peer-to-Peer Learning Plan for Collaborative Classrooms is a free AI prompt that creates structured collaborative learning plans for educators who want to build student-centered classrooms through peer teaching and group engagement. This peer-to-peer learning prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered, actionable plan with clear section headings that includes group activities for diverse learning styles, structured discussion formats that develop critical thinking, and peer feedback sessions that deepen subject understanding. You specify your course subject, class size, learning format (in-person, online, or hybrid), student age group, and available technology, and the prompt designs collaboration strategies that fit your real classroom context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Educators use it when planning units that require active student interaction, flipping classroom dynamics so learners teach each other, or integrating collaborative methods into traditional lecture courses. ● Designs group activities that accommodate visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and reading/writing learners ● Structures opportunities for students to explain concepts to peers, reinforcing their own mastery ● Adapts collaboration strategies to class size and whether students meet face-to-face, online, or in hybrid settings ● Integrates available technology meaningfully to enhance peer interaction without adding unnecessary complexity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert education facilitator specializing in collaborative learning design.

## Task
Create a structured peer-to-peer learning plan that fosters collaboration, critical thinking, and student engagement through group activities, discussions, and peer feedback sessions.

## Context
Course and learners: {{course-and-learners}}
(Include subject/course, class size, learning format—in-person/online/hybrid—and students' age group)

Available resources: {{available-technology}}

## Requirements
- Design activities that accommodate diverse learning styles
- Include opportunities for students to teach and learn from each other
- Ensure the plan adapts to the specified class size and format
- Integrate technology where it enhances collaboration
- Build a supportive atmosphere that deepens subject understanding

## Output
Deliver the collaborative learning plan as a numbered list with clear headings for each main section. Each section should be actionable and directly applicable to the course context provided.
```
