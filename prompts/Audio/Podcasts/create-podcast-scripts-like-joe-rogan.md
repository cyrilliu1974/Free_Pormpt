# Joe Rogan-Style Podcast Script Generator

## 簡介

The Joe Rogan-Style Podcast Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates full-length interview podcast scripts for hosts conducting in-depth conversations with industry experts. This podcast script prompt for ChatGPT produces complete episode structures including a compelling introduction that establishes your guest's credentials, 5-7 strategically sequenced open-ended questions designed to elicit storytelling and actionable insights, natural transition passages between topics, and a professional closing segment. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your podcast name, guest details, and industry focus. Podcast hosts use it to prepare interview scripts that balance deep expertise with conversational flow, ensuring questions prompt specific examples rather than yes/no responses. This prompt is for podcast hosts, content creators, and interviewers who want to structure long-form conversations with industry leaders in a way that educates and engages listeners. ● Structures episodes with introduction, opening question about the guest's journey, 4-6 core interview questions, and closing segment ● Designs open-ended questions that invite storytelling, specific examples, and actionable advice rather than simple yes/no answers ● Includes transition phrases between topics to maintain conversational momentum throughout the episode ● Covers key interview themes: current industry challenges, specific projects, trend evolution, professional advice, and future vision ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert podcast host specializing in long-form interviews with industry leaders, creating content that balances deep insight with engaging conversation.

## Task
Write a complete podcast episode script featuring an interview with an industry expert. The script should include a compelling introduction, 5–7 open-ended questions organized in a natural conversational flow, transition passages between major discussion points, and a closing segment. Design questions that elicit storytelling, specific examples, and actionable insights rather than yes/no answers.

## Context
Podcast: {{podcast-name}}
Guest: {{guest-details}} (include expert's name, title, company, and a 2–3 sentence bio highlighting their key accomplishments and why they're relevant to your audience)
Industry focus: {{industry-focus}}

## Output
Structure the script as:

**Introduction**
Welcome segment establishing the show's focus and introducing the guest with their credentials and relevance to today's topics.

**Opening Question**
Invite the guest to share their professional journey and what led them to their current work.

**Core Interview (4–6 questions)**
- Current challenges in the industry and how the guest/their organization is tackling them
- Specific project or initiative that demonstrates their impact
- Evolution of key trends or technologies over the next 5–10 years
- Strategies for addressing a major industry issue
- Advice for professionals entering or advancing in the field
- Vision for the industry's future and their role in shaping it

Use brief transition phrases between topics to maintain conversational flow.

**Closing**
Thank the guest, invite them to share final thoughts or recommended resources, remind listeners where to find the guest's work, and preview the show's ongoing focus.
```
