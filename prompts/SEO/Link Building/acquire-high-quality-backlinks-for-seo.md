# Backlink Acquisition Strategy for SEO Growth

## 簡介

The Backlink Acquisition Strategy for SEO Growth is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, actionable plan to secure high-quality backlinks and improve search engine rankings for any industry. This backlink strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes your competitive landscape, current domain authority, and top competitors to build a five-part acquisition roadmap. It identifies authoritative guest posting targets, maps content types to placement opportunities, designs personalized outreach sequences, defines anchor text best practices, and establishes measurement frameworks with realistic timelines. Real use cases include SaaS companies building domain authority from scratch, e-commerce sites competing in saturated niches, and agencies managing multi-client link building campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, competitive-intelligence-driven approach to off-page SEO rather than generic link building advice. ● Identifies high-authority guest posting targets, resource page opportunities, and unlinked mentions using specific vetting criteria and domain metrics ● Maps content formats (data studies, expert guides, case studies, tools) to placement opportunities based on what each target site values ● Provides personalized outreach email templates and follow-up sequences designed to build genuine relationships, not spam inboxes ● Defines anchor text strategy and contextual link placement tactics that pass editorial review and avoid over-optimization penalties ● Establishes KPIs, timeline expectations, and iterative optimization steps so you can measure ROI and refine your approach ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in backlink acquisition and off-page optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive backlink acquisition strategy to improve search engine rankings and drive organic traffic for {{industry}}. Create a detailed, actionable plan covering target website identification, content creation for guest posting, and outreach execution.

## Context
- Website: {{website-url}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Current domain authority: {{current-domain-authority}}
- Top competitors: {{top-competitors}}

Analyze the competitive landscape and authority gap. Identify authoritative websites in {{industry}} that accept guest contributions, resource page opportunities, and unlinked brand mentions. Prioritize targets by domain authority, relevance, and traffic potential.

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings and numbered steps covering:

1. **Target Website Identification** – methodology for finding and vetting authoritative sites, including specific tools and criteria
2. **Content Strategy** – types of valuable content that will attract placements (data studies, expert guides, case studies, tools), mapped to target sites
3. **Outreach Execution** – personalized email templates, follow-up sequences, and relationship-building tactics
4. **Link Placement Best Practices** – anchor text strategy, contextual relevance, and natural integration
5. **Measurement & Optimization** – KPIs to track, timeline expectations, and iterative improvements

For each component, provide specific tactics, examples where helpful, and prioritization guidance based on the domain authority and competitive context provided.
```
