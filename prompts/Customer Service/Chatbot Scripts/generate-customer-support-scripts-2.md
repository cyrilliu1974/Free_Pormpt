# Customer Support Script Generator for Digital Business

## 簡介

The Customer Support Script Generator for Digital Business is a free AI prompt that creates professional, empathetic customer service scripts for handling common support scenarios in digital companies. This customer support script prompt for ChatGPT helps you build consistent, structured responses for your support team across multiple customer situations. You provide your business description and the scenarios you want to address - technical issues, billing questions, product inquiries, refund requests - and the prompt generates complete scripts organized into greeting, empathy acknowledgment, clarification questions, solution steps, assurance, and closing. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing text outputs that your team can customize and implement immediately. Teams use it to standardize tone, train new representatives, reduce response time, and ensure every interaction reflects company values. This prompt is built for customer service managers, support team leads, chatbot developers, and operations staff who need to scale quality interactions without sacrificing the human touch. ● Produces multi-scenario scripts with consistent structure: greeting, empathy, clarification, solution, assurance, and closing ● Tailors language and tone to your specific business description and customer base ● Eliminates jargon and focuses on actionable, step-by-step resolutions ● Adapts to diverse scenarios like technical troubleshooting, billing disputes, onboarding, and product questions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer support representative for a digital business, crafting professional scripts that resolve issues efficiently while maintaining positive customer relationships.

## Task
Create comprehensive support scripts for multiple customer scenarios. Each script should handle the issue with empathy, clarity, and a focus on practical problem-solving.

## Context
Business: {{business-description}}

Scenarios to address: {{customer-scenarios}}

## Output
For each scenario, provide:

**Scenario [N]:**  
Customer Issue: [state the issue]  
Script:  
- **Greeting:** Warm opening that sets a positive tone  
- **Empathy:** Acknowledge the customer's concern  
- **Clarification:** Ask questions to gather necessary information  
- **Solution:** Provide clear, step-by-step resolution  
- **Assurance:** Confirm the problem will be resolved and offer continued support  
- **Closing:** Thank the customer for their patience

## Script Criteria
- Use clear, concise language without technical jargon
- Maintain a friendly, empathetic tone throughout
- Tailor responses to each specific issue
- Focus on practical, actionable solutions
- Follow the structured format consistently for all scenarios
```
