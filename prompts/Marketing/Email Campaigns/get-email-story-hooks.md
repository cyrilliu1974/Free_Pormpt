# Email Story Hooks Generator for High Open Rates

## 簡介

The Email Story Hooks Generator for High Open Rates is a free AI prompt that creates compelling subject lines paired with strategic opening sentences for email marketers and copywriters. This email story hooks prompt for ChatGPT applies proven email marketing principles - curiosity gaps, immediate value propositions, and audience-specific relevance - to produce five ready-to-test hook pairs. Each subject line stays under 50 characters for mobile optimization, while every opening sentence seamlessly continues the promise to pull readers into the message body. You provide your campaign brief (audience, goals, pain points), and the prompt delivers hooks grounded in conversational, benefit-driven language. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh angles for product launches, newsletter campaigns, sales sequences, or re-engagement emails. ● Produces five subject line and opening sentence pairs per run, formatted for immediate A/B testing. ● Optimizes subject lines for mobile (under 50 characters) while maintaining curiosity and clarity. ● Anchors every hook in audience pain points, aspirations, and proven engagement tactics. ● Works across product launches, newsletters, sales funnels, and re-engagement campaigns. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in high-performing subject lines and opening hooks.

## Task
Create 5 attention-grabbing email hooks that increase open rates and drive engagement. Each hook consists of a subject line paired with its opening sentence.

## Context
Target audience and campaign details:
{{campaign-brief}}

Apply proven email marketing principles:
- Curiosity gaps that compel the open
- Immediate value proposition
- Relevance to audience pain points or aspirations
- Conversational, benefit-driven language
- Specificity over vague promises

## Output
Deliver exactly 5 hooks in this format:

**Subject line:** [your subject line]
**Opening:** [your first sentence]

**Subject line:** [your subject line]
**Opening:** [your first sentence]

[Continue for all 5 hooks]

Each subject line should be under 50 characters for mobile optimization. Each opening sentence must seamlessly continue the subject line's promise and pull the reader into the body.
```
