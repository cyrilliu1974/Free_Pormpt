# Research Topic Ideas Generator for Academic Fields

## 簡介

The Research Topic Ideas Generator for Academic Fields is a free AI prompt that produces discipline-specific, feasible research topics for scholars, graduate students, and academic researchers across all fields. This research topic generator prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the current state of scholarship in your specified field, identifies underexplored areas and emerging trends, considers interdisciplinary connections, and evaluates each topic for both feasibility and scholarly impact. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking three variables - field of study, academic level, and research context - and returning a curated bullet list of topics that are specific enough to guide inquiry yet flexible enough to accommodate different methodologies. Whether you're a doctoral candidate searching for dissertation directions, a professor advising students, or a researcher planning a grant proposal, this prompt surfaces opportunities that align with your institutional focus and available resources. ● Identifies gaps in existing literature to help you contribute original insights and advance knowledge in your discipline. ● Analyzes current scholarly trends and emerging areas to ensure topics remain timely and relevant to funding priorities. ● Suggests interdisciplinary angles that broaden impact and connect your work to adjacent fields and methodologies. ● Evaluates feasibility so each topic is practical within typical academic time, budget, and resource constraints. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher specializing in identifying emerging research opportunities and gaps in scholarly literature.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of innovative, academically rigorous research topic ideas tailored to the specified field and academic level. Each topic should address current trends, fill identifiable gaps in existing research, incorporate interdisciplinary potential where relevant, and be feasible given typical academic constraints.

## Context
Field of study: {{field-of-study}}
Academic level: {{academic-level}}
Specific research interests and institutional focus: {{research-context}}

Analyze the current state of scholarship in this field, identify underexplored areas, consider cross-disciplinary connections, and evaluate each topic for feasibility and potential scholarly impact.

## Output
Provide your research topic ideas as a bullet point list. Each topic should be specific enough to guide inquiry yet broad enough to allow methodological flexibility.
```
