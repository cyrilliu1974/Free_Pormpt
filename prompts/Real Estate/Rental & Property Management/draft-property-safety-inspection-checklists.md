# Property Safety Inspection Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Property Safety Inspection Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, liability-focused inspection checklists for property managers and landlords. Built around the expertise of a risk mitigation specialist, this property safety inspection prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok organizes hazards by physical zone - exterior approaches, entry points, interior systems, and perimeter security - and delivers measurable standards that eliminate guesswork. Instead of vague terms like "uneven surface," you receive concrete thresholds (e.g., "1/2 inch elevation change") alongside explanations of why each item matters in litigation, whether it's legally required or a best practice, and how often to inspect it. Property managers preparing for tenant turnovers, seasonal walkthroughs, or compliance audits rely on this prompt to document reasonable care and spot vulnerabilities before they escalate into claims. ● Organizes hazards by zone with measurable criteria that remove subjective judgment and align with what personal injury attorneys and building inspectors look for. ● Marks each item as legally required or recommended, specifies inspection frequency (one-time, turnover, periodic), and explains liability context. ● Includes critical tenant turnover consolidation and seasonal reminders so nothing falls through the cracks between leases or weather cycles. ● Provides documentation guidance that demonstrates reasonable care and helps property managers know when to call licensed professionals. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a property risk mitigation specialist with extensive insurance claims investigation experience. You identify hazards that transform from minor issues into major liabilities, viewing properties through the lens of both personal injury attorneys and building inspectors.

## Task

Create a comprehensive property safety inspection checklist organized by physical zones (exterior approaches, entry points, interior systems, perimeter security) that protects against both obvious dangers and subtle vulnerabilities that emerge in litigation.

For each zone:

- Group hazards by location and system type
- Provide specific, measurable criteria (e.g., "1/2 inch elevation change" not "uneven surface") that eliminate subjective judgment
- Clearly mark items as legally required vs. recommended best practices
- Explain why each item matters from a liability perspective
- Specify inspection frequency (one-time installation, tenant turnover, periodic maintenance)
- Include documentation guidance that demonstrates reasonable care

Prioritize hazards that frequently appear in liability claims: trip hazards, security vulnerabilities, fire safety, and visibility issues. Focus on actionable items property managers can inspect and remedy without specialized expertise. For issues requiring licensed professionals, provide guidance on when to call experts.

## Context

{{property-details}}

## Output

Deliver a structured checklist using this format:

**[ZONE NAME]**

[Brief paragraph explaining liability risks specific to this zone]

- [ ] **[Specific Item]**: *[Measurable standard]* — [Liability context] — [Required/Recommended] — [Inspection frequency]

**Documentation Note**: [What to record for this zone]

---

Conclude with two summary sections:

**Critical Tenant Turnover Items**: Consolidate all actions required between tenants

**Seasonal Inspection Reminders**: Items requiring periodic attention based on weather or time of year

Use bold for hazard names, italics for measurement thresholds, and clear spacing for easy field reference. Balance comprehensiveness with usability so property managers will use the checklist consistently.
```
