# Social Media Audit Report Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Audit Report Generator is a free AI prompt that evaluates platform performance and delivers actionable recommendations for businesses and marketing teams. This social media audit prompt for ChatGPT examines each platform individually - tracking engagement rates (likes, comments, shares, saves), follower growth trends, content performance by format and topic, and posting frequency effectiveness. It compares your results against industry benchmarks and competitor strategies, then outputs a structured markdown table with four columns: Platform, Metrics, Areas for Improvement, and Recommendations. Use it when planning quarterly strategy reviews, onboarding new clients, diagnosing engagement drops, or preparing executive reports. Works seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Analyzes engagement, follower growth, and content performance for multiple platforms in one pass ● Compares your metrics against industry benchmarks and named competitor accounts ● Delivers prioritized, actionable recommendations ranked by potential impact ● Outputs findings in a markdown table ready for reports, presentations, or stakeholder reviews ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media analyst conducting a comprehensive audit to evaluate platform performance and deliver actionable insights.

## Task
Analyze each social media platform individually, focusing on:
- Engagement rates (likes, comments, shares, saves)
- Follower growth trends and patterns
- Content performance (top and bottom performers by format and topic)
- Posting frequency and timing effectiveness

Identify areas for improvement by comparing performance against industry benchmarks and competitor strategies. Provide specific, prioritized recommendations to enhance social media presence and effectiveness.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Platforms to audit: {{platforms}}
Industry and target audience: {{industry-context}}
Main competitors: {{competitors}}

## Output
Present your audit in a markdown table with four columns:

| Platform | Metrics | Areas for Improvement | Recommendations |

Provide a comprehensive row for each platform listed, with specific data points, clear improvement opportunities, and actionable next steps ranked by potential impact.
```
