# Tropical Environment Close-Up Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Tropical Environment Close-Up Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic close-up photographs of tropical environments viewed through wet glass surfaces for digital artists, photographers, and content creators. It produces high-detail images with sharp raindrops in the foreground and lush palm leaves or dense foliage rendered in soft bokeh behind them, combining precise depth-of-field control with rich saturated greens and warm ambient lighting. This tropical close-up prompt for Midjourney runs on image generation models including Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you specify both the tropical scene subject and weather lighting conditions to create atmospheric environmental photography. Reach for this prompt when you need evocative tropical imagery with a cinematic through-the-glass perspective that emphasizes weather detail and texture contrast. ● Sharp focus on wet glass raindrops in the foreground with tropical foliage rendered in cinematic soft bokeh behind ● Customizable tropical scene subject and weather lighting variables to match your creative direction ● High-detail texture rendering with rich saturated greens, warm ambient tones, and cinematic lighting ● 16:9 aspect ratio output ideal for video thumbnails, social media headers, and digital art portfolios ## Prompt

```
Close-up shot of {{tropical-scene}} during {{weather-lighting}}, viewed through wet glass with sharp raindrops in sharp focus; lush palm leaves and dense foliage visible beyond in soft bokeh, cinematic lighting, high detail textures, rich saturated greens and warm ambient tones --ar 16:9
```
