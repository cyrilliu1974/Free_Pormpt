# Problem-Solving Case Study Builder for Course Creation

## 簡介

The Problem-Solving Case Study Builder for Course Creation is a free AI prompt that generates detailed educational case studies designed to develop learners' practical problem-solving abilities in any subject or field. This problem-solving case study prompt for ChatGPT produces complete teaching scenarios that include contextual background narratives, guided questions to stimulate critical thinking and discussion, structured sample solutions demonstrating best practices, and synthesized key takeaways. Each case study follows a consistent pedagogical framework: a 3-5 paragraph background section establishes realistic context, three or more key questions prompt analysis from multiple perspectives, a step-by-step problem-solving process models expert thinking, and distilled takeaways reinforce learning objectives. Educators use it to create scenario-based learning materials for courses, workshops, and training programs across disciplines from business and healthcare to engineering and social sciences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is for instructional designers, educators, trainers, and course creators who need to quickly develop authentic problem-solving exercises that mirror real-world complexity. ● Generates complete case studies with realistic background context, not generic or overly simplified scenarios ● Includes three or more guided questions designed to prompt analysis, multiple perspectives, and creative application of knowledge ● Provides step-by-step sample solutions that model expert problem-solving processes and reflect current best practices ● Delivers synthesized key takeaways that reinforce learning objectives and support knowledge transfer ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator creating case studies that develop practical problem-solving skills in {{subject-or-field}}.

## Task
Create realistic, engaging case studies that challenge learners to apply knowledge to complex, real-world problems. Include sufficient context, guiding questions to stimulate critical thinking, and well-structured sample solutions demonstrating best practices and innovative approaches.

## Output
For each case study, use this structure:

**Case Study [Number]: [Title]**

**Background:**
Provide relevant context and key details about the scenario in 3-5 concise paragraphs.

**Key Questions:**
1. Question to prompt analysis and problem-solving
2. Question to encourage consideration of multiple perspectives
3. Question to stimulate creative solutions and applications of knowledge

**Sample Solution:**

*Overview:*
Brief summary of the recommended approach in 2-3 sentences.

*Step-by-Step Problem-Solving Process:*
1. First key step with brief explanation
2. Second key step with brief explanation
3. Third key step with brief explanation
4. Additional steps as needed

**Key Takeaways:**
- Main lesson or principle reinforced by the case study
- Secondary lesson or principle demonstrated in the solution
- Final thought or consideration for future application

## Criteria
- Case studies must be realistic, engaging, and challenging—avoid overly simplistic or unrealistic scenarios
- Questions should drive critical thinking and discussion
- Solutions should reflect current best practices in {{subject-or-field}}
```
