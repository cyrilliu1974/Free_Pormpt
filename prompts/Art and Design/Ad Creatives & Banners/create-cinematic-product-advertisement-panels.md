# Cinematic Three-Panel Product Ad Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Three-Panel Product Ad Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 21:9 ultra-wide product advertisements structured as three horizontal panels for luxury tech brands. It produces a hero product shot in dramatic nighttime lighting, a lifestyle scene showing the product in use, and a macro close-up highlighting engineering detail - all unified by moonlit cinematography and customizable color grading. This three-panel product ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers high-end advertising visuals with controlled low-key lighting, sharp star fields, and premium material rendering that conveys innovation and refinement. Ideal for designers, marketers, and brand teams launching high-end tech products who need advertising creative without a photo shoot. ● Structured three-panel layout in 21:9 aspect ratio: hero placement, user experience moment, and precision engineering detail ● Nighttime astrophotography aesthetic with moonlit rim lighting, visible Milky Way, and thousands of sharp stars ● Customizable by product name and color palette while maintaining luxury tech visual language ● Professional low-key exposure balancing star visibility, product clarity, and deep detailed shadows without light pollution or overexposure ## Prompt

```
Premium 3×1 ultra-wide cinematic advertisement in 21:9 aspect ratio, three horizontal panels showcasing a luxury telescope in nighttime astrophotography style.

Deep but detailed shadows. {{color-palette}} color grading. Futuristic minimalist aesthetic with high-detail product focus and epic wide composition.

Visionary, refined, innovative brand personality. Luxury tech advertising meets astrophotography realism.
```
