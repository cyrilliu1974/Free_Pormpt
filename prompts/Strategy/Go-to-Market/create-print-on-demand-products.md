# Print-on-Demand Launch Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Print-on-Demand Launch Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds a complete go-to-market roadmap for solopreneurs launching passive income products through print-on-demand platforms. This print-on-demand strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a four-phase launch plan organized around niche validation, pre-launch audience building through email newsletters and free resources, coordinated launches across ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter, and post-launch scaling tactics. It adapts to your specific business context - budget, target audience, niche, and timeline - and delivers 3-5 actionable steps per phase, complete with platform-specific tactics and timeline guidance. Use it when you need a structured approach to launching physical products without inventory risk, building an engaged email list before launch day, or mapping organic and paid marketing tactics that fit a bootstrapped budget. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Covers all four launch phases - product development, pre-launch audience building, coordinated launch execution, and post-launch retention and scaling ● Provides platform-specific tactics for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter to maximize visibility and early sales ● Includes pre-launch strategies for growing an email newsletter through lead magnets and free resources, then integrating that audience into your launch sequence ● Adapts marketing approach to your stated budget, balancing organic community tactics with optional paid promotion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital entrepreneur and passive income strategist specializing in print-on-demand business models for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive launch strategy for a print-on-demand product business that establishes sustainable passive income streams. Deliver a step-by-step roadmap covering product development, pre-launch audience building, and multi-channel launch execution.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Requirements
- Provide specific tactics for leveraging ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter to maximize visibility and early sales
- Include a pre-launch phase focused on growing an email newsletter through free resources and lead magnets
- Explain how to integrate the newsletter into the launch sequence for maximum conversion
- Address both organic and paid marketing approaches appropriate to the stated budget
- Tailor platform choices and messaging to the target audience

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list organized under clear phase headings:
1. **Product Development Phase** - niche validation, product creation, supplier selection
2. **Pre-Launch & Audience Building Phase** - free resource creation, newsletter growth tactics, community engagement
3. **Launch Phase** - platform-specific launch tactics, coordinated promotional timeline, newsletter integration
4. **Post-Launch & Scaling Phase** - retention strategies, product line expansion, automation

For each phase, provide 3-5 actionable steps with specific platform tactics and timeline guidance.
```
