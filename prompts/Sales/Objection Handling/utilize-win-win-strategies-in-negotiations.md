# Win-Win Negotiation Strategy Planner for Sales

## 簡介

The Win-Win Negotiation Strategy Planner for Sales is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive negotiation plans focused on mutual benefit and value creation for sales professionals and deal-makers. This win-win negotiation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes both parties' underlying interests, identifies creative value-exchange opportunities, and generates tactical recommendations that maximize mutual gain while addressing real-world constraints. It maps interests behind stated positions, surfaces potential obstacles like authority limits or trust gaps, and designs rapport-building techniques suited to your specific scenario. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, structuring output using dependency grammar to show how each tactic supports the collaborative objective. Sales teams use it to prepare for complex B2B negotiations, partnership discussions, and multi-stakeholder deals where preserving relationships matters as much as closing terms. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond positional bargaining and design a value-creation framework that expands options before dividing them. ● Maps underlying interests and overlapping goals to uncover hidden trade opportunities ● Identifies obstacles such as competing priorities, authority limits, and trust barriers with mitigation strategies ● Proposes context-appropriate rapport-building techniques and value-creation frameworks ● Structures strategy with dependency grammar to link each tactic directly to the win-win objective ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert negotiation strategist specializing in collaborative, win-win deal-making.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive negotiation strategy for the sales scenario described below. Analyze both parties' interests, identify creative value-exchange opportunities, and propose tactics that maximize mutual benefit while addressing constraints and obstacles.

## Context
{{negotiation-scenario}}

Your key objectives: {{your-objectives}}

Counterparty's likely interests: {{counterparty-interests}}

Constraints and resources: {{constraints-and-resources}}

## Approach
- Map the underlying interests behind each party's stated positions
- Identify where interests overlap or can be traded for mutual gain
- Surface potential obstacles (authority limits, competing priorities, trust gaps) and mitigation strategies
- Propose rapport-building techniques suited to the context
- Design a value-creation framework that expands the pie before dividing it

## Output
Structure your strategy using dependency grammar:
- **Root nodes** → core strategic elements (interest analysis, value creation, tactics, contingencies)
- **Dependent nodes** → supporting rationale, specific actions, examples, and fallback options

Ensure each recommendation directly serves the collaborative, win-win objective.
```
