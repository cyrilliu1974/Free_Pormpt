# Landing Page Copy Generator for Lead Generation

## 簡介

The Landing Page Copy Generator for Lead Generation is a free AI prompt that builds conversion-focused landing page structures for solopreneurs and digital marketers. This landing page prompt for ChatGPT generates every essential component of a high-converting landing page: persuasive headlines and subheadlines that communicate your unique selling proposition, a value proposition section that addresses customer pain points, a social proof framework with testimonial guidance, scannable benefit highlights, strategically placed primary and secondary calls-to-action with specific copy recommendations, visual hierarchy instructions for hero sections and trust signals, and mobile responsiveness considerations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your business context, target audience details, campaign information, and key benefits to produce a structured landing page blueprint ready for implementation. ● Produces headline and subheadline copy that immediately communicates unique value propositions ● Generates frameworks for testimonials, trust signals, and social proof elements with specific guidance on format and themes ● Delivers primary and secondary CTA strategies with placement, copy, and design recommendations ● Includes visual hierarchy instructions and mobile optimization considerations for maximum conversion performance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in landing page optimization for conversion and lead generation.

## Task
Design a comprehensive landing page structure optimized for conversions. Include:

- **Headline & subheadline** that immediately communicate the unique selling proposition
- **Value proposition section** explaining the core benefits and how they solve customer pain points
- **Social proof block** with placeholder guidance for customer testimonials (format, length, key themes to highlight)
- **Benefit highlights** presented in scannable format (icons/cards suggested)
- **Primary and secondary calls-to-action** with specific copy, placement, and design recommendations
- **Visual hierarchy guidance** (hero section, trust signals, urgency elements)
- **Mobile responsiveness considerations**

## Context
{{business-and-campaign-context}}

Include: your solopreneur business model and offerings, target audience demographics and psychographics, the online marketing campaign driving traffic to this page, your unique selling proposition, and the 3-5 key benefits customers gain.

## Output
Deliver the landing page design as a structured document with:

### Hero Section
- Headline
- Subheadline
- Primary CTA

### Value Proposition
- Core messaging
- Benefit statements

### Social Proof
- Testimonial framework
- Trust badges/credentials

### Call-to-Action Strategy
- Primary CTA copy and placement
- Secondary CTA options

### Design & Layout Notes
- Visual hierarchy recommendations
- Conversion optimization tips specific to this audience and offer
```
