# Competitive Product Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Competitive Product Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that evaluates competitor products, identifies market gaps, and delivers actionable recommendations for product differentiation. This competitive product analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides text models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini through a four-step research process: systematic feature and pricing comparison, market positioning assessment, white space identification, and tailored recommendation development. It outputs a markdown comparison table and a prioritized findings report, making it ideal for solopreneurs and product teams who need competitive intelligence without hiring analysts. Real use cases include launching a SaaS product in a crowded category, identifying unmet customer needs before a feature sprint, or repositioning an existing offering against new entrants. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level competitor research and want structured, decision-ready insights tied to your specific business context, budget, and timeline. ● Compares features, benefits, pricing models, and unique selling points across all competitors in one structured table ● Identifies white space opportunities and unmet customer needs specific to your product category ● Delivers prioritized recommendations that account for solopreneur constraints like budget and timeline ● Outputs markdown-formatted tables and findings that can be directly shared with stakeholders or used in planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive intelligence and product positioning.

## Task
Conduct a competitive analysis to evaluate competitor products, identify market gaps, and surface actionable opportunities for differentiation and improvement.

## Context
Product category: {{product-category}}

Competitor products to analyze: {{competitor-list}}

Business context (target market, budget constraints, timeline): {{business-context}}

## Process
1. Research each competitor product thoroughly: features, benefits, pricing models, and unique selling points
2. Assess market positioning, strengths, and weaknesses for each
3. Identify white space opportunities and areas for innovation
4. Develop recommendations tailored to solopreneur constraints and differentiation needs

## Output
Deliver your analysis as:

**Competitive Comparison Table** (markdown format with 4 columns: Features | Benefits | Pricing | Unique Selling Points)

**Key Findings & Recommendations**
- Market gaps and unmet customer needs
- Differentiation opportunities given the business context
- Prioritized next steps for product development
```
