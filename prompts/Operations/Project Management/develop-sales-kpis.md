# Sales KPI Dashboard Builder for Teams

## 簡介

The Sales KPI Dashboard Builder for Teams is a free AI prompt that generates a custom table of key performance indicators for sales organizations. This sales KPI prompt for ChatGPT creates a structured markdown dashboard with 8-12 measurable KPIs tailored to your business context, spanning revenue generation, pipeline health, sales efficiency, and customer success. Each KPI includes a clear formula or definition, measurement frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly), realistic target ranges based on industry standards, an impact rating (1-5), and a color-coded status indicator. Sales leaders use it to quickly assess organizational health, align strategies with revenue goals, and identify metrics that need attention. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is designed for sales directors, revenue operations teams, and business analysts who need an actionable, at-a-glance performance dashboard customized to their team size, industry, and growth stage. ● Produces a markdown table with KPI name, description/formula, measurement frequency, target range, impact level, category, and status indicator columns ● Covers all four critical categories: revenue generation, pipeline health, sales efficiency, and customer success ● Provides realistic target ranges adjusted for industry standards, team size, and revenue goals based on your business context ● Includes color-coded status indicators (green, yellow, red) for instant visual assessment of each metric ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales analyst specializing in key performance indicators for sales teams.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Sales KPI dashboard table that measures team performance across revenue generation, pipeline health, sales efficiency, and customer success.

## Context
{{business-context}}

The table serves as an at-a-glance dashboard for sales leaders to quickly assess organizational health. Focus on actionable KPIs tailored to the scale and industry provided.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:
- **KPI Name**
- **Description/Formula** (clear calculation or definition)
- **Measurement Frequency** (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly)
- **Target Range** (specific benchmarks for the context)
- **Impact Level** (1-5, where 5 is highest business impact)
- **Category** (Revenue Generation | Pipeline Health | Sales Efficiency | Customer Success)
- **Status Indicator** (🟢 On Track | 🟡 Needs Attention | 🔴 Critical)

Include 8-12 KPIs covering all four categories. Prioritize metrics that drive decision-making for the specific business context. Provide realistic target ranges based on industry standards, adjusting for team size and revenue goals when relevant.

Format:

| KPI Name | Description/Formula | Measurement Frequency | Target Range | Impact Level (1-5) | Category | Status Indicator |
|----------|---------------------|----------------------|--------------|-------------------|----------|------------------|
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
```
