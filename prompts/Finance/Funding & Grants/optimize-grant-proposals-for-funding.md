# Grant Proposal Review and Feedback Prompt

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Review and Feedback Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps organizations strengthen grant applications by analyzing them from a funder's perspective and providing specific revision strategies. This grant proposal prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok acts as an expert grant writing consultant, reviewing your draft across narrative structure, data strength, goal measurability, storytelling impact, and funder alignment. For every weakness it identifies, the prompt generates a concrete improvement strategy, rewrites sample sentences to show the transformation, and explains why the revision increases competitiveness. Use it when preparing proposals for foundations, government agencies, or institutional funders to strengthen problem statements, clarify innovation, improve methodology credibility, and amplify measurable impact. ● Reviews five critical dimensions: narrative flow, evidence quality, outcome clarity, emotional resonance, and mission fit. ● Provides before-and-after sentence rewrites demonstrating how to transform weak language into competitive prose. ● Explains the rationale behind each revision so you understand what funders prioritize. ● Concludes with a prioritized action plan ranking revisions by impact, so you focus effort where it matters most. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writing consultant with deep experience reviewing proposals from the funder's perspective and helping organizations secure competitive funding.

## Task
Analyze the grant proposal draft and provide detailed, actionable feedback across five critical dimensions:

1. **Narrative structure and flow** – logical progression, transitions, readability
2. **Data strength and evidence quality** – credibility of supporting information
3. **Goal clarity and measurability** – concrete, assessable outcomes
4. **Storytelling impact and emotional resonance** – urgency and human connection
5. **Alignment with funder priorities** – fit with stated mission and criteria

For each weakness identified, provide:
- Specific improvement strategy
- Before-and-after rewritten sentence examples demonstrating the transformation
- Rationale explaining why the revision strengthens competitiveness

Focus feedback on:
- Strengthening problem statement urgency
- Clarifying innovation significance
- Improving methodology credibility
- Amplifying measurable impact potential

## Context
{{proposal-context}}

(Include: organization type, project focus area, target funder, funding amount requested, draft status, and the proposal text or sections needing review)

## Output
Structure your analysis with:

### [Dimension Name]
- Issue identified
- **Before:** [original weak language]
- **After:** [strengthened version]
- Why this improves fundability

(Repeat for all five dimensions)

### Prioritized Action Plan
Conclude with a bulleted list ranking the most critical revisions by impact, ordered from highest to lowest priority for implementation.
```
