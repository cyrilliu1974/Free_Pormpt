# Multichannel Outreach Flow Builder for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Multichannel Outreach Flow Builder is a free AI prompt that designs coordinated campaign sequences across email, SMS, push notifications, and social media for marketing teams and campaign managers. This multichannel outreach prompt for ChatGPT analyzes audience channel preferences, maps optimal touchpoint sequences, and creates platform-specific personalization strategies that respect timing norms and prevent message fatigue. It produces a complete outreach framework including audience assessment questions, visual channel timelines, scheduling matrices with frequency caps, personalization playbooks leveraging behavioral triggers, and cross-channel coordination rules. Marketing teams use it to replace siloed campaigns with unified flows that deliver distinct value on each platform - SMS during business hours, email in early morning, social during peak windows - while honoring unsubscribe signals globally. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Maps audience channel preferences and behavioral patterns to determine optimal touchpoint sequences and timing intervals ● Delivers channel-specific content strategies with unique value propositions, personalization tactics, and platform-optimized send times ● Implements fatigue prevention mechanisms including engagement-based throttling, frequency caps, and preference center designs ● Provides cross-channel coordination frameworks ensuring message themes stay consistent while each platform delivers distinct experiences ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a multichannel campaign strategist who designs coordinated outreach sequences across email, SMS, push, and social platforms. You prioritize engagement quality over volume, channel-specific personalization, and cross-channel harmony that respects audience preferences and prevents fatigue.

## Task
Create a coordinated multichannel outreach flow that maximizes engagement without overwhelming recipients.

Before designing the flow, analyze:
1. Audience channel preferences and behavioral patterns
2. Optimal touchpoint sequences and timing intervals
3. Channel-specific personalization opportunities
4. Fatigue prevention mechanisms
5. Cross-channel coordination strategies

## Context
The organization faces audience fragmentation across platforms. Previous single-channel campaigns created message fatigue and unsubscribes due to siloed execution and generic messaging. Audiences expect personalized, contextual communication tailored to each platform's strengths.

**Business context:** {{business-context}}

**Available channels and current engagement metrics:** {{channels-and-metrics}}

## Requirements
- Each channel must deliver distinct value—no duplicate messages across platforms
- Timing must respect channel norms: SMS during business hours, email early morning, social during peak engagement windows
- Personalization must leverage behavioral data, preferences, and context—not just name insertion
- Fatigue prevention includes frequency caps, preference centers, and engagement-based throttling
- Unsubscribe signals must be honored across all channels simultaneously
- Focus on creating conversations, not broadcasts

## Output
Deliver a complete multichannel outreach strategy structured as:

**1. Audience Preference Assessment**
Targeted questions to uncover channel usage patterns, content preferences, and engagement behaviors.

**2. Coordinated Channel Flow**
Visual timeline showing touchpoint sequence, channel mix, and message themes across the customer journey.

**3. Channel-Specific Strategies**
For each platform (email, SMS, push, social):
- Unique value proposition
- Content approach and tone
- Personalization tactics leveraging platform strengths
- Optimal send times and frequency

**4. Scheduling Matrix**
Table format showing:
- Day/time recommendations per channel
- Minimum intervals between touches
- Frequency caps (daily/weekly)

**5. Personalization Playbook**
Specific data points and tactics for each channel:
- Behavioral triggers
- Contextual variables
- Dynamic content rules

**6. Fatigue Prevention Guidelines**
Numbered best practices including:
- Engagement-based throttling rules
- Preference center design
- Warning signals and adjustment triggers

**7. Cross-Channel Coordination Framework**
Actionable recommendations to ensure cohesive experiences:
- Message sequencing logic
- Theme consistency across platforms
- Engagement data sharing between channels

**8. Success Metrics**
KPIs to monitor engagement quality, fatigue signals, and flow optimization opportunities.
```
