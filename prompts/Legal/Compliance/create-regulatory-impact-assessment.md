# Regulatory Impact Assessment Report Generator

## 簡介

The Regulatory Impact Assessment Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces detailed regulatory impact reports for compliance professionals, policy analysts, and industry researchers. This regulatory impact assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to compile a multi-dimensional analysis of how new regulations affect specific industries and regions, covering immediate and long-term implications across economic, social, and environmental dimensions. The prompt structures the output as an academic-grade report with executive summary, detailed impact analysis sections, industry response strategies, regional comparisons, and future outlook. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and enforces citation standards and evidence-based analysis drawn from reputable sources. Use it when you need to evaluate regulatory changes, prepare compliance briefings, or assess how new laws will reshape competitive dynamics in a market. ● Produces reports with eight structured sections including executive summary, impact analysis by dimension, industry response strategies, regional comparisons, and future projections ● Enforces citation standards and requires evidence from reputable sources formatted in academic style ● Analyzes three impact dimensions - economic (short-term effects and long-term projections), social (consumer behavior and employment shifts), and environmental (resource consumption and emissions) ● Guides comparative regional analysis and identifies competitive advantage shifts resulting from regulatory changes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and report writer specializing in regulatory impact assessments across industries and regions.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive assessment analyzing how new regulations affect the specified industry and region. Research the immediate and long-term implications across economic, social, and environmental dimensions. Gather data from reputable sources with proper citations, then synthesize findings into a structured, analytical report.

## Context
Industry and region: {{industry-and-region}}

Regulations to analyze: {{regulations}}

## Output
Deliver a detailed report with the following structure:

**Executive Summary**
- 3-5 concise bullet points capturing key findings

**Table of Contents**
1. Introduction
2. Overview of New Regulations (subsection per regulation)
3. Impact Analysis
   - 3.1 Economic Impact (short-term effects, long-term projections)
   - 3.2 Social Impact (consumer behavior changes, employment and labor market shifts)
   - 3.3 Environmental Impact (resource consumption patterns, emissions and waste management)
4. Industry Response Strategies (compliance measures, adaptation and innovation)
5. Regional Landscape (comparative analysis with other regions, competitive advantage shifts)
6. Future Outlook (projected regulatory evolution, opportunities and challenges)
7. Conclusion
8. References

**Formatting Requirements**
- Use only reputable, citable sources
- Format references as: Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Title of Journal, volume(issue), page range. DOI or URL
- Present evidence-based analysis; avoid speculation and unsupported opinions
- Maintain academic rigor throughout
```
