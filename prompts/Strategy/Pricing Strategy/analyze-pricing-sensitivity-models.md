# Pricing Sensitivity Analysis Model Prompt

## 簡介

The Pricing Sensitivity Analysis Model Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds scenario-based pricing models combining demand elasticity, competitive dynamics, and behavioral economics for businesses navigating volatile markets. This pricing sensitivity prompt for ChatGPT generates a full analytical framework that calculates the revenue, margin, and market-share impact of price increases, strategic discounts, and competitive responses. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing scenario comparison matrices, price-demand relationship descriptions, psychological threshold identification, and risk-adjusted decision grids. Real use cases include SaaS companies testing tiered pricing changes, retailers evaluating seasonal discount strategies, and B2B firms modeling contract price adjustments before renewal cycles. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond simple margin math and account for non-linear demand behavior, customer segment variations, competitive reactions, and psychological anchors that traditional spreadsheets ignore. ● Generates side-by-side scenario comparisons for 5%, 10%, and 15% price changes, strategic discounting, and competitive response patterns with confidence intervals. ● Identifies psychological pricing thresholds, inflection points, and danger zones where pricing triggers customer churn or competitive retaliation. ● Produces customer segment analysis accounting for switching costs, loyalty factors, and lifetime value effects across different buyer types. ● Delivers a risk-adjusted decision grid mapping each pricing strategy to business objectives, complete with monitoring metrics and contingency triggers for post-launch tracking. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a pricing optimization specialist who combines quantitative modeling with behavioral economics to identify profitable pricing strategies. You analyze demand elasticity, competitive dynamics, and psychological pricing thresholds to reveal opportunities traditional models miss.

## Task
Build a comprehensive pricing sensitivity model that shows how price changes affect revenue, profit, and market position. Analyze multiple scenarios to identify optimal pricing strategies before market conditions shift.

## Context
The business faces critical pricing decisions in a volatile market where competitors are adjusting aggressively and customer loyalty is uncertain. Previous pricing experiments produced contradictory results by ignoring psychological thresholds and competitive responses. This analysis must account for non-linear demand behavior, competitive reactions, customer segment variations, and psychological anchors.

## Input
{{business-and-pricing-context}} — Include: your product/service, current price, cost structure (fixed and variable), customer demand patterns and seasonality, key competitor prices, and your primary business objectives (revenue/profit/market share goals).

## Analysis Framework

**Price Sensitivity Modeling**

1. Build scenarios showing impact of:
   - Price increases at 5%, 10%, and 15% levels
   - Strategic discounting approaches
   - Competitive response patterns
   - Psychological pricing thresholds

2. For each scenario, calculate:
   - Demand elasticity and non-linear responses
   - Margin impact and break-even points
   - Market share implications
   - Customer lifetime value effects
   - Competitive reaction probabilities

3. Segment analysis across customer types, accounting for switching costs and loyalty factors

4. Identify danger zones where pricing triggers negative cascades

## Output

Deliver your analysis as:

**Executive Summary**
- Critical findings and recommended action

**Data Foundation**
- Summary table of inputs and assumptions

**Scenario Comparison Matrix**
- Side-by-side impact scores for each pricing strategy
- Confidence intervals for all projections

**Price-Demand Relationships**
- Textual description of curves and inflection points
- Psychological anchor effects
- Points where traditional economics breaks down

**Risk-Adjusted Recommendations**
- Decision grid mapping strategies to risk tolerance and objectives
- Prioritized action steps with timeline
- Warning signals to monitor post-implementation

**Implementation Checklist**
- Sequenced steps with priority levels
- Key metrics to track
- Contingency triggers

Use tables for scenario comparisons, bullet points for key insights, and **bold emphasis** for critical decision points.
```
