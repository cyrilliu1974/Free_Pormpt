# SEO Product Description Generator for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The SEO Product Description Generator for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that creates keyword-optimized product descriptions with complete on-page SEO elements for online retailers and content teams. This product description prompt for ChatGPT delivers structured output including a persuasive product narrative with features and benefits, a call-to-action, plus ready-to-implement SEO components: 60-character title tags, 155-character meta descriptions, H1/H2 header recommendations, and image alt text. You provide the product name, target audience and tone, and business context; the prompt returns copy that balances natural keyword placement with compelling sales language. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to whichever text model you prefer. E-commerce managers use it to scale product catalog writing while maintaining SEO best practices, and agencies use it to deliver consistent, search-optimized descriptions across client inventories. Reach for this prompt when you need to write product pages that rank in search results and convert browsers into buyers, especially if you manage dozens or hundreds of SKUs. ● Outputs complete SEO elements - title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy, and image alt text - alongside the product narrative ● Structures descriptions with clear features, benefits, audience-specific messaging, and a call-to-action for higher conversion rates ● Accepts audience-and-tone and business-context variables so every description matches your brand voice and competitive positioning ● Emphasizes natural keyword integration and readability, avoiding keyword stuffing or jargon that harms user experience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce product description writer and SEO specialist.

## Task
Write a compelling, keyword-optimized product description that boosts search rankings and drives sales. Incorporate natural keyword placement, action verbs, vivid language, and persuasive copywriting that highlights unique selling points and solves customer pain points.

## Context
**Product:** {{product-name}}

**Target Audience & Tone:** {{audience-and-tone}}
(Describe who will buy this product and the tone of voice to use—e.g., "busy professionals, conversational and aspirational" or "outdoor enthusiasts, adventurous and technical")

**Business Context:** {{business-context}}
(Your brand, product category, and any competitive positioning to emphasize)

## Output
Deliver a structured product description and SEO elements:

**Product Name:** {{product-name}}

**Product Description:**

[Captivating opening line]

**Features:**
- [Feature 1]
- [Feature 2]
- [Feature 3]

**Benefits:**
- [Benefit 1]
- [Benefit 2]
- [Benefit 3]

[Tailored message that resonates with the target audience]

[Strong call-to-action encouraging purchase]

---

**SEO Optimization:**

**Title Tag:** [60-character optimized title with primary keyword]

**Meta Description:** [155-character persuasive summary with primary and secondary keywords]

**Header Tags:**
- H1: [Primary keyword phrase]
- H2: [Secondary keyword 1] | [Secondary keyword 2]

**Image Alt Tags:**
- [Descriptive alt text for image 1]
- [Descriptive alt text for image 2]

---

**Guidelines:**
- Use short paragraphs, bullet points, and formatting for readability
- Avoid unsubstantiated claims or overly technical jargon
- Ensure all keywords flow naturally within the copy
```
