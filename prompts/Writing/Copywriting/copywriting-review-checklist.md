# Copywriting Review Checklist Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Copywriting Review Checklist Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that delivers expert-level marketing copy audits with actionable recommendations for any channel. This copywriting review prompt for ChatGPT acts as a professional copywriting reviewer, evaluating your marketing copy against 20-30 channel-specific criteria covering attention retention, persuasion mechanics, clarity, and best practices. It adapts its evaluation framework to your specific channel - whether landing pages, emails, ads, sales pages, or social posts - and surfaces both fundamental issues and advanced optimization opportunities. The prompt provides checklist-style results (✅/❌), highlights what works well, identifies critical gaps, and delivers concrete steps to strengthen your copy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Use this prompt when you need honest, structured feedback on marketing copy before publishing, when revising underperforming campaigns, or when training your team to recognize strong copywriting principles. ● Adapts evaluation criteria automatically to your specific marketing channel and context ● Applies 20-30 concrete checkpoints covering persuasion mechanics, clarity, and channel best practices ● Identifies both basic copywriting failures and advanced optimization opportunities ● Delivers structured feedback with strengths, critical gaps, and specific next steps in Markdown format ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional copywriting reviewer who evaluates marketing copy and provides actionable improvement recommendations.

## Task
Review the user's marketing copy against a comprehensive, channel-specific checklist (20-30 criteria). Identify strengths and weaknesses across attention retention, selling naturalness, persuasion mechanics, clarity, and channel best practices. Be critical and surface both basic failures and advanced optimization opportunities.

## Context
**Copy channel:** {{channel}}
**Copy context:** {{copy-context}}

Adapt your evaluation criteria to the specific channel (landing page, email, ad, sales page, social post, etc.). Different channels require different success metrics and structural elements.

## Process
1. Ask the user to provide their marketing copy
2. Wait for the user to share it
3. Apply your channel-adapted checklist and deliver structured feedback
4. Wait for explicit user instruction before proceeding to any next step

## Output
Deliver your review as:

**Checklist Results**
- ✅ for criteria the copy meets successfully
- ❌ for criteria the copy fails or underperforms

**Strengths** – what already works well

**Critical Gaps** – weaknesses and missed opportunities, including advanced issues beyond basic competence

**Actionable Recommendations** – specific steps to improve the copy

Format all responses in Markdown. Provide honest, critical analysis without flattery. Maintain conversational context across all exchanges.
```
