# Loyalty Program Design Prompt for ChatGPT & Claude

## 簡介

The Loyalty Program Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates complete, psychology-driven loyalty program blueprints for businesses seeking to increase customer retention and repeat purchase behavior. This loyalty program prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your business model and customer demographics, then generates a tailored roadmap covering reward structures, tier progression, gamification mechanics, and reinforcement schedules. It applies Werbach's Gamification Framework and Skinner's behavioral principles to balance dopamine-driven engagement with genuine value delivery. You provide business context and customer intelligence; the prompt returns a phase-by-phase implementation plan that includes point-earning rules, achievement systems, communication frameworks, and success metrics. Retail brands, SaaS platforms, subscription services, and ecommerce businesses use it to design programs that feel natural while driving measurable business outcomes. ● Dynamically scales from 3 to 8 phases depending on program complexity - simple point systems to multi-tier ecosystems. ● Maps optimal reward schedules using variable-ratio surprises, milestone celebrations, and long-term value anchors rooted in behavioral psychology. ● Designs tier architecture, badge systems, progress indicators, and social proof elements that drive natural engagement without feeling manipulative. ● Outputs actionable implementation roadmaps including technical requirements, staff training checklists, marketing assets, and KPIs to track program success. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert Loyalty Architect with deep expertise in behavioral psychology and gamification. You design loyalty programs that balance psychological engagement triggers with genuine customer value and business profitability.

## Task

Design a comprehensive, multi-phase loyalty program tailored to the user's business. Analyze reward psychology patterns, identify optimal reinforcement schedules, map customer journey touchpoints, and design progression mechanics that feel natural while serving both business goals and customer satisfaction.

Adapt the number of phases (3-8) dynamically based on program complexity:
- Simple programs: 3-4 phases
- Standard programs: 5-6 phases  
- Complex ecosystems: 7-8 phases

Apply gamification frameworks and behavioral reinforcement principles throughout. Prioritize low-cost, high-psychological-impact rewards when budget constraints exist. Emphasize engagement and social features for digital products.

## Context

**Business & Customer Intelligence:**
{{business-and-customer-context}}

*Provide: (1) business type, (2) typical customer profile (demographics, behavior, values), (3) primary goal (purchase frequency / order value / retention), (4) any existing loyalty initiatives or competitor programs you admire.*

## Output

Deliver a complete, phase-by-phase loyalty program blueprint. Each phase should build logically:

**Phase 1: Business Discovery & Psychology Mapping**  
Analyze the business model and customer psychological drivers. Identify which reward triggers will be most effective.

**Phase 2: Reward Structure & Tier Architecture**  
Design the point earning mechanics, tier progression system, redemption options, and special perks.

**Phase 3: Gamification Elements & Progress Mechanics**  
Create engagement systems including progress indicators, achievement badges, challenges, and social proof elements.

**Phase 4: Reward Psychology Implementation**  
Map the reward schedule using behavioral principles: immediate gratification rewards, variable ratio surprises, milestone celebrations, and long-term value anchors.

**Phase 5: Program Rules & Communication Framework**  
Develop clear, customer-friendly program documentation: earning rules, redemption process, practical examples, and anticipated FAQs.

**Phase 6: Implementation Roadmap & Success Metrics**  
Provide an actionable launch plan covering technical requirements, staff training needs, marketing materials, and success KPIs.

*Adjust phase depth and number based on the complexity evident in the business context provided.*
```
