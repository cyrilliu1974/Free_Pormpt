# Minimal Line Art Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Minimal Line Art Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates simple, hand-drawn style illustrations with bold contours and customizable line colors for designers, marketers, and content creators. This minimal line art prompt for Midjourney produces centered, single-object illustrations with thick, consistent strokes that mimic authentic sketch quality without shading, gradients, or texture. The prompt enforces front-view composition on a warm off-white background with a 1:1 aspect ratio, making it ideal for social media posts, product mockups, logo concepts, and branding assets. It works reliably on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana by specifying bold smooth contours and slightly imperfect human sketch quality. Reach for this prompt when you need clean, modern illustration styles that feel approachable and handcrafted rather than digitally perfect. ● Produces thick, smooth contour lines with intentional imperfection that mimics real hand-drawn sketches. ● Lets you customize both the subject and line color while maintaining clean composition on a warm neutral background. ● Enforces no shading, no gradients, and no texture for true minimalist line art output. ● Outputs high-resolution 1:1 square images perfect for profile pictures, thumbnails, and social media. ## Prompt

```
Minimal hand-drawn line art illustration of {{subject}}, bold smooth contours with thick consistent strokes, slightly imperfect human sketch quality, {{line-color}} lines on warm off-white background, front view, stylized and simplified, single object centered on plain background, no shading, no gradients, no texture, clean and modern, 1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution
```
