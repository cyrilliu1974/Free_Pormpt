# Pixel Art Character Generator

## 簡介

The Pixel Art Character Generator is a free AI prompt that creates 16-bit style pixel art characters with sharp edges, limited color palettes, and nostalgic gaming aesthetics for digital artists and game designers. This pixel art character prompt for Midjourney produces retro-style characters set against bold neon geometric backgrounds on clean square canvases. You describe your character concept - warrior, robot, alien, hero, or any design - and the prompt renders it in authentic 16-bit pixel art style with collectible NFT composition. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, maintaining sharp pixel edges and the limited color palettes that defined classic gaming art. Use it to build character rosters for indie games, design collectible digital art, or create profile avatars with vintage arcade charm. Reach for this prompt when you need authentic pixel art characters without manual sprite work or when building collections that require consistent retro style across multiple designs. ● Produces characters with sharp pixel edges and limited color palettes true to classic 16-bit games ● Places designs on bold neon geometric backgrounds for modern collectible appeal ● Uses 3:4 aspect ratio and square canvas composition optimized for profile art and digital collections ● Maintains consistent nostalgic gaming vibe across multiple character designs ## Prompt

```
16-bit pixel art character: {{character-description}}. Bold neon geometric background, clean square canvas composition. Collectible NFT aesthetic with sharp pixel edges, limited color palette, nostalgic gaming vibe. --ar 3:4 --style raw
```
