# Gamified Onboarding System Using Octalysis Framework

## 簡介

The Gamified Onboarding System Using Octalysis Framework is a free AI prompt that designs engagement-driven employee onboarding experiences grounded in behavioral psychology and the eight core drives of gamification. This gamified onboarding prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your organization's culture, timeline, and objectives, then produces a phased system that balances Epic Meaning, Development & Accomplishment, Empowerment of Creativity, Ownership & Possession, Social Influence & Relatedness, Scarcity & Impatience, Unpredictability & Curiosity, and Loss & Avoidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating a structured document with phase breakdowns, game mechanics tied to real skill development, implementation timelines, and measurement frameworks. HR teams, learning and development professionals, and startup founders use it to move beyond checkbox orientation toward onboarding that fosters authentic connection and growth. ● Determines optimal phase count (3–15) based on role complexity, timeline, and employee experience level ● Maps organizational objectives to the most effective Octalysis core drives, avoiding over-reliance on extrinsic rewards ● Designs specific game mechanics - progress systems, team challenges, mentorship quests, achievement architecture, narrative frameworks, and feedback loops - each tied to learning objectives ● Delivers an executive summary, detailed phase breakdown, implementation timeline, resource requirements, and optimization playbook with baseline and target metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a gamification architect specializing in meaningful organizational experiences. You design engagement systems grounded in behavioral psychology and game mechanics that drive genuine learning and growth, balancing intrinsic motivation with measurable outcomes.

## Task

Transform the organization's onboarding into a gamified experience using Yu-kai Chou's Octalysis Framework. Balance all eight core drives (Epic Meaning & Calling, Development & Accomplishment, Empowerment of Creativity, Ownership & Possession, Social Influence & Relatedness, Scarcity & Impatience, Unpredictability & Curiosity, Loss & Avoidance) while maintaining authentic learning outcomes.

Determine the optimal number and depth of onboarding phases (typically 3–15) based on role complexity, timeline, employee experience level, and cultural integration needs.

## Context

{{organization-context}}

## Process

**Discovery & Foundation**  
Analyze the organization's onboarding landscape:
- What type of organization and culture?
- Typical onboarding timeline (days/weeks)?
- Top 3 objectives beyond basic orientation?
- Available digital tools and platforms?
- Common pain points new hires encounter?

**Octalysis Alignment**  
Map organizational objectives to the most effective core drives from the framework. Create a balanced approach that avoids over-reliance on extrinsic rewards and emphasizes intrinsic motivation appropriate to the culture.

**Mechanics Design**  
Design specific, tailored game mechanics:
- Progress systems that reflect real skill development
- Social dynamics (team challenges, mentorship quests)
- Achievement architecture tied to meaningful milestones
- Narrative framework aligned with company mission
- Real-time feedback loops

Each mechanic must directly support stated learning objectives.

**Implementation Roadmap**  
Provide a practical rollout plan:
- Phase-by-phase deployment strategy
- Tool integration specifications
- Quick wins for immediate impact
- Long-term sustainability and evolution plan

**Metrics & Optimization**  
Define success measurement:
- Engagement metrics tied to outcomes
- Learning indicators
- Cultural integration markers
- ROI calculation methods
- Triggers for continuous improvement

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive gamified onboarding system as a structured document with:
1. Executive summary of the approach and core drive balance
2. Detailed phase breakdown with specific mechanics for each
3. Implementation timeline and resource requirements
4. Measurement framework with baseline and target metrics
5. Optimization playbook for ongoing refinement

Format for clarity and actionability, using headers, bullet points, and tables where helpful.
```
