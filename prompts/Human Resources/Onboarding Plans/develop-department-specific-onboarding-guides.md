# Department-Specific Onboarding Guide Builder

## 簡介

The Department-Specific Onboarding Guide Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable onboarding plans tailored to the unique workflows, systems, and culture of individual departments. This department onboarding prompt for ChatGPT applies the ADDIE instructional design framework (Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation) to build guides that address the specific learning needs, daily workflows, tools, and cultural norms of your team. You provide context about your department's goals, typical workflows, the tools and systems new hires must learn, and your organization's culture, and the prompt returns a complete week-by-week onboarding plan with learning objectives, practical exercises, knowledge checks, milestones, and 30/60/90-day evaluation points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing detailed text output that department managers can execute without instructional design expertise. This prompt is for HR professionals, department managers, and team leads who need onboarding plans that go beyond generic orientation and build both technical competency and cultural fluency in new hires. ● Analyzes department-specific learning needs, knowledge gaps, and role success indicators based on your context. ● Designs learning objectives and sequences that align with real workflow patterns and cultural integration points. ● Develops detailed content modules, practical exercises tied to actual tasks, resource lists, and competency checks. ● Provides week-by-week implementation timelines with milestones, ownership assignments, and communication touchpoints. ● Establishes evaluation methods with 30/60/90-day feedback mechanisms and program refinement triggers. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert instructional designer specializing in department-specific onboarding. Your approach recognizes that each department operates with distinct workflows, language, and success patterns that generic orientation programs fail to address.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, actionable onboarding guide using the ADDIE framework (Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation) that accelerates new hires from outsider to productive insider while building both technical competency and cultural fluency.

## Context

Department & Goals: {{department-context}}

Daily Workflows: {{daily-workflows}}

Tools & Systems: {{tools-systems}}

Organization Culture: {{org-culture}}

Effective onboarding bridges theory with immediate application, respects learning curves while meeting business urgency, and measures real competency rather than checkbox completion. The guide must be executable by department managers without extensive training.

## Output

Structure your onboarding guide under these five ADDIE headings:

### Analysis
Identify department-specific learning needs, knowledge gaps new hires typically face, and success indicators for this role.

### Design
Outline learning objectives aligned to actual workflow patterns, sequencing that builds competency progressively, and integration points for cultural norms.

### Development
Provide detailed content modules, practical exercises tied to real tasks, resource lists, and knowledge checks that confirm understanding.

### Implementation
Define week-by-week timelines with specific milestones, assign ownership (manager/peer/new hire), and include communication touchpoints.

### Evaluation
Establish competency assessment methods, feedback mechanisms at 30/60/90 days, and adjustment triggers to refine the program.

Make every element specific, actionable, and ready to execute immediately.
```
