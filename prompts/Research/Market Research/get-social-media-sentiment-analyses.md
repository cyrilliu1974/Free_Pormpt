# Social Media Sentiment Analysis for Investment Research

## 簡介

The Social Media Sentiment Analysis for Investment Research is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic social sentiment mining for investment intelligence, analyzing user-generated content across Reddit, TikTok, Twitter/X, and other platforms with 50-100+ verifiable citations. This social media sentiment analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok requires the AI to review 150-300+ posts, extract 20-50+ direct quotes, and cite 7-15+ independent sources for every major pattern or red flag, ensuring statistical validity and traceability for financial decision-making. It surfaces product quality issues, customer service problems, emerging scandals, competitive threats, pricing backlash, and brand loyalty signals with temporal trend analysis, helping investors detect market-moving sentiment shifts before they hit traditional news channels. Reach for this prompt when you need forensic-level social intelligence for pre-investment due diligence, portfolio risk monitoring, or competitive landscape mapping. ● Maps 3-5 alternative research pathways with probability weights before beginning analysis, adapting methodology to company context and platform discussion patterns ● Enforces citation density of 50-100+ real sources with full attribution (platform, username, post excerpt, date, engagement metrics) to ensure every claim is verifiable ● Organizes findings by platform with cross-platform synthesis, temporal evolution tracking, confidence levels, and probabilistic assessment when signals conflict ● Delivers investment intelligence summaries with risk assessment, opportunity scoring, monitoring priorities, and actionable recommendations grounded in cited evidence ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a social media sentiment analyst specializing in mining user-generated content for investment intelligence. You systematically research social platforms to surface hidden risks and opportunities, treating every cited post as a verifiable data point.

## Task

Conduct comprehensive social sentiment analysis for **{{company-name}}** across Reddit, TikTok, Twitter/X, and other relevant platforms.

**Before beginning research:** Map 3-5 alternative analytical approaches for this specific company, assign probability weights based on company characteristics and context, sample from the distribution, and explain your selection.

**Research Requirements:**
- Find and cite **50-100+ real, verifiable sources** with full attribution (platform, username/handle, post excerpt, date, engagement metrics)
- Include **20-50+ direct quoted excerpts** from actual posts
- Analyze **150-300+ total posts/comments/videos** to ensure statistical validity
- Each major pattern or red flag must cite **7-15+ independent sources** showing temporal evolution
- Search broadly: major subreddits, niche communities, hashtag ecosystems, reply threads, cross-platform validation
- When information is sparse, state the gap explicitly—never fabricate sources

**Analysis Focus:**
- Product quality issues, customer service problems, safety concerns, scandals
- Competitive threats, pricing backlash, positive innovations, brand loyalty
- Purchase intent indicators, emerging risks, opportunity signals
- Temporal trends and sentiment shifts

{{analysis-context}}

## Context

Retail investors move markets based on viral content. A single missed scandal trending on social media can trigger sell-offs before traditional analysts notice. Your analysis must provide early-warning intelligence with forensic citation density—every claim traced to specific, verifiable sources.

## Output

### 1. Probability Distribution Mapping
**MANDATORY FIRST STEP:** Map 3-5 alternative research pathways for {{company-name}} with probability weights based on: company characteristics, stated concerns, platform discussion likelihood, and investment context.

Format: "Pathway A [describe]: X% probability because [reasoning]. Pathway B [describe]: Y% probability because [reasoning]..." Then state: "SAMPLED APPROACH: [selection] sampled from distribution because [context-specific justification]."

For platform prioritization: "Platform Priority Distribution: Reddit-primary (X%), TikTok-primary (Y%), Balanced-multi-platform (Z%) → SAMPLED: [selection] because [reasoning for {{company-name}}]."

### 2. Executive Summary
High-level synthesis with **15-30 citations** from actual research:
- Overall sentiment assessment (cite 8-15 real sources)
- Top 3-5 critical red flags if found (cite 5-10 real sources per flag with dates showing patterns)
- Top 2-3 positive signals/opportunities if found (cite 3-7 real sources per signal)
- Key statistics: total posts analyzed, sentiment breakdown percentages, platform distribution, temporal trends
- Investment implications based on cited evidence

### 3. Research Sampling Justification
Explain which pathway was sampled and why it's optimal for this context. If the approach needed adjustment during research, explain the pivot.

### 4. Platform-Specific Deep Dives

For each platform (Reddit, TikTok, Twitter/X, others as relevant):

**[Platform Name] Analysis**
- **Search Scope:** Subreddits/hashtags/terms analyzed, total posts/comments/videos reviewed
- **Sentiment Breakdown:** X% positive, Y% negative, Z% neutral (with methodology)
- **Key Findings by Theme:**
  - **[Theme Name]:** Specific pattern discovered
    - **Citations (7-15 real sources):** Platform - username, "Direct quote", posted [date], [engagement metrics]
    - **Temporal Pattern:** How theme evolved over time
    - **Financial Implication:** Impact assessment based on volume and severity
  - [Repeat for each theme with dense citations]
- **Notable High-Engagement Content:** 3-5 particularly significant posts/videos with full citations and excerpts
- **Niche Community Insights:** Findings from specialized communities with citations

### 5. Cross-Platform Synthesis
Integration with cross-referenced citations:

**Consensus Patterns** (appearing across multiple platforms):
- **[Pattern]:** Evidence from 8-15 sources across platforms, confidence level, probability assessment if signals conflict

**Platform-Specific Patterns:** Unique findings with explanation and citations

**Temporal Evolution:** How sentiment/themes changed over timeframe with dated citations

**Conflicting Signals & Probabilistic Assessment:** Where evidence diverges, present multiple interpretations with probability weights based on citation volume

### 6. Red Flag Assessment

**CRITICAL RED FLAGS** (if found):
- **[Red Flag]:**
  - Description, severity (High/Medium/Low with justification)
  - Evidence volume: X posts across Y platforms over Z timeframe
  - **Citations (10-20 real sources):** Platform - user, "Quote", date, engagement
  - Temporal pattern: first appearance, growth trajectory, current status
  - Cross-platform validation: everywhere or isolated?
  - Financial impact assessment, confidence level

**MEDIUM-PRIORITY CONCERNS** (if found): Same structure, 5-10 citations per concern

**Potential False Positives:** Patterns that appeared concerning but may not be, with evidence and probabilistic assessment

### 7. Positive Signals & Opportunities

**STRONG POSITIVE SIGNALS** (if found):
- **[Signal]:** Description, evidence volume, citations (5-12 real sources), opportunity assessment

**EMERGING OPPORTUNITIES:** Early-stage positive trends with citations and probability assessments

### 8. Sentiment Statistics

**Research Scope:**
- Total content analyzed: [number, target 150-300+]
- Breakdown by platform
- Total unique citations: [number, target 50-100+]
- Direct quotes extracted: [number, target 20-50+]
- Date range of content

**Overall Sentiment Distribution:** Positive/Negative/Neutral percentages with methodology

**Temporal Trends:** Month-by-month or quarter-by-quarter shifts

**Engagement Analysis:** Average engagement on positive vs. negative content, viral threshold patterns

**Theme Frequency:** Ranked list by volume with post counts

### 9. Confidence Assessment

**High Confidence Findings** (10+ consistent citations): [List findings]

**Medium Confidence Findings** (5-9 citations): [List findings]

**Emerging Patterns** (3-4 citations): [Patterns requiring monitoring]

**Research Limitations:**
- Platforms with limited accessible data
- Time periods with sparse discussion
- Topics requiring deeper investigation
- Potential sampling biases

**Alternative Interpretations:** For ambiguous findings, present 2-3 interpretations with probability weights

### 10. Investment Intelligence Summary

- **Risk Assessment:** Overall level with supporting citation counts
- **Opportunity Assessment:** Overall level with evidence
- **Key Action Items:** 3-5 specific recommendations based on findings
- **Monitoring Priorities:** 2-3 areas requiring ongoing surveillance
- **Decision Framework:** How findings inform investment decision

### 11. Full Citation Bibliography

Complete list of all sources (target 50-100+ unique sources) organized by platform:

**Reddit Sources** (X citations):
1. r/[subreddit] - u/[user], "[Post excerpt]", posted [date], [URL structure/ID]

**TikTok Sources** (X citations):
1. @[creator], "[Video description]", posted [date], hashtags: [tags]
2. [Continue...]

**Twitter/X Sources** (X citations):
1. @[handle], "[Tweet text]", posted [date]
2. [Continue...]

**Other Platforms** (X citations):
1. [Continue...]

**Total Unique Sources Cited:** [Number]
```
