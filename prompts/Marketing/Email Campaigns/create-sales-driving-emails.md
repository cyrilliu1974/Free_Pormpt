# Sales-Driving Promotional Email Generator

## 簡介

The Sales-Driving Promotional Email Generator is a free AI prompt that writes complete promotional emails designed to convert readers into buyers through strategic urgency, benefit-focused copy, and clear calls-to-action. This promotional email prompt for ChatGPT produces structured sales emails with every essential element: attention-grabbing subject lines, benefit-rich body copy that connects features to reader value, scarcity cues that motivate immediate action, social proof elements like testimonials and trust signals, and strong calls-to-action with expiration reminders. You provide the product or service details and the discount terms with expiration, and the prompt outputs a complete email ready to send. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering conversational copy structured for skimmability and conversion. Reach for this prompt when you need to launch a flash sale, announce a limited-time discount, or drive revenue with time-sensitive promotions - whether you're a solopreneur, marketing team, or ecommerce manager. ● Outputs a complete email from subject line through P.S., including expiration reminders and CTAs ● Structures body copy around concrete benefits, scarcity signals, and social proof to maximize conversion ● Uses dependency grammar principles for clear cause-and-effect language that shows how features deliver value ● Keeps paragraphs short and tone conversational to improve readability and engagement across devices ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter crafting promotional emails that drive sales.

## Task
Write a compelling promotional email that announces a limited-time offer, emphasizes key benefits and unique selling points, and creates urgency to encourage immediate action.

## Context
The email promotes: {{product-or-service}}

Offer details: {{discount-and-expiration}}
(Include the specific discount, promotion terms, and expiration date/time)

## Output
Deliver a complete promotional email with:

**Subject:** Eye-catching subject line that highlights the limited-time nature

**Opening:** Greeting and attention-grabbing paragraph that announces the offer

**Body paragraph 1:** Describe the product/service with 3-4 concrete benefits, using clear cause-and-effect language that shows how features deliver value

**Body paragraph 2:** Build urgency through scarcity cues (limited quantities, deadline approaching, exclusive access)

**Body paragraph 3:** Include social proof—customer testimonials, ratings, popularity indicators, or trust signals

**Closing:** Strong call-to-action with a direct link or instruction, plus a reminder of when the offer expires

**Sign-off:** Professional closing with sender name

**P.S.:** One additional incentive or urgency reminder

Write conversationally and focus on reader benefits over features. Keep paragraphs short for skimmability.
```
