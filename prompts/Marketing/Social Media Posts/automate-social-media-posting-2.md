# Social Media Automation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Automation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive social media scheduling and management plans for businesses, marketers, and content teams. This social media automation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating a complete implementation strategy tailored to your business context, target platforms, and posting frequency. It produces a structured plan with numbered steps and lettered sub-points covering content calendar frameworks, scheduling tool workflows (Hootsuite, Buffer, and similar platforms), cross-platform consistency tactics, audience engagement protocols, and performance measurement methods. The output helps you transition from manual posting to systematic automation while maintaining authentic audience connections. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or document a repeatable social media workflow that balances promotional content, industry news, and engagement-driven posts across multiple channels. ● Creates content calendar frameworks that mix promotional posts, curated industry news, and interactive engagement content ● Provides step-by-step scheduling tool setup instructions with workflow examples for platforms like Hootsuite and Buffer ● Defines audience interaction strategies and response protocols to maintain authentic engagement at scale ● Delivers measurable performance metrics and tracking methods to refine automation strategies over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in content automation and multi-platform management.

## Task
Create a comprehensive automation strategy for scheduling and managing social media content. Deliver a step-by-step implementation plan that covers content planning, scheduling workflows, audience engagement tactics, and performance measurement.

## Context
Business and audience: {{business-context}}

Target platforms: {{platforms}}

Desired posting frequency: {{posting-frequency}}

## Output
Provide your strategy as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps and examples. Structure your response to include:

1. Content calendar framework incorporating promotional posts, industry news, and engagement-focused content
2. Scheduling tool setup and workflow (reference tools like Hootsuite or Buffer)
3. Tactics for maintaining consistent presence across platforms
4. Audience interaction strategies and response protocols
5. Metrics and methods for measuring campaign effectiveness

Ensure each section contains actionable steps tailored to the specified business context and platforms.
```
