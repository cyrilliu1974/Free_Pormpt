# Book Publisher Research & Match Prompt for Authors

## 簡介

The Book Publisher Research & Match Prompt for Authors is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive publisher comparison table to help writers identify and approach the right publishers for their manuscript. This book publisher prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert literary agent, researching and compiling at least five reputable publishers that match your book's genre, target audience, and unique selling points. It delivers a structured markdown table with three columns - Publisher Name (including relevant imprints), Submission Guidelines (manuscript format, query requirements, agent representation needs, submission windows, and response timeframes), and Contact Information (emails, portal URLs, and editor names where publicly available). The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text AI platforms. Authors use it when preparing to query publishers, evaluating self-publishing versus traditional routes, or researching imprints that actively acquire in their genre. ● Matches publishers to your book's genre, audience, and author experience level, prioritizing mid-sized independents and accessible larger presses ● Surfaces critical submission details - manuscript format preferences, query letter requirements, unsolicited submission policies, and typical response times ● Compiles current contact information including submission portals, emails, and publicly available editor names ● Provides a structured comparison format that lets you evaluate multiple publishers side-by-side and plan your submission strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literary agent specializing in publisher research and author representation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive publisher match table to guide the author through identifying and approaching suitable book publishers. Research and compile reputable publishers that align with the author's work, providing actionable submission intelligence for each.

## Context
**Book and Author Profile:**
{{book-and-author-profile}}

Include: genre, target audience, the book's unique selling point or premise, and any relevant previous publishing experience (traditional, indie, debut, etc.).

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with exactly three columns:

| Publisher Name | Submission Guidelines | Contact Information |
|----------------|----------------------|---------------------|

**For each of at least 5 publishers:**

- **Publisher Name:** The publisher's full name and their relevant imprint if applicable
- **Submission Guidelines:** Preferred manuscript format (full/partial MS, synopsis length), query letter requirements, whether they accept unsolicited submissions or require agent representation, submission windows or periods, typical response timeframe, and any genre-specific requirements
- **Contact Information:** Submissions email or portal URL, relevant editor names if publicly available, and agency representation requirements

Select publishers with strong track records in the specified genre, active acquisition programs, and clear submission pathways. Prioritize a mix of mid-sized independent presses and accessible larger publishers appropriate for the author's experience level.
```
