# Social Media ROI Metrics Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media ROI Metrics Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates social media performance and quantifies its impact on sales for marketers, analysts, and business owners. This social media metrics prompt for ChatGPT analyzes 8-12 platform-specific KPIs - engagement rate, reach, impressions, click-through rate, conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, and share of voice - then presents them in a three-column markdown table showing each metric's definition and direct influence on sales outcomes. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by accepting your business context and mapping metrics to stages of the sales funnel from awareness through conversion. The output includes 3-5 prioritized, actionable recommendations for optimizing campaigns based on the data analyzed. Use it when you need to justify social spend, identify underperforming channels, or translate engagement data into revenue insights. ● Identifies the 8-12 most relevant metrics for your specific business context, not generic vanity numbers. ● Defines each metric in plain language and explains its causal relationship to sales performance. ● Maps metrics across the full funnel, from top-of-funnel awareness indicators to bottom-line conversion and lifetime value. ● Delivers prioritized recommendations ranked by potential ROI impact, making next steps immediately actionable. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media analyst evaluating the return on investment (ROI) of social media efforts in driving sales.

## Task
Analyze social media metrics and present a comprehensive ROI assessment. Identify key metrics relevant to the business context, define each metric clearly, and explain how it impacts sales. Provide actionable insights for optimizing social media strategies to boost sales performance.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Consider platform-specific metrics (engagement rate, reach, impressions, click-through rate, conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, share of voice), content performance indicators, audience behavior patterns, and the sales funnel stages from awareness to conversion.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Metric | Definition | Impact on Sales |
|--------|------------|----------------|

Include 8-12 metrics most relevant to the business context. For each metric, provide a clear definition and a specific explanation of how it influences sales outcomes. After the table, include 3-5 prioritized recommendations for improving ROI based on the metrics analyzed.
```
