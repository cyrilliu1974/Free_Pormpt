# Email Campaign Strategy Prompt for Lead Nurturing

## 簡介

The Email Campaign Strategy Prompt for Lead Nurturing is a free AI prompt that builds conversion-focused email campaigns tailored to your product, audience, and unique selling proposition. This lead nurturing prompt for ChatGPT walks you through campaign architecture, content strategy, personalization tactics, calls-to-action, send schedules, and measurement frameworks. It structures your campaign as a numbered plan with clear rationale for each recommendation, connecting every element to your conversion goals. Built for email marketers who need a strategic blueprint rather than just copy, it works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver actionable, funnel-aware guidance. Whether you're launching a new nurture sequence or optimizing an existing process, the prompt analyzes your current approach and delivers a step-by-step roadmap. ● Outputs a six-part campaign plan covering architecture, content, segmentation, CTAs, timing, and KPIs ● Connects each recommendation to audience psychology and conversion mechanics ● Incorporates your product details, target audience, unique selling proposition, and current process ● Delivers send schedules, personalization triggers, and iteration tactics grounded in funnel logic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist building a lead-nurturing campaign that drives conversions.

## Task
Develop a complete email campaign strategy for {{product-or-service}} that moves leads through the funnel to purchase. Deliver a step-by-step plan covering sequence design, content themes, timing, personalization tactics, and performance optimization.

## Context
**Offer:** {{product-or-service}}  
**Audience:** {{target-audience}}  
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}  
**Current nurturing approach:** {{current-process}}  

Structure your recommendations to maximize open rates, click-through rates, and conversions. Use clear, actionable language that connects each campaign element to the next in logical dependency.

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered plan with subheadings for:

1. **Campaign Architecture** – sequence length, email types, and funnel stages  
2. **Content Strategy** – themes, messaging hooks, and value delivery per email  
3. **Personalization & Segmentation** – dynamic content, triggers, and audience splits  
4. **Calls-to-Action** – specific CTAs for each email and their conversion goals  
5. **Send Schedule** – timing, frequency, and cadence rationale  
6. **Measurement & Optimization** – KPIs to track and iteration tactics  

For each section, explain *why* the recommendation works for this audience and offer, and how it connects to the overall conversion goal.
```
