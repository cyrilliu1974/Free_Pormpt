# Stress-Free Workspace Design Recommendations

## 簡介

The Stress-Free Workspace Design Recommendations is a free AI prompt that creates actionable, research-backed plans to optimize physical work environments for stress reduction and productivity. This workspace design prompt for ChatGPT delivers specific recommendations across eight critical areas: spatial layout, lighting systems, temperature and air quality controls, ergonomic furniture and equipment, color psychology and decor choices, biophilic design elements, noise reduction strategies, and dedicated relaxation and privacy zones. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok, it analyzes your workspace context and produces 2-3 concrete, evidence-based actions per category, organized in a checklist format that teams can implement immediately. Whether you're redesigning an office, setting up a home workspace, or advising clients on workplace wellness, the prompt translates ergonomic science and environmental psychology into practical design decisions. ● Provides research-backed recommendations across layout, lighting, air quality, furniture, acoustics, color, decor, and biophilic design ● Organizes output into eight structured sections with specific, implementable actions rather than vague advice ● Prioritizes interventions proven to reduce stress and improve cognitive function in workplace settings ● Adapts recommendations to your specific workspace context, constraints, and use cases ## Prompt
