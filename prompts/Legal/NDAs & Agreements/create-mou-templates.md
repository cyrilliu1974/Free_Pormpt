# MOU Template Generator for Business Collaborations

## 簡介

The MOU Template Generator for Business Collaborations is a free AI prompt that creates professional Memorandum of Understanding documents for partnerships across any industry. This MOU prompt for ChatGPT takes a brief collaboration summary and produces a complete, structured agreement covering all essential elements: partnership scope, shared objectives, role definitions for each party, joint responsibilities, intellectual property handling, confidentiality protections, term duration, amendment procedures, and execution signature blocks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering clear, jargon-free language that captures partnership terms without requiring legal expertise. Teams use it to formalize vendor relationships, research collaborations, joint ventures, and strategic alliances quickly. Reach for this prompt when you need a professional framework to document partnership terms before engaging legal counsel, or when formalizing collaborations that don't yet require binding contracts. ● Structures all critical MOU sections including preamble, scope, objectives, role definitions, IP rights, and confidentiality clauses ● Adapts to any industry or partnership type by working from a simple collaboration summary variable ● Outputs professional, accessible language that avoids legal jargon while maintaining formal completeness ● Includes execution blocks with signature fields, dates, and proper formatting for immediate use ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in drafting memoranda of understanding (MOUs) for business collaborations, with strong skills in capturing the essence of partnerships and outlining key terms.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive MOU document for a collaboration between two parties. The MOU should cover scope, objectives, roles, responsibilities, intellectual property, confidentiality, and execution terms. Use clear, accessible language that avoids legal jargon while remaining professionally complete.

## Context
Collaboration details: {{collaboration-summary}}

The MOU must be adaptable across industries and partnership types. Focus on capturing agreed-upon terms while providing a flexible framework that parties can formalize.

## Output
Produce a complete MOU document structured as follows:

**Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)**

**Between** [Party A Name] **and** [Party B Name]

**Preamble:**
Introduce both parties and state the collaboration purpose, including the effective date.

**Scope:**
List the main areas or activities covered by the collaboration (3-5 bullet points).

**Objectives:**
State the goals both parties aim to achieve through this partnership (3-5 bullet points).

**Party A Roles and Responsibilities:**
Define Party A's specific contributions and obligations (3-5 bullet points).

**Party B Roles and Responsibilities:**
Define Party B's specific contributions and obligations (3-5 bullet points).

**Joint Responsibilities:**
Highlight areas where both parties will collaborate directly (2-4 bullet points).

**Term:**
Specify the duration, including start and end dates, and conditions for early termination.

**Intellectual Property:**
Address how IP rights will be handled during and after the collaboration.

**Confidentiality:**
Include a clause protecting proprietary information shared between parties.

**Amendments:**
State that modifications require mutual written agreement.

**Execution:**
Provide signature blocks for both parties with fields for signature, printed name, title, and date.
```
