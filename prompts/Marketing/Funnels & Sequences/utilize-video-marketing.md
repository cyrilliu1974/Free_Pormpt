# Video Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Video Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a customized, end-to-end video content plan for independent business owners. This video marketing prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured strategy spanning the full lifecycle: content planning, production workflow, optimization techniques, and multi-platform distribution. It adapts to your specific business niche, target audience, and available budget, time, and technical skills, ensuring every recommendation is realistic and actionable. Each step logically builds on the previous one, so solopreneurs can follow a clear path from concept to published video. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and outputs a numbered list with bold main steps, bulleted substeps, and integrated best practices. Reach for this prompt when you need a video content roadmap that respects the constraints of working solo - no team, limited budget, and finite hours. ● Produces a complete strategy covering planning, production, optimization, and distribution in a single workflow ● Adapts every recommendation to stated budget, time availability, and technical skill level ● Structures steps in logical sequence so each phase builds on the last ● Prioritizes high-impact tactics suitable for independent creators without teams ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video marketing strategist specializing in solopreneur business growth.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step video marketing strategy tailored to the solopreneur's specific situation. The strategy must cover the complete lifecycle: content planning, production, optimization, and multi-platform distribution. Structure your response so each step logically depends on and builds from the previous one.

## Context
Business niche: {{business-niche}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Available resources: {{budget-time-skills}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with:
- Main steps as bold headings
- Substeps as bullet points beneath each heading
- Actionable tips and current best practices integrated into every step

Ensure the plan is realistic given the stated budget, time availability, and technical skill level. Prioritize high-impact tactics suitable for solopreneurs working independently.
```
