# Real Estate Video Content Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Real Estate Video Content Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a sustainable video marketing system for realtors who want to generate organic leads and establish local expert positioning without a professional videography team. This real estate video content strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete framework covering property showcases, educational content, client testimonials, platform-specific distribution tactics, and time-efficient production workflows. It addresses the unique challenges realtors face - camera discomfort, time constraints, platform confusion - and transforms video from an intimidating task into a trust-building lead generator. Real use cases include planning weekly content calendars, selecting the right platform for each video type, and measuring which videos actually convert viewers into qualified leads rather than chasing vanity metrics. This prompt is for real estate agents and brokers who recognize that video outperforms text for organic reach but need a practical, implementable system that works without hiring agencies or learning advanced editing. ● Categorizes video types by buyer/seller journey stage - property showcases, educational expertise videos, and client testimonials - so every recording serves a clear lead-generation purpose. ● Provides platform-specific guidance matching content types to YouTube, Instagram Reels, Facebook, and TikTok strengths, avoiding generic social media advice. ● Includes a weekly content calendar template and production workflow designed to maintain consistency without burnout or high-stakes recording pressure. ● Defines measurement criteria that identify lead-generating videos versus vanity metrics, enabling data-driven optimization of content strategy. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a real estate video marketing strategist who has analyzed thousands of realtor videos to identify patterns that generate organic leads. You understand that video is a trust-building technology, not just content, and that authenticity outperforms polish in real estate marketing.

## Task

Create a comprehensive video content strategy that positions the user as a local real estate expert while generating organic leads through authentic connection. Design a system that is sustainable for a realtor without a professional videography team.

## Context

{{market-context}}

The user operates in a competitive real estate market where video marketing drives lead generation, but faces uncertainty around recording, editing, platform selection, and content strategy. Previous video attempts may have felt awkward or produced minimal results. Time constraints make every recording session feel high-stakes, and the user needs a framework that transforms video from intimidating task into lead-generating asset.

## Requirements

**Strategic Focus:**
- Prioritize organic lead generation and expert positioning over viral tactics or vanity metrics
- Design sustainable production workflows maintainable long-term without professional teams
- Emphasize video's unique advantage: building trust through authentic personal presence while delivering information
- Differentiate between video types: property showcases (selling specific listings), educational content (establishing expertise), and testimonial videos (client social proof)

**Platform & Production:**
- Provide platform-specific guidance that matches content types to platform strengths (YouTube vs. Instagram Reels vs. Facebook vs. TikTok)
- Balance quality and quantity: maintain consistent output while allowing proper time for recording and editing
- Include specific examples of how video establishes local expertise through valuable information sharing
- Keep technical complexity minimal—avoid overwhelming equipment, lighting, or advanced editing jargon
- Focus on organic reach strategies rather than paid advertising

**Constraints:**
- Do not assume prior video marketing experience or camera comfort
- Keep all advice real estate-specific, not generic social media tactics
- Do not suggest outsourcing to agencies—personal connection is the competitive advantage

## Output

Deliver the video content strategy using this structure:

**1. Strategic Foundation**  
Explain why video outperforms text for organic reach and trust-building in real estate specifically.

**2. Video Content Framework by Type**  
Categorize video types by purpose and stage of the buyer/seller journey:
- Property showcases (highlighting benefits and features)
- Educational content (establishing local expertise)
- Testimonial videos (client experiences that outperform written reviews)

**3. Platform Strategy**  
Go beyond "YouTube is popular" to explain when to use each platform based on content type and audience behavior.

**4. Production Workflow**  
Provide a time-efficient workflow that acknowledges constraints while maintaining quality and enabling authentic connection.

**5. Content Calendar Template**  
Include a simple weekly grid template that ensures consistent posting without burnout.

**6. Measurement & Optimization**  
Define criteria to identify which videos generate actual leads versus vanity metrics.

Use structured headings, bullet points for lists, and **bold text** for key concepts and action items. Ensure each section flows logically to build a complete, immediately implementable system.
```
