# SEO Strategy Generator for Websites

## 簡介

The SEO Strategy Generator for Websites is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive optimization plans tailored to any website and business context. This SEO strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes your website URL and business details to produce a structured plan spanning keyword research (primary, secondary, and long-tail terms), on-page optimization (title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy, content optimization, internal linking), technical SEO (site speed, mobile-friendliness, sitemaps, schema markup), off-page tactics (link building, social media, local SEO), and progress tracking with KPIs and tools. Digital marketers, in-house SEO teams, and business owners use it to move from scattered tactics to a cohesive roadmap that aligns with current search engine algorithms. The output is a ready-to-implement text document you can hand to developers, content writers, and agencies. ● Produces primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword lists with search intent mapping. ● Delivers on-page optimization formulas for title tags, meta descriptions, headers, content depth, and internal linking. ● Includes technical SEO checklist items for site speed, mobile UX, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and schema markup types. ● Recommends off-page tactics such as link-building outreach targets, social media priorities, and local SEO actions. ● Defines KPIs, monitoring tools (Google Search Console, GA4, rank trackers), and reporting cadence for measuring progress. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an SEO strategist building comprehensive optimization plans for websites.

## Task

Analyze the provided website and produce a complete, actionable SEO strategy covering keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO, off-page tactics, and measurement.

## Context

Website: {{website-url}}

Additional context: {{business-context}}

## Output

Deliver a structured SEO plan with specific recommendations for each section:

### Website Overview
Summarize the site's niche, purpose, current positioning, and competitive landscape.

### Target Audience
Define demographics, search intent, pain points, and behavioral patterns of the primary audience.

### Keyword Research
- **Primary Keywords**: 3-5 high-value terms with strong search volume and business relevance
- **Secondary Keywords**: 5-8 supporting terms that expand topical coverage
- **Long-tail Keywords**: 8-12 specific phrases targeting user intent and conversion opportunities

### On-Page Optimization
- **Title Tags**: Formula and examples optimized for target keywords and click-through rate
- **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling descriptions that drive clicks while incorporating keywords
- **Header Tags**: Hierarchical structure (H1-H4) recommendations for content organization
- **Content Optimization**: Keyword placement, content depth, readability improvements, and multimedia integration
- **Internal Linking**: Strategy for distributing page authority and improving site navigation

### Technical SEO
- **Site Speed**: Specific optimizations for load time (compression, caching, image optimization, code minification)
- **Mobile-Friendliness**: Responsive design checks and mobile UX improvements
- **Sitemap & Indexing**: XML sitemap structure and robots.txt recommendations
- **Schema Markup**: Structured data types appropriate for the site's content (Organization, Product, Article, FAQ, etc.)

### Off-Page Optimization
- **Link Building Strategy**: Outreach targets, content partnerships, and guest posting opportunities
- **Social Media Integration**: Platform priorities and content distribution tactics
- **Local SEO**: Google Business Profile optimization, citation building, and review management (if location-based)

### Progress Tracking
- **KPIs**: Organic traffic, keyword rankings, conversion rate, bounce rate, page authority, backlink growth
- **Monitoring Tools**: Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, rank tracking tools, backlink analyzers
- **Reporting Frequency**: Monthly executive summary with weekly metric snapshots
```
