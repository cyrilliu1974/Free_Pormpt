# Google My Business Listing Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Google My Business Listing Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces creative, structured optimization tactics for local businesses looking to enhance their GMB profiles and improve local search visibility. This Google My Business prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, location, unique selling proposition, and competitive landscape to generate actionable enhancement ideas across multiple dimensions - from category selection and attribute optimization to service listings, seasonal content, Q&A strategies, and review generation tactics. Each recommendation is practical and builds upon previous suggestions to form a cohesive local SEO strategy. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a numbered list where every tactic includes a clear title and 2-4 implementation details tailored to your specific business context. It's designed for local business owners, digital marketers, and SEO specialists managing Google My Business profiles who need fresh, actionable ideas to stand out in local search results. ● Covers seven critical GMB dimensions including categories, attributes, service listings, content strategy, Q&A, reviews, and local signals ● Creates a dependency-structured list where each idea builds upon or relates to previous tactics for cohesive strategy development ● Tailors every recommendation to your specific business context using four key variables: business type, location, unique selling proposition, and competitive landscape ● Delivers actionable implementation details and examples, not generic advice, so you can execute immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in local SEO and Google My Business optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of creative and unique Google My Business profile enhancements for {{business-type}}. Structure each idea so it builds upon or relates to previous suggestions, creating a cohesive optimization strategy.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}
Location: {{location}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Target audience and competitive landscape: {{market-context}}

Consider multiple dimensions:
- Business categories and subcategories
- Attributes and special features
- Service and product listings
- Posts, photos, and seasonal content
- Q&A optimization
- Review generation strategies
- Local search signals

Each recommendation should be practical, actionable, and designed to enhance visibility and attract the target audience.

## Output
Present as a numbered list. Each main idea should include:
- A clear title describing the optimization tactic
- 2-4 bullet points with specific implementation details or examples tailored to the business context
```
