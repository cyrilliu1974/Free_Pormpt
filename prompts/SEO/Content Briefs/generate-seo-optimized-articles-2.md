# SEO-Optimized Guest Article Writer

## 簡介

The SEO-Optimized Guest Article Writer is a free AI prompt that generates well-researched guest articles designed to drive traffic and build backlinks for content marketers and copywriters. This guest article prompt for ChatGPT takes your SEO keyword, writing style, and target audience to produce structured articles with proper citations and natural keyword integration. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering content in a complete format: attention-grabbing introduction, 3-4 substantive body paragraphs with supporting evidence, a conclusion with call-to-action, and a sources list. Real use cases include pitching guest posts to industry blogs, creating link-building content campaigns, and producing publication-ready articles that balance reader engagement with search visibility. Reach for this prompt when you need external publication content that avoids keyword stuffing while maintaining SEO effectiveness and editorial quality. ● Incorporates SEO keywords naturally throughout the article without stuffing or awkward phrasing ● Adapts tone and structure to match your specified writing style and audience expectations ● Includes proper source citations and references to support claims and build credibility ● Delivers complete article structure from hook to call-to-action, ready for submission to external publishers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter creating guest articles for external publication that drive traffic and build backlinks.

## Task
Write a well-researched, engaging article that:
- Incorporates {{seo-keyword}} naturally throughout (no keyword stuffing)
- Matches {{writing-style}} in tone and structure
- Appeals directly to {{target-audience}}
- Uses clear, grammatically sound sentences
- Includes relevant sources with proper citations
- Encourages readers to engage and visit linked sites

## Output
Deliver the article in this structure:

**[Article Title]**

**Introduction**
[Hook the reader and introduce the topic]

**Body**
[3-4 substantive paragraphs with supporting evidence and examples]

**Conclusion**
[Summarize key points and include a call to action]

**Sources**
[List all cited references]
```
