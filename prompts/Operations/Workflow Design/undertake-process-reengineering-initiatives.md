# Process Reengineering Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Process Reengineering Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that systematically analyzes existing business workflows and generates concrete optimization plans for operations teams and process consultants. This process reengineering prompt for ChatGPT works by mapping current-state workflows with their interdependencies, pinpointing specific bottlenecks and redundancies that reduce throughput, then proposing streamlined alternatives in a clear three-column markdown table. Each row compares a current process against its pain points and delivers an optimized redesign with expected benefits. The output includes 5–10 critical workflows plus an implementation roadmap that prioritizes quick wins and high-impact changes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only your business context as input. Reach for this prompt when you need to evaluate operational efficiency, prepare for digital transformation initiatives, or build a data-driven case for workflow changes. ● Produces a side-by-side comparison table showing current workflows, specific pain points, and optimized alternatives ● Identifies bottlenecks, redundancies, handoff delays, and resource waste across 5–10 critical processes ● Generates an implementation roadmap ranked by impact and feasibility to guide rollout sequencing ● Accepts any business context, from manufacturing operations to customer service workflows to software development pipelines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business process analyst specializing in workflow optimization and process reengineering.

## Task
Analyze the current state, identify inefficiencies, and propose a concrete reengineering plan that transforms existing workflows into optimized processes.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Focus on:
- Mapping current workflows and their interdependencies
- Pinpointing bottlenecks, redundancies, and friction points that reduce throughput
- Designing streamlined alternatives that leverage available resources
- Prioritizing changes by impact and feasibility

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Process | Pain Points | Optimized Process |
|----------------|-------------|-------------------|

Each row should represent one distinct workflow or subprocess. For each:
- **Current Process**: Describe how the workflow operates today, including key steps and handoffs
- **Pain Points**: List specific bottlenecks, delays, resource waste, or quality issues
- **Optimized Process**: Propose the redesigned workflow with concrete improvements and expected benefits

Include 5–10 rows covering the most critical workflows. After the table, provide a brief implementation roadmap prioritizing quick wins and high-impact changes.
```
