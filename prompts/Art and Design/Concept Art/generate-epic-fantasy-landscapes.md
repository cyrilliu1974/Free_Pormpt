# Epic Fantasy Landscape Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Epic Fantasy Landscape Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates wide establishing shots of ancient stone temples carved into cliffsides for concept artists, game developers, and fantasy worldbuilders. This fantasy landscape prompt for Midjourney produces detailed, atmospheric concept art blending realism with epic fantasy aesthetics. The prompt uses chaos 33 for natural variation, a cinematic 16:9 aspect ratio, and stylize 400 to achieve painterly detail suited for game environments, book covers, and worldbuilding portfolios. It renders ancient architecture integrated into dramatic natural settings with the scale and mood of fantasy epics.
