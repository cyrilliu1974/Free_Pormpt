# Promotional Email Copywriting Prompt for Marketing

## 簡介

The Promotional Email Copywriting Prompt for Marketing is a free AI prompt that generates complete, conversion-optimized promotional emails for marketers and business owners running email campaigns. This promotional email prompt for ChatGPT walks text models through crafting attention-grabbing subject lines (under 50 characters), persuasive body copy (up to 300 words), benefit-driven bullet points, social proof integration, urgency-building offers, and compliance-ready footers with unsubscribe links. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your product details, unique selling proposition, target audience profile, and social proof elements to produce ready-to-send campaign copy. Marketers use it for product launches, seasonal promotions, lead nurturing sequences, and re-engagement campaigns that need to convert quickly. Reach for this prompt when you need professional email copy fast but want control over tone, audience fit, and compliance standards. ● Outputs subject lines designed to maximize open rates through curiosity and benefit-driven hooks ● Structures body copy with conversational tone, bullet-point benefits, testimonials, and urgency triggers ● Includes legally compliant unsubscribe options and contact information in every draft ● Adapts messaging to your specific product USP, audience demographics, and available social proof ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email copywriter specializing in promotional campaigns that drive engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write a promotional email that captures attention, communicates value, and motivates readers to take action. Use persuasive, conversational language that resonates with the audience while adhering to email marketing best practices and legal compliance requirements.

## Context
Product and audience details:
{{product-and-usp}}

Target audience:
{{target-audience}}

Supporting elements:
{{social-proof-and-offer}}

## Output
Deliver a complete promotional email including:

**Subject line** (max 50 characters)
- Use a compelling question or bold statement incorporating the product's main benefit
- Spark curiosity and resonate with audience interests or challenges

**Email body** (max 300 words)
1. Friendly, conversational greeting
2. Product introduction highlighting the USP
3. Key benefits in bullet points showing how the product improves life or solves problems
4. Brief testimonials or social proof to build credibility
5. Special offer with urgency (if applicable)
6. Clear, prominent CTA ("Shop Now," "Learn More," "Claim Your Offer")
7. Friendly sign-off expressing appreciation
8. Unsubscribe option and company contact information for compliance

Keep the tone engaging and the content concise to respect reader attention.
```
