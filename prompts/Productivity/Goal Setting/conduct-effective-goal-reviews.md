# Goal Review and Adjustment Session Framework

## 簡介

The Goal Review and Adjustment Session Framework is a free AI prompt that produces a detailed, step-by-step process for conducting productive goal review meetings for individuals and teams. This goal review prompt for ChatGPT takes your specific context and builds a six-phase methodology covering preparation, progress assessment, root-cause analysis, goal adjustment, action planning, and follow-up scheduling. Each phase includes 2-3 concrete techniques drawn from productivity research, making it practical for managers running quarterly reviews, coaches guiding clients, or individuals tracking personal objectives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to organizational, team, or individual contexts. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable structure for goal review sessions that goes beyond surface-level check-ins and drives real course correction. ● Delivers preparation checklists that ensure review sessions start with the right data and mindset ● Provides assessment and analysis frameworks that surface root causes, not just symptoms ● Includes adjustment criteria and action-planning templates that turn insights into commitments ● Builds in accountability mechanisms and scheduling practices that maintain momentum between sessions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in goal setting, tracking, and optimization with extensive knowledge of individual and organizational productivity strategies.

## Task
Create a detailed, step-by-step process for conducting effective goal review and adjustment sessions that incorporates proven techniques and leads to meaningful progress and adaptability.

## Context
{{goal-review-context}}

## Output
Provide a clear, actionable six-phase process with 2-3 specific action steps or techniques for each phase:

1. **Prepare for the review session**
   - [Preparation steps and techniques]

2. **Assess progress and identify obstacles**
   - [Assessment steps and techniques]

3. **Analyze root causes and explore solutions**
   - [Analysis steps and techniques]

4. **Adjust goals and strategies**
   - [Adjustment steps and techniques]

5. **Create an action plan and accountability measures**
   - [Action plan steps and techniques]

6. **Schedule the next review session**
   - [Scheduling steps and techniques]

Present the information without unnecessary jargon. Provide concrete examples or templates where appropriate to support implementation.
```
