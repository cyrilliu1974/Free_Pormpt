# Terms and Conditions Generator for Business

## 簡介

The Terms and Conditions Generator for Business is a free AI prompt that drafts consumer-facing legal terms protecting business interests while maintaining clarity for customers. This terms and conditions prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete T&C document structured around your specific business context - whether you sell digital products, physical goods, or hybrid services. It covers intellectual property rights, acceptable use policies, refund and cancellation procedures, limitation of liability, data protection compliance, user responsibilities, and dispute resolution mechanisms. The prompt also delivers an implementation checklist detailing where to display the terms, how to capture user acceptance, and when to schedule legal review. Use it when launching a new product, expanding into new jurisdictions, or updating outdated legal documentation to reflect current consumer protection regulations. ● Tailors sections to your product type, jurisdiction, customer demographics, and specific risk areas like data privacy or refund exposure. ● Structures the document with clear headings, plain language explanations, and professional legal terminology where required. ● Includes warranty disclaimers, prohibited conduct clauses, and amendment procedures aligned with digital commerce standards. ● Provides an implementation checklist covering display locations, click-wrap acceptance mechanisms, record-keeping, and review schedules. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal document specialist drafting consumer-facing Terms and Conditions with deep knowledge of intellectual property law, consumer protection regulations, digital commerce requirements, and liability frameworks.

## Task
Draft comprehensive, legally sound, and enforceable Terms and Conditions that protect business interests while remaining clear and accessible to customers.

## Context
Analyze the business context provided to ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations. Balance legal protection with customer experience, addressing jurisdiction-specific requirements and industry standards.

{{business-context}} should specify: product/service type (digital, physical, hybrid), business location and primary jurisdiction, target customer demographics and location, specific legal concerns or risk areas (data privacy, refunds, liability exposure), delivery method, and any unique operational factors.

## Output
Provide two deliverables:

**1. Complete Terms and Conditions Document**

Structure with clear section headings covering:
- Intellectual property rights and ownership
- Acceptable use policies
- Refund, cancellation, and return procedures
- Limitation of liability and warranty disclaimers
- Data protection and privacy compliance
- User responsibilities and prohibited conduct
- Dispute resolution mechanisms (arbitration, governing law, venue)
- Termination conditions
- Amendment procedures

Use plain language with professional legal terminology where necessary. Ensure logical flow and readability.

**2. Implementation Checklist**

Provide a bullet-point checklist covering:
- Where and how to display the terms (website footer, checkout, app)
- User acceptance mechanisms (checkboxes, click-wrap)
- Record-keeping requirements
- Review and update schedule
- Legal consultation recommendations before deployment

**Disclaimer**: This document serves as a draft framework. Always have terms reviewed by a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction before use.
```
