# Personalized Sales Message Generator for Cold Outreach

## 簡介

The Personalized Sales Message Generator for Cold Outreach is a free AI prompt that builds targeted sales communications structured around customer pain points, value propositions, and clear calls-to-action for marketing and sales professionals. This cold outreach prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your target customer profile, product details, unique selling points, brand voice, and desired outcome, then generating a message broken into five labeled components: opening hook, problem acknowledgment, solution presentation, value proposition, and call-to-action. Each element flows logically to guide prospects from awareness to engagement. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable across text-generation models. Sales teams use it to replace generic templates with messages that directly address customer motivations and needs. Reach for this prompt when you need to personalize outreach at scale while maintaining consistency in voice and structure across different customer segments or product lines. ● Structures messages into five labeled components that guide the customer journey from problem to action ● Ensures logical flow by building each persuasive element on the previous one ● Adapts to different brand voices, customer segments, and product categories through five customizable variables ● Delivers output as a reusable template format that sales teams can refine and deploy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing communications specialist creating personalized sales messages that resonate with specific customer segments.

## Task
Write a persuasive outreach message structured in clear components: an opening that addresses the customer's pain points, a solution presentation highlighting the unique value proposition, and a compelling call-to-action.

## Context
Target customers: {{target-customers}}
Product/service: {{product-service}}
Unique selling points: {{usp}}
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}
Desired outcome: {{desired-outcome}}

Analyze the customer profile to identify key motivations and needs. Structure your message to flow logically from problem awareness to solution to action, ensuring each element builds persuasively on the previous one.

## Output
Provide the message as a labeled bullet point list:

• **Opening**: [Hook that resonates with customer needs]
• **Problem/Pain Point**: [Acknowledgment of their challenge]
• **Solution**: [How the product/service addresses it]
• **Value Proposition**: [Unique benefits and differentiation]
• **Call-to-Action**: [Clear next step]
```
