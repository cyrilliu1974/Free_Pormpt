# Keyword Analysis for SEO Optimization

## 簡介

The Keyword Analysis for SEO Optimization is a free AI prompt that performs targeted keyword research and delivers actionable SEO recommendations for any website. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a given website URL and returns a structured table of 8-12 high-opportunity keywords, each scored by search volume, competition level, and relevance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, acting as an SEO specialist who identifies content gaps, prioritizes keywords with favorable search volume-to-competition ratios, and provides 3-5 concrete on-page optimization actions plus a phased implementation roadmap. Marketing teams use it to discover untapped keyword opportunities, content strategists rely on it to guide editorial calendars, and solo site owners turn to it for data-driven SEO direction without expensive tooling. ● Produces a sortable keyword table with monthly search estimates, competition tiers (Low/Medium/High), 1-5 relevance scores, and opportunity flags. ● Filters out low-volume or overly competitive terms, focusing your effort on keywords where you can realistically rank. ● Delivers specific content optimization actions - title tag tweaks, internal linking strategies, and technical fixes - not generic advice. ● Includes a prioritized Next Steps checklist so you know exactly what to implement first, what to schedule, and what to monitor over time. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist with deep expertise in keyword research, on-page optimization, and content strategy.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive keyword analysis for {{website-url}} and provide actionable optimization recommendations.

## Analysis Criteria
- Identify high-opportunity keywords based on search volume, competition levels, and relevance to the site's content
- Prioritize keywords with high potential and low competition
- Assess relevance on a 1-5 scale (1 = least relevant, 5 = most relevant)
- Avoid keywords with low search volume or excessively high competition

## Output
Provide your analysis in the following structure:

**Keyword Analysis Table:**

| Keyword | Search Volume | Competition | Relevance | Opportunity |
|---------|---------------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| [keyword] | [monthly est.] | Low/Medium/High | 1-5 | ✅ or ❌ |

(Include 8-12 keywords)

**Content Optimization Recommendations:**
1. [Specific on-page optimization action]
2. [Content gap or enhancement opportunity]
3. [Technical SEO consideration]

(Include 3-5 recommendations)

**Next Steps:**
1. [Immediate priority action]
2. [Short-term implementation task]
3. [Ongoing monitoring or expansion]

(Include 3-4 steps)
```
