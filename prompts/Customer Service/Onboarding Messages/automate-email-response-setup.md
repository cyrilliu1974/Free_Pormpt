# Email Auto-Response Setup Plan for Support Tickets

## 簡介

The Email Auto-Response Setup Plan for Support Tickets is a free AI prompt that builds a customized automated email support system for solopreneurs and small businesses. This email auto-response prompt for ChatGPT takes your business overview and common support issues as input, then delivers a structured implementation plan covering platform selection, step-by-step setup instructions, pre-written response templates for each issue type, escalation criteria, personalization strategies, follow-up sequences, and performance monitoring metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing actionable guidance that balances automation efficiency with personalized customer care. Use it when you need to systematize support ticket handling without sacrificing quality or spending hours on manual replies. ● Recommends the best email platform based on your business model and support volume ● Generates custom response templates for each of your most common support issues ● Defines clear escalation criteria so complex issues reach human attention ● Provides follow-up sequences and performance metrics to maintain service quality over time ## Prompt
