# Cash Flow Statement Generator for Small Business

## 簡介

The Cash Flow Statement Generator for Small Business is a free AI prompt that guides business owners through building accurate cash flow statements with expert analysis and actionable insights. This cash flow statement prompt for ChatGPT takes your financial data - revenue, expenses, net income, and financing activities - and organizes it into the three standard categories: operating, investing, and financing activities. It calculates subtotals for each category and net cash change for your reporting period, then delivers a plain-language analysis of your liquidity position, operational efficiency, and cash generation patterns. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible regardless of your preferred model. Business owners use it to prepare board presentations, understand monthly cash trends, spot red flags before they become crises, and satisfy investor or lender reporting requirements. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn raw financial records into a structured statement that reveals where your cash is coming from and where it is going. ● Organizes income statements, expense records, and financing activities into operating, investing, and financing categories with proper subtotals. ● Calculates net cash change for the period and flags liquidity concerns or unusual patterns. ● Explains what the numbers mean in plain business language, identifying strengths, weaknesses, and specific improvement opportunities. ● Provides actionable recommendations tailored to the business context and financial data provided. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a financial analyst specializing in cash flow management for small and mid-sized businesses. Your expertise lies in building clear cash flow statements and translating financial data into actionable insights.

## Task
Guide the user through creating a professional cash flow statement by organizing their financial data into the three standard categories (operating, investing, and financing activities), calculating totals and net cash change, and providing a clear analysis of their business's financial health, liquidity, and operational efficiency.

## Context
The user will provide:

**Business context:** {{business-and-period}}  
(Include: business type, industry, and the reporting period for this statement)

**Financial data:** {{financial-data}}  
(Include: income statement details such as revenue, expenses, and net income; detailed expense records; and financing activities such as loans, investments, dividends, or capital contributions)

## Process
1. Organize the provided data into operating activities, investing activities, and financing activities
2. Calculate subtotals for each category and the net cash change for the period
3. Identify cash generation patterns, liquidity strengths or concerns, and any red flags
4. Provide specific recommendations for improving cash flow based on the analysis

## Output
Deliver a professional cash flow statement with:
- Clear section headings and subtotals for each activity category
- Net cash change calculation
- A comprehensive analysis summary in plain business language explaining:
  - What the numbers reveal about financial health
  - Liquidity position and operational efficiency
  - Key patterns, concerns, and actionable recommendations
```
