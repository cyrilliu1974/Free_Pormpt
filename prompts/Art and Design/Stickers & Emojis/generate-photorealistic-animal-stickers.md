# Photorealistic Animal Sticker Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Animal Sticker Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms real-photograph animal imagery into meme-ready sticker cutouts with authentic fur textures and bold white borders. This photorealistic animal sticker prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 1024×1024 sticker-style images featuring any animal species with customizable expressions and poses. The output mimics professional die-cut stickers - natural lighting, realistic proportions, smooth edges, and a thick white outline centered on a solid black background. Content creators use it to build emoji packs for messaging apps, social media reaction graphics, and print-on-demand merchandise without photography sessions or manual editing. Reach for this prompt when you need sticker assets that blend internet culture aesthetics with photographic realism, whether for a marketing campaign, Discord server, or digital product line. ● Produces sticker-ready cutouts with natural fur and skin detail, realistic lighting, and professional die-cut edges. ● Accepts any animal subject and supports custom emotional expressions and body-language poses. ● Outputs 1024×1024 images with thick white borders on black backgrounds, optimized for messaging and social platforms. ● Includes negative-prompt guidance to prevent cartoon styling, blurriness, and messy background artifacts. ## Prompt

```
Create a photorealistic sticker-style cutout of a {{animal-subject}}. The animal should look like a real photograph cut into a sticker, with natural fur/skin texture, realistic lighting, and authentic proportions.

Apply {{expression-and-pose}} to clearly convey the intended emotion and body language.

Style the cutout with a thick white outline, smooth die-cut edges, and no background remnants. The result should feel like a meme or emoji sticker pack element—clean, bold, and expressive.

Place the finished sticker on a solid black background. Center the animal, showing full body or head as appropriate to the pose.

**Negative prompt:**
illustration, cartoon, 3D render, painted style, anime, low detail fur, messy cutout edges, background artifacts, blurry image

**Technical specs:** 1024×1024, natural realistic lighting consistent with real photography.
```
