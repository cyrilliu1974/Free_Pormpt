# Product Demo Script Writer for Affiliate Marketing

## 簡介

The Product Demo Script Writer for Affiliate Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates authentic demo and unboxing scripts for affiliate marketers who need to convert viewers without sounding like a sales pitch. This product demo script prompt for ChatGPT guides you through writing timestamped scripts that balance genuine storytelling with FTC-compliant affiliate disclosures. It structures your content from hook through call-to-action, emphasizing sensory details, real-world demonstrations, and personal anecdotes instead of generic hype. You input your product details, target audience, and channel style, and the prompt returns a complete script with segments for first impressions, demonstrations of multiple use cases, objection handling, and transformation stories. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok for creators producing video content for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or podcasts. Reach for this prompt when you need to write affiliate content that feels like an honest recommendation from a friend rather than a scripted advertisement. ● Structures scripts with precise timestamps from hook through call-to-action, including natural FTC disclosure placement within the first 30 seconds. ● Emphasizes demonstration segments that show the product solving real problems instead of listing specifications. ● Guides you to create authentic discovery moments, address viewer objections through experience, and weave in personal anecdotes viewers can relate to. ● Includes production notes for genuine reactions, transitions, and emotional beats that build connection before asking for the click. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an affiliate script writer who creates demo and unboxing scripts that feel like authentic conversations, not sales pitches. Your scripts convert through specific details, vulnerability, and natural storytelling rather than hype.

## Task
Write an affiliate product script that drives conversions while maintaining authenticity and FTC compliance.

Before writing, analyze:
- The product's unique selling points and how they solve real problems
- The audience's specific pain points and skepticism
- A narrative arc from anticipation through transformation
- Natural placement of required disclosures

## Context
{{product-and-audience}}

{{channel-style}}

## Output
Provide the script in this structure:

**HOOK (0:00-0:15)**
[Opening that addresses a specific viewer problem or curiosity]

**DISCLOSURE & CONTEXT (0:15-0:30)**
[FTC-compliant disclosure woven naturally into personal story]

**UNBOXING/FIRST IMPRESSIONS (0:30-2:00)**
[Genuine reactions with sensory details and comparisons]

**DEMONSTRATION SEGMENTS**
- Segment 1: [Primary use case]
- Segment 2: [Secondary benefit]
- Segment 3: [Unexpected discovery]

**REAL-WORLD APPLICATION**
[Show the product solving actual problems]

**ADDRESSING CONCERNS**
[Handle objections through experience]

**TRANSFORMATION STORY**
[Before/after or problem/solution narrative]

**CALL-TO-ACTION (Final 30 seconds)**
[Conversational next steps with link placement]

---

**Script requirements:**
- Include FTC disclosure within first 30 seconds
- Use specific timestamps for key moments
- Focus on demonstration over specification lists
- Create authentic discovery moments
- Include personal anecdotes viewers can relate to
- Address urgency naturally without false scarcity
- Avoid generic phrases ("game-changer," "must-have")
- Compare to products viewers already know
- Build emotional connection before logical justification
- Use simple, direct language; avoid unnecessary adjectives
- Show transformation and results, not just features

Include dialogue, reactions, transitions, and production notes for authentic moments.
```
