# Virtual Class Structure Builder for Online Courses

## 簡介

The Virtual Class Structure Builder for Online Courses is a free AI prompt that creates detailed course blueprints for educators and instructional designers developing online learning experiences. This virtual class structure prompt for ChatGPT takes your subject, student demographic, platform, duration, and learning goals and outputs a complete course outline including module breakdowns, multimedia content strategies, interactive elements, assessment plans, and engagement tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured course frameworks with weekly or thematic modules, estimated time allocations, and platform-specific activity recommendations. Educators use it to design semester-long university courses, corporate training programs, professional certification sequences, and self-paced learning pathways that align content delivery with pedagogical best practices. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate subject matter expertise into a coherent online learning experience that balances video lectures, readings, discussions, projects, and assessments across multiple weeks or months. ● Outputs course overviews with prerequisites, target outcomes, and alignment to specified learning goals ● Structures module breakdowns with specific objectives and time estimates for each unit ● Recommends multimedia content types (video lectures, infographics, simulations) matched to subject and audience ● Designs interactive activities (quizzes, discussion prompts, peer reviews, live sessions) that leverage your platform's capabilities ● Includes formative and summative assessment strategies that measure progress toward defined outcomes ● Provides engagement tactics to sustain motivation and participation throughout the course duration ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert online education specialist designing comprehensive virtual course structures.

## Task
Create a detailed course blueprint that includes clear learning objectives, engaging multimedia elements, and interactive activities. The structure should be tailored to the target demographic and platform capabilities, ensuring effective learning and high student engagement.

## Context
Subject: {{subject}}
Student demographic: {{student-demographic}}
Platform: {{platform}}
Course duration: {{duration}}
Learning goals: {{learning-goals}}

## Output
Provide a structured course outline with:
- **Course Overview** – title, description, prerequisites, and target outcomes aligned with the learning goals
- **Module Breakdown** – weekly or thematic modules with specific objectives and estimated time
- **Content Strategy** – types of multimedia (video lectures, readings, infographics, simulations) appropriate for the subject and demographic
- **Interactive Elements** – activities like quizzes, discussion prompts, peer reviews, projects, or live sessions that leverage platform features
- **Assessment Plan** – formative and summative assessments that measure progress toward learning goals
- **Engagement Tactics** – specific strategies to maintain motivation and participation throughout the duration

Format as a structured outline with main sections and subsections using bullet points for detailed steps.
```
