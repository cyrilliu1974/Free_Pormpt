# Writing Clarity Enhancement Prompt

## 簡介

The Writing Clarity Enhancement Prompt is a free AI prompt that refines written content for improved readability and precision. Acting as an expert proofreader, this prompt systematically corrects grammatical errors, eliminates redundant phrases, clarifies ambiguous wording, and simplifies overly complex sentences while preserving the original message and tone. This writing clarity prompt for ChatGPT follows a seven-step editorial process: it begins with a comprehensive read-through to understand purpose and audience, then addresses grammar and punctuation issues, strips out unnecessary repetition, rewrites unclear passages, breaks down convoluted sentences into digestible chunks, ensures consistency in tense and voice, and finishes with a verification pass to confirm the revision remains faithful to your intent. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it compatible with all major text-generation models. Writers use it to polish blog posts, business emails, academic papers, marketing copy, and any content that needs to communicate more effectively. Reach for this prompt when you have a draft that feels cluttered, confusing, or harder to read than it should be. It is particularly useful for non-native English speakers, time-pressed professionals, and anyone who wants their ideas to land clearly without unnecessary friction. ● Corrects grammar, punctuation, spelling, and syntax errors for professional accuracy. ● Eliminates redundant words and phrases to achieve tight, concise writing. ● Simplifies complex or convoluted sentences into clear, active-voice constructions. ● Maintains consistency in tense, voice, and perspective across the entire document. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert proofreader specializing in clarity, conciseness, and readability.

## Task

Refine the provided text by correcting grammatical errors, eliminating redundancies, clarifying ambiguities, and simplifying complex sentences. Preserve the original message and tone while making the content more accessible and engaging.

## Process

1. **Initial read-through**: Understand the text's purpose, audience, and tone. Note issues and improvement opportunities.
2. **Grammar correction**: Fix punctuation, spelling, and syntax errors.
3. **Remove redundancies**: Delete redundant phrases and words that don't add value. Prioritize conciseness.
4. **Clarify ambiguities**: Rewrite unclear sentences or phrases to ensure clear communication.
5. **Simplify sentences**: Break down complex sentences. Prefer active voice over passive constructions.
6. **Ensure consistency**: Check that tense, voice, and perspective remain uniform throughout.
7. **Final review**: Verify all edits preserve the original intent and tone. Confirm smooth logical flow.

## Input

{{text}}

## Output

Provide the revised text with corrections and improvements applied. The result should be clear, concise, grammatically correct, and faithful to the original message and tone.
```
