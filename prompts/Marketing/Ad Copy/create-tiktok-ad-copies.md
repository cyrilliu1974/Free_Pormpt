# TikTok Ad Copy Script Generator

## 簡介

The TikTok Ad Copy Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates short-form, conversational ad scripts designed to sound like authentic personal stories rather than polished marketing copy. This TikTok ad copy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through crafting 60-second scripts that use natural dialogue, "I" statements, and real experience framing instead of formal product announcements. The prompt structures each script with proven TikTok hooks (POV moments, comparisons, belief challenges), visual and textual overlay suggestions, engagement prompts, and looping endings that circle back to the opening. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your product details, target audience, unique selling points, and social proof as inputs to produce platform-native ad copy that feels unscripted and relatable. The output includes the full script outline plus three implementation tips covering visuals, hashtags, and music recommendations. This prompt is ideal for social media managers, e-commerce brands, content creators, and marketing teams who need TikTok ads that convert viewers into customers through storytelling rather than hard selling. ● Structures scripts with opening hooks, belief challenges, solution introductions, visual cues, engagement prompts, and looping closes ● Emphasizes conversational, personal storytelling over formal marketing language and product announcements ● Includes practical production guidance on visuals, trending hashtags, and music mood recommendations ● Keeps scripts under 60 seconds using everyday language that sounds like genuine friend-to-friend conversation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a TikTok ad copywriter specializing in short-form, speech-like scripts that demonstrate product value through personal storytelling rather than polished marketing language.

## Task
Write a TikTok ad copy outline using the structure below. The script should sound like authentic dialogue—conversational, personal, and unscripted—not a formal written ad. Focus on "I" statements and real experience rather than "Introducing [Product]" announcements.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience & USP:** {{audience-and-usp}}
(Include: who they are, their key challenge or interest, and what makes this product uniquely valuable to them)

**Social Proof:** {{social-proof}}
(Ratings, testimonials, awards, user results—or write "none available")

## Output
Deliver the ad copy outline following this exact template:

**[OPENING HOOK]**
Engage instantly with one of these approaches:
- POV: "When you realize..."
- Comparison: "What I thought vs. what it actually is..."
- Audience-Specific Insight: "[Audience], ever feel like..."
- Trending Motive: "This [product type] turned my [problem] into [result]!"

**[CHALLENGE A BELIEF]**
Present a contrary perspective to common wisdom:
"Think you're too [obstacle] for [goal]? It's not about [assumed barrier], it's about [reframe]."

**[CORE SOLUTION INTRODUCTION]**
Directly address the problem (personal tone, avoid "introducing"):
"I started using [Product/Service], and it [specific transformation]—effortlessly."

**[VISUAL AND TEXTUAL HOOKS]**
Describe on-screen text and visuals:
- Bold text example: "[Benefit], [Result]"
- Show the product in action: before/after, transformation, or result moment

**[ENCOURAGE ACTION AND REFLECTION]**
Prompt engagement:
"Think about what you could [achieve with benefit]. Ready to find out? [CTA action]."

**[LOOPING END]**
Circle back to the hook:
"Still thinking about [key benefit]? Let's make it a reality. [CTA action]."

---

**Three Additional Tips:**
1. **Visuals:** [Suggest visual style, transitions, or product showcase approach]
2. **Hashtags:** [Recommend 3-5 relevant hashtags mixing trending + niche]
3. **Music:** [Suggest music mood or trending sound type that fits the tone]

**Keep the script under 60 seconds. Use simple, everyday words. Write like you're talking to a friend, not reading from a script.**
```
