# Lifestyle Beverage Ad Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Lifestyle Beverage Ad Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates polished 3×2 panel beverage advertisements in portrait format for brands, marketers, and product photographers. It produces six coordinated lifestyle beverage ad panels - hero bottle shot, macro condensation detail, active lifestyle moment, dynamic water splash, minimal counter display, and signature clean finish - rendered in professional product photography style with soft daylight and modern minimalist surfaces. This lifestyle beverage ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana works by specifying product name, beverage type, and color palette to generate crisp studio shots paired with real-world lifestyle contexts, ideal for social media campaigns, e-commerce landing pages, and pitch decks. Reach for it when you need a complete ad grid that balances technical product detail with aspirational lifestyle imagery in a single cohesive 4:5 portrait layout. ● Six distinct panel compositions in one grid: hero bottle, macro droplet texture, outdoor lifestyle hold, frozen splash capture, kitchen counter scene, and backlit signature shot. ● Built-in styling for condensation effects, eco-friendly packaging cues, soft blue gradients, light concrete textures, and natural daylight mood. ● Includes negative guidance to avoid crushed packaging, harsh flash, cartoon rendering, and overexposed highlights. ● Customizable with product name, beverage type (sparkling water, juice, functional drink), and brand color palette. ## Prompt

```

**Grid Panels (3×2 equal portrait panels):**

1. **Hero Bottle Shot** – Bottle centered upright with visible condensation droplets. Front-facing symmetrical composition. Pure, refreshing mood.

4. **Water Splash Scene** – Bottle surrounded by dynamic frozen water splash. High-speed capture lighting. Dynamic, crisp mood.

5. **Minimal Counter Display** – Bottle placed in clean kitchen environment. Modern, daily essential mood.

6. **Signature Clean Finish** – Bottle silhouetted against soft blue gradient background with soft rim light. Iconic, simple mood.

**Visual Style:** Professional product and lifestyle photography with high realism and fresh daylight aesthetic. Studio hero shots, macro droplet details, lifestyle outdoor scenes, and high-speed splash captures. Clean modern sans-serif typography. {{color-palette}}. Clean, reliable, modern brand personality.

Crisp balanced exposure with soft clean shadows. No cloudy water, dirty bottles, crushed packaging, harsh flash lighting, overexposed highlights, cartoon style, low-resolution textures, watermarks, or text overlays.
```
