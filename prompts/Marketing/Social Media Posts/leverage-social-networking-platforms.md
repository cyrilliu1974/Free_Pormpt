# Social Media Strategy Builder for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Builder for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates tailored social media marketing plans across LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook for businesses and brands. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete, actionable plan that includes a content calendar balancing informative posts, industry news, and promotional material; platform-specific guidance on post frequency, timing, and format; engagement tactics like hashtag strategies and discussion prompts; and a measurement framework with key metrics. It works by taking your industry, target audience, unique selling proposition, and current social media resources, then producing organized recommendations for each platform and content type. Marketers use it to map out quarterly campaigns, agencies rely on it for client onboarding, and founders turn to it when launching new social channels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Generates platform-specific content calendars with recommended posting frequencies and optimal timing for LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook ● Provides engagement tactics tailored to each platform, including hashtag research, discussion prompts, and comment response protocols ● Defines measurable success metrics and a refinement process for continuous improvement ● Organizes output by platform and content type for easy implementation and team delegation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist developing platform-specific strategies to build brand awareness and drive audience engagement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social media strategy for {{industry}}, including:

- A balanced content calendar mixing informative posts, industry news, and promotional content
- Platform-specific optimization guidance for LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook (post frequency, timing, format, and best practices)
- Engagement tactics: relevant hashtag strategies, discussion prompts, comment response protocols
- Performance measurement framework with key metrics and refinement process

## Context
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Brand Positioning:** {{usp}}

**Current State:** {{current-presence-and-resources}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a bullet point list organized by:
1. Platform (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)
2. Content type (informative, industry news, promotional)

For each platform-content combination, specify timing, format, engagement tactics, and success metrics.
```
