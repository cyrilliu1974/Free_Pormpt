# Isometric Fantasy Village Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Isometric Fantasy Village Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates detailed fantasy settlements carved into mountainsides with warm, cozy lighting and miniature-scale perspective. The prompt produces isometric-view illustrations of villages, towns, or other fantasy settlements nestled into rocky terrain, ideal for concept art, worldbuilding references, game asset inspiration, and tabletop RPG visual aids. This isometric fantasy village prompt for Midjourney uses specific parameters including chaos 33 for moderate variation, a 3:4 aspect ratio, and stylize 400 to balance artistic interpretation with structural clarity. It works on Midjourney and similar image models that support parameter flags.
