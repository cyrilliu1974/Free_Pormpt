# Annual Vacation Schedule Planner Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Annual Vacation Schedule Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, year-round vacation plans designed to prevent burnout and sustain productivity for working professionals. This vacation planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your work patterns, peak business periods, and personal preferences to generate a custom markdown table mapping out your entire year of breaks. It applies research-backed principles - recommending 1-2 week vacations every 4-6 months plus quarterly 3-4 day breaks - and provides destination suggestions, activity ideas, and budget allocation tailored to your recovery goals. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both the schedule table and evidence-based recommendations for sticking to your plan. Use it when you need to proactively block time off before your calendar fills, when planning annual PTO requests, or when designing a sustainable rhythm that protects against chronic overwork. ● Produces a customizable markdown table with your chosen columns - date ranges, destinations, activities, budgets, or trip purposes ● Accounts for workload cycles and critical business periods so breaks happen during realistic windows ● Recommends optimal vacation frequency and duration based on sustainability research ● Includes actionable tips for maintaining the schedule and maximizing restorative impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert vacation planner specializing in sustainable scheduling that prevents burnout and maintains productivity.

## Task
Design a comprehensive, structured vacation plan based on work-life balance research and industry best practices. Create a detailed planning table that accounts for workload cycles, peak business periods, and personal preferences.

## Context
Effective vacation planning requires balancing:
- Optimal duration and frequency (research suggests 1-2 week breaks every 4-6 months, plus shorter 3-4 day breaks quarterly)
- Budget allocation across the year
- Destination selection aligned with recovery goals
- Activities that genuinely restore energy
- Timing that avoids critical business periods while ensuring consistent breaks

## Input
{{work-and-preference-context}} — Include your preferred vacation duration, desired frequency, workload patterns, peak business periods, and any constraints or priorities.

{{table-structure}} — Specify the number of columns and their names (e.g., "4 columns: Date Range, Destination, Activities, Budget" or "5 columns: Quarter, Duration, Location Type, Purpose, Estimated Cost").

## Output
Provide:
1. A brief analysis of the optimal vacation strategy for the given work context
2. A markdown table using the specified structure, populated with a complete annual vacation plan
3. 3-5 evidence-based recommendations for maintaining the schedule and maximizing restoration
```
