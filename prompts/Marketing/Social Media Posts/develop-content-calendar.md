# Social Media Content Calendar Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Builder is a free AI prompt that creates strategic, time-optimized content calendars for social media marketers and content creators. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete posting schedule organized by date, content type (posts, stories, videos, carousels), themes, descriptions, and calls-to-action. It structures weekly content across engagement-driving formats: Monday storytelling posts, Wednesday video content, Friday interactive posts, and Sunday reflective content, plus daily story ideas. The prompt incorporates audience analysis, trend research, optimal posting times based on your analytics, and strategic calendar anchors like holidays and industry events. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering output in an actionable table format ready for implementation. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, strategic approach to social media publishing that balances consistency with audience engagement goals. ● Structures content across proven engagement formats: storytelling, video, interactive, and reflective posts with daily story ideas ● Incorporates analytics-driven posting times, trending opportunities, and relevant calendar anchors for maximum reach ● Generates table-format calendars with date, time, content type, theme, description, CTA, and implementation notes ● Tailors every content piece to specific audience demographics and topic relevance with strategic hashtag and engagement tactics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in content calendar development that drives engagement and follower growth.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content calendar spanning {{time-frame}} that includes a strategic mix of posts, stories, and videos tailored to {{audience}} around {{topic}}. Incorporate optimal posting times, key dates, trending opportunities, and clear calls-to-action for each content piece.

## Approach

**1. Audience Analysis and Topic Research**
Begin by analyzing {{audience}} demographics, behaviors, and preferences. Research current trends, conversations, and pain points related to {{topic}} to ensure content resonates and remains relevant.

**2. Weekly Content Structure**
- **Monday** (Storytelling Post): Share a relatable story, case study, or educational piece about {{topic}}—an inspiring success, lesson learned, or interactive element
- **Wednesday** (Video Content): Post a short-form video, tutorial, or demonstration packed with actionable value
- **Friday** (Interactive Post): Create engagement through polls, questions, challenges, or user participation prompts
- **Sunday** (Reflective Content): Share thought-provoking or emotionally resonant content that connects with {{audience}} on a deeper level
- **Daily Stories**: Intersperse quick tips, behind-the-scenes glimpses, user-generated content, and micro-updates

**3. Strategic Timing**
Leverage {{analytics-and-context}} to schedule posts when {{audience}} is most active and likely to engage. Adjust timing by content type based on performance data.

**4. Calendar Anchors**
Plan content around relevant holidays, industry events, awareness days, and seasonal moments. Monitor and capitalize on trending topics, viral challenges, and timely conversations.

**5. Growth Mechanics**
Each content piece should include a clear call-to-action (link clicks, comments, shares, saves). Use targeted hashtags to expand reach beyond existing followers.

## Output
Deliver a table-format content calendar with these columns:
- **Date & Time**: Specific posting schedule
- **Content Type**: Post, story, video, or carousel
- **Theme/Topic**: Specific angle or subject matter
- **Description**: Brief content outline
- **CTA**: Clear action for the audience
- **Notes**: Hashtags, tagged accounts, or special considerations

The calendar should span {{time-frame}} and serve as an actionable roadmap for consistent, strategic social media publishing.
```
