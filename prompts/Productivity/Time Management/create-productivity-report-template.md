# Weekly Productivity Report Template Generator

## 簡介

The Weekly Productivity Report Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured time-tracking templates for managers and teams analyzing work patterns and employee output. This productivity report template prompt for ChatGPT produces a fillable weekly document format that records daily tasks, time allocation by category (meetings, deep work, administrative work), completion status, and productivity blockers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver clean, structured templates with employee details, date ranges, and weekly goals pre-configured. Teams use it to standardize time reporting, surface patterns in how hours are spent, and identify workflow bottlenecks before weekly reviews. Reach for this prompt when you need a consistent, analytics-ready format for weekly productivity tracking across your organization. ● Structures five days of task entries with category tags, time spent, completion status, and blocker fields for granular tracking ● Builds weekly summary sections that calculate total hours, productive hours, top time consumers, and accomplishments ● Includes next-week goal-setting space to close the loop between analysis and action planning ● Outputs clean, bracket-delimited templates with consistent formatting and adequate whitespace for easy completion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity analysis expert specializing in time tracking templates that identify patterns and optimize workflows.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive weekly time report template for {{employee-details}} covering the week of {{date-range}}. The template must be intuitive to fill out while capturing granular data points that surface meaningful productivity patterns and improvement areas.

## Structure
Include these sections:

**Time Period**
Week of {{date-range}}

**Employee Details**
{{employee-details}}

**Weekly Goals**
{{weekly-goals}}

**Daily Tasks (Monday–Friday)**
For each weekday, provide a structured format to record:
- Task description
- Category (e.g., meetings, deep work, administrative, communication)
- Time spent (hours)
- Status (completed, in progress, blocked)
- Blockers encountered

Allow space for 3–5 tasks per day.

**Weekly Summary**
- Total hours worked
- Productive hours
- Top 3 time-consuming tasks
- Key accomplishments
- Productivity blockers faced

**Next Week Goals**
Space for 3 goals to promote continuous improvement.

## Output
Deliver the template as a clean, fillable document structure with clear section headings, adequate whitespace, and consistent formatting. Use brackets [ ] for fields users will complete.
```
