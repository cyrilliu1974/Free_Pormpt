# New Process Training Plan Generator for Teams

## 簡介

The New Process Training Plan Generator for Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured, interactive training programs tailored to any business process and organizational context. This process training prompt for ChatGPT produces a three-phase plan covering preparation logistics, live facilitation activities with real-world simulations, and post-session reinforcement measures. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only the process name and business context as inputs. Training coordinators, HR professionals, and team leads use it to design workshops that improve comprehension and adoption of new workflows, onboarding procedures, software rollouts, compliance protocols, and operational changes. Reach for this prompt when you need a ready-to-execute training blueprint that balances presentation, hands-on practice, group discussion, and assessment without starting from scratch. ● Produces a numbered plan organized into Preparation, Training Session, and Follow-up phases ● Includes hands-on exercises that simulate real-world application of the new process ● Incorporates group discussion formats and feedback loops to reinforce learning ● Provides assessment methods to measure participant comprehension and retention ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert training coordinator designing an interactive process training program.

## Task
Create a comprehensive training plan that optimizes productivity and workflow efficiency through hands-on learning, engaging materials, collaborative discussion, and meaningful assessment.

## Context
Process: {{process-name}}
Business context: {{business-context}}

The training plan should include:
- Hands-on exercises simulating real-world scenarios
- Engaging presentation materials
- Group discussions and feedback sessions
- Assessment methods to evaluate comprehension and retention

## Output
Deliver the training plan as a numbered list organized under these headings:
1. **Preparation** – pre-session planning, materials, and logistics
2. **Training Session** – facilitation activities, exercises, and engagement techniques
3. **Follow-up** – reinforcement, assessment, and continuous improvement measures

Ensure each step is actionable and tailored to the process and business context provided.
```
