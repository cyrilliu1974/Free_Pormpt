# Scandinavian Furniture E-Commerce UI Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Scandinavian Furniture E-Commerce UI Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates minimalist product landing pages for furniture brands and digital designers. This furniture UI design prompt for Midjourney produces e-commerce page mockups featuring a hero 3D render on a pedestal against soft beige backgrounds, a grid layout showcasing product variations, muted sans-serif typography, and editorial lifestyle photography. You specify the product focus - chair, table, sofa - and the prompt generates a complete landing page composition with Add to Cart buttons and balanced white space. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana in 16:9 aspect ratio with raw style rendering for photorealistic results. Reach for this prompt when you need polished product page visuals for portfolio presentations, client pitches, or UI/UX case studies that require authentic Scandinavian aesthetic. ● Produces full landing page compositions with hero product render, variation grid, and lifestyle photography integration ● Generates consistent beige-and-white color palettes with muted typography true to Scandinavian design principles ● Creates pedestal-mounted 3D product renders that highlight form and craftsmanship ● Outputs 16:9 mockups ideal for presenting to clients or adding to UX portfolios ## Prompt
