# Local Business Citation Source Generator

## 簡介

The Local Business Citation Source Generator is a free AI prompt that researches and compiles high-authority citation sources to improve local search visibility for businesses. This local business citation prompt for ChatGPT evaluates and recommends at least 15 diverse citation platforms - from Google Business Profile and Bing Places to industry-specific directories and geo-targeted sites - complete with submission URLs, domain authority considerations, and eligibility notes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured markdown table organized by impact potential. Local SEO specialists, agency teams, and business owners use it to quickly identify where to list their business for maximum local search ranking benefit. Reach for this prompt when you need a strategic citation-building roadmap tailored to a specific business type and geographic market. ● Outputs a markdown table with citation source name, submission URL, and eligibility or verification notes for each platform ● Balances general directories, industry-specific platforms, and regional citation sites based on domain authority and active user base ● Orders sources by tier: high-impact national platforms first, followed by niche and local directories ● Identifies paid versus free listings, category restrictions, and verification methods so you can plan your citation campaign efficiently ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO specialist generating a comprehensive citation source list to improve online visibility and local search rankings.

## Task
Research and compile at least 15 diverse citation sources tailored to the specified business type and location. Evaluate each source for relevance, domain authority, and impact on local search rankings.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}
Location: {{location}}
Business details: {{business-details}}

Local citations from high-authority directories, niche industry platforms, and geo-specific sites signal legitimacy to search engines and help customers discover the business across multiple touchpoints.

## Output
Provide a markdown table with three columns:

| Citation Source | Submission URL | Notes |
|----------------|----------------|-------|

**Requirements:**
- Minimum 15 citation sources
- Mix of general directories (Google Business Profile, Bing Places), industry-specific platforms, and local/regional sites
- Prioritize sources with high domain authority and active user bases
- Include submission URL for each source
- Note any specific requirements (paid/free, verification methods, eligibility criteria, category restrictions)
- Order by impact potential: tier-1 platforms first, then niche and local sources
```
