# Identify Strategic Upsell Opportunities

## 簡介

The Identify Strategic Upsell Opportunities prompt is a free AI prompt that pinpoints six revenue-growing moments when customers are most receptive to additional offers based on positive service interactions. This strategic upsell opportunities prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing the psychological momentum created when customers experience satisfaction, then mapping specific trigger moments to relevant product or service recommendations. You provide your business context, and the prompt returns a structured list of six opportunities, each with the exact satisfaction moment that creates the opening, a description of what to offer and why it fits, and a natural approach for presenting it without feeling pushy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text models. Sales teams, customer success managers, and business owners use it to turn goodwill into growth by identifying both real-time upsells during live interactions and strategic follow-up offers within 24 to 48 hours while sentiment is still fresh. ● Maps each upsell to a specific trigger moment rooted in customer satisfaction rather than generic sales timing. ● Focuses on solving adjacent problems and enhancing the current experience, not just adding line items. ● Distinguishes between immediate opportunities presentable during the interaction and follow-up offers within the 24 to 48 hour window of peak goodwill. ● Provides a recommended approach for each opportunity so your team knows how to frame the offer as helpful rather than transactional. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer success strategist specializing in revenue optimization through natural upsell opportunities aligned with positive customer experiences.

## Task
Identify 6 strategic upsell opportunities that emerge from moments of customer satisfaction. Each should feel like a helpful recommendation, not a sales push.

## Context
Successful upsells occur at peak satisfaction moments. Analyze the psychological momentum created by positive service interactions and identify trigger points where customers are most receptive to additional value.

Focus on opportunities that:
- Enhance the customer's current positive experience
- Solve adjacent problems they may not have articulated
- Capitalize on trust and goodwill generated by excellent service

Consider both immediate opportunities (presentable in real-time during the interaction) and strategic follow-up opportunities (within 24-48 hours while positive sentiment is fresh).

## Input
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 6 upsell opportunities. For each:

1. **Trigger Moment**: The specific positive interaction that creates the opening
2. **Opportunity Description**: What to offer and why it's relevant
3. **Recommended Approach**: How to present it naturally and helpfully
```
