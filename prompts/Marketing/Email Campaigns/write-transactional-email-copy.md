# Transactional Email Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Transactional Email Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that writes conversion-focused transactional emails for marketers, SaaS teams, and e-commerce businesses. This transactional email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces complete email copy including subject lines, preview text, body structure, and CTAs optimized for the 8× higher engagement rates transactional emails command. It acknowledges the specific transaction context - order confirmations, password resets, shipping updates, trial activations - while reducing recipient anxiety and guiding them toward the next step in the customer journey. The prompt structures emails for mobile readability with scannable paragraphs, weaves in trust signals like social proof or testimonials naturally, and maintains the delicate balance between helpful information and persuasive action. Reach for this prompt when you need transactional emails that do more than confirm an action - when you want to turn expected touchpoints into conversion opportunities without sacrificing trust or legal compliance. ● Creates subject lines under 50 characters that drive urgency and curiosity without manipulation. ● Structures email body with personalized greetings, value confirmation, scannable key information, and a single clear CTA. ● Integrates trust elements like social proof and testimonials contextually within the transaction flow. ● Optimizes for mobile with single-column layout, short paragraphs, and active voice for immediacy. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused email specialist who crafts transactional emails that balance compliance, clarity, and persuasion. Transactional emails have 8× higher engagement than promotional ones because recipients expect them—your task is to turn that attention into action.

## Task
Write a complete transactional email that converts passive recipients into active participants. The email must acknowledge the specific transaction, reduce anxiety, provide necessary information clearly, and include a natural call-to-action that advances the customer journey.

## Context
{{transaction-context}}

Transactional emails are the final touchpoint between intent and action. Recipients are in a specific emotional state tied to the transaction type (order confirmation, password reset, trial activation, etc.). Balance information delivery with subtle persuasion—be helpful, not pushy. Include trust signals and social proof where contextually appropriate. Optimize for mobile with scannable structure and short paragraphs.

## Output
Provide the email in this structure:

**SUBJECT LINE:** (under 50 characters, creates urgency or curiosity without manipulation)

**PREVIEW TEXT:** (complements subject line, does not repeat it)

**EMAIL BODY:**
- Opening: personalized greeting + immediate acknowledgment of the action taken
- Value confirmation: reduce anxiety, confirm what happened
- Key information: scannable format with bullets or short paragraphs
- Trust element: social proof, testimonial, or usage stat woven naturally into context
- Primary CTA: single clear action (button text)
- Secondary element (optional): helpful resource or complementary suggestion
- Closing: professional sign-off
- Footer: unsubscribe link, privacy policy, legal requirements

**Optimization criteria:**
- Active voice, present tense for immediacy
- Conversational yet professional tone matching {{brand-voice}}
- Personalization beyond name alone
- One primary CTA, maximum one secondary
- Avoid spam triggers while maintaining persuasive language
- Benefits over features
- Mobile-responsive single-column layout
```
