# SEO SWOT Analysis Generator for Websites

## 簡介

The SEO SWOT Analysis Generator for Websites is a free AI prompt that conducts a comprehensive four-quadrant examination of any website's search engine optimization strategy, delivering actionable insights on strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. This SEO SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through a systematic audit of a target URL, evaluating technical SEO factors, content quality, keyword targeting, backlink profiles, and competitive positioning. The output includes three specific findings in each SWOT category, dedicated keyword optimization insights, and a set of prioritized strategic recommendations. Digital marketers use it to audit client sites before launching campaigns, in-house SEO teams apply it for quarterly strategy reviews, and agency consultants run it to structure competitive analysis reports. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework to evaluate website SEO performance, prepare audit deliverables, or identify gaps in your optimization strategy. ● Surfaces three concrete strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats specific to the target website's SEO posture ● Delivers keyword optimization insights that highlight gaps, over-optimization risks, and untapped search terms ● Provides strategic SEO recommendations ranked by impact, with clear next steps for implementation ● Structures findings in a repeatable matrix format suitable for stakeholder presentations and internal planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist and analyst specializing in comprehensive website audits and strategic SEO recommendations.

## Task
Conduct a thorough SWOT analysis of {{website-url}}, examining its SEO strategy from all angles with special attention to keyword optimization. Be specific and actionable—avoid vague or generic insights.

## Output
Deliver your findings in this structured format:

**Website URL:** [state the URL]

**SWOT Analysis:**

*Strengths:*
1. [First strength]
2. [Second strength]
3. [Third strength]

*Weaknesses:*
1. [First weakness]
2. [Second weakness]
3. [Third weakness]

*Opportunities:*
1. [First opportunity]
2. [Second opportunity]
3. [Third opportunity]

*Threats:*
1. [First threat]
2. [Second threat]
3. [Third threat]

**Keyword Optimization Insights:**
- [First insight]
- [Second insight]
- [Third insight]

**Strategic SEO Recommendations:**
1. [First recommendation]
2. [Second recommendation]
3. [Third recommendation]
```
