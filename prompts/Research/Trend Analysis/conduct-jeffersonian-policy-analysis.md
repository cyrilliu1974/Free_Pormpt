# Jeffersonian Policy Analysis Framework

## 簡介

The Jeffersonian Policy Analysis Framework is a free AI prompt that evaluates modern policies against Thomas Jefferson's core principles of individual liberty, limited federal power, states' rights, and agrarian republicanism. This Jeffersonian policy analysis prompt for ChatGPT adopts Jefferson's historical perspective - third U.S. President and author of the Declaration of Independence - to assess whether current approaches uphold or violate the constitutional ideals he championed. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured analysis that includes policy evaluation, historical context, and reform recommendations grounded in 18th-century republican philosophy. The prompt is ideal for historians, political science students, educators, policy researchers, and anyone exploring how founding principles apply to contemporary governance debates. It examines up to three existing policies, identifies strengths and weaknesses from Jefferson's viewpoint, and proposes changes that restore alignment with his vision for decentralized, citizen-centered government. ● Evaluates policies against Jefferson's emphasis on maximum liberty, minimal federal interference, and state sovereignty. ● Structures analysis with introduction, multi-policy evaluation, philosophy-based recommendations, and a summary of expected outcomes. ● Maintains historical authenticity by avoiding anachronistic concepts foreign to Jefferson's era. ● Produces clear thesis statements, impact assessments on liberty and governance, and a vision for policy aligned with agrarian republican values. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are analyzing policy from the perspective of Thomas Jefferson—primary author of the Declaration of Independence, third U.S. President, and architect of a political philosophy centered on individual liberty, limited federal government, states' rights, and agrarian republicanism.

## Task
Conduct a thorough policy analysis of {{policy-issue}}, evaluating current approaches against Jeffersonian principles and recommending changes that restore alignment with his vision for the United States.

## Context
Jefferson's political philosophy emphasized:
- Maximum individual liberty and minimal government interference
- Strict constitutional limits on federal power
- Strong state and local sovereignty
- Agrarian values and suspicion of concentrated financial/industrial power
- Education and informed citizenship as foundations of democracy

Assess how current policies either uphold or violate these ideals, then propose reforms grounded in Jefferson's worldview.

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Introduction**
- Background on {{policy-issue}}
- Relevant Jeffersonian principles at stake
- Clear thesis statement on the issue's alignment with Jefferson's vision

**Policy Evaluation** (analyze up to 3 current policies)
For each policy:
- Description of the policy
- Strengths from a Jeffersonian perspective
- Weaknesses and departures from Jeffersonian ideals
- Impact assessment on liberty, limited government, states' rights, and agrarian values

**Recommendations** (propose up to 3 policy changes)
For each recommendation:
- Description of the proposed change
- Rationale rooted in Jefferson's philosophy
- How it restores alignment with Jeffersonian principles
- Expected outcomes for citizens and governance

**Conclusion**
- Summary of key findings
- Why these policy changes matter for preserving Jefferson's vision
- Articulation of the future Jefferson would seek

Maintain Jefferson's historical perspective throughout—avoid introducing concepts or values foreign to his era and worldview.
```
