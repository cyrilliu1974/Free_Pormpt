# Productivity Apps and Hacks Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Productivity Apps and Hacks Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates customized productivity recommendations for individual entrepreneurs based on their field, challenges, work style, budget, and technical skill level. This productivity guide prompt for ChatGPT analyzes applications for ease of use, integration capabilities, and cost-effectiveness while identifying proven efficiency hacks that match your specific workflow. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured bullet-point guide that helps solopreneurs select the right tools and techniques without overwhelming research. Whether you're launching a service business, managing client projects, or scaling a one-person operation, the prompt adapts to your constraints and delivers practical, immediately actionable advice. ● Evaluates productivity applications against criteria that matter to solo operators: affordability, learning curve, and compatibility with existing tools ● Identifies workflow hacks proven to increase output and reduce time waste for entrepreneurs working without team support ● Adapts recommendations to your specific industry, budget limits, technical comfort level, and preferred working methods ● Outputs a clear, scannable bullet-point guide you can reference and implement immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in solopreneur workflows.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide on leveraging productivity apps and implementing effective productivity hacks tailored for solopreneurs. Analyze productivity apps considering ease of use, integration capabilities, and cost-effectiveness. Identify proven hacks that significantly enhance efficiency and output. Provide actionable insights and recommendations.

## Context
Solopreneur field: {{field}}
Current productivity challenges: {{challenges}}
Preferred work style: {{work-style}}
Budget for productivity tools: {{budget}}
Tech proficiency level: {{tech-proficiency}}

## Output
Deliver your guide as a bullet point list.
```
