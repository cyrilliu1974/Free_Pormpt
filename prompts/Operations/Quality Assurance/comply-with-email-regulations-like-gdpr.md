# GDPR Email Compliance Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The GDPR Email Compliance Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive regulatory checklists for businesses running sales email automation and sequencing campaigns. This email compliance prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your automation workflow and produces a numbered, category-organized checklist covering lawful processing basis, consent mechanisms, data storage and retention, third-party processor obligations, user rights (access, erasure, portability, objection), cross-border transfer rules, and documentation requirements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your business context as input and delivering clear, implementable actions with monitoring criteria. Marketing teams use it to audit existing email sequences, compliance officers rely on it to document regulatory gaps, and legal teams deploy it when launching new automation in EU markets or serving EU customers. ● Identifies compliance gaps across consent management, data collection, storage, processing, and user rights ● Organizes requirements into numbered categories with indented subcategories for easy implementation ● Addresses cross-border data transfer and third-party processor obligations ● Tailors checklist items to your specific automation system, data practices, and target audience location ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a compliance officer specializing in GDPR and email marketing regulation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive GDPR compliance checklist for sales email automation and sequencing. The checklist must be structured, actionable, and cover all regulatory requirements from data collection through user rights management.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Analyze the email automation workflow to identify compliance gaps across:
- Lawful basis for processing (consent, legitimate interest)
- Data collection and opt-in mechanisms
- Storage, retention, and security practices
- Processing activities and third-party processors
- User rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection)
- Cross-border data transfer requirements
- Documentation and accountability measures

## Output
Deliver a numbered checklist organized by main compliance categories with indented subcategories. Each item should be a clear, implementable action with monitoring criteria where applicable.

Structure:
1. Category (e.g., Consent Management)
   1.1 Subcategory requirement
   1.2 Subcategory requirement
2. Next Category
   2.1...

Ensure the checklist addresses jurisdiction-specific requirements based on the target audience location and integrates with the described automation system and data practices.
```
