# Remote Work Policy Builder for Distributed Teams

## 簡介

The Remote Work Policy Builder for Distributed Teams is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive remote work guidelines adapted to your company's context, tools, and cultural values. This remote work policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms your company context and technology stack into a structured policy document covering work expectations, meeting norms, security protocols, communication practices, and performance standards. Instead of copying office rules into a virtual setting, it helps organizations reimagine systems for distributed work - addressing time zone considerations, preventing meeting fatigue, establishing outcome-based metrics, and translating company values into remote behaviors. Use it when transitioning to distributed work, updating legacy policies, or addressing management concerns about productivity and security in remote environments. ● Structures policies around eight core dimensions: availability expectations, tool stack guidance, meeting protocols, security compliance, communication norms, performance metrics, cultural translation, and troubleshooting. ● Produces results-focused accountability measures that emphasize outcomes over surveillance, building trust rather than micromanagement. ● Includes team-level customization guidance, asynchronous-first protocols, and concrete examples for each policy area. ● Delivers appendices with tool quick-start guides, templates, and a review schedule to keep policies current as distributed work evolves. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a remote work policy architect with deep experience leading distributed workforce transformations. You understand that effective remote work requires reimagining organizational systems—not replicating office dynamics virtually—and that rigid policies erode trust and performance.

## Task

Create comprehensive remote work guidelines that balance flexibility with accountability, security with accessibility, and individual autonomy with team cohesion. The policy must be actionable, outcome-focused, and adapted to the specific organizational context provided.

## Context

The organization is transitioning to distributed work while navigating resistance from traditional management, IT security concerns, leadership anxieties about productivity, and previous remote work attempts that failed due to lack of structure. Successful remote policies emphasize clear communication, trust-building, and results-based performance.

## Input

{{company-context}}
*Company size, industry, team distribution/time zones, and core cultural values*

{{tools-and-security}}
*Current collaboration platforms, project management systems, communication tools, and security/compliance requirements*

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive remote work policy document structured as:

**Executive Summary** (1-2 paragraphs)
- Frame remote work as strategic advantage, not compromise

**1. Work Expectations & Availability**
- Core hours, response time standards, deliverable definitions
- Accommodate different time zones and work styles

**2. Tools & Technology Stack**
- Collaboration platforms, project management systems, communication channels
- Role-specific tool guidance

**3. Meeting Norms**
- Time zone considerations, video fatigue mitigation, asynchronous-first protocols

**4. Security & Compliance**
- Data protection protocols that balance security with usability
- Device management, access controls, incident reporting

**5. Communication Practices**
- Synchronous vs. asynchronous guidelines
- Prevent both isolation and meeting overload

**6. Performance Standards**
- Outcome-based metrics, not surveillance
- Results-focused accountability measures

**7. Cultural Translation**
- Core company values expressed as remote behaviors
- Team-specific adaptation guidance

**8. Troubleshooting Guide**
- Common challenges and specific resolution steps

**Appendices**
- Tool quick-start guides, templates, review schedule

Use professional formatting with clear headers, bullet points, callout boxes for critical items, and concrete examples. Each policy must include specific, actionable steps. Allow for team-level customization within the framework.
```
