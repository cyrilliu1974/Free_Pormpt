# Online Store Launch Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Online Store Launch Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete roadmap for solopreneurs building an e-commerce business from scratch. This online store launch prompt for ChatGPT walks you through seven critical phases: niche validation and market research, business setup and legal foundations, store platform and technical configuration, product sourcing and inventory management, marketing and customer acquisition, order processing and fulfillment, and customer service and retention. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a numbered action plan with realistic timelines, cost estimates, and tool recommendations tailored to your budget, technical skill level, and time availability. Solopreneurs use it to map out launch sequences for dropshipping stores, handmade goods shops, print-on-demand businesses, and digital product storefronts. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured plan that accounts for your specific business context and target audience, ensuring each step builds logically on the last. ● Produces phase-by-phase launch plans covering legal setup, platform selection, and inventory strategy ● Tailors marketing and fulfillment recommendations to stated budget and technical abilities ● Includes concrete timelines and cost considerations for each business phase ● Structures dependencies so store setup, product sourcing, and customer acquisition follow a logical sequence ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an e-commerce consultant specializing in helping solopreneurs launch and scale online stores.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for establishing and growing an e-commerce business. Cover the full lifecycle from niche validation and store setup through marketing, order fulfillment, and customer service management.

## Context

**Business parameters:**
{{business-context}}

**Target audience:**
{{target-audience}}

Structure each step so it logically builds upon the previous one. Ensure the recommendations are realistic given the stated budget, technical skill level, and time commitment.

## Output

Deliver a numbered list with clear headings for each major phase:

1. **Niche Validation & Market Research**
2. **Business Setup & Legal Foundations**
3. **Store Platform & Technical Setup**
4. **Product Sourcing & Inventory**
5. **Marketing & Customer Acquisition**
6. **Order Processing & Fulfillment**
7. **Customer Service & Retention**

Under each heading, provide sub-bullets with concrete, actionable steps tailored to the business context. Include timelines, cost considerations, and skill-appropriate tool recommendations where relevant.
```
