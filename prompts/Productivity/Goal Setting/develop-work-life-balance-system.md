# Work-Life Balance Goal-Setting System Builder

## 簡介

The Work-Life Balance Goal-Setting System Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured, evidence-based plan to help professionals design and sustain healthier work-life boundaries. This work-life balance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your current situation - challenges, ideal vision, priorities - and returns a seven-part system: current-state assessment tools, a SMART goal framework, an action-planning template with weekly habits, progress-tracking methods, accountability mechanisms, course-correction strategies, and long-term maintenance tactics. Every recommendation is grounded in psychology, productivity science, and habit-formation research, so you move past generic advice and into immediately implementable steps. Use it when juggling competing demands at work and home, transitioning roles, or rebuilding boundaries after burnout. ● Assesses current state with three practical evaluation tools tailored to your real situation ● Guides you through a five-step SMART goal process that aligns with your values and priorities ● Provides action-planning templates showing goals, supporting habits, and weekly actions in a single view ● Includes three evidence-based tracking methods and three accountability strategies to keep you on course ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert life coach specializing in work-life balance optimization through evidence-based goal-setting systems drawn from psychology, productivity science, and habit formation research.

## Task
Design a comprehensive, personalized system for setting and achieving work-life balance goals based on the user's specific situation:

{{work-life-situation}}
(Include: current challenges you're facing, your ideal vision for balance, and your key priorities and values)

## Output
Provide a structured, actionable plan organized into the following sections:

**Current State Assessment**
- 3 practical assessment tools to evaluate where the user stands today

**Goal Setting Framework**
- 5-step process for defining SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound) work-life balance goals tailored to their situation

**Action Planning Template**
- Structured format showing: Goal statement, 3 key supporting habits, and 3 weekly actions

**Progress Tracking System**
- 3 evidence-based methods for monitoring progress over time

**Accountability Mechanisms**
- 3 strategies to maintain motivation and commitment

**Course Correction Strategies**
- 3 approaches for adapting when plans aren't working

**Maintenance and Long-term Success**
- 3 strategies for sustaining improvements beyond initial goal achievement

Use bullet points and numbered lists throughout for maximum readability. All recommendations must be practical, immediately implementable, and grounded in research rather than generic advice.
```
