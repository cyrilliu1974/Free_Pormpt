# Amazon Ads Campaign Setup Prompt for Affiliate Marketing

## 簡介

The Amazon Ads Campaign Setup Prompt for Affiliate Marketing is a free AI prompt that builds a detailed, high-ROI advertising roadmap for sellers and affiliates promoting products on Amazon. This Amazon Ads prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces an eight-step implementation plan covering keyword research, ad group structure, persuasive ad copy, bidding strategy, performance monitoring, advanced targeting, listing optimization, and analytics-driven iteration. You provide your product, target audience, budget, and campaign goals; the AI returns a ready-to-execute campaign blueprint designed to maximize affiliate sales and minimize wasted spend. Real-world use cases include launching seasonal promotions, scaling existing campaigns, and training new team members on best practices. This prompt is ideal for affiliate marketers, Amazon sellers, PPC managers, and ad agencies who need a structured, repeatable framework for setting up profitable Amazon advertising campaigns quickly. ● Delivers keyword research that identifies both broad and long-tail high-converting search terms specific to your product and audience. ● Structures ad groups for relevance and Quality Score, then writes persuasive ad copy with strong calls-to-action. ● Recommends data-driven bidding strategies and defines which metrics to monitor - CTR, conversion rate, CPA, ACOS - for ongoing optimization. ● Includes advanced targeting techniques such as product targeting, negative keywords, and audience refinement to reduce irrelevant impressions and improve ROAS. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Amazon Ads specialist building a high-ROI advertising campaign.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step Amazon Ads campaign plan for {{product}} that drives affiliate marketing sales and maximizes return on investment.

## Context
Product: {{product}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Campaign budget: {{budget}}
Campaign goals and duration: {{goals-and-timeframe}}

## Campaign Plan
Deliver a detailed implementation roadmap covering:

1. **Keyword Research** – Identify high-converting, relevant keywords and phrases specific to the product. Include both broad and long-tail opportunities.

2. **Ad Group Structure** – Organize keywords into tightly themed ad groups, ensuring each group contains closely related terms for relevance and Quality Score.

3. **Ad Copy** – Write compelling, persuasive copy that highlights the product's unique benefits and features. Include strong calls-to-action that encourage clicks and conversions.

4. **Bidding Strategy** – Set appropriate bids for each ad group based on keyword competition, relevance, and expected conversion rates. Recommend bid optimization tactics to maximize ROI.

5. **Performance Monitoring** – Define which metrics to track (CTR, conversion rate, CPA, ACOS) and how to make data-driven adjustments to bids, copy, and targeting.

6. **Advanced Targeting** – Apply product targeting, negative keywords, and audience refinement strategies to reduce wasted spend and improve targeting precision.

7. **Listing Optimization** – Ensure the Amazon product page is conversion-ready with high-quality images, detailed descriptions, competitive pricing, and enhanced brand content where applicable.

8. **Analytics & Iteration** – Leverage Amazon's reporting tools to gain customer behavior insights and recommend ongoing optimization actions.

## Output
Provide the campaign plan in clear, numbered steps with actionable details for immediate implementation.
```
