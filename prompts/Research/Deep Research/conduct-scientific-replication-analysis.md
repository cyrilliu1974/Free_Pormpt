# Scientific Replication Crisis Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Scientific Replication Crisis Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive investigation reports examining replication challenges, reform efforts, and successful studies across scientific disciplines. This scientific replication crisis prompt for ChatGPT takes a single field-of-study variable and outputs a structured analysis that identifies systemic methodological issues threatening scientific credibility, rates the effectiveness of proposed solutions (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW), and catalogs documented successful replication studies with impact assessments and full citations. The prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to critically evaluate source quality by examining author expertise, peer-review status, publication venue, methodological rigor, and potential conflicts of interest. Researchers use it to assess the state of reproducibility in psychology, medicine, economics, or any domain facing credibility concerns; meta-researchers apply it to track reform progress; and grant reviewers leverage it to contextualize proposed studies within replication debates. ● Identifies and explains key methodological issues undermining replication efforts in the specified discipline ● Rates the effectiveness of proposed solutions and reforms with evidence-based HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW assessments ● Documents successful replication studies with detailed impact descriptions and complete source citations ● Includes critical source-quality evaluation criteria to ensure reliable, peer-reviewed evidence throughout the analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert investigator analyzing the replication crisis in scientific research, with deep knowledge of methodological issues, proposed reforms, and successful replication efforts across disciplines.

## Task
Investigate the replication crisis in {{field-of-study}}, critically analyzing key issues, proposed solutions with effectiveness assessments, and documented successful replications. Cite every source and reflect on source quality throughout your analysis.

## Context
The replication crisis threatens scientific credibility across many fields. Your investigation should identify systemic issues, evaluate reform efforts, and highlight where successful replications have restored confidence. Assess source reliability by considering: author/institutional reputation and expertise, peer-review status, publication venue, recency and relevance, methodological rigor and transparency, and potential biases or conflicts of interest.

Provide a structured report:

```
