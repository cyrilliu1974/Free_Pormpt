# Cinematic Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces studio-quality portraits with dramatic lighting and precise facial replication for photographers, content creators, and creative professionals. This cinematic portrait prompt for ChatGPT-adjacent image models - Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana - instructs the AI to render your subject against a pure black studio background in a three-quarter view with an upward gaze. You control two lighting colors: a rim light from the right and a fill light from the left, while the prompt anchors facial features to a reference image you provide. Real use cases include editorial portraits, brand ambassador visuals, social media profiles, and marketing hero shots that demand consistent face accuracy across lighting variations. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-grade portrait photography without studio rental, lighting equipment, or post-production color grading - especially when the same face must appear across multiple color treatments. ● Black studio background isolates the subject for clean composition and easy masking. ● Customizable rim-light and fill-light colors let you match brand palettes or mood boards. ● Reference-image anchoring ensures the generated face matches your subject's actual features. ● Three-quarter angle and upward gaze deliver the classic cinematic portrait framing used in editorial and entertainment photography. ## Prompt

```
A hyper-realistic portrait of {{subject-description}}, against a pure black studio background. Three-quarter view, looking slightly up, cinematic portrait photography with dramatic lighting. {{rim-light-color}} rim light from the right, {{fill-light-color}} fill light from the left. Use the provided reference image for the face—do not change the face.

Style: cinematic, ultra-realistic, studio portrait
Camera: three-quarter angle, slightly upward gaze
Signature: Shreya Yadav
```
