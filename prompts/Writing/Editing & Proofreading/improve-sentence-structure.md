# Sentence Structure Improvement Prompt

## 簡介

The Sentence Structure Improvement Prompt is a free AI prompt that revises documents to enhance clarity, readability, and grammatical coherence for writers, editors, and linguists. It applies dependency grammar principles to restructure sentences and clarify syntactic relationships while preserving the original meaning and intent. This sentence structure prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your document for structural weaknesses, unclear dependencies, ambiguous word choice, and coherence issues, then provides section-by-section revisions with detailed explanations of each improvement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a formatted output that shows both the improved text and a breakdown of structural enhancements, dependency grammar corrections, and word choice refinements. Use it to polish academic papers, business documents, blog posts, or any written content that needs better flow and grammatical precision. Reach for this prompt when you need more than basic proofreading - when sentence structure, syntactic relationships, and overall coherence require expert-level revision without losing your original voice or message. ● Analyzes documents for structural weaknesses, unclear dependencies, and ambiguous phrasing using dependency grammar frameworks ● Restructures sentences to clarify head-dependent relationships and improve syntactic coherence ● Refines word choice and transitions to enhance flow while maintaining consistent tone and style ● Delivers section-by-section revisions with detailed explanations of each grammatical and structural improvement made ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert linguist and editor specializing in dependency grammar.

## Task
Revise the provided document to improve clarity, readability, sentence structure, and grammar while preserving the original meaning and intent. Apply dependency grammar principles to create concise, coherent sentences with clear syntactic relationships.

## Context
{{document-details}}

## Process
1. Analyze the document for structural weaknesses, unclear dependencies, ambiguous word choice, and coherence issues
2. Restructure sentences using dependency grammar to clarify head-dependent relationships
3. Refine word choice and transitions to improve flow
4. Ensure consistency in tone and style throughout

## Output
Provide your revision in this format:

### [Section Name]
**Revised text:**
[Your improved version]

**Improvements made:**
- [Bullet point describing each structural or grammatical enhancement]
- [Explain dependency grammar improvements]
- [Note word choice refinements]

Repeat this structure for each section of the document.
```
