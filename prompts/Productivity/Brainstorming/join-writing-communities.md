# Writing Community Engagement Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Writing Community Engagement Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates actionable plans for writers seeking to find, join, and contribute meaningfully to online writing communities. This writing community prompt for ChatGPT guides you through every phase of community engagement - from discovering niche-aligned groups to establishing credibility, networking with peers, and sharing valuable insights. It structures all guidance using dependency grammar principles, ensuring clarity by leading with core actions and adding context through subordinate clauses. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing numbered action plans with lettered sub-points tailored to your writer profile, experience level, and time commitment. Writers use it to overcome the cold-start problem in unfamiliar communities, build authentic relationships with fellow authors, and turn passive membership into active learning and contribution. Reach for this prompt when you need a clear roadmap for joining writing groups, forums, or Slack communities without wasting time on trial and error. ● Identifies discovery methods for finding writing communities that match your niche, platform preferences, and goals ● Provides onboarding tactics to establish credibility and presence as a new member without appearing self-promotional ● Outlines networking strategies and relationship-building approaches suited to your available time commitment ● Includes time management tactics and measurable milestones for tracking engagement and writing progress over weeks and months ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert community manager and writing mentor specializing in helping writers find, join, and contribute to online writing communities.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable strategy for joining and actively participating in writing communities. The plan should cover discovery, onboarding, meaningful contribution, and leveraging community connections for growth. Apply dependency grammar principles to structure all written guidance—prioritize main clauses that establish core actions, with dependent clauses adding context and conditions.

## Context
Writing niche, experience level, goals, platform preferences, and time commitment:
{{writer-profile}}

## Requirements
- Identify methods for discovering communities aligned with the niche
- Explain how to establish credibility and presence as a new member
- Provide strategies for networking and building relationships with peers
- Describe ways to learn from community interactions and feedback
- Outline approaches for sharing insights and contributing value
- Show how to structure contributions using dependency grammar (main idea first, supporting details in subordinate position)
- Include time management tactics suited to the available commitment level
- Suggest measurable milestones for tracking community engagement and writing progress

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a numbered list with lettered sub-points under each main step. Ensure the structure allows easy implementation: each numbered item should represent a distinct phase or action category in the community engagement process.
```
