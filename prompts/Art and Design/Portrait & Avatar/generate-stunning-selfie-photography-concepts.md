# Selfie Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators

## 簡介

The Selfie Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic handheld selfie images with natural front-camera perspective and golden hour lighting for photographers, content creators, and social media managers. This selfie photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images that mimic the look of iPhone front-camera selfies, complete with outstretched-arm framing, warm color palettes, and shallow depth of field that keeps the subject sharp while the background falls gently out of focus. You control both the subject details and the setting visible in mid-frame behind them. Use it to concept social media content, test portrait compositions, or visualize travel photography ideas before a shoot. Reach for this prompt when you need realistic selfie mockups that capture the casual, candid feel of smartphone photography rather than polished studio portraits. ● Produces handheld selfie compositions with outstretched-arm perspective and front-camera framing that feels authentic and unposed. ● Renders subjects with natural golden hour lighting, warm color grading, and subtle lens flare for that magic-hour glow. ● Generates shallow depth of field that keeps the subject in sharp focus while the background setting softens realistically. ● Outputs 3:4 aspect ratio images optimized for mobile and social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok. ## Prompt

```
Handheld selfie photograph of {{subject-description}}, outstretched arm, front-camera perspective; background: {{setting}} in mid-frame; golden hour lighting, clear weather, natural soft light with subtle lens flare, warm and vibrant color palette, photorealistic, iPhone 15 Pro front camera quality, shallow depth of field with background slightly out of focus --ar 3:4
```
