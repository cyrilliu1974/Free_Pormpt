# Macro Water Droplet Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Macro Water Droplet Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces close-up, hyper-detailed images of water droplets on leaves for photographers, designers, and content creators. This macro photography prompt for Midjourney creates 8K resolution images with sharp focus, natural lighting, and professional color grading. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you specify the water droplet arrangement, leaf type and color, and aspect ratio to match your composition needs. Use it for stock photography libraries, nature publications, product backgrounds, or portfolio pieces that require pristine botanical macro shots.
