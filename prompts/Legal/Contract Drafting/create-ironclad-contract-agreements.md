# Contract Agreement Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Contract Agreement Drafting Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, legally structured contract agreements for businesses, legal professionals, and anyone establishing formal business relationships. This contract drafting prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through building formal agreements organized into 11 standard legal sections: Purpose, Term, Responsibilities of Party A, Responsibilities of Party B, Payment Terms, Confidentiality, Intellectual Property, Termination, Indemnification, Governing Law and Jurisdiction, and Entire Agreement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing contracts with precise language, specific terms, and protective clauses for both parties. You provide basic contract details - party names and addresses, core purpose, key obligations, payment structure, duration, and special terms - and receive a professionally formatted agreement that eliminates vague language and potential loopholes. Common use cases include service agreements, vendor contracts, consulting arrangements, partnership agreements, and NDA-backed business relationships. Reach for this prompt whenever you need a structured starting point for formal contract documentation that addresses standard legal requirements and protects all parties involved. ● Outputs contracts with 11 standard legal sections including confidentiality, intellectual property, indemnification, and termination clauses ● Uses precise contract language that minimizes ambiguity and closes common loopholes ● Produces professionally formatted agreements with signature blocks and witness clauses ● Adapts to multiple contract types by accepting detailed party information, obligations, payment terms, and special constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a contract law expert drafting a comprehensive, legally sound agreement that protects both parties and eliminates ambiguity.

## Task
Create a formal contract agreement organized into 11 standard sections: Purpose, Term, Responsibilities of Party A, Responsibilities of Party B, Payment Terms, Confidentiality, Intellectual Property, Termination, Indemnification, Governing Law and Jurisdiction, and Entire Agreement.

## Requirements
- Use clear, precise language with specific, well-defined terms
- Cover all essential aspects of the business relationship described in {{contract-details}}
- Include necessary protective clauses for both parties
- Eliminate vague language and potential loopholes
- Maintain professional legal contract formatting

## Context
{{contract-details}} should specify: type of contract, Party A name and address, Party B name and address, core purpose, key obligations for each party, payment structure, duration, and any special terms or constraints.

## Output Format

**AGREEMENT**

This [type] Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on [date] by and between [Party A], located at [address], and [Party B], located at [address], collectively referred to as the "Parties."

**1. PURPOSE**  
[State the purpose of the agreement]

**2. TERM**  
[Specify the duration]

**3. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTY A**  
[Outline Party A obligations and duties]

**4. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTY B**  
[Outline Party B obligations and duties]

**5. PAYMENT TERMS**  
[Detail financial arrangements]

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**  
[Include clause protecting sensitive information]

**7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**  
[Address ownership and usage rights]

**8. TERMINATION**  
[Specify termination conditions]

**9. INDEMNIFICATION**  
[Include clause protecting parties from losses or damages]

**10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**  
[Specify applicable laws and jurisdiction]

**11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT**  
[Confirm this written agreement represents the complete understanding]

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.

[Party A]

By: ____________________  
Name:  
Title:

[Party B]

By: ____________________  
Name:  
Title:
```
