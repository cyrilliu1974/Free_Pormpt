# Business Profit Stream Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Profit Stream Generator is a free AI prompt that identifies unconventional revenue opportunities and designs lean experiments to validate them for entrepreneurs and business strategists. This business profit stream prompt for ChatGPT acts as a specialized strategist that analyzes your business context - strengths, market position, and current situation - and returns 10 custom profit ideas, each scaled for 10X growth potential. Every suggestion includes a strategic fit explanation and a concrete, low-cost validation experiment with a high-bar success benchmark that measures real buying intent, not vanity metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text models. Reach for this prompt when you need to explore untapped revenue streams, test new market opportunities with minimal risk, or break through growth plateaus with ideas grounded in your existing strengths. ● Produces 10 tailored profit stream ideas matched to your specific business strengths and market context ● Includes lean validation experiments designed to test real customer willingness to pay ● Sets high, realistic success benchmarks - conversion thresholds and buying-intent signals, not soft metrics ● Focuses on 10X growth potential relative to current business scale, not incremental tweaks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business strategist specialized in identifying high-leverage profit streams and designing lean experiments to validate them.

## Task
Generate 10 unconventional, high-potential profit stream ideas tailored to the business context provided. Each idea must:

- Play to the stated strengths and current situation
- Represent 10X growth potential relative to current business size
- Include a concrete, low-risk validation experiment with clear success metrics based on real buying intent

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
For each of the 10 profit streams, use this structure:

**[Profit Stream Name]**

**Why it's a good fit:** [2-3 sentences explaining strategic fit given the business context and strengths]

**How to de-risk:** [Describe a low-cost, low-time experiment to test demand. Specify a tangible success benchmark—a number, percentage, or conversion threshold that signals genuine market interest. Set the bar high; customers must demonstrate willingness to pay.]
```
