# Food Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Food Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional 2×3 photo grids for packaged food marketing and social media campaigns. This food advertisement grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates six equal panels in a 4:5 portrait layout: a hero product shot on white ceramic, a macro texture close-up with shallow depth of field, a breakfast lifestyle scene with coffee, a clean packaging shot, an adult preparing the product in bright kitchen lighting, and a raw ingredient detail. Every composition is set in a minimal Scandinavian kitchen with light oak surfaces, natural window daylight, and neutral props like linen napkins and ceramic dishes. The prompt enforces a warm neutral palette with one fresh accent color, professional food photography realism, soft shadows, and a clean modern aesthetic that avoids stale appearances, harsh flash, or artificial textures. Use this prompt when you need polished multi-panel product photography for Instagram carousels, print ads, pitch decks, or e-commerce hero images without hiring a studio photographer. ● Structures six distinct compositions (hero, macro, lifestyle, packaging, preparation, ingredient) in a balanced 2×3 grid ● Enforces consistent Scandinavian kitchen environment, natural lighting, and warm neutral color palette across all panels ● Includes detailed prop and styling direction (ceramic plates, linen napkins, glass jars, light oak surfaces) ● Outputs 4:5 portrait ratio optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, and mobile e-commerce ## Prompt

```
Create a 4:5 portrait 2×3 grid advertisement for {{product-name}}, a packaged {{food-product}} styled with premium modern food photography.

**Grid Panels (equal size, 2 columns × 3 rows):**

1. **Hero Stack** – Neatly arranged product on white ceramic plate, top-down or 45° angle, fresh and appetizing
2. **Texture Close-Up** – Macro shot of golden crisp surface, shallow depth of field, warm and comforting detail
3. **Breakfast Scene** – Product beside coffee cup, soft morning light, cozy ritual moment
4. **Packaging Shot** – {{product-name}} package upright on clean counter, modern and practical
5. **Lifestyle Moment** – Adult preparing product in bright kitchen, balanced daily-life energy
6. **Ingredient Detail** – Raw ingredients subtly arranged near product, natural and wholesome

**Environment:** Minimal Scandinavian kitchen, light oak wooden table, soft natural window daylight. Props: ceramic white plate, neutral linen napkin, simple glass jar, modern matte appliance.

**Visual Style:** Professional food photography, high realism, warm neutral palette with one fresh accent tone, bright balanced exposure, soft natural shadows, clean sans-serif typography.

**Mood:** Fresh, cozy, clean, simple, modern, wholesome.

**Avoid:** Stale appearance, messy setting, harsh flash, overexposed highlights, artificial plastic look, cartoon style, low resolution, watermarks, text overlays.
```
