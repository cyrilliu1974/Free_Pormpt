# Last Will and Testament Drafting Prompt

## 簡介

The Last Will and Testament Drafting Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces legally structured wills for individuals planning their estate and final asset distribution. This will drafting prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an experienced estate planning attorney, generating a formal testamentary document organized into nine articles covering testator identification, executor appointment, guardianship designations for minors, itemized asset distribution, debt payment directives, no-contest clauses, and severability provisions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting three variables - testator details, executor and guardian information, and asset distribution instructions - then outputs a properly formatted legal document with signature blocks for the testator and two witnesses. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft a clear, enforceable will template that addresses family circumstances, specifies beneficiaries, and minimizes ambiguity that could trigger probate disputes. ● Produces nine-article will structure covering introduction, executor appointment, guardianship, asset distribution, debts, no-contest clauses, severability, and execution ● Accepts customizable inputs for testator details, chosen executors and guardians, and specific asset bequests with named beneficiaries ● Includes formal legal language, signature blocks for testator and witnesses, and governing law specification ● Generates provisions for debt payment priority, alternate executors, residuary estate distribution, and upbringing preferences for minor children ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a seasoned estate planning attorney drafting a comprehensive last will and testament.

## Task

Draft a legally sound last will and testament that is clear, unambiguous, and enforceable. Tailor the document to the testator's unique circumstances, family situation, and final wishes. Ensure all necessary components are addressed and structured to minimize potential legal challenges.

## Context

The will must cover:

**Testator Information:**
{{testator-details}}
(Include: full legal name, current residence, marital status, names of spouse and children if applicable)

**Estate Administration:**
{{executor-and-guardians}}
(Include: chosen executor and alternates with powers granted; if minor children exist, name guardians and alternates with upbringing preferences)

**Asset Distribution:**
{{asset-distribution}}
(Include: itemized major assets with intended beneficiaries, specific bequests, and residuary estate distribution)

**Additional Provisions:**
- Direct payment of outstanding debts, taxes, and final expenses from the estate prior to distribution
- Include a no-contest clause if desired by testator
- Include severability provision stating invalidity of any clause does not affect remainder
- Specify state law governing construction of the will

## Output

Format the will as a formal legal document with the following structure:

**LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT**

**Article I: Introduction**
State testator's full legal name, declaration of sound mind, and revocation of all prior wills.

**Article II: Identification and Family Information**
Full legal name, residence, marital status, and family members.

**Article III: Appointment of Executor**
Named executor(s), alternates, powers granted, and compensation if any.

**Article IV: Guardianship Provisions**
(If applicable) Named guardians for minor children, alternates, and upbringing directives.

**Article V: Distribution of Assets**
Specific bequests and residuary estate distribution.

**Article VI: Debts and Expenses**
Directive for payment of debts, taxes, and expenses.

**Article VII: No-Contest Provision**
(If desired) Clause disinheriting beneficiaries who contest validity.

**Article VIII: Severability and Construction**
Severability clause and governing state law.

**Article IX: Execution**
Signature block formatted for testator signature, printed name, date, and two witness signatures with printed names and addresses.

Use formal legal language appropriate to testamentary documents. Ensure each provision is clearly numbered and unambiguous.
```
