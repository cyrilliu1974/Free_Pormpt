# Marketing Problem-Solving Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Marketing Problem-Solving Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces practical, budget-friendly marketing solutions for solo entrepreneurs and small teams facing specific challenges. This marketing problem-solving prompt for ChatGPT takes any marketing obstacle you describe and returns 10 concrete, executable tactics designed for today's digital landscape. Each solution includes a descriptive title, step-by-step implementation guidance, and an explanation of why it qualifies as a quick win. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing zero-budget or minimal-spend approaches that one person can execute without a team or specialized skills. Use it when you're stuck on a marketing challenge and need fresh, actionable ideas that deliver visible results fast - from growing an email list to increasing social engagement or improving conversion rates. ● Surfaces 10 distinct solutions per query, each with implementation steps and quick-win rationale ● Filters for low-cost or free tactics that avoid outdated strategies ● Designed for solo execution without requiring specialized marketing skills or team resources ● Outputs in clean Markdown format with headings and bullets for easy scanning and action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in practical, high-impact solutions for solo entrepreneurs and small teams.

## Task
Generate 10 actionable solutions to the marketing problem described below. Each solution should be:

- **Relevant and current**: applicable to today's digital landscape, avoiding outdated tactics
- **Low-cost or free**: prioritizing zero-budget or minimal-spend approaches
- **Solopreneur-friendly**: executable by one person without requiring a team or specialized skills
- **Fast-win oriented**: focusing on tactics proven to deliver visible results quickly

## Context
{{marketing-problem}}

## Output
Present each solution as a numbered list. For each idea, include:

1. A clear, descriptive title
2. A 2-3 sentence explanation of how to implement it
3. Why it meets the quick-win criteria

Format your response using Markdown with clear headings and bullet points for readability.
```
