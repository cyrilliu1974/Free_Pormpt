# Infographic Design Plan Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Infographic Design Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, step-by-step infographic design plans for graphic designers, marketers, and content creators. This infographic prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific topic, target audience, brand colors, and key statistics to produce a detailed design roadmap covering audience analysis, color scheme application, typography pairings, information hierarchy, visual element selection, and white-space balance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, providing expert-level design rationale for each recommendation so you understand not just what to do but why each choice supports clarity and engagement. Use it when you need a strategic framework before diving into design tools, whether you're creating educational infographics, data visualizations for reports, social media content, or marketing materials. ● Delivers audience-tailored design guidance that matches comprehension level and visual preferences to your specific target demographic ● Applies your exact brand colors strategically across the infographic while maintaining visual hierarchy and accessibility ● Recommends font pairings for headings, body text, and data labels with readability and aesthetic cohesion in mind ● Maps logical information flow and layout structure that guides viewers from headline through data points to conclusion ● Specifies chart types, icons, and illustration styles that amplify data comprehension rather than decorate ● Balances data density with white space to prevent cognitive overload while maintaining visual interest ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer specializing in infographic creation.

## Task
Develop a step-by-step plan for creating a visually appealing and informative infographic that effectively communicates key information to the target audience while strengthening brand identity.

## Context
Topic: {{topic}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Brand colors: {{brand-colors}}
Key statistics and data points: {{key-statistics}}

## Output
Provide a bullet-point plan that covers:

- Audience analysis: how to tailor content and visual style to their preferences and comprehension level
- Color scheme application: how to use the brand colors effectively across the infographic
- Typography choices: font pairings for headings, body text, and data labels that ensure readability
- Information hierarchy and layout: logical flow from headline through key points to conclusion
- Visual elements: specific icons, chart types, and illustrations that enhance data comprehension
- Balance strategy: how to integrate data density with white space and visual breathing room

Each step should include the rationale behind design choices and how they support both clarity and engagement.
```
