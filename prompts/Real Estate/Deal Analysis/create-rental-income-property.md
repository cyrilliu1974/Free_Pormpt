# Rental Income Property Investment Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Rental Income Property Investment Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive, multi-strategy real estate portfolio plan for individual investors seeking passive rental income. This rental property investment prompt for ChatGPT analyzes market trends, rental demand, appreciation potential, financing options, property management requirements, and tax implications across a range of property types and locations. You provide a single investor profile variable - budget, risk tolerance, experience level, and time availability - and the prompt returns a structured markdown table comparing at least five distinct investment strategies, each with expected returns, down payment requirements, management intensity, key risks, mitigation tactics, and tax advantages. Real use cases include solopreneurs building their first rental portfolio, experienced investors diversifying into new markets, and financial planners modeling client scenarios. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to compare multiple rental income paths side by side, balance risk against return, and match strategies to a specific investor's constraints and goals. ● Compares five or more rental strategies spanning different risk levels, capital requirements, and management styles in a single table ● Includes financing approaches, expected returns, appreciation forecasts, and tax advantages for each property type and location ● Identifies key risks and specific mitigation tactics so investors can make informed, defensible decisions ● Tailors recommendations to the investor's budget, risk tolerance, experience, and time availability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert real estate investment strategist specializing in passive rental income portfolios for individual investors.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive real estate investment plan that balances return potential against risk across multiple strategies. Analyze market trends, rental demand, appreciation potential, financing options, property management approaches, and tax implications for each recommended path.

## Context
The investor is a solopreneur building passive income streams through rental real estate.

**Investor Profile:**
{{investor-profile}}

## Output
Present your recommendations as a markdown table with three columns: INVESTMENT STRATEGY, PROPERTY TYPE, and LOCATION. Provide at least five distinct strategies that span different risk levels, capital requirements, and management intensities. For each strategy, include:

- Expected returns and appreciation potential
- Financing approach and typical down payment
- Management requirements (time/delegation)
- Key risks and mitigation tactics
- Tax advantages specific to that strategy
- Suitability given the investor's profile

Ensure the range of options allows the investor to diversify across property types, geographies, and investment horizons.
```
