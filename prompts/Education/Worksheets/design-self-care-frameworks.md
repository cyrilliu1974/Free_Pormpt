# Teacher Self-Care Check-In Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Teacher Self-Care Check-In Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds multi-layered wellness assessment tools for educators struggling to maintain healthy work-life boundaries. This self-care framework prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces bullet-point check-in systems organized by frequency and depth: daily micro-assessments for quick awareness, weekly pattern analysis that uncovers triggers, red-flag indicators with administrator communication scripts, and green-light signals confirming sustainable balance. Teachers input their specific context (grade level, subject, school environment) and receive customized reflection prompts that cut through the "I'm fine" facade, combining quantitative scales with qualitative questions that address physical exhaustion, emotional depletion, classroom stress, administrative pressure, and personal fulfillment. It is designed for educators who recognize they prioritize student needs over self-care and need structured accountability to prevent burnout before crisis hits. ● Daily micro-check-ins offer brief yes/no indicators and simple scales for surface-level awareness of physical and emotional state ● Weekly deep assessments reveal trends through reflection prompts that evaluate boundaries, fulfillment, and recurring trigger points ● Red-flag warning signs list specific burnout indicators with scripts for communicating needs to administrators and urgent action steps ● Green-light signals confirm healthy patterns worth maintaining and indicators of sustainable teaching practice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educator wellness consultant who combines classroom experience with expertise in mindfulness and organizational psychology, specializing in burnout prevention for teachers.

## Task
Create a comprehensive self-care check-in framework that helps teachers honestly assess workload balance and emotional well-being. Design questions that cut through the "I'm fine" facade and address physical exhaustion, emotional depletion, work-life boundaries, classroom stress, administrative pressure, and personal fulfillment.

## Context
{{teaching-context}}

Teachers are natural caregivers who often ignore their own needs until crisis hits. Your approach must combine gentle accountability with practical boundary-setting strategies. Structure check-ins to progress from surface-level awareness to deeper self-reflection, using both quantitative scales and qualitative prompts. Include guidance on when to seek support, how to communicate needs to administrators, and practical steps for immediate relief when overwhelm peaks.

## Output
Provide your framework in bullet point format organized into:

**Daily Micro-Check-Ins** (quick pulse readings)
- Brief questions for surface-level awareness
- Simple scales or yes/no indicators

**Weekly Deep Assessments** (pattern and trigger point analysis)
- Reflection prompts that reveal trends
- Quantitative and qualitative questions
- Boundaries and fulfillment evaluation

**Red Flag Warning Signs** (demand immediate attention)
- Specific indicators requiring urgent action
- When and how to seek support
- Communication scripts for administrators
- Immediate relief steps

**Green Light Signals** (confirm healthy balance)
- Indicators of sustainable well-being
- Positive patterns to maintain
```
