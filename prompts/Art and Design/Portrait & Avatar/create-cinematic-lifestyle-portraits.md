# Cinematic Luxury Car Portrait Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Luxury Car Portrait Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates high-end lifestyle photography featuring subjects in upscale vehicle interiors at night for marketers, content creators, and brand designers. This cinematic lifestyle portrait prompt for ChatGPT image models produces magazine-quality scenes with subjects seated in luxury sedan back seats, complete with visible leather upholstery, electronic seat controls, and illuminated urban exteriors through windows and sunroofs. It specifies shallow depth of field, rich contrast, and a moody low-light aesthetic lit by streetlights and ambient car glow. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, and accepts two variables: subject-description (physical appearance, styling, clothing) and activity (what the subject is doing). Photographers, automotive brands, and lifestyle marketers use it to generate candid, cinematic imagery that communicates sophistication and modern urban luxury. Reach for this prompt when you need premium vehicle-based lifestyle visuals without organizing physical shoots, renting locations, or hiring professional photographers. ● Specifies luxury sedan interior details including light-colored leather seats and electronic controls for authentic upscale vehicle settings. ● Defines low-light moody atmosphere with streetlight and interior ambient glow, creating soft shadows and cinematic highlights. ● Controls composition with shallow depth of field, sharp subject focus, and a refined color palette of blacks, beiges, muted blues, and styling accents. ● Produces candid lifestyle photography aesthetic suitable for automotive campaigns, fashion lookbooks, and premium brand visual marketing. ## Prompt

```
{{subject-description}} sitting in the back seat of a luxury sedan at night, positioned facing toward the front of the car, slightly turned, {{activity}}. Clean upscale interior with visible electronic seat controls and light-colored leather seats. Through the window and sunroof, softly illuminated building exterior in a high-end urban setting. Low-light moody atmosphere with lighting from streetlights and interior ambient car glow creating soft shadows and highlights. Shallow depth of field, sharp focus on subject, rich contrast and high detail. Candid cinematic lifestyle photography aesthetic. Color palette: blacks, soft whites and beiges, muted blues, with accent pops from subject styling.
```
