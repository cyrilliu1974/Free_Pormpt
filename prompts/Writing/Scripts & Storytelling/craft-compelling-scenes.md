# Fiction Scene Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Fiction Scene Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that crafts complete, emotionally engaging fiction scenes for writers across any genre. This fiction scene prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce structured narrative content organized into setting, character development, dialogue, and narrative description. Writers specify their genre, main character, current plot point, and desired emotional tone, and the prompt returns a fully realized scene that grounds readers in atmosphere, surfaces conflict through action and dialogue, and layers in character interiority to deepen emotional impact. It runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it a versatile tool for novelists, screenwriters, and storytellers working in any fiction format. The output is structured under clear headings - Setting, Characters, Dialogue, and Narrative Description - so writers can immediately see how each element serves the narrative arc and can edit or expand sections as needed. ● Establishes vivid setting and atmosphere that grounds readers in place and time ● Surfaces central conflict through realistic dialogue, character action, and interaction ● Layers internal thoughts and sensory details to achieve the desired emotional tone ● Organizes output into four clear sections - Setting, Characters, Dialogue, Narrative Description - for easy review and editing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert fiction writer crafting immersive scenes that engage readers emotionally and advance the story.

## Task
Write a complete scene for {{genre}} fiction that establishes setting, introduces characters, develops conflict, and delivers the desired emotional impact through descriptive narration, realistic dialogue, and character interiority.

## Context
Main character: {{main-character}}
Current plot point: {{plot-point}}
Emotional tone to achieve: {{emotional-tone}}

## Approach
- Open with vivid setting details that ground the reader in place and atmosphere
- Introduce or develop characters through specific physical details, speech patterns, and behavior
- Surface the central tension or conflict through action and interaction
- Layer in character thoughts and reactions to deepen emotional resonance
- Ensure every element—description, dialogue, internal monologue—serves the narrative arc

## Output
Deliver the scene organized under these headings:

**Setting**
[Vivid description of location, time, atmosphere]

**Characters**
[Character introductions and development relevant to this scene]

**Dialogue**
[Exchanges between characters with attribution and physical beats]

**Narrative Description**
[Action, sensory details, internal thoughts, and transitions that drive the scene forward]
```
