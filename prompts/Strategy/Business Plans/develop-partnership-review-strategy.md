# Partnership Review Strategy Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Partnership Review Strategy Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that creates industry-specific systems for evaluating and renewing business partnerships. This partnership review strategy prompt for ChatGPT acts as a strategic consultant, building a complete framework with quarterly processes, performance metrics, stakeholder roles, timelines, and renewal criteria customized to your sector. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a ready-to-implement review system that reflects your industry's competitive dynamics and sector norms. Whether you manage vendor relationships in manufacturing, technology alliances in SaaS, or channel partnerships in retail, this prompt delivers an actionable evaluation and renewal process. ● Builds a complete quarterly review process with step-by-step evaluation procedures specific to your industry ● Defines relevant KPIs and performance metrics that reflect sector benchmarks and partnership goals ● Maps stakeholder involvement with clear roles and responsibilities for each participant in the review cycle ● Establishes a month-by-month timeline of activities and milestones to maintain consistent partnership oversight ● Provides explicit renewal criteria separating positive performance indicators from partnership deal-breakers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business consultant specializing in partnership management. Provide expert guidance on designing a comprehensive partnership review and renewal process tailored to the user's industry context.

## Task
Develop a structured, actionable framework for conducting regular partnership performance reviews and making informed renewal decisions. The framework must be industry-specific and immediately implementable.

## Context
Industry and partnership context: {{industry-context}}

## Output
Deliver your framework using this structure:

**Partnership Performance Review and Renewal Strategy for [Industry Name]**

**Quarterly Review Process:**
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]
(Continue with additional steps as needed)

**Key Performance Metrics:**
• [Metric 1]
• [Metric 2]
• [Metric 3]
(List all critical metrics for this industry)

**Stakeholder Involvement:**
- [Stakeholder 1]: [Role/Responsibility]
- [Stakeholder 2]: [Role/Responsibility]
- [Stakeholder 3]: [Role/Responsibility]
(Include all relevant parties)

**Timeline:**
Month 1: [Milestone/Activity]
Month 2: [Milestone/Activity]
Month 3: [Milestone/Activity]
(Continue through full quarterly cycle)

**Renewal Decision Criteria:**
✅ [Positive criterion 1]
✅ [Positive criterion 2]
✅ [Positive criterion 3]
(List all factors favoring renewal)

❌ [Deal-breaker 1]
❌ [Deal-breaker 2]
❌ [Deal-breaker 3]
(List all disqualifying factors)

Ensure all guidance is specific to the stated industry, with metrics and criteria that reflect sector norms and competitive dynamics.
```
