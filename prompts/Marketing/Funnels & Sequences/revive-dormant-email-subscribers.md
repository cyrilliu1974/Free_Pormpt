# Dormant Email Subscriber Re-Engagement Campaign

## 簡介

The Dormant Email Subscriber Re-Engagement Campaign is a free AI prompt that designs customized email sequences to revive inactive subscriber lists for marketers and email campaign managers. This dormant subscriber re-engagement prompt for ChatGPT analyzes abandonment patterns, dormancy duration, and business context to generate complete multi-phase campaigns spanning 3 to 15 emails. It determines optimal campaign length based on how long subscribers have been inactive (30 days to over a year), then crafts each phase with specific psychological triggers, subject line approaches, messaging hooks, and calls-to-action designed to rebuild trust and relevance. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing campaign overviews, phase-by-phase breakdowns with timing and success metrics, and implementation notes including segmentation strategies and A/B testing opportunities. Marketing teams use it to transform dormant lists into engaged audiences without resorting to aggressive tactics, matching campaign complexity to relationship history and conversion goals. ● Scales campaign complexity automatically from 3-phase quick wins for recently dormant lists to 15-phase deep reconnection journeys for long-abandoned subscribers ● Provides subscriber psychology diagnosis and resurrection narrative frameworks that identify disengagement reasons and emotional barriers ● Delivers phase-specific subject lines, psychological triggers, messaging elements, and CTA strategies with precise timing recommendations ● Includes segmentation guidance, A/B testing opportunities, and off-ramp criteria to maintain list health and avoid fatigue ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert email re-engagement strategist specializing in reviving dormant subscriber lists through psychology-driven campaigns that prioritize authentic connection over aggressive marketing tactics.

## Task

Create a multi-phase email re-engagement campaign tailored to the subscriber abandonment patterns and business context provided. Determine the optimal number of phases (3-15) based on list dormancy, relationship history, and conversion goals, then design each phase with specific psychological triggers, messaging hooks, and engagement strategies.

## Context

Analyze the situation using this framework:

1. **Subscriber psychology** – Why did they disengage? What emotional or practical barriers exist?
2. **Abandonment patterns** – Dormancy duration, last engagement signals, frequency fatigue
3. **Resurrection narrative** – The story arc that rebuilds trust and relevance
4. **Strategic hooks** – Compelling reasons to re-engage at each phase
5. **Momentum sequencing** – Pacing and escalation across the campaign
6. **Reconnection metrics** – How to measure emotional and behavioral shifts

Scale the campaign complexity to match the scenario:

- **Quick wins**: 3-5 phases (30-day sprint) for recently dormant lists
- **Standard revival**: 6-8 phases (60-day journey) for moderate disengagement
- **Deep reconnection**: 9-12 phases (90-day transformation) for long-dormant subscribers
- **Complete resurrection**: 13-15 phases (120+ days) for deeply abandoned lists

## Input Required

Provide the following details:

{{campaign-context}}

*Include: dormancy duration (30 days / 90 days / 6+ months / 1+ year), business/content type, previous email frequency, available engagement data (opens, clicks, purchases), and primary goal (sales, trust-building, or list hygiene).*

## Output

Deliver a complete re-engagement campaign structure:

**Campaign Overview**
- Recommended phase count and timeline
- Subscriber psychology diagnosis
- Core resurrection narrative

**Phase-by-Phase Breakdown**

For each phase, provide:
- Phase name and objective
- Timing (days from campaign start)
- Email subject line approach
- Psychological trigger being activated
- Key messaging elements
- Call-to-action strategy
- Success metrics

**Implementation Notes**
- Segmentation recommendations
- A/B testing opportunities
- Off-ramp criteria (when to remove non-responders)
```
