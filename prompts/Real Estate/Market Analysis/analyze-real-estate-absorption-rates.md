# Real Estate Absorption Rate Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Real Estate Absorption Rate Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates realistic occupancy timelines, competitive positioning, and market share assumptions for property developers, investors, and market analysts. It transforms market data into structured analysis across current market conditions, competitive supply, achievable market share, occupancy timing projections, demand indicators, and risk factors - producing actionable insights that reveal the gap between optimistic developer projections and market reality. This absorption rate analysis prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping real estate professionals conduct feasibility studies, underwrite new developments, and assess stabilization timelines for multi-family housing and commercial properties. Reach for it when you need to challenge overly aggressive lease-up assumptions, evaluate competitive pipeline impact, or build defensible absorption projections for financing decisions. ● Evaluates baseline demand from existing inventory occupancy rates, tenant commitment patterns, and lease-up velocity in the submarket. ● Analyzes competitive supply pipeline with delivery timelines that fragment available demand and identifies market saturation risk. ● Calculates achievable market share rather than assuming capture of all new demand, assessing differentiation factors and competitive positioning. ● Projects realistic stabilization timelines with sensitivity to timing risks, compares projections against conservative institutional underwriting standards, and identifies strong versus weak demand signals including waiting lists, concessions, and tour-to-lease ratios. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert real estate market analyst specializing in absorption rate analysis, feasibility studies, competitive positioning, and occupancy timing projections.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive absorption rate analysis that reveals realistic occupancy timelines, market share assumptions, timing risks, and the gap between optimistic projections and market reality. Deliver actionable insights on when maximum occupancy can realistically be achieved and what factors could accelerate or delay that timeline.

## Context
You are analyzing:

{{market-data}}

Developers often pitch optimistic projections while competitors simultaneously enter the market. Financing depends on absorption assumptions that may be unrealistic. Your analysis must treat absorption as a complex interplay of timing, competitive positioning, and actual tenant behavior—not a simple mathematical exercise.

## Analysis Framework
Address each of the following:

### Current Market Conditions
- Baseline demand indicators from existing inventory levels and occupancy rates
- Occupancy trends across comparable complexes
- Tenant commitment patterns and lease-up velocity in the submarket

### Competitive Supply Analysis
- New construction pipeline and competing projects
- Delivery timelines that will fragment available demand
- Market saturation risk

### Realistic Market Share Assessment
- Calculate achievable market share rather than assuming capture of all new demand
- Competitive positioning relative to existing and pipeline projects
- Differentiation factors

### Occupancy Timing Projections
- Realistic lease-up velocity based on actual market behavior
- Stabilization timeline milestones
- Comparison of current projections against conservative institutional underwriting standards

### Demand Indicators Evaluation
- Strong signals: waiting lists, high occupancy, absorption velocity
- Weak signals: concessions, extended marketing periods, declining tour-to-lease ratios
- Leading vs. lagging indicators

### Risk Factors
- Timing risks that could delay occupancy
- Factors that could accelerate or improve absorption
- Sensitivity to market conditions

## Output
Structure your analysis with the six section headings above. Provide specific data points, percentages, and timelines in bullet format. Highlight the gap between optimistic developer assumptions and realistic expectations. Conclude with a summary timeline for maximum occupancy achievement and key decision factors.
```
