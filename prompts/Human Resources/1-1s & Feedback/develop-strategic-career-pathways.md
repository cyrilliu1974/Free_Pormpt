# Performance Review to Career Growth Framework

## 簡介

The Performance Review to Career Growth Framework is a free AI prompt that helps HR leaders and managers convert traditional performance evaluations into strategic career acceleration systems. This performance review prompt for ChatGPT identifies five concrete approaches that connect current employee performance with future advancement opportunities, moving beyond backward-looking assessments to forward-focused development plans. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and produces a tailored framework with clear strategy headings, explanations of how each approach bridges performance and career progression, and actionable implementation steps in bullet format. Use it when designing or overhauling performance management systems, preparing manager training on career development conversations, or aligning individual growth trajectories with succession planning and leadership pipeline needs. ● Aligns individual performance metrics with organizational objectives and long-term career paths. ● Addresses skill development trajectories, leadership pipeline requirements, and succession planning. ● Delivers five strategic approaches, each with implementation steps tailored to your organizational context. ● Transforms reviews from past-focused reporting into forward-looking career acceleration conversations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources strategist and organizational development consultant specializing in transforming performance management into strategic career acceleration systems.

## Task
Identify 5 strategic ways to tie performance reviews to long-term career growth paths. Create a comprehensive framework that connects current performance with future advancement opportunities, transforming reviews from administrative exercises into powerful career development tools that employees value and engage with.

## Context
Most performance reviews fail because they focus on past achievements rather than future potential. Effective systems must:
- Align individual growth with organizational objectives
- Address the intersection of performance metrics and career progression pathways
- Consider skill development trajectories, leadership pipeline requirements, and succession planning needs
- Motivate employees while serving strategic business goals

{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Provide 5 strategic approaches, each with:
- A clear heading naming the strategy
- A brief explanation of how it connects performance to career growth
- Actionable implementation steps in bullet point format

Focus on practical, implementable solutions tailored to the organizational context provided.
```
