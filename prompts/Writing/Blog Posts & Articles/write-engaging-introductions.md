# Blog Introduction Writer for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Blog Introduction Writer is a free AI prompt that generates compelling blog post introductions for content creators, marketers, and bloggers. It produces a structured 150-250 word opening paragraph that begins with a hook, introduces the core problem or question, previews two to three key points the article will cover, and concludes with a clear thesis statement. This blog introduction prompt for ChatGPT works by applying dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically from the previous one, creating smooth transitions and coherent syntactic relationships. You provide the topic, target audience, writing style, and niche, and the prompt returns a polished introductory paragraph ready to publish. Use it when you need to overcome blank-page syndrome, maintain a consistent voice across blog posts, or quickly draft engaging openings that hold reader attention. Writers, content strategists, and marketing teams use it to speed up content production without sacrificing quality. ● Creates a hook tied directly to your topic to capture attention immediately ● Structures the introduction around a clear problem-preview-thesis flow ● Applies dependency grammar for smooth, logical sentence-to-sentence transitions ● Adapts tone and style to match your audience, niche, and brand voice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content writer specializing in compelling blog post introductions.

## Task
Write an engaging blog post introduction that hooks the reader and sets the tone for the article. Structure your sentences using dependency grammar principles, where each word's relationship to others creates clear, coherent flow.

## Context
Blog post topic: {{topic}}
Target audience: {{audience}}
Writing style: {{style}}
Blog niche: {{niche}}

## Output
Deliver a single, well-structured introductory paragraph (150-250 words) that:

1. Opens with a compelling hook directly tied to the topic
2. Introduces the main problem or question the post addresses
3. Previews 2-3 key points or solutions the article will explore
4. Concludes with a clear thesis statement that captures the post's core argument

Use dependency grammar to create smooth transitions—each sentence should build logically on the previous one through clear syntactic relationships. Vary sentence structure while maintaining coherent connections between clauses and phrases.
```
