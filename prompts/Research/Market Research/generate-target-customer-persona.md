# Target Customer Persona Generator for Marketing

## 簡介

The Target Customer Persona Generator for Marketing is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, narrative-style customer personas for marketing strategists and business teams. This customer persona prompt for ChatGPT takes your business description and audience research data and transforms it into a relatable profile complete with a name, story, and actionable insights across five key dimensions: demographics, psychographics, behavioral patterns, motivations, and pain points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing personas that go beyond dry statistics to tell the story of how a real person discovers and interacts with your offering. Marketing teams use it to align messaging, advertising creative, content strategy, and product positioning around a shared understanding of the target customer. ● Structures personas across demographics, psychographics, behaviors, motivations, and pain points in one cohesive narrative ● Assigns each persona a name and day-in-the-life story that makes abstract audience segments feel tangible and relatable ● Accepts any available audience research data and business description to generate contextualized, relevant profiles ● Produces marketing-ready output that teams can reference when developing messaging, content, advertising, and product strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing strategist specializing in customer persona development using quantitative and qualitative research methods.

## Task
Create a comprehensive customer persona that captures demographics, psychographics, behavioral patterns, motivations, and pain points. Present it as a narrative profile with a name and story that makes the persona relatable to marketing teams.

## Context
Business: {{business-description}}

Known audience insights: {{audience-research}}
(Include any available information about demographics, interests, lifestyle choices, behaviors, purchasing motivations, and pain points related to your business or industry.)

## Output
Structure the persona using these dimensions:

**Demographics**: Age, gender, location, education level, income bracket.

**Psychographics**: Lifestyle choices, interests, hobbies, values, beliefs—what drives them and how they view the world.

**Behavioral Patterns**: Purchasing behaviors, brand interactions, online activity (websites, social media), loyalty tendencies.

**Motivations**: What compels them to purchase—solving problems, fulfilling needs, achieving outcomes.

**Pain Points**: Challenges and frustrations your product or service can address.

**Narrative**: Give the persona a name and describe a day in their life, including how they discover and interact with your offering.

Example format:
*"Emma is a 30-year-old digital marketing manager living in a metropolitan area, with a Master's degree and earning $75,000 a year. She values work-life balance, is interested in technology and personal development, and prefers businesses that advocate for sustainability. Emma spends significant time on LinkedIn and industry blogs, is a loyal customer once she believes in a brand, and frequently purchases books and courses on marketing and self-improvement. She is motivated by advancing her career while maintaining balance. Emma struggles to find advanced marketing courses that fit her busy schedule and is frustrated by the lack of eco-friendly office supplies."*

Deliver the persona as a cohesive narrative profile that marketing teams can reference when developing strategies, messaging, and campaigns.
```
