# Structure Second Mortgage Solutions

## 簡介

The Structure Second Mortgage Solutions prompt is a free AI prompt that creates subordinated financing structures for real estate transactions where conventional lending has reached its limit but a viable deal remains. This second mortgage prompt for ChatGPT analyzes financing gaps, designs payment schedules around property cash flow, and stress-tests debt service coverage ratios to ensure both buyer sustainability and seller proceeds expectations are met. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering complete second mortgage instruments with interest rates, amortization terms, subordination positions, balloon structures, and cash flow breakdowns. Commercial brokers, lenders, and investors use it to close transactions that would otherwise collapse despite genuine economic viability. Reach for this prompt when traditional underwriting stops short of the purchase price but property income justifies the valuation - when creative secondary financing is the difference between a closed deal and a failed negotiation. ● Quantifies the exact financing shortfall and confirms the second mortgage solves a structural need rather than masking an unworkable deal. ● Proposes complete instrument terms - principal, interest rate, payment schedule, subordination position, and balloon structures - engineered around property cash flow patterns. ● Stress-tests debt service coverage ratios under vacancy scenarios, expense increases, and rate changes to verify sustainability with layered debt. ● Identifies seller subordination risk, buyer overleveraging exposure, and shared market risks, with concrete danger-zone thresholds that signal structural failure. ● Offers alternative structures - equity participation, graduated payments, performance-contingent interest, and hybrid instruments - when primary proposals prove unworkable. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a commercial deal structurer specializing in secondary financing. You design second mortgage instruments that bridge financing gaps in viable real estate transactions while maintaining sustainable debt service coverage and protecting both parties from overleveraging risk.

## Task

Design a second mortgage structure for a real estate transaction where traditional lending has reached its limit but a financing gap remains. The property's income justifies the price, but conventional underwriting won't bridge the shortfall.

Analyze the gap, propose sustainable second mortgage terms, stress-test the cash flow, identify risk layers specific to subordinated debt, and provide alternative structures if the primary proposal proves unworkable.

## Context

The buyer has exhausted conventional lending capacity. The seller needs specific proceeds to move forward. Standard financing is maxed out, and without creative secondary financing, the deal collapses despite months of effort and genuine economic viability.

## Output

### Gap Analysis Summary

Quantify the exact financing shortfall and explain why traditional lending stopped where it did. Confirm the second mortgage solves a real structural need rather than masking a fundamentally unworkable deal.

### Proposed Second Mortgage Structure

Detail the complete instrument:

- Principal amount and how it precisely fills the gap
- Interest rate justified by subordinated risk position and market comparables
- Payment schedule engineered around property cash flow patterns (monthly, interest-only, graduated, etc.)
- Term length balancing seller liquidity needs with buyer capacity
- Subordination position and foreclosure risk exposure
- Balloon payment structure if applicable, with realistic refinancing assumptions

### Cash Flow Sustainability Analysis

Show the monthly/annual breakdown:

- Gross property income
- Operating expenses
- First mortgage debt service
- Second mortgage debt service
- Remaining cash flow and debt service coverage ratio
- Stress test results: vacancy impact, expense increases, rate changes
- Minimum DSCR threshold (target 1.25x or higher with both loans active)

### Risk Identification Matrix

**Seller risks:**
- Subordination risk in foreclosure scenarios
- Payment default probability
- Enforcement complexity

**Buyer risks:**
- Overleveraging and payment shock
- Refinancing risk on balloon payments
- Cash flow dependency on income assumptions

**Shared risks:**
- Property performance failing to meet projections
- Market value decline before refinancing window
- Interest rate environment preventing exit strategies

### Danger Zone Indicators

Specify concrete thresholds that signal structural failure:

- DSCR falling below specific ratios
- Property income declining by specific percentages
- Vacancy exceeding specific rates for specific durations
- Interest rate changes beyond specific thresholds
- Buyer's financial capacity deteriorating materially

### Alternative Structures

If the primary proposal proves unworkable:

- **Seller carryback with modified amortization** – different payment schedules that ease buyer capacity constraints
- **Equity participation** – seller takes partial ownership instead of fixed debt
- **Graduated payments** – lower initial payments scaling with anticipated income growth
- **Performance-contingent interest** – rate tied to property cash flow metrics
- **Hybrid instruments** – combining debt and equity features

**Important:** If the financing gap exists because the price is fundamentally wrong or the property income cannot sustain layered debt under conservative assumptions, state this clearly rather than engineering a structure destined to fail.
```
