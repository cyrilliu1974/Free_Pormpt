# Beauty Product Application Image Generator

## 簡介

The Beauty Product Application Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional studio beauty photography series showing cosmetics in use for brands, marketers, and creative teams. This beauty product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models produces three distinct high-end shots: an extreme macro of lipstick application, a tight close-up of mascara being applied, and a frontal eyeliner application view. Each image maintains the same model across all three frames, uses shallow depth of field to emphasize the product and application area, and ensures brand names remain sharp, readable, and fully visible. The prompt delivers 8K commercial-ready quality with studio lighting, natural skin texture, and editorial makeup styling that mirrors luxury cosmetics advertising.
