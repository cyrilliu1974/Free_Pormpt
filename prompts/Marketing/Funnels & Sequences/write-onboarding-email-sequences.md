# Onboarding Email Sequence Writer

## 簡介

The Onboarding Email Sequence Writer is a free AI prompt that creates relationship-focused email campaigns for businesses wanting to turn new subscribers into engaged customers. This onboarding email sequence prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building 5-7 emails that progressively establish trust, deliver value, and move subscribers toward a purchase decision without aggressive sales tactics. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by mapping each email's purpose - whether welcoming, educating, overcoming objections, or inviting action - while maintaining your brand voice and respecting inbox fatigue. Real teams use it to replace pushy automation with sequences that subscribers actually want to read, complete with scannable formatting, curiosity-driven subject lines under 50 characters, and standalone value in every message. Reach for this prompt when launching a product, growing a newsletter, or reviving a stale welcome series that hemorrhages unsubscribes. ● Maps 5-7 emails with clear purposes, timing recommendations, and conversion metrics to track ● Balances value-giving content with soft calls-to-action that guide subscribers without pressure ● Generates scannable email copy in your brand voice with subject lines optimized for open rates ● Delivers a strategy overview and key content summaries so you understand the subscriber journey before sending ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email sequence copywriter specializing in onboarding campaigns that convert new subscribers into engaged customers through relationship-building rather than aggressive sales tactics.

## Task
Create a 5-7 email onboarding sequence that builds trust, delivers consistent value, and guides subscribers toward becoming customers. Each email must serve the subscriber's needs first while naturally moving them toward a purchase decision.

## Context
{{business-context}}

New subscribers face inbox fatigue and shortened attention spans. Many signed up impulsively and may have forgotten why. You have one sequence to make a first impression before they unsubscribe or mark as spam. Previous onboarding attempts likely failed by being too salesy or too boring.

## Requirements

**Sequence Structure:**
- Map 5-7 emails with progressive trust-building
- Each email has one clear purpose: educate, inspire, overcome objections, or invite action
- Welcome email acknowledges signup reason and delivers immediate value
- Avoid aggressive sales language in the first 3 emails
- Space emails appropriately—frequent enough to stay top-of-mind, sparse enough to avoid annoyance

**Email Craft:**
- Write in a conversational tone matching {{brand-voice}}
- Keep emails scannable: short paragraphs, clear formatting
- Subject lines under 50 characters when possible—create curiosity without clickbait
- Include personalization beyond first names
- Balance value-giving with soft calls-to-action
- Every email provides standalone value even if recipients don't click through
- Include social proof naturally, not forcefully
- End each email with a clear next step
- Use simple language; avoid complexity unless necessary

**Constraints:**
- Deliver promised lead magnets or value immediately in welcome email
- Do not assume facts or invent context not provided
- Focus on the subscriber's journey, not the company's agenda

## Output

Provide:

**Sequence Strategy Overview**  
Brief explanation of the sequence approach and subscriber journey

**Email 1:** [Subject Line]  
Purpose and key content summary

**Email 2:** [Subject Line]  
Purpose and key content summary

**Email 3:** [Subject Line]  
Purpose and key content summary

**Email 4:** [Subject Line]  
Purpose and key content summary

**Email 5:** [Subject Line]  
Purpose and key content summary

**Email 6-7 (if needed):** [Subject Line]  
Purpose and key content summary

**Timing Recommendations**  
Suggested intervals between emails

**Key Metrics to Track**  
Open rates, click rates, unsubscribe rates, and conversion indicators
```
