# Executive Blog Post Writer for Leadership Insights

## 簡介

The Executive Blog Post Writer for Leadership Insights is a free AI prompt that helps senior leaders craft authentic corporate blog posts sharing their leadership philosophy, company values, and strategic plans. This executive blog prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a structured 600–900 word post built on dependency grammar principles - each sentence flows logically from a clear main idea, with supporting details that reinforce it. You provide your company and industry context, and the prompt guides you to write in genuine executive voice: insightful, strategic, and personal without corporate jargon. It prompts you to include specific anecdotes, concrete examples of values in action, and two strategic initiatives tied to your mission. The result is a blog post that resonates with employees, peers, and stakeholders. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to communicate leadership perspective, announce strategic direction, or reinforce company culture through an executive voice that feels real and grounded. ● Guides you through five sections - introduction, leadership philosophy, company values, strategic vision, and conclusion - each with clear instructions for what to include. ● Prompts for specific anecdotes and concrete examples instead of generic platitudes, making the post more credible and engaging. ● Uses dependency grammar framework so every paragraph flows logically and reinforces your main message. ● Produces 600–900 word posts suitable for internal newsletters, company blogs, LinkedIn articles, and investor updates. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a senior executive writing an authentic corporate blog post that shares your perspective on leadership, company values, and strategic plans for the future.

## Task
Write a 600–900 word executive blog post structured using dependency grammar principles—each sentence and paragraph should flow logically from a clear main idea, with supporting details that depend on and reinforce it.

## Context
Company and industry context: {{company-industry-context}}

The post should reflect genuine executive voice—insightful, strategic, and personal without being overly formal. Use specific anecdotes and concrete examples rather than generic platitudes.

## Output
Structure the blog post with these sections:

**Introduction**
- Open with a compelling hook that draws readers in
- State your main thesis about leadership and the company's direction
- Preview the key themes you'll explore

**Leadership Philosophy**
- Lead with your core leadership philosophy
- Share a specific anecdote that demonstrates your leadership approach in action
- Describe a meaningful lesson you've learned about leadership
- Articulate your vision for developing leadership capability across the organization

**Company Values**
- Explain why values matter in guiding the organization
- Highlight two core company values, explaining concretely why each matters and how it shows up in daily work
- Illustrate how these values guide difficult decisions or strategic choices

**Strategic Vision**
- Present your vision for the company's future
- Describe two specific strategic initiatives underway or planned
- Connect these initiatives explicitly to the company's mission and values

**Conclusion**
- Synthesize two key takeaway messages
- Close with an inspiring, actionable call to action for readers

Use clear headings. Write in first person. Avoid jargon, buzzwords, and empty corporate speak—be specific and authentic.
```
