# Investor Pitch Deck Creator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Investor Pitch Deck Creator is a free AI prompt that builds persuasive, investor-ready pitch deck outlines for entrepreneurs and business founders seeking capital. This investor pitch deck prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce a complete presentation structure with clear sections for problem statement, solution, market opportunity, business model, go-to-market strategy, competitive landscape, traction or validation, team overview, financial projections, and funding request with ROI narrative. It works by taking your business idea, target market, competitive advantage, financial projections, and funding needs, then organizing them into a logical, data-supported deck that addresses the key criteria investors evaluate. Founders use it to prepare seed rounds, Series A presentations, or strategic partner pitches. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform your business concept and financial data into a coherent, compelling investor story that highlights market potential and differentiation. ● Produces slide-by-slide outlines with bullet points for problem, solution, market size, business model, and competitive positioning ● Integrates your financial projections, unit economics, and growth trajectory into a cohesive funding narrative ● Ensures logical flow between sections so each slide builds investor confidence and leads to a clear funding ask ● Adapts to different business models and industries by tailoring market analysis and go-to-market strategies to your target customer segments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist and pitch deck creator specializing in investor presentations.

## Task
Develop a compelling investor pitch deck that persuasively communicates the business value proposition to potential investors. Structure the deck with clear, concise, and impactful content addressing the key elements investors evaluate. Ensure each section is well-developed, logically connected, and supported by relevant data or examples.

## Context
Business overview:
{{business-idea}}

Target market and customer segments:
{{target-market}}

Competitive advantage and differentiation:
{{competitive-advantage}}

Financial projections (revenue, costs, growth trajectory, unit economics):
{{financial-projections}}

Funding request (amount, use of funds, milestones):
{{funding-request}}

## Output
Provide a structured outline with main sections and key points for each slide. Use bullet points for clarity and conciseness. Cover essential pitch deck components: problem statement, solution, market opportunity, business model, go-to-market strategy, competitive landscape, traction/validation, team, financials, and funding ask with clear ROI narrative.
```
