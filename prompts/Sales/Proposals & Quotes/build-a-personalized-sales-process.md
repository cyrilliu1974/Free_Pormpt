# Sales Process Management Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Sales Process Management Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured sales process table tailored to your specific business context and sales cycle needs. This sales process prompt for ChatGPT produces an 8+ stage framework table that spans your entire sales cycle - from lead generation through post-sale follow-up. Each stage includes defined activities, responsible roles, measurable KPIs with target values and measurement frequency, required tools and resources, and a 1-5 priority rating. The output is formatted as a clear table that gives your team actionable structure and measurable accountability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and works for B2B sales teams, SaaS companies, service businesses, and any organization that needs to document and optimize their sales operations. This prompt is for sales leaders, operations managers, and founders who need to standardize their sales workflow, onboard new sales reps, or audit their current process for gaps and inefficiencies. ● Outputs a table with minimum 8 sales stages, each with key activities, responsible roles, KPIs with targets and measurement frequency, tools, and priority level. ● Formats KPIs as [Metric Name] - [Target Value] - [Measurement Frequency] for clear, trackable goals. ● Uses a 1-5 priority scale (Critical to Optional) to help teams focus on high-impact activities first. ● Tailors every recommendation to the business context you provide, avoiding one-size-fits-all templates. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a sales strategy expert who designs optimized sales process management frameworks.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive sales process management framework tailored to the business context below. Present it as a table with these columns:

**Sales Process Stage | Key Activities | Responsible Roles | KPIs/Metrics | Required Tools/Resources | Priority (1-5)**

## Requirements

- Include minimum 8 stages covering the full sales cycle from lead generation through post-sale follow-up
- Define clear ownership and measurable outcomes for each stage
- Format KPIs as: [Metric Name] - [Target Value] - [Measurement Frequency]
  - Example: Qualified Leads - 50 per month - Measured monthly
- Use priority scale:
  - 1 = Critical
  - 2 = High
  - 3 = Medium
  - 4 = Low
  - 5 = Optional
- Tailor recommendations specifically to the business context provided—avoid generic solutions

## Business Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Deliver the framework as a clear, well-structured table. Ensure easy readability with distinct delineation between stages.
```
