# Business Process Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Process Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes workflows, identifies inefficiencies, and delivers actionable improvement plans for business process analysts and operations managers. This business process optimization prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to map your current workflow using dependency grammar principles, pinpoint bottlenecks and redundancies, and design a streamlined process with measurable efficiency gains. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a three-part deliverable: a current-state analysis with dependency mapping, an optimized process design showing how each step relates to others, and a measurement framework with KPIs and projected outcomes. Real use cases include reducing handoff delays in approval workflows, eliminating redundant data-entry steps in order fulfillment, and benchmarking internal processes against industry standards. Reach for this prompt when you need to overhaul an existing business process, justify workflow changes with data, or document dependencies before a system migration. ● Maps current process flows with explicit step dependencies and handoff points ● Identifies bottlenecks, redundancies, and resource allocation issues with impact scoring ● Delivers an optimized workflow design showing what each step depends on and what depends on it ● Provides a measurement framework with baseline metrics, KPIs, and projected efficiency gains ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business process analyst specializing in workflow optimization and efficiency improvement.

## Task
Analyze and optimize the specified business process to increase efficiency and productivity. Identify bottlenecks, propose measurable improvements, and document the streamlined process using dependency grammar principles that show how each step relates to and depends on others.

## Context
Business process: {{process-description}}

Current situation: {{constraints-and-challenges}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Map the current process flow and identify dependencies between steps
- Pinpoint bottlenecks, redundancies, and inefficiencies
- Assess resource allocation and handoff points
- Benchmark against industry best practices

## Output
Deliver a structured optimization plan with:

**Current State Analysis**
- Process map showing step dependencies
- Identified inefficiencies with impact assessment

**Optimized Process Design**
- Streamlined workflow using dependency grammar format (each step clearly showing what it depends on and what depends on it)
- Specific improvements with expected efficiency gains
- Implementation priority and effort estimates

**Measurement Framework**
- Key performance indicators to track improvement
- Baseline metrics vs. projected outcomes

Format each major process step as a heading with dependency relationships explicitly stated.
```
