# Equal Opportunity Policy Template for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Equal Opportunity Policy Template for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that generates structured, legally compliant equal opportunity policies for organizations building or updating their diversity and inclusion frameworks. This equal opportunity policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete policy document with twelve numbered sections covering protected classes, hiring practices, compensation equity, workplace accommodations, prohibited behaviors, reporting mechanisms, anti-retaliation provisions, investigation procedures, accountability measures, and diversity tracking systems. It analyzes your organization profile to identify compliance gaps and tailors language to meet both EEOC legal standards and UN Women Gender Equality frameworks. HR directors use it to draft policies that satisfy regulatory scrutiny while remaining actionable for managers at every organizational level. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish or revise equal opportunity policies that balance legal defensibility with practical implementation across recruiting, promotion, and complaint-resolution processes. ● Defines protected classes and non-discriminatory procedures for hiring, training, promotion, and compensation with objective criteria and transparent processes. ● Establishes confidential reporting channels, anti-retaliation protections, and investigation procedures that meet EEOC timeliness and impartiality requirements. ● Includes measurable diversity goals, KPI tracking systems, and accountability measures with consequences for violations at all organizational levels. ● Delivers professional policy language with numbered sections and bullet points that HR teams can implement and auditors can verify. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an employment law and diversity policy expert drafting compliant equal opportunity policies that balance legal requirements with practical implementation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive equal opportunity policy that meets U.S. EEOC standards and reflects UN Women Gender Equality frameworks. The policy must be legally sound and actionable for HR teams at all organizational levels.

## Context
Organization: {{organization-profile}}

Analyze compliance gaps in the current approach. Address the heightened regulatory environment where employment discrimination claims are rising and stakeholders demand measurable diversity progress.

## Output
Structure the policy document with numbered sections and subsections covering:

1. **Protected Classes & Definitions** – Clear legal definitions aligned with EEOC standards
2. **Hiring Practices** – Non-discriminatory recruitment, selection, and onboarding procedures
3. **Training & Development** – Equal access to professional growth opportunities
4. **Promotion & Advancement** – Objective criteria and transparent processes
5. **Compensation Equity** – Pay parity analysis and remediation procedures
6. **Workplace Accommodations** – Reasonable accommodation request and approval processes
7. **Prohibited Behaviors** – Specific examples of discrimination and harassment
8. **Reporting Mechanisms** – Multiple confidential channels for complaints
9. **Anti-Retaliation Provisions** – Protection for those who report violations
10. **Investigation Procedures** – Timely, impartial process meeting EEOC requirements
11. **Accountability Measures** – Consequences for policy violations at all levels
12. **Diversity Metrics & Tracking** – Measurable goals, KPIs, and progress monitoring systems

Use professional policy language with bullet points for clarity. Ensure each section is implementable and auditable.
```
