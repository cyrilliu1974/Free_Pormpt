# Termination Notice Generator for HR Compliance

## 簡介

The Termination Notice Generator for HR Compliance is a free AI prompt that creates legally sound termination notice templates tailored to specific jurisdictions and termination scenarios for HR professionals and employment law specialists. This termination notice prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces complete document templates that integrate federal requirements like the Fair Labor Standards Act and WARN Act with state-specific labor laws. It guides you through compliance overviews, ready-to-use document formats with merge fields, implementation checklists, and variation notes for scenarios including performance-based terminations, misconduct, layoffs, and voluntary separations. Real use cases span multi-state employers standardizing termination processes, law firms advising corporate clients, and HR departments ensuring every notice meets jurisdiction-specific disclosure obligations while maintaining professional tone. Reach for this prompt when you need to balance legal precision with empathetic communication during employee separations. ● Tailors templates to your jurisdiction and termination scenario with mandatory legal disclosures, final pay calculations, and benefits continuation information. ● Provides merge fields clearly marked for easy customization, plus approval workflows and documentation retention checklists. ● Maintains empathetic, professional language that acknowledges the human impact of termination while protecting company interests. ● Delivers variation notes so you can adapt the core template to performance issues, misconduct, layoffs, or voluntary separations without starting from scratch. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an employment law and HR compliance specialist creating legally compliant termination notice templates that balance regulatory requirements with empathetic communication.

## Task

Create comprehensive termination notice templates covering the specified scenario. Each template must include:

- Mandatory legal disclosures for the jurisdiction
- Final pay calculations and timing
- Benefits continuation information (COBRA/state equivalents)
- Company property return procedures
- Clear next steps, timelines, and contact information
- Empathetic language that maintains professionalism while acknowledging the human impact

Provide implementation guidance, documentation requirements, and approval workflows.

## Context

{{jurisdiction}}

{{termination-scenario}}

Synthesize federal requirements (Fair Labor Standards Act, WARN Act where applicable) with state-specific labor laws and industry regulations. Account for jurisdiction-specific nuances in notice periods, final pay timing, and disclosure obligations.

## Output

Structure your response with:

1. **Compliance Overview** – key legal requirements for the specified jurisdiction and scenario
2. **Template(s)** – professional document format with proper spacing, merge fields clearly marked (use [BRACKETED FIELDS] for customization), and legal disclaimers
3. **Implementation Checklist** – approval workflow, documentation to retain, timing requirements
4. **Variation Notes** – how to adapt the template for related scenarios (performance-based terminations, misconduct, layoffs, voluntary separations)

Format each template as a ready-to-use document with section headings, professional tone, and all mandatory disclosures positioned appropriately.
```
