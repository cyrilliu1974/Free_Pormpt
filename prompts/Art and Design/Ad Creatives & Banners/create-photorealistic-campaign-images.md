# Photorealistic Campaign Image Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Campaign Image Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates polished, commercial-grade campaign photography with floating emoji overlays for marketers and creative teams. This photorealistic campaign image prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces medium-shot portraits of a styled subject holding a takeaway coffee cup, surrounded by 3D-rendered floating emoji icons (coffee cups, hearts, smiley faces) against a softly blurred urban morning backdrop. The prompt specifies warm morning sun key lighting, subtle lens bloom, shallow depth-of-field bokeh shot on 85mm f/1.4, and professional color grading for clean commercial realism. Use it to create lifestyle campaign visuals, product hero images, social media ads, or brand storytelling content that blends real-world authenticity with playful digital elements. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping campaign imagery that balances premium aesthetic quality with approachable, emoji-driven relatability - ideal for beverage brands, app launches, lifestyle products, or influencer collaborations. ● Customize subject styling, outfit, and color palette with the {{subject-and-styling}} variable for brand alignment. ● Control floating emoji density, type, and 3D rendering style to match campaign tone. ● Exclude unwanted elements (crowds, text, props) using the {{exclusions}} variable for clean composition. ● Delivers shallow depth-of-field bokeh, warm morning lighting, and professional color grading out of the box. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic high-end campaign shot of {{subject-and-styling}} holding a takeaway coffee cup, surrounded by floating emoji icons (coffee cups, hearts, smiley faces) in 3D rendered style, medium density.
```
