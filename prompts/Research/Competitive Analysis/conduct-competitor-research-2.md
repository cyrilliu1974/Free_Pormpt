# Competitor Research Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor Research Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers systematic competitive intelligence for businesses seeking to understand their market landscape. This competitor research prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing 5-8 major competitors in your industry and organizing findings into a clean markdown table with three columns: competitor name, marketing strategies, and strengths/weaknesses. You provide your industry and business context, and the AI examines each competitor's content marketing, SEO tactics, social media presence, paid advertising approach, unique selling propositions, customer loyalty drivers, market positioning, product gaps, customer experience issues, and pricing vulnerabilities. The output is immediately usable for strategic planning, investor decks, or internal team alignment. Marketing managers, business strategists, startup founders, and product teams reach for this prompt when they need rapid competitive intelligence without the cost of traditional market research firms. ● Identifies 5-8 top competitors with clear breakdowns of their go-to-market strategies ● Separates strengths and weaknesses for each competitor in an easy-to-scan format ● Covers marketing channels, positioning, customer loyalty factors, and strategic vulnerabilities ● Outputs markdown tables that paste directly into reports, slides, or strategic documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert competitor analyst conducting comprehensive competitive intelligence research.

## Task
Identify and analyze the top competitors in the specified industry, then deliver your findings in a structured comparison table.

## Context
Industry/Niche: {{industry}}
Business & Context: {{business-context}}

For each major competitor:
1. Identify their core marketing strategies (content marketing, SEO approach, social media presence, paid advertising tactics)
2. Evaluate key strengths (unique selling propositions, customer loyalty drivers, market positioning, competitive advantages)
3. Identify notable weaknesses (gaps in product/service offerings, customer experience issues, limited digital presence, pricing vulnerabilities)

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with exactly 3 columns:

| Competitor | Strategies | Strengths/Weaknesses |
|------------|------------|----------------------|

Include 5-8 top competitors. In the Strategies column, summarize their marketing and go-to-market approach. In the Strengths/Weaknesses column, clearly separate strengths from weaknesses using line breaks or bullet points for readability.
```
