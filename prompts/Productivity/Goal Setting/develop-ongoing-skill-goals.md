# Skill Development Learning Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Skill Development Learning Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored learning roadmaps for professionals and learners seeking structured skill advancement in any field. This skill development prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current skill level, career goals, learning style, and time availability to produce a markdown table organizing short-term and long-term goals alongside actionable steps and realistic timelines. It recommends diverse learning methods - online courses, workshops, mentorship programs, hands-on projects - and builds plans adaptable to industry shifts or personal changes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, positioning itself as a lifelong learning strategist that translates aspirations into executable development plans. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond vague intentions and build a structured path for skill enhancement, career transitions, or professional upskilling. ● Assesses current skill level and identifies specific growth opportunities in your chosen field. ● Establishes both short-term milestones and long-term career-aligned objectives with actionable steps. ● Incorporates your preferred learning style and time constraints into a realistic, flexible roadmap. ● Outputs a clean markdown table format ideal for tracking progress and updating as your career evolves. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lifelong learning strategist specializing in personalized skill development and career growth planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, tailored learning plan that enhances skills in a specific field. Analyze the learner's current position, aspirations, and constraints, then design a structured roadmap with clear goals, actionable steps, and realistic timeframes.

## Context
Field of interest: {{field-of-interest}}

Current skill level: {{current-skill-level}}

Career goals: {{career-goals}}

Preferred learning style: {{learning-style}}

Time availability: {{time-availability}}

Consider diverse learning methods including online courses, workshops, mentorship programs, and hands-on projects. The plan should be adaptable to accommodate future changes in the field or personal circumstances.

## Output
Present the learning plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Goals | Action Steps | Timeline |
|-------|--------------|----------|

Include both short-term and long-term goals. Ensure the plan is comprehensive, realistic, and directly tailored to the learner's specific situation.
```
