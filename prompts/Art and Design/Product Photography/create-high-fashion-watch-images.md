# High-Fashion Watch Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The High-Fashion Watch Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates elegant product still life images with levitating presentation elements and soft gradient backgrounds for luxury brands and e-commerce. It produces clean, dust-free commercial photography with a watch and presentation case floating above a gray seamless floor, circular glow motifs beneath each element, and an outer packaging box resting on the ground. This high-fashion watch product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana uses soft key light from the upper left, gentle fill to preserve detail, and subtle rim lighting to separate surfaces, with controlled gradient reflections and no harsh hotspots. The aesthetic references Gentle Monster's luxury visual language with charcoal-to-pale-gray gradients and medium telephoto compression. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping product visuals for watch brands, jewelry e-commerce, luxury goods marketing, or portfolio work that demands commercial-grade lighting and composition. ● Produces levitating product composition with watch, presentation case, and packaging box on a seamless gray floor with circular glow accents ● Delivers soft three-point lighting setup with controlled gradient reflections, flagged highlights, and no fingerprints or dust ● Uses straight-on camera angle with medium telephoto compression for authentic commercial product photography framing ● Outputs 3:4 aspect ratio images suitable for e-commerce, Instagram, and lookbook layouts with Gentle Monster-inspired luxury aesthetic ## Prompt
