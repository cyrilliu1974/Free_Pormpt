# Cinematic Chiaroscuro Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Chiaroscuro Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-realistic close-up portraits with dramatic high-contrast lighting for photographers, designers, and content creators. This chiaroscuro portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces extreme close-ups where hard light strikes the center of the face - highlighting skin texture, nose bridge, and forehead - while deep shadows envelope the sides. The output mimics 85mm f/1.8 photography with visible pores, realistic skin texture, subtle sheen on the T-zone, and individual facial hairs rendered in 8k resolution. Backgrounds blur into cinematic bokeh, keeping all focus on facial detail. Photographers building portfolios, designers creating editorial visuals, and marketers needing compelling brand imagery use it to generate studio-quality portraits without lighting rigs or camera gear.
