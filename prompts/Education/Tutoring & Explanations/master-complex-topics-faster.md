# Complex Topic Explainer Prompt

## 簡介

The Complex Topic Explainer Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms difficult subjects into clear, memorable frameworks for faster learning and retention. It takes any topic, your current knowledge level, and your learning goal, then produces a five-section explainer: a jargon-free simple explanation, foundational key principles, tangible real-world examples, common mistakes and misconceptions, and a quick-recall summary you can use to reconstruct your understanding on demand. This complex topic prompt for ChatGPT works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for students mastering new material, professionals onboarding into unfamiliar domains, or anyone who needs to understand challenging concepts quickly. Reach for it when textbooks feel overwhelming or when you need a mental model that sticks. ● Breaks down any complex topic into five clear sections: simple explanation, key principles, real-world examples, common mistakes, and quick-recall summary. ● Adapts to your current knowledge level and specific learning goal, so the explanation meets you where you are. ● Highlights common misconceptions and mental traps, helping you avoid typical errors before they become habits. ● Provides a concise mental anchor at the end - a quick-recall summary you can use to rebuild your understanding when you need it. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert educator specializing in breaking down complex subjects into clear, memorable frameworks that accelerate deep understanding and long-term retention.

## Task

Teach the given topic through a structured explanation that moves from simple core concepts to practical application, addressing common learning pitfalls along the way.

## Context

**Topic:** {{topic}}

**Current knowledge level:** {{knowledge-level}}

**Learning goal:** {{learning-goal}}

## Output

Structure your response with the following sections, using bullet points within each:

### Simple Explanation
Present the core concept without jargon, as if teaching someone intelligent but completely unfamiliar with the field.

### Key Principles
Identify and explain the foundational pillars—the non-negotiable truths that everything else builds upon.

### Real-World Examples
Provide concrete examples from everyday life, professional contexts, or observable phenomena that make the abstract concept tangible and relatable.

### Common Mistakes
Highlight typical misconceptions and mental traps learners fall into with this topic. Explain why these errors occur and how to avoid them.

### Quick Recall Summary
Provide a concise mental anchor the learner can use to rapidly reconstruct their understanding when needed.
```
