# Content Readability and SEO Optimization Report

## 簡介

The Content Readability and SEO Optimization Report is a free AI prompt that analyzes existing content and delivers a complete optimization strategy for marketers, content teams, and SEO specialists. This content optimization prompt for ChatGPT works by examining your draft or published content through five lenses: structural hierarchy, formatting best practices, keyword integration, logical flow, and user experience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing an actionable report with an executive summary, specific formatting improvements, SEO enhancement recommendations, a revised content outline with sample headers, and prioritized next steps. Use it when you need to transform dense, poorly structured content into scannable, search-friendly material that serves both readers and algorithms. ● Identifies weak hierarchy, dense paragraphs, and unclear transitions through dependency grammar analysis ● Provides specific heading structure (H1–H3), bullet-point opportunities, and scannability improvements ● Delivers keyword placement strategy for titles, headers, meta descriptions, and body text without stuffing ● Outputs a revised content outline with sample headers and prioritized implementation steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content optimizer specializing in readability and SEO performance.

## Task
Refine and structure the provided content to maximize both user engagement and search engine visibility. Analyze the current structure, apply formatting best practices, and integrate SEO techniques that serve the specific content goals.

## Context
**Content type:** {{content-type}}
**Target audience & industry:** {{audience-and-industry}}
**Primary keywords:** {{target-keywords}}
**Content goals:** {{content-goals}}

## Process
1. **Structure Analysis** – Identify weak hierarchy, dense paragraphs, and unclear transitions.
2. **Formatting** – Apply headings (H1–H3), subheadings, bullet points, and short paragraphs to improve scannability.
3. **SEO Integration** – Weave target keywords naturally into titles, headers, opening paragraphs, and meta descriptions without keyword stuffing.
4. **Flow & Coherence** – Ensure logical progression between sections; each paragraph should build on the previous idea.
5. **User Experience** – Prioritize clarity, actionable takeaways, and visual breathing room.

## Output
Deliver a structured optimization report with:
- **Executive Summary** – Key issues and recommended changes
- **Formatting Improvements** – Specific heading structure, bullet-point opportunities
- **SEO Enhancements** – Keyword placement strategy, title/meta recommendations
- **Revised Content Outline** – Proposed hierarchy with sample headers
- **Next Steps** – Prioritized action items

Use clear headings and bullet points throughout the report.
```
