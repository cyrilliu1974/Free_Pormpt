# Predictive Sales Forecast Model Builder

## 簡介

The Predictive Sales Forecast Model Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven sales forecasts and market trend analyses for any product or service. This predictive sales modeling prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a complete forecasting workflow: identifying credible data sources, preprocessing datasets, engineering predictive features, applying statistical and machine learning techniques, validating model performance with quantitative metrics, and delivering actionable recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured reports that include data citations, evaluation scores, forecast visualizations, and evidence-based strategic insights. Use it when you need to forecast quarterly sales, anticipate market shifts, or build a business case backed by predictive analytics. ● Structures the full predictive modeling pipeline - data sources, preprocessing, feature engineering, modeling techniques, and evaluation metrics - so nothing is overlooked. ● Requires citation of every claim, data point, and technique, ensuring forecasts are evidence-based and defensible to stakeholders. ● Outputs sales forecast charts and market trend visualizations alongside quantitative model performance scores. ● Incorporates macroeconomic factors, consumer behavior trends, and industry variables for realistic, context-aware predictions. ## Prompt
