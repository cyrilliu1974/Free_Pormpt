# Phone Photo Style Prompt for Image Generators

## 簡介

The Phone Photo Style Prompt for Image Generators is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, smartphone-captured photographs of any subject in any setting with Instagram-style raw processing. This phone photo prompt for Midjourney and compatible image generators produces images that mimic the natural look of mobile phone photography, complete with raw style processing (unfiltered, minimal post-processing) and customizable aspect ratios. By specifying a subject, setting, and aspect ratio, you get photos that look like they were taken on a phone and posted directly to Instagram - perfect for social media mockups, product photography concepts, lifestyle content creation, and realistic character photography. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, and other image models that support style parameters and stylization control. It strips away AI over-processing by setting stylization to zero and applying raw style, resulting in grounded, believable phone photos rather than hyper-polished renders. Reach for this prompt when you need realistic, candid-looking images that don't scream "AI-generated" - ideal for marketers, content creators, and designers testing visual concepts. ● Mimics real phone camera output with raw, unfiltered style and zero stylization for natural results. ● Accepts any subject (people, products, pets, food) and any setting (indoor, outdoor, urban, nature). ● Configurable aspect ratio to match Instagram posts, stories, or other social formats. ● Produces images that look like genuine user-generated content, not polished studio renders. ## Prompt

```
phone photo of a {{subject}}, in {{setting}}, posted on Instagram --style raw --s 0 --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
