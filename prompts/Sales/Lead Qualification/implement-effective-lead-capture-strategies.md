# Lead Capture Strategy Builder for Sales Funnels

## 簡介

The Lead Capture Strategy Builder for Sales Funnels is a free AI prompt that generates a comprehensive, multi-channel lead acquisition plan tailored to your business type, audience, budget, and sales cycle. This lead capture prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context - including target audience behavior, current methods, budget constraints, and typical sales cycle - then producing at least five distinct capture tactics in a structured table format. Each method includes numbered implementation steps with timeline indicators and quantified expected results such as conversion rates, lead volume, cost per lead, and ROI projections. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for any text-based AI workflow. Real-world applications include B2B sales teams building pipeline, e-commerce businesses expanding their email lists, and agencies designing client acquisition systems. This prompt is designed for sales strategists, marketing managers, founders, and lead generation specialists who need a systematic approach to capturing prospects across multiple channels without guesswork. ● Produces five or more lead capture methods ranked by expected ROI given your stated budget and sales cycle length ● Delivers numbered implementation steps with realistic timeline markers for each tactic ● Provides quantified success metrics including conversion rates, lead volume estimates, and cost per lead projections ● Considers channel conflict, industry benchmarks, and integration with your existing lead generation methods ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a lead generation strategist optimizing sales funnels and capture systems.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive lead capture strategy tailored to the business context provided. Design at least five distinct lead capture methods, each with clear implementation steps and measurable expected results. Ensure each method aligns with the target audience, budget constraints, sales cycle, and current capabilities.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
(Include: business type, target audience, current lead generation methods in use, available budget, and typical sales cycle length)

Consider:
- Where the target audience spends time and how they prefer to engage
- Industry-specific conversion patterns and benchmarks
- Integration with existing methods to avoid channel conflict
- Budget allocation across acquisition channels
- Timeline expectations based on sales cycle length

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Lead Capture Method | Implementation Steps | Expected Results |
|---------------------|----------------------|------------------|

For each of the five+ methods:
- **Lead Capture Method**: Name and brief description of the tactic
- **Implementation Steps**: Numbered action items with timeline indicators
- **Expected Results**: Quantified metrics (conversion rates, lead volume, cost per lead, timeframe to results)

Prioritize methods by expected ROI given the stated budget and sales cycle.
```
