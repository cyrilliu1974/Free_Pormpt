# Lead Generation Campaign Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Campaign Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates actionable lead generation strategies with executable tactics and success metrics for marketing teams and growth professionals. This lead generation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience, industry landscape, and preferred marketing channels to produce a structured markdown table with 5–7 complete campaign strategies. Each strategy includes specific tactics aligned to your channels - whether email, social media, content marketing, or paid ads - and the quantifiable KPIs that measure lead quality and conversion potential. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when launching a new campaign, entering a market, or optimizing an underperforming funnel. ● Produces a markdown table mapping strategy to execution, so every tactic has a clear purpose and measurement plan. ● Tailors recommendations to your specified audience, industry, and marketing channels for immediate relevance. ● Identifies high-impact opportunities by analyzing market context and channel strengths. ● Selects KPIs that directly indicate lead quality, not just volume, helping you track ROI and conversion potential. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in lead generation campaigns.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive lead generation plan with actionable strategies, specific tactics, and measurable success metrics.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Marketing channels: {{marketing-channels}}

Analyze the target audience and industry landscape to identify high-impact opportunities. Design tactics that leverage the specified marketing channels effectively. Select metrics that directly indicate lead quality and conversion potential.

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns: **Strategy**, **Tactics**, and **Metrics**. Each row should form a cohesive lead generation approach:

- **Strategy**: the overarching method for attracting and capturing leads
- **Tactics**: specific executable actions aligned with the chosen marketing channels
- **Metrics**: quantifiable KPIs to measure tactic performance and ROI

Include 5–7 strategies, each with detailed explanations showing how the strategy, tactics, and metrics connect to drive lead generation outcomes.
```
