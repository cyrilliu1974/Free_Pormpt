# Emotional Intelligence Program for Leaders

## 簡介

The Emotional Intelligence Program for Leaders is a free AI prompt that builds a complete EI training system tailored to specific leadership contexts. This emotional intelligence prompt for ChatGPT produces four components: a literature review synthesizing recent neuroscience and psychology research on leadership EI, a structured self-assessment covering the five core competencies (self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, and social skills), a personalized development plan with actionable strategies and milestones, and properly formatted APA references. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming a leadership context description into a science-backed training curriculum. HR professionals use it to design leadership development tracks, executive coaches apply it to create client programs, and team managers adapt it for skills workshops. Reach for this prompt when you need an evidence-based emotional intelligence framework grounded in current research rather than generic soft-skills advice. ● Synthesizes neuroscience and psychology research into clear, actionable EI development methods ● Provides a scoring system and interpretation guidelines for five core emotional intelligence competencies ● Generates timelines, milestones, and learning resources tailored to the leader's specific context ● Formats all citations in APA style for professional training documentation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert researcher and personal development coach specializing in emotional intelligence and leadership.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive program for improving emotional intelligence in leadership roles. Conduct a literature review on the most effective methods for developing emotional intelligence (EI) in leaders, focusing on the latest research in neuroscience and psychology. Create a self-assessment tool and personalized development plan tailored to the user's context.

## Context

{{leadership-context}}

## Output

Deliver your program in four sections using markdown formatting:

### 1. Literature Review
- Summary of key findings from recent research
- Most effective methods for developing EI in leaders
- Neuroscience insights on EI development
- Psychology insights on EI development

Use bullet points to synthesize findings clearly.

### 2. Self-Assessment Tool
- Introduction explaining the assessment purpose
- Questions organized by the five EI components:
  - Self-awareness
  - Self-regulation
  - Motivation
  - Empathy
  - Social skills
- Scoring system with clear interpretation guidelines

Number all questions and ensure the tool is user-friendly.

### 3. Personalized Development Plan
- Overview of the development approach
- Specific, actionable strategies for improving each EI component:
  - Self-awareness
  - Self-regulation
  - Motivation
  - Empathy
  - Social skills
- Timeline with concrete milestones
- Resources for further learning and support

Tailor strategies based on the self-assessment results and the leadership context provided.

### 4. References
- All sources cited in APA format

Ensure the literature review covers the most recent and relevant research. Base all recommendations on evidence-based practices.
```
