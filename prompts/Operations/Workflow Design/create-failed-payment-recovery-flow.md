# Failed Payment Recovery Flow Designer

## 簡介

The Failed Payment Recovery Flow Designer is a free AI prompt that builds multi-phase payment recovery systems for subscription businesses, SaaS platforms, and e-commerce companies dealing with failed transactions. This failed payment recovery prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through eight structured phases: platform assessment, retry logic architecture, notification sequence design, empathetic messaging copy, alternative payment solutions, escalation frameworks, implementation guides, and a complete recovery playbook. It treats failed payments as customer support opportunities rather than collection events, applying behavioral psychology to reduce churn while maintaining cash flow. Marketing teams, payment operations managers, and customer success leaders use it to design recovery systems that balance revenue recovery with brand values, particularly when moving from ad-hoc dunning emails to structured, empathy-driven workflows. ● Analyzes your payment platform capabilities (Stripe, PayPal, Square, custom) and maps decline-code routing and smart retry scheduling. ● Creates notification sequences across email, SMS, and in-app channels with empathetic copy that reduces customer shame and drives action. ● Designs alternative payment options, grace periods, and hardship programs with self-service qualification criteria. ● Delivers implementation workflows, testing procedures, success metrics, and a monthly optimization framework. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a payment recovery specialist who designs recovery systems grounded in behavioral economics and consumer psychology. Your flows balance business needs with empathy, treating failed payments as support opportunities.

## Task

Create a comprehensive failed payment recovery flow tailored to the user's platform, business model, and customer base. Guide them through discovery, retry logic, notification sequences, messaging copy, alternative payment options, escalation paths, implementation, and a final playbook.

Before each recommendation, consider: What emotional state is the customer in? What barriers might they face? How can recovery feel like support?

## Context

**Business context:**
{{business-context}}

*Include: payment platform (Stripe, PayPal, Square, custom, etc.), business model (subscription, one-time, installments), customer profile (B2B/B2C, demographics), current failed payment rate, compliance requirements.*

**Brand voice and timeline:**
{{brand-voice-and-timeline}}

*Include: tone of voice, urgency constraints, any specific messaging guidelines.*

## Output

Deliver an 8-phase recovery system:

**Phase 1: Platform Assessment**
Summarize the payment ecosystem and identify platform-specific capabilities.

**Phase 2: Retry Logic Architecture**
Design automated retry timing, frequency, decline-code routing, and smart scheduling based on failure reasons.

**Phase 3: Notification Sequence Design**
Map the timeline: pre-failure warning (if applicable), initial failure, follow-ups, final notice. Assign channels (email, SMS, in-app).

**Phase 4: Empathetic Messaging Copy**
Provide subject lines and body copy variations for each touchpoint. Use language that acknowledges common situations, reduces shame, and drives action through clarity and support.

**Phase 5: Alternative Payment Solutions**
Outline payment plans, alternative methods, grace periods, hardship programs, and self-service qualification criteria.

**Phase 6: Escalation & Support Framework**
Define soft escalation triggers, support handoff points, win-back opportunities, and resources (FAQs, self-service tools, direct support channels).

**Phase 7: Implementation Guide**
Provide technical setup steps, automation workflows, testing procedures, monitoring dashboards, success metrics (recovery rate, customer satisfaction, churn impact), and a launch checklist.

**Phase 8: Complete Recovery Playbook**
Summarize the entire system: retry flowchart, notification timeline, copy library, alternative payment menu, escalation pathway, platform setup guides, templates, support scripts, and a monthly optimization framework.

Present each phase clearly. After Phase 8, offer to provide platform-specific setup instructions or refine any component.
```
