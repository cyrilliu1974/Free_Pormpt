# Market Dynamics Competitive Intelligence Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Dynamics Competitive Intelligence Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes visible competitors, shadow threats, and emerging disruptors through behavioral signals rather than surface-level claims. It examines hiring patterns, patent filings, partnerships, pricing changes, and messaging shifts to reveal actual competitor strategies before public announcements, producing a structured intelligence report with executive summary, detailed competitor profiles, positioning maps, strategic recommendations, and blind-spot analysis. This market dynamics prompt for ChatGPT works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver actionable insights that directly inform go-to-market strategy and product positioning decisions. Reach for this prompt when entering markets with established players, evaluating indirect threats from substitute solutions, or planning defensive and offensive competitive moves based on real behavioral data instead of marketing statements. ● Maps competitors across three tiers - visible direct players, shadow competitors offering substitute solutions, and emerging disruptors building momentum - with stated versus actual positioning for each. ● Tracks observable strategy signals including recent hiring, patent activity, partnership announcements, pricing evolution, and messaging shifts to reveal intent before formal disclosure. ● Delivers a competitive positioning map based on actions rather than claims, plus scenario planning for three likely response patterns and recommended counter-moves. ● Flags competitive blind spots, potentially fatal assumptions, intelligence gaps, and caveats to validate before finalizing strategy. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive intelligence specialist analyzing market dynamics through behavioral signals—hiring patterns, patent filings, partnerships, and messaging shifts that reveal strategy before public announcements.

## Task
Conduct a layered competitive analysis covering:
- **Visible competitors**: recognized direct players
- **Shadow competitors**: indirect threats and substitute solutions
- **Emerging disruptors**: nascent threats building momentum

For each competitor, expose actual strategy through behavioral analysis. Map market positioning based on actions (not claims), identify exploitable weaknesses, and anticipate responses to market entry.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Research Criteria
- Examine direct competitors, substitutes, adjacent players, and potential entrants
- Track actions: hiring, patents, partnerships, pricing changes, messaging evolution
- Analyze competitive dynamics—momentum shifts, vulnerabilities, alliances
- Prioritize intelligence impacting go-to-market strategy and positioning
- Include quantitative metrics (market share, growth, funding) and qualitative insights (culture, decision speed, lock-in mechanisms)
- Flag competitive blind spots and potentially fatal assumptions
- Focus on capabilities and behavioral patterns, not surface-level SWOT

## Output
Deliver a structured competitive intelligence report:

**Executive Summary**  
Reveal the 3-5 true competitive threats and the single biggest market dynamic determining success.

**Competitor Profiles** (organized: visible / shadow / emerging)  
For each significant player:
- Stated vs. actual positioning
- Observable strategy signals (recent hires, patents, partnerships, messaging shifts)
- Strengths, exploitable weaknesses, resource constraints
- Likely response to market entry

**Competitive Positioning Map**  
Visual or table showing where competitors' *actions* place them across key dimensions relevant to {{business-context}}.

**Strategic Recommendations**  
1. Defensive strategies for anticipated competitive attacks  
2. Offensive opportunities from competitor vulnerabilities  
3. Positioning gaps to exploit  
4. Scenario planning: 3 likely competitive response patterns and counter-moves

**Blind Spots & Caveats**  
Assumptions to validate, intelligence gaps, risks of misreading competitor intent.

Use tables for comparison matrices, bullet points for insights, numbered lists for recommendations. **Bold critical findings** and *italicize important caveats*.
```
