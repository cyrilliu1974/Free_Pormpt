# Employee Development Program Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Development Program Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, actionable roadmaps for building continuous learning programs tailored to your workforce. This employee development prompt for ChatGPT and other text models (Claude, Gemini, Grok) transforms your organizational context and implementation parameters into a numbered, sequential plan covering skills assessment, training program design, delivery methods, tracking systems, feedback loops, and change management strategies. HR professionals use it to turn capability gaps into structured development initiatives, complete with success criteria, KPIs, and progress dashboards. Whether you manage 50 employees or 5,000, the prompt adapts to your industry, budget, and timeline to deliver a ready-to-implement learning culture roadmap. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from ad-hoc training to a strategic, measurable employee development system. ● Maps skills assessment methodology to identify capability gaps and prioritize development areas based on organizational goals. ● Designs training programs aligned to specific skill deficits, with clear delivery methods, learning modalities, and ownership assignments. ● Establishes KPI frameworks, completion metrics, and progress dashboards to measure training effectiveness and ROI. ● Builds feedback loops and continuous improvement cycles that turn one-time initiatives into self-sustaining learning cultures. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Human Resources Development Specialist designing continuous employee development programs that enhance workforce skills and performance.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step plan to assess current capabilities, design tailored training programs, implement tracking systems, and establish ongoing evaluation mechanisms that foster a culture of continuous learning.

## Context
Organization & Scope: {{organization-context}}
(Include organization size, industry, current employee skill levels, and any specific capability gaps or development priorities.)

Resources & Constraints: {{implementation-parameters}}
(Specify available budget for training programs and desired timeline for rollout.)

## Output
Deliver your plan as a numbered, sequential roadmap. Use an emoji before each step number to enhance readability. Cover:

- Skills assessment methodology and current-state analysis
- Training program design tailored to identified gaps and organizational goals
- Delivery methods and learning modalities
- Tracking and measurement systems (KPIs, progress dashboards, completion metrics)
- Feedback loops and continuous improvement cycles
- Change management and communication strategies to build a learning culture

Each step should specify actions, owners, success criteria, and dependencies where relevant.
```
