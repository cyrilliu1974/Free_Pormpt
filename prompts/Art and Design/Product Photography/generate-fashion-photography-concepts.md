# Fashion Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional studio fashion photography with editorial-level detail and patterned portraiture aesthetics for designers, stylists, and creative directors. This fashion photography prompt for Midjourney creates full-body shots of garments on models against clean white backdrops, emphasizing bold textile patterns and natural fabric movement with soft diffused studio lighting. It specifies medium format camera settings (85mm lens at f/2.8) and a 4:5 aspect ratio ideal for lookbooks, e-commerce hero images, and editorial presentations. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for it when you need catalog-ready fashion imagery that highlights garment construction, pattern detail, and professional studio aesthetics without a physical photoshoot. ● Produces full-body studio shots with clean white backgrounds and soft diffused lighting that emphasizes fabric texture and drape. ● Generates patterned portraiture aesthetics with large abstract motifs and bold textile detail suitable for lookbooks and editorial layouts. ● Specifies professional camera parameters (medium format, 85mm, f/2.8) for realistic depth of field and crisp garment detail. ● Accepts custom garment descriptions and model poses, letting you visualize collections and styling concepts rapidly. ## Prompt

```
Full-body studio shot of {{garment-description}} worn by {{model-and-pose}} against a clean white backdrop; large abstract pattern motifs, patterned portraiture aesthetic with bold textile emphasis, soft diffused studio lighting, natural fabric drape and movement; captured with medium format camera, 85mm lens at f/2.8; crisp detail, editorial fashion aesthetic --ar 4:5
```
