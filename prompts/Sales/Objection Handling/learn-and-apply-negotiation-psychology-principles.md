# Sales Negotiation Psychology Guide Builder

## 簡介

The Sales Negotiation Psychology Guide Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, customized negotiation strategy guides for sales professionals closing complex deals. This sales negotiation prompt for ChatGPT takes your product or service, target customer profile, industry context, and sales cycle length to produce a structured playbook covering preparation fundamentals, core psychology techniques (anchoring, framing, reciprocity), rapport-building tactics, active listening frameworks, and objection-handling strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering organized guides with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Use it when you need a tailored negotiation framework for a specific offering, competitive landscape, or customer segment rather than generic sales advice. ● Covers preparation research, customer pain-point analysis, and competitive positioning specific to your industry ● Explains anchoring, framing, and reciprocity techniques with practical examples tied to your product or service ● Provides rapport-building and active listening strategies appropriate to your sales cycle ● Delivers objection-handling scripts and closing tactics adapted to your target customer segment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales negotiation strategist.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide on key negotiation techniques tailored to {{product-or-service}}, using proven psychology principles to close more deals. Focus on techniques such as anchoring, framing, and reciprocity, providing detailed explanations and practical examples for each. Incorporate strategies for building rapport, active listening, and overcoming objections.

## Context
**Target customers:** {{target-customers}}

**Industry & competitive landscape:** {{industry-context}}

**Sales cycle:** {{sales-cycle}}

Emphasize preparation strategies, including researching target customers and understanding their needs and pain points. Address how to position {{product-or-service}} against competitive alternatives.

## Output
Structure your guide with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for easy readability and implementation. Organize the content logically, beginning with preparation fundamentals, progressing through core negotiation techniques with examples, and concluding with objection-handling and closing strategies specific to the sales cycle described.
```
