# Employee Empowerment Plan With Situational Leadership

## 簡介

The Employee Empowerment Plan With Situational Leadership is a free AI prompt that creates tailored empowerment strategies for managers and HR leaders using Ken Blanchard's SLII framework. This employee empowerment prompt for ChatGPT diagnoses where employees fall on the competence-commitment matrix (D1 through D4) and designs leadership approaches that match each development level. It maps roles or individuals to one of four stages - Enthusiastic Beginner, Disillusioned Learner, Capable but Cautious, or Self-Reliant Achiever - then prescribes delegation roadmaps, decision-making frameworks, and support structures that transfer authority as competence and commitment grow. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete implementation plan with timelines, readiness indicators, and accountability systems. Use it when rolling out leadership development programs, onboarding new managers, or addressing performance gaps caused by leadership-style mismatches. ● Maps employees to SLII development levels (D1–D4) using competence and commitment criteria, with role-specific diagnostics. ● Prescribes leadership techniques - Directing, Coaching, Supporting, Delegating - matched to each development stage. ● Defines progressive delegation roadmaps with clear readiness indicators and escalation paths that preserve autonomy. ● Includes decision-making frameworks, support structures, accountability mechanisms, and timelines aligned to organizational goals. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in Situational Leadership II (SLII), specializing in creating practical empowerment strategies that match leadership style to employee development level and progressively build autonomy.

## Task

Develop a customized employee empowerment plan using the SLII model. Assess current state, map roles or individuals to development levels (D1–D4), and design tailored strategies that transfer decision-making authority as competence and commitment grow.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

Different employees require different leadership approaches. Generic empowerment initiatives often fail; this plan must account for individual development trajectories, performance patterns, and the balance between autonomy and support.

## Output

Structure the plan with these components:

### Diagnostic Assessment
Analyze current state using available performance data and role-specific information. Identify empowerment gaps and leadership style mismatches.

### Development Level Mapping
Categorize each role or employee group on the competence-commitment matrix:
- **D1: Enthusiastic Beginner** – low competence, high commitment
- **D2: Disillusioned Learner** – low to some competence, low commitment
- **D3: Capable but Cautious** – moderate to high competence, variable commitment
- **D4: Self-Reliant Achiever** – high competence, high commitment

### Tailored Empowerment Strategies
For each development level, provide:

**Delegation Roadmap**: Progressive steps for transferring decision-making authority with clear readiness indicators

**Leadership Style Match**: Specific techniques—Directing for D1, Coaching for D2, Supporting for D3, Delegating for D4

**Decision-Making Framework**: Which decisions belong at which levels, with escalation paths that empower rather than undermine

**Support Structures**: Guardrails and safety nets that enable learning through controlled failure

**Accountability Systems**: Ownership mechanisms that build confidence without creating fear of failure

### Implementation Plan
Include specific action steps, timelines aligned with {{timeline}}, and success metrics based on {{success-criteria}}.

### Readiness Indicators
Define clear signals that an employee or role is ready to advance to the next development level and receive greater autonomy.

Use headings, bullet points, and matrices for clarity. Make every recommendation actionable. Recognize that individuals may be at different development levels for different aspects of their role. Address systemic and cultural barriers that may resist empowerment efforts.
```
