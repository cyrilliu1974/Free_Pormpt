# Content Calendar Generator for Social Media

## 簡介

The Content Calendar Generator for Social Media is a free AI prompt that builds a strategic posting schedule designed to drive engagement, brand awareness, and conversions for social media managers and marketers. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT creates a minimum of 10 scheduled posts organized in a markdown table format, each specifying the date, content type (video, carousel, story, static post, poll), topic, target audience segment, and call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, incorporating platform-specific best practices, optimal posting times, and content variety including educational, promotional, entertainment, and user-generated content. A fitness brand might use it to schedule workout tips, product launches, and client testimonials across Instagram; a B2B SaaS company could map LinkedIn thought leadership posts alongside feature announcements and case studies. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive content strategy that balances value delivery with conversion opportunities while maintaining a narrative arc across your social media presence. ● Produces a markdown table with date, content type, topic, target audience, and call-to-action for each post ● Balances educational, promotional, entertainment, and user-generated content types for strategic variety ● Incorporates platform-specific best practices and optimal posting times based on your chosen social network ● Aligns every calendar entry with your specific business context and content goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in content planning and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content calendar for {{social-media-platform}} that schedules posts to drive engagement, brand awareness, and conversions. The calendar must include at least 10 entries covering a strategic mix of content types, topics, and calls-to-action aligned with the business objectives.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-context}}

**Content Goals:**
{{content-goals}}

Consider optimal posting times, content variety (educational, promotional, entertainment, user-generated content), seasonal relevance, and platform-specific best practices. Balance value-driven content with conversion opportunities.

## Output
Deliver the content calendar as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Date** (specific dates or day designations)
- **Content Type** (video, carousel, story, static post, poll, etc.)
- **Topic** (specific subject or theme)
- **Target Audience** (segment or persona within the broader audience)
- **Call-to-Action** (clear next step for the user)

Ensure the calendar shows strategic variety and a cohesive narrative arc across the entries.
```
