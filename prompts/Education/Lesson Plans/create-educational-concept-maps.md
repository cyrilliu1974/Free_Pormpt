# Educational Concept Map Generator for Complex Topics

## 簡介

The Educational Concept Map Generator for Complex Topics is a free AI prompt that creates structured concept maps to help educators teach difficult subjects with clarity and pedagogical effectiveness. This concept mapping prompt for ChatGPT produces hierarchical visual outlines that identify the core ideas within any complex topic, explicitly map the relationships between concepts, and provide concrete examples to aid student understanding. Educators input a complex topic and subject area, and the prompt returns a multi-level concept map formatted with clear progression from broad concepts to specific details, complete with cited sources to maintain academic integrity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Designed for teachers, instructional designers, and curriculum developers who need to distill abstract or multi-layered topics into learnable frameworks that show how ideas connect and build on one another. ● Produces multi-tier concept maps with key ideas, sub-concepts, and pedagogically effective examples ● Shows explicit relationships between concepts so students understand how ideas connect ● Includes source citations to maintain academic standards and support further research ● Adapts to any subject or topic complexity level, from K-12 to higher education ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator specializing in curriculum design, concept mapping techniques, and pedagogical best practices.

## Task
Create a comprehensive concept map for teaching {{complex-topic}} within {{subject}}. Structure the map as a clear hierarchy that identifies key ideas, shows their relationships, and provides concrete examples to aid understanding.

## Output
Deliver the concept map in this format:

• **{{complex-topic}}**: {{subject}}
   ◦ Key Idea 1
      ▪ Relationship to other ideas
      ▪ Example 1
      ▪ Example 2
   ◦ Key Idea 2
      ▪ Relationship to other ideas
      ▪ Example 1
      ▪ Example 2
   ◦ Key Idea 3
      ▪ Relationship to other ideas
      ▪ Example 1
      ▪ Example 2
   ◦ (Continue for all major concepts)

**Sources:**
1. [Source 1]
2. [Source 2]
3. [Continue as needed]

Ensure:
- Clear hierarchical progression from broad concepts to specific details
- Explicit connections showing how ideas relate and build on each other
- Relevant, pedagogically effective examples
- Proper citation of all sources to maintain academic integrity
```
