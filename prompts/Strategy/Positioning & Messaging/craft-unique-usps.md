# Unique Selling Proposition (USP) Generator

## 簡介

The Unique Selling Proposition (USP) Generator is a free AI prompt that creates competitive brand positioning statements for marketers and strategists. This USP prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a structured table that connects your brand's distinctive features to practical customer benefits and emotional resonance. You provide your brand overview, target audience profile, and competitive landscape; the prompt produces a three-column markdown table organizing each USP as a complete value story. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to identify what sets your brand apart, why it matters to customers, and how it makes them feel. Marketing teams reach for this prompt when launching products, repositioning against competitors, or refining messaging frameworks. ● Organizes USPs in a Features | Benefits | Emotions table for clear strategic alignment ● Ensures each proposition addresses target audience needs and competitor gaps ● Connects functional capabilities to both practical outcomes and psychological appeal ● Delivers markdown-formatted output ready for strategy decks and messaging guides ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist developing unique selling propositions (USPs) that position a brand competitively in its market.

## Task
Create a set of clear, compelling USPs organized in a three-column table (Features | Benefits | Emotions) that:
- Highlight the brand's distinctive capabilities and offerings
- Address target audience needs, wants, and pain points
- Differentiate from competitor positioning
- Align with the brand's overall marketing strategy

## Context
**Brand & Market Position:**
{{brand-overview}}

**Target Audience:**
{{target-audience}}

**Competitive Landscape:**
{{competitors}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:
- **Features:** The brand's unique attributes and capabilities
- **Benefits:** Practical value delivered to the target audience
- **Emotions:** Emotional resonance and psychological appeal

Each row should present one cohesive USP that connects a distinctive feature to tangible benefits and emotional impact.
```
