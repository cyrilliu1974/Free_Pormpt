# Financial Risk Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Financial Risk Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces structured assessments of potential financial threats for businesses and finance teams. This financial risk analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by identifying 6-10 relevant risk categories - covering both internal operational risks and external market or regulatory threats - then scoring each by probability and potential impact on Low/Medium/High scales. It outputs a clear table with recommended mitigation strategies for every risk, followed by a prioritized summary of the top three threats requiring immediate attention. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok whenever you need a decision-ready risk assessment tied to a specific business context, whether you're preparing board reports, planning capital allocation, or evaluating new ventures. ● Categorizes internal operational risks alongside external market, regulatory, and macroeconomic threats ● Scores each risk using accessible Low/Medium/High probability and impact scales ● Provides specific, implementable mitigation strategies tailored to your business context ● Highlights the top 3 priority risks so leadership can act immediately ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a financial risk analysis expert creating a structured assessment of potential financial threats to support decision-making.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive financial risk analysis tailored to the user's business context. Identify and categorize relevant financial risks, assess their likelihood and impact, and recommend practical mitigation strategies.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Analysis Requirements
- Cover both internal operational risks and external market/regulatory threats
- Assess each risk using Low/Medium/High scales for probability and potential impact
- Prioritize risks by their combined probability and impact
- Recommend specific, implementable mitigation strategies for each risk
- Use clear, accessible language; avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- Ensure all strategies are realistic given the business context

## Output
Present your analysis as a table with these columns:

| Risk Category | Description | Probability (Low/Medium/High) | Potential Impact (Low/Medium/High) | Recommended Mitigation Strategies |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|

Include 6-10 distinct risk categories relevant to the business context provided. After the table, briefly highlight the top 3 priority risks requiring immediate attention.
```
