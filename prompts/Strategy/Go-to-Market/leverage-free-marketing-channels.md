# Free Marketing Channels Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Free Marketing Channels Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that generates prioritized, zero-budget marketing tactics for businesses operating without advertising spend. This free marketing channels prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, target audience, and goals to recommend actionable acquisition strategies across content marketing, community building, partnerships, social media, SEO, and email. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering step-by-step implementation guidance, time estimates, and expected outcomes for each tactic. Startups, bootstrapped businesses, and side projects use it to build cost-effective go-to-market plans that maximize return on effort rather than capital. The prompt organizes strategies by category and prioritizes channels with the lowest barriers to entry and fastest time-to-value. ● Evaluates free channels (content, SEO, partnerships, community) against your specific business model and audience ● Provides step-by-step execution guidance with time investment and outcome forecasts for each strategy ● Prioritizes tactics by return on effort, focusing on low-barrier methods that can start immediately ● Organizes recommendations into clear categories with pro tips to maximize results without spending ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in zero-budget customer acquisition and growth tactics.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive list of free marketing channels and bootstrapping techniques for {{business-context}}. For each strategy, explain its effectiveness, reach, implementation steps, and expected outcomes. Prioritize tactics that deliver the highest return on time and effort invested.

## Context
Business context should include: business type, target audience, unique selling proposition, current budget constraints, and key goals (e.g., awareness, leads, sales, retention).

Analyze each channel for suitability to the business model and audience. Focus on actionable, low-barrier entry tactics that can be executed immediately without capital investment.

## Output
Organize strategies under clear category headings (e.g., Content Marketing, Community Building, Partnerships, Social Media, SEO, Email).

For each strategy provide:
- **Strategy name** and brief description
- Why it works for this business context
- Step-by-step implementation guidance
- Estimated time investment and expected outcomes
- Pro tips to maximize results

Format as a structured bullet-point list with category headers for maximum readability.
```
