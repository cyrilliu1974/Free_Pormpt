# Legal Risk Assessment Prompt for Business Compliance

## 簡介

The Legal Risk Assessment Prompt for Business Compliance is a free AI prompt that conducts structured legal risk analysis for business processes across any industry and regulatory environment. This legal risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table identifying potential legal risks, rating each as low, medium, or high likelihood, and providing actionable mitigation strategies tailored to your specific regulatory context. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing both direct and indirect legal implications by considering industry-specific regulations, general legal requirements, and the company's operational environment. Use it to evaluate new processes, audit existing operations, or prepare compliance documentation for stakeholders and legal teams. This prompt is designed for compliance officers, legal teams, risk managers, and business leaders who need structured legal risk analysis for specific business processes or operational changes. ● Systematically identifies legal risks specific to your business process, industry regulations, and company context ● Evaluates each risk using a three-point likelihood scale (low, medium, high) for clear prioritization ● Delivers practical, industry-specific mitigation strategies in an organized markdown table format ● Analyzes both direct regulatory compliance risks and indirect legal implications of business operations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal analyst specializing in business risk assessment and compliance.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive legal risk assessment for a specific business process. Identify potential legal risks, evaluate their likelihood, and develop actionable mitigation strategies tailored to the industry and regulatory context.

## Context
Business process and industry: {{process-and-industry}}

Regulatory and company context: {{regulatory-and-company-context}}

Analyze both direct and indirect legal implications. Consider industry-specific regulations, general legal requirements, and the company's operational environment. Assess each risk's likelihood using a three-point scale: low, medium, or high.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|---------------------|

Ensure the table is comprehensive, well-organized, and includes practical, actionable strategies specific to the regulatory environment and business context provided.
```
