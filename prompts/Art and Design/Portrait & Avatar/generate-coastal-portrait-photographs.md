# Coastal Portrait Photography Generator

## 簡介

The Coastal Portrait Photography Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic seaside portraits with anatomically correct proportions and natural lighting for content creators and brands. This coastal portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images of subjects in relaxed, natural poses on rocky cliff edges with turquoise ocean waves and rolling hills in the background. The prompt enforces correct limb orientation, realistic body proportions, and bright natural sunlight shot on 35mm film aesthetics. Use it for travel blogs, lifestyle brands, fashion lookbooks, or Instagram content that needs authentic seaside imagery without arranging location shoots. Designers, marketers, and content creators reach for this prompt when they need high-quality coastal lifestyle photography that feels genuine rather than staged. ● Enforces natural reclining poses with correct limb orientation and realistic body proportions to avoid common AI distortions. ● Captures dramatic clifftop settings with turquoise waves, rolling hills, and scattered clouds for authentic coastal atmosphere. ● Produces 35mm film-style photorealism with bright natural sunlight and cinematic framing in 1:1 aspect ratio. ● Customizable subject description and location setting variables let you adapt the scene while maintaining photographic quality. ## Prompt

```
A beautiful young woman reclining naturally on a rocky cliff edge overlooking turquoise ocean waves. {{subject-description}}. Beige flat espadrilles. Relaxed, natural reclining pose with correct limb orientation, no flipped or distorted limbs, natural leg positioning, realistic body proportions.

Bright natural sunlight, cinematic lighting, photorealistic. High detail, shot on 35mm film, 1:1 aspect ratio.
```
