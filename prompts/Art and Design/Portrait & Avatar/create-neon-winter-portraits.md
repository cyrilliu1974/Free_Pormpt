# Neon Winter Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Neon Winter Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates layered cinematic portrait compositions combining urban neon aesthetics with natural winter elements for photographers and visual creators. This neon winter portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana structures images in three vertical zones: sharp eyes reflecting city lights with frosted lashes (upper third), a figure on a snow-dusted rooftop wrapped in custom attire with neon glow bouncing off fabric (middle third), and an intimate close-up of face and hands shielding bright neon light with snowflakes clinging to jewelry (lower third). Content creators use it for fashion editorials, social media campaigns, and brand storytelling that requires moody urban winter atmospheres without location shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality portraits that merge architectural grit with delicate winter details - frost patterns, falling snow, and dramatic neon color casts. ● Produces layered vertical compositions with distinct focal zones - eyes and reflections, body and environment, face and light interaction. ● Renders realistic atmospheric details including frost on lashes, snowflakes on clothing, neon glow on fabric, and city light reflections. ● Accepts two variables for full creative control: aesthetic-style (film noir, cyberpunk, editorial realism) and outfit-description (streetwear, couture, winter layers). ● Generates high-detail imagery suitable for fashion lookbooks, album covers, social media content, and editorial publications. ## Prompt

```
A cinematic portrait composition in three vertical zones. Upper third: her eyes sharp and reflecting blurred city lights below, thin frost touching her lashes, wind pushing strands of hair across her forehead, rendered in {{aesthetic-style}}. Middle third: her body lying on a rooftop ledge dusted with snow, wrapped in {{outfit-description}}, soft neon glow bouncing off snowflakes on her sleeves. Lower third: close-up of her face and hands, facing camera with one hand shielding a bright pinkish neon light above her eyes, snowflakes clinging to her jewelry, tiny drops forming near her lips. Urban winter atmosphere, dramatic lighting, high detail.
```
