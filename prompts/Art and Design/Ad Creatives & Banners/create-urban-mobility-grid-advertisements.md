# Urban Mobility Grid Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Urban Mobility Grid Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional six-panel grid layouts for electric scooters, e-bikes, and personal mobility devices. This urban mobility grid advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 4:5 portrait-format 2×3 grid with six distinct commercial photography panels: a studio hero shot with directional lighting, a city commute lifestyle scene with motion blur, a close-up of the folding or convenience mechanism, a macro detail of the drivetrain or motor, a dusk safety shot highlighting LED lighting, and an everyday-use scene near cafés or transit hubs. Each panel uses consistent brand palette colors and a premium tech lifestyle aesthetic. Marketing teams, product launch managers, and e-commerce brands reach for it when they need cohesive multi-angle product stories in a single visual. ● Produces hero studio shots with soft directional lighting, lifestyle commute scenes with natural motion blur, and macro engineering details with shallow depth of field. ● Highlights folding mechanisms, motor components, LED safety lighting at dusk, and real-world parking scenarios across six equal panels. ● Applies your brand color palette and maintains a clean, high-realism commercial photography style with no toy-like rendering or cartoon aesthetics. ● Outputs 4:5 portrait grids optimized for Instagram ads, product launch pages, and digital marketing campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Create a modern 2×3 grid advertisement in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio with six equal panels showcasing an urban mobility product. Professional commercial photography style with high realism, clean composition, and premium tech lifestyle aesthetic.

**Panel 1 – Hero Urban Presence:** Studio-style hero shot of {{product-name}} angled for power and balance. Soft directional lighting with crisp edges against concrete gray or urban gradient background. Mood: strength, innovation, urban confidence.

**Panel 2 – City Commute Lifestyle:** Adult riding {{product-name}} through modern city street. Natural daylight with slight background motion blur. Mood: freedom, efficiency, momentum.

**Panel 3 – Convenience Feature:** Close-up of folding mechanism showing detail and user interaction. Shallow depth of field, precise focus. Mood: convenience, smart design.

**Panel 4 – Performance Detail:** Macro shot of core performance component—drivetrain, motor, suspension, or frame engineering. High-contrast highlights on metallic, rugged materials. Mood: power, durability, engineering precision.

**Panel 5 – Safety Visibility:** {{product-name}} in use at dusk with LED lighting active in low-light conditions. Urban glow lighting focused on illuminated safety elements. Mood: safety, visibility, confidence.

**Panel 6 – Everyday Utility:** {{product-name}} parked near café, office, or transit hub. Warm natural light in lifestyle urban setting. Mood: practical, stylish, daily use.

**Branding:** {{brand-palette}} color scheme. Bold, efficient, future-forward personality. Minimal industrial logo presentation, precision-aligned. Overall mood: urban, fast, reliable, modern, empowering, efficient.

**Avoid:** Low resolution, toy-like appearance, distorted proportions, unreadable branding, cluttered scenes, CGI or cartoon style, over-saturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
