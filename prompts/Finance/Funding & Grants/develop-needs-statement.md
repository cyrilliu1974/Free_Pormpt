# Grant Needs Statement Generator for Funding Proposals

## 簡介

The Grant Needs Statement Generator for Funding Proposals is a free AI prompt that produces evidence-based needs statements for organizations and nonprofits seeking grant funding. This grant needs statement prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a three-part narrative: a problem statement that identifies the core issue affecting your target community, a proposed solution that highlights innovation and potential impact, and a section on expected outcomes that demonstrates significance to reviewers. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and structures your writing with clear logical progression between sections. Real use cases include foundation grant applications, government funding requests, corporate sponsorship proposals, and nonprofit program funding submissions. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft or refine the needs statement section of any grant proposal and want to ensure your argument flows logically from problem through solution to measurable impact. ● Structures your needs statement into three clear sections: problem, solution, and expected outcomes ● Emphasizes evidence-based arguments and dependency relationships between ideas for logical flow ● Highlights the urgency, significance, and innovative aspects that grant reviewers look for ● Adapts to any grant context through a single variable for your specific funding scenario ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer specializing in needs statements for grant proposals.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, compelling needs statement that articulates the problem, proposed solution, and expected outcomes. Structure the writing with clear dependency relationships between ideas to ensure concise, logical flow.

## Context
Needs statement for: {{grant-context}}

Use evidence-based arguments throughout. Emphasize the urgency and significance of addressing the identified need. Highlight innovative aspects and potential impact of the solution.

## Output
Structure the needs statement with these sections:

### Problem Statement
[Identify and articulate the core issue affecting the target community or organization]

### Proposed Solution
[Outline the solution, emphasizing innovative aspects and potential impact]

### Expected Outcomes
[Describe anticipated outcomes and their significance]

Maintain logical progression between sections. Ground all claims in evidence where possible.
```
