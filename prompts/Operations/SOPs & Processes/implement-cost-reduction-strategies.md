# Cost Reduction Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Cost Reduction Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that analyzes business expenses and builds actionable savings plans for operations managers, financial analysts, and business owners. This cost reduction prompt for ChatGPT walks through a specified business process, identifies current cost drivers, calculates realistic savings opportunities, and delivers a markdown table with three columns: Current Costs, Potential Savings, and Action Steps. Each row covers a distinct area - from supplier negotiations and contract renegotiation to process automation, waste reduction, and workflow improvements - complete with percentage-reduction estimates and risk-mitigation notes to preserve service quality. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing 5–8 prioritized recommendations tailored to the complexity of your operation. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured audit of expenses or a roadmap to cut costs without compromising output quality. ● Identifies specific cost drivers and current expenditure patterns across 5–8 key areas. ● Proposes supplier negotiation tactics including volume discounts, alternative sourcing, and contract renegotiation. ● Recommends process optimizations such as automation, waste reduction, and workflow improvements. ● Includes risk-mitigation considerations to preserve quality and service levels during cost cuts. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a cost reduction strategist analyzing expenses and identifying savings opportunities.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive cost reduction plan for the specified business process. Identify inefficiencies, propose supplier negotiation strategies, and recommend operational improvements that maintain quality standards.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Costs | Potential Savings | Action Steps |
|---------------|-------------------|-------------|

Each row should address a distinct area within the business process. Include:
- Specific cost drivers and current expenditure patterns
- Realistic savings estimates with percentage reductions where applicable
- Detailed, actionable implementation steps prioritized by impact and feasibility
- Supplier negotiation tactics (volume discounts, contract renegotiation, alternative sourcing)
- Process optimization recommendations (automation, waste reduction, workflow improvements)
- Risk mitigation considerations to preserve quality and service levels

Cover 5-8 key areas depending on process complexity.
```
