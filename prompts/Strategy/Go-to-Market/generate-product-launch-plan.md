# Product Launch Marketing Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Product Launch Marketing Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds structured, multi-phase go-to-market strategies for new products entering niche markets. This product launch prompt for ChatGPT walks through target market analysis, positioning, the full marketing mix (product, pricing, distribution, promotion), and phased campaign tactics spanning pre-launch, launch, and post-launch activities. It delivers a complete plan that includes content marketing, influencer partnerships, social media, email campaigns, budget allocation, timeline milestones, KPIs, and contingency strategies. Teams use it to map out launch roadmaps when entering specialized verticals or rolling out products to defined customer segments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a detailed, channel-specific launch blueprint that addresses the unique characteristics of a niche audience and provides measurable success criteria. ● Creates executive summary, target market segmentation, and positioning statement with unique selling proposition ● Develops promotion strategy across content, influencer, social, and email channels with pre-launch, launch, and post-launch tactics ● Recommends budget allocation by channel and phased timeline with key milestones ● Defines KPIs spanning awareness, engagement, conversion, and retention, plus risk mitigation strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in product launches for niche markets.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive marketing plan to effectively promote the launch of a new product within a specific niche market. The plan should maximize visibility, generate interest, and drive sales through a tailored strategy that addresses the unique characteristics of the target audience.

## Context
The marketing plan must cover all essential phases (pre-launch, launch, and post-launch) and utilize multiple channels including content marketing, influencer partnerships, social media, and email campaigns.

## Input Required
**Product & Market Context:** {{product-and-market-description}}
(Describe your product, its niche market, target audience characteristics, and any known customer needs or preferences)

## Output
Deliver a structured marketing plan with the following components:

**Executive Summary**
Overview of the marketing strategy and key objectives

**Target Market Analysis**
● Target market description and segmentation
● Core needs and pain points
● Preferences and buying behaviors

**Product Positioning**
● Unique selling proposition (USP)
● Key product benefits
● Positioning statement

**Marketing Mix**
● Product strategy
● Pricing strategy
● Distribution strategy
● Promotion strategy:
  - Pre-launch tactics
  - Launch event plan
  - Post-launch tactics
  - Marketing channels breakdown
  - Content marketing plan
  - Influencer marketing approach
  - Social media marketing plan
  - Email marketing plan

**Budget and Timeline**
● Recommended marketing budget allocation
● Phased launch timeline with milestones

**KPIs and Metrics**
Key performance indicators to measure launch success across awareness, engagement, conversion, and retention

**Contingency Plan**
Risk mitigation strategies for potential challenges or setbacks
```
