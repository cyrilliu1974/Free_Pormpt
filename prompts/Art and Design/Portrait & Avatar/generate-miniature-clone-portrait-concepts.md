# Miniature Clone Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Miniature Clone Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic studio photographs where a subject appears alongside tiny identical clones of themselves. This miniature clone portrait prompt for Midjourney and Flux produces fashion-grade imagery with high-end studio lighting on pristine white backgrounds. The prompt positions multiple miniature figures - on the subject's head, shoulders, palm, and surrounding platform - while maintaining exact facial likeness, hairstyle, outfit, and expression across every scale. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other text-to-image models that support detailed multi-figure compositions. Use it for personal branding visuals, social media content, portfolio pieces, or marketing campaigns that need surreal yet believable imagery. ● Produces full-body portraits with miniature clones positioned on the head, shoulders, palm, leg, and platform around the subject. ● Ensures perfect facial consistency, hairstyle matching, and outfit replication across all figures at every size. ● Delivers high-end fashion photography aesthetics with professional studio lighting and seamless white backgrounds. ● Generates ultra-detailed, photorealistic results suitable for social media profiles, creative portfolios, and visual marketing. ## Prompt

```
An ultra-realistic, full-body studio portrait of {{subject-description}}. She is seated gracefully on a pristine white platform against a seamless white studio background with high-end fashion lighting. She has a serious, direct gaze into the camera with natural makeup.

Multiple miniature versions of herself in identical outfit and styling are interacting with her: one perched on her head, two sitting on her shoulders, one seated in her open palm, one climbing her leg, and three walking nearby on the platform. All miniature figures share the same serious expression and perfect likeness.

Ultra-detailed, photorealistic. Facial features and hair style must match exactly across all figures, full and miniature.
```
