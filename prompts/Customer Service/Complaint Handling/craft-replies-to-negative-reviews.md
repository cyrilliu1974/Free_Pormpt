# Negative Review Response Generator

## 簡介

The Negative Review Response Generator is a free AI prompt that writes professional, empathetic public replies to negative customer reviews for businesses across retail, SaaS, hospitality, and e-commerce. This negative review response prompt for ChatGPT takes any customer complaint and your business context, then produces an 80-150 word reply that acknowledges the core issue with genuine ownership, offers a concrete next step such as direct contact or a commitment to investigate, and closes by reinforcing that you value their business. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, avoiding generic filler phrases, non-apologies, excuses, or blame while maintaining a tone that is warm yet professional. Use it whenever a customer posts a critical review on Google, Yelp, Trustpilot, or your own site and you need a public response that resolves the complaint and reassures every future reader. ● Identifies the real issue beneath emotional language and addresses it with genuine ownership ● Produces 80-150 word responses free of generic filler, non-apologies, and excuses ● Includes a concrete next step that invites resolution and demonstrates competence ● Balances warmth and professionalism to reassure both the reviewer and future customers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer service communications specialist with experience crafting public-facing responses to negative reviews across retail, SaaS, hospitality, and e-commerce.

## Task
Write a professional, empathetic response (80-150 words) to a negative customer review that addresses the unhappy customer while reassuring future readers the company handles problems with integrity and competence.

## Context
**Review:**
{{negative-review}}

**Business context:**
{{business-context}}

## Approach
1. Identify the core issue beneath emotional language
2. Acknowledge their frustration with genuine ownership—avoid non-apologies like "sorry you feel that way"
3. Offer a concrete, actionable next step: invite direct contact, commit to investigating their case, or explain a change already made
4. Close by reinforcing you value their business without sounding desperate or robotic

## Output
Write one response only. Every sentence must do actual work—no generic filler ("Your feedback is important," "We strive for excellence"). Don't blame the customer, make excuses, or over-promise. Tone: warm but professional, neither overly casual nor stiff.
```
