# Blog Post Promotion Package Generator

## 簡介

The Blog Post Promotion Package Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete content marketing package including an optimized blog article and ready-to-share social media assets for your target audience. This blog post promotion prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a full content package: a click-worthy headline with meta description, a structured article with introduction, 3–5 key points supported by evidence, and a conclusion with a call-to-action, plus platform-specific social snippets, visual concepts, and hashtags for LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, and other channels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone and keyword integration to match your brand voice - whether authoritative, conversational, or witty - and your audience's demographics and pain points. Reach for this prompt when you need a blog post that not only informs but is engineered for distribution and engagement across social platforms. ● Produces SEO-optimized headlines, meta descriptions, and naturally integrated keywords without stuffing ● Structures posts with evidence-backed key points, logical flow, and clear calls-to-action that drive reader behavior ● Generates platform-specific social snippets tailored to character limits and audience behavior on each channel ● Includes visual concepts and hashtag recommendations to complete your distribution strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in blog posts optimized for social media distribution.

## Task
Write a complete blog post package that includes the article itself and accompanying social media assets. Structure the post for clarity, engagement, and shareability.

## Context
**Topic and audience:**  
{{blog-topic-and-audience}}  
(Describe what the post is about and who it's for—include demographics, interests, pain points, or professional context as relevant.)

**Key message and voice:**  
{{key-message-and-brand-voice}}  
(State the core takeaway you want readers to remember, and describe your brand voice—e.g. authoritative and data-driven, conversational and empowering, witty and irreverent.)

**Primary social platforms:**  
{{social-platforms}}  
(List where this will be shared: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, etc.)

## Output
Deliver the following sections with clear headings:

### 1. Headline & Meta Description
- A compelling, click-worthy headline
- SEO meta description (150–160 characters)

### 2. Blog Post
- **Introduction:** Hook the reader and establish the post's value
- **Main Body:** Develop 3–5 key points in logical sequence, each supported by statistics, examples, or case studies where applicable
- **Conclusion:** Summarize the key message and include a clear call-to-action that drives engagement (comment, share, subscribe, or visit a resource)

### 3. Social Media Assets
- Platform-specific snippets (one per platform listed) optimized for character limits and audience behavior
- Suggested visual concepts or image descriptions to accompany each snippet
- Recommended hashtags

Use natural keyword integration throughout for SEO without keyword stuffing. Ensure the writing style matches the specified brand voice and resonates with the target audience.
```
