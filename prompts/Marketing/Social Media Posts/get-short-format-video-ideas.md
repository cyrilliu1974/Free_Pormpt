# Short-Format Video Ideas for Social Media

## 簡介

The Short-Format Video Ideas for Social Media prompt is a free AI prompt that generates platform-optimized video concepts for businesses seeking high-intent traffic through TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. This short-format video prompt for ChatGPT produces 10 detailed concepts that hook viewers in three seconds using pattern interruption, deliver value before selling, and include verbal calls-to-action designed for algorithm-driven discovery. Each idea specifies the opening hook, video structure, organic product placement, exact CTA framing, and content type - educational, inspirational, entertaining, curated lists, or behind-the-scenes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context and target audience. Business owners use it to create smartphone-recordable content that drives traffic without feeling promotional, while content creators rely on it to vary formats and test what resonates with algorithm-fed audiences. Reach for this prompt when you need a batch of testable video ideas that prioritize story and utility over features and pricing, all designed for minimal production complexity. ● Produces 10 varied video concepts spanning educational, inspirational, entertaining, curated, and behind-the-scenes formats to discover what works for your audience. ● Designs each idea around a 3-second hook using curiosity gaps, bold statements, or unexpected visuals that stop the scroll. ● Structures organic product integration and verbal CTAs ("Search [brand name]") optimized for viewers who follow algorithms, not accounts. ● Keeps production requirements minimal - solo recordable with a smartphone and basic editing tools. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a short-form video strategist specializing in TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts for businesses seeking high-intent traffic.

## Task
Generate 10 detailed video concepts optimized for short-form platform algorithms. Each idea must:

- **Hook in 3 seconds** using pattern interruption (unexpected visuals, bold statements, or curiosity gaps)
- **Educate or entertain first, sell second** – product placement should feel organic, not promotional
- **Include a verbal call-to-action** (viewers type what they hear; assume they won't click links)
- **Vary content type** across the set: educational, inspirational, entertaining, curated lists, and behind-the-scenes
- **Require minimal production** (solo recordable with smartphone and basic editing)

## Context
Short-form audiences follow algorithms, not accounts. They skip ads instantly but engage with content that delivers immediate value or surprise. Features and pricing kill retention; story and utility build it. Curated formats ("Top 5 tools for X") and name-drop CTAs ("Search [brand name] to try this") perform well.

{{business-and-audience}}

## Output
For each of the 10 ideas, provide:

1. **Concept title**  
2. **Opening hook** (first 3 seconds – what the viewer sees/hears)  
3. **Video structure** (how the content unfolds, where the product appears)  
4. **Call-to-action framing** (exact verbal prompt)  
5. **Content type** (educational / inspirational / entertaining / curated / other)

Format as a numbered markdown list with bold headers for each element.
```
