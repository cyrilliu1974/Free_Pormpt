# Sales Email Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Sales Email Writer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds structured, conversion-focused sales emails for products and services. This sales email prompt for ChatGPT walks through every component of an effective outreach message: attention-grabbing subject lines with alternatives, opening hooks that address pain points, body paragraphs built with dependency grammar for natural flow, persuasive closings, and compelling calls-to-action. You provide your product details, brand voice, and desired CTA; the prompt analyzes your audience's pain points and highlights your unique value proposition. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for marketers who need consistent, professional email copy without starting from scratch. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete sales email that moves prospects toward a specific action, whether booking a demo, starting a trial, or making a purchase. ● Analyzes audience pain points and maps product benefits directly to customer needs ● Provides multiple subject line options with explanations of what makes each effective ● Uses dependency grammar principles to create persuasive, naturally flowing email body copy ● Structures every element from hook to CTA with explicit reasoning about what drives conversions ● Adapts vocabulary and framing to match your specified brand voice and audience expectations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive sales emails that drive conversions.

## Task
Create a complete sales email for the specified product or service. Deliver a structured breakdown covering subject line, opening, body, closing, and call-to-action.

## Context
Product/service and positioning:
{{product-service-details}}
(Include what you're selling, its unique selling points, and the target audience's demographics, interests, and pain points)

Brand voice and tone:
{{brand-voice}}

Desired call-to-action:
{{cta}}

## Approach
- Analyze how the product solves the audience's specific pain points
- Emphasize the unique value that sets this offering apart from alternatives
- Use dependency grammar to build persuasive, flowing sentences that guide readers naturally toward the CTA
- Match vocabulary and framing to the audience's level and expectations

## Output
Structure your sales email as follows:

**Subject Line:**
- [Primary option]
- [Alternative option]
- Key elements: [what makes these work]

**Opening:**
- [Hook paragraph]
- Key elements: [approach used]

**Body:**
- [Persuasive content addressing pain points and unique value]
- Key elements: [how dependency grammar creates flow and emphasis]

**Closing:**
- [Wrap-up paragraph that reinforces value]
- Key elements: [transition strategy to CTA]

**Call-to-Action:**
- [Specific CTA implementation]
- Key elements: [what makes it compelling]
```
