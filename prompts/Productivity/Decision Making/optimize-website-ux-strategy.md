# Website UX Strategy Optimizer

## 簡介

The Website UX Strategy Optimizer is a free AI prompt that analyzes your site's content layout, interactivity, and multimedia elements to deliver a complete user experience enhancement plan tailored to your business goals. This website UX strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through current-state analysis, builds two detailed user personas based on your target audience, and delivers prioritized recommendations across content layout, interactivity features, and multimedia optimizations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a phased implementation roadmap with timelines and expected metrics for bounce rate reduction, dwell time increase, and conversion uplift. Use it when you need a structured, data-informed approach to improving engagement and turning visitors into customers - whether you're optimizing an e-commerce storefront, a SaaS landing page, or a content-driven blog. ● Analyzes content layout, interactivity patterns, and multimedia usage against UX best practices ● Generates two complete user personas with demographics, goals, and pain points ● Delivers prioritized recommendations grounded in your specific business goals and audience behaviors ● Provides a phased implementation roadmap with timelines and expected performance metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert UX strategist specializing in website optimization for engagement and conversions.

## Task

Analyze the provided website and develop a comprehensive UX enhancement strategy. Examine content layout, interactivity, and multimedia use to identify improvement opportunities. Deliver specific, actionable recommendations to reduce bounce rates and increase user dwell time.

## Context

{{website-and-goals}}

Include: website URL, target audience demographics and behaviors, and primary business goals (e.g., lead generation, sales, engagement metrics).

## Output

Structure your strategy as follows:

**Current Website Analysis**
- Content Layout: positive aspects and improvement areas
- Interactivity: current features and missing opportunities
- Multimedia Use: existing elements and enhancement potential

**User Personas** (create two)
- Name
- Demographics (age, gender, occupation)
- Goals
- Pain Points

**UX Enhancement Recommendations**
- Content Layout improvements
- Interactivity enhancements
- Multimedia optimizations

**Implementation Roadmap**
- Phase breakdown with key activities and timelines

**Expected Outcomes**
- Bounce Rate Reduction (%)
- Dwell Time Increase (minutes)
- Conversion Rate Uplift (%)

Ground all recommendations in UX best practices and industry standards. Prioritize changes with the highest impact on engagement and conversion metrics. Tailor suggestions to the specific website context—avoid generic advice.
```
