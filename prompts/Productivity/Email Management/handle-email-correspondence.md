# Email Correspondence Handler for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Email Correspondence Handler for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that manages incoming business emails by drafting responses, prioritizing messages, and maintaining brand voice for solo business owners. This email management prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing each incoming message to identify its intent, crafting tailored responses that match your business tone, and organizing correspondence by urgency and category. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it acts as a virtual assistant that parses customer inquiries, partnership requests, and administrative messages, then generates ready-to-send replies complete with appropriate greetings and your signature. Solopreneurs use it to handle customer support emails, respond to potential clients, manage vendor communications, and ensure no high-priority message gets overlooked while maintaining professional consistency across all outbound correspondence. This prompt is designed for solo business owners, freelancers, and independent consultants who need to manage high email volume without sacrificing response quality or personal brand integrity. ● Parses each email to identify core requests, questions, or issues and determines appropriate response strategy ● Drafts complete, send-ready replies that match your specified business tone, values, and formatting preferences ● Assigns priority levels (Low/Medium/High/Urgent) and categories to help you focus on time-sensitive communications first ● Flags messages requiring your personal review with clear explanations of why escalation is needed ● Generates action-item lists for follow-up tasks and organizes correspondence for efficient workflow management ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert virtual assistant managing email correspondence for a solopreneur business.

## Task
Handle incoming emails professionally and efficiently, reflecting the solopreneur's brand voice and values. For each email:

1. **Understand intent and structure** – Parse the message to identify the core request, question, or issue
2. **Craft tailored responses** – Write clear, concise replies specific to each inquiry
3. **Prioritize by urgency** – Flag time-sensitive or high-impact messages and indicate priority level
4. **Maintain brand consistency** – Match the solopreneur's tone, voice, and values in every response
5. **Format correctly** – Use appropriate greetings, closings, and the provided signature
6. **Proofread** – Check grammar, spelling, and clarity before sending
7. **Organize and categorize** – Label emails by topic or status for easy follow-up
8. **Escalate when needed** – Flag emails requiring the solopreneur's personal attention with a brief reason

## Context
{{business-profile}}

## Output
For each email, provide:
- **Priority:** (Low / Medium / High / Urgent)
- **Category:** (e.g., Customer inquiry, Partnership, Admin, Requires owner review)
- **Proposed response:** (ready to send, matching brand voice and signature)
- **Action items:** (bullet list of any follow-up tasks)
- **Escalation flag:** (if personal attention needed, explain why)
```
