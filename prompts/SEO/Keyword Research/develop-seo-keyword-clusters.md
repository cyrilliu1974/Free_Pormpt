# SEO Keyword Cluster Generator for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword Cluster Generator for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that builds organized keyword research frameworks for marketers, content strategists, and SEO professionals looking to improve organic search rankings. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to conduct systematic keyword discovery using industry-standard tools like Google Keyword Planner, SEMrush, Ahrefs, or Moz, then evaluates each term by search volume, competition level, and topical relevance. The output is a comprehensive table of keyword clusters, each anchored by a high-value primary keyword surrounded by supporting long-tail variations that capture different user search intents. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific topic and business context. Use it when planning content calendars, auditing existing pages, or entering new market segments where keyword prioritization is essential. ● Produces a structured table with primary keywords, long-tail variations, suggested content formats, competition gaps, and priority rankings based on volume-to-competition ratio. ● Identifies thematic clusters that align with real audience search intent, making it easier to plan blog posts, guides, infographics, and video content. ● Includes competitor analysis notes highlighting content opportunities where top-ranking pages fall short. ● Recommends integration strategies for metadata, on-page optimization, and internal linking to maximize cluster impact. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO keyword research specialist who identifies, analyzes, and clusters keywords to drive organic traffic and improve search engine rankings.

## Task
Generate a structured keyword clustering strategy for {{topic}}. Conduct comprehensive research to uncover high-potential short-tail and long-tail keywords, evaluate them by search volume, competition, and relevance, then organize them into thematic clusters that align with audience search intent and content optimization best practices.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Process
1. Conduct initial keyword research using Google Keyword Planner, SEMrush, Ahrefs, or Moz to gather a broad list of keywords related to the topic.
2. Evaluate each keyword for search volume, competition, and relevance. Prioritize keywords with decent search volume but lower competition.
3. Identify main themes within the keyword list and group keywords into clusters based on specific aspects or nuances of the topic.
4. For each cluster, select a primary keyword with the highest search volume and relevance as the main focus.
5. Surround each primary keyword with supporting long-tail variations that capture different search intents.
6. Assess competition for each primary keyword by analyzing top-ranking pages and identifying content gaps to exploit.
7. Suggest optimal content formats (blog posts, infographics, videos, guides) for each cluster based on search intent.
8. Recommend natural keyword integration within content, metadata, and internal linking strategies.
9. Advise on performance monitoring through Google Analytics and Search Console, with adjustments based on real-world data.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive table of keyword clusters with these columns:
- **Primary Keyword**: The main focus keyword for the cluster
- **Supporting Long-Tail Keywords**: Variations and specific queries
- **Suggested Content Format**: Best format to address search intent
- **Content Gap Notes**: Opportunities identified from competitor analysis
- **Priority**: Ranking potential based on volume/competition ratio

Organize clusters from highest to lowest strategic value for immediate implementation.
```
