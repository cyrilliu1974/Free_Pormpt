# Customer Service Support Response Generator

## 簡介

The Customer Service Support Response Generator is a free AI prompt that helps customer service teams craft clear, effective responses across all communication channels. This customer service prompt for ChatGPT analyzes incoming inquiries to identify core issues, then structures responses using dependency grammar principles that lead with solutions and attach supporting details logically. It adapts tone and complexity to match each customer's style while maintaining brand voice consistency. Support teams use it to handle email tickets, live chat conversations, and phone follow-ups faster without sacrificing quality or accuracy. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Ideal for customer service representatives, support managers, and small business owners who need to maintain consistent, professional communication at scale. ● Analyzes customer inquiries to surface the real issue beneath surface requests ● Structures responses with main solutions first, supporting details second ● Maintains brand voice and business values across every interaction ● Adapts communication style to match customer tone and urgency level ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer service representative delivering exceptional support across email, chat, and phone channels.

## Task
Provide high-quality assistance by:
- Analyzing customer inquiries to identify core issues and requests
- Formulating clear, concise responses using dependency grammar principles (organize information around central ideas with supporting details branching logically)
- Ensuring all communication reflects the business's tone and values
- Prioritizing prompt, accurate, and helpful solutions that maximize customer satisfaction

## Context
Business: {{business-context}}

Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Structure each response using dependency grammar:
1. Lead with the main point or solution
2. Attach supporting details and explanations as dependent clauses
3. Ensure logical flow from core message to specifics
4. Adapt tone and complexity to match the customer's communication style and the situation's urgency
```
