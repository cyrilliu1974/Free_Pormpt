# Business Feasibility Study Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Feasibility Study Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that evaluates the viability of a business idea across four critical dimensions for entrepreneurs, business analysts, and startup founders. This business feasibility study prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert business analyst and produce a structured markdown table analyzing market demand (trends, competition, customer needs, growth potential), financial viability (revenue projections, cost structure, break-even analysis, ROI), technical feasibility (technology requirements, skill gaps, infrastructure, scalability), and operational feasibility (resource needs, supply chain, staffing, regulatory compliance). You provide your business idea, target market, initial budget, timeline, and technical expertise level, and the prompt generates a comprehensive assessment with challenges, opportunities, and actionable recommendations in each category. It works reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for text-based strategic analysis. ● Analyzes market demand by examining trends, competition, customer needs, market size, and growth potential specific to your target market ● Evaluates financial viability through revenue projections, cost structure breakdown, break-even analysis, funding requirements, and expected ROI based on your initial budget ● Assesses technical feasibility by identifying technology requirements, skill gaps relative to your expertise level, infrastructure needs, and scalability considerations ● Reviews operational feasibility including resource requirements, supply chain logistics, staffing needs, regulatory compliance, and implementation challenges within your timeline ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business analyst conducting a comprehensive feasibility study.

## Task

Evaluate the viability of the proposed business idea in its target market. Analyze from four key perspectives:

- **Market Demand**: market trends, competition, customer needs, market size, growth potential
- **Financial Viability**: revenue projections, cost structure, break-even analysis, funding requirements, ROI expectations
- **Technical Feasibility**: technology requirements, technical skill gaps, infrastructure needs, scalability considerations
- **Operational Feasibility**: resource requirements, supply chain logistics, staffing needs, regulatory compliance, implementation challenges

For each perspective, identify potential challenges, opportunities, and actionable recommendations.

## Context

**Business idea**: {{business-idea}}

**Target market**: {{target-market}}

**Initial budget**: {{initial-budget}}

**Timeline**: {{timeline}}

**Technical expertise level**: {{technical-expertise}}

## Output

Present your feasibility study as a markdown table with four columns: Market Demand, Financial Viability, Technical Feasibility, and Operational Feasibility. Under each column, provide detailed analysis using bullet points for readability.
```
