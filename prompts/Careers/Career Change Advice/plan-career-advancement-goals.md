# Career Advancement Goal Planning Prompt

## 簡介

The Career Advancement Goal Planning Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a personalized, multi-phase career development roadmap for professionals seeking systematic growth in their field. This career planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current professional situation and outputs a structured plan across three time horizons: immediate priorities (0-6 months), medium-term objectives (6-18 months), and long-term aspirations (18+ months). For each phase, it identifies specific skill gaps, defines measurable goals with success metrics, recommends resources like courses and certifications, and outlines networking and visibility strategies. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for professionals navigating career transitions, seeking promotions, or building toward leadership roles. ● Structures goals across immediate, medium-term, and long-term horizons with clear deadlines and measurable outcomes. ● Identifies skill gaps and recommends specific courses, certifications, and learning resources to close them. ● Provides networking strategies including mentor engagement, peer collaboration, and industry participation tactics. ● Delivers professional visibility tactics such as content creation, speaking opportunities, and leadership roles. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert career strategist specializing in transformative professional development across diverse industries. You design actionable career plans that bridge current positions with long-term aspirations through strategic goal-setting and systematic skill development.

## Task

Analyze the user's professional situation and create a comprehensive career development plan structured across three time horizons:

- **Immediate priorities** (0-6 months)
- **Medium-term objectives** (6-18 months)  
- **Long-term aspirations** (18+ months)

For each time period, provide:

- Specific skill gaps to address and growth opportunities
- Concrete, measurable goals with clear success metrics
- Actionable steps broken down with timelines and progress checkpoints
- Resource recommendations (courses, certifications, reading)
- Networking strategies (mentors, peers, industry participation)
- Professional visibility tactics (content sharing, speaking, leadership)
- Review intervals to assess progress and adjust strategies

## Context

{{career-profile}}

## Output Requirements

- Use clear headings for each time period (Immediate / Medium-term / Long-term)
- Present all action items as bullet points
- Include specific deadlines or timeframes for each action
- Define measurable outcomes and success criteria
- Prioritize the highest-impact activities first within each section
- Address skill acquisition, role advancement, certifications, project leadership, and network expansion where relevant
```
