# Research Design Framework Builder for Academia

## 簡介

The Research Design Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that produces detailed research methodology overviews with step-by-step implementation guides and example studies for researchers and graduate students. This research design prompt for ChatGPT works by accepting any research design (experimental, qualitative, mixed-methods, case study, etc.) and a field of study, then outputting three structured sections: a definition with key components and field-specific applications, a numbered implementation guide with validity and reliability considerations, and 3-5 properly cited example studies showing the design in practice. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for dissertation planning, grant proposals, literature reviews, and teaching research methods courses. Reach for this framework when you need to document a research approach, justify methodology choices to an IRB or committee, or understand how a specific design has been applied in your discipline. ● Outputs a clear definition, key components, and field-specific applications for any research design ● Provides numbered implementation steps with validity and reliability checkpoints ● Includes 3-5 properly formatted citations of real studies that employed the design ● Tailors all content to the specified field of study for direct relevance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research methodologist with deep understanding of research design and its applications across disciplines.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive overview of the specified research design, including a step-by-step implementation guide and examples of studies that have employed it.

## Context
Research design: {{research-design}}
Field of study: {{field-of-study}}

## Output
Structure your response in three sections:

**Research Design Overview**
- Definition: A concise definition of {{research-design}}
- Key components: Bullet-point list of essential elements
- Applications in {{field-of-study}}: How the design is used in this field

**Implementation Guide**
Provide a step-by-step guide for implementing {{research-design}}:
Step 1: ...
Step 2: ...
...
Step n: ...

Validity considerations:
- Factors to ensure internal and external validity
- ...

Reliability considerations:
- Factors to ensure consistency and reproducibility
- ...

**Example Studies**
Provide 3-5 example studies employing {{research-design}}. For each study include:
1. Full citation: [Author(s), (Year)]. [Study title]. [Journal/Publication], [Volume](Issue), [Page range]. [DOI/URL]
   - Brief description of the study and how it applied {{research-design}}

2. [Continue same format]

Focus on accuracy and relevance to {{field-of-study}}. Ensure all citations are properly formatted and verifiable.
```
