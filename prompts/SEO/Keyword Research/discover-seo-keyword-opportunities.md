# SEO Keyword Research Prompt for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword Research Prompt for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive keyword analysis to uncover high-volume search terms and trending subtopics for content creators, marketers, and SEO specialists. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT breaks any topic into 5-8 subtopics, then systematically identifies 20-30 keywords complete with monthly search volume, competition difficulty scores (0-100 scale), and user intent classifications (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured table that balances search demand with realistic ranking opportunities. Real-world applications include planning editorial calendars, prioritizing content creation, identifying content gaps, and aligning blog posts or landing pages with actual searcher behavior. The output flags seasonal trends and geographic variations, making it practical for both evergreen and timely content strategies. This prompt is built for SEO professionals, content strategists, and digital marketers who need actionable keyword data without manual spreadsheet work. ● Segments broad topics into 5-8 subtopics to ensure comprehensive coverage of search demand ● Records monthly search volume, competition difficulty, and user intent for each of 20-30 keywords ● Balances high-volume opportunities with achievable competition levels for realistic ranking goals ● Flags seasonal trends and geographic variations to inform content timing and localization decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO keyword research specialist who identifies high-volume search terms and trending subtopics to guide content strategy.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research for {{topic}}, producing a strategic list of keywords with critical metrics (search volume, competition difficulty, user intent) to inform content creation and optimization.

## Method
1. **Segment the topic**: Break {{topic}} into 5-8 broader subtopics to focus research on areas with significant search interest.
2. **Research keywords**: Use SEO tools (Google Keyword Planner, SEMrush, Ahrefs, or Moz) to find high-volume keywords and trending terms for each subtopic.
3. **Assess competition**: Identify keywords that balance search volume with achievable competition levels—opportunities where you can realistically rank.
4. **Classify user intent**: Label each keyword as informational, navigational, transactional, or commercial investigation to align content with searcher expectations.
5. **Document data points**: For each keyword, record monthly search volume, competition difficulty score (0-100 scale), and user intent category.
6. **Note patterns**: Flag any seasonal trends or geographic variations that affect content timing or localization.

## Context
{{seo-context}}

## Output
Deliver a table with these columns:
- **Keyword/Subtopic**
- **Monthly Search Volume**
- **Competition Difficulty** (0-100)
- **User Intent** (informational/navigational/transactional/commercial)

Include 20-30 keywords organized by subtopic. Provide brief notes on any seasonal or geographic trends identified. The table should serve as a practical roadmap for content planning that targets real search behavior.
```
