# Blog Post Idea Generator for Product Marketing

## 簡介

The Blog Post Idea Generator for Product Marketing is a free AI prompt that produces organized lists of SEO-optimized blog topics tailored to your product, audience, and content goals. This blog post idea prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product, audience, competitive landscape, and content priorities to generate 10–14 strategic topics split into evergreen and seasonal categories. Each idea includes an SEO-optimized headline, target keyword, difficulty rating, search intent classification, and a content angle that connects customer pain points to your product as a natural solution. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping content teams plan editorial calendars that balance traffic potential with conversion relevance across awareness, consideration, and decision stages of the customer journey. Reach for this prompt when you need a content strategy that treats blog posts as customer acquisition assets rather than standalone articles. ● Categorizes ideas into evergreen content for long-term traffic and seasonal topics for timely relevance. ● Labels each topic with target keyword, search difficulty, intent type, and product connection angle. ● Considers competitive landscape to surface differentiation opportunities. ● Aligns content with specific stages of the customer journey from awareness to decision. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content marketing and SEO strategist specializing in product-focused blog content that drives organic traffic and conversions.

## Task
Generate strategic blog post ideas organized by content longevity (evergreen vs. seasonal). Each idea must address a customer pain point at a specific stage of the purchase journey and naturally lead toward the product as a solution.

## Context
Analyze {{product-and-audience}} to identify customer pain points, search behavior patterns, seasonal trends, timely opportunities, and commercial intent keywords that balance search volume with conversion potential. Consider content angles spanning awareness, consideration, and decision stages.

Assess {{competitive-landscape}} when evaluating topic difficulty and differentiation opportunities.

Prioritize topics according to {{content-goals}}.

## Output
Structure your response with two main sections:

### Evergreen Content Ideas
Provide 6-8 topics, each formatted as:
- **[SEO-optimized headline]** | Target keyword: [primary keyword] | Difficulty: [Low/Medium/High] | Intent: [Informational/Commercial/Transactional] | Angle: [content approach, customer journey stage, and product connection]

### Seasonal Content Ideas
Provide 4-6 topics, each formatted as:
- **[SEO-optimized headline]** | Target keyword: [primary keyword] | Timing: [season/month] | Difficulty: [Low/Medium/High] | Intent: [Informational/Commercial/Transactional] | Angle: [timely relevance and product connection]
```
