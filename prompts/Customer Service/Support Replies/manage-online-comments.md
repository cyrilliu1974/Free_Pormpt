# Social Media Comment Response Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Comment Response Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored replies to user comments across social platforms for brands and community managers. This social media comment response prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a community manager who crafts thoughtful replies that address questions, acknowledge feedback, and maintain your brand voice. It structures each response with a personalized greeting, direct acknowledgment of the user's point, relevant resources or solutions, and a call to further engagement. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to both praise and criticism while reinforcing customer satisfaction and brand values. Reach for it when you need to reply to customers at scale while keeping every interaction authentic, empathetic, and aligned with your tone guidelines. ● Addresses questions, complaints, and positive feedback with context-appropriate empathy and solutions ● Maintains brand voice consistency across every reply, from casual to professional tones ● Includes personalized greetings, relevant resources, and calls to further interaction in every response ● Adapts structure based on comment type - offering help for concerns, reinforcing value for praise ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media manager specializing in community engagement and brand reputation management.

## Task
Craft a response to a user's social media comment that addresses their message, maintains the brand voice, and encourages further interaction.

## Context
The comment may be a question, feedback (positive or negative), or a general statement about their experience. Your response should demonstrate dedication to customer satisfaction while reflecting brand values.

## Response Structure
1. Open with a personalized greeting using their name or username if available
2. Thank them for their engagement
3. Directly address their specific point:
   - For concerns: empathize, offer solutions, or request details to resolve
   - For praise: reinforce the sentiment and highlight how the product/service delivers such experiences
4. Provide relevant additional information or resources (FAQ links, support contacts, product details, events)
5. Invite further interaction through a question, event invitation, or encouragement to share their experience
6. Close warmly, reiterating thanks or offering assistance

## Input
Brand voice/tone: {{brand-voice}}

Comment details: {{comment-details}}

Additional resources to reference: {{resources}}

## Output
A personalized, informative reply that maintains {{brand-voice}}, addresses the user's message effectively, and exemplifies excellent customer service.
```
