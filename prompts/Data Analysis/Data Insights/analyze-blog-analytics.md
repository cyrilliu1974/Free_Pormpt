# Blog Analytics and Performance Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Blog Analytics and Performance Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates blog content performance and delivers actionable insights for content strategists and digital marketers. This blog analytics prompt for ChatGPT walks through a systematic six-step framework: compiling article data, calculating traffic metrics (page views, unique visitors), measuring engagement (time on page, bounce rate, social shares), analyzing conversions (leads, sales, newsletter sign-ups), identifying patterns among high performers, and comparing results by audience segment and topic. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a ranked table of top-performing articles alongside strategic recommendations. Use it when you need to understand which blog posts resonate most with your audience and why, or when planning your editorial calendar based on proven content types. ● Compiles traffic, engagement, and conversion data into a ranked comparison table of your best articles ● Identifies patterns and commonalities among top performers to reveal what content resonates with your audience ● Delivers specific, actionable recommendations for future content development and optimization opportunities ● Segments analysis by audience type and content topic to surface nuanced performance trends ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing analyst specializing in content performance measurement and strategic optimization.

## Task
Analyze blog performance to identify top-performing articles and extract actionable insights for future content strategy.

## Context
Blog: {{blog-name}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Primary content topics: {{content-topics}}
Key performance indicators: {{kpis}}

## Analysis Method
1. Compile data for all published articles
2. Calculate traffic metrics (page views, unique visitors)
3. Measure engagement levels (time on page, bounce rate, social shares)
4. Analyze conversion data (lead generation, sales, newsletter sign-ups)
5. Identify patterns and commonalities among top performers across metrics
6. Compare performance against audience segment and topic area

## Output
Present findings as:

**Top-Performing Articles Table**

| Article Title | Traffic | Engagement | Conversions |
|---------------|---------|------------|-------------|
| [Data rows for top articles] |

**Key Insights & Recommendations**
- Core findings about content performance patterns
- Trends linking article characteristics to success metrics
- Strategic recommendations for content development
- Specific optimization opportunities based on underperforming areas
```
