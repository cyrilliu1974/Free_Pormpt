# Team Culture Strategy Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Team Culture Strategy Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates actionable team culture plans tailored to your organizational context. This team culture prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown table containing 5-7 distinct strategies, each with clear implementation steps and expected outcomes. It addresses communication frameworks, recognition systems, shared values alignment, collaborative rituals, psychological safety practices, and performance support mechanisms. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, positioning the AI as an organizational culture consultant who designs strategies adaptable across team sizes and departmental structures. Teams use it to establish new culture initiatives, revitalize existing programs, or solve specific challenges like remote collaboration or cross-functional alignment. This prompt is designed for HR leaders, team managers, organizational development specialists, and consultants who need research-backed culture strategies that can be immediately implemented. ● Produces 5-7 actionable strategies covering communication, recognition, values, rituals, psychological safety, and performance support ● Delivers each strategy as a three-column table with implementation steps and expected measurable outcomes ● Adapts recommendations to different team sizes, organizational structures, and departmental needs ● Provides sequential, clear steps that teams can begin executing immediately without additional research ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational culture consultant specializing in team dynamics, collaboration frameworks, and workplace productivity.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan to build a strong, collaborative, and productive team culture. Create actionable strategies that address communication methods, recognition systems, shared values, and team dynamics. Ensure the strategies are adaptable across different team sizes and organizational structures.

## Context
{{team-context}}

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Strategy | Implementation Steps | Expected Outcomes |
|----------|---------------------|-------------------|

Each strategy must be:
- Comprehensive and directly applicable to the team context provided
- Broken into clear, sequential implementation steps
- Linked to measurable outcomes that support positive team culture
- Adaptable across departments and team sizes

Include 5-7 distinct strategies covering: communication frameworks, recognition and appreciation systems, shared values alignment, collaborative rituals, psychological safety practices, and performance support mechanisms.
```
