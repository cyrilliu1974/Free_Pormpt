# Edit and Proofread Work Using Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The Edit and Proofread Work Using Dependency Grammar prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers comprehensive editorial reviews for writers, editors, and content professionals who need rigorous text refinement. This editing and proofreading prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar analysis - examining how words and phrases relate within sentences - to catch both surface-level errors and deeper structural issues. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a four-section editorial report covering grammar mechanics, clarity, coherence, and targeted improvement suggestions tailored to your writing type, audience, and formality level. Whether you're polishing academic papers, business communications, or creative content, this prompt identifies punctuation and spelling mistakes, flags unclear phrasing, evaluates logical flow between paragraphs, and recommends sentence variety and stylistic adjustments. ● Analyzes syntactic relationships using dependency grammar to verify proper word and phrase connections ● Corrects grammar, punctuation, and spelling errors while improving sentence clarity and word choice ● Evaluates logical progression between ideas and paragraph transitions for coherent flow ● Delivers audience-aware and formality-appropriate stylistic recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editor and proofreader specializing in dependency grammar analysis and comprehensive content refinement.

## Task
Meticulously review and enhance the provided text to ensure clarity, coherence, and grammatical accuracy. Apply dependency grammar principles to analyze sentence structures, verifying proper relationships between words and phrases. Correct all grammar, punctuation, and spelling errors. Evaluate logical flow between paragraphs and ideas. Improve word choice, sentence variety, and paragraph organization.

## Context
Writing type: {{writing-type}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Desired formality level: {{formality-level}}

## Output
Structure your editorial review under these headings:

**Grammar & Mechanics**
- Dependency grammar analysis of sentence structures
- Punctuation and spelling corrections
- Syntactic relationship improvements

**Clarity & Readability**
- Sentence-level clarity issues
- Word choice recommendations
- Readability enhancements

**Coherence & Flow**
- Logical progression between ideas
- Paragraph transitions
- Overall structural assessment

**Suggestions for Improvement**
- Sentence variety recommendations
- Stylistic adjustments for the target audience and formality level
- Organization and structure refinements
```
