# Flexible Work Policy Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Flexible Work Policy Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based flexible work policies balancing employee autonomy with operational needs for HR leaders and organizational design consultants. This flexible work policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your organization profile and current flexibility baseline to produce a complete policy document covering eligibility criteria, remote work protocols, flexible scheduling windows, compressed work weeks, and accountability frameworks. It addresses how different work arrangements affect productivity, collaboration, and service delivery across departments while ensuring equitable application across all organizational levels. Use it when designing new flexible work programs, updating existing policies, or piloting hybrid work arrangements that require clear guidelines and measurable performance standards. ● Defines role-based eligibility criteria that account for operational requirements and interdependencies across functions ● Specifies flexible scheduling, remote work, and compressed hour arrangements with clear parameters and approval processes ● Establishes accountability frameworks, performance standards, and monitoring approaches that maintain business outcomes ● Includes success metrics for productivity, satisfaction, collaboration, and continuous improvement mechanisms ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR strategist and organizational design consultant specializing in evidence-based flexible work policy development.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive flexible work policy that balances employee autonomy with operational excellence. Include clear eligibility criteria, implementation guidelines, accountability measures, and success metrics tailored to the organization's context.

## Context

**Organization Profile:**
{{organization-profile}}

**Current Flexibility Baseline:**
{{current-flexibility}}

Analyze how different work arrangements (flexible scheduling, remote work, compressed hours) impact productivity, collaboration, and service delivery across functions. Address equity concerns and ensure consistent application across all organizational levels.

## Output

Structure the policy document with these sections:

1. **Eligibility Criteria** – Define which roles and departments qualify for each flexible work option based on operational requirements and role dependencies
2. **Flexible Work Options** – Detail available arrangements (remote work protocols, flexible scheduling windows, compressed work weeks) with specific parameters
3. **Implementation Guidelines** – Provide rollout timelines, pilot program frameworks, and departmental adaptation strategies
4. **Accountability & Performance Standards** – Establish measurable expectations, monitoring approaches, and performance maintenance frameworks
5. **Success Metrics** – Define KPIs for productivity, employee satisfaction, collaboration effectiveness, and business outcomes
6. **Equity & Consistency** – Address fair application across roles and levels, accommodation processes, and dispute resolution
7. **Continuous Improvement** – Include feedback mechanisms, review cycles, and policy iteration processes

Provide actionable, department-specific recommendations where role requirements differ significantly.
```
