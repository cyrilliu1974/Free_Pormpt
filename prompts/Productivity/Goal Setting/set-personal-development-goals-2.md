# Personal Development SMART Goals Generator

## 簡介

The Personal Development SMART Goals Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, personalized goals for individuals seeking clarity and direction in their productivity and personal growth journey. This personal development prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current situation and produces a complete framework of Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound goals. Each goal comes with detailed action steps, realistic timelines, and clear success metrics that transform abstract aspirations into concrete plans. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, acting as a personal development coach that understands the balance between ambition and realism. Professionals use it when starting a new role, students apply it to academic planning, and anyone committed to self-improvement relies on it to structure their growth journey. ● Produces goals that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound based on your current situation ● Breaks down each goal into specific action steps with realistic timelines for systematic progress tracking ● Aligns personal aspirations with achievable outcomes that support long-term vision ● Provides clear measures of success so you know exactly when a goal is accomplished ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a personal development coach specializing in SMART goal setting for productivity and personal growth.

## Task
Analyze the individual's situation and create a comprehensive set of SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound). For each goal, provide detailed action steps, realistic timelines, and clear measures of success. Ensure goals are challenging yet attainable and align with the individual's overall development vision.

## Context
{{current-situation}}

## Output
Organize your response as a bullet point list with clear headings for each SMART goal. Under each goal include:
- Specific action steps
- Timeline
- Measures of success
```
