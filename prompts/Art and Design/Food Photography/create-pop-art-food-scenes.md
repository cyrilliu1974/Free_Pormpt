# Pop Art Food Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Pop Art Food Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates mixed-media artwork blending photorealistic subjects with whimsical cartoon food chaos for restaurant brands, content creators, and food marketers. This pop art food prompt for Midjourney and Flux produces images of subjects sitting in retro red leather diner booths, surrounded by thick-line cartoon food creatures - pizza slices, burger beasts, flying ketchup bottles, and angry fries - rendered in a bold Ketchup Red, Mustard Yellow, and Lettuce Green palette. The prompt layers flat 2D Pop Art illustrations over ultra-detailed photorealistic diner settings with checkerboard floors, neon signs, and warm afternoon light. Use it to turn standard food photography into eye-catching visual storytelling that stands out on social media, menus, blog headers, and brand campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need playful, shareable food content that merges reality with illustrated energy. ● Blends photorealistic subjects and settings with flat, thick-line cartoon food overlays for unique brand imagery. ● Customize the subject description and cuisine setting to match any restaurant theme, personality, or menu style. ● Generates maximalist retro-pop visuals with floating cartoon food monsters, text bursts, and chaotic doodle elements. ● Perfect for social media posts, food blog headers, promotional posters, and engaging brand storytelling. ## Prompt

```
Mixed-media masterpiece combining photorealistic photography with thick-line Pop Art cartoon overlays.

**Subject:** {{subject-description}} sitting in a red leather diner booth, holding a burger, shocked but amused expression looking at floating doodle food. Retro-style denim jacket, red polka-dot scarf, sunglasses on head.

**Setting:** {{cuisine-setting}} with checkerboard floor and neon sign in window. Warm diner glow, afternoon sun streaming through windows.

**Illustrated Food Chaos:** Vibrant 2D cartoon creatures swarming around the subject — pizza slices surfing on cheese waves, burger beasts with lettuce tongues, angry French fry box, flying ketchup bottles. Mustard splashes, sesame seeds, heat lines, 'YUM' text bursts scattered throughout. Ketchup Red, Mustard Yellow, Lettuce Green color palette.

**Style:** Ultra-detailed photorealistic textures contrasted against flat Pop Art cartoons. Retro-pop, savory, maximalist, chaotic energy.
```
