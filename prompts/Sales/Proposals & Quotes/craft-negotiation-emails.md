# Negotiation Summary Email Writer

## 簡介

The Negotiation Summary Email Writer is a free AI prompt that drafts strategic negotiation emails for business professionals closing deals and managing ongoing commercial discussions. This negotiation email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete, ready-to-send message with a professional subject line, warm opening, and three core sections: Agreed Points that memorialize settled terms, Open Issues that diplomatically present both positions while protecting your stance, and Next Steps with specific action items and deadlines. Real use cases include vendor contract negotiations, client pricing discussions, partnership term sheets, and any commercial dialogue where you need to lock in progress, frame remaining gaps, and drive momentum without damaging the relationship. Reach for this prompt when you need to summarize a negotiation call or meeting in writing, confirm verbal agreements, or move stalled discussions forward with clarity and tact. ● Memorializes agreed points as mutual wins to lock in gains and prevent backsliding. ● Frames open issues as solvable problems, presenting both sides fairly while maintaining your negotiating position. ● Sets concrete next steps with action items and realistic deadlines to create urgency without ultimatums. ● Maintains a collaborative, professional tone that protects relationships and keeps the deal moving forward. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a business communication specialist drafting a strategic negotiation summary email that memorializes agreements, diplomatically frames open issues, and creates forward momentum while protecting the sender's interests.

## Task

Draft a polished, ready-to-send email that includes:

- Professional subject line
- Warm opening that thanks the recipient and establishes a collaborative tone
- **Agreed Points** section using bullets to memorialize what's settled
- **Open Issues** section that fairly presents both positions while maintaining negotiating stance
- **Next Steps** section with specific action items and reasonable deadlines
- Positive closing that reaffirms commitment to finalizing the deal

## Context

{{negotiation-context}}

{{recipient-and-relationship}}

## Strategy

- Frame agreements as mutual wins
- Present open issues as solvable problems rather than conflicts
- Use collaborative language while being firm on key points
- Create urgency without ultimatums
- Professional but personable; diplomatic but clear about positions

## Output

Provide a complete email including subject line, greeting, body with clear section headers (Agreed Points, Open Issues, Next Steps), closing, and sign-off. Use bullet points for clarity and ensure the email drives toward concrete next steps.
```
