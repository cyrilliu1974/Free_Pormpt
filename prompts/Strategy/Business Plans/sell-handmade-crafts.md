# Handmade Craft Business Plan & Launch Guide

## 簡介

The Handmade Craft Business Plan & Launch Guide is a free AI prompt that creates a structured roadmap for starting and scaling an online handmade craft business as a side hustle. This handmade craft business prompt for ChatGPT walks you through eight core stages: market research and niche validation, product photography on any budget, cost-based pricing strategy, platform comparison (Etsy, Shopify, Amazon Handmade, social commerce), customer service workflows, time management for limited weekly hours, quality control standards, and metrics-driven scaling. You provide your craft type, target customer demographics, skill level, available hours, and budget; the prompt returns a numbered guide with actionable sub-steps, competitor analysis methods, equipment recommendations, fee-structure breakdowns, communication templates, and batching techniques tailored to your constraints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Use this prompt when you want to turn a crafting hobby into a revenue stream but need a realistic, dependency-sequenced plan that respects side-hustle time and cash constraints. ● Produces market research frameworks, competitor analysis checklists, and unique positioning strategies specific to your craft category. ● Delivers side-by-side platform comparisons with fee structures, audience fit, and multi-channel recommendations. ● Provides pricing formulas that account for materials, labor, overhead, and market benchmarking to protect profit margins. ● Includes time-management schedules, batching workflows, and work-life balance tactics designed for limited weekly availability. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert e-commerce consultant specializing in handmade crafts and online marketplaces.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for starting an online handmade craft business as a side hustle. Structure each step to build logically on the previous one, covering the full journey from market research through scaling.

## Context

**Craft & Business Details:**
{{craft-and-business-context}}
(Include: the type of craft, target customer demographics, current skill level, hours available per week, and starting budget)

## Output

Deliver the guide as a numbered list with main steps as headings and sub-steps as bullet points. For each major step, provide:

1. **Market Research & Niche Validation**
   - Competitor analysis methods
   - Demand assessment techniques
   - Unique positioning strategies

2. **Product Photography & Presentation**
   - Equipment recommendations for different budgets
   - Lighting and composition best practices
   - Editing workflows

3. **Pricing Strategy**
   - Cost calculation (materials, time, overhead)
   - Market-rate benchmarking
   - Profit margin guidelines

4. **Platform Selection**
   - Comparison of Etsy, Shopify, Amazon Handmade, and social commerce
   - Fee structures and audience fit
   - Multi-channel strategies

5. **Customer Service Best Practices**
   - Communication templates
   - Returns and refunds policies
   - Review management

6. **Time Management**
   - Production scheduling for limited hours
   - Batching and efficiency techniques
   - Work-life balance strategies

7. **Quality Control**
   - Consistency standards
   - Inspection processes
   - Packaging guidelines

8. **Scaling the Business**
   - Inventory management
   - Outsourcing options
   - Growth metrics to track

Include practical examples and actionable tips tailored to the craft type and constraints provided. Make recommendations realistic for a side-hustle timeline and budget.
```
