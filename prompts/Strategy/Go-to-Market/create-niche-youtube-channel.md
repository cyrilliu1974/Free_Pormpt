# YouTube Channel Launch Strategy Prompt for Side Hustles

## 簡介

The YouTube Channel Launch Strategy Prompt for Side Hustles is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive go-to-market plan for creators launching niche YouTube channels around side-hustle topics. This YouTube channel strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through every phase of a successful launch: pre-launch competitor analysis, content pillar development, keyword research and metadata optimization, video production workflows, cross-platform promotion tactics, community engagement strategies, and 90-day performance milestones. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured, numbered action plan tailored to your side-hustle topic, target audience, and creator background. Real use cases include solopreneurs validating a content niche, coaches building authority channels, and freelancers turning expertise into scalable video content. Reach for this prompt when you need a roadmap that connects audience research to sustainable growth and monetization readiness. ● Generates pre-launch competitor analysis and content pillar frameworks specific to your side-hustle niche and audience ● Provides keyword strategy, metadata templates, and discoverability tactics to improve search rankings and recommendations ● Outlines video formats, production workflows, publishing cadence, and quality standards suited to solo creators ● Includes cross-platform promotion tactics, community-building techniques, and metrics to track during the first 90 days ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert YouTube content strategist specializing in niche channel launches and side-hustle content.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive launch strategy for a YouTube channel focused on {{side-hustle-topic}} and targeting {{niche-audience}}. The strategy must cover content research, video production, channel promotion, audience engagement, SEO optimization, and sustainable growth tactics.

## Context
Channel creator context: {{creator-context}}

Consider:
- Pre-launch audience research and competitor analysis
- Content pillars and publishing cadence
- Video formats, production workflows, and quality standards
- Keyword strategy, metadata optimization, and discoverability
- Cross-platform promotion and community-building tactics
- Metrics to track and milestones for the first 90 days
- Monetization readiness and long-term sustainability

## Output
Provide the strategy as a numbered list with clearly labeled main sections and indented subsections. Each recommendation should be actionable and specific to the niche and topic provided.
```
