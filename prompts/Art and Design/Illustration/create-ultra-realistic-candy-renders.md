# Ultra-Realistic Candy Text 3D Render Prompt

## 簡介

The Ultra-Realistic Candy Text 3D Render Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms any text into photorealistic candy-shaped letterforms set inside a customizable candy world scene. It produces octane-quality 3D renders with volumetric lighting, soft highlights, contact shadows, reflective surface finishes, internal light refraction, and detailed sugar crystal textures - perfect for logos, event graphics, product packaging mockups, and social media visuals. This candy render prompt for Midjourney works across Flux, Nano Banana, and similar diffusion models, letting you specify both the text to render and the surrounding candy environment in two simple variables. Designers, brand creatives, and content creators reach for it when they need eye-catching typographic illustrations that combine technical realism with playful subject matter. ● Renders any word or short phrase as glossy hard-candy letterforms with internal light refraction and sugar crystal detail. ● Configurable candy world background - specify colors, shapes, scattered candies, or abstract sugar landscapes to frame the text. ● Built-in octane render quality parameters, volumetric lighting, subtle contact shadows, and reflective surface finish for photorealism. ● Square 1:1 aspect ratio output optimized for social posts, profile images, and thumbnail graphics. ## Prompt

```

```
