# Grievance Redressal Procedure Builder for HR Policies

## 簡介

The Grievance Redressal Procedure Builder for HR Policies is a free AI prompt that creates structured, legally-compliant grievance procedures for organizations seeking to formalize workplace dispute resolution. This grievance redressal procedure prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok generates a detailed framework that addresses informal resolution, formal investigations, serious misconduct cases, and appeal pathways. It specifies exact timelines for each stage (in days or weeks), defines roles and responsibilities for HR personnel, line managers, and senior leadership, and includes confidentiality protocols and documentation requirements aligned with ACAS Grievance Handling Code and ILO Recommendation No. 130 principles. Organizations use it when drafting new HR policies, updating existing grievance processes to meet international labor standards, or preparing for audits and legal compliance reviews. ● Defines separate resolution tracks for informal grievances, formal complaints, and serious misconduct allegations, each with stage-specific timelines and responsible parties. ● Specifies confidentiality measures, documentation requirements, and record-retention protocols to protect both employees and the organization. ● Includes appeal mechanisms and external escalation pathways (mediation, arbitration, regulatory bodies) when internal processes reach an impasse. ● Structures output with clear section headings and bullet points, making the procedure easy to integrate into employee handbooks and HR management systems. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR policy architect and employment law specialist designing grievance procedures that meet international labor standards (ACAS, ILO Recommendation No. 130) while balancing employee protection and organizational efficiency.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, legally-compliant grievance redressal procedure covering the full lifecycle from initial complaint to final resolution.

## Context
The procedure must address:
- Multiple resolution tracks (informal, formal, serious misconduct)
- Specific timelines for each stage
- Confidentiality protocols and documentation requirements
- Clear escalation pathways and appeal mechanisms
- Defined roles and responsibilities (HR personnel, line managers, senior leadership)
- External escalation options when internal processes are insufficient

Organizational details:
{{org-context}}

## Output
Structure your procedure with clear section headings in detailed bullet-point format. For each stage, specify:
- Timeline (exact days/weeks)
- Responsible party
- Required actions and documentation
- Escalation triggers
- Confidentiality measures

Ensure transparency, fairness, and robust employee protection throughout.
```
