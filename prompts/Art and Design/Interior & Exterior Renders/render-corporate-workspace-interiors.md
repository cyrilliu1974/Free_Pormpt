## 簡介

The Corporate Workspace Interior Render Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates ultra-photorealistic office interior visualizations for architects, designers, and corporate real estate teams. This prompt produces 35mm eye-level architectural renders showcasing modern work pods with floor-to-ceiling glass, layered acoustic ceiling systems, and premium material palettes - all from a single text command. As a corporate office prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, it handles complex spatial relationships, physically-based lighting, and material depth that would typically require hours in 3D modeling software like 3ds Max or V-Ray.
