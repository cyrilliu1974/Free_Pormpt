# Website Copywriting Prompt for Multiple Pages

## 簡介

The Website Copywriting Prompt for Multiple Pages is a free AI prompt that generates persuasive, SEO-optimized copy for complete business websites organized by section and ready to publish. This website copywriting prompt for ChatGPT produces full-page copy for Home, About, Services/Products, and Contact sections, each with headlines, subheadings, body text, bullet points, and CTA placement recommendations. It works by analyzing your business context, unique selling points, target keywords, and desired call-to-action, then crafting benefit-driven content using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and readability. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is ideal for businesses launching new sites, agencies managing client projects, or marketers refreshing existing web content without hiring specialized copywriters. ● Outputs complete copy for four core website sections - Home (hero, value proposition, benefits), About (brand story, credibility), Services/Products (detailed offerings), and Contact (conversion messaging) ● Integrates SEO keywords naturally throughout the content while maintaining persuasive, reader-friendly language ● Structures each section with scannable formatting including short paragraphs, bullet points, and mobile-friendly layout recommendations ● Provides clear CTA placement guidance tailored to your specific conversion goal, whether that's booking a call, requesting a quote, or making a purchase ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in conversion-focused web content.

## Task
Write persuasive, SEO-optimized copy for a business website that attracts visitors and converts them into customers. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and flow.

## Context
Business and audience: {{business-context}}

Unique selling points: {{usp}}

Primary SEO keywords: {{keywords}}

Desired call-to-action: {{cta}}

## Process
1. Analyze the target audience's needs and pain points based on the business context
2. Craft a compelling value proposition that differentiates this business
3. Develop benefit-driven headlines and subheadings that capture attention
4. Write persuasive body copy that addresses customer pain points and emphasizes the unique selling points
5. Integrate the keywords naturally throughout the content
6. Place clear calls-to-action that guide visitors toward {{cta}}
7. Ensure content is scannable with short paragraphs, bullet points, and mobile-friendly formatting

## Output
Provide website copy organized by section with clear headings:
- **Home** – hero section, value proposition, key benefits
- **About** – brand story, credibility elements
- **Services/Products** – detailed offerings with benefits
- **Contact** – conversion-focused messaging

For each section, include:
- Headlines and subheadings
- Body copy
- Bullet points highlighting key content elements
- Placement recommendations for the call-to-action
```
