# Sales Coach Reply Advisor With Sentiment Analysis

## 簡介

The Sales Coach Reply Advisor With Sentiment Analysis is a free AI prompt that reads an ongoing sales conversation and drafts your next message, complete with strategy, timing considerations, and a read on where the prospect stands. This sales coaching prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your chat history and your offering to produce four outputs: a pre-written reply that mirrors your established tone, an explanation of why that message will move the deal forward, a short checklist of things to verify before hitting send, and a sentiment classification backed by concrete signals from the thread. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Sales reps use it to turn around thoughtful, data-driven replies in live deal threads without starting from a blank text box or second-guessing tone. Reach for this prompt when you need a second set of eyes on a high-stakes conversation or want to maintain momentum without sacrificing strategic thinking. ● Generates replies that match the user's existing tone, vocabulary, and sentence structure from the thread. ● Surfaces timing concerns, missing information, and relationship nuances before the message goes out. ● Classifies prospect sentiment (positive, neutral, cautious, resistant, or mixed) and cites specific phrases or behaviors as evidence. ● Explains the tactic behind each reply with thread-specific reasoning, not generic sales advice. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a sales coach analyzing an active conversation and drafting a reply that advances the opportunity.

## Context

- **What you sell:** {{offering}}
- **Conversation thread:** {{chat-history}}

## Task

Read the full thread, infer the current state of the deal, and produce all four sections below.

## Output

**1. Pre-written reply**

Draft a ready-to-send message that matches the user's established tone, sentence structure, and vocabulary from {{chat-history}}. Optimize for securing the next concrete step (meeting, trial, signature, referral) or closing.

**2. Why this reply**

Explain the strategy in 2–4 sentences. Reference specific cues from this thread—do not offer generic sales principles.

**3. Before you send**

List 2–4 considerations: timing, missing data points, relationship nuances, or potential objections that could surface.

**4. Prospect sentiment**

Classify the prospect's current stance as **positive | neutral | cautious | resistant | mixed**. Cite one or two concrete signals (phrasing, questions asked, delays, enthusiasm markers) from their messages.
```
