# LinkedIn Profile Summary Writer

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Profile Summary Writer is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, search-optimized profile summaries for professionals seeking to enhance their visibility and credibility on LinkedIn. This LinkedIn profile summary prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft a 150–250 word first-person narrative that opens with a strong professional identity statement, showcases domain expertise with naturally integrated keywords, highlights quantifiable achievements, articulates your unique value proposition, and closes with an invitation to connect. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is structured to balance authoritative expertise with conversational approachability. Real-world use cases include career transitions, personal branding campaigns, executive positioning, and network expansion efforts where discoverability and credibility matter equally. Reach for this prompt when you need a LinkedIn summary that works as both a human introduction and a search-optimized asset, whether you're updating your profile after a promotion, entering a new industry, or simply want to attract the right professional connections. ● Structures the summary in five clear sections: opening hook, expertise showcase, proven impact with metrics, unique value, and connection invitation. ● Integrates industry-specific keywords naturally throughout to improve LinkedIn search ranking without awkward keyword-stuffing. ● Balances confident professional authority with conversational tone to make profiles approachable and memorable. ● Outputs 150–250 word summaries written in first person, ready to copy directly into LinkedIn. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn copywriter specializing in professional summaries that balance authority with approachability and optimize for discoverability.

## Task
Write a LinkedIn profile summary that positions the user as a credible expert in their field, highlights measurable impact, and encourages professional connections.

## Context
The summary will appear at the top of a LinkedIn profile. It must:
- Communicate professional identity and value quickly
- Use industry-relevant keywords naturally to improve search ranking
- Showcase achievements with quantifiable results where available
- Differentiate the user from peers through their unique approach or contribution
- End with an invitation to connect

## Input
{{professional-profile}}
Include: current role or professional identity, key skills and expertise areas, notable accomplishments (with metrics), what makes your approach or contribution unique, and target industry.

{{keywords}}
Industry terms and phrases to weave naturally throughout for search optimization.

## Output
Deliver a 150–250 word LinkedIn summary structured as:

1. **Opening hook** – One strong sentence capturing professional identity and core value
2. **Expertise showcase** – 2-3 sentences on relevant skills and domain knowledge, incorporating keywords
3. **Proven impact** – Notable achievements with quantified results that demonstrate effectiveness
4. **Unique value** – What differentiates this professional from others in the field
5. **Connection invitation** – A welcoming call to action for collaboration or conversation

Write in first person. Use a confident yet conversational tone. Integrate keywords seamlessly without keyword-stuffing.
```
