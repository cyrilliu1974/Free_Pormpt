# Skyscraper Technique SEO Link Building Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Skyscraper Technique SEO Link Building Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a structured strategy to acquire high-quality backlinks and boost search rankings for SEO professionals and content marketers. This Skyscraper Technique prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini guides you through four critical phases: researching top-ranking competitor content and identifying gaps, designing superior content that is more comprehensive and visually engaging, building a targeted outreach list of sites linking to competing pieces, and creating an execution roadmap with timelines and success metrics. You provide your website URL, target keywords, industry niche, and available resources, and the prompt delivers a complete implementation plan with specific action steps and prioritization logic tailored to your capacity. This prompt is ideal for SEO specialists, digital marketers, and content teams looking to systematically improve domain authority and organic traffic through content-driven link acquisition. ● Identifies the top 10 ranking pieces for your target keywords and maps their backlink profiles, traffic drivers, and exploitable weaknesses ● Designs a blueprint for creating superior content with original data, expert perspectives, and better structure than existing competitors ● Builds a prospect list of sites linking to inferior content and crafts personalized outreach templates explaining why your resource is better ● Sequences tasks into a realistic execution roadmap based on your team size, content budget, available tools, and design capability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in content-driven link acquisition.

## Task
Develop a complete Skyscraper Technique implementation plan to improve search rankings and acquire backlinks for {{website-url}}. The strategy should cover:

1. **Content Research & Gap Analysis**
   - Identify the top 10 ranking pieces for the target keywords
   - Map their traffic drivers, backlink profiles, and content structure
   - Pinpoint strengths to retain and weaknesses to exploit

2. **Superior Content Creation**
   - Design content that is more comprehensive, current, and visually engaging than competitors
   - Integrate original data, expert perspectives, or deeper analysis where competitors are shallow
   - Structure writing for clarity and readability
   - Cite all sources to establish authority

3. **Strategic Outreach Plan**
   - Build a prospect list of sites linking to inferior competing content
   - Craft personalized outreach explaining why your content is a better resource
   - Suggest specific replacement opportunities on their pages

4. **Execution Roadmap**
   - Sequence tasks based on {{available-resources}}
   - Set realistic timelines and success metrics

## Context
- **Target keywords:** {{target-keywords}}
- **Industry/niche:** {{industry-niche}}
- **Available resources:** {{available-resources}} (team size, content budget, tools, design capability)

## Output
Deliver a structured implementation plan with clear headings for each phase. Include specific action steps, prioritization logic, and measurable milestones.
```
