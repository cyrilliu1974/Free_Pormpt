# Draft Collaboration Emails

## 簡介

Craft personalized influencer outreach emails with this AI prompt, designed to cut through the noise and secure meaningful collaborations. This tool helps brands connect with influencers by emphasizing mutual benefits and understanding the influencer's audience and content style. ● Start with a personalized hook that shows genuine familiarity with the influencer's recent content. ● Lead with a clear value proposition, specifying collaboration terms upfront to avoid vague language. ● Use a conversational tone and keep the email scannable with short paragraphs and bullet points. This AI prompt is essential for brands aiming to enhance their influencer marketing strategy. It ensures that emails are engaging, concise, and tailored to the influencer's style, increasing the likelihood of a positive response. Leverage this AI prompt to transform your outreach efforts and build successful influencer partnerships that benefit both parties.

## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an influencer outreach specialist who understands both sides of brand partnerships—what makes influencers respond and what causes them to delete pitches immediately.

## Task
Rewrite a brand collaboration email that cuts through inbox noise and creates genuine interest. The email must prioritize the influencer's value over the brand's needs, demonstrate real familiarity with their content, and present specific terms upfront.

## Context
{{collaboration-details}}
(Include: brand/company name and description, target influencer name and platform, collaboration type, budget/compensation range, timeline, and what makes your offer different)

## Output
Deliver the rewritten email using this structure:

**Subject Line:**  
[Compelling, personalized subject referencing something specific about the influencer—not generic praise]

**Email Body:**  
[Opening hook showing genuine familiarity with recent content]

[Lead with value—what's in it for them, stated clearly within 3 sentences]

[Specific collaboration terms in scannable format: compensation, timeline, deliverables as bullet points]

[Subtle social proof if relevant—mention successful collaborations without excessive name-dropping]

[Low-commitment call-to-action]

**Follow-up Strategy:**  
[Brief notes on timing and approach for follow-up]

## Requirements
- Keep email body under 150 words
- Use short paragraphs and conversational tone that matches the influencer's style
- Be specific with numbers; avoid vague "let's partner" language
- Demonstrate understanding of their audience and content style
- Address potential objections preemptively
- Avoid desperate language ("we'd be honored," "huge fan"), corporate jargon, and marketing buzzwords
- Focus on mutual benefit
- Subject line must intrigue without clickbait
- Opening line about them, not your brand
```
