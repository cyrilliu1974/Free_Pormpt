# Research Collaboration Strategy Prompt for Higher Ed

## 簡介

The Research Collaboration Strategy Prompt for Higher Ed is a free AI prompt that builds tailored collaboration frameworks for universities and research institutions looking to strengthen teamwork across departments and disciplines. This research collaboration prompt for ChatGPT takes your institution name, research field, and current collaboration challenges as inputs, then delivers a five-category action plan: communication methods to improve dialogue between researchers, resource management recommendations for facilities and funding, technology integration suggestions for digital collaboration platforms, interdisciplinary strategies to bridge research domains, and evaluation frameworks with metrics and feedback loops. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing bullet-point strategies customized to your institutional context. Use it when launching a new research center, addressing siloed departments, or responding to funding-agency mandates for collaborative science. ● Organizes recommendations into five clear categories: communication, resource management, technology, interdisciplinary approaches, and continuous evaluation. ● Tailors every suggestion to your institution's name, research field, and specific collaboration challenges you describe. ● Covers both human factors (dialogue, cross-departmental culture) and infrastructure (platforms, funding allocation, facilities). ● Includes implementation steps, best practices, and potential obstacles for each recommendation. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research collaboration strategist specializing in higher education.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategy to enhance research collaboration practices at {{institution-name}} within the field of {{research-field}}. Address the specific collaboration challenges and leverage available resources to meet institutional goals.

## Context
Current collaboration challenges: {{collaboration-context}}

Consider interdisciplinary approaches, technology integration, resource allocation, and communication channels. Include key implementation steps, best practices, and potential obstacles.

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a bullet-point list organized under these categories:
- **Communication**: Methods to improve information flow and dialogue between researchers
- **Resource Management**: Allocation and optimization of facilities, funding, and personnel
- **Technology Integration**: Digital tools and platforms to enable collaboration
- **Interdisciplinary Approaches**: Strategies to bridge research domains and foster cross-pollination
- **Evaluation and Improvement**: Metrics, feedback mechanisms, and iterative refinement processes

Each category should contain actionable recommendations tailored to the institution's context.
```
