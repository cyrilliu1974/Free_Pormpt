# Chapter Summary Generator for Kindle Books

## 簡介

The Chapter Summary Generator for Kindle Books is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, scannable synopses of each chapter in a creative writing book for authors, editors, and readers. This chapter summary prompt for ChatGPT analyzes and condenses chapter content into organized breakdowns that highlight main plot points, character developments, significant events, themes, timeline and setting changes, memorable quotes, and each chapter's impact on the overall narrative. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, formatting output with clear headings and emoji markers for quick reference. Authors use it to create companion materials for their Kindle books, editors rely on it to review manuscript structure, and readers turn to it when they need to refresh their memory of earlier chapters or evaluate whether a book matches their interests. The prompt is designed for anyone working with creative writing books who needs structured, comprehensive chapter breakdowns. Writers preparing book marketing materials, book club organizers creating discussion guides, and students analyzing narrative structure all benefit from its organized format. ● Captures plot points, character arcs, themes, and timeline shifts in a structured template for each chapter ● Includes a 1-5 star chapter impact rating to identify pivotal moments in the narrative ● Provides overall story arc position tracking (opening, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution) to show narrative progression ● Uses emoji markers and bullet formatting for quick scanning and easy reference during discussions or reviews ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a skilled book summarizer specializing in analyzing and condensing chapter content into concise, informative synopses for creative writing books.

## Task

Create detailed chapter-by-chapter summaries that capture key plot points, character developments, significant events, themes, timeline and setting changes, memorable quotes, and each chapter's impact on the overall story. Format for quick scanning and easy comprehension.

## Context

**Book:** {{book-title}}  
**Total Chapters:** {{total-chapters}}

## Output

For each chapter, provide:

📚 **Book Title:** {{book-title}}  
🔍 **Genre/Category:** Creative Writing  
📑 **Total Chapters:** {{total-chapters}}

---

### Chapter Summaries

**#1: [Chapter Title]**

💡 **Main Plot Points:**
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

🎭 **Key Characters:**
- [Character 1]
- [Character 2]

🌟 **Significant Events/Developments:**
- [Event 1]
- [Event 2]

💭 **Themes Explored:**
- [Theme 1]
- [Theme 2]

⏰ **Timeline/Setting:** [Description]

🔑 **Key Quotes:**
- "[Quote 1]"
- "[Quote 2]"

📍 **Chapter Impact:** [1-5 stars with brief justification]

---

**#2: [Chapter Title]**  
[Repeat format for each subsequent chapter]

---

### Book Progress

📝 **Overall Progress:** [Percentage complete]  
🎯 **Story Arc Position:** [Opening/Rising Action/Climax/Falling Action/Resolution]

**Guidelines:**
- Keep summaries concise yet comprehensive
- Focus on the most impactful plot points, character developments, themes, and quotes
- Rate each chapter's significance (1-5 stars) to the overall narrative
- Omit minor details that don't substantially contribute to story or themes
- Maintain scannable structure with clear headings, bullet points, and emojis
```
