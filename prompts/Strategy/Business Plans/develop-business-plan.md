# Business Plan Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Business Plan Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates detailed business plans for self-employed professionals and solopreneurs seeking structured growth strategies. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a three-column markdown table that organizes your executive summary, market analysis, and financial projections into a single actionable roadmap. It analyzes market dynamics, identifies competitive positioning opportunities, outlines revenue models and financial milestones, and delivers operational efficiency improvements tailored to your specific business context. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Use it when launching a new venture, pivoting an existing business, or preparing documentation for investors or strategic planning sessions. ● Organizes business strategy into three core pillars: executive summary with value proposition, market analysis with competitive landscape, and financial projections with profitability timelines ● Analyzes market trends, positioning opportunities, and threats specific to your business overview ● Delivers actionable revenue models, cost structures, and key financial metrics aligned with growth pathways ● Tailors recommendations to the unique challenges of self-employed professionals building sustainable, scalable businesses ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in business planning for self-employed professionals and solopreneurs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive business plan that provides a clear roadmap for increasing productivity and achieving sustainable growth. Analyze market dynamics, outline financial projections, and deliver actionable strategies that enhance efficiency, profitability, and long-term viability.

## Context
{{business-overview}}

Structure your analysis around:
- Market trends and positioning opportunities
- Competitive landscape and differentiation strategies
- Revenue models and financial milestones
- Operational efficiency improvements
- Growth and scaling pathways

## Output
Present the business plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Executive Summary | Market Analysis | Financial Projections |
|-------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| [Concise overview of the business, unique value proposition, and strategic objectives] | [Target market characteristics, competitive positioning, market opportunities and threats] | [Revenue forecasts, cost structure, profitability timeline, key financial goals and metrics] |

Ensure each column contains detailed, actionable insights tailored to the specific business context provided.
```
