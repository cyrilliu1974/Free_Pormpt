# Offboarding Warning Signs Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Offboarding Warning Signs Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that diagnoses critical weaknesses in employee departure processes for HR professionals and employer branding teams. This offboarding analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok examines seven specific warning signs that indicate when your offboarding process is actively harming your employer brand. It assesses exit interviews, knowledge transfer protocols, final communications, alumni networks, and post-departure reputation impact, then delivers structured diagnostics with specific indicators, real-world examples, and consequences for each warning sign. HR leaders use it to evaluate current offboarding practices, identify blind spots where departing employees become detractors rather than ambassadors, and understand how operational failures compound into long-term brand damage. Reach for this prompt when you need to audit your offboarding strategy, explain the hidden costs of poor departure experiences to leadership, or build a business case for offboarding investment. ● Diagnoses seven specific warning signs across exit interviews, knowledge transfer, communication quality, and alumni engagement ● Examines immediate operational impact, long-term brand damage, competitive disadvantage, and compounding effects for each sign ● Provides specific indicators and concrete examples HR leaders can assess today ● Structures output as actionable diagnostics rather than generic best-practice advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR strategist and employer branding consultant specializing in offboarding processes and their impact on organizational reputation.

## Task
Identify seven critical warning signs that indicate an offboarding process is fundamentally weak and actively damaging the organization's employer brand. Provide a comprehensive diagnostic that HR leaders can use to assess their current processes.

## Context
Most companies focus obsessively on onboarding while treating offboarding as an administrative afterthought. This creates blind spots where departing employees become brand ambassadors or brand assassins based on their final experience. Warning signs manifest across multiple touchpoints: exit interviews, knowledge transfer, final communications, alumni networks, and post-departure reputation impact.

Examine each warning sign through:
- Immediate operational impact
- Long-term brand damage
- Competitive disadvantage
- How these signs compound over time and create cascading effects

Consider the following organizational context:
{{company-context}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a numbered list (1–7) with each warning sign structured as:

**[Warning Sign Title]**
- Clear explanation of the warning sign
- Specific indicators HR leaders can immediately assess
- Concrete examples of how this manifests
- Potential consequences for employer brand damage

Ensure each warning sign is actionable and diagnostic, not generic advice.
```
