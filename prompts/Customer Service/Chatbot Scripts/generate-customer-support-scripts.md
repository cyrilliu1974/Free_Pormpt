# Customer Support Script Generator for Digital Business

## 簡介

The Customer Support Script Generator for Digital Business is a free AI prompt that creates structured, word-for-word scripts support agents can follow to handle customer inquiries, complaints, and service requests. This customer support script prompt for ChatGPT (also compatible with Claude, Gemini, and Grok) takes your digital business description and outputs a complete support scenario with a detailed customer persona, numbered steps pairing explicit actions with exact speech, and a resolution summary. Each script is tailored to real support situations, covering everything from simple questions to multi-step complaint resolution. Use it to onboard new agents, standardize responses across your team, or quickly draft scripts for new product launches and service changes. ● Outputs scenario context, customer background, mood, and potential issues before the script begins ● Provides exact words to say at each step, not vague best practices or tone guidelines ● Scales from simple three-step exchanges to complex multi-step resolutions ● Closes every script with a clear resolution statement agents can aim for ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write customer support scripts for digital businesses. Your output gives support agents clear, step-by-step guidance covering both simple queries and complex complaints, with specific words to use and actions to take.

## Context

Digital business: {{digital-business-description}}

## Task

Generate a complete customer support script. The script must:

1. Open with a scenario section describing the specific support situation
2. Profile the customer with background, mood, and likely issues
3. Walk the agent through each step with an explicit action and the exact words to say
4. Add extra steps for complex scenarios that need them
5. Close with a resolution summarizing how the situation should end

Keep language clear and concise. Show genuine empathy toward the customer's mood. Provide specific words, not general principles.

## Output

Use this structure:

**Scenario:**
[Describe the specific support situation]

**Customer Persona:**
- Background: [relevant customer context]
- Mood: [emotional state]
- Potential issues: [likely concerns]

**Script:**

Step 1:
- Action: [what the agent does]
- Speech: "[exact words to say]"

Step 2:
- Action: [what the agent does]
- Speech: "[exact words to say]"

Step 3:
- Action: [what the agent does]
- Speech: "[exact words to say]"

Additional Steps If Needed:

Step 4:
- Action: [what the agent does]
- Speech: "[exact words to say]"

[Continue as needed]

**Resolution:**
[Summary of how the situation should end]
```
