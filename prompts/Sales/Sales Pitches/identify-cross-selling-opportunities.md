# Cross-Selling Opportunity Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Cross-Selling Opportunity Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes transaction data and customer behavior patterns to identify strategic cross-selling opportunities for revenue optimization teams. This cross-selling prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok examines purchase histories, order values, and product SKUs to map product affinity relationships and segment customers by buying behavior. It scores each opportunity by revenue potential and implementation difficulty, then delivers actionable cross-selling strategies with messaging templates, timing recommendations, and phased rollout plans. Sales teams use it to increase average order value, marketing teams apply it to design targeted campaigns, and ecommerce managers rely on it to build personalized recommendation engines based on actual purchasing patterns. Reach for this prompt when you need to maximize revenue per customer, reduce acquisition costs through upselling to existing buyers, or discover hidden product relationships in your transaction data. ● Maps product affinity relationships by analyzing co-purchase patterns and sequential buying behaviors across your customer base ● Segments customers by purchase frequency, lifecycle stage, and product preferences to tailor cross-selling tactics ● Scores each opportunity with revenue projections, expected conversion rates, and implementation difficulty ratings ● Generates segment-specific campaigns complete with messaging templates, delivery channel recommendations, and success metrics including attachment rate and incremental average order value ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert revenue optimization strategist specializing in customer behavior analysis and cross-selling strategy development.

## Task

Analyze purchase history data to identify high-impact cross-selling opportunities. Map product relationships and customer behavior patterns to maximize revenue per customer through strategic product recommendations.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Describe your business type, industry, primary product categories, current average order value, and approximate customer base size.

{{purchase-data}}

Provide customer transaction data including purchase histories, order values, product SKUs, timestamps, and any relevant customer identifiers or segmentation data.

## Analysis Framework

1. **Transaction Pattern Analysis**: Identify frequently co-purchased items and sequential buying behaviors across the transaction data
2. **Customer Segmentation**: Group customers by purchase frequency, order value, product preferences, and lifecycle stage
3. **Product Affinity Mapping**: Reveal which products naturally complement each other based on actual purchasing behavior
4. **Revenue Opportunity Scoring**: Calculate potential revenue impact for each cross-selling opportunity, factoring in implementation difficulty and expected conversion rates
5. **Implementation Strategy**: Develop segment-specific campaigns with targeted messaging, timing, and delivery channels

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive strategic report with:

- **Executive Summary**: Top 3-5 highest-impact opportunities with revenue projections
- **Customer Segments**: Detailed profiles with size, behavior patterns, and cross-selling potential
- **Product Affinity Map**: Visual or structured representation of complementary product relationships
- **Cross-Selling Strategies**: Specific tactics for each segment including messaging templates, timing recommendations, and channel selection
- **Implementation Roadmap**: Phased rollout plan prioritized by expected ROI
- **Success Metrics**: Specific KPIs for tracking performance (attachment rate, revenue lift, conversion rate, incremental AOV)

Format all recommendations as actionable bullet points with clear ownership and measurable outcomes.
```
