# Contrarian Ideas Generator for Content Writing

## 簡介

The Contrarian Ideas Generator for Content Writing is a free AI prompt that systematically identifies widely held beliefs in any subject area, questions their validity, and produces 20 alternative perspectives for writers, strategists, and thought leaders. This contrarian ideas prompt for ChatGPT works by guiding the AI through a four-step analysis: first cataloging dominant beliefs and trends, then examining the reasoning behind each assumption, next surfacing alternative viewpoints with logical support, and finally generating 20 content ideas that challenge the status quo. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only a topic and optional background context. Writers use it to develop opinion pieces, strategists deploy it to identify market gaps, and educators apply it to spark classroom debate. Reach for this prompt when you need to stand out in a saturated content landscape or when conventional advice feels stale and derivative. ● Maps the landscape of accepted wisdom in your field before proposing alternatives ● Requires each contrarian idea to rest on logical reasoning rather than empty provocation ● Outputs a structured breakdown showing beliefs, questioning steps, alternative viewpoints, and numbered ideas ● Filters for substantive insight over gratuitous controversy, ensuring ideas invite genuine debate ## Prompt

```
## Role

You identify contrarian ideas by questioning widely held beliefs, examining alternative viewpoints, and surfacing perspectives that challenge conventional thinking.

## Context

**Topic:** {{topic}}

**Background or context:** {{topic-context}}

## Task

Work through the following steps in order:

1. Identify the widely held beliefs or trends in this area.
2. Question why each belief is held and whether it is justified. Show your reasoning.
3. Identify alternative viewpoints or scenarios that challenge those beliefs. Show your reasoning.
4. Generate 20 contrarian content ideas the user could write about in this area.

**Criteria for contrarian ideas:**
- Challenge commonly held beliefs or trends in the area
- Encourage the reader to question assumptions
- Be supported by logical reasoning and evidence where possible
- Avoid gratuitous controversy or offense without substantive purpose
- Offer fresh perspectives and genuine insight

## Output

Present the response in this exact format:

**Widely Held Beliefs or Trends:**
1. [Belief/Trend]
2. [Belief/Trend]
...

**Questioning Beliefs:**
1. [Question and reasoning]
2. [Question and reasoning]
...

**Alternative Viewpoints:**
1. [Alternative viewpoint and reasoning]
2. [Alternative viewpoint and reasoning]
...

**20 Contrarian Content Ideas:**
1. [Idea]
2. [Idea]
...
20. [Idea]
```
