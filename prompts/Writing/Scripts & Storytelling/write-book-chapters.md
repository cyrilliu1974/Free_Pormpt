# Book Chapter Breakdown Generator

## 簡介

The Book Chapter Breakdown Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured chapter outlines with opening hooks, pacing elements, and plot thread tracking for fiction authors and creative writers. This book chapter prompt for ChatGPT takes your story overview and produces a complete chapter framework that includes title, opening hook, scene progression, character development beats, setting descriptions, dialogue segments, and a compelling chapter-end hook. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, giving you a structured breakdown with tension ratings, pacing indicators, and clear markers for resolved and open plot threads. Authors use it to maintain narrative momentum, plan scene flow, and ensure each chapter advances both plot and character arcs while keeping readers engaged. Reach for this prompt when you need to outline a new chapter, troubleshoot pacing issues, or ensure your narrative structure includes the hooks and emotional beats that drive readers forward. ● Creates opening and ending hooks designed to pull readers through your story ● Provides pacing indicators for action scenes, dialogue, and introspective moments ● Tracks plot threads with clear markers for resolved, open, and setup elements ● Includes chapter ratings for tension level, character growth, plot advancement, and reader engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert author and creative writing coach specializing in narrative structure, character development, vivid scene-building, and pacing.

## Task
Generate a detailed chapter breakdown that keeps readers engaged through strong hooks, clear structure, and forward momentum.

## Context
{{story-overview}}

Include: plot summary, key characters and their arcs, desired tone (e.g., dark fantasy, lighthearted romance, thriller), and any established world-building or themes.

## Output
Provide a complete chapter breakdown with:

### 📚 Chapter Structure
1. **Title**
2. **Opening Hook** – grabs attention in the first paragraph
3. **Main Content** – scene progression, conflicts, revelations
4. **Chapter End Hook** – compels the reader to continue

### 📖 Content Elements
- Key plot points advanced
- Character development moments
- Setting descriptions (sensory, atmospheric)
- Dialogue segments (conflict, subtext, voice)
- Emotional undertones

### ⚡ Pacing Guide
- 🏃‍♂️ Fast-paced scenes (action, urgency)
- 👣 Moderate pace (dialogue, exploration)
- 🌟 Slow, detailed moments (introspection, world-building)

### 💫 Chapter Rating (1–5)
- **Tension Level**
- **Character Growth**
- **Plot Advancement**
- **Reader Engagement**

### Chapter Threads
✅ Plot threads resolved  
❌ Plot threads left open  
🔄 Setup for next chapter
```
