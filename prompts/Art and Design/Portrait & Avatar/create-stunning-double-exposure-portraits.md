# Double Exposure Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Double Exposure Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates layered portrait images combining a subject with glitched neon environments and motion-blurred urban scenes for photographers, designers, and digital artists. This double exposure portrait prompt for Midjourney produces editorial-quality compositions that merge futuristic cyberpunk aesthetics with vintage film characteristics. You describe your subject and set the mood, and the prompt layers them against a glitched neon background featuring UI fragments and chromatic aberration, then overlays a blurred foreground city street with passing cars, distant pedestrians, and ambient neon lighting. The result is a three-quarter angle portrait shot with 50mm lens perspective, shallow depth of field, film grain, light leaks, mild halation, and gentle vignette. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering a 16:9 editorial composition that balances dynamic urban energy with vintage analog warmth. Reach for this prompt when you need striking portrait artwork that fuses modern glitch aesthetics with retro film textures, or when you want to create mood-driven character visuals that feel cinematic and layered. ● Customizable subject and mood variables let you tailor the portrait to any character concept or emotional tone. ● Automatic layering of glitched neon backgrounds, cyberpunk UI fragments, chromatic aberration, and vintage film grain for a distinctive visual style. ● Motion-blurred city street foreground with passing cars and ambient neon lighting adds dynamic depth and urban energy. ● Editorial three-quarter composition with 50mm lens perspective, shallow depth of field, and 35mm film look including halation and vignette. ## Prompt
