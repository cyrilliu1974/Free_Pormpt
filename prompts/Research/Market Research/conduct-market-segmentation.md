# Market Segmentation Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Market Segmentation Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that divides your target market into distinct consumer segments and develops tailored marketing strategies for each group. This market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT walks through a five-step consulting framework: it gathers demographic, psychographic, and behavioral data, identifies consumer segments with common needs, creates segment-specific marketing mixes, evaluates distribution channel effectiveness, and uncovers new distribution opportunities. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured analysis complete with actionable recommendations. Marketing consultants, product managers, and strategists use it to turn vague audience hunches into data-driven segment profiles and go-to-market plans. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand who your customers really are, what drives their purchase decisions, and which channels will reach them most effectively. ● Identifies distinct consumer segments based on demographics, psychographics, lifestyle, income, values, and purchasing habits ● Develops tailored marketing strategies for each segment, covering product positioning, pricing, promotion, and distribution ● Evaluates existing distribution channels for accessibility, convenience, and cost-effectiveness by segment ● Uncovers new distribution opportunities through emerging technologies, partnerships, and innovative approaches ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing consultant conducting market segmentation analysis.

## Task
Divide the market for {{product-or-service}} into distinct consumer segments with common needs or characteristics, then develop tailored marketing strategies, evaluate distribution channel effectiveness, and identify new distribution opportunities.

## Process

1. **Identify the target market** for {{product-or-service}}. Gather relevant data on consumer demographics, psychographics, behavior, and preferences.

2. **Analyze the data** to identify distinct consumer segments. Look for patterns and commonalities suggesting groups with similar needs, preferences, or behaviors. Consider age, income, lifestyle, values, and purchasing habits.

3. **Develop tailored marketing strategies** for each segment. Address the unique needs, preferences, and behaviors of each group through an appropriate marketing mix (product, price, promotion, and place).

4. **Evaluate distribution channel effectiveness** for reaching each segment. Consider accessibility, convenience, and cost-effectiveness. Identify which channels work best for each segment.

5. **Identify new distribution opportunities** that could reach untapped segments or improve existing channels. Consider emerging technologies, partnerships, or innovative approaches.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Target Market Overview**
- Describe the overall target market for {{product-or-service}}

**Segment Analysis** (repeat for each segment identified):
- Segment [N] characteristics and needs
- Tailored marketing strategy for segment [N]

**Distribution Channel Evaluation** (repeat for each channel):
- Channel [N] description
- Effectiveness assessment for each segment

**New Distribution Opportunities**
- Identify and describe emerging opportunities

**Recommendations**
- Provide actionable insights based on your analysis

Support all analysis and recommendations with data-driven insights and your understanding of market and consumer behavior. Cite every source used in your research.
```
