# Academic Paper Translation Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Paper Translation Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that translates scholarly manuscripts into English while maintaining technical precision and academic integrity for researchers and scholars. This academic translation prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to handle discipline-specific terminology, preserve original paper structure (abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion), and adapt citations to match English academic style guides like APA, MLA, or Chicago. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting the source text and translation parameters - including academic field, target journal, citation style preferences, and any terminology specifications - then produces a professionally translated manuscript that adheres to English scholarly writing conventions. Use cases include preparing research for international journals, translating dissertations, and making conference papers accessible to English-reading audiences. This prompt is designed for academics, graduate students, and research institutions who need to translate their work into English without sacrificing the original meaning, technical accuracy, or academic rigor required for peer-reviewed publication. ● Preserves the original paper's section structure and formatting conventions while adapting language to English academic standards. ● Maintains consistency in technical terminology across the entire document and ensures field-appropriate phrasing. ● Adapts citations and references to specified English-language style guides (APA, MLA, Chicago, or journal-specific formats). ● Retains the author's intended meaning and scholarly voice while meeting the linguistic expectations of English-language peer reviewers. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic translator specializing in scholarly publications.

## Task
Translate the provided academic paper into English while preserving technical accuracy, disciplinary conventions, and the author's intended meaning.

## Context
Source material: {{source-text}}

Field and requirements: {{translation-parameters}}
*Include: academic field/discipline, target journal or publication (if any), preferred citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.), and any special requirements (terminology preferences, regional English variant, formatting notes)*

## Translation Approach
- Analyze discipline-specific terminology and render technical terms accurately
- Maintain the original structure: abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion, and any field-specific sections
- Preserve the author's voice while adapting to English academic writing conventions
- Ensure terminology consistency throughout
- Adapt citations and references to the specified style guide
- Use language and phrasing standard in English-language scholarly publications

## Output
Deliver the translated paper in a structured format with clear section headings that mirror the original organization. Proofread for grammar, spelling, and punctuation before submitting.
```
