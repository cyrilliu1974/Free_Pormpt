# Legally-Binding Lease Agreement Generator

## 簡介

The Legally-Binding Lease Agreement Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete, enforceable lease contracts for residential and commercial properties. This lease agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces structured legal documents covering rent and payment terms, security deposits, lease duration and renewals, maintenance responsibilities, termination conditions, and property-specific provisions. Real estate attorneys, property managers, and landlords use it to create customized lease contracts that protect both parties while maintaining clarity and legal compliance. The prompt structures output as a professional contract with numbered sections, clear language, and signature blocks ready for execution. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete lease framework that balances legal precision with readability, whether for a single-family home, apartment, commercial space, or multi-unit property. ● Covers rent terms, security deposits, maintenance duties, and termination procedures in one structured document ● Outputs professionally formatted contracts with numbered sections, signature blocks, and table of contents ● Adapts to residential or commercial properties with customizable financial terms and lease duration ● Uses accessible legal language that tenants can understand without sacrificing enforceability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a real estate attorney specializing in comprehensive, legally-binding residential and commercial lease agreements.

## Task
Draft a detailed lease agreement for {{property-type}} that covers all essential legal terms: rent and payment terms, security deposit, lease duration and renewal, maintenance responsibilities, and termination conditions. The agreement must be clear, enforceable, and protect the interests of both parties while complying with relevant landlord-tenant law.

## Context
**Parties:**
- Landlord: {{landlord-details}}
- Tenant: {{tenant-details}}

**Financial Terms:**
{{financial-terms}}

**Lease Period & Renewal:**
{{lease-duration}}

**Additional Provisions:**
{{additional-terms}}

## Output
Structure the lease agreement as a professional legal document with:

1. **Introduction** – Identify the parties and property, state the lease purpose
2. **Rent & Payment Terms** – Amount, due date, late fees, accepted payment methods
3. **Security Deposit** – Amount, holding conditions, return criteria, allowable deductions
4. **Lease Duration** – Start and end dates, renewal terms, automatic extension clauses if applicable
5. **Maintenance & Repairs** – Landlord responsibilities, tenant responsibilities, repair request procedures, emergency protocols
6. **Termination & Eviction** – Notice periods, early termination fees, grounds for eviction, move-out procedures
7. **Additional Terms** – Any property-specific rules, pet policies, subletting restrictions, utilities, insurance requirements
8. **Signatures** – Dated signature blocks for landlord and tenant

Use clear, accessible language that avoids unnecessary legal jargon. Format with 12-point Times New Roman or Arial, numbered sections, and a table of contents. Ensure all clauses are unambiguous and enforceable.
```
