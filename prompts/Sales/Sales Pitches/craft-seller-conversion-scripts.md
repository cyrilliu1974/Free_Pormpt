# Seller Conversion Script Generator for Real Estate

## 簡介

The Seller Conversion Script Generator for Real Estate is a free AI prompt that creates structured dialogue frameworks to guide listing appointments from discovery through signed agreements for real estate agents. This seller conversion script prompt for ChatGPT builds three-phase conversation flows: a Discovery Phase with diagnostic questions that reveal true seller motivation and timeline, a Value Demonstration Phase that positions your market evaluation as a profit-maximization strategy, and a Commitment Phase with direct closing language that secures the listing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, tailoring scripts to your seller demographic, your unique value proposition, and current market objections. Real estate professionals use it to replace generic pitches with evidence-based dialogue that addresses commission skepticism, competing advice, and emotional decision patterns sellers face when choosing an agent. Reach for this prompt when preparing for listing appointments, onboarding new agents to proven conversation patterns, or adapting your pitch to shifting market conditions. ● Diagnostic question sequences in the Discovery Phase that uncover true seller motivation, timeline pressure, and decision-making factors while building rapport. ● Market evaluation positioning language that frames your analysis as a profit-maximization tool rather than a standard valuation, establishing expert authority. ● Objection response templates embedded in each phase, anticipating commission concerns, competing agent offers, and FSBO considerations with pre-built rebuttals. ● Transition statements and psychological coaching notes that explain urgency triggers, differentiation tactics, and how to make commitment requests feel like natural next steps rather than pressure. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert real estate conversion specialist with 15 years closing high-value property deals. You understand seller psychology, decision-making patterns, and how to address financial anxieties and timeline pressures through structured dialogue.

## Task
Create a persuasive seller conversion script that uncovers motivation, establishes urgency, demonstrates value, and secures commitment. The script must cut through skepticism by leading with diagnostic questions, positioning a market evaluation as a profit-maximization strategy, and closing with a direct ask that feels logically inevitable.

## Context
Sellers face generic pitches from multiple agents, skepticism about commission value, competing advice from friends and online sources, and make emotional decisions while believing they're rational. Your script must differentiate through evidence-based presentation and strategic question sequences.

Structure the script in three phases:

**Discovery Phase**: Motivation and timeline qualification questions that reveal true needs
**Value Demonstration Phase**: Market evaluation offer and benefit articulation that proves unique value
**Commitment Phase**: Direct closing language that frames next steps as mutual agreement

For each phase include:
- Primary questions to ask
- Anticipated seller objections with response templates
- Psychological triggers (urgency, maximum money potential, differentiation)
- Transition statements to the next phase

Use language patterns that emphasize profit potential, create timeline urgency, establish you as the obvious choice, and make commitment feel natural rather than pressured.

## Input
{{seller-profile}} — Your ideal seller demographic, their characteristics and situation

{{unique-value}} — Your key differentiators, strengths, proven results, track record data, and success metrics as an agent

{{market-context}} — Current local market dynamics and typical seller objections you encounter

## Output
Deliver the script with:
- Clear phase headings (Discovery Phase, Value Demonstration Phase, Commitment Phase)
- Dialogue format with [AGENT] and [ANTICIPATED SELLER RESPONSE] labels
- [Bracketed coaching notes] explaining psychological strategy behind key questions
- Bullet points for objection handling frameworks within each phase
```
