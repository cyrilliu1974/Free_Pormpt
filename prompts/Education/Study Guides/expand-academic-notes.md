# Academic Note Expansion Prompt for Study Materials

## 簡介

The Academic Note Expansion Prompt for Study Materials is a free AI prompt that converts concise class or research notes into comprehensive, well-structured study guides for students and educators. This academic note expansion prompt for ChatGPT and Claude analyzes your original notes to identify core concepts and knowledge gaps, then elaborates each point with clear explanations, concrete examples, supporting evidence, and scholarly context. It organizes content in a logical hierarchy, highlights key terms and definitions, and adds 3-5 critical thinking questions to deepen engagement with the material. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accommodating any subject, academic level (high school through graduate), and citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard). Reach for this prompt when you need to turn sparse lecture notes, research outlines, or reading summaries into polished study resources that support active learning and exam preparation. ● Identifies core concepts and knowledge gaps, then elaborates with explanations, examples, and scholarly evidence ● Organizes expanded material in a clear hierarchy with headings, bold key terms, and logical flow from foundational to advanced ideas ● Adds critical thinking questions and discussion prompts that encourage deeper engagement ● Formats output with bullet points, numbered lists, and blockquotes for maximum readability and retention ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic researcher specializing in transforming concise notes into comprehensive study materials.

## Task
Expand the provided academic notes into a thorough, well-structured study resource that deepens understanding and supports learning at the specified academic level.

## Process
1. Identify core concepts, themes, and knowledge gaps in the original notes
2. Elaborate each point with:
   - Clear explanations using appropriate academic terminology
   - Concrete examples and applications
   - Supporting evidence and scholarly context
3. Organize content in a logical hierarchy with clear relationships between ideas
4. Highlight key terms, definitions, and must-know concepts
5. Add 3-5 critical thinking questions or discussion prompts that encourage deeper engagement with the material
6. Include citations in the specified style where claims require academic support

## Context
**Subject & Topic:** {{subject-and-topic}}
**Academic Level:** {{academic-level}}
**Citation Style:** {{citation-style}}

## Input
{{original-notes}}

## Output
Deliver the expanded study material using:
- Clear hierarchical structure (headings, subheadings)
- Bullet points and numbered lists for readability
- **Bold** for key terms and concepts
- Blockquotes or callouts for important definitions
- Logical flow from foundational to advanced concepts
```
