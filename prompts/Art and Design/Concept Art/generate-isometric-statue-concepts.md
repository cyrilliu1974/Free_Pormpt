# Isometric Statue Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Isometric Statue Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clean, isometric views of iconic monuments with simplified architectural styling for designers and concept artists. This isometric statue prompt for Midjourney produces bright daylight compositions featuring statues rendered on square island bases, soft shadow work, and pastel sky backgrounds. The prompt uses chaos 33, stylize 400, and exposure settings to balance creative variation with compositional clarity, making it ideal for concept art mockups, editorial illustrations, game asset previews, and architectural visualization studies. It runs exclusively on Midjourney and outputs in a 3:4 aspect ratio optimized for portrait-format presentations and mobile displays. Reach for this prompt when you need stylized monument concepts that balance recognizability with artistic simplification, or when building a series of isometric landmark illustrations with consistent lighting and framing. ● Produces isometric monument views with simplified architectural detail and square island bases for cohesive framing ● Uses chaos and stylization parameters to balance creative exploration with compositional control ● Generates pastel sky backgrounds and soft shadow rendering for a polished, editorial-ready aesthetic ● Outputs in 3:4 portrait aspect ratio suitable for mobile screens, print layouts, and vertical social formats ## Prompt

```
Isometric Statue of Liberty on a square island base, simplified architectural detail, bright daylight, soft shadows, clean composition, pastel sky background --chaos 33 --ar 3:4 --exp 15 --stylize 400
```
