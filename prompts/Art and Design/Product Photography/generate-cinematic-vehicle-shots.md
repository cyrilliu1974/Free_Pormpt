# Cinematic Product Launch Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Product Launch Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-resolution, photorealistic images of products being launched or placed by hands against textured backgrounds. This cinematic product photography prompt for Midjourney produces 8K-resolution images with dramatic lighting, glossy finishes, and attention to surface details like metallic accents and chrome highlights. You specify the hand action (launching, placing, releasing) and the product, and the prompt generates a close-up composition on a textured brick road with professional-grade lighting. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other advanced image generation models. Photographers, e-commerce brands, and marketing teams use it to create hero shots for product launches, social media campaigns, and advertising materials without the cost of a studio shoot. ● Produces 8K photorealistic close-ups with cinematic lighting that emphasizes product details, glossy finishes, and metallic surfaces. ● Two variables let you control the hand action (launching, placing, holding) and the specific product being featured. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images ideal for video thumbnails, website headers, and social media ads. ● Captures realistic textures in both the product and the brick road surface, adding depth and tactile appeal to your marketing materials. ## Prompt

```
Close-up shot of {{hand-action}} a {{product}} onto a textured brick road, cinematic product lighting, glossy finish with metallic red and chrome details, photorealistic, 8K resolution --ar 16:9
```
