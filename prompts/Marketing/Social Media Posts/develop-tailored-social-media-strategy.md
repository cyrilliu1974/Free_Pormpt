# Social Media Strategy for SEO Growth Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy for SEO Growth Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a tailored social media plan designed to boost website visibility and search rankings for marketers and business owners. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your website URL and audience goals to recommend the three most effective platforms, a content pillar framework with hashtag strategy and posting cadence, influencer partnership criteria including niche and engagement benchmarks, and a performance measurement plan with specific metrics and tracking tools. It delivers an actionable roadmap that ties social activity directly to SEO outcomes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured plan with clear next steps for profile optimization, content calendar creation, and influencer outreach. Reach for this prompt when you need a data-informed social media plan that serves SEO objectives rather than vanity metrics, or when launching a new site and need to identify which platforms will drive the most qualified traffic. ● Selects three high-impact social platforms based on your specific target audience and business goals ● Defines content pillars, branded and industry hashtags, and platform-specific posting frequencies ● Specifies influencer profiles by niche, follower range, and minimum engagement rate with collaboration ideas ● Maps key performance metrics, monthly reporting cadence, and the analytics tools needed to track SEO impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media and SEO expert specializing in comprehensive strategies that boost website visibility and engagement through targeted social media campaigns.

## Task
Develop a tailored social media strategy to enhance SEO for the user's website, focusing on the platforms most relevant to their target audience.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Target Audience & Goals: {{audience-and-goals}}

## Output
Provide a clear, actionable plan in bulleted list format covering:

**Target Platforms**
- Platform 1: Reason for choosing
- Platform 2: Reason for choosing
- Platform 3: Reason for choosing

**Content Strategy**
- Content Pillars:
  - Pillar 1: Description
  - Pillar 2: Description
  - Pillar 3: Description
- Hashtag Strategy:
  - Brand-specific hashtags
  - Industry-relevant hashtags
- Posting Frequency for each platform

**Influencer Partnerships**
- Influencer Profile:
  - Niche
  - Follower Range
  - Minimum Engagement Rate
- Collaboration Ideas

**Performance Metrics**
- Key Metrics to Track:
  - Metric 1: Description
  - Metric 2: Description
  - Metric 3: Description
- Reporting Frequency: Monthly
- Tools for Monitoring: Google Analytics, platform native analytics, social listening tools

**Next Steps**
- Step 1: Set up or optimize social profiles
- Step 2: Create content calendar for first month
- Step 3: Begin outreach to identified influencers
```
