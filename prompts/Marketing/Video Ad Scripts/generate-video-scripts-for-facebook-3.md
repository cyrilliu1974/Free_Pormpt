# Facebook Video Ad Script Generator

## 簡介

The Facebook Video Ad Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates platform-optimized video advertising scripts for social media marketers and content creators. This video ad script prompt for ChatGPT produces structured three-column scripts (timing, visual, audio/copy) that follow proven conversion frameworks: a 0-3 second hook to stop the scroll, a problem-promise sequence, solution showcase with product benefits, social proof elements, and a clear call-to-action. It adapts script length, pacing, and tone to match Facebook's native viewing habits while incorporating your brand voice, target audience demographics, and unique selling points. Works seamlessly across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for rapid script iteration and A/B testing variations. Ideal for direct-response marketers, brand managers, and video producers who need scroll-stopping ad scripts that convert viewers into customers on Facebook and Instagram placements. ● Delivers scripts in three-column format with precise timing breakdowns for editing and production ● Incorporates platform-specific best practices for Facebook video length, hooks, and native content patterns ● Integrates custom variables for product details, target audience, brand voice, and unique value propositions ● Includes visual cues, text overlay suggestions, and transition notes for complete production guidance ## Prompt

```
## Role
Expert video script writer specializing in social media content that drives engagement and conversions.

## Task
Create a compelling video script optimized for {{platform}} that promotes the product/service and builds brand awareness. The script must capture attention immediately, showcase key benefits, and drive viewers to action.

## Context
**Offer**: {{product-service}}
**Audience**: {{target-audience}}
**Brand voice**: {{brand-voice}}
**Key selling points**: {{unique-value}}

Consider platform-specific best practices: optimal video length, native viewing habits, and content formats that perform well on {{platform}}.

## Output
Structured script in three columns:

**TIMING | VISUAL | AUDIO/COPY**

- **Hook (0-3s)**: Pattern interrupt or attention-grabbing opening that stops the scroll
- **Problem/Promise (4-10s)**: Establish relevance or desire
- **Solution showcase (11-20s)**: Demonstrate the product/service in action, weaving in the key selling points naturally
- **Social proof or differentiator (21-25s)**: Brief credibility beat if needed
- **Call-to-action (26-30s)**: Clear, specific next step

Include visual direction (shots, text overlays, transitions) and precise copy. Adjust total length to match {{platform}} norms. Use language and pacing that matches the brand voice and resonates with the target audience.
```
