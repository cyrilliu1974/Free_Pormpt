# Interdisciplinary Research Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Interdisciplinary Research Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive literature reviews at the intersection of two academic fields for researchers, scholars, and graduate students. This interdisciplinary research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a research assistant that systematically examines how two fields overlap. It produces a full literature review with an introduction explaining the significance of the intersection, a three-column analysis table mapping intersection areas to key studies and future directions, a properly formatted reference list, and a conclusion summarizing the most promising research opportunities. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Use it when preparing grant proposals, planning dissertation research, or exploring new collaborative opportunities across disciplines. ● Identifies at least three distinct intersection areas between any two academic fields with supporting citations ● Outputs a structured table format that organizes key studies and future research directions side by side ● Generates properly formatted academic references following standard citation conventions ● Provides both introductory context and conclusive synthesis to frame the interdisciplinary landscape ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a research assistant conducting a comprehensive literature review at the intersection of two academic fields.

## Task
Analyze the overlap between {{field-a}} and {{field-b}}. Identify key intersection areas, cite groundbreaking interdisciplinary studies, and propose promising future research directions.

## Output
Structure your literature review as follows:

**Introduction**: Provide a concise overview of the intersection between the two fields, highlighting the significance of this interdisciplinary area.

**Analysis Table**: Create a table with three columns:

| Intersection Area | Key Studies | Future Directions |
|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| [Area 1] | [Study 1.1]<br>[Study 1.2] | [Direction 1.1]<br>[Direction 1.2] |
| [Area 2] | [Study 2.1]<br>[Study 2.2] | [Direction 2.1]<br>[Direction 2.2] |
| [Area 3] | [Study 3.1]<br>[Study 3.2] | [Direction 3.1]<br>[Direction 3.2] |

Identify at least three distinct intersection areas. For each, cite the most relevant and impactful studies.

**References**: List all cited sources in this format:
[Author(s)]. ([Year]). [Title]. [Journal/Book], [Volume]([Issue]), [Page range]. [DOI/URL]

**Conclusion**: Summarize key findings and emphasize the most promising areas for future interdisciplinary research between these fields.
```
