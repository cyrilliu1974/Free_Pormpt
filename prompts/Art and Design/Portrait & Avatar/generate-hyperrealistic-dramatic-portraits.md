# Hyperrealistic Dramatic Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Dramatic Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates studio-quality portraits with diagonal spotlight effects and photoreal skin detail for photographers, content creators, and visual artists. This hyperrealistic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 8K-resolution headshots featuring a dramatic single-beam lighting setup that cuts diagonally across the subject from top-left to lower-right. The beam illuminates individual hair strands and casts irregular, hair-shaped shadows across the face, creating a mysterious, film-noir aesthetic against a pure black background. The prompt preserves natural skin texture - visible pores, fine lines, and realistic subsurface scattering - while maintaining shallow depth of field and cinematic contrast. Use it when you need editorial-grade portrait imagery without access to professional studio equipment or lighting rigs. ● Produces diagonal beam lighting that interacts naturally with hair strands, creating gobo-like shadow patterns and strand-level highlights. ● Renders photoreal human skin with pore-level detail, micro-textures, and true-to-life shading instead of artificial smoothing. ● Maintains high contrast falloff so everything outside the illuminated area falls into deep shadow, enhancing the cinematic mood. ● Outputs 8K ultra-detailed images with realistic lens bokeh, subtle film grain, and natural color grading for editorial and brand use. ## Prompt

```
Hyperrealistic portrait of {{subject}} against a completely black background. Dark, dramatic, mysterious mood. Diagonal angled beam of light from top-left shining toward lower-right — the beam hits the long dark hair first, illuminating individual hair strands and creating scattered highlights; the light passes through hair strands and casts broken, irregular shadow patterns across the face (no straight horizontal light band). Hair: long, dark, slightly tousled with loose strands; some strands glow brightly where hit by the beam, other strands fade into deep shadow. Pose: {{pose}}. Expression: eyes looking directly at the camera with {{expression}}. Skin: natural human skin texture with visible pores, micro-details, fine lines, soft imperfections, realistic subsurface scattering and true-to-life shading (no stylized smoothing). High falloff outside the beam — everything outside the illuminated diagonal area falls into deep darkness; sweater and torso blend into shadows. Composition: tight head-and-shoulders portrait, diagonal spotlight across hair and face, strong contrast, ultra-detailed. Camera: shallow depth of field, realistic lens bokeh on background (background completely black), subtle film grain for texture. Lighting specifics: angled top-left diagonal beam shaped by hair; beam edges irregular, hair-caused gobo-like shadows on cheek and lips; highlights on hair are strand-level; avoid even or flat fill light — preserve dramatic one-beam lighting. Style & rendering: hyperrealistic, photoreal detail, 8K ultra-detailed (very high resolution), realistic materials, natural color grading, cinematic contrast.

Negative prompt: anime smoothness, plastic skin, overly stylized or cartoon rendering, flat lighting, straight horizontal light band, visible background elements, busy background, extra limbs, extra fingers, low detail, soft-focus over-blur, overexposed highlights, harsh clipping, unnatural eye shapes, exaggerated anatomy, watermark, text, signature, obvious 3D render artifacts, overly-saturated colors
```
