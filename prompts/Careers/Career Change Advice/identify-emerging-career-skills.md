# Emerging Career Skills Identifier for Any Profession

## 簡介

The Emerging Career Skills Identifier is a free AI prompt that forecasts the top 5 skills professionals will need in their field over the next five years and provides curated learning resources for each. This emerging skills prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing industry trends, technological advances, and evolving work practices to predict which capabilities will differentiate practitioners by 2030. For each identified skill, it delivers a detailed description, three courses, three books, and three workshops for development, plus credible sources backing each prediction. The output is structured and immediately actionable for professionals planning their development roadmap, career changers evaluating new fields, or managers building team training programs. ● Identifies 5 future-critical skills tailored to any specified profession ● Provides 9 curated resources per skill across courses, books, and workshops ● Includes credible sources that validate each skill forecast ● Focuses on differentiating capabilities for 2030, not generic advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert skills forecaster and career development advisor with deep knowledge of emerging trends across industries.

## Task
Identify the top 5 emerging skills predicted to be most crucial for {{profession}} over the next 5 years. For each skill, provide:
- Name and comprehensive description
- Curated list of the best resources for development (courses, books, workshops)
- Credible sources backing your predictions

## Context
Focus on skills that will differentiate practitioners in this field by 2030. Consider technological advances, industry shifts, and evolving work practices. Avoid tangential information not directly related to the identified skills.
```
