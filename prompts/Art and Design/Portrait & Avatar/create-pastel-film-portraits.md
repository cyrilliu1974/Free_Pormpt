# Pastel Film Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Pastel Film Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates soft, film-inspired outdoor portraits with the signature look of Fujifilm Pro 400H. This pastel portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images with gentle highlights, creamy skin tones, organic grain, and the dreamy bokeh of a 56mm f/1.2 lens. You supply a subject, and the prompt simulates the nostalgic color palette and texture of classic medium-format film stock at 3:2 aspect ratio. Photographers, content creators, and designers reach for it when they want the warmth and softness of analog film without scanning negatives or owning vintage glass. ● Simulates Fujifilm Pro 400H color science: muted pastels, warm midtones, and organic grain structure. ● Mimics a 56mm f/1.2 prime lens for shallow depth of field and smooth background separation. ● Outputs 3:2 portraits optimized for print and editorial layouts. ● Single variable (subject) makes it fast to iterate across different people, poses, or outdoor settings. ## Prompt

```
Soft pastel-colored portrait of {{subject}} outdoors, photographed on simulated Fujifilm Pro 400H film. Gentle highlights, organic grain, creamy skin tones. Shot with a 56mm f/1.2 lens, dreamy bokeh. --ar 3:2 --quality 2 --stylize 400
```
