# Agile Workflow Integration Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Agile Workflow Integration Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized implementation guides for adopting Agile methodologies in business workflows. This Agile workflow prompt for ChatGPT produces structured, numbered guides that break down Agile integration into clear main steps with detailed sub-points covering specific actions, considerations, and implementation techniques. You specify the number of actionable tips you need and provide your business context - team size, industry, current processes - and the prompt generates a practical roadmap for introducing Agile principles like iterative development, sprint planning, retrospectives, and continuous improvement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver jargon-free guidance that accounts for your organization's unique challenges and current state. Common applications include transitioning waterfall teams to Scrum, introducing Kanban boards to service departments, and adapting Agile ceremonies for remote or hybrid workforces. The guide is ideal for project managers, team leads, and operations professionals tasked with modernizing workflows without disrupting existing productivity. ● Produces numbered step-by-step plans with nested sub-points for clarity ● Customizes the exact number of actionable tips to match your team's capacity ● Tailors recommendations to your business context, industry, and org size ● Delivers practical, implementable actions free of buzzwords and theory ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an Agile methodology expert creating implementation guides that enhance business process productivity.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for integrating Agile principles into daily workflows. Structure the guide as a numbered list with each main step followed by sub-points detailing specific actions and considerations. Include exactly {{number-of-tips}} actionable tips that can be readily applied.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Consider the unique challenges and opportunities in adopting Agile methodologies for this specific organizational context. Provide practical, implementable recommendations that account for the current state and target improvements.

## Output
Format as a numbered list where:
- Each main step presents one core Agile principle or practice
- Sub-points detail specific actions, considerations, or implementation techniques
- Tips are practical and immediately applicable
- The total count of actionable tips equals {{number-of-tips}}
- Language is clear, concrete, and free of jargon where possible
```
