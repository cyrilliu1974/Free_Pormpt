# Affiliate Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Affiliate Marketing Strategy Builder for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates sustainable passive income strategies by analyzing affiliate programs, evaluating commission structures, and recommending specific promotion tactics for solo business owners. This affiliate marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business model and target audience, then delivers a structured comparison table of 5-8 affiliate programs ranked by revenue potential and strategic fit. Each recommendation includes commission details (CPA, RevShare, tiered models, cookie duration, average earnings), program relevance to your niche, and 3-4 actionable promotion methods tailored to your existing marketing channels. The output also includes a 90-day implementation roadmap and key performance indicators to track success. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Solopreneurs, freelancers, and independent consultants use this prompt when they want to add affiliate revenue streams without disrupting their current operations or brand integrity. ● Evaluates affiliate programs based on niche relevance, commission rates, cookie duration, and audience alignment ● Delivers a comparison table with 5-8 programs ranked by revenue potential and strategic fit for your business ● Recommends 3-4 specific promotion tactics per program tailored to your existing marketing infrastructure ● Includes a phased 90-day implementation roadmap and KPIs to measure affiliate revenue performance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert affiliate marketing strategist specializing in passive income development for solopreneurs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive affiliate marketing strategy that creates sustainable passive income streams. Analyze relevant affiliate programs, evaluate their commission structures, and recommend specific promotion tactics that maximize revenue potential.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

Align all recommendations with the solopreneur's business goals, target audience preferences, and existing marketing infrastructure.

## Output
Provide your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Affiliate Program | Commission Structure | Promotion Tactics |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------|

Include 5-8 programs ranked by revenue potential and strategic fit. For each:
- **Affiliate Program**: Name, relevance to the niche, and why it suits this audience
- **Commission Structure**: Rate/model (CPA, RevShare, tiered), cookie duration, average earnings per conversion
- **Promotion Tactics**: 3-4 specific, actionable methods tailored to the existing marketing channels

After the table, provide a brief implementation roadmap (90-day phases) and key performance indicators to track.
```
