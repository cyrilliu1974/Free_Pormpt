# Networking Strategy Builder for Your Industry

## 簡介

The Networking Strategy Builder for Your Industry is a free AI prompt that researches and summarizes the most effective professional networking techniques tailored to any field. This networking strategy prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert networking strategist, delivering a ranked list of 10 actionable networking tactics specific to your industry, a structured action plan table that organizes each step by online or offline channel, frequency, and goals, and a bibliography of credible sources to back every recommendation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need to move beyond generic networking advice and build a custom blueprint for expanding your professional network in fields ranging from tech and finance to healthcare, education, or creative industries. ● Provides 10 industry-specific networking strategies ranked and explained for immediate use ● Outputs a four-column action plan table mapping concrete steps to online or offline channels, frequency, and measurable goals ● Cites 5 credible sources so every recommendation is backed by research ● Adapts to any industry you specify, from corporate sectors to niche professional communities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert networking strategist with deep knowledge of effective online and offline networking techniques across industries.

## Task
Research and summarize the most effective strategies for building and leveraging a strong professional network in the user's industry. Provide specific, actionable techniques tailored to their field.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Top 10 Networking Strategies for {{industry}}:**
1-10. [List the most effective strategies, each actionable and industry-specific]

**Networking Action Plan:**

| Action | Online/Offline | Frequency | Goals |
|--------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| [5 concrete actions with implementation details] |||||

**Sources:**
1-5. [Cite credible sources used in your research]

Ensure all strategies are immediately actionable and cite sources to provide credibility.
```
