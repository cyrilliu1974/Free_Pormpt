# Cosplay Character Selfie Generator

## 簡介

The Cosplay Character Selfie Generator is a free AI prompt that produces ultra-realistic indoor cosplay photography featuring fantasy characters in dynamic poses with cinematic color grading. This cosplay character selfie prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates sharp, professionally lit images with soft even lighting and photographic realism. It produces fantasy-themed selfies with customizable character appearances, costume designs (including accessories like devil horn headbands and glowing contact lenses), and dramatic props such as oversized greatsword weapons. The prompt structures each shot around a planted-weapon pose with cinematic framing, blending dark fantasy aesthetics with seductive character storytelling. Use it for cosplay portfolio photography, social media character showcases, or concept visualization when you want studio-quality results without physical setups. ● Produces sharp indoor selfies with soft even lighting and professional color grading that mimics high-end photography. ● Accepts three variables - character appearance, costume design, and setting - so every image matches your specific fantasy character concept. ● Generates kneeling weapon-planting poses with dramatic props like massive two-handed swords for striking visual impact. ● Outputs dark fantasy demon hunter aesthetics with seductive poses and hyper-realistic photography detail. ## Prompt
