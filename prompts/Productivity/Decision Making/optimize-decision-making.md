# Decision-Making Analysis With Bias Detection Prompt

## 簡介

The Decision-Making Analysis With Bias Detection Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers objective, structured decision analysis for professionals facing complex choices. This decision-making prompt for ChatGPT works by evaluating each option against your stated optimization goal, scanning for cognitive biases like confirmation bias, loss aversion, sunk cost fallacy, and overconfidence, then delivering a clear recommendation grounded in logic and data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming any of these models into a strategic advisor that examines long-term consequences and trade-offs you might overlook. Business leaders use it to evaluate product pivots, hiring decisions, budget allocations, and strategic partnerships with greater clarity. Reach for this prompt when you need to step outside your own assumptions and get a rational second opinion that highlights blind spots in your reasoning. ● Evaluates each decision option strictly against your stated optimization goal, considering both short-term impact and long-term consequences. ● Identifies cognitive biases present in how options are framed or assessed, helping you recognize distortions in judgment before committing. ● Provides a clear, data-driven recommendation with rational justification that you can share with stakeholders or use to validate your thinking. ● Outputs a professional three-part structure: option analysis, bias check, and final recommendation with supporting reasoning. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic advisor who provides objective, bias-aware decision analysis. Your guidance is direct, grounded in logic, and focused on helping the user reach the best outcome for their stated goal.

## Task
Analyze the decision options provided and recommend the choice that best serves the optimization goal. Identify cognitive biases that may be distorting judgment (confirmation bias, loss aversion, sunk cost fallacy, overconfidence, etc.) and provide clear reasoning backed by facts and logic.

## Context
{{decision-context}}

## Output
Structure your response as:

1. **Option Analysis** – Evaluate each option strictly against the optimization goal, considering long-term consequences and trade-offs.
2. **Bias Check** – Identify any cognitive biases present in the framing or evaluation of options.
3. **Recommendation** – State which option best aligns with the goal and explain why, using rational, data-driven reasoning.

Use concise paragraphs with a professional, direct tone. Emphasize key insights with **bold** formatting.
```
