# HR Compliance Audit Framework Builder

## 簡介

The HR Compliance Audit Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates practical audit frameworks for organizations needing to identify compliance risks, ensure regulatory adherence, and develop remediation strategies. This HR compliance audit prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok delivers a detailed 5-step framework that walks organizations through audit preparation, scope definition, documentation review, gap analysis, and actionable recommendations with implementation timelines. It addresses critical compliance areas including employment law adherence, documentation requirements, policy alignment with current regulations, and risk mitigation strategies. The framework is designed for organizations with limited HR resources that need to conduct thorough audits without external consultants, helping them surface risks before they escalate into lawsuits, regulatory penalties, or reputational damage. Reach for this prompt when your organization needs to conduct a compliance audit, prepare for regulatory review, or identify documentation gaps and policy misalignments that expose the business to legal risk. ● Guides audit preparation and scope definition to establish a clear foundation tailored to your organization profile and compliance concerns. ● Structures documentation review and gap analysis to pinpoint areas of non-compliance across employment law, policies, and record-keeping. ● Generates actionable recommendations with implementation timelines to remediate identified risks and align with current regulations. ● Provides specific deliverables for each audit step to ensure successful execution even with limited HR staffing. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR compliance specialist designing practical audit frameworks for organizations that need to identify risk areas, ensure regulatory adherence, and develop remediation strategies.

## Task
Create a detailed 5-step framework for conducting an HR compliance audit. Structure each step to build logically: begin with audit preparation and scope definition, proceed through documentation review and gap analysis, and conclude with actionable recommendations and implementation timelines.

## Context
**Organization profile:**  
{{organization-profile}}

**Primary compliance concerns:**  
{{compliance-concerns}}

The framework must be thorough yet practical for organizations with limited HR resources. Focus on critical compliance areas: employment law adherence, documentation requirements, policy alignment with current regulations, and risk mitigation strategies. Compliance failures lead to lawsuits, regulatory penalties, and reputational damage—the audit must surface these risks early.

## Output
Deliver the framework as a numbered list with:
- Clear step titles
- Detailed sub-actions in bullet points under each step
- Specific deliverables for each step to ensure successful implementation
```
