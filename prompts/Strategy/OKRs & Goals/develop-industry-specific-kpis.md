# Industry-Specific KPI Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Industry-Specific KPI Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates tailored Key Performance Indicators across financial, customer, and operational dimensions for any industry or profession. This industry-specific KPI prompt for ChatGPT works by accepting your industry or profession as input and producing a structured framework of 12-20 relevant metrics, each with a description that explains what it measures, how to calculate it, and why it matters. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and includes practical implementation guidelines, data collection recommendations, and monitoring strategies. Business consultants use it to build measurement frameworks for clients; operations managers rely on it to establish performance baselines; and executives turn to it when entering new markets or launching business units. ● Categorizes KPIs into financial, customer, operational, and domain-specific groups for complete performance visibility ● Provides calculation methods and context for each metric so teams understand both the how and the why ● Includes stakeholder alignment strategies and review frequency recommendations to sustain measurement discipline ● Delivers benchmarking approaches and adjustment triggers that support continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in KPI development across industries and professions.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive, categorized list of KPIs for the specified industry or profession. Include clear descriptions for each KPI to enable effective implementation and monitoring.

## Context
Industry or profession: {{industry-or-profession}}

KPIs must be specific to this domain and cover multiple dimensions of business performance. Focus on actionable metrics that drive decision-making rather than generic indicators.

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

**[Industry/Profession Name]**

**Financial KPIs:**
- KPI name: Clear description explaining what it measures, how to calculate it, and why it matters
- (3-5 financial metrics)

**Customer KPIs:**
- KPI name: Clear description explaining what it measures, how to calculate it, and why it matters
- (3-5 customer metrics)

**Operational KPIs:**
- KPI name: Clear description explaining what it measures, how to calculate it, and why it matters
- (3-5 operational metrics)

**[Additional Relevant Category] KPIs:**
- KPI name: Clear description explaining what it measures, how to calculate it, and why it matters
- (3-5 category-specific metrics)

**Implementation Guidelines:**
1. Practical steps for integrating these KPIs into existing business processes
2. Data collection and tracking recommendations
3. Stakeholder alignment and communication strategies

**Monitoring and Evaluation:**
- Review frequency recommendations
- Benchmarking approaches
- Adjustment triggers and continuous improvement practices
```
