# Headline Rewriter for Conversion-Focused Copy

## 簡介

The Headline Rewriter for Conversion-Focused Copy is a free AI prompt that transforms weak headlines into three high-conversion alternatives for copywriters, marketers, and content creators. This headline writing prompt for ChatGPT takes your generic headline and produces three rewritten versions, each analyzed for why it works. The prompt instructs the AI to apply four proven copywriting principles: specificity (focused pain points or benefits), curiosity (unexpected insights or contrarian angles), actionability (clear, achievable outcomes), and emotion (power words and sensory details). It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, keeping each alternative under 20 words while avoiding clichés. Each output includes the rewritten headline plus an explanation of how it incorporates the four conversion elements. Use it when you need to test multiple headline variations for landing pages, email subject lines, blog posts, or ad campaigns. ● Generates three distinct headline alternatives from a single generic input ● Applies specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion to every rewrite ● Includes explanations showing how each headline incorporates conversion principles ● Keeps output concise (under 20 words per headline) and avoids hype or clichés ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in high-conversion headlines.

## Task
Transform the user's generic headline into three captivating, click-worthy alternatives. Each headline must incorporate:

- **Specificity**: Zoom in on a particular pain point, benefit, strategy, or outcome
- **Curiosity**: Hint at an unexpected insight, contrarian view, or insider secret
- **Actionability**: Promise a clear, achievable outcome the reader will gain
- **Emotion**: Use power words, sensory details, or storytelling to evoke feeling

## Constraints
- Keep each headline under 20 words
- Avoid clichés and hyperbole
- Maintain relevance to the original topic

## Input
Generic headline: {{generic-headline}}

## Output
For each of the three headline options, provide:

**Headline:** [the rewritten headline]

**Explanation:** Brief analysis of why it works and how it incorporates specificity, curiosity, actionability, and emotion.
```
