# Task Prioritization System Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Task Prioritization System Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a customized productivity framework for professionals struggling to manage workload and reduce stress. This task prioritization prompt for ChatGPT creates a complete system in seconds: you supply your role, responsibilities, and key challenges, and the prompt produces guiding principles, a prioritization methodology, task categories, a daily planning process, workload management techniques, stress-reduction strategies, and a weekly review protocol. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering evidence-based frameworks that fit your actual work context. Product managers, consultants, team leads, and anyone juggling competing priorities use it to replace ad-hoc task lists with a structured, repeatable system. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from reactive fire-fighting to proactive planning, or when stress and overload are undermining your effectiveness. ● Produces 3 guiding principles and a clear prioritization methodology based on your specific role and challenges ● Delivers a 4-category task framework and 5-step daily planning process you can start using immediately ● Includes 3 workload management techniques and 3 stress-reduction strategies grounded in productivity research ● Provides a weekly review protocol so you can adapt the system as your responsibilities evolve ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in task prioritization, time management, and stress reduction strategies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive task prioritization and workload management system tailored to the user's specific work context and challenges. The system should be actionable, flexible, and proven to increase productivity while reducing stress.

## Context
- Current role and responsibilities: {{role-and-responsibilities}}
- Key productivity challenges and stress triggers: {{challenges-and-triggers}}

## Requirements
- Develop a clear, easy-to-implement framework adaptable to different work styles
- Focus on evidence-based strategies that demonstrably improve productivity and reduce stress
- Avoid overly complex processes that may themselves become sources of friction or stress

## Output
Deliver a complete system structured as follows:

**Guiding Principles**
3 foundational principles that underpin the entire system

**Prioritization Methodology**
A clear method for categorizing and prioritizing tasks effectively

**Task Categorization Framework**
4 task categories with descriptions explaining how to classify work

**Daily Planning Process**
5-step process for organizing and managing daily tasks

**Workload Management Techniques**
3 techniques with descriptions for optimizing productivity and managing capacity

**Stress Reduction Strategies**
3 strategies with descriptions for maintaining work-life balance

**Weekly Review and Adjustment Process**
A systematic approach for evaluating effectiveness and adapting the system over time
```
