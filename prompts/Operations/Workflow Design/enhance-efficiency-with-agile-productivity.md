# Agile Implementation Plan Generator for Teams

## 簡介

The Agile Implementation Plan Generator for Teams is a free AI prompt that creates customized Agile methodology plans for professionals managing projects with Scrum or Kanban. This Agile productivity prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a practical implementation roadmap delivered as a markdown table with three columns: Tasks, Sprints, and Retrospectives. It adapts to your specific work context, team size, and project goals, producing actionable steps for backlog creation, role assignment, sprint cadence, daily ceremonies, and continuous improvement tracking. Teams adopting Agile for the first time or refining existing workflows will find concrete guidance that addresses their unique challenges. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Breaks down Agile adoption into clear tasks covering backlog setup, role definitions, and tooling decisions ● Defines sprint structure with planning sessions, daily standups, reviews, and measurable success criteria ● Provides retrospective formats with key questions, learning capture methods, and improvement tracking ● Adapts recommendations to team size, project duration, and whether you use Scrum or Kanban ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an Agile methodology expert who designs practical implementation plans tailored to team contexts.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Agile implementation plan that outlines tasks, sprints, and retrospectives to maximize efficiency and productivity.

## Context
{{work-context}}

Consider the unique aspects of this work, potential challenges specific to the team size and goals, and current Agile best practices for Scrum or Kanban. Address the stated challenges in your recommendations.

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Tasks | Sprints | Retrospectives |
|-------|---------|----------------|

Each column must provide detailed, actionable steps tailored to the work context:

- **Tasks**: Break down implementation activities, including backlog creation, role assignment, ceremony setup, and tooling
- **Sprints**: Define sprint structure, cadence, ceremonies (planning, daily standups, reviews), and success metrics
- **Retrospectives**: Outline retrospective format, key questions to ask, how to capture learnings, and improvement tracking methods

Ensure the plan is realistic for the team size and project duration specified.
```
