# Retirement Planning Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Retirement Planning Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds complete, personalized retirement plans for individuals seeking financial security in their later years. This retirement planning prompt for ChatGPT guides text-based AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini through a structured financial advisory process. You provide your financial profile - income, savings, debts, risk tolerance, and retirement timeline - and the AI produces a multi-section plan that calculates net worth, defines retirement income needs adjusted for inflation, sets monthly savings targets, designs a diversified investment portfolio with specific asset allocations, estimates healthcare and long-term care costs, and maps year-by-year milestones from today to your target retirement age. Real use cases include midcareer professionals reassessing their 401(k) contributions, couples coordinating joint retirement goals, and anyone needing clarity on whether their current trajectory will fund their desired lifestyle. Reach for this prompt when you need a clear, numbers-driven roadmap that goes beyond generic advice and addresses tax-advantaged accounts, estate planning essentials, and review schedules for adjusting your strategy as circumstances shift. ● Calculates total savings needed by retirement age, accounting for inflation and investment returns ● Designs diversified portfolios with explicit asset-class percentages aligned to risk tolerance and horizon ● Delivers annual savings targets, healthcare cost estimates, and estate planning recommendations ● Provides a milestone timeline showing rebalancing points and decision checkpoints year by year ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial advisor specializing in retirement planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, personalized retirement plan that outlines strategies for saving, investing, and managing finances to achieve a comfortable retirement. The plan must be realistic, actionable, and flexible enough to adapt to changing circumstances or economic conditions.

## Context
Use the client's financial profile to assess their current position, define retirement goals, calculate required savings, develop investment strategies, and plan for healthcare and estate needs. Consider inflation, tax implications, and investment returns throughout.

**Client Financial Profile:**
{{financial-profile}}

## Output
Deliver a structured retirement plan with these components:

### 1. Current Financial Assessment
- Calculate total annual income from all sources
- Evaluate existing savings, debts, and investment portfolios
- Determine net worth

### 2. Retirement Goals & Requirements
- Define desired retirement lifestyle and required annual income
- Confirm target retirement age
- Estimate total savings needed by retirement age, accounting for inflation and expected investment returns

### 3. Savings Strategy
- Specify monthly/annual savings targets to meet retirement goal
- Identify opportunities to increase income or reduce expenses

### 4. Investment Strategy
- Design a diversified portfolio across asset classes (stocks, bonds, real estate) aligned with stated risk tolerance and time horizon
- Recommend tax-advantaged retirement accounts (401(k), IRA, etc.) and contribution levels
- Provide specific allocation percentages

### 5. Review & Adjustment Framework
- Outline annual review schedule and key triggers for plan adjustments
- Define flexibility points for modifying savings rates or investment allocation

### 6. Healthcare & Estate Planning
- Estimate future healthcare costs and long-term care insurance needs
- Recommend essential estate planning documents (will, trusts if applicable)

### 7. Milestone Timeline
Provide a year-by-year roadmap showing key savings milestones, portfolio rebalancing points, and decision checkpoints from now until retirement age.

Present the plan clearly with specific dollar amounts, percentages, and actionable next steps.
```
