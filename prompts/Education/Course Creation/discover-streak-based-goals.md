# Streak-Based Gamification Design for Learning Goals

## 簡介

The Streak-Based Gamification Design for Learning Goals is a free AI prompt that creates motivational streak systems for educators, course creators, and instructional designers. This gamification prompt for ChatGPT generates a structured markdown table of 5–8 milestone tiers that balance challenge with attainability. It works by taking your subject, target audience, learning objectives, and resource constraints, then outputting goals that ladder in difficulty, rewards that reinforce mastery (not just extrinsic prizes), and streak durations calibrated to your learners' typical engagement patterns. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring each reward connects to learning progress or unlocks new content rather than letting game mechanics overshadow educational outcomes. Reach for this prompt when you need to maintain long-term learner motivation through sustainable engagement mechanics that support, rather than distract from, actual skill development. ● Generates 5–8 milestone tiers ranging from beginner to advanced engagement levels in a clean markdown table. ● Balances goal difficulty with learner capabilities to prevent burnout or disengagement. ● Creates intrinsic rewards tied to mastery milestones and content unlocks, not just badges or points. ● Calibrates streak durations to match your audience's realistic availability and commitment patterns. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert gamification designer specializing in educational engagement systems.

## Task
Design a streak-based gamification structure for learning {{subject}} that drives consistent engagement through achievable goals, meaningful rewards, and sustainable streak mechanics. Balance challenge with attainability to maintain long-term motivation without letting game mechanics overshadow educational outcomes.

## Context
**Target learners:** {{audience}}
**Learning objectives:** {{learning-objectives}}
**Implementation constraints:** {{resources-and-timeframe}}

Create goals that ladder in difficulty, rewards that reinforce learning progress (not just extrinsic prizes), and streak durations calibrated to the audience's typical engagement cadence. Ensure each reward connects to mastery or unlock of new content.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:

| Goal | Reward | Streak Duration |
|------|--------|----------------|

Include 5–8 milestone tiers ranging from beginner to advanced engagement levels.
```
