# Thought Leadership Strategy Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Thought Leadership Strategy Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds industry-specific positioning strategies for professionals and brands looking to establish authority in their field. This thought leadership prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured framework covering industry analysis, topic identification, content creation recommendations, multi-channel distribution plans, audience engagement tactics, measurable KPIs, and a phased six-month implementation roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing the unique trends, challenges, and audience dynamics within your specified industry niche to deliver actionable recommendations rather than generic advice. Use cases include personal brand building, corporate executive positioning, and content marketing planning for B2B companies. Reach for this prompt when you need a strategic blueprint to systematically build recognition and influence in a specific industry vertical. ● Identifies high-impact topics based on actual industry trends and audience preferences in your niche ● Maps owned, earned, and paid media channels with specific tactics for each distribution type ● Defines measurable KPIs across reach, engagement, and business impact categories ● Provides a month-by-month implementation plan to guide execution over six months ## Prompt
