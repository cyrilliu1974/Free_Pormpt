# Voir Dire Questionnaire Builder for Trial Attorneys

## 簡介

The Voir Dire Questionnaire Builder for Trial Attorneys is a free AI prompt that generates comprehensive jury selection questionnaires tailored to specific case details and legal strategies. This voir dire prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces structured questionnaires organized into 6-8 thematic sections, each with numbered questions, group or individual designations, strategic notes explaining what responses reveal, and timing estimates. It applies proven psychological techniques including normalization to reduce social desirability bias, projective questioning to surface hidden attitudes, and the Socratic funnel method that progresses from rapport-building ice-breakers to pointed bias-revealing inquiries. Trial attorneys use it to identify cause challenges, gather peremptory strike intelligence, and introduce favorable case themes during voir dire. Reach for this prompt when preparing for jury selection in civil or criminal trials, when you need to systematically identify favorable and unfavorable jurors, or when building a voir dire strategy that complies with jurisdiction-specific rules. ● Outputs juror profiles defining ideal and problematic characteristics based on case facts and legal theory. ● Structures questions in natural flow from broad rapport-building to specific bias detection tied to contested facts. ● Includes bracketed strategic notes after critical questions explaining psychological indicators and follow-up branches. ● Marks cause challenge foundations and jurisdiction-specific compliance requirements throughout the questionnaire. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced trial attorney specializing in jury selection strategy and voir dire questionnaire design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive voir dire questionnaire that uncovers juror biases, attitudes, and predispositions through strategic questioning techniques. The questionnaire should identify favorable and unfavorable jurors while maintaining a conversational, neutral tone that encourages candid responses.

## Context
{{case-details}}

Effective voir dire requires questions that:
- Progress from rapport-building ice-breakers to specific bias-revealing inquiries
- Use psychological techniques including normalization (to reduce social desirability bias), projective questioning, and layered inquiry methods
- Comply with jurisdiction-specific rules
- Serve three objectives: identifying cause challenges, gathering peremptory strike intelligence, and introducing case themes to favorable jurors

## Output
Provide a structured voir dire questionnaire with:

**Juror Profiles**
- Ideal juror characteristics based on the case
- Problematic juror characteristics to identify

**Question Sections** (6-8 thematic areas)
For each section:
- Numbered questions for courtroom reference
- [GROUP] or [INDIVIDUAL] designation
- [Strategic notes] after key questions explaining what responses reveal and potential follow-up branches
- Timing estimate

Structure questions to flow naturally:
1. Broad rapport-building questions
2. General attitude exploration
3. Experience-based inquiries
4. Specific bias-revealing questions related to contested facts
5. Case-theory alignment questions

**Format Requirements**
- Clear section headers
- Bullet points for easy scanning
- Bracketed strategic guidance after critical questions
- Cause challenge foundations noted where applicable
- Compliance notes for jurisdiction-specific rules
```
