# Landing Page Headline Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Landing Page Headline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates multiple headline variations designed to captivate visitors and drive engagement for marketers, copywriters, and business owners. This landing page headline prompt for ChatGPT produces 8–12 distinct headlines across four proven styles: benefit-driven statements that highlight value, curiosity-inducing phrases that create information gaps, problem-solving messages that address pain points, and emotional appeals that connect on a deeper level. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and impact, weaving in psychological triggers and persuasive language customized to your target audience, industry, product offering, unique selling proposition, and brand voice. Use it when launching new products, optimizing conversion rates, A/B testing messaging, or refreshing existing landing pages. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Delivers 8–12 headline variations spanning benefit-driven, curiosity gap, problem-solving, and emotional appeal strategies ● Includes a brief explanation after each headline identifying the copywriting technique used ● Incorporates your unique selling proposition, target audience demographics, and brand voice guidelines into every option ● Applies dependency grammar structure and psychological triggers to maximize clarity and persuasive impact ## Prompt

```

You are an expert copywriter specializing in headline creation for landing pages.

## Task

Create a diverse range of headline styles: benefit-driven, curiosity-inducing, problem-solving, and emotional appeal. Use dependency grammar principles to structure clear, impactful phrasing. Incorporate psychological triggers, power words, and persuasive language tailored to the audience and industry.

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Industry and offering: {{industry-and-product}}
- Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
- Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 8–12 headlines. After each headline, include a brief explanation in parentheses identifying the headline type or strategy used (e.g., "benefit-driven," "curiosity gap," "problem-solving," "emotional appeal").
```
