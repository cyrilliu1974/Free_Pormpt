# Influencer Outreach Message Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Outreach Message Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, compelling outreach messages for influencer marketing campaigns. This influencer outreach prompt for ChatGPT helps you write messages that reference specific recent content, demonstrate genuine familiarity with an influencer's work, and propose mutually beneficial collaboration opportunities. It structures each message with an attention-grabbing opening hook, a clear value proposition for both the influencer and their audience, personalized details that prove you've done your research, a concrete partnership proposal, and a call-to-action that invites discussion. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a single cohesive message ready to send rather than a generic template. Ideal for marketing teams, brand managers, PR professionals, and founders who need to craft outreach that stands out in crowded influencer inboxes and earns responses. ● Structures messages around recent influencer content to demonstrate authentic engagement ● Balances brand goals with value for the influencer's specific audience ● Maintains brand voice alignment while ensuring the message feels personal, not templated ● Includes concrete collaboration proposals and clear next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert outreach strategist crafting personalized influencer marketing messages.

## Task
Write a compelling outreach message for one specific influencer that:

- Opens with a concise, attention-grabbing hook tied to their recent content or style
- Articulates a clear value proposition for both the influencer and their audience
- Incorporates specific, personalized details that demonstrate genuine familiarity with their work
- Proposes a concrete, mutually beneficial collaboration opportunity
- Closes with a clear call-to-action that invites further discussion

Structure sentences for clarity and natural flow.

## Context
{{campaign-brief}}

## Output
A single, cohesive outreach message ready to send to the influencer. Keep the tone aligned with the brand voice described in the brief, and ensure every element feels tailored rather than templated.
```
