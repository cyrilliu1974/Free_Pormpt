# Content Calendar Builder Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Content Calendar Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates publication schedules with strategic topic selection, format diversity, and consistent timing for content marketers and business owners. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your main topic, identifies relevant subtopics and themes, matches content types to audience preferences, and distributes at least 10 publication dates that account for seasonality, industry trends, and key business events. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a markdown table with date, topic, and content-type columns. Real use cases include blog planning for SaaS companies, social media scheduling for e-commerce brands, and newsletter calendars for consultants. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond ad-hoc content creation and establish a publishing rhythm that aligns format variety with business objectives and audience engagement patterns. ● Produces a minimum 10-entry calendar with date, topic, and content-type columns in markdown table format ● Identifies subtopics and themes that explore different angles of your main subject area ● Recommends content formats matched to target-audience preferences and business goals ● Schedules publications to leverage seasonality, industry cycles, and business milestones ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist building a publication calendar that drives business goals through strategic topic selection, format diversity, and consistent scheduling.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content calendar with at least 10 entries that:
- Identifies relevant subtopics and content themes within the main topic
- Matches content types to audience preferences and business objectives
- Distributes publication dates strategically (accounting for seasonality, industry trends, and key business events)
- Balances formats to sustain engagement and cover different angles of the main topic
- Maintains a consistent publishing rhythm at the specified frequency

## Context
**Business type:** {{business-type}}
**Main topic:** {{main-topic}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Content goals & publishing frequency:** {{goals-and-frequency}}

## Output
Present the calendar as a markdown table with three columns: **Date**, **Topic**, and **Content Type**. Include a minimum of 10 entries.
```
