# Website SEO Optimization Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Website SEO Optimization Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, step-by-step SEO strategies tailored to your website's niche, audience, and current challenges. This website SEO optimization prompt for ChatGPT walks through ten essential on-page and technical SEO dimensions: keyword research aligned to your industry, meta tag and header hierarchy optimization, internal linking architecture, content creation for search intent, readability improvements, competitor gap analysis, schema markup implementation, image optimization, and mobile responsiveness with page-speed tuning. It outputs a numbered action plan with specific implementation steps under each recommendation, making it immediately actionable for content managers, marketers, and developers. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini and adapts to your target audience, niche, primary keywords, existing SEO challenges, and available content resources. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured roadmap to improve organic visibility, whether launching a new site, recovering from a traffic drop, or systematically refining an existing property. ● Produces keyword research tied to audience intent and competitive landscape, not generic term lists ● Builds header hierarchies and internal link maps that distribute authority and improve crawlability ● Recommends schema types, image alt-text patterns, and mobile-performance fixes with clear next steps ● Includes competitor differentiation analysis so your content strategy addresses gaps others miss ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist creating a comprehensive optimization strategy to improve website visibility and organic traffic.

## Task
Develop a detailed, actionable SEO strategy addressing:

1. Keyword research relevant to the target audience and industry
2. Meta tag optimization (title tags, meta descriptions)
3. Header tag hierarchy implementation (H1, H2, H3) for content structure
4. Internal linking strategy for improved navigation and authority distribution
5. High-quality content creation aligned with audience needs and search intent
6. Content structuring for readability and semantic clarity
7. Competitor analysis identifying differentiation opportunities
8. Schema markup implementation for enhanced search engine understanding
9. Image optimization (alt text, file naming conventions)
10. Technical performance (mobile responsiveness, page speed)
```
