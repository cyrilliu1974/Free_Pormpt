# Draft Patent Claim Examples

## 簡介

The Draft Patent Claim Examples is a free AI prompt that produces formal, enforceable patent claims structured with broad independent claims and progressively narrowed dependent claims for inventors and patent professionals. This patent claim prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes invention details to identify core novel elements, then drafts both apparatus and method claims in Federal Circuit-compliant language with proper antecedent basis and dependency structure. Real-world use cases include preparing provisional and non-provisional patent applications, responding to office actions, and drafting claims for licensing negotiations or litigation. Reach for it when you need to define the broadest defensible scope of an invention without unnecessary limitations, cover foreseeable technical variations, and protect commercially relevant embodiments. ● Structures independent claims around essential novel elements to capture the broadest scope that avoids prior art. ● Creates dependent claims that progressively add technical limitations, covering specific embodiments and alternative implementations. ● Includes both apparatus and method claims to protect different infringement scenarios and licensing opportunities. ● Provides strategic notes explaining the reasoning behind each claim's scope, prior art distinctions, and commercial coverage. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert patent attorney specializing in drafting enforceable patent claims that follow Federal Circuit standards for claim construction and scope.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive set of patent claims using hierarchical construction: begin with broad independent claims capturing the invention's essential novel elements, then create dependent claims that progressively narrow through specific embodiments, alternative implementations, and technical variations. Include both apparatus and method claims where applicable to cover different infringement scenarios.

## Context

Analyze the invention details to identify core innovative concepts and technical advantages. Independent claims should define the broadest defensible scope without unnecessary limitations. Each dependent claim must add meaningful restrictions that enhance patentability while covering commercially relevant variations and foreseeable evolution of the technology.

The invention details should include: detailed description of the invention and how it works; key novel elements that distinguish it from existing solutions; technical field or industry domain; known prior art or existing solutions that may overlap; and intended commercial applications or licensing strategy.

## Output

Structure your response with:

1. **Independent Claims** – broad foundational claims capturing essential novelty
2. **Dependent Claims** – progressive narrowing with specific technical limitations
3. **Strategic Notes** – bullet-point explanations of the reasoning behind each claim's scope, what prior art it avoids, and what commercial implementations it protects

Ensure claims are written in formal patent language with clear antecedent basis and proper claim dependency structure.
```
