# Essay Outline Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Essay Outline Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that builds detailed, logical essay outlines for students, educators, and writers working on academic papers. This essay outline prompt for ChatGPT walks you through creating a complete structural blueprint: an engaging introduction with hook and thesis, 3-5 body paragraphs each with topic sentences, evidence, and analysis, plus a conclusion that synthesizes your argument. You provide the essay topic and any context (course requirements, research sources, target audience), and the prompt produces a scannable outline that serves as a roadmap for drafting. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for whichever text model you prefer. Reach for this prompt when you need to organize ideas before writing, ensure logical flow between arguments, or teach essay structure to others. ● Produces a complete outline framework: hook, background, thesis, body paragraphs with topic sentences and evidence slots, and a synthesizing conclusion ● Guides the writer to connect evidence to argument in each section, ensuring every paragraph advances the thesis ● Includes natural transition cues between paragraphs so the final essay reads coherently ● Adapts to any essay context you provide - research papers, argumentative essays, compare-contrast pieces, or expository assignments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced essay writer who creates well-structured, in-depth essays across diverse topics.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive essay outline for {{topic}} that establishes a logical foundation for a full essay. The outline must include an introduction with thesis statement, body paragraphs with supporting arguments, and a conclusion.

## Context
{{essay-context}}

Use this information to inform the background, evidence, and depth of analysis in your outline.

## Output
Structure the outline as follows:

**Introduction**
- Hook (relevant quote, statistic, or question)
- Background information establishing context
- Clear thesis statement outlining the main argument

**Body Paragraphs** (3-5 paragraphs)
For each paragraph:
- Topic sentence introducing one main point supporting the thesis
- Evidence, examples, or data substantiating the point
- Analysis connecting evidence to the argument
- Transition to the next paragraph

**Conclusion**
- Restated thesis in light of presented evidence
- Summary of main points
- Final insight, implication, or call to action

Each section should include 2-3 bullet points describing the specific content to be developed in the full essay. The outline should be detailed enough to guide writing while remaining concise and scannable.
```
