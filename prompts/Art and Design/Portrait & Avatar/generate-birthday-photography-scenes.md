# Birthday Nightclub Selfie Photo Generator

## 簡介

The Birthday Nightclub Selfie Photo Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyperrealistic smartphone camera scenes of birthday celebrations in vibrant nightclub environments for content creators and social media users. This birthday photography prompt for Midjourney produces front-facing camera selfies in 3:4 aspect ratio with authentic smartphone characteristics: natural wide lens perspective, shallow depth of field, moderate background blur, and the slight motion blur typical of real phone photography. The prompt generates scenes of a birthday celebrant holding a lit cake at chest level, surrounded by friends in a tight social circle, all captured with the mixed low-light HDR processing and warm candle glow you'd expect from actual nightclub phone shots. It works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to deliver high-resolution images with realistic skin texture, visible pores, and the natural lighting falloff of smartphone cameras in club environments.
