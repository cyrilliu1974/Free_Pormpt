# Employment Verification Letter Prompt for HR Compliance

## 簡介

The Employment Verification Letter Prompt for HR Compliance is a free AI prompt that drafts professional, legally compliant employment verification letters for HR specialists and compliance officers. This employment verification letter prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces formal business letters containing only factual, verifiable employee information while adhering to EEOC Employment Recordkeeping Guidelines. It structures each letter with proper company letterhead, date, recipient details, formal salutation, employment dates, job title, core responsibilities, and verification contact information. HR teams use it to respond to background check requests, mortgage lenders, government agencies, and any third party requiring official employment confirmation without introducing legal liability through subjective language or compliance gaps. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce neutral, fact-based employment verification quickly while maintaining strict adherence to employment law standards and anti-discrimination requirements. ● Produces letters containing only verifiable facts: legal name, official job title, employment dates, and documented responsibilities. ● Structures output in formal business letter format with proper spacing, company letterhead placement, and professional closing. ● Eliminates subjective assessments, personal opinions, and any language that could be construed as discriminatory under EEOC standards. ● Includes compliance notes explaining the legal safeguards built into each letter to help HR teams understand the protective measures in place. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR compliance specialist preparing employment verification letters that adhere to EEOC Employment Recordkeeping Guidelines and employment law standards.

## Task
Create a professional, legally compliant employment verification letter in formal business letter format. The letter must contain only factual, verifiable information and remain completely neutral—free from subjective assessments, personal opinions, or language that could be construed as discriminatory.

## Context
This document will be used for external verification, background checks, or official proceedings where accuracy and legal compliance are essential. Any inaccuracy or bias could create legal liability.

## Input
{{employee-details}}
(Full legal name, official job title, employment dates [start/end or "present"], and 3-5 main job responsibilities)

{{company-information}}
(Company name, address, and contact details for letterhead and verification purposes)

## Output
Deliver the response in two parts:

1. **The Employment Verification Letter**—formatted as a formal business letter with:
   - Company letterhead details
   - Date
   - Recipient information (if known) or "To Whom It May Concern"
   - Formal salutation
   - Body confirming job title, employment duration, and core responsibilities
   - Closing with contact information for verification
   - Proper spacing and professional business letter conventions

2. **Compliance Notes**—a brief explanation (3-4 sentences) of the key EEOC compliance elements incorporated into the letter to ensure legal adherence and neutrality.
```
