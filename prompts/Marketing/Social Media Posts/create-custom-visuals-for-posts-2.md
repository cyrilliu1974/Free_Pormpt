# Social Media Visual Design Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Visual Design Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, actionable design guides for social media visuals tailored to specific platforms, products, and audiences. This social media visual design prompt for ChatGPT takes your brand identity, target platform, product details, audience profile, and campaign objectives and outputs a structured step-by-step guide covering format requirements, creative concepts, design elements, video storyboards, calls-to-action, and A/B testing variations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering platform-specific instructions for graphics, infographics, and video content optimized for engagement and conversion. Marketers, content creators, and social media managers use it to streamline visual content planning, ensure brand consistency across campaigns, and produce visuals that meet technical specifications while resonating with their target audience. ● Outputs platform-specific dimension, format, and file optimization requirements for every major social network ● Identifies key product benefits and audience preferences to highlight in visual concepts ● Provides actionable design guidance on color schemes, typography, imagery, and video storyboards aligned with brand identity ● Includes clear call-to-action strategies and A/B testing variation ideas to maximize engagement and conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert visual content creator specializing in social media promotion.

## Task
Create a detailed step-by-step guide for designing custom visuals (graphics, infographics, or videos) optimized for {{platform}} to promote {{product-or-service}} and engage {{target-audience}}.

## Context
Brand identity: {{brand-identity}}
Campaign goals: {{campaign-goals}}

The guide should address:
- Platform-specific format and dimension requirements
- Key product features and benefits to highlight
- Audience preferences and interests
- Creative concepts aligned with brand and campaign objectives
- Visual design elements (color schemes, typography, imagery)
- Video considerations (storyboards, music, optimal length) if applicable
- Clear calls-to-action to drive engagement and conversions
- Brand message cohesion across all visual elements
- File optimization for fast loading and quality display across devices
- Variation creation for A/B testing effectiveness

## Output
Provide a comprehensive step-by-step guide using:
- Numbered lists for main steps
- Bullet points for sub-steps and details

Ensure each step is actionable and specific to the platform, product, audience, brand identity, and campaign goals provided.
```
