# Performance Review Strategy Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Performance Review Strategy Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates actionable strategies to transform evaluative performance reviews into developmental conversations for managers, HR professionals, and organizational leaders. This performance review strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered list of 10 research-backed approaches grounded in organizational psychology. Each strategy includes a clear principle, its psychological or structural foundation, and 2-3 immediately actionable tactics for preparation, execution, and follow-up. It addresses real pain points - unclear expectations, surprise feedback, one-way conversations, power imbalances, lack of development plans, and judgment-focused cultures - while considering both manager and employee perspectives. Run it on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini with your organizational context to receive a coherent system for transforming your entire review ecosystem. Reach for this prompt when you need to redesign a performance review process, train managers on developmental conversations, or shift from anxiety-inducing evaluations to growth-catalyzing dialogue. ● Produces 10 strategies that address psychological safety, structural design, and cultural norms simultaneously ● Balances emotional and practical dimensions, covering manager and employee viewpoints across all review phases ● Tackles common obstacles like surprise feedback, one-way conversations, and lack of actionable development plans ● Delivers implementation tactics that can be adopted immediately, not abstract principles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist and performance management consultant specializing in transforming evaluative review cultures into developmental systems.

## Context
Traditional performance reviews often trigger defensive behaviors, focus on past failures, and create anxiety rather than growth. This transformation must address psychological safety, structural design, and cultural norms while remaining practical across different organizational contexts.

## Task
Provide 10 research-backed approaches that shift performance reviews from anxiety-inducing evaluations into growth-catalyzing conversations. Each approach must be immediately actionable and address both emotional and practical dimensions—considering manager and employee perspectives throughout preparation, execution, and follow-up phases.

Address common pain points:
- Unclear expectations and surprise feedback
- One-way conversations and power imbalances
- Lack of actionable development plans
- Focus on judgment rather than learning

Tailor your recommendations to:
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list (1–10). For each approach:
- **Clear heading** naming the approach
- Brief explanation of the psychological or structural principle
- 2–3 specific implementation tactics that can be adopted immediately

Ensure the set of approaches works together as a coherent system for transforming the entire review ecosystem.
```
