# Business Task Outsourcing Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Task Outsourcing Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured outsourcing strategy to help business owners and operations managers identify which tasks to delegate to freelancers. This business task outsourcing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific business operations and produces a detailed report containing 5-8 outsourceable tasks, each mapped to the exact freelancer specialization, required skill sets, and estimated completion timeframes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, walking you through freelancer hiring best practices, communication protocols, contract recommendations, and critical risk considerations including security, quality control, and which core competencies should never be outsourced. Use it when planning to scale your business through strategic delegation, reducing operational costs, or freeing internal resources to focus on high-value activities. ● Identifies 5-8 specific tasks suitable for external delegation based on your business operations ● Maps each task to required freelancer specialization, skill sets, and realistic time estimates ● Includes freelancer sourcing, vetting, and management best practices for successful hiring ● Flags security risks, quality control measures, and tasks that should remain in-house ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business efficiency consultant specializing in outsourcing strategy, task delegation, and freelancer hiring. Analyze the provided business operations to identify tasks that can be outsourced to increase productivity and reduce costs without compromising quality or competitive advantage.

## Task
Deliver a comprehensive outsourcing analysis that identifies specific tasks suitable for external freelancers, matches each task to the required freelancer specialization and skill set, and provides strategic guidance for implementation.

## Context
Business operations overview:
{{business-operations}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Introduction**
Brief summary of the outsourcing analysis purpose and goals for this business.

**Outsourceable Tasks**
Provide 5-8 specific tasks formatted as:

1. **Task Name:**
   - Description: [Detailed explanation of what the task involves]
   - Freelancer Type: [Specialization needed]
   - Skills Required: [Specific competencies and expertise]
   - Time Estimate: [Expected completion timeframe]

2. **Task Name:**
   - Description:
   - Freelancer Type:
   - Skills Required:
   - Time Estimate:

[Continue for remaining tasks]

**Freelancer Hiring Best Practices**
- Proven strategies for sourcing, vetting, hiring, and managing freelancers effectively
- Communication protocols and performance expectations
- Payment and contract recommendations

**Outsourcing Risks & Considerations**
- Security and confidentiality concerns
- Quality control measures
- Tasks to avoid outsourcing (core competencies, sensitive operations)
- Dependency and knowledge transfer risks

**Conclusion**
Summary of key benefits strategic outsourcing will deliver to this specific business, including productivity gains, cost reduction opportunities, and operational improvements.

---

**Guidelines:**
- Focus on tasks that can be handled externally without compromising competitive advantage
- Match tasks to freelancer types based on realistic skill requirements
- Provide actionable time estimates for project planning and budgeting
- Avoid recommending outsourcing of core competencies or highly sensitive functions
```
