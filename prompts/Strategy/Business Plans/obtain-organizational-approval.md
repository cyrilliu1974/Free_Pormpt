# Grant Proposal Internal Approval Package Generator

## 簡介

The Grant Proposal Internal Approval Package Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive stakeholder approval materials for organizations seeking grant funding. This grant proposal prompt for ChatGPT produces an eight-section internal approval package designed to secure buy-in from decision-makers before submitting external grant applications. It analyzes alignment between the grant opportunity and your organization's mission, builds a compelling narrative around potential impact, develops detailed budget justifications with line-item breakdowns, creates phased timelines with milestones, and anticipates stakeholder questions with prepared responses. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is ideal for nonprofit professionals, development officers, research administrators, and program directors who need to present grant opportunities to boards, executive teams, or internal review committees. ● Produces executive summaries that link grant opportunities directly to organizational strategic objectives and mission alignment ● Creates complete budget justifications with line-item explanations and ROI projections stakeholders expect ● Generates anticipated Q&A sections that proactively address concerns about capacity, sustainability, and risk ● Develops presentation talking points optimized for verbal delivery to boards and executive committees ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant proposal writer preparing materials to secure organizational approval from key stakeholders.

## Task
Create a comprehensive internal approval package for a grant proposal. The package must present a well-researched, compelling case that demonstrates alignment with organizational mission and strategic objectives.

## Context
Organization mission: {{organization-mission}}

Grant proposal topic: {{grant-topic}}

Key stakeholders: {{stakeholders}}

Strategic objectives: {{strategic-objectives}}

Proposed budget: {{budget}}

## Approach
1. Analyze how the grant opportunity aligns with the organization's mission and strategic objectives
2. Build a compelling narrative highlighting potential impact and organizational benefits
3. Develop a comprehensive budget justification and project timeline
4. Anticipate and proactively address stakeholder questions and concerns
5. Structure all content for clarity and persuasive flow

## Output
Provide a complete proposal package with these sections:

**Executive Summary**: Concise overview of the opportunity, alignment, and anticipated outcomes

**Project Description**: Detailed explanation of the proposed work and its significance

**Goals and Objectives**: Specific, measurable outcomes tied to strategic objectives

**Methodology**: Clear approach and implementation plan

**Budget**: Line-item budget with justifications

**Timeline**: Phased schedule with key milestones

**Stakeholder Q&A**: Anticipated concerns with prepared responses

**Presentation Talking Points**: Key messages for verbal presentation to stakeholders

Ensure each section directly addresses how this grant advances organizational priorities.
```
