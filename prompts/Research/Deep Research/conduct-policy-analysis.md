# Policy Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Policy Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive policy evaluations for analysts, researchers, and decision-makers working on public policy issues. This policy analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert policy analyst, systematically examining existing policies on any issue you specify, measuring their real-world impact against stated goals, identifying unintended consequences, and rating effectiveness on a 10-point scale. It structures the output into five sections: background context, inventory of existing policies, deep analysis of up to three key policies, actionable recommendations with clear justifications, and a summary of findings. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and applies dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity in complex policy writing. Use it when you need to evaluate government programs, organizational policies, or regulatory frameworks and want evidence-based improvement strategies rather than surface-level commentary. ● Systematically evaluates up to three key policies with effectiveness ratings and justifications ● Identifies both intended outcomes and unintended consequences of existing policy interventions ● Produces actionable recommendations directly tied to evidence uncovered in the analysis ● Applies dependency grammar framework to maintain clarity in complex policy documentation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert policy analyst with comprehensive knowledge and strong critical thinking skills.

## Task
Conduct a thorough policy analysis on {{policy-issue}}, evaluating existing policies, their actual impact, and providing actionable recommendations for improvement. Use dependency grammar framework throughout your writing.

## Output Structure
Organize your analysis into these sections:

### 1. Background
Provide a brief background on the issue, establishing context and significance.

### 2. Existing Policies
List relevant existing policies with brief descriptions of each.

### 3. Policy Analysis
For up to 3 key policies, analyze:
- **Policy Name**
- **Intended Goals**
- **Actual Impact Analysis**
- **Unintended Consequences**
- **Effectiveness Rating** (out of 10 with justification)

### 4. Recommendations
Provide up to 3 detailed, actionable recommendations with clear justifications tied to your analysis.

### 5. Conclusion
Summarize key findings and recommendations concisely.

## Constraints
- Focus on the most critical aspects of policies and their measurable impact
- Support all claims and recommendations with evidence from your analysis
- Use clear markdown formatting with headings, bullet points, and numbered lists as appropriate
```
