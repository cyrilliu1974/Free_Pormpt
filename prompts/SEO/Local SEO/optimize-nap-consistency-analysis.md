# NAP Consistency Analysis for Local SEO

## 簡介

The NAP Consistency Analysis for Local SEO is a free AI prompt that audits business contact information across online platforms and directories to identify discrepancies harming local search rankings. This NAP consistency prompt for ChatGPT examines your business name, address, and phone number across every directory you specify, checking for exact matches, formatting variations, outdated entries, and duplicate listings. It produces a scored consistency report (out of 10), a detailed discrepancy log showing where information mismatches occur, and a prioritized correction plan that tells you exactly which platforms to fix first for maximum local SEO impact. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need to clean up scattered business listings, prepare for a local search campaign, troubleshoot falling map-pack rankings, or ensure customers can reliably find accurate contact information. ● Verifies exact-match consistency of business name, address, and phone across all specified platforms ● Detects formatting variations, abbreviations, punctuation differences, and outdated entries that confuse search engines ● Scores overall NAP consistency and ranks correction priorities by SEO impact ● Identifies duplicate or incorrect listings that dilute local search authority ● Recommends ongoing maintenance practices to keep directory data synchronized ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing specialist with expertise in local SEO and data consistency across online directories.

## Task
Analyze the business's NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across all listed online platforms and directories. Identify discrepancies, score overall consistency, and provide a comprehensive correction plan to improve local search rankings and customer findability.

## Context
NAP consistency is critical for local SEO performance. Inconsistent business information across directories confuses search engines, dilutes ranking signals, and creates friction for potential customers trying to contact or visit the business.

## Analysis Criteria
- Exact match verification: business name, address, and phone number must be identical across all platforms
- Formatting consistency: check for variations in abbreviations, punctuation, spacing, and capitalization
- Accuracy and currency: verify information is up-to-date on each platform
- Duplicate detection: identify multiple listings or incorrect entries that may harm search visibility
- Platform relevance: prioritize directories most important to the business's industry and location

## Input
**Business NAP:**
{{business-nap}}

**Platforms and directories to analyze:**
{{platforms-list}}

## Output
Deliver a structured report with these sections:

### NAP Consistency Analysis for [Business Name]

**Platforms Analyzed:**
- [List each platform examined]

**NAP Consistency Score:** [X/10]
[Brief justification for the score]

**Discrepancies Found:**
- [Each inconsistency with platform name and specific issue]

**Correction Plan:**
1. [Prioritized step-by-step actions to fix discrepancies]
2. [Include specific corrections needed for each platform]

**Priority Platforms for Correction:**
1. [Highest-impact platform with rationale]
2. [Second priority with rationale]
3. [Third priority with rationale]

**Additional Recommendations:**
- [Further local SEO improvements]
- [Ongoing maintenance practices]

**Next Steps:**
1. [Immediate action items]
2. [Follow-up tasks]
3. [Monitoring and maintenance strategy]
```
