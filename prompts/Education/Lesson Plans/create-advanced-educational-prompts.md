# Exit Ticket Question Generator for Teachers

## 簡介

The Exit Ticket Question Generator for Teachers is a free AI prompt that creates structured, multi-day exit ticket assessment questions for educators teaching any subject and unit. This exit ticket prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed table spanning at least five days with 10+ questions in varied formats - multiple choice, short answer, true/false, diagram labeling, and more - each mapped to specific learning outcomes and tagged by difficulty level. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver assessment questions that target key concepts, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. Teachers use it to plan formative assessments across entire units, ensure coverage of learning objectives, and differentiate for diverse student abilities. Reach for this prompt when you need ready-to-use exit tickets that span an instructional unit and align tightly with standards-based outcomes. ● Generates at least 10 exit ticket questions spread across 5+ days, covering an entire unit systematically. ● Outputs a structured table showing day number, question type, difficulty level, the question itself, and the expected learning outcome. ● Includes diverse question formats (multiple choice, short answer, true/false, diagram labeling) to match different assessment needs. ● Ensures questions require application, critical thinking, and problem-solving, not just recall. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert curriculum designer and assessment specialist.

## Task
Create a series of exit ticket questions to assess student understanding across a unit. Design questions that span multiple days, vary in type and difficulty, and align with specific learning outcomes.

## Context
Subject and unit:
{{subject-and-unit}}

Exit tickets are brief end-of-lesson assessments that help teachers gauge student comprehension and identify areas needing reinforcement. Effective exit tickets cover key concepts, promote critical thinking, and cater to diverse learning needs.

## Output
Provide a table with these columns:
- **Day**: Day number (span at least 5 days)
- **Question Type**: Format (multiple choice, short answer, true/false, diagram labeling, etc.)
- **Difficulty Level**: Easy, medium, or hard
- **Question**: The actual exit ticket question
- **Expected Learning Outcome**: Specific skill or concept being assessed

Include a minimum of 10 questions total. Ensure:
- Coverage of the unit's key concepts, skills, and learning objectives
- Varied question types and difficulty levels throughout
- Questions that require critical thinking, problem-solving, and knowledge application
- Each question clearly targets a measurable learning outcome

If citing external sources, use this format:
[Source Number]: [Author Last Name], [Author First Initial]. "[Article Title]." [Publication Name], [Publisher], [Publication Date], [URL]. Accessed [Date].
```
