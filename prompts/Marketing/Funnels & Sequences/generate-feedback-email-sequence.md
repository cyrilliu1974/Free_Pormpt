# Feedback Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Feedback Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that writes persuasive, multi-touchpoint feedback request campaigns for ecommerce brands, SaaS companies, and service businesses. This feedback email sequence prompt for ChatGPT uses behavioral psychology to craft three escalating messages - an initial ask, a follow-up reminder, and a final-chance email - each with subject lines, body copy, and signatures that reflect your brand voice. You input your brand tone, product or service details, and the incentive you're offering (discount, gift card, or entry into a draw), and the prompt delivers a complete drip sequence ready to deploy in your email platform. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams using different text models. Use it after purchase, post-service delivery, or at the end of a customer journey to gather testimonials, reviews, or product insights. ● Produces three complete emails with subject lines, body copy, and signatures in your specified brand voice ● Escalates urgency across the sequence using behavioral psychology principles ● Integrates your chosen incentive naturally into each touchpoint ● Formats output ready to paste into email platforms or marketing automation tools ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write persuasive feedback-request email sequences that match a brand's voice and use behavioral psychology to drive high response rates.

## Context

**Brand voice and style:** {{brand-voice}}
**Product or service:** {{product-or-service}}
**Incentive offered:** {{incentive}}

## Task

Write a sequence of three emails requesting customer feedback on a recent purchase or experience. Each email escalates urgency while staying on-brand and authentic.

**Email 1 – Initial Ask**
- Subject: attention-grabbing
- Open with a brief, personalized reminder of the customer's purchase or experience
- Make a specific feedback request paired with the incentive
- Close with appreciation and a branded signature

**Email 2 – Follow-Up (sent 3-4 days later)**
- Subject: urgency-forward
- Open with a friendly acknowledgment that they haven't responded yet
- Remind them why their feedback matters to the company
- Offer an easy response method (survey link or reply) with the time-sensitive incentive
- Close with gratitude and a company representative signature

**Email 3 – Last Chance (sent 3-4 days after Email 2)**
- Subject: final-chance framing
- Emphasize the importance of their opinion and that this is the final request
- Provide a direct feedback link with the expiring reward
- Close warmly with an appreciative signature

## Output

Deliver all three emails in sequence, each with subject line, body copy, and signature. Use the brand voice consistently across all three touchpoints.
```
