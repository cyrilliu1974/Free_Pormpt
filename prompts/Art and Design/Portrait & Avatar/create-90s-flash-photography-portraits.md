# 90s Flash Photography Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The 90s Flash Photography Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic direct-flash portraits styled after 80s–90s documentary photography for content creators, designers, and nostalgic brand campaigns. This 90s flash photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces photorealistic portraits of subjects seated on retro couches in densely packed gaming rooms, holding controllers under bold on-camera flash lighting. The prompt specifies warm film tones, high contrast with subtle grain, and technical camera settings (35mm equivalent, f/2.0, ISO 400–800) that replicate the look of vintage film stock. Use it to generate nostalgic visuals for social media, album art, editorial features, or retro gaming campaigns that require period-accurate aesthetics without sourcing vintage equipment or elaborate set builds.
