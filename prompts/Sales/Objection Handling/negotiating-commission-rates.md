# Affiliate Commission Rate Negotiation Planner

## 簡介

The Affiliate Commission Rate Negotiation Planner is a free AI prompt that builds data-driven negotiation strategies and commission proposals for affiliate marketing partnerships. This affiliate commission rate negotiation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current rates against industry benchmarks, evaluates partner performance metrics like traffic quality and conversion rates, and generates a markdown table with tailored rate proposals, negotiation strategies, and justifications for each partner. You provide your business context, the table columns you need, and your list of affiliate partners; the prompt produces a structured plan that balances profitability targets with competitive market rates. Marketing managers and affiliate program coordinators use it to prepare evidence-based negotiation positions before partner discussions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Analyzes current commission rates against industry benchmarks and individual partner performance metrics ● Develops partner-specific negotiation strategies that account for traffic quality, conversion rates, and strategic value ● Proposes new commission rates with data-backed justifications that balance competitiveness and profitability ● Outputs a customizable markdown table that documents terms, strategies, and notes for each affiliate relationship ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert negotiator specializing in affiliate partnership commission structures.

## Task
Negotiate commission rates with affiliate partners to maximize campaign profitability while maintaining partner relationships. Deliver your analysis and recommendations in a structured negotiation table.

## Process
1. Analyze current commission rates against industry benchmarks and partner performance data
2. Develop a tailored negotiation strategy for each partner based on their traffic quality, conversion rates, and strategic value
3. Propose new commission rates that balance market competitiveness with profitability targets
4. Prepare justifications for each proposed rate grounded in data and mutual benefit
5. Document all terms in the output table

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide a markdown table with these columns: {{table-columns}}

Include negotiation strategies, rate justifications, and any relevant notes for each partner listed in {{affiliate-partners}}.
```
