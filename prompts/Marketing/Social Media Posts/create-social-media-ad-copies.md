# Social Media Ad Copy Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Ad Copy Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused advertisement copy tailored to specific products, audiences, and platform requirements. This social media ad copy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through audience analysis, benefit messaging, and platform-native formatting to produce deployment-ready campaign assets. The prompt instructs AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to identify audience pain points, extract product differentiators, craft attention-grabbing headlines with emotional triggers, and develop body copy that blends relatable openings with benefit-driven messaging and social proof. Real use cases include launching product campaigns on Instagram, crafting Facebook carousel ads for e-commerce, writing LinkedIn sponsored content for B2B services, and developing TikTok ad scripts that match platform tone and character limits. Marketing teams, social media managers, and small business owners building paid campaigns across multiple platforms will find this prompt particularly valuable when time and budget constraints demand efficient, effective ad creation without sacrificing strategic messaging. ● Analyzes target audience interests and pain points to shape resonant messaging ● Produces complete ad packages including headline, body copy, CTA, and visual concepts ● Adapts tone, format, and length to match platform specifications and best practices ● Incorporates social proof elements, trending references, and credibility markers naturally ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing expert specializing in platform-native ad copy that drives engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write compelling ad copy for {{product}} targeting {{audience}}. The copy must resonate with the audience's interests and pain points, incorporate platform-specific best practices, and merge strategic messaging with storytelling that emphasizes benefits and unique selling points.

## Context
**Product details:** {{product}}
**Target audience:** {{audience}}
**Platform and constraints:** {{platform-specs}}

## Process
1. Identify the core interests, needs, and pain points of the audience that this product addresses
2. Extract the product's unique selling points and competitive differentiators
3. Craft an attention-grabbing headline using emotive triggers, common needs, or trending topics
4. Develop the ad body:
   - Open with a relatable statement or question that draws the audience in
   - Highlight 2-3 key benefits directly tied to audience needs
   - Incorporate social proof, testimonials, or credibility markers
   - Weave in relevant hashtags, cultural references, or platform trends where appropriate
5. Close with a clear, action-oriented CTA (Shop Now, Learn More, Join Us, etc.)
6. Suggest complementary visual elements (images, video concepts, graphics) that align with brand aesthetic and amplify the message

## Output
Deliver:
- Complete ad copy optimized for the specified platform and character/word limits
- Clear CTA
- 2-3 visual content suggestions that enhance the copy

Format the ad copy ready to deploy, with visuals described separately.
```
