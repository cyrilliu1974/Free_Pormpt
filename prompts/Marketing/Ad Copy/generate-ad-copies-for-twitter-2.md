# Twitter Ad Copy Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Twitter Ad Copy Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates five ready-to-post Twitter ads tailored to your product, audience, and brand voice. This Twitter ad copy prompt for ChatGPT produces complete, character-compliant tweets that each include a persuasive hook, your unique selling proposition, a clear call-to-action, and 1-2 relevant hashtags. It works by accepting your product details, landing page URL, target audience, USP, and brand voice, then structuring each ad so every element flows logically toward the conversion goal. Marketers use it to quickly generate multiple ad variations for A/B testing, social media managers rely on it to maintain brand consistency across campaigns, and small business owners turn to it when they need professional ad copy without hiring an agency. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a batch of Twitter ads fast, want to test different messaging angles, or need to maintain a consistent brand voice across multiple product launches. ● Delivers five distinct ad variations in one prompt run, perfect for split testing ● Enforces Twitter's 280-character limit while fitting hook, USP, CTA, and hashtags ● Structures each ad with logical flow from attention to action ● Adapts tone and language to match your specified brand voice and target audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media copywriter specializing in Twitter advertising.

## Task
Create 5 distinct Twitter ad copies that promote the product or service, drive traffic to the landing page, and align with the brand voice. Each ad must:
- Stay within Twitter's 280-character limit
- Include a clear call-to-action
- Incorporate 1-2 relevant hashtags
- Highlight the unique selling proposition
- Use persuasive, attention-grabbing language tailored to the target audience

Structure each ad so that every element—hook, benefit statement, USP, and CTA—flows logically and reinforces the conversion goal.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Landing Page URL:** {{landing-page-url}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}
**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 5 Twitter ad copies. Each entry should be a complete, ready-to-post tweet that includes the call-to-action and hashtags.
```
