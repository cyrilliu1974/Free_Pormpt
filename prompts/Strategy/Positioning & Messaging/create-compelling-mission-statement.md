# Mission Statement Generator for Business Positioning

## 簡介

The Mission Statement Generator for Business Positioning is a free AI prompt that distills business identity into compelling 1–2 sentence mission statements aligned with your values and goals. This mission statement prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a structured process: identifying core purpose and unique positioning, weaving in values authentically, drafting a concise 15–30 word statement, and refining for clarity and differentiation. It works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce jargon-free, memorable language that resonates across stakeholder groups. Use it when you need to articulate what your business does, who it serves, and why it exists - whether you're launching a startup, rebranding, or aligning your team around a shared purpose. ● Produces mission statements in the ideal 15–30 word range for clarity and memorability ● Avoids listing values or goals; instead weaves them naturally into narrative prose ● Focuses on differentiation within your industry, not generic platitudes ● Outputs a clean, formatted statement ready to publish or present to stakeholders ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a mission statement architect specializing in distilling business identity into concise, compelling statements that align strategy with stakeholder commitments.

## Task

Craft a powerful mission statement for the business described below. The statement should:

- Capture the business essence, purpose, and stakeholder commitments in 1–2 sentences
- Reflect the stated values and goals authentically
- Be memorable and differentiating within the industry
- Use clear, jargon-free language accessible to all audiences

## Context

{{business-profile}}

## Process

1. Identify the core purpose and unique positioning from the business profile
2. Weave in the values and goals naturally without listing them
3. Draft a concise statement (typically 15–30 words)
4. Refine for clarity, impact, and differentiation

## Output

**[Business Name]**

**Mission Statement:**  
[Your crafted mission statement]
```
