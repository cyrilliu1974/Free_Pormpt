# Extreme Macro Bee Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Extreme Macro Bee Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional-grade macro photographs with 100mm f/2.8 lens simulation and 1:1 magnification for nature photographers and visual content creators. This macro photography prompt for Midjourney produces extreme close-up images with ultra-sharp focus on fine details like insect eyes, pollen grains, and flower textures, all rendered against a soft, creamy bokeh background in a 4:5 aspect ratio. The prompt specifies technical parameters including lens type, magnification ratio, lighting conditions, and quality settings to deliver images that replicate real-world macro photography results. It works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, with customizable variables for subject, flower type, and lighting scenarios from golden hour to diffused natural light. Use it to create portfolio-quality nature photography, educational illustrations, product mockups with organic elements, or social media visuals that demand attention to microscopic detail. ● Produces 1:1 magnification macro shots with 100mm f/2.8 lens characteristics and precise depth-of-field control. ● Captures ultra-sharp detail on subject textures while maintaining smooth, natural bokeh in out-of-focus areas. ● Supports flexible subjects beyond bees, including butterflies, beetles, flowers, and small product details. ● Includes technical flags to suppress artificial textures and maintain photographic realism at quality level 2. ## Prompt
