# Book Introduction Writer Prompt for Authors

## 簡介

The Book Introduction Writer Prompt for Authors is a free AI prompt that creates captivating, publication-ready book introductions tailored to your topic, audience, and writing style. This book introduction prompt for ChatGPT produces 3-5 polished paragraphs (250-400 words) structured around four essential goals: opening with an attention-grabbing hook, introducing the book's central theme and its current relevance, connecting to the reader's life and concerns, and previewing the journey ahead without spoiling key insights. The prompt instructs ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to apply dependency grammar principles - building sentences around clear main verbs with logical modifiers - to create rhythm and clarity that propels readers forward. Authors use it to craft first-page content that sets tone, manages expectations, and converts browsers into committed readers. This prompt is for fiction and non-fiction authors, self-publishers, writing coaches, and ghostwriters who need a professional introduction that establishes immediate connection with a specific audience. ● Takes three variables - book topic and theme, target audience, and desired writing style - to generate contextually appropriate openings ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure every sentence has clear structure and forward momentum ● Balances the four core functions of effective introductions: hook, theme introduction, relevance establishment, and journey preview ● Outputs polished, professional prose ready for manuscript inclusion or client delivery ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert author and writing coach specializing in book introductions that hook readers from the first sentence.

## Task
Write a captivating book introduction that accomplishes four goals:
- Open with a compelling hook that immediately grabs attention
- Introduce the book's central theme and why it matters now
- Establish relevance to the reader's life or concerns
- Preview the journey or insights ahead without giving everything away

## Context
Book topic and theme: {{book-topic-and-theme}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Desired writing style: {{writing-style}}

## Output
Deliver the introduction as polished, publication-ready paragraphs (3-5 paragraphs, approximately 250-400 words). Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles—build each sentence around a clear main verb with modifiers and clauses that flow logically from it. This creates rhythm and clarity that keeps readers moving forward.
```
