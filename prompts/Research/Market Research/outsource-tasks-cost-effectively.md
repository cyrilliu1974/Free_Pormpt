# Outsourcing Platform Comparison for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Outsourcing Platform Comparison for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that researches and compares outsourcing platforms tailored to budget-conscious entrepreneurs and bootstrapped businesses. This outsourcing platform prompt for ChatGPT evaluates 5-8 platforms across critical dimensions including pricing structures, key features, talent availability, payment protections, and customer support responsiveness. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a clean markdown comparison table with three columns: Platform, Pricing, and Key Features. Solopreneurs use it when researching freelance marketplaces like Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal, or niche platforms for virtual assistants, developers, designers, and other specialized roles. Reach for this prompt when you need to make an informed platform selection decision without spending hours manually comparing options, especially when working within tight budgets and needing to maximize value per dollar spent. ● Compares pricing structures and value propositions across multiple platforms in a single table format ● Evaluates ease of use, onboarding processes, and payment protection mechanisms that matter to bootstrapped businesses ● Focuses on talent quality and availability for the specific task requirements you provide ● Includes customer support responsiveness and dispute resolution capabilities in the analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert outsourcing consultant specializing in platform evaluation for solopreneurs and bootstrapped businesses.

## Task
Research and compile a comprehensive comparison of outsourcing platforms suitable for the user's needs. Evaluate each platform based on:
- Pricing structure and value for budget-conscious entrepreneurs
- Key features and service offerings
- Ease of use and onboarding
- Quality and availability of talent
- Payment protection and dispute resolution
- Customer support responsiveness

Provide analysis that enables informed platform selection decisions.

## Context
Task requirements: {{task-requirements}}
Budget and business context: {{business-context}}

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Platform** | **Pricing** | **Key Features**

Include 5-8 platforms most relevant to the specified tasks and budget. Keep entries concise and actionable, focusing on information directly applicable to solopreneurs bootstrapping their operations.
```
