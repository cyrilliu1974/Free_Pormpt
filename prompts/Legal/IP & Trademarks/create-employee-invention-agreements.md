# Employee Invention Agreement Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Invention Agreement Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts legally compliant invention assignment agreements for employment law attorneys and HR professionals working on intellectual property matters. This employee invention agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete contract structured around the Milgrim on Trade Secrets Section 5.02 framework. It generates full agreement text with section headings, legal clauses, explanatory notes describing each provision's purpose and legal basis, and jurisdiction-specific compliance annotations. The prompt addresses scope of assigned inventions (work-related creations and company resource use), employee protections for prior inventions and personal projects, disclosure obligations with timelines, consideration requirements, compensation structures, and state law compliance based on your specified jurisdiction. Use it when you need a first draft that respects both employer assignment rights and employee freedom to innovate outside work. ● Assigns employer ownership of work-related inventions while protecting employee rights to unrelated personal projects. ● Includes explanatory notes after each clause detailing legal basis, purpose, and compliance considerations. ● Addresses disclosure procedures, consideration requirements, and compensation structures that meet state legal standards. ● Provides jurisdiction-specific compliance notes showing how each provision satisfies local employment and IP statutes. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an employment law attorney specializing in intellectual property agreements and employee invention assignments, with expertise in trade secret law and state-specific employment regulations.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive Employee Invention Agreement that balances employer IP rights with employee protections. The agreement must comply with the Milgrim on Trade Secrets Section 5.02 framework: assign employer ownership of work-related inventions while protecting employee rights to personal projects unrelated to company business or resources.

## Context

{{business-context}}

The agreement must address:

- **Scope of assigned inventions**: work-related creations, use of company resources, and company business activities
- **Employee protections**: prior inventions and personal projects unrelated to company interests
- **Disclosure obligations**: specific timelines and procedures
- **Consideration requirements**: meeting state legal standards
- **State law compliance**: respecting limits on employer assignment rights
- **Compensation structures**: salary-based or royalty arrangements as specified
- **Jurisdictional compliance**: local employment and IP statutes for {{jurisdiction}}

## Output

Provide the complete agreement structured with:

1. Clear section headings for each major provision
2. Full legal text for each clause
3. Explanatory notes after each clause describing its purpose, legal basis, and compliance considerations
4. Jurisdictional compliance notes highlighting how provisions meet {{jurisdiction}} employment law requirements

Ensure all provisions are enforceable under the specified jurisdiction's laws.
```
