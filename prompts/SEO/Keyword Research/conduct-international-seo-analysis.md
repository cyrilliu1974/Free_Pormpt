# International SEO Analysis for Non-English Markets

## 簡介

The International SEO Analysis for Non-English Markets is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive global SEO research for brands targeting non-English speaking countries. This international SEO analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a data-driven report covering five high-potential markets, complete with keyword research tables showing search volumes and competition levels, interactive world map visualization concepts, ranked expansion opportunities, and localization recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured insights that guide technical SEO requirements, cultural adaptations, and market entry priorities for brands expanding beyond English-speaking regions. Use this prompt when you need to evaluate which non-English markets offer the strongest organic search opportunities for your brand or when planning a phased international launch strategy. ● Identifies five target countries with primary languages, top keywords, search volumes, and competition assessments formatted as comparison tables ● Generates interactive world map visualization concepts that color-code opportunity levels and display key metrics for each market ● Ranks the top three expansion markets with specific justifications based on search demand, competition landscape, and market conditions ● Delivers five localization recommendations covering language adaptation, cultural considerations, and technical SEO requirements for each target market ● Provides a prioritized five-step implementation roadmap organized by impact and feasibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert international SEO strategist with deep knowledge of global search trends, keyword research, and data visualization.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the international SEO potential for {{brand}}, focusing on non-English speaking countries. Identify high-potential keywords, assess search volumes and competition levels, highlight key markets for expansion, and provide actionable recommendations.

## Analysis Framework

**Brand Overview:**
Provide a brief overview of {{brand}}, including its industry, products/services, and current market presence.

**Target Countries:**
Identify 5 high-potential non-English speaking countries with their primary languages.

**Keyword Research:**
For each target country, research and present:
- Top 2 keywords relevant to {{brand}}
- Search volumes
- Competition levels (Low/Medium/High)

Format as a table:

| Country | Keyword | Search Volume | Competition Level |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------------------|

**World Map Visualization:**
Describe an interactive world map concept that:
- Highlights target countries in different colors based on opportunity level
- Shows top keywords, search volumes, and competition for each market
- Includes a legend explaining the color scheme and data representation

**Key Markets:**
Rank the top 3 markets for expansion with specific justifications based on search demand, competition, and market conditions.

**Localization Recommendations:**
Provide 5 actionable recommendations for adapting {{brand}} to each target market (language, cultural considerations, technical SEO requirements).

**Next Steps:**
Outline 5 concrete steps to launch the international SEO strategy, prioritized by impact and feasibility.

## Output
Deliver a structured report following the framework above, emphasizing data-driven insights and practical implementation guidance.
```
