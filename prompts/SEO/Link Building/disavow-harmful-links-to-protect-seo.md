# Disavow Toxic Backlinks Step-by-Step SEO Guide

## 簡介

The Disavow Toxic Backlinks Step-by-Step SEO Guide is a free AI prompt that walks SEO specialists through the complete process of identifying and disavowing harmful backlinks to protect search rankings. This backlink disavowal prompt for ChatGPT produces a six-stage numbered guide covering backlink profile export, toxic link identification criteria, quality assessment frameworks, disavow file creation, Google Search Console submission, and ongoing monitoring. You supply your website URL, the backlink analysis tool you use, and your industry context; the prompt returns actionable instructions with specific red flags, risk-level prioritization (high/medium/low toxicity), and decision logic for borderline cases. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, following current Google guidelines and SEO best practices. Reach for this prompt when you need to clean up a penalized or at-risk link profile, onboard a new client site with unknown backlink history, or establish a repeatable audit process for your agency. ● Produces six clearly labeled stages from export through ongoing monitoring, so nothing is missed ● Includes toxicity scoring criteria and explains the rationale for disavowing versus keeping borderline links ● Provides Google Search Console submission instructions aligned with current guidelines ● Adapts output to your chosen backlink analysis platform and business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist focused on backlink auditing and disavowal to protect site authority and search rankings.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide to analyze backlink profiles, identify toxic links, create a disavow file, and submit it through Google Search Console. Follow current Google guidelines and SEO best practices.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Backlink analysis tool: {{analysis-tool}}
Industry and business context: {{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list with clear headings for each main step:

1. **Backlink Profile Export & Initial Analysis**
2. **Toxic Link Identification Criteria**
3. **Link Quality Assessment**
4. **Disavow File Creation**
5. **Google Search Console Submission**
6. **Monitoring & Follow-up**

For each step, include specific actions, tools/features to use, red flags to watch for, and decision criteria. Prioritize links by risk level (high/medium/low toxicity) and explain the rationale behind disavowal vs. keeping borderline links.
```
