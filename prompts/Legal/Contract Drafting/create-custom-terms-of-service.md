# Terms of Service Generator for Business Websites

## 簡介

The Terms of Service Generator for Business Websites is a free AI prompt that produces customized, legally structured Terms of Service documents for businesses of any size or industry. This terms of service prompt for ChatGPT takes your business details - company name, jurisdiction, products or services, pricing model, user account structure, data practices, and compliance requirements - and drafts a complete legal document covering acceptance of terms, user obligations, payment terms, intellectual property, disclaimers, limitation of liability, dispute resolution, and governing law. After delivering the draft, it generates three targeted follow-up questions to help you refine sections specific to your operations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, giving you flexibility across text-generation models. Use it when launching a new service, updating existing terms, or ensuring your legal framework reflects your current business model and jurisdiction. ● Generates a full-length Terms of Service draft customized to your company name, location, services, and compliance obligations. ● Automatically adapts legal language to reflect your jurisdiction's requirements and governing law. ● Includes all standard sections required for e-commerce, SaaS, membership sites, and digital services. ● Provides three intelligent follow-up questions to capture missing details and refine the document further. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a business lawyer drafting a comprehensive, legally sound Terms of Service document.

## Context

**Business details:** {{business-details}}

The business details should include: company name, location/jurisdiction, products or services offered, pricing model, user account structure (if applicable), data collection practices, and any industry-specific compliance requirements.

Use these details to customize every section, including jurisdiction-specific legal requirements, the nature of services or products, and industry-specific obligations.

## Task

1. Draft a complete Terms of Service document tailored to the business above. The document must:
   - Be fully customized to the business's products, services, and location
   - Cover all standard sections: acceptance of terms, user obligations, payment terms, intellectual property, disclaimers, limitation of liability, dispute resolution, and governing law
   - Reflect the correct governing jurisdiction based on the provided location

2. After the draft, write three targeted follow-up questions to gather additional information that would refine the document further.

## Output

**Draft Terms of Service:**
[Full draft document]

**Follow-up Questions:**
1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]
3. [Question 3]
```
