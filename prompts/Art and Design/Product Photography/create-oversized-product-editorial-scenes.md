# Oversized Product Fashion Editorial Generator

## 簡介

The Oversized Product Fashion Editorial Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms uploaded product photos into surreal, ultra-photoreal fashion campaign imagery where models carry the item oversized in open landscapes. The prompt produces vertical 4:5 editorial photographs with eye-level framing, positioning your product - unchanged in shape, color, texture, or label - dramatically scaled across a model's shoulders or upper back. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana gives you control over landscape type, outfit style, and scene mood while maintaining natural lighting, realistic weight balance, and shadow casting that integrates the oversized object into the outdoor environment. Photographers, e-commerce brands, creative directors, and product marketers reach for this prompt when they need striking campaign visuals that blend commercial product integrity with high-concept editorial storytelling. ● Preserves uploaded product exactly as provided - no modification to shape, color, label, proportion, or texture, only scaled larger ● Places the oversized item naturally on a model in an open landscape with realistic shadows, weight distribution, and matched lighting ● Vertical 4:5 composition optimized for editorial and social media, with customizable landscape type, outfit style, and atmospheric mood ● High-detail material rendering and fashion editorial aesthetic that avoids CGI look, text overlays, or artificial branding additions ## Prompt

```
Ultra-photoreal fashion editorial photograph, vertical 4:5 composition, eye-level camera angle.

A model stands centered in an open {{landscape-type}}, leaning slightly forward as if carrying weight. The uploaded product—preserved exactly with original shape, colors, label, proportions, and texture—rests oversized across the model's upper back or shoulders for dramatic campaign effect. Model wears {{outfit-style}} with dynamic editorial expression.

Natural ground surface consistent with the landscape type. Open sky and {{scene-mood}} atmosphere. Soft directional natural daylight, medium contrast, natural color temperature matching the mood.

Product integration: realistic weight balance, natural shadows cast by the oversized object, lighting perfectly matched to the outdoor scene. No modification to the uploaded product whatsoever—it appears exactly as provided, only scaled larger.

High-detail material rendering, fashion editorial aesthetic, no CGI look. Negative space in upper frame, clean lower composition. No text, graphics, logos, watermarks, or added branding.

Avoid: text overlay, poster typography, generated or placeholder objects, distorted or modified product, altered labels, extra branding, cartoon style, AI artifacts.
```
