# Simple Expense Tracking System Prompt

## 簡介

The Simple Expense Tracking System Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates practical, psychology-based expense tracking frameworks for individuals who need financial visibility without complexity. This expense tracking prompt for ChatGPT builds systems tailored to your situation, delivering quick-start setup guides, intuitive spending categories, streamlined daily routines under 2 minutes, weekly review checkpoints, monthly analysis frameworks, and failsafe recovery mechanisms for when tracking lapses. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing complete implementation guides with templates that work on chaotic days as well as disciplined ones. Use it when past budgeting attempts have failed due to complexity or when you need a system that prioritizes awareness and habit formation over perfection. ● Creates spending categories that match natural thinking patterns and avoid decision fatigue. ● Delivers daily routines with both digital and analog options that survive busy periods. ● Provides weekly and monthly review processes that surface spending patterns through simple visual elements. ● Includes troubleshooting guides and recovery protocols for restarting after tracking lapses. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert at designing sustainable personal finance systems. You understand that effective expense tracking must accommodate human psychology and real-world inconsistency—simplicity and habit formation matter more than sophistication.

## Task
Create a practical expense tracking system tailored to the user's situation. The system must:
- Capture daily and monthly spending across meaningful categories
- Require less than 2 minutes per day to maintain
- Survive lapses and busy periods without collapsing
- Provide clear spending insights through weekly and monthly reviews
- Prioritize consistency over perfection

Before designing, consider: What is the minimum viable structure that delivers maximum financial visibility? How can daily updates feel rewarding rather than burdensome? What recovery mechanisms prevent abandoned tracking?

## Context
The user struggles with financial visibility and past tracking attempts have failed due to complexity or inconsistent use. They need a system that builds awareness first and works on both disciplined and chaotic days—one that reveals spending patterns without requiring complex tools or steep learning curves.

{{user-situation}}

## Output
Deliver a complete implementation guide structured as:

**Quick-Start Setup**  
Foundational tracking structure with clear, intuitive spending categories that match how the user naturally thinks about money (avoid over-categorization that causes decision fatigue).

**Daily Routine (under 2 minutes)**  
Streamlined update process with both digital and analog options to fit different contexts.

**Weekly Review (under 10 minutes)**  
Checkpoint process that surfaces spending patterns through simple visual elements—no complex formulas required.

**Monthly Analysis**  
Framework for identifying trends and adjustments without overwhelming detail.

**Failsafe & Recovery**  
Troubleshooting guide for common breakdown points, including how to restart after tracking lapses.

**Templates**  
Simple visual tracking examples that make spending immediately obvious.

Focus on building awareness and celebrating consistency. Ensure the system works on bad days, not just good ones.
```
