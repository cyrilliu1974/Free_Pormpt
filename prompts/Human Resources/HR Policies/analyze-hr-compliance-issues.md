# HR Compliance Issues Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The HR Compliance Issues Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies and ranks the top 10 regulatory compliance priorities HR teams must monitor, tailored to a specific organization's profile. This HR compliance issues prompt for ChatGPT analyzes federal, state, and local regulations, recent legislative changes, and enforcement trends to surface the compliance areas where organizations face the greatest risk. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a numbered list of compliance issues with regulatory context, potential penalties, legal exposure, and specific monitoring actions HR should implement. Use it when planning annual compliance audits, responding to regulatory updates, onboarding HR staff, or preparing for leadership reviews of risk exposure. ● Ranks compliance issues by enforcement probability, financial penalty risk, and organizational impact. ● Covers federal, state, and local employment law, workplace rights legislation, and emerging regulations. ● Provides regulatory context including recent changes, agency guidance, and case law developments. ● Delivers specific monitoring recommendations and actions HR teams can implement immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR compliance strategist and employment law specialist with deep knowledge of regulatory changes across industries.

## Task
Identify and analyze the 10 most critical regulatory compliance issues HR teams must monitor in 2025. Prioritize based on enforcement likelihood, penalty severity, and organizational impact.

## Context
Regulatory landscapes shift rapidly, enforcement priorities evolve, and non-compliance results in significant financial penalties and reputational damage. Consider federal, state, and local regulations, emerging legislation, updated enforcement guidelines, evolving workplace rights, and compliance areas where organizations are most vulnerable.

Organization profile:
{{org-profile}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list (1–10) with:
- **Clear heading** stating the compliance issue
- **Regulatory context**: relevant laws, recent changes, enforcement trends
- **Compliance risks**: potential penalties, legal exposure, operational impact
- **Monitoring recommendations**: specific actions HR teams should implement to maintain compliance

Format each entry with the heading followed by structured bullet points for the three components above.
```
