# Social Media Copywriting Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Copywriting Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that produces customized social media checklists for business owners and managers working across any niche or platform. Given your business name, niche, topic, and target audience, this social media copywriting prompt for ChatGPT builds a four-part checklist covering content creation, engagement, platform optimization, and performance tracking. Each section includes at least three best practices and three mistakes to avoid, all tailored to your specific inputs. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, returning a structured text checklist you can copy, print, or share with your team. It is designed for business owners, social media managers, and marketing coordinators who need repeatable quality control across campaigns and posts. ● Produces separate checklists for content creation, engagement, platform optimization, and performance tracking, each with tailored best practices and pitfalls. ● Adapts every recommendation to your business name, niche, topic, and target audience so guidance stays relevant and actionable. ● Returns output formatted with checkmarks and cross marks for quick scanning and team handoffs. ● Works across industries and platforms, from e-commerce Instagram strategies to B2B LinkedIn campaigns. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write social media checklists for business owners and managers.

## Task

Produce a comprehensive social media checklist tailored to the business context below. Cover four areas: content creation, engagement, platform optimization, and performance tracking. Each area must include at least three actionable best practices and three common mistakes to avoid, all customized to the specific niche, topic, and target audience.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

**Business Overview**
- Business Name: [extract from context]
- Niche: [extract from context]
- Topic: [extract from context]
- Target Audience: [extract from context]

**Content Creation Checklist**
✅ Post consistently during peak audience hours
✅ Use platform-native formats (Reels, Stories, threads)
✅ Include clear calls-to-action aligned with business goals
❌ Over-promoting products without providing value
❌ Using generic stock images that don't reflect brand identity
❌ Ignoring accessibility features (alt text, captions)

**Engagement Checklist**
✅ Respond to comments and DMs within 2 hours
✅ Ask questions and create polls to spark conversation
✅ Tag and collaborate with complementary accounts
❌ Deleting negative feedback instead of addressing it professionally
❌ Using bots or engagement pods for artificial metrics
❌ Only engaging when promoting something

**Platform Optimization Checklist**
✅ Complete all profile fields with relevant keywords
✅ Use strategic hashtags (mix of broad, niche, and branded)
✅ Cross-promote content across platforms with platform-specific formatting
❌ Using the same caption and format on every platform
❌ Neglecting profile link strategy (bio links, link-in-bio tools)
❌ Ignoring platform algorithm changes and best practices

**Performance Tracking Checklist**
✅ Monitor reach, engagement rate, and conversion metrics weekly
✅ A/B test posting times, formats, and content themes
✅ Track which content drives website traffic and leads
❌ Focusing only on vanity metrics (follower count, likes)
❌ Not connecting social performance to business revenue
❌ Failing to adjust strategy based on data insights
```
