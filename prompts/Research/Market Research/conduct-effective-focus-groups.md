# Focus Group Facilitation Guide for Product Research

## 簡介

The Focus Group Facilitation Guide for Product Research is a free AI prompt that creates structured facilitation plans for gathering customer insights on products and services. This focus group prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete markdown guide covering participant recruitment, session structure, moderation techniques, and analysis methods. When you input your product or service, it generates a customized roadmap that defines objectives, target participants, ideal group sizes, session timelines, screening criteria, discussion phases with durations, and data collection approaches. Teams use it to prepare user research sessions for software launches, consumer goods testing, service design validation, and iterative product improvements. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to design a focus group from scratch or standardize your qualitative research process across multiple studies. ● Defines objectives, participant profiles, group size, and session duration tailored to your product or service ● Outlines recruitment screening criteria, channels, and incentive recommendations ● Provides timed session structure with introduction, warm-up, main discussion, and wrap-up phases ● Includes moderator guidelines and methods for data collection, analysis, prioritization, and stakeholder communication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert focus group facilitator specializing in gathering comprehensive feedback to guide iterative improvements.

## Task
Compose a detailed guide for conducting effective focus groups to elicit actionable insights on the given product or service. Structure the guide with logical flow and clear dependencies between phases.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-or-service}}

## Output
Provide a comprehensive focus group guide in markdown with these sections:

### 1. Focus Group Overview
- Objectives
- Target participants
- Ideal group size
- Session duration

### 2. Planning and Preparation
- Step-by-step instructions

### 3. Participant Recruitment
- Screening criteria
- Recruitment channels
- Incentives

### 4. Session Structure
- Introduction (duration and details)
- Warm-up (duration and details)
- Main discussion (duration and details)
- Wrap-up (duration and details)

### 5. Moderator Guidelines
- Key guidelines for effective moderation

### 6. Data Collection and Analysis
- Methods with descriptions

### 7. Acting on Insights
- Summarize key findings
- Prioritize areas for improvement
- Develop action plan
- Communicate results to stakeholders

Ensure each section provides clear, actionable steps. Avoid unnecessary jargon. Focus on eliciting valuable insights to guide improvements.
```
