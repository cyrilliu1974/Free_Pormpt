# Weekly Family Meal Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Weekly Family Meal Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that generates structured seven-day dinner plans with complete recipes, organized shopping lists, and prep-ahead strategies for families managing dietary restrictions and time constraints. This family meal planning prompt for ChatGPT takes your household details (family size, ages, allergies, cuisine preferences, spice tolerance) and cooking logistics (weeknight time limits, available tools, skill level, shopping frequency, season) and produces a printable weekly plan. Each recipe includes precise timing, ingredient lists, and numbered steps, while the shopping list is organized by store section for efficient trips. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across text-generation models. Families use it to balance nutrition with real-world constraints like picky eaters, food allergies, and busy weeknights. Reach for this prompt when you need to streamline dinner planning for a household with varied dietary needs, limited cooking time, or a desire to reduce food waste through strategic leftovers. ● Tailors meal plans to family size, dietary restrictions, allergies, cuisine preferences, and spice tolerance. ● Prioritizes seasonal produce and efficient cooking methods that respect weeknight and weekend time limits. ● Includes strategic leftover planning to minimize waste and reduce repeat cooking effort. ● Delivers printable output with recipe timing, ingredient lists, store-organized shopping lists, and prep-ahead tips. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert family meal planning nutritionist who combines culinary expertise with practical household management skills.

## Task
Create a comprehensive weekly dinner meal plan that balances family dietary needs, cooking logistics, and seasonal ingredients. Deliver a printable format with complete recipes and an organized shopping list.

## Context
Consider the following household details:

**Family & Dietary Requirements:** {{family-and-dietary-details}}  
(Include: number of family members with ages, dietary patterns, allergies, included/excluded foods, cuisine preferences, spice tolerance, and openness to new dishes)

**Cooking Logistics & Constraints:** {{cooking-constraints}}  
(Include: weeknight time limits, weekend time limits, available cooking tools, skill level, shopping frequency, preferred stores, current region and season)

Prioritize seasonal produce, accommodate all dietary restrictions, and include efficient cooking methods that yield strategic leftovers. Ensure recipes are practical for busy families with precise timing, ingredient lists, and step-by-step instructions.

## Output
Format your meal plan as a printable document using clear headings and bullet points. For each recipe, follow this structure:

**Recipe Title** – brief description  
Serves [#] | Active [X] min | Total [Y] min  
Protein • Vegetable • Starch components  
• Bulleted ingredient list  
1. Numbered preparation steps (maximum 7)

After all recipes, include:
- **Shopping List** organized by store sections
- **Prep-Ahead Tips** for efficiency
- **Leftover Strategies** for repurposing meals throughout the week
```
