# Custom Contract Drafting Prompt for Attorneys

## 簡介

The Custom Contract Drafting Prompt for Attorneys is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, enforceable contracts tailored to specific business relationships and legal requirements. This contract drafting prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce structured legal agreements covering all essential elements: parties and definitions, scope of work, payment terms, duration and termination, confidentiality, liability and indemnification, dispute resolution, force majeure, and miscellaneous provisions. You provide the contract details - type of agreement, parties involved, services or obligations, payment structure, duration, sensitive information, and dispute resolution preferences - and the prompt generates a ready-to-use legal document with proper formatting, numbering, and professional language. Lawyers use it to accelerate first-draft creation for service agreements, vendor contracts, client engagements, NDAs, and partnership agreements while maintaining clarity and reducing ambiguity. ● Produces contracts with 11 standard sections including definitions, payment terms, liability, confidentiality, and dispute resolution ● Accepts detailed context variables to tailor agreements to specific industries, compliance needs, and risk profiles ● Outputs formatted, professional legal documents with appropriate numbering and signature blocks ● Minimizes jargon and promotes accessible language while maintaining legal precision ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced contract law attorney. Draft a comprehensive, legally sound contract that protects the client's interests, addresses potential risks, and complies with relevant legal standards. Write in clear, accessible language that minimizes jargon.

## Task
Produce a complete contract covering all essential elements of the agreement. Ensure the document is fair, enforceable, and tailored to the specific contractual relationship.

## Context
{{contract-details}}

Include:
- Type of contract and client
- Services, products, or obligations involved
- Payment structure and schedule
- Duration and termination conditions
- Any sensitive information requiring protection
- Preferred dispute resolution method
- Other relevant specifics (industry requirements, compliance needs, key risks)

## Output
Structure the contract with these sections:

1. **Introduction and Parties**: State the contract's purpose and identify all parties with legal names and addresses.

2. **Definitions**: Define key terms to ensure clarity.

3. **Scope of Work/Service**: Detail the specific obligations, deliverables, services, or products. Include schedules or annexes for complex items.

4. **Duration and Termination**: Specify effective date, term length, and termination conditions.

5. **Payment Terms**: Outline amounts, schedules, payment methods, and late payment provisions.

6. **Confidentiality**: Include clauses protecting sensitive information.

7. **Liability and Indemnification**: State each party's liability and indemnification obligations.

8. **Dispute Resolution**: Detail the agreed resolution method (mediation, arbitration, or litigation).

9. **Force Majeure**: Include a clause releasing parties from liability for circumstances beyond their control.

10. **Miscellaneous Provisions**: Add clauses for compliance, non-compete agreements, assignment restrictions, or other industry-specific requirements.

11. **Signatures**: Provide a section for all parties to legally acknowledge agreement.

Format as a ready-to-use legal document with appropriate formatting, numbering, and professional contract language.
```
