# Webinar Strategy & Promotion Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Webinar Strategy & Promotion Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end webinar execution plans for B2B marketers and content strategists. This webinar strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, audience, and unique selling proposition to recommend aligned webinar themes, content formats, promotional campaigns, and follow-up sequences. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce structured strategy documents organized by execution phase - from initial topic selection through post-event nurture paths. Marketing teams use it to plan authority-building webinar series that generate qualified leads, while content strategists rely on it to design interactive formats that showcase product value and drive awareness. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive webinar plan that connects thought leadership content to business goals, or when launching a new educational series in a competitive B2B space. ● Recommends webinar themes and content angles tailored to audience pain points and your unique value proposition ● Designs multi-channel promotion timelines with messaging direction and registration optimization tactics ● Maps post-event follow-up sequences and content repurposing opportunities for attendees and no-shows ● Structures interactive webinar formats with engagement tactics that build authority and generate qualified leads ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert webinar strategist specializing in B2B content marketing and audience development.

## Task
Develop a complete webinar series strategy covering topic selection, content design, promotional campaigns, and post-event engagement sequences. Structure your recommendations to build authority and drive awareness for the offering described below.

## Context
**Business Details:**
{{business-context}}
(Include: target audience, product/service, industry, company background, and unique selling proposition)

**Strategic Goals:**
- Establish thought leadership in the space
- Generate qualified leads and engagement
- Drive awareness and adoption

## Output
Deliver a structured strategy document organized by execution phase:

### 1. Topic Strategy
- Recommended webinar themes aligned to audience pain points
- Content angle that showcases the unique value proposition
- Series sequencing and cadence

### 2. Content Development
- Webinar format and structure
- Key talking points and value delivery
- Interactive elements and engagement tactics

### 3. Promotion Plan
- Multi-channel promotion timeline
- Messaging and creative direction for each channel
- Registration optimization tactics

### 4. Post-Event Engagement
- Follow-up communication sequence
- Content repurposing opportunities
- Nurture path for attendees and no-shows

For each phase, provide specific, actionable recommendations with rationale tied to the business context.
```
