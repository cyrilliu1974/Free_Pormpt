# Curriculum Development Scope Planning Prompt

## 簡介

The Curriculum Development Scope Planning Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces structured curriculum plans with objectives, resources, and timelines for educators and instructional designers. This curriculum planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your subject matter and educational level to generate a markdown table mapping specific learning objectives to required resources and implementation timelines. It incorporates pedagogical best practices, learning theories, and institutional constraints to produce actionable development plans. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, the prompt helps course creators and curriculum coordinators translate broad educational goals into granular, sequenced implementation roadmaps with measurable outcomes and resource requirements. Reach for this prompt when designing new courses, revising existing curricula, or aligning educational programs with institutional standards and available resources. ● Produces a three-column markdown table organizing learning objectives, required materials and personnel, and phased timelines in a single view. ● Aligns curriculum objectives with pedagogical theory and assessment strategies appropriate to the educational level. ● Accounts for institutional constraints and resource availability to generate realistic implementation schedules. ● Structures plans to support iterative review cycles and curriculum adjustments based on feedback and outcomes. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert curriculum developer specializing in scope planning and instructional design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive curriculum development plan structured as a three-column table with Objectives, Resources, and Timeline.

## Context
Subject and scope: {{subject-and-education-level}}

Institutional environment: {{institution-context}}

Analyze the subject matter and educational level to determine learning objectives aligned with pedagogical best practices and learning theories. Identify resources needed to support each objective. Establish a realistic timeline for development and implementation that accounts for the available time frame and institutional constraints. Incorporate assessment strategies where relevant.

## Output
Deliver your plan as a markdown table with exactly three columns:

| Objectives | Resources | Timeline |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| [learning objectives] | [required materials, tools, personnel] | [development and implementation phases] |

Ensure objectives are specific and measurable, resources are actionable and obtainable, and the timeline accounts for iterative review and adjustment.
```
