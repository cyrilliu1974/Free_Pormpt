The Luxury Car Advertising Poster Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic automotive marketing visuals for brands, designers, and creative professionals. This luxury car poster prompt for Midjourney produces hero-angle compositions with vehicles displayed on dramatic structural platforms rising above modern cityscapes. The prompt combines golden-hour lighting, ultra-realistic photography style, and dynamic reflections across body panels to create aspirational advertising imagery. You specify the vehicle details (brand, model, color) and provide your tagline and typography style; the output delivers a 2:3 portrait poster with balanced brand placement in the upper third, ready for print or digital campaigns.
