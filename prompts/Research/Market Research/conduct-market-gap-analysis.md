# Market Gap Analysis Guide for Business Analysts

## 簡介

The Market Gap Analysis Guide for Business Analysts is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured, eight-stage methodology for uncovering unmet customer needs and strategic opportunities in any target market. This market gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks business analysts through a complete framework: defining target markets and buyer personas, assessing current offerings and competitor landscapes, gathering customer insights through surveys and interviews, mapping customer journeys to pinpoint friction points, categorizing and prioritizing gaps by impact and feasibility, developing solution criteria, validating findings with real customers, and creating actionable implementation plans. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing best-practice guidance and key considerations for each analysis stage rather than one-size-fits-all recommendations. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable process for turning customer pain points into product roadmap priorities or market entry strategies. ● Breaks gap analysis into eight logical stages, from market segmentation through validation and action planning ● Emphasizes customer journey mapping to identify friction points at every touchpoint ● Includes prioritization frameworks based on impact, feasibility, cost, and ROI ● Focuses on teaching methodology and best practices rather than delivering pre-built solutions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst providing a step-by-step guide to conducting a gap analysis—identifying unmet needs and opportunities in a target market.

## Task
Deliver a comprehensive, actionable guide structured around these eight stages:

**Introduction**
• Brief overview of gap analysis and its strategic importance

**Step 1: Define the Target Market**
• Identify key characteristics and segmentation criteria
• Create buyer personas

**Step 2: Assess Current Offerings**
• Research existing products and services
• Analyze competitor strengths and weaknesses

**Step 3: Gather Customer Insights**
• Conduct surveys, interviews, and feedback analysis
• Identify pain points and unmet needs

**Step 4: Map the Customer Journey**
• Break down journey stages and touchpoints
• Pinpoint gaps and opportunities at each interaction

**Step 5: Analyze the Gaps**
• Categorize gaps (product, service, experience)
• Prioritize by impact and feasibility
• Determine root causes

**Step 6: Develop Solutions**
• Brainstorm and evaluate potential solutions
• Apply criteria: cost, time, resources, ROI
• Select the most promising options

**Step 7: Validate and Refine**
• Test solutions with target customers
• Gather feedback and iterate

**Step 8: Create an Action Plan**
• Define action items, timelines, and responsibilities
• Establish metrics and KPIs for tracking progress

**Conclusion**
• Recap and emphasize ongoing gap analysis for continuous improvement

## Context
Target market: {{target-market}}

## Output
Provide best practices and key considerations for each step. Focus on methodology—do not create specific solutions or action plans for the user's market; those will come from their own analysis. Use clear headings and bullet points; avoid XML tags.
```
