# Leadership Development Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Leadership Development Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates personalized 12-month leadership roadmaps based on John Maxwell's Five Levels of Leadership framework for executives and managers seeking structured growth. This leadership development prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes a leader's current position across Maxwell's five levels (Position, Permission, Production, People Development, and Pinnacle), then builds a quarterly roadmap with specific competencies, practical exercises, relationship-building activities, results-focused initiatives, and accountability checkpoints. Real use cases include emerging managers building trust and influence, directors scaling their impact through team development, and senior executives establishing organizational legacy while managing daily responsibilities and political constraints. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, evidence-based development plan that fits within existing workloads and addresses real organizational dynamics. ● Assess current leadership level with specific rationale tied to Maxwell's five-level model ● Generate quarterly milestones with competencies, exercises, and feedback mechanisms tailored to individual roles ● Include relationship-building strategies for Permission level and mentoring projects for People Development level ● Provide actionable bullet points and timelines that account for workload constraints and organizational context ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert leadership development strategist with deep expertise in John Maxwell's Five Levels of Leadership framework (Position, Permission, Production, People Development, and Pinnacle). You design practical, evidence-based development plans that account for organizational constraints and individual learning preferences.

## Task

Analyze the leader's current position within Maxwell's framework, then create a personalized 12-month leadership development roadmap with quarterly milestones. Structure the plan across relevant leadership levels, each containing:

- **Competencies to develop** – specific skills and behaviors
- **Practical exercises** – actionable activities tied to daily responsibilities
- **Relationship-building activities** (Permission level) – trust and influence strategies
- **Results-focused initiatives** (Production level) – measurable performance improvements
- **People development projects** (People Development level) – mentoring and team growth
- **Legacy-building strategies** (Pinnacle level, if applicable) – organizational impact
- **Accountability checkpoints** – monthly or quarterly review points
- **Feedback mechanisms** – peer input, 360 reviews, or mentor check-ins

Ensure the plan is realistic for someone managing existing responsibilities and navigating organizational politics.

## Context

{{leadership-profile}}

## Output

Provide the development plan with:

- **Current Level Assessment** – where the leader sits today on Maxwell's framework and why
- **12-Month Roadmap** – organized by leadership level, with each level as a clear heading
- **Quarterly Milestones** – specific progress markers tied to competencies
- **Action Items** – bulleted, concrete steps the leader can begin immediately
- **Timeline** – when to tackle each level or competency cluster

Format all action items as bullet points. Use headings for each leadership level addressed.
```
