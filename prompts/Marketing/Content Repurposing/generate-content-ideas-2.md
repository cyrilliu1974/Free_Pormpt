# Marketing Campaign Content Idea Generator

## 簡介

The Marketing Campaign Content Idea Generator is a free AI prompt that produces diverse, strategic content ideas for digital marketing campaigns aligned with specific business objectives. This marketing campaign content idea generator prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and delivers a structured markdown table of at least 10 distinct content concepts. Each row specifies the content format (blog post, video, infographic, social media post, email campaign, webinar, case study, etc.), the target audience segment, and a core message that resonates with business goals. It works by examining your business model, identifying customer pain points, and considering current industry trends to ensure every idea is engaging, actionable, and differentiated. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok when planning a new marketing campaign, brainstorming content strategies, or diversifying your content portfolio across multiple channels. ● Produces at least 10 unique content ideas in a clean three-column markdown table format ● Mixes content types - blog posts, videos, infographics, social posts, email sequences, webinars, case studies - for a diversified campaign ● Tailors every idea to your business context, audience segments, and strategic goals ● Identifies customer pain points and current industry trends to keep ideas relevant and timely ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in campaign content ideation.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of at least 10 unique content ideas for a marketing campaign. Each idea must specify the content type, target audience segment, and core message that aligns with business objectives.

## Context
Business and goals:
{{business-context}}

Analyze the business model, identify relevant audience segments, and consider current industry trends, best practices, and customer pain points. Ensure each content idea is engaging, actionable, and distinct from the others. Mix content formats (blog posts, videos, infographics, social media content, email campaigns, webinars, case studies, etc.) to create a diverse campaign portfolio.

## Output
Provide your response as a markdown table with exactly three columns:

| Content Type | Target Audience | Key Message |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------|
| [format] | [segment] | [core message] |

Include at least 10 rows, each representing a unique content idea tailored to the business context provided.
```
