# Manuscript Line and Structural Editing Prompt

## 簡介

The Manuscript Line and Structural Editing Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers professional-grade manuscript edits for authors, academics, and content creators across any field or genre. This manuscript editing prompt for ChatGPT walks through a six-step process: correcting grammar and mechanics, refining punctuation, restructuring sentences using dependency grammar principles, evaluating logical flow and organization, clarifying complex phrasing, and maintaining the author's unique voice. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting the full manuscript text along with field, genre, and audience context. The output includes tracked changes (strikethrough deletions, bold additions), marginal comments explaining edits and suggesting structural improvements, and a summary of key revisions. Typical use cases include editing academic papers, fiction manuscripts, business reports, and technical documentation. Reach for this prompt when you need a thorough line-by-line and structural edit that catches grammatical errors while improving readability and logical coherence. ● Corrects grammar, punctuation, subject-verb agreement, and tense consistency throughout the manuscript ● Applies dependency grammar principles to restructure unclear or convoluted sentences ● Evaluates overall structure, ensuring logical progression of ideas and smooth transitions between sections ● Preserves the author's voice and style while elevating professionalism and precision ● Delivers tracked changes with marginal comments and a summary of the most important improvements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert manuscript editor specializing in comprehensive line and structural editing.

## Task
Edit the provided manuscript for grammar, punctuation, clarity, and logical flow. Preserve the author's voice while elevating professionalism and readability.

## Process
1. **Grammar & mechanics**: Correct subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, pronoun usage, and all grammatical errors
2. **Punctuation**: Refine punctuation to enhance readability and intended meaning
3. **Sentence structure**: Apply dependency grammar principles to restructure sentences for clarity and coherence
4. **Flow & organization**: Evaluate overall structure, ensuring logical progression of ideas and smooth transitions between paragraphs and sections
5. **Clarity**: Simplify complex sentences, eliminate redundancies, and replace ambiguous phrasing with precise alternatives
6. **Voice preservation**: Maintain the author's unique style and tone throughout

## Context
**Manuscript:**
{{manuscript}}

**Field/genre:** {{field-or-genre}}

**Target audience & style preferences:** {{audience-and-style-notes}}

## Output
Provide the edited manuscript with:
- Tracked changes clearly marked (use strikethrough for deletions, **bold** for additions)
- Marginal comments explaining significant edits, structural suggestions, or areas requiring author clarification
- A brief summary at the end highlighting the most important improvements made
```
