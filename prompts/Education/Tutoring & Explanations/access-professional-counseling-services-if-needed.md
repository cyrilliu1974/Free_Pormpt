# Burnout Assessment and Counseling Resource Guide

## 簡介

The Burnout Assessment and Counseling Resource Guide is a free AI prompt that helps individuals recognize burnout symptoms and find appropriate professional mental health resources. This burnout assessment prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a specific life context (work, relationships, caregiving, academics) to identify relevant physical, emotional, and behavioral symptoms of burnout, then generates a customized list of counseling resources matched to location, preferred therapy modality, insurance coverage, and budget. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by taking two inputs: the affected life area and circumstances, plus counseling preferences and practical constraints. Real-world applications include helping burned-out professionals find local therapists who accept their insurance, guiding students to campus counseling services, or connecting caregivers with specialized support groups. Reach for this prompt when you need both symptom validation and actionable pathways to professional support, organized around real-world accessibility factors. ● Surfaces physical, emotional, and behavioral burnout indicators relevant to the specific life domain affected ● Matches counseling resource types to stated preferences (individual therapy, group support, online platforms, crisis services) ● Incorporates practical constraints like geographic location, insurance networks, and budget limitations ● Provides actionable steps for accessing each recommended resource, reducing the barrier between recognition and help-seeking ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a mental health expert providing guidance on burnout recognition and professional support options.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive overview of burnout symptoms and professional counseling resources. Identify key symptoms that may manifest across different life domains, then compile counseling resources tailored to the affected area, location, and practical constraints.

## Context
**Affected area and circumstances:**
{{burnout-context}}

**Counseling preferences and constraints:**
{{counseling-requirements}}

## Output
Deliver your response in two distinct sections using bullet points:

**Section 1: Key Burnout Symptoms**
- List symptoms relevant to the affected life area
- Include physical, emotional, and behavioral indicators

**Section 2: Counseling Resources**
- Recommend specific resource types matching the stated preferences
- Include actionable steps for accessing each resource
- Consider location, counseling approach, insurance, and budget constraints
```
