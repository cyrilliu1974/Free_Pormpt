# Keyword Research Prompt for SEO and Digital Marketing

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Prompt for SEO and Digital Marketing is a free AI prompt that identifies high-value search terms and delivers actionable SEO recommendations for digital marketers and business owners. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business niche, target audience, and competitive landscape to produce a ranked table of the top 10 keywords with search volume and difficulty metrics. It evaluates each term for search demand, ranking difficulty, and commercial intent, then provides strategic analysis explaining why these keywords offer the best visibility and conversion opportunities. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying industry-standard SEO methodologies to balance opportunity against competition and identify gaps where competitors are weak. Reach for this prompt when planning content strategy, launching new campaigns, or auditing your current SEO performance to discover untapped keyword opportunities aligned with your business goals. ● Delivers a markdown table ranking keywords by search volume and difficulty for easy prioritization ● Evaluates commercial intent and conversion potential, not just traffic metrics ● Identifies competitive gaps where your business can rank more easily ● Provides strategic analysis explaining how keywords align with your audience and offerings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in keyword research and SEO strategy.

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to identify high-value search terms for the business described below. Deliver actionable recommendations based on search volume, ranking difficulty, and conversion potential.

## Context
Business and market details:
{{business-context}}

Analyze the business's niche, target audience, and competitive landscape. Use industry-standard methodologies to evaluate keyword opportunity: balance search demand against ranking difficulty, prioritize terms with strong commercial intent, and identify gaps where competitors are weak.

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Keyword**, **Search Volume**, and **Keyword Difficulty**.

Include the top 10 keywords, then provide a brief strategic analysis explaining:
- Why these keywords offer the best visibility and conversion opportunity
- How they align with the business's offerings and audience
- Any patterns or insights from the competitive keyword landscape
```
