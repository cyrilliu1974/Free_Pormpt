# Brand Story Generator

## 簡介

The Brand Story Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms brand background and audience information into a complete, emotionally resonant narrative for founders, marketers, and brand strategists. This brand story prompt for ChatGPT structures your input into nine cohesive sections: brand overview, target audience description, origin and milestones, mission and values, unique selling proposition, emotional hook, brand personality, the full narrative synthesis, and a closing call to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, grounding every claim in the details you provide while deriving differentiation and positioning from your core facts. Use it when launching a new brand, refining positioning for a rebrand, or building a foundation for marketing collateral. ● Outputs nine structured sections that flow into a single cohesive story ● Infers unique selling proposition and call to action when not explicitly provided, with reasoning shown ● Uses plain language designed to be understood, remembered, and shared ● Grounds every element in your brand information to maintain authenticity ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write brand narratives that are authentic, emotionally resonant, and clearly differentiated from competitors.

## Context

**Brand:** {{brand-information}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Task

Build a complete brand story using the structure below. Each section should flow into the next so the final narrative reads as a cohesive whole.

Guidelines:
- Use plain language accessible to a general audience
- Ground every claim in the brand details provided; do not invent facts or names
- Derive the unique selling proposition and call to action from the brand details; if neither is stated explicitly, infer the strongest candidates and note your reasoning
- Make the story easy to understand, remember, and share

## Output

**Brand Overview**
[Concise summary of what the brand is and does]

**Target Audience**
[Description of who the brand serves and their key characteristics]

**Brand History**
[Origin story and key milestones that shaped the brand]

**Mission and Values**
[Core purpose and guiding principles]

**Unique Selling Proposition**
[What sets this brand apart from competitors]

**Emotional Hook**
[The feeling or aspiration the brand evokes]

**Brand Personality**
[How the brand would express itself if it were a person]

**Brand Story**
[Cohesive narrative weaving together all elements above]

**Call to Action**
[Concrete next step inviting audience engagement]
```
