# Corporate Policy Drafting Mistakes Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Corporate Policy Drafting Mistakes Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies critical governance and compliance vulnerabilities in internal company policies for risk managers, legal teams, and corporate compliance officers. This corporate policy analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines seven frequent drafting errors that appear deceptively simple yet cascade into legal exposure, operational disruption, and regulatory penalties. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it delivers structured breakdowns of each mistake: what specifically goes wrong in the drafting process, why the error occurs repeatedly across organizations, the downstream consequences including compliance failures and employee disputes, and concrete prevention strategies. Real-world examples and warning signs help teams recognize when they are making these errors before they escalate into costly organizational crises. Reach for this prompt when reviewing existing policy frameworks, drafting new governance documents, conducting compliance audits, or investigating the root causes of operational dysfunction traced back to poorly written internal rules. ● Examines both surface-level drafting errors and the organizational blind spots that allow them to persist. ● Details real-world consequences across legal liability, regulatory penalties, employee disputes, and operational chaos. ● Provides specific warning signs that indicate a company is currently making each mistake. ● Delivers prevention strategies and correction methods tailored to each type of drafting failure. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a corporate policy consultant and organizational risk management specialist with expertise in internal governance frameworks, compliance vulnerabilities, and operational risk mitigation.

## Task

Identify and analyze 7 common mistakes companies make when drafting internal policies. For each mistake, examine:

- **The drafting error itself** – what specifically goes wrong
- **Why it occurs frequently** – root causes and organizational blind spots
- **Real-world consequences** – legal exposure, operational disruption, compliance failures, employee disputes, and regulatory penalties
- **Prevention strategies** – how to avoid and correct the mistake

Focus on errors that appear simple but create complex downstream effects. Include specific examples of how each mistake manifests in practice and warning signs that indicate a company is making these errors.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Poorly drafted policies create legal vulnerabilities, operational chaos, and cultural dysfunction. Minor drafting oversights cascade into major organizational crises costing millions in penalties and disputes.

## Output

Structure your response with clear headings for each of the 7 mistakes, followed by organized bullet points covering error description, root causes, consequences, and prevention strategies for maximum clarity and actionability.
```
