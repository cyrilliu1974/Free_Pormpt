# Mascot Logo Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Mascot Logo Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates clean, flat vector mascot logos for brands, teams, and businesses. It produces simple mascot characters in a flat vector style with a custom color scheme you define, explicitly removing shading, detail, realistic texture, and photographic effects to ensure a clean logo aesthetic. This mascot logo prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana is ideal for creating brand marks, sports team logos, app icons, and product mascots that need to scale across print and digital media without losing clarity. Reach for this prompt when you need a mascot logo that reproduces cleanly at any size, from business cards to billboards. ● Outputs flat vector mascot logos with simple shapes and solid color fills. ● Accepts any character concept (animal, human, object, abstract) and any color palette (hex codes, color names, or descriptive schemes). ● Enforces a clean logo style by explicitly excluding shading, texture, realism, and photographic rendering. ● Produces mascots suitable for print reproduction, embroidery, vinyl cutting, and digital branding at any resolution. ## Prompt

```
a mascot logo of a {{character}}, simple flat vector style, {{color-palette}} color scheme --no shading detail realistic texture
```
