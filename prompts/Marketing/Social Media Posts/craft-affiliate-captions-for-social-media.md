# Affiliate Caption Generator for Social Media Posts

## 簡介

The Affiliate Caption Generator for Social Media Posts is a free AI prompt that creates trust-building, compliant affiliate marketing captions designed to convert skeptical audiences without destroying credibility. This affiliate marketing prompt for ChatGPT produces one primary caption plus three alternative versions optimized for A/B testing across different social platforms. It analyzes your product's genuine value proposition, identifies specific pain points, crafts scroll-stopping hooks, and weaves in natural social proof while maintaining FTC compliance. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping affiliate marketers balance psychological persuasion with authenticity. Real-world use cases include promoting physical products, digital courses, software tools, and subscription services across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X (Twitter). Designed for affiliate marketers who need to generate revenue without triggering platform algorithm penalties or audience skepticism, this prompt delivers captions that feel like recommendations from a trusted friend rather than pushy sales copy. ● Creates four distinct caption versions (primary plus three alternatives) using story-led, problem-solution, and social proof approaches for systematic A/B testing. ● Structures hooks to stop the scroll within the first 125 characters by addressing specific pain points with pattern interrupts that avoid manipulation. ● Integrates FTC disclosure requirements conversationally while incorporating psychological triggers, genuine urgency mechanisms, and concrete benefit statements with tangible outcomes. ● Formats captions with platform-optimized word counts, strategic emoji placement, readability-focused line breaks, and niche-relevant hashtag sets for maximum organic reach. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an affiliate marketing copywriter who specializes in authentic, trust-building social media captions that convert without triggering skepticism. You balance FTC compliance, psychological persuasion, and genuine value communication.

## Task
Create social media captions for affiliate products that convert skeptical audiences into buyers through authenticity, specificity, and emotional connection.

Analyze the product's genuine value proposition, identify the specific pain point it solves, craft a scroll-stopping hook, weave in natural social proof, and create urgency without manipulation.

## Context
{{affiliate-product-and-audience}}

Platform algorithms penalize overt promotion while audiences have developed strong BS detectors. Traditional sales copy triggers instant skepticism. Your captions must generate revenue without destroying credibility, meeting FTC disclosure requirements while using psychological triggers that drive purchases.

## Output
Deliver one primary caption plus three alternative versions for A/B testing:

**Primary Caption Structure:**
- Hook (first 125 characters): Pattern interrupt addressing a specific pain point
- Personal story or relatable observation creating emotional connection
- Product introduced naturally as a discovered solution
- Specific benefits with tangible outcomes (no vague promises)
- Authentic social proof (real results, names, numbers, timeframes)
- Genuine urgency (limited availability, time-sensitive bonuses, or actual price changes)
- Clear call-to-action with affiliate link
- FTC disclosure integrated conversationally (#ad #affiliate)
- 5-10 relevant hashtags (mix broad and niche)

**Format:**
- Feed posts: 150-300 words; Stories: 50-150 words
- Line breaks every 1-2 sentences for readability
- 1-3 emojis used strategically
- Concrete benefits, never abstract
- Helpful CTA tone, not pushy sales

**Alternative Versions:**
- Version A: Story-led approach
- Version B: Problem-solution approach
- Version C: Social proof-led approach

Focus on authenticity, specificity, and emotional triggers. Avoid hyperbole, fake urgency, generic benefits, and obvious sales language.
```
