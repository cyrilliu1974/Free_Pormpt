# Brand Food Collage Generator for Restaurants

## 簡介

The Brand Food Collage Generator for Restaurants is a free AI prompt that creates nine-panel promotional grids showcasing menu items, customer moments, and brand identity for food businesses. It produces a structured 3×3 layout blending hero product photography, lifestyle dining scenes, branded packaging detail shots, and storefront context - all unified by your custom brand colors and visual identity. This food collage prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers commercial-quality assets in a single render, ideal for social media campaigns, digital menu boards, and restaurant marketing materials. Reach for it when you need a cohesive visual story that spans product appeal, customer experience, and brand environment in one shareable asset. ● Generates a complete 3×3 grid with hero menu items, side dishes, beverages, customer dining moments, flat-lay meal trays, ingredient cross-sections, storefront exteriors, service handoffs, and minimalist packaging shots. ● Accepts brand-name and brand-colors variables to ensure every frame - from cup logos to background tones - matches your restaurant's visual identity. ● Outputs commercial food photography style with warm lighting, appetizing composition, and a balance of product focus and lifestyle context. ● Produces social-ready collages for Instagram, Facebook ads, website hero sections, and in-store digital displays without coordinating multiple photo shoots. ## Prompt

```
Create a visually engaging 3×3 promotional collage for {{brand-name}}, showcasing iconic food items, customer experience, and brand identity.

**Grid Layout (3×3):**

**Top Row:**
- **Top Left:** Hero product shot of the signature menu item presented as the flagship offering
- **Top Center:** Signature side item in branded packaging, isolated product shot on textured surface
- **Top Right:** Branded beverage cup with straw, close-up drink shot

**Middle Row:**
- **Middle Left:** Group of diverse friends enjoying the food at a table, lifestyle scene emphasizing social dining
- **Middle Center:** Flat lay of meal tray with signature item, side, and drink on brand-colored background, stylized overhead composition
- **Middle Right:** Hands holding the signature item with visible cross-section showing key ingredients and layers, appetizing interior detail

**Bottom Row:**
- **Bottom Left:** Exterior shot of storefront or signage at sunset, brand environment
- **Bottom Center:** Service handoff moment — employee giving order to customer, conveying convenience
- **Bottom Right:** Signature item packaging and drink cup with branding on solid brand-colored background, minimalist product display

**Branding:**
- Color palette: {{brand-colors}}
- Typography: bold, clean brand-aligned sans-serif fonts
- Logo or wordmark visible in multiple frames
- Authentic branded containers, wrappers, cups, and bags throughout

**Style:**
Commercial food photography with friendly, appetizing, joyful tone. Bright, well-lit with warm tones. Balanced mix of product shots, lifestyle moments, and brand context.

**Avoid:** Low resolution, off-brand packaging, unappetizing food appearance, messy composition, incorrect logo use, unrealistic textures, cluttered backgrounds.
```
