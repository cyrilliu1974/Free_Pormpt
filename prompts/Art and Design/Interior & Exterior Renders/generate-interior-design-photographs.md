# Interior Design Photography Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Interior Design Photography Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that produces editorial-quality interior architectural photographs with natural lighting and professional composition for designers, real estate professionals, and content creators. This interior design photography prompt for Midjourney creates eye-level shots with 35mm focal length, shallow depth of field, and soft natural light filtering through windows. You define the room type, furniture materials, and flooring, and the prompt handles the technical photography parameters - producing images with clean lines, negative space, and balanced symmetry. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, and is ideal for mood boards, portfolio presentations, real estate listings, or social media content that requires a warm, inviting aesthetic. Reach for this prompt when you need professional interior shots without a physical photoshoot or when exploring design concepts before committing to materials and layout. ● Customizable room descriptions, furniture materials, and flooring types let you visualize any interior concept. ● Natural window light with sheer curtains produces soft, warm illumination and a cozy atmosphere in every render. ● Editorial photography framing with 35mm lens perspective, shallow depth of field, and balanced symmetry matches high-end design magazines. ● Fixed technical parameters (16:9 aspect ratio, quality and stylization settings) ensure consistent, professional results across all room types. ## Prompt

```
Interior architectural photograph of a {{room-description}} with {{furniture-and-materials}}, {{flooring-type}} flooring. Natural light from a large window with sheer white curtain creates soft, warm lighting for a cozy atmosphere. Clean lines, negative space, balanced symmetry—shot as editorial interior photography from eye level with a 35mm lens, shallow depth of field. --ar 16:9 --s 250 --q 2 --c 10
```
