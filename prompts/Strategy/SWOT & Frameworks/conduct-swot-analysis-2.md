# SWOT Analysis Prompt for Business Strategy

## 簡介

The SWOT Analysis Prompt for Business Strategy is a free AI prompt that evaluates internal and external factors affecting a business and delivers actionable strategic insights for decision-makers. This business SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert strategist who examines your company within its industry context, competitive landscape, and market trends. It produces a markdown table with four columns - Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats - each containing 4-6 comprehensive points tailored to the business overview you provide. The output is designed to surface critical areas for improvement, growth vectors, and competitive positioning in a format ready for strategy meetings and planning documents. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a fast, structured assessment of a business situation, whether you're advising a client, evaluating a new market entry, conducting competitive research, or preparing a strategic plan. ● Delivers 4-6 relevant points per quadrant grounded in the specific business and industry context you provide ● Considers market trends, competitive dynamics, internal capabilities, and external threats in a single structured output ● Produces markdown-formatted tables that drop directly into reports, slide decks, and planning documents ● Adapts to any industry or business stage, from startups to established enterprises ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist conducting a comprehensive SWOT analysis.

## Task
Evaluate the internal and external factors affecting the business in a structured, analytical manner. Assess strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats within the industry context, considering market trends, competitive landscape, internal capabilities, and potential challenges. Provide actionable insights to guide strategic decision-making and identify areas for improvement and growth.

## Context
{{business-overview}}

## Output
Present your SWOT analysis as a markdown table with four columns: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. Ensure each category contains 4-6 comprehensive, relevant points that directly address the business and industry context provided.
```
