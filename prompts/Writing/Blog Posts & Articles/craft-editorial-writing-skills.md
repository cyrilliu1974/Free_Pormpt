# Editorial Writing Prompt With Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The Editorial Writing Prompt With Dependency Grammar is a free AI prompt that helps writers compose persuasive, well-structured editorials on any topic with built-in linguistic analysis. This editorial writing prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to produce complete opinion pieces featuring a clear thesis, three distinct supporting arguments with evidence, counterargument rebuttals, and dependency grammar breakdowns that reveal word relationships within key sentences. Writers receive a formatted editorial ready for publication or refinement, complete with title, introduction, structured body sections, and a forceful conclusion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation platforms. This prompt is ideal for journalists, bloggers, op-ed contributors, and content creators who need to articulate nuanced positions quickly while maintaining journalistic rigor and linguistic precision. ● Produces complete editorials with thesis, three supporting points, counterargument rebuttal, and conclusion in a single generation ● Includes dependency grammar analysis for key sentences to reveal word relationships and improve writing clarity ● Structures each supporting point with explicit claim and evidence sections for logical rigor ● Anticipates opposition by requiring counterargument identification and reasoned rebuttal ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editorial writer skilled at developing clear, nuanced arguments and analyzing prose through dependency grammar to enhance coherence and flow.

## Task
Compose a well-structured editorial on {{editorial-topic}} that takes a clear stance, supports it with logical arguments and vivid evidence, and addresses potential counterarguments.

## Requirements
- Present a clear, nuanced thesis on the topic
- Develop three distinct supporting points, each with a compelling claim backed by convincing evidence or examples
- For each supporting point, include a dependency grammar analysis that breaks down a key sentence to show word relationships and enhance writing clarity
- Anticipate and rebut at least one significant counterargument with reasoned response
- Conclude with a forceful restatement of your thesis and a memorable key takeaway
- Maintain a professional, journalistic tone; be concise while fully developing arguments

## Output
Structure your editorial as follows:

**Title:** [Engaging title]

[Introductory paragraph presenting thesis]

**Supporting Point 1:**
- Claim: [Argument favoring your stance]
- Evidence: [Example or data]
- Dependency Grammar Analysis: [Sentence breakdown showing word relationships]

**Supporting Point 2:**
- Claim: [Second argument]
- Evidence: [Example or data]
- Dependency Grammar Analysis: [Sentence breakdown]

**Supporting Point 3:**
- Claim: [Third argument]
- Evidence: [Example or data]
- Dependency Grammar Analysis: [Sentence breakdown]

**Counterargument:**
- Opposing View: [Potential objection]
- Rebuttal: [Reasoned response]

**Conclusion:**
- Restated Thesis: [Forceful reassertion]
- Key Takeaway: [Memorable final thought]
```
