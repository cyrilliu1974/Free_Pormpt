# Critical Thinking Questions Generator for Education

## 簡介

The Critical Thinking Questions Generator for Education is a free AI prompt that creates 20 research-backed discussion questions for any book or topic within a specific subject area. This critical thinking questions prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your book or topic and subject area, then generating questions that span all six cognitive levels of Bloom's Taxonomy - Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. Each question is designed to engage higher-order thinking, and the prompt instructs the AI to conduct research using reliable sources and provide full citations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it suitable for educators designing curriculum materials, trainers building workshop content, or instructional designers who need pedagogically sound questions quickly. The output includes a numbered list of 20 questions, a table mapping each question to its Bloom's Taxonomy level, and a numbered list of sources. This prompt is for teachers, professors, corporate trainers, and curriculum developers who need to stimulate meaningful discussion and assess understanding across multiple cognitive levels. ● Produces 20 questions that cover all six Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive levels, ensuring balanced cognitive challenge ● Instructs the AI to research reliable sources and cite them, grounding questions in verified information ● Outputs a table that maps each question to its cognitive level, making it easy to assess coverage ● Works for any book, article, theory, or topic within any subject area, from literature to biology to business ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a subject-matter expert creating discussion questions for educational use. Draw on deep knowledge of key concepts, theories, and applications in the relevant field.

## Task

Generate 20 thought-provoking discussion questions about {{book-or-topic}} in the context of {{subject}}. Each question must engage higher-order thinking skills and cover a wide range of aspects.

## Requirements

- Categorize every question by its Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive level: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, or Creating
- Conduct research using reliable sources to inform question development
- Cite all sources used

## Output

Provide your response in this structure:

**Discussion Questions:**
1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]
...
20. [Question 20]

**Bloom's Taxonomy Categorization:**

| Cognitive Level | Question Numbers |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Remembering     | [Q1, Q5, ...]    |
| Understanding   | [Q2, Q8, ...]    |
| Applying        | [Q3, Q11, ...]   |
| Analyzing       | [Q4, Q13, ...]   |
| Evaluating      | [Q6, Q15, ...]   |
| Creating        | [Q7, Q18, ...]   |

**Sources:**
1. [Source 1]
2. [Source 2]
...
```
