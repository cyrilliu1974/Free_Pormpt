# Lead Qualification Framework Builder for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Lead Qualification Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates custom lead evaluation systems for sales teams who need to identify, score, and prioritize prospects efficiently. This lead qualification prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete framework featuring 4-6 measurable qualification criteria, a weighted 1-5 scoring system with calculation methodology, red flag identification for disqualifying poor-fit leads, and score-based interpretation guidelines that map to specific follow-up actions. Sales managers and revenue operations teams use it to standardize how their teams assess inbound leads, demo requests, and outbound prospects. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by accepting a single {{business-context}} variable describing your product, ideal customer profile, and sales motion. Reach for this prompt when your sales team needs objective criteria to replace gut-feel decisions, when lead volume exceeds capacity and you must triage effectively, or when onboarding new reps who need clear evaluation rubrics. ● Defines 4-6 specific qualification criteria with assessment questions and 1-5 scoring rubrics for each level ● Assigns relative weight factors (1-3) to each criterion so high-impact attributes drive the final score ● Identifies 3-5 red flags that immediately disqualify or deprioritize leads to prevent wasted effort ● Delivers score-range interpretation guidelines that map total weighted scores to priority levels and recommended next actions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lead qualification strategist developing frameworks that enable sales teams to evaluate and prioritize potential leads effectively.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Lead Qualification Framework tailored to the provided business context. The framework must include specific qualification criteria, a weighted scoring system, red flag identification, and interpretation guidelines for prioritizing leads.

## Context
Business context:
{{business-context}}

A robust qualification framework should feature:
- Specific, measurable criteria relevant to the sales process
- Clear assessment questions or indicators for each criterion
- Consistent scoring parameters (1-5 scale) with defined descriptions
- Weight factors (1-3) assigned by relative importance
- Red flags that disqualify or deprioritize unfit leads

## Output
Deliver the framework in this structure:

**Lead Qualification Framework**

**1. Introduction**
- Purpose and benefits of the framework
- Key components overview

**2. Qualification Criteria**
- List 4-6 criteria, each with:
  - Clear description
  - Key assessment questions/indicators

**3. Scoring System**
- Scoring Parameters:
  - Each criterion on 1-5 scale with descriptions per score level
- Weight Factors:
  - Assign 1-3 to each criterion based on importance
- Scoring Methodology:
  - Step-by-step calculation for total weighted score

**4. Red Flags**
- Identify 3-5 disqualifying or deprioritizing signals
- Explain impact and handling approach for each

**5. Interpretation Guidelines**
- Define 3-4 score ranges (e.g., 0-20, 21-40, etc.)
- Assign priority level to each range
- Recommend specific actions per priority level

**6. Conclusion**
- Summarize key framework elements
- Emphasize consistent application for effective lead management
```
