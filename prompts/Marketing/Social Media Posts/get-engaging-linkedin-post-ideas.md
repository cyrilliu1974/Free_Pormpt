# LinkedIn Post Ideas Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Post Ideas Generator for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates high-engagement LinkedIn content packages for professionals, thought leaders, and business owners. This prompt transforms your expertise and stories into scroll-stopping posts that spark conversations and build credibility. This LinkedIn content prompt for ChatGPT delivers a complete post package: hook options (question, story, or contrarian opening), a full post body optimized for mobile reading, three alternative versions tailored to different tones, strategic hashtag recommendations, and engagement tactics to maximize early comments. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, asking clarifying questions about your role, audience, goal, and message before generating content that balances personal authenticity with professional authority. Real use cases include founders building thought leadership, consultants generating leads through value-driven posts, recruiters attracting talent, and executives sharing industry insights. The prompt applies 2025 algorithm best practices - hooks within the first three lines, mobile-scannable formatting, and native LinkedIn content structure - to help posts reach beyond your immediate network. ● Produces three hook options (question, story, contrarian) with recommendations for what works best with your audience ● Generates a full post body plus three alternative versions (personal, data-driven, action-focused) for A/B testing ● Includes hashtag strategy across industry-specific, broad professional, and personal brand categories ● Provides comment triggers, quotable one-liners, success benchmarks, and early response tactics to boost algorithm visibility ## Prompt
