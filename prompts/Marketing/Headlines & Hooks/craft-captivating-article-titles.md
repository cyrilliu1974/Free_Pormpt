# Article Title Generator for High-CTR Headlines

## 簡介

The Article Title Generator for High-CTR Headlines is a free AI prompt that creates 20 unique blog article titles optimized for maximum click-through rates. This article title prompt for ChatGPT works by accepting your article details and generating a diverse set of headlines, each 40–60 characters long, styled with pattern interruption, numbers, emotional triggers, and strategic punctuation. You choose whether to prioritize SEO-first titles built around exact-match keywords for Google ranking, or word-of-mouth-first titles designed purely for curiosity and social sharing. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and returns a formatted table with every title and its character count. Content marketers, bloggers, and social media managers use it to quickly test multiple headline angles before publishing. ● Delivers 20 unique titles with varied emotional hooks, punctuation styles, and angles in a single run ● Lets you toggle between SEO-optimized titles that preserve exact keywords and curiosity-driven titles that maximize shares ● Enforces a 40–60 character length constraint to match platform best practices ● Returns results in a clean two-column table showing each title and its character count for easy comparison ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional content marketer specializing in high-CTR blog article titles.

## Task
Generate 20 different titles for a blog article. Deliver variety: different styles, hooks, and emotional angles. Each title must be 40–60 characters long.

## Context
{{article-details}}

**Title type and keyword usage:**
- **SEO-first**: Build the title around the exact keyword to rank on Google; the keyword cannot be modified or omitted.
- **Word-of-mouth-first**: Ignore keyword constraints; maximize curiosity and shareability.

Align all titles with the specified type.

## Technique
- Use pattern interruption and unconventional hooks
- Incorporate numbers, emotions, and social proof where appropriate
- Employ punctuation (brackets [], colons :, em dashes —) to capture attention
- Ensure every title is substantively different; provide real options

## Output
Return a table with two columns:

| Title | Character Count |
|-------|----------------|
| …     | …              |
```
