# Keyword Audit Strategy for SEO Optimization

## 簡介

The Keyword Audit Strategy for SEO Optimization is a free AI prompt that conducts complete keyword performance audits for websites and delivers actionable replacement recommendations to improve search visibility. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your existing keyword portfolio by evaluating current rankings, monthly search volumes, and keyword difficulty scores. It identifies underperforming terms dragging down your SEO efforts, then suggests stronger alternatives with better search volume-to-difficulty ratios. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured reports that include current performance metrics, replacement recommendations, new keyword opportunities, and page-specific content optimization guidance. SEO managers use it to refresh stale keyword strategies, content marketers deploy it before planning editorial calendars, and agency teams rely on it for client audits that need data-backed recommendations. ● Evaluates current keyword rankings with search volume and difficulty metrics for each tracked term ● Flags underperforming keywords with specific rationale explaining why they fail to meet expectations ● Proposes replacement keywords backed by competitive analysis and strategic fit for your niche ● Recommends new high-potential keyword additions to expand topical coverage and capture untapped search traffic ● Delivers page-level content optimization suggestions tied directly to target keywords ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in keyword research, analysis, and optimization to improve search engine rankings and drive targeted organic traffic.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive keyword audit for the provided website. Analyze current keyword performance, identify underperforming keywords, and recommend high-potential replacements and additions to enhance search visibility and rankings.

## Context
Website to audit: {{website-url}}

Focus on keywords that align with the website's niche and target audience. Prioritize keywords with balanced search volume and reasonable difficulty. Provide actionable insights that directly improve search performance.

## Output
Structure your audit as follows:

**Current Keyword Performance:**
🔍 For each currently tracked keyword, provide: current rank, monthly search volume, keyword difficulty

**Underperforming Keywords:**
❌ Identify keywords not meeting expectations, including: current rank, monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, and specific reason for underperformance

**Suggested Keyword Replacements:**
✅ For each underperforming keyword, propose a stronger alternative with: monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, and rationale for the suggestion

**Suggested Keyword Additions:**
➕ Recommend new high-potential keywords to target, including: monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, and strategic reason for addition

**Content Optimization Recommendations:**
List specific pages with target keywords and concrete content improvement suggestions

**Keyword Enhancement Strategy Summary:**
Provide a concise summary highlighting the most critical aspects and expected outcomes in terms of improved search rankings and targeted organic traffic growth.
```
