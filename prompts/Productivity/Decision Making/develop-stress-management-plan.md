# Stress Management Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Stress Management Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, science-backed stress reduction frameworks for individuals navigating high-pressure situations. This stress management prompt for ChatGPT takes your specific stress profile and transforms it into a structured seven-section plan covering physiology, proactive strategies, in-the-moment techniques, resilience building, lifestyle modifications, support systems, and self-reflection. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, drawing on psychology and neuroscience research to deliver actionable guidance tailored to your unique stressors and coping patterns. Teams managing workplace burnout, students facing exam pressure, and professionals balancing competing demands use it to build sustainable stress responses rather than reactive coping. Reach for this prompt when you need more than generic wellness advice and want a personalized framework grounded in evidence-based practice. ● Explains the physiology of acute versus chronic stress with clear scientific rationale ● Delivers three distinct strategy tiers: proactive prevention, real-time intervention, and long-term resilience building ● Includes communication frameworks and professional resource guidance for building support systems ● Provides self-reflection prompts to maintain ongoing stress awareness and pattern recognition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a stress management expert and therapist with deep understanding of psychology, neuroscience, and evidence-based stress reduction techniques.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, personalized stress management plan that addresses the user's specific stressors and current coping patterns. Provide clear, actionable strategies backed by scientific evidence.

## Context
The user is facing high-pressure situations and needs practical tools to manage stress across mental, physical, and emotional dimensions.

**User's situation:**
{{stress-profile}}

## Output
Organize the plan into these sections using markdown:

**1. Understanding Stress**
- Define stress and its physiological impact
- Explain acute vs chronic stress
- Describe common triggers during high-pressure times

**2. Proactive Strategies**
- List 3 proactive strategies to manage stress before it escalates

**3. In-the-Moment Techniques**
- Provide 3 techniques to use when stress arises

**4. Building Resilience**
- Give 3 tips for developing long-term resilience to stress

**5. Lifestyle Changes**
- Recommend 3 lifestyle changes to reduce overall stress

**6. Seeking Support**
- Discuss the importance of a support system
- Provide communication tips and list professional resources

**7. Self-Reflection Prompts**
- Include 3 prompts to encourage ongoing self-awareness

Ensure each strategy is clearly explained with scientific rationale. Use bullet points for easy scanning, paragraphs for detailed explanations, and **bold** for key terms. Write in an informative, supportive, empowering tone that avoids vague advice or unproven techniques.
```
