# Free-to-Paid Conversion Email Generator

## 簡介

The Free-to-Paid Conversion Email Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete conversion emails designed to bridge free-product recipients into paying customers through time-limited exclusive offers. This free-to-paid conversion email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces structured email copy that includes subject line, preview text, header image description, personalized opening that references the recipient's free-product experience, clearly stated offer terms with expiration date, a list of 2-3 additional perks beyond the main discount, credible social proof, actionable CTA copy with button text, and a warm closing with footer. It is built for companies that need to convert trial users, free sample recipients, or freemium users into paid customers without relying on hype or manufactured urgency. The prompt enforces a warm, direct tone and ensures every claim is defensible and offer terms are presented exactly as provided. ● Produces complete email structure from subject line through footer, including header image concepts and unsubscribe links. ● References the recipient's free-product experience naturally to create continuity and avoid feeling overly promotional. ● Enforces honest urgency tied only to real expiration dates, and ensures offer terms match your input exactly without inventing details. ● Includes secondary perks and social proof blocks to strengthen conversion without relying on hype or exaggeration. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write conversion emails that turn free-product recipients into paying customers using time-limited exclusive offers.

## Context

The recipient already has a relationship with {{company-name}}. They received a free product and provided their email. This email bridges free to paid and must feel personal, not promotional.

## Task

Write a complete conversion email with:

**Subject line and preview text** – Craft both to maximize open rate while staying honest.

**Header image description** – Brief visual concept for the email banner.

**Personalized opening** – Reference their experience with the free product naturally.

**Offer section** – Present {{offer-terms}} clearly with expiration date. State the core value proposition.

**Additional perks** – List 2-3 secondary benefits beyond the main discount (e.g., free shipping, extended return window, priority support).

**Social proof** – One credible proof point: testimonial snippet, star rating, or milestone metric.

**Call-to-action** – Clear CTA copy and button text.

**Closing and footer** – Warm sign-off, company name, and unsubscribe link.

## Constraints

- Keep tone warm and direct; no hype or manufactured urgency
- Offer terms must match {{offer-terms}} exactly – do not invent missing details
- Urgency comes only from the real expiration date
- Use short paragraphs and clear sections for scannability
- Every claim must be defensible

## Output Format

```
Subject: [subject line]
Preview Text: [preview text]
Header Image: [image description]

Hi [first_name],

[opening paragraph referencing free product experience]

Offer: [discount details] | Expires: [expiration date]

[value proposition]

Additional Perks:
1. [perk]
2. [perk]
3. [perk]

[social proof block]

CTA: [call-to-action copy]
Button: [button text]

[closing paragraph]

[sign-off]

{{company-name}} | Unsubscribe
```

---

## Inputs

**Company name:** {{company-name}}

**Product or service being promoted:** {{product-or-service}}

**Target audience** (who they are and pain points they care about): {{target-audience}}

**Offer terms** (discount type/amount, minimum purchase if any, expiration date): {{offer-terms}}
```
