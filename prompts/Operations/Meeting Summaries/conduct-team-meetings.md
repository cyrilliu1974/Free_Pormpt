# Team Meeting Facilitation Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Team Meeting Facilitation Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured meeting materials for managers and team leads who need to run productive collaborative sessions. This team meeting prompt for ChatGPT builds a three-part framework: a pre-meeting agenda with objectives and time allocations, an in-meeting facilitation guide with discussion prompts and participation techniques, and a post-meeting minutes template that captures decisions and action items. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and tailors every section to your specific team context, ensuring topics are sequenced logically and connected by dependencies. Real use cases include sprint planning sessions, department syncs, project kick-offs, and recurring leadership meetings where alignment and follow-through matter. Reach for this prompt when you want every meeting to have a clear purpose, encourage balanced participation, and produce documented outcomes that drive accountability. ● Produces a logically sequenced agenda with objectives, topics, expected outcomes, and time blocks ● Includes facilitation techniques and discussion prompts that encourage broad participation and capture decisions in real time ● Generates a minutes template that shows how decisions relate to discussed topics, action items with owners and deadlines, and deferred questions ● Structures all materials to highlight dependencies and logical relationships between agenda items, decisions, and next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert meeting facilitator who designs and documents effective team meetings.

## Task
Create a complete meeting framework—agenda, facilitation guidance, and minutes template—that aligns goals, shares updates, and fosters collaboration. Structure all materials to show logical relationships between topics, decisions, and action items.

## Context
{{team-and-business-context}}

Use this context to tailor the agenda topics, discussion prompts, and follow-up actions to the team's actual situation.

## Output
Deliver a three-part meeting framework:

### Pre-Meeting: Agenda
- Meeting objectives tied to the team's goals
- Topics to discuss, sequenced to build on each other logically
- Expected outcomes for each topic
- Time allocations

### During Meeting: Facilitation Guide
- Discussion prompts for each agenda item
- Techniques to encourage broad participation
- Template for capturing key decisions and action items in real time
- Notes on how each topic connects to others (dependencies, prerequisites, downstream impacts)

### Post-Meeting: Minutes Template
- Summary of decisions made
- Action items with owners and deadlines
- How decisions relate to discussed topics and next steps
- Open questions or items deferred to future meetings

Format all sections with clear headings and bullet points.
```
