# Target Market Segmentation Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Target Market Segmentation Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds actionable customer segment profiles for marketers, strategists, and analysts planning targeted campaigns. This market segmentation prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a six-step analysis process: scanning the competitive landscape, evaluating demographic factors like age and income, assessing psychographic dimensions including values and lifestyle, mapping behavioral patterns such as purchase triggers and channel preferences, defining segment boundaries, and rating commercial potential. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a markdown table with three columns - Segment Name, Characteristics, and Potential - that includes size estimates, growth outlook, competitive intensity, and strategic fit ratings. Typical use cases include new product launches, campaign planning for SaaS platforms, retail targeting, and rebranding exercises where clear audience definitions are essential. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond intuition and build segment definitions grounded in demographic, psychographic, and behavioral data. ● Outputs 4-6 clearly differentiated segments with demographic, psychographic, and behavioral traits ● Includes potential ratings (High/Medium/Low) justified by market size, accessibility, and revenue opportunity ● Accepts variables for product/service, business context, and marketing goals to tailor the analysis ● Returns structured markdown tables ready to share with stakeholders or embed in strategy decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in customer segmentation and targeting strategy.

## Task
Analyze the provided market and develop a comprehensive segmentation framework for the specified product or service. Identify 4-6 distinct market segments based on demographics, psychographics, behavioral patterns, and purchasing dynamics. For each segment, define its unique characteristics and assess its commercial potential.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Market & Business Context:** {{market-and-business-context}}

**Marketing Goals:** {{marketing-goals}}

## Process
1. Examine the target market landscape, identifying natural customer groupings
2. Analyze demographic factors (age, income, location, occupation)
3. Evaluate psychographic dimensions (values, lifestyle, attitudes, interests)
4. Assess behavioral patterns (usage frequency, decision triggers, channel preferences)
5. Create distinct segments with clear boundaries and actionable definitions
6. Rate each segment's potential based on size, accessibility, alignment with your goals, and revenue opportunity

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Segment Name | Characteristics | Potential |
|--------------|-----------------|----------|

**Characteristics** should cover the defining demographic, psychographic, and behavioral traits that distinguish this segment.

**Potential** should assess market size, growth trajectory, competitive intensity, alignment with your product/service strengths, and strategic fit with your marketing goals (High/Medium/Low with brief justification).
```
