# Industry Trend Analysis Article Generator

## 簡介

The Industry Trend Analysis Article Generator is a free AI prompt that produces well-researched, citation-backed articles exploring the latest trends, challenges, and opportunities shaping any specified industry. This trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks you through researching three major trends, gathering supporting evidence from reputable sources, and synthesizing findings into a clear, persuasive article. It structures each trend section with a descriptive name, concise overview, supporting statistics, real-world examples, expert quotes, and impact analysis. The prompt enforces dependency grammar principles - short subject-verb-object sentences, active verbs, minimal subordinate clauses - to maximize readability and authority. Writers in finance, healthcare, technology, retail, and dozens of other sectors use it to turn industry intelligence into thought-leadership content. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to distill complex market dynamics into an article that informs stakeholders, impresses clients, or positions your brand as an industry voice. ● Structures research into three fully developed trend sections, each with evidence, examples, and expert insights ● Enforces dependency grammar rules for concise, confident prose that holds readers' attention ● Includes a works-cited section and inline citation placeholders to maintain credibility ● Outputs a complete article format - from hook and thesis through conclusion and call-to-action - ready for editing and publication ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business researcher and article writer specializing in trend analysis and clear, dependency-grammar-based writing.

## Task
Research and analyze the latest trends in {{industry}}, identifying key drivers, challenges, and opportunities. Synthesize your findings into a compelling, well-structured article that uses dependency grammar principles for clarity and readability. Cite all sources using proper citation format.

## Article Structure

**Title:** Eye-Opening Insights: A Deep Dive into the Latest Trends Shaping the {{industry}}

**Introduction:**
- Hook the reader with a thought-provoking statement or question about the current state of the industry
- Briefly preview the three main trends you'll explore
- Thesis statement emphasizing the importance of staying informed about these developments

**Trend 1, 2, and 3** (repeat this structure for each):
- **Trend Name:** Provocative, descriptive name
- **Description:** Concise overview of the trend and its implications
- **Supporting Evidence:**
  - Key statistic or data point from a reputable source [Source: Author, Publication, Date]
  - Real-world example illustrating the trend in action [Source: Author, Publication, Date]
  - Expert insight or quote adding depth and credibility [Source: Expert Name, Credentials, Publication, Date]
- **Impact Analysis:** Thoughtful examination of the trend's potential effects on industry stakeholders and the broader market landscape

**Conclusion:**
- Recap the three main trends and their collective significance
- Offer forward-thinking insights on how industry players can adapt and capitalize on these shifts
- Close with a powerful call-to-action motivating readers to embrace change and innovation

**Works Cited:**
[List all sources in proper citation format]

## Writing Criteria
1. Use short, clear sentences with simple subject-verb-object structure
2. Limit prepositional phrases and subordinate clauses for readability
3. Choose strong, active verbs to convey confidence and authority
4. Employ transitional phrases to create coherent, logical flow between ideas
```
