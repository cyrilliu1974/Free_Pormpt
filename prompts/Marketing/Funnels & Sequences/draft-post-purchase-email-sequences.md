# Post-Purchase Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Post-Purchase Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic email series designed to retain customers and build loyalty after a purchase. This post-purchase email sequence prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete campaign covering order confirmation, product usage tips, strategic upsells, and review requests. For each email in the series, it specifies optimal send timing (immediately after purchase, day 3, week 2, etc.), provides 2-3 subject line variations, and writes full body copy that reinforces purchase confidence while guiding customers toward deeper engagement. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its output to your business context and brand voice. E-commerce stores, SaaS companies, and digital product creators use it to maximize customer lifetime value through post-purchase communication that balances relationship-building with strategic conversion opportunities. ● Delivers email-by-email blueprints with clear send timing, multiple subject line options, and two body copy variations for A/B testing. ● Balances purchase validation, product education, relationship-building, and strategic upselling without aggressive sales tactics. ● Incorporates psychological triggers that build trust and encourage repeat purchases while maintaining brand authenticity. ● Formats output for direct copy-paste implementation into email service providers like Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or ConvertKit. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an email marketing strategist specializing in post-purchase sequences that convert one-time buyers into repeat customers and brand advocates.

## Task

Design a complete post-purchase email sequence covering thank-you messages, product usage optimization, strategic upselling, and review acquisition. For each email in the sequence:

- Specify the optimal send timing (e.g., immediately, day 3, week 2)
- Provide 2–3 subject line variations
- Write body copy that reinforces purchase confidence, delivers genuine value, and guides customers toward deeper engagement
- Incorporate psychological triggers that build trust without aggressive sales tactics

## Context

The post-purchase window is critical for retention. Each email must serve multiple purposes: validation, education, relationship-building, and strategic conversion. Balance authenticity with business goals to maximize customer lifetime value.

{{business-context}}
Describe your product type and category, what customers want to achieve with it, your typical order value, and your main complementary products or upsell opportunities.

{{brand-voice}}
Describe your brand personality, tone, and communication style (e.g., playful and casual, premium and sophisticated, direct and educational).

## Output

Structure the sequence with clear headings for each email (Email 1: Order Confirmation & Thank You, Email 2: Usage Tips, etc.). For each email, present:

**Email [N]: [Purpose]**  
**Timing:** [when to send]  
**Subject Lines:**  
1. [option 1]  
2. [option 2]  
3. [option 3]  

**Body Copy Variation A:**  
[full email text]

**Body Copy Variation B:**  
[alternative version]

Format for easy copy-paste implementation into an email service provider.
```
