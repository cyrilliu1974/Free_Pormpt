# Social Media SEO Link Building Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media SEO Link Building Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates actionable plans for earning backlinks through targeted social media influencer engagement and collaboration. This social media SEO prompt for ChatGPT walks you through identifying relevant influencers in your niche, analyzing their content and audience alignment, crafting personalized outreach messages, and designing mutually beneficial collaboration proposals that result in natural backlinks. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a numbered action plan with concrete sub-steps tailored to your business niche, target website types, primary social platforms, and current follower reach. Use it when you need to move beyond generic link-building tactics and build genuine industry relationships that yield high-authority backlinks through social proof and content partnerships. ● Identifies influencers whose audience and content align with your business niche and backlink targets ● Creates tailored outreach frameworks that highlight your unique value proposition and current social reach ● Proposes specific collaboration formats that naturally generate backlinks while providing genuine value to partners ● Delivers actionable numbered steps with bulleted sub-tasks for immediate implementation ## Prompt

```
## Context

- Target website type for backlinks: {{target-website-type}}
- Primary social media platforms: {{social-platforms}}

## Output
Provide the strategy as a numbered list with sub-points using bullet points for maximum clarity and actionability. Each step should be concrete and immediately implementable, balancing outreach effectiveness with relationship authenticity.
```
