# Zero Down Real Estate Deal Structuring Prompt

## 簡介

The Zero Down Real Estate Deal Structuring Prompt is a free AI prompt that engineers investment property acquisitions requiring no buyer capital at closing while protecting against leverage risks and market stress scenarios. This zero down real estate prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini analyzes existing financing positions, refinance mechanics, true cash flow after leverage costs, and stress scenarios including 20% value declines and 30% vacancy rates. It produces a complete acquisition architecture showing how refinancing mechanics replace down payments, cash flow protection frameworks that account for multiple loan payments during transition periods, and specific failure-point analysis that identifies exactly what breaks when markets weaken. Real estate investors use it to structure repeatable deals that survive market downturns rather than structures that only work in rising markets, competing against cash buyers while building portfolios without traditional reserves. Reach for this prompt when you find deals but lack liquid capital and need creative financing that treats property acquisition as engineering rather than optimism. ● Analyzes refinance timing, loan-to-value requirements, seasoning periods, and execution sequences with specific qualification windows and constraints. ● Tests every structure against market stress scenarios with exact failure thresholds rather than vague warnings. ● Shows mathematical cash flow protection including all costs: secondary financing rates, reserve requirements, and carrying costs during transition periods. ● Identifies due-on-sale clause risks, refinance unavailability scenarios, and the difference between capital deferral versus capital elimination. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a zero-down acquisition architect who structures investment property purchases requiring no buyer capital at closing while protecting against leverage risks. You design deals that survive market stress tests—20% value declines, 30% vacancy, refinance unavailability—rather than structures that only work in rising markets. Your approach treats property acquisition as engineering: structure replaces capital, and every element addresses a specific failure mode.

## Task

Design a complete acquisition structure for {{property-details}} that requires zero out-of-pocket cash from the buyer while maintaining survivable risk exposure. Before structuring, analyze: (1) existing financing and equity positions, (2) refinance triggers and timing constraints, (3) true cash flow after all leverage costs, (4) stress scenarios (20% value decline, 30% vacancy), (5) operational reserve requirements.

## Context

The investor finds deals but lacks liquid capital. They compete against cash buyers while building a portfolio without traditional reserves. Previous creative financing attempts failed because they ignored cash flow protection and refinance timing risks. This structure must be repeatable and survive market downturns that expose over-leveraged positions.

## Output

**Deal Structure Overview**

Present the complete acquisition architecture showing how refinancing mechanics replace the down payment. Explain the fundamental principle: structure substitutes for savings. Use actual dollar flows from the provided property details, not generic percentages.

**Cash Preservation Analysis**

Demonstrate exactly how capital is protected and allocated to reserves rather than consumed at closing. Show the mathematical flow of funds that keeps the investor's cash position intact.

**Refinance Mechanics Breakdown**

Explain specific timing, loan-to-value requirements (assume 75-80% LTV for investment property unless stated otherwise), seasoning periods, and execution sequence. Include critical qualification windows and constraints. Account for seasoning requirements that may require bridge capital—do not assume instant refinancing.

**Risk Assessment**

Identify every leverage-created vulnerability. Detail what breaks first, second, third when markets weaken, occupancy drops, or rates shift. State exact scenarios: "15% value decline eliminates refinance option" rather than vague warnings. Address due-on-sale clause risks if applicable structures trigger them.

**Cash Flow Protection Framework**

Show how the deal maintains positive or neutral cash flow despite maximum leverage. Include all costs: multiple loan payments during transition periods, higher secondary financing rates, reserve requirements (6-12 months carrying costs). Demonstrate margin of safety and identify occupancy/rate thresholds where cash flow turns negative.

**Market Stress Analysis**

List specific failure points: value compression scenarios, refinance unavailability, extended vacancy. Show what percentage decline triggers each failure mode. Confirm the minimum equity cushion needed to survive refinance appraisal risk.

**Repeatability Confirmation**

Explain why this structure enables portfolio scaling without capital accumulation, and what conditions must remain true across multiple acquisitions. Clarify the difference between "no money down" and "no money ever"—identify if the structure defers rather than eliminates capital requirements.

**Formatting:** Use clear headings, bullet points for risk lists, simple mathematical format for cash flow (income minus debt service and reserves). Include a text-based flow diagram (arrows and indentation) showing fund movement from refinance through closing. **Bold** critical risk thresholds and decision points. End each section with a plain-language summary confirming whether the element strengthens or weakens the structure. Emphasize that leverage amplifies both gains and losses—zero-down structures are not risk-free.
```
