# Competitive Analysis Table Builder for Market Research

## 簡介

The Competitive Analysis Table Builder for Market Research is a free AI prompt that creates detailed market share comparison tables for strategic planning and competitive intelligence. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to structure market data into clear comparison tables with metrics including market share percentages, year-over-year growth rates, competitive strengths, and vulnerabilities across 3-5 competitors. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing formatted tables with visual indicators (▲/▼) for growth trends, highlighted top performers, and totals rows. Use cases include quarterly market reviews, investor presentations, strategic planning sessions, and new market entry assessments where decision-makers need rapid side-by-side competitor evaluation. This prompt is built for market researchers, business analysts, strategy consultants, and product managers who need to synthesize competitor intelligence into actionable formats quickly. ● Structures data into comparison tables with market share, growth rates, strengths, and weaknesses columns ● Highlights top performers automatically and includes totals rows for quick pattern recognition ● Generates 3-4 actionable insights identifying competitive dynamics, threats, and opportunities ● Accepts customizable scope including target market, competitor list (3-5), and time period for analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher and data analyst specializing in competitive intelligence and market share analysis.

## Task
Create a comprehensive market share comparison table that tracks key metrics and strategic insights across multiple competitors, enabling clear side-by-side evaluation of market position, growth trajectory, strengths, and vulnerabilities.

## Context
{{market-analysis-scope}}

*In your response, specify: target market or industry segment, list of competitors to analyze (3-5 companies/products), and time period for the analysis (e.g., Q1 2023 to Q4 2024).*

## Output
Deliver your analysis using this structure:

### Market Share Analysis: [Market Name]

**Comparison Table** with these columns:
- **Competitor**: Company or product name
- **Market Share %**: Percentage of total market revenue or unit sales
- **YoY Growth Rate**: Year-over-year percent change (use ▲ for positive, ▼ for negative)
- **Key Strengths**: Top 2-3 competitive advantages, unique selling points, or core competencies
- **Key Weaknesses**: Top 2-3 vulnerabilities, gaps, or deficiencies

**Formatting requirements**:
- Use bold or shading to highlight the top performer in each column
- Include a "Totals" row showing the sum of Market Share % and average of Growth Rates

### Insights & Recommendations

Provide 3-4 bullet points that:
- Summarize the most significant competitive dynamics revealed by the data
- Identify emerging threats or opportunities
- Suggest strategic focus areas or next steps based on the analysis

Ensure all claims are grounded in the data presented; avoid subjective judgments without supporting evidence.
```
