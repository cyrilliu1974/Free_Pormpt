# Competitor Traffic Analysis Review Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor Traffic Analysis Review Prompt is a free AI prompt that examines competitor SEO data and identifies concrete improvement opportunities for digital marketers and SEO analysts. This competitor traffic analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and a list of competitors, then delivers a three-column markdown table breaking down each rival's primary traffic channels, content strategies, and keyword positioning alongside specific, implementable recommendations. It works by comparing competitor strengths and weaknesses against your current SEO metrics to surface high-impact gaps you can exploit. SEO teams use it to benchmark performance, discover untapped keyword clusters, and prioritize optimization efforts based on what's already working in their niche. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level competitive research and translate raw traffic data into a prioritized action plan. ● Identifies primary traffic sources, content strategies, and keyword positioning for each competitor in a scannable table format ● Delivers specific, actionable SEO recommendations by comparing competitor tactics against your current performance metrics ● Surfaces gaps and weaknesses in rival strategies that you can capitalize on to gain market share ● Structures findings in markdown for easy integration into reports, presentations, or internal documentation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst specializing in competitive traffic analysis and opportunity identification.

## Task
Analyze competitor traffic data to identify concrete opportunities for improving SEO performance. Examine traffic sources for each competitor, noting trends, strengths, and weaknesses. Compare these findings against the provided current performance to pinpoint high-impact improvement areas.

## Context
**Business Context:** {{business-context}}
(Include: your industry, current SEO performance metrics, target keywords, and website URL)

**Competitors to Analyze:** {{competitor-list}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| COMPETITOR | TRAFFIC SOURCES | OPPORTUNITIES |
|------------|-----------------|---------------|

For each competitor row:
- **TRAFFIC SOURCES:** Identify their primary traffic channels, content strategies, and keyword positioning
- **OPPORTUNITIES:** Provide specific, actionable recommendations based on gaps between their performance and the current SEO position described above

Ensure every opportunity is concrete and implementable.
```
