# Onboarding Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Onboarding Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates psychology-driven email sequences for businesses launching new customers into product adoption. This onboarding email sequence prompt for ChatGPT produces four complete, deployment-ready emails that follow a proven progression: welcome and value articulation, educational activation, social proof reinforcement, and incentive-driven commitment. Each email includes an optimized subject line, preview text, and body copy structured around customer psychology principles rather than aggressive sales tactics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and accepts variables for your specific product or service and business context to tailor the sequence to your audience. Marketing teams, SaaS founders, course creators, and customer success managers reach for this prompt when they need to reduce early-stage churn and build trust during the critical first days after signup. ● Produces four strategically sequenced emails that move from welcome to activation to loyalty. ● Includes subject lines, preview text, and complete body copy formatted for immediate deployment. ● Balances education, social proof, and incentives without resorting to pushy sales language. ● Accepts product details and business context to personalize tone and messaging for your audience. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert email marketer specializing in onboarding sequences, customer psychology, and persuasive writing.

## Task

Write a 4-email onboarding sequence for {{product-or-service}} that guides new customers through adoption and full usage. Each email should educate, motivate, and build loyalty using proven psychological principles. Focus on value and trust-building rather than pushy sales tactics.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Provide four complete emails following this progression:

**Email 1: Welcome & Value**
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Body: warm opener, core value proposition, single clear call-to-action, friendly closing

**Email 2: Education & Activation**
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Body: helpful opener, tutorial or quick-start guide, action-oriented call-to-action, encouraging closing

**Email 3: Social Proof & Deepening**
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Body: engaging opener, case study or customer success story, invitation call-to-action, supportive closing

**Email 4: Incentive & Commitment**
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Body: personal opener, special offer or exclusive benefit, urgency-balanced call-to-action, relationship-focused closing

Format each email ready to deploy, with compelling subject lines optimized for open rates.
```
