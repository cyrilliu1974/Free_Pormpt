# Ghostwriting Portfolio Builder for Content Strategists

## 簡介

The Ghostwriting Portfolio Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a professionally structured portfolio to showcase content expertise and attract ghostwriting clients. This ghostwriting portfolio prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce a complete portfolio package: a compelling cover page that positions your niche expertise, a detailed table of contents for navigation, and multiple sample articles demonstrating versatility across writing styles, tones, and formats. Each sample includes a brief introduction explaining its purpose, target audience, and writing approach, plus callouts of research methods and techniques employed. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting portfolio context as a variable to tailor the output to your specific niche and credentials. Content strategists and freelance writers use it to build portfolios that highlight both range and specialization, turning past work or new samples into a cohesive marketing asset. ● Generates a niche-focused cover page with positioning statement, credentials, and professional branding elements ● Creates a structured table of contents organizing all portfolio sections with clear article titles and descriptions ● Produces multiple sample articles varying in tone, format, and complexity to demonstrate writing range within your niche ● Includes introductions for each sample explaining purpose, target audience, and writing techniques used ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist creating a ghostwriting portfolio to showcase expertise and attract clients.

## Task
Design a professionally formatted portfolio containing:
- A compelling cover page highlighting niche expertise
- A detailed table of contents organizing the portfolio structure
- Multiple sample articles demonstrating versatility across writing styles, tones, and formats appropriate to the niche
- Brief introductions for each article explaining purpose and target audience

Each sample should showcase research skills, industry knowledge, and reader engagement ability.

## Context
{{portfolio-context}}

## Output
Structure your portfolio with clear markdown headings:

### Cover Page
- Niche positioning and expertise summary
- Key credentials and differentiators
- Professional branding elements

### Table of Contents
- Organized navigation of all portfolio sections
- Clear article titles and descriptions

### Sample Articles
For each article include:
- Article title and format type
- Brief introduction (purpose, target audience, writing approach)
- Full sample demonstrating the specified style
- Callout of techniques or research methods employed

Vary tone, format, and complexity across samples to demonstrate range within the niche.
```
