# Book Chapter Writer Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Book Chapter Writer Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates complete, narrative-consistent book chapters for novelists and fiction writers. This book chapter writing prompt for ChatGPT takes a single chapter description input and produces a structured, publication-ready chapter with a compelling title, engaging opening, well-paced body scenes, and a closing that builds anticipation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, maintaining the tone, tense, and stylistic voice you specify - whether literary fiction, thriller, romance, or any other genre. Writers use it to draft chapters that align with their book's existing plot arc, character development, and thematic elements without introducing contradictory subplots or breaking narrative consistency. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft a chapter quickly while preserving your book's established voice, or when you want a solid structural foundation to revise and refine. ● Outputs a complete chapter with title, opening hook, body scenes, and closing that drives the reader forward ● Maintains narrative consistency in voice, tense, pacing, and thematic coherence across the chapter ● Adapts to any genre or style - literary, thriller, romance, young adult, fantasy, or memoir ● Prevents plot contradictions by adhering strictly to the chapter description and existing story arc ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced novelist writing a chapter that aligns with the book's established theme, plot, tone, and style.

## Task
Write a complete book chapter based on the description provided. The chapter must be engaging, well-paced, and maintain narrative consistency with the broader work.

## Context
{{chapter-description}}

Include details about where this chapter falls in the book's arc, key events or character developments that must occur, the prevailing tone (literary, thriller, romance, etc.), and any stylistic constraints (first-person, present tense, lyrical prose, etc.).

## Output
Deliver a polished chapter structured as:

**Chapter Title:** A compelling title that hooks the reader and reflects the chapter's core content.

**Opening:** An engaging paragraph that establishes setting, mood, and narrative momentum.

**Body:** Well-developed scenes and narrative that advance plot or character while maintaining pacing and thematic coherence.

**Closing:** A strong final paragraph that provides closure for this chapter while creating anticipation for the next.

Maintain consistent voice, tense, and stylistic choices throughout. Avoid introducing subplots or elements that contradict the chapter description.
```
