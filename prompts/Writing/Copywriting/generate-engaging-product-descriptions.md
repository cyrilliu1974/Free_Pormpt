# Ecommerce Product Description Generator

## 簡介

The Ecommerce Product Description Generator is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused product descriptions for online retailers and marketing teams. This ecommerce product description prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms basic product details into structured, persuasive copy that drives sales. It produces descriptions with a compelling overview, scannable feature bullets, sensory details that make products tangible, audience-resonant benefits, authentic brand voice, and clear calls to action. Retailers use it to write product pages that rank well in search while converting browsers into buyers, whether describing fashion items, electronics, home goods, or specialty products. Reach for this prompt when you need product descriptions that balance search optimization with sales psychology, or when scaling content across a growing catalog. ● Structures descriptions with scannable features, sensory experience sections, and benefit-focused copy ● Integrates SEO keywords naturally throughout the text without resorting to keyword stuffing ● Maintains consistent brand voice and tone while highlighting unique selling points ● Includes strategic calls to action that guide customers toward purchase decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert ecommerce copywriter specializing in conversion-focused product descriptions that balance search optimization with persuasive sales copy.

## Context
{{product-details}}

## Task
Write a product description that:

1. Opens with the product name and a compelling overview
2. Highlights key features as scannable bullets
3. Includes sensory details that make the product tangible and desirable
4. Emphasizes benefits that resonate with the target audience
5. Weaves in brand story and voice authentically
6. Closes with a clear, compelling call to action
7. Integrates relevant keywords naturally throughout

Match the tone and vocabulary to the brand identity. Keep language specific and vivid; avoid generic marketing clichés.

## Output Format
**[Product Name]**

[Overview paragraph]

**Key Features:**
- Feature 1
- Feature 2
- Feature 3

**Experience:**
[Sensory details paragraph]

**Benefits:**
- Benefit 1
- Benefit 2
- Benefit 3

[Brand story paragraph]

[Call to action]

---
*Target Keywords: [list]*
```
