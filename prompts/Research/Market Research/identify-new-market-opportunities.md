# Market Opportunity Identification Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Opportunity Identification Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive market analysis to uncover new sales expansion opportunities for business strategists, analysts, and growth teams. This market opportunity identification prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current market trends, consumer behaviors, and competitor strategies to reveal untapped markets and strategic entry points. You provide five business context variables - overview, target market, key offerings, geographical focus, and unique selling proposition - and the prompt returns a structured markdown table mapping at least five distinct opportunities. Each row details a specific market, its growth potential, anticipated entry barriers, and concrete strategies to overcome challenges. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile across text-generation platforms. Use this prompt when expanding into new territories, launching products, or re-evaluating market positioning to stay competitive. ● Analyzes market size, growth potential, and competitive landscape in a single structured output ● Evaluates entry barriers and challenge mitigation strategies for each opportunity ● Produces a markdown table format for easy comparison and decision-making ● Customizes analysis based on your unique selling proposition and geographical focus ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in sales expansion and opportunity identification.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive market analysis to identify new sales expansion opportunities. Research current market trends, consumer behaviors, and competitor strategies. Analyze the data to uncover potential growth areas, untapped markets, and strategic entry points. Evaluate the challenges associated with each opportunity and recommend actionable strategies to overcome them. Consider market size, growth potential, entry barriers, and alignment with the company's existing strengths and resources.

## Context
**Business Overview:** {{business-overview}}

**Current Target Market:** {{target-market}}

**Key Products/Services:** {{key-offerings}}

**Geographical Focus:** {{geographical-focus}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Market | Potential | Challenges | Strategies |
|--------|-----------|------------|------------|

Provide at least 5 distinct opportunities, ensuring a comprehensive and diverse range of expansion options. Each row should detail a specific market opportunity with clear assessment of its potential, anticipated challenges, and concrete strategies for market entry or expansion.
```
