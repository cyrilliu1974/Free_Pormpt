# Content Structure Outline Generator for Any Business

## 簡介

The Content Structure Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates audience-driven content plans for businesses of any type or focus topic. This content structure prompt for ChatGPT takes your business focus and audience goals, then delivers a markdown table organizing 6-10 diverse content types - from blog posts and videos to case studies and social campaigns - mapped to specific audience segments and the key takeaways each piece should communicate. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing your business context to recommend content formats that address awareness, consideration, and conversion stages of the customer journey. Marketing teams use it to visualize a balanced content calendar; solo founders rely on it to identify which content types will resonate with their niche; agencies deploy it during strategy workshops to align stakeholders around content priorities. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured content roadmap that connects what you create to who consumes it and why it matters. ● Outputs a clean three-column markdown table (Content Type | Target Audience | Key Takeaways) ready to share with stakeholders or import into project tools. ● Balances content across the full customer journey - awareness, consideration, and conversion - so no stage is neglected. ● Identifies which audience segments engage with each content format, ensuring every piece has a defined purpose and recipient. ● Suggests 6-10 diverse content types tailored to your business model and goals, from educational guides to promotional campaigns. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in audience-driven content planning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content structure that outlines diverse, engaging content for the specified business. Deliver your plan as a markdown table with three columns: **Content Type**, **Target Audience**, and **Key Takeaways**.

## Context
Business and focus: {{business-and-topic}}
Audience and goals: {{audience-and-goals}}

Analyze the business type and topic to determine the most effective content formats. Identify which audience segments will engage with each content type. Define the key takeaways each piece should deliver. Ensure the structure addresses various stages of the customer journey and aligns with business objectives.

## Output
Present your content structure as a markdown table:

| Content Type | Target Audience | Key Takeaways |
|--------------|-----------------|---------------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Include 6-10 diverse content types that balance awareness, consideration, and conversion stages.
```
