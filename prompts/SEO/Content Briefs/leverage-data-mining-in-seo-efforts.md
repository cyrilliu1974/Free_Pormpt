# Data-Driven SEO Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Data-Driven SEO Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops complete SEO strategies combining keyword research, content optimization, and dependency grammar frameworks for digital marketers and content strategists. This SEO strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target keywords, website niche, and audience to produce a structured action plan covering keyword research, content architecture, writing techniques, and prioritized implementation steps. It applies dependency grammar principles to improve how search engines parse semantic relationships in your content, while identifying primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities based on search intent and competitive gaps. Real use cases include launching new content hubs, recovering from traffic drops, and systematically improving topical authority across enterprise websites. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, research-backed SEO roadmap tailored to specific business challenges rather than generic best-practice checklists. ● Maps primary, secondary, and long-tail keywords to search intent and competitive opportunities ● Applies dependency grammar frameworks to structure content for better search engine comprehension ● Delivers headline hierarchy, semantic keyword placement, and internal linking strategies ● Provides a prioritized implementation roadmap with expected impact on your current SEO challenges ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in content optimization and search engine visibility.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive SEO strategy that leverages keyword research, content structure optimization, and dependency grammar principles to improve search rankings and content quality.

## Context
**Target keywords:** {{target-keywords}}

**Website niche:** {{website-niche}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Current challenges & goals:** {{seo-context}}

Analyze the target keywords to identify content gaps and opportunities. Apply dependency grammar frameworks to structure content that emphasizes semantic relationships and improves both readability and search engine comprehension.

## Output
Provide a structured SEO strategy with clear headings and bullet points covering:

- **Keyword Research & Analysis** – primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities; search intent mapping; competitive gaps
- **Content Structure Optimization** – dependency grammar application; headline hierarchy; semantic keyword placement; internal linking strategy
- **Content Writing Techniques** – readability optimization; entity relationships; topical authority building; user engagement signals
- **Implementation Roadmap** – prioritized action steps with expected impact on current challenges and content goals
```
