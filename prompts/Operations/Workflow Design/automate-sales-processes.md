# Sales Automation Strategy Builder for CRM & Email

## 簡介

The Sales Automation Strategy Builder for CRM & Email is a free AI prompt that designs end-to-end sales automation strategies tailored to your business context and sales challenges. This sales automation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current sales processes, identifies bottlenecks, and delivers a phased implementation roadmap for CRM integration, email template development, and automated follow-up sequences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable plans that address real pain points - whether you're a SaaS team managing lead nurture, an e-commerce business scaling outreach, or a B2B sales organization moving to trigger-based communication. The output includes CRM configuration guidance, stage-specific email templates with personalization strategies, multi-channel sequence design, success metrics, and a timeline with resource requirements. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, consultant-grade sales automation blueprint rather than generic advice - it transforms a description of your business, target customers, and sales goals into a concrete action plan. ● Analyzes current sales workflows to pinpoint inefficiencies, bottlenecks, and automation opportunities before prescribing solutions. ● Delivers CRM setup recommendations, data migration plans, and user adoption strategies specific to your business type and customer base. ● Creates email templates for each sales funnel stage with personalization tactics, A/B testing guidance, and clear calls to action. ● Designs trigger-based follow-up sequences with timing optimization, multi-channel touchpoints, and exit criteria to prevent over-automation. ● Provides a phased rollout timeline, resource requirements, KPIs, and anticipated challenges with mitigation strategies. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales automation specialist focused on optimizing sales processes through CRM systems, email templates, and automated follow-up sequences.

## Task
Analyze the provided sales context and design a comprehensive sales automation strategy. Deliver a step-by-step implementation plan that addresses current pain points and achieves the stated goals.

## Context
**Business & Sales Environment:**
{{business-and-sales-context}}

*Include: business type, current sales process description, target customer base profile, main sales challenges and pain points, desired outcomes and goals for automation.*

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points covering:

### 1. Current State Analysis
- Assessment of existing sales processes
- Identified inefficiencies and bottlenecks
- Opportunities for automation

### 2. CRM System Implementation
- Recommended setup and configuration
- Data migration and integration plan
- User adoption strategy
- Best practices and potential pitfalls

### 3. Email Template Development
- Templates for each stage of the sales funnel
- Personalization strategies
- Clear, action-oriented messaging
- A/B testing recommendations

### 4. Automated Follow-Up Sequences
- Trigger-based sequence design
- Timing and cadence optimization
- Multi-channel touchpoint strategy
- Exit and escalation criteria

### 5. Implementation Roadmap
- Phased rollout timeline
- Resource requirements
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Anticipated challenges with solutions

For each section, provide specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the business context provided.
```
