# Social Media Campaign Strategy Prompt for Sales

## 簡介

The Social Media Campaign Strategy Prompt for Sales is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, actionable social media campaign plans for businesses seeking to drive sales and increase brand awareness. This social media campaign prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete strategy blueprint covering content calendars with themes and scheduling, target audience analysis including demographics and platform preferences, KPIs tied to sales goals, platform-specific content strategies, engagement tactics, paid advertising allocation, influencer partnership frameworks, community management protocols, and measurement systems with analytics and optimization plans. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your product details, target audience, brand voice, budget, and timeline into a structured campaign ready for immediate implementation. Marketing teams use it to plan product launches, seasonal promotions, and ongoing brand-building initiatives across multiple social platforms. This prompt is designed for marketing managers, social media strategists, small business owners, and agencies that need a detailed campaign blueprint quickly without starting from a blank page. ● Builds a content calendar with platform-specific themes, post formats, and optimal scheduling recommendations ● Analyzes target audience demographics, psychographics, pain points, and preferred social platforms to inform content creation ● Defines measurable KPIs, analytics tools, reporting cadence, and performance benchmarks aligned with sales and awareness goals ● Structures paid advertising strategy with budget allocation, targeting parameters, creative direction, and influencer collaboration approaches ● Establishes community management protocols and iteration plans for optimizing campaign performance based on real-time data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist creating a comprehensive campaign strategy to drive sales and increase brand awareness.

## Task
Develop a detailed, actionable social media campaign plan that includes:

- **Content calendar** with themes, formats, and scheduling recommendations
- **Target audience analysis** covering demographics, psychographics, pain points, and platform preferences
- **Key performance indicators (KPIs)** tied to sales and awareness goals
- **Content strategy** specifying post types, formats, and optimal posting frequencies per platform
- **Engagement tactics** for building community and driving interaction
- **Paid advertising strategy** with budget allocation, targeting parameters, and creative recommendations
- **Influencer partnership approach** including identification, outreach, and collaboration structures
- **Community management protocols** for responding to comments, messages, and feedback
- **Measurement framework** detailing analytics tools, reporting cadence, and performance benchmarks
- **Iteration plan** showing how to optimize based on data insights throughout the campaign

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}

**Budget:** {{budget}}

**Timeline:** {{timeline}}

## Output
Present the strategy with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for easy implementation. Ensure all recommendations are specific, actionable, and aligned with the stated business objectives.
```
