# Competitor Pricing Analysis Prompt for Market Research

## 簡介

The Competitor Pricing Analysis Prompt for Market Research is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic competitor pricing strategy analysis for businesses and market analysts seeking competitive advantage. This competitor pricing analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides you through researching competitor pricing models, identifying gaps in the market, and surfacing actionable opportunities for strategic positioning. It examines base prices, promotional patterns, bundling options, subscription models, and contract terms across your competitive landscape. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a markdown table of competitor insights plus strategic recommendations tailored to your industry, product, target market, and current pricing model. Use it when entering a new market, adjusting pricing strategy, or preparing competitive intelligence reports. ● Maps competitor pricing tiers, discount patterns, bundling strategies, and subscription models into a comparison table ● Identifies specific gaps and weaknesses in competitor pricing strategies that create market opportunities ● Delivers 3-5 strategic insights with concrete recommendations for gaining competitive advantage ● Provides market positioning guidance on adjusting your current pricing model based on competitive landscape ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive pricing strategy.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor pricing analysis to identify opportunities for competitive advantage in the specified industry.

## Context
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Target Market:** {{target-market}}
**Current Pricing Model:** {{current-pricing-model}}
**Main Competitors:** {{competitors}}

## Analysis Framework
1. Research and list the main competitors (use the provided list as a starting point, expand if needed)
2. Investigate each competitor's pricing strategy:
   - Base prices and pricing tiers
   - Discounts and promotional patterns
   - Bundling options and package deals
   - Subscription models and contract terms
3. Analyze alignment with market trends and customer preferences
4. Identify gaps or weaknesses in competitors' pricing strategies
5. Highlight opportunities to achieve competitive advantage

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Competitor | Pricing Model | Key Takeaways |
|------------|---------------|---------------|

Below the table, provide:
- **Strategic Opportunities:** 3-5 actionable insights where pricing gaps or weaknesses can be leveraged
- **Market Positioning Recommendations:** How the current pricing model could be adjusted for competitive advantage
```
