# Business Performance Metrics Generator

## 簡介

The Business Performance Metrics Generator is a free AI prompt that develops a strategic framework of five actionable KPIs tailored to any business type, covering financial performance, operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, employee engagement, and market position. This business performance metrics prompt for ChatGPT acts as a strategic business analyst, building a complete measurement system with clear definitions, calculation formulas, target benchmarks, and importance rationale for each metric. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering practical KPIs that reveal business health and growth trajectory. Business owners, operations managers, and strategic analysts use it to establish consistent tracking systems, align teams around measurable goals, and create data-driven reporting dashboards. The prompt also recommends measurement frequency and reporting formats, ensuring the metrics are not only well-designed but immediately implementable. Reach for this prompt when launching a new business, restructuring performance tracking, preparing for investor reviews, or establishing accountability frameworks across departments. ● Covers all critical dimensions - financial health, operations, customers, employees, and competitive position - in one coherent framework ● Provides calculation formulas, industry benchmarks, and clear rationale for why each metric matters to business success ● Includes implementation guidance on tracking frequency, reporting structure, and interdependencies between metrics ● Adapts to any business type, from SaaS startups to retail chains to professional services firms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business analyst specializing in performance measurement frameworks and KPI design across industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive set of 5 key metrics to measure and track the overall health and progress of the business described below. Cover multiple dimensions: financial performance, operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, employee engagement, and market position.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}

## Output
For each of the 5 metrics, provide:

**Metric [N]: [Name]**
- **Definition:** Clear explanation of what the metric measures
- **Calculation:** Formula or method to compute it
- **Target/Benchmark:** Specific goal or industry standard
- **Importance:** How this metric reveals business health and growth trajectory

After presenting all 5 metrics, include:

**Additional Considerations:**
Any contextual factors, dependencies between metrics, or implementation notes

**Measurement Frequency:**
How often each metric should be tracked (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly)

**Reporting Format:**
Recommended dashboard or report structure for ongoing monitoring

Ensure metrics are practical to measure, actionable, and avoid unnecessary complexity.
```
