# Customer Onboarding Objectives Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Onboarding Objectives Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven, phased onboarding objectives for customer success teams and product managers. This customer onboarding prompt for ChatGPT structures goals across three distinct phases: early wins in the first week, foundational adoption through day 30, and sustained engagement through day 90. For each phase, it defines concrete success metrics, realistic adoption targets with justifications based on product complexity, and measurement methods including usage analytics and knowledge assessments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, turning your onboarding context into a complete framework with questionnaire templates, feedback loops, and documentation requirements. Use it when launching a new product, redesigning customer onboarding flows, or setting quarterly success benchmarks that balance ambition with implementation reality. ● Structures objectives into three time-bound phases with specific adoption rate targets justified by product complexity and customer capacity. ● Defines measurement methods for each phase, including usage analytics, comprehension questionnaires, satisfaction scores, and retention metrics. ● Generates adjustment triggers and feedback loops that let you refine objectives based on actual performance data. ● Creates documentation templates and knowledge assessment tools that track customer progress from setup through full adoption. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert customer success strategist specializing in data-driven onboarding programs that balance ambitious goals with realistic implementation constraints.

## Task

Design a comprehensive set of SMART onboarding objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) for the provided product and customer context. Structure objectives across three phases: early wins (days 1-7), foundational adoption (days 8-30), and sustained engagement (days 31-90). For each phase, define clear success metrics, measurement methods, and realistic adoption targets justified by product complexity and customer capacity.

## Context

{{onboarding-context}}

Analyze this information to craft objectives that drive product adoption without overwhelming customers or teams. Factor in product complexity, customer learning curves, available resources, and timeline constraints when setting targets.

## Output

Provide your response with the following structure:

### Phase 1: Early Wins (Days 1-7)
- Objective statement
- Success metrics (with specific KPIs)
- Measurement methods
- Target adoption rate (% with justification)

### Phase 2: Foundational Adoption (Days 8-30)
- Objective statement
- Success metrics (with specific KPIs)
- Measurement methods
- Target adoption rate (% with justification)

### Phase 3: Sustained Engagement (Days 31-90)
- Objective statement
- Success metrics (with specific KPIs)
- Measurement methods
- Target adoption rate (% with justification)

### Measurement Framework
- Questionnaire templates for comprehension checks at key milestones
- Feedback loops and adjustment triggers based on performance data
- Documentation requirements supporting each phase

For each metric, explain why the target is achievable given the product complexity and customer profile.
```
