# Cinematic Mirror Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Mirror Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces dramatic, film-like bathroom mirror portraits with warm golden-hour lighting and moody atmosphere for content creators and visual storytellers. This cinematic mirror selfie prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana renders a subject photographing themselves in a tiled bathroom as warm light streams through a window, creating glowing rim-light around their silhouette. Steam from a recent shower diffuses the scene with atmospheric haze while dust particles catch the light. The prompt specifies tan stone tiles, backlit mirrors, and rich contrast to deliver magazine-quality results. Creators use it for lifestyle content, fashion portfolio work, personal branding imagery, and editorial storytelling that requires authentic yet cinematic aesthetics. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-looking mirror selfies without a studio setup or when your project calls for that moody, golden-hour film aesthetic. ● Produces warm golden-hour window light with realistic rim-lighting effects and glowing dust particles suspended in the air. ● Includes atmospheric steam layer and backlit mirror details that add depth and cinematic mood to the composition. ● Customizable subject appearance and wardrobe while maintaining consistent dramatic lighting and rich tonal contrast. ● Outputs high-resolution portraits suitable for social media, editorial layouts, brand campaigns, and portfolio presentation. ## Prompt

```
{{subject}} takes a cinematic mirror selfie in a bathroom lit by warm golden-hour light streaming through a small window. {{wardrobe-and-styling}}. Their silhouette glows with warm rim-light. ```
```
