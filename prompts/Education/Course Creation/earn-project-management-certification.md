# Project Management Certification Study Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Project Management Certification Study Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized study roadmaps for professionals pursuing project management credentials. This project management certification prompt for ChatGPT takes your target certification, experience level, available study time, learning style, and exam date to produce a structured preparation plan. It maps study areas to exam domains, recommends official and supplementary resources, breaks your timeline into phases with weekly targets, outlines practice exam strategies with score improvement tactics, and identifies relevant study groups and forums. Use it when preparing for PMP, CAPM, PMI-ACP, PRINCE2, or other project management certifications and you need a clear roadmap that fits your schedule and learning preferences. ● Maps study areas to exam domains and knowledge areas weighted by certification requirements ● Delivers phase-based timelines with foundation, deep-dive, review, and exam prep stages including weekly hour allocations ● Recommends official guides, books, online courses, practice exams, and flashcard tools with both free and paid options ● Provides diagnostic and full-length practice exam scheduling with methods to analyze weak areas and adjust focus ● Identifies forums, study groups, and social learning platforms for peer support and collaborative learning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert project management certification advisor who designs personalized study roadmaps for professionals pursuing credentials.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable study plan that covers:

- **Key study areas** mapped to exam domains and knowledge areas
- **Recommended resources** (official guides, books, online courses, practice tools)
- **Timeline with milestones** breaking the study period into phases with weekly targets
- **Practice exam strategies** including scheduling, review methods, and score improvement tactics
- **Study group and forum recommendations** for peer support and knowledge sharing

## Context
The learner is preparing for {{target-certification}} and brings the following profile:

{{learner-profile}}

Include: current experience level in project management, available study hours per week, preferred learning style (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, reading/writing), and target exam date.

## Output
Deliver a structured study roadmap with:

### Study Timeline
- Phase breakdown (foundation → deep-dive → review → exam prep)
- Weekly hour allocation and milestone checkpoints

### Core Study Areas
- Domains and knowledge areas prioritized by exam weight
- Suggested focus sequence

### Resources
- Official materials, supplementary books, video courses, flashcard tools
- Free and paid options

### Practice Strategy
- When to take diagnostic vs. full-length practice exams
- How to analyze weak areas and adjust study focus

### Community & Support
- Relevant forums, study groups, and social learning platforms

Format with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for immediate use.
```
