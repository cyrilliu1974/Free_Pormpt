# Cold Email Copy Generator for Sales Outreach

## 簡介

The Cold Email Copy Generator for Sales Outreach is a free AI prompt that writes industry-specific cold emails designed to get replies by addressing real pain points and positioning products as solutions. This cold email prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the target industry and ideal customer profile to craft personalized emails under 150 words. It identifies sector-specific challenges, maps your product benefits to those needs, and structures each email with an attention-grabbing subject line, personalized opener, problem-focused body, and a single low-friction call-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering formatted output with four labeled sections ready to send or adapt. Reach for this prompt when you need to scale cold outreach without sacrificing personalization, or when entering a new vertical and want emails that demonstrate genuine industry knowledge. ● Maps product benefits directly to industry pain points rather than listing generic features ● Structures each email with subject line, opening, body, and CTA as separate labeled sections ● Enforces brevity by keeping total email length under 150 words for higher read rates ● Uses dependency grammar principles to create logical flow from problem to solution to action ## Prompt

```

**Process:**
1. Identify common challenges and pain points in {{target-industry}}

3. Craft an attention-grabbing subject line that speaks to {{icp}}

6. Close with one clear, low-friction call-to-action
```
