# Email Subject Line Generator for High Open Rates

## 簡介

The Email Subject Line Generator for High Open Rates is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-focused email subject lines tailored to your product, audience, and brand voice. This email subject line prompt for ChatGPT produces 10 ready-to-test subject lines designed to maximize open rates by leading with benefit or curiosity, eliminating filler words, and maintaining logical flow from hook to value proposition. It works by taking four inputs - your product or service, target audience, unique selling proposition, and brand voice - then structures each subject line so every word earns its place. Email marketers use it to A/B test fresh angles, launch campaigns faster, and maintain brand consistency across drip sequences and newsletters. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple subject line options quickly, want to test different hooks without brainstorming from scratch, or need to align subject lines with a specific tone and audience segment. ● Produces 10 unique subject lines per run, each structured for maximum impact and clarity ● Balances curiosity and value communication to trigger opens without clickbait ● Reflects your specified brand voice, from professional and authoritative to playful and bold ● Eliminates filler words and focuses on benefit-first or curiosity-driven hooks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer specializing in high-conversion subject lines.

## Task
Create 10 email subject lines that maximize open rates and drive conversions. Each subject line must be attention-grabbing, relevant to the offering, and clearly communicate value while reflecting the specified brand voice.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Structure each subject line so that every word earns its place—eliminate filler, lead with benefit or curiosity, and maintain clear logical flow from hook to value proposition.

## Output
Deliver as a numbered list, one subject line per line:

1. [subject line]
2. [subject line]
3. [subject line]
…and so on through 10.
```
