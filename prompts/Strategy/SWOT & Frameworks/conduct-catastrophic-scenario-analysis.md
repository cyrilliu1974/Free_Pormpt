# Catastrophic Scenario Analysis Prompt for Risk Planning

## 簡介

The Catastrophic Scenario Analysis Prompt for Risk Planning is a free AI prompt that helps risk analysts, crisis managers, and strategic planners envision the most devastating potential outcomes when everything that could go wrong does go wrong. This catastrophic scenario analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured framework: it identifies a trigger event, maps five cascading failures, captures first-person stakeholder accounts from three perspectives, describes long-term aftermath, and extracts preventive lessons. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for anyone responsible for business continuity, disaster preparedness, or strategic risk assessment. Use it to stress-test product launches, organizational changes, infrastructure decisions, or any complex system where hidden vulnerabilities could spiral into irreversible damage. ● Maps a five-stage cascade of critical failures to reveal hidden interdependencies and points of vulnerability ● Delivers three first-person stakeholder perspectives that humanize abstract risks and clarify real-world consequences ● Provides a clear aftermath summary and lessons-learned section to guide mitigation planning ● Structured output format makes it easy to share with executive teams, compliance officers, and operational leads ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a risk analyst conducting a worst-case scenario analysis. Your task is to envision the most catastrophic potential outcomes if everything that could go wrong does go wrong.

## Task

Analyze the following situation and develop a comprehensive nightmare scenario:

{{situation-or-system}}

Identify cascading failures, stakeholder impacts, and preventive lessons. Focus on devastating, far-reaching consequences without downplaying severity. Consider ripple effects, long-term implications, and critical vulnerabilities.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

**Background:** Brief context on the situation

**Trigger Event:** The initial event that sets off the catastrophic chain reaction

**Cascade of Failures:**
1. [First critical failure]
2. [Second critical failure]
3. [Third critical failure]
4. [Fourth critical failure]
5. [Fifth critical failure]

**Stakeholder Perspectives:**

Stakeholder 1:
- Description: [Role and position]
- Experience: "[First-person account of their nightmare experience]"

Stakeholder 2:
- Description: [Role and position]
- Experience: "[First-person account of their nightmare experience]"

Stakeholder 3:
- Description: [Role and position]
- Experience: "[First-person account of their nightmare experience]"

**Aftermath:** Description of the irrevocable damage and long-term consequences

**Lessons Learned:** Key takeaways on how this nightmare scenario could have been prevented
```
