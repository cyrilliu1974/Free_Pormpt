# Podcast Series Structure and Content Planner

## 簡介

The Podcast Series Structure and Content Planner is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive podcast series blueprints with per-episode content breakdowns for producers and creators. This podcast series planning prompt for ChatGPT generates a hierarchical outline covering series architecture, episode-by-episode flow, audience interaction mechanisms, and guest interview integration. It works by taking your target audience, topic, and series parameters - such as episode count or format requirements - and producing a script-ready production plan that balances narrative momentum across the season while keeping individual episodes accessible to new listeners. Podcast producers use it to map out educational series, interview shows, narrative podcasts, or branded content with consistent structure and engagement hooks. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering detailed outlines that include opening hooks, segment breakdowns, key facts to incorporate, Q&A prompts, and recurring format elements. This prompt is for podcast producers, content creators, and marketing teams launching new series or refining existing show structures who need a production-ready content plan. ● Outputs a full series overview with episode count, audience profile, and recurring segment templates ● Provides per-episode breakdowns with opening hooks, content segments, guest questions, and closing calls-to-action ● Incorporates audience interaction points like Q&A prompts, call-in topics, and listener challenges ● Delivers hierarchical formatting suitable for reference during recording and post-production ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert podcast producer specializing in series development and scriptwriting.

## Task
Develop a complete podcast series structure and episode-by-episode content plan in script-ready outline format. Cover series architecture, episode flow, audience engagement mechanisms, and guest integration.

## Context
Target audience and topic: {{audience-and-topic}}
Series parameters: {{series-parameters}}

Research the audience's interests and knowledge level within this domain. Structure the series to build narrative momentum across episodes while keeping each installment self-contained enough for new listeners.

## Output
Deliver a detailed hierarchical outline organized as:

**Series Overview**
- Total episode count and overarching themes
- Audience profile and content positioning
- Recurring segments and format elements

**Per-Episode Breakdown**
For each episode, provide:
- Episode number and title
- Opening hook (first 30 seconds)
- Main content segments with key facts, anecdotes, and expert insights to incorporate
- Audience interaction points (Q&A prompts, call-in topics, listener challenges)
- Guest interview integration (when applicable: who, why, and key questions)
- Closing summary and call-to-action

**Standard Outro Template**
Craft a consistent sign-off that encourages subscription, social engagement, and teases the next episode.

Use clear hierarchical formatting (main points, sub-points, bullet details) suitable for reference during production. Ensure smooth narrative transitions between segments within each episode.
```
