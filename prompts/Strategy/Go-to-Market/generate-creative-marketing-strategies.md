# Guerrilla Marketing Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Guerrilla Marketing Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates unconventional, budget-conscious marketing campaigns for entrepreneurs and startups competing against well-funded rivals. This guerrilla marketing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your project brief and delivers five concrete campaign ideas, each with implementation steps, psychological rationale, expected impact, and a timeline. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured output that includes project positioning analysis, detailed creative concepts, and a prioritized quick-start guide. Use it when you need breakthrough marketing tactics that rely on creativity and authentic engagement rather than large ad budgets - perfect for product launches, lean startups, or campaigns where traditional marketing spend isn't an option. ● Produces five distinct marketing ideas optimized for minimal financial investment and maximum attention capture ● Includes specific implementation steps, psychological triggers, and expected outcomes for each concept ● Analyzes your project's unique positioning to identify leverage points competitors overlook ● Prioritizes the most impactful idea with concrete first actions you can execute immediately ## Prompt
