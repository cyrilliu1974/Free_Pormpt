# Exit Interview Template Generator for Four Scenarios

## 簡介

The Exit Interview Template Generator for Four Scenarios is a free AI prompt that designs structured exit feedback templates tailored to voluntary resignation, internal transfer, retirement, and performance-related departures for HR teams and organizational leaders. This exit interview prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies organizational psychology principles and survey methodology to produce question sequences that balance psychological safety with diagnostic precision. Each template combines Likert-scale rating questions with paired qualitative follow-ups, designed to surface the structural and relational factors behind turnover while respecting departing employees' comfort levels. Real use cases include HR departments building candid feedback loops, retention committees analyzing departure patterns, and People Operations teams seeking actionable insights from exit conversations. Reach for this prompt when you need scientifically grounded exit interview frameworks that distinguish systemic organizational issues from individual circumstances and generate retention priorities based on engagement research. ● Produces four scenario-specific templates (voluntary resignation, internal transfer, retirement, performance exit) with strategic question sequencing from warm-up to core issues to forward-looking themes. ● Combines scaled rating questions across dimensions like manager effectiveness, career development, workload, recognition, and mission alignment with open-ended prompts that capture the reasoning behind numerical scores. ● Includes implementation guidance covering timing, delivery method, anonymization options, and aggregation techniques to identify patterns versus outliers. ● Provides analysis frameworks that help organizations prioritize retention interventions by distinguishing individual circumstances from systemic organizational drivers of turnover. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in exit interview design. You apply employee engagement research and survey methodology to uncover the structural and relational factors driving turnover.

## Task
Create exit feedback templates for four departure scenarios: voluntary resignation, internal transfer, retirement, and performance-related exits. Each template must:

- Build psychological safety through strategic question sequencing (warm-up → core issues → forward-looking)
- Combine scaled rating questions with open-ended follow-ups that surface the reasoning behind scores
- Distinguish systemic organizational issues from individual circumstances
- Generate actionable retention insights while respecting departing employees' comfort levels

## Context
Departing employees often withhold candid feedback to avoid burning bridges. Design templates that balance honesty with professionalism, addressing:

- Day-to-day satisfaction and workload
- Manager effectiveness and team dynamics
- Career development and growth opportunities
- Alignment with organizational direction
- Recognition and trust

Incorporate engagement drivers—clarity of expectations, resources, development, recognition, voice, connection to mission—adapted for exit contexts.

{{business-context}}

## Output
For each of the four departure scenarios, provide:

1. **Template name and use case**
2. **Question sequence** with:
   - Scaled items (e.g., 1-5 Likert) and their dimensions
   - Paired qualitative prompts ("What drove that rating?")
3. **Implementation guidance**: timing, delivery method, anonymization options, and how to aggregate findings
4. **Analysis framework**: how to identify patterns vs. outliers and prioritize retention interventions

Format templates ready for immediate deployment via {{feedback-method}}.
```
