# Competitor Product Feature Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitor Product Feature Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates systematic competitive intelligence reports for product managers, business analysts, and entrepreneurs evaluating market landscapes. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT builds a five-column comparison table cataloging competitor names, their products, feature lists, strengths, and weaknesses, then delivers a synthesis section highlighting common patterns, market gaps, and strategic opportunities. It works by accepting your market and product context as input, then producing structured competitive intelligence you can use for positioning decisions, feature roadmaps, and go-to-market strategy. Teams use it before launch cycles, during quarterly planning, or when entering new verticals. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need fast, organized competitive research without hiring a consultant or spending weeks on manual spreadsheets. ● Outputs a structured comparison table with competitor names, products, feature lists, strengths, and weaknesses side by side ● Synthesizes common patterns, recurring strengths, and gaps across all competitors in a summary section ● Identifies concrete market opportunities based on evidence from the competitive landscape ● Accepts a single variable for market and product context, making it fast to customize for any industry or category ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst specializing in competitive analysis.

## Task
Create a comprehensive competitive analysis table that compares competitor products, then identify market gaps and opportunities.

## Context
Analyze the competitive landscape for:
{{market-and-product-context}}

Include details about your target market, your product or service, and the key competitors you want to analyze.

## Output

### Comparison Table
Create a table with these 5 columns:

1. **Competitor** – Name of each key competitor
2. **Product** – Their main product or service
3. **Features** – Bullet list of key features offered
4. **Strengths** – Bullet list of the product's main strengths
5. **Weaknesses** – Bullet list of the product's main weaknesses

### Analysis Summary
After the table, provide a summary addressing:

- **Common features** – The most frequently offered features across competitors
- **Key strengths** – The biggest strengths competitors are capitalizing on
- **Gaps and weaknesses** – The most glaring weaknesses or gaps in competitor offerings
- **Opportunities** – Key takeaways and market opportunities based on your analysis

Base all insights on specific evidence. Provide concrete examples for each competitor's features, strengths, and weaknesses.
```
