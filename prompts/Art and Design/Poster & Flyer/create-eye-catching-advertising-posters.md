# Advertising Poster With Product and Typography

## 簡介

The Advertising Poster With Product and Typography is a free AI prompt that creates modern advertising layouts featuring customizable headline text, a product held by a manicured hand, and scattered bold typography on a light cream background. This advertising poster prompt for Midjourney builds a 2:3 vertical composition with your primary slogan at the top, large bottom text, and a hero product - such as a milkshake, beverage, or cosmetic - held by a hand with manicured nails and gold rings. The prompt layers bold sans-serif fonts with decorative microcopy and feature headlines in a playful, asymmetric layout. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, making it ideal for social media ads, print campaigns, and product launches that need eye-catching visuals fast. Reach for this prompt when you need professional product photography styling combined with typographic design in a single image, without hiring a photographer or graphic designer. ● Customizable primary slogan, bottom text, and product description let you adapt the poster to any brand or campaign. ● Hand-held product styling with manicured nails and gold rings adds human warmth and luxury cues. ● Bold sans-serif typography scattered across the layout creates visual interest and guides the viewer's eye. ● Light cream background and playful asymmetric composition keep the design modern and approachable. ## Prompt

```
Advertising poster with headline text "{{primary-slogan}}" at top and large bottom text "{{bottom-text}}", hero product: {{product-description}} held by hand with manicured nails and gold rings, typography: bold sans-serif fonts scattered with decorative microcopy and feature headlines, light cream background, playful but modern asymmetric advertising layout --ar 2:3
```
