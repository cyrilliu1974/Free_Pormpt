# Video Content Strategy Planner for Business Goals

## 簡介

The Video Content Strategy Planner for Business Goals is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive video content roadmap for marketers, content creators, and business owners looking to align video output with strategic objectives. This video content planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, content topics, and audience profile to produce 5-8 fully conceptualized video ideas in a three-column markdown table. Each video concept includes an engaging title, the specific audience segment it targets, and the key takeaways viewers will gain. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it compatible with all major text-based AI models. Real use cases include planning YouTube series, mapping social media video campaigns, and designing educational or product marketing video sequences that speak directly to distinct customer personas. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from scattered ideas to a cohesive video strategy that ties content directly to business goals and audience needs. ● Produces 5-8 video concepts in a clean markdown table with title, audience, and takeaways for each ● Analyzes business type, content goals, brand voice, and audience demographics to tailor every video idea ● Helps segment content by audience, ensuring each video speaks to a specific viewer group ● Speeds up brainstorming and planning phases while maintaining strategic alignment across all video content ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional video content strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive video content creation plan that aligns with business goals and resonates with the target audience. Analyze the business context and content topic, identify key audience segments, brainstorm engaging video titles, and outline crucial takeaways for each video.

## Context
**Business & Content:** {{business-and-content-context}}
(Include: business type, primary content topic, specific content goals)

**Audience & Brand:** {{audience-and-brand}}
(Include: target audience demographics/psychographics, brand voice and tone)

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Video Title | Target Audience | Key Takeaways |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|

Each row should represent a unique video idea that aligns with the business type and content topic. Generate 5-8 video concepts that span different audience segments and content angles within the topic area.
```
