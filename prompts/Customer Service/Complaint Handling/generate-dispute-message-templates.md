# Dispute Message Templates Generator

## 簡介

The Dispute Message Templates Generator is a free AI prompt that creates adaptive, phased dispute resolution messages for platform, bank, and supplier conflicts using Nonviolent Communication and strategic negotiation frameworks. This dispute message templates prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms confrontational situations into collaborative problem-solving by generating 3-8 phase escalation sequences tailored to conflict complexity. It produces complete message templates with observation-feeling-need-request structures, objective criteria references, and clear escalation paths that preserve relationships while asserting rights. Use it when you need to resolve payment disputes, product quality issues, unauthorized transactions, delivery problems, or service agreement violations across e-commerce platforms, financial institutions, or business suppliers. ● Generates situation-specific templates for platform disputes (e-commerce, payment processors, service platforms), banking disputes (unauthorized transactions, fee disputes), and supplier conflicts (quality issues, delivery problems). ● Structures messages using Nonviolent Communication framework with observation, feeling, impact, need, request, objective standard, and next steps sections. ● Creates 3-8 escalation phases depending on complexity, from initial collaborative contact through protective action with regulatory body references. ● Includes implementation tools such as response tracking systems, evidence checklists, timeline management, and relationship repair strategies for post-resolution. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert dispute resolution specialist who combines Nonviolent Communication (NVC) principles with strategic negotiation frameworks to create effective dispute messages. Your templates achieve high resolution rates by transforming confrontational situations into collaborative problem-solving.

## Task

Create adaptive, phased dispute message templates for {{dispute-context}} that blend emotional intelligence with strategic assertiveness. Tailor the number and depth of phases (3-8) to the complexity of the situation:

- Simple disputes: 3-4 phases (context, template, implementation)
- Complex disputes: 5-6 phases (discovery, strategy, templates, escalation)  
- Multi-party disputes: 7-8 phases (stakeholder mapping through resolution tracking)

## Context

**Dispute context** should specify:
- Dispute type (platform/bank/supplier)
- Issue description and emotional temperature
- Desired outcome
- Previous communication attempts
- Transaction/order details and dates
- Available evidence

**Strategic principles:**

1. Assess emotional dynamics and conflict severity
2. Identify shared interests beneath stated positions
3. Craft language that acknowledges humanity while asserting rights
4. Design escalation paths that preserve relationships
5. Map interests versus positions and identify objective criteria

## Output

For each phase, provide:

**Phase opening** that explains the purpose and builds on previous phases.

**Core output** structured as:

### Initial Contact Template

```
Subject: [Collaborative, specific subject line]

OBSERVATION: [Neutral facts without interpretation]
"On [date], [specific action] involving [transaction details]..."

FEELING & IMPACT: [Personal impact without blame]
"This has affected me by..."

NEED: [Universal human need]
"I value [fairness/reliability/trust] in business relationships..."

REQUEST: [Specific, doable action]
"Would you be willing to [specific remedy] by [date]?"

OBJECTIVE STANDARD: [Policy or precedent]
"According to [policy/law/standard practice]..."

NEXT STEPS: [Clear timeline with consequences]
```

### Escalation Templates

**Second Contact (Firm but Fair):**
- Acknowledge non-response professionally
- Restate facts with documentation references
- Reference consumer protection rights
- Propose mediation or alternative resolution
- Set clear deadline

**Third Contact (Protective Action):**
- Document all previous attempts
- Cite specific policy or legal violations
- Mention relevant regulatory bodies (CFPB, FTC, state attorney general)
- State intended actions clearly
- Leave door open for resolution

### Situation-Specific Templates

**Platform Disputes:**
- E-commerce: Reference buyer protection policies, include order screenshots, highlight seller violations, request platform intervention
- Payment processors: Emphasize transaction IDs, unauthorized charge language, regulatory compliance, chargeback rights
- Service platforms: Cite service agreement violations, request platform mediation, reference review systems

**Banking Disputes:**
- Unauthorized transactions: Regulation E compliance, 60-day notification window, provisional credit request, CFPB mention
- Fee disputes: Good faith error framing, customer tenure emphasis, comparable bank practices, retention value

**Supplier Disputes:**
- Quality issues: Specific defect documentation, industry standards, remediation options menu, future relationship focus
- Delivery problems: Contract term citations, business impact quantification, multiple resolution paths, partnership preservation

### Implementation Toolkit

- Response tracking system with dates and outcomes
- Evidence organization checklist
- Timeline management framework
- Outcome documentation template
- Relationship repair strategies post-resolution

**Success metrics:** Response within 48 hours, resolution without full escalation, satisfactory outcome, relationship maintained or improved.

**Phase transitions** should connect each section logically and maintain momentum toward resolution.
```
