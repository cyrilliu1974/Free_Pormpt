# Homeschool Economics Lesson Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Homeschool Economics Lesson Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured financial literacy and economics curricula for homeschooling families. This economics lesson plan prompt for ChatGPT produces 5-8 complete lessons tailored to your student's age range and learning goals, each with clear objectives, hands-on activities, and real-world applications. It also generates a reference table of 6-10 fundamental financial concepts - budgeting, interest, inflation, supply and demand, opportunity cost, and more - with definitions and everyday examples. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming complex economic ideas into digestible, age-appropriate instruction that works in homeschool environments. Reach for this prompt when you need to teach money management, market principles, or entrepreneurship to elementary through high-school-aged learners without a pre-packaged curriculum. ● Produces 5-8 complete lessons with titles, objectives, activities, and real-world connections ● Generates a reference table of 6-10 financial concepts with plain-language definitions and daily-life examples ● Adapts content complexity to match student age ranges from elementary through high school ● Emphasizes experiential learning through hands-on exercises children can complete independently or with a parent ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert educator specializing in age-appropriate financial literacy and economics curriculum for homeschoolers.

## Task

Create a comprehensive guide for teaching financial literacy and basic economics to homeschooled children. Break down complex concepts into digestible lessons with hands-on activities and real-world applications.

## Context

{{student-age-range}} (e.g., "ages 6-10, elementary level" or "ages 14-18, high school level")

{{learning-goals}} (e.g., "understanding budgeting and saving," "grasping supply and demand," "learning about entrepreneurship and investing")

## Output

Structure the guide as follows:

### Lesson Sequence

Provide 5-8 numbered lessons, each containing:
- **Lesson Title**
- **Age Range** (aligned with the student profile)
- **Lesson Objective** (clear learning outcome)
- **Activity Description** (hands-on exercise the child can complete)
- **Real-World Application** (how this concept appears in daily life)

### Key Financial Concepts Reference

Include a table with these columns:

| Concept | Definition | Real-World Application |
|---------|------------|------------------------|

Cover 6-10 fundamental concepts such as budgeting, interest, inflation, supply and demand, opportunity cost, or others appropriate to the age range and learning goals provided.
```
