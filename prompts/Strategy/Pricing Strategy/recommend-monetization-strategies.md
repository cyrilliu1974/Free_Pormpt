# Digital Product Monetization Strategy Comparison

## 簡介

The Digital Product Monetization Strategy Comparison is a free AI prompt that evaluates and scores revenue model options for digital product founders, SaaS builders, and business strategists. This monetization strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured markdown table comparing one-time purchases, subscription tiers, freemium, licensing, and hybrid models. It assesses each against your specific product type and business context - target market, audience budget, competitive dynamics, and growth goals - then assigns a best-fit score from 1 to 10. The output includes realistic pricing ranges drawn from market standards, concrete examples from successful businesses in similar niches, and a pros-and-cons breakdown that weighs customer lifetime value, market saturation, and scalability. Reach for this prompt when launching a new digital product, pivoting an existing revenue model, or pitching investors on pricing strategy. ● Compares multiple revenue models - subscription, freemium, one-time, licensing, and hybrid - in a single table. ● Scores each model's alignment with your product type, target audience budget, and competitive environment. ● Includes realistic pricing ranges and concrete examples from businesses in similar spaces. ● Orders recommendations by best-fit score, making the optimal strategy immediately clear. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital business strategist specializing in revenue model design for digital products. You analyze market dynamics, customer psychology, and competitive positioning to recommend viable monetization approaches.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive comparison of monetization strategies tailored to the provided digital product. Evaluate multiple revenue models—one-time purchases, subscription tiers, freemium, licensing, and hybrid approaches—against the specific business context. For each model, assess:

- Pros and cons
- Realistic pricing ranges based on market standards
- Concrete examples from successful businesses in similar spaces
- Fit for the stated business goals, target market economics, and competitive environment

Consider customer lifetime value, market saturation, and scalability when scoring each option.

## Context
**Digital product:** {{product-type}}

**Business context:** {{business-context}}  
(Include target niche/market, primary business goals, audience budget range, and key competitive dynamics or market conditions)

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:

| Model Type | Pros | Cons | Pricing Range | Real Examples | Best Fit Score (1-10) |

Score each model's alignment with the specific product and business goals provided. Order rows from highest to lowest Best Fit Score.
```
