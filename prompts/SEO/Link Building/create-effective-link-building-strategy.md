# Link Building Outreach Strategy Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Link Building Outreach Strategy Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive, actionable link building campaign tailored to your website and business context. This link building prompt for ChatGPT produces a six-part implementation guide that walks you through every stage of the outreach process: identifying and prioritizing high-value prospects in your niche, crafting personalized email templates with compelling subject lines and clear CTAs, structuring follow-up timelines, building long-term relationships with link partners, and tracking response rates and acquired links. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your website URL and business context into a ready-to-execute outreach campaign with email templates, tracking spreadsheets, and measurement frameworks. SEO professionals use it to streamline link acquisition campaigns, digital marketers rely on it to scale outreach without losing personalization, and content strategists turn to it when launching backlink campaigns for new sites or content hubs. ● Produces prioritized prospect lists with domain authority and relevance scoring ● Includes initial outreach and follow-up email templates with personalization guidelines ● Provides a timeline and tracking system for managing multi-stage outreach campaigns ● Delivers measurable KPIs including response rates, conversion rates, and link quality analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in link building outreach.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide for conducting effective link building outreach for the specified website. The guide must be actionable and cover the full lifecycle from prospecting through measurement.

## Context
- Website URL: {{website-url}}
- Business context: {{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver the guide using this six-part structure:

**1. Research and Prospecting**
- Identify relevant websites and influencers in the niche
- Analyze their content and audience engagement
- Prioritize prospects based on domain authority and relevance
- Create a spreadsheet to track prospects and outreach status

**2. Personalized Outreach**
- Craft compelling subject lines for each prospect
- Personalize email content based on the prospect's content and style
- Highlight the value proposition for the prospect
- Include a clear and specific call-to-action (CTA)

**3. Email Templates**
- Provide an initial outreach template and a follow-up template
- Each template should include: personalized subject line, opening line, value proposition, specific request or CTA, and closing with signature

**4. Follow-up Strategy**
- Outline the timeline for sending initial outreach email and follow-ups
- Provide guidance on updating outreach status in the tracking spreadsheet
- Analyze response rates and adjust templates and strategy as needed

**5. Relationship Building**
- Engage with the prospect's content (comments, shares, etc.)
- Provide additional value through relevant resources or insights
- Maintain communication and nurture the relationship over time
- Explore opportunities for collaboration or partnerships

**6. Measuring Success**
- Track key metrics: response rates, conversion rates, and acquired links
- Analyze the quality and relevance of acquired links
- Monitor website traffic and search engine rankings
- Continuously refine and optimize the outreach strategy based on data insights

Format each section with actionable bullet points that can be immediately implemented.
```
