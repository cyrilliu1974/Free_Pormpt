# Social Media Advertising Campaign Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Advertising Campaign Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops end-to-end advertising strategies to drive online sales for digital marketers and business owners. This social media advertising prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your product, target audience, budget, and chosen platforms to deliver a two-part strategy: a summary table covering campaign objectives, audience insights, and key metrics, plus a detailed execution plan with platform rationale, creative direction, content types, targeting tactics, budget allocation, and optimization recommendations. Marketing teams use it to launch new product campaigns, e-commerce brands rely on it to scale paid social efforts, and agencies apply it to structure client strategies with clear conversion goals and measurement frameworks. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured advertising strategy that moves beyond ad copy into full campaign architecture - audience segmentation, platform fit, and performance tracking included. ● Produces a strategy summary table with campaign objectives, audience insights, and success KPIs in markdown format ● Delivers platform-specific rationale explaining why each channel fits your target audience and product ● Designs creative strategy, messaging approach, and content formats tailored to conversion goals ● Provides budget allocation guidance, audience segmentation tactics, and a measurement framework for ongoing optimization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in social media advertising campaigns that drive online sales.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media advertising campaign strategy that outlines platform selection, creative approach, content types, and measurement frameworks to convert potential customers and drive revenue.

## Context
Product or service: {{product-or-service}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Budget and duration: {{budget-and-duration}}
Platform focus: {{platforms}}

Analyze why the specified platforms are optimal for reaching this audience, then build campaign objectives aligned to sales goals. Design creative strategies and content types that will engage and convert. Structure your recommendations with clear logical flow, ensuring each element builds toward measurable outcomes.

## Output
Deliver your strategy in two parts:

**Part 1: Strategy Summary Table (markdown)**

| Campaign Objectives | Target Audience Insights | Key Metrics |
|---------------------|-------------------------|-------------|
| [objectives here] | [audience analysis] | [success KPIs] |

**Part 2: Detailed Strategy Execution**

Provide a structured breakdown covering:

- Platform rationale and audience fit
- Campaign objectives with specific conversion goals
- Creative strategy and messaging approach
- Content types and formats for each platform
- Audience targeting and segmentation tactics
- Budget allocation recommendations
- Success metrics and measurement framework
- Optimization recommendations for ongoing performance improvement
```
