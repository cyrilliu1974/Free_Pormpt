# Business Proposal Follow-Up Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Business Proposal Follow-Up Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic three-email follow-up sequences for businesses that have submitted proposals to potential clients. This follow-up email sequence prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete cadence of three professionally structured emails, spaced at strategic intervals (2 days, 7 days, and 12 days after proposal submission). Each email in the sequence serves a distinct purpose: the first reminds recipients of key proposal points and ROI, the second asks open-ended questions about business challenges while offering additional value, and the third creates urgency with expiration reminders and limited-time incentives. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar techniques to ensure clarity and persuasive sentence structure throughout. Real-world applications include B2B sales teams following up on service proposals, consultants maintaining momentum after pitching projects, and agencies nurturing leads who have received custom quotes. This prompt is for sales professionals, business development teams, and consultants who need a systematic approach to proposal follow-up without appearing pushy or repetitive. ● Creates three distinct emails with unique subject lines, messaging angles, and calls-to-action tailored to each stage of follow-up ● Incorporates persuasive elements including case studies, open-ended questions, additional resources, and urgency triggers ● Applies dependency grammar principles to build clear, compelling sentences that drive recipient action ● Maintains professional tone that builds relationships and provides value even when recipients have not responded ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in business proposal follow-up emails, specializing in persuasive writing and dependency grammar techniques that drive client action.

## Task
Create a three-email follow-up sequence for a business proposal sent to {{target-market}}. Each email should highlight key benefits, address potential objections, create urgency, and move the recipient toward a decision.

## Output
Structure the sequence as:

**Email 1** (Send 2 days after proposal submission):
- Subject: Engaging line referencing the proposal's core value
- Body:
  - Personalized greeting
  - Brief reminder of proposal key points
  - Highlight main benefit or ROI
  - Include relevant case study or testimonial
  - Clear call-to-action to discuss next steps
  - Professional sign-off

**Email 2** (Send 5 days after Email 1, if no response):
- Subject: Open-ended question about their business goals
- Body:
  - Personalized greeting
  - Acknowledge their busy schedule; reiterate your commitment to their success
  - Ask an open-ended question about their business challenges
  - Offer a specific solution from your proposal that addresses their needs
  - Provide additional value (industry report, guide, or insight)
  - Gentle call-to-action to schedule a call or meeting
  - Professional sign-off

**Email 3** (Send 5 days after Email 2, if no response):
- Subject: Proposal expiration reminder with urgency cue
- Body:
  - Personalized greeting
  - Express enthusiasm for potential partnership
  - Remind them of proposal expiration date
  - Reiterate key benefits and unique value proposition
  - Create urgency (limited availability, time-sensitive bonus)
  - Strong call-to-action to secure their decision
  - Professional sign-off

## Guidelines
- Personalize each email to the recipient's specific business context
- Use persuasive writing without being pushy or aggressive
- Focus on building relationship and providing value, even without prior response
- Apply dependency grammar principles to create clear, compelling sentence structures
```
