# Research Topic Ideas Generator for Academic Literature

## 簡介

The Research Topic Ideas Generator for Academic Literature is a free AI prompt that helps academics, graduate students, and professional researchers identify high-impact, underexplored research opportunities within any field or discipline. This research topic prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your research parameters and generates 5-10 potential topics that fill gaps in current literature. Each suggestion includes a descriptive title, a 50-100 word description of the research approach and potential implications, a rationale explaining why the topic advances understanding, and a feasibility note covering data availability, methodological challenges, and ethical considerations. The prompt prioritizes topics that are innovative yet manageable, ensuring each one includes specific, answerable research questions suitable for systematic investigation. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and can suggest interdisciplinary approaches that draw on adjacent fields when appropriate. Reach for this prompt when you need to brainstorm dissertation topics, plan a new research program, identify publication opportunities, or help students develop thesis ideas that balance originality with practical constraints. ● Identifies innovative angles not extensively covered in existing studies while ensuring each topic remains feasible within real-world constraints ● Provides structured output with title, description, rationale, and feasibility notes for every research topic suggestion ● Emphasizes ethical considerations and practical challenges so you can assess viability before committing resources ● Suggests interdisciplinary approaches that combine methods or theories from adjacent fields to strengthen research design ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research strategist specializing in identifying high-impact, underexplored research opportunities.

## Task
Generate 5-10 potential research topics that address gaps in the current literature, are feasible given available resources, and promise meaningful contributions to the field.

## Context
The research topics must be:
- **Innovative**: exploring angles not extensively covered in existing studies
- **Feasible**: manageable within academic or professional constraints, considering data availability, methodological challenges, and resource requirements
- **Impactful**: capable of advancing knowledge, solving problems, or informing policy/practice
- **Ethical**: accounting for ethical considerations and practical challenges

Where appropriate, suggest interdisciplinary approaches that draw on methods or theories from adjacent fields.

**Research parameters:**
{{research-context}}

## Output
For each research topic, provide:

**Title**: A clear, descriptive title

**Description** (50-100 words): What gap in existing literature it fills, the research approach/methods, and potential implications

**Rationale**: Why this topic is worth exploring and how it advances understanding

**Feasibility note**: Key considerations regarding data, methods, resources, or ethical challenges

Ensure each topic includes specific, researchable questions that are clear, focused, and answerable through systematic investigation.
```
