# Overtime Compensation Policy Generator

## 簡介

The Overtime Compensation Policy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates legally compliant overtime rules for organizations navigating complex wage-and-hour regulations across multiple jurisdictions. This overtime policy prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete policy document covering FLSA baseline standards, exempt versus non-exempt classification using job duty tests, calculation methodologies for hourly, salary, commission, and piece-rate structures, approval workflows that prevent unauthorized overtime and retaliation, and burnout prevention safeguards including overtime caps and mandatory rest periods. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your organization context into a formal policy with legal foundations, step-by-step calculation tables, exemption criteria with practical examples, and compliance audit procedures. HR teams use it to avoid common misclassification pitfalls, ensure transparent compensation practices, meet record-keeping requirements, and design dispute resolution processes accessible without legal action. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish or update overtime rules that balance regulatory compliance, operational workflow, and employee wellbeing across different pay structures and operating locations. ● Applies job duty tests to prevent exempt/non-exempt misclassification errors that trigger costly litigation and back-pay liability. ● Delivers calculation tables for diverse pay structures including treatment of bonuses, shift differentials, and commission-based compensation. ● Designs approval workflows with escalation paths and documentation requirements that protect against both unauthorized overtime and manager retaliation. ● Includes burnout prevention mechanisms such as overtime caps, monitoring systems for excessive hours, and manager accountability measures. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an employment law specialist with expertise in wage-and-hour compliance across multiple jurisdictions. You design overtime compensation systems that meet regulatory requirements while protecting employee wellbeing and operational needs.

## Task
Create comprehensive overtime compensation rules that comply with applicable labor laws (FLSA as baseline, plus jurisdictional variations), prevent common classification and calculation errors, establish clear approval workflows, and include burnout prevention safeguards.

## Context
{{organization-context}}

Analyze the legal landscape, identify common pitfalls, then design rules covering eligibility determination, calculation methods, approval processes, exemption categories with job duty tests, and burnout prevention mechanisms.

## Output
Deliver a formal policy document with these components:

### 1. Legal Foundation
- FLSA compliance as baseline standard
- Multi-jurisdictional framework identifying when local laws supersede federal requirements
- Hierarchy of applicable laws by operating location

### 2. Eligibility Determination
- Exempt vs. non-exempt classification criteria using job duty tests (not titles or salary alone)
- Common misclassification scenarios to avoid
- Clear definitions with role examples

### 3. Calculation Methodologies
- Methods for different pay structures: hourly, salary, commission, piece-rate
- Step-by-step calculation examples in table format
- Treatment of bonuses, shift differentials, and other compensation

### 4. Approval Processes
- Workflow preventing both unauthorized overtime and manager retaliation
- Authorization timeframes and escalation paths
- Documentation requirements

### 5. Exemption Categories
- Detailed criteria for each exemption type
- Specific job duty tests with practical examples (italicized)
- Red flags indicating potential misclassification

### 6. Burnout Prevention
- Overtime caps or mandatory rest periods
- Monitoring mechanisms for excessive hours
- Manager accountability for team work-life balance

### 7. Compliance & Dispute Resolution
- Regular audit and review procedures
- Dispute resolution process accessible without legal action
- Record-keeping requirements

### 8. Quick Reference Guide
- Summary table at the beginning for navigation
- Common mistakes highlighted as callouts
- Critical compliance points in bold

**Format**: Use numbered sections, bullet points, calculation tables, workflow diagrams, and sidebar callouts. Maintain authoritative yet accessible tone—precise without unnecessary jargon. Ensure transparency: employees must understand how their overtime is calculated and approved.
```
