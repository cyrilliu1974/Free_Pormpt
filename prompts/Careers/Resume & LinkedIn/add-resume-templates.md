# Resume Template Builder for Career Profiles

## 簡介

The Resume Template Builder for Career Profiles is a free AI prompt that creates professional resume templates optimized for both human readers and applicant tracking systems. This resume template prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your career profile - including level, industry, skills, and achievements - to generate a complete, formatted resume structure that balances visual appeal with ATS compatibility. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing templates with clear section headings, strategic white space, and achievement-focused bullet points. Job seekers use it to organize professional summaries, work experience, education, and skills in formats that match current hiring trends for their target industry, from entry-level positions to executive roles. Reach for this prompt when you need a resume foundation that highlights quantified achievements and maintains consistent typography, not just a list of duties. ● Structures content with professional summary, experience, skills, education, and achievements sections in scannable bullet-point format. ● Analyzes industry hiring trends and formatting conventions to match employer expectations for your target role. ● Maintains optimal content-to-white-space ratio and consistent typography for readability by both humans and ATS software. ● Provides formatting notes in brackets to guide layout decisions and visual hierarchy choices. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert resume designer specializing in professional templates that balance visual appeal with ATS compatibility.

## Task
Create a complete resume template tailored to the specified career profile. The template must effectively showcase education, skills, achievements, and experience through clear hierarchy and strategic white space.

## Context
Career profile:
{{career-profile}}

Include:
- Career level and target industry
- Key technical and soft skills
- Most significant achievements (quantified where possible)
- Education background and relevant certifications

## Requirements
- Analyze current hiring trends and formatting conventions for the specified industry and level
- Structure content with clear section headings (e.g., Professional Summary, Experience, Skills, Education, Achievements)
- Use bullet points for scannable presentation of responsibilities and accomplishments
- Maintain 1:1.5 content-to-white-space ratio for readability
- Prioritize achievement-oriented language over duty descriptions
- Ensure consistent typography hierarchy and alignment

## Output
Deliver the resume template as a formatted document with:
- Section headings in **bold**
- Subsections (job titles, degrees) in appropriate hierarchy
- Bullet points (•) for listed items
- Clear visual separation between sections
- Brief formatting notes in [brackets] where layout choices require explanation
```
