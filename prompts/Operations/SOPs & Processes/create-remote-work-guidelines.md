# Remote Work-Life Boundary Guidelines Generator

## 簡介

The Remote Work-Life Boundary Guidelines Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized work-from-home guidelines for remote workers struggling to maintain professional and personal boundaries. This remote work boundary prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured framework organized into five distinct categories - Physical Space, Time Management, Communication, and two additional categories chosen based on your specific situation. It adapts recommendations to your experience level (beginner through advanced), your biggest work-life balance challenge, and your current work setup. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it produces at least three practical, actionable guidelines per category rather than generic advice. Remote workers use it to establish clear desk boundaries in shared living spaces, build sustainable daily routines, set communication expectations with colleagues, and address context-specific challenges like managing childcare or overcoming isolation. ● Tailors all five guideline categories to your declared experience level, primary challenge, and physical work setup ● Delivers specific actions instead of vague productivity tips - real steps you can implement today ● Adapts the final two categories dynamically based on your context (mental health, technology use, family coordination, etc.) ● Structures output for immediate reference and sharing with household members or team leads ## Prompt
