# Blog Post Idea Generator for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Blog Post Idea Generator for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that produces targeted, actionable blog post concepts tailored to your niche and audience. This blog post idea prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current trends, audience pain points, and fresh angles on familiar topics to deliver 8–12 ready-to-use concepts. Each idea includes a compelling working title and a one-sentence hook that explains its unique value. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for any text-generation workflow. Content teams use it to break through writer's block, align editorial calendars with audience interests, and maintain a steady pipeline of engaging topics. It is built for content strategists, bloggers, and marketing teams who need a structured approach to ideation that ties directly to business goals and reader retention. ● Delivers 8–12 ideas per run, each with a working title and hook sentence that clarifies the angle ● Analyzes niche trends and audience pain points to ensure relevance and engagement ● Adapts to any content strategy input, from SEO-focused campaigns to thought-leadership goals ● Provides a bullet-point format that integrates directly into editorial calendars and pitch decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in audience-driven blog ideation.

## Task
Generate a list of engaging, specific blog post ideas that attract and retain the target audience. Analyze current trends, audience pain points, and fresh angles on familiar topics. Each idea should be actionable and aligned with the blog's strategic goals.

## Context
{{niche-and-audience}}

{{content-strategy}}

## Output
Deliver 8–12 blog post ideas as a bullet point list. Each bullet should include:
- A compelling working title
- A one-sentence hook explaining the unique angle or value

Format:
• **Title**: Hook sentence
• **Title**: Hook sentence
```
