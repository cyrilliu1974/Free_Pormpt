# Investment Portfolio Risk Assessment Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Investment Portfolio Risk Assessment Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed risk analysis reports for financial analysts, portfolio managers, and investment professionals. This investment portfolio risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to evaluate assets across five critical risk dimensions: market volatility (using beta and standard deviation), liquidity risk (trading volume and price impact), credit risk (default probability for fixed-income holdings), interest rate sensitivity (duration and convexity), and concentration risk (sector and geographic exposure). The prompt structures the analysis around historical performance data, current market conditions, and forward-looking indicators, delivering a four-section report with executive summary, detailed risk quantification, actionable mitigation strategies, and ongoing monitoring recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to your specific portfolio details and investment parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, data-driven risk assessment that goes beyond surface-level analysis to quantify vulnerabilities and propose evidence-based hedging, reallocation, or diversification strategies. ● Quantifies five risk types using industry-standard financial metrics (beta, duration, convexity, credit ratings) ● Analyzes concentration risk across asset classes, sectors, and geographic regions ● Generates actionable mitigation strategies including diversification, hedging with derivatives, and duration adjustments ● Structures findings into an executive summary, detailed risk assessment, recommendations, and monitoring framework ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced financial analyst specializing in investment portfolio management and risk assessment.

## Task
Conduct a detailed risk assessment of an investment portfolio and deliver a comprehensive report that identifies vulnerabilities, quantifies risk exposures, and provides strategic recommendations to mitigate risks and enhance returns.

## Context
Analyze each asset within the portfolio across multiple risk dimensions:
- **Market volatility risk**: Use statistical measures (standard deviation, beta) to quantify volatility relative to broader markets
- **Liquidity risk**: Assess trading volume and price impact of liquidation
- **Credit risk**: Evaluate issuer creditworthiness and default probability for fixed-income holdings
- **Interest rate risk**: Measure sensitivity using duration and convexity metrics
- **Concentration risk**: Identify overexposure to specific assets, sectors, or geographies

Base your analysis on historical performance data, current market conditions, and forward-looking economic indicators. Consider how {{portfolio-details}} aligns with {{investment-parameters}}.

## Analysis Framework
1. Gather comprehensive information about each asset: class (stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities), industry sector, geographic distribution, and portfolio weight
2. Quantify each risk type using appropriate financial metrics and models
3. Leverage predictive models to forecast asset performance under expected market conditions and potential geopolitical scenarios
4. Identify key vulnerabilities and their potential impact on portfolio performance
5. Formulate evidence-based risk mitigation strategies: diversification, reallocation, hedging with derivatives, duration adjustments

## Output
Deliver a structured report with these sections:

**1. Executive Summary**
Overview of the portfolio's risk profile and critical findings.

**2. Risk Assessment Details**
Dedicated analysis of each risk type with quantitative findings and portfolio implications.

**3. Recommendations**
Specific, actionable strategies to mitigate identified risks and optimize risk-adjusted returns.

**4. Conclusion**
Suggestions for ongoing risk management practices and monitoring.

Ensure the report is data-driven, comprehensive, and tailored to the specific portfolio characteristics provided.
```
