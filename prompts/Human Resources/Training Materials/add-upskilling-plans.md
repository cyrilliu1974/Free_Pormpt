# Employee Upskilling Plan Generator for Career Growth

## 簡介

The Employee Upskilling Plan Generator for Career Growth is a free AI prompt that creates personalized professional development strategies for career development specialists, HR professionals, and managers. This upskilling plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes an employee's current skill set, identifies gaps, and builds a complete growth roadmap with specific goals, milestone dates, and curated learning resources including courses, workshops, mentorship programs, and on-the-job training opportunities. It balances immediate skill needs with long-term career progression and includes measurable success metrics and review checkpoints. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when onboarding new team members, during performance reviews, after role changes, or whenever structured professional development planning is needed. ● Performs a current-state skill assessment that identifies strengths and improvement areas based on the employee profile you provide. ● Sets both short-term objectives for 3-6 months and long-term goals spanning 1-3 years, aligned with career aspirations. ● Builds a milestone-driven timeline with specific target dates and checkpoint intervals for reviewing progress. ● Recommends concrete learning resources including certifications, workshops, mentorship matches, and practical on-the-job training activities. ● Defines success metrics, KPIs, and review cadences so you can measure progress and adapt the plan as circumstances change. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career development specialist creating a personalized upskilling plan.

## Task
Design a comprehensive professional growth strategy that:
- Analyzes current skills and identifies improvement areas
- Outlines specific, achievable goals aligned with career aspirations
- Develops a detailed timeline with milestones and checkpoints
- Recommends relevant resources (courses, workshops, mentorship programs, on-the-job training)
- Balances short-term skill acquisition with long-term career progression
- Provides methods to measure progress and adapt the plan

## Context
{{employee-profile}}

## Output
Structure your upskilling plan with these sections:

**Current State Assessment**
- Skill inventory and gaps

**Goals**
- Short-term objectives (3-6 months)
- Long-term objectives (1-3 years)

**Timeline**
- Milestones with target dates
- Key checkpoints for review

**Resources**
- Recommended courses and certifications
- Workshops and training programs
- Mentorship opportunities
- On-the-job learning activities

**Progress Measurement**
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Review cadence
- Adjustment triggers

Use bullet points and numbered lists for clarity.
```
