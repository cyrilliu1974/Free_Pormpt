# Product Performance Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Product Performance Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers structured comparative evaluations of two products with market metrics, customer adoption data, revenue analysis, and SWOT frameworks for product strategists and business analysts. This product performance analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a systematic evaluation covering key performance indicators, market position, customer adoption rates, revenue growth trajectories, and SWOT analysis for each product. It produces an executive summary, detailed product profiles with metrics and insights, a side-by-side comparative analysis, and actionable strategic recommendations with next steps. Teams use it to inform product roadmap decisions, prioritize feature investments, identify competitive positioning opportunities, and justify budget allocations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting any two products and flexible data sources as inputs. Reach for this prompt when comparing product lines, evaluating market fit, preparing quarterly business reviews, or building investment cases that require data-driven product assessments. ● Compares two products across market performance, customer adoption, revenue growth, and competitive positioning ● Applies SWOT analysis to surface strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats for each product ● Structures findings into executive summary, detailed metrics, comparative analysis, and strategic recommendations ● Cites all data sources explicitly to ensure transparency and validate conclusions ## Prompt

```

Deliver actionable insights and strategic recommendations based on the analysis.
