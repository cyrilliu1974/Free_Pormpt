# Minimalist Sofa Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimalist Sofa Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates back-angle editorial photographs of sofas with professional lighting and composition for furniture brands, interior designers, and e-commerce sellers. This sofa photography prompt for ChatGPT (Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models) produces three-quarter rear perspective shots of sofas against plain walls, with afternoon sunlight casting soft shadows that add depth and a calm, sophisticated mood. The prompt specifies full-frame camera settings at f/2.8, 85mm, ISO 400 to emphasize upholstery detail, fabric texture, and the interplay of shadow and light. Use it to create product catalog images, editorial spreads, or e-commerce listings that highlight clean lines, negative space, and material quality. Reach for this prompt when you need furniture product photography that balances technical precision with editorial aesthetic, or when showcasing minimalist design requires attention to natural light and shadow. ● Produces back-angle views with three-quarter rear perspective and slightly elevated camera position for editorial composition. ● Specifies afternoon natural light from large windows to create soft shadows and sophisticated mood across upholstery. ● Controls technical parameters (f/2.8, 85mm, ISO 400) to emphasize fabric detail, seams, and texture. ● Delivers clean lines, negative space, and professional product photography framing ready for catalogs or marketing. ## Prompt

```
Back-angle photograph of a {{sofa-description}} sofa set against a plain wall as sunlight from a large window falls across it; soft shadows add depth and a calm, sophisticated mood. Captured from three-quarter rear using a full-frame camera at f/2.8, 85mm, ISO 400; emphasis on upholstery detail and the interplay of shadow and light. ```
```
