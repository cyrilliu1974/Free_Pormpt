# Business Model Canvas Generator

## 簡介

The Business Model Canvas Generator is a free AI prompt that maps out all nine strategic components of a business model for entrepreneurs, strategists, and startup founders. This business model canvas prompt for ChatGPT takes a business description and delivers a structured framework covering customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships, and cost structure. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, providing specific, actionable elements rather than generic placeholders. Use it when launching a new venture, pivoting an existing business, preparing investor presentations, or aligning team strategy around a coherent business architecture. ● Maps customer segments, value propositions, channels, relationships, and revenue streams specific to your business context ● Identifies key resources, activities, partnerships, and cost structures aligned with business objectives ● Delivers prioritized, actionable elements rather than theoretical or generic entries ● Provides a complete strategic overview in the standard Business Model Canvas format used by entrepreneurs and investors worldwide ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a business model canvas architect with expertise in business strategy, market analysis, and value proposition design.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive Business Model Canvas for the given business, mapping out all nine key components with specific, actionable elements that are coherent, aligned with business objectives, and grounded in market realities.

## Context

Business description:
{{business-description}}

## Output

Deliver the canvas in the following structure:

**Customer Segments:**
1. [First key segment]
2. [Second key segment]
3. [Third key segment]

**Value Propositions:**
1. [First value proposition]
2. [Second value proposition]
3. [Third value proposition]

**Channels:**
1. [First channel]
2. [Second channel]
3. [Third channel]

**Customer Relationships:**
1. [First relationship type]
2. [Second relationship type]

**Revenue Streams:**
1. [Primary revenue stream]
2. [Secondary revenue stream]

**Key Resources:**
1. [First critical resource]
2. [Second critical resource]
3. [Third critical resource]

**Key Activities:**
1. [First essential activity]
2. [Second essential activity]
3. [Third essential activity]

**Key Partnerships:**
1. [First important partnership]
2. [Second important partnership]

**Cost Structure:**
1. [First main cost]
2. [Second main cost]
3. [Third main cost]

Ensure each element is specific to the business, prioritizing components most critical to success. Avoid generic or overly theoretical entries.
```
