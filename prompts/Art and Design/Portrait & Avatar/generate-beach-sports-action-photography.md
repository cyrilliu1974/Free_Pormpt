# Beach Sports Action Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Beach Sports Action Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic golden-hour images of two athletes competing barefoot on beach sand at sunset. The prompt produces editorial-quality sports lifestyle photography with dramatic lighting, long shadows across textured sand, frozen sand particles mid-air, and a glowing horizon backdrop. This beach sports action photography prompt for Midjourney runs on image models like Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, using advanced camera simulation (28mm wide-angle, 1/2000s shutter speed, shallow depth of field) to capture intense yet playful moments between two players. Variables let you specify the sport (volleyball, frisbee, soccer) and player descriptions (age, gender, build, clothing) to tailor the scene.
