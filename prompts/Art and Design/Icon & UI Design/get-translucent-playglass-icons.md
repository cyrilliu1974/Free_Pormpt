# Translucent Playglass Icon Retexture Prompt

## 簡介

The Translucent Playglass Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms uploaded images into aqua-tinted translucent glass objects while preserving their original geometry and silhouette. This translucent playglass prompt for Midjourney and Flux uses shape-lock mode to apply geometry-sensitive material mapping, retexturing your image with high-gloss crystal, embedded micro air bubbles, and frosted green accents on tips and stems. It produces hyper-realistic glass objects centered on a pure white background with high-angle natural daylight, soft diffused shadows, and strong specular highlights on curves. Designers reach for this prompt when creating icon sets, product mockups, or UI elements that need a premium glass-material aesthetic without manual 3D modeling. ● Preserves the original silhouette and geometry of any uploaded image using shape-lock mode with geometry-sensitive mapping. ● Applies aqua-tinted translucent glass with high-gloss crystal, micro air bubbles, and frosted green accents on edges. ● Renders light-refracting bubble clusters that replicate original bump textures with smooth curved ridges and subtle glints. ● Delivers a centered top-down composition on a pure white background with high-angle daylight, sharp reflections, and strong specular highlights. ## Prompt

