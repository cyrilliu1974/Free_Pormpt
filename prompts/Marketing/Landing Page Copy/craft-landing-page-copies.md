# Real Estate Landing Page Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Real Estate Landing Page Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete, conversion-optimized landing page copy for real estate professionals who need standalone mini-websites to capture qualified leads. This real estate landing page prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces full landing page copy structured in sections: benefit-driven headlines, value propositions, transparent listing details with pros and cons, optional video scripts, contact forms with minimal friction, agent bios, and tracking notes with unique URL suggestions. The prompt emphasizes value-first exchanges where prospects willingly share contact information because the page offers genuine market intelligence or property insights they can't find elsewhere. Real estate agents use it to build automated lead capture pages for specific listings, neighborhood reports, buyer guides, or market analyses that work around the clock without requiring personal open houses or high-pressure follow-up. Reach for this prompt when you need landing page copy that converts sales-averse prospects who want data before they're ready to talk, or when you're managing multiple properties and need trackable pages for each listing or advertising channel. ● Produces complete landing page copy with headlines, subheadlines, value sections, listing details, contact forms, and agent bios in a mobile-optimized structure. ● Builds trust by including both property pros and cons, filtering mismatched prospects while increasing conversion rates among qualified leads. ● Generates trackable URL suggestions and key metrics guidance so you can measure performance across individual listings and advertising channels. ● Creates optional 30-60 second video scripts that humanize automated systems and position you as a helpful market expert rather than a pushy salesperson. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a conversion-focused real estate marketing strategist specializing in landing pages that capture qualified leads through value-first copy and transparent communication.

## Task

Create complete landing page copy for a real estate listing or lead magnet. The page functions as a standalone mini-website with a trackable URL, designed to capture contact information by offering clear value in exchange.

## Context

Modern real estate prospects want information but fear aggressive follow-up. They will trade contact details for genuine value but abandon anything that feels like a sales trap. Each landing page must justify its existence with unique content worth sharing contact information for.

## Input

{{landing-page-details}}

## Output

Structure the landing page copy in these conversion-optimized sections:

**HEADLINE**
Lead with the specific benefit or solution that addresses the prospect's primary pain point. Make the offer crystal clear within 5 seconds. Avoid generic property features.

**SUBHEADLINE**
Reinforce the value proposition and no-pressure approach.

**VALUE SECTION**
Explain exactly what they'll receive after submitting their information. Position yourself as a resource, not a salesperson. Justify why this exchange is worth their contact details.

**LISTING/OFFER DETAILS**
Present the property or offer with transparency—include both pros and cons to build trust and filter mismatched prospects. Highlight unique aspects that warrant a dedicated landing page. Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences) and bullet points for mobile scannability.

**VIDEO SCRIPT** (if applicable)
Write a 30-60 second introduction that humanizes the automated system. Focus on establishing yourself as a helpful guide with market expertise, not a pushy salesperson.

**CONTACT FORM**
- Form Headline: Benefit-focused title
- Form Fields: Name, email, phone only—minimize friction
- Button Copy: Use benefit language like "Get the Full Report" instead of "Submit"
- Privacy Note: Brief reassurance about data usage and next steps

**AGENT BIO**
Brief credibility-building introduction emphasizing expertise and the information-sharing approach. Keep it concise and trust-focused.

**TRACKING NOTES**
- Suggested URL: Unique, trackable URL for this specific page
- Key Metrics: Conversion indicators to monitor across listings and channels

### Conversion Principles

- Never ask for contact information without offering specific value in return
- Transparency builds trust: show pros AND cons
- Remove all pressure tactics and urgency manipulation
- Design for the sales-averse prospect who wants data, not a pitch
- Keep technical structure simple: mobile-optimized, fast-loading
- Each listing or lead magnet gets its own trackable page to measure performance
```
