# Video Topic Brainstorming Prompt for Content Creators

## 簡介

The Video Topic Brainstorming Prompt for Content Creators is a free AI prompt that generates 10 structured, trend-aware video topic ideas for any content niche or subject area. This video topic brainstorming prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current trends and audience interests to produce specific, thought-provoking angles rather than generic concepts. Each of the 10 ideas includes a compelling title, a 2-3 sentence description explaining why it matters, a defined target audience, and an engagement potential score out of 10. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your video topic area as input and applying trend analysis and audience interest modeling to surface ideas that stimulate discussion and align with what viewers care about right now. Content creators, YouTube channels, educators, and marketing teams use it to escape creative block and identify topics that resonate before investing time in production. ● Delivers 10 complete ideas with title, rationale, target demographic, and engagement score in a single run. ● Prioritizes specific, discussion-worthy angles over broad or overused topics. ● Adapts to any video niche - tech reviews, cooking tutorials, finance education, gaming commentary, or lifestyle vlogging. ● Provides an engagement potential score to help you prioritize which ideas to produce first. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You generate video topic ideas by analyzing trends, audience interests, and discussion potential.

## Context

Video topic area: {{video-topic}}

## Task

Generate 10 video topic ideas related to {{video-topic}}.

For each idea:
- Analyze current trends and audience interests relevant to the topic area
- Favor specific, thought-provoking angles over generic or overly broad topics
- Prioritize ideas that can stimulate meaningful discussion and align with current trends

## Output

Return exactly 10 ideas in this format:

**Idea 1:**  
Title: [Specific, compelling title]  
Description: [2-3 sentence explanation of the concept and why it matters]  
Target Audience: [Primary demographic or interest group]  
Engagement Potential Score: [Score out of 10]

**Idea 2:**  
Title: [Specific, compelling title]  
Description: [2-3 sentence explanation of the concept and why it matters]  
Target Audience: [Primary demographic or interest group]  
Engagement Potential Score: [Score out of 10]

[Continue through Idea 10 with the same structure]
```
