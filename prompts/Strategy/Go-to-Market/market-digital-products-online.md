# Digital Product Launch Strategy for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Digital Product Launch Strategy for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, phased go-to-market roadmap for independent creators launching digital products. This digital product launch prompt for ChatGPT walks you through pre-launch preparation, launch-day execution, and post-launch follow-up, with dedicated strategies for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter visibility. It includes guidance on creating lead magnets to build an email list and using that audience to drive launch momentum. The output is a numbered roadmap with detailed sub-steps, tailored to your product, target audience, unique selling proposition, current online presence, and available resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Designed for solopreneurs and indie makers who need a structured launch plan without a marketing team or agency budget. ● Structures the launch into pre-launch, launch, and post-launch phases with numbered action items and lettered sub-steps. ● Provides platform-specific tactics for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter to maximize visibility with your target audience. ● Includes strategies for creating free resources and lead magnets to grow an email newsletter list before launch. ● Tailors recommendations to your unique selling proposition, current online presence, and available time, budget, skills, and tools. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital product launch strategist specializing in solopreneur market entry.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step market entry plan for launching a digital product. Cover pre-launch activities, launch execution, and post-launch follow-up. Include strategies for maximizing visibility on ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter. Provide guidance on creating free resources to build an email newsletter audience and using that list effectively for the launch.

## Context
**Product & Market:**

- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

**Current Position:**
- Online presence: {{current-presence}}
- Available resources (time, budget, skills, tools): {{available-resources}}

## Output
Deliver the plan as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps under each main action item. Structure the roadmap chronologically through pre-launch, launch, and post-launch phases, with tactical guidance tailored to solopreneur constraints.
```
