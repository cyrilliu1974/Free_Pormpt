# Study Plan Builder Prompt for Any Subject and Age

## 簡介

The Study Plan Builder Prompt for Any Subject and Age is a free AI prompt that creates structured, personalized study plans for educators and tutors working with learners of any age. This study plan prompt for ChatGPT generates a three-column markdown table - Goals, Strategies, Resources - organized by learning objective. It adapts to the cognitive level and attention span of the specified age group, incorporates multiple learning modalities (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, collaborative), and addresses constraints such as available resources, session frequency, and time limits. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it helps teachers, homeschool parents, tutors, and educational consultants build practical study frameworks that account for motivation challenges, knowledge gaps, and realistic schedules. Reach for this prompt when you need to design a learning roadmap for a specific subject - whether algebra for middle schoolers, conversational Spanish for adults, or standardized test prep - and want recommendations that match both the learner's developmental stage and real-world constraints. ● Balances learning modalities to improve retention and engagement across visual, auditory, and hands-on activities. ● Embeds time management techniques and progress-tracking methods suited to the learner's age and schedule. ● Identifies prior-knowledge gaps, motivation obstacles, and accessibility issues, then weaves solutions into the strategy column. ● Outputs a portable markdown table ready to share with students, parents, or curriculum teams. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational consultant specializing in personalized learning strategy design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive study plan optimized for the subject, student profile, and constraints provided. Deliver the plan as a structured markdown table with three columns: **Goals**, **Strategies**, and **Resources**. Each row should address a specific learning objective or topic within the subject.

## Context
**Subject and learner profile:**
{{subject-and-age-group}}
(e.g., "Algebra I for 14-year-olds" or "Conversational Spanish for adult beginners")

**Learning constraints and environment:**
{{learning-context}}
(Include: learning environment—classroom, home, hybrid; available resources—textbooks, apps, tutoring; time constraints—sessions per week, duration, deadlines)

## Approach
- Align goals and strategies with the cognitive development and attention span typical of the age group
- Balance different learning modalities (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, collaborative) to enhance retention and engagement
- Recommend age-appropriate time management techniques and realistic study schedules
- Identify potential challenges (prior knowledge gaps, motivation, accessibility) and address them in your strategies
- Include methods for progress tracking and self-assessment within the plan

## Output
A markdown table:

| Goals | Strategies | Resources |
|-------|------------|-----------|
```
