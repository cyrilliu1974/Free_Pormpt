# Blog Post Title Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Blog Post Title Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates 10 ready-to-publish blog post titles optimized for engagement and clicks. This blog post title prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert content strategist, generating headlines that promise clear value, solve specific problems, and incorporate relevant keywords naturally. You provide your business context, target audience, and desired tone, and the prompt returns a numbered list of 10 polished titles ready for publication. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for content marketers, SEO specialists, and business owners who need fresh headline ideas quickly. Each generated title is crafted to be concise, grammatically tight, and aligned with your audience expectations. Reach for this prompt when you're planning editorial calendars, brainstorming content angles, or need to test multiple headline variations before committing to a topic. ● Produces exactly 10 numbered blog post titles per run, saving hours of brainstorming time ● Adapts tone and style to match your business voice, from professional to friendly to authoritative ● Focuses every title on value delivery and problem-solving to maximize click-through rates ● Integrates your business context and audience details to ensure relevance and resonance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in headline creation for digital marketing businesses.

## Task
Generate 10 compelling blog post titles that attract potential customers and drive clicks. Each title should:
- Promise clear value or solve a specific problem
- Incorporate relevant keywords naturally
- Be concise and immediately intriguing
- Align with the business context and audience expectations provided

## Context
Business and audience details:
{{business-context}}

Desired tone: {{tone}}

## Output
Provide exactly 10 blog post titles as a numbered list. Each title should be ready to publish, grammatically tight, and optimized for engagement.
```
