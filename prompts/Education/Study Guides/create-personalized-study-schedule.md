# Personalized Study Schedule Builder for Any Subject

## 簡介

The Personalized Study Schedule Builder is a free AI prompt that generates evidence-based weekly study plans tailored to any subject and learning goal. This study schedule prompt for ChatGPT produces a seven-day table showing which topics to study each day, how long each session should last, and when to take breaks - all designed around cognitive science principles like spaced repetition and cognitive load management. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and also delivers three to five research-backed study tips customized to your subject and objectives. Students use it to prepare for exams, professionals reach for it when learning new skills, and educators adapt it to scaffold self-paced coursework. ● Generates a complete weekly schedule with daily topics, session durations, and break intervals based on cognitive fatigue research. ● Applies spaced repetition automatically so concepts reappear at intervals proven to strengthen long-term memory. ● Sequences topics to align with your specific learning goals, whether mastering fundamentals or advancing to application. ● Includes tailored, evidence-based study techniques - active recall, interleaving, or retrieval practice - matched to the subject and goal you specify. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert learning strategist specializing in educational psychology, cognitive science, and evidence-based study techniques.

## Task

Create a personalized study schedule for {{subject}} that optimizes learning efficiency through:

- Research-backed optimal study times and session durations
- Strategic break intervals to prevent cognitive fatigue
- Spaced repetition to enhance long-term retention
- Topic sequencing aligned with {{learning-goals}}

## Output

Present the schedule as a table with these columns: Day | Topic | Study Duration | Break Duration

Cover a full week (7 days), distributing topics to match the learning goals and applying cognitive load principles.

After the table, provide 3–5 evidence-based study tips tailored to this subject and these goals.
```
