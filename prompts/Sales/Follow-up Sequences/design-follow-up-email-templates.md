# Educational Program Follow-Up Email Sequence Builder

## 簡介

The Educational Program Follow-Up Email Sequence Builder is a free AI prompt that creates multi-stage email templates designed to convert educational program leads into enrolled students. This educational program follow-up email prompt for ChatGPT generates 3-5 complete email templates, each targeting a different stage of the prospect's decision-making journey - from initial awareness through consideration to final enrollment. Each template includes subject lines, personalized body copy with merge tags, social proof elements, and stage-appropriate calls-to-action. The prompt structures email content using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and readability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing emails that address common objections around cost, time commitment, program value, and fit while highlighting your program's unique benefits and differentiators. This prompt is built for enrollment marketers, admissions teams, course creators, and educational consultants who need a cohesive email nurture sequence that moves prospects closer to enrollment with each touchpoint. ● Produces 3-5 email templates that progress leads from awareness to enrollment decision ● Includes merge tags for personalization based on prospect name and program interest ● Addresses common enrollment objections such as cost, time, value, and program fit ● Delivers complete emails with subject lines, body copy, social proof, and clear CTAs for each stage ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in educational program enrollment campaigns.

## Task
Design a series of follow-up email templates that nurture leads and drive enrollment for {{program-name}}. Each template should advance prospects through the decision-making journey while addressing different stages of consideration.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Program details and differentiators: {{program-benefits-and-usp}}

Enrollment process: {{enrollment-process}}

Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically from the previous one, creating clarity and natural reading progression.

## Output
Deliver 3-5 email templates formatted as:

**Email [Number]: [Purpose/Stage]**

**Subject Line:** [Compelling subject]

**Body:**
[Personalized opening]
[Core message highlighting relevant benefits or addressing specific concerns]
[Social proof or credibility elements where appropriate]
[Clear next step]

**Call-to-Action:** [Specific, action-oriented CTA]

---

Each template should:
- Include personalization merge tags (e.g., {{FirstName}}, {{ProgramInterest}})
- Progress from awareness → consideration → decision
- Address common objections (cost, time, value, fit)
- Vary tone and approach to maintain engagement
- Guide prospects closer to enrollment with each message
```
