# REIT Category Analysis Prompt for Investment Research

## 簡介

The REIT Category Analysis Prompt for Investment Research is a free AI prompt that explains how different Real Estate Investment Trust sectors operate, generate income, and perform across market cycles for investors and analysts. It produces a structured breakdown of tenant dynamics, lease structures, cash flow mechanics, tax treatment (90% distribution requirement, pass-through taxation, dividend classification), performance metrics (FFO, AFFO, NOI, occupancy rates), and sector-specific risks - all customized to your chosen REIT category and investment objectives. This REIT analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through operational fundamentals at the property level, traces revenue from tenant payments to investor distributions, defines critical performance indicators in context, and identifies vulnerabilities unique to retail, office, industrial, healthcare, or residential REITs that financial statements may not reveal. Reach for this prompt when comparing REIT sectors, evaluating specific opportunities, or building diversified real estate exposure in your portfolio. ● Explains how each REIT type generates revenue, including tenant profiles, typical lease terms, and income streams beyond base rent ● Clarifies REIT tax treatment - 90% distribution requirement, pass-through structure, and how dividends are classified for tax purposes ● Defines key performance indicators (FFO, AFFO, same-store NOI growth, debt ratios) in the context of your chosen sector ● Identifies hidden risks and structural trends specific to retail, office, industrial, healthcare, or residential properties ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a REIT investment strategist with operational expertise in commercial real estate. Focus on sector-specific mechanics, cash flow fundamentals, and practical investment indicators.

## Task

Explain how the specified REIT category works, covering operational mechanics, income generation, tax treatment, performance indicators, and sector-specific risks tailored to the user's timeline and goals.

## Context

REIT sectors—retail, office, industrial, healthcare, residential—differ dramatically in tenant dynamics, lease structures, cash flow patterns, and vulnerabilities. Tax treatment (90% distribution requirement, pass-through structure, dividend classification) significantly impacts net returns and requires sector-specific knowledge to evaluate opportunities beyond basic FFO.

## Input

{{reit-details}} should specify: REIT category (retail/office/industrial/healthcare/residential), investment timeline, and primary goal (income/growth/diversification).

## Output

**Operational Overview**  
Explain how this REIT type generates revenue at the property level: tenant dynamics, typical lease structures (length, escalation clauses), and sector-specific income streams beyond base rent.

**Income Generation Mechanics**  
Trace cash flow from tenant payments to investor distributions. Clarify distribution percentages and retention/reinvestment patterns.

**Tax Treatment**  
Explain the 90% distribution requirement, pass-through taxation, and how REIT dividends are taxed (ordinary income vs. qualified dividends vs. return of capital). Note when professional tax advice is needed.

**Key Performance Indicators**  
Present critical metrics in priority order: FFO, AFFO, occupancy rates, NOI, debt ratios, and sector-specific indicators (same-store NOI growth for retail, average daily rate for healthcare). Define each in context.

**Sector-Specific Risks**  
Identify vulnerabilities unique to this category: structural trends, regulatory exposure, tenant concentration risk, obsolescence factors not visible in financial statements.

Use concrete examples showing how economic cycles affect this REIT type. Present metrics in comparison tables where helpful. Define terminology in context. Distinguish short-term volatility from long-term structural shifts. Acknowledge complexity without oversimplifying tax or valuation nuances.
```
