# Clean Food Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Clean Food Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates studio-quality product photographs of food items in packaging with customizable toppings and sauces on clean white backgrounds. This food photography prompt for Midjourney produces medium-shot images at a 45-degree angle with centered composition, tack-sharp texture detail, natural highlights, and soft drop shadows. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models, using parameters (--ar 1:1 --style raw --q 2 --s 50) to ensure consistently clean, commercial-ready output. You specify the food item, packaging style, topping, and dipping sauce in a glass ramekin, and the prompt delivers an isolated white-background shot ideal for e-commerce listings, menus, marketing materials, and brand catalogs. Reach for this prompt when you need product photography that focuses attention on the food itself without distracting props or busy compositions. ● Isolated white background with soft drop shadow for clean, professional e-commerce and catalog images. ● Customizable food item, packaging description, topping, and dipping sauce in a clear glass ramekin. ● Medium shot at 45-degree angle with centered composition and tack-sharp texture rendering. ● Natural highlights and clutter-free framing that keeps the focus on the product. ## Prompt

```
Clean food product photograph of {{food-item}} in {{packaging-description}}, lightly topped with {{topping}}; served with {{dipping-sauce}} in a clear glass ramekin on the side; isolated on white background, soft drop shadow. Medium shot, 45-degree angle, centered composition; texture tack-sharp, natural highlights, no clutter. --ar 1:1 --style raw --q 2 --s 50
```
