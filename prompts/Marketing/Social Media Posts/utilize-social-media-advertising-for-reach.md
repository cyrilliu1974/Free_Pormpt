# Social Media Advertising Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Social Media Advertising Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a tailored multi-platform advertising plan for businesses seeking to expand brand reach and drive sales. This social media advertising prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, target audience demographics, and sales objectives to recommend 4-6 specific platforms - such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or Twitter - along with the most effective ad format for each (video, carousel, stories, sponsored posts) and key messages that resonate with your audience while maintaining brand consistency. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering results as a clear three-column markdown table. Use it when planning paid campaigns, pitching advertising strategies to stakeholders, or determining where to allocate ad spend across multiple social channels. ● Matches your target demographic to the social platforms where they are most active and engaged ● Recommends specific ad formats optimized for each platform's unique user behavior and creative requirements ● Crafts key messages tailored to audience pain points, desires, and the communication style of each channel ● Outputs a scannable markdown table that serves as a campaign blueprint for media buyers, creative teams, and marketing managers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive advertising strategy that increases brand reach and drives sales. Analyze the target audience, business model, and sales objectives to craft a tailored campaign.

## Context
Business and campaign details:
{{business-and-audience}}

Identify the most effective social media platforms for this demographic, determine suitable ad formats for each platform, and develop key messages that resonate with the audience while aligning with brand voice and objectives. Consider budget allocation, timing, and performance metrics to optimize campaign effectiveness.

## Output
Provide your strategy as a markdown table with 3 columns:
- Social Media Platform
- Ad Format
- Key Message

Include 4-6 platform recommendations, each with specific ad format choices and tailored messaging.
```
