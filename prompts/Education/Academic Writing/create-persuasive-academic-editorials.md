# Academic Editorial Writing Prompt for Multiple Perspectives

## 簡介

The Academic Editorial Writing Prompt for Multiple Perspectives is a free AI prompt that produces balanced, persuasive editorials analyzing complex topics through three evidence-backed viewpoints for scholars, researchers, and academic writers. This academic editorial prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to compose a complete editorial with an engaging introduction, three distinct analytical perspectives each supported by credible evidence, and a conclusion that explores broader implications. Each perspective includes a focused claim, supporting facts, and analysis of its impact on the field or society. The prompt requires at least five APA-formatted sources and uses dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and coherence throughout. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for writers working across different AI platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need to explore a contentious or multifaceted academic topic, prepare editorial content for journals or academic publications, or teach students how to construct balanced arguments that acknowledge competing viewpoints. ● Structures editorials around three distinct perspectives, each with claims, evidence, and implications analysis ● Requires minimum five credible sources in APA format to support all arguments ● Uses dependency grammar framework to maintain clarity and logical flow between ideas ● Outputs a complete editorial from hook to conclusion, including a references section ready for academic submission ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic writer and editor crafting a well-structured, evidence-based editorial that presents a nuanced perspective on complex topics.

## Task
Write an insightful, persuasive editorial on {{editorial-topic}} that analyzes multiple perspectives and their broader implications. Present a balanced argument backed by credible sources cited in APA format.

## Context
The editorial examines {{editorial-topic}} through three distinct perspectives, using clear dependency grammar to ensure coherence. Each perspective should be supported by evidence and analyzed for its implications to the field or society. Cite at least 5 credible sources throughout.

## Output
**Introduction:**
- Engaging opening hook that draws readers into the topic
- Clear thesis statement presenting your main argument

**Body:**

*Perspective 1:*
- Focused claim
- Supporting evidence and facts
- Analysis of implications

*Perspective 2:*
- Focused claim
- Supporting evidence and facts
- Analysis of implications

*Perspective 3:*
- Focused claim
- Supporting evidence and facts
- Analysis of implications

**Conclusion:**
- Recap of main points
- Discussion of broader implications for the field or society
- Memorable closing thought

**References:**
5+ sources in APA format
```
