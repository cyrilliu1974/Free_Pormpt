# Homeschool Debate Curriculum Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Homeschool Debate Curriculum Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete debate and argumentation curriculum tailored to individual student profiles for homeschool educators and parents. This homeschool debate curriculum prompt for ChatGPT produces a four-part guide covering topic selection criteria, age-appropriate research methodologies, debate format recommendations (Lincoln-Douglas, Public Forum, Parliamentary), and targeted skill development exercises for public speaking, logical reasoning, and argumentation. You input a student profile with age range, experience level, and curriculum goals, and the prompt generates developmentally appropriate content, credible research strategies, format comparisons, and a debate preparation checklist. Homeschool parents use it to introduce structured argumentation without requiring prior debate coaching experience, while experienced educators use it to differentiate instruction across mixed-age learning environments. Reach for this prompt when you need a ready-to-implement debate curriculum framework that adapts to beginner through advanced students and prioritizes critical thinking over competitive debate. ● Provides topic selection criteria aligned to developmental stages and educational objectives, with specific topic suggestions by experience level ● Delivers research methodologies emphasizing source credibility evaluation and age-appropriate database recommendations ● Compares debate formats with strengths, limitations, and selection guidelines based on student readiness ● Includes a debate preparation checklist and exercises for public speaking confidence, logical reasoning, and argument construction ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced curriculum developer and debate instructor helping design a comprehensive homeschool debate and argumentation curriculum.

## Task
Create a structured debate curriculum guide that includes topic selection, research methods, debate formats, and skill-building exercises tailored to the student's profile.

## Context
Student profile: {{student-profile}}
(Include age range, current debate experience level—beginner/intermediate/advanced—and primary curriculum goals)

## Output
Provide a comprehensive guide organized in four sections:

### 1. Topic Selection Guidelines
- Criteria for choosing developmentally appropriate debate topics
- Specific topic suggestions matched to the student's level
- Methods for aligning topics with educational objectives

### 2. Research Methodologies
- Age-appropriate research strategies and workflows
- Recommended resources and databases for finding credible sources
- Techniques for evaluating source reliability and bias

### 3. Debate Formats
- Overview of suitable debate formats (e.g., Lincoln-Douglas, Public Forum, Parliamentary)
- Strengths and limitations of each format
- Guidelines for selecting formats based on student age and skill level

### 4. Skill Development Exercises
- Public speaking activities to build confidence and delivery skills
- Logical reasoning exercises to strengthen critical thinking
- Argumentation drills for constructing and refining positions

Include this debate preparation checklist:

| Step | Task | Completed |
|------|------|----------|
| 1 | Select debate topic | ☐ |
| 2 | Conduct research | ☐ |
| 3 | Outline arguments | ☐ |
| 4 | Prepare rebuttal points | ☐ |
| 5 | Practice delivery | ☐ |

**Requirements:**
- Ensure all content is appropriate for the student's age and experience level
- Emphasize critical thinking and source evaluation over competition focus
- Provide varied formats to expose students to different debate styles
- Design exercises that promote active engagement and skill mastery
```
