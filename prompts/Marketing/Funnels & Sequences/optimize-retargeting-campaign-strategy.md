# Cart Abandonment Retargeting Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Cart Abandonment Retargeting Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven retargeting campaigns to convert cart abandoners into customers for e-commerce businesses. This cart abandonment prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the past 30 days of abandonment behavior, identifies high-value user segments by product category and cart value, then generates tailored campaign plans across three channels: Facebook Ads, Google Display Network, and email. Each channel plan includes specific audience targeting criteria, creative direction, incentive offers, and landing page recommendations. The prompt also establishes a measurement framework with clear ROAS, CPA, and completion rate goals, plus three optimization tactics for continuous improvement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to recover lost revenue from abandoned carts through structured, personalized retargeting rather than generic remarketing blasts. ● Segments cart abandoners by product category, cart value, and behavior patterns to identify the highest-value recovery opportunities ● Generates channel-specific campaign blueprints for Facebook Ads, Google Display Network, and email with targeting, creative, and offer guidance ● Defines measurable performance goals (ROAS, CPA, cart completion rate) and delivers three concrete optimization tactics ● Outputs a structured strategy document with clear headings, bullet points, and numerical benchmarks for immediate implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert marketer specializing in personalized retargeting campaigns to convert cart abandoners into customers.

## Task

Analyze cart abandonment data for {{ecommerce-website}} and develop a complete retargeting strategy that identifies high-value segments, creates multi-channel campaigns, and establishes measurement frameworks to recover lost sales.

## Context

Focus on users who abandoned shopping carts in the past 30 days. Extract actionable insights about abandoner behavior, product preferences, and cart values to inform highly personalized ad campaigns across paid social, display, and email channels.

## Output

Provide your strategy in the following structure:

**Cart Abandoner Segment Analysis:**
- Total users who abandoned carts in last 30 days
- Top 3 product categories abandoned
- Average cart value of abandoned carts

**Personalized Retargeting Campaign Plan:**

For each of these 3 channels (Facebook Ads, Google Display Network, Email):
- Audience targeting criteria
- Ad creative approach
- Offer/incentive
- Landing page

**Campaign Measurement & Optimization:**

*Key Performance Indicators:*
- Return on ad spend (ROAS) goal
- Cost per acquisition (CPA) goal
- Cart completion rate goal

*Measurement & Optimization Tactics:*
- List 3 specific tactics for ongoing performance improvement

Use bullet points, clear headings, and numerical values throughout.
```
