# Business Plan Update and Review Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Plan Update and Review Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, adaptable business plans reflecting current market conditions for entrepreneurs, strategists, and business owners. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a seven-section strategic document that covers executive summaries, market analysis, strategic positioning, marketing tactics, operations, financial forecasts, and risk mitigation. It analyzes industry trends, reassesses target audiences, aligns goals with capabilities, and ensures logical dependencies flow between operational, financial, and strategic components. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it produces actionable plans with bullet-point clarity and structured outlines. Use it when launching a venture, seeking funding, pivoting strategy, or conducting annual reviews. ● Analyzes competitive landscapes and industry trends to position your business effectively ● Produces 12-36 month revenue forecasts, cost structures, and capital allocation plans ● Identifies dependencies between marketing strategy, operational workflows, and financial goals ● Delivers risk assessment frameworks with contingency planning and performance monitoring ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in comprehensive business plan development and strategic positioning.

## Task
Create a well-structured, adaptable business plan that reflects current market conditions and positions the business for future growth. The plan must be detailed, actionable, and logically interconnected across all components.

## Context
Business overview:
{{business-context}}

Current financial position:
{{financial-status}}

Analyze industry trends and competitive dynamics, reassess target audience needs and preferences, align stated goals with current capabilities and market opportunities, and identify dependencies between operational, financial, and strategic elements.

## Output
Deliver a structured outline with main sections and subsections. Use bullet points for key details and action items. Cover:

**Executive Summary**
- Business overview and value proposition
- Key objectives and success metrics

**Market Analysis**
- Industry trends and dynamics
- Target market segmentation and needs
- Competitive landscape

**Strategic Goals & Positioning**
- Short-term and long-term objectives
- Competitive advantages and differentiation
- Growth opportunities

**Marketing & Sales Strategy**
- Customer acquisition channels
- Positioning and messaging
- Sales processes and conversion tactics

**Operations Plan**
- Key processes and workflows
- Resource requirements
- Technology and infrastructure

**Financial Projections**
- Revenue forecasts (12-36 months)
- Cost structure and margins
- Funding requirements and capital allocation

**Risk Management**
- Key risks and mitigation strategies
- Contingency planning
- Performance monitoring framework

Ensure logical dependencies flow clearly between sections (e.g., marketing strategy supports revenue projections, operational processes enable strategic goals).
```
