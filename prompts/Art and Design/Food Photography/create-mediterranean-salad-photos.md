# Mediterranean Salad Overhead Food Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Mediterranean Salad Overhead Food Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professionally styled overhead shots of dishes arranged in perfect color harmony. This food photography prompt for Midjourney produces images with soft natural diffused daylight and a 35mm f/2 lens aesthetic, capturing the vibrant colors of ingredients like olives, cherry tomatoes, feta cheese, and fresh herbs. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering high-quality food images suitable for restaurant menus, food blogs, recipe sites, and social media content. It is ideal for food bloggers, restaurant marketers, recipe developers, and content creators who need consistent, appetizing overhead photography without a camera or studio setup. ● Produces overhead shots with soft natural diffused daylight and 35mm f/2 lens characteristics for professional depth and warmth. ● Arranges dish elements in perfect color harmony, emphasizing the natural vibrancy of Mediterranean ingredients. ● Excludes hands and distractions (--no hands flag) to keep the focus entirely on the food. ● Outputs square 1:1 aspect ratio images optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, and recipe cards. ## Prompt

```
Overhead food photography of {{dish-description}}, arranged in perfect color harmony. Soft natural diffused daylight, 35mm f/2 lens. --no hands --ar 1:1 --quality 2
```
