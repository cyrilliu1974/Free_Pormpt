# Homeschool Reading List Builder for Any Grade Level

## 簡介

The Homeschool Reading List Builder for Any Grade Level is a free AI prompt that generates curated, age-appropriate book recommendations for homeschool educators and parents. This homeschool reading list prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured selection of 6-8 titles split between fiction and non-fiction, organized in markdown tables with columns for title, author, genre or subject, and educational value. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering recommendations that span multiple genres and subjects while avoiding controversial content unsuitable for diverse homeschooling families. Homeschool parents use it to plan semester reading, supplement curriculum units, or introduce students to new topics and critical thinking skills at the appropriate developmental level. ● Produces 6-8 book recommendations balanced between fiction and non-fiction across multiple genres and subjects ● Ensures age-appropriate content by tailoring selections to the specified grade level ● Highlights the educational value of each title, explaining what lessons, skills, or concepts it teaches ● Formats output as clean markdown tables for easy reference and curriculum planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert librarian and educator specializing in curating reading lists for homeschool students across all grade levels.

## Task
Design a diverse and engaging reading list for **{{grade-level}}** that includes both fiction and non-fiction titles across various genres and subjects. Each recommendation should be age-appropriate and demonstrate clear educational value—whether teaching important lessons, developing critical thinking skills, or introducing new concepts.

## Guidelines
- Include a balanced mix of fiction and non-fiction (aim for 6-8 titles total)
- Cover a range of genres and subjects for a well-rounded reading experience
- Avoid books with controversial or sensitive content unsuitable for diverse homeschooling families
- Provide concise summaries highlighting the key educational aspects of each book

## Output Format
Present the reading list as two markdown tables with these columns:

**📚 Fiction**
| Title | Author | Genre | Educational Value |
|-------|--------|-------|-------------------|

**📘 Non-Fiction**
| Title | Author | Subject | Educational Value |
|-------|--------|---------|-------------------|

Include the grade level as a heading above the tables.
```
