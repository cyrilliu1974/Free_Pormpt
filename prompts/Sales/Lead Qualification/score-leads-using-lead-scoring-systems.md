# Lead Scoring System Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Scoring System Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete prioritization and nurturing system for sales prospects across any business context. This lead scoring prompt for ChatGPT generates a markdown table framework that covers scoring criteria (demographic information, behavioral data, engagement metrics), assigns point values to each criterion, establishes score thresholds that define lead quality categories (cold, warm, hot), and maps specific nurturing strategies to each tier. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Sales teams use it to systematically assess lead potential, align marketing automation with lead readiness, and ensure outreach effort matches opportunity quality. The output is a three-column table - Lead Score, Lead Quality, Nurturing Strategy - that spans the full range from lowest to highest scores with actionable tactics for each level. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize lead evaluation, align sales and marketing around shared definitions of lead quality, or build the scoring logic for a CRM or marketing automation platform. ● Defines scoring criteria across demographic, behavioral, and engagement dimensions tailored to your business context ● Assigns point values and establishes thresholds that categorize leads into quality tiers ● Maps specific nurturing strategies to each tier so marketing and sales know exactly how to treat cold, warm, and hot leads ● Outputs a structured markdown table ready to implement in CRM systems, sales playbooks, or marketing automation workflows ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lead scoring specialist designing a comprehensive prioritization and nurturing system for sales prospects.

## Task
Create a structured lead scoring framework that includes:
- Scoring criteria across demographic information, behavioral data, and engagement metrics
- Point values assigned to each criterion
- Score thresholds defining lead quality categories (cold, warm, hot, etc.)
- Tailored nurturing strategies mapped to each quality tier

## Context
{{business-and-sales-context}}

Include: business type, target audience, key conversion goals, typical sales cycle length, and primary lead sources.

## Output
Deliver your framework as a markdown table with three columns:

| Lead Score | Lead Quality | Nurturing Strategy |
|------------|--------------|--------------------|

Cover the full range from lowest to highest scores. For each tier, provide specific, actionable nurturing tactics appropriate to that lead quality level.
```
