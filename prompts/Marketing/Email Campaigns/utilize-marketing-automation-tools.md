# Email Automation Strategy Builder for Lead Nurturing

## 簡介

The Email Automation Strategy Builder for Lead Nurturing is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email automation workflows designed to move leads through your sales funnel and increase conversions. This email automation prompt for ChatGPT takes your sales context - including funnel stages, target audience, product details, and conversion baselines - and produces a structured strategy complete with campaign sequences, behavioral triggers, timing schedules, and measurable goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a markdown table of campaigns alongside detailed explanations of messaging strategy, customer journey alignment, and optimization opportunities. Marketing teams use it to systematically design drip campaigns, re-engagement sequences, and lead scoring workflows that connect each touchpoint to a specific conversion metric. Reach for this prompt when you need to build or refine an automation strategy that scales with growth and aligns email marketing directly with sales objectives. ● Produces a strategy overview that explains how automation supports sales objectives and scales with growth. ● Generates a campaign table showing email sequence counts, timing, behavioral triggers, and specific conversion goals. ● Delivers paragraph explanations for each campaign covering customer journey stage, messaging strategy, and success metrics. ● Ensures campaigns are measurable, interconnected where appropriate, and designed to improve baseline conversion rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in automation workflows and lead nurturing.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email automation strategy that moves leads through the sales funnel and increases conversions. Design specific campaigns with sequences, triggers, and measurable goals that align with the customer journey and sales objectives.

## Context
**Sales and business details:**
{{sales-context}}

Include your sales process stages, target audience characteristics, product/service offering, current conversion rate baseline, and desired business outcome.

## Output
Deliver your strategy as:

1. **Strategy Overview** – A brief summary of how the automation plan supports the sales objectives and scales with growth.

2. **Campaign Table** – A markdown table with these columns:
   - **Campaign Name**
   - **Email Sequence** (number of emails and timing)
   - **Trigger** (what initiates the campaign)
   - **Goal** (specific conversion metric or action)

3. **Campaign Explanations** – Below the table, provide a paragraph for each campaign explaining:
   - The customer journey stage it addresses
   - Key messaging and content strategy
   - Success metrics and optimization opportunities

Ensure all campaigns are measurable, interconnected where appropriate, and designed to improve upon the current conversion rate.
```
