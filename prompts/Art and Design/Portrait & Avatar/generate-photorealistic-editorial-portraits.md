# Photorealistic Editorial Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Editorial Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces magazine-quality portrait photography with cinematic lighting, architectural settings, and premium fashion aesthetics for brands, marketers, and content creators. This photorealistic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana simulates full-frame DSLR photography shot with an 85mm portrait lens at f/1.8 aperture. It renders sharp subject focus with natural bokeh backgrounds, intricate architectural texture, high dynamic range lighting, and flawless skin detail. Use it to create brand photography, editorial content, marketing visuals, or social media assets that require the look of a professional studio shoot.
