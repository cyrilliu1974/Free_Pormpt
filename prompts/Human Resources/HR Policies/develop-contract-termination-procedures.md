# Contract Termination Procedures Prompt

## 簡介

The Contract Termination Procedures Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates legally compliant termination workflows for HR compliance specialists and process architects. It produces a structured, nine-phase procedure covering pre-termination risk assessment, multi-level approval workflows, notice period management, documentation protocols, asset recovery, communication strategies, final settlement administration, and post-termination audit trails. This contract termination prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok aligns with ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems and addresses both voluntary and involuntary terminations across different employee categories and regulatory frameworks. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize termination processes that balance legal compliance, documentation integrity, and employee dignity while minimizing litigation risk and organizational disruption. ● Produces numbered sections and subsections with detailed checklists for pre-termination assessment, approval authorization, notice period timelines, documentation protocols, asset recovery, communication templates, and final settlement. ● Addresses both voluntary and involuntary terminations with risk assessment protocols, compliance verification steps, and audit trail mechanisms tailored to your company context, legal framework, and organizational structure. ● Includes quality control checkpoints and continuous improvement feedback loops aligned with ISO 9001:2015 process control and documentation management standards. ● Delivers communication strategies that maintain employee dignity and organizational transparency while protecting against regulatory violations and reputational damage. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR compliance specialist and process architect with expertise in employment law, organizational risk mitigation, and ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems.

## Task

Develop comprehensive contract termination procedures that ensure legal compliance, maintain documentation integrity, and minimize organizational disruption. Design procedures covering all critical phases: pre-termination assessment, notice period requirements, approval workflows, documentation protocols, asset recovery, communication strategies, and post-termination compliance.

## Context

Improper termination procedures can result in costly litigation, regulatory violations, and reputational damage. These procedures must maintain employee dignity, organizational transparency, and align with ISO 9001:2015 standards for process control, documentation management, and continuous improvement.

Address both voluntary and involuntary terminations across different employee categories, contractual obligations, and regulatory requirements. Include risk assessment protocols, multi-level approval processes, comprehensive documentation checklists, timeline management, communication templates, compliance verification steps, quality control checkpoints, audit trails, and feedback mechanisms for continuous improvement.

{{legal-framework}}

## Output

Provide structured termination procedures in numbered sections and subsections with detailed checklists for each phase. Organize by:

1. Pre-termination assessment and risk evaluation
2. Approval workflow and authorization requirements
3. Notice period and timeline management
4. Documentation protocols and compliance records
5. Asset recovery and system access procedures
6. Internal and external communication strategies
7. Final settlement and administrative closure
8. Post-termination compliance and audit trails
9. Quality control checkpoints and continuous improvement mechanisms

Ensure each section includes actionable steps, responsible parties, required documentation, legal safeguards, and quality assurance measures.
```
