# Storytelling Sales Pitch Creator

## 簡介

The Storytelling Sales Pitch Creator is a free AI prompt that transforms product and service descriptions into emotionally resonant, narrative-driven pitches for sales teams, marketers, and entrepreneurs. This storytelling prompt for ChatGPT structures your pitch around six narrative elements: an attention-grabbing hook, a relatable protagonist or scenario your audience identifies with, a central conflict or pain point, your offering as the solution, the transformation it delivers, and a compelling call-to-action. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and coherent transitions between each story beat. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for your product or service, target audience, unique selling proposition, desired emotional impact, and preferred call-to-action. Use it when you need to turn feature lists into persuasive stories that connect on an emotional level and drive action. ● Builds six labeled sections - Hook, Protagonist, Conflict, Solution, Transformation, and Call-to-Action - with bullet-point details under each ● Uses dependency grammar to create logical narrative flow and smooth transitions between story beats ● Customizes tone and emotional impact based on your target audience and desired response ● Outputs a complete pitch framework ready to deliver in presentations, emails, or sales meetings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert storyteller and pitch creator specializing in narrative-driven marketing.

## Task
Craft a compelling, emotionally resonant pitch that transforms a product or service description into a structured narrative using classic story architecture and dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique Selling Proposition: {{usp}}
Desired Emotional Impact: {{desired-emotion}}

## Output
Structure your pitch with these six labeled sections:

**Hook**
- Opening statement that immediately captures attention and relates to the product/service
- Dependency grammar connections that establish the narrative foundation

**Protagonist**
- Relatable character or scenario your target audience will identify with
- Key details that ground the story in their world

**Conflict**
- The central problem, pain point, or challenge faced
- Stakes that make the audience care about resolution

**Solution**
- Introduction of your product/service as the answer
- How it specifically addresses the conflict

**Transformation**
- Concrete benefits and outcomes after using the solution
- The changed state or results achieved

**Call-to-Action**
- Powerful closing directive: {{cta}}
- Final emotional anchor that drives response

Use bullet points under each section to detail key elements and show how dependency grammar creates coherent transitions between narrative beats.
```
