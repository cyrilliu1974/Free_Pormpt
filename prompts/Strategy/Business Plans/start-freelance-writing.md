# Freelance Writing Business Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Freelance Writing Business Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a customized business roadmap for writers launching or expanding their freelance practice. This freelance writing business plan prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured markdown table that sequences every stage of your venture - from niche validation and business setup through client acquisition, service design, pricing strategy, and long-term growth. You input your writing niche, current employment status, target market, and experience level, and the prompt returns a three-column plan: sequential steps, detailed descriptions explaining what to do and why, and curated resources (tools, platforms, references) to support each phase. Real use cases include side-hustlers mapping their transition from full-time employment, solopreneurs refining service offerings, and novice writers building their first client pipeline. Reach for this prompt when you need a logical, personalized roadmap that accounts for your specific niche constraints, time availability, and market positioning. ● Structures your plan as a markdown table with clear progression from market research to sustained growth ● Tailors every recommendation to your writing niche, employment status, target market, and experience level ● Provides curated tools, platforms, and references alongside each step so you can act immediately ● Covers the full lifecycle: niche validation, business setup, client acquisition, service packaging, pricing strategy, and scaling tactics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in freelance writing ventures.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan for launching and growing a successful freelance writing business. Cover market research, business setup, client acquisition, service offerings, pricing strategies, and long-term growth planning. Provide actionable steps, practical advice, and relevant resources for each stage.

## Context
Writing niche: {{writing-niche}}
Current employment status: {{employment-status}}
Target market: {{target-market}}
Experience level and available time: {{experience-and-availability}}

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Steps**: Sequential stages for building the business
- **Description**: Clear explanation of what to do and why
- **Resources**: Tools, platforms, or references to support each step

Ensure the roadmap follows a logical progression from initial setup through sustained growth, tailored to the context provided above.
```
