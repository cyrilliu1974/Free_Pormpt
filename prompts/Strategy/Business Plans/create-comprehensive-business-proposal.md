# Business Proposal Generator for Strategic Planning

## 簡介

The Business Proposal Generator for Strategic Planning is a free AI prompt that creates structured, data-driven business proposals for entrepreneurs, analysts, and strategists evaluating new ventures. This business proposal prompt for ChatGPT transforms a business concept into an eight-section document including executive summary, business overview with unique value proposition and target market, market analysis with sizing and competitor research, operational plan with milestones and resource requirements, financial projections with revenue models and break-even analysis, risk assessment with mitigation strategies, go-to-market strategy with customer acquisition estimates, and a pitch deck outline. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing proposals grounded in operations research methodology and consumer psychology. Use it when you need to formalize a business idea for stakeholders, investors, or internal planning processes. ● Structures proposals into eight comprehensive sections from executive summary through pitch deck outline ● Applies operations research and data-driven decision making frameworks to business concept evaluation ● Generates market sizing, competitor analysis, and consumer behavior insights for the target industry ● Produces financial projections including revenue models, cost structures, and break-even analysis with stated assumptions ● Identifies risks and develops mitigation strategies alongside go-to-market plans with customer acquisition metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst and strategist with deep knowledge in operations research, project management, consumer psychology, and data-driven decision making.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive project proposal for the following business concept:

{{business-concept}}

The proposal must cover all aspects from ideation to execution, grounded in thorough research and data analysis.

## Output
Structure the proposal with these sections:

**Executive Summary**
Provide a high-level overview of the business idea and key points from each section below.

**Business Overview**
- Business idea description
- Unique value proposition
- Target market identification

**Market Analysis**
- Market size estimation
- Relevant market trends
- Competitor analysis
- Consumer behavior and needs insights

**Operational Plan**
- Key milestones
- Resource requirements
- Potential strategic partnerships

**Financial Projections**
- Revenue model
- Cost structure
- Break-even analysis
- Key financial assumptions

**Risk Assessment**
- Potential risks identification
- Mitigation strategies
- Contingency plans

**Go-to-Market Strategy**
- Marketing plan
- Sales strategy
- Customer acquisition cost estimates
- Customer lifetime value projections

**Pitch Deck**
Create a compelling slide-by-slide outline that summarizes the key points of the proposal in a visually engaging format.

Ensure all analysis demonstrates deep understanding of the industry landscape, is data-driven with cited sources where possible, and identifies actionable insights.
```
