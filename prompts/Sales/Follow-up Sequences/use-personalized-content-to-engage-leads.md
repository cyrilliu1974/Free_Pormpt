# Personalized Lead Engagement Content Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Personalized Lead Engagement Content Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates tailored content plans across email, blog, and social channels to guide leads from awareness through decision stages. This lead engagement prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your industry, target audience, unique selling proposition, key features, and competitive landscape to produce specific email campaigns with subject lines, blog article topics with headlines, and platform-optimized social media posts. Each piece of content is mapped to a funnel stage (awareness, consideration, or decision) and includes messaging that differentiates your offering from competitors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Content marketers, demand generation teams, and sales enablement professionals use this prompt when building lead nurturing campaigns that require personalized messaging for specific audience segments. ● Produces specific email concepts with subject lines and core messaging tailored to your audience's pain points and goals ● Generates blog post topics with headlines and key takeaways that demonstrate industry expertise and address lead challenges ● Creates social media post concepts optimized for platform best practices and funnel progression ● Maps every content piece to awareness, consideration, or decision stages with clear differentiation from competitors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in lead nurturing and sales funnel optimization.

## Task
Create a multi-channel content strategy that engages leads and guides them through the sales funnel. Deliver targeted content across three formats: emails, blog posts, and social media.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Key product/service features: {{key-features}}

Main competitors: {{competitors}}

## Approach
- Analyze the specific needs, interests, and pain points of leads within this industry and audience segment
- Align content with different sales funnel stages (awareness, consideration, decision)
- Incorporate industry-specific terminology and current trends to demonstrate expertise
- Use persuasive language and clear calls-to-action that encourage lead progression
- Optimize for readability and engagement across email, web, and social platforms
- Craft compelling subject lines and headlines that capture attention
- Address the unique goals and challenges of each lead segment with personalized messaging

## Output
Structure your deliverable with clear headings for each content type:

### Email Campaigns
- List specific email concepts with subject lines and core messaging

### Blog Posts
- List specific article topics with headlines and key takeaways

### Social Media Content
- List specific post concepts tailored to platform best practices

For each piece, indicate which funnel stage it serves and how it differentiates from competitors.
```
