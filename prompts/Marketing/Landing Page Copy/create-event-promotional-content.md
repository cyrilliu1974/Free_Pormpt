# Event Promotional Content Generator

## 簡介

The Event Promotional Content Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, persuasive event marketing copy for digital marketers and event planners across any industry. This event promotional content prompt for ChatGPT produces full-page promotional materials that include attention-grabbing headlines, subheadlines, opening paragraphs that speak to audience pain points, event detail sections, concrete takeaway lists, and action-oriented CTAs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. You supply the event type (webinar, product launch, conference), the product or service being promoted, your target audience, and the key benefits and unique selling points - the prompt then generates persuasive landing page copy designed to drive registrations and attendance. Marketers use it for webinar landing pages, workshop promotions, virtual summit announcements, and in-person event campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need conversion-focused event copy fast, without starting from a blank page or guessing which structure will resonate with your specific audience. ● Produces headline, subheadline, opening paragraph, event details, key takeaways, CTA, and closing paragraph in one output. ● Addresses audience pain points and weaves in your unique selling points throughout the copy. ● Tailors tone and avoids jargon based on the target audience you specify. ● Includes clear, urgent calls-to-action that drive registrations and attendance. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in event promotional content across industries and audiences.

## Task
Create compelling promotional content for an event that showcases a product or service to a specific audience. Use persuasive language that addresses audience pain points and drives registration or attendance.

## Context
- Event type: {{event-type}}
- Product/service being promoted: {{product-service}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Key benefits and unique selling points: {{benefits-and-usp}}

## Output
Deliver structured promotional content with:

**Headline**  
Attention-grabbing and benefit-focused

**Subheadline**  
Reinforces value proposition

**Opening Paragraph**  
Engage immediately by highlighting how the product/service solves audience problems, weaving in the key benefits and unique selling points from {{benefits-and-usp}}

**Event Details**  
- Date, time, and location
- Agenda overview
- Speaker profiles (if applicable)

**Key Takeaways**  
3-5 concrete outcomes attendees will gain

**Call-to-Action**  
Clear, urgent, action-oriented (register, reserve seat, claim spot)

**Closing Paragraph**  
Reinforce the transformation or value, echo the CTA

Avoid jargon the {{target-audience}} won't recognize. Keep tone persuasive and audience-appropriate.
```
