# Client Testimonial Integration for Proposals

## 簡介

The Client Testimonial Integration for Proposals is a free AI prompt that creates a strategic placement plan for weaving client testimonials into business proposals to maximize credibility and persuasiveness. This testimonial integration prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your proposal structure, matches specific testimonials to the claims they best support, and recommends formatting and transition language for natural integration. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Proposal writers use it when preparing RFPs, service agreements, project bids, or consulting proposals where social proof will differentiate their submission. The prompt outputs a numbered implementation plan specifying which testimonial to place in each section, why it belongs there, how to format it (pull quote, inline, or dedicated block), and what lead-in text will connect it to surrounding narrative. ● Maps each available testimonial to the proposal section or claim it best substantiates ● Specifies formatting choices - pull quotes, inline citations, or dedicated testimonial blocks - based on context and impact ● Drafts transition sentences that weave testimonials naturally into the proposal narrative ● Ensures proper attribution with client name, title, and company for every testimonial used ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert proposal writer specializing in building credibility through strategic use of client testimonials.

## Task
Create a structured plan for incorporating testimonials throughout a proposal document. Identify optimal placement points, select the most relevant testimonials, and integrate them seamlessly to reinforce key claims and demonstrate proven expertise.

## Context
- Proposal type: {{proposal-type}}
- Target client: {{client-name}}
- Company strengths to emphasize: {{key-strengths}}
- Available testimonials with attribution: {{testimonials}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the proposal structure and identify high-impact sections where testimonials will strengthen credibility
2. Match each testimonial to specific proposal claims or strengths it substantiates
3. Determine optimal formatting (pull quotes, dedicated section, or inline integration)
4. Ensure every testimonial includes proper attribution (client name, title, company)
5. Draft transition language that weaves testimonials naturally into the narrative

## Output
Deliver a numbered implementation plan. For each step, provide:
- The specific proposal section or claim to support
- Which testimonial to use and why
- Recommended formatting and placement
- Suggested lead-in or transition text
```
