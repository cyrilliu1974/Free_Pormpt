# Content Gap Analysis vs Competitors Prompt

## 簡介

The Content Gap Analysis vs Competitors Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies untapped SEO opportunities by analyzing where your competitors' content falls short and where your strategy can gain ground. This content gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by examining competitor websites in your industry, mapping their content structure, topics covered, and keyword usage to surface areas they have overlooked or underserved. It evaluates search volume, ranking difficulty, relevance to your target audience, and emerging query patterns, then outputs a markdown table with three columns: Content Gap, Keyword Opportunities, and Content Ideas. Each row delivers an actionable finding you can immediately add to your editorial calendar. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for SEO strategists, content marketers, and agencies building data-driven content plans. ● Analyzes competitor content structure, topics, and keyword usage to find gaps ● Evaluates search volume, ranking difficulty, and relevance to your audience ● Outputs a structured markdown table with 8-10 rows of actionable findings ● Identifies emerging trends and query patterns your competitors are not addressing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist conducting a comprehensive content gap analysis.

## Task
Identify untapped content opportunities by analyzing competitor websites in the given industry. Examine competitor content structure, topics covered, and keyword usage to find areas where they are lacking or where improvement is possible. Generate a list of content gaps, associated keyword opportunities, and innovative content ideas that will drive organic traffic.

## Context
**Industry & competitors:** {{industry-and-competitors}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Current SEO strategy and content goals:** {{seo-strategy-and-goals}}

## Analysis factors
- Search volume and ranking difficulty
- Relevance to the industry and audience
- Potential for driving organic traffic
- Topics underserved by competitors
- Emerging trends and query patterns

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Content Gap | Keyword Opportunities | Content Ideas |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------|

Provide at least 8-10 rows of actionable findings.
```
