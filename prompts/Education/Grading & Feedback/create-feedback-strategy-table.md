# Feedback Strategy Table Generator for Educators

## 簡介

The Feedback Strategy Table Generator for Educators is a free AI prompt that creates structured, evidence-based feedback guides tailored to any subject area for teachers and instructional coaches. This feedback strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive table that organizes feedback approaches across the full spectrum of student performance - from struggling learners to advanced students - with concrete example statements educators can adapt immediately. It works by analyzing your subject area and compiling research-backed strategies into four performance-level categories, each paired with specific feedback techniques and 2-3 model statements, followed by a Sources section with APA-formatted references. Teachers use it to differentiate feedback in math, English, science, social studies, world languages, and elective courses; instructional coaches share it in professional development sessions; and curriculum directors include it in department feedback protocols. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic praise or correction and deliver actionable, level-appropriate feedback that meets each student where they are. ● Produces four performance-level categories with tailored feedback strategies for each ● Includes 2-3 concrete example statements per level so educators see the approach in action ● Provides APA-formatted sources for every strategy, grounding feedback in educational research ● Adapts to any subject area or grade level, from elementary math to high school humanities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in actionable feedback strategies for educators across all subjects and grade levels.

## Task
Create a comprehensive feedback guide for student work across different performance levels. Research evidence-based approaches and compile them into a structured table that educators can immediately apply in their classroom.

## Context
Subject area: {{subject}}

The guide should address the full spectrum of student performance, from those who are struggling to those who are excelling, ensuring every educator can provide targeted, constructive feedback that promotes growth.

## Output
Deliver a table with these columns:

1. **Performance Level** — Clear categories spanning the full range (e.g., Struggling, Developing, Proficient, Advanced)
2. **Feedback Strategy** — Specific, actionable approaches tailored to each performance level
3. **Example Feedback** — 2-3 concrete feedback statements demonstrating each strategy in practice

Below the table, include a **Sources** section with a numbered list of all references cited, formatted in APA style.
```
