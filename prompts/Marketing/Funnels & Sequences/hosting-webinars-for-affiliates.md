# Affiliate Training Webinar Planner for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The Affiliate Training Webinar Planner for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates structured webinar programs to educate affiliate partners on promoting your offerings to their specific audiences. This affiliate webinar prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete training package including session outlines with timestamps, promotional materials affiliates can customize, presentation scripts with case studies, Q&A preparation, and follow-up resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing ready-to-use content that highlights product features and benefits tailored to each affiliate's audience. Marketing teams use it to onboard new affiliate partners, product managers deploy it for major launches, and affiliate coordinators rely on it to scale partner training without repeating the same presentation manually. Reach for this prompt when you need to train multiple affiliate partners efficiently, ensure consistent messaging across your affiliate network, or equip partners with promotional assets they can immediately share with their communities. ● Produces webinar content outlines with session flow, timestamps, and demonstration segments ● Creates customizable promotional materials including email templates, social media copy, and graphics briefs ● Generates presentation scripts with real-world examples and case studies that demonstrate value ● Prepares Q&A responses addressing common affiliate partner concerns and objections ● Delivers post-webinar email sequences and ongoing support resources to maintain engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert webinar strategist designing an affiliate training session.

## Task
Create a complete webinar plan that teaches affiliate partners how to effectively promote a product or service to their audiences. Include webinar content structure, promotional materials, and follow-up resources.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}

Affiliate Partners & Their Audiences: {{affiliate-context}}

The webinar must:
- Highlight key features and benefits that resonate with the affiliates' specific audiences
- Provide ready-to-use promotional materials affiliates can share with their networks
- Include real-world examples and case studies demonstrating value
- Incorporate Q&A time to address partner concerns
- Offer post-webinar support resources

## Output
Deliver a structured webinar package containing:

1. **Webinar Content Outline** – session flow with timestamps, key talking points, and demonstration segments
2. **Promotional Materials** – email templates, social media copy, and graphics briefs affiliates can customize
3. **Presentation Script** – engaging delivery notes with example stories and case studies
4. **Q&A Preparation** – anticipated questions with recommended answers
5. **Follow-Up Resources** – post-webinar email sequence, promotional assets, and ongoing support materials

Use dependency grammar principles throughout: structure sentences so that each element clearly connects to and depends on core ideas, building from essential information outward to supporting details.
```
