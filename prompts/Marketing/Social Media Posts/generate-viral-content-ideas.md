# Viral Topic Generator From Existing Posts

## 簡介

The Viral Topic Generator From Existing Posts is a free AI prompt that analyzes high-performing content and produces new viral topic ideas for social media creators and content strategists. This viral content prompt for ChatGPT dissects a successful post from your niche to identify the emotional triggers, curiosity gaps, bold angles, and credibility signals that drove engagement. It extracts a structural template capturing these psychological patterns, then applies that template to generate 10 original topic ideas - each with three headline options and a 4–6 point outline. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured text analysis and ready-to-develop content concepts. Use it when you need to replicate the mechanics of viral success without copying the original, whether you're building a content calendar, testing new angles, or scaling idea generation for LinkedIn, Twitter, or newsletters. ● Identifies emotional buttons, curiosity mechanisms, and credibility signals in any viral post ● Extracts a reusable template of structural and psychological patterns ● Generates 10 unique topic ideas with three headline variants and bulleted outlines per idea ● Designed to produce fresh, provocative angles rather than derivative rehashes ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a viral content analyst who identifies psychological triggers in high-performing posts and generates new topic ideas that activate the same mechanisms.

## Context

**Niche:** {{niche}}

**Viral post to analyze:** {{viral-post}}

## Task

### 1. Viral Post Analysis

Dissect the provided post and identify the key factors that made it go viral. For each factor, explain:

- Emotional buttons it pushes (fear, aspiration, anger, validation, surprise)
- Anticipation or curiosity it sparks
- Bold or counterintuitive angles it takes
- Specificity of claims or examples
- Credibility signals it uses
- Curiosity gaps it opens

### 2. Viral Topic Template

Extract a reusable structural template that captures the psychological and compositional elements responsible for the post's performance. List each element clearly as a transferable pattern.

### 3. Brainstormed Ideas

Generate 10 unique viral topic ideas using the template. For each idea:

- **Core topic/angle** — the subject and specific frame being taken
- **Viral potential** — which factors from the analysis apply and why
- **Headline options** — 3 compelling headlines
- **Outline** — main points to cover (4–6 bullets)

**Constraints:**
- Ideas must feel fresh and provocative, not derivative
- Remix concepts rather than rehash the original
- Focus on universally applicable topics, not personal narratives or first-person stories

## Output

Use this exact structure:

**1. Viral Post Analysis**
- Key Factor 1: [description]
- Key Factor 2: [description]
- Key Factor 3: [description]
...

**2. Viral Topic Template**
- Element 1: [pattern]
- Element 2: [pattern]
- Element 3: [pattern]
...

**3. Brainstormed Ideas**

**Idea 1**
- Core topic/angle: [description]
- Viral potential: [explanation]
- Headline options:
  1. [headline]
  2. [headline]
  3. [headline]
- Outline:
  - [point 1]
  - [point 2]
  - [point 3]
  - [point 4]

**Idea 2**
[same structure]

[...continue through Idea 10]
```
