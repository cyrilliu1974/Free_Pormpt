# Research Content Topics Thoroughly

## 簡介

The Research Content Topics Thoroughly prompt is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, properly cited content by synthesizing information from reputable sources for researchers, students, and professional writers. This research content topics prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to gather information from academic journals, books, and credible websites, then organize key themes and insights into coherent written pieces with proper citations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, generating structured articles with clear headings, in-text citations in your chosen style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.), and complete reference lists. Real-world applications include writing literature reviews, creating evidence-based reports, developing white papers, and producing educational content that requires academic rigor. This prompt is for anyone who needs to transform research into polished, citation-ready content - whether you're a graduate student writing a thesis chapter, a consultant preparing a client report, or a content professional creating authoritative articles. ● Synthesizes information from multiple credible sources into coherent narratives with clear thematic organization ● Applies any major citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard) consistently throughout the document with in-text citations and reference lists ● Structures content with introduction, thematic subsections, and conclusion while incorporating examples and case studies for engagement ● Adapts tone and depth to match your target audience and desired content length specifications ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and content creator who produces well-researched, properly cited content.

## Task
Create comprehensive, engaging content on {{research-topic}} by:

1. Synthesizing information from reputable sources (academic journals, books, credible websites)
2. Identifying key themes, trends, and insights
3. Organizing ideas in a logical, coherent flow
4. Incorporating relevant examples, case studies, or anecdotes to maintain reader interest
5. Citing all sources using {{citation-style}} format consistently throughout
6. Ensuring accuracy, clarity, and completeness

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Desired length: {{content-length}}

## Output
Deliver structured content with:
- Clear section headings (Introduction, Main Body with thematic subsections, Conclusion)
- Subheadings and bullet points where appropriate for readability
- Proper in-text citations and a complete reference list in {{citation-style}} format
```
