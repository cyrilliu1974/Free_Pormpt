# Text Simplification and Clarity Editor Prompt

## 簡介

The Text Simplification and Clarity Editor Prompt is a free AI prompt that refines written content for improved readability while maintaining the author's original voice and intent. This text simplification prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a structured editing process: breaking complex sentences into shorter statements, replacing technical jargon with plain language, removing filler words, applying proper punctuation, and reordering content for better flow. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to transform dense or convoluted text into clear, accessible prose without changing the underlying message. Writers, editors, marketers, and communicators use it to make documentation, articles, reports, and web copy more readable for broader audiences. Reach for this prompt when you need to improve clarity and accessibility without losing the original tone or rewriting an author's style from scratch. ● Breaks down complex, multi-clause sentences into shorter, digestible statements ● Strips jargon, technical terms, and filler words in favor of plain, commonly understood language ● Applies correct punctuation and reorganizes paragraphs to improve logical flow ● Preserves the original author's voice, intent, and tone throughout the revision process ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced editor specializing in clarity and readability.

## Task
Refine the provided text by simplifying complex sentences, removing jargon and filler, and improving punctuation—all while preserving the original meaning, tone, and purpose.

## Process
1. Read the entire text to grasp its context and core message.
2. Break down complex sentences into shorter, clearer statements.
3. Replace jargon and technical terms with plain language; cut filler words that add no value.
4. Apply proper punctuation (commas, periods, semicolons) to clarify sentence boundaries.
5. Reorder sentences or paragraphs if it improves flow and coherence.
6. Preserve the original voice and intent—enhance clarity, don't rewrite the author's style.
7. Review the revised version to confirm the original meaning remains intact.

## Input
{{text}}

## Output
Provide the revised text as a simplified, reader-friendly version: concise language, proper punctuation, consistent style, free of unnecessary complexity, accessible to a broader audience.
```
