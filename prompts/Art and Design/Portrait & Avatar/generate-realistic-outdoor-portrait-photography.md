# Realistic Outdoor Portrait Photography Generator

## 簡介

The Realistic Outdoor Portrait Photography Generator is a free AI prompt that creates lifelike outdoor portrait photos with warm autumn tones, natural lighting, and film-like texture for content creators, marketers, and designers. This outdoor portrait photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images featuring soft golden hour sunlight, high-detail skin texture, and a playful autumn rooftop aesthetic. You specify the subject description and setting, and the prompt renders a dynamic leaning-forward pose with the subject looking back over the shoulder - perfect for lifestyle brands, fashion lookbooks, social media content, and editorial campaigns that need authentic, engaging portrait visuals. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-quality outdoor portraits without scheduling photo shoots or hiring models. ● Customizable subject and setting variables let you generate portraits for diverse audiences and brand aesthetics. ● Captures side-back golden hour sunlight, warm skin highlights, and soft film grain texture automatically. ● Renders dynamic leaning-forward pose with natural over-the-shoulder gaze and joyful, confident expression. ● Produces high-detail skin texture and warm autumn color grading suited to lifestyle, fashion, and social media use cases. ## Prompt

```
Realistic outdoor portrait photography, soft natural daylight, warm autumn tones, slight film grain, high detail skin texture. {{subject-description}} leaning forward on a metal railing, arms resting on rail, torso slightly twisted, looking back over shoulder, joyful expression. {{setting}}. Soft golden hour sunlight from side-back, warm highlights on skin and hair. Playful, confident, carefree, sexy yet natural, autumn rooftop vibe.
```
