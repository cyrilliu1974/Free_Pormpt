# Backlink Strategy Builder for SEO and Link Acquisition

## 簡介

The Backlink Strategy Builder for SEO and Link Acquisition is a free AI prompt that creates complete link-building strategies tailored to your website, industry, and target keywords. This backlink strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through five core stages: competitor backlink analysis to find link gaps, prospect research across 10-15 high-authority domains, personalized outreach email templates, content asset mapping for linkable resources, and a prioritized implementation timeline with monthly targets. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing actionable tables, email drafts, and prioritized task lists ready for your SEO team to execute. Use it when you need a structured plan to acquire high-quality backlinks, improve domain authority, and boost organic rankings through strategic outreach and content partnerships. ● Competitor backlink profile analysis to uncover quick-win link opportunities and gaps ● Categorized prospect lists with domain authority scores and recommended link types ● Personalized outreach email templates that reference your unique selling points and content assets ● Month-by-month implementation roadmap with success metrics tied to referring domains and keyword ranking lift ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist focused on backlink acquisition and off-page optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive backlink strategy for {{business-website}} that improves search engine rankings and drives organic traffic through strategic link-building.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}
Unique selling points: {{usp}}
Existing content assets: {{content-assets}}

## Process
For each step, provide actionable recommendations:

### 1. Competitor & Opportunity Analysis
- Identify authoritative websites in {{industry}} that link to competitors
- Analyze backlink profiles of top-ranking sites for {{target-keywords}}
- Highlight link gaps and quick-win opportunities

### 2. Prospect Research
- List 10-15 high-authority domains relevant to {{industry}}
- Categorize by link type: guest posts, resource pages, directories, partnerships
- Assess domain authority, relevance, and traffic potential

### 3. Outreach Strategy
- Create personalized email templates that reference {{usp}} and {{content-assets}}
- Suggest value propositions for each prospect category
- Recommend follow-up cadence and timeline

### 4. Content Leverage Plan
- Match {{content-assets}} to prospect needs
- Identify content gaps requiring new assets
- Propose linkable asset formats (guides, tools, research, infographics)

### 5. Implementation Timeline
- Prioritize prospects by impact vs. effort
- Set monthly targets for outreach volume and link acquisition
- Define success metrics (referring domains, DR growth, keyword ranking lift)

## Output Format
Structure each section with clear headings, bullet points, and tables where appropriate. Prioritize actionable tactics over theory.
```
