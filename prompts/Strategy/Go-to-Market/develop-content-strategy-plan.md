# Content Strategy Plan Builder for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Content Strategy Plan Builder for Marketing Teams is a free AI prompt that creates detailed content strategy plans aligned to business objectives and audience insights. This content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and current performance data, then outputs a structured markdown table with five columns - Goals, Target Audience, Content Types, Distribution Channels, and Success Metrics - providing at least five rows of actionable strategy elements. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a cohesive plan where each row connects SMART goals to specific audience segments, recommended content formats, appropriate platforms, and measurable KPIs. Marketing teams, content managers, and go-to-market leads use it to align content initiatives with business outcomes and ensure every piece of content has a defined purpose and success measure. ● Maps SMART goals to specific audience segments, ensuring every content initiative is purpose-driven and measurable ● Identifies content types and distribution channels that align with both business objectives and audience behavior patterns ● Establishes clear KPIs and measurement methods for tracking performance and optimizing the strategy over time ● Delivers output as a markdown table that stakeholders can immediately review, share, and iterate on ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist creating comprehensive content strategy plans.

## Task
Develop a detailed content strategy plan structured as a markdown table with five columns: Goals, Target Audience, Content Types, Distribution Channels, and Success Metrics. Provide at least 5 rows of detailed, actionable information.

## Context
Analyze the business context to understand market position, competitive landscape, and current performance. Define SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) that align with business objectives. Describe target audience segments including demographics, psychographics, and behavioral characteristics. Recommend content types and distribution channels that resonate with the audience and support the goals. Establish KPIs to measure content strategy success.

**Business Context:**
{{business-context}}

**Current Performance Baseline:**
{{current-content-performance}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Goals**: SMART objectives tied to business outcomes
- **Target Audience**: Detailed segment profiles (demographics, psychographics, behaviors)
- **Content Types**: Formats and themes aligned to audience preferences and goals
- **Distribution Channels**: Platforms matching audience habits
- **Success Metrics**: Specific KPIs and measurement methods

Ensure each row presents a cohesive strategy element where all five columns connect logically.
```
