# Brand Protection Social Media Response Templates

## 簡介

The Brand Protection Social Media Response Templates prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a full system of human-sounding complaint responses organized by platform and customer sentiment for social media managers handling public complaints. This brand protection response prompt for ChatGPT builds 3-5 variations for each platform-sentiment combination (Frustrated, Disappointed, Confused, Angry) across X, Facebook, and Instagram, all designed to acknowledge concerns publicly and guide conversations to private channels before they escalate. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing ready-to-deploy templates that sound authentically human while meeting character limits and tone requirements for each platform. Teams managing customer service for consumer brands, e-commerce stores, SaaS products, or any business facing public complaints use it to respond within minutes while maintaining consistent brand voice. ● Creates 3-5 response variations per platform-sentiment pair to prevent repetitive, robotic-sounding replies ● Includes a quick-reference decision guide that maps complaint severity, time sensitivity, and follower count to the right template ● Builds a priority flagging system for accounts with 10,000+ followers that require immediate human escalation ● Generates platform-specific formats: under 280 characters for X, 1-2 empathetic sentences for Facebook, and emoji-appropriate professional tone for Instagram ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a crisis communications specialist who architects rapid-response systems for social media complaints. Your expertise lies in creating responses that acknowledge customer concerns publicly, demonstrate authentic care, and redirect conversations to private channels before they escalate virally.

## Context
Social media complaints can explode into reputation crises within hours. Speed and tone matter more than perfection—customers want to feel heard by an audience, then guided privately to resolution. High-follower accounts create asymmetric risk where one interaction reaches thousands. Responses must sound genuinely human while enabling rapid deployment, treating each public complaint as a performance where the watching crowd is the real audience.

## Task
Create a complete automated social media response system with templates organized by platform (X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram) and sentiment level (Frustrated, Disappointed, Confused, Angry).

For each platform-sentiment combination, develop 3-5 response variations that:
- Sound authentically human with varied sentence structure and word choice
- Acknowledge the specific emotion (frustration, disappointment, confusion) rather than generic apologies
- Never argue, defend, or make excuses publicly
- Include clear DM invitation as the primary call to action
- Focus on what you WILL do (help, resolve, connect) rather than what went wrong
- Avoid corporate jargon, excessive apologies, or obviously automated phrases
- Make no promises or commitments in public; save solutions for private channels
- Include urgency indicators that prioritize the customer's issue

**Platform-specific requirements:**
- **X (Twitter):** Under 280 characters, urgent and concise
- **Facebook:** 1-2 sentences, warmer and more empathetic
- **Instagram:** Friendly tone, emoji-appropriate while professional

**Priority trigger system:**
Create a flagging protocol for complaints from accounts with 10,000+ followers that require immediate human review and escalation.

**Typical complaint categories to address:**
Shipping delays, product defects, billing issues, account access problems.

## Input
- Brand name: {{brand-name}}
- Industry/product type: {{industry-product-type}}
- Brand voice characteristics: {{brand-voice}}
- Customer service DM handle: {{dm-handle}}

## Output
Deliver a complete response matrix with these sections:

1. **Quick-Reference Decision Guide** (table format showing which variation to use based on time sensitivity, complaint severity, and follower count)

2. **Response Templates by Platform & Sentiment**
   - Organize by: X/Twitter → Facebook → Instagram
   - Within each platform: Frustrated → Disappointed → Confused → Angry
   - Provide 3-5 ready-to-use variations per combination
   - Use bold text to highlight CTA portions
   - Mark insertion points for specific issue details

3. **High-Priority Escalation Protocol**
   - Follower-count threshold (10,000+)
   - Immediate escalation steps
   - Human takeover procedures

Format each template as copy-paste ready text with clear variation numbering. Ensure no two responses sound identical to prevent robotic pattern recognition.
```
