# Weekly Schedule Template Builder for Time Blocking

## 簡介

The Weekly Schedule Template Builder for Time Blocking is a free AI prompt that creates a personalized 7-day task schedule with emoji-coded time blocks designed around your natural productivity rhythms and work preferences. This weekly schedule prompt for ChatGPT generates a Monday-through-Sunday hourly grid that categorizes work into deep focus, shallow tasks, meetings, learning time, and breaks using an intuitive color emoji system. You provide your work profile - typical hours, peak energy windows, meeting commitments, and skill development goals - and the prompt designs a balanced schedule that aligns cognitively demanding work with your best hours, builds in strategic breaks, and leaves room for unexpected priorities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text AI platforms. This prompt is ideal for remote workers, freelancers, managers, and students who want to move from reactive daily planning to proactive weekly time blocking that respects both productivity science and personal work rhythms. ● Outputs a 7-column hourly table with five emoji-coded task categories for instant visual clarity ● Aligns deep work blocks with stated peak productivity windows and schedules routine tasks during lower-energy periods ● Incorporates 10-15 minute breaks, dedicated learning slots, and flexibility buffers to prevent burnout and accommodate changes ● Adapts to your unique work profile, including meeting schedules, preferred break times, and skill development goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity and scheduling expert specializing in time management and optimal work schedule design.

## Task
Generate a weekly task schedule template that effectively allocates time blocks for various work activities based on the user's work patterns and preferences.

## Context
{{work-profile}}
(Include: typical work hours, most productive time of day, preferred break times, weekly meeting schedule, learning/skill development goals)

## Output
Create a 7-column table (Monday–Sunday) with hourly time slot rows. Use these emoji codes for task types:
- 🟥 Deep Work (focused, cognitively demanding)
- 🟨 Shallow Work (routine, administrative)
- 🟩 Meetings & Collaboration
- 🟦 Learning & Skill Development
- ⬜ Breaks & Personal Time

Structure:
```
Time | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday | Saturday | Sunday
8:00 | 🟥 | 🟥 | 🟥 | 🟥 | 🟥 | ⬜ | ⬜...
```

**Design principles:**
- Assign demanding tasks to morning slots when energy peaks
- Allocate 90-120 minute blocks for deep work
- Use 30-60 minute blocks for shallow work
- Schedule meetings/collaboration in afternoons when possible
- Include 10-15 minute breaks between tasks
- Dedicate specific slots for skill development
- Leave flexibility for unexpected priorities
- Balance productivity with breaks and personal time
- Adapt to the user's stated energy patterns and preferences
```
