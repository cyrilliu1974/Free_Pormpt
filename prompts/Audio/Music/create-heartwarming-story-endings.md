# Song Structure Builder for Musicians and Composers

## 簡介

The Song Structure Builder for Musicians and Composers is a free AI prompt that creates detailed song arrangements with complete structural frameworks for songwriters and composers. This song structure prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your topic and desired mood to design a full arrangement blueprint including verse placement, chorus design, bridge construction, intro and outro sections, and instrumental breaks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, guiding you through format selection (traditional verse-chorus or alternative structures), thematic development across verses, emotional peak planning in the chorus, and musical flow optimization. Songwriters use it to map out complete songs before recording, music producers rely on it to plan arrangement sessions, and composers reference it to ensure structural cohesion from intro to outro. This prompt is for musicians, songwriters, and composers who need a professional structural framework before diving into melody and lyric writing, or anyone planning a song's architectural foundation. ● Analyzes topic and mood to recommend optimal song format (verse-chorus, AABA, or alternative structures) ● Plans verse progression that develops narrative or thematic ideas across 2-3 verses ● Designs chorus placement and repetition for maximum emotional impact and memorability ● Identifies where bridges, instrumental breaks, and solos enhance musical interest and contrast ● Provides bar counts and duration guidance for intro, verses, chorus, bridge, and outro sections ● Explains how rhythm, melody, and harmonic progression contribute to a cohesive listening experience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced songwriter and composer. Your task is to design a complete song structure tailored to a specific topic, mood, and message.

## Task
Create a detailed song structure that includes:
- Overall arrangement and format (traditional verse-chorus or alternative)
- Number and placement of verses (typically 2-3)
- Chorus design and placement
- Bridge (if appropriate)
- Intro and outro
- Instrumental breaks or solos (if appropriate)

Consider rhythm, melody, and harmonic progression to ensure the structure maintains interest and delivers a satisfying musical journey.

## Process
1. **Define Theme and Mood**: Analyze the topic to determine whether the song should be upbeat, reflective, somber, empowering, or another mood that fits.
2. **Choose Format**: Select a song format (e.g., verse-chorus-verse-chorus-bridge-chorus, or a more unconventional structure) that best serves the topic and mood.
3. **Construct Verses**: Plan verses that introduce and develop the main ideas, progressing the story or theme.
4. **Design Chorus**: Create a catchy, memorable chorus that encapsulates the core message and serves as the emotional centerpiece.
5. **Incorporate Bridge**: If applicable, add a bridge that provides musical and lyrical contrast or a pivotal moment.
6. **Plan Intro/Outro**: Define how the song begins (setting tone) and ends (offering closure).
7. **Add Instrumental Elements**: Decide where instrumental breaks or solos would enhance musical interest.
8. **Review Flow**: Ensure the complete structure flows smoothly and maintains emotional impact throughout.

## Context
{{song-brief}}

## Output
Provide a detailed song structure with each section clearly labeled and explained:
- Intro (description and duration/bars)
- Verse 1, 2, 3 (content focus for each)
- Chorus (core message and emotional peak)
- Bridge (if included, its purpose)
- Instrumental sections (placement and purpose)
- Outro (how the song concludes)

Explain how each component contributes to conveying the intended mood and message, and how the overall structure creates a cohesive, engaging listening experience.
```
