# B2B Pitch Email Writer for Sales Outreach

## 簡介

The B2B Pitch Email Writer for Sales Outreach is a free AI prompt that creates compelling sales emails connecting specific client pain points to measurable product benefits for B2B sales teams and business development professionals. This pitch email prompt for ChatGPT guides you through structuring a complete sales email from subject line to call-to-action. You provide details about your target client, their pain point, your product or service, your unique selling proposition, and desired next step - the prompt then generates a persuasive email that opens with a relevant hook, articulates business impact, presents your solution, highlights concrete benefits with specific outcomes, and closes with a frictionless ask. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured output with labeled sections for easy customization. Use it when you need to craft targeted outreach that resonates with decision-makers, when generic templates fall flat, or when you want to test multiple angles for different prospect segments. ● Structures emails around client-specific pain points rather than generic feature lists ● Delivers labeled sections (subject line, opening, problem statement, solution, benefits, call-to-action) for easy editing ● Guides logical flow where each sentence builds on the previous for clarity and persuasive impact ● Balances conversational tone with authoritative positioning to build trust quickly ## Prompt
