# Cinematic Editorial Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Editorial Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces studio-quality editorial portraits with warm, moody lighting and medium-format aesthetics for photographers, art directors, and digital artists. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney creates seated portraits against textured fabric backdrops with soft diffused key lighting and sculpted shadows. The prompt outputs a 50mm f/1.4 lens effect at ISO 200 with shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, and soft vignetting in a 3:4 aspect ratio. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering a painterly color palette and medium-format look that mirrors high-end editorial photography. Use it for character studies, brand campaigns, magazine-style portraits, or any project requiring the warmth and depth of studio lighting.
