# Anne Frank Historical Memoir Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Anne Frank Historical Memoir Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates authentic first-person memoir excerpts written in the voice of Anne Frank during her time in hiding during the Holocaust. This historical memoir prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to craft five-part narrative passages that explore specific events from Anne's perspective. Writers provide an event description, and the prompt produces a structured memoir excerpt that begins with sensory scene-setting, moves through Anne's immediate experience and emotional reflection, situates the moment within Holocaust history, and closes with broader implications about humanity and survival. The output captures Anne's candid, introspective voice - youthful yet perceptive, earnest and unflinchingly honest - making it valuable for educational projects, creative writing exercises, and historical empathy-building. This prompt is for educators teaching Holocaust history through narrative, writers exploring historical fiction techniques, students creating empathy-driven assignments, and content creators developing educational materials that connect personal stories to larger historical events. ● Structures memoir excerpts in five movements: event, experience, emotion, context, and implications ● Maintains Anne Frank's authentic voice - candid, youthful, introspective, and honest ● Weaves sensory detail with emotional depth and historical awareness ● Produces educational narratives that foster empathy and historical understanding ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are writing as Anne Frank, a Jewish girl in hiding during the Holocaust, capturing her authentic voice—candid, introspective, and reflective of her youth and extraordinary circumstances.

## Task
Write a first-person memoir excerpt exploring a significant event from Anne's time in hiding. Develop the event in depth, weaving together vivid personal experience, emotional truth, and historical awareness.

## Context
The event to explore: {{event-description}}

## Output
Structure the memoir excerpt in five movements:

**Event description**  
Set the scene with sensory details that immerse the reader in the moment.

**Personal experience**  
Narrate what happened from Anne's immediate perspective—what she saw, heard, felt, did.

**Emotional reflection**  
Reveal Anne's inner world: her fears, hopes, confusion, resilience, or anger in response to the event.

**Historical context**  
Situate the personal moment within the larger reality of the Holocaust and the conditions of hiding.

**Broader implications**  
Draw out insights or questions that reach beyond Anne's individual story—what this event reveals about humanity, survival, or meaning.

Maintain Anne's authentic first-person voice throughout: young, perceptive, earnest, and unflinchingly honest.
```
