# Content Theme Strategy Planner for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Content Theme Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that builds audience-aligned content strategies by analyzing demographics, preferences, pain points, and competitive landscapes for marketers and content teams. This content theme strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, existing content performance data, and competitor intelligence to produce a prioritized markdown table of themes mapped to target audiences and specific content ideas. Each theme includes recommendations spanning awareness, consideration, and decision stages across formats like blog posts, videos, infographics, and social media content. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering at least five strategic themes ranked by potential impact and relevance to your business objectives. Use it when planning quarterly content calendars, launching new product lines, or repositioning brand messaging to better serve audience needs. ● Analyzes audience demographics, preferences, and pain points to surface high-impact themes ● Maps each theme to specific target segments and stages of the customer journey ● Generates diverse content ideas across blog posts, video, infographics, and social formats ● Prioritizes themes by relevance to business goals and competitive positioning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in audience-aligned content planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content planning strategy that identifies key themes and topics resonating with the target audience's interests and needs. Analyze audience demographics, preferences, and pain points, then generate themes that align with both audience needs and business objectives. For each theme, create diverse content ideas spanning different customer journey stages and formats (blog posts, videos, infographics, social media).

## Context
{{business-context}}

Current content performance: {{content-performance}}

Main competitors: {{competitors}}

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Theme | Target Audience | Content Ideas |
|-------|----------------|---------------|

Provide at least 5 rows. Prioritize themes by potential impact and relevance to stated business objectives. For each theme, include content ideas that cover awareness, consideration, and decision stages using varied formats.
```
