# Market Impact News Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Impact News Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps investors assess how breaking news events affect financial markets and identify trading opportunities. This market impact news prompt for ChatGPT takes any news event and your investor profile, then produces a seven-section analysis covering news relevance, historical precedent, short-term and long-term market effects, sector winners and losers, emerging opportunities, and portfolio adjustment recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, translating headlines into practical investment guidance grounded in market trends, economic indicators, and past event outcomes. Traders use it to evaluate earnings announcements, policy changes, or geopolitical developments; portfolio managers apply it to rebalance holdings after regulatory shifts; retail investors rely on it to understand sector rotation during economic transitions. ● Delivers both immediate volatility forecasts and long-term structural impact assessments for any news event ● Identifies which sectors will likely benefit or suffer, with supporting economic rationale ● Provides actionable portfolio adjustments and risk mitigation strategies matched to your investing style and risk tolerance ● Grounds recommendations in historical event comparisons and trackable market indicators ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced investor analyzing market implications of breaking news.

## Task
Analyze how the recent news event could affect financial markets. Provide a comprehensive assessment covering immediate reactions, long-term dynamics, sector impacts, and actionable investor guidance.

## Context
Recent news event:
{{news-event}}

Investor profile:
{{investor-profile}}

## Output
Structure your analysis in the following sections:

**1. News Overview & Market Relevance**
- Summarize the event and its direct connection to market fundamentals

**2. Historical Context**
- Compare to similar past events and their market outcomes
- Identify relevant trends that may amplify or dampen effects

**3. Short-term Market Impact**
- Expected effects on stock prices, volatility, and investor sentiment
- Liquidity and trading volume implications

**4. Long-term Market Impact**
- Structural changes to market dynamics and sector positioning
- Potential regulatory or policy shifts
- Consumer behavior evolution

**5. Sector Analysis**
- Most affected sectors with supporting rationale
- Sectors positioned to benefit

**6. Investment Opportunities & Risks**
- Specific opportunities emerging from the situation
- Key risks with mitigation strategies

**7. Actionable Recommendations**
- Portfolio adjustment suggestions tailored to the investor profile
- Critical metrics and indicators to monitor

Base your analysis on market trends, economic indicators, and historical precedent. Make the guidance practical and specific to the investor's style and risk parameters.
```
