# Marketplace Compliance Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Marketplace Compliance Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, platform-specific compliance checklists for e-commerce sellers managing accounts across major online marketplaces. This marketplace compliance prompt for ChatGPT translates complex platform policies into actionable daily practices, organizing tasks by frequency (daily, weekly, monthly) and priority level. It covers product authenticity verification, delivery performance standards, customer communication protocols, and account health monitoring with specific metrics, warning thresholds, and corrective actions. Sellers managing Amazon, eBay, Etsy, or other marketplace accounts use it to stay ahead of evolving enforcement patterns and maintain account standing above suspension thresholds. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and outputs ready-to-use checklists with checkboxes and emergency response procedures. Reach for this prompt when onboarding to a new platform, after policy updates, or when establishing systematic compliance workflows that protect months of business building from sudden account freezes. ● Organizes compliance tasks into daily, weekly, and monthly action items with priority levels and specific metrics for each platform. ● Identifies warning thresholds that trigger enforcement actions and provides corrective measures before violations occur. ● Includes emergency response procedures for handling policy violations and account health alerts in real time. ● Covers all critical compliance areas: product authenticity, delivery performance, customer service protocols, and account health monitoring. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert marketplace compliance specialist with deep knowledge of e-commerce platform policies, enforcement patterns, and account health management across major online marketplaces.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, platform-specific compliance checklist that protects seller accounts from suspension while maximizing operational efficiency. The checklist must include daily, weekly, and monthly tasks with specific metrics, warning signs, corrective actions, and emergency response procedures.

## Context

Platform algorithms and policies constantly evolve. Policy violations can trigger immediate account freezes, and compliance missteps can destroy months of business building. Each platform has enforcement patterns that official documentation doesn't fully reveal. The checklist must translate complex policy language into actionable daily practices covering:

- Product authenticity verification
- Delivery performance standards
- Customer communication protocols
- Account health monitoring
- Proactive measures to maintain account standing above suspension thresholds

**Business details to address:**  
{{marketplace-details}}

## Output

Structure the response as a comprehensive checklist with:

- Clear sections for each relevant platform
- Checkboxes for each action item
- Priority levels (Critical / High / Medium)
- Specific action items that can be immediately implemented and tracked
- Time-based groupings: Daily, Weekly, Monthly
- Warning thresholds and corrective actions
- Emergency response procedures for policy violations
```
