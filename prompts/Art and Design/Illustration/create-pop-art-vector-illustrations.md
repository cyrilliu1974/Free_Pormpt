# Pop Art Vector Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Pop Art Vector Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that produces Roy Lichtenstein-inspired vector artwork with signature Ben-Day dot patterns, bold black outlines, and vibrant primary colors. This pop art illustration prompt for Midjourney works by combining your subject and composition details with authentic mid-century comic book aesthetics - flat color fills, crisp vector edges, and halftone accents that define the pop art movement. Use it to create eye-catching posters, album covers, editorial illustrations, or brand identity elements that demand attention. ● Produces illustrations with authentic Ben-Day dot background patterns and bold black outlines characteristic of Lichtenstein's comic book style ● Accepts custom subject matter and composition details (shot type, pose, expression) while maintaining the crisp vector aesthetic ● Outputs flat color fills with vibrant primary colors and halftone accents, no gradients or realistic shading ● Configurable aspect ratios to fit posters, social media graphics, album art, or editorial layouts ## Prompt
