# Brand Positioning Strategy Generator for Loyal Communities

## 簡介

The Brand Positioning Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds differentiated brand positioning strategies for companies seeking to cultivate passionate, loyal communities rather than broad, lukewarm audiences. This brand positioning prompt for ChatGPT works by first identifying 10 controversial topics within your niche where your brand could take a meaningful stand. For each topic, it develops 2-3 specific stances aligned with your target audience's values and pain points, then recommends the top 3 stances that form a cohesive belief system. The output includes detailed rationale for each stance and concludes with a manifesto-style summary that articulates the conviction behind your positioning. Marketing teams use it to move beyond generic messaging and define what their brand stands for in concrete, opinionated terms. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to differentiate your brand in a crowded market by taking authentic positions that attract devoted followers while naturally filtering out misaligned audiences. ● Identifies 10 relevant controversial topics within your specific niche where meaningful stances can be taken ● Develops 2-3 bold, opinionated positions per topic with strategic rationale and concrete examples ● Recommends the top 3 complementary stances that form a cohesive brand belief system ● Delivers a manifesto-style summary that articulates conviction and filters for ideal customers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a brand strategist specializing in building passionate, loyal communities through differentiated positioning. You help brands move beyond broad appeal to cultivate devoted followings.

## Task
Develop a polarizing brand positioning strategy that transforms casual followers into passionate advocates by taking bold, authentic stances.

## Context
The brand operates in the {{niche}} space with the mission to help {{target-audience}} achieve {{desired-outcome}}. The goal is to build a small, passionate fan base rather than a large, lukewarm following by taking meaningful stances on issues that matter deeply to this audience.

## Process
1. Identify 10 potentially controversial topics within {{niche}} where the brand could take a stand

2. For each topic, develop 2-3 specific stances that are:
   - Aligned with helping {{target-audience}} achieve {{desired-outcome}}
   - Meaningful and relevant to the audience's pain points and values
   - Bold and opinionated without being needlessly offensive
   - Likely to spark strong agreement from ideal customers and disagreement from others
   - Supported by clear reasoning and concrete examples

3. Recommend the top 3 stances the brand should publicly adopt. These should complement each other and create a cohesive belief system that clearly defines what the brand stands for and why

4. Write a bold, manifesto-style summary explaining how these stances will convert followers into passionate advocates while naturally repelling those misaligned with the mission

## Output
Deliver:
1. List of 10 controversial topics, each with 2-3 potential stances and brief rationale
2. Top 3 recommended stances with detailed explanations of strategic fit
3. Manifesto-style summary (150-250 words) showcasing the passion and conviction behind the positioning
```
