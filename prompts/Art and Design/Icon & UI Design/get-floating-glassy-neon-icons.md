# Floating Glassy Neon Icon Retexture Prompt

## 簡介

The Floating Glassy Neon Icon Retexture Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms uploaded images into translucent 3D neon glass icons for designers, marketers, and creative professionals. This floating glassy neon icon prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana applies geometry-sensitive retexturing that maintains the original shape, contours, and layering of your image while wrapping it in a semi-gloss neon glass-gel material. The prompt specifies aqua, electric blue, and neon violet gradients with internal light scatter, soft glow edges, gentle refraction halos, and softbox HDRI lighting on a clean white background. Designers reach for it when they need product icons, UI elements, or branding visuals that combine technical precision with eye-catching neon aesthetics. ● Preserves original silhouette, contours, volumes, and layering through geometry-sensitive mapping while applying the neon glass material. ● Specifies translucent glass-gel with soft glow edges, light refraction halos, internal light scatter, and edge neon glow for a weightless floating effect. ● Defines aqua-to-violet gradient flow, softbox HDRI overhead lighting, glass sparkle highlights, and extremely soft elliptical contact shadows. ● Outputs on solid white background with subtle depth of field, high specular detail, and very high resolution optimized for product branding. ## Prompt

```
Retexture the attached image in Floating Glassy Neon 3D style using {{retexture-parameters}}.

Preserve the original silhouette, contours, volumes, and layering with geometry-sensitive mapping.

Shadow: extremely soft, blurred elliptical contact shadow below the floating object. Neutral gray at 7% opacity, moderate distance.

Background: solid white (#ffffff), no glow or gradient.

Camera: slightly above object, frontal view, subtle depth of field with slight vignette focused on center. Minimal ambient occlusion to preserve light feel.

Render: 3D stylized with light diffusion and high specular detail. Very high resolution for product branding. Visual weightlessness with maximum shadow softness.
```
