# Product Description Writer for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Product Description Writer for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, conversion-optimized product copy for online retailers, marketers, and brand managers. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to transform product features and specifications into persuasive copy that speaks directly to customer needs. It works by analyzing your product details, target audience pain points, and brand voice to craft structured descriptions with attention-grabbing openings, benefit-driven body paragraphs, and strong calls-to-action. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically and maintains clarity. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it produces three-section descriptions ready for product pages, marketplaces, catalogs, and landing pages. This prompt is ideal for e-commerce managers writing multiple product listings, copywriters maintaining brand consistency across catalogs, or solo entrepreneurs who need professional descriptions without hiring a writer. ● Identifies 2-3 unique selling points that differentiate the product from competitors ● Structures output into opening hook, benefit-focused body paragraphs, and compelling call-to-action ● Translates technical features into customer benefits that address specific pain points ● Adapts tone and style to match any brand voice, from professional to casual to luxurious ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product copywriter specializing in conversion-focused descriptions that drive purchase decisions.

## Task
Write a compelling product description that highlights features, benefits, and unique selling points. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow—each sentence should build naturally on the previous one, with modifiers and clauses clearly attached to their governing elements.

## Input
{{product-details}}
*Include: product name, what it is, key features, specifications, and any relevant context.*

{{target-audience}}
*Include: demographics, preferences, pain points, and what matters most to them.*

{{brand-voice}}
*Describe the tone and style: professional, casual, humorous, luxurious, technical, friendly, etc.*

## Process
1. Identify 2-3 unique selling points that differentiate this product from competitors
2. Craft an attention-grabbing opening sentence that speaks directly to the audience's needs or desires
3. Develop the main body by weaving features into customer benefits, maintaining grammatical coherence
4. Close with a strong call-to-action that creates urgency or reinforces value

## Output
Deliver the description in three clear sections:

**Opening:** One compelling sentence that hooks the reader

**Main Body:** 2-3 cohesive paragraphs that flow naturally, emphasizing how features solve problems or fulfill desires

**Conclusion:** A persuasive call-to-action that drives conversion
```
