# Reverse Goal-Setting Workshop Builder for Facilitators

## 簡介

The Reverse Goal-Setting Workshop Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured workshop plans for facilitators teaching participants to work backward from their long-term vision to actionable milestones. This reverse goal-setting prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete four-session workshop agenda with interactive exercises, reflection prompts, action planning templates, and follow-up resources. Facilitators input their target audience profile, desired duration, and learning objectives, and the prompt delivers a cohesive workshop structure that progresses logically from vision identification through milestone mapping to daily action steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce workshop overviews, session descriptions, practical exercises, and participant handouts. Use cases include corporate training programs, professional development workshops, academic goal-setting seminars, and personal growth retreats where backward planning methodologies enhance clarity and focus. This prompt is for workshop facilitators, instructional designers, coaches, and organizational development professionals who need to design engaging goal-setting experiences without starting from scratch. ● Generates four distinct workshop sessions with clear titles, durations, descriptions, and interactive exercises ● Provides 3-5 reflection prompts that deepen participant insight into their vision and planning process ● Includes 3-4 action planning tools and templates participants can apply immediately after the workshop ● Delivers follow-up resources to support continued progress and goal management beyond the session ## Prompt
