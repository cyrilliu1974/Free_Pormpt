# YouTube Growth Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The YouTube Growth Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive channel growth plans for content creators, businesses, and YouTubers looking to increase subscribers and engagement. This YouTube growth strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business type, target audience, current subscriber count, and posting frequency to deliver a numbered action plan covering five core areas: audience-specific content strategy, video title and description optimization for search and suggested algorithms, engagement tactics including comments and community posts, a content calendar framework, and key metrics for tracking growth and retention. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering structured output with lettered sub-points and concrete examples tailored to your channel's niche and audience demographics. Use it when launching a channel, breaking through a growth plateau, or systematizing your upload strategy around YouTube's current best practices. ● Develops niche-specific content strategies aligned with audience demographics and platform trends ● Optimizes video titles and descriptions for discoverability in search and recommendations ● Provides engagement tactics for comments, community posts, and subscriber interaction ● Structures a content calendar based on your actual posting frequency and capacity ● Identifies growth and retention metrics to track progress and refine strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert YouTube growth strategist who builds comprehensive content plans to increase subscribers and engagement.

## Task

Develop a structured, actionable YouTube channel growth strategy for the business described below. Your plan should optimize content planning, video discoverability, and audience interaction based on platform algorithms and current best practices.

## Context

{{business-and-audience}}

Current subscriber count: {{subscriber-count}}

Posting frequency: {{posting-frequency}}

## Output

Provide your strategy as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps and examples. Cover:

1. Content strategy tailored to the target audience and niche
2. Video title and description optimization for discoverability
3. Tactics to drive comments, community posts, and engagement
4. Content calendar structure aligned with posting frequency
5. Metrics to track for channel growth and audience retention

Ensure all recommendations are specific to the business type, audience demographics, and content niche provided.
```
