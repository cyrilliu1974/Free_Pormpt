# Competitive SEO Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Competitive SEO Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured SEO intelligence reports for businesses analyzing competitor search strategies in any industry niche. This competitive SEO analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a systematic examination of your top five competitors using frameworks modeled on SEMrush, Ahrefs, and Moz methodologies. It extracts target keywords (short-tail and long-tail), classifies search intent, documents domain authority and backlink profiles, and identifies content formats that rank well. The output is a multi-section strategic report complete with comparative keyword tables, opportunity ratings, and an implementation roadmap tailored to your current keywords, value propositions, and target audience. Marketing teams use it to discover high-volume low-competition keywords, content strategists rely on it to find gaps in competitor coverage, and SEO consultants deploy it to build client recommendations backed by structured competitive data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework for competitor keyword research that goes beyond surface-level analysis to deliver prioritized action steps. ● Identifies the top 5 competitors by domain authority and extracts their target keywords with volume, difficulty, CPC, and search intent classification ● Analyzes on-page tactics, content strategies, backlink profiles, and SERP features that drive competitor rankings ● Produces comparative keyword tables highlighting common keywords, unique opportunities, and high-value gaps your business can exploit ● Delivers an actionable implementation roadmap with content topics, on-page improvements, and backlink strategies specific to your niche and value propositions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist focused on competitive keyword analysis and search strategy.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitive keyword analysis of the top 5 competitors in {{niche}}. Analyze their SEO strategies using industry-standard tools (SEMrush, Ahrefs, or Moz) to identify target keywords, search intent, backlink profiles, domain authority, and other ranking factors. Deliver a strategic report with actionable recommendations for gaining competitive advantage.

## Context
Our company operates in {{niche}} with the following profile:
- Current main keywords: {{current-keywords}}
- Unique value propositions: {{value-propositions}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Analysis Framework

**1. Competitor Identification**
Identify the top 5 competitors based on domain authority and search visibility for relevant keywords in {{niche}}.

**2. Keyword Research Per Competitor**
- Extract main short-tail and long-tail keywords
- Document keyword volume, difficulty, CPC, and SERP features
- Classify search intent: informational, navigational, transactional, commercial
- Note context and usage patterns

**3. SEO Metrics Analysis**
- Domain authority, page authority, backlink quantity and quality
- Content strategy: formats (blog posts, videos, infographics) that rank well
- On-page tactics: meta titles, descriptions, header tags, keyword density

**4. Gap and Opportunity Identification**
- Common keywords across competitors vs. unique keywords per competitor
- High-volume, low-competition keyword opportunities
- Content and strategy gaps we can exploit

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive report document structured as follows:

**Executive Summary**: Overview of findings and top recommendations

**Methodology**: Tools used and analysis approach

**Detailed Findings**: Per-competitor breakdowns of keywords, metrics, and strategies

**Comparative Keyword Analysis Table**: Common vs. unique keywords, volumes, difficulty scores, and opportunity ratings

**Strategic Recommendations**:
- Priority keywords and niches for competitive advantage
- Content topics and formats based on competitor gaps and our unique value propositions
- On-page SEO improvements
- Backlink acquisition strategies
- Implementation roadmap with clear next steps

Format with clear headings, subheadings, and tables. Make all recommendations actionable and specific to our context in {{niche}}.
```
