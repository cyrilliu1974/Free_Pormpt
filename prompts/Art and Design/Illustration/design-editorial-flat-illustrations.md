# Editorial Flat Illustration Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Editorial Flat Illustration Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that produces striking conceptual graphics with flat shading, simplified human figures, and bold visual metaphors for articles, presentations, and brand storytelling. This editorial illustration prompt for Midjourney creates centered compositions with a restricted three-color palette (deep black, white, and bright blue), oversized metaphor elements, and generous negative space ideal for overlaying text or headlines. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, letting you translate abstract themes into instant visual communication. Content creators, marketers, and editorial teams use it to illustrate complex ideas - technology versus humanity, growth, isolation, connection - without hiring illustrators or learning design tools. Reach for this prompt when you need magazine-quality graphics that communicate a concept at a glance, whether for blog headers, social media, pitch decks, or editorial campaigns. ● Produces flat-shaded illustrations with very high contrast and no gradients, ensuring clean reproduction across print and digital. ● Centers simplified human silhouettes against solid dark backgrounds with oversized symbolic elements that convey abstract ideas instantly. ● Uses a three-color palette (black #0E0E11, white #FFFFFF, blue #4D7CFE) for consistent brand-ready assets. ● Outputs 1:1 high-resolution images with ample negative space, perfect for adding headlines, captions, or logos. ## Prompt

