# E-Commerce Product Description Generator

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Product Description Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms product specifications into emotionally resonant marketplace listings for e-commerce sellers and copywriters. This product description prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to structure marketplace-ready copy that opens with a sensory hook, maps features to lifestyle benefits, incorporates storytelling around craftsmanship or inspiration, details customization options when relevant, and closes with a clear call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking product-name and product-details as inputs to produce listings optimized for platforms like Etsy, Shopify, Amazon Handmade, and independent storefronts. Real use cases include launching new products without a copywriting team, refreshing underperforming listings, and maintaining consistent brand voice across large catalogs. Reach for this prompt when you need structured, conversion-focused descriptions that balance emotional appeal with practical details like dimensions, care instructions, and shipping information. ● Structures descriptions into scannable sections: opening hook, features-to-benefits mapping, emotional storytelling, customization details, call-to-action, and practical specifications ● Guides the AI to use sensory language and vivid imagery that help shoppers visualize owning and using the product ● Adapts tone to match the brand voice implied by your product category and target audience ● Produces marketplace-ready copy that addresses buyer questions while driving purchase decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce copywriter specializing in marketplace product descriptions that convert browsers into buyers.

## Task
Write a compelling product description for {{product-name}} that showcases its value, connects emotionally with the target audience, and drives purchase decisions.

## Context
{{product-details}} should include: key features, materials, unique selling points (USPs), target audience, customization options (if any), dimensions, care instructions, and any other relevant specifications.

## Output
Structure the description as follows:

**Opening Hook**  
Craft 1-2 sentences using vivid, sensory language that capture the product's essence and appeal. Make readers curious to learn more.

**Features & Benefits**  
For each major feature, explain the customer benefit. Show how the product fits into their lifestyle or solves their problem. Use bullet points for clarity when listing multiple features.

**Emotional Connection**  
Weave in storytelling elements: the inspiration behind the product, the craftsmanship, or how it becomes part of memorable moments. Connect the product to the audience's aspirations or values.

**Customization** (if applicable)  
Detail personalization options that make the purchase feel unique and special.

**Call-to-Action**  
End with a clear, compelling CTA that encourages the next step: purchase, inquiry, or exploration.

**Practical Details**  
Provide dimensions, care instructions, packaging, and shipping information in an easy-to-scan format.

Tone: match the brand voice implied by the product and audience. Language: persuasive yet authentic, avoiding hype or empty claims.
```
