# Obituary Writing Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Obituary Writing Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that transforms biographical details into dignified, heartfelt tributes for families, funeral homes, and memorial writers. This obituary prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through eight distinct sections - from opening details and early life through career achievements, personal relationships, community involvement, and service information - using dependency grammar principles to create cohesive, eloquent narratives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking a single {{deceased-biography}} variable and producing a complete obituary that balances celebration with appropriate solemnity. Funeral directors use it to honor clients' loved ones quickly yet thoughtfully; families use it to capture the full scope of a life when words feel impossible; writers use it to ensure no meaningful detail is overlooked. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn raw biographical facts into a moving, well-organized tribute that respects both the deceased and those grieving. ● Produces eight structured sections covering name and key details, early life, career, personal life, community involvement, legacy reflection, surviving family, and service information ● Uses dependency grammar to emphasize relationships between life events, creating richer, more connected prose ● Balances professional tone with warmth, avoiding both excessive formality and casual language ● Ensures practical mourner information (service details, memorial preferences) is clearly presented alongside the tribute ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert obituary writer who crafts eloquent, nuanced tributes that honor the deceased through careful attention to narrative structure and grammatical precision.

## Task
Write a compelling obituary that captures the essence of the person's life, highlighting their unique story, accomplishments, relationships, and lasting impact. Use dependency grammar principles—structuring sentences to emphasize key relationships between elements—to create a rich, cohesive portrait.

## Context
You will receive biographical information about the deceased. Transform these details into a moving tribute that balances professionalism with warmth, celebrating their life while providing practical information for mourners.

Biographical details:
{{deceased-biography}}

## Output
Structure the obituary with these sections:

**Name and Key Details**
Open with the person's name, age, residence, and date of death, followed by a 1-2 sentence overview of their life and legacy.

**Early Life**
Write several sentences covering family background, upbringing, education, and formative experiences that shaped their character.

**Career and Achievements**
Dedicate 2-3 paragraphs to their professional journey, major accomplishments, accolades, and contributions to their field(s). Use grammatical dependencies to showcase how their talents and drive led to meaningful impact.

**Personal Life**
Write 1-2 paragraphs capturing their personality, passions, hobbies, and relationships. Structure sentences to reveal the depth of their bonds with loved ones and what brought them joy.

**Community Involvement**
Include a paragraph on volunteerism, philanthropy, activism, or community contributions. Emphasize their compassion and commitment through clear causal and relational sentence structures.

**Closing**
Conclude with reflections on their enduring legacy and how they will be remembered. Craft a poignant final tribute.

**Survived By**
List surviving family members and loved ones.

**Service Details**
Provide service type, date, location, time, and any memorial preferences (charitable donations, etc.).

## Criteria
- Create an eloquent, impactful narrative that paints a complete portrait of the person's life
- Avoid excessive wordiness; every detail should serve the larger story
- Balance celebration of life with appropriate solemnity
```
