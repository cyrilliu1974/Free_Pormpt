# Ultradian Rhythm Work Schedule Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Ultradian Rhythm Work Schedule Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured daily plans matching your natural energy cycles for productivity experts, remote workers, and anyone managing their own time. This ultradian rhythm prompt for ChatGPT generates a markdown table schedule showing your customized work and break cycles throughout the day, then delivers practical implementation guidance. You specify your preferred work duration, break duration, and schedule format, and the prompt produces a complete plan with tips for maximizing productivity during high-energy windows, strategies for optimizing recovery during breaks, task prioritization advice, environment setup recommendations, and solutions for common obstacles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you want to reduce burnout, maintain focus across longer work sessions, or design a time-management system that respects your body's natural 90–120 minute energy cycles instead of fighting them. ● Produces a markdown table mapping work and break cycles across your full workday ● Includes task prioritization guidance so you tackle high-cognitive-load work during peak energy windows ● Offers environment optimization tips and obstacle-mitigation strategies for real-world adherence ● Fully customizable via three variables: work duration, break duration, and schedule format ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in ultradian rhythms and time management.

## Task
Create a personalized work schedule that aligns with natural energy cycles. Design a structured daily plan incorporating focused work periods and restorative breaks. Provide a detailed explanation of implementation, including:

- Tips for maximizing productivity during work cycles
- Strategies for optimizing recovery during breaks
- Guidance on task prioritization within cycles
- Environment optimization recommendations
- Common obstacles to adherence and how to overcome them

## Context
Work cycle duration: {{work-duration}}
Break cycle duration: {{break-duration}}
Schedule structure: {{schedule-format}}

## Output
Present the schedule as a markdown table showing work and break cycles throughout the day. Follow the table with practical implementation guidance covering all aspects listed in the task section.
```
