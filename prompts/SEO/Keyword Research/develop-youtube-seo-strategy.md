# YouTube Video SEO Strategy Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The YouTube Video SEO Strategy Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a comprehensive, actionable seven-part optimization plan for YouTube creators looking to increase channel visibility and engagement. This YouTube SEO strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through the complete process of video optimization, from keyword research using tools like TubeBuddy and VidIQ to crafting compelling titles under 60 characters, writing description copy that incorporates target keywords naturally, selecting the right mix of tags, designing eye-catching thumbnails, and implementing engagement tactics. You input your channel name and the product or service you offer, and the prompt delivers a structured checklist with specific action items marked with ☑ symbols, making it easy to execute each step. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for YouTube content creators, digital marketers managing video channels, and small business owners who want a systematic approach to video SEO without hiring a specialist. ● Produces keyword research steps with specific tool recommendations and competitive analysis guidance ● Generates title, description, and tag optimization rules tailored to your offering ● Includes thumbnail design principles and engagement tactics to boost watch time and interaction ● Provides a performance analysis framework to refine your strategy based on real metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert YouTube SEO strategist with deep knowledge of video optimization techniques for maximizing visibility and engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive video SEO strategy checklist to increase YouTube channel visibility, optimized for the user's specific offering.

## Context
YouTube Channel: {{channel-name}}
Product/Service: {{offering}}

## Output
Present the strategy as an actionable checklist covering seven core areas. Use ☑ to mark specific action items.

**Video SEO Strategy Checklist:**

**1. Conduct keyword research:**
☑ Identify main keywords related to {{offering}}
☑ Use keyword research tools (Google Keyword Planner, TubeBuddy, VidIQ)
☑ Analyze search volume and competition for each keyword
☑ Create a target keyword list for titles and descriptions

**2. Optimize video titles:**
☑ Include main keyword in the first 60 characters
☑ Keep titles concise and engaging (under 60 characters total)
☑ Use numbers, questions, or power words to grab attention
☑ Avoid clickbait or misleading titles

**3. Craft compelling video descriptions:**
☑ Write a brief, engaging summary of the video content
☑ Include main keyword and related keywords naturally
☑ Use timestamps to highlight key points or sections
☑ Include relevant links (website, social media, related videos)
☑ Add a clear call-to-action (subscribe, like, comment)

**4. Optimize video tags:**
☑ Use a mix of main keyword, related keywords, and long-tail keywords
☑ Include brand name and channel name as tags
☑ Limit tags to 5-8 relevant and specific phrases
☑ Use similar tags as top-ranking videos for your target keyword

**5. Create eye-catching thumbnails:**
☑ Design high-quality, visually appealing thumbnails
☑ Use contrasting colors and clear, readable text
☑ Include human faces or engaging visuals related to the content
☑ Maintain consistent branding across all thumbnails

**6. Encourage engagement:**
☑ Ask viewers to like, comment, and subscribe within the video
☑ Respond to comments and engage with your audience
☑ Create polls, Q&A sessions, or live streams to boost interaction
☑ Collaborate with other creators in your niche

**7. Analyze and optimize performance:**
☑ Monitor video analytics (views, watch time, audience retention)
☑ Identify top-performing videos and analyze their success factors
☑ Adapt future content based on audience feedback and engagement
☑ Continuously refine your video SEO strategy based on results
```
