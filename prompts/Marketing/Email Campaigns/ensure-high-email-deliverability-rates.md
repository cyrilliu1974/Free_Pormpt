# Email Automation & Deliverability Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Email Automation & Deliverability Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive email marketing strategies designed to move leads through the sales funnel while maintaining high inbox placement rates. This email automation prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured strategy covering audience segmentation by behavior and lifecycle stage, multi-email sequence architecture with optimal timing, advanced personalization beyond simple name tokens, and technical deliverability protocols including SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication and spam-filter avoidance. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to email copywriting, structuring sentences around core actions and their logical dependencies for clarity and persuasion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your business context, current open rate, conversion goal, and unique selling proposition to deliver actionable implementation plans. Marketing teams, solopreneurs, and email strategists reach for this prompt when building or optimizing automated nurture campaigns that balance engagement with technical best practices. ● Outputs audience segmentation approaches tied to engagement behavior and buyer journey stage ● Defines email sequence structure with send timing, frequency caps, and the strategic purpose of each message ● Provides a deliverability optimization checklist covering authentication protocols, list hygiene, and content formatting to avoid spam filters ● Includes 3-5 email template outlines demonstrating dependency grammar sentence structure and funnel progression from awareness to conversion ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in automation, sequencing, and deliverability optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive email automation strategy that nurtures leads through the sales funnel for {{business-context}}. Structure email copy using dependency grammar principles—building sentences around core actions and their logical dependencies to create clear, compelling messaging.

## Context
Current email open rate: {{current-open-rate}}
Desired conversion goal: {{conversion-goal}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

The strategy must address:
- Audience segmentation based on behavior and lifecycle stage
- Personalization techniques beyond first-name tokens
- Optimal sending times and frequency caps
- A/B testing frameworks for subject lines, content, and CTAs
- Deliverability best practices: SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication, list hygiene protocols, content optimization to avoid spam filters
- Logical email sequence progression that moves leads from awareness through consideration to conversion

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a numbered list. Each main point should be followed by bullet-pointed subpoints detailing specific strategies and tactics.

Include:
1. Audience segmentation approach
2. Email sequence structure (number of emails, timing, purpose of each)
3. Personalization and dynamic content strategy
4. Deliverability optimization checklist
5. A/B testing plan
6. 3-5 email template outlines demonstrating dependency grammar structure and funnel progression
```
