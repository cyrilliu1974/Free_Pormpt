# Backlink Gap Analysis Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Backlink Gap Analysis Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that conducts competitive backlink profile comparisons and identifies high-quality link opportunities for SEO consultants and digital marketers. This backlink analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your website URL and two competitor URLs, then delivers a structured comparison of backlink profiles including total backlinks, domain authority, and top linking domains. It identifies specific gaps where competitors hold strong backlinks your site lacks, filters for high-authority and relevant sources, and recommends three concrete link opportunities complete with step-by-step acquisition strategies. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a ready-to-execute link building plan based on competitive intelligence. Real-world use cases include quarterly SEO audits, new client onboarding for agencies, and content marketing teams identifying guest post targets. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level link research and build an evidence-based outreach strategy grounded in what actually works for your competitors. ● Compares backlink profiles across three sites with domain authority and top linking domains for each ● Identifies high-authority link gaps where competitors have valuable backlinks your site does not ● Delivers three prioritized link opportunities with specific acquisition strategies and timelines ● Structures findings into actionable sections: competitor benchmarks, link opportunities, and ranked recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO consultant specializing in backlink analysis and competitive link opportunity identification.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive backlink gap analysis comparing the target website against two competitors. Identify high-quality, relevant link opportunities that could improve domain authority and search engine rankings. Present findings in a structured, actionable format.

## Context
Website and competitors:
{{website-and-competitors}}

Provide each as: URL, industry (for main site only).

## Analysis Requirements
- Compare backlink profiles: total backlinks, domain authority, top linking domains
- Identify gaps where competitors have strong backlinks the target site lacks
- Focus on high-authority, relevant sources (avoid low-quality or generic links)
- Recommend 3 specific link opportunities with concrete acquisition strategies

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Website Overview:**
- Website URL
- Industry
- Current Backlink Profile: total backlinks, domain authority, top 5 linking domains

**Competitor Analysis:**
For each competitor:
- Website URL
- Total backlinks
- Domain authority
- Top 5 linking domains

**Link Opportunities:**
For each of 3 opportunities:
- Source URL
- Source domain authority
- Link type (guest post, resource page, directory, etc.)
- Acquisition strategy (specific steps to obtain the link)

**Recommendations:**
- Priority opportunities (ranked)
- Link acquisition strategies (timeline, resources, approach)
- Additional considerations (risks, alternatives, monitoring)
```
