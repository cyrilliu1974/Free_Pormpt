# Lead Generation Funnel Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Generation Funnel Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates scalable, cost-effective lead generation systems for solo entrepreneurs and small business owners. This lead generation funnel prompt for ChatGPT takes your business description, target audience, and current resources - then outputs a structured, multi-stage funnel plan covering awareness through conversion. Each stage includes actionable tactics, tool recommendations (prioritizing free and low-cost options), automation opportunities, templates, and measurable success metrics. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles so every stage builds logically on the previous one. Solopreneurs in coaching, consulting, e-commerce, and digital services use it to map out email sequences, content funnels, and lead-nurturing workflows without needing a marketing team or enterprise budget. ● Outputs stage-by-stage funnel plans with clear objectives, tactics, and transitions ● Recommends specific tools and platforms suited to one-person businesses ● Includes success metrics and KPIs for each funnel stage ● Prioritizes automation and efficiency to maximize results without a team ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in scalable, cost-effective lead generation systems for solo entrepreneurs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step lead generation funnel strategy tailored to a solopreneur in the online marketing space. The plan must cover how to attract, nurture, and convert leads through specific tactics, recommended tools, and resources for each funnel stage. Prioritize scalability and budget-consciousness suitable for a one-person operation.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

**Current Resources:**
{{current-assets-and-budget}}

## Approach
Structure the strategy using dependency grammar principles—each stage builds logically on the previous, with clear cause-effect relationships between actions and outcomes. Consider multiple digital marketing channels, content strategies, and automation opportunities that maximize efficiency without requiring a team.

## Output
Present the funnel strategy in clear stages with these components for each:

- **Stage name and objective**
- **Specific tactics** (bulleted)
- **Recommended tools** (bulleted, with free/low-cost options prioritized)
- **Key resources or templates** (bulleted)
- **Success metrics**

Ensure smooth transitions between stages that show how leads progress from awareness to conversion.
```
