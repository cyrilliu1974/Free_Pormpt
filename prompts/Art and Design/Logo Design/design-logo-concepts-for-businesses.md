# Business Logo Design Prompt for AI Image Generators

## 簡介

The Business Logo Design Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that creates professional vector-style logos with clean lines and customizable colors for businesses, brands, and entrepreneurs. This logo design prompt for ChatGPT-adjacent image models produces square (1:1 aspect ratio) logo concepts in a modern vector aesthetic. You define the logo type (wordmark, icon, combination mark), the central subject or symbol, background color, and overall color palette, and the prompt renders a clean, professional mark suitable for branding materials. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other text-to-image platforms that support style modifiers and aspect-ratio flags. Designers use it to generate initial concepts for client pitches, startups employ it to prototype brand identity before hiring an agency, and solo founders rely on it to create placeholder marks for MVPs and pitch decks. Reach for this prompt when you need a crisp, professional logo mockup quickly - whether you are exploring visual directions, testing color schemes, or preparing a brandbook. ● Specify logo type (wordmark, icon, emblem, or combination) to match your brand architecture. ● Control background color and color palette for on-brand consistency across print and digital. ● Outputs square 1:1 logos optimized for social avatars, app icons, and favicon use. ● Modern vector style with clean lines ensures scalability and a professional finish. ## Prompt

```
{{logo-type}} logo of a {{subject}} on {{background-color}} background, {{color-palette}}, modern vector style, clean lines, professional, --ar 1:1
```
