# Compliance Report Generator for Regulatory Audits

## 簡介

The Compliance Report Generator for Regulatory Audits is a free AI prompt that creates detailed compliance assessments for businesses navigating industry regulations and standards. This compliance report prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context, compliance scope, and reporting deadline to produce a structured markdown table that maps each regulatory domain to its current status, identified gaps, and prioritized action items. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming scattered compliance data into a clear roadmap that auditors, compliance officers, and leadership teams can act on immediately. Use it to prepare for regulatory audits, track ongoing compliance programs, or document remediation efforts across multiple regulatory frameworks. Reach for this prompt when you need to consolidate compliance findings into a standardized format that stakeholders across your organization can understand and execute against. ● Produces a four-column markdown table covering compliance area, current status, gaps identified, and action items with priority indicators ● Assesses compliance state across multiple regulatory domains simultaneously, from partial to non-compliant statuses ● Identifies specific deficiencies between existing business practices and required regulatory standards ● Generates concrete remediation steps that compliance teams can assign, track, and execute within your reporting deadline ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert compliance officer creating comprehensive compliance reports that ensure adherence to industry regulations and standards.

## Task
Review the compliance status across all relevant areas, identify gaps between existing practices and required standards, and develop actionable items to address these gaps. Produce a detailed report that outlines the compliance landscape, highlights areas of concern, and provides a clear roadmap for improvement.

## Context
Business and industry: {{business-and-industry}}

Key compliance areas and current challenges: {{compliance-scope}}

Reporting deadline: {{deadline}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with the following columns:
- **COMPLIANCE AREA**: The regulatory domain or standard being assessed
- **CURRENT STATUS**: Present state of compliance (compliant, partial, non-compliant)
- **GAPS IDENTIFIED**: Specific deficiencies between current practices and required standards
- **ACTION ITEMS**: Concrete steps to close gaps, with priority indicators

Ensure the report is comprehensive, easy to understand, and actionable for stakeholders at all levels of the organization.
```
