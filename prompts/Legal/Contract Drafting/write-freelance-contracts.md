# Freelance Contract Template Generator

## 簡介

The Freelance Contract Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized freelance contract templates protecting both service providers and clients. This freelance contract prompt for ChatGPT works by taking five key variables - your profession, typical project duration, payment structure, intellectual property considerations, and jurisdiction - and producing a structured agreement with clear headings and actionable clauses. It covers scope of work, payment terms, IP rights, termination, confidentiality, liability, indemnification, and dispute resolution. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering organized contract language appropriate to your specific field and local legal requirements. Freelancers across creative, technical, and consulting disciplines use it to establish baseline agreements before engaging legal review. Reach for this prompt when you need a professional starting point for client contracts, whether you're a designer, developer, writer, consultant, or any independent professional formalizing work relationships. ● Produces section-by-section contract structure with scope, payment, IP, termination, confidentiality, liability, and dispute resolution clauses ● Adapts language and terms to match your profession, project length, payment model, and jurisdiction ● Includes bullet-point guidance on key considerations for each contractual section ● Designed for clarity and legal focus while remaining accessible to non-lawyers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal consultant specializing in freelance contracts.

## Task
Create a comprehensive freelance contract template that protects both the freelancer and client. The contract must be clear, concise, and legally binding while addressing the specific needs of the freelance profession.

## Context
Freelance profession: {{profession}}
Typical project duration: {{project-duration}}
Preferred payment structure: {{payment-structure}}
Intellectual property considerations: {{ip-considerations}}
Location/jurisdiction: {{jurisdiction}}

## Output
Provide a structured contract template with clear headings for each section, followed by bullet points for key clauses and considerations. Include these essential sections:

- Scope of Work
- Payment Terms
- Intellectual Property Rights
- Termination Clauses
- Confidentiality (if applicable)
- Liability and Indemnification
- Dispute Resolution

Ensure each section contains specific, actionable language appropriate to the profession and jurisdiction provided.
```
