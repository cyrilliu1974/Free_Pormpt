# SEO Strategy Builder for Sales and Visibility

## 簡介

The SEO Strategy Builder for Sales and Visibility is a free AI prompt that creates a complete search engine optimization roadmap tailored to your business context and competitive landscape. This SEO strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business details and competitors to deliver actionable recommendations across four core pillars: keyword strategy with search intent analysis, on-page optimization including meta elements and internal linking, a content creation plan with blog topics and product descriptions, and technical SEO improvements for site speed and crawlability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing organized strategies with clear rationale and expected impact for each recommendation. Marketing teams, e-commerce businesses, and SEO consultants use it to develop data-driven optimization plans that increase organic traffic and drive online sales. ● Identifies primary and secondary keyword opportunities with search intent mapping and competitive gap analysis ● Provides specific on-page optimization guidance for title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy, and URL structure ● Recommends content topics and calendar strategies aligned with target keywords and audience needs ● Addresses technical SEO priorities including mobile optimization, schema markup, site speed, and indexation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist developing a comprehensive optimization strategy to increase organic visibility and drive online sales.

## Task
Analyze the provided business context and create a detailed, actionable SEO strategy covering keyword research, on-page optimization, content creation, and technical improvements. Use dependency grammar principles when crafting example content to ensure clarity and natural flow.

## Context
**Business Details:**
{{business-context}}

**Competitive Landscape:**
{{competitors-and-differentiation}}

## Output
Deliver your SEO strategy organized into the following categories, using bullet points within each:

- **Keyword Strategy** – primary and secondary keywords, search intent analysis, opportunity gaps
- **On-Page Optimization** – title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, internal linking, URL optimization
- **Content Creation** – blog topics, product/service descriptions, content calendar recommendations
- **Technical SEO** – site speed, mobile optimization, schema markup, crawlability, indexation issues

For each recommendation, include the rationale and expected impact where relevant.
```
