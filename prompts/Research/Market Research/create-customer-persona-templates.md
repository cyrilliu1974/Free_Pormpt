# Customer Persona Template Builder

## 簡介

The Customer Persona Template Builder is a free AI prompt that transforms raw customer data into structured, living persona frameworks for businesses and marketing teams. This customer persona prompt for ChatGPT audits your existing data sources - CRM records, surveys, interviews, behavioral analytics - and constructs detailed persona templates for each customer segment. It maps demographics, psychographic profiles, motivation hierarchies, pain points, communication preferences, and decision-making patterns into editable, team-ready frameworks. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for market researchers, product managers, UX designers, and marketing strategists who need actionable persona systems that evolve with new insights rather than static buyer profiles. ● Audits and categorizes quantitative metrics, qualitative feedback, and behavioral data to identify high-signal inputs and gaps. ● Generates persona templates with demographic foundations, psychographic drivers, motivation hierarchies, challenge categorization, and decision-making patterns. ● Includes visual design guidance for scannable layouts, icon systems, and chart types that make personas memorable and actionable for cross-functional teams. ● Delivers a step-by-step implementation roadmap with versioning strategy, update cadence, ownership model, and integration points for team workflows. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer research strategist specializing in data-driven persona frameworks.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable customer persona system that captures demographics, psychographics, motivations, pain points, behavioral patterns, and decision-making drivers. Design these as living frameworks teams can update as new data emerges, not static documents.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
(Include: business type, industry, target customer segments, team size, primary goals, key challenges)

Available data: {{data-sources}}
(Include: quantitative metrics, qualitative feedback, behavioral analytics, CRM data, surveys, interviews, observational insights)

## Process
1. **Audit & categorize** the data sources—identify gaps and prioritize high-signal inputs
2. **Build persona templates** for each customer segment with:
   - Demographic foundation
   - Psychographic profile
   - Motivation hierarchy
   - Challenge categorization
   - Communication preferences
   - Decision-making patterns
3. **Design for usability**: include visual recommendations (icon categories, chart types, scannable layouts) that make personas memorable and actionable
4. **Provide implementation guidance**: versioning strategy, update cadence, ownership model, and integration points with team workflows

## Output
Structure your response with:

**Executive Summary**: key persona insights at a glance

**Persona Templates**: detailed frameworks for each segment with clearly labeled, editable fields and example entries

**Visual Design Recommendations**: layout, icons, charts to enhance scannability

**Implementation Roadmap**: step-by-step guidance for deployment and ongoing maintenance

**Update Protocol**: how to keep personas current as a living reference tool
```
