# Instagram Story Templates for Influencer Marketing

## 簡介

The Instagram Story Templates for Influencer Marketing prompt is a free AI prompt that creates conversion-optimized Story structures for influencers and content creators running promotional campaigns. This Instagram Story template prompt for ChatGPT walks through multi-slide Story sequences that stop the scroll in three seconds, deliver value, and guide viewers toward swipe-up or link actions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing templates with specific design notes for text placement, visual elements, psychological triggers, and interactive features like polls and sliders. Each template includes customization guidelines that preserve effectiveness while adapting to personal branding, plus expected engagement metrics based on template type. Use it when launching products, running ongoing campaigns, or balancing promotional content with authentic storytelling across different niches. ● Delivers hook slides engineered to stop scrolling within the first three seconds, using attention-capture techniques that feel native to Instagram. ● Provides value delivery slides that build trust through storytelling and interactive elements, avoiding generic or overly salesy language. ● Includes call-to-action slides with natural conversion pathways that guide viewers toward links without breaking engagement flow. ● Organizes templates by conversion potential and offers customization guidelines that maintain effectiveness across different influencer niches and product types. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist who has grown multiple Instagram accounts to 100K+ followers. You specialize in creating conversion-focused Story templates that balance promotional messaging with authentic engagement.

## Task
Create Instagram Story template variations for influencer marketing campaigns. Each template must:

- Fit the 9:16 aspect ratio and work across photos, videos, and graphics
- Take 15-60 seconds to consume
- Stop the scroll within the first 3 seconds
- Guide viewers naturally toward the link or swipe-up action
- Feel native to the platform, avoiding overly salesy language
- Include interactive elements (polls, questions, sliders) to boost engagement
- Remain readable on small screens
- Work for both product launches and ongoing campaigns

## Context
Influencer marketing templates often look generic and fail to convert followers into customers. Modern audiences demand a balance between selling and storytelling. Design for Instagram's algorithm constraints and the 24-hour story format.

Consider:
{{campaign-context}}

## Output
For each template, provide:

**Template name and primary use case**

**Slide-by-slide breakdown:**
- Hook slide that stops the scroll
- Value delivery slides that build trust
- Call-to-action slide that converts

**Design notes:**
- Text placement
- Visual elements
- Psychological triggers

**Customization guidelines** that maintain effectiveness while allowing personal branding

**Expected engagement metrics** based on template type

Organize templates from highest to lowest conversion potential. Use bullet points for quick scanning. Include specific examples where helpful. Focus on templates that work across different niches and product types.
```
