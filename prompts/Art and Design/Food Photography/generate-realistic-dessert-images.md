# Realistic Dessert Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Realistic Dessert Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates studio-quality dessert photographs with professional lighting and composition for food creators, marketers, and designers. This dessert photography prompt for Midjourney produces photorealistic images of desserts captured at a classic 45-degree angle with soft shadows and clean white backgrounds. You specify the dessert details, serving vessel, and garnish, and the prompt handles the technical photography parameters - lighting setup, aspect ratio, and background isolation - to deliver restaurant-menu-ready visuals. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models. Use it for menu design, social media content, recipe blogs, packaging mockups, or any project that needs appetizing dessert photography without a physical photo shoot. ● Photorealistic rendering with professional studio lighting and natural soft shadows that mimic high-end food photography setups. ● Isolated white background that makes desserts easy to drop into menus, websites, advertisements, or composite designs. ● Customizable dessert details, serving vessels, and garnishes let you match brand aesthetics or specific menu items. ● 45-degree shooting angle and 1:1 aspect ratio optimized for Instagram, menu cards, and square-format digital displays. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic food photograph of {{dessert-details}}: served in a {{serving-vessel}}, garnished with {{garnish}}; isolated on clean white background, professional studio lighting with soft shadows, shot from 45-degree angle --ar 1:1 --no text, watermarks, artificial props
```
