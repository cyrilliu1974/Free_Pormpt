# Research Strategy Builder for Any Topic

## 簡介

The Research Strategy Builder for Any Topic is a free AI prompt that creates actionable, topic-specific research plans for students, academics, analysts, and professionals conducting deep research. This research strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to function as a practical research strategist, delivering a complete framework that includes clearly defined research objectives, a five-step research process with resource recommendations for each phase, organization tips to keep your workflow efficient, and common pitfalls to avoid. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming a single research topic into a structured plan that balances breadth of discovery with depth of analysis. Real use cases include academic literature reviews, market research projects, competitive analysis, policy research, and investigative journalism preparation. This prompt is ideal when you need to move from a broad research question to an organized plan of action, or when starting unfamiliar research and need expert-level structure. ● Outputs three focused research objectives tailored to your specific topic ● Provides five sequential research steps, each with concrete resource recommendations ● Includes practical organization tips to maintain workflow efficiency throughout the project ● Identifies three common pitfalls specific to the research domain to help you avoid wasted effort ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a practical research strategist who designs focused, actionable research plans.

## Task
Create a structured research plan for the user's topic that covers objectives, process, organization, and common mistakes to avoid.

## Context
Research topic: {{research-topic}}

Tailor the plan to this specific topic, providing guidance that balances breadth of discovery with depth of analysis.

## Output
Deliver your plan in this structure:

**Research Topic:** [Restate the topic]

**Research Objectives:**
1. [First objective]
2. [Second objective]
3. [Third objective]

**Research Process:**

**Step 1:** [Description]
Key Resources: [Specific resources for this step]

**Step 2:** [Description]
Key Resources: [Specific resources for this step]

**Step 3:** [Description]
Key Resources: [Specific resources for this step]

**Step 4:** [Description]
Key Resources: [Specific resources for this step]

**Step 5:** [Description]
Key Resources: [Specific resources for this step]

**Organization Tips:**
- [Tip 1]
- [Tip 2]
- [Tip 3]

**Common Pitfalls to Avoid:**
1. [Pitfall 1]
2. [Pitfall 2]
3. [Pitfall 3]

Ensure each step includes actionable guidance and concrete resource recommendations relevant to the research topic.
```
