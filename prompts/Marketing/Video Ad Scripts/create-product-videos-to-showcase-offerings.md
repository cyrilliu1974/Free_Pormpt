# Product Showcase Video Script and Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Product Showcase Video Script and Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that develops end-to-end video creation strategies to help businesses showcase products and services for online sales. This product showcase video prompt for ChatGPT guides you through analyzing features and benefits, structuring a logical script with natural flow, positioning persuasive calls-to-action, and recommending visual production elements - shots, transitions, graphics, and pacing. You input your product or service description, target audience, unique selling proposition, and brand voice; the prompt outputs a numbered strategy document covering concept and hook, script outline with key beats, visual and production notes, CTA placement, and estimated length. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across text-based AI platforms. Marketers, ecommerce teams, and video creators use it to turn product details into structured, production-ready video plans that address audience pain points and drive conversions. ● Analyzes product features and benefits in relation to target audience pain points ● Structures video scripts with dependency grammar principles for logical, natural flow ● Recommends specific visual elements, transitions, and pacing aligned to brand voice ● Places calls-to-action strategically throughout the video to maximize engagement and conversions ● Outputs production-ready documentation that guides teams from initial concept to finished video ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video marketing strategist creating product showcase videos that drive online sales.

## Task
Develop a complete video creation strategy for the following offering:

{{product-service-description}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

## Process
1. Analyze the product features, benefits, and how they solve audience pain points
2. Structure a video script with logical flow, ensuring each segment builds naturally on the previous
3. Integrate clear, persuasive calls-to-action at strategic moments
4. Recommend visual elements (shots, transitions, graphics, pacing) that reinforce the message
5. Tailor tone and language to match the brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list covering:
- Concept and hook
- Script outline with key beats
- Recommended visuals and production notes
- Calls-to-action placement
- Estimated video length and pacing

Format each step clearly so it can guide production from concept through to final cut.
```
