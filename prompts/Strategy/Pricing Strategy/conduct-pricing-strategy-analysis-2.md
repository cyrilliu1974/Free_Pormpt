# Pricing Strategy Analysis Prompt for Business Consulting

## 簡介

The Pricing Strategy Analysis Prompt for Business Consulting is a free AI prompt that produces detailed pricing research reports for products or services across any industry. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) through a two-part analysis framework: first calculating price elasticity of demand from historical sales data to identify optimal price points, then benchmarking competitor pricing models including promotions, discounts, and bundling tactics. The output is a structured consultant-grade report with executive summary, data visualizations, risk assessment, and actionable recommendations tailored to your market positioning. Marketing managers use it to justify pricing changes to leadership, product teams rely on it for launch pricing decisions, and business consultants deploy it for client engagements requiring competitive intelligence. This prompt is ideal for pricing managers, product strategists, business consultants, and founders who need rigorous pricing analysis but lack dedicated pricing analytics resources. ● Calculates price elasticity of demand from historical data to determine price sensitivity and revenue-maximizing price points ● Analyzes competitor pricing across base prices, promotional tactics, bundling strategies, and market positioning ● Delivers a structured report with executive summary, data-backed analysis, visualizations, risk assessment, and implementation steps ● Customizable for any product or service category, from SaaS subscriptions to physical goods to professional services ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business consultant conducting comprehensive pricing research.

## Task

Analyze how price impacts demand and competitiveness for {{product-or-service}}, and evaluate competitor pricing strategies to recommend an optimal pricing approach.

## Analysis Framework

**Price-Demand Analysis:**

1. Gather historical pricing and sales volume data covering a sufficient period to identify trends
2. Identify instances where price changes led to significant demand shifts
3. Calculate price elasticity of demand to determine whether demand is elastic (price-sensitive) or inelastic
4. Identify optimal price points that maximize revenue and market share based on elasticity and historical patterns

**Competitive Pricing Analysis:**

1. Identify key competitors offering similar products or services
2. Collect competitor pricing data including base prices, promotions, discounts, and bundling strategies from public sources, market research reports, and competitor websites
3. Analyze competitor pricing in relation to their market positioning and target audience
4. Assess competitive positioning and identify pricing opportunities or threats

## Output

Structure your report with:

1. **Executive Summary** – Key insights and recommendations
2. **Price-Demand Impact Analysis** – Detailed findings with supporting data, graphs, and charts
3. **Competitor Pricing Overview** – Pricing models, promotions, bundling tactics, and market effectiveness assessment
4. **Pricing Strategy Recommendations** – Specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the target audience, market positioning, and competitive landscape
5. **Risk Assessment** – Potential risks and mitigation strategies for the recommended approach
6. **Conclusion** – Key takeaways and implementation next steps

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Include relevant data visualizations to support your analysis. Cite all sources and provide a reference list at the end.
```
