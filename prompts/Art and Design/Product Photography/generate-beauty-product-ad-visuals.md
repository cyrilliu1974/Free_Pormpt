# Beauty Product Ad Visual Generator

## 簡介

The Beauty Product Ad Visual Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic, editorial-quality beauty campaign images with exact brand matching and studio lighting for marketing teams, beauty brands, and creative agencies. This beauty product ad visual prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 3:4 ultra high-resolution editorial images featuring products integrated into a sophisticated studio composition. You provide reference images of your brand's packaging, specify a color palette, and the prompt renders tight editorial crops with realistic product physics, controlled shadows, soft directional lighting, and 85mm portrait lens aesthetics. Use it to prototype campaign visuals, test product arrangements, or generate hero images for social media, e-commerce, and print advertising without scheduling a full studio shoot. Reach for this prompt when you need publication-ready beauty visuals that balance aspirational editorial style with brand-safe, compliant framing - no floating products, no logo distortion, no over-retouched skin. ● Replicates exact product shape, logo, color, and packaging from your reference images with readable brand names and controlled highlights ● Places products with realistic physics - fabric compression, contact shadows, vertical support - inside a tailored editorial composition ● Applies soft directional studio lighting, 85mm portrait lens depth of field, and custom color grading to match your brand palette ● Enforces brand-safe defaults: non-celebrity models, natural skin texture, no nudity, no over-smoothing, no distorted logos ## Prompt

```
Ultra high-resolution 3:4 photorealistic editorial beauty campaign image.

Model: Adult female with unique, non-celebrity face. Soft oval jawline, moderate natural cheekbones, straight nose with slightly rounded tip, natural full lips, almond-shaped eyes with neutral spacing. Dark brown hair sleek and smoothed back with soft movement, subtle off-center part. Calm, confident expression, softly closed lips. Natural skin texture with realistic pores, no over-smoothing.

Wardrobe: Deep red lace bra with full coverage, opaque lace, supportive cups with realistic fabric tension (no nipples visible, fully clothed). Tailored dark blazer over top, structured shoulders, relaxed open front.

Products: Use attached {{brand}} reference images to replicate exact shape, size, color, logo, and packaging. Place four products naturally within and against the bra cups—left cup, center, right of center, right cup—with realistic physics: slight fabric compression, vertical support from cup structure, products leaning with contact shadows. No floating, no clipping, no distortion. Logos and brand names clearly visible and readable.

Composition: Tight editorial crop from collarbone to mid-torso, product-first framing. Clean symmetry with slight organic variation. Soft neutral studio backdrop.

Lighting: Soft directional studio lighting angled to sculpt collarbones and fabric texture. Subtle fill light preserving natural shadows. Controlled product highlights, no glare.

Camera: 85mm portrait lens, f/2.8 aperture, shallow depth of field with sharp product focus, true-to-life proportions.

Color grading: {{color-palette}}, medium contrast, refined realistic saturation.

Style: Modern {{brand}} campaign aesthetic—confident femininity, editorial minimalism, sensual but brand-safe, non-celebrity model energy.

Avoid: Celebrity or public figure resemblance, extra products, floating objects, incorrect physics, nudity, nipples, explicit content, over-smoothed skin, logo distortion.
```
