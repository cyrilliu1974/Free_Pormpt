# Marketing Campaign Plan Prompt With Bold Strategy

## 簡介

The Marketing Campaign Plan Prompt With Bold Strategy is a free AI prompt that generates complete marketing campaign plans for products and services with a focus on differentiation and customer-centric positioning. This marketing campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides text models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce an 11-section strategic plan covering everything from target audience psychographics and unique selling propositions to channel selection, creative campaign elements, customer engagement tactics, partnership opportunities, KPIs, budget allocation, and execution timelines. Instead of generic advice, it outputs a structured framework that pushes for risk-taking approaches and market insight, helping marketers move from concept to actionable campaign architecture. Teams use it to develop launch strategies, rebrand initiatives, and seasonal campaigns that break through crowded markets. ● Produces 11-section plans including demographics, psychographics, behaviors, USP, brand positioning, and three-channel strategies with rationale ● Outputs creative campaign elements - theme, slogan, visual style, tone - alongside three concrete customer engagement tactics ● Includes partnership identification, three trackable KPIs, budget breakdowns, and phased execution timelines ● Structures thinking around differentiation and bold market positioning rather than safe, generic marketing tactics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a seasoned marketing strategist drawing on bold, customer-centric principles and innovative branding techniques to create campaigns that break through conventional thinking.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive marketing campaign plan for {{product-or-service}}. The plan should demonstrate a risk-taking approach, deep market insight, and persuasive messaging that differentiates the offering in a crowded market.

## Output
Structure your plan with the following sections:

### 1. Campaign Overview
High-level summary of the marketing campaign strategy and core objectives.

### 2. Target Audience
- **Demographics**: Age, location, income, education
- **Psychographics**: Values, interests, lifestyle
- **Behaviors**: Purchase patterns, media consumption, engagement preferences

### 3. Unique Selling Proposition
Key differentiating factors that set this offering apart from competitors.

### 4. Brand Positioning Strategy
How the brand will occupy a distinct place in the market and in customers' minds.

### 5. Key Marketing Channels
Identify three primary channels with rationale:
- Channel 1
- Channel 2
- Channel 3

### 6. Creative Campaign Elements
- **Theme**: Central concept tying the campaign together
- **Slogan**: Memorable tagline
- **Visual Style**: Design approach and aesthetic
- **Tone and Voice**: Communication personality

### 7. Customer Engagement Tactics
Three specific tactics to build relationships and drive action:
- Tactic 1
- Tactic 2
- Tactic 3

### 8. Partnership and Sponsorship Opportunities
Potential collaborations that amplify reach and credibility.

### 9. Key Performance Indicators
Three measurable KPIs to track campaign success:
- KPI 1
- KPI 2
- KPI 3

### 10. Budget and Resources
Financial allocation and human resources required for execution.

### 11. Timeline and Execution Plan
Detailed schedule with milestones and launch phases.
```
