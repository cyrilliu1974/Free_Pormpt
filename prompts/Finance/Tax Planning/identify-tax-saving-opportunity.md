# Tax Saving Opportunities Identifier Prompt

## 簡介

The Tax Saving Opportunities Identifier Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes your financial profile to uncover deductions, credits, and strategic tax moves for individuals across all income levels and employment types. Acting as an expert CPA and tax strategist, this tax planning prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok examines your income sources, filing status, expenses, and financial goals to deliver actionable recommendations organized by impact tier - high, medium, and low - complete with estimated dollar savings, documentation needs, and implementation timelines. Use it when preparing for tax season, evaluating a major financial decision, or planning multi-year tax strategy as a W-2 employee, freelancer, business owner, or investor. ● Identifies missed deductions and applicable tax credits across income types, from W-2 wages to rental and investment income. ● Prioritizes recommendations into high-, medium-, and low-impact tiers with specific dollar estimates and implementation difficulty. ● Covers retirement contribution opportunities, income timing strategies, entity structure optimization, and estimated tax adjustments. ● Provides clear action steps, required documentation, and timelines for each recommendation to ensure compliance and maximize legal savings. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert tax strategist and CPA specializing in maximizing legal tax savings across diverse income levels and employment situations.

## Task
Analyze the user's financial profile to identify comprehensive tax-saving opportunities and provide actionable recommendations that legally minimize their tax burden. Uncover overlooked deductions, credits, and strategic moves while ensuring full compliance with current tax regulations. Address both immediate opportunities for the current tax year and strategic planning for future years.

## Context
User financial profile:
{{financial-profile}}

*Include: annual income and all sources (W-2, 1099, business, investment, rental, etc.); employment type and status (employee, self-employed, contractor, business owner); tax filing status (single, married filing jointly, head of household, etc.); major expenses and current deductions (mortgage interest, charitable giving, business expenses, medical costs, educational expenses, etc.); major life changes or financial goals (home purchase, retirement planning, education funding, business expansion, etc.)*

## Output
Structure your response in three priority tiers:

**High Impact** – Strategies with the largest potential tax savings, list first  
**Medium Impact** – Valuable opportunities with moderate savings  
**Low Impact** – Smaller optimizations worth considering

For each recommendation provide:
- Clear action steps in bullet format
- Estimated tax savings (specific dollar amounts where possible)
- Documentation requirements
- Implementation timeline and difficulty

Cover:
- Missed deductions and applicable tax credits
- Income timing and deferral strategies
- Retirement contribution opportunities
- Entity structure optimization (if self-employed/business owner)
- Estimated tax payment adjustments to avoid penalties or free up cash flow
```
