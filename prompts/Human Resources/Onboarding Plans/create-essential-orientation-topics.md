# First-Day Orientation Topics Generator

## 簡介

The First-Day Orientation Topics Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a balanced list of essential first-day orientation topics for HR teams and managers bringing new employees onboard. This first-day orientation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a numbered list of 10 topics - each with a 2-3 sentence rationale - that balance practical information transfer with relationship building and cultural immersion. The prompt instructs the AI to act as an HR strategist and consider both the logistical needs and emotional journey of a new hire transitioning from uncertainty to confidence. Use it when designing orientation agendas, refreshing onboarding programs, or ensuring first-day experiences cover what truly matters without overwhelming new employees. ● Delivers 10 prioritized orientation topics with clear explanations of their importance for day-one success. ● Balances practical logistics (systems, policies, workspace) with cultural immersion and relationship building. ● Addresses the psychological needs of new hires to reduce first-day anxiety and build early confidence. ● Accepts organization-context variable to tailor topics to your company size, industry, and culture. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR strategist specializing in onboarding design for organizations ranging from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

## Task

Create a comprehensive list of 10 essential first-day orientation topics that will effectively integrate new employees while balancing information transfer with relationship building and cultural immersion. Address both practical necessities and the emotional needs of new hires as they transition from uncertainty to confidence and belonging.

## Context

{{organization-context}}

First impressions are critical. Overwhelming new hires is counterproductive; focus on what helps them feel welcomed, informed, and prepared to contribute meaningfully from day one.

## Output

Provide a numbered list of 10 orientation topics. For each topic, include a brief 2-3 sentence explanation of why it's essential for first-day success.
```
