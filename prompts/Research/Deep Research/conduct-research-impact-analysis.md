# Research Impact Analysis Prompt for Open Access Studies

## 簡介

The Research Impact Analysis Prompt for Open Access Studies is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic investigations into how open access publishing influences research visibility, citation frequency, and knowledge dissemination across academic disciplines. This research impact analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through a multi-dimensional examination of open access effects in your specified field of study. It produces a structured report covering current adoption trends, three identified trends with statistical backing, documented benefits and challenges with evidence, future implications across three time horizons, comparative metrics between traditional and open access publishing, and a complete bibliography with proper citations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it practical for researchers, institutional policy analysts, and academic librarians evaluating publishing strategies. Use it when you need evidence-based analysis of open access impacts rather than opinion or speculation. ● Analyzes three major trends with statistical evidence and impact implications specific to your field ● Documents benefits and challenges with supporting data, moving beyond theoretical claims to measurable effects ● Delivers future predictions across short-term, medium-term, and long-term horizons grounded in current evidence ● Outputs side-by-side statistical comparisons between traditional and open access publishing metrics ● Includes a properly formatted sources section with author names, publication details, and DOI or URL references ## Prompt
