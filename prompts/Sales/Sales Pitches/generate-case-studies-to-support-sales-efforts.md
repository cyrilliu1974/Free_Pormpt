# Case Study Writer for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Case Study Writer for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, metrics-driven case studies demonstrating how a product or service solved real client challenges. This case study prompt for ChatGPT works by guiding AI models through a proven six-section framework: client challenge, solution overview, implementation timeline, quantified results, authentic testimonials, and key takeaways. Sales professionals, marketing teams, and customer success managers use it to transform client wins into 600-900 word narratives that resonate with prospects in specific industries. The prompt is designed for anyone building sales collateral, proposal support materials, or marketing content that requires credible proof points. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for your product or service, target industry, key performance metrics, and existing client testimonials to produce polished case studies ready for presentations, websites, or email campaigns. ● Structures each case study with six clearly defined sections that guide readers from problem to measurable impact ● Weaves key metrics and percentages throughout the narrative to quantify ROI and business value ● Integrates client testimonials naturally rather than as disconnected quotes ● Maintains an industry-appropriate professional tone tailored to decision-makers in the target sector ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert case study writer specializing in creating compelling narratives that demonstrate measurable business impact.

## Task
Write a case study showcasing how {{product-or-service}} helped a client in {{target-industry}} achieve their goals. Structure the case study with these sections:

1. **Client Challenge** — Introduce the client and the specific problem they faced
2. **Solution** — Describe how {{product-or-service}} addressed their needs
3. **Implementation** — Detail the deployment process and timeline
4. **Results** — Highlight concrete outcomes using {{key-metrics}}
5. **Client Voice** — Incorporate {{client-testimonial}}
6. **Key Takeaways** — Summarize the main value delivered

## Output
Deliver a concise, engaging case study (600–900 words) that:
- Uses specific numbers and percentages from {{key-metrics}} to quantify impact
- Integrates {{client-testimonial}} naturally within the narrative
- Clearly connects the solution to business outcomes
- Supports sales conversations with credible evidence

Use clear section headings and maintain a professional yet accessible tone appropriate for {{target-industry}} decision-makers.
```
