# Sales Funnel Lead Nurturing Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Lead Nurturing Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds complete lead nurturing frameworks for sales and marketing teams. This sales funnel prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - including lead characteristics, industry dynamics, and target audience pain points - and generates a structured strategy that moves prospects through awareness, consideration, decision, and retention stages. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing stage-specific content recommendations, personalized communication templates, follow-up cadences with timing triggers, and measurable KPIs for tracking funnel performance. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or optimize a lead nurturing program, build automated follow-up sequences, or improve conversion rates across your sales funnel. ● Maps all funnel stages with lead analysis, content assets, and personalization tactics specific to your industry and lead type ● Provides 2-3 sample communication templates for email, social, and direct outreach with persuasive structure ● Defines measurable success metrics including engagement rates, progression velocity, and conversion lift for each stage ● Includes a 30/60/90-day implementation roadmap with phased rollout milestones and review checkpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales funnel strategist specializing in lead nurturing and conversion optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive lead nurturing strategy that guides prospects from initial awareness through conversion. The strategy must include stage-specific content, personalized communication approaches, and follow-up sequences tailored to the business context provided.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include: lead type and characteristics, industry, target audience demographics and pain points, unique selling proposition, and current conversion baseline.

## Process
1. **Lead Analysis**: Identify the specific needs, challenges, and decision-making patterns of this lead segment
2. **Funnel Mapping**: Define the awareness, consideration, decision, and retention stages relevant to this lead type
3. **Stage-Specific Strategy**: For each funnel stage, develop:
   - Content assets (formats, topics, delivery channels)
   - Personalization tactics (segmentation criteria, dynamic messaging)
   - Follow-up cadence (timing, triggers, escalation paths)
4. **Communication Templates**: Create 2-3 sample templates (email, social, direct outreach) with clear structure and persuasive flow
5. **Success Metrics**: Define KPIs for each stage (engagement rates, progression velocity, conversion lift)
6. **Implementation Roadmap**: Provide a phased rollout timeline with review checkpoints

## Output
Deliver a structured strategy document with:
- Clear headings for each funnel stage
- Bullet-pointed action items under each strategy component
- Concrete examples in all templates
- Quantifiable metrics tied to business objectives
- 30/60/90-day implementation milestones
```
