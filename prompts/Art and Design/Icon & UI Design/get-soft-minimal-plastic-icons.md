# Soft Minimal 3D Plastic Icon Generator

## 簡介

The Soft Minimal 3D Plastic Icon Generator is a free AI prompt that retextures uploaded images into soft minimal 3D plastic renderings while preserving the original object's silhouette and shape for designers, UI artists, and brand teams. This icon design prompt for Midjourney, Nano Banana, and Flux transforms photographs or illustrations into high-resolution isometric 3D renders with smooth matte plastic surfaces, metallic edge trim in satin finish, and semi-reflective black screen surfaces, ideal for app icons, product showcases, UI element libraries, and brand asset kits. The prompt applies soft gradient ambient lighting, minimal shadows, rounded bevels, and a cool grey, steel blue, and soft charcoal color palette with light lavender and pastel yellow accents against a pastel gradient background from blue to cream. Reach for this prompt when you need to unify disparate visual assets into a cohesive modern aesthetic or create icon sets that feel premium and minimalist. ● Retextures uploaded images into smooth matte plastic with metallic trim and semi-reflective surfaces while locking the original silhouette. ● Flattens complex details into simplified geometry blocks with rounded bevels and soft surface transitions. ● Applies soft gradient ambient lighting, diffused reflections, and pastel gradient backgrounds (blue to cream). ● Outputs high-resolution isometric 3D renders with sharp central focus, subtle bloom, and a cohesive cool grey, steel blue, and soft charcoal palette. ## Prompt
