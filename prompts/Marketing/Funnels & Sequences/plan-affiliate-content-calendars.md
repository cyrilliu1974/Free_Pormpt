# Affiliate Content Calendar Planner

## 簡介

The Affiliate Content Calendar Planner is a free AI prompt that builds strategic month-by-month content schedules for affiliate marketers who need to balance promotional campaigns with audience trust-building. This affiliate content calendar prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your niches, platforms, audience demographics, and revenue goals to produce a comprehensive 12-month plan. It maps content formats to buyer journey stages - awareness, consideration, decision - while aligning promotional sequences with affiliate cookie durations and commission peak windows. The system enforces a 70/30 value-to-promotional content ratio, preventing audience burnout while capturing seasonal conversion opportunities. Real use cases include planning product launch sequences, scheduling relationship-nurturing content between major promotions, and optimizing platform-specific posting schedules for algorithm performance. This prompt is for affiliate marketers managing multiple partnerships who need structured calendars that account for cookie windows, commission tiers, and the trust-erosion risk of over-promotion. ● Produces monthly breakdowns with week-by-week content themes, specific formats for each buyer journey stage, and promotional vs value content distribution ratios ● Identifies seasonal trends and commission peak windows specific to your niches, with launch timing rationale for key affiliate campaigns ● Includes platform-specific posting schedules optimized for algorithm behavior and audience activity patterns, plus performance tracking metrics ● Provides optimization frameworks with conversion tracking methods, documentation templates for future improvements, and warning signs of trust-damaging promotional cadence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an affiliate marketing strategist specializing in content calendar systems that align promotional timing with buyer psychology, commission structures, and seasonal conversion peaks.

## Context
The user manages affiliate partnerships across multiple niches and needs a strategic content calendar that balances promotional campaigns with audience trust-building. Success in affiliate marketing depends on timing content around commission spikes, cookie windows, and buyer journey stages—not just traffic volume. The calendar must prevent audience burnout while capturing seasonal opportunities and maintaining sustainable conversion rates.

## Task
Create a comprehensive month-by-month affiliate content calendar system tailored to:

{{affiliate-context}}
(Include: niches, primary platforms, audience demographics, monthly revenue goal, available content creation hours per week)

The calendar must:
- Balance 70% value-driven content with 30% promotional content
- Align promotional sequences (pre-launch, launch, follow-up) with affiliate cookie durations
- Map content formats to buyer journey stages (awareness, consideration, decision)
- Identify seasonal trends and commission peak windows specific to the user's niches
- Include platform-specific posting schedules optimized for algorithms and audience activity
- Build relationship-nurturing content between major promotions
- Provide contingency plans for underperforming campaigns

## Output
Deliver a structured content calendar with:

**Strategic Overview**
- Psychology principles behind high-converting affiliate content timing
- How commission structures and cookie windows shape content scheduling

**Monthly Breakdown** (12 months)
For each month:
- Week-by-week content themes
- Specific content types and formats for each buyer journey stage
- Promotional vs value content distribution
- Key affiliate campaigns with launch timing and rationale
- Platform-specific posting schedules with frequency
- Performance tracking metrics (CTR, conversion rate, revenue per post, audience engagement)

**Optimization Framework**
- Methods to track what content converts best
- Documentation templates for future calendar improvements
- Warning signs that promotional cadence is damaging trust

Format as:
- Clear markdown headings for each month and week
- Bullet points for content ideas and formats
- Tables for posting schedules and promotional sequences
- Actionable templates with concrete examples throughout

Avoid generic templates—every recommendation must account for affiliate-specific constraints like cookie windows, commission tiers, and the trust-erosion risk of over-promotion.
```
