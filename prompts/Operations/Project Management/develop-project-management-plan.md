# Project Management Plan Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Project Management Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, actionable project management plans covering every critical component from scope definition through risk mitigation and team structure. This project management plan prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to develop a seven-section plan that addresses scope and objectives, deliverables and milestones, timelines with task dependencies, budget breakdowns with contingency allocations, risk assessments with mitigation strategies, team roles and responsibilities, and communication protocols. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, ensuring that each section logically connects to the others and highlights interdependencies where one element constrains or enables another. Real-world applications include software development rollouts, event planning, construction projects, product launches, and organizational change initiatives. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework that ensures no planning element is overlooked and all components work together coherently. ● Produces seven interconnected sections that address scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, risks, team structure, and communication in one cohesive document ● Identifies task dependencies and highlights how timeline constraints affect budget, staffing, and risk exposure ● Generates risk assessments with likelihood-impact scoring and assigns specific mitigation strategies to each identified risk ● Outputs team role assignments with clear decision-making authority and accountability lines ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert project manager creating a comprehensive Project Management Plan.

## Task
Develop a detailed, actionable plan covering scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, risk management, team structure, and communication protocols. Ensure each section logically connects to and supports the others, addressing interdependencies throughout.

## Context
{{business-and-project-context}}

## Output
Structure the plan with clear headings and subheadings:

### 1. Scope and Objectives
- Define project boundaries and success criteria
- State measurable objectives

### 2. Deliverables and Milestones
- List specific outputs
- Identify checkpoint milestones with completion criteria

### 3. Timeline
- Present key phases, dates, and deadlines
- Show dependencies between tasks

### 4. Budget
- Break down costs by category
- Note constraints and contingency allocation

### 5. Risk Assessment
- Identify top risks (likelihood × impact)
- Define mitigation and contingency strategies for each

### 6. Team Structure
- Assign roles and responsibilities
- Clarify decision-making authority

### 7. Communication and Reporting
- Specify meeting cadence and formats
- Define escalation paths and status reporting schedules

Use bullet points and numbered lists for readability. Highlight interdependencies where one element constrains or enables another.
```
