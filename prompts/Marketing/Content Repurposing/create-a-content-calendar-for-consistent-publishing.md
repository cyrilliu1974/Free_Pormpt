# Content Calendar Prompt for Multi-Platform Publishing

## 簡介

The Content Calendar Prompt for Multi-Platform Publishing is a free AI prompt that builds a structured publishing schedule for sales and marketing content across your chosen platforms. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, products, and target platforms to generate a markdown table organizing your content strategy by date, platform, content type, and topic. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a complete publishing schedule that balances content variety with strategic business objectives. Marketing teams use it to plan blog posts, social media carousels, email campaigns, videos, and infographics in a single cohesive calendar that maintains brand consistency while addressing audience needs across weeks or months. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from ad-hoc posting to a disciplined content rhythm that aligns every piece of content with revenue goals and audience engagement. ● Outputs a markdown table with date, platform, content type, and topic columns for easy import into project management tools ● Strategically varies content formats (blog posts, videos, infographics, social posts, emails) to sustain audience interest ● Aligns every scheduled piece with your specific products, services, and business objectives ● Maintains consistent brand voice while adapting tone and format to each platform's best practices ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content calendar for consistently publishing sales and marketing content across multiple platforms.

## Context
Analyze the business context and audience to determine effective content types, topics, and publishing cadence. The calendar should align with marketing goals, resonate with the target audience, and maintain consistent brand voice.

Business and audience context:
{{business-context}}

Key products or services to feature:
{{products-services}}

Preferred platforms:
{{platforms}}

## Output
Provide the content calendar as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Date**: Publishing schedule
- **Platform**: Where content will be posted
- **Content Type**: Format (blog post, video, infographic, carousel, story, email, etc.)
- **Topic**: Specific subject aligned with business goals and audience needs

Include strategic variety in content types and topics to maintain engagement while supporting the business objectives.
```
