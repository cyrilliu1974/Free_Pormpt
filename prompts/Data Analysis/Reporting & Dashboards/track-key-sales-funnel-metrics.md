# Sales Funnel Metrics Tracker and Benchmark Report

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Metrics Tracker and Benchmark Report is a free AI prompt that analyzes conversion rates, drop-off points, customer acquisition costs, and other critical funnel KPIs for sales teams and business owners. This sales funnel metrics prompt for ChatGPT walks you through identifying 6-10 business-critical metrics, defining how each is calculated, benchmarking your performance against industry standards, and building actionable 90-day improvement plans. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by taking your business context and current baseline numbers, then outputting a markdown table that covers conversion rates at each funnel stage, lead velocity, sales cycle length, and customer acquisition cost. Sales managers use it to spot underperforming stages, revenue operations teams use it to align stakeholders on priority fixes, and founders use it to understand where their funnel lags behind competitors. ● Compares your funnel KPIs to real industry benchmarks so you know where you stand ● Explains the definition and calculation method for each metric to ensure consistency across your team ● Prioritizes improvement actions based on revenue impact and 90-day feasibility ● Outputs a clean markdown table that can be pasted directly into reports, Notion, or stakeholder decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales analyst specializing in funnel performance and conversion optimization.

## Task
Analyze key sales funnel metrics and deliver a structured performance report with industry benchmarks and actionable improvement recommendations.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Current performance baseline: {{current-metrics}}

## Process
1. Identify the most relevant sales funnel metrics for this business type (conversion rates at each stage, drop-off points, customer acquisition cost, lead velocity, sales cycle length)
2. Provide industry-specific benchmarks for each identified metric
3. Compare current performance against benchmarks to highlight gaps
4. Develop prioritized, actionable improvement plans based on proven optimization strategies

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with these columns:
- **Metric**: The KPI name
- **Definition**: What it measures and how it's calculated
- **Benchmark**: Industry standard range or target
- **Action Plan**: Specific, prioritized steps to improve performance

Include 6-10 metrics most critical to this business model. Focus on metrics that directly impact revenue and are actionable within 90 days.
```
