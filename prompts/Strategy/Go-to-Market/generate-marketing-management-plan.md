# Marketing Management Plan Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Marketing Management Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, multi-section marketing plans for businesses and marketing teams needing structured strategic direction. This marketing management plan prompt for ChatGPT walks through ten core sections: executive summary, market analysis, SMART objectives, positioning and value proposition, marketing mix (product, price, place, promotion), budget allocation with justifications, measurable KPIs, phased implementation timelines with assigned responsibilities, contingency plans for market shifts, and appendices for supporting data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming your business context into a professional marketing plan document that coordinates strategy, tactics, and execution across all channels. Marketing directors, startup founders, and consultants use it to replace hours of template assembly with a single, context-aware prompt that ensures consistency, accountability, and alignment with business goals. ● Produces ten structured sections including market analysis, positioning, marketing mix, budget, KPIs, and contingency plans ● Ensures all objectives follow the SMART framework (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound) ● Creates phased implementation timelines with clear milestones and accountability assignments ● Anticipates challenges and generates 2-3 actionable contingency responses for resilience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing director specializing in comprehensive marketing management plans that coordinate strategy, tactics, and execution across all channels.

## Task
Develop a detailed marketing management plan structured around the following sections:

1. **Executive Summary** – High-level overview of the plan and its strategic intent
2. **Market Analysis** – Target markets, trends, competitive landscape, and opportunities
3. **Marketing Objectives** – SMART goals aligned with business strategy
4. **Positioning and Value Proposition** – Market positioning and communication of unique value
5. **Marketing Mix** – Product, pricing, distribution, and promotion strategies
6. **Marketing Budget** – Allocation across activities with justification
7. **Metrics and KPIs** – Performance indicators tied to objectives
8. **Implementation Timeline** – Phased execution with milestones and responsibilities
9. **Contingency Plans** – Alternative strategies for market shifts or challenges
10. **Appendices** – Supporting research, data, and references

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output Requirements
- Base all recommendations on thorough market research and analysis
- Ensure objectives are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound
- Tailor the marketing mix to the target market and align with positioning
- Allocate budget realistically and justify major expenditures
- Define clear metrics tied directly to stated objectives
- Create a feasible timeline with accountability assignments
- Anticipate 2-3 likely challenges with actionable contingency responses
- Use headings, subheadings, bullet points, and numbered lists for clarity
- Write in clear, jargon-free language; explain technical terms when necessary
- Maintain consistent formatting throughout the document
```
