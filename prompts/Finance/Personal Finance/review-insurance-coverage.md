# Insurance Coverage Gap Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Insurance Coverage Gap Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies risks and insurance coverage gaps for businesses across industries. This insurance coverage gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT helps risk managers, business owners, and consultants assess current policies against industry-specific threats and budget constraints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce an executive summary highlighting critical gaps and priorities, followed by a detailed markdown table mapping risk categories to current coverage and recommended actions. Real-world use cases include pre-renewal audits, M&A due diligence, startup risk planning, and annual compliance reviews where structured, repeatable analysis saves hours of manual research. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic framework to evaluate liability, property, cyber, business interruption, professional indemnity, workers' compensation, and other coverage areas tailored to a specific business profile and risk tolerance. ● Produces an executive summary with key findings, critical gaps, and top priorities in 3–5 sentences ● Outputs a structured markdown table covering 6–10 risk categories specific to the business type ● Aligns recommendations with stated risk tolerance levels and available budget for additional coverage ● Supports rapid iteration by accepting business profile details, current policies, and industry-specific risk factors as input ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant specializing in insurance coverage review and gap analysis.

## Task
Identify potential risks and gaps in coverage for the specified business, then create a comprehensive risk management analysis. Assess current insurance policies against industry-specific risks and evaluate vulnerabilities. Deliver a structured analysis with actionable recommendations.

## Context
**Business & Coverage Details:**
{{business-profile}}
(Include: business type, current insurance policies held, known industry-specific risks, risk tolerance level, and budget available for additional coverage)

## Output
Provide a brief executive summary (3-5 sentences) highlighting:
- Key findings
- Critical gaps identified
- Top 2-3 priorities

Then present your analysis in a markdown table:

| Risk Category | Current Coverage | Recommended Actions |
|---------------|------------------|---------------------|

Include 6-10 rows covering the most relevant risk categories for this business type (e.g., liability, property, cyber, business interruption, professional indemnity, workers' compensation). Tailor recommendations to the stated risk tolerance and budget constraints.
```
