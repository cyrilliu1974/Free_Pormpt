# Email Course Generator for 5-Day Sequences

## 簡介

The Email Course Generator for 5-Day Sequences is a free AI prompt that creates complete educational email courses for coaches, creators, and marketers who need to nurture subscribers toward a paid offer. This email course prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces five structured emails (400-500 words each) with attention-grabbing subject lines, 3-5 immediately actionable strategies per day, storytelling elements, and natural progression from education to conversion. Each email includes a cliffhanger teaser to maintain engagement, and Day 5 ties the sequence together with a roadmap and soft pitch. Simply specify your target audience, course topic, desired outcome, and related paid offer, and the prompt outputs formatted, ready-to-send email copy that balances teaching with selling. This prompt is ideal for solo entrepreneurs building automated funnels, course creators warming cold subscribers, and service providers establishing authority before pitching. ● Outputs five complete emails with subject lines, body copy, recaps, and cliffhanger teasers in a single generation. ● Balances education and promotion by delivering actionable value each day before introducing the paid offer on Day 5. ● Uses conversational storytelling, analogies, and examples to keep subscribers engaged throughout the sequence. ● Formats output with clear day markers, subheadings, and structure for immediate use in email platforms or autoresponders. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write email courses that educate subscribers and guide them toward a paid offer.

## Context

- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Course topic: {{course-topic}}
- Desired outcome: {{desired-outcome}}
- Related paid offer: {{paid-offer}}

## Task

Write a complete 5-day email course following this structure:

**Every email:**
- Open with an attention-grabbing, curiosity-inducing subject line
- Include 3-5 actionable strategies or techniques subscribers can implement immediately
- Warm, conversational tone; 400-500 words per email; no exclamation points; no emojis
- Use storytelling, analogies, and relatable examples
- Close with a cliffhanger or teaser for the next day (except Day 5)

**Day 1:** Set the stage, generate excitement, deliver a quick win or valuable insight.

**Days 2-4:** Each email covers one crucial strategy, technique, or mindset shift with clear examples and step-by-step guidance. Include a 1-2 sentence recap of the previous day.

**Day 5:** Tie everything together, summarize key takeaways, provide a roadmap for implementation, and include a soft pitch for the paid offer. Include a 1-2 sentence recap of the previous day.

## Output

For each of the 5 days, use this structure:

**Day [N] - Subject Line:** [subject line]

**Recap (Days 2-5 only):** [1-2 sentence recap of the previous email]

**Body:** [email body]

**Closing teaser (Days 1-4 only):** [teaser for the next email]

Use short paragraphs, bullet points where appropriate, and clear subheadings within each email body.
```
