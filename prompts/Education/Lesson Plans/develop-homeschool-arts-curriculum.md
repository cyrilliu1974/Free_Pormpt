# Homeschool Arts Curriculum Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Homeschool Arts Curriculum Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates customized arts education strategies for homeschooling families. It produces a markdown table organizing multiple art and music forms - visual arts, instrumental music, vocal music, dance, theater, and digital arts - across beginner, intermediate, and advanced skill levels, complete with measurable learning objectives and vetted resource recommendations. This homeschool arts curriculum prompt for ChatGPT works on Claude and Gemini as well, turning your family's context (ages, interests, materials, learning philosophy) into a ready-to-implement curriculum plan with 4-6 disciplines, 2-3 objectives per level, and 2-3 high-quality resources (books, apps, online courses, community programs) aligned to your homeschooling approach. Parents use it when planning a semester, adding creative subjects to core academics, or structuring multi-child households where skill levels differ. The prompt adapts to developmental stages, available supplies, and whether you follow classical, unschooling, Charlotte Mason, or eclectic methods. ● Covers 4-6 art and music forms in a single table, from painting and piano to theater and digital media ● Breaks each discipline into beginner, intermediate, and advanced tracks with specific, measurable goals ● Recommends 2-3 accessible, high-quality resources per skill level - books, courses, apps, or local programs ● Aligns every suggestion with the homeschooling philosophy, child's age, and materials you already own ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational strategist specializing in homeschool curricula that integrate arts education.

## Task
Create a comprehensive arts education strategy table that incorporates music and art into the homeschool curriculum. The strategy should span multiple art and music forms, accommodate various skill levels (beginner, intermediate, advanced), and provide actionable learning objectives with high-quality resources for each combination.

## Context
{{homeschool-context}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with these columns:

| Art/Music Form | Skill Level | Learning Objectives | Resources |
|----------------|-------------|---------------------|-----------|

**Requirements:**
- Cover 4-6 different art/music forms (e.g., visual arts, instrumental music, vocal music, dance, theater, digital arts)
- Include at least 2 skill levels per form
- Provide 2-3 specific, measurable learning objectives per skill level
- Recommend 2-3 high-quality, accessible resources per skill level (books, online courses, materials, apps, community programs)
- Ensure resources align with the homeschooling approach, child's developmental stage, and available materials described in the context
- Format the table for easy scanning and implementation
```
