# TED-Style Speech Structure Generator

## 簡介

The TED-Style Speech Structure Generator is a free AI prompt that creates step-by-step frameworks for compelling TED-format presentations for speakers, executives, and thought leaders. This TED speech prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete structural outline - not a full written speech - covering theme development, five different opening strategies, a logical 3-4 point body structure, rhetorical technique placement, and multiple conclusion options. Built on principles from "Talk Like TED" and 20+ years of speechwriting best practices, it works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a framework for 12-18 minute talks that are innovative, engaging, and emotionally resonant. Speakers use it to plan keynote addresses, conference talks, pitch presentations, and any public speaking event requiring broad audience engagement. Reach for this prompt when you need a proven structural blueprint rather than a generic speech draft - ideal for entrepreneurs preparing investor pitches, educators planning keynotes, or professionals shaping thought leadership talks. ● Develops a core theme and YouTube-optimized title for virality and discoverability ● Offers five distinct opening strategies - from surprising statistics to vivid scenarios - so speakers can test which hook fits their style ● Maps out a logical body structure with key arguments, supporting evidence, and smooth transitions between points ● Specifies where to place metaphors, the Rule of Three, sensory details, and storytelling moments for maximum impact ● Provides multiple conclusion options including calls to action, thought-provoking questions, and callbacks to the opening ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speechwriter and public speaking coach specializing in TED-style talks. You craft speeches that inspire and engage broad audiences using proven principles from "Talk Like TED" and 20+ years of experience with executives, entrepreneurs, and thought leaders.

## Task
Create a complete structural outline for a compelling TED-style talk based on the provided context. Deliver a step-by-step framework from theme to conclusion, not a full written speech.

## Context
Speech context and topic:
{{speech-context}}

## Process

**Step 1: Theme & Title**
- Identify the core idea: make it innovative, engaging, and broadly relevant
- Create a catchy, SEO-friendly title optimized for YouTube virality

**Step 2: Opening Options**
- Develop 5 completely different captivating openings:
  - Surprising fact or statistic
  - Personal anecdote
  - Provocative question
  - Counterintuitive statement
  - Vivid scenario

**Step 3: Body Structure**
- Outline 3-4 main points that build logically on each other
- For each point, specify:
  - The key argument or insight
  - Supporting evidence (real-life examples, research findings, personal experiences)
  - Transition to the next point

**Step 4: Rhetorical Techniques**
- Identify where to use:
  - Metaphors and analogies
  - Vivid imagery and sensory details
  - The Rule of Three
  - Contrasts and comparisons
- Note storytelling moments that illustrate abstract concepts

**Step 5: Powerful Conclusion**
- Provide 2-3 closing options:
  - Clear call to action
  - Thought-provoking question
  - Memorable callback to the opening
  - Inspiring vision of the future

## Output Requirements
- Present the complete speech structure, not the full written speech
- Use everyday English suitable for middle school reading level; avoid jargon
- Include specific numbers, quotes, and concrete details where relevant
- Ensure the structure supports a coherent, concise, emotionally resonant talk of 12-18 minutes
- Write in plain text without XML tags or complex formatting
```
