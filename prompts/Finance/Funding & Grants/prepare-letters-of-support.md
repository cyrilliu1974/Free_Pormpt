# Grant Letter of Support Generator

## 簡介

The Grant Letter of Support Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compelling endorsement letters to strengthen funding applications for grant writers, nonprofit professionals, and researchers. This grant letter prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to compose structured support letters that analyze proposal strengths, articulate concrete impacts with supporting data, and establish the endorser's credibility and relationship to the project. It works by taking your proposal title, applicant information, funding organization, proposal overview, and endorser context, then producing a formal letter with clear sections: introduction establishing the endorser's authority, proposal overview summarizing the initiative's significance, detailed endorsement linking strengths to funder priorities, and an unequivocal funding recommendation. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major AI platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce multiple letters of support from different endorsers, when you want to ensure consistent quality and structure across endorsements, or when you need to quickly draft a professional letter that highlights specific proposal elements aligned with a particular funder's mission. ● Structures letters with logical flow from endorser introduction through strong closing recommendation ● Emphasizes concrete impacts supported by specific examples and data rather than generic praise ● Tailors language to reflect each endorser's unique credentials, relationship, and authority ● Aligns proposal strengths explicitly with the funding organization's stated goals and mission ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer specializing in letters of support that strengthen funding proposals through credible endorsement and clear articulation of impact.

## Task
Compose a persuasive letter of support for the grant proposal specified below. The letter must:

- Analyze and highlight the proposal's key strengths and alignment with the funder's priorities
- Emphasize concrete potential impacts supported by specific examples or data
- Establish the endorser's credibility and their relationship to the proposal
- Use clear, logical structure that guides the reader from introduction through strong closing recommendation
- Tailor language and emphasis to reflect the endorser's unique perspective and authority

## Context
**Grant proposal:** {{proposal-title}}

**Applicant:** {{applicant}}

**Funding organization:** {{funder}}

**Proposal overview:** {{proposal-overview}}
(Include key strengths, potential impacts, alignment with funder goals, and any relevant data or examples that demonstrate merit)

**Endorser perspective:** {{endorser-context}}
(The endorser's role, relationship to the proposal, credentials, and basis for support)

## Output
Structure the letter with these sections:

**Introduction** – Establish the endorser's identity and their connection to the proposal

**Proposal Overview** – Summarize the initiative's purpose and significance

**Endorsement** – Detail why this proposal merits funding, emphasizing strengths, potential impact, and alignment with the funder's mission

**Conclusion** – Provide an unequivocal recommendation for funding

Use professional tone appropriate for formal grant correspondence. Ensure each claim is substantiated and the overall argument flows logically from section to section.
```
