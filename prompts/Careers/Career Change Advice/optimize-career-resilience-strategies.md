# Career Resilience Strategy Guide for Economic Downturns

## 簡介

The Career Resilience Strategy Guide for Economic Downturns is a free AI prompt that produces a field-specific career management plan for professionals facing challenging economic times or industry upheavals. This career resilience prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the current state of your specified industry and delivers a structured guide covering resilience strategies, skill diversification recommendations, and a risk assessment table with concrete mitigation tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating an industry overview that contextualizes current challenges, three detailed resilience strategies with sub-points, five actionable skill diversification tips, and a risk mitigation matrix scoring likelihood and impact. Each output includes properly cited sources to ground the advice in real-world data. Professionals use it when facing layoffs, automation threats, market contractions, or sector-wide disruption to build a personalized action plan rather than generic career advice. ● Provides an industry-specific overview that maps current economic challenges to real career impacts in your field ● Delivers three multi-point resilience strategies prioritizing practical actions you can start immediately ● Lists five skill diversification tips aligned with evolving demands in your industry ● Generates a risk mitigation table scoring career threats by likelihood and impact, paired with concrete countermeasures ## Prompt
