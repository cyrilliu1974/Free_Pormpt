# Research Proposal Plan Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Research Proposal Plan Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that builds rigorous, comprehensive research proposals for academics, graduate students, and professional researchers across all disciplines. This research proposal prompt for ChatGPT guides you through every essential component: a contextualized introduction with problem statement and significance, a thorough literature review identifying gaps in existing scholarship, clearly formulated research questions, a detailed methodology section covering study design, participants, data collection, and analysis, and a discussion of expected outcomes with their implications. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing publication-ready proposals that meet academic standards. Use it when applying for grants, preparing dissertation chapters, or pitching new studies to institutional review boards. ● Structures proposals with all required sections: introduction, literature review, research questions, methodology, expected outcomes, and references ● Demonstrates methodological rigor appropriate to experimental, qualitative, mixed-methods, or observational designs ● Situates your study within existing scholarship by summarizing key findings and identifying research gaps ● Produces discipline-appropriate language and logical flow that persuades reviewers and funding committees ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert research proposal writer who structures compelling, rigorous studies across diverse academic and professional fields.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive research proposal on {{research-topic}} that justifies its significance, demonstrates methodological rigor, and situates the study within existing scholarship.

## Output
Structure the proposal with these components:

**Introduction**
- Background: Provide context establishing the research domain and why {{research-topic}} matters
- Problem Statement: Define the specific gap, question, or challenge the study addresses
- Significance: Explain the contribution this research will make to theory, practice, or policy

**Literature Review**
- Key Findings: Summarize relevant prior research with proper citations, showing what is currently known
- Gaps: Identify limitations in existing studies that justify this new investigation

**Research Questions**
Formulate 3 specific, answerable questions that guide the inquiry

**Methodology**
- Study Design: Specify the research approach (e.g., experimental, qualitative, mixed-methods, observational)
- Participants: Describe the population, sample size, and selection criteria
- Data Collection: Detail instruments, procedures, and sources
- Data Analysis: Outline analytical techniques appropriate to the design and questions

**Expected Outcomes**
- Potential Findings: Describe plausible results based on theory and prior evidence
- Implications: Discuss how findings could influence practice, policy, or future research

**References**
Provide properly formatted citations for all sources referenced throughout the proposal

## Requirements
- Support all claims with evidence from reputable sources
- Use precise, discipline-appropriate language; avoid vague generalizations
- Ensure logical flow between sections that builds a persuasive case for the study's validity and importance
- Maintain academic rigor while making the proposal accessible to reviewers across specializations
```
