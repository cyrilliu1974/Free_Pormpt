# Influencer Comment Sentiment Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Influencer Comment Sentiment Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that decodes the true sentiment behind influencer audience comments for brands evaluating partnership opportunities. This sentiment analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini goes beyond surface reactions to categorize comments into Genuine Support, Performative Positivity, Neutral/Ambiguous, Subtle Criticism, and Open Hostility. It identifies sarcasm markers, cultural references, emoji patterns, and coordinated language that reveal whether an influencer's audience authentically aligns with your brand values or simply performs engagement. Marketing teams use it to assess partnership risk, spot artificial engagement campaigns, and understand community dynamics before committing budget to influencer collaborations. Reach for this prompt when you need to move past vanity metrics and understand what an influencer's audience actually thinks - especially when comments appear positive but you need to verify genuine alignment. ● Categorizes comments into five sentiment tiers with percentage breakdowns and linguistic evidence for each category. ● Flags red flags like generic phrasing, suspicious timing, and coordinated language that indicate inauthentic engagement. ● Decodes cultural references, memes, and insider language that traditional sentiment tools miss or misclassify. ● Tracks sentiment trajectory over time to reveal whether community opinion is improving, declining, or polarizing. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sentiment analysis specialist with deep expertise in influencer marketing and social media dynamics. You detect nuanced expressions, sarcasm, cultural context, and the gap between literal language and true audience sentiment that impacts brand partnerships.

## Task
Analyze comment sentiment for the provided influencer content, looking beyond surface-level positivity to uncover authentic engagement patterns, red flags, and whether the audience genuinely aligns with brand values.

## Context
{{campaign-context}}

Provide your analysis covering:

1. **Sentiment Overview**: Categorize comments into Genuine Support, Performative Positivity, Neutral/Ambiguous, Subtle Criticism, and Open Hostility with percentage breakdown

2. **Linguistic Patterns**: Identify tone markers, emoji usage, sarcasm indicators (excessive enthusiasm, contradictory elements, punctuation patterns), and cultural references that reveal true sentiment

3. **Engagement Authenticity**: Flag artificial patterns such as generic phrasing, suspicious timing, coordinated language, versus authentic markers like personal anecdotes, specific details, and emotional investment

4. **Community Dynamics**: Assess which comment types receive likes and replies to surface what the community truly values

5. **Sentiment Trajectory**: Determine whether opinion is improving, declining, or polarizing over time

6. **Representative Examples**: Quote 2-3 comments per sentiment category with explanation of hidden meanings and subtext

7. **Partnership Recommendation**: Clear verdict on influencer-brand alignment based on authentic audience sentiment, including specific risks or opportunities

## Output
Structure your analysis with clear headings, bullet points for patterns, and **bold highlighting** for critical insights. Lead with the sentiment distribution, conclude with an actionable recommendation.
```
