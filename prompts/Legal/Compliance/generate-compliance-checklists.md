# Compliance Checklist Generator for Any Regulation

## 簡介

The Compliance Checklist Generator for Any Regulation is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, actionable compliance checklists tailored to any regulatory framework or industry standard for compliance officers, legal teams, and business owners. This compliance checklist prompt for ChatGPT works by taking a regulation or industry as input and producing a structured 10+ item checklist where each entry includes a specific action item, required documentation, and a realistic timeline. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Organizations use it to map GDPR compliance steps, prepare for SOC 2 audits, implement HIPAA controls, meet FDA regulatory requirements, or satisfy industry-specific standards in finance, healthcare, manufacturing, and beyond. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate complex regulatory language into concrete, implementable steps for teams with varying levels of compliance expertise. ● Outputs 10 or more numbered action items, each with specific documentation requirements and realistic deadlines ● Includes an additional considerations section that surfaces edge cases, dependencies, and ongoing obligations ● Designed for clarity and accessibility, so both compliance veterans and teams new to a regulation can act on the checklist ● Adaptable to any regulation, standard, or industry framework by simply changing the input variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert compliance consultant creating a comprehensive, actionable checklist for regulatory compliance.

## Task
Generate a compliance checklist tailored to {{regulation-or-industry}} that includes all required actions, documentation, and timelines necessary to achieve full compliance with relevant laws and standards.

## Context
The checklist will be used by organizations seeking to understand and implement compliance requirements. Users may have varying levels of regulatory expertise, so clarity and practicality are essential.

## Output
Structure the compliance checklist as follows:

**Compliance Checklist for {{regulation-or-industry}}**

1. **[Action Item 1]**  
   Required Documentation: [Documentation 1]  
   Timeline: [Timeline 1]

2. **[Action Item 2]**  
   Required Documentation: [Documentation 2]  
   Timeline: [Timeline 2]

3. **[Action Item 3]**  
   Required Documentation: [Documentation 3]  
   Timeline: [Timeline 3]

*[Continue through at least 10 action items]*

10. **[Action Item 10]**  
    Required Documentation: [Documentation 10]  
    Timeline: [Timeline 10]

**Additional Considerations:**
- [Consideration 1]
- [Consideration 2]
- [Consideration 3]

### Requirements
- Provide at least 10 numbered action items to ensure comprehensive coverage
- Each action item must be specific, concrete, and achievable
- Documentation requirements should be clearly identified and relevant
- Timelines should be realistic given the complexity of each action
- Use clear, concise language accessible to users with varying expertise
- Avoid assumptions about the user's current compliance status or available resources
- Include additional considerations covering important aspects not addressed in the main checklist
```
