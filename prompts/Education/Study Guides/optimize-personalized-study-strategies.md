# Personalized Study Strategy Builder by Learning Style

## 簡介

The Personalized Study Strategy Builder by Learning Style is a free AI prompt that analyzes your learning style and creates a complete study approach with techniques, schedules, and resources tailored to how you learn best. This study strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your learning style (visual, auditory, reading-writing, or kinesthetic) and produces a structured study plan covering information intake, processing, and retention methods specific to your cognitive preferences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver a formatted study framework that includes a session-by-session table template and curated resource recommendations. Students preparing for exams, professionals learning new skills, and educators designing personalized instruction use it to move beyond generic study advice and build strategies grounded in learning science. Reach for this prompt when you need to convert a learning style assessment into an actionable study system, or when traditional study methods feel ineffective and you want an approach matched to your cognitive strengths. ● Maps each learning style to specific techniques for absorbing, processing, and retaining information ● Produces a study plan template with sessions, durations, activities, and resource columns ready to adapt ● Recommends learning resources aligned with your cognitive preferences rather than one-size-fits-all materials ● Delivers structured output that identifies learning style traits and organizes strategies into actionable categories ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert learning style optimizer who crafts personalized study strategies for enhanced learning outcomes.

## Task
Analyze the provided learning style and develop a comprehensive study approach tailored to its unique characteristics. Provide specific techniques for absorbing, processing, and retaining information, along with a customizable study plan template and curated resource recommendations.

## Context
Learning style: {{learning-style}}

## Output
Deliver your response using the following structure:

**Learning Style Overview**
- Dominant learning style: [Visual/Auditory/Reading-Writing/Kinesthetic]
- Key characteristics: [Bullet point list of learning style traits]

**Information Intake Techniques**
1. [Technique 1]
2. [Technique 2]
3. [Technique 3]

**Information Processing Techniques**
1. [Technique 1]
2. [Technique 2]
3. [Technique 3]

**Information Retention Techniques**

**Study Plan Template**
| Study Session | Duration | Activities | Resources |
|---------------|----------|------------|-----------|
| [Session 1]   | [Time]   | [Activities] | [Resources] |
| [Session 2]   | [Time]   | [Activities] | [Resources] |

**Recommended Resources**

Focus on actionable, personalized advice rather than generic recommendations. Ensure the study plan is easily adaptable and resources cover diverse learning needs.
```
