# Local SEO Schema Markup Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Local SEO Schema Markup Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized schema markup plans to improve local search visibility for any business type and location. This local SEO schema prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type and location to recommend three relevant schema types complete with key attributes, step-by-step implementation instructions, optimization tips, and expected benefits. Whether you run a restaurant, dental practice, retail store, or service business, the prompt delivers a structured strategy that helps search engines understand and display your business information more effectively in local results. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable markup recommendations you can implement immediately. Ideal for local business owners, SEO specialists, and web developers who need to enhance local search presence through structured data without deep technical knowledge of schema.org vocabulary. ● Recommends three relevant schema types with specific attributes matched to your business category and local market ● Provides clear, numbered implementation steps that guide you through adding structured data to your website ● Includes optimization tips to maximize schema effectiveness and avoid common local SEO mistakes ● Specifies concrete benefits for each schema type so you understand the search visibility impact ## Prompt
