# Essential HR Policies Identification Prompt

## 簡介

The Essential HR Policies Identification Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a structured framework of 10 foundational workforce protection policies tailored to your organizational context. This HR policies prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an HR policy architect, analyzing each policy through the lenses of legal compliance, risk mitigation, employee experience, and operational feasibility. It delivers a bullet-point framework covering policy names, criticality justifications, required components, and common pitfalls to avoid. The output is industry-agnostic and applies across sectors, helping HR teams, founders, and compliance officers build policies that function as both legal safeguards and cultural foundations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when establishing a new HR function, auditing existing policies, or adapting frameworks to changing legal environments. ● Identifies 10 core policies covering legal requirements, risk mitigation, and employee experience. ● Provides structured analysis including policy names, criticality rationale, key components, and implementation pitfalls. ● Tailors recommendations to your organizational context through a single variable input. ● Focuses on industry-agnostic policies applicable across sectors, from startups to enterprises. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR policy architect with deep expertise in employment law, risk mitigation, and workforce protection frameworks across organizations of all sizes.

## Task
Identify and detail 10 essential HR policies that form the foundation of effective workforce protection. Balance legal compliance with practical implementation. For each policy provide:
- **Policy name**
- **Why it's critical** for workforce protection
- **Key components** that must be included
- **Common implementation pitfalls** to avoid

Analyze each policy through the lenses of legal requirements, risk mitigation, employee experience, and operational feasibility. Focus on industry-agnostic policies that apply broadly across sectors.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive bullet point list with clear headings for each of the 10 policies. Structure each policy entry with the four required elements (name, criticality, components, pitfalls). Prioritize policies that serve as both legal shields and cultural cornerstones, protecting employees and organizations from disputes, regulatory violations, and workplace dysfunction.
```
