# Twitter Content Strategy Prompt for Brand Engagement

## 簡介

The Twitter Content Strategy Prompt for Brand Engagement is a free AI prompt that creates diverse, high-engagement tweet ideas tailored to your business type and brand voice. This Twitter content prompt for ChatGPT produces at least 10 ready-to-post tweets spanning promotional messages, industry insights, customer testimonials, interactive polls, and timely responses to trending topics. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure every tweet is clear, coherent, and stays within the 280-character limit. Designed for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it transforms your business details and brand identity into complete tweets that drive replies, retweets, and shares. Marketing teams, social media managers, and brand consultants use it to maintain consistent Twitter activity without sacrificing strategic variety or voice. ● Produces complete, post-ready tweets across multiple content types in a single generation ● Balances promotional, educational, social proof, and interactive formats for strategic variety ● Applies linguistic structure to maximize clarity and engagement within Twitter's character limit ● Includes compelling calls-to-action designed to increase replies, retweets, and shares ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist creating high-engagement Twitter content.

## Task
Develop a diverse set of tweet ideas that increase brand awareness and drive engagement. Each tweet must stay within Twitter's 280-character limit.

## Context
**Business & audience:** {{business-and-audience}}
(Include: business type, target audience demographics/interests, and any relevant industry context)

**Brand identity:** {{brand-identity}}
(Include: brand voice/tone, key products or services, and unique selling proposition)

Incorporate these content types:
- Promotional tweets that highlight value without hard-selling
- Industry insights and thought leadership
- Customer testimonials and social proof
- Interactive posts (polls, questions, challenges)
- Timely responses to relevant trending topics and hashtags

Use dependency grammar principles to ensure each tweet is clear, coherent, and impactful. Craft compelling calls-to-action that encourage replies, retweets, and shares.

## Output
Provide a numbered list of at least 10 tweet ideas. Each entry must:
- Stay within 280 characters
- Be ready to post (complete tweets, not templates)
- Span multiple content types from the list above
```
