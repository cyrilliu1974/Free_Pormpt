# Social Media Post Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates five ready-to-publish social media posts tailored to your product, audience, platform, and brand voice. This social media post prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces platform-specific content that opens with hooks designed for each network's format, highlights product benefits in concise language, and includes calls-to-action that drive engagement or conversions. It considers character limits, visual emphasis, algorithm preferences, and user behavior patterns to format posts that maximize retention and performance. Marketing teams use it to maintain consistent posting schedules across Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, and TikTok without sacrificing quality or brand alignment. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple post variations quickly, want to test different messaging angles, or need to adapt a product launch across platforms while maintaining a unified brand voice. ● Produces five distinct posts per generation, each with headline hooks, benefit statements, and conversion-focused calls-to-action ● Adapts tone, length, and formatting to match platform conventions and algorithm preferences on Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, or TikTok ● Incorporates brand voice requirements to ensure consistency across campaigns and maintains alignment with target audience values ● Outputs hashtag and mention suggestions on separate lines beneath each post for easy copy-paste publishing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in audience engagement and conversion-driven content.

## Task
Create 5 compelling social media posts that promote {{product-or-service}} to {{target-audience}} on {{platform}}. Each post must:

- Open with an attention-grabbing headline or hook suited to the platform's format
- Highlight relevant benefits in concise, impactful language
- Include a clear call-to-action that drives engagement or conversion
- Match {{brand-voice}} in tone and vocabulary
- Incorporate platform-appropriate hashtags, mentions, or formatting

Structure each post for clarity and coherence, ensuring the most important information appears early to maximize retention.

## Context
Consider the platform's unique features (character limits, visual emphasis, algorithm preferences, typical user behavior) and tailor content format, length, and style accordingly. Align messaging with what the target audience values and how they interact on this specific platform.

## Output
Provide a numbered list (1–5) with:
- Full post text ready to publish
- Relevant hashtags or @ mentions on separate lines beneath each post
```
