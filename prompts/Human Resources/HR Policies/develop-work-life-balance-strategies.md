# Work-Life Balance Strategy Builder for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Work-Life Balance Strategy Builder for HR Teams is a free AI prompt from God of Permit that creates structured, actionable strategies to prevent burnout and promote employee well-being in corporate environments. This work-life balance prompt for ChatGPT produces a five-section hierarchical outline covering company-wide policies, management initiatives, employee empowerment practices, wellness programs, and measurable evaluation metrics. You provide your company culture as context, and the prompt generates fifteen concrete strategies (three per category) each with two implementation or benefit subpoints. The output is formatted as a ready-to-present outline that runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering practical recommendations without generic platitudes. HR professionals use it to draft policy proposals, workplace consultants apply it when advising clients on burnout prevention, and organizational leaders reference it during strategic planning sessions. ● Creates fifteen tailored strategies across five critical dimensions of workplace well-being, each with detailed implementation guidance ● Adapts recommendations to your specific company culture rather than offering one-size-fits-all advice ● Structures output as a hierarchical outline that translates directly into presentations, policy documents, or implementation roadmaps ● Prioritizes low-cost, high-impact initiatives that can be deployed without major budget allocation ● Includes concrete metrics and evaluation methods so progress can be tracked and communicated to stakeholders ## Prompt
