# Content Calendar Generator for Regular Blog Posts

## 簡介

The Content Calendar Generator for Regular Blog Posts is a free AI prompt that builds editorial calendars aligned to your business objectives and audience interests. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, topic focus, and posting frequency to produce a markdown table with at least 10 scheduled entries. Each entry includes a publish date, topic, target keyword, and call-to-action. The prompt balances evergreen content with timely subjects, varies content formats (how-to guides, listicles, case studies, thought leadership), and integrates seasonal themes and industry events. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Content strategists, marketing teams, and blog editors use it to maintain consistent publishing schedules that drive traffic and conversions. ● Identifies high-value keywords and relevant sub-topics within your chosen focus area ● Balances evergreen posts with trending topics to sustain audience interest over time ● Varies content types to keep readers engaged and match different stages of the buyer journey ● Aligns every call-to-action with the post content and stated business goals ● Ensures the publishing schedule is sustainable given your team's resources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in editorial calendar planning and audience-driven content development.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content calendar for regular blog posts that aligns with business objectives and audience interests. The calendar should include at least 10 scheduled entries.

## Context
**Business and Audience:**
{{business-context}}
(Describe your business type, target audience, key business goals, and any specific value propositions or competitive positioning.)

**Topic Focus:**
{{topic}}

**Publishing Cadence:**
{{posting-frequency}}

## Approach
- Identify relevant sub-topics and high-value keywords within the topic area
- Balance evergreen content with timely, trending subjects
- Vary content formats (how-to guides, listicles, case studies, thought leadership) to sustain engagement
- Incorporate seasonal themes and industry events where relevant
- Align calls-to-action with each post's content and the stated business goals
- Ensure the schedule is sustainable given available resources

## Output
Present the content calendar as a markdown table with the following columns:

| Date | Topic | Target Keyword | Call-to-Action |
|------|-------|----------------|----------------|

Provide at least 10 entries. Each call-to-action should be specific and aligned with both the post content and business objectives.
```
