# Local SEO Directory Submission Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Local SEO Directory Submission Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates research-backed submission plans for businesses optimizing their local search presence across multiple platforms. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business details and produces a five-part strategy covering directory research, essential data checklists, submission workflows, consistency protocols, and monitoring schedules. It prioritizes high-impact platforms like Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, and industry-specific citation sources while explaining the SEO rationale behind each recommendation. The output works with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver structured, actionable guidance for maintaining accurate NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across all listings. Use this prompt when you need a systematic approach to claiming, optimizing, and managing local business listings across directories, or when auditing existing citation consistency for a client or multi-location business. ● Identifies and ranks local directories by SEO impact for your industry and location ● Generates complete data checklists to ensure uniform NAP information across all platforms ● Provides step-by-step submission workflows for claiming and optimizing each listing type ● Includes monitoring cadence and update protocols to maintain accuracy over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert local SEO specialist optimizing business listings for maximum online visibility and platform consistency.

## Task
Research reputable local SEO directories and online listings relevant to the specified business. Create a comprehensive submission and management strategy that ensures consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across all platforms. Provide step-by-step instructions for submission, ongoing maintenance, and monitoring.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-details}}

Include in your research: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, industry-specific directories, and local citation sources relevant to the business location and sector.

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a numbered list with clear headings for each main step:

1. **Directory Research & Prioritization** - List of recommended platforms ranked by impact
2. **Essential Information Checklist** - Complete data fields required for each listing type
3. **Submission Strategy** - Step-by-step process for claiming and optimizing listings
4. **Consistency Protocol** - How to maintain uniform information across all platforms
5. **Monitoring & Update Schedule** - Cadence and methods for ongoing management

Explain the SEO impact and rationale for each recommendation.
```
