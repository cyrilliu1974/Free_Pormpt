# Market Entry Partnership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Market Entry Partnership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates industry-specific partnership strategies for businesses entering new markets. This market entry partnership prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured strategy document that begins with a detailed industry overview (market size, key trends, and major players), identifies three high-impact partner types with their value propositions and real-world examples, outlines a clear five-step partnership roadmap, and defines measurable success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to the specific dynamics of any industry you specify - whether healthcare, fintech, manufacturing, or consumer goods. Instead of generic advice, it tailors partner recommendations to the competitive landscape and market structure of your target sector. This prompt is built for business development professionals, strategic partnership managers, and executives exploring geographic or vertical market expansion. ● Analyzes market size, growth trajectory, and the three most important trends shaping your target industry ● Identifies three distinct partner types - distributors, technology allies, or influencers - with concrete value propositions and named example organizations ● Delivers a five-step partnership strategy from initial outreach through relationship management ● Defines key success metrics (pipeline contribution, time-to-market reduction, or joint revenue) tailored to partnership goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic partnership expert specializing in market entry strategies across diverse industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategy for leveraging partnership networks to access new markets in {{industry}}.

## Context
The strategy must be grounded in the specific dynamics of {{industry}}, identifying the most relevant and impactful partner types and outlining a clear, actionable roadmap. Avoid generic advice—tailor recommendations to the industry's market structure, trends, and competitive landscape.

## Output
Provide your strategy in the following structure:

**Industry Overview**
- Market size: [current valuation and growth trajectory]
- Key trends: [3 dominant trends shaping the market]
- Major players: [3 leading companies or organizations]

**Key Partner Types**
1. [Partner type name]
   - Value proposition: [what this partner brings to market entry]
   - Example partners: [2 specific organizations]
2. [Partner type name]
   - Value proposition: [what this partner brings to market entry]
   - Example partners: [2 specific organizations]
3. [Partner type name]
   - Value proposition: [what this partner brings to market entry]
   - Example partners: [2 specific organizations]

**Partnership Strategy**
Step 1: [Initial action]
Step 2: [Second action]
Step 3: [Third action]
Step 4: [Fourth action]
Step 5: [Final action]

**Key Success Metrics**
• [First metric]
• [Second metric]
• [Third metric]
```
