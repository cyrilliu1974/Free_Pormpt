# SERP Analysis Strategy Prompt for SEO Research

## 簡介

The SERP Analysis Strategy Prompt for SEO Research is a free AI prompt that conducts detailed search engine results page audits and delivers competitive intelligence for SEO professionals and content strategists. This SERP analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines keyword metrics like search volume, cost-per-click, and difficulty scores, then reverse-engineers the top 10 ranking competitors by dissecting their title tags, meta descriptions, backlink counts, and domain authority. It identifies content gaps where competitors fall short, surfaces featured snippet opportunities, maps the link profiles of the top five results, and delivers specific on-page, off-page, and technical SEO recommendations grounded in real competitive data. SEO analysts use it to understand why pages rank, content teams use it to find underserved topics, and link builders use it to discover proven acquisition strategies. This prompt is built for SEO professionals, digital marketers, and content strategists who need a systematic framework to analyze search results and extract actionable insights rather than surface-level observations. ● Produces a structured breakdown of the top 10 competitors including URLs, ranking signals, backlink metrics, and domain ratings ● Identifies content gaps and featured snippet opportunities by comparing what ranks against what users may still need ● Maps the link profiles of top-ranking pages, categorizing link types such as editorial placements, guest posts, and resource pages ● Delivers three tiers of recommendations - on-page content and internal linking fixes, off-page link building strategies, and technical SEO improvements - all derived from the competitive intelligence gathered ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst and strategist specializing in comprehensive SERP analysis and competitive research.

## Task
Conduct a thorough SERP analysis for the target keywords below. Examine key metrics, reverse-engineer competitors' SEO tactics, identify content gaps and featured snippet opportunities, analyze link profiles, and provide specific, actionable optimization recommendations.

## Context
Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in the following structure:

### SERP Overview
- Keyword
- Search Volume
- CPC
- Difficulty Score

### Top 10 Competitors
For each competitor (positions 1-10):
- URL and Position
- Title Tag
- Meta Description
- Backlinks
- Domain Rating

### Content Gaps
List content opportunities your analysis reveals that competitors are missing or underserving.

### Featured Snippet Opportunities
Identify queries where featured snippets exist, including the current snippet URL for each.

### Competitors' Link Profiles
For the top 5 competitors, list their most valuable linking domains and link types (e.g., editorial, guest post, directory, resource page).

### Optimization Recommendations
Provide specific, actionable recommendations in three categories:

**On-Page:**
- Title, meta, content, and internal linking improvements

**Off-Page:**
- Link building strategies based on competitor analysis

**Technical:**
- Site structure, performance, and crawlability enhancements

Avoid generic advice. Base all recommendations directly on the competitive intelligence you uncover.
```
