# Gallery-Style Interior Visualization Prompt

## 簡介

The Gallery-Style Interior Visualization Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces photorealistic open-plan residential interiors with art-gallery aesthetics for designers, architects, and marketers. This interior visualization prompt for Midjourney and Flux creates spacious contemporary living spaces that blend architectural precision with curated art gallery ambiance. It generates wide-angle interior shots featuring customizable material palettes, floor-to-ceiling glass, natural daylight, and sculptural focal points, all rendered with physically accurate lighting and ultra-detailed textures. Interior designers use it for client presentations, real estate teams deploy it for high-end property marketing, and architects rely on it to visualize spatial concepts without expensive rendering software. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-quality architectural visualizations that balance understated luxury, clean geometry, and intentional negative space. ● Produces photorealistic open-plan interiors with configurable material palettes, color tones, and art curation styles. ● Features architectural lighting setups with recessed spotlights, track lighting, and global illumination for depth and realism. ● Generates eye-level wide-angle shots with deep focus, clean lines, and modular furniture in soft organic forms. ● Avoids common pitfalls: generic luxury tropes, over-decoration, cartoon aesthetics, and unrealistic material rendering. ## Prompt
