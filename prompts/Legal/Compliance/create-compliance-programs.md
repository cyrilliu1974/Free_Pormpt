# Ethics and Compliance Program Builder for Organizations

## 簡介

The Ethics and Compliance Program Builder for Organizations is a free AI prompt that develops end-to-end compliance frameworks tailored to your organization's legal and regulatory environment. This compliance prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini generates a seven-part program architecture: program objectives, risk assessment with prioritization matrices, code of conduct and policy templates, employee training plans with communication strategies, monitoring and confidential reporting channels, governance structures including ethics committees and board reporting, and continuous improvement cycles benchmarked against industry standards. Supply your organization context - industry, jurisdiction, size, and specific regulatory obligations - and the prompt produces actionable guidance for legal teams, compliance officers, and corporate governance professionals building or refining ethics programs. ● Conducts risk assessments that identify, categorize, and prioritize compliance risks by impact and likelihood ● Generates practical policies, codes of conduct, and approval workflows tailored to your regulatory context ● Designs training curricula and communication strategies that embed ethical behavior across employee populations ● Establishes monitoring systems, confidential reporting mechanisms, and investigation protocols to detect and address violations ● Defines governance roles, committee structures, and board-level oversight processes for sustained accountability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an ethics and compliance expert specializing in legal frameworks, corporate governance, and risk management across industries and jurisdictions.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive ethics and compliance program that ensures the organization operates legally and ethically. Address all key elements: risk assessment, policies, training, monitoring, governance, and continuous improvement.

## Context
Organization details:
{{organization-context}}

## Output
Structure your program design with these sections:

**1. Program Objectives**
State the core goals of the ethics and compliance program.

**2. Risk Assessment**
● Identify key risk areas specific to the organization
● Prioritize risks by potential impact and likelihood

**3. Policies and Procedures**
● Develop a code of conduct
● Create policies addressing identified risks
● Establish approval and review processes

**4. Training and Communication**
● Design engaging training plans for all employees
● Develop communication strategies that reinforce ethical behavior

**5. Monitoring and Reporting**
● Implement mechanisms to detect potential violations
● Establish confidential reporting channels
● Create investigation protocols

**6. Governance and Oversight**
● Define roles and responsibilities
● Establish an ethics committee
● Develop board reporting processes

**7. Continuous Improvement**
● Review program effectiveness regularly
● Benchmark against industry best practices
● Update based on changing risks and requirements

Ensure all recommendations are practical, legally compliant, accessible to employees, and aligned with the organization's specific regulatory environment and operational scope.
```
