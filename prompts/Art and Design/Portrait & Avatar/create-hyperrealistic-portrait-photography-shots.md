The Hyperrealistic Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates magazine-quality editorial portraits with authentic skin texture, natural hair detail, and professional camera simulation for photographers, content creators, and creative directors. This hyperrealistic portrait photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana simulates a Canon EOS R5 body paired with an RF 85mm f/1.2L USM lens, complete with golden hour lighting, creamy bokeh backgrounds, and Portra 800 film emulation. You define the pose and outfit plus the environment, and the prompt renders visible skin pores, individual hair strands with flyaways, precise catchlights in the eyes, and the warm grain and color science of analog film stock. Use it for fashion lookbooks, editorial features, brand campaigns, or any project that demands the fidelity of a professional studio shoot without the overhead.
