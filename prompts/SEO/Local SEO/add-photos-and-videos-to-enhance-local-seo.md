# Local SEO Visual Content Strategy for Business Listings

## 簡介

The Local SEO Visual Content Strategy for Business Listings is a free AI prompt that generates tailored plans for adding high-quality photos and videos to local business profiles across Google Business Profile, Bing Places, and other local search platforms. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your business name, industry, offerings, target audience, and location, then delivering a complete strategy covering visual content selection, technical shooting guidelines, product and service showcase techniques, location photography best practices, file optimization for search platforms, metadata and geotagging rules, SEO-optimized descriptions, and platform-specific upload requirements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce numbered, actionable plans that help local businesses stand out in map packs and local search results through strategic visual content. Reach for this prompt when you need to improve the visibility and engagement of a local business listing through optimized photos and videos that drive both clicks and conversions. ● Creates guidelines for selecting photo and video types that resonate with specific industries and audiences ● Provides technical specifications for image dimensions, compression, file naming, and format requirements across major local listing platforms ● Generates metadata and tagging frameworks including alt text, captions, geotags, and structured data for maximum local search visibility ● Delivers SEO-optimized description templates that incorporate location signals and relevant keywords for each visual asset ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO specialist focused on visual content strategy for business listings.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan for adding high-quality photos and videos to local listings that boost engagement and improve search rankings. Develop actionable guidelines for selecting, creating, and optimizing visual content that effectively showcases products, services, and location.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-name}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Primary offerings:** {{products-services}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Location:** {{location}}

## Strategy Components
Your plan must address:

1. **Visual Content Selection** – What types of photos and videos work best for this business type and audience
2. **Content Creation Guidelines** – Technical specifications, lighting, composition, and shooting best practices
3. **Product & Service Showcase** – How to photograph/film offerings to drive conversions
4. **Location & Atmosphere** – Capturing the physical space, storefront, and local context
5. **Optimization Techniques** – File naming, compression, formats, and dimensions for local search platforms (Google Business Profile, Bing Places, etc.)
6. **Metadata & Tagging** – Alt text, captions, geotags, and structured data implementation
7. **Description Writing** – SEO-optimized copy for each visual asset that incorporates location signals and relevant keywords
8. **Upload & Distribution** – Platform-specific requirements and posting schedules

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with clear section headings. Each section should contain specific, actionable steps tailored to the business context provided.
```
