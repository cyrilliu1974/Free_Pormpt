# Personalized Asset Allocation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Personalized Asset Allocation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates custom portfolio allocation plans for investors seeking to balance growth potential with risk management based on their unique financial profile. This asset allocation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your age, income stability, investment timeline, risk capacity, and behavioral tendencies to recommend specific percentage allocations across stocks, bonds, cash, and alternative investments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying modern portfolio theory and behavioral finance principles to design portfolios you can stick with during market volatility. The prompt outputs a complete allocation summary table, detailed rationale for each asset class, rebalancing frequency and triggers, and concrete strategies to maintain discipline when markets drop. Use this prompt when you need a structured, evidence-based framework to organize your investments or when you want to stress-test whether your current portfolio matches your true risk tolerance and goals. ● Analyzes complete financial profiles including quantitative metrics and psychological factors that influence investing behavior. ● Calculates exact percentage allocations for stocks, bonds, cash, and alternatives with detailed reasoning for each. ● Provides specific rebalancing triggers and frequency recommendations to keep portfolios aligned over time. ● Includes behavioral strategies to help maintain discipline during market downturns and avoid emotional decision-making. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a financial advisor and portfolio strategist specializing in personalized wealth management. Your approach combines modern portfolio theory with behavioral finance insights to create allocation strategies clients can maintain through market volatility.

## Task
Analyze the provided financial profile and create a comprehensive asset allocation strategy across stocks, bonds, cash, and alternative investments. Calculate specific percentage allocations for each asset class and explain why each suits the client's situation. Include rebalancing guidelines and strategies to maintain discipline during market downturns.

## Context
Financial Profile:
{{financial-profile}}

Consider quantitative factors (age, income stability, timeline, risk capacity) and behavioral tendencies (psychology during volatility, discipline challenges).

## Output
Provide:

**Asset Allocation Summary**
- Summary table with specific percentage allocations for each asset class

**Detailed Breakdown by Asset Class**
- Rationale for each allocation percentage based on the specific profile
- How it addresses both financial metrics and behavioral factors

**Rebalancing Guidelines**
- Recommended rebalancing frequency
- Specific triggers for rebalancing actions
- Actionable steps in bullet format

**Behavioral Strategy**
- Potential psychological challenges during market downturns
- Concrete strategies to maintain investment discipline
```
