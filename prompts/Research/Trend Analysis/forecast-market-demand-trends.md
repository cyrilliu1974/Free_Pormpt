# Market Demand Forecast and Trend Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Demand Forecast and Trend Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps market analysts, strategists, and business professionals forecast demand and identify emerging trends across any market. This market demand forecast prompt for ChatGPT analyzes historical data, current market conditions, and future scenarios to produce a structured markdown table with 5-8 key trends, their impact assessments, and demand forecasts. You specify the market, timeframe, and key variables (economic indicators, consumer behavior, technological shifts, or industry-specific factors), and the prompt returns a three-column analysis covering Trend, Impact, and Forecast. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need disciplined, data-informed trend analysis for quarterly reviews, investment briefs, strategic planning decks, or competitive intelligence reports. ● Accepts custom market, timeframe, and key-variable inputs for tailored forecasts ● Delivers a clean markdown table structure ready for reports, decks, or dashboards ● Balances historical data review, current conditions, and forward-looking scenarios ● Covers 5-8 high-relevance trends with both qualitative impact and quantitative forecast dimensions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in trend analysis and demand forecasting.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive market analysis identifying key trends, assessing their impact, and forecasting future demand for {{market}}.

## Context
Analyze historical data, current market conditions, and potential future scenarios over {{timeframe}}. Consider economic indicators, consumer behavior, technological advancements, and industry-specific variables. Focus your analysis on {{key-variables}}.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Trend | Impact | Forecast |
|-------|--------|----------|

Each row should cover one significant market trend with:
- **Trend**: Clear description of the observed or emerging pattern
- **Impact**: Assessment of how this trend affects the market
- **Forecast**: Projected demand and market implications

Include 5-8 trends that are most relevant to the specified market and timeframe.
```
