# Humanoid Robot Assembly Line Image Generator

## 簡介

The Humanoid Robot Assembly Line Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-realistic wide-angle views of robotic assembly environments for concept artists, sci-fi illustrators, and designers exploring futuristic manufacturing themes. This robot assembly line prompt for Midjourney produces cinematic 3:2 aspect ratio images with controlled stylization (--stylize 300) and elevated chaos parameters (--chaos 40) to introduce organic variation into the mechanical subject matter. The prompt includes two locked style reference codes (--sref 1895230408 1067756127) at 400 weight, ensuring visual consistency across generations while depicting assembly line scenarios for humanoid robots or other subjects you specify.
