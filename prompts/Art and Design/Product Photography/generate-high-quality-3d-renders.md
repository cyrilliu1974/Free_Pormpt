# 3D Product Render Prompt for Garments

## 簡介

The 3D Product Render Prompt for Garments is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic apparel renders with studio-quality lighting and intricate fabric detail for designers, e-commerce sellers, and fashion marketers. It produces high-fidelity 3D visuals that show realistic cloth simulation, visible stitch and seam work, and soft reflections on clean backgrounds, making it an ideal 3D product photography prompt for ChatGPT-adjacent image generators like Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. The prompt takes a garment description and background choice as inputs, then renders the item with vibrant floral patterns, controlled lighting, and a premium aesthetic free from common artifacts like texture stretching or color banding. Use this prompt when you need scroll-stopping product imagery without a physical photo shoot, or when prototyping apparel designs before manufacturing. ● Renders garments with realistic cloth simulation, capturing stitch fidelity, seam detail, and natural fabric draping. ● Applies studio lighting with soft reflections and a clean background to emphasize product quality and premium appeal. ● Includes quality and stylization flags (--q 2, --s 100) and negative prompts to eliminate common 3D rendering issues like texture stretching, low-poly artifacts, and unwanted elements. ● Outputs square 1:1 aspect ratio images optimized for e-commerce thumbnails, social media posts, and portfolio presentation. ## Prompt

```
High-detail 3D product render of {{garment-description}} adorned with intricate, vibrant floral patterns; studio lighting with soft reflections on a clean {{background}}. Realistic cloth simulation, stitch and seam fidelity, premium aesthetic. --ar 1:1 --q 2 --s 100 --no body, face, watermark, low-poly, texture stretching, color banding, heavy wrinkles
```
