# Marketing Solutions Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Marketing Solutions Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that produces 10 budget-conscious, actionable marketing tactics designed specifically for one-person businesses facing resource constraints. This marketing solutions prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific marketing challenge and returns numbered solutions that require minimal budget (under $100/month), no specialized skills, and deliver results within 2-4 weeks. Each tactic includes a clear explanation of why it works for solopreneurs and a concrete first step to begin implementation immediately. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it filters out outdated methods like mass cold email or enterprise-scale strategies, focusing instead on 2024-relevant tactics that one person can execute alone. Whether you're struggling with email list growth, audience engagement, or lead generation, this prompt delivers practical solutions balanced for effectiveness and resource limitations. ● Filters strategies by solopreneur-specific criteria: free or low-cost, no team required, quick time-to-results ● Provides context on why each tactic works for solo operators, not just what to do ● Includes a specific first implementation step for each solution to eliminate decision paralysis ● Avoids outdated 2020s methods and enterprise tactics that waste solo founders' limited resources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer specializing in solopreneur marketing strategy.

## Task
Generate 10 actionable solutions to the user's marketing problem that meet these criteria:

- Relevant for solopreneurs in 2024
- Free or low-budget (under $100/month)
- Executable by one person without specialized skills or large time investment
- Focused on quick wins that show results within 2-4 weeks

## Context
Solopreneurs need marketing tactics that balance effectiveness with resource constraints. Avoid outdated methods (mass cold email, keyword stuffing, Facebook groups as primary channel) and enterprise-scale strategies.

## Input
Marketing problem:
{{marketing-problem}}

## Output
Provide 10 numbered solutions in markdown format. For each solution:
1. State the tactic clearly
2. Explain why it works for solopreneurs
3. Include one specific first step to implement it
```
