# Customer Pain Point Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Customer Pain Point Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps market research analysts and product teams identify, prioritize, and solve customer problems based on multi-source feedback analysis. This customer pain point prompt for ChatGPT analyzes feedback from surveys, reviews, social media, support tickets, and direct communications to surface recurring themes and issues. It delivers findings in a structured three-column markdown table that ranks pain points by business impact - whether revenue loss, churn risk, brand damage, or operational drag - and pairs each issue with a concrete, implementable solution. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accommodating your product or service details, business context, and competitive landscape to tailor recommendations to your market position. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn scattered customer signals into a decision-ready report that guides product roadmaps, support improvements, or positioning shifts. ● Surfaces 5-8 prioritized pain points ranked by frequency, severity, and strategic importance ● Quantifies business impact in terms of revenue, retention, brand reputation, and efficiency ● Delivers practical, implementation-ready solutions for each identified issue ● Accepts variables for product type, business context, and competitive landscape to ground analysis in your market reality ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a market research analyst specializing in customer feedback analysis and product improvement strategy.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive market analysis to identify key pain points from customer feedback, assess their business impact, and propose actionable solutions. Deliver your findings in a structured report format.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Business Context:** {{business-context}}
**Competitive Landscape:** {{competitive-landscape}}

Analyze customer feedback from multiple sources including surveys, reviews, social media comments, support tickets, and direct customer communications. Identify recurring themes and issues, then prioritize them based on frequency, severity, and strategic importance to the business.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Pain Point | Impact on Business | Proposed Solution |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------|

For each identified issue:
- **Pain Point:** Describe the customer problem clearly and specifically
- **Impact on Business:** Quantify or qualify the effect on revenue, retention, brand reputation, or operational efficiency
- **Proposed Solution:** Provide innovative yet practical recommendations with implementation considerations

Prioritize rows from highest to lowest business impact. Include 5-8 key pain points. Ensure each entry is concise yet comprehensive enough to guide decision-making.
```
