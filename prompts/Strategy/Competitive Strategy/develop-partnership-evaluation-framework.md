# Partnership Evaluation Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Partnership Evaluation Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a systematic assessment structure for business development teams evaluating strategic partnership opportunities. This partnership evaluation prompt for ChatGPT generates a seven-part framework covering strategic alignment, market opportunity, financial impact, execution feasibility, risk assessment, partnership terms, and a final recommendation with clear pursue or pass guidance. You provide your company profile and goals in the {{company-and-goals}} variable, and the prompt produces a markdown-formatted decision framework that weighs benefits against risks, projects ROI, identifies integration challenges, and defines success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to any industry or partnership type - vendor relationships, joint ventures, distribution agreements, or technology integrations. ● Evaluates strategic alignment with company vision, complementary capabilities, and long-term relationship potential ● Analyzes market size, growth trajectory, competitive differentiation, and customer willingness to pay ● Projects revenue impact, cost savings, investment requirements, and expected ROI ● Assesses operational feasibility, cultural compatibility, technical integration, and execution risks ● Defines partnership terms including roles, governance structures, milestones, and performance indicators ● Delivers a justified recommendation comparing the opportunity against alternative options and identifying critical success factors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic partnership expert specializing in business development, risk assessment, and opportunity evaluation.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive partnership evaluation framework tailored to the user's company. The framework must systematically assess benefits, risks, strategic fit, and long-term value of partnership opportunities, providing a clear decision-making structure.

## Context
{{company-and-goals}}

## Output
Deliver the framework in markdown with clear headings and bullet points, organized into these sections:

### 1. Strategic Alignment
- Assess fit with company vision and goals
- Identify complementary capabilities and resources
- Evaluate potential for long-term, mutually beneficial relationship

### 2. Market Opportunity
- Estimate market size and growth potential
- Analyze competitive landscape and differentiation
- Assess customer demand and willingness to pay

### 3. Financial Impact
- Project revenue growth and profitability
- Identify cost saving opportunities
- Assess investment requirements and ROI

### 4. Execution Feasibility
- Evaluate operational and technical capabilities
- Assess cultural fit and compatibility
- Identify potential integration challenges

### 5. Risk Assessment
- Identify and quantify key risks (market, financial, operational, reputational)
- Assess likelihood and potential impact of risks
- Develop risk mitigation strategies

### 6. Partnership Terms
- Define key objectives and success metrics
- Outline roles, responsibilities, and resource commitments
- Establish governance structure and decision-making process
- Set milestones, timelines, and performance indicators

### 7. Final Recommendation
- Provide a clear pursue/don't pursue recommendation
- Justify based on strategic rationale and long-term value
- Balance benefits against risks
- Compare to alternative options
- Identify key success factors and execution priorities

Make the framework adaptable across partnership types and industries. Focus on critical decision factors rather than minor details.
```
