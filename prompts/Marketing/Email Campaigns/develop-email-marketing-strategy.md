# Email Marketing Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds complete email marketing plans for businesses, marketers, and growth teams looking to engage subscribers and drive conversions. This email marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks through target audience analysis, segmentation approaches, content frameworks using dependency grammar principles for clear hierarchical messaging, subject line formulas with examples, call-to-action design recommendations, email cadence planning, personalization tactics, and automation workflows including welcome series, behavioral triggers, and re-engagement campaigns. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting your business context, unique selling proposition, and current list size to produce a structured strategy document with clear headings and actionable recommendations for each component. Marketing teams use it to replace generic templates with strategies specific to their audience and business model, while solo founders rely on it to establish professional email programs without hiring agencies. ● Analyzes target audiences and outputs segmentation criteria based on subscriber behavior and demographics ● Provides subject line formulas, content frameworks, and CTA placement guidance grounded in dependency grammar for reader-focused messaging ● Designs automation workflows for welcome sequences, behavioral triggers, and re-engagement flows specific to your list size ● Defines KPIs and email cadence recommendations aligned with your unique selling proposition and conversion goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive email marketing strategy that engages subscribers and drives conversions. The strategy must include:

- Target audience analysis and segmentation approach
- Email content framework using dependency grammar principles (structured, hierarchical messaging that guides reader attention)
- Subject line formulas and examples
- Call-to-action design and placement recommendations
- Email cadence and scheduling plan
- Personalization tactics based on subscriber data
- Automation workflow suggestions (welcome series, behavioral triggers, re-engagement)
- Key performance indicators to track

## Context
**Business overview:**
{{business-context}}

**Unique selling proposition:**
{{usp}}

**Current email list size:**
{{list-size}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy in a structured document with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for each section. Make all recommendations specific and actionable.
```
