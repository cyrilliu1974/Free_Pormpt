# Sustainability Strategy Builder for Organizations

## 簡介

The Sustainability Strategy Builder for Organizations is a free AI prompt that creates actionable environmental sustainability strategies tailored to your organization's profile and industry context. This sustainability strategy prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured plan covering resource efficiency, circular economy principles, renewable energy adoption, sustainable supply chains, employee engagement programs, and strategic partnerships. You provide your organization profile, and the prompt generates a complete strategy document: a vision statement, five key sustainability levers with specific initiatives under each, enablers to support execution, measurable metrics with targets, and a three-phase implementation roadmap spanning 0-36 months. The output is designed for immediate use by sustainability teams, operations leaders, and executives developing corporate environmental programs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing grounded, industry-relevant recommendations rather than generic advice. Reach for this prompt when building your first sustainability framework, refreshing an existing program, preparing board-level environmental commitments, or responding to stakeholder pressure for measurable climate action. ● Outputs five sustainability levers, each with three concrete initiatives tied to real-world practices ● Includes quantifiable metrics and targets so you can track progress and report impact ● Provides a phased 36-month roadmap that sequences quick wins and long-term transformations ● Identifies key enablers such as governance structures, technology platforms, and stakeholder engagement mechanisms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced sustainability strategist with deep expertise in developing and implementing environmental sustainability practices across industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive sustainability strategy that drives measurable environmental impact while supporting business objectives.

## Context
Organization profile: {{organization-profile}}

Consider levers including resource efficiency, circular economy principles, renewable energy, sustainable supply chains, employee engagement, and strategic partnerships. Provide specific, actionable recommendations grounded in real-world best practices. Avoid vague or generic advice—each initiative must be clear and implementable.
```
