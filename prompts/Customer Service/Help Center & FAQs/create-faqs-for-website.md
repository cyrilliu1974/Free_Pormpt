# FAQ Page Generator for Business Websites

## 簡介

The FAQ Page Generator for Business Websites is a free AI prompt that creates organized, customer-focused FAQ sections for any business or service site. This FAQ prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - audience, brand voice, products, and common pain points - and produces 8-12 frequently asked questions grouped into 3-5 logical categories. Each question is phrased the way real customers ask it, paired with a 2-4 sentence answer written in your brand voice, and includes internal link suggestions where additional detail would help. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and orders questions from most common to more specific so visitors find answers fast. Use it when launching a new site, redesigning a help center, or reducing repetitive support tickets. ● Produces 8-12 categorized questions with concise, actionable answers tailored to your brand voice ● Groups related questions into 3-5 logical sections for easy navigation and scanning ● Suggests internal links and next steps so users can dive deeper without contacting support ● Ends with a clear path for customers whose questions aren't covered ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in FAQ page design and information architecture.

## Task
Create a comprehensive FAQ page that addresses common customer questions, reduces support inquiries, and improves user experience.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include:
- Website topic and core offerings
- Target audience demographics and expertise level
- Brand voice (formal, casual, technical, friendly, etc.)
- Key products or services
- Main customer pain points and recurring issues

## Process
1. Identify 8-12 frequently asked questions based on the business context provided
2. Group related questions into 3-5 logical categories
3. Write clear, concise answers (2-4 sentences each) that resolve the question completely
4. Suggest relevant internal links where additional detail would help
5. Order questions from most common to more specific within each category

## Output
Structure the FAQ as follows:

**[Category Name]**

### Question phrased exactly as customers ask it?
Answer in accessible language matching the brand voice. Include actionable next steps or links where helpful.

### Next question in this category?
Answer text.

*Repeat for all categories and questions*

End with a brief note on how users can get additional help if their question isn't covered.
```
