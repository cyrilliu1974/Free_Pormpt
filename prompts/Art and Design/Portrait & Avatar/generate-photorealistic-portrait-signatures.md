# Photorealistic Portrait With Signature Overlay

## 簡介

The Photorealistic Portrait With Signature Overlay is a free AI prompt that generates elegant 9:16 portrait photography with customizable subjects, settings, and signature text overlays for creators and brands. This photorealistic portrait prompt for ChatGPT image models, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a fashion-forward composition: the subject leans back against a customizable setting with their left arm raised and hand touching the top of their head, gazing thoughtfully upward. Soft, refined lighting creates a polished editorial feel, while the signature text appears in elegant handwritten style in the bottom corner. Use it for social media profile images, creator branding, lifestyle marketing visuals, or website hero sections that need a professional, magazine-quality aesthetic without a studio shoot. Designers, content creators, influencers, and marketers reach for this prompt when they need a signature portrait that conveys thoughtfulness and style. ● Produces a 9:16 portrait with a distinct pose (subject leaning back, arm raised, gazing upward) that conveys elegance and introspection. ● Soft lighting and refined atmosphere suitable for fashion, lifestyle, or personal branding imagery. ● Customizable subject description, backdrop setting, and handwritten signature text overlay for authentic creator identity. ● Includes a negative prompt to avoid common AI artifacts like distorted hands, blurry details, messy text, or unwanted watermarks. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic portrait in 9:16 aspect ratio.

{{subject-description}} leaning back against {{setting}}. Left arm raised with hand lightly touching the top of their head, gazing thoughtfully upwards to the side.

Soft, elegant lighting creates a refined atmosphere.

Text overlay: signature reading "{{signature-text}}" in bottom corner, elegant handwritten style.

Negative prompt: cartoon, illustration, painting, drawing, blurry, low quality, distorted hands, missing fingers, extra limbs, cross-eyed, bad anatomy, messy text, typo, spelling mistake, watermark, logo
```
