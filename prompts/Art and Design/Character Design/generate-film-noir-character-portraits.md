# Film Noir Character Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Film Noir Character Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic femme fatale portraits with authentic 1940s Hollywood atmosphere and photorealistic detail for visual artists and designers. This film noir portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces high-contrast images featuring a femme fatale character with sharp facial features, pale skin, realistic pores and freckles, and dramatic over-the-shoulder poses on rain-soaked cobblestone streets. The prompt renders 8K photorealistic quality with film grain texture, bokeh car lights, steam rising from vents, and low-key rim lighting that outlines the character's silhouette. Use it for concept art, mood boards, editorial illustration, social media content, or any project requiring moody vintage Hollywood aesthetics. Reach for this prompt when you need atmospheric character portraits that capture the mystery and visual drama of classic detective films without organizing a location shoot or hiring a photographer. ● Renders realistic skin detail including visible pores, freckles, faint veins, and imperfections for true photorealism. ● Applies high-contrast bluish color grading, film grain, and wet reflective street surfaces for authentic noir atmosphere. ● Includes customizable variables for attire-and-styling and scene-setting so you can adapt the character and environment. ● Simulates shallow depth-of-field bokeh effects and rim lighting as if shot on a 50mm f/1.8 lens. ## Prompt
