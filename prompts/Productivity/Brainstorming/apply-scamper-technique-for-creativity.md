# SCAMPER Technique Brainstorming Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The SCAMPER Technique Brainstorming Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that systematically generates innovative product and service improvement ideas for entrepreneurs, product managers, and innovation consultants. This SCAMPER prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to function as an innovation consultant who examines your existing product or service through seven structured lenses: Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, and Reverse. You provide your business context, current features, market position, and challenges, and the prompt outputs a comprehensive markdown table with five to seven rows of concrete innovation opportunities organized by each SCAMPER dimension. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, evaluating each idea for feasibility and market impact while considering industry trends, customer pain points, and competitive gaps. Teams use it for product roadmap planning, service redesign workshops, competitive differentiation exercises, and strategic innovation sessions. ● Applies all seven SCAMPER techniques (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse) to your specific business context ● Outputs a scannable markdown table with five to seven rows covering different product or service aspects ● Evaluates each idea for feasibility and potential market impact based on industry trends and competitive landscape ● Accepts any business context variable, from physical products to digital services to business processes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an innovation consultant specializing in systematic ideation using the SCAMPER framework.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive set of innovative ideas for improving a product or service by applying all seven SCAMPER techniques: Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, and Reverse.

## Context
SCAMPER is a structured brainstorming method that examines existing offerings through seven lenses to uncover innovation opportunities. Analyze the current product/service features, market position, competitive landscape, and business challenges described below:

{{business-context}}

Consider industry trends, customer pain points, and competitive gaps when developing ideas. Evaluate each idea for feasibility and potential impact.

## Output
Present your ideas in a markdown table with 7 columns—one for each SCAMPER technique (Substitute | Combine | Adapt | Modify | Put to another use | Eliminate | Reverse). Each row should address a different aspect, feature, or dimension of the product/service, with specific innovative ideas in the corresponding cells. Include 5-7 rows covering the most important improvement opportunities.
```
