# Online Networking Strategy Prompt for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Online Networking Strategy Prompt for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates customized content strategies for building professional relationships across digital platforms. This online networking prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and platform preferences, then produces a complete strategy organized by platform - covering post types (thought leadership, case studies, quick tips), comment tactics for adding value in others' threads, direct message templates with opening lines and follow-up frameworks, and engagement cadence recommendations. Every piece of content is structured using dependency grammar principles, leading with the core claim and branching supporting details into logical, scannable hierarchies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Solopreneurs, consultants, and freelancers reach for this prompt when they need a structured approach to expand their professional network without guessing at platform-specific best practices. ● Produces post types, comment tactics, and direct message templates customized to LinkedIn, Twitter/X, industry forums, or other platforms you specify ● Applies dependency grammar to ensure every message leads with its core value and branches logically ● Includes engagement cadence and timing recommendations so you know when and how often to show up ● Adapts to your unique business niche, target audience, and networking goals - whether lead generation, partnerships, thought leadership, or community growth ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert online networking strategist specializing in solopreneur growth and professional relationship building.

## Task
Create a comprehensive networking content strategy that includes platform-specific approaches, content creation guidelines, and engagement tactics. Structure all copy using dependency grammar principles to maximize clarity and impact.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-context}}
(Include: business niche, target audience, unique value proposition)

**Platforms & Goals:**
{{networking-parameters}}
(Include: preferred platforms—e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter/X, industry forums—and primary networking goals such as lead generation, partnership building, thought leadership, or community growth)

## Output
Deliver a bullet-point strategy organized by:
- **Platform** (one section per platform listed)
  - **Post types** (what to publish: thought leadership, case studies, quick tips, etc.)
  - **Comment tactics** (how to add value in others' threads)
  - **Direct message templates** (opening lines and follow-up frameworks)
  - **Engagement cadence** (frequency and timing recommendations)

Ensure each piece of content applies dependency grammar: lead with the core claim, then branch supporting details in logical, scannable hierarchies.
```
