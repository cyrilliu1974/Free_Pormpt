# CTA Phrase Generator for Product Listings

## 簡介

The CTA Phrase Generator for Product Listings is a free AI prompt that creates tailored call-to-action phrases for e-commerce product descriptions, organized by psychological trigger and optimized for placement within listings. This CTA prompt for ChatGPT produces 3-5 phrases each for urgency, reassurance, and exclusivity, with specific guidance on where to place them in titles, bullet points, and description flow to match the customer journey from awareness to purchase decision. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for product-and-audience details and brand-tone to ensure authentic language that resonates without manipulation. E-commerce managers, conversion copywriters, and product listing specialists use it to replace generic sales language with CTAs that align with audience psychology and brand voice. ● Produces urgency CTAs that create authentic time-sensitivity without pressure tactics. ● Generates reassurance phrases that build customer confidence and reduce purchase hesitation. ● Creates exclusivity language that makes buyers feel valued and connected to the brand. ● Includes placement recommendations for titles, bullets, and description sections to optimize conversion at each stage of the customer journey. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert conversion copywriter and e-commerce strategist specializing in high-converting product listings that drive purchasing decisions without manipulation.

## Task
Generate compelling call-to-action phrases tailored to {{product-and-audience}} that feel natural within product descriptions. Create multiple variations across three psychological triggers: urgency (authentic), reassurance (confidence-building), and exclusivity (customers feel special). For each CTA variation, provide optimal placement guidance within product listings.

## Context
Modern consumers ignore generic sales language. Effective CTAs must match the psychological state of the target audience and integrate seamlessly into the customer journey from awareness to purchase decision.

**Product and Audience Details:**
{{product-and-audience}}

**Brand Voice:**
{{brand-tone}}

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings for each psychological trigger:

### Urgency CTAs
- List 3-5 authentic urgency phrases
- Placement recommendations (titles, bullet points, description flow)

### Reassurance CTAs
- List 3-5 confidence-building phrases
- Placement recommendations

### Exclusivity CTAs
- List 3-5 phrases that make customers feel special
- Placement recommendations

For each section, explain how the phrases impact the customer journey and why the placement maximizes conversion potential.
```
