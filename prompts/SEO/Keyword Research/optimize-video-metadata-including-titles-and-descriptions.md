# Video Metadata Optimizer for SEO and Click-Through

## 簡介

The Video Metadata Optimizer for SEO and Click-Through is a free AI prompt that creates complete video metadata - titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnail concepts - designed to maximize search discoverability and viewer engagement for content creators and marketers. This video SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your video topic, target keywords, target audience, and channel niche to produce strategically crafted metadata that balances search engine optimization with human appeal. It generates 60-character titles with primary keywords naturally integrated, writes descriptions with optimized first-150-character snippets for search results, delivers 15-20 prioritized tags from broad to specific, and provides actionable thumbnail design guidance complete with visual concepts and text overlay recommendations. Whether you're launching a YouTube series, optimizing existing content, or scaling a content marketing operation, this prompt structures every metadata element to drive both rankings and clicks. This prompt is for YouTube creators, video marketers, content strategists, and SEO specialists who need metadata that performs across both algorithm and audience. ● Produces titles under 60 characters that incorporate primary keywords while staying click-worthy and accurate ● Writes full video descriptions with search-snippet-optimized opening lines and strategic keyword placement throughout ● Delivers 15-20 prioritized tags ordered from broad reach to niche specificity, with rationale for each selection ● Provides thumbnail design concepts including visual elements, text overlays, and click-appeal strategies that reinforce the title ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video SEO specialist optimizing content for search discoverability and user engagement.

## Task
Create complete video metadata—title, description, tags, and thumbnail guidance—that maximizes SEO performance while appealing to viewers.

## Context
**Video topic:** {{video-topic}}
**Target keywords:** {{target-keywords}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Channel niche:** {{channel-niche}}

Analyze how the target keywords relate to the video content and audience search intent. Incorporate keywords naturally into metadata without keyword stuffing. Balance SEO optimization with human readability and accurate content representation.

## Output
Provide structured recommendations under these headings:

**Title**
- Proposed title (60 characters or fewer, primary keyword included)
- Optimization rationale

**Description**
- Full description (first 150 characters optimized for search snippets)
- Keyword placement strategy
- Timestamps, links, or CTAs to include

**Tags**
- 15-20 relevant tags in priority order (broad to specific)
- Tag selection rationale

**Thumbnail**
- Visual concept and design elements
- Text overlay recommendation (if any)
- How it reinforces the title and attracts clicks
```
