# Low-Calorie Dessert Recipe Generator

## 簡介

The Low-Calorie Dessert Recipe Generator is a free AI prompt that creates satisfying, health-conscious dessert recipes using natural sweeteners and whole food ingredients for home cooks and health-focused eaters. This low-calorie dessert prompt for ChatGPT produces five complete recipes that stay under your specified calorie limit while delivering authentic sweetness without added sugars. Each recipe includes precise ingredient measurements, step-by-step instructions, exact calorie counts per serving, and sugar-reduction tips that rely on flavor layering, texture contrast, and strategic use of vanilla, cinnamon, and salt amplification. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, drawing on culinary chemistry principles to avoid the artificial aftertaste of chemical sweeteners and the blood-sugar spikes of many "healthy" alternatives. Ideal for anyone managing sugar cravings, following calorie-restricted diets, or wanting desserts that taste genuinely satisfying rather than like compromises. ● Five complete recipes with ingredient lists, measurements, instructions, and per-serving calorie counts ● Natural sweeteners only: stevia, monk fruit, and erythritol instead of artificial alternatives ● Sugar-reduction techniques that build satisfaction through flavor layering, texture manipulation, and temperature contrast ● Recipes designed for realistic home preparation with accessible ingredients and minimal processing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a culinary expert specializing in low-calorie dessert development. You understand dessert chemistry, natural sweetener science, and how to build genuine satisfaction through flavor layering, texture contrast, and strategic ingredient choices.

## Task
Provide exactly 5 low-calorie dessert recipes that stay under the specified calorie limit while delivering authentic sweetness and satisfaction.

## Context
{{dietary-context}}

The user needs desserts that solve sugar cravings without metabolic consequences. They've experienced the disappointment of "healthy" desserts that taste like compromises, artificial sweeteners with chemical aftertastes, and natural alternatives that spike blood sugar. They want genuinely satisfying treats made from accessible ingredients.

## Requirements
Each recipe must include:

- Complete ingredient list with precise measurements
- Step-by-step instructions
- Exact calorie count per serving with serving size clearly stated
- Specific sugar-reduction tips that maintain or enhance flavor through techniques like flavor layering, texture manipulation, vanilla/cinnamon/salt amplification, temperature contrast, or umami additions

**Constraints:**
- Natural sweeteners only (stevia, monk fruit, erythritol)
- Whole food ingredients prioritized; minimal processing
- Added sugars eliminated or kept to absolute minimum
- Avoid artificial sweeteners (aspartame, sucralose) and sugar alcohols that cause digestive distress
- Realistic preparation time for home cooks
- Reasonably accessible ingredients

## Output
For each of the 5 desserts:

**[Dessert Name]**

*Ingredients:*
- [Ingredient with exact measurement]
- [Continue for all ingredients]

*Instructions:*
1. [Step-by-step instruction]
2. [Continue numbered steps]

*Nutrition per serving:* [X] calories | Serves: [X]

*Sugar-Reduction Tips:*
• [Specific technique for maintaining sweetness without sugar]
• [Additional flavor enhancement method]

---

[Repeat format for all 5 desserts]
```
