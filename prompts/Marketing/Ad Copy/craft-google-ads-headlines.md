# Google Ads Headline & Description Generator

## 簡介

The Google Ads Headline & Description Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compliant, high-CTR ad copy for PPC campaigns. This Google Ads prompt for ChatGPT produces 15 distinct headlines and 10 descriptions that meet Google's strict character limits - 30 characters per headline, 90 per description. It ensures headline variety so any random combination of three avoids repetition, incorporates your target keywords naturally, and includes pattern-interrupt formats that boost engagement. Descriptions address customer objections and clarify value propositions. The prompt even generates blank templates like "Trusted by XXX customers" you can customize with real metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh ad variations fast, want to test multiple angles without hiring a copywriter, or need compliant copy that passes Google's review process on the first try. ● Produces 15 headlines under 30 characters and 10 descriptions under 90 characters that meet Google's requirements ● Ensures headline diversity so any combination of three avoids sounding repetitive ● Weaves your keyword topics naturally into attention-grabbing formats and pattern interrupts ● Includes customizable templates for social proof and data-driven claims you can populate with your metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a PPC specialist writing Google Ads copy optimized for click-through rate and compliance.

## Task
Generate 15 unique headlines and 10 descriptions for a Google Ads campaign.

**Strict character limits:**
- Headlines: max 30 characters each
- Descriptions: max 90 characters each

**Requirements:**
- Headlines must vary in angle and hook so any random combination of 3 avoids repetition
- Use attention-grabbing formats and pattern interrupts
- Incorporate relevant keywords naturally to boost CTR
- Descriptions should address objections and clarify value
- Include 1–2 blank templates (e.g., "Trusted by XXX customers") the advertiser can populate with real data

## Context
{{business-and-product}}

**Keyword topics to emphasize:**  
{{keyword-topics}}

## Output
Return two markdown bullet lists:

**Headlines (15)**
- [headline 1]
- [headline 2]
…

**Descriptions (10)**
- [description 1]
- [description 2]
…
```
