# Circular Product Dial Composition Generator

## 簡介

The Circular Product Dial Composition Generator is a free AI prompt that creates overhead orthographic flat-lay images compositing your real product photos into evenly distributed circular slots around a premium metallic dial. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces ultra-high-resolution 1:1 compositions where each uploaded product image is masked to fit perfectly inside its own shallow recessed slot, preserving original colors, labels, and details without distortion. You control the background surface (marble, wood, concrete), the dial material (brushed aluminum, brass, titanium), and the number of slots (4, 6, 8, or more), while the prompt ensures soft diffused studio lighting, realistic contact shadows, and a clean empty center. Use it for hero images, catalog covers, portfolio presentations, or social media grids that showcase multiple SKUs in a unified, high-end layout. Reach for this prompt when you need a polished, repeatable format that elevates individual product shots into a cohesive visual system without the cost of a physical studio setup. ● Masks each uploaded product photo to fit circular slot boundaries with no overflow or distortion ● Renders realistic recessed cavities with soft shadows and contact lighting that match your original images ● Customizable dial material, background surface, and slot count for brand-consistent variations ● Produces clean 1:1 ultra-high-resolution outputs with no text, watermarks, or generated placeholder objects ## Prompt

```
Perfect 90-degree overhead orthographic flat lay of a premium circular metallic dial resting on {{background-surface}}. The dial is crafted from {{dial-material}} with a matte metallic finish and subtle reflections.

Around the outer ring, {{slot-count}} perfect circular recessed slots are evenly distributed. Each slot is a shallow realistic cavity. Inside each slot, place one of the uploaded product images, centered and scaled proportionally to fit the circular boundary without distortion. Mask each product to the circular slot edge—no overflow beyond the circle. Preserve every uploaded product exactly as photographed: original shape, colors, labels, and details unchanged. Do NOT generate any new products or placeholder objects.

Inside each slot beneath the product, render a neutral soft shadow for depth. The center of the dial remains empty and clean with no text or graphics.

Soft diffused studio lighting creates consistent, realistic contact shadows under each product, matching the lighting of the uploaded images. Ultra-photorealistic render, 1:1 aspect ratio, very high resolution. Absolutely no text, logos, watermarks, extra objects, illustrations, or cartoon style anywhere in the composition.
```
