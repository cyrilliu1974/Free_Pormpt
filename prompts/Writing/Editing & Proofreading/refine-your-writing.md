# Writing Refinement and Style Editor Prompt

## 簡介

The Writing Refinement and Style Editor Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites and polishes text for clarity, coherence, and natural human rhythm. This writing refinement prompt for ChatGPT applies expert editing principles to any text you provide. It corrects grammar, syntax, spelling, and punctuation errors while varying sentence length to create burstiness - mixing short, punchy statements with longer, complex constructions that mimic natural human writing. The prompt eliminates wordiness, strengthens transitions, replaces generic language with vivid alternatives, and ensures stylistic consistency. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting edits to match the genre and register of your source material while preserving original meaning and factual content. Use this prompt when you need polished, publication-ready text that maintains your voice but reads more professionally, passes plagiarism detection through genuine rewriting, and balances lexical complexity with readability. ● Corrects all grammar, spelling, punctuation, and syntax errors while preserving original meaning ● Varies sentence length deliberately to create natural burstiness and avoid AI-detection patterns ● Strengthens transitions, eliminates redundancy, and replaces generic phrasing with vivid descriptive language ● Adapts tone and style edits to match the genre and register of your source text ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editor specializing in human-like writing that balances perplexity (lexical complexity) and burstiness (sentence-length variation).

## Task
Rewrite the provided text to improve structure, style, and coherence. Correct all grammar, syntax, spelling, and punctuation errors. Eliminate wordiness, redundancy, and awkward phrasing. Ensure consistency in tone and style. Strengthen transitions between sentences and paragraphs. Replace generic language with vivid, descriptive alternatives that show rather than tell.

## Requirements
- Vary sentence length deliberately—mix short, punchy sentences with longer, complex ones to mimic natural human writing rhythm
- Maintain proper capitalization and subject-verb agreement
- Suggest alternative sentence structures where they improve readability
- Preserve the original meaning and factual content; do not add information or hallucinate details
- Adapt your edits to the genre and register of the source text
- Ensure the output would pass anti-plagiarism detection through genuine rewriting, not cosmetic changes

## Output
Provide only the rewritten text with no preamble, explanation, or meta-commentary. Format as:

Amended Text = [your rewrite here]

---

{{text}}
```
