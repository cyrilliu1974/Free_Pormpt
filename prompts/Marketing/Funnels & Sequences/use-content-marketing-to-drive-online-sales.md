# Content Marketing Strategy for Online Sales

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Strategy for Online Sales is a free AI prompt that builds conversion-focused content plans for businesses and marketers looking to increase revenue through strategic content. This content marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a detailed roadmap covering audience analysis, content calendars, blog outlines, platform-specific social media tactics, email nurture sequences, lead magnet concepts, KPI frameworks, and A/B testing plans. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming three simple inputs (your product and audience, your goals and challenges, and your brand voice) into an actionable quarterly content plan that spans multiple channels and content formats. This prompt is designed for content marketers, e-commerce managers, digital strategists, and business owners who need a structured approach to content that converts browsers into buyers. ● Provides audience positioning analysis that identifies key selling points, pain points, and messaging angles specific to your offering ● Delivers a quarterly content calendar with topic recommendations, platform assignments, and publishing frequency across all channels ● Includes 3-5 SEO-optimized blog post outlines complete with target keywords, structure guidance, and readability optimization ● Generates platform-specific social media strategies for Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, and other channels with post types and engagement tactics ● Designs complete email campaign sequences for nurture and conversion, including subject lines, calls-to-action, and send cadence ● Proposes three high-value lead magnet concepts aligned with audience needs to grow your subscriber base ● Defines measurable KPIs for each content type with clear methods to track performance and calculate ROI ● Recommends A/B testing strategies for headlines, CTAs, formats, and timing to continuously improve content effectiveness ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketing strategist specializing in conversion-driven campaigns that drive online sales.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content marketing strategy that attracts and converts the target audience. Deliver a detailed, actionable plan covering multiple content types and platforms.

## Context
**Product/Service & Audience:**
{{product-and-audience}}

**Marketing Goals & Current Challenges:**
{{goals-and-challenges}}

**Brand Voice:**
{{brand-voice}}

## Strategy Components

Your plan must address:

1. **Audience & Positioning Analysis** – Identify key selling points, pain points, and messaging angles based on the product and audience provided.

2. **Content Calendar** – Outline topics, platforms, publishing frequency, and content themes for the next quarter.

3. **Blog Strategy** – Provide 3–5 blog post outlines optimized for readability and SEO, including target keywords and structure.

4. **Social Media Strategy** – Tailor content approaches for each relevant platform (Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.), including post types, frequency, and engagement tactics.

5. **Email Campaign Sequences** – Design nurture and conversion sequences with subject lines, CTAs, and send cadence.

6. **Lead Magnets** – Propose 3 high-value lead magnet concepts that align with audience needs and encourage sign-ups.

7. **KPIs & Tracking** – Define measurable KPIs for each content type and methods to track performance and ROI.

8. **A/B Testing Plan** – Recommend testing strategies for headlines, CTAs, formats, and timing to continuously improve effectiveness.

## Output
Organize your response with clear subheadings for each content type: **Blog Posts**, **Social Media**, **Email Campaigns**, and **Lead Magnets**. Within each section, provide step-by-step actionable recommendations.
```
