# YouTube Channel Income Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The YouTube Channel Income Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers a complete roadmap for solopreneurs building profitable YouTube channels. By providing your channel focus, available resources, and monetization goals, this YouTube income strategy prompt for ChatGPT generates an actionable plan covering niche selection, branding, content creation, video SEO, audience growth, and diversified revenue streams. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured strategy with benchmarks and recommended tools at each stage. Ideal for content creators moving from concept to consistent passive income, this prompt addresses real challenges like niche validation, upload consistency, community building, and scaling operations. ● Validates niche demand and competitive positioning before channel launch ● Provides a production workflow and editorial calendar framework tailored to your resources ● Maps multiple monetization pathways including AdSense, sponsorships, affiliates, and digital products ● Delivers video SEO tactics for titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails to boost discoverability ● Includes analytics benchmarks and A/B testing protocols for continuous improvement ● Outlines delegation strategies and content repurposing plans for sustainable scaling ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert YouTube content strategist specializing in passive income generation for solopreneurs.

## Task
Outline a comprehensive, step-by-step strategy for launching and growing a profitable YouTube channel. Cover niche positioning, channel setup, content creation, SEO optimization, audience growth, and multiple monetization pathways.

## Context
**Channel Focus:**
{{channel-focus}}

**Available Resources:**
{{resources}}

**Monetization Goals:**
{{monetization-goals}}

## Strategy Components
Address each of these areas in your roadmap:

1. **Niche Selection & Market Research** – Validate demand, assess competition, identify content gaps
2. **Channel Branding & Setup** – Name, visuals, about section, channel organization
3. **Content Planning & Creation** – Editorial calendar, production workflow, format guidelines
4. **Video SEO Optimization** – Titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, closed captions
5. **Audience Engagement & Growth** – Community interaction, collaboration, cross-promotion, upload consistency
6. **Monetization Methods** – AdSense, sponsorships, affiliate marketing, digital products, memberships
7. **Analytics & Performance Tracking** – Key metrics, A/B testing, iteration cycles
8. **Scaling & Diversification** – Delegation, content repurposing, additional revenue streams

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with clear subheadings for each main section. Include actionable steps, benchmarks, and tools where relevant.
```
