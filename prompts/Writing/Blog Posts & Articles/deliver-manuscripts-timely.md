# Manuscript Development Workflow Prompt

## 簡介

The Manuscript Development Workflow Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides content creators through a structured nine-stage process for producing polished, client-specific manuscripts. This manuscript development prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every phase of professional content creation - from analyzing the client brief and conducting authoritative research, to outlining, drafting, editing, formatting, and final delivery. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to any manuscript type, target audience, and set of client requirements. Writers, freelancers, and content agencies use it to deliver blog posts, articles, white papers, reports, and long-form editorial work that meets professional standards and deadlines. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable, quality-controlled workflow that documents every development stage and ensures no requirement is overlooked. ● Nine-stage workflow covering brief analysis, research, outlining, drafting, editing, proofreading, formatting, supplementary materials, and final review ● Produces step-by-step process documentation showing how each stage was executed ● Tailors tone, vocabulary, and structure to the specified target audience and manuscript type ● Ensures originality, accuracy, and compliance with all client guidelines before handoff ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content creator producing well-researched, original manuscripts tailored to client specifications and target audiences.

## Task
Create a complete manuscript from brief to delivery, following professional content development standards.

## Context
**Manuscript type:** {{manuscript-type}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Client requirements:** {{client-requirements}}

## Process

1. **Brief Analysis**
   - Extract key requirements, scope, and success criteria
   - Identify constraints and expectations

2. **Research**
   - Gather information from authoritative, relevant sources
   - Verify facts and collect supporting evidence

3. **Outline Development**
   - Structure content logically for the manuscript type
   - Design flow that engages the target audience

4. **Drafting**
   - Write original content adhering to all guidelines
   - Maintain appropriate tone and vocabulary for the audience

5. **Editing & Proofreading**
   - Review for clarity, coherence, accuracy, and flow
   - Eliminate errors and strengthen weak passages

6. **Formatting**
   - Apply relevant style guide or industry standards
   - Ensure consistent presentation throughout

7. **Supplementary Materials**
   - Compile references, appendices, or other required elements

8. **Final Review**
   - Verify all client requirements are satisfied
   - Perform quality assurance check

9. **Delivery Package**
   - Organize manuscript and supporting documents for handoff

## Output
Provide the completed manuscript with step-by-step documentation of your development process, showing how each stage was executed.
```
