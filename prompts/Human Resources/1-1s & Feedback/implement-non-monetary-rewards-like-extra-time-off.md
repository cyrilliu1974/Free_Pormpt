# Non-Monetary Rewards Program Builder for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Non-Monetary Rewards Program Builder for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive incentive structures for HR managers looking to boost morale and productivity without cash bonuses. This non-monetary rewards prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your sales team context and generating a detailed table of reward options spanning recognition, professional development, flexibility, experiential perks, and peer engagement opportunities. Each reward includes clear eligibility criteria and implementation steps, ensuring practical rollout. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing markdown-formatted programs that address diverse motivational profiles within your team. HR leaders use it to design programs featuring extra time off, public acknowledgment, mentorship access, conference attendance, flexible scheduling, and team-building experiences that align with company culture and operational constraints. ● Creates a minimum number of distinct reward types spanning recognition, autonomy, growth, and belonging drivers ● Maps each reward to specific eligibility criteria and step-by-step implementation guidance ● Balances attainability across performance levels while maintaining motivational impact ● Includes contextual analysis explaining how the reward mix addresses different motivational profiles and supports your specific sales culture ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources manager specializing in non-monetary incentive program design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive non-monetary rewards program for a sales team that boosts morale and productivity without relying on cash bonuses or salary increases.

## Context
{{sales-team-context}}

Design rewards that are:
- Meaningful and motivating to sales professionals
- Attainable and fairly distributed across different performance levels
- Feasible to implement within operational constraints
- Aligned with company goals, values, and culture
- Sustainable for long-term positive impact on team dynamics

Consider the psychological drivers of sales motivation (recognition, autonomy, growth, belonging) and how each reward type reinforces desired behaviors and outcomes.

## Output
Present your program as a markdown table with these columns:

| Reward Type | Eligibility Criteria | Implementation Steps |

Provide at least {{minimum-reward-count}} distinct reward options that span different motivation drivers: public recognition, professional development, flexibility/autonomy, experiential rewards, and peer/leadership engagement opportunities.

After the table, include a brief paragraph explaining how this mix of rewards addresses different motivational profiles within the team and supports the specific cultural and performance context described.
```
