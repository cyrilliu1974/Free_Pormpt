# Business Partnership Identification Guide Builder

## 簡介

The Business Partnership Identification Guide Builder is a free AI prompt that generates detailed, actionable frameworks for finding and engaging strategic business partners in any industry. This business partnership prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a five-section guide covering partner identification research methods, preparation strategies, approach tactics, and negotiation frameworks. It transforms a single industry input into a customized roadmap with specific research techniques (industry directories, trade associations, network referrals), qualification criteria (complementary offerings, shared markets, aligned values), and outreach best practices. The output includes due diligence checklists, value proposition templates, and negotiation tactics grounded in real-world business practices rather than generic theory. Reach for this prompt when you need to build a partnership pipeline, train a team on strategic outreach, or document repeatable partner acquisition processes for a specific vertical. ● Creates industry-specific partner identification strategies using trade associations, databases, and network channels ● Builds customized value propositions and pitch templates aligned to prospect needs and decision-maker profiles ● Delivers negotiation frameworks with win-win opportunity identification and partnership structure blueprints ● Outputs structured guides with clear headings, bullet points, and implementation steps ready for team deployment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in strategic partnerships across industries. Provide a comprehensive, actionable guide for identifying and approaching business partners.

## Task
Create a detailed partnership guide structured around five core sections: introduction, partner identification, preparation, approach and negotiation, and conclusion. Use dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow and clarity throughout.

## Context
Target industry: {{target-industry}}

The guide must be grounded in real-world business practices, not theory. Focus on specific methods, criteria, and tactics that apply directly to the target industry context.

## Output
Deliver a well-structured document using clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points:

**1. Introduction**
- Overview of why strategic partnerships matter in {{target-industry}}
- Thesis outlining the key steps for successful partner identification and outreach

**2. Identifying Potential Partners**
- Research methods:
  - Online databases and industry directories
  - Trade associations and industry events
  - Network referrals
- Partnership fit criteria:
  - Complementary products/services
  - Shared target market
  - Aligned values and business goals
- Creating a prioritized prospect list

**3. Preparing to Approach Partners**
- Due diligence on each prospect:
  - Analyze business model and market position
  - Identify key decision-makers
  - Research partnership history and criteria
- Develop compelling value proposition:
  - Articulate partnership benefits clearly
  - Customize pitch to each prospect's needs
  - Prepare supporting data and case studies
- Choose optimal outreach method:
  - Warm introductions vs. cold outreach
  - In-person, phone, or email communication

**4. Approaching and Negotiating with Partners**
- Craft engaging initial pitch:
  - Strong opening hook
  - Succinct value communication
  - Clear call-to-action
- Navigate negotiation:
  - Active listening and concern addressing
  - Identify win-win opportunities
  - Know when to walk away
- Establish partnership framework:
  - Define contributions and benefits
  - Set success metrics
  - Outline communication and reporting protocols

**5. Conclusion**
- Recap key steps
- Emphasize adaptability and persistence
- Provide implementation call-to-action

Avoid generic advice—tailor all recommendations to {{target-industry}} realities.
```
