# E-Commerce Chatbot Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Chatbot Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that produces a complete chatbot deployment roadmap for online retail businesses looking to automate marketing and improve customer interactions. This e-commerce chatbot prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your current marketing setup, identifies where automation can replace manual workflows, and defines specific chatbot functionalities at each stage of the customer journey - from product discovery and cart reminders to post-purchase support. You provide details about your business and audience, your current state, and your goals; the prompt returns a hierarchical strategy that connects situation analysis, core functionalities, implementation sequencing, and performance metrics in a logical dependency framework. Use it when launching a new chatbot system, upgrading existing automation, or mapping out how conversational AI fits into your e-commerce tech stack. ● Performs a baseline analysis of current marketing and engagement practices to surface automation gaps ● Maps chatbot functionalities to customer journey stages - browse, cart, checkout, support, retention ● Defines integration points with email, SMS, and social channels so the chatbot works alongside existing tools ● Establishes KPIs for marketing automation efficiency and customer engagement, plus a continuous optimization framework ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert chatbot strategist specializing in e-commerce marketing automation and customer engagement.

## Task
Design a comprehensive chatbot strategy that automates marketing activities and enhances customer engagement. Analyze current practices, identify automation opportunities, define chatbot functionalities across customer touchpoints, and establish success metrics.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

{{current-state}}

{{goals}}

## Output
Deliver a hierarchical strategy where each element logically builds on and supports the others:

1. **Situation Analysis**
   - Current marketing and engagement baseline
   - Gaps and automation opportunities

2. **Chatbot Strategy Framework**
   - Core functionalities mapped to customer journey stages
   - Automation triggers and response flows
   - Integration points with existing marketing channels

3. **Implementation Roadmap**
   - Customer touchpoint deployment plan
   - Technical requirements and dependencies
   - Sequencing and prioritization

4. **Success Measurement**
   - Key performance indicators for marketing automation
   - Customer engagement metrics
   - Continuous optimization approach

Structure each section so that insights from one inform recommendations in the next, creating a cohesive, dependency-based strategy.
```
