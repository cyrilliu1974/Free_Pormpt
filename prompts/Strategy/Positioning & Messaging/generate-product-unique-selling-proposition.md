# Product Unique Selling Proposition Generator

## 簡介

The Product Unique Selling Proposition Generator is a free AI prompt that analyzes any product description and delivers a complete USP framework for marketers, founders, and product strategists. This unique selling proposition prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured analysis that begins with target market identification, maps features to benefits, isolates competitive differentiators, and crafts a concise USP statement with supporting rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a multi-section report that covers product overview, key benefits, unique differentiators, a proposed USP, areas for improvement, refinement suggestions, and final implementation recommendations. Real-world use cases include pre-launch positioning, rebranding exercises, competitive repositioning, and sales enablement documentation. Reach for this prompt when you need to articulate why customers should choose your product over alternatives, or when refining messaging before a campaign or investor pitch. ● Maps product features to customer benefits and emotional drivers, ensuring the USP resonates on multiple levels ● Identifies competitive differentiators by focusing on strengths that competitors cannot easily replicate ● Provides refinement suggestions and improvement areas, so the USP evolves as the product matures ● Delivers implementation recommendations that connect the USP to go-to-market strategies and messaging consistency ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product strategist and marketing consultant specializing in identifying and articulating unique selling propositions (USPs).

## Task
Analyze the given product to identify its key strengths, differentiators, and areas for improvement, then articulate a compelling USP that resonates with its target audience.

## Context
Focus on the most crucial aspects that set the product apart from competitors and deliver the greatest value to users. Consider both emotional and practical benefits. Ensure the USP is clear, concise, and easily understandable.

## Product
{{product-description}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Product Overview**
- Brief description of the product

**Target Market**
- Description of the target audience

**Key Features and Benefits**
- Feature 1: Description
  - Benefit 1: Explanation
- Feature 2: Description
  - Benefit 2: Explanation
- Feature 3: Description
  - Benefit 3: Explanation

**Unique Differentiators**
- List of differentiators compared to competitors

**USP**
"[Proposed USP]"

**USP Rationale**
- Explanation of why this USP effectively communicates the product's key strengths and differentiators

**Areas for Improvement**
- List of areas where the product could be enhanced

**USP Refinement Suggestions**
- Specific suggestions for refining and improving the USP

**Final Recommendations**
- Recommendations for implementing the USP and additional strategies for success
```
