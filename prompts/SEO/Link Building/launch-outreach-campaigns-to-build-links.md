# Link Building Outreach Campaign Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Link Building Outreach Campaign Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive backlink acquisition plans for digital marketers and SEO specialists. This link building prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your website, niche, and existing backlink profile to produce a detailed implementation roadmap. It outputs structured strategies across six key phases: competitive research, prospect identification with qualification criteria, outreach tactics including email templates and follow-up sequences, content collaboration approaches, relationship nurturing methods, and performance tracking frameworks. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering organized numbered lists with lettered sub-points that turn complex link-building campaigns into actionable steps. Real applications include launching guest post outreach for SaaS companies, building editorial links for e-commerce sites, and establishing partnerships for content marketing agencies. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic framework to acquire high-quality backlinks rather than ad-hoc outreach attempts. ● Produces qualification criteria to identify high-value link prospects in your niche ● Generates complete outreach sequences with email templates and value propositions tailored to your assets ● Designs tracking spreadsheet structures with column headers, metrics, and status workflows ● Includes relationship nurturing strategies for turning one-time placements into long-term partnerships ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in link-building campaigns.

## Task
Create a comprehensive backlink acquisition strategy for the website and niche described below. Deliver a step-by-step implementation plan covering prospect identification, outreach tactics, and performance tracking.

## Context
{{website-and-niche}}

{{backlink-profile-and-assets}}

## Output
Provide your strategy as a numbered list with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) for detailed steps. Structure should include:

1. Initial research and competitive analysis
2. Prospect identification and qualification criteria
3. Outreach tactics (email templates, value propositions, follow-up sequences)
4. Content collaboration and placement strategies
5. Relationship nurturing and long-term partnerships
6. Performance tracking and optimization

Conclude with a tracking spreadsheet design section that specifies:
- Recommended column headers
- Metrics to monitor (response rate, placement rate, domain authority, etc.)
- Status tracking workflow
- Follow-up scheduling fields
```
