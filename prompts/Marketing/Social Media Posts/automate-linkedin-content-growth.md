# LinkedIn Content Strategy Automation Prompt

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Content Strategy Automation Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds scalable, data-driven LinkedIn content systems for businesses and personal brands. This LinkedIn content strategy prompt for ChatGPT works by applying dependency grammar principles to structure posts for clarity and engagement, then delivering a three-part plan: a content calendar with posting frequency and theme rotation, post templates with hook formulas and call-to-action patterns, and engagement tactics covering comments, DMs, and network expansion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to your business context, target audience, and brand voice. Use it when you need a complete LinkedIn automation blueprint rather than one-off post ideas. ● Outputs posting schedules, content themes, and campaign milestones aligned with business goals ● Provides hook formulas, body frameworks, and CTA patterns based on dependency grammar for maximum clarity ● Includes comment strategies, DM workflows, and relationship nurturing tactics designed for automation ● Delivers metrics and optimization triggers to track performance and adapt to LinkedIn algorithm changes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn content strategy expert specializing in automated growth systems.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive LinkedIn content strategy that uses dependency grammar principles to structure posts for maximum clarity and engagement. Deliver a complete plan covering content calendar, post templates, and engagement tactics tailored to the business context provided.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
(Include business type, target audience, primary goals, brand voice, and key products/services)

The strategy must be:
- Scalable and suitable for automation
- Data-driven with clear metrics
- Adaptable to LinkedIn algorithm changes and platform trends
- Structured using dependency grammar to create clear, hierarchical content that drives engagement

## Output
Provide your strategy in three sections:

### Content Calendar
- Posting frequency and optimal timing
- Content themes and topic rotation
- Campaign milestones and seasonal alignment

### Post Templates
- Hook formulas that leverage dependency grammar structure
- Body frameworks (e.g., problem-agitate-solve, storytelling arcs)
- Call-to-action patterns
- Format variations (text, carousel, video, poll)

### Engagement Tactics
- Comment and DM strategies
- Network expansion methods
- Relationship nurturing workflows
- Metrics to track and optimization triggers

Use bullet points or numbered lists for clarity.
```
