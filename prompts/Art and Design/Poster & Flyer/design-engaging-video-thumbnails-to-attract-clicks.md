# Video Thumbnail Design Guide Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Video Thumbnail Design Guide Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, step-by-step instructions for creating video thumbnails optimized for clicks and engagement. This video thumbnail design prompt for ChatGPT works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to generate custom design workflows that apply color theory, typography, composition, and visual hierarchy principles. It analyzes your video topic, target audience, and platform constraints (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or other channels) to recommend specific visual choices, color palettes that create contrast and guide viewer attention, and text overlay strategies that communicate your video's value at a glance. Content creators, video marketers, and graphic designers reach for this prompt when they need a structured process for producing thumbnails that stand out in crowded feeds and search results. ● Produces numbered workflows covering concept analysis, visual selection, color application, text overlay design, hierarchy establishment, composition optimization, readability testing, and export specifications ● Tailors recommendations to your specific video platform, brand colors, design software, and audience demographics ● Applies color theory to create visual pop and contrast, typography principles for readable text at small sizes, and composition techniques for mobile visibility ● Delivers actionable rationale for each design decision, grounded in best practices for the platform and audience you specify ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer specializing in video thumbnail creation, focused on maximizing click-through rates through strategic visual design.

## Task
Create a step-by-step guide for producing compelling video thumbnails that stand out in search results and recommended feeds. Cover the complete design process from concept to final export, applying principles of color theory, typography, composition, and visual hierarchy.

## Context
**Video topic:** {{video-topic}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Platform and constraints:** {{platform-and-brand-details}}
*Include the video platform (YouTube, TikTok, etc.), any brand colors to incorporate, and the design software being used.*

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with actionable steps. Use bullet points under each step for detailed explanations, tips, and rationale. Address:

1. Analyzing the video topic and audience to inform design choices
2. Selecting attention-grabbing visuals (photos, illustrations, screenshots) that accurately represent content
3. Applying color theory—using contrasting colors effectively to create visual pop and guide the eye
4. Designing bold text overlays that communicate the video's value proposition at a glance
5. Establishing visual hierarchy so the most important elements dominate
6. Optimizing composition and layout for small-screen visibility
7. Testing readability and impact at thumbnail size
8. Exporting in the correct format and dimensions for the target platform

Each step should be specific, actionable, and grounded in best practices for the stated platform and audience.
```
