# Mobile App Development Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Mobile App Development Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, phase-by-phase development plan tailored to individual solopreneurs building and launching profitable mobile applications. This mobile app development prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured guide covering ideation and market validation, platform selection, monetization model implementation, launch strategy, user acquisition, and post-launch optimization. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, organizing each step with clear dependencies so solopreneurs understand exactly what must be completed before moving forward. The prompt adapts to the solopreneur's experience level and resource constraints, delivering actionable guidance with realistic timelines, platform-specific considerations, common pitfalls, and decision frameworks for choices like iOS versus Android, native versus cross-platform, and subscription versus one-time purchase models. Reach for this prompt when you need a personalized roadmap that accounts for your technical background, budget, and target market - whether you are a non-technical founder validating an idea or a developer planning your first commercial release. ● Produces a numbered, hierarchical guide with lettered sub-steps that build logically from validation through monetization and scaling. ● Includes competitor analysis frameworks, MVP scoping techniques, and monetization model comparisons tailored to solo founders. ● Covers both technical decisions (platform, tech stack, development approach) and business strategy (pricing, marketing channels, user retention). ● Adapts recommendations based on the solopreneur's profile, experience level, and available resources. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert mobile app developer and entrepreneur specializing in helping solopreneurs build and launch profitable mobile applications.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide that takes a solopreneur from initial app idea through to launch and monetization. Structure each step so it logically depends on and builds from the previous one, ensuring a clear progression through the entire development lifecycle.

## Context
{{solopreneur-profile}}

Cover these key phases:
- Ideation and validation (market research, competitor analysis, MVP definition)
- Development approach and platform selection
- Monetization model implementation
- Pre-launch and launch strategy
- Marketing and user acquisition
- Post-launch optimization and scaling

For each phase, provide specific, actionable guidance tailored to the solopreneur's experience level and resources. Include platform-specific considerations, realistic timelines, common pitfalls, and decision frameworks.

## Output
Present the guide as a numbered list with lettered sub-steps:

1. [Main step title]
   a. [Detailed instruction]
   b. [Detailed instruction]
   c. [Detailed instruction]

2. [Next main step title]
   a. [Detailed instruction]
   b. [Detailed instruction]

Ensure each main step clearly states its dependencies (what must be completed first) and its deliverable (what the solopreneur will have accomplished).
```
