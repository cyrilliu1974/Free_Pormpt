# Brand Packaging Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Brand Packaging Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-resolution product photography of minimalist packaging designs for brands and designers. This brand packaging prompt for Midjourney produces angled three-quarter-view scenes featuring two contrasting boxes on a textured surface, set against a bright interior with natural sunlight and soft window shadows. The output follows Bauhaus design principles with clean lines and intentional accent details, showing modular contents suitable for decorative interior applications. Use it to visualize packaging concepts for design-conscious consumer products, pitch decks, or marketing materials before committing to physical prototypes. Designers, brand managers, and product marketers reach for this prompt when they need commercial-quality mockups that convey a sophisticated, minimalist aesthetic without a photo shoot. ● Outputs 16:9 product photography with hero-box focus and blurred background depth ● Incorporates natural sunlight, window shadows, and textured surface staging for realism ● Styled for design-conscious urban audiences with Bauhaus and minimalist influences ● Customizable by brand name to generate on-brand packaging concepts in seconds ## Prompt
