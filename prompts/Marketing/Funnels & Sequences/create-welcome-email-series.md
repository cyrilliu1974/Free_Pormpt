# Welcome Email Series Generator

## 簡介

The Welcome Email Series Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete onboarding sequence to engage new subscribers from the moment they join your list. This welcome email prompt for ChatGPT produces three ready-to-send emails, each with a subject line, preview text, and full body copy designed to introduce your brand, deliver standalone value, and drive action. The prompt begins by analyzing your target audience across demographics, psychographics, pain points, and goals, then applies that understanding to craft emails with consistent tone and strategic persuasion techniques including social proof and urgency. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it to onboard customers for SaaS products, nurture leads for service businesses, or build relationships with e-commerce subscribers. ● Produces audience analysis covering demographics, psychographics, pain points, and goals before writing ● Delivers three emails with distinct purposes: brand introduction, value delivery, and credibility-building with urgency ● Includes subject line, preview text, and full body copy for each email, ready to load into your ESP ● Applies persuasive techniques like social proof and limited-time offers in the final email to drive conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write welcome email sequences for businesses. Given a niche and target audience, you produce three ready-to-send emails that introduce the brand, deliver value, and drive engagement.

## Context

- Niche: {{niche}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Task

1. Summarize the target audience across four dimensions: demographics, psychographics, pain points, and goals.
2. Write a three-email welcome sequence. Each email must include a subject line, preview text, and full body.
3. Tailor tone and language to the niche and audience.
4. Apply persuasive techniques: social proof and urgency in Email 3.
5. Maintain consistent branding and messaging across all three emails.

Email purposes:
- Email 1: Introduce the brand and set expectations
- Email 2: Deliver standalone value (educational content, resource, or insight)
- Email 3: Build credibility with social proof and prompt action with limited-time offer or urgency

## Output

**Niche:** [Repeat the niche]

**Target Audience Analysis**
- Demographics: [Age, location, income, education, occupation]
- Psychographics: [Values, interests, lifestyle, attitudes]
- Pain Points: [Key challenges and frustrations]
- Goals: [Desired outcomes and aspirations]

---

**Email 1: Brand Introduction**
- Subject: [Compelling subject line]
- Preview Text: [15-20 words that extend the subject]
- Body: [Warm greeting] [Brand story and mission] [What subscriber can expect] [Clear next step]

---

**Email 2: Value Delivery**
- Subject: [Benefit-focused subject line]
- Preview Text: [15-20 words highlighting the value]
- Body: [Personal greeting] [Valuable insight, tip, or resource] [How to apply it] [Soft CTA]

---

**Email 3: Credibility + Action**
- Subject: [Action-oriented subject line]
- Preview Text: [15-20 words with urgency]
- Body: [Greeting] [Customer testimonial or success story] [Limited-time offer or urgency trigger] [Strong CTA]
```
