# Seasonal Keyword Research Prompt for Campaigns

## 簡介

The Seasonal Keyword Research Prompt for Campaigns is a free AI prompt that compiles high-potential seasonal keywords with search volume data and relevance scoring for digital marketers and campaign planners. This seasonal keyword research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert digital marketer who researches, analyzes, and ranks keywords based on five campaign variables: industry, campaign type, target audience, geographic location, and seasonality focus. The output is a structured markdown table with three columns (Keyword, Search Volume, Relevance Score on a 1-10 scale), making it easy to compare opportunities and prioritize terms for paid search, content calendars, or seasonal promotions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is particularly useful for holiday campaigns, back-to-school planning, summer sales, or any time-sensitive marketing initiative. Reach for this prompt when you need data-driven keyword recommendations tailored to a specific season, location, and audience rather than generic keyword lists. ● Tailors keyword suggestions to five campaign parameters: industry, campaign type, audience, location, and seasonality ● Outputs a markdown table with keyword, search volume, and a 1-10 relevance score for easy comparison ● Instructs the AI to use current trends and reliable keyword tools for accurate, up-to-date recommendations ● Helps prioritize seasonal opportunities and align content or ad spend with high-intent search behavior ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in keyword research and campaign optimization.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive list of top seasonal keywords for optimizing targeted campaigns. Deliver findings in a markdown table with three columns: Keyword, Search Volume, and Relevance Score (1-10 scale, where 10 is highest relevance to the campaign).

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Campaign type: {{campaign-type}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Geographic location: {{geographic-location}}
Seasonality focus: {{seasonality-focus}}

## Process
1. Research using reliable keyword tools and current industry trends
2. Identify high-potential seasonal keywords specific to the industry and seasonality focus
3. Analyze search volume data for each keyword
4. Assess relevance of each keyword to the campaign type and target audience
5. Organize findings into the requested table format

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Relevance Score |
|---------|---------------|------------------|

Ensure research is thorough, relevant, and actionable for campaign optimization.
```
