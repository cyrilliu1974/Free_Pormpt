# Cinematic Lifestyle Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Lifestyle Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces magazine-quality portrait photography in minimal luxury interior settings for content creators and brand marketers. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney creates photorealistic images of subjects seated on designer furniture in high-end interiors, styled with professional wardrobe choices and lit by soft ambient window light that mimics DSLR 85mm f/1.4 photography. The prompt specifies architectural elements like sculptural décor and sheer curtains, wardrobe details down to fabric texture, pose direction, and camera settings that deliver 8k resolution output with realistic skin tones and atmospheric depth. It works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana for lifestyle brands, social media campaigns, editorial mockups, and corporate visual assets that traditionally require studio shoots and professional styling teams. Reach for this prompt when you need polished, publication-ready lifestyle imagery that conveys sophistication and modern elegance without the cost of location rentals or model bookings. ● Produces DSLR-quality portraits with natural skin texture, sharp fabric detail, and cinematic lighting that avoids overexposure or harsh shadows. ● Customizable subject description variable lets you specify gender, ethnicity, age, and styling to match brand identity and audience representation goals. ● Includes negative prompt tuning to eliminate common AI artifacts like blurriness, distorted faces, cartoon rendering, and busy backgrounds. ● Delivers 9:16 aspect ratio images optimized for Instagram Stories, Pinterest pins, and mobile-first social platforms. ## Prompt
