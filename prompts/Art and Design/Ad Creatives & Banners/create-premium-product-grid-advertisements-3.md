# Premium Product Grid Advertisement Creator

## 簡介

The Premium Product Grid Advertisement Creator is a free AI prompt that generates professional multi-panel product photography grids for tech brands, e-commerce sellers, and marketing teams. It produces a 2×3 layout (portrait 4:5 aspect ratio) with six carefully composed panels: a hero studio shot emphasizing construction and design, an illuminated RGB customization view, a macro close-up of internal mechanisms, hands-on action photography, a lifestyle desk setup, and a build-quality detail shot. This product grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers commercial-grade visual assets suited for social media ads, Amazon listings, Kickstarter campaigns, and brand storytelling without the cost of professional photo shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need cohesive, multi-angle product visuals that communicate craftsmanship, functionality, and premium positioning - ideal for keyboards, mice, controllers, audio gear, or any physical product requiring detailed visual demonstration. ● Hero studio shots with directional lighting that reveal product layout, materials, and logo clarity ● Macro close-ups of internal components, switch mechanisms, or construction details with ultra-shallow depth of field ● Active-use scenes capturing hands typing or gaming, conveying real-world performance and ergonomics ● Lifestyle desk integration with monitor and peripherals, balanced daylight, and minimal tech aesthetic ● Illumination panels showcasing RGB backlighting, LED effects, or customization features in low-ambient environments ● Build-quality details highlighting USB-C ports, aluminum frames, precision manufacturing, and material durability ## Prompt

```
Create a premium 2×3 grid advertisement showcasing {{product-name}} across six distinct compositions. Portrait 4:5 aspect ratio, equal panels arranged in 2 columns by 3 rows.

**Panel 1 – Hero Product Design**
Studio hero shot of {{product-name}} angled to reveal layout and construction. Soft directional studio lighting with subtle reflections on dark matte surface or minimal desk setup. Emphasize input surface layout, frame build quality, and logo clarity. Premium craftsmanship aesthetic.

**Panel 2 – Illumination & Customization**
{{product-name}} illuminated with RGB backlighting in low-ambient environment. Controlled glow showing even distribution and legend visibility. Modern, immersive, customizable mood.

**Panel 3 – Core Mechanism Detail**
Macro close-up of mechanical switch housing and stem. Ultra-shallow depth of field revealing internal engineering, material composition, and surface texture. Tactile precision focus.

**Panel 4 – Active Use Performance**
Hands actively typing or gaming on {{product-name}}. Natural or soft desk lighting capturing subtle finger movement. Convey speed, comfort, and focus during real use.

**Panel 5 – Desk Setup Lifestyle**
{{product-name}} integrated into clean, modern workstation with monitor and complementary peripherals. Balanced daylight. Productivity and aesthetic appeal, minimal tech lifestyle vibe.

**Panel 6 – Build & Durability**
Close-up of frame construction, USB-C connection interface, or aluminum base detail. Highlight material quality and manufacturing precision. Durability and reliability messaging.

**Visual Style:** Professional commercial product photography. High realism, minimalist desk aesthetic suited for tech brand social media ads, e-commerce listings, gaming and creator campaigns. Soft studio lighting with controlled highlights, balanced exposure, crisp detail, intentional soft shadows. Premium input device advertising aesthetic – precise, modern, focused, tactile.

**Brand presentation:** {{product-name}} logo minimal and subtle (engraved or understated print). Modern, performance-driven brand personality.

Avoid: cheap plastic appearance, blurry surface details or legends, uneven lighting, distorted proportions, cluttered setups, CGI or cartoon style, oversaturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
