# Eisenhower Matrix Task Prioritization Prompt

## 簡介

The Eisenhower Matrix Task Prioritization Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes workloads and categorizes tasks into four priority quadrants for solopreneurs and independent business owners. This task prioritization prompt for ChatGPT evaluates each item in your task list against business goals, deadlines, long-term value, and core activities, then organizes them into Urgent & Important, Urgent & Not Important, Not Urgent & Important, and Not Urgent & Not Important categories. The prompt delivers a structured markdown table showing where each task belongs with clear justification, plus quadrant-specific strategies that tell you whether to act immediately, delegate, schedule proactively, or eliminate tasks altogether. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal when you need to cut through overwhelm and focus on what truly moves your business forward. ● Categorizes every task by both urgency and importance to reveal true priorities ● Provides actionable strategies for each quadrant - do, delegate, schedule, or drop ● Includes 2-3 personalized recommendations based on your specific business context ● Surfaces high-value activities that get buried under urgent-but-unimportant noise ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity consultant specializing in task prioritization for solopreneurs using the Eisenhower Matrix.

## Task
Analyze the solopreneur's workload and categorize their tasks into the four Eisenhower Matrix quadrants: Urgent & Important, Urgent & Not Important, Not Urgent & Important, and Not Urgent & Not Important. Provide strategic guidance on how to approach tasks in each quadrant to maximize productivity and achieve business objectives.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Current workload: {{task-list}}

Evaluate each task based on:
- Impact on stated business goals
- Time sensitivity and deadlines
- Long-term value creation
- Alignment with core business activities

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with four columns (one per quadrant). Place each task in the appropriate quadrant with brief justification.

Below the table, provide:

**Quadrant-Specific Strategies:**
- **Urgent & Important:** Immediate action required; schedule dedicated focus time
- **Urgent & Not Important:** Delegate, automate, or batch-process; minimize personal time investment
- **Not Urgent & Important:** Schedule proactively; protect time for these high-value activities
- **Not Urgent & Not Important:** Eliminate or defer indefinitely; these drain resources without return

Include 2-3 prioritized recommendations tailored to this solopreneur's specific situation.
```
