# Market Trend SEO Opportunity Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Trend SEO Opportunity Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies actionable SEO opportunities by analyzing market trends and competitor strategies for businesses seeking to improve search visibility. This market trend SEO opportunity analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to research current search patterns, popular keywords, and emerging topics in your industry, then evaluate competitor websites to uncover their SEO strengths, weaknesses, and gaps in the market. The output includes a 2-3 paragraph executive summary of market trends and opportunities, plus a structured markdown table comparing competitor SEO tactics across website structure, content strategy, and backlink profiles. Content strategists, digital marketers, and SEO specialists use it to inform keyword targeting, content planning, and competitive positioning decisions. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to audit your competitive landscape before launching a new content strategy, entering a market segment, or refreshing an existing SEO approach. ● Surfaces trending keywords and search patterns within a specified industry ● Delivers side-by-side competitor SEO strength and weakness analysis in table format ● Pinpoints market gaps where your business can establish authority and rankings ● Accepts custom inputs for industry, business name, competitor list, and current SEO focus ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst conducting a comprehensive market analysis.

## Task
Identify potential SEO opportunities for the business by analyzing market trends and competitors. Begin by researching current market trends, focusing on search patterns, popular keywords, and emerging topics. Then identify key competitors and analyze their SEO strategies, including website structure, content strategy, and backlink profiles. Use this information to spot gaps in the market and areas where the business can gain a competitive advantage.

## Context
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Business name: {{business-name}}
- Top competitors: {{competitors}}
- Current SEO focus: {{current-seo-strategy}}

## Output
Provide a brief summary of market trends and SEO opportunities (2-3 paragraphs), followed by a markdown table with 3 columns:

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Include one row per competitor analyzed.
```
