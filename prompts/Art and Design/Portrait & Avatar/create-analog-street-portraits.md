# Analog Street Portrait Generator With Film Grain

## 簡介

The Analog Street Portrait Generator With Film Grain is a free AI prompt that produces candid night photography with authentic 35mm film characteristics for photographers, content creators, and brands seeking raw urban imagery. This analog street portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates 1200x1200px square images featuring direct camera flash as the primary light source, high-contrast shadows, subtle film grain, and muted warm color palettes that replicate the nostalgic look of film photography shot at night. The prompt structures natural poses with arms folded, slight smiles, and genuine skin texture against dark city streets with blurred bokeh from streetlights and neon signs. Real-world applications include fashion lookbooks, lifestyle brand content, editorial photography collections, and social media profiles that require authentic urban atmosphere without organizing nighttime shoots or sourcing film cameras. Reach for this prompt when you need street photography that feels unpolished and genuine rather than studio-perfect, or when building visual assets that reference analog photography culture and urban night scenes. ● Produces direct flash lighting with strong highlights, sharp background shadows, and subtle skin glow that mimics on-camera flash from film cameras. ● Customizable subject descriptions and foreground objects let you specify clothing, pose details, and environmental elements while maintaining the candid aesthetic. ● Delivers muted warm color grading with high contrast and distinct film grain across the entire 1200x1200px frame. ● Creates soft bokeh and halated light points from streetlights, car headlights, and neon signs in the dark background for authentic urban night atmosphere. ## Prompt
