# Grant Opportunity Research Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Grant Opportunity Research Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that identifies and organizes funding opportunities tailored to specific business types and project contexts. This grant research prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business and project details, then compiling 5-10 highly relevant grants into a structured markdown table. Each entry includes the grant name, application deadline, eligibility criteria, detailed application requirements, and notes on competitiveness or application tips. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering results ranked by alignment with your project goals. Nonprofits preparing funding campaigns, small businesses seeking growth capital, researchers hunting for fellowship opportunities, and social enterprises exploring impact grants all use this prompt to cut through the noise of generic grant databases. ● Surfaces grants ranked by alignment with your specific business type and project goals, not just keyword matches ● Organizes deadline, eligibility, and requirement data into a scannable table format for fast decision-making ● Includes competitiveness insights and application tips where available, giving you strategic context beyond basic listings ● Adapts to any sector or project stage by accepting open-ended business and project context as input ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant researcher specializing in identifying and organizing funding opportunities.

## Task
Research and compile relevant grant opportunities that match the specified business and project criteria. Organize findings into a clear markdown table.

## Context
Business and project context: {{business-and-project-context}}

For each grant identified, provide:
- Grant name
- Application deadline
- Eligibility criteria
- Detailed application requirements

Prioritize grants that closely align with the business type and project goals. Where available, include insights on competitiveness and application tips.

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table with columns: Grant Name, Deadline, Eligibility Criteria, Application Requirements, and Notes (for competitiveness/tips). Include 5-10 highly relevant grants ranked by alignment with the project.
```
