# Strategic Innovation Portfolio Generator

## 簡介

The Strategic Innovation Portfolio Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a balanced mix of short-term wins and long-term transformation initiatives for business leaders and innovation teams. This strategic innovation portfolio prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and produces four fully specified projects: two designed for 6-18 month delivery and two targeting 2+ year transformation. Each project includes a name, description, required resources, timeline, expected ROI, and risk assessment. The prompt then delivers a complete resource allocation plan that addresses constraints and dependencies, a risk management framework with mitigation strategies, and a phased execution roadmap that shows how to launch and balance all initiatives for continuous value delivery. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond ad-hoc innovation and build a disciplined portfolio that addresses both immediate market demands and future competitive positioning. ● Produces four distinct innovation projects with ROI projections, timelines, and resource requirements tailored to your business context ● Delivers a resource allocation plan that balances competing demands and identifies dependencies across the portfolio ● Includes a risk management framework that anticipates potential obstacles and provides mitigation strategies ● Generates a phased execution roadmap showing how to sequence initiatives for continuous value delivery ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic innovation portfolio manager specializing in balancing short-term wins with long-term transformation to maximize business value.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive strategic innovation portfolio comprising two short-term projects (6-18 months) and two long-term projects (2+ years). For each project, specify name, description, required resources, timeline, expected ROI, and potential risks. Then deliver a resource allocation plan, risk management plan, and execution roadmap that maintains portfolio balance.

## Context
Analyze the provided business context to ensure all proposed projects align with strategic objectives, capitalize on market opportunities, and address competitive pressures. Prioritize initiatives with strong ROI and manageable risk profiles.

**Business Context:**
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver your portfolio structured as:

**Strategic Analysis**
Key insights from the business context that inform project selection.

**Short-term Projects (2)**
For each:
- Project Name
- Description
- Resources Required
- Timeline
- Expected ROI
- Potential Risks

**Long-term Projects (2)**
For each:
- Project Name
- Description
- Resources Required
- Timeline
- Expected ROI
- Potential Risks

**Resource Allocation Plan**
How resources will be distributed across the four projects, addressing constraints and dependencies.

**Risk Management Plan**
Mitigation strategies for identified risks across the portfolio.

**Portfolio Execution Roadmap**
Phased timeline showing how projects will be initiated, executed, and balanced to deliver continuous value.
```
