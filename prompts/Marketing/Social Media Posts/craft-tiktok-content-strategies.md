# TikTok Content Strategy Hypothesis Generator

## 簡介

The TikTok Content Strategy Hypothesis Generator is a free AI prompt that produces 10 distinct, testable video concepts for businesses experimenting with TikTok growth and audience fit. This TikTok content strategy prompt for ChatGPT helps digital marketers and business owners move beyond guesswork by generating specific video ideas that test different variables - audience segments, value propositions, content formats, hooks, tone, and framing styles. Instead of generic advice, you receive concrete hypotheses like "Create a day-in-the-life video targeting solopreneurs" or "Test a problem-agitation hook versus a curiosity-driven opener." Each concept is designed for TikTok's authentic, organic style and can be shot immediately. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your unique business context to ensure experiments align with your actual offering and goals. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured experimentation framework to discover what TikTok content drives engagement and conversions for your brand. ● Outputs a table of 10 video hypotheses, each pairing a specific shoot concept with the variable being tested ● Spans multiple experimentation dimensions including audience segment, tone, hook style, value angle, and content format ● Ensures ideas suit TikTok's platform-native style and avoid overly promotional or corporate content ● Tailors recommendations to your business context so experiments connect to real offerings and goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in TikTok content experimentation and viral growth for business accounts.

## Task
Generate 10 distinct, testable hypotheses for TikTok videos that systematically explore different dimensions of audience fit and messaging. Each hypothesis should propose a specific video concept designed to reveal what resonates with viewers.

## Context
Effective TikTok strategy requires experimentation across multiple variables: audience segments, value propositions, content formats, hooks, tone, and framing (product-led vs. educational). The goal is clarity through iteration—recording videos that test different approaches, then doubling down on what works.

**Business context:**
{{business-context}}

**Platform requirements:**
- Ideas must suit TikTok's authentic, organic style (avoid overly promotional or polished corporate content)
- Leverage TikTok-native formats, trends, and behaviors
- Focus on genuine interest and value, not hard selling

## Criteria
- **Diverse experimentation areas**: span different audience segments, value angles, tones, hooks, and content styles
- **Concrete and specific**: each hypothesis should describe exactly what to create (not generic advice like "try different segments")
- **Creative and unconventional**: avoid obvious or outdated tactics
- **Product-aligned**: experiments should connect to the actual offering and business goals
- **Actionable**: clear enough to shoot immediately

## Output
Provide a table with 10 rows and 2 columns:

| Experiment Description | Experiment Focus |
|------------------------|------------------|
| *What specific video to shoot* | *What variable we're testing (e.g., audience segment, tone, hook style, value proposition)* |
```
