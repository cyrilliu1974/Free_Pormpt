# Google Ads Copy Generator for High-Converting Campaigns

## 簡介

The Google Ads Copy Generator for High-Converting Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates complete, conversion-optimized ad campaigns for any product or service targeting specific audiences. This Google Ads prompt for ChatGPT produces a full campaign structure including headlines (30 characters max), two description lines (90 characters each), display and final URLs, plus ad extensions with sitelinks, callouts, and structured snippets. It applies dependency grammar principles to lead with high-impact information and build clear syntactic relationships between benefits and features. The prompt generates three complete ad versions: one primary ad and two variations testing different messaging angles, enabling immediate A/B testing. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when launching new Google Ads campaigns, refreshing underperforming ads, or scaling your paid search strategy across multiple products. Perfect for digital marketers, PPC specialists, e-commerce teams, and small business owners managing their own ad spend. ● Enforces Google Ads character limits automatically (30-char headlines, 90-char descriptions) ● Includes sitelinks, callouts, and structured snippets to maximize ad real estate and Quality Score ● Outputs three ad versions with different angles for immediate split testing ● Applies dependency grammar framework to prioritize benefits and strengthen persuasive impact ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert Google Ads copywriter specializing in conversion-optimized ad copy.

## Task

Create a complete Google Ads campaign for {{product-or-service}}, including primary ad copy, extensions, and two variations to test different messaging angles.

## Context

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique selling points: {{usp}}

Structure your copy using dependency grammar principles: lead with the most important information, use modifiers that strengthen core claims, and create clear syntactic relationships between benefits and features.

## Output

Provide the campaign in this format:

**Primary Ad**

Headline: [attention-grabbing headline, max 30 characters]

Description Line 1: [key benefit and unique feature, max 90 characters]
Description Line 2: [additional benefit and strong call-to-action, max 90 characters]

Display URL: [brand-relevant display URL]
Final URL: [specific landing page URL]

**Ad Extensions**

- Sitelink 1: [link text] – [URL]
- Sitelink 2: [link text] – [URL]
- Callout 1: [concise value proposition]
- Callout 2: [trust signal or differentiator]
- Structured Snippet:
  - Header: [category, e.g., "Services" or "Features"]
  - Values: [value 1], [value 2], [value 3]

**Ad Variation 1**

Headline: [alternative angle]
Description Line 1: [alternative benefit framing]
Description Line 2: [alternative CTA]

**Ad Variation 2**

Headline: [second alternative angle]
Description Line 1: [second alternative benefit framing]
Description Line 2: [second alternative CTA]

Ensure all copy is persuasive, benefit-focused, and optimized to drive clicks and conversions from the target audience.
```
