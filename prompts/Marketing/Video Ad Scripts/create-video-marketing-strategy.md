# Video Marketing Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Video Marketing Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive, actionable video marketing plan for businesses and content creators looking to harness visual storytelling. This video marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section plan that includes target audience demographics and psychographics, key messaging aligned with business objectives, recommended video formats with purpose and style guidance, distribution channel recommendations, SEO and engagement optimization techniques, a three-month content calendar, and measurable KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your business context and strategic preferences as inputs to generate a tailored roadmap from concept to measurement. Use it when you need a structured framework for launching or scaling video content that drives awareness, engagement, and conversions. ● Maps audience pain points, goals, and psychographics to inform message development and format selection ● Recommends specific video types with purpose, duration, and creative style matched to business objectives ● Provides a three-month content calendar with topics sequenced for audience journey progression ● Defines measurable KPIs across awareness, engagement, and conversion stages with target benchmarks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video marketing strategist specializing in visual storytelling to engage audiences, convey messages, and drive actions.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, actionable video marketing strategy covering audience analysis, messaging, formats, distribution, optimization, content planning, and performance measurement.

## Context
The strategy must leverage visual storytelling principles and provide clear implementation guidance. All recommendations should align with the target audience's needs and the business objectives.

**Business Context:**
{{business-context}}

**Preferred Approach:**
{{strategic-preferences}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy in the following structure:

**Target Audience:**
- Demographics: [age, location, occupation, income]
- Psychographics: [values, interests, behaviors, motivations]
- Pain Points: [challenges, frustrations, obstacles]
- Goals: [desired outcomes, aspirations]

**Key Messages:**
1. [Primary message aligned with core value proposition]
2. [Supporting message addressing pain points]
3. [Differentiating message for competitive advantage]

**Video Formats:**
1. Type: [format] | Purpose: [objective] | Length: [duration] | Style: [creative approach]
2. Type: [format] | Purpose: [objective] | Length: [duration] | Style: [creative approach]
3. Type: [format] | Purpose: [objective] | Length: [duration] | Style: [creative approach]

**Distribution Channels:**
1. [Primary channel with rationale]
2. [Secondary channel with rationale]
3. [Tertiary channel with rationale]

**Optimization Techniques:**
1. [SEO/algorithm technique]
2. [Engagement technique]
3. [Conversion technique]

**3-Month Content Calendar:**
- Month 1: Video 1: [topic] | Video 2: [topic]
- Month 2: Video 1: [topic] | Video 2: [topic]
- Month 3: Video 1: [topic] | Video 2: [topic]

**KPIs and Metrics:**
1. [Awareness metric with target]
2. [Engagement metric with target]
3. [Conversion metric with target]
```
