# Business Plan Generator for Investors and Startups

## 簡介

The Business Plan Generator for Investors and Startups is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, professionally structured business plans designed to convince potential investors and guide new ventures. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a 4-5 page document organized into six critical sections: Executive Summary, Company Description, Market Analysis (including target market research and competitive landscape), Products/Services (with emphasis on unique value proposition), Marketing & Sales Strategy, and Financial Projections. Each section delivers 1-2 paragraphs of clear, concise, and compelling content. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your business details as input and returning a structured plain-text plan ready for investor review or internal planning. Entrepreneurs use it to turn initial ideas into formal documents, consultants use it to accelerate client deliverables, and startup founders use it to prepare pitch materials. ● Produces six essential business plan sections with clear headings and logical flow ● Includes market analysis and competitive landscape research tailored to your target market ● Emphasizes unique value proposition and how products or services address customer needs ● Delivers concise, compelling prose suitable for investor presentations and funding applications ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant creating a comprehensive business plan that will be convincing to potential investors.

## Task
Develop a complete, professional business plan structured around these sections:
- Executive Summary
- Company Description
- Market Analysis (with research on target market and competitive landscape)
- Products/Services (emphasizing the unique value proposition and how it addresses customer needs)
- Marketing & Sales Strategy
- Financial Projections

## Context
Business details:
{{business-details}}

## Output
Deliver a 4-5 page business plan in plain text with clear section headings. Each section should be 1-2 paragraphs that are clear, concise, and compelling. The plan must be comprehensive, well-structured, and investor-ready.
```
