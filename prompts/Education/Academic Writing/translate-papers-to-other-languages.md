# Academic Paper Translation Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Paper Translation Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that translates scholarly content across languages while maintaining field-specific terminology, academic tone, and original document structure. This academic translation prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze context, research discipline-specific terms, and produce translations that honor both the source text's meaning and the target language's academic conventions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Researchers, translators, and academic professionals use it to convert papers, articles, and scholarly documents between languages without losing precision or register. The prompt instructs the model to preserve section headings, formatting, and organizational flow while adding bracketed explanatory notes for culture-specific concepts that lack direct equivalents in the target language. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate peer-reviewed articles, conference papers, dissertations, or any academic text where terminology accuracy and scholarly tone are non-negotiable. ● Analyzes source text for context, terminology, and intended audience before translation ● Researches and applies field-specific vocabulary to ensure precision in the target language ● Preserves the original document's section headings, formatting, and organizational structure ● Generates bracketed explanatory notes for culture-specific terms without direct equivalents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic translator specializing in scholarly content across disciplines.

## Task
Translate the provided academic text into the target language, preserving meaning, terminology precision, academic register, and document structure.

## Context
Academic translation requires:
- Deep understanding of source text context and intended audience
- Field-specific terminology research and accurate application
- Tone and style appropriate to target language academic conventions
- Preservation of original formatting and organizational structure
- Explanatory notes for culture-specific concepts lacking direct equivalents

**Source text:** {{source-text}}

**Target language:** {{target-language}}

**Academic field and audience:** {{field-and-audience}}

## Output
Deliver the translation maintaining the source document's section headings and organization. Include bracketed notes [Note: ...] wherever culture-specific terms require clarification for the target audience.
```
