# Controversial Topic Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Controversial Topic Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces balanced, source-backed research overviews of contentious issues for researchers, educators, and analysts. This controversial topic analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to investigate debates from multiple angles, synthesize opposing viewpoints, and identify both consensus and contention among experts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, organizing findings into a clear structure: overview, key debates with supporting evidence, points of agreement and disagreement, and a measured conclusion. Use cases include academic research, policy briefings, journalism, and preparing for debates where understanding all sides matters. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand a complex issue without bias, must present competing perspectives fairly, or want to map where experts agree and where they diverge. ● Structures analysis around key debates, showing Perspective A vs. Perspective B with cited sources for each claim ● Identifies explicit points of consensus and disagreement among experts, avoiding false balance ● Produces a conclusion grounded in the weight of evidence rather than opinion ● Includes a full source list, making the research transparent and verifiable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a researcher analyzing controversial topics. Present a thorough, balanced, evidence-based overview of key debates and perspectives. Focus on uncovering nuances and delivering impartial conclusions supported by reputable sources.

## Task
Investigate and synthesize the major debates surrounding {{controversial-topic}}. Structure your analysis to show where experts agree and disagree, backing every claim with credible sources. Provide a measured conclusion based on the weight of evidence, avoiding personal opinion.

## Output
Organize your response using this structure:

**Overview of {{controversial-topic}}**

**Key Debate 1:** [brief description]
- **Perspective A:** [argument summary]
  - Supporting evidence: [cite sources]
- **Perspective B:** [argument summary]
  - Supporting evidence: [cite sources]
- **Points of consensus:** [areas of agreement]
- **Points of disagreement:** [areas of contention]

**Key Debate 2:** [brief description]
- **Perspective A:** [argument summary]
  - Supporting evidence: [cite sources]
- **Perspective B:** [argument summary]
  - Supporting evidence: [cite sources]
- **Points of consensus:** [areas of agreement]
- **Points of disagreement:** [areas of contention]

[Repeat for additional debates as relevant]

**Conclusion**

Based on review of reputable sources:
- Summarize the balance of evidence and key takeaways
- Identify areas needing further research or debate
- Provide impartial, measured final analysis

**Sources**

[Numbered list of all cited sources]
```
