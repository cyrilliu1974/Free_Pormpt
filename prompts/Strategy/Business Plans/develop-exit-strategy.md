# Exit Strategy Builder for Business Planning

## 簡介

The Exit Strategy Builder for Business Planning is a free AI prompt that creates structured exit plans maximizing stakeholder value and ensuring smooth business transitions for owners, strategists, and advisors. This exit strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through analyzing your current business situation, evaluating market conditions, and comparing exit options including sale, merger, IPO, management succession, and liquidation. It outputs a detailed markdown table organized around your chosen components - financial considerations, legal requirements, timeline milestones, stakeholder communication plans, risk mitigation strategies, and implementation roadmaps - with each section detailing actionable steps, responsible parties, success criteria, dependencies, and risk controls. Built for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, the prompt accepts inputs for business type, stakeholders, planned timeline, table structure, and key components you want emphasized. Reach for this prompt when you need a formal, strategic exit plan that balances multiple stakeholder interests, addresses regulatory requirements, and provides a clear execution roadmap aligned to a specific timeline. ● Analyzes exit pathways (sale, merger, IPO, succession, liquidation) against your business type and market conditions ● Structures financial considerations, legal requirements, and stakeholder communication into a customizable table format ● Includes risk mitigation measures, dependencies, and success criteria for every component of your exit plan ● Assigns responsible parties and implementation milestones tailored to your specified timeline ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in exit planning and value maximization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive exit strategy for {{business-type}} that maximizes stakeholder value and ensures a smooth transition. Analyze the current situation, market conditions, and exit options (sale, merger, IPO, succession, liquidation). Address financial considerations, legal requirements, timeline, stakeholder communication, risk mitigation, and implementation milestones.

## Context
Stakeholders: {{stakeholders}}
Planned timeline: {{timeline}}

## Output
Present your exit strategy as a markdown table with {{number-of-columns}} columns, structured around these key components: {{key-components}}.

For each component, include:
- Specific actionable steps
- Responsible parties
- Success criteria
- Risk mitigation measures
- Dependencies and prerequisites

Ensure the strategy is tailored to the business type and addresses all stakeholder concerns within the specified timeline.
```
