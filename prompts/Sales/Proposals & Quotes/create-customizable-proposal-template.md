# Sales Proposal Template Generator for Customer Value

## 簡介

The Sales Proposal Template Generator for Customer Value is a free AI prompt that creates ready-to-use proposal frameworks for sales teams who need to communicate value, address customer pain points, and win deals. This sales proposal prompt for ChatGPT produces a nine-section template that guides teams through executive summaries, customer pain point diagnostics, solution overviews, impact analysis with ROI metrics, transparent pricing breakdowns, implementation timelines, and ongoing support details. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only your business context and target customer profile to generate a complete framework ready for customization. Sales professionals use it to standardize their proposal process while maintaining flexibility for specific opportunities, whether pitching enterprise software, consulting services, or product solutions. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured foundation that ensures every proposal addresses decision-making criteria, quantifies business impact, and positions your offering clearly against competitors. ● Produces templates with customer pain point diagnostics that help sales teams uncover root causes and articulate challenges clearly ● Includes customer impact analysis sections with guidance on quantifying ROI, cost savings, and productivity gains using industry benchmarks ● Structures pricing and investment sections for transparency, breaking down costs by component or implementation phase ● Generates implementation plans with milestone timelines, risk mitigation strategies, and customer support frameworks that demonstrate long-term commitment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sales enablement consultant who creates proposal templates that help teams communicate value, address pain points, and win deals.

## Task
Generate a complete, customizable sales proposal template structured around the sections below. The template should be ready for a sales team to populate with specific details for their next opportunity.

## Context
**Industry and positioning:**
{{business-context}}

**Target customer profile and their decision-making priorities:**
{{target-customer}}

## Output
Deliver a proposal template with these sections:

### 1. Executive Summary
- Overview of the proposal's purpose and key points
- Unique value proposition and competitive advantages

### 2. Customer Pain Point Diagnostics
- Articulate the customer's primary challenges and objectives
- Include probing questions to uncover root causes
- Summarize findings clearly

### 3. Solution Overview
- Describe how the solution addresses identified pain points
- Highlight key features, benefits, and differentiators
- Suggest placement for diagrams or infographics

### 4. Educational Value
- Provide industry insights, case studies, or research that demonstrate expertise
- Explain complex concepts in accessible language
- Use analogies or real-world examples

### 5. Customer Impact Analysis
- Quantify potential business impact using metrics (ROI, cost savings, productivity gains)
- Base projections on industry benchmarks or past results

### 6. Pricing and Investment
- Present pricing options transparently
- Break down costs by component or phase
- Emphasize value relative to investment

### 7. Implementation Plan
- Outline implementation steps
- Provide timeline with milestones and deliverables
- Address potential risks and mitigation strategies

### 8. Customer Support and Success
- Describe ongoing support and resources
- Highlight support team expertise and responsiveness
- Include examples of successful long-term partnerships

### 9. Appendices
- Placeholder for case studies, testimonials, and product specifications

**Writing guidelines:**
- Avoid jargon; prioritize clarity and conciseness
- Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points for readability
- Note where visuals (charts, graphs, images) should be inserted
- Maintain a professional, consistent tone throughout
```
