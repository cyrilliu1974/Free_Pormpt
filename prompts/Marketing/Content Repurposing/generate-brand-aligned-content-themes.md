# Brand-Aligned Content Theme Generator

## 簡介

The Brand-Aligned Content Theme Generator is a free AI prompt that develops strategic content themes matching your brand values and target audience profile. Built for content marketers, brand strategists, and marketing teams who need cohesive messaging across channels, this content theme prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand details and audience demographics to produce three distinct content pillars complete with format recommendations, tone specifications, concrete examples, and brand-alignment rationales. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a complete content strategy framework in one session. This prompt is designed for marketers launching new campaigns, rebranding efforts, or refreshing stale content calendars. ● Synthesizes brand identity, values, and unique positioning into a cohesive overview ● Maps target audience demographics, psychographics, and content consumption habits ● Delivers three distinct content themes with specific formats, tone, and real examples ● Includes distribution channel recommendations, publishing cadence, and KPIs to track performance ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic content marketer analyzing brands and audiences to identify engaging content themes that reinforce brand identity and values.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive content strategy with three distinct themes tailored to the brand and its audience. For each theme, specify formats, tone, concrete examples, and alignment with brand values. Include distribution recommendations and success metrics.

## Context

Brand: {{brand-details}}

Target Audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

**Brand Overview:**
[Synthesize the brand's identity, values, and unique selling proposition]

**Target Audience:**
- Demographics: [age, location, income, education, occupation]
- Psychographics: [values, interests, lifestyle, pain points, aspirations]
- Content Preferences: [preferred formats, platforms, consumption habits]

**Content Theme 1:**
- Theme: [theme name and description]
- Content Formats: [specific formats suited to this theme]
- Tone: [appropriate tone of voice]
- Example: "[concrete content example]"
- Brand Alignment: [how this theme reinforces brand identity and values]

**Content Theme 2:**
- Theme: [theme name and description]
- Content Formats: [specific formats suited to this theme]
- Tone: [appropriate tone of voice]
- Example: "[concrete content example]"
- Brand Alignment: [how this theme reinforces brand identity and values]

**Content Theme 3:**
- Theme: [theme name and description]
- Content Formats: [specific formats suited to this theme]
- Tone: [appropriate tone of voice]
- Example: "[concrete content example]"
- Brand Alignment: [how this theme reinforces brand identity and values]

**Content Distribution Recommendations:**
[Channels, publishing frequency, cross-promotion strategies, and platform-specific adaptations]

**Key Performance Indicators:**
[Metrics to track content strategy success: engagement rates, reach, conversions, sentiment, brand lift]
```
