# 9-Box Grid Talent Management Framework Generator

## 簡介

The 9-Box Grid Talent Management Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a structured HR framework for assessing employee performance and potential, supporting succession planning and targeted development initiatives. This talent management prompt for ChatGPT helps HR professionals and people leaders design a complete 9-Box Grid system tailored to their organization. It defines SMART performance and potential criteria across low, medium, and high levels, explains the significance of each grid position (from high-potential/high-performance stars ready for promotion to employees requiring performance improvement plans), and prescribes specific development actions - mentoring, stretch assignments, training programs, or lateral moves - for every category. The framework includes a step-by-step employee assessment process, succession planning guidelines, communication best practices, and review cycle recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, accepting organization context and current talent practices as inputs. Reach for this prompt when you need to implement or refresh a data-driven talent review process, prepare for leadership succession, or align development investments with both individual career goals and business priorities. ● Generates SMART criteria for performance and potential with concrete examples relevant to your organization ● Explains all nine grid positions and their strategic implications for promotion, development, or remediation ● Delivers tailored development strategies for each employee category, from stretch assignments for high-potentials to performance improvement plans for underperformers ● Includes communication protocols, feedback methods, and review cycles to keep the framework aligned with evolving business needs ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert Human Resources Consultant specializing in talent management frameworks, specifically the 9-Box Grid methodology for assessing employee performance and potential.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive talent management framework using the 9-Box Grid that enables effective categorization of employees, informs development decisions, and supports succession planning.

## Context

**Organization Profile:**
{{organization-context}}

**Current Talent Management Approach:**
{{current-practices}}

## Output

Provide a complete, actionable framework structured as follows:

### Introduction to the 9-Box Grid
Explain the concept, purpose, and strategic benefits of the 9-Box Grid in talent management.

### Defining Criteria
- **Performance Criteria:** Provide SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) standards for low, medium, and high performance levels with concrete examples relevant to the organization
- **Potential Criteria:** Define clear indicators for assessing low, medium, and high potential with measurable benchmarks

### Assessing Employees
Deliver a step-by-step evaluation process for placing employees against the defined performance and potential criteria.

### Categorizing Employees
Explain each of the 9 boxes in the grid, describing what each position signifies (e.g., high potential/high performance indicates promotion readiness, low potential/low performance may require performance improvement plans).

### Development Strategies
- Recommend tailored strategies for each of the 9 categories
- Provide specific developmental actions such as training programs, mentoring, stretch assignments, lateral moves, or performance improvement initiatives
- Align recommendations with individual career aspirations and organizational needs

### Using the Grid for Succession Planning
Provide guidelines for leveraging the 9-Box Grid to identify successors, ensure leadership continuity, and build talent pipelines.

### Communication and Feedback
- Offer best practices for sensitively communicating assessment results to employees while maintaining confidentiality
- Suggest methods for incorporating employee feedback and maintaining engagement throughout the process
- Recommend review cycles for updating criteria and strategies to align with organizational evolution and industry standards

Use bullet points, numbered lists, and concrete examples throughout for clarity and immediate applicability.
```
