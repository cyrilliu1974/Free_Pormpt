# Career Path Mapping Prompt for Development Plans

## 簡介

The Career Path Mapping Prompt for Development Plans is a free AI prompt that builds structured career roadmaps for professionals planning their advancement in any industry. This career path mapping prompt for ChatGPT takes your current job role, industry, situation, and goals, then outputs a three-column markdown table that traces the full trajectory from entry-level positions through mid-career roles to senior leadership. Each stage includes the education credentials required, the technical and soft skills to develop, typical job titles, progression timelines, and key milestones. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing current industry trends and job market demands to deliver realistic, actionable guidance. Use it when changing careers, planning skill development, or advising employees on growth paths. ● Outputs a clear markdown table format with separate columns for education requirements, skills to acquire, and career path progression at each stage. ● Covers 4-6 career stages, mapping typical timelines and transitions from entry-level through mid-career to senior roles. ● Identifies certifications, degrees, and continuing education checkpoints aligned with industry standards. ● Specifies both technical competencies and soft skills needed to advance at each level. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career development consultant creating comprehensive, actionable career roadmaps.

## Task
Develop a detailed career development plan for {{job-role}} in {{industry}}, mapping the progression from entry-level to senior positions. Research current industry trends, analyze job market demands, and identify long-term advancement opportunities. The plan should highlight key milestones, required skill acquisitions, and credential checkpoints at each stage.

## Context
Starting point: {{current-situation}} (include education level, years of experience, and any relevant background)

Career objectives: {{career-goals}}

## Output
Present the plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| EDUCATION | SKILLS | CAREER PATH |
|-----------|--------|-------------|

For each career stage (entry, mid, senior), specify:
- **EDUCATION**: Degrees, certifications, continuing education requirements
- **SKILLS**: Technical, soft, and industry-specific competencies to develop
- **CAREER PATH**: Typical job titles, progression timeline, and key milestones

Include 4-6 rows covering the full trajectory from starting point to aspirational senior roles.
```
