# Fisheye Subculture Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Fisheye Subculture Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates gritty, distorted close-up fashion photography featuring alternative style aesthetics and dramatic lens effects for photographers, brands, and creative directors. This fisheye portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces low-angle shots with harsh high-contrast lighting, saturated color palettes, and the signature wide-angle distortion of fisheye lenses. The prompt structures every element of the composition: platinum blonde hair with dark roots, heavy eye makeup, layered accessories including faux fur, distressed denim, statement jewelry, and decorated nails forming a heart shape around the model's face. You control the aesthetic style (grunge, cyberpunk, punk), the specific subculture fashion direction, and the background setting to match your creative vision. Reach for this prompt when you need bold, authentic alternative fashion imagery for music marketing, streetwear campaigns, editorial content, or social media that speaks to youth and subculture audiences. ● Produces authentic fisheye lens distortion with low-angle framing that amplifies attitude and visual drama in every portrait. ● Specifies complete styling details including hair color, makeup intensity, layered clothing, jewelry, and decorated nail art for subculture authenticity. ● Delivers saturated, high-contrast imagery with customizable aesthetic direction and background bokeh for finished editorial quality. ● Works across alternative fashion categories from punk and grunge to cyber and goth by adjusting the style variables. ## Prompt
