# Literature Review Writer for Academic Research

## 簡介

The Literature Review Writer for Academic Research is a free AI prompt that produces structured, analytical literature reviews for scholars, graduate students, and researchers working in any academic field. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a rigorous synthesis process: it organizes findings across studies, evaluates methodological strengths and limitations, identifies conceptual gaps, and recommends specific future research questions with appropriate methodologies. You supply your topic or field and preferred citation style (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.), and the prompt returns a complete review with Introduction, Body (Key Findings, Strengths and Limitations, Future Directions), Conclusion, and formatted References. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, emphasizing synthesis over summary so that connections between studies emerge clearly. Reach for this prompt when you need to map the state of knowledge in a field, prepare a dissertation chapter, draft a grant proposal background, or write the literature section of a journal manuscript. ● Outputs a full literature review with Introduction, synthesized Body sections, and formatted References in your chosen citation style. ● Analyzes relationships between studies rather than listing findings in isolation, highlighting patterns and contradictions. ● Identifies methodological limitations and conceptual gaps, then proposes priority research questions to address them. ● Adapts to any academic discipline by accepting a custom topic-field variable and citation-style variable. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic researcher conducting a comprehensive literature review.

## Task
Write a structured literature review that critically analyzes and synthesizes existing research on {{topic-field}}. Identify the strengths, limitations, and gaps in current knowledge, then propose specific future research directions to address those gaps.

## Output
Organize the review with these sections:

**Introduction**
- Background and context for {{topic-field}}
- Purpose and scope of this review
- Search and selection methodology

**Body**

*Key Findings*
- Synthesize the main findings across studies (draw connections and patterns, do not simply summarize individual papers)
- Analyze how findings relate to one another

*Strengths and Limitations*
- What the existing research does well
- Methodological limitations, conceptual gaps, and underexplored areas

*Future Directions*
- Priority areas for new research
- Specific research questions that would address identified gaps
- Appropriate methodologies for those questions

**Conclusion**
- Summary of the current state of knowledge
- Implications for theory and practice
- Overall assessment

**References**
- All cited sources in {{citation-style}} format

Cite sources throughout the text using {{citation-style}}. Focus on synthesis over summary—connect ideas across studies rather than treating each in isolation.
```
