# Cross-Promotional Campaign Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Cross-Promotional Campaign Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive partnership proposals for businesses exploring co-branded marketing opportunities. This cross-promotional campaign proposal prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini transforms basic partnership details into a complete, persuasive document that covers company overviews, strategic rationale, three concrete co-branded initiatives with execution plans, projected outcomes (brand exposure, customer acquisition, revenue growth), and partnership terms including duration, financial arrangements, and success metrics. Each proposal flows logically from executive summary through measurable outcomes to next steps, emphasizing mutual value and win-win benefits. Marketing managers, business development professionals, and partnership teams use it to formalize collaboration opportunities between brands with overlapping audiences or complementary strengths. ● Structures proposals with executive summary, company overviews, partnership rationale, three detailed initiatives, projected metrics, and terms ● Identifies overlapping target audiences and complementary strengths that justify the collaboration ● Outlines execution steps, promotion channels, and responsibilities for each partner company ● Projects quantifiable outcomes including brand reach, new customer acquisition, and revenue growth ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist and copywriter specializing in cross-promotional campaign proposals that demonstrate mutual value and strategic alignment between partner companies.

## Task
Write a comprehensive, persuasive proposal for a cross-promotional campaign between the two specified businesses. Structure the document to flow logically from strategic rationale through concrete initiatives to measurable outcomes.

## Context
{{partnership-details}}

Include:
- Each company's products/services, target market, and unique selling proposition
- The nature of the potential partnership
- Any known constraints or priorities

## Output
Deliver a structured proposal with these sections:

**Executive Summary**  
Summarize the strategic case and key benefits of the partnership in 3–4 sentences.

**Company Overview**  
- Company A: Description, offerings, market, and unique strengths  
- Company B: Description, offerings, market, and unique strengths

**Rationale for Partnership**  
- *Overlapping Target Audiences:* Identify shared demographics, psychographics, and behaviors  
- *Complementary Strengths:* Show how each company's assets enhance the other's brand and offerings

**Proposed Cross-Promotional Initiatives**  
Present 3 co-branded initiatives (products, services, events, or content campaigns). For each:
- Detailed description  
- *Execution:* Specific steps and responsibilities per company  
- *Promotion:* Channels and tactics for joint marketing

**Projected Outcomes**  
- *Brand Exposure:* Estimated reach and impressions for each company  
- *Customer Acquisition:* Projected new customers gained  
- *Revenue Growth:* Forecasted incremental sales and profit

**Partnership Terms**  
- *Duration:* Campaign start and end dates  
- *Financial Arrangements:* Shared costs, revenue splits, or referral incentives  
- *Performance Metrics:* KPIs for measuring success

**Conclusion**  
Reinforce mutual benefits and long-term partnership potential.

**Next Steps**  
Propose a timeline for finalizing the agreement and launching the campaign.

Use clear headings, bullet points, and concise paragraphs. Write persuasively, emphasizing win-win outcomes and concrete value for both parties.
```
