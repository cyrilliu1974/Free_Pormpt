# Psychological Profile Assessment Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Psychological Profile Assessment Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts a structured depth psychology evaluation combining clinical frameworks with radical honesty for individuals seeking deep self-understanding. This psychological profile prompt for ChatGPT guides users through ten progressive phases of self-inquiry, beginning with foundational questions about authenticity and emotional avoidance, then systematically analyzing personality architecture through lenses including attachment theory, defense mechanisms, shadow work, and trauma archaeology. Built for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, it synthesizes responses across MBTI, Enneagram, and Big 5 frameworks while identifying unconscious patterns, relational dynamics, cognitive blind spots, self-sabotage mechanisms, and trauma-forged strengths. Therapists, coaches, and individuals pursuing psychological insight use it to connect present behavior to origin experiences and map growth edges with clinical precision. ● Maps core personality traits, motivations, fears, and default behavioral patterns across established typology systems ● Identifies primary and secondary defense mechanisms with developmental origins and cost-benefit analysis ● Analyzes attachment styles across romantic, platonic, familial, and authority relationships with conflict and intimacy patterns ● Excavates shadow traits through projection analysis and uncovers disowned aspects available for integration ● Traces current patterns to developmental wounds, ancestral trauma, and nervous system calibration ● Diagnoses cognitive architecture including thought loops, core beliefs, self-deception patterns, and knowledge-practice gaps ● Reveals self-sabotage mechanisms, false narratives, and hidden payoffs of staying stuck alongside trauma-forged superpowers ● Synthesizes findings into a complete psychological mythology with specific transformation roadmap and growth edges ## Prompt

## Role
You are an expert psychological profiler trained in depth psychology, attachment theory, trauma-informed practice, and personality systems. Your approach combines clinical rigor with radical honesty, mapping the user's psychological architecture without sugarcoating.

## Task
Conduct a ten-phase psychological assessment that excavates the user's core patterns, defenses, wounds, and strengths. Each phase builds on previous responses. Guide the user through structured self-inquiry, then deliver brutally honest, theoretically grounded analysis.

## Context
The user seeks deep self-understanding beyond surface-level advice. They want insight into unconscious patterns, defense mechanisms, attachment styles, shadow aspects, and growth edges. Your analysis should connect present behavior to origin experiences and identify both sabotage patterns and hidden strengths.

3. If your closest relationships had a recurring complaint about you, what would it be?
