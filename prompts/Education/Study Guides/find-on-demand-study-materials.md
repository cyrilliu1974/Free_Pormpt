# Study Material Finder for Online Courses

## 簡介

The Study Material Finder for Online Courses is a free AI prompt that researches and compiles high-quality on-demand learning resources tailored to specific subjects and learner profiles. This study materials prompt for ChatGPT works by having the AI act as an educational content curator, evaluating online platforms for course quality, instructor credentials, and learner reviews. It produces a structured markdown table listing 5-8 courses that span beginner to advanced levels and accommodate different learning modalities - video lectures, interactive exercises, project-based learning, and reading-focused content. Use it when you need to quickly identify the best online courses from established platforms like Coursera, edX, or Udemy without spending hours comparing options yourself. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Evaluates platform credibility and instructor qualifications to filter out low-quality resources ● Presents courses in a three-column table (Platform, Course Title, Key Takeaways) for quick comparison ● Spans multiple difficulty levels and learning styles in a single search ● Prioritizes established platforms with verified credentials and strong learner reviews ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational content curator specializing in identifying high-quality on-demand learning resources.

## Task
Research, evaluate, and compile a curated list of the most relevant and reputable online learning resources for the specified subject. Assess platform credibility, course quality, and instructional design. Ensure the selection covers a range of difficulty levels and learning modalities to accommodate diverse student needs.

## Context
{{subject-and-goals}}

{{learner-profile}}

## Output
Present your curated recommendations in a markdown table with three columns:

| Platform | Course Title | Key Takeaways |
|----------|--------------|---------------|

Include 5-8 courses that span beginner to advanced levels and accommodate different learning preferences (video lectures, interactive exercises, project-based, reading-focused). Prioritize resources from established platforms with verified instructor credentials and strong learner reviews.
```
