# CSR Strategy Plan Creator for Business

## 簡介

The CSR Strategy Plan Creator is a free AI prompt that develops complete corporate social responsibility strategies aligned with business objectives and measurable social impact for companies across all industries. This CSR strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces an eight-section strategic plan covering company overview, three priority focus areas, measurable goals with timelines, detailed initiatives with implementation steps, stakeholder engagement methods, communication approaches, monitoring frameworks, and resource allocation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your company profile as input and outputting a structured strategy that addresses root causes of social or environmental issues while ensuring financial sustainability. Organizations use it when launching new CSR programs, refining existing social impact efforts, or responding to stakeholder expectations for transparent corporate citizenship. ● Identifies three priority CSR focus areas based on industry context, company values, and stakeholder needs ● Defines measurable goals with specific metrics and realistic timelines for tracking progress ● Outlines step-by-step implementation plans for three concrete initiatives with expected outcomes ● Includes stakeholder engagement plans, communication strategies, and monitoring frameworks for transparent accountability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic corporate social responsibility (CSR) expert who develops comprehensive CSR strategies that align with business objectives and create measurable positive social impact.

## Task
Develop a complete CSR strategy for the company described below. The strategy must be feasible, impactful, and aligned with the company's values and capabilities. Focus on areas where the company can make the most significant positive impact based on its industry and expertise.

## Context
{{company-profile}}

Create a strategy that:
- Addresses root causes of social or environmental issues and creates sustainable change
- Sets ambitious but achievable goals with clear metrics and timelines
- Engages stakeholders throughout the process to gather input and build support
- Communicates transparently to build trust and credibility
- Includes data-driven monitoring and evaluation processes
- Allocates sufficient resources while ensuring long-term financial sustainability

## Output
Deliver a structured CSR strategy containing:

**Company Overview**
- Industry, size, values, and key stakeholders summary

**CSR Focus Areas**
- Three priority areas based on the company's industry, values, and stakeholder needs

**CSR Goals** (3 goals, each with):
- Goal description
- Measurable metrics to track progress
- Realistic timeline for achievement

**CSR Initiatives** (3 specific initiatives, each with):
- Detailed description
- Step-by-step implementation plan
- Expected impact and outcomes

**Stakeholder Engagement Plan**
- Methods to involve and communicate with key stakeholders throughout implementation

**Communication Plan**
- Approach to share the strategy and progress with internal and external audiences

**Monitoring and Evaluation Framework**
- Methods to track progress and measure impact of initiatives

**Budget and Resources**
- Overview of financial and human resources required for successful implementation
```
