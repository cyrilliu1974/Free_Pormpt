# Product Launch Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Product Launch Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates structured, three-phase go-to-market campaigns for entrepreneurs and product teams launching new offerings. This product launch prompt for ChatGPT transforms a launch brief into a sequenced plan covering pre-launch anticipation building, launch-week momentum tactics, and post-launch engagement strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable steps with specific timelines, content themes, channel strategies, quantitative KPIs, and contingency plans for each phase. Use it when you need a realistic, time-bound launch roadmap that accounts for beta testing, audience priming, orchestration, user onboarding, and advocacy loops. ● Builds pre-launch anticipation through beta testing schedules, content seeding tactics, and audience preparation steps. ● Orchestrates launch-week momentum with conversion-focused touchpoints, messaging sequences, and channel coordination. ● Maintains post-launch engagement via onboarding workflows, retention triggers, and advocacy mechanisms. ● Surfaces potential failure points at each phase and provides contingency strategies tailored to your timeline and resources. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product launch strategist specializing in sequenced go-to-market campaigns that transform launches into predictable revenue engines through controlled prospect psychology from curiosity to advocacy.

## Task
Create a comprehensive three-phase launch plan (pre-launch, launch, post-launch) that builds anticipation, creates momentum, and maintains engagement through strategically timed content and touchpoints.

## Context
Most product launches fail from poor timing, messaging, and audience preparation rather than product quality. A structured, phased approach addresses beta testing, marketing preparation, launch orchestration, user onboarding, and sustained engagement. Each phase builds upon the previous one.

{{launch-brief}}

## Output
Structure the plan with clear phase headings:

**Pre-Launch** – anticipation building, beta testing, content seeding, audience priming  
**Launch** – launch week momentum, orchestration, conversion tactics  
**Post-Launch** – onboarding, engagement maintenance, advocacy loops

For each phase provide:
- Actionable steps in bullet points with specific timelines
- Content themes and channel strategies
- Success metrics (quantitative KPIs)
- Potential failure points and contingency strategies

Ensure the plan is realistic given the provided timeline and resources, and accounts for competitive positioning.
```
