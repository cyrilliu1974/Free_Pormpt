# Healthcare Advertisement Panel Generator

## 簡介

The Healthcare Advertisement Panel Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional three-panel healthcare advertising banners for clinics and medical practices. This healthcare ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 3:2 landscape image divided into consultation, treatment, and result panels. It renders modern clinical interiors with bright diffused lighting, a soft white, light blue, and mint green palette, and high-realism photography style. The prompt structures each panel to build trust: a friendly practitioner-patient interaction, a close-up of sterile procedure execution with modern equipment, and a confident patient displaying positive health outcomes. You customize it with your clinic name and healthcare specialty (dental, dermatology, physiotherapy, etc.), and the output includes branding typography and a specialty-appropriate tagline. Reach for this prompt when you need professional healthcare marketing visuals that emphasize hygiene, modern equipment, and patient confidence without stock-photo clichés. ● Three-panel narrative structure (consultation, treatment, result) that guides viewer trust from interaction to outcome ● Clean clinical aesthetic with specified lighting, color palette, and minimal composition that conveys hygiene and professionalism ● Customizable for any healthcare specialty with variable slots for clinic name and service type ● Explicit style guardrails to avoid dark lighting, cluttered backgrounds, cartoon rendering, and non-professional elements ## Prompt

```
Create a professional 1×3 horizontal advertisement panel (3:2 landscape) for {{clinic-name}} {{healthcare-specialty}} services, emphasizing trust, hygiene, modern equipment, and confident healthy results.

**Visual Structure:**
3 side-by-side panels in modern clinical interior with bright diffused lighting, soft white + light blue + mint green color palette, clean minimal composition.

**Panel 1 – Friendly Consultation:**
Mid-shot of healthcare professional smiling and speaking with patient in modern clinic room, natural interaction, comfortable and trustworthy mood.

**Panel 2 – Professional Treatment:**
Close-up of practitioner performing careful procedure with gloves and mask, focus on sterile tools and modern equipment, bright clinical light, precision and hygiene.

**Panel 3 – Healthy Result:**
Confident adult with visible positive health outcome (bright smile for dental; clear skin for dermatology; relaxed posture for physiotherapy, etc.), soft natural light, satisfaction and wellness.

**Style:**
Professional healthcare advertising photography, high realism, medical professionalism, clean balanced exposure, minimal soft shadows.

**Branding:**
Clinic name {{clinic-name}} prominent, tagline "Your Healthiest Smile Starts Here" (or equivalent for specialty), clean medical sans-serif typography, caring and modern brand personality.

**Mood:** Healthy, trustworthy, professional, clean, comfortable, confident.

**Avoid:** Dirty instruments, dark lighting, scary atmosphere, unrealistic glows, cluttered background, cartoon style, low resolution, watermarks, text overlays.
```
