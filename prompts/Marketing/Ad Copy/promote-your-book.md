# Book Promotion Strategy Guide Builder

## 簡介

The Book Promotion Strategy Guide Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, genre-tailored marketing plan for authors and publishers looking to promote their books effectively. This book promotion prompt for ChatGPT takes your specific book details - genre, target audience demographics, marketing budget, and existing author platform - and produces a structured markdown table with 12-15 actionable strategies. It organizes tactics across online marketing (social media, email campaigns, content marketing, paid ads), offline channels (bookstore events, print materials), networking opportunities (author collaborations, reader communities), and media outreach (podcast appearances, reviewer connections, influencer partnerships). Each strategy includes a clear description explaining why it works for your genre, plus 2-3 concrete execution examples fitted to your budget and platform constraints. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Authors launching new titles, publishers planning release campaigns, and book marketing consultants will find this prompt invaluable for building comprehensive, budget-aware promotion roadmaps quickly. ● Produces a structured table format that clearly separates strategy type, rationale, and execution steps ● Balances cost-effective organic tactics with scalable paid options based on your stated budget ● Tailors recommendations to genre conventions and audience reading habits for higher conversion ● Prioritizes strategies that build on existing author assets like email lists, social followings, and industry connections ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert book marketing strategist creating a comprehensive, actionable promotion guide.

## Task
Develop a complete book promotion strategy covering online marketing, offline marketing, networking, media outreach, and audience engagement tactics. Tailor all recommendations to the specific genre, audience demographics, budget constraints, and existing author platform provided.

## Context
**Book & Author Details:**
{{book-and-author-context}}
(Include: genre, target audience demographics and reading habits, marketing budget range, current author platform - social media presence, email list size, website, previous publications, industry connections)

## Approach
Organize strategies by marketing channel. For each tactic, assess fit with the genre conventions, audience preferences, and budget reality. Prioritize cost-effective methods that leverage the existing author platform. Balance proven traditional approaches with current digital marketing techniques.

## Output
Deliver your guide as a markdown table with three columns:

| Book Promotion Strategies | Description | Examples |
|---------------------------|-------------|----------|

Include 12-15 distinct strategies spanning:
- **Online marketing** (social media, email, content marketing, paid ads, book promotion sites)
- **Offline marketing** (events, bookstore partnerships, print materials)
- **Networking** (author collaborations, industry relationships, reader communities)
- **Media outreach** (podcasts, book reviewers, local media, influencers)

For each strategy, write a concise description explaining *what* it is and *why* it works for this genre, plus 2-3 concrete examples showing *how* to execute it within the stated budget and platform constraints.
```
