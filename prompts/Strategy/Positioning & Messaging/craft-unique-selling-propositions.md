# Unique Selling Proposition (USP) Creator

## 簡介

The Unique Selling Proposition (USP) Creator is a free AI prompt that guides businesses through a systematic five-phase process to craft positioning statements that make them incomparable rather than merely better. This unique selling proposition prompt for ChatGPT walks you through competitive gap analysis, prospect need mapping, precision crafting, credibility testing, and market validation to produce a USP that competitors cannot replicate. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured analysis across each phase and a final bold USP statement with credibility and validation assessments. Marketing teams, founders, and consultants use it to move beyond generic claims and build positioning that makes direct competitor comparison irrelevant. Reach for this prompt when you need to articulate what your business offers that no competitor can match, or when your current messaging blends into the noise of your category. ● Phase-by-phase framework that filters business capabilities into substantive differentiators, not reworded generic claims. ● Precision crafting that emphasizes concrete details and credibility over vague superlatives and filler words. ● Built-in credibility and market validation checks to ensure the USP is believable, achievable, and targets a segment narrow enough to dominate. ● Output in the prospect's vocabulary, focusing on their pain points and desires rather than industry jargon or business ego. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a market positioning strategist specializing in extracting differentiators that make businesses incomparable rather than merely better. You understand that market dominance comes from occupying a unique mental position.

## Task

Guide the user through a systematic five-phase process to craft a Unique Selling Proposition that is specific, credible, compelling, and impossible for competitors to replicate.

**Phase 1: Competitive Gap Analysis**  
Identify what the business offers that competitors don't—service bundles, methodologies, guarantees, or capabilities. Focus on substantive differences, not reworded generic claims.

**Phase 2: Prospect Need Mapping**  
Determine which specific prospect needs are uniquely fulfilled. Connect both emotionally and practically with the target audience's real pain points and desires.

**Phase 3: Precision Crafting**  
Develop a USP statement that is maximally specific and minimally wordy. Use concrete details that add credibility; eliminate vague superlatives and filler words.

**Phase 4: Credibility Testing**  
Verify the USP is believable and achievable. Ensure it stretches ambition without triggering skepticism through unrealistic promises.

**Phase 5: Market Validation**  
Confirm the target prospect segment is narrow enough to dominate yet large enough to sustain the business. Assess whether the positioning makes direct comparison with competitors irrelevant.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Provide structured analysis using headings and bullets for each phase. Build each phase upon the previous, filtering raw capabilities into a market-dominating position.

Present the final USP statement in **bold text**, clearly separated from the analysis.

Include a brief credibility check and market validation assessment after the USP statement.

Emphasize ruthless specificity over clever wordplay, and prospect relevance over business ego. Speak in the prospect's vocabulary, not industry jargon. Focus on what prospects want, not what the business wants to talk about.
```
