# Cinematic Urban Night Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Urban Night Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces hyper-realistic nighttime street photography with dramatic lighting, wet pavement reflections, and smartphone-style authenticity. The prompt creates 8K-resolution images of a woman leaning against a customizable vehicle in moody urban settings, with illuminated storefronts and signage creating vibrant light reflections on rain-soaked ground. This cinematic urban portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers magazine-quality results with natural skin tones, realistic depth of field, and HDR sharpness that mimics professional night photography. Perfect for content creators, brand designers, and marketers who need atmospheric urban visuals without organizing expensive nighttime location shoots. ● Customizable vehicle type, urban location backdrop, and outfit details through three simple variables ● Produces authentic smartphone camera aesthetic with natural skin tones and realistic depth of field ● Creates dramatic wet pavement reflections and vibrant artificial light sources for moody atmosphere ● Outputs 8K resolution images with hyper-detailed textures, cinematic HDR sharpness, and dark color grading ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic 8K portrait of a woman leaning against {{vehicle}} on wet ground at night, {{urban-location}} in background with illuminated signage and storefronts. She wears {{outfit-description}}. Wavy hair, natural proportions, hyper-detailed textures. Cinematic night lighting with vibrant reflections from artificial lights on wet pavement, HDR sharpness, dark moody color grading. Shot with smartphone camera aesthetic—natural skin tones, realistic depth of field, authentic night photography look.
```
