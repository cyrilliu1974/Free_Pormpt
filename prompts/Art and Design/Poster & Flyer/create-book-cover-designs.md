# Book Cover Design Generator for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Book Cover Design Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional book cover mockups for authors, publishers, and designers. This book cover prompt for Midjourney and similar image models (Flux, Nano Banana) produces print-ready covers in 5:7 aspect ratio with customizable design styles, title text placement, and visual elements. You specify the aesthetic (minimalist, vintage, modern, illustrated), your book title, and the imagery or compositional elements you want, and the prompt renders a cohesive cover design. Writers use it to visualize self-published titles, designers prototype client concepts, and marketers generate mockups for A/B testing before hiring illustrators. Reach for this prompt when you need a quick cover concept, want to explore multiple design directions, or need a reference image to brief a professional designer. ● Outputs covers in 5:7 aspect ratio optimized for print and digital storefronts. ● Three flexible variables let you control typography style, title wording, and visual composition separately. ● Works across fantasy, romance, thriller, non-fiction, and literary genres by adjusting design-style and visual-elements. ● Raw style flag ensures clean, unfiltered rendering suitable for professional use. ## Prompt

```
A {{design-style}} book cover design with the stylized words "{{title-text}}", {{visual-elements}}, --ar 5:7 --style raw
```
