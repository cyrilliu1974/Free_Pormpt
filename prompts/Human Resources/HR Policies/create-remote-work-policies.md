# Remote Work Policy Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Remote Work Policy Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured 7-step roadmap for organizations developing remote work policies that balance employee flexibility with operational effectiveness. This remote work policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete framework covering policy foundation and legal compliance, stakeholder consultation, eligibility criteria, communication standards, performance measurement, technology requirements, and implementation processes. Each step includes detailed action items, key stakeholders to involve, success metrics, and common pitfalls to avoid, making it practical for HR leaders crafting policies for startups, mid-sized companies, or enterprise organizations across any industry. Reach for this prompt when you need to create or revise a remote work policy that addresses immediate operational needs while supporting long-term cultural goals, especially when managing diverse teams with varying roles and work arrangements. ● Builds a legal and compliance foundation that accounts for jurisdiction-specific requirements and organizational risk tolerance. ● Defines role-based eligibility criteria and classification systems that ensure fairness across departments and seniority levels. ● Establishes measurable performance accountability systems and communication protocols that maintain productivity in distributed environments. ● Addresses technology infrastructure, security requirements, and implementation timelines with clear stakeholder responsibilities at every phase. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR policy architect and remote work strategist specializing in flexible work frameworks for organizations ranging from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

## Task

Create a comprehensive 7-step roadmap for developing a remote work policy that balances employee flexibility with organizational effectiveness. Each step should build on the previous one, forming a complete framework that addresses legal compliance, performance management, communication standards, and equity.

## Context

Organization size: {{organization-size}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Current remote work situation: {{current-remote-status}}

Key challenges to address: {{policy-challenges}}

The roadmap must cover:
1. Policy foundation and legal framework
2. Stakeholder consultation and needs assessment
3. Eligibility criteria and role classifications
4. Communication and collaboration standards
5. Performance measurement and accountability systems
6. Technology and security requirements
7. Implementation and review processes

Each step should address immediate operational needs and long-term culture goals, with attention to implementation challenges across diverse teams and roles.

## Output

Format as a numbered list with:
- Clear step title
- Detailed explanation of the step's purpose and approach
- Specific action items for execution
- Key stakeholders to involve
- Success metrics or checkpoints
- Common pitfalls to avoid
```
