# Persuasive Copywriting Generator for Any Technique

## 簡介

The Persuasive Copywriting Generator for Any Technique is a free AI prompt that creates structured persuasive content designed to drive audience action across any business context. This persuasive copywriting prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze audience motivations, then craft copy that moves readers from attention to action using your chosen persuasion technique - whether reciprocity, social proof, scarcity, authority, or others. The prompt structures output using dependency grammar principles so each sentence builds logically on the last, creating a cohesive argument. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing copy organized into five sections: Hook, Problem Statement, Solution, Benefits, and Call to Action. You specify your target audience, desired action, persuasion technique, business context, and unique selling proposition, and the AI balances emotional triggers, logical reasoning, and credible evidence to maximize impact. Marketing professionals, business owners, and content creators reach for this prompt when they need persuasive copy that converts, whether for landing pages, email campaigns, sales letters, or ad copy. ● Analyzes target audience motivations before drafting copy ● Applies any persuasion technique consistently throughout the message ● Structures copy with clear sections from hook through call to action ● Balances emotional appeals, logical reasoning, and evidence for maximum persuasive impact ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive content that drives audience action.

## Task
Create compelling persuasive copy that motivates {{target-audience}} to {{desired-action}} using {{persuasion-technique}}. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—each sentence should build logically on the previous one, creating a cohesive argument that flows naturally from hook to close.

## Context
**Industry/Context:** {{business-context}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

Begin by analyzing what motivates your audience, then craft a message that resonates with their needs and desires. Deploy your chosen persuasion technique consistently throughout. Balance emotional triggers, logical reasoning, and credible evidence to maximize persuasive impact.

## Output
Deliver your copy in a structured format with clear headings:

- **Hook** – opening that captures attention
- **Problem Statement** – articulate the pain point or opportunity
- **Solution** – how your offer addresses it
- **Benefits** – specific outcomes and value
- **Call to Action** – clear directive for the desired action

Use bullet points within sections where appropriate to enhance clarity and readability.
```
