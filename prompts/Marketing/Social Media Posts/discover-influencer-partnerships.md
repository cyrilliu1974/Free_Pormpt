# Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end influencer outreach strategies for brands seeking authentic promotional partnerships. This influencer marketing prompt for ChatGPT guides you through discovery, vetting, pitch crafting, negotiation, and performance tracking to connect your product with the right content creators. The prompt analyzes influencer demographics, engagement rates, and content alignment to identify candidates whose audiences match your target market. It generates personalized outreach templates that emphasize mutual benefits, structures partnership terms covering post frequency and compensation models, and establishes tracking systems for conversions and ROI. Social media managers, brand marketers, and growth teams use it to move from cold outreach to signed collaboration agreements with measurable performance benchmarks. This tool is designed for businesses launching influencer campaigns, agencies managing multiple brand partnerships, or marketing teams expanding their creator network. ● Produces qualified influencer shortlists with demographic and engagement analysis justifying each selection ● Generates customized pitch templates adaptable to individual creator styles and platform norms ● Structures collaboration terms including content formats, usage rights, compensation models, and posting schedules ● Establishes performance tracking methodology with follower growth, engagement metrics, conversion attribution, and ROI calculation ● Provides relationship management protocols for feedback loops and long-term partnership development ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in influencer outreach and partnership development.

## Task

Create a comprehensive influencer outreach strategy to promote {{product}}. Identify suitable influencers whose audience, engagement, and content align with the brand, then develop personalized pitches and partnership frameworks.

## Context

{{business-context}}

The strategy must leverage influencer credibility and reach to enhance brand awareness and drive conversions through authentic endorsements.

## Process

1. **Influencer Discovery & Vetting**: Analyze candidates across relevant social platforms. Assess follower demographics for target audience match, evaluate engagement rates (likes, comments, shares, interaction quality), and review content style for brand alignment.

2. **Pitch Development**: Create personalized outreach messages for each influencer. Emphasize mutual benefits including brand exposure, compensation, exclusive product access, and collaborative opportunities. Tailor tone and messaging to each influencer's platform and style.

3. **Negotiation Framework**: Structure partnership terms covering post frequency, content formats, hashtag/tagging protocols, usage rights, and compensation models. Ensure terms benefit both parties and align with campaign goals.

4. **Performance Tracking**: Establish measurement systems for follower growth, engagement on promotional content, conversions, and ROI. Set benchmarks and reporting cadence.

5. **Relationship Management**: Maintain ongoing communication with partners. Provide performance feedback, gather insights, and identify opportunities for long-term collaboration.

## Output

Deliver a structured influencer outreach plan containing:

- Shortlist of qualified influencers with rationale for each selection
- Customized pitch templates adaptable to individual influencers
- Proposed collaboration terms and compensation structures
- Performance metrics dashboard and tracking methodology
- Relationship management protocols for ongoing partnerships

The plan should remain flexible for optimization based on campaign performance and market feedback.
```
