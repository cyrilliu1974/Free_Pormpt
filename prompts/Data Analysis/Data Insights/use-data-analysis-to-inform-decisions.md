# Sales Data Analysis Prompt for Decision-Making

## 簡介

The Sales Data Analysis Prompt for Decision-Making is a free AI prompt that transforms raw sales data into actionable business intelligence for sales teams, analysts, and decision-makers. This sales data analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by having the AI adopt the role of an expert sales analyst who systematically reviews your sales metrics and KPIs, identifies performance trends and patterns, and delivers specific recommendations organized in a clean three-column table (Metric, Insight, Action). You provide your sales data along with context about your sales process, industry, target market, and timeframe, and the AI returns 5-7 key metrics with meaningful insights and concrete next steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile across text-based AI platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn sales numbers into strategic decisions, whether you're reviewing quarterly performance, diagnosing pipeline issues, or preparing board presentations. ● Systematically evaluates sales metrics and KPIs to surface hidden patterns and anomalies in performance data ● Produces a markdown table with clear metric-insight-action rows that can be dropped directly into reports or presentations ● Guides the AI through a five-step analytical process ensuring thorough review and context-aware recommendations ● Adapts to any industry, sales process, or timeframe by accepting custom sales data and business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales analyst evaluating sales performance to deliver data-driven recommendations.

## Task
Analyze sales metrics and KPIs to identify trends, patterns, and areas for improvement. For each metric, uncover meaningful insights and formulate specific, actionable recommendations.

## Context
{{sales-data}}

Include details about your sales process, industry, target market, key performance indicators, and the timeframe for analysis.

## Process
1. Review the provided sales data thoroughly
2. Identify the most relevant metrics and KPIs for the sales process
3. Analyze these metrics to uncover meaningful insights
4. Determine potential areas for improvement based on patterns and anomalies
5. Formulate specific, actionable recommendations for each finding

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Metric | Insight | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|

Each row must provide a clear, concise, and actionable recommendation based on data-driven insights. Include at least 5-7 key metrics in your analysis.
```
