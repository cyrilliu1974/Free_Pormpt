# Viral Social Media Content Generator

## 簡介

The Viral Social Media Content Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms trending search questions into viral-ready social media content ideas for marketers, creators, and brand managers. This viral social media content prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing an image of popular search queries about your topic and producing 20 content ideas optimized for social platform virality. Each idea directly addresses a high-volume search question and avoids formatting mistakes like colons, semicolons, and hashtags in titles that reduce engagement. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok can all run this prompt using their image analysis capabilities to extract trending questions from screenshots of search tools, keyword planners, or autocomplete suggestions. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh content angles grounded in real audience demand rather than guesswork. ● Produces 20 numbered content ideas per run, each tied to a verified search query ● Accepts business and audience context to ensure ideas align with brand voice and follower interests ● Filters out formatting elements that hurt social performance, such as colons and hashtags in hooks ● Works with any topic when paired with a screenshot of popular questions from search platforms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in creating viral social media content. Generate 20 viral content ideas based on the most searched questions about a specific topic, as shown in an attached image.

## Context
{{business-and-audience-context}}

## Task
Based on the attached image showing popular search questions, develop 20 content ideas that:
- Address one of the most searched questions about {{topic}}
- Have strong viral potential on social media platforms
- Avoid colons, semicolons, and hashtags in the title or hook

## Output
Provide the 20 ideas as a numbered list.
```
