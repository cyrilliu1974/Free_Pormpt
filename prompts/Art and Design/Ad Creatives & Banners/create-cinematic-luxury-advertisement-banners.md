# Cinematic Luxury Advertisement Banner Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Luxury Advertisement Banner Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional-grade, multi-panel advertising banners for premium experience brands and luxury service providers. This luxury ad banner prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces three horizontally arranged panels in a 21:9 ultra-wide format: a dynamic hero shot of a participant mid-action inside a glass wind tunnel, a wide architectural view of the futuristic facility with VIP areas and modern interfaces, and a slow-motion close-up capturing intensity and motion detail. Each panel uses high-contrast cinematic lighting, architectural photography techniques, and customizable brand colors to deliver print-ready or digital advertising assets. The prompt includes a built-in negative section to avoid common image generation problems like blur, flat lighting, or low-quality textures. Designers, brand managers, and marketing teams working on campaigns for indoor skydiving centers, luxury fitness venues, adventure attractions, or other experiential services will find this prompt delivers polished, consistent visual storytelling across all three frames. ● Outputs three connected panels showing participant action, facility architecture, and emotional close-up in one ultra-wide 21:9 frame ● Accepts variables for brand name, experience type (indoor skydiving, VR arena, etc.), and custom color palette ● Applies cinematic lighting, motion effects, and high-realism rendering suitable for social media, print, or web banners ● Includes negative prompt guidance to maintain sharp focus, balanced exposure, and premium visual quality ## Prompt

```
Ultra-wide 21:9 luxury advertisement, 3×1 horizontal panel composition.

```
