# Cozy Window Portrait Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Cozy Window Portrait Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates heartwarming photorealistic portraits of a woman sitting by a window in seasonal settings with customizable companions and atmospheres. This cozy portrait prompt for Midjourney produces 8k-resolution scenes featuring consistent facial features, warm soft lighting, and comforting details like hot chocolate and scarves. You control three variables - seasonal weather conditions, outfit descriptions, and companion type - while the prompt maintains the subject's face across generations. The result is lifestyle imagery perfect for social media, blog headers, wellness brands, and emotional storytelling campaigns. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Stable Diffusion, and similar image models. Reach for this prompt when you need relatable, cozy lifestyle visuals without organizing photoshoots or hiring models. ● Preserves the same woman's face across all generations while changing outfits, seasons, and companions. ● Produces warm, soft lighting and photorealistic textures in 8k resolution for professional use. ● Includes atmospheric details like hot chocolate mugs and scarves to amplify emotional warmth. ● Ideal for lifestyle brands, wellness companies, content creators, and anyone building approachable visual identities. ## Prompt

```
A cute woman with rosy cheeks, sitting by a window on a {{season-weather}} day, wearing {{outfit-description}}. She is warmly hugging a large {{companion}} with a red scarf around its neck. A hot chocolate mug sits beside her. The lighting is warm, soft, and cozy, creating an extremely heartwarming and photorealistic atmosphere in 8k resolution. Do not change the woman's face.
```
