# Competitive Landscape Analysis Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitive Landscape Analysis Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces structured competitive intelligence reports for businesses evaluating their market position and identifying growth opportunities. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to identify and profile competitors, assess their strengths and weaknesses, map market trends, and surface differentiation opportunities based on your business profile and analysis scope. The output includes a competitive matrix table, trend assessments with impact ratings, ranked opportunities, and 3-5 prioritized strategic recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for market analysts, business strategists, and founders who need systematic competitor intelligence without manual research overhead. ● Profiles 3-8 competitors with strengths and weaknesses in a structured matrix format ● Maps market trends with explicit impact assessments on your industry segment ● Surfaces ranked growth and differentiation opportunities based on competitive gaps ● Delivers 3-5 prioritized strategic actions synthesized from competitive intelligence findings ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive landscape assessments.

## Task
Produce a comprehensive competitive analysis report that identifies key players, market trends, and growth opportunities for the specified business.

## Context
**Business Profile:**
{{business-profile}}

Include: business type, target market, geographical focus, business size (startup/SME/enterprise), and main product or service offering.

**Analysis Scope:**
{{analysis-scope}}

Specify: number of competitors to analyze (typically 3-8), specific market segments or niches to focus on, time horizon for trend analysis, and any particular strategic questions or concerns.

## Process
1. Identify and profile the main competitors operating in this space
2. Analyze each competitor's core strengths and critical weaknesses
3. Map current market trends and assess their industry impact
4. Surface opportunities for differentiation, growth, or innovation
5. Synthesize findings into an overall competitive landscape SWOT analysis
6. Derive actionable strategic insights

## Output
**Competitive Matrix:**
Present findings in a markdown table with three columns: Competitor Name | Strengths | Weaknesses

**Market Intelligence:**
- **Trends:** Bullet-point list of significant market trends with impact assessment
- **Opportunities:** Bullet-point list of concrete opportunities ranked by potential value
- **Strategic Recommendations:** 3-5 prioritized actions based on the analysis
```
