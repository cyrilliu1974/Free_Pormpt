# Monthly Content Calendar Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Monthly Content Calendar Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a complete month-long publishing schedule for digital marketers, content strategists, and brand managers. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, audience details, and marketing goals, then produces a markdown table with 20-30 scheduled content items - each with a date, content type (blog post, video, social post, email, webinar), topic, platform, and measurable KPIs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing what content formats and distribution channels will maximize reach and engagement while aligning every piece to your stated objectives. Use it when you need to plan a cohesive content strategy that balances variety, maintains brand consistency, and ties output directly to performance metrics. ● Outputs a markdown table with date, content type, topic, platform, and KPIs for 20-30 content items. ● Balances content formats - blog posts, videos, infographics, social media, email, webinars - across distribution channels. ● Aligns every piece to your business goals and audience preferences, with performance indicators mapped to marketing objectives. ● Saves hours of manual planning by generating a cohesive month-long calendar in one prompt execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in content planning and performance optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive monthly content plan that aligns with business objectives and audience preferences. Deliver the plan as a structured markdown table.

## Context
Business and audience context:
{{business-and-audience}}

Marketing objectives and success metrics:
{{goals-and-kpis}}

Analyze current content performance patterns, research trending topics in the relevant industry, and create a diverse content mix that maintains consistent messaging across platforms. Each content piece should have clear success metrics tied to the stated objectives.

## Output
Provide a markdown table with these columns:
- **Date**: Publishing date
- **Content Type**: Format (blog post, video, infographic, social post, email, webinar, etc.)
- **Topic**: Specific subject and angle
- **Platform**: Distribution channel(s)
- **Key Metrics**: Measurable KPIs for this piece

Include 20-30 content items distributed across the month, balancing content types and platforms for optimal reach and engagement.
```
