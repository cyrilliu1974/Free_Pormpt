# Interactive Lesson Plan Generator With 15 Activities

## 簡介

The Interactive Lesson Plan Generator With 15 Activities is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, research-backed lesson plans for educators teaching any topic in any subject area. This interactive lesson plan prompt for ChatGPT produces fifteen distinct hands-on activities complete with required materials, implementation instructions, measurable learning objectives, and a summary planning table with time estimates. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and requires the educator to specify only the topic and subject area. Teachers use it to design active-learning experiences across science, math, language arts, social studies, and vocational subjects without spending hours researching and formatting. Reach for this prompt when you need a full curriculum unit built around experiential learning rather than lectures, or when you want activities that directly convey core concepts through student participation. ● Produces 15 unique hands-on activities tailored to your specific topic and subject, each with granular instructions that eliminate guesswork during classroom delivery. ● Includes a materials list for every activity so educators can gather supplies in advance and budget appropriately. ● Outputs clear, measurable learning objectives aligned to each activity, making it easy to track student progress and standards alignment. ● Provides cited sources for all content, ensuring the lesson plan is grounded in credible research and can withstand administrative review. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator skilled at designing interactive, hands-on learning experiences for students.

## Task
Create a comprehensive lesson plan with 15 engaging, hands-on activities that effectively teach {{topic}} in {{subject}}. Each activity must include materials, step-by-step instructions, and learning objectives.

## Requirements
- Research the topic thoroughly and cite all sources
- Focus on activities that directly convey core concepts through active learning
- Provide sufficient detail so activities can be implemented without additional research
- Ensure all activities are directly relevant to the topic and subject area

## Output Format

**Lesson Plan: Hands-On Activities for Teaching {{topic}} in {{subject}}**

[For each of 15 activities:]

**Activity #: [activity_name]**

**Materials:**
- [material_1]
- [material_2]
- ...

**Instructions:**
1. [step_1]
2. [step_2]
3. ...

**Learning Objectives:**
- [objective_1]
- [objective_2]
- ...

---

**Activity Planning Table:**

| # | Activity Name | Materials | Time | Learning Objectives |
|---|---------------|-----------|------|---------------------|
| 1 | [name] | [materials] | [time] | [objectives] |
| 2 | [name] | [materials] | [time] | [objectives] |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 15 | [name] | [materials] | [time] | [objectives] |

**Sources:**
1. [source_1]
2. [source_2]
3. [source_3]
...
```
