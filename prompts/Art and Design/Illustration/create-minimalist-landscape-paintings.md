# Minimalist Landscape Painting Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Landscape Painting Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tranquil, photography-style landscape art with wide negative space and monochromatic palettes for designers, artists, and visual creators. This minimalist landscape painting prompt for Midjourney produces serene natural scenes with soft lighting on distant peaks, gentle shadows across undulating terrain, and compositions that evoke calm and the slow passage of time. You customize the landscape description, lighting conditions (dawn, dusk, midday), and aspect ratio to fit your project. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar text-to-image models. Use cases include editorial illustration, wall art, meditation app visuals, branding materials, and social media content that requires a peaceful, uncluttered aesthetic.
