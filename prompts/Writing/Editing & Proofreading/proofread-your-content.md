# Proofread Your Content With Expert Copywriting Review

## 簡介

The Proofread Your Content With Expert Copywriting Review is a free AI prompt that evaluates written copy for grammatical correctness, message clarity, brand alignment, and call-to-action effectiveness for marketers, copywriters, and content creators. This proofreading prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your text against a four-part checklist covering grammar and mechanics, message clarity, brand voice consistency, and CTA strength. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce a structured report with specific error identification, concrete improvement suggestions, and numeric scores across impact, readability, and persuasion dimensions. Use it to refine landing pages, email campaigns, social posts, ad copy, or any business messaging before publication. Reach for this prompt when you need more than a spell-checker - when brand tone, persuasive structure, and conversion-focused language matter as much as correct punctuation. ● Flags spelling, punctuation, and grammar errors with specific line-by-line citations ● Evaluates whether messaging is immediately clear and persuasive to the target audience ● Assesses tone consistency with the brand identity present in the copy ● Scores impact, readability, and persuasion on a 1-10 scale with justification for each rating ## Prompt
