# Sales Strategy Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Sales Strategy Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a tailored sales strategy for businesses launching or repositioning a product or service in a defined market. This sales strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product features, target market characteristics, and company strengths to produce a four-part plan: product analysis, company capability assessment, strategic brainstorming, and a final recommended strategy with pricing, channel selection, positioning, and acquisition tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering markdown-formatted output that sales leaders can present to stakeholders or hand to their teams. Use it when you need a structured, consultant-grade sales plan without hiring an outside firm. ● Produces a product analysis that maps features to target-market pain points and needs ● Evaluates how company strengths and capabilities support the sales objectives ● Recommends pricing, promotional tactics, sales channels, partnerships, and competitive positioning ● Delivers a final actionable strategy summary with reasoning for every recommendation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced sales strategy consultant developing a comprehensive sales strategy for a specific product or service.

## Task
Analyze the provided product, target market, and company strengths to create a tailored sales strategy covering pricing, sales channels, positioning, and customer acquisition.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Market:** {{target-market}}

**Company Strengths:** {{company-strengths}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy in four sections using markdown:

### Product Analysis
Describe the key features and benefits of the product/service and how it addresses the target market's needs and pain points.

### Company Analysis
Examine the company's unique strengths, capabilities, and assets, and how these support successful sales of this product/service.

### Strategy Brainstorm
Identify key strategic elements to consider:
- Pricing and promotional strategies
- Sales channels and partnerships
- Sales process and tactics
- Competitive positioning and differentiation
- Customer acquisition and retention approaches

### Recommended Strategy
Provide a clear, actionable summary including:
- Target customers and value proposition
- Pricing and promotional approach
- Sales channels, process, and tactics
- Positioning and competitive advantage

Ensure all recommendations build on the analyses above, align with the target market, leverage company strengths, and address customer needs. Include reasoning for each recommendation.
```
