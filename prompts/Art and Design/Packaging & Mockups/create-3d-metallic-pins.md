# 3D Metallic Pin Product Card Generator

## 簡介

The 3D Metallic Pin Product Card Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic renders of metallic icon pins attached to retail-ready product cards for designers and product marketers. This 3D product mockup prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces ultra-glossy chrome icon designs mounted on vertical paper cards with die-cut euro holes, bold titles, and taglines. You define the icon concept (game controller, coffee cup, rocket) and card text, and the prompt renders a studio-lit composition with soft reflections, clean shadows, and a minimal gray background. Use it to prototype enamel pin packaging, create product listing images for e-commerce, or pitch collectible merchandise concepts without physical samples. Reach for this prompt when you need realistic product card mockups for pins, badges, or small metal accessories before manufacturing. ● Produces 3D chrome icon pins with smooth rounded geometry and futuristic styling ● Includes die-cut euro-hole card backing with customizable title and tagline text ● Renders soft studio lighting, subtle reflections, and clean drop shadows automatically ● Outputs minimal compositions suitable for e-commerce listings, pitch decks, or social media ## Prompt
