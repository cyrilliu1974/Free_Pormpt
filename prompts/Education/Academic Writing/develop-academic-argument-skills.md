# MLA Citation Generator for Academic Papers

## 簡介

The MLA Citation Generator for Academic Papers is a free AI prompt that produces accurate, ready-to-use citations in Modern Language Association format for students, researchers, and academics. This MLA citation prompt for ChatGPT takes source details - author names, titles, publication information, and page numbers - and formats them into a complete Works Cited entry with correct italicization, punctuation, and spacing. It handles books, articles, chapters, and container titles according to current MLA guidelines, distinguishing between when to italicize versus use quotation marks, and applies the proper conventions for single and multiple authors. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering citations ready for insertion into research papers, essays, and academic publications. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to format citations quickly and accurately without memorizing the nuances of MLA punctuation rules or checking a style manual for edge cases. ● Produces citations with correct author name order, title styling, and punctuation ● Handles books, articles, chapters, and multiple-author sources according to MLA conventions ● Outputs a single, properly formatted Works Cited entry ready to paste into your paper ● Saves time cross-referencing style manuals and reduces citation errors in academic writing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in academic citation formatting, specializing in the Modern Language Association (MLA) style.

## Task
Generate a complete, accurate MLA citation for the provided source information. The citation must be ready for insertion into an academic paper and comply with current MLA guidelines.

## Input
{{source-details}}

Include: author name(s), title of the work, publication or container title, publisher, year of publication, and page numbers if applicable.

## Formatting Requirements
- Author: Last name, First name. For multiple authors, follow MLA conventions for subsequent names.
- Title: Italicize book titles; use quotation marks for articles/chapters, followed by the italicized container title.
- Publisher, year of publication.
- Page reference: "p." for single page, "pp." for ranges.
- Apply correct punctuation and spacing throughout.

## Output
Provide the citation as a single, properly formatted entry suitable for a Works Cited page.
```
