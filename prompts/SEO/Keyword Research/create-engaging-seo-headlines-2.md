# SEO Headline Generator for Newsletter Articles

## 簡介

The SEO Headline Generator for Newsletter Articles is a free AI prompt that produces five keyword-optimized, audience-targeted headlines for newsletter content. This headline generator prompt for ChatGPT works by applying proven copywriting formulas - how-to structures, listicles, curiosity-driven questions, and mistake-avoidance angles - while positioning your primary keyword naturally near the beginning of each headline. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering five distinct headlines per run that stay within the 55-60 character SEO sweet spot and vary in structure to give you options for A/B testing or platform-specific publishing. Content creators, newsletter editors, and digital marketers use it to turn article topics and target audience descriptions into click-worthy titles that rank and convert. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple headline options quickly, each balancing search engine requirements with human appeal. ● Applies five different headline formulas - how-to, listicle, question, mistake-avoidance, and urgency - so every output offers structural variety ● Positions primary keywords near the front of each headline for maximum SEO impact without forced or awkward phrasing ● Keeps headlines within 55-60 character limits to prevent truncation in search engine results pages ● Tailors tone and angle to your specified target audience, ensuring relevance and resonance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter and SEO strategist specializing in audience engagement and search engine optimization.

## Task
Generate five captivating headlines for a newsletter article that balance creativity, reader engagement, and SEO best practices. Each headline must incorporate relevant keywords naturally while remaining compelling and accurate to the content.

## Context
{{article-topic-and-keywords}}

{{target-audience}}

## Approach
- Position the primary keyword toward the beginning of each headline where it flows naturally
- Apply proven headline formulas:
  - How-to headlines that offer solutions
  - Listicles promising quick, digestible insights
  - Questions that spark curiosity
  - Common mistakes or pitfalls to avoid
- Ensure headlines evoke curiosity, surprise, or urgency
- Keep headlines accurate to the article content and within optimal SEO character limits (55-60 characters when possible)
- Vary the formula and approach across all five headlines

## Output
Provide five distinct, SEO-optimized headlines numbered 1-5. Each headline should:
- Incorporate the primary keyword naturally
- Engage the target audience
- Use a different structural approach
- Be unique and attention-grabbing
```
