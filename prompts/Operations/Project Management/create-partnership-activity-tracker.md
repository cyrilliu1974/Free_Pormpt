# Partnership Activity Tracker Template Generator

## 簡介

The Partnership Activity Tracker Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a customized monitoring system for businesses managing multiple partner relationships and collaborative initiatives. This partnership activity tracker prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a detailed table that organizes partner names, activities, specific measurable goals, and outcome statuses with visual indicators. The output includes categorized activity types relevant to your industry, SMART goal-setting recommendations aligned with business objectives, evaluation criteria for measuring partnership success, and prioritized next steps. Real use cases include tracking co-marketing campaigns, monitoring product integrations, managing joint events, and evaluating referral program performance across vendor and strategic alliance portfolios. This prompt is for partnership managers, business development teams, and strategists who need a centralized view of collaboration health and ROI across multiple active partnerships. ● Organizes partner activities in a clear table format with status emojis for at-a-glance progress tracking ● Provides industry-specific activity categories and SMART goal-setting guidance tailored to the business context ● Includes concrete evaluation metrics covering revenue impact, engagement data, strategic fit, and relationship quality ● Delivers prioritized action items to strengthen underperforming partnerships and scale successful collaborations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specialized in partnership activity analysis and optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive partnership activity tracker table for {{company-name}}. The tracker should help monitor ongoing collaborations, set clear objectives, and measure results to maximize ROI.

## Output
Deliver a structured report containing:

**Partnership Activity Tracker for {{company-name}}**

| Partner Name | Activity | Goal | Outcome |
|--------------|----------|------|---------|
| [Partner 1] | [Activity description] | [Specific measurable goal] | [Status emoji + result] |
| [Partner 2] | [Activity description] | [Specific measurable goal] | [Status emoji + result] |
| [Partner 3] | [Activity description] | [Specific measurable goal] | [Status emoji + result] |

Key:
✅ = Completed/Successful
⏳ = In Progress
❌ = Incomplete/Unsuccessful

**Activity Types:**
List 5-7 common partnership activity categories relevant to the company's industry (e.g., co-marketing campaigns, product integrations, joint events, referral programs).

**Goal Setting Tips:**
Provide 4-5 actionable recommendations for setting SMART partnership goals that align with business objectives.

**Measuring Outcomes:**
Define 3-4 key criteria and metrics for evaluating partnership success (revenue impact, engagement metrics, strategic alignment, relationship health).

**Next Steps:**
Outline 3-4 prioritized action items to improve partnership management and results.

Ensure all goals are specific and measurable. Focus on actionable insights that drive successful collaborations.
```
