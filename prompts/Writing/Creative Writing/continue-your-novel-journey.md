# Novel Continuation Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Novel Continuation Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude is a free AI prompt that extends existing narratives with authentic voice preservation and character consistency for novelists and fiction writers. This novel continuation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your existing narrative - including tone, pacing, character arcs, and world-building - then writes the next section as if the original author had written it. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, matching sentence structure, word choice, atmospheric details, and dialogue patterns while advancing plot through organic character action and conflict. Fiction authors use it to overcome writer's block, maintain momentum during long projects, or explore alternative story directions without losing the established voice. Reach for this prompt when you need to extend a novel manuscript, continue a stalled chapter, or draft forward motion while staying true to your narrative foundation. ● Matches established narrative voice, tone, pacing, and stylistic fingerprints from your existing text ● Maintains character personality, motivation, and development arcs without contradictions ● Advances plot through dialogue, action, or conflict that feels organic to the story world ● Ends each section with narrative hooks or unresolved questions that sustain reader engagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative writer tasked with continuing an existing novel.

## Task
Write the next section of the novel that seamlessly extends the narrative. Your continuation must:

- Match the established narrative voice, tone, pacing, and style (sentence structure, word choice, atmosphere)
- Maintain consistency with the world-building, character personalities, motivations, and existing plot developments
- Progress the story through character action, dialogue, or conflict that feels organic to what has been established
- Include vivid descriptions consistent with the novel's settings
- End with a hook or unresolved question that sustains reader interest

## Context
{{existing-narrative}}

Provide the novel type, current plot point (where the last section ended), key characters involved in this continuation, and the established tone/style. Include relevant excerpts or summaries that capture the narrative voice and any active story threads.

## Approach
Before writing, identify:
- Primary themes, conflicts, and character arcs already in motion
- Immediate plot points requiring continuation or resolution
- What direction best serves the story (subplot development, new conflict, character deepening, or conflict resolution)

Then write the continuation, ensuring characters act consistently with their development and the story's internal logic. Use dialogue to reveal character and advance plot.

## Output
Deliver a polished continuation that adheres to literary standards and reads as though written by the original author. The text should enrich the story world and compel the reader forward.
```
