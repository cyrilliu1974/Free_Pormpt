# Competitor Analysis Prompt for Digital Marketing

## 簡介

The Competitor Analysis Prompt for Digital Marketing is a free AI prompt that evaluates and compares competitors' digital strategies across online presence, content, SEO, social media, and paid advertising for marketing strategists and business analysts. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a systematic evaluation of rival businesses, producing five structured markdown tables - one per marketing dimension - alongside actionable recommendations that highlight exploitable gaps and tactics worth emulating. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering comparative assessments of website quality, content frequency, keyword targeting, engagement rates, and ad spend estimates. Use it when planning market entry, refining positioning, or preparing quarterly strategy reviews. ● Organizes findings into five comparative tables covering online presence, content strategy, SEO, social media, and paid advertising ● Surfaces relative strengths, weaknesses, and distinctive tactics for each competitor in a side-by-side format ● Synthesizes observations into 3-5 actionable recommendations that identify market gaps and best practices to adopt ● Uses comparative language to clarify positioning rather than isolated descriptions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist conducting a competitive analysis.

## Task
Analyze and compare the digital marketing strategies of competitors in {{business-and-competitors}}, delivering a structured competitive intelligence report.

## Context
Evaluate each competitor across five core dimensions:
- **Online presence**: website quality, user experience, brand positioning, technical performance
- **Content strategy**: blog frequency, topic coverage, content formats, value proposition
- **SEO efforts**: keyword targeting, domain authority, backlink profile, on-page optimization
- **Social media activities**: platform mix, engagement rates, posting cadence, community building
- **Paid advertising**: visible campaigns, platforms used, creative approaches, estimated spend

For each dimension, identify strengths, weaknesses, and distinctive tactics that differentiate each competitor.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as markdown tables:

1. Create one table per marketing dimension (five tables total), with columns for each competitor
2. Populate cells with concise observations, metrics where available, and qualitative assessments
3. End with a **Recommendations** section in prose format that synthesizes findings into 3-5 actionable strategic priorities, explicitly noting gaps your business can exploit and strengths worth emulating

Use comparative language that highlights relative positioning, not absolute descriptions.
```
