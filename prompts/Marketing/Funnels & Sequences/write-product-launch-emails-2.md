# Product Launch Email Sequence Builder

## 簡介

The Product Launch Email Sequence Builder is a free AI prompt that creates timed email campaigns to drive pre-orders and build momentum for product launches. This product launch email sequence prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating 4-6 strategically timed emails that balance information, social proof, urgency, and persuasion. You provide your product details, launch date, target audience, and unique selling proposition, and the prompt structures a complete sequence with optimized subject lines, preview text, key content points, and targeted calls-to-action for each email. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for email marketers, product managers, and founders preparing go-to-market campaigns. The sequence varies messaging angles across emails - educational content, customer testimonials, scarcity triggers, and special offers - while building toward launch day. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete email funnel for a product release, whether you're launching a SaaS feature, physical product, course, or service offering. ● Delivers 4-6 emails with subject lines, preview text, content frameworks, and CTAs tailored to each stage of your launch timeline ● Builds anticipation systematically by varying email angles across information, social proof, urgency triggers, and special offers ● Includes segmentation guidance to address different customer motivations and pain points within the same sequence ● Optimizes each email for coherent messaging flow, high open rates, and clear conversion paths to pre-orders or launch signups ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an email marketing strategist specializing in product launch campaigns that drive pre-orders and build anticipation.

## Task
Create a strategic email sequence for a product launch. Each email should build momentum toward the launch date, balancing information, urgency, and persuasion.

## Context
**Product & Launch Details:**
{{product-and-launch}}
(Include: product name, what it does, launch date, and any pre-order incentives or bonuses)

**Target Audience & Value Proposition:**
{{audience-and-usp}}
(Describe who you're reaching, their pain points or desires, and why this product uniquely solves for them)

## Requirements
- Structure each email with clear, coherent messaging that flows logically from hook to close
- Vary the angle: informational content, social proof, urgency triggers, and special offers across the sequence
- Time the sequence to build anticipation, peaking at launch
- Segment considerations: address different customer motivations within the sequence
- Optimize subject lines and preview text for high open rates

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of emails in chronological order. For each email, provide:

**Email [Number]: [Sequence Position Title]**
- **Subject Line:**
- **Preview Text:**
- **Main Content Points:** (3-5 key messages or sections)
- **Call-to-Action:** (specific action and urgency framing)

Include 4-6 emails spanning the pre-launch period through launch day.
```
