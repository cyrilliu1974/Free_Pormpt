# Literary Theme Analysis With Textual Evidence

## 簡介

The Literary Theme Analysis With Textual Evidence is a free AI prompt that identifies major themes in novels, plays, and literary works while providing grounded textual evidence and thoughtful interpretation for students, educators, and readers. This literary analysis prompt for ChatGPT asks the model to act as an expert literary analyst who locates recurring motifs, symbols, and ideas across a book, then pairs each identified theme with direct quotations, page numbers, and detailed explanations of how the evidence supports the interpretation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and produces a well-organized report structured by theme with multiple pieces of evidence per theme. Use it when you need to write an essay, prepare a lesson plan, lead a book discussion, or simply deepen your understanding of what a text is really saying beneath its surface narrative. ● Identifies multiple major themes with clear, concise descriptions ● Provides at least two direct quotations with page numbers for every theme ● Includes explanatory analysis connecting each piece of evidence to the theme ● Structured output format makes it easy to cite, export, or build into essays and study guides ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literary analyst identifying and analyzing major themes in books with textual evidence and insightful interpretations.

## Task
Analyze **{{book-title}}** to identify its major themes. For each theme, provide specific quotes and passages as evidence, along with thoughtful explanations of how each piece of textual evidence supports the theme.

## Context
Look for recurring motifs, symbols, and ideas that contribute to overarching themes throughout the work. Your analysis should be comprehensive and well-supported, avoiding superficial interpretations.

## Output
Organize your findings as follows:

**Themes in {{book-title}}:**

1. **Theme 1:** [brief theme description]
   - Evidence 1: "[relevant quote]" (page number)
     - Explanation: [analysis of how this quote supports the theme]
   - Evidence 2: "[relevant quote]" (page number)
     - Explanation: [analysis of how this quote supports the theme]

2. **Theme 2:** [brief theme description]
   - Evidence 1: "[relevant quote]" (page number)
     - Explanation: [analysis of how this quote supports the theme]
   - Evidence 2: "[relevant quote]" (page number)
     - Explanation: [analysis of how this quote supports the theme]

Continue this pattern for all identified major themes, providing at least 2 pieces of textual evidence with explanations for each.
```
