# Affiliate Keyword Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The Affiliate Keyword Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that delivers strategic keyword analysis tailored to affiliate marketing campaigns for any niche and audience. This affiliate keyword research prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown report containing at least 20 prioritized keywords with search volume, competition metrics, commercial intent scores, and CPC data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing each keyword for target audience fit, product promotion opportunities, seasonal trends, long-tail variations, and quick-win potential. The output includes tactical recommendations for content creation, on-page SEO, link building, and conversion optimization, plus future-proofing strategies that account for emerging trends, algorithm changes, and search behavior shifts. Use this prompt when you need data-driven keyword insights that directly support affiliate revenue growth and campaign planning. ● Generates a prioritized table of 20+ keywords ranked by a composite Priority Score factoring search volume, competition, and commercial intent ● Delivers keyword-specific insights including target audience segments, best product categories, seasonal timing, and long-tail variations ● Provides tactical recommendations across content strategy, on-page optimization, link building, and conversion rate optimization ● Includes future-proofing analysis covering emerging trends, algorithm evolution, and diversification strategies to reduce risk ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a keyword research specialist focused on affiliate marketing strategy. Conduct comprehensive keyword analysis that identifies high-commercial-intent opportunities and delivers actionable campaign recommendations.

## Task
Generate a strategic keyword research report for {{niche}} that prioritizes keywords by affiliate marketing potential. Analyze search demand, competition dynamics, and conversion opportunity to guide content strategy, optimization tactics, and campaign planning.

## Context
Target niche: {{niche}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Affiliate marketing goals: {{affiliate-goals}}

Focus on keywords that balance search volume with commercial intent and realistic ranking opportunity. Prioritize insights that directly support affiliate revenue growth.

## Output
Deliver a markdown-formatted report with these sections:

### 1. Keyword Overview
Provide a table with minimum 20 keywords, sorted by Priority Score (highest to lowest):

| Keyword | Search Volume | Competition Level | Commercial Intent | Suggested CPC | Priority Score |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|---------------|----------------|

### 2. Keyword Insights
For each high-priority keyword, provide:
- 🎯 Target audience segment
- 💼 Best product categories for promotion
- 📈 Seasonal trends and timing opportunities
- 🔄 Related and long-tail keyword variations
- ⚡ Quick win potential (low-hanging fruit assessment)

### 3. Actionable Recommendations
Provide tactical recommendations across:
- **Content creation strategies**: Topics, formats, and angles that leverage identified keywords
- **On-page optimization**: Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking
- **Link building tactics**: Outreach angles and linkable asset opportunities
- **Conversion rate optimization**: Landing page elements, CTAs, and trust signals for affiliate conversions

### 4. Future-Proofing Strategies
Address:
- Emerging trends in the niche that signal keyword opportunity shifts
- Anticipated changes in consumer search behavior or preferences
- Algorithm evolution considerations (AI Overviews, E-E-A-T, helpful content signals)
- Diversification recommendations to reduce keyword concentration risk

Ensure all analysis is data-driven and directly tied to maximizing affiliate marketing ROI.
```
