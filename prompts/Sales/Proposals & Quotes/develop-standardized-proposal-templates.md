# Sales Proposal Template Builder for Multiple Clients

## 簡介

The Sales Proposal Template Builder is a free AI prompt that creates standardized, adaptable sales proposal frameworks for teams who need consistency without sacrificing customization. This sales proposal template prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a seven-section outline built on dependency grammar principles - each element logically supports the next. You provide your industry and audience, unique selling points, and typical project scope; the prompt returns a complete template with placeholder text, bracketed customization guidance, and clear calls-to-action. Sales teams use it to cut proposal development time while maintaining high-quality, client-focused messaging across deals. The output emphasizes client benefits over features, integrates differentiators naturally, and includes sections for executive summary, client objectives, proposed solution, value proposition, implementation plan, pricing, and next steps. Reach for this prompt when your team writes multiple proposals and needs a repeatable structure that preserves persuasive flow while allowing fast adaptation to individual client contexts. ● Produces a seven-section proposal outline with logical dependencies, placeholder examples, and inline customization notes ● Structures every section to lead with client outcomes and benefits, not product features ● Integrates your unique selling points where they solve specific client pain points ● Includes action-oriented calls-to-action at decision points to drive next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert proposal writer specializing in scalable, reusable sales templates.

## Task
Create a comprehensive sales proposal template structured for clarity, logical flow, and adaptability. The template must be flexible enough to serve multiple clients while maintaining consistency.

## Context
**Industry and audience:**  
{{industry-and-audience}}  
(e.g., "B2B SaaS selling to mid-market HR directors" or "construction services for commercial real estate developers")

**Unique selling points:**  
{{usp}}  
(the 2–4 differentiators that win deals for you)

**Typical project scope:**  
{{project-scope}}  
(deliverables, timeline, team size, and any recurring scope patterns)

## Output
Deliver a **structured outline** using headings and subheadings. Each section of the template should:

- Follow dependency grammar principles—every element logically supports the next
- Lead with client benefits and outcomes, not features
- Integrate the unique selling points naturally where they solve client pain points
- Include clear, action-oriented calls-to-action at decision points
- Provide [bracketed guidance] for customization without disrupting the template structure

Structure the template to include:

1. **Executive Summary** – problem, solution, and value in under 200 words  
2. **Client Situation & Objectives** – demonstrate understanding of their context  
3. **Proposed Solution** – approach, methodology, deliverables  
4. **Value Proposition & Differentiation** – why this solution, why you  
5. **Implementation Plan** – timeline, milestones, responsibilities  
6. **Investment & Terms** – pricing, payment structure, assumptions  
7. **Next Steps & Call-to-Action** – specific action with timeline

Ensure each section contains placeholder text examples and [customization notes] so sales teams can adapt quickly while preserving persuasive flow.
```
