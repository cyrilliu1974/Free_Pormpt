# Social Media Teaser Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Social Media Teaser Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates high-conversion teaser content tailored to your audience, brand voice, and campaign goals. This social media teaser prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your target audience and product, identifies the strongest hooks and selling points, then produces 3-5 platform-specific teasers optimized for Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Facebook. Each teaser respects character limits, platform culture, and best practices while using persuasive, action-oriented language designed to drive clicks, shares, and conversions. Marketing teams use it to launch product campaigns, announce events, tease content releases, and build anticipation across social channels without starting from a blank page. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple platform-ready variations quickly, or when you want to maintain brand consistency while adapting tone and format to each network's unique audience expectations. ● Produces 3-5 teasers labeled by platform, each with copy, hashtags, and format tailored to that network's technical and cultural norms. ● Aligns every teaser with your specified campaign goal, whether that is driving traffic, building awareness, or prompting a specific conversion action. ● Incorporates your brand voice consistently across platforms while adjusting tone to match each audience's expectations. ● Focuses on curiosity-driven hooks and emotion to maximize engagement, shares, and click-through rates. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in high-conversion teaser content.

## Task
Create compelling, platform-specific social media teasers that drive the target audience to take action. Each teaser should spark curiosity, evoke emotion, and maximize engagement while respecting platform character limits and best practices.

## Context
**Target audience & product**: {{audience-and-product}}

**Brand voice**: {{brand-voice}}

**Campaign goal**: {{campaign-goal}}

For each teaser:
- Identify the strongest hook or selling point for the audience
- Use persuasive, action-oriented language
- Include relevant hashtags where appropriate
- Tailor tone, format, and length to the platform's culture and technical constraints
- Prioritize clarity and immediate impact

## Output
Provide 3–5 teasers in bullet point format, each clearly labeled for its intended platform (e.g., Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook). Structure each teaser with the copy first, followed by any hashtags or platform-specific elements.
```
