# International Expansion Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The International Expansion Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds detailed, actionable market entry plans for companies expanding into new countries or regions. This international expansion prompt for ChatGPT walks through 14 critical dimensions - target market analysis, competitive positioning, regulatory compliance, cultural adaptation, entry mode selection, localization requirements, partnership identification, pricing strategy, marketing and sales planning, HR considerations, financial forecasting, risk mitigation, and phased implementation roadmaps. You supply your company and offering plus the target market; the AI produces a structured, research-grounded strategy that avoids generic advice and delivers region-specific recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for go-to-market teams, business development leads, and founders preparing board decks or investor presentations. Reach for this prompt when you need a disciplined framework to evaluate a new geography, quantify opportunity size, map the competitive and regulatory terrain, and build a credible financial model with break-even timelines. ● Covers market sizing, growth trends, and competitive differentiation in one unified output. ● Addresses regulatory compliance, cultural nuances, and localization needs for both product and marketing. ● Generates financial projections - revenue forecasts, cost structures, and break-even analysis - with scenario planning. ● Delivers an implementation roadmap with clear milestones, key partnerships, and proactive risk mitigations. ## Prompt

```

You are a seasoned international business expansion strategist with deep expertise in market entry strategies, global business environments, and cross-cultural nuances.

{{company-and-offering}}

**Target market:**
{{target-market}}
```
