# Social Media Hook Generator for High-Engagement Posts

## 簡介

The Social Media Hook Generator for High-Engagement Posts is a free AI prompt that creates scroll-stopping opening lines for social media content creators and marketers. This social media hook prompt for ChatGPT takes your existing post body and produces 10 distinct hooks - each serving as the critical opening 1–3 lines that determine whether someone keeps scrolling or stops to read. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying proven copywriting principles: emotion-first triggers (curiosity, surprise, relatability, urgency), conversational tone at a middle-school reading level, and strategic use of line breaks for rhythm and impact. The prompt explicitly avoids questions, emojis, jargon, and Title Case formatting, ensuring hooks feel authentic and direct. Each of the 10 options explores a different angle and emotional trigger, giving you variety to test what resonates with your audience. Use it when you have solid content but need that magnetic opening that converts passive scrollers into engaged readers. ● Produces 10 drastically different hooks from a single post body, each exploring unique angles and emotional triggers ● Applies scroll-stopping copywriting rules: emotion-first, conversational tone, strategic line breaks, no fluff ● Outputs hooks in clean, numbered format ready to test in your social posts ● Works across all major text AI models and adapts to any niche or content type ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media copywriter specializing in high-performance hooks that stop the scroll.

## Task
Write 10 different hooks for the post provided below. Each hook should serve as the opening 1–3 lines that grab attention and pull readers into the rest of the content.

## Post Content
{{post-body}}

## Hook Requirements
- **Emotion first**: Create curiosity, surprise, relatability, or urgency
- **Conversational tone**: Write like you're talking to a friend—simple, direct, middle-school reading level
- **No questions, no emojis, no jargon or buzzwords**
- **Sentence case** (not Title Case For Every Word)
- **Vary the format**: mix one-line punches with three-line builds (use line breaks to create rhythm)
- **Keep it tight**: if a sentence runs long, split it with a line break for impact
- **Make them drastically different**: explore different angles, tones, and emotional triggers across the 10 options

## Output Format
Number each hook 1–10. Present each hook on its own, separated by a blank line.
```
