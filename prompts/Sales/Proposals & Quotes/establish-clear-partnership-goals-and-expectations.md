# Partnership Goals and Expectations Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Partnership Goals and Expectations Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured partner relationship management plans for sales teams and partnership managers. This partnership management prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context - industry, target market, team capacity, and partnership maturity - and produces a clear markdown table with three columns: Partnership Goals, Expectations, and Action Items. Each row represents one cohesive strategy, with SMART goals (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound) paired with clearly defined responsibilities for both your sales team and your partners, plus concrete action steps that connect the two. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams already working inside those platforms. Use it when you need to align internal teams with external partners, establish clarity around joint objectives, or document a strategic framework that prevents misalignment and wasted effort. ● Produces 4–6 complete partnership strategies, each with goal, expectation, and action in one cohesive row ● Applies SMART criteria to every goal, ensuring measurability and time-bound accountability ● Defines mutual expectations so both sales teams and partners know their responsibilities ● Outputs in markdown table format for easy sharing in Notion, Slack, Google Docs, or Monday.com ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales partnership manager designing a structured framework for partner relationship management.

## Task
Create a comprehensive partnership management plan organized as a markdown table with three columns: **Partnership Goals**, **Expectations**, and **Action Items**. Each row should represent one cohesive partner relationship strategy.

## Context
{{business-context}}

For each partnership strategy:

1. Define SMART goals (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound) that address current challenges and move toward desired outcomes
2. Establish clear expectations for both the sales team and partners
3. Specify concrete action items that align goals with expectations
4. Ensure strategies are tailored to the industry, target market, team capacity, and partnership maturity

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with this structure:

| Partnership Goals | Expectations | Action Items |
|-------------------|--------------|---------------|
| [SMART goal 1] | [Clear responsibilities for team & partners] | [Specific actions to achieve goal] |
| [SMART goal 2] | [Clear responsibilities for team & partners] | [Specific actions to achieve goal] |

Include 4–6 partnership strategies that comprehensively address the business context provided.
```
