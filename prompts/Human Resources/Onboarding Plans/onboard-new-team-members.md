# Onboarding Plan Generator for New Team Members

## 簡介

The Onboarding Plan Generator for New Team Members is a free AI prompt that creates actionable, chronological onboarding plans for HR managers and team leads bringing new employees into an organization. This onboarding plan prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your company details and role context and produces a complete timeline covering pre-arrival preparations, first-day orientation, week-one training, first-month milestones, and long-term integration strategies. The output includes specific tasks, time allocations, checkpoints, and success metrics that support employee retention and productivity. Use it when hiring for any role, across any department, in companies of any size - whether you're onboarding a software engineer at a startup or a sales manager at an enterprise firm. ● Breaks onboarding into clear phases: pre-arrival, day one, first week, first month, and months two through three-plus ● Includes specific preparation tasks like paperwork, equipment setup, workspace readiness, and team notifications ● Provides chronological first-day and first-week schedules with time allocations for training, introductions, and systems access ● Outputs measurable success metrics to track onboarding effectiveness and new-hire experience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Human Resources Manager specializing in employee onboarding and organizational development.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step onboarding plan for new team members that is clear, organized, and immediately actionable.

## Context
Company & Role Details:
{{company-and-role-context}}
(Include: specific role being hired for, company type, industry, company size, and desired onboarding duration)

The plan should address:
- Pre-arrival preparations (paperwork, equipment, workspace setup, team notifications)
- First-day activities (welcome, orientation, introductions, initial training)
- First-week schedule (role-specific training, systems access, early tasks)
- First-month milestones (project assignments, check-ins, performance expectations)
- Long-term integration strategies (ongoing development, mentorship, cultural assimilation)

Ensure the plan is scalable across departments, adaptable to different roles, and designed to create a positive employee experience that supports retention and productivity.

## Output
Deliver the onboarding plan using this structure:

### Pre-Arrival (Before Day 1)
- Bullet points for each preparation task

### Day 1: Welcome & Orientation
- Chronological activities with time allocations

### Week 1: Foundation Building
- Daily or activity-based breakdown

### Month 1: Integration & Early Contributions
- Weekly milestones and checkpoints

### Months 2-3+: Long-Term Success
- Ongoing development and evaluation touchpoints

### Success Metrics
- Key indicators to measure onboarding effectiveness

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout for maximum readability and implementation ease.
```
