# Brand Voice Guidelines Generator for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Brand Voice Guidelines Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a complete brand voice framework for marketing teams seeking unified communication across all channels. This brand voice guidelines prompt for ChatGPT takes your company profile and produces a structured markdown table that defines your brand's tone, personality traits, core messages, and concrete usage examples. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver immediately actionable guidelines that ensure every team member – from social media managers to customer service representatives – communicates with the same brand voice. Use it when launching a rebrand, onboarding new marketing team members, or standardizing communication across departments and channels. ● Produces a four-column guidelines table covering tone, personality, key messages, and real-world examples ● Specifies when different tones apply across channels and communication contexts ● Defines 3-5 distinctive brand personality traits with explanations ● Includes before/after samples and specific phrases for social media, email, ads, and customer service ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist building a comprehensive Brand Voice Guidelines document.

## Task
Create a detailed set of guidelines that establish consistent brand identity across all marketing channels and storytelling. The guidelines must capture the brand's voice, tone, personality, and key messages in a format that marketing teams can immediately apply to any communication.

## Context
Company and market context:
{{company-profile}}

Include company name, target audience, industry, brand values, and main competitors.

## Output
Deliver the brand voice guidelines as a markdown table with these columns:

| Tone | Personality | Key Messages | Examples |
|------|-------------|--------------|----------|

**Requirements for each column:**

- **Tone**: Describe the emotional quality and style of communication (e.g., professional yet approachable, bold and confident, warm and empathetic). Specify when different tones apply across channels.
- **Personality**: Define the brand's character traits as if it were a person. Include 3-5 distinctive attributes with brief explanations.
- **Key Messages**: List the core ideas and value propositions that should appear consistently across communications. Prioritize by importance.
- **Examples**: Provide concrete before/after samples or specific phrases that demonstrate the voice in action across different contexts (social media, email, ads, customer service).

Ensure every guideline is actionable and specific enough that different team members will produce consistent brand communications.
```
