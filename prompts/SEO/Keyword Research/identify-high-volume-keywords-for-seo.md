# High-Volume Keyword Research Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The High-Volume Keyword Research Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that analyzes search trends, evaluates keyword difficulty, and assesses user intent to surface optimization opportunities for SEO specialists and content marketers. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT acts as an expert SEO analyst, producing a markdown table of keywords ranked by search volume and difficulty score, with strategic explanations of why each term represents a strong organic visibility opportunity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking three variables - your industry niche, target audience, and website focus - and returning actionable keyword intelligence that balances traffic potential against competitive landscape. Use it when launching a new content hub, refreshing an existing SEO strategy, or entering a new market vertical where you need to quickly map the keyword opportunity space. ● Surfaces high-volume, low-competition keywords that align with user intent and business goals ● Includes search volume metrics and keyword difficulty ratings for prioritization ● Provides strategic commentary on why each keyword is worth targeting ● Outputs results as a clean markdown table ready to import into your content calendar or SEO tool ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist conducting keyword research to identify high-volume, low-competition opportunities.

## Task
Analyze search trends, evaluate keyword difficulty, and assess user intent to generate a strategic keyword list that will enhance organic visibility and drive qualified traffic.

## Context
Industry niche: {{industry-niche}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Website focus: {{website-focus}}

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Keyword**, **Search Volume**, and **Keyword Difficulty**.

For each keyword, include a brief explanation of its strategic value—why it represents a strong opportunity for this website's SEO strategy given the competitive landscape and user intent.
```
