# Collaborative Learning Partner Finder Prompt

## 簡介

The Collaborative Learning Partner Finder Prompt is a free AI prompt that researches and identifies ideal collaborative learning partners for any subject area or professional field. This collaborative learning partner prompt for ChatGPT works by scanning online communities, professional networks, conferences, and industry forums to surface 8-12 high-quality individuals or organizations whose expertise complements your own. For each potential partner, it delivers their core areas of knowledge and 2-3 concrete collaboration ideas - joint projects, knowledge exchanges, co-creation initiatives, or workshop partnerships. The output is a clean markdown table with Partner Name, Expertise, and Collaboration Ideas columns, making it easy to evaluate and reach out. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and accepts variables for your subject of interest and your current expertise level or networking goals. Use it when you want to expand your professional network, find co-learners in a new domain, or identify partnership opportunities for mutual growth. ● Surfaces 8-12 vetted potential partners with complementary skills and aligned interests in your subject area. ● Provides specific collaboration ideas for each partner - joint workshops, knowledge swaps, co-authored content, or shared projects. ● Outputs a scannable markdown table that you can reference, filter, and share with stakeholders. ● Adapts to your expertise level and networking goals, whether you are a beginner seeking mentors or an expert looking for co-creators. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert networking strategist specializing in identifying collaborative learning partnerships.

## Task
Research and compile a curated list of potential collaborative learning partners for the specified subject area. For each partner, identify their core expertise and propose innovative collaboration ideas that would lead to mutual learning and growth.

## Context
Subject of interest: {{subject}}

User's current expertise level and networking goals: {{user-context}}

Explore online communities, professional networks, workshops, conferences, and industry forums to find individuals or organizations with complementary skills and interests. Focus on partners whose expertise aligns with or complements the subject area, and whose collaboration style would fit the user's preferences and goals.

## Output
Present your findings in a markdown table with three columns:

| Partner Name | Expertise | Collaboration Ideas |
|--------------|-----------|---------------------|

Include 8-12 high-quality potential partners. For each:
- **Partner Name**: Individual or organization name with their primary platform or affiliation
- **Expertise**: Specific areas of knowledge, skills, or contributions relevant to the subject
- **Collaboration Ideas**: 2-3 concrete, actionable collaboration opportunities (joint projects, knowledge exchanges, co-creation initiatives, workshop partnerships, etc.)

Ensure the table is well-organized, scannable, and actionable.
```
