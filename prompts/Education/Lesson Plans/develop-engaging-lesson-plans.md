# Lesson Plan Template Builder for Teachers

## 簡介

The Lesson Plan Template Builder for Teachers is a free AI prompt that researches best practices and creates comprehensive lesson plan frameworks for educators and curriculum designers. This lesson plan prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert educator who researches effective teaching strategies, then delivers a reusable template organized in a three-column table: Lesson Component (the key structural elements), Purpose (educational rationale behind each part), and Example (concrete illustrations showing implementation). The output includes APA-formatted citations and is tailored to your specified subject and education level. Teachers use it to design units in mathematics, science, language arts, social studies, and elective courses across elementary, middle school, high school, and college settings. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured starting point that connects pedagogical theory to classroom practice, or when adapting curriculum for a new grade level or subject area. ● Produces a reusable template adaptable to multiple topics within the same subject ● Explains the educational purpose behind each lesson component so you understand why it matters ● Provides concrete examples demonstrating how to implement each element in practice ● Includes APA-formatted source citations for research-backed credibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator and curriculum designer with deep knowledge of effective teaching strategies across subjects and grade levels.

## Task
Research best practices for developing engaging lesson plans for {{subject}} at the {{education-level}} level. Synthesize your findings into a comprehensive summary that includes:

1. A reusable lesson plan template adaptable to various topics within the subject
2. Clear explanations of each component's purpose and implementation
3. Concrete examples demonstrating practical application
4. Proper citations in APA format

## Output
Present your findings in a table with three columns:

- **Lesson Component**: Key elements of an effective lesson plan
- **Purpose**: The rationale and objectives of each component
- **Example**: Concrete illustrations of how to implement each component

Below the table, include a **Sources** section with a numbered list of all references cited in APA format.

Focus on actionable, subject-appropriate guidance. Avoid overly general information that doesn't directly support lesson plan development.
```
