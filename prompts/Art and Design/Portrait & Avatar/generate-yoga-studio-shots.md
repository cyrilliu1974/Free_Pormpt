# Yoga Studio Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Yoga Studio Portrait Generator is a free AI image prompt that produces serene fitness photography in a minimalist studio setting for photographers, wellness brands, and content creators. It renders a woman holding a tree pose against white walls with diffused natural light, shot in a 35mm f/2 aesthetic that delivers soft, airy tones and a calm, focused atmosphere. This yoga studio prompt for Midjourney works on Flux, Nano Banana, and other text-to-image models to create professional fitness portraits without a photoshoot. Reach for it when you need authentic wellness visuals that convey tranquility, balance, and elegance for social media, marketing campaigns, or editorial content. ● Produces a woman in tree pose with clean studio composition, white walls, and diffused natural light. ● Mimics 35mm f/2 depth of field for soft, airy tones and a calming, professional mood. ● Outputs 3:4 aspect ratio at quality 2, ideal for Instagram, print, and wellness marketing materials. ● Works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana without modification, delivering consistent minimalist fitness portraits. ## Prompt

```
Clean fitness portrait of a woman holding a tree pose in a bright minimalist studio, diffused natural light, white walls, serene energy. Shot on 35mm f/2, soft tones, airy mood. --ar 3:4 --quality 2
```
