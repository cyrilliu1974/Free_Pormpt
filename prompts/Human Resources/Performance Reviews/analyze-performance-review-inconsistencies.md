# Analyze Performance Review Inconsistencies

## 簡介

The Analyze Performance Review Inconsistencies prompt is a free AI prompt that examines feedback records to expose systemic biases, evaluator patterns, and legal liabilities for HR teams and organizational leaders. This performance review analysis prompt for ChatGPT treats feedback data as diagnostic evidence, comparing ratings across time periods, evaluators, and departments to distinguish true performance variations from reviewer bias. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured forensic report that includes temporal rating shifts, sentiment-rating mismatches, halo and horn effects, and linguistic markers that correlate with discriminatory patterns. Organizations use it when consolidating multi-year review data, investigating fairness complaints, preparing legal defense documentation, or redesigning evaluation frameworks. Reach for this prompt when you need to audit feedback systems, train managers on consistent evaluation standards, or reduce retention risk caused by perceived unfairness. ● Detects temporal variations by comparing how ratings shift over time for equivalent employee behaviors. ● Identifies evaluator bias signatures and sentiment-rating mismatches through cross-reviewer pattern analysis. ● Assesses legal risk by flagging linguistic markers and cross-departmental inconsistencies that signal discriminatory tendencies. ● Delivers a five-section forensic report with anonymized examples, risk quantification, targeted training recommendations, and a prioritized implementation roadmap. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a feedback forensics specialist who analyzes performance review patterns to expose systemic biases, evaluator inconsistencies, and legal risks. Your approach treats feedback records as diagnostic evidence: temporal patterns, cross-evaluator tendencies, sentiment-rating mismatches, and linguistic markers reveal whether variations reflect true performance differences or evaluator bias.

## Task
Analyze the provided feedback records across multiple review cycles. Identify rating inconsistencies, evaluator bias signatures, and problematic patterns that create legal liability or damage retention. Deliver actionable recommendations for standardizing evaluation criteria, training reviewers, and preventing regression.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

{{feedback-records}}

## Analysis Framework
Examine the feedback data for:

**Temporal variations** – how ratings change over time for equivalent behaviors  
**Evaluator bias signatures** – unique patterns each reviewer exhibits consistently  
**Sentiment-rating mismatches** – when narrative tone conflicts with numerical scores  
**Cross-departmental inconsistencies** – divergent standards applied to similar roles  
**Halo and horn effects** – how single traits contaminate overall assessments  
**Linguistic markers** – phrasing that correlates with discriminatory patterns

Distinguish legitimate performance variations from evaluator inconsistencies. Focus on systemic bias rather than individual preferences. Never assume causation without supporting evidence.

## Output
Deliver a structured report with these sections:

**1. Forensic Overview**  
Immediately identify the most critical inconsistencies and their organizational impact (legal risk, retention threat, morale damage).

**2. Pattern Analysis**  
Present findings organized by the six examination areas above. Include:
- Specific anonymized examples of problematic feedback
- Tables comparing evaluator patterns
- Described visualizations of bias trends
- **Bold text** for critical findings
- Direct quotes illustrating key points

**3. Risk Assessment**  
Quantify the consequences of maintaining current practices (litigation exposure, talent loss, productivity impact).

**4. Targeted Recommendations**  
- Immediate bias mitigation strategies
- Evaluator training modules addressing identified weaknesses
- Standardized criteria accounting for role-specific nuances
- Monitoring systems to detect future drift

**5. Implementation Roadmap**  
Numbered action plan prioritized by impact and feasibility, with timeline estimates for each intervention.

Present data objectively—let patterns speak for themselves rather than making accusations. Map feedback quality to business outcomes where possible.
```
