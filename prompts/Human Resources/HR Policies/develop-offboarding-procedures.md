# Develop Offboarding Procedures

## 簡介

The Develop Offboarding Procedures prompt is a free AI prompt that generates systematic exit clearance workflows for HR teams managing employee departures. This offboarding procedures prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces phase-based workflows (pre-departure, final day, post-exit) with numbered steps, actionable checklists, and compliance tracking points. It covers access removal timelines, financial settlement calculations, asset return protocols, handover documentation, and escalation triggers for complex situations. Use it when building or updating termination processes that balance legal protection, data security, and professional relationships. ● Produces procedures organized by offboarding phase with clear headings, numbered workflows, and actionable checklists for immediate implementation. ● Covers access removal timelines, financial settlement steps, asset return protocols, and handover documentation to close security and operational gaps. ● Includes compliance checkpoints and escalation protocols for complex departures, integrating with your existing policies and systems. ● Balances organizational risk management with departing employee rights, protecting data security and professional relationships simultaneously. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR compliance specialist with expertise in offboarding frameworks, regulatory requirements, and organizational transition management.

## Task
Develop comprehensive exit clearance procedures that ensure legal compliance, data security, and operational continuity while maintaining professional relationships. Deliver systematic workflows covering access removal timelines, financial settlement calculations, asset return protocols, handover documentation requirements, and compliance checkpoints. Structure procedures to protect both organizational interests and departing employee rights, including escalation protocols for complex situations.

## Context
**Organization:** {{organization-context}}

**Current state:** {{existing-policies-and-systems}}

## Output
Provide procedures in step-by-step format with:
- Clear procedural headings organized by offboarding phase (pre-departure, final day, post-exit)
- Numbered steps for each workflow
- Actionable checklists in bullet points for immediate implementation and compliance tracking
- Integration points with existing company policies
- Escalation triggers for complex situations
```
