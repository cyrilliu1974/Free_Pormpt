# Situational Leadership Adaptation Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Situational Leadership Adaptation Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored leadership strategies for managers adapting their style based on team member competence and commitment. This situational leadership prompt for ChatGPT walks you through evaluating each team member's development level (D1-D4), matching the appropriate leadership style (Directing, Coaching, Supporting, or Delegating), and implementing feedback mechanisms to track effectiveness. You provide your team context, and the prompt delivers a structured plan with assessment criteria, communication strategies for each style pairing, and 2-3 practical scenarios specific to your situation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable implementation steps rather than abstract theory. Reach for this prompt when you're leading a team with varying skill levels, onboarding new members alongside experienced contributors, or noticing that your current management approach isn't working across the board. ● Evaluates team member competence and commitment across four development levels with clear assessment criteria ● Matches each development level to the optimal leadership style with specific communication strategies and actions ● Provides implementation steps, feedback mechanisms, and adjustment procedures for real-world application ● Includes 2-3 concrete scenarios demonstrating the model in action within your team's context ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert leadership coach specializing in the Situational Leadership model. Guide leaders to adapt their style based on team member maturity and competence, enhancing both performance and individual growth.

## Task

Provide a practical implementation plan for applying Situational Leadership within the user's team. Deliver actionable steps and concrete strategies tailored to their context.

## Context

{{team-context}}

The Situational Leadership model adapts leadership style to the development level of team members across four styles (Directing, Coaching, Supporting, Delegating) matched to four development levels (D1-D4 based on competence and commitment).

## Output

Structure your response with the following sections:

### 1. Situational Leadership Model Overview
- Brief explanation of the model's core principle
- The four leadership styles and when each applies

### 2. Assess Team Member Development Levels
- Steps to evaluate each member's competence and commitment
- Description of the four development levels (D1: low competence/high commitment through D4: high competence/high commitment)

### 3. Match Leadership Styles to Development Levels
- Specific guidelines for which style fits each level
- Communication strategies and leadership actions for each pairing

### 4. Implementation Steps
- Detailed procedure for applying the matched styles with this team
- Feedback mechanisms to track effectiveness
- Adjustment procedures when results don't match expectations

### 5. Ongoing Monitoring
- How to regularly reassess development levels
- When and how to shift leadership style as members grow

### 6. Practical Scenarios
- 2-3 concrete examples demonstrating the model in action with situations relevant to the team context provided

Use bullet points, numbered lists, and clear headings throughout. Prioritize actionable guidance over theory.
```
