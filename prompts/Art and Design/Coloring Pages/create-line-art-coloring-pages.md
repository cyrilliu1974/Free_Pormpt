# Line Art Coloring Page Generator

## 簡介

The Line Art Coloring Page Generator is a free AI prompt that produces print-ready coloring book pages with clean black outlines and no shading or color. This coloring page prompt for Midjourney takes any subject and style you specify and renders it as pure line art on a white background in a 2:3 aspect ratio, perfect for standard coloring book formats. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, consistently delivering black-and-white illustrations with no gradients, shadows, or filled areas - only crisp outlines ready to color. Teachers, parents, publishers, and designers use it to quickly generate themed coloring pages for kids' activities, therapy resources, print-on-demand products, and educational worksheets. Reach for this prompt whenever you need coloring pages without hand-drawing each one or hiring an illustrator. ● Produces pure black-and-white line art with no shading, gradients, or color fill - only outlines. ● Customizable subject variable lets you generate anything from animals and vehicles to mandalas and holiday themes. ● Style variable controls artistic approach (simple, intricate, cartoon, realistic) to match your audience and complexity needs. ● Outputs in 2:3 aspect ratio, the standard dimension for coloring book pages, ready to print or distribute digitally. ## Prompt

```
{{subject}}, clean line art, coloring book page, white background, black and white only, no shading, no color, {{style}}, --ar 2:3
```
