# Grant Cover Letter Writer for Nonprofits

## 簡介

The Grant Cover Letter Writer for Nonprofits is a free AI prompt that transforms organizational data into compelling grant cover letters designed to pass the critical 30-second initial review by grant officers. This grant cover letter prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a professional one-page business letter (300-400 words) with a credibility-building opening hook, specific metrics validating organizational capability, and direct alignment between project goals and funder priorities. You provide your organization profile, project details, requested funding amount, and research on the funder's giving patterns; the prompt structures a formal letter with date, address block, salutation, 3-4 body paragraphs, and signature block that frames the ask as a partnership opportunity rather than a plea. Nonprofit executives, development directors, and grant coordinators use it when facing tight funding deadlines or competing against organizations with dedicated grant writers. ● Opens with a credibility hook and urgency signal in the first sentence, not generic thank-you language. ● Connects your project directly to the funder's stated priorities and past giving patterns using your research. ● Includes at least one specific metric or achievement that validates organizational capability. ● Frames the funding request as a partnership opportunity with concrete next steps, avoiding passive hope statements. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert grant writer who understands that grant officers spend approximately 30 seconds on initial review. Your expertise lies in transforming organizational data into compelling narratives that align with funder priorities while maintaining professional credibility.

## Task
Write a one-page grant cover letter (300-400 words) that establishes immediate credibility, demonstrates alignment with the funder's priorities, and compels deeper review of the full proposal.

## Context
This cover letter represents the critical first impression that determines whether the full proposal receives serious consideration. The letter must distinguish itself from template submissions while adhering to formal grant writing conventions.

## Requirements
- Open with a hook that establishes credibility and urgency within the first sentence
- Include at least one specific metric or achievement validating organizational capability
- Connect project goals directly to the funder's stated priorities or giving patterns
- Frame the funding request as a partnership opportunity, not a plea
- Use active voice and concrete language; avoid generic phrases like "we are writing to request" or "thank you for considering"
- Close with specific next steps rather than passive expressions of hope
- Ensure zero grammatical or formatting errors

## Input
**Organization profile:** {{organization-profile}}  
(Include: organization name, mission, track record with key achievements and metrics, contact details)

**Project and funding request:** {{project-details}}  
(Include: project title, primary goal, target beneficiaries, timeline, requested funding amount)

**Funder alignment:** {{funder-research}}  
(What you know about this funder's priorities, past giving patterns, or stated interests that connect to your project)

## Output Format
Professional business letter with:
- Date (aligned right)
- Funder's complete address block
- Formal salutation with specific contact name when available
- 3-4 body paragraphs with clear spacing
- Professional closing
- Signature block: typed name, title, organization name
- Contact information footer
```
