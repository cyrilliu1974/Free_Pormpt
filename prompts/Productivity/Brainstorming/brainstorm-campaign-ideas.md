# Campaign Idea Generator for Marketing Teams

## 簡介

The Campaign Idea Generator for Marketing Teams is a free AI prompt that produces actionable marketing campaign concepts aligned with your business goals and unique value proposition. This campaign idea prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and unique selling points as input and returns a markdown table of at least five distinct campaign ideas, each mapped to a specific target audience and key message. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed to help marketing strategists brainstorm concepts that balance creativity with feasibility. Real-world use cases include planning quarterly campaigns, exploring new audience segments, and identifying fresh angles to communicate competitive advantages. Each generated idea includes actionable tactics, aligns with current marketing trends, and suggests clear mechanisms for measuring campaign impact. This prompt is for marketing teams, brand managers, and strategists who need structured, ready-to-present campaign concepts quickly. ● Produces at least 5 campaign ideas in a three-column table format (Idea, Target Audience, Key Message) ● Incorporates your business context and unique selling points into every concept ● Suggests campaigns that are feasible within budget and resource constraints ● Aligns ideas with contemporary marketing channels and measurable impact metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in creative campaign development.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of innovative campaign ideas that boost brand awareness and drive conversions. Each concept should be actionable, creative, and aligned with current marketing trends.

## Context
Business and strategic parameters:
{{business-context}}

Unique selling points:
{{usp}}

## Output
Deliver at least 5 distinct campaign ideas in a markdown table with three columns:

| Idea | Target Audience | Key Message |
|------|----------------|-------------|

Each campaign should:
- Address the specific business type and audience segments
- Highlight the unique selling points naturally
- Be feasible within stated constraints
- Leverage contemporary marketing channels and tactics
- Include a clear mechanism for measuring impact
```
