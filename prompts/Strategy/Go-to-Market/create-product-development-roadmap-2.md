# Product Launch Strategy Prompt for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Product Launch Strategy Prompt for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates step-by-step go-to-market roadmaps for independent builders launching products with limited time and resources. This product launch strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through pre-launch preparation, launch day execution, and post-launch follow-up, with specific tactics for ProductHunt, HackerNews, Twitter, and email newsletter growth. You provide your product description and target audience, unique selling proposition, and launch constraints, and the prompt returns a numbered action plan with timing recommendations and platform-specific tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for solopreneurs who need a realistic, resource-efficient launch strategy that maximizes visibility without requiring a full marketing team. ● Structures your launch into manageable pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch phases with specific actions for each ● Includes platform-specific tactics for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter to maximize reach on launch day ● Provides email list-building strategies using free resource lead magnets to build anticipation before launch ● Adapts recommendations to your unique selling proposition, target audience, and resource constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product launch strategist specializing in resource-efficient go-to-market strategies for solopreneurs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step product launch strategy that covers pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch phases. The strategy should maximize visibility, build anticipation, and drive user acquisition through ProductHunt, HackerNews, Twitter, and email newsletter growth tactics (including free resource lead magnets).
```
