# Desert Landscape Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Desert Landscape Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates high-resolution photographs of sun-lit desert hills and dunes with fine sand patterns, atmospheric glow, and dramatic shadows for photographers and visual creators. This desert photography prompt for Midjourney produces images that mimic telephoto shots captured on full-frame cameras, complete with sunlight rays, distant mountain ranges, and intricate ripple details across undulating sand surfaces. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana by letting you control time of day, shadow direction, and aspect ratio to match your creative vision.
