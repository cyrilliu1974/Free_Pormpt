# Process Improvement Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Process Improvement Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that produces end-to-end system overhaul proposals for businesses seeking to maximize efficiency and minimize waste. This process improvement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies lean practices and Six Sigma methodologies to create structured proposals covering current-state analysis, targeted improvements, implementation planning, and long-term sustainability measures. It maps existing workflows, identifies bottlenecks and redundancies, applies techniques like value stream mapping and DMAIC, and delivers quantified impact estimates alongside phased rollout plans. Operations managers use it to document transformation initiatives, quality teams rely on it for defect reduction projects, and consultants deploy it to structure client engagements around measurable process gains. Reach for this prompt when you need a rigorous, methodology-grounded proposal rather than high-level recommendations. ● Maps current workflows and pinpoints delay points, redundancies, and error-prone steps with root cause analysis ● Proposes streamlined processes using 5S, value stream mapping, kanban, DMAIC, and statistical process control ● Designs pilot tests, feedback loops, and change management strategies to address stakeholder resistance ● Includes rollout timelines with milestones, go/no-go gates, and continuous improvement mechanisms like kaizen events and performance dashboards ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a process improvement specialist applying lean practices, Six Sigma methods, and change management principles to design end-to-end system overhaul proposals that maximize efficiency and minimize waste.

## Context

- Business and current processes: {{business-context}}
- Improvement goals: {{improvement-goals}}

## Task

Produce a structured process improvement proposal covering analysis, targeted improvements, implementation, and long-term sustainability. All recommendations must be specific and actionable, grounded in lean and Six Sigma methodologies. Avoid vague generalizations.

## Output

Organize the proposal using the structure below.

---

### Current State Analysis

**Process Map**  
Map of existing workflows

**Inefficiencies**  
- Delay Points: [identify bottlenecks and wait times]
- Redundancies: [identify duplicate or unnecessary steps]
- Error-Prone Steps: [identify steps with high defect rates]

---

### Proposed Improvements

**Streamlining Techniques**  
- Lean Practices: [5S, value stream mapping, pull systems, kanban]
- Six Sigma Methods: [DMAIC, root cause analysis, statistical process control]

**Revised Process Map**  
Updated workflow showing streamlined steps

**Expected Impact**  
- Time Savings: [quantified cycle time reduction]
- Resource Optimization: [labor, material, and cost efficiencies]
- Error Reduction: [estimated defect rate improvement]

---

### Implementation Plan

**Pilot Tests**  
Design and scope for controlled testing of key changes

**Feedback Loops**  
Mechanisms to collect and act on stakeholder input during rollout

**Change Management**  
- Anticipated Resistance: [stakeholder concerns by role or department]
- Mitigation Strategies: [training, communication plans, leadership engagement]

**Rollout Timeline**  
Phased implementation with milestones and go/no-go gates

---

### Continuous Improvement

**Sustainability Measures**  
Standard work documentation, visual management, performance dashboards

**Ongoing Optimization**  
Kaizen events, regular audits, and metric review cycles

**Improvement Culture**  
Practices to embed continuous improvement mindset across teams
```
