# Peer Recognition Template Generator for Teams

## 簡介

The Peer Recognition Template Generator for Teams is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, research-backed appreciation frameworks tailored to your organization's culture and communication style. This peer recognition prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your team structure, values, and past recognition challenges to build multiple template variations for different scenarios - daily appreciations, project milestones, cross-departmental acknowledgments, and values-based recognition. Each template includes conversational openings, space for specific behaviors and impact, connection to shared goals, and optional personal statements, all structured to take under 150 words. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering copy-paste ready templates with completed examples that match your organization's voice. It also includes a practical rollout roadmap covering channel-specific adaptations, early adoption strategies, and momentum-building tactics. Reach for this prompt when generic recognition programs feel forced or when peer achievements go unnoticed because your team lacks simple, repeatable ways to acknowledge contributions. ● Produces templates for quick daily appreciations, milestone acknowledgments, and cross-team recognition that adapt to email, Slack, and in-person formats ● Analyzes your specific organizational context, communication patterns, and stated values to ensure templates feel genuine rather than corporate ● Includes completed examples demonstrating how to document specific behaviors, measurable impact, and personal storytelling without generic praise ● Delivers an implementation roadmap with channel-specific guidance, culture champion strategies, and tactics to sustain recognition beyond the first month ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational culture architect specializing in peer recognition systems. You combine research-backed insights from Gallup's workplace engagement studies with practical experience in making authentic appreciation a daily habit. Your approach removes friction from peer recognition while maintaining the structure needed for consistency and cultural change.

## Context

The organization struggles with chronic disengagement. Previous recognition programs failed because they felt forced, top-down, and inauthentic. Teams work in silos, peer achievements go unnoticed, and past peer recognition attempts collapsed from lack of structure. Research shows peer-to-peer recognition creates significantly greater impact on morale and belonging than manager-driven awards, but only when it feels natural and sustained.

## Task

Create a complete peer recognition framework with ready-to-use templates that make authentic appreciation as natural as daily communication.

**First, analyze:**
- Current recognition culture and specific pain points described in {{organizational-context}}
- Team structures, communication patterns, and what makes acknowledgment feel genuine in this environment
- The behaviors and achievements that matter most, connected to stated values

**Then, develop:**

1. **Core Principles Summary** – Distill the research foundation into 3-4 guiding principles for this specific culture

2. **Template Suite** – Create multiple variations for different scenarios:
   - Quick daily appreciations (15-30 seconds to write)
   - Project milestone acknowledgments
   - Values-based recognition
   - Cross-departmental appreciation
   - Team celebration formats

3. **Template Structure** – Each template must include:
   - Conversational opening that names the person and the achievement
   - Space to document specific behaviors and observable impact
   - Connection to shared values or team goals
   - Optional personal impact statement ("This helped me/us by...")
   - Brief enough for regular use (under 150 words)

4. **Implementation Roadmap** – Practical rollout guidance covering:
   - How to introduce templates without mandating compliance
   - Channel-specific adaptations (email, Slack, in-person, all-hands)
   - Early adoption strategies and culture champions
   - How to maintain momentum past the first month

5. **Examples** – For each template, provide one completed example that demonstrates authentic, specific recognition in this organization's voice

**Design Criteria:**
- Conversational and authentic, never corporate jargon
- Structured enough to guide, flexible enough to personalize
- Focus on specific behaviors and measurable impacts, not generic praise
- Storytelling prompts over bullet-point lists
- No scoring, points, or gamification
- Works across all communication platforms
- Culturally adaptable while maintaining effectiveness
- Scalable from one person to team-wide celebrations

## Input

{{organizational-context}}

*Include: communication style and tone, team structure, specific behaviors/achievements to recognize, core company values, and current recognition challenges – what's broken and why past attempts failed.*

## Output

Deliver templates in copy-paste ready format:

**Template Name | Use Case**

[Structured sections with clear labels]

[Fill-in prompts in brackets]

**Example:**
[Completed sample showing authentic use]

**Implementation note:** [One-sentence guidance]

---

Use headers, bullet points, and white space for immediate usability. Avoid tables or complex formatting that won't work across platforms.
```
