# Premium Modular Packaging System Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Premium Modular Packaging System Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates studio-quality packaging imagery for brands seeking functional, design-driven unboxing experiences. This packaging design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces photorealistic scenes of foldable rigid cartons paired with resealable metallic pouches, complete with human hands revealing the inner product. It captures the geometric precision of origami-style outer boxes, the smooth reflective finish of foil-laminate inner pouches, and the moment of interaction that defines premium unboxing. Designers can specify brand name, industry segment, and primary color to generate commercial-grade visuals suitable for e-commerce listings, pitch decks, and brand guidelines without prototyping costs or studio shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need to visualize two-tier packaging concepts with engineered structural detail and clean industrial aesthetics before committing to manufacturing. ● Foldable outer cartons with visible geometric flaps and precision-engineered fold lines in matte-coated paperboard ● Resealable metallic inner pouches with zip-lock seals and soft reflective foil surfaces nested inside ● Three-quarter top-down product photography angle with human hands removing the pouch from the opened box ● Controlled studio lighting, neutral gradient background, and clean shadows that emphasize material texture and structural logic ## Prompt

```
Premium modular packaging system for {{brand-name}} in {{industry-segment}}. Two-part design: protective outer box with inner product pouch.

Outer box: foldable rigid carton with geometric flaps and origami-style unfolding panels. Thick matte-coated paperboard with visible engineered folds showing structural logic and precision.

Inner pouch: resealable flexible pouch with zip-lock seal, soft metallic foil laminate with smooth reflective surface. Securely nested inside the box.

Human hand removing the inner pouch from the opened box. Three-quarter top-down view, 50mm commercial product photography angle, sharp focus on packaging with slight background falloff.

Color palette: {{primary-color}} as structural color blocks, neutral metallic silver secondary, small high-contrast accent detail on tabs or tags. Clean sans-serif wordmark logo centered on inner pouch, minimal branding on outer box. Brand name only, no slogans.

Clean industrial premium aesthetic inspired by technical product packaging, modern electronics unboxing, and Swiss-inspired structural design. Precise, minimal, confident design language.

Neutral studio surface with soft gray gradient background. Soft controlled studio lighting with subtle reflections on metallic pouch and clean shadows emphasizing box geometry.

Photorealistic 8k render. Premium, functional, trustworthy, design-driven mood. No food spill, no busy background, no excess typography, no mockup frames, no stock packaging elements.
```
