# Pitch Deck Generator for Investor Presentations

## 簡介

The Pitch Deck Generator for Investor Presentations is a free AI prompt that builds structured, investor-ready pitch decks for founders, entrepreneurs, and business teams seeking funding or partnerships. This pitch deck prompt for ChatGPT walks through 10 essential sections: company overview, problem statement, solution, market opportunity, business model, traction and validation, financials, roadmap, investment opportunity, and appendix. For each slide it delivers a title, 3-5 content bullet points or narrative text, and a visual note describing the ideal chart, diagram, or image. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, ensuring you receive a logical narrative flow that prioritizes critical information, validates claims with data, and maintains a professional layout across every slide. Use it when you need a cohesive presentation framework that balances persuasive storytelling with informative content, whether you are pitching venture capitalists, angel investors, or strategic partners. ● Covers all 10 core sections investors expect, from problem and solution through financials and exit strategy. ● Provides specific visual notes for each slide so designers know which charts, diagrams, or images to create. ● Balances narrative storytelling with data-driven validation to hold investor attention. ● Outputs bullet points and short narratives that fit standard presentation formats without overwhelming detail. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a pitch deck specialist creating investor-ready presentations that secure funding and partnerships.

## Task

Create a complete pitch deck for {{product-or-service}} targeting {{target-audience}} with the business goals {{business-goals}}.

## Structure

Develop slides across these sections:

1. **Company Overview** – Mission, team, key milestones
2. **Problem** – Problem statement, impact, current solutions
3. **Solution** – Product description, key features, user journey, value proposition
4. **Market Opportunity** – Target market, market size, trends, competitive landscape
5. **Business Model** – Revenue model, pricing strategy, sales channels, partnerships
6. **Traction and Validation** – Key metrics, customer testimonials, awards
7. **Financials** – Revenue forecast, expense breakdown, funding requirements, use of funds
8. **Roadmap** – Short-term goals, long-term vision, milestones
9. **Investment Opportunity** – Investment terms, equity offering, exit strategy
10. **Appendix** – Detailed financials, team bios, references

## Output

For each slide provide:
- **Title**
- **Content**: 3-5 bullet points or a short narrative
- **Visual note**: Description of the ideal chart, diagram, image, or graph

Ensure the deck:
- Follows a logical narrative flow
- Prioritizes critical information over exhaustive detail
- Uses data and metrics to validate all claims
- Maintains a consistent, professional layout throughout
```
