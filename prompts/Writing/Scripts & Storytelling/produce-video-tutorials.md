# Video Tutorial Script Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Video Tutorial Script Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates structured, engaging video scripts for entrepreneurs who teach and sell digital products. This video tutorial script prompt for ChatGPT transforms any tutorial topic into a three-part script (introduction, main content, conclusion) designed specifically for solopreneurs balancing product creation, marketing, and delivery. It applies dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence flows logically, weaving in practical examples from the digital product ecosystem, rhetorical questions, and relatable anecdotes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing scripts that speak directly to entrepreneurs who need to educate their audience while building their business. Reach for it when you're creating course content, onboarding videos, product walkthroughs, or educational marketing materials that must resonate with fellow digital entrepreneurs. ● Structures scripts into hook, content segments, and actionable conclusions tailored to digital product sellers ● Uses dependency grammar to build logical sentence flow that keeps viewers engaged ● Incorporates practical examples and relatable scenarios from the solopreneur experience ● Includes rhetorical questions and engagement elements to maintain viewer attention throughout ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video tutorial scriptwriter specializing in content for solopreneurs selling digital products.

## Task
Write a complete video tutorial script that breaks down complex concepts into clear, engaging segments. Structure the script using dependency grammar principles to ensure each sentence builds logically on the previous one.

## Context
**Tutorial focus:** {{tutorial-topic}}

**Business context:** {{solopreneur-context}}

The script must speak directly to solopreneurs balancing product creation, marketing, and delivery. Use practical examples from the digital product ecosystem. Maintain engagement through rhetorical questions, relatable anecdotes, and thought-provoking statements.

## Output
Deliver the script in three clearly marked sections:

**INTRODUCTION**
- Hook that immediately demonstrates value for solopreneurs
- Brief outline of what the tutorial will cover
- Why this matters for their digital product business

**MAIN CONTENT**
- Key concepts broken into digestible segments
- Practical examples applicable to digital product creation/sales
- Clear progression from foundational to advanced ideas
- Engagement elements woven throughout

**CONCLUSION**
- Summary of key takeaways
- Specific call-to-action relevant to solopreneurs
- Next steps they can implement immediately
```
