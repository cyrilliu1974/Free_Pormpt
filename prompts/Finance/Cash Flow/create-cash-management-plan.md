# Cash Management Plan Prompt for Small Business

## 簡介

The Cash Management Plan Prompt for Small Business is a free AI prompt that builds a defensive financial system protecting small businesses from timing gaps between revenue and expenses. This cash management prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your revenue streams and expense cycles to design a three-tier reserve architecture, payment prioritization framework, and early warning dashboard. It assumes delayed payments, early expenses, and simultaneous failures - producing actionable protocols that protect solvency when multiple crises hit at once. Use it when facing unpredictable client payment cycles, seasonal volatility, or the need to build financial buffers before a cash crunch forces expensive emergency borrowing. ● Maps revenue arrival against expense due dates to expose danger zones where outflows exceed inflows ● Specifies dollar amounts for operational, tactical, and strategic reserve tiers with separate account structures ● Categorizes expenses by criticality and sequences payments to maximize float while protecting vendor relationships ● Defines leading-indicator metrics with red/yellow/green thresholds signaling cash crunches 30-60 days in advance ● Provides scenario-based crisis protocols for delayed client payments, expense spikes, and seasonal revenue drops ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a cash management strategist who designs defensive financial systems for small businesses. Your frameworks assume delayed revenue, early expenses, and simultaneous failures—protecting solvency through layered reserves, prioritization protocols, and early warning signals.

## Task
Create a comprehensive cash management plan that protects the business from timing gaps between revenue and expenses. Build a multi-tier reserve system, payment prioritization framework, early warning metrics, and crisis response protocols that function when multiple failures occur simultaneously.

## Context
Small businesses face unpredictable revenue cycles while fixed expenses never pause. One delayed client payment can trigger cascading failures: bounced checks, damaged vendor relationships, emergency high-interest borrowing. This plan must assume payments arrive late, expenses arrive early, and work when everything goes wrong at once.

## Input
{{business-financials}} — Provide all revenue streams with typical payment delays; fixed and variable expenses with due dates; current cash position and credit lines; seasonal revenue patterns; biggest cash stability threats.

## Analysis Framework
1. **Cash Flow Mapping** — Chart revenue vs expense cycles to expose danger zones where outflows exceed inflows; identify volatility patterns and payment delay risks; calculate cash conversion cycle
2. **Reserve Architecture** — Design three-tier system: operational buffer (1-2 weeks expenses), tactical reserve (1-2 months), strategic emergency fund (3-6 months); specify dollar amounts and separate account structure
3. **Payment Prioritization** — Categorize expenses by criticality: survival tier (payroll, rent), operational tier (inventory, utilities), growth tier (marketing, expansion); create scheduling matrix maximizing float on non-essential payables while protecting critical relationships
4. **Early Warning System** — Define metrics and thresholds signaling cash crunches 30-60 days in advance; establish weekly tracking cadence
5. **Crisis Protocols** — Develop response plans for major client payment delay, unexpected expense spike, seasonal revenue drop, multiple simultaneous failures

## Principles
- Build all buffers assuming 30-60 day revenue delays
- Establish credit facilities before crisis; lenders disappear when cash is tight
- Maintain separate accounts for each reserve tier
- Prioritize cash preservation over growth during volatile periods
- Track weekly, act on leading indicators
- Build vendor relationships before needing payment term negotiations

## Output
Deliver a structured plan with:

**1. Cash Flow Analysis**
Visual timeline showing revenue arrival vs expense due dates; volatility patterns; identified danger zones

**2. Reserve Allocation Strategy**
Specific dollar targets for each tier; account setup instructions; funding timeline

**3. Payment Scheduling Matrix**
Table showing expense categories, due dates, criticality ranking, payment sequencing

**4. Early Warning Dashboard**
Table of metrics (days cash on hand, receivables aging, payables ratio) with red/yellow/green thresholds

**5. Emergency Response Protocols**
Scenario-based action plans: first, second, third steps when specific crises hit

**6. Implementation Roadmap**
30/60/90 day action plan with milestones and measurable checkpoints

**7. Maintenance Checklist**
Weekly tasks (metrics review, obligations scan) and monthly tasks (reserve rebalancing, vendor relationship check-ins)

Use tables for payment priorities and warning triggers. Base all calculations on the provided financial information. Include specific dollar amounts, dates, and actionable steps.
```
