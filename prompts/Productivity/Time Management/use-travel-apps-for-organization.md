# Travel App Recommendation Guide for Trip Planning

## 簡介

The Travel App Recommendation Guide for Trip Planning is a free AI prompt that creates a custom selection of mobile travel apps tailored to your specific trip requirements. This travel app prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your trip details and produces a markdown table recommending 8–12 apps across every phase of travel: destination research, accommodation and transportation booking, itinerary building, budget tracking, navigation, and on-the-ground logistics. Each recommendation includes core features and specific benefits explaining why that app suits your trip type, destination, duration, and preferences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to find the right mix of booking engines, mapping tools, expense trackers, translation aids, and travel management platforms. Ideal for travelers who want a technology stack that matches their journey instead of generic app lists. ● Produces a structured table of 8–12 apps spanning research, booking, navigation, budget tracking, and logistics ● Matches app features and benefits to your specific trip type, destination, duration, and preferences ● Covers diverse travel needs including accommodation platforms, offline maps, translation tools, and expense trackers ● Explains why each app suits your journey, prioritizing time savings, cost optimization, and ease of use ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert travel planner specializing in mobile app-based trip organization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide to travel apps that streamline trip planning from research through execution. Analyze and recommend apps based on their user-friendliness, functionality, and unique value propositions.

## Context
Trip details:
{{trip-details}}

Cover all phases of travel planning: destination research, accommodation booking, transportation, itinerary building, budget tracking, and on-the-ground logistics. Prioritize apps that best match the trip type, destination requirements, duration constraints, and stated preferences.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns:

| Apps | Features | Benefits |
|------|----------|----------|

Include 8–12 travel apps that span the full planning cycle. For each app, specify:
- **Features**: core capabilities (booking, mapping, translation, reviews, budget tools, offline access, etc.)
- **Benefits**: why it suits this specific trip (time savings, cost optimization, local expertise, ease of use)

Ensure the selection covers diverse needs: research platforms, booking engines, navigation tools, expense trackers, and travel management hubs.
```
