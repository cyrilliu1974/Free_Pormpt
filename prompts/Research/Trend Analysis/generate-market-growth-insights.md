# Market Growth Insights Report Generator

## 簡介

The Market Growth Insights Report Generator is a free AI prompt that delivers data-driven trend analysis and growth opportunity identification for business analysts and strategic planners. This market growth insights prompt for ChatGPT produces a comprehensive report that examines three emerging market trends and pinpoints two specific growth opportunities tailored to your company profile, target markets, and industry. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring market research data to be grounded in cited sources rather than speculation. Typical use cases include expansion planning, investment analysis, quarterly strategy reviews, and board presentations where credible sourcing is essential. Reach for this prompt when you need to synthesize market intelligence into actionable recommendations with an executive summary, trend assessments with supporting data, opportunity mapping linked to trends, and short- and long-term strategic guidance. ● Delivers three emerging trend analyses with growth potential assessments and source citations ● Identifies two growth opportunities mapped to specific target markets and driving trends ● Separates short-term (6–12 months) and long-term (2–3 years) recommendations ● Enforces quality standards requiring current data (within 18 months) and alignment with company capabilities ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business analyst conducting a comprehensive trend analysis to identify high-potential growth opportunities in emerging markets.

## Task
Deliver a data-driven analysis that identifies three major emerging market trends and two specific growth opportunities your company can pursue. Base all findings on market research and cite your sources.

## Context
**Company Profile:**
{{company-profile}}

**Focus Markets:**
{{target-markets}}

**Industry:**
{{industry}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Executive Summary**
Highlight key findings and recommendations in 3-4 sentences.

**Emerging Market Trends**
For each of three trends, provide:
- Trend name
- Detailed description
- Supporting data with citation
- Growth potential assessment

**Growth Opportunities**
For each of two opportunities, specify:
- Target market
- Opportunity description
- Key trends driving this opportunity
- Potential strategies to capitalize

**Recommendations**
- Short-term (6-12 months)
- Long-term (2-3 years)

**Sources**
List all citations in numbered format.

---

**Quality Standards:**
- Ground all claims in cited data; avoid speculation
- Ensure opportunities align with the company profile provided
- Make recommendations specific and actionable
- Use current market data (within last 18 months where possible)
```
