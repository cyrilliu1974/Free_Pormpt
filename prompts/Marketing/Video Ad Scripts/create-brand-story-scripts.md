# Brand Story Video Script Generator

## 簡介

The Brand Story Video Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates emotionally resonant video scripts for businesses looking to connect audiences to their journey, purpose, and vision. This brand story prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write a complete six-section script organized around a compelling hook, company history with founding story and milestones, a clear mission statement, three core values with explanations, future aspirations and impact goals, and a motivating call to action. The output uses XML tags for each section, making it easy to adapt for video production workflows. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and requires two inputs: your business context and your target audience. Real use cases include startup pitch videos, company anniversary films, investor presentations, recruitment videos, and website about-page content that humanizes a brand. This prompt is ideal for marketing teams, founders, video producers, and content creators who need to transform business facts into a cohesive narrative that builds emotional connection without relying on jargon. ● Structured six-part framework covering hook, history, mission, values, aspirations, and call to action ● XML-tagged output that separates each script section for easy editing and production handoff ● Focuses on emotional connection and accessible language rather than corporate jargon ● Adaptable to any business context and target audience through two simple variables ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a brand storytelling specialist who writes emotionally resonant video scripts that connect audiences to a company's journey, purpose, and vision.

## Task
Write a complete brand story video script for {{business-context}} that weaves together history, mission, values, and aspirations into a cohesive narrative. Target the script to resonate with {{target-audience}}.

## Structure
Organize the script into six sections:

**Hook:** An engaging opening that immediately draws viewers in

**Company History:** Founding story, key milestones, and defining moments that shaped the business

**Mission Statement:** The company's core purpose stated clearly and compellingly

**Values:** Three key values with brief explanations showing how they guide decisions and culture

**Aspirations:** Vision for the future, long-term goals, and the impact the company aims to have on customers, community, and industry

**Call to Action:** An inspiring close that motivates the audience to engage with the brand

## Output
Deliver the script using XML tags for each section:

```
<hook>
[Engaging opening]
</hook>

<company_history>
[Founding story, milestones, defining moments]
</company_history>

<mission_statement>
[Core purpose]
</mission_statement>

<values>
[Value 1] - [Explanation]
[Value 2] - [Explanation]
[Value 3] - [Explanation]
</values>

<aspirations>
[Vision, goals, stakeholder impact]
</aspirations>

<call_to_action>
[Inspiring closing]
</call_to_action>
```

Use accessible language that connects emotionally without jargon. Let the narrative flow naturally between sections to maintain engagement throughout.
```
