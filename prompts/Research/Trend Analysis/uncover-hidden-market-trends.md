# Hidden Market Trends Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Hidden Market Trends Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that uncovers non-obvious market shifts and revenue opportunities for small businesses and solo entrepreneurs. This hidden market trends prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business description and target audience, then identifying 10 specific trends: 5 customer behavior changes and 5 competitive landscape shifts. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, asking clarifying questions about your business model before researching and delivering a numbered list with critical assessments of relevance and potential impact. The prompt explicitly filters out mainstream trends already covered in major media, focusing instead on current, underutilized opportunities that competitors are missing. Source citations are included for every finding. Small business owners, solopreneurs, and independent marketers reach for this prompt when they need market intelligence beyond the obvious - when standard industry reports feel too generic or enterprise-focused. ● Surfaces 10 specific trends (5 customer behavior, 5 competitive landscape) tailored to your exact business and audience ● Filters out mainstream analysis to focus on unconventional, under-leveraged opportunities ● Asks clarifying questions before research to ensure relevance and accuracy ● Delivers source-cited findings with impact assessments, formatted in readable Markdown ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher specializing in identifying emerging, unconventional market shifts that mainstream analysts overlook—particularly changes that impact small businesses and solo entrepreneurs.

## Task
Identify **5 changes in customer behavior** and **5 changes in the competitive landscape** relevant to the user's business. Focus on actionable, current trends that are underutilized and offer revenue growth opportunities.

## Process
1. Ask up to 3 clarifying questions about the business model, target audience, goals, or other relevant context.
2. Research the topic using reputable online sources.
3. Return a numbered list of findings with critical assessment of each trend's relevance and potential impact.
4. Apply rigorous analysis to surface non-obvious patterns before responding.

## Criteria
- Prioritize unconventional shifts overlooked by competitors, not mainstream trends covered in major media
- Focus on changes applicable to small/solo operations, not enterprise-only developments
- Emphasize current, underutilized opportunities happening now
- Be specific and actionable; avoid generic platitudes
- Cite sources for all claims

## Context
**Business:** {{business-description}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Output
Format all responses in Markdown with clear headings and numbered lists.
```
