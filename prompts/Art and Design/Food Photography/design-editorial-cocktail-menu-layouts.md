# Editorial Cocktail Menu Layout Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Editorial Cocktail Menu Layout Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates sophisticated, magazine-style cocktail menu layouts with photorealistic drink photography and professional typography for hospitality businesses. This cocktail menu design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces clean editorial grid layouts featuring individual cocktail photographs arranged in a 2-row card system. Each card includes the drink name in bold uppercase typography, a flavor-driven description, and pricing - all set against warm neutral backgrounds with soft natural lighting. The prompt delivers fashion-magazine-inspired typography hierarchy with generous spacing, clean dividers, and restrained color palettes of off-white, sand tones, deep olive, dark brown, and muted burgundy. Real use cases include creating visual marketing materials for upscale bars, restaurant beverage programs, and hospitality brand presentations without expensive photoshoots. This prompt is ideal for bar owners, restaurant marketers, graphic designers, and hospitality brands who need professional menu visuals quickly. ● Produces photorealistic cocktail photography arranged in clean grid layouts with editorial typography and generous white space. ● Customizable menu headlines, cocktail names, flavor descriptions, and pricing through the menu-customization variable. ● Delivers 4K vertical-format designs with soft natural lighting, minimal styling, and warm neutral color palettes. ● Generates modern bar aesthetic meets design magazine composition - refined, atmospheric, and ready for print or digital use. ## Prompt

```
Design a contemporary cocktail menu presented as a clean editorial grid layout. The menu is displayed on a neutral, textured background inspired by stone or concrete, photographed in soft natural daylight. The layout features a strong typographic hierarchy with a confident headline set in a modern serif or high-contrast sans-serif typeface.

The menu is organized into a 2-row grid of cocktail cards. Each card contains a single cocktail photographed individually from a slightly elevated angle with minimal glassware styling and natural shadows. No hands, no people, no props beyond subtle garnish.

Each cocktail card includes: cocktail name in bold uppercase typography, a short flavor-driven description (not recipe-like), and price aligned consistently.

{{menu-customization}}

Color palette: warm-neutral and sophisticated with off-white or sand-toned background, deep olive, dark brown, muted burgundy, or charcoal typography accents. No bright reds or loud colors.

Typography is editorial and fashion-magazine inspired with large titles, generous spacing, clean dividers, and calm rhythm. Text feels premium and restrained.

Lighting: soft natural or diffused studio light with realistic shadows. Composition: clean grid-based layout, evenly spaced cocktail cards, strong white space. Overall mood is modern cocktail bar meets design magazine — refined, confident, and atmospheric.

Photorealistic, editorial, portfolio-ready quality. 4k resolution, vertical aspect ratio.
```
