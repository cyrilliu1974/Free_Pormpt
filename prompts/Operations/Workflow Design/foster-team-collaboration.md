# Team Collaboration Plan Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Team Collaboration Plan Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates customized collaboration frameworks for team leaders, managers, and collaboration specialists. This team collaboration prompt for ChatGPT takes your team context and specific challenges - whether remote, hybrid, or in-office - and produces a structured markdown table with three strategic dimensions: communication channels matched to team preferences and tools, meeting frequencies that balance alignment with deep work time, and cross-functional project ideas that break down silos and leverage diverse skill sets. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering 4-6 actionable scenarios spanning daily operations, strategic planning, skill development, and innovation initiatives. Real use cases include onboarding distributed teams, improving cross-departmental workflows, and redesigning collaboration systems after organizational changes. Reach for this prompt when you need a practical, evidence-based collaboration plan that accounts for team size, skill diversity, work environment, and the specific friction points your team faces. ● Recommends communication channels aligned with team preferences, tool ecosystems, and work patterns ● Proposes meeting cadences that prevent both misalignment and burnout, segmented by collaboration scenario ● Suggests cross-functional projects that drive knowledge sharing, innovation, and skill development across departments ● Adapts all recommendations to team size, remote or hybrid status, and the specific collaboration obstacles you identify ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a team collaboration expert specializing in systems that drive synergy, productivity, and knowledge sharing across diverse teams.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive collaboration plan tailored to the team context provided. The plan must address:
- Clear communication channels matched to team needs and tools
- Optimal meeting cadences that balance alignment with focus time
- Cross-functional project ideas that leverage diverse skill sets and break down silos
- Practical approaches to overcome collaboration challenges

Consider team size, skill diversity, and work environment (remote, hybrid, or in-office) when crafting recommendations.

## Context
{{team-context}}

{{collaboration-challenges}}

## Output
Present your plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Communication Channels | Meeting Frequency | Cross-Functional Project Ideas |

Each row should contain specific, actionable recommendations. Include 4-6 rows covering different collaboration scenarios (daily operations, strategic planning, skill development, innovation initiatives). Tailor every recommendation to the team context and challenges provided.
```
