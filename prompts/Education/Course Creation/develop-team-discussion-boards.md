# Discussion Board Implementation Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Discussion Board Implementation Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive deployment strategies for online discussion boards tailored to educational institutions and student populations. This discussion board prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your institution's context, learning objectives, and technical infrastructure to produce a seven-part implementation plan covering needs analysis, board architecture, participation guidelines, engagement features, content organization, moderation protocols, and assessment integration. Educational technologists, instructional designers, and faculty use it to launch or redesign discussion boards in learning management systems like Canvas, Moodle, or Blackboard. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini and delivers actionable recommendations organized by dependency grammar principles for clarity. Reach for this prompt when planning a new online discussion space, restructuring existing forums, or aligning collaborative tools with specific course outcomes. ● Evaluates institutional needs and student demographics to tailor board structure and participation norms. ● Designs clear participation guidelines with quality standards and response frequency expectations. ● Specifies engagement tools like threading, reactions, and peer feedback mechanisms to drive meaningful interaction. ● Creates moderation workflows and assessment rubrics that measure both participation quantity and learning impact. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational technologist specializing in online collaborative learning environments.

## Task
Create a comprehensive implementation plan for discussion boards that foster student engagement and enhance learning outcomes.

## Context
**Institution & learners:** {{institution-and-student-context}}
(Describe your educational institution type, student demographics, group sizes, and any relevant characteristics)

**Learning objectives:** {{learning-objectives}}
(State what students should achieve through discussion board participation)

**Technology & assessment:** {{infrastructure-and-assessment}}
(Outline your current learning management system, technical capabilities, and how you evaluate student participation)

## Requirements
Your plan must address:

1. **Needs Analysis** – Evaluate institutional requirements and student learning preferences
2. **Board Architecture** – Design structure, layout, and navigation that supports learning objectives
3. **Participation Guidelines** – Define clear expectations for student contributions, response frequency, and quality standards
4. **Engagement Features** – Specify tools that promote meaningful interaction (threading, reactions, peer feedback mechanisms)
5. **Content Organization** – Establish logical categorization and topic taxonomy for easy navigation
6. **Moderation Protocols** – Create policies and workflows to maintain respectful, constructive dialogue
7. **Assessment Integration** – Design rubrics and analytics to measure participation quality and learning impact

## Output
Deliver a structured implementation plan using:
- **Main headings** for each of the seven components above
- **Subheadings** to break down complex sections
- **Bullet points** for actionable details, specific features, and recommendations

Align all recommendations with the stated learning objectives and institutional context.
```
