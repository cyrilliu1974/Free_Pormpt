# Household Product Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Household Product Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional 2×2 grid advertisements for household cleaning products in modern home environments. This household product ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces four equal-sized panels: a hero bottle shot on a clean countertop, a pour-action closeup showing the product in use, a fresh laundry result with natural lighting, and an organized home scene with neatly folded towels. The prompt specifies a bright modern laundry room setting with white quartz surfaces, soft natural daylight, and a {{color-palette}} brand color scheme you customize alongside the {{product-name}} variable. Every detail is calibrated for trustworthy, fresh product photography that balances aspirational lifestyle imagery with practical demonstration shots. Designers, marketers, and e-commerce teams working on detergent, fabric care, or cleaning-product campaigns will find this prompt saves hours of art direction and delivers consistent, retail-ready visuals. ● Structured 2×2 layout with hero bottle, pour action, fresh result, and organized-home panels ● Bright modern laundry room environment with white quartz, natural daylight, and minimalist décor ● Customizable product name and brand color palette via two simple variables ● Professional product photography style with soft shadows, balanced exposure, and high realism ## Prompt

```
Create a 2×2 grid advertisement in 5:4 aspect ratio showcasing {{product-name}} household detergent in a modern home setting.

**Environment & Styling:**
Bright modern laundry room with white quartz countertop, soft natural daylight from window. Background includes folded white towels, washing machine, soft green indoor plant, minimal wicker basket. Clean, fresh, organized mood. {{color-palette}} brand color scheme with clean bold sans-serif typography.

**Product Details:**
Large ergonomic plastic bottle with measuring cap. Fresh scent formula, deep stain removal, color protection technology, family-size packaging.

**Panel Layout (equal-sized quadrants):**

**Panel 1 – Hero Bottle Shot:**
Product bottle centered on clean countertop, front-facing angle. Reliable, clean mood.

**Panel 2 – Pour Action:**
Detergent being poured into measuring cap. Bright lighting with liquid clarity, practical and effective mood.

**Panel 3 – Fresh Laundry Result:**
Crisp white shirt hanging with sunlight behind. Fresh, soft mood.

**Panel 4 – Organized Laundry Scene:**
Folded clean towels stacked neatly. Comfort and home mood.

**Technical Execution:**
Professional product and lifestyle photography. Soft diffused daylight, bright balanced exposure, soft natural shadows. High realism, minimal home aesthetic, modern household photography style. Trustworthy, fresh, practical brand personality.

**Avoid:**
Dirty laundry, cluttered room, overexposed highlights, harsh flash lighting, cheap packaging texture, cartoon style, low-resolution detail, watermarks, text overlays.
```
