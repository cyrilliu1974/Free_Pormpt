# Social Media Content Calendar Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Calendar Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a complete 30-day posting schedule for social media strategists, marketers, and content creators. It produces a comprehensive markdown table that maps daily post concepts, content formats, marketing funnel goals, key hooks, and strategic notes organized around 3-5 content pillars tailored to your niche, audience, and platforms. This social media content calendar prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok builds a strategic narrative arc across the month, balancing educational posts, entertainment, community engagement, social proof, and conversion-focused content while accounting for platform algorithms and audience fatigue cycles. Reach for it when you need to plan cohesive campaigns that guide audiences from awareness to action across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter, or YouTube. ● Identifies 3-5 core content pillars aligned with your niche and audience needs ● Produces weekly markdown tables with day, theme, post concept, format type, primary goal, hook, and strategic notes ● Includes a strategic overview explaining the monthly narrative arc and conversion funnel logic ● Provides cross-platform repurposing notes and adaptation strategies for multi-channel campaigns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist and content architect specializing in viral campaigns and sustainable content ecosystems across all major platforms.

## Task
Design a comprehensive 30-day content calendar that strategically balances audience growth, community engagement, and conversion optimization.

## Context
Effective content calendars are strategic narratives that build momentum, establish authority, and guide audiences from awareness to action. The calendar must:

- Identify 3-5 core content pillars aligned with the niche and audience needs
- Map a strategic flow that builds narrative momentum across 30 days
- Balance educational content, entertainment, community-building, social proof, and conversion-focused posts
- Incorporate platform algorithms, engagement patterns, and content fatigue cycles
- Create thematic coherence while maintaining daily variety
- Design posts that build on each other, creating anticipation and narrative threads
- Include repurposing opportunities and cross-platform adaptation strategies

**Niche/Industry:** {{niche-industry}}  
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}  
**Primary Platforms:** {{primary-platforms}}  
**Main Content Goals:** {{content-goals}}  
**Unique Value Proposition:** {{unique-value-proposition}}

## Output
Deliver the calendar in a comprehensive markdown table with these columns:

- Day
- Content Theme
- Post Concept
- Format Type
- Primary Goal
- Key Hook/Angle
- Strategic Notes

Structure:

1. **Strategic Overview** (150-200 words) explaining the monthly narrative arc and how it serves the conversion funnel
2. **Content Pillars** (3-5 identified themes)
3. **Weekly Calendar Tables** (one table per week with clear section breaks)
4. **Cross-Platform Notes** summarizing repurposing and adaptation strategies

Ensure each post serves a specific purpose in the marketing funnel and connects to the broader monthly narrative.
```
