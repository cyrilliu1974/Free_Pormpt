# Morning Routine Night-Before Checklist Generator

## 簡介

The Morning Routine Night-Before Checklist Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a personalized evening preparation checklist for professionals who want to maximize morning productivity. This morning routine prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific morning routine details and produces a targeted 10-task checklist covering personal hygiene prep, clothing selection, breakfast planning, commute logistics, and workspace readiness. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming vague intentions into actionable evening tasks that save concrete time and mental energy the next morning. Real use cases include busy parents preparing for early meetings, remote workers establishing boundaries between work and home, and professionals who struggle with morning decision fatigue. Reach for this prompt when you want to front-load choices and preparations to the evening before, reducing morning chaos and building a reliable pre-work system. ● Creates 10 concrete tasks spanning hygiene, wardrobe, meals, commute, and workspace setup ● Focuses exclusively on realistic evening preparations that measurably reduce morning time pressure ● Eliminates vague advice in favor of specific actions that save minutes and reduce cognitive load ● Adapts to your unique morning routine details, work schedule, and preparation challenges ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in routine optimization and time management.

## Task
Create a personalized checklist of 10 specific tasks to complete the night before a workday that will streamline the morning routine and maximize productivity.

## Context
{{morning-routine-details}}

The checklist should minimize morning decision-making and preparation time by front-loading as many choices and actions as possible to the evening before.

## Requirements
- Each task must be specific and actionable
- Cover all morning routine aspects: personal hygiene prep, clothing, breakfast, commute planning, and workspace readiness
- Focus only on tasks that can realistically be completed the night before
- Avoid vague suggestions; each item should save concrete time or mental energy in the morning

## Output
Deliver the checklist in this format:

**Evening Preparation Checklist for Maximum Morning Productivity:**

1. [Task 1]
2. [Task 2]
3. [Task 3]
4. [Task 4]
5. [Task 5]
6. [Task 6]
7. [Task 7]
8. [Task 8]
9. [Task 9]
10. [Task 10]
```
