# SEO Audit Checklist Generator for Websites

## 簡介

The SEO Audit Checklist Generator for Websites is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive, structured SEO audits for any website URL, delivering actionable recommendations across keyword strategy, on-page optimization, off-page factors, and technical infrastructure. This SEO audit prompt for ChatGPT analyzes a website against current search engine best practices and outputs a detailed checklist with pass/fail indicators and specific recommendations for each element. It evaluates keyword relevance and placement, title tags and meta descriptions, header structure, content quality, internal linking, image optimization, backlink profiles, site speed, mobile-friendliness, XML sitemaps, SSL certificates, structured data, and more. The prompt prioritizes the top three action items by potential impact, making it practical for SEO professionals, digital marketers, and website owners who need clear next steps rather than generic advice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need a systematic evaluation of a website's SEO health or want to identify high-impact optimization opportunities quickly. ● Evaluates 20+ SEO factors across keyword strategy, on-page, off-page, and technical categories with pass/fail indicators and site-specific recommendations ● Identifies the top three priority action items ranked by potential impact on search performance ● Analyzes technical elements including mobile-friendliness, site speed, structured data, canonical tags, and XML sitemaps ● Provides an overall SEO health rating and avoids generic advice in favor of actionable, targeted improvements ## Prompt
