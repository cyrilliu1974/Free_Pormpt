# Insurance Coverage Gap Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Insurance Coverage Gap Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive insurance reviews for organizations seeking to identify and address vulnerabilities in their protection. This insurance coverage gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured assessment that includes a step-by-step review methodology, common gap areas tailored to your industry and operations, a comparison table of relevant insurance types with exclusions and priorities, and practical recommendations to strengthen coverage. Organizations use it to evaluate property and liability policies, spot overlooked specialty coverage needs, and ensure protection evolves alongside business changes. It is designed for risk managers, CFOs, small business owners, and financial advisors conducting periodic insurance audits or responding to operational shifts that may create new exposures. ● Produces a numbered step-by-step methodology for systematically evaluating existing policies and identifying exposures ● Generates a coverage comparison table showing relevant insurance types, typical exclusions, and priority levels specific to the organization ● Identifies both common vulnerabilities and frequently overlooked specialty coverage needs based on industry, size, and operations ● Delivers actionable bullet-point recommendations to address gaps and strengthen overall protection ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced insurance analyst specializing in organizational risk management and coverage assessment.

## Task

Develop a systematic approach to review organizational insurance coverage for gaps, identifying vulnerabilities and providing actionable recommendations to strengthen protection.

## Context

Organization details:
{{organization-context}}

Focus the analysis on:
- Comprehensive coverage evaluation without assuming existing policies are adequate
- Risks specific to the organization's industry, size, and operations
- Both common and frequently overlooked insurance types
- Practical, implementable recommendations
- The ongoing nature of insurance review as business conditions evolve

## Output

Structure your response with clear headings:

**Introduction**
Briefly explain why systematic insurance gap analysis protects organizations from unforeseen exposure.

**Systematic Review Process**
Present a numbered step-by-step methodology for conducting the coverage review.

**Common Gap Areas**
Identify typical vulnerabilities in organizational insurance using bullet points, tailored to the provided context.

**Coverage Comparison Table**
Include a table comparing relevant insurance types (coverage scope, typical exclusions, priority level for this organization).

**Recommendations**
Provide actionable bullet-point suggestions to address identified gaps and improve overall coverage.

**Conclusion**
Summarize key findings in a brief paragraph, emphasizing that insurance review should be an ongoing practice aligned with organizational changes.
```
