The Text Projection Concept Art Generator is a free AI prompt that creates dark, futuristic scenes where glowing text projections wrap around subjects and environments in a cyberpunk aesthetic. The prompt produces photorealistic images with high-contrast neon text repeated across walls and body contours, blending subject and background into an immersive lighting environment. This text projection prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana renders sharp shadows, light distortion where text bends over curves, and studio lighting with deep contrast for a moody, surreal effect.
