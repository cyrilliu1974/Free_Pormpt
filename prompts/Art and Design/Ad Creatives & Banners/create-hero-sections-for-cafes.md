# Cafe Hero Section Image Generator

## 簡介

The Cafe Hero Section Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates professional fullscreen hero photographs for artisanal café and bakery websites. This café hero section prompt for Midjourney produces food photography compositions with your chosen baked goods arranged on rustic wooden counters, bathed in warm golden morning light with shallow depth of field. The prompt structures elegant serif typography overlays, subtle animated steam effects, soft parallax scrolling cues, and clean call-to-action button placement in the lower third - all optimized for 16:9 aspect ratio website headers. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, balancing appetizing close-ups with negative space for text legibility. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping website hero images for cafés, bakeries, coffee shops, or any food business that wants a sophisticated, inviting online presence. ● Specify any baked goods (croissants, pastries, bread, cakes) and custom headline text via two simple variables. ● Outputs professional food photography with rustic aesthetic, warm lighting, and shallow depth optimized for website headers. ● Includes design cues for typography overlay, animated steam, parallax scrolling, and CTA button placement. ● Maintains negative space and text legibility so your headline and call-to-action remain readable over the image. ## Prompt
