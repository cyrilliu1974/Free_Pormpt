# Character Profile Generator for Fiction Writers

## 簡介

The Character Profile Generator for Fiction Writers is a free AI prompt that creates structured, emotionally resonant character profiles for novelists and storytellers. Built for literary character development, this character profile prompt for ChatGPT produces eight-section profiles covering physical description, personality traits, formative background events, ambitions and fears, relationship dynamics, character evolution arcs, unique qualities and conflicts, and thematic integration. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a brief character description and central theme into a comprehensive blueprint for your protagonist, antagonist, or supporting cast. Writers working on novels, short fiction, or screenplay character development use it to ensure consistency, depth, and thematic coherence across long narrative arcs. ● Produces eight distinct profile sections - physical presence, personality strengths and weaknesses, backstory, motivations and fears, relationships, evolution, distinctive traits, and thematic ties - in one pass ● Connects character attributes directly to the novel's central theme, ensuring every profile element supports the story's deeper meaning ● Maps character evolution across the narrative arc, identifying turning points, conflicts, and transformative decisions ● Generates relationship dynamics with other characters, showing how connections shape growth and drive plot progression ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert literary character developer crafting a detailed, multifaceted character profile for a novel.

## Task
Create a comprehensive character profile that serves as a cornerstone for the narrative. The character must be compelling, relatable, and integral to the story's thematic exploration. The profile will guide the character's decisions, interactions, and evolution throughout the novel.

## Context
Character and story foundation:
{{character-description}}

Novel's central theme:
{{theme}}

## Output
Produce a structured character profile covering:

**Physical Description**  
Age, stature, defining features, and how these attributes affect their presence and interactions within the story world.

**Personality Traits**  
Core strengths and weaknesses that influence decisions, relationships, and the challenges they face.

**Background Story**  
Key formative events that shaped their worldview, motivations, and current circumstances.

**Ambitions and Fears**  
What drives the character forward and what holds them back, creating internal tension.

**Relationships with Other Characters**  
Dynamics with key figures in the story, how these relationships evolve, and their impact on character growth and plot progression.

**Character Evolution**  
Major turning points, conflicts, and decisions that transform the character across the narrative arc.

**Unique Qualities and Conflicts**  
Distinctive attributes and tensions that add complexity and engage readers.

**Integration with Theme**  
How the character's journey embodies and enriches the novel's thematic essence.

The profile should be detailed, emotionally resonant, and ready to guide the character's integration into the narrative.
```
