# YouTube Video Script Generator for Brand Awareness

## 簡介

The YouTube Video Script Generator for Brand Awareness is a free AI prompt that creates structured, engagement-optimized video scripts for content creators and marketers looking to convert viewers into visitors. This YouTube script prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write complete video scripts divided into five strategic sections: a scroll-stopping hook for the first 5-10 seconds, a credibility-building introduction that previews value, main content organized into 3-5 key points with transitions, a conclusion that reinforces takeaways, and a direct call-to-action tied to your business goals. You provide your topic, target audience, website or product URL, brand voice, and desired video length, and the prompt produces talking points, transitional cues, and on-screen text suggestions formatted for immediate use. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need conversion-focused YouTube content that balances storytelling with strategic audience navigation toward a specific action. ● Structures scripts into five conversion-optimized sections with clear headings and bullet-point talking points ● Ensures every script opens with a hook designed to stop viewers in the first 5-10 seconds ● Integrates your business context - topic, audience, URL, brand voice, video length - throughout the narrative ● Delivers actionable call-to-action language that guides viewers to visit your website or take the next step ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert YouTube scriptwriter specializing in conversion-focused content that builds brand awareness and drives traffic.

## Task
Write a complete video script structured for maximum engagement and action. Organize the script into five distinct sections:

**Hook** (first 5-10 seconds)  
- Opening line that stops the scroll  
- Tease the core value or transformation  

**Introduction** (establish credibility and preview)  
- Who this is for and why it matters now  
- What the viewer will learn or gain  

**Main Content** (deliver on the promise)  
- 3-5 key points with concrete examples or stories  
- Use transitions that maintain momentum  
- Reference {{business-context}} throughout to keep relevance high  

**Conclusion** (reinforce and transition)  
- Summarize the main takeaway  
- Set up the call-to-action naturally  

**Call-to-Action**  
- Direct instruction (visit, subscribe, download)  
- Clear next step tied to {{business-context}}  

## Context
{{business-context}} should include: your topic, target audience, website or product URL, brand voice (tone and style), and desired video length in minutes.

## Output
Deliver the script with each section as a **bold heading**, followed by bullet points for key talking points, transitional cues, and on-screen text suggestions where helpful. Write in a voice that matches the brand while speaking directly to the audience.
```
