# Streetwear Packaging Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Streetwear Packaging Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic studio photography of colorful apparel mailer bags for fashion brands and streetwear labels. This streetwear packaging mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces high-resolution images of plastic mailer bags stacked in dynamic, editorial-style compositions. Each generated image features asymmetrical label placements, mixed color palettes, and playful text blocks that capture the energy of indie streetwear culture. The prompt outputs top-down or angled studio shots with soft lighting, natural wrinkles in the packaging material, and subtle shadows that create depth and texture. Fashion brands use it to generate lookbook imagery, website hero visuals, and social media content without organizing physical product photography shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need distinctive packaging mockups that feel personal and culture-driven rather than generic retail, especially for emerging streetwear labels, apparel startups, and creative agencies building visual identities for fashion clients. ● Produces photorealistic mailer bag mockups with customizable brand names, graphic styles, and color palettes specified through three simple variables. ● Generates asymmetrical, non-uniform label designs across each package, with playful text blocks and visual stickers in confident, ironic tones. ● Creates studio lighting effects with gentle reflections on semi-gloss plastic, natural wrinkles, and organic stacking patterns that show texture and material weight. ● Outputs tight-cropped, high-resolution compositions that balance visual chaos with color harmony, referencing fashion editorials and concept store aesthetics. ## Prompt

```
## Scene
Photorealistic studio shot of vibrant fashion apparel packaging stacked casually with abundance and movement. Soft plastic mailer bags with semi-gloss finish, natural wrinkles, and organic irregular stacking creating visible compression and subtle inner shadows through slightly opaque material.

## Brand & Design
{{brand-name}} prominently featured across packages. {{graphic-style}} applied with asymmetrical placement, each package using a different non-uniform design. {{color-palette}} as base colors combined with deep red, electric blue, and black accents—each package uses a different color combination that pops against neutral studio surface.

Small bold text blocks mixed with visual stickers in playful, confident, ironic tone—short phrases or single words, rotated and offset with imperfect alignment. Labels feel personal and collectible, not generic retail.

## Composition & Lighting
Top-down or slight angle view, tight crop emphasizing texture and repetition. Visual chaos balanced with color harmony. Soft diffused studio lighting creates gentle reflections on plastic and subtle shadows between packages. Editorial, fashion-forward mood referencing fashion editorials, indie brands, and concept stores.

High resolution, no people present, no real brand logos, culture-driven streetwear aesthetic.
```
