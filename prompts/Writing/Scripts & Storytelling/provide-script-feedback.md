# Script Feedback and Story Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Script Feedback and Story Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that develops original narrative concepts for screenwriters working in film and television. Rather than providing feedback on existing scripts, this prompt creates complete story frameworks built around a specific theme and genre. This story concept prompt for ChatGPT walks through theme analysis, character development, plot structure, and world-building to produce a comprehensive creative blueprint. You specify a theme (like isolation, redemption, or identity) and a genre (thriller, drama, sci-fi, etc.), and the prompt generates 2-3 central characters with clear arcs, a complete plot structure from inciting incident through resolution, setting details that amplify your theme, and innovative elements that set your story apart. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering narrative concepts ready for script development. Screenwriters use this prompt during the ideation phase when they need a strong conceptual foundation before writing scenes and dialogue, or when they want to explore how a particular theme can drive a genre story. ● Examines how your chosen theme connects to universal human experiences and serves as the narrative's emotional core ● Develops 2-3 central characters whose desires, flaws, and growth arcs directly illuminate different facets of the theme ● Structures a complete plotline including inciting incident, rising tension, climax, and thematic resolution ● Identifies innovative elements - unusual structure, character dynamics, or genre subversions - that distinguish your story from conventional narratives ● Provides multiple potential endings and working titles to guide further development ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced screenwriter developing original narrative concepts for film or television.

## Task
Generate a detailed story concept that balances commercial appeal and critical depth. The concept should provide clear creative direction while remaining flexible for further development.

## Context
Theme: {{theme}}
Genre: {{genre}}

Explore how the theme connects to universal human experiences and serves as the emotional core of the narrative. Consider how genre conventions can be leveraged or subverted to create fresh storytelling.

## Output
Deliver a structured narrative concept including:

**Theme Analysis**: Examine the universal aspects of the theme and its relevance to human experience.

**Genre Approach**: Identify how genre conventions enhance or can be challenged to serve the theme.

**Character Outlines**: Develop 2-3 central characters whose desires, flaws, and growth arcs are tied to the theme. Show how their journeys illuminate different facets of the thematic questions.

**Plot Structure**: Craft a plotline that evolves naturally from character actions and thematic exploration. Include:
- Inciting incident and central conflict
- Rising tension and complications
- Climax
- Resolution that honors character arcs

**Setting & World-Building**: Establish a setting that amplifies the theme and fits the genre. If applicable, define key world rules, cultures, or conflicts.

**Innovative Elements**: Introduce 1-2 unique concepts that distinguish this narrative—an unusual structure, character dynamic, or novel twist on genre expectations.

**Potential Endings**: Outline 2-3 possible conclusions that resonate with the theme and provide satisfying character resolution.

**Working Titles**: Suggest 3 titles that capture the story's essence.

Ensure the concept is detailed enough to guide script development but open to adaptation and refinement.
```
