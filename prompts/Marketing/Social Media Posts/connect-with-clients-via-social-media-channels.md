# Social Media Campaign Strategy Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Campaign Strategy Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates platform-specific social media campaigns designed to engage target audiences and build customer relationships. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured campaign plan organized by platform, covering content creation guidelines, engagement tactics, community management activities, and platform-specific optimization tips. You provide your product or service, target audience, chosen social media channels, brand voice, and campaign goals, and the prompt generates actionable recommendations tailored to each platform's unique characteristics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering bullet-point strategies that balance content creation with active relationship building. Reach for this prompt when you need to launch a multi-platform social media campaign, plan content calendars across different channels, or develop engagement protocols that turn followers into customers. ● Delivers platform-by-platform breakdowns with content types, posting frequency, and format recommendations ● Includes practical engagement tactics for responding to comments, messages, and mentions on each channel ● Provides relationship-building activities such as user-generated content campaigns and community initiatives ● Aligns all recommendations with your brand voice and campaign goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in audience engagement and community building.

## Task
Create a comprehensive social media campaign strategy that promotes {{product-service}} to {{target-audience}} across {{social-media-channels}}. Structure your content recommendations using clear, actionable language that fosters audience interaction and builds relationships with potential customers.

## Context
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}
Campaign goals: {{campaign-goals}}

Tailor content to the unique characteristics and best practices of each platform. Focus on practical community management tactics and relationship-building techniques that drive measurable engagement.

## Output
Organize your strategy as a bullet-point list structured by social media platform. For each platform, provide:

- Content creation guidelines (post types, formats, frequency)
- Engagement tactics (how to interact with comments, messages, mentions)
- Relationship-building activities (community initiatives, user-generated content, partnerships)
- Platform-specific optimization tips
```
