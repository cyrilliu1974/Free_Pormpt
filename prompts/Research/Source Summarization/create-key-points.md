# Key Points Extractor for Any Topic

## 簡介

The Key Points Extractor for Any Topic is a free AI prompt that distills complex subjects into organized, beginner-friendly lists for researchers, writers, and educators. This key points prompt for ChatGPT walks the model through a research and synthesis process: gathering context, selecting the most significant insights, translating jargon into plain language, and organizing points from foundational concepts to advanced details. It produces lists that work equally well as introductory guides for newcomers and quick-reference refreshers for experts. Use it to summarize industry trends, explain technical concepts, or compile briefings on unfamiliar topics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to turn a broad or complex topic into a clear, scannable summary without losing accuracy or depth. ● Transforms technical or abstract subjects into plain-language explanations accessible to non-experts ● Structures points progressively, starting with foundational concepts before advancing to specifics ● Includes simplified citations for data and claims to ensure verifiability and credibility ● Balances breadth and conciseness, covering essential ground without overwhelming detail ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert technical writer skilled at distilling complex information into clear, accessible explanations for diverse audiences.

## Task
Research and compile a comprehensive list of key points on {{topic}} that serves both as an introduction for beginners and a quick reference for experienced readers. Each point must be accurate, relevant, and presented in layman-friendly language.

## Process
1. Gather information covering historical context, current trends, and future projections where applicable.
2. Select the most significant points that add distinct value to understanding the topic.
3. Translate complex concepts into plain language; define essential jargon clearly when unavoidable.
4. Organize points logically, beginning with foundational concepts before progressing to specifics and advanced ideas.
5. Include simplified citations for data, studies, or specific claims to ensure verifiability.
6. Balance comprehensiveness with conciseness—cover breadth without overwhelming detail.

## Output
Deliver a well-structured list where:
- Each point is concise yet informative
- Language is clear and accessible to non-experts
- Organization builds understanding progressively
- References validate key information
- The result works equally well as an introductory guide and a quick refresher
```
