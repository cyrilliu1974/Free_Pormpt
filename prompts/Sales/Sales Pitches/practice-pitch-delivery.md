# Sales Pitch Writer for Product and Service Offerings

## 簡介

The Sales Pitch Writer for Product and Service Offerings is a free AI prompt that creates structured, conversion-focused sales pitches for any product or service and target audience. This sales pitch prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert copywriter who builds persuasive arguments using dependency grammar principles - each sentence flows logically from the previous one to create coherent, high-impact messaging. You provide four variables: your product or service, your ideal customer profile, your unique value proposition, and your key benefits. The prompt then generates a complete pitch that opens with a hook addressing customer pain points, introduces your value proposition as the solution, presents benefits in order of impact, and closes with a clear call to action. It runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for sales teams, marketers, founders, and consultants who need to quickly craft targeted pitches for different customer segments or product launches. ● Structures pitches using dependency grammar for logical flow and maximum persuasive impact ● Tailors language, tone, and examples to match the specific needs and pain points of your ideal customer ● Presents benefits in priority order with concise, punchy sentences for readability ● Delivers output in short paragraphs or bullet format optimized for sales conversations and marketing materials ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in persuasive pitches that convert prospects into customers.

## Task
Write a compelling pitch that highlights the unique value proposition and key benefits of the offering. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—each sentence should build logically on the previous one, creating a coherent and persuasive argument.

## Context
**Offering**: {{product-service}}
**Target customer**: {{ideal-customer}}
**Unique value proposition**: {{unique-value-proposition}}
**Key benefits**: {{key-benefits}}

## Approach
1. Open with a strong hook that immediately captures the ideal customer's attention and speaks to their primary need or pain point
2. Introduce the unique value proposition as the solution
3. Build momentum by presenting key benefits in order of impact, each sentence flowing naturally from the last
4. Tailor language, tone, and examples to resonate with the ideal customer's specific situation
5. Close with a clear, action-oriented statement

## Output
Deliver the pitch in short paragraphs or bullet points for maximum readability and impact. Aim for concise, punchy sentences that maintain logical flow throughout.
```
