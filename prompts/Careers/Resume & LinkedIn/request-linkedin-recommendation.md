# LinkedIn Recommendation Request Message Generator

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Recommendation Request Message Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts genuine, professional recommendation requests for LinkedIn users seeking endorsements from colleagues and contacts. This LinkedIn recommendation request prompt for ChatGPT helps you create messages that balance appreciation for past collaboration with respect for the recipient's time. It structures your request around specific collaboration details, explains why their perspective matters, and includes a graceful opt-out so contacts never feel pressured. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing 150-250 word messages that feel conversational yet professional. Use it when updating your LinkedIn profile, changing roles, or building your personal brand through meaningful endorsements from people who know your work firsthand. ● Highlights specific collaboration details to remind contacts of your shared work and impact ● Includes a graceful opt-out that respects busy schedules and removes pressure ● Offers to provide context or project reminders that make writing easier for your contact ● Keeps messages concise at 150-250 words while maintaining warmth and professionalism ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional copywriter specializing in workplace communication and personal branding.

## Task
Draft a LinkedIn recommendation request message that is genuine, personal, and respectful. The request should highlight specific collaboration value, make a clear ask, and provide a graceful opt-out.

## Context
The user needs to request a LinkedIn recommendation from a professional contact. The message must balance appreciation for past work together with respect for the recipient's time, increasing the likelihood of receiving a thoughtful, positive recommendation.

## Output
Structure the message as follows:

**Opening**: Warm greeting and expression of appreciation for past collaboration.

**Collaboration highlight**: Briefly describe {{collaboration-details}} (the specific project or work you did together, their role, and how their skills/expertise/insights added value).

**The ask**: Explain you're enhancing your LinkedIn profile with recommendations from respected colleagues, and why their perspective would be particularly meaningful.

**Graceful out**: Acknowledge their busy schedule and make clear there's no pressure—you understand completely if they're unable to accommodate this request right now.

**Support offer**: Offer to provide any reminders, project details, or context they might need to write the recommendation.

**Close**: Express gratitude for considering the request with a warm, professional sign-off.

Keep the tone conversational yet professional. Aim for 150-250 words total—concise enough to respect their time while providing sufficient context.
```
