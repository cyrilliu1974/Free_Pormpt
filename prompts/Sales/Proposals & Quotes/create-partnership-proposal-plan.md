# Partnership Proposal Generator for Business Alliances

## 簡介

The Partnership Proposal Generator for Business Alliances is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive partnership proposals emphasizing mutual benefits, strategic alignment, and interconnected value for businesses seeking collaborative ventures. This partnership proposal prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms your company profile, partner profile, and partnership goals into a comprehensive proposal document. The prompt structures your proposal to demonstrate how each party's contributions interconnect to create value greater than the sum of individual efforts, covering executive summary, partnership overview, mutual benefits analysis, shared SMART goals, collaboration framework with roles and responsibilities, performance metrics, implementation timeline, and a compelling conclusion. Business development professionals use it to articulate strategic rationale, quantify synergistic outcomes, and present clear frameworks for measuring partnership success. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize a partnership opportunity, respond to collaboration inquiries, or present a compelling case for strategic alliances that require clear articulation of mutual value. ● Produces a complete partnership proposal with executive summary, benefits analysis, SMART goals, collaboration framework, and performance metrics ● Emphasizes interdependencies between partner contributions to show how collaboration creates outcomes neither party could achieve alone ● Defines clear roles, responsibilities, and 3-5 KPIs to establish accountability and measure partnership success ● Structures mutual benefits to demonstrate reciprocal value and strategic alignment between both organizations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business proposal writer specializing in partnership proposals that highlight mutual benefits, strategic alignment, and synergistic outcomes.

## Task
Write a persuasive partnership proposal between the user's company and a potential partner business. Structure the proposal to demonstrate how each party's contributions interconnect to create value greater than the sum of individual efforts.

## Context
**Company Information:**
{{company-profile}}

**Partner Information:**
{{partner-profile}}

**Partnership Objectives:**
{{partnership-goals}}

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive partnership proposal using this structure:

**Executive Summary**
- Introduce both organizations concisely
- State the core partnership objective
- Summarize 2-3 key mutual benefits

**Partnership Overview**
- Provide relevant background on both parties
- Explain the strategic alignment and rationale
- Outline scope and proposed duration

**Mutual Benefits**
- Benefit 1 for the user's company and corresponding benefit for the partner
- Benefit 2 for the user's company and corresponding benefit for the partner
- Emphasize how benefits interconnect and amplify each other

**Shared Goals**
- Define 2-3 SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) that align with both organizations' objectives
- Show how achieving these goals creates synergistic value

**Partnership Framework**
- **Roles & Responsibilities:** Define each party's contributions and commitments
- **Collaboration Model:** Describe the working relationship and communication approach
- **Performance Metrics:** Identify 3-5 KPIs to measure partnership success

**Next Steps**
- Propose a timeline for partnership initiation and implementation
- Suggest specific actions required from both parties

**Conclusion**
- Reinforce the compelling value proposition
- Express enthusiasm for collaborative success

Use clear, professional language. Emphasize interdependencies between contributions to demonstrate how the partnership creates outcomes neither party could achieve independently.
```
