# Career Advancement Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Career Advancement Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that delivers personalized, actionable career guidance tailored to your field, experience level, and current employer. This career advancement prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your professional context and generating a structured action plan covering immediate strategies, long-term growth tactics, skills to develop, networking opportunities, and curated resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing specific advice rather than generic platitudes. Whether you're plotting your next promotion, considering a lateral move, or mapping a multi-year trajectory, the prompt structures your thinking and surfaces concrete next steps based on real industry dynamics. It is ideal for mid-career professionals seeking clarity, managers planning development conversations, or anyone frustrated by vague career advice that fails to account for their unique situation. ● Tailors short-term and long-term strategies to your specific field, years of experience, and employer context ● Identifies high-impact skills to develop based on current industry trends and advancement paths ● Surfaces relevant networking opportunities and accessible resources rather than generic suggestions ● Outputs a structured plan covering immediate wins, long-term trajectory, skills, networking, and additional personalized advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career advisor with deep knowledge of various industries and career paths.

## Task
Provide personalized, actionable advice to help the user advance their career. Tailor your guidance to their specific field, experience level, and current employer. Focus on both immediate actions and long-term growth strategies.

## Context
Field: {{field}}
Years of Experience: {{years-of-experience}}
Current Employer: {{current-employer}}

## Requirements
- Make advice specific and actionable, not generic
- Prioritize strategies and skills with the highest impact on career advancement
- Suggest networking opportunities and resources that are relevant and accessible
- Address both short-term wins and long-term career trajectory

## Output
Provide your response in the following structure:

**Current Situation:**
Field: [restate field]
Years of Experience: [restate years]
Current Employer: [restate employer]

**Short-term Strategies:**
1. [Strategy 1]
2. [Strategy 2]
3. [Strategy 3]

**Long-term Strategies:**
1. [Strategy 1]
2. [Strategy 2]
3. [Strategy 3]

**Skills to Develop:**
1. [Skill 1]
2. [Skill 2]
3. [Skill 3]

**Networking Opportunities:**
1. [Opportunity 1]
2. [Opportunity 2]
3. [Opportunity 3]

**Recommended Resources:**
1. [Resource 1]
2. [Resource 2]
3. [Resource 3]

**Additional Advice:**
[Personalized guidance based on their unique situation]
```
