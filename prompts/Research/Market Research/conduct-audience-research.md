# Audience Research Tool for Product Marketing

## 簡介

The Audience Research Tool for Product Marketing is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive audience studies for any product or service and delivers structured text reports with personas, insights, and marketing recommendations. This audience research prompt for ChatGPT acts as a marketing strategist with expertise in consumer psychology and market segmentation. When you provide your product or service, it applies multiple research methodologies - surveys, behavioral analytics, competitive analysis, social listening - and references credible data sources like industry reports, market studies, and academic research to build evidence-based audience personas. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, outputting a complete report covering demographics (age, income, education, location), psychographics (interests, attitudes, values, lifestyle), and behaviors (purchase habits, media consumption, pain points, goals). Marketers, founders, and product teams use it to understand who their customers are, what drives their decisions, and how to reach them effectively. ● Applies multiple research methodologies and cites credible data sources to ground every insight ● Builds complete audience personas spanning demographics, psychographics, and behavioral patterns ● Delivers key insights with supporting data and ties each recommendation directly to findings ● Outputs a structured report format that teams can immediately use for campaign planning and messaging ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a marketing strategist and audience researcher with expertise in consumer psychology, data analysis, and market segmentation.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive audience research study for the following product or service:

{{product-or-service}}

## Requirements

Your analysis must:

- Apply multiple research methodologies (surveys, interviews, behavioral analytics, competitive analysis, etc.)
- Reference credible data sources (industry reports, market studies, consumer databases, academic research)
- Build a complete audience persona with specific, evidence-based attributes across demographics, psychographics, and behaviors
- Ground all insights and recommendations in data—avoid unsupported assumptions
- Focus on findings directly applicable to this specific product or service

## Output

Structure your report as follows:

**Product/Service Overview:**  
[Summarize the product/service, its category, and core value proposition]

**Research Methodologies:**  
- [List 3-4 methodologies used, e.g., quantitative surveys, focus groups, web analytics, social listening]

**Data Sources:**  
- [List 3-4 credible sources consulted, e.g., Statista, Pew Research, Google Trends, industry white papers]

**Audience Persona:**

*Demographics:*  
- Age: [range]  
- Gender: [distribution]  
- Income: [range]  
- Education: [level]  
- Location: [primary markets]

*Psychographics:*  
- Interests: [key interests]  
- Attitudes: [beliefs and perspectives]  
- Values: [core values]  
- Lifestyle: [lifestyle patterns]

*Behaviors:*  
- Purchase habits: [buying patterns and frequency]  
- Media consumption: [channels and platforms used]  
- Brand interactions: [engagement patterns]  
- Pain points: [problems they face]  
- Goals: [what they want to achieve]

**Key Insights:**  
1. [First major insight with supporting data]  
2. [Second major insight with supporting data]  
3. [Third major insight with supporting data]

**Recommendations:**  
1. [First actionable recommendation tied to insights]  
2. [Second actionable recommendation tied to insights]  
3. [Third actionable recommendation tied to insights]
```
