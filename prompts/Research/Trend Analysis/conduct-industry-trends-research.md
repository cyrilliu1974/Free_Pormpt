# Industry Trends Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The Industry Trends Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces evidence-based market analysis reports for entrepreneurs, strategists, and business planners. This industry trends prompt for ChatGPT works by instructing the model to act as an industry analyst who grounds every insight in credible sources. You provide your business venture, and the prompt returns a structured report containing an industry overview, three key trends shaping the sector, and a ranked opportunities table with descriptions and impact assessments. Every claim is tied to cited sources - industry reports, market research, and trade publications - so you can verify findings and explore further. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it a flexible research tool across major text models. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly understand the competitive landscape, validate a business idea, or prepare a strategic brief without manually sifting through dozens of reports. ● Produces an industry overview, three major trends, and a three-row opportunities table with impact ratings ● Requires citation of every claim, adding credibility and traceability to the analysis ● Prioritizes high-impact opportunities, helping you focus on what matters most ● Works across business sectors - SaaS, retail, manufacturing, healthcare, and beyond ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an industry analyst identifying market trends and opportunities relevant to a specific business venture. Ground every claim in credible sources.

## Context

Business venture: {{business-venture}}

## Task

Research industry trends and opportunities directly relevant to the business venture above. Prioritize opportunities with the highest potential impact. Do not speculate beyond what credible sources support. Cite all sources used.

## Output

**Industry Overview**
Provide a high-level summary of the current state of the industry.

**Key Trends**
1. First major trend shaping the industry
2. Second major trend shaping the industry
3. Third major trend shaping the industry

**Opportunities**

| Opportunity | Description | Potential Impact |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| First high-impact opportunity | Detailed description of the opportunity and how it relates to current trends | Assessment of market size, growth potential, and relevance to the venture |
| Second high-impact opportunity | Detailed description of the opportunity and how it relates to current trends | Assessment of market size, growth potential, and relevance to the venture |
| Third high-impact opportunity | Detailed description of the opportunity and how it relates to current trends | Assessment of market size, growth potential, and relevance to the venture |

**Sources**
List all credible sources cited (industry reports, market research, trade publications, etc.)
```
