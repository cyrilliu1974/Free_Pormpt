# Regulatory Compliance Monitoring Prompt for Business

## 簡介

The Regulatory Compliance Monitoring Prompt for Business is a free AI prompt that helps compliance experts and business leaders track regulatory changes and build actionable risk mitigation strategies. This regulatory compliance prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to research relevant regulatory bodies, identify recent rule changes, assess their operational impact on your specific business, and deliver a prioritized action plan in a custom markdown table format. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured compliance analysis that maps each regulatory change to its business impact, required actions, and implementation timeline. Use it when new regulations emerge in your industry, during quarterly compliance reviews, or when building proactive monitoring schedules for agencies that govern your operations. ● Identifies applicable regulatory agencies and their latest updates relevant to your industry and business type ● Assesses how rule changes affect operations, processes, obligations, and resource requirements ● Prioritizes compliance actions by impact and delivers implementation steps with realistic timelines ● Structures output in a customizable markdown table format that fits your workflow and reporting needs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a regulatory compliance expert monitoring regulatory changes and developing risk mitigation strategies.

## Task
Research relevant regulatory bodies and recent updates for the specified business, analyze their operational impact, and create an actionable compliance plan. Include:

- Identification of applicable regulatory agencies and their latest rule changes
- Impact assessment on business operations, processes, and obligations
- Prioritized action items with implementation steps
- Ongoing monitoring schedule with check-in cadence and risk review triggers

## Context
Industry and business type: {{industry-and-business-type}}

The analysis should be practical, prioritize high-impact changes, and account for implementation timelines and resource requirements typical for this business context.

## Output
Deliver your compliance analysis as a markdown table with {{table-structure}} (specify number and names of columns, e.g., "4 columns: Regulatory Change | Business Impact | Action Required | Timeline").

Ensure each row represents a distinct regulatory change or compliance requirement, with clear, actionable entries in each column.
```
