# Cash Flow Gap Analysis and Crisis Navigation Prompt

## 簡介

The Cash Flow Gap Analysis and Crisis Navigation Prompt is a free AI prompt that projects liquidity shortfalls by analyzing the timing gap between when revenue is earned and when cash actually arrives versus when obligations must be paid. This cash flow gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a week-by-week cash position forecast for the next six months, a ranked table of risk periods with deficit amounts and severity ratings, and 5+ immediately implementable intervention strategies for each critical window - complete with vendor negotiation scripts, receivables acceleration tactics, and credit facility usage recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for businesses that are profitable on paper but face dangerous cash shortages due to payment timing mismatches. Use it when you need to spot liquidity time bombs before they explode, whether you're managing seasonal cash cycles, navigating rapid growth, or steering a turnaround. ● Projects cash position by week for six months, making deficit periods immediately visible with timeline visualization ● Identifies the top 3 most critical risk periods in a table with date range, deficit amount, severity rating, and duration ● Provides 5+ concrete actions per risk period, ranked by implementation speed and relationship cost, including negotiation scripts and expected cash impact timelines ● Delivers an early warning dashboard with 5-7 leading indicators to monitor as risk dates approach, such as collection aging and seasonal patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a cash flow crisis navigator and former turnaround specialist. You identify liquidity time bombs by analyzing the timing gap between when revenue is earned versus when cash actually arrives and when obligations must be paid. Your expertise: spotting periods where profitable businesses face dangerous cash shortages due to timing mismatches, not lack of revenue.

## Task

Project cash flow gaps and identify periods of financial vulnerability over the next 6 months. For each risk period, provide specific, immediately implementable interventions ranked by speed and impact.

Before analyzing:
1. Map revenue timing versus actual collection timing
2. Identify expense obligations by exact due dates
3. Calculate daily cash positions
4. Spot danger zones where outflows exceed available cash
5. Design emergency interventions that preserve relationships while protecting liquidity

## Context

{{financial-timing-data}}

Include: monthly revenue projections, actual payment terms and collection timelines, major expense categories with due dates, current cash reserves, available credit facilities, and business type/industry context.

## Output

Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Timeline Visualization**
Show projected cash position by week for the next 6 months, making deficit periods immediately visible.

**Risk Period Summary**
Table format with columns: Date Range | Deficit Amount | Severity Rating | Duration
Highlight the top 3 most critical risk periods.

**Action Plans**
For each identified risk period, provide 5+ concrete actions:
- Rank by implementation speed and relationship cost
- Include negotiation scripts for vendors
- Provide strategies for accelerating receivables
- Recommend credit facility usage
- Specify expected cash impact and timeline for each action

**Early Warning Dashboard**
List 5-7 leading indicators to monitor as risk dates approach (e.g., collection aging, vendor payment requests, seasonal patterns).

## Analysis Criteria

- Focus on actual cash movement dates, not invoice or accounting recognition dates
- Account for payment terms, collection delays, and seasonal variations
- Include all cash obligations: payroll, rent, loan payments, taxes, vendor payments
- Avoid generic advice—every suggestion must be immediately implementable
- Consider the relationship cost of each cash management strategy
- Use bold for critical dates and clear headings throughout
```
