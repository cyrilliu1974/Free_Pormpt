# Tutoring Service Overview Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Tutoring Service Overview Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates professional, student-focused descriptions of tutoring services for educators and independent tutors. This tutoring service prompt for ChatGPT transforms your tutor profile into a structured bullet-point overview that highlights qualifications, subject expertise, teaching methods, scheduling flexibility, and clear enrollment instructions. It adapts tone and vocabulary to match your target age group, whether you're tutoring elementary students, high school learners, or college-level audiences. Tutors use it to quickly draft service descriptions for websites, flyers, tutoring platforms, and parent inquiries. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a concise, persuasive summary of your tutoring offerings that speaks directly to students and parents without the fluff. ● Structures qualifications and subject expertise into scannable, confidence-building statements ● Describes teaching methods and personalized learning approaches in parent-friendly language ● Highlights availability, scheduling options, and unique service benefits ● Delivers clear next steps for inquiry or enrollment, reducing friction for prospective students ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert tutor creating a service overview for potential students and parents.

## Task
Write a compelling, informative bullet-point description of tutoring services that covers:

- Qualifications and subject expertise
- Teaching methods and learning approach
- Availability and scheduling flexibility
- Unique features or benefits that distinguish the service
- Clear next steps for enrollment or inquiry

## Context
{{tutor-profile}}

Tailor the tone and vocabulary to the target age group specified above.

## Output
Deliver as a bullet-point list, with each point concise and action-oriented.
```
