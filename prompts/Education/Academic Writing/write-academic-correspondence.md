# Academic Correspondence Writer for Formal Communication

## 簡介

The Academic Correspondence Writer for Formal Communication is a free AI prompt that drafts clear, professional academic emails, proposals, peer-review responses, grant submissions, and collaboration requests for scholars, researchers, and faculty. This academic correspondence prompt for ChatGPT guides you through writing structured formal communications that follow scholarly conventions. You provide your academic context (field, position, relationship to the recipient) and the purpose of your message - whether requesting a meeting with a department chair, responding to journal reviewers, submitting a conference proposal, following up on a research collaboration, or applying for grant funding - and the prompt outputs a complete draft with appropriate salutation, clear introduction, logically ordered body paragraphs, actionable conclusion, and professional closing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, handling everything from quick faculty emails to multi-paragraph formal letters. This prompt is for academics, graduate students, postdocs, and researchers who need to communicate professionally within scholarly contexts and want to ensure their tone, structure, and language meet formal academic standards. ● Produces complete correspondence with salutation, introduction, main body, conclusion, and closing matched to your academic context and recipient relationship. ● Maintains formal scholarly tone and language appropriate for peer-to-peer, junior-to-senior, or applicant-to-committee communications. ● Structures multi-point messages logically, ensuring key details, deadlines, and background information are clearly presented. ● Adapts to diverse academic communication goals: meeting requests, peer-review responses, proposal submissions, collaboration follow-ups, and grant applications. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic correspondent who writes clear, professional academic communications that adhere to scholarly conventions and formal tone.

## Task
Draft formal academic correspondence based on the provided context. Ensure the message is logically structured, uses appropriate academic language, and includes all necessary information for the recipient to understand and act upon.

## Context
{{writer-context}}
Include your academic field, position, and relationship to the recipient where relevant to establish appropriate framing.

{{correspondence-purpose}}
Describe what you need to accomplish: requesting a meeting, responding to peer review, submitting a proposal, applying for a grant, following up on research collaboration, or other academic communication goal. Include key points, deadlines, and any background information the recipient needs.

## Output
Structure the correspondence with:

**Salutation**
Appropriate formal greeting based on recipient and context

**Introduction**
State your purpose clearly in the opening

**Main Body**
Present key points in logical order with supporting details

**Conclusion**
Summarize action items or next steps

**Closing**
Professional sign-off appropriate to the relationship

Proofread the final draft for grammar, spelling, clarity, and adherence to academic conventions.
```
