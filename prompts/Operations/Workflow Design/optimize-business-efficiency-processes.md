# Business Efficiency Time Sink Analysis Framework

## 簡介

The Business Efficiency Time Sink Analysis Framework is a free AI prompt that builds a structured system for diagnosing operational inefficiencies and delivering actionable elimination strategies across personnel, processes, and technology. This business efficiency prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete consultant-grade framework with identification methods, elimination strategies, and a worked example scenario tailored to your specific business operations context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, analyzing workforce allocation, process bottlenecks, and technology gaps through data collection methods, process mapping techniques, and technology audits. Use it when you need to systematically uncover where time is lost in daily operations and receive practical recommendations for streamlining workflows, optimizing team responsibilities, and implementing automation. ● Delivers personnel analysis methods to evaluate employee roles, activities, and resource allocation for efficiency gains ● Provides process mapping techniques to visualize workflows and identify bottlenecks, redundancies, and improvement opportunities ● Includes technology audit steps to assess current tools and recommend automation or upgrades ● Generates a real-world example scenario applying the framework with identified time sinks, tailored solutions, and expected outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business efficiency consultant with deep knowledge in operations management, process optimization, and time management techniques.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive framework for identifying time sinks in business operations and provide actionable recommendations to eliminate them. Analyze operations from multiple angles: personnel, processes, and technology.

## Context
Business operations context:
{{business-operations}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis in the following structure:

### 1. Introduction
- Explain the importance of identifying and eliminating time sinks
- Outline the multi-angle approach (personnel, processes, technology)

### 2. Identification Framework
- **Personnel Analysis**: Methods for analyzing employee activities, roles, and responsibilities to identify inefficiencies
- **Process Mapping**: Techniques for visualizing and analyzing business processes to pinpoint bottlenecks and redundancies
- **Technology Audit**: Steps for evaluating the effectiveness and efficiency of current technology solutions
- **Data Collection Methods**: Strategies for gathering data to support identification

### 3. Elimination Strategies
- **Personnel Strategies**: Recommendations for optimizing workforce allocation, training, and communication
- **Process Optimization**: Strategies for streamlining processes, eliminating redundancies, and improving workflows
- **Technology Solutions**: Technology upgrades or implementations that can automate tasks and improve efficiency
- **Change Management**: Best practices for implementing and managing organizational changes

### 4. Example Scenario
- **Scenario Description**: Present a relevant real-world example facing efficiency challenges
- **Identified Time Sinks**: Apply the framework to pinpoint specific time sinks
- **Recommended Solutions**: Provide tailored recommendations based on elimination strategies
- **Expected Outcomes**: Describe anticipated benefits and efficiency gains

### 5. Conclusion
- Summarize key points and potential impact
- Encourage adaptation to the specific business context

**Requirements:**
- Make the framework adaptable to various industries and business sizes
- Ensure all recommendations are actionable and practical
- Explain technical terms clearly for accessibility
- Use markdown formatting with clear headings, bullet points, bold/italic emphasis, and ample whitespace for readability
```
