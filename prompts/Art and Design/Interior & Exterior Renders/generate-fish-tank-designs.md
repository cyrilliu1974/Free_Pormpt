# Fish Tank Design Generator

## 簡介

The Fish Tank Design Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic aquarium visuals for designers, hobbyists, and content creators exploring aquatic aesthetics. This fish tank design prompt for Midjourney produces wide-angle images of customizable fish subjects placed inside user-defined aquarium environments, rendered with cinematic lighting in a 16:9 aspect ratio. You control the rendering style, the type of fish or aquatic subject, and the aquarium setting - from minimalist modern tanks to elaborate reef installations - making it ideal for interior designers prototyping aquarium features, aquascaping enthusiasts visualizing builds, pet retailers creating marketing content, or artists exploring underwater compositions. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image models that respect style tokens and lighting parameters. Reach for this prompt when you need production-quality aquarium renders without a camera, a physical tank, or stock-photo licensing fees. ● Specify the fish subject - betta, goldfish, koi, tropical schools, or abstract aquatic creatures - and see it rendered inside your chosen tank. ● Define the aquarium setting, from desktop nano cubes and wall-mounted displays to floor-to-ceiling reef systems or fantasy underwater worlds. ● Apply any visual style, including photorealistic, watercolor, low-poly 3D, architectural rendering, or editorial photography. ● Cinematic lighting and 16:9 framing ensure every output is presentation-ready for portfolios, pitch decks, social media, or client previews. ## Prompt

```
{{style}} of {{fish-subject}} inside {{aquarium-setting}}, cinematic lighting --ar 16:9
```
