# Podcast Content Strategy and Episode Idea Generator

## 簡介

The Podcast Content Strategy and Episode Idea Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive content roadmaps for podcast creators seeking audience-aligned episode concepts and formats. This podcast content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's demographics and interests, then produces organized topic categories with specific episode ideas, guest speaker recommendations with episode concepts, varied format suggestions, storytelling techniques to boost engagement, and attention-grabbing episode titles. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver a structured content blueprint spanning audience profiling, thematic organization, and actionable episode plans. Podcasters use it to move beyond simple topic lists into strategic content planning that considers format variety, guest alignment, and narrative techniques that keep listeners returning. Reach for this prompt when launching a new podcast series, refreshing an existing show's direction, or planning seasonal content arcs that resonate with specific listener demographics. ● Profiles your target audience by demographics, psychographics, and core interests to ground all recommendations ● Organizes episode ideas into 3-5 thematic categories with multiple specific topics per category ● Recommends credible guest speakers with relevant expertise and tailored episode concepts for each ● Provides storytelling techniques and episode title formulas that signal clear listener value ## Prompt
