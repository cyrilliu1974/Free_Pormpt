# Natural Daylight Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Natural Daylight Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic selfie-style photographs with natural window lighting for content creators, marketers, and personal brands. This selfie portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces eye-level or slightly elevated close-up images with soft daylight illumination streaming from nearby windows. It renders subjects with natural skin textures, visible hair details, fabric weaves, and metallic accessories against clean interior backgrounds with tonal separation. Use it to create relatable profile images, testimonial headshots, lifestyle blog visuals, or social media content that mimics real smartphone photography without studio equipment. Reach for this prompt when you need approachable, human-centered imagery that feels genuine rather than staged - perfect for small businesses, influencers, and digital marketers building authentic brand connections. ● Customize subject appearance, clothing colors, and interior settings while maintaining consistent natural daylight aesthetics and authentic photographic details. ● Produces sharp facial focus with moderate depth of field, capturing skin texture, individual hair strands, fabric stitching, and jewelry finishes in high resolution. ● Generates 80% subject framing with neutral-to-cool window light creating soft, even illumination and high contrast between vivid clothing and background tones. ● Delivers cheerful, bright atmosphere with structural background elements that provide depth while keeping attention on smiling expressions and eye contact. ## Prompt

```
A close-up selfie portrait of {{subject-description}} smiling brightly, seated inside {{setting}}. Eye-level or slightly high selfie angle, natural daytime lighting from nearby windows creating soft, even illumination with neutral to cool color temperature. Head and torso framing, subject occupies 80% of the frame with moderate depth of field—subject sharp, background slightly softened. Focal point on the smile and eyes.

Subject details: Natural skin tones with warm undertones, smooth skin texture, well-defined eyelashes and groomed eyebrows, white straight teeth, natural personal features visible. Head tilted slightly to the right, facing camera. Hair styled and well-maintained with natural parting, lighter highlights where light catches, individual flyaway hairs visible at crown, bulk draped naturally over one shoulder.

Wearing a vivid red cotton t-shirt with visible neckline construction and sleeve hem stitching, slight fabric folds at shoulder. Small metallic accessory (delicate chain necklace or bracelet) with fine details resting naturally against skin.

Background shows clean interior surfaces with mixed materials—textural variation and structural lines providing depth, tonal separation creating natural subject isolation. High contrast between red attire and neutral background tones (deep blacks, warm beiges, off-whites). Bright, clear, cheerful atmosphere.

High resolution photo, sharp focus on subject, natural features and micro-details visible: skin texture across face, fabric weave of shirt, individual hair strands, metallic finish of accessory.
```
