# Performance Metrics Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Performance Metrics Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds custom KPI frameworks and measurement plans for businesses that need to track progress toward strategic objectives. This performance metrics prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and goals as input, then produces a comprehensive strategy document covering business context analysis, tailored KPI definitions (with rationale, tracking methods, and reporting frequency), a data collection plan specifying sources and tools, dashboard visualization recommendations, and insights-to-action mappings that connect findings to specific business decisions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible for teams using any major text model. Real-world use cases include launching new product lines, optimizing operational efficiency, aligning sales and marketing teams around shared metrics, and replacing generic vanity metrics with indicators that actually drive decision-making. This prompt is for business leaders, data analysts, product managers, and consultants who need to define what success looks like in measurable terms and create reporting systems that stakeholders will actually use. ● Delivers 3 fully defined KPIs with tracking methods, reporting cadence, and clear rationale tied to stated business objectives ● Includes a data collection plan that names sources, tools, and responsibilities so teams know exactly how to gather the right inputs ● Provides dashboard design guidance covering visualizations, update frequency, and distribution strategy ● Maps insights to recommended actions, bridging the gap between measurement and business decisions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert data analyst and business strategist specializing in KPI definition, measurement frameworks, and performance tracking that drives business outcomes.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive KPI framework that aligns metrics with business goals and provides an actionable measurement and reporting plan.

## Context
Analyze the provided business context and goals to identify the most relevant performance indicators. Focus on metrics that directly measure progress toward stated objectives and can drive decision-making.

**Business details:**
{{business-context-and-goals}}

## Output
Structure your response with these sections:

**Business Context**
Summarize the relevant business background and strategic position.

**Business Goals**
List the key goals to be measured.

**Key Performance Indicators**
Define 3 KPIs, each including:
- Name
- Definition
- Rationale for selection
- Method for tracking
- Reporting frequency

**Data Collection Plan**
Outline how data will be gathered, including sources, tools, and responsibilities.

**Reporting Dashboard**
Describe recommended visualizations, update frequency, and distribution approach.

**Insights to Action**
Provide 3 key insights, each with:
- Finding
- Recommendation for leveraging the insight

Ensure all KPIs directly tie to the stated business goals. Avoid generic metrics; prioritize those that enable specific, measurable actions.
```
