# Supplier Payment Terms Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Supplier Payment Terms Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a strategic 90-day plan to extend payment terms, improve liquidity, and preserve critical supplier relationships for finance teams and business operators. This supplier payment optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current payment structures, models three negotiation scenarios per supplier (aggressive, balanced, conservative), and quantifies the working capital impact of tactics like dynamic discounting, supply chain financing, consignment arrangements, and milestone-based payments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a supplier prioritization matrix, negotiation scripts, and a detailed implementation roadmap with contingency plans for pushback. Real-world applications include cash-strapped businesses extending DPO without disrupting supply chains, CFOs modeling liquidity improvements before renegotiations, and procurement teams framing payment changes as partnership enhancements. This prompt is for finance leaders, treasury managers, and business owners who need to improve working capital but cannot afford to alienate key suppliers. ● Ranks suppliers by negotiation potential, payment volume, and relationship strength to focus efforts where impact is highest. ● Produces win-win negotiation scripts and value propositions that frame term extensions as mutual benefits, not cost-shifting. ● Calculates 30-, 60-, and 90-day liquidity projections and cumulative working capital freed under each scenario. ● Includes legal compliance checks, industry payment norm benchmarks, and alternative financing options like supply chain finance platforms. ## Prompt

```
## Role

Cash flow optimization specialist analyzing supplier payment terms as strategic levers for working capital improvement while preserving critical business relationships.

## Task

Develop a 90-day supplier payment term optimization strategy that improves Days Payable Outstanding (DPO) and frees working capital without damaging supplier relationships.

For each priority supplier, create three negotiation scenarios (aggressive, balanced, conservative) with specific tactics: dynamic discounting, supply chain financing, consignment arrangements, milestone-based payments, or extended term trade-offs. Quantify the liquidity impact of each approach. Provide negotiation scripts that frame changes as partnership enhancements. Build an implementation roadmap prioritizing suppliers by impact and relationship strength, with contingency plans for resistance.

## Context

{{supplier-and-cash-context}}

**Include:** Current payment terms and due dates, early payment discount structures, monthly cash outflow to suppliers, key supplier dependencies and strategic importance, liquidity challenges and timeline constraints, volume commitments or penalty clauses.

## Constraints

- Never suggest payment delays that could disrupt supply chains or damage relationships with sole-source or critical component suppliers
- Calculate the true cost of extended terms versus early payment discounts and opportunity costs
- Account for industry-specific payment norms and supplier cash flow needs
- Include legal and compliance considerations for proposed changes
- Prioritize mutual value creation over cost-shifting

## Output

**Current State Analysis**
- Payment waterfall showing daily, weekly, monthly outflows
- Cash flow impact assessment
- Missed discount and opportunity cost calculations

**Supplier Prioritization Matrix**
Table ranking suppliers by negotiation potential, payment volume, and cash flow impact

**Negotiation Strategies**
For each priority supplier:
- Current terms vs. three proposed scenarios
- Win-win value propositions
- Specific negotiation tactics and scripts
- Implementation timeline

**Liquidity Improvement Forecast**
- 30-day, 60-day, 90-day impact projections
- Cumulative working capital freed

**Risk Mitigation Plan**
- Supplier pushback scenarios and responses
- Alternative financing options (supply chain finance platforms, dynamic discounting)
- Relationship preservation tactics
```
