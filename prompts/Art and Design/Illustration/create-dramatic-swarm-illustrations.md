# Dramatic Swarm Illustration Prompt

## 簡介

The Dramatic Swarm Illustration Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates striking, high-contrast character portraits surrounded by dynamic swarms and intense atmospheric lighting for digital artists and content creators. This swarm illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces cinematic low-angle compositions where customizable subjects in hooded attire are engulfed by chaotic swirls of your chosen swarm element - birds, leaves, embers, insects, or abstract particles - all merging into vibrant red and orange glows against deep shadows. The prompt templates a dramatic backlighting setup with smoky foreground depth, creating book covers, game character art, album artwork, and social media visuals that immediately capture attention. Reach for this prompt when you need eye-catching character-focused illustrations with a dark, atmospheric, and intense aesthetic for fantasy projects, thriller branding, or editorial content. ● Low-angle shot composition with powerful red backlighting that creates depth and cinematic drama. ● Two customizable variables: subject-and-attire and swarm-element, allowing precise creative control. ● Defined color palette of intense red, vibrant orange, black, and smoky dark grey for consistent brand-ready output. ● Produces professional-grade illustrations suitable for book covers, game assets, poster design, and marketing campaigns. ## Prompt

```
Digital illustration in a striking, high-contrast style. {{subject-and-attire}} wearing a red hooded jacket, head tilted upwards, shot from a low angle. A chaotic swirl of {{swarm-element}} takes flight around the figure, merging into a vibrant, intense red and orange glow. The lower foreground is shrouded in dark, smoky shadows. Dramatic lighting from a powerful red backlight illuminates the swarm and surrounding atmosphere. Color palette: intense red, vibrant orange, black, and smoky dark grey.
```
