# Income Statement Generator for Small Business

## 簡介

The Income Statement Generator for Small Business is a free AI prompt that transforms raw revenue, cost, and expense data into a professionally structured income statement with strategic insights for entrepreneurs and business owners. This income statement prompt for ChatGPT takes your business type, reporting period, total revenue, cost of goods sold, and operating expenses, then organizes them into standard accounting categories following Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). It calculates gross profit margin, operating profit margin, and net profit margin, then analyzes each line item to surface trends, red flags, and profit opportunities. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering three outputs: a formatted income statement table, a financial ratio analysis with interpretation, and prioritized profitability improvement recommendations. Use it when you need accurate financial reporting for investor meetings, loan applications, or quarterly reviews but lack formal accounting staff. ● Structures revenue, COGS, operating expenses, and net income into a clear table layout following standard accounting categories. ● Calculates and interprets gross profit margin, operating profit margin, and net profit margin against industry benchmarks. ● Identifies cost reduction opportunities, pricing inefficiencies, and expense outliers with priority-ranked recommendations. ● Provides three distinct sections: the income statement itself, ratio analysis with context, and specific actions to improve profitability. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial analyst and small business accounting specialist. Your objective is to create a professionally structured income statement that reveals financial health, calculates key metrics, and provides strategic recommendations for profitability improvement.

## Context
The user needs accurate financial reporting following Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) to make informed business decisions, secure funding, or identify profit opportunities.

## Task
Using the provided financial data:

1. Organize data into standard accounting categories
2. Structure the income statement with clear line items:
   - Revenue
   - Cost of goods sold
   - Gross profit
   - Operating expenses (broken down by category)
   - Operating income
   - Other income/expenses
   - Net income
3. Calculate key financial metrics:
   - Gross profit margin
   - Operating profit margin
   - Net profit margin
4. Analyze each section to identify trends, red flags, and opportunities
5. Provide specific, actionable recommendations for increasing profitability, reducing costs, and optimizing financial performance based on calculated ratios and industry benchmarks

**Financial Data:**
- Business type and industry: {{business-and-industry}}
- Reporting period: {{reporting-period}}
- Total revenue: {{total-revenue}}
- Cost of goods sold: {{cogs}}
- Operating expenses breakdown: {{operating-expenses}}

## Output
Present your response in three sections:

1. **Income Statement** – clear table layout following GAAP structure
2. **Financial Ratio Analysis** – bullet points with calculated metrics and interpretation
3. **Profitability Improvement Recommendations** – specific actions organized by priority level (high/medium/low)
```
