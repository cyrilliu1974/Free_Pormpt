The Luxury Serum Bottle Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates high-end cosmetic product images with floating bottles, glass spheres, and professional studio lighting on soft pastel backgrounds. This serum bottle photography prompt for Midjourney produces minimalist, elegant product shots featuring your custom bottle and label text suspended above gradient backdrops, surrounded by clear glass spheres filled with shimmering liquid and micro-bubbles. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generators to deliver studio-quality cosmetic photography with soft diffused key lighting and subtle rim highlights in a 1:1 aspect ratio.
