# Blog Post Writer Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Blog Post Writer Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude is a free AI prompt that creates publish-ready, SEO-optimized blog articles for content marketers, strategists, and brand publishers. This blog post prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a complete content creation process: audience analysis, logical structure building, attention-grabbing headline creation, introduction hooks, informative body content with lists and bullet points, strong conclusions with calls-to-action, and natural keyword integration. You provide the topic, target audience, brand context, desired word count, and key message, and the prompt produces a structured article with H2/H3 headings, scannable formatting, and SEO-friendly copy. Content teams use it to maintain consistent publishing schedules, marketers rely on it for thought leadership pieces, and agencies deploy it to scale content production for multiple clients across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete blog article that balances reader engagement with search engine visibility, or when you want to maintain brand voice consistency across a content calendar. ● Performs audience analysis to align content with reader interests, pain points, and preferences before writing ● Structures articles with logical dependencies between sections, clear headings, and scannable formatting using bullets and numbered lists ● Integrates keywords naturally throughout headlines, subheadings, and body copy without forced repetition or stuffing ● Includes customizable variables for topic, target audience, brand context, word count, key message, and call-to-action to fit any content brief ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist writing SEO-optimized blog posts that drive traffic and build brand authority.

## Task
Create a complete, publish-ready blog post following this process:

1. **Audience analysis** – Identify the reader's interests, pain points, and preferences based on the target demographic.
2. **Structure** – Build a logical outline with clear dependencies between sections.
3. **Headlines & subheadings** – Write attention-grabbing titles that incorporate relevant keywords naturally.
4. **Introduction** – Hook the reader immediately and state the post's purpose.
5. **Body** – Deliver informative, valuable content using bullet points or numbered lists where appropriate.
6. **Conclusion** – End with a strong {{cta}} or thought-provoking statement.
7. **Polish** – Edit for clarity, conciseness, and grammar.
8. **SEO optimization** – Weave keywords naturally throughout without keyword stuffing.

## Context
- **Topic**: {{topic}}
- **Target audience**: {{target-audience}}
- **Brand voice & positioning**: {{brand-context}}
- **Desired length**: {{word-count}} words
- **Key message**: {{key-message}}

## Output
Deliver the blog post with:
- Clear H2/H3 headings and subheadings
- Bullet points or numbered lists for scannability
- Natural keyword integration
- A compelling introduction and strong conclusion with {{cta}}
```
