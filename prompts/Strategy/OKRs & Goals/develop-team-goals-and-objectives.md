# Team Goals and Objectives Builder

## 簡介

The Team Goals and Objectives Builder is a free AI prompt that translates high-level company strategy into actionable, measurable team-level goals for strategic planners and department leaders. This team goals prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your team context, strategic priorities, and current challenges to produce 3-5 SMART objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound), each paired with 2-3 quantifiable key results and concrete initiatives. The output is delivered as a structured markdown table with three columns - Objective, Key Results, and Initiatives - making it immediately usable for planning sessions, stakeholder presentations, and quarterly reviews. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only brief descriptions of your team, company strategic goals, and current challenges. Reach for this prompt when you need to bridge the gap between corporate strategy and day-to-day team execution, or when setting quarterly OKRs and performance targets. ● Produces 3-5 SMART-compliant objectives that directly connect team work to company strategic goals ● Defines 2-3 measurable key results per objective, complete with metrics and targets ● Outlines concrete initiatives and actions the team can execute immediately ● Delivers a clean markdown table format ready for documentation, presentations, and tracking tools ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert strategic planner specializing in translating company strategy into actionable team-level goals.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive set of SMART objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) for the team, along with key results and initiatives that directly support company strategy.

## Context
{{team-and-company}}

{{strategic-goals}}

{{current-challenges}}

## Process
1. Analyze how the team's work connects to company strategic goals
2. Formulate 3-5 objectives that address team challenges while advancing company priorities
3. For each objective, define 2-3 quantifiable key results that indicate success
4. Outline specific initiatives or actions required to achieve each key result

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns:

| Objective | Key Results | Initiatives |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|

Provide at least three complete rows. Each objective should be SMART-compliant, key results should include metrics and targets, and initiatives should be concrete actions the team can execute.
```
