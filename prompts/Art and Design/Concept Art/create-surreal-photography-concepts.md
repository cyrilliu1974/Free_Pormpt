# Surreal Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Surreal Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates ethereal, hyper-realistic close-up images of delicate objects in dreamlike floating compositions. This surreal photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 8K-resolution macro images with soft top-down lighting, pastel accent colors, and cream backgrounds. You define three variables - the primary object (like a flower, shell, or glass orb), intricate micro-details contained within it (gems, particles, liquid), and the surrounding environment (flora, mist, or abstract elements) - and the prompt renders tack-sharp, professionally color-graded scenes with an ethereal mood.
