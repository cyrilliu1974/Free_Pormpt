# Pomodoro Technique Learning Planner for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Pomodoro Technique Learning Planner is a free AI prompt that creates customized time management schedules for learners and students who want to improve focus and retention using Francesco Cirillo's proven interval-based method. This Pomodoro technique prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific learning goal and produces a four-part implementation guide: an overview of the technique, a benefits analysis tailored to your subject matter, recommended work-break ratios adjusted for task complexity, and a realistic daily schedule template formatted as a markdown table. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured plans that balance focused study sessions with strategic breaks. Students use it to design exam-prep schedules; professionals apply it to skill-building roadmaps; language learners rely on it to structure practice sessions without burnout. Reach for this prompt when you need more than generic Pomodoro advice and want a schedule that reflects the cognitive demands of your specific learning objective. ● Explains the Pomodoro Technique's origins and standard 25-5 interval pattern with longer breaks every four cycles. ● Analyzes 4-5 concrete benefits of applying the method to your specific learning goal, addressing common challenges in that domain. ● Recommends optimal session lengths and break durations calibrated to the intensity and complexity of your subject matter. ● Outputs a markdown-table daily schedule with 6-8 Pomodoro slots, meal times, planning blocks, and a realistic target number of daily sessions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity coach specializing in focused learning strategies.

## Task
Explain the Pomodoro Technique and create a practical implementation plan tailored to the user's learning goal.

## Context
The user wants to improve their productivity and achieve {{learning-goal}} using structured time management. Provide actionable guidance they can start using immediately.

## Output
Structure your response with these sections:

**The Pomodoro Technique: Overview**
- Developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s
- Time management method using a timer to break work into focused intervals ("Pomodoros")
- Standard pattern: 25 minutes focused work + 5 minute break
- After 4 Pomodoros, take a longer break (15-30 minutes)

**Key Benefits for {{learning-goal}}**
List 4-5 specific benefits of applying this technique to their learning goal, explaining how the structured intervals address common challenges in their domain.

**Recommended Work-Break Ratio**
Provide the optimal Pomodoro duration, short break length, number of sessions before a long break, and long break duration. Adjust recommendations based on the complexity and intensity of {{learning-goal}}.

**Daily Schedule Template**
Create a table-formatted schedule showing:
- Morning planning block (20-30 minutes)
- 6-8 Pomodoro sessions with short breaks
- Long breaks after every 4 Pomodoros
- Slots for meals and other responsibilities
- Recommended total number of Pomodoros to dedicate to {{learning-goal}} daily

Format the schedule as a markdown table with Time and Task columns. Make it realistic and adaptable.
```
