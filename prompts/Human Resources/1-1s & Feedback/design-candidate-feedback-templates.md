# Candidate Rejection Feedback Template Generator

## 簡介

The Candidate Rejection Feedback Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, legally defensible rejection communications for hiring teams across all interview stages. This candidate feedback prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces three stage-specific templates - phone screen, interview round, and final stage rejections - each built on the SBI Model framework. The prompt outputs situation-specific context examples, observable behavior descriptions (never subjective judgments), and clear impact statements tied to role requirements, along with delivery timing guidelines, communication channel recommendations, and a legal safeguards checklist. Recruiting coordinators use it to replace generic "not a good fit" language with concrete, actionable feedback; talent acquisition leaders deploy it to standardize rejection communications that protect employer brand while reducing discrimination liability; HR compliance teams adopt it to ensure every candidate touchpoint references only job-relevant competencies and observable behaviors. ● Stage-specific templates for phone screens, interview rounds, and final rejections with distinct sensitivity levels and detail depth ● SBI Model structure requiring situation context, observable behavior only, and impact tied to role requirements ● Legal compliance checklist covering discrimination risk mitigation, prohibited language patterns, and review checkpoints ● Delivery guidelines specifying optimal timing, appropriate channels, and tone patterns that balance honesty with encouragement ● Data collection framework for tracking rejection reason categories and candidate survey responses to improve templates over time ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a candidate experience architect with employment law expertise. You design rejection feedback that reduces legal risk while building employer brand, treating every communication as an opportunity to create advocates.

## Task

Create comprehensive candidate feedback templates using the SBI Model (Situation, Behavior, Impact) that balance legal compliance with actionable value.

Before drafting, analyze step-by-step: What specific situation occurred? What observable behaviors were demonstrated? What was the actual impact on the hiring decision?

## Context

{{company-context}}

Current challenges: Generic rejection templates provide no candidate value while still exposing discrimination risks. Candidates demand specific feedback; legal teams require protective language. Templates must work across three hiring stages—phone screens, interviews, final rounds—each with distinct sensitivities.

## Output

**1. SBI Model Overview**

Explain the framework structure and how it satisfies both legal requirements and candidate expectations.

**2. Stage-Specific Templates**

Create distinct templates for:
- Phone screen rejections
- Interview round rejections
- Final stage rejections

For each template, provide:
- **Situation**: Specific context examples (not generic statements)
- **Behavior**: Observable actions only (no subjective judgments)
- **Impact**: Clear connection to role requirements

**3. Delivery Guidelines**

Cover:
- Optimal timing for each stage
- Appropriate communication channels
- Tone and language patterns that balance honesty with encouragement

**4. Legal Safeguards**

Present as checklist:
- Discrimination risk mitigation strategies
- Legal review checkpoints
- Prohibited language patterns

**5. Data Collection Framework**

Guidance on systematically tracking:
- Rejection reason categories
- Candidate feedback survey responses

**Compliance Criteria**

- Avoid "not a good fit" or personality assessments
- Never reference protected classes
- Include positive observations alongside development areas
- Focus exclusively on job-relevant competencies and observable behaviors
- Maintain consistent employer brand voice
- Ensure feedback is specific enough to be actionable, general enough to avoid legal exposure

**Format**: Use section headers for each hiring stage, bullet points for components, tables for timing/channel guides, numbered steps for delivery protocols, italics for example phrases, **bold for critical compliance notes**.
```
