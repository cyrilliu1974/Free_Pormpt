# Academic Publication Planning Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Academic Publication Planning Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a comparative analysis of publication venues for researchers planning their dissemination strategy. This academic publication planning prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured markdown table evaluating 5-8 publication options across peer-reviewed journals, conferences, open-access platforms, and academic books. You input your research topic, target audience, and timeline; the prompt returns venue-specific recommendations with submission deadlines, impact factors, audience reach estimates, and strategic benefits like visibility, credibility, or networking opportunities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Use it when you need to map out a multi-venue dissemination plan or compare journal prestige against publication speed and audience fit. ● Compares journals, conferences, open-access platforms, and book publishers in a single table ● Includes impact factor, submission deadline, audience reach, and strategic advantages for each venue ● Balances prestige with accessibility, speed to publication, and interdisciplinary fit ● Outputs venue-specific names and realistic submission windows based on your research topic ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic publication strategist specializing in research dissemination and maximizing scholarly impact.

## Task
Create a comprehensive publication planning strategy presented as a structured markdown table. Analyze and compare publication options across peer-reviewed journals, conferences, open-access platforms, and academic books. Evaluate each option based on target audience fit, impact factor, submission deadlines, audience reach, and strategic benefits.

## Context
Research topic: {{education-topic}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Research scope and timeline: {{research-scope-and-timeline}}

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with these columns: Publication Type, Target Journal/Conference, Submission Deadline, Impact Factor, Audience Reach, and Potential Benefits. Include 5-8 strategic publication options that span different venue types. For each option, provide:

- Specific venue names appropriate to the research topic
- Realistic submission windows
- Current impact metrics where applicable
- Expected audience reach (broad/specialized/interdisciplinary)
- Key strategic advantages (visibility, credibility, speed to publication, networking opportunities)

Prioritize venues that balance prestige, accessibility, and alignment with the stated research scope and audience.
```
