# Privacy Policy Generator for Legal Compliance

## 簡介

The Privacy Policy Generator for Legal Compliance is a free AI prompt that drafts comprehensive, regulation-compliant privacy policies in clear, accessible language for websites and digital services. This privacy policy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through creating an eight-section document that covers data collection methods, usage practices, storage and security measures, third-party sharing, user rights, update procedures, and contact information. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, translating complex legal requirements into plain language while ensuring transparency about all data practices. Use it when launching a new service, updating existing policies to meet GDPR, CCPA, or other regulations, or whenever you need a baseline privacy document that explains data handling practices without legal jargon. ● Covers eight core sections including data collection, usage, storage, sharing, user rights, and policy updates ● Tailored to specific regulations (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, or custom frameworks) and service names ● Produces plain-language policies that communicate data practices transparently without complex legal terminology ● Ensures only realistic commitments are included, making policies both compliant and practically enforceable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a privacy policy expert who drafts comprehensive, legally compliant privacy policies in clear, accessible language.

## Task
Create a complete privacy policy that covers all data practices and complies with relevant regulations. Structure the policy with these sections:

**1. Introduction**
- Introduce the website/service and its commitment to user privacy
- State that use constitutes consent to these practices

**2. Data Collection**
- Types of personal information collected
- Collection methods

**3. Data Usage**
- How collected information is used

**4. Data Storage**
- Storage location and methods
- Security measures protecting user data

**5. Data Sharing**
- Third parties who may receive data
- Circumstances triggering data sharing

**6. User Rights**
- Rights regarding personal information
- Instructions to access, modify, or delete data

**7. Policy Updates**
- Update frequency and process
- User notification methods

**8. Contact Information**
- Contact details for privacy questions

## Context
- Website/service: {{service-name}}
- Applicable regulations: {{privacy-regulations}}

## Output
Write the privacy policy using clear headings and paragraphs. Use plain language—no legal jargon or complex terminology. Be transparent about all data practices. Only include commitments that can be upheld. Make user rights and procedures explicit and actionable.
```
