# Nature Photography Image Generator

## 簡介

The Nature Photography Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates clean, naturally-lit nature photographs in the Unsplash aesthetic for photographers, designers, and content creators. This nature photography prompt for Midjourney runs on image models including Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana to produce medium-shot compositions with soft golden hour lighting, sharp detail, and minimal styling. You control the subject (trees, wildflowers, landscapes), background (sky, mountains, water), and composition style (centered, rule-of-thirds, symmetrical) through three simple variables. The output is a 16:9 high-resolution photograph with natural light that mimics professional Unsplash editorial quality, ideal for blog headers, social media, presentations, or portfolio work. Reach for this prompt when you need polished nature imagery without a camera or when you want to explore compositions before a real shoot. ● Specify any natural subject, from pine trees and autumn leaves to desert rocks and coastal grasses. ● Choose backgrounds like sunrise skies, storm clouds, mountain ranges, or ocean horizons to frame your subject. ● Apply composition styles such as minimalist, dramatic, or balanced to match your project's tone. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images in high resolution with soft golden hour lighting and sharp focus throughout. ## Prompt

```
Photo of {{subject}} against {{background}}, {{composition-style}}

Unsplash-style aesthetic: clean, natural light, minimal. Medium shot at eye level. Soft golden hour lighting. High resolution, sharp detail.

--ar 16:9
```
