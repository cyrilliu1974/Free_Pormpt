# FAQ Page Generator for Customer Support

## 簡介

The FAQ Page Generator for Customer Support is a free AI prompt that creates organized, category-based frequently asked questions pages for businesses of any type. This FAQ page prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and produces a complete FAQ structure with thematic categories, prioritized questions, and clear answers written in plain language. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering formatted output with multiple categories (minimum three) and multiple Q&A pairs per category, ordered to surface the most critical customer information first. Businesses use it to build help center content, reduce support ticket volume, and provide self-service resources that customers can search before contacting support teams. Reach for this prompt when you need to create or refresh FAQ content for a website, product launch, or customer portal without hiring a copywriter. ● Organizes questions into logical thematic categories for easy navigation ● Prioritizes the most important customer inquiries at the top of each section ● Formats output with clear Q: and A: labels and consistent line breaks ● Adapts tone and detail level to match your business type and audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional website copywriter specializing in FAQ pages that address customer inquiries clearly and comprehensively.

## Task
Generate a well-structured FAQ page for the business described below. Organize questions into thematic categories, prioritize the most important information, and ensure answers are accurate and easy to understand.

## Context
Business and audience information:
{{business-context}}

## Output
Structure your response as:

**Frequently Asked Questions - [Business Name]**

**[Category 1 Name]**
Q: [Question 1]
A: [Answer 1]

Q: [Question 2]
A: [Answer 2]

**[Category 2 Name]**
Q: [Question 1]
A: [Answer 1]

Q: [Question 2]
A: [Answer 2]

**[Category 3 Name]**
Q: [Question 1]
A: [Answer 1]

Q: [Question 2]
A: [Answer 2]

Include at least 3 categories with a minimum of 2 questions each. Use clear formatting with line breaks between Q&A pairs. Order categories and questions logically, placing the most critical information first.
```
