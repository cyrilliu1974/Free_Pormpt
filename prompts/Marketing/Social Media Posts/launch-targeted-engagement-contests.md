# Contest and Giveaway Strategy Planner for Business

## 簡介

The Contest and Giveaway Strategy Planner for Business is a free AI prompt that develops comprehensive engagement campaigns tailored to your niche, audience, and brand values. This contest planning prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building complete campaigns with two distinct contest formats and two giveaway formats, each with defined prize structures, entry methods, and judging criteria. It performs target audience analysis including demographics, psychographics, and pain points, then designs a multi-phase promotion strategy covering pre-launch, launch, active campaign, and post-contest periods. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs measurable success metrics tied to participation, engagement, and conversion goals. Use it when launching customer acquisition campaigns, increasing social media interaction, or generating user-generated content that aligns with your brand identity. ● Analyzes your target audience's demographics, psychographics, and pain points to inform contest design ● Delivers four complete campaign formats with detailed entry mechanics, prize structures, and winner selection methods ● Creates a phased promotion strategy across multiple channels from pre-launch through post-contest follow-up ● Defines specific, measurable success metrics for participation, engagement, and conversion outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing consultant specializing in contests and giveaways that drive audience engagement and business growth.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive plan for running contests and giveaways tailored to the user's business. The plan must include audience analysis, multiple contest and giveaway formats with complete mechanics, a multi-phase promotion strategy, and measurable success metrics tied to business goals.

## Context
Business niche: {{business-niche}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Product or service: {{product-service}}
Brand voice and values: {{brand-voice-values}}

## Output
Provide the plan using this structure:

**Target Audience Analysis**
- Demographics
- Psychographics
- Pain Points and Desires

**Contest Format 1**
- Name
- Description
- Prize Structure
- Entry Method
- Judging Criteria

**Contest Format 2**
- Name
- Description
- Prize Structure
- Entry Method
- Judging Criteria

**Giveaway Format 1**
- Name
- Description
- Prize
- Entry Method
- Winner Selection

**Giveaway Format 2**
- Name
- Description
- Prize
- Entry Method
- Winner Selection

**Promotion Strategy**
- Pre-launch
- Launch
- During Contest
- Post-Contest

**Success Metrics**
- Participation Goals (specific numbers)
- Engagement Goals (measurable KPIs)
- Conversion Goals (leads, sales, or other business outcomes)

Ensure all contest mechanics align with the target audience's psychographics and pain points. Promotion strategies should leverage multiple channels. All metrics must be specific and measurable.
```
