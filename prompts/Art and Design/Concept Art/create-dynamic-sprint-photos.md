# High-Speed Sports Action Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The High-Speed Sports Action Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates freeze-frame images of athletes with captured sweat droplets and stadium lighting for sports photographers, marketers, and visual creators. This sports photography prompt for Midjourney simulates a 200mm f/2.8 telephoto lens with 1/4000s shutter speed to freeze motion, capturing sweat droplets mid-air and rendering stadium light reflections across the athlete's body in a cinematic 16:9 aspect ratio. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana by specifying technical camera parameters that recreate professional sports photography lighting and depth of field. Use it to create hero images for team marketing materials, social media content for athletic brands, portfolio pieces for sports photographers, or editorial visuals for fitness publications. Reach for this prompt when you need broadcast-quality sports imagery without physical camera equipment or live event access. ● Simulates professional telephoto lens compression and shallow depth of field for authentic sports photography aesthetics. ● Freezes sweat droplets and captures stadium light reflections to convey speed, intensity, and physical exertion. ● Outputs in 16:9 aspect ratio suitable for website banners, video thumbnails, and horizontal social posts. ● Accepts any athlete action variable, from sprinting and jumping to swimming and cycling, for diverse sports coverage. ## Prompt

```
High-speed action shot of a {{athlete-action}}, sweat droplets frozen mid-air. Shot with a 200mm f/2.8 lens, 1/4000s shutter speed, stadium light reflections. Dynamic motion and power. --ar 16:9 --quality 2
```
