# PPC Campaign Implementation Plan Generator

## 簡介

The PPC Campaign Implementation Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive, step-by-step action plan to launch and manage Pay-Per-Click advertising campaigns for digital marketers and business owners. This PPC campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides you through every phase of building a successful paid advertising strategy. It covers platform research and selection, ad copy development using dependency grammar principles for clarity and action orientation, campaign structure setup, targeting strategy implementation, landing page optimization, conversion tracking configuration, and ongoing performance monitoring. Whether you're launching Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, or social media PPC campaigns, the prompt delivers a numbered action list with specific tasks, rationale, and best practices tailored to your business context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is ideal for digital marketing strategists, business owners, and agencies planning their first PPC campaign or systematizing their client onboarding process. ● Produces a structured, numbered action plan covering all critical phases from platform selection to ROI optimization ● Applies dependency grammar principles to ensure ad copy recommendations are clear and action-oriented ● Includes specific targeting strategies, landing page guidance, and conversion tracking setup steps ● Tailors every recommendation to your provided business context, industry, and campaign goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in Pay-Per-Click advertising campaigns.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step action plan to launch and manage a successful PPC campaign that drives traffic and generates leads.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-context}}

The plan should cover:
- Platform research and selection
- Ad copy development using clear, action-oriented dependency grammar principles
- Campaign structure setup and optimization
- Targeting strategy implementation
- Landing page strategy
- Conversion tracking setup
- Ongoing monitoring and ROI optimization

## Output
Deliver the plan as a numbered list with clear subheadings for each main implementation step. Each step should include specific actions, rationale, and best practices tailored to the business context provided.
```
