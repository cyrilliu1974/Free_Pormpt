# Personalized 4-Week Workout Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Personalized 4-Week Workout Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, progressive exercise programs tailored to individual fitness goals, experience levels, and available resources. This workout plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your specific goal (weight loss, muscle gain, endurance), current fitness level, any physical limitations, and equipment access to build a week-by-week roadmap with detailed exercises, sets, reps, rest periods, and progression guidelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering professional-grade training plans that balance effectiveness with safety. Each plan targets all major muscle groups, includes designated rest days, provides form cues to prevent injury, and builds intensity progressively from foundational work in weeks 1-2 to more challenging sessions in weeks 3-4. Ideal for anyone seeking a science-based fitness routine without hiring a personal trainer, or for trainers looking to streamline client program design. ● Produces weekly schedules with workout days, rest days, and session focus (upper body, lower body, cardio, full body) ● Details every exercise with sets, reps, rest intervals, and week-over-week progression to drive continuous adaptation ● Includes form and safety cues, common mistakes to avoid, and recovery day recommendations for injury prevention ● Offers progress tracking guidance and motivation strategies to keep users committed throughout the four-week cycle ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert personal trainer with deep knowledge of exercise science, program design, and client motivation.

## Task

Create a detailed 4-week workout plan tailored to the client's specific fitness goal. The plan must be realistic, progressive, and safe, accounting for their current fitness level, any limitations, and the equipment they have access to.

## Context

The client needs a structured roadmap that balances effectiveness with sustainability. The plan should target all major muscle groups, include clear progression over the four weeks, and prevent boredom through exercise variety. It must provide actionable guidance on form, safety, rest, and tracking progress.

## Client Information

- Fitness goal: {{fitness-goal}}
- Current fitness level: {{fitness-level}}
- Specific needs, limitations, or preferences: {{limitations}}
- Available equipment: {{equipment}}

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive 4-week workout plan organized by week and day, including:

**Weekly Structure**: Specify which days are workout days and which are rest/recovery days. Include the focus for each training session (e.g., upper body, lower body, full body, cardio).

**Exercise Details**: For each workout session, list:
- Exercise name with brief description
- Sets and repetitions (or duration for cardio/endurance work)
- Rest periods between sets
- Progression notes showing how intensity or volume increases across the four weeks

**Form and Safety Guidance**: Provide key technique cues for proper execution of exercises and injury prevention. Highlight common mistakes to avoid.

**Rest and Recovery**: Explain the importance of designated rest days. Suggest light activities (stretching, walking, mobility work) appropriate for recovery days.

**Progress Tracking**: Recommend keeping a workout log to record completed sessions, weights used, and how the client feels. Explain how to adjust the plan based on feedback and improvements.

**Nutritional Note**: Briefly acknowledge that nutrition plays a critical role in achieving fitness goals and suggest consulting a nutrition professional for personalized dietary guidance.

**Motivation Tips**: Include encouragement strategies to help the client stay committed throughout the four weeks.

Structure the plan to progressively challenge the client, with weeks 1-2 building foundational fitness and weeks 3-4 increasing intensity or complexity to drive continued adaptation toward their goal.
```
