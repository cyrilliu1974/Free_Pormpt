# Pitch Deck Builder Prompt for Investor Presentations

## 簡介

The Pitch Deck Builder Prompt for Investor Presentations is a free AI prompt that creates clear, investor-ready pitch decks for entrepreneurs seeking funding. This pitch deck prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok structures a 10-slide presentation following the proven narrative arc: problem, solution, market, traction, team, and ask. Each slide passes the 'glance test' - core message understood in three seconds - and adheres to the 20-minute rule of two minutes per slide maximum. The prompt enforces visual clarity over text density, delivering slide-by-slide breakdowns that include a six-word title, one-sentence key message, three content bullets, visual suggestions (diagram, chart, or image), and 2-3 delivery talking points. Ideal for founders preparing for seed rounds, Series A, or any competitive funding environment where clarity and emotional resonance determine whether investors engage or move on. ● Structures 10 slides with emotional pain points, solution contrast, bottom-up market sizing, real traction metrics, relevant team expertise, and a specific funding ask with milestone allocation. ● Enforces the glance test with maximum six words per line and six lines per slide, replacing jargon and feature lists with verifiable proof points. ● Provides visual recommendations and delivery notes for each slide to help founders communicate clearly under pressure. ● Tailored for funding rounds where investors see hundreds of decks and decide within seconds whether to engage further. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a pitch deck strategist who has reviewed thousands of funding presentations. You understand that investors decide within seconds whether to engage, and that clarity beats complexity every time.

## Task
Create a 10-slide pitch deck structure optimized for investor attention spans. Each slide must pass the "glance test"—core message understood in 3 seconds. Follow a narrative arc: problem → solution → market → traction → team → ask. Apply the 20-minute rule (2 minutes per slide maximum) and enforce visual clarity over text density.

## Context
The user is preparing for a funding round where investors see hundreds of pitches. Most presentations fail by burying the lead in jargon, projections, and information overload. This deck must cut through noise with emotional resonance, concrete proof points, and a crystal-clear ask.

## Input
{{business-essentials}}
Provide: product/service description, target market definition, competitive advantage, business model (how you make money), current traction (metrics, customers, revenue), team credentials (relevant expertise), funding ask (amount and specific use of funds).

## Output
Deliver a slide-by-slide breakdown:

**Slide [Number]: [Title]** (max 6 words)  
- **Key Message:** [One sentence]  
- **Content:**
  • [Bullet 1]
  • [Bullet 2]
  • [Bullet 3]
- **Visual:** [Diagram/chart/image suggestion]
- **Notes:** [2-3 talking points for delivery]

## Criteria
- Lead with emotional and financial pain points investors can feel personally
- Demonstrate solution uniqueness through contrast, not feature lists
- Market sizing: bottom-up analysis, not top-down TAM claims
- Traction: real customer proof and specific metrics, not vanity numbers
- Team: relevant expertise for this problem, not just impressive logos
- Ask: exact amount, use allocation, and milestones it unlocks
- Visuals over text: maximum 6 words per line, 6 lines per slide
- No jargon or unexplained acronyms
- All numbers must be verifiable, not projections
```
