# Mixed-Use Architecture Visualization Prompt

## 簡介

The Mixed-Use Architecture Visualization Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates photorealistic renders of vertically integrated buildings combining shopping malls, office towers, and residential spaces for architects, developers, and urban planners. This mixed-use architecture prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces ultra-realistic architectural imagery with distinct three-part vertical composition: a transparent 3–4 level retail podium with double-height spaces and public terraces, a rational 10–15 level office tower with sky lobbies and green buffer zones, and a refined 15–20 level residential tower with private balconies and sky gardens. Each programmatic layer reads as architecturally distinct while maintaining visual cohesion through shared proportions, integrated landscape terraces, and unified urban presence. The prompt captures early evening blue-hour lighting that differentiates each function - bright inviting mall interiors, controlled professional office glow, and warm residential window light - shot from a street-level three-quarter view that emphasizes depth, human scale, and urban context. Reach for this prompt when presenting complex mixed-use developments, pitching urban infill projects, or communicating vertical program stacking to clients and stakeholders. ● Renders three distinct architectural zones with customizable facade materials and treatments that reflect shopping, office, and residential functions. ● Captures blue-hour architectural photography with realistic crowd placement, interior lighting gradients, and physically based material rendering. ● Includes urban plaza design with water features, landscape planters, and separate entrance hierarchies for each building program. ● Outputs 4K resolution images in 3:4 aspect ratio suitable for architectural presentations, competition boards, and development proposals. ## Prompt

```
Ultra photorealistic architectural visualization of a vertically layered mixed-use building combining shopping mall, office tower, and residential tower. Stepped vertical composition with horizontal setbacks and landscaped terraces separating each function. Strong base with lighter vertical towers rising above.

**Podium (3–4 levels):** Shopping mall with open, transparent, highly active character. Double-height retail spaces, interior atrium with skylight, public terraces, direct street access from multiple sides. {{podium-materials-and-facade}}

**Middle Tower (10–15 levels):** Office spaces with rational, efficient, professional character. Flexible floor plates, sky lobby, shared meeting terraces, green buffer floors. {{office-materials-and-facade}}

**Upper Tower (15–20 levels):** Residential with quiet, private, refined character. Deep balconies, corner living rooms, private sky gardens, separate residential lobby. {{residential-materials-and-facade}}

Each function reads differently while sharing proportions and alignment. Shading devices, natural ventilation zones, terrace gardens, green roofs, and sky gardens integrated throughout.

**Urban Context:** {{urban-setting}} Acts as a city landmark and active urban node. Ground-level urban public plaza with integrated seating, water feature, trees in architectural planters, outdoor cafes. Clear separation of mall, office, and residential entrances.

**Scene:** Early evening blue hour. Mall has bright inviting interior glow, office has controlled professional lighting, residential has warm calm window lights. Subtle facade accents emphasizing vertical hierarchy. Crowds shopping at mall level, professionals visible behind glass in offices, few silhouettes on residential balconies. Realistic human proportions for scale.

**Camera:** Street-level three-quarter view, 28–35mm architectural photography lens. Foreground plaza, mid-ground podium, vertical towers above. Strong depth and scale perception. Physically based rendering with high-resolution materials showing realistic wear. Minimal post-processing, cinematic realism.

Avoid: sci-fi architecture, floating structures, fantasy towers, cartoon style, low poly, overly glossy materials, random greenery, unrealistic proportions, copies of existing landmarks.

Aspect ratio 3:4, 4K resolution.
```
