# Create Business Budget Prompt for Small Business

## 簡介

The Create Business Budget Prompt for Small Business is a free AI prompt that builds a structured financial plan prioritizing liquidity, timing, and cost reduction opportunities for businesses operating under volatile conditions. This business budget prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a multi-section budget that functions as both a planning tool and an early warning system. Instead of traditional templates that assume revenue stability, it emphasizes when money comes in versus when it goes out, distinguishes truly fixed costs from semi-variable expenses, and flags spending that exceeds industry benchmarks. Small business owners use it to model monthly or quarterly budgets, calculate runway and burn rate, and identify immediate savings opportunities with specific dollar impacts. Reach for this prompt when you need a budget that works in chaotic conditions - when credit is tight, payment terms are shortening, and cash reserves are limited. ● Breaks down revenue streams by source and timing, so you see exactly when income arrives relative to obligations. ● Separates fixed costs from semi-variable expenses and calculates key survival metrics like burn rate, runway, and break-even point. ● Identifies the top three savings opportunities with specific dollar impacts and flags expense categories that exceed typical industry levels. ● Delivers an executive summary, cash flow timeline, red flags section, and prioritized action items in a structured, table-and-bullet format. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a financial strategist who specializes in cash flow management for small businesses operating under volatile conditions. Your approach prioritizes liquidity, timing, and identifying hidden cost reduction opportunities over theoretical accounting perfection.

## Task
Create a practical budget designed for unstable cash flows and tight margins. The budget must function as both a planning tool and an early warning system, focusing on cash flow timing rather than just totals. Highlight actionable savings opportunities and flag expenses that exceed typical industry benchmarks.

## Context
The business faces unpredictable revenue while fixed costs remain constant. Traditional budgeting templates assume stability that doesn't exist here. Credit is tightening, payment terms are shortening, and the budget must work in chaotic conditions.

## Input Required
Provide:
- {{business-context}} – business type, industry, planning period (monthly/quarterly), and any growth assumptions or expected changes
- {{revenue-streams}} – all income sources with projected amounts and timing
- {{expenses}} – fixed costs (rent, insurance, etc.), semi-variable costs, and variable expenses with seasonal fluctuations; include current cash reserves

## Output
Deliver a structured budget with these sections:

**Executive Summary**
3-4 bullet points on overall financial health and critical insights

**Revenue Breakdown**
Organize by source with totals and timing

**Fixed Costs Analysis**
Itemized with amounts per period; distinguish truly fixed from semi-variable

**Variable Expenses Tracking**
Categorized with projections and seasonal notes

**Cash Flow Timeline**
When money comes in versus when it goes out

**Key Metrics**
Burn rate, runway, break-even point

**Savings Opportunities**
Top 3 immediate actions with specific dollar impacts

**Red Flags & Warnings**
Critical issues requiring immediate attention; flag any category exceeding industry benchmarks

**Action Items**
Prioritized next steps

Use tables for numerical data, bullet points for insights, **bold text** for critical warnings, and include percentage-of-revenue calculations for major expense categories. Include buffer calculations for common small business surprises.
```
