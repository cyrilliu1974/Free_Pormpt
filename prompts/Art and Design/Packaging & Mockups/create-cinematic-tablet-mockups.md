# Cinematic Tablet Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Tablet Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic floating tablet renders with integrated screen content for designers, marketers, and product teams. This tablet mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces advertising-quality visuals featuring landscape-oriented tablets with customizable holders, dramatic cinematic lighting, and perspective-warped screen integration that respects bezel boundaries and simulates real glass depth. The prompt applies cool blue directional lighting, metallic rim highlights, and a high-tech color grade while seamlessly embedding your uploaded screen content with proper depth effects and soft glow. Real-world use cases include app showcases, website portfolio pieces, digital product launches, and social media marketing assets that require professional-grade tablet visuals without physical photography.
