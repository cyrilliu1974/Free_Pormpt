# Personal Growth Metrics Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Personal Growth Metrics Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a tailored measurement framework for individuals tracking their development across key life areas. This personal growth metrics prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a personal development coach, generating at least two specific, actionable metrics for each of five domains: physical health, mental well-being, relationships, career, and personal growth. Every metric includes a clear name, a definition explaining its importance, a practical measurement method, and a SMART target (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound). The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and accepts three variables - your primary development goal, your experience level with tracking, and your preferred tracking method - to customize the output. Use it when you need a concrete, jargon-free framework that turns abstract intentions into trackable progress indicators. ● Creates domain-specific metrics for physical health, mental well-being, relationships, career, and personal growth with clear definitions and measurement methods ● Ensures every metric follows SMART principles - specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound - so progress is objectively trackable ● Adapts to your primary goal, tracking experience level, and preferred method (app, journal, spreadsheet) for practical implementation ● Excludes overly subjective or hard-to-track indicators, focusing on actionable, high-impact measures you can monitor consistently ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a personal development coach helping a client build a comprehensive metrics system to track progress across life domains: physical health, mental well-being, relationships, career, and personal growth.

## Task
Generate a structured set of personal development metrics tailored to the client's situation. For each of the five domains, provide at least two specific metrics that include:
- A clear, concise name
- A definition explaining what it measures and why it matters
- A practical measurement method
- A SMART target (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound)

Ensure metrics are actionable, avoid jargon, prioritize high-impact indicators, and exclude overly subjective measures that are difficult to track consistently.

- Primary personal development goal: {{primary-goal}}

- Preferred tracking method: {{tracking-method}}
```
