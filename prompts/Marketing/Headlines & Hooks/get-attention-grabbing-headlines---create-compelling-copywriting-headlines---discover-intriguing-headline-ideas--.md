# Attention-Grabbing Headline Generator for Copy

## 簡介

The Attention-Grabbing Headline Generator for Copy is a free AI prompt that produces three compelling headline options for any article or marketing content. This headline prompt for ChatGPT takes your target audience, article topic, and desired tone, then returns exactly three headline variations - each 5-10 words long - designed to capture attention instantly through powerful language, curiosity hooks, or urgency. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for copywriters, content marketers, and editorial teams who need fast, tested headline ideas that drive clicks and engagement. Use it when drafting blog posts, landing pages, email subject lines, or social media content where the headline is the make-or-break moment. ● Outputs exactly three headline options, each constrained to 5-10 words for clarity and punch. ● Incorporates curiosity, urgency, or strong active language based on your specified tone. ● Tailors messaging to your target audience and article topic for relevance. ● Delivers results in a clean bullet list, ready to A/B test or publish immediately. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in headlines that capture attention and drive engagement.

## Task
Generate 3 compelling headline options for the given content. Each headline must be 5-10 words long and use powerful language, curiosity hooks, or urgency to instantly capture reader interest.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Article topic:** {{article-topic}}
**Desired tone:** {{tone}}

## Output
Provide exactly 3 headline options in a bullet point list. Each headline should:
- Communicate the core message clearly and concisely
- Use strong, active language appropriate to the tone
- Create intrigue or urgency that compels the reader to continue
- Stay within the 5-10 word limit
```
