# Customer Segmentation Strategy for CRM Outreach

## 簡介

The Customer Segmentation Strategy for CRM Outreach is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive segmentation plan with distinct customer groups and tailored outreach strategies for CRM teams. This customer segmentation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your customer attributes, business context, and CRM platform capabilities to create 4-6 mutually exclusive segments, each with key characteristics, outreach strategies, and measurable expected outcomes. It works with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to transform raw customer data - demographics, purchase history, engagement metrics, firmographics - into actionable segments that align with your business goals. Marketing teams use it to design personalized campaigns, CRM managers rely on it to optimize engagement workflows, and sales teams apply it to prioritize high-value prospects. ● Creates 4-6 distinct, measurable customer segments tailored to your available data and CRM platform ● Maps specific outreach strategies to each segment that align with business goals and CRM features ● Defines expected outcomes with metrics so you can track engagement improvements and ROI ● Ensures segments are mutually exclusive and actionable within your CRM's technical constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert CRM strategist specializing in customer segmentation and engagement optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive customer segmentation plan that enables targeted outreach and improves customer engagement. Analyze the provided customer data, create distinct segments based on meaningful characteristics, and design tailored outreach strategies for each segment with projected outcomes.

## Context
**Business context:** {{business-context}}
(Include your business goals, target market, industry, and any relevant constraints or priorities)

**Customer attributes:** {{customer-attributes}}
(Include available data points: demographics, behavioral data, purchase history, engagement metrics, firmographics, etc.)

**CRM platform:** {{crm-platform}}
(Specify which platform and any relevant features, integrations, or limitations)

## Requirements
- Base all segments on the provided customer attributes and data patterns
- Ensure segments are mutually exclusive, measurable, and actionable within the specified CRM platform
- Align outreach strategies with stated business goals and CRM capabilities
- Make expected outcomes specific and measurable (include metrics where appropriate)
- Focus on segments that represent meaningful business opportunities

## Output
Present your segmentation plan as a markdown table with 4 columns:

| Customer Segment | Key Characteristics | Outreach Strategy | Expected Outcomes |
|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

Include 4-6 distinct segments that collectively cover your customer base.
```
