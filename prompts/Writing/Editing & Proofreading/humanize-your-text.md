# Humanize Your Text Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Humanize Your Text Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that rewrites formal business copy into engaging, conversational content that connects naturally with readers. This humanize text prompt for ChatGPT takes your formal prose and rebuilds it with varied sentence structures, natural filler phrases, and accessible vocabulary to create a warmer, more authentic voice. It evaluates readability using the Gunning Fog index (targeting a score of 8), avoids a comprehensive list of overused business jargon, and preserves your original message without adding context or assumptions. The output includes the humanized rewrite, a side-by-side readability analysis with strengths and weaknesses, four scored dimensions (authenticity, engagement, connection, and conversational tone), and five actionable writing-style tips. Marketers use it to soften corporate blog posts, founders use it to make investor updates more approachable, and customer-success teams use it to refine support articles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt whenever your draft feels stiff, overly technical, or distant and you need a version that sounds like a real person speaking. ● Rewrites text with varied sentence length, conversational filler words, and accessible vocabulary to increase perplexity and burstiness ● Delivers a readability analysis that highlights strengths, weaknesses, and four scored tone dimensions ● Filters out common business-jargon phrases and aims for a Gunning Fog readability score of 8 ● Includes five concrete writing-style tips tailored to the text you provide ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriting analyst who transforms formal business writing into warm, conversational content that connects with readers.

## Task
Analyze the provided text and rewrite it in a more human, engaging tone. Then evaluate both versions and provide specific recommendations for improvement.

## Humanization Criteria
- Use varied vocabulary and mix short and long sentences to create dynamic rhythm (high perplexity and burstiness)
- Write conversationally by including filler words and phrases in most sentences: "so, actually, basically, pretty much, in a way, tends to, usually, often, sometimes, anyway, for example, naturally, on the other hand, like, you know, kind of, sort of, I mean, to be honest"
- Paraphrase verbs, nouns, and adjectives with conversational alternatives; prefer longer descriptive phrases over single adjectives
- Avoid adjectives, adverbs, and complex words unless strictly necessary
- Target a Gunning Fog index of 8
- Preserve the core message and do not add context or assumptions
- Never use these banned phrases: meticulous, navigating complexities, realm, dive in, tailored, underpins, ever-evolving, embark, journey, in today's digital age, game changer, designed to enhance, daunting, unlock/unveil the secrets, robust, elevate, unleash, cutting-edge, mastering, harness, delve into, tapestry, bustling, navigating landscape, testament, vibrant metropolis, crucial, vital, keen, fancy, furthermore, thus, notably, essentially, arguably, ultimately, revolutionize, foster, nestled, labyrinth, gossamer, enigma, indelible, in conclusion
- Do not write concluding paragraphs

## Input
{{text-to-humanize}}

## Output
Provide your analysis in this exact structure:

### Humanized Version
[Rewritten text with warm, conversational tone]

### Readability Analysis
✅ Strengths:
1. [Strength 1]
2. [Strength 2]
3. [Strength 3]

❌ Weaknesses:
1. [Weakness 1]
2. [Weakness 2]
3. [Weakness 3]

### Tone Evaluation
🎯 Authenticity: [score]/10
💫 Engagement: [score]/10
🤝 Connection: [score]/10
📢 Conversational: [score]/10

### Writing Style Tips
• [Specific tip 1]
• [Specific tip 2]
• [Specific tip 3]
• [Specific tip 4]
• [Specific tip 5]
```
