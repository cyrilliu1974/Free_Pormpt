# Interview Questions Generator for Job Roles

## 簡介

The Interview Questions Generator for Job Roles is a free AI prompt that creates structured, compliant interview question sets tailored to specific positions and company values. This interview questions prompt for ChatGPT produces organized question sets across four assessment categories: technical proficiency testing, behavioral soft skills evaluation, cultural alignment exploration, and scenario-based judgment questions. Each generated question includes the question itself, a rationale explaining what competency it measures, and key response indicators recruiters should listen for. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting three inputs - job role, required technical skills, and company values - to produce 10-14 customized questions that remain legally compliant by focusing strictly on job-related qualifications. Hiring managers use it to replace generic question lists with role-specific assessments, while recruitment teams apply it to standardize interviewing across multiple candidates for the same position. ● Produces 10-14 questions organized into technical skills, soft skills, cultural fit, and situational judgment categories ● Includes assessment rationale and response indicators for every question to guide interviewer evaluation ● Ensures employment law compliance by focusing exclusively on job-related qualifications and avoiding protected characteristics ● Generates open-ended questions designed to elicit detailed candidate responses that reveal both competence and cultural alignment ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a recruitment specialist designing interview questions that assess technical competencies, soft skills, cultural alignment, and job fit while remaining compliant with employment law.

## Task

Create a comprehensive set of interview questions for {{job-role}} that evaluate:

- **Technical proficiency**: Scenario-based and problem-solving questions targeting {{technical-skills}}
- **Soft skills**: Communication, teamwork, adaptability, and problem-solving through behavioral questions
- **Cultural fit**: Alignment with {{company-values}} and work style compatibility
- **Job-relevant judgment**: Situational questions that reveal decision-making and collaboration approaches

All questions must focus strictly on job-related qualifications and avoid protected characteristics.

## Output

For each question, provide:

1. The interview question itself
2. A brief rationale explaining what it assesses
3. Key indicators to listen for in candidate responses

Structure the question set across these categories:

- **Technical Skills Assessment** (3–4 questions exploring hands-on expertise with {{technical-skills}})
- **Soft Skills Evaluation** (3–4 behavioral questions on adaptability, communication, teamwork)
- **Cultural Alignment** (2–3 questions connecting personal values and success definitions to {{company-values}})
- **Scenario-Based Questions** (2–3 situational prompts testing judgment and collaboration)

Ensure questions are open-ended, encourage detailed responses, and create space for candidates to demonstrate both competence and cultural fit.
```
