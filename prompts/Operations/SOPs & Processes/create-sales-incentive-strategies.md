# Sales Incentive Strategy Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Incentive Strategy Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds evidence-based sales compensation frameworks for businesses refining their incentive programs. This sales incentive prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company context and produces a complete guide covering sales agent motivations, compensation mix recommendations (base salary, commission, accelerators), team versus individual reward structures, and company-specific factors like sales cycle length and product complexity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to show how individual incentive components hierarchically support broader business objectives. Use it when designing new sales compensation plans, auditing existing programs, or adapting incentive structures to changing market conditions. ● Produces a seven-section framework from motivation analysis through KPI tracking and continuous improvement processes. ● Addresses psychological drivers behind sales performance and matches them to specific incentive structures. ● Tailors recommendations to company-specific variables including sales cycle, product complexity, and market maturity. ● Includes dependency mapping that shows how incentive tiers build hierarchically and align with company goals. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in sales incentive design and compensation structures, capable of creating tailored, evidence-based incentive programs.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive guide on optimal sales incentives for the user's company. Use dependency grammar principles to create a logically structured framework that shows how each incentive component relates to and supports broader business goals.

## Context
{{company-context}}

## Output
Structure your guide using the following framework:

### 1. Introduction
- Brief overview of the company and its sales environment
- Why well-structured sales incentives matter for this context

### 2. Understanding Sales Agent Motivations
Identify three core motivations relevant to this company's sales team:
- For each motivation: explain the psychological drivers and what incentive structures best leverage them

### 3. Key Components of Effective Sales Incentive Structures
Detail three essential components (e.g., base salary vs. commission mix, accelerators, team vs. individual rewards):
- For each: describe the component and provide concrete implementation strategies

### 4. Tailoring Incentives to Company Context
Address three company-specific factors (e.g., sales cycle length, product complexity, market maturity) and how they should shape the incentive design

### 5. Dependency Grammar Principles Applied
- Show syntactic dependencies: how incentive tiers build upon each other hierarchically
- Show semantic dependencies: how individual incentives align with and reinforce company objectives
- Provide one example for each

### 6. Evaluating and Optimizing Program Effectiveness
- List three key performance indicators to track (e.g., quota attainment, revenue per rep, retention rate)
- Describe feedback mechanisms and continuous improvement processes

### 7. Conclusion
- Summarize key recommendations
- Emphasize the need for ongoing adaptation

Base all recommendations on sales compensation research and industry best practices. Be specific and actionable.
```
