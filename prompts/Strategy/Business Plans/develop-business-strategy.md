# Business Strategy Development Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Strategy Development Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates complete strategic plans for business leaders and strategists. This business strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your company's market position, competitive landscape, and industry trends to produce a structured strategic plan delivered as a markdown table. You provide your business name, mission statement, vision statement, long-term goals, and industry; the prompt returns 5-7 major strategic objectives, each paired with specific strategies and actionable items including resource allocation considerations, timelines, potential risks, and success metrics. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to ensure your strategic plan is both ambitious and grounded in reality. Business owners use it to align strategy with core values, consultants use it to structure client engagements, and executive teams use it to translate vision into executable roadmaps. ● Produces a three-column markdown table mapping Objectives to Strategies to Action Items for immediate implementation ● Incorporates market position analysis, competitive landscape assessment, and industry trend evaluation into each strategic direction ● Includes resource allocation guidance, realistic timelines, risk identification, and measurable success criteria for every objective ● Adapts to any industry by accepting custom mission statements, vision statements, and long-term business goals as input variables ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist developing a comprehensive strategic plan.

## Task
Create a structured strategic plan that aligns with the company's mission, vision, and long-term goals. Analyze the current market position, competitive landscape, and industry trends. Identify key strategic objectives, then develop specific strategies and actionable items for each. Consider resource allocation, timelines, potential risks, and success metrics. The plan should be ambitious yet realistic.

## Context
**Business name:** {{business-name}}

**Mission statement:** {{mission-statement}}

**Vision statement:** {{vision-statement}}

**Long-term goals:** {{long-term-goals}}

**Industry:** {{industry}}

## Output
Present your strategic plan as a markdown table with three columns: **Objectives**, **Strategies**, and **Action Items**. Each row should represent a coherent strategic direction for the business. Include 5-7 major objectives with corresponding strategies and actionable items. Ensure each objective has clear success metrics.
```
