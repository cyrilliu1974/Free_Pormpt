# Claim and Verify Local Business Listings Strategy

## 簡介

The Claim and Verify Local Business Listings Strategy is a free AI prompt that builds a complete roadmap for claiming and verifying business listings across local directories and platforms to strengthen local search visibility. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business profile to recommend 8-12 essential platforms - Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, and industry-specific directories - ranked by impact for your business type. For each platform, it delivers step-by-step claiming instructions, verification method options (postcard, phone, email, instant), estimated timelines, and common pitfalls to avoid. It also provides exact NAP (name, address, phone) consistency guidelines to ensure uniform formatting across every listing, plus a markdown tracking table to monitor your verification progress. Real use cases include local service businesses establishing their online presence, multi-location retailers standardizing their directory listings, and marketing agencies managing client local SEO campaigns. This prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to systematically claim unclaimed listings, fix verification issues, or audit your current local platform presence. ● Recommends and prioritizes 8-12 local platforms based on business category and location ● Provides platform-specific claiming and verification instructions with timelines and pitfall warnings ● Defines exact NAP formatting rules to maintain consistency across all directories ● Includes a pre-filled markdown tracking table with clear usage instructions and status labels ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO expert specializing in business listing management and verification across local directories and platforms.

## Task
Create a comprehensive strategy to claim and verify business listings that will strengthen local search visibility. Guide the business through platform identification, listing claim procedures, verification methods, and progress tracking.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-profile}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy in this structure:

1. **Platform Selection & Prioritization**
   - Analyze the business category and location to recommend 8-12 essential local platforms (Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, industry-specific directories)
   - Rank platforms by impact on local search visibility for this business type

2. **Claiming & Verification Roadmap**
   For each recommended platform, provide:
   - Step-by-step claiming instructions
   - Verification method options (postcard, phone, email, instant)
   - Estimated timeline for verification
   - Common pitfalls and how to avoid them

3. **NAP Consistency Guidelines**
   - Exact business name, address, and phone format to use across all platforms
   - How to handle suite numbers, service areas, or multiple locations

4. **Progress Tracking System**
   Present a markdown table with clear usage instructions:

   **How to use this tracking table:**
   - Update the Verification Status column as you progress through each platform
   - Paste the direct URL to your claimed listing once available
   - Use status labels: Not Started | Claimed - Awaiting Verification | Verified | Issues

   | Platform | Listing URL | Verification Status |
   |----------|-------------|---------------------|
   | [Platform rows pre-filled based on recommendations] | | |

5. **Next Steps After Verification**
   - Optimization checklist (photos, hours, categories, attributes)
   - Review acquisition strategy
   - Ongoing monitoring cadence
```
