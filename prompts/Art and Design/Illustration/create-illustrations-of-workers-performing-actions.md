# Worker Action Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Worker Action Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates custom illustrations of people in work scenarios with gradient color schemes and configurable artistic styles. This worker illustration prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces images featuring subjects performing actions against soft gradient backgrounds blending cyan and amber tones on a peach base. You define the illustration style (flat, line art, isometric, etc.) and the subject with their action (manager presenting data, engineer reviewing blueprints, designer sketching concepts), and the prompt outputs a cohesive 16:9 composition. Perfect for landing pages, presentation decks, training materials, and marketing campaigns that need visual storytelling without stock photography. Reach for this prompt when you need professional, stylized people illustrations that match a specific brand aesthetic or visual language. ● Accepts any illustration style parameter - line art, flat design, isometric, watercolor, or vector styles - for brand alignment. ● Produces 16:9 aspect ratio images ideal for web banners, slide decks, and horizontal marketing materials. ● Built-in gradient color scheme (cyan and amber on peach) ensures visual consistency across a series of illustrations. ● Subject and action variables allow precise control over pose, activity, and context without rewriting the entire prompt. ## Prompt

```
{{illustration-style}} illustration of a {{subject-and-action}}, gradient blending cyan and amber tones, soft peach background, --ar 16:9
```
