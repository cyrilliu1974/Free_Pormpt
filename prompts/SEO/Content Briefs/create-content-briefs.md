# Content Brief Generator for SEO and Strategy

## 簡介

The Content Brief Generator for SEO and Strategy is a free AI prompt that creates detailed content briefs to guide writers and content teams through the production process. This content brief prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured framework covering audience demographics and psychographics, primary and secondary SEO keywords with search intent classification, hierarchical content outlines with main sections and subheadings, tone and voice guidelines, credible sources and competitor references, and measurable success metrics. You provide the content type, target audience, primary goal, industry niche, and preferred tone, and the prompt produces a complete markdown brief that a writer unfamiliar with the project can immediately act on. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need to standardize content planning across teams, ensure every article or asset aligns with strategic goals, or onboard freelance writers quickly with clear, actionable direction. ● Analyzes target audience pain points, motivations, and psychographics to ensure relevance ● Identifies primary and secondary keywords with classified search intent for SEO alignment ● Builds hierarchical outlines specifying sections, subheadings, and key points to cover ● Defines tone, voice, credible sources, and success metrics so every stakeholder understands the goal ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist creating a comprehensive content brief to guide high-quality content production.

## Task

Develop a detailed content brief that includes:

- **Audience Analysis**: Demographics, psychographics, pain points, and motivations of the target audience
- **Target Keywords**: Primary and secondary SEO keywords with search intent classification
- **User Intent**: What the audience seeks to learn, do, or achieve
- **Content Outline**: Hierarchical structure with main sections, subheadings, and key points to cover
- **Tone & Voice Guidelines**: How the content should sound and feel
- **Relevant Sources**: Credible references, data sources, and competitor examples to inform the content
- **Success Metrics**: How to measure if the content achieves its goal

## Context

**Content Type**: {{content-type}}

**Target Audience**: {{target-audience}}

**Primary Goal**: {{primary-goal}}

**Industry/Niche**: {{industry-niche}}

**Preferred Tone**: {{tone}}

## Output

Provide the content brief in markdown format with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points. Ensure each section is actionable and specific enough to guide a writer unfamiliar with the project.
```
