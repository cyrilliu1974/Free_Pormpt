# Business Risk Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Business Risk Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic risk assessments across four critical business dimensions for managers, consultants, and analysts evaluating strategic decisions. This risk analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes any business scenario and produces a structured report identifying three key risks in each category: operational, financial, legal, and reputational. Each risk includes an actionable mitigation strategy and a credible data source citation. The output also delivers probability and impact assessments using industry benchmarks, key risk indicators to monitor, and business continuity plans. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, acting as an expert risk analyst to help decision-makers anticipate vulnerabilities before they materialize. Real-world use cases include evaluating new market entries, M&A due diligence, product launches, regulatory changes, and strategic pivots. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework to surface blind spots and document risk posture for stakeholders, boards, or compliance teams. ● Identifies three specific risks in each of four categories (operational, financial, legal, reputational) with paired mitigation strategies and credible sources ● Includes probability and impact assessments grounded in objective criteria and industry benchmarks ● Provides key risk indicators for ongoing monitoring and contingency plans for business continuity ● Delivers a professional, citation-backed report suitable for board presentations, compliance documentation, and strategic planning ## Prompt
