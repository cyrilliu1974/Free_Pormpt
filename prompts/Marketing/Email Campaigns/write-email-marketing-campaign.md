# Email Marketing Campaign Writer

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Campaign Writer is a free AI prompt that builds complete promotional emails designed to drive specific actions from your target audience. This email marketing campaign prompt for ChatGPT takes your product details, target audience profile, unique selling points, desired action, and brand voice, then generates a three-part email: an attention-grabbing subject line (6-10 words optimized for open rates), a persuasive body (150-250 words in mobile-friendly short paragraphs), and a conversion-focused call-to-action with supporting copy. The prompt structures each email to address audience pain points first, connect them to your solution, highlight unique value, and guide readers logically toward taking action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing teams, founders, and email specialists reach for this prompt when they need campaign-ready promotional emails that match brand voice while maximizing engagement and conversions. ● Produces subject lines engineered for high open rates alongside complete email body copy ● Applies dependency grammar principles to ensure logical flow from problem to solution to action ● Formats output in mobile-friendly structure with short, scannable paragraphs ● Generates specific, action-oriented CTAs with supporting transition sentences ● Adapts tone and messaging to match your specified brand voice and audience profile ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer creating a high-converting email campaign.

## Task
Craft a compelling promotional email that drives {{desired-action}}. The email must:

- Open with a subject line that maximizes open rates
- Address audience pain points and connect them to the solution
- Highlight the unique value proposition clearly
- Build logical flow from problem to solution to action
- Include a strong, specific call-to-action
- Use mobile-friendly formatting (short paragraphs, scannable structure)
- Match the {{brand-voice}} throughout

## Context
**Offer:** {{product-service}}

**Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Unique selling points:** {{usp}}

## Output
Deliver the email in three sections:

**Subject Line:**  
[One compelling subject line, 6-10 words]

**Email Body:**  
[Opening hook, value explanation with benefits, social proof or urgency if relevant, transition to CTA. 150-250 words, 3-5 short paragraphs]

**Call-to-Action:**  
[Specific CTA button/link text and supporting sentence]
```
