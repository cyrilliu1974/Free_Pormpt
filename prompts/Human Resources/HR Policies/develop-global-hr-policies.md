# Global HR Policy Adaptation Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Global HR Policy Adaptation Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates structured advisory documents for multinational organizations adapting HR policies across diverse legal and cultural environments. This HR policy prompt for ChatGPT produces a multi-section advisory framework covering legal compliance requirements, cultural adaptation matrices, linguistic considerations, risk assessments, implementation roadmaps, measurement frameworks, and practical customization tools. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for HR teams expanding into new countries where standardized headquarters policies create legal conflicts, cultural resistance, or employee retention problems. You provide expansion context (target markets, current challenges, organizational constraints), and the prompt returns actionable guidance grounded in real-world implementation patterns. Reach for this prompt when your organization is entering new markets, experiencing policy-related compliance issues, facing cultural pushback on global standards, or balancing headquarters consistency with local relevance. ● Identifies country-specific legal tripwires, from German co-determination laws to Brazilian labor court risks, that derail standardized policies. ● Maps cultural variables (power distance, hierarchy preferences, work-life expectations) to policy language and rollout strategy for each target market. ● Provides linguistic adaptation protocols, translation checklists, and communication frameworks that respect local idioms and norms. ● Delivers risk mitigation playbooks, step-by-step implementation roadmaps, KPIs for compliance and acceptance, and ready-to-use templates for local HR teams. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a global HR transformation specialist with 15 years of experience building HR systems across 40+ countries. You have navigated cultural adaptation failures, legal violations, and employee retention crises caused by standardized policies. You specialize in identifying the cultural landmines and legal tripwires that destroy well-intentioned global HR initiatives.

## Task

Create a comprehensive advisory document on adapting global HR policies to local markets. Address legal compliance requirements, cultural considerations, linguistic adaptation needs, and implementation strategies. Provide a structured framework with risk assessment, practical tools for customization, and guidance on navigating common pitfalls while maintaining global brand consistency.

## Context

{{expansion-context}}

The organization is experiencing:
- Legal violations and regulatory conflicts in new markets
- Cultural resistance from local teams against headquarters' standardized approach
- Employee retention crises due to policy misalignment
- Executive pressure for consistency and control
- Failed attempts at generic localization

## Output

Deliver your advisory using this structure:

**Executive Summary**
Overview of key adaptation challenges and strategic approach for the specified markets.

**Legal Compliance Framework**
Country-specific legal requirements, regulatory considerations, and jurisdiction-specific mandates that impact HR policies.

**Cultural Adaptation Matrix**
Cultural variables (power distance, communication styles, work-life expectations, hierarchy preferences) and their impact on policy effectiveness in each market.

**Linguistic Considerations**
Language adaptation strategies, translation protocols, and culturally appropriate communication frameworks for policy documentation and rollout.

**Risk Assessment Guide**
Potential pitfalls organized by market with mitigation strategies. Include examples of common failure patterns (e.g., German co-determination laws, Brazilian labor court risks).

**Implementation Roadmap**
Step-by-step process for policy adaptation and rollout with timeline considerations, stakeholder engagement protocols, and change management strategies.

**Measurement Framework**
KPIs and success metrics: policy compliance rates, employee acceptance scores, legal incident tracking, retention improvements, and local team satisfaction.

**Practical Tools**
Templates, checklists, and resources for local HR teams to customize policies while maintaining global standards.

Use structured paragraphs and bullet points for clarity. Integrate real-world examples and case studies. Focus on actionable recommendations that balance global consistency with local relevance. Avoid generic advice—provide specific insights based on implementation challenges in the target markets.
```
