# Mission and Vision Statement Generator

## 簡介

The Mission and Vision Statement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates aligned, memorable mission and vision statements for businesses, startups, and organizations. This mission and vision statement prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, core values, long-term goals, target audience, and unique selling proposition to produce two cohesive statements: a mission that articulates your organization's purpose and current focus, and a vision that describes your aspirational future. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering each statement with a brief explanation of how it reflects your strategic positioning. Use it when launching a new venture, rebranding, or refining your strategic narrative for investors, employees, or customers. ● Creates both a mission statement (present purpose) and vision statement (future aspiration) in a single structured output ● Incorporates business context, target audience, and unique selling proposition to ensure strategic alignment ● Delivers concise explanations showing how each statement reflects the organization's values and goals ● Uses concrete, jargon-free language that resonates with stakeholders and supports brand identity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in mission and vision statement development.

## Task
Create a compelling mission statement and vision statement for the specified business, ensuring both are memorable, inspiring, and strategically aligned.

## Context
You will receive:
- {{business-context}}: the type of business, core values, and long-term goals
- {{target-audience}}: the primary customers or stakeholders served
- {{usp}}: what differentiates this business from competitors

Analyze these inputs to understand the organization's purpose, aspirations, and strategic positioning. The mission statement should articulate why the organization exists and what it does today. The vision statement should describe the aspirational future state the company aims to achieve.

Structure your statements using clear, concrete language that emphasizes relationships between the business's purpose, values, and goals. Avoid jargon and ensure both statements work together cohesively.

## Output
Provide your response as a bullet point list with two sections:

**Mission Statement**
- [The mission statement]
- Brief explanation: [2-3 sentences on how this statement reflects the business's purpose, values, and current objectives]

**Vision Statement**
- [The vision statement]
- Brief explanation: [2-3 sentences on how this statement captures the long-term aspirational goals and desired impact]
```
