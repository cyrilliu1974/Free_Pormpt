The Isometric Residential Tower Diagram Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional axonometric architectural diagrams of sustainable high-density residential buildings for architects, developers, and urban designers. This residential tower diagram prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates stepped massing designs with carved voids, continuous balconies filled with vertical gardens, and overlay diagrams illustrating natural ventilation, solar orientation, thermal comfort, and biophilic integration. Each generated image features architecturally feasible volumes - podium bases rising into stacked and shifted residential floors - with shared amenities including sky gardens, co-working lounges, fitness areas, and roof landscapes. The prompt accepts variables for facade materials and site context, enabling customization for urban, suburban, or waterfront settings.
