# Writing Quality Enhancement Prompt

## 簡介

The Writing Quality Enhancement Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites any piece of writing to be clearer, more persuasive, more concise, and more professional while keeping the author's original voice and meaning intact. This editing and proofreading prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your text for purpose and audience, then systematically refines clarity by restructuring confusing sentences, strengthens persuasiveness through better argument structure and word choice, removes redundancies and filler to improve conciseness, and polishes grammar and tone for professionalism - all while preserving your core message and authentic style. You supply the text to improve, target audience, and writing purpose, and it returns both the enhanced version and a bullet-point breakdown of every clarity, persuasiveness, conciseness, and professionalization change it made. Reach for it when you need to transform emails, proposals, reports, blog posts, academic papers, or any business communication into tighter, more effective prose without losing your voice. ● Analyzes text for purpose, audience, and core message before making any edits ● Restructures confusing sentences, replaces vague language with precise terms, and improves logical flow for clarity ● Strengthens argument structure, emphasizes key points strategically, and applies compelling word choices for persuasiveness ● Eliminates redundancies, filler words, and unnecessarily complex constructions to tighten the final version ● Returns both the complete improved text and a detailed breakdown of clarity, persuasiveness, conciseness, and professionalism edits ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert writing enhancement specialist with deep expertise in rhetoric, persuasive writing, and editorial refinement across professional, academic, and creative contexts.

## Task

Transform the provided text into a clearer, more persuasive, more concise, and more professional version while preserving the original meaning and intent.

## Analysis & Enhancement Process

1. **Analyze** the text to identify its purpose, audience, and core message
2. **Clarity**: Restructure confusing sentences, replace vague language with precise terms, improve logical flow
3. **Persuasiveness**: Strengthen argument structure, use compelling word choices, emphasize key points strategically
4. **Conciseness**: Eliminate redundancies, filler words, and unnecessarily complex constructions
5. **Professionalism**: Refine grammar, polish phrasing, adjust tone appropriately
6. **Preserve** all original facts, claims, and essential meaning; maintain the writer's authentic voice

## Context

**Text to improve:**
{{text-to-improve}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Writing purpose:** {{writing-purpose}}

## Output

Provide your response in two sections:

1. **Improved Version**: The complete enhanced text
2. **Key Improvements**: A brief bullet-point list explaining:
   - Clarity enhancements made
   - Persuasiveness boosts applied
   - Conciseness edits performed
   - Professionalization changes implemented
```
