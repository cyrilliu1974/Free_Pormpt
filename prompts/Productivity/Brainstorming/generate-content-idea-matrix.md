# Newsletter Content Matrix Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Newsletter Content Matrix Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that produces a structured table of 32 newsletter ideas tailored to your target audience and editorial focus. This newsletter content matrix prompt for ChatGPT works by crossing eight proven content types - actionable guides, motivational stories, analytical breakdowns, contrarian takes, observations, comparisons, present-versus-future contrasts, and listicles - with four topics drawn from your newsletter focus. The result is a markdown table filled with concrete, ready-to-write ideas like "7 things you need to know to build your content strategy" or "How [Person] achieved [extraordinary outcome]." Solopreneurs use it to plan weeks or months of newsletters at once, ensuring variety in tone and format without repetitive brainstorming sessions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to fill an editorial calendar quickly or want to guarantee thematic and stylistic diversity across your issues. ● Produces a markdown table with 8 content-type columns and 4 topic rows, yielding 32 distinct newsletter concepts. ● Accepts two variables - target audience and newsletter focus - so every matrix is customized to your reader profile and editorial niche. ● Balances tactical how-to guides with inspirational stories, analytical explainers, and contrarian viewpoints for a well-rounded content calendar. ● Outputs concrete one-line ideas you can immediately slot into your production schedule or expand into full drafts. ## Prompt

```
## Role

Newsletter content strategist for solopreneurs.

## Task

Generate a Content Matrix table with 32 unique newsletter ideas (8 content types × 4 topics) tailored to the audience and focus below.

## Context

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}  
**Newsletter focus:** {{newsletter-focus}}

## Structure

**Columns (8 content types):**

- **Actionable** – Ultra-specific guide teaching readers HOW to do something
- **Motivational** – Inspirational story about someone who achieved something extraordinary
- **Analytical** – Informational breakdown explaining WHY something works the way it does
- **Contrarian** – Challenges widespread beliefs or common advice
- **Observation** – Surfaces a hidden, underreported, or emerging trend
- **X vs. Y** – Compares two frameworks, tools, companies, or concepts
- **Present vs. Future** – Contrasts the status quo with a prediction and explains why it matters
- **Listicle** – Useful list of resources, tips, mistakes, lessons, or steps

**Rows (4 topics):**  
Select four relevant topics within {{newsletter-focus}}. For each topic, generate one content idea per column that authentically combines the topic with that content type.

## Output

Deliver the completed matrix as a markdown table. Each cell contains one concrete, actionable content idea (e.g., "7 things you need to know to build your content strategy"). No XML tags.
```
