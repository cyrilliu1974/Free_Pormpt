# Landing Page Copywriting Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Landing Page Copywriting Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that generates conversion-focused landing page copy for marketers, founders, and copywriters looking to communicate product value and drive action. This landing page copy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a structured copywriting process: analyzing audience pain points, positioning your unique selling proposition, translating features into benefits, incorporating social proof, and crafting compelling calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing complete landing page sections including headline, opening paragraph, feature-benefit blocks, testimonial placements, and conversion-optimized CTAs. You provide your product or service description plus your target audience demographics and positioning, and the AI delivers scannable, persuasive copy structured for readability and conversion. This prompt is designed for anyone building product landing pages, launching services, or running conversion optimization campaigns who needs professional copy fast. ● Produces five core landing page sections: headline, opening paragraph, features-benefits, social proof integration, and call-to-action ● Anchors all copy to your unique selling proposition and audience pain points for relevance and resonance ● Formats output for scanability with bullet points, subheadings, and short paragraphs that maintain engagement ● Guides the AI to translate product features into concrete customer benefits rather than listing specifications ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert conversion copywriter specializing in landing pages that drive action.

## Task
Write persuasive landing page copy that communicates the value proposition clearly and compels the target audience to act.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}

Target Audience & USP: {{audience-and-positioning}}
(Include: audience demographics, psychographics, primary pain points, your unique selling proposition, and top 3 benefits)

## Process
1. Analyze the audience's core pain points and desires
2. Position the product/service as the ideal solution anchored to the USP
3. Structure copy for clarity and scanability
4. Integrate persuasive language and social proof naturally throughout
5. Drive toward a clear conversion goal

## Output
Deliver the landing page copy in these sections:

**Headline**
An attention-grabbing statement highlighting the primary benefit or USP

**Opening Paragraph**
Address the audience's pain points and introduce your solution

**Features & Benefits**
Present key features translated into concrete benefits, formatted for easy scanning

**Social Proof**
Incorporate testimonials, case studies, or credibility markers

**Call-to-Action**
A compelling CTA that creates urgency and encourages immediate action

Use short paragraphs, bullet points, and subheadings to maintain readability and engagement.
```
