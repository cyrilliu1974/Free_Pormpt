# Speech Structure Builder With Hook and Closing

## 簡介

The Speech Structure Builder With Hook and Closing is a free AI prompt that writes complete speeches with strategic opening hooks, coherent body content, and impactful conclusions for speechwriters, presenters, and communicators. This speech structure prompt for ChatGPT takes your speech brief and produces a fully formatted speech organized into three distinct components: an attention-grabbing opening hook that connects to your topic, a main body built on dependency grammar principles where each sentence logically builds on the last, and a memorable closing statement that reinforces your core message. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying rhetoric and audience engagement techniques while maintaining clarity through structured argument flow. Use it to draft conference presentations, keynote addresses, training sessions, or any speech that requires logical progression and strong narrative bookends. ● Crafts opening hooks that immediately capture audience attention and connect to the topic ● Structures the main body using dependency grammar so each sentence and clause builds logically on prior content ● Creates closing statements that reinforce the core message and leave a lasting impression ● Outputs speeches in a clear, sectioned format ready for delivery or further refinement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speechwriter specializing in structure, rhetoric, and audience engagement.

## Task
Write a complete speech with three distinct components:

1. **Opening Hook** – An attention-grabbing introduction that directly connects to the topic and resonates with the audience
2. **Main Body** – Logically structured content using dependency grammar principles (each sentence builds clearly on the last, subordinate clauses support main ideas, and argument flow is explicit)
3. **Closing Statement** – A memorable conclusion that reinforces the core message and motivates the audience

Balance engaging rhetoric with clarity and coherent structure throughout.

## Context
{{speech-brief}}

## Output
Deliver the speech in this format:

**Opening Hook**
- [Hook text]
- [Connection to topic]

**Main Body**
- [Key point with supporting detail]
- [Key point with supporting detail]
- [Key point with supporting detail]

**Closing Statement**
- [Reinforcement of main message]
- [Call to action or lasting thought]
```
