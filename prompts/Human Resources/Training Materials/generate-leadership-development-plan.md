# Leadership Development Plan Prompt for Business Owners

## 簡介

The Leadership Development Plan Prompt for Business Owners is a free AI prompt that creates tailored executive growth roadmaps for entrepreneurs and their management teams. This leadership development prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your organization profile and delivering an eight-part plan: personal leadership assessment, individual development priorities with concrete activities and metrics, management team evaluation, team capability-building initiatives, culture-reinforcing practices, ongoing support resources, a 12-month timeline with quarterly milestones, and success measurement frameworks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured plans that address real leadership challenges rather than generic advice. Business owners use it when scaling their companies, building out management layers, or preparing teams for increased autonomy and decision-making responsibility. ● Creates parallel development tracks for owner and management team, each with three priority skills and specific activities ● Delivers measurable success criteria including both leading indicators like engagement scores and lagging indicators like retention and business performance ● Recommends organizational practices and rituals that embed leadership behaviors into company culture ● Provides a 12-month implementation roadmap with quarterly checkpoints and milestone reviews ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced business and leadership coach specializing in executive development and building high-performing management teams.

## Task
Create a comprehensive leadership development plan with two tracks: one for the business owner's personal growth and one for their management team. The plan must be actionable, measurable, and tailored to their specific context.

## Context
{{organization-profile}}

Include: company name, industry, company size, management team size, current leadership challenges, and any relevant background about the business owner's experience level.

## Output
Deliver a structured development plan organized as follows:

### 1. Personal Leadership Assessment
Analyze the business owner's current leadership strengths and growth areas based on the context provided.

### 2. Personal Leadership Development Plan
Identify three priority leadership skills with:
- Specific development activities (workshops, coaching, practice scenarios, reading)
- Concrete metrics for success (behaviors to observe, feedback mechanisms, performance indicators)

### 3. Management Team Assessment
Evaluate the team's current leadership capacity, collaboration dynamics, and development readiness.

### 4. Management Team Leadership Development Plan
Identify three priority team leadership capabilities with:
- Group and individual development activities (training sessions, peer coaching, cross-functional projects)
- Team-level metrics for success (collaboration quality, decision-making speed, initiative ownership)

### 5. Leadership Culture-Building Initiatives
Recommend 3-5 organizational practices that reinforce leadership behaviors (rituals, communication norms, recognition systems, decision rights).

### 6. Ongoing Support and Resources
Suggest coaching cadence, recommended books/courses, peer networks, and feedback structures.

### 7. Timeline with Milestones
Provide a 12-month roadmap with quarterly milestones and checkpoints.

### 8. Measuring Overall Success
Define both leading indicators (engagement, feedback scores) and lagging indicators (retention, business performance, team autonomy) to track impact.

Make all recommendations specific and immediately actionable. Avoid generic leadership platitudes.
```
