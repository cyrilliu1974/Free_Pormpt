# Museum Exhibit Product Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Museum Exhibit Product Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates three-panel exhibition-style product photography with museum-quality presentation for brands, marketers, and product teams. This museum exhibit product grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 16:9 horizontal layout with three distinct panels: a studio hero shot on a museum pedestal with dimensional specs, a technical cutaway diagram with macro material close-ups and labeled component callouts, and a lifestyle portrait showing the product in realistic use. Each panel includes professional museum-style typography and informational labels that explain materials, construction, and features with gallery-level clarity. Use it when you need to transform a simple product description into polished visual marketing assets that educate viewers while maintaining premium brand credibility. This prompt is designed for product managers launching new items, marketing teams building presentation decks, e-commerce brands creating storytelling visuals, or designers who need exhibition-grade imagery without a photo studio. ● Produces a unified 3×1 grid showing the same product as a hero studio shot, an annotated technical cutaway with material close-ups, and a realistic lifestyle scene. ● Generates clean infographic callouts identifying core components, materials, dimensions, and specifications in a minimal modern sans-serif hierarchy. ● Maintains consistent product design and lighting across all three panels with photorealistic textures, accurate shadows, and premium reflections. ● Outputs ad-grade 16:9 imagery suitable for product launch decks, website hero sections, investor presentations, and social media campaigns. ## Prompt

```
## Structure
3-panel horizontal grid (3×1), 16:9 aspect ratio, museum exhibition layout with 2.5% panel spacing and 4% outer margin.

## Global Style
Premium museum photography aesthetic with clean infographic elements. Minimal gallery gray gradient background, subtle wall texture, soft vignette. Museum spotlight with soft studio fill lighting, realistic shadows. Neutral-warm color grade with accurate blacks and natural highlights. Photorealistic materials, believable reflections and shadows. No real brand names, logos, or trademarks—create generic premium product design.

## Typography
Clean modern sans-serif hierarchy: bold titles, medium labels, regular details. White or near-white text with subtle cool accent. Crisp and minimal, no distorted letters.

---

### Panel 1: EXHIBIT A — HERO PRODUCT
Studio hero shot, 3/4 view, 70mm equivalent, medium depth of field. {{product-description}} centered on minimal museum pedestal or matte plinth. Product shown in primary presentation state (open, unfolded, or resting), one component optionally placed in front for scale. Soft contact shadow, premium reflections, clean finish with realistic micro-scratches only where appropriate.

Museum plaque (brushed aluminum or matte acrylic) on pedestal front or floating label, displaying: product name and model designation, object classification, date, materials list, key dimension or weight, accession number.

---

### Panel 2: EXHIBIT B — MATERIALS & INTERIOR
Technical museum display, slight top-down angle, 85mm equivalent. Split layout with main image plus two small inset close-ups and compact spec block.

**Main image**: Clean cross-section cutaway of {{product-description}} revealing internal components realistically (not toy-like).

**Inset 1**: Macro close-up of primary outer surface texture and seam detail, shallow DOF.

**Inset 2**: Macro close-up of secondary material or interface detail, shallow DOF.

**Infographic callouts**: 4 thin clean lines with round endpoints pointing to: (1) core functional component, (2) power/energy component, (3) input/output component, (4) comfort/ergonomic feature. Each with component name and one-line descriptor.

**Spec block** (museum label card, lower right): 4 technical details covering surface finish, mechanical construction, fit/form factor, and charging/connectivity/interface.

---

### Panel 3: EXHIBIT C — IN USE
Lifestyle portrait, 3/4 face close-up, 85mm equivalent, shallow depth of field (product and face sharp). {{lifestyle-context}} in clean modern museum-like interior or minimal studio backdrop. {{product-description}} clearly visible in use. Realistic skin texture, natural hair detail, authentic lighting. No celebrity or recognizable person.

Caption label (small museum strip, bottom of panel): One-line museum-voice description of the product in everyday use.

---

## Quality Requirements
Same product design across all three panels. Avoid illegible or garbled text, warped product geometry, real brand logos or trademarked names, celebrity likeness, overcrowded labels. Output must be crisp, premium, ad-grade.
```
