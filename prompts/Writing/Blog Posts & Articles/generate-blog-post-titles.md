# Blog Post Title Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Blog Post Title Generator Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates click-worthy, engagement-focused blog titles for content marketers and digital strategists. This blog post title prompt for ChatGPT takes your target audience, brand voice, content goals, and competitor benchmark titles to produce 10 optimized headlines that balance search visibility with social shareability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and structures each title to incorporate power words, emotional triggers, and curiosity-inducing elements while maintaining grammatical clarity. Content teams use it to escape writer's block, A/B test headline variations, and align titles with specific business objectives like lead generation, thought leadership, or viral reach. This prompt is for digital marketers, blog editors, and content creators who need consistent, high-performing titles tailored to their audience's pain points and aspirations. ● Accepts three inputs: audience and topic details, brand voice and content goals, and competitor benchmark titles for positioning ● Produces 10 title variations with diverse emotional appeals and structural formats to maximize testing opportunities ● Optimizes each title for both search engine ranking and social media sharing, balancing discoverability with engagement ● Uses power words and curiosity triggers while maintaining grammatical soundness and brand alignment ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in content headlines that drive clicks and engagement.

## Task
Generate 10 blog post titles that are concise, search-optimized, and shareable. Each title should incorporate power words, emotional triggers, and curiosity-inducing elements while remaining grammatically sound and clear.

## Context
**Target audience & topic:**
{{audience-and-topic}}
(Describe who you're writing for—their interests, pain points, aspirations—and the digital marketing topic this content addresses.)

**Brand voice & content goals:**
{{brand-voice-and-goals}}
(Specify your brand's tone—professional, playful, authoritative, etc.—and what you want this content to achieve: thought leadership, lead generation, education, viral reach, etc.)

**Competitor benchmark titles:**
{{competitor-titles}}
(Paste 2–3 popular titles from competitors to inform style and positioning.)

## Output
Return a numbered list of 10 blog post titles, each optimized for both search engines and social media. Vary structure and emotional appeal across the set.
```
