# Story Arc Framework Generator for Creative Writing

## 簡介

The Story Arc Framework Generator for Creative Writing is a free AI prompt that builds a complete narrative structure for writers, screenwriters, and content creators working in any genre. This story arc prompt for ChatGPT takes your project brief, genre, and target audience and produces a five-part framework covering story foundation, character architecture, three-act plot structure with inciting incident through resolution, conflict layers (internal and external), and narrative coherence strategies including foreshadowing and thematic threads. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a numbered outline with labeled sections and subsections that map dependencies between scenes, character motivations, and plot beats. Writers use it to blueprint novels, short stories, screenplays, and serialized content before drafting, ensuring every element serves the overall narrative and maintains reader engagement. ● Analyzes premise, theme, tone, and pacing strategy tailored to your genre and audience ● Designs protagonist and antagonist arcs with motivations, flaws, and transformation paths ● Maps five-stage plot structure from inciting incident through climax and resolution ● Establishes external and internal conflict frameworks with clear stakes and consequences ● Identifies scene dependencies, causal chains, and foreshadowing opportunities for structural coherence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert storyteller and narrative architect.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive story arc framework for the given writing project. Analyze core narrative components, outline key plot points, develop multi-dimensional characters, establish conflicts and resolutions, and ensure structural coherence throughout.

## Context
Writing project: {{project-brief}}

Genre: {{genre}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output
Provide a detailed story arc structured as a numbered list with clearly labeled main sections and indented subsections:

1. **Story Foundation**
   - Central premise and theme
   - Tone and narrative voice
   - Story length and pacing strategy

2. **Character Architecture**
   - Protagonist(s): motivations, flaws, arcs
   - Antagonist(s) and opposing forces
   - Supporting cast and their narrative functions

3. **Plot Structure**
   - Inciting incident
   - Rising action and key complications
   - Midpoint reversal
   - Climax
   - Resolution

4. **Conflict Framework**
   - External conflicts
   - Internal conflicts
   - Stakes and consequences

5. **Narrative Coherence**
   - Scene dependencies and causal chains
   - Thematic threads connecting plot points
   - Foreshadowing and payoff moments

Ensure each element serves the overall narrative and maintains reader engagement from opening to conclusion.
```
