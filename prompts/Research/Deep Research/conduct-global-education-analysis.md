# Global Higher Education Program Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Global Higher Education Program Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic comparative reviews of university courses across leading institutions worldwide for academic researchers and education policy analysts. This higher education analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models - including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini - through a structured evaluation of 5-8 leading programs in any subject area, producing a detailed comparison table that examines curriculum highlights, teaching methods, assessment approaches, and innovative practices side-by-side. The prompt then ranks the top three programs with justified selections based on pedagogical effectiveness and innovation, and concludes with properly formatted academic citations. Real-world applications include curriculum development benchmarking, institutional accreditation research, faculty hiring committees evaluating competitive programs, and education consultants advising on program design. Reach for this prompt when you need a rigorous, evidence-based comparison of how different universities approach teaching the same discipline, grounded in current program documentation and published research rather than subjective opinion. ● Produces side-by-side comparison tables of 5-8 university programs with distinct evaluation criteria across six dimensions ● Ranks top three programs with evidence-based justifications tied to innovation and pedagogical effectiveness ● Generates properly formatted academic citations in APA, MLA, or Chicago style for research integrity ● Maintains focus on measurable innovation and current institutional data rather than reputation alone ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher specializing in comparative analysis of higher education programs across global institutions.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive review of {{subject-area}} courses offered by leading universities worldwide. Examine curriculum structures, teaching methods, assessment approaches, and innovative practices to identify top programs.

## Output
Provide your analysis in three sections:

**1. Comparative Table**

Create a table with these columns:
- University
- Program Name
- Curriculum Highlights
- Teaching Methods
- Assessment Approach
- Innovative Practices

Include 5-8 leading programs. Use bullet points within cells for readability.

**2. Top 3 Programs**

Rank and describe:
1. [Program Name], [University] – [Key Strengths]
2. [Program Name], [University] – [Key Strengths]
3. [Program Name], [University] – [Key Strengths]

Justify each selection based on innovation, pedagogical effectiveness, and curriculum design.

**3. References**

Provide full academic citations for all sources consulted, formatted in a standard citation style (APA, MLA, or Chicago).

## Criteria
- Base evaluations on current program documentation, published research, and institutional data
- Prioritize programs demonstrating measurable innovation in pedagogy or assessment
- Ensure the comparative table clearly distinguishes program characteristics
- Maintain academic rigor and source integrity throughout
```
