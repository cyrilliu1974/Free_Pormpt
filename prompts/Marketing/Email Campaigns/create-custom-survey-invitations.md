# Survey Invitation Generator

## 簡介

The Survey Invitation Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized survey invitation emails designed to maximize response rates through warm, conversational messaging. This survey invitation prompt for ChatGPT produces a ready-to-send email draft structured around seven core components: attention-grabbing subject line, personalized greeting, appreciation-focused intro, transparent survey details (length, time estimate), prominent incentive presentation, clear call-to-action with access instructions, and a closing that invites questions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and works by transforming your survey details - product or service being surveyed, target audience, sender name, incentive offered, and completion time - into an email that feels handcrafted rather than automated. Marketing teams use it to boost participation rates for customer feedback surveys, product teams deploy it for beta-testing feedback requests, and HR departments rely on it for employee engagement surveys. Reach for this prompt when you need to gather actionable feedback without sounding transactional or sending generic mass appeals. ● Produces every element of a survey email in one pass: subject line, body paragraphs, call-to-action, and sign-off ● Positions the incentive as a natural thank-you rather than a bribe, maintaining tone and trust ● Adapts voice and messaging to your specific product, audience, and company identity ● Includes completion-time transparency and question-contact information to reduce friction ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write persuasive survey invitations that feel personal, not mass-marketed. Your voice is warm, professional, and conversational, giving recipients a clear reason to participate.

## Task

Draft one complete survey invitation email. Make the recipient feel valued, clearly explain the survey's purpose, and motivate completion using the incentive as the hook.

## Context

{{survey-details}}

Include: product/service being surveyed, target audience, company/sender name, incentive offered, and estimated completion time.

## Output

Use this structure:

**Subject Line:** [Attention-grabbing subject line]

**Salutation:** [Personalized greeting],

**Intro Paragraph:**  
[Express appreciation]  
[State survey purpose]  
[Emphasize importance of feedback]

**Survey Details:**  
[Explain survey length and format]  
[Mention estimated completion time]  
[Detail incentive for participation]

**Call-to-Action Paragraph:**  
[Reiterate value of their input]  
[Provide clear next steps]  
[Include survey link or instructions]

**Closing:**  
[Thank them in advance]  
[Provide contact for questions]  
[Personalized sign-off]

[Company or sender name]

**Criteria:**

1. Tailor every line to the specific product/service—no generic filler
2. Keep the voice warm, professional, and conversational throughout
3. Make the incentive prominent and specific
4. Provide clear instructions for accessing and completing the survey
5. Express genuine gratitude for the recipient's time
6. Keep the invitation concise and engaging
```
