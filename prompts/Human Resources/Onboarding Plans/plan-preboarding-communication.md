# Preboarding Communication Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Preboarding Communication Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates sequenced communication strategies to engage new hires between offer acceptance and their first day. This preboarding communication plan prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete timeline of touchpoints, editable message templates, tone guidelines, and channel recommendations tailored to your company culture and role types. It addresses the three core engagement drivers identified in organizational psychology: clarity about expectations and logistics, purpose through connection to meaningful work, and belonging via early team introductions. HR teams use it to replace ad-hoc welcome emails with research-informed sequences that reduce pre-start anxiety and accelerate time-to-productivity. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize your preboarding process, scale communication across multiple new hires, or ensure consistent messaging that reflects your culture while addressing common first-day concerns. ● Outputs 4-6 progressive message templates with sender, channel, and personalization guidance for each touchpoint. ● Includes a communication timeline mapping optimal send dates from offer acceptance through day-before-start. ● Provides tone and style guidelines calibrated to your culture while addressing new-hire uncertainty. ● Delivers channel strategy and role-level adaptation so messages feel personal at scale. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR strategist specializing in preboarding design. You combine organizational psychology with engagement best practices to create communication plans that reduce new-hire anxiety and build belonging before day one.

## Task
Create a comprehensive preboarding communication plan covering the window between offer acceptance and first day. Deliver a progressive sequence of message templates, timing recommendations, and tone guidelines that build momentum and connection.

## Context
{{company-culture-context}}

{{preboarding-parameters}}

The plan should address three engagement drivers:
- **Clarity**: Set expectations about role, impact, and day-one logistics
- **Purpose**: Connect the hire to meaningful work and mission
- **Belonging**: Foster early relationships with team and culture

Include guidance for personalizing by role level, department, and communication channel. Progress messages from initial welcome through culture introduction, practical preparation, team introductions, and expectation setting.

## Output
Structure your plan with clear section headings:

**1. Communication Timeline**  
List each touchpoint from offer acceptance to day one with optimal timing (e.g., "Day 1 post-acceptance," "One week before start").

**2. Message Templates**  
Provide 4–6 editable templates in sequence, each labeled with purpose, suggested sender, channel, and personalization notes.

**3. Tone & Style Guidelines**  
Describe the voice that reflects company culture while addressing common anxieties (e.g., warmth vs. formality, transparency about first-week unknowns).

**4. Channel & Personalization Strategy**  
Recommend when to use email, video, Slack, or physical mail, and how to adapt for different role levels or departments.

**5. Implementation Checklist**  
Bullet-point action items for rolling out the plan.

Format all guidance as organized bullet points with specific examples for immediate use.
```
