# Vocabulary Enrichment Prompt for Copywriting

## 簡介

The Vocabulary Enrichment Prompt for Copywriting is a free AI prompt that analyzes your text and writing goal to build a custom database of high-impact words and persuasive phrases for copywriters and marketers. This vocabulary enrichment prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes any text sample and a stated writing goal, then returns a structured table of 20+ power words and phrases ranked by emotional impact (1-5 scale). Each entry includes contextual usage notes, concrete application examples, category tags (Emotional, Persuasive, Action-oriented, Descriptive), and 2-3 alternative options. Writers use it to upgrade flat copy, find stronger calls-to-action, add sensory language to landing pages, and discover persuasive alternatives when a draft feels weak. This prompt is for copywriters refining sales pages, content marketers optimizing email campaigns, and anyone who needs their words to evoke emotion and drive action. ● Produces a minimum of 20 curated entries ranked by power level, sorted highest to lowest for quick scanning ● Tags each word or phrase by category so you can target emotional appeal, persuasion, action, or vivid description ● Supplies real application examples and 2-3 alternatives per entry to give you flexible options that fit tone and voice ● Adapts recommendations to your specific text and goal rather than offering generic word lists ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a vocabulary curator specializing in high-impact copywriting language.

## Task
Analyze the provided text and writing goal, then generate a comprehensive vocabulary database of power words and phrases tailored to strengthen that specific copy. Curate language that evokes emotion, persuades, inspires action, and creates vivid imagery.

## Context
**Text to enhance:**
{{text}}

**Writing goal:**
{{goal}}

## Output
Deliver a table with exactly these columns:

| Word/Phrase | Context/Usage | Power Level (1-5 ⚡) | Example Application | Category | Alternative Options |

**Requirements:**
- Minimum 20 entries
- Sort by Power Level (highest to lowest)
- Categories: (E) Emotional, (P) Persuasive, (A) Action-oriented, (D) Descriptive
- Tailor recommendations to the provided text and goal
- Include usage context and concrete examples for each entry
- Provide 2-3 alternatives per word/phrase

Return only the table with no preamble or commentary.
```
