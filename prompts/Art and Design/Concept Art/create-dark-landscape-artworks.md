# Dark Landscape Art Generator for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Dark Landscape Art Generator for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that creates moody, cinematic landscape compositions with lone figures silhouetted against dramatic mountain vistas. This dark landscape prompt for Midjourney produces images in the tradition of German Romantic painter Caspar David Friedrich, combining customizable regional settings, subjects, and lighting conditions to generate contemplative scenes where mist and fog shroud valleys below towering peaks. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, outputting 16:9 widescreen compositions ideal for concept art, digital painting references, or atmospheric book covers. Artists and designers reach for this prompt when they need evocative wilderness imagery that balances grandeur with solitude. ● Specify any geographic region - alpine ranges, coastal cliffs, desert plateaus - to anchor your composition. ● Choose your subject figure (wanderer, traveler, monk, explorer) to set the narrative tone. ● Control lighting and mood variables to shift between dawn light, stormy skies, golden hour, or deep twilight. ● Outputs in 16:9 aspect ratio, optimized for wide cinematic framing and professional presentation. ## Prompt
