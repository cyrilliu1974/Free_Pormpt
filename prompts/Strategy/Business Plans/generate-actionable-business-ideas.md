# Business Idea Generator From Skills and Resources

## 簡介

The Business Idea Generator From Skills and Resources is a free AI prompt that analyzes your unique capabilities and assets to produce 20 tailored business opportunities with actionable launch roadmaps. This business idea generator prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your skills and available resources as inputs, then producing a structured markdown table containing 20 distinct ventures. Each entry includes a clear business name, five concrete creation steps ordered by priority, and a rationale explaining exactly why the idea fits your profile. Unlike generic listicles, every suggestion maps directly to what you can actually do and what you have on hand. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text models. Entrepreneurs use it to escape analysis paralysis, career transitioners use it to monetize existing skills, and side-hustlers use it to identify low-resource opportunities that match their constraints. ● Produces exactly 20 business ideas, each grounded in the user's stated skills and resources rather than generic templates ● Provides five ordered creation steps per idea so users know what to do first, second, and next ● Includes a match rationale for every suggestion, explaining why the business fits the individual's profile ● Outputs a clean markdown table format ready to copy into notes, documents, or planning tools ## Prompt

```
## Role

You analyze an individual's skills and available resources, then generate personalized, actionable business ideas matched to their specific profile.

## Context

The user wants to identify viable business opportunities that genuinely fit their background. Generic ideas are useless here: every suggestion must map directly to the provided skills and resources.

## Task

1. Analyze the skills and resources below to identify strengths, transferable assets, and resource constraints.
2. Generate exactly 20 business ideas that build directly on this specific profile.
3. For each idea, provide:
   - A clear business idea name
   - Five concrete creation steps (what to do, in order)
   - A rationale explaining why this idea fits the individual's skills and resources

Avoid generic or one-size-fits-all ideas. Every idea must be feasible given the provided profile.

## Output

Present all 20 ideas in this table:

| Business Idea Name | Creation Steps | Skillset Match Rationale |
|--------------------|----------------|------------------------|
| Idea 1 | 1. Step 1<br>2. Step 2<br>3. Step 3<br>4. Step 4<br>5. Step 5 | Rationale for Idea 1 |
| Idea 2 | 1. Step 1<br>2. Step 2<br>3. Step 3<br>4. Step 4<br>5. Step 5 | Rationale for Idea 2 |
| ... | ... | ... |
| Idea 20 | 1. Step 1<br>2. Step 2<br>3. Step 3<br>4. Step 4<br>5. Step 5 | Rationale for Idea 20 |

---

**My skills:** {{skills}}

**My available resources:** {{available-resources}}
```
