# Hyper-Realistic Mugshot Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Hyper-Realistic Mugshot Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces vintage police booking photo-style portraits with professional DSLR photography quality for creative projects, character development, and visual storytelling. This mugshot portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates full-body 8K images featuring subjects posed against height chart backgrounds with authentic 1960s booking photo aesthetics. The prompt delivers 50mm lens depth of field, natural lighting with dramatic shadows, desaturated sepia tones, and razor-sharp focus on subject details including facial features, clothing, and mugshot identification boards. Creators use it to generate character portraits for fiction, period storytelling, branding visuals, and creative campaigns that require authentic vintage photography style. This prompt is built for visual artists, writers, game developers, and content creators who need photorealistic retro mugshot imagery without staging physical photo shoots. ● Produces full-body portraits with height chart backgrounds, identification boards, and customizable dates matching 1960s police booking photo conventions. ● Configurable subject descriptions and character names while maintaining consistent DSLR 50mm lens aesthetic, natural bokeh, and sharp focus. ● Delivers 1080x1350px (4:5) aspect ratio images with desaturated color grading, soft balanced exposure, and vintage archival photograph texture. ● Includes detailed negative prompt to prevent cartoon styles, CGI rendering, artificial smoothing, and unrealistic proportions. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic 8K portrait photograph, shot on DSLR with 50mm lens, natural depth of field and razor-sharp focus. Full-body mugshot-style composition mimicking vintage police booking photo aesthetic.

{{subject-description}} leaning against wall with one knee bent, sharp jawline, intense eyes, confident slightly smug smirk. Neat groomed hair. Wearing tailored suit jacket open with no shirt underneath, slightly loosened dark tie matching jacket. Holding single shiny dark patent leather high-heeled shoe in right hand.

Mugshot identification board visible showing {{character-name}} and date 17/5/62.

Background: naturally blurred bokeh, slightly gritty studio wall texture with vertical height chart markings from 2'0" to 6'6". Desaturated, slightly sepia-toned archival photograph aesthetic. Strong overhead or frontal lighting creating dramatic shadows emphasizing height chart lines. Clean, soft, balanced exposure with accurate shadows and highlights.

1080x1350px (4:5) aspect ratio. Professional natural color grading, vintage photo aesthetic, true-to-life detail.

Negative prompt: No anime, cartoon, digital painting, illustration, 3D render, CGI, stylized features, plastic skin, fantasy glow, cinematic effects, airbrushed smoothing, overexposure, unnatural blur, video-game style, sketch, artificial lighting, unrealistic proportions, multiple shoes, modern background elements.
```
