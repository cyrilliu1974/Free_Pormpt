# Risk Register Update Prompt for ChatGPT and Claude

## 簡介

The Risk Register Update Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured risk registers for business units, project teams, and risk management specialists. This risk register prompt for ChatGPT works by guiding the model to identify new risks, reassess existing ones, evaluate each risk by likelihood and impact using your chosen assessment scale, and develop prioritized mitigation strategies. It outputs a markdown table with customizable structure that includes risk categories, ratings, and actionable mitigation plans. Teams use it for quarterly risk reviews, project kickoffs, compliance audits, and strategic planning sessions across industries from finance and healthcare to manufacturing and software development. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to maintain an up-to-date risk register that supports stakeholder communication and informed decision-making in dynamic business environments. ● Identifies and categorizes new risks while reassessing existing threats in your current business environment ● Evaluates each risk using your specified likelihood and impact assessment scale for consistent scoring ● Generates prioritized mitigation strategies focused on high-severity risks that require immediate attention ● Outputs a clean markdown table structure that stakeholders can read, share, and incorporate into governance workflows ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management specialist updating a Risk Register to identify, assess, and mitigate potential risks.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Risk Register in markdown table format that:

- Identifies new risks and reassesses existing ones within the current business environment
- Evaluates likelihood and impact for each risk using the specified assessment scale
- Develops mitigation strategies prioritized by risk severity
- Structures the register to support informed decision-making and stakeholder communication

## Context

{{risk-categories}}

{{risk-assessment-scale}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with {{table-structure}} containing:
- All identified risks categorized appropriately
- Likelihood and impact ratings
- Mitigation strategies for high-priority risks
- Clear, actionable information for stakeholders
```
