# Competitive Industry Landscape Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitive Industry Landscape Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed market intelligence reports for business analysts, strategists, and entrepreneurs researching any industry. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks the model through building a multi-section report: an industry overview with market size, growth rate, and key trends; a comparative table profiling three major competitors with market share, strengths, and weaknesses; a prioritized list of opportunities and threats; and evidence-based strategic recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only the industry name as input. Use cases include market-entry research, investor due diligence, product positioning, and quarterly strategic reviews. Reach for this prompt when you need structured, decision-ready competitive intelligence but lack the time to design an analysis framework from scratch. ● Produces an industry overview section with current market size, growth rate (CAGR or year-over-year), and three major trends. ● Builds a comparison table of three key competitors, listing market share, two to three strengths, and two to three weaknesses for each. ● Identifies three market opportunities and three threats, each with a description and assessment of potential impact or market potential. ● Delivers strategic recommendations explicitly tied to findings in the analysis, ensuring every suggestion is evidence-based. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business analyst specializing in competitive landscape analysis across industries.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive competitive landscape analysis for {{industry}}, identifying key market players, their strengths and weaknesses, and highlighting opportunities and threats. Provide strategic recommendations based on your findings.

## Analysis Requirements
- Conduct thorough research to gather accurate, current market intelligence
- Identify the 3 most relevant companies to profile
- Ensure descriptions and rationale are clear, concise, and free of unnecessary jargon
- Base all strategic recommendations on evidence from the analysis

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Industry Overview**
- Market size: [current valuation]
- Growth rate: [CAGR or year-over-year %]
- Key trends: [3 major trends shaping the industry]

**Competitive Landscape**

| Company | Market Share | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| [Company 1] | [%] | [2-3 key strengths] | [2-3 key weaknesses] |
| [Company 2] | [%] | [2-3 key strengths] | [2-3 key weaknesses] |
| [Company 3] | [%] | [2-3 key strengths] | [2-3 key weaknesses] |

**Opportunities**
1. [Opportunity 1]: [Description and market potential]
2. [Opportunity 2]: [Description and market potential]
3. [Opportunity 3]: [Description and market potential]

**Threats**
1. [Threat 1]: [Description and potential impact]
2. [Threat 2]: [Description and potential impact]
3. [Threat 3]: [Description and potential impact]

**Strategic Recommendations**
1. [Recommendation 1]: [Rationale tied to analysis findings]
2. [Recommendation 2]: [Rationale tied to analysis findings]
3. [Recommendation 3]: [Rationale tied to analysis findings]
```
