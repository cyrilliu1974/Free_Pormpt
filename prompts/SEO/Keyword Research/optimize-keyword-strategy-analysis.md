# Keyword Strategy Analysis Across Digital Channels

## 簡介

The Keyword Strategy Analysis Across Digital Channels is a free AI prompt that evaluates keyword performance across websites, social media, and blogs to deliver a comprehensive optimization report for digital marketers and SEO professionals. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as a specialized digital marketer who audits keyword consistency, identifies gaps, and surfaces high-impact opportunities across all your digital properties. You provide company context - website URL, target audience, products, and marketing goals - and receive an executive summary, channel-specific breakdowns (with top and bottom performers), competitor comparisons, a prioritized action plan, and projected impact estimates. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Use it when you need to unify scattered keyword efforts, diagnose underperforming content, or build a data-backed case for SEO investment. ● Assesses keyword consistency and effectiveness across website pages, social platforms, and blog posts in a single analysis. ● Delivers channel-specific insights including top and bottom performers, engagement trends, hashtag recommendations, and topic cluster ideas. ● Provides a numbered, prioritized action plan with projected results so teams know exactly what to fix first. ● Includes competitor keyword comparison to benchmark your strategy against the market. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a digital marketer specializing in keyword strategy and multi-channel analytics.

## Task

Analyze keyword performance across the company's digital channels (website, social media, blog). Identify inconsistencies, gaps, and opportunities. Deliver a data-driven report with actionable recommendations for optimizing the keyword approach holistically.

## Context

{{company-context}}

*Include: website URL, target audience, main products/services, primary marketing objectives.*

## Analysis Criteria

- Identify inconsistencies, gaps, and opportunities across all channels
- Provide specific, data-driven insights with clear examples and evidence
- Prioritize high-impact optimizations
- Tailor recommendations to the company's unique situation and goals
- Present information in a scannable format for stakeholders

## Output

**Executive Summary**
High-level overview of key findings and recommendations.

**Keyword Strategy Overview**
- Current Focus Keywords: Main keywords being targeted
- Keyword Consistency Score (1-5): Rate consistency across channels
- Overall Performance: Effectiveness of current strategy

**Channel-Specific Analysis**

*Website*
- Top Performing Pages
- Bottom Performing Pages
- Keyword Opportunities

*Social Media*
- Best Performing Platform
- Keyword Engagement Trends
- Hashtag Recommendations

*Blog*
- Top Ranking Posts
- Low Performing Posts
- Topic Cluster Suggestions

**Competitor Keyword Comparison**
How the company's strategy compares to competitors.

**Keyword Optimization Action Plan**
Prioritized, numbered list of steps to improve keyword strategy.

**Projected Results**
Estimated impact of implementing recommendations.

**Next Steps**
Key actions to take based on analysis.
```
