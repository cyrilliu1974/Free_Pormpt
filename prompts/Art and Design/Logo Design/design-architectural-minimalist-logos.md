# Architectural Minimalist Logo Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Architectural Minimalist Logo Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates clean, professional property sector logos using flat vector design and abstract architectural forms. This architectural minimalist logo prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces brand-ready visuals that pair structural symbols with strong sans-serif wordmarks on clean backgrounds, perfect for real estate firms, property management companies, and development agencies that need scalable assets for signage, websites, print materials, and digital marketing. The prompt accepts three variables - company name, property sector (residential, commercial, industrial), and brand color palette - and outputs logos that communicate solidity, trustworthiness, and modern professionalism without gradients or ornamental complexity. Reach for this prompt when you need corporate-grade logo design that works across every medium, from business cards to building signage, without hiring a design agency or managing revision rounds. ● Flat vector output with abstract structural symbols derived from architectural forms like beams, frames, and facades ● Fully customizable company name wordmark, property sector focus, and brand color palette ● Scalable for all applications: signage, brochures, websites, social media, and print collateral ● Clean white or transparent backgrounds ensure versatility across light and dark layouts ## Prompt

```
Flat vector logo design for {{company-name}}, a {{property-sector}} real estate company. Architectural minimalism style with abstract symbol derived from structural forms paired with strong sans-serif wordmark. Brand personality: solid, trustworthy, modern. Color palette: {{brand-colors}}. Clean white or transparent background, no gradients, fully scalable for signage, print, and digital use.
```
