# Cozy Product Photography Prompt for Midjourney

## 簡介

The Cozy Product Photography Prompt for Midjourney is a free AI prompt that generates warm, sunlit interior product shots with branded shipping boxes, balloons, and elegant confetti for e-commerce sellers, unboxing campaigns, and social media marketers. This cozy product photography prompt for Midjourney produces a 45-degree elevated shot of a cardboard shipping box bursting open with muted earth-tone balloons (terracotta, sage, cream), shiny gold alphabet balloons spelling a custom message, and floating confetti in your chosen color palette. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering soft natural window light, shallow depth of field (f/2.8), warm color grading with slight desaturation, and background elements like potted monstera plants. Use it for product launch announcements, subscription box marketing, thank-you campaign visuals, or Instagram-ready unboxing content that feels celebratory yet refined. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping product photography that balances professionalism with warmth, or when flat-lay shots feel too sterile and you want an inviting, lifestyle-driven aesthetic. ● Control the message spelled out in alphabet balloons, the confetti color palette, and additional scene details through three variables. ● Built-in styling for soft natural light, shallow depth of field, and warm desaturated color grading that feels organic and inviting. ● Eliminates plastic clutter, harsh shadows, and oversaturated colors with negative prompting baked into the template. ● Outputs 16:9 aspect ratio images optimized for web banners, email headers, and social media posts. ## Prompt

```
Product photography in a cozy sunlit interior; a cardboard shipping box with subtle branding on a wooden surface, shot from a 45-degree elevated angle. The box bursts open—organic latex balloons in muted earth tones (terracotta, sage, cream), plus shiny gold alphabet balloons spelling {{message}}. Elegant confetti in {{color-palette}} floats, creating a celebratory-yet-refined mood. Soft natural light from a nearby window, potted monstera in background, shallow depth of field (f/2.8), warm color grade with slight desaturation. {{scene-details}} --ar 16:9 --s 250 --q 2 --c 10 --no plastic clutter, harsh shadows, oversaturated colors
```
