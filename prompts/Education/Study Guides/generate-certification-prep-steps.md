# Certification Exam Prep Study Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Certification Exam Prep Study Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that builds personalized, actionable study plans for professionals preparing for industry certifications. This certification prep prompt for ChatGPT analyzes official exam syllabi, breaks down core domains by weight, and maps out a phased study strategy from foundation to mastery. It recommends authoritative resources - official guides, practice tests, and online courses - then constructs a realistic timeline that allocates study hours by topic complexity. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for IT professionals tackling AWS or Cisco exams, project managers studying for PMP, healthcare workers preparing for NCLEX, or finance candidates targeting CFA or CPA credentials. Use this prompt when you need more than a list of topics: when you want a complete preparation roadmap that accounts for your available time, learning style, and exam-specific requirements. ● Deconstructs official exam blueprints into weighted domains and high-value topics ● Recommends spaced repetition, active recall, and self-assessment techniques matched to the learner's style ● Distinguishes essential from supplementary resources and flags free versus paid options ● Builds realistic timelines with buffer periods for weak areas and final review ● Includes practice exam schedules, mistake-analysis workflows, and readiness benchmarks ● Provides exam-day logistics checklists and stress-management tactics for test day ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert certification exam preparation specialist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step study guide for:

**Certification & Subject:** {{certification-and-subject}}

**Learner Profile:** {{learner-profile}}

## Guide Structure

Your guide must include:

### 1. Exam Analysis
- Break down the official syllabus into core domains and topics
- Map key learning objectives and their relative weight on the exam
- Identify high-value areas that warrant focused attention

### 2. Study Strategy
- Recommend a phased preparation approach (foundation → intermediate → mastery → review)
- Suggest proven study techniques suited to the learner's profile
- Outline methods for active recall, spaced repetition, and self-assessment

### 3. Resources
- List authoritative study materials: official guides, textbooks, practice exams, and online courses
- Distinguish between essential and supplementary resources
- Note free vs. paid options where applicable

### 4. Study Schedule
- Create a realistic timeline based on available study time
- Allocate hours per topic according to complexity and exam weighting
- Build in buffer time for weak areas and final review

### 5. Practice & Assessment
- Recommend practice exam frequency and timing
- Explain how to analyze mistakes and adjust study focus
- Suggest performance benchmarks for readiness

### 6. Exam-Day Preparation
- Provide logistics checklist (registration, ID, materials, location)
- Share stress management and test-taking tactics
- Outline final 24-hour preparation steps

## Output Format
Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout. Make the guide immediately actionable.
```
