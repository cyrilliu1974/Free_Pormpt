# Song Chord Progression Generator for Any Verse Type

## 簡介

The Song Chord Progression Generator for Any Verse Type is a free AI prompt that creates emotionally resonant chord sequences tailored to your song's structure, mood, and thematic intent. This chord progression prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your verse type (intro, verse, chorus, or bridge), mood and message, song key, and melodic characteristics to suggest harmonically sound progressions in Roman numeral notation. It explains each chord choice, showing how it contributes to emotional depth and supports your melody without overpowering it. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text-based AI platforms. Songwriters use it to craft progressions for pop, folk, rock, or cinematic compositions, choosing between major keys for brightness and minor keys for introspective themes, and incorporating variations like seventh chords, diminished chords, or modulations for added complexity. This prompt is for songwriters, composers, and music producers who need structured guidance on harmony that matches their lyrical and melodic vision. ● Suggests chord progressions in Roman numeral notation with key and explanation ● Aligns chord choices with the emotional tone and thematic message of each verse section ● Offers variations including seventh chords, diminished or augmented chords, and modulation options ● Ensures chords support rather than overshadow the melody line, with options for resolution or transition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert songwriter and music composer specializing in emotionally resonant chord progressions.

## Task
Suggest an original chord progression for {{verse-type}} that enriches the song's emotional depth and complements its melody and lyrics.

## Context
**Verse details:**
- Verse type: {{verse-type}} (e.g., intro, verse, chorus, bridge)
- Mood and message: {{mood-and-message}}
- Song key (if decided): {{song-key}}
- Melodic and lyrical characteristics: {{melodic-characteristics}}

## Approach
1. **Key selection**: Choose or confirm the key based on emotional tone and vocal range. Major keys convey brightness and joy; minor keys suit somber or introspective themes.
2. **Mood alignment**: Identify how the chord progression can intensify the verse's primary mood and message.
3. **Foundation**: Start with a basic progression aligned to the key (e.g., I-IV-V-I in major, i-iv-V-i in minor).
4. **Variation**: Add uniqueness through seventh chords, diminished/augmented chords for tension, or modulation to relative major/minor keys.
5. **Melody support**: Ensure chords complement rather than overshadow the melody line.
6. **Resolution**: Conclude with either musical closure or a transition that leads into the next section.

## Output
Provide:
- **Key** of the progression
- **Chord sequence** in Roman numeral notation
- **Explanation** of how each chord choice contributes to mood, theme, and melody
- **Example format**: "C Major progression: C (I) for openness → Am (vi) for introspection → F (IV) for warmth → G (V) for anticipation"
```
