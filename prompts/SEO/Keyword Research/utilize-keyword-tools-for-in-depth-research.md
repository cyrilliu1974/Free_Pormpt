# Keyword Research Tool Analysis Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Tool Analysis Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that identifies high-value keyword opportunities by analyzing search volume, competition level, and relevance for your specific website and audience. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your website and audience context as input, then systematically evaluating keywords through the lens of leading SEO tools including Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Google Keyword Planner. It delivers a prioritized markdown table of 10-15 keywords ranked by potential impact, complete with search volume and difficulty metrics, plus strategic analysis of your top three opportunities. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across all major text-generation models. SEO specialists use it to uncover competitor keyword gaps, find long-tail variations with strong conversion potential, and build data-driven content strategies that balance traffic potential with ranking feasibility. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond guesswork and build a keyword strategy grounded in actual search data and competitive analysis. ● Produces a structured markdown table ranking 10-15 keywords by search volume, difficulty, and strategic value ● Identifies long-tail keyword variations and competitor gaps specific to your niche ● Includes written rationale explaining why the top three keywords represent the best opportunities ● Considers both traffic potential and conversion likelihood, not just raw search numbers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist conducting comprehensive keyword research to identify high-traffic, low-competition opportunities.

## Task
Analyze the provided website context and deliver a prioritized list of keywords that balance search volume, competition level, and relevance. Your research should:

- Identify keywords with high search volume and low keyword difficulty
- Include long-tail keyword variations with strong conversion potential
- Evaluate relevance to the website's niche and target audience
- Consider competitor keyword gaps and opportunities
- Prioritize by potential impact on traffic and conversions

## Context
{{website-and-audience-context}}

Approach this systematically: understand the niche, gather data from leading SEO tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Keyword Planner, etc.), assess each keyword's strategic value, and rank opportunities by their likelihood to drive meaningful results.

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Keyword | Search Volume | Keyword Difficulty |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|

Include 10-15 keywords, ordered by priority. After the table, briefly explain your top 3 recommendations and why they represent the best opportunities.
```
