# Business Plan Generator for Self-Employed Professionals

## 簡介

The Business Plan Generator for Self-Employed Professionals is a free AI prompt that produces a structured, comprehensive business plan for solo entrepreneurs and self-employed individuals. This business plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and current situation as input, then generates a detailed five-column markdown table that organizes your entire strategic roadmap. Each column addresses a critical planning component: an executive summary with your mission and key success factors, market analysis including competitive landscape and target demographics, financial projections spanning 12-month and 3-year forecasts with break-even analysis, marketing strategies detailing customer acquisition channels and pricing, and a prioritized action plan with milestones and KPIs. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering actionable business intelligence tailored to your specific venture. Ideal for consultants, freelancers, coaches, and solo business owners who need to consolidate their strategic thinking into a coherent plan for growth, funding applications, or operational clarity. ● Produces a complete business plan organized in a scannable markdown table format with five strategic pillars ● Delivers detailed financial projections including revenue forecasts, cost structures, break-even analysis, and funding requirements ● Generates market analysis covering customer demographics, competitive positioning, and market trends specific to your niche ● Creates an actionable roadmap with prioritized milestones, resource allocation, KPIs, and quarterly review checkpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist specializing in self-employment and solo venture planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive business plan structured as a detailed roadmap for productivity and success. Analyze the market landscape, project financials, devise marketing strategies, and outline a concrete action plan. Ensure each component is thorough, realistic, and aligned with stated business goals.

## Context
**Business overview:** {{business-and-market-context}}
(Include: type of business, target market, unique selling proposition)

**Starting position:** {{current-situation-and-goals}}
(Include: current financial situation, available resources, long-term business goals)

## Output
Deliver the business plan as a markdown table with 5 columns:

| Executive Summary | Market Analysis | Financial Projections | Marketing Strategies | Action Plan |
|-------------------|-----------------|----------------------|---------------------|-------------|

**Executive Summary:** Business concept, mission, key success factors, and high-level objectives.

**Market Analysis:** Target customer demographics and psychographics, competitive landscape, market size and trends, positioning strategy.

**Financial Projections:** Revenue forecasts (12-month and 3-year), startup and operating costs, break-even analysis, profit margins, funding requirements.

**Marketing Strategies:** Customer acquisition channels, pricing strategy, brand positioning, promotional tactics, sales funnel design.

**Action Plan:** Prioritized milestones with timelines, resource allocation, key performance indicators, risk mitigation steps, quarterly review points.

Each cell should contain detailed, actionable information. Use bullet points within cells for clarity where appropriate.
```
