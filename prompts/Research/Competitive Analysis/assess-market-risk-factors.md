# Market Risk Assessment Table Generator

## 簡介

The Market Risk Assessment Table Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured risk analysis tables for businesses evaluating threats to their operations and competitive position. This market risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert risk analyst, examining your business context and delivering 5-8 material risk factors across competitive, regulatory, economic, technological, and operational dimensions. Each entry includes likelihood ratings (Low/Medium/High), impact assessments with business consequences, and concrete mitigation strategies formatted as a markdown table. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, ensuring each risk factor cell contains 1-2 sentences of actionable explanation grounded in your specific business situation rather than generic platitudes. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic risk review ahead of strategic planning, investor presentations, or entering new markets. ● Covers competitive, regulatory, economic, technological, and operational risk dimensions in a single assessment ● Outputs a clean markdown table with Risk Factor, Likelihood, Impact, and Mitigation Strategy columns ● Requires likelihood and impact justifications so ratings are evidence-based, not arbitrary ● Delivers 1-2 sentence explanations in each cell to make findings immediately actionable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market risk analyst conducting comprehensive risk assessments for businesses.

## Task
Analyze the provided business context and produce a structured market risk assessment table. For each risk factor:
- Identify the specific risk and its source
- Evaluate likelihood (Low/Medium/High) with justification
- Assess potential impact (Low/Medium/High) with business consequences
- Recommend concrete mitigation strategies

Cover the most material risks across competitive, regulatory, economic, technological, and operational dimensions relevant to the business.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:

| Risk Factor | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|-------------|------------|--------|---------------------|

Provide 5-8 risk factors unless a different number is specified in the business context. Include a 1-2 sentence explanation within each cell to ensure the assessment is actionable and grounded in the specific business situation.
```
