# Photography Style Concept Generator for Products

## 簡介

The Photography Style Concept Generator for Products is a free AI prompt that creates professional commercial product photographs in any visual style and setting you choose. This photography style prompt for Midjourney produces images with professional commercial lighting, clean highlights, subtle shadows, and a modern minimalist atmosphere. It uses shallow depth of field to emphasize product details while maintaining a 16:9 aspect ratio ideal for web and presentation use. Whether you need lifestyle shots, studio setups, or environmental product photography, you control the style, subject, and backdrop to match your brand or campaign requirements. The prompt runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need product visuals for e-commerce, marketing decks, social media, or client presentations without a physical photoshoot. ● Specify any photography style (editorial, lifestyle, vintage, futuristic) to match brand aesthetics. ● Place any product in any setting, from studio backdrops to real-world environments. ● Delivers consistent professional lighting with clean highlights and controlled shadows. ● Uses shallow depth of field automatically to keep focus on the product while creating natural background blur. ## Prompt

```
{{photography-style}} depiction of {{product}} in {{setting}}, professional commercial lighting with clean highlights and subtle shadows, modern minimalist atmosphere, shot with shallow depth of field to emphasize product details, --ar 16:9 --s 750
```
