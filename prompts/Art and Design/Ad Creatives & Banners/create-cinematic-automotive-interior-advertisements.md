# Cinematic Automotive Interior Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Automotive Interior Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that produces premium three-panel EV interior visuals for automotive marketers and creative teams. This automotive interior prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates a 1×3 horizontal layout in 21:9 aspect ratio, delivering three connected scenes: a wide interior shot highlighting dashboard design and ambient lighting, a close-up of the digital interface and controls, and a driver's perspective through the windshield at golden hour. It transforms two simple variables - interior details and brand name - into cohesive luxury automotive advertising that communicates quiet innovation and refined comfort. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade vehicle interior visuals without studio shoots or physical vehicle access. ● Produces three compositionally balanced panels showing interior craftsmanship, technology displays, and serene driving context in a single horizontal layout ● Controls lighting balance with natural daylight spill and soft ambient LEDs, avoiding harsh glare and oversaturation ● Guides mood for each panel - luxury and calm for materials, innovation and clarity for interfaces, confidence and effortless mobility for the driving scene ● Supports customizable interior details and brand integration with minimal, illuminated logo presentation ## Prompt

```
Create a cinematic 1×3 horizontal advertisement in 21:9 aspect ratio highlighting the interior experience of an electric sedan, focusing on luxury materials, advanced technology, and calm driving comfort.

**Panel 1 – Interior Design & Materials**
Wide interior shot showcasing dashboard, seats, and ambient lighting. Soft ambient interior lighting with daylight spill. {{interior-details}}. Mood: luxury, calm, refinement.

**Panel 2 – Technology & Interface**
Close-up of digital dashboard and center display. Focus on seamless UI, navigation, and vehicle controls. Clean screen glow with low reflections. Mood: innovation, clarity, control.

**Panel 3 – Driving Serenity**
Driver's perspective through windshield showing open highway at golden hour, hands relaxed on steering wheel. Natural daylight. Mood: confidence, comfort, effortless mobility.

**Branding & Style**
Product name: {{brand-name}}. Brand personality: quiet luxury, advanced, future-forward. Logo presentation: minimal, illuminated or embossed.

Visual style: luxury automotive advertising, high realism, minimalist interior design, cinematic composition, premium EV branding. Automotive interior and lifestyle photography with natural daylight combined with soft ambient LEDs. Balanced exposure with low glare, soft controlled shadows.

Overall mood: calm, luxurious, advanced, quiet, modern, effortless.

**Avoid:** Cheap interior materials, overly glossy surfaces, cluttered dashboard, unreadable screens, harsh lighting, oversaturated colors, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
