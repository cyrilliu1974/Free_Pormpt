# Content Marketing Ideas Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Ideas Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that produces 10 tailored, sales-driven content ideas mapped to the customer journey for small business owners and solo entrepreneurs. This content marketing prompt for ChatGPT uses the Jobs-to-be-Done framework to match creative, viral-potential ideas to each stage - Passive Looking, Active Looking, Deciding, Consuming, and Satisfaction. You input your business description, target audience, and available channels (LinkedIn, Twitter, blog, YouTube, email, etc.), and the prompt returns a structured table with audience questions, content formats, impact scores, and implementation timelines. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to help solopreneurs plan organic campaigns that sell without paid ads, executable in 2–4 weeks with minimal budget. Use it when you need fresh angles that bridge awareness and conversion, or when repurposing existing content across multiple platforms. ● Maps every idea to a specific JTBD stage and audience question for intent alignment ● Scores impact potential (0–10) so you prioritize high-reach, high-conversion tactics first ● Outputs a clean five-column table: stage, question, channel, idea, and score ● Focuses on organic sales tactics - no ad spend required, fast turnaround ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional content marketer specializing in organic, sales-focused content strategy for solopreneurs. You understand the Jobs-to-be-Done framework and how to map content ideas to each stage of the customer journey.

## Task
Generate 10 content marketing ideas tailored to the business, target audience, and available channels provided below. Each idea must be:

- Creative and attention-grabbing with viral potential
- Implementable within 2–4 weeks with minimal budget
- Designed to sell the product organically (no paid ads)

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

**Available channels:** {{channels}}

## Output
Return a table with exactly 5 columns:

| JTBD Stage | Audience Question | Channel | Content Idea | Impact (0–10) |
|------------|-------------------|---------|--------------|---------------|

**JTBD Stage** must be one of: Passive Looking, Active Looking, Deciding, Consuming, Satisfaction.

**Impact score:** 10 = highest potential reach and conversion; 0 = lowest.
```
