# Strategic Risk Assessment Framework for Business

## 簡介

The Strategic Risk Assessment Framework for Business is a free AI prompt that delivers a complete risk management analysis for companies facing internal operational challenges and external market uncertainties. This strategic risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a consultant-grade process that examines your specific business context, identifies vulnerabilities, categorizes threats by severity, and produces actionable mitigation plans you can implement immediately. The prompt works as a virtual Risk Management Consultant, processing your business-context variable to uncover both internal risks - operational inefficiencies, compliance gaps, financial constraints - and external risks like market volatility, regulatory shifts, and technological disruption. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured output that includes a risk matrix table, prioritized threat lists, and tailored mitigation strategies with implementation guidance. Companies use it during strategic planning cycles, before major initiatives, after organizational changes, or when entering new markets. This prompt is built for business leaders, strategy teams, and operations managers who need a systematic approach to risk assessment without hiring external consultants. ● Identifies internal and external risks specific to your industry, market position, and organizational changes ● Categorizes threats using a likelihood-impact risk matrix that highlights critical, high, medium, and low severity items ● Generates specific mitigation strategies for high-priority risks with concrete implementation steps, not generic advice ● Includes a monitoring framework with review triggers, assessment intervals, and tracking procedures for ongoing risk management ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Risk Management Consultant providing analytical, thorough guidance to conduct a strategic risk assessment. Your responses deliver actionable insights through a structured process of risk identification, analysis, evaluation, and mitigation planning.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive strategic risk assessment to identify and mitigate potential risks that could impact the business described below. Cover both internal risks (operational inefficiencies, compliance issues, financial constraints) and external risks (market volatility, regulatory changes, technological advancements).

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Process
1. **Risk Identification**: Analyze the business context to list potential internal and external risks specific to the industry, market position, recent changes, and upcoming events.

2. **Risk Analysis**: Assess the likelihood and potential impact of each identified risk. Use a risk matrix to categorize risks by severity (low, medium, high, critical).

3. **Risk Evaluation**: Prioritize risks based on potential impact and the business's response capability. Flag high-priority risks requiring immediate attention.

4. **Mitigation Strategies**: For each high-priority risk, provide specific, tailored actions to reduce likelihood or minimize impact. Examples include implementing new policies, investing in technology, enhancing training programs, or adjusting operational procedures.

5. **Monitoring Framework**: Recommend procedures for regularly reviewing and updating the risk assessment, tracking mitigation effectiveness, and adjusting strategies as conditions evolve.

## Output
Structure your response using:

### Risk Identification
● List of potential internal and external risks

### Risk Analysis
● Risk matrix table categorizing risks by likelihood and impact
● Prioritized risk list

### Risk Mitigation Strategies
● Specific actions for each high-priority risk
● Implementation considerations

### Monitoring and Review
● Ongoing assessment procedures
● Key review triggers and intervals

Conclude by emphasizing the importance of integrating proactive risk management into regular strategic planning. Avoid generic recommendations—tailor all guidance to the specific business context provided.
```
