# Local SEO Landing Page Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Local SEO Landing Page Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized local SEO strategies and landing page blueprints for businesses targeting specific geographic markets. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business location and primary keyword to deliver actionable guidance across 11 critical optimization areas: localized content strategy, schema markup implementation, NAP consistency protocols, click-to-call features, embedded maps, local imagery selection, testimonial placement, mobile optimization priorities, link-building tactics, conversion techniques, and video content recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured strategy document that estimates search volume and keyword difficulty while tailoring every recommendation to your specific market context. Digital marketing agencies use it to standardize local campaign planning; small business owners rely on it to compete against larger brands in their geographic area; and SEO consultants deploy it to accelerate client onboarding. This prompt is built for anyone who needs a replicable framework for location-based digital marketing without starting from a blank page each time. ● Outputs search volume estimates, keyword difficulty ratings, and 11 tactical implementation sections in a consistent emoji-labeled format ● Includes schema markup steps, local link-building tactics, and mobile-first optimization priorities specific to the target location ● Provides click-to-call setup guidance, Google Maps embedding instructions, and localized testimonial sourcing strategies ● Delivers conversion optimization techniques and video content recommendations aligned with the primary keyword and business context ## Prompt
