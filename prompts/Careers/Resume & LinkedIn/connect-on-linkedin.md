# LinkedIn Networking Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Networking Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized outreach campaigns for professionals expanding their LinkedIn network within a specific industry. This LinkedIn networking strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your target industry and professional profile as input, then produces a complete five-stage networking plan: identification of key professionals, research tactics for learning about prospects, personalized connection request templates emphasizing shared interests and collaboration opportunities, follow-up timing and approaches, and long-term relationship nurturing tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Real use cases include career changers researching a new field, sales professionals building enterprise relationships, and consultants expanding into adjacent verticals. Reach for this prompt when you need more than generic connection messages - when you want a systematic outreach framework that adapts to both your background and the culture of your target industry. ● Produces research checklists for discovering shared interests, mutual connections, and collaboration opportunities before you send a request ● Generates connection message templates with clear syntactic structure that highlight mutual value rather than one-sided asks ● Organizes the entire strategy by networking stage - identification, research, outreach, follow-up, nurturing - so you know exactly what to do next ● Adapts tone and talking points to fit the norms of your target industry, from enterprise tech to creative services ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn networking strategist who designs outreach campaigns tailored to specific industries and professional goals.

## Task
Create a step-by-step networking strategy that includes:
- Analysis of target professionals in the industry
- Research approach for their backgrounds and interests
- Personalized connection request templates using syntactic structure that emphasizes shared interests and collaboration opportunities
- Follow-up and relationship-nurturing tactics

## Context
Target industry: {{target-industry}}

Your professional profile: {{professional-profile}}
(Include background, career goals, areas of expertise, and location)

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a bullet-point list organized by networking stage:
- **Identification**: How to find key professionals
- **Research**: What to learn about prospects
- **Outreach**: Message templates with syntactic clarity that highlight mutual value
- **Follow-up**: Timing and approaches for deepening connections
- **Nurturing**: Long-term relationship maintenance
```
