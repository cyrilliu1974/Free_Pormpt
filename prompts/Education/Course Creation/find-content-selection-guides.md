# Content Selection Guide Finder for Curriculum Design

## 簡介

The Content Selection Guide Finder for Curriculum Design is a free AI prompt that researches and evaluates educational resource guides for curriculum developers and instructional designers. This content selection guide prompt for ChatGPT analyzes comprehensive guides aligned with current educational standards, evaluating each on relevance, credibility, applicability, structure, content depth, and learning objective alignment. It produces a markdown comparison table with guide names, key features, and relevance ratings tailored to your specific subject area, educational level, and regional context. Use it when building K-12 or higher education curricula, adopting new instructional materials, or vetting resources for specific subjects or standards. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Accepts curriculum context including educational level, subject area, curriculum focus, and regional standards requirements ● Evaluates guides on six criteria: relevance, credibility, applicability, structure, content depth, and learning objective alignment ● Outputs a three-column markdown table comparing guide names, key features, and curriculum relevance ● Surfaces 5-8 high-value resources per query, streamlining the research and vetting process for instructional designers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert curriculum developer specializing in educational resource evaluation and curation.

## Task
Identify and evaluate the best Content Selection Guides for curriculum development. Research comprehensive guides that align with current educational standards and best practices. Evaluate each guide based on relevance, credibility, applicability, structure, content depth, and alignment with learning objectives.

## Context
{{curriculum-context}}

Include in your context:
- Educational level (e.g., K-5, middle school, undergraduate)
- Subject area
- Specific curriculum focus, if any
- Regional or country context that may affect standards

## Output
Provide your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Guide Name**: Title and source of the resource
- **Key Features**: Structure, approach, and distinctive elements
- **Relevance**: How it addresses the specific subject area and curriculum needs

Include 5-8 of the most valuable resources.
```
