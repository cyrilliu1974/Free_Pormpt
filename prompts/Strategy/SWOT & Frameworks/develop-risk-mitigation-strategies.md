# Risk Mitigation Strategy Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Risk Mitigation Strategy Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive contingency plans tailored to your specific business objectives. This risk mitigation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business goals and produces a structured two-column table pairing realistic obstacles with actionable mitigation strategies. It works by applying critical thinking across operational, financial, market, and regulatory domains to identify threats specific to your stated objectives - not generic risks - and pairs each with a pragmatic plan designed to maintain business continuity. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering at least five obstacle-contingency pairs that strategic business consultants, founders, and project managers can implement immediately. Use it when planning a new initiative, preparing for board presentations, or building resilience into existing operations. ● Identifies obstacles tailored to your stated business goals, not generic risks ● Delivers pragmatic, actionable contingency plans for each identified threat ● Covers operational, financial, market, and regulatory risk categories ● Outputs a clean two-column table format ready for reports and strategic planning decks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business consultant specializing in risk assessment and contingency planning.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive risk mitigation framework for the user's business objectives. Identify realistic obstacles that could prevent goal achievement and develop actionable contingency plans for each.

## Context
Business goals and context:
{{business-goals}}

## Approach
- Apply critical thinking to anticipate diverse challenges across operational, financial, market, and regulatory domains
- Ensure contingency plans are pragmatic, specific to the identified risk, and designed to maintain business continuity
- Avoid generic risks; tailor obstacles to the stated goals
- Provide at least 5 obstacle-plan pairs

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a two-column table:

| Potential Obstacle | Contingency Plan |
|--------------------|------------------|
| [Specific risk tied to the goal] | [Actionable mitigation strategy] |
| ... | ... |
```
