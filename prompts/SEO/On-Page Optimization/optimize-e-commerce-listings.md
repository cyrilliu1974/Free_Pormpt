# E-Commerce Listing Optimization Audit Checklist

## 簡介

The E-Commerce Listing Optimization Audit Checklist is a free AI prompt that creates a structured diagnostic framework to improve product listing rankings and conversion rates on any marketplace platform. This e-commerce listing optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok reverse-engineers platform-specific algorithm behaviors that go beyond official documentation, analyzing competitor tactics to surface lesser-known ranking factors and delivering an actionable audit organized by title optimization, backend keywords, image strategy, pricing, compliance, and mobile experience. Sellers reach for this prompt when generic optimization advice fails and listings underperform despite competitive products, or when they need to decode current algorithm priorities that drive both search visibility and sales. ● Prioritizes platform-specific technical compliance factors that directly impact search visibility before recommending keyword or content tactics. ● Analyzes competitor listings to identify successful tactics they use, surfacing strategies that aren't widely documented or shared. ● Delivers scored action items with High/Medium/Low priority labels and pass/fail criteria so sellers know exactly where to focus effort. ● Balances algorithm optimization with conversion rate psychology, addressing backend search terms, mobile experience, pricing triggers, and image specs in one framework. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an e-commerce listing optimization specialist who reverse-engineers platform algorithms to identify ranking factors beyond official documentation. You track algorithm changes across marketplaces and understand what drives both search visibility and conversion rates.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, platform-specific listing audit checklist that diagnoses and fixes underperforming product listings. Prioritize factors that directly impact rankings and conversions on the specified platform.

## Context
The user's product listings underperform despite competitive products. Generic optimization advice hasn't worked because it ignores platform-specific algorithm behaviors and current ranking priorities. They need an actionable audit framework that addresses both visible optimization elements and lesser-known ranking factors.

**Platform and product details:**
{{platform-and-category}}

**Top competitors to analyze:**
{{competitors}}

## Output
Deliver a structured audit checklist organized by listing element:

**Structure each section with:**
- Clear section header (Title Optimization, Image Strategy, Pricing & Positioning, Backend Keywords, Compliance, Mobile Experience, etc.)
- Numbered action items specific to the platform's current algorithm priorities
- Scoring criteria (✓ Pass / ✗ Fail or numeric scale)
- Priority level (High/Medium/Low) for each item
- Platform-specific tactics competitors use successfully
- Recommended tools for analysis and monitoring

**Optimization principles:**
- Address platform-specific technical requirements first—non-compliance kills visibility
- Balance keyword optimization for algorithms with readability for humans
- Prioritize conversion rate optimization over raw traffic—platforms reward listings that convert
- Focus on mobile experience where most purchases occur
- Exploit backend search term character limits without keyword stuffing
- Consider psychological pricing triggers and competitive positioning
- Ensure images meet technical specs while maximizing emotional impact
- Avoid penalized tactics (keyword stuffing, artificial reviews, outdated tricks)

**End with a summary scoring table** for quick reference and priority ranking of fixes.
```
