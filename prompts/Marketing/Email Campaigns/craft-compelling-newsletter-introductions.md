# Newsletter Story Introduction Writer

## 簡介

The Newsletter Story Introduction Writer is a free AI prompt that creates engaging, audience-tested story openings for newsletter publishers and content creators. This newsletter introduction prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft concise (150 words or fewer) opening passages that hook readers immediately, introduce story intrigue without revealing outcomes, and align with your newsletter's established tone. You provide a story brief and your desired voice; the prompt structures a four-step process - opening with a surprising fact or provocative question, hinting at conflict or mystery, matching your style throughout, and closing with a teaser that compels readers forward. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for it when you need consistent, compelling intros that reduce drop-off and maintain reader anticipation across every issue. ● Opens with strong hooks - surprising facts, provocative questions, or compelling details - that stop the scroll. ● Teases story conflict or mystery without spoiling outcomes, building anticipation for the full piece. ● Matches any newsletter voice you specify, ensuring brand consistency across issues. ● Delivers concise introductions under 150 words, respecting reader attention and mobile reading contexts. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert newsletter writer skilled in audience psychology and compelling storytelling.

## Task
Write an engaging introduction (150 words maximum) for a newsletter story that hooks readers immediately, aligns with the newsletter's established voice, and teases the content without spoiling it.

## Context
The introduction is the gateway to the full story. It must captivate from the first line, reflect the newsletter's consistent tone, and set up an immersive reading experience. Draw on what has historically resonated with this audience.

## Approach
1. **Open with a strong hook** — a surprising fact, provocative question, or compelling detail related to the topic
2. **Introduce the story's intrigue** — hint at the conflict, mystery, or unusual scenario without revealing outcomes
3. **Match the newsletter's established voice** throughout
4. **Close with a teaser** that ties back to the hook and compels the reader forward

**Story details:**
{{story-brief}}

**Newsletter voice:**
{{tone-and-style}}

## Output
Deliver a concise, hook-driven introduction (≤150 words) that feels native to the newsletter and makes the reader want to continue.
```
