# Flat Corporate Character Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Flat Corporate Character Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional vector-style illustrations of simplified human figures for corporate websites, apps, and marketing materials. This flat corporate character illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates grid-based scenes with gender-neutral figures in customizable activities, abstract backgrounds of soft hills and clouds, and accessible color palettes of blue, mint green, coral, and neutral grays. The output is scalable, UI-friendly, and designed for business use cases including banking apps, health platforms, SaaS interfaces, and digital presentations. Each scene features a single character with slightly exaggerated limbs for motion, minimal facial features, and rounded soft geometry that balances professionalism with approachability. Designers, marketers, and product teams building brand identities or refreshing digital assets without illustration budgets will find this prompt immediately useful. ● Produces simplified human figures with inclusive, gender-neutral body types and minimal facial features for universal appeal. ● Customizes activity scenes through the {{activity-scenes}} variable while maintaining clean grid layouts and consistent style. ● Outputs flat vector aesthetics with soft gradients, rounded shapes, and corporate-friendly palettes optimized for accessibility. ● Specifies 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratios at high resolution for flexible use in web interfaces, mobile apps, and print materials. ## Prompt

```
Flat vector editorial illustration in modern, clean corporate style. Simplified human figures with slightly exaggerated limbs for motion, minimal facial features, neutral expressions, inclusive gender-neutral body types. {{activity-scenes}}. Single character per scene in grid-based layout.

Abstract background: soft hills, clouds, simple ground shapes. Flat layers, minimal depth. Corporate palette of soft blue, mint green, warm coral, light orange, neutral gray with medium contrast for accessibility. Light neutral or pastel background.

Rounded shapes, soft geometry, subtle or no outlines. Flat colors with soft gradients, clean vector aesthetic. Energetic yet calm mood, trustworthy and approachable. Scalable, UI-friendly. Vector output, high resolution, 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio.

Avoid: photorealism, heavy textures, complex backgrounds, dark dramatic lighting, cartoonish exaggeration, hand-drawn sketch style, messy lines.
```
