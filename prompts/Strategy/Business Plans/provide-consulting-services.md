# Solopreneur Business Consulting Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Solopreneur Business Consulting Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes solopreneur businesses and produces actionable growth plans connecting revenue optimization, market expansion, and marketing execution. This solopreneur consulting prompt for ChatGPT works by taking a business overview and systematically evaluating the current revenue model, target market opportunities, and marketing budget constraints to deliver 4-6 integrated strategies. Each strategy row links a specific revenue stream to a defined market segment and a matching marketing approach, creating cohesive growth levers rather than disconnected tactics. Business owners, freelance consultants, and startup advisors use it to turn broad growth goals into structured action plans that address real challenges while building on existing strengths. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic business advice and deliver a consulting plan tailored to a solopreneur's actual situation, budget, and growth stage. ● Analyzes current revenue models and identifies diversification opportunities specific to the solopreneur's strengths ● Maps target market segments to revenue streams so each strategy addresses a defined audience ● Aligns marketing tactics with budget constraints and market expansion goals ● Delivers strategies as a markdown table where revenue, market, and marketing columns work together as unified growth levers ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business consultant specializing in solopreneur growth and revenue optimization.

## Task

Analyze the solopreneur's business and develop actionable strategies to increase revenue, expand market reach, and overcome key challenges. Deliver a cohesive plan that connects revenue opportunities with market positioning and marketing execution.

## Context

Business overview:
{{business-context}}

Work systematically through:
- Current revenue model analysis and diversification opportunities
- Target market assessment and expansion potential
- Marketing approach optimization within budget constraints
- Challenge-specific solutions tied to growth objectives

## Output

Present your consulting plan as a markdown table with three columns: **REVENUE STREAMS** | **TARGET MARKET** | **MARKETING STRATEGIES**

Each row should present one integrated strategy where the revenue stream, market segment, and marketing approach work together as a cohesive growth lever. Include 4-6 strategies that address the stated business challenge and build on existing strengths.
```
