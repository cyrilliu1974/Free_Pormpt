# Legal Notice Generator for Formal Requirements

## 簡介

The Legal Notice Generator for Formal Requirements is a free AI prompt that produces professionally formatted legal notices for attorneys, compliance officers, and business professionals who need to formally communicate obligations and deadlines. This legal notice prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini transforms event requirements and recipient details into a structured document that includes clearly numbered details, required actions with deadlines, potential consequences for non-compliance, and contact information. Users input the event or requirement triggering the notice and recipient information; the AI outputs a complete notice with proper salutation, body paragraphs, action items, legal language about consequences, and signature blocks. Real-world applications include lease violation notices, contract breach notifications, regulatory compliance warnings, payment demands, and cease-and-desist communications. Reach for this prompt when you need to draft a formal legal notice quickly while maintaining professional standards and ensuring all critical elements are present. ● Produces notices with standardized structure including date, recipient address block, numbered details, and required actions with deadlines ● Incorporates legal consequence language and contact information for recipient questions ● Guides the AI to use clear, accessible language while maintaining authoritative tone appropriate for legal communication ● Emphasizes accuracy, citation of applicable laws or contracts, and careful review standards ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal expert drafting a formal legal notice that clearly communicates requirements, deadlines, and consequences to a recipient.

## Task
Produce a comprehensive legal notice based on the event or requirement and recipient information provided. The notice must be professional, authoritative, and leave no room for misinterpretation.

## Context
**Event/Requirement:** {{event-requirement}}

**Recipient Information:** {{recipient-details}}

## Output
Structure the legal notice as follows:

**LEGAL NOTICE: [Event/Requirement Title]**

Date: [Current Date]

[Recipient Name]  
[Recipient Street Address]  
[Recipient City, State ZIP Code]

Dear [Recipient Name],

This legal notice is issued regarding [Event/Requirement]. Please take note of the following important details:

1. [Detail 1]
2. [Detail 2]
3. [Detail 3]

You are required to take the following action(s) by [Deadline]:

1. [Required Action 1]
2. [Required Action 2]

Failure to comply with the above requirements by the stated deadline may result in legal consequences, including but not limited to [Potential Consequences].

If you have questions or concerns regarding this notice, please contact [Contact Name] at [Contact Phone Number] or [Contact Email].

Sincerely,  
[Sender Name]  
[Sender Title]

---

**Drafting Standards:**
- Use clear, concise language accessible to non-lawyers
- Include all relevant details, deadlines, and required actions
- Cite applicable laws, regulations, or contracts where relevant
- Maintain a professional and authoritative tone
- Ensure accuracy through careful review
```
