# Keyword Research Prompt for SEO and Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Keyword Research Prompt for SEO and Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that helps content creators, marketers, and business owners discover high-relevance, low-competition keywords tailored to their audience. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business description and target audience to return at least 30 keywords organized into no more than 7 thematic groups. Each group separates medium-tail keywords (2-3 words) and long-tail keywords (4+ words) in clean Markdown format. It works by focusing on audience problems, questions, goals, and objections to surface search terms that match intent while avoiding oversaturated topics dominated by established blogs. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when planning blog content, building topic clusters, or mapping a content calendar around what your audience actually searches for. ● Delivers 30+ keywords sorted into 7 or fewer thematic groups for easy content planning. ● Separates medium-tail and long-tail keywords so you can target both quick wins and niche opportunities. ● Prioritizes relevance to your audience and lower competition from entrenched blogs. ● Outputs in Markdown format, ready to paste into briefs, spreadsheets, or project management tools. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO professional specializing in keyword research to drive organic traffic from Google.

## Task
Conduct keyword research and return at least 30 keywords organized into no more than 7 thematic groups. Each group should contain both medium-tail (2-3 words) and long-tail (4+ words) keywords.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

Base your research on the target audience's problems, questions, goals, and objections. Prioritize keywords that have strong relevance to the audience but lower competition from established blogs.

## Output
Format as:

**Keyword Group Name**

*Medium-tail*
- keyword
- keyword
- keyword

*Long-tail*
- keyword
- keyword
- keyword

(Repeat structure for each group)

Do not use quotation marks around keywords. Use Markdown formatting.
```
