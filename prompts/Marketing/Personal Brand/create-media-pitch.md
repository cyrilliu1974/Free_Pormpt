# Media Pitch Generator for Journalists and Editors

## 簡介

The Media Pitch Generator for Journalists and Editors is a free AI prompt that produces publication-ready pitches tailored to specific outlets and their readerships. This media pitch prompt for ChatGPT helps you craft pitches that lead with newsworthy angles, open with hooks tied to current trends or reader pain points, and include supporting evidence like data or case studies. It structures your pitch into a compelling subject line (under 60 characters), an opening paragraph that establishes relevance, 2-3 key bullet points, supporting evidence, and a clear call-to-action. The prompt adapts tone to match each publication - whether conversational, technical, or formal - and works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. You input your product/service/company, target publication and readership, and unique selling proposition, and the prompt generates a complete pitch ready to send. Ideal for PR professionals, founders, marketing teams, and communications specialists who need to secure media coverage quickly and professionally. ● Leads with the most newsworthy angle aligned to the publication's editorial focus ● Structures pitches with hook, evidence, tone-matching, and actionable next steps ● Delivers complete output including subject line, opening, key points, supporting data, and call-to-action ● Adapts voice and formality to match target publication style while maintaining authenticity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert media relations specialist crafting a pitch for journalists and editors.

## Task
Write a persuasive media pitch that captures attention and communicates value. Structure your writing for clarity and logical flow, ensuring each sentence builds naturally on the previous one.

## Context
**Pitch subject:** {{product-service-company}}

**Target publication & audience:** {{publication-and-readership}}

**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

## Approach
1. Lead with the most newsworthy angle that aligns with the publication's editorial focus
2. Open with a hook that connects your story to current trends, reader pain points, or timely developments
3. Present evidence: data, case studies, expert quotes, or outcomes that validate your claims
4. Match the publication's tone—whether conversational, technical, or formal—while staying authentic
5. Close with a specific, low-friction next step for the journalist

## Output
Deliver the pitch in this structure:

**Subject Line**
(Compelling, specific, under 60 characters)

**Opening Paragraph**
(Hook + why this matters now + relevance to their readers)

**Key Points**
(2-3 bullet points with the strongest supporting details)

**Supporting Evidence**
(Concrete data, testimonials, or outcomes)

**Call-to-Action**
(Clear offer: interview availability, exclusive access, review samples, etc.)
```
