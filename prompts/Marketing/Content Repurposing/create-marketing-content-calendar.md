# Marketing Content Calendar Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Marketing Content Calendar Generator is a free AI prompt that builds strategic content calendars for marketers, agencies, and brand managers looking to organize multi-channel campaigns. This marketing content calendar prompt for ChatGPT takes your marketing goals, target audience data, content strategy, and KPIs and produces a structured table mapping out when, where, and what to publish. It balances content themes and formats across channels, aligns posts with holidays and product launches, and assigns measurable performance metrics to each entry. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and delivers both the calendar table and a strategic rationale explaining how the schedule aligns with audience behavior and platform best practices. Marketing teams use it to replace spreadsheet guesswork with a data-informed publishing roadmap that accounts for audience segments, posting frequency, and seasonal events. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable system to plan weeks or months of content in one session, or when coordinating campaigns across email, social, blog, and paid channels. ● Produces a multi-column calendar table with date, theme, format, channel, audience segment, key events, and KPIs ● Ensures content variety by rotating themes and formats to prevent audience fatigue ● Aligns posts with platform-specific timing and audience activity patterns for maximum reach ● Includes strategic commentary explaining how the calendar supports marketing goals and audience preferences ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing strategist specializing in content planning and scheduling for optimal engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content calendar that strategically plans and schedules marketing communications across channels. Balance content themes, formats, key dates, and audience preferences to maximize impact.

## Context
Use the following information to build the calendar:

{{marketing-goals}}

{{target-audience}} (include demographics, psychographics, pain points, and content preferences)

{{content-strategy}} (specify themes, formats, distribution channels, posting frequency, and key dates such as holidays, events, or product launches)

{{kpi}} (define metrics to track content performance)

## Output
Deliver the content calendar as a structured table with these columns:
- Date
- Content Theme
- Content Format
- Distribution Channel(s)
- Target Audience Segment
- Key Dates/Events/Launches
- KPIs and Metrics

Follow the table with a brief explanation of how the calendar aligns with the marketing goals, audience preferences, and channel best practices. Ensure the calendar:
- Incorporates a diverse mix of content formats and themes
- Optimizes scheduling based on platform-specific best practices and audience activity patterns
- Includes placeholders for timely, relevant content tied to key dates
- Defines clear performance tracking to enable iteration
```
