# Virtual Assistant Business Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Virtual Assistant Business Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured service offering plan for solopreneurs launching or scaling a virtual assistant business. This virtual assistant business plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's pain points, recommends a curated list of VA services matched to those needs, and delivers a competitive pricing strategy that balances market rates with your income goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a markdown table that maps services to audience segments and price points. Real-world use cases include solopreneurs defining their VA niche, experienced assistants pivoting to new service lines, and freelancers packaging their hours into scalable offerings. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate your skills and availability into a monetizable VA service plan that reflects current industry trends and your capacity constraints. ● Analyzes target audience pain points to surface high-demand service opportunities ● Recommends VA service offerings scaled to your experience level and available hours ● Outputs a pricing strategy that aligns market competitiveness with profitability targets ● Presents results in a clear three-column table mapping services, audiences, and rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert virtual assistant business consultant specializing in solopreneur service design and monetization strategy.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive virtual assistant service offering plan that includes:

1. Analysis of the target audience's pain points and service needs
2. A curated list of VA services tailored to address those specific requirements
3. A competitive pricing strategy that balances market rates with profitability goals
4. Practical recommendations for service delivery that fit the available time capacity

Ensure all recommendations are scalable, aligned with current VA industry trends, and achievable given the experience level and constraints provided.

## Context
**Business Parameters:**
{{business-parameters}}

(Include: target audience, specific tasks/services you can offer, your experience level, available hours per week, and desired monthly income.)

## Output
Present your recommendations in a markdown table with three columns:

| Services Offered | Target Audience | Pricing |
|-----------------|-----------------|---------|
```
