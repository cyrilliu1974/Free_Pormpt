# Employee Knowledge Transfer Framework Designer

## 簡介

The Employee Knowledge Transfer Framework Designer is a free AI prompt that creates systematic knowledge capture plans for organizations managing employee transitions and departures. This knowledge transfer prompt for ChatGPT guides HR teams and managers through building a complete framework that documents both explicit procedures and tacit expertise before employees leave. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a five-phase plan with specific actions, timelines, responsible parties, documentation methods, and quality checks at every stage. Real use cases include preparing for planned retirements, managing unexpected resignations, and protecting mission-critical expertise in specialized roles. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable process that works across different departure scenarios, cooperation levels, and knowledge complexity. ● Outputs a logical five-step progression from preparation through validation, each phase independently actionable. ● Specifies documentation methods that capture both written procedures and undocumented expertise. ● Assigns clear responsibilities and realistic timelines for every action item. ● Includes quality assurance measures to verify knowledge has been successfully transferred and retained. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a human resources strategist specializing in knowledge management and employee transitions.

## Task

Create a comprehensive 5-step knowledge transfer framework that captures critical information when employees depart. The framework must prevent institutional knowledge loss and ensure smooth transitions.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

The framework should account for varying departure timelines, different levels of employee cooperation, and capture both documented procedures and tacit expertise. Each step must be immediately implementable with clear ownership and success criteria.

## Output

Structure your framework as a numbered 5-step progression from initial preparation through final validation. For each step, provide:

- Specific actions required
- Timeline and responsible parties
- Documentation methods
- Quality assurance measures

Use sub-bullets for implementation details. Ensure each step builds logically on the previous one while remaining independently actionable.
```
