# Architectural Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Architectural Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed wide-angle building photography concepts for designers, architects, and visual storytellers. This architectural photography prompt for Midjourney produces editorial-quality images featuring buildings captured at golden hour with wet pavement reflections, pedestrian silhouettes in puddles, and precise camera specifications including focal length, aperture, and ISO settings. You customize three variables - architectural style (modernist, brutalist, art deco), artist reference (Julius Shulman, Iwan Baan, Hélène Binet), and facade material (glass, concrete, terracotta) - and the prompt generates images with professional composition techniques like leading lines, negative space, and eye-level perspective. It works across Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models.
