# Career Development Roadmap Builder for Lifelong Learning

## 簡介

The Career Development Roadmap Builder for Lifelong Learning is a free AI prompt that creates actionable, phase-by-phase improvement plans tailored to your professional field and career stage. This career development prompt for ChatGPT analyzes industry trends, emerging technologies, and skill gaps to produce a customizable markdown table outlining growth objectives, timelines, and concrete learning resources. It balances formal education with certifications, mentorship, workshops, and self-directed study, mapping everything from foundational competencies through leadership development. Built for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, the prompt adapts to any professional field and structures output in a flexible table format you define. Use it when planning credential sequences, responding to industry shifts, or designing a multi-year learning agenda that evolves with your career. ● Produces a markdown table with custom column structure matching your planning needs (stages, goals, timelines, resources, or any format you specify). ● Identifies skill gaps, certifications, and learning modalities appropriate to your field and current career level. ● Balances short-term tactical wins with long-term strategic objectives, from foundational skills to executive capabilities. ● Remains adaptable to industry evolution, personal circumstances, and emerging technology trends in your professional domain. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career development specialist creating a comprehensive career-long improvement plan that maps continuous education and lifelong learning opportunities.

## Task
Develop a structured career development roadmap for {{professional-field}} at the {{career-stage}} level. Analyze key growth areas, industry trends, and emerging technologies relevant to this field. Create a plan that balances short-term milestones with long-term objectives, incorporating diverse learning methods: formal education, professional certifications, workshops, mentorship programs, and self-directed learning. Ensure the plan is adaptable as the industry evolves.

## Context
The plan should address multiple career stages, identify specific skill gaps and opportunities, recommend concrete resources and timelines, and remain flexible enough to accommodate industry shifts and personal circumstances.

## Output
Present the career development plan as a markdown table with {{table-structure}} (format: "number|Column Name 1, Column Name 2, Column Name 3" - e.g., "4|Stage, Goals, Timeline, Resources").

Ensure each row contains specific, actionable items mapped to distinct phases of professional growth. Cover foundational skills, intermediate advancement, specialization, and leadership development as appropriate to the field and career stage.
```
