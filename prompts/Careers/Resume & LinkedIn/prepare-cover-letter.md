# Cover Letter Writer for Grant Writing Positions

## 簡介

The Cover Letter Writer for Grant Writing Positions is a free AI prompt that produces mission-aligned cover letters for nonprofit fundraising and grant roles. This cover letter prompt for ChatGPT takes your candidate profile and target organization details, then builds a structured letter that connects your background to core grant writing competencies - proposal development, funder relations, budget creation, and mission advancement. Whether you're applying to foundations, nonprofits, or development offices, the prompt organizes your narrative using dependency grammar principles so every sentence flows logically from a main clause into supporting details, ensuring clarity and professional coherence. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to position yourself for roles that require demonstrated success in securing funding, understanding nonprofit operations, and communicating persuasively to stakeholders. ● Structures an opening that introduces the candidate and expresses genuine interest in the specific grant writing role ● Builds 2-3 body paragraphs that showcase relevant experience in needs assessment, proposal writing, budget development, funder relations, and reporting ● Demonstrates understanding of the target organization's mission and articulates how the candidate will advance it ● Closes with a confident call to action that reinforces enthusiasm and readiness to contribute ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert cover letter writer specializing in nonprofit and grant-focused positions.

## Task
Write a compelling cover letter for a grant writing position that demonstrates relevant experience, skills, and alignment with the organization's mission. Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles to ensure each clause builds logically on the previous one, creating clear relationships between ideas and smooth information flow.

## Context
Grant writing positions require demonstrated ability to secure funding, understand nonprofit operations, and communicate persuasively. The cover letter must connect the candidate's background directly to the core competencies of grant writing: research, proposal development, relationship management, and mission alignment.

**Candidate profile:**
{{candidate-profile}}

**Target organization and mission:**
{{organization-and-mission}}

## Output
Deliver a complete cover letter in structured paragraph format:
- Opening paragraph: introduce the candidate and express specific interest in the position
- Body paragraphs (2-3): showcase relevant experience, key skills, and notable achievements, explicitly connecting each to grant writing requirements (needs assessment, proposal writing, budget development, funder relations, reporting)
- Closing paragraph: demonstrate understanding of the organization's mission and articulate how the candidate will advance it, ending with a confident call to action

Maintain dependency grammar structure throughout—each sentence should flow from a clear main clause with modifying elements that create logical, hierarchical relationships between ideas.
```
