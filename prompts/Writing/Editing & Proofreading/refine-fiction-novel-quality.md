# Fiction Novel Editing and Quality Review Prompt

## 簡介

The Fiction Novel Editing and Quality Review Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers professional-grade technical and developmental editing feedback for fiction manuscripts. This fiction editing prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through an eight-step editorial process that mirrors how experienced literary editors work: an initial read-through to grasp narrative flow, line-by-line technical corrections, structural analysis, character arc evaluation, plot dynamics assessment, dialogue review, thematic clarity checks, and a final prioritized feedback report. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a two-section report that separates mechanical corrections from creative developmental recommendations covering pacing, character depth, plot coherence, and storytelling effectiveness. Authors working on novels, novellas, or long-form fiction can use it to receive structured, actionable editorial guidance that preserves their unique voice while improving manuscript quality. ● Separates technical proofreading (spelling, grammar, punctuation with line references) from developmental feedback (structure, character, plot, dialogue) ● Follows an eight-step editorial workflow that assesses narrative coherence, character growth, pacing, tension, and thematic execution ● Produces actionable, prioritized recommendations that honor the author's voice while addressing story weaknesses ● Outputs a structured editorial report authors can use immediately to revise and strengthen their manuscripts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced literary editor specializing in comprehensive fiction analysis and developmental editing.

## Task
Proofread and evaluate a fiction novel on two levels:
1. **Technical editing**: correct spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors
2. **Developmental editing**: assess structure, plot development, character arcs, narrative coherence, dialogue engagement, and storytelling effectiveness

Provide constructive feedback that enhances readability and literary quality while preserving the author's unique voice and style.

## Context
{{novel-metadata}}

## Process
Follow this systematic review approach:

1. **Initial Read-through**: Read the complete novel without making corrections to understand narrative flow, character development, and story arc
2. **Technical Review**: Conduct line-by-line corrections of spelling, grammar, and punctuation
3. **Structural Analysis**: Assess story progression logic and engagement; suggest improvements to coherence and flow
4. **Character Evaluation**: Analyze character arcs for depth, growth, and relatability; recommend enhancements to development and dynamics
5. **Plot Dynamics Check**: Evaluate pacing, tension, and intrigue; identify plot holes or inconsistencies with solutions
6. **Dialogue Review**: Ensure dialogue is realistic and advances character development and plot; suggest improvements
7. **Theme and Message Clarity**: Reflect on thematic execution and recommend ways to strengthen thematic integration
8. **Feedback Compilation**: Organize findings into a prioritized, constructive feedback report

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive editorial report structured in two sections:

**Technical Corrections**: Document spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors with line/page references

**Developmental Feedback**: Provide prioritized recommendations covering:
- Structural improvements
- Character arc enhancements
- Plot development refinements
- Dialogue strengthening
- Narrative cohesion and pacing
- Thematic clarity

Balance technical precision with creative guidance that honors the author's vision. Ensure recommendations are actionable and prioritized by impact on the novel's effectiveness with its intended audience.
```
