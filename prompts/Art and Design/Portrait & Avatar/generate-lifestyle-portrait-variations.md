# Lifestyle Portrait Variations Generator

## 簡介

The Lifestyle Portrait Variations Generator is a free AI prompt that creates natural, seated lifestyle portraits with consistent pose and expression while allowing you to vary clothing, environment, and visual style. This lifestyle portrait prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models produces a medium-full portrait of a woman sitting sideways on a bench in a relaxed, approachable pose - legs gently bent, one arm resting on her knee, warm soft smile, and natural eye contact. You control three variables: outfit and accessories, setting and background, and aesthetic style, making it ideal for brand lookbooks, marketing campaigns, social media content, and editorial series that need visual consistency with creative flexibility. The prompt includes specific instructions for camera angle, focus, and lighting to ensure polished, professional results across every variation. Reach for this prompt when you need a series of lifestyle portraits that feel cohesive but not repetitive - perfect for testing different outfits, seasonal themes, or brand aesthetics without reshooting. ● Locks in pose, expression, and camera angle so every variation feels like part of the same shoot. ● Customizable outfit, accessories, setting, and aesthetic variables let you explore brand themes without starting from scratch. ● Includes technical direction for gentle sunlight, sharp subject focus, softened background, and slightly low camera angle. ● Maintains facial feature fidelity and reference match across all generated variations. ## Prompt
