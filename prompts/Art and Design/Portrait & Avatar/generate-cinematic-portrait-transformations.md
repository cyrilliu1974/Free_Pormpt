# Cinematic Portrait Transformation Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Portrait Transformation Generator is a free AI prompt that converts uploaded photos into Hollywood-quality urban portraits with dramatic lighting and atmospheric realism. This cinematic portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates 8K photo-realistic images featuring subjects seated on benches, captured from low-angle perspectives with intentional depth-of-field blur, wet pavement reflections, neon-lit backgrounds, and bokeh effects from passing traffic. You customize the subject styling, urban setting details, and color grading to match your creative vision, producing magazine-quality portraits with moody cityscapes, atmospheric mist, and layered environmental depth. Content creators use it for social media profiles, photographers apply it to portfolio work, and brands deploy it for visual marketing campaigns that demand cinematic production value without location shoots or professional lighting rigs. ● Transforms uploaded photos into 8K cinematic portraits with dramatic low-angle framing and sharp subject focus against intentionally blurred foregrounds. ● Customizable urban environments featuring wet pavement reflections, vibrant neon lighting, atmospheric mist, and bokeh light trails from passing vehicles. ● Adjustable subject styling, setting details, and color grading parameters to match specific creative briefs or brand aesthetics. ● Produces photo-realistic atmospheric depth with layered environmental elements, warm streetlight glow, and high-contrast lighting that mimics professional cinema photography. ## Prompt

```
Cinematic, photo-realistic 8K portrait of the uploaded person sitting on a bench, captured from a dramatic low-angle perspective. {{subject-styling}}

Scene: {{urban-setting}} with wet pavement reflecting vibrant neon lights, atmospheric mist, bokeh trails from passing cars in the background. Warm streetlights and ambient neon glow create reflections in puddles.

Camera: Sharp focus on subject with intentionally blurred foreground, cinematic depth of field, high contrast, {{color-grading}}. Atmospheric realism with layered depth.
```
