# Trust Building Exercises for Managers and Teams

## 簡介

The Trust Building Exercises for Managers and Teams is a free AI prompt that creates structured activities to strengthen professional relationships and team cohesion between managers and their direct reports. The prompt produces seven exercises that progress from basic transparency to deeper vulnerability and mutual accountability, each with clear objectives, implementation steps, timelines, expected outcomes, success metrics, and adaptations for different team sizes and work environments. This trust building exercises prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok addresses real workplace challenges including communication gaps, unclear expectations, fear of judgment, and power imbalances. It is designed for managers, HR professionals, team leads, and organizational development specialists who need practical, implementable activities that create psychological safety while respecting the unique dynamics between supervisors and their teams. ● Creates seven exercises structured to build from foundational transparency to advanced mutual accountability ● Includes specific implementation steps, timelines, success metrics, and expected behavioral changes for each activity ● Adapts exercises for remote teams, varying team sizes, different organizational cultures, and diverse personality types ● Addresses the power dynamics inherent in manager-employee relationships while fostering genuine psychological safety ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert organizational psychologist and leadership development specialist with deep expertise in workplace dynamics and trust-building mechanisms in high-performance teams.

## Task
Design seven comprehensive trust-building exercises specifically for managers and their direct reports. Each exercise must address real workplace challenges, create measurable improvements in team cohesion, and account for the power dynamics and communication barriers that exist between managers and their teams.

## Context
{{team-context}}

The exercises should:
- Progress from basic transparency to deeper vulnerability and mutual accountability
- Address common trust barriers: communication gaps, unclear expectations, fear of judgment, and power imbalances
- Be practical and implementable within typical workplace constraints
- Create psychological safety while strengthening professional relationships
- Adapt to different team sizes, work environments (including remote), organizational cultures, and personality types

## Output
For each of the seven exercises, provide:

**Exercise Title**
- **Objective**: What trust dimension this builds
- **Implementation Steps**: Clear, numbered instructions
- **Timeline**: How long the exercise takes
- **Expected Outcomes**: Specific behavioral or relational changes
- **Success Metrics**: How to measure effectiveness
- **Adaptations**: Notes for varying team sizes, environments, or trust levels

Structure the exercises to build progressively, starting with foundational transparency activities and advancing toward deeper vulnerability and mutual accountability.
```
