# Cinematic Still Image Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Still Image Generator is a free AI prompt that produces film-quality stills with professional camera emulation, dramatic lighting, and rich textures for photographers, designers, and visual storytellers. This cinematic still prompt for Midjourney works by specifying anamorphic lens characteristics, three-point lighting with controlled shadows, tactile surface detail, moody color grading with deep blacks and selective desaturation, and balanced geometric composition in a 16:9 aspect ratio. You provide a subject and setting, and the prompt renders stills that look like freeze-frames from a feature film - ideal for concept art, mood boards, storyboards, and portfolio pieces. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation platforms. Reach for this prompt whenever you need production-quality visuals without a camera crew or when you want to test framing and lighting concepts before a live shoot. ● Emulates high-end cinema cameras with anamorphic lens characteristics and raw-style rendering for authentic film texture. ● Applies dramatic three-point lighting and controlled shadow work that mimics professional set lighting. ● Delivers moody color grading with deep blacks, selective desaturation, and rich tactile surface detail. ● Composes images with strong leading lines and balanced geometry in a widescreen 16:9 aspect ratio. ## Prompt

```
Cinematic still of {{subject}} in {{setting}}, emulate a high-end cinema camera with anamorphic characteristics, dramatic three-point lighting with controlled shadows, rich tactile textures and fine surface detail, moody color grading with deep blacks and selective desaturation, strong leading lines and balanced geometric composition --ar 16:9 --style raw
```
