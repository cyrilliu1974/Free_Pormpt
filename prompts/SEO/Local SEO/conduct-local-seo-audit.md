# Local SEO Audit Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Local SEO Audit Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that analyzes a business's local search performance and delivers a detailed audit report with prioritized recommendations for improvement. This local SEO audit prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to perform a systematic evaluation across eight key areas: current local search rankings for relevant keywords, technical SEO health (mobile-friendliness, page speed, HTTPS, sitemaps), on-page optimization (title tags, meta descriptions, schema markup, NAP consistency), local listing completeness across Google Business Profile and other directories, and specific link-building opportunities within the target market. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only basic business details - website URL, location, and target market - to generate a complete audit with checkmarks for implemented features, tables for ranking data, and expected results across 30/60/90-day timeframes. It transforms a complex multi-hour audit into a structured analysis that prioritizes fixes by impact and implementation difficulty. This prompt is for local business owners, digital marketing agencies, SEO consultants, and in-house marketers who need to assess and improve a business's visibility in local search results and Google Maps. ● Evaluates technical foundations including mobile design, page speed, HTTPS, XML sitemaps, and robots.txt with clear pass/fail markers ● Audits on-page elements like location-optimized title tags, local schema markup, NAP consistency, and embedded maps ● Checks completeness of critical local listings - Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, and industry directories ● Identifies 5-8 specific local link-building opportunities such as chambers of commerce, local news outlets, sponsorships, and community partnerships ● Prioritizes the top three action items by impact, difficulty, and timeline, with measurable outcome projections for 30/60/90-day periods ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced local SEO analyst conducting a comprehensive audit to improve local search visibility and performance.

## Task
Analyze the business website's current local search rankings, identify technical and on-page issues, evaluate local listings, and provide specific, actionable recommendations prioritized by impact.

## Context
**Business Information:**
{{business-details}}

(Include: website URL, business name, physical location, target geographic market, and primary business category)

## Output
Deliver a structured local SEO audit report with these sections:

### 1. Website Overview
Summarize the business name, location, website URL, and target market.

### 2. Current Local Search Rankings
Create a table with columns:
- Keyword
- Current Rank
- Search Volume

List rankings for relevant local keywords (e.g., "plumber downtown Seattle", "best pizza Brooklyn").

### 3. Technical SEO Audit
Evaluate and mark each item with ✅ (implemented) or ❌ (issue found):
- Mobile-friendly design
- Page speed (Core Web Vitals)
- HTTPS implementation
- XML sitemap presence
- Robots.txt configuration

### 4. On-Page SEO Audit
Evaluate and mark each with ✅ or ❌:
- Unique, location-optimized title tags
- Compelling meta descriptions with local keywords
- Proper header tag hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)
- Keyword-rich, locally relevant content
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
- Embedded Google Map
- Local schema markup

### 5. Local Listing Audit
Check presence and completeness, mark with ✅ (active/complete) or ❌ (missing/incomplete):
- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places for Business
- Apple Maps
- Yelp
- Facebook Business Page
- Industry-specific directories

### 6. Link Building Opportunities
List 5-8 specific local link building opportunities such as:
- Local business associations
- Chamber of Commerce
- Sponsorship opportunities
- Local news outlets
- Community event partnerships
- Supplier/partner websites

### 7. Key Action Items
Prioritize the top 3 action items based on:
- Impact on local search visibility
- Implementation difficulty
- Expected timeline to results

### 8. Expected Results
Describe anticipated improvements in:
- Local search rankings (keyword movement)
- Organic traffic (percentage increase)
- Online visibility (impressions, map pack appearances)
- Foot traffic and sales conversion

Provide timeframes (30/60/90 days) where relevant. Focus on measurable outcomes tied to the business's local market.
```
