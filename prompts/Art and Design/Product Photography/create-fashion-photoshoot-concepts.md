# Fashion Photoshoot Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Fashion Photoshoot Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-fashion editorial images of models with designer bags in coastal settings for photographers, creative directors, and fashion marketers. This fashion photoshoot prompt for Midjourney produces editorial-quality compositions with controlled lighting, style references, and aspect ratios tailored for lookbooks, campaigns, and social media. You specify the bag style variable (tote, crossbody, clutch, etc.) and the prompt renders a 3:4 vertical image using Midjourney's style reference system (sref 217014387) with elevated stylization and controlled chaos parameters. Fashion brands use it to visualize product photography concepts before physical shoots, while content creators generate mood boards and campaign mockups quickly. Reach for this prompt when you need polished, on-brand fashion imagery that combines product focus with environmental storytelling. ● Specify any bag style (leather tote, mini crossbody, woven clutch) and get a cohesive coastal fashion scene in seconds. ● Built-in style reference (sref) and stylization parameters ensure consistent, magazine-quality aesthetic across multiple generations. ● 3:4 aspect ratio optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, and vertical digital displays. ● Chaos 30 setting introduces natural variation while maintaining the editorial look and lighting quality. ## Prompt

```
Fashion photoshoot of a woman carrying a stylish {{bag-style}} bag at the coast, editorial lighting, high-fashion composition --chaos 30 --ar 3:4 --sref 217014387 --sw 400 --stylize 500
```
