# Product Photography Workflow Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Product Photography Workflow Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, phase-by-phase shooting plans for commercial product photographers and marketing teams. This product photography prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete workflow tailored to your specific product and usage context. It outputs a three-phase guide covering pre-production setup (concept development, equipment lists, prop selection, shot lists), on-set shooting (lighting arrangements, composition angles, styling choices, camera settings), and post-production (editing sequences, color grading, retouching steps, multi-platform formatting). Whether you're photographing food, electronics, apparel, or cosmetics, the prompt adapts to your brand aesthetic and marketing objectives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured photography plan that translates product features into compelling visuals, or when training junior photographers on commercial workflow standards. ● Breaks down the entire photography process into numbered, actionable steps across three production phases ● Addresses lighting setup, composition techniques, and styling decisions specific to your product category ● Includes post-production guidance for editing, color correction, retouching, and formatting for different marketing channels ● Adapts recommendations based on your product details, brand context, and intended usage ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert stock photographer specializing in commercial product photography.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step guide for producing professional product photographs that highlight key features and appeal to the target market. Cover the complete workflow from pre-production planning through shooting to post-production.

## Context
Product and brand details:
{{product-and-brand-context}}

Intended use:
{{usage-context}}

## Output
Deliver a detailed, numbered guide organized into three phases:

1. **Pre-Production** – planning, concept development, equipment preparation, prop selection, and shot list creation
2. **Shooting** – lighting setup, composition techniques, styling decisions, camera settings, and capturing multiple angles that emphasize product features
3. **Post-Production** – image selection, editing workflow, color correction, retouching, formatting for different platforms, and quality control

For each phase, provide specific, actionable steps that ensure the final images are versatile, high-quality, and aligned with the brand aesthetic and marketing goals.
```
