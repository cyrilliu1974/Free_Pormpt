# Article Headline Generator With Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The Article Headline Generator With Dependency Grammar is a free AI prompt that creates five unique headline options with supporting subheadings for any article topic, each crafted using linguistic dependency grammar principles to maximize clarity and reader engagement. This article headline prompt for ChatGPT applies dependency grammar - a linguistic framework that organizes words around their syntactic relationships - to produce concise, scannable titles where modifiers and clauses clearly attach to their head words. Each generated headline explores a different angle or appeals to a different reader segment, accompanied by two subheadings that elaborate on distinct facets of the main idea. Writers, content marketers, and editors use it to quickly explore multiple headline approaches while maintaining grammatical clarity and avoiding clickbait. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to test multiple headline angles for the same article topic, or when you want structurally sound titles that enhance comprehension and flow rather than relying on sensationalism. ● Produces five headline variations per topic, each targeting a different angle or audience segment ● Includes two supporting subheadings per headline to flesh out key facets and drive reader interest ● Applies dependency grammar structure to ensure syntactic clarity and immediate comprehension ● Discourages clickbait by requiring headlines to accurately represent the article content ## Prompt
