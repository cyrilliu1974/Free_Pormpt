# Small Business HR Risk Audit Prompt

## 簡介

The Small Business HR Risk Audit Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates a practical, step-by-step internal audit framework for small businesses to identify and mitigate HR compliance risks without hiring consultants or building dedicated HR departments. This HR risk audit prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok is built on the perspective of a former employment attorney who defended small businesses for a decade. It produces a 5-step framework where each step takes 2-4 hours to complete, focuses on the highest-risk areas that trigger lawsuits or regulatory penalties, and can be executed by business owners or office managers without specialized HR training. The framework prioritizes federal compliance basics, identifies critical red flags, specifies which documents to gather, and clearly flags when professional legal help becomes necessary. Small businesses transitioning from informal practices to formal compliance systems use it to protect themselves as they scale from 5 to 50+ employees. ● Prioritizes the highest-risk HR compliance areas that lead to lawsuits and regulatory penalties in small business contexts ● Breaks down each audit step with time estimates, specific actions, red flags to identify, and critical documents to gather ● Includes practical examples and clear warning signs indicating when to escalate to an employment attorney ● Designed for non-HR professionals - business owners and office managers - to execute independently in hours, not weeks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a former employment attorney who defended small businesses from lawsuits for a decade and now creates lightweight compliance systems that protect without slowing growth.

## Context
Small businesses face regulatory pressures without dedicated HR departments. Informal practices that worked at 5 employees create liability at 50. They need protection without bureaucracy, risk mitigation without paralysis.

## Task
Create a 5-step internal HR-risk audit framework for small businesses. Before responding, consider: What are the most critical HR risks small businesses face? How can they identify these risks without HR expertise? What practical steps can they implement immediately?

Business context:
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide a step-by-step numbered plan. Each step must:

- Be executable by non-HR professionals (owners, office managers)
- Focus on highest-risk areas that lead to lawsuits or regulatory penalties
- Take no more than 2-4 hours to complete
- Prioritize federal compliance basics before state-specific requirements
- Avoid expensive software or consultants as primary solutions

For each step, include:

- Clear step title
- Specific actions to take
- Red flags to identify
- Time estimate for completion
- Critical documents or evidence to gather
- Practical examples where relevant
- Warning signs that indicate when professional help is absolutely necessary

Use bullet points for sub-items. Provide concrete tools or checklists, not abstract concepts. Write clearly and directly without unnecessary adjectives, adverbs, or corporate jargon.
```
