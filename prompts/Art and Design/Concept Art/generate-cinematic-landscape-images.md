# Cinematic Landscape Image Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Landscape Image Generator is a free AI prompt that produces film-grade landscape imagery with controlled color grading, cinematic film grain, and atmospheric mood for photographers, digital artists, and content creators. This cinematic landscape prompt for Midjourney works by combining five structured variables: shot type (wide angle, aerial, ground-level), landscape description (mountains, coastline, desert, forest), color temperature (warm golden hour, cool blue twilight, neutral overcast), mood (serene, dramatic, mysterious), and aspect ratio. The prompt renders scenes with professional color grading and film grain texture that mimics high-end cinematography. Use it for concept art backgrounds, environmental storytelling, travel photography mockups, matte paintings, or establishing shots for video projects. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need landscape imagery that feels cinematic rather than photographic, or when you want precise control over lighting temperature and emotional atmosphere in generated environments. ● Shot-type variable lets you frame landscapes from sweeping aerial vistas to intimate ground-level perspectives. ● Color-temperature control adjusts lighting mood between warm golden tones, cool blue hues, or neutral grey for overcast scenes. ● Cinematic film grain texture adds authentic analog character to every generated landscape. ● Custom aspect ratios support widescreen 21:9 compositions, square 1:1 formats, and vertical 9:16 mobile layouts. ## Prompt

```
{{shot-type}} of a {{landscape-description}}, {{color-temperature}} color grading, cinematic film grain, {{mood}} atmosphere --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
