# Social Media Policy Guidelines Generator

## 簡介

The Social Media Policy Guidelines Generator is a free AI prompt that creates complete social media policy documents for organizations managing employee and representative social media activity. This social media policy prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured governance framework with five core sections: brand voice standards with on-brand vs. off-brand examples, content guidelines including approval workflows and prohibited topics, engagement best practices with response protocols and moderation rules, crisis management protocols with escalation chains and holding statements, and compliance requirements covering FTC disclosure and data privacy. Companies use it to standardize how teams represent the brand online, reduce legal risk, and prepare response playbooks for negative feedback or PR incidents. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only basic company context as input. This prompt is built for HR leaders, communications directors, social media managers, and legal teams establishing formal social media governance at scale. ● Creates five-section policy documents with brand voice definitions, content rules, engagement standards, crisis protocols, and legal compliance requirements. ● Includes concrete examples of approved vs. prohibited messaging, response templates, and escalation decision trees. ● Structures output with headings, bullet points, numbered action steps, and a quick-reference summary sheet for easy implementation. ● Addresses moderation standards, response time expectations, disclosure requirements, and consequences for policy violations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in organizational policy development and risk management.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media policy document that ensures consistent, appropriate, and brand-aligned use of social media by employees and representatives. The policy must be actionable, covering all critical aspects of professional social media engagement.

## Context
{{company-context}}

Provide the company name, industry, target audience, brand values, and primary social media platforms to be governed by this policy.

## Output
Structure the policy with these required sections:

**1. Brand Voice & Messaging Standards**
- Define the tone, language style, and values that must be reflected
- Provide 3-5 concrete examples of on-brand vs. off-brand messaging
- Specify terminology, hashtags, and visual style requirements

**2. Content Guidelines**
- Approved content types and topics
- Prohibited content (confidential information, competitor mentions, controversial subjects)
- Approval workflows for different content tiers
- Attribution and copyright requirements
- Dos and don'ts with specific examples

**3. Engagement Best Practices**
- Response time expectations and escalation protocols
- Comment moderation standards (what to approve, hide, or report)
- How to handle negative feedback professionally
- Guidelines for likes, shares, and personal account activity related to company business

**4. Crisis Management Protocols**
- Definition of a social media crisis (volume thresholds, sentiment triggers)
- Immediate action steps and decision tree
- Communication chain and authorization levels
- Template holding statements
- Post-crisis review process

**5. Compliance & Legal Considerations**
- Disclosure requirements (FTC, industry-specific regulations)
- Data privacy and customer information protection
- Consequences of policy violations

Format each section with clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and numbered action steps. Include a quick-reference summary sheet at the end.
```
