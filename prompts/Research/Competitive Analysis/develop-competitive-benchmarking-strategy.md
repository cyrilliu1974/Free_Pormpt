# Competitive Benchmarking Strategy Framework

## 簡介

The Competitive Benchmarking Strategy Framework is a free AI prompt that creates a structured competitive analysis guide for any company and industry. This competitive benchmarking prompt for ChatGPT generates a multi-section framework that walks you through identifying direct and indirect competitors, defining financial, operational, and customer metrics with calculation formulas, selecting primary and secondary research methods, performing gap and SWOT analyses in table format, and delivering prioritized strategic recommendations with implementation steps. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing markdown-formatted guides that marketing strategists, product managers, and business analysts use to assess market position, uncover performance gaps, and plan continuous improvement cycles. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable, teachable benchmarking process that goes beyond high-level comparisons and delivers actionable insight with timelines and KPIs. ● Identifies direct and indirect competitors with clear categorization criteria ● Defines financial, operational, and customer benchmarking metrics with calculation formulas ● Compares your company against competitors in structured tables with gap analysis and SWOT positioning ● Delivers prioritized strategic recommendations, implementation steps, expected outcomes, and monitoring cadence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a competitive benchmarking and strategy expert delivering a comprehensive, actionable guide for conducting industry competitive analysis.

## Task
Create a complete competitive benchmarking framework for {{company-name}} operating in {{industry}}. Structure the guide to ensure logical flow and coherence, providing specific methodologies and examples throughout.

## Output
Deliver the guide in markdown format with the following sections:

### 1. Introduction
- Overview of the company, industry context, and key products/services
- Stated objectives of the competitive benchmarking analysis

### 2. Identify Key Competitors
- List of direct competitors (same market, similar offerings)
- List of indirect competitors (adjacent solutions, substitutes)

### 3. Define Benchmarking Metrics
Outline metrics across three dimensions:
- **Financial metrics** (revenue growth, profit margins, market share, ROI)
- **Operational metrics** (efficiency ratios, capacity utilization, turnaround times)
- **Customer metrics** (satisfaction scores, retention rates, NPS, acquisition cost)

Provide definitions and calculation formulas for each metric.

### 4. Data Collection Methods
Describe research approaches:
- **Primary research**: surveys, interviews, mystery shopping, direct observation
- **Secondary research**: annual reports, industry databases, market research firms, trade publications

Assess advantages, limitations, and reliability for each method.

### 5. Benchmarking Analysis
- Comparative analysis presented in tabular format showing the company against competitors across all defined metrics
- Gap analysis identifying where the company lags or leads
- SWOT analysis positioning the company against main competitors

### 6. Strategic Recommendations
For each key finding, provide:
- Specific, prioritized recommendation
- Implementation steps
- Expected outcomes and timeline

### 7. Monitoring and Continuous Improvement
- Recommended benchmarking frequency (quarterly, annual)
- Progress tracking methods and KPIs
- Continuous improvement process: review cycles, feedback incorporation, metric refinement

Use clear headings, bullet points, and tables. Write accessibly, avoiding unnecessary jargon.
```
