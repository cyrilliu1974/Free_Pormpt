# Transparent Cutaway Product Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Transparent Cutaway Product Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates split-view product photography revealing internal mechanisms alongside external surfaces for designers, marketers, and educators. This transparent cutaway product mockup prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces macro photographs where products are split vertically - one half displaying the normal glossy exterior, the other half rendered transparent to expose gears, electronics, and structural details inside. White annotation lines with custom technical labels point to specific components you define, while dramatic cinematic lighting emphasizes textures and depth. Use it to explain how toys, electronics, tools, or consumer goods work without physically disassembling them, or to create eye-catching visuals for Kickstarter campaigns, user manuals, and social media. Reach for this prompt when you need technical storytelling visuals that combine aesthetic appeal with educational clarity, whether you're launching a product, building training materials, or creating portfolio pieces. ● Produces split-view compositions with one half showing normal product finish and the other half transparent to reveal internal mechanics, gears, and components. ● Accepts custom annotation labels and subject descriptions so you can highlight specific features, materials, or technical details relevant to your product. ● Delivers macro photography styling on a wooden table with dramatic cinematic lighting that enhances both surface textures and internal structures. ● Works for toys, gadgets, tools, packaging prototypes, and any physical product where showing the inside story adds value to your marketing or education goals. ## Prompt
