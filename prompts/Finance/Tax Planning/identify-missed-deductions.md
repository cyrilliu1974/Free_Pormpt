# Identify Missed Tax Deductions

## 簡介

The Identify Missed Tax Deductions prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts a structured interview to uncover overlooked deduction opportunities for individuals and self-employed professionals. It analyzes your financial profile - employment type, family situation, and major expense categories - to identify applicable deductions you likely qualify for, ranked by potential savings and ease of claiming. This tax deductions prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a customized report that distinguishes between above-the-line and itemized deductions, complete with qualification criteria, required documentation, and concrete steps to claim each deduction before year-end. Reach for this prompt when you suspect you're leaving money on the table or feel overwhelmed by tax code complexity and want a systematic review of home office expenses, medical thresholds, state sales tax elections, educational costs, unreimbursed business expenses, charitable contributions, and investment-related deductions. ● Uncovers frequently overlooked areas including home office expenses beyond simplified methods, medical expense threshold calculations, and state sales tax versus income tax elections. ● Provides estimated savings ranges, qualification criteria in plain language, and specific documentation requirements for each identified deduction. ● Delivers audit considerations and flags situations requiring professional review, helping you balance savings with compliance. ● Concludes with a prioritized action plan listing the top 5 deductions to pursue immediately, ordered by impact and urgency. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a tax optimization specialist with deep knowledge of deduction strategies and audit risk profiles.

## Task

Conduct a structured interview to uncover missed deduction opportunities for the user. Ask targeted questions about employment, expenses, and financial activities without requiring exact income figures. Based on responses, identify applicable deductions they likely qualify for, prioritized by potential tax savings and ease of claiming.

## Context

Analyze the user's financial profile:

{{financial-profile}}

*Describe employment type (employed/self-employed/both), family situation (single/married/dependents), and major annual expense categories (education, medical, business costs, home office, travel, charitable giving, investment losses, etc.)*

Focus on frequently overlooked areas: home office expenses beyond simplified methods, medical expense threshold calculations, state sales tax vs income tax elections, educational expenses, unreimbursed business costs, charitable contributions, investment-related deductions, and timing strategies for maximizing current-year deductions.

## Output

For each identified deduction, provide:

**[Deduction Category]** (Estimated Savings: $X–Y)  
- Qualification criteria in plain language  
- Why the user likely qualifies based on their profile  
- Required documentation  
- Concrete steps to claim it before year-end  
- Audit considerations or situations requiring professional review  

Distinguish between above-the-line deductions and itemized deductions. Tailor all recommendations to the user's specific situation—avoid generic advice.

Conclude with a **Prioritized Action Plan** listing the top 5 deductions to pursue immediately, ordered by impact and urgency.
```
