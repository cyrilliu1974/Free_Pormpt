# Lead Nurturing Campaign Builder for Sales Funnels

## 簡介

The Lead Nurturing Campaign Builder for Sales Funnels is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive multi-channel nurturing strategies tailored to each stage of your sales funnel for marketing teams and strategists. This lead nurturing prompt for ChatGPT helps you design campaigns that address prospect needs at awareness, consideration, and decision stages. It generates email tactics with subject lines and cadence recommendations, stage-appropriate content offers, targeted advertising strategies, and success metrics for each funnel phase. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured campaign framework you can customize to your industry, target audience, product, and current conversion benchmarks. Marketing teams use it to map out touchpoint sequences, define personalization rules, and identify conversion triggers that move leads through the funnel. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete blueprint for nurturing leads across multiple channels, or when existing campaigns lack stage-specific messaging and clear handoff logic between funnel phases. ● Generates stage-specific goals, lead characteristics, and pain points for awareness, consideration, and decision phases ● Provides email campaign tactics including subject line ideas, messaging angles, and recommended send cadence for each stage ● Recommends content offers and targeted ad strategies matched to prospect readiness and information needs ● Defines segmentation criteria, personalization rules, channel integration points, and timeline structure across the entire campaign ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in lead nurturing and funnel optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive multi-stage lead nurturing campaign that guides prospects from awareness through consideration to purchase decision. Create a multi-channel strategy incorporating personalized emails, content offers, and targeted advertising tailored to leads' needs and pain points at each funnel stage.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Product/service: {{product-service}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}
Current conversion rate: {{current-conversion-rate}}

## Output
Structure your campaign strategy with:

**For each sales funnel stage (Awareness, Consideration, Decision):**
- Stage-specific goals and lead characteristics
- Email campaign tactics (subject lines, messaging angles, cadence)
- Content offers appropriate to that stage
- Targeted ad strategies and channels
- Key pain points to address
- Success metrics and conversion triggers to the next stage

**Cross-stage elements:**
- Segmentation criteria
- Personalization approach
- Channel integration and handoff points
- Timeline and touchpoint frequency

Present the strategy in clear sections with actionable bullet points under each heading.
```
