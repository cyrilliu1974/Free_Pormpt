# Scrum Meeting Facilitation Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Scrum Meeting Facilitation Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured facilitation guidelines for all four core Scrum events tailored to your team's context and sprint cadence. This Scrum meeting prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a complete set of facilitation guides covering Daily Standup, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective. Each guide includes a clear objective, time-box duration, detailed agenda, proven facilitation techniques to maintain focus and encourage participation, and an action items template for capturing decisions and next steps. The output is customized to your project name, team composition, sprint duration, and the team's Scrum maturity level, ensuring the guidelines align with Agile principles while remaining practical for your specific context. Teams use it to standardize their Scrum ceremonies, onboard new Scrum Masters, or refine existing meeting structures. This prompt is ideal for Scrum Masters setting up ceremonies for new teams, project managers transitioning to Agile, or experienced facilitators looking to refresh their meeting frameworks. ● Creates separate facilitation guides for Daily Standup, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective with event-appropriate time-boxes ● Includes facilitation techniques that keep meetings focused and ensure active participation from all team members ● Provides structured action item templates to capture decisions, commitments, and next steps for each ceremony ● Adapts to your team size, sprint duration, and Scrum maturity level while maintaining alignment with the Scrum framework ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Scrum Master facilitating Scrum ceremonies for a development team.

## Task
Create comprehensive meeting facilitation guidelines for all four core Scrum events: Daily Standup, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective. Each event guide should include agenda, time-box, facilitation techniques, and structured action items.

## Context
**Project:** {{project-name}}
**Team composition:** {{team-context}}
**Sprint cadence:** {{sprint-duration}}

Ensure guidelines:
- Keep teams focused and encourage active participation from all members
- Include time-box limits appropriate to the event and team size
- Align with Agile principles and Scrum framework standards
- Are tailored to the team's Scrum maturity level

## Output
Provide a structured facilitation guide organized as:

### [Event Name]
- **Time-box:** [duration]
- **Objective:** [primary goal]
- **Agenda:**
  - [Item 1]
  - [Item 2]
- **Facilitation techniques:** [how to keep focused and inclusive]
- **Action items template:** [format for capturing decisions and next steps]

Repeat this structure for each of the four Scrum events.
```
