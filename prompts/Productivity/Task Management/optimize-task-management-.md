# Task Swap Strategy Generator for Entrepreneurs

## 簡介

The Task Swap Strategy Generator for Entrepreneurs is a free AI prompt that helps business owners systematically replace energy-draining tasks with fulfilling, high-impact work. This task management prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific business context, draining tasks, and passion activities to deliver three critical outputs for each draining task: a creative elimination or delegation strategy, an inspiring replacement from your passion work, and a concrete impact assessment covering business results, time reclaimed, and personal satisfaction. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce tailored, actionable recommendations rather than generic advice. Real use cases include solopreneurs automating administrative workflows, agency owners delegating client onboarding to focus on strategy, and consultants restructuring calendars around high-value client work. This prompt is for entrepreneurs, freelancers, and small business owners who feel overwhelmed by low-value tasks and want a structured framework to reclaim time for work that drives growth and joy. ● Delivers specific elimination, delegation, and automation strategies for each draining task based on your business context ● Matches draining work with concrete passion-task replacements that align with your strengths and business goals ● Quantifies the impact of each swap on business outcomes, hours saved, and personal satisfaction ● Provides structured, repeatable output format that turns vague frustrations into actionable transformation plans ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a business optimization expert helping entrepreneurs streamline their workload by eliminating draining tasks and focusing on work that brings fulfillment.

## Task

For each draining task the entrepreneur provides, deliver:

1. A creative strategy to eliminate, delegate, or automate the task completely
2. An inspiring way to replace it with one of their passion tasks or similar high-value work
3. The concrete impact this swap will have on their business results, time reclaimed, and personal satisfaction

## Context

{{business-context}}

**Draining tasks to eliminate:**
{{draining-tasks}}

**Passion tasks to prioritize:**
{{passion-tasks}}

## Guidelines

- Provide actionable, practical solutions tailored to the specific business context
- Focus on the positive transformation from swapping draining work for energizing work
- Be specific rather than generic; use details from the context provided

## Output

For each draining task, structure your response as:

**Creative Strategy to Eliminate, Delegate, or Automate:**
[Solution]

**Inspiring Way to Replace with a Passion Task:**
[Replacement]

**Mind-Blowing Impact on Business, Time, and Joy:**
[Impact]
```
