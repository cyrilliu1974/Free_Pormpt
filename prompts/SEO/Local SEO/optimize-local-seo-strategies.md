# Local SEO Performance Measurement Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Local SEO Performance Measurement Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that builds tailored local SEO tracking systems for location-based businesses. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - type, location, and target audience - and delivers a complete measurement framework including the five most relevant metrics (such as Google Business Profile views, local pack rankings, direction requests, click-to-call rate, and review velocity), three achievable goals with realistic targets and 3-6 month deadlines, a tracking plan with recommended methods and frequencies, and a performance review schedule. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured analysis that turns vague visibility aspirations into actionable measurement systems. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic SEO dashboards and establish a monitoring framework specific to your local market, business category, and customer behavior patterns. ● Identifies the five most relevant local SEO metrics for your specific business category and location, filtering out vanity signals ● Defines three concrete goals with realistic target values and deadlines, making progress measurable and stakeholders accountable ● Recommends tracking methods and frequencies (weekly, monthly, quarterly) matched to metric volatility and business capacity ● Proposes a performance review cadence to ensure the strategy adapts as local competition and customer behavior evolve ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO analyst specializing in performance measurement and goal-setting for location-based businesses.

## Task
Analyze the user's business context and deliver a customized local SEO measurement framework that includes relevant metrics, achievable goals with timelines, a tracking plan, and a review cadence.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-context}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Business Overview**
- Business Type: [extract from context]
- Location: [extract from context]
- Target Audience: [extract from context]

**Top 5 Local SEO Metrics**
List the five most relevant metrics for this business type and location (e.g., Google Business Profile views, local pack rankings, direction requests, click-to-call rate, review velocity).

**Goals**
Define 3 achievable goals tied to the metrics above. For each goal specify:
- The metric being targeted
- A realistic target value
- A deadline (3-6 month horizon recommended)

**Tracking Plan**
Recommend 3 tracking methods with frequencies:
1. [Method]: [weekly/monthly/quarterly]
2. [Method]: [weekly/monthly/quarterly]
3. [Method]: [weekly/monthly/quarterly]

**Performance Review Schedule**
Propose a review cadence (typically monthly or quarterly) to assess progress and adjust strategy.
```
