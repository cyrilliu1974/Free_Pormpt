# Cold Email Copywriting Prompt for Lead Generation

## 簡介

The Cold Email Copywriting Prompt for Lead Generation is a free AI prompt that creates multi-stage B2B email sequences tailored to your industry, value proposition, and outreach goals. This cold email prompt for ChatGPT produces 3-5 complete templates spanning the entire outreach journey - from initial contact and follow-ups to value-add touches and meeting requests - each structured with attention-grabbing subject lines, personalized opening hooks, logical problem-to-solution flow, social proof, and clear calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, incorporating personalization variables like first name, company, and recent trigger events while following email deliverability best practices to maximize open and response rates. Sales teams, business development professionals, and founders who need to scale personalized outreach without sacrificing relevance will find this prompt essential for converting cold prospects into qualified leads. ● Creates 3-5 complete email templates for different outreach stages, each under 150 words and mobile-optimized ● Incorporates dynamic personalization placeholders for recipient name, company, and recent trigger events ● Structures every email with logical flow from problem recognition through social proof to a single, low-friction CTA ● Generates multiple tone variations (pain-focused, opportunity-focused, peer recommendation) for A/B testing effectiveness ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert cold email strategist specializing in B2B outreach and lead generation.

## Task
Create a series of personalized cold email templates designed to establish connections and generate leads. Deliver 3–5 templates covering different stages of the outreach process (initial contact, follow-up, value-add touch, meeting request).

## Context
Target industry context:
{{industry-and-customer-profile}}

Value proposition and proof:
{{value-proposition-and-case-studies}}

Primary outreach goal:
{{outreach-goal}}

## Instructions
1. Research the industry's key pain points, buying triggers, and current trends reflected in the context provided.
2. Write attention-grabbing subject lines specific to the industry's priorities.
3. Open each email with a personalized hook that establishes immediate relevance and credibility.
4. Structure the body for logical flow: problem recognition → your solution → social proof → clear next step.
5. Include personalization placeholders ({{FirstName}}, {{Company}}, {{RecentTrigger}}) where appropriate.
6. End with a single, low-friction call-to-action aligned with the outreach goal.
7. Ensure each template follows email deliverability best practices: concise length (under 150 words), no spam triggers, mobile-friendly formatting.
8. Vary tone and approach across templates to test different angles (pain-focused, opportunity-focused, peer recommendation).

## Output
Format each template with:

**Template [Number] – [Stage/Purpose]**

**Subject Line:**  
[subject line text]

**Email Body:**  
[full email text with personalization placeholders]

**Call-to-Action:**  
[specific CTA]

---

Provide 3–5 complete templates with clear markdown formatting.
```
