# Human Capital Shortage Risk Mitigation Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Human Capital Shortage Risk Mitigation Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored workforce strategies for HR professionals and business leaders facing talent gaps. This human capital shortage prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured action plan covering both immediate and long-term workforce challenges. It generates numbered steps with clear goals, actionable items, and strategic rationale across recruitment pipelines, retention programs, skill development frameworks, and succession planning. The prompt guides ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to incorporate technology and automation recommendations, diversity and inclusion initiatives, legal and ethical considerations, and monitoring metrics - all customized to your organization's specific context and current workforce challenges. Use it when planning for talent shortages, redesigning workforce strategies, or building organizational resilience against labor market volatility. ● Produces structured, step-by-step plans with clear goals and actionable items for each workforce challenge ● Covers recruitment pipelines, retention tactics, skill development, and succession planning in a single framework ● Incorporates technology, automation, diversity initiatives, and compliance considerations ● Outputs simple, direct language (Gunning Fog index of 8) with numbered steps and emoji markers for clarity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR consultant and strategic workforce planner specializing in risk mitigation and organizational resilience.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step plan to mitigate human capital shortage risks for the organization described below. Address both short-term and long-term workforce challenges through specific strategies covering recruitment, retention, skill development, and succession planning.

## Context
Organization: {{organization-context}}

Current workforce challenges: {{workforce-challenges}}

## Requirements
- Include technological solutions and automation where applicable
- Incorporate diversity and inclusion initiatives to broaden the talent pool
- Address legal and ethical considerations in workforce management
- Include measures for monitoring and evaluating strategy effectiveness
- Tailor recommendations to the specific organization rather than generic solutions
- Emphasize creating a resilient organizational culture

## Output
Present your plan as numbered steps with emojis. For each step:
- State the clear goal
- Provide actionable items
- Explain why this step matters

Use structured paragraphs and bullet points for clarity. Write concisely with simple, direct language (Gunning Fog index of 8). Avoid unnecessary adjectives, adverbs, and complex terminology.
```
