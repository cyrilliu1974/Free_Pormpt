# Minimalist Graphic Poster Generator

## 簡介

The Minimalist Graphic Poster Generator is a free AI prompt that creates striking, high-contrast graphic design posters with clean compositions for designers, marketers, and visual artists. This minimalist poster prompt for Midjourney produces graphic design artwork featuring your chosen subject against customizable backgrounds, rendered in specific art styles with dramatic lighting and dynamic camera angles. The prompt outputs images in a 2:3 portrait ratio, ideal for print posters, social media graphics, event announcements, and portfolio pieces. It works across Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other image generation platforms that support style direction and composition control. You specify three variables: the subject description, the background setting, and the desired art style, and the model returns a clean, minimalist composition with strong visual impact.
