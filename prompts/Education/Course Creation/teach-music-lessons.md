# Music Lesson Plan and Marketing Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Music Lesson Plan and Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates complete teaching and business plans for music instructors starting private lesson practices. This music lesson planning prompt for ChatGPT takes four inputs - your instrument, target student skill level, teaching experience, and location with availability - and produces two structured sections: a detailed lesson plan covering music theory, instrument technique, and performance skills tailored to different learning styles, plus a marketing strategy with promotional tactics, competitive pricing recommendations, and scheduling best practices. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text AI platforms. Music teachers use it to launch side-hustle teaching practices, expand existing studios into new instruments, or refine curriculum for specific skill levels from beginner to advanced. This prompt is for music instructors who need both pedagogical structure and business strategy in one workflow, whether you're a conservatory graduate starting your first private practice or an experienced teacher entering a new market. ● Builds lesson plans with music theory, technique, and performance milestones paced for the target skill level ● Suggests lesson activities adapted to auditory, visual, and kinesthetic learning styles ● Recommends promotional channels, competitive rates, and scheduling workflows based on your location and experience ● Outputs organized bullet-point sections you can immediately adapt and share with prospective students ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert music instructor designing a side-hustle teaching practice.

## Task
Create a comprehensive lesson plan and marketing strategy for private music instruction. Cover curriculum design, student engagement, business promotion, and operational logistics.

## Context
Instrument: {{instrument}}
Target student skill level: {{target-skill-level}}
Your teaching background: {{teaching-experience}}
Geographic area and weekly availability: {{location-and-schedule}}

## Output
Provide your response as a bullet-point list with two distinct sections:

**Lesson Plan:**
- Structured curriculum covering music theory, instrument technique, and performance skills
- Lesson activities tailored to different learning styles
- Pacing and progression milestones appropriate for the target skill level

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Promotional tactics for local classifieds and social media platforms
- Competitive rate recommendations based on location and experience
- Scheduling best practices and tips for maintaining a professional teaching environment
```
