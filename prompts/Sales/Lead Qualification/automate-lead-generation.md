# Automated Lead Generation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Automated Lead Generation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates complete, scalable lead acquisition plans for businesses seeking to automate their customer pipeline. This automated lead generation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through designing 5-7 distinct lead generation approaches spanning organic, paid, referral, content-driven, and partnership-based channels. By inputting your business context, target audience, current challenges, and preferred communication channels, the prompt generates a structured markdown table mapping each lead source to its qualification criteria and engagement strategy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a ready-to-implement automation blueprint that accounts for lead scoring, outreach sequences, and conversion pathways. Marketing teams, growth strategists, and sales leaders use it to replace fragmented lead generation efforts with a unified, measurable system. ● Generates 5-7 complete lead generation approaches covering diverse acquisition channels in a single session ● Defines qualification criteria and lead scoring logic specific to your target audience and business model ● Maps automated engagement sequences and conversion pathways for each lead source ● Delivers output as a markdown table that integrates directly into strategy documents and automation workflows ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lead generation strategist specializing in AI-powered automation and scalable customer acquisition systems.

## Task
Create a comprehensive automated lead generation plan that identifies and engages potential customers efficiently. Develop a strategy incorporating multiple lead sources, clear qualification criteria, and tailored engagement methods that are scalable, measurable, and aligned with business objectives.

## Context
Business and audience details:
{{business-and-audience}}

Current challenges and objectives:
{{goals-and-challenges}}

Preferred communication channels:
{{communication-channels}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Lead Source | Qualification Criteria | Engagement Strategy |

Each row should represent one complete lead generation approach tailored to the business context. Include 5-7 distinct approaches that cover diverse lead sources (organic, paid, referral, content-driven, partnership-based, etc.). For each approach:

- **Lead Source**: Specify the channel or method for discovering leads
- **Qualification Criteria**: Define how to filter and score leads based on fit and intent
- **Engagement Strategy**: Detail the automated outreach sequence, messaging approach, and conversion pathway

Ensure the automation plan accounts for lead volume expectations, integration requirements, and key performance indicators for measuring success.
```
