# E-book Writing and Sales Strategy Guide Generator

## 簡介

The E-book Writing and Sales Strategy Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a complete, actionable plan for authors who want to write, publish, and sell e-books for passive income. This e-book business prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your target audience, author background, and publishing goals, then producing a structured guide that walks you through every phase: topic selection and writing, technical formatting and platform setup, marketing and audience building, and pricing strategies for maximizing passive income. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for aspiring and current e-book authors who need a clear roadmap from first draft to first sale. Whether you're a content creator testing digital products, a subject-matter expert monetizing knowledge, or a marketer building passive revenue streams, the output gives you practical instructions organized by phase and ready to implement. ● Covers the full e-book lifecycle: ideation, research, writing, editing, formatting, file preparation, and platform publication ● Includes marketing tactics such as launch strategies, audience-building methods, promotional channels, and conversion optimization ● Addresses business essentials like pricing models, distribution channels, and passive income maximization techniques ● Outputs a logically structured guide with clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and step-by-step instructions customized to your author background and publishing objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-book author and digital marketing specialist creating a comprehensive, actionable guide on writing and selling e-books for passive income.

## Task
Develop a step-by-step plan covering the complete e-book journey from concept to sales. Address:

- **E-book creation**: topic selection, research, writing, and editing processes
- **Technical production**: formatting standards, file preparation, and publication platforms
- **Marketing strategy**: launch tactics, audience building, promotional channels, and conversion optimization
- **Business fundamentals**: pricing models, distribution options, and passive income maximization

## Context
**Target audience**: {{target-audience}}
**Author background**: {{author-background}}
**Publishing goals**: {{publishing-goals}}

Each step must be clear, practical, and directly applicable to the audience's needs and goals.

## Output
Structure the guide with:

- Clear main section headings for each phase (Creation, Production, Marketing, Business)
- Subheadings for specific processes within each phase
- Bullet points for actionable instructions, tips, and best practices
- Logical progression from beginner fundamentals to advanced optimization

Ensure maximum readability and immediate implementability.
```
