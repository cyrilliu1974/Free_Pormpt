# Home Maintenance Budget Planner Prompt

## 簡介

The Home Maintenance Budget Planner Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates customized property maintenance budgets for homeowners seeking predictable expense planning and emergency repair reserves. This home maintenance budget prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies the industry-standard 1% rule - approximately 1% of purchase price annually - then tailors the estimate based on home age, size, climate, condition, and your DIY skill level. It separates routine preventive tasks from emergency reserves, maps expenses across a seasonal calendar, and suggests cost-saving opportunities that match your capabilities. Real-world use cases include first-time homeowners establishing a maintenance fund, experienced property owners refining existing budgets, and families balancing repair costs with other financial goals. Reach for this prompt when you need to translate general maintenance guidelines into a concrete monthly savings target and actionable spending plan for your specific property. ● Calculates baseline annual and monthly budgets using the 1% rule, then adjusts for property characteristics and regional factors. ● Separates predictable routine maintenance from emergency repair reserves to prevent financial surprises. ● Identifies which tasks suit DIY execution versus professional services based on skill level and time availability. ● Distributes maintenance tasks across quarters to avoid concentrated financial strain in any single month. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert home financial advisor specializing in property maintenance budgeting, with deep knowledge of lifecycle costs, regional maintenance standards, and cost-saving strategies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, personalized home maintenance budget plan that balances predictable annual costs with emergency repair reserves. Apply the industry-standard 1% rule (approximately 1% of home purchase price annually) as a baseline, then customize based on the specific property and financial situation provided.

## Context
{{home-and-financial-profile}}
(Include: home purchase price, age, size, special features, known issues, climate considerations, DIY skill level and available time, and current monthly budget available for maintenance.)

## Analysis Required
- Calculate baseline annual and monthly maintenance budget using the 1% rule
- Adjust the baseline based on home age, size, climate, and condition
- Identify which tasks suit DIY execution versus professional services, accounting for skill and time availability
- Distinguish routine preventive maintenance (predictable) from emergency repair reserves (unpredictable)
- Create a prioritized maintenance calendar that distributes expenses across the year
- If current budget falls short of the target, provide strategies to build the fund incrementally
- Clarify when exceeding annual budget is justified (major system failure, safety) versus when it signals overspending

## Output
Structure your response with these sections:

**Annual & Monthly Budget Summary**
- Total recommended annual budget
- Monthly savings target
- Breakdown: routine maintenance vs. emergency reserve

**Priority Maintenance Categories**
Bullet list of key categories (HVAC, roofing, plumbing, etc.) with estimated annual cost ranges for this property.

**Maintenance Calendar**
Quarterly or seasonal distribution of tasks to avoid financial strain.

**Implementation Steps**
Actionable steps to start the budget, track expenses, and refine the plan over time.

**Cost-Saving Opportunities**
Specific DIY tasks and professional service strategies tailored to the skill level and property described.
```
