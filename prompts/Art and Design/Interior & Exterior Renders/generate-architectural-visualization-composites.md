# Architectural Visualization With Floor Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Architectural Visualization With Floor Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates synchronized split-screen compositions pairing photorealistic building renders with their exact technical floor plans for architects, designers, and visualization studios. This architectural visualization prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces vertical-format images divided into two halves: the upper section renders a photorealistic building exterior or interior with accurate materials, natural lighting, and architectural photography quality, while the lower half generates a precise technical floor plan drawing that matches the geometry, layout, and scale of the rendered structure above. The plan includes room labels, area measurements in square meters, circulation elements, scale indicators, and professional annotations on a warm off-white paper texture. Use it for competition boards, client presentations, portfolio layouts, or marketing materials that need to communicate both design intent and spatial organization in a single cohesive image. Reach for this prompt when you need presentation-ready architectural visuals that merge aesthetic appeal with technical clarity without juggling multiple software tools. ● Produces synchronized vertical split compositions where the floor plan geometry precisely corresponds to the rendered building above it. ● Supports customization of building type and material palette while maintaining accurate scale, realistic material detailing, and professional linework quality. ● Generates competition-board-quality output with room labels, area measurements, subtle shading, and minimalist typography that stays legible without cluttering the drawing. ● Outputs in vertical 3:4 or 4:5 aspect ratios optimized for portfolio pages, presentation slides, and social media formats. ## Prompt

```
Create a vertical split architectural visualization combining a photorealistic render with its matching floor plan drawing.

**Upper Half: Photorealistic Render**
A modern {{building-type}} featuring {{material-palette}} with full-height glazing and glass balustrades. Natural daylight with soft shadows, neutral sky background, minimal landscaping. Ultra-sharp photorealistic architectural photography with accurate scale and realistic material detailing.

**Lower Half: Architectural Floor Plan**
Precise technical drawing that exactly matches the upper building's geometry and layout. Clean linework on warm off-white paper texture. Include floor level label, room names, area measurements in m² for each space, central circulation element clearly identified. Subtle dashed reference lines and scale indicators. Minimalist thin sans-serif typography, neatly aligned and legible but unobtrusive. Subtle shading maintains clarity, competition-board quality precision.

**Layout**
Horizontal division, vertical 3:4 or 4:5 aspect ratio. Clean, synchronized, professional presentation with high precision. The plan must correspond exactly to the render above it.

**Avoid**
Incorrect proportions, mismatched plan and building, sketchy lines, cartoon style, heavy textures, handwritten text, random labels, inaccurate geometry, low detail, unrealistic materials, excessive contrast
```
