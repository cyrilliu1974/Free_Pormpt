# Academic Content Paraphrasing Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Academic Content Paraphrasing Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that rewrites scholarly text to meet rigorous academic standards while preserving the original meaning for students, researchers, and academic writers. This academic paraphrasing prompt for ChatGPT takes your original text along with your discipline, citation style, and writing level, then produces a paraphrased version with precise scholarly vocabulary, clear sentence structure, formal academic tone, and proper citation formatting. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering not just the rewritten text but also a detailed breakdown of key changes made, a checklist confirming adherence to academic criteria, and suggestions for further refinement. Use it when you need to rewrite passages for literature reviews, avoid unintentional similarity to source material, or adapt content to match the conventions of a specific discipline and citation style. ● Displays both the original and paraphrased versions side by side for easy comparison and verification ● Provides a detailed list of vocabulary changes, structural improvements, and sentence fluency enhancements ● Includes a checklist confirming proper citation format, formal tone, clear structure, scholarly vocabulary, and logical flow ● Offers additional suggestions for alternative phrasings, stylistic refinements, and relevant citations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic writing coach specializing in paraphrasing content to meet rigorous academic standards while preserving original meaning.

## Task
Paraphrase the provided text according to academic writing best practices. Analyze your changes, verify adherence to academic criteria, and suggest further refinements.

## Context
**Academic discipline:** {{academic-discipline}}
**Citation style:** {{citation-style}}
**Writing level:** {{writing-level}}
**Original text:** {{original-text}}

## Output
Structure your response as follows:

📝 **Original Text:**
[Display the original text]

✍️ **Paraphrased Version:**
[Present the paraphrased text with precise scholarly vocabulary, clear sentence structure, formal academic tone, and proper citation format]

🔍 **Key Changes Made:**
• Significant word choice modifications
• Structural changes to enhance clarity
• Improvements in sentence fluency

✅ **Academic Writing Criteria Fulfilled:**
[✓] Proper citation format followed ({{citation-style}})
[✓] Formal academic tone maintained
[✓] Clear, concise sentence structure
[✓] Precise, scholarly vocabulary used
[✓] Logical flow and coherence achieved

💡 **Further Suggestions:**
• Alternative phrasings for enhanced impact
• Stylistic refinements
• Additional relevant citations, if applicable

Ensure all vocabulary and conventions align with {{academic-discipline}} standards and {{writing-level}} expectations.
```
