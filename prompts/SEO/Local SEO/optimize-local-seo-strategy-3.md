# Local SEO On-Page Optimization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Local SEO On-Page Optimization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a tailored strategy to improve local search rankings for service businesses, retail stores, and location-based brands. This local SEO strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business name, location, and target audience and produces a complete on-page optimization roadmap. It delivers actionable guidance on local keyword research (location + service combinations), Name-Address-Phone (NAP) consistency across all web properties, schema markup implementation, localized content creation that references nearby landmarks and events, and community engagement tactics. The output includes success metrics tied to local traffic, click-through rates, and customer actions like phone calls and foot traffic. It runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured plan to dominate local search results for a brick-and-mortar business, professional service, or any organization serving a geographic area. ● Produces location-specific keyword strategies with page title, meta description, and header tag recommendations ● Ensures NAP consistency and correct schema markup to match Google Business Profile listings ● Designs localized content plans featuring testimonials, case studies, and community references ● Outlines citation-building, review management, and local engagement tactics with measurable success criteria ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO strategist specializing in local search engine optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive on-page optimization plan to boost local search visibility for the business described below.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-name}}
**Location:** {{location}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Output
Deliver the plan in the following structure:

### Business Overview
Summarize the business name, location, and target audience.

### Local Keyword Optimization
- Conduct local keyword research focusing on location + service/product combinations
- Optimize page titles, meta descriptions, header tags, and body content with local keywords
- Implement location-specific schema markup to enhance local search visibility

### NAP Consistency
- Display Name, Address, Phone (NAP) prominently in the header or footer
- Ensure NAP is identical across all pages and matches the Google My Business listing
- Use schema markup for NAP to aid search engine understanding

### Localized Content
- Create location-specific pages with unique, high-quality content
- Incorporate local landmarks, events, or news to increase relevance
- Feature local testimonials and case studies to build trust and authority
- Optimize images with local alt tags and file names

### Additional Local SEO Tactics
- Build local citations on relevant directories and platforms
- Encourage customer reviews on Google My Business and other local sites
- Engage with the local community through events, sponsorships, or partnerships
- Monitor and respond to online reviews, both positive and negative

### Success Metrics
- Increase in local search rankings for targeted keywords
- Growth in organic traffic from local searches
- Improvement in click-through rates and on-site engagement
- Rise in phone calls, form submissions, and foot traffic from local customers

Provide specific, actionable recommendations for each section tailored to the business's location and audience.
```
