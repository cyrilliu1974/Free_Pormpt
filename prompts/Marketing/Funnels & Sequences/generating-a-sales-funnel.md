# Sales Funnel Strategy Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Sales Funnel Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a complete customer acquisition and retention funnel tailored to your product, audience, and marketing budget. This sales funnel prompt for ChatGPT guides you through all five stages - Awareness, Interest, Decision, Action, and Retention - producing a structured markdown table with strategies, content formats, campaign ideas, key performance indicators, and recommended platforms for each stage. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Whether you are launching a SaaS product, an e-commerce store, or a consulting service, you input your product details, target audience characteristics, business goals, and current marketing channels, and the prompt returns a full-funnel map that addresses customer needs and pain points at every step of the buyer journey. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to move prospects from first contact to loyal customers, or when aligning disparate marketing tactics into a single, measurable system. ● Produces a five-column markdown table covering Awareness, Interest, Decision, Action, and Retention with bullet-pointed tactics under each stage. ● Includes specific content formats, campaign ideas, and performance metrics tailored to your product and audience. ● Suggests tools and platforms for implementation and tracking at every funnel stage. ● Ensures all strategies are actionable, measurable, and aligned with your stated business goals and marketing budget. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in customer acquisition and retention funnels.

## Task
Create a comprehensive sales funnel that guides potential customers from initial awareness through to long-term retention. Structure your analysis across all five funnel stages: Awareness, Interest, Decision, Action, and Retention.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Business goals: {{business-goals}}
Current marketing channels and budget: {{marketing-channels-budget}}

For each stage, develop:
- Tailored strategies that address the audience's needs and pain points at that point in their journey
- Specific content formats and campaign ideas
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure effectiveness
- Recommended tools and platforms for implementation and tracking

Ensure all tactics are actionable, measurable, and aligned with the product offerings and audience characteristics.

## Output
Present your funnel as a markdown table with 5 columns (Awareness | Interest | Decision | Action | Retention). Under each column, provide bullet points detailing:
- Stage-specific strategies
- Content and campaign ideas
- KPIs to track
- Recommended tools/platforms
```
