# Documentary Horse Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Documentary Horse Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, unposed photographs of horses in their natural settings for equestrians, photographers, and animal lovers. This horse portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images styled after classic Magnum Photos documentary work, capturing genuine moments with soft ambient light, muted color palettes, and the intimate perspective of a 35mm f/2 lens. The 3:2 aspect ratio mimics traditional photojournalism while the quality-2 setting ensures fine detail in texture and expression. Use it to document rescue horses, create gallery prints of your stable, or generate reference material for equine illustration and painting. The prompt works for photographers seeking documentary-style equine imagery without staging or directing animals, and for anyone who values emotional authenticity over studio perfection. ● Produces unposed, candid portraits that capture real behavior and expression rather than staged compositions ● Applies Magnum Photos documentary aesthetic with soft natural lighting and desaturated, film-inspired tones ● Uses 35mm f/2 lens simulation for shallow depth of field and intimate, human-perspective framing ● Outputs 3:2 aspect ratio suitable for fine-art prints, editorial use, and traditional photography presentation ## Prompt

```
Raw documentary portrait of a {{subject}} in their natural environment, unposed moment, Magnum Photos style. Soft ambient light, muted tones, 35mm f/2 lens. Emotional authenticity. --ar 3:2 --quality 2
```
