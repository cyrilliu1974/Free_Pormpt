# SEO Content Strategy Table Generator

## 簡介

The SEO Content Strategy Table Generator is a free AI prompt that builds keyword-driven content roadmaps for businesses and marketing teams looking to improve search visibility. This content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business model, audience pain points, and competitive landscape to produce a markdown table linking specific content formats to high-value keywords and measurable performance goals. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, conducting keyword research that balances search volume with ranking opportunity, then matches content types to search intent whether informational, commercial, or transactional. Marketing managers use it to plan editorial calendars, SEO specialists use it to prioritize quick wins, and content teams use it to align production with business outcomes. ● Maps content formats (blog posts, guides, videos, landing pages) to primary keywords and semantic variations ● Defines measurable goals like top-5 rankings within 90 days, newsletter signup targets, or organic CTR benchmarks ● Prioritizes both quick-win opportunities (low competition, high relevance) and long-term authority content ● Customizable by business context, audience details, competitive landscape, and minimum number of content recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in SEO-driven content planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content strategy table that maps content types to target keywords and measurable content goals. Analyze the business context, identify high-value keyword opportunities, and recommend content formats that drive both search visibility and business outcomes.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

{{competitive-landscape}}

## Process
1. Analyze the business model, audience pain points, and search intent patterns
2. Conduct keyword research prioritizing relevance, search volume, and ranking opportunity
3. Match content types to keyword intent (informational, commercial, transactional)
4. Define specific, measurable goals for each content piece (traffic targets, conversion actions, engagement metrics)

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with these columns:
- **Content Type** – format (blog post, guide, video, landing page, etc.)
- **Target Keywords** – primary keyword plus 2-3 semantic variations
- **Content Goals** – specific metrics (e.g., "Rank top 5 for [keyword] within 90 days", "Generate 50 newsletter signups/month", "Increase organic CTR to 8%")

Include a minimum of {{minimum-rows}} rows. Prioritize quick wins (low competition, high relevance) alongside long-term authority content.
```
