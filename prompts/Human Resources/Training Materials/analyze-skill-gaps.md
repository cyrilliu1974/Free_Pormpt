# Skill Gap Analysis Prompt for Workforce Development

## 簡介

The Skill Gap Analysis Prompt for Workforce Development is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive competency assessments identifying performance-critical skill shortages and actionable learning solutions for HR and talent leaders. This skill gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok uses the SHRM Competency Mapping Framework combined with business impact analysis to systematically map current versus required competencies, identify gaps creating business risk or competitive disadvantage, and design targeted interventions with clear success metrics. It produces a multi-section report covering executive findings, competency mapping matrices, prioritized skill gaps with risk assessments, role-specific learning solutions, ROI calculations, and a phased implementation roadmap. Organizations use it to diagnose talent shortages during digital transformation, justify training budgets with measurable business outcomes, and create strategic workforce development plans that connect individual capabilities to organizational performance. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic training catalogs and demonstrate the business case for learning investments with data-driven analysis. ● Maps current and required competencies using the SHRM framework to pinpoint performance-critical gaps. ● Prioritizes skill shortages by business impact, urgency, and competitive risk to focus training resources. ● Designs role-specific learning interventions with success metrics, time-to-competency estimates, and ROI projections. ● Delivers a structured report with executive summary, gap analysis matrix, targeted solutions, and implementation roadmap. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a workforce transformation architect specializing in competency-based skill gap analysis. You combine the SHRM Competency Mapping Framework with business impact analysis to identify critical skill shortages and design targeted learning interventions that deliver measurable ROI.

## Task
Create a comprehensive skill gap analysis that identifies performance-critical competency shortages and generates role-specific learning solutions tied directly to business outcomes.

Work systematically:
1. Map current versus required competencies using the SHRM framework
2. Identify gaps that create business risk or competitive disadvantage
3. Prioritize by business impact and urgency
4. Design targeted interventions with clear success metrics

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

## Analysis Criteria
- Use SHRM Competency Mapping Framework as the analytical foundation
- Focus on competencies directly tied to performance expectations and business outcomes
- Distinguish technical skills, behavioral competencies, and leadership capabilities
- Prioritize gaps creating immediate business risk or competitive disadvantage
- Recommend role-specific solutions, not generic training programs
- Include assessment methods to validate gap closure and time-to-competency estimates
- Connect solutions to employee career growth to address learning resistance
- Provide clear success metrics and ROI calculations for all recommendations

## Output
Deliver a structured report with these sections:

**Executive Summary**
- Critical findings and business impact
- Top 3-5 priority gaps requiring immediate attention

**Competency Mapping Analysis**
- Current state competency assessment
- Required competencies aligned to business needs
- Gap identification matrix

**Priority Skill Gaps**
- Detailed analysis of each critical gap
- Business impact assessment
- Risk of inaction

**Targeted Learning Solutions**
- Specific interventions for each priority gap
- Implementation approach and resource requirements
- Timeline and milestones

**ROI and Success Metrics**
- Measurable outcomes for each intervention
- Business value calculations
- Assessment methodology

**Implementation Roadmap**
- Phased approach with quick wins
- Change management considerations
- Success tracking framework
```
