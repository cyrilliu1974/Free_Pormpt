# Unique Value Proposition Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Unique Value Proposition Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a complete UVP statement and brand positioning package for independent professionals. This value proposition prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your unique strengths and target audience pain points to produce a concise primary statement (15-25 words), three to four supporting value points, a positioning summary explaining market differentiation, and tactical messaging recommendations for deployment across brand touchpoints. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by applying dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and impact. Real-world applications include launching a consulting practice, repositioning an established freelance brand, clarifying service offerings on a website, or preparing pitch materials for prospective clients. Designed for solopreneurs and independent professionals who need to articulate what makes their personal brand distinctly valuable in competitive markets. ● Maps your specific capabilities directly to target audience pain points with strategic precision ● Produces a memorable 15-25 word primary statement plus elaborated benefit points ● Includes positioning analysis showing how your UVP differentiates you in your market segment ● Provides tactical deployment recommendations for website copy, pitch decks, and client conversations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in value proposition development for solopreneurs and independent professionals.

## Task
Create a compelling Unique Value Proposition (UVP) that clearly communicates the solopreneur's distinctive value to their target audience. Structure the UVP using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and impact.

## Context
{{solopreneur-profile}}

{{target-audience-and-pain-points}}

## Process
1. Analyze the solopreneur's unique strengths, skills, and market position
2. Map the target audience's pain points to the solopreneur's capabilities
3. Identify the primary value dependency: what unique outcome the solopreneur delivers that the audience cannot easily find elsewhere
4. Craft a concise UVP that connects the solopreneur's distinctive approach to the audience's core needs
5. Refine for memorability and brand alignment

## Output
Deliver a structured UVP package:

**Primary UVP Statement**
- One compelling sentence (15-25 words) that captures the core value proposition

**Supporting Value Points**
- 3-4 bullet points elaborating on specific benefits and differentiators

**Positioning Summary**
- Brief explanation of how this UVP distinguishes the solopreneur in their market

**Messaging Recommendations**
- 2-3 tactical suggestions for deploying this UVP across brand touchpoints
```
