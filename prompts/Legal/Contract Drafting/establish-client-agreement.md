# Client Agreement Drafting for Ghostwriting Projects

## 簡介

The Client Agreement Drafting for Ghostwriting Projects is a free AI prompt that produces professional service contracts tailored specifically for writers and creative professionals working with clients. This ghostwriting contract prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to draft complete agreements with clearly defined sections including parties, scope of work, timeline and deliverables, payment terms, intellectual property rights transfer, confidentiality provisions, revision processes, dispute resolution mechanisms, and termination conditions. The prompt structures output using dependency grammar principles to ensure logical clause flow and legal clarity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, allowing writers to customize agreements based on specific project details while maintaining professional legal language that protects both service provider and client interests. Reach for this prompt whenever you need a legally sound contract framework for ghostwriting, book writing, content creation, or similar creative service arrangements where IP ownership will transfer to the client. ● Covers all critical contract elements: payment schedules, milestone deliverables, copyright transfer, confidentiality obligations, and revision limits ● Structures agreements with professional section headings and clear clause formatting ready for client signature ● Uses unambiguous legal language designed to prevent disputes and misunderstandings about scope or ownership ● Includes termination provisions and dispute resolution procedures to protect both writer and client ## Prompt

```

- Clear section headings for each major component (Parties, Scope of Work, Timeline & Deliverables, Payment Terms, Intellectual Property Rights, Confidentiality, Revisions, Dispute Resolution, Termination, Signatures)
```
