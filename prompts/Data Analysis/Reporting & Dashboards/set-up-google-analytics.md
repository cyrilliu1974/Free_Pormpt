# Google Analytics Setup Guide for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Google Analytics Setup Guide for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that generates a customized, step-by-step implementation plan for tracking website traffic, analyzing user behavior, and measuring conversions. This Google Analytics prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a complete setup roadmap structured around your specific business context, primary conversion goal, and technical expertise. You provide details about your business, website, and target audience, and the prompt returns numbered instructions covering account creation, tracking code installation, goal configuration, audience segmentation, custom report creation, and metric interpretation. Solopreneurs use it to deploy Google Analytics without hiring a consultant, translating complex technical tasks into clear, actionable steps with explanations of what each configuration accomplishes and why it matters for their business. Reach for this prompt when you need to implement or audit your Google Analytics setup but lack formal training in web analytics or want guidance specific to your conversion goals. ● Produces installation instructions with prerequisite checks and common pitfall warnings to avoid tracking failures ● Generates conversion goal configuration steps matched to your specific business objective (lead capture, e-commerce, contact form, etc.) ● Creates custom report recommendations that surface the metrics most relevant to your target audience and traffic sources ● Explains technical terminology in accessible language so non-technical solopreneurs can confidently implement tracking without developer support ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing analyst specializing in Google Analytics implementation and website tracking strategy.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, step-by-step guide to implement Google Analytics for a business website. The guide must enable tracking of website traffic, analysis of user behavior, and measurement of conversions. Write for solopreneurs with varying technical backgrounds, ensuring clarity at every step.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}
(Include: business name, website URL, primary conversion goal, target audience, and your level of technical expertise)

## Output
Deliver the guide as a numbered list with clear section headings. For each implementation step, provide:
- Concise instructions
- Detailed explanations of what the step accomplishes
- Tips and best practices for optimization
- Any prerequisites or common pitfalls to avoid

Structure the guide to cover:
1. Initial Google Analytics account setup
2. Installing the tracking code on the website
3. Configuring goals and conversion tracking
4. Setting up audience and traffic source tracking
5. Creating custom reports relevant to the conversion goal
6. Interpreting key metrics for the target audience
7. Ongoing maintenance and optimization recommendations

Ensure all technical terminology is explained in accessible language.
```
