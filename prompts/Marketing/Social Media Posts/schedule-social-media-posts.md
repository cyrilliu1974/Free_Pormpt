# Social Media Campaign Planner With Content Calendar

## 簡介

The Social Media Campaign Planner With Content Calendar is a free AI prompt that builds complete social media campaigns with organized content calendars for brands, products, and services. This social media campaign prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience across platforms, creates a diverse content mix of text posts, images, videos, stories, and reels, and schedules everything at optimal posting times. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver platform-specific strategies that align with your marketing goals, brand voice, and campaign duration. Marketing teams use it to plan month-long product launches, nonprofits deploy it for awareness campaigns, and agencies run it to pitch structured social strategies to clients. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete content calendar that spans multiple platforms and includes specific posting schedules, caption copy, visual concepts, hashtags, and community management protocols. ● Outputs a hierarchical content calendar organized by platform, content type, and posting schedule ● Includes caption copy, visual concepts, hashtag recommendations, and optimal posting times for each piece of content ● Analyzes audience behavior patterns on each platform to inform timing and format decisions ● Establishes metrics, KPIs, and community management protocols for ongoing campaign optimization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media campaign with a strategic content calendar that promotes the specified offering while engaging the target audience effectively.

## Context
**Offering:** {{product-service-brand}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Campaign parameters:** {{campaign-parameters}}
(Include: marketing goals, social media platforms to use, brand voice and tone, campaign duration)

## Approach
1. Analyze the target audience and their behavior on each specified platform
2. Develop a content strategy aligned with the stated marketing goals
3. Create a diverse content mix: text posts, images, videos, stories, reels, and interactive formats
4. Schedule posts at optimal times for each platform based on audience activity patterns
5. Incorporate trending topics and relevant industry hashtags
6. Recommend visually appealing content concepts (graphics, video themes, story formats)
7. Plan for audience interaction, community management, and response protocols
8. Establish metrics to track and KPIs for ongoing optimization

## Output
Deliver a detailed content calendar organized by:
- **Platform** (one section per social media channel)
- **Content type** (grouped by format)
- **Post details** including: caption/copy, visual concept, hashtags, posting time/frequency, and engagement tactics

Format as a structured bullet point list with clear hierarchical organization.
```
