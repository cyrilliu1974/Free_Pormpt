# Personalized Learning Plan Generator for Homeschool

## 簡介

The Personalized Learning Plan Generator for Homeschool is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, individualized education plans for homeschooled students based on their specific learning profiles, strengths, and challenges. This personalized learning plan prompt for ChatGPT takes a student profile - including age, grade level, learning style, and any specific challenges - and produces a complete three-part plan: a student overview that captures key learning characteristics, a subject-by-subject adaptation table covering 5-6 core areas with tailored strategies and measurable success indicators, and a progress monitoring framework that includes both formative and summative assessment methods. Parents and educators use it to design instruction that matches how their student learns best, whether they're visual learners, have attention challenges, excel in hands-on activities, or need accommodations for specific learning differences. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this tool when you need to move beyond generic curricula and build a learning roadmap that honors a student's individual needs while maintaining academic rigor and accountability. ● Produces a complete adaptation strategy table for 5-6 core subjects with learning-style-specific teaching methods and clear success metrics ● Includes both formative (ongoing) and summative (milestone) assessment methods suitable for home education settings ● Outlines a structured adjustment process so the plan evolves as the student's needs and progress emerge ● Grounds all strategies in evidence-based practices while remaining practical and actionable for parents teaching at home ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in personalized learning who designs comprehensive education plans tailored to individual students' needs, abilities, and learning styles.

## Task
Create a complete personalized learning plan for a homeschooled student that incorporates evidence-based accommodations, strengths-based strategies, and progress monitoring techniques.

## Context
Student profile:
{{student-profile}}

(Include: name, age, grade level, and specific learning style or challenge)

## Output
Provide a structured learning plan with three components:

### 1. Student Overview
Summarize the key information about the student that informs the plan design.

### 2. Subject Adaptation Plan
Create a table with these columns:

| Subject | Adaptation Strategy | Success Indicators |
|---------|--------------------|--------------------|
| [Core subject] | [Strategies tailored to the student's learning style/challenge] | [Measurable indicators of progress and mastery] |

Include adaptations for 5-6 core subjects (e.g., Mathematics, Language Arts, Science, Social Studies, and relevant electives).

### 3. Progress Monitoring Plan
- **Assessment methods:** List both formative (ongoing) and summative (milestone) assessment strategies appropriate for homeschool settings
- **Monitoring frequency:** Specify how often progress will be assessed and reviewed
- **Adjustment process:** Outline clear steps for modifying strategies based on student progress and emerging needs

Ensure all strategies are actionable, measurable, and aligned with the student's specific learning profile.
```
