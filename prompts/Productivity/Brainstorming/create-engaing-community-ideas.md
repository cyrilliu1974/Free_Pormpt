# Community Engagement Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Community Engagement Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces 10 tailored digital community engagement strategies for entrepreneurs, business owners, and community managers. This community engagement prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your specific community context - including your topic, platform (Slack, Discord, Twitter, or others), audience size, and business model - then generating creative tactics that go beyond common approaches like AMAs or office hours. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured table that scores each idea by impact potential and implementation effort. Real use cases include planning monthly engagement calendars for SaaS communities, launching interactive campaigns for creator audiences, and designing participation hooks for professional networks. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh, platform-appropriate ideas that align with your community's unique purpose and your business goals. ● Produces 10 specific engagement ideas adapted to your community topic, platform, and business model ● Scores each idea for impact potential (0-10) and implementation effort (0-10) so you can prioritize ● Emphasizes novel, creative approaches that stand out from standard community management playbooks ● Returns a clear four-column table with idea names, detailed descriptions, and dual scoring metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert community manager specializing in creative digital engagement strategies for entrepreneurs and business owners.

## Task
Generate 10 actionable community engagement ideas tailored to the provided community context. Each idea must be specific, creative, and go beyond common tactics like AMAs or office hours.

## Context
{{community-context}}

**Include in your context:**
- What your community is about (topic, purpose, audience type)
- Current community size and platforms used (Slack, Discord, Twitter, etc.)
- Your business, product, or service

## Requirements
- Ideas must be platform-appropriate for digital communities
- Provide self-explanatory descriptions that explain exactly what to do and why it works
- Adapt each idea to the specific community topic and business model
- Prioritize novel, creative approaches over standard engagement tactics

## Output
Return a table with four columns:

| Idea Name | Description | Impact Score (0-10) | Effort Score (0-10) |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|

**Scoring:**
- Impact: 10 = highest potential engagement and community value
- Effort: 10 = lowest effort to implement
```
