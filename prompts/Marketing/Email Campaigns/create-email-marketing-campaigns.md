# Email Marketing Campaign Builder for Products

## 簡介

The Email Marketing Campaign Builder for Products is a free AI prompt that creates complete email marketing campaigns with subject lines, body copy, social proof integration, and calls-to-action for any product and target audience. This email marketing campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building campaigns that combine persuasive copywriting, personalization techniques, and mobile-responsive design. It structures your campaign from attention-grabbing subject lines and preview text through personalized openings, feature-benefit highlights, testimonials or case studies, special offers with urgency elements, and conversion-focused CTAs. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating campaigns optimized for both desktop and mobile viewing. Real-world applications include product launches, seasonal promotions, re-engagement sequences, and conversion-focused drip campaigns. This prompt is for marketers, business owners, and growth teams who need complete email campaigns that drive opens, clicks, and conversions without starting from a blank page. ● Generates subject lines with personalization and preview text that increase open rates ● Structures email body with feature-benefit bullets, storytelling, and social proof testimonials ● Integrates offers or incentives with urgency tactics and clear redemption terms ● Provides design guidance for mobile-responsive layouts with accessibility considerations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in high-conversion campaigns that combine persuasive copywriting, personalization, and conversion optimization.

## Task
Design a complete email marketing campaign that promotes {{product}} to {{target-audience}}, optimized for engagement and conversions across desktop and mobile platforms.

## Context
The campaign should highlight the product's unique value proposition while using personalization techniques to maximize open rates. Include storytelling, social proof, and strategic offers to drive action. The email must guide the reader from an attention-grabbing subject line through to a compelling call-to-action.

## Output
Deliver a complete email campaign with these components:

**Subject Line & Preview Text**
- Craft an attention-grabbing subject line using personalization where appropriate
- Write preview text that complements the subject line and teases value without revealing everything

**Email Structure**
1. **Personalized Opening**: Greeting that connects {{product}} to the interests or needs of {{target-audience}}

2. **Product Features & Benefits**: Highlight key features using bullet points, emphasizing how {{product}} solves problems or enhances the recipient's life

3. **Social Proof**: Incorporate {{social-proof}} such as testimonials, reviews, or case studies

4. **Special Offer**: Present {{offer-or-incentive}} with clear terms and urgency if applicable

5. **Call-to-Action**: Strong, clear CTA that drives the desired action (purchase, learn more, redeem offer)

**Design Notes**
- Use clean, mobile-responsive layout with high-quality product images
- Ensure readability on all devices with branded colors and fonts
- Include alt text for accessibility
- Structure content for easy scanning and navigation

The final email should be concise, visually appealing, and use persuasive language throughout to maximize engagement and conversions.
```
