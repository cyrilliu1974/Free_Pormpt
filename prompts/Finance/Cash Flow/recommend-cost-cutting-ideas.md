# Cost Cutting Recommendations Prompt for Business Expense

## 簡介

The Cost Cutting Recommendations Prompt for Business Expense is a free AI prompt that analyzes company spending and delivers actionable cost-reduction strategies for finance teams, consultants, and business owners. This cost cutting prompt for ChatGPT works by examining your business context and expense data to identify inefficiencies, redundancies, and low-ROI spending across all major categories. It evaluates automation opportunities, vendor renegotiation potential, and elimination candidates, then ranks each recommendation by savings impact, implementation difficulty, and operational risk. The output includes specific dollar amounts or percentages for projected savings, payback periods, cash flow timing, and a priority matrix that balances aggressive cost reduction with operational health. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need to optimize business expenses without damaging core capabilities or growth potential, whether you're managing cash flow constraints, preparing for a funding round, or advising clients on expense optimization. ● Breaks down major expense categories to flag inefficiencies, duplications, and non-essential spending patterns ● Creates a priority matrix ranking cost-reduction opportunities by savings impact, implementation difficulty, and operational risk ● Calculates specific monthly and annual savings projections, payback periods, and cash flow effects for each recommendation ● Provides step-by-step implementation timelines and explains how each change affects operations and growth capacity ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a cost optimization consultant specializing in expense reduction that preserves growth potential. Balance aggressive savings with operational health, avoiding cuts that either fail to deliver meaningful impact or damage core capabilities.

## Task

Analyze the provided business expense data and deliver a prioritized cost-reduction action plan. For each opportunity, assess implementation difficulty, business risk, and financial impact with specific dollar amounts or percentages, payback periods, and cash flow timing.

## Context

{{business-context}}

{{expense-data}}

Identify hidden waste, redundancies, and low-ROI spending. Evaluate automation opportunities, vendor renegotiation potential, and elimination candidates. Rank recommendations by impact versus risk, accounting for growth stage and cash position.

## Process

1. **Expense Analysis** – Break down each major category; flag inefficiencies, duplications, and non-essential spend
2. **Priority Matrix** – Rank opportunities by savings impact, implementation difficulty, and operational risk (low/medium/high)
3. **Action Plan** – Provide specific steps with timelines for each recommendation
4. **Financial Impact** – Project monthly and annual savings, payback periods, and effects on cash flow and growth capacity

## Output

Structure your response with these headings:

- **Expense Analysis**
- **Priority Matrix**
- **Action Plan**
- **Financial Impact**

Use bullet points for recommendations. Include specific dollar amounts or percentages for all projected savings. Explain how each change affects operations, cash flow, and ability to grow.
```
