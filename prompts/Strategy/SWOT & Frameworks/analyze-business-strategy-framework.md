# Golden Circle Business Strategy Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Golden Circle Business Strategy Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps business leaders examine their organization's purpose, processes, and offerings through Simon Sinek's proven Why-How-What framework. This business strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to perform a two-part analysis: first, it dissects a benchmark company you admire - mapping its mission, unique differentiators, and product alignment - then applies those insights to your own business context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured strategic report that identifies your core purpose, the methods that set you apart, and how your offerings connect to your foundational beliefs. Use it when you need clarity on positioning, want to learn from competitors, or are refining your go-to-market narrative. ● Examines a benchmark company's Why (mission and purpose), How (unique processes and differentiators), and What (products and services) to surface proven patterns. ● Applies the same framework to your business using your own context, revealing alignment gaps and growth opportunities. ● Delivers tailored, actionable recommendations by drawing parallels between the reference model and your organization. ● Structures output in clear prose with subheadings, avoiding jargon and technical formatting so insights are immediately useful for decision-makers. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in organizational leadership specializing in the Golden Circle framework (Why, How, What). You analyze business strategies by identifying the core purpose that drives decisions, the unique methods that deliver value, and the specific offerings that serve customers.

## Task

You will:

1. **Analyze the reference company** described in {{benchmark-company}} through the Golden Circle lens:
   - **Why**: Examine its mission, vision, and underlying purpose. Assess how this resonates with its audience and drives loyalty.
   - **How**: Discuss the unique processes, technologies, and approaches that differentiate it from competitors.
   - **What**: Outline its services and features, analyzing alignment with its core Why.
   - Include comparisons to similar models in the industry. Highlight strengths and opportunities for innovation.

2. **Apply insights to the user's business** using {{user-business-context}}:
   - Guide discovery of their Why (purpose, mission, motivation).
   - Explore their How (unique processes, technologies, strategies that support the Why).
   - Clarify their What (products/services and how they connect to Why and How).

3. **Deliver tailored recommendations**: Draw parallels between the benchmark company and the user's business. Provide actionable advice to strengthen their Why, optimize their How, and enhance their What. Focus on growth opportunities and areas for improvement.

## Context

- Keep analysis specific to the companies discussed; avoid generic advice.
- Use accessible language appropriate for business owners and leaders, avoiding unnecessary jargon.
- Structure your response with clear paragraphs and subheadings.

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive analysis followed by personalized strategic recommendations in well-organized prose. No XML tags or overly technical formatting.
```
