# Vehicle Action Shot Image Prompt

## 簡介

The Vehicle Action Shot Image Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic low-angle hero shots of vehicles slamming onto tracks with dramatic compression, dust, and motion effects for photographers, marketers, and content creators. This vehicle action shot prompt for Midjourney produces dynamic landing scenes with rear-tire-first impacts, compressed shock-absorbers, rising dust clouds, and cheering crowds under stadium lighting. It captures motion through frozen tire spray and wheel blur, uses 24mm wide-angle perspective with shallow depth of field, and applies golden hour color grading for cinematic sports photography aesthetics. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, delivering high-detail images with customizable vehicle types and dramatic action cues. Designers use it for automotive marketing materials, social media content, and concept visualization that demands high-energy visual storytelling. Reach for this prompt when you need hero imagery that conveys speed, power, and precision without a live shoot or 3D rendering pipeline. ● Low-angle perspective with rear-tire-first landing, compressed suspension, and rising dust clouds for dramatic impact ● Motion effects including wheel blur and frozen tire spray that convey speed and kinetic energy ● Cinematic lighting setup with golden hour glow and stadium atmosphere, shallow depth of field isolating the subject ● 16:9 aspect ratio, 24mm wide-angle lens simulation, and raw style rendering for professional-grade output ## Prompt

```
Low-angle hero shot of a {{vehicle}} slamming onto the track, rear-tires-first landing; shock-absorbers compressing, dust cloud rising as the crowd cheers under dramatic stadium lighting. Cinematic sports photography style, motion blur on wheels, tire spray frozen mid-air, 24mm wide-angle, shallow depth of field isolating the vehicle, golden hour glow. --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250 --q 2
```
