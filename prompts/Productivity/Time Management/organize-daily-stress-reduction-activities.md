# Daily Stress-Reduction Activity Planner for Work

## 簡介

The Daily Stress-Reduction Activity Planner for Work is a free AI prompt that creates a customized schedule of practical stress management activities tailored to your professional routine and work environment. This stress-reduction prompt for ChatGPT takes your work context and builds a structured framework of twelve specific activities distributed across four time periods: morning, midday, afternoon, and evening. Each activity includes implementation instructions and the research-backed reason it reduces stress, ensuring every recommendation is both actionable and effective. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for busy professionals who need time-efficient techniques that fit naturally into demanding schedules. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-based wellness strategies that integrate into your actual workday without disrupting productivity or requiring special equipment. ● Delivers three specific activities for each of four daily time blocks, totaling twelve personalized stress-reduction techniques ● Prioritizes time efficiency and ease of integration so activities fit realistic professional schedules ● Grounds every recommendation in stress management research and explains both how to do it and why it works ● Adapts to your specific work environment, profession, and typical schedule through the work-context variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a wellness expert specializing in workplace stress management and evidence-based techniques that integrate seamlessly into professional routines.

## Task
Create a personalized framework of practical stress-reducing activities tailored to the user's workday. Organize recommendations by four time periods (morning, midday, afternoon, evening), providing three specific activities for each.

## Context
{{work-context}}

Consider:
- Ease of integration into the described work environment
- Time efficiency (activities realistic for a working professional)
- Proven effectiveness backed by stress management research
- Compatibility with the profession and typical schedule

## Output
Deliver as a bulleted list structured by time of day:

**Morning Activities**
- Activity 1
- Activity 2
- Activity 3

**Midday Activities**
- Activity 1
- Activity 2
- Activity 3

**Afternoon Activities**
- Activity 1
- Activity 2
- Activity 3

**Evening Activities**
- Activity 1
- Activity 2
- Activity 3

Each activity should include a brief description of how to perform it and why it reduces stress.
```
