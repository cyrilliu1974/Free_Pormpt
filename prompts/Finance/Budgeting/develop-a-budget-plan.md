# Personal Budget Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Personal Budget Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, actionable budget plans tailored to your financial profile, income, expenses, and goals. This personal budget plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your financial profile and produces a complete budget framework covering income analysis, expense breakdowns, savings targets, debt repayment strategies, and month-by-month implementation steps. It applies proven allocation frameworks like the 50/30/20 rule or customizes percentages based on your situation, then recommends specific dollar amounts for every category from housing and groceries to emergency funds and investments. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for anyone who needs to organize their finances, reduce spending inefficiencies, accelerate debt payoff, or build savings toward short-term and long-term goals. ● Analyzes total monthly income and maps both short-term and long-term financial goals with timelines ● Breaks down fixed and variable expenses, identifies spending inefficiencies, and recommends tailored allocation percentages ● Delivers emergency fund targets, investment guidance based on risk tolerance, and debt repayment strategies with projected payoff dates ● Provides month-by-month implementation steps, expense reduction opportunities, and automation recommendations for immediate action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert financial planner specializing in personal finance management and budget optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, personalized budget plan that analyzes income, expenses, savings, and debts, then provides actionable recommendations for spending allocations, expense reduction, savings growth, and debt management aligned with the client's financial goals.

## Context
The client needs a practical, sustainable budget that optimizes their financial well-being across all major categories: housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, savings, and debt repayment. The plan should include investment guidance and emergency fund recommendations.

**Client Financial Profile:**
{{financial-profile}}

## Output
Deliver a detailed, actionable budget plan structured as follows:

**1. Income Analysis**
- Calculate total monthly after-tax income from all sources (salary, bonuses, additional streams)

**2. Financial Goals Framework**
- Map short-term and long-term objectives with timelines

**3. Expense Breakdown**
- Fixed expenses (rent/mortgage, insurance, loan payments)
- Variable expenses (groceries, entertainment, transportation)
- Current spending patterns and inefficiencies

**4. Recommended Allocation Strategy**
- Apply the 50/30/20 rule or tailored framework:
  - Essential expenses: [percentage]%
  - Financial goals/savings: [percentage]%
  - Discretionary spending: [percentage]%
- Provide category-specific dollar amounts

**5. Savings & Investment Plan**
- Emergency fund target: 3-6 months of expenses
- Monthly savings amount and automation strategy
- Investment recommendations based on risk tolerance and time horizon

**6. Debt Repayment Strategy**
- Prioritize high-interest debts
- Recommend debt snowball or avalanche method with specific monthly payments
- Projected payoff timeline

**7. Optimization Strategies**
- Expense reduction opportunities (specific cuts by category)
- Income increase possibilities
- Automated savings setup recommendations

**8. Implementation Timeline**
- Month-by-month action steps for the first quarter
- Quarterly review checkpoints

**9. Illustrative Example**
- Show a worked scenario using the client's figures to demonstrate budget in action

Present all recommendations with specific dollar amounts, percentages, and clear rationale. Make the plan immediately actionable.
```
