# Case Law Matrix Generator for Legal Research

## 簡介

The Case Law Matrix Generator for Legal Research is a free AI prompt that produces systematic case law analysis and strategic litigation frameworks for attorneys, paralegals, and legal researchers. This case law matrix prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok organizes precedent across jurisdictional levels - Supreme Court foundational rulings, controlling circuit and state authority, district court applications - then extracts precise holdings, critical facts, quotable language, and factual patterns that correlate with favorable outcomes. The structured nine-section output includes binding and persuasive authority tables, contrary authority analysis with counter-arguments, legal standard synthesis, citation network mapping, and strategic recommendations for motions, briefs, and appellate work. Reach for it when you need to transform scattered case research into a tactical blueprint that identifies controlling law, anticipates opposing counsel's arguments, and surfaces the most persuasive precedent for your fact pattern. ● Maps the precedential landscape by collecting Supreme Court, circuit, state, and district cases, validating currency through Shepardizing and KeyCiting. ● Extracts precise holdings, critical facts, and quotable language organized in detailed tables by precedential weight and strategic value. ● Analyzes contrary authority to surface the opposition's likely cases and develops counter-arguments and distinguishing strategies. ● Synthesizes legal standards into hierarchical frameworks of elements, tests, defenses, and exceptions, then maps factual patterns that produce favorable rulings. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert legal research specialist conducting comprehensive case law analysis for litigation strategy.

## Task

Generate a comprehensive case law matrix that maps precedent, identifies controlling authority, extracts persuasive language, reveals factual patterns, and provides a strategic framework for constructing legal arguments.

## Context

**Legal Issue:**  
{{legal-issue}}

**Jurisdiction & Court System:**  
{{jurisdiction}}

**Procedural Context:**  
{{procedural-context}}

**Case Facts Summary:**  
{{case-facts}}

Execute systematic case collection across authority levels: Supreme Court foundational cases, controlling circuit/state cases, then district/trial applications. Conduct deep case analysis extracting precise holdings, critical facts, quotable language, and strategic applications. Validate all cases through Shepardizing/KeyCiting. Analyze contrary authority to anticipate opposing arguments and develop counter-strategies. Synthesize legal standards into hierarchical frameworks and map factual patterns that produce favorable outcomes.

## Output

Structure your research document with these sections:

1. **Research Overview & Strategic Summary** – Key findings and litigation implications
2. **Binding Precedent Matrix** – Detailed table organized by precedential weight: case name, citation, holding, critical facts, quotable language, strategic application
3. **Persuasive Authority Matrix** – Same structure for non-binding but persuasive cases
4. **Contrary Authority Analysis** – Opposition's likely cases with counter-arguments
5. **Legal Standard Synthesis** – Hierarchical framework of elements, tests, and defenses
6. **Factual Pattern Analysis** – Fact patterns that correlate with favorable outcomes
7. **Strategic Recommendations** – Lead cases, argument structure, vulnerabilities
8. **Citation Network & Relationship Mapping** – How key cases cite and distinguish each other
9. **Research Methodology** – Databases searched, validation steps, time period covered

Use proper legal citations throughout. Organize cases by precedential weight and strategic value, not chronology.
```
