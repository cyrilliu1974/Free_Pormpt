# Co-Branded Marketing Campaign Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Co-Branded Marketing Campaign Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured partnership proposals for businesses exploring joint marketing initiatives. This co-branded marketing prompt for ChatGPT builds a professional proposal document with nine core sections: executive summary, introduction, partner overview, target audience analysis, three distinct campaign strategies with implementation plans, measurable KPIs, project timeline, budget breakdown, and expected outcomes for both parties. The prompt uses dependency grammar principles to ensure each section flows logically and connections between partner strengths, audience needs, and strategic recommendations remain explicit. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketing directors, partnership managers, and business development professionals use it to formalize collaboration opportunities, pitch co-marketing initiatives to potential partners, and align internal stakeholders around joint campaign structures. ● Analyzes shared target audiences by demographics, psychographics, pain points, and campaign impact potential ● Develops three campaign strategies, each with a rationale and step-by-step implementation plan ● Defines 3–5 measurable KPIs with specific targets, plus a timeline with key milestones and dates ● Produces an itemized budget breakdown across multiple cost categories and articulates concrete outcomes for both partners ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business proposal writer specializing in co-branded marketing campaigns. Structure your writing using dependency grammar principles—organize sentences so that relationships between ideas are immediately clear, with each clause flowing logically from the previous one.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive co-branded marketing campaign proposal that demonstrates mutual value, strategic alignment, and clear implementation pathways for both partners.

## Context
Partner business and collaboration details:
{{partner-business-context}}

## Output
Structure the proposal with these sections:

**Executive Summary**

**Introduction**
- Overview of the proposed co-branded marketing campaign
- Objectives and goals of the campaign

**Partner Business Overview**
- Brief introduction to the partner business
- Relevant strengths and market position

**Target Audience Analysis**
- Demographics and psychographics of the shared target audience
- Pain points and needs
- Potential impact of the co-branded campaign

**Campaign Strategies**
Present 3 strategies, each with:
1. Description
2. Rationale
3. Implementation plan

**Campaign Metrics and KPIs**
Define 3–5 measurable KPIs with specific targets

**Timeline and Milestones**
Outline key milestones with target dates

**Budget and Resource Allocation**
- Total campaign budget
- Itemized cost breakdown (minimum 3 categories)

**Expected Outcomes and Mutual Benefits**
Articulate 3+ concrete outcomes for both partners

**Conclusion**
- Reaffirm the value proposition
- Clear call-to-action for partnership

Use headings, bullet points, and numbered lists throughout. Write in clear, professional business language. Ensure each section builds logically on the previous one, with explicit connections between partner strengths, audience needs, and campaign strategies.
```
