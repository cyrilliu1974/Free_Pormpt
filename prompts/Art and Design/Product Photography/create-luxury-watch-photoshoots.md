# Luxury Watch Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Luxury Watch Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates commercial-quality studio shots of luxury watches with dramatic lighting and premium advertisement styling for marketers, photographers, and eCommerce teams. This luxury watch photography prompt for Midjourney produces top-down macro shots on wet black marble surfaces with crisp edge reflections, specular highlights, and softbox lighting that mimics professional studio setups. It outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images optimized for social media, print ads, and product catalogs, and works across Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar text-to-image models. The prompt specifies a 100mm f/11 macro lens perspective and premium advertisement mood to deliver polished, high-end visuals suitable for watch brands, jewelry retailers, and luxury goods marketing. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-quality product shots without a physical studio or when prototyping campaign concepts for high-value accessories. ● Produces commercial product shots with wet black marble, dramatic top-down softbox lighting, and accurate metallic reflections. ● Outputs 4:5 aspect ratio images at quality setting 2, ideal for Instagram, Pinterest, and print advertisements. ● Specifies 100mm f/11 macro lens characteristics for realistic depth-of-field and edge sharpness. ● Adaptable to any luxury product by changing the {{product}} variable - watches, jewelry, pens, or other high-end accessories. ## Prompt

```
Commercial product shoot of a {{product}} on wet black marble surface, dramatic top-down softbox lighting creating crisp edge reflections and specular highlights, 100mm f/11 macro lens, premium advertisement mood, studio photography --ar 4:5 --quality 2
```
