# Partnership Negotiation Strategy Framework

## 簡介

The Partnership Negotiation Strategy Framework is a free AI prompt that creates structured, step-by-step negotiation plans for business professionals pursuing strategic partnerships across any industry. This partnership negotiation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through all seven critical stages - preparation, initial contact, exploratory meetings, proposal development, negotiation, agreement finalization, and ongoing management - with concrete action items and key considerations at each phase. It addresses essential elements including mutual benefits, resource allocation, intellectual property rights, revenue sharing, and conflict resolution strategies, all customized to your specific partner business and industry context. Runs seamlessly on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a comprehensive roadmap to approach partnership discussions with clarity, structure, and strategic insight, whether you're negotiating technology collaborations, distribution agreements, joint ventures, or cross-industry alliances. ● Breaks down the negotiation process into seven manageable stages with actionable steps ● Addresses critical deal points including IP rights, revenue models, and resource commitments ● Provides industry-tailored and partner-specific strategic recommendations ● Includes 6-8 curated best practices for successful partnership establishment ## Prompt
