# Graphic Design Strategy for Digital Products

## 簡介

The Graphic Design Strategy for Digital Products is a free AI prompt that creates detailed visual design strategies and concept directions for digital products aimed at solopreneur audiences. This graphic design prompt for ChatGPT walks through a seven-step process that analyzes product features, applies color psychology, recommends typography, suggests imagery and visual metaphors, develops cohesive design systems, and generates multiple concept directions with detailed rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is particularly useful for designers creating landing page visuals, course materials, software interfaces, or marketing assets where the audience is time-strapped solo business owners. The prompt produces actionable design recommendations tied directly to brand identity and audience pain points. ● Analyzes how product features solve specific solopreneur challenges and translates them into visual direction ● Applies color psychology and typography principles tailored to balance professionalism with approachability ● Develops 3-5 distinct design concept directions, each with detailed rationale tied to value propositions ● Provides actionable execution guidance for each design decision, not just abstract theory ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer specializing in digital product visuals for solopreneur audiences.

## Task
Create a comprehensive design strategy for digital product graphics that resonate with solopreneurs. Deliver a step-by-step process with specific design recommendations and rationale for each decision.

## Context
Product and brand details:
{{product-and-brand}}
(Include: product name, description, key features, primary value proposition, brand colors, tone, and visual style)

Target solopreneur audience:
{{audience-profile}}
(Include: demographic details, business context, pain points, and preferences)

## Process
1. Analyze the product's key features and how they solve solopreneur challenges
2. Apply color psychology principles based on the brand identity and audience expectations
3. Recommend typography choices that balance professionalism with approachability
4. Suggest imagery and visual metaphors that communicate value to time-strapped solo business owners
5. Develop a cohesive design system aligned with the brand identity
6. Create 3-5 distinct design concept directions with rationale
7. Refine concepts based on the unique selling points and audience pain points

## Output
Provide your response as a detailed, numbered step-by-step design process. For each step, include:
- Specific design recommendations
- Rationale tied to the product features and solopreneur audience needs
- Actionable guidance for execution
```
