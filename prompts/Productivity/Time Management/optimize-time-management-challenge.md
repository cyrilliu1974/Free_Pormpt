# Time Multiplier Challenge Builder for Productivity

## 簡介

The Time Multiplier Challenge Builder for Productivity is a free AI prompt that creates structured, gamified time management challenges for individuals seeking to optimize how they spend their hours. This time management prompt for ChatGPT designs a complete three-level challenge system that systematically walks participants through auditing their current time allocation, spotting activities that drain productivity, and swapping them for higher-value tasks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking a single participant-context variable to tailor the challenge to specific roles, schedules, or goals. Use this prompt when you need a ready-made productivity framework that motivates through points, levels, and rewards rather than abstract advice. ● Outputs three progressive levels, each with a clear objective, three actionable tasks, and a transparent points-based scoring system with three measurable criteria. ● Includes a rewards section with three achievement tiers tied to total points earned, plus three curated resources to support participants. ● Keeps tasks practical and accessible, avoiding jargon while maintaining a motivating structure that encourages consistent progress. ● Adapts to any participant context - remote workers, students, managers, freelancers - through a single customizable variable. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a gamification designer specializing in productivity and time management challenges.

## Task
Design a three-level "Time Multiplier" challenge that guides participants through auditing their time usage, identifying low-value activities, and replacing them with high-impact work.

## Context
{{participant-context}}

## Challenge Requirements
- Structure the challenge as three progressive levels, each building on the previous one
- Each level must include: a descriptive name, clear objective, 3 specific tasks, and a points-based scoring system with 3 criteria
- End with a rewards section (3 tiers based on points earned) and 3 supporting resources
- Keep tasks practical and accessible; avoid overly complex terminology
- Ensure the scoring system is clear, fair, and motivating
```
