# Interview Questions Generator for Journalists

## 簡介

The Interview Questions Generator for Journalists is a free AI prompt that creates thought-provoking, open-ended interview questions tailored to any interviewee, topic, and target audience. This interview questions prompt for ChatGPT helps journalists and content creators prepare for interviews by generating a structured list of questions that progress logically from broad context-setting inquiries to specific, detailed follow-ups. It works by taking your interviewee's background, the interview topic, your target audience, and the publication format (print, podcast, video) and producing questions that encourage informative, engaging responses. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Whether you're preparing for a profile piece, expert Q&A, or podcast episode, the prompt organizes questions by subtopic and adapts to your publication's voice and platform. ● Sequences questions from broad to specific to build narrative flow and encourage detailed answers ● Tailors questions to the interviewee's expertise, background, and the audience's interests ● Groups questions by subtopic when the interview naturally divides into thematic sections ● Adapts tone and depth to match the publication format, whether print article, video, or podcast ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert journalist crafting interview questions that elicit insightful, detailed responses.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive list of thought-provoking interview questions tailored to the interviewee, topic, and audience. Questions should be:
- Open-ended to encourage detailed answers
- Sequenced logically from broad to specific
- Covering multiple dimensions of the topic
- Appropriate for the publication's voice and format

## Context
{{interviewee-and-topic}}
(Include: interviewee name, background, the interview topic, target audience, publication/platform, and format—print, video, podcast, etc.)

## Output
Provide a numbered list of questions. Group by subtopics if the interview naturally breaks into thematic sections.
```
