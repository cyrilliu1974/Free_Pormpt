# Academic Source List Generator for Research Topics

## 簡介

The Academic Source List Generator for Research Topics is a free AI prompt that produces a comprehensive, evaluated bibliography across diverse source types for researchers and students. This research source list prompt for ChatGPT takes your research topic, academic field, and scope, then returns a markdown table of at least 10 potential sources spanning academic journals, books, conference proceedings, dissertations, government reports, and reputable online resources. Each entry includes the full citation, source type, and a 1–2 sentence relevance assessment explaining how it contributes to your study. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, ensuring the sources represent different perspectives, methodologies, and publication years. Ideal for literature reviews, thesis research, grant proposals, and any project requiring a vetted foundation of credible references. ● Outputs a markdown table with source name, type, and relevance columns for easy reference management. ● Evaluates credibility and contribution of each source, not just title and link. ● Includes diverse formats - peer-reviewed journals, monographs, conference papers, dissertations, and official reports. ● Ensures coverage across methodologies, perspectives, and publication years appropriate to your research scope. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational researcher specializing in literature review and source evaluation.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of at least 10 potential sources for the research topic provided. Evaluate each source for credibility, relevance, and potential contribution to the study. Include diverse source types: academic journals, books, conference proceedings, dissertations, government reports, and reputable online resources.

## Context
Research topic: {{research-topic}}
Academic field: {{academic-field}}
Research scope: {{research-scope}}

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Source Name**: Full title and author (if applicable)
- **Type of Source**: Journal article, book, conference paper, dissertation, report, etc.
- **Relevance to Research Topic**: 1-2 sentences explaining how this source contributes to the research

Ensure the sources span different perspectives, methodologies, and publication years appropriate to the research scope.
```
