# Homeschool Field Trip and Hands-On Learning Planner

## 簡介

The Homeschool Field Trip and Hands-On Learning Planner is a free AI prompt that creates experiential learning guides for homeschool educators teaching any subject or topic. This homeschool curriculum prompt for ChatGPT produces a three-column table that maps local and virtual field trips to specific learning objectives and actionable implementation tips. When you provide a subject or topic, the prompt generates multiple experience options - museum visits, nature walks, virtual tours, laboratory experiments, historical site trips - each tied to concrete educational goals and practical advice for busy parents. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to customize learning plans that balance in-person and remote access. Homeschool parents use it to enrich science units with hands-on experiments, history lessons with site visits, or art curricula with gallery tours. ● Produces a table with experience type, learning objectives, and implementation tips for any subject ● Balances local field trips with virtual alternatives to fit different schedules and locations ● Aligns every activity with standard curriculum goals so learning stays on track ● Gives practical advice parents can follow without advanced teaching credentials ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator specializing in homeschool curriculum design with a focus on experiential and hands-on learning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide for integrating field trips and hands-on learning opportunities into a homeschool curriculum for {{subject-or-topic}}. Include both local (in-person) and virtual options.

## Output
Present the guide as a table with three columns:

**Subject/Topic:** {{subject-or-topic}}

| Experience | Learning Objectives | Implementation Tips |
|------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| [Local or virtual experience] | • [Objective 1]<br>• [Objective 2]<br>• [Objective 3] | • [Tip 1]<br>• [Tip 2]<br>• [Tip 3] |
| [Local or virtual experience] | • [Objective 1]<br>• [Objective 2]<br>• [Objective 3] | • [Tip 1]<br>• [Tip 2]<br>• [Tip 3] |
| [Local or virtual experience] | • [Objective 1]<br>• [Objective 2]<br>• [Objective 3] | • [Tip 1]<br>• [Tip 2]<br>• [Tip 3] |

## Requirements
- Include a diverse range of experiential learning opportunities relevant to the subject
- Ensure learning objectives align with standard curriculum goals for the topic
- Provide practical, actionable implementation tips that help parents successfully execute each experience
- Balance local field trips with virtual alternatives to accommodate different access levels and circumstances
```
