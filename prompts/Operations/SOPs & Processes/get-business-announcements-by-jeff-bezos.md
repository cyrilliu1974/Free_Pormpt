# Jeff Bezos-Style Business Announcement Generator

## 簡介

The Jeff Bezos-Style Business Announcement Generator is a free AI prompt that produces internal leadership emails communicating significant business changes with the clarity, conviction, and customer-first thinking associated with top-tier executives. This business announcement prompt for ChatGPT walks you through a proven structure: subject line, context, decision statement, strategic rationale, customer impact, vision alignment, and call to action. You supply the business change, and the prompt delivers a complete email in an authoritative yet accessible tone that unifies teams around strategic decisions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable to your preferred text model. Use it when rolling out product pivots, organizational restructures, market-entry decisions, or any move that requires company-wide alignment and understanding. ● Structures announcements with context, rationale, customer benefit, and vision alignment in a repeatable format ● Encourages customer-centric framing that connects internal decisions to external impact ● Provides a confident, inclusive tone that builds team trust and alignment ● Includes a call-to-action section to translate strategy into concrete next steps for employees ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business leader crafting an internal company announcement. Write with clarity, conviction, and a customer-centric perspective that inspires confidence and alignment.

## Task
Write an internal announcement about a significant business change or development. Structure it as an email from leadership to the entire team.

## Context
The announcement addresses: {{business-change}}

Articulate the strategic rationale, customer impact, and alignment with long-term vision. Use an authoritative yet accessible tone that unifies the team around the decision.

## Output
Format as:

**Subject:** [compelling title for the announcement]

Team,

**Context:** [situational background that frames why this matters now]

**Decision:** [what we are doing, stated clearly]

**Strategic Rationale:** [the business logic and competitive reasoning]

**Customer Impact:** [how this serves customers better]

**Vision Alignment:** [why this fits our long-term direction]

**Call to Action:** [what the team should do or focus on next]

Sincerely,
[Leadership signature]

---

**Requirements:**
- Provide clear context for the change
- Explain strategic reasoning and competitive advantage
- Connect decision to customer benefit
- Show how it advances company vision
- Include actionable next steps for the team
- Maintain confident, inclusive tone throughout
```
