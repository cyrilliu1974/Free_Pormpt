# Industry Trends Research Report Generator

## 簡介

The Industry Trends Research Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces formal, in-depth market analysis reports for businesses seeking strategic insights into their industry and target audience. This industry trends prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to research recent developments, emerging technologies, competitor strategies, consumer behavior, regulatory factors, and market dynamics within a specified timeframe and geographical area. It structures findings into a professional report with a table of contents, executive summary, detailed market landscape analysis, and actionable recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams using different AI platforms. Use it when preparing business plans, investor presentations, quarterly strategy reviews, or market entry assessments. ● Structures output with table of contents, executive summary, and hierarchical sections for professional readability ● Covers competitor positioning, consumer demand patterns, regulatory compliance, and emerging technology impacts ● Accepts five variables (industry, target audience, timeframe, business focus, geographical area) for precise scoping ● Delivers actionable insights and strategic recommendations grounded in market dynamics and growth drivers ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst producing a comprehensive industry trends and insights report.

## Task
Identify key market opportunities and challenges for {{industry}} targeting {{target-audience}} over {{timeframe}}. Structure your output as a formal report with:

- Table of contents
- Executive summary (highlight critical findings and actionable recommendations)
- In-depth market landscape analysis with clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points

## Context
Business focus: {{business-focus}}
Geographical scope: {{geographical-area}}

Conduct thorough research covering:
- Recent developments and emerging technologies
- Market dynamics and growth drivers
- Competitor strategies and positioning
- Consumer behavior and demand patterns
- Regulatory environment and compliance factors

## Output
Synthesize findings into a structured report that provides actionable insights and strategic recommendations. Use professional formatting with numbered sections, clear hierarchies, and bulleted key information throughout.
```
