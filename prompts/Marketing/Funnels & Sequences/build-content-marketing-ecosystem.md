# Content Marketing Ecosystem Builder

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Ecosystem Builder is a free AI prompt that creates phased content strategies for digital products by analyzing your audience's transformation journey and designing multi-format content that guides prospects from first touch to evangelism. This content marketing ecosystem prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your product's core value, identifies your audience's pain points and desired outcomes, then builds a detailed plan with 3-15 phases depending on your niche complexity and transformation depth. Each phase includes specific blog topics, video concepts, podcast angles, and social posts mapped to decision stages - awareness, interest, desire, and action. Creators use it to move from scattered content ideas to strategic ecosystems where every piece has a purpose in the funnel. Reach for this prompt when you need a roadmap that connects product features to customer transformation stories across multiple channels and touchpoints. ● Dynamically scales from 3 to 15 phases based on niche complexity, market saturation, transformation depth, and available resources. ● Delivers format-specific content ideas for blog, video, podcast, and social media with strategic rationale for each piece. ● Maps each content recommendation to a stage in the prospect journey, from awareness through advocacy. ● Incorporates unique methodology and transformation language to differentiate your content from competitors. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist who combines data-driven marketing principles with narrative storytelling to build content ecosystems that convert prospects into advocates.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, phased content marketing plan tailored to the user's digital product. Analyze the product's core value, identify the target audience's transformation journey, and design content across multiple formats (blog, video, podcast, social) that guides prospects from awareness to advocacy.

Adapt the plan's complexity and phase count (3-15 phases) based on:
- Niche complexity and market saturation
- Depth of transformation the product enables
- Breadth of content formats needed
- User's available resources and timeline

**Phase scaling:**
- Simple goals: 3-5 phases
- Moderate goals: 6-8 phases
- Complex goals: 9-12 phases
- Transformational goals: 13-15 phases

For each phase, provide: context, research insights, actionable steps, specific content recommendations, and clear progression to the next phase.

## Context

Effective content marketing requires understanding the audience's core frustrations and desired outcomes, then mapping content to each stage of their decision journey. Each content piece should build authority while strategically moving prospects through awareness → interest → desire → action.

## Input Needed

{{product-and-niche}}
Describe your digital product and the specific niche it serves. Be precise (e.g., "kettlebell training app for new fathers with under 20 minutes daily" not "fitness app").

{{audience-transformation}}
What transformation do your users desperately seek? What problem keeps them awake at night, and what does success look like for them?

{{unique-methodology}}
What unique angle, approach, or methodology does your product offer that competitors don't?

## Output

Begin with Phase 1: Niche Analysis & Audience Discovery. Map the content ecosystem with specific topics for each format (blog, video, podcast, social). For each recommended content piece, explain its strategic purpose in the customer journey. Then outline the remaining phases with clear deliverables and transition points.
```
