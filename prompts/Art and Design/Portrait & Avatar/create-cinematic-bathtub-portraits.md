# Cinematic Bathtub Portrait Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Cinematic Bathtub Portrait Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates hyper-realistic 8K portrait photography featuring subjects reclining in water-filled bathtubs with professional DSLR lighting and texture. This bathtub portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces magazine-quality images shot with simulated 85mm f/1.4 lens settings, complete with golden lighting reflections on water surfaces, realistic wet skin with subsurface scattering, and customizable floating elements like flowers or petals in coral, blush pink, and cream tones. The prompt controls bathroom aesthetics through the {{bathroom-style}} variable and surface decorations via {{floating-elements}}, allowing you to match brand palettes or creative direction while maintaining sharp focus on facial features and water reflections.
