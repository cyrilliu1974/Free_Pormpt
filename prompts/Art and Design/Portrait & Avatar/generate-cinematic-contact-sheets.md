# Cinematic Contact Sheet Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Contact Sheet Generator is a free AI prompt that produces authentic 3×3 film photography grids with luxury fashion and narrative cinematography progression for photographers, designers, and creative directors. This cinematic contact sheet prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates nine frames arranged in a 16:9 grid, each shot with a different focal length and framing - from extreme long shots to macro close-ups - all unified by dramatic rim lighting, film grain, and black-and-white or muted color film-stock emulation (Ilford Delta 3200, Kodak Vision3 500T). You customize the subject and environment variables, and the prompt delivers three narrative rows: isolation (wide establishing shots), engagement (medium character study), and intimacy (close-up texture and power dynamics). Use it for portfolio mockups, mood boards, brand visual stories, or fashion editorial concepts. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive, narrative-driven character study that looks like a professional film contact sheet without a physical shoot. ● Produces nine photorealistic frames in a single 3×3 grid, each with a specified lens (24mm, 50mm, 85mm, 100mm macro) and shot type (extreme long, close-up, low angle, high angle). ● Applies authentic film-stock rendering with grain, volumetric lighting, chiaroscuro contrast, and 8k skin texture detail. ● Guides narrative progression across three rows: isolation and environment, character engagement, then intimate texture and power dynamics. ● Fully customizable subject and environment variables let you adapt the contact sheet to any creative brief or brand identity. ## Prompt

```
Create a 3×3 contact sheet grid in 16:9 aspect ratio showcasing a cinematic character study with luxury fashion undertones. Shot on Ilford Delta 3200 (black & white) or Kodak Vision3 500T (muted color) film stock. Subject: {{subject}}. Environment: {{environment}}.

Lighting: Dramatic rim lighting to separate subject from background, soft fill on face, deep velvet shadows. All frames: photorealistic skin texture, volumetric lighting, film grain, 8k resolution, fashion photography aesthetic, sharp focus.

**Row 1 – The Isolation (Establishing the Atmosphere)**
24mm wide angle lens. Monochromatic or desaturated cold tones.

• Panel 1 (Extreme Long Shot): Subject stands as a silhouette against the dominant architectural feature. Background blurs. Emphasizes solitude and scale.
• Panel 2 (Long Shot): Full body profile view. Subject positioned near key furniture piece. Lighting catches defining texture of their most prominent feature. Elegant posture.
• Panel 3 (American Shot, 3/4 view): Framed from knees up. Subject turns slightly towards camera, engaged in subtle gesture or adjustment. Environment falls dark and out of focus.

**Row 2 – The Engagement (The Character Study)**
50mm prime lens. High contrast chiaroscuro.

• Panel 4 (Medium Shot): Waist up. Subject looking slightly off-camera. Wardrobe's defining silhouette line is prominent. Lighting highlights upper body structure.
• Panel 5 (Medium Close-Up): Chest up. Direct eye contact with lens. Unreadable, mysterious expression. Most striking facial feature is sharp and focal.
• Panel 6 (Close-Up): Face framing. Shallow depth of field (f/1.4). Environment dissolves into bokeh. Focus entirely on eyes and skin texture.

**Row 3 – The Intimacy (The Texture & Power)**
85mm & 100mm macro lens. Deep blacks and stark highlights.

• Panel 7 (Extreme Close-Up): Macro detail on subject's most striking cosmetic or textural feature and catchlight in eye. High-fashion detail texture.
• Panel 8 (Low Angle Power Shot): Looking up at subject. They look down imperiously. Lighting creates halo effect on hair or silhouette (rim light). Dominant presence.
• Panel 9 (High Angle Vulnerability): Looking down at seated subject. They look up through lashes. Angle emphasizes facial geometry and defining line of signature feature.
```
