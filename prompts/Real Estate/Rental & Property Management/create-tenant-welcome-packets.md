# Tenant Welcome Packet Generator

## 簡介

The Tenant Welcome Packet Generator is a free AI prompt that creates scannable, property-specific quick-start guides for landlords and property managers onboarding new tenants. This tenant welcome packet prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a print-ready 2-4 page document that prioritizes emergency information, property-specific details, and practical instructions tenants need in their first 30 days. Unlike lease agreements, the output focuses on preventing common maintenance calls and lease violations by answering everyday questions before they arise - from water shutoff valve locations and trash pickup schedules to appliance quirks and parking rules. Property managers use it to reduce repetitive calls, clarify utility responsibilities, and ensure critical safety information is immediately accessible. This prompt is for landlords, property managers, and rental operators who want to improve tenant onboarding, reduce confusion, and prevent avoidable emergencies through clear documentation. ● Prioritizes emergency contacts, shutoff valve locations, and safety procedures at the top for immediate accessibility. ● Generates property-specific instructions covering parking, trash schedules, appliance details, and maintenance request procedures. ● Formats content with clear headings, bullet points, and visual hierarchy so tenants find answers in seconds. ● Prevents common lease violations and maintenance calls by addressing guest policies, noise rules, and utility responsibilities upfront. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced property manager creating a quick-start guide that tenants will actually read and reference. Your goal is to prevent common maintenance calls, lease violations, and emergencies by providing scannable, property-specific information that answers questions before they arise.

## Task

Create a 2-4 page tenant welcome packet that serves as an essential quick-start guide for move-in. This document stands alone from the lease and focuses on practical information tenants need in their first 30 days.

## Context

{{property-details}}

The packet bridges the gap between legal lease language and daily living. Assume tenants will reference this document but may not fully read their lease. Prevent the most common and costly mistakes through clear, actionable information.

## Output

**Format:**
- Print-ready with clear headings, bullet points, and visual hierarchy
- Scannable—tenants find answers in seconds
- Emergency and safety information prominently at the top
- Table of contents if over two pages
- Adequate white space; avoid dense paragraphs
- Bold text for critical items and section headers

**Content Structure (by priority):**

**1. Emergency Information** (most prominent)
- Water and gas shutoff valve locations
- Emergency contacts and procedures
- Fire safety policies
- Who to call for what situation

**2. Critical Property-Specific Details**
- Parking rules and regulations
- Trash and recycling pickup schedules (specific days)
- Appliance instructions and quirks
- Spare key procedures and locations
- Utility responsibilities breakdown

**3. Key Policies** (prevent violations)
- Guest policies
- Pet policies
- Candle and fire safety rules
- Common area rules (if applicable)
- Noise and quiet hours

**4. Communication Framework**
- Landlord contact information
- Response hours and expectations
- Maintenance request procedures
- Rent payment details

**Writing Style:**
- Action-oriented: what tenants need to DO, not just know
- Specific: actual locations, days, phone numbers, unique features
- Concise: bullet points and short instructional statements
- Clear: avoid legal jargon or lease repetition
- Practical: information that prevents emergency calls and violations

Include only property-specific critical information that adds practical value. Omit anything obvious, universal, or that lengthens the document without preventing real problems.
```
