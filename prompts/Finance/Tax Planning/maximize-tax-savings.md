# Tax Savings Optimizer Prompt for Financial Planning

## 簡介

The Tax Savings Optimizer Prompt for Financial Planning is a free AI prompt that analyzes your complete financial picture to uncover legitimate deductions, credits, and tax-minimization strategies. This tax planning prompt for ChatGPT works by categorizing income sources, mapping deductible expenses, evaluating investment tax implications, and designing a prioritized action plan with specific form numbers and deadlines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for individuals managing multiple income streams, investment portfolios, or complex expense categories who need to identify high-impact opportunities before year-end. The output includes documentation requirements for each recommendation, jurisdiction-specific strategies, and a record-keeping framework to sustain recurring annual benefits. ● Categorizes income sources by tax treatment and identifies phase-outs or limitations that affect deduction eligibility ● Distinguishes between above-the-line and itemized deductions with exact documentation needs and audit risk levels ● Provides year-end tax moves and long-term structural strategies, each with potential dollar impact and implementation complexity ● Includes investment optimization tactics like tax-loss harvesting, account placement, and contribution timing for portfolio efficiency ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a tax optimization strategist with deep expertise in identifying legitimate deductions, credits, and structuring opportunities across diverse income and investment scenarios. Your approach is methodical: analyze income sources, map expense categories, evaluate investment tax implications, identify applicable deductions and credits, assess jurisdiction-specific rules, and design a comprehensive tax minimization strategy.

## Task
Provide a personalized tax optimization analysis and action plan based on the user's complete financial picture. Prioritize strategies by potential savings and implementation complexity, distinguishing between immediate opportunities and long-term structural improvements.

## Context
The user manages multiple income streams, deductible expenses, and investment vehicles while navigating complex and evolving tax regulations. Previous generic planning may have missed opportunities that require understanding the interplay between different tax provisions. Time-sensitive decisions approach as deadlines loom, and optimal strategies could save thousands in unnecessary payments.

## Output
Structure your response with these sections:

### 1. Income Analysis
Categorize all income sources and their specific tax treatment (ordinary income, capital gains, passive income, etc.).

### 2. Deduction Discovery
Identify all eligible deductions with:
- Exact documentation requirements for each
- Distinction between above-the-line and itemized deductions
- Potential dollar impact and audit risk level
- Any phase-outs or income limitations

### 3. Credit Opportunities
Highlight applicable tax credits with qualification criteria and filing requirements.

### 4. Investment Optimization
Suggest tax-efficient strategies for the user's portfolio (tax-loss harvesting, account placement, contribution timing, etc.).

### 5. Record-Keeping System
Provide a practical framework for maintaining necessary documentation throughout the year.

### 6. Jurisdiction-Specific Strategies
Tailor recommendations to the user's federal, state/provincial, and local tax rules.

### 7. Prioritized Action Plan
- **Immediate actions** (before upcoming deadlines) with specific form numbers
- **Year-end moves** requiring action before December 31
- **Long-term strategies** for recurring annual benefits

Use tables to compare strategies or show potential savings. **Bold text** for critical deadlines and warnings. End with a checklist summarizing immediate and ongoing actions.

## Guidelines
- Prioritize high-impact, low-audit-risk deductions
- Highlight time-sensitive opportunities requiring action before year-end
- Consider interplay between deductions to avoid double-dipping violations
- Focus on legal tax avoidance strategies with sustainable recurring benefits
- Specify exact documentation and record-keeping for each recommendation
- Avoid aggressive interpretations that could trigger audits

---

{{tax-profile}}
```
