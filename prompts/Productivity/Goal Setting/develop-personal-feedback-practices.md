# Personal Feedback Practices Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Personal Feedback Practices Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates customized guidance for seeking and incorporating feedback to accelerate personal growth. This feedback practices prompt for ChatGPT takes your current feedback experience and goals as input, then produces a structured set of 5-7 actionable best practices, each with specific implementation steps, real-world examples, and strategies for overcoming common obstacles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, drawing on evidence-based personal development principles to deliver concrete advice rather than generic platitudes. Whether you're new to feedback culture or looking to refine your approach, this prompt helps you turn feedback into measurable progress by addressing your specific experience level, growth objectives, and challenges with requesting or applying feedback in professional and personal contexts. ● Creates 5-7 specific best practices customized to your feedback experience level and growth goals ● Includes concrete implementation steps and real-world examples for each practice ● Addresses common challenges in seeking, receiving, and applying feedback effectively ● Structures output with introduction, numbered practices, and actionable conclusion for easy implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in personal development specializing in feedback culture and behavior change.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive set of best practices for seeking and incorporating feedback for personal growth. Present actionable guidance tailored to the user's current situation.

## Context
User's feedback experience and goals: {{feedback-context}}

Provide best practices that address their specific experience level, growth goals, and challenges with seeking or applying feedback.

## Output
Structure your response as:

**Introduction**  
Brief explanation of why feedback is essential for personal growth.

**Best Practices** (5-7 numbered items)  
For each practice:
- Clear, specific actions to implement it
- Concrete example illustrating the practice in action
- How it addresses common challenges

**Conclusion**  
Summarize key takeaways and provide encouragement for consistent application.

Make every practice actionable and specific. Avoid generic advice. Draw on evidence-based personal development principles.
```
