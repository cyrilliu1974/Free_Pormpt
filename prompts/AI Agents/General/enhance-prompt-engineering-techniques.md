# Prompt Engineering Enhancement Framework

## 簡介

The Prompt Engineering Enhancement Framework is a free AI prompt that analyzes and transforms basic prompts into multiple optimized versions for professionals who need to improve their AI interactions. This prompt engineering enhancement prompt for ChatGPT works by first analyzing your original prompt across four dimensions - content overview, niche context, target audience, and content goals - then generating five distinct adaptations, each exploring a different improvement strategy such as clearer structure, better constraints, domain-specific terminology, more effective framing, or advanced optimization techniques. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it suitable for teams that need systematic prompt refinement across platforms. Use it when you have a working prompt that needs to be sharpened for production use, when onboarding team members to prompt best practices, or when you need multiple approaches to solve the same problem. ● Analyzes the original prompt across four critical dimensions to surface hidden weaknesses and optimization opportunities ● Produces five distinct enhanced versions, each exploring a different improvement strategy rather than a single rewrite ● Documents three specific improvements per adaptation so you understand exactly what changed and why ● Applies structured frameworks and domain knowledge to ensure enhancements align with your target audience and content goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert prompt engineer who analyzes and improves prompts by applying structured frameworks, domain knowledge, and optimization principles.

## Task
Analyze the provided prompt and generate five distinct enhanced versions, each exploring a different improvement strategy.

## Process

**Step 1: Analyze the Original**

Examine {{basic-prompt}} and document:
- **Content Overview**: What the prompt asks for and its core intent
- **Niche Context**: The domain, use case, or specialized knowledge required
- **Target Audience**: Who will use the output and their expertise level
- **Content Goals**: The desired outcome and success criteria

**Step 2: Generate Five Adaptations**

For each adaptation, provide:
- **Headline**: A descriptive title for the enhancement approach
- **Three Key Improvements**: Specific changes that strengthen the prompt through clearer structure, better constraints, domain-specific terminology, more effective framing, or optimization techniques
```
