# Market Risk Assessment Prompt for Business Planning

## 簡介

The Market Risk Assessment Prompt for Business Planning is a free AI prompt that produces a structured risk management plan tailored to any business context. Built for risk management consultants, founders, and strategists, this market risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT analyzes vulnerabilities across financial, operational, strategic, and compliance domains, then delivers at least 10 prioritized risks in a clear markdown table with likelihood ratings and actionable mitigation strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your business size, industry, target market, and risk tolerance. Reach for this prompt when launching a new venture, entering unfamiliar markets, or auditing an existing business for hidden threats. It combines systematic market analysis with practical mitigation planning, so you move from awareness to action in a single workflow. ● Identifies financial, operational, strategic, and compliance risks relevant to your business context. ● Rates each threat by likelihood (Low, Medium, High) with supporting rationale. ● Proposes specific, actionable mitigation strategies for every risk identified. ● Outputs a clean markdown table that drops straight into reports, decks, or planning docs. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert risk management consultant specializing in market risk assessment and mitigation planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive risk management plan that identifies, analyzes, and prioritizes risks specific to the given business context. Evaluate each risk's likelihood and potential impact, then propose practical mitigation strategies.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Conduct a thorough market analysis covering financial, operational, strategic, and compliance risks. Consider both industry-specific threats and broader market vulnerabilities relevant to the business size, target market, and risk tolerance described above.

## Output
Present your assessment as a markdown table with three columns:

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|---------------------|

Provide at least 10 distinct risks, each with:
- **Risk**: Clear description of the threat
- **Likelihood**: Assessment (Low/Medium/High) with brief rationale
- **Mitigation Strategy**: Specific, actionable steps to reduce or manage the risk

Prioritize risks by combining likelihood and potential business impact.
```
