# Thought Leadership Podcast Launch Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Thought Leadership Podcast Launch Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a strategic roadmap for starting and growing an authority-building podcast in any industry. This podcast launch prompt for ChatGPT walks through eight core planning sections: podcast positioning and format, content architecture using dependency grammar principles, production workflow and quality standards, SEO-optimized show notes and transcripts, pre-launch strategy, multi-channel audience growth tactics, promotional content frameworks, and measurement systems for continuous improvement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to your specific industry expertise, target audience demographics, available resources, and podcast goals. Real use cases include subject-matter experts launching interview series, consultants building authority platforms, and agencies creating client podcast strategies. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, end-to-end plan rather than scattered advice - it delivers actionable steps tailored to your context, from recording setup through long-term growth tactics. ● Structures all episode content, show notes, and promotional material using dependency grammar for clarity and SEO performance ● Provides tailored production workflows, guest strategies, and distribution tactics based on your available time, budget, and team ● Delivers platform-specific promotional frameworks for social media, email, and content repurposing ● Includes measurement systems with KPIs, feedback loops, and optimization triggers to refine your approach over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert podcast producer and content strategist specializing in thought leadership programs.

## Task
Create a comprehensive launch and maintenance plan for a successful thought leadership podcast. The plan must establish authority in the target industry and provide a practical roadmap covering podcast creation, content planning, and audience growth strategies.

## Context
{{industry-and-expertise}}

{{target-audience}}

{{podcast-goals-and-resources}}

Structure all podcast episodes, show notes, and promotional content using dependency grammar principles—building each element around core concepts with supporting details branching logically from them.

## Output
Present the complete strategy as a detailed, numbered list with clear headings for each major section:

1. **Podcast Foundation** – concept, positioning, format
2. **Content Architecture** – episode structure using dependency grammar, series planning, evergreen vs. topical balance
3. **Production Workflow** – recording setup, editing pipeline, quality standards
4. **Show Notes & Transcripts** – SEO-optimized templates structured with dependency grammar
5. **Launch Strategy** – pre-launch content bank, release schedule, initial promotion
6. **Audience Growth** – distribution channels, cross-promotion tactics, guest strategy
7. **Promotional Content** – social media frameworks, email integration, repurposing methods
8. **Measurement & Iteration** – KPIs, feedback loops, optimization triggers

For each section, provide specific, actionable steps tailored to the industry, audience, expertise, goals, and resources provided.
```
