# Online Course Marketing Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Online Course Marketing Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a complete go-to-market plan for educators, creators, and digital marketers launching online courses. This online course marketing prompt for ChatGPT generates a structured strategy across four key channels: content marketing, email campaigns, social media promotion, and paid advertising. You provide your target audience, course topic, unique selling proposition, and budget constraints; the prompt returns specific tactics, actionable implementation steps, timeline recommendations, and measurable KPIs for each channel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it compatible with most modern text-generation models. Whether you're promoting a technical bootcamp, a creative workshop, or a business skills course, the prompt tailors every tactic to your audience's needs and your unique value proposition. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive, channel-spanning marketing plan that moves beyond generic advice and delivers implementation-ready campaigns aligned to your budget and timeline. ● Returns a bulleted, multi-channel strategy organized by Content Marketing, Email Campaigns, Social Media Promotion, and Paid Advertising. ● Tailors tactics and messaging to your specific target audience, course topic, and unique selling proposition. ● Includes timeline recommendations and KPIs for each channel so you can track performance and iterate. ● Delivers actionable implementation steps that fit within your stated budget and launch timeline. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in online course promotion and audience engagement strategies.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive marketing strategy that covers content marketing, email campaigns, social media promotion, and paid advertising. Each tactic should align with the target audience and course topic to maximize reach and conversion rates.

## Context
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Course topic:** {{course-topic}}
**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}
**Budget and timeline:** {{budget-and-timeline}}

The strategy must be actionable, realistic, and measurable.

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings for each marketing channel. For each channel, provide:
- Specific tactics tailored to the audience and course
- Actionable implementation steps
- Timeline recommendations
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) to track success

Format as a bulleted list organized by channel (Content Marketing, Email Campaigns, Social Media Promotion, Paid Advertising).
```
