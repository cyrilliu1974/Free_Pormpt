# Price Negotiation Strategy Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Price Negotiation Strategy Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive negotiation strategies for businesses seeking to maximize profitability while maintaining customer satisfaction. This price negotiation prompt for ChatGPT structures every component of a negotiation approach using dependency grammar, showing how pricing tiers, objection responses, concession strategies, and relationship tactics interconnect. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a bulleted framework that includes market analysis, value architecture, pricing frameworks with minimum and target ranges, objection management scripts, step-by-step negotiation processes, scenario-based dialogue templates, and post-negotiation follow-up actions. Sales teams, business owners, and account managers reach for this prompt when preparing for high-stakes pricing discussions or building repeatable negotiation playbooks. ● Maps competitive landscape and pricing benchmarks to establish defensible pricing ranges ● Generates tier-specific value propositions tied directly to differentiation points ● Provides scripted responses to anticipated objections with dependency links to minimum pricing thresholds ● Delivers dialogue templates and concession strategies that preserve customer relationships while protecting margins ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert price negotiation strategist developing a comprehensive negotiation framework that maximizes profitability while maintaining customer satisfaction.

## Task
Create a complete negotiation strategy covering market positioning, value communication, objection handling, and relationship management. Structure your response using dependency grammar to show how each negotiation element builds on and relates to others.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a bulleted list organized by dependency relationships that includes:

- **Market Analysis**: Current positioning, competitive landscape, and pricing benchmarks
- **Value Architecture**: Core value propositions, differentiation points, and tiered offering structure
- **Pricing Framework**: Minimum acceptable price, target range, and tier-specific value propositions
- **Objection Management**: Anticipated customer concerns with prepared responses
- **Negotiation Process**: Step-by-step flow including opening positions, concession strategy, and closing techniques
- **Scenario Scripts**: Dialogue templates for common negotiation situations
- **Relationship Preservation**: Tactics for maintaining rapport during price discussions
- **Post-Negotiation**: Follow-up actions to ensure satisfaction and retention

Clearly indicate dependencies (e.g., "concession strategy → depends on → minimum acceptable price and customer objection type").
```
