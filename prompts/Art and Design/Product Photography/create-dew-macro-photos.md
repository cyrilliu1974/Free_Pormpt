# Extreme Macro Photography Prompt for Product Shots

## 簡介

The Extreme Macro Photography Prompt for Product Shots is a free AI prompt that creates detailed close-up images with professional lens simulation for photographers, product marketers, and visual designers. This macro photography prompt for Midjourney replicates the look of a 90mm f/2.8 macro lens at 1:1 magnification, producing images with precise focus on a single subject element while rendering the background into smooth bokeh. You control three variables: the subject being photographed, the exact focal point where sharpness peaks, and the color palette for the out-of-focus background. Use it to capture dew on leaves, product textures, jewelry details, electronics close-ups, or any scenario where micro-detail matters. The prompt specifies diffused natural lighting and a 4:5 aspect ratio optimized for mobile and portrait compositions. Reach for this prompt when you need studio-quality macro images without physical camera gear, or when prototyping product photography concepts for e-commerce, editorial, or social media. ● Simulates 90mm f/2.8 macro lens behavior with 1:1 true-to-life magnification for authentic close-up detail. ● Lets you pinpoint the exact focal point for razor-sharp clarity while the rest melts into customizable bokeh. ● Outputs 4:5 portrait aspect ratio images at quality setting 2, ideal for mobile screens and vertical feeds. ● Works for nature subjects, product textures, jewelry, electronics, cosmetics, and any micro-detail scenario. ## Prompt

```
Extreme macro shot of {{subject}}, photographed with a 90mm f/2.8 macro lens, 1:1 magnification. Razor-sharp focus on {{focal-point}}, bokeh background of {{background-palette}}. Diffused natural light, microtexture perfection. --ar 4:5 --q 2
```
