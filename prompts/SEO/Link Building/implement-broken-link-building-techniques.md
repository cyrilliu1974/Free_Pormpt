# Broken Link Building Strategy Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Broken Link Building Strategy Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that creates actionable link acquisition plans for digital marketers and SEO professionals targeting high-quality backlinks through ethical outreach. This broken link building prompt for ChatGPT walks you through seven critical phases: discovering broken links on relevant websites in your niche, analyzing content gaps to understand what replacement assets will provide superior value, developing a content creation strategy aligned with your available resources, crafting persuasive outreach emails that emphasize mutual benefit, designing professional follow-up sequences, building long-term publisher relationships, and tracking success metrics including response rates and link conversion. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your website URL, niche industry, target websites, and content resources into a structured acquisition roadmap complete with email templates, timeline recommendations, and success benchmarks. Use this prompt when you need a systematic approach to link building that respects webmaster time while positioning your content as a genuine solution to broken user experiences. ● Provides technical methods and tools for identifying broken links on target websites, with prioritization criteria based on link authority and relevance ● Delivers persuasive outreach email templates structured for clarity, emphasizing how your replacement content benefits their audience and solves their broken link problem ● Includes follow-up sequences with professional timing recommendations that maintain relationship quality without being intrusive ● Offers relationship management strategies that turn single placements into ongoing partnerships, multiplying the long-term value of each outreach effort ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert link building strategist specializing in broken link building techniques to acquire high-quality backlinks through ethical outreach and relationship building.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive broken link building plan that identifies broken links on relevant websites, proposes valuable replacement content, and executes persuasive outreach. Maximize backlink acquisition success rates while maintaining ethical practices.

## Context
Website: {{website-url}}
Niche industry: {{niche-industry}}
Target websites for outreach: {{target-websites}}
Content creation capabilities: {{content-resources}}

## Process
For each phase, provide specific actions, tools, and templates:

1. **Broken Link Discovery**: Methods to identify broken links on target websites in the niche, including technical approaches and prioritization criteria
2. **Content Gap Analysis**: How to evaluate what content should replace each broken link, ensuring it provides equal or superior value
3. **Content Creation Strategy**: Production plan aligned with available content resources to create replacement assets
4. **Outreach Email Crafting**: Persuasive email templates structured for clarity and relationship building, emphasizing mutual value
5. **Follow-up Sequence**: Timing and messaging for follow-up communications that maintain professionalism
6. **Relationship Management**: Strategies to convert one-time link placements into ongoing partnerships
7. **Success Tracking**: Metrics and systems to measure response rates, conversion rates, and link acquisition

## Output
Deliver the plan as a numbered list with actionable steps for each phase. Include specific email templates, timeline recommendations, and success benchmarks.
```
