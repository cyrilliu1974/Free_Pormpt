# Cinematic Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces ultra-realistic full-body fashion photography with bold street-style aesthetics and professional cinematic lighting for brands, marketers, and content creators. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates editorial-quality images featuring subjects in modern streetwear against vibrant textured backgrounds. You control three core variables: wall color for the background, subject description for physical appearance and styling, and key garment to showcase specific clothing pieces. The prompt delivers sharp highlights, vivid color contrast, and photographic depth that mirrors high-end studio sessions. Use it for lookbooks, social media content, e-commerce product displays, or advertising campaigns that require polished fashion visuals without the cost of models, photographers, or location shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need confident, street-style fashion imagery that balances editorial polish with accessible modern energy. ● Produces full-body portraits with detailed textures, smooth gradients, and strong edge clarity that replicate professional photography. ● Customizable subject appearance, wardrobe elements, and background colors to align with brand identity and campaign themes. ● Delivers vivid, high-contrast images with bright cinematic lighting and sharp shadows ideal for digital and print use. ● Captures confident, relaxed posing and modern street-fashion mood suitable for youth-oriented and contemporary fashion brands. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic full-body cinematic fashion portrait outdoors against a vibrant {{wall-color}} textured wall. Bright cinematic lighting with sharp color contrast, crisp highlights and shadows, vivid modern fashion-photography mood. Bold, high-fashion street style atmosphere.

{{subject-description}}: clean modern haircut, trimmed beard, sunglasses. Standing confidently with one leg crossed over the other, both hands in pockets, relaxed and confident expression. Wearing {{key-garment}}, white t-shirt, black cargo pants, white sneakers.

Visual style: highly detailed textures, strong edge clarity, smooth cinematic gradients, photographic realism and depth. Bright, sharp, vivid tones with bold contrast. Editorial-grade quality.
```
