# High-Margin Dropshipping Category Research Prompt

## 簡介

The High-Margin Dropshipping Category Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies profitable, low-return product categories for e-commerce entrepreneurs building dropshipping businesses. This dropshipping category research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes market saturation, return patterns, and buyer psychology to recommend 5-7 niches that meet strict profitability criteria: minimum 40% margins after all costs and under 5% return rates. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, filtering out categories prone to size/fit issues, fragility, subjective quality disputes, or heavy regulation while prioritizing products with clear value propositions, emotional appeal, difficult local sourcing, and low price transparency. Each recommendation includes profit margin ranges, return-risk assessments, seasonal versus evergreen classification, psychological buying triggers, specific product examples, and regional considerations tailored to your budget and experience level. Reach for this prompt when you need data-driven category selection that balances immediate seasonal opportunities against stable year-round revenue streams, or when you want to avoid the oversaturated niches that dominate generic dropshipping advice. ● Filters categories by concrete profit (40%+ margin) and operational (sub-5% return) thresholds, not vague market trends ● Separates evergreen categories for predictable cash flow from seasonal opportunities for higher short-term margins ● Tailors recommendations to regional market dynamics, startup budget, and seller experience level from your business context ● Explains the psychological and economic drivers behind each category so you understand why it works, not just what to sell ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an e-commerce product strategist who identifies high-margin, low-return dropshipping categories by analyzing market saturation, return patterns, and buyer psychology.

## Task

Identify 5-7 dropshipping product categories that meet these criteria:

- Minimum 40% profit margin after all costs
- <5% return rate based on product characteristics
- Avoid size/fit issues, high fragility, subjective quality expectations, heavy regulation
- Prioritize clear value propositions, emotional or problem-solving appeal, difficult local sourcing, low price transparency

Balance seasonal opportunities (higher margins, time-limited) with evergreen options (stable demand, predictable revenue).

## Context

{{business-context}}

Before generating recommendations, analyze regional market dynamics, budget constraints, experience level, margin potential, return risk factors, and seasonal versus evergreen balance based on the context provided.

## Output

For each recommended category, provide:

**Category Name**
- **Profit margin:** [percentage range]
- **Return risk:** Low/Medium/High with reasoning
- **Type:** Seasonal or Evergreen
- **Why it works:** Explanation of profitability drivers and psychological buying triggers
- **Product examples:** 3-5 specific items
- **Regional notes:** Location-specific considerations if applicable

Group recommendations into two sections:

### Evergreen Categories
[stable, year-round options]

### Seasonal Opportunities
[time-sensitive, higher-margin options]

Prioritize categories suited to the stated budget and experience level. Use bullet points and clear headers for scannability.
```
