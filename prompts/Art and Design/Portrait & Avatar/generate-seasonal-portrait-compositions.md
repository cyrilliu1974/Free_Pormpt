# Seasonal Portrait Composition Generator

## 簡介

The Seasonal Portrait Composition Generator is a free AI prompt that creates outdoor portrait photos across spring, summer, fall, and winter while maintaining strict facial consistency and identity matching. This seasonal portrait prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and other image generators produces natural outdoor scenes with customizable seasonal elements - appropriate ground cover, trees, lighting, and color tones that shift with each season while keeping the subject's facial features, expression, and identity identical across all variations. You control the subject details and season, and the prompt ensures soft depth of field, natural daylight, and subtle seasonal color palettes that feel authentic. Use it to create brand imagery, social media content, portfolio pieces, or personal keepsakes that show the same person or scene across the calendar year. ● Preserves exact facial features, expressions, and identity across all seasonal variations with strict reference matching. ● Adapts background elements automatically - grass and greenery in spring, warm foliage in fall, light snow in winter. ● Applies season-appropriate natural lighting and color tones without manual editing or post-processing. ## Prompt
