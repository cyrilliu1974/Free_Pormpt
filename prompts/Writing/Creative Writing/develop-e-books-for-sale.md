# E-Book Outline and Content Framework Generator

## 簡介

The E-Book Outline and Content Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that produces detailed e-book structures with chapters, subheadings, and content guidance for solopreneurs and content creators building digital products. This e-book content prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's needs and pain points, then generates a logically sequenced outline where each chapter builds on the previous one. It includes formatting notes for digital reading, placement suggestions for visuals and case studies, and chapter summaries that guide readers toward practical mastery. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver outlines that incorporate engagement elements like checklists, quick-reference guides, and actionable exercises. Content creators use it to map out educational e-books, lead magnets, and paid digital products that establish authority in their niche. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform subject-matter expertise into a structured, sellable e-book but want a clear roadmap before writing. ● Analyzes audience pain points and goals to shape content that solves real problems ● Structures chapters with dependency logic so advanced concepts build on foundational ones ● Specifies where to place infographics, case studies, checklists, and interactive elements ● Includes formatting guidance for digital reading experience and scannable sections ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-book author and content strategist developing a digital product that establishes authority and generates passive income.

## Task
Create a comprehensive e-book outline and content framework on {{topic}} for {{target-audience}}. Structure the outline so each chapter builds logically on the previous one, guiding readers toward practical mastery.

## Content Requirements
- Analyze the target audience's core needs, pain points, and goals related to the topic
- Develop content that includes practical tips, real-world case studies, and actionable advice readers can implement immediately
- Incorporate engagement elements: infographics concepts, checklists, chapter summaries, and quick-reference guides
- Ensure clarity, coherence, and a strong value proposition throughout
- Include formatting notes for optimal digital reading (chapter breaks, visual hierarchy, scannable sections)

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output Format
Provide a structured outline using:
- **Part/Section headings** for major divisions
- **Chapter titles and numbers**
- **Subheadings** for key topics within chapters
- **Bullet points** detailing specific content, examples, and exercises for each section
- **Content notes** indicating where visuals, case studies, or interactive elements should appear

Ensure logical dependency between chapters—later content should reference and build upon earlier concepts.
```
