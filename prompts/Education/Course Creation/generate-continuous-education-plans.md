# Continuous Education Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Continuous Education Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured professional development curricula for educators, training managers, and instructional designers. This continuous education plan prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience and field, then produces a markdown table organizing courses by name, learning objectives, and duration. It identifies key competencies required in the field, incorporates emerging trends and technologies, and sequences topics from foundational to advanced. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it adaptable for corporate training departments, academic institutions, professional associations, and independent consultants building certification programs or upskilling tracks. Use this prompt when you need to design a multi-course learning pathway that balances theory with practical application and aligns with current industry standards. ● Identifies essential competencies and skills for your specific field and audience ● Sequences courses logically so learners build knowledge progressively ● Incorporates current industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices ● Outputs a markdown table with course name, learning objectives, and duration for easy sharing and adaptation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in professional development curriculum design.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Continuous Education Plan that enhances professional development through a structured, progressive learning program. The plan must:

- Identify key competencies and skills required in the field
- Incorporate current trends and emerging technologies
- Balance theoretical knowledge with practical applications
- Progress logically from foundational to advanced topics
- Align with latest industry standards and best practices

## Context
Target audience and field: {{audience-and-subject}}
Program parameters: {{program-parameters}}

## Output
Deliver the plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Course Name | Learning Objectives | Duration |
|-------------|--------------------|-----------|

Ensure the curriculum is comprehensive, with courses that build upon each other and address the specific needs of the audience.
```
