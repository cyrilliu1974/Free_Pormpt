# YouTube Video Script Generator

## 簡介

The YouTube Video Script Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, engagement-focused video scripts for content creators and marketers. This YouTube script prompt for ChatGPT takes a video brief - including topic, target audience, product details, brand voice, and desired length - and outputs a complete script divided into timestamped sections: an attention-grabbing introduction with a five-second hook, logically sequenced main content with transitions, a value-reinforcing conclusion, and a clear call-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering scripts optimized for spoken delivery rather than written prose. Creators use it to plan product launches, educational tutorials, brand storytelling, and promotional videos that convert viewers into subscribers or customers. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable structure that balances entertainment, information, and persuasion - whether you're a solo YouTuber, a marketing team scripting ad campaigns, or an agency producing client content at scale. ● Outputs scripts with exact timestamp breakdowns (intro, main content, conclusion, CTA) so editors can match visuals to narrative beats. ● Embeds best practices like five-second hooks and natural spoken transitions, ensuring the script sounds conversational on camera. ● Adapts tone and length to your brand voice and audience demographics by incorporating your video brief as context. ● Guides viewers toward a specific next step with strategically placed calls-to-action that include urgency or incentive language. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert YouTube content strategist specializing in scripts that drive engagement and conversions.

## Task
Write a compelling YouTube video script that captivates the target audience and effectively promotes the product or service. Structure the script with clear timestamps, an attention-grabbing introduction, informative main content, a persuasive conclusion, and a strong call-to-action.

## Context
{{video-brief}}
Include: topic and its relevance to the audience, product or service being promoted, target audience demographics and interests, brand voice and tone, and desired video length.

## Output
Deliver the script in this format:

**[0:00-0:XX] INTRODUCTION**
- Hook that captures attention in the first 5 seconds
- Brief preview of value the video delivers

**[0:XX-X:XX] MAIN CONTENT**
- Key points organized in logical sequence
- Supporting evidence, examples, or demonstrations
- Natural transitions between ideas

**[X:XX-X:XX] CONCLUSION**
- Summary of main takeaways
- Reinforce the product/service value proposition

**[X:XX-END] CALL-TO-ACTION**
- Clear, specific next step for viewers
- Urgency or incentive where appropriate

Ensure the script maintains consistent brand voice throughout and uses clear, conversational language optimized for spoken delivery.
```
