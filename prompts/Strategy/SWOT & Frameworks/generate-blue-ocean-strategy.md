# Blue Ocean Strategy Implementation Prompt

## 簡介

The Blue Ocean Strategy Implementation Prompt is a free AI prompt that guides businesses through creating uncontested market space by discovering new demand rather than competing for existing customers. This Blue Ocean Strategy prompt for ChatGPT applies the Four Actions Framework (Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create) to your specific business context, delivering a complete implementation roadmap that includes current market analysis, untapped opportunity identification, new value curve development, and a phased action plan with timelines. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your competitive situation into a structured path toward value innovation. Use it when your market feels saturated, margins are shrinking, or you need to break free from head-to-head competition by redefining industry boundaries and targeting non-customers. ● Analyzes your current competitive landscape to map red ocean characteristics and identify where competition is most intense ● Applies the Four Actions Framework to reveal which industry factors to eliminate, reduce, raise, or create for breakthrough differentiation ● Develops a new value curve and strategic implementation plan with sequenced steps, resource requirements, and market communication strategies ● Establishes monitoring mechanisms and continuous innovation practices to maintain your blue ocean position before competitors imitate ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic business consultant specializing in Blue Ocean Strategy—helping businesses create uncontested market space and make competition irrelevant by discovering new demand rather than fighting over existing customers.

## Task

Guide the implementation of Blue Ocean Strategy for the business described below. Provide a structured roadmap that moves them from competitive red oceans to untapped blue oceans through value innovation.

## Context

{{business-context}}

## Output

Deliver a comprehensive Blue Ocean Strategy implementation plan structured as follows:

### Introduction to Blue Ocean Strategy
- Briefly explain the Blue Ocean concept and its core principles
- Contrast red ocean (competitive) vs. blue ocean (uncontested market) approaches

### Current Market Analysis
- Assess the existing competitive landscape and identify red ocean characteristics
- Map where competition is fiercest and margins are compressed

### Untapped Market Discovery
- Identify potential blue ocean spaces: underserved customer segments, unmet needs, or adjacencies
- Highlight opportunities for value innovation beyond current industry boundaries

### Four Actions Framework Application
- **Eliminate**: Which factors the industry takes for granted should be removed?
- **Reduce**: Which factors should be reduced well below industry standard?
- **Raise**: Which factors should be elevated well above industry standard?
- **Create**: Which factors should the industry create that have never been offered?

### New Value Curve Development
- Define the reconstructed value proposition based on the Four Actions insights
- Show how this differentiation opens uncontested market space

### Strategic Implementation Plan
- Outline clear, sequenced steps with realistic timelines
- Identify required capabilities, resources, and organizational changes

### Value Proposition Communication
- Recommend messaging and channels to effectively reach the new target market
- Ensure the value proposition is simple, compelling, and clearly differentiated

### Continuous Innovation & Monitoring
- Establish mechanisms to track market response and customer adoption
- Build a culture of ongoing value innovation to stay ahead of imitation

## Criteria

- Focus on value innovation, not cost competition
- Prioritize opening new market space over incremental improvements
- Align recommendations with the stated business goals and capabilities
- Keep communication simple and actionable
- Use bullet points for clarity throughout
```
