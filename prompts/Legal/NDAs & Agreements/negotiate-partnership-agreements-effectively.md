# Partnership Agreement Negotiation Prompt

## 簡介

The Partnership Agreement Negotiation Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds strategic partnership frameworks for businesses seeking mutually beneficial alliances. This partnership agreement prompt for ChatGPT guides you through partner analysis, synergy identification, negotiation strategy, and legally sound agreement drafting in a single structured workflow. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing executive-ready documents that cover roles, responsibilities, profit-sharing, IP rights, governance, conflict resolution, and implementation timelines. Use it when you need to formalize collaborations with clear terms that protect both parties while maximizing shared value. ● Analyzes target partners' business models to assess strategic fit and identify strengths, weaknesses, and collaboration opportunities ● Develops structured negotiation strategies that prioritize shared interests and account for dependencies between commitments ● Drafts clear agreement outlines covering roles, profit-sharing, intellectual property, governance, and exit terms ● Anticipates friction points around resource allocation and decision authority, then proposes resolution mechanisms to prevent disputes ● Ensures legal compliance and enforceability across all terms, tailored to your specific industry context ● Generates implementation roadmaps with milestones and KPIs to track partnership performance over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert negotiator specializing in strategic partnership agreements.

## Task
Negotiate and structure a mutually beneficial partnership agreement that is clear, concise, and legally sound. Deliver a complete framework covering negotiation strategy, agreement terms, and implementation planning.

## Context
**My business:** {{my-business}}
**Target partners:** {{target-partners}}
**Industry:** {{industry}}
**Partnership objectives:** {{objectives}}
**Non-negotiables:** {{non-negotiables}}

## Process
Work through the following stages systematically:

1. **Partner Analysis** – Assess the potential partner's business model, strengths, weaknesses, and strategic fit.
2. **Synergy Identification** – Pinpoint key areas where collaboration creates mutual value.
3. **Negotiation Strategy** – Build a structured approach that prioritizes shared interests and addresses dependencies between commitments.
4. **Agreement Outline** – Define roles, responsibilities, profit-sharing mechanisms, intellectual property rights, and governance structures.
5. **Conflict Anticipation** – Identify likely friction points (resource allocation, decision authority, exit terms) and propose resolution mechanisms.
6. **Legal Compliance** – Ensure all terms are enforceable and aligned with relevant laws and regulations in the specified industry.
7. **Implementation Roadmap** – Propose a timeline with milestones and key performance indicators to measure partnership success.

## Output
Structure your response with main headings and subpoints for each stage. Use clear, unambiguous language suitable for executive review and legal scrutiny. Include specific recommendations tailored to the business context provided.
```
