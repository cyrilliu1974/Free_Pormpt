The Visual Art Specification Generator for AI Images is a free AI prompt that builds complete image generation commands by combining your subject, art style, and composition choices into a ready-to-run format for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. This visual art prompt for Midjourney takes three variables (subject-and-action, art-style, and scene-composition) and outputs a fully formed prompt string that includes cinematic lighting, a complementary color palette, high detail rendering, and technical parameters like aspect ratio (16:9), quality (2), and stylization (750). Artists, designers, and content creators use it to quickly generate concept art, marketing visuals, and illustration references without manually assembling prompt syntax each time. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models that accept structured parameter strings.
