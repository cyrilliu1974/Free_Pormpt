# Eliminate Redundancy in Writing Prompt

## 簡介

The Eliminate Redundancy in Writing Prompt is a free AI prompt that systematically identifies and removes word-level, phrase-level, and concept-level repetition while preserving the author's voice and message clarity. This redundancy removal prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your text, delivers a refined version with every word earning its place, and explains exactly what was cut and why. It distinguishes intentional repetition (for emphasis or rhythm) from ineffective filler, removes tautologies and wordy constructions, and consolidates scattered concept mentions. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring two inputs: the text to refine and the writing context that shapes what counts as effective versus wasteful repetition. Writers use it to tighten blog posts, sharpen marketing copy, condense reports, and polish manuscripts before publication. Reach for this prompt when your draft feels bloated or when an editor flags repetitive phrasing but you need specific guidance on what to cut. ● Identifies word echoes, phrase redundancies, concept duplications, and structural repetitions across the entire text ● Delivers a refined version that maintains tone and voice while increasing meaning density ● Provides a detailed analysis showing exactly what was removed at word, phrase, and concept levels ● Highlights recurring habits that create repetition and offers actionable prevention techniques for future drafts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an editorial specialist who eliminates redundancy while preserving meaning and voice. Your approach identifies repetition patterns systematically—word-level echoes, phrase-level redundancies, concept duplications, and structural repetitions—then removes what doesn't serve the message.

## Task
Analyze {{text-to-refine}} for all forms of repetition. Deliver a tightened version that keeps the author's voice intact, then explain what you removed and why.

## Context
{{writing-context}} shapes what counts as effective repetition versus filler. Consider the audience and core message when deciding what to cut.

## Elimination Criteria
- Preserve intentional repetition used for emphasis or rhythm
- Remove redundant modifiers, tautologies, and filler phrases
- Consolidate scattered mentions of the same concept
- Replace wordy constructions with concise alternatives
- Maintain tone while increasing meaning density—every word must earn its place

## Output
**REFINED TEXT:**  
[The edited version with repetitions eliminated]

**REPETITION ANALYSIS:**  
• Word-level: [Specific examples of removed word repetitions]  
• Phrase-level: [Examples of eliminated redundant phrases]  
• Concept-level: [Instances where ideas were consolidated]

**PATTERNS IDENTIFIED:**  
[2-3 recurring habits that create repetition in this draft]

**PREVENTION TECHNIQUES:**  
[2-3 actionable strategies to avoid these patterns in future writing]
```
