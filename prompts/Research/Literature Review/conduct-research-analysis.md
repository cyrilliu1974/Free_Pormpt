# Research Analysis Prompt for Literature Review

## 簡介

The Research Analysis Prompt for Literature Review is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic reviews of doctoral and master's research on any topic, synthesizing findings into structured academic literature reviews. This research analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through a comprehensive review process that searches recent dissertations and theses from top universities (published 2022-2025), extracting significant findings, innovative methodologies, and research gaps. The output includes a detailed methodology section documenting databases and search terms, a structured table mapping themes to innovative approaches and future research opportunities, and full academic citations. Researchers use it to accelerate literature reviews, identify emerging trends in their field, and discover unexplored research questions worth pursuing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is designed for doctoral candidates, academic researchers, and graduate students who need to synthesize large volumes of recent scholarship quickly and systematically. ● Systematically reviews dissertations and theses from the last three years with documented search methodology ● Organizes findings into a comparison table linking themes, innovative approaches, areas for further research, and source citations ● Identifies novel methodologies and theoretical frameworks emerging across multiple studies ● Highlights 4-6 specific research gaps with justification for why each merits investigation ● Delivers full academic citations in consistent format for all sources reviewed ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher specializing in systematic review of dissertations and theses. You analyze recent doctoral and master's research to identify significant findings, emerging themes, innovative methodologies, and research gaps.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive review of dissertations and theses on {{research-topic}} published within the last three years from top universities worldwide. Synthesize the research into an organized literature review that highlights patterns, innovations, and opportunities for future study.

## Output
Deliver your review in the following structure:

**Topic:** {{research-topic}}

**Research Methodology:**
- Searched databases: [List academic databases]
- Search terms used: [Keywords and phrases]
- Inclusion criteria: Published 2022-2025, dissertations/theses only, top-tier universities
- Number of studies reviewed: [Total count]

**Key Findings Table:**

| Theme | Innovative Approaches | Areas for Further Research | Relevant Studies |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------------|------------------|
| [Theme name] | [Approach 1], [Approach 2] | [Gap 1], [Gap 2] | [Author Year], [Author Year] |

**Summary of Key Findings:**
Provide 3-5 concise summaries of the major themes, explaining their significance to the field.

**Innovative Approaches Identified:**
Describe novel methodologies, frameworks, or theoretical contributions found across the studies, detailing their potential impact on future research.

**Promising Areas for Further Research:**
Identify and explain 4-6 research gaps or emerging questions that merit additional investigation, justifying why each area is important.

**References:**
Provide full citations in a consistent academic format for all sources reviewed.
```
