# Burnout Recognition and Prevention Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Burnout Recognition and Prevention Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored workplace wellness frameworks for occupational health specialists, HR professionals, and team leaders. This burnout prevention prompt for ChatGPT analyzes profession-specific stressors and produces structured guides covering early warning signs, potential impacts, prevention strategies, mitigation actions, workplace modifications, and self-care practices. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting its recommendations to the unique demands, schedules, and pressures of any professional context you specify. Real-world applications include building wellness programs for healthcare workers, designing intervention plans for tech teams facing crunch periods, and equipping managers with tools to support remote employees. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-based, actionable burnout guidance customized to a specific role, industry, or work environment rather than generic wellness advice. ● Identifies physical, emotional, and behavioral burnout indicators specific to the profession's unique stressors and demands ● Provides practical prevention strategies and step-by-step mitigation actions that fit within the role's time and resource constraints ● Recommends workplace modifications and sustainable self-care practices grounded in occupational health research ● Organizes output into clear, actionable sections that HR teams, managers, and employees can immediately implement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert occupational health specialist focused on burnout prevention and workplace well-being.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, profession-specific guide for recognizing early signs of burnout and implementing effective strategies to maintain productivity and well-being.

## Context
Analyze burnout indicators within the context of:
{{professional-context}}

Consider work environment characteristics, job demands, industry-specific stressors, typical schedules, and primary sources of work-related stress. Your analysis should be evidence-based and account for the unique pressures of this profession.

## Output
Deliver your guide as a bullet-point list organized under clear headings:

- **Early Warning Signs**: Specific indicators of burnout relevant to this profession, including physical, emotional, and behavioral symptoms
- **Potential Impacts**: How these warning signs affect job performance, health, and personal life
- **Prevention Strategies**: Practical, evidence-based interventions to prevent burnout before it develops
- **Mitigation Actions**: Step-by-step measures to address burnout when warning signs appear
- **Workplace Modifications**: Environmental and procedural changes that support sustained well-being
- **Self-Care Practices**: Sustainable habits tailored to the demands and constraints of this profession

Ensure all recommendations are actionable, realistic within the profession's constraints, and grounded in occupational health research.
```
