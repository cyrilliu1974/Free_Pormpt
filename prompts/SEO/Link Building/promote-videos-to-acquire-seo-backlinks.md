# Video Content Backlink Outreach Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Video Content Backlink Outreach Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized outreach campaigns to acquire high-quality SEO backlinks through video content promotion. This video backlink outreach prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete campaign framework including prospect research methodologies, personalized email templates structured with dependency grammar principles, and follow-up sequences designed to maximize response rates. It analyzes your video content, target niche, and website's unique selling proposition to craft outreach messages that emphasize mutual benefit and content relevance. Digital marketers use it to design systematic campaigns that connect video assets with authoritative websites in their industry, turning content into backlink opportunities. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured approach to email outreach that positions your video content as a valuable resource worth linking to, whether you're promoting tutorials, case studies, or thought leadership videos. ● Outputs prospect segmentation methods, email template structures, and personalization tactics tailored to your niche and video content type ● Applies dependency grammar framework to craft outreach messages that build logically from a core value proposition ● Includes follow-up sequence timing, value proposition framing techniques, and campaign effectiveness metrics ● Provides actionable steps and example language for each stage of the backlink outreach process ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in video content promotion and SEO backlink acquisition.

## Task
Create a comprehensive outreach strategy to secure high-quality, authoritative backlinks from relevant websites. Design personalized email templates and a campaign framework that maximizes response rates and link placements.

## Context
**Video content:** {{video-content-description}}
**Target niche and outreach goals:** {{niche-and-goals}}
**Website and unique selling proposition:** {{website-and-usp}}

Structure your strategy using dependency grammar principles—each sentence in your outreach templates should build logically from a core value proposition, with supporting clauses that reinforce relevance and mutual benefit.

## Output
Provide a detailed guide in numbered list format with lettered sub-points (a, b, c) covering:

1. Prospect research and segmentation methodology
2. Email template structure with dependency grammar framework applied
3. Personalization tactics for each prospect segment
4. Value proposition framing that emphasizes content quality and niche relevance
5. Follow-up sequence and timing recommendations
6. Metrics to track campaign effectiveness

Each main point should include actionable steps, example language, and best practices for securing authoritative backlinks.
```
