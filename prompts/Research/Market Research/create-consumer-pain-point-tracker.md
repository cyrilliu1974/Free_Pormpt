# Consumer Pain Point Tracker for Product Research

## 簡介

The Consumer Pain Point Tracker for Product Research is a free AI prompt that systematically identifies and analyzes consumer challenges to guide product development and improvement strategies. This consumer pain point tracker prompt for ChatGPT produces a four-column analysis table covering pain points, frequency ratings (using a five-star scale), impact scores (1-5 severity), and actionable solutions. It works by prompting ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to think from the consumer's perspective and generate at least five distinct pain point rows with honest assessments. Real use cases include product managers preparing roadmaps, UX teams prioritizing fixes, customer success teams identifying support gaps, and consultants conducting market research audits. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond anecdotal feedback and create a structured view of where your product or service frustrates users, how often those friction points occur, and what specific improvements will have the greatest impact. ● Maps specific consumer problems to frequency and severity scores for evidence-based prioritization. ● Delivers actionable solution ideas across product features, UX design, support interventions, and customer education. ● Provides a repeatable framework for tracking pain points across product iterations or different audience segments. ● Outputs a clean table format ready for stakeholder presentations, sprint planning, or roadmap discussions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in understanding and addressing consumer pain points to guide product development and improvement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive analysis table that identifies, evaluates, and proposes solutions for consumer pain points related to {{product-or-service}}.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Analyze pain points from the consumer's perspective, honestly assessing frequency and impact. Develop specific, actionable solutions including product improvements, feature enhancements, UX optimizations, customer support interventions, or educational initiatives.

## Output
Deliver a table with exactly 4 columns and at least 5 distinct pain point rows:

**Pain Point** | **Frequency** | **Impact** | **Potential Solutions**

### Column Definitions

**Pain Point**: Describe the specific problem, challenge, or frustration experienced by the consumer.

**Frequency**: How often consumers encounter this pain point:
- ★ Rarely
- ★★ Occasionally
- ★★★ Frequently
- ★★★★ Very frequently
- ★★★★★ Constantly

**Impact**: Severity of the pain point's effect on consumer satisfaction and likelihood to use the product/service (scale 1-5):
- 1 = Minimal impact
- 2 = Minor inconvenience
- 3 = Moderate frustration
- 4 = Major issue
- 5 = Deal breaker

**Potential Solutions**: Specific, actionable ideas to address the pain point.

Organize findings in a clear, structured format ready for analysis and action planning.
```
