# Architectural Photography Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Architectural Photography Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that produces wide-angle interior photographs with editorial composition and professional architectural styling for designers, photographers, and real estate marketers. This architectural photography prompt for Midjourney works by specifying camera position (behind the door), lens focal length (24mm), composition rules (clean lines, negative space), and depth of field settings to produce editorial-quality room shots. It accepts two variables: a room description and an interior style, then renders the space from eye level with a front view that captures the full design. Use it to visualize interior concepts, create portfolio pieces, generate marketing visuals for properties, or explore how different styles transform the same space in Midjourney, Flux, or Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need professional architectural photography without a physical shoot, or when prototyping interior design directions across multiple aesthetics. ● Specifies 24mm wide-angle lens, shallow depth of field, and eye-level angle for authentic architectural photography aesthetics. ● Captures rooms from behind the door with a front view, showing the full space with editorial composition and negative space. ● Accepts any room type and interior style as variables, from minimalist studios to maximalist living rooms. ● Outputs in 16:9 aspect ratio with clean lines and professional lighting suitable for portfolios, presentations, and marketing materials. ## Prompt

```
Wide-angle architectural photograph of a {{room-description}} with {{interior-style}} interior. Shot from behind the door providing a front view of the space. Editorial composition, clean lines, negative space, eye-level angle, 24mm lens, shallow depth of field, professional architectural photography style. --ar 16:9
```
