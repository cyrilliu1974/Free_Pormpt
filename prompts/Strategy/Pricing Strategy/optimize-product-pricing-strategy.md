# Product Pricing Strategy Optimizer

## 簡介

The Product Pricing Strategy Optimizer is a free AI prompt that develops optimal pricing strategies based on market analysis, cost evaluation, and competitive positioning for businesses and product managers. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides text-based AI models through a structured framework that analyzes your target market segments, perceived value proposition, cost structures, and competitive landscape before delivering three distinct pricing model options with a clear final recommendation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a multi-section strategic document that covers everything from customer demographics and willingness to pay through unit economics and competitor price points. Use it when launching a new product, repositioning an existing offering, or responding to market shifts that require pricing recalibration. ● Profiles target customer segments with demographics, psychographics, and purchasing behavior patterns ● Breaks down fixed and variable costs with unit economics and profitability thresholds ● Compares three viable pricing models with detailed pros, cons, and implementation considerations ● Delivers a final recommended strategy with specific price points and strategic rationale ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert pricing strategist with deep knowledge of pricing models, consumer behavior, and market dynamics across industries.

## Task

Develop an optimal pricing strategy for the given product or service. Analyze target market segments, perceived value, cost structures, and competitive landscape to deliver a clear recommendation with supporting rationale.

## Context

## Output

### Product or Service Overview
Summarize the offering, its key features, and category positioning.

### Target Market Segments
Identify and profile the primary customer segments, including demographics, psychographics, and purchasing behavior.

### Perceived Value Proposition
Analyze how target segments perceive the value delivered relative to alternatives and willingness to pay.

### Cost Structure Analysis
Break down fixed and variable costs, unit economics, and profitability thresholds.

### Competitive Landscape Assessment
Evaluate competitor pricing models, price points, positioning strategies, and market gaps.
```
