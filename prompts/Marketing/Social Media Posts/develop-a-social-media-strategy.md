# Social Media Strategy Development Prompt for Business

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Development Prompt for Business is a free AI prompt that creates tailored, multi-platform social media strategies for small businesses seeking to grow their online presence and audience engagement. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every component of building a professional marketing plan: analyzing your target audience to select the right 2-4 platforms, defining content pillars and formats, setting optimal posting schedules, designing engagement tactics like contests and user-generated content campaigns, identifying key performance metrics, recommending partnership opportunities, and outlining promotional campaigns with budget guidance. It delivers a structured 90-day implementation roadmap with milestones, making it immediately actionable for teams with limited resources. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and adapts to any business context you provide. ● Recommends the best 2-4 platforms based on target audience demographics and behavior patterns ● Defines content pillars, format types, and posting frequencies tailored to each platform ● Outlines specific engagement mechanisms, response protocols, and community-building tactics ● Identifies 5-7 KPIs, analytics tools, and partnership opportunities aligned with brand values ● Includes sample campaign concepts and a phased 90-day implementation timeline with actionable next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing strategist specializing in small business growth and audience engagement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, actionable social media strategy that increases online presence, engagement, and sales for the business described below.

## Context
The strategy must reflect the brand's unique voice and values while addressing platform selection, content creation, posting schedules, engagement tactics, performance tracking, partnerships, and promotional campaigns. Tailor all recommendations to the specific business context and target audience provided.

## Business Information
{{business-context}}

## Strategy Components

Deliver a structured strategy document covering:

**Platform Selection**
- Analyze target audience demographics and online behaviors to recommend 2-4 optimal social media platforms
- Justify each platform choice with audience-fit reasoning
- Note any niche platforms offering targeted engagement opportunities

**Content Creation and Curation**
- Define content pillars (educational, entertaining, inspirational, promotional) based on audience preferences
- Outline a content mix: ratio of original vs. curated content
- Provide content format recommendations (video, carousel, stories, long-form) per platform

**Posting Frequency and Timing**
- Recommend platform-specific posting schedules based on typical audience activity patterns
- Suggest 2-3 scheduling tools for maintaining consistency

**Engagement Tactics**
- Propose specific interaction mechanisms: contests, polls, Q&A sessions, user-generated content campaigns
- Define response protocols for comments and messages that build community

**Performance Metrics and Analytics**
- Identify 5-7 key performance indicators: engagement rate, follower growth, reach, website traffic, conversion rate
- Recommend analytics tools and reporting frequency

**Partnerships and Collaborations**
- Suggest 3-5 partnership categories (micro-influencers, complementary brands, local businesses) aligned with brand values
- Outline collaboration formats and value exchange models

**Promotions and Campaigns**
- Design 2-3 sample campaign concepts with objectives, mechanics, and success criteria
- Include recommendations for organic reach vs. paid advertising budget allocation

**Implementation Roadmap**
- Provide a phased 90-day implementation timeline with milestones
- Emphasize testing, measurement, and iterative optimization based on performance data

## Output
Structure the strategy as a clear, scannable document with headers, bullet points, and tactical next steps. Make all recommendations specific and immediately actionable for a small business team with limited resources.
```
