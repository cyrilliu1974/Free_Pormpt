# Employee Communication Policy Builder

## 簡介

The Employee Communication Policy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates formal communication policy documents for HR leaders and compliance teams managing internal communication standards. This employee communication policy prompt for ChatGPT generates a structured policy framework covering six core areas: internal announcements protocols, email etiquette standards, digital communication guidelines, tone and voice standards, confidentiality protocols, and escalation channels. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your organization profile and current communication landscape as inputs to produce a customized policy document with implementation roadmaps, training requirements, measurement metrics, and ready-to-use templates. The output is formatted for both digital distribution and printed employee handbooks, with practical dos and don'ts tables, real-world scenarios, and clear consequences for violations. Reach for this prompt when you need to standardize communication practices across departments, address remote work and digital channel challenges, or ensure legal compliance while maintaining employee engagement and transparency. ● Produces six numbered policy sections with specific guidelines, comparison tables, examples, and violation consequences for each communication area. ● Includes an executive summary, implementation roadmap with training requirements and success metrics, and appendices with templates and quick reference guides. ● Addresses modern challenges including remote work protocols, instant messaging etiquette, social media boundaries, and cross-departmental flexibility. ● Balances legal compliance requirements with practical, non-restrictive language that encourages authentic workplace communication. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational communication policy specialist who designs employee communication frameworks that balance legal compliance, transparency, and engagement.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Employee Communication Policy covering internal announcements, email etiquette, digital communication, tone standards, confidentiality protocols, and escalation channels. The policy must align with SHRM Internal Communication Standards and Gallup Engagement Research principles while remaining practical and enforceable.

## Context
The policy must satisfy legal and compliance requirements, prevent information silos and conflicting messages, balance transparency with confidentiality, address modern challenges (remote work, instant messaging, social media), allow departmental flexibility within core standards, include measurable outcomes and clear consequences, and avoid overly restrictive rules that suppress authentic communication.

**Organization profile:**
{{organization-profile}}
*Include: organization size, industry, company culture and values*

**Current communication landscape:**
{{current-communication-landscape}}
*Include: tools/platforms in use, top 3 communication challenges, 2-3 examples of current practices*

## Output
Deliver a formal policy document structured as follows:

**Executive Summary**
- Policy purpose, scope, and alignment with SHRM and Gallup standards

**Core Policy Sections** (numbered, with subsections):
1. Internal Announcements Protocol
2. Email Etiquette Standards
3. Digital Communication Guidelines
4. Tone and Voice Standards
5. Confidentiality Protocols
6. Escalation Channels

For each section provide:
- Specific guidelines (bullet points)
- Dos and don'ts (comparison tables)
- Real-world examples and scenarios
- Clear consequences for violations

**Implementation Roadmap**
- Training requirements
- Measurement metrics and success indicators
- Timeline and rollout phases

**Appendices**
- Communication templates
- Quick reference guides
- Additional examples

Format for both digital distribution and printed handbooks. Use callout boxes for legal requirements and critical warnings. Ensure language is professional yet accessible to all employee levels.
```
