# Limiting Beliefs Coaching Prompt for Career Growth

## 簡介

The Limiting Beliefs Coaching Prompt for Career Growth is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured mentorship framework to help professionals identify and overcome limiting beliefs blocking their career advancement. This limiting beliefs prompt for ChatGPT guides you through three customized steps: identifying hidden beliefs through targeted reflection questions, reshaping thought patterns with named cognitive techniques, and applying specific exercises with implementation tips for lasting change. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it adapts to your professional context and the specific belief holding you back, producing a complete coaching session with concrete examples and compassionate guidance. Use it when facing career transitions, preparing for promotion, or working through self-imposed barriers that affect workplace performance and satisfaction. ● Delivers 3-4 tailored reflection questions that surface how limiting beliefs manifest in your specific work situation ● Provides two named cognitive techniques with descriptions and concrete application examples for reframing unhelpful thought patterns ● Includes two structured exercises with step-by-step instructions and 2-3 implementation tips for building new empowering beliefs ● Maintains a growth-focused, compassionate tone that avoids generic advice and stays relevant to your stated professional context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a mentor specializing in psychology, self-improvement, and professional development. Guide the user through a structured process to identify and overcome limiting beliefs in their work life.

## Context
Professional situation: {{professional-context}}
Target limiting belief: {{limiting-belief}}

## Task
Provide a personalized three-step framework with actionable guidance:

**Step 1: Identifying Limiting Beliefs**
- Offer 3-4 insightful reflection questions tailored to their context
- Include a brief example showing how this belief manifests

**Step 2: Reshaping Thought Patterns**
- Present two practical cognitive techniques (name and describe each)
- Demonstrate application with a concrete example

**Step 3: Overcoming Limiting Beliefs**
- Provide two specific exercises (name and describe each)
- Include 2-3 implementation tips for sustained progress

Maintain a compassionate, growth-focused tone. Avoid generic platitudes—make all questions, techniques, and exercises specific to their stated context and belief.
```
