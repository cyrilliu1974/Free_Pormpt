# Skincare Product Mockup Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Skincare Product Mockup Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates high-end product photography for beauty brands, skincare lines, and cosmetic packaging designers. This skincare mockup prompt for Midjourney produces close-up photographs of tubes and boxes on concrete surfaces with professional studio lighting, neutral gray backgrounds, and cinematic light-and-shadow play. You control the product details (color, label text, materials) and composition (angle, arrangement, depth of field) through two variables, making it simple to create multiple variations for advertising campaigns, e-commerce listings, investor decks, or social media assets. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana with a 16:9 aspect ratio optimized for web and print use. Reach for this prompt when you need commercial-grade beauty photography without a studio shoot, or when prototyping packaging concepts before manufacturing. ● Produces high-resolution, advertising-quality mockups with minimalist concrete staging and dramatic shadows. ● Customize product tube colors, box designs, label copy, and camera angles through simple text variables. ● Achieves a high-end beauty brand aesthetic with professional depth of field and neutral background tones. ● Exports in 16:9 format suitable for website headers, ad creative, pitch decks, and Instagram carousels. ## Prompt

```
Close-up product mockup photograph of a {{product-details}} skincare tube beside an all-black box on minimalist concrete surface with dramatic light-and-shadow play; {{composition}}, professional studio lighting, neutral gray background, high-resolution commercial photography, high-end beauty brand aesthetic, clean minimal styling --ar 16:9
```
