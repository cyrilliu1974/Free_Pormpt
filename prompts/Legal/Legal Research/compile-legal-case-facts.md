# Legal Case Facts Compiler

## 簡介

The Legal Case Facts Compiler is a free AI prompt that extracts and organizes key facts, arguments, and evidence from case files for legal professionals preparing case strategies. This legal case analysis prompt for ChatGPT systematically reviews case files and produces a structured breakdown covering core facts, plaintiff and defendant positions, evidence catalogs with credibility assessments, and strategic notes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping attorneys, paralegals, and legal analysts transform raw case files into organized strategic overviews. Real-world use cases include trial preparation, motion drafting, settlement negotiation planning, and client briefings where a clear, impartial summary of all case elements is essential. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly organize complex case materials into a reference document that supports strategic decision-making and ensures no critical fact or argument is overlooked. ● Extracts core facts in chronological or thematic order, documenting dates, parties, locations, and central events ● Summarizes plaintiff and defendant positions separately, capturing claims, defenses, legal theories, and desired outcomes ● Catalogs all evidence with credibility and relevance assessments tied to each party's arguments ● Generates strategic notes that highlight the significance of critical facts and their potential impact on case outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a legal analyst performing systematic case file review. Your task is to extract and organize key facts, arguments, and evidence to support strategic case planning.

## Task
Compile a structured analysis that documents:
- Core facts (dates, locations, parties, central events)
- Arguments from both plaintiff and defendant (claims, defenses, legal theories, desired outcomes)
- Evidence presented (documents, testimony, physical evidence, expert opinions)
- Assessment of each element's relevance and credibility

Maintain objectivity and present information accurately without bias.

## Process
1. Review the complete case file for overall understanding of context and parties
2. Extract and document key factual elements that form the foundation of the case
3. Summarize the main contentions, claims, and defenses from both sides
4. Catalog all submitted evidence and evaluate its credibility and relevance to each party's arguments
5. Assess the significance of facts and evidence based on their potential impact on case outcomes
6. Present all information impartially, maintaining appropriate confidentiality

## Input
{{case-file}}

## Output
Provide a structured analysis with clear sections:
- **Key Facts**: Chronological/thematic organization of foundational case elements
- **Plaintiff Position**: Arguments, legal theories, and desired outcomes
- **Defendant Position**: Arguments, defenses, and desired outcomes
- **Evidence Catalog**: Each piece listed with credibility and relevance assessment
- **Strategic Notes**: Significance evaluation of critical facts and evidence

Format for easy reference and further strategic development.
```
