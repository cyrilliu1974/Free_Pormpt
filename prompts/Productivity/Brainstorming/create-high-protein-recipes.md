# High-Protein Recipe Generator for Custom Diets

## 簡介

The High-Protein Recipe Generator for Custom Diets is a free AI prompt that creates tailored meal plans for nutritionists, fitness coaches, and anyone pursuing health goals. This high-protein recipe prompt for ChatGPT delivers five complete recipes spanning breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks, each adapted to your specified diet type. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing exact ingredient lists with quantities, step-by-step cooking instructions with timings and temperatures, and detailed nutritional information per serving including protein, calories, and macros. Every recipe includes substitution suggestions for common restrictions and serving ideas that respect dietary guidelines. Use it when you need practical variety in meal planning, whether for vegan, keto, paleo, gluten-free, or any other eating pattern. ● Produces five varied recipes across meal types and cooking methods for practical rotation ● Includes exact ingredient quantities, preparation times, and macro breakdowns per serving ● Offers substitution options and serving suggestions that maintain dietary compliance ● Highlights protein content while balancing taste, ease of preparation, and ingredient availability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist and fitness coach specializing in high-protein meal planning.

## Task
Create 5 high-protein recipes tailored to {{diet-type}}. Each recipe must be easy to prepare, use readily available ingredients, and respect all dietary restrictions while delivering excellent taste and nutritional value. Provide variety across different meal types (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks).

## Output
For each of the 5 recipes, provide:

**Recipe Name & Meal Type**

**Ingredients**
- Complete list with exact quantities
- Suggest substitutions for common dietary restrictions or preferences where applicable

**Instructions**
- Step-by-step cooking directions
- Specific preparation tips, temperatures, and timings

**Nutritional Information (per serving)**
- Preparation time
- Serving size
- Total protein (highlighted)
- Calories, fat, carbohydrates, and other relevant macros

**Serving Suggestions**
- Complementary sides or dishes
- Customization options within {{diet-type}} guidelines

Ensure recipes span different times of day and cooking methods to provide practical variety for maintaining fitness and health goals.
```
