# Hyperrealistic Cosplay Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Hyperrealistic Cosplay Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces studio-quality, full-body cosplay photographs with custom costume details, professional lighting, and 8K texture rendering. This cosplay portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates photorealistic images of cosplayers in tailored costumes featuring embossed patterns, glossy textures, and custom emblems. You control the costume design through {{costume-details}}, specify hairstyle and makeup looks, and define the pose and expression. The output mimics professional studio photography with soft lighting that highlights fabric textures, realistic skin rendering, and a clean mirror-selfie aesthetic against plain backgrounds. Content creators use it for portfolio pieces, character design references, and marketing visuals without the cost of physical costumes or photo shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need publication-ready cosplay imagery, character concept art, or visual assets that showcase detailed costume work and realistic human features. ● Produces 8K resolution images with photorealistic skin texture, fabric rendering, and studio lighting that rivals professional photography. ● Customizable costume elements including embossed patterns, emblems, glossy materials, and texture details through the {{costume-details}} variable. ● Full control over hairstyle, makeup intensity, pose, gaze direction, and hand positioning for creative or brand-specific needs. ● Includes a negative prompt to avoid common AI image artifacts like deformed hands, blurry focus, and oversaturated colors. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic full-body portrait of a female cosplayer wearing {{costume-details}}, featuring subtle embossed patterns and a matching emblem on the chest. {{hairstyle-and-makeup}} with soft bangs framing her face, dramatic smoky eyes, and deep glossy red lips. She poses {{pose-description}}, fingers lightly positioned, gaze intense and direct. Soft studio-quality lighting highlights the glossy texture of the suit and vibrant hair color. Subject stands in front of a plain white or light gray wall with a slight reflection, suggesting a mirror selfie aesthetic. Professional cosplay photography, photorealistic, 8K resolution with detailed skin and texture rendering.

Negative prompt: low quality, blurry, deformed hands, out of focus, cartoon style, drawing style, oversaturated colors, poorly rendered body, floating elements, distorted anatomy, grainy textures
```
