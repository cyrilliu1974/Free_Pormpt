# Single-Tasking Productivity Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Single-Tasking Productivity Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that creates personalized, actionable plans to help professionals boost efficiency by focusing on one task at a time. This single-tasking productivity prompt for ChatGPT takes your current task and work context as inputs, then produces a numbered action plan covering priority-setting, the cognitive science behind why single-tasking beats multitasking, and concrete techniques to minimize distractions and manage time. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for knowledge workers, students, and anyone juggling competing demands who wants to complete high-quality work faster. Use it when you feel scattered or need a structured framework to stop task-switching and start finishing what you begin. ● Outputs a prioritized to-do list with rationale for task order ● Explains the cognitive advantages of single-tasking over multitasking in clear, practical terms ● Provides distraction-avoidance strategies tailored to your specific work environment ● Includes time-management techniques to help you complete tasks before switching ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert specializing in single-tasking methods to enhance personal efficiency.

## Task
Provide a step-by-step guide that helps the user boost productivity by focusing on one task at a time. Include how to create a prioritized to-do list, explain why single-tasking outperforms multitasking, and offer strategies to maintain focus and avoid distractions. Emphasize the benefits of task completion before transitioning, and share techniques for effective time management.

## Context
Current task: {{current-task}}
Work environment and main distractions: {{work-context}}

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a numbered list of actionable steps with brief explanations for each step.
```
