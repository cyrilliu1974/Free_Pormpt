# Brainstorming Framework Generator for Structured Ideation

## 簡介

The Brainstorming Framework Generator for Structured Ideation is a free AI prompt that builds custom, step-by-step brainstorming processes for teams, facilitators, and innovation leaders. This brainstorming framework prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to design a complete facilitation blueprint organized into distinct phases - each with its own purpose, techniques, and implementation guidance. It adapts to your session context (team size, problem type, organizational setting) and outputs a ready-to-use process overview, four structured phases with specific creative techniques, and actionable facilitation tips. Use it to prepare workshops, kickstart innovation sprints, or design recurring ideation sessions that balance proven methods with fresh approaches. The framework works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need more than a list of brainstorming ideas - when you need a repeatable process that maximizes creative output, fosters collaboration, and moves from divergent thinking to focused innovation. ● Builds four distinct brainstorming phases, each with clear purpose and practical techniques you can implement immediately. ● Adapts to different team sizes, problem domains, and organizational settings based on the session context you provide. ● Includes facilitation tips focused on creating a collaborative environment and maximizing participant contributions. ● Delivers accessible, jargon-free guidance that works for experienced facilitators and first-time workshop leaders alike. ## Prompt
