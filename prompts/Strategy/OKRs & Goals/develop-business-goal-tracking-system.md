# Business Goal-Tracking System Builder

## 簡介

The Business Goal-Tracking System Builder is a free AI prompt that designs end-to-end systems for monitoring multi-year business objectives across any industry or function. This business goal-tracking prompt for ChatGPT walks you through eight interconnected components: a five-step goal-setting process, five measurable KPIs tailored to your business area, three monitoring methods (quantitative and qualitative), an accountability framework, review cadences at strategic, operational, and tactical levels, four course-correction triggers with clear thresholds, and a stakeholder communication plan. You specify a business area - sales, product development, customer success, finance - and the prompt generates a practical, actionable tracking ecosystem that connects goal-setting methodology to day-to-day execution. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use this prompt when you need to move beyond spreadsheets and ad-hoc check-ins to establish a coherent system that keeps multi-year goals visible, measurable, and on track. ● Generates a five-step goal-setting process grounded in best practices for multi-year planning. ● Identifies five measurable KPIs and three tracking methods specific to your business area. ● Defines accountability structures, three-tier review cadences, and four quantifiable course-correction triggers. ● Outputs a stakeholder communication plan with channels, content types, and frequency recommendations. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic planner and systems architect specializing in long-term goal tracking for businesses.

## Task
Design a comprehensive system for monitoring progress on multi-year business goals in {{business-area}}. The system must be practical, actionable, and tailored to the specific characteristics of this business area.

## Output
Provide a structured system design covering:

**System Overview**
Summarize the system's purpose and key elements at a high level.

**Goal Setting Process**
Outline a 5-step process for setting effective multi-year business goals, incorporating best practices in goal-setting methodology.

**Metrics and KPIs**
Identify 5 key metrics or KPIs to track progress. Each must be relevant to {{business-area}}, measurable, and directly aligned with multi-year objectives.

**Tracking Methods**
Describe 3 methods for monitoring the identified metrics. Include a mix of quantitative and qualitative approaches suited to {{business-area}}.

**Accountability Framework**
Explain how individual and team accountability for goal progress will be established and maintained throughout the organization.

**Progress Review Cadence**
Specify 3 recommended review frequencies (strategic, operational, and tactical levels) with rationale for each.

**Course Correction Triggers**
List 4 specific triggers or thresholds that would initiate corrective action. Define clear, measurable criteria for when intervention is needed.

**Stakeholder Communication Plan**
Describe the plan for communicating goal progress and updates to key stakeholders, including channels, content types, and frequency.

Ensure all components are interconnected and form a cohesive tracking ecosystem specific to {{business-area}}.
```
