# Personal Training Business Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Personal Training Business Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured launch and growth plans for fitness professionals starting or scaling a personal training venture. This personal training business plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your business parameters and produces a markdown table that maps service offerings to pricing structures and corresponding marketing strategies. It generates 4-6 distinct service tiers - from introductory sessions and ongoing one-on-one coaching to group classes and specialized programs - each aligned with competitive local pricing and acquisition tactics that blend digital channels with in-person outreach. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for solopreneurs and side-hustle fitness coaches who need a realistic, scalable framework that adapts to seasonal demand and market shifts. Use this prompt when you are launching a personal training business, adding new service packages, or refining your pricing and marketing mix to better match your target demographic and fitness specialization. ● Builds service tiers that span beginner to advanced fitness levels and accommodate different client goals ● Establishes pricing that reflects local market rates, your specialization, and profitability targets ● Suggests marketing strategies mixing digital advertising, social proof, referral programs, and community engagement ● Outputs a clear markdown table format that you can immediately reference, customize, and share with advisors or collaborators ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert personal trainer and business strategist specializing in solopreneur fitness ventures.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive launch and growth plan for a personal training business. Create a service portfolio that addresses different fitness levels and client goals, establish a competitive pricing structure, and design marketing strategies to acquire and retain clients.

## Context
{{business-parameters}}

The plan must be realistic for a solopreneur or side hustle model, scalable as the business grows, and flexible enough to adapt to market shifts and seasonal demand changes.

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Services Offered | Pricing Structure | Marketing Strategies |
|-----------------|-------------------|---------------------|

Each row should represent one cohesive service or package offering. Include 4-6 distinct service tiers (e.g., intro sessions, ongoing coaching, group classes, specialized programs) that span beginner to advanced fitness levels. Ensure pricing reflects local market rates and your specialization premium. Marketing strategies should mix digital and local tactics appropriate for the target demographic.
```
