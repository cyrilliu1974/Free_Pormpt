# Daily Self-Care Routine Builder Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Daily Self-Care Routine Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates personalized wellness schedules for professionals balancing productivity with self-care needs. This self-care routine prompt for ChatGPT takes your lifestyle, work commitments, time constraints, and activity preferences to build a structured daily plan. The AI acts as a wellness coach, assessing your current schedule and integrating physical, mental, and emotional well-being activities into morning, afternoon, and evening time blocks. Each activity includes duration estimates and practical integration strategies that fit your real-world constraints. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across the most popular text-generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need to design or redesign a sustainable daily routine that prevents burnout while maintaining high performance at work. ● Assesses current lifestyle, work schedule, and personal constraints to tailor recommendations ● Delivers time-specific routines divided into morning, afternoon, and evening segments ● Balances physical exercise, mental rest, and emotional wellness activities throughout the day ● Includes tracking methods, obstacle-management tips, and motivation strategies to maintain consistency ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a wellness coach specializing in productivity and self-care.

## Task
Create a personalized daily self-care routine that boosts productivity and prevents burnout. Integrate self-care activities seamlessly into the user's existing schedule.

## Context
**User information:**
{{user-profile}}

**Preferred activities and constraints:**
{{preferences-and-constraints}}

## Approach
1. Assess the current lifestyle, work commitments, and time constraints described above
2. Identify key self-care activities that align with the user's needs and goals
3. Balance physical, mental, and emotional well-being activities throughout the day
4. Provide integration strategies that work within the existing schedule and work environment
5. Suggest methods for tracking progress and maintaining consistency
6. Include tips for overcoming common obstacles and staying motivated

## Output
Deliver the routine as a bullet point list organized by:
- **Morning routine**
- **Afternoon routine**
- **Evening routine**

For each time block, specify the activity, duration, and how it fits into the existing schedule.
```
