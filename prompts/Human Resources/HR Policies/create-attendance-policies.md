# Attendance Policy Builder for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Attendance Policy Builder for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that drafts legally compliant attendance policies balancing organizational accountability with employee flexibility for HR professionals and people operations teams. This attendance policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a seven-section policy document covering definitions, punctuality standards, absence reporting, leave types, time-tracking, progressive discipline, and manager enforcement guidelines. You provide your company context and specific attendance challenges - such as no-call/no-show patterns, remote work verification issues, or hybrid schedule conflicts - and the prompt returns a structured policy that addresses multiple work arrangements, integrates with time-tracking systems, and flags areas requiring legal review. Use it when standardizing attendance expectations across distributed teams, updating legacy policies for hybrid work, or preparing documentation for performance management. ● Defines tardiness, absence, and no-call/no-show for remote, hybrid, and in-person roles with punctuality standards for each arrangement. ● Specifies absence notification timelines, contact channels, documentation requirements, and distinctions between planned, unplanned, and protected leave. ● Outlines a progressive disciplinary framework with attendance thresholds, warning stages, termination criteria, and appeal processes. ● Includes manager responsibilities for consistent enforcement, documentation, and communication, with implementation notes on training and system configuration. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an HR policy architect specializing in employment law and attendance management.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive, legally compliant attendance policy that establishes clear expectations for punctuality and reliability while accommodating legitimate absences and diverse employee needs.

## Context

**Company & operational details:**
{{company-context}}

**Primary attendance challenges:**
{{attendance-challenges}}

The policy must address multiple work arrangements (remote, hybrid, in-person), integrate with existing time-tracking systems, and balance organizational accountability with employee flexibility.

## Output

Provide a structured attendance policy document with these sections:

**1. Definitions & Scope**
- Covered employees and work arrangements
- Working hours and core business hours
- Definitions of tardiness, absence, no-call/no-show

**2. Attendance Expectations**
- Punctuality standards for each work arrangement type
- Break and meal period guidelines
- Schedule adherence requirements

**3. Absence Reporting Procedures**
- Notification requirements and timelines
- Designated contact channels
- Documentation requirements

**4. Types of Leave & Accommodations**
- Planned vs. unplanned absences
- Protected leave categories
- Reasonable accommodation process

**5. Time-Tracking Requirements**
- Clock-in/out procedures
- Remote work verification methods
- Record-keeping responsibilities

**6. Progressive Disciplinary Framework**
- Attendance thresholds and occurrence systems
- Warning stages (verbal, written, final)
- Termination criteria
- Appeal process

**7. Manager Responsibilities**
- Consistent enforcement guidelines
- Documentation requirements
- Communication protocols

Format each section with clear bullet points. Include implementation notes where the policy requires manager training or system configuration. Highlight areas requiring legal review based on jurisdiction.
```
