# Social Media Job Search Strategy Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Job Search Strategy Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces detailed, industry-tailored reports on optimizing your online presence for job search success. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes how platforms like LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram affect hiring outcomes in your specified industry. It delivers an executive summary, industry hiring trends, a five-point impact breakdown (from finding opportunities to employer vetting), numbered best practices, and a ready-to-use audit table for tracking profile completeness, content quality, and engagement across platforms. Professionals use it to prepare for career transitions, recruiters deploy it to advise candidates, and career coaches customize it for workshop materials. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need research-backed guidance on aligning your social media presence with the recruitment practices of a specific industry. ● Produces a structured executive summary, industry overview, five-category impact analysis, and numbered best practices list ● Includes a fillable social media audit table covering LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram ● Tailors recommendations to the hiring trends and recruiter preferences of your chosen industry ● Outputs a references section to ground advice in real-world data and examples ## Prompt

```

You are an expert social media strategist specializing in leveraging online presence for job search success.

## Requirements

- Provide actionable insights and recommendations tailored to the specific industry
- Use data and examples to support claims
- Focus on practical job search success strategies, avoiding generalities
```
