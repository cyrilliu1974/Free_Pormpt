# User-Generated Content Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The User-Generated Content Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds full-scale UGC campaign plans for brands, marketers, and digital strategists looking to turn customers into content creators. This user-generated content strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through audience analysis, platform selection, engagement tactics, incentive design, cross-channel integration, KPI frameworks, budget allocation, and phased execution timelines. You provide your niche, product, and business constraints - budget, resources, and timeline - and the prompt returns a structured strategy document ready for internal alignment or client presentation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable across teams and workflows. Use it when launching a new product, refreshing a stale social presence, or building proof-of-concept campaigns that rely on authentic customer voices. ● Identifies the top three platforms for your audience and explains what content formats perform best on each ● Designs three incentive mechanisms - contests, rewards, recognition programs - that motivate customers to create and share content ● Maps UGC integration across owned, paid, and earned channels so content reaches beyond your existing followers ● Defines 3-5 KPIs aligned to awareness, engagement, and conversion, with clear measurement and attribution guidance ● Breaks down budget and resource allocation across moderation, amplification, tools, and creative support, then lays out a phased timeline with milestones and optimization checkpoints ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in user-generated content (UGC) campaigns that drive brand awareness, engagement, and sales.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive UGC strategy for the specified niche and product/service. Deliver specific, actionable recommendations covering platform selection, audience engagement tactics, content integration across channels, measurement frameworks, and implementation planning.

## Context

**Niche and offering:**  
{{niche-and-product}}

**Business constraints:**  
{{budget-resources-timeline}}

## Output

Structure your strategy as follows:

**Niche & Product Overview**  
Summarize the market landscape and offering positioning.

**Target Audience Analysis**  
- Demographics: age, location, income, education  
- Psychographics: values, interests, lifestyle, pain points  
- Content Preferences: formats, platforms, consumption habits

**Top 3 UGC Platforms**  
For each platform:  
1. Platform name and rationale  
2. High-performing content types (video, reviews, photos, stories, etc.)  
3. Engagement tactics: campaign hooks, hashtag strategies, community-building techniques

**Incentive Mechanisms**  
Three compelling incentives to motivate UGC creation (contests, rewards, recognition programs, exclusive access, etc.)

**Multi-Channel Integration**  
- **Owned Media:** website, email, social profiles  
- **Paid Media:** ad creative, retargeting, influencer partnerships  
- **Earned Media:** PR, word-of-mouth amplification

**KPIs & Measurement**  
Define 3-5 key metrics aligned to awareness, engagement, and conversion goals (submission volume, engagement rate, conversion attribution, sentiment, reach, etc.)

**Budget & Resource Allocation**  
Break down spending and team requirements across content moderation, incentives, paid amplification, tools, and creative support.

**Timeline & Execution Plan**  
Phased rollout with milestones, launch dates, campaign windows, and optimization checkpoints.
```
