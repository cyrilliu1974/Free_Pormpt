# Email Lead Magnet Ideas Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Email Lead Magnet Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that produces customized, conversion-focused lead magnet concepts for businesses building their email marketing funnels. This lead magnet prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, target audience, and unique selling proposition to deliver at least 10 distinct ideas spanning formats like eBooks, checklists, templates, webinars, toolkits, case studies, mini-courses, and assessments. Each suggestion is presented in a markdown table showing the lead magnet idea, its intended audience segment, and the core benefit it delivers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible across major text-generation models. Real-world applications include SaaS companies brainstorming free trial hooks, coaches developing content upgrades, e-commerce brands planning discount incentives, and B2B firms creating whitepaper concepts that address specific client pain points. This prompt is for digital marketers, growth teams, and business owners who need a diverse set of actionable lead magnet concepts that align with their expertise and resonate with their audience's immediate needs. ● Produces 10+ unique lead magnet ideas across multiple content formats and delivery methods ● Tailors every suggestion to your business's expertise and target audience's pain points ● Outputs a clean markdown table with idea, audience, and benefit columns for easy evaluation ● Encourages diversity in format - from downloadable resources to interactive webinars and assessments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in email marketing and lead generation.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of engaging, high-converting email lead magnet ideas tailored to the specified business. Each idea should provide immediate value, address specific audience challenges, and align with the business's expertise.

## Context
Business: {{business-type}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Consider the audience's needs, pain points, and interests. Draw from diverse formats including eBooks, checklists, templates, webinars, toolkits, resource libraries, case studies, mini-courses, assessments, and exclusive content.

## Output
Present as a markdown table with three columns: **Lead Magnet Idea**, **Target Audience**, and **Key Benefit**. Provide at least 10 unique, practical ideas that showcase the business's expertise and deliver actionable value.
```
