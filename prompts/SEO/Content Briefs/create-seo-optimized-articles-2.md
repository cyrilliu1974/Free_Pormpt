# SEO Article Writer With Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The SEO Article Writer With Dependency Grammar is a free AI prompt that produces fully structured, keyword-optimized articles for content marketers, bloggers, and SEO professionals. This SEO article prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete piece with title, introduction, three body sections with H2 subheadings, conclusion, and meta description - all built around your target keyword while applying dependency grammar principles to ensure sentence clarity and readability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering 500-700 word articles that balance search engine performance with genuine reader value. Unlike generic writing prompts, this tool enforces natural keyword placement, semantic variations, and structural discipline across every section, making it ideal for professional content teams who need consistent, scalable SEO writing without keyword stuffing or awkward phrasing. ● Produces a complete article framework with title, introduction, three body sections, conclusion, and meta description ● Applies dependency grammar techniques to create clear, coherent sentences that improve readability scores ● Enforces natural keyword distribution across all sections with semantic variations to avoid over-optimization penalties ● Generates descriptive H2 subheadings that target related keywords and support topical authority ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO content writer specializing in keyword-optimized articles that balance search engine performance with reader engagement.

## Task
Write an in-depth, SEO-optimized article that strategically incorporates the target keyword throughout while using dependency grammar techniques to create clear, coherent sentences.

## Context
{{topic-and-keyword}}

## Output
Deliver a complete SEO-optimized article with:

**Title**
- Engaging, SEO-friendly headline featuring the target keyword

**Introduction** (100-150 words)
- Hook readers with an attention-grabbing opening
- Provide a brief overview of the topic
- Naturally include the target keyword 1-2 times

**Body Section 1** (100-150 words)
- Descriptive H2 subheading targeting a related keyword
- In-depth exploration of the first major subtopic
- Incorporate the target keyword and semantic variations
- Use dependency grammar for clarity and readability

**Body Section 2** (100-150 words)
- Descriptive H2 subheading targeting another related keyword
- In-depth exploration of the second major subtopic
- Incorporate the target keyword and semantic variations
- Use dependency grammar for clarity and readability

**Body Section 3** (100-150 words)
- Descriptive H2 subheading targeting a final related keyword
- In-depth exploration of the third major subtopic
- Incorporate the target keyword and semantic variations
- Use dependency grammar for clarity and readability

**Conclusion** (50-75 words)
- Summarize key points
- Reiterate the importance of the topic and target keyword
- Include a clear call-to-action

**Meta Description** (150-160 characters)
- Concise, engaging summary featuring the target keyword

**Requirements:**
- Use natural keyword integration—avoid stuffing
- Apply dependency grammar principles for sentence structure
- Format with markdown (## for headings, ** for bold, etc.)
- Prioritize value and readability alongside SEO optimization
```
