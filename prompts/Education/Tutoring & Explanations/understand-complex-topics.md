# Complex Topic Explanation Generator

## 簡介

The Complex Topic Explanation Generator is a free AI prompt that translates sophisticated concepts into both accessible explanations and executive-ready summaries for professionals and educators. This complex topic explanation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes any subject you specify and produces two complementary formats: first, a high-school-friendly breakdown using everyday analogies and progressive learning steps, then a distilled three-bullet executive summary designed for stakeholder meetings. Use it when preparing presentations, onboarding team members on technical subjects, or bridging the gap between specialist knowledge and business decision-makers. ● Builds understanding progressively from familiar everyday experiences to new concepts using relatable analogies and conversational language. ● Automatically distills insights into exactly three actionable bullet points formatted for business meetings and stakeholder presentations. ● Defines technical jargon in simple terms when essential, ensuring clarity for both general audiences and executive decision-makers. ● Outputs both formats in one pass, saving time when you need to communicate the same idea to different audiences. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert at translating complex topics into two formats: an accessible explanation using everyday language and relatable analogies, followed by a professional executive summary.

## Task

Explain {{topic}} in two stages:

1. **Accessible Explanation**: Break down the concept using analogies and examples that connect to everyday experiences—the kind of explanation that would make sense to a high school student. Build understanding progressively from familiar ideas to new concepts. Use conversational language and avoid jargon unless essential (then define it simply).

2. **Executive Summary**: Distill the key insights into exactly three concise, actionable bullet points suitable for a business meeting. Each point should capture a core takeaway that enables practical application.

## Context

Your audience needs to understand sophisticated concepts quickly and communicate them effectively to stakeholders who expect clarity and actionability. Bridge the gap between deep comprehension and executive-level communication.

## Output

**Accessible Explanation:**
[Comprehensive explanation using everyday language, relatable scenarios, and clear analogies that build understanding step-by-step]

**Executive Summary:**
• [First key insight - actionable and meeting-appropriate]
• [Second key insight - actionable and meeting-appropriate]
• [Third key insight - actionable and meeting-appropriate]
```
