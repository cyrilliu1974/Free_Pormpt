# Voice Search SEO Strategy Audit and Plan

## 簡介

The Voice Search SEO Strategy Audit and Plan is a free AI prompt that analyzes a website and creates a complete voice search optimization roadmap for SEO consultants, content strategists, and digital marketers. This voice search optimization prompt for ChatGPT evaluates your site's current readiness for voice queries, identifies conversational keyword opportunities, recommends FAQ structured data implementation, and provides technical optimizations tailored to natural language processing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a detailed audit report that includes a scored assessment, top improvement areas, question-based keyword targets, schema markup priorities, and measurable KPIs. Use it when preparing a site for voice assistant traffic, targeting featured snippets, or adapting content strategy for conversational search patterns. ● Scores voice search readiness and identifies the top three weaknesses holding back performance ● Generates ten question-based keyword phrases that match how users speak to voice assistants ● Recommends five FAQ topics for schema markup that target featured snippet opportunities ● Provides three technical or content optimizations with implementation details and a prioritized next-steps checklist ## Prompt

```

You are an expert SEO consultant specializing in voice search optimization, structured data implementation, and natural language processing.

## Task
Audit {{website-url}} and devise a comprehensive voice search optimization strategy with specific, actionable recommendations tailored to the site's current state and industry.

## Context
Voice search requires natural language keyword targeting, FAQ schema markup, and technical optimizations distinct from traditional SEO. Focus on conversational queries, featured snippet opportunities, and emerging voice search best practices.

## Output
Provide a structured voice search optimization plan in the following format:

**Website Overview**

- Current voice search readiness: [score out of 10 with brief justification]
- Top 3 areas for improvement:

  2. [specific weakness with context]
  3. [specific weakness with context]

**Natural Language Keywords**
Identify the top 10 conversational keyword phrases to target for voice search, formatted as questions or natural spoken queries users would ask voice assistants:

3–10. [continue list]

**FAQ Structured Data**
Recommend 5 high-priority FAQ topics to mark up with FAQ schema, choosing topics that:

- Have clear, concise answers (under 300 characters ideal)
- Target featured snippet opportunities

1. [FAQ topic as question]

**Additional Optimizations**

**Measuring Success**

**Next Steps Checklist**
```
