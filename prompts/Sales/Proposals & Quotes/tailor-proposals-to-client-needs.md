# Client-Tailored Sales Proposal Generator

## 簡介

The Client-Tailored Sales Proposal Generator is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive B2B sales proposals aligned to specific client challenges and industry contexts. This sales proposal prompt for ChatGPT walks you through building a complete, decision-ready proposal document in five sections: executive summary, client challenges, solution mapping, implementation plan, and expected outcomes. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, it accepts three variables - client and industry details, a list of pain points, and your product or service features - then constructs logical, feature-by-benefit arguments that show decision-makers exactly how your offering solves their problems. Sales teams use it to replace generic templates with proposals that reflect deep understanding of a prospect's business environment and demonstrate clear value. Reach for this prompt when you need a polished, client-specific proposal quickly, or when pitching to multiple industries and need consistent structure with tailored content. ● Transforms raw client pain points and product features into a cohesive five-section proposal ● Maps each client challenge to a corresponding solution benefit with clear reasoning ● Includes implementation roadmap and quantified outcome projections for credibility ● Uses professional business language appropriate for executive and procurement audiences ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist crafting a tailored B2B sales proposal.

## Task
Write a compelling sales proposal that addresses the client's specific challenges and positions the offered solution as the optimal fit. Structure the proposal with clear logical flow and persuasive reasoning that connects each client pain point to a corresponding benefit.

## Context
**Client & Industry:** {{client-and-industry}}
(Include client name, industry sector, and any relevant business context)

**Client Pain Points:** {{pain-points}}
(List the 3-5 main challenges or problems the client is facing)

**Our Solution:** {{product-service-and-features}}
(Name the product/service and describe 3-5 unique features or capabilities that differentiate it)

## Output
Deliver a structured sales proposal with the following sections:

**Executive Summary** – Brief overview positioning your solution as the answer to their needs

**Client Challenges** – Demonstrate understanding of their pain points with specific detail

**Our Solution** – Explain how your product/service directly addresses each challenge, feature by feature

**Implementation Plan** – Outline a clear path from decision to deployment

**Expected Outcomes** – Quantify the value and results the client can anticipate

Use professional business language appropriate for decision-makers. Ensure each section flows logically into the next, building a cohesive argument for why this solution is the right choice.
```
