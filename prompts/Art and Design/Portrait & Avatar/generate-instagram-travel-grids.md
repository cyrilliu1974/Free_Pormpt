# Instagram Travel Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Instagram Travel Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates a 3×3 layout of nine realistic, unfiltered smartphone travel photos featuring the same subject exploring a destination's iconic landmarks. This Instagram travel grid prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces candid tourism shots with authentic smartphone imperfections - motion blur, grain, uneven exposure, lens distortion, and natural lighting - while maintaining subject consistency across all nine frames in a cohesive 1:1 aspect-ratio layout. Perfect for creators building travel personas, visual storytellers prototyping Instagram profiles, or designers who need realistic tourist photography mockups without the polished influencer aesthetic. ● Nine distinct landmark scenes with one consistent subject across the entire grid ● Authentic low-quality smartphone look: grain, motion blur, unbalanced framing, washed-out colors ● Natural skin texture and candid expressions with no beauty filters or retouching ● Customizable destination city, subject description, and specific landmark list ## Prompt

```
3x3 Instagram grid layout, 1:1 aspect ratio, nine candid smartphone travel photos of the same extremely beautiful woman exploring {{destination-city}}. {{subject-description}}. Natural attractiveness, realistic skin texture with no smoothing or glossiness, minimal makeup, relaxed candid expressions.

Nine different scenes at iconic landmarks: {{landmark-scenes}}.

Authentic low-quality smartphone aesthetic: motion blur, grain and noise, slight over/underexposure, lens distortion, unbalanced framing, uneven natural sunlight. No beauty filters, no skin retouching, washed-out or inconsistent colors, authentic dynamic range. Carefree European summer travel vibe, tourist lifestyle candids. Same person consistent across all nine photos, forming cohesive Instagram profile layout.

--ar 1:1
```
