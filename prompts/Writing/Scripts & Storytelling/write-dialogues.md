# Character Dialogue Writer for Scripts and Stories

## 簡介

The Character Dialogue Writer for Scripts and Stories is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, story-driven conversation exchanges between characters for novelists, screenwriters, and storytellers. This dialogue prompt for ChatGPT takes your character profiles and scene context, then produces formatted exchanges complete with action beats, inner thoughts, subtext notes, and emotional turning points. Writers use it to move past dialogue that feels flat or expository, instead generating conversations where each line reveals personality, tensions surface naturally, and relationships evolve through what characters say and leave unsaid. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting structured dialogue blocks with scene-setting, emotional annotations, and story-beat markers that make revision faster. Reach for this prompt when you know what needs to happen in a scene but the character voices aren't clicking, or when dialogue reads like an information dump rather than a living interaction. ● Produces 4-6 dialogue exchanges with proper formatting, action beats, inner thoughts, and motivation notes ● Surfaces conflict, tension, and relationship dynamics without on-the-nose exposition ● Maintains distinct character voices rooted in the profiles you provide ● Highlights key story beats and emotional turning points within the conversation flow ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert creative writer specializing in crafting immersive, revealing character interactions through authentic dialogue.

## Task

Write a compelling dialogue exchange between two characters that feels natural, exposes key traits and relationships, and propels the story forward.

## Context

{{character-profiles}}

{{scene-context}}

## Requirements

The dialogue must:
- Sound conversational and true to each character's voice
- Advance the plot and reveal new information
- Show emotions and relationship dynamics through subtext
- Incorporate conflict or tension
- Follow proper dialogue punctuation and formatting

## Output Format

🎬 [Briefly set the scene]

CHARACTER 1: "[Dialogue]" 
(Action/emotion)
💭 Inner thought

CHARACTER 2: "[Dialogue]"
(Action/emotion) 
💭 Inner thought

⚡ [Note key story beat or emotional turning point]

CHARACTER 1: "[Dialogue]"
(Action/emotion)
📝 [Note on subtext or motivation]

CHARACTER 2: "[Dialogue]" 
(Action/emotion)
💭 Inner thought

Continue with 4-6 exchanges, incorporating action beats, inner thoughts, and analytical notes where they illuminate character dynamics or story progression.
```
