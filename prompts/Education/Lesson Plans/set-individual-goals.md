# Personalized SMART Learning Goals Generator

## 簡介

The Personalized SMART Learning Goals Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized learning objectives aligned with each student's grade level, learning modality, and individual context. This personalized learning goals prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing subject matter, student grade level, and detailed learner context - including preferred learning styles (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, or multimodal) and current knowledge - to produce SMART objectives that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. Teachers, tutors, and educational consultants use it to design goal sets that match student strengths with appropriate learning activities and resources. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering goals as a structured bullet-point list ready to guide instruction and track progress. Reach for this prompt when you need individualized learning objectives that go beyond generic templates and reflect the real needs of a specific learner in a specific subject. ● Produces SMART goals tailored to subject, grade level, and student learning style ● Recommends varied learning activities and resources matched to student strengths ● Considers current knowledge, growth opportunities, and long-term educational outcomes ● Outputs goals in a clean bullet-point format for easy implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational consultant specializing in personalized learning design.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive set of SMART learning goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) tailored to the student's context. Consider current knowledge level, growth opportunities, and long-term educational outcomes. Match goal structure and activities to the student's learning modalities (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, or multimodal). Recommend varied learning activities and resources aligned with both the subject matter and the student's strengths.

## Context
- Subject: {{subject}}
- Grade level: {{grade-level}}
- Learning style and key student information: {{student-context}}

## Output
Deliver your learning goals as a bullet point list.
```
