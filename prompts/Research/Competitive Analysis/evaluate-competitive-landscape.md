# Competitive Landscape Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Competitive Landscape Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured market intelligence reports for businesses evaluating their competitive position. This competitive analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to research and document key competitors, market share data, competitive advantages, and vulnerabilities within a specified industry and timeframe. It outputs a two-part deliverable: a markdown-formatted competitive matrix table mapping each competitor's market share, strengths, and weaknesses, followed by curated lists of key market trends and strategic opportunities aligned with your business context. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming industry and business-context variables into actionable market intelligence. Use cases include pre-launch market assessment, quarterly competitive reviews, investor due diligence preparation, and strategic planning cycles. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework for competitive research that organizes findings into decision-ready formats rather than unstructured analysis. ● Outputs a markdown table mapping competitor names, market share percentages, strengths, and weaknesses for side-by-side comparison ● Identifies emerging industry trends and untapped opportunities specific to your business context and timeframe ● Separates competitor intelligence from strategic opportunities so teams can distinguish between reactive and proactive moves ● Accepts industry, business-context, and timeframe variables to tailor the scope and relevance of the analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive landscape assessment.

## Task
Analyze the competitive landscape for the specified industry and deliver a structured report identifying key players, market trends, and strategic opportunities.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Business context: {{business-context}}
Analysis timeframe: {{timeframe}}

Conduct thorough research to identify major competitors and gather current market data. Examine each competitor's market position, competitive advantages, and vulnerabilities. Identify emerging trends and untapped opportunities relevant to the business context.

## Output
Deliver your analysis in two parts:

1. **Competitive Matrix** – Present findings as a markdown table:

| Competitor Name | Market Share | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------------|--------------|-----------|------------|

2. **Market Intelligence** – Follow the table with:
   - **Key Market Trends**: Bullet-point list of significant industry movements
   - **Strategic Opportunities**: Bullet-point list of actionable opportunities aligned with the business context
```
