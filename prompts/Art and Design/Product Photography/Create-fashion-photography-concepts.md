# Fashion Photography Concept Prompt for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Fashion Photography Concept Prompt for AI Image Models is a free AI prompt that generates high-end cinematic fashion visuals with commercial-grade camera aesthetics for photographers, creative directors, and brand teams. This fashion photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and other AI image models simulates the look of anamorphic prime lenses shot on commercial cinema cameras, complete with dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, and professional color grading. You describe your scene - a model in a flowing dress on a rooftop at golden hour, a close-up of leather accessories against textured concrete, or a full-body editorial shot in an industrial warehouse - and the prompt renders it with the production value of a high-budget campaign shoot. It is ideal for mood boards, client pitches, social media content, and pre-visualization before physical shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need fashion imagery that looks like it came from a cinema camera rig, not a smartphone, and want full control over aspect ratio to match Instagram, print, or billboard formats. ● Simulates anamorphic prime lenses and commercial cinema camera aesthetics without technical rigging. ● Applies dramatic lighting and shallow depth of field automatically for high-end editorial looks. ● Supports custom aspect ratios to fit Instagram stories, magazine spreads, or outdoor advertising. ● Works with any scene description - studio portraits, street fashion, product close-ups, or environmental editorials. ## Prompt

```
{{scene-description}}, shot on high-end commercial cinema camera with anamorphic prime lens, cinematic product photography style with dramatic lighting and shallow depth of field, professional color grading --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
