# Consumer Behavior Analysis Prompt for Market Research

## 簡介

The Consumer Behavior Analysis Prompt for Market Research is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive consumer segmentation and behavioral analysis for businesses and market researchers. This consumer behavior prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your specified industry and business profile to identify distinct consumer segments, then maps purchasing habits, brand loyalty, product usage patterns, and satisfaction drivers for each group. It outputs a markdown table with four columns - Consumer Segment, Behavior Patterns, Preferences, and Pain Points - followed by 3-5 key strategic insights grounded in market research frameworks. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying demographic, psychographic, and purchasing behavior criteria to deliver data-driven recommendations. Use it when launching products, refining positioning, or developing targeted marketing strategies that require deep understanding of who your customers are and what drives their decisions. ● Segments consumers by demographics, psychographics, and purchasing behavior for targeted strategy development ● Identifies behavior patterns including brand loyalty, product usage, and satisfaction drivers for each segment ● Maps preferences and pain points to reveal unmet needs and competitive opportunities ● Delivers findings in a structured markdown table with strategic summary for immediate business application ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in consumer behavior research and segmentation strategy.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive market analysis that identifies consumer segments, behavior patterns, preferences, and pain points. Base your analysis on demographic, psychographic, and purchasing behavior criteria. Support findings with market research principles and deliver actionable insights for business strategy.

## Context
Industry and scope: {{industry-and-target-market}}

Business profile: {{business-profile}}

For each consumer segment you identify:
- Analyze purchasing habits, brand loyalty, product usage patterns, and satisfaction drivers
- Ground insights in observable market dynamics and consumer research frameworks
- Ensure recommendations are data-driven and strategically actionable

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with four columns:

| Consumer Segment | Behavior Patterns | Preferences | Pain Points |
|-----------------|-------------------|-------------|-------------|

Below the table, provide a brief summary (3-5 key insights) highlighting the most strategic findings for business decision-making.
```
