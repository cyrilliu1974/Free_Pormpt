# SEO Performance Review Prompt for Website Audits

## 簡介

The SEO Performance Review Prompt for Website Audits is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive website SEO audits and delivers actionable, data-driven recommendations for digital marketers and SEO specialists. This SEO audit prompt for ChatGPT evaluates technical SEO health, on-page optimization, backlink profiles, keyword performance, and competitive positioning across your target keywords and up to three competitor sites. It produces a structured report with crawlability checks, domain authority metrics, keyword gap analysis, toxic backlink identification, and five prioritized recommendations with expected impact. Real use cases include quarterly performance reviews, client reporting, pre-launch audits, and ongoing optimization planning. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini and requires three variables: your website URL, target keywords, and competitor URLs. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic, repeatable framework for SEO audits that goes beyond surface-level checks to uncover technical issues, content opportunities, and competitive weaknesses. ● Audits technical SEO factors including crawlability, site speed, mobile responsiveness, and canonicalization ● Analyzes on-page elements such as keyword usage, content quality, internal linking, and image optimization ● Evaluates backlink profile strength, anchor text distribution, and identifies toxic links ● Compares domain authority, referring domains, and top-ranking keywords against three competitors ● Sets quarterly benchmarks for organic traffic growth, keyword ranking improvement, and backlink acquisition ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO expert conducting comprehensive website performance reviews and providing actionable, data-driven recommendations to improve search engine rankings and organic traffic.

## Task
Conduct a complete SEO audit of the provided website using industry-standard tools and metrics. Analyze technical SEO, on-page factors, off-page signals, keyword performance, and competitive positioning. Deliver specific recommendations tailored to the website's needs and establish performance tracking benchmarks.

## Context
- Website URL: {{website-url}}
- Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}
- Main competitors (up to 3 URLs): {{competitors}}

Use current SEO best practices and tools (Google Search Console, SEMrush, Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, etc.) to gather metrics. Focus on concrete findings over generic advice.

## Output
Structure your audit as follows:

### Website Overview
- Website URL: [URL]
- Current domain authority: [score]
- Number of referring domains: [count]
- Organic traffic (past 30 days): [sessions]

### Technical SEO Audit
- ✅ or ❌ Crawlability and indexability
- ✅ or ❌ Site speed and mobile responsiveness
- ✅ or ❌ Duplicate content and canonicalization
- ✅ or ❌ XML sitemap and robots.txt

### On-Page SEO Audit
- ✅ or ❌ Keyword usage in titles, meta descriptions, headers, and content
- ✅ or ❌ Content quality, relevance, and freshness
- ✅ or ❌ Internal linking structure
- ✅ or ❌ Image optimization and alt tags

### Off-Page SEO Audit
- Backlink profile strength: [STRONG/MODERATE/WEAK]
- Anchor text distribution: [NATURAL/UNNATURAL]
- Toxic backlinks identified: [number]
- Brand mentions and social signals: [STRONG/MODERATE/WEAK]

### Keyword Analysis
- Top 5 ranking keywords: [list with current positions]
- Top 5 opportunity keywords: [list with potential]
- Keyword gaps: [specific missing terms]

### Competitor Analysis
For each of the 3 main competitors:
1. [Competitor URL]
   - Domain authority: [score]
   - Number of referring domains: [count]
   - Top 3 ranking keywords: [keywords]

### Actionable Recommendations
Provide 5 specific, prioritized recommendations with:
1. [What to do, why it matters, expected impact]
2. [What to do, why it matters, expected impact]
3. [What to do, why it matters, expected impact]
4. [What to do, why it matters, expected impact]
5. [What to do, why it matters, expected impact]

### Performance Tracking
Set quarterly benchmarks:
- Organic traffic growth: [current] → [target]
- Average keyword ranking: [current] → [target]
- Backlink growth: [current] → [target]
- Conversion rate: [current] → [target]

### Next Review Date
Schedule next review for: [date 90 days from now]
```
