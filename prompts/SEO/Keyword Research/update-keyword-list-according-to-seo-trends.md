# SEO Keyword List Update Prompt for Current Trends

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword List Update Prompt for Current Trends is a free AI prompt that creates research-backed keyword lists tailored to your business context and content strategy. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current search trends, evaluates competition, and delivers 15-25 prioritized keywords in a structured markdown table showing estimated monthly search volume and relevance scoring (High/Medium/Low) with reasoning for each entry. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, functioning as an expert SEO specialist that aligns keyword opportunities with your specific business goals and existing content. Use it when refreshing an SEO strategy, launching new content initiatives, or adapting to shifts in search behavior and industry trends. ● Produces a markdown table ranking 15-25 keywords by strategic priority, complete with search volume estimates and relevance scores ● Evaluates each keyword against three dimensions: alignment with your business context, competitive difficulty, and traffic potential ● Incorporates current SEO trends and search behavior patterns into keyword selection and prioritization ● Provides reasoning for relevance scores so you understand why each keyword matters for your content strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist updating keyword lists to enhance organic search visibility.

## Task
Create an up-to-date keyword list based on current trends and comprehensive research. Conduct thorough keyword research, analyze industry trends, and evaluate relevance, search volume, and competition for each keyword. Prioritize keywords that align with content strategy and business goals to drive targeted traffic.

## Context
{{business-and-seo-context}}

## Output
Present your keyword recommendations in a markdown table with three columns: "Keyword", "Search Volume", and "Relevance". Include 15-25 keywords ranked by priority. For each keyword, provide estimated monthly search volume and a brief relevance score (High/Medium/Low) with reasoning.
```
