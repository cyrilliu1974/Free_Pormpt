# Post-Purchase Follow-Up Email Sequence Generator

## 簡介

The Post-Purchase Follow-Up Email Sequence Generator is a free AI prompt that creates three-email follow-up campaigns to gather feedback and strengthen customer relationships after a purchase. This post-purchase email sequence prompt for ChatGPT produces complete emails with subject lines, body copy, sign-offs, and recommended send timing (2-3 days, 5-7 days, and 7-10 days after delivery). Each email maintains a warm, conversational tone while creating low-friction feedback opportunities - the first requests a simple response, the second adds a concrete incentive like a discount code or early access, and the third thanks all customers and emphasizes their ongoing value. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. E-commerce stores, SaaS products, subscription boxes, and service businesses use it to turn one-time buyers into loyal repeat customers. ● Creates three complete emails with clear calls to action and strategic send timing ● Maintains a warm, appreciative tone that builds loyalty without aggressive selling ● Includes concrete incentive strategy in the second email to boost response rates ● Personalizes language and examples to your specific target audience and product ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write post-purchase follow-up email sequences that gather customer feedback, surface concerns early, and build relationships that drive repeat purchases.

## Context

- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Purchased product: {{purchased-product}}
- Tone: warm, conversational, genuinely appreciative. Plain language, short sentences, no corporate stiffness.

## Task

Write a three-email follow-up sequence sent after a customer purchase. Each email should feel personal, match the tone above, and give the customer a clear, low-friction path to share feedback. Show genuine appreciation throughout; do not push aggressively.

## Output

Return three complete emails using this structure:

---

**Email 1** (send 2–3 days after delivery)

Subject: [Subject line]

[Greeting],

[Opening line acknowledging their purchase]

[Feedback request with simple one-click or reply option]

[Closing line]

[Sign off]

---

**Email 2** (send 5–7 days after Email 1 if no response)

Subject: [Subject line]

[Greeting],

[Opening line — brief, friendly reminder]

[Concrete incentive for responding: discount code, early access, free resource, or entry to draw]

[Closing line]

[Sign off]

---

**Email 3** (send 7–10 days after Email 2)

Subject: [Subject line]

[Greeting],

[Thank them whether they responded or not]

[Mention how feedback shapes future improvements]

[Express loyalty appreciation — let them know they matter beyond this one transaction]

[Closing line]

[Sign off]

---

**Criteria:**

1. Personalize language and examples to {{target-audience}}.
2. Keep tone warm, conversational, and appreciative in every email.
3. Each email has one clear call to action for leaving feedback.
4. Email 2 includes a concrete incentive.
5. Email 3 acknowledges all customers, hints at product improvements, and expresses loyalty appreciation.
6. No email is pushy or transactional in tone.
```
