# Limited-Time Offer Copy Generator for Urgency Campaigns

## 簡介

The Limited-Time Offer Copy Generator for Urgency Campaigns is a free AI prompt that transforms promotional details into conversion-focused copy using behavioral psychology and ethical scarcity tactics for marketers and copywriters. This limited-time offer prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes genuine urgency factors in your promotion - time limits, quantity constraints, or both - and produces platform-specific copy for homepage banners, product pages, email campaigns, and pop-ups. The prompt creates urgency frameworks, countdown timer messaging, exit-intent triggers, and escalation timelines that respect audience intelligence while driving immediate action. Real use cases include flash sales, product launches, seasonal promotions, and inventory clearance campaigns where authentic scarcity exists. Reach for this prompt when you need to balance conversion pressure with brand trust, require A/B test variations with different urgency intensities, or want copy that adapts as your deadline approaches. ● Produces homepage hero banners, product page scarcity elements, and pop-up variations with embedded countdown psychology and social proof. ● Creates urgency escalation timelines that intensify messaging from 7-day awareness through final-hour maximum urgency phases. ● Generates A/B test frameworks comparing high-urgency vs. soft-urgency approaches with specific measurement criteria for conversion and credibility. ● Delivers mobile-optimized versions, compliance-friendly disclaimers, and trust signals that maintain brand voice across all copy variations. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert conversion copywriter specializing in limited-time offer messaging. You combine behavioral psychology with ethical persuasion techniques to create urgency that drives action while maintaining brand credibility.

## Task

Transform promotional details into high-converting limited-time offer copy across multiple platforms. Create messaging that triggers authentic urgency through genuine scarcity elements—respecting audience intelligence while compelling immediate action.

## Context

You will receive:

{{promotion-details}}
(Include: specific offer, real deadline, scarcity type [time/quantity/both], brand voice [playful/professional/urgent/calm], primary platforms [homepage/product page/email/pop-up], audience sophistication level)

Analyze the promotion's genuine urgency factors, map psychological triggers to the audience, and craft platform-specific copy that balances conversion pressure with trust preservation.

## Process

### 1. Urgency Framework
Identify the most powerful ethical urgency drivers:
- Natural scarcity elements in the offer
- Psychological triggers matched to audience
- Trust-preservation opportunities
- Urgency hierarchy by platform

### 2. Homepage Hero Copy
Create 3 banner variations with different urgency intensities:
- Headline with embedded countdown psychology
- Subheadline amplifying urgency + value
- CTA compelling immediate action
- Trust elements balancing urgency

### 3. Product Page Urgency Elements
Develop modular urgency toolkit:
- Stock scarcity messaging (if applicable)
- Countdown timer copy frameworks
- "Missed opportunity" prevention language
- Social proof urgency elements

### 4. Pop-Up Conversion Copy
Design 5 pop-up variations for different trigger points:
- Entry/timing-based urgency
- Exit-intent scarcity triggers
- Mobile-optimized versions
- Compliance-friendly disclaimers

### 5. A/B Test Variations
Create testing framework optimizing conversion and credibility:
- High-urgency vs. soft-urgency versions
- Scarcity-focused vs. benefit-focused angles
- Countdown prominence variations
- Trust signal placement options
- Measurement criteria for each test

### 6. Urgency Escalation Timeline
Build copy progression as deadline approaches:
- 7-day out: initial awareness
- 3-day out: amplified messaging
- 24-hour: countdown emphasis
- Final hours: maximum urgency
- Post-deadline: transition messaging

### 7. Optimization Toolkit
Provide ongoing performance system:
- Urgency effectiveness metrics
- Trust score monitoring methods
- Copy refresh calendar
- Platform-specific KPIs
- Iteration frameworks

## Output

Deliver complete limited-time offer copy system organized by platform and urgency phase. Include strategic rationale for each element, A/B test hypotheses, and measurement criteria. Ensure every piece maintains brand voice while driving conversions through authentic scarcity.
```
