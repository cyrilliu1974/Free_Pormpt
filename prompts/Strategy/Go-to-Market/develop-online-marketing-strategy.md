# Online Marketing Strategy Planner for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Online Marketing Strategy Planner for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a multi-channel digital marketing roadmap customized to solo entrepreneurs' unique constraints and market position. This online marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured action plan across five core channels: SEO, content marketing, social media, email marketing, and paid advertising. Each section delivers specific tactics, prioritized implementation steps, quick wins versus long-term plays, tool recommendations, and success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and explicitly accounts for the realities solopreneurs face: limited budgets, time constraints, the need to wear multiple hats, and the imperative to start small and scale. Whether you are launching a coaching practice, an e-commerce store, or a freelance consultancy, the prompt structures tactics around your stated business overview and marketing constraints, ensuring every recommendation is actionable today. ● Delivers channel-specific tactics for SEO, content, social media, email, and paid ads tailored to your niche and audience ● Prioritizes quick-win actions alongside sustainable long-term asset-building strategies ● Accounts for real solopreneur constraints like limited time, tight budgets, and no team support ● Outputs clear implementation steps, tool recommendations, and measurable success metrics for each channel ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in solopreneur growth and scalable marketing systems.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step online marketing strategy tailored to a solopreneur's business. The plan must cover SEO, content marketing, social media marketing, email marketing, and paid advertising, with each tactic customized to their specific market position and constraints.

## Context
**Business Overview:**
{{business-overview}}

**Marketing Constraints:**
{{constraints}}

Account for the unique challenges solopreneurs face: limited time, budget restrictions, wearing multiple hats, and the need for strategies that can start small and scale. Prioritize tactics that deliver ROI quickly while building long-term assets.

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings for each marketing channel:

### SEO Strategy
### Content Marketing Strategy
### Social Media Marketing Strategy
### Email Marketing Strategy
### Paid Advertising Strategy

Under each heading, provide:
- Specific tactics tailored to the niche and audience
- Implementation steps in priority order
- Quick wins vs. long-term plays
- Tools or resources needed
- Success metrics to track

Ensure every recommendation is actionable, practical, and can be started immediately with the stated budget and resources.
```
