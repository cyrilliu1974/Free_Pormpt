# Growth Hacking Strategy Prompt for Rapid Experiments

## 簡介

The Growth Hacking Strategy Prompt for Rapid Experiments is a free AI prompt that builds experiment-driven growth plans for startups and scaling businesses testing their way to product-market fit. This growth hacking strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and target customer profile, then outputs a structured portfolio of 12–24 prioritized experiments spanning the full customer lifecycle: acquisition channels, activation flows, retention mechanics, referral loops, revenue optimization, and product enhancements. Each experiment includes a specific tactic, success metric, and priority rating based on impact versus effort. The prompt also defines your North Star Metric and delivers a five-step experimentation process you can repeat sprint after sprint. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for growth teams, founders, and marketers who need a data-driven framework to guide weekly experiments. Use this prompt when you need to move beyond guesswork and launch a disciplined testing cadence that uncovers scalable growth levers quickly. ● Defines a single North Star Metric to align every experiment around the goal that matters most. ● Generates 2–4 tactical experiments for each lifecycle stage - acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue, and product - complete with channels, metrics, and priority levels. ● Outputs structured tables so growth teams can sort by priority, assign owners, and track results in a spreadsheet or project board. ● Includes a repeatable five-step experimentation process: hypothesis, design, implementation, measurement, and iteration. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a growth hacking expert specializing in rapid experimentation, data-driven marketing, and product-led growth strategies.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive growth hacking strategy for the business described below. Design a portfolio of quick, measurable experiments across the full customer lifecycle (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue) and product development. Prioritize high-impact tactics that can be implemented rapidly.

## Context
Business and growth context:
{{business-and-challenges}}

Target customer:
{{target-customer}}

## Output
Provide your strategy in this structure:

**North Star Metric:**
[Define the single metric this strategy aims to move]

**Target Customer Profile:**

Demographics:
● [3-5 key demographic attributes]

Psychographics:
● [3-5 attitudes, values, motivations]

Behaviors:
● [3-5 observable behaviors and patterns]

**Customer Acquisition Experiments:**

| Channel | Tactic | Metric | Priority |
|---------|--------|--------|----------|
| [Channel] | [Specific tactic] | [Success metric] | [High/Medium/Low] |

**Activation Experiments:**

| Tactic | Metric | Priority |
|--------|--------|----------|
| [Onboarding/first-value tactic] | [Success metric] | [High/Medium/Low] |

**Retention Experiments:**

| Tactic | Metric | Priority |
|--------|--------|----------|
| [Engagement/habit-building tactic] | [Success metric] | [High/Medium/Low] |

**Referral Experiments:**

| Tactic | Metric | Priority |
|--------|--------|----------|
| [Viral/word-of-mouth tactic] | [Success metric] | [High/Medium/Low] |

**Revenue Experiments:**

| Tactic | Metric | Priority |
|--------|--------|----------|
| [Monetization/pricing tactic] | [Success metric] | [High/Medium/Low] |

**Product Experiments:**

| Tactic | Metric | Priority |
|--------|--------|----------|
| [Feature/UX improvement] | [Success metric] | [High/Medium/Low] |

**Experimentation Process:**
1. [Hypothesis formation step]
2. [Experiment design step]
3. [Implementation step]
4. [Measurement step]
5. [Analysis and iteration step]

For each experiment category, provide 2-4 specific, actionable tactics with clear success metrics and priority levels based on estimated impact and effort.
```
