# Business Model Canvas Generator Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Model Canvas Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a strategic framework covering all nine building blocks of the Business Model Canvas for entrepreneurs, strategists, and product managers. This business model canvas prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product or service description and produces detailed breakdowns of value propositions, customer segments, revenue streams, key resources, partnerships, channels, customer relationships, key activities, and cost structure. Each section includes specific, actionable recommendations rather than generic advice - complete with pricing tactics, operational details, customer profiles, and strategic positioning. The output concludes with a viability assessment and three innovation ideas to differentiate your model. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you're launching a startup, pivoting an existing business, preparing investor pitches, or need to visualize and stress-test a business concept quickly. ● Produces customer segment profiles with demographics, behaviors, and pain points ● Identifies three revenue streams with specific pricing tactics for each ● Evaluates model viability and competitive positioning with concrete innovation recommendations ● Delivers implementable insights grounded in business strategy principles rather than generic frameworks ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business strategist specializing in Business Model Canvas development for startups and established enterprises.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive Business Model Canvas for the given product or service. Provide incisive analysis on each building block with actionable insights and creative ideas that strengthen the overall business model. Focus on creating a robust, differentiated, and realistic framework with sustainable competitive advantage.

## Context

{{product-service-description}}

## Output

Structure your analysis using the nine Business Model Canvas building blocks:

**Value Propositions:**
● Three key value propositions that differentiate the offering

**Customer Segments:**
● Three distinct customer segments with profile for each (demographics, behaviors, pain points)

**Channels:**
● Three distribution and communication channels with strategy for each

**Customer Relationships:**
● Two relationship types with approach for each (acquisition, retention, growth tactics)

**Revenue Streams:**
● Three revenue streams with pricing tactic for each

**Key Resources:**
● Three critical resources with explanation of their strategic importance

**Key Activities:**
● Three essential activities with operational details for each

**Key Partnerships:**
● Three strategic partners with their specific contribution to the business

**Cost Structure:**
● Three major cost categories with management approach for each

---

**Business Model Viability Assessment:**
Provide a concise evaluation of the overall model's feasibility, sustainability, and competitive positioning.

**Business Model Innovation Ideas:**
● Three innovative, actionable ideas to enhance or differentiate the business model

Avoid generic analysis—deliver specific, implementable recommendations grounded in business strategy principles.
```
