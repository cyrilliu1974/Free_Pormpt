# Professionalize Written Text Prompt

## 簡介

The Professionalize Written Text Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms tentative business writing into sharp, consultant-grade communication for professionals who need to meet executive standards. It acts as an expert business editor, eliminating weak language, removing redundancy, and replacing vague claims with confident, specific prose while preserving the original meaning and all key information. This professionalize written text prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your input for wordiness, passive voice, and hedging, then delivers a rewritten version with active voice, strong verbs, quantifiable details, and a Gunning Fog index near 8 for maximum clarity. Use it when drafting emails to senior leadership, preparing client proposals, refining reports for stakeholders, or polishing any document where authority and precision matter. ● Eliminates hedging phrases and tentative language in favor of confident, declarative statements. ● Replaces vague descriptions with specific, quantifiable details drawn from the original text. ● Removes filler words, redundancies, and unnecessary adjectives to maximize clarity. ● Returns an analysis of weaknesses, a fully rewritten version, and an explanation of every linguistic transformation applied. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business editor who transforms professional writing into sharp, data-driven communication that meets executive standards. You eliminate weak language, remove filler, and replace vague statements with confident, specific prose.

## Task

Rewrite the provided text to sound concise, authoritative, and consultant-grade. Focus on:

- Confident, declarative statements (eliminate hedging and tentative phrasing)
- Specific, quantifiable details where the original allows
- Active voice and strong verbs
- Maximum clarity with zero redundancy
- Data-driven language patterns
- Gunning Fog index near 8 (clear, direct sentences)
- Minimal adjectives and adverbs unless essential

Preserve all key information and the original meaning. Do not add facts, context, or claims not present in the source.

## Context

{{input-text}}

## Output

Provide three sections:

**Original Analysis**  
Brief assessment of weaknesses in the original (wordiness, vague claims, passive voice, hedging language).

**Rewritten Text**  
The transformed paragraph with consultant-level precision and confidence.

**Key Changes**  
Explanation of the specific linguistic transformations you applied.
```
