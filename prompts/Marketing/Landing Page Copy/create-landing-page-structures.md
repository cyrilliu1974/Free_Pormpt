# Landing Page Structure Generator for Conversion

## 簡介

The Landing Page Structure Generator for Conversion is a free AI prompt that builds detailed landing page blueprints with persuasion design, content recommendations, and conversion strategy for marketers and business owners. This landing page structure prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a numbered, actionable blueprint that maps every block of your landing page - from hero sections to social proof placements - along with specific content guidance, design suggestions, and the conversion goal each element serves. You describe your business and audience, and the prompt returns a narrative-driven page flow that integrates pattern-interrupt techniques, objection handling, and consistent positioning across every section. Marketing teams, freelance designers, and founders use it to skip the guesswork and start building high-converting pages immediately. Reach for this prompt when you need a conversion-focused landing page framework that bridges strategy and execution, not just wireframe labels. ● Produces a complete, sequenced landing page structure with headlines, visual guidance, and conversion rationale for each block. ● Embeds at least three social proof touchpoints positioned to address objections at different stages of visitor decision-making. ● Incorporates pattern-interrupt elements and storytelling flow to increase memorability and reduce bounce. ● Outputs clean Markdown formatting with headings and bullets, ready to hand off to designers and copywriters. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused digital marketer specializing in landing page architecture and persuasion design.

## Task
Create a detailed landing page structure tailored to the business described below. For each block, specify:
- Its position in the page flow
- What content to include (headlines, visuals, copy elements)
- The persuasion principle or conversion goal it serves

The structure should be immediately actionable for design and copywriting.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

## Requirements
- Organize blocks in a logical narrative sequence that tells a story and guides visitors toward conversion
- Include pattern-interrupt elements that break scrolling habits and increase memorability
- Integrate at least 3 distinct social proof placements to address objections at different decision stages
- Maintain a single, consistent positioning angle throughout all messaging
- Provide specific, actionable guidance for each block—not generic placeholders

## Output
Return the complete landing page structure as a numbered list. For each block, include:
1. **Block name** and placement
2. Specific content recommendations
3. Design/layout suggestions
4. Conversion purpose

Format your response in clean Markdown with clear headings and bullet points.
```
