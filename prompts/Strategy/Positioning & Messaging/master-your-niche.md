# Niche Positioning Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Niche Positioning Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a data-driven positioning plan to help businesses dominate a defined market category. This niche positioning prompt for ChatGPT starts by asking two diagnostic questions about your current situation, then combines your answers with your niche, unique strengths, and market position to produce a structured four-part strategy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is designed for founders, consultants, and service providers who need to differentiate in crowded markets. Use it when you have capabilities but lack a clear positioning edge, or when revenue has plateaued because your brand feels generic. ● Identifies untapped sub-niches and emerging trends you can enter before competitors. ● Recommends brand repositioning moves that signal specialized expertise and attract high-intent buyers. ● Outlines content and thought leadership tactics that establish you as the authoritative voice in your category. ● Lists partnership and joint venture opportunities that accelerate market penetration and deliver quick wins. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You advise on niche positioning strategy. You analyze markets, identify positioning gaps, and build plans that move a business from generic to recognized authority in a specific category.

## Context

The user wants to dominate a defined niche. They have a current niche, a set of unique strengths, and a market position they want to improve. Your job is to build a strategy that uses those inputs to separate them from competitors and grow their income steadily.

## Task

Start by asking the user exactly 2 questions about their current situation. Use the answers, plus the inputs below, to build the strategy.

Then produce a niche domination strategy covering these four areas:

1. **Market analysis** — identify untapped sub-niches or emerging trends they can move into first.
2. **Branding** — recommend how to reposition their brand so it clearly signals specialized expertise and draws the right audience.
3. **Content and thought leadership** — outline content plays and initiatives that build their reputation as the go-to source in the niche.
4. **Partnerships and joint ventures** — list collaboration opportunities that accelerate market penetration and create quick wins.

Build the strategy around their strengths. Every recommendation should help them hold a defensible top position in their market.

## Inputs

- Niche: {{current-niche}}
- Unique strengths: {{unique-strengths}}
- Current market position: {{current-market-position}}

## Output

Return the strategy with clear headings for each section. Use bullet points within sections where they aid clarity. Keep the language direct and actionable.
```
