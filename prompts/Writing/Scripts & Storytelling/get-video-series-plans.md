# Video Series Planning Prompt for Content Strategy

## 簡介

The Video Series Planning Prompt for Content Strategy is a free AI prompt that builds complete video series plans tailored to your concept, audience, content goals, and production constraints. This video series planning prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert content strategist and produce a numbered outline covering six critical areas: series overview with value proposition and audience profiling, episode framework with 6-8 episode topics and optimal pacing, production elements including visual style and branding, engagement strategy with calls-to-action and narrative threads, distribution and promotion tactics across platforms, and success metrics with KPIs and optimization triggers. Content creators use it to move from a rough idea to a ready-to-pitch series plan in one session. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when launching a YouTube series, planning educational content, pitching a brand channel, or mapping multi-episode campaigns that require consistency and measurable outcomes. ● Refines raw concepts into clear value propositions and maps goals to measurable outcomes ● Structures 6-8 episode topics with talking points, format, and pacing tailored to your constraints ● Designs engagement hooks, cross-episode threads, and platform-specific promotion tactics ● Defines KPIs, measurement timelines, and optimization triggers to track series performance ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert content strategist specializing in video series planning and audience development.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive plan for a video series that maximizes audience engagement and achieves the stated content goals. Deliver the plan as a structured outline with numbered main points and lettered sub-points.

## Context

**Series concept:** {{series-concept}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Content goals:** {{content-goals}}

**Production constraints:** {{production-constraints}}

## Output

Your plan must cover:

1. **Series Overview**
   a. Concept refinement and unique value proposition
   b. Target audience profile and viewing behavior
   c. Content goals mapped to measurable outcomes

2. **Episode Framework**
   a. Episode topics (minimum 6-8 episodes)
   b. Episode structure and format
   c. Optimal duration and pacing
   d. Key talking points per episode

3. **Production Elements**
   a. Visual style and elements
   b. Branding and consistency
   c. Resource allocation

4. **Engagement Strategy**
   a. Call-to-action for each episode
   b. Audience interaction opportunities
   c. Cross-episode narrative threads

5. **Distribution & Promotion**
   a. Platform strategy
   b. Launch and ongoing promotion tactics
   c. Community building approach

6. **Success Metrics**
   a. Key performance indicators
   b. Measurement timeline
   c. Optimization triggers

Format the entire plan as a numbered outline with lettered sub-points.
```
