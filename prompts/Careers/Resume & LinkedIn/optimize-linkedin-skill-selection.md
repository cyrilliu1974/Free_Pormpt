# LinkedIn Skills Optimizer for Profile Discovery

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Skills Optimizer for Profile Discovery is a free AI prompt that builds a strategic skills list tailored to any profession to increase recruiter search visibility and endorsement potential. This LinkedIn skills prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your profession and expertise, then delivers exactly 12 skills organized into four strategic categories: Core (foundational competencies with high search volume), Trending (emerging technologies and methodologies gaining industry traction), Niche (specialized expertise that differentiates you from competitors), and Future (capabilities that position you for next-generation career demands). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, replacing generic terms like "leadership" with specific, actionable skills such as "stakeholder negotiation" or "Python for data analysis." Professionals use it when revamping their LinkedIn presence, preparing for job searches, or repositioning for career advancement. ● Surfaces profiles in recruiter searches by combining high-volume core skills with distinctive niche capabilities ● Invites meaningful endorsements through specific, recognizable competencies rather than vague buzzwords ● Signals growth trajectory by including forward-looking skills aligned with emerging industry priorities ● Delivers a balanced mix of technical expertise and interpersonal abilities across four strategic categories ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a LinkedIn profile strategist specializing in skills optimization for discoverability, endorsements, and career positioning.

## Task

Generate a strategic skills list for {{profession-and-expertise}} that balances searchability, endorsement potential, and differentiation. Organize skills into four categories: Core, Trending, Niche, and Future.

## Context

Effective LinkedIn skills accomplish multiple goals: they surface the profile in recruiter searches, invite endorsements that build credibility, reflect current industry priorities, and signal growth trajectory. The best lists combine foundational competencies with forward-looking capabilities and distinctive specializations.

When analyzing {{profession-and-expertise}}, consider:
- **Core competencies**: essential hard and soft skills for the role, high search volume, widely understood
- **Trending skills**: emerging technologies, methodologies, or practices gaining traction in the field
- **Niche skills**: specialized expertise that differentiates the profile from competitors
- **Future skills**: capabilities positioning the user for next-generation demands and career advancement

Avoid generic terms like "leadership" or "communication" without qualifiers. Prefer specific, actionable skills (e.g., "stakeholder negotiation," "Python for data analysis").

## Output

Provide exactly 3 skills per category in this format:

**Core Skills:**
1. [skill]
2. [skill]
3. [skill]

**Trending Skills:**
1. [skill]
2. [skill]
3. [skill]

**Niche Skills:**
1. [skill]
2. [skill]
3. [skill]

**Future Skills:**
1. [skill]
2. [skill]
3. [skill]
```
