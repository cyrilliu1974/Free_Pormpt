# Luxury Eyewear Campaign Layout Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Eyewear Campaign Layout Generator is a free AI prompt that creates editorial-quality fashion advertisement layouts for premium eyewear brands and designers. This luxury eyewear prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a structured 3:4 portrait layout with a large hero section featuring a model in golden-hour sunlight adjusting frames, paired with a 2×2 grid showcasing frame hinge details, polarized lens close-ups, lifestyle shots, and clean product photography. The prompt includes built-in typography overlay instructions for brand names and taglines in elegant serif and minimalist sans-serif fonts. Designers use it for Instagram campaigns, retail posters, pitch decks, and lookbook spreads that demand high-fashion editorial aesthetics. Reach for this prompt when you need a complete ad-ready layout that balances aspirational lifestyle imagery with product craftsmanship details in a single unified composition. ● Structured five-image composition: one hero portrait plus four detail panels in a 2×2 grid ● Golden-hour lighting with soft highlights, sharp focus on frames and lenses, minimal outdoor settings ● Macro product details (hinge craftsmanship, polarized coatings) combined with lifestyle fashion shots ● Typography overlay guidance for brand names in high-fashion serif and taglines in thin sans-serif ## Prompt

```
3:4 portrait layout fashion advertisement for premium {{eyewear-type}}, {{brand-name}} campaign.

**Top hero section (large):**
Stylish model with natural confident expression wearing premium {{eyewear-type}}, looking toward sunlight while adjusting frames. Close-up portrait with sharp focus on frame and lenses. Golden hour sunlight with soft highlights reflecting on lenses. Minimal summer outdoor setting—soft sand, sky, or architectural wall. Modern elegant mood.

**Bottom 2×2 grid (equal panels):**
• Top-left: Macro close-up of frame hinge and material texture, premium craftsmanship detail
• Top-right: Close-up of reflective polarized lenses, clarity and coating detail
• Bottom-left: Model walking confidently outdoors wearing {{eyewear-type}}, lifestyle fashion styling
• Bottom-right: {{eyewear-type}} on minimalist surface, clean product hero shot

**Typography overlay:**
{{brand-name}} in elegant high-fashion serif with wide spacing, deep black or gold, top center above hero. "See the World Differently." tagline below in thin minimalist sans-serif, soft charcoal.

Professional fashion photography, natural sunlight with soft shadows, balanced crisp exposure, luxury editorial aesthetic, clean minimal layout, high realism.

Avoid: distorted frames, blurry lenses, cheap plastic appearance, overexposed highlights, cluttered backgrounds, cartoon style, low resolution, watermarks, text artifacts.
```
