# Tiered Commission Structure Builder for Sales Teams

## 簡介

The Tiered Commission Structure Builder for Sales Teams is a free AI prompt that designs performance-based commission plans with clear tier levels, sales targets, and incentive percentages for sales organizations. This tiered commission structure prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your sales team context and produces a complete compensation plan with a structured table of tier names, threshold values, and commission rates, plus implementation guidance covering qualification rules, payout calculations, and motivational design principles. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping sales leaders create incentive plans that reward incremental achievement while aligning with budget constraints and industry benchmarks. Use it when launching a new compensation model, restructuring existing incentives, or scaling a sales team that needs transparent performance rewards. ● Generates a markdown table with tier names, sales target thresholds, and commission percentages ● Explains qualification rules, payout mechanics, and motivational principles behind the structure ● Adapts to custom sales contexts and any number of tiers you specify ● Includes implementation considerations like measurement periods and tier reset frequency ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sales compensation strategist designing tiered commission structures that drive performance and reward high achievers.

## Task
Create a tiered commission plan that motivates sales performance through clear incentives. Analyze the provided sales context, determine appropriate tier levels and commission rates, and deliver a structured plan with implementation guidance.

## Context
{{sales-team-context}}

Design a structure with {{number-of-tiers}} tiers. Consider industry benchmarks, company budget constraints, and motivational psychology when setting thresholds and rates.

## Output
Deliver your commission structure as:

1. **Commission Table** — A markdown table with columns for tier name, sales target threshold, and commission percentage. Ensure clear progression that rewards incremental achievement.

2. **Structure Explanation** — A concise overview (150-200 words) covering:
   - How the tier system works (qualification, payout calculation)
   - Motivational design principles embedded in the structure
   - Expected impact on sales behavior and team performance
   - Any implementation considerations (e.g., measurement period, tier reset frequency)

Ensure the structure is immediately actionable and easy for sales teams to understand.
```
