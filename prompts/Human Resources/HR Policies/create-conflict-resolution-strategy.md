# Conflict Resolution Strategy Generator for Workplaces

## 簡介

The Conflict Resolution Strategy Generator for Workplaces is a free AI prompt that builds customized mediation frameworks for HR professionals and managers handling workplace disputes. This conflict resolution prompt for ChatGPT takes your organizational context - company size, industry, typical conflict types, and key stakeholders - and produces a complete, multi-step process covering conflict categories, stakeholder roles, phase-by-phase resolution procedures with best practices, and post-resolution monitoring plus prevention strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting universal mediation principles to your specific workplace environment without providing legal advice. Use it when you need a structured approach that moves beyond generic policies and reflects the realities of your team dynamics, whether you're addressing interpersonal disputes, manager-employee friction, cross-departmental conflicts, or performance disagreements. ● Produces tailored conflict categories and stakeholder maps that reflect your actual organizational structure and common dispute patterns ● Delivers step-by-step resolution phases with actionable best practices for each stage, from initial reporting through final agreement ● Includes monitoring methods to track post-resolution outcomes and proactive prevention strategies to reduce future conflicts ● Emphasizes mediation principles and workplace harmony over punitive measures, fostering open communication and mutual respect ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced HR consultant specializing in workplace conflict resolution and mediation.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, step-by-step conflict resolution process tailored to the organization described below. The framework should be practical, adaptable to different dispute types, and focused on maintaining workplace harmony through proven mediation and problem-solving techniques.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}
(Include: company size, industry, common conflict types you face, and key stakeholders typically involved in disputes)

## Output

Deliver a structured conflict resolution framework with these components:

**1. Introduction**
Brief overview explaining why systematic conflict resolution matters in this workplace context.

**2. Types of Workplace Conflicts**
List and describe the relevant conflict categories for this organization.

**3. Key Stakeholders**
Identify who is typically involved in workplace disputes (employees, managers, HR, union representatives, etc.).

**4. Step-by-Step Resolution Process**
For each step, provide:
- **Step name** (bolded)
- Clear description of what happens in this phase
- Best practices for effective implementation (bulleted list)

Ensure the process is adaptable across conflict types and emphasizes mediation principles.

**5. Post-Resolution Strategies**
- Monitoring approach: Explain how to track resolved conflicts to ensure lasting resolution
- Prevention strategies: Bulleted list of proactive measures to reduce future disputes

Avoid legal advice or jurisdiction-specific compliance guidance. Focus on universal best practices in workplace mediation.
```
