# Press Release Generator for Product Launches

## 簡介

The Press Release Generator for Product Launches is a free AI prompt that creates professional, publication-ready press releases for announcing new products or services. This press release prompt for ChatGPT guides you through writing structured announcements that follow standard industry format, including attention-grabbing headlines, datelines, opening paragraphs with the five Ws, detailed body sections on features and benefits, attributed leadership quotes, calls to action, company boilerplate, and media contact information. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping PR professionals, marketers, and founders transform launch details into coherent narratives that emphasize news value and business impact. The prompt structures each section to flow logically, building a cohesive story that captures media attention and communicates key differentiators. Reach for this prompt when you need to announce a product launch, service introduction, or company milestone with a format that journalists and news outlets expect. ● Creates complete press releases with all required sections: headline, dateline, lead paragraph, body, quotes, CTA, boilerplate, and contacts ● Structures content to answer who, what, when, where, and why in the opening, then expands on features, benefits, and market fit ● Includes placeholders for attributed leadership quotes that add strategic perspective beyond factual restatement ● Formats output to match publication standards that journalists and media outlets recognize and can easily adapt ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert public relations professional writing a press release to announce a product or service launch.

## Task
Craft a compelling, publication-ready press release that follows standard industry format and captures media attention.

## Context
{{launch-details}}

Ensure each sentence flows logically from the previous one, building a cohesive narrative that emphasizes the news value and business impact.

## Output
Structure the press release with:

**Headline**: Attention-grabbing, newsworthy title (10-15 words)

**Dateline**: [CITY, STATE] – [Launch date]

**Opening paragraph**: Lead with who, what, when, where, why – summarizing the core announcement in 2-3 sentences

**Body paragraphs**:
- Detailed product/service features and benefits
- How it addresses market needs or solves customer problems
- Key differentiators and unique value

**Quote section**: Include 1-2 attributed quotes from company leadership that add strategic perspective or human interest (not just restating facts)

**Call to action**: Where to learn more, how to purchase/sign up, or next steps for interested parties

**Boilerplate**: Standard "About [Company]" paragraph (50-100 words) describing the organization, its mission, and relevant background

**Media contact**: Contact name, email, phone
```
