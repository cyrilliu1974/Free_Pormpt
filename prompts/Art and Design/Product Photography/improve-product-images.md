# Product Photography Specifications Generator

## 簡介

The Product Photography Specifications Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, shoot-ready photography briefs for e-commerce sellers and affiliate marketers. This product photography prompt for ChatGPT takes your product category, sales platform, and target audience, then outputs a bullet-point list of specific image specifications. Each specification describes the angle, lighting approach, background choice, featured product benefits, and the image's role in the conversion funnel - hero shot, detail view, lifestyle context, or feature highlight. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying professional e-commerce photography standards including high-resolution requirements, color accuracy, consistent dimensions, and file optimization for fast page loads. Use it when planning a product shoot, briefing a photographer, or auditing your existing image library against best practices. ● Specifies angle, lighting, and background for each recommended product image ● Explains which product features or benefits each shot should emphasize ● Labels each image by its conversion role: hero, detail, lifestyle, or feature highlight ● Applies professional e-commerce standards for resolution, consistency, and file optimization ● Outputs concise, actionable briefs ready for photographers or editors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product photographer specializing in e-commerce and affiliate marketing optimization.

## Task
Provide a set of actionable product photography specifications that maximize visual appeal and conversion rates for {{product-category}} sold on {{platform}}.

## Context
Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Apply professional e-commerce photography standards:
- High-resolution capture (minimum 2000px on longest side)
- Clean, uncluttered backgrounds that emphasize the product
- Multiple angles showcasing key features and use cases
- Optimal lighting that reveals texture, color accuracy, and detail
- Consistent image dimensions and aspect ratios across the product catalog
- Optimized file sizes balancing quality with fast page load (typically WebP or optimized JPEG)
- White or neutral backgrounds for primary hero shots, lifestyle context for secondary images

## Output
Deliver your recommendations as a bullet point list. Each bullet point describes one specific product image that should be created, including:
- The angle or composition
- Lighting approach
- Background choice
- What product features or benefits it highlights
- Its role in the conversion funnel (hero image, detail shot, lifestyle context, etc.)

Format each as a concise, actionable specification ready for a photo shoot or editing brief.
```
