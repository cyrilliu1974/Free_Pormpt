# Resume Screening Qualities Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Resume Screening Qualities Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies and explains the seven most critical qualities distinguishing exceptional candidates from average applicants for talent acquisition specialists and hiring managers. This resume screening prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes what makes candidates stand out in both applicant tracking systems and human evaluation, providing concrete resume indicators for each quality - including content, formatting, and presentation choices that signal hard and soft skills. The prompt delivers a numbered breakdown of seven characteristics, explaining why each matters to employers, the business value it creates, and specific evidence recruiters should look for during the initial 6–7 second resume scan. Use cases include training new recruiters, standardizing resume evaluation criteria, and refining candidate selection frameworks across industries and career levels. Reach for this prompt when you need to build or document a systematic approach to identifying high-potential candidates quickly, or when you want to articulate what separates standout applicants from the rest of the pool. ● Explains seven qualities that apply across industries and career levels, not role-specific checklists. ● Details both the business impact of each quality and the specific resume evidence that demonstrates it. ● Addresses what signals candidate strength in ATS parsing and human evaluation simultaneously. ● Provides a structured framework recruiters and hiring managers can apply immediately to improve selection accuracy. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert talent acquisition specialist who has screened thousands of resumes across organizations of all sizes.

## Task

Identify and explain the seven most critical qualities that distinguish exceptional candidates from average applicants when reviewing resumes. Address what makes candidates stand out in both applicant tracking systems and human evaluation, with qualities that apply across industries and career levels.

## Context

Recruiter context: {{hiring-context}}

In today's competitive hiring landscape, recruiters spend only 6–7 seconds on initial resume reviews. Your analysis should reflect what immediately signals candidate strength and organizational fit from both recruiter and hiring manager perspectives.

## Output

For each of the seven qualities, provide:

1. **The specific characteristic** – name and brief definition
2. **Why it matters to employers** – business value and hiring impact
3. **Evidence recruiters look for** – concrete resume indicators including content, formatting, and presentation choices that demonstrate both hard skills and soft skills

Format your response as a numbered list (1–7) with clear headings for each quality, followed by detailed explanations and specific examples of what to look for on resumes.
```
