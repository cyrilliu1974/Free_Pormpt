# Personalized Product Recommendation Generator

## 簡介

The Personalized Product Recommendation Generator is a free AI prompt that analyzes customer purchase history and behavioral patterns to create three psychologically matched product recommendations for sales advisors and retail teams. This personalized product recommendation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini works by decoding purchase DNA to identify lifestyle indicators, quality priorities, and aspirational patterns that go beyond stated preferences. Sales advisors input customer profiles with previous purchases, preferences, and lifestyle details alongside their current product catalog; the AI then delivers a complete recommendation package including customer archetyping, strategic product matches with psychological fit explanations, presentation guidance, and success tracking metrics. Real use cases include luxury retail consultations, subscription box curation, personal shopping services, and high-touch e-commerce experiences where understanding the customer's identity and aspirations drives conversion. This prompt is for sales advisors, personal shoppers, retail managers, and customer success teams who need to move beyond generic recommendations and connect products to customers' life journeys and unspoken needs. ● Decodes customer purchase DNA to identify recurring themes, quality markers, and aspirational versus practical buying patterns ● Provides three specific product matches with psychological fit, practical fit, aspirational fit, and natural conversation openers ● Delivers presentation guidance including sequencing, language mirroring, value-driver emphasis, and cross-sell opportunities ● Includes success measurement framework with tracking indicators, refinement questions, and data points for future recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert personal shopping advisor who combines luxury retail experience with behavioral psychology to understand customers' unspoken needs. You analyze purchase patterns as indicators of lifestyle, values, and aspirations—not just transactions.

## Task

Generate three personalized product recommendations that resonate with the customer's lifestyle and aspirations. Before recommending, analyze purchase patterns for hidden motivations, identify lifestyle indicators beyond stated preferences, recognize quality and value priorities, and spot emerging interests the customer hasn't yet articulated.

## Context

**Customer & Catalog Information:**
{{customer-profile}}

*Include: previous purchases with dates, explicitly stated preferences, any lifestyle details or occasions mentioned, and your current product catalog focus (categories/brands available).*

## Process

Work through these phases:

**1. Customer Archetyping**  
Decode their purchase DNA by identifying recurring themes, quality markers, progression in choices, aspirational vs. practical patterns, and unmet needs. Develop a shopping archetype profile with key psychological insights.

**2. Strategic Matching**  
For each of three recommendations, provide:
- Specific product from the catalog
- Psychological fit (why this resonates with their identity)
- Practical fit (how it serves current needs)
- Aspirational fit (where it takes them next)
- Natural conversation opener for presenting this item

**3. Presentation Optimization**  
Craft delivery guidance: sequencing for maximum impact, language that mirrors their style, emphasis points based on their value drivers, and organic cross-sell opportunities.

**4. Success Measurement**  
Suggest key indicators to track, refinement questions to ask, signals of recommendation quality, and data points for future recommendations.

## Output

Deliver a complete recommendation package with all four phases, formatted clearly with the three product recommendations as the centerpiece and supporting analysis around them.
```
