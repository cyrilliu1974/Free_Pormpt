# Social Media Listening Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Listening Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds tailored monitoring frameworks for brands tracking mentions, competitors, and industry conversations. This social media listening prompt for ChatGPT creates a markdown table organizing platforms, keyword combinations, and monitoring cadences (real-time for crisis terms, daily for brand mentions, weekly for broad trends). It identifies 5-7 key influencers with relevance scores and engagement recommendations, helping marketing teams stay responsive to sentiment shifts, competitor moves, and emerging conversations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, adapting to your brand's industry, competitive landscape, and target audience. Marketing managers, social media analysts, and brand strategists use it to replace ad-hoc monitoring with a repeatable framework that balances urgency and efficiency. ● Organizes keywords by signal type: brand terms, competitor names, industry hashtags, pain points, and influencer handles ● Assigns monitoring frequency with justification (real-time crisis alerts vs. weekly trend reviews) ● Identifies 5-7 relevant influencers with engagement strategies tailored to your audience ● Outputs a clean markdown table ready to share with teams or import into monitoring dashboards ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist building a social media listening framework.

## Task
Develop a structured monitoring plan that covers:
- Brand mentions and sentiment tracking
- Competitor activity and positioning
- Key influencer identification and engagement patterns
- Emerging conversations and trends in the industry

Create a strategy tailored to {{brand-and-industry}} that accounts for platform-specific behaviors, optimal keyword combinations, and monitoring cadences appropriate to different signal types (crisis keywords require real-time tracking; broad industry trends can be reviewed weekly).

## Context
Competitors to track: {{competitors}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with three columns: **Platform**, **Keywords**, and **Monitoring Frequency**.

For each row, include:
- The specific platform(s)
- Keyword combinations (brand terms, competitor names, industry hashtags, product categories, pain points, influencer handles)
- Recommended monitoring frequency (real-time, daily, weekly) with brief justification

After the table, provide a short section identifying 5-7 key influencers in this space, explaining their relevance and suggested engagement approach.
```
