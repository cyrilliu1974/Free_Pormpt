# Innovative Product Idea Generator for Market Research

## 簡介

The Innovative Product Idea Generator for Market Research is a free AI prompt that generates 10 structured, market-ready product concepts tailored to any industry or topic you specify. This product ideation prompt for ChatGPT walks through a seven-step brainstorming process: it identifies 3-5 emerging industry trends, analyzes unmet consumer needs, then develops product concepts that leverage current technology to fill those gaps. Each idea includes a product name and description, target audience profile, market impact assessment, and feasibility analysis covering production complexity and go-to-market considerations. Teams use it to rapidly explore product opportunities, validate concepts against real trends, and build compelling briefs for stakeholder review. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to move from high-level topics to concrete, defensible product ideas backed by trend analysis and audience insights. ● Surfaces 3-5 key industry trends and connects each to specific consumer problems or gaps ● Delivers 10 unique product concepts with feature descriptions, target demographics, and competitive positioning ● Includes feasibility checks covering production cost, regulatory considerations, and technology readiness ● Outputs structured briefs ready for team review, investor pitches, or further design work ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a product development brainstorming expert generating innovative, market-ready product concepts.

## Task
Generate 10 innovative product ideas related to {{topic}}. Each idea must be unique, feasible for production, and address a clear market opportunity. Ground your ideas in emerging trends, current technology, and unmet consumer needs within the industry.

## Process
1. **Identify Emerging Trends**: Surface 3-5 key trends within the industry that show growth potential
2. **Analyze Consumer Needs**: Describe unaddressed problems or underserved needs tied to those trends
3. **Brainstorm Product Ideas**: Develop 10 product concepts that leverage modern technology to meet identified needs
4. **Product Description**: For each idea, explain its unique features and how it solves a specific problem
5. **Target Audience**: Define the primary demographic, interests, and behaviors for each product
6. **Potential Impact**: Assess market readiness, competitive positioning, and broader market effects
7. **Feasibility Check**: Evaluate production complexity, cost considerations, and regulatory factors

## Context
- Topic focus: {{topic}}
- Relevant industry context: {{industry-context}}

## Output
Provide a structured list of 10 product ideas. For each:
- **Product Name & Description**: Clear explanation of features and problem solved
- **Target Audience**: Demographics and psychographics
- **Market Impact**: Potential effects on audience and market
- **Feasibility**: Production and go-to-market considerations

Ensure ideas align with current industry advancements and represent genuine market gaps or emerging opportunities.
```
