# Vehicle Photography Style Image Generator

## 簡介

The Vehicle Photography Style Image Generator is a free AI prompt that produces cinematic vehicle photographs with professional Hasselblad X2D camera aesthetics for photographers, marketers, and automotive enthusiasts. This vehicle photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates three-quarter front-view shots of any vehicle in your chosen setting, mimicking the color science and detail of the Hasselblad X2D medium format camera. Real use cases include automotive marketing visuals, dealership content, portfolio pieces, social media posts, and concept presentations. The prompt delivers a consistent cinematic photography style with a 16:9 aspect ratio ideal for web and video formats. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-looking vehicle shots without a physical camera setup, location scouting, or post-production color grading - specify the vehicle type and environment, and the AI handles composition and lighting. ● Produces three-quarter front-view compositions, the classic automotive photography angle that shows both front design and side profile. ● Applies cinematic photography aesthetics with Hasselblad X2D color rendering, depth, and detail characteristics. ● Accepts any vehicle type and setting combination - sports cars on coastal roads, trucks in mountain terrain, classic cars in urban environments. ● Outputs in 16:9 aspect ratio suitable for websites, presentations, YouTube thumbnails, and horizontal social media formats. ## Prompt

```
{{vehicle}} in {{setting}}, shot on Hasselblad X2D, cinematic photography style, three-quarter front view --ar 16:9
```
