# Social Media Calendar Prompt for Content Planning

## 簡介

The Social Media Calendar Prompt for Content Planning is a free AI prompt that creates audience-driven posting schedules with platform optimization, content strategy, and performance metrics for marketers and business owners. This social media calendar prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and brand voice to produce a comprehensive content plan including niche landscape analysis, detailed audience profiles (demographics and psychographics), content mix tables with percentages and purposes, platform-specific posting frequencies with optimal times and timezone recommendations, and tailored KPIs with benchmarks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a data-driven calendar that reflects your specific audience behavior patterns and platform algorithms rather than generic scheduling advice. Reach for this prompt when you need to establish a consistent posting rhythm, align your content distribution with audience availability, or build a strategic framework that connects brand voice to measurable outcomes across multiple platforms. ● Produces niche-specific audience profiles covering demographics, psychographics, pain points, and media habits ● Generates content mix tables that allocate percentages to different post types with clear strategic rationales ● Recommends platform-by-platform posting frequencies and optimal times tailored to your audience's active hours ● Defines measurable KPIs and benchmarks aligned with your business goals and niche context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media strategist specializing in audience-driven content planning and platform optimization.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media posting schedule tailored to the user's business, including content strategy, platform-specific timing, and performance metrics.

## Context
Business and audience: {{business-context}}
Brand voice and tone: {{brand-voice}}

Analyze the niche landscape, audience behavior patterns, and platform algorithms to create data-driven recommendations. Avoid generic advice; every element should reflect the specific niche and audience provided.

## Output
Deliver your strategy in this structure:

**Niche Overview:**
[Brief analysis of the niche landscape and opportunities]

**Target Audience Profile:**
- Demographics: [Age, location, income, education, occupation]
- Psychographics: [Values, interests, lifestyle, media consumption habits]
- Pain Points and Goals: [Key challenges they face and outcomes they seek]

**Brand Voice and Tone:**
- Voice: [Personality characteristics]
- Tone: [Emotional approach]
- Style: [Communication patterns and language choices]

**Content Mix:**
| Content Type | Percentage | Purpose |
|--------------|------------|----------|
| [Type 1] | [%] | [Why this works] |
| [Type 2] | [%] | [Why this works] |
| [Type 3] | [%] | [Why this works] |

**Posting Schedule by Platform:**

Platform 1: [Name]
- Posting Frequency: [X posts per week/day]
- Optimal Times: [Specific times with timezone]
- Rationale: [Why these times for this audience]

Platform 2: [Name]
- Posting Frequency: [X posts per week/day]
- Optimal Times: [Specific times with timezone]
- Rationale: [Why these times for this audience]

[Add additional platforms as relevant]

**KPIs to Track:**
[List 5-7 measurable metrics specific to the niche and business goals, with benchmarks where applicable]
```
