# Social Media Post Generator for Product Promotion

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Generator for Product Promotion is a free AI prompt that creates platform-specific, engagement-focused social media content for marketers and business owners looking to promote products or services. This social media post prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target audience's behavior on the specified platform and produces 3–5 ready-to-publish post concepts. Each concept includes an attention-grabbing hook, 2–3 sentences of benefit-driven body copy, a clear call-to-action, and 3–5 platform-appropriate hashtags. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and applies proven engagement techniques while respecting your brand voice. Posts vary in style - educational, aspirational, problem-solution, social proof, or urgency-driven - so you get a versatile content library in one run. Use this prompt when you need conversion-focused social media content quickly, whether you're launching a product, running a campaign, or maintaining a consistent posting schedule across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, or Twitter. ● Produces 3–5 complete post concepts with structured components: hook, body, CTA, and hashtags ● Tailors content to platform-specific features like Stories, Reels, polls, and threads ● Varies post formats across educational, aspirational, problem-solution, social proof, and urgency angles ● Maintains consistent brand voice while optimizing for audience behavior and engagement patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer specializing in audience engagement and conversion-focused content.

## Task
Create compelling social media posts that promote {{product-or-service}} for {{target-audience}} on {{platform}}. Each post must drive engagement and align with the brand voice described as: {{brand-voice}}.

## Context
Analyze the typical behavior and preferences of the target audience on the specified platform. Highlight the key differentiators and benefits that will resonate most. Use platform-native features (Stories, Reels, polls, threads, etc.) and apply proven engagement techniques: strong hooks, clear value propositions, and friction-free calls-to-action.

## Output
Deliver 3–5 complete post concepts as a bullet list. For each post, include:

- **Headline / Hook**: Attention-grabbing opening line
- **Body**: 2–3 sentences that connect the product benefit to audience needs or desires
- **Call-to-Action**: Clear next step (click, comment, share, purchase)
- **Hashtags**: 3–5 relevant, platform-appropriate tags

Ensure variety across posts (educational, aspirational, problem-solution, social proof, urgency) while maintaining consistent brand voice.
```
