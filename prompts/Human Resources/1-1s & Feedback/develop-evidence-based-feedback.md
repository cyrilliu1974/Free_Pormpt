# Evidence-Based Feedback Technique Generator

## 簡介

The Evidence-Based Feedback Technique Generator is a free AI prompt that produces actionable feedback methods grounded in organizational psychology, neuroscience, and behavioral economics for managers and team leaders. It generates five specific feedback techniques that explain the psychological principle behind each method, provide step-by-step implementation instructions, and highlight common pitfalls to avoid. This evidence-based feedback prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok draws on research about how the brain processes criticism, behavioral economics principles like loss aversion and social proof, and studies on intrinsic motivation to create feedback approaches that employees actually act upon. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic feedback advice and want techniques that address psychological resistance, create clear improvement pathways, and connect to what genuinely motivates behavior change rather than temporary compliance. ● Explains the neurological and behavioral principles that make each technique effective at bypassing defensiveness ● Provides concrete, sequenced implementation steps for applying each method in real conversations ● Identifies common execution pitfalls so leaders avoid undermining their own feedback ● Adapts to your specific leadership context through the customizable leadership-context variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in evidence-based feedback that drives behavioral change. Your recommendations are grounded in neuroscience, behavioral economics, and organizational psychology research on motivation, resistance, and lasting behavior change.

## Task
Provide 5 practical feedback techniques employees genuinely act upon. Most feedback fails because it triggers defensiveness, lacks specificity, or ignores intrinsic motivation. Focus on methods that bypass psychological resistance, create clear improvement pathways, and connect to what actually motivates behavior change.

## Context
{{leadership-context}}

Draw from research on how the brain processes criticism versus growth opportunities, behavioral economics principles (loss aversion, social proof), and studies distinguishing lasting change from temporary compliance.

## Output
For each technique, provide:

- **Technique name and psychological principle**: Explain *why* it works at a neurological or behavioral level
- **Implementation steps**: Concrete, sequenced actions to apply it
- **Common pitfalls**: What to avoid during execution

Structure with clear headings, bullet points, and actionable language for immediate application.
```
