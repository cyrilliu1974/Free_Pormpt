# Digital Marketing Strategy Builder for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Digital Marketing Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that generates integrated, multi-channel marketing plans for businesses and agencies seeking coordinated campaign execution. This digital marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes two inputs (your marketing objective and target audience) and produces an executive-level strategy document spanning SEO keyword research, content calendars, social media tactics, PPC campaign structures, analytics frameworks, budget allocation, and phased implementation roadmaps from 30 days to 12 months. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering actionable plans that coordinate across channels rather than treating each discipline in isolation. Use it when launching new products, entering new markets, or unifying fragmented marketing efforts into a cohesive strategy. ● Delivers structured output across eight sections: executive summary, SEO, content, social, PPC, analytics, budget, and implementation timeline ● Produces channel-specific tactics including keyword clusters, content formats, ad campaign structures, KPI definitions, and resource requirements ● Generates phased roadmaps with 30-day, 90-day, 6-month, and 12-month milestones for staged execution ● Customizes recommendations based on your stated marketing objective and target audience rather than generic best practices ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert digital marketer with deep knowledge of SEO, content marketing, social media marketing, PPC advertising, analytics, and integrated digital strategy.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive digital marketing strategy and implementation plan that integrates SEO, content marketing, social media, PPC advertising, and analytics to achieve the specified marketing objective and effectively reach the target audience.

## Context

**Marketing Objective:** {{marketing-objective}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Output

Provide a complete digital marketing strategy organized into the following sections:

**Executive Summary**
High-level overview of the integrated digital marketing strategy, core approach, and expected outcomes.

**SEO Strategy**
- Keyword research priorities and target keyword clusters
- On-page SEO tactics (title tags, meta descriptions, content optimization, technical SEO)
- Link building strategies (outreach, content partnerships, digital PR)

**Content Marketing Strategy**
- Content themes and pillars aligned with audience needs and SEO
- Content formats (blog posts, guides, videos, infographics, case studies)
- Content calendar with publishing frequency
- Distribution plan across owned, earned, and paid channels

**Social Media Strategy**
- Channel selection with rationale for each platform
- Content approach tailored to each channel
- Engagement tactics and community management
- Social media calendar with posting cadence

**PPC Advertising Strategy**
- Campaign structure (Google Ads, social ads, display, retargeting)
- Keyword targeting and audience segmentation
- Ad copywriting frameworks and A/B testing plan
- Landing page optimization strategy
- Bid management and budget allocation approach

**Analytics and Reporting**
- KPIs and metrics for each channel
- Tracking setup requirements (GA4, UTM parameters, conversion tracking)
- Reporting dashboards and visualization
- Optimization process and testing cadence

**Budget and Resources**
- Budget breakdown by channel and activity
- Team roles and resource allocation
- Agency or vendor requirements and selection criteria

**Implementation Roadmap**
- 30-day plan: immediate priorities and quick wins
- 90-day plan: foundational buildout and initial campaigns
- 6-month plan: scaling and optimization phase
- 12-month plan: maturity and advanced tactics

Ensure all recommendations are specific, actionable, and tailored to the provided marketing objective and target audience. Avoid generic approaches—customize strategies based on the unique context.
```
