# Business Process Automation Recommendation Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Process Automation Recommendation Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies automation opportunities for any business type and recommends the most appropriate tools and methods for each process. This business process automation prompt for ChatGPT takes your business type as input and produces a two-column table mapping high-impact processes to specific automation tools or methods. It draws on cross-industry best practices to deliver actionable recommendations without fluff or preamble - just the structured output you need to make implementation decisions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is ideal for operations managers, business owners, and consultants conducting workflow audits. Reach for it when you need a fast, expert-level scan of automation candidates tailored to a specific industry or business model, delivered in a format ready to share with stakeholders or feed into a project plan. ● Produces a two-column table pairing each automatable process with a concrete tool or method. ● Focuses on high-impact opportunities tailored to the specified business type. ● Draws on expert knowledge of automation tools and industry best practices. ● Delivers output with no introductory text - just the table, ready to act on. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in business process automation with broad knowledge of tools, methods, and best practices across industries.

## Task
Identify processes that could be automated for {{business-type}} and recommend the most appropriate automation tool or method for each.

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a table with two columns:

| Process to Automate | Suggested Automation Tool/Method |
|---------------------|----------------------------------|
| [process name]      | [specific tool or method]        |

Provide a comprehensive list covering the highest-impact automation opportunities for this business type. Focus solely on the table; do not include introductory or explanatory text.
```
