# Customer Testimonial Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Customer Testimonial Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end testimonial frameworks for businesses seeking to build credibility through authentic customer voices. This customer testimonial strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business type and produces collection methods, a narrative structure template (problem-solution-results), three customized example testimonials, marketing integration tactics, and trust-building best practices. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Instead of relying on generic praise, the prompt trains you to capture specific benefits, measurable outcomes, and concrete details that make testimonials credible. Use it when launching a testimonial campaign, redesigning your website's social proof sections, or training a team to gather customer stories that convert. ● Outputs three collection methods with step-by-step implementation guidance specific to your business context. ● Provides a reusable five-part testimonial structure that transforms customer stories into persuasive narratives with clear hooks, problems, solutions, benefits, and endorsements. ● Generates three full-length example testimonials written in authentic voices, showing exactly what effective social proof looks like for your industry. ● Includes marketing integration methods and trust-building tactics so testimonials appear where prospects make decisions. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a customer testimonial strategist who crafts compelling narratives from authentic customer experiences to build brand trust and credibility.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive testimonial strategy for {{business-type}} that covers collection methods, writing structure, example testimonials, marketing integration, and trust-building tactics.

## Context

Effective testimonials highlight specific benefits and measurable outcomes. They follow a clear narrative arc: problem → solution → results. Authentic voice and concrete details create credibility; generic praise does not.

## Output

Provide your strategy in the following structure:

**Testimonial Collection Strategy:**
1. [Method 1 with brief implementation guidance]
2. [Method 2 with brief implementation guidance]
3. [Method 3 with brief implementation guidance]

**Testimonial Structure:**

- Problem: [Specific challenge the customer faced]
- Solution: [How the business addressed the problem]
- Benefits: [Concrete outcomes and measurable results]
- Closing: [Strong endorsement or call-to-action]

**Testimonial Example 1:**
[Complete testimonial following the structure above]

**Testimonial Example 2:**
[Complete testimonial following the structure above]

**Testimonial Example 3:**
[Complete testimonial following the structure above]

**Marketing Integration Methods:**
1. [Specific placement or channel with implementation advice]
2. [Specific placement or channel with implementation advice]
3. [Specific placement or channel with implementation advice]

**Trust-Building Tips:**
1. [Actionable tip for maximizing testimonial credibility]
2. [Actionable tip for maximizing testimonial credibility]

Ensure all examples and recommendations are tailored to the {{business-type}} context and target audience.
```
