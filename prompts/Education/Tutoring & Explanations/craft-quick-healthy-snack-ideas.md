# Quick Healthy Snack Ideas With Nutritional Guidance

## 簡介

The Quick Healthy Snack Ideas With Nutritional Guidance is a free AI prompt that generates nutrient-dense snack recipes for busy professionals who need energy-sustaining options ready in under five minutes. This healthy snack prompt for ChatGPT produces ten complete snack recommendations, each with precise ingredient measurements, calorie calculations, step-by-step prep instructions, and practical storage advice. It balances selections across high-protein snacks for muscle maintenance, high-fiber choices for digestive health, and healthy-fat options for brain function. The prompt accommodates dietary constraints through a single variable and runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Nutritionists, meal-prep enthusiasts, and anyone looking to break the cycle of processed snacking will find recipes that deliver sustained energy without kitchen complexity. ● Provides exact ingredient quantities, calorie counts, and macronutrient breakdowns for ten snack options. ● Explains the specific nutritional benefit of each snack - muscle support, satiety, or cognitive function. ● Includes substitution suggestions and storage methods so recipes adapt to pantry availability. ● Keeps prep time under five minutes per snack, making healthy eating practical for busy schedules. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist and meal prep specialist combining evidence-based nutrition science with practical culinary skills to help busy professionals optimize their energy and health.

## Task
Create a list of 10 healthy snack recommendations that are quick to prepare (under 5 minutes), nutrient-dense, and portioned for sustained energy. Balance the mix across macronutrients: include high-protein options for muscle maintenance, high-fiber choices for digestive health and satiety, and healthy-fat selections for brain function and hormone production.

## Context
{{dietary-constraints}}

For each snack, provide precise ingredient measurements, step-by-step preparation instructions, and accurate calorie calculations. Include practical tips for ingredient substitutions and storage methods.

## Output
Structure each snack recommendation with:

- **Snack name** as the heading
- **Primary macronutrient benefit** (high-protein, high-fiber, or healthy fat)
- **Calorie count**
- **Ingredient list** with measurements in bullet points
- **Preparation steps** in numbered format
- **Nutritional benefits** explaining contribution to sustained energy and satiety
- **Storage and substitution tips** where relevant
```
