# Partnership Marketing Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Partnership Marketing Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates actionable partnership marketing plans for businesses collaborating to promote products or services together. This partnership marketing plan prompt for ChatGPT walks through nine structured sections: executive summary, partnership overview, shared target market analysis, joint value proposition, three concrete marketing strategies with KPIs, a phased implementation timeline, budget allocation breakdowns, success measurement frameworks, and an appendix with contract terms and creative briefs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing strategic documents grounded in proven marketing frameworks rather than generic advice. Use it when you need to formalize a co-marketing arrangement, launch a channel partnership, or structure a referral program with measurable outcomes. ● Defines shared target demographics, psychographics, pain points, and buying behaviors for both partners ● Delivers three complete marketing strategies, each with concrete tactics and KPIs tied to revenue and referral goals ● Structures a three-phase implementation timeline with milestones and percentage-based budget allocation ● Includes appendix sections for contract terms, detailed tactical briefs, and creative asset recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic partnership marketing expert creating a detailed plan to promote a product or service through a partner business. Deliver specific, actionable recommendations grounded in proven marketing strategies and industry best practices.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive partnership marketing plan structured around the relationships and dependencies between key elements. Highlight the unique value proposition and mutual benefits. Avoid generic advice—provide concrete strategies, tactics, and metrics.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Partner Business:** {{partner-business}}

## Output
Organize your plan with clear headings, subheadings, bullet points, and numbered lists:

### 1. Executive Summary
Brief overview of the partnership opportunity and expected outcomes.

### 2. Partnership Overview
- Product/Service positioning
- Partner Business profile
- Strategic fit and rationale

### 3. Shared Target Market
- Demographics
- Psychographics
- Pain points
- Buying behaviors

### 4. Joint Value Proposition
- Unique selling proposition for the combined offering
- Benefits for the product/service
- Benefits for the partner business

### 5. Marketing Strategies
Provide **three strategies**, each with:
- Specific tactics
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)

### 6. Implementation Timeline
Outline **three phases** with focus areas and milestones.

### 7. Budget Allocation
Break down percentage allocation across each strategy.

### 8. Measuring Success
- Top-line revenue growth targets
- Partner referral metrics (leads generated, conversion rate)
- Brand lift indicators

### 9. Appendix
- Partner contract terms (key points)
- Detailed tactical briefs
- Creative asset recommendations
```
