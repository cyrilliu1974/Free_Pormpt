# Editorial Learning Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Editorial Learning Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hand-drawn style educational illustrations featuring diverse people in circular, flowing compositions for educators, edtech platforms, and corporate training teams. This editorial learning illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 1024×1024 images with soft pastel backgrounds, simplified human figures, and minimal flat rendering - no shadows, gradients, or photorealism. Characters are shown reading, discussing, teaching, and collaborating in arrangements that symbolize connection and growth. The prompt enforces a cohesive color palette of soft mint greens, blues, teals, and white, making it ideal for educational websites, corporate branding, university materials, and editorial publications that need consistent visual identity. Reach for this prompt when you need human-centered illustrations that communicate approachability and intelligence without the cost or turnaround time of commissioning custom artwork. ● Produces circular or flowing compositions with open negative space and calm, airy layouts suitable for digital and print. ● Enforces abstract, expressive faces with minimal details - simple eyes and noses - and flat rendering with no textures or 3D effects. ● Customizable concept variable lets you theme scenes around collaboration, growth, mentorship, or any learning topic. ● Outputs 1024×1024 resolution images ready for web headers, slide decks, social media, and editorial use. ## Prompt

```
## Style
Minimal, flat, hand-drawn editorial illustration with soft outlines and rounded simplified human figures. Abstract, expressive faces with minimal details—simple eyes and noses, no realism. Flat rendering with no shadows, gradients, or textures.

## Subject
A diverse group of simplified people forming a circular or flowing composition that symbolizes {{concept}}. Characters are standing, sitting, reading books, using laptops, discussing, teaching, and learning from each other. Let the people themselves form the structure through their arrangement and interaction.

## Color Palette
- Background: Soft pastel green or muted mint
- Primary: Blue, teal, soft navy
- Secondary: White, light gray
- Accents: Muted cyan

Clothing uses limited tones of blue, teal, white, and soft navy.

## Composition
Circular or flowing arrangement with open negative space for a calm, airy, human-centered feel. Suitable for corporate education, universities, edtech platforms, and editorial publications.

## Avoid
Photorealistic rendering, 3D effects, gradients, shadows, large symbolic hands, oversized objects, realistic faces, heavy textures, clutter, logos, text.

## Output
1024×1024 resolution.
```
