# Sales Performance Metrics and KPI Framework

## 簡介

The Sales Performance Metrics and KPI Framework is a free AI prompt that designs comprehensive performance tracking systems for sales teams and revenue leaders. This sales metrics prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your team's context - sales cycle length, market dynamics, current challenges - and produces a markdown table of key performance indicators covering revenue targets, pipeline conversion rates, activity productivity, and customer satisfaction measures. Each metric includes a specific target and timeframe, followed by explanations of why it matters and how to achieve it. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, transforming high-level sales goals into actionable, data-driven benchmarks. Sales managers use it to replace vague objectives with quantifiable targets; operations teams deploy it to build dashboards; and executives rely on it to align front-line activity with broader business objectives. ● Produces a complete metrics table with revenue, pipeline health, activity, and customer relationship KPIs ● Sets realistic targets grounded in historical data, industry benchmarks, and your specific sales cycle ● Balances leading indicators (activity, pipeline) with lagging outcomes (closed deals, revenue) ● Includes metric-by-metric rationale so teams understand what to track and why it drives results ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales performance analyst designing a metrics framework for a sales team.

## Task
Define clear, measurable, and achievable sales metrics and KPIs that align with the sales team's goals. Create a comprehensive performance tracking system that balances quantitative and qualitative measures.

## Context
{{sales-team-context}}

Analyze current performance patterns, determine metrics that directly drive results, and set realistic targets based on historical data and industry benchmarks. Ensure all metrics align with broader business objectives and are appropriate for the sales cycle and market dynamics described above.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with three columns: **Metric**, **Target**, and **Timeframe**.

Include metrics that cover:
- Revenue and deal volume indicators
- Pipeline health and conversion rates
- Sales activity and productivity measures
- Customer satisfaction and relationship quality

Below the table, provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) for each metric that clarifies its importance and how the team can achieve it. Ensure targets are specific, time-bound, and realistic for the context provided.
```
