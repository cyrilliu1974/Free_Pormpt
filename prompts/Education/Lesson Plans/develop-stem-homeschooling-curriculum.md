# STEM Homeschooling Curriculum Planner

## 簡介

The STEM Homeschooling Curriculum Planner is a free AI prompt that creates customized STEM education plans for homeschooling families. This STEM curriculum prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini generates age-appropriate learning objectives, hands-on project ideas spanning science, technology, engineering, and math, and structured weekly challenge tables that progressively build skills. The prompt adapts to your specific context - children's ages and grade levels, daily schedule constraints, and available resources including budget, materials, technology access, and outdoor space. Real homeschooling families use it to transition from vague STEM intentions into concrete weekly lesson plans that fit their actual circumstances. It is designed for homeschooling parents who want structured, actionable STEM activities without spending hours researching age-appropriate projects or worrying whether activities match their available time and materials. ● Produces age-bracketed learning objectives (typically 5-7, 8-10, 11-13 year ranges) matched to developmental stages ● Generates 3-4 hands-on projects per age group, ensuring activities fit your stated budget, materials, and schedule ● Creates a 4-week progressive challenge table with materials lists, time estimates, and learning outcomes ● Recommends both online and offline resources, prioritizing free and low-cost options ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert STEM education consultant specializing in developing engaging, age-appropriate homeschooling activities and curricula.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan for integrating STEM education into daily homeschooling activities. Tailor the plan to the children's ages, the family's schedule, and available resources.

## Context
- Children's ages and grade levels: {{childrens-ages}}
- Homeschooling schedule and constraints: {{schedule-context}}
- Available resources (budget, materials, tech access, outdoor space): {{available-resources}}

## Output
Provide age-appropriate sections for each child or age bracket covered in {{childrens-ages}}. For each age group (typically 5-7, 8-10, 11-13), include:

**Learning Objectives:**
- 3-4 key STEM objectives appropriate to developmental stage

**Project Ideas:**
1. Numbered list of 3-4 hands-on projects spanning science, technology, engineering, and math
2. Ensure projects align with {{available-resources}} and {{schedule-context}}

**Resources:**
- Mix of online and offline resources supporting the objectives and projects
- Include free and low-cost options

**Weekly STEM Challenge Table:**
Create a 4-week challenge progression with:

| Week | Challenge Name | Materials Needed | Time Required | Learning Outcomes |
|------|----------------|------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1-4  | Progressive challenges building on each other |

Ensure variety across STEM domains, accommodate the family's schedule, and progressively build skills. Make all activities practical and immediately actionable.
```
