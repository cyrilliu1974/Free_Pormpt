# Eisenhower Matrix Task Prioritization System

## 簡介

The Eisenhower Matrix Task Prioritization System is a free AI prompt that creates a customized workflow optimization framework for professionals seeking structured task management based on urgency and importance. This task prioritization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your specific job role and generates a complete Eisenhower Matrix adaptation that categorizes tasks into four quadrants: Urgent and Important (Do), Important but Not Urgent (Decide), Urgent but Not Important (Delegate), and Neither (Delete). The system identifies typical task types for your role, defines clear prioritization criteria for each quadrant, and provides concrete management strategies tailored to your industry's pressures and stakeholder expectations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering implementation guidelines, daily review processes, delegation protocols, and quantifiable success metrics. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond generic productivity advice and build a prioritization system that reflects the real-world complexity of your position, including role-specific deadlines, reporting structures, and strategic objectives. ● Receives your job role as input and generates task categories, prioritization rules, and management strategies customized to your responsibilities ● Provides detailed guidance for all four Eisenhower Matrix quadrants with role-specific examples and delegation opportunities ● Includes step-by-step implementation instructions, review cadences, tracking methods, and adjustment triggers ● Delivers measurable success metrics aligned with your role's productivity indicators and quality standards ## Prompt
