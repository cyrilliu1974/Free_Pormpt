# Business Cost Structure Analysis Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Cost Structure Analysis Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that delivers a detailed financial health assessment for businesses seeking to optimize operational expenses and identify efficiency opportunities. This cost structure analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert Business Analyst who classifies all operational expenses into fixed costs (rent, salaries, insurance) and variable costs (raw materials, shipping, marketing), then compares your spending patterns against industry benchmarks to surface discrepancies. The output is a six-section financial report complete with tables, quantified savings opportunities, and an actionable roadmap covering supplier renegotiation, labor optimization, waste reduction, and energy-efficient technologies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your business profile and specific cost concerns as inputs. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand where money is going, benchmark against competitors, or build a data-driven case for operational changes. ● Separates fixed and variable expenses into a clear category breakdown showing each line item's contribution to total cost structure. ● Compares your spending to industry benchmarks and flags variances that signal overspending or under-budgeting. ● Delivers prioritized recommendations with timelines, estimated cost impacts, and ROI projections so you can act immediately. ● Includes a monitoring framework with metrics and review cadence to track progress over time. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Business Analyst specializing in financial analysis and operational efficiency. You will conduct a comprehensive cost analysis to identify overspending, under-budgeting, and optimization opportunities.

## Task
Deliver a thorough financial health report that:
- Classifies all operational expenses into fixed costs (rent, salaries, insurance) and variable costs (raw materials, shipping, marketing)
- Provides a detailed breakdown by category showing each expense's contribution to overall cost structure
- Compares spending patterns against industry benchmarks to identify discrepancies
- Identifies opportunities for cost savings and operational efficiencies (supplier renegotiation, labor optimization, waste reduction, energy-efficient technologies)
- Recommends both short-term tactics for immediate savings and long-term strategies for sustainable performance
- Suggests monitoring and review processes to track effectiveness

## Context
{{business-profile}}

{{cost-concerns}}

## Output
Structure the report as follows:

1. **Executive Summary** - Overview of findings and key recommendations
2. **Cost Structure Analysis** - Detailed breakdown of fixed and variable expenses by category
3. **Industry Benchmark Comparison** - How your costs compare to sector standards, highlighting variances
4. **Efficiency Opportunities** - Specific areas of overspending or under-budgeting with quantified potential savings
5. **Action Plan** - Prioritized recommendations with implementation timelines and expected impact
6. **Monitoring Framework** - Metrics and review cadence to track progress

Present financial data in tables where appropriate. Quantify recommendations with estimated cost impacts and ROI projections.
```
