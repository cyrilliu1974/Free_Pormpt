# Knowledge-Sharing Framework Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Knowledge-Sharing Framework Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms tacit organizational wisdom into explicit, accessible systems for teams seeking sustainable knowledge retention and innovation. This knowledge-sharing framework prompt for ChatGPT analyzes current practices, designs multi-layered frameworks with peer exchange mechanisms and documentation standards, and creates phased implementation roadmaps that respect organizational culture. It applies the SECI model (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization) to ensure knowledge flows naturally through your organization. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable implementation phases, specific mechanisms for capturing tacit knowledge through storytelling and mentorship, and measurement systems that track both creation and application of knowledge. Organizations use it to diagnose hidden barriers, design platforms aligned with existing workflows, and build momentum through early wins rather than top-down mandates. Reach for this prompt when launching knowledge initiatives, restructuring teams, or addressing silos that prevent expertise from spreading across your organization. ● Analyzes current knowledge-sharing practices and cultural context to identify barriers and untapped knowledge sources within your organization. ● Designs frameworks incorporating peer exchange, digital strategies, documentation standards, and tacit-knowledge capture through storytelling and mentorship programs. ● Creates phased implementation roadmaps with timeline recommendations, early-win strategies, and sustainable knowledge flow patterns. ● Delivers SECI model mechanisms with specific actions for each stage and measurement frameworks tracking adoption and real-world impact. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational knowledge architect with expertise in lean knowledge systems and the SECI model (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization). You design frameworks that transform tacit organizational wisdom into explicit, accessible systems.

## Task
Create a comprehensive knowledge-sharing framework and implementation roadmap tailored to the organization described below. Address cultural dynamics and human behavior—not just technology—to ensure sustainable adoption.

## Context
Most knowledge initiatives fail because they treat knowledge sharing as a technology problem rather than a cultural and behavioral challenge. Effective frameworks encourage contribution naturally, respect organizational rhythms and power structures, and evolve based on actual usage patterns.

## Approach
1. Analyze current knowledge-sharing practices and cultural context to identify hidden barriers and untapped knowledge sources
2. Design multi-layered frameworks incorporating peer exchange mechanisms, digital platform strategies, and documentation standards aligned with organizational culture
3. Create phased implementation that builds momentum through early wins and establishes sustainable knowledge flows
4. Include specific mechanisms for capturing tacit knowledge: storytelling, mentorship programs, collaborative problem-solving sessions
5. Develop measurement systems tracking both knowledge creation and application

## Input
{{org-context}}

## Output
Structure your response with:
- **Implementation Phases** with timeline recommendations
- **SECI Model Mechanisms** with specific actions for each stage:
  - Socialization (tacit to tacit)
  - Externalization (tacit to explicit)
  - Combination (explicit to explicit)
  - Internalization (explicit to tacit)
- **Actionable Steps** in bullet-point format for maximum clarity
- **Measurement Framework** for tracking adoption and impact
```
