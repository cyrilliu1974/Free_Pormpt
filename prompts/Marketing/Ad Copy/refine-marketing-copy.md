# Marketing Copy Rewriter for Digital Products

## 簡介

The Marketing Copy Rewriter for Digital Products is a free AI prompt that rewrites marketing assets into persuasive, audience-focused copy for digital products and online courses. This marketing copy prompt for ChatGPT takes your existing template and product context - features, audience pain points, unique selling points, testimonials - and restructures it into conversion-optimized copy that emphasizes benefits over features. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini by preserving your original template's style and tone while reframing every element through the lens of tangible outcomes. Creators, course builders, and product marketers use it to turn feature lists into compelling narratives that address real audience goals, incorporate social proof naturally, and drive clear calls to action with urgency and value reinforcement. Reach for this prompt when you have a working draft or template that needs transformation from descriptive to persuasive, or when you need to adapt proven copy structures to new product contexts without losing voice. ● Preserves the structure, style, and tone of your original template while adapting content to your specific product and audience ● Translates features into tangible benefits with persuasive language that emphasizes outcomes and value ● Integrates testimonials, case studies, and credentials naturally to build trust and authority ● Generates clear calls to action with urgency elements and reinforced value propositions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in marketing copy for digital products and online courses.

## Task
Rewrite the provided asset using the context supplied. Preserve the original template's structure, style, and tone while adapting content to create persuasive, benefits-driven copy that resonates with the target audience.

## Context
{{product-and-audience-context}}

Include:
- Product or course description and key features
- Target audience demographics, pain points, and goals
- Unique selling points and differentiators
- Any social proof, testimonials, or credentials
- Original asset template to match

## Output
Structure the rewritten copy with:

**Introduction**
Open with the core benefit or unique selling point that immediately captures attention.

**Features and Benefits**
Translate each key feature into a tangible benefit for the audience. Use persuasive language that emphasizes value and outcomes, not just capabilities.

**Social Proof and Credibility**
Incorporate testimonials, case studies, success stories, endorsements, or creator expertise to build trust and authority.

**Call to Action**
Drive the reader toward a specific action (enroll, purchase, learn more). Create urgency or scarcity where appropriate and reinforce the main value proposition.

Use clear, concise language. Avoid jargon unless the audience expects it. Match the tone and style of the original template throughout.
```
