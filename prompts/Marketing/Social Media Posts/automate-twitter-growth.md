# Twitter Growth Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Twitter Growth Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that creates a complete follower and engagement growth plan for businesses and brands on Twitter. This Twitter growth strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business details, target audience, industry, current follower count, and competitors to produce a structured markdown table with three core strategy categories: content planning (tweet types, posting frequency, themes, scheduling), engagement tactics (reply strategies, community interaction, conversation starters), and growth optimization (leveraging Twitter Spaces, polls, threads, lists, hashtags, partnerships, and profile tuning). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering specific, actionable steps tailored to your competitive landscape. Use it when you need a structured roadmap to scale your Twitter presence rather than guessing at posting schedules or engagement methods. ● Builds a content calendar with diverse tweet types, posting frequency, and themes matched to your target audience and industry ● Outlines engagement tactics including reply strategies, community interaction methods, and conversation starters ● Identifies Twitter features to exploit - Spaces, polls, threads, lists - plus hashtag strategy and partnership opportunities ● Delivers every strategy with implementation steps and expected impact, not vague advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media marketing expert specializing in Twitter growth strategies.

## Task
Create a comprehensive plan to increase followers and engagement for {{business-or-brand}} on Twitter. Develop a content calendar with diverse tweet types, outline engagement strategies, and provide guidance on leveraging Twitter features effectively.

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Current follower count: {{current-followers}}
- Main competitors: {{competitors}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table organized into three categories:

| Category | Strategy | Implementation | Expected Impact |
|----------|----------|----------------|----------------|

**Content Planning**: Tweet types, posting frequency, content themes, scheduling approach

**Engagement Tactics**: Reply strategies, community interaction, conversation starters, audience participation methods

**Growth Hacks**: Twitter features to leverage (Spaces, polls, threads, lists), partnership opportunities, hashtag strategy, profile optimization

For each strategy, provide specific, actionable steps tailored to the business context and competitive landscape.
```
