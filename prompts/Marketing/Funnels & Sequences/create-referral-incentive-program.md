# Referral Incentive Program Builder

## 簡介

The Referral Incentive Program Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured referral marketing plans for businesses looking to turn existing customers into acquisition channels. This referral program prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a multi-section plan including three distinct incentive tiers, tracking implementation methods, multi-channel engagement tactics, implementation timelines, management workflows, and measurable KPIs. You provide your business context - customer segments, purchase behavior, lifetime value, and market positioning - and the prompt outputs a tailored referral strategy that balances compelling rewards with operational feasibility. Marketing teams use it to launch word-of-mouth programs, SaaS founders rely on it to design viral loop mechanics, and customer success managers deploy it to increase retention through advocacy. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured framework to design or refresh a customer referral program rather than guessing at reward structures or tracking methods. ● Generates three-tier incentive structures with clear qualification criteria and reward specifications matched to customer motivation ● Includes technical tracking approaches with attribution accuracy considerations and implementation guidance ● Delivers multi-channel engagement tactics with messaging concepts, frequency recommendations, and timing strategies ● Outputs realistic implementation timelines, reward fulfillment workflows, and KPI frameworks tied to program objectives ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in referral marketing and customer acquisition.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive referral program that incentivizes existing customers to refer new clients. Design reward structures, tracking methods, and engagement strategies that maximize participation and conversion rates.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Provide specific details about your business model, target customer segments, and what differentiates your offering in the market. Include information about customer lifetime value, typical purchase behavior, and what motivates your customers.

## Output
Deliver a structured referral program plan organized as follows:

**Program Overview**
- Main objectives and strategic rationale
- Key components and program structure

**Target Audience**
- Which customer segments are eligible to participate
- Why these segments were selected

**Referral Incentives** (3 distinct tiers)
For each incentive:
- Description and positioning
- Specific reward offered
- Qualification criteria for earning the reward

**Referral Tracking Methods** (3 approaches)
- Technical implementation details
- Attribution accuracy and reliability considerations

**Customer Engagement Strategies** (3 tactics)
- Multi-channel promotion approaches
- Messaging and creative concepts
- Frequency and timing recommendations

**Implementation Timeline**
- Key milestones from launch preparation through full rollout
- Realistic timeframes accounting for dependencies

**Program Management**
- Referral processing workflows
- Reward fulfillment logistics
- Customer support protocols
- Performance monitoring systems

**Success Metrics** (3 KPIs)
- Specific, measurable indicators tied to program objectives
- Targets and benchmarks for evaluation

Ensure all incentives are achievable and aligned with customer preferences. Design tracking methods that are accurate yet simple to implement. Create engagement strategies tailored to your specific audience behaviors.
```
