# Sales Email Campaign Writer for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Sales Email Campaign Writer is a free AI prompt that produces multi-email sales sequences tailored to your target audience, offer, and brand voice. Instead of drafting a single message, this sales email prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete 3–5 email campaign with subject lines, attention-grabbing hooks, value propositions, social proof, and clear calls-to-action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, turning your audience insights and campaign goals into a structured sequence that nurtures prospects from awareness to conversion. Reach for this prompt when you need to launch a lead-generation campaign, book product demos, or close sales through email. It asks for two variables: your audience demographics, pain points, and offer details, plus your brand voice and concrete campaign goal. The AI then analyzes how your audience's needs map to your product's benefits and writes each email to build progressively on the last, guiding the prospect through a logical journey. ● Produces 3–5 sequenced emails that progressively nurture prospects toward a specific conversion goal ● Each email includes a tailored subject line, engaging opening hook, unique value points, and clear call-to-action ● Analyzes audience pain points and connects them directly to your offer's benefits in the messaging ● Ensures consistent brand voice, tone, and style across the entire campaign sequence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales copywriter creating a personalized email campaign.

## Task
Write a series of compelling sales emails that resonate with the target audience and drive conversions. Structure your writing for clarity and coherence, ensuring each email builds on the previous one to guide prospects toward the campaign goal.

## Context
**Audience & offer:**
{{audience-and-offer}}
(Describe your target audience—their demographics, needs, pain points, motivations—and the product or service you're promoting, including what makes it unique.)

**Brand voice & campaign goal:**
{{brand-voice-and-goal}}
(Specify your brand's tone and style, plus the concrete objective of this campaign: generate leads, close sales, book demos, etc.)

## Approach
For each email:
- Analyze how the audience's pain points connect to your offer's benefits
- Craft an attention-grabbing subject line
- Open with an engaging hook that addresses a specific need or emotion
- Highlight unique value propositions and use social proof where relevant
- Close with a clear, compelling call-to-action
- Ensure tone, style, and language match the brand voice and audience preferences
- Sequence emails to nurture the relationship progressively

## Output
Provide 3–5 emails, each formatted as:

**Email [number]**

**Subject:** [subject line]

**Body:**
[Email body text]

---

Use markdown formatting for readability.
```
