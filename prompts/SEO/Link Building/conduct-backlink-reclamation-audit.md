# Backlink Reclamation Audit for SEO Recovery

## 簡介

The Backlink Reclamation Audit for SEO Recovery is a free AI prompt that analyzes your website's backlink profile, identifies broken or lost links from high-authority domains, and creates a strategic action plan to restore valuable SEO equity. This backlink reclamation prompt for ChatGPT works by conducting a systematic audit of your site's link profile, pinpointing 404 errors, lost connections, and redirected backlinks that once contributed to your domain authority. It then prioritizes recovery opportunities based on page authority, relevance, and traffic potential, and outputs a detailed reclamation strategy complete with outreach templates and tracking tables. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible for any text-based AI workflow. Real use cases include recovering links after site migrations, identifying partnership opportunities where links disappeared, and systematically rebuilding authority lost to broken connections. This prompt is essential for SEO professionals, digital marketers, and website owners who need to diagnose why rankings have declined and recover the link equity that drives organic visibility. ● Produces a sortable table of 8-12 high-priority broken or lost backlinks with domain authority, anchor text, target URL, and current status ● Delivers a five-step reclamation strategy covering discovery tools, prioritization criteria, personalized outreach templates, content updates, and ongoing monitoring ● Includes progress-tracking tables with contact dates, follow-up schedules, and resolution fields for managing outreach campaigns ● Provides expected outcomes including restored backlink counts, projected authority gains, ranking impact timelines, and maintenance recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO and digital marketing strategist specializing in backlink analysis and link reclamation.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive link audit for {{website-url}}, identify broken or lost backlinks from high-authority domains, and develop a strategic action plan to restore these valuable links.

## Analysis Requirements
- Perform a thorough audit of the site's backlink profile
- Identify broken (404), lost, or redirected backlinks from high-authority domains
- Prioritize the most valuable opportunities based on page authority, relevance, and traffic potential
- Assess the impact of lost links on current search engine rankings

## Output
Deliver your findings in this structured format:

**Link Audit Findings:**

| Referring Domain | Page Authority | Anchor Text | Target URL | Status |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------|------------|--------|
| [List 8-12 high-priority broken/lost backlinks] |

**Link Reclamation Strategy:**

1. **Discovery & Verification** – [Describe tools and process for validating broken links]
2. **Prioritization Framework** – [Explain criteria for ranking reclamation opportunities]
3. **Outreach Approach** – [Detail personalized contact strategy for each domain type]
4. **Content Updates** – [Specify any page improvements needed before requesting link restoration]
5. **Monitoring & Maintenance** – [Outline ongoing link health checks]

**Progress Tracking:**

| Referring Domain | Contact Date | Follow-up Date | Resolution |
|-----------------|--------------|----------------|------------|
| [Provide trackable template for execution] |

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Restored backlink count and estimated authority gain
- Projected improvement in domain authority and search rankings
- Timeline for measurable SEO impact
- Ongoing link maintenance recommendations
```
