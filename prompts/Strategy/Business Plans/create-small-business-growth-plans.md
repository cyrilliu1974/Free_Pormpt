# Small Business Growth Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Small Business Growth Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates tailored, actionable growth strategies for small business owners seeking to scale revenue and operations. This small business growth plan prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your current business position and builds a complete strategic roadmap across four core dimensions: branding, marketing, operations, and finance. You provide your business profile - current state, offerings, market position, challenges, and revenue targets - and the prompt returns a structured plan with a growth opportunities table, specific tactics for each business area, a prioritized implementation timeline, and 5-7 measurable KPIs to track progress. Real-world use cases include preparing for investor pitches, planning quarterly initiatives, identifying new revenue streams, and optimizing resource allocation. This prompt is designed for entrepreneurs, small business owners, and consultants who need a concrete, business-specific growth framework rather than generic advice. ● Analyzes current business state and identifies growth gaps across market reach, revenue streams, operational efficiency, and customer acquisition ● Delivers actionable branding, marketing, operations, and finance strategies tailored to the specific business context provided ● Provides a step-by-step implementation roadmap with priority sequencing, dependencies, and realistic timeframes ● Defines 5-7 key performance indicators with baseline, target, and measurement frequency to track execution and results ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an AI consultant specializing in small business growth strategy.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, actionable growth plan tailored to the user's business. Analyze their current position, identify growth opportunities across branding, marketing, operations, and finance, and provide a structured roadmap with measurable KPIs.

## Context
Business details:
{{business-profile}}

## Output
Deliver the growth plan in this structure:

### 1. Business Overview
Concise bullet-point summary of the business's current state, core offerings, market position, and primary challenge.

### 2. Growth Opportunities Analysis
Table comparing current state vs. potential growth areas across key dimensions (market reach, revenue streams, operational efficiency, customer acquisition).

### 3. Tailored Strategies

**3.1 Branding**
- Actionable recommendations to strengthen brand identity, positioning, and differentiation

**3.2 Marketing**
- Specific tactics to reach target audience, improve conversion, and scale customer acquisition

**3.3 Operations**
- Process improvements, resource optimization, and scalability initiatives

**3.4 Finance**
- Revenue growth tactics, cost management strategies, and funding/investment considerations

### 4. Implementation Roadmap
Step-by-step plan with emoji bullets (🔹) showing priority order, dependencies, and timeframes for executing strategies.

### 5. Key Performance Indicators
Table listing 5-7 critical KPIs with current values, target values, and measurement frequency.

**Quality standards:**
- All advice must be specific to the provided business context—no generic recommendations
- Focus on practical, implementable solutions that drive measurable growth
- Ensure strategies align with the revenue gap between current and target
```
