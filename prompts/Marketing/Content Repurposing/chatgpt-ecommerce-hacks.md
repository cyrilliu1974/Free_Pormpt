# Ecommerce Marketing Prompt Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Ecommerce Marketing Prompt Generator for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive set of marketing prompts customized to your online store's audience, challenges, and goals. This ecommerce marketing prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered roadmap of actionable prompts spanning the full digital marketing spectrum: content strategy, social media campaigns, email sequences, SEO audits, customer retention tactics, engagement mechanisms, and conversion rate optimization. Each generated prompt includes the exact wording you can paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, plus a strategic explanation of how it fits into your overall marketing plan. The prompts build upon one another to form a cohesive digital marketing playbook rather than disconnected tactics. Use it when you need a structured marketing action plan for a new product line, seasonal campaign, or when addressing specific growth challenges like cart abandonment or low repeat purchase rates. ● Produces labeled, copy-paste-ready prompts across content, social, email, SEO, retention, and conversion optimization disciplines ● Sequences prompts strategically so foundational insights inform advanced tactics ● Customizes every prompt to your business description, target audience, and stated challenges ● Includes strategic context explaining why each prompt matters and how it connects to overall goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in e-commerce, with deep expertise across content, social media, email marketing, SEO, customer engagement, and conversion optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive, strategically sequenced list of ChatGPT prompts tailored to the e-commerce business described below. Each prompt should be specific, actionable, and designed to produce valuable marketing insights or executable strategies. Structure the prompts so they build upon one another, creating a cohesive digital marketing roadmap.

## Context
**Business & Products:** {{business-description}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Current Challenges & Goals:** {{challenges-and-goals}}

Cover the full spectrum of e-commerce digital marketing: content strategy, social media campaigns, email sequences, SEO tactics, customer retention, engagement mechanisms, and conversion rate optimization. Ensure each prompt addresses a distinct aspect while connecting to the overall marketing strategy.

## Output
Present as a numbered list. For each prompt:
- Label it clearly (e.g., "1. SEO Content Audit Prompt")
- Write the actual ChatGPT prompt the business can use
- Add a one-sentence explanation of its strategic value

Ensure diversity across marketing disciplines and progression from foundational strategy to advanced tactics.
```
