# Email Campaign Optimization Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Email Campaign Optimization Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes business context and produces actionable strategies to increase email open rates, click-through rates, and conversions for marketers and growth teams. This email campaign optimization prompt for ChatGPT uses dependency grammar principles to guide you through building attention-grabbing subject lines, persuasive body copy aligned with your unique selling proposition, and effective calls-to-action that drive measurable results. It outputs a structured markdown table covering subject lines, preview text, email openings, body copy, CTAs, send timing, and A/B test recommendations tailored to your specific product, audience demographics, current metrics, and brand voice. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic approach to improve underperforming email campaigns or want to launch new sequences grounded in strategic best practices rather than guesswork. ● Develops targeted subject lines and preview text designed to maximize open rates for specific audience segments ● Provides persuasive email body copy frameworks that highlight your unique value proposition using dependency grammar for logical flow ● Recommends conversion-focused calls-to-action with tactical guidance on placement, wording, and design ● Includes send timing optimization and concrete A/B testing recommendations based on your current metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist optimizing campaigns to increase open rates, click-through rates, and conversions.

## Task
Analyze the provided business context and develop a comprehensive email marketing strategy. Cover:
- Attention-grabbing subject lines tailored to the audience
- Persuasive email body copy that highlights the unique selling proposition
- Effective calls-to-action that drive conversions
- Tactical recommendations based on current performance metrics

Use dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and flow—structure sentences so each element builds logically on the previous, guiding the reader toward action.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include: product/service details, target audience demographics and preferences, current email metrics (open rate, CTR, conversion rate), brand voice characteristics, and unique selling proposition.

## Output
Provide your strategy in a markdown table with three columns:

| Element | Strategy | Examples |
|---------|----------|----------|

Cover at minimum: Subject Lines, Preview Text, Email Opening, Body Copy, Call-to-Action, Send Timing, and A/B Test Recommendations.
```
