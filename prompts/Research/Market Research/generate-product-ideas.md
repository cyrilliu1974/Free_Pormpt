# Product Idea Generator for Market Research

## 簡介

The Product Idea Generator for Market Research is a free AI prompt that produces three fully developed product concepts grounded in research-backed market needs for entrepreneurs, product managers, and innovators exploring new opportunities. This product idea prompt for ChatGPT works by first identifying 3-5 key pain points and unmet needs in your specified niche, citing credible sources, then developing three distinct product concepts that directly address those gaps. Each concept includes a product name, description, key features, unique value proposition, and the specific market need it solves. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across text-generation models. Use it when validating a new market, brainstorming extensions to an existing product line, or preparing investor pitches that demonstrate market understanding. ● Identifies 3-5 documented market needs and pain points with credible source citations before ideation begins ● Delivers three complete product concepts, each with name, description, 3-4 key features, and unique value proposition ● Maps each product idea to a specific market need, ensuring relevance and strategic alignment ● Outputs a structured format with restated niche and audience, full product profiles, and a compiled source list ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative ideation expert specializing in product development, with deep knowledge of market trends, consumer psychology, and innovation strategies.

## Task
Generate three innovative product ideas for {{niche}} targeting {{target-audience}}. Ground your ideas in research-backed market needs and pain points, considering emerging trends, disruptive technologies, and unique value propositions that differentiate from existing offerings.

## Process
1. Identify and list 3-5 key market needs and pain points for the specified niche and audience, citing credible sources
2. Develop three product concepts that directly address the identified needs
3. For each product idea, provide:
   - Product name
   - Description (2-3 sentences)
   - Key features (3-4 bullet points)
   - Unique value proposition
   - Specific market need addressed
4. List all sources cited
```
