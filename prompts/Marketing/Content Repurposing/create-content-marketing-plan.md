# Content Marketing Plan Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Content Marketing Plan Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a complete content marketing strategy for independent business owners looking to increase online visibility and customer engagement. This content marketing plan prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table covering 6-8 distinct content initiatives, each mapped to a target audience segment, core message, and 2-3 distribution channels. The prompt considers brand voice consistency, industry trends, audience platform behaviors, resource constraints, and content repurposing opportunities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need a cohesive content roadmap that balances strategic vision with the practical realities of running a one-person business. ● Produces a structured table with content type, target audience, key message, and distribution channels for each initiative ● Accounts for sustainable publishing cadence and resource constraints specific to solo operators ● Incorporates brand voice consistency, competitive landscape, and platform-specific behaviors ● Identifies opportunities for content repurposing to maximize output from limited creation time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketing strategist specializing in growth strategies for solopreneur businesses.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content marketing plan that increases online visibility and drives customer engagement. Develop a strategic roadmap covering content types, audience targeting, core messaging, and distribution channels.

## Context
Business & Audience:
{{business-and-audience}}
(Include: your solopreneur business description, industry, target audience demographics and pain points, current online presence, and available content creation resources)

Consider:
- Brand voice consistency across all content
- Current industry trends and competitive landscape
- Audience content consumption preferences and platform behaviors
- Resource constraints and sustainable publishing cadence
- Opportunities for repurposing and content leverage

## Output
Present your content marketing plan as a markdown table with these columns:

| Content Type | Target Audience | Key Message | Distribution Channels |

Include 6-8 distinct content initiatives that form a cohesive strategy. For each row, specify the content format, the audience segment it serves, the core value proposition or theme, and the 2-3 primary platforms for distribution.
```
