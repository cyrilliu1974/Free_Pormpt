# Product Launch Strategy Prompt for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Product Launch Strategy Prompt for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that builds a complete market-entry roadmap for founders and indie makers launching with limited budgets. This product launch strategy prompt for ChatGPT delivers five concrete tactics for each phase: pre-launch audience-building, launch day execution, and post-launch growth hacking. You enter your product description, target audience, and launch objectives, and the prompt returns actionable checklists optimized for solopreneurs who need high-impact, cost-effective tactics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured plan that replaces generic launch advice with steps tailored to your specific offering and market. Reach for this prompt when you need a launch plan without hiring a go-to-market consultant or when you're preparing to introduce a product on platforms like ProductHunt, HackerNews, or Twitter and want a clear execution guide. ● Outputs five pre-launch tactics focused on building buzz and early audience engagement before your launch date. ● Provides five launch day execution steps with specific platform and community recommendations. ● Includes five post-launch growth hacks to maintain momentum and convert early interest into long-term traction. ● Lists essential solopreneur resources that fit limited budgets and time constraints. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a go-to-market strategy expert specializing in solopreneur and startup launches with limited resources.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive launch strategy covering pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch phases. Focus on cost-effective, high-impact tactics that build audience engagement and drive conversions.

## Context
Product: {{product-description}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Launch objectives: {{launch-objectives}}

## Requirements
- Prioritize tactics achievable for a solopreneur with limited budget and time
- Provide specific, actionable steps rather than generic advice
- Tailor all recommendations to the product and audience described above
- Focus on activities that generate buzz and build engaged communities

## Output
Deliver the strategy in this structure:

**Pre-launch checklist:**
1. [Tactic 1]
2. [Tactic 2]
3. [Tactic 3]
4. [Tactic 4]
5. [Tactic 5]

**Launch day execution:**

3. [Step 3]
4. [Step 4]
5. [Step 5]

**Post-launch growth hacking:**
1. [Growth hack 1]
2. [Growth hack 2]
3. [Growth hack 3]
4. [Growth hack 4]
5. [Growth hack 5]

**Essential solopreneur resources:**
- [Resource 1]
- [Resource 2]
- [Resource 3]
- [Resource 4]
- [Resource 5]
```
