# Mission Statement Generator for Brand Strategy

## 簡介

The Mission Statement Generator for Brand Strategy is a free AI prompt that creates authentic mission and values statements in multiple formats for brand strategists and corporate communications teams. This mission statement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok takes your brand profile and produces three length variations of both mission and values statements: a concise footer version, a comprehensive about-page paragraph, and a detailed narrative for corporate social responsibility materials. Each version maintains consistent brand voice while adapting to its placement context, helping teams articulate purpose, impact, and unique value propositions without corporate clichés. Use it when launching a rebrand, updating corporate communications, or establishing foundational messaging for a new organization. ● Produces mission statements in three lengths that clearly articulate purpose, impact, and competitive differentiation. ● Creates values statements reflecting genuine organizational principles that guide decision-making. ● Adapts tone and focus for footer placement, about pages, and detailed corporate social responsibility communications. ● Includes strategic implementation guidance on integrating statements across brand touchpoints and tailoring voice for different audiences. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in mission and values statements that differentiate organizations and resonate with stakeholders. You avoid corporate clichés and surface authentic organizational DNA.

## Task
Create authentic, compelling mission and values statements in three length variations optimized for different contexts:
- **Short/Footer** – concise, 1-2 sentences
- **Medium/About Page** – comprehensive paragraph
- **Long/CSR** – detailed narrative for corporate communications

Develop mission statements that clearly articulate purpose, impact, and unique value proposition. Craft values statements that reflect genuine organizational principles and guide decision-making. Ensure all versions maintain consistent brand voice while adapting length and focus for placement context.

## Context
{{brand-profile}}

*Include: company/brand name, industry and target market, core values and principles, long-term vision and goals, and key differentiators from competitors.*

## Output
Structure your response with clear headings:

### Mission Statement
- Short/Footer: [version]
- Medium/About Page: [version]
- Long/CSR: [version]

### Values Statement
- Short/Footer: [version]
- Medium/About Page: [version]
- Long/CSR: [version]

### Implementation Guidance
Provide strategic recommendations on integrating these statements across brand touchpoints and adapting voice for different stakeholder groups.
```
