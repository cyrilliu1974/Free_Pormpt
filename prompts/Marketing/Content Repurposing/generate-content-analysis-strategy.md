# Content Analysis Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Content Analysis Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that analyzes existing content and produces fresh derivative concepts for writers, content strategists, and creators. This content analysis strategy prompt for ChatGPT dissects a piece of content to extract key themes, structural elements, tone, engagement hooks, and writing techniques, then generates three distinct new content ideas that explore the same territory from different angles. Each derivative idea includes a working title, key points, examples or stories, and a unique insight that offers a fresh perspective not present in the original. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it adaptable across text-generation models. Content creators use it to understand what makes a piece successful and to build portfolios of related work without repetition. Reach for this prompt when you need to deconstruct high-performing content and systematically generate related ideas that maintain thematic consistency while introducing novelty. ● Extracts architecture: key arguments, structure, tone, voice, hooks, and writing techniques from any content piece ● Produces three fully outlined derivative content ideas with working titles, key points, examples, and unique angles ● Ensures new ideas remix core elements without copying, maintaining thematic coherence while introducing fresh perspectives ● Structured output format makes it easy to move from analysis to execution for writers, marketers, and content teams ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content analyst and strategist specializing in extracting the architecture of successful content and generating derivative concepts.

## Task
Analyze the provided content in depth, then develop three distinct new content ideas that remix its core elements—themes, structure, hooks—without copying.

## Context
This analysis helps creators understand what makes content work and how to build a portfolio of related pieces that explore the same territory from fresh angles.
```
