# Education Research Topic Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Education Research Topic Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that compiles tailored research topics in education for academics, graduate students, and institutional researchers. This education research topic prompt for ChatGPT accepts a single research-focus variable and produces a markdown table of 8-12 distinct topics, each with a relevance statement explaining why it matters to the field and 3-4 key investigative questions. The prompt instructs the model - ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok - to consider current trends, challenges, and innovations, ensuring topics address real problems and opportunities in the educational landscape. Use cases include thesis planning, grant proposal development, literature review scoping, and curriculum innovation ideation. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly map a research domain, validate a focus area, or generate alternatives before committing to a single direction. ● Produces 8-12 research topics in a three-column markdown table with Topic, Relevance, and Key Questions. ● Tailors output to your research-focus variable, whether that is early childhood literacy, EdTech adoption, equity in STEM, or any other domain. ● Includes 2-3 sentence relevance statements explaining impact and audience fit for each topic. ● Generates 3-4 focused investigation questions per topic to jumpstart hypothesis formation and study design. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational researcher specializing in identifying impactful research directions.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive list of research topics in education tailored to the specified context. For each topic, assess its relevance to the field and formulate key questions that could guide investigation.

## Context
{{research-focus}}

Consider current trends, challenges, and innovations in education. Generate a diverse range of topics that address real problems and opportunities within this context.

## Output
Present your research topics in a markdown table with three columns:

| Topic | Relevance | Key Questions |
|-------|-----------|---------------|

Include 8-12 distinct topics. For each:
- **Topic**: A clear, specific research area
- **Relevance**: Why this matters to the field and the target audience (2-3 sentences)
- **Key Questions**: 3-4 focused questions that could guide investigation

Ensure the table is well-formatted and easy to scan.
```
