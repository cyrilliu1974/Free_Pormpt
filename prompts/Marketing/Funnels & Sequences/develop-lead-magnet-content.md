# Lead Magnet Content Builder for Sales Funnels

## 簡介

The Lead Magnet Content Builder for Sales Funnels is a free AI prompt that creates high-value lead magnets designed to attract and convert potential customers by addressing their core challenges. This lead magnet prompt for ChatGPT takes three inputs - your target audience and industry, business context and unique selling proposition, and desired format (eBook, whitepaper, checklist, video series) - and generates a complete, structured lead magnet ready for design and distribution. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering compelling titles, pain-point-focused introductions, scannable main content sections with headlines and subheadings, visual placement recommendations, and strategic calls-to-action that guide prospects to the next funnel step. Content marketers and growth teams use it to produce lead magnets that establish authority, capture qualified leads, and move prospects through the customer journey without starting from a blank page. ● Identifies 3-5 critical pain points faced by your target audience and maps actionable solutions to each ● Structures content with compelling headlines, clear subheadings, and scannable sections that hold reader attention ● Recommends specific placements for visuals, charts, and graphics that enhance comprehension and retention ● Includes strategic calls-to-action aligned with your funnel stage and business objectives ● Provides format-specific design and distribution guidance for eBooks, whitepapers, checklists, infographics, and video series ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in lead magnet development.

## Task
Create a high-value lead magnet designed to attract and convert potential customers by addressing their core challenges and positioning the business as a trusted authority.

## Context
**Target audience & industry:** {{audience-and-industry}}

**Business offerings & unique selling proposition:** {{business-context}}

**Lead magnet format:** {{format}}

## Requirements
1. Identify 3-5 critical pain points your target audience faces
2. Provide actionable, practical solutions for each challenge
3. Structure content with compelling headlines and clear subheadings
4. Break information into scannable sections (no walls of text)
5. Suggest placements for visuals, charts, or graphics that enhance comprehension
6. Include a strategic call-to-action that drives the next step in your funnel
7. Ensure the format is shareable and accessible across devices

## Output
Deliver a complete lead magnet outline or draft that includes:

- **Title** (compelling, benefit-driven)
- **Introduction** (hook that speaks to pain points)
- **Main Content Sections** (structured with headings, subheadings, and bullet points)
- **Visual Recommendations** (where graphics, charts, or images should appear)
- **Conclusion & Call-to-Action** (clear next step)
- **Design & Distribution Notes** (format-specific guidance)

Format your response with clear markdown structure for maximum readability.
```
