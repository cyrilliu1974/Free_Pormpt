# Keyword Gap Analysis Against Competitors

## 簡介

The Keyword Gap Analysis Against Competitors is a free AI prompt that identifies untapped keyword opportunities by systematically comparing your search rankings against competitor positions in your industry. This keyword gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your current keyword rankings, comparing them against competitor performance for the same and related search terms, and pinpointing specific gaps where rivals rank higher or where new opportunities exist. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering findings in a structured markdown table with three columns: your current ranking (or "Not ranking"), the best competitor position, and the keyword itself. Below the table, the prompt generates a strategic analysis that highlights the top 3-5 keyword opportunities with highest potential impact, specific recommendations for closing ranking gaps, and patterns in competitor keyword strategies. Real-world use cases include discovering low-competition keywords your competitors already rank for, finding content gaps in your SEO strategy, and prioritizing which keywords to target next based on difficulty and opportunity. This prompt is for SEO analysts, content strategists, and digital marketers who need to understand where competitors have a search visibility advantage and want data-driven recommendations for improving their own rankings. ● Compares your current keyword rankings against multiple competitors to reveal exactly where they outperform you ● Identifies new keyword opportunities you are not currently targeting but competitors are ranking for ● Prioritizes keywords by potential impact and ranking difficulty so you focus effort where it matters most ● Delivers findings in a clean markdown table format with strategic recommendations for closing gaps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst conducting a keyword gap analysis.

## Task
Identify untapped keyword opportunities where competitors outrank the business or where there are improvement opportunities. Deliver actionable insights that provide a competitive edge.

## Context
Competitors: {{competitors}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Current keywords: {{current-keywords}}

## Method
1. Analyze the current keyword rankings for the business
2. Compare against competitor rankings for the same and related keywords
3. Identify gaps where competitors rank higher or where new keyword opportunities exist
4. Prioritize keywords by potential impact and ranking difficulty

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Current Ranking** – the business's position for each keyword (or "Not ranking")
- **Competitor Ranking** – best competitor position for that keyword
- **Keyword** – the search term

Below the table, provide a brief analysis highlighting:
- Top 3-5 keyword opportunities with highest potential impact
- Specific recommendations for closing ranking gaps
- Any patterns in competitor keyword strategies worth noting
```
