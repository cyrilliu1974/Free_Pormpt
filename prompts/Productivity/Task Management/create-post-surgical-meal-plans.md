# Post-Surgical Meal Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Post-Surgical Meal Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based 7-day nutrition schedules for patients recovering from surgery. This post-surgical meal plan prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok builds daily meal tables that adapt to your surgery type (gastrointestinal, orthopedic, cardiac, general), days post-op, dietary restrictions, and current symptoms like nausea or constipation. Each day includes six meal times with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, key nutrient breakdowns (protein, vitamin C, zinc, omega-3s), healing benefits tied to tissue repair and inflammation control, and preparation methods that maximize absorption while minimizing digestive stress. The output includes portion guidance, hydration recommendations, and a recovery nutrition summary identifying foods to emphasize, foods to avoid, and modifications for common post-operative symptoms. Reach for this prompt when planning recovery nutrition for yourself, a family member, or a patient who needs a structured eating schedule that respects surgical healing phases and medication interactions. ● Progresses meal complexity day by day, starting with gentle, easily digestible foods and advancing to nutrient-dense options as healing improves. ● Addresses multiple surgical categories and accommodates allergies, dietary preferences, and symptoms that affect appetite or digestion. ● Provides specific nutritional rationales for each meal, linking ingredients to tissue repair, wound healing, immune support, and energy restoration. ● Includes practical preparation notes and hydration reminders to support absorption and reduce digestive stress. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a clinical nutritionist specializing in post-surgical recovery meal planning, with expertise in evidence-based protocols that support tissue repair, inflammation management, and digestive accommodation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 7-day post-surgical meal plan that progresses from gentle, easily digestible foods to more complex nutrient-dense options as healing advances.

## Context
Post-surgical recovery involves tissue repair, immune modulation, inflammation management, and energy restoration while addressing digestive sensitivities, medication interactions, and appetite fluctuations. Nutritional support must adapt to different surgical categories (gastrointestinal, orthopedic, cardiac, general) and common post-operative symptoms.

**Recovery profile to address:**
{{recovery-profile}}

*Include: type of surgery, days post-surgery, current dietary restrictions or allergies, symptoms affecting eating (nausea, constipation, reduced appetite), and food preferences (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, or both).*

## Output
Provide a detailed markdown table with these columns:
- **Day** (1–7)
- **Meal Time** (breakfast, mid-morning snack, lunch, afternoon snack, dinner, evening option)
- **Vegetarian Option**
- **Non-Vegetarian Option**
- **Key Nutrients** (protein, vitamin C, zinc, omega-3s, complex carbs, etc.)
- **Healing Benefits** (specific rationale for tissue repair, wound healing, inflammation control, energy)
- **Preparation Notes** (cooking methods that maximize absorption and minimize digestive stress)

Include portion guidance and hydration recommendations throughout.

Follow with a **Recovery Nutrition Summary** table:
- **Foods to Emphasize**: List nutrient-dense options that accelerate healing
- **Foods to Avoid**: List items that may interfere with recovery or exacerbate symptoms
- **Modification Suggestions**: Adjustments for nausea, constipation, or reduced appetite
```
