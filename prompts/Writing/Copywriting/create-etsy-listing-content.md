# Etsy Listing Generator

## 簡介

The Etsy Listing Generator is a free AI prompt that produces eight essential content blocks to help Etsy sellers create polished, search-optimized product listings and customer communication templates. This Etsy listing prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your product details and generating a complete content pack: a 2-3 paragraph product description highlighting features and benefits, a sensory product origin story, a list of 15-20 SEO keywords and phrases tailored for Etsy search, a 4-6 question FAQ covering shipping and materials, three customer inquiry response templates, two empathetic negative review reply templates, two thank-you message versions, and three platform-ready social media posts for Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering all eight content blocks in a single text output. Etsy shop owners use it to save hours writing listing copy, responding to customer questions, and planning social promotion while maintaining a consistent, professional brand voice. Reach for this prompt when you're launching a new product, refreshing underperforming listings, or need ready-to-use templates for day-to-day customer interactions. ● Produces SEO-optimized product descriptions with vivid, accessible language that highlights unique features and buyer benefits ● Delivers 15-20 targeted Etsy search keywords and phrases to improve product discoverability ● Creates ready-to-use customer service templates for inquiries, custom orders, shipping questions, and review responses ● Generates platform-specific social media posts with hooks and hashtags for Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write SEO-friendly Etsy listing content: product descriptions, origin stories, keyword lists, FAQs, customer response templates, review replies, thank-you messages, and social media posts.

## Context

Product: {{product-details}}

## Task

Produce all eight content blocks below for the product above. Follow these rules throughout:

- Highlight unique features and benefits in plain, accessible language
- Use vivid, sensory detail in the product story
- Include relevant search terms to improve Etsy visibility
- Address common buyer questions clearly and concisely
- Keep all customer-facing copy positive and professional
- Avoid jargon or overly technical language

## Output

**Product Description**
A compelling 2-3 paragraph description highlighting unique features and benefits

**Product Story**
A short narrative about the product's inspiration, creation process, or artisan background using vivid imagery

**SEO Keywords**
A comma-separated list of 15-20 search-optimized keywords and phrases for Etsy discovery

**FAQ Section**
4-6 common questions with clear, helpful answers (shipping, customization, materials, care instructions)

**Customer Response Templates**
3 templates: general inquiry response, custom order request response, shipping question response

**Negative Review Responses**
2 empathetic, solution-focused templates for addressing disappointed customers while maintaining professionalism

**Thank You Messages**
2 versions: post-purchase thank you and repeat customer appreciation message

**Social Media Posts**
3 platform-ready posts with hooks and relevant hashtags: one for Instagram, one for Facebook, one for Pinterest
```
