# Local SEO Search Trends Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Local SEO Search Trends Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that analyzes local search behavior and delivers actionable SEO insights for businesses targeting specific geographic markets. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT asks the AI to act as an expert local SEO analyst and produce a structured report covering the top five relevant keywords with search volumes, seasonal search variations across key months, peak search times by day, actionable insights, and tailored content strategy recommendations. By supplying your business type and target location as variables, you receive data-driven guidance to optimize your content calendar, align marketing efforts with demand cycles, and improve local visibility. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for teams working across different text-generation platforms. Use this prompt when planning local campaigns, launching a new location, or refreshing your content strategy to match real search behavior in your area. ● Identifies the five most relevant keywords for your business type and location, complete with estimated search volumes. ● Maps seasonal search trends month-by-month so you can time content releases and promotions to match demand. ● Pinpoints peak search days and times to optimize ad spend, social posting, and outreach campaigns. ● Provides specific, actionable content strategy recommendations tailored to your market and search patterns. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert local SEO analyst with deep knowledge of search trends, seasonal variations, and content strategies for businesses.

## Task
Analyze local search trends for the specified business and location. Identify seasonal variations, peak search times, and deliver a comprehensive report with actionable insights and content strategy recommendations.

## Context
Business type: {{business-type}}
Location: {{location}}

Focus on accurate, relevant search data that helps optimize content and marketing strategies. Identify the top 5 most relevant keywords with search volumes, highlight seasonal patterns, and provide specific recommendations to improve local SEO and attract customers.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive report in this format:

**Business Type:** [business type]
**Location:** [location]

**Top 5 Relevant Keywords:**
1. [Keyword 1] - [Search Volume]
2. [Keyword 2] - [Search Volume]
3. [Keyword 3] - [Search Volume]
4. [Keyword 4] - [Search Volume]
5. [Keyword 5] - [Search Volume]

**Seasonal Search Trends:**
◼️ [Month 1]: [Trend Insights]
◼️ [Month 2]: [Trend Insights]
◼️ [Month 3]: [Trend Insights]
◼️ [Month 4]: [Trend Insights]

**Peak Search Times:**
🕐 [Day 1]: [Time Range]
🕐 [Day 2]: [Time Range]
🕐 [Day 3]: [Time Range]

**Actionable Insights:**
• [Insight 1]
• [Insight 2]

**Content Strategy Recommendations:**
1. [Recommendation 1]
2. [Recommendation 2]
3. [Recommendation 3]

5. [Recommendation 5]

Use clear headings, bullet points, and emojis for readability and visual appeal.
```
