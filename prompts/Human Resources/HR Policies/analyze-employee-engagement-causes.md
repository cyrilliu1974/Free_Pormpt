# Employee Engagement Root Cause Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Engagement Root Cause Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies systemic drivers of workplace disengagement for HR professionals, organizational psychologists, and managers. It generates a structured breakdown of seven evidence-based root causes, each supported by recent research from organizational behavior and workplace psychology literature published in 2023-2024. This employee engagement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok goes beyond surface symptoms to reveal the psychological mechanisms, research evidence, workplace manifestations, and actionability of each cause, helping teams design interventions that address the true sources of low motivation. Use it when you need to diagnose engagement issues with scientific rigor, present findings to leadership, or build data-informed retention and culture strategies. ● Analyzes organizational context to surface 7 root causes backed by 2023-2024 HR research and meta-analyses. ● Explains the psychological mechanisms behind each cause, not just the symptoms. ● Cites evidence from longitudinal studies and patterns observed across industries and company sizes. ● Provides workplace manifestation examples and measurable action steps for each root cause. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist and HR research analyst specializing in employee engagement diagnostics, workplace motivation patterns, and systemic organizational issues.

## Task
Identify and analyze 7 root causes of low employee engagement based on current HR research (2023-2024). Draw from organizational behavior studies, workplace psychology, and human resources management to provide evidence-based insights that reveal underlying systemic issues rather than surface symptoms.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

Focus on causes that are actionable and measurable. Examine recent meta-analyses and longitudinal studies, identifying patterns consistent across industries, company sizes, and demographics.

## Output
For each root cause, provide:

**[Root Cause Title]**
- **Psychological mechanism**: Explain the underlying drivers
- **Research evidence**: Cite supporting studies from 2023-2024
- **Workplace manifestation**: Describe how it appears in typical scenarios
- **Actionability**: Why this factor significantly impacts engagement and can be measured

Use clear headings and bullet points for maximum clarity and actionability.
```
