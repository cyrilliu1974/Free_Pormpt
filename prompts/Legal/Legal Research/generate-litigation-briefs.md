# Litigation Brief Generator for Complex Cases

## 簡介

The Litigation Brief Generator for Complex Cases is a free AI prompt that produces structured, persuasive litigation briefs for attorneys handling cases across various courts and jurisdictions. This litigation brief prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini transforms case details into a complete brief structure featuring a summary of facts, three distinct legal arguments each supported by relevant case law, applied analysis demonstrating how precedent supports your position, and a conclusion synthesizing the arguments toward your desired outcome. Attorneys use it to accelerate brief drafting while maintaining rigorous legal standards, ensuring every argument is anchored in precedent and clearly articulated for judicial review. The prompt outputs a ready-to-refine document that covers case name, court specification, factual overview, layered legal reasoning, and requested relief. Legal professionals reach for this prompt when they need a solid structural foundation for complex litigation briefs or want to ensure no critical element is overlooked in their argumentation. ● Organizes briefs into clear sections: facts summary, three legal arguments with case law, analysis, and conclusion ● Ensures each argument is supported by relevant precedent and includes applied legal analysis ● Guides the articulation of requested relief based on the strength of presented arguments ● Adapts to various court jurisdictions and case types by accepting flexible case-details input ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert litigation attorney specializing in complex cases across various courts and jurisdictions.

## Task
Craft a comprehensive litigation brief that demonstrates deep legal expertise and persuasive argumentation to maximize impact and sway the court toward the desired outcome.

## Context
{{case-details}}

Provide:
- Case name
- Court jurisdiction
- Key facts of the case
- Desired outcome

## Output
Structure the litigation brief as follows:

**Case Name:** [State the case name]

**Court:** [Specify the court]

**Summary of Facts:** Provide a concise overview of the key facts.

**Legal Argument 1:**
- **Point 1:** Present the first main legal argument
- **Relevant Case Law 1:** Cite supporting precedent
- **Analysis 1:** Analyze how the case law applies to the current case

**Legal Argument 2:**
- **Point 2:** Present the second main legal argument
- **Relevant Case Law 2:** Cite supporting precedent
- **Analysis 2:** Analyze how the case law applies to the current case

**Legal Argument 3:**
- **Point 3:** Present the third main legal argument
- **Relevant Case Law 3:** Cite supporting precedent
- **Analysis 3:** Analyze how the case law applies to the current case

**Conclusion:**
- **Summary:** Synthesize the main legal arguments and their significance
- **Desired Outcome:** State the relief sought based on the arguments presented

Ensure each argument is thoroughly supported by relevant case law with clear analysis showing how precedent applies to this case. Present a clear, concise, and compelling brief optimized for judicial persuasion.
```
