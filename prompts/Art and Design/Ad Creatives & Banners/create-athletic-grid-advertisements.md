# Athletic Apparel Grid Advertisement Generator

## 簡介

The Athletic Apparel Grid Advertisement Generator is a free AI prompt that produces professional six-panel product advertisements for fitness brands and performance apparel companies. This athletic apparel prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates a 4:5 portrait grid with six coordinated panels: a studio hero shot highlighting fit and silhouette, an explosive action sequence capturing athletic movement, a macro fabric detail showcasing material innovation, a real-world training environment shot, a construction close-up of seams and stitching, and a post-workout recovery moment. Each panel follows professional sports photography standards with balanced natural lighting, clean compositions, and premium brand aesthetics. Customize the product name and athletic discipline to generate complete visual content packages for social media, e-commerce galleries, and digital advertising campaigns. This prompt is built for fitness marketers, apparel brand designers, and e-commerce teams who need cohesive multi-angle product photography without organizing complex photo shoots. ● Produces hero apparel shots with studio lighting that emphasize fit, logo placement, and athletic confidence. ● Captures explosive motion sequences frozen mid-action with crisp detail and natural daylight. ● Showcases fabric technology through macro close-ups of texture, weave, and material performance. ● Includes lifestyle training environments, construction detail shots, and post-workout recovery moments for authentic brand storytelling. ## Prompt

```
Create a dynamic 2×3 grid advertisement showcasing {{product-name}} athletic apparel through movement, material innovation, and everyday lifestyle. 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, six equal panels.

Panel 1 — Hero Apparel Look: Studio-style shot of {{product-name}} worn by an athlete. Soft directional studio lighting, neutral gradient or concrete background. Emphasize fit, silhouette, and logo placement. Mood: strength, confidence, performance.

Panel 2 — Dynamic Motion: Athlete in explosive action performing {{athletic-discipline}}. Natural daylight, frozen motion with crisp detail. Mood: energy, power, momentum.

Panel 3 — Fabric Technology: Macro close-up of fabric texture stretched or flexed during movement. Show breathability, weave detail, material performance. Shallow depth of field. Mood: innovation, engineering precision.

Panel 4 — Everyday Training: Athlete training in typical {{athletic-discipline}} environment. Balanced natural light, urban or outdoor fitness space. Mood: consistency, lifestyle authenticity.

Panel 5 — Comfort & Fit Detail: Close-up of construction details—waistband, seams, stitching, closure system. Focus on quality craftsmanship and comfort engineering. Mood: precision, durability.

Panel 6 — Post-Workout Calm: Athlete in recovery or cool-down moment. Warm natural light. Mood: recovery, balance, well-being.

Brand personality: driven, modern, performance-focused. Minimal logo placement. Professional sports and lifestyle photography aesthetic, high realism, clean compositions, premium fitness branding. Natural daylight mixed with soft studio fill, balanced exposure with crisp highlights and defined shadows.

Avoid: baggy or ill-fitting clothing, low-quality fabric appearance, motion blur on subject, distracting backgrounds, oversaturated colors, cheap logo placement, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
