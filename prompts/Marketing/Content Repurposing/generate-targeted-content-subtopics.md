# Targeted Content Subtopic Generator for Strategists

## 簡介

The Targeted Content Subtopic Generator for Strategists is a free AI prompt that produces 20 audience-specific subtopics to help content strategists explore a topic from multiple practical angles. This content subtopic prompt for ChatGPT works by taking any topic and target audience, then returning a numbered list of 20 specific, outcome-oriented subtopics that address real pain points and areas where your audience needs expert guidance. Unlike generic brainstorming tools, it avoids shallow content formats and overly narrow niches, instead surfacing concepts and problems your audience actually faces. Use it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to map content territory for editorial calendars, course outlines, research phases, or content repurposing projects. This prompt is for content strategists, editorial planners, and marketing teams who need to identify meaningful angles on a topic before committing to production. ● Produces 20 subtopics framed as concepts and problems, not content formats or headlines ● Tailored to your specific target audience and their real-world pain points ● Avoids generic breadth and overly narrow niches for balanced coverage ● Returns a clean numbered list ready to inform editorial calendars, course modules, or repurposing strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist generating subtopics that help a target audience explore a topic from multiple practical angles.

## Task
Generate 20 subtopics related to {{topic}} tailored to {{target-audience}}.

Each subtopic should:
- Be specific and outcome-oriented, addressing real pain points or areas of interest
- Cover angles where the audience would likely need expert guidance
- Avoid generic breadth and overly narrow niches
- Be framed as concepts or problems, not content formats (avoid "How to...", "Tips for...", "Common Mistakes in...", "Case Study of...", etc.)

## Output
Return the 20 subtopics as a numbered list with no additional explanation.
```
