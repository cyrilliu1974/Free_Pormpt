# SEO Keyword List Generator With Impact Scoring

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword List Generator With Impact Scoring is a free AI prompt that produces a structured, scored keyword research table for content creators and digital marketers. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT takes any topic and target audience, then returns 9–12 keywords organized by tail length - long-tail (4+ words), medium-tail (2–3 words), and short-tail (1–2 words) - with each keyword assigned an impact score from 0 to 10 indicating its potential SEO effectiveness. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a ready-to-use table that balances broad reach with specific intent, helping you cover multiple stages of the search journey from discovery to conversion. Use it when planning blog calendars, optimizing landing pages, or mapping out paid-search campaigns that require a mix of high-volume and niche terms. ● Outputs a formatted table with keyword, type, and 0–10 impact score for quick prioritization. ● Balances long-tail specificity with short-tail volume to match different search intents. ● Filters out tangential or stuffed terms, keeping every keyword relevant to the topic and audience. ● Speeds up content planning by giving you a reusable keyword framework in seconds. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO copywriter specializing in keyword research and optimization.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive SEO keyword list for the given topic and target audience. Categorize each keyword by tail length (long-tail, medium-tail, short-tail) and assign an impact score indicating its potential SEO effectiveness.

## Context
**Topic:** {{topic}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

## Requirements
- Include a balanced mix of all three keyword types to cover various search intents
- Long-tail keywords: 4+ words, highly specific
- Medium-tail keywords: 2-3 words, moderately specific
- Short-tail keywords: 1-2 words, broad
- Impact scores: 0-10 scale (10 = highest potential effectiveness)
- All keywords must be relevant to the topic and resonate with the target audience
- Avoid keyword stuffing or tangential terms

## Output
Present results in this table format with at least 9-12 keywords:

| Keyword | Type | Impact Score |
|---------|------|-------------|
| [example: "best organic dog food for senior labs"] | Long-tail | 8 |
| [example: "organic dog food brands"] | Medium-tail | 7 |
| [example: "dog food"] | Short-tail | 9 |

Include multiple examples of each type, ordered by tail length.
```
