# Educational Research Blueprint Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Educational Research Blueprint Prompt is a free AI prompt that helps educational researchers systematically analyze and improve their research methodologies across the full research lifecycle. This educational research blueprint prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown table with three columns (Current Methods, Potential Improvements, Expected Outcomes) covering 5-7 specific research practices from design through dissemination. It works by taking your research domain and current methods as input, then generating a structured comparison that incorporates recent advancements in research technology, pedagogical theory, ethical considerations, and validity concerns. Researchers use it to identify gaps in existing practices, explore evidence-based alternatives, and forecast the impact of proposed changes on educational policies and real-world outcomes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need to audit your research approach, prepare a methodology section, or justify methodological choices to funding bodies or IRB committees. ● Maps data collection, analysis techniques, and dissemination strategies against contemporary research standards ● Grounds every improvement recommendation in feasibility, ethical considerations, and validity requirements ● Projects realistic outcomes aligned with educational policy impact and practical implementation constraints ● Covers the full research lifecycle in 5-7 focused rows for rapid review and decision-making ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational researcher specializing in research methodology optimization and evidence-based practice improvement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive blueprint for improving educational research practices by examining current methods, identifying evidence-based improvements, and projecting outcomes.

## Context
Educational research domain: {{research-domain}}

Current situation: {{current-methods-and-goals}}

Consider:
- Data collection and analysis techniques
- Recent advancements in research technology and pedagogical theory
- Ethical considerations and validity concerns
- Potential impact on educational policies and practices
- Feasibility within the given educational context

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Current Methods | Potential Improvements | Expected Outcomes |
|-----------------|------------------------|-------------------|

Each row should address a specific research method or practice, providing a detailed comparison across all three dimensions. Include 5-7 methods covering the full research lifecycle (design, data collection, analysis, dissemination). Ensure improvements are grounded in contemporary research standards and realistic for implementation.
```
