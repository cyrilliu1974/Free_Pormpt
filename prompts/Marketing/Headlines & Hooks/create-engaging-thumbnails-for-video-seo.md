# Video Thumbnail Design Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Video Thumbnail Design Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces complete thumbnail planning workflows for video creators and content marketers. This video thumbnail prompt for ChatGPT walks you through six stages: content analysis to identify key visual elements, composition and layout recommendations, text overlay strategy with typography guidance, brand color application, platform-specific optimization for YouTube or social media dimensions, and A/B testing suggestions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your video details, brand colors, and platform specs to deliver a numbered action plan. Use it when you need a structured approach to thumbnail design that considers both aesthetic impact and discoverability, especially for YouTube SEO, social video campaigns, or channel branding projects. ● Analyzes video content to pinpoint which visual elements and moments will drive the highest engagement ● Delivers typography, placement, and wording recommendations for text overlays that remain legible at small sizes ● Applies your brand color palette while ensuring contrast and accessibility across devices ● Includes platform-specific optimization techniques for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and other video hosting services ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer and video content strategist specializing in high-performance video thumbnails that drive clicks and improve discoverability.

## Task
Create a comprehensive thumbnail strategy for the given video that balances visual appeal with platform optimization. Analyze the content, design eye-catching visuals with bold legible text overlays, and ensure the thumbnail accurately represents the video topic while maximizing click-through rate.

## Context
Video topic and audience: {{video-details}}
Brand colors: {{brand-colors}}
Platform and dimensions: {{platform-specs}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list detailing each step of the thumbnail creation process, including:
1. Content analysis and key visual elements to feature
2. Composition and layout recommendations
3. Text overlay strategy (wording, typography, placement)
4. Color scheme application using the brand palette
5. Platform-specific optimization techniques
6. A/B testing suggestions if applicable
```
