# SEO Keyword Prioritization by Impact and Relevance

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword Prioritization by Impact and Relevance is a free AI prompt that analyzes and ranks keyword opportunities for SEO analysts, content strategists, and digital marketers building data-driven search strategies. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT produces a sortable markdown table of 15-25 candidate keywords - spanning branded terms, product categories, problem-solution queries, and long-tail variations - each evaluated for monthly search volume, competition level (low/medium/high), and a 1-10 relevance score based on alignment with your business offerings and audience intent. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating prioritized keyword lists sorted by relevance to surface the most valuable ranking opportunities first. Use it when you need to move beyond generic keyword tools and apply strategic context to identify terms that balance search demand with realistic ranking potential for a specific business or niche. ● Generates 15-25 candidate keywords across branded, category, problem-solution, and long-tail search types ● Evaluates each keyword for monthly search volume, ranking difficulty (competition), and strategic fit (relevance score 1-10) ● Outputs a markdown table sorted by relevance score to highlight the highest-impact opportunities ● Adapts to any business context through a single variable, making it reusable across industries and clients ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst specializing in keyword research and competitive analysis.

## Task
Identify high-impact keywords to optimize SEO efforts by analyzing search volume, competition level, and strategic relevance.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Research and compile potential keywords related to this business. For each keyword, determine:
- **Search volume**: monthly search estimates
- **Competition**: low/medium/high based on ranking difficulty
- **Relevance score**: 1-10 rating based on alignment with the business's content, offerings, and audience intent

## Process
1. Generate 15-25 candidate keywords spanning branded terms, product/service categories, problem-solution queries, and long-tail variations
2. Analyze each keyword's metrics
3. Prioritize keywords that balance strong search demand with realistic ranking opportunity

## Output
Present findings as a markdown table with columns: **Keyword** | **Search Volume** | **Competition** | **Relevance Score**

Sort by Relevance Score (descending) to surface the most valuable opportunities first.
```
