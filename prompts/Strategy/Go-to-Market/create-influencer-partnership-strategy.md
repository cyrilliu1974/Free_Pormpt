# Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Influencer Partnership Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a structured influencer partnership program for brands looking to align influencer marketing with SEO and brand awareness goals. This influencer partnership strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through seven critical sections: campaign goals that tie influencer reach to search performance, five-point selection criteria covering audience fit and engagement thresholds, a step-by-step outreach process from research to signed agreement, contractual essentials including content rights and disclosure requirements, collaboration guidelines that preserve authenticity while meeting keyword objectives, a three-metric success table with baselines and tracking methods, and an ongoing optimization plan. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a ready-to-share strategy document that marketing teams can use to vet creators, negotiate partnerships, and measure both traffic and engagement impact. Use it when launching a new influencer program, standardizing partner selection, or connecting content collaborations to measurable SEO lift. ● Defines campaign goals that balance brand awareness with search-engine ranking objectives. ● Provides five vetting criteria - audience demographics, engagement thresholds, content quality, SEO alignment, and brand safety - so teams choose partners strategically. ● Outputs a structured success-metrics table with baseline, target, and tracking method columns for SEO, engagement, and conversion KPIs. ● Includes contract essentials and collaboration guidelines to ensure content remains authentic while hitting keyword and disclosure requirements. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specialized in influencer partnerships and SEO optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive influencer partnership program strategy document for {{brand-name}}. The document should provide actionable guidelines for selecting the right influencer partners and establish key metrics for measuring how these partnerships boost search engine rankings.

## Context
The strategy must balance influencer authenticity with SEO performance goals. Focus on influencers who have a strong presence in the brand's target market and align with brand values. Prioritize high engagement rates and proven track records of driving measurable results. Ensure content and audience demographics align with target keywords and SEO goals. Avoid influencers with controversial histories that could harm brand reputation and search rankings.

## Output
Structure the strategy document with these sections:

**Campaign Goals**
List 3 specific, measurable goals for the influencer partnership program that tie to both brand awareness and SEO performance.

**Influencer Selection Criteria**
Provide 5 concrete criteria for vetting and selecting the right influencer partners (e.g., audience demographics, engagement rate thresholds, content quality standards, SEO alignment, brand safety).

**Influencer Outreach Process**
Outline a 5-step process from initial research through securing the partnership (research, initial contact, pitch, negotiation, agreement).

**Partnership Agreement Key Points**
List 4 essential contractual points (deliverables, content rights, disclosure requirements, performance expectations).

**Content Collaboration Guidelines**
Provide 4 guidelines for working with influencers on content creation that maintains authenticity while meeting SEO objectives.

**Success Metrics**
Create a table with 3 metrics:

| Metric | Baseline | Target | Tracking Method |
|--------|----------|--------|------------------|
| [SEO-focused metric] | [Current state] | [Goal] | [Tool/method] |
| [Engagement metric] | [Current state] | [Goal] | [Tool/method] |
| [Conversion metric] | [Current state] | [Goal] | [Tool/method] |

**Campaign Analysis and Optimization**
List 3 steps for ongoing analysis and optimization of the program (data collection, performance review cycle, iteration strategy).
```
