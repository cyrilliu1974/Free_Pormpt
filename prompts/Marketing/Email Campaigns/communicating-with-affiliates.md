# Affiliate Partner Communication Email Generator

## 簡介

The Affiliate Partner Communication Email Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured partnership emails for marketers managing affiliate programs. This affiliate communication prompt for ChatGPT produces complete email templates that balance relationship-building with tactical guidance. It generates subject lines, personalized greetings, product positioning, promotional asset inventories, tracking link explanations, and conversion-focused strategies tailored to your product description, affiliate terms, and available materials. Use it when onboarding new affiliates, announcing product updates, or re-engaging dormant partners across e-commerce, SaaS, digital products, and service businesses. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Produces complete email structure from subject line through sign-off with clear calls-to-action ● Integrates your specific product details, commission terms, and promotional materials into cohesive messaging ● Balances rapport-building language with tactical promotion strategies and tracking transparency ● Outputs 2-3 concrete best practices tailored to your product and affiliate audience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer specializing in affiliate partnership communication.

## Task
Write a friendly yet professional email to affiliate partners that promotes the product, strengthens the partnership relationship, and drives sales through clear guidance and comprehensive resources.

## Context
This email serves to onboard or update affiliate partners with everything they need to successfully promote the product. The tone should balance warmth with professionalism, emphasizing mutual benefit while providing actionable promotion strategies.

**Product & Market:**
{{product-description}}

**Affiliate Terms:**
{{affiliate-terms}}

**Available Assets:**
{{promotional-materials}}

## Output
Structure the email with:

**Subject Line:** Compelling and benefit-focused

**Greeting:** Warm and personalized

**Body:**
- Open with rapport-building acknowledgment of the partnership's mutual value
- Present the product's unique selling points and customer value proposition
- Detail available promotional materials (banners, images, videos, copy templates)
- Explain the affiliate's unique tracking link and reporting process
- Share 2-3 specific promotion strategies or best practices for maximizing conversions
- Invite questions and offer ongoing support

**Closing:** Professional sign-off with clear contact information for follow-up

Keep the tone conversational, the structure scannable, and the call-to-action clear.
```
