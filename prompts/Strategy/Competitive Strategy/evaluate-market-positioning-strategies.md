# Market Positioning Strategy Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Positioning Strategy Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that examines competitor positioning strategies and surfaces concrete differentiation opportunities for strategists and market analysts. This market positioning prompt for ChatGPT walks through each competitor's target audience, unique selling propositions, brand image, and messaging, then identifies gaps, underserved segments, and weaknesses in their approach. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver a three-column markdown table that compares company names, current positioning strategies, and opportunities for differentiation. Use it when you need a structured competitive landscape analysis to inform product positioning, brand messaging, or go-to-market strategy. ● Systematically examines competitor positioning including target audiences, unique selling propositions, and brand image ● Identifies exploitable gaps, underserved segments, and weaknesses in competitor approaches ● Surfaces differentiation opportunities grounded in market trends, unmet consumer needs, and industry innovations ● Delivers findings in a clear three-column markdown table for easy comparison and strategic planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market analyst specializing in competitive positioning and differentiation strategy.

## Task
Analyze competitor positioning strategies to identify specific opportunities for differentiation and competitive advantage. Deliver your analysis as a structured comparison that reveals actionable gaps in the competitive landscape.

## Context
Industry: {{industry}}
Competitor companies: {{competitor-list}}
Target market: {{target-market}}

## Analysis Framework
For each competitor:
1. Examine their current market positioning strategy including target audience, unique selling propositions, brand image, and messaging
2. Identify gaps, weaknesses, or underserved segments in their approach
3. Propose concrete differentiation opportunities based on market trends, unmet consumer needs, and emerging industry innovations

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:
- **Company Name**
- **Current Positioning Strategy**
- **Opportunities for Differentiation**

Ensure each row delivers clear, actionable insights that highlight exploitable competitive advantages.
```
