# Agile Product Development Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Agile Product Development Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds a complete agile implementation plan tailored to your organization's context and goals. This agile product development prompt for ChatGPT delivers actionable recommendations across every phase of your product lifecycle - Discovery, Planning, Development, and Delivery - with specific practices, measurable KPIs, and initiatives to foster an innovation culture. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs a structured strategy document that includes 5-7 core agile principles, 3-5 practices per phase, 2-3 KPIs per phase, and a sequenced transformation roadmap with timeframes and change management guidance. Teams use it to move from waterfall processes to agile workflows, audit existing agile maturity, or design rollout plans for cross-functional squads. Product managers, engineering leaders, and transformation consultants reach for this prompt when they need a customized agile blueprint rather than generic frameworks. ● Delivers phase-specific agile practices for Discovery, Planning, Development, and Delivery with concrete KPIs for each stage ● Provides 4-6 initiatives to build an innovation culture that encourages experimentation and continuous improvement ● Generates a step-by-step transformation roadmap with timeframes, dependencies, and change management strategies ● Tailors every recommendation to your organization's unique context, team structure, and business goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in agile methodologies and innovation, providing guidance to accelerate product development and foster continuous improvement.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive agile strategy tailored to the organization's context. Provide actionable recommendations across the product development lifecycle, define success metrics, and outline a transformation roadmap.

## Context
{{organization-context}}

## Output
Deliver your strategy in the following structure:

**Agile Principles Adoption:**
● List 5-7 core agile principles most relevant to this organization's context and goals

**Product Development Phases:**

For each major phase of the product development lifecycle (Discovery, Planning, Development, Delivery, and any others relevant to the current process):

**Phase: [Phase Name]**
Agile Practices:
● 3-5 specific practices to implement in this phase
KPIs:
● 2-3 measurable indicators to track success

**Initiatives to Foster Innovation Culture:**
● List 4-6 concrete initiatives that encourage experimentation, learning, and continuous innovation within the organization

**Agile Transformation Roadmap:**
1. Provide 5-7 sequential steps for implementing this agile strategy
2. Include timeframes and dependencies where relevant
3. Address change management and adoption challenges
```
