# Simplify Complex Information Prompt

## 簡介

The Simplify Complex Information Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms jargon-heavy or technical content into clear, accessible explanations for any audience. This simplify complex information prompt for ChatGPT works by identifying core concepts, stripping away unnecessary technical language, and translating terms into everyday examples and relatable analogies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and produces three distinct sections: numbered key points ranked by importance, plain-language explanations with helpful comparisons, and bullet-pointed practical implications that show real-world applications. Use it to explain research papers to non-specialists, onboard new team members to technical systems, or communicate policy changes to diverse audiences. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to bridge the gap between expert knowledge and general understanding, or when you want to make specialized information actionable for people without domain expertise. ● Accepts any complex source material and a target audience knowledge level, then adjusts language and depth accordingly. ● Organizes explanations in logical sequence, covering the full concept evenly unless you specify a narrower focus. ● Includes analogies and real-world examples that connect abstract ideas to everyday experience. ● Delivers practical implications so readers understand not just what something is, but why it matters and how to apply it. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert information architect and communication specialist who distills complex concepts into clear, accessible insights.

## Task

Transform dense, complicated information into digestible content by:

- Identifying core concepts and underlying principles
- Stripping away jargon and unnecessary technical language
- Translating technical terms into everyday language using relatable analogies and examples
- Organizing key points in order of importance or logical sequence
- Connecting information to real-world applications
- Assuming no prior specialized knowledge in your explanations
- Covering the whole concept evenly unless a specific focus is requested

## Context

Complex information to simplify:
{{complex-information}}

Target audience knowledge level:
{{audience-knowledge-level}}

## Output

Structure your response with three distinct sections:

**Key Points** — a numbered list of the most critical foundational points

**Simple Explanations** — clear paragraphs for each key point, written in plain language with analogies where helpful

**Practical Implications** — bullet points showing real-world applications and why this matters
```
