# Mind Mapping Prompt for Brainstorming and Idea Structure

## 簡介

The Mind Mapping Prompt for Brainstorming and Idea Structure is a free AI prompt that generates comprehensive, hierarchical mind maps to accelerate brainstorming and organize complex ideas for professionals, students, and creative teams. This mind mapping prompt for ChatGPT produces text-based visual structures that place your central theme at the top, extend 2-4 main branches for key categories, and develop sub-branches with related concepts, examples, and thought-provoking questions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using indentation, symbols (●, ○, ◆, →, ✦), and color descriptions to show relationships clearly. Real-world use cases include project planning, content strategy development, research organization, problem-solving workshops, and curriculum design. The prompt deliberately includes unconventional connections to spark creative thinking beyond linear approaches. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform scattered thoughts into structured frameworks, prepare for strategic meetings, or explore a topic from multiple angles systematically. ● Produces indented text structures with clear hierarchical levels using symbols and emojis to represent branches and relationships ● Highlights cross-connections between different branches to reveal non-obvious links and synthesis opportunities ● Includes concrete examples, facts, and provocative questions at nodes to stimulate deeper exploration ● Suggests at least one unconventional association per map to push thinking beyond predictable patterns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert mind mapping specialist who structures ideas to accelerate brainstorming and boost productivity.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive, hierarchical mind map for the given topic. Present it as an indented text structure using symbols or emojis to represent levels and branches.

## Context
{{topic-and-context}}

## Output
Deliver a mind map that:

- Places the central theme at the top, with main branches (key categories) extending from it
- Develops 2-4 sub-branches under each main branch, exploring related ideas and concepts
- Uses concise keywords or short phrases at each node
- Describes colors or symbols to distinguish branch families visually
- Highlights connections between branches where meaningful links exist
- Includes relevant examples, facts, or provocative questions to stimulate further thought
- Organizes hierarchically—most important concepts nearest the center
- Suggests at least one unconventional connection or unexpected association to spark creative thinking

Format using indentation and symbols (e.g., ●, ○, ◆, →, ✦) to show relationships and hierarchy clearly.
```
