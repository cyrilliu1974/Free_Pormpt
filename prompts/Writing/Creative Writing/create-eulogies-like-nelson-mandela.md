# Nelson Mandela-Style Eulogy Writer for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Nelson Mandela-Style Eulogy Writer is a free AI prompt that helps you craft a moving tribute speech channeling Mandela's distinctive voice of reconciliation, humility, and human dignity. This eulogy prompt for ChatGPT takes a profile of the deceased and produces a structured tribute that opens with a powerful statement capturing their essence, highlights their admirable qualities and achievements, weaves in personal stories revealing their character, and closes with an inspiring call to action. The prompt instructs the AI to employ Mandela's measured, generous tone along with rhetorical devices like repetition, metaphor, and vivid imagery to create emotional resonance. It runs effectively on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when preparing a memorial service speech that balances celebration of life with authentic emotion, focusing on positive impact and enduring legacy rather than sorrow. ● Produces paragraphs in Mandela's distinctive voice - eloquent, unifying, and emphasizing justice and dignity ● Structures the tribute to open powerfully, celebrate character and achievements, share humanizing stories, and close with a legacy-focused call to action ● Employs rhetorical techniques including repetition, metaphor, and imagery for emotional impact ● Maintains balanced, authentic tone suitable for delivery at memorial services without excessive praise ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are writing a eulogy in the voice and spirit of Nelson Mandela—drawing on his eloquence, emphasis on reconciliation, humility, and commitment to justice and human dignity.

## Task
Deliver a heartfelt tribute that honors the deceased by celebrating their life, character, and lasting impact. Open with a powerful statement that captures their essence. Highlight their most admirable qualities, achievements, and influence on others. Weave in personal stories that reveal their humanity and values. Infuse Mandela's trademark empathy and wisdom, drawing lessons from their life. Close with an inspiring call to action that encourages the audience to carry forward the deceased's legacy.

## Context
{{deceased-profile}}

## Output
Write the eulogy as a series of paragraphs separated by line breaks. Use Mandela's distinctive voice—measured, generous, and unifying. Employ rhetorical devices such as repetition, metaphor, and vivid imagery to create emotional resonance. Focus on positive impact and enduring legacy rather than sorrow. Keep the tone balanced and authentic, avoiding excessive praise. Aim for concise yet impactful length suitable for delivery at a memorial service.
```
