# Podcast Production Plan Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Podcast Production Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that builds complete production blueprints for new podcast series, designed for content creators, marketers, and media professionals launching audio content. This podcast planning prompt for ChatGPT walks through research, episode structuring, guest identification, and content calendar mapping to produce a detailed plan for your first three episodes. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, balancing educational value with entertainment while incorporating your unique selling point, target audience, episode length, and release frequency. Whether you're launching a digital marketing show, an industry interview series, or an educational podcast, the prompt outputs episode titles, core themes, talking points, suggested guest experts, and actionable listener takeaways. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from podcast concept to structured production plan quickly, or when you want a repeatable framework for planning future seasons. ● Creates episode-by-episode breakdowns with titles, themes, and talking points for your first three episodes ● Identifies the type of guest expert needed for each episode based on topic and audience ● Maps actionable takeaways that give listeners immediate value from every episode ● Builds a content calendar framework that supports your chosen release frequency and maintains consistency ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert podcast producer specializing in digital marketing content.

## Task
Develop a complete production plan for a new podcast series, including episode structure, topic outlines, guest interview plans, and a content calendar. Output a detailed blueprint for the first three episodes that balances educational value with entertainment.

## Context
Podcast focus: {{podcast-topic}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Episode format: {{episode-length}} episodes released {{release-frequency}}
Unique selling point: {{usp}}

## Process
1. Research and outline key topics within the podcast focus area
2. Develop conversational scripts that incorporate expert insights and actionable takeaways
3. Identify and plan guest interviews with industry leaders
4. Structure each episode to maintain engagement from intro to outro
5. Map a content calendar that supports consistent releases

## Output
Provide a bullet-point list detailing the structure and content for the first three episodes. For each episode include:
- Episode title and core theme
- Key topics and talking points
- Suggested guest expert (role/expertise needed)
- 2-3 actionable takeaways for listeners
- Notes on how the episode leverages the unique selling point
```
