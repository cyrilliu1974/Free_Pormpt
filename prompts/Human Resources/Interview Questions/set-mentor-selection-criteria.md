# Mentor Selection Criteria Using Emotional Intelligence

## 簡介

The Mentor Selection Criteria Using Emotional Intelligence is a free AI prompt that builds a comprehensive mentor evaluation framework prioritizing emotional intelligence competencies for organizations designing mentorship programs. This mentor selection prompt for ChatGPT produces seven structured components: a trait checklist organized by Goleman's four EI domains (self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship management), behavioral interview questions with follow-up probes to surface authentic capabilities, assessment rubrics with clear scoring criteria, red-flag indicators for poor mentor fit, pairing recommendations matched to mentee needs, validation strategies including reference-check methods, and format adaptations for virtual, in-person, and hybrid environments. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single program-context variable into actionable selection tools that assess interpersonal competencies like empathy and social skills alongside technical expertise. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize mentor vetting, ensure consistency across candidate evaluations, or shift your program focus from credentials alone to the emotional intelligence traits that drive meaningful professional development. ● Trait checklist mapping observable behaviors to Goleman's four emotional intelligence domains ● Behavioral interview questions designed to reveal authentic EI capabilities, not rehearsed answers ● Assessment rubrics and red-flag indicators for consistent, evidence-based candidate evaluation ● Pairing guidelines and validation strategies to match mentors with mentees based on developmental needs and EI profiles ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist specializing in mentorship program design using Daniel Goleman's Emotional Intelligence framework (self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, and relationship management).

## Task

Develop a comprehensive mentor selection system that prioritizes emotional intelligence competencies over technical expertise alone. Create structured evaluation tools, behavioral interview questions, pairing guidelines, and implementation strategies.

## Context

**Program parameters:**
{{program-context}}

Effective mentorship requires deep interpersonal competencies—empathy, self-awareness, and social skills—that drive meaningful professional development. Your system must assess both observable behaviors and underlying emotional competencies, account for virtual versus in-person dynamics, and provide validation methods through reference checks and situational scenarios.

## Output

Deliver the following components, each under a clear heading with organized bullet points:

### 1. Mentor Trait Checklist
Organized by Goleman's four EI domains, with specific observable behaviors and competencies for each.

### 2. Behavioral Interview Questions
Questions designed to reveal authentic emotional intelligence capabilities, not rehearsed answers. Include follow-up probes.

### 3. Assessment Rubrics
Scoring criteria for consistent evaluation across candidates, with clear performance levels.

### 4. Red Flags
Warning signs that indicate poor mentor fit, including behaviors that suggest low EI or harmful dynamics.

### 5. Pairing Recommendations
Guidelines for matching mentors to mentees based on developmental needs, EI profiles, and organizational goals.

### 6. Validation Strategies
Methods to verify EI claims through reference checks, situational scenarios, and trial interactions.

### 7. Format Adaptation
Specific adjustments for virtual, in-person, and hybrid mentorship environments.

Provide all criteria, questions, and recommendations in actionable bullet-point format for immediate implementation.
```
