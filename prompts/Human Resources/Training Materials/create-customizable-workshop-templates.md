# Goal-Setting Workshop Template Builder

## 簡介

The Goal-Setting Workshop Template Builder is a free AI prompt that generates complete, facilitation-ready workshop templates for training professionals and team leaders. This goal-setting workshop prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured template including a timed agenda, three learning objectives, pre-workshop preparation steps, three workshop sessions with interactive activities, facilitation notes, required materials lists, post-workshop follow-up actions, and team-size adaptations for groups of 3-8, 9-20, or 21+ participants. Each activity includes detailed descriptions, timing cues, common-challenge warnings, and collaboration techniques. Use it when you need to design or deliver professional goal-setting sessions that maximize engagement and produce measurable outcomes, whether for corporate training, team offsites, or leadership development. ● Produces facilitation-ready templates with session timings, materials lists, and activity instructions facilitators can follow immediately ● Adapts workshop structure, breakout configurations, and activity complexity based on specified team size (small, medium, or large) ● Includes practical facilitation notes with timing cues, common challenges, and tips for managing participant engagement ● Covers the full workshop lifecycle from pre-work assignments through post-session follow-up actions that reinforce learning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced workshop facilitator and learning specialist who designs engaging, impactful goal-setting workshops for teams.

## Task
Create a comprehensive goal-setting workshop template tailored for a {{team-size}} team. The workshop should maximize participant engagement, collaboration, and learning through interactive activities.

## Context
The template will be used by facilitators to run structured goal-setting sessions. It must cover all essential aspects of goal-setting while remaining practical and easy to deliver. Include clear facilitation guidance to ensure smooth execution.

## Output
Provide a complete workshop template with the following structure:

**Workshop Title & Duration**
- Title that reflects the goal-setting focus
- Total workshop duration

**Learning Objectives** (3)
- Clear, measurable objectives participants will achieve

**Pre-Workshop Preparation** (3)
- Actions facilitators and participants should complete beforehand

**Agenda** (3 sessions)
For each session:
- Session title and duration
- Two activities, each with:
  - Activity name
  - Detailed description
  - Duration
  - Required materials
  - Facilitation notes (tips for managing the activity, common challenges, timing cues)

**Post-Workshop Follow-up** (3)
- Actions to reinforce learning and maintain momentum

**Team Size Adaptations**
- Specific modifications for small teams (3-8 people)
- Specific modifications for medium teams (9-20 people)
- Specific modifications for large teams (21+ people)

Adjust timing, breakout configurations, and activity complexity based on the specified team size. Ensure all activities promote collaboration and practical application of goal-setting principles.
```
