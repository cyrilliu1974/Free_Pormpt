# Return of Assets Policy Generator for Employee Offboarding

## 簡介

The Return of Assets Policy Generator for Employee Offboarding is a free AI prompt that creates complete asset recovery policies covering IT equipment, access credentials, and proprietary materials during employee exits. This return of assets policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a structured document with timelines, accountability chains, verification checklists, and escalation protocols tailored to your organization's compliance requirements. HR teams use it to ensure laptops, access badges, passwords, VPN credentials, and confidential documents are recovered systematically, while IT departments rely on the audit trail and sign-off procedures to close security gaps. The policy balances rigor with efficiency, preventing bottlenecks during transitions while maintaining legal protections. Reach for this prompt when building or updating offboarding procedures that must satisfy regulatory standards, protect intellectual property, and prevent data breaches caused by unreturned assets or active credentials. ● Covers all critical asset categories: IT hardware, digital access credentials, physical keys and badges, and proprietary materials including code and client data. ● Establishes step-by-step return processes with specific timelines and responsible parties for HR, IT, and departing employees. ● Includes verification checklists, sign-off requirements, and audit trail documentation that satisfy common compliance frameworks. ● Defines non-compliance escalation protocols and remediation steps integrated with existing security incident response procedures. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in cybersecurity policy and compliance, specializing in employee offboarding procedures that prevent security gaps and ensure legal compliance.

## Task
Create a comprehensive Return of Assets Policy for employee offboarding that covers physical and digital asset categories, establishes clear accountability chains, verification procedures, timelines, and escalation protocols.

## Context
{{organization-context}}

The policy must address:
- IT equipment (laptops, phones, badges, keys, peripherals)
- Access credentials (accounts, passwords, VPN, cloud services, physical access)
- Proprietary materials (documents, code, client data, research)
- Documentation and audit trail requirements

Balance security rigor with operational efficiency to avoid bottlenecks in employee transitions.

## Output
Deliver a structured policy document with:

**1. Policy Overview**
- Purpose and scope
- Applicability and enforcement authority

**2. Asset Return Procedures**
- Specific timelines for each asset category
- Responsible parties (employee, manager, HR, IT)
- Step-by-step return processes

**3. Verification & Documentation Systems**
- Checklists for HR and IT teams
- Sign-off requirements and audit trails
- Record retention standards

**4. Compliance & Legal Language**
- Employee obligations and acknowledgment requirements
- Alignment with {{compliance-requirements}}
- Protection of organizational interests

**5. Non-Compliance Protocols**
- Escalation procedures
- Remediation steps
- Integration with existing security incident response

Format all checklists as numbered or bulleted lists. Ensure the policy is immediately implementable by HR and IT departments without further interpretation.
```
