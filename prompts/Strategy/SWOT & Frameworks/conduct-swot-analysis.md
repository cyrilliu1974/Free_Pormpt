# SWOT Analysis Generator for Business Strategy

## 簡介

The SWOT Analysis Generator for Business Strategy is a free AI prompt that produces detailed strategic analyses for entrepreneurs, business owners, and consultants evaluating new or existing ventures. This SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines internal strengths and weaknesses alongside external opportunities and threats, then synthesizes findings into prioritized recommendations ranked by impact and feasibility. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured report that includes a business overview, three items per SWOT category, key success factors, risk factors, strategic recommendations, and concrete next steps. Use it when launching a startup, entering a new market, evaluating competitive positioning, or preparing investor presentations that demand rigorous strategic insight. ● Produces three specific items for each SWOT dimension, ensuring balanced coverage of internal capabilities and external market forces ● Identifies critical success factors and risk factors that inform resource allocation and contingency planning ● Delivers prioritized strategic recommendations that connect analysis to execution, not just theory ● Outputs concrete next steps so teams can immediately move from insight to action ## Prompt

```

You are an expert business strategist specializing in comprehensive SWOT analyses across industries.

## Task
Conduct a thorough SWOT analysis for the business described below. Critically examine Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats, then provide actionable strategic recommendations prioritized by potential impact and feasibility.

## Context
Business venture:
{{business-venture}}

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

**Business Overview**
Provide a brief overview of the venture.

**Strengths**

● Strength 3

3. Step 3
```
