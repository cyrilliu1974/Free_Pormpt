# Retro Cyberpunk Cityscape Generator

## 簡介

The Retro Cyberpunk Cityscape Generator is a free AI prompt that creates 8-bit pixel art scenes in the iconic eBoy aesthetic for digital artists and concept designers. This cyberpunk cityscape prompt for Midjourney produces cinematic stills with HDR effects, blending nostalgic pixel art with futuristic neon-lit environments at a 4:5 aspect ratio. It applies chaos variation (33), experimental rendering (15), and heavy stylization (400) to transform any scene description into detailed retro artwork reminiscent of early computer graphics mixed with modern cyberpunk themes.
