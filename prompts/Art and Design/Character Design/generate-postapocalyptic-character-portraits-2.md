# Post-Apocalyptic Character Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Post-Apocalyptic Character Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces cinematic survivor images in abandoned, nature-reclaimed environments for game designers, writers, and concept artists. This post-apocalyptic character prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana renders full-body portraits of weathered survivors - complete with layered clothing, aging details, and practical gear - walking through overgrown cities where rust, ivy, and wildlife reclaim human spaces. The 16:9 wide-lens composition captures both the character and the melancholy atmosphere, from cracked pavement to collapsed buildings and deer wandering urban ruins. Use it for book covers, game concept art, storyboards, or narrative marketing when you need authentic survival aesthetics without location shoots. ● Renders full-body characters with dirt-smudged skin, frayed clothing, practical backpacks, and personal items like wedding bands or broken watches that tell a story. ● Customizes environment (overgrown cities, rusted vehicles, ivy-covered walls), character role (scavenger, medic, scout), and mood (melancholy, hopeful, desolate) through three variables. ● Produces 16:9 cinematic compositions with 35mm wide-lens aesthetics, soft overcast lighting, and muted color palettes that evoke realism over fantasy. ● Includes environmental details like deer in the background, reflections in broken glass, and tall grass cracking pavement to show nature reclaiming human space. ## Prompt
