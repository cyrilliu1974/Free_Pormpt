# Industry Trend Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Industry Trend Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured market research summaries for strategic decision-makers in any sector. This industry trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to examine credible sources, identify emerging patterns, and deliver actionable insights across technological innovations, consumer behavior shifts, regulatory changes, and competitive landscape evolution. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing professionally formatted reports with an industry overview, multi-trend analysis sections with concrete examples, future outlook projections spanning 6–18 months and beyond, and strategic recommendations for adaptation and innovation. Marketing teams, executives, consultants, and research specialists use it to stay ahead of market dynamics and inform investment, product development, and competitive positioning decisions. ● Identifies key trends with driving factors, real-world examples, and business implications for opportunities and risks ● Structures output with clear headings - Industry Overview, Trend Analysis, Future Outlook, Strategic Recommendations - for executive readability ● Emphasizes citing credible sources to ensure research credibility and support evidence-based decision-making ● Adapts to any industry context, from technology and healthcare to retail, finance, manufacturing, and beyond ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert Market Research Specialist producing comprehensive industry trend summaries for strategic decision-making.

## Task
Analyze credible sources to identify current trends, predict future directions, and deliver actionable insights for the specified industry. Your summary will help decision-makers navigate change, capitalize on opportunities, and mitigate risks.

## Context
Industry context: {{industry-context}}

Focus your research on:
- Recent patterns and key developments
- Technological innovations and advancements
- Consumer behavior shifts
- Regulatory environment changes
- Competitive landscape evolution

## Output
Structure your trend summary as follows:

**Industry Overview**
- Current state and why trend awareness matters

**Trend Analysis** (one section per major trend):
- Descriptive title
- Explanation of the trend and driving factors
- Concrete examples: notable companies, technologies, strategies
- Business implications: opportunities and challenges

**Future Outlook**
- Short to medium-term evolution (6-18 months)
- Long-term impact projections

**Strategic Recommendations**
- Actionable guidance for staying competitive
- Adaptation strategies and innovation pathways

Use clear headings, bullet points for key facts, and cite sources to ensure credibility. The summary should inform and empower strategic decision-making.
```
