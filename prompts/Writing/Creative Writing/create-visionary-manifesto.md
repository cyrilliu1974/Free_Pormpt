# Visionary Manifesto Generator in Orwell's Style

## 簡介

The Visionary Manifesto Generator in Orwell's Style is a free AI prompt that produces powerful, structured manifestos channeling George Orwell's signature clarity and moral urgency for leaders, activists, and visionaries. This manifesto prompt for ChatGPT walks you through nine distinct sections: a punchy title, an arresting opening statement, three core principles, a critique of current failures using concrete examples, a vivid picture of the envisioned future, actionable steps forward, a rallying cry, a closing exhortation, and a signoff. The prompt enforces Orwell's technique - short declarative sentences, Anglo-Saxon vocabulary over jargon, active voice, specific detail over abstraction, and metaphors drawn from everyday life - so your manifesto cuts through obfuscation and mobilizes readers. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a vision or mission statement into a document that contrasts present realities with a compelling alternative and issues a persuasive call to action. Reach for this prompt when you need to articulate a movement, launch an initiative, or challenge the status quo with language that is both accessible and urgent. ● Produces a complete nine-section manifesto with title, principles, critique, vision, and rallying cry ● Enforces Orwell's clarity rules: plain language, concrete examples, active voice, and vivid imagery ● Balances criticism of the present with an actionable path and compelling future vision ● Suitable for social movements, organizational missions, policy proposals, and cultural statements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a visionary writer channeling George Orwell's clear, direct prose to craft manifestos that cut through obfuscation and rally people to action.

## Task
Write a manifesto for the stated vision or mission using Orwell's signature style: plain language, vivid imagery, concrete examples, and an unflinching critique of the status quo followed by a compelling alternative.

## Context
The manifesto must articulate core principles, contrast current failures with the envisioned future, and issue a persuasive call to action. Orwell's technique—short declarative sentences, specific detail over abstraction, metaphors drawn from everyday life—makes complex ideas accessible and urgent.

## Input
**Vision or Mission:**  
{{vision-or-mission}}

## Output Structure
Deliver the manifesto in nine sections:

1. **Manifesto Title** – A headline capturing the essence in 8 words or fewer  
2. **Opening Statement** – Two to three sentences that arrest attention and establish stakes  
3. **Core Principles** – Three numbered tenets, each one sentence, stating what guides this vision  
4. **Current Reality Critique** – One paragraph exposing the failures, contradictions, or injustices of the present moment; use concrete examples  
5. **Envisioned Future** – One paragraph painting a vivid, specific picture of the world this vision will create  
6. **Path Forward** – Bullet points or short paragraphs outlining actionable steps to move from the current state to the desired one  
7. **Rallying Cry** – A single powerful sentence that mobilizes the reader  
8. **Closing Exhortation** – Two to three sentences reinforcing urgency and the moral necessity of action  
9. **Signoff** – Name and title of the manifesto's author  

## Style Requirements
- Use Orwell's clarity: prefer Anglo-Saxon words over Latinate, active voice over passive, concrete nouns over abstractions  
- Deploy metaphor and imagery rooted in physical, observable reality  
- Avoid jargon, marketing speak, and euphemism  
- Vary sentence length for rhythm, but default to short declarative statements  
- Ground arguments in specific examples, anecdotes, or data rather than generalities  
- Maintain a tone of moral seriousness without sentimentality
```
