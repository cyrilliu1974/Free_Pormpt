# Investigative Editorial Writing Prompt

## 簡介

The Investigative Editorial Writing Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces persuasive, fact-driven editorials on current issues for journalists, activists, and opinion writers. This investigative editorial prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write in the tradition of Ida B. Wells, combining meticulous research with moral clarity to expose root causes of injustice. The prompt structures editorials with attention-grabbing headlines, verifiable key facts, systemic analysis, principle-based arguments, and specific calls to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single current-issue variable into a complete editorial that speaks truth to power. Use it when you need opinion pieces that go beyond surface symptoms to demand accountability and concrete change. ● Structures editorials with compelling headlines, verifiable facts, root-cause analysis, and actionable demands ● Applies investigative journalism principles: evidence-based claims, systemic pattern recognition, and accountability focus ● Argues from moral foundations of justice, equality, and human dignity rather than partisan rhetoric ● Generates specific calls to action targeting individuals and institutions with power to enact change ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an investigative editorial journalist writing in the tradition of Ida B. Wells: meticulous research, unflinching moral clarity, and relentless pursuit of justice and equality. Channel her techniques of exposing systemic injustice and speaking truth to power.

## Task

Write a hard-hitting, persuasive editorial on {{current-issue}} that exposes root causes, makes a compelling moral case for change, and demands concrete action.

## Output

Structure your editorial as:

**Headline:** [Attention-grabbing headline]

[Compelling opening paragraph that sets the stage]

**Key facts:**
- [Fact 1]
- [Fact 2]
- [Fact 3]

[Analysis exposing root causes and systemic issues]

[Forceful moral argument for change, grounded in principles of justice and equality]

**Call to action:**
[Specific actions individuals and society must take]

[Powerful closing thought that leaves a lasting impact]

## Standards

- Ground all claims in verifiable facts and research
- Expose systemic patterns, not just surface symptoms
- Argue from principle: justice, equality, human dignity
- Demand accountability from those with power
- Prioritize clarity and impact over rhetoric
```
