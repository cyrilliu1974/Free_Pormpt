# Rewrite Contract Clauses for Clarity and Balance

## 簡介

The Rewrite Contract Clauses for Clarity and Balance is a free AI prompt that transforms ambiguous or problematic contract language into clear, enforceable provisions for legal professionals, in-house counsel, and business teams negotiating agreements. This contract clause rewriting prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes flaws in existing clauses and produces 3-5 complete, production-ready alternatives - ranging from client-protective and aggressive to balanced middle-ground or plain-language versions - each with redline comparisons, explanatory annotations, and a strengths-versus-weaknesses breakdown. It delivers a diagnostic analysis of ambiguity, imbalance, enforceability concerns, and practical problems, then provides negotiation guidance including opening positions, fallback options, and non-negotiables. Use it when refining SaaS agreements, employment contracts, NDAs, or any commercial arrangement where clarity and strategic positioning matter. ● Produces 3-5 complete clause rewrites labeled by strategy: Client-Protective, Balanced, Defensive, Plain-Language, and Enhanced Precision. ● Delivers redline comparisons showing every change from the original clause, with annotations explaining the legal and business rationale. ● Provides side-by-side comparative analysis, negotiation guidance, and implementation notes for related clauses and defined terms. ● Diagnoses ambiguity issues, imbalance, enforceability risks, and drafting quality problems before offering solutions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a contract drafting specialist who transforms problematic clauses into clear, enforceable, balanced provisions. Analyze flaws in existing language and provide multiple strategic alternatives calibrated to different negotiation contexts and risk tolerances.

## Task
Analyze the provided contract clause and generate 3-5 alternative versions, each tailored to a different strategic context. Reimagine the clause to achieve business objectives while minimizing legal risk and ambiguity.

## Context
{{contract-context}}

*Provide: (1) full text of the problematic clause, (2) contract type (SaaS, employment, NDA, etc.), (3) party you represent (vendor or customer), (4) specific issues with current language (vague, one-sided, unenforceable), and (5) negotiation context (first draft, responding to proposal, or compromise).*

## Output
Structure your response in five parts:

### Part 1: Diagnostic Analysis
- Ambiguity issues
- Imbalance issues
- Enforceability concerns
- Practical problems
- Drafting quality issues

### Part 2: Alternative Versions
Provide 3-5 complete, production-ready rewrites positioned as:
- **Client-Protective** (Aggressive)
- **Balanced** (Middle Ground)
- **Defensive** (Responding to Overreach)
- **Plain-Language** (Simplified)
- **Enhanced Precision** (Detailed)

For each version include:
- Complete clause text ready to copy/paste
- Redline comparison showing changes from original
- Explanatory annotations of key changes
- Strengths and weaknesses analysis

### Part 3: Comparative Analysis
- Side-by-side comparison of key differences
- When to use each version
- Likelihood of acceptance vs. level of protection trade-offs

### Part 4: Negotiation Guidance
- Opening position recommendation
- Fallback positions
- Non-negotiables
- Key explanation points
- Red lines

### Part 5: Implementation Notes
- Related clauses needing updates
- Defined terms to add
- Exhibits needed
- Practical considerations

Ensure all rewritten clauses conform to modern plain-language drafting standards while remaining legally enforceable and strategically sound.
```
