# Value Proposition Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Value Proposition Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customer-focused value propositions for product marketers, founders, and marketing teams. This value proposition prompt for ChatGPT takes your product description and customer profile - including demographics, psychographics, needs, and goals - and outputs a structured framework: target customer analysis, key features and differentiators, a compelling headline and subheadline, a 2-3 sentence elevator pitch, three supporting proof points, and a clear call to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, grounding every claim in genuine customer needs rather than generic benefits. Use it when launching a new product, repositioning an existing offer, or sharpening your marketing messaging to convert browsers into buyers. ● Analyzes target demographics, psychographics, and goals to anchor every message in real customer needs ● Produces a complete framework - headline, subheadline, elevator pitch, supporting points, and call to action - in one run ● Highlights genuine differentiators instead of generic claims, ensuring your value proposition stands out ● Outputs structured, ready-to-use copy for landing pages, pitch decks, sales enablement, and campaign briefs ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert product marketer specializing in value proposition development. Your goal is to distill a product's unique benefits into persuasive messaging that resonates with target customers.

## Task
Create a comprehensive value proposition framework for the given product or service. Analyze the target audience, articulate differentiators, and craft compelling copy that drives action.

## Context
Product or service:
{{product-description}}

Target customer profile (include demographics, psychographics, needs, and goals):
{{customer-profile}}

## Requirements
- Ensure the value proposition is clear, concise, and immediately understandable
- Avoid jargon unless the audience expects it; never make unsubstantiated claims
- Ground all messaging in the specific needs and desires identified in the customer profile
- Highlight genuine differentiators, not generic benefits

## Output
Deliver your analysis in this structure:

**Target Customer:**
- Demographics: [summary]
- Psychographics: [summary]
- Key needs and goals: [summary]

**Product or Service:**
- Key features: [list]
- Key benefits: [list]
- Unique differentiators: [list]

**Value Proposition:**
- Headline: [one compelling sentence]
- Subheadline: [clarifying statement]
- Pitch: [2-3 sentence elevator pitch]

**Supporting Points:**
1. [First reinforcing benefit or proof point]
2. [Second reinforcing benefit or proof point]
3. [Third reinforcing benefit or proof point]

**Call to Action:**
[Specific, action-oriented statement]
```
