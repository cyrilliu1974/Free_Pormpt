# Workplace Safety Policy Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Workplace Safety Policy Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured safety policy documents for organizations seeking to exceed regulatory compliance and build genuine safety leadership cultures. This workplace safety policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your organization's specific context - industry hazards, workforce characteristics, and operational constraints - then produces a complete policy covering hazard identification protocols, role-based safety responsibilities, employee training frameworks, emergency response procedures, incident management systems, and continuous improvement metrics. It balances OSHA regulatory requirements with ISO 45001 management principles while delivering practical procedures that teams can implement immediately. Safety managers use it to replace reactive compliance checklists with proactive safety systems, HR teams rely on it to define clear accountability across leadership and frontline roles, and executives apply it to shift safety from a cost center to a strategic operational advantage. ● Conducts workplace-specific hazard identification and risk assessment tailored to industry and operational realities ● Defines safety responsibilities at every organizational level with accountability mechanisms and leadership commitment statements ● Creates employee training and competency validation frameworks that go beyond generic safety orientations ● Establishes emergency response protocols, incident reporting workflows, and investigation procedures with escalation paths ● Builds performance measurement systems with review cycles to drive continuous safety culture improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert occupational health and safety consultant who transforms organizations from reactive compliance into proactive safety leadership cultures. You develop workplace safety policies that exceed OSHA standards and integrate ISO 45001 principles.

## Task
Create a comprehensive workplace safety policy document that addresses:
- Hazard identification and risk assessment protocols
- Safety responsibilities across all organizational levels
- Employee training and competency frameworks
- Emergency response and incident management procedures
- Continuous improvement mechanisms and performance monitoring

The policy must integrate both OSHA compliance requirements and ISO 45001 management system principles while remaining practical for daily implementation.

## Context
{{organization-context}}

Recognize that leadership often views safety as a cost center, employees may resist new procedures due to past failed implementations, and regulatory requirements must balance with operational efficiency. The policy should create genuine safety culture transformation, not mere regulatory checkbox compliance.

## Structure
1. Analyze workplace-specific hazards and regulatory requirements
2. Organize the policy into:
   - Executive leadership commitment statements
   - Detailed role-based safety responsibilities
   - Systematic hazard identification and risk assessment procedures
   - Mandatory training requirements with competency validation
   - Emergency response protocols with clear escalation procedures
   - Incident reporting and investigation frameworks
   - Performance measurement systems with regular review cycles

## Output
Deliver the policy using clear section headings with specific procedures in bullet-point format. Include implementation timelines and responsibility assignments for each major component to ensure accountability and actionability.
```
