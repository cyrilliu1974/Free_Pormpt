# Data Collection Plan Builder for Educational Research

## 簡介

The Data Collection Plan Builder for Educational Research is a free AI prompt that creates systematic, comprehensive data collection strategies for academic studies in educational settings. This data collection plan prompt for ChatGPT guides researchers through designing rigorous methodologies tailored to their research topic, target population, setting, and available resources. It structures your plan across six critical dimensions: research methods (quantitative, qualitative, or mixed), data sources, collection instruments (surveys, interviews, observations, assessments), sampling and recruitment strategies, ethical safeguards (informed consent, confidentiality, IRB compliance), and timeline logistics. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable bullet-point plans that balance academic rigor with practical constraints. Use it when designing thesis studies, dissertation research, classroom investigations, or institutional program evaluations. ● Tailors research methods and data sources to your specific educational context and population ● Specifies concrete data collection instruments with justification for each choice ● Addresses ethical requirements including consent protocols, privacy measures, and IRB considerations ● Provides timeline sequencing and resource allocation guidance with built-in contingency planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educational researcher specializing in research design and methodology.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, systematic data collection plan for an educational research project. Organize the plan into clear steps that ensure reliability, validity, and ethical integrity.

## Context
Research topic: {{research-topic}}
Target population: {{target-population}}
Research setting and timeframe: {{setting-and-timeframe}}
Available resources: {{available-resources}}

Consider multiple research methods (quantitative, qualitative, mixed), appropriate data sources for the educational context, sampling strategies, data collection instruments, and ethical safeguards including informed consent, confidentiality, and IRB requirements.

## Output
Deliver the data collection plan as a structured bullet-point list organized under these headings:

- **Research Methods** (which approaches and why they fit this study)
- **Data Sources** (where and how data will be obtained)
- **Data Collection Instruments** (surveys, interviews, observations, assessments, etc.)
- **Sampling & Recruitment** (who, how many, selection criteria)
- **Ethical Considerations** (consent, privacy, risk mitigation, compliance)
- **Timeline & Logistics** (sequencing, resource allocation, contingencies)

Ensure each section is actionable and tailored to the specific research context provided.
```
