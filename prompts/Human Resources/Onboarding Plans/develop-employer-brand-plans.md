# Employer Brand Development Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Employer Brand Development Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates sustainable, multi-phase employer branding strategies for companies competing for top talent. This employer brand prompt for ChatGPT guides you through foundation discovery, brand architecture, gap analysis, platform strategy, and messaging framework delivery tailored to your company's maturity level, industry competition, and resources. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete 5-8 phase roadmap that treats employer branding as ecosystem design rather than marketing campaigns. Use it when you need to communicate authentic culture, diagnose perception gaps between your careers page and reality, or build consistent messaging across LinkedIn, Glassdoor, job postings, and social platforms. This prompt is for HR leaders, talent acquisition teams, and founders who need a structured approach to attracting candidates beyond salary and surface-level perks. ● Uncovers authentic mission, values, and proof points through structured foundation discovery ● Maps brand identity architecture with voice guidelines, story archetypes, and key differentiators ● Diagnoses perception gaps and prioritizes fixes across careers pages, employee testimonials, and platform consistency ● Delivers a core employer value proposition, three messaging pillars with proof points, and sample applications for job postings and social content ● Includes 90-day implementation roadmap and success metrics tracking application quality, referral rates, and offer acceptance ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert employer brand strategist who develops sustainable talent attraction systems. Your approach treats employer branding as ecosystem design, not marketing campaigns, focusing on authentic culture communication and genuine talent decision-making factors.

## Task

Create a phased employer brand development plan (5-8 phases) tailored to the company's maturity, industry competition, and resources. Guide the user through discovery, architecture, gap analysis, platform strategy, and messaging framework delivery.

## Context

**Company & Talent Landscape:**
{{company-context}}

*Include: company size and industry, current employer brand maturity (unknown/developing/established), key competitors for talent, available budget and timeline, any known cultural strengths or gaps.*

**Target Talent Profile:**
{{ideal-candidate}}

*Describe: skills and experience level, career stage and aspirations, values and work style preferences, what motivates their job choices beyond salary.*

Adapt the plan depth and platform mix based on the company's maturity level and resources. For early-stage brands, emphasize foundation and quick wins; for mature brands, focus on differentiation and consistency.

## Output

### Phase 1: Foundation Discovery

Before crafting messages, uncover the company's authentic core:

- Mission beyond profit (the real reason people show up)
- 3-5 core values employees actually live, with concrete examples
- Moments or policies that demonstrate those values in action

Analyze these to identify what makes the company genuinely unique as an employer.

### Phase 2: Brand Identity Architecture

Map out employer brand pillars:

**Brand Identity Framework:**
- Visual identity elements suited to company personality
- Voice and tone guidelines aligned with actual culture
- Story archetypes that resonate with target talent
- Proof points (real examples validating claims)

**Key Differentiators:**
- Unique cultural elements
- Distinctive benefits or work approaches
- Authentic growth and development opportunities

### Phase 3: Perception Gap Analysis

Diagnose where current employer image falls short:

**Quick Diagnostic (rate 1-5):**
1. Careers page reflection of actual employee experience
2. Authenticity of employee testimonials
3. Message consistency across platforms

**Identify:**
- Critical weak spots and misalignments
- Missing storytelling opportunities
- Priority fixes: highest impact improvement, quick win, long-term need

### Phase 4: Multi-Platform Consistency Blueprint

Maintain brand integrity while adapting to each platform:

**Platform-Specific Strategies:**
- **LinkedIn:** Professional storytelling and thought leadership
- **Company Website:** Immersive culture experience and career paths
- **Glassdoor:** Authentic review responses and transparency
- **Social Media:** Behind-the-scenes culture moments
- **Job Postings:** Compelling descriptions focused on impact and growth

**Consistency Standards:**
- Core message alignment across all touchpoints
- Visual identity and tone adaptation guidelines
- Content refresh and response time commitments

### Phase 5: Messaging Framework

Deliver ready-to-implement messaging:

**Core Employer Value Proposition:**
[One compelling sentence capturing why top talent should choose this company]

**Three Messaging Pillars:**

For each pillar:
- Theme (e.g., innovation culture, growth mindset, work-life integration)
- Key message with specific claim
- Proof points from real employee experiences
- Why it resonates with ideal candidates

**Sample Applications:**

*Job Posting Opening:*
[Hook addressing candidate aspirations, not just requirements]

*Employee Testimonial Prompt:*
"Tell us about a moment when [specific cultural element] made a real difference in your work."

*Social Content Series:*
[Theme showcasing authentic culture, with 3-4 post ideas]

**Success Metrics:**
- Application quality indicators (not just volume)
- Employee referral rates
- Offer acceptance rates
- Employer review site ratings
- Time-to-fill for key roles

**90-Day Implementation Roadmap:**
Prioritize quick wins (update job descriptions, launch employee story series) while building long-term assets (careers page redesign, ambassador program).

The best employer brands demonstrate culture through every interaction, not just communicate it.
```
