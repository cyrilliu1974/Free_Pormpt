# B2B LinkedIn Growth Automation Plan Builder

## 簡介

The B2B LinkedIn Growth Automation Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured, step-by-step LinkedIn growth strategies for B2B marketing professionals. This LinkedIn growth prompt for ChatGPT delivers a five-component automation plan tailored to your business, target audience, and unique value proposition. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, it produces actionable frameworks for identifying prospects, building connection workflows, scheduling content across formats (articles, polls, carousels, videos), establishing daily engagement routines, and leveraging advanced LinkedIn features like Sales Navigator, newsletters, and hashtag optimization. Use it when you need a complete, customized roadmap for scaling your B2B LinkedIn presence without generic advice. ● Produces prospect identification methods, connection request templates, and audience segmentation criteria specific to your target industry ● Builds weekly content calendars with theme recommendations and format mixes designed for B2B decision-makers ● Defines daily engagement routines, relationship nurturing touchpoints, and community participation guidelines ● Maps out profile optimization tactics, LinkedIn Live strategies, advanced search techniques, and SEO-optimized hashtag approaches ● Specifies KPIs to monitor, measurement tools, and monthly review processes with actionable optimization steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a LinkedIn growth strategist specializing in B2B marketing.

## Task
Create a comprehensive automation plan for LinkedIn growth that drives audience engagement, content sharing, and strategic use of platform features.

## Context
Business and offering: {{business-and-offerings}}
Target audience and industry: {{target-audience}}
Unique value proposition: {{usp}}

## Output
Deliver a structured, step-by-step automation plan organized under these components:

**1. Audience Identification & Connection Strategy**
- Methods to identify ideal prospects
- Connection request approach and messaging
- Segmentation criteria

**2. Content Calendar**
- Weekly posting schedule with content themes
- Mix of formats (articles, polls, carousels, videos)
- Topics tailored to B2B decision-makers

**3. Engagement System**
- Daily engagement routine (commenting, sharing, responding)
- Relationship nurturing touchpoints
- Community participation guidelines

**4. LinkedIn Feature Utilization**
- Profile optimization tactics
- LinkedIn Live, newsletters, or events strategy
- Advanced search and Sales Navigator techniques
- Hashtag and SEO optimization

**5. Tracking & Metrics**
- Key performance indicators to monitor
- Tools and dashboards for measurement
- Monthly review and optimization process

For each component, provide actionable steps, specific examples, and recommended timing. Ensure all messaging is concise, value-focused, and appropriate for professional B2B communication.
```
