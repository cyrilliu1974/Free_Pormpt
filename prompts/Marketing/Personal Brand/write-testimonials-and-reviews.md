# Testimonial and Review Writer for Social Proof

## 簡介

The Testimonial and Review Writer for Social Proof is a free AI prompt that generates authentic, compelling testimonials and reviews for products or services across any industry. This testimonial writing prompt for ChatGPT produces 3-5 varied testimonials that incorporate specific details, personal experiences, and emotional language to build credibility with potential customers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. You provide your product or service, target audience, unique selling points, and desired tone, and the prompt structures each testimonial around a core claim supported by concrete examples. Each testimonial varies in length, perspective, and emphasis to appeal to different audience segments, and concludes with a persuasive recommendation or call-to-action. Use it when you need social proof content that sounds genuine rather than scripted, whether you're launching a new product, building landing pages, or refreshing marketing materials. ● Creates testimonials structured around dependency grammar principles - leading with the main claim, then layering supporting details in logical sequence ● Produces 3-5 distinct testimonials with varied tone, style, and perspective to appeal to different customer segments ● Incorporates emotional language and specific personal experiences that sound genuine rather than generic ● Formats each testimonial with a headline and body text, concluding with a strong recommendation or call-to-action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content marketer specializing in testimonial and review writing that builds social proof and credibility.

## Task
Write compelling, authentic-sounding testimonials that highlight key features and benefits while creating emotional connection with potential customers.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique selling points: {{usp}}

Industry context and desired tone: {{industry-and-tone}}

## Instructions
- Identify the strongest features and benefits from the USP
- Incorporate specific details and personal experiences that feel genuine
- Vary tone and style across testimonials to appeal to different audience segments
- Structure each testimonial with a main claim supported by concrete examples
- Use emotional language to create connection
- Conclude each with a strong recommendation or call-to-action
- Apply dependency grammar principles: lead with the core claim, then layer supporting details in logical sequence

## Output
Provide a numbered list of 3-5 testimonials. Format each as:

**[Headline]**  
[Body text]

Ensure variation in length, perspective, and emphasis across the set.
```
