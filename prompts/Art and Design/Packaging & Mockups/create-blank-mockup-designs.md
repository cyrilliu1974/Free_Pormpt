# Blank Mockup Design Generator for Product Photography

## 簡介

The Blank Mockup Design Generator for Product Photography is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic blank mockup images of devices, packaging, or surfaces in professional studio settings. This blank mockup prompt for Midjourney and other image AI models produces clean, high-resolution product photography with studio lighting and professional composition, perfect for designers who need empty device screens, packaging templates, or surface mockups ready for client presentations or portfolio work. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other text-to-image platforms, letting you specify any device or medium (phone, tablet, book cover, apparel) and any scene (minimalist desk, outdoor lifestyle, storefront display). Reach for this prompt when you need a blank canvas mockup without manually setting up a photo shoot or hunting through stock libraries. ● Produces empty, blank screens or surfaces with photorealistic studio lighting and clean professional framing. ● Accepts any device or medium variable (smartphone, laptop, tote bag, billboard, book) and any scene context (office desk, cafe table, outdoor park bench). ● Outputs high-resolution product photography style images suitable for overlaying designs, branding, or UI screenshots. ● Saves time and cost compared to hiring photographers or purchasing individual mockup templates from stock sites. ## Prompt

```
Mockup of empty, blank {{device-or-medium}} in {{scene}}, photorealistic studio lighting, clean professional composition, high-resolution product photography style
```
