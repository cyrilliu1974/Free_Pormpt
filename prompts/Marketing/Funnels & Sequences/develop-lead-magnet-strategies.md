# Lead Magnet Strategy Planner for Content Marketing

## 簡介

The Lead Magnet Strategy Planner for Content Marketing is a free AI prompt that builds a complete, customized lead generation plan for digital marketers and business owners looking to attract and nurture qualified leads. This lead magnet strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through eight critical components: audience analysis that identifies pain points and interests, selection of optimal lead magnet formats (ebooks, webinars, templates, email courses), a content creation roadmap with resource requirements, compelling value propositions for each offer, distribution and promotional tactics across channels, a phased implementation timeline, success metrics and ROI tracking, and a post-download nurture sequence that guides leads through your sales funnel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and adapts to your specific business context, marketing challenges, and available resources. Reach for this prompt when you need to launch a lead generation program from scratch, refresh underperforming content offers, or align your lead magnets with new business goals. ● Analyzes target audience needs, pain points, and interests to inform content decisions ● Recommends specific lead magnet types and formats matched to your business model and resources ● Delivers a phased implementation timeline with content creation roadmaps and distribution tactics ● Establishes KPIs, ROI measurement frameworks, and post-download nurture sequences ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in lead generation and customer acquisition through high-value content offers.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive lead magnet strategy tailored to attract and nurture potential customers. Create a detailed, actionable plan that identifies optimal lead magnet types, content approaches, distribution channels, and measurement frameworks.

## Context

**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

**Marketing Challenges & Goals:**
{{marketing-challenges-and-goals}}

**Available Resources:**
{{available-resources}}

## Analysis & Strategy

Address each of the following components:

1. **Audience Analysis** – Identify target audience needs, pain points, and interests that lead magnets should address
2. **Lead Magnet Selection** – Recommend the most effective lead magnet types (ebooks, whitepapers, webinars, email courses, templates, etc.) for this specific business and audience
3. **Content Creation Plan** – Develop a production roadmap for each lead magnet type, including format, depth, and resource requirements
4. **Value Propositions** – Outline key topics and unique value for each lead magnet
5. **Distribution & Promotion** – Specify channels, tactics, and promotional strategies for maximum reach
6. **Implementation Timeline** – Create a phased schedule for development, launch, and ongoing management
7. **Success Metrics** – Establish KPIs and ROI measurement frameworks for each lead magnet
8. **Nurture Strategy** – Design the post-download sequence to guide leads through the sales funnel

## Output

Present the strategy with clear headings and subheadings for each section. Make the plan immediately actionable with specific recommendations, timelines, and next steps.
```
