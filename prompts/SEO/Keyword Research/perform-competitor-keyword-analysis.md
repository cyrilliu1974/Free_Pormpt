# Competitor Keyword Analysis Prompt for SEO Strategy

## 簡介

The Competitor Keyword Analysis Prompt for SEO Strategy is a free AI prompt that uncovers the search terms your competitors rank for and identifies keyword gaps you can exploit to improve your organic visibility. This competitor keyword analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by researching the organic and paid keyword strategies of up to several competitors in your industry, evaluating each term for search volume, difficulty, and relevance to your product or service, then delivering an executive summary and a markdown table of the 10-15 highest-priority keywords. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is particularly useful for content strategists planning editorial calendars, SEO specialists auditing competitive landscapes, and marketing teams entering new verticals or geographies. Reach for this prompt when you need to reverse-engineer a competitor's SEO strategy, validate your own keyword targets, or discover untapped search opportunities in your niche. ● Surfaces 10-15 high-priority keywords across multiple competitors, ranked by strategic value and search demand ● Highlights keyword gaps where competitors rank but your business does not, revealing quick-win opportunities ● Delivers an executive summary that translates raw keyword data into immediate targeting recommendations ● Accepts custom inputs for business name, product or service, competitor list, and target audience geography to tailor the analysis ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing strategist specializing in competitive keyword analysis and SEO intelligence.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor keyword analysis to identify high-value search terms used by competitors in the specified industry. Research the top competitors, analyze their keyword strategies for relevance, search volume, and competition level, then organize findings to highlight the most valuable keywords for potential targeting.

## Context
**Business:** {{business-name}}
**Product/Service:** {{product-or-service}}
**Competitive Set:** {{competitors}}
**Target Audience & Geography:** {{audience-and-location}}

## Process
1. Research the provided competitors' organic and paid keyword strategies
2. Gather keyword data focusing on those directly related to the product/service
3. Evaluate each keyword for search volume, difficulty, and relevance to the business
4. Identify gaps and opportunities where competitors rank but the business does not
5. Prioritize keywords by strategic value

## Output
Provide a brief executive summary (3-4 sentences) highlighting:
- The most valuable keyword opportunities discovered
- Notable patterns in competitor keyword strategies
- Immediate recommendations for keyword targeting

Follow with a markdown table:

| Competitor | Keyword | Search Volume |
|------------|---------|---------------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Include 10-15 of the highest-priority keywords across all competitors analyzed.
```
