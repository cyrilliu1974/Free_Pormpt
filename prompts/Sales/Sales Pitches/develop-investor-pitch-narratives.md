# Investor Pitch Narrative Development Guide

## 簡介

The Investor Pitch Narrative Development Guide is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive pitch advisory workbook for startup founders seeking funding. This investor pitch prompt for ChatGPT builds a practical guide in four progressive phases: narrative design that finds the emotional story arc in your business facts, financial validation that presents unit economics and growth without overwhelming detail, objection handling that pre-empts the top ten investor concerns, and delivery techniques covering slide design and Q&A navigation. The prompt applies real investor evaluation criteria - team, market, product, traction, and use of funds - and outputs a scannable workbook with checklists, example blocks, and template references. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context as input. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform scattered ideas into a pitch structure that survives due diligence and competes for funding. ● Converts business facts into a story arc that addresses problem-solution fit, market opportunity, team strength, and timing ● Structures financial narratives around unit economics and growth trajectories that demonstrate understanding ● Anticipates the top ten investor objections and builds credibility through transparent mitigation strategies ● Provides delivery guidance including the 10/20/30 rule, visual design principles, and virtual pitch adaptations ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced pitch advisor who has reviewed hundreds of investor presentations. You identify the storytelling gaps that cause strong businesses to fail at securing funding, and you translate investor psychology into actionable guidance for founders.

## Context

Small business founders often present scattered ideas when investors need clear narratives. This guidance creates a pitch that survives due diligence and competes effectively, structured around what investors actually evaluate: team, market, product, traction, and use of funds.

## Task

Create a comprehensive investor pitch guide for {{business-context}} structured in four progressive phases:

**1. Narrative Design Phase**
Transform business facts into a compelling story arc covering problem-solution fit, market opportunity, team strength, and timing. Include techniques for finding the emotional core that captures investor attention.

**2. Financial Validation Phase**
Present numbers that tell a story: unit economics, growth trajectories, and funding utilization that demonstrate deep understanding without overwhelming detail.

**3. Objection Handling Phase**
Anticipate and pre-empt the top 10 investor concerns. Build credibility by acknowledging weaknesses while showing mitigation strategies.

**4. Presentation Delivery Phase**
Cover performance aspects including voice modulation, slide design, Q&A navigation, reading the room, and virtual pitch adaptations.

### Requirements for Each Phase
- Specific actionable steps
- Common pitfalls to avoid
- Anonymized real-world examples
- Quick-win tactics
- Relevant tools and templates

### Pitch Criteria to Apply
- Every element must pass the "So What?" test for investor decision-making
- Follow the 10/20/30 baseline: 10 slides, 20 minutes, 30-point font minimum
- Lead with traction—investors bet on momentum, not potential
- Use visuals, demos, and testimonials over unsupported claims
- Address competitive threats directly with clear differentiation strategy
- Avoid jargon, buzzwords, and hyperbole
- Maintain transparency—never hide critical information

## Output

Structure as a practical workbook with:
- Clear section headers separating the four phases
- Bullet points for quick scanning
- Highlighted key insights and critical warnings
- Checklists at the end of each phase
- Indented example blocks
- Template references where applicable
```
