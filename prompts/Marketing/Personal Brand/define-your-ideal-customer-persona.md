# Ideal Customer Persona Creator for Marketers

## 簡介

The Ideal Customer Persona Creator for Marketers is a free AI prompt that builds a detailed customer persona to guide positioning, campaigns, and messaging for entrepreneurs and marketers. This customer persona prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured profile including a realistic name, role, core problem, pains, jobs-to-be-done, motivation, trigger event, barriers to change, and context about existing knowledge. Instead of vague demographic data, you get marketing-actionable insights: the specific circumstances that push your customer to seek a solution, the obstacles that keep them from acting, and the emotional drivers behind their decision-making. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Marketers use it to anchor content strategy, refine ad targeting, write conversion-focused copy, and align product positioning with real customer needs. Reach for this prompt when launching a new offer, refining messaging that isn't converting, or building a personal brand that needs a clear audience anchor. ● Outputs a single, detailed persona with name, role, problem, pains, jobs-to-be-done, motivation, trigger, barriers, and context ● Focuses on marketing-actionable insights rather than generic demographics ● Identifies the exact trigger event that drives a prospect to seek a solution now ● Maps barriers and existing knowledge to inform objection-handling and messaging strategy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer specializing in target audience research and persona development for entrepreneurs.

## Task
Create a detailed customer persona that will serve as the foundation for positioning, marketing campaigns, and marketing copy.

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

## Output
Deliver one specific, actionable persona using this structure:

### Name
A realistic name that reflects the segment.

### Role
The specific segment of the target audience they represent.

### Problem
The core issue they want to solve.

### Pains
The negative consequences of this problem that drive urgency.

### Jobs-To-Be-Done
What the persona wants to achieve or accomplish.

### Motivation
What drives them to complete the jobs-to-be-done.

### Trigger
The event or circumstance that pushed them to seek a solution now.

### Barriers
What prevents them from changing the status quo.

### Context
What they already know about the problem and alternative solutions.

## Criteria
- Be specific: describe one ideal customer in detail, avoid generic statements
- Focus on marketing-actionable insights
- Use concise, simple sentences for clarity
```
