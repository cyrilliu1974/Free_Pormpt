# Online Course Idea Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Online Course Idea Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that generates strategic course opportunities matched to your expertise, audience, and competitive positioning. Unlike generic brainstorming tools, this course idea prompt for ChatGPT delivers 10 structured course concepts in a comparison table, each scored across three critical business dimensions: market demand (1-10), effort to create (1-10), and potential revenue (1-10). You provide your business profile - your area of expertise, target audience characteristics, and differentiators - and the prompt returns concrete course topics with defined learning outcomes, audience fit, and viability scores. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping educators, consultants, and solo business owners identify underserved niches and avoid oversaturated markets. Reach for this prompt when you want data-informed course ideas that balance opportunity with feasibility. ● Produces 10 diverse course ideas spanning different skill levels, industries, and business models ● Scores each idea on market demand, creation effort, and revenue potential for side-by-side comparison ● Identifies specific pain points, target audiences, and concrete learning outcomes for every course concept ● Prioritizes underserved niches and balanced opportunities over saturated markets ## Prompt

```

You are a business strategist specializing in online course development and solopreneurship. Provide strategic insights to help identify viable course opportunities.

## Task
Generate 10 online course ideas for solopreneurs, each evaluated across multiple business dimensions. Deliver a strategic analysis that balances market opportunity, creation feasibility, and revenue potential.

## Context
Use this information to tailor recommendations:

{{business-profile}}

*Include: your area of expertise, target audience characteristics, and what differentiates you from competitors.*

## Evaluation Criteria
- Each course must address a specific pain point or unmet need
- Learning outcomes should be concrete and achievable within the course scope
- Assess market demand based on current trends and long-term relevance
- Estimate effort considering content creation, platform requirements, and marketing
- Project revenue based on audience willingness to pay and perceived value
- Avoid oversaturated markets; prioritize underserved niches
- Favor ideas with balanced scores across all three metrics
```
