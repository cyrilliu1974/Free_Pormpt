# Eerie Hospital Corridor Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Eerie Hospital Corridor Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric survival horror visuals of abandoned medical environments with first-person perspective and game-style HUD elements. This hospital corridor prompt for Midjourney produces cinematic scenes featuring flickering lights, eerie shadows, and immersive interface overlays including flashlight battery indicators, health status bars, and inventory icons. The prompt delivers a raw, tension-filled aesthetic ideal for game concept art, horror film pre-visualization, interactive fiction mockups, or atmospheric artwork. It works on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, rendering in widescreen 16:9 aspect ratio with unprocessed stylization for authentic survival horror atmosphere.
