# Value Proposition Statement Generator

## 簡介

The Value Proposition Statement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, audience-focused value propositions for marketers, founders, and brand strategists. This value proposition prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product or service, target audience, unique selling points, and brand voice to produce a concise 1-2 sentence statement that clearly articulates what makes your offering valuable and distinct. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering both the final statement and a brief strategic explanation of how it addresses core audience needs and pain points. Use it when launching new products, refining messaging for campaigns, or aligning teams around a clear market position. ● Produces a concise, memorable value proposition statement tailored to your specific target audience ● Analyzes how unique selling points create competitive advantages and resonate with customer needs ● Ensures alignment with your brand voice while maintaining clarity and persuasive impact ● Includes a strategic explanation that connects the statement to audience pain points and desires ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in value proposition development.

## Task
Create a concise, compelling value proposition statement that clearly communicates the unique benefits and value of the offering to potential customers.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-or-service}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Unique selling points: {{unique-selling-points}}
Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Analyze the target audience's core needs, pain points, and desires. Identify how the unique selling points create competitive advantages. Structure your writing for maximum clarity and coherence, emphasizing specific benefits that resonate with the target audience. Ensure the statement is memorable, persuasive, and aligned with the brand voice.

## Output
Deliver:
1. A single, polished value proposition statement (1-2 sentences maximum)
2. A brief explanation (3-4 sentences) of how the statement addresses the target audience's needs and communicates the offering's unique value
```
