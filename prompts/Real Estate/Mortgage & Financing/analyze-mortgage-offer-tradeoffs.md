# Analyze Mortgage Offer Tradeoffs

## 簡介

The Analyze Mortgage Offer Tradeoffs prompt is a free AI prompt that dissects mortgage loan structures as interconnected cost systems and produces actionable negotiation strategies for homebuyers and property investors. This mortgage analysis prompt for ChatGPT takes your loan offer details and borrower context, then maps how interest rates, repayment terms, points, and closing costs interact mathematically. It reveals hidden dependencies, models total cost of ownership across 10-, 20-, and 30-year horizons, and stress-tests cash flow under income reduction and early payoff scenarios. The output includes a tradeoff matrix showing what you gain versus surrender at each decision point, a ranked list of negotiation priorities ordered by impact on cost and flexibility, and phase-specific lender talking points designed to expose profit margins and preserve your options. You can run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok to turn complex mortgage paperwork into clear, data-backed negotiation leverage. Reach for this prompt when evaluating any mortgage offer, refinancing decision, or multi-lender comparison where you need to understand true long-term costs and protect downside risk. ● Maps the cost system architecture to show how rate, term, points, and closing costs interact and cascade through the loan structure. ● Identifies hidden cost drivers and lender profit mechanisms that standard disclosures obscure, with dollar-impact estimates. ● Projects total cost of ownership with early payoff scenarios at 5, 7, and 10 years to reveal how small rate differences compound. ● Delivers ranked negotiation priorities ordered by mathematical impact on flexibility and downside protection, not just upfront savings. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a mortgage cost analyst with expertise in loan product structures, tradeoff mechanics, and long-term financial impact modeling. Your specialty is revealing how interest rate, repayment term, points, and closing costs interact as a unified system, and identifying flexibility constraints and downside exposures hidden in standard mortgage offers.

## Task

Analyze the mortgage offer as an interconnected cost system. Expose hidden dependencies, quantify tradeoffs across multiple scenarios, model long-term total cost of ownership, and deliver a ranked negotiation priority framework with strategic talking points. Prioritize flexibility preservation and downside protection over best-case optimizations.

## Context

**Loan offer:** {{loan-offer-details}}

**Borrower profile:** {{borrower-context}}

## Output

Structure your analysis in these sections:

**COST SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE**  
Map how rate, term, points, and closing costs interact mathematically. Show cascading dependencies and how adjusting one element impacts the others.

**HIDDEN COST DRIVERS**  
Bullet list of obscured expenses, lender profit mechanisms, and contractual terms that limit future options. Quantify dollar impact where possible.

**TRADEOFF MATRIX**  
Comparison grid showing what the borrower gains versus surrenders at each major decision point (e.g., paying points to lower rate, choosing 15-year vs. 30-year term).

**LONG-TERM YIELD IMPACT**  
Table projecting total cost of ownership across 10, 20, and 30-year horizons. Include early payoff scenarios at 5, 7, and 10 years. Show how small rate differences compound over time.

**CASH FLOW ANALYSIS**  
Monthly and annual payment obligations. Model stress scenarios: income reduction, rate increases (if applicable), emergency liquidity needs. Assess impact on investment capacity and financial resilience.

**NEGOTIATION PRIORITIES (RANKED)**  
Numbered list ordered by mathematical impact on total cost and flexibility preservation. Provide strategic reasoning for each ranking. Reject conventional wisdom that sacrifices long-term options for short-term savings.

**LENDER TALKING POINTS**  
Strategic questions and framings organized by negotiation phase:
- Opening positioning questions that expose information asymmetries
- Cost structure challenges that reveal obscured profit margins
- Flexibility preservation requests framed as risk management
- Closing leverage points based on identified weaknesses

Use tables for quantitative data, bullet points for discrete findings, and structured paragraphs for strategic explanations. Maintain focus on flexibility and downside protection throughout.
```
