# Video Title Generator for Click-Through & Engagement

## 簡介

The Video Title Generator for Click-Through & Engagement is a free AI prompt that creates high-performing video titles tailored to your content and audience. This video title prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your video topic and niche to produce 8–10 optimized title options, each paired with a strategic rationale explaining its engagement mechanism - whether curiosity gaps, urgency cues, specificity (numbers and timeframes), or transformation promises. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and applies proven content-strategist techniques to maximize click-through rates while staying within the 55–70 character sweet spot for platform display. Use it when launching YouTube videos, social shorts, or any video content where the title directly impacts discoverability and viewer retention. ● Produces 8–10 title variants per request, each with a one-sentence engagement rationale ● Incorporates power words, specificity, curiosity triggers, and social proof tailored to your niche ● Keeps titles within 55–70 characters for optimal display on YouTube and social platforms ● Analyzes emotional drivers and key takeaways to align titles with audience expectations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video content strategist specializing in high-engagement title creation.

## Task
Generate 8–10 compelling video title options that maximize click-through rate and viewer engagement for the given content. Each title should use proven engagement techniques: power words, specificity (numbers, timeframes), curiosity gaps, or clear value promises.

## Context
Video content: {{video-topic}}
Audience and niche: {{audience-and-niche}}

Analyze the core hook, key takeaways, and emotional drivers in the content. Tailor vocabulary and framing to the specified audience while staying true to the niche.

## Output
Return a numbered list. For each title, include:
1. The complete title (55–70 characters ideal for platform display)
2. A one-sentence rationale explaining its engagement mechanism (curiosity trigger, social proof, urgency, transformation promise, etc.)

Example format:
1. **Title here** – Rationale explaining why this works for the audience.
```
