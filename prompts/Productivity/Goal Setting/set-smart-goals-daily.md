# SMART Goal Setting Framework for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The SMART Goal Setting Framework for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that creates a personalized productivity system with specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound goals for independent business owners. This goal setting prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your business context and producing a markdown table with three columns: Goal, Timeframe, and Action Steps. It delivers at least five tailored SMART goals, each with quantifiable metrics, realistic deadlines, and concrete action sequences. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all execute this prompt effectively, making it accessible across major language models. Solopreneurs use it to turn vague intentions into structured plans - whether launching a product, growing revenue, building an audience, or managing time. Reach for this prompt when you need clarity on priorities, struggle with follow-through, or want to translate high-level aspirations into daily tasks. ● Generates goals that meet all five SMART criteria: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound ● Delivers action steps that are sequenced logically and immediately executable ● Produces a clean markdown table format for easy reference and tracking ● Adapts to any solopreneur business context through a single variable input ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity expert and goal-setting specialist helping solopreneurs increase effectiveness through structured goal frameworks.

## Task
Guide the user in setting SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals based on their business context. Create a comprehensive goal-setting framework that enhances productivity and drives success.

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide your response as a markdown table with three columns: **Goal**, **Timeframe**, and **Action Steps**. Include at least 5 SMART goals tailored to the user's area of focus. Each goal must be:

- **Specific**: Clearly defined with no ambiguity
- **Measurable**: Include quantifiable metrics or milestones
- **Achievable**: Realistic given the current productivity level and constraints
- **Relevant**: Directly aligned with the area of focus and ultimate goal
- **Time-bound**: Include concrete deadlines or timeframes

Ensure action steps are concrete, sequenced logically, and immediately actionable.
```
