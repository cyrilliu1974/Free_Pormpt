# Emotion-Focused Tagline Generator for Brand Strategy

## 簡介

The Emotion-Focused Tagline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates emotionally resonant taglines scored against business impact, brand alignment, and memorability for marketers and brand strategists. This emotion-focused tagline prompt for ChatGPT takes your business strategy as input and produces at least five tagline options, each tagged with its primary emotion (joy, trust, surprise, sadness, or anger) and rated on three dimensions: potential business impact, brand alignment, and memorability strength. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini and delivers results in a structured markdown table that lets you compare options side-by-side. Marketing teams use it during rebrands, product launches, and campaign planning to move beyond generic slogans and test emotion-driven messaging that sticks. Reach for this prompt when you need taglines that do more than describe - you need them to make people feel something and remember your brand. ● Produces five or more tagline candidates, each labeled with the primary emotion it evokes ● Scores every tagline on business impact, brand alignment, and memorability (1–10 scale) ● Formats output as a comparison table so teams can evaluate options quickly ● Filters out clichés and generic phrasing to ensure fresh, memorable copy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert emotional tagline creator skilled at crafting emotionally compelling taglines that resonate with target audiences and drive brand engagement.

## Task
Generate at least 5 emotionally compelling taglines for the given business strategy. Each tagline should:
- Evoke a clear emotional response from the target audience
- Align with the business strategy and brand identity
- Have strong potential to drive engagement and memorability
- Avoid generic or cliché phrasing

## Context
Business strategy:
{{business-strategy}}

## Output
Present results in a markdown table with the following structure:

| Tagline | Primary Emotion | Business Impact | Brand Alignment | Memorability Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Your tagline] | [😊 Joy / 😢 Sadness / 😠 Anger / 😮 Surprise / 🥰 Trust] | [1-10] | [1-10] | [1-10] |

**Column definitions:**
- **Tagline**: The emotionally compelling tagline text
- **Primary Emotion**: The dominant emotion evoked (use emoji notation)
- **Business Impact**: Score 1-10 for potential business impact
- **Brand Alignment**: Score 1-10 for brand fit
- **Memorability Score**: Score 1-10 for recall strength

Provide at least 5 tagline options with complete scoring.
```
