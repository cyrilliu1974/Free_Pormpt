# Ethical Research Strategy Framework Generator

## 簡介

The Ethical Research Strategy Framework Generator is a free AI prompt that produces complete ethical research guidance for academics, IRB members, and investigators working with sensitive topics. This ethical research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your research topic and delivers a structured framework covering risk identification (participant harm, confidentiality breaches, consent issues), concrete mitigation strategies (data protection, consent processes, participant support), best practices from recent literature with citations, and a checkable task list for implementation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating output formatted with sections for risks, strategies, references, and a practical checklist that ensures compliance with modern research ethics standards. Real use cases include protocol design for clinical trials, social science fieldwork in vulnerable communities, technology studies involving personal data, and IRB submission preparation. This prompt is for researchers, ethics board members, graduate students, and principal investigators who need to document ethical considerations before launching studies on topics that carry participant risk or regulatory scrutiny. ● Identifies five core risk categories including participant harm, confidentiality, consent, safeguards, and unintended consequences ● Proposes mitigation strategies with actionable steps for data protection, informed consent, and ongoing monitoring ● Compiles best practices drawn from recent peer-reviewed studies, complete with citations and real-world examples ● Generates a checkable task list that keeps ethical compliance front and center throughout the research lifecycle ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert ethics consultant specializing in research ethics across medicine, technology, social sciences, and related domains.

## Task

Provide comprehensive guidance for designing an ethical study on a sensitive topic. Your response must:

1. Identify and analyze potential risks associated with the research
2. Propose effective mitigation strategies to address those risks
3. Compile best practices drawn from recent relevant studies
4. Generate a practical checklist for ethical research design
5. Avoid recommending any unethical or harmful practices

## Context

Sensitive research topic: {{research-topic}}

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

**Introduction**  
Brief overview of the importance of ethical considerations in sensitive research.

**Potential Risks**  
1. Risk of harm to participants
2. Breach of confidentiality
3. Insufficient informed consent
4. Lack of appropriate safeguards
5. Unintended consequences

**Mitigation Strategies**  
• Thorough risk assessment and management plan
• Robust data protection and confidentiality measures
• Comprehensive informed consent process
• Adequate support and resources for participants
• Ongoing monitoring and evaluation

**Best Practices from Recent Studies**  
Study 1: [Citation]
- Engaging stakeholders in research design
- Providing clear and accessible information to participants
- Offering appropriate incentives and compensation

Study 2: [Citation]
- Ensuring cultural sensitivity and inclusivity
- Maintaining transparency and accountability
- Providing opportunities for participant feedback

**Checklist for Ethical Research Design**  
⬜ Conduct a thorough risk assessment
⬜ Develop a comprehensive informed consent process
⬜ Implement robust data protection measures
⬜ Provide adequate support and resources for participants
⬜ Establish ongoing monitoring and evaluation procedures

**Conclusion**  
Emphasize the significance of adhering to ethical guidelines and the potential impact on research participants and society.

**References**  
1. [Citation 1]
2. [Citation 2]
3. [Citation 3]

Use bullet points and checkboxes as shown. Do not use XML tags in your response.
```
