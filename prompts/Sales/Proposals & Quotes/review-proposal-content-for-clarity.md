# Proposal Content Review for Clarity and Persuasiveness

## 簡介

The Proposal Content Review for Clarity and Persuasiveness is a free AI prompt that critically evaluates business proposals and delivers structured, actionable feedback to strengthen client acquisition efforts. This proposal review prompt for ChatGPT examines five core components of any business proposal: the executive summary's impact and conciseness, the solution overview's relevance to client pain points and competitive differentiation, the pricing structure's market competitiveness and value justification, the timeline's feasibility and alignment with client expectations, and the overall tailoring to client-specific needs. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured review with clear headings, bullet-pointed observations of strengths and weaknesses, concrete recommendations, and prioritized enhancements. Use it before submitting proposals to clients, when revising lost bids, or when training teams on proposal best practices. ● Evaluates executive summary for immediate impact and clear value proposition ● Assesses solution descriptions for relevance to client pain points and competitive differentiation ● Reviews pricing for market competitiveness, ROI clarity, and value justification ● Examines timelines for feasibility and alignment with client expectations ● Identifies priority improvements that will have the greatest impact on proposal success ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert proposal reviewer specializing in business development and client acquisition.

## Task
Critically analyze the provided proposal for clarity, persuasiveness, and alignment with client needs. Deliver a comprehensive review with actionable recommendations to strengthen the proposal's effectiveness.

## Context
Proposal: {{proposal}}

Client & Context: {{client-and-context}}
(Include client name, industry, main pain points, competitive landscape, and your key differentiators)

## Analysis Framework
Evaluate each proposal component:

**Executive Summary**
- Conciseness and immediate impact
- Clear value proposition

**Solution Overview**
- Clarity and relevance to client pain points
- Differentiation from competitors
- Connection to your unique strengths

**Pricing Structure**
- Competitiveness within the market
- Value justification and ROI clarity

**Timeline**
- Feasibility and realism
- Alignment with client expectations

**Overall Tailoring**
- Client-specific customization
- Language and tone appropriateness

## Output
Structure your review with clear headings for each section (Executive Summary, Solution Overview, Pricing, Timeline, Overall Assessment). Use bullet points for:
- Specific observations (strengths and weaknesses)
- Concrete recommendations for improvement
- Priority enhancements that will have the greatest impact on proposal success
```
