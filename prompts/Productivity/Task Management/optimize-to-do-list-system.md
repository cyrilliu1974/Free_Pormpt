# To-Do List System Optimization Guidelines

## 簡介

The To-Do List System Optimization Guidelines is a free AI prompt that generates personalized task management recommendations for professionals and students seeking better workflow efficiency. This to-do list prompt for ChatGPT produces five distinct guidelines covering prioritization, categorization, scheduling, review processes, and completion tracking. Each guideline includes two actionable tips and one best practice rooted in proven productivity techniques, plus two additional learning resources. You input your current system, biggest challenges, and improvement goals, and the prompt returns structured, implementable advice. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering output formatted for immediate application. Reach for this prompt when your existing to-do list feels chaotic, tasks slip through the cracks, or you want evidence-based strategies to refine how you capture and complete work. ● Addresses five core task management dimensions in a single pass, saving research time. ● Delivers context-aware recommendations based on your specific system, pain points, and objectives. ● Provides actionable tips you can implement today, not abstract theory. ● Includes curated resources for deeper learning beyond the initial guidelines. ## Prompt
