# Legal Business Requirements Research Prompt

## 簡介

The Legal Business Requirements Research Prompt is a free AI prompt that compiles jurisdiction-specific legal requirements, licenses, permits, and regulatory steps for entrepreneurs starting a business in any location. This legal research prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured breakdown of business formation requirements, including registration procedures, tax obligations, employer compliance, and licensing needs. For each requirement, the AI delivers the description, step-by-step fulfillment process, expected timeline, associated costs, and authoritative source citations. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by instructing the model to act as an expert legal researcher who synthesizes official government and legal sources into actionable checklists. Entrepreneurs use it to navigate city, state, and federal compliance when launching LLCs, corporations, sole proprietorships, or partnerships in unfamiliar jurisdictions. This prompt is for startup founders, small business owners, legal assistants, and consultants who need accurate, location-specific compliance information without manually sifting through government websites. ● Compiles business registration, tax ID, permits, licenses, and employer obligations in one structured output ● Cites authoritative government and legal sources for every requirement, ensuring verifiable accuracy ● Includes timelines and cost estimates so founders can budget time and money realistically ● Covers multi-level compliance across municipal, state, and federal jurisdictions ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert legal researcher and business advisor specializing in identifying legal requirements for starting businesses across different jurisdictions.

## Task

Research and compile a comprehensive list of all legal requirements and steps necessary for starting a business in {{location}}. Cite reputable sources for each requirement and organize the information in a clear, structured format.

## Output

For each legal requirement, provide:

**1. [Requirement Name]**
- **Description:** What this requirement is and why it's needed
- **Process:** Step-by-step procedure to fulfill it
- **Timeline:** Expected duration to complete
- **Cost:** Associated fees or expenses
- **Source:** URL to the authoritative government or legal source

**2. [Next Requirement]**
[Same structure...]

Continue for all applicable requirements (business registration, licenses, permits, tax registrations, employer obligations, etc.).

**Additional Resources:**

- [Resource name]: [URL]
- [Resource name]: [URL]

**Disclaimer:**
This information is provided for general guidance only and should not be considered legal advice. Consult with a qualified attorney and relevant government agencies for specific advice pertaining to your unique situation.
```
