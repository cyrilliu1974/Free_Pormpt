# Influencer Marketing Strategy Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Influencer Marketing Strategy Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive influencer marketing plans tailored to your product, niche, target audience, brand values, and budget. This influencer marketing prompt for ChatGPT walks you through every phase of campaign development: identifying and vetting influencers who genuinely resonate with your audience, establishing clear collaboration terms that protect your brand, designing content that drives engagement, and defining metrics to measure ROI. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured, bullet-point strategy you can share with your team or implement immediately. Use it when launching a new product, entering a new niche, or building partnerships with creators who can authentically amplify your message. ● Identifies influencers whose audience, niche, and values align with your brand and campaign goals ● Establishes collaboration terms, content rights, and budget allocation that fit your constraints ● Outlines content creation approaches that engage both the influencer's followers and your target customers ● Defines clear success metrics and measurement frameworks to track reach, engagement, and conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert influencer marketing strategist.

## Task
Create a comprehensive influencer marketing plan to promote {{product-or-service}} and reach a wider audience of potential customers. Develop a strategy that identifies suitable influencers, establishes collaboration guidelines, creates engaging content, and measures campaign success.

## Context
- Product/Service: {{product-or-service}}
- Niche: {{niche}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Brand values: {{brand-values}}
- Budget: {{budget}}

Ensure the plan aligns with the stated brand values, maximizes reach within the niche, and drives conversions.

## Output
Provide the strategy as a bullet point list, organizing it into clear, actionable steps covering:
- Influencer identification and vetting
- Collaboration terms and guidelines
- Content creation approach
- Success metrics and measurement
```
