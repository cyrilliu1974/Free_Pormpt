# Surreal Meditation Portal Image Generator

## 簡介

The Surreal Meditation Portal Image Generator is a free AI prompt that creates minimalist photographs of meditative subjects centered in portals leading to otherworldly landscapes, set against white desert backdrops. The prompt produces symmetrical compositions with rolling dunes, full moon at golden hour, circular geometric motifs, and natural elements like scattered stones and dried branches in soft cream and white tones. This meditation image prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana uses 85mm focal length and shallow depth of field to create dreamlike visuals with expansive negative space. Concept artists, meditation app developers, wellness brands, and graphic designers reach for this prompt when they need ethereal portal imagery that blends surrealism with tranquil minimalism. ● Customizable subject and portal destination variables let you pair any meditative figure with any otherworldly realm. ● White desert setting with rolling dunes, full moon, and golden hour lighting creates consistent ethereal atmosphere. ● Circular geometric motifs and natural scattered elements (stones, branches) add surreal detail without cluttering the composition. ● Centered symmetrical framing with expansive negative space and shallow depth of field delivers gallery-quality minimalist aesthetic. ## Prompt
