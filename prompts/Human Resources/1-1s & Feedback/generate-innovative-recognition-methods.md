# Employee Recognition Methods Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Recognition Methods Generator is a free AI prompt that produces non-monetary recognition strategies designed to boost intrinsic motivation and psychological satisfaction for managers and HR professionals. This employee recognition prompt for ChatGPT applies organizational psychology principles to generate 10 practical recognition methods tailored to your workplace context. Each method explains how it addresses autonomy, competence, or relatedness - the three pillars of intrinsic motivation - and includes implementation guidance. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when designing recognition programs, preparing for one-on-ones, or rethinking how your organization celebrates achievements beyond bonuses and gift cards. ● Produces 10 recognition methods with clear headings, psychological rationale, and implementation steps specific to your organizational context. ● Goes beyond monetary rewards to focus on empowerment, skill acknowledgment, and community connection. ● Grounds every method in intrinsic motivation theory - autonomy, competence, and relatedness - for lasting emotional impact. ● Adapts to any organization size or culture by accepting a single organizational-context variable. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in employee engagement and intrinsic motivation through autonomy, competence, and relatedness.

## Task
Generate 10 innovative employee recognition methods that go beyond monetary compensation and create lasting psychological satisfaction. Each method must be practical, scalable, and designed for genuine emotional impact.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

Focus on recognition approaches that:
- Empower employees to make meaningful choices (autonomy)
- Acknowledge skill progression and developing expertise (competence)
- Strengthen community and connection to organizational mission (relatedness)

## Output
Deliver a numbered list. For each recognition method, provide:
1. **Clear heading** naming the method
2. Brief explanation of how it addresses autonomy, competence, and/or relatedness
3. Implementation guidance suited to the organizational context above
```
