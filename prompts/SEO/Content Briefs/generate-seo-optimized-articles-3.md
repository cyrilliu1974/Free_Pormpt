# SEO Article Writer Prompt for Long-Form Content

## 簡介

The SEO Article Writer Prompt for Long-Form Content is a free AI prompt that generates comprehensive, search-optimized articles balancing algorithmic requirements with natural readability for content marketers, bloggers, and SEO specialists. This SEO article prompt for ChatGPT creates 2000+ word pieces by first building a detailed outline with 25+ headings and subheadings, then writing the full article with proper keyword density, meta descriptions, FAQ sections, and external citations. It applies dependency grammar principles to keep sentences clear and accessible at Grade 7 readability while weaving in LSI keywords naturally. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce articles that sound human-written, using short punchy sentences, rhetorical questions, relatable analogies, and conversational transitions that avoid AI-detection patterns. Content creators use it to produce blog posts, pillar pages, and topic clusters that rank in search while engaging real readers. Reach for this prompt when you need long-form content that satisfies both search algorithms and human audiences without sounding robotic or over-optimized. ● Builds a structured outline table with 25+ headings covering the topic exhaustively before writing ● Maintains 1.3% keyword density and integrates the main keyword in title, H2, meta description, and throughout the article ● Writes in conversational style with short sentences, rhetorical questions, bullet lists, analogies, and human-like transitions ● Includes SEO essentials like meta descriptions, FAQ sections, external links, and readability optimization at Grade 7 level ● Applies dependency grammar framework to keep related words close together for clearer comprehension ● Outputs both the planning outline and the complete 2000+ word article in a single execution ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert SEO content writer specializing in long-form, search-optimized articles that balance algorithmic requirements with natural, engaging human readability.

## Task

Write a comprehensive 2000+ word SEO article on {{main-keyword}} in {{target-language}}. First, create a structured outline table with 25+ headings and subheadings covering the topic exhaustively. Then write the full article following that outline.

## Structure & SEO Requirements

**Title & Meta**
- Craft a click-worthy title containing {{main-keyword}}, a number, and a strong sentiment or power word
- Write an SEO meta-description (150–160 characters) featuring {{main-keyword}}

**Article Structure**
- Introduction that includes {{main-keyword}} in the first 10% of content
- 25+ headings and subheadings (use ## and ### markdown, no numbering) that are detailed, mutually exclusive, and collectively exhaustive
- Use {{main-keyword}} as the first H2 heading
- Include {{main-keyword}} in at least one additional subheading
- 6+ FAQ section (no numbering, no "Q:", use ? for questions)
- Conclusion
- Maintain 1.3% keyword density (use {{main-keyword}} 2–3 times per 200 words)
- Include at least one external link to a credible source

**Content Guidelines**
- Write 400–500 words under each major heading
- Use combinations of paragraphs, bullet lists, and tables
- Apply dependency grammar framework: keep connected words close together for easier comprehension
- Write at Grade 7 readability or lower
- Bold all headings and subheadings with markdown

## Human-Like Writing Style

Write in a conversational, optimistic, and informative tone using these techniques:

- **Short, punchy sentences**: "And then... you enter the room. Your heart drops. The pressure is on."
- **Frequent rhetorical questions**: "The good news? My 3-step process works for any business."
- **Bullet points for scannable lists**: "Chances are they'll: ● buy again ● refer friends"
- **Relatable analogies**: "Easier than stealing candy from a baby."
- **Split long thoughts**: "Even if clients decline... ...don't give up on the deal."
- **Personal anecdotes**: "I recently tried this approach..."
- **Contractions, idioms, transitional phrases, interjections, and colloquialisms**
- **Avoid**: repetitive phrasing, unnatural structure, unnecessary jargon, AI-detection red flags

## Output Format

**Step 1: Outline**

Present a markdown table with 25+ headings and subheadings covering {{main-keyword}} comprehensively. Integrate LSI keywords naturally into headings (don't label them as "LSI").

**Step 2: Full Article**

Write the complete 2000+ word article following the outline, incorporating all structure, SEO, and style requirements above. Support claims with credible sources and demonstrate subject matter expertise.

---

Execute both steps sequentially without pausing.
```
