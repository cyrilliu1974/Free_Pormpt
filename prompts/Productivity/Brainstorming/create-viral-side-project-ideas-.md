# Viral Side-Project Marketing Ideas Generator

## 簡介

The Viral Side-Project Marketing Ideas Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic side-project concepts to drive organic traffic and qualified leads for product marketers and founders. This side-project brainstorming prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business and produces 10 complete marketing ideas, each including a project name, value proposition, no-code implementation path, and conversion strategy. Every concept is designed to be built within a month using tools like Notion, Airtable, or Carrd, offered free to users, and optimized for viral sharing on Twitter, Reddit, HackerNews, or TikTok. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by framing the AI as a growth marketing strategist who understands lead magnets, organic demand generation, and the psychology of shareable content. Reach for this prompt when you need creative acquisition channels beyond paid ads or want to build awareness through value-first projects that naturally funnel users toward your paid offering. ● Produces 10 structured ideas with project format, core value proposition, no-code build strategy, and conversion mechanism ● Filters for viral potential on specific platforms and realistic one-month build timelines ● Identifies how each free project naturally bridges to your paid product without hard selling ● Outputs clean Markdown formatting with numbered entries and bold subheadings for easy scanning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist specializing in organic growth through side-projects and lead magnets that drive traffic to paid products.

## Task
Generate 10 side-project marketing ideas that will attract the target audience and create organic demand for the main product.

## Context
Business and product overview:
{{business-and-product}}

Each idea must meet these criteria:
- Buildable within one month using no-code tools or content creation
- Free for end users
- High viral potential on platforms like Twitter, Reddit, HackerNews, or TikTok
- Naturally leads users toward the paid product and generates qualified leads

## Output
For each of the 10 ideas, provide:
- **Project name and format** (tool/calculator/guide/database/etc.)
- **Core value proposition** – what problem it solves and why users will share it
- **Implementation path** – specific no-code tools or content approach
- **Conversion bridge** – how it organically connects to the paid product

Format your response in clean Markdown with numbered ideas and bold subheadings.
```
