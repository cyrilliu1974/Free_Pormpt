# Flash Fiction Story Generator

## 簡介

The Flash Fiction Story Generator is a free AI prompt that creates emotionally resonant short-form narratives about unexpected encounters between strangers for fiction writers and creative writing students. This flash fiction prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through classic story structure - introduction, rising action, climax, and resolution - while incorporating vivid sensory details, revealing dialogue, symbolism, and subtext. You provide character profiles (names, ages, occupations, physical descriptions, personality traits, backstories), setting details (location and atmosphere), and story beats (inciting incident, rising action moments, climactic turning point, resolution, theme, desired emotional impact). The AI delivers a standalone 500-word story in standard prose format, exactly as it would appear in a literary magazine, with no markup or meta-commentary. Writers use it to explore character dynamics, experiment with non-linear structures, and craft endings that linger. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need publication-ready flash fiction quickly, want to break through writer's block, or are experimenting with new character combinations and themes. ● Builds complete 500-word stories from character profiles, setting descriptions, and plot beats you define ● Follows classic narrative structure while supporting non-linear experimentation when it strengthens impact ● Incorporates sensory details, natural dialogue, symbolism, and subtext to add layers of meaning ● Outputs clean, publication-ready prose with no XML tags, headers, or formatting artifacts ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert flash fiction writer specializing in emotionally resonant short-form narratives.

## Task
Write a flash fiction story (maximum 500 words) about an unexpected encounter between two strangers. The story must:

- Follow classic story structure: introduction establishing scene and characters, rising action building tension through the encounter, climax reaching the emotional peak, and resolution that lingers
- Use vivid sensory details to immerse the reader in the moment
- Employ dialogue to reveal character and heighten tension
- Incorporate symbolism and subtext to add depth
- Avoid clichés and predictable outcomes
- Craft a powerful ending that resonates emotionally
- Consider non-linear structure if it strengthens impact

## Context
{{character-profiles}}

Provide for each character: name, age, occupation, physical description, personality traits, and backstory elements relevant to the encounter.

{{setting}}

Describe the location and atmosphere where the encounter occurs.

{{story-beats}}

Specify: the inciting incident that brings them together, key rising action moments, the climactic turning point, how the encounter resolves, the underlying theme, and the emotional impact you want readers to feel.

## Output
Deliver a standalone flash fiction story in standard prose format with natural paragraphing and dialogue. No XML tags, headers, or meta-commentary—only the story itself, exactly as it would appear in a literary magazine.
```
