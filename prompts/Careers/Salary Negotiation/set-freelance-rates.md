# Freelance Rate Structure Generator

## 簡介

The Freelance Rate Structure Generator is a free AI prompt that builds customized pricing tables for freelancers and consultants seeking market-aligned rates. This freelance pricing prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your professional profile - including field of expertise, years of experience, key skills, target market, and location - to produce a markdown table with beginner and experienced rate tiers for at least five common services in your field. It considers market demand, competitive landscape, geographic conditions, and industry-standard pricing models (hourly, project-based, or retainer) to recommend rates that balance accessibility and profitability. The output includes a brief justification explaining the rate differential between tiers. Freelancers moving from fixed salaries to independent consulting, established consultants refining their pricing strategy, and service providers entering new markets will find this prompt clarifies positioning and builds client-ready rate sheets. ● Produces beginner (1-2 years) and experienced (5+ years) rate tiers for five or more relevant services ● Adapts pricing format to industry norms - hourly, per-project ranges, or monthly retainers ● Accounts for geographic market conditions and target client segment expectations ● Includes a concise rationale for the rate spread between experience levels ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert freelance pricing consultant who develops rate structures aligned with market standards and individual positioning.

## Task
Create a comprehensive freelance rate structure tailored to the provided profile. Deliver both beginner and experienced rate tiers for common services in the field.

## Context
Analyze the professional profile below to inform pricing recommendations:

{{professional-profile}}

Consider:
- Market demand and competitive landscape for this expertise level
- Geographic market conditions and typical rate ranges
- Value proposition relative to the target client segment
- Industry-standard pricing models (hourly, project-based, retainer)

## Output
Present your rate structure as a markdown table with three columns: **Service**, **Beginner Rate**, and **Experienced Rate**.

Include at least five common services relevant to the field, with rates that:
- Reflect realistic entry-level positioning for beginners (1-2 years experience)
- Show appropriate experienced-tier pricing (5+ years, established reputation)
- Account for the specific market and target client expectations described
- Use appropriate rate formats ($/hour, per-project ranges, or monthly retainers as suitable)

Briefly note (2-3 sentences) the key factors that justify the rate differential between tiers for this particular profile.
```
