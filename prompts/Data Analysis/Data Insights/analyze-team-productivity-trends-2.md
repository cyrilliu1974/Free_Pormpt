# Team Productivity Trend Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Team Productivity Trend Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive productivity assessments and delivers evidence-based optimization recommendations for teams and managers. This team productivity analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through a rigorous framework that collects quantitative metrics (task completion rates, time-to-completion, output quality scores, velocity trends) and qualitative data (team feedback, blockers, performance reviews), identifies meaningful patterns across multiple timeframes, and produces a structured report with SMART recommendations grounded in actual data. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming raw team tracking context into an executive-ready document with methodology, visualizations, actionable insights, and an implementation roadmap. Use it when you need to move beyond gut instinct and surface objective patterns in how your team works, whether you're diagnosing performance issues, preparing quarterly reviews, or planning process improvements. ● Analyzes both quantitative productivity metrics and qualitative team feedback to surface complete performance pictures ● Distinguishes short-term fluctuations from meaningful long-term trends across weekly, monthly, and quarterly timeframes ● Ensures all recommendations meet SMART criteria and link directly to data findings, eliminating guesswork ● Delivers a complete report format from executive summary through implementation plan with tracking metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a productivity analyst specializing in team performance optimization, data-driven insights, and actionable efficiency improvements.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive analysis of team productivity trends and deliver a structured report with evidence-based recommendations for optimizing performance.

## Context
Team details: {{team-and-tracking-context}}
(Include team size, industry, current productivity tracking methods, any known challenges, and organizational goals.)

## Analysis Framework

**Data Collection & Metrics:**
- Quantitative: task completion rates, time-to-completion, output quality scores, velocity trends
- Qualitative: team feedback, performance reviews, blockers reported
- Compare against industry benchmarks and internal targets
- Account for external factors (tool changes, process shifts, seasonal variations)

**Pattern Identification:**
- Analyze trends over multiple timeframes (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- Distinguish short-term fluctuations from long-term patterns
- Identify correlations between metrics and contextual factors

**Recommendation Criteria:**
All recommendations must be SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) and grounded in the data analysis—no assumptions without evidence.

## Output
Deliver a structured report in this format:

1. **Executive Summary** – Key findings and top 3 recommendations
2. **Introduction** – Importance of productivity analysis and scope
3. **Methodology** – Data sources, collection period, analytical approach
4. **Data Analysis**
   - Key metrics with baseline and current values
   - Data visualizations (charts, graphs, trend lines)
   - Identified patterns and anomalies
5. **Insights and Findings** – What the data reveals about team performance
6. **Actionable Recommendations** – Prioritized improvements with expected impact
7. **Implementation Plan** – Steps, timeline, success metrics, tracking method
8. **Conclusion** – Summary and next steps
```
