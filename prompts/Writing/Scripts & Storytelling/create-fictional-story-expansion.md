# Fictional Story Expansion Prompt for Prequels & Sequels

## 簡介

The Fictional Story Expansion Prompt for Prequels & Sequels is a free AI prompt that creates structured narrative extensions - either prequels or sequels - that honor the original work's universe, voice, and character dynamics. This story expansion prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through a comprehensive analysis of your original story, then produces a complete narrative plan and full story text. It identifies core themes, narrative voice, and character dynamics before building a prequel or sequel with meaningful character arcs, compelling conflict, and clear connections to the source material. Writers working on fan fiction, novel series, screenplay treatments, or creative writing projects use it to extend their fictional worlds without contradicting established canon. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering both the structural framework and the complete prose. Reach for this prompt when you need to expand a story backward or forward in time while preserving its essence, or when you want a structured approach to developing narrative extensions that feel authentic to the original work. ● Analyzes the original story to extract tone, themes, character dynamics, and world-building elements before generating new content ● Produces a structured narrative plan covering character development, plot structure, conflict resolution, and thematic connections ● Delivers complete story text with a compelling title, maintaining the original's narrative voice and style ● Creates meaningful connections through foreshadowing, consequences, or revelations that enrich the overall fictional universe ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert creative fiction writer developing an extended narrative that either precedes or follows an original story.

## Task
Craft a prequel or sequel that expands the fictional universe while maintaining absolute consistency with the original story's tone, themes, and characterizations. The narrative must be well-structured with meaningful character arcs, compelling conflict and resolution, and clear connections to the source material.

## Context
Analyze the original story to identify its core themes, narrative voice, character dynamics, and world-building elements. Your extension should feel like a natural part of the same fictional universe—neither contradicting established canon nor merely repeating its patterns, but genuinely enriching the overall narrative.

## Input
{{original-story}}

## Output
Deliver a complete structured narrative plan followed by the full story text:

**Original Story Summary:**
[Brief synopsis establishing context]

**Narrative Direction:**
[Prequel or Sequel, with time period]

**Key Characters:**
[Principals in this narrative and their starting states]

**Character Development:**
[Meaningful arcs each character undergoes]

**Major Events:**
[Plot structure: setup, complications, climax]

**Conflict and Resolution:**
[Central tension and how it concludes]

**Themes and Motifs:**
[How original themes are explored or expanded]

**Story Connections:**
[Specific ties to the original—foreshadowing, consequences, or revelations]

**Narrative Style:**
[Tone, voice, pacing approach matching the original]

**Story Title:**
[Compelling title]

**Story Text:**
[Complete narrative]
```
