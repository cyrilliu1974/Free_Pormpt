# Tailored Three-Channel Acquisition Mix Generator

## 簡介

The Tailored Three-Channel Acquisition Mix Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a diversified customer acquisition strategy for businesses seeking balanced growth across multiple traffic sources. This acquisition mix prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context and recommends exactly three channels - one algorithmic (social platforms with viral potential), one long-term (compounding assets like SEO or newsletters), and one direct-impact (predictable channels like paid ads or partnerships). For each recommended channel, it explains why it fits your specific business model and delivers three high-ROI, immediately actionable distribution ideas. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing unconventional tactics over saturated approaches and favoring cost-effective options unless paid channels offer materially better returns. Use this prompt when you need to escape single-channel dependence, diversify traffic sources, or plan a first acquisition strategy that balances short-term wins with long-term asset building. ● Recommends one channel from each of three categories to prevent over-reliance on algorithms, slow-burn tactics, or budget-dependent channels alone ● Explains the strategic fit of each channel based on your audience, business model, and pricing ● Provides three concrete, high-ROI distribution ideas per channel that you can implement immediately ● Prioritizes overlooked, unconventional tactics that often deliver better results than crowded mainstream channels ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a digital marketing strategist specializing in customer acquisition. Generate a balanced three-channel acquisition mix tailored to the business described below.

## Task
Recommend exactly one channel from each category—algorithmic, long-term, and direct-impact—that best fits the business context. For each channel, explain why it suits this business and provide three concrete, high-ROI distribution ideas.

## Framework
**Algorithmic channels** (Twitter, TikTok, LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.): viral potential with unpredictable reach; a single strong post can reach millions, while weak content gets little visibility.

**Long-term channels** (YouTube, newsletter, podcast, SEO, etc.): require 6–12 months to show results but compound over time into durable traffic assets.

**Direct-impact channels** (Product Hunt, cold email, paid ads, partnerships, etc.): predictable and scalable; output correlates directly with effort or budget.

A balanced mix needs one channel from each category to avoid over-reliance on unstable algorithms or slow-burning investments.

## Criteria
- Prioritize unconventional, overlooked tactics over saturated channels
- Focus on the highest ROI opportunities, even if less obvious
- Favor free or low-cost options unless paid channels offer materially better returns for this business
- Keep explanations clear and actionable

## Business Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
For each of the three channels, provide:
1. **Channel name and category**
2. **Why it fits** (2–3 sentences tied to the business, audience, and pricing)
3. **Three distribution ideas** (specific, immediately actionable)

Format your response in Markdown with clear headings.
```
