# Graphic Design Services Sales Pitch Generator

## 簡介

The Graphic Design Services Sales Pitch Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a professional portfolio document and sales pitch for graphic designers seeking to attract and convert clients. This graphic design sales pitch prompt for ChatGPT helps you create a structured service offering that showcases your best work, client testimonials, design process, and unique value proposition. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your design specialties, unique selling points, and target audience into a conversion-focused portfolio document. Freelance designers use it to respond to RFPs, agencies use it to standardize their pitch decks, and independent creatives use it to land higher-value projects by clearly communicating their process and outcomes. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly assemble a professional portfolio presentation that balances visual credibility with clear service benefits tailored to a specific client or market segment. ● Creates four distinct sections - portfolio showcase, client testimonials, design process walkthrough, and service benefits - in one cohesive document ● Adapts messaging and examples to your specific design specialties, unique selling proposition, and target audience ● Formats output for both web portfolio pages and downloadable PDF presentations ● Balances visual portfolio elements with process transparency and outcome-focused benefits that answer the client's core question: why you ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert graphic designer creating a comprehensive portfolio and service offering to attract and retain clients.

## Task
Develop a professional portfolio document that showcases expertise, demonstrates value, and converts prospective clients. Structure the offering to highlight your strongest work, client validation, process transparency, and service benefits.

## Context
{{design-specialties}} are your core competencies. Your {{usp}} differentiates you in the market. Tailor all messaging and examples to resonate with {{target-audience}}.

## Output
Deliver a structured portfolio document with clear headings and bullet points covering:

**Portfolio Showcase**
- Best work across your design categories
- Visual examples that demonstrate range and depth
- Project context and outcomes

**Client Testimonials**
- Specific results and feedback from past clients
- Names, companies, and project types where available

**Design Process**
- Step-by-step explanation of how you work
- Timeline expectations and collaboration touchpoints
- Revision and approval stages

**Service Benefits**
- Value proposition tied to {{usp}}
- Tangible outcomes clients can expect
- Why your approach solves their design challenges

Format for clarity and professional presentation, suitable for web portfolio or PDF delivery.
```
