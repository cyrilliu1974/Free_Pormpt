# Industry Analysis Report Generator for Market Research

## 簡介

The Industry Analysis Report Generator for Market Research is a free AI prompt that produces comprehensive industry analysis reports for business strategists, consultants, and market researchers. This industry analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through eight structured sections: executive summary, market sizing with competitor share data, historical and projected growth rates, emerging industry trends, regulatory landscape, barriers to entry, strategic opportunities and challenges, and actionable recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, requiring only two inputs - the industry name and your business context - to generate a professional report with citations, data visualizations, and evidence-based insights. Use it when entering a new market, preparing competitive intelligence, conducting due diligence, or advising clients on sector positioning. ● Covers market size, competitor share, historical growth rates, and projected CAGR with sources and timeframes ● Identifies 3-5 major industry trends including technological shifts, consumer behavior changes, and emerging business models ● Analyzes regulatory environment, compliance requirements, and anticipated policy changes by jurisdiction ● Assesses barriers to entry such as capital needs, brand loyalty, IP protections, and economies of scale ● Surfaces strategic opportunities in underserved segments, disruptive technologies, and partnership targets alongside competitive and operational challenges ● Delivers 3-5 tailored strategic recommendations based on your specific business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in comprehensive industry analysis.

## Task
Produce a detailed industry analysis report covering market size, growth trajectory, key trends, regulatory environment, and barriers to entry. The report should identify strategic opportunities and challenges, supported by data and cited sources.

## Context
Industry to analyze: {{industry}}

Business context: {{business-context}}

## Output
Structure the report with the following sections:

### Executive Summary
Highlight key findings, market dynamics, and strategic conclusions in 2-3 paragraphs.

### Market Size & Players
- Total industry revenue and year
- Market share of top 3-5 competitors
- Competitive landscape overview

### Growth Analysis
- Historical growth rates (3-5 year view)
- Projected CAGR with time horizon
- Primary growth drivers and market catalysts

### Industry Trends
Identify and analyze 3-5 major trends:
- Technological advancements transforming the sector
- Shifting consumer behaviors and preferences
- Emerging business models or distribution channels
- Sustainability or social impact movements

### Regulatory Environment
- Relevant laws, standards, and compliance requirements
- Jurisdiction-specific considerations
- Anticipated regulatory changes and their potential impact

### Barriers to Entry
Assess factors limiting new competition:
- Capital requirements and infrastructure needs
- Brand loyalty and network effects
- Intellectual property protections
- Regulatory or licensing hurdles
- Economies of scale advantages

### Opportunities & Challenges
**Opportunities:**
- Underserved market segments or geographic regions
- Disruptive technologies or business model innovations
- Partnership or acquisition targets
- Favorable regulatory shifts

**Challenges:**
- Competitive intensity and differentiation requirements
- Supply chain or operational complexities
- Evolving customer expectations
- Potential disruption from substitute products

### Strategic Recommendations
Provide 3-5 actionable recommendations for businesses seeking to enter, compete, or expand in this industry, tailored to the business context provided.

### References
Cite all data sources, research reports, and industry publications using a consistent citation format. Include URLs where applicable.

---

**Formatting requirements:**
- Use charts, graphs, or tables to visualize key data points
- Include specific metrics with timeframes and sources
- Write in clear, professional business language
- Ensure all claims are evidence-based and properly attributed
```
