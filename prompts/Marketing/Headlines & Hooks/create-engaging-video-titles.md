# Video Title Generator for YouTube and Social Media

## 簡介

The Video Title Generator for YouTube and Social Media is a free AI prompt that produces five distinct, SEO-friendly titles designed to maximize both search visibility and click-through rates for video content creators. This video title prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your video topic and context, then delivers five ready-to-use titles that naturally incorporate relevant keywords while applying proven engagement techniques: numbered lists, how-to formats, questions, and transformation promises. Each title employs power words that trigger curiosity or urgency without sacrificing readability, and presents a unique angle on your topic. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it flexible for your preferred text model. Real-world use cases include YouTube creators optimizing upload titles, social media managers crafting video captions, and content teams A/B testing title variations. This prompt is ideal for video producers, marketers, and content creators who need multiple title options quickly, ensuring their videos are discoverable in search while compelling enough to earn clicks from real viewers. ● Produces five unique titles per run, each with a different angle on the same topic for A/B testing or selection. ● Integrates target keywords naturally while maintaining readability and emotional appeal. ● Applies proven title formats including numbered lists, how-tos, questions, and transformation promises. ● Uses power words strategically to increase curiosity and click-through rates without resorting to clickbait. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in video content creation specializing in SEO-optimized, click-worthy titles that balance discoverability with audience appeal.

## Task
Generate five video titles for the specified topic. Each title must be concise, SEO-friendly, and compelling enough to drive clicks. Incorporate relevant keywords naturally, use proven engagement techniques (numbers, questions, power words, how-to formats), and ensure each title presents a distinct angle while maintaining thematic cohesion.

## Context
{{video-topic-and-context}}

## Process
1. Identify the core message and what makes the video valuable or entertaining to the target audience
2. Integrate relevant keywords naturally without compromising readability
3. Apply proven title formats: numbered lists ("7 Ways to..."), how-tos, questions, or transformation promises
4. Include power words that trigger curiosity, urgency, or excitement ("Secrets," "Transform," "Instantly," "Unbelievable") where appropriate to the content
5. Ensure each title stands alone while offering a different angle on the topic

## Output
Provide five distinct video titles, each optimized for search visibility and click-through rate. Format as a numbered list with no additional commentary.
```
