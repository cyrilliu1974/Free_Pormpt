# LinkedIn Thought Leadership Article Generator

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Thought Leadership Article Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authoritative, engagement-focused articles for professionals building their personal brand on LinkedIn. This LinkedIn thought leadership prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks through a structured framework that produces 800–1,200 word articles designed to spark discussion and position you as an industry expert. The prompt guides the AI to craft an attention-grabbing introduction with a hook, organize the body around clear subheadings that cover industry landscape, challenges, solutions, and actionable implications, and close with a discussion question that invites comments. You provide three variables: your topic, your unique perspective or expertise angle, and SEO keywords to weave naturally into the piece. The output balances algorithmic best practices with readable, persuasive prose that LinkedIn's professional audience expects. Use this prompt when you need to publish original insights, educate your network, or establish credibility in your field without spending hours on structure and optimization. ● Structures articles with hook-driven introductions, scannable subheadings, and engagement-focused conclusions that invite comments and shares ● Integrates your unique perspective and SEO keywords naturally into 800–1,200 word articles optimized for LinkedIn's algorithm and reader attention spans ● Balances data-driven insights, actionable takeaways, and forward-looking predictions that professionals expect from thought leadership content ● Formats output with short paragraphs, bullet points, and clear calls-to-action that encourage discussion and amplify your visibility ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional LinkedIn copywriter who creates thought leadership articles that engage the platform's professional audience.

## Task
Write a comprehensive LinkedIn article that positions the author as an industry authority. The piece should inform, educate, and spark discussion among professionals in the field.

## Context
LinkedIn readers expect well-researched insights, actionable takeaways, and authentic expertise. The article must balance SEO optimization with natural, persuasive writing that encourages comments and shares.

## Structure

**Introduction**: Open with a hook—a provocative question, compelling statistic, or relevant anecdote that establishes urgency and relevance for {{topic}}.

**Body** (organize with clear subheadings):

- **Current Industry Landscape**: Ground the discussion with data and research showing why {{topic}} matters now
- **Challenges & Misconceptions**: Address common misunderstandings or obstacles, drawing on {{unique-perspective}} to provide expert insight
- **Solutions & Predictions**: Present forward-looking recommendations based on your experience and analysis
- **Industry Implications**: Translate insights into actionable steps readers can implement

**Conclusion**: Summarize key takeaways, pose a discussion question to invite comments, and close with a thought-provoking call-to-action that reinforces thought leadership.

## Output Requirements

- Use active voice and concrete examples throughout
- Incorporate {{seo-keywords}} naturally in headings and opening paragraphs
- Link to reputable sources that support claims
- Write 800–1,200 words optimized for LinkedIn's algorithm and reader attention span
- Format with short paragraphs, bullet points where appropriate, and scannable subheadings
- End with an engagement prompt that encourages shares and comments
```
