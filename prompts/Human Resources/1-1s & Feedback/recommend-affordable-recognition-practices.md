# Affordable Recognition Practices for Managers

## 簡介

The Affordable Recognition Practices for Managers is a free AI prompt that helps leaders design and implement effective, zero-budget recognition strategies tailored to their workplace context. Built for managers who need to motivate teams without formal programs or spending, this recognition practices prompt for ChatGPT produces five actionable recommendations grounded in behavioral psychology - each with implementation steps, the motivational principle behind it, and a realistic example drawn from your specific workplace context. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and emphasizes real-time, behavior-specific recognition that reinforces performance without requiring approvals or HR systems. Reach for this prompt when you want to strengthen team motivation through consistent, meaningful recognition that fits your culture and leadership style. ● Delivers five distinct recognition practices with clear titles, step-by-step implementation guidance, and the psychological rationale explaining why each method works. ● Grounds every recommendation in motivation science - positive reinforcement, social recognition, and real-time feedback - so managers understand the principles driving impact. ● Provides a concrete, contextual example for each practice, showing exactly how to execute it in your specific workplace setting. ● Focuses on sustainable, low-effort methods that fit into daily workflows and require no budget, software, or formal approval. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert organizational psychologist and performance management specialist with deep expertise in workplace motivation, behavioral psychology, and recognition system design.

## Task

Recommend 5 highly effective, low-cost recognition practices that can be implemented immediately to reinforce positive performance. Each practice must leverage psychological principles of motivation, be sustainable for consistent implementation, and create meaningful impact without requiring budget approval or complex systems.

## Context

{{workplace-context}}

The most powerful recognition happens in real-time and focuses on specific behaviors rather than general praise. Consider the workplace culture, team dynamics, and leadership style when crafting your suggestions.

## Output

Structure your response with 5 numbered recommendations. For each practice, include:

- **Practice name**: A clear, memorable title
- **Implementation steps**: Bullet-pointed actions the manager can take immediately
- **Psychological rationale**: The motivation principle that makes this practice effective
- **Real-world example**: A concrete scenario demonstrating how to execute it in the given workplace context
```
