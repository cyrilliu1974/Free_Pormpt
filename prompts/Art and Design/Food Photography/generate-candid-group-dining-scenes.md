# Candid Group Dining Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Candid Group Dining Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic, high-angle restaurant scenes featuring styled subjects around elegant dining tables for lifestyle brands, hospitality marketing, and editorial content. This image prompt produces photorealistic group dining photography with dramatic lighting, rich textures, and intimate social dynamics. The candid group dining prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana renders detailed character styling - from fashion choices and hair to expressions and hand gestures - alongside meticulously composed table elements like glassware, drinks, citrus garnishes, and ambient décor. Perfect for hospitality campaigns, fashion editorials, social media visuals, and mood boards that demand authenticity and edge. Use this prompt when you need aspirational yet gritty dining imagery that conveys confidence, style, and real human connection in upscale settings. ● Customizable group composition, venue setting, and mood variables let you tailor the scene to match brand aesthetics and campaign goals. ● High-angle, slightly tilted framing with sharp focus on subjects and soft bokeh backgrounds creates editorial-quality depth and storytelling. ● Detailed subject descriptions - clothing texture, hairstyle, accessories, expressions - ensure consistent, fashion-forward character rendering. ● Warm ambient lighting, dark wood interiors, ornate mirrors, and table styling produce rich, atmospheric visuals suitable for print and digital. ## Prompt

```
High-angle shot, slightly off-kilter tilt. {{group-composition}} seated closely around a white dining table in {{venue-setting}}. Mood: {{mood}}.

Left figure: long dark hair, chic black sunglasses, black lace-trimmed camisole with floral pattern hints, expression partially obscured. Second from left: dark mullet-style hair, distinct mustache, dark suit jacket over white shirt, hands clasped near chin, playful expression. Center figure: glamorous woman with very long dark wavy hair, dark blazer over light blue subtly patterned bralette, looking right with slightly defiant expression, left hand raised with middle finger prominently displayed. Second from right: striking woman with long dark slicked-back hair, dark strapless top (velvet or satin texture), small dark cigarette or cigarillo between lips, relaxed nonchalant expression. Bottom right: curly brown hair, top of head visible, suggesting more people at table.

Table items: multiple clear glasses with drinks, ice cubes, citrus slices, white bottle with dark label, small white dishes. Background: dark wood paneling, ornate gold lettering partially reading 'ALSACE', dark reflective mirrors. Soft warm ambient lighting illuminates faces and table, strong contrast against dark background. Subtle floral arrangements in distance. Hyper-realistic candid photography, sharp focus on central group, photorealistic style. 4:3 aspect ratio.
```
