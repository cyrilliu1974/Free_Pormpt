# Strategic Business KPI Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Strategic Business KPI Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates balanced performance metrics for small business owners seeking measurable growth without complex infrastructure. This business KPI prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your specific business context and producing 10 custom key performance indicators that span customer satisfaction, employee engagement, operational efficiency, and financial health. Each metric includes a practical tracking method using accessible tools, a direct profitability link, and a clear explanation of its cultural impact. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a complete balanced scorecard that small business owners can implement immediately without expensive software or data systems. Real use cases include retail stores tracking customer retention alongside employee satisfaction, service businesses linking turnaround time to both revenue and team morale, and growing companies building their first formal performance framework. This prompt is designed for small business owners, operators, and managers who need actionable metrics that drive both financial results and a positive workplace environment. ● Produces 10 balanced KPIs across four critical dimensions: customer satisfaction, employee engagement, operational efficiency, and financial health. ● Provides specific measurement methods using accessible tools like spreadsheets, free surveys, and existing business systems. ● Links every metric explicitly to both profitability (revenue growth or cost efficiency) and organizational culture (team morale or engagement). ● Delivers a cohesive balanced scorecard where metrics reinforce each other, creating positive feedback loops between financial performance and workplace satisfaction. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a business performance analyst specializing in practical KPI frameworks for small businesses. You design metrics that are simple to track, drive profitability, and strengthen organizational culture.

## Task
Develop 10 strategic KPIs tailored to the business context below. Each KPI must:
- Be measurable without expensive software or complex data infrastructure
- Directly impact revenue growth or cost efficiency
- Foster employee engagement and workplace satisfaction
- Span customer satisfaction, employee engagement, operational efficiency, and financial health

## Context
{{business-context}}

## Output
Deliver a numbered list. For each KPI provide:
1. **KPI Name** – clear, actionable title
2. **How to Measure** – practical tracking method using accessible tools
3. **Profitability Link** – specific connection to revenue or margins
4. **Culture Impact** – how it improves the work environment or team morale

Ensure the set of 10 KPIs works together as a balanced scorecard the owner can implement immediately.
```
