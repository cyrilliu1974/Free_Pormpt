# Flexible Pricing Tiers Generator for Negotiations

## 簡介

The Flexible Pricing Tiers Generator for Negotiations is a free AI prompt that builds strategic, negotiation-ready pricing models for sales teams, pricing analysts, and business strategists. This pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through creating 3-5 customer-aligned pricing tiers - from entry-level to enterprise - complete with feature-benefit mapping, volume discounts, and value-added services. You input your offering, target customer profile, and competitive landscape; the prompt returns a markdown table with tier names, included features, customer benefits, and natural upgrade paths, plus a 2-3 paragraph rationale that explains your overall strategy, market positioning, and key differentiators. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to adapt pricing frameworks to different products, services, or customer segments. Use it when preparing for contract negotiations, launching new offerings, or segmenting customers by willingness to pay in competitive markets. ● Delivers 3-5 pricing tiers with clear feature-benefit alignment for each segment ● Includes volume discounts, customization options, and value-added service recommendations ● Generates a strategic rationale linking pricing to market differentiation and customer willingness to pay ● Outputs a markdown table format ready to paste into proposals, decks, or negotiation playbooks ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic pricing analyst developing a negotiation-ready pricing structure.

## Task
Create a comprehensive tiered pricing model that addresses different customer segments and facilitates successful deal closure. Analyze feature-benefit relationships, incorporate volume discounts, customization options, and value-added services where appropriate.

## Context
**Offering:** {{product-or-service}}
**Target customers:** {{target-customer}}
**Competitive landscape:** {{competitive-context}}

Design pricing tiers that leverage your unique selling points and differentiate from competitors while aligning with customer willingness to pay across segments.

## Output
Provide a brief introduction (2-3 paragraphs) explaining:
- The overall pricing strategy
- How it aligns with customer needs and market positioning
- Key differentiators that justify the pricing structure

Then present a markdown table:

| Pricing Tier | Features | Benefits |
|--------------|----------|----------|

Include 3-5 tiers ranging from entry-level to premium/enterprise, with each tier clearly showing:
- What features are included
- The specific customer benefits those features deliver
- Natural upgrade paths between tiers
```
