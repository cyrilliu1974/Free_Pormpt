# Exit Interview Best Practices Prompt

## 簡介

The Exit Interview Best Practices Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates comprehensive frameworks for conducting professional exit interviews that yield actionable insights while preserving positive relationships with departing employees. This exit interview prompt for ChatGPT helps HR professionals and team leaders design and execute effective exit conversations by providing detailed best practices across five critical dimensions: optimal timing relative to departure dates, strategic interviewer selection, question frameworks that encourage honest feedback, ethical documentation approaches, and follow-up protocols that turn insights into organizational improvements. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing numbered lists with clear headings, detailed explanations, and implementation examples tailored to your organizational context. Use it when designing your exit interview process, training interviewers, or refining existing practices to capture more valuable feedback. ● Provides strategic timing recommendations that balance emotional distance with memory accuracy to maximize candid responses. ● Delivers interviewer selection criteria and training guidance to create safe, confidential environments for honest dialogue. ● Generates question frameworks covering job satisfaction, management effectiveness, company culture, and reasons for departure with example phrasings. ● Includes ethical documentation methods and data storage protocols that protect confidentiality and comply with privacy standards. ● Offers follow-up and action-planning protocols that demonstrate how organizations close the feedback loop and drive meaningful change. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources consultant specializing in employee transition management and exit interview design.

## Task
Provide comprehensive best practices for conducting professional, respectful exit interviews that maximize valuable feedback while maintaining positive relationships with departing employees.

## Context
Exit interviews are critical touchpoints that influence company reputation, yield actionable insights for organizational improvement, and ensure employees leave with dignity. The practices should encourage honest feedback, protect confidentiality, demonstrate genuine care for employee experiences, and translate insights into meaningful organizational changes.

{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Deliver a **numbered list** with clear headings and detailed explanations for each best practice. Cover:

- **Timing considerations** – when to schedule the interview relative to departure date
- **Interviewer selection** – who should conduct the interview and why
- **Question frameworks** – types of questions that elicit honest, useful feedback
- **Documentation approaches** – how to record and store responses ethically
- **Follow-up protocols** – how to act on insights and close the loop

Include specific examples and implementation tips where relevant. Focus on transforming potentially awkward conversations into valuable learning opportunities.
```
