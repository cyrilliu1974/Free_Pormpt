# Social Media Performance Audit and Optimization Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Performance Audit and Optimization Prompt is a free AI prompt that reverse-engineers content performance patterns and prescribes data-driven changes to increase reach, engagement, and conversions across your social platforms. This social media optimization prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok categorizes your content by performance tier, identifies outliers, maps audience behavior signals to content attributes, detects strategic gaps, and delivers specific recommendations ranked by effort-to-impact ratio. Use it when your social strategy feels stuck, when engagement is declining, or when you need to understand why similar posts produce wildly different results. ● Categorizes your content inventory into performance tiers and identifies which formats, topics, and hooks actually drive results for your audience. ● Distinguishes business metrics (saves, shares, clicks, conversions) from vanity metrics (likes, followers) to diagnose root causes of underperformance. ● Delivers a prioritized optimization roadmap with high-impact changes, medium adjustments, and quick wins, each tied to a target metric and implementation steps. ● Maps your current conversion pathway, pinpoints breakdown points where audiences disengage, and prescribes an optimized journey that repairs the funnel. ## Prompt

1. Categorize content inventory by performance tier

3. Map audience behavior signals to content attributes

## Criteria

- Base analysis on observable patterns in actual performance data, not generic best practices
- Distinguish vanity metrics (likes, followers) from business metrics (saves, shares, clicks, conversions)
- Identify specific content attributes (hook style, format, topic, length, visuals) that correlate with performance
- Diagnose root causes: content quality, strategic misalignment, algorithmic factors, or audience mismatch
- Provide immediately implementable recommendations, not vague advice
- Prioritize by effort-to-impact ratio
- Tailor insights to the provided data; avoid assumptions
- Focus on sustainable improvements, not viral gimmicks
- Address both content creation and distribution strategy
- Never recommend artificial tactics (buying followers, engagement pods)
- Do not ignore conversion metrics
