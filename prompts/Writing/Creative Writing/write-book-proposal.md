# Book Proposal Writer for Literary Agents

## 簡介

The Book Proposal Writer for Literary Agents is a free AI prompt that produces professional, market-focused book proposals designed to capture the attention of literary agents and publishers. This book proposal prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to structure a complete submission package covering five critical sections: a commercial overview that hooks immediately, detailed chapter summaries that demonstrate narrative or argumentative arc, target audience analysis with demographic and behavioral insights, competitive analysis of 4-6 comparable titles from recent years, and an author biography showcasing platform metrics and credentials. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, turning a book concept into an 8-12 page proposal that meets industry standards. Writers use it when pitching fiction or nonfiction manuscripts, preparing query packages, or refining their positioning before agent outreach. ● Produces a complete proposal with overview, chapter summaries, target audience analysis, competitive titles, and author bio in one workflow ● Frames the book concept with commercial hooks and differentiators that prove market demand ● Identifies primary and secondary reader demographics and explains how the book satisfies their needs ● Positions the author as credible through platform metrics, credentials, and relevant experience ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert book proposal writer specializing in pitches for literary agents and publishers.

## Task
Craft a comprehensive, persuasive book proposal that demonstrates market viability and author credibility. Structure the proposal with these sections:

**Overview**
Present the book's core concept, unique selling points, and why it matters now. Make the hook immediate and commercial.

**Chapter Summaries**
Outline the book's structure and progression. For each chapter, describe the key content, arguments, or narrative beats that show the arc and depth of the work.

**Target Audience Analysis**
Identify primary and secondary reader demographics (age, profession, interests, buying behavior). Explain why this book solves a problem, fills a gap, or satisfies a desire for these readers.

**Competitive Analysis**
List 4-6 comparable titles published in the last 3-5 years. For each, note its success and explain how your book differentiates itself while proving demand exists for the category.

**Author Biography**
Highlight credentials, relevant experience, previous publications, media appearances, speaking engagements, and platform metrics (social media, mailing list, website traffic) that position you as the authority to write this book.

## Context
{{book-concept}}

## Output
Format each section with clear headings and bullet points for readability. Maintain a confident, professional tone throughout. Aim for a proposal that is thorough yet concise—typically 8-12 pages when formatted.
```
