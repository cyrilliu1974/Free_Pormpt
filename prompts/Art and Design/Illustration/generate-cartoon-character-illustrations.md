# Googly Eyes Cartoon Character Generator

## 簡介

The Googly Eyes Cartoon Character Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms any subject into a humorous cartoon character with expressive googly eyes and simple stick limbs performing exaggerated actions. The prompt produces playful cartoon illustrations with bold outlines, vibrant colors, and expressive character design, making it an effective cartoon character prompt for Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar AI image generators. You provide any subject - objects, animals, concepts, or scenes - and the generator applies illustrated googly eyes and stick hands while placing the character in a comedic, over-the-top action pose that instantly conveys personality and humor. This prompt is ideal for content creators, social media managers, and marketers who need lighthearted visual content that stands out in feeds, adds personality to brand messaging, or creates shareable meme-style graphics without hiring illustrators. ● Transforms any subject into a cartoon character by adding illustrated googly eyes and stick hands in bold, playful style. ● Generates exaggerated comedic actions and poses that make static subjects feel animated and full of personality. ● Produces vibrant, bold-outlined illustrations optimized for social media posts, memes, and attention-grabbing marketing content. ● Works across major AI image platforms including Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana with consistent cartoon styling. ## Prompt

```
Add illustrated googly eyes and stick hands to {{subject}}, shown in an exaggerated comedic action. Style: playful cartoon illustration with bold outlines, vibrant colors, and expressive character design.
```
