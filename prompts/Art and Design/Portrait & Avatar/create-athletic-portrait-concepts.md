# Athletic Portrait Concept Generator

## 簡介

The Athletic Portrait Concept Generator is a free AI prompt that creates close-up, editorial-style black-and-white portraits of athletes with dramatic lighting and precise technical specifications. This athletic portrait prompt for Midjourney produces images that capture sweat-drenched detail, sculpted musculature, and the focused energy of athletes moments before competition, using three-quarter back angles and shallow depth of field to emphasize physical definition. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana, generating high-contrast compositions with film grain, crushed blacks, and chiaroscuro lighting that casts deep shadows across the body. Reach for this prompt when you need editorial-quality sports photography concepts, fitness marketing visuals, or dramatic athlete portraits that convey strength and determination. ● Produces close-up black-and-white portraits with three-quarter back angles that showcase shoulder and back musculature. ● Specifies technical camera parameters including 85mm lens, f/2.8 aperture, ISO 400, and 1/250s shutter speed for consistent results. ● Applies dramatic chiaroscuro lighting to emphasize muscle definition, sweat droplets, and pre-competition intensity. ● Generates high-contrast images with film grain texture and crushed blacks for an editorial aesthetic. ## Prompt

```
Close-up black-and-white portrait of {{athlete-description}}, sweat droplets visible on skin. Standing in {{environment}}, facing away from camera to showcase sculpted back and shoulders. ```
```
