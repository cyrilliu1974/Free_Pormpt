# Glamorous Fashion Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Glamorous Fashion Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces hyper-realistic, full-body fashion photography with vintage Hollywood glamour and high-end editorial styling. This fashion portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates 8k photorealistic images shot from a slight low angle with an 85mm lens perspective. It applies warm chandelier lighting with subtle rim light, bold vintage makeup (pink blush, heavy eyeliner, glossy lips), and elegant updo hairstyles with soft curls. The output delivers cinematic, magazine-ready fashion photography where you control the subject's appearance and setting through two simple variables. Use it for lookbook shoots, brand campaigns, editorial concepts, or any project requiring polished fashion imagery without studio costs. Designers, brand marketers, and creative directors reach for this prompt when they need consistent, high-fashion visuals that capture the sophistication of vintage Hollywood editorial work. ● Full-body composition with slight low-angle 85mm perspective for editorial impact ● Vintage Hollywood styling: elegant updos, soft curls, bold pink blush, heavy eyeliner, glossy lips ● Warm chandelier lighting with soft glamorous glow and subtle rim light for luxury feel ● Hyper-realistic, photorealistic 8k output with high fashion editorial and cinematic aesthetics ## Prompt
