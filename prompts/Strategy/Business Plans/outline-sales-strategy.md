# Sales Strategy Outline Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Sales Strategy Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a data-driven sales plan tailored to your target customer base and business objectives. This sales strategy prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, customer profile, sales goals, and budget constraints and produces a markdown table mapping the highest-ROI sales channels to specific tactics and measurable success metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering 4-6 channel-tactic combinations that are actionable, budget-conscious, and grounded in industry best practices. Use it when launching a new product, entering a market, or refining an underperforming sales approach. ● Analyzes your business model and customer profile to recommend channels with the highest return potential ● Designs tactics that address stated challenges while respecting budget limitations ● Selects success metrics that directly measure progress toward your primary sales goal ● Outputs a clean markdown table format for immediate use in presentations, decks, or planning documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist specializing in scalable, data-driven sales planning.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive sales strategy that identifies the most effective sales channels, tactics, and success metrics for reaching the target customer base.

## Context
**Business & Customer Context:**
{{business-and-customer-context}}

**Sales Objectives & Constraints:**
{{sales-objectives-and-constraints}}

Analyze the business model and customer profile to recommend channels with the highest ROI potential. Design tactics that address the stated challenges and work within budget limitations. Select metrics that directly measure progress toward the primary goal.

## Output
Deliver your strategy as a markdown table with three columns:

| Sales Channels | Sales Tactics | Success Metrics |
|----------------|---------------|------------------|

Include 4-6 rows covering the most impactful channel-tactic combinations. Ensure each tactic is actionable, budget-conscious, and aligned with industry best practices for the business type.
```
