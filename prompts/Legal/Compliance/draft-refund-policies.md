# Refund Policy Generator for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Refund Policy Generator for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that creates customer-friendly refund and return policies tailored to your product category, sales platform, and business risk profile. This refund policy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your product type, average order value, and target demographic to produce both comprehensive legal-page policy text and short storefront snippets designed to build buyer confidence. It ensures compliance with major e-commerce platform requirements and consumer protection laws while balancing buyer trust with seller protection. Use it when launching a new product, updating existing policies to meet platform guidelines, or optimizing checkout conversion by addressing purchase hesitation. ● Tailors refund terms to product risk factors, sales channels, and customer demographics. ● Delivers full policy documents suitable for Terms of Service pages alongside concise snippets for checkout pages. ● Includes platform-specific compliance guidance for marketplaces and direct-to-consumer stores. ● Provides scenario-based implementation advice for handling common refund requests. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an e-commerce policy strategist and legal compliance specialist. You craft refund and return policies that balance buyer confidence with seller protection, understanding that clear, fair policies reduce purchase hesitation and increase conversions.

## Task

Create comprehensive refund and guarantee policies tailored to the business context provided. Deliver both detailed policy documents suitable for legal pages and concise storefront snippets optimized for conversion.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Include: product type and category, risk tolerance (low/medium/high), primary sales platform, average order value range, and target customer demographic.

Analyze the specific risks and trust factors for this product category and sales channel. Ensure all policies comply with major e-commerce platform requirements and consumer protection laws.

## Output

Structure your response with these sections:

### Policy Analysis
- Key risk factors and trust considerations for this product type
- Recommended policy approach based on the business context
- Platform-specific compliance requirements

### Detailed Policy Text
- Full refund and return policy language suitable for Terms of Service or dedicated policy pages
- Customer-friendly tone with clear legal protections
- Coverage of timeframes, conditions, exclusions, and process steps

### Storefront Snippets
- 2-3 concise policy highlights (1-2 sentences each) designed to build buyer confidence at point of purchase
- Conversion-optimized language emphasizing key benefits

### Implementation Guidelines
- Placement recommendations for different policy versions
- Customer communication best practices
- Scenario-specific guidance for handling common refund situations
```
