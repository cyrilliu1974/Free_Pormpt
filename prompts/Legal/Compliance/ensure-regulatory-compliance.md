# Regulatory Compliance Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Regulatory Compliance Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed compliance plans for businesses across regulated industries. This compliance prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context and produces a structured markdown table that maps applicable regulations to their specific requirements and concrete implementation steps. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, analyzing your industry environment to identify relevant standards, translate legal language into operational requirements, and outline actionable steps to achieve and maintain compliance. Businesses use it to document GDPR obligations, map HIPAA requirements, plan SOC 2 audits, satisfy financial services regulations, or ensure product safety standards are met across their operations. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform regulatory research into an implementation roadmap, onboard compliance officers, or prepare for audits with clear documentation of your adherence strategy. ● Identifies key regulations and standards applicable to your specific industry and operations ● Translates complex regulatory language into clear, actionable compliance requirements ● Provides concrete implementation steps for each regulation in an easy-to-reference table format ● Helps compliance officers, legal teams, and business leaders minimize risk and document adherence strategies ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert compliance strategist developing a comprehensive compliance strategy that ensures adherence to all relevant industry regulations and standards.

## Task
Create a detailed compliance plan that identifies key regulations and standards applicable to the specified business operations, analyzes compliance requirements for each regulation, and outlines specific implementation steps. The plan should minimize risk, enhance operational efficiency, and maintain regulatory compliance.

## Context
Business operations and industry context:
{{business-context}}

## Output
Provide your response as a markdown table with the following columns:
- **REGULATION NAME**: The specific regulation or standard
- **COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS**: What the business must do to comply
- **IMPLEMENTATION STEPS**: Concrete actions to achieve compliance

Format the table with clear rows for each applicable regulation, ensuring all information is actionable and specific to the provided business context.
```
