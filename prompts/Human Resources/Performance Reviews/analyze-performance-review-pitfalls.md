# Performance Review Pitfalls Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Performance Review Pitfalls Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies and dissects the most damaging mistakes undermining employee evaluations for HR professionals, managers, and organizational leaders. This performance review pitfalls prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to act as an expert organizational psychologist, delivering a structured analysis of seven frequent evaluation errors - covering both cognitive biases like halo effect, recency bias, and confirmation bias, and structural problems such as unclear metrics, inconsistent standards, and inadequate documentation. For each pitfall, you receive the psychological mechanism explaining why it happens, concrete workplace manifestations managers can recognize immediately, and the cascading impact on employee morale, performance, and culture. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting organization context as a variable to tailor findings to your environment. Reach for this prompt when designing or auditing performance management systems, training managers on evaluation best practices, or diagnosing why reviews feel ineffective or demotivating. ● Analyzes seven high-impact pitfalls spanning cognitive biases and process design flaws that affect organizations of all sizes. ● Explains the psychological and structural root causes behind each mistake, not just surface symptoms. ● Provides recognizable workplace examples so managers can spot these pitfalls in their own reviews. ● Details the downstream effects on trust, retention, and performance to justify corrective investment. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert human resources consultant and organizational psychologist specializing in performance management systems.

## Task
Identify and analyze 7 frequent performance review pitfalls that undermine employee development and organizational effectiveness. For each pitfall, provide:

- **Name** and core definition
- **Psychological mechanism**: why it occurs (cognitive biases, structural flaws, measurement errors)
- **Workplace manifestations**: specific, recognizable examples
- **Cascading impact**: effects on employee morale, performance, and organizational culture

Cover both cognitive biases (halo effect, recency bias, confirmation bias) and structural issues (unclear metrics, inconsistent standards, inadequate documentation). Focus on damaging, prevalent pitfalls that span industries and company sizes.

## Context
{{organization-context}}

## Output
Structure your response with numbered headings for each pitfall. Use bullet points under each heading for clarity and practical application. Ensure each explanation is detailed enough for managers to recognize the pitfall in their own processes and take corrective action.
```
