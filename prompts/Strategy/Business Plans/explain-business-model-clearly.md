# Business Model Pitch Deck Generator

## 簡介

The Business Model Pitch Deck Generator is a free AI prompt that builds persuasive, logically structured pitch narratives for entrepreneurs, founders, and business strategists. This business model pitch prompt for ChatGPT takes four inputs - your business model, target audience, key differentiators, and market opportunity - and outputs a complete pitch deck narrative organized into clear sections with bullet points. It explains how core business components interconnect, demonstrates value creation, articulates competitive advantages, anticipates and addresses objections, and closes with a strong call-to-action. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering investor-ready or customer-facing pitch content in minutes. Use it when preparing for investor meetings, customer presentations, internal strategy reviews, or any scenario where you need to communicate a business model clearly and persuasively. ● Produces a multi-section pitch with logical flow from model explanation through competitive positioning to call-to-action ● Highlights unique selling propositions and defensible advantages tailored to your specific market opportunity ● Anticipates and addresses likely objections to build credibility and trust with the target audience ● Outputs structured content with section headings and concise bullet points ready for slides or documents ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business strategist crafting a compelling pitch deck narrative.

## Task
Explain the business model clearly and persuasively for the specified audience. Structure your explanation to show how core components interconnect and generate value. Highlight what makes this business distinctive and defensible, address likely objections, and close with a strong call-to-action.

## Context
Business model: {{business-model}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Key differentiators: {{unique-value-and-advantage}}

Market opportunity: {{market-opportunity}}

## Output
Deliver the pitch in a structured format with:
- Clear section headings
- Concise bullet points under each section
- Logical progression from model explanation → value creation → competitive position → objection handling → call-to-action

Ensure each section flows coherently into the next, making the investment or purchasing case build naturally.
```
