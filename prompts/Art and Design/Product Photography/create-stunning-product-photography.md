# Product Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators

## 簡介

The Product Photography Prompt for AI Image Generators is a free AI prompt that creates studio-quality commercial product images with professional lighting and balanced composition for e-commerce brands, marketers, and content creators. This product photography prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates clean, modern product shots with soft lighting, subtle shadows for depth, and studio-style framing around any item you specify. You define the product and the scene or styling elements - decorative objects, backgrounds, color themes - and the prompt renders a polished image suitable for online stores, social media, or marketing campaigns. It works across Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and other text-to-image models that accept detailed scene descriptions. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, professional product visuals without a physical studio setup or when testing concepts before a full shoot. ● Studio composition with balanced framing that keeps the product the hero of every shot. ● Professional soft lighting with subtle shadows that add dimension and realism. ● Clean modern aesthetic that works for fashion, tech, beauty, home goods, and more. ● Customizable scene and styling variables to match brand guidelines or campaign themes. ## Prompt

```
Product photography of {{product}}, {{scene-and-styling}}, studio composition with balanced framing, clean modern aesthetic, professional soft lighting with subtle shadows for depth
```
