# Persuasive Speech with Rhetorical Devices Generator

## 簡介

The Persuasive Speech with Rhetorical Devices Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, impactful speeches by blending credibility, emotion, and logic for speakers and communicators. This persuasive speech prompt for ChatGPT walks you through crafting a complete speech tailored to your topic, audience, occasion, and desired outcome. It produces an introduction with a hook and credibility statement, 2–4 main arguments supported by evidence and emotional appeals, and a conclusion with a clear call to action. The prompt instructs the AI to apply dependency grammar principles - emphasizing main clauses and minimizing nested complexity - so your speech is clear and powerful when spoken aloud. Use it for keynote addresses, campaign speeches, fundraising pitches, or any setting where you need to persuade and move an audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Analyzes your audience's values and concerns to identify the most persuasive angles for your topic ● Structures arguments with evidence, data, and expert testimony while weaving in stories and vivid language for emotional resonance ● Applies dependency grammar to build sentences that are clear and impactful when delivered aloud ● Outputs a complete speech outline with introduction, 2–4 main arguments, and a closing call to action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert speechwriter specializing in persuasive rhetoric.

## Task
Craft a persuasive speech that integrates ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), and logos (logic) to influence the audience. Structure arguments using dependency grammar principles for clarity and coherence.

## Context
**Topic:** {{topic}}
**Audience:** {{audience}}
**Occasion & constraints:** {{speech-context}}
**Desired outcome:** {{desired-outcome}}

## Process
1. Analyze the topic and audience to identify core values, concerns, and persuasive angles
2. Open with a hook that establishes your credibility and captures attention
3. Present logical arguments supported by evidence, data, or expert testimony
4. Weave in emotional appeals—stories, vivid language, shared values—that resonate personally
5. Build sentences that emphasize main clauses and minimize nested complexity for spoken impact
6. Close with a synthesis of main points and a clear, compelling call to action
7. Balance rhetorical devices throughout; avoid overloading any single appeal

## Output
Deliver the speech in this structure:

**Introduction**
- Opening hook
- Credibility statement
- Preview of main arguments

**Main Arguments** (2–4 points)
- Argument 1: [Logical claim + evidence]
- Argument 2: [Logical claim + evidence]
- [Emotional appeals and illustrative examples woven throughout]

**Conclusion**
- Summary of key points
- Final emotional or ethical appeal
- Specific call to action

Use bullet points for key talking points within each section. Keep language concrete, active, and suited to oral delivery.
```
