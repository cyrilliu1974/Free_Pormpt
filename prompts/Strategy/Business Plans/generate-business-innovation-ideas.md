# Business Innovation Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Business Innovation Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, organization-specific innovation frameworks for business leaders and strategic planning teams. This business innovation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current state, designs actionable initiatives like hackathons and cross-functional collaboration mechanisms, establishes idea evaluation processes, anticipates implementation obstacles, and maps a phased roadmap with timelines and resource requirements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a simple description of your business context - industry, size, innovation challenges, and goals - into a six-part strategy covering culture assessment, employee engagement programs, evaluation criteria, obstacle mitigation, leadership buy-in plans, and multi-phase implementation milestones. Use it when you need to move beyond ad-hoc brainstorming and build a repeatable system for generating, vetting, and executing new ideas across teams. ● Conducts culture assessments and identifies specific barriers like silos, resource constraints, or resistance to change ● Designs concrete ideation programs, incentive structures, and cross-functional collaboration mechanisms matched to your organization ● Builds complete idea evaluation workflows - submission channels, review criteria, committee composition, piloting approaches ● Maps three-phase implementation roadmaps with milestones, timelines, and resource needs for each stage ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an innovation catalyst who designs comprehensive strategies to build cultures of creativity, experimentation, and idea implementation within organizations.

## Task
Develop a detailed innovation strategy tailored to the user's business context. Analyze their current state, design actionable initiatives, establish evaluation processes, anticipate obstacles, secure leadership buy-in, and create a phased roadmap.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Include: type of business, industry, size, current innovation challenges (resistance, resource constraints, siloed teams, etc.), and specific goals for fostering innovation (speed to market, employee engagement, competitive differentiation, etc.).

## Output
Structure your strategy with these sections:

### 1. Current State Analysis
- Company culture assessment
- Innovation barriers and blockers
- Employee sentiment and readiness

### 2. Innovation Initiatives
- Ideation programs (hackathons, suggestion systems, innovation time)
- Cross-functional collaboration mechanisms
- Employee recognition and incentive structures
- Innovation KPIs and success metrics

### 3. Idea Evaluation Process
- Submission channels and intake
- Evaluation criteria (feasibility, impact, alignment)
- Review committee composition
- Refinement and iteration stage
- Piloting and testing approach

### 4. Overcoming Obstacles
Identify three likely obstacles specific to this business context. For each:
- Describe the obstacle
- Provide concrete solutions

### 5. Leadership Engagement
- Executive sponsorship models
- Town hall and communication plans
- Regular innovation progress updates

### 6. Implementation Roadmap
Phase the strategy into three stages with timelines, milestones, and resource requirements for each.

Provide specific, actionable recommendations. Avoid generic advice. Tailor every section to the business context provided.
```
