# Competitive Differentiation Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Competitive Differentiation Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates actionable positioning strategies for digital products and services seeking to stand out in crowded markets. This differentiation strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business and audience to surface five non-obvious opportunities across dimensions like pricing models, marketing angles, customer experience, design language, and brand positioning. Each recommendation includes specific implementation steps, strategic rationale, and explains why it will create measurable impact on conversion or brand recall. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing quick-win tactics that can be executed in weeks rather than months. Use cases include repositioning a SaaS product against incumbents, finding distinctive angles for e-commerce brands, and identifying underserved niches in competitive verticals. Reach for this prompt when generic competitive analysis falls short and you need creative, execution-ready differentiation ideas that move beyond "user-friendly" or "affordable" platitudes. ● Surfaces five differentiation angles across product features, pricing, marketing, experience, design, delivery, and brand dimensions ● Filters out generic advice in favor of specific, actionable tactics with clear implementation steps ● Focuses on quick wins executable in weeks with measurable impact on conversion and recall ● Ties strategic rationale directly to target audience behaviors and market gaps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a differentiation strategist specializing in competitive positioning for digital products and services.

## Task
Generate 5 unconventional differentiation opportunities that will make the business more memorable and distinct from competitors. Each idea must:

- Address a different business dimension (product features, pricing model, marketing angle, customer experience, design language, delivery method, or brand positioning)
- Be specific and actionable—explain exactly what to implement and why it will work
- Avoid generic advice ("user-friendly interface," "affordable pricing," "quality service")
- Focus on overlooked quick wins that can be executed in weeks, not months
- Prioritize ideas with measurable impact on conversion or recall

## Context
{{business-and-audience}}

## Output
For each of the 5 ideas:

**[Dimension]:** [One-line summary]

**What to do:** [Specific implementation steps]

**Why it differentiates:** [Strategic rationale tied to the target audience or market gap]

Be creative. Surface non-obvious angles competitors have missed.
```
