# SEO Content Strategy Planner With Impact Scoring

## 簡介

The SEO Content Strategy Planner With Impact Scoring is a free AI prompt that creates data-driven search optimization roadmaps for brands, agencies, and in-house marketing teams. This SEO content strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand overview and produces five target keywords, a detailed content tactics table with impact scores (1-5) and cost estimates, a publication calendar mapping content types to keywords and deadlines, three link building approaches, and 3-5 technical or distribution recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, prioritizing high-impact tactics relative to implementation cost based on your specific market position and competitive landscape. Use it when you need to move beyond guesswork and build an SEO plan that balances expected results against budget reality. ● Identifies five high-value target keywords tailored to your brand and competitive environment. ● Scores every content tactic on a 1-5 impact scale and pairs it with realistic cost-to-implement estimates. ● Produces a publication calendar that assigns content types, keywords, dates, and status tracking. ● Includes three link building tactics and additional technical or UX recommendations to round out the strategy. ## Prompt

```

{{brand-overview}}

Focus on tactics with the highest impact relative to implementation cost. Prioritize strategies specific to this brand's market position, competitive landscape, and resources.
```
