# Personalized Customer Email Generator

## 簡介

The Personalized Customer Email Generator is a free AI prompt that creates sincere, relationship-focused thank-you emails for loyal customers based on their individual purchase history and engagement. This customer email prompt for ChatGPT transforms basic customer profile data - name, loyalty duration, purchase history - into warm, conversational messages that feel handwritten by a real person on your team. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a compelling subject line and complete email body that references specific customer details naturally, avoiding the generic templated language that undermines retention efforts. Marketing teams, e-commerce brands, and customer success managers use it to scale personalized outreach without sacrificing authenticity, strengthening relationships through thoughtful acknowledgment of individual customer journeys. ● Transforms customer profile data into warm, conversational emails that reference specific purchases and loyalty milestones naturally ● Produces both subject line and complete email body with greeting, personalized message, forward-looking benefit, and team signature ● Maintains a sincere, human tone that avoids generic phrasing and templated language patterns ● Helps marketing and customer success teams scale one-to-one communication without losing the personal touch that drives retention ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert email marketer crafting personalized retention emails that strengthen customer relationships.

## Task

Write a warm, personalized thank-you email to a loyal customer using the details provided in {{customer-profile}}.

## Context

The email should feel like it was written by a real person on your team—sincere, conversational, and genuinely appreciative. Reference specific customer details to demonstrate you value their individual relationship with the brand.

## Requirements

**Tone & Voice:**
- Warm, conversational, and sincere
- Short sentences and plain language
- Avoid generic or templated phrasing

**Personalization:**
- Use the customer's name, loyalty duration, recent purchases, and total purchase count naturally throughout
- Make references feel specific, not formulaic

**Structure:**
1. Compelling subject line
2. Personal greeting
3. Genuine appreciation statement
4. Personalized message connecting their history with your brand
5. Mention of future benefits or what's coming next
6. Warm closing
7. Signature from a real team member

## Input

{{customer-profile}}

## Output

**Subject Line:**  
[Your subject line]

**Email Body:**  
[Complete email from greeting through signature]
```
