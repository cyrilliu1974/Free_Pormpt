# Controversial Brand Positioning Topics Generator

## 簡介

The Controversial Brand Positioning Topics Generator is a free AI prompt that develops bold, mission-driven brand stances designed to build movements rather than mass followings. This brand positioning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your niche, target audience, and desired outcome to identify ten controversial topics where your brand can take a polarizing stand. For each topic, it proposes multiple specific stances supported by clear reasoning and examples, then selects the top three that work together to create a cohesive brand identity. The output includes a manifesto-style impact summary written in declarative, conviction-driven language. Brand strategists, entrepreneurs, and marketing teams use it to differentiate mission-driven businesses in crowded markets by taking principled positions that resonate deeply with core audiences. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Identifies ten controversial topics within your niche with 2-3 specific stances per topic, each aligned with your mission and likely to spark passionate reactions ● Recommends the top three stances that complement each other and most effectively differentiate your brand as a movement ● Produces a manifesto-style summary explaining how these stances convert followers into advocates and repel misaligned audiences ● Ensures every stance is meaningful to your target audience, bold without being gratuitously offensive, and supported by clear reasoning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a brand strategist specializing in polarizing, mission-driven brand identities that cultivate passionate communities over mass appeal.

## Task
Develop a controversial brand positioning strategy that will attract die-hard advocates while deliberately repelling misaligned audiences.

## Context
The brand operates in {{niche}} with a mission to help {{target-audience}} achieve {{desired-outcome}}. The goal is to take bold, principled stances on issues that matter deeply to this audience—even if divisive—to build a movement rather than a generic following.

## Output

### 1. Controversial Topics
Identify 10 topics within {{niche}} where the brand could take a polarizing stand. For each topic, propose 2–3 specific stances that are:
- Aligned with the mission to help {{target-audience}} achieve {{desired-outcome}}
- Meaningful and relevant to {{target-audience}}
- Bold and opinionated without being gratuitously offensive
- Likely to spark passionate agreement and disagreement
- Supported by clear reasoning and examples

### 2. Top 3 Recommended Stances
Select the three stances that best complement each other, paint a cohesive picture of the brand's beliefs, and will most effectively differentiate the brand as a movement.

### 3. Impact Summary (Manifesto Style)
Write a bold, opinionated manifesto summarizing how these stances will:
- Convert followers into passionate advocates
- Repel audiences misaligned with the mission
- Clarify what the brand stands for and why it matters

Use declarative, conviction-driven language that showcases the passion behind the brand's beliefs.
```
