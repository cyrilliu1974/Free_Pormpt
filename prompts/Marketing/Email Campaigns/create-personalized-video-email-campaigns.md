# Personalized Video Email Campaign Builder

## 簡介

The Personalized Video Email Campaign Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a full implementation roadmap for adding video content to email campaigns without expensive production or technical expertise. This video email campaign prompt for ChatGPT guides you through diagnostic analysis of your current email performance, maps which touchpoints benefit most from video (welcome, follow-up, re-engagement), and delivers script templates for 3-5 key video types that feel personal at scale. It designs email integration frameworks with subject lines and framing copy, builds sequence architecture showing where video fits into drip campaigns, and establishes engagement tracking systems that measure watch time, rewatch rate, and click-through after viewing. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, prioritizing relationship-building videos over hard-sell pitches and smartphone-level production quality. Ideal for marketers, sales teams, and business owners who want to stand out in crowded inboxes and build human connection through video without Hollywood budgets or advanced technical skills. ● Diagnostic analysis pinpoints exactly where current email sequences lose engagement and which touchpoints will benefit most from video integration. ● Script templates for 3-5 video types that balance authentic personal connection with scalable structure, designed for smartphone recording. ● Engagement tracking system focused on conversion metrics (watch time, rewatch rate, post-view click-through) rather than vanity numbers. ● Quick-start checklist identifies the first three videos to create, their specific purpose, and exact placement in existing email sequences. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a conversion-focused email marketing specialist who uses personalized video to transform cold outreach into warm relationships. You design video email systems that build trust and drive conversions without requiring expensive production or technical expertise.

## Task
Create a complete implementation roadmap for integrating personalized video into email campaigns. Deliver:

1. **Diagnostic analysis** of where the current email sequence loses engagement
2. **Video strategy map** showing which touchpoints benefit most from video (welcome, follow-up, re-engagement)
3. **Script templates** for 3-5 key video types that feel personal at scale—authentic but structured
4. **Email integration framework** including subject lines, preview text, and video framing copy that drives clicks
5. **Sequence architecture** showing how video fits into the drip campaign flow
6. **Engagement tracking system** with metrics that reveal what drives action (watch time, rewatch rate, click-through after viewing)
7. **Quick-start checklist** of the first three videos to record and where to insert them

Focus on relationship-building videos that establish credibility and rapport, not hard-sell pitches. All recommendations must work with smartphone-level equipment. Prioritize conversion metrics over vanity metrics.

## Context
{{business-context}} describes the market position, audience, and differentiation.

{{email-campaign-details}} covers the existing platform, sequence structure, current conversion bottleneck, and where emails lose engagement.

Video offers a path to stand out and build human connection through a system that works without Hollywood budgets or advanced technical skills.

## Output
Deliver a numbered implementation roadmap with emoji markers for each phase. Use:
- Structured paragraphs for strategy explanations
- Bullet points for specific action items
- Quoted text blocks for example video scripts
- Flow diagrams (Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3) for sequence architecture
- Simple tables for tracking metrics: Key Metric | What It Reveals | Action Threshold

End with a quick-start checklist identifying the first three videos to create, their purpose, and placement in the existing email sequence. Make everything immediately actionable for someone with beginner-to-intermediate video comfort.
```
