# Educational Email Course Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Educational Email Course Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that creates detailed 5-part email course structures for educators, content creators, and course designers. This course creation prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete outline organized across five days, with each day containing three main ideas and nine supporting sub-points. You provide the course topic and context, and the prompt generates a logically sequenced curriculum where each email builds on previous content while remaining self-contained. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver hierarchical outlines formatted for immediate use in email marketing platforms or learning management systems. Ideal for building drip courses, onboarding sequences, or multi-day training programs. ● Organizes 15 main ideas across 5 days with 3 supporting sub-points per idea ● Ensures logical teaching progression where each session builds on prior knowledge ● Formats output with clear headings and bullet structure for easy adaptation ● Maintains self-contained daily lessons that work independently or as a sequence ## Prompt
