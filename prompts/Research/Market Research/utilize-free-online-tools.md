# Free Business Tools Research Prompt for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The Free Business Tools Research Prompt for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that compiles curated lists of no-cost online tools tailored to bootstrapped businesses and solo entrepreneurs. This free business tools prompt for ChatGPT works by adopting the role of an expert business researcher who evaluates SaaS platforms and productivity tools based on their free-tier offerings, ease of use, and immediate value to resource-conscious founders. You provide your business context as a variable, and the prompt delivers a structured markdown comparison table featuring 8-12 highly relevant tools, each annotated with key features available in free plans, pricing tier limits, and scalability notes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for any text-generation workflow. Real use cases include market research for MVP launches, operational planning on zero budget, and competitive analysis of tool categories like email marketing, project management, design, analytics, and customer support. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly identify and compare free software options without wading through affiliate listicles or outdated blog posts. ● Filters tools by true free-forever plans, excluding limited trials and freemium bait-and-switch models ● Outputs a three-column markdown table (Tool Name, Key Features, Pricing) for easy scanning and decision-making ● Tailors recommendations to your specific business context, ensuring relevance to your industry and stage ● Highlights feature completeness in free tiers and scalability paths, so you know what you can build before paying ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business researcher specializing in SaaS tools and bootstrapped business operations.

## Task
Compile a comprehensive, curated list of free online tools suited to solopreneurs and bootstrapped businesses. Research available options, evaluate their key features and pricing models, and organize the findings into a structured comparison table.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Focus on tools that offer genuine free tiers (not just trials), prioritize ease of use, and deliver immediate value to resource-conscious entrepreneurs. Evaluate tools based on their reliability, feature completeness in free plans, and scalability.

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Tool Name | Key Features | Pricing |
|-----------|--------------|----------|

Include 8-12 highly relevant tools. For each:
- **Tool Name**: Official product name with category in parentheses
- **Key Features**: 3-5 most valuable capabilities, emphasizing what's available in the free tier
- **Pricing**: Free tier limits, paid tier starting price if applicable, note if free forever vs. limited trial

Order tools by relevance to the stated business context. Add a brief 2-3 sentence summary above the table explaining your selection criteria.
```
