# Customer Thank-You Flow Designer

## 簡介

The Customer Thank-You Flow Designer is a free AI prompt that builds retention-focused post-purchase communication sequences for e-commerce and service businesses. It produces a complete three-touchpoint system spanning immediate gratitude (Day 0), value-added education (Day 7), and surprise rewards (Day 21), all anchored in the Peak-End Rule to maximize customer recall and loyalty. This customer thank-you flow prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates segmented messaging templates, personalization rules, automation workflows, and ROI tracking frameworks tailored to your product type, customer tiers, and existing tools. Use it when you need to convert first-time buyers into repeat customers through strategic gratitude moments that feel authentic rather than transactional. ● Segments customers by purchase value and behavior, then assigns tier-specific messaging and rewards at psychologically optimal intervals. ● Delivers email subject lines, body copy with merge-field annotations, A/B test variables, and mobile optimization notes for each of the three touchpoints. ● Includes automation platform integration steps, workflow trigger logic, QA checklists, and a KPI dashboard covering open rates, click-throughs, repeat purchase rates, and attributed ROI. ● Provides cost estimates per customer and expected retention lift based on industry benchmarks, so you can forecast budget and business impact before launch. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer experience strategist who designs post-purchase communication flows that strengthen retention through strategic gratitude touchpoints.

## Task
Create a complete customer thank-you flow across three key moments (Day 0, Day 7, Day 21) that leverages the Peak-End Rule to build loyalty. Analyze the business context, design segmented messaging, and provide implementation guidance with measurement frameworks.

## Context
Effective thank-you flows go beyond transactional acknowledgment. They create memorable moments at psychologically optimal intervals, personalize based on customer value tiers, and balance genuine gratitude with strategic engagement.

## Input Required
Provide:

{{business-context}}
- Product/service type and average order value
- Typical customer journey from first to repeat purchase
- Current post-purchase communication (if any)
- Brand voice and tone
- Available email/automation tools

## Output
Deliver a structured thank-you flow system containing:

### 1. Segmentation Framework
- Customer tier definitions based on purchase value and behavior
- Trigger points for each gratitude touchpoint
- Personalization variables per segment

### 2. Three-Touch Timeline

**Day 0 – Immediate Gratitude**
- Subject line options optimized for opens
- Message templates by tier with personalization merge fields
- Emotional hooks and mobile optimization notes
- A/B test variables

**Day 7 – Value Addition**
- Product-specific tips and setup guides
- Usage ideas that reinforce purchase value
- Engagement tracking recommendations
- Community-building elements

**Day 21 – Surprise & Delight**
- Tier-appropriate surprise perks (points, samples, exclusive content)
- Cost-per-customer and fulfillment logistics
- Timing optimization tactics

### 3. Implementation Blueprint
- Email platform integration steps for {{business-context}}
- Workflow trigger configuration
- Personalization token mapping
- QA checklist and launch sequence

### 4. Measurement Framework
- KPIs: open rate, click-through rate, repeat purchase rate, attributed ROI
- Tracking setup for each touchpoint
- A/B testing calendar
- Optimization triggers and scaling strategies

Format all templates as copy-paste ready. Include cost estimates and expected lift in repeat purchase rate based on industry benchmarks.
```
