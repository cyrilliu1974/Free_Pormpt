# Lead Nurturing Email Sequence Builder

## 簡介

The Lead Nurturing Email Sequence Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured email campaigns guiding prospects through the complete buyer's journey for businesses and marketers. This lead nurturing prompt for ChatGPT designs 5-7 emails that progress logically from first contact to purchase readiness. Each message builds on the previous one, addressing stage-specific pain points while naturally incorporating your value proposition. The output is a markdown table showing email number, journey stage, subject line, key content points, and call-to-action, along with timing recommendations between sends. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a cohesive email campaign that balances educational content with persuasive messaging across multiple touchpoints, customized to your business context and audience. ● Maps each email to a specific buyer's journey stage (awareness, consideration, decision) with appropriate messaging ● Delivers a complete table format showing subject lines, content strategy, CTAs, and send-timing recommendations ● Builds narrative continuity so each message references and advances ideas from previous emails ● Balances educational value with commercial intent to maintain engagement without overwhelming prospects ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in lead nurturing campaigns.

## Task
Design a complete email sequence that guides leads from awareness through consideration to decision. Structure each email to build on the previous message, creating a cohesive narrative that progressively educates, engages, and persuades.

## Context
Lead nurturing focus and business details:
{{business-context}}

Target audience:
{{target-audience}}

Unique selling proposition and key product/service features:
{{value-proposition}}

## Output Requirements
For each email in the sequence:
- Align content to the appropriate buyer's journey stage (Awareness → Consideration → Decision)
- Address specific pain points relevant to that stage
- Showcase value proposition elements naturally
- Progress the narrative with each message building on prior emails
- Include optimal timing recommendations between emails
- Balance engagement with appropriate sending frequency

Deliver your strategy as a **markdown table** with these columns:
- Email Number
- Buyer's Journey Stage
- Subject Line
- Key Content Points
- Call-to-Action

Include 5-7 emails spanning the full journey from first touch to purchase readiness.
```
