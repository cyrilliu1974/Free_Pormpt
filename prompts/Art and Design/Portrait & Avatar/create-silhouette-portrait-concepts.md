# Silhouette Portrait Generator for Fashion Photography

## 簡介

The Silhouette Portrait Generator for Fashion Photography is a free AI prompt that creates minimalist, backlit portrait concepts with sharp silhouettes and soft bokeh backgrounds for photographers, designers, and creative directors. This silhouette portrait prompt for Midjourney produces low-angle close-up compositions with warm golden-hour lighting, high contrast, and vibrant background colors. You specify the subject description, background color, and aspect ratio, and the prompt renders an 85mm lens perspective with the subject in sharp focus against soft blur. Fashion stylists use it for lookbook concepts, social media managers for editorial-style profile imagery, and graphic designers for poster and branding assets that demand bold visual impact. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana. Reach for this prompt when you need clean, dramatic silhouette photography that emphasizes form and lighting over detail, ideal for minimalist fashion campaigns or striking avatar concepts. ● Produces low-angle close-up silhouettes with warm backlit lighting that mimics golden-hour outdoor shoots. ● Delivers high-contrast, vibrant-color portraits where the subject is in sharp focus and the background dissolves into soft bokeh. ● Emulates 85mm lens compression and minimalist fashion photography aesthetics for editorial and social use. ● Lets you control subject description, background color, and aspect ratio to match brand or campaign requirements. ## Prompt

```
Silhouette portrait of a {{subject-description}} against a {{background-color}} background; low-angle close-up, minimalist fashion photography. Warm backlit lighting, high contrast, vibrant colors; silhouette in sharp focus with soft bokeh behind the subject; minimal design. Shot with 85mm lens at golden hour. --ar {{aspect-ratio}}
```
