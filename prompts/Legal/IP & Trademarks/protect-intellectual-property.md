# Intellectual Property Protection Guide for Freelancers

## 簡介

The Intellectual Property Protection Guide for Freelancers is a free AI prompt that produces tailored legal guidance on safeguarding creative work, inventions, and business ideas for solopreneurs and independent professionals. This intellectual property prompt for ChatGPT generates a bullet-point guide covering copyrights, trademarks, patents, and trade secrets, with specific advice on client contracts, portfolio usage, and online presence. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting five variables - industry, offerings, target clients, location, and current IP concerns - to deliver contextualized strategies. Freelancers can use it to understand how to register trademarks for service marks, include protective clauses in client agreements, assert ownership over deliverables, and prevent misuse of portfolio samples. The output addresses the real-world challenges solo entrepreneurs face when balancing collaboration with clients and protecting proprietary methods or creative assets. ● Explains copyright registration, work-for-hire clauses, and licensing terms in client contracts ● Covers trademark basics for service names, logos, and brand identities ● Offers patent and trade-secret strategies for proprietary methods or inventions ● Provides portfolio and online-presence guidance to prevent unauthorized use of your work ## Prompt

```

Address the unique challenges solopreneurs face, including client contracts, portfolio usage, and online presence. Offer practical strategies for safeguarding creative work, inventions, and business ideas throughout the freelancing process.

## Context
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Primary services/products: {{offerings}}
- Target client base: {{target-clients}}
- Geographical location: {{location}}
- Current IP concerns: {{ip-concerns}}

## Output
Deliver your guidance as a bullet point list.
```
