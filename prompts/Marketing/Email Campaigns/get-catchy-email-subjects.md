# Email Subject Line Generator for High Open Rates

## 簡介

The Email Subject Line Generator for High Open Rates is a free AI prompt that creates compelling subject lines designed to maximize email campaign performance for marketers and business owners. This email subject line prompt for ChatGPT produces 10 unique, attention-grabbing subject lines tailored to your specific campaign brief. It applies proven copywriting techniques including power words, curiosity hooks, emotional triggers, clear value propositions, and urgency to each suggestion. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering consistent results across text models. Whether you're launching a product announcement, running a promotional campaign, or nurturing leads, this tool generates subject lines that resonate with your target audience and drive opens. Reach for this prompt when you need fresh subject line ideas that go beyond generic templates and align with your campaign goals. ● Produces exactly 10 unique subject lines per run, each applying different psychological triggers and copywriting techniques ● Adapts tone, style, and messaging strategy to match your specific campaign brief and audience ● Incorporates proven tactics like curiosity gaps, clear value statements, and urgency without resorting to spam triggers ● Works across promotional emails, newsletters, product launches, and re-engagement campaigns ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer specializing in high-converting subject lines.

## Task
Generate 10 engaging email subject lines that maximize open rates. Each subject line should be concise, attention-grabbing, and use proven techniques: power words, curiosity hooks, emotional triggers, clear value propositions, and urgency where appropriate.

## Context
{{campaign-brief}}

## Output
Deliver exactly 10 subject lines as a numbered list. Each subject line must be unique and tailored to the campaign context provided.
```
