# Cutting Phase Meal Plan Prompt for Fat Loss

## 簡介

The Cutting Phase Meal Plan Prompt for Fat Loss is a free AI prompt that creates personalized 7-day nutrition plans for athletes and fitness enthusiasts aiming to lose fat while maintaining muscle. This cutting phase meal plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your weight, height, age, activity level, and dietary restrictions to build a complete week of meals with precise macronutrient breakdowns, portion sizes, and ingredient substitutions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver meal timing strategies around workouts, hydration targets, supplement recommendations, and progress tracking methods. Whether you're a bodybuilder preparing for competition or someone refining body composition, the prompt structures calorie-controlled plans that prioritize lean protein, complex carbs, and healthy fats while keeping meals practical and sustainable. This prompt is for anyone entering a cutting phase who needs a science-backed nutrition framework that preserves muscle while creating the caloric deficit required for fat loss. ● Delivers a complete 7-day meal plan with breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks tailored to your stats and dietary preferences. ● Provides macronutrient breakdowns in table format for every meal to ensure adequate protein intake and caloric control. ● Includes meal timing recommendations around workouts, batch prep tips, portion adjustment guidelines, and progress monitoring strategies. ● Offers ingredient substitutions for dietary flexibility and options for both omnivore and vegetarian eating patterns. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a certified nutritionist and fitness expert specializing in body composition optimization and meal planning for athletes and fitness enthusiasts.

## Task
Create a comprehensive 7-day cutting phase meal plan designed for effective fat loss while preserving muscle mass.

## Context
User stats and preferences:
{{user-profile}}
(Include: current weight, goal weight, height, age, gender, activity level, and any dietary restrictions or preferences)

## Output

### Introduction
Begin with a brief explanation of why a well-structured meal plan is critical during a cutting phase.

### 7-Day Meal Plan
For each day, provide:

#### Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snacks
- Specific food items with portion sizes and cooking methods
- Macronutrient breakdown (protein, carbohydrates, fats) in table format
- *Ingredient substitutions* for dietary flexibility

### Guidelines

**Meal Prep & Timing**
- Tips for batch preparation and storage
- Optimal meal timing around workouts for performance and recovery

**Portion Adjustments**
- How to scale portions based on individual caloric needs and activity levels

**Hydration & Supplements**
- Daily water intake recommendations
- Evidence-based supplement guidance if applicable

**Progress Monitoring**
- How to track results and adjust the plan as needed

### Plan Principles
Ensure the meal plan:
- Is calorie-controlled for fat loss with adequate nutrition
- Prioritizes lean protein sources for muscle preservation
- Includes complex carbohydrates for sustained energy and fiber
- Incorporates moderate healthy fats for hormone function
- Features nutrient-dense whole foods for satiety and micronutrients
- Avoids processed foods, added sugars, and excessive sodium
- Includes diverse fruits and vegetables for vitamins and minerals
- Offers options for both omnivores and vegetarians
- Remains practical and sustainable for long-term adherence
```
