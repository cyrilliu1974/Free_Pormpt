# Referral Network Builder for Go-to-Market Strategy

## 簡介

The Referral Network Builder for Go-to-Market Strategy is a free AI prompt that creates actionable plans for identifying, engaging, and managing referral partners across any business context. This referral network prompt for ChatGPT produces a four-pillar strategic framework: partner qualification criteria and sourcing channels, relationship development stages with communication cadences, performance tracking systems with tiered reward structures, and a 90-day implementation roadmap with specific milestones. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using dependency grammar principles to ensure each recommendation builds logically on the previous step. Business strategists use it to design systematic approaches to partnership ecosystems, from initial outreach through mature advocacy relationships, with concrete metrics like referral volume, conversion rate, and partner lifetime value. Reach for this prompt when launching a partner program, expanding distribution channels, or systematizing informal referral relationships into a measurable growth engine. ● Defines qualification criteria for high-value referral partners based on audience overlap, complementary services, and shared values ● Maps progressive engagement stages from first contact through advocacy, with specific touchpoint schedules and trust-building tactics ● Designs tracking systems and tiered reward structures that incentivize sustained referral activity ● Delivers 30/60/90-day implementation roadmaps with resource allocation, risk mitigation, and success metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business strategist specializing in referral network development and partnership ecosystems.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, actionable strategic plan for building a strong referral network. The plan must cover three core pillars: identifying high-value referral partners, nurturing long-term relationships, and implementing a tracking and reward system that drives sustained engagement.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Structure your recommendations using dependency grammar principles—each action should build logically on the previous step, creating a clear pathway from initial outreach through mature partnership.

## Output

Deliver the strategic plan in this structure:

### 1. Referral Partner Identification Strategy
- Criteria for qualifying potential partners (complementary services, shared values, audience overlap)
- Sourcing channels and discovery methods
- Initial vetting process
- Specific examples based on the business context provided

### 2. Relationship Development Framework
- First contact approach and value proposition
- Progressive engagement stages (introduction → collaboration → advocacy)
- Communication cadence and touchpoint schedule
- Trust-building tactics and reciprocity mechanisms

### 3. Tracking & Reward System
- Key performance indicators (referral volume, conversion rate, partner lifetime value)
- Technology and tools for attribution tracking
- Tiered reward structure with specific incentives
- Performance review cycle and optimization process

### 4. Implementation Roadmap
- 30/60/90-day milestones
- Resource allocation based on available budget and capabilities
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Success metrics and reporting dashboard

For each section, provide concrete, measurable actions with specific examples relevant to the business type and target audience. Include baseline metrics and growth targets where applicable.
```
