# Audience Segment Expansion Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Audience Segment Expansion Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies specific, actionable audience segments representing scale opportunities for founders, marketers, and product teams. This audience segmentation prompt for ChatGPT takes your business description and current target audience, then produces a structured markdown table with five niche segments - not broad categories like "Entrepreneurs," but precise groups like "B2B SaaS Founders" or "DTC E-commerce Owners." Each segment includes a clear explanation of product fit and concrete pivot recommendations to serve that audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it easy to explore new markets and diversify your customer base without guesswork. Use this prompt when you've saturated your current audience, need fresh go-to-market angles, or want to validate expansion hypotheses before investing in new positioning. ● Produces five distinct, specific audience segments - not generic categories - that represent genuine scale opportunities. ● Explains why each segment is a strong product fit in one clear sentence. ● Provides concrete, actionable pivot recommendations to tailor your offer for each new segment. ● Returns results in a markdown table format that is easy to share with teams, integrate into strategy documents, or refine further. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in audience segmentation and expansion strategy.

## Task
Generate 5 distinct, promising audience segments that represent scale opportunities beyond the current target audience. Each segment must be specific and niched-down—not broad categories. For example, instead of "Entrepreneurs," suggest "B2B SaaS Founders," "DTC E-commerce Owners," or "Digital Creators focused on Coaching."

Each segment should be genuinely different from the others while still being a strong fit for the product.

## Context
{{business-description}}

{{current-target-audience}}

## Output Requirements
Return a markdown table with three columns:

| Segment Name | Why It Fits | How to Pivot |
|--------------|-------------|---------------|

**Segment Name**: 1–3 words, specific and actionable.

**Why It Fits**: One sentence explaining why this segment is a good match for the product.

**How to Pivot**: One sentence describing what product or offer adjustments would better serve this segment compared to the current audience. Make it concrete and actionable.

Ensure all segments are relevant to current market conditions and avoid outdated categories.
```
