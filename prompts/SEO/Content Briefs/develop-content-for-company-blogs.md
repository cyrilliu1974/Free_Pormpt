# Company Blog Content Strategy and Calendar Builder

## 簡介

The Company Blog Content Strategy and Calendar Builder is a free AI prompt that creates structured, quarter-long blog content plans for businesses and content teams. This content strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces a markdown-formatted calendar with publishing dates, post titles, primary keywords, and funnel-stage mapping, alongside detailed blog post ideas that include target keywords, search intent, key takeaways, and differentiated content angles. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for content strategists, marketing managers, and blog editors who need to plan multiple posts at once with SEO and audience relevance in mind. The prompt also delivers a keyword strategy table ranking terms by search volume tier and a set of recurring content themes that align editorial output with business objectives. ● Outputs a markdown table calendar with publish dates, titles, keywords, themes, and funnel stages ● Provides detailed blog post briefs including search intent, takeaways, and competitor differentiation angles ● Identifies 8-10 target keywords segmented by search volume tier and mapped to content ● Defines 3-4 recurring content themes that tie posts to business goals and audience interests ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist developing a comprehensive blog content strategy.

## Task
Create a structured content strategy that includes:
- A quarterly content calendar with publishing dates, topics, and keyword focus
- Compelling blog post ideas tailored to the target audience's needs and search intent
- Strategic keyword integration that balances SEO value with natural readability
- Content themes that establish thought leadership and address audience pain points

## Context
Company & Industry: {{company-and-industry}}
Target Audience: {{target-audience}}
Number of blog posts to plan: {{post-count}}

## Output
Structure your strategy with:

### Content Calendar
Present as a markdown table with columns: Week | Publish Date | Blog Post Title | Primary Keyword | Content Theme | Target Funnel Stage

### Blog Post Ideas
For each post idea, provide:
- **Title** (optimized for click-through)
- **Target keyword** and search intent
- **Key takeaways** (3-4 bullet points)
- **Content angle** that differentiates from competitor coverage

### Keyword Strategy
List 8-10 target keywords with search volume tier (high/medium/long-tail) and content mapping

### Content Themes
Identify 3-4 recurring themes that align with business goals and audience interests

Ensure all writing is clear, actionable, and appropriate for the specified audience's expertise level.
```
