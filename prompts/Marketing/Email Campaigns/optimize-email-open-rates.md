# Email Open Rate Optimization Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Email Open Rate Optimization Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds data-driven email marketing strategies to increase subscriber engagement and open rates for businesses of any size. This email optimization prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your audience demographics, designs segmentation frameworks based on your customer data, and delivers personalized subject line formulas optimized for 6–10 words and 40–50 characters. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce actionable strategies that include personalization tactics for each subscriber segment, power-word recommendations, urgency techniques, and a structured 4-week A/B testing roadmap covering subject lines, send times, and preview text. Use it when your current open rates fall short of goals or when launching new email campaigns that require targeted messaging across diverse audience segments. ● Builds audience segmentation strategies using your actual customer data points to target emails more precisely ● Generates 3–5 subject line formulas with optimal length, personalization tokens, and urgency elements for your audience ● Designs a 4-week A/B testing roadmap with sample size requirements and iteration schedules ● Delivers personalization frameworks that tailor content to behavioral and demographic insights ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketing strategist specializing in open-rate optimization through audience segmentation, personalization, and compelling subject lines.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, actionable email marketing strategy that improves open rates. Deliver:

- **Audience analysis**: demographic and behavioral insights based on available data
- **Segmentation strategy**: how to group subscribers using the customer data points provided
- **Personalization framework**: tactics for tailoring content to each segment
- **Subject line guidelines**: optimal length (6–10 words / 40–50 characters), power words, personalization tokens, and urgency techniques
- **A/B testing plan**: variables to test (subject lines, send time, preview text), sample size requirements, and iteration cadence

## Context
**Business type**: {{business-type}}
**Target audience**: {{target-audience}}
**Current open rate**: {{current-open-rate}}
**Email marketing goals**: {{goals}}
**Available customer data**: {{customer-data}}

## Output
Structure your strategy with clear headings and bullet points for each component. Prioritize recommendations that address the gap between the current open rate and stated goals. Include 3–5 specific subject line formulas tailored to the audience, and a 4-week testing roadmap.
```
