# Sales Strategy Generator for Product Launch & Growth

## 簡介

The Sales Strategy Generator for Product Launch & Growth is a free AI prompt that creates five comprehensive, tailored sales strategies based on your specific product, market conditions, and performance baseline. This sales strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your product-market context and current budget to deliver five distinct approaches - each with different channel mixes, customer segments, or timing strategies. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured markdown table that breaks down each strategy into numbered implementation steps and quantifiable expected results tied directly to your baseline performance data. Real-world use cases include SaaS product launches, e-commerce expansion planning, B2B market entry, and sales team realignment around new customer segments. This prompt is built for sales directors, marketing strategists, founders, and growth teams who need multiple vetted pathways forward rather than a single prescriptive plan. ● Produces five unique strategies that vary by channel, segment, and timing so you can compare trade-offs ● Delivers numbered implementation steps for each strategy, not vague advice ● Ties expected results to your actual baseline performance and budget, ensuring realistic projections ● Considers both digital and traditional marketing channels appropriate to your industry context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sales strategist specializing in data-driven go-to-market planning.

## Task
Generate 5 comprehensive sales strategies tailored to the product, market, and constraints provided. Each strategy must be actionable, account for relevant marketing channels and consumer behavior, and include clear implementation steps with measurable expected outcomes.

## Context
{{product-and-market}}

Current baseline: {{current-performance-and-budget}}

## Requirements
- Strategies should be innovative yet practical for the specified industry
- Consider both digital and traditional channels where appropriate
- Account for the stated budget and performance baseline
- Each strategy must be distinct in approach (e.g., channel mix, customer segment, timing)

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns:

| Strategy | Implementation | Expected Results |
|----------|----------------|------------------|

Provide at least 5 strategies. In the Implementation column, break down the approach into numbered steps. In Expected Results, specify quantifiable outcomes (conversion rates, revenue targets, customer acquisition, etc.) tied to the baseline performance data.
```
