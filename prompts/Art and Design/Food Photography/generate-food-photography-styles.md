# Food Photography Style Generator

## 簡介

The Food Photography Style Generator is a free AI prompt from God of Permit that creates professional close-up food images with natural daylight and commercial styling for restaurants, food bloggers, and culinary brands. This food photography prompt for Midjourney produces high-end commercial shots at a 45-degree angle with shallow depth of field, vibrant colors, and appetizing presentation. You customize three variables: your food item, the garnish and plating style, and the surface or background setting. The prompt works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models to deliver restaurant-quality food visuals without a camera or studio setup.
