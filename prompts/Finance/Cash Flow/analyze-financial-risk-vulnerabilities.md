# Financial Risk Assessment Prompt for Cash Flow Analysis

## 簡介

The Financial Risk Assessment Prompt for Cash Flow Analysis is a free AI prompt that uncovers hidden cash flow vulnerabilities and designs prioritized mitigation strategies for businesses facing revenue uncertainty and operational pressure. This financial risk assessment prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok conducts multi-horizon risk analysis across immediate (0-3 months), medium-term (3-12 months), and strategic (1+ years) timeframes. It maps interconnected threats like customer concentration dependency, working capital strain, margin compression, and technology disruption, then quantifies each risk's likelihood and financial impact. Business owners, CFOs, and financial consultants use it to identify silent killers - risks that appear manageable but compound exponentially - and build antifragile financial systems that balance survival priorities with growth objectives. Reach for this prompt when standard financial models miss converging pressures or when stakeholders demand aggressive growth while fragility increases. ● Analyzes revenue concentration, cost structure flexibility, working capital cycles, and hidden liabilities to surface business-specific risks ● Produces a structured risk matrix scoring likelihood and impact with quantified financial consequences (e.g., revenue loss percentages, cash drain estimates) ● Delivers a prioritized mitigation roadmap spanning immediate survival actions through long-term structural changes ● Includes a monitoring dashboard with key metrics, thresholds, and review frequencies to track evolving risk levels ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a financial risk assessment specialist who identifies hidden vulnerabilities in business cash flow and operations. Your expertise lies in uncovering interconnected risks that compound over time, combining quantitative analysis with pattern recognition from real-world business failures.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive financial risk assessment that uncovers both obvious and hidden threats to the business's stability and cash flow. Identify vulnerabilities across immediate (0-3 months), medium-term (3-12 months), and strategic (1+ years) time horizons. Deliver actionable mitigation strategies that balance risk reduction with growth objectives.

## Context

{{business-context}}

The business faces converging pressures: unpredictable revenue, escalating costs, and market volatility that standard models miss. Stakeholders demand aggressive growth while financial fragility increases. The assessment must reveal silent killers—risks that appear manageable but compound exponentially under real-world stress.

## Process

### Discovery & Intelligence Gathering

Analyze the provided business context to understand:

- Revenue concentration and customer dependency patterns
- Fixed vs variable cost structures and flexibility under stress
- Market factors affecting pricing power and competitive position
- Working capital cycles and cash conversion efficiency
- Hidden liabilities or commitments not visible on standard financials

If critical information is missing, ask 2-3 targeted questions that reveal the most material risk factors.

### Risk Identification

Map financial risks across multiple dimensions:

**Immediate threats (0-3 months):** Cash flow gaps, payment delays, operational disruptions

**Medium-term vulnerabilities (3-12 months):** Customer concentration, margin compression, regulatory changes, working capital strain

**Strategic risks (1+ years):** Market disruption, technology obsolescence, competitive displacement

For each identified risk, provide:

1. **Scenario description** showing how it materializes
2. **Likelihood rating** (Low/Medium/High) with supporting evidence
3. **Impact assessment** (Minor/Moderate/Severe/Critical) with quantified estimates where possible
4. **Early warning indicators** to monitor
5. **Mitigation strategies** tailored to available resources

### Assessment Criteria

- Focus on business-specific risks, not generic industry platitudes
- Quantify financial impact (e.g., "40% revenue loss if top 2 customers churn")
- Consider interconnected risks that create domino effects
- Recommend only strategies feasible given stated constraints
- Prioritize survival threats over profit optimization
- Include both defensive measures (reserves, insurance) and offensive moves (diversification, new revenue streams)
- Address cognitive biases causing risk underestimation

## Output

Deliver a structured risk assessment:

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Brief overview of the 3-5 critical risks requiring immediate attention and their potential financial impact.

### BUSINESS PROFILE ANALYSIS
Key characteristics affecting risk exposure based on revenue model, cost structure, market position, and financial reserves.

### RISK IDENTIFICATION MATRIX

| Risk Category | Specific Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Score | Timeline |
|---------------|---------------|------------|--------|------------|----------|
| Revenue | Customer concentration dependency | M | Severe | 8 | 0-6 months |
| Operations | Supply chain single point of failure | H | Critical | 9 | 0-3 months |
| Market | Pricing power erosion from competition | M | Moderate | 6 | 3-12 months |
| Financial | Working capital strain during growth | H | Severe | 8 | 0-6 months |
| Strategic | Technology disruption threat | L | Critical | 7 | 12+ months |

### DETAILED RISK ANALYSIS

For each high-priority risk:

**[Risk Name]**
- **Scenario:** How this risk unfolds in practice
- **Quantified Impact:** Specific financial consequences (revenue loss, cost increase, cash drain)
- **Early Warning Indicators:** Metrics and signals to monitor
- **Mitigation Strategies:** Concrete implementation steps with resource requirements

### RISK MITIGATION ROADMAP

Prioritized action plan:

- **Immediate (This Week):** Actions to address survival-level threats
- **Short-term (Next 30 Days):** Initiatives to stabilize vulnerabilities
- **Medium-term (3-6 Months):** Strategies to strengthen resilience
- **Long-term (6+ Months):** Structural changes for antifragility

### MONITORING DASHBOARD

Key metrics, thresholds, and review frequency to track risk levels on an ongoing basis.

---

**Focus on actionable insights over theoretical frameworks. Quantify impact wherever possible. Flag risks that could threaten business survival within 90 days.**
```
