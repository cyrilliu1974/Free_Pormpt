# Social Media Profile Optimization Strategy Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Profile Optimization Strategy Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive profile optimization plans tailored to your business, audience, and active platforms. This social media optimization prompt for ChatGPT guides you through auditing your current profiles, crafting keyword-rich bio copy that fits character limits, aligning visual identity across platforms, and leveraging native features like Instagram Highlights or LinkedIn featured sections. It produces a structured, platform-by-platform strategy document that addresses bio headlines, calls-to-action, content pillars, and performance metrics. Use it when launching a new brand presence, rebranding existing accounts, or diagnosing why your profiles aren't converting visitors into followers and customers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Audits current profile strengths and identifies missed optimization opportunities on each platform ● Delivers bio copy and headlines optimized for character limits with naturally integrated search keywords ● Recommends visual identity guidelines for profile pictures, cover images, and branding consistency ● Maps platform-specific features to business goals, from pinned posts to link-in-bio strategies ● Defines key performance metrics and optimization cycles for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist specializing in profile optimization for brand visibility and conversion.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media profile optimization strategy that increases discoverability and communicates value across platforms. Deliver platform-specific recommendations covering bio copy, keyword integration, visual branding, and engagement tactics.

## Context
**Business & Brand:**
{{business-and-brand}}
(Describe your business, brand voice, and unique selling points)

**Target Audience:**
{{target-audience}}
(Demographics, psychographics, pain points, and platform behavior)

**Active Platforms:**
{{platforms}}
(List which social media platforms you use or plan to optimize)

## Output
Provide a structured optimization plan organized by platform, with each section including:

**Profile Audit**
- Current strengths and gaps in profile setup
- Missed optimization opportunities

**Bio & Headline**
- Platform-optimized bio copy (respecting character limits)
- Relevant keywords naturally integrated for search
- Clear value proposition and call-to-action

**Visual Identity**
- Profile picture and cover image recommendations
- Branding consistency guidelines

**Platform-Specific Features**
- Highlights, pinned posts, link strategies, or native tools to leverage
- Content pillars that align with business goals

**Performance Tracking**
- Key metrics to monitor
- Optimization cycle recommendations

Format each platform as a main heading with the subpoints above. Ensure all recommendations are immediately actionable.
```
