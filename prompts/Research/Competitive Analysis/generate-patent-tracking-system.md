# Patent Tracking System Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Patent Tracking System Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates a comprehensive, systematic process for monitoring, analyzing, and reporting on patents and innovations relevant to your industry and competitors. This patent tracking prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a structured workflow covering relevant patent databases (USPTO, EPO, WIPO, etc.), supplementary data sources, specialized search and analysis tools, key search parameters, data capture methods, categorization frameworks, and reporting formats. You provide your industry and competitive landscape; the AI delivers a complete end-to-end system including a process flow diagram and credible references. IP professionals, competitive intelligence teams, and R&D strategists use it to establish rigorous tracking protocols that capture emerging technologies, monitor competitor filings, and surface actionable innovation insights. ● Covers major patent databases, supplementary sources, and specialized analysis tools suited for patent data ● Defines search parameters, capture workflows, and categorization schemes that ensure comprehensive coverage ● Provides reporting methods and process flow diagrams that translate patent data into strategic intelligence ● Includes credible references to support methodology and validate best practices ## Prompt
