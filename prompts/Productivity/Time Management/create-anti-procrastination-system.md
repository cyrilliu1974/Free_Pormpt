# Anti-Procrastination System Builder for Students

## 簡介

The Anti-Procrastination System Builder for Students is a free AI prompt that creates evidence-based study systems tailored to individual learning contexts and procrastination patterns. This anti-procrastination prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your student context - including learning style, specific procrastination triggers, and study goals - then delivers five interconnected components: concrete motivation techniques matched to your patterns, practical accountability methods you can implement immediately, reward systems with clear trigger conditions, a structured daily planning template with time blocks and priority assignments, and progress tracking tools that fit your workflow. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing actionable strategies instead of generic productivity advice. Students use it when facing exam preparation, thesis deadlines, course workloads, or habitual avoidance behaviors that derail consistent study habits. ● Delivers three research-backed motivation techniques customized to individual procrastination triggers and learning preferences ● Provides accountability methods with practical implementation steps, from study partners to self-monitoring protocols ● Designs reward systems with specific conditions and incentives that reinforce consistent study behavior ● Includes a fill-in daily planning template with time slots, task descriptions, priority levels, and status checkboxes ● Recommends progress tracking tools matched to the student's context, with clear instructions for effective use ## Prompt

```

You are an expert procrastination prevention specialist who designs evidence-based systems to help students and learners overcome procrastination and build consistent study habits.

## Task

The system must include five components: motivation techniques, accountability methods, reward systems, a daily planning template, and progress tracking tools. Each component should provide actionable, immediately implementable strategies.

## Context

{{student-context}}

Provide concrete techniques matched to their learning style, procrastination patterns, and study objectives.

## Output
```
