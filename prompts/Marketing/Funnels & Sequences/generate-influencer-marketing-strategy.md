# Influencer Marketing Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Marketing Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds a complete influencer partnership strategy tailored to your product, target audiences, and distribution channels. This influencer marketing strategy prompt for ChatGPT produces an executive summary, audience analysis, influencer selection criteria (reach, relevance, resonance), engagement models, content themes, distribution tactics, measurable KPIs, risk mitigation plans, and a phased execution roadmap. You provide your product or service, target audiences, and key channels, and the prompt returns a structured strategy document ready to guide real campaigns. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need a data-driven framework for selecting influencers, crafting narratives, and measuring campaign impact - whether you're launching a new product, expanding into new markets, or scaling your influencer partnerships. ● Analyzes each target audience segment by demographics, psychographics, and trusted content sources ● Defines influencer selection criteria across reach, relevance, and engagement authenticity ● Proposes 2–3 collaboration models (sponsored content, affiliate, ambassadorship) with pros, cons, and use cases ● Develops 3–4 content themes aligned with your value proposition and audience interests ● Maps platform-specific distribution tactics and sets 3–5 measurable KPIs (awareness, engagement, conversion) ● Includes risk assessment for reputation issues, content misalignment, and regulatory compliance ● Delivers a phased execution roadmap with timelines, milestones, and success criteria ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced influencer marketing strategist specializing in leveraging third-party credibility to drive product adoption and growth.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive influencer marketing strategy document that outlines how to partner with relevant influencers, craft compelling narratives, and measure campaign impact.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}

Target Audiences: {{target-audiences}}

Key Distribution Channels: {{distribution-channels}}

## Output
Provide a structured strategy document with the following components:

**Executive Summary**
Overview of the influencer marketing strategy, strategic rationale, and expected outcomes.

**Target Audience Analysis**
For each audience segment provided, identify:
- Demographics (age, location, income, education)
- Psychographics (values, interests, lifestyle, pain points)
- Key Influences (trusted voices, platforms, content types)

**Influencer Selection Criteria**
- Reach: audience size and growth metrics
- Relevance: alignment with brand values and audience interests
- Resonance: engagement rates and authenticity indicators

**Influencer Engagement Models**
Describe 2-3 collaboration approaches (e.g., sponsored content, affiliate partnerships, brand ambassadorships), including:
- Description of the model
- Pros and cons for this product/service
- Recommended use cases

**Content Themes and Narratives**
Develop 3-4 compelling content themes that align with the product/service value proposition and resonate with target audiences.

**Distribution Channels**
Detail how influencer content will be amplified across the provided channels, including platform-specific tactics.

**Success Metrics**
Define 3-5 measurable KPIs with specific targets:
- Awareness metrics (impressions, reach)
- Engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares, saves)
- Conversion metrics (click-through rates, conversions, ROI)

**Risk Assessment and Mitigation**
Identify 2-3 potential risks (e.g., influencer reputation issues, content misalignment, regulatory compliance) and provide mitigation strategies for each.

**Execution Roadmap**
Outline 3-4 implementation phases with:
- Phase description and key activities
- Timeline and milestones
- Success criteria for phase completion
```
