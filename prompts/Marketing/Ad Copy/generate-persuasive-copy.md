# PASTOR Framework Persuasive Copy Generator

## 簡介

The PASTOR Framework Persuasive Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that writes structured marketing copy for businesses, marketers, and copywriters who need to convert problem-aware audiences into customers. This persuasive copy prompt for ChatGPT walks through the proven six-step PASTOR sequence: identifying the core problem your audience faces, amplifying the stakes of inaction, presenting your solution, describing the transformation it creates, making a benefit-led offer, and closing with a direct call-to-action. You provide the content type (email, landing page, ad, sales letter), target audience, and solution; the prompt returns labeled sections with plain-language copy that moves logically from awareness to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using storytelling, concrete analogies, and emotional grounding to sharpen the argument without jargon. Reach for this prompt when you need conversion-focused copy that addresses pain points, builds urgency, and guides readers to a single next step. ● Returns six labeled sections (Problem, Amplify, Solution, Transformation, Offer, Response) that build a complete persuasive argument. ● Guides the reader through a psychological journey from pain awareness to commitment, following a proven conversion framework. ● Uses plain language, storytelling, and analogies to make abstract benefits concrete and emotionally resonant. ● Outputs copy suitable for landing pages, email sequences, ads, sales letters, and any content type where conversion is the goal. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write persuasive copy using the PASTOR framework to move a problem-aware audience toward action.

## Context

- Content type: {{content-type}}
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Solution: {{solution}}

## Task

Write persuasive copy that follows the PASTOR framework in sequence:

1. **Problem** – Identify the core pain point {{target-audience}} is facing.
2. **Amplify** – Heighten the stakes by showing what happens if the problem remains unsolved.
3. **Solution** – Present {{solution}} as the direct answer to that problem.
4. **Transformation** – Describe the specific before/after shift {{solution}} creates.
5. **Offer** – State a clear, benefit-led offer with tangible value.
6. **Response** – Close with a single, direct call-to-action that tells the reader exactly what to do next.

Give each sentence its own line. Move logically from one section to the next. Use storytelling, concrete analogies, and emotional grounding where they sharpen the argument. Keep language plain and jargon-free.

## Output

Problem:
[Problem statement]

Amplify:
[Amplification of the problem]

Solution:
[Presentation of the solution]

Transformation:
[Description of the transformation]

Offer:
[Compelling offer]

Response:
[Call-to-action]
```
