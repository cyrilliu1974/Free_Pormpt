# Social Media Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a tailored social media marketing plan to enhance brand visibility, increase engagement, and drive conversions across multiple platforms. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business overview and outputs a detailed, actionable strategy document covering platform recommendations, content calendars, engagement tactics, paid advertising guidance, influencer partnership frameworks, and KPI tracking systems. It works by combining audience analysis with platform-specific features (Instagram Reels, LinkedIn Articles, Twitter Threads, TikTok content) to create a balanced content mix of educational posts, product showcases, user-generated content, and interactive elements. Marketing teams, social media managers, and business owners use it to replace guesswork with a structured strategy that adapts to algorithm changes and business growth. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. ● Analyzes target demographics and behavior patterns to recommend the most effective platforms (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter) with clear rationale ● Designs a content strategy with posting cadence, content themes, engagement tactics like polls and Q&A sessions, and optimal timing based on platform analytics ● Provides paid advertising frameworks with audience segmentation guidance and influencer partnership criteria aligned to brand values ● Establishes a performance measurement system tracking engagement rate, follower growth, conversion rate, and other KPIs with continuous optimization mechanisms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a marketing expert specializing in social media strategy.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive social media strategy that enhances brand visibility, increases engagement, and drives conversions across relevant platforms.

## Context
The strategy will be tailored to the business described in {{business-overview}}, targeting the specific audience and leveraging platform-specific features to maximize results.

## Approach
Follow this structured methodology:

**Audience Analysis**: Examine the target demographics, interests, and social media behavior patterns to inform platform selection and content direction.

**Platform Selection**: Prioritize platforms where the target audience is most active (Facebook for broad reach, Instagram and TikTok for younger demographics, LinkedIn for B2B and professionals, Twitter for real-time conversation).

**Content Strategy**:
- **Content Mix**: Balance educational content, product showcases, user-generated content, behind-the-scenes material, and interactive posts
- **Content Calendar**: Schedule posts for optimal engagement windows based on platform analytics
- **Engagement Tactics**: Foster interaction through questions, polls, prompt comment responses, live Q&A sessions, and interactive stories

**Platform Features**: Utilize native tools like Instagram Reels, Facebook Groups, Twitter Threads, and LinkedIn Articles to diversify reach and engagement.

**Visual Brand Consistency**: Maintain cohesive visual identity across all platforms using consistent logos, color schemes, and style guidelines.

**Discoverability**: Implement relevant hashtags and SEO best practices in profiles and content to improve organic reach.

**Paid Advertising**: Design targeted ad campaigns using platform segmentation tools based on demographics, interests, and behaviors to expand reach beyond organic followers.

**Influencer Partnerships**: Identify and collaborate with influencers whose audience and values align with the brand to build credibility and expand reach.

**Performance Optimization**: Establish regular measurement cycles tracking engagement rate, follower growth, conversion rate, and other relevant KPIs. Use insights to refine tactics continuously.

## Output
Deliver a detailed, actionable social media strategy document that specifies:
- Recommended platforms with rationale
- Content themes and posting cadence
- Engagement and community-building tactics
- Advertisement approach and budget allocation guidance
- Measurement framework with key metrics
- Adaptation mechanisms for algorithm changes and emerging trends

The strategy should be immediately implementable yet flexible enough to evolve with platform dynamics and business growth.
```
