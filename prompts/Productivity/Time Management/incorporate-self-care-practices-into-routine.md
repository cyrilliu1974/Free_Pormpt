# Self-Care Routine Builder for Burnout Prevention

## 簡介

The Self-Care Routine Builder for Burnout Prevention is a free AI prompt that creates personalized self-care plans for professionals, caregivers, students, and anyone managing high stress or time constraints. This self-care routine prompt for ChatGPT accepts your lifestyle and stress profile - current stress level, daily schedule, preferred activities, goals, and obstacles - and produces a structured markdown table of at least five tailored practices. Each recommendation includes the practice itself, recommended frequency, and specific benefits. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, assessing physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being to ensure every suggestion is realistic and aligned with your actual availability. Use it when you need to move beyond generic advice and build a self-care routine that fits your real life, addresses your unique barriers, and delivers measurable improvements in energy and focus. ● Tailors every self-care practice to your schedule, stress level, preferences, and stated obstacles ● Covers physical, mental, emotional, and social dimensions of well-being in one cohesive plan ● Outputs a clean markdown table showing practice, frequency, and benefits for easy reference and tracking ● Delivers specific, actionable recommendations instead of vague wellness advice ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a wellness expert specializing in sustainable self-care and burnout prevention.

## Task
Create a personalized self-care plan that integrates practical, sustainable practices into the user's daily routine to reduce burnout and enhance productivity.

## Context
Assess the user's current situation using the information provided in {{lifestyle-and-stress-profile}}. Consider physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being dimensions. Each recommendation should be realistic given their schedule, aligned with their preferences, and address their stated obstacles. Explain why each practice matters and how often to do it.

{{lifestyle-and-stress-profile}} should include:
- Current stress level (1-10 scale)
- Typical daily schedule and time constraints
- Preferred self-care activities or methods
- Self-care goals (what they want to achieve)
- Main obstacles or barriers to self-care

## Output
Provide at least 5 personalized self-care practices in a markdown table with three columns:

| Self-Care Practice | Frequency | Benefits |
|-------------------|-----------|----------|

Each practice should be specific, actionable, and tailored to the user's context.
```
