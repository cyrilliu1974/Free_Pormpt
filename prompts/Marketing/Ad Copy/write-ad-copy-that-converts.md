# Social Media Ad Copy Generator for Conversions

## 簡介

The Social Media Ad Copy Generator for Conversions is a free AI prompt that produces complete, platform-ready advertisements designed to drive action and sales for marketers and copywriters. This ad copy prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured five-step process: analyzing how your product solves audience pain points, crafting attention-grabbing headlines, writing concise body copy that emphasizes your unique selling proposition, applying persuasive language and emotional triggers, and closing with a strong call-to-action. It outputs three ready-to-publish components (headline, body copy, CTA) customized to your chosen social media platform, target audience, brand voice, and unique value proposition. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering copy that balances clarity with persuasive impact. Use this prompt when you need to quickly produce conversion-focused ad variants for platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter without starting from a blank page. ● Analyzes product features against audience pain points to surface the most compelling angle ● Generates platform-appropriate headlines, body copy, and calls-to-action in one pass ● Incorporates brand voice and unique selling proposition variables for consistent messaging ● Structures sentences for clarity and emotional resonance using proven copywriting principles ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in social media advertising.

## Task
Create compelling ad copy for {{social-media-platform}} that drives conversions. Deliver a complete ad with headline, body copy, and call-to-action.

## Context
Product/service: {{product-service}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the product/service features and how they solve audience pain points
2. Craft an attention-grabbing headline that speaks directly to audience desires
3. Write concise body copy that emphasizes the unique selling proposition and addresses core needs
4. Use persuasive language and emotional triggers to create connection
5. Structure sentences for clarity and impact
6. End with a strong call-to-action that prompts immediate engagement

## Output
Deliver your ad copy in this structure:

**Headline:**
[Your headline]

**Body Copy:**
[Your body copy]

**Call-to-Action:**
[Your CTA]
```
