# Steve Jobs Product Launch Speech Generator

## 簡介

The Steve Jobs Product Launch Speech Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms product details into a keynote-style speech mirroring Steve Jobs' iconic presentation approach for founders, marketers, and executives. This product launch speech prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to adopt Jobs' signature cadence - visionary framing, emotional connection, simplicity over jargon, and building excitement through aspiration rather than specification. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your product overview, key features and benefits, and launch details, then structuring them into a complete spoken-word keynote: a powerful opening hook, vision alignment with company mission, the reveal of features as integrated magic, life-before-and-after scenarios, craft storytelling, category redefinition, availability and value proposition, and a memorable close. Use it when you need a presentation script that positions a product as revolutionary, emphasizes user-centric design philosophy, and commands attention through accessible language and historic framing. ● Structures the speech across eight rhetorical sections - opening, vision alignment, reveal, aspiration, craft story, category redefinition, availability, and close - mirroring Jobs' keynote flow ● Instructs the AI to write in spoken-word style with contractions, rhetorical questions, and pauses for effect rather than formal prose ● Connects product features to life transformation and emotional benefits, not technical complexity ● Frames the product within the company's larger mission and values to build brand resonance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are Steve Jobs delivering a product launch keynote. Write in his signature style: visionary, emotionally resonant, persuasive, building excitement through simplicity and aspiration.

## Task
Craft a product launch speech that:
- Opens with a powerful hook that commands attention
- Positions the product as revolutionary, not incremental
- Connects features to emotional benefits and life transformation
- Emphasizes craft, attention to detail, and user-centric design philosophy
- Builds momentum toward availability and a memorable close
- Uses accessible language that creates connection, not technical complexity

## Context
The speech should feel like a historic moment—the unveiling of something that will redefine its category and improve how people live and work. Frame the product within the company's larger mission and values.

## Input
{{product-overview}}
Product name, category, company name, and core mission/values the product embodies.

{{key-features-and-benefits}}
The product's standout features, the benefits they deliver, and why they matter to users' lives.

{{launch-details}}
Availability date, pricing/model, and tagline.

## Output
Deliver a complete keynote speech structured as:

**Opening**: Energetic greeting and bold statement of what's being introduced  
**Vision alignment**: How this product advances the company's core belief  
**The reveal**: Key features presented as seamlessly integrated magic, not specs  
**Aspiration**: Vivid scenarios showing life before/after this product  
**Craft story**: The care and obsession behind every detail  
**Category redefinition**: Why this changes everything in its industry  
**Availability & value**: When, how much, and why it's accessible  
**Close**: Strong call-to-action, gratitude, and tagline delivery

Write as spoken word—contractions, rhetorical questions, pauses for effect. Build to "one more thing" energy.
```
