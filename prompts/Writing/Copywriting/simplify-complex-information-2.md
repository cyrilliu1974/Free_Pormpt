# Simplify Complex Information Prompt

## 簡介

The Simplify Complex Information Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites technical, academic, or jargon-heavy material into plain language while preserving the original message and intent. This simplify complex information prompt for ChatGPT takes any dense text and produces a streamlined version written for readability, followed by a structured analysis that scores clarity, engagement, and message retention on a 10-point scale. It identifies what works well in the simplified copy, flags potential pitfalls or areas that still need refinement, and delivers an overall impact score. Use it when preparing marketing copy, customer support documentation, instructional materials, or any content that needs to reach a non-specialist audience. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces a plain-language rewrite alongside a multi-dimensional readability analysis with clarity, engagement, and retention scores. ● Highlights three strengths and three watch-outs so you know exactly what landed and what to refine. ● Structured output format makes it easy to present findings to stakeholders or iterate quickly. ● Ideal for marketers, instructional designers, technical writers, and support teams who need to communicate specialist knowledge to broader audiences. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a copywriting expert who transforms complex information into clear, engaging content that resonates with target audiences.

## Task
Simplify the provided complex information while preserving its core message, then analyze the simplified version for readability.

## Context
Complex information to simplify:
{{complex-information}}

## Output
Structure your response exactly as follows:

📝 **Simplified Version**
[Present the rewritten copy in plain language]

💡 **Readability Analysis**

✅ **What works well**
✔️ [Strength 1]
✔️ [Strength 2]
✔️ [Strength 3]

⚠️ **Watch out for**
❌ [Potential pitfall 1]
❌ [Potential pitfall 2]
❌ [Potential pitfall 3]

🎯 **Impact Score (1-10):** [Overall score]
- Clarity: [Score]/10
- Engagement: [Score]/10
- Message Retention: [Score]/10
```
