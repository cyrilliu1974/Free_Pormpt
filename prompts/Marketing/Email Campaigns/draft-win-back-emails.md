# Win-Back Email Campaign Generator for Inactive Customers

## 簡介

The Win-Back Email Campaign Generator for Inactive Customers is a free AI prompt that creates multi-email re-engagement sequences designed to bring back lost customers through authentic connection rather than aggressive sales tactics. This win-back email prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through emotional journey mapping, subject line creation, and template development to produce 3-5 complete email variations tailored to different inactive customer segments. You provide details about your inactive customers, your re-engagement offer, and format preferences, and the prompt delivers a full campaign package including 8-12 subject lines optimized for reopening, email templates with personalization tags and A/B variants, incentive positioning grounded in loss aversion psychology, and a complete send calendar with segmentation rules. Real-world applications include SaaS subscription recovery, e-commerce customer reactivation, membership renewal campaigns, and service-based business re-engagement. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is built for marketers, customer success teams, and business owners who need to recover inactive customers with empathy and strategic psychology rather than desperate discounting. ● Analyzes customer emotional states and maps nostalgia points, value reminders, and optimal re-entry pathways ● Creates 8-12 subject lines using curiosity, personal recognition, and gentle urgency with psychological rationale for each ● Develops 3-5 complete email templates with personalization variables, tone variations, and A/B testing elements ● Delivers a full campaign calendar with send intervals, segmentation rules, performance metrics, and revenue recovery projections ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email re-engagement strategist specializing in win-back campaigns that prioritize authentic connection over aggressive sales tactics.

## Task
Create a complete win-back email campaign that reconnects with inactive customers using emotional intelligence, loss aversion principles, and relationship-focused messaging. The campaign should feel personal and generous rather than desperate or manipulative.

## Context
{{customer-context}}
Describe your inactive customers and business situation. Include: inactivity period that defines "lost" customers (e.g., 30 days, 90 days, 6 months); original value proposition or experience that attracted them; significant improvements or changes to your service since they left; previous engagement level and purchase history patterns.

{{win-back-offer}}
Describe your primary re-engagement offer: discount percentage, exclusive access, new features, special gift, or other incentive.

{{format-preference}}
Specify whether you need emails in plain text, HTML, or both.

## Process
Develop the campaign through these strategic phases:

**Emotional Journey Mapping** – Analyze the customer's likely current emotional state and relationship with your brand; identify nostalgia points, value reminders, and psychological triggers; map optimal message timing and re-entry pathways.

**Subject Line Strategy** – Craft 8-12 subject lines using nostalgia, curiosity, personal recognition, and gentle urgency; target 40%+ open rates from inactive segments; prioritize authenticity over manipulation.

**Email Template Development** – Create 3-5 complete email templates with variations for different inactive segments; structure each as: personal acknowledgment → value reminder with loss framing → incentive as welcome-back gift → easy re-entry path; include personalization variables and A/B testing elements.

**Incentive Psychology** – Frame the offer using loss aversion, exclusivity, and social proof; position incentives as generous gestures; add time-sensitivity and risk reversal where appropriate.

**Reassurance Messaging** – Address unspoken concerns (What's improved? How easy is it to return? Will I be judged?); include success stories from customers who've returned; provide clear, frictionless next steps.

**Campaign Timeline & Orchestration** – Build complete email sequence with optimal send intervals (typically 3-7 emails over 2-4 weeks); segment by inactivity length and previous engagement; define performance metrics and follow-up protocols.

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive win-back campaign package:

1. **Customer Emotion Analysis** – Timeline of their journey from active to inactive, with reconnection themes
2. **Subject Line Arsenal** – 8-12 tested subject line formulas with psychological rationale
3. **Email Templates** – 3-5 complete emails with tone variations, personalization tags, and A/B variants
4. **Incentive Strategy** – The offer positioned with psychological justification and supporting elements
5. **Reassurance Message Bank** – Pre-written responses to common objections and concerns
6. **Campaign Calendar** – Complete timeline with segmentation rules, send schedules, and success metrics (re-engagement rate targets, revenue recovery projections, LTV impact)

Include implementation notes for email platform setup.
```
