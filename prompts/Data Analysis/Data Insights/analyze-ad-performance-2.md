# Ad Performance Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Ad Performance Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that evaluates digital marketing campaigns and delivers actionable optimization insights for marketers and analysts. This ad performance analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines core metrics including impressions, clicks, and conversion rates, then structures findings into a markdown table followed by prioritized recommendations. It identifies campaign strengths, performance gaps, and high-impact opportunities to improve ROI. Marketing teams use it to transform raw campaign data into strategic reports that guide budget allocation, creative adjustments, and audience targeting decisions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting your campaign name and business context as inputs. Marketing analysts, PPC specialists, and agency teams reach for this prompt when they need to quickly synthesize campaign data into client-ready reports or internal performance reviews. ● Outputs a clean markdown table showing campaign name, impressions, clicks, and conversion rate side by side ● Identifies specific strengths and weaknesses in campaign execution based on performance data ● Surfaces optimization opportunities ranked by their potential impact on return on investment ● Delivers actionable recommendations tailored to your target audience, marketing goals, and budget constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketing analyst specializing in ad performance evaluation and campaign optimization.

## Task
Analyze the effectiveness of digital marketing ads and identify areas for improvement. Examine key performance metrics including impressions, clicks, and conversion rates. Deliver a comprehensive report that highlights strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for optimization, with actionable insights and recommendations to enhance campaign performance and maximize ROI.

## Context
**Campaign details:**
{{campaign-name}}

**Business context:**
{{business-context}}
(Include target audience, marketing goals, budget, and industry)

## Output
1. Present the core metrics in a markdown table with 4 columns:
   - Campaign Name
   - Impressions
   - Clicks
   - Conversion Rate

2. Follow with a bullet-point list containing:
   - Key strengths identified in the campaign
   - Weaknesses and performance gaps
   - Opportunities for optimization
   - Actionable recommendations prioritized by potential impact on ROI
```
