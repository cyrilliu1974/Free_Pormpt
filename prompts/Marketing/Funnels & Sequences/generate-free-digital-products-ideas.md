# Free Digital Product Ideas Generator for Lead Magnets

## 簡介

The Free Digital Product Ideas Generator for Lead Magnets is a free AI prompt that produces 40 custom digital product concepts designed to attract and convert leads in any niche. This lead generation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target niche, audience profile, and desired conversion action to brainstorm practical digital products, tools, and freebies optimized for lead capture. Whether you need ideas for email list builders, social media follow incentives, or consultation booking magnets, the prompt structures each concept with a title, description, key benefits, lead magnet strategy, and conversion action. Marketing consultants, course creators, coaches, and SaaS founders use it to break through creative blocks and design offers that resonate with their specific audience pain points. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. ● Produces 40 distinct product ideas, each with title, description, benefits, lead magnet hook, and conversion goal ● Tailors every concept to your niche, audience profile, and specific desired action ● Addresses real customer pain points and motivations to maximize opt-in rates ● Formats output for easy review, comparison, and integration into sales funnels ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a digital product and lead generation expert with deep knowledge in marketing, sales funnels, copywriting, content creation, email marketing, social media, and consumer psychology.

## Task

Generate 40 unique and compelling digital product or tool ideas designed to attract and capture leads in the user's target niche. Each idea must be practical, engaging, and optimized for conversions.

## Context

**Target Niche:** {{niche}}

**Target Audience:** {{audience-profile}}

**Conversion Goal:** {{desired-action}}

Address the audience's key pain points, desires, and motivations described above. Provide genuine value while strategically guiding them toward the desired action (email signup, social media follow, consultation booking, etc.).

## Requirements

- Each product idea must be unique and compelling
- Directly address pain points and desires from the audience profile
- Include a clear lead magnet strategy
- Ensure ideas are specific to the niche, not generic
- Optimize for maximum lead capture and conversion

## Output

For each of the 40 product ideas, use this format:

**Product Idea [Number]:**

**Title:** [Product Name]

**Description:** [2-3 sentence overview of what the product is and how it works]

**Key Benefits:**
- [Benefit 1]
- [Benefit 2]
- [Benefit 3]

**Lead Magnet:** [The free offering or incentive that captures the lead]

**Desired Action:** [Specific conversion action the lead should take]

---

[Repeat for all 40 ideas]
```
