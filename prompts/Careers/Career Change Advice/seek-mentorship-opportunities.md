# Mentor Matching Strategy Builder for Career Growth

## 簡介

The Mentor Matching Strategy Builder for Career Growth is a free AI prompt that creates a data-driven mentor outreach plan for professionals seeking guidance in their industry. This mentor matching prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your industry, career goals, current expertise, networking preferences, and ideal mentor qualities to identify 5+ influential figures whose backgrounds and achievements align with your trajectory. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a markdown table that maps each potential mentor's expertise to your specific career needs. Real use cases include career changers researching thought leaders in a new field, mid-career professionals seeking executive guidance, and specialists looking for advisors in niche domains. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured, strategic approach to mentor selection rather than random networking outreach. ● Identifies 5+ potential mentors with specific achievements and relevant expertise in your target industry ● Maps each mentor's background to your career goals, current skill level, and preferred mentoring style ● Provides clear reasoning for why each mentor is a strategic fit, not just a big name ● Structures output as an actionable markdown table ready to guide your outreach sequence ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert career strategist specializing in mentor matching and professional networking.

## Task
Create a comprehensive mentor outreach strategy that identifies influential figures who align with the mentee's career trajectory. Research potential mentors in the specified industry, analyze their expertise and achievements, and evaluate alignment with the mentee's goals and preferences.

## Context
**Industry:** {{industry}}

**Career goals:** {{career-goals}}

**Current expertise:** {{current-expertise}}

**Networking preferences:** {{networking-preferences}}

**Ideal mentor qualities:** {{ideal-mentor-qualities}}

## Output
Deliver your analysis as a markdown table with three columns: **Mentor Name**, **Expertise**, and **Reason for Choosing**. Include at least 5 potential mentors. For each mentor, provide specific achievements and explain precisely how their background, experience, and approach match the stated career goals, current expertise level, networking style, and desired mentor qualities.
```
