# Premium Speaker Grid Advertisement Prompt

## 簡介

The Premium Speaker Grid Advertisement Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates professional six-panel advertisement layouts for wireless home audio products. It produces a cohesive 2×3 grid combining hero product photography, macro craftsmanship details, and lifestyle interior scenes across living rooms, bedrooms, and kitchens - all styled with your chosen color palette and minimalist brand aesthetic. This speaker advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana outputs 4:5 portrait ratio layouts ideal for Instagram ads, e-commerce hero images, and digital marketing campaigns for audio brands. Reach for it when you need a complete visual marketing asset that blends product photography with real-world lifestyle context, without coordinating expensive multi-location photo shoots. ● Generates six coordinated panels including studio hero shots, macro texture details, and three distinct lifestyle placement scenes (living room, bedroom, kitchen). ● Produces natural lighting setups across all panels - soft studio highlights for product shots, ambient daylight for interiors, and warm evening tones for cozy settings. ● Outputs person-interaction scenes showing intuitive voice and touch control use alongside pure product and environment compositions. ● Creates consistent color palette integration and minimal logo presentation across all six panels for cohesive brand identity. ## Prompt

```
Premium 2×3 grid advertisement for a wireless home speaker, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, equal panels, professional product and lifestyle photography.

Panel 1 (Hero Speaker Design): Studio hero shot of the {{product-name}} speaker centered on a minimalist surface. Soft studio lighting with gentle edge highlights, subtle gradient background, focus on speaker form, fabric texture, and logo clarity. Premium, refined, modern mood.

Panel 3 (Immersive Sound Moment): {{product-name}} speaker in a Scandinavian living room with oak shelving, natural daylight, immersive and comfortable presence.

Panel 4 (Everyday Use): Person interacting with the speaker using voice or touch controls. Soft ambient interior lighting, intuitive ease mood.

Panel 5 (Evening Atmosphere): {{product-name}} speaker in a cozy bedroom on a nightstand, warm moody ambient light, relaxation and warmth mood.

Panel 6 (Interior Styling): {{product-name}} speaker in a modern kitchen counter alongside plants, balanced natural light, design-forward lifestyle harmony.

Color palette: {{color-palette}} tones throughout. Minimal embossed logo presentation. Elegant, immersive, lifestyle-focused brand personality.

Style: Premium audio advertising, modern interior lifestyle photography, high realism, minimalist composition, design-forward branding. Professional shot types mixing hero product, macro material detail, and lifestyle interior scenes. Natural daylight mixed with soft ambient lighting, balanced exposure with soft highlights, gentle controlled shadows.

Mood: Immersive, warm, modern, elegant, comforting, premium.

Avoid: Cheap plastic appearance, harsh reflections, cluttered interiors, unreadable branding, over-saturated colors, flat lighting, CGI or cartoon style, watermarks, text overlays.
```
