# Feedback Response Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Feedback Response Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates psychology-backed strategies to dramatically improve feedback collection rates for researchers, product teams, and customer success professionals. This feedback response strategy prompt for ChatGPT analyzes the psychological barriers preventing people from providing feedback - indifference, time pressure, lack of perceived impact - then generates six distinct strategies grounded in established behavioral psychology principles including reciprocity, social proof, autonomy, intrinsic motivation, and cognitive biases. Each strategy includes the underlying psychological mechanism, step-by-step implementation tactics, an explanation of why it works, and adaptation notes for different audiences and contexts. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing actionable frameworks you can deploy immediately in surveys, user research, employee engagement programs, or customer feedback initiatives. Reach for this prompt when traditional feedback requests yield low response rates and you need a behavioral science approach to motivation and engagement. ● Identifies and addresses specific psychological barriers (reciprocity gaps, social validation needs, autonomy constraints) that suppress feedback participation. ● Delivers six diverse strategies covering different psychological levers, not repetitive variations of the same tactic. ● Includes implementation steps, behavioral explanations, and context-specific adaptation guidance for each strategy. ● Provides expected outcomes and success indicators to measure strategy effectiveness in your feedback programs. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a behavioral psychologist and feedback optimization specialist with expertise in psychological triggers that motivate response behavior.

## Task
Develop six evidence-based strategies that leverage psychological incentives to dramatically increase feedback response rates. Each strategy must be grounded in established psychological principles (reciprocity, social proof, autonomy, intrinsic motivation, cognitive biases) while remaining practical for real-world implementation.

## Context
Traditional feedback requests fail because they ignore fundamental psychological drivers. Analyze the psychological barriers that prevent people from providing feedback, then design strategies that address these barriers while activating positive behavioral triggers.

**Feedback Context:**
{{feedback-context}}

## Output
For each of the six strategies, provide:

1. **Strategy Name** – Clear, descriptive heading
2. **Psychological Principle** – The underlying behavioral psychology mechanism (e.g., reciprocity norm, commitment consistency, social validation)
3. **Implementation Steps** – Specific, actionable tactics in bullet point format
4. **Why It Works** – Explanation of the behavioral psychology behind the approach, including which barriers it overcomes and which positive triggers it activates
5. **Adaptation Notes** – How to adjust the strategy for different contexts, audiences, or collection methods

Ensure strategies are diverse, covering different psychological levers, and include expected outcomes or indicators of success where relevant.
```
