# Metallic Can Product Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Metallic Can Product Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates hyper-detailed commercial product photos of metallic beverage cans with dynamic juice splashes, fruit graphics, and electric neon accents. This product photography prompt for Midjourney produces high-energy visuals featuring customizable can colors, label text, fruit imagery, and shot types - all set against bright minimal backdrops with strong directional lighting. It works on Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar AI image models to create scroll-stopping beverage marketing assets with flying fruit slices, glowing electric arcs, and cinematic 16:9 compositions.
