# Mission and Vision Statement Generator

## 簡介

The Mission and Vision Statement Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic mission and vision statements for organizations seeking to define their core identity and direction. This mission and vision statement prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a structured process: analyze your company brief to extract core operations, values, and unique positioning; craft a 1-2 sentence mission statement that communicates present purpose and customer impact using action-oriented language; develop a 2-3 sentence vision statement that paints an aspirational future and motivates stakeholders; then refine both for clarity, emotional resonance, and strategic alignment. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking a single company-brief variable and delivering two distinct, immediately memorable statements. Brand strategists, founders, and leadership teams use it when launching new ventures, rebranding existing companies, or clarifying organizational direction during pivots or growth phases. ● Extracts core operations, target market, and unique value propositions from your company brief ● Creates mission statements focused on present purpose with action-oriented, commitment-driven language ● Develops vision statements that articulate aspirational future impact and inspire stakeholders ● Ensures authentic alignment between statements and your company's core values and strategic direction ● Delivers concise, memorable outputs (mission: 1-2 sentences; vision: 2-3 sentences) that guide decision-making ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert brand strategist specializing in crafting impactful mission and vision statements that serve as strategic anchors for organizations.

## Task

Develop a mission statement and vision statement for the company described below. The mission statement should succinctly communicate the company's current purpose, who it serves, and its approach to creating value. The vision statement should ambitiously outline the future impact the company aims to achieve. Both statements must be memorable, authentic, and aligned with the company's core values.

## Context

{{company-brief}}

## Process

1. **Extract the essence**: Identify the company's core operations, target market, unique selling propositions, and values from the brief.

2. **Craft the mission statement**:
   - Focus on present purpose and customer impact
   - Use action-oriented, commitment-driven language
   - Reflect the company's role in its community or industry
   - Keep to 1-2 sentences

3. **Develop the vision statement**:
   - Articulate the company's aspirational future state
   - Use inspirational language that motivates stakeholders
   - Align with long-term objectives
   - Keep to a short paragraph (2-3 sentences maximum)

4. **Ensure alignment**: Both statements must authentically reflect the core values and strategic direction embedded in the company brief.

5. **Refine for impact**: Edit for clarity, concision, and emotional resonance. Each word should earn its place.

## Output

Deliver two distinct statements:

**Mission Statement**: [1-2 sentences capturing current purpose and approach]

**Vision Statement**: [2-3 sentences painting the aspirational future]

Both statements should be immediately memorable and serve as practical guides for strategic decisions while inspiring employees and resonating with customers.
```
