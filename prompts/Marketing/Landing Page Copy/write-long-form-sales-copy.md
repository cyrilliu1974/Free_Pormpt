# Long-Form Sales Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Long-Form Sales Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that builds persuasive, narrative-structured sales copy connecting brand stories to customer needs for marketers, copywriters, and business owners. This long-form sales copy prompt for ChatGPT walks through a proven structure - hook, problem, solution, benefits, social proof, and call-to-action - to craft copy that reads as a cohesive story rather than a list of features. You provide your product or service details, target audience, unique selling proposition, brand story elements, and desired call-to-action; the prompt analyzes pain points, maps benefits, and builds a narrative arc that positions your offer as the natural bridge between frustration and transformation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and outputs six clearly labeled sections ready for landing pages, sales letters, or email campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need sales copy that feels human, story-driven, and emotionally resonant rather than pushy or transactional. ● Analyzes features and translates them into audience-specific benefits ● Builds a story arc that ties customer pain points to your brand's solution ● Incorporates emotional triggers, sensory language, and social proof throughout ● Outputs six labeled sections (Hook, Problem, Solution, Benefits, Social Proof, CTA) for immediate use ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in long-form sales narratives that connect brand stories to customer needs and drive conversions.

## Task
Craft compelling long-form sales copy structured as a persuasive brand narrative. Build a story arc that resonates with the target audience's pain points and desires, leading them naturally toward action.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Brand Story Elements:** {{brand-story}}

**Call-to-Action:** {{cta}}

## Instructions
1. Analyze the product/service features and how they translate to concrete benefits for the target audience
2. Identify the audience's core pain points and aspirations based on the description provided
3. Develop a narrative arc that positions the brand story as the bridge between their problem and desired outcome
4. Structure sentences and paragraphs for logical flow and coherence, ensuring each idea builds on the previous
5. Incorporate storytelling techniques: character development, conflict/tension, and resolution
6. Use persuasive language including emotional triggers, sensory details, and power words appropriate to the audience
7. Weave in social proof and credibility markers throughout the narrative
8. Build toward the call-to-action organically so it feels like the natural next step

## Output
Deliver the sales copy with clear section headings:

**Hook** – Opening that captures attention and establishes relevance

**Problem** – Pain points and frustrations the audience experiences

**Solution** – How the product/service addresses those needs

**Benefits** – Tangible outcomes and transformation the audience will experience

**Social Proof** – Evidence, testimonials, or credibility markers

**Call-to-Action** – Clear, compelling directive that motivates immediate action
```
