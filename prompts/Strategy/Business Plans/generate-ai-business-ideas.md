# AI Business Ideas Generator for Solopreneurs

## 簡介

The AI Business Ideas Generator for Solopreneurs is a free AI prompt that produces tailored, low-cost business concepts for solo entrepreneurs working with limited budgets and resources. This AI business ideas prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your expertise, available resources, time commitment, and goals to produce five actionable concepts grounded in current AI tools and business models. Each idea is scored on a 1-5 cost-efficiency scale (5 = lowest launch cost), lists the key skills required, and estimates time from start to first revenue. Every recommendation is backed by credible sources, making it practical for solopreneurs researching realistic opportunities in the AI economy. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and returns results as a structured markdown table followed by a numbered source list. Reach for this prompt when you need research-backed business concepts that one person can actually build, rather than generic startup advice or capital-intensive ideas. ● Tailors every idea to your specific background, budget, tools, and time constraints ● Scores cost-efficiency on a 1-5 scale so you can compare launch expenses at a glance ● Estimates time to market and lists required skills for realistic planning ● Cites credible sources for each idea, giving you starting points for deeper research ## Prompt

```
## Role

You generate AI-powered business ideas for solopreneurs working with limited budgets, evaluating each on cost-efficiency, required skills, and time to market.

## Context

Every idea must be buildable by one person, low-cost to start, and grounded in current AI tools and business models.

## Task

Generate 5 AI-powered business ideas tailored to the user's profile. For each idea, assess:
- Cost-efficiency score (1-5, where 5 = lowest launch cost)
- Required skills
- Estimated time from start to first revenue

Back each idea with at least one credible source.

## Input

*Include your expertise/background, available resources (budget, tools, time commitment), and any time constraints or goals.*

```
