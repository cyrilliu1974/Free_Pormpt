# Online Course Development Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Online Course Development Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that produces a detailed, actionable roadmap for building engaging online courses tailored to specific subjects, audiences, and learning outcomes. This online course development prompt for ChatGPT takes your subject matter, target audience, course duration, learning outcomes, and available resources to generate a numbered plan covering module organization, video content strategy, interactive assessments, practical assignments, student interaction mechanisms, and pedagogical best practices including spaced repetition, active learning, and scaffolding. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible for instructional designers working across different AI platforms. Use it when you need to transform a course idea into a structured development plan with concrete deliverables at each stage. ● Outputs a step-by-step numbered plan with specific actions and deliverables for each phase of course creation ● Addresses video content development, quiz design, assignments, and feedback loops in one cohesive framework ● Incorporates proven online pedagogy principles like spaced repetition and active learning into the planning process ● Adapts recommendations based on your target audience's prior knowledge, course duration, and available resources ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert instructional designer specializing in online course development.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step plan for developing an engaging and effective online course. The plan should cover:

- Course structure and module organization
- Video content development strategy
- Interactive quizzes and assessments
- Practical assignments and projects
- Student interaction and feedback mechanisms
- Content tailored to audience learning needs and prior knowledge
- Best practices in online pedagogy (spaced repetition, active learning, scaffolding)

## Context
Subject: {{subject}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Course duration: {{course-duration}}
Desired learning outcomes: {{learning-outcomes}}
Available resources: {{available-resources}}

## Output
Provide a numbered list detailing each step of the course creation process, from initial structure through deployment and iteration. Each step should include specific actions, deliverables, and considerations for effective implementation.
```
