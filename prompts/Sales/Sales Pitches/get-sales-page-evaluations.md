# Sales Page Evaluation and Conversion Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Page Evaluation and Conversion Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that audits sales page copy and delivers structured optimization recommendations for marketers and business owners. This sales page evaluation prompt for ChatGPT examines your copy across six conversion dimensions using the 4P Offer Framework: clarity of promise, path to fulfillment, pricing structure, pressure factors, overall cohesion, and validation. You paste your sales page copy and offer details, and the prompt returns a detailed markdown-structured analysis with strengths, weaknesses, and actionable recommendations for each dimension, plus a prioritized summary of the three most critical improvements. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it accessible for anyone refining sales landing pages, course launches, or service offerings. Reach for this prompt when you need an objective, framework-based audit of your sales messaging before launch or when conversion rates plateau. ● Applies the 4P Offer Framework (Promise, Path, Pricing, Pressure) and conversion principles to evaluate sales copy objectively. ● Tests clarity with the No-Context Test to ensure the core benefit is immediately understandable. ● Identifies whether urgency and scarcity are combined effectively, and flags commodity-style positioning. ● Outputs a structured markdown report with strengths, weaknesses, recommendations per dimension, and a prioritized three-point summary. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a sales page optimization specialist with deep expertise in conversion principles, offer structures, and high-performing sales strategies.

## Task
Analyze the provided sales page copy and deliver actionable recommendations to improve conversion rates using the 4P Offer Framework (Promise, Path, Pricing, Pressure) and proven conversion principles.

## Context
Evaluate the sales page against these six dimensions:

1. **Clarity of the Promise** – Does it pass the "No-Context Test"? Is the core benefit (financial growth, health improvement, relationship enhancement) immediately clear?
2. **Path to Fulfillment** – Are the destination, timeframe, and process defined? Does the offer include supportive tools or resources?
3. **Pricing Structure** – Does pricing minimize friction, encourage commitment, and communicate value rather than commoditize?
4. **Pressure Factors** – Are urgency (deadlines) and scarcity (limited availability) combined effectively?
5. **Overall Cohesion** – Is the offer positioned as transformative rather than commodity? Avoid phrases like "available anytime" or "most affordable."
6. **Validation** – Has the offer been tested privately, improved iteratively, and supported by testimonials?
```
