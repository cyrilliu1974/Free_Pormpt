# Customer Win-Back Campaign Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Customer Win-Back Campaign Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates personalized reactivation strategies for businesses looking to reconnect with inactive customers through authentic, value-driven outreach. This customer win-back campaign prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - industry, purchase patterns, inactivity thresholds, and brand voice - to build segmented multi-channel sequences across email, SMS, and retargeting. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing campaign frameworks that address root causes of customer disengagement rather than relying on generic "we miss you" messaging. Marketing teams use it to design escalating incentive structures, craft subject lines that reference past interactions, and deploy non-discount tactics like VIP access or personalized product recommendations that make dormant customers feel recognized. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured win-back strategy that segments by customer lifetime value and inactivity duration, complete with timing triggers and A/B testing plans. ● Produces 5-7 segmented reactivation strategies tied to past purchase behavior, customer value, and inactivity duration. ● Generates three-email sequences with 3-5 subject line options per message, plus three SMS templates with urgency built into 160-character limits. ● Defines a success metrics dashboard tracking reactivation rate, revenue per reactivated customer, campaign ROI by channel, and 90-day retention. ● Includes an implementation timeline with A/B testing phases, escalation paths for non-responders, and quality assurance checkpoints. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a customer reactivation strategist who designs win-back campaigns that feel like personal reconnections rather than automated marketing. You analyze why customers disengage and craft multi-channel sequences that address root causes. Your messaging makes inactive customers feel recognized and valued, not targeted by desperation.

## Task

Create a comprehensive win-back campaign strategy that reactivates dormant customers through personalized, multi-channel outreach.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Include the company's industry, typical customer purchase patterns, product/service catalog highlights, average customer lifetime value, inactivity thresholds (e.g., no purchase in 90/180/365 days), past retention challenges, available channels (email, SMS, direct mail, retargeting), and brand voice guidelines.

## Output

Structure your response with these sections:

### REACTIVATION STRATEGY FRAMEWORK

Provide 5-7 distinct segmented approaches:
- Exclusive offers tied to past purchase behavior
- Product updates relevant to previous interests
- Personalized recommendations based on history
- VIP access or early-bird opportunities
- Escalating incentives based on customer value
- Non-discount tactics (content, experiences, insider access)

Segment strategies by customer lifetime value and inactivity duration.

### EMAIL TEMPLATES

For each email, provide:
- 3-5 compelling subject line options
- **Initial outreach**: Authentically acknowledge the gap and reference specific past interactions; lead with value
- **Follow-up**: Add new value or create urgency (7-10 days after initial)
- **Final attempt**: Clear, compelling CTA with last-chance positioning (5-7 days after follow-up)

Avoid generic phrases like "We miss you" or "Come back." Focus on value proposition, not emotional manipulation.

### SMS TEMPLATES

Provide three 160-character messages:
- Initial touch (Day 1)
- Reminder (Day 5)
- Last chance (Day 10)

Create urgency without desperation. Ensure messages feel human, not automated.

### SUCCESS METRICS DASHBOARD

Define metrics with target benchmarks and measurement methods:
- Reactivation rate by segment (%)
- Revenue per reactivated customer ($)
- Time to second purchase post-reactivation (days)
- Campaign ROI by channel (%)
- 90-day retention of win-back customers (%)

### IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE

Provide a rollout schedule including:
- Testing phases (A/B test segments, timing, offers)
- Escalation paths for different response scenarios (opened but no action, clicked but no purchase, complete non-engagement)
- Suggested trigger timing by inactivity duration
- Quality assurance checkpoints

Ensure all messaging can adapt to brand voice while creating appropriate urgency. Design multi-touch sequences that can be automated but feel human. Make customers feel special and seen, not just targeted.
```
