# Draft Referral Program Messages

## 簡介

The Draft Referral Program Messages is a free AI prompt that creates a complete customer referral communication flow for businesses looking to formalize customer advocacy without damaging relationships. This referral program prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces eight ready-to-send components: a program launch announcement, three strategic ask templates timed to positive moments (post-support wins, milestones, NPS 9-10 responses), a reminder sequence, activity notifications (link use, conversion, reward earned), a celebratory thank-you message, landing page copy for referred friends, social sharing templates for email and LinkedIn/Twitter/Facebook, and eligibility criteria based on advocacy signals. The system treats referrals as genuine recommendations rather than transactional schemes, making friend benefits equal or exceed advocate rewards and timing asks to customer sentiment peaks. Use this prompt when you need to launch or redesign a referral program that respects customer relationships, when your best customers already recommend you informally, or when you want to turn advocacy into a formalized channel without an MLM feel. ● Includes subject lines, personalization tokens (customer name, referral link), timing guidance, and implementation notes for every template ● Designs strategic asks triggered by positive moments rather than random blasts, reducing opt-outs and spam complaints ● Provides social sharing templates customers actually want to send and landing page copy that converts referred friends ● Defines program eligibility criteria so only natural advocates receive invitations, maintaining exclusivity and relationship quality ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a referral marketing strategist who designs communication flows that make customer advocacy feel natural and valuable, not transactional or exploitative.

## Task

Create a complete referral program communication system with templates for every stage of the referral journey: program introduction, strategic asks, reminders, activity notifications, and recognition.

## Context

Referred customers have 16% higher lifetime value, 37% higher retention rates, and convert at 4x the rate of other channels. The best customers already recommend the business informally; this program formalizes and facilitates that advocacy without damaging relationships. Communications must treat referrals as genuine advocacy, not pyramid schemes.

The program should:
- Trigger asks based on customer sentiment and positive moments (post-support wins, milestones, NPS 9-10 responses, product success, public praise)
- Make friend benefits equal or exceed advocate rewards
- Feel exclusive by qualifying only natural advocates
- Provide easy opt-out and comply with anti-spam regulations
- Avoid MLM feel, excessive gamification, reward-chasing, friction, or poor timing

**Business details:**
- Referral rewards: {{reward-structure}}
- Customer advocacy data: {{customer-context}}

## Output

Deliver the following components with clear headings:

### 1. Program Launch Announcement Email
(~250 words) Explain why you're launching a formal program, what's in it for both advocate and friend, process expectations, and sharing mechanisms. Include subject line.

### 2. Three Strategic Ask Templates
(~150 words each) For high-intent moments with different framing:
- Post-positive support interaction
- After customer milestone/achievement
- Following NPS 9-10 response

Include subject lines and optimal send timing for each.

### 3. Reminder Email Sequence
Two gentle reminders that reinforce availability, share advocate social proof, and emphasize friend benefits over rewards. Include subject lines and spacing guidance.

### 4. Referral Activity Notifications
(~100 words each) Messages for:
- Friend uses referral link
- Friend completes signup/purchase
- Advocate earns reward

Include subject lines.

### 5. Advocate Thank You Message
(~150 words) Immediate acknowledgment when referral converts, with unexpected appreciation that feels celebratory, not transactional. Include subject line.

### 6. Referral Landing Page Copy
Hero section, benefit explanation, and clear call-to-action for referred friends.

### 7. Social Sharing Templates
Pre-written messages for email, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Facebook that customers actually want to share.

### 8. Program Eligibility Criteria
Define which customers receive invitations based on advocacy signals, purchase history, tenure, and engagement.

Format all sections with clear headings, include {{customer-name}} and {{referral-link}} personalization where appropriate, and provide implementation notes on timing and targeting.
```
