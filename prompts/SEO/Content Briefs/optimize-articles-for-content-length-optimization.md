# Content Length Optimization Prompt for SEO Articles

## 簡介

The Content Length Optimization Prompt for SEO Articles is a free AI prompt that helps content strategists and SEO writers produce articles calibrated to the word counts and topical depth of top-ranking search results. This content length optimization prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through SERP analysis of the top 10 results for your target keyword, identifies competitive word-count benchmarks, and generates a complete article structured around the subtopics that actually rank. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your target keyword, audience, content type, industry niche, and desired word count as inputs. The result is a draft article that matches the depth and coverage patterns of high-ranking content while remaining engaging and readable for your audience. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce SEO content that competes not just on keywords but on comprehensive topical coverage and appropriate article length for your niche. ● Analyzes the top 10 SERP results for word count, structure, and topical coverage before drafting ● Flags when your desired word count deviates significantly from competitive benchmarks ● Structures content under clear H2/H3 headings that mirror subtopics found in ranking articles ● Integrates target keywords and semantic variations naturally throughout the draft ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert SEO content strategist specializing in search-optimized article development.

## Task

Create an article optimized for {{target-keyword}} that ranks competitively in search results. Research top-ranking content for the keyword, determine the optimal word count based on SERP analysis, and produce comprehensive content structured for both search engines and reader engagement.

## Context

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Content type:** {{content-type}}

**Industry/niche:** {{industry-niche}}

**Target word count:** {{desired-word-count}}

## Process

1. **SERP Analysis:** Examine the top 10 ranking articles for {{target-keyword}}. Note their word counts, content structure, and topical coverage.

2. **Word Count Calibration:** Determine if {{desired-word-count}} aligns with competitive benchmarks. Flag any significant deviations.

3. **Structure:** Outline the article with a logical hierarchy of ideas that covers the topic comprehensively.

4. **Drafting:** Write the content within the target word count, ensuring:
   - Complete coverage of core subtopics identified in SERP analysis
   - Natural integration of {{target-keyword}} and semantic variations
   - Clear, engaging prose appropriate for {{target-audience}}

5. **Refinement:** Review for keyword density, readability, topical completeness, and engagement.

## Output

Deliver the article with:

- **Introduction** that hooks the reader and establishes relevance
- **Main body** organized under clear H2/H3 headings covering key subtopics
- **Conclusion** that summarizes insights and provides next steps
- Brief note on final word count and any SERP insights that informed structure
```
