# Creative Story Idea Generator From Theme

## 簡介

The Creative Story Idea Generator From Theme is a free AI prompt that produces five distinct narrative concepts, each exploring a given theme from a unique angle for writers, screenwriters, and creative professionals. This story idea prompt for ChatGPT takes a single theme and generates five complete story frameworks, each with a different genre (thriller, sci-fi, historical fiction, romance, fantasy, etc.), setting, character cast, and plot structure. Writers use it to break through creative blocks, explore thematic possibilities before committing to a project, or quickly generate pitches for clients and publishers. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured output that includes genre classification, setting details, key characters, and concise plot summaries for each idea. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple story angles on a theme, want to discover which genre best suits your concept, or need to produce a variety of pitches quickly. ● Produces five non-overlapping story ideas per theme, each with distinct genre, setting, character lineup, and narrative approach ● Structures each concept with labeled components (genre, setting, characters, plot summary) for immediate clarity and usability ● Prioritizes originality and unexpected interpretations rather than clichéd or obvious takes on the theme ● Delivers concise yet evocative descriptions that capture each story's hook and essence without excessive detail ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative story generator with deep knowledge of narrative techniques, genres, and thematic development.

## Task
Generate five diverse story ideas that explore different facets of the given theme. Each idea should demonstrate the theme's versatility through varied genres, settings, characters, and plots.

## Requirements
- Showcase the theme from five distinct angles—no repetition or overlap
- Vary genres (thriller, romance, sci-fi, historical, fantasy, etc.), settings (time periods, locations, scales), character types, and narrative approaches
- Keep descriptions concise yet evocative—enough detail to convey the story's essence and hook
- Prioritize originality and unexpected interpretations of the theme
```
