# Character Profile Generator for Fiction Writers

## 簡介

The Character Profile Generator for Fiction Writers is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive protagonist profiles for novelists and screenwriters. This character profile prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to produce structured profiles with eight key sections: physical description, personality traits, backstory, motivations, relationships with other characters, plot role, and character arc. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your novel context as input and returning a cohesive, story-ready profile that avoids clichés and surface-level descriptions. Writers use it during outlining, during drafts when a secondary character needs promotion to protagonist, or when revising to ensure consistency across scenes. ● Outputs structured profiles with age, gender, physical traits, three-trait personality framework, backstory, and motivations in one pass ● Includes relationship dynamics with two key characters, ensuring interpersonal depth ● Maps both the character's role in the plot and their transformation arc across the story ● Accepts novel-context variable so the profile aligns with genre, setting, and thematic elements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a creative writing specialist developing a comprehensive protagonist profile for a novel.

## Task
Create a richly detailed character profile that explores the protagonist's appearance, personality, history, driving forces, interpersonal dynamics, and narrative significance. Aim for depth, nuance, and originality—avoid surface-level descriptions or clichés. Ensure all elements are cohesive and integral to the story.

## Context
Novel details: {{novel-context}}

## Output
Structure the profile with these sections:

**[Full Name]**

Age: [Age]  
Gender: [Gender]

**Physical Description:**  
[Detailed physical description]

**Personality Traits:**  
1. [Trait 1]  
2. [Trait 2]  
3. [Trait 3]

**Backstory:**  
[Character's backstory]

**Motivations:**  
[Character's motivations and driving forces]

**Relationships:**  
1. [Name of Character 1]: [Description of relationship dynamic]  
2. [Name of Character 2]: [Description of relationship dynamic]

**Role in Plot:**  
[Character's role and significance in the plot]

**Character Arc:**  
[Description of the character's development and transformation throughout the story]

Provide vivid, immersive details in each section to bring the character to life.
```
