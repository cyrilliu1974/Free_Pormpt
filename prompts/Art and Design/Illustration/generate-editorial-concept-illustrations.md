# Editorial Concept Illustration Generator

## 簡介

The Editorial Concept Illustration Generator is a free AI prompt that creates thoughtful, metaphor-driven illustrations for brands, publishers, and organizations communicating complex ideas visually. This editorial illustration prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces clean line-art compositions featuring simplified human figures, abstract geometric buildings arranged vertically, and a central symbolic object that serves as the visual anchor. The aesthetic is minimalist black-and-white with strategic flat color accents in green, yellow, and blue applied only to key elements. You control three variables: the underlying concept, the main action being depicted, and the symbolic object that carries the metaphor. The result is publication-ready artwork that feels thoughtful, hopeful, and editorial in tone. Reach for this prompt when you need visual storytelling for sustainability reports, thought leadership articles, corporate presentations, or any context where abstract ideas must be communicated through accessible, symbolic imagery. ● Produces balanced vertical compositions with geometric architecture, minimalist human figures, and a central symbolic object as the focal metaphor. ● Maintains a cohesive black-and-white line-art aesthetic with flat accent colors applied selectively to buildings or symbols for visual interest. ● Outputs clean, publication-ready illustrations suitable for sustainability reports, brand storytelling, and thought leadership content. ● Avoids photorealism, gradients, and clutter in favor of simplified, editorial-style visual language that communicates ideas clearly. ## Prompt

```
Create a modern editorial illustration representing {{concept}}. The scene shows {{main-action}} in a symbolic and metaphorical way. Human figures are simplified, minimal, and stylized, drawn with thin black line art and calm, neutral expressions. Buildings are geometric and abstract, stacked vertically to form a balanced structure. At the top of the composition, place {{symbol-object}} as the main visual metaphor. Use a limited color palette with flat accent colors—green, yellow, and blue—applied only to selected elements such as buildings or the symbol. All other elements remain black and white. No gradients, no realistic shadows. Clean white background. The overall feeling should be thoughtful, hopeful, and editorial.

Negative prompt: photorealistic, 3D render, realistic anatomy, detailed faces, gradients, shadows, neon colors, cluttered background, cartoon style
```
