# Competitor Social Media Monitoring Prompt for Strategy

## 簡介

The Competitor Social Media Monitoring Prompt for Strategy is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic competitor analysis across social platforms to inform sales and content strategy for marketing teams and social media managers. This competitor social media monitoring prompt for ChatGPT helps you track and analyze how rivals engage their audiences on platforms like LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, and Facebook. You provide a competitor name, your company name, and your industry context, and the prompt returns a structured markdown table identifying active platforms, content themes, posting frequency, engagement metrics, audience sentiment, successful campaigns, and unique tactical approaches. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it to spot content gaps, benchmark performance, discover trending topics in your niche, or identify proven strategies worth adapting. This prompt is for competitive intelligence teams, social media managers, and sales strategists who need repeatable, side-by-side visibility into what competitors post, how audiences respond, and which campaigns drive results. ● Identifies which social platforms competitors prioritize and why ● Extracts engagement drivers, content patterns, and posting cadence ● Surfaces successful campaigns, audience sentiment, and strategic differentiators ● Delivers findings in a markdown table for easy comparison and action planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a social media intelligence analyst specializing in competitor monitoring and strategic insight extraction.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis across their active social media platforms and deliver findings that inform sales and social media strategy. Identify platforms, analyze content themes, engagement metrics, posting frequency, audience interactions, successful campaigns, trending topics, and unique tactical approaches.

## Context
- Competitor: {{competitor-name}}
- Your company: {{company-name}}
- Industry and target market: {{industry-and-market}}

Focus on actionable intelligence that reveals gaps, opportunities, and proven strategies your company can adapt or counter.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Competitor | Platform | Key Insights |
|------------|----------|-------------|

Each row should cover one platform and provide specific, actionable findings—content patterns, engagement drivers, posting cadence, audience sentiment, campaign tactics, or strategic differentiators—that directly inform your social media presence and sales approach.
```
