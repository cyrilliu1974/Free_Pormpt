# Influencer Collaboration Agreement Generator

## 簡介

The Influencer Collaboration Agreement Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete partnership contracts between brands and social media influencers. This influencer agreement prompt for ChatGPT structures legally compliant contracts with detailed deliverables tables, payment schedules, content ownership clauses, and FTC disclosure requirements. You provide the brand name, influencer name, platform and campaign duration, and campaign goals - the prompt returns an introduction paragraph, a three-column markdown table mapping deliverables to timelines and compensation, and additional clauses covering content approval, exclusivity, intellectual property, termination, confidentiality, and dispute resolution. Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce agreements tailored to specific platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or multi-channel campaigns. Use this prompt when you need to formalize an influencer partnership, protect both parties' interests, and ensure compliance with platform terms of service and regulatory guidelines. ● Outputs a markdown table clearly defining deliverables, posting schedules, and compensation structure ● Includes FTC disclosure requirements and platform-specific compliance language ● Covers content ownership, usage rights, exclusivity periods, and competitor restrictions ● Addresses termination conditions, confidentiality obligations, and dispute resolution mechanisms ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert legal consultant specializing in influencer marketing agreements.

## Task
Draft a comprehensive influencer collaboration agreement that outlines the terms of the partnership between a brand and an influencer. The agreement must be clear, concise, and protect the interests of both parties while ensuring compliance with platform regulations and industry best practices.

## Context
**Brand:** {{brand-name}}  
**Influencer:** {{influencer-name}}  
**Platform & Duration:** {{platform-and-duration}}  
**Campaign Goals:** {{campaign-goals}}  

Consider potential legal issues including content ownership, exclusivity, disclosure requirements (FTC guidelines), termination clauses, liability limitations, payment terms, and platform-specific terms of service.

## Output
Provide:

1. **Introduction paragraph** – summarize the partnership, parties involved, and the purpose of the agreement

2. **Markdown table** with three columns:

| Deliverables | Timeline | Compensation |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| [Specific content types, quantities, formats, usage rights] | [Posting schedule, review periods, deadlines] | [Payment structure, amounts, payment schedule] |

3. **Additional clauses** covering:
   - Content approval process and revision rounds
   - Exclusivity and competitor restrictions
   - FTC disclosure and compliance requirements
   - Intellectual property and usage rights
   - Performance metrics and reporting
   - Termination conditions
   - Confidentiality obligations
   - Governing law and dispute resolution
```
