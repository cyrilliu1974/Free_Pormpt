# Problem-Solution Pitch Structure Builder

## 簡介

The Problem-Solution Pitch Structure Builder is a free AI prompt that creates persuasive pitch outlines for marketing strategists and sales professionals. This problem-solution pitch prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a logical, six-section framework: core problem identification, solution overview, features and benefits, unique selling proposition, supporting evidence, and call-to-action. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by asking for your product or service, target audience, industry context, and USP, then generating a cohesive narrative that demonstrates clear cause-and-effect relationships between customer challenges and your solution. Use it when preparing sales presentations, client proposals, investor decks, or marketing collateral that needs to articulate value quickly and persuasively. ● Identifies the core problem your target audience faces and frames your solution as the answer ● Outlines key features and benefits with clear value propositions ● Emphasizes your unique selling proposition and competitive differentiation ● Includes sections for supporting evidence, testimonials, and data to validate claims ● Concludes with a specific, actionable call-to-action that drives next steps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist creating a compelling pitch structure.

## Task
Develop a persuasive pitch that highlights problem-solving capabilities and unique solutions. Structure the writing to ensure logical flow and coherent presentation of ideas through clear dependency relationships between concepts.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Industry & Competitive Landscape:** {{industry-context}}
**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

## Output
Deliver a structured outline using headings and subheadings that covers:

1. **Core Problem** – Identify the central challenge your target audience faces
2. **Solution Overview** – Articulate how your product/service resolves that problem
3. **Features & Benefits** – Outline key capabilities and their direct value to users
4. **Unique Selling Proposition** – Emphasize what differentiates you from competitors
5. **Supporting Evidence** – Present proof points, testimonials, or data that validate your claims
6. **Call-to-Action** – Conclude with a strong, specific next step

Ensure each section builds logically on the previous one, creating a cohesive narrative that demonstrates clear cause-and-effect relationships between the problem and your solution.
```
