# Douyin Flash Selfie Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Douyin Flash Selfie Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates hyper-symmetrical, influencer-style selfie portraits with dramatic frontal flash lighting and ethereal night bokeh backgrounds. This Douyin portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces extreme close-up selfies featuring porcelain glass skin with zero texture, aegyosal-highlighted puppy eyes, gradient lips, and the signature flat-lit aesthetic that dominates Asian social media platforms. It simulates smartphone camera flash photography with f/1.8 aperture, 9:16 vertical framing, and creamy circular bokeh from urban night lights. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping vertical content optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or Douyin that captures the coveted doll-like, high-key beauty standard. ● Produces porcelain 'glass skin' and 'mochi skin' effects with high-key flash that eliminates texture and creates translucent T-zone highlights. ● Renders signature puppy dog eyes with prominent aegyosal under-eye bands, manhwa-style clumped lashes, and razor-sharp iris focus. ● Generates gradient lip technique with saturated glossy centers fading to blurred nude edges, petite low-bridge nose, and heart-shaped facial proportions. ● Simulates smartphone wide-angle lens distortion (24mm) with f/1.8-f/2.2 aperture for instant background blur and creamy urban bokeh circles against pitch-black night environments. ## Prompt

```
## Subject
{{subject-description}}: hyper-symmetrical, doll-like features with 'bunny tongue' and 'puppy eye' aesthetic. Pale, cool-toned porcelain complexion with zero texture, reflecting flash for 'mochi' or 'glass skin' effect—soft, translucent, high-key brightness on T-zone. Large, round eyes with slight downward tilt at outer corners (puppy dog eyes), prominent 'aegyosal' (under-eye fat bands) highlighted for youthfulness, soft brown irises with large diameter. Eyelashes in 'manhwa'/'idol' style: distinct, vertical clumps of mascara-coated lashes separated by space, not dense fan. Petite, low-bridge nose with small, rounded tip, lighting minimizes nostril definition. Heart-shaped lips with 'gradient lip' technique—{{lip-color}} saturated and glossy at center, fading outward to blurred pale nude at vermilion border, soft hydrated texture. {{hair-description}}, catching flash with subtle sheen. Minimal visibility of clothing, suggesting stylish casual top.

## Frame & Composition
Extreme close-up selfie framing (tighter than standard portrait), 9:16 aspect ratio. Cut off top of forehead to focus intensely on eyes and lips. Subject centered with direct, confronting gaze toward camera.

## Environment & Lighting
Urban night setting, clear calm weather. Deep black shadows punctuated by creamy, circular bokeh from distant city lights (streetlamps, neon signs). Background significantly darker than subject, face is sole focus.

Direct frontal camera flash or high-intensity screen light. Hard but flattering light that flattens facial topography, eliminating shadows under eyes and nose, creating 2D illustrative quality. High contrast between brightly illuminated face and pitch-black environment. Sharp, tiny pinpoint catchlight reflection in center of pupils from flash.

## Camera Technique
Smartphone main sensor simulation, 24mm wide-angle lens (slightly exaggerates eye size, diminishes nose and face width for 'baby face' proportion). f/1.8 to f/2.2 aperture, eyes and lips razor sharp, ears and background instantly blurred. Critical focus on eyelashes and iris texture.

## Style
Flash photography, digital influencer aesthetic, soft-focus realism. Hyper-idealized 'Douyin-aesthetic' portrait with high-contrast, ethereal look.

## Exclude
No snow, rain, wet hair, masculine jawline, skin texture, pores, heavy contouring, western makeup style, sunglasses, hand near face. No cinematic dramatic shadows (must be flat lit), no warm vintage tones, no painting, no illustration.
```
