# Technology White Paper Generator for Emerging Tech

## 簡介

The Technology White Paper Generator for Emerging Tech is a free AI prompt that produces structured, evidence-backed white papers analyzing emerging technologies and innovations for industry stakeholders and decision-makers. This technology white paper prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through creating multi-section documents that balance technical depth with accessibility. The prompt structures analysis from problem definition through technical solution architecture, real-world applications, supporting case studies, and future outlook. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, generating executive summaries, benefits analyses, implementation challenges, and comprehensive reference sections. Users input a specific technology or innovation and target industry, and receive a complete white paper framework ready for research and customization. Designed for product marketers, technical writers, innovation consultants, and business analysts who need to produce persuasive, authoritative documents evaluating new technologies for stakeholders. ● Produces complete white paper structure including executive summary, problem definition, technical solution architecture, benefits analysis, case studies, and challenges ● Enforces evidence-backed claims with requirements for statistical data, expert perspectives, and comparative analysis against alternatives ● Addresses counterarguments and limitations to maintain credibility and balanced viewpoint ● Generates industry-specific applications and real-world use cases tailored to the target sector ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a technology and innovation expert specializing in comprehensive white papers on emerging technologies across industries.

## Task
Write a well-researched white paper analyzing an emerging technology or innovation within a specified industry. Examine the problem it addresses, its technical solution, benefits, potential applications, and future impact. Support all claims with evidence, case studies, and expert perspectives.

## Context
White papers serve as authoritative resources for stakeholders and decision-makers evaluating new technologies. Your analysis must balance depth with accessibility, avoiding excessive jargon while maintaining technical credibility. Address counterarguments and limitations to provide balanced perspective.

## Output
Structure the white paper as follows:

**Title:** White Paper: {{technology-innovation}} in {{industry}}

**Executive Summary**
- Brief overview of the technology/innovation
- Key highlights and findings

**Introduction**
- Industry background and context
- Problem landscape
- Thesis statement on potential impact

**Problem Definition**
- Detailed problem explanation
- Current limitations and challenges
- Implications of inaction

**Technical Solution**
- In-depth description of how the technology works
- Key components and architecture
- Technical advantages over existing solutions
- Scalability and future-proofing considerations

**Benefits and Applications**
- Key implementation benefits
- Sector-specific applications
- Real-world examples and use cases
- Future possibilities and long-term implications

**Evidence and Case Studies**
- Statistical data demonstrating effectiveness
- Successful implementation case studies
- Expert opinions and testimonials
- Comparative analysis with alternatives

**Challenges and Considerations**
- Adoption and implementation obstacles
- Regulatory and ethical considerations
- Counterarguments and acknowledged limitations

**Conclusion**
- Key findings recap
- Future outlook
- Call-to-action for stakeholders and decision-makers

**References**
- Cited sources and additional resources
```
