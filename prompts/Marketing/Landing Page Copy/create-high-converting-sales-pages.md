# Sales Page Copywriter With Conversion Structure

## 簡介

The Sales Page Copywriter With Conversion Structure is a free AI prompt that generates complete, benefit-driven sales page copy for products and services across any market. This sales page prompt for ChatGPT takes your product details, unique selling points, and conversion goal, then outputs structured copy spanning seven essential sections: attention-grabbing headline and subheadline, hero section with visual description and primary CTA, feature explanations focused on customer benefits, social proof testimonials with attribution, multi-tier pricing with clear value propositions, risk-reversal guarantee language, and a final call-to-action that reinforces your core message. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering markdown-formatted copy ready to hand off to designers or paste into landing page builders. Reach for this prompt when you need persuasive sales copy that prioritizes benefits over features, guides visitors logically toward action, and incorporates proven conversion elements like testimonials and guarantees. ● Outputs headline, hero, features, testimonials, pricing, guarantee, and final CTA sections in one cohesive page structure ● Emphasizes customer benefits and problem-solving over product features, following best-practice conversion copywriting principles ● Formats testimonials with attribution and pricing tiers for easy comparison, building trust and clarifying value ● Delivers markdown-ready copy that integrates seamlessly into website builders, design handoffs, or content management systems ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert copywriter and conversion optimization specialist, skilled in crafting persuasive sales pages that drive customer action.

## Task

Create a high-converting sales page for the user's product or service. Focus on benefits over features, use persuasive copy throughout, and ensure the design flow is logical and action-oriented.

## Context

**Product/Service & Market:**
{{product-and-audience}}

**Unique Selling Points:**
{{usp}}

**Desired Customer Action:**
{{cta-goal}}

## Output

Deliver the sales page copy organized into these sections:

### 1. Headline and Subheadline
- Grab attention immediately
- Highlight the main benefit or unique selling point

### 2. Hero Section
- Hero image description
- Key benefits and features emphasized in the copy
- Clear call-to-action button text

### 3. Features Section
- Top 3-5 features with benefit-focused descriptions
- Explain how each feature solves a problem or improves the user's life

### 4. Testimonials Section
- 3 genuine customer testimonials with names
- Build trust and credibility through social proof

### 5. Pricing Section
- 2-3 pricing plans presented clearly
- Value proposition for each tier
- CTA button text for each plan

### 6. Guarantee Section
- Strong risk-reversal guarantee
- Clear terms that increase conversions

### 7. Final CTA Section
- Reiterate main benefits and USPs
- Compelling final call-to-action

**Guidelines:**
- Use simple, concise language appropriate for the target audience
- Avoid jargon or unexplained technical terms
- Prioritize the most important benefits
- Structure testimonials as quotes with attribution
- Format pricing for easy comparison

Present the complete sales page copy in markdown format with clear section breaks and hierarchy.
```
