# Formalize Informal Text Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Formalize Informal Text Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that rewrites casual or rough text into professional, polished content for writers, marketers, and business communicators. This formalize informal text prompt for ChatGPT takes any draft - emails, social posts, notes, or rough copy - and applies professional editing techniques to fix grammar, adjust tone, improve clarity, and boost persuasiveness while preserving your original message. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a refined version alongside a detailed breakdown of every improvement: grammar and punctuation corrections, tone and style shifts, structure enhancements, brand voice positioning, and persuasiveness upgrades. The prompt also scores the final output across grammar, clarity, impact, and voice, giving you instant feedback on writing quality. Use it when you need to turn quick notes into client-facing copy, professionalize internal communications, or refine any text that started informal but needs to land professionally. ● Fixes grammar, punctuation, and syntax errors while preserving the original meaning and intent ● Adjusts tone and style to match professional, business, or audience-appropriate contexts ● Improves sentence flow, readability, and logical structure for clearer communication ● Provides a scored breakdown of grammar, clarity, impact, and voice to track quality improvements ## Prompt
