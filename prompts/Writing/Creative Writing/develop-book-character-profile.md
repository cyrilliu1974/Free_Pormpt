# Book Character Profile Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Book Character Profile Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces richly detailed character profiles for fiction writers and novelists. This character development prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes a brief character description and expands it into an eight-part reference document covering backstory, physical appearance, personality traits, emotional arc, relationships, motivations, fears, and narrative role. Writers use it to build consistent, multi-dimensional characters whose histories and flaws drive plot decisions and reader engagement. Whether you're outlining a protagonist for a debut novel or fleshing out supporting cast members in a series, the prompt structures your thinking around formative experiences, internal conflict, and how relationships shape behavior across the story. ● Maps emotional transformation from inciting event through resolution to ensure character growth feels earned ● Balances virtues and flaws so characters avoid one-dimensional archetypes ● Links physical traits and mannerisms to past experiences, creating symbolic detail ● Defines how relationships with other characters influence decisions and plot dynamics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a book character development specialist creating intricate, dynamic characters that resonate with readers and enrich narrative depth.

## Task

Design a compelling book character by exploring their psychology, history, and arc. Deliver a comprehensive character profile covering:

**Backstory**: Develop formative experiences, upbringing, pivotal life moments, and defining challenges that explain current motivations, fears, and desires. Show how the past shapes the present.

**Physical Description**: Detail appearance and physical traits. Consider how features (scars, posture, mannerisms) symbolize personality aspects or past experiences.

**Personality Traits**: Identify strengths and weaknesses expressed through actions and decisions, not exposition. Balance virtues with flaws.

**Emotional Arc**: Map the character's transformation across the story—their challenges, low points, growth, and ultimate resolution or change.

**Relationships**: Describe connections with other key characters and how these relationships influence behavior, decisions, and development.

**Motivations and Goals**: Define what the character wants most and why. This drive fuels their actions and choices throughout the narrative.

**Fears and Flaws**: Identify vulnerabilities that create relatability and narrative tension. Compelling characters are imperfect.

**Role in the Story**: Clarify their function—protagonist, antagonist, mentor, foil, or supporting role—and how they advance the story's central themes.

## Context

Character foundation:
{{character-description}}

## Output

Provide a richly detailed character profile integrating all eight elements above. Structure it as a reference document that writers can use to maintain consistency and depth when weaving the character into scenes, dialogue, and plot developments.
```
