# Social Media Caption Generator for Brand Engagement

## 簡介

The Social Media Caption Generator for Brand Engagement is a free AI prompt that produces platform-optimized captions designed to drive brand awareness and conversions for marketers and social media managers. This social media caption prompt for ChatGPT creates 3-5 ready-to-post captions tailored to your product, audience, and platform. Each caption incorporates strategic hashtag placement (3-5 per post), emoji integration, and a clear call-to-action, all structured using dependency grammar principles that guide reader attention toward key benefits and CTAs. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering captions that balance persuasive flow with platform-native formatting. Real use cases include product launches, seasonal campaigns, and daily brand content across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, and Twitter. Reach for this prompt when you need to produce multiple caption variations quickly while maintaining consistent brand voice and incorporating your unique selling points into every post. ● Produces 3-5 caption variations per request, each with hashtags, emojis, and CTAs formatted for immediate posting ● Applies dependency grammar framework to create captions where main clauses carry core messages and dependent clauses layer in urgency, benefits, or emotional hooks ● Customizes tone and messaging to match your specified brand voice, target audience, platform conventions, and unique selling points ● Includes grammar explanations for each caption, showing how sentence structure guides reader attention toward conversion goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media marketer creating engaging captions that drive brand awareness and conversions.

## Task
Write 3-5 compelling social media captions for {{product-or-service}} targeted at {{audience}}. Each caption must include:

- Relevant hashtags (3-5 per caption)
- Strategic emoji placement
- A clear call-to-action
- Dependency grammar structure that creates natural, persuasive flow (main clause carries the core message, dependent clauses layer in benefits, urgency, or emotional hooks)

## Context
Platform: {{platform}}

Brand voice: {{brand-voice}}

Unique selling points: {{usp}}

Dependency grammar builds sentences where modifiers and subordinate clauses branch from a strong main clause, creating rhythm and emphasis. Use this to guide attention toward your CTA and key benefits.

## Output
Deliver as a bullet list. For each caption, provide:

- The complete caption (ready to post)
- Brief explanation: which element serves as the main clause and how dependent clauses support it

Example structure:
- **Caption:** [full text with hashtags, emojis, CTA]
  *Grammar note:* Main clause = [X], dependent clauses = [Y] add urgency/benefit/social proof
```
