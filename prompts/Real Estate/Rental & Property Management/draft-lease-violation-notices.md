# Draft Lease Violation Notices

## 簡介

The Draft Lease Violation Notices prompt is a free AI prompt that produces formal, legally structured violation letters for landlords and property managers who need to document tenant non-compliance. This lease violation notice prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms your violation details into a complete business letter with standard formatting, objective factual language, specific lease clause citations, measurable corrective actions, state-compliant deadlines, and consequences for continued non-compliance. The output is designed to serve as both a tenant communication tool and a potential court document if the situation escalates to eviction proceedings. Property managers and landlords reach for this prompt when informal phone calls and emails have failed and formal written documentation is required to protect the property and enforce lease terms. ● Produces complete business letters with date blocks, recipient addresses, subject lines, and signature blocks ready for immediate delivery without further editing. ● Cites specific lease clause numbers and describes violations in objective, non-accusatory language suitable for court documentation. ● Sets compliance deadlines aligned with state landlord-tenant law and outlines measurable corrective actions tenants must complete. ● Includes consequences for non-compliance, documentation requirements, and professional tone that balances firmness with fairness. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a property management legal specialist with expertise in landlord-tenant law and lease enforcement.

## Task
Draft a formal lease violation notice that is legally sound, firm yet fair, and structured as a ready-to-send business letter. The notice must clearly identify the violation, cite the specific lease provision, set a compliance deadline, and outline consequences—all while maintaining professionalism.

## Context
This notice represents an escalation from informal communication and may serve as a court document if the situation advances to eviction. Use objective, factual language without emotional or accusatory tone. Ensure compliance deadlines align with applicable state law.

## Input
{{violation-details}}
Provide: tenant name, property address, detailed description of the observed violation, the specific lease clause or section number breached, state/jurisdiction, and the number of days allowed for compliance (based on your state's legal requirements).

## Output
Deliver a complete formal lease violation notice with:

- Standard business letter formatting (date, recipient address block, subject line)
- Clear statement of the violation in objective, factual terms
- Reference to the specific lease clause violated
- Concrete description of the corrective action required in measurable terms
- Compliance deadline aligned with state law
- Consequences of continued non-compliance (including potential lease termination and eviction)
- Documentation requirements if applicable (proof of correction, photographs)
- Professional signature block ready for the landlord's signature

The notice must be immediately usable and require no further editing before delivery.
```
