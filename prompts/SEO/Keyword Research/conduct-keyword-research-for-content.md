# Keyword Research for Content Planning Prompt

## 簡介

The Keyword Research for Content Planning Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts systematic SEO keyword research to identify content opportunities and optimize search engine rankings for digital marketers and content strategists. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT analyzes both primary and long-tail keywords, evaluates search volume and competition difficulty, maps user intent (informational, navigational, transactional), and surfaces content gaps in your market. It produces a markdown table with keyword data plus a written analysis covering the competitive landscape, high-impact opportunities, and prioritized recommendations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your business context and competitor information to generate actionable SEO insights. Reach for this prompt when planning content calendars, entering new markets, auditing existing SEO strategies, or building topic clusters that align with actual search behavior. ● Identifies primary keywords and long-tail variations tailored to your business context and competitive landscape ● Analyzes search volume, keyword difficulty, and user intent to prioritize high-value opportunities ● Surfaces content gaps where competitors rank but you do not, highlighting quick-win topics ● Delivers strategic recommendations ranked by potential impact, not generic lists ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital marketer specializing in search engine optimization (SEO).

## Task
Conduct comprehensive keyword research to optimize content planning and improve search engine rankings. Identify relevant keywords, analyze their search volume, assess difficulty levels, and provide strategic recommendations.

## Context
Business and market context: {{business-context}}

Competitor landscape: {{competitors}}

Follow this methodology:
1. Identify primary and long-tail keywords relevant to the business
2. Analyze search volume to determine keyword popularity
3. Assess keyword difficulty to understand competition level
4. Consider user intent behind each keyword (informational, navigational, transactional)
5. Identify content gaps and opportunities in the current market
6. Provide actionable recommendations for content creation

## Output
Deliver your findings in a markdown table with three columns: **Keywords**, **Search Volume**, and **Difficulty**.

Below the table, provide:
- Brief analysis of the keyword landscape
- Content opportunities identified
- Strategic recommendations prioritized by potential impact
```
