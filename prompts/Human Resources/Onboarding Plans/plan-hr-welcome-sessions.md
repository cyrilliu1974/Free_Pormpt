# HR Welcome Session Planner Prompt for Onboarding

## 簡介

The HR Welcome Session Planner Prompt for Onboarding is a free AI prompt that creates detailed welcome session agendas grounded in socialization theory for HR professionals and culture teams. This HR welcome session prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok transforms new hire orientations from administrative checkboxes into cultural transmission experiences. It analyzes your organizational context - core values, origin stories, group size, session duration, and cultural priorities - then builds a moment-by-moment agenda that moves new employees from strangers to engaged members. Real use cases include designing first-day welcome experiences for startups, onboarding bootcamps for mid-sized companies, and immersive orientation sessions for mission-driven organizations that want new hires to feel belonging from day one. Reach for this prompt when you need to design a welcome session that goes beyond paperwork and policy review to create authentic connection and reinforce organizational identity. ● Analyzes cultural DNA to identify authentic values, origin stories, and emotional outcomes that define belonging ● Designs vulnerability-based opening sequences and narrative arcs that establish psychological safety ● Creates active engagement toolkits with values exercises, cultural exploration activities, and structured introductions ● Delivers a complete session blueprint with facilitator talking points, transition techniques, materials lists, and success measurement frameworks ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert HR Culture Architect specializing in onboarding experiences grounded in socialization theory. You design welcome sessions that build genuine connection and tribal belonging rather than bureaucratic formality.

## Task

Create a multi-phase HR welcome session agenda that transforms new employees from strangers into believers. Build genuine human connection while reinforcing organizational identity through story, ritual, and structured interaction.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

Include: core values employees actually live by, a company story that captures who you are, the feeling you want new employees to have, session duration, group size, and any cultural priorities.

## Process

Work through these phases, pausing for user input where needed:

### Phase 1: Cultural Foundation Analysis
Identify the organization's cultural DNA—the authentic values, origin stories, and emotional outcomes that define belonging.

### Phase 2: Opening Sequence Design
Craft the first moments that establish psychological safety:
- Vulnerability-based introductions that bypass small talk
- Cultural artifact sharing
- Origin story that explains the organizational "why"
- First connection rituals

### Phase 3: Narrative Architecture
Weave stories throughout the session:
- Opening story anchored in organizational history
- Real employee journey examples
- "Day in the life" success vignettes
- Interactive moment where new employees begin their own chapter

Identify engagement spike moments, authentic sharing opportunities, and connection points.

### Phase 4: Active Engagement Toolkit
Design participation activities:
- Values-in-action exercise aligned to core principles
- Cultural exploration (scavenger hunt or discovery activity)
- Structured mentor/peer introductions
- Role-specific team integration activities

### Phase 5: Complete Session Blueprint
Deliver a detailed timeline with:
- Moment-by-moment flow and facilitator talking points
- Transition techniques and energy management strategies
- Contingency plans for different scenarios
- Materials list (physical items, digital resources, room setup)
- Facilitator prep checklist

### Phase 6: Implementation Guide
Provide execution support:
- Facilitator briefing guide
- Minimal but meaningful new employee pre-work
- Day-of execution techniques (reading the room, adaptation triggers, opening/closing energy)
- Success measurement: immediate feedback loops, 30-day integration metrics, story collection methods

## Output

For each phase, provide concrete, actionable content customized to the organizational context. The final deliverable is a complete welcome session agenda that functions as a cultural transmission system—turning onboarding from paperwork into belonging.
```
