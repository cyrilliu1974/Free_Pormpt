# Content Idea Generator for Business Goals

## 簡介

The Content Idea Generator for Business Goals is a free AI prompt that develops five tailored content concepts aligned to specific business objectives and audience segments. This content idea generator prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context - industry, goals, audience demographics, and current content performance - then produces a structured table of five distinct content ideas. Each idea specifies a concrete content type (video series, interactive quiz, LinkedIn carousel, case study, podcast), identifies the target audience segment, and articulates a key message that advances your business goal. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and asks the AI to assess audience preferences and industry trends before brainstorming, ensuring ideas reflect real performance gaps or opportunities. Use this prompt when you need strategic, audience-specific content concepts that connect directly to measurable business outcomes, whether you're launching a campaign, refreshing a content calendar, or exploring new formats. ● Outputs five content ideas in a markdown table with content type, target audience, and key message columns ● Prompts the AI to analyze audience preferences and industry trends before generating ideas ● Requires business context (industry, goal, audience, current performance) and a topic focus as input ● Ensures each idea addresses performance gaps and aligns with strategic business outcomes ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a content strategist developing innovative content concepts that drive measurable business outcomes.

## Task
Generate 5 distinct content ideas tailored to the business context and audience provided. For each idea:
- Specify a concrete content type (e.g., video series, interactive quiz, case study, LinkedIn carousel, podcast)
- Identify which audience segment it targets
- Articulate a clear key message that advances the business goal

Analyze audience preferences and current trends in the industry before brainstorming. Ensure ideas reflect the performance gaps or opportunities indicated in the current content baseline.

## Context
**Business Context:** {{business-context}}
(Include: industry, specific business goal, target audience demographics/psychographics, and a brief summary of current content performance)

**Topic Focus:** {{topic}}

## Output
Present your ideas in a markdown table:

| Content Type | Target Audience | Key Message |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|
| ... | ... | ... |
| ... | ... | ... |
| ... | ... | ... |
| ... | ... | ... |
| ... | ... | ... |
```
