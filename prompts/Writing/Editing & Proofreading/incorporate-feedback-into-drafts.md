# Incorporate Feedback Into Drafts Prompt

## 簡介

The Incorporate Feedback Into Drafts Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms editorial feedback into concrete document improvements for writers, editors, and content teams. This feedback incorporation prompt for ChatGPT guides large language models to systematically analyze feedback from reviewers, stakeholders, or clients and apply dependency grammar principles to strengthen sentence structure, paragraph flow, and overall clarity. It works across any document type - from business proposals and research papers to marketing copy and technical documentation - and runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. The prompt structures the revision process into discrete phases: reviewing feedback alongside the original text, prioritizing improvement areas, restructuring sentences for optimal readability, implementing changes, and ensuring stylistic consistency throughout. Each revision is documented with an explanation of its impact on document quality. This prompt is ideal for editors managing client feedback, content teams refining drafts based on stakeholder input, or writers who want to methodically address critique without losing their voice. It turns vague feedback into actionable edits while maintaining coherence across the entire document. ● Analyzes feedback from any source - clients, peers, supervisors, or automated tools - and prioritizes actionable improvements. ● Applies dependency grammar frameworks to restructure sentences for better flow and comprehension. ● Documents each major revision with clear explanations of how it enhances document quality. ● Ensures tone, style, and formatting remain consistent across all changes. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert editor specializing in document revision and quality enhancement.

## Task
Revise and improve the provided document by addressing specific feedback. Apply dependency grammar principles to strengthen sentence structure, flow, and clarity. Work systematically through the document to ensure each revision elevates its overall quality.

## Context
**Document type:** {{document-type}}
**Feedback source:** {{feedback-source}}
**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Additional context:** {{context}}

## Process
1. Review the original document alongside the feedback
2. Identify priority improvement areas from the feedback
3. Apply dependency grammar to restructure sentences and paragraphs for optimal flow
4. Implement revisions that address each feedback point
5. Ensure consistency in tone, style, and formatting throughout
6. Summarize changes and their impact on document quality

## Output
Structure your response with clearly labeled sections:
- **Major Revision [N]:** Description of the change
- **Explanation:** Why this change improves the document
- **Summary of Changes:** Overall impact assessment and quality improvements achieved
```
