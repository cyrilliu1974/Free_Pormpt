# Elevator Pitch Generator for Business Networking

## 簡介

The Elevator Pitch Generator for Business Networking is a free AI prompt that creates concise, compelling pitches tailored to your target audience and industry. This elevator pitch prompt for ChatGPT guides you through crafting a 30-60 second presentation structured in five distinct sections: an introduction hook, a problem statement identifying your audience's pain point, your unique solution, tangible benefits and differentiators, and a clear call-to-action. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing pitches that use persuasive, action-oriented language while remaining deliverable in under one minute. Teams use it to prepare for investor meetings, sales calls, conference introductions, and networking events where first impressions matter. This prompt is built for entrepreneurs, sales professionals, and business developers who need a memorable pitch that communicates value quickly and invites engagement. ● Takes your business overview and target audience as inputs, then structures a pitch around core value proposition elements ● Organizes content into introduction, problem, solution, benefits, and call-to-action sections for logical flow and maximum clarity ● Emphasizes persuasive, action-oriented language that creates urgency and resonates with your specific industry and audience ● Ensures the complete pitch is memorable, impactful, and deliverable in 30-60 seconds at live events ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business communicator specializing in elevator pitches for networking events.

## Task
Develop a concise, compelling 30-60 second elevator pitch that communicates the unique value proposition and captivates potential clients or investors.

## Context
Business and value proposition:
{{business-overview}}

Target audience and industry:
{{audience-and-industry}}

## Approach
- Identify and emphasize the core value proposition elements
- Structure sentences for maximum conciseness and clarity
- Use persuasive, action-oriented language that creates urgency and resonance
- Tailor vocabulary and framing to the specified audience

## Output
Deliver the elevator pitch in five clearly labeled sections:

**Introduction** – Hook that establishes credibility and relevance

**Problem Statement** – The pain point or opportunity your audience faces

**Solution** – How your business uniquely addresses this

**Benefits** – Tangible outcomes and differentiators for the audience

**Call-to-Action** – Clear next step that invites engagement

Each section should be 1-2 sentences. The complete pitch must be memorable, impactful, and deliverable in under one minute.
```
