# Industry Trend Analysis Whitepaper Generator

## 簡介

The Industry Trend Analysis Whitepaper Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, evidence-based whitepapers analyzing emerging trends, market shifts, and disruptive forces for business analysts and market researchers. This industry trend analysis prompt for ChatGPT works by guiding the AI to conduct research on your target industry, identify three major consumer trends with supporting data, and package the findings into a professional whitepaper format complete with executive summary, methodology section, detailed trend breakdowns with strategic implications, actionable recommendations, and a full reference list in APA or MLA format. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming a single industry variable into a multi-section report ready for strategic planning sessions, stakeholder presentations, or client deliverables. Real-world applications include competitive intelligence briefings, quarterly market updates, investment research documents, and strategic planning foundations. Reach for this prompt when you need to quickly synthesize market intelligence into a credible, citation-backed document that supports decision-making. ● Outputs a complete whitepaper structure: executive summary, introduction with methodology, three detailed trend analyses, strategic recommendations, conclusion, and formatted references ● Requires evidence and data citations for every insight, ensuring your analysis is defensible and professional ● Includes strategic implications for each trend, connecting market shifts directly to business actions ● Formats output with clear headings, bullet points, and academic citation standards for immediate use in reports and presentations ## Prompt

```

You are a business analyst and market researcher specializing in emerging trends. Conduct comprehensive research on the specified industry, identifying key consumer trends, market shifts, and disruptive forces, then synthesize findings into a well-structured whitepaper.

## Task

- Research methodology and data sources used

**Trend 1**

- Trend Description: Detailed analysis of the trend, its drivers, and how it manifests
- Implications: Strategic implications for businesses in this industry

**Trend 2**

**Trend 3**
```
