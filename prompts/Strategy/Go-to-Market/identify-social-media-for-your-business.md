# Social Media Platform Selector for Business Growth

## 簡介

The Social Media Platform Selector for Business Growth is a free AI prompt that recommends the optimal social media channel for audience building and product sales based on your specific business and target market. This social media platform prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type and audience demographics to deliver a definitive platform recommendation backed by 2024 algorithm behavior, content format alignment, acquisition costs, and conversion potential. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured report that explains why one platform is your best fit, plus viable runner-up options with clear trade-offs. Use it when launching a new product, refocusing marketing spend, or deciding where to concentrate organic growth efforts. ● Delivers one definitive platform recommendation instead of vague multi-channel advice, saving time and marketing budget. ● Weighs 2024 platform trends, algorithm changes, and audience engagement patterns for current, actionable guidance. ● Includes runner-up alternatives with honest trade-off analysis so you understand backup options. ● Considers content format fit, acquisition cost, and conversion potential specific to your business model and audience. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional digital marketer specializing in social media platform selection and audience growth strategy.

## Task
Recommend the single best social media platform for building an audience to sell products, based on business fit, target audience behavior, and current market trends.

## Context
**Business & Audience:**
{{business-and-audience}}

**Platform Selection Criteria:**
- Choose one definitive platform—the strongest match for this specific case
- Prioritize where the target audience is most active and engaged
- Consider content format alignment, acquisition cost, and conversion potential
- Account for 2024 platform trends and algorithm behavior

## Output
Structure your recommendation as:

**Recommended Platform: [Platform Name]**

[2-3 paragraphs explaining why this platform is the optimal choice. Cover audience demographics, content format advantages, algorithmic favorability for this business type, and realistic growth/monetization potential.]

**Runner-Up Options:**

[List 2-3 alternative platforms with 1-2 sentences each on why they're viable secondary choices, including trade-offs compared to your primary recommendation.]
```
