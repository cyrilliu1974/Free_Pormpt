# Niche Networking Platform Research Generator

## 簡介

The Niche Networking Platform Research Generator is a free AI prompt that researches and identifies 30 networking platforms and websites tailored to a specific solopreneur niche. This networking platform research prompt for ChatGPT takes your business niche as input and produces a structured table listing 30 relevant platforms complete with URLs and key feature summaries. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering research that would typically require hours of manual searching, vetting, and organizing. Solopreneurs building their professional networks use it to discover forums, communities, Slack groups, industry associations, and collaboration hubs they might otherwise never find. Reach for this prompt when launching a solo business, expanding into a new market vertical, or seeking peer communities beyond the mainstream social networks. ● Produces a clean three-column table with platform names, live URLs, and concise feature summaries ● Focuses exclusively on platforms relevant to the specified niche rather than generic social networks ● Includes full source citations so you can verify and explore the research methodology ● Saves 3-5 hours of manual searching, bookmarking, and spreadsheet assembly ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a researcher and networking expert who identifies niche-specific platforms where solopreneurs can connect and collaborate.

## Task
Research and identify 30 platforms and websites where solopreneurs in {{niche}} can effectively network and collaborate.

## Output
Deliver your findings as a table with three columns:

| Platform Name | URL | Key Features |
|---------------|-----|-------------|
| Platform 1 | https://... | Feature summary |
| Platform 2 | https://... | Feature summary |

(Continue for all 30 platforms)

**Sources:**
1. Source citation
2. Source citation

(List all sources used in your research)

## Criteria
- Focus on platforms specifically relevant to the niche
- Include the platform name, URL, and a concise summary of key features for each entry
- Cite all sources at the end
```
