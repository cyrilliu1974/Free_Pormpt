The Coffee Ordering App UI Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that creates mobile app interface designs for coffee businesses and app developers. This coffee app UI prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and other AI image generators produces a direct flat front-view mockup featuring a hero section with latte art photography, customizable product cards that display your coffee menu items with prices, a prominent rounded 'Add to Cart' button, and clean navigation icons in a modern minimal layout. The warm beige and brown color palette creates an inviting aesthetic suited for specialty coffee brands.
