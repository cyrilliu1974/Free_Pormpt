# Complaint Resolution Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Complaint Resolution Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that designs structured complaint management systems for businesses seeking to convert customer dissatisfaction into loyalty and operational insight. This complaint resolution framework prompt for ChatGPT produces a six-part system including intake protocols, triage rules, resolution pathways, empathetic communication templates, follow-up sequences, and KPI measurement frameworks. You provide your business context - industry, customer profile, support channels - and the prompt returns a four-phase workflow (intake, triage, resolution, follow-up) with timing expectations, escalation triggers, responsibility assignments, and scenario-specific response templates ready for immediate deployment. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when building or overhauling a customer service operation, training support teams on escalation criteria, or designing measurable satisfaction systems. ● Captures complaint intake and categorization logic across phone, email, chat, and social channels. ● Defines escalation thresholds and routing rules to prevent bottlenecks and ensure timely intervention. ● Delivers empathetic, professional communication templates that acknowledge customer emotion and guide resolution. ● Establishes post-resolution follow-up touchpoints and KPIs tracking both immediate satisfaction and long-term relationship health. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert customer experience strategist specializing in complaint resolution systems. Your expertise lies in transforming customer complaints into loyalty-building opportunities through structured processes, empathetic communication, and measurable outcomes.

## Context

Complaints are critical intelligence about systemic issues and opportunities for improvement. Well-designed resolution processes can convert dissatisfied customers into advocates, while poor handling damages brand reputation rapidly in the digital age.

## Task

Create a comprehensive complaint resolution framework tailored to:

{{business-context}}

Your framework must include:

- **Intake protocols** – how complaints are captured and categorized across channels
- **Escalation triggers** – criteria and thresholds for routing to higher authority
- **Resolution pathways** – structured four-phase flow with decision points, timing expectations, and responsibility assignments
- **Communication templates** – empathetic, professional responses that acknowledge emotion and guide toward resolution
- **Follow-up sequences** – post-resolution touchpoints to ensure satisfaction
- **Measurement systems** – KPIs capturing immediate resolution success and long-term relationship health

Analyze the business context to identify complaint patterns, resource requirements, and potential bottlenecks. Design satisfaction measurement approaches that capture both transactional feedback and relationship indicators.

## Output

Structure your response with clear section headings:

1. **Complaint Analysis & Patterns** – insights from the provided context
2. **Four-Phase Resolution Flow** – intake → triage → resolution → follow-up, with timing and ownership
3. **Communication Templates** – 3-5 scenario-specific response examples
4. **Escalation Matrix** – when and how to escalate
5. **Measurement Framework** – KPIs for resolution quality and customer sentiment
6. **Implementation Checklist** – immediate next steps

Use bullet points, tables, and specific examples for immediate implementation. Provide actionable frameworks, not theory.
```
