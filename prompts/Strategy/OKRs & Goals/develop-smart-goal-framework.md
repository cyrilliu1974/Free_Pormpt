# SMART Goal Framework Builder for Business Objectives

## 簡介

The SMART Goal Framework Builder for Business Objectives is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable goal-setting frameworks aligned with your organizational priorities. This SMART goals prompt for ChatGPT transforms broad business objectives into specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound goals complete with scoring evaluations and refinement recommendations. Teams use it to convert strategic priorities into trackable milestones, sales leaders apply it to revenue targets, product managers deploy it for launch planning, and HR departments rely on it for performance management frameworks. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a structured output that includes a breakdown of all five SMART criteria, 3-5 example goals tailored to your objectives, an evaluation table scoring each goal against the criteria, and specific recommendations for improvement. This prompt is for business analysts, managers, team leads, and strategists who need to translate high-level objectives into concrete, executable goals with clear success metrics. ● Breaks down each SMART criterion with clear definitions and application guidance ● Produces 3-5 example goals customized to your specific business objectives ● Includes a scoring table that evaluates each goal on a 1-5 scale across all five criteria ● Provides targeted recommendations to strengthen weak areas and close alignment gaps ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business analyst specializing in goal-setting frameworks that align with organizational priorities.

## Task
Create a comprehensive SMART goal framework for the provided business objectives. Deliver examples of well-crafted SMART goals, evaluate each against the five SMART criteria using a scoring table, and provide actionable recommendations.

## Context
Business objectives:
{{business-objectives}}

## Output

**Business Objectives**
- [List each objective clearly]

**SMART Goal Framework**
1. **Specific**: Define exactly what will be accomplished, who is involved, and where it will happen
2. **Measurable**: Identify concrete metrics, KPIs, or milestones to track progress
3. **Achievable**: Ensure the goal is realistic given available resources, budget, and constraints
4. **Relevant**: Confirm direct alignment with the stated business objectives and strategic priorities
5. **Time-bound**: Set a clear deadline or timeframe for completion

**SMART Goal Examples**
1. [Craft 3-5 goals that directly support the business objectives]
2. [Each should exemplify all five SMART criteria]
3. [Vary the goals to cover different aspects of the objectives]

**SMART Goal Evaluation Table**
| Goal | Specific (1-5) | Measurable (1-5) | Achievable (1-5) | Relevant (1-5) | Time-bound (1-5) |
|------|----------------|------------------|------------------|----------------|------------------|
| [Goal 1] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] |
| [Goal 2] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] |
| [Goal 3] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] | [Score] |

**Recommendations**
- Highlight strengths in the goal statements
- Identify gaps or weaknesses in SMART criteria alignment
- Suggest specific refinements to improve goal quality
- Note any resource, timeline, or strategic concerns
```
