# B2B Whitepaper Outline Generator for Lead Generation

## 簡介

The B2B Whitepaper Outline Generator for Lead Generation is a free AI prompt that creates structured whitepaper frameworks to establish authority and drive qualified leads through the sales funnel. This whitepaper prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete hierarchical outline including executive summary, introduction, main body sections with content placement notes, solution framework, conclusion, and supporting elements. It works by identifying target audience pain points, structuring content using dependency grammar principles where each section builds logically on the previous, and incorporating placeholders for statistics, case studies, expert insights, and visual content. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Marketing teams creating thought leadership content for B2B audiences, demand generation specialists developing lead magnets, and content strategists positioning products through educational resources use this prompt to produce whitepapers that balance authority-building with strategic persuasion. ● Creates complete whitepaper outlines with executive summary, numbered sections, subsections, and bulleted content notes for statistics, case studies, and visual elements ● Identifies decision criteria and pain points specific to your target audience while weaving your unique value proposition throughout the structure ● Positions calls-to-action at natural decision points and includes sidebar content, pull quotes, and resource link suggestions ● Applies dependency grammar framework to ensure each section builds logically into a cohesive, persuasive argument ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert content strategist specializing in B2B whitepaper development and lead generation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive whitepaper outline designed to attract and convert {{target-audience}} by addressing their core challenges and positioning {{product-service}} as the solution.

## Context
The whitepaper will serve as a lead magnet that establishes authority in {{industry}}, differentiates from competitors, and drives qualified leads through the sales funnel. It must balance educational value with persuasive positioning.

## Approach
- Identify the primary pain points, challenges, and decision criteria relevant to {{target-audience}}
- Structure content using dependency grammar principles: each section builds logically on the previous, creating a cohesive argument
- Incorporate supporting evidence: industry statistics, case studies, expert perspectives, and data-driven insights
- Weave {{unique-value-proposition}} throughout without overt selling
- Position strategic calls-to-action at natural decision points

## Output
Deliver a structured whitepaper outline containing:

### Executive Summary
- Hook that speaks directly to audience pain points
- Key findings preview
- Value proposition statement

### Introduction
- Problem definition and scope
- Why it matters now
- What readers will gain

### Main Body (3-5 sections)
- Section headlines (benefit-driven, specific)
- Subsections with core arguments
- Bullet points indicating:
  - Statistics/data to include
  - Case study opportunities
  - Expert insight placements
  - Visual content suggestions (charts, diagrams, infographics)

### Solution Framework
- Approach overview
- Implementation considerations
- Success factors

### Conclusion
- Key takeaways
- Next steps for readers
- Primary call-to-action

### Supporting Elements
- Sidebar content ideas
- Pull quotes
- Resource links

Format as a clear hierarchical outline with numbered sections, lettered subsections, and bulleted content notes.
```
