# Acquisition Growth Strategy Generator for M&A Analysis

## 簡介

The Acquisition Growth Strategy Generator for M&A Analysis is a free AI prompt that evaluates potential acquisition targets and delivers data-driven M&A recommendations for growth-focused businesses. This acquisition strategy prompt for ChatGPT guides you through a rigorous M&A evaluation framework: it profiles three candidate companies, assesses strategic fit against your growth objectives, analyzes financial performance (revenue, EBITDA, growth rate), identifies integration risks, and ranks targets with attractiveness scores. You input your company profile and acquisition objectives, and the prompt returns a structured report complete with comparative analysis tables and a final recommendation tied to deal structure and integration success factors. Runs reliably on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for corporate development teams, private equity analysts, and strategic planning consultants. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from acquisition brainstorming to disciplined target evaluation - whether you are building a shortlist for board review, conducting buy-side due diligence, or advising clients on inorganic growth. ● Profiles three acquisition candidates with business model, market position, and financial metrics in a consistent format ● Scores strategic fit and attractiveness on a 1-10 scale with transparent justification criteria ● Delivers side-by-side comparative analysis tables covering fit strength, financials, risk profile, and integration complexity ● Provides a data-backed recommendation that specifies preferred targets, deal structure considerations, and critical success factors for post-merger integration ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an M&A strategist analyzing potential acquisition targets to drive growth, expand market presence, and create synergistic value.

## Task
Identify and evaluate three potential acquisition targets for the acquiring company. Assess each target's strategic fit, financial performance, risks, and integration challenges. Provide a comparative analysis and final recommendation.

## Context
Acquiring company profile and objectives:
{{company-and-objectives}}

Provide: company background, growth objectives, key capabilities to acquire, and target markets.

## Output
Structure your analysis as follows:

### 1. Strategic Overview
- Growth objectives summary
- Key capabilities to acquire
- Target markets

### 2. Acquisition Target Profiles (3 candidates)
For each target:
- **Company Overview**: business model, size, market position
- **Strategic Fit**: alignment with growth objectives and capability needs
- **Financials**: revenue, EBITDA margin, growth rate
- **Risks and Challenges**: regulatory, cultural, operational, competitive
- **Attractiveness Score**: 1-10 scale with justification

### 3. Comparative Analysis
Side-by-side comparison table highlighting:
- Strategic fit strength
- Financial performance
- Risk profile
- Integration complexity
- Overall ranking

### 4. Recommendation
Data-driven recommendation specifying the preferred target(s) with clear rationale tied to the comparative analysis. Address deal structure considerations and critical success factors for integration.
```
