# Skate Photography Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Skate Photography Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that creates high-impact skateboarding action images with fisheye distortion, dramatic lighting, and gritty street aesthetics. This skate photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 16:9 images that capture skaters mid-trick with low-angle perspectives, high-contrast treatment, and customizable contact effects like sparks or friction at the board-rail interface. You control the skater and trick, the visual effect at the contact point, and the lighting environment to match your creative vision. Photographers, skate brands, content creators, and designers reach for this prompt when they need authentic street-skate imagery without a camera crew or perfect weather. ● Fisheye lens distortion and low-angle framing that immerses viewers in the action and emphasizes trick difficulty. ● Customizable contact effects such as sparks, friction glow, or scrape marks where board meets rail or ledge. ● Dramatic lighting and shadow control to set time of day, mood, and contrast levels. ● High-contrast gritty treatment that replicates the raw, textured look of classic skate magazines and street videos. ## Prompt

```
Skate photography, {{skater-and-trick}} locking into the rail, {{contact-effect}} on the board contact point, {{lighting-and-environment}} with dramatic shadows, fisheye lens for immersive distortion, low angle shot, high-contrast gritty treatment --ar 16:9
```
