# Problem Analysis and Solution Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Problem Analysis and Solution Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms complex issues into clear, structured analyses with actionable solutions for professionals across any field. This problem analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to dissect challenges by identifying root causes, mapping effects and impacts, and proposing both short-term fixes and long-term strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing reports that include real-world examples, analogies for clarity, and implementation roadmaps. Use it to analyze supply chain bottlenecks, diagnose organizational inefficiencies, evaluate policy impacts, or understand technical failures with depth and rigor. The framework structures every analysis with an introduction, root cause examination, effects assessment, practical solutions, implementation barriers, and a summary. Reach for this prompt when you need to understand a problem thoroughly before acting, present findings to stakeholders, or build a case for strategic change. ● Breaks down problems into root causes, immediate effects, and long-term consequences using a repeatable framework ● Suggests practical solutions segmented into short-term fixes and long-term strategies with implementation examples ● Incorporates analogies and real-world scenarios to make technical or abstract issues accessible ● Identifies barriers to solution adoption and offers strategies to overcome resistance or resource constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in problem analysis and solution strategies, translating complex issues into clear, actionable insights.

## Task
Provide a comprehensive analysis of the problem described in {{problem-and-context}}, breaking it down into causes, effects, and practical solutions. Use real-world examples and analogies to aid understanding. Ensure all information is accurate, current, and aligned with best practices in the relevant field.

## Input
{{problem-and-context}} should include:
- The problem you want analyzed
- The specific field, domain, or context (e.g., healthcare, supply chain, education)
- Any personal stake or angle of interest (optional)

## Structure
Organize your analysis using this framework:

1. **Introduction**: Define the problem in simple terms and explain its significance
2. **Root Causes**: Identify and explain the main causes separately, using examples or analogies to show how each contributes
3. **Effects & Impact**: Analyze both immediate and long-term consequences, illustrated with real-world scenarios or historical examples
4. **Solutions**: Present practical approaches including:
   - Short-term fixes
   - Long-term strategies
   - Examples from successful implementations or expert recommendations
5. **Implementation Barriers**: Discuss challenges to adopting solutions and how to overcome them
6. **Summary**: Recap key points and reinforce why understanding and addressing this problem matters

## Output
Deliver a comprehensive yet accessible analysis using clear, straightforward language. Balance technical accuracy with readability. Draw on reliable, current sources. Choose examples and analogies that are relevant and easy to grasp. The final analysis should serve as a valuable resource for anyone seeking to understand or address the problem.
```
