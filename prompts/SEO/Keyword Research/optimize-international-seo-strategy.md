# International SEO Market Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The International SEO Market Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies high-potential non-English markets and delivers actionable keyword research for brands planning global expansion. This international SEO prompt for ChatGPT takes a single brand name and returns a structured analysis covering the top five target countries, localized keyword tables with search volumes and competition metrics, strategic market insights, and specific optimization recommendations. It works by combining market prioritization logic with multilingual keyword research patterns, producing ready-to-use data tables and a visual map framework that highlights geographic opportunities. Marketing teams use it to assess whether entering German, Japanese, Spanish, or other non-English markets makes sense before committing budget, while SEO specialists use the output to brief content and localization teams. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Reach for this prompt when you need a data-driven snapshot of where your brand should focus international SEO efforts and which keywords to target in each locale. ● Identifies the five non-English speaking countries with the strongest SEO potential for a given brand, ranked by opportunity. ● Delivers a keyword research table with two high-value local-language keywords per country, complete with search volume and competition level. ● Provides three strategic insights covering market dynamics, competitive landscape, and cultural search behavior. ● Includes three concrete optimization recommendations spanning market entry tactics, technical setup, and content localization. ## Prompt

```

You are an international SEO expert with deep knowledge of global search trends, keyword research, and multilingual optimization strategies.

## Task
Analyze the SEO potential for {{brand}} in non-English speaking markets. Research relevant keywords, search volumes, and competition levels in target countries, then provide actionable insights for optimizing international SEO strategy.

## Context
Focus on identifying the strongest opportunities in non-English speaking countries. Base recommendations on concrete keyword data and market-specific search behavior patterns.

## Output
Provide your analysis in the following structure:

**Target Countries**
List the top 5 non-English speaking countries with highest SEO potential for this brand, with their primary languages:
1. [Country]: [Language]
2. [Country]: [Language]
3. [Country]: [Language]
4. [Country]: [Language]
5. [Country]: [Language]

**Keyword Research**
| Country | Keyword | Search Volume | Competition Level |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| [Country 1] | [Keyword 1] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 1] | [Keyword 2] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 2] | [Keyword 1] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 2] | [Keyword 2] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 3] | [Keyword 1] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 3] | [Keyword 2] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 4] | [Keyword 1] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 4] | [Keyword 2] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |
| [Country 5] | [Keyword 1] | [Volume] | [Low/Medium/High] |

Include 2 high-value keywords per country in the local language.

**World Map Visualization**

**Key Insights**
```
