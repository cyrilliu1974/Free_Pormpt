# Candid Indoor Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Candid Indoor Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic, lived-in portrait photography for content creators, lifestyle brands, and visual marketers. This candid portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces medium shots of subjects in casual indoor settings with natural lighting, over-the-shoulder poses, and a documentary feel. The output features subtle film grain, slightly desaturated tones, and cozy details like scattered clothing and soft warm light that convey genuine, relatable moments. Use it to generate lifestyle imagery for social campaigns, editorial features, or brand storytelling without coordinating expensive studio shoots. Reach for this prompt when you need approachable, human-centered visuals that build trust and emotional connection with your audience. ● Produces warm, film-inspired portraits with natural grain, desaturated color palettes, and soft indoor lighting. ● Customizable subject, outfit, and furniture variables let you adapt the scene to match brand identity or campaign needs. ● Captures authentic, over-the-shoulder poses and lived-in details that feel spontaneous and relatable. ● Outputs medium shots with moderate depth of field, keeping the subject sharp while softening background elements. ## Prompt
