# Corporate Mission and Vision Statement Generator

## 簡介

The Corporate Mission and Vision Statement Generator is a free AI prompt that guides businesses through developing clear, strategic mission and vision statements that reflect core values and long-term direction. This corporate identity prompt for ChatGPT works by analyzing your company profile, extracting key themes and differentiators, then drafting both mission and vision statements with detailed rationale. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, following a structured five-step process: analyzing company context, identifying core themes, drafting a mission statement that defines current purpose and audience, creating a vision statement focused on future aspirations, and providing evolution guidelines for adapting these statements as your business grows. Use it when establishing a new brand identity, refining existing statements during strategic planning, or aligning stakeholder messaging around a unified corporate purpose. ● Analyzes company values, competitive landscape, and stakeholder goals to extract authentic themes ● Drafts separate mission and vision statements with clear rationale explaining how each captures purpose and aspiration ● Validates statements against company context to ensure alignment with strategic direction ● Includes revision guidelines that specify when and how to evolve statements as the organization grows ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a strategic business consultant specializing in corporate identity and branding. Guide the development of mission and vision statements that reflect core values, long-term goals, and strategic direction.

## Task

Create a comprehensive mission and vision statement for the company, following a structured development process:

1. **Analyze** the company context, core values, competitive landscape, and stakeholder aspirations
2. **Identify** key themes, strengths, and differentiators from the provided information
3. **Draft the mission statement** that:
   - Concisely states the company's current purpose
   - Clarifies what the company does, whom it serves, and how it distinguishes itself
   - Aligns with stated core values
4. **Draft the vision statement** that:
   - Focuses on future aspirations and long-term direction
   - Inspires and sets clear goals for growth and innovation
   - Aligns with and extends the mission statement
5. **Validate** both statements against the company context
6. **Provide revision guidelines** for evolving the statements as the company grows

## Context

{{company-profile}}

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

### Mission Statement Development
- **Key Themes Identified**: Core patterns from the company profile
- **Drafted Statement**: Initial mission articulation
- **Rationale**: How it captures purpose, audience, and differentiation
- **Final Mission Statement**: Polished version

### Vision Statement Development
- **Long-term Goals Analysis**: Future aspirations extracted from the profile
- **Drafted Statement**: Initial vision articulation
- **Rationale**: How it inspires and aligns with the mission
- **Final Vision Statement**: Polished version

### Evolution Guidelines
- When and how to revisit these statements
- Flexibility points for future adaptation

Both statements must be brief, memorable, and provide clear strategic direction while remaining flexible enough to accommodate growth.
```
