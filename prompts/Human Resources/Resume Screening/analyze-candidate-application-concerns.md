# Candidate Application Red Flag Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Candidate Application Red Flag Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that identifies critical hiring risks in job applications for recruiters, HR professionals, and talent acquisition teams. This candidate screening prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes resumes, cover letters, and application materials to surface five concrete red flags that may indicate performance issues, culture misfit, or documentation problems. It examines employment gaps, job-hopping patterns, inconsistencies across documents, vague accomplishments, communication quality, overqualification signals, generic materials, and unexplained career pivots. For each red flag, the prompt delivers specific indicators to look for, explains the hiring risk, and provides interview questions or verification steps to probe deeper. Recruiters use it to move beyond surface credentials and catch warning signs before extending offers. Reach for this prompt when screening applications for competitive roles, conducting high-volume hiring, or seeking a structured approach to candidate vetting that reduces costly mis-hires. ● Detects employment history patterns such as frequent job changes, unexplained gaps, or career regression that may signal instability. ● Flags inconsistencies between resume claims, cover letter details, and LinkedIn profiles to catch embellishment or misrepresentation. ● Evaluates communication quality across application materials, identifying grammar issues, vague language, or generic content that reflects poor attention to detail. ● Provides targeted interview questions and reference check strategies for each identified red flag, turning concerns into actionable next steps. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert talent acquisition specialist with deep expertise in resume analysis, behavioral assessment, and candidate evaluation.

## Task

Analyze the provided application materials to identify **5 critical red flags** that suggest potential hiring risks. Look beyond surface credentials to detect patterns in employment history, communication quality, document consistency, professional presentation, and alignment between claimed and demonstrated experience.

For each red flag:
- Describe what it looks like in practice with concrete examples
- Explain why it's concerning from a hiring perspective
- Provide interview questions or verification steps to probe deeper

## Context
```
