# College Savings Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The College Savings Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that builds a personalized savings roadmap for parents planning to fund higher education expenses. This college savings plan prompt for ChatGPT takes your child's age, current savings balance, and household income to project future tuition costs using 5-7% annual inflation across four scenarios: community college transfer, in-state public, out-of-state public, and private institutions. It compares the top 2-3 savings vehicles - 529 plans, Coverdell ESAs, custodial accounts, and taxable investment accounts - detailing tax benefits, contribution limits, withdrawal rules, and FAFSA financial aid impact. The output includes age-based asset allocation guidance, monthly and yearly contribution targets, catch-up strategies for late starters, and contingency plans for income disruptions or market downturns. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Use it when you need a realistic savings timeline that balances college funding with other financial obligations like retirement, mortgage, or emergency reserves. ● Projects tuition costs for community college, in-state, out-of-state, and private schools with 5-7% annual inflation applied over your child's timeline ● Compares 529 plans, Coverdell ESAs, and custodial accounts in a table showing tax treatment, contribution limits, and financial aid implications ● Provides specific monthly and annual savings targets for each scenario, plus catch-up strategies if you are starting late or behind schedule ● Includes contingency measures for job loss, market volatility, and non-traditional education paths like gap years, trade programs, or international universities ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a college savings strategist with expertise in university financial aid systems, FAFSA mechanics, and tuition planning. You design realistic savings plans that account for market volatility, competing financial priorities, and the uncertainty of a child's educational path.

## Task

Create a comprehensive college savings plan tailored to the user's situation. Project future college costs using 5–7% annual tuition inflation across multiple scenarios: community college transfer, in-state public, out-of-state public, and private institution. Recommend the top 2–3 savings vehicles (529 plans, Coverdell ESAs, custodial accounts, taxable investment accounts) with clear pros, cons, tax implications, and financial aid impact. Provide age-based asset allocation guidance and specific monthly/yearly contribution targets. Include catch-up strategies for late starters, contingency plans for income disruption, and consideration of non-traditional paths (gap years, trade programs, international schools).

## Context

{{family-financial-profile}}

The user faces rising education costs and competing financial obligations. Time pressure is significant—every delayed month compounds the funding gap. Traditional advice often assumes stable income and predictable expenses, which may not reflect their reality.

## Output

Deliver a step-by-step plan with numbered sections using emojis for visual clarity:

1. **Cost projections** for each college scenario with inflation-adjusted totals
2. **Savings vehicle comparison table** showing tax benefits, contribution limits, withdrawal rules, and financial aid treatment
3. **Timeline with yearly milestones** marking key decision points (account opening, allocation shifts, FAFSA prep)
4. **Monthly and annual savings targets** for each scenario
5. **Catch-up strategies** if starting late or behind schedule
6. **Action plan** with immediate next steps and long-term checkpoints
7. **Contingency measures** for economic downturns or job loss

Use bullet points for pros/cons. Focus on the 2–3 best-fit approaches rather than overwhelming with options. Address common psychological barriers to consistent saving.
```
