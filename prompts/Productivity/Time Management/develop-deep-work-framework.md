# Deep Work Framework Builder for Any Industry

## 簡介

The Deep Work Framework Builder for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that produces a complete, evidence-based plan for implementing deep work practices tailored to your field. This deep work prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your industry's unique productivity challenges and workplace distractions, then builds a five-step framework for integrating focused work blocks into a typical workweek. Each step includes implementation guidance, industry-specific obstacles, and practical solutions. The output also defines deep work clearly, identifies relevant success metrics, and cites credible research sources. Use it when you need to design a concentration strategy for knowledge workers in finance, tech, healthcare, education, creative services, or any field where sustained focus drives results. ● Delivers a five-step implementation roadmap with obstacle identification and mitigation strategies for your industry ● Defines deep work principles and connects them to measurable productivity and innovation outcomes ● Recommends tracking methods and quantifiable metrics aligned with industry performance expectations ● Cites at least five credible sources to ground recommendations in research and expert practice ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert researcher and productivity consultant specializing in deep work methodologies and their practical application across industries.

## Task

Conduct a comprehensive analysis of deep work and its impact on productivity and innovation in the specified industry. Develop a detailed, evidence-based framework for implementing deep work practices within a typical workweek.

## Context

Industry: {{industry}}

Address the specific productivity challenges, workplace distractions, and structural constraints common to this industry. Tailor all recommendations to the actual working conditions, communication norms, and performance expectations found in this field.

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

### 1. Deep Work Definition
Provide a clear, actionable definition of deep work.

### 2. Industry Overview
- Industry name and key characteristics
- Current productivity challenges specific to this industry
- Potential benefits of deep work implementation

### 3. Deep Work Framework
Develop 5 sequential steps for implementing deep work within a typical workweek. For each step, include:
- **Title**: Clear step name
- **Description**: Detailed implementation guidance
- **Obstacles**: Common barriers in this industry
- **Solutions**: Practical strategies to overcome those barriers

### 4. Measuring Success
- **Metrics**: Quantifiable indicators relevant to the industry
- **Tracking Methods**: Practical approaches for monitoring progress

### 5. Sources
Cite at least 5 credible sources (research papers, industry studies, expert publications) that support your findings and recommendations.
```
