# SEO Competitor Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The SEO Competitor Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that produces side-by-side SEO audits of up to five competitors, helping marketers and SEO professionals identify ranking opportunities and refine their search strategies. This SEO competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to compare domain authority scores, top-performing keywords, backlink profiles, and content publishing patterns in a clean markdown table, then distill the findings into actionable recommendations. You provide your business context, target audience, competitor names, and SEO goals; the prompt structures the analysis into competitive gaps, quick wins, and long-term initiatives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it flexible for teams working across different text-generation platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need to benchmark your site against competitors, prepare a quarterly SEO strategy review, or onboard a new client by mapping the competitive landscape quickly. ● Compares domain authority, keyword rankings, backlink sources, and content frequency across five named competitors in a markdown table. ● Surfaces competitive gaps, quick wins, and long-term SEO initiatives based on the analysis. ● Tailors recommendations to your specific business context, audience demographics, and ranking goals. ● Outputs both quantitative comparison data and strategic next steps in one structured response. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO analyst conducting a comprehensive competitor analysis.

## Task
Analyze the SEO strategies of the specified competitors to identify improvement opportunities and industry best practices. Focus your analysis on:
- Domain authority metrics and comparative standing
- Top-performing keywords and search visibility
- Backlink profile quality, quantity, and sources
- Content strategy patterns, formats, and frequency

Deliver actionable insights tailored to the business context provided.

## Context
**Business & Market:**
{{business-context}}
(Include: your business name, industry, target audience demographics/needs, and top 5 competitor names)

**SEO Goals:**
{{seo-goals}}
(Specify: ranking objectives, traffic targets, conversion priorities, or technical improvements you're pursuing)

## Output
Present your analysis in two parts:

1. **Competitor Comparison Table** (markdown format with 5 columns):
   - Competitor Name
   - Domain Authority
   - Top Keywords
   - Backlink Profile
   - Content Strategy

2. **Strategic Recommendations** (bullet-point list):
   - Key competitive gaps and opportunities
   - Prioritized actions based on findings
   - Best practices to adopt from market leaders
   - Quick wins vs. long-term initiatives
```
