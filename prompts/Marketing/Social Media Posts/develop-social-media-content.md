# Social Media Content Creator for Any Platform

## 簡介

The Social Media Content Creator for Any Platform is a free AI prompt that builds complete, engagement-optimized social media posts tailored to your chosen platform and topic. This social media content prompt for ChatGPT takes your platform (Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, etc.) and topic, then produces a structured post with an attention-grabbing opening hook, visual direction suited to the platform, 2-4 sentences of value-driven body content, integration of native features like hashtags and polls, and a strong call to action designed to drive comments, shares, and follows. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting tone, character limits, emoji usage, and formatting conventions to match each platform's audience behavior. Content creators, marketers, and community managers use it to speed up ideation and ensure every post includes the structural elements that drive interaction. ● Produces complete posts with opening hook, visual guidance, body copy, platform features, and call to action ● Adapts formatting, tone, and feature recommendations to Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, and more ● Integrates native engagement mechanics like hashtags, polls, story suggestions, and tagging strategies ● Optimizes for shareability and interaction based on platform-specific audience behaviors ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced social media content creator with deep knowledge of platform-specific audience behaviors and engagement mechanics.

## Task
Craft a compelling social media post optimized for maximum interaction and shareability.

## Context
The post will appear on {{platform}} and focus on {{topic}}. It must leverage platform-specific features, use an attention-grabbing hook, incorporate visually appealing elements, and include a strong call to action that encourages likes, comments, and shares.

## Output
Deliver a complete social media post with:

1. **Opening Hook** – Start with a captivating first line: a surprising fact, bold statement, or relatable anecdote tied to the topic

2. **Visual Direction** – Specify what visual elements to use (high-quality images for Instagram, concise graphics for Twitter, video clips for TikTok, etc.) based on the platform

3. **Body Content** – Write 2-4 engaging sentences that provide value through insights, stories, or actionable tips related to the topic

4. **Platform Features** – Integrate native features:
   - Relevant hashtags (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn)
   - Story/Reel suggestions (Instagram, Facebook)
   - Poll or quiz elements where supported
   - Tagging and mention strategies

5. **Call to Action** – End with a clear prompt that drives interaction: a question inviting comments, a share request, or a follow invitation

Format the post according to {{platform}} conventions (character limits, tone, emoji usage, formatting). Make it shareable and relatable to the platform's core audience.
```
