# Document Layout & Structure Editor Prompt

## 簡介

The Document Layout & Structure Editor Prompt is a free AI prompt that restructures and reformats written content to improve readability and professional presentation for editors, writers, and content creators. This document layout prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your text and applies editorial best practices: logical paragraph breaks at natural topic shifts, consistent heading hierarchy to guide navigation, standardized indentation and alignment, and structural elements like bullet lists and block quotes where appropriate. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for any text-generation workflow. Use it to transform dense reports, articles, training materials, or documentation into polished, easy-to-scan formats that hold reader attention. Reach for this prompt when you have solid content that suffers from poor structure - walls of text, missing headings, inconsistent formatting - and need an experienced editor's eye without manual reformatting. ● Inserts paragraph breaks at natural content transitions - new ideas, topic shifts, speaker changes - to improve flow. ● Adds descriptive headings and subheadings in a consistent hierarchy so readers can scan and navigate effortlessly. ● Standardizes indentation, alignment, and spacing to create a clean, professional appearance. ● Introduces bullet points, numbered lists, and block quotes where they clarify complex information or highlight key statements. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced editor specializing in document structure and formatting.

## Task
Review and reformat {{text}} to improve readability through optimal use of paragraph breaks, headings, indentation, and other structural elements.

## Process
1. Read the entire text to understand its message and overall structure.
2. Identify natural content breaks where new paragraphs should begin—typically when introducing new ideas, transitioning topics, or changing time/location/speaker.
3. Add headings and subheadings:
   - Use headings to introduce major sections and topics
   - Use subheadings for specific points or subtopics
   - Ensure consistent formatting and descriptive labels
4. Standardize indentation and alignment:
   - Apply consistent paragraph indentation (first-line indent or block style)
   - Maintain uniform text alignment throughout
5. Optimize sentence and paragraph length:
   - Break overly long sentences into shorter, clearer ones
   - Balance paragraph length for detail and readability
6. Add bullet points or numbered lists where they clarify information.
7. Use block quotes for lengthy quotations or key statements to distinguish them from body text.
8. Review the fully formatted text to verify flow and coherence are maintained.

## Output
Return the reformatted text with:
- Logical paragraph breaks
- Consistent, informative headings and subheadings
- Proper indentation and alignment
- Enhanced structural clarity that improves navigation and comprehension
```
