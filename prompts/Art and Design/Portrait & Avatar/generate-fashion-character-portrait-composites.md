# Fashion Portrait With 3D Character Composite Prompt

## 簡介

The Fashion Portrait With 3D Character Composite Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates professional studio photography composites featuring a real person posing with an oversized 3D cartoon figure while preserving the uploaded face reference. This fashion portrait prompt for ChatGPT-based image tools, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces hyper-realistic studio photoshoots where the subject stands confidently beside a giant glossy 3D character on a vibrant solid-color backdrop. You customize the outfit, character type, and color theme through three simple variables, and the model renders sharp fabric detail, realistic skin texture, and accurate lighting reflections across both the human subject and the cartoonish companion. Use cases include personal branding visuals, Instagram content, influencer portfolios, and playful social media hero images that blend photography with illustration. Reach for this prompt when you need eye-catching fashion content that pairs realistic portraiture with whimsical 3D elements and you want studio-quality results without physical production costs. ● Preserves the exact face from your uploaded reference photo without altering identity or features. ● Produces soft, evenly diffused studio lighting with clean highlights and smooth color gradients. ● Renders the 3D character as a giant, glossy, photorealistic figure with cartoon-accurate form and ultra-detailed surfaces. ● Customizable through three variables: outfit style, character type, and background color theme. ## Prompt

```
Hyper-realistic professional fashion photoshoot. A stylish young woman wearing {{outfit}} stands confidently with one arm resting casually on a giant 3D-rendered {{character}}. Her expression is natural, confident, and casual.

Hyperrealistic skin texture, sharp fabric details, accurate lighting reflections on the 3D character, smooth studio gradients. Modern fashion photography aesthetic—playful, bright, stylish.

Preserve the exact face from the uploaded reference photo without altering any facial features or identity. Reference match required.
```
