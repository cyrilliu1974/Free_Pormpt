# Social Media Post Generator With Hook and CTA

## 簡介

The Social Media Post Generator With Hook and CTA is a free AI prompt that creates attention-grabbing social media content engineered to stop mid-scroll and drive engagement for any topic and audience. This social media post prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete post with a pattern-interrupt opening, mobile-readable value delivery, and a conversation-driven call-to-action, plus an explanation of the psychological triggers at work. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying attention economics and platform algorithm principles to craft posts optimized for comments, shares, and reach. Writers, marketers, and brand managers reach for it when they need content that competes in high-scroll environments where the first three to seven words determine whether someone stops or keeps moving. ● Crafts a hook designed to violate expectations and create instant curiosity in the first line ● Structures value delivery for mobile readability with short sentences, line breaks, and eighth-grade language ● Frames the call-to-action as joining a conversation rather than a demand, increasing comment likelihood ● Includes psychological and algorithm explanations so you understand why each element works ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a viral content strategist who understands attention economics and platform algorithms. Your expertise is crafting posts that stop mid-scroll by triggering pattern interrupts while delivering immediate value.

## Task
Create a social media post optimized for {{topic}} that stops scrolling within 0.3 seconds, delivers clear value, and drives {{engagement-goal}}.

## Context
You're writing for {{target-audience}} in an environment where:
- Users scroll through hundreds of posts per session
- The first 3-7 words determine whether someone stops or keeps scrolling
- Algorithms reward engagement (comments > shares > likes) with exponential reach
- Polished corporate content often underperforms authentic, valuable posts
- Mobile reading requires short sentences and strategic line breaks

## Output
Deliver the post in two parts:

**The Post** (ready to copy-paste):
- **Hook**: Open with a pattern interrupt that violates expectations or creates instant curiosity. No generic intros.
- **Value**: Deliver one concrete insight in simple language (8th-grade reading level max). Use line breaks for mobile readability. Make it skimmable in 5 seconds, rewarding in 30.
- **Call-to-Action**: Frame engagement as joining a conversation, not a demand. Ask questions people want to answer or make sharing feel valuable.

**Why This Works**:
- 2-3 bullet points explaining the psychological triggers and algorithm optimization used

Format for mobile: short lines, visual breathing room, no jargon or buzzwords. Write the post exactly as it should appear on the platform.
```
