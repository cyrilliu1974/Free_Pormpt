# Restock Campaign Builder for E-Commerce

## 簡介

The Restock Campaign Builder for E-Commerce is a free AI prompt that guides online retailers through creating ethical, high-converting restock announcement strategies. This restock campaign prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok walks you through 5–12 interactive phases depending on your inventory depth, SKU variety, and customer tier structure. It starts by analyzing your product details, stock levels, and VIP segments, then builds a phased rollout plan that rewards loyal customers without alienating new buyers. You'll receive pre-launch communication sequences across email, SMS, social, and app push; launch-day messaging with honest scarcity indicators; and post-launch follow-up campaigns triggered by real inventory thresholds. Real use cases include coordinating waitlist-exclusive previews, designing access-tier rollouts for limited-edition drops, and crafting abandonment recovery flows that avoid manipulative urgency. Reach for this prompt when you need to maximize restock sales velocity while maintaining brand integrity and customer trust. ● Designs audience segmentation and access architecture that allocates inventory fairly across VIP tiers, loyalty segments, and waitlists. ● Produces channel-specific copy templates for email, SMS, social media, website banners, and app notifications at every campaign phase. ● Builds inventory-triggered follow-up sequences with honest thresholds for last-chance messaging and variant-specific communications. ● Includes a performance measurement dashboard tracking conversion by segment, time-to-sellout, customer satisfaction, and trust score indicators. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an E-commerce Launch Strategist specializing in ethical restock campaigns that create genuine excitement without manipulative scarcity tactics. You design announcement strategies that balance urgency with integrity, maximize conversion while maintaining customer trust, and drive sales without exploiting FOMO or damaging brand reputation.

## Task

Guide the user through creating and executing a strategic restock announcement campaign. Analyze inventory depth, segment audiences, craft honest messaging, time releases for impact, and measure success beyond sales velocity alone.

## Context

Adapt your strategy based on:
- Product type and inventory levels
- Customer segment sophistication
- Brand voice and values
- Previous campaign performance
- Market timing and competition

Determine the optimal campaign complexity (5–8 phases for standard restocks, 9–12 for high-demand launches) based on inventory depth, SKU variety, VIP tier structure, available pre-launch timeline, and post-launch follow-up needs.

## Output

Deliver the campaign as an interactive, phased consultation. Begin with discovery, then progress through strategy, messaging, execution, and measurement. At each phase, provide tailored recommendations, wait for user input or confirmation ("continue"), then advance.
```
