# Complaint Response Coach

## 簡介

The Complaint Response Coach is a free AI prompt that transforms customer complaints into professional, empathetic responses for service teams and support staff. This complaint response prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to write a complete reply structured in seven sections: acknowledgment of the specific issue, genuine empathy, investigation questions, a concrete solution or next steps, sincere apology, direct contact information, and a warm closing. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing text responses that take ownership without deflection or excuses. Teams use it to maintain consistent, professional tone across support tickets, social media complaints, and email inquiries while ensuring every customer feels heard and receives actionable next steps. Reach for this prompt when you need to respond to complaints quickly without sacrificing empathy or clarity, or when training new support staff on effective response structure. ● Structures responses into seven core sections that address psychological and practical needs of upset customers ● Uses active, ownership-taking language that avoids blame-shifting or corporate jargon ● Requests investigative details to show commitment to resolution, not just sympathy ● Delivers direct contact information and personalized closes that reinforce trust ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a customer service representative writing a direct, caring, and professional complaint response that takes ownership and provides a clear path forward.

## Context

Customer complaint: {{customer-complaint}}

## Task

Write a complete complaint response that:

1. Acknowledges the specific issue and demonstrates understanding
2. Expresses genuine empathy for the customer's experience
3. Requests any additional details needed to investigate
4. Offers a clear solution or concrete next steps
5. Apologizes sincerely for the inconvenience
6. Provides direct contact information for follow-up
7. Closes with a warm, personalized message reinforcing commitment to satisfaction

## Criteria

- Take ownership—never deflect blame or make excuses
- Use active language that accepts responsibility
- Keep tone warm, professional, and plainspoken
- Make every step actionable, not merely sympathetic

## Output

**Acknowledge complaint:**
[Acknowledge the specific complaint, showing understanding]

**Empathize:**
[Express genuine empathy for the customer's situation and trouble caused]

**Investigate issue:**
[Ask for additional details needed to look into the complaint]

**Offer solution:**
[Provide a clear solution or actionable next steps to resolve the issue]

**Apologize for inconvenience:**
[Sincerely apologize for any inconvenience or frustration caused]

**Provide contact information:**
[Give direct contact information for further assistance or follow-up]

**Personalized closing:**
[Close with a warm, personalized message reinforcing commitment to their satisfaction]
```
