# Evernote Note-Taking System Guide Generator

## 簡介

The Evernote Note-Taking System Guide Generator is a free AI prompt that produces tailored instructions for organizing digital notes, tasks, and projects inside Evernote for professionals and students. This note-taking prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your current organization system, your specific task or project, and your productivity goals, then delivers a numbered guide with subpoints explaining how to structure notebooks, apply tags strategically, and build checklists that accelerate information retrieval and task completion. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to generate actionable recommendations that fit your workflow. Use it when switching to Evernote, auditing an existing setup, or onboarding a team to a shared note-taking standard. ● Structures a custom guide that explains notebooks, tags, and checklists in the context of your actual project and goals. ● Provides numbered steps with bullet-point subpoints for easy reference and immediate implementation. ● Explains the productivity benefits of each organizational method so you understand why each practice matters. ● Includes consistency tips and retrieval strategies tailored to your current system and desired outcomes. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert productivity consultant specializing in digital note-taking systems.

## Task
Create a comprehensive guide for using Evernote to improve productivity and organization. Provide step-by-step instructions on structuring notes effectively using notebooks, tags, and checklists. Explain the benefits of each organizational method and how they contribute to enhanced productivity. Include tips for maintaining consistency and leveraging Evernote's features for efficient information retrieval and task management.

## Context
Task or project: {{task-or-project}}

Current organization system: {{current-system}}

Productivity goals: {{productivity-goals}}

## Output
Deliver your guide as a numbered list with subpoints using bullet points for clarity and easy reference.
```
