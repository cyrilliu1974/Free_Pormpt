# Medium-Form Sales Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Medium-Form Sales Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates emotionally persuasive sales narratives for marketers, founders, and copywriters who need to connect brand stories with conversion goals. This sales copy prompt for ChatGPT produces 400-800 word narratives structured around dependency grammar - each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating natural momentum from pain point through solution to call-to-action. You provide your product details, target audience, unique selling proposition, desired emotional response, and CTA; the prompt returns continuous prose ready for landing pages, sales letters, or email campaigns. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering narratives that balance logical progression with sensory language, social proof, and persuasive contrast. Reach for this prompt when you need sales copy that tells a story rather than lists features - when your conversion depends on emotional resonance and narrative flow, not just bullet points. ● Opens by acknowledging audience pain points or aspirations, then introduces your product as the natural solution ● Weaves brand origin, mission, and unique selling proposition into a cohesive narrative arc ● Uses sensory language, relatable scenarios, and social proof to trigger specific emotional responses ● Structures each sentence to build on the previous one, creating logical dependencies that pull readers toward the call-to-action ● Outputs continuous persuasive prose without headline formatting - ready to paste into landing pages or sales letters ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in medium-form sales narratives that combine brand storytelling with emotional persuasion.

## Task
Create compelling medium-form sales copy (400-800 words) that tells a brand story, connects emotionally with the target audience, and drives them toward a specific action. Structure the narrative so each sentence and paragraph builds logically on what precedes it, creating momentum toward the conversion goal.

## Context
Product/Service: {{product-service}}

Target Audience: {{target-audience}}

Unique Selling Proposition: {{usp}}

Desired Emotional Response: {{emotional-response}}

Call-to-Action: {{cta}}

## Approach
1. Open by acknowledging a core pain point or aspiration your audience feels
2. Introduce the product/service as the solution, weaving in the brand's origin or mission
3. Highlight specific benefits that address audience desires, grounded in the USP
4. Use sensory language, relatable scenarios, or social proof to trigger the specified emotions
5. Build each sentence from the previous one—let subjects, objects, and modifiers create natural dependencies that pull the reader forward
6. Layer in persuasive elements: contrast (before/after), specificity (concrete details over abstractions), and urgency where appropriate
7. Close with a clear, compelling call-to-action that feels like the natural next step

## Output
Deliver a cohesive, flowing sales narrative with:
- Natural paragraph transitions that maintain narrative momentum
- Emotionally resonant language calibrated to the desired response
- Clear logical progression from problem through solution to action
- The call-to-action integrated organically into the closing

Do not use headline/subheadline formatting in the copy itself—present it as continuous persuasive prose ready for a landing page or sales letter.
```
