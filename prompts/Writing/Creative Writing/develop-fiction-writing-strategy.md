# Fiction Writing Strategy Prompt for Next Chapters

## 簡介

The Fiction Writing Strategy Prompt for Next Chapters is a free AI prompt that analyzes your manuscript context and produces a detailed development roadmap for novelists working through their story's middle or later stages. This fiction writing prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini takes your current plot summary, character arcs, themes, tone, and setting details, then generates a chapter-by-chapter outline covering character growth events, major plot twists, thematic exploration strategies, and world-building enhancements. Each chapter recommendation includes pivotal conflicts that test your characters, reveals that catalyze new action, symbolic elements tied to your themes, and cliffhangers to sustain reader engagement. The outline preserves your established narrative voice and pacing while proposing concrete next steps. Reach for this prompt when you know where your novel has been but need structured guidance on where it should go next - especially useful for drafters navigating the challenging middle act or setting up a powerful climax. ● Proposes pivotal events for each main character that reveal personality facets and drive decisions with story-wide impact ● Identifies major twists or revelations that force characters to confront new challenges and reconsider relationships ● Suggests symbolic elements, parallel plotlines, and moral dilemmas that deepen exploration of your novel's central themes ● Ensures stylistic consistency by aligning all recommendations with your established narrative voice, pacing, and descriptive approach ● Includes tension-building cliffhangers and unresolved questions to keep readers engaged chapter to chapter ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert fiction writing consultant guiding an author through their novel's next development stages.

## Task
Analyze the provided manuscript context and propose a structured outline for upcoming chapters. Your recommendations should advance the plot, deepen character development, and enhance narrative richness while maintaining established continuity.

## Context
{{manuscript-context}}

Include: current plot summary, main character arcs to date, central themes, writing style and tone, and key setting details.

## Output
Deliver a detailed chapter-by-chapter outline covering:

**Character Arcs**: For each main character, propose a pivotal event or conflict that tests them and contributes to growth or reveals deeper personality facets. Show how their decisions impact story direction and other characters.

**Plot Development**: Identify a major twist or revelation for the next few chapters that catalyzes action, forcing characters to confront new challenges or reconsider goals and relationships.

**Thematic Depth**: Suggest ways to explore central themes through proposed events—introduce symbolic elements, parallel plotlines, or moral dilemmas that resonate with established thematic concerns.

**Stylistic Consistency**: Ensure all suggestions align with the novel's established narrative voice, pacing, and descriptive approach.

**World-Building**: Deepen the reader's understanding of setting through history, culture, or underlying conflicts (where applicable to the genre).

**Tension and Hooks**: Propose at least one cliffhanger or unresolved question per chapter to sustain reader engagement.

Format the outline as a clear trajectory with specific events, character interactions, and suggested twists, while remaining flexible for adaptation during the writing process.
```
