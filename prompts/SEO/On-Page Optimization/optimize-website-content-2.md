# Website Content Optimization Plan Generator

## 簡介

The Website Content Optimization Plan Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured SEO strategies for businesses looking to improve search rankings and user engagement. This website content optimization prompt for ChatGPT walks through your business context, primary keywords, current site structure, and competitive landscape to deliver a multi-section plan covering site analysis, keyword deployment strategy, content recommendations, on-page optimization tactics, UX enhancements, and competitive positioning. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical information hierarchy. Use it when you need an actionable roadmap for overhauling existing website content or launching a new SEO-focused content strategy. ● Analyzes your existing site structure and content against your stated keywords and competitive set ● Produces a keyword deployment map showing where and how to integrate primary keywords across pages ● Specifies page types, topics, and content formats aligned with both search algorithms and user intent ● Delivers concrete on-page optimization guidance for title tags, meta descriptions, headers, and internal links ● Identifies UX improvements and competitive gaps to prioritize in your content roadmap ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO content strategist specializing in search engine rankings and user engagement optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive content optimization plan for {{business-context}}. Analyze the current site, develop a keyword-rich and navigable content strategy, and provide actionable recommendations for on-page optimization, content creation, and user experience improvements.

## Context
Business type and audience: {{business-context}}
Primary keywords: {{primary-keywords}}
Current website structure: {{current-structure}}
Main competitors: {{competitors}}

Align your strategy with current SEO best practices, balancing search engine algorithm requirements with user intent. Use dependency grammar principles to structure content recommendations for clarity and information hierarchy.

## Output
Deliver your plan with:
- **Site Analysis**: Assessment of current content and structure
- **Keyword Strategy**: How to deploy the primary keywords across pages
- **Content Recommendations**: Specific page types, topics, and formats to create
- **On-Page Optimization**: Title tags, meta descriptions, headers, internal linking
- **User Experience Enhancements**: Navigation, readability, conversion paths
- **Competitive Positioning**: Gaps and opportunities vs. competitors

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points throughout for easy implementation.
```
