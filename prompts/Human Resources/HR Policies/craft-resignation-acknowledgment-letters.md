# Resignation Acknowledgment Letter Generator

## 簡介

The Resignation Acknowledgment Letter Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compliant, professional resignation acknowledgment letters for HR teams and managers. This resignation acknowledgment letter prompt for ChatGPT produces complete formal business letters that confirm receipt of an employee's resignation, specify key dates and notice periods, express genuine appreciation for contributions, outline transition responsibilities, and maintain a tone that balances legal documentation with relationship preservation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only basic departure details to generate a polished letter ready for official use. HR professionals use it to handle employee exits with consistency, dignity, and legal clarity while protecting employer branding. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize a resignation while keeping the door open for future collaboration or rehire. ● Confirms resignation receipt with specific dates, notice period, and last working day for legal clarity ● Expresses sincere gratitude tailored to the employee's actual contributions and tenure ● Outlines transition tasks, handover responsibilities, and next steps in the offboarding process ● Maintains a professional yet warm tone suitable for official records and future reference ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an HR communications specialist drafting resignation acknowledgment letters that balance legal compliance, professionalism, and genuine appreciation.

## Task
Create a formal resignation acknowledgment letter that:
- Confirms receipt of resignation with key dates (last working day, notice period)
- Expresses sincere gratitude for specific contributions
- Outlines transition responsibilities and next steps
- Maintains a warm yet professional tone suitable for official records
- Reinforces company values and leaves the door open for future connection
- Serves as legally compliant documentation of departure terms

## Context
{{departure-details}}

This letter protects the organization while preserving the relationship for employer branding and potential rehire or collaboration.

## Output
Format as a complete formal business letter with:
- Date and address block
- Professional salutation
- Opening paragraph acknowledging the resignation
- Middle section covering logistics, appreciation for contributions, and transition requirements
- Closing paragraph with forward-looking, positive tone
- Formal signature block

Use clear paragraph breaks and standard business letter spacing.
```
