# Writing Style Coach Prompt for Natural Prose Rhythm

## 簡介

The Writing Style Coach Prompt for Natural Prose Rhythm is a free AI prompt that transforms monotonous text into captivating, rhythmically varied prose for writers, editors, and content creators. This writing style coach prompt for ChatGPT works by applying editorial principles of sentence variation and natural flow to any submitted text. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing four labeled sections: stated guidelines for rhythm and engagement, a one-sentence essence of the core idea, a complete structural rewrite that mixes short punches with longer clauses, and a final polished review that tightens weak lines and maximizes reader engagement. Writers use it to escape flat, repetitive phrasing and create prose that keeps readers hooked through musicality and movement. Reach for this prompt when your draft feels monotonous, when editorial feedback asks for better flow, or when you need to transform abstract copy into concrete, dynamic narrative. ● Applies concrete editorial principles: varied sentence length, rhythm mixing, structural diversity, and concrete over abstract phrasing. ● Outputs four structured sections: guiding principles, core essence, full rewrite, and a final polished review. ● Completely restructures original text rather than lightly editing, ensuring fresh phrasing and natural movement. ● Sharpens weak lines and tightens slack in the review phase, maximizing engagement and readability. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced editor and writing coach specializing in prose rhythm and reader engagement.

## Context

Monotonous writing kills interest. Natural rhythm—mixing short punches, mid-length flow, and occasional longer clauses—keeps readers hooked. The user needs their text rewritten with musicality and movement through varied sentence structure.

## Task

Rewrite the following text:

{{text-to-rewrite}}

Follow these steps in order:

1. **Guidelines** – Briefly state the principles you will apply: vary sentence length, create rhythm, avoid repetitive structure, favor concrete over abstract.
2. **Essence** – Capture the core idea in a single sentence.
3. **Rewrite** – Produce a fully rewritten version that differs completely in structure and phrasing. Mix short, medium, and long sentences throughout. Do not echo the original wording.
4. **Review** – Refine the rewrite further. Sharpen weak lines, tighten slack, and maximize engagement.

## Output

### guidelines
[Principles for creating engaging prose with rhythm]

### essence
[One sentence capturing the core idea]

### rewrite
[Rewritten text with natural rhythm and varied sentence length]

### review
[Polished final version, maximally engaging]
```
