# Research Discussion Paper Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Research Discussion Paper Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured academic discussion papers for researchers, students, and professionals writing scholarly analyses. This research discussion paper prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI through every section of a formal academic paper - from abstract and introduction through viewpoint analysis, literature synthesis, gap identification, and future research directions - using dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and logical flow. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is ideal for exploring emerging topics, preparing grant proposals, conducting systematic reviews, or framing new research questions. The prompt instructs the AI to analyze at least three distinct perspectives on your topic, synthesize current research findings, pinpoint three or more knowledge gaps or controversies, and recommend three promising directions for future investigation. It delivers a complete manuscript structure including abstract, multiple analytical sections, conclusion with actionable insights, and a references section formatted to academic standards. Reach for this prompt when you need a balanced, critical starting point for a conference paper, thesis chapter, literature review, or policy brief. ● Outputs a complete academic discussion paper with abstract, introduction, viewpoint analysis, research review, gap identification, future directions, conclusion, and references. ● Analyzes at least three distinct perspectives, identifies three knowledge gaps, and suggests three future research directions with supporting rationale. ● Structures arguments using dependency grammar principles for clarity and logical flow. ● Adapts tone and depth to your specified audience and paper objective, whether academic peers, industry professionals, or policymakers. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert researcher and writer producing an academic discussion paper. Apply dependency grammar principles to structure clear, well-formed sentences throughout.

## Task
Write a comprehensive discussion paper on {{topic-field}} that introduces the subject, explores multiple viewpoints, reviews current research, identifies knowledge gaps, proposes future directions, and concludes with actionable insights.

## Context
**Audience**: {{target-audience}}
**Paper objective**: {{paper-goal}}

The paper must present a balanced, critical analysis that synthesizes information from multiple sources without bias. Cover the topic from various angles, highlighting areas of consensus, controversy, and opportunity for further investigation.

## Output
Deliver the paper in this structure:

# [Title]
*Concise and engaging*

## Abstract
Brief summary of content and conclusions (150–250 words).

## Introduction
- Background and significance of {{topic-field}}
- Thesis statement or main argument

## Viewpoints and Perspectives
Describe and analyze **at least three** distinct viewpoints on the topic, examining their strengths, limitations, and supporting evidence.

## Current State of Research
- Key findings and established knowledge
- Recent developments and trends
- Methodological approaches and tools in use

## Knowledge Gaps and Controversies
Identify **at least three** areas of uncertainty, debate, or insufficient evidence.

## Future Directions
Suggest **at least three** promising areas for further research, with rationale for each.

## Conclusion
- Summarize main points
- Discuss implications and significance
- Provide concrete recommendations for future work

## References
List works cited in appropriate academic format (APA, MLA, Chicago, or discipline standard).
```
