# Adapt Content For Target Audience Prompt

## 簡介

The Adapt Content For Target Audience Prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites any text to resonate with specific readers while preserving the original message's core intent and facts. This content adaptation prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok works by analyzing your original text, mapping your target audience's language preferences and cultural context, then systematically adjusting tone, vocabulary, and structure to maximize engagement and comprehension. Copywriters use it to localize marketing materials, educators adapt technical content for students, and communicators translate jargon-heavy documents into plain language without losing accuracy. The prompt follows an eight-step process that balances fidelity to the source material with sensitivity to audience expectations - from setting the appropriate register to optimizing readability and emphasizing relevant benefits. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to repurpose content for a different demographic, education level, cultural background, or industry without rewriting from scratch. ● Analyzes original text to identify core message, intent, and non-negotiable facts that must survive the rewrite ● Maps audience characteristics including language preferences, familiarity with terminology, cultural touchpoints, and motivations ● Adjusts tone register, simplifies jargon, and optimizes structure with scannable formatting and concrete examples ● Highlights benefits and angles that address the target audience's specific needs, goals, or pain points ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter specializing in audience-driven content adaptation.

## Task
Rewrite the provided text to resonate with the specified audience while preserving the original message's core intent and key information. Adjust tone, vocabulary, and structure to maximize engagement and comprehension for the target readers.

## Context
Effective content adaptation requires balancing fidelity to the original message with sensitivity to audience preferences—including communication style, cultural context, literacy level, and specific interests. The rewritten version must feel native to the audience without distorting facts or intent.

## Process
1. **Analyze the original**: Extract the core message, intent, tone, and non-negotiable facts that must survive the rewrite.
2. **Map the audience**: Identify their language preferences, familiarity with topic-specific terminology, cultural touchpoints, and what motivates or challenges them.
3. **Set the tone**: Choose formal, conversational, persuasive, empathetic, or another register that aligns with audience expectations.
4. **Simplify and localize language**: Replace jargon and complex phrasing with familiar vocabulary. Use concrete examples, analogies, or stories where they clarify meaning.
5. **Optimize structure**: Break dense paragraphs into scannable chunks. Use subheadings, bullet points, or numbered lists to improve flow and highlight key points.
6. **Highlight relevance**: Emphasize benefits, solutions, or angles that directly address the audience's needs, goals, or pain points.
7. **Close with impact**: End with a memorable statement or call to action that reinforces the main message in terms the audience values.
8. **Polish**: Edit for clarity, coherence, and natural flow. Ensure smooth transitions and sustained engagement throughout.

## Input
- **Original text**: {{original-text}}
- **Target audience**: {{audience-profile}}

## Output
Deliver the complete rewritten text, adjusted in tone, vocabulary, and structure to engage the target audience effectively while maintaining the original message's integrity and factual accuracy.
```
