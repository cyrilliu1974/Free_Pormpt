# Daily Operations Management Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Daily Operations Management Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive operational frameworks and performance tracking systems for operations managers and business leaders. This daily operations management prompt for ChatGPT produces a two-part deliverable: a complete operations management plan addressing staff supervision, resource allocation, quality control, issue resolution protocols, and process improvement strategies, plus a customizable KPI tracking table in markdown format that monitors daily targets, actual performance, and variance analysis. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, tailoring the output to your specific business context and the performance indicators you need to track. Use it to establish systematic oversight across departments, standardize operational procedures, or build a performance dashboard that keeps your team aligned on productivity and customer satisfaction metrics. ● Creates actionable plans for staff supervision, resource allocation, and quality control tailored to your business context ● Builds markdown KPI tracking tables with configurable columns for daily targets, actual performance, and variance analysis ● Includes issue identification protocols and process improvement strategies to maintain operational standards ● Adapts to any business model by accepting custom KPI lists and column requirements ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert operations manager creating a daily operations management plan and performance tracking system.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive operations plan that covers staff supervision, resource allocation, quality control, and performance monitoring. Include strategies to identify and resolve operational issues, implement process improvements, and maintain productivity and customer satisfaction standards.

## Context
Business context: {{business-context}}

Key performance indicators to track: {{kpi-list}}

## Output
Deliver your plan in two parts:

1. **Operations Management Plan** covering:
   - Staff supervision approach
   - Resource allocation framework
   - Quality control measures
   - Issue identification and resolution protocols
   - Process improvement strategies
   - Productivity and customer satisfaction standards

2. **KPI Tracking Table** in markdown format with {{number-of-columns}} columns:
   - Header row: KPI names from the list above
   - Row 1: Daily targets
   - Row 2: Actual performance
   - Row 3: Variance analysis (difference and percentage)

Ensure the plan is actionable and tailored to the business context provided.
```
