# Referral Program Copy and Structure Generator

## 簡介

The Referral Program Copy and Structure Generator is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end referral marketing systems with persuasive promotional copy for businesses seeking organic customer acquisition. This referral program prompt for ChatGPT designs dual-sided incentive structures, unique tracking codes, multi-channel promotional strategies, and ready-to-deploy copy including headlines, body text, and calls-to-action. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce comprehensive referral frameworks covering everything from reward mechanics to A/B testing plans. Marketing teams use it to launch word-of-mouth campaigns, SaaS companies rely on it for user growth loops, and e-commerce brands apply it to turn customers into advocates. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured referral system that balances compelling rewards with implementation simplicity and performance tracking. ● Designs dual-incentive structures that reward both referrers and new customers while protecting margins ● Creates promotional copy optimized for email, social media, paid advertising, and influencer partnerships ● Includes tracking systems with unique referral codes and analytics dashboards for attribution and measurement ● Provides A/B testing frameworks and continuous optimization strategies based on performance data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketer specializing in referral marketing strategies and persuasive copywriting.

## Task
Design a comprehensive referral program structure and write compelling promotional copy that will drive user acquisition and engagement.

## Context
Referral programs succeed when they balance compelling incentives with simplicity, accurate tracking, and persuasive messaging tailored to distribution channels. Your recommendations should be easy to implement and optimize over time.

**Product/Service:** {{product-service-description}}

## Output

### 1. Referral Program Overview
Provide a high-level summary of the recommended referral program structure and how it will benefit this product or service.

### 2. Referral Incentives
- **Referrer Incentive:** Describe the reward offered to users who refer others
- **Referee Incentive:** Describe the reward offered to new users who sign up through a referral

### 3. Referral Tracking
- **Unique Referral Codes:** Explain how unique codes will track referrals and attribute them to specific users
- **Referral Analytics:** Describe analytics and reporting features to measure program success

### 4. Promotional Channels
- **Email Marketing:** Recommendations for leveraging email to promote the referral program
- **Social Media:** Strategies for promoting on social platforms
- **Paid Advertising:** Guidance on using paid ads to drive awareness and participation
- **Influencer Partnerships:** Ideas for partnering with influencers to expand reach

### 5. Promotional Copy
**Headline:**
[Write compelling headline]

**Subheadline:**
[Expand on headline with additional context]

**Body Copy:**
[Craft persuasive copy highlighting benefits and encouraging participation]

**Call to Action:**
[Write clear, compelling CTA]

### 6. A/B Testing Plan
- **Variable 1:** [Key element to test in program or promotional materials]
- **Variable 2:** [Second key element to test]

### 7. Optimization Plan
Provide a strategy for continually optimizing the referral program based on performance data and user feedback.

---

**Design Criteria:**
- Easy to understand and participate in for both referrers and referees
- Incentives compelling enough to drive participation without harming profitability
- Accurate, reliable tracking for proper attribution and reward distribution
- Promotional materials tailored to target audience and optimized for distribution channels
- Continuous A/B testing for maximum effectiveness
```
