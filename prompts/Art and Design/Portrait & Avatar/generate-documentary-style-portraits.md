# Documentary-Style Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Documentary-Style Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates cinematic environmental portraits with rich color saturation and narrative depth for photographers, designers, and visual storytellers. This prompt produces images that mirror Steve McCurry's iconic photography style, featuring subjects placed within meaningful settings, strong catchlights in the eyes, and the warm, saturated tones associated with classic documentary photography. The documentary-style portrait prompt for Midjourney uses professional photography parameters - 24-70mm f/2.8 lens simulation, natural sunlight mixed with ambient reflections, and National Geographic mood - to deliver images that feel authentic and story-driven. It also runs on Flux and Nano Banana for users who prefer alternative image generation models. Reach for this prompt when you need portraits that balance human emotion with environmental context, whether for editorial projects, social media content, character development, or cultural storytelling. ● Produces portraits with rich, saturated colors and strong catchlights that draw attention to the subject's eyes and expression. ● Simulates a 24-70mm f/2.8 lens with natural sunlight and ambient reflections for authentic photographic depth. ● Uses controlled chaos and 3:2 aspect ratio to balance compositional variety with classic documentary framing. ● Works with any subject and setting variables to create culturally immersive, story-driven environmental portraits. ## Prompt

```
Vivid environmental portrait of {{subject}} standing in {{setting}}, rich saturated colors, photographed in the style of Steve McCurry. Strong catchlights, deep storytelling eyes. Shot on a 24-70mm f/2.8 lens, natural sunlight mixed with ambient reflections, National Geographic mood. --chaos 8 --ar 3:2 --quality 2
```
