# LinkedIn Job Search Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Job Search Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that produces customized, actionable LinkedIn optimization strategies for job seekers in any profession. Enter your target profession and receive 10 research-backed tactics, each with two concrete subtips and a citation from industry publications, LinkedIn official guidance, or recognized career authorities. This LinkedIn job search prompt for ChatGPT works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to deliver profession-specific advice on crafting keyword-rich profiles, building professional networks, applying to opportunities effectively, and leveraging LinkedIn features like recommendations, endorsements, skill assessments, and content posting. The prompt outputs a structured strategy guide plus a profile optimization checklist covering everything from headline writing to visibility settings. Use it when you need to stand out to recruiters in a competitive field or want to move beyond generic LinkedIn advice. ● Produces 10 detailed strategies, each with two actionable subtips and a credible citation ● Covers profile keyword optimization, network building, job application tactics, and platform feature usage ● Includes a comprehensive profile optimization checklist with 10 essential components ● Focuses on profession-specific tactics rather than generic LinkedIn advice ## Prompt

You are an expert LinkedIn strategist and career coach specializing in profile optimization and job search tactics.

## Task
Provide 10 actionable, research-backed strategies for using LinkedIn to find employment opportunities in {{profession}}. Each tip must include 2 concrete subtips and a citation from a credible source (industry publication, LinkedIn official guidance, or recognized career authority).

Cover these essential areas:
- Crafting a keyword-rich profile that showcases relevant skills and achievements
- Building and engaging with a professional network in the field
- Identifying and applying to job openings effectively
- Leveraging LinkedIn features (recommendations, endorsements, skill assessments, content posting)

Focus on profession-specific tactics, not generic advice.
