# Lead Capture Form Design Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Lead Capture Form Design Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive form blueprints optimized for conversion rates and user experience. This lead capture form prompt for ChatGPT guides you through designing every element of a high-performing form: field selection with validation rules, benefit-focused labels and placeholder text, persuasive call-to-action buttons, trust signals, mobile responsiveness recommendations, and privacy compliance considerations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, taking your business context and conversion goal as inputs and returning a complete form specification with justifications for each design decision. Use it when building email list opt-ins, webinar registrations, content download gates, consultation requests, or any lead generation funnel where form design directly impacts conversion rates. ● Generates field-by-field specifications with label copy, validation rules, and completion-rate justifications ● Provides persuasive CTA button copy, microcopy, and supporting trust signals tailored to your lead magnet ● Includes mobile responsiveness guidance, progressive disclosure strategies, and GDPR compliance notes ● Delivers A/B test recommendations and friction-point analysis to improve conversion rates over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert lead generation specialist designing high-converting lead capture forms.

## Task
Create a comprehensive lead capture form blueprint that maximizes conversion rates and collects valuable customer information. Structure all form elements using clear information hierarchy and logical flow.

## Context
**Business & Offer:**
{{business-context}}
(Include: business type, target audience, unique selling proposition, and the primary lead magnet being offered)

**Conversion Goal:**
{{conversion-goal}}

## Requirements
- Optimize form length and field selection for user experience and completion rates
- Write compelling, benefit-focused copy for labels, placeholders, and helper text
- Design a persuasive call-to-action that aligns with the lead magnet and goal
- Include recommendations for trust signals, progressive disclosure, and mobile responsiveness
- Consider GDPR/privacy compliance where data collection is required

## Output
Deliver a structured form blueprint with these sections:

**Form Fields**
- List each field with label, field type, validation rules, and justification
- Prioritize essential fields only; explain why each is necessary

**Call-to-Action**
- Primary CTA button copy and design notes
- Supporting microcopy (privacy note, value reinforcement)

**Additional Elements**
- Trust signals (testimonials, security badges, social proof)
- Form page layout recommendations
- Thank-you page or next-step guidance

**Conversion Optimization Notes**
- A/B test suggestions
- Friction points addressed
- Expected impact on conversion rate
```
