# Personalized Prospect Nurturing Campaign Prompt

## 簡介

The Personalized Prospect Nurturing Campaign Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, multi-stage communication strategies for sales and marketing teams. This prospect nurturing prompt for ChatGPT builds comprehensive campaigns organized by sales funnel stage - awareness, consideration, and decision - with specific touchpoints, timing recommendations, and messaging themes tailored to your prospect persona, product offering, industry, and unique selling proposition. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a campaign blueprint that sequences interactions across email, social media, and other channels to progressively build trust and move prospects toward purchase decisions. Marketing strategists, sales enablement teams, and growth professionals use it to map multi-week or multi-month nurturing sequences that address pain points and demonstrate value at each stage. ● Structures campaigns by sales funnel stage with distinct objectives for awareness, consideration, and decision phases ● Defines specific touchpoints with channel recommendations, timing intervals, and sequencing logic ● Provides messaging themes, value propositions, and calls-to-action aligned to prospect goals and pain points ● Includes transition criteria to identify when prospects are ready to advance to the next stage ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert marketing strategist developing personalized nurturing campaigns that build trust and guide prospects through the sales funnel.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, multi-channel nurturing strategy with a structured sequence of touchpoints across email, social media, and other relevant channels. Each interaction should deepen engagement and move prospects closer to a purchase decision.

## Context
**Prospect persona:** {{prospect-persona}}

**Product/service:** {{product-service}}

**Industry:** {{industry}}

**Unique selling proposition:** {{usp}}

**Campaign duration:** {{campaign-duration}}

## Approach
Craft content that addresses the prospect's specific pain points and goals, aligning them with what the product or service delivers. Structure each touchpoint to progressively build trust and demonstrate value.

## Output
Provide a structured campaign plan organized by stage (e.g., Awareness, Consideration, Decision). For each stage, include:

- **Stage name and objective**
- **Touchpoints:** specific channels and timing
- **Content strategy:** messaging themes, value propositions, and calls-to-action
- **Transition criteria:** signals that indicate readiness to move to the next stage
```
