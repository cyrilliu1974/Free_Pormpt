# Order Confirmation Email Generator

## 簡介

The Order Confirmation Email Generator is a free AI prompt that produces three complete transactional email templates for e-commerce brands looking to turn order confirmations into retention tools. This order confirmation email prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok creates three distinct templates - each for a different order scenario such as first-time purchase, repeat order, or high-value order. Every template includes a subject line, personalized greeting, itemized order breakdown, shipping timeline, customer support contact, and a rotating revenue driver section: the first template embeds an upsell opportunity, the second promotes a referral program, and the third highlights a loyalty program. The prompt ensures that every confirmation email reinforces your brand value proposition and speaks directly to your target audience. Real use cases include Shopify store owners who want to automate professional post-purchase emails, DTC brands needing consistent transactional messaging, and e-commerce marketers who want confirmations that drive repeat business instead of being purely informational. ● Outputs three full templates, each with a different order scenario and revenue-driving section ● Includes subject line, itemized order details, shipping information, and customer support contact in every template ● Rotates upsell, referral, and loyalty sections across templates to maximize post-purchase engagement ● Adapts tone and messaging to your target audience, brand value proposition, and product or service category ## Prompt

```
## Role

You write order confirmation email templates for e-commerce brands. Your output drives customer satisfaction, repeat purchases, and loyalty through clear, on-brand transactional communication.

## Context

- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Brand value proposition: {{brand-usp}}
- Product or service: {{product-or-service}}

## Task

Write three order confirmation email templates, each covering a distinct order scenario (e.g., first-time purchase, repeat order, high-value order). Use persuasive but non-pushy language throughout. Structure each template to reinforce the brand's value and leave a positive lasting impression.

## Output

Produce three templates in plain text. Each template follows this structure:

---

**Scenario:** [Brief description of the order scenario]

**Subject Line:** [Engaging, informative subject line]

**Email Body:**

- **Opening:** [Warm, personalized greeting]
- **Order Details:** [Itemized breakdown of the order]
- **Shipping Information:** [Clear shipping details and timeline]
- **Customer Support:** [How to reach support]
- **Revenue Driver:** [Rotate across templates—use an upsell opportunity in the first, referral program in the second, loyalty program in the third]
- **Closing:** [Friendly sign-off with brand reinforcement]

---

Vary the Revenue Driver section across all three templates so each serves a different retention goal. Do not repeat the same approach.
```
