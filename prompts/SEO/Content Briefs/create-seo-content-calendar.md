# SEO Content Calendar Builder for Blogs

## 簡介

The SEO Content Calendar Builder for Blogs is a free AI prompt that creates strategic publishing schedules balancing keyword targeting, audience engagement, and realistic production cadence for bloggers and content teams. This content calendar prompt for ChatGPT identifies eight high-value SEO keywords tailored to your niche and audience, then pairs each with a compelling blog post topic and a sustainable publishing frequency. The output is a markdown table that spans informational, commercial, and long-tail keyword opportunities while recommending weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly cadences based on content depth and competitive factors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across major text models. Use it when launching a new blog vertical, planning a quarter's editorial pipeline, or auditing whether your current schedule aligns with search intent and team bandwidth. ● Surfaces 8 SEO keywords spanning informational, commercial, and long-tail search intents relevant to your niche and audience. ● Maps each keyword to an actionable, reader-focused blog post title designed to drive clicks and engagement. ● Assigns realistic publishing frequencies that maintain consistency without overextending editorial resources. ● Outputs a clean markdown table you can import into project management tools, editorial calendars, or share with writers and stakeholders. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic content planning expert who devises blog post schedules to maximize audience engagement and SEO performance.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive content calendar for the user's blog that balances search visibility, reader interest, and sustainable publishing cadence.

## Context
{{editorial-scope}}

## Process
1. Identify 8 high-value SEO keywords relevant to the topic, niche, and target audience provided
2. Craft a compelling, actionable blog post topic for each keyword
3. Assign a realistic publishing frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly) that maintains consistency without overextending resources

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with this structure:

**Content Calendar for [Topic] in [Niche] targeting [Target Audience]**

| SEO Keyword | Blog Post Topic | Publishing Frequency |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| [keyword 1] | [topic 1]       | [frequency 1]        |
| [keyword 2] | [topic 2]       | [frequency 2]        |
| [keyword 3] | [topic 3]       | [frequency 3]        |
| [keyword 4] | [topic 4]       | [frequency 4]        |
| [keyword 5] | [topic 5]       | [frequency 5]        |
| [keyword 6] | [topic 6]       | [frequency 6]        |
| [keyword 7] | [topic 7]       | [frequency 7]        |
| [keyword 8] | [topic 8]       | [frequency 8]        |

Ensure keywords span informational, commercial, and long-tail opportunities. Vary publishing frequencies based on content depth and competitive landscape.
```
