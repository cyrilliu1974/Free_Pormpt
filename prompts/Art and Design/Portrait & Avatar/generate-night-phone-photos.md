# Night Phone Photo Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Night Phone Photo Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that creates authentic smartphone-style portraits illuminated by neon lighting at night for photographers, artists, and content creators. It simulates a 26mm smartphone camera with f/1.6 aperture and Night Mode long exposure, producing soft handheld blur, vibrant urban glow, and moody atmospheres with glass reflections. This night phone photo prompt for Midjourney works on Flux and Nano Banana, making it easy to generate street photography aesthetics without HDR oversharpening. Use it when you want realistic phone camera results - imperfect, atmospheric, and authentic - rather than polished studio shots. ● Simulates genuine smartphone Night Mode with 26mm focal length, f/1.6 aperture, and natural handheld motion blur ● Produces vibrant neon reflections on glass and skin without artificial HDR sharpening or over-processing ● Outputs in 9:16 portrait aspect ratio, ready for social media and mobile-first platforms ● Captures moody urban atmospheres with the organic imperfections of real phone photography at night ## Prompt

```
Natural phone-style portrait of {{subject}} illuminated by {{neon-lighting}} at night. Slight reflections on glass, moody atmosphere. Shot using a simulated 26mm smartphone camera, f/1.6, Night Mode long exposure, soft handheld blur. Vibrant urban glow. --ar 9:16 --q 2 --no HDR oversharpening
```
