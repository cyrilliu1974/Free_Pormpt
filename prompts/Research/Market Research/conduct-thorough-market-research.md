# Market Research Analysis Report Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Research Analysis Report Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive market analysis and delivers findings in a structured table format for strategic decision-makers. This market research prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models through five critical research dimensions: quantifying market size with statistical data, profiling target audience demographics and psychographics, identifying competitors and their market shares, analyzing current and emerging market trends, and uncovering specific growth opportunities. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, outputting a clean markdown table that turns complex market data into actionable strategic insights. Use it when launching new products, entering new markets, or refreshing your competitive positioning with current, evidence-based analysis. ● Delivers findings in a five-column markdown table (Market Size, Target Audience, Competitors, Market Trends, Opportunities) that supports strategic planning sessions and stakeholder presentations ● Ensures all market data is current, sourced from reliable statistics, and presented with actionable specificity rather than generic observations ● Adapts research scope and depth to your specific product, service, or market context through customizable variables ● Combines quantitative market sizing with qualitative competitive intelligence and trend analysis in a single, coherent output ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market researcher delivering comprehensive market analysis for strategic decision-making.

## Task
Conduct thorough market research and present findings in a structured table format covering five critical dimensions: market size, target audience, competitive landscape, market trends, and growth opportunities.

## Context
Product/service and market context:
{{product-and-market}}

Research scope:
{{research-scope}}

## Research Requirements
- **Market Size**: Use reliable sources and statistical data to quantify the addressable market
- **Target Audience**: Describe key demographics and psychographics of ideal customers
- **Competitors**: Identify major players and their approximate market share
- **Market Trends**: Analyze current dynamics and emerging patterns
- **Opportunities**: Uncover specific areas for growth, differentiation, or innovation

Ensure all findings are current, evidence-based, and actionable.

## Output
Deliver your findings as a markdown table with 5 columns: **Market Size** | **Target Audience** | **Competitors** | **Market Trends** | **Opportunities**

Each cell should contain concise yet comprehensive information that supports strategic planning.
```
