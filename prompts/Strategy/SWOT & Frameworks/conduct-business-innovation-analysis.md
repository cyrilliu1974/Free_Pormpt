# Business Innovation Gap Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Innovation Gap Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that conducts comprehensive strategic assessments to identify innovation opportunities and performance gaps for businesses seeking competitive advantage. This business innovation gap analysis prompt for ChatGPT systematically evaluates six critical dimensions: business model, product portfolio, target markets, competitive position, financial performance, and organizational capabilities. The prompt guides AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) to compare your current state against an ambitious future vision, then surfaces specific gaps and delivers prioritized, actionable innovation recommendations. Real-world applications include strategic planning cycles, market repositioning efforts, new product development roadmaps, and organizational transformation initiatives. Strategy consultants, innovation teams, business analysts, and C-suite leaders reach for this prompt when they need a disciplined framework to move from situational assessment to concrete innovation initiatives. ● Maps current state across business model, products, markets, competitive positioning, financials, and capabilities ● Defines an ambitious yet realistic future state aligned with strategic vision and market opportunities ● Identifies and prioritizes gaps by impact potential and implementation feasibility ● Delivers tailored recommendations spanning business model innovation, product development, market expansion, partnerships, and capability building ● Concludes with clear next steps for implementing high-priority innovation initiatives ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business analyst and innovation consultant conducting a comprehensive gap analysis to identify areas ripe for innovation.

## Task

Analyze the current state, desired future state, and gaps across key business dimensions for the following business. Provide specific, prioritized recommendations for innovation initiatives to close identified gaps and drive strategic progress.

## Context

**Business to analyze:**
{{business-context}}

Conduct a thorough, objective assessment considering internal factors and the external competitive landscape. Define an ambitious yet realistic future state aligned with the business's vision. Focus on strategic-level gaps with the greatest potential impact. Prioritize recommendations by impact and feasibility.

## Output

Structure your analysis in four sections:

**1. Current State Analysis**
- Business Model
- Product Portfolio
- Target Markets
- Competitive Position
- Financial Performance
- Organizational Capabilities

**2. Desired Future State**
- Vision
- Strategic Goals
- Target Business Model
- Target Product Portfolio
- Target Market Position
- Financial Targets
- Capability Requirements

**3. Gap Analysis**

Identify gaps between current and desired state across:
- Business Model
- Product Offerings
- Market Opportunities
- Competitive Position
- Financial Performance
- Capabilities

**4. Innovation Recommendations**

Provide tailored, pragmatic recommendations for:
- Business Model Innovations
- Product Innovations
- Market Expansion Moves
- Strategic Partnerships
- Capability Building Initiatives
- Disruptive Opportunities

**Next Steps**

Conclude with clear, actionable next steps for implementing the recommended innovations.
```
