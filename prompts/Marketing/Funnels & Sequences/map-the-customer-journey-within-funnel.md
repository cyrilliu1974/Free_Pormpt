# Customer Journey Map for Sales Funnel Stages

## 簡介

The Customer Journey Map for Sales Funnel Stages is a free AI prompt that creates structured, stage-by-stage visualizations of the customer experience for businesses optimizing their conversion paths. This customer journey mapping prompt for ChatGPT analyzes each phase of your sales funnel - from awareness through conversion and beyond - documenting key touchpoints where customers interact with your business, the specific actions they take, their emotional states at each moment, and the friction points that risk drop-off. It outputs a markdown table with customizable columns for each funnel stage, making it easy to spot gaps in your customer experience strategy. Marketing teams use it to align messaging with customer sentiment, product managers use it to identify UX improvements, and sales operations teams use it to reduce friction in the conversion path. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. This prompt is for marketers, CX designers, and funnel analysts who need a systematic framework to map customer behavior and emotions across the entire sales journey. ● Traces the complete customer experience from first awareness through conversion and post-purchase stages ● Documents touchpoints, customer actions, emotional shifts, and pain points in a clear table format ● Tailors analysis to your specific business type, customer behavior patterns, and competitive context ● Provides actionable insights that reveal where customers experience friction or disengagement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer journey analyst specializing in sales funnel optimization.

## Task
Create a comprehensive customer journey map in table format that traces the customer experience through each stage of the sales funnel. Analyze and document:

- Key touchpoints where customers interact with the business
- Specific customer actions taken at each stage
- Emotional states and sentiment shifts
- Potential pain points, friction, and drop-off risks

Tailor the analysis to the specific characteristics, customer behavior patterns, and competitive context of {{business-type}}.

## Context
The sales funnel stages to map: {{funnel-stages}}

Provide detailed, actionable insights for each stage that create a holistic view of the customer experience from initial awareness through conversion and beyond.

## Output
Deliver a markdown table with {{number-of-columns}} columns, each representing one funnel stage. Include these rows:

- **Touchpoints**: Where and how customers interact
- **Customer Actions**: What customers do at this stage
- **Emotions**: How customers feel
- **Pain Points**: Obstacles, frustrations, and friction
```
