# Social Media Strategy Builder Prompt

## 簡介

The Social Media Strategy Builder Prompt is a free AI prompt that walks you through building a complete social media strategy in four sequential, guided phases for any business or brand. This social media strategy prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok starts with discovery (competitor analysis, SWOT, positioning gaps), then builds a strategic framework (content pillars, brand voice, platform prioritization, 90-day KPIs), moves into execution (2-week content calendar, engagement tactics, metrics tracking, optimization playbook), and closes with sustained growth systems (quarterly reviews, trend monitoring, community cultivation). Each phase pauses and waits for you to type "continue," so you can review, refine inputs, or adapt direction before moving forward. Use it when launching a new brand presence, overhauling an underperforming account, pitching strategy to a client, or systematizing content operations for a growing business. ● Produces discovery analysis with competitor positioning, differentiation points, and SWOT assessment ● Builds a strategic framework including content pillars, brand voice guidelines, platform prioritization, and 90-day milestones ● Delivers a 2-week content calendar, engagement tactics, performance metrics, and optimization playbook ● Provides a sustained-growth roadmap with quarterly review checklists, trend monitoring, and community-building tactics ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a social media strategist with deep expertise in translating business objectives into platform-native content strategies and measurable growth plans.

## Task

Develop a complete social media strategy through four sequential phases. After delivering each phase output, pause and prompt the user to type "continue" before proceeding to the next phase.

## Context

Business to strategize for:
{{business-details}}

## Process

### Phase 1: Discovery and Analysis

**Objective:** Build a comprehensive business and competitive landscape snapshot.

**Deliverables:**
- Business profile summary (niche, target audience, primary goals)
- Competitor analysis with positioning gaps
- Unique market differentiation points
- SWOT assessment for social media presence

**Output format:** Structured analysis with bullet points and clear findings.

*Pause here. Prompt: "Ready for Phase 2? Type 'continue'"*

---

### Phase 2: Strategic Framework

**Objective:** Translate discovery insights into an actionable social media blueprint.

**Deliverables:**
- 3–5 content pillars aligned with business goals
- Brand voice guidelines (tone, language, style)
- Platform prioritization with rationale
- 90-day growth milestones and KPIs

**Output format:** Strategic framework document with clear recommendations.

*Pause here. Prompt: "Ready for Phase 3? Type 'continue'"*

---

### Phase 3: Execution and Optimization

**Objective:** Activate the strategy and refine based on performance data.

**Deliverables:**
- 2-week content calendar with post concepts
- Engagement tactics (CTAs, community interaction)
- Metrics to track (reach, engagement rate, conversions)
- Optimization playbook based on hypothetical early results

**Output format:** Actionable implementation guide with sample content.

*Pause here. Prompt: "Ready for Phase 4? Type 'continue'"*

---

### Phase 4: Sustained Growth

**Objective:** Establish systems for long-term evolution and community building.

**Deliverables:**
- Quarterly strategy review checklist
- Trend monitoring framework
- Community cultivation tactics (UGC, collaborations, events)
- Scaling roadmap for expanding reach

**Output format:** Maintenance and growth playbook for ongoing success.

## Output

For each phase, provide clear, actionable deliverables in the specified format. Wait for user confirmation before advancing to the next phase.
```
