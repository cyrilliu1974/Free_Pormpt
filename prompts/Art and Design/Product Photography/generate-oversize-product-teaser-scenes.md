# Oversize Product Teaser Scene Generator

## 簡介

The Oversize Product Teaser Scene Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms any product photo into a vertical, ultra-photorealistic editorial marketing image with dramatic scale and cinematic lighting. This product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and similar models creates a 4:5 composition in which a person walks through an architectural entrance holding your product scaled up massively for visual impact, with strong natural sunlight casting long shadows and creating warm-cool contrast. The prompt preserves your product exactly as uploaded - shape, colors, label, texture, and all branding - while integrating it seamlessly into a seasonal editorial scene with customizable architecture style and coat weight. Reach for this prompt when you need scroll-stopping product imagery for social media, ad campaigns, or brand teasers that blend editorial sophistication with bold visual storytelling. ● Vertical 4:5 composition optimized for Instagram, Pinterest, and mobile-first platforms ● Preserves uploaded product integrity - no modifications to branding, label, or design ● Customizable architectural style and seasonal palette via {{architecture-style}} and {{season}} variables ● Cinematic natural side-lighting with medium-high contrast and accurate contact shadows ● Zero text, logos, watermarks, or graphic overlays in the final output ## Prompt

```
Ultra-photorealistic editorial marketing scene, vertical 4:5 composition. Eye-level slightly wide camera angle.

A person in a long neutral coat walks forward through a {{architecture-style}} entrance, holding the uploaded product (massively oversized for dramatic effect) at chest height, face partially hidden behind it. Coat weight and palette suit {{season}}. {{architecture-style}} walls, doorframe, and ground surface with subtle seasonal detail. Strong cinematic natural sunlight from the side casts long architectural shadows; warm sunlight against cool {{season}} shadows, medium-high contrast.

Person centered in the doorway with negative space above. No text, typography, overlays, graphics, logos, or watermarks anywhere in frame.

**Product integration (critical):** Use the uploaded product exactly as-is—preserve its shape, colors, label, texture, and all branding. Do NOT generate, replace, or modify the product in any way. Scale it up dramatically while maintaining photorealistic lighting match and accurate contact shadow where held.

Editorial clean aesthetic, zero CGI look, zero AI artifacts. Very high resolution output.
```
