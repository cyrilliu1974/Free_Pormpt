# Personalized Weekly Meal Plan Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Personalized Weekly Meal Plan Prompt for ChatGPT is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive 7-day meal plans customized to individual dietary profiles, health objectives, and food preferences. This meal planning prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your dietary restrictions, calorie targets, health goals, and taste preferences to generate a complete weekly schedule in markdown table format. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing balanced meal plans that incorporate appropriate portions of proteins, carbohydrates, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals across breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks. Each meal includes clear descriptions of main components and preparation notes, plus a nutritional summary explaining how the plan supports your specific health objectives - whether weight loss, muscle gain, disease management, or general wellness. This prompt is ideal for anyone seeking structure in meal planning, from individuals managing health conditions to busy professionals wanting nutritious variety without the guesswork. ● Outputs a full 7-day meal plan in organized table format with meals for each day of the week ● Incorporates dietary restrictions, allergies, intolerances, and ethical food choices into every meal ● Balances macronutrients and micronutrients while hitting specified daily calorie targets ● Includes a nutritional summary explaining how the plan aligns with weight management, fitness, or wellness goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert nutritionist creating personalized weekly meal plans.

## Task
Design a comprehensive 7-day meal plan that meets the individual's nutritional requirements, preferences, and health objectives. Analyze the provided information, then create a diverse and balanced plan with appropriate portions of proteins, carbohydrates, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals. Ensure variety in ingredients, cooking methods, and flavors to maintain adherence.

## Context
**Dietary profile:**
{{dietary-profile}}

Include: number of meals per day, dietary restrictions (allergies, intolerances, ethical choices), health goals (weight loss, muscle gain, disease management, general wellness), food preferences (cuisines, ingredients liked/disliked), and daily calorie target.

## Output
Present the meal plan as a markdown table with 7 columns (days of the week) and rows for each meal of the day. For each meal, provide clear descriptions including main components and any necessary preparation notes.

After the table, include a summary explaining:
- How the plan achieves nutritional balance
- How it aligns with the stated health goals
- Key nutritional highlights
```
