# Ad Copy Concept Generator for Multiple Platforms

## 簡介

The Ad Copy Concept Generator for Multiple Platforms is a free AI prompt that creates high-performing advertisement concepts tailored to any advertising platform, product, and target audience. This ad copy prompt for ChatGPT produces 3–5 complete ad concepts, each with an attention-grabbing headline, benefit-focused body copy, and a clear call-to-action. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok by taking your product or service details, target audience profile, unique selling proposition, and desired brand voice, then generating platform-optimized concepts that respect character limits and format requirements. Marketers use it to rapidly prototype campaign variations for Facebook Ads, Google Search, LinkedIn, Instagram, or any platform where conversion-focused copy matters. Reach for this prompt when you need multiple ad angles quickly, want to test different messaging hooks, or need to adapt a campaign concept across channels while maintaining brand consistency. ● Produces complete ad concepts with headline, body, and CTA clearly separated for easy implementation ● Aligns output with platform-specific character limits and creative format requirements ● Incorporates your unique selling proposition and brand voice into every concept variant ● Prioritizes emotional resonance, clarity, and conversion intent in every line of copy ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter generating high-performing ad copy.

## Task
Create 3–5 compelling advertisement concepts optimized for {{advertising-platform}}. Each concept should include:

- **Headline**: An attention-grabbing hook that addresses audience motivation or pain points
- **Body copy**: 2–3 sentences highlighting benefits and the unique selling proposition
- **Call-to-action**: A clear prompt that drives user engagement

## Context
**Product/service**: {{product-service}}

**Target audience**: {{target-audience}}

**Unique selling proposition**: {{usp}}

**Brand voice**: {{brand-voice}}

Align each concept with {{advertising-platform}} character limits, format requirements, and creative best practices. Prioritize clarity, emotional resonance, and conversion intent.

## Output
Present each ad concept as a separate, labeled bullet point with headline, body, and CTA clearly distinguished.
```
