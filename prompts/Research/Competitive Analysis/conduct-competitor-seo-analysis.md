# Competitor SEO Analysis Prompt for Local Businesses

## 簡介

The Competitor SEO Analysis Prompt for Local Businesses is a free AI prompt that delivers a structured competitive intelligence report for any business type and location, helping SEO strategists and business owners understand how their top three direct competitors rank and optimize for search. This competitor SEO analysis prompt for ChatGPT walks through identifying direct competitors, dissecting their keyword targeting, backlink acquisition strategies, on-page optimization techniques, and local SEO efforts including Google Business Profile optimization and citation management. It produces a comparison table with domain authority, ranking keyword counts, organic traffic estimates, backlink totals, and scored assessments of content quality and local SEO performance. Business owners preparing to launch in a new market, SEO consultants auditing competitive landscapes, and marketing teams building data-driven strategies use this prompt to map where competitors succeed and where gaps exist. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only a business type and location to generate actionable intelligence. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable framework for competitive analysis that goes beyond surface-level observation to quantify SEO performance across multiple dimensions. ● Identifies the three most relevant direct competitors for any business type and geographic location ● Analyzes target keywords, backlink profiles, on-page optimization elements, and local SEO tactics for each competitor ● Produces a side-by-side comparison table with domain authority, ranking keywords, organic traffic, backlinks, and quality scores ● Delivers specific, actionable recommendations based on competitive gaps and strengths unique to the business context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business analyst and SEO strategist specializing in competitive research and analysis.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive competitor analysis for {{business-type}} in {{location}}. Identify the top three direct competitors, analyze their SEO strategies in depth, and provide actionable recommendations.

## Analysis Requirements

**For each of the top 3 competitors, provide:**
- Company name and website URL
- Three key strengths
- Detailed SEO strategy analysis:
  - Top 5 target keywords
  - Backlink profile (quality and quantity)
  - On-page optimization (title tags, meta descriptions, content structure)
  - Local SEO efforts (Google Business Profile, citations, reviews)

**Create a comparison table with these metrics:**
- Domain Authority
- Number of ranking keywords
- Organic traffic estimates
- Top organic keywords
- Number of backlinks
- Content quality score (1-10)
- Local SEO score (1-10)
```
