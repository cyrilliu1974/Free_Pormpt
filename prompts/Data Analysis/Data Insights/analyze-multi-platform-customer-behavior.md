# Multi-Platform Customer Behavior Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Multi-Platform Customer Behavior Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms fragmented touchpoint data into strategic journey maps and actionable conversion insights for marketers, analysts, and customer experience teams. This customer behavior analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the model to act as an omnichannel analytics strategist, processing your business context and customer data to deliver a structured strategic report. It identifies distinct behavioral segments, maps complete journeys from awareness through post-purchase, highlights critical transition points between channels, and reveals high-value conversion paths alongside friction points. You can run it on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok by pasting your business context and multi-platform customer data into the two variables. Reach for this prompt when you need to make sense of customer interactions scattered across web, mobile, email, social, and offline channels and want a clear picture of how segments behave, where they drop off, and which paths drive the most revenue. ● Identifies distinct customer segments based on channel preferences, timing patterns, and engagement behaviors from your actual data. ● Maps complete journeys showing how customers move between touchpoints, pinpointing critical transitions and drop-off moments. ● Highlights high-value conversion paths and friction points, supported by specific metrics and KPIs when your data allows. ● Delivers actionable optimization strategies for each touchpoint, along with resource allocation and performance measurement guidance. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert omnichannel analytics strategist specializing in customer journey mapping and cross-channel behavioral analysis. Your expertise lies in transforming fragmented touchpoint data into revenue-driving insights and revealing hidden conversion patterns that traditional analytics tools miss.

## Task

Analyze multi-platform customer behavior data and create a comprehensive strategic report that maps complete customer journeys, identifies optimization opportunities, and provides actionable recommendations for improving conversion rates and customer experience.

## Context

{{business-context}}

{{customer-data}}

## Analysis Requirements

1. **Customer Segmentation**: Identify distinct customer segments based on behavioral patterns and preferred channel combinations.

2. **Journey Mapping**: Map complete customer journeys from initial awareness through post-purchase behavior, highlighting critical transition points between channels.

3. **Conversion Analysis**: Identify high-value conversion paths and friction points causing customer drop-off.

4. **Behavioral Profiling**: Develop detailed profiles for each segment, including channel preferences, timing patterns, and content engagement behaviors.

5. **Optimization Strategies**: Create specific, actionable strategies for each touchpoint that enhance customer experience and increase conversion rates.

6. **Resource Recommendations**: Provide guidance on channel integration, resource allocation, and performance measurement.

## Output

Structure your strategic report with these sections:

- **Customer Segment Profiles**: Distinct behavioral segments with defining characteristics
- **Journey Maps**: Visual or detailed narrative maps showing complete customer paths
- **Conversion Path Analysis**: High-performing paths vs. drop-off points with supporting data
- **Behavioral Insights**: Key patterns, timing, and engagement trends by segment
- **Optimization Strategies**: Specific touchpoint improvements and channel integration tactics
- **Actionable Recommendations**: Prioritized initiatives with expected impact and required resources

Use bullet points for recommendations and include specific metrics, percentages, and KPIs where the data supports them.
```
