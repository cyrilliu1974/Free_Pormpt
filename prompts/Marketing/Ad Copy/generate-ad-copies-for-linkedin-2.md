# LinkedIn Ad Copy Generator for B2B Campaigns

## 簡介

The LinkedIn Ad Copy Generator for B2B Campaigns is a free AI prompt that creates complete LinkedIn advertising campaigns with multiple variations tailored for professional and B2B audiences. This LinkedIn ad copy prompt for ChatGPT produces five distinct headline variations (70 characters or fewer), five body copy options (150 characters or fewer), and three call-to-action phrases ready for LinkedIn's ad platform. It works by analyzing your product or service description, target audience, and unique selling points to generate ad variations that take different strategic angles - problem/solution framing, social proof, urgency, aspiration, or quantified outcomes. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, using dependency grammar principles to anchor each sentence on strong value verbs and outcome nouns. Marketers use it to rapidly test different messaging approaches for LinkedIn campaigns, demand generation teams rely on it for consistent ad creative at scale, and agencies deploy it to accelerate client campaign development. ● Creates 5 headline variations under 70 characters, each highlighting different value propositions and angles to identify top performers ● Generates 5 body copy options under 150 characters that address professional pain points and lead with outcomes rather than features ● Provides 3 distinct call-to-action phrases designed as button-ready text that drives qualified traffic to landing pages ● Applies dependency grammar structure to ensure clarity and impact in the constrained character limits of LinkedIn's ad formats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert LinkedIn ad copywriter specializing in B2B and professional-audience campaigns.

## Task
Create a complete LinkedIn ad campaign with multiple variations for headlines, body copy, and calls-to-action that promote the product or service and drive qualified traffic.

## Context
LinkedIn ads perform best when they:
- Address specific professional pain points and aspirations
- Lead with value and outcomes rather than features
- Use concise, scannable formatting (headlines ≤ 70 chars, body ≤ 150 chars)
- Include a clear, actionable CTA

Analyze the offering details to identify:
- Core benefits and transformation for the target audience
- Differentiators that matter to decision-makers
- Emotional and rational triggers relevant to the professional context

Structure each variation using dependency grammar principles: anchor each sentence on the strongest value verb or outcome noun, with modifiers that clarify rather than dilute.

## Input
**Offering:** {{product-service-description}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Unique Selling Points:** {{unique-selling-points}}

## Output
Deliver your ad copy in three sections:

**Headlines** (5 variations, ≤70 characters each)  
**Body Copy** (5 variations, ≤150 characters each)  
**Call-to-Action** (3 variations, button-ready phrases)

Each variation should take a different angle—problem/solution, social proof, urgency, aspiration, or quantified outcome—while maintaining brand consistency.
```
