# Beverage Photography Prompt for Product Shoots

## 簡介

The Beverage Photography Prompt for Product Shoots is a free AI prompt that generates close-up commercial product photographs of beverages in glass bottles with professional styling and lighting for brands, e-commerce stores, and marketers. This beverage photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces high-resolution product images with shallow depth of field, soft diffused lighting, and clean white surfaces that mirror studio-quality commercial photography. It positions your drink in a glass bottle against customizable backgrounds, scatters fresh ingredients around the composition to reinforce naturalness, and emphasizes vivid drink colors through neutral backdrops. Use it to create marketing visuals for social media, packaging mockups, e-commerce listings, or client pitch decks without a physical photoshoot. Reach for this prompt when you need professional beverage product photography but lack access to a studio, photographer, or physical samples. ● Specify the beverage description (flavor, color, texture) and background style to match your brand aesthetic and product positioning. ● Produces images with shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the bottle, and soft diffused lighting that replicates studio conditions. ● Scatters fresh ingredients around the bottle to build a colorful, natural composition that communicates freshness and quality. ● Outputs in 4:5 aspect ratio suited for Instagram, Pinterest, e-commerce thumbnails, and mobile-first marketing channels. ## Prompt

```
Close-up product photograph of {{smoothie-description}} in a glass bottle, positioned against {{background}}; neutral backdrop emphasizes vivid drink color; fresh ingredients scattered around for colorful composition highlighting naturalness; soft diffused lighting, clean white surface, shallow depth of field with sharp focus on bottle, high resolution commercial photography --ar 4:5
```
