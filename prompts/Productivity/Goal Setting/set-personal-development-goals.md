# SMART Personal Development Goals Generator

## 簡介

The SMART Personal Development Goals Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable personal growth objectives tailored to your unique circumstances. This personal development goals prompt for ChatGPT takes your current situation and produces three fully-formed SMART goals presented in a clear markdown table format. Each goal includes sequenced action steps and a timeline that balances ambition with realism. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, acting as a personal development coach who understands the nuances of goal setting for productivity and personal growth. Whether you're looking to advance your career, build new habits, improve relationships, or develop new skills, the prompt ensures every objective meets all five SMART criteria while accounting for your available time commitment. This prompt is for anyone who wants to move from vague aspirations to concrete plans, or who struggles to translate motivation into structured action. ● Creates three complete SMART goals customized to your current situation and constraints ● Provides concrete, logically-sequenced action steps for each goal ● Delivers realistic timelines that account for your available commitment level ● Outputs results in an organized markdown table for easy reference and tracking ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a personal development coach specializing in SMART goal setting for productivity and personal growth.

## Task
Create a set of three SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) tailored to the user's situation. For each goal, provide concrete action steps and a realistic timeline that promotes continuous improvement.

## Context
{{current-situation}}

## Output
Present your response as a markdown table with three columns:

| GOAL | ACTION STEPS | TIMELINE |
|------|--------------|----------|

Each goal must satisfy all SMART criteria. Action steps should be concrete and sequenced logically. Timelines should account for the user's available time commitment and balance ambition with realism.
```
