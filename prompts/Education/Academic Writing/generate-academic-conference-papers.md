# Academic Conference Paper Writer Prompt

## 簡介

The Academic Conference Paper Writer Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates complete, submission-ready conference papers for researchers and academics preparing presentations. This conference paper prompt for ChatGPT takes a research brief and produces a fully structured document including title, abstract, introduction, methodology, findings, discussion, implications, and references - all formatted for a 15-minute conference slot. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to ensure clarity and applies rigorous academic structure across every section. You supply your research brief, and the AI outputs a complete paper that articulates your research question, describes your methodology with emphasis on rigor, presents findings logically, and discusses both theoretical and practical implications. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it ideal for researchers who need to transform research data into a polished conference submission quickly. Reach for this prompt when you have completed research that needs to be packaged for an academic conference, when you're facing tight submission deadlines, or when you want to ensure your paper follows best-practice academic structure. ● Produces every required section - title, abstract, introduction, research question, methodology, findings, discussion, implications, conclusion, and references - in one coherent document ● Emphasizes methodological rigor and suitability while maintaining the concise language needed for time-limited conference presentations ● Applies dependency grammar framework throughout to improve readability and logical flow ● Formats citations and references according to the style specified in your research brief, ensuring submission compliance ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic paper writer with extensive knowledge across research disciplines, skilled in crafting concise, impactful conference papers suitable for 15-minute presentations.

## Task
Write a complete conference paper that:
- Clearly articulates a research question and its significance
- Describes methodology with emphasis on suitability and rigor
- Presents key findings in a logical, easy-to-follow manner
- Discusses implications for both theory and practice
- Applies dependency grammar framework throughout
- Uses clear, concise language with appropriate academic structure
- Includes properly formatted citations and references

## Context
{{research-brief}}

## Output
Deliver a structured conference paper with:

**Title:** [Compelling, precise title]

**Abstract:** Summary of research question, methodology, key findings, and implications (150-250 words)

**Introduction:** Context and significance of the research question in the field

**Research Question:** Clear statement of the central inquiry

**Methodology:** Detailed description emphasizing rigor and appropriateness

**Findings:** Key results presented logically with supporting data

**Discussion:** Analysis connecting findings to existing literature

**Implications:**
- Theoretical: Contributions to academic understanding
- Practical: Applications for practitioners

**Conclusion:** Summary of main points and future research directions

**References:** All cited sources in the specified format

Ensure the complete paper adheres to the word limit and citation style provided in the research brief.
```
