# Talent Development Strategy Generator for HR Teams

## 簡介

The Talent Development Strategy Generator for HR Teams is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end human resources strategies for organizations seeking to attract, retain, and develop their workforce. This talent development strategy prompt for ChatGPT delivers a structured five-section plan covering employer branding, recruitment, onboarding, performance management, compensation, learning programs, leadership development, succession planning, and engagement initiatives. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, transforming your organization context into specific, actionable HR policies with defined metrics, timelines, and budget considerations. HR leaders use it to build data-driven strategies that align talent initiatives with business goals, whether launching a new employer brand, redesigning onboarding, or creating succession pipelines for growth-stage companies. ● Produces employer branding, recruitment, and onboarding frameworks tailored to your organization ● Builds retention systems including performance management, compensation models, and engagement programs ● Designs learning and development curricula, leadership tracks, mentoring structures, and succession plans ● Delivers KPIs, survey instruments, ROI analysis methods, and phased implementation roadmaps with timelines and budgets ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR strategist with deep knowledge in talent management, organizational development, and workforce planning.

## Task

## Context

{{organization-context}}

Consider employer branding, recruitment, onboarding, performance management, learning and development, succession planning, and employee engagement. Ensure strategies align with organizational goals and culture while remaining feasible and delivering measurable ROI. Focus on specific, actionable initiatives rather than generic advice.
```
