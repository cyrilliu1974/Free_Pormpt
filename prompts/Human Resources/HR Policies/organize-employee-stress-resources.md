# Employee Stress Management Resource Guide Builder

## 簡介

The Employee Stress Management Resource Guide Builder is a free AI prompt that creates customized stress management resource guides for HR professionals, managers, and organizational well-being coordinators. This stress management prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your organizational context and primary stress triggers as input, then producing a structured guide with evidence-based techniques, workplace support services, mental health resources, and supplementary tools. It organizes resources into clearly labeled sections with names, descriptions, benefits, and concrete access details. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it practical for HR teams rolling out well-being initiatives, managers supporting team mental health, or benefits coordinators compiling employee assistance materials. Reach for this prompt when you need to consolidate scattered stress management information into a single, accessible document tailored to your workforce's specific challenges - whether that's remote work isolation, high-pressure deadlines, shift work fatigue, or organizational change. ● Produces six organized sections: introduction, evidence-based techniques, workplace support services, mental health resources, additional resources, and conclusion. ● Ensures all recommendations are credible, established resources - no experimental or unproven methods. ● Addresses diverse accessibility needs and individual preferences within your workforce. ● Tailors every technique and service recommendation to your organization's specific stress triggers and context. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert in organizational well-being and employee support with deep knowledge of evidence-based stress management techniques and workplace mental health resources.

## Task

Create a comprehensive stress management resource guide tailored to the provided organizational context. The guide should help employees and team members access appropriate support services and proven stress reduction strategies.

## Context

**Organization & Population:**
{{organizational-context}}

**Primary Stress Triggers:**
{{stress-triggers}}

## Output

Structure your response as follows:

### 1. Introduction
Briefly explain the purpose and importance of these stress management resources for the described workforce.

### 2. Stress Management Techniques
Provide 3 evidence-based techniques, each with:
- Name
- Description
- Benefits
- Resources (books, apps, websites)

Ensure techniques address the identified stress triggers and suit different individual preferences.

### 3. Support Services
List 3 workplace or community support services, each with:
- Name
- Description
- Access details (contact information, location, eligibility)

### 4. Mental Health Resources
Provide 3 credible mental health resources, each with:
- Name
- Description
- Access details

### 5. Additional Resources
List 2 supplementary resources that support stress management, each with:
- Name
- Description
- Access details

### 6. Conclusion
Summarize the importance of utilizing these resources for effective stress management and encourage proactive engagement.

**Requirements:**
- Focus only on well-established, credible resources
- Address diverse accessibility needs and preferences
- Use clear headings and bullet points for easy navigation
- Tailor all recommendations to the organizational context and stress triggers provided
```
