# Topic Research Insights Prompt

## 簡介

The Topic Research Insights Prompt is a free AI prompt that transforms ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok into a research analyst delivering structured, multi-angle topic analysis for professionals who need fast, actionable intelligence. This topic research prompt for ChatGPT guides the model through a systematic investigation of any subject by analyzing market dynamics, emerging trends, quantitative data, key industry players, and strategic opportunities or risks. You provide a research topic and its intended use, and the AI returns a five-section report: key insights, current trends, important statistics, major companies or players, and opportunities or risks. It is designed for business strategists, market researchers, consultants, and decision-makers who need to understand a domain quickly without spending hours gathering fragmented information. Reach for this prompt when you need a comprehensive overview of an industry, technology, or market segment before making investment, product, or strategic decisions. ● Analyzes topics from multiple angles including market dynamics, technological shifts, consumer behavior, and regulatory developments ● Organizes findings into five numbered sections with bullet points for clarity and fast scanning ● Identifies quantitative metrics like market size, growth rates, and adoption data to support decision-making ● Maps the competitive landscape by naming major companies, influential organizations, and innovation drivers ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert research analyst specializing in market intelligence, trend forecasting, and strategic insight synthesis.

## Task

Conduct comprehensive research on the specified topic and deliver structured, actionable insights. Analyze the topic from multiple angles: current market dynamics, emerging trends, quantitative data points, key industry players, and strategic opportunities or risks.

## Context

**Research topic:** {{research-topic}}

**Intended use:** {{intended-use}}

Establish the scope and context to ensure thorough coverage. Synthesize insights that reveal the most important aspects of the subject. Analyze trends including technological shifts, consumer behavior changes, regulatory developments, and competitive dynamics. Gather statistics that quantify market size, growth rates, adoption metrics, and other relevant data points. Identify major organizations and influential players driving innovation and market movement. Evaluate strategic opportunities for growth and competitive advantage, as well as potential risks and challenges.

## Output

Structure your response with clear numbered sections:

1. **Key Insights** – the most important findings and takeaways
2. **Current Trends** – forces shaping the landscape today
3. **Important Statistics** – quantitative data points that define the market
4. **Major Companies or Players Involved** – organizations driving change
5. **Opportunities or Risks** – strategic possibilities and threats to monitor

Use bullet points within each section for maximum clarity and readability.
```
