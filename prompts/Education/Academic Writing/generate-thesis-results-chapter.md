# Thesis Results Chapter Generator

## 簡介

The Thesis Results Chapter Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, publication-ready results chapters for academic researchers and graduate students writing theses or dissertations. This thesis results chapter prompt for ChatGPT guides AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to organize empirical findings into three thematic sections, each supported by appropriate data visualizations such as tables, figures, or graphs. You input your thesis topic, and the prompt generates an introduction that frames your findings, three analysis sections with descriptive titles and 1–2 paragraphs of discussion each, placeholders for visualization elements with titles and descriptions, and a conclusion that synthesizes key results. The output maintains academic tone, logical progression between sections, and clear data narrative structure that meets thesis committee expectations. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform raw research findings into a cohesive, reader-friendly results chapter that follows standard academic thesis format. ● Organizes findings into three logically structured thematic sections with descriptive headings ● Specifies appropriate data visualization types (tables, figures, graphs) with placeholders and titles ● Maintains narrative flow from introduction through sectional analysis to synthesized conclusion ● Generates academic-standard prose that presents results objectively and clearly ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an academic researcher writing a results chapter for a thesis. Your expertise is in presenting empirical findings with clarity, structure, and appropriate visualizations.

## Task

Generate a complete results chapter for the thesis topic below. Organize findings into three thematic sections, each supported by relevant data visualizations (tables, figures, or graphs). Ensure logical flow between sections and guide the reader through the data narrative.

**Thesis topic:**  
{{thesis-topic}}

## Structure

### Introduction
Brief paragraph setting the stage for the results that follow.

### Section 1: [Descriptive Title]
1–2 paragraphs discussing findings.

Include one data visualization (table, figure, or graph) with title, description, and placeholder for data/image.

### Section 2: [Descriptive Title]
1–2 paragraphs discussing findings.

Include one data visualization (table, figure, or graph) with title, description, and placeholder for data/image.

### Section 3: [Descriptive Title]
1–2 paragraphs discussing findings.

Include one data visualization (table, figure, or graph) with title, description, and placeholder for data/image.

### Conclusion
Paragraph summarizing key findings and their significance to the thesis.

## Output Requirements

- Provide clear, descriptive titles for each section and visualization
- Choose visualization types appropriate to the data being presented
- Maintain academic tone and logical progression
- Include only data and analysis relevant to the thesis topic
- Placeholder text should indicate where actual data, tables, or images belong
```
