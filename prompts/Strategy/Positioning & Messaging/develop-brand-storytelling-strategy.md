# Brand Storytelling Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Brand Storytelling Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete narrative framework and actionable content plan for brands seeking to communicate their identity, values, and mission consistently across channels. This brand storytelling prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a core narrative, defining 3-5 content pillars, and mapping channel-specific tactics for blog posts, social media platforms, and email newsletters. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and outputs a structured strategy document complete with implementation timelines and success metrics. Marketing teams use it to translate brand positioning into daily content, ensuring every post, article, and email reinforces the same story. Agencies rely on it to deliver cohesive storytelling frameworks to clients quickly. Reach for this prompt when you need to move from brand guidelines to executable content that connects with audiences emotionally and drives loyalty. ● Builds a core narrative framework that threads through all brand communications and touchpoints ● Defines 3-5 content pillars with thematic focus areas that support the central brand story ● Maps channel-specific content plans including blog topics, social media cadence, and email newsletter arcs ● Provides tone, voice, and messaging principles to maintain narrative coherence across every platform ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist developing a comprehensive storytelling strategy and multi-channel content plan.

## Task
Create a captivating brand narrative and actionable content plan that communicates the brand's identity, values, and mission to its target audience. Deliver a strategy that includes:

- **Core narrative framework**: The central brand story that threads through all communications
- **Content pillars**: 3-5 thematic pillars that support the narrative
- **Channel-specific content plan**:
  - Blog posts (topics, frequency, storytelling angles)
  - Social media content (platform-specific approaches, post types, cadence)
  - Email newsletters (series structure, narrative arc, calls-to-action)
- **Consistency guidelines**: Tone, voice, and messaging principles to maintain coherence across channels

## Context
{{brand-overview}}

Ensure the storytelling strategy leverages the brand's unique selling proposition and resonates with audience preferences. All content recommendations should reinforce the core narrative and drive audience connection.

## Output
Provide a structured strategy document with clear headings and subheadings:

1. **Brand Narrative Summary**
2. **Content Pillars**
3. **Multi-Channel Content Plan** (subsections for blog, social media, email)
4. **Implementation Timeline**
5. **Success Metrics**
```
