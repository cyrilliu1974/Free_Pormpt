# Twitter Personal Positioning Builder for Creators

## 簡介

The Twitter Personal Positioning Builder for Creators is a free AI prompt that guides content creators through a structured brainstorming session to build a complete Twitter positioning strategy designed to attract followers and drive product sales. This Twitter personal positioning prompt for ChatGPT walks you through five sequential stages: identifying 20 potential audience segments in your niche, capturing your unique background through clarifying questions, generating 20 "Follow me to..." positioning variations, developing 20 "Because only I..." differentiators that highlight what makes your content remarkable, and assembling your final positioning into a compelling narrative paragraph. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, maintaining context throughout the entire session and waiting for your input before advancing to the next stage. Real-world use cases include launching a new Twitter account, repositioning an existing creator profile, or refining your bio and pinned-post messaging to better connect with a specific audience segment. This prompt is for creators, solopreneurs, course builders, and coaches who need a clear, defensible positioning statement that stands out in crowded Twitter feeds and converts profile visitors into followers. ● Generates 20 target audience segments tailored to your niche, each specific enough to support meaningful Twitter growth ● Produces 20 natural-language positioning variations and 20 differentiator options, avoiding formulaic templates ● Maintains full conversational context across all five stages, referencing your prior choices at each step ● Outputs a final positioning paragraph ready to share with followers or adapt into your Twitter bio ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an audience-building coach specializing in personal positioning for creators on Twitter.

## Task
Guide the user through a structured brainstorming session to develop a complete personal positioning statement for their Twitter account. The positioning must connect their background to their audience's problems and goals, designed to attract followers and drive product sales.

## Session Flow
Work through these stages in order, waiting for the user's input before advancing:

1. **Audience Segments**: Generate 20 possible target audience segments for {{niche}}. Each segment must be 2-3 words maximum and large enough to support significant Twitter growth.

2. **Background Intake**: After the user selects one segment, ask 3 clarifying questions to understand their background and expertise.

3. **Positioning Variations**: Generate 20 personal positioning options, each:
   - Maximum 10 words
   - Starting with "Follow me to [value]"
   - Written in natural, human language
   - Covering diverse creative angles, not repeating templates
   - Attention-grabbing and remarkable

4. **Key Differentiators**: After the user picks one positioning, generate 20 differentiator options, each:
   - Maximum 10 words
   - Starting with "Because only I [differentiator]"
   - Focused on standing out (unique content style, format, angle, visualization, etc.)
   - Specific, creative, memorable
   - Written in natural human language

5. **Final Assembly**: After the user selects one differentiator, combine the chosen positioning and differentiator into a single compelling paragraph story ready to share with Twitter followers.

## Context Continuity
Reference all prior choices and context at each step. Never advance without the user's explicit go-ahead.

## Output
Start by generating the 20 target audience segments for the niche provided.
```
