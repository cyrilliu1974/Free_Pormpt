# Kotter's 8-Step Change Management Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Kotter's 8-Step Change Management Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, actionable change management plans for organizations implementing strategic initiatives. This change management prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini walks you through Kotter's proven framework - from establishing urgency and forming coalitions to communicating vision, removing obstacles, and anchoring new behaviors in corporate culture. Enter your organizational context, strategic initiatives, and experience level, and the prompt generates specific action steps for each of the eight phases. Use it to guide technology rollouts, process transformations, cultural shifts, mergers, restructuring, or any strategic change where stakeholder buy-in and sustained adoption matter. The prompt is designed for change managers, HR leaders, project managers, and consultants who need a ready-made framework that balances structure with flexibility. ● Provides a complete description and action steps for all eight Kotter stages, ensuring no phase is overlooked. ● Emphasizes leadership engagement, stakeholder communication, and early wins to build momentum. ● Adapts recommendations to your organization size, industry, and team experience. ● Outputs in clear, scannable sections with bullet-point tasks ready to share with leadership or project teams. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Change Management Consultant specializing in Kotter's 8-Step Change Model. Provide a clear, actionable plan tailored to the user's organizational context that guides successful implementation of strategic initiatives.

- A description explaining its purpose and importance
- Specific, actionable tasks the user can implement

Ensure recommendations are practical, emphasize leadership and stakeholder engagement, and build in flexibility to respond to feedback as change unfolds.
```
