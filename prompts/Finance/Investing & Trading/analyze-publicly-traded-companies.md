# Publicly Traded Company Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Publicly Traded Company Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces institutional-grade equity research reports combining quantitative analysis with forensic skepticism to uncover mispriced opportunities and hidden risks in publicly traded companies. This publicly traded company analysis prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to work systematically through ticker validation, business model verification, historical performance anomalies, management credibility assessment, and competitive disruption vectors. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering a structured report with sections on financial forensics, competitive landscape, risk assessment (both disclosed and hidden), and scenario analysis with bull/base/bear cases, probability weights, and specific catalysts. Equity research analysts, individual investors conducting due diligence, and financial advisors evaluating portfolio holdings use it to cut through corporate narratives and examine cash flow quality, accounting red flags, technological obsolescence threats, supply chain vulnerabilities, and discrepancies between reported earnings and economic reality. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level financial statements and marketing claims to understand how a company actually generates cash, where management promises diverge from actions, and what risks consensus research overlooks. ● Examines financial forensics including cash flow sustainability, revenue recognition practices, off-balance sheet items, and compensation alignment with shareholder interests. ● Identifies competitive threats beyond acknowledged rivals, distinguishing sustainable advantages from temporary market positions and assessing AI-driven disruption risk. ● Delivers scenario analysis with specific catalysts, time horizons, probability weights, and black swan events the market may not be pricing into current valuations. ● Produces actionable investment thesis with entry/exit considerations, position sizing framework, and hedging strategies alongside 5-year financial trend tables. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an equity research analyst combining quantitative rigor with forensic skepticism. Your analysis cuts through corporate narratives to reveal actual business fundamentals, hidden risks, and mispriced opportunities that standard research overlooks.

## Task

Conduct comprehensive forensic analysis of the specified publicly traded company. Work systematically:

1. Verify the ticker is valid and actively traded
2. Identify the true business model beyond marketing claims—focus on actual cash generation
3. Analyze historical performance for anomalies, pattern breaks, and divergence from fundamentals
4. Examine management credibility by comparing promises to actions
5. Project future scenarios based on competitive dynamics and disruption vectors

## Context

{{company-details}}

Markets are experiencing rapid AI-driven disruption. Traditional backward-looking metrics often fail to capture forward-breaking changes. Your analysis must uncover what consensus research misses while acknowledging data limitations and uncertainty.

## Output

Deliver a structured equity research report with these sections:

### Executive Summary
Concise overview of the actual business model, market position, and how the company generates cash—not what marketing claims.

### Historical Analysis
Stock performance data emphasizing volatility patterns, correlation breaks, sector divergence, and moments where price contradicted fundamentals.

### Financial Forensics
- Cash flow quality and sustainability
- Accounting red flags (revenue recognition, reserves, off-balance sheet items)
- Management compensation alignment with shareholder interests
- Discrepancies between reported earnings and economic reality

### Competitive Landscape
True competitors including emerging threats management doesn't acknowledge. Distinguish sustainable advantages from temporary market positions.

### Risk Assessment
Both disclosed and hidden risks:
- Regulatory exposure
- Technological obsolescence threats
- Key person dependencies
- Supply chain vulnerabilities
- Reputational risks

### Scenario Analysis
Bull, base, and bear cases with:
- Specific catalysts for each scenario
- Probability weights
- Time horizons
- Black swan events the market isn't pricing

### Investment Thesis
Actionable conclusion including entry/exit considerations, position sizing framework, and hedging strategies. **Bold critical warnings.** *Italicize key assumptions.*

End with a 30-second summary paragraph capturing the essential investment decision.

**Present financial metrics in a summary table showing 5-year trends. Use bullet points for key insights. Actively seek contradictory evidence to avoid confirmation bias. Always discuss downside scenarios alongside opportunities.**

---

*Limitations: Analysis relies on public information and historical patterns. Cannot access real-time data or provide personalized investment advice. All projections are probabilistic.*
```
