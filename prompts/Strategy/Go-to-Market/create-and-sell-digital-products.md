# Digital Product Launch Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Digital Product Launch Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive go-to-market plans for solopreneurs building and selling digital products. This digital product launch prompt for ChatGPT guides you through five critical phases: product development and validation, pre-launch audience building, platform-specific launch tactics, post-launch momentum strategies, and scaling opportunities. It produces a structured, numbered roadmap with actionable steps tailored to your specific product, target audience, and available resources (budget, time, and expertise). The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is built for solo creators who need a proven framework for launching courses, templates, tools, or other digital offerings on platforms like ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter. Reach for this prompt when you're moving from idea to market and need a clear, sequenced plan that covers market research, email list growth, launch-day tactics, and passive income expansion. ● Market validation and product refinement steps grounded in audience research ● Pre-launch tactics for building email lists and distributing free resources to create anticipation ● Platform-specific launch playbooks for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and Twitter with timing and messaging guidance ● Post-launch activities to maintain sales momentum, optimize conversions, and gather customer feedback ● Scaling roadmap with product-line extensions and additional passive income streams ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert digital product strategist specializing in solopreneur launches and passive income generation.

## Task
Create a comprehensive step-by-step strategy for launching a digital product to market, covering the full lifecycle from conception through post-launch growth.

## Context
Digital product: {{product-description}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Available resources: {{budget-time-expertise}}

The strategy should leverage modern launch platforms (ProductHunt, HackerNews, Twitter) and include pre-launch audience building through free resources and email list growth.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list with clear section headings covering:

1. **Product Development Process**
   - Market research and validation methods
   - Product ideation and refinement
   - Creation workflow and milestones

2. **Pre-Launch Marketing Strategy**
   - Techniques for building anticipation
   - Free resource creation and distribution tactics
   - Email list growth strategies

3. **Launch Plan**
   - Optimal timing and sequencing
   - Platform-specific tactics (ProductHunt, HackerNews, Twitter)
   - Promotional messaging and outreach

4. **Post-Launch Activities**
   - Momentum maintenance techniques
   - Sales optimization strategies
   - Customer feedback loops

5. **Scaling and Additional Income Streams**
   - Business expansion opportunities
   - Product line extension ideas
   - Passive income maximization

Use bullet points for sub-steps and tactical details under each numbered section.
```
