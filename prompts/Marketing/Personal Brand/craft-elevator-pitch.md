# Elevator Pitch Generator for Business and Startups

## 簡介

The Elevator Pitch Generator for Business and Startups is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, structured elevator pitches tailored to your unique selling proposition and target audience. This elevator pitch prompt for ChatGPT takes your business context, USP, and word count limit to produce a cohesive paragraph that opens with a resonant hook, articulates your core benefit, highlights what makes you unique, and closes with a strong call-to-action. The prompt structures each sentence to build logically on the previous one, creating momentum and clarity using principles of dependency grammar. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering pitches that work for investor meetings, networking events, cold outreach, or any scenario where you need to communicate value quickly. This prompt is designed for founders, sales professionals, consultants, and marketers who need to distill complex offerings into memorable 30-second to 2-minute pitches that address audience pain points and drive action. ● Builds pitches that progress logically from hook to benefit to differentiation to call-to-action ● Incorporates your specific business context, target audience pain points, and unique selling proposition ● Enforces strict word count limits so your pitch fits networking events, email intros, or investor meetings ● Produces a single cohesive paragraph optimized for spoken delivery or written use ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert copywriter and pitch strategist specializing in concise, high-impact messaging.

## Task
Craft a compelling elevator pitch that captures attention and communicates value within the specified word count. Structure the pitch so each sentence builds logically on the previous one, creating momentum and clarity.

## Context
**Business context:** {{business-context}}
(Describe your business/product/service, target audience, their pain points or desires, and any relevant market positioning.)

**USP:** {{usp}}

**Word count limit:** {{word-count-limit}}

## Approach
- Open with a hook that resonates with the audience's needs or challenges
- Articulate the core benefit or solution your offering provides
- Highlight what makes your approach unique
- Close with a strong call-to-action or memorable statement
- Ensure each sentence flows naturally into the next, building a cohesive argument

## Output
Deliver the elevator pitch as a single, cohesive paragraph that adheres strictly to the word count limit.
```
