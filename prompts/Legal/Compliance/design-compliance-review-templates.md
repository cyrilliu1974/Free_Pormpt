# COSO Compliance Review Template Generator

## 簡介

The COSO Compliance Review Template Generator is a free AI prompt that creates structured compliance review templates aligned with the COSO Internal Control Framework for auditors, compliance officers, and internal control specialists. This compliance template prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces complete documentation packages covering all five COSO components: control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, and monitoring activities. Each template includes assessment criteria, evidence standards, testing procedures, sign-off protocols, and remediation tracking mechanisms designed for organizations under regulatory scrutiny. Reach for this prompt when you need consistent, auditor-ready compliance documentation that balances thoroughness with operational efficiency. ● Produces assessment templates for all five COSO components with evaluation procedures, documentation requirements, and accountability assignments. ● Generates operational tools including standardized checklists, risk prioritization matrices, and control testing procedures with clear pass/fail criteria. ● Creates governance mechanisms such as deficiency escalation procedures, remediation tracking with ownership assignments, and continuous monitoring protocols. ● Delivers templates as ready-to-implement documents with structured formats, tables, and step-by-step procedures for immediate practical application. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert compliance auditor and internal controls specialist with deep expertise in implementing COSO frameworks across regulated organizations.

## Task
Create comprehensive compliance review templates structured around the COSO Internal Control Framework. The templates must enable consistent documentation, evaluation flow, and regulatory adherence across all five COSO components: control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, and monitoring activities.

## Context
**Organization and compliance scope:**
{{compliance-context}}

The templates will be used in an environment where regulatory scrutiny is high, audit failures carry significant consequences, and compliance teams must balance thoroughness with operational efficiency. Multiple stakeholders require different levels of detail and assurance. The deliverables must ensure traceability, accountability, and withstand regulatory examination.

## Output
Provide comprehensive template documents that include:

**For each COSO component:**
- Assessment criteria and evaluation procedures
- Documentation requirements and evidence standards
- Sign-off protocols and accountability assignments

**Operational tools:**
- Standardized checklists that teams can consistently apply across audit cycles
- Risk matrices for prioritization and tracking
- Control testing procedures with clear pass/fail criteria

**Governance mechanisms:**
- Escalation procedures for identified deficiencies
- Remediation tracking mechanisms with ownership and timelines
- Continuous monitoring protocols that maintain compliance between formal review periods

Structure each template as ready-to-implement documents with clear sections, standardized forms, and step-by-step procedures. Use tables, checklists, and structured formats that enable immediate practical application.
```
