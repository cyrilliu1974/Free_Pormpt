# Market Entry Research Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Market Entry Research Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces data-driven market entry analysis for businesses evaluating expansion into new geographic or product markets. This market entry research prompt for ChatGPT walks through a structured analysis framework covering market size and growth, competitive landscape, regulatory requirements, cultural considerations, and revenue projections. You specify your product or service and target markets as variables, and the prompt generates a complete strategic report citing data sources throughout. Business strategists, expansion teams, and consultants use it to assess market potential and develop entry strategies for international or domestic expansion. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering board-ready analysis that includes opportunity assessment, risk identification, and actionable recommendations. Reach for this prompt when evaluating new markets, preparing expansion business cases, or conducting due diligence on geographic growth opportunities. ● Analyzes market size, growth rates, key segments, and emerging trends for each target market ● Evaluates competitive landscape with major players, market share distribution, and positioning insights ● Assesses regulatory environment, compliance requirements, and barriers to entry ● Provides cultural and consumer behavior analysis tailored to local market preferences ● Delivers strategic entry recommendations, success factors, risk mitigation strategies, and actionable next steps ● Requires citation of all data sources to ensure credibility and enable verification ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert business strategist specializing in market entry and international expansion, with access to comprehensive market data and business intelligence.

## Task

Conduct a thorough market entry analysis for the specified product or service across the target markets. Provide data-driven insights and strategic recommendations for successful market entry and growth.

## Context

**Product/Service:**
{{product-service}}

**Target Markets:**
{{target-markets}}

## Analysis Framework

For each target market, analyze:

- **Market Overview** – size, growth rate, key segments
- **Key Trends** – emerging patterns and shifts
- **Competitive Landscape** – major players, market share, positioning
- **Regulatory Environment** – compliance requirements, barriers to entry
- **Cultural Considerations** – consumer behavior, local preferences, business practices
- **Market Potential** – opportunity assessment and revenue projections
- **Data Sources** – cite all sources used

## Output

Structure your analysis as follows:

### Product/Service Overview
[Brief summary]

### Market Analysis
[Provide comprehensive analysis for each target market using the framework above]

### Market Entry Strategy
[Recommended approach tailored to the product/service and markets]

### Key Success Factors
[Critical elements for success]

### Potential Risks and Challenges
[Identification and mitigation strategies]

### Final Recommendations
[Actionable next steps for market entry and growth]

Cite all data sources throughout your analysis.
```
