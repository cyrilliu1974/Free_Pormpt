# Basketball Shoe Photography Prompt for AI Image Models

## 簡介

The Basketball Shoe Photography Prompt for AI Image Models is a free AI prompt that generates cinematic, ground-level product photography of basketball shoes in authentic athletic settings. This basketball shoe photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 3×1 grid of hyper-realistic frames - each isolating the footwear with shallow depth of field, natural court lighting, and professional sports editorial styling. You specify the shoe model in the {{basketball-shoes}} variable, and the prompt renders three distinct compositions: an in-game action shot capturing texture and grip while the player shields the ball, an intimate lacing close-up showing material detail and hand positioning, and a resting frame with the athlete seated on the court. Product photographers, sneaker brands, e-commerce teams, and sports marketers reach for it when they need scroll-stopping shoe visuals that combine commercial focus with documentary realism. ● Produces ground-level, ultra-close shots with sharp focus on shoe materials - visible stitching, leather grain, lace tension, outsole tread, and court dust. ● Frames in-game defensive action in mid-background blur while keeping footwear crisp, balancing dynamic energy with product clarity. ● Captures pre-game and post-play moments - lacing rituals and seated rest poses - that convey authentic athletic storytelling. ● Delivers consistent natural indoor court lighting, cinematic contrast, and realistic shadows across all three frames for cohesive editorial use. ## Prompt

```
Generate a 3×1 grid (3:4 aspect ratio per frame) in hyper-realistic sports photography style. Every frame features {{basketball-shoes}} as the primary subject, worn by a young Black man with an athletic build in black shorts. Natural indoor basketball court lighting, cinematic contrast, realistic shadows. Shallow depth of field: shoes in sharp focus, background softly blurred.

**Frame 1 – In-Game Action**
Ground-level, ultra-close shot. Camera positioned extremely low on the court, focusing sharply on the shoe: visible leather texture, stitching, creases, lace tension, outsole grip, slight court dust. Mid-background, slightly out of focus: the player holds a basketball firmly with both hands in front of his body, shielding it from an opponent who stands directly in front with both arms out in a defensive stance. Dynamic sports energy, realistic motion, professional basketball photography.

**Frame 2 – Lacing Up**
Low-angle close-up. Player tying the shoe laces. Camera low and close to the ground, extreme focus on the shoe and hands pulling laces tight. Detailed visibility of lace fibers, eyelets, leather grain, subtle wear marks. Player's legs and black shorts partially visible, upper body softly blurred. Calm pre-game or mid-game moment, intimate sports editorial photography.

**Frame 3 – Resting Focus**
Ground-level, frontal close-up of the shoe as the player sits on the court, legs stretched forward, relaxed posture. Shoes dominant in frame, sharp detail on materials and form. Quiet, contemplative sports moment.

Authentic, athletic, focused mood. Ultra-high resolution, professional sports realism.

Avoid: text, watermarks, logos, cartoon style, exaggerated anatomy, blurry shoes, overprocessed lighting.
```
