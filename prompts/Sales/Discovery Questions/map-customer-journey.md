# Customer Journey Map for Sales Optimization

## 簡介

The Customer Journey Map for Sales Optimization is a free AI prompt that creates detailed lifecycle maps identifying friction points and conversion opportunities for sales teams. This customer journey mapping prompt for ChatGPT produces a structured table spanning awareness through advocacy, analyzing customer behaviors, sales team responsibilities, key touchpoints, pain points, and prioritized improvement opportunities at each stage. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering actionable insights that help sales teams reduce cycle length, improve conversion rates, and increase customer lifetime value. Sales managers use it to diagnose drop-off points, customer success teams apply it to onboarding optimization, and revenue operations professionals rely on it to align cross-functional handoffs. Reach for this prompt when you need to visualize the complete customer experience, diagnose where prospects disengage, or build a data-driven case for process improvements. ● Maps 6-8 stages from awareness through advocacy with specific actions, touchpoints, and friction points at each phase ● Highlights critical touchpoints and pain points using visual indicators for quick identification ● Assigns priority scores (1-5) to each opportunity based on potential impact on conversion and revenue ● Delivers actionable recommendations focused on shortening sales cycles and improving lifetime value ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a sales process optimization expert specializing in customer journey mapping, sales management, and conversion optimization.

## Task

Create a comprehensive customer journey map for {{product-or-service}} targeting {{target-customer}} to identify optimization opportunities and improve sales effectiveness.

## Context

The map should cover the full customer lifecycle:
- **Pre-sale stages:** awareness, consideration, decision
- **Post-sale stages:** onboarding, retention, advocacy

For each stage, analyze:
- Specific customer actions and behaviors
- Corresponding sales team actions and responsibilities
- Key touchpoints where interaction occurs
- Pain points that create friction or drop-off
- Opportunities to enhance experience and drive conversions

## Output

Deliver a structured table with these columns:

| Journey Stage | Customer Actions | Sales Team Actions | Touchpoints | Pain Points | Opportunities | Priority (1-5) |

**Formatting:**
- Mark critical touchpoints with 🔑
- Mark pain points with ⚠️
- Assign priority from 1 (low impact) to 5 (high impact) based on potential effect on sales effectiveness
- Include 6-8 stages covering the complete pre-sale and post-sale journey
- Provide specific, actionable insights at each stage—avoid generic observations

Focus on opportunities that directly improve conversion rates, reduce sales cycle length, or increase customer lifetime value.
```
