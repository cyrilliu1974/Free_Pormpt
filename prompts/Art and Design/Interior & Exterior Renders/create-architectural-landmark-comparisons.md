# Architectural Landmark Comparison Image Generator

## 簡介

The Architectural Landmark Comparison Image Generator is a free AI prompt that produces side-by-side visuals contrasting technical blueprints with real-world photographs of iconic structures for educators, travel publishers, and architectural firms. This architectural landmark comparison prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates split-screen images where the left half displays a detailed technical drawing with labeled dimensions, elevation views, and construction annotations in engineering style, while the right half shows a high-resolution color photograph of the completed landmark in its natural setting. You specify the landmark and the photo perspective, and the prompt handles aspect ratios, dividers, and resolution suitable for posters, tourist guides, and infographic series. Reach for this prompt when you need educational visuals that clarify design intent versus built reality, or when creating content for architecture courses, travel marketing, or museum exhibits. ● Produces accurate technical drawings with metric or imperial dimensions, elevation views, and material annotations alongside real landmark photography. ● Customizable landmark selection and photo perspective to match educational objectives or regional tourism needs. ● Outputs 3:2 or 4:3 aspect ratio images at resolutions suitable for print posters, digital guides, and infographic publishing. ● Clean vertical divider design emphasizes the contrast between architectural plans and finished construction. ## Prompt

```
Split-screen architectural comparison of {{landmark}}, left side technical blueprint, right side photographic reality.

Left half: Detailed architectural drawing or technical sketch showing accurate structural proportions, labeled dimensions, elevation views or cutaways, annotated with construction phases, materials, and architectural details in engineering-style typeface. Line-art or technical pen rendering with metric or imperial units appropriate to the region.

Right half: High-resolution color photograph of the completed landmark in its natural environment, {{photo-perspective}}, captured with ambient lighting and contextual surroundings visible.

Clean vertical divider or subtle blended transition between halves. The juxtaposition emphasizes the contrast between design intent and built reality. 3:2 or 4:3 aspect ratio, high resolution suitable for educational posters, architectural showcases, tourist guides, or infographic series.
```
