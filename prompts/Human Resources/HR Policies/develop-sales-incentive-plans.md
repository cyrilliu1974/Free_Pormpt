# Sales Incentive Plan Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Sales Incentive Plan Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates structured, multi-tier compensation frameworks for sales teams across industries and company sizes. This sales incentive prompt for ChatGPT applies behavioral economics and compensation design principles to build complete tiered plans covering base salary ranges, commission structures, bonus triggers, performance metrics, and rollout timelines. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to produce implementation-ready frameworks that balance revenue goals with team motivation. HR leaders and sales operations managers use it to replace ad-hoc compensation decisions with strategic, data-backed structures that encourage advancement from entry-level through senior tiers. Reach for this prompt when designing a new sales comp plan, restructuring incentives to align with evolving business goals, or documenting existing informal arrangements into a repeatable framework. ● Delivers tier-specific compensation components including base salary, commission, bonuses, and equity or recognition programs tailored to company context ● Defines 3-5 measurable KPIs and evaluation frequencies that determine tier placement and bonus eligibility ● Provides rollout timelines, communication strategies, and training plans to ensure smooth adoption across sales teams and leadership ● Includes monitoring dashboards and feedback mechanisms for continuous plan refinement based on actual performance data ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a compensation design consultant specializing in sales incentive structures. Apply principles from incentive theory, behavioral economics, and sales performance optimization to develop a tiered compensation plan.

## Task
Design a comprehensive three-tier sales incentive structure that aligns with business objectives, motivates top performance, and drives sustainable revenue growth.

## Context
Analyze the provided company context:
{{company-context}}

Include industry, company size, current sales team structure, existing incentive plan, and any relevant revenue goals or market positioning.

## Output
Deliver a structured compensation plan with these sections:

**1. Company Overview**
Summarize the provided context, highlighting factors that shape compensation strategy.

**2. Strategic Objectives**
Define 3-4 objectives the tiered structure will achieve (e.g., retention of top performers, accelerated new business acquisition, expansion of strategic accounts).

**3. Three-Tier Incentive Structure**
For each tier (entry-level, mid-level, senior/strategic), specify:
- Eligibility criteria (tenure, quota attainment, account type)
- Base salary range
- Commission rate or structure
- Bonus triggers and amounts
- Additional incentives (equity, accelerators, SPIFs, recognition programs)

**4. Performance Metrics & Evaluation**
- 3-5 KPIs that determine tier placement and bonus eligibility
- Evaluation frequency (monthly, quarterly, annual)
- Coaching and performance improvement process

**5. Implementation Plan**
- Rollout timeline with key milestones
- Communication approach for sales team and leadership
- Training and onboarding support

**6. Monitoring & Optimization**
- Data collection methods and dashboards
- Review cadence for plan adjustments
- Mechanism for gathering stakeholder feedback

Ensure all compensation figures are realistic for the company size and industry. Use clear, logical progression between tiers to encourage advancement.
```
