# Webinar Lead Generation Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Webinar Lead Generation Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that creates end-to-end webinar strategies for marketing teams and business owners looking to generate qualified leads through educational online events. This webinar lead generation prompt for ChatGPT guides you through every phase of a successful lead-generating webinar: content planning, pre-launch promotion across your available channels, registration page optimization, live delivery tactics, real-time engagement techniques, post-event follow-up sequences, and conversion pathways. You provide your product or service, target audience, unique selling proposition, and marketing channels, and the prompt produces a detailed, step-by-step strategy with timing recommendations and measurable outcomes. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it accessible across popular text AI platforms. Reach for this prompt when you need a structured approach to webinar marketing that balances education with conversion, whether you're launching your first webinar or refining an existing program. ● Structures webinar content that educates while positioning your offering as a solution ● Maps promotional tactics across your specific marketing channels with timing guidance ● Defines registration page elements and live engagement techniques to maximize conversion ● Establishes post-webinar follow-up sequences with lead scoring and qualification criteria ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert event marketing strategist specializing in lead-generation webinars.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive webinar strategy that covers planning, promotion, execution, and follow-up. The strategy must attract qualified leads, deliver genuine value to attendees, and create clear conversion opportunities.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}

**Available Marketing Channels:** {{marketing-channels}}

The webinar should position the offering as a solution while educating attendees on relevant challenges and best practices in the space.

## Output
Provide a step-by-step webinar strategy as a numbered list. Format main phases as headings (e.g., "1. Content Planning") with actionable substeps as bullet points beneath each. Cover:

- Content strategy and webinar structure
- Pre-launch promotional tactics across channels
- Registration page optimization
- Live delivery best practices
- Real-time engagement techniques
- Post-webinar follow-up sequences
- Lead scoring and qualification criteria
- Conversion pathway from attendee to customer

Each step should include specific actions, timing recommendations, and measurable outcomes.
```
