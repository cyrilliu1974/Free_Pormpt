# Funko Pop Figure Generator

## 簡介

The Funko Pop Figure Generator is a free AI prompt that creates boxed vinyl collectible figures in authentic Funko Pop style for designers, collectors, and creative professionals. This Funko Pop prompt for Midjourney produces realistic product photography of stylized figures with oversized heads, black button eyes, and simplified features inside custom packaging that mimics the official "POP! Icons" collector boxes complete with figure numbers, window displays, and side-panel artwork. The prompt runs on Midjourory, Flux, Nano Banana, and similar image generation models, delivering studio-lit collectible photography with realistic vinyl textures and tech-inspired packaging accents. Reach for it when you need to visualize custom character concepts as retail-ready collectibles, prototype figure designs, or create fan art in the signature Funko aesthetic. ● Produces authentic Funko Pop styling with oversized heads, button eyes, confident smiles, and simplified facial features. ● Includes complete packaging design with "POP! Icons" branding, figure numbers, clear window display, and illustrated side panels. ● Generates clean studio lighting and realistic vinyl texture for collector-quality product photography. ● Accepts any character name and outfit combination through two simple variables: character-name and outfit-and-accessories. ## Prompt

```
A boxed vinyl collectible figure in Funko Pop! style, featuring {{character-name}} wearing {{outfit-and-accessories}}, stylized with oversized head, black button eyes, simplified facial features, and a confident smile. The figure stands in white packaging with sleek tech-inspired design accents in blue, silver, and neon green. Box displays "POP! Icons" logo in the top left corner and figure number #42 in bold on the upper right. The side panel shows a close-up art illustration of the character with signature items in the background. Clear window reveals the complete figure, lit with clean studio lighting. Realistic vinyl texture, highly detailed collector-style product photography.
```
