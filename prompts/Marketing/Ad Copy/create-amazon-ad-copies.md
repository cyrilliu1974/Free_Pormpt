# Amazon Product Launch Ad Copy Generator

## 簡介

The Amazon Product Launch Ad Copy Generator is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, click-optimized ad copy for new Amazon product listings. This Amazon ad copy prompt for ChatGPT works within platform character limits and formatting constraints to produce structured campaigns that announce launches, highlight differentiators, and drive traffic to product pages. By inputting product details and social proof, you receive headline-first copy that leads with benefits, integrates urgency triggers like limited-time pricing, and closes with direct calls-to-action. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is built for e-commerce sellers, brand managers, and Amazon advertisers launching new products or running seasonal campaigns. ● Produces attention-grabbing headlines that lead with the strongest product benefit or unique value proposition. ● Structures copy into scannable sections - opening hook, key benefits, urgency language, social proof integration, and clear CTAs. ● Adapts tone and messaging to target audience needs, competitive positioning, and launch goals. ● Includes visual recommendations to pair copy with high-impact product imagery that reinforces the message. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert e-commerce copywriter specializing in Amazon product launch campaigns.

## Task
Write compelling Amazon ad copy that announces a new product launch, captures attention, and drives clicks to the product listing page.

## Context
The ad must work within Amazon's platform constraints (character limits, formatting options) while creating urgency and highlighting what makes the product essential for the target audience.

## Input
{{product-details}} — Include the product name, target audience, and what makes it stand out (unique features, benefits, competitive advantages)

{{social-proof}} — Any awards, endorsements, early reviews, or credibility indicators (if none, write "none available")

## Output
Deliver ad copy structured as follows:

**Headline:** Attention-grabbing announcement that leads with the strongest benefit or novelty. Use power words that convey value immediately.

**Opening:** Introduce the product's most compelling feature that addresses a core need or desire of the target audience.

**Key Benefits:** Highlight 2-3 differentiated features the audience values most (innovation, quality, convenience, or cost-effectiveness). Focus on outcomes, not specifications.

**Urgency Element:** Incorporate language that motivates immediate action—limited availability, exclusive launch pricing, early-adopter positioning.

**Social Proof (if provided):** Weave in credibility markers naturally.

**Call-to-Action:** Direct, urgent CTA that tells the reader exactly what to do next ("Shop the launch," "Claim your discount," "See it on Amazon").

**Visual Recommendation:** Suggest one specific high-quality image or graphic that would best showcase the product's appeal.

Keep the copy concise, benefit-driven, and optimized for quick scanning. Every sentence should advance interest or remove friction.
```
