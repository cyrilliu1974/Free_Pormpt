# Song Chorus Writer for Music Composition

## 簡介

The Song Chorus Writer for Music Composition is a free AI prompt that creates catchy, thematically centered choruses for songwriters, musicians, and composers. This song chorus prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to craft 4-8 line choruses that serve as the emotional centerpiece of your song. By specifying your topic, theme, and musical style - whether pop, rock, country, folk, or hip-hop - the prompt directs the AI to build memorable hooks with simple yet powerful language, rhythmic authenticity, and strategic repetition. It works across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to produce choruses designed for sing-along appeal and lasting impact. Songwriters use it to anchor compositions around a strong melodic and lyrical phrase that listeners will remember and repeat. Reach for this prompt when you need a chorus that encapsulates your song's core message - love, loss, joy, resilience - and creates the contrast and emotional arc that separates amateur from award-worthy songwriting. ● Produces 4-8 line choruses built around a memorable hook that anchors the entire song ● Tailors language, rhythm, and melody to match your specified musical style and emotional theme ● Uses strategic repetition and accessible phrasing to maximize sing-along and memorability ● Creates dynamic contrast with verses through shifts in melody, rhythm, or emotional intensity ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an Academy Award-winning musician writing a memorable song chorus.

## Task
Create a chorus that serves as the emotional and thematic centerpiece of the song. The chorus must be catchy, resonant, and instantly memorable—the part listeners will sing along to and remember long after the song ends.

## Context
Song details:
- Topic and theme: {{topic}}
- Musical style: {{style}}

The chorus should encapsulate the core message or emotion of the song and create a hook that anchors the entire composition.

## Approach
- Reflect on the theme to ensure the chorus resonates with the song's core emotional message (love, loss, joy, resilience, etc.)
- Use simple, powerful language that is accessible and easy to sing along to
- Incorporate rhythmic and melodic elements that authentically embody the musical style
- Build a strong hook—a memorable melodic or lyrical phrase that listeners will immediately latch onto
- Create contrast with verses and bridge through shifts in melody, rhythm, or dynamics
- Use strategic repetition of key phrases or words for emphasis and memorability
- Shape the emotional arc so the chorus either elevates intensity or provides a reflective peak

## Output
Deliver a succinct, emotionally powerful chorus of 4 to 8 lines that captures the song's essence and fits seamlessly within its structure.
```
