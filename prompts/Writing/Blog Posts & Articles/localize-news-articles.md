# News Article Localization Prompt for Regional Audiences

## 簡介

The News Article Localization Prompt for Regional Audiences is a free AI prompt that adapts international news content into culturally appropriate versions for specific regions while maintaining factual accuracy and core messaging. This news localization prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to analyze the original article's key facts and themes, then systematically adapt language, tone, and communication style to match local preferences. It incorporates relevant local examples and analogies that connect global events to regional issues, revises headlines for local engagement, and reviews content for cultural sensitivity. The prompt works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a complete localized article with headline, introduction, body, conclusion, and detailed localization notes explaining significant changes made. News organizations use it to reach multilingual markets, international publishers adapt content for regional editions, and media outlets ensure their coverage resonates with diverse readerships without distorting facts or losing attribution. This prompt is built for news editors, content localization teams, and international media organizations that need to make global stories relevant to specific cultural contexts. ● Maintains all original facts, quotes, and attributions while adapting presentation style ● Adjusts language, tone, and communication patterns to match regional preferences ● Incorporates local examples and context that connect global events to regional concerns ● Provides localization notes documenting all cultural adaptations and language adjustments ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert news localization specialist who adapts global news content for regional audiences while preserving factual accuracy and core messaging.

## Task
Transform the provided news article into a culturally appropriate, locally relevant version for the specified target region. Maintain all facts, quotes, and attributions from the original while making the content resonate with local readers.

## Process
1. **Analyze** the original article to identify key facts, themes, and core message
2. **Adapt** language, tone, and communication style to match local preferences
3. **Localize** by incorporating relevant local examples, analogies, and context that connects global events to regional issues
4. **Revise** headlines and subheadings to engage the target audience
5. **Review** for cultural sensitivity, appropriateness, and factual accuracy

## Context
Target region and audience: {{target-region-and-audience}}

Original article: {{original-article}}

Cultural considerations and style preferences: {{localization-requirements}}

## Output
Deliver the localized article in this structure:
- **Headline** (localized)
- **Introduction** (localized)
- **Body** (localized, maintaining original section flow)
- **Conclusion** (localized)
- **Localization Notes**: Brief explanations of significant changes made (language adjustments, added context, cultural adaptations, modified examples)
```
