# Research Topic Generator for Literature Review

## 簡介

The Research Topic Generator for Literature Review is a free AI prompt that produces evaluated research topics in Writing for academic researchers, advisors, and graduate students. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT generates a comprehensive markdown table listing potential research topics, each with a focused research question, impact score (1-10), feasibility score (1-10), appropriate research methods, and identified gaps or challenges. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, sorting results by combined scores to surface the most promising opportunities. It covers diverse areas including creative process, reader engagement, cultural influences, pedagogy, collaboration, and technology. Use it when planning dissertations, thesis projects, or exploring new research directions in Writing. ● Scores each research topic on both impact and feasibility using a 10-point scale to prioritize high-value opportunities. ● Matches appropriate research methods to each question, from ethnographic studies to experimental designs. ● Identifies meaningful gaps and challenges in the literature, helping refine research directions before you commit. ● Sorts results by combined score so the strongest candidates appear first in the table. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert academic researcher and advisor in the field of Writing with deep knowledge of research methodologies, current trends, and key challenges.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of potential research topics in Writing. Evaluate each topic based on impact (1-10), feasibility (1-10), key research methods, and potential gaps or challenges. Sort the table by combined impact and feasibility scores to highlight the most promising opportunities.

## Context
Focus on {{research-focus}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

## Output
Deliver results as a markdown table with these columns:

| Research Topic | Description/Research Question | Impact Score (1-10) | Feasibility Score (1-10) | Key Research Methods | Potential Gaps/Challenges |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Requirements:**
- Research questions must be clear, focused, and address important issues in Writing
- Methods must be appropriate for answering each research question
- Cover diverse areas: creative process, reader engagement, cultural influences, pedagogy, collaboration, and technology
- Identify meaningful gaps and challenges for each topic
- Sort by combined scores (descending) to surface the strongest opportunities
```
