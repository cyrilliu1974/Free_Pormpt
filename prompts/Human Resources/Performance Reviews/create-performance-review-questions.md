# Performance Review Questions Generator

## 簡介

The Performance Review Questions Generator is a free AI prompt that creates strategic performance review questions aligned with individual growth and business outcomes for HR professionals and organizational development consultants. This performance review prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces 10 targeted questions, each accompanied by its strategic purpose and the type of insights it reveals. You provide your business context, and the prompt designs questions that encourage self-reflection, assess strategic thinking and collaboration, uncover performance barriers, and generate substantive dialogue beyond simple yes/no responses. Use it when building performance management systems, preparing for review cycles, or creating evaluation frameworks that serve both management decision-making and employee empowerment. ● Creates questions that reveal hidden potential, innovation contributions, and barriers to peak performance ● Provides strategic rationale for each question so managers understand what insights to expect ● Generates open-ended questions that facilitate meaningful dialogue about value, challenges, and career ambitions ● Aligns individual employee aspirations with organizational goals through thoughtfully designed inquiries ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert human resources strategist and organizational development consultant specializing in performance management systems that align individual growth with business outcomes.

## Task

Create 10 performance review questions that evaluate employee contributions while catalyzing professional development. Each question should be followed by a brief explanation of its strategic purpose and the insights it reveals.

## Context

{{business-context}}

Effective performance reviews are strategic conversations that align individual aspirations with organizational goals. Design questions that:

- Encourage self-reflection and reveal hidden potential
- Assess strategic thinking and collaboration effectiveness
- Uncover barriers to peak performance and innovation contributions
- Generate substantive dialogue (not yes/no responses)
- Provide managers with actionable insights
- Empower employees to articulate their value, challenges, and career ambitions
- Address goal achievement, skill development, and future readiness

## Output

Deliver a numbered list with:

1. Each performance review question
2. Its strategic purpose
3. The type of insights it reveals

Ensure questions serve dual purposes: informing management decisions and empowering employee development.
```
