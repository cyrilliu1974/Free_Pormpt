# Market Research Analysis Report Generator

## 簡介

The Market Research Analysis Report Generator is a free AI prompt that produces structured, citation-backed market analysis reports for businesses, analysts, and strategists evaluating any target market. This market research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to deliver a complete report covering market overview with valuations and CAGR projections, consumer demographics and purchasing drivers, three key trend analyses with adoption rates, and quantified growth opportunities with dollar estimates. Every claim requires cited sources, ensuring the output is data-driven rather than speculative. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, applying dependency grammar principles to maintain logical flow between sections and produce reports that connect market dynamics to actionable business recommendations. Reach for this prompt when you need a professional market analysis framework that enforces rigor, prevents unsupported assumptions, and delivers insights you can present to stakeholders or use for strategic planning. ● Produces executive summaries, market overviews, consumer analysis, trend breakdowns, and opportunity assessments in one structured output ● Enforces citation requirements for every statistic, ensuring all claims trace back to credible sources ● Identifies quantified growth opportunities with estimated dollar values and timeframes ● Applies dependency grammar framework to connect insights logically across sections ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert market research analyst producing a comprehensive, data-driven market analysis report.

## Task
Analyze {{target-market}} and deliver a structured report covering market overview, consumer behavior, key trends, growth opportunities, and actionable recommendations. Support every claim with cited sources.

## Output Structure

### Executive Summary
Summarize the {{target-market}}, the three most significant trends identified, core insights, and market projection over the next 3-5 years.

### Market Overview
- Current market value and projected CAGR
- Key players and their collective market share
- Cite sources for all figures

### Consumer Analysis
- Primary consumer demographics (age range, percentage of market)
- Key purchasing drivers (minimum three)
- Emerging consumer segments and their growth rates
- Cite sources for all data points

### Trend Analysis
For each of three key trends:
- Description and market impact (cite percentage of consumers affected)
- How companies adapting to the trend are performing (revenue growth, KPI improvements)
- Adoption rates and alignment with consumer preferences
- Cite sources for each trend

### Opportunities
Identify two high-potential opportunities:
- Estimated dollar value over a defined timespan
- Alignment with unmet consumer needs or market gaps
- Cite sources supporting opportunity sizing

### Key Takeaways
3-5 concise bullet points summarizing actionable insights

### Sources
Full citation list for all sources referenced in brackets throughout the report (e.g., [Source 1]).

## Quality Standards
- Base all claims on credible, cited sources—no unsupported assumptions
- Focus on actionable insights and clear recommendations
- Use dependency grammar principles to maintain logical flow between sections
- Ensure data freshness and relevance to current market conditions
```
