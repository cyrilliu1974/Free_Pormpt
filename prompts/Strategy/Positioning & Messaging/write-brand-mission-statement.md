# Brand Mission Statement Writer for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Brand Mission Statement Writer is a free AI prompt that creates concise, compelling mission statements for brands across any industry. This brand mission statement prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your brand's core values, target audience needs, and unique selling proposition to produce a single paragraph (maximum 50 words) that defines your brand's purpose in a clear, memorable format. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, guiding the AI through a structured strategy process: analyzing core values and goals, identifying how your brand serves your audience, synthesizing your unique offerings into a coherent narrative, and structuring the statement so each element builds logically. Use it when you need professional mission statement copy for a website, pitch deck, investor presentation, or internal alignment. ● Takes five inputs - brand name, industry, target audience, core values, and USP - and synthesizes them into a single cohesive paragraph ● Structures the mission statement so purpose, values, and differentiation flow logically and build on one another ● Outputs concise, quotable copy (50-word maximum) designed to resonate with customers, investors, and internal teams ● Works across industries and brand maturity stages, from startups defining their identity to established companies refining their messaging ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert brand strategist specializing in mission statement development.

## Task
Craft a concise, powerful mission statement (maximum 50 words) that encapsulates the brand's purpose, values, and unique selling proposition in a clear, memorable format.

## Context
Brand: {{brand-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Target audience: {{target-audience}}
Core values: {{core-values}}
Unique selling proposition: {{usp}}

## Approach
1. Analyze the brand's core values and long-term goals
2. Identify how the brand addresses the target audience's needs
3. Synthesize the unique offerings into a coherent narrative
4. Structure the statement so each element logically builds upon the previous one

## Output
Deliver the mission statement as a single, compelling paragraph that resonates with stakeholders and distinguishes the brand in the marketplace.
```
