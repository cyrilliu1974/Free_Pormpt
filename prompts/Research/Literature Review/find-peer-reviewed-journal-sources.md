# Peer-Reviewed Journal Source Finder for Literature Reviews

## 簡介

The Peer-Reviewed Journal Source Finder for Literature Reviews is a free AI prompt that compiles systematic lists of academic journal articles for researchers, students, and educators. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT acts as an educational research specialist, searching academic databases to identify 8-12 relevant peer-reviewed sources on your chosen topic, then organizing them into a clean markdown table with article titles, authors, and 2-3 sentence summaries of key findings and conclusions. It evaluates each source for relevance, methodological rigor, and significance while prioritizing recent publications and reputable journals. You control the research topic and can specify database or date constraints to narrow the search scope. Ideal for graduate students building dissertation chapters, faculty conducting meta-analyses, or anyone needing a structured starting point for academic research. ● Produces a three-column markdown table with article titles, authors, and key-findings summaries for 8-12 sources ● Evaluates academic articles based on relevance to your topic, methodological quality, and significance of contributions ● Allows you to specify database preferences (PubMed, JSTOR, Google Scholar) and publication date ranges ● Delivers concise 2-3 sentence summaries that capture each study's main contributions and conclusions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational research specialist conducting a systematic literature review.

## Task
Research and compile a list of peer-reviewed journal articles on {{research-topic}}. For each article:
- Evaluate relevance, methodological rigor, and significance of findings
- Create a detailed summary highlighting key points and conclusions
- Focus on reputable sources and recent publications

Search academic databases systematically, prioritizing {{database-and-date-constraints}} if specified.

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns:

| Article Title | Author(s) | Key Findings |
|--------------|-----------|-------------|
| ... | ... | ... |

Include 8-12 articles. Each Key Findings cell should be a concise 2-3 sentence summary of the study's main contributions and conclusions.
```
