# Realistic Device Mockup Generator

## 簡介

The Realistic Device Mockup Generator is a free AI prompt that produces technical product showcase images with multiple device angles and detailed material specifications for designers and hardware documentarians. This device mockup prompt for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates split-layout compositions showing three distinct views - top-down, front, and side - alongside material swatches and texture closeups. The prompt renders on-screen content within the device display while maintaining era-appropriate styling and correct aspect ratios, perfect for portfolio presentations, product documentation, and vintage hardware showcases. Reach for it when you need professional-grade technical visuals without 3D modeling software. ● Produces three device perspectives in a single composition with top-down, front, and side views under consistent photorealistic lighting. ● Displays custom screen content matching the device's original era and maintains authentic aspect ratios and display technology characteristics. ● Generates material breakdown section with annotated texture swatches for casing, buttons, and screen cover linked to the device via callout lines. ● Renders on a minimal white or neutral background in 3:2 aspect ratio with vertical divider separating device views from material specifications. ## Prompt

```
Realistic product showcase image with split layout composition, 3:2 aspect ratio, minimal white or neutral background.

**Left section: Device Perspectives**

Three views of {{device}} arranged vertically:
- Top-down view showing full interface with on-screen content displayed
- Front view with content rendered in native display style, maintaining correct aspect ratio and perspective
- Side profile showing ports, controls, and thickness

Screen displays era-appropriate version of the content matching the device's original characteristics. Consistent photorealistic lighting and rendering across all views.

**Right section: Material & Surface Breakdown**

Three material swatches with close-up textures:
- Casing: {{material-palette}} for primary body, showing grain and texture with thin annotation line to shell
- Buttons/Controls: control surface material and color, with color-coded callouts
- Screen cover: screen material and finish, with pointer to screen area

**Visual treatment:**

Vertical divider separates sections. Matching shadows, typography, and labeling throughout. Authentic hardware rendering with period-correct screen display technology.
```
