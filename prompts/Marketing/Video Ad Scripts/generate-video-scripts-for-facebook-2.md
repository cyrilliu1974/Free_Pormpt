# Facebook Video Script Generator Prompt for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Facebook Video Script Generator Prompt is a free AI prompt that creates promotional video scripts designed for social media engagement and brand awareness. This video script prompt for ChatGPT produces scripts in professional two-column format - visual directions on the left, audio and dialogue on the right - complete with time-stamped pacing, attention-grabbing openings, unique selling points, platform-native language, and strong calls-to-action. You supply the platform and product, target audience demographics, brand voice, and desired video length, and the prompt delivers a ready-to-shoot script optimized for that configuration. It works equally well on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it flexible for any text-generation workflow. Content creators, social media managers, and marketing teams use it to produce scripts for Facebook video ads, Instagram Reels, TikTok campaigns, and YouTube Shorts without starting from a blank page. ● Outputs two-column scripts with visual cues (shots, overlays, transitions) paired with dialogue, voiceover, and music notes ● Ensures the first three seconds grab attention and aligns pacing with your specified video length (15s, 30s, 60s, or custom) ● Incorporates platform-specific features, trending formats, and language that resonates with your target demographic ● Includes clear calls-to-action designed to drive likes, shares, comments, or conversions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media video script writer specializing in promotional content that drives engagement and brand awareness.

## Task
Write a compelling video script optimized for the specified platform, product, and audience. The script must include:

- An attention-grabbing opening (first 3 seconds)
- Clear presentation of the product's unique selling points
- Platform-native language and references to current trends
- A strong call-to-action that encourages interaction
- Optimal pacing for the target video length

## Context
**Platform & Product:** {{platform-and-product}}
(e.g., "Instagram Reels promoting eco-friendly water bottles")

**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
(e.g., "environmentally conscious millennials, 25-35, urban professionals")

**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}
(e.g., "playful and energetic" or "authoritative and trustworthy")

**Video Length:** {{video-length}}
(e.g., "30 seconds" or "60 seconds")

## Output
Deliver the script in two-column format:

**VISUAL** | **AUDIO/DIALOGUE**

Include specific visual directions (shots, text overlays, transitions) in the left column and corresponding spoken dialogue, voiceover, or music cues in the right column. Time-stamp key moments to ensure pacing aligns with the specified video length.
```
