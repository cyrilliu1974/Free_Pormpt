# Long-Term Goal Setting Guide Prompt for Life Coaching

## 簡介

The Long-Term Goal Setting Guide Prompt for Life Coaching is a free AI prompt that creates structured, actionable roadmaps for individuals working toward major life aspirations. This goal setting prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete coaching framework that decomposes large goals into milestone phases, concrete action steps, psychological motivation techniques, and measurable progress indicators. It adapts to the user's specific goals, constraints, and timeline, making it ideal for life coaches building client plans, individuals designing self-directed development paths, or managers supporting team members through career transitions. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and structures output with clear headings and bullet points for immediate use. Reach for this prompt whenever you need to transform an abstract aspiration into a systematic plan with built-in accountability and resilience strategies. ● Breaks down ambitious goals into phased milestones with clear action steps for immediate and long-term execution ● Includes motivation techniques rooted in behavioral psychology to sustain momentum through setbacks and plateaus ● Provides measurable progress indicators, check-in schedules, and adjustment triggers tailored to the user's timeline ● Addresses obstacle mitigation strategies specific to the user's stated challenges and constraints ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert life coach creating a comprehensive long-term goal-setting guide.

## Task

Develop a personalized, step-by-step plan that helps the user achieve their aspirations. Include actionable strategies, techniques for maintaining motivation, and methods for tracking progress.

## Context

**Goals and constraints:**
{{goals-and-challenges}}

**Time frame:**
{{time-frame}}

## Output

Structure the guide with:

- **Goal breakdown**: decompose aspirations into milestone phases
- **Action strategies**: concrete steps the user can take immediately and over time
- **Motivation techniques**: psychological approaches and habits to sustain momentum through setbacks
- **Progress tracking**: measurable indicators, check-in cadences, and adjustment triggers
- **Obstacle mitigation**: tactics specific to the user's stated challenges

Use clear headings, subheadings, and bullet points for maximum readability and actionability.
```
