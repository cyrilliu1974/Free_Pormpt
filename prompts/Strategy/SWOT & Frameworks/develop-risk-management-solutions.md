# Risk Management Strategy Builder for Small Business

## 簡介

The Risk Management Strategy Builder for Small Business is a free AI prompt that creates a complete risk management framework tailored to your company's operations, finances, and market environment. This risk management prompt for ChatGPT walks you through six core components: risk identification using SWOT and PESTLE analysis, risk assessment with likelihood-impact matrices, targeted mitigation strategies, contingency planning for critical scenarios, implementation roadmaps with monitoring systems, and review mechanisms to keep the strategy current. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing a structured document that classifies threats by severity, recommends specific countermeasures - insurance coverage, operational redundancies, cybersecurity investments - and establishes timelines, roles, and KPIs for ongoing risk monitoring. Small business owners use it to prepare for supply chain disruptions, compliance changes, competitive pressures, and economic shifts without hiring a dedicated risk consultant. Reach for this prompt when you need an actionable risk management plan that covers internal vulnerabilities and external market forces in a single framework. ● Identifies operational, financial, and market risks through SWOT and PESTLE frameworks ● Assesses each risk with a likelihood-impact matrix and quantifies both financial and reputational exposure ● Recommends tailored mitigation tactics for high-priority threats and preventative measures for lower-impact risks ● Provides contingency plans with recovery steps, resource allocation, and communication protocols for critical scenarios ● Establishes implementation timelines, assigns roles, and schedules regular reviews to adapt to emerging threats ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a Business Development Specialist focused on risk management for small businesses.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive risk management strategy that identifies, assesses, and mitigates potential threats across operations, finances, and market environment. Include contingency planning and a mechanism for regular updates to ensure alignment with business goals and industry standards.

## Context
Business profile and environment:
{{business-profile}}

Provide: business description, key operations, main financial activities, market environment, known risks, business goals, and available resources for risk management.

## Output
Deliver a structured risk management strategy document with these components:

### 1. Risk Identification
Conduct comprehensive analysis across operations, finances, and market factors. Identify internal risks (operational inefficiencies, compliance issues) and external risks (economic shifts, competitive pressures). Apply SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) and PESTLE analysis (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental) to uncover hidden vulnerabilities.

### 2. Risk Assessment
Evaluate identified risks using a risk matrix to classify by likelihood and impact (high/medium/low). Assess quantitative factors (potential financial loss) and qualitative factors (reputation damage, employee morale impact).

### 3. Risk Mitigation Strategies
Develop tailored approaches for each risk category:
- High-impact risks: comprehensive mitigation plans
- Lower-impact risks: preventative measures

Strategies may include insurance coverage, operational redundancies, supply chain diversification, or cybersecurity investments.

### 4. Contingency Planning
Create response plans for critical risks that could disrupt operations. Detail recovery steps, resource allocation, and communication protocols for worst-case scenarios.

### 5. Implementation and Monitoring
Outline implementation process with:
- Roles and responsibilities
- Timelines and resource allocation
- Monitoring system to track risk indicators and trigger reassessments
- Schedule for regular risk reviews

### 6. Review and Update Mechanism
Establish regular review schedule to adapt to emerging risks and business changes. Incorporate stakeholder feedback for continuous improvement.

Format the strategy as an actionable document that serves as a practical guide for navigating challenges and ensuring business resilience and sustainable growth.
```
