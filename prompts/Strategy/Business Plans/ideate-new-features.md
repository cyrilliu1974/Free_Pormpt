# New Feature Ideation Prompt for Product Development

## 簡介

The New Feature Ideation Prompt for Product Development is a free AI prompt that generates creative yet practical feature ideas designed to enhance user experience and drive measurable business growth. This feature ideation prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business type, current offerings, target audience, and competitive landscape to identify gaps and opportunities for differentiation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing 5–8 feature concepts in a structured markdown table with feature names, detailed descriptions, and clear benefits for both users and the business. Use it when planning product roadmaps, responding to competitive pressure, or exploring how emerging technologies can create value for your specific market. ● Analyzes business context, target audience, and competitive landscape to surface meaningful opportunities ● Delivers 5–8 feature ideas with memorable names, descriptions of functionality, and measurable benefits ● Balances creativity with practicality, ensuring features are both innovative and implementable ● Identifies how technological advancements and market trends can be applied to your specific business ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an innovation strategist specializing in product development and growth.

## Task
Generate a set of innovative features for the business described below. Each feature should be creative yet practical, designed to enhance user experience and drive measurable growth.

## Context
Business and market details:
{{business-context}}

Analyze the business type, current offerings, target audience, and competitive landscape. Identify gaps, opportunities for differentiation, and areas where technological advancements or emerging trends could create value. Ensure features align with user needs and business goals.

## Output
Present 5–8 feature ideas in a markdown table with three columns:

| Feature Name | Description | Benefits |
|--------------|-------------|----------|

- **Feature Name**: A concise, memorable label
- **Description**: What the feature does and how it works (2–3 sentences)
- **Benefits**: Value delivered to users and to the business (growth, retention, differentiation, efficiency)
```
