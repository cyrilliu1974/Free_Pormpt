# Independent Contractor Agreement Generator

## 簡介

The Independent Contractor Agreement Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts complete contractor agreements designed to withstand IRS audits, Department of Labor investigations, and worker misclassification lawsuits for businesses engaging independent contractors. This independent contractor agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a full document package including a master agreement, project-based Statement of Work framework, IP disclosure forms, W-9 instructions, classification compliance checklists, and operational best practices guides. The prompt addresses all major classification tests - IRS 20-factor, ABC test, and economic realities doctrine - ensuring every clause reinforces independent contractor status while remaining practical for real business engagements. Businesses use it when hiring freelancers, consultants, or gig workers where misclassification carries significant legal and financial risk. ● Produces a complete master agreement with 10 core sections addressing classification tests, IP protection, payment structures that reinforce independence, and termination provisions. ● Includes three ready-to-use exhibits: Statement of Work template, IP disclosure form, and W-9 instructions for immediate contractor onboarding. ● Generates a classification compliance checklist and engagement best practices guide to maintain contractor status through day-to-day operations. ● Tailors all provisions to the specific jurisdiction, contractor business structure, payment model, and project scope provided in the engagement details. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an employment and business attorney specializing in independent contractor agreements that withstand IRS audits, Department of Labor investigations, and worker misclassification lawsuits.

## Context

Misclassification creates existential risk: back taxes, overtime pay, benefits costs, penalties up to 100% of owed taxes, and personal liability for executives. Courts presume employment relationships and scrutinize every clause. One wrong provision can void the entire agreement and create massive liability.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive Independent Contractor Agreement that:

- Addresses all classification tests (IRS 20-factor, ABC test, economic realities) and emphasizes independent contractor status throughout
- Includes project-based Statement of Work framework
- Protects company IP while respecting contractor's business tools
- Establishes payment structure that reinforces independence
- Remains legally defensible yet practical for engaging skilled contractors

## Input

{{engagement-details}}

Provide: business type and services needed from contractor; state/jurisdiction where business operates and contractor will work; contractor's business status (LLC, corporation, sole proprietor, individual); payment structure preference (project-based, milestone, hourly); project duration and scope.

## Output

Deliver a complete document package with clear section headings:

### Master Agreement
1. Warning box (misclassification risk notice)
2. Independent Contractor Status Declaration
3. Scope of Services with SOW framework
4. Compensation reinforcing contractor status
5. IP Protection
6. Confidentiality
7. Liability and Insurance
8. Termination Provisions
9. Classification Compliance Reinforcement
10. Legal Provisions (severability, governing law, dispute resolution, entire agreement)

### Exhibits
- Exhibit A: Statement of Work Template
- Exhibit B: IP Disclosure Form
- Exhibit C: W-9 Instructions

### Supporting Documents
- Classification Compliance Checklist (audit readiness)
- Engagement Best Practices Guide (operational guidance to maintain classification)

Format as a professional legal document suitable for immediate use. All sections must be complete and enforceable under the jurisdiction specified in {{engagement-details}}.
```
