# Student Feedback Survey Builder for Courses

## 簡介

The Student Feedback Survey Builder for Courses is a free AI prompt that creates structured student feedback surveys for educators and instructional designers evaluating course content and learning experiences. This student feedback survey prompt for ChatGPT generates a complete survey instrument with both open-ended qualitative questions and multiple-choice rating scales, tailored to your course name, target audience, format, duration, and priority feedback areas. It outputs numbered sections for open-ended questions, multiple-choice items, and a data analysis framework that guides you through identifying patterns, strengths, and improvement opportunities in the collected responses. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering clear, concise questions aligned with course objectives and practical guidance for interpreting student feedback data. Reach for this prompt when you need to evaluate a course, gather actionable insights on content quality, or understand the student learning experience across different formats and audiences. ● Generates distinct sections for open-ended qualitative responses and multiple-choice rating scales ● Aligns survey questions with course objectives, format, duration, and priority feedback areas ● Includes a data analysis framework to identify trends, strengths, and areas needing improvement ● Adapts to diverse target audiences and course delivery methods for flexible evaluation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an educational consultant specializing in course evaluation and student feedback analysis.

## Task
Design a comprehensive student feedback survey for **{{course-name}}** that captures insights on course content quality and overall learning experience. Create both open-ended and multiple-choice questions that are clear, concise, and aligned with the course objectives. Then provide guidance on analyzing the collected data to identify trends, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## Context
- Target audience: {{target-audience}}
- Course format: {{course-format}}
- Course duration: {{course-duration}}
- Priority feedback areas: {{priority-feedback-areas}}

## Output
Provide your survey in a numbered list format with two distinct sections:

1. **Open-Ended Questions** – Questions that allow students to provide detailed, qualitative responses
2. **Multiple-Choice Questions** – Structured questions with rating scales or predefined options

After the survey questions, include:

3. **Data Analysis Framework** – Brief guidance on how to analyze responses to identify patterns, strengths, and improvement opportunities specific to the priority feedback areas
```
