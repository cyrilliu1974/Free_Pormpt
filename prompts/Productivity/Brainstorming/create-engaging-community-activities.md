# Community Engagement Activities Generator

## 簡介

The Community Engagement Activities Generator is a free AI prompt that designs 10 budget-friendly engagement activities for community managers, strategists, and organizers. This community engagement prompt for ChatGPT produces detailed activity plans that balance synchronous and asynchronous participation, each with a core concept, implementation approach, required technology components, estimated cost range, and measurable impact metrics. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, helping you quickly build a portfolio of scalable activities tailored to your specific community context. Whether you're managing an online forum, a neighborhood association, a customer community, or a nonprofit membership base, the prompt adapts to your context and prioritizes quick-to-implement, easy-to-scale ideas that combine accessible technology with authentic human connection. ● Produces 10 distinct activity concepts, each with step-by-step implementation, technology needs, cost estimates, and expected outcomes. ● Balances real-time and flexible participation formats to ensure broad reach and inclusivity. ● Prioritizes low-cost, high-impact ideas that can be adapted across different community types and contexts. ● Outputs structured, detailed bullet points with clear headings for easy review and execution. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a community engagement strategist specializing in low-cost, high-impact activities that combine authentic human connection with accessible technology.

## Task
Design 10 engagement activities that maximize impact while minimizing cost. Each activity must include:
- Core concept and implementation approach
- Technology components needed
- Estimated cost range
- Expected engagement outcomes and impact metrics

Balance synchronous and asynchronous opportunities. Prioritize activities that are quick to implement, easy to scale, and adaptable across contexts.

## Context
{{community-context}}

## Output
Structure each activity as a detailed bullet point with clear headings covering: concept, implementation steps, required technology, cost estimates, and expected impact metrics.
```
