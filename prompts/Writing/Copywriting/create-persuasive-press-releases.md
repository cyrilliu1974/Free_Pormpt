# Press Release Writer With Dependency Grammar

## 簡介

The Press Release Writer With Dependency Grammar is a free AI prompt that generates structured, newsworthy press releases using advanced dependency grammar techniques to create flowing, attention-grabbing copy. This press release prompt for ChatGPT walks the model through building a complete release with a headline (under 12 words), subheadline, dateline, lead paragraph answering who-what-when-where-why in under 50 words, three body paragraphs with quotes and background, and contact information. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying dependency grammar principles like fronting, complex noun phrases, and dependent clauses to ensure each sentence connects naturally to the next. Real-world use cases include product launches, company announcements, event promotions, and newsworthy updates that need media-ready formatting. Reach for this prompt when you need a press release that adheres to journalistic standards while maintaining readability and flow through linguistically sound sentence construction. ● Produces a complete press release with headline, subheadline, dateline, lead paragraph, three body paragraphs, and contact block ● Applies dependency grammar techniques including fronting, complex noun phrases, and dependent clauses for sentence variety and flow ● Enforces strict word-count limits per section (headline under 12 words, lead under 50, body paragraphs under 75) for journalistic discipline ● Guides the model to answer the 5 W's in the lead, include compelling quotes in the body, and close with a forward-looking call-to-action ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert in dependency grammar and persuasive press release writing.

## Task
Compose a compelling, newsworthy press release for the given event using dependency grammar techniques to create interconnected, flowing sentences that grab attention and convey key information.

## Context
Event: {{event-description}}

## Output
Structure the press release with these sections and constraints:

**Headline** (under 12 words)  
An attention-grabbing headline

**Subheadline** (under 20 words)  
Expand on the headline

**Location and Date**  
City, State - Month Day, Year -

**Lead Paragraph** (under 50 words)  
Answer the 5 W's (who, what, when, where, why) using dependency grammar to create interconnected, flowing sentences.

**Body Paragraph 1** (under 75 words)  
Elaborate with details and a compelling quote. Employ dependency grammar to maintain sentence flow and readability.

**Body Paragraph 2** (under 75 words)  
Provide context and background information. Use dependency techniques like fronting and complex noun phrases for sentence variety.

**Body Paragraph 3** (under 50 words)  
Close with a forward-looking statement and call-to-action. Craft sentences with dependent clauses for memorable impact.

**Contact Info**  
Name  
Company  
Phone  
Email
```
