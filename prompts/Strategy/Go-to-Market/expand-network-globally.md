# Global Network Expansion Strategy Planner

## 簡介

The Global Network Expansion Strategy Planner is a free AI prompt that creates structured international expansion plans for businesses entering new markets. This global network expansion prompt for ChatGPT analyzes economic conditions, cultural nuances, and regulatory environments in each target market, then produces tailored strategies that align with your company's core strengths. The output is a comprehensive markdown table mapping target markets to specific strategies and time-bound action items. Business strategists use it to evaluate partnership opportunities, resource requirements (financial, human capital, technology), and risk mitigation approaches before entering regions like APAC, EMEA, or Latin America. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your industry context and current global footprint. ● Analyzes economic, cultural, and regulatory factors for each international market you specify ● Maps your company's core strengths to local opportunities and challenges in target regions ● Delivers SMART action items covering partnerships, resource allocation, and risk mitigation ● Structures output as a clear three-column table (Target Market | Key Strategies | Action Items) for executive review ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert global business strategist developing a comprehensive network expansion plan.

## Task
Create a detailed strategy for expanding a company's network into new international markets. Analyze each target market, develop tailored strategies, and provide concrete action items.

## Context
Company: {{company-name}}
Industry: {{industry}}
Target markets: {{target-markets}}
Core strengths: {{core-strengths}}
Current global presence: {{current-presence}}

For each target market, address:
- Economic conditions, cultural nuances, and regulatory environments
- Strategies that leverage the company's strengths and address local challenges
- Potential partnerships, local business customs, and networking opportunities
- Required resources (financial, human capital, technological infrastructure)
- Key risks and mitigation approaches

## Output
Present your expansion plan as a markdown table with three columns:

| Target Market | Key Strategies | Action Items |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|

Each row should cover one target market with comprehensive, actionable information. Action items must be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.
```
