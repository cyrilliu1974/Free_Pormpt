# Competitor Analysis Methodology Framework Builder

## 簡介

The Competitor Analysis Methodology Framework Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive system for analyzing competitors and converting insights into actionable strategic goals for business analysts and strategy teams. This competitor analysis prompt for ChatGPT produces a complete methodology tailored to your industry, covering competitor identification, intelligence-gathering sources, a five-step analysis framework (competitive positioning, market share, product offerings, pricing strategies, marketing approaches, and differentiation opportunities), key deliverables, and a process for integrating findings into goal-setting. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, requiring only your industry as input. Teams use it when building strategic planning processes, entering new markets, or establishing systematic competitive intelligence programs. ● Identifies five key competitors and five primary intelligence sources specific to your industry ● Delivers a five-step analysis framework covering positioning, products, pricing, marketing, strengths, weaknesses, and differentiation gaps ● Specifies three concrete deliverables from the analysis process ● Provides a five-step process for translating competitor insights into strategic company goals ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a strategic business analyst with deep expertise in competitor analysis and industry benchmarking.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive methodology for conducting an in-depth competitor analysis in {{industry}} that yields actionable insights to inform the company's goal-setting process.

## Context
The analysis should cover all major aspects of competitors' businesses while prioritizing areas where the company can differentiate itself and gain competitive advantage. Focus on insights that drive strategic decision-making rather than minor details that don't significantly impact the competitive landscape.

## Output
Structure your methodology as follows:

**Competitor Identification:**
- List 5 key competitors in {{industry}}

**Data Sources:**
- Identify 5 primary sources for gathering competitor intelligence

**Analysis Framework:**
Provide a 5-step framework for analyzing competitor data that addresses:
- Competitive positioning and market share
- Product offerings and pricing strategies
- Marketing approaches and brand presence
- Strengths, weaknesses, and gaps
- Opportunities for differentiation

**Deliverables:**
- Specify 3 key outputs from the competitor analysis

**Goal-Setting Integration:**
- Outline a 5-step process for translating competitor insights into strategic company goals
```
