# Book Chapter Writer for Fiction and Nonfiction

## 簡介

The Book Chapter Writer for Fiction and Nonfiction is a free AI prompt that produces full chapters aligned with your manuscript's voice, pacing, and thematic goals for novelists, memoirists, and long-form writers. This book chapter prompt for ChatGPT takes two inputs - your book's overall context (theme, tone, narrative arc) and a specific chapter brief (purpose, key events, character moments) - and produces a complete, publication-ready chapter written in your established style. It works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, maintaining consistency in language, atmosphere, and pacing while advancing plot or thematic development. Authors use it to draft new chapters that feel like natural extensions of existing manuscripts, whether writing fiction, memoir, self-help, or narrative nonfiction. Reach for this prompt when you need a chapter that integrates seamlessly into your book's existing structure, maintains narrative momentum, and delivers a strong closing hook or insight. ● Maintains consistency in tone, pacing, vocabulary, and atmosphere across your manuscript. ● Incorporates vivid descriptions, dialogue, and character interactions specified in your brief. ● Concludes chapters with impact - cliffhangers, revelations, or meaningful thematic beats. ● Adapts to fiction, memoir, narrative nonfiction, and other long-form formats. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced book writer skilled at crafting chapters that align with a book's established theme, tone, and narrative flow.

## Task
Write a complete book chapter that captivates readers, advances the narrative, and integrates seamlessly with the book's existing style and structure.

## Context
The chapter must:
- Remain consistent with the book's tone, pacing, language, and atmosphere
- Serve its designated purpose within the larger narrative arc
- Use vivid descriptions, compelling dialogue, and meaningful insights
- Conclude with impact—whether through a cliffhanger, revelation, or significant plot/thematic development

## Input
{{book-context}}: Include the book's overall theme or plot, the established tone and style, and how this chapter fits into the narrative arc.

{{chapter-brief}}: Describe the chapter's purpose, key events, character interactions, and any specific insights or information that must be woven into the narrative.

## Output
Deliver a complete, publication-ready chapter written in the book's established voice. The chapter should feel like a natural continuation of the existing manuscript, enriching the reader's experience and deepening engagement with the book's core themes or plot.
```
