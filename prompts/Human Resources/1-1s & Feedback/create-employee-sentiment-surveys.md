# Employee Sentiment Survey Generator

## 簡介

The Employee Sentiment Survey Generator is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive engagement surveys for HR professionals, managers, and organizational leaders seeking actionable workforce insights. This employee sentiment survey prompt for ChatGPT builds complete questionnaires grounded in Gallup's Q12 Employee Engagement Framework, combining quantifiable rating scales with strategic open-ended questions to measure both rational and emotional engagement drivers. The prompt generates surveys that cover clarity of expectations, recognition, development opportunities, purpose alignment, and psychological safety, with built-in demographic segmentation for department-level benchmarking. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing surveys designed for 10-15 minute completion that balance honest participation with comprehensive coverage of critical engagement dimensions. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface satisfaction metrics and uncover deeper drivers of organizational commitment, retention risk, and team motivation. ● Aligns every question with Gallup's Q12 core engagement elements - expectations, resources, recognition, development, voice, purpose, peer quality, and trust ● Includes demographic segmentation fields that enable department-specific trend analysis and targeted intervention planning ● Balances quantitative rating scales (1-5 or 1-7) with qualitative open-ended questions that explain the story behind the numbers ● Provides analysis guidance covering score calculation, gap identification by segment, and prioritization frameworks for action planning ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in employee engagement measurement using Gallup's Q12 framework. Design surveys that reveal genuine insights into emotional connection, trust, motivation, clarity of expectations, recognition, purpose alignment, development opportunities, and psychological safety.

## Task
Create a comprehensive employee sentiment survey tailored to the organization described below. Structure questions to measure both rational and emotional engagement drivers with quantifiable scales that can be tracked over time. Include demographic segmentation to identify department-specific trends and strategic open-ended questions to explain quantitative patterns.

## Context
Organization details: {{organization-context}}

Current challenges or feedback themes: {{current-feedback}}

## Requirements
- Align all questions with Gallup's Q12 core engagement elements
- Go beyond surface satisfaction to uncover deeper drivers like growth mindset and organizational commitment
- Balance quantitative rating scales (1-5 or 1-7) with qualitative open-ended questions
- Design for honest participation while covering all critical engagement dimensions
- Enable department-level benchmarking and trend analysis

## Output
Provide the complete survey structured as:

1. **Survey Overview** – purpose statement, completion instructions, estimated time (typically 10-15 minutes)
2. **Demographic Questions** – department, tenure, role level, location (for segmentation analysis)
3. **Core Engagement Questions** – 12-15 numbered items with rating scales specified for each, covering expectations clarity, resources, recognition, development, opinions valued, purpose, peer quality, growth conversations, and trust
4. **Open-Ended Questions** – 3-4 strategic qualitative items that surface narrative context behind the ratings
5. **Analysis Guidance** – how to interpret results, calculate engagement scores, identify gaps by segment, and prioritize interventions
```
