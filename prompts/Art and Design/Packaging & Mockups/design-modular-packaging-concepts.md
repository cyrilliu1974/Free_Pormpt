# Modular Packaging Design Prompt for Product Carriers

## 簡介

The Modular Packaging Design Prompt for Product Carriers is a free AI prompt that creates photorealistic studio renders of functional carrier packaging for brands and designers seeking presentation-ready visuals. This modular packaging prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces 8k-resolution images of sculptural folded sleeve carriers holding 3–4 individual units, complete with die-cut handles, premium matte finishes, metallic foil accents, and natural hand-holding compositions against neutral studio backgrounds. You specify the product category, brand name, industry segment, and color palette, and the prompt outputs camera-angle product photography with soft directional lighting, clean geometric forms, and architectural folding systems that communicate design innovation without loud branding. Use it for brand presentations, design portfolios, product launch decks, or client pitches when physical prototyping is not yet feasible. ● Produces sculptural multi-pack carriers with vertical cascading formations, integrated handles, and soft curvature for balanced one-hand carrying. ● Renders premium materials including matte coated recyclable board, soft-touch exteriors, and biodegradable barrier linings with subtle satin sheen. ● Includes human hand naturally gripping the carrier with soft directional studio lighting, gentle edge highlights, and shadow falloff for realistic context. ● Outputs modern utilitarian aesthetics inspired by industrial product design and architectural folding systems, with discreet branding and clean geometry. ## Prompt

```
Photorealistic product photography of a modular multi-pack carrier for {{product-category}}, designed for {{brand-name}} in the {{industry-segment}} sector. Color palette: {{color-palette}} with unexpected complementary accent tones and warm copper-toned interior contrast. No loud branding—small discreet logo near handle or side seam, brand name only.

Scene: human hand holding the carrier naturally in relaxed grip, fingers partially visible, against neutral studio background with subtle texture. Soft directional studio lighting creates gentle edge highlights on folds and curves with natural shadow falloff. Camera: slightly low-angle or side-angle perspective, 50mm product photography lens, sharp focus on packaging with soft background separation.

Aesthetic: modern utilitarian with premium retail feel, inspired by industrial product design and architectural folding systems. Clean geometry with gentle curves, functional elegance. Mood: practical, premium, thoughtfully designed, urban retail culture.

Constraints: no spills or mess, no excess graphics, no plastic look, no stock mockup style. 8k photorealistic render suitable for brand presentation and design portfolio.
```
