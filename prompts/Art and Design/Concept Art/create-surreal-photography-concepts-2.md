# Surreal Water Surface Photography Prompt

## 簡介

The Surreal Water Surface Photography Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates hyper-saturated macro photographs of liquid surfaces with abstract, dream-like lighting and color. This surreal photography prompt for Midjourney produces close-up images of water with psychedelic ripples in blues, purples, and oranges, overlaid with thin organic mist patterns and overhead lighting that casts long reflections for an underwater glow effect. You control the water surface description and aspect ratio through two variables, and the prompt applies shallow depth-of-field macro lens aesthetics with centered composition, high stylization (750), and maximum quality rendering. Use it to create album art, editorial visuals, concept mood boards, or abstract fine-art photography that transforms everyday liquids into otherworldly scenes. It runs on Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana.
