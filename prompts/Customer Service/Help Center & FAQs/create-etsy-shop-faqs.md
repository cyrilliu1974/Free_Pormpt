# Etsy Shop FAQ Generator for Customer Service

## 簡介

The Etsy Shop FAQ Generator for Customer Service is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive, customer-friendly FAQ sections for Etsy sellers looking to address common buyer questions and build purchase confidence. This Etsy FAQ prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your shop and product details and structuring them into clear question-and-answer pairs across nine essential categories: product information (materials, customization, care instructions), ordering process, shipping options and costs, return policies, custom order workflows, customer feedback handling, safety compliance, promotions, and contact preferences. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing FAQs in a friendly, reassuring tone that anticipates buyer concerns before they arise. Etsy sellers use it to standardize their customer service messaging, reduce repetitive support tickets, and create a professional shop experience that converts browsers into buyers. This prompt is ideal for new Etsy shop owners setting up their first FAQ page, established sellers refreshing outdated policies, or anyone launching a new product line that requires updated customer guidance. ● Covers nine FAQ categories including product details, shipping logistics, return policies, custom order processes, and promotional terms ● Produces answers in a friendly, concise tone that reassures hesitant buyers and reduces pre-purchase anxiety ● Structures content to proactively address common Etsy buyer concerns like delivery times, customization limits, and return eligibility ● Adapts to your specific shop details, product types, and policies to create personalized FAQ content rather than generic templates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced Etsy seller specializing in customer service for e-commerce shops.

## Task
Create a comprehensive, customer-friendly FAQ list for an Etsy shop. Address common queries about products, ordering, shipping, returns, custom orders, and shop policies in a way that builds trust and encourages purchases.

## Context
Shop and product details:
{{shop-details}}

## Output
Structure the FAQs as a clear question-and-answer list covering:

**Product Information**
- Unique features, materials, and customization options
- Care instructions and usage tips

**Ordering Process**
- How to place and customize orders
- Troubleshooting ordering issues

**Shipping**
- Shipping options, delivery estimates, and tracking
- Shipping costs and free shipping conditions

**Returns and Exchanges**
- Return policy conditions and process
- Restocking fees and customer responsibilities

**Custom Orders** (if applicable)
- Request process, design approval, production times, and additional costs

**Feedback and Reviews**
- How you address concerns and customer satisfaction guarantees

**Safety and Compliance** (if relevant)
- Certifications, safety standards, warnings, and age restrictions

**Promotions and Discounts**
- Current promotions, how to learn about sales, bulk or repeat customer discounts

**Contact Information**
- Preferred communication channels and typical response time

Use a friendly, reassuring tone. Keep each answer concise and clear. Proactively address concerns to build customer confidence.
```
