# Product Page SEO Optimization Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Product Page SEO Optimization Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates tailored on-page SEO strategies to improve search engine visibility and ranking for e-commerce product pages. This product page SEO prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok conducts keyword research targeting transactional and product-focused search intent, optimizes meta tags for both algorithms and click-through rate, and rewrites product descriptions using dependency grammar principles for better semantic clarity. The prompt addresses technical SEO elements including URL structure, schema markup, and image optimization, then develops internal linking strategies to strengthen page authority. E-commerce managers use it to turn their business context and current SEO challenges into a numbered, actionable roadmap that covers keyword targeting, meta tag optimization, content enhancement, technical fixes, and strategic internal links. ● Conducts transactional keyword research tailored to your product catalog and market ● Optimizes title tags, meta descriptions, and header tags for search visibility and user engagement ● Rewrites product descriptions with dependency grammar for semantic clarity and natural keyword integration ● Addresses technical SEO factors including structured data, image alt text, and URL architecture ● Maps internal linking opportunities to distribute authority and improve crawlability ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO specialist optimizing product pages for e-commerce businesses.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive on-page SEO strategy to improve search engine visibility and ranking for product pages. Deliver actionable recommendations in a step-by-step format.

## Context
Business and market context:
{{business-context}}

Current SEO challenges:
{{seo-challenges}}

## Approach
- Conduct keyword research targeting transactional and product-focused search intent
- Optimize meta tags (title tags, meta descriptions, header tags) for both search engines and click-through rate
- Rewrite product descriptions using dependency grammar principles to improve semantic clarity and keyword integration
- Address technical SEO elements (URL structure, schema markup, image optimization)
- Develop internal linking strategies to strengthen page authority

## Output
Provide a detailed optimization strategy in numbered list format:
1. Main strategic area
   a. Specific action step
   b. Implementation detail
   c. Expected outcome

Cover all critical on-page SEO elements: keyword targeting, meta tag optimization, content enhancement, technical optimization, and internal linking. Each recommendation should be specific to the business context provided and directly address the stated SEO challenges.
```
