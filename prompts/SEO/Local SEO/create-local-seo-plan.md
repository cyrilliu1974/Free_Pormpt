# Local SEO Plan Generator for Google My Business

## 簡介

The Local SEO Plan Generator for Google My Business is a free AI prompt that builds a structured, actionable local search optimization plan for any business location. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT takes a business location as input and produces a four-section checklist covering Google My Business profile optimization, local citations strategy across major directories, on-page SEO elements with local keyword integration, and advanced tactics like review generation and local link building. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering specific steps rather than generic advice - each item is tailored to geographic market realities and includes implementation details for NAP consistency, schema markup, location pages, and mobile optimization. Use it when you need a complete local search audit and action plan, whether launching a new location, fixing citation inconsistencies, or competing in a crowded local market. ● Produces a four-part plan with Google My Business optimization, citation platform actions, on-page elements, and additional local tactics ● Includes specific steps for profile completeness, category selection, review strategy, and NAP consistency across directories ● Covers title tags, meta descriptions, headers, schema markup, location pages, and local content strategy ● Tailors recommendations to the geographic market and business type for actionable, market-specific guidance ## Prompt

```
(Address title tags, meta descriptions, headers, schema markup, NAP placement, location pages, content localization)

Provide specific, actionable steps for each item—avoid generic advice. Tailor recommendations to the geographic market and business type when relevant details are apparent.
```
