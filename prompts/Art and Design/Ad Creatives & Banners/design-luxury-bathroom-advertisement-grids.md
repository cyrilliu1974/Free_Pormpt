# Luxury Bathroom Advertisement Grid Generator

## 簡介

The Luxury Bathroom Advertisement Grid Generator is a free AI prompt that creates four-panel advertisement layouts for premium bathroom fixture brands and interior design marketers. This luxury bathroom advertisement prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces a 1:1 square grid with four coordinated panels: an architectural hero shot in a minimalist spa environment with natural light and Italian marble, a macro close-up of water flowing over the fixture surface, a lifestyle moment capturing serene ritual use with ambient spa lighting, and an extreme detail view of precision controls and metal finish craftsmanship. Each panel is designed with professional architectural photography aesthetics, soft natural daylight, and clean modern composition. Customizable variables let you specify the product name and metal finish to match your brand identity. This prompt is ideal for luxury home brands, bathroom fixture manufacturers, interior designers, and marketing teams creating visual assets for social media campaigns, product launches, or brand storytelling without commissioning photo shoots. ● Four coordinated panels in a single 2×2 layout: architectural hero shot, water flow macro, lifestyle ritual scene, and material detail close-up. ● Spa-inspired minimalist aesthetic with natural daylight, Italian marble environments, and refined modern interiors. ● Customizable product name and metal finish variables to align with specific fixture collections and brand palettes. ● Professional photography style with shallow depth of field, crisp detail rendering, and elegant soft shadows for print and digital use. ## Prompt
