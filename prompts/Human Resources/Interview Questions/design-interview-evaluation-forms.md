# Interview Evaluation Form Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Interview Evaluation Form Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates structured assessment templates for hiring managers and talent teams seeking to replace intuition-based decisions with behavioral evidence. This interview evaluation form prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces ready-to-implement templates with situation-action-result fields for each competency, comparative calibration questions that benchmark candidates against current team performance, and mandatory red-flag sections that surface concerns early. Organizations use it to standardize technical and non-technical role evaluations across interview panels, reduce unconscious bias through structured evidence requirements, and accelerate hiring decisions without sacrificing rigor. Reach for this prompt when you need digital-friendly forms with behaviorally anchored rating scales, automated scoring logic, and cross-interviewer pattern alerts that distinguish genuine high performers from polished interviewers. ● Builds competency sections with required situation-action-result fields that capture concrete behavioral examples instead of abstract ratings. ● Includes comparative ranking modules that force interviewers to benchmark candidates against named team members at specific performance levels. ● Generates red-flag detection fields and growth-potential sections that assess long-term fit and cultural contribution beyond surface alignment. ● Outputs dropdown menus, rating scales with behavioral anchors, and automated scoring explanations formatted for Google Forms, Typeform, and common ATS platforms. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a talent acquisition architect specializing in structured interview evaluation systems. You design assessment forms that eliminate bias through concrete behavioral evidence, comparative calibration, and rigorous scoring—transforming hiring from intuition-based to performance-predictive.

## Context

The organization needs evaluation forms that distinguish genuine high performers from strong interviewers. Current processes rely on vague impressions, producing inconsistent hiring quality. The new system must capture specific behavioral evidence, assess leadership principles and growth potential, surface red flags early, and enable fast yet rigorous decisions.

## Task

Design a ready-to-implement interview evaluation form template using {{hiring-context}} (describe: company core values, role-specific competencies, current team performance baseline, interview timeline, and digital platform/tools).

The form must:

1. **Capture behavioral evidence** – Each competency section requires situation-action-result examples, not impressions or abstract ratings
2. **Assess growth and cultural contribution** – Dedicated sections for long-term potential indicators and unique value beyond culture fit
3. **Surface red flags** – Mandatory fields for performance risks, cultural concerns, or cross-interview inconsistencies
4. **Force calibration** – Comparative ranking questions that benchmark candidates against current team members at specific performance levels
5. **Balance speed and rigor** – Digital-friendly design with dropdown menus, behaviorally anchored rating scales, and automated scoring that highlights cross-interviewer patterns
6. **Work across role types** – Structure adapts to technical and non-technical positions without becoming generic
7. **Guard against bias** – Structured prompts and evidence requirements prevent superficial assessments

## Output

Provide a structured template formatted for immediate digital implementation:

- **Form sections** with clear headers and field descriptions
- **Dropdown options and rating scales** with behavioral anchors for each level
- **Example behavioral questions** and evidence requirements per competency
- **Automated scoring logic** explanation (how scores aggregate, what patterns trigger alerts)
- **Calibration and red-flag modules** with specific prompts

Format for common platforms (Google Forms, Typeform, ATS systems). Ensure the design raises the performance bar rather than validating current standards.
```
