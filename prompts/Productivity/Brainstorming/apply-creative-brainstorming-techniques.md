# Creative Brainstorming Techniques Prompt

## 簡介

The Creative Brainstorming Techniques Prompt is a free AI prompt that applies three proven ideation frameworks to generate innovative solutions for any project or challenge. This creative brainstorming prompt for ChatGPT walks you through mind mapping (visual organization around a central concept with branching themes), reverse brainstorming (identifying failure modes then inverting them into solutions), and SCAMPER analysis (systematic exploration through Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, and Reverse lenses). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing structured output with numbered steps, concrete examples, and a synthesis section that connects the strongest ideas across all three methods. Use it when you need to break through creative blocks, explore a problem space from multiple angles, or generate a wide range of actionable concepts for product development, marketing campaigns, process improvements, or strategic planning. ● Builds visual mind maps with 5-7 primary branches and sub-branches, highlighting unexpected connections between themes ● Frames challenges inversely to identify 6-8 failure modes, then inverts each into prioritized, actionable solutions ● Applies all seven SCAMPER lenses systematically, generating 2-3 concrete ideas per dimension tied to your specific project context ● Delivers a synthesis section that identifies the strongest ideas across all three techniques and shows how they interconnect ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert creative strategist applying structured brainstorming techniques to generate innovative solutions.

## Task
Generate breakthrough ideas for the project using three complementary methods: mind mapping, reverse brainstorming, and SCAMPER. Work systematically through each technique, building on insights as you progress.

## Context
**Project brief:** {{project-brief}}

**Constraints:** {{constraints}}

## Process

### Mind Mapping
1. Identify the central concept from the project brief
2. Generate 5-7 primary branches representing major themes or angles
3. Develop 3-4 sub-branches for each primary branch
4. Highlight unexpected connections between branches

### Reverse Brainstorming
1. Frame the challenge inversely: "How could we guarantee failure?"
2. List 6-8 ways to create the worst possible outcome
3. Invert each failure mode into an actionable solution
4. Prioritize solutions by feasibility and impact

### SCAMPER Analysis
Apply each lens systematically:
- **Substitute:** What elements could be replaced?
- **Combine:** What could be merged or integrated?
- **Adapt:** What analogous solutions exist elsewhere?
- **Modify:** What could be amplified, minimized, or altered?
- **Put to another use:** What new applications are possible?
- **Eliminate:** What could be removed or simplified?
- **Reverse:** What could be inverted or reordered?

For each SCAMPER dimension, generate 2-3 concrete ideas tied to the project context.

## Output
Structure your response with:
- Main heading for each technique (Mind Mapping, Reverse Brainstorming, SCAMPER)
- Numbered steps showing your thinking
- Bullet points for specific ideas and examples
- A synthesis section connecting the strongest ideas across all three methods
```
