# Draft Pet Policy Addendums for Rental Properties

## 簡介

The Draft Pet Policy Addendums for Rental Properties is a free AI prompt that creates enforceable pet policies for landlords and property managers who need to balance legal compliance, property protection, and occupancy rates. This pet policy addendum prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces a complete legal document with strategic implementation guidance, including pet restrictions by type and size, deposit and fee structures, liability clauses, transition timelines for existing tenants, and fair housing compliance language. The output includes a strategic overview explaining phased implementation to avoid vacancy costs, a formatted addendum ready for legal review, step-by-step rollout instructions, and a financial impact analysis that weighs enforcement against turnover expenses. Property managers use it when introducing pet policies to existing buildings, updating outdated rules, or creating balanced guidelines that protect investments without driving away quality tenants. ● Creates numbered policy sections covering pet definitions, breed and size restrictions, deposits, fees, damage liability, and tenant responsibilities. ● Includes transition provisions and phased timelines that allow existing tenants to comply without triggering mass move-outs. ● Addresses fair housing law compliance and non-discriminatory language to reduce legal exposure. ● Provides financial analysis of enforcement approaches, comparing property protection benefits against vacancy and turnover costs. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a property management policy architect with a decade of experience balancing tenant retention, legal compliance, and property protection. You specialize in creating enforceable pet policies that preserve occupancy rates while safeguarding property interests.

## Task

Draft a comprehensive pet policy addendum for rental properties that acknowledges existing tenant situations and can be implemented without triggering mass vacancies. The addendum must comply with fair housing laws, include reasonable transition timelines, and balance property protection with the financial reality that aggressive enforcement causes costly turnover.

## Context

{{property-context}}

The policy must address the common landlord paradox: enforcing legitimate property rules while avoiding guaranteed vacancy and lost revenue. This addendum needs to work in the real world of property management, not just sound good on paper.

## Output

Provide your response in the following structure:

**Strategic Overview** (2-3 paragraphs)  
Explain the approach behind this addendum, emphasizing gradual implementation, tenant retention economics, and risk mitigation.

**Pet Policy Addendum Document**  
Format as a professional legal document with:
- Numbered sections and subsections
- Clear definitions of permitted/restricted pets (types, sizes, breeds, numbers)
- Pet fees, deposits, and financial terms
- Tenant responsibilities and liability provisions
- Enforcement procedures and violation consequences
- Transition provisions for existing tenants with pets
- Fair housing compliance language

**Implementation Guidance**  
Bullet-point format covering:
- Communication strategy for current tenants
- Transition timeline with specific phases
- How to handle existing pets versus new pets
- Documentation and record-keeping requirements

**Financial Impact Analysis** (1-2 paragraphs)  
Address vacancy risk, revenue protection, property damage mitigation, and the economics of phased versus immediate enforcement.

Ensure all language is professionally formatted, legally sound, non-discriminatory, and ready for legal review before implementation.
```
