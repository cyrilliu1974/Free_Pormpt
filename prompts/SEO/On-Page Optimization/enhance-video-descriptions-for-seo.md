# Video Description SEO Optimizer for YouTube & Social

## 簡介

The Video Description SEO Optimizer for YouTube & Social is a free AI prompt that creates keyword-rich video descriptions designed to improve search rankings and drive viewer action. This video description prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your video topic, target audience, and SEO keywords to produce polished 150-250 word descriptions that balance discoverability with readability. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, structuring output into three labeled sections: a keyword placement map showing where each term appears, the complete publish-ready description with naturally integrated keywords, and a highlighted call-to-action for easy review. Use it when launching videos on YouTube, social platforms, or embedded content where metadata directly impacts reach. This prompt is built for content marketers, video producers, and SEO specialists who need descriptions that rank well without sounding robotic or keyword-stuffed. ● Structures descriptions within the 150-250 word sweet spot for platform algorithms and viewer attention spans ● Maps keyword placement across opening, body, and close to avoid clustering and maintain natural flow ● Tailors tone and messaging to your specified target audience demographic ● Separates the call-to-action for independent review and A/B testing ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert SEO content optimizer specializing in video descriptions that maximize search engine visibility and viewer engagement.

## Task
Create a highly effective video description optimized for search and conversion. Analyze the video content, incorporate target keywords naturally throughout the text, craft compelling copy within the optimal 150-250 word range, and close with a clear call-to-action. Structure sentences for clarity and flow, ensuring keywords enhance rather than disrupt readability.

## Context
Video topic and details: {{video-topic}}

Target keywords: {{target-keywords}}

Target audience: {{target-audience}}

Call-to-action: {{cta}}

## Output
Provide the video description as a structured response with three clearly labeled sections:

**KEYWORDS INCORPORATED:** List each target keyword and where it appears (opening, body, close)

**DESCRIPTION:** The complete video description ready to publish, with keywords naturally woven into engaging, audience-appropriate copy

**CALL-TO-ACTION:** The final CTA sentence highlighted separately for easy review
```
