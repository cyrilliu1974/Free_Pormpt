# Cinematic Product Hero Shot Generator

## 簡介

The Cinematic Product Hero Shot Generator is a free AI prompt that creates atmospheric product photography with magical lighting, seasonal decorations, and integrated branding for marketers and creative teams. This cinematic product photography prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces low-angle hero shots with crystal-clear product detail, glowing fairy lights, and dreamy bokeh effects in outdoor festive settings. It renders products at dusk surrounded by twinkling lights, atmospheric particles, and warm mist, with shallow depth of field that keeps the product sharp while backgrounds glow softly out of focus. The prompt includes customizable campaign themes, headline overlays with festive serif typography, subheadlines, and brand logo placement - delivering ready-to-use visual assets for social media, advertising, and holiday campaigns.
