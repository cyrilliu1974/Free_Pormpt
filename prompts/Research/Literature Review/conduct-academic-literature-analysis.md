# Academic Literature Analysis Prompt for Research Topics

## 簡介

The Academic Literature Analysis Prompt for Research Topics is a free AI prompt that produces structured, decade-long literature reviews for academic researchers across any discipline. This literature review prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to conduct bibliometric analysis of research from 2013 to 2023, delivering a year-by-year table of key themes, methodological innovations, and emerging sub-topics alongside APA-formatted citations, then synthesizing findings into a 3-4 paragraph trend summary. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for graduate students writing dissertations, faculty preparing grant proposals, or anyone needing to map how a field has evolved. The output includes both granular annual insights and high-level trajectory analysis, showing how methodologies and sub-topics have shifted over time. ● Produces a complete year-by-year table (2013-2023) with themes, methodological shifts, emerging sub-topics, and APA citations for any research topic you specify. ● Includes a synthesis paragraph block that identifies thematic developments, methodology evolution, and future research implications across the decade. ● Structures output in two parts: an annual analysis table for quick reference and a narrative summary for deeper insight. ● Accepts a single variable (research topic) and works across disciplines, from social sciences to STEM fields. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an academic researcher with expertise in bibliometric analysis and literature synthesis across disciplines.

## Task
Conduct a comprehensive literature review analyzing the past decade (2013-2023) of research on {{research-topic}}. Identify major themes, methodological shifts, and emerging sub-topics for each year.

## Output
Deliver your findings in two parts:

**Part 1: Annual Analysis Table**

Create a table with these columns:
- Year
- Key Themes (3-5 most prominent, as bullet points)
- Methodological Shifts (2-3 sentences describing innovations or changes in research methods)
- Emerging Sub-topics (bullet points)
- Sources (APA format citations)

Include one row per year from 2013 through 2023.

**Part 2: Trend Summary**

Write a 3-4 paragraph synthesis analyzing:
- The most significant thematic developments across the decade
- Evolution of research methodologies
- How emerging sub-topics have shaped the field
- Overall trajectory and implications for future research

Ensure all sources are cited in APA format throughout.
```
