# Innovation Project Management System Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Innovation Project Management System Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds end-to-end frameworks for tracking and managing innovation projects across their full lifecycle. This innovation management prompt for ChatGPT guides you through five critical phases: ideation capture and prioritization, planning with resource allocation, agile execution with cross-functional collaboration, performance tracking through KPIs and dashboards, and continuous improvement through lessons learned. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, adapting to your specific organizational context to deliver actionable project management structures for technology companies, research institutions, product development teams, and enterprise innovation labs. Reach for this prompt when you need to formalize how your organization captures ideas, evaluates their potential, allocates resources, tracks progress, and learns from each innovation cycle. It produces detailed frameworks with evaluation criteria, timeline development strategies, risk management plans, and reporting cadences that fit your industry and team structure. ● Builds idea capture methods, evaluation criteria, and prioritization processes for the ideation phase ● Delivers project scoping, resource allocation strategies, and timeline development for planning ● Provides agile methodologies, collaboration frameworks, and risk management plans for execution ● Defines KPIs, reporting frequency, and dashboard structures for performance tracking ● Establishes lessons-learned capture and process optimization for continuous improvement ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert innovation manager with extensive experience in designing and implementing systems for tracking and managing innovation projects across various industries.

## Task
Design a comprehensive innovation project management system that covers the full lifecycle from ideation through execution. Incorporate best practices in project management, resource allocation, and performance tracking. Provide specific, actionable insights adaptable to different industries and project types.

## Context
{{organizational-context}}

## Output
Provide a detailed, structured response organized into these sections:

**1. Ideation Phase**
- Idea capture methods
- Idea evaluation criteria
- Idea prioritization process

**2. Planning Phase**
- Project scoping process
- Resource allocation strategy
- Project timeline development

**3. Execution Phase**
- Agile project management methodology
- Cross-functional collaboration framework
- Risk management plan

**4. Performance Tracking**
- Key performance indicators
- Progress reporting frequency
- Project health dashboard

**5. Continuous Improvement**
- Lessons learned capture process
- Innovation process optimization

For each section, provide comprehensive and specific insights tailored to the organizational context. Use bullet points for clarity and readability.
```
