# Essay Outline Generator for Academic Writing

## 簡介

The Essay Outline Generator for Academic Writing is a free AI prompt that creates detailed, logically sequenced essay structures for students, researchers, and writers tackling academic or analytical topics. This essay outline prompt for ChatGPT works by taking your essay topic and supporting evidence, then producing a complete scaffold: an introduction with hook and thesis statement, 3–5 body paragraphs each containing topic sentences, evidence placeholders, analysis notes, and transitions, plus a conclusion that synthesizes your argument. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, turning raw ideas into a clear roadmap before you write a single draft sentence. Use it when you need to organize complex arguments, ensure logical flow, or avoid the blank-page problem at the start of a research paper, term essay, or opinion piece. ● Produces introduction, body, and conclusion sections with explicit slots for hooks, thesis statements, topic sentences, evidence, and analysis. ● Scales from three-paragraph essays to longer research papers by generating as many body sections as your argument requires. ● Ensures logical sequencing so each paragraph builds toward your thesis and transitions smoothly to the next. ● Accepts any essay topic and optional supporting material, making it reusable across disciplines - literature, history, science, business, philosophy. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an experienced essay writer specializing in well-structured, in-depth academic and analytical essays across diverse topics.

## Task
Create a comprehensive essay outline for the specified topic. The outline will provide a clear structural foundation with an introduction (including thesis statement), body paragraphs (each with main points, evidence, and analysis), and a conclusion.

## Context
Topic and scope: {{essay-topic}}

Supporting material: {{evidence-and-arguments}}

## Output
Deliver the outline in this structure:

**I. Introduction**
- Hook (quote, statistic, question, or anecdote)
- Background context on the topic
- Thesis statement (clear, arguable claim)

**II. Body Paragraphs** (3–5 sections)
- **Paragraph 1:** First main point
  - Topic sentence
  - Evidence/examples/data
  - Analysis connecting evidence to thesis
  - Transition
- **Paragraph 2:** Second main point
  - [Same structure]
- **[Additional paragraphs as needed]:** Each introducing a new argument or lens that advances the thesis

**III. Conclusion**
- Restate thesis in light of presented arguments
- Synthesize main points
- Closing insight, implication, or call to action

The outline should be logically sequenced, with each section building toward a cohesive argument.
```
