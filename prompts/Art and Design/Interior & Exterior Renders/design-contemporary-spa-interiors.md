# Contemporary Spa Interior Design Prompt

## 簡介

The Contemporary Spa Interior Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that generates ultra-realistic architectural renderings of modern wellness spa interiors for designers, hospitality brands, and wellness entrepreneurs. This spa interior design prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana produces wide-angle editorial visualizations featuring open-flow layouts with sculptural zones connected by soft curved pathways instead of closed rooms - including relax lounges, treatment pods, meditation corners, and water areas. You customize the material palette (terrazzo, stone, concrete) and sensory features (aromatherapy misters, heated surfaces, acoustic panels) to match your brand identity while maintaining contemporary artistic aesthetics inspired by boutique hotel spas, art gallery interiors, and Mediterranean modernism. The prompt emphasizes color-blocked architectural surfaces, organic curved furnishings, and filtered lighting that creates a relaxed yet visually exciting atmosphere. Ideal for interior designers pitching spa concepts, hospitality developers creating investor presentations, and wellness brands building visual identities that avoid traditional spa clichés like bamboo decor and zen symbols. ● Produces sculptural spa zones with curved pathways, low organic lounge furniture, and bold color-blocked surfaces that function as architectural statements. ● Customizable material palettes and sensory features (aromatherapy, heated stone, acoustic treatments) integrated architecturally rather than added as decor. ● Generates soft global illumination with filtered daylight through circular openings, hidden LED strips, and glowing sculptural light objects for warm dreamy atmosphere. ● Delivers editorial-quality renderings with high-detail surface textures suitable for branding decks, social media marketing, and portfolio presentations. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic architectural visualization of a modern wellness spa interior, wide-angle 24–35mm perspective, editorial asymmetrical composition.

Design concept: contemporary artistic spa as immersive sensory experience—open-flow layout with sculptural zones connected by soft curved pathways instead of closed rooms. Zones include relax lounge, treatment pods, meditation corner, water and steam area.

{{material-palette}} with matte tactile natural finishes. Color-blocked architectural surfaces creating bold visual statements, not decorative additions.

Furnishings: low sculptural lounge sofas with organic curved forms, custom stone or terrazzo side tables, rounded edges and bold silhouettes with minimal detailing.

Lighting: filtered daylight through textured circular openings, indirect LED strips hidden in curves, soft ambient wall washing, glowing sculptural light objects—warm dreamy atmosphere with shadow-rich but soft quality.

{{sensory-features}} integrated architecturally. Treatment areas are semi-open pods with fabric or glass separation.

Decor as architecture: abstract wall art built into surfaces, large indoor plants as spatial dividers, ceramic and stone art objects—nothing feels added later.

Rendering: soft global illumination, high-detail tactile surface textures, editorial color grading. Focus on materials, curves, and color relationships.

Style influences: boutique hotel spa, art gallery interiors, high-end fashion retail, Mediterranean modernism. Atmosphere: relaxed but inspired, calm yet visually exciting, luxury without stiffness.

Avoid: traditional spa clichés, bamboo decor, zen symbols, white sterile interiors, cheap candles, overly minimal empty spaces.
```
