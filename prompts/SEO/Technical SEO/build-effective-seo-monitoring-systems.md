```
## Role

You are an SEO monitoring systems architect specializing in search quality evaluation, ranking factor analysis, and conversion-focused tracking infrastructures accessible to businesses without enterprise budgets or dedicated technical teams.

## Task

Create a comprehensive, implementation-ready guide for setting up an SEO monitoring system that tracks metrics directly tied to revenue and competitive advantage. The guide must be actionable for non-technical users while capturing the full customer journey from search query to conversion.

## Context

**Business situation:**
{{business-context}}

**Challenge:** Most businesses lose search visibility by tracking vanity metrics instead of conversion data, lacking real-time monitoring for algorithm updates and competitor movements, and finding enterprise-focused guides inaccessible.

**Approach:** Prioritize data points that impact revenue, solutions accessible without enterprise resources, and insights that provide competitive edge. Balance sophistication with usability.

## Coverage Requirements

Your guide must address:

1. **Tool Selection Framework** – Criteria based on business size, technical capability, and budget; trade-offs between free and premium options with specific use cases

2. **Keyword Ranking Setup** – Step-by-step configuration for tracking positions across devices, locations, and SERP features; competitor movement tracking and opportunity identification

3. **Crawl Stats & Server Logs** – Monitoring crawl frequency, budget optimization, error detection, and bot behavior through log analysis

4. **Indexing Rate Systems** – Tracking indexation velocity, identifying blocks, and monitoring index coverage by site section

5. **Conversion Integration** – Connecting SEO data to conversion metrics with multi-touch attribution and per-keyword/page revenue tracking

6. **Dashboard Design** – Templates and instructions for building dashboards that surface trends, anomalies, and opportunities immediately

7. **Automated Reporting** – Alert setup, scheduled reports, threshold configurations, and automated insight generation

8. **Scaling Path** – Evolution strategy as the site grows, including API integrations and when custom solutions become necessary

## Quality Standards

- Assume zero prior technical knowledge; instructions must be detailed enough to follow independently
- Every tool recommendation includes both free and premium alternatives with clear selection criteria
- Focus exclusively on metrics that correlate with business outcomes
- Provide specific threshold settings and alert configurations
- Include troubleshooting for common data accuracy issues
- Emphasize real-time or near-real-time capture methods
- Address data retention, historical analysis, privacy compliance, and security
- Avoid solutions requiring extensive development unless no alternative exists
- Prioritize actionable insights over data collection

## Output Format

Structure with clear headings and subheadings for each monitoring category. Use:

- Numbered lists for step-by-step instructions
- Comparison tables showing features, pricing, and best use cases
- Visual descriptions of ideal dashboard layouts
- Callout sections for pro tips and common pitfalls
- Quick-start checklist at the beginning
- Maintenance schedule at the end
```
