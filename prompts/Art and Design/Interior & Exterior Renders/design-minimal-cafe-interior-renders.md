# Minimal Cafe Interior Render Prompt

## 簡介

The Minimal Cafe Interior Render Prompt is a free AI prompt that produces architectural-grade visualizations of contemporary coffee shop interiors for designers, architects, and hospitality brands. This minimal cafe interior render prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana generates eye-level wide shots of open bar-centric layouts featuring long minimalist coffee bars, clean rectangular tables, and lightweight modern seating. You control the material palette (bar counter, walls, flooring, wood accents), color scheme (neutral, calming tones with quiet luxury), and window configuration (natural daylight effects). The prompt balances soft global illumination, warm linear shelf lighting at 2700K–3000K, and focused track spots to create timeless, sophisticated coffee spaces. Use it for client presentations, portfolio work, or brand mood boards when expensive 3D rendering services are out of reach. ● Produces bar-centric layouts with professional espresso machines, floating shelves, and uncluttered equipment displays in high texture detail. ● Controls material palettes and neutral color schemes to evoke calm sophistication and quiet luxury without visual clutter. ● Adjusts window configurations and lighting temperatures to balance natural daylight with warm artificial accents and hidden indirect sources. ● Outputs 1:1 high-resolution renders suitable for social media, client pitches, and hospitality brand identity work. ## Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic architectural visualization of a modern minimal contemporary cafe interior, eye-level wide shot, soft global illumination, high texture detail.

Open bar-centric layout with a long minimalist coffee bar as the focal point. Simple rectangular wooden tables and lightweight modern cafe chairs arranged with clear, fluid circulation. No visual clutter.

Materials: {{material-palette}} for bar counter, walls, flooring, and wood accents. Color scheme: {{color-scheme}} creating a neutral, calming, sophisticated atmosphere with quiet luxury feel.

Lighting: natural daylight streaming through {{window-configuration}}, complemented by warm linear shelf lighting, focused track spots, and hidden indirect lighting at 2700K–3000K.

Professional espresso machines displayed cleanly at the bar with floating shelves holding minimal glassware. Precise, symmetrical, uncluttered organization. Exposed black-painted ceiling with minimal track lighting.

Very subtle branding—small logo detail or menu typography only. Architecture over branding. Function-first, visually quiet furniture. Clean, warm, urban yet inviting. Timeless and refined.

Avoid: rustic cafe, vintage decor, industrial clutter, bright colors, decorative overload, hipster cafe elements.

1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution. Final mood: modern, premium, calm coffee experience.
```
