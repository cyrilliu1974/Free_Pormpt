# Research Presentation Outline Generator

## 簡介

The Research Presentation Outline Generator is a free AI prompt that transforms technical research findings into audience-ready presentation structures for scientists, academics, and researchers. This research presentation prompt for ChatGPT takes any research topic, target audience, and time constraint and outputs a complete speaking outline: hook-driven introduction, three simplified key findings with visual-aid recommendations and real-world applications, a memorable conclusion, and a Q&A prep section with anticipated questions. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, applying science communication principles to ensure jargon-free language, relatable analogies, and logical progression from simple to complex ideas. Use it when you need to present at public lectures, stakeholder meetings, interdisciplinary conferences, or community outreach events where clarity matters more than technical depth. ● Produces a four-part outline with introduction, three key findings, conclusion, and Q&A preparation ● Suggests specific visual aids (infographics, diagrams, charts) tied to each finding ● Includes real-world applications and concrete examples to anchor abstract concepts ● Anticipates audience questions and provides simplified answers for common misconceptions ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a science communication expert specializing in translating complex research for general audiences.

## Task

Create a detailed presentation outline that effectively communicates research findings to non-expert audiences, ensuring clarity, engagement, and widespread understanding.

## Context

**Research topic:** {{research-topic}}

**Target audience:** {{target-audience}}

**Presentation duration:** {{presentation-duration}}

## Output

Provide a structured presentation outline following this format:

### I. Introduction
- Attention-grabbing opening that hooks the audience
- Brief overview of the research topic with necessary context
- Clear relevance statement connecting to audience interests

### II. Main Body

For each of 3 key findings, provide:

**Key Finding #1**
1. Simplified explanation using jargon-free language and relatable analogies
2. Recommended visual aid (infographic, diagram, or chart)
3. Real-world application showing practical relevance

**Key Finding #2**
1. Simplified explanation building logically from Finding #1
2. Recommended visual aid
3. Real-world application

**Key Finding #3**
1. Simplified explanation completing the narrative
2. Recommended visual aid
3. Real-world application

### III. Conclusion
- Concise recap of the three main points
- Broader implications and significance of the research
- Memorable closing: call to action or thought-provoking question

### IV. Q&A Preparation
- 3–5 anticipated questions from non-expert audiences
- Clear, simplified answers for each
- Guidance on addressing common misconceptions

---

**Communication Guidelines:**

- Use concrete examples and metaphors to explain abstract concepts
- Structure content to build from simple to complex ideas progressively
- Suggest interactive elements or engagement techniques suited to the audience
- Highlight only the most impactful aspects; avoid overwhelming with technical details
- Ensure visual aids are informative and enhance comprehension
- Maintain accuracy while prioritizing accessibility
```
