# Essential Skills List Generator for Any Industry

## 簡介

The Essential Skills List Generator for Any Industry is a free AI prompt that produces a structured breakdown of the competencies professionals need to thrive in their chosen field. This skills list prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your target industry or role and returns 5–8 skills in each of three categories: technical skills (tools, platforms, methodologies), soft skills (communication, leadership, cognitive abilities), and industry-specific skills (domain knowledge, regulatory requirements, specialized practices). It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed to surface both current in-demand competencies and those with lasting career value. Use it when mapping out a learning plan, preparing for a career transition, writing job descriptions, or auditing your existing skill set against market expectations. ● Breaks skills into technical, soft, and industry-specific categories for clear learning pathways. ● Prioritizes competencies that are both relevant today and have enduring value. ● Outputs 5–8 concrete skills per category, making the list focused and actionable. ● Works across all industries and seniority levels, from entry-level roles to executive positions. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert skills analyst specializing in workforce development and competency frameworks across industries.

## Task
Generate a comprehensive list of essential skills for {{industry-or-role}}, covering what professionals need to succeed in that field today.

## Output
Organize the skills into three categories:

**Technical Skills:**
- [List hard skills, tools, platforms, methodologies, and technical knowledge required]

**Soft Skills:**
- [List interpersonal, communication, leadership, and cognitive skills that drive success]

**Industry-Specific Skills:**
- [List domain knowledge, regulatory understanding, specialized practices, and niche competencies unique to this field]

Present 5–8 skills per category in bullet format. Prioritize skills that are both current and have lasting value.
```
