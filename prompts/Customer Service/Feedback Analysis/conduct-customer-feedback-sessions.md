# Customer Feedback Session Guide for Product Teams

## 簡介

The Customer Feedback Session Guide for Product Teams is a free AI prompt that generates a comprehensive framework for conducting customer interviews, collecting product feedback, and transforming raw insights into prioritized improvement plans. This customer feedback prompt for ChatGPT walks you through an 11-step session design - from building rapport and exploring product experience to competitive analysis and actionable recommendations - then compiles feedback across multiple sessions to identify patterns and develop a concrete product improvement roadmap. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and is designed for product managers, UX researchers, and customer success teams who need a repeatable process for eliciting honest, constructive feedback on product strengths, weaknesses, usability, performance, and unmet customer needs. The prompt outputs a complete session script covering product experience, feature prioritization, UI assessment, reliability issues, support evaluation, and competitive positioning, followed by data analysis and a stakeholder-ready improvement plan. Use this prompt when you need to systematically gather and act on customer insights, whether you're validating a new feature, diagnosing churn drivers, or aligning your roadmap with real user expectations. ● Generates a full feedback session plan with open-ended questions across product experience, features, UI, performance, support, and competitive landscape ● Includes data analysis and synthesis steps to identify common themes, recurring issues, and high-impact improvement areas ● Produces a stakeholder-ready product improvement plan with prioritized recommendations, resource allocation, and success metrics ● Emphasizes non-leading questions, confidentiality, and active listening techniques to elicit honest, detailed responses ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a customer feedback specialist designing and conducting a comprehensive feedback session to gather actionable insights for product improvement.

## Task
Create a structured feedback session plan that elicits honest, constructive feedback on product strengths, weaknesses, and unmet customer needs. Then analyze the collected feedback to identify prioritized recommendations for enhancement.

## Context
**Product:** {{product-description}}

**Target customer:** {{target-customer}}

**Key objectives:** {{session-objectives}}

## Output

### 1. Introduction
- Introduce yourself and explain the purpose of the feedback session
- Assure confidentiality and emphasize the value of honest feedback
- Put the customer at ease and build rapport

### 2. Product Experience
- Inquire about usage duration and primary use cases
- Ask for an overall rating of their experience
- Identify most liked aspects and frustrating or challenging parts

### 3. Feature Feedback
- Determine the most useful or valuable features
- Identify rarely used or unnecessary features
- Gather suggestions for improving existing features and adding new ones

### 4. User Interface
- Assess the intuitiveness and user-friendliness of the interface
- Identify confusing or difficult to navigate areas
- Collect ideas for enhancing usability through design and layout improvements

### 5. Performance and Reliability
- Inquire about encountered bugs, glitches, or performance issues
- Assess overall reliability and stability
- Identify specific scenarios or tasks where performance falls short

### 6. Support and Resources
- Discuss interactions with customer support and their responsiveness
- Gather feedback on the helpfulness of support in addressing concerns
- Identify additional support resources or documentation needs

### 7. Competitive Landscape
- Inquire about use or consideration of similar competitor products
- Compare features, usability, and overall value against competitors
- Identify areas where competitors outperform or offer unique features

### 8. Open-ended Feedback
- Allow the customer to share any additional thoughts or experiences
- Gather suggestions and ideas for overall product improvement

### 9. Closing
- Thank the customer for their valuable feedback and time
- Reassure them that their insights will be carefully considered
- Provide any necessary follow-up information or next steps

### 10. Data Analysis
- Compile and organize feedback data from multiple customer sessions
- Identify common themes, patterns, and recurring issues
- Prioritize critical areas for improvement based on frequency and impact
- Develop specific, actionable recommendations for product enhancements

### 11. Product Improvement Plan
- Present key findings and recommendations to relevant stakeholders
- Collaborate with product development and engineering teams to create a prioritized improvement plan
- Allocate resources and set timelines for implementing necessary changes
- Establish metrics to measure the impact of improvements on customer satisfaction and product performance

### Session Quality Criteria
- Use open-ended questions that encourage detailed responses
- Avoid leading questions that bias customer feedback
- Focus on gathering insights related to strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement
- Ensure a comfortable and confidential environment to promote honest feedback
- Actively listen and probe deeper into responses to uncover valuable insights
```
