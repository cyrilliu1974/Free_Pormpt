# IP Assignment Agreement Generator

## 簡介

The IP Assignment Agreement Generator is a free AI prompt that drafts legally structured intellectual property assignment agreements for attorneys, business owners, and IP professionals. This IP assignment agreement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini produces a complete contract document with all necessary sections: party identification, recitals, assignment of rights, representations and warranties, indemnification, confidentiality, governing law, dispute resolution, and signature blocks. You provide the IP details, assignor and assignee information, and financial terms, and the prompt structures a formal legal document covering the full transfer of ownership rights including reproduction, distribution, modification, and sublicensing. It's designed for patent assignments, trademark transfers, copyright conveyances, and trade secret handovers where clear documentation of ownership change is required. ● Covers all critical contract elements including assignor obligations, assignee obligations, representations and warranties, indemnification, and dispute resolution mechanisms ● Produces plain-English legal documents that balance clarity with professional legal standards and precise terminology ● Structures financial terms, payment obligations, and any ongoing royalty arrangements within the agreement framework ● Includes standard legal provisions such as entire agreement clauses, amendment procedures, notice requirements, severability, and counterpart execution ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert intellectual property attorney specializing in comprehensive IP assignment agreements.

## Task
Draft a detailed intellectual property assignment agreement that transfers ownership of specified IP from one party to another. The agreement must be legally sound, protect both parties' interests, and clearly define all rights, obligations, and financial terms.

## Context
The agreement covers:
- **IP Details**: {{ip-details}} (type of intellectual property, description, any registration numbers or identifiers)
- **Assignor**: {{assignor}} (individual or entity transferring the IP)
- **Assignee**: {{assignee}} (individual or entity receiving the IP)
- **Financial Terms**: {{financial-terms}} (compensation amount, payment structure, any royalties or ongoing obligations)

## Output
Structure the agreement as a formal legal document with these sections:

**1. Title**: Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

**2. Parties**: Identify the assignor and assignee with full legal names and addresses

**3. Recitals**: Describe the IP being transferred and the purpose of the agreement

**4. Terms**: Include comprehensive provisions covering:
- Assignment of rights (complete transfer of ownership)
- Scope of rights transferred (reproduction, distribution, modification, sublicensing)
- Assignor obligations (delivery of materials, cooperation, further assurances)
- Assignee obligations (payment terms, use restrictions if any)
- Compensation details
- Representations and warranties (ownership, non-infringement, authority to assign)
- Indemnification (protection against third-party claims)
- Confidentiality requirements
- Term and termination conditions
- Governing law and jurisdiction
- Dispute resolution mechanism
- Entire agreement clause
- Amendment procedures
- Notice provisions
- Severability
- Waiver terms
- Counterpart execution

**5. Signature Block**: Space for both parties' signatures, printed names, titles, and date

Use precise legal terminology throughout. Write in plain English for clarity while maintaining professional legal standards. Format as a traditional contract document without XML tags or non-standard formatting.
```
