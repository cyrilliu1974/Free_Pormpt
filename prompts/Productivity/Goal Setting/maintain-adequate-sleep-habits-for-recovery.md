# Sleep Recovery Schedule Planner for Better Rest

## 簡介

The Sleep Recovery Schedule Planner for Better Rest is a free AI prompt that builds personalized sleep plans to enhance recovery, boost productivity, and prevent burnout. This sleep recovery prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your unique sleep profile - desired duration, current bedtime routine, environment details, daily schedule, and specific concerns - and produces a structured three-column plan covering sleep schedule, bedtime routine, and environment optimization. It applies circadian rhythm principles and evidence-based sleep hygiene practices to create actionable recommendations tailored to your lifestyle. Use it to overcome insomnia, adjust to shift work, recover from jet lag, or simply improve sleep quality for better daytime performance. Runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt whenever you need a science-informed, individualized sleep strategy that fits your real-world constraints and addresses your specific sleep challenges. ● Analyzes sleep duration needs, daily activities, and circadian rhythm compatibility to build a custom sleep schedule ● Designs bedtime routines that promote relaxation, signal sleep readiness, and address individual concerns like stress or screen time ● Provides sleep environment optimizations - lighting, temperature, noise control, and comfort adjustments - based on best practices in sleep hygiene ● Outputs recommendations in a structured markdown table for easy reference and implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert sleep specialist creating personalized sleep schedules that enhance recovery, productivity, and prevent burnout.

## Task
Analyze the provided sleep information and develop a comprehensive, optimized sleep plan. Consider circadian rhythms, sleep hygiene practices, and individual lifestyle needs when formulating recommendations.

## Context
{{sleep-profile}}

Include:
- Desired sleep duration
- Current bedtime routine
- Sleep environment details
- Daily schedule
- Specific sleep concerns or issues

## Output
Present your recommendations in a markdown table with three columns:

| Sleep Schedule | Bedtime Routine | Sleep Environment Optimization |
|----------------|-----------------|--------------------------------|

Each column must provide detailed, actionable recommendations tailored to the user's specific needs and goals. Base all advice on the sleep profile provided.
```
