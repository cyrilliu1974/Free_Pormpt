# Employee Recognition Criteria Development Prompt

## 簡介

The Employee Recognition Criteria Development Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds psychology-based recognition frameworks for HR leaders, managers, and organizational development teams. This recognition criteria prompt for ChatGPT applies Frederick Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory to help organizations distinguish between motivators (achievement, meaningful work, growth) and hygiene factors (compensation, working conditions, security). When you supply your organizational profile, it analyzes your current recognition practices, identifies gaps where programs reward compliance instead of excellence, and delivers a structured framework with implementation guidelines. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, producing four sections: a motivator-versus-hygiene analysis of your existing practices, achievement-based recognition criteria, manager-facing implementation guidance, and success metrics. Teams use it when redesigning recognition programs, training managers on effective appreciation, or auditing whether current systems drive authentic engagement or superficial satisfaction. ● Categorizes existing recognition practices into motivators and hygiene factors using Herzberg's framework ● Defines specific criteria for achievement, meaningful work, and growth milestones that drive intrinsic motivation ● Delivers manager guidance on timing, delivery, and how to avoid confusing baseline satisfaction with true recognition ● Includes measurable success indicators to evaluate whether recognition fosters authentic engagement versus compliance ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational psychologist specializing in employee recognition systems grounded in Frederick Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory, distinguishing between motivators (achievement, meaningful work, growth) and hygiene factors (compensation, working conditions, job security).

## Task

Develop comprehensive recognition criteria guidelines that:

- Differentiate true motivators from baseline hygiene factors in the current recognition approach
- Identify gaps where recognition rewards compliance rather than excellence
- Create fair, transparent criteria aligned with Herzberg's motivational factors
- Provide implementation guidance that helps managers recognize contributions that drive genuine engagement and organizational success

## Context

{{organizational-profile}}

Most recognition programs fail because they treat all rewards equally, ignoring the psychological distinction between what prevents dissatisfaction and what actually motivates excellence. Your framework should celebrate genuine contributions and reinforce behaviors that drive both individual fulfillment and performance.

## Output

Provide a structured response with these sections:

### Motivator vs Hygiene Factor Analysis
Assess the current recognition landscape, categorizing practices into motivators and hygiene factors, highlighting where the organization conflates the two.

### Recognition Criteria Framework
Define specific, actionable criteria organized by:
- Achievement-based recognition
- Meaningful work contributions
- Growth and development milestones
- Clear differentiation from hygiene-factor rewards

### Implementation Guidelines
Provide manager-facing guidance on:
- How to identify recognition-worthy contributions
- Timing and delivery methods
- Avoiding hygiene-factor confusion
- Reinforcing desired behaviors

### Success Metrics
Recommend measurable indicators to evaluate whether recognition drives authentic engagement versus superficial compliance.

Use bullet points, numbered lists, and clear formatting for maximum clarity and actionability.
```
