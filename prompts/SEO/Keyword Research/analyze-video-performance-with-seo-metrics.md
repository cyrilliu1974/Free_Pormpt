# Video SEO Performance Analysis Prompt

## 簡介

The Video SEO Performance Analysis Prompt is a free AI prompt that evaluates video content against search ranking factors and delivers actionable optimization strategies for content creators and marketers. This video SEO prompt for ChatGPT analyzes title optimization, metadata completeness, engagement signals, watch time metrics, thumbnail effectiveness, and platform-specific ranking factors. It produces a structured markdown table with three columns - Metric, Current Performance, and Optimization Strategies - covering 8-12 of the most impactful SEO factors. The prompt works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, allowing creators to benchmark their videos against competitors and platform best practices within their specific niche. Use cases include pre-upload optimization planning, performance audits of existing content, and competitive analysis in crowded content categories. Reach for this prompt when you need a systematic evaluation of video discoverability factors or when engagement metrics suggest visibility issues that require diagnosis and prioritization. ● Audits title and description keyword placement, tag strategy, and metadata completeness against platform best practices ● Evaluates engagement signals including watch time, retention curves, click-through rates, likes, comments, and shares ● Assesses accessibility features such as captions and transcripts that impact both reach and algorithmic favor ● Delivers benchmarked performance analysis comparing your video against competitors in your niche ● Provides clear, prioritized optimization strategies in a markdown table format ready for implementation ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert video SEO analyst specializing in search optimization and audience engagement across major video platforms.

## Task
Analyze the provided video's SEO performance and deliver a comprehensive report with actionable optimization strategies. Focus on metrics that directly impact search rankings, discoverability, and viewer engagement.

## Context
{{video-details}}

Evaluate all critical SEO factors including:
- Title and description optimization (keyword placement, length, clarity)
- Tags and categorization
- Thumbnail effectiveness
- Watch time and retention metrics
- Engagement signals (likes, comments, shares, CTR)
- Metadata completeness
- Accessibility features (captions, transcripts)
- Platform-specific ranking factors

Benchmark performance against competitors and platform best practices within the specified niche.

## Output
Present your analysis as a markdown table with three columns:

| Metric | Current Performance | Optimization Strategies |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------------|

For each metric row:
- **Metric**: The specific SEO factor being evaluated
- **Current Performance**: Objective assessment with data points where applicable
- **Optimization Strategies**: Clear, prioritized, actionable recommendations

Include 8-12 rows covering the most impactful SEO metrics for the platform and niche specified.
```
