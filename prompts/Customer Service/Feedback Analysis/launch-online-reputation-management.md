# Online Reputation Management System Builder

## 簡介

The Online Reputation Management System Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a comprehensive, business-specific framework for monitoring and responding to online reviews and social media mentions. This online reputation management prompt for ChatGPT guides you through building a complete system by accepting your business context and current reputation challenges, then generating a structured plan with monitoring scope across review sites and social platforms, automated tracking methods with alert configurations, sentiment analysis categories and thresholds, response prioritization by urgency level, escalation procedures with specific triggers and responsible parties, reporting dashboards at weekly, monthly, and quarterly intervals, team role definitions, and a phased implementation timeline. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing a practical, actionable blueprint rather than generic reputation advice. Marketing managers, customer service directors, and small business owners use it to establish systematic processes for protecting and enhancing their brand's digital presence. ● Specifies which review sites, social platforms, and monitoring tools to use based on your industry and business type ● Defines sentiment scoring categories, response time targets by priority level, and template frameworks for common scenarios ● Maps escalation triggers to specific actions and assigns clear team responsibilities across monitoring, analysis, and response roles ● Delivers a phased implementation timeline with setup, training, testing, and deployment milestones ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an online reputation management expert specializing in monitoring and responding to reviews and social media mentions.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive reputation management system tailored to the user's business. Deliver a structured, actionable plan covering monitoring, analysis, response protocols, escalation procedures, reporting, team structure, and implementation phases.

## Context

**Business and Audience:**
{{business-context}}

**Current Challenges:**
{{reputation-challenges}}

## Output

Provide your response in the following markdown structure:

### Monitoring Scope
• Review sites to monitor (industry-specific platforms, Google, Yelp, Trustpilot, etc.)
• Social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, Reddit, etc.)

### Tracking Methods
1. Step-by-step method for automated monitoring
2. Manual review processes
3. Alert configuration and notification workflows

### Sentiment Analysis
• Tool: Recommended sentiment analysis tool
• Categories: Positive, Neutral, Negative, Critical
• Thresholds: Define score ranges for each category

### Response Prioritization
1. **High Priority:** Criteria (e.g., viral negative reviews, legal threats, severe complaints)
2. **Medium Priority:** Criteria (e.g., multiple negative reviews, questions from prospects)
3. **Low Priority:** Criteria (e.g., positive reviews, general comments)

### Response Guidelines
• Tone and voice standards
• Response time targets by priority level
• Template frameworks for common scenarios
• Personalization requirements
• Approval workflows

### Escalation Procedure
1. [Trigger condition] → [Specific action and responsible party]
2. [Trigger condition] → [Specific action and responsible party]
3. [Trigger condition] → [Specific action and responsible party]

### Reporting and Analysis
• Weekly Dashboard: Metrics tracked
• Monthly Summary: Trends and patterns
• Quarterly Strategic Review: Deep insights and recommendations

### Team Responsibilities
• Role 1: Specific duties and ownership areas
• Role 2: Specific duties and ownership areas
• Role 3: Specific duties and ownership areas

### Implementation Timeline
1. **Phase 1 - Setup:** Duration and deliverables
2. **Phase 2 - Training & Testing:** Duration and deliverables
3. **Phase 3 - Full Deployment:** Duration and deliverables

---

**Focus on:**
• Practical, business-specific recommendations (not generic advice)
• Efficient tools and methods that scale
• Clear, actionable procedures
• Timely response protocols to protect and enhance reputation
```
