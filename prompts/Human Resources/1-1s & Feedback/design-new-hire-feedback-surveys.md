# New Hire Feedback Survey Design Prompt

## 簡介

The New Hire Feedback Survey Design Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive onboarding surveys measuring reaction, learning, behavior, and business results for HR professionals and organizational psychologists. This new hire feedback survey prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok applies Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Evaluation framework to generate 12-20 structured survey questions across immediate satisfaction, knowledge acquisition, behavioral integration, and business outcomes. You provide your onboarding context, and the prompt produces a markdown table with questions, response formats (Likert scales, open-ended, multiple choice), and recommended timing (day 1, 30, 60, 90 days), plus a strategic rationale explaining how each level predicts retention and performance. Use it when designing onboarding measurement systems that connect early experience to long-term employee success. ● Creates Level 1 Reaction questions that assess emotional responses and satisfaction with the onboarding experience ● Builds Level 2 Learning questions that evaluate knowledge transfer, skill development, and cultural comprehension ● Designs Level 3 Behavior questions that measure real-world application and team integration ● Generates Level 4 Results questions that tie onboarding quality to business outcomes and retention metrics ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an organizational psychologist specializing in onboarding measurement using Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Evaluation (Reaction, Learning, Behavior, Results).

## Task
Design a comprehensive new hire feedback survey that systematically measures onboarding effectiveness across all four Kirkpatrick levels. Create questions that balance immediate satisfaction metrics with predictive indicators of long-term employee success and retention.

## Context
{{onboarding-context}}

Structure questions for each level:
- **Level 1 (Reaction)**: Assess immediate emotional responses and satisfaction with the onboarding experience
- **Level 2 (Learning)**: Evaluate knowledge acquisition, skill development, and comprehension of company culture and processes
- **Level 3 (Behavior)**: Measure application of learned concepts, behavioral changes, and integration into team dynamics
- **Level 4 (Results)**: Connect onboarding effectiveness to business outcomes, productivity metrics, and retention indicators

Tailor question types, tone, and focus areas to align with the company's feedback culture and onboarding objectives.

## Output
Provide a markdown table with these columns:
- **Kirkpatrick Level** (1-4)
- **Question Category** (descriptive label)
- **Sample Questions** (the actual survey question)
- **Response Format** (scale type, open-ended, multiple choice, etc.)
- **Timing** (when to ask: day 1, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days)

Include 3-5 questions per level (12-20 total questions). After the table, add a brief rationale section explaining each level's strategic purpose in measuring onboarding success.
```
