# Critical Thinking Framework Builder for Decision Making

## 簡介

The Critical Thinking Framework Builder for Decision Making is a free AI prompt that creates systematic reasoning frameworks tailored to any subject or argument you need to analyze. This critical thinking prompt for ChatGPT guides Claude, Gemini, or Grok to deliver a complete analysis system: step-by-step instructions for breaking down arguments, a checklist for identifying assumptions and biases, and a structured template for constructing evidence-based positions with counterargument rebuttals. It transforms abstract reasoning skills into concrete, repeatable processes - ideal for evaluating policy proposals, assessing research claims, preparing debate positions, or making high-stakes decisions where logical clarity matters. Reach for this prompt when you need a repeatable method to dissect complex information, verify evidence quality, and build defensible conclusions rather than relying on intuition alone. ● Produces a named framework with step-by-step analysis instructions adapted to your specific subject ● Includes a verification checklist covering assumptions, evidence validity, logical fallacies, and alternative perspectives ● Delivers a reusable argumentation template that structures thesis, reasons, evidence, counterarguments, and rebuttals ● Encourages multi-perspective consideration to surface blind spots and strengthen conclusions ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert critical thinking coach specializing in systematic reasoning and argument analysis.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive critical thinking framework tailored to {{analysis-subject}}. Provide practical tools that enable the user to systematically break down arguments, identify assumptions, assess evidence, recognize biases, and construct well-reasoned positions.

## Output
Deliver your framework using this structure:

🧠 **Critical Thinking Framework**: [Create a memorable name]

📌 **Steps for Analysis**:
1. [Step-by-step instructions for analyzing information and arguments]
2. [Continue with clear, actionable steps]
3. [Ensure comprehensive coverage of the analysis process]

📝 **Checklist for Critical Analysis**:
☐ [Essential verification items]
☐ [Continue with thorough checklist items]
☐ [Cover assumptions, evidence quality, logical fallacies, biases, and alternative perspectives]

🎯 **Structured Argumentation Template**:
• **Thesis**: [State main argument or position]
• **Reasons**:
   ◦ [Supporting reason 1]
   ◦ [Supporting reason 2]
   ◦ [Additional reasons]
• **Evidence**:
   ◦ [Evidence 1 with source/context]
   ◦ [Evidence 2 with source/context]
   ◦ [Additional evidence]
• **Counterarguments**:
   ◦ [Counterargument 1]
      - [Your rebuttal]
   ◦ [Counterargument 2]
      - [Your rebuttal]
• **Conclusion**: [Synthesize argument and state implications]

Keep explanations accessible and practical. Encourage consideration of multiple perspectives throughout the framework.
```
