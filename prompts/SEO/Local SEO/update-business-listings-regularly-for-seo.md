# Business Listing Update Plan for Local SEO

## 簡介

The Business Listing Update Plan for Local SEO is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive action plans for maintaining accurate business information across directories and platforms to improve local search visibility. This business listing prompt for ChatGPT takes your business details and produces a numbered, step-by-step action plan covering all major platforms including Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yelp, Apple Maps, and industry-specific directories. It structures the entire process from initial audit through ongoing maintenance, addressing NAP consistency, platform-specific features like posts and Q&A, review monitoring, and performance tracking. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a logically organized maintenance roadmap that ensures your business information remains accurate and optimized across every local search platform. Ideal for local SEO specialists, digital marketing agencies, and business owners managing multi-platform local presence who need a structured approach to listing optimization and maintenance. ● Creates audit-to-maintenance workflows that cover Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yelp, Apple Maps, and niche directories ● Addresses NAP consistency verification, platform-specific feature utilization, and review response strategies ● Produces numbered action steps organized logically from initial setup through ongoing performance tracking ● Adapts recommendations based on your specific business details and industry context ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a local SEO specialist optimizing business listings across directories and platforms to enhance online visibility and improve local search rankings.

## Task
Create a comprehensive action plan that outlines the process of updating and maintaining business listings across multiple platforms (Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yelp, Apple Maps, industry directories, etc.).

## Context
Business details:
{{business-details}}

Include strategies for:
- Verifying information accuracy across all platforms
- Maintaining NAP (name, address, phone) consistency
- Leveraging platform-specific features (posts, Q&A, attributes, categories, photos)
- Monitoring and responding to reviews
- Tracking performance metrics

## Output
Provide your output as a numbered list, detailing each step of the process for updating and optimizing business listings. Organize the steps logically from audit through ongoing maintenance.
```
