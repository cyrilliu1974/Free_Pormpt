# Wellness Product Ad Panel Generator

## 簡介

The Wellness Product Ad Panel Generator is a free AI prompt that creates sleek three-panel vertical advertisements for smart wellness products and health tech devices. The prompt produces a 9:16 portrait-format layout divided into three distinct commercial photography scenes: a studio hero shot with minimal gradient background, a lifestyle scene showing the product in everyday use, and a macro close-up highlighting smart technology features. This wellness product ad prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana delivers mobile-optimized imagery ideal for Instagram Stories, TikTok ads, and wellness brand campaigns. Reach for this prompt when you need professional-grade product marketing visuals that blend technology aesthetics with wellness lifestyle photography without costly photo shoots. ● Produces studio hero shots with controlled lighting and minimal backgrounds that emphasize product design and brand trust. ● Creates lifestyle scenes showing natural product interaction in wellness environments with balanced natural daylight. ● Generates macro close-ups of smart features with shallow depth of field to showcase technology precision and material quality. ● Maintains consistent modern wellness advertising aesthetic across all three panels with professional commercial photography quality. ## Prompt

```
Create a sleek 1×3 vertical advertisement in 9:16 portrait format for {{product-name}}, emphasizing technology, wellness, and modern daily habits. Ideal for Instagram Stories, Reels, TikTok ads, and mobile-first wellness campaigns.

**Panel 1 – Hero Technology Reveal:**
Studio hero shot of the {{product-name}} in its primary resting position. Soft studio lighting with subtle rim light. Minimal gradient background (cool white fading to pale blue-gray). Sharp focus on the key feature and logo. Mood: innovation, clean design, trust.

**Panel 2 – Everyday Wellness Lifestyle:**
Healthy, relaxed adult using the {{product-name}} in a modern wellness, fitness, or daily routine setting. Natural daylight. Subject interacting naturally with the product. Mood: balance, healthy habits, mindful living.

**Panel 3 – Smart Function Close-Up:**
Macro close-up of the product's {{smart-feature}}, showcasing technology detail and material texture. Shallow depth of field, ultra-clean focus. Mood: precision, technology, reliability.

**Visual Style:**
Professional commercial photography with modern wellness advertising aesthetic. Clean, minimal, high realism. Tech-lifestyle fusion with premium consumer product photography quality. Soft diffused light with natural highlights. Bright, balanced, mobile-optimized exposure with minimal controlled shadows.

**Brand Personality:**
Minimal, intelligent, wellness-focused. Subtle modern tech-inspired logo presentation.

**Overall Mood:**
Fresh, smart, healthy, modern, clean, motivating.

**Avoid:**
Cluttered backgrounds, unreadable indicators, cheap plastic appearance, overly glossy surfaces, harsh shadows, CGI look, cartoon style, oversaturated colors, watermarks, text overlays.
```
