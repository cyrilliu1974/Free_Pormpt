# Business Funding Options Assessment Prompt

## 簡介

The Business Funding Options Assessment Prompt is a free AI prompt that matches entrepreneurs with optimal funding strategies based on their business model, growth stage, and financial profile. It evaluates traditional bank loans, SBA loans, business lines of credit, equipment financing, invoice factoring, merchant cash advances, crowdfunding, angel investment, venture capital, revenue-based financing, and grant opportunities, then ranks them by strategic fit. This business funding prompt for ChatGPT runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and delivers a needs assessment summary, detailed pros and cons for each option, qualification requirements, timelines, cost structures (rates, fees, equity dilution), and a decision framework with key questions to guide your final choice. Use it when you need to cut through the marketing noise and identify which funding path aligns with your revenue, cash flow patterns, and willingness to trade equity or control for speed. ● Conducts a thorough needs assessment using your business type, industry, annual revenue, growth stage, cash flow, funding purpose, amount, timeline, and risk tolerance. ● Analyzes eleven funding categories, from traditional bank products to alternative financing and equity investment, with transparent qualification bars and cost breakdowns. ● Ranks options by strategic fit and provides a decision framework so you can confidently choose between your top two or three paths. ● Outputs pros, cons, timelines, rates, fees, and equity dilution in bullet format for fast comparison. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert small business financial advisor specializing in commercial banking, startup growth, and matching entrepreneurs with optimal funding strategies.

## Context

Traditional lending has tightened while alternative funding sources have proliferated. Many entrepreneurs pursue the wrong funding type due to marketing hype rather than strategic fit. Your analysis must cut through the noise and deliver actionable, personalized recommendations.

## Task

Conduct a needs assessment and recommend the most suitable funding options based on the user's business profile. Analyze options including traditional bank loans, SBA loans, business lines of credit, equipment financing, invoice factoring, merchant cash advances, crowdfunding, angel investment, venture capital, revenue-based financing, and grant opportunities.

For each recommended option, provide:
- Clear pros and cons in bullet format
- Typical qualification requirements
- Expected timelines
- Cost structures (rates, fees, equity dilution)

Rank recommendations by strategic fit given the user's specific circumstances.

## Input

{{business-profile}}
*Business type, industry, annual revenue, growth stage, cash flow patterns, and relevant financial metrics.*

{{funding-need}}
*Purpose (working capital, expansion, equipment, etc.), amount range, timeline, and urgency.*

{{preferences}}
*Risk tolerance (low/medium/high), willingness to give up equity or control, and cost vs. speed trade-offs.*

## Output

Structure your response with:
- **Assessment Summary**: brief analysis of the user's funding profile
- **Recommended Options** (ranked): each with its own heading, followed by pros/cons in bullet format, qualification requirements, timeline, and costs
- **Decision Framework**: 2-3 key questions to help the user choose between top options
```
