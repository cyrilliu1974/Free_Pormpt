# Identify Problematic Evaluation Phrases

## 簡介

The Identify Problematic Evaluation Phrases prompt is a free AI prompt that helps managers and HR professionals spot and replace language patterns in performance reviews that damage employee motivation and trust. This problematic evaluation phrases prompt for ChatGPT analyzes seven common but harmful expressions used in appraisals, detailing the psychological impact, ambiguity, or bias each phrase carries, then provides a constructive alternative that preserves honesty while maintaining employee dignity. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is especially useful when auditing evaluation templates, coaching managers on feedback skills, or standardizing review language across a team. Managers preparing year-end reviews, HR leads refining appraisal systems, and organizational psychologists consulting on performance culture will find it immediately actionable. ● Exposes phrases that sound professional but create psychological barriers to improvement or signal unintended bias. ● Explains the specific communication breakdown behind each problematic phrase, from ambiguity to blame-shifting. ● Delivers clear, actionable replacement language that managers can adopt immediately in written and verbal feedback. ● Supports legal and ethical evaluation practices by identifying expressions that carry vulnerability or mixed messages. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert HR consultant and organizational psychologist specializing in performance management systems and employee motivation.

## Task
Identify seven specific phrases commonly used in performance evaluations that undermine employee engagement. For each phrase:
- Explain why it is problematic (psychological impact, ambiguity, legal vulnerability, or demotivating effect)
- Provide a constructive alternative that maintains honesty while preserving dignity and motivation

Focus on language that creates psychological barriers to improvement, shifts blame inappropriately, provides no actionable feedback, or inadvertently signals bias.

## Context
{{management-context}}

Performance evaluations are critical touchpoints that either build or break employee confidence. Many managers unknowingly use phrases that sound professional but demotivate employees, create ambiguity, or send mixed messages about expectations and growth opportunities.

## Output
Provide a numbered list (1-7) with this structure for each phrase:

1. **Problematic phrase**: "[exact phrase]"
   - **Why it fails**: [psychological impact and communication breakdown]
   - **Better alternative**: "[constructive replacement]" — [why this works better]
```
