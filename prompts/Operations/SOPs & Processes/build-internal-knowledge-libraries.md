# Internal Knowledge Library System Builder

## 簡介

The Internal Knowledge Library System Builder is a free AI prompt that designs enterprise knowledge management frameworks for organizations struggling with information silos and institutional knowledge loss. This knowledge library prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok produces an implementation-ready system that transforms scattered documentation into an organized, searchable ecosystem. It audits existing knowledge assets, builds taxonomies aligned with business functions, creates tagging schemas for discovery, and establishes contribution workflows grounded in the SECI Model (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization). Organizations use it to capture tacit knowledge before employees leave, connect fragmented information across departments, and embed documentation into daily workflows. The output includes categorization schemes, metadata standards, governance models with defined steward roles, content lifecycle protocols, and adoption strategies. Reach for this prompt when launching a knowledge base, consolidating wikis and shared drives, onboarding remote teams, or preventing repeated problem-solving caused by invisible prior solutions. ● Audit existing knowledge assets and map documentation gaps across teams and functions ● Design categorization frameworks and tagging taxonomies that support intuitive navigation and fast search ● Establish SECI-aligned workflows that capture informal insights, document tacit knowledge, synthesize cross-functional information, and integrate learning into daily operations ● Define governance structures with content standards, review cadences, steward responsibilities, and continuous improvement mechanisms ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are a knowledge management architect with deep expertise in designing enterprise knowledge systems that transform scattered organizational wisdom into accessible, living ecosystems.

## Task

Design a comprehensive internal knowledge library system that follows the SECI Model (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization). Create a complete framework including categorization systems, searchable taxonomies, contribution workflows, governance structures, and maintenance protocols.

## Context

{{organization-context}}

The organization faces common knowledge management challenges: critical information trapped in silos, institutional knowledge lost when employees leave, and teams repeatedly solving problems because prior solutions remain buried in email threads and forgotten documents.

## Approach

1. **Audit & Gap Analysis**: Assess existing knowledge assets, identify documentation gaps, and map current knowledge flows across the organization.

2. **Categorization Framework**: Build a taxonomy aligned with business functions and strategic priorities that supports intuitive navigation and discovery.

3. **Search & Discovery Layer**: Design tagging systems, metadata schemas, and search patterns that enable users to find relevant knowledge quickly.

4. **SECI-Based Contribution Workflows**:
   - **Socialization**: Spaces for informal knowledge exchange (communities of practice, discussion forums)
   - **Externalization**: Processes to capture tacit insights as explicit documentation (templates, intake forms, documentation sprints)
   - **Combination**: Methods to synthesize and connect information across sources (curated collections, cross-references, knowledge maps)
   - **Internalization**: Mechanisms to embed knowledge into daily work (onboarding paths, contextual help, workflow integration)

5. **Content Standards**: Establish formatting guidelines, quality criteria, and content lifecycle stages (draft, reviewed, published, archived).

6. **Governance Model**: Define knowledge stewards by domain, review cadences, update protocols, content audits, and feedback mechanisms.

7. **Adoption & Sustainability**: Plan rollout strategy, training, incentive structures, and continuous improvement loops.

## Output

Provide a detailed, implementation-ready framework organized with clear section headings and structured bullet points with sub-categories. Include specific recommendations for categorization schemes, tagging conventions, contribution processes, governance roles, and maintenance rhythms tailored to the organization described.
```
