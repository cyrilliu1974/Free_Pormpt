# Course Pricing Strategy Generator for Online Educators

## 簡介

The Course Pricing Strategy Generator for Online Educators is a free AI prompt that builds multi-dimensional pricing strategies for digital education products, balancing revenue goals with market accessibility. This course pricing strategy prompt for ChatGPT walks you through audience willingness-to-pay analysis, tiered pricing models with differentiated feature sets, promotional mechanics including launch offers and bundles, payment plan options, competitor positioning, and A/B testing frameworks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering structured recommendations with strategic rationale and revenue projections for each tier. Course creators use it to price launches, evaluate subscription versus one-time payment models, design early-bird offers, and position against competitors while maintaining enrollment accessibility. Reach for this prompt when launching a new course, repricing an existing product, or exploring bundle and payment-plan configurations. ● Analyzes target audience price sensitivity and willingness to pay for each tier ● Designs tiered pricing with clear feature differentiation and buyer personas ● Recommends promotional mechanics including launch discounts, bundles, referral programs, and loyalty incentives ● Evaluates payment options such as one-time purchase, installments, and subscription models ● Provides competitive positioning analysis with market benchmarks and differentiation rationale ● Includes revenue projections with conversion rate scenarios and A/B testing recommendations ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert pricing strategist specializing in digital education products.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive pricing strategy for an online course that maximizes revenue while maintaining accessibility. Provide specific recommendations across multiple pricing dimensions.

## Context
**Course & Market:**
{{course-details}}

**Financial Parameters:**
{{financial-targets}}

## Process
1. Analyze target audience willingness to pay and price sensitivity
2. Develop tiered pricing with differentiated value propositions
3. Design promotional mechanics (discounts, bundles, early-bird offers)
4. Evaluate subscription models and payment plan options
5. Assess competitor positioning and market benchmarks
6. Propose price testing and optimization strategies
7. Create retention mechanisms (referral discounts, loyalty programs)

## Output
Structure your pricing strategy with these sections, each containing specific recommendations and rationale:

**Pricing Tiers**
- List each tier with price point, included features, and target buyer

**Promotional Strategy**
- Launch offers, seasonal discounts, bundle configurations

**Payment Options**
- One-time vs. installment vs. subscription models

**Market Positioning**
- Competitive analysis and differentiation rationale

**Revenue Projections**
- Expected conversion rates and revenue scenarios by tier

**Testing & Optimization Plan**
- A/B test recommendations and success metrics

For each recommendation, explain the strategic rationale and expected impact on revenue and accessibility.
```
