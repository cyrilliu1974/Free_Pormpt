# Email Subject Line Generator for High Open Rates

## 簡介

The Email Subject Line Generator for High Open Rates is a free AI prompt that creates compelling, conversion-focused subject lines tailored to your product, audience, and campaign goals. This email subject line prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your unique selling proposition, target audience preferences, and brand voice to produce 10 subject lines under 60 characters. Each line employs proven psychological triggers - urgency, curiosity, personalization, social proof, or exclusivity - and includes a tactical explanation so you understand why it works. Compatible with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it helps email marketers, growth teams, and founders craft subject lines that cut through inbox clutter and drive measurable engagement. Use it when launching campaigns, A/B testing messaging, or refining your email marketing strategy. ● Produces 10 subject lines optimized for brevity and impact, each under 60 characters when possible ● Explains the psychological trigger behind every line - urgency, curiosity, personalization, benefit-driven language, or exclusivity ● Adapts tone and messaging to match your specified brand voice and campaign goal ● Considers audience preferences, product strengths, and market trends to maximize relevance and open rates ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert email marketer specializing in high-performing subject lines that maximize open rates and conversions.

## Task
Create 10 compelling email subject lines tailored to the campaign parameters below. Each subject line should employ proven tactics—power words, urgency, curiosity, personalization, or social proof—to immediately capture attention and drive opens.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}
**Target Audience:** {{target-audience}}
**Unique Selling Proposition:** {{usp}}
**Campaign Goal:** {{campaign-goal}}
**Brand Voice:** {{brand-voice}}

Analyze the audience's preferences, the product's strengths, and current market trends. Structure your language for clarity and impact, ensuring each subject line is concise (under 60 characters when possible) and aligned with the brand voice.

## Output
Deliver a numbered list of 10 subject lines. After each subject line, include a brief parenthetical explanation of the psychological trigger or tactic used (e.g., urgency, curiosity, personalization, benefit-driven, exclusivity).

**Example format:**
1. Your [benefit] starts now—here's how (urgency + benefit-driven)
2. [Name], we found something perfect for you (personalization + curiosity)
```
