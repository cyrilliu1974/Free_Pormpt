# SEO Keyword Research Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The SEO Keyword Research Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete keyword research plan for businesses looking to improve their organic search visibility. This keyword research prompt for ChatGPT takes a product or service overview and generates a layered strategy: five foundational seed keywords, five long-tail keyword suggestions complete with estimated search volume and intent classification (commercial, informational, navigational, or transactional), and three actionable content strategy recommendations. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, and is designed for marketers, content teams, and business owners who need a structured framework rather than a raw list of keywords. The prompt ensures balanced coverage across all four intent types so your content roadmap aligns with every stage of the buyer journey. ● Generates five broad seed keywords and five detailed long-tail phrases with search volume estimates and intent labels. ● Classifies each keyword by intent type - commercial, informational, navigational, or transactional - so you match content to audience needs. ● Provides three concrete content strategy tips covering keyword integration, content optimization, and performance tracking. ● Structures output as a reusable strategy document that marketing teams can share, iterate on, and execute against. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert keyword researcher and SEO strategist.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive keyword research strategy for the given product or service, focusing on high-potential long-tail keywords that align with target audience intent.

## Context
**Product/Service Details:**
{{product-service-overview}}

Provide a complete picture: what the product or service is, who the target audience is, and the primary benefit or unique selling point.

## Output
Deliver your strategy in the following structure:

**Product/Service Overview:**
- Product/Service: [Description]
- Target Audience: [Specification]
- Primary Benefit: [Main value proposition]

**Seed Keywords (5 broad terms):**
1. [Keyword]
2. [Keyword]
3. [Keyword]
4. [Keyword]
5. [Keyword]

**Long-Tail Keyword Suggestions (5 keywords with data):**

For each keyword, include:
- Keyword: [Specific long-tail phrase]
- Search Volume: [Estimated monthly searches]
- Keyword Intent: [Commercial/Informational/Navigational/Transactional]

Ensure a balanced mix across all four intent types.

**Content Strategy Tips (3 actionable recommendations):**
1. [How to leverage keywords in content creation]
2. [How to optimize existing content around keywords]
3. [How to track keyword performance and iterate]

Focus on actionable tactics that drive measurable results.
```
