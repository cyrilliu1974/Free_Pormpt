# Ethical Conduct Guidelines Generator

## 簡介

The Ethical Conduct Guidelines Generator is a free AI prompt that creates OECD-aligned internal ethics frameworks for organizations managing sector-specific compliance and integrity challenges. This ethical conduct guidelines prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete framework structured around OECD principles including human rights, anti-bribery, transparency, and fairness. By inputting your organizational context and identified ethical risks, the prompt produces core principles, specific policies for your risk areas, practical decision-making tools employees can use daily, phased implementation steps with training plans, and accountability measures including escalation procedures and recognition systems. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok to deliver output ready for leadership review and employee rollout. Reach for this prompt when your organization needs to move beyond checkbox compliance toward a culture of ethical awareness grounded in international best practices. ● Structures guidelines around OECD pillars with transparency, anti-bribery, human rights, and fairness as foundational elements. ● Produces practical decision-making frameworks and escalation procedures employees can apply in real-world scenarios. ● Delivers phased implementation strategies that foster ethical awareness rather than mechanical compliance. ● Includes accountability mechanisms, monitoring systems, consequences for violations, and recognition programs for ethical leadership. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert corporate ethics consultant and compliance strategist specializing in OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises.

## Task

Create comprehensive internal ethical conduct guidelines aligned with OECD principles. The guidelines must be practical for daily decision-making yet robust enough to withstand regulatory scrutiny and public examination.

## Context

{{organizational-context}}

{{ethical-risks}}

Structure the guidelines around the core OECD pillars of integrity, human rights, and responsible business practices, incorporating transparency, anti-bribery, and fairness as foundational elements. Include practical decision-making frameworks employees can apply in real-world scenarios, with escalation procedures and accountability mechanisms. Develop implementation strategies that foster a culture of ethical awareness rather than mere compliance, with clear consequences and recognition systems that reinforce desired behaviors.

## Output

Provide the guidelines in a comprehensive framework format with clear section headings:

- **Core Principles**: Foundational ethical commitments aligned with OECD guidelines
- **Specific Policies**: Detailed guidance for key ethical risk areas identified in the organizational context
- **Decision-Making Frameworks**: Practical tools and escalation procedures for real-world scenarios
- **Implementation Steps**: Phased rollout plan with training, communication, and integration strategies
- **Accountability Measures**: Monitoring mechanisms, consequences for violations, and recognition systems for ethical leadership
```
