# Fisheye Boardwalk Portrait Generator

## 簡介

The Fisheye Boardwalk Portrait Generator is a free AI prompt that produces dynamic street photography portraits with dramatic fisheye lens distortion for photographers, marketers, and content creators. This fisheye portrait prompt for Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana creates full-body portraits shot from a low angle with an ultra-wide 12mm lens on a sunny boardwalk. The prompt renders distinct barrel distortion, wooden plank surfaces, colorful background stalls, hard summer sunlight, and sharp shadows. You customize the subject description and outfit details while the prompt maintains the authentic street photography aesthetic - relaxed poses, one hand in pocket, chunky blue sunglasses, and blurred boardwalk activity in the distance. Perfect for fashion lookbooks, lifestyle brand campaigns, social media content, and editorial photography that needs energetic summer vibes without expensive location shoots or specialized lenses. ● Produces ultra-wide 12mm fisheye lens effects with realistic barrel distortion that bends the frame edges naturally. ● Customizable subject descriptions and outfit details while maintaining consistent boardwalk setting, hard sunlight, and street photography composition. ● Generates low-angle shots looking up at the subject with wooden planks, colorful stalls, and clear blue sky in proper perspective. ● Delivers full-body portraits with relaxed, natural poses and summery, energetic mood suitable for fashion, lifestyle, and brand storytelling. ## Prompt

