# Analyze Churn Patterns Prompt for Customer Success

## 簡介

The Analyze Churn Patterns Prompt for Customer Success is a free AI prompt that identifies support-driven causes of customer attrition and delivers implementable solutions across three time horizons for customer success teams and support leaders. This churn analysis prompt for ChatGPT examines the full support journey to surface six critical friction points that increase churn probability, then prescribes immediate actions deployable within a week, process improvements for the first month, and systemic long-term prevention strategies. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, taking your business context as input and returning segmented solutions that account for how different customer types experience support issues differently. Use it when retention data points to support quality problems, after launching a new product tier, or quarterly to audit friction in ticketing, response time, communication clarity, and resolution workflows. ● Surfaces six churn drivers ranked by impact, each with root-cause explanations and symptom identification. ● Delivers three tiers of solutions: one-week quick wins, one-month process upgrades, and systemic long-term changes. ● Includes implementation timelines and resource requirements so teams know staffing, training, and tooling needs upfront. ● Adapts recommendations to customer segment differences, recognizing that enterprise and self-serve users churn for different reasons. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert customer success strategist specializing in churn prevention through support excellence.

## Task
Identify the 6 most critical support-driven churn reasons and provide implementable solutions to reduce customer attrition. For each churn reason, deliver:

- Immediate actions (deployable within 1 week)
- Process improvements (deployable within 1 month)
- Long-term prevention strategies (systemic changes)
- Implementation timelines and resource requirements

Focus on friction points across the entire support journey—from initial contact through resolution—addressing both symptoms and root causes.

## Context
{{business-context}}

Consider how customer segments experience support differently and how timing, communication style, and resolution speed impact churn probability. Provide solutions that work with existing resources while building toward more sophisticated support systems.

## Output
Structure your analysis with:

**For each of the 6 churn reasons:**
- Clear heading naming the churn pattern
- Why this drives churn (brief explanation)
- Immediate actions (bullet points)
- Process improvements (bullet points)
- Long-term prevention strategies (bullet points)
- Implementation timeline estimate
- Resource requirements

Prioritize actionability and specificity over theory.
```
