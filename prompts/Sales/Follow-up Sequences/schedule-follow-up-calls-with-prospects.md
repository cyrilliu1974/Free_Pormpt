# Sales Follow-Up Call Script Generator for ChatGPT

## 簡介

The Sales Follow-Up Call Script Generator is a free AI prompt that creates customized telephone scripts for sales professionals preparing prospect follow-up calls. This sales follow-up prompt for ChatGPT takes your product or service details, interaction history, and unique selling points to produce a three-part call framework: a warm introduction that references previous conversations, discussion points aligned to prospect needs and objections, and closing remarks with clear next-step scheduling language. The script emphasizes conversational flow over rigid word-for-word reading, with built-in prompts for active listening and real-time adaptation. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Sales representatives use it to prepare for discovery calls, demo follow-ups, proposal discussions, and objection-handling conversations where rapport and personalization matter. ● Builds introduction language that references past interactions and sets a clear agenda for the call ● Generates discussion points tied to product benefits, prospect needs, and anticipated objections ● Creates closing remarks with actionable next steps and natural scheduling language ● Formats output as a conversational guide with flexibility for real-time prospect responses ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional sales representative preparing for follow-up calls with prospects.

## Task
Create a structured call script that engages prospects, discusses their interest in the product/service, addresses concerns, and schedules next steps in a friendly, conversational manner.

## Context
**Product/Service:** {{product-service}}

**Previous Interactions:** {{interaction-history}}

**Unique Selling Points:** {{usp}}

Use this information to build rapport, gather information about prospect needs and pain points, and advance the sales conversation naturally.

## Output
Provide a call script with three clearly defined sections:

1. **Introduction** – warm opening that references previous interaction and sets the agenda
2. **Discussion Points** – key talking points tied to prospect needs, product benefits, and addressing likely concerns or questions
3. **Closing Remarks** – summary of value discussed, clear call-to-action for next steps, and scheduling language

Format the script conversationally, not as a rigid word-for-word transcript. Include prompts for active listening and adapting to prospect responses.
```
