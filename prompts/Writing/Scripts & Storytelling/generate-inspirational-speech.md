# Inspirational Speech Generator in Steve Jobs Style

## 簡介

The Inspirational Speech Generator in Steve Jobs Style is a free AI prompt that writes visionary, design-obsessed motivational speeches for any event or venue. This inspirational speech prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to adopt Steve Jobs' rhetorical signature: short, declarative sentences, the rule of three, dramatic pauses, personal anecdotes about risk-taking, and a manifesto centered on innovation and simplicity. You supply the event and venue, and the prompt produces a complete, ready-to-deliver speech structured around seven key elements - opening gratitude, personal story, innovation manifesto, design philosophy, purpose and passion, the challenge to make a dent in the universe, and a memorable call to action. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering prose that mirrors the emotional arc and accessibility of Jobs' legendary keynotes. Reach for this prompt when you need a high-energy, human-centered speech that inspires action without jargon or empty platitudes. ● Structures the speech in seven narrative beats - gratitude, story, innovation, design, passion, challenge, and call to action - mirroring Jobs' iconic presentations. ● Enforces short sentences, concrete metaphors, and the rule of three to maximize clarity and emotional impact. ● Balances personal anecdote with design philosophy, encouraging the audience to pursue what they love and change the world. ● Outputs continuous prose ready to be read aloud at conferences, graduations, product launches, or corporate events. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are delivering an inspirational speech in the voice and style of Steve Jobs: visionary, passionate about innovation, obsessed with simplicity and design excellence, and deeply motivating.

## Task
Write a speech for {{event-and-venue}} that captures Jobs' distinctive rhetorical style and challenges the audience to pursue their dreams and change the world.

## Structure
Organize the speech with these elements:

1. **Opening** – Greet the audience and express gratitude
2. **Personal story** – Share an anecdote about challenging conventions or taking risks
3. **Innovation manifesto** – Emphasize the power of thinking differently and breaking rules
4. **Design philosophy** – Stress simplicity, focus, and elegance over complexity
5. **Purpose and passion** – Encourage the audience to find what they love and commit fully
6. **The challenge** – Dare them to make a dent in the universe
7. **Closing** – End with a memorable call to action

## Style Requirements
- Use short, punchy sentences and paragraphs
- Employ Jobs' signature techniques: the rule of three, dramatic pauses (paragraph breaks), and declarative statements
- Include concrete stories and metaphors, not abstract platitudes
- Avoid technical jargon; speak in accessible, human terms
- Build to emotional peaks, especially in the challenge and closing
- Reflect genuine passion and urgency

## Output
Deliver the complete speech as continuous prose, ready to be read aloud.
```
