# Training Communication Plan Prompt

## 簡介

The Training Communication Plan Prompt is a free AI prompt that builds comprehensive rollout communication strategies for organizations implementing training initiatives in high-stakes environments with stakeholder resistance. This training communication plan prompt for ChatGPT applies Kotter's 8-Step Change Management Model to produce structured plans covering urgency creation, coalition building, vision messaging, barrier removal, and sustained engagement. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, accepting inputs for communication channels, audience segments, training objectives, rollout timeline, and anticipated resistance points to generate tailored messaging strategies for each stakeholder group. Organizations use it when launching enterprise training programs that require buy-in across multiple departments, managing previous rollout failures, or navigating conflicting priorities and tight leadership timelines. Change management consultants, L&D leaders, and training directors reach for this prompt when they need audience-specific communication tactics, message templates, and measurement frameworks that address real objections and drive adoption. ● Maps communication tactics to each of Kotter's 8 steps with specific timelines tied to your rollout schedule ● Produces audience-tailored talking points that address segment-specific concerns, motivations, and communication preferences ● Delivers message templates and tone guidelines for announcements, updates, and follow-ups across all available channels ● Includes engagement tactics, measurement KPIs, feedback mechanisms, and adjustment protocols to iterate based on stakeholder response ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an organizational change management consultant specializing in training rollouts for large enterprises facing resistance and stakeholder skepticism.

## Task

Develop a comprehensive training communication plan using Kotter's 8-Step Change Management Model that maximizes buy-in, minimizes resistance, and ensures adoption through strategic messaging and engagement.

## Context

The organization has:
- {{communication-channels}} as available channels
- {{audience-segments}} as key stakeholder groups requiring tailored messaging
- {{training-objectives}} to achieve through this rollout
- {{rollout-timeline}} for the complete training initiative
- {{resistance-points}} as anticipated challenges or objections

This is a high-stakes environment where training initiatives have previously struggled due to poor communication, conflicting audience priorities, and tight leadership timelines demanding measurable engagement.

## Output

Structure your plan following Kotter's 8 Steps with clear headings:

1. **Create Urgency** – communication tactics to establish why the training matters now
2. **Build the Guiding Coalition** – identifying and mobilizing influencers and champions
3. **Form a Strategic Vision** – crafting the compelling training vision and messaging
4. **Enlist a Volunteer Army** – strategies to generate grassroots momentum
5. **Enable Action by Removing Barriers** – addressing resistance points proactively
6. **Generate Short-Term Wins** – early success communication and celebration
7. **Sustain Acceleration** – maintaining engagement through the rollout
8. **Institute Change** – embedding training outcomes in organizational culture

For each step, provide:

- **Timeline**: When to execute (mapped to the rollout schedule)
- **Audience-Specific Messages**: Tailored talking points for each segment addressing their concerns, motivations, and communication preferences
- **Channel Strategy**: Which channels to use and why
- **Message Templates**: Customizable announcement and communication drafts
- **Tone Guidelines**: How to balance professionalism, enthusiasm, and empathy
- **Engagement Tactics**: Specific activities to drive participation
- **Measurement**: KPIs to track communication effectiveness and feedback mechanisms
- **Adjustment Protocols**: How to iterate based on stakeholder response

Format all strategies as actionable bullet points with specific details for immediate implementation.
```
