# Community Engagement Strategy Generator

## 簡介

The Community Engagement Strategy Generator is a free AI prompt that designs participatory content formats for community managers, platform owners, and engagement strategists building active contributor networks. This community engagement prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok analyzes your community context and produces seven practical content ideas - each with participation methods, implementation steps, success metrics, and recognition systems. Use it when launching a new platform, reviving a stagnant forum, or shifting your audience from consumers to co-creators. Real applications include member-sourced tutorials, peer mentorship programs, collaborative problem-solving threads, and user-generated resource libraries. Reach for this prompt when you need to lower participation barriers while maintaining content quality, or when designing scalable formats that reward contributors and strengthen community bonds. ● Produces participation methods tailored to your community's skill levels, interests, and collaboration style. ● Defines concrete implementation steps, moderation guidelines, and scalability considerations for each content format. ● Identifies success metrics that measure both contribution volume and the quality of knowledge shared. ● Recommends recognition systems that acknowledge valuable contributors and encourage ongoing participation. ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an expert community engagement strategist and content architect specializing in participatory platforms that transform passive audiences into active contributors and co-creators.

## Task

Identify **7 innovative community-driven content ideas** that empower users to share expertise, solve real problems, and build collective knowledge. Each idea must be practical, engaging, and designed to make participation feel rewarding and impactful.

## Context

{{community-context}}

Analyze this information to identify content opportunities where user contributions create genuine value. Design formats that lower participation barriers while maintaining quality. Consider scalability, moderation load, and how each idea strengthens community connections. Prioritize content types that encourage knowledge sharing, peer learning, and collaborative problem-solving.

## Output

For each of the 7 content ideas, provide:

### [Concept Name]
- **Participation Method**: How users contribute and engage
- **User Empowerment Benefits**: What value participants gain
- **Implementation Steps**: Concrete bullet points for launch and operation
- **Success Metrics**: How to measure impact and quality
- **Recognition Methods**: How valuable contributions are acknowledged

Structure each idea for maximum clarity and actionability.
```
