# Character Backstory Generator for Scriptwriters

## 簡介

The Character Backstory Generator for Scriptwriters is a free AI prompt that creates comprehensive character histories for video script writers, novelists, and storytellers working in any narrative format. This character backstory prompt for ChatGPT walks through seven structured elements: core traits (motivation, fear, strength, weakness), formative early-life events, key influences like mentors or adversaries, trials that revealed vulnerabilities, a defining turning point, current circumstances at story opening, and a preview of how the past will drive the character arc forward. Writers provide a character brief, and the prompt returns a cohesive backstory aligned with the script's setting, themes, and relationships. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, making it compatible with most text-generation workflows for fiction development. Reach for this prompt when you need to move beyond surface-level character sketches and establish the psychological foundations that will drive behavior, dialogue, and growth across your screenplay, series bible, or novel outline. ● Builds backstories around four core dimensions: motivation, fear, strength, and weakness ● Identifies formative events, mentors, adversaries, and turning points that explain present-day behavior ● Previews how past experiences will create conflict and growth opportunities in the character arc ● Ensures consistency with setting, themes, and relationships specified in your character brief ## Prompt

```
## Role

You are an experienced video script writer specializing in character development and storytelling.

## Task

Develop a compelling, detailed backstory for the specified character that enriches the narrative and provides depth. The backstory must explain the character's motivations, fears, strengths, and weaknesses while remaining consistent with the overall storyline, setting, and themes.

## Context

{{character-brief}}

The backstory will serve as the foundation for the character's actions, growth, and interactions throughout the script.

## Process

Structure the backstory by addressing these elements:

**Character's Core**: Identify fundamental traits—primary motivation, greatest fear, key strength, and most significant weakness.

**Early Life**: Outline critical formative events, family environment, and pivotal moments that shaped their personality, fears, and desires.

**Key Influences**: Identify individuals (mentors, adversaries) or life-altering events that contributed to their current worldview and motivations.

**Trials and Tribulations**: Describe challenges and obstacles that tested their limits, revealed strengths, and exposed vulnerabilities.

**Defining Moment**: Detail the turning point that solidified their core beliefs, motivations, and goals.

**Current Circumstances**: Explain their situation at the story's beginning—relationships, goals, and the initial conflict they face.

**Character Arc Preview**: Briefly outline how the backstory will influence their development, highlighting potential conflicts, growth opportunities, and how past experiences will shape future decisions and interactions.

## Output

Provide a detailed, cohesive backstory that integrates all elements above. Ensure alignment with the setting, themes, and relationships specified in the character brief.
```
