# Minimalist Portrait Generator for Haute Couture Style

## 簡介

The Minimalist Portrait Generator for Haute Couture Style is a free AI prompt that creates fashion-editorial portraits with studio lighting and dramatic contrast for photographers, designers, and visual artists. This minimalist portrait prompt for Midjourney produces isolated subjects in haute couture garments against bright backgrounds, blending dark mystique with cinematic lighting for a high-fashion editorial aesthetic. You customize the subject description, garment color, and aspect ratio to generate portfolio-ready portrait concepts. Use it for mood boards, client pitches, fashion lookbooks, or creative exploration when you need polished, studio-quality portrait compositions without a full photoshoot. The prompt works across Midjourney, Flux, and Nano Banana image models.
