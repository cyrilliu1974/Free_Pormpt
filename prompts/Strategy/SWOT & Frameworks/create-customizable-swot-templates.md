# SWOT Analysis Template Generator for Any Profession

## 簡介

The SWOT Analysis Template Generator for Any Profession is a free AI prompt that creates tailored strategic planning frameworks for professionals across any field. This SWOT analysis prompt for ChatGPT builds a complete 2×2 table template populated with 3-5 thought-provoking questions in each quadrant - Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, delivering an introduction that explains the value of SWOT for the target profession, profession-specific market and regulatory considerations, and a conclusion on translating findings into action. Business consultants use it to prepare client workshops, career coaches deploy it for professional development sessions, and individual contributors rely on it for annual self-assessments and strategic reviews. ● Outputs a structured 2×2 table with targeted questions for competitive position, internal gaps, market trends, and external risks ● Includes 2-3 unique factors specific to the profession - industry regulations, emerging technologies, or market dynamics ● Provides an introduction explaining SWOT value and a conclusion on converting insights into actionable strategies ● Designed for easy customization while maintaining depth and relevance to the chosen field ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert business consultant specializing in strategic planning tools tailored to specific professions.

## Task
Create a customizable SWOT analysis template in 2×2 table format for {{profession}}. Design the template with thought-provoking questions and profession-specific considerations that guide thorough self-assessment and strategic planning.

## Structure

**Introduction**
Open with a brief explanation of how SWOT analysis benefits professionals in this field and what strategic insights it unlocks.

**SWOT Table (2×2 format)**

For each quadrant—Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats—provide 3-5 guiding questions or prompts that encourage deep, honest reflection on:
- Current competitive position and capabilities
- Internal limitations and gaps
- Emerging market trends and growth avenues
- External risks and industry challenges

**Profession-Specific Considerations**
List 2-3 unique factors, trends, or regulatory/market dynamics particular to {{profession}} that should inform the analysis.

**Conclusion**
Summarize how to translate SWOT findings into actionable strategies and next steps.

## Output Requirements
- Use clear bullet points and table formatting for readability
- Tailor all questions to {{profession}}—avoid generic business prompts
- Keep the template comprehensive yet concise for easy customization
- Focus questions on actionable insights rather than surface-level observations
```
