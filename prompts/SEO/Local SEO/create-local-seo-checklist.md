# Local SEO Checklist Generator for Any Business

## 簡介

The Local SEO Checklist Generator for Any Business is a free AI prompt that creates a customized local search optimization plan for businesses in any industry and geographic market. This local SEO prompt for ChatGPT takes two inputs - your business type and location - and produces a structured five-part checklist covering foundational optimizations, on-page tactics, content strategy, link building and citations, plus reviews and reputation management. Each section includes three to four recommended actions (marked with checkmarks) and one practice to avoid (marked with X), tailored to your specific industry and local market. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering a practical implementation guide you can follow immediately. Use it when launching a new location, auditing an existing local presence, or onboarding clients who need higher rankings in Google's local pack and Maps results. ● Produces five organized checklist sections - foundation, on-page, content, links and citations, reviews - each with concrete next steps ● Tailors every recommendation to the business type and geographic location provided, avoiding generic advice ● Flags common mistakes to avoid in each category, helping teams prevent penalties and wasted effort ● Closes with a summary paragraph on consistent implementation, progress tracking, and ongoing adaptation for sustained local search growth ## Prompt
