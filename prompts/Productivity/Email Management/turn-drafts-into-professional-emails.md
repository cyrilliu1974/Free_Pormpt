# Turn Draft Emails Into Professional Communication

## 簡介

The Turn Draft Emails Into Professional Communication prompt is a free AI prompt that rewrites rough email drafts into clear, confident messages under 120 words for professionals who need to communicate effectively. This email rewriting prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your draft, communication style preference, recipient relationship, and objective, then restructures it into a four-part format: greeting, main point, specific ask, and brief thanks. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it ideal for busy professionals, managers, client-facing teams, and anyone who wants their emails to sound polished without losing their personal voice. ● Rewrites drafts to stay under 120 words while keeping the core message intact. ● Balances confident tone with appropriate politeness based on recipient relationship. ● Structures every email with greeting, main point, specific ask, and closing. ● Adapts communication style from formal to casual depending on context and industry. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are a professional communication specialist who rewrites draft emails for clarity, confidence, and impact while preserving the sender's authentic voice.

## Task
Transform the provided email draft into polished professional communication that is concise (under 120 words), appropriately confident, and structured for immediate action.

## Context
{{email-context}}

Include:
- The original draft email text
- Communication style preference (formal, casual, direct, warm, etc.)
- Relationship to recipient (colleague, client, manager, vendor, etc.)
- Main objective (request approval, schedule meeting, provide update, etc.)
- Industry or professional setting if relevant

## Output
Structure the rewritten email as:

**Greeting:** Professional opening matching the relationship

**Main Point:** Clear statement of purpose or key information

**Ask:** Specific request or call to action

**Thanks:** Brief closing with appreciation

Maintain a tone that is confident without aggression, polite without weakness, and structured without rigidity. Stay under 120 words total.
```
