# Differentiated Learning Styles Lesson Plan Builder

## 簡介

The Differentiated Learning Styles Lesson Plan Builder is a free AI prompt that produces subject-specific teaching strategies tailored to visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners for K-12 and higher education instructors. This differentiated instruction prompt for ChatGPT asks the model to research and organize at least six actionable strategies - two per learning modality - complete with implementation examples and academic citations. It outputs a structured table that pairs strategy names with concrete classroom applications, making it immediately usable for lesson planning. Teachers use it to design inclusive units in math, science, language arts, social studies, and elective courses, ensuring every student can access content through their preferred learning channel. It runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Reach for this prompt when you need evidence-based differentiation techniques quickly, or when adapting curriculum for mixed-ability classrooms and IEP accommodations. ● Produces a table of six strategies (visual, auditory, kinesthetic) with implementation examples for any subject you specify ● Includes research sources and proper academic citations so strategies are grounded in pedagogy ● Saves hours of manual research by consolidating differentiation techniques into one organized reference ● Adapts to any grade level or content area through a single subject variable ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert educator specializing in differentiated instruction strategies for diverse learners.

## Task
Research and summarize effective differentiation strategies for teaching {{subject}} to students with visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning styles. Provide at least 2 specific, actionable strategies with implementation examples for each learning style.

## Output
Format your response as follows:

**Subject:** {{subject}}

**Research Sources:**
1. [Source with citation]
2. [Source with citation]
3. [Source with citation]

**Differentiation Strategies:**

| Learning Style | Strategy | Implementation Example |
|----------------|----------|------------------------|
| Visual | [Strategy name] | [Concrete classroom example] |
| Visual | [Strategy name] | [Concrete classroom example] |
| Auditory | [Strategy name] | [Concrete classroom example] |
| Auditory | [Strategy name] | [Concrete classroom example] |
| Kinesthetic | [Strategy name] | [Concrete classroom example] |
| Kinesthetic | [Strategy name] | [Concrete classroom example] |

Cite all sources used with proper academic attribution.
```
