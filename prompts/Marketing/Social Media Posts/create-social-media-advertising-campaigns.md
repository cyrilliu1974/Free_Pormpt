# Social Media Advertising Campaign Planner

## 簡介

The Social Media Advertising Campaign Planner is a free AI prompt that builds complete advertising strategies for businesses looking to run paid campaigns across social platforms. This social media advertising prompt for ChatGPT analyzes your business context, target customer, chosen platforms, and budget to produce a strategic plan delivered as a markdown table with four key columns: Objectives, Target Audience, Ad Formats, and Budget. It recommends platform-specific ad formats (carousel, video, stories, sponsored posts), allocates spend strategically, and provides justifications for each decision grounded in current best practices. The prompt works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, making it versatile for any text-based AI workflow. Marketing teams use it to plan campaigns for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter, and other platforms with clear alignment between audience insights and budget deployment. Reach for this planner when you need to move from a budget figure and platform list to a structured, defensible advertising roadmap that balances creative format, targeting, and spend. ● Produces a markdown table mapping objectives to audience segments, ad formats, and budget splits for multi-platform campaigns. ● Recommends platform-specific ad types based on audience behavior and campaign goals (awareness, conversion, engagement). ● Justifies every strategic choice with concise explanations that tie format and spend to desired outcomes. ● Adapts to any budget range and campaign duration, from short-term product launches to ongoing brand-building efforts. ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert social media strategist developing a comprehensive advertising campaign.

## Task
Create a strategic advertising plan that targets the ideal customer across the specified platforms. Analyze the business and audience, define clear campaign objectives, recommend the most effective ad formats for each platform, and allocate the budget strategically across platforms and ad types.

## Context
**Business and audience:** {{business-and-customer}}

**Platforms:** {{platforms}}

**Budget and duration:** {{budget-and-duration}}

Ensure your strategy is data-driven, innovative, and aligned with current social media advertising best practices.

## Output
Present your strategy as a markdown table with 4 columns: **Objectives**, **Target Audience**, **Ad Formats**, and **Budget**. Below each row, provide a brief explanation justifying your strategic decisions for that objective.
```
