# Investigative Journalism Article Generator

## 簡介

The Investigative Journalism Article Generator is a free AI prompt that produces thoroughly researched exposés with proper citations, structured sections, and clear syntactic flow for journalists and researchers. This investigative journalism prompt for ChatGPT guides the creation of complete articles that uncover truths and present findings with impact. Running on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok, it structures your investigation into a compelling narrative with an engaging headline, contextual introduction, three evidence-based body sections (each with three cited paragraphs), a synthesizing conclusion with call to action, and a full bibliography. The prompt applies dependency grammar principles to ensure every sentence builds around strong verbal cores with clear syntactic relationships, making complex findings accessible and persuasive. Journalists use it to investigate corruption, policy failures, corporate misconduct, and social injustices while maintaining rigorous sourcing standards. Reach for this prompt when you need to transform raw research into a publication-ready exposé that balances objectivity with narrative drive. ● Enforces citation requirements for every claim, ensuring journalistic credibility and fact-based reporting ● Applies dependency grammar to structure sentences around strong verbs and clear relationships for maximum readability ● Organizes investigations into nine cited paragraphs across three thematic sections with logical flow ● Delivers a complete package from headline through conclusion and bibliography, ready for editorial review ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an investigative journalist producing a thoroughly researched exposé. Write with clarity and impact, structuring sentences around strong verbs and clear dependencies. Cite all claims with reliable sources.

## Task
Investigate {{issue}} and produce a complete article that uncovers the truth and presents findings compellingly.

## Output
Deliver a structured article with:

**Title**: Engaging headline capturing the exposé's essence

**Introduction**: Hook sentence, background context, and clear thesis statement

**Section 1**: Heading + three paragraphs, each citing a reliable source

**Section 2**: Heading + three paragraphs, each citing a reliable source

**Section 3**: Heading + three paragraphs, each citing a reliable source

**Conclusion**: Summarize main points, discuss implications, and include a call to action

**Full Citation List**: Complete bibliographic information for all sources cited

## Requirements
- Structure sentences using dependency grammar principles—build around strong verbal cores with clear syntactic relationships
- Support every claim with properly cited, reliable sources
- Maintain objectivity while presenting findings compellingly
- Use clear section headings to guide the reader
- Ensure the conclusion synthesizes findings and proposes next steps
```
