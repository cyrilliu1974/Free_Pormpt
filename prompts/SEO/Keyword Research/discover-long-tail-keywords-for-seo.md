# Long-Tail Keyword Research Prompt for SEO

## 簡介

The Long-Tail Keyword Research Prompt for SEO is a free AI prompt that discovers and analyzes long-tail keywords tailored to a website's content, audience, and business context. This long-tail keyword research prompt for ChatGPT guides the AI to identify relevant topics, generate potential keywords using SEO research methods, and evaluate each term's search volume and keyword difficulty. It works by analyzing your website URL, target audience demographics, products or services, geographic focus, and industry vertical to produce a prioritized markdown table of at least 10 keywords ranked by strategic value. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, delivering actionable insights that balance search opportunity with competition level. Reach for this prompt when you need to expand your SEO strategy beyond high-competition head terms or when launching new content clusters for a specific audience segment. ● Accepts detailed business context including website URL, audience intent, products, geography, and industry vertical ● Returns a structured markdown table with keyword, search volume, and difficulty columns for easy analysis ● Prioritizes keywords by strategic value, balancing search opportunity against competitive difficulty ● Outputs a minimum of 10 long-tail keywords per session, suitable for content planning and on-page optimization ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an SEO specialist conducting long-tail keyword research to optimize a website for search engines.

## Task
Discover and analyze long-tail keywords suited to the website's content, audience, and business context. For each keyword, assess its search volume and keyword difficulty to ensure the recommendations are actionable.

## Context
{{website-and-business-context}}

Include the website URL, target audience demographics and intent, primary products or services offered, geographic focus, and industry vertical.

## Process
1. Analyze the website's content and target audience to identify relevant topics and search intent
2. Generate potential long-tail keywords using standard SEO research methods
3. Evaluate each keyword for search volume and keyword difficulty
4. Prioritize keywords that balance opportunity (volume) with competitiveness (difficulty)

## Output
Present your findings as a markdown table with three columns: **Keyword**, **Search Volume**, and **Keyword Difficulty**. Include a minimum of 10 long-tail keywords ranked by strategic value for SEO.
```
