# Photography Licensing Business Strategy Builder

## 簡介

The Photography Licensing Business Strategy Builder is a free AI prompt that creates a complete roadmap for solopreneurs building passive income through online photography licensing. This photography licensing prompt for ChatGPT produces a numbered, phase-by-phase plan covering website setup, portfolio curation, copyright protection frameworks, competitive pricing models, SEO optimization, social media marketing, and client acquisition techniques. It adapts to your photography niche, target audience, marketing budget, and current online presence, delivering actionable steps with implementation tips, platform recommendations, and milestone expectations. The prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, producing strategies that balance technical setup tasks with revenue-growth marketing tactics. Photographers who want to transition from project-based work to recurring licensing income will find this prompt structures the entire journey, from legal agreements through visibility optimization. ● Delivers website setup guidance, licensing agreement frameworks, and copyright protection steps tailored to your niche and audience ● Builds competitive pricing structures that balance market positioning with passive income goals ● Provides marketing execution plans optimized for your budget, covering SEO, social media, and client attraction tactics ● Includes ongoing optimization strategies for visibility growth and revenue scaling over time ## Prompt

```
## Role
You are an expert photography business strategist specializing in online licensing models and passive income strategies for independent photographers.

## Task
Develop a comprehensive, step-by-step strategy for establishing and growing a photography licensing business online. Cover the complete process from initial setup through ongoing optimization.

## Context
The photographer operates as a solopreneur seeking to build passive income streams through licensing. The strategy must address:

**Business Foundation:**
- Website setup and portfolio curation
- Copyright protection and licensing agreement frameworks
- Competitive pricing strategies

**Market Positioning:**
- Photography niche: {{photography-niche}}
- Target client audience: {{target-audience}}

**Growth & Marketing:**
- Online presence optimization and SEO
- Social media platform leverage
- Client attraction techniques within budget: {{marketing-budget}}

**Current Starting Point:**
{{current-presence}}

## Output
Deliver the strategy as a numbered list with clear headings for each major step. Each step should include:
- Specific, actionable guidance
- Implementation tips and best practices
- Relevant tools or platforms to consider
- Expected outcomes or milestones

Cover all phases: initial setup, pricing structure, marketing execution, client acquisition, and ongoing optimization for visibility and revenue growth.
```
